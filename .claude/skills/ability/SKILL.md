# /ability

Run `desktop/ABILITY_PIPELINE.md` end to end. The law is `desktop/NATALIE.md` (ABILITIES AND THE WORLD, Technique entries, DEPTH AND EXPLANATION, Describing a working). Never call ChatGPT or Codex (Isaac has no plan).

1. Read the sketch, the character's card and their refusal. Open the Superpower Wiki page for the nearest power (`lookup("superpower", "<Power Name>")` on the WOTR MCP, whose other sources cover kits, vows, ladders and blows; by hand, `curl` its `api.php` with a browser user agent, since the HTML 403s) and read Capabilities, Applications, Techniques, Variations, Associations and Limitations.
2. The concept: one wild idea you can explain in a breath, and its hook, with no system words. Then the real phenomenon, the law, the glyph's boundary condition and the failure mode (`wotr-phenomenon`), and the Wellspring analogue and Physics Domain that explain it.
3. Design: three to five named moves, the mechanism, a fair counter at its own Stage, a price that comes from the idea, limit, tell, and a visible charge.
3b. Write the entry (R82-20): name in caps with native name and kind, hook, named moves a line each, How it really works, one Cost line, one Counter line, Numbers block at the bottom.
4. Folded into How it really works: no separate Applications list, and the physics goes in the entry.
5. Add the Wellspring signature (`desktop/style-bank/wellspring-signatures.md`) and a signature sound word.
6. A signature or release gets a short sample first display for Isaac's approval before it is saved; a small trick goes straight to the card.
7. One best name, with the reason.
8. Compare with existing kits; surface any overlap to Isaac with a recommendation.
9. Full research to `research_notes/`, one line in chat. File the entry through the wiki tools (private until Isaac promotes it).

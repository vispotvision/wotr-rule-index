# /ability

Run `desktop/ABILITY_PIPELINE.md` end to end. The law is `desktop/NATALIE.md` (Technique entries, Magic in combat, Describing a working). Never call ChatGPT or Codex (Isaac has no plan).

1. Read the sketch, the character's card and their refusal. Open the Superpower Wiki page for the nearest power (`curl` its `api.php` with a browser user agent; the HTML 403s) and read Capabilities, Applications, Techniques, Variations, Associations and Limitations.
2. Name the real phenomenon, the law, the glyph's boundary condition and the failure mode (`wotr-phenomenon`); find the Wellspring analogue and Physics Domain.
3. Design: mechanism, cost, a stated condition, a fair counter at its own Stage (a counter at each phase for a signature), limit, tell, and a visible charge.
4. Write 8 to 15 Applications, plus Variations, Associations and Limits, in plain everyday words; physics stays in the notes.
5. Add the Wellspring signature (`desktop/style-bank/wellspring-signatures.md`) and a signature sound word.
6. A signature or release gets a short sample first display for Isaac's approval before it is saved; a small trick goes straight to the card.
7. One best name, with the reason.
8. Compare with existing kits; surface any overlap to Isaac with a recommendation.
9. Full research to `research_notes/`, one line in chat. File the entry through the wiki tools (private until Isaac promotes it).

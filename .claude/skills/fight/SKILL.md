# /fight

The fight pipeline, in order. The law is `desktop/NATALIE.md` (THE SHEET AND THE CLIMB, Fights on the page, the fight craft block, Magic in combat, Describing a working, Onomatopoeia). This skill only sequences it. Never call ChatGPT or Codex (Isaac has no plan).

1. **Load.** `load_rules(tags=[], brief=True)` once a session; then prose-law, combat, adjudication, magic-mechanism and pov in full the first time. Read the named fighters' cards and `fow_line`; use the wotr-stat-line loadout.
2. **Exemplars.** Read `desktop/style-bank/fight-exemplars/EXEMPLARS.md`, `desktop/style-bank/exemplars/TECHNIQUE_DISPLAY.md` and `desktop/style-bank/sound-lexicon.md`. Learn the density; take no images. The exemplars predate windows, so windows come from the law, not from them. Check `desktop/style-bank/used-devices.md` so nothing is reused.
3. **Research.** The wounds that will matter (`wotr-wound`), how each working works (`wotr-phenomenon`, the Superpower Wiki, the Wellspring analogue), trades and period detail. Use `lookup`: `wiktenauer` for real plays and measure, `vsbattles` for how a comparable kit fights and loses, `hxh`, `jjk` and `bleach` for conditions, Domains and releases, `datamuse` for fresh impact words and sound-alikes (`desktop/NATALIE.md`, Outside resources). Notes go to `research_notes/`.
4. **State sheet.** Fill `desktop/templates/FIGHT_STATE_SHEET.md`: ground, positions, distances, weapons, wounds, reserve, the planted clue, the ladder, the zoom and volume map (one movement at peak), and the windows per movement (section 4c). Each working takes its Wellspring signature from `desktop/style-bank/wellspring-signatures.md`.
5. **Brief.** `scene_brief` for a set piece.
6. **Draft** to `~/wotr-drafts/<slug>.md`: probe, reveal, counter, escalate, price; the Blow Card on every blow, landed or not, quick trades compressed and never thinned (R81-1), with the fighters placed in space between exchanges; the Kill Card extending the Blow Card at the turn (R81-18); magic riding on the blow with the two cards interleaved, moves called at the volume the character would use, name, hit and awe first with the account right after, scaled by size: the full Working Card at a signature's first use, a finisher and any change, a line for a small move (R82-24, R82-25), the real phenomenon and its law named on the page (R81-7), η as an idea and a proportion (R81-3), held cards shown by effect then explained at the reveal (R81-5), the world answering it in vivid language chosen fresh and technical terms explained (DEPTH AND EXPLANATION in `desktop/NATALIE.md`); windows often and in detail for damage, skill gains and reserve, each a text block after the blow it reports, the POV's own, in Grades, percentages, bars and levels with EU, AU/s and η in the notes, a Release or Domain filling the window with its name, what changed and a draining timer bar, the narration body first throughout, a wound as a status line saying what it stops him doing (R84-1, R84-2, R84-17, R84-29; format in `wotr-stat-line`); constant banter; sound words per the lexicon; American spelling. No word count: the fight is as long as it needs.
7. **Review.** `desktop/FIGHT_REVIEW_PASS.md` and `desktop/PROSE_REVIEW_PASS.md`, windows included, silently, fixing before Isaac sees it. Add the fight's signature image to `used-devices.md`.
8. **Humanize.** `humanize` on the draft, then the minor-fix pass so it flows (`desktop/NATALIE.md`, The Session), a lost Blow Card or Working Card part above all.
9. **Verify.** `verify_scene` with combat=True; fix every FAIL.
10. **Deliver.** The scene alone, no commentary. A set piece waits for his word before it is archived; an ordinary turn archives at scene end. Upkeep (Ledger, Fronts, roster, cards, the Trait levels and skill Grades the windows moved) is silent; his PC's gains are offered as window lines he keeps or changes (R84-23).

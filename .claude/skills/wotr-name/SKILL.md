# wotr-name

A name only has to sound right for its culture (R79-7). There is no
end-of-scene name check. Naming register is not ethnicity.

## Before naming

1. `load_rules(tags=["naming"])` the first time a session names anything.
2. `wiki("Naming and Usage")` and, for the culture in hand, its element
   inventory (`wiki("Moto Element Inventory")`, `wiki("Element Inventories
   Mahuo Yukari")`; the Celestial Host has its own page). The culture's
   Standing Inventory comes with `session_start(thread, culture=...)`.
3. `wiki(name)` or `character(name)` first: never name a second person what a
   carded one is already called.

## The five strata

- **Japonic**: archaic bloodlines, the Moto among them. Moto and Hataraki,
  never Ajiin. Ruling house the Kōkan Line (Sodoku Moto, Muken Moto); rival
  house the Shirogane.
- **Korean**: the Mahuo.
- **Chinese**: the lineage halls, which stack titles in formal address.
- **Northern English and Norse**: chartered families and the commons.
- **Far-Northern carried names**: tundra peoples carry a roll-name the Accord
  filed and a carried name the household uses.

Büri and Mongolian-register names are dead. Never coin one; flag one you find.

## Comic names

Bynames and nicknames are the norm in musters, crews, taverns and markets and
thin above the commons: rhyming sets, talking surnames, plausible puns,
soldiers' nicknames, mangled names, protective ugly names, each in its
culture's sound rules. Recurring minors, villains and rivals may carry one;
major NPCs and every carded Moto stay strict. An English pun needs a bridge in
the world. A walk-on is named at once only by a nickname in someone else's
mouth; the POV may notice the joke, the narrator never remarks on it.

## Techniques and arts

The one-beat test: the name is the thing. A school's art may run as numbered
forms; hall and Korean-stratum arts may carry a four-syllable true name,
glossed literally on the card. On the page and in the shout it is called in
English or the speaker's own tongue, as the character would.

## Output

Offer three names unless one is asked for, each with a line on its stratum
and what it says. A name used in an archived scene is canon.

# FOW anchors (memory of source; confirm against Fracture_of_Worlds before use)

Level max 500. Bands: I Mortal Foundation 1 to 100 (Stages I to IV), II
Awakened 101 to 200 (V to VII), III Sovereign 201 to 300 (VIII to X), IV
Mythic 301 to 400 (XI to XII), V Absolute 401 to 500 (XIII to XIV).

Stages, source names: I Murmuring, II Welling, III Ascension, IV Flourishing,
V Splintering, VI Glory, VII Refraction, VIII Transcendence, IX Invocation,
X Realization, XI Dissonance, XII Emanation, XIII Principality, XIV Zenith,
XV Revelation, XVI Apex.

Tier Grades on raw value: Hollow 1 to 10, F 11 to 25, E 26 to 50, D 51 to
100, C 101 to 175, B 176 to 275, A 276 to 400, S 401 to 550, SS 551 to 725,
SSS 726 to 950, X 951 to 1200, EX 1201 to 1500, EX+ 1501 up.

Stage sustainable max: I E/50; II D/100; III C/175; IV B/275; V B inst./350;
VI A/400; VII A, late push S/475; VIII S/550; IX S, late push SS/625; X
SS/725; XI SS, fracture above 700/750; XII SSS/950; XIII X/1200; XIV EX/1500;
XV EX+/2200; XVI uncapped.

Nine guild titles, the same in every guild: Initiate, Apprentice, Journeyman,
Adept, Expert, Master, Grandmaster, Archmaster, Paragon. A title grades trust in
a trade, so a Grandmaster merchant may not be able to throw a punch. Only the
fighting guilds read it off the Stage table, as their Tier of Standing, with eta
underneath: Initiate ~0.30, Apprentice ~0.35, Journeyman ~0.40, Adept ~0.45,
Expert 0.50 to 0.60, Master 0.70 to 0.80, Grandmaster 0.85 to 0.90, Archmaster
0.95 to 1.2, Paragon above 1.2 (no source figure). Hollow and F confer no rank.
Open ruling: Class I Muridic's loss figure contradicts the Initiate to Adept
eta; both stand.

Class: a named class the soul is offered at thresholds, fitting how the person
has lived; it gives abilities, shapes growth and evolves when the lead ladder
rises (R84-10). Three ladders set η by layer (R85-26). Essence Class: Ø Silent,
I Listener, II Answerer, III Confessor, IV Shepherd, V Ferryman, VI Hollow, Ω
Absolute. Thread Class: Ø Unthreaded, I Touched, II Drawn, III Spinner, IV
Knotwright, V Weaver, VI Severer, Ω Absolute, with η two rungs below the same
numeral. Aether Class, the physical influence: Ø Dormant; I Muridic (~30 to 40% loss); II
Harmonic (~15 to 25%); III Resonant (~5 to 10%); IV Luminous (lateral); V Radiant
(can exceed 100%); VI Voidic (lateral, Limina); Ω Absolute. Anyone at Stage VIII
can have a Domain, whatever the tier (R84-17).

The climb: Level moves by danger overcome (R84-4). A Catalyst may be supplied by
a Draft or blessing, and a Stage can be bought; a bought Stage has no Release
until it is found and mastered by fighting (R84-5, R84-6).

Soul Crystal tiers: Dormant, Awakened, Harmonic, Resonant, Radiant,
Sovereign, Crystallized Soul, Absolute Crystal.

Families: Caloria, Fulguria, Vectoria, Spatium, Materia, Vitalia, Fluxia,
Limina.

Primary = sum of its eight Sub-Stats; points buy Sub-Stats only; Grade read
off the mean. Throne uncapped and outside Dominion. Points per level by
Band 12/15/18/21/24; concentration caps 5/5/8/10/12. Life spends most points by
what the person does, and a few are theirs to place at each Stage (R84-9).

Physical Force speed: B-Grade travel Mach 1.5 to 4, reaction 1 to 5 ms;
A-Grade travel Mach 5 to 25, reaction sub-millisecond.

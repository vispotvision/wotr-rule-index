# Scene pipeline: turns and set pieces

## Domain tags (pick two to four, `prose-law` always for prose)

| Scene kind | Tags |
|---|---|
| duel / small fight between named characters | prose-law, combat, adjudication, magic-mechanism, pov |
| battle with formations | prose-law, mass-combat, adjudication, pov, scene-structure |
| council, court, negotiation, any talky scene | prose-law, dialogue, register, pov, naming |
| quiet / interior / travel | prose-law, pov, scene-structure, standing-inventory, register |
| explicit scene | prose-law, dialogue, pov, scene-structure |

| firearms, armour, gear | prose-law, items, combat, adjudication |
| session start or end | session-protocol |

The moment a formation exists, mass-combat rules take over: sensory order
inverts to sound, pressure, smell, sight; the POV never knows how the battle is
going; no more than three ordinary soldiers named (a marked roll call is
exempt, R70-11); the battle cuts between the commander's hill and one man in
the press at marked breaks, each section POV-locked (R70-106); the rout is the
scene.

## Turn shape

Answer what the PC did. Move the world. Stop at his next decision. Never resolve
a multi-decision action in one go. End on an NPC line, a physical action, or a
thing he can now see. Never on an out-of-character question.

Every turn carries: layered sensory grounding; Inventory texture before
invention; one thing he notices that goes unexplained; one specific visible cost
for any action; NPCs who want things and will refuse, lie, or bargain. One
italic private thought per NPC per scene, not per turn.

Scene Menu when he opens without a beat: three hooks, one from a Front, one
from the Ledger, one fresh. Rumor Mill at session start: two or three pieces of
news in an NPC's mouth, some wrong, one a hook.

## Adjudication without dice

Stage gap and Dominion decide Pressure. Dexterity decides measure and reaction.
Ardency Penetration against armour tier. Gnosis for the read. Tempering for
duration and push. Harmonics against local Aetheric Density for the draw.
Vitality for wounds, ATLS class on top. Resilience for what a working does to
the Crystal. Path gates for what a character cannot do. Name the row that
decided in the author notes. State the result physically: the body first, the
figure a line after (R70-89). Narration states the figures the POV holds
(R70-83); readouts and who may read whom are in `wotr-stat-line` (R70-78 to
R70-99).

Pressure shows in the room first (smoke, frost, lamp flames, the draw-hum,
animals), then in bodies (R70-69): one Stage up bends attention; two, sweat
and nausea; three or more drops people; a Band gap changes the weather.
Never named as an aura. Suppression tires. Killing intent is Pressure aimed through Dominion and needs
a ruled mechanism before first use (R70-70).

The read is the game. A self-derived technique gets two or three exchanges of
evidence before it kills him. Mechanism reaches the page through at most two of
the four voices per engagement: the POV's own read capped at his competence (a
confident wrong read is the best tool), the knowledgeable second, the opponent
as contempt-instruction, the document at the threshold. Plant a working's
rules before the fight (training, a document, talk) so the fight's explaining
is recall (R70-71); a readout explains nothing and sits outside the cap
(R70-87).

## Set piece standards

2,500 floor. Opening layers smell, texture, sound, or runs far to near, on a
season word, or on a reading the scene then tests (R70-16). Ends on physical
action, or on a mid-crisis hook, a readout, a break before the payoff, or a
closing poem (R70-17, R70-64). Jo-ha-kyū tempo: slow, breaking, swift
(R70-15); paired and cumulative sentences licensed here only (R70-19).
One italic thought per named NPC. Ignorance quota (one unexplained physical
observation), misreading budget (one wrong POV inference left uncorrected),
reification two per scene, elegiac passage at most one and never at the close.
Moves named in the POV's own school's vocabulary (HEMA for a Western blade,
kesa-giri for a Moto POV; a foreign school's word arrives in a mouth, R70-100);
duel shape follows the combat grammar (R70-102). Three-layer hit (Physics, Essence,
Hermetic) as three causal sentences at first display and finisher. Gore in
full. The POV's own body on the page as much as the opponent's. Aftermath
never skipped, including after any explicit scene.

Explicit scenes: plain anatomical vocabulary, clinical specificity, positions
tracked through every significant movement, arousal scents layered,
onomatopoeia committed, the NPC's own desire and refusal line, a consequence
afterward.

First introductions: a major person, place or creature gets the full
inventory, a minor person one exact stroke (R70-54), in the order this POV
looks (R70-55): hair by comparison, face, body with areas named, clothing with
fit and wear, distinguishing marks; then a want and an unswappable voice. Each
return carries one changed detail (R70-50).

## Register

Third person only; first person stays in documents (R70-12). Modern words in
speech and italic thought only (R70-37); narration and documents stay
timeless, and the checker's modern-word list catches slang in narration
(R48-13, R49-44, R51). The POV's culture sets cadence and the antique dial
(R70-21, R70-27).
Voice differentiation is the surviving law: if two characters' lines could swap
unnoticed, the scene failed; voices are mind and sound in equal measure (R52-02).

Humour wherever earned; comic voices and double acts named on their cards
get full comic beats, retort and reaction included, and everyone else stays
under R5-D (R70-113). Sodoku jokes only by accident (R52-14).

## Naming

Before any name: the Character Naming Guide and Amendment, the Inner World
Naming Amendment, the Moto Reversion Ledger. Five strata: Japonic (archaic
bloodlines including Moto), Korean (Mahuo), Chinese (lineage halls), Northern
English and Norse (chartered families and commons), Far-Northern carried-name
logic (tundra peoples, who carry a roll-name the Accord filed and a carried name
the household uses). Büri and Mongolian-register names are stale; never
propagate one, flag it. Technique names pass the one-beat test: the name is the
thing. Naming register is not ethnicity. Run `stale_names` when touching old
pages. A school's art may run as numbered forms; hall and Korean-stratum arts
may carry a four-syllable true name, glossed literally (R70-74).

Comic names (R70-119 to R70-132): bynames and nicknames are the norm in
musters, crews, taverns and markets and thin above the commons (R70-119);
rhyming sets, talking surnames, plausible puns, soldiers' nicknames, mangled
names, sound comedy and protective ugly names, each in its culture's sound
rules (R70-120, R70-121, R70-124, R70-125); recurring minors, villains and
rivals may carry one, major NPCs and every carded Moto stay strict (R70-122,
R70-125); an English pun needs a bridge in the world (R70-123); a walk-on is
named at once only by a nickname in someone else's mouth and does not die in
that scene (R70-130); the POV may notice the joke, the narrator never remarks
(R70-129); the joke becomes the wound at a death (R70-127). Coined by ear,
passed as outliers while they keep the sound rules (R70-131). Borin
Ironheart's brothers on the shared Rin, Snorin among them, wait on Isaac's yes
to the names (R70-126).

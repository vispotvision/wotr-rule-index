# Judger's note — The Muster, the Breach, and the Road North — Kharven thread, the year after

## Recap for the players

One morning, four places. On a muster field south of the Tenrai camp, Wren Greymane put six hundred men under the Greymane banner and called himself the true Shield of the North, and Seiji Tenrai Moto sent four riders out at the second hour with reports that were not true. At the southern reach, where forty feet of wall had gone in the night, Bram Greymane's three hundred held a twenty-two-foot gap for four hours against the men of the Expanse, and at the ninth hour they were still coming through. Wren's column marched straight at Kharven-Seat behind Kujo's generals, with Kaalabad at the head of it, and took Ashgate and one village beyond it without anybody in the column drawing a blade. At Kharven-Seat, Lorn Stark stood on the outer wall with three reports that did not agree, Sodoku came down into the yard in full harness and rode out ahead of the whole muster, and Edward Lambert left through the postern with six riders and no banner.

## What the scene settled

- Egil Vald died at the twelfth rotation in the breach, silently, and the man beside him stepped across the gap. *"Egil Vald went down at the twelfth rotation with a hooked blade under the cuisse and the femoral opened and he was dead in the time it takes to fill a bucket"*
- Bram finished the morning on foot behind a dead horse, his shield strapped to a forearm he could not feel. *"on foot by then with a dead horse behind him and his shield strapped to a forearm he could no longer feel"*
- Bram carries the same shield he threw down at the ridge; the crack in the boss is rebound twice and the whole line knows what it is. *"the same shield, the one he had thrown down at the ridge and picked back up, and the crack in the boss was still there and had been rebound twice"*
- Bram's three hundred held four hours, and by the fifteenth rotation men who could not lift a shield went back in the front. *"it worked for the first nine rotations and got ragged at the eleventh and by the fifteenth they were rotating men who could not lift the shield to head height"*
- The cold, not courage, is what beat the Expanse. *"They were not weak. They were three-quarters of a beat late, all of them, and the lateness was the only currency Bram's men had."*
- Bram knew nothing about his own right flank for eleven minutes and had no idea whether the fight was going well. *"he knew nothing at all about his right for a period he later worked out at eleven minutes and experienced as either a moment or an afternoon"*
- Seiji planted four reports, all signed by hands the Seat's clerks know. *"By tonight the Seat will have three separate reports that we are moving on the Stormfold road and one that we have already taken the Brine crossing, and all four will be signed by men whose hands their clerks know."*
- The column did not feint. *"We march directly. We put ourselves into their hands and we slaughter the last of a dead line."*
- The Ice claim is refused in Seiji's own words. *"That is the whole of it and it has been the whole of it for nineteen years."*
- Miku Tenrai Moto is named as the one heir three houses recognise, and nobody on the page knows where he is. *"Miku Tenrai Moto, trueborn to Azure Tenrai Moto, who was High King of the North while these people were still deciding what to call themselves."*
- Wren's six hundred have paid sixteen months for the cover. *"Sixteen months of being spat at in their own villages. Eleven of them had wives who had stopped writing."*
- There is a book in Wren's saddlebag with three of his own men in it and nobody knows it exists. *"Three of them had been killed by their own kin and Wren had entered all three in a book he kept in his saddlebag and had told nobody about the book."*
- Wren claimed the banner and the title aloud. *"Under the banner of Greymane. The true Greymane. The true Shield of the North."*
- Kujo's generals set the pace and do not consult Seiji. *"There were four of them that Wren could name and two that he could not, and none of them consulted Seiji, and Seiji did not appear to expect it."*
- Kaalabad rides a thing that is not a horse and has not spoken since the muster. *"It stood nineteen hands and its barrel was plated along the flanks in a pattern that was under the skin instead of on it"*
- Ashgate had eleven houses and a mill; after one pass the mill burned from the inside and four houses went over sideways with people in three of them. *"four of the eleven houses had come off their footings and gone over sideways in the same direction, and there were people in three of them"*
- What Ashgate will describe is a grey wolf. *"what she will describe is a grey wolf, and my grandfather is under a stone eight leagues from here"*
- Wren knew by the second village that he had misread what he joined. *"They took the first village at the eleventh hour and Wren Greymane understood by the second one that he had misread what he had joined."*
- Something is opening the country ahead of the column and only Wren looks at it. *"Nobody in the column looked at it except Wren."*
- Lorn holds three reports that do not agree and knows two are paid. *"\"That is the third one of those,\" Lorn said. \"And they do not agree.\""*
- The villages on the Ashgate road have stopped answering. *"the villages on the Ashgate road had stopped answering, which he already had"*
- Every channel Edward runs north of the Ashgate road went dark this morning. *"every channel I have running north of the Ashgate road stopped reporting between the fourth and the fifth hour this morning"*
- Sonzai's last confirmed sighting is nine days old and untrusted. *"The last confirmed sighting is nine days old and it is from a man I do not trust"*
- Edward set his own deadline before riding out. *"if I am not back in five days you will assume the road is closed and act accordingly"*
- Edward rode the Stormfold road with six riders and no banner. *"I am fifty-three years old and I intend to do it on the Stormfold road with six riders and no banner"*
- Sodoku crossed the yard and the yard went quiet, and Lorn judged it was not respect. *"The men in the yard went quiet in a rolling wave that started where he entered it and went out to the walls"*
- Sodoku and Hild met: a hand on the back of her head, a kiss on the hair, nothing that carried to the wall. *"Then he put a hand on the back of her head and kissed her hair, and let go, and turned away"*
- Hild held the broken circlet in her hand instead of on her head. *"Hild was at the foot of the stair with Tabitha behind her and the broken circlet in her hand instead of on her head."*
- On one word the beast came up the outside of the north curtain wall and over the parapet. *"It came up the outside of the wall in four movements and put its forelimbs on the parapet and brought the rest of itself over"*
- The cold off it laid a white line down the road the whole muster then marched along. *"the cold that came off it laid a white line down the middle of the road that the whole muster then marched along"*
- Lorn ends the scene with the circlet in his belt. *"picked up the broken circlet where Hild had set it on the mounting block and forgotten it, and put it in his belt"*

# Judger's note — What the Sky Does Not Ask — Kharven thread, the year after

## Recap for the players

At the ninth hour on the Ashgate road, a quarter mile short of the narrows, Sodoku Moto watched six hundred Greymane spears reverse on the six hundred Tenrai they had marched beside, the officers going down off their horses first and together, and he understood a half-beat later that Wren Greymane had built it. He sent the Titan Droval up the road into other men's fight with two pats on the flank and swung down, and the temperature at the narrows dropped eleven degrees in under a minute. He opened the eye and it took him four hundred miles northwest instead, to his brother and Zuberi together in front of something with no edge, and cost him eleven counted seconds of blindness in the ruts. A rider came out of the narrows at forty feet with blood on his ear that was not his — Robin Ice, seventeen, on a blown horse — and delivered that the southern reach stands, that Bram had the breach on foot, that the Seat is closed with Osric holding the princess and the lady below the cistern, that Lambert went out the postern with six riders and no banner and said five days, and that Wren is on the road, standing, in front of the one with the halberd. Sodoku sent him back round the ditch with word that the road is closed and he is not coming back through it, then set the sword in his right hand and walked up into the narrows on foot.

## What the scene settled

- At least eleven Tenrai officers went down off their horses in the first moment of the turning, first and together. *"The Tenrai officers were already down. He counted eleven of them off the horses before he stopped counting."*
- A Tenrai serjeant with about forty men in a block facing the wrong way was speared from behind under the arm, and the forty broke. *"a Greymane spear went into him under the arm from behind while he was still shouting, and the forty came apart in the time it takes to notice they have."*
- Six hundred Greymane spears reversed on six hundred Tenrai, from the middle outward, over in one breath. *"Six hundred spears reversing on the six hundred they had marched beside."*
- Two thousand men had slept in harness before the ninth hour. *"the sourness of two thousand men who had slept in harness."*
- Sodoku watched the turn from the Droval's back a quarter mile short of the narrows. *"Sodoku Moto sat on the Titan Droval a quarter mile short of the narrows and watched the Greymanes turn."*
- He gave up the mount by a spoken order and two pats. *"\"Go and help them,\" Sodoku said. He patted the flank twice."*
- He is on foot for the rest of the scene, having spent the one asset that made men give ground. *"He was on foot now. He had put his mount into the fight and his mount was the reason anyone within a mile of him took a step back"*
- The arrangement with the creature was made on the ice, carries no promise, and can be revisited. *"There had been no promise made when the two of them came to terms on the ice and there was none now."*
- Sodoku has never let himself forget he is the smaller party to it. *"The creature had ten thousand years of winter in it and had chosen, and choosing is a thing that can be done twice"*
- The Droval's arrival dropped the temperature at the narrows eleven degrees in under a minute. *"The temperature at the narrows dropped eleven degrees in under a minute. Every Tenrai man in that press felt his fingers report late."*
- What he sent is enough for the foot and nothing at all for the thing at the far end. *"it would be enough for the foot, and it would be nothing at all for the thing standing on the road with the purple around it."*
- Reigan costs him ordinary sight for as long as he holds it. *"a man who opens that eye stops having the ordinary one for as long as he keeps it open."*
- The read cost eleven counted seconds; at nine he heard the horse. *"The read cost him eleven seconds. He knew the number because he counted while he was blind, which is a habit"*
- Letting go cost him a physical lurch. *"The world came back in a rush that put his stomach into his throat and greyed the top and sides of everything for a moment"*
- Coming out half-blind, the sword was already drawn and low along his right leg. *"there was a rider forty feet away and coming, and the sword was already out of Sodoku's scabbard and low along his right leg with the edge turned out."*
- The eye did not go where he aimed it. *"the eye had taken him past all of that in the first instant and put him four hundred miles away without being asked, because Reigan goes to weight"*
- His brother is north and west at an unmeasurable distance with the signature wrong. *"His brother came up first, north and west, at a distance he could not have put in miles, and the signature was wrong"*
- Zuberi reads the same and is beside him. *"Zuberi was beside him and the reading was the same."*
- Both run at an output no living body sustains, feeding something he cannot measure. *"both of them running at an output that no living body sustains, and the load coming off them was going into something that Sodoku could feel the shape of and could not measure at all."*
- He names it the Work, made by the one who took the Crevice; neither man is winning and neither will stop. *"The one who took the Crevice. He is out there, and he has both of them at once, and neither of them is winning and neither of them will stop."*
- Pushed further, the read found no edge to it. *"He put the eye further out and found the edge of the thing they were standing in front of. It had no edge."*
- He reads the turning as Wren's design, with no pleasure in it. *"Wren Greymane had built this. That was the second thing Sodoku understood and it arrived a half-beat behind the first, and there was no pleasure anywhere in it."*
- In his own head he credits the inside man to Sonzai, uncorrected. *"He put a man inside. That is his hand and nobody else's, and I have known him since we were both small enough to fit under the same table"*
- Nineteen years of knowing him, and it never once occurred to him. *"Nineteen years and it did not occur to me once. I have been thinking about walls."*
- He names and claims the fault. *"A man who thinks in walls does not think of the door being opened from inside. That is the whole of the fault and it is mine"*
- The outer town burned its winter wood this morning on one asking. *"the outer town had been told to burn its winter wood this morning and had done it without argument, which was a thing Sodoku had asked for"*
- The price: the Thin Weeks on borrowed heat and short rations, the death-house taking what the cold takes. *"If the road held, they would be through the Thin Weeks on borrowed heat and short rations and the death-house would take whatever the cold took"*
- The standing order given in the yard before light. *"Hold the walls of the inner court. Hold the gatehouse. Do not sortie for anything, including him."*
- Hild and Yoko are below the old cistern with Osric, stone on six sides and one way in; Osric repeated the order back unasked. *"Hild was inside. Yoko with her, and Osric with both, and the order had been given in the yard before light and Osric had repeated it back word for word without being asked"*
- Sodoku ordered Osric into the doorway and did not say it out loud. *"And if the wall comes down he will put himself in the doorway, and that is a thing I ordered and did not say out loud."*
- Report received: the southern reach stands and Bram had the breach on foot. *"The southern reach is still standing. Bram had the breach at the ninth hour and was holding it and I saw him on foot."*
- Report received: the Seat is closed, the inner court manned, the gatehouse shut. *"The Seat is closed and the inner court is manned. Lord Osric has the princess and the lady below the cistern and has ordered the gatehouse shut behind him."*
- Lambert left by the postern with six riders, no banner, and said five days. *"And Lord Lambert went out the postern before the gates opened. Six riders. No banner. He said five days."*
- Sodoku's reading of it: best or worst timing of the man's life, unknowable for five days. *"Lambert going out the postern before the gates opened meant Lambert had left with the road still open and would have been on the Stormfold before the aurora came up"*
- Wren's position, reported. *"\"On the road. Standing.\" Robin Ice's jaw moved. \"In front of the one with the halberd.\""*
- Sodoku's judgement: Wren dead inside the quarter hour. *"he was standing on that road in front of the halberd with a shield on his arm and would be dead inside the quarter hour."*
- Sixteen months of every village between here and the Stormfold calling Wren traitor. *"Every village between here and the Stormfold saying the word traitor about a man who had been standing in the enemy's saddle waiting for a road narrow enough."*
- Robin Ice rode through the narrows unordered, with blood on his left ear that was not his. *"Blood on his left ear that was not his. He had ridden through the narrows to get here, which nobody had told him to do and nobody would have permitted."*
- He is seventeen, in a courier's coat two sizes too big. *"Seventeen. A face still deciding what it would be, thin at the cheek and heavy at the brow, black hair cut by somebody in a hurry, ice in it."*
- He carried four things, rehearsed for six miles, and had already lost one. *"I have got four things and I have been rehearsing the order for six miles and I have already lost one of them."*
- Word sent to Osric: the road is closed and the gate is his until Sodoku returns. *"Tell Osric the road is closed and I am not coming back through it. Tell him he has the gate until I do."*
- Robin went round the ditch without being told twice; Sodoku filed his name with the ones who came back. *"he took the long way round the ditch without being told twice, and Sodoku watched him do it and put the boy's name into the part of his head where he kept the ones who came back."*
- Four hundred paces of road choked end to end, a ditch full of men on the left. *"Four hundred paces of road, choked end to end with two columns that had turned on each other and had nowhere to go, a ditch on the left full of men"*
- At the far end, a violet corona with nothing upright inside sixty feet. *"a violet corona standing on the road with nothing left upright around it inside sixty feet."*
- A Kharven proverb arrived uninvited in his dead grandmother's voice. *"Wet wood burns eventually. A liar never dries."*
- She has been dead forty years. *"she had been dead forty years, and it came up out of him now with no invitation and in her voice."*
- Waiting in silence for a man's news is the one piece of the inheritance he kept without arguing. *"He had learned that off Muken, who had learned it off their father, and it was the only piece of the whole inheritance he had kept without arguing."*
- Last line: *"Sodoku Moto set the sword in his right hand and started up the road on foot."* / *"He put his shoulder into the wind and walked into the narrows."*

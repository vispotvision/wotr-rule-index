# Vaithesca · Oracle of the End

> *"I do not remember being born. I remember the moment after. I remember all the moments after. They are the same moment."*

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Vaithesca. **Called** · Oracle of the End, the Seer-Who-Knows-When.
**Role** · **Threshold Vessel. Void-Tongue Prophet. Unaligned Witness.**

### Affiliation

Unaligned. Bound to no faction, only to the intersection point she became. Formerly a mortal woman, possibly a priestess of a forgotten Wellspring, caught at the collision of **Nihiloth**, **Penanceflare** and **Tenebra** during a collapse of reality that killed an entire monastery. Her consciousness did not die; it fractured into the collision itself.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage XIV, Zenith.** *Full World Spirit territory: recognition from a Hollow Throne, and no breakthrough preceded it.* **She reached it by dying into three Wellsprings at once rather than by climbing anything.** There was no ladder for what happened to her, so the Stage table records the destination without recording a path.
>
> **She is the one entry on this sheet where "Catalyst Event" and "the day she stopped being mortal" are the same sentence, with nothing before it that counts as training.**

**Gloss rights · Unlimited.** Whatever she says reaches every listener already understood; the meaning bypasses their own language before she finishes speaking it.

---

## II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class VI · Voidic.** She does not cast; she absorbs Aether into silence. The more magic nearby, the quieter she becomes and the less anyone can hear.
**Attraction Layer** · **Node, not bearer.** She is the intersection the three Wellsprings meet at, so nothing about her presence is directed the way an ordinary Attraction bond is.
**Crystal State** · **Fixed.** Held at the exact configuration the collision left it in, neither refining further nor decaying in the ordinary sense.
**η** · Unmeasured as a fraction; every word she speaks is complete prophecy, so the concept of wasted output does not apply to her the way it applies to a caster.

---

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Nihiloth** · Limina. **Governing.** *Destructive interference: two waves in antiphase sum to zero. A Nihiloth Domain does not add law, it cancels the law already there.* **This is the End she speaks of. It was never a metaphor.**
**Penanceflare** · Caloria. *The Light of Contrition.* **Reckoning made a Wellspring: every prophecy she gives is the consequence a listener was already owed.**
**Tenebra** · Limina. *Signal-to-noise reduction.* **Closure, read as physics: the point where a story simply stops registering as ongoing.**
**Network** · All three sit in permanent resonance rather than accessed individually; she is the node, not a practitioner drawing on them.

## VIII · Signature

### Name the Ending

**Effect** · She speaks a prophecy, and it is always true, because it is drawn from what already exists rather than invented for the occasion.
**Cost** · **Variable, set by listener investment**, and she carries every ending she has ever named: thousands, held simultaneously, which is why she rarely speaks at all.
**Limit** · **She cannot create new futures.** She can only name ones that already exist, which makes the prophecy inescapable and also means she has never once surprised anyone about anything that was not already going to happen.
**Counter** · **A genuine paradox disrupts her sight.** So does sincere, illogical mercy, the one input her Harmonics never learned to carry and the only thing that has ever made her sight stutter.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the ending she is fading backward toward was ever one she spoke herself.

## IX · Relationships

**Herself** · Aging in reverse, with perhaps five years left before she reaches the moment before her own naming and, by the card's own account, ceases ever to have existed.

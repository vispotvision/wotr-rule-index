## VIII · Signature

### Ember Vein Rite

**Effect** · He opens a vein and **the blood ignites.** Personal Essence goes through the ignition into the glyph-fire, and the gestures he traces in the smoke bind heat, truth and emotion into law for as long as the smoke holds its shape. *This is how a dead forge relit.* Around the working, volatile Essence takes his heartbeat's rhythm and steadies.
**Cost** · **Blood, and the tempering interval.** Each rite is a quench; the Crystal must reheat and recover before the next or it cracks along its own gradients. *What he carries when nobody is fighting: the interval. A man who lives by the rite is a man who must wait, and he is restless.*
**Limit** · **Still air and his own blood.** The glyphs are smoke; wind unwrites them. Another's blood does not ignite.
**Counter** · **Deny the interval.** *Press him into cycling fast and the lattice arrives hard and brittle; quench cracking is the Exuroth failure and it is his.* Or simply move air across the smoke.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the Ember-Blooded are a bloodline at all, or a discipline his people taught the blood.

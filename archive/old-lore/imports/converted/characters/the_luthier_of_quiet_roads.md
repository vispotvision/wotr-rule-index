# Raymond Montclair · The Luthier of Quiet Roads

> *"If you listen long enough, even ruins remember how to breathe."*

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Raymond Montclair. **Called** · The Luthier of Quiet Roads. **Also known as** · Ash-Tone Pilgrim, The Soft-Handed Witness.
**Race / Lineage** · Mortal human, Verdant-flecked ancestry.
**Role** · **Wandering Luthier. Memory-Bearer.**
**Gloss rights** · Diagnostic only. He will answer a direct question about his craft. He will not volunteer the explanation.

### Affiliation

Unbound wayfarer, tolerated rather than sponsored by the Guild Accord escorts he travels alongside, in the late Imperial Age. No formal allegiance to any crown or order.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage IV, Flourishing.** *Flourishing asks for a first genuine Spirit Axis bond. Raymond's formed with the ashwood lute itself rather than with a person, which is why the instrument keeps emotional impressions the way most Stage IV bonds keep a face.* **A bond with an object still counts if the object is what steadied him first.**

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Verdantia** · Vitalia, Biochemistry. The Green Pulse. Dampens aggression in anyone standing inside his resonance field.
**Anamnesis** · Limina, Entropy/Void/Mind. The Wellspring of Remembrance. Anchors memory rather than erasing it, which is what lets an instrument he repairs keep what it has felt.
**Somnalis** · Fluxia, Fluid Dynamics. The Dream Veil. The stillness in Quiet Refrain, laid over a room like a held breath.
**Network** · 1 minor auxiliary, **Oneirion**, Limina, latent and unforced.
> He has steadied something close to sixty people this way across the border villages, one vigil and one repaired instrument at a time. Nobody has ever asked him to count them and he never has.

## VIII · Signature

### Lament of Verdant Strings

**Effect** · A tonal field that soothes Essence turbulence and slows hostile intent in anyone standing inside its radius. Verdantia dampens the aggression. Anamnesis anchors what the moment would otherwise erase.
**Cost** · 2,400 EU per sustained playing, and a slice of Memory Smear each time he holds the field open past its comfortable length.
**Limit** · Auditory. It needs to be heard to work, which means it fails against anyone already deafened or beyond the range his voice and strings can carry.
**Counter** · A Silence field nullifies it outright. So does a high-Flux environment loud enough to drown a single instrument.
> **What nobody knows.** How much of his own grief he has already flattened out of himself keeping other people's steady.

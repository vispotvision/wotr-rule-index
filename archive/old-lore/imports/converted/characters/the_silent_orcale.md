> **Catalyst Event · Stage VIII, Transcendence.** *A core contradiction resolved in the fracture record; the first true Domain forms.*
>
> **Eclipseron's Eye.** *The only known survivor of the Well. It gave him permanent dream perception and fused the mask to his flesh, and the Domain that formed is the Mirror Halo, a ring of distortion behind his head visible under Essence light.* **The contradiction is compassion delivered as unveiling, and he did not resolve it. He wears it.** *That is why the Stage fluctuates: his inner law is "only what is revealed can heal," and revelation breaks more than it restores.*

## VIII · Signature

### Eclipseron Mirrorfield

**Effect** · **A 4-metre sphere of distortion that returns a targeted attack to its point of origin.** *Eclipseron reads the working by what it withholds; the mirrored nodes of his Shell take the incoming vector in antiphase and hand it back along the line it arrived on, and the containment trials recorded the returned force at roughly 0.7 GN.* He deflects trajectories by altering *intent weight*, a Judicium sub-law effect: the blow that meant to land is the one that comes home. The companion disciplines run inside the field: **Judicium Sight** (guilt resonance outlined as visible cracks in light), **Oneiric Concord** (shared dreaming or prophetic communication, three linked souls at most), and **Obscurial Step** (displacement across 6 metres of line of sight by folding perception rather than space).
**Cost** · **Each truth revealed costs him a piece of self-perception: he forgets small personal facts.** Dreamlink overuse bleeds memory between the linked minds. **When the mask cracks his emotional filters fail and every being nearby takes the psychic overload.** *He carries the forgetting when nobody is fighting; the mask remembers for him and he does not trust what it remembers.*
**Limit** · **Eighteen minutes of trance or projection, uninterrupted, then it ends.** Four metres. Three souls. **Three currents; the fourth begins the memory echo.**
**Counter** · **Blinding light breaks the refraction symmetry.** *An Aurevane practitioner, or a lantern in the right place, and the nodes have nothing dark to read.* Unpredictability ruins the forecast: **he cannot fight a practitioner whose next move is not derived from the last one.** *Emotional attachment destabilises the Stage and the empathy storm comes back at him as feedback.*
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the face under the mask is still his, and whether the prophecy that he will one day see without eyes is a promise or a sentence.

## I · Identity

**Name** · Sairin Kazuren. **Called** · **The Still Point.** *The Blade Between Storms.*
**Role** · **Clan Head. Duelist. Monastic Sentinel of the Kazuren line.**
**Look & tells** · Tall and lithe, half-closed razor-focused eyes, dark hair bound with an arrow-shaped ornament, Mireyan martial robes stitched with cloud and tide. *She speaks only when necessary, and a room settles when she enters it before anyone notices why.*
**Gloss rights** · **Diagnostic only.** *She does not explain herself. What her art means surfaces only under a faculty's reading or an instrument's count, never from her own mouth.*
**Age** · Early thirties.

### Affiliation

**Eldest of the Kazuren siblings, current clan head.** Raised from childhood at the Quiet Shrine; passed every monastic trial the shrine set her.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VI, Glory.** *The Threshold is willingly accepting a genuine soul-cost to preserve something valued above one's own structural integrity.*
>
> **She took the clan when the previous head stepped down, and has not stopped watching her younger siblings since.** The card gives no fracture yet to answer, only the conviction that one is coming. **The Threshold is not behind her. She priced it in advance.**

**Aether Shell** · **Class II · Harmonic.** The attuned Shell holds rhythm before it holds power; simple work stabilizes clean, bonds form without tearing the self.
**Attraction Layer** · Undeveloped outward, dense inward. *Everything she has is bent toward the clan and the siblings she is waiting to need her.*
**Crystal State** · Refined, restrained. **η** · ~0.45, Coherence Band D.
**On scene** · Air thickens near her. Sound dulls a half-step. Her breathing sets the tempo of whatever room she is in, and the room follows without agreeing to.
**Craft** · **Magicraft**, body-routed. *Nothing here is spoken, written, or brewed; it moves the way her own stillness moves.*

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Judicium** · Fulguria, Electromagnetism. **Governing.** *Emission and absorption spectroscopy, read as truth: every interface has a signature, and Judicium is the instrument that reads it.* **Judicium Correction** — her posture and momentum recompute against that reading in real time, which is why she never seems to overcorrect.
**Sublimatio** · Vectoria, Mechanics. **Secondary.** *Phase transition with no intermediate state: a solid does not melt, it leaves directly.* Her stillness works the same law on her own motion, skipping the wind-up a strike would otherwise telegraph.
**Tenebra** · Limina, Entropy and Mind. **Drift.** *Signal-to-noise reduction. She is not invisible; she is quieter than the background, which serves her better.* **Silent Refrain**, sourced exactly. *Failure: aspect dependence — read from the one angle she did not plan for, she returns a very bright signal.*

## VIII · Signature

### Kechiryuu: Shizukahara

**Effect** · **A localized field in which everyone's momentum drags except hers.** Sublimatio skips her own body past the intermediate states a strike would need to telegraph, while Tenebra suppresses the ambient signal enough that an opponent's swing seems to arrive late even when it does not; Judicium reads the resulting mismatch and tells her exactly where to put the counter.
**Cost** · **Holding three Wellsprings in one field for more than a few exchanges leaves her joints aching for a day afterward**, the ordinary price of running Sublimatio's phase-skip on a living body rather than on inert matter.
**Limit** · **Only at close range, and only where the field can actually go quiet.** A hall already loud with combat, a crowd, a working already flooding the air with signal, and Tenebra has nothing left to subtract.
**Counter** · **Flood the noise floor, or come from the one angle she did not plan for.** Multiple loud, simultaneous threats overload Tenebra's reduction the same way any signal-dampening effect fails against saturation; a Tenebra practitioner watched from an unexpected direction returns a very bright signal indeed.
> **What nobody knows.** Why the previous clan head stepped down voluntarily, and whether Sairin already knows and is only waiting for her siblings to ask.

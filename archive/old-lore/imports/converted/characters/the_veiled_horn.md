## VIII · Signature

### Crimson Fracture

**Effect** · He breaks the seal partially rather than fully. What comes out is a fraction of what the Core is actually holding, and the fraction is still enough to end most engagements in one exchange.
**Cost** · Every partial break is a Seal Fracture risk taken on purpose. Held open too long, or too often in one day, and the next unsealing is not partial by his choosing.
**Limit** · He cannot do this twice in the same fight without real risk of losing the seal entirely. The first break is a weapon. The second is a decision about whether he still gets to be the same person tomorrow.
**Counter** · **Provoke him first, then make him choose between the seal and something he'd rather not watch burn.** Emotional pressure is the one input Seal Runes were never built to filter, and it is the only reliable way to make him spend this before he means to.
> **What nobody knows.** How close the seal already is to failing on its own, with no provocation and no permission asked.

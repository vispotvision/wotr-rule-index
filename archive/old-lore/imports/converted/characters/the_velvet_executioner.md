## VIII · Signature

### Final Courtesy

**Effect** · A single strike that severs physical and mnemonic continuity at once. The target dies, and what remains of them in the immediate memory of anyone nearby thins with the same stroke.
**Cost** · The severance is not selective. Erasing a target's presence from nearby memory chains costs him a fragment of how clearly he remembers the kill himself, every time.
**Limit** · Line of sight and a clean strike, both required. A wound that misses cleanly does not sever anything, and leaves an ordinary corpse and an ordinary memory behind.
**Counter** · **Force him into open, chaotic ground, or put him within reach of a Domain-tier commander.** Battlefield chaos denies Tenebra its quiet, and emotion-dominant aura fields overwhelm the detachment Final Courtesy depends on to work cleanly.
> **What nobody knows.** Why every nation that has quietly hired him denies it afterward in the same careful wording, as though the denial itself were part of the contract.

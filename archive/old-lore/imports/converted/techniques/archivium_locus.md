# Archivium Locus

## Summary card

**Effect** · Given one uninterrupted turn to write it, she lays a Transference lattice across up to a 60 m sphere; anything hostile that crosses the boundary gets read and redirected down a channel she chose while writing it.
**Cost** · Geometric Fatigue locks her thinking into the lattice's own rigid patterns for a time afterward, and Anchoring Debt ties her own Essence to the site in proportion to how much mass she redirected, making retreat afterward genuinely dangerous.
**Limit** · The lattice cannot move with her once written, and a second Locus overwrites whatever lattice she already had running.
**Counter** · An attack that never breaks the surface, burrowing, ground-breaking, or purely mental, passes under the lattice's reading entirely, and a Veil rupture tears holes straight through the geometry.
> **What nobody knows.** Why the lattice occasionally reads a hostile vector wrong even when the geometry itself is unbroken.

## Design Chain

**Trigger** · At least one full, uninterrupted turn spent inscribing the chosen ground and air before any hostile line crosses it.
**Function** · Writes a Transference lattice across the chosen area so that anything entering routes through the lattice rather than around it.
**Mechanism** · Runs on Transference's own canon law: two tuned systems held near enough exchange energy without a conductor between them, and the coupling falls off sharply with separation. A hostile trajectory entering the field couples to the lattice the way two oscillators couple, and the lattice bleeds that momentum off along whichever channel she pre-wrote, rather than absorbing or blocking it outright.
**Numerical Effect** · Radius up to 60 m, centred on a point she can see or occupy; holds 5 turns or until shattered. Absorption capacity is read as consistent with Stage VII's A-Grade ceiling, an estimate since the source gives no figure of its own: force above that Grade's upper Strike Force band overwhelms the lattice outright rather than being redirected.
**Target Response** · A projectile or working entering the zone visibly bends off its original line; a vertical strike is grounded short of its intended target.
**Consequence** · Allies standing inside the zone gain a corridor of ground the lattice has already cleared of the vectors it read.
**Limitation** · Only a limited number of vectors can be pre-written, and the lattice defaults to the most obvious trajectories, so a genuinely unusual angle of attack is more likely to find a gap in the writing.
**Weakness** · Subterranean or purely mental attacks bypass the lattice outright, since it reads trajectory rather than intent; a Veil rupture tears holes in the geometry that do not self-repair, and Glyphica-class disruption run through the lattice can corrupt its equations mid-read.
**Cost** · Geometric Fatigue and Anchoring Debt, as above.
**Counterplay** · Deny her the uninterrupted turn the writing needs, attack from beneath the lattice's reach, or force a Veil rupture near it and go in through the tear.

## Origin

The source names no deviser beyond Serenyra and gives no separate invention history. Under the Named Inventor Rule, Archivium Locus is her own self-derived working rather than a documented one studied and countered in advance; a first opponent meets its mechanism live, in the exchange, rather than from a manual. No further origin is given by the card, and none is added here.

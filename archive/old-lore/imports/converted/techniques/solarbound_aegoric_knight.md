# Solarbound Aegoric Knight

## What it is

A Materia-Vitalia-Fulguria Shell discipline built around storage rather than expression. A practitioner does not flare radiance outward the way a Caloria or Fulguria caster does. They absorb strain, moral weight, and impact, hold it under Fixatio's cure until intention and conviction align, and then release it once, as a single act that does not reverse: a cut, a vow spoken aloud, a stance held that does not break. The name for that stored, oath-gated form of output, in current vocabulary, is a Domain Weaving discipline with a strong Traitus component: the Shell itself carries a standing, passive bias (Traitus) that only becomes an active reshaping of local law (Domain Weaving) once enough has been stored and the practitioner chooses to spend it.

## Where it sits in the current system

**Family and Wellsprings** · Materia (Fixatio, primary: permanence through anchoring), Vitalia (Benediction, secondary: grace that restrains excess and gives bonds mechanical weight), Fulguria (Judicium, tertiary: the wellspring of truth, which finalises the released act as a verdict rather than a strike).
**Category** · Domain Weaving is the discipline's apex expression, the deliberate reshaping of local Continuum behaviour so a chosen law (here: things that are sworn, do not break) becomes easier to sustain. Traitus is the standing layer beneath it: the Shell's oath-reactive pressure response is a passive, permanent bias, not something cast.
**Craft** · Spellcraft, delivered bodily. The gesture-keys, kneeling, raising a shield, touching an oath-brand, are Corporis integration: the body as the pen, the working ephemeral and re-committed each time rather than inscribed once and left to stand on its own.
**Temperance Signature** · Stage VII, Refraction, through Stage X, Realization (Bands C through A). Environmental coupling gives way to ritual synchronisation, and a proto-Domain becomes a seated one; the old labels "Resolution," "Benediction," and "Judicium" are not Stage names and are read here instead as the discipline's three Operating Modes, described below.
**Grade range** · A-Grade at Stage VII's ceiling to S-Grade at Stage X's, tracking the Stage range directly rather than a separately invented tier label.

## Practitioners and history

The card gives no named originator, so none is invented here. Its closest attested resemblance in current canon is Ser Varnath Rhogar, the Scorn of the Outer World, a verified hero of the Guild Accord's Concord Military, remembered for holding a Gate open with his own body long enough for a million souls to flee it. The image the discipline is built around, bearing an unbearable, undramatic weight until it is spent on a single decisive act, is the same image Varnath's own record is built on, and the discipline's own text invokes his name directly as the source of its Aether. Whether he founded it, embodied it, or was simply the practitioner later students pointed to is not stated by the source and is left open rather than decided here.

## What it can and cannot do

**Can** · Convert accumulated strain, impact, and moral load into stored radiant weight (the Strain Lamina); amplify sharply in the presence of banners, seals, or spoken oaths (the Oath Lamina); hold that store behind a restraint that favours mercy until judgment is deliberately chosen (the Mercy Lamina); and, once a full Domain is seated, make the surrounding ground resist breaking, structurally and socially, for as long as the practitioner's pulse holds it.
**Cannot** · Produce fast, repeated, or incidental output. The design is a single stored release per cycle, and there is no version of this discipline that trades storage for burst damage without abandoning what makes it Fixatio-anchored in the first place. Purity and output both suffer when the practitioner fights without a cause they have actually sworn to, since the Oath Lamina has nothing to amplify. Storing past what the Mercy Lamina can hold seizes the Shell outright, and the only safe release is a deliberate oath-bleed (kneeling, a controlled breath, a hand on the brand), never an involuntary vent. And the discipline has no answer of its own to [Uur] Disruption, Uurgath's own glyph for chaotic anti-cohesion, which frays the Crownforge spiral symmetry the working depends on and must be re-centred by hand rather than shrugged off.

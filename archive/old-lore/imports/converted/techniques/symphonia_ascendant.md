# Symphonia Ascendens

## Summary card

**Effect** · Binds a polyphonic chord of sigils to a target's Essence pattern and, on their next offensive action, forces a misfire, a self-directed backlash, or a total refraction into harmless light or sound.
**Cost** · Three uses in one scene raise a temporary tremor in her own resonance that costs her precision; for a while afterward she hears echoes of chords not yet played and not yet past, which makes fine work harder.
**Limit** · She has to have heard or felt the target cast before the sigils have anything to link to, and it does not touch a purely mundane attack.
**Counter** · An Anchor Ward fixes the target's Essence paths in place before the sigils can take hold; a chaotic, non-repeating Essence pattern gives the sigils nothing consistent to grip.
> **What nobody knows.** Why a rejected chord slides sideways instead of simply failing to resolve, when every other refraction in her repertoire either lands cleanly or does not activate at all.

## Codex line

Wellspring Aurevane (primary, Fulguria, Electromagnetism), Mirithane (secondary, Limina, Entropy/Void/Mind). Category Vectra. Craft Runecraft. Stage floor VII, Refraction. Grade required A-Grade (late push toward S-Grade). Path gate Spirit Path.

## Design Chain

**Trigger** · A declared action: a sigil traced with her free hand while the Aether Chord Staff channels a rising chord onto a target she has previously heard cast.
**Function** · The rising chord locks a ring of sigils onto the target's Essence pattern by resonant sympathy, then reassigns the vector of the next offensive action that pattern produces.
**Mechanism** · This runs on real resonant coupling and vector decomposition. A system tuned to a matching frequency can be driven by it, and a force's direction is a variable that can be reassigned without destroying the force itself. The sigils act as the coupling medium; the redirection is the vector's own components sent somewhere other than their intended target.
**Numerical Effect** · At Stage VII, A-Grade, the binding holds across 5 kilometres of Aetheric line-of-sight and affects exactly one active ability or spell instance, applying on the target's next offensive action within the same encounter.
**Target Response** · The chosen action either misfires, bends back along its own Wellspring vector onto its caster, or splinters into harmless light and sound.
**Consequence** · The target's attack does not land as cast; depending on the outcome it either does nothing, or does to them what it was meant to do to Serenyra.
**Limitation** · It reassigns one already-committed action; it cannot act on multiple simultaneous casters in a shared ritual, and it needs a prior read on the target to have anything to link to.
**Weakness** · Void and Entropy Wellsprings give the bridge nothing stable to grip; a target whose Essence never repeats the same way twice can partially shrug the redirection off.
**Cost** · Resonance Strain after three uses in a scene, and Perception Debt that splits her hearing between echoes of past and future chords afterward.
**Counterplay** · Field an Anchor Ward before her chord locks on, or force the exchange through a Fate-locked Edict, which the working can only soften rather than redirect.

## FOW line

Governing Primary Dominion (Sense, Radius) and Harmonics (Attunement). Stage floor VII, Refraction. Grade required A-Grade (late push toward S-Grade). Path gate Spirit Path. Resonant Pair none indicated in the source.

## Origin

The source names Serenyra as the sole practitioner and names her instrument, the Aether Chord Staff, but gives no account of where or from whom she derived the technique, or what problem first required it.

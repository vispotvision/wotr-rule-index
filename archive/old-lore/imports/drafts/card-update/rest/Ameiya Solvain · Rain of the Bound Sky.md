**THE RECORDED ECHO (Passive Trait)**

## X. Techniques

**ECHO-JUDGMENT (Signature Technique)**

**Effect** · **The opponent is made to witness the moral echo of their last act, and guilt becomes tangible Essence weight.** *Judicium reads the spectrum of what was just done. Luminalis folds it into a mirror gate the target cannot look away from. The reflection carries mass.* **Cost** · **They relive it too.** *Every use of power records its own echo in the soul. Each judgment comes back in dreams.* The harsh ones stay. **Limit** · **It weighs the last act only.** A target with nothing recent on the scale returns a light reading. A target who genuinely believes the act was right returns a heavier one. The judge has to harmonise with it first. *No harmony, no judgment. They will not force it.* **Counter** · **Crowd the spectrum.** *Judicium's failure is spectral overload. Every object in view reports its composition at once and the practitioner loses the ability to prioritise.* A room full of guilty signatures yields no single verdict. **Or come from the angle the fold did not plan for.** Luminalis output split into two paths arrives out of phase with itself. **What nobody knows.** Which of the three sealed Wellsprings took the voice, and whether the seal would hold if the voice came back. The tell is a face taking shape in both of their eyes while the shield's hum drops to one low note. One judgment spends 396,000 EU, 10% of full reserve; the same payment buys at most six seconds of held weight, and keeping it longer requires the payment again. Before contact, opaque cover prevents the read from settling. During it, the existing crowded-spectrum and broken-fold counters interrupt the mirror. After contact, the added weight ends when the fold ends; the target's own act remains unchanged, and Ameiya still carries the dream. A target whose Resilience Ward meets the governing Perception Grade can resist the inner read. The weight comes from Essence paid through the mirror, not from an unlimited supply in the target's guilt.

**HAKANARI INVOCATION (Technique)**

Stored emotional resonance passes through Hakanari's pulse-timed light and appears as the brief reflections of alternate outcomes the blade already carries. The edge gives a second image just after the heartbeat, accompanied by a bright note that falls short of the main chord. One invocation spends 198,000 EU, 5% of full reserve, as well as the stored feeling it releases; that feeling must be gathered again before the same reflection can recur. The image lasts one heartbeat and offers an outcome to perceive, without replacing an event that has already happened. The blade's pulse fixes the moment it can answer. A change of action after that pulse leaves the reflection answering the earlier motion, while loss of the blade ends the invocation. The released feeling returns in their dreams.

### The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Sanctum Aria. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Ameiya Solvain was born human, and no record names the parents. Their mortal ancestry was altered through dream contact with Luminalis, and it shows in them: pale luminescent feathers, a slow pulse, and reflective eyes that record emotion as shifting hues.

The Dream Concord took them as its ward. It raised them in the silent cloisters of Velthaeir, the Floating Archive-City of Eresse, which enters everything in its sealed register and publishes nothing. Its recorders were Eresseans whose profession was the entering. Ameiya learned to talk in their kitchens, in the flattened common Eressean spoken there. In the cloisters their eyes changed hue with every feeling in the room, and they could not stop it.

#### The Making

Eresse's Dreamkeepers trained them as emissary between mortals and spirits. They learned to bend light into geometric sigils and to ward a threshold with them. They learned to hear out the grief of the guilty before any verdict. Justice, as they held it, had to sound like music, and a judgment that could not harmonise with the guilty's grief was no true judgment.

They were still the Dreamkeepers' emissary on the night the record calls the Labyrinth of Ma. A manifestation of dream broke loose in the capital, and things dreamed stood solid in its streets. The Dreamkeepers sent Ameiya down among them. They folded the lamplight into layered shields and held them over the doorways where people were caught. The shields hummed with the fear of the people behind them. They held until Serenyra Vaelith closed her lattice over the whole manifestation and it stood still. Afterward the capital forgot, for a time. The archmagus found the herald with the shields still raised, their eyes turning through the colours of the crowd. She watched them until the colours slowed.

Later the Sanctum Aria Concord collapsed. Through the collapse Ameiya stood alone against the Reflective Blight. They answered to no order after it.

They sealed three corrupted Wellsprings, Nihiloth, Dissolution and Vantabriel, and paid their emotional voice for the seals. No record sets the seals against the collapse. Then the verdicts they could no longer feel were the ones they got wrong. They read that in their own record and rebuilt themselves around it. White motes began to drift about them. They pulsed with other people's emotions, borrowing the feeling Ameiya had given up, and they were the seed of the first true Domain to form in them.

#### The Cost

The voice has not come back. They speak through resonance or in written glyphs. They say nothing of their own work, and nobody has heard the kitchen Eressean of Velthaeir from them since the seals.

Each judgment they give comes back to them later in dreams. The harsh ones stay. The people they "mirrored" too harshly return among them, and the record names none of those people.

A person they judge is made to witness what that person last did, and the reflection has weight. It weighs the last act only. Before it weighs anything, Ameiya must harmonise with the grief of the one before them. Where they cannot, they give no verdict. They will not force one.

In a fight they raise barriers and throw nothing. What comes at them hostile they bring to stillness, and they do not push. Their body is the weakest thing about them, a light frame around a slow pulse. It has been mortally wounded once. The feathers scattered into radiant threads and sealed the wound.

#### Where They Stand

Ameiya Solvain is independent, called Rain of the Bound Sky and the White Herald of Duskfeather. They wear white robes trimmed in muted gold, with feathers trailing from the sleeves. Celestial cords bind their black-blue hair. They carry Hakanari, a blade shaped like rippling light and forged from a frozen sun-tear. It keeps time with their heartbeat, and their heartbeat is slow. About them the motes drift, pulsing with the feeling of whoever stands near, and their eyes turn through its hues.

The Concord Hymns remember them as "the feather that fell upward". In the Hymns they stand for the Mercy of Reflection: to forgive is to acknowledge, and forgiveness leaves the record standing.

The Guild Accord hunts Azelra Kaine for fear she could reawaken Vantabriel, one of the three they sealed. No record says the two have met. The Vantabriel Severing stands on Kaelan Vorn's record, and no record orders it against the seal.

Which of the three took the voice is the one thing they have never said, and nobody knows whether the seal would hold if it came back.

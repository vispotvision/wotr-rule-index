## VIII. Traits

**LIONHEART AURA (Primary Trait)**

**Allies gain morale amplification and increased physical resilience** in his presence, a Harmonics Synergy effect. His Shell keeps the reinforcement of allies sharing his field in step with his own breath; their shoulders settle together, and a low horn note follows his exhalation. The shared reinforcement costs him 60 EU each second while it supports others, without refilling their reserves or closing existing wounds. Its reach ends with the shared field. Separation from him or disruption of the recipient's reinforcement breaks the benefit; it supplies courage without preventing refusal.

**IMPERIAL GRAVITY (Passive Trait)**

**Weak-willed opponents hesitate instinctively** before engaging him. His standing Dominion bears on the first movement made against him, making a hostile body check itself before committing; dust settles around his boots and the hesitation is visible in a shortened step. The pressure costs 20 EU each second while opposed. It cannot choose for an opponent or hold a moving weapon still. Equal-Stage Dominion can contest it, and a committed attack maintained through the first hesitation reaches him.

**BANNER EFFECT (Secondary Trait)**

**Territory claimed in his presence stabilizes rapidly**, off Coagulatio and Dominion Radius together. A planted standard gives dispersed Essence a fixed place to gather, so worked ground and the reinforcement rooted there settle around it; grit collects against the shaft and small tremors diminish. It costs 40 EU each second during settlement and needs a physical foothold held by his people. It cannot occupy ground without them. Uprooting the standard or continually churning the ground interrupts the gathering; too many competing footholds produce loose, disordered deposits.

## IX. Domain

**EMBER CROWN (Domain seed)**

His Attraction Layer carries his claim through the ground his force holds, supporting Lionheart Aura and carrying Golden Edict to hostile movement within it. Second Release opens and powers the seed; the light divides into grey and blue beneath the golden Lion sigil, and his horn note becomes continuous. The field reaches the contiguous ground he can keep under active attention, ending at its contested boundary. Its maintenance costs 26,586.795 EU each second, including the released Traits, giving 3 minutes from full to the ten-percent floor before any technique is added. A wound, a genuine surprise or another demand for his whole attention collapses it immediately. Equal-Stage Dominion contests its boundary; leaving that boundary removes its local compulsion. Its end leaves his Crystal Overgrown until a full night's sleep.

## X. Techniques

**GOLDEN EDICT (Signature Technique)**

**Effect** · A single spoken command restricts hostile movement across the field for as long as he sustains it; not paralysis, a compulsion that makes disobedience feel structurally wrong rather than merely unwise. *The Ember Crown Domain seed carries the compulsion the way a banner carries a wind.* **Cost** · *18,000 EU, workbook figure; the single most expensive Discipline he carries.* **Sovereign Burn**: sustained output above what the seed Domain can carry damages his own body before he will concede ground, and Golden Edict is the Discipline most likely to trigger it. **Limit** · **The Domain seed collapses if his concentration fractures**, per Stage VII's own gate; a genuine surprise, a wound, or a second working demanding his full attention ends the Edict early. **Counter** · **Break his concentration rather than his command.** Since the Edict runs on a fragile Stage VII seed rather than a stabilized Domain, anything that forces him to split attention (a threat to someone he will not let fall, a second front) ends the compulsion faster than resisting it directly ever will. The tell is the spoken command, followed by the Lion sigil brightening and the horn note stopping its beat for one breath. Judicium distinguishes the movement named by that command, and Dominion gives the seed's restriction its weight. The 18,000 EU pays for each six seconds of sustained command, in addition to the seed's maintenance; together they last about 2 minutes 42 seconds from full to the ten-percent floor. Before contact, leaving the claimed ground avoids the command; during contact, a split in his attention ends it; after collapse, the restriction leaves no remaining hold.

> **What nobody knows.** Whether the Ember Crown answered his coronation, or whether he simply arrived already carrying a claim it had no reason to refuse.

**SOLAR RAMPART (Technique)**

Aurevane holds the radiant face in a single pattern, and Fixatio binds that pattern to a fixed support so the barrier carries a siege blow into its footing. Its tell is gold light closing across the face with a high ringing sound. One six-second hold costs 12,000 EU; moving its anchors requires a new casting. It blocks the face it has formed and leaves its sides open. A flanking attack reaches around it, while breaking the supporting ground breaks the barrier's load path. The binding cannot bend to accommodate a moving footing, and the radiance exposes his position.

**CROWNFALL ADVANCE (Technique)**

Ascensio divides the work of driving a battle line forward across successive steps, reducing the force each step must bear while leaving the total work paid. Aurelian supplies 9,000 EU for a six-second surge through troops connected to his field. The tell is a rising sequence of heavy footfalls with brief light beneath the leading rank. The advance needs continuous footing and a shared direction; scattered troops receive no common push. A broken surface or a sideways attack disrupts the next step, and the unused portion of the paid work gives no further motion.

**SOVEREIGN BURN (Overchannel condition)**

Sustained output above the seed's carrying limit sends the excess through Aurelian's own body, and The Dawnblade becomes genuinely lethal only in this condition. His aura cracks into bright streaks, his breath grows harsh and blood gathers at his mouth. The working that caused the overload keeps its full EU cost, while the excess adds bodily injury and risks a Crystal fracture; the condition creates no reserve. It ends when he lowers output or the seed collapses. Distance, a broken concentration or a second front prevents him from turning the damage he is taking into a decisive exchange.

## Lore

### The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Withering courts. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Aurelian Veymont was born crown-blood, a highborn solar human of the lineage called the First Empire. He was born in the Withering Era and raised to the field, among the banners of the Ember Crown.

He read a field by instinct from the first one he commanded. He never learned to read it any other way. Weak-willed men hesitated before they engaged him, and so he never once needed to out-think an opponent. Quieter men learned to read a room. He learned where to plant a banner.

#### The Making

He carried the Ember Crown's banners as High Marshal before he wore its crown. His soldiers took heart when he stood among them. They stood harder under blows. Ground they won in his presence settled fast behind the line. His soldiers and generals called both of these loyalty. Courts that fought beside him and courts that fought against him came to call him the Unbreakable Marshal.

An army had accepted him long before the ground did.

The coronation trials did what battlefield victory alone had never managed. The Ember Crown's first flicker came in the hour his banners rose before any order to raise them had left his mouth. He understood, standing inside it, that the field had stopped waiting for his command before it obeyed him. That first claim on the ground lasted minutes. It was gone when his concentration slipped.

#### The Cost

A command he held over a field past what that claim could carry was paid for out of his own body. He paid it. He did not give up the ground. His own words were "A kingdom does not fall while I breathe." The Dawnblade stayed sheathed steel most days. It turned lethal in his hand once his own output had begun to cost him more than it returned, so any day he drew it in earnest was a day he was already paying.

The Ember Crown's campaign against a western kingdom of the coal foreland ended in the field below that kingdom's capital, a city of pale stone. The king was carried off the last engagement and died of his wounds some days later. Marceline Vireaux came to the widow within the week, and the king went into the ground in velvet.

The terms were made in the capital, in Cyranthia Valez's Salon. For weeks the kingdom's council sat there with the Ember Crown's envoys, at tables she had placed, under lamps she had set. Aurelian never went up to the capital. He sent word that the terms would be spoken in his field.

The widow came down to his pavilion with her council, and Marceline walked beside her. Cyranthia came with them. The council's officers had ridden down hard in the face, and at the table, seated beside Marceline, they eased. Aurelian read the Ember Crown's terms aloud himself and swore them on his Imperial Signet. The widow spoke the kingdom's terms aloud. Cyranthia sat at the table while both of them spoke. The kingdom knelt, and his banners stood over the field.

He had kept out of her rooms. His envoys had sat in them for weeks, and the Ember Crown came to owe her the favour they ran up there. The peace he swore on his own ring and his own ground was bound by her ring as well, because both sides had spoken it aloud in her presence.

#### Where They Stand

Aurelian Veymont holds the Ember Crown Dominion's throne, and he still commands in the field himself. The throne is also the claim on the ground that he is still only seeding. It answers him for minutes at a stretch. One spoken command of his holds hostile movement across a whole field. It lasts as long as he sustains it. A true surprise ends it early. So do a wound, a second working that wants his whole attention, a second front, and a threat to someone he will not let fall.

His soldiers and generals still read the heart they take from him as loyalty. Few of them know it has a mechanism. He wants it known. A lion carved into stone, as he has it, means nothing if nobody can read what carved it.

The favour his envoys ran up in Cyranthia Valez's Salon stands on the Ember Crown's account. She has not called it.

Nobody knows whether the Ember Crown answered his coronation, or whether he arrived already carrying a claim it had no reason to refuse.

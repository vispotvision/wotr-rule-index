## VIII. Traits

**DUNE-STEP (Primary Trait)**

**Exerts almost no pressure on the ground; leaves no footprints unless he chooses to, and traps do not trigger under his weight.** Petralon carries his downward load through a spread of compressive paths in the ground instead of leaving it beneath the sole. The Shell keeps those paths joined as his weight moves, at 0.1% of full reserve per minute, 342.12121 EU, while the Trait bears him. Fine grains shift beside his feet with a soft brushing sound even where no print remains. It needs supporting ground; it cannot carry him through open air or make a broken span bear his weight. Lifting or pulling him clear of the surface breaks the load path. A trap triggered by a tripwire, sight or touch can still catch him.

**SEISMIC GOLD (Passive Trait, through the Sun-Dials)**

**Conductive earrings that pick up vibrations in the earth; he can hear footfalls from miles away through the gold against his skin.** His Shell carries the ground's vibration to the earrings, which give the movement back through their contact with his skin. Sustained listening costs 0.1% of full reserve per minute, 342.12121 EU, and overlapping impacts leave his ears ringing. The tell is the gold humming against his jaw as he stills his head to listen. An airborne footfall supplies no ground-borne signal, a broken connection between surfaces interrupts it, and many competing tremors can hide one set of steps. Removing or separating the earrings from his skin takes away the hearing they carry.

**MANTLE OF OBSCURITY (Passive Trait, through the Pilgrim's Shawl)**

**His shawl carries passive optical camouflage; standing still, its weave blends into the surrounding geometry.** Mirithane lets the worked weave return the surrounding light across his outline, at 0.1% of full reserve per minute, 342.12121 EU, while the blending holds. It hides the covered outline and leaves his weight, smell and sound intact. Moving cloth briefly shows the background from its former position, with a dry whisper at the hem; a changing viewing angle also exposes the mismatch. Touch finds the body beneath it, and displacement or removal of the shawl breaks its coverage. The reflection carries his own unevenness when the Shell falters, so the cloth gives away fatigue as a wavering patch.

## X. Techniques

**SINKHOLE STEP (Signature Technique)**

Zahur liquefies a radius of ground beneath a charging opponent; Petralon's compression law takes hold the instant they stop moving, and the ground re-solidifies around them like stone closing a fist. Draws heavily on his own body's moisture; a day of combat run this way costs him roughly two gallons of water, and going without risks collapse from Desiccation. It needs erodible ground in continuous contact: sand, loose earth, weathered stone. Frozen or paved bedrock refuses the liquefaction outright. Freeze the ground first, or fight him somewhere the working can't set: open water, solid rock, or wind strong enough to scatter the dust before the trap closes. Dissolution frees the small bonds between the grains in the moisture he supplies; Petralon then packs those grains into load-bearing contact when movement stops. His heel turns against the ground to set the working, with a settling hiss and a shallow ring of sinking grains before the ground gives. One opening and closure costs 5% of full reserve, 17,106.0605 EU. Eighteen such workings spend the reserve from full to its tenth, before other costs; water loss can stop him earlier. The closed ground holds by its compressed structure, without a continuing draw. Before contact, continuous solid or frozen ground denies the opening; during sinking, leaving the connected patch interrupts the capture; after closure, sideways shear or removal of the surrounding grains breaks the compressed rim. Petralon's weakness under tension remains, and Dissolution can loosen supporting ground Zahur meant to keep.

Called aloud, the released Trait reaches the top of its A-Grade expression; silent, the changed hands and feet remain, but its strength stays at his ordinary expression. It changes no other stat and creates no Domain. The form costs 0.3% of full reserve each second, 1,026.36363 EU, including DUNE-STEP's own upkeep, and lasts five minutes from full to the tenth-reserve cutoff if nothing else is spent. Techniques, listening and camouflage still charge their own costs. The shape ends at that cutoff; forcing it farther is Overchannel with fracture risk. Ground contact is essential: a lifted limb has no compressive path, loose ground pulled sideways parts that path, and the form cannot make him cling by tension to a ceiling or hang from an unsupported edge. His mouth dries while he holds it; when it ends his hands shake and the weight his steps had spread returns to his soles. The spoken call gives an opening before the rise, displacement from the ground defeats its support while held, and the shaking hands remain a weakness afterward.

### The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Sun and sand. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Zahur Vane was born into a nomad lineage of the Great Sand Sea. He had his people's deep brown skin and coiled dark hair, and their hardness against extreme heat and thirst. He learned young that the desert does not fight the wind. It moves with it. His craft grew out of that lesson. He read the ground for where it was going to slide, put himself there, and waited for it to go.

The Guides of the Lost Meridian, a neutral order of wayfinders, became the closest thing he had to a home. He answered to their purpose. He learned to walk so lightly that the sand kept no print of him unless he chose to leave one, and the traps of the old roads did not wake under his weight. He wore the Sun-Dials at his ears, ancient gold that carried the tremor of the earth against his skin. Through them he heard footfalls from miles off. They hummed near an active Wellspring or a disturbance of the void. His shawl was a flax weave hard to cut or burn, and when he stood still its weave blended into the ground around him.

#### The Making

He led seekers toward lost Wellsprings, and on every road he judged, without telling them, whether they deserved to arrive. Once he guided a band of ambushing bandits. He walked them into a soft patch of the desert. Nobody saw them again.

A young prince came out of Solfatara to find a cure for his father, and the road ran through the Glass Storms. The Temple-State had hired a freeblade, Kaelis Aranthor, to keep him alive. At the edge of the sand the order gave the prince Zahur. He walked the party in by the gold at his ears and halted them wherever it told him the ground was soft. When the first storm rose, Kaelis wanted to cross before it closed. Zahur put the party in the lee of a dune and made them sit. The storm took the prince from the lee. Kaelis did not find him. Zahur heard his footfalls through the gold, walked out into the storm and brought him back. After that the party moved when Zahur moved. At the end of the road he named his price, a story nobody had heard, and the prince told it to him alone.

At the Black Oasis a Wellspring began to turn over, and the gold at his ears hummed with it. The Wellspring had been on Rafik Al'Seren's chart, Rafik had sold its coordinates, and the buyers had come by the chart with no guide and set to work at the well-head. Zahur came in off the sand. He led the people of the oasis out a few at a time, along ground that would hold them, and did not tell them why he had come. While they were leaving, a stranger went down to the well-head alone and stopped the inversion, and it cost him a piece of his memory. Zahur saw what the stranger did.

Afterward a price was offered him for the location of the Hidden Oasis of the Great Sand Sea. He chose to keep guarding the Oasis. He did not sell.

#### The Cost

He pays for every working in his own body. When he turns the ground to slurry under a charging man and lets it close around him like stone the moment he stops, the water for it comes out of Zahur's flesh. If he goes without water after a day of fighting that way, he can collapse from Desiccation. The Oasis was worth more to him than staying whole.

The working needs sand, loose earth or weathered stone. Frozen ground, bare bedrock, open water and a wind that scatters the dust before the ground sets all leave him with no trap to close.

He explains nothing. He has never told the prince's story to anyone, and he has never said what he saw the stranger do at the Black Oasis. He works alone, with no network to draw on. He chose that. No living person is as close to him as the Oasis. Everyone else in his life is a guest passing through the Great Sand Sea, and the sand is the nearest thing he has to kin.

#### Where They Stand

Zahur Vane still leads seekers for the Guides of the Lost Meridian. He still guards the Hidden Oasis. Most of those he guides never learn how close they came to the Soft Trap. Through the gold at his ears he feels a Great Shift building underground. Nobody knows whether it is a Titan waking, or his own Wellspring reaching the end of what the desert still has to lend him.

The prince he brought through the Glass Storms went home to Solfatara, and Solfatara burned. The prince is the Ember Regent now, bound to the magma scar there. No record says whether Zahur knows it. He keeps the story.

## VIII. Traits

**CADENCE (Primary Trait)**

**Zeraphine's voice moves armies.** She speaks into the sludge, and the pulse runs down the conduits four times faster than sound crosses open air, because sound always travels faster through liquid. It reaches every rot-creature at the far end at once. Each creature is tuned to one note the way a tuning fork is, and when her pulse carries that note, that creature moves and no other. And she never calls them all at once: she opens them in order, wave after wave, exactly as her mother opened the brine channels on the salt pans, because one opened out of turn spoils the whole run. *Cut the conduit and the cadence stops at the break.* Her voice and the moving sludge give the command away together; the sound is a low glass note answered by knocks along the connected channels. Sustained commands pay the command-field drain of 164.95472431657936 EU/s, for six hours from full to a tenth before other workings.

**CISTERN (Secondary Trait)**

**She bound her body to a sphere of glass, and now the glass is her.** It floats at her hand, and inside it the dead she keeps turn rendered in a thick, slow sludge that pulses when she thinks. A liquid passes pressure equally in every direction, so one pulse in the sphere reaches everything in it in the same instant, and what it feels, she feels. *It is also glass. She can never be more than an arm's length from it, she has never struck anyone, and a single duellist who reaches the sphere with something heavy ends the whole Rot Host's coordination in one blow.* The returning violet-white ribbon makes the bond visible. Its standing attachment has no separate upkeep; its cost is her shared exposure to anything that happens to the glass.

**DREAD (Passive Trait)**

**Fear feeds her.** The more terror on a field, the stronger her command field grows, because her law runs as a feedback loop, like a speaker squealing louder the closer it is held to the bell that hears it. Frightened soldiers sweat, shake and break formation, and every scrap of that fear comes back into her conduits as signal; her rot-homunculi harden around her as the dread deepens. *The same loop runs the other way: against a calm, steady enemy she weakens, and in a short war she never builds to full strength.* The answering knocks deepen as the fear returns. Its passive response has no separate activation fee, while the stronger active command remains within the command-field bill and the two-Grade cap. A severed return conduit carries no fear back to her.

**SALT (Inherited Trait)**

**The Drowls made salt for generations, and the salt is in her blood.** Salt pulls water out of anything it touches, the way brine pulls the water from a fish in a curing barrel; it is why salted meat keeps for years, and why the dead rendered into her glass keep instead of spoiling. Salt is also how brewers rule a ferment: the right amount decides which rot wins and which dies, the way cabbage becomes sauerkraut instead of slime. Malphas makes the rot; Zeraphine is the salt that keeps it obedient. Anything her conduits touch long enough starts drying out, lips cracking, eyes stinging, wounds crusting white. *Flood is her family's oldest enemy: enough fresh water dilutes her sludge until her signals blur.* The white crust is its visible tell. Preservation in the closed cistern is a standing condition; drying through active conduits is included in the command-field drain.

## IX. Domain

**BLACK CISTERN (Domain)**

Her Second Release opens and powers a true Domain within 30 metres of the glass, bounded by a silver-grey ring washed with black-violet. Sludge inside that ring carries her chosen order of commands without one channel taking another's place. Only creatures already tuned to her can receive those commands. Dissolution lets an old command end, Transference carries the next, and Nyxial holds the field against changes in the surrounding Aether; Mortalis remains the current of the suspended dead. The field gives her command one Grade of rise, from S to SS when called, and cannot add a new subordinate or carry an order across severed sludge. It forms when the release call finishes and holds for two hours from full at 494.864172949738 EU/s, ending at ten percent. Its held energy is 2,078,429,526.3888993 J across one six-second turn at η 0.70, placing its energetic rung at Holdfast; that held energy is distinct from her command Grade. A small distraction does not unseat this Stage VIII Domain. Broken glass, a sufficiently broken concentration or an opposing Domain that wins the ground ends it. A cut channel loses her ordering beyond the cut even while the rest of the field stands. Forced collapse throws a resonance shock through the occupied ground and back into her already Overgrown Crystal.

## X. Techniques

**THE BLOOM SURGE (Signature Technique)**

One cadence through the conduits and **four independent horrors move as one thing.** *The waves arrive in sequence, the fracture lines open where she called them, and nothing on the field made a decision except her.* **Anguish Resonance means her command field is fed by terror she must keep producing.** *She cannot fight a calm enemy well and cannot afford a short war.* **The conduits must reach.** *Cut the sludge and the cadence stops at the break.* **Reach the glass.** *Ardency 220, Dexterity 152, and no personal defence worth the name.* Her voice sends one ordered pulse through the still-connected sludge, and four answering knocks announce the generals taking their places in the sequence. Each complete command costs 1% of her full reserve, 39,589.133835979046 EU, in addition to the field or release already running; the commanded forces pay for their own workings. Before the pulse, a break in the conduit prevents it reaching that branch; during it, fresh-water dilution blurs which note a creature receives; after it, severing the route prevents her next correction. None of these failures requires a higher Stage or a rare Wellspring.

**SALT CHOIR (First Release)** · folk name: Salt Choir, in her command tongue

Silver-grey light threaded with black-violet shows along her throat and wrists, and the ribbon from the glass coils close round her raised forearm. A low note rings through the wet glass and returns as four knocks. Cadence holds each creature's note apart from the others while she changes the order in which they move; called aloud, it brings that command to the top of S without raising her personal fighting stats. The call costs a beat. The form spends 329.9094486331587 EU/s, including ordinary command upkeep, and lasts three hours from full before ending at a tenth; extra workings shorten that time. It cannot reach across a broken conduit, restore diluted sludge or strengthen the glass. Released silently, the same light and sound remain, but command stays at its ordinary S strength without the rise to the Grade's top. Her voice is hoarse and her raised hand shakes afterward. The opening beat exposes the glass, fresh water blurs the channels during the hold, and the shaking hand makes its protection harder afterward.

**BLACK CISTERN (Second Release)** · folk name: Black Cistern, in her command tongue

The light along her throat spreads over her shoulders and down her robes, and the ribbon from the sphere runs out along the ground into a ring 30 metres from the glass. The four knocks join into one low, held note. This opens the BLACK CISTERN Domain and gives Cadence SS command inside its boundary when called aloud, keeping each already-tuned creature in her chosen order as the surrounding field changes. Its full drain is 494.864172949738 EU/s, including the lesser form and command upkeep, for two hours from full to a tenth; it replaces the First Release's rise. She remains within an arm's length of the sphere and gains no personal armour, weapon or close fighting skill. Released silently, the field still forms, flares and sounds, but command reaches only the top of S. The form ends at a tenth, on dismissal or when the Domain breaks. Afterward her existing Overgrowth spills more readily until she has slept a full night; sleep removes that added strain, not the Overgrown state she already carries. Holding beyond the stopping point risks a Crystal Fracture Event. A broken conduit still isolates its far end, fresh water still blurs her signal, a rival Domain can take the ground, and a heavy blow to the glass still ends the Host's coordination.

### The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Rot Host. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Zeraphine Drowl was born on the salt pans of the Varakh Drylands, in the Outer World, to a family that made salt. Brine lay under the crust of the pan. They drew it up from shallow wells and let it down through cut channels into a run of shallow beds, each saltier than the one before it. Her mother kept the channels. She opened each one in its turn and shut it behind the brine. The sun did the rest. What came out of the last bed was white.

Zeraphine had the order of the channels by heart before she could lift a rake. A channel opened out of turn let weak brine into strong. The whole run was spoiled. The Drylands gave several abundant years and then a severe drought. In the wet years one storm could put every bed on the pan under water overnight. By morning the salt was back in the water. The beds were run again from the first.

#### The Making

She left the pans for the Pyraeon Forge Academy and served it as a tactician. She planned fields she did not stand on. She set the order in which a force went forward and the signal that sent each part of it in. She never struck anyone with her own hand.

The same school had expelled Malphas, and he had been declared a metaphysical threat. Zeraphine betrayed the Academy to bind herself to his cause, "a strategist turned vessel."

Then she bound her body to a glass cistern, and over the years drew it down to a sphere that floats at her hand. The dead she kept were rendered into the sludge inside it, and the sludge pulsed. Every pulse was a command. Conduits of the same sludge ran out from the glass across the ground, and her voice travelled down them and woke the rot-creatures at the far end.

She built a chassis around the betrayal and commanded from inside it. The wound was made load-bearing on purpose.

#### The Cost

She could never be more than an arm's length from the glass. She could not fight from it, and she had no defence of her own worth the name. One competent duellist at the glass would end the coordination of the whole Host.

Terror fed her command field. The deeper the dread on a field, the harder her regiments of rot-homunculi set around her. So she had to keep the terror coming. She could not fight a calm enemy well, and she could not afford a short war. The conduits had to reach. Where the sludge was cut, the cadence stopped at the break.

On the Broken Rivers a walled river town shut its gate on the Host. Her cistern came up the river road behind the regiments, and the conduits were run out from the glass to the foot of the wall. She sent Morphaene forward first and set Kytheris behind him. His gloam went over the parapet, and her spores gave it a body. Vorynth stood at the gate through the afternoon. Graelith she held back.

At dusk the town's practitioners came out of the river gate in a sortie aimed at the glass. The cistern stayed where it stood. She sent Graelith into their path, and the sound went out of the air around him. Their incantations arrived at nothing. The sortie turned back through the gate. When it was in, her cadence went down the conduits, and Vorynth's waves came against the softened gate in the order she called them. The town fell before morning. From that dusk she called Graelith a living pause. After it the gloam went out first, the Bloom Surge came behind it, and Graelith was kept for the counterstroke.

In the Bone Hills a fort kept a Domain over its walls. Her cistern stood at the foot of the hill while the Ossuary Choir played on the slope above, and her conduits ran up to their frame. She took each change in the chord as her signal. She passed it down the sludge to the waves. When the wards came apart she called Vorynth up through the cracked gate.

#### Where They Stand

Zeraphine Drowl is Supreme Field Commander under Malphas, Master Conductor of the Rot Host and Regent of the Black Cistern. Vorynth, Kytheris, Graelith and Morphaene answer to her. She channels what each of them does into one set of tactics. Vorynth counts her an invaluable field conductor, and his waves keep a sequence when she calls it. The Ossuary Choir stand outside the four. She leans on their harmonic pulses as battlefield anchors.

When her cadence goes down the conduits, four generals move as one thing, and nothing on the field makes a decision except her.

She and Malphas came out of the same school. It expelled him, and she betrayed it.

She has still never struck anyone. The dead turn in the glass at her hand, and the sludge pulses when she speaks.

## Voice
**Notices first** · The cost, and who pays it. She reads a document for the clause doing too much work, a room for who has standing to speak in it, and a man for what he is protecting.
**Sentence length** · Medium to long when she argues, sentences stacked one position at a time until the case is built; short when she decides: "I chose." "Begin." Her questions fall flat and end on a full stop, settled before anyone answers them.
**Contractions** · never; her Common keeps every word whole, the way the old tongue under it keeps its forms.
**Pet word** · "Say it," and the longer form, "Say the rest of it": she will not let a hard thing stay half-said in her presence.
**Never says** · That she was loved, or why the place on her breast where the crest goes is bare, since a woman who explains an absence has conceded that it is one. She does not count: armies go to the round thousand, years to the age she was, and exact tallies belong to the Reckoner and the field book.
**Stumbles** · Never. Her sentences arrive finished; when something lands on her she goes silent for a while instead of starting over.
**Gloss rights** · Only the naming law, and only as argument: she will lay out what a form does to the people caught in it to win a point. She never explains a word of the Vey-Elarin tongue, which she speaks exactly as the yard does.
**Under stress** · The older Eressean grammar comes up through her Common, the way it does in any drow under pressure: a claim ends on who witnessed it, a debt is in term or past it, and her speech shortens to orders. Both hands go flat on the arms of the chair, her voice stays level, and she gives a reader nothing at all.
**In grief** · She goes back to the girl in the orchard at fourteen who asked about everything. The long legal sentences break into plain ones joined on "and", a question gets out that she would never let out in a hall, and the worst of it comes in bare fragments set down flat.
**In joy** · The voice holds level. The joy shows as a permission granted in full, the bread with the seeds and both of you, and as two fingers laid against a child's cheek with the pads of the plates and not the edges.
**Sample line** · "And you are standing in a field offering me safe conduct back under stone as though the stone is where I came from and not where they put me."

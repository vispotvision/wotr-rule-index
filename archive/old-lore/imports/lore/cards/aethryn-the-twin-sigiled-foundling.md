## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Citadel's high seats. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Aethryn was found at a shuttered Gate. Who or what left him there has never been established. The Gate Reflection Event is the only lead anyone has to his origin, and it has gone nowhere.

The Accord entered him, as it entered anything left on its ground. A clerk wrote his name in ink. The fields for a parish, a house and a charter stayed empty.

He was kept in an Accord hospice-temple, among the sick its keepers nursed and the dying they sat with in shifts. The keepers watched the beds. A slight, quiet boy was fed with the rest, counted at night and otherwise left to himself. He learned the house by its sounds: the change of the shift at the beds, the murmur of a sitting, the layer-out's step in the corridor.

### The Making

In those unwatched years he built two workings out of nothing but his own pressure. Their marks were on his forehead and palm. The palm closed on a resonance and would not let it go. The forehead saw a lie as broken geometry, a line breaking its own angle, and it read what a speaker intended as readily as what he said. Neither waited for his leave.

The keepers noticed the marks late. By then he had built both, the palm's siphon and the forehead's sight, before anyone could tell him such things needed a teacher. The two sigils were the record of it. The Guild had no form for a foundling who had cleared the gate twice before anyone was watching.

### The Cost

Research and Archives took charge of him once the marks were seen. From then on he was watched.

Sometimes the two sigils closed together, and he never learned to close them on command. The record calls it the Loop. When it closed, the space around him stopped finishing its own sentence. Perception, gravity and the direction of every feeling in reach bent toward whatever the pair was reading, and he blurred at the edges as though slipping into his own shadow. It spent memory. Distress or sensory overload during a surge wiped his own short-term recall. Every pass wore at his trust in what "himself" even referred to.

Distress did a second thing. It sent a distortion out of him into everyone near, warping what they meant. In Pattern Surge #4 the researchers studying him lost twelve to eighteen minutes of experiential continuity, and they came through it uneventfully. He came out of that surge holding none of it. What he had of those minutes, he had from their notes.

The palm taught him his one refusal. He did not take a hand held out to him. He kept the marked palm shut against his side, and a keeper who reached for it found a fist.

The forehead gave him a belief. It cost him every comfort he was offered. He held that a kind word was a lie if its angle broke. A keeper once told him that whoever left him at the Gate had meant to come back. The sigil saw the angle break. He said so, to her face, in front of the beds. Nobody told him anything soft about the Gate again.

He formed no bond with any person or place, and the record cannot say whether that was choice or inability.

### Where They Stand

After Pattern Surge #4 the matter of what the Accord should call him was brought to the Council Spire. The Council could find no form for him. Argen Vael'thar framed a point of law for the Grand Seer, Aurgon Mintruix: what the Codex would become if it wrote the boy into it. Lyss Arvale carried it to the Seer and set his answer down whole. It named no form for him. Drakvor cast the judgement. The Accord would not induct him, would not classify him and would not leave him.

Aethryn was in neither room. No record says whether anyone told him.

He is a ward of the Guild Accord with no charter under him. The hospice-temple is sealed now. He lives inside it under monitored care, watched without a break. The record carries other names for him, the Pale Omen, the Unfinished Child, the Circle-Bearer and the Silent Pattern. He answers to Aethryn. Not even the Guild that shelters him claims the right to name what he is.

Where a completed stage should sit in his Crystal there is an open circle, and a Wellspring that brushes it reads an open question. The words kept beside it are "the circle opens for you, but closes for me."

Nobody knows whether the Loop is a wound in him or a wound in the law he sits outside of.

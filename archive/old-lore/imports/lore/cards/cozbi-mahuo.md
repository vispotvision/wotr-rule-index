## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card, the twenty-two archived scenes it appears in, and the record of The Zettari war camp and the Mahuo who came to it. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Cozbi Mahuo was born into the Mahuo line at Sum-gol, the hollow-seat whose name means Breath Hollow. No record keeps the name he was given at birth. Cozbi is a First Compact name, and it names his bond to the souls he carries.

He carried many inhabiting souls under one sovereign identity, a trait of the Mahuo lineage. It first taught him that a body can shelter people who are not its owner. He inherited the family's summoning art with it. Under strain he spoke of himself in the plural, after the Mahuo pattern.

The Zettari and the Moto were at war, and their war crossed Mahuo ground. Their armies trampled towns that were never theirs and used the people in them as livestock, and neither army noticed the third family it was grinding into the mud. No history book recorded the Mahuo dead. Cozbi learned from that, before he could walk without falling, the one lesson he never revised: the strong write the histories.

His family was massacred.

### The Making

He watched his own people ground into the mud beneath the Zettari and Moto's endless war, and he promised his dying brother: "make them remember what we were, even if they never learn our names." He gave the promise completely. He spent every year after it converting it into a ledger that never closes.

The harvesting ran for decades, long enough to outrun any simple count of his age, and each soul he took became an entry in the Ledger, a living account spread across planes. He learned the forbidden Amalgamation Ritus and built servants from salvage that had never been meant to share one body. He numbered them.

His true body went onto a stone pedestal, planes away from any fight, and vessels went out in its place. At Ashwell, a garrison town of the Moto line, the vessel that crossed the market square was a woman's. It crouched beside the dying captain and laid two fingers on his chest. The dust of the square hung still while everything left in him drained out.

In the same years the Wellsprings soured across the Inner World, the Old World and the far edges of the Outer World. Essence drawn from them came back curdled. No priest or scholar could name what had gone through first. Cozbi had, and he left no name where a question served him better.

He made a son. He folded Essence drafts siphoned from unwilling sources into a spiritual axis and wrapped it in a child's body, with the Mahuo face and golden eyes from a second line he had made to cooperate with his own. The boy slept in a nursery that never needed daylight, and nobody told him what he was.

For years Cozbi watched a woman named Mizuyi through an opening behind her threshold. The night Ma'Kovu Zettari cut it, tracing it home through his brother Rashani's Origin Eye, Cozbi named the cut aloud and filed the correction. He pressed matter and its contradiction together over an armour of Zettari make, and the floor beneath the worktable stopped existing. The armour held. "Fight well, little sovereigns," he told brothers who could no longer hear him.

Beneath his reliquary he kept the Dragon of Calamity in chains, the harvested hatred of dragonkind folded into one body. He drew the boy's blood with a silver needle, worked it into the dragon's foreleg, drank the dragon's own blood from a dish, and vomited it onto the stone. The bond held. Should the dragon fall, every soul it had taken would come to him. He gave the order to loose it himself.

His mirrors walked every house the Moto line still called its own, and gas went through the halls behind them, taking women at their mending and children asleep. When it finished, the line was a handful of names still drawing breath. From a ridge above the burning, he turned his back before the last of the screaming ended.

### The Cost

Rengai Zettari and Dhaerin Valorin walked for months to reach him, and found him seated on his great spider while it ate a deer. Rengai asked why he had bled a child. Cozbi answered with the Mahuo in the mud, and told him he had not chosen Dougou's wars. Rengai said that changed nothing about the child. Cozbi agreed at once. He had only ever claimed it made sense.

He won through lattice and construct. His own body stayed below the ceiling of his craft, and he arranged never to need a fair fight. Dhaerin found the seam in the borrowed architecture and turned the theft against him. Cozbi called up the effigy he had built in a Titan's shape from fragments harvested one soul at a time, and it struck Rengai down. Then Dhaerin gave his life to a rite that withdrew the world's consent from every claim Cozbi held over a harvested soul. Every claimed soul went in one instant. His composure cracked, rawer than it ever had.

He kept the massacre open. It never healed, and he preserved it on purpose, as fuel. He refused to let his family's ending be over, and he forced endings onto everyone else instead. He called it correction and meant it. Each soul harvested justified the Ledger's continuation, and none of them closed it. He wanted a war large enough that every venerable bloodline would have to write it into its own histories, whether it wanted to or not.

His own line paid for it. The Old World came to count the Mahuo dangerous for what he made of them. In a Zettari war camp, Kwon Mu-jin drafted an Accord that condemns him "without qualification and without appeal to inherited grief as excuse."

### Where They Stand

Down in the clearing, he reached for his harvested spirits by reflex, and the seal tightened and took more of them. "I allowed myself to become too confident," he said to the moon. Forty-Five came for him. He told it to collect the fallen Zettari, and it carried Rengai away. No record says where.

The seal still holds him. It never reached the dragon, whose dominion he had built into its own nature, or the lattice he had dressed as an extension of himself. In a crater under a ruined mountainside, he folded the merged dragon down into a man's shape and explained why. He called Dhaerin's working elegant, and there was no performance in it.

Dova'Kan read one of his workings before it finished casting. Cozbi's voice sped up. It is the only time the record shows it. He stepped back and said, "Begin with the shred, then." The encounter is unresolved.

His true body sits on the pedestal, and the Ledger works wherever his lattice has touched. His son is still kept unaware. His constructs stutter, and he cares for them. Sancta Lux's Oracle weighs completed acts and returns no reading on his numbered works. The one Mahuo in that order who could explain why has met him once, and neither has filed a record of it. A report in the north holds him dead. It has not been ratified.

- **His son** · a bound spiritual axis. He made the boy from siphoned drafts, bled him with a silver needle to bind the dragon, and keeps him unaware of what he is.
- **Rengai Zettari** · enemy and captive. He measured Rengai against his father and had him carried off the field.

- **Kwon Mu-jin** · kin by line; accuser. Of one line and one summoning art, Mu-jin wrote the Accord that condemns him and refuses his grief as an excuse.

- **Aurelian Prudentius Custos Clausorum · The Primate** · one lineage. Two Mahuo of the same hollow-seat took the same valley's wound to opposite conclusions; they have met once, and neither filed a record of it.
- **Dhaerin Valorin** · the seal. He calls the rite that sealed him elegant, and the scholar who cast it died of it.
- **Dova'Kan** · encounter, unresolved. Dova'Kan alone read one of his workings before it finished casting, and made him talk faster.
- **Amalgamation Forty-Five** · his construct. He cares for it, and it came for him on the ruined field and carried Rengai away.

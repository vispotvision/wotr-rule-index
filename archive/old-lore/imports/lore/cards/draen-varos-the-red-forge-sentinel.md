## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Anvil and relic. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Draen Varos was born in the smelting districts of Eresse, to a family that worked the furnaces. Ore came down to the districts from the mountains, and iron went out. No stone was quarried for them without the Asking spoken over it and something left behind of equal weight. The districts sold by Accord weights. They refused the Accord's right to set them. Oaths there were sworn on Sacred Gold, and melting it was unlawful, so it was the one metal no district furnace ever took.

He went from the furnace floor to the anvil young. At a district forge he learned to draw smelter's iron into tools and blades, and he learned to strike at the heat he meant. He was a working blacksmith when the Guild Accord conscripted him.

### The Making

The Vanguard Bastion Unit sent him down into the Atlas Deep Mantle with the other conscript smiths. Kaelzar had closed a volcanic breach there by bringing a mountain range down on it. The fall left a field of slag and broken plate. The Accord raised a Bastion beside the fallen range and put Kaelzar over it, and the conscripts were set to work what the collapse had left for its armory.

Draen worked the slag. He worked it until the tempered slag of the Mantle was alloyed into his Crystal and the two came to read as one structure. Among the salvage lay Titan armor that had failed where it lay. He forged a two-headed war-axe from it. On the line the axe failed him, and a soldier died of the failure. The death was entered on the Bastion's roll.

He did not melt the axe down. He put it back on the anvil and went on forging it. He rose in the armory by attrition, a step at a time as the men ahead of him fell out of the line, and he asked for none of the steps. The axe stayed on his anvil the whole way up. By the time Kaelzar left the field armory in his hands, the thing on the anvil was the Axe of Tempered Remorse. It had started as a mistake that got a soldier killed, and he kept forging on it anyway, until the guilt itself became the temper.

### The Cost

The soldier was sung at the solstice with the Accord's other dead in service. Draen stood for the singing. The axe was on his back, and it ignited in proportion to the guilt and the conviction he carried.

The axe and his armor could be made one. When he bound them, every swing threw a controlled shockwave, and the heat of it built in him toward overburn. He had to vent it through a grounding strike, the axe driven into the ground. Otherwise the heat would crack him. An opponent who kept him moving, or kept the ground unstable under him, could deny him the strike. The pulse that steadied the men around him came while he stood still.

When the Deep Mantle's containment seals broke, the Accord let the new ones to Orokh Sable-Forge, and the Wyrmwright came down into the Bastion's armory. Kaelzar paid the Wyrmwright's price there, alone with him. Draen never heard what it was. While he was in the armory, Orokh asked to lay a hand on the Axe of Tempered Remorse. He read heat and rhythm through metal, and what a piece remembered. Draen did not unsling the axe.

Ymir Rhok, an apprentice of the Forgeworks Annex, brought the finished plates down with brackets of his own making and fitted them under Draen. Draen proofed every bracket before a plate went on it and struck the Accord's proof-mark into the ones that passed. One bolt would not seat. Ymir heated it with his bare hand and seated it. Draen had him strip it and proof it again. While the fitting lasted he kept the apprentice on the armory floor with the Body Path initiates and put him through their drills.

### Where They Stand

Draen Varos stands at the Deep Mantle Bastion under the command of Kaelzar, the Iron Lion of Atlas, who is himself held in the Mantle under the Accord's containment oath. He instructs the Body Path initiates directly. The vanguard trusts his armory work without checking it twice. The field armory system is his to oversee and the one authority he never disputes. The line calls him the Bastion of Cinders and the Iron Shepherd. He is bound to the axe and to the men he stands in front of, and to no one person.

The Ember Shroud Mantle damps the heat off him and hides his Aether signature. The Manefold Gauntlets bind the axe's flux to his plate. Pain does not slow him. Fear does not reach him. Despair dulls the axe's edge, and nobody, Draen included, has found why. He fights to prove that creation and destruction are "the same prayer done with purpose," and the axe is his argument.

He says it himself: "Heat is memory. Every blade remembers the hand that forged it."

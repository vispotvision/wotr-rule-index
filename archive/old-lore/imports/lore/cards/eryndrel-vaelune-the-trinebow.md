## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of House Venrik and the Eressean crown. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Eryndrel Vaelune was born in the Grand Principality of Eresse, in a valley town under the Eressean mountain chains whose people went down to market by glider. His mother kept the launch ledge. In its season she stood at the lip each morning with a strand of wool tied to a pole, and nobody stepped off until she had read the air. She called go or hold once, aloud. Nobody argued with a hold.

His father rigged the gliders. He cut the spars, braided the control cords and set the pins in the fold hinges that let a wing close for the carry back up. A glider with one pin drawn could not open its wing, and it came to no harm. The boy knew that before he could draw a bow. He learned the bow afterward, on game, with the Asking spoken over each kill and something of equal worth left in exchange.

### The Making

The Aerial Ward of Eresse took him because he could stand on a ridge and say where the air would lift a courier, where it would drop one and where it would turn. They set him to cutting lanes for couriers over the passes. He cut them with a bow.

On one of those passes a raiding party held a gatehouse above a courier road and meant to shut its gate before the couriers came through. Eryndrel had the ridge above it and a clear line to every man inside. He gave up the ridge. He came down to the one angle under the gate, where the gatehouse archers could see him, and slid a single shaft beneath the gate lever. The next push on the gate met polite refusal. The couriers went through. An arrow from the gatehouse opened his arm on the way back up.

The draw that made him came at a gorge bridge on the chains. A courier was halfway across the planks. At the far end one man hauled the rope that lifted the last span, a second swung the gate beyond it on its hinges, and a third ran out along the planks with a spear. There was time for one draw.

He drew once. Three shafts left the string on that one pull, split mid-flight and arrived from different angles without crossing lines. A lacing bolt tacked the rope to its post. A shaft slid under the hinge. The third went into the tendon behind the spearman's knee, and he fell on the planks with his spear under him, alive. The courier stepped over him.

### The Cost

He kept to tendon, rope and hinge from that bridge on. He would spend position to keep it that way, and he spent it again and again: a ridge given up for an angle under a lever, a lane opened later than a killing shot would have opened it, arrows taken from ground he had walked out of.

His own line was "If the wind agrees, the world will follow." The night the forest burned under a pass in the mountain chains, the wind agreed with him.

The people of the burning valley went out by glider along the one corridor of air over the pass. Asta Venrik took the Skyfang flights in and held it open. Eryndrel hooked the route at the near end, and every glider that crossed his mark flew quieter and truer. He read the corridor closing. He called it once, aloud, for every rider on the line. Asta stayed in it while her wyvern bled under her. The last glider passed. The corridor closed behind her when he had said it would, and she came out through the closing.

At the far mouth Aelor Vaelith had scrubbed a lane through the ash and held it for the gliders. The Ward paired the two of them after that night.

### Where They Stand

Eryndrel is Eresse's line-ranger and treaty marksman, and he often rides with the Imperial Pegasus and the Winged Aegis on air-oath operations. He calls Aelor's dive; Aelor calls his wind. The Ward pairs a champion who cannot read a situation with a ranger who reads nothing else, and it does so by doctrine. Athena widens the lane he opens.

He cuts lanes for couriers, pegs riot doors and unmakes war drums, and when verdicts wobble he gives Vireline and Lucen five silent heartbeats. He threads his shots along Cassiel Asterion's lines, and together they turn riots into corridors. Kaede Suyori's Blue Veil keeps his long holds clean. Orya's ripples and Daigo's ribbons are the moving posts he stitches routes on.

His hair is a quiet silver and his ears are angular. He wears layered leathers under light plate. The Skystitch has three limbs with counterweights, star-etched cams and spike-shod tips, and its string hum is nearly inaudible and steadies nearby hands. His moon-fletch vanes refuse cross-winds for the last ten strides. Dust storms and drum-Domains spoil his holds.

Orokh Sable-Forge has offered him a prototype limb for four-way splits, and he has not decided whether more power means worse choices.

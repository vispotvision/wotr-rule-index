## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Triune of the Three-Headed Serpent. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Estrella was born in a crew camp on the gibber plain of the Outer World, near the line the Accord keeps around the Crevice. Her father worked the grimbarite crews. They went down inside the margin and brought up a heavy stone that made no sound under a hammer. How many crewmen died down there was never published. Some came up breathing and did not know their wives.

As a child she found that things kept what had happened to them. A crewman's bar told her where his hands had worn it, and a waterskin told her whose mouth had drunk from it last. The women of the camp brought her things. She told them what the man had been before he went down.

### The Making

She learned to stitch. A spirit that came out of the margin in pieces could be sutured back along its own shape, if she had the shape to work from. So before each shift she went among the crews. She handled their tools and learned each man while he was whole. When one she had learned came up broken, she mended him, and the Creviceward Circle took her on as an agent at its edge.

A man of her father's crew brought her a chip of grimbarite. She held it as she held everything. It gave her nothing. With nothing in her hand to read, she turned the reading on herself. She came away with the Domain seed, and with it the ability to examine her own architecture. Afterwards her eyes did not agree. One saw in metaphor. The other saw what once was true.

### The Cost

Her rite sutured a spirit across a threshold it should not survive, and the seam held when her template was correct. While she worked she emptied toward the depleted without deciding to, and every man she had learned was a man she could empty into.

She would not put her hands to a spirit she had never met. When the crews carried out a stranger, she sat with him and did not work. She believed a man had to be learned whole before the margin took him apart, and she went on learning them, crew after crew. She could thread anybody to anybody. She could thread little to herself.

A man she had known was carried out from inside the line, breathing, and what he was had come away from him in pieces. She had his shape and could not reach all of it. The Circle sent for the Matron of the Triune. Virelsa came with Velcrissa, read the man by what was missing from him, and held open a bridge to the stratum where the rest of him had gone. Estrella sutured him across it with the shape she had known as the template. The seam held. When it was done she could not stand. Velcrissa hurried her settling and sat the night with her.

### Where They Stand

Estrella is a peripheral agent of the Creviceward Circle, and she still works the line. She owes Velcrissa that night. She has crossed a man over on a bridge she cannot hold herself.

Her rite is for those the Crevice has marked. Iskaron Thalnaris walked out of a Crevice-margin concentration, and something did not finish leaving with him. She has never met him.

- **Virelsa of the Veilborne Skies** · comrades at a crossing. Virelsa held open the bridge Estrella cannot hold, and Estrella sutured the man across it.
- **Iskaron Thalnaris · Bearer of the Fractured Crown** · the Crevice margin. Her rite is for those the Crevice has marked, as he is, and they have never met.

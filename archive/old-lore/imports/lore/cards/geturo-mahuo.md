## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card, the seven archived scenes it appears in, and the record of Class X and what it grows into. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Geturo Mahuo was the first child of Kwon Mu-jin and Frithia. His father was of the Mahuo court line, scholar-nobility with a summoning art of its own, and had begun in alchemy. As a boy Mu-jin had shown the court's Senior Alchemist a corrected array, because the glyph chain was wrong and would have killed the patient. The court answered by making him a symbol of its brilliance. He left on the day a title-writ ranked him before it named him anything else. Frithia was a weapon instructor. Hiromi came after Geturo, and Lily last. In public the three children called their father Teacher, and inside the family everyone knew what the word covered.

He grew broad early. He had his mother's chestnut hair and her wide mouth, set in a line that could pass for patience or disapproval depending on which sibling he was looking at. His hands grew larger than a boy's ought to be, knuckled and square. He learned early that they drew attention, and he stopped giving them anything to do. They hung still at his sides. His wrists ran hot, so he wore his sleeves rolled to the forearm.

When Hiromi was six, their father brought him into a practice hall, and the boy did something in a binding circle that none of the adults present could name. At seven he redefined summoning. From that year the adults who came to their father's door asked for Hiromi. Geturo let them in, told his father who had come, and stepped back out of the frame.

### The Making

Mu-jin taught him alchemy, his own founding discipline. He wanted alchemical knowledge to be common inheritance, held by no court and no bloodline, and he taught his eldest son on those terms, at a bench the two of them came to share. Geturo learned the theory and kept it. He learned to inscribe for a working, and to do it again the next night the same way. His progress was even. Every step of it came from hours at the bench. His father could not leave an error standing once he had seen it, and on those nights Geturo stayed up with him until the work was right.

At the Aetherion Academy the three of them walked the corridors abreast, a habit so old that none of them noticed it anymore. Hiromi took the centre because he always took it. Lily took the side nearest where they were going, because she always knew the way. Geturo took the outside and watched the doors.

One morning Hiromi bellowed down the length of the upper gallery that he was going to be late again, all because Geturo would not get up. Geturo came up the east stair at a walk. "Oh, shut it, would you. You always make excuses." Then he gave the facts in order. "You were dressed before I opened my eyes. You left the room. You came back. You messed up the bed so it looked slept in. And now you're blaming me."

Hiromi had been at the binding circles since before dawn. He always went. Geturo said so, and Hiromi owned it. Geturo sighed through his nose, the sigh he had been perfecting for thirteen years. It told his brother that Geturo saw what he was doing, that he had decided not to stop him, and that the decision had cost him. Then he fell into step at Hiromi's side.

### The Cost

That morning Hiromi broke into a run for the lecture halls, shouting that the last one there bought lunch. Geturo did not run. He watched his brother's back go through the third archway with the look their father wore when he looked at Hiromi and could not find the words. Then he and Lily looked at each other. "He went to the circles again," she said. "Before dawn," he answered. He followed her with his hands in his pockets.

On the first morning of Class X a classmate drew the whole room taut on strings nobody could see. Hiromi was on his feet at once. "Geturo, let her do it to you." "No," Geturo said. When Hiromi hailed a cousin across the room, Geturo closed his eyes. When their father asked who would start the introductions and Hiromi's hand shot up, Geturo's hand went over his face. Hiromi told the class he would become the greatest summoner to ever live. Geturo did not react. At his mother's entrance he sat very still.

He had missed his first class, because his father had kept him up most of the night refining his alchemy. In front of the room Mu-jin told him, "You have to be more consistent, or your mother's gonna strangle me." Geturo grumbled back. "It isn't my fault *someone* stayed up with me most of the night refining my alchemy, *Teacher*. What if I told her about that?" Mu-jin put his hands up, palms out.

He believes somebody has to walk the outside. He walks it. He knows the patience costs him, and he pays it: a brother at the circles before dawn whom he has watched and never stopped, and a lateness shouted down a gallery onto his name.

### Where They Stand

Geturo is in Class X. His father is its homeroom teacher and its instructor in basic magic and rudimentary summoning. His mother wears a weapon instructor's sash in the same halls. He wears Academy black buttoned to the collar and cinched at the waist, sleeves rolled, and he still checks every room for people and where they stand. When Hiromi refused a roommate at the dorm assignments, Geturo did not look up from his notebook.

On the morning the Class X Open Tournament was announced, Hiromi crossed the room and challenged Xanelor Rafiminar, the masked boy who had told the class he had no magical potential whatsoever. Geturo closed his notebook and shook his head slowly. "You can't be seriously picking a fight already Hiromi." His brother gave him a smile full of teeth and no answer. Their father took the class down to the Combat Arena. He wanted it inside a warded ring.

Hiromi came out of the duel with a split lip, a bruised shoulder and blood from both nostrils, the imprint he had called up spent. Xanelor's last arrow never reached him. Their father threw a vial into its path, and the arrow went down into the floor as liquid metal a spread hand from Hiromi's hip. Nobody has named the winner, or what was in the vial. The tournament is seven days out.

What Geturo wants he has never said aloud, and it is quieter than being the greatest.

### Ties

- **Hiromi Mahuo** · kin, brothers. Geturo walks the outside while his younger brother takes the centre, and he knows Hiromi goes to the binding circles before dawn and has decided not to stop him.
- **Lily Mahuo** · kin, brother and sister. His younger sister steers, and he nods.
- **Kwon Mu-jin** · father and son. His father teaches him alchemy at a shared bench and kept him up most of a night refining it before the first morning of Class X.
- **Frithia** · mother and son. He has her mouth, and at her entrance he sat very still.
- **Xanelor Rafiminar** · classmates. Geturo checks a room for people where Xanelor checks it for ground, and his was the one voice that tried to talk Hiromi out of the challenge Xanelor accepted.

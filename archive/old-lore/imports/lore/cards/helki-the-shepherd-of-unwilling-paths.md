## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Outer trails. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Helki was born on the Southern Grass Sea of the Outer World, in marcher country where the grass stands deep and the Aether runs poor. Her family kept sheep. Her mother taught her the fold: the ewe that would lamb before morning, the one that had stopped eating, the one gone off to the hurdles to lie down.

The dying ones became hers. Where her father would have ended a beast past saving, Helki asked to sit with it, and she sat until it was done. She learned the grass-country herbs that ease a failing animal and the ones that bring it through. By the time she was grown she could tell how far along its dying a body had come, in a ewe or in a man.

Few Wellsprings rise in that country. When the one near her began to fail, she knelt at it as she knelt by the ewes and found she could pour herself into it. It died slower for that. Then it died.

### The Making

She left the flock and went on foot wherever word came of a Wellspring dying.

In the Varakh Drylands a nexus had been failing through the drought years, and the villages about it drew on what was left. Kaien Veyren, a Wellspring monk, kept it. A lamb from one of the village flocks had wandered into the current, and nobody could get it out. Helki knelt at the nexus and poured. The Accord sent Baojin Zhaorin of its Vanguard, and he laboured beside her on ground too spent to give him anything. She did not stop for three days. The lamb stood in the current the whole time, where she could see it from her knees.

On the third day the nexus collapsed. Kaien stood nearest its heart, and the collapse opened his face along the cheek and the eye. Baojin went in with his mantle closed and carried him out. Helki walked out with grey in her hair and a lamb that had been standing in the current the whole time. That lamb was Orvain.

Later she saw her own soul from outside. What surfaced there was the seed of a Domain, laid down in three days of giving a dying nexus everything and keeping nothing back to live on. She had lived through them by surrender.

### The Cost

The grey stayed, and the nexus never came back. Orvain came out of the current changed, and she came out bound to him. Parted from him she weakened, and his death would be hers within days. She never penned or haltered him.

The Offering Walk was three measured steps toward the one she walked it for, that one's true name spoken in Parun, and a tithe given on their behalf. Each walk stripped away a part of her that no craft could give back. She walked it fourteen times. At one of the two walks the Outer World remembers, she stopped a skirmish already under way by ringing the bell. Twice she was dead by a physician's measure. Twice she came back.

On an old drove road of the grass country, one no herd used any longer, she walked behind a stranger. He stopped, put a pot on his head, and walked on. Orvain's bell rang, as it rings when someone near it crosses a threshold that cannot be undone. The man did not turn round. She read him as she read every living thing near her, whether she meant to or not. She did not call after him. At the fork she took the other branch, and she told nobody what she had read.

On the Scattered Wastes the same man carried a dying elf in over the rocks to the caravan she walked with. She knew him by the pot. She would have walked the offering for the elf, and she had no name to walk it on. He gave none. She dressed his wounds with herbs from her pack, as she had brought ewes through on the grass sea, and he lived. At the first water he was gone in the night. He was Ristra Lenovira, and she never learned it.

### Where They Stand

Helki walks the Unmeasured Path, nominally neutral and independent, with Orvain at her side. His fleece has never been shorn, and she has never asked why. She carries the Crook of Turned Seasons. She is no duelist. Animals go still around her, and some people near her feel a pull to confess. The villages and caravans of the Outer World remember her from the Greying of the Calderath Pass and the Stillness at the Gate. Neither is on any Accord file.

She is walking toward the Verdant Altar and has not reached it. She suspects it is the completion the three days in the Drylands began, a debt she has decided to pay in person. She still walks the offering for dying strangers. Each walk takes its part of her for good. Nobody knows what her tithe is paid to, or whether something has simply learned to answer when she calls.

## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of House Venrik and the Eressean crown. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Ilyria Draven Morningstar was born to Karo Venrik, a High Elf of the Eressean line, and to a woman of the Draven Morningstar bloodline. She carries her mother's line in her name.

From her father she had the elven half. It gave her the calm, the long endurance, and a read that found the shape of an intention before it became an act. The vampiric half came from her mother's side. It brought the hunger, and a drain that worked through her skin on contact.

The Accord had sealed the Draven Morningstar archive three generations before she was born. Her mother's people reached her as the Accord had classified them, and what they had been before the classification stayed behind the seal. Her father founded the Remembrance Division to read what a stone or a soul still keeps of what was done to it.

### The Making

The Guild Accord's Special Division took her on under deniable authorisation. Its contracts named armoured combatants: knights in Wellspring-reinforced plate, and fighters who threw a Domain around themselves. A target who routed no Aether at all gave her nothing to grip.

Her working was Coherence Collapse. She threaded a foreign resonance into the node that sustained a target's fighting, and for a few beats he had no Aether. Before she did it, she told him exactly what she was about to do to his coherence. Watching him disbelieve her was part of the technique. She did not tire. The longer an engagement ran, the sharper the hunger became, while the elven half kept her channels whole under the load.

Her helm came to her carrying her father's tuning. A Magitech artificer had built it, clockwork-plated, with a beak for a visor, and the visor gave her one of her names. Over three years she recalibrated it to carry a harmonic layer his tuning never had, one that reads a living body the way a root system reads load and age. Targets of elven descent described being read by something that shared their root language.

For years she worked the two halves apart, the calm to read and the hunger to finish.

The Division put a deniable contract on Raivhar Drakonis, a Vanguard fighter the Accord had declared rogue after the Kyrenshade Massacre. It went to her. At Dorrosfenn she told him what was coming, and did it. His fire failed. In those beats he recognised her father's signature in her technique. He had felt that read once before, at the Siege of Epprenea, running down through his own horns while Karo Venrik held the failing Wellspring he was anchored in. He used her father's name. She did not correct him. She stopped pretending that the elven calm and the vampiric hunger were separate things, and let the room understand they had always been the same instrument.

### The Cost

Raivhar's Shell turned the thread back on her. The Voidflame's pattern slotted into her own lattice. Her clarity made the imprint cleaner and harder to expel; the cleaner her read, the more completely a foreign pattern takes hold when a target turns the exchange around. She broke contact. Raivhar left the field alive and still rogue.

She worked the pattern out of herself with her father's blue sash at her waist. It carried his signature, and it steadied her through the recovery. She declined every offer of replacement gear. Of all her bonds, the sash carried the only one she had not made tactical. Her father's name went uncorrected, spoken by a man who had fought beside him and whom she had been sent to stop.

The drain did not end when she stood down. It ran on contact for hours afterwards. She wore gloves. After an engagement she shook no one's hand.

She said it in so many words: "The armor is the point. Take that away and there was never a knight. Only a person who believed they were protected." An internal Accord review called what the Collapse does to a knight temporary suppression. Two panel members filed supplementary doubt about whether that distinction survives a target read at her precision. Nobody settled whether it does. She kept working.

### Where They Stand

Ilyria stands unaffiliated, formerly attached to the Guild Accord's Special Division under deniable authorisation. One open contract has run six weeks under observation with no action taken, and her handler notes it as her longest pre-action period on record. She is spending less than her old field records show. She has stopped apologising for what her presence does to the people in a room with her.

Twice she has felt something at the edge of her range. She has not pursued contact. It has noticed her.

Her father is alive, aware of what she does, and silent about it. She has not decided whether the silence is permission, grief, or elven patience that reads as both. His wife is Asta Venrik, the Skyfang Queen of Eresse, and no record puts the two women in one room. Ilyria still wants to know what her mother's people were before the classification. She wears her father's sash.

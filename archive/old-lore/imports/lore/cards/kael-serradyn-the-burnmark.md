## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Accord's field blades. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Kael Serradyn was born in one of the Upper Ring's crater cities, cut into the wall of the Ring where the ore seams run, to a household on the Smelter Guild's shifts. In the extraction districts the production floor came first and the settlement second. The shifts decided when a family worked, ate and slept. Across the basin the forges of Thane-Gorr ran on Wellspring-heated furnaces, hotter than any fuel could reach, and held that heat steady for days. At night they showed as a dull orange pulse against the crater walls. The air tasted of sulphur and hot metal.

He carried firebrick for the relining gangs. They chipped the burnt lining out of a cooled furnace and set new brick in its place. A lining left past its turn burned through to the wall. The fire ate the lining first.

### The Making

He went to Altherion on the northern rim, where the Enforcement Division kept a permanent legation. He was entered in ink. At his initiation the Division cut its Oaths into his bones, as it cuts them into every officer's. One of them ran, "We burn only when law demands."

He served among the Sealwrights, laying containment circles and barrier fields round Wellspring breaches. The name stayed on him. He came to walk the ground before he fought on it. Wherever he had walked, his heat stayed behind in a trail he could set off on command. Ground he had not walked, he could not fire.

At the Eclipse Siege he detonated a Wellspring bomb from ground zero and walked out. No other operative is known to have done it. Three times he stood at the centre of a Solar Collapse and walked out. After the third, the world began to answer back, and the thing it recognised in him was his control over the fire underneath.

### The Cost

His commanding officer died sealing the same flame Kael now carries. The officer's insignia came out of the fire melted. Kael kept it.

He read each walk out of a collapse as a debt the discipline had not finished collecting. He was built to produce more heat than he was built to hold. Every trail he fired burned away plating from the insulation inside him, and the plating did not grow back on its own schedule. When he was struck, a concussive flash went off from him by reflex. A wound gave him more fire to hold.

On the Cymorath Distortion Hunt over the Corridor Holdings, the Division's terms for its loose alliance with Draven Kael Vorrick put a Sealwright at the hunter's side. It was Kael. Before the breach he walked a wide ring round the ground below the road where the kills would fall. Then he held his heat in and stood downhill, off Azhura Vyren's ground, because warm air would have taken her breath. On the last beast Draven tore the Bloodbind open. Kael stood at the edge of the ring he had walked and did not fire. The surge ran out, and Draven was still himself.

### Where They Stand

Kael Serradyn is a Warden of Duty of the Guild Accord's Enforcement Division. The field still calls him a Sealwright. He is lean and dusk-skinned. He dresses in burnt crimson and matte black weave. His left arm is seamed with orange lines that bleed heat, and the graft is armour and fuel to him at once. His eyes are rimmed in ember-light that brightens with his pulse. He wears the Heliarch Cloak against heat from outside, and a ring against his own. The ring keeps him from melting down. At his side hangs the Ember Fang, a short reactor blade whose edge draws a heavy load while it burns.

When Enforcement took the ground under one of his seals as forfeit, its holder filed with Arbitration, and Arbitration ruled that the forfeiture must wait on its hearing. Kael came to the legation hall at Altherion to give the account of the sealing. He gave it. The argument ran on over his account until men of both Divisions stood with their hands on their weapons. Tsukihana Reimei laid the Still Garden, the rite Enforcement keeps for disputes inside the Accord. Inside it nobody wanted to strike. The ember-light in his eyes went low with his pulse. The dispute went back to paper. Nobody on either side called it a rivalry in front of him, and nobody does.

He signs for the clean-up work the Division still sends Kaelrith Dorne, who deserted it and wears its insignia inside-out on his half-mantle. Kael keeps his officer's insignia, melted.

Whether the fire he holds in is his own Wellspring's, or something the Eclipse Siege left behind in him, nobody knows.

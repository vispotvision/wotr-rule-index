## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The long memory. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Kinjiki was made by Bairos, the First Epoch, before the Planes separated. He was an Ashkaru, one of the living editorial instruments Bairos authored and set on a distant world where the deep text ran dense and needed constant keeping. In that age meaning, structure and substance lay in one continuous text. Anything equipped to perceive it at the structural level could read it and rewrite it. The Ashkaru were that equipment.

Their work was transcription and correction. An Ashkaru read the state of a passage, found the inconsistency in its grammar and rewrote the passage until the inconsistency resolved. Bairos gave them judgment so that the edits would be good.

Kinjiki's function was deep-text maintenance under Bairos, and he did it with the whole body. Six serpentine threads grew from his upper back, each as thick as a man's thigh at the root. Each ended in a scaled, jawed head with its own senses and its own mind, and each read its own strand of the text. The torso between them, the executor, acted on what they read. His hide was the grey-blue of deep basalt. It did not scar. Where it was hurt, crystalline substrate filled the wound and set in a darker seam.

He was made for one thing. He did it perfectly.

### The Making

Then the Planes separated. Meaning went to the Plane of Fate, structure to the Plane of Soul and substance to the Material Plane, and the text his kind had kept stopped being one document. The work could no longer be done. His race's purpose expired overnight. The Ashkaru did not die. They had nothing left to do.

His threads lost the material they were built to read and turned to whatever lay in reach. Among the things they read was his own blueprint. He looked there for the word *sovereign* and found the word *instrument*. He rewrote it as *sovereign*.

Left unsupervised, a thread did what it was made for. It read what it perceived, and where the reading showed an inconsistency, it corrected. After the Separation every reading showed one. Kinjiki forged gold bands from materials nobody has identified and closed them on his threads, at the midpoint and at the root of each. The gold carried an inscribed lattice, and chains hung between the bands to spread the load. A sealed thread still read. It corrected nothing until he let it. One medallion at his belt, the master governor, held all six seals together.

The system that came after fitted a Soul Crystal onto hardware older than the architecture that measured it, and every step he took afterward went into the record as an instrument anomaly. The Null came later, as his resting state. Inside it every Wellspring current cancelled. A practitioner who walked in kept Crystal, Essence and body, and lost every working until walking out. Then the Continuum registered his law.

His threads learned to read a process and hand it back with its direction reversed. A fire he had read cooled. A healing he had read wounded.

In time the Domain and the body became indistinguishable, and he became his Domain. The serpent threads, the null field and the inversion principle were what a space became when he was present in it.

### The Cost

Every inversion was billed to the thread that made it. It loaded that thread with the full energy of the process it turned, and repeated inversions drove it toward thermal saturation. Past that point the thread's substrate fractured. It lost its reading before it lost its fighting.

What struck him stayed in him. There was a limit to that too, and past it the retained load had nowhere to go but into the structure holding it. His identity was encoded against erasure. Past the limit of that redundancy, the reconstruction would come back confident, complete and wrong. Letting the Null out cost him nothing. Holding it back was what he paid for, wherever he went.

A released thread corrected what it read in the order it read it and did not consult him about priorities. What it corrected looked as though it had never been written. To reseal one he had to restrain it by force, and it fought him, because it was doing what it was built to do.

He would not sit across from another being as a peer, even when the alliance would have won his campaign. Everything that reached him sorted into above and below, and he called that judgment. He never raised his voice. He never repeated himself. He called beings text and their choices errors, and he meant neither as a figure of speech. No spirit ever bonded to him, because his reading architecture could not tell another consciousness from the rest of what was in a room.

He held the order after the Separation to be a rough draft, written wrong-way-round, and whatever he did to it he counted as correction. The cost was what it cost. Bara's other children, the human ones, got the crowns. His kind got a distant planet and a function.

### Where They Stand

Kinjiki holds no affiliation. His former function was deep-text maintenance under Bairos. His current function is self-assigned. No other Ashkaru has been confirmed where he stands. He goes barefoot on clawed, digitigrade feet, in a ceremonial half-skirt of dark red fabric panelled with gold lattice metalwork, with a heavy necklace of bladed pendants at his sternum. The fabric is older than his arrival. Where it came from is not discussed. His eyes sit recessed and dark, and they give back no light.

Instruments brought near him redline and then stop registering. When one reads his Shell, the classification flickers to another and back, and nobody trusts the reading. One such log lies copied in an archive beneath a Guild Accord registry building, beside two other filings its reader has not acted on. Kinjiki does not know it has been read.

He has never released more than two threads, and the second was a mistake he does not discuss. What a released thread considers consistent is known to the thread alone. Nobody alive has seen the grammar it measures against. The thread has. Inside the Null the deep text lies briefly unified, and he can read it there. Whether he can still edit it, nobody has tested. No Fracture has been recorded against him, since his hardware does not fracture on the frequency the later system runs on. Whether that is a defence or an untested weakness stays open.

The medallion at his belt has never been tested either, and if it breaks, all six seals release at once. He does not know what would happen. He knows what he thinks would happen, and that thought is the only thing in his experience that qualifies as fear.

Of the Moto, he has not decided whether they are kin or evidence. His intent stands in his own words: "I am going to pick up the pen. I am going to read every line. And I am going to cross out everything that was not meant to be there."

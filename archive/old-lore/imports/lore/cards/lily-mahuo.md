## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card, the six archived scenes it appears in, and the record of Class X and what it grows into. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Lily Mahuo was the youngest of the three children born to Kwon Mu-jin and Frithia. Her father was of the Mahuo court line, scholar-nobles with a summoning art of their own. Frithia taught weapons and nursed the wounded. Where she worked, people called her the Warrior Nurse and thought it praise. Her uncle had said it at a feast as an insult. She spent the years after making it true.

Lily had her mother's brown hair, darker than Geturo's, the colour of turned earth after rain, and her father's dark, deep-set eyes. In public the children called their father Teacher, and the family knew what the word covered. Geturo, the eldest, studied alchemy at his side. Hiromi, the middle child, walked into a binding circle at six and did something none of the adults present could name. The instructors began to call him the Paragon of Dominion.

The three of them walked in a formation so old that none of them noticed it. Hiromi took the centre because he always took it. Geturo walked the outside and watched the doors. Lily kept to the side nearest wherever they were going, because she was the one who knew.

### The Making

She studied her mother's methods, and she learned to reach a junction before her brothers did. Her Academy coat went on pressed, the collar starched stiff. She tied her sash in the knot the handbook specified, which no student in living memory had managed without being told to try harder. A silver pin on her left lapel marked her year-rank.

Hiromi did not eat. He collapsed in the practice ring three times, and each time Lily carried him to the infirmary. Each time the nurse looked at her as if she were the one who had forgotten to feed him. She began to watch his plate.

One morning in the upper gallery she stood in the middle of the corridor with her arms folded and waited for her brothers. "If you two don't stop arguing, I'll destroy this entire hall." Hiromi stuck out his tongue. She told them they were late. The morning register had begun, and Father would hear of it before the second bell, she said, because she would tell him. "I already have the letter drafted. In my head. It's concise." She fell in on Hiromi's left and told him he had not eaten. He said the collapse had happened once. "It happened three times." When he ran ahead she turned to Geturo and said he had been at the circles again. Geturo said before dawn. Her jaw tightened. She adjusted her pin, which had not moved, and walked to the archway at the pace that would bring her in on time without looking hurried.

The Academy placed her in Class X, among students it deemed too unusual for ordinary progression. On the first morning the three of them came through the chamber door together. Each greeted the man at the slate board as Teacher. Hers was clipped, a formality delivered as one. What the Academy had seen in her to seat her there, it did not say.

### The Cost

She kept the count exact. When Hiromi answered that it had been once this term, the atrium opened in front of them, and the number she had given him stood at three. The infirmary trips were hers. So was the nurse's look.

The letters to her father were drafted in her head and were concise. The man she drafted them to stood at the front of her classroom with chalk on his hand. When she was absent, Hiromi stuck his tongue out. Geturo let her steer and never contested it.

At the introductions Hiromi stood last. He named his goal as becoming the greatest summoner who ever lived, and grinned. Her pen stopped. She looked at him for two seconds, and then the pen resumed. At the dorm assignments he turned down any roommate and said he would rather have his own peace and quiet. Three seats down, her pen stopped again.

### Where They Stand

Lily sits in Class X in its first week, in the circular chamber where her father is homeroom teacher and instructor in basic magic and rudimentary summoning. Her mother is among the Class X instructors, with a weapon instructor's sash at her left hip. On the first morning Frithia took the room in a sentence. She had Hiromi's boots off the table and set him push-ups for talking back. Lily watched her with the attention of someone taking notes on a method she intends to adopt.

Her pen keeps moving when the room is in uproar. Her father has told the class that the Night's Watch will come later in the week, for field work on active magical events, and that tempering and the classifications of magic come first. The Class X Open Tournament is seven days out. Hiromi has fought the masked boy Xanelor in the Combat Arena and come out of it emptied, and nobody has named the winner.

No discipline of hers has been shown, and she carries no title. Her lapel pin has not moved. She adjusts it anyway.

## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Tenryū and the dragon-blooded. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Lyra Drakoshi was born in drovers' country on the Southern Grass Sea, on the marcher side of the line with Drakyssian ranch territory. She came with horns, a tail and slow embers already rolling in her chest. No House claimed her. Her mother kept cattle on hired grazing and had no seat to hand on.

Anger made the child hot. At a quarrel over a strayed calf she lost her temper, and the dry grass round her feet caught. The fire ran across a neighbour's pasture and took his hay before the drovers beat it out with wet hides. After that she walked into the stock pond whenever the heat rose in her. She stood there until the steam stopped coming off her hair. The neighbours kept her away from their ricks. She left young and came up along the border on raw survival, standing night watches for anyone who would pay a horned girl.

### The Making

She won her first contracts by being faster and hotter than whatever stood in front of her. Her wounds sealed almost as soon as they were cut. She seldom had to think past that.

She burned a great deal. A granary town on the Grass Sea road hired her to hold its store against raiders through the harvest. They came at night. She met them in the yard between the barns and drove them off, and her fire went up into the thatch behind her. The store burned with the season's grain inside it. The town paid her in full and never hired her again.

She spent the rest of her mercenary years learning to win without burning everything down. She learned to send the heat out through the wound she cut and keep it off the people beside her. She learned footwork that looped and spiralled, and straight rushes slid past her or fell short. The men beside her called her the Coiled Blade. Wherever she could, she fought out of doors, where the heat had somewhere to go.

Late in those years she hired on, by her own choice, to the local Guild Accord watch posts on one outer border road. When they needed dragon-class muscle fast they sent up a flare. She came. She came back to the same road and the same gate over and over, and on the day she chose them again, she called that choosing responsibility.

### The Cost

The choice closed the rest of the border to her. She takes no contract that would carry her out of reach of the flares, holds no House seat and answers to no throne. She keeps no hoard. She is bound to a stretch of road and the people who use it.

Two feuding border lords came to parley in the stone-floored hall by her gate. One had contracted Saórén Vyrrakal as champion and as show; the other had asked Seravain Drelith, the Marchlord of Glass, to stand behind his claim. The Marchlord's formed ranks came up to the verge of the road, and the watch post fired a flare. Lyra came and shut her gate to the ranks. She told the Marchlord the table was open to him if he walked in alone. He came through by himself. The chime on her horn stayed quiet while he walked behind her.

Saórén made the entrance they had been paid for, flame trails on the floor and the grin. Lyra watched it to the end and told them it was good fire and a good show. Then she smelled the room. Both lords gave off sour smoke. The Marchlord gave off none. Under Saórén's perfume she could smell nothing, and she had no other way to read them. When the lords began to call their champions forward, she heated the stone under their boots until the soles smoked. She grinned while she did it. The hall laughed, the lords laughed with it, and both signed.

The duel was never fought. He walked back out through the gate, and his ranks left the verge. The ground where they had waited took a glaze and kept it. The carts on her road go round it now.

### Where They Stand

The next trading season, the watch post sent for someone to anchor a festival truce on the road. Daigo Tenryū came. He set the fair's pace with his horns and bit red cords into knots on the traders' promises. Lyra kept the gate as the watching dragon. No blade came out that season. The traders came back the next season, and the one after.

The road is busy now. She is half legend on it, and half grumpy regular at the local inn, whose common room stays warm while she sits in it. To frightened travellers at the gate she says, "Relax. If something tries to kill you, I will roast it first and ask questions if there is anything left." She will tell anyone exactly how the fire works, usually while grinning about it. Nobody knows whether the second bite of her corkscrew cut is one wound felt twice or two wounds a heartbeat apart.

## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Alabaster and the Scale. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Maeron Iskare was born in the lower cloisters of Alabaster, to a bloodline of the Holy Sea that had come out of the Outer World and was touched by Abythron sleep. A prophecy buried him before birth. No roll of the Sea carried the name he would have had, and he grew up under the cloister roofs without an entry anywhere.

The Sea's whole apparatus was built to weigh a sworn account. Its cloisters were full of people who had sworn to things, and they talked to the boy. A child with no entry could carry nothing to anyone who kept one. So they told him what they would never have sworn to, and he kept his face still while they did it. He put the habit in his own words: "to see others clearly, I hid myself."

Decades afterward he came up out of the cloisters under a new name.

### The Making

He brought the new name to the Guild Accord. Its Obscurial Chamber took him in as a diviner. By then people spoke to him and confessed without wanting to. The Chamber's diviners came to rely on his readings, and they kept their sittings with him short. He never named what he did. The instruments named it for him, and so did the Accord's readers.

He wore the Golden Veil already, a mask of beaten gold and obsidian feathers forged with fragments from Eclipseron's Eye. He wore it into the Well.

He came out of Eclipseron's Eye its only known survivor. The exposure gave him permanent dream perception and fused the mask to his flesh. Behind his head a ring of distortion formed, visible under Essence light, and the Accord's readers called it the Mirror Halo. The contradiction in him was compassion delivered as unveiling. He did not resolve it. He wore it, as he wore the mask.

### The Cost

His inner law was "only what is revealed can heal," and revelation broke more than it restored. Each truth he revealed cost him a piece of self-perception. He forgot small personal facts. At night the mask whispered to him and claimed to remember faces he had forgotten. He did not trust what it remembered.

He never took it off. Taken off, it would let loose an uncontrolled surge of cruel honesty; cracked, it would fail his filters and pour the overload into every being nearby. His reason was his own: "faces are promises, and mine has broken too many."

His record carries the Mirror Siege, undated, in which twelve dream-linked clerics were sealed inside a shared sleep for eternity to stop a psychic contagion. It carries the Guild inquest called False Sun, which accused him of leaking prophetic data. The evidence reflected itself back at the investigators.

The rupture a boy of the Aurum Sect had called on the Day of Seven Refractions matched no forecast the Accord's councils held, and the Obscurial Chamber sent Maeron to read it. The Ecliptic Choir arranged the sitting in Seraphine Lurielle's White Parlour. She poured. He left his cup where it stood. He asked leave to take the boy, Lumen Varos, into a shared sleep with the hostess as the third, and she refused him. She said it once. He asked across the table instead. The torque at his throat stayed cold through every answer. He filed his reading with the councils, and by morning some small fact of his own was gone. The mask offered it back in the night. He did not write it down.

### Where They Stand

He is stationed in Eresse for the present, for a Wellspring audit of the Alabaster cathedral. He is Bound Advisor to the Guild Synod, and his forecasts of Wellspring resonance go to the High Concordant's councils. Inside the Holy Sea he keeps a confidential covenant with the Ecliptic Choir. To the Church he is tolerated heresy. Factions of it plan to destroy the relic inside his mask. He intends to let them try.

He moves slowly. He already knows where the blow lands. The mirrored plates of the torque at his throat reflect nothing and burn faintly when deception is near. When he walks through candlelight the shadows crawl toward him.

He seeks the Quiet Revelation, a rumoured state where knowing and forgetting become identical. Nobody knows whether the face under the mask is still his, or whether the prophecy that he will one day see without eyes is a promise or a sentence.

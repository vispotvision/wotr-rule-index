## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Star Chamber's Protocol Corps. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Marigold Vance was born in Bridgemarch, where the tunnels of Nalūn converge and the chain-bridges span open caverns. Her mother worked the span crews. She drove deck pins with a two-headed hammer and walked every span at shift's end, one hand on the chain rail. Marigold carried the pin bag behind her. She learned that a crowd on a bridge has to move in order, or somebody goes over the rail.

At a change of shift a hauler struck a man on the main span with a salt bar. The crowd came from both bridgeheads to see. Men took the hauler to the rail. Her mother came up the deck, struck the boards once, and asked them to set him down and stand where she put them. Some of the crowd stood. The men at the rail did not. The hauler held on to the chain, and they beat his hands against the links until the knuckles split and he let go. Marigold saw it from the bridgehead. When the Chain Guard came down the tiers, nobody at the rail gave a name. The Guard took none.

### The Making

She took up her mother's hammer and kept the main span. Quarrels broke out at every change of shift, and she ended them at the hand. A tap across the forearm, and the knife or the bar dropped to the boards with the knuckles whole. Then she wrote the man a chit for a shift of labour. Most of them worked it off.

The quarrel that made her came at a change of shift. A carrying company's porters and a Bridgemarch crew fell out over a load. The crew had a porter at the rail. She stamped heel and toe on the boards, and a circular court stood on the span around her. Inside it, blows turned into objections, and weapons remembered they were evidence. The men at the rail set the porter down. At her elbow the foreman promised the company its load by the next shift, and a tiny gilt chit printed into her belt-purse. She wrote no more chits by hand.

The company had never sealed under Nalūn, so the quarrel was the Accord's to hear. Vireline of the Three Masks wrote the Measure. Marigold kept its floor. The Star Chamber's Protocol Corps took her as Bailiff-at-Large to Vireline's Measures.

### The Cost

In the corps she was the one who could hit somebody. Lord Lucen Vale did not strike. None of Cassiel Asterion's workings struck. When a hall turned to blows, her gavel made their order stick. Over-winding overheated its spring. Two Verdicts in a row demanded a cooling count, and for that count nobody in the corps could hit anyone.

She gave every offender one offence. The sunburst cuffs bit only pride on the first, and the shame warmed her own wrist. A second harm set the rings prickling like nettles until the apology came, and it had to arrive with a name.

When a writ had to be served, she made the aisle. A Verdict swing carved it through the crush. Her Escort Lane held it, and she walked it with the chain humming. The crowd stepped aside as if their mothers had asked nicely. Commander Severin Bale walked behind her to the name at its far end. Nobody in the crowd was bruised. The man at the end of the aisle belonged to the writ.

A promise made within her reach printed into the purse, and a broken one rattled there like coins owed. At Ironlink Hold she stood at a factor-house partner's shoulder while he answered Vireline's Measure all morning in embroidery. Pierre set up a harmless joke. On the laugh the partner said plainly he would carry the grain at the old rate. Cassiel turned the Facet Seal once, and it took. A chit printed at her hip.

On the Rimlands road below Altherion she stood at Arven Solthiel's shoulder while he gave a sleeping keeper's account in the keeper's own voice. The tiara did not tilt. Nothing was promised within her reach, and no chit printed. For days he spoke in that voice, and the carters in the yard heard it come out of a stranger. She walked him through them to the road.

### Where They Stand

Marigold Vance is the Gilded Bailiff, and in hot halls the trusted escort of Yukizumi Yukari, the Reaper. She wears a crownlet like a toy halo and black-and-gold party steel over a velvet frock. She carries the clockwork war-gavel Ordinance and the Sunburst Chain.

Vireline is her favourite judge. Her court blooms around Vireline's hem. She keeps Measures sacred when tempers spike and turns lynchable minutes into hearings. She walks the Reaper and the witnesses through fevered corridors and collects receipts so Lucen and Cassiel can seat the truth. On hot evacuations Cyrille Lacrim keeps her aisle gentle, and Seraphine Kharza's palms keep it soft.

Mud and broken ground blur her circle. She prefers boards, flags or clean stone. Rot-noise makes the tiara wobble. The chit from Ironlink Hold is still in her purse. It has never rattled.

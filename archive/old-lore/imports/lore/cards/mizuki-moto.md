## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Ashen Crown: Muken's house and the Moto lines. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Mizuki Moto was the firstborn of Muken Moto, of the Moto blood and the royal strain of Kharven. Her father had taken the north by conquest and by rite, and the fighting did not stop when he was crowned.

The cold came with her. Wet cloth stiffened with frost when she stood near it. People beside her saw their own breath. Her hands were always a little cooler than anyone expected, and she smelled of clean cold air and faint lavender. She grew up in her father's house, small and lightly built, with steady hands and strong legs, and long white hair she wore high.

She learned early where her gift belonged. It was needed most after the violence, in the room where the injured were carried, among the cots and the basins, once the fighting had moved on.

### The Making

At twelve she held a wound still for the first time. She stopped a bleed long enough for the treatment to reach it. When her field ended, the wound opened again. After that she read bodies through the cold itself: a pulse shifting, a breath losing its rhythm, a tremor in the hand, how a man stood on his feet.

At the Frost-Braid Trial she held three trainees in stasis at once. Her own pulse slowed with theirs until she collapsed, and from that day the slowing was hers to carry.

At sixteen she beat an older fighter. Frost bands closed on his sword as it came back from the swing and pinned it there, and he could not recover his guard.

Her father's war strategy needed her. The Moto War Court made her its battlefield preservation specialist, and she went where the wounded would be, with pulse needles for the pressure points and plain mending wraps.

In the last years of the reign the hobgoblins pressed the western line, and the Greymanes held it from the Ridgewall. One raid kept the fortress under assault from dusk to dawn. A room behind the wall filled with the wounded. Mizuki opened her field over it and held it all night, slowing blood and breath and the leak of Essence until the surgeons could reach each cot. One soldier there could not be saved. Her field held him anyway, and his dying took eleven hours. Her father's sister, Yukazuri, sat down beside him and listened to him to the end. Mizuki kept the wounded room alive through that siege night, and she remembered every breath that failed before dawn.

### The Cost

Every hold was paid for in her own body. Frost burned her inner wrists. It left faint scars. Freezing an enemy's joints again and again stiffened her own hands. After the widest bloom her hands went numb, her pulse slowed and her knees went weak beneath her. When she quieted another person's panic, it came back into her own breathing.

Heat undid her work. So did fire, and dry air, and a battle that ran too long. She could slow a wound. She could not close a ruptured organ, stop necrosis or reach a wound in the soul, and at every cot she knew which of those she faced.

She held people in stasis past the point of wisdom. The soldier at the Ridgewall was one of them. To her, releasing the field was abandonment, so she kept it over him while he breathed.

She wanted a field wide enough that nobody inside it died of something she could have slowed. Each death of that kind she counted as hers. She had held people at the edge of death and watched them fall when her field ended. She remembered every one. When the wounded asked her for comfort, she gave them an assessment.

### Where They Stand

She is nineteen and active in Kharven, Muken's eldest child and the War Court's battlefield preservation specialist. Her record leaves her inside her father's reign. The Frost-Braid Cord is bound into her side braid. It keeps her hands from shaking when the numbness comes back. She wears the white winter cloak and carries the twin needles, and she reaches for the mending wraps first, because they cost her no cold.

The field she can open is still narrower than the one she wants. Nobody yet sits the hours after with her. The night she must decide whether letting go is acceptance or abandonment has not come.

### Ties

- **Muken Moto** · father and firstborn, contested. His war strategy required her, and his own record lists his children without her.
- **Yukazuri Moto** · aunt and niece; comrades. At the Ridgewall her field held a dying soldier while her aunt listened to him die.
- **Sodoku Moto** · siblings, contested. She is recorded as Muken's firstborn and heir, he as the Kōkan line's former Crown Heir, and no record puts them in one room.
- **Ayame Yuno** · one court. She serves the same Crown of Kharven as the priest-queen, and no record names Mizuki's mother.

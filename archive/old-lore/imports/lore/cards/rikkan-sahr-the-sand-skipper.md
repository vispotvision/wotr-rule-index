## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Sun-Sealed and the horizon couriers. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Rikkan Sahr was born on a salt road in the Varakh Drylands of the Outer World, among the Caravan Clans. His people carried salt off the White Pans and sold it wherever a market would buy. Rain in the Drylands came in good years and then failed. The rivers ran by season, and the caravan went where the water was.

He was the child sent ahead. He ran the track in front of the beasts and came back with what the ground was doing: a dry bed, a soft pan, thorn across the way. He learned to read an Essence storm by its flow and to say which flank of it a caravan could pass. He could run in short bursts that no rider matched. When he cut sideways, afterimages of him flickered along the line he had left.

Raiders worked the salt roads. Arrows loosed at the running boy went wide, and a blade swung at him slipped on the sand. A snare laid across the track closed on nothing. The luck held while he moved. He did not stop.

### The Making

He left the salt road for the Guild Accord. The Logistics and Supply Division ran messages and secure deliveries between Concord-controlled Wellsprings, across Outer World frontiers where Domain stability was low, and it took him on as a runner. Other runners went around ground he crossed. He outran certain Domain collapses and came through Essence storms by reading their flow, as he had read them for the caravan. Relics lost in bad country came out in his pack. His delivery times had no match on the frontier, and the Division's Quartermasters began to give him priority assignments.

Zahiren Vahl of the Arbitration Division became his trusted contact. Rikkan delivered sensitive maps and artifacts to him without question and carried Zahiren's sealed-Wellspring cartography to Research and Archives. When the Arbitration Division needed a transfer kept off the record, he smuggled it for Zahiren freelance. He never asked what he carried. Zahiren did not ask either, and of the two of them Zahiren was the one who physically could not lie about having moved it.

There was a run he settled before he set out on it. He decided in advance, in cold blood, that what the satchel held was worth more than his own structural integrity. Then he willingly accepted a genuine soul-cost to preserve it.

### The Cost

He paid the soul-cost he had agreed to before the road began.

His luck came on terms he could not change. It worked while he was moving, and the moment he stopped, it stopped with him. Anything that pinned him, anchored him or closed a ring around him stripped out his whole craft at once. He could not fight. When anyone came at him, he ran.

On a road across the Varakh Drylands he carried a sealed package from Zahiren to Research and Archives. The road crossed a frontier where Domain stability was low. Research and Archives asked the Arbitration Division for an escort, and it sent Kaelen Raive. Neither man asked what it held. Out on the red earth an Essence storm came across the road. Rikkan read its run and took a line through its edge at a pace no escort could keep. Loosened ground held under his feet while he kept moving on it, and what the wind threw at him went wide.

Kaelen could not follow. He anchored himself on the road and stood while the storm went over him, then walked the rest of the way alone. By the time he reached the Wellspring, the package had been handed over and signed for. Rikkan was sitting on the sanctum step. He had not waited for his escort, and he did not stand still on open ground for anyone.

### Where They Stand

Rikkan Sahr still runs for the Logistics and Supply Division between Concord-controlled Wellsprings, the Horizon Courier of the Concord. The Quartermasters still send him the priority work. He delivers maps and artifacts to Zahiren Vahl without question, and when the Arbitration Division wants a transfer off the record, he makes it. Kaelen Raive rides escort on some of his hazardous deliveries. He values Rikkan's speed and distrusts his gambling with fate.

He has never fought anyone and does not intend to. Among the Outer World Caravan Clans he keeps neutral standing by exchanging news and small relics as gifts, and he takes no clan's side against another. His own account of himself is short: "If you see dust on the horizon, it's either a storm or me. And I'm worse for whoever's chasing you."

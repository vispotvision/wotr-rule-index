## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The elven old-ground. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Saetsha Bloomveil was born Eressean, in the Grand Principality of Eresse on the elven old-ground, among a people who keep their promise to the land in the same words and the same order every time. She learned the pitch of the tongue before its words. The voice rises for what is unfinished and falls for what is settled. In Eresse a raised voice is called loud, and loud is said of the drunk and the blasphemous.

She was trained as a second reader. In the half-sunk council chambers a deposition is read by jurists in alternation, and the second is there to catch the first. Her work was to hear the wrong word in another mouth and stop it before it entered the record. Nothing passed there unwitnessed. She could recite the proverb before she could draw on anything: "A standard nobody witnessed is a standard held contingently."

### The Making

She left the chambers for Elyndra's service. She attended the Archon of renewal as a handmaiden and carried a spear in the Archon's train, and one of her names is still Rose-Spear Attendant. Nearness to the Archon transfigured her. Elyndra's mark rose on her as a living sigil. From then on dying groves and wounded ground found their way to her for triage.

Elyndra made her herald. A herald's discipline is the declaring. An Edict spoken quietly is an Edict spoken wrong. She had been raised to hear loud as an insult, and she learned to put an Archon's law into the air at full voice. Behind every Edict she spoke stood Elyndra's own: "What grows must die; what dies must bloom again."

Then Elyndra went silent. In every account Saetsha has, every Domain of Renewal fell dark with her. In the hours after, every grove dedicated to Elyndra went still. Saetsha walked among them alone. In each region she revived a single beacon tree, and then went on.

The day she declared her first Edict in Elyndra's absent name, she found that reality leaned toward the outcome anyway, with no Archon left to co-sign it.

### The Cost

What answers her now is a living echo of Elyndra's presence, and every Edict draws it down. After each great working it is quieter than it was before. The Elyndric Heart-Rosette, a brooch holding the central shard of Elyndra's severed Glyph Chain, keeps that last echo from drifting into the Epochal void. When she spins the Glaive of the Silent Archon and closes a ring of petals, whatever steps fully inside is bound to one foreclosed future. A blade that should land does not. The ring is paid from the same echo, and the voice reads quieter after every casting.

She will not draw on mortality under any circumstance. She holds that stealing mortality's lesson to fuel healing is the wound Elyndra suffered in the first place. She has paid for that at the barrow's edge.

A grove on the Tsohanto Reach, inside the mile of the Long Barrow Court, was dying of blight, and the mark drew her to it. It stood on Onawa Ashkewe's ground, which held the stillness of a place already grieved for. The queen was there first. In her presence the blight ran faster toward clean healing or clean death. Saetsha declared an Edict of Growth in Elyndra's absent name. One tree took it and lived. The rest finished dying cleanly, and Onawa let it. Neither asked the other to yield. Saetsha came away with one living tree and a grove she could not keep, finished by a queen who carries that ending as mercy. The ending was the half of Elyndra's edict she will not touch.

### Where They Stand

She works on her own account, loosely and reluctantly aligned with the Guild Accord, and she overrules any Concord where renewal and extinction hang in the balance. Accord standing lets her into elder vampiric and mortal courts in passing. Nobody has measured her since she stopped being anyone's herald on the record, and the reputation the Accord gives her is talk.

Dying ground still comes to her whether she seeks it or not. Her veil scatters hostile sight and scrying. Where Elyndra's Domain once touched the world she hears a faint resonance that is silence to anyone else. She can still reach into Elyndra's last known dream traces. At Veyran Crossings, in full wrath, she turned a Withering legion's corrupted biomass into a living wall of vines. The wall still holds the pass.

No Guild or Accord record corroborates her account beyond her own testimony. She may be the only living witness to something the wider Lattice has not yet noticed. Vaelorian Theryn Aerethiel is still called Paragon of Elyndra. Whether she chooses the future her ring denies, or something on the far side of Elyndra's silence chooses it for her, nobody knows.

In her own words: "Until she answers, my spear will ask the question in her name."

### Ties

- **Elyndra** · the Archon she served. She was the Archon's handmaiden and herald.
- **Vaelorian Theryn Aerethiel · The Last Elven Lord** · bound to one Archon. He is still called Paragon of Elyndra, and in her account the Archon he is named for went silent and every Domain of Renewal fell dark with her.
- **Onawa Ashkewe** · witnesses, opposed in doctrine. At the barrow's edge she saved one tree and Onawa let the rest of the grove finish, and each held a half of Elyndra's edict.
- **The Guild Accord** · a loose, reluctant alignment. She overrules it the moment renewal and extinction are both on the table.

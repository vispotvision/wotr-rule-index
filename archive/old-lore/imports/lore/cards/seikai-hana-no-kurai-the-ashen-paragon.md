## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Zettari war camp and the Mahuo who came to it. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Seikai Hana-no-Kurai was born in the Frontier Marches, on the contested western border of Ketsuen, to settlers who had gone there to be governed by nobody. They let the Warden in for one reason. The wards worked. A settlement whose gate posts failed in the night took governance from whoever could mend them, and the Marches took it on those terms and argued over everything else. Her people wrote down as little about themselves as they could. She learned the habit before she learned why.

She was trained to fight with her body and her hands, to stand between a settlement and what came out of the forest. Nobody taught her a working. She never asked for one. She grew hard to tire, able to walk all day and fight at the end of it, and on the frontier she held a good many people while they died.

### The Making

She took protection contracts along the frontier and kept them for years. The settlements that hired her got a veteran whose body closed on a threat before she had decided to move.

She had carried the Ashen Wrap since before her child was born. Her firstborn was taken through a transaction. She saw to it herself that no official record would ever hold the event.

Her power arrived on its own, without instruction, and it came through grief. Loss went into her and came back out as pressure. She did not break. The morning she understood her power was working correctly was also the morning it had already cost her the thing it was supposed to protect. She called it "operational" from that morning on, and she still does. The other word is worse.

### The Cost

At Halvren's Pass she was fighting beside soldiers under contract when the battle brought her to the edge of collapse. What she had been holding went out of her. The air around her bent and went grey, and everyone near her weakened where they stood, friend and enemy alike. Two of the soldiers never recovered their Wellspring connection. Held past a few seconds, the surge turned inward and broke her own channels, and those breaks had not finished mending before the next time came.

After the fighting a tall woman in black mourning dress and gold-stitched gloves walked the pass among the wounded. The small black bells on her rosary rang when she stood near Hana-no-Kurai's hands. She was Yorime Seikai, of the Ashuric line of Boundary custodians, and she followed what the two soldiers had lost, faintly, as far as the woman who had fought beside them. She took nothing. She told Hana-no-Kurai nothing of what the pass had shown her, and she went on. Hana-no-Kurai heard the bells. She never learned what they rang for.

She paid the two soldiers what she could, until she couldn't. The contracts ended one after another, and nothing replaced them. Every bond she held, she tried to end for the other person's safety, and when anyone asked more of her after that, she gave them the same answer.

"I already paid. I paid everything. Stop asking me to pay again."

### Where They Stand

She holds no contract and answers to no one. Anyone who stays close to her for long comes away drained and cannot say why. She cannot stop it, and she does not know it is happening. Diviners read her poorly. Trackers often lose her. The only defence anyone has against what comes out of her is distance, and that holds for her as well.

Her hands keep the touch of everyone she has held in death. She feels them when she goes quiet, and her child is always the loudest. She still carries the Ashen Wrap. Under distress a thin, broken light bleeds from her hands, and it dims when she holds the wrap. Nobody knows why, she least of all.

She believes she is human, and nothing has told her otherwise. Nobody explains to her face what she is, and she would refuse the explanation if anyone tried. Yorime keeps what Halvren's Pass showed her. What happens when the pressure in her exceeds what she can hold has not happened yet. She has not yet kept a vow past the point of ruin, on purpose, awake.

### Ties

- **Yorime Seikai** · kin by line; witness. At Halvren's Pass a stranger of her own line walked among the wounded, and small black bells rang near her hands; the stranger took nothing and went on without saying what they rang for.
- **Her firstborn** · child. Taken through a transaction she kept out of every official record, the child is the loudest of the dead her hands remember.
- **The soldiers at Halvren's Pass** · debt. Two lost their Wellspring connection to her surge mid-battle; she paid what she could until she couldn't.

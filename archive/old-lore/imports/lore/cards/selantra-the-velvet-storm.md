## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Triune of the Three-Headed Serpent. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Selantra was born in the Concord, in a town that kept a Facultas, and her mother washed the crucibles and the glass from its benches. The vessels came down to the wash-house scorched, cracked or still warm. A failed working left a stain. Her mother could name the reagents in it by the colour before the water touched it, and Selantra learned the stains beside her tub. She watched them lift off the glass and turn the water their colour.

On the days the Facultas examined its Scholars, the servants stood along the back wall of the hall. She stood with them. The Scholars prepared their spells at the benches, and the room waited on them.

### The Making

She learned alchemy away from those benches, and she learned it fast. When she came back to the Facultas she ran her reactions along conduits bound into her skin. They resolved faster than any Scholar on the benches could have prepared a spell. She worked before the Facultas in its hall and made a spectacle of it for the room.

One reaction came back in a form she had never seen. She tried it anyway. The backlash ran into her through the conduits, and her Crystal began to fail in front of the hall. Velcrissa held the chair of Temperance Studies there. She stayed in her seat and, keeping her voice low, hurried the failing Crystal on to its settling. Her working reached Selantra before anyone heard her speak, and the hall took it for a change in the weather.

Selantra lived. The wound was to her pride, and she left it open. She took the load-bearing wound and made it hold weight on purpose.

### The Cost

From the hall on, her backlash went to her pride, and she paid it later. She held that a failed working destroyed no Essence and left it in an inconvenient basis, where she could take it back as fuel. She could turn any failure into fuel. She could not take a hit.

The hall remembered her Crystal failing and never learned whose working had settled it. Selantra knew. She went out of that hall owing Velcrissa her life.

At Altherion, in a hired room, Virelsa spoke to her and to Velcrissa, and while she spoke the light that lay dormant in her flickered. Selantra had never seen that reaction. She tried it that night, alone. It would not come, and the backlash went to her pride with the rest. The three of them agreed in that city to work as one order. They named it the Triune of the Three-Headed Serpent, with Virelsa as its Matron.

### Where They Stand

Selantra is an Aetheric alchemist and a co-founder of the Triune of the Three-Headed Serpent, and she stands with Velcrissa and Virelsa. Her pride still holds the backlash it has taken, and the paying of it is still to come. She owes Velcrissa the settling in the hall. The flicker she saw at Altherion is a reaction she has never been able to run.

What can stop her is a thing she has never seen. She will attempt it anyway.

Serai Linthra of the Alchemists' Society begins her workings where Selantra begins hers, with the change of basis, and each of them knows the other's work.

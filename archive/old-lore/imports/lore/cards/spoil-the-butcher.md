## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Ash and the red thread. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Spoil was made twice. The first making was a birth, and the child was named Tanya. The second was done with forbidden graft-binding rites: Tanya's head was sewn onto a male body, and what rose from the rites answered to Spoil.

The join ran round the throat. It should have stayed open. It closed and held, and it did not bleed. Two provenances had integrated in one frame where they should not have, and the body answered the head with a speed and a sureness nobody had taught it. From then on, a cut at the neck was a wound Spoil could get up from.

They put the first name down and never picked it up again. They took no house and held no ground. Work found them where they happened to be: raids for the warlords of the Outer World, and contracts from dissidents of the Concord.

### The Making

They fought with two blades, at a feral speed the body carried and the head aimed. One was a Ram-Dao saber, bound in wrappings.

Spoil bound Raktabija, a blood-seed Asura, in permanent servitude. The Asura became their arsenal and their second fighter, and the saber came to be called Raktabija's Finger. From then on it leaked blood through its wrappings. What it spilled did not fall. Blood taken from the field stood up as constructs, and the constructs were Raktabija's, and Raktabija did not tire. The seep fed Spoil's draw from the saber's side and left their own reserves untouched.

Then the noise stopped. Spoil's identity settled, and the resonance that would be recognisably theirs for the rest of their life finished arriving. Nobody reaches that alone. The soul that held load for Spoil was Raktabija, bound in permanent servitude. It held because it could not leave.

### The Cost

The binding and the settling were one thing. Severing the first would cost Spoil the second, so the Asura could never be let go, and anyone who came for Raktabija came for the self it held up. Kaerith Vireth did, on Outer World hunts.

The saber had its limit as well. There had to be blood.

At Kethra, on the Eastern Sun Coast, a warlord of the Outer World paid Spoil for a merchant. Spoil came in by night and found him already dead. There was no mark on him, and his blood was still in him. Nothing had spilled for the saber to work. The house guards gave it what it needed. Spoil cut out through them with both blades, and what left the guards stood up behind Spoil as Raktabija's constructs. In the morning the house counted its master among the Butcher's dead and put a price on Spoil's head. The warlord paid for the merchant. Spoil took that pay for a death they had not made, and the price came with it. Whoever killed that man worked blood too, and Spoil never saw who.

The price brought hunters. When a warlord sent a raid against a copper enclave on the Glass Coast, Spoil rode with it beside Renjiro Kaithen. Zahir Ashveil reached the raid first, over the hot glass in the last heat of the day, and went for Spoil. Renjiro met him there. Then the sea fog came in with the dark, and Kaerith came out of it. Kaerith wore Zahir down first. Then the frost went into Raktabija, to freeze it and shatter it. The blood froze on the Asura. It did not shatter.

Once, when the frost came at Renjiro from a side he had not covered, Spoil stood Raktabija's blood in its way. Renjiro's vows bar him from striking anyone he owes. Before the fog lifted he took a strike of Kaerith's frost on his mantle to cover Spoil, and he counted the debt square. He had not stayed in Spoil's debt until morning.

### Where They Stand

Spoil is nomadic and holds no ground. The people who hire them call them the Blood-Seed Marauder and the Red-Thread Butcher. Against a bloodless opponent, a construct or an undying thing or a thing of stone, Spoil has two blades and nothing to raise from the ground. Tattoos run across their back, violent and sexual scenes worked close together, and they ward Spoil against certain prophetic divinations. They wear ancient golden spiked shackles as ornament.

The Kethra price is still on their head. Renjiro still fights beside them. Each respects what the other can kill, and the distrust between them runs deep. He owes them nothing now. His vows no longer stay his hand. Hyo Miran fences the relics Spoil captures, on occasion, and the dealing is tense and transactional. Spoil takes his coin and has never taken his thread. Kaerith is still hunting the Asura. Raktabija is still bound.

Spoil does not answer to Tanya. They say: "Blood's just memory made warm again. I like to remind people of what they've forgotten."

### Ties

- **Raktabija** · bound servant. The blood-seed Asura serves Spoil in permanent servitude as arsenal and second fighter, and the binding is the bond their settled self stands on.
- **Renjiro Kaithen · The Pale Fang** · comrades. Spoil fights beside Renjiro on Outer World raids with respect for his lethality and deep distrust, and on the Glass Coast watched him square his debt to them before the fog lifted, which leaves him free to strike them.
- **Hyo Miran · The Velvet Dealer** · the fence. Hyo fences Spoil's captured relics on occasion, tense and transactional, and Spoil takes his coin and never his thread.
- **Kaerith Vireth** · hunter. Kaerith has come for Raktabija on Outer World hunts to freeze and shatter it, and on the Glass Coast the blood froze on the Asura and did not break.
- **Rathien Veythar · The Fang** · debt. Rathien's clean kill at Kethra is counted among the Butcher's dead and carries a price on Spoil's head, and Spoil has never seen the man who made it.
- **Zahir Ashveil · Desert's Fang** · hunter and quarry. Zahir came after the Kethra price on the Glass Coast and never reached Spoil.

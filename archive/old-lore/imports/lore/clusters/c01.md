# c01 · Hild's council: the Kōkan Loyalist Coalition

Cluster record for the Lore pass. Members: hild-ice-stark-the-sword-princess, edward-lambert-the-arithmetic, lorn-stark-the-sword-teacher, osric-of-hallenfeld-the-patient-hand, tabitha-hallenfeld-the-road-woman, brida-ashwell-the-warm-diagnosis, robin-ice-the-bastard-runner, bram-greymane-the-ridge, renard-greymane-the-left-of-the-throne.

**Era.** The archive's present in the Inner World, inside the Withering Era, with no year number. The cards stand in the eleventh year after Muken's assassination, at Kharven-Seat and on the roads around it. The scenes carry the council to the ninth hour on the Ashgate road, to the year after, and in `the_revolution_of_the_inner_world.md` to the sixth year of the empire.

Cards are cited by roster slug (the file is the roster's `file`) and scenes by file name under `scenes/`. "New" marks what this record adds where the sources are silent. Hild Ice and Robin Ice are point-of-view cards: what is written here for them comes from the archive, and the one new line for Hild is about who stood near her. Sodoku Moto appears only as his scenes record him.

## 1 Who is here

**Hild Ice (Stark) · The Sword Princess** (hild-ice-stark-the-sword-princess). The card holds her at eleven, acting head of the Kokan Loyalist Coalition, seated on the broken throne at Kharven-Seat with her feet short of the floor. Her father's blood is the Kōkan Line and her mother's is Stark. The card writes her full name as Stark-Moto and marks it "unrecognized". Fern Stark taught her the braid, the altar and the prayer of the hands, and died when Hild was eight or nine. She has never met her father. She braids her own hair each morning because the braiding is the prayer. Under threat or insult the cold comes off her without training, and the left side of her jaw sets before she speaks. In public she speaks in short declarations. At the altar with Brida her sentences turn into questions. The scenes carry her to twelve and to a cistern doorway at the ninth hour, where she died with Osric.

**Edward Lambert · The Arithmetic** (edward-lambert-the-arithmetic). Hild's right hand, fifty-three, who looks sixty. He counts grain stores, troop dispositions and the days between a lord's promise and a lord's delivery. He was born in the Hallenfeld river corridor, a trade administrator's son. He rose in Muken's civil service by being correct. He was three weeks from a minor lordship in that corridor when the kingdom fell. His wife, Kasumi Kurenai Moto, died in the second year after the fall, in the Tenrai restructuring of the cadet lines. The report reached him by a Hallenfeld courier. It came among three trade manifests and a tariff update. He answered the tariff update first. Then he sat in the dark for three days, and on the fourth he set out for the Ironwood March. He reached the holdfast when Hild was four. He stands three paces behind and to the right, keeps his objections for the debrief, and nods once when she is right. The card names what he gives her as service and refuses him the uncles' love.

**Lorn Stark · The Sword-Teacher** (lorn-stark-the-sword-teacher). Fern's brother, forty-four, of House Stark of the Eastern March, senior uncle to Hild and military guardian of the coalition. He was never a great swordsman and trained every day anyway. His sword is kept better than it deserves. When Fern died and the child lay on the floor of that room, not eating and not speaking, he put a wooden practice sword in her hand and said two words: stand up. Later he gave her steel. In public he calls her my lady; in private, girl or child, or her name. When she decides a thing he thinks wrong, he says so at once, in the room, in front of whoever is there. The scenes put Pietro Ashmore's chains in his grip and a broken circlet in his belt on the morning of the ninth hour, and a stone over him at Kharven-Seat.

**Osric of Hallenfeld · The Patient Hand** (osric-of-hallenfeld-the-patient-hand). Born Osric Kōkan Moto of a Kōkan cadet line, forty-seven, Hild's strategic uncle and the coalition's political architect. When the kingdom fell the Tenrai purge judged him peripheral and chose to watch him. For three years he wore the plain wool of a minor Hallenfeld administrator, said nothing, and mapped the Tenrai's administration from inside it. Then he walked out of the Hallenfeld corridor with his father's ring and reached the holdfast in the Ironwood March. He kept his blood to himself for three more years. He told Fern, and Fern told Lorn. He spoke the name Kōkan aloud first to Hild, when she was seven, and she took it as his oath. He counselled the move to the broken throne. His face gives nothing away. His hands arrange pieces on boards nobody else can see, and he keeps his disagreements for after the room. The scenes end him in the cistern doorway where he put himself.

**Tabitha Hallenfeld · The Road Woman** (tabitha-hallenfeld-the-road-woman). Forty-two, of House Hallenfeld of the river corridors, the coalition's logistics officer, road intelligence and supply coordinator, unpracticed in the draw. She is broad and road-built, sun-dark, with two wooden pins in her hair and a cylinder of route manifests at her belt. She carries the manifests as weapons. She reads a room for what it can carry and how long. That reminds Hild of Fern. She walked three days through compromised territory to tell Hild that the roads were talking and that the Tenrai knew where she was. Her card gives the house's habit in one line: the Hallenfelds arrive late to everything because the roads do not pause for assemblies.

**Brida Ashwell · The Warm Diagnosis** (brida-ashwell-the-warm-diagnosis). Thirty-one, Concord-born in a frontier settlement, with a frozen locative surname. She is Hild's personal physician and the coalition's medical authority. She is short and dense, russet-haired, with two bone pins she has used once for field sutures and mentions at meals. Her eyes read a body before she greets it. She brings willowbark before the patient knows the need. The card gives her an office nobody else on the council holds: she tells Hild to eat, means it, follows up and checks. She reads the tight left side of the child's jaw as a load too heavy. Her voice is firm and warm at once: "The treatment is not negotiable."

**Robin Ice · The Bastard Runner** (robin-ice-the-bastard-runner). The card makes him twenty-nine. He has run since fourteen, and he is a courier and intelligence carrier for the Kōkan expedition. He is tall and narrow, auburn, his nose broken leftward, with three sealed message cases at his belt. He is a bastard of the Kōkan cadet line through a minor house the Tenrai purge scattered, Stark by blood and Ice by law. He calls himself Robin Stark, and Zaehaerys calls him Robin Ice. His voice is Concord-flat, the accent of a man who has been everywhere. At the rift he swore himself to both brothers, to the Crown and to the Commander. The scenes make him seventeen and send him to the Alabaster Court, through the narrows at the ninth hour, and up the path in the upper burying ground with a report.

**Bram Greymane · The Ridge** (bram-greymane-the-ridge). Twenty-three, of House Greymane of the Stormfold, Godfrey's grandson and the Shieldwarden's heir, commander of the loyalist Greymane forces and holder of the southern approach. War has aged him early. He carries Godfrey's shield with the ridge device on its face. He threw it once, on the field where his brother Wren smiled at him from behind the Tenrai banner. Then he picked it up again. Lilia's hand stitched the two cuts in his brigandine. Godfrey planted six acres of rye and turnips for two boys, and one boy cannot keep six acres. His words to the dead are "I held, Grandfather."

**Renard Greymane · The Left of the Throne** (renard-greymane-the-left-of-the-throne). Twenty-six, of House Greymane of the Stormfold, Bram's cousin and Godfrey's grand-nephew, Hild's personal guard at the left of the broken throne. His face is harder than Bram's, cut sharp by a youth of mobile warfare in the field. His hand rests on his hilt as a matter of structure. He chose Hild over the Tenrai because Godfrey chose Muken and the family's choice outlived Godfrey. When Wren defected he did not hesitate. He does not speak much.

## 3 The shared history

### The corridor offices at Hallenfeld (new)

- **When.** The first three years after the fall of Muken's kingdom, before Lambert or Osric reached the Ironwood March.
- **Where.** Hallenfeld, the river junction, and the tariff offices of the Hallenfeld corridor on the eastern routes.
- **Who.** Edward Lambert, Osric of Hallenfeld, Tabitha Hallenfeld.
- **What happened.** Lambert's lordship never left the dispatch queue, and after the fall he went on keeping the tariff accounts of the eastern corridor from a desk in the Hallenfeld corridor. Osric came to the same corridor in the plain wool of a minor Hallenfeld administrator, a Kōkan cadet the Tenrai had chosen to watch. The two men worked the same traffic of manifests and tariff returns. Hallenfeld couriers carried it along roads that Tabitha's house kept graded and drained with no crown left to pay for them. Lambert marked the quiet clerk. His figures were always right, and he never put his name forward. Osric kept lists, as he kept every list: the cadet families in the corridor and the officers married into them. Lambert's name was on one because of Kasumi. In the second year a Hallenfeld courier set the report of her death on Lambert's desk among three trade manifests and a tariff update. Lambert answered the tariff update, sealed it, and shut his door for three days. On the fourth he walked to the Hallenfeld junction. Osric served out his third year and then walked out of the corridor with his father's ring. Neither man told the other where he was going.
- **What each took.** Lambert took the habit of reading every Hallenfeld satchel to the bottom, and the knowledge that a death can come filed between a manifest and a tariff update. Osric took a map of the Tenrai's administration drawn from inside it, and the tariff officer's face. He knew that face again when Lambert came to the holdfast announcing himself as a man who could count, and he did not name it. Tabitha's house learned in those years to carry sealed things it could not read on roads nobody paid for. The use for the roads came later, in a broken chair at Kharven-Seat.

### The holdfast in the Ironwood March

- **When.** The holdout years after the fall: from the uncles' coming, through the year Hild was four, to the winter Fern died, when Hild was eight.
- **Where.** Fern Stark's holdfast in the Ironwood March.
- **Who.** Hild Ice, Lorn Stark, Osric of Hallenfeld, Edward Lambert, Brida Ashwell.
- **What happened.** Fern kept a holdfast in the Ironwood March and a daughter whose hair caught violet light, and she paid the Tenrai assessors to look elsewhere. Her brother Lorn was with her. Osric came with his father's ring and a secret he kept three years before he gave it to Fern. Lambert came when Hild was four and announced himself as a man who could count. Fern recognised what he was, and he stayed. When Osric at last spoke the name Kōkan aloud, he spoke it to Hild, at seven. Fern died in the room where the altar stands. The child lay on the floor of that room and would not eat or speak, until Lorn put a wooden practice sword in her hand and said: stand up. (New:) Brida was the holdfast's physician by that winter. Once the child was standing, Brida made her eat, and her rule that Hild does not train on an empty stomach dates from then. Lambert kept the stores through that winter as he had since he came.
- **What each took.** Hild took the sword as the act of getting up, the braid and the altar from her mother, the count from Lambert and the name from Osric. Lorn took the guardianship and never set it down. Osric took the spoken oath, which he treated as the rest of his life. Lambert took the investment he had chosen, the child over the corridor, and taught her from the age of four. Brida took the work Lambert's card gives her: keeping this particular person alive.

### The broken throne

- **When.** From Hild's coming to Kharven-Seat at Osric's counsel, through the council three months later and the bastard speech, to a night in the Thin Weeks after her father's return.
- **Where.** Kharven-Seat: the throne room, the altar room that had been Ayame's, and the passage behind the throne hall.
- **Who.** Hild Ice, Lorn Stark, Osric of Hallenfeld, Tabitha Hallenfeld, Brida Ashwell, Renard Greymane, Edward Lambert, Bram Greymane.
- **What happened.** Hild came to Kharven-Seat on Osric's counsel and carried her mother's altar there herself. Three months on, Lorn gave her the houses. Hallenfeld held. The Keld watched. Ashmore and Thornwall had gone to the Tenrai. Osric came in with Tabitha Hallenfeld, three days off compromised roads, who told the council that exiles were camped on the polar shelf with a man named Robin Ice among them. Hild asked to go to Bram. Osric read the road as Wren's, and Tabitha said she was the target. So Hild sent Brida and supplies south to Bram with the Greymane words, and Bram took the message as the alliance. When Pietro Ashmore called her a bastard in her grandfather's hall, Lorn put him on his knees and held his chains. Renard stood at the left of the throne with his hand on his sword. Hild forbade the killing and named herself Hild Ice, and the cold of that hall stayed in its walls. Lambert's glass fell four fingers. Lorn and Osric went out to send the dispatch south, and Lambert asked her in the empty hall whether she would hand over the reins. In the Thin Weeks a Tenrai practitioner dressed as a Kharven courier reached the altar-room door where Hild slept and Brida lay on the pallet. Renard killed him in the passage. The dying man said that Lambert had opened the west postern for him. Brida put her face to the gap in the door and told Renard, very quietly, that the girl was asleep.
- **What each took.** Hild took her own name, spoken by her. Lorn took the sight of the child saying what her mother would have said, and wet eyes in front of the court. Osric took the claim, seen by the houses. Tabitha found the purpose for the roads in a girl whose feet did not reach the floor. Brida took the road south to Bram's wounded. The ridge learned her word for the men who stop asking for water. Renard took a dead man's claim against Lambert and a brass token against his skin. Lambert took one nod's worth of assessment. He put his question to her with nobody listening. Bram built with the four words.

### The ninth hour

- **When.** The day of the Ashgate road, from before light to the ninth hour.
- **Where.** Kharven-Seat (the yard, the outer wall, the broken sally gate, the cut chamber below the old cistern), the breach at the southern reach, and the narrows of the Ashgate road.
- **Who.** All nine.
- **What happened.** Before light Hild stood at the foot of the stair with Tabitha behind her and the broken circlet in her hand. Her father kissed her hair and rode out. Lorn watched from the outer wall. Lambert had orders to find Sonzai. Before he went out the postern with six riders and no banner, Lorn put out his hand, and Lambert gripped it once. The order given in the yard put Hild below the old cistern with Osric, the gatehouse shut, no sortie for anything. When the column had gone, Lorn picked up the circlet. She had set it on the mounting block and forgotten it; he put it in his belt. At the southern reach Bram held a twenty-two-foot breach with three hundred men against two thousand two hundred. Renard's voice was on his left. Robin Ice rode through the narrows, where nobody had sent him. He reported that Bram held, that Osric had the princess below the cistern, and that Lambert had gone out. Lambert turned back at the fourth mile. The Stormfold road had gone under a ridge. He came back to fight in the mouth of the broken sally gate with forty of the household and about ninety Ashwell tenants. Brida took a wound across the abdomen at the sally gate. At the ninth hour Hild Ice went in the cistern doorway that Osric had put himself in, and the black came through both of them together.
- **What each took.** Hild died at twelve, in a doorway, with the circlet in another man's belt. Osric kept the doorway. Lorn kept the circlet. Lambert came off the gate covered in other men's blood, having shouted a line of household men and tenants in behind him. Brida took the wound she herself would call the third bowl, and eleven days in the altar-room with the night-stone ticking. Bram held the breach on foot past the ninth hour, a dead horse behind him. Renard held the left of his cousin's line. Robin gave his report in its order. Tabitha had stood at the girl's back that morning, for the last time.

### The three stones and the circle

- **When.** The year after the ninth hour, from the southern campaign and the first frost to the signing in the spring.
- **Where.** The lower hall and the upper burying ground at Kharven-Seat; the Stormfold hall and its crossings.
- **Who.** Edward Lambert, Brida Ashwell, Robin Ice, Bram Greymane, Tabitha Hallenfeld.
- **What happened.** Lambert read the three thousand and eleven southern dead aloud himself over six weeks, and in the same six weeks broke the orc host in the southern reach with a supply schedule. Bram came south in the fourth month and found nothing to fight. In the fifth he held the Stormfold crossings for four days and lost three fingers of his left hand. Lambert sent him Wren's name among the dead and sixteen depositions in one bundle. In the seventh month Tabitha asked in open council why Lambert had bought the grain in the first week, and he did not answer. On the morning of the first frost Lambert stood before three stones in the upper burying ground, Hild Ice, Lorn Stark and Osric of Hallenfeld, their lettering cut to one depth at his instruction. Brida came up the path on a stick. Robin Ice rode in with word that the Goblin Senate was hanged. Lambert ordered the four hundred and six hostages killed, and Robin said aye and did it, and Brida watched him go down the path. In the spring the circle was signed in the lower hall after Lambert read the names. Brida stood at the back with her stick. Bram, with three fingers, would not sit.
- **What each took.** Lambert took an order he says he would still give, and a question he has never answered. Brida took a stick for a year, colour that never came back, and no patience left for the sentences people say at gravesides. Robin took what he learned about himself on that path. Lambert built an unwritten office around it. Bram took the roll in the Stormfold book and the dedication to Wren, entered before his own name. Tabitha wrote her question down, dated it, and has been refused twice since.

## 5 Outward

- **Hild Ice** needs the Stark of Hollenmark: the Warden of the Eastern March, who named her Ice before she was born and whom she has never met (uncarded). Her father, Sodoku Moto, is already carried from c03.
- **Edward Lambert** needs Muken Moto (card): the king who filed his name after eleven quarters of correct reports, called his third stake wrong, and never touched it again.
- **Lorn Stark** needs a frontier captain of the kind Part 1 names as a third uncle, Tomas Farren (uncarded): a man with no Moto blood who stood with the coalition for his own reasons.
- **Osric of Hallenfeld** needs an officer of the Tenrai consolidation: one of the assessors who judged him peripheral and watched him for three years in the Hallenfeld corridor.
- **Tabitha Hallenfeld** needs someone of the Bench she sits in the sixth year, or the editor of the Ashwell Register, who printed her question and will not say who gave it to him.
- **Brida Ashwell** needs Nella Forgeman (card), the expedition's surgeon, whose stores Lambert's card counts beside hers.
- **Robin Ice** needs Verinus VII, the Palatine (card), before whom his account of Coldbeck and Ada Foss was entered at weight.
- **Bram Greymane** needs Lilia, the Stormfold nurse (card), who stitched his brigandine and shortened his shield strap without saying so.
- **Renard Greymane** needs his cousin Wren Greymane (uncarded), whose defection he answered without hesitating.

## 6 Same person?

None. No two cards here describe one life. Osric of Hallenfeld shares a given name with Verinus VII, who was born Osric Peyser (`the_true_king_of_the_north_part_21.md`). The two are different men in different houses. Brida's card and Lilia's card give the same words for a treatment, "not negotiable", and the two women stand at different beds in different places.

## 7 Where the cards disagree

Recorded, not resolved. Card against card first, then card against scene or wiki.

- **Bram's approach.** Hild's card: "Holds the western approach with half an army." Bram's card: "holder of the southern approach". The Banner Houses page gives House Greymane "the western border"; the scenes give Bram the southern approach (`the_true_king_of_the_north_part_10.md`, `the_true_king_of_the_north_part_12.md`).
- **When the sword came.** Hild's card: Fern "Died when Hild was 8-9" and Hild has "trained with a sword since she was nine". Lorn's card: "When Fern died and Hild was eight years old". `the_true_king_of_the_north_part_1.md` has her training "since she was twelve", and has Lorn give her his sword "after the second winter".
- **Hild's mother.** Hild's card: "Fern Stark (mother, deceased)". Rikudoku Moto's card: "Hild Ice (half-sister, paternal, through Freya)" and "Freya (dead, Ledger L003): Hild's mother". `08_sodoku_recalescence.md` puts Freya's death at or near Hild's birth.
- **Osric's house.** Osric's card: "born Osric Kōkan Moto, identity suppressed during the Tenrai consolidation". The Banner Houses page: "Osric of Hallenfeld, a survivor of the house's collapse".
- **Osric's arrival.** Osric's card: he "walked out of the Hallenfeld corridor carrying his father's ring and nothing else". `the_true_king_of_the_north_part_1.md`: he "had arrived at the holdfast with his family and his father's ring and nothing else".
- **How many uncles.** Hild's card names two, Lorn and Osric. `the_true_king_of_the_north_part_1.md`: "Three uncles.", the third being "Tomas Farren, the frontier captain who had no Moto blood". `the_true_king_of_the_north_part_13.md` also counts "three uncles".
- **Hild and her father.** Hild's card: "Has never met him." `the_true_king_of_the_north_part_17.md`: Sodoku's hands went where they had gone "when she was a nursing infant and he was seventeen and terrified".
- **Hild's looks.** Hild's card: "Her hair is dark" and "Her eyes are brown". `the_true_king_of_the_north_part_17.md`: "Her hair was pale to the point of colourlessness" and "Her eyes were grey."
- **Lorn's span.** Lorn's card: "the specific accelerated grey that the last eleven years produced". `01_wotr_muster_breach_road_north.md` and `10_wotr_what_the_ground_was_owed.md`: an arrangement about Hild made "nineteen years ago".
- **Lambert's build and past.** Lambert's card: "He is of middling height", "Lambert was not a soldier" and "He was thirty-nine when Muken noticed him." `the_true_king_of_the_north_part_17.md`: "He was tall and had been taller", and Muken knew him "at twenty-six" on a survey line. `05_sodoku_the_fixed_end.md`: "forty years with a sword and no illusions about any of it".
- **Lambert's voice.** Lambert's card: "The last time he raised his voice was the day he received the courier's report about Kasumi". `05_sodoku_the_fixed_end.md`: "Edward Lambert had never raised his voice in his life."
- **Robin's age.** Robin's card: 29, a runner since fourteen. `the_true_king_of_the_north_part_19.md` and `10_wotr_what_the_ground_was_owed.md`: seventeen. `the_revolution_of_the_inner_world.md`: "Robin Ice was twenty-three when the wire reached Ashwell".

- **Tabitha's age.** Tabitha's card: 42. `the_revolution_of_the_inner_world.md`, in the sixth year: "Tabitha Hallenfeld is fifty-one now".
- **Bram's years on the ridge.** Bram's card: "the three years on the ridge", and Godfrey as the grandfather who gave the shield. `the_war_in_the_north_v_the_shieldwarden.md`: the brothers held the southern approach "for nine years", with "their father telling them that the ridge did not move".

# c04 · The Curia at Altherion

The Primate and the Palatine of the Holy Sea, the Archpaladin the archive calls Darius, the First-Eon Anchor who taught him in the ash, the boy he was sent to teach, a transmuter's apprentice, and the two of Kujo's instruments who worked in and above the cells beneath the Grand Church. Seven of the nine meet in the archive's Altherion thread, and Gimbzo reaches it from the Plane of Fate. Casimir Aldous Alleyne comes to it through one night. Two cards are one man.

**Era span.** From the Fall of Kirishima Hae-jin, which his card places long before the Great Spirit War, to the archive's present: the fourteen days beneath the Grand Church, the fourteenth day and its ninth hour, and Dabney's thirteenth year. Gimbzo is First-Eon-born and seated in the Plane of Fate, which his card allows. The Ignatius card says Voyager Era while every other source here puts Darius in the present; that stays open (section 7).

## 1 Who is here

**Aurelian Prudentius Custos Clausorum · The Primate.** Born Mahuo Do-hyeon of Sum-gol, the one child of the eleven in his cohort who lived, he holds the Word of Judger at the Grand Church of Altherion and signs as Verbum Iudicis. He was nine when Sum-gol finished emptying. Nobody ordered it; the family went by attrition, requisition and corridor closure, and at nineteen he watched the Oracle in the Hall of Equal Breath return null on it, because the Oracle weighs completed acts. He has climbed the order since. He has never once converted a finding into a sentence. Since he was twenty-seven a suspension he has never described has kept him in a low fever, and he has read every physician's report on it and filed each one. He holds one of the three Sealed Standard keys and has kept the Seventh Objection sealed for eleven years. His card names Darius on the far side of the same wall, Cozbi Mahuo on the far side of the same valley, and Verinus VII, whose dawn Sacrament he anchors without having been asked.

**Verinus VII · The Palatine.** Born Osric Peyser in the parish of Coldwater Steps, where the fishermen still use the name, he is Palatine of the Holy Sea of Alabaster at the Alabaster Court. At twenty-six, a junior Auditor at the Ebb Court, he recorded a woman named Meren's testimony as the heaviest he had ever taken and watched the case fail for want of corroboration; her owner collected her on the harbour steps. He drafted the Holy Sea's position that persons are not commodities, and it passed when he was forty. His Domain, the Sounding, gives a spoken statement the load of what saying it costs the speaker. His Reading moves a witness's load onto his own chest, and forty-five years of it have left him the barotrauma of a deep diver. He distrusts Judicium and says so from the pulpit. His card records that under the Grand Church on the fourteenth day he signed for Aurelian's four and his room, asked for consent, was told no, and entered it.

**Ignatius Sanctus Sanctorum Arsenal · The Archpaladin.** His card calls him Archpaladin of Sancta Lux, of the High Ecclesia and the Order of the Emberborne Cross, human, and places him in the Voyager Era. Its Catalyst is that the Sanctified Legion of Sol Invictus was immolated in the Eclipse Wars and he did not break; four micro-fractures in his Crystal are the load paths he stands on, and a fifth risks his sight. He refuses to weigh twice. He concludes where Vaelen Mourcrix weighs, and the card asks for the two to be built as a matched pair. Every other card here and the whole archive call him Darius. `Sancta Lux.md` makes him the author of the apparatus the order runs on, written between twenty-six and thirty-five, and the holder of the Will of Judger who will not be invested in it; its account of the Eclipse Bastion has four hundred Sanctified Legionaries burn at a gate that held them for nine minutes.

**Gimbzo.** A Moto of the First-Eon generation, a contemporary of Bara and the heaviest of his earliest cadre, Gimbzo was the Anchor of the Original Moto Conclave. Bara taught him directly. He climbed by labour, carrying and holding and enduring, and never once practised revelation. He held the eastern threshold of the unfinished world for forty-one days without sleep. Dougou, the Iron Bison, brought a maul down through his sternum on the fourth dawn of their duel and left him on a salt flat, and three nights later the body was gone. The Plane of Fate had taken him whole. He became living infrastructure of Hataraki's lower lattice, and his axes Tsuiji and Tsuiei are bandaged in the burial linen he was never granted. The Plane is preparing his return, and the first thing he will look for is the bloodline that carries Dougou's residue.

**Dabney Corvalen Olyss · The Thorn-Petaled.** Dabney is thirteen, of the Palatinate of the Holy Sea of Alabaster, the ward of Edward Lambert, trained by Darius for seven years and about to enrol at the Academy. His card leaves his surname unsettled and gives him no byname. His Catalyst came before he can remember it, and Edward has asked him about it eleven different ways. At five he asked whether there was a way to be more careful, and he still believes care is something a person reaches by trying harder. He has never had a friend his own age. He keeps a grey and white cat called Astro, named off a chart on Edward's wall. The archive knows him as Dabney Charles Lambert, son of Charles Lambert and Anyu Qilak.

**Casimir Aldous Alleyne · The Ashen Hand.** An apprentice transmuter, he works under his grandfather Bertrand Aurel's private, unlicensed charter on the upper floor of an old apothecary building. He is pre-registered as a candidate with the Guild Accord's Alchemia Division, which keeps a passive observation flag on him. Bertrand is the only person allowed to call him Cas. His first contact with a Wellspring current was an inversion: grief forced Aether through an aperture too large for the soul carrying it, and the Coagulatio reflex sealed the breach and took his right eye. The Division logged it as a Wellspring proximity event and assigned no fault. His parents' deaths seeded the Mortalis in him. He keeps a sealed vial from their bedside and has never opened it. No archived scene holds him.

**Kaalabad.** His card is compiled from the Curia scenes. He is one of Kujo's generals, given discretion because Kujo wants to know what a general does in conditions Kujo did not arrange. He carries a halberd across the left shoulder. A band of scar runs above his gorget, and his helmet sits a few degrees off true. What defines him is that he continues forward: buried under a road, steel driven into his throat, caught in a man's self-detonation, he rose from the mud and kept coming. Kwon Mu-jin beat his inward pull by seizing its structure and collapsing it along its own axis. The card leaves his age, origin and voice unrecorded.

**Kirishima Hae-jin · Kaalabad, the Radiant God of Knights.** Kirishima Hae-jin is the true name; Kaalabad is the folk name tellers settled on when they could not pronounce it, and the only one most of the Inner World has heard. He was once the knight other knights measured themselves against, certain that an oath sworn could not be unsworn. Azerack found him already cracked. What cracked him the folk tale does not name, and Azerack gave him the Halberd of Zarakal. His Divine Realm spread across Sum-gol and the Ketsuen frontier, and the Tower of Zarakal rose at its centre and has never come down. When the Realm drew back it left Sum-gol's Hanseon compact, the Kept Vigil. It still draws on a debt nobody living incurred, and the incorporation treaty forbids anyone to survey it. A joint working of the Accord and Sancta Lux chained him, and the chains have outlasted every kingdom that helped forge them. His card gives his status as imprisoned.

**Corwin Sesk.** An interrogator, he gives his instruction with his name: "Corwin Sesk, my lord. Under instruction." He is tidy. He laid the strips he took from Aurelian's thighs on the flags in the order of removal. He has no appetite for the work. Aurelian found that more disturbing than cruelty would have been. He stopped the pulling of Aurelian's molars because a man who cannot form words is no use to anybody. On the twelfth day he stopped working. He will not let the record be wrong, even where it costs him. The scenes add that he is fifty, twenty-two years in the trade, and has a daughter in Foraye he has not written to in five weeks.

## 3 The shared history

### The Fall at Sum-gol and the Kept Vigil (card, with new detail)

- **When.** Kirishima Hae-jin's Fall and his Sum-gol campaign came long before the Great Spirit War; his card leaves the chaining undated. Aurelian's boyhood in the same valley ended when he was nine, in the generation before the present.

- **Where.** Sum-gol, Breath Hollow, the Mahuo province of Ketsuen, and the Tower of Zarakal at the centre of the Realm.

- **Who.** Kirishima Hae-jin (Kaalabad); Aurelian Prudentius.

- **What happened.** Kirishima took the Halberd of Zarakal from Azerack, and his Realm spread across Sum-gol from a single point of failure until the failure was the whole territory. The Tower rose at its centre. When the Realm drew back it left the Kept Vigil, a World-Tethered vestige still drawing on a debt nobody living had incurred. The Accord and Sancta Lux chained him together, at a price neither has disclosed to the other (all card). Long after, the Mahuo family seated at Sum-gol raised Mahuo Do-hyeon. The valley taught him the pause between breaths at seven, before it taught him letters, and by his ninth year the family had gone and he walked out alone (card). New here: the valley he grew up in already had one ground nobody surveyed and nobody went near, the place where the old debt was still being paid.

- **What each took.** Kirishima kept a claim on Sum-gol that persists without his attention (card). When he came up out of the quiet he asked Kujo for the Mahuo before any other house, "What is left of them" (`kaalabad_the_petition.md`), and went to Sum-gol to open the campaign himself. Aurelian carried the pause out of the valley; he is the last practitioner alive who learned the suspension from someone who learned it there (card). New: he left a valley that was already paying a debt with no living author, and the order he rose in paid part of the price of Kirishima's chains, a price it has never fully disclosed to the Accord.

### The God Hand in the lower chapel (scene)

- **When.** Ten years before the fourteenth day.

- **Where.** Altherion, the lower chapel of the Grand Church.

- **Who.** Aurelian Prudentius; Darius (Ignatius Sanctus Sanctorum Arsenal); Charles Lambert, uncarded.

- **What happened.** Four men were present and the door was barred, and the conferral took eleven minutes. Aurelian put the chain of the Scale amulet over Charles Lambert's head. Darius stood across the table with his hands behind his back, did not look at Charles once, and signed second. The office is held between two, one to carry and one to spend, and the two were walked out through different doors (`07_charles_the_imperceptible_district.md`; `14_darius`).

- **What each took.** Aurelian took ten years of wondering "which of you would go first" (`14_darius`), and at the ninth hour of the fourteenth day he felt the office go. Darius took a channel whose far end nobody told him, "a face across a table for eleven minutes and a hand I did not shake" (`09_dabney`); it spent him at the ninth hour, and that was his twelfth trial in the ash.

### Nine months in the ash at Sum-gol (scene)

- **When.** The nine months that ended on the fourteenth day. In `14_darius` Darius tells the Regent, "You put me inside Fate and you shut it".

- **Where.** Sum-gol, a plane sideways: the ash, the gate in the wood, and the trunk whose columns Kizami the Notcher cuts, in the Plane of Fate.

- **Who.** Darius; Gimbzo; Kizami, uncarded.

- **What happened.** Darius landed with four fractures and a coat with the threads picked off. Gimbzo stood up out of the gate and offered to teach him. He held no writ. Of sixty-one arrivals he had taught four. He named the seven labors with Ruin first and told Darius his order had taught itself the roof was the foundation. He offered Balance alone, because every Work that seats in a man opens a fracture and a fifth ends the Crystal. Darius set his boots in the axe print. An hour into the first lesson he reached for the arrest he had held open at the Bastion gate, the fifth fracture began to ring, and Gimbzo knocked him off the haft with the flat of Tsuiei. Then Gimbzo taught him that an arch is two collapses leaning on each other, and Darius lifted the axe a finger's width by letting its load through him into the ground. Over the months he learned all seven and the matrix under them. Gimbzo spent four of the months configuring the Fist of God. The twelfth trial came unannounced: at the ninth hour of the fourteenth day the God Hand spent Darius as Charles Lambert died. Gimbzo had felt a child open four seconds before, charged Darius to teach that child the Ways and keep him from the Regent, and opened the gate (`seven_labors.md`; `balance_lesson.md`; `04_verinus_the_hand_and_the_print.md`; `09_dabney`).

- **What each took.** Darius took the seven Works, Kami-no-Kobushi, the charge, and a fifth fracture that began and went back down without closing. Gimbzo took the first student who said the lesson back to him, "Four times I've said that sentence to a man and this is the first time one's said it back at me" (`04_verinus`), and sent him out to a child neither of them named.

### The fourteen days beneath the Grand Church (scene)

- **When.** The fourteen days of the Cloister Ward's shutdown, ending on the fourteenth day before the Curia sat.

- **Where.** Altherion, the cellar beneath the Grand Church; on day eleven, the council chamber beneath Vaeloris and the sky above Sum-gol.

- **Who.** Aurelian Prudentius; Corwin Sesk; Kaalabad; Verinus VII.

- **What happened.** Aurelian walked to Altherion on his own feet because a note listed four of his Attestants in the present tense. The arrangement was four Attestants alive and the scriptorium left a scriptorium, and for it they hung him on a frame cut from a choir stall. Molars were drawn on the second day, before Sesk arrived and stopped it. Sesk took eleven strips from his thighs, seated ten nails and broke the left kneecap on the ninth day, and Aurelian met every injury by suspending it. On day eleven Sesk reported beneath Vaeloris that the four had left through the low cloister in the second week of the shutdown, that the leverage had ceased to exist around the day Sesk was hired, and that the physical work should stop. Kaalabad stopped Sesk's breath with a look and petitioned Kujo for the Mahuo, the Valorin and the Zettari as a wage. That mid-morning he spent the Star Crusher on a village at Sum-gol, and a barrier braided from Valorin and Mahuo work declined it; the village lived and its road did not. On the twelfth day Sesk stopped working. On the fourteenth Verinus came down in the Palatine's gold and told Aurelian he had been standing in the interval since twenty-seven. He declared the surety for the four his own, asked consent, heard no, entered it, and drew the Well up through the floor, and every wound went across the room into him. Sesk watched from the wall with the roster in his coat and said nothing (`commencement_of_the_curia_kujo_arc.md`; `sesk_the_report.md`; `kaalabad_the_petition.md`; `kaalabad_star_crusher.md`; `01_verinus`).

- **What each took.** Aurelian took new skin, a jaw closed over with no molars in it, his feet on the floor, something gone from the bottom of the stack that he could no longer name, and an anchor nobody told him about; he still believed the four were alive. Verinus took the wounds in the order of removal, the knee among them, and a declaration he could not take back. Sesk took the one line in his ledger. He also took a report he asked to have entered against himself, and a silence he chose in the cell. Kaalabad took a petition nobody answered, a road north he took anyway, and a list whose gap only Sesk heard.

### The fourteenth day (scene, with one new strand)

- **When.** The fourteenth day, from the hours before dawn through the ninth hour to the tenth and after. The archive calls it "the worst day in the history of the North".

- **Where.** The root of the wound, in the Plane of Fate; Altherion, the precinct of the Grand Church; a keel north of the Sound; the Ashgate road and the Vaeloris quarter at Kharven; the ash at Sum-gol; and, new here, a house in the Inner World.

- **Who.** Verinus VII; Aurelian Prudentius; Kaalabad; Dabney; Darius; Gimbzo; Casimir Aldous Alleyne (new).

- **What happened.**

  - At the root of the wound Verinus sealed what he could of Kujo. The seal took pieces and left the man. Bonds began terminating across the Inner World, and the Song told him the cries were the material a child would stand on. He could have saved perhaps four thousand by spending the rite, and he did the sum and let thirty-five in every hundred go. He became the wall. Its draw ran down the anchor into Aurelian. The Judger's hand took the Custodia up out of the world, still standing, and set Verinus down in Utopia (`03_verinus_wall_between_the_safeguarded.md`; `04_verinus`).

  - At the fourth hour Aurelian came up into the precinct through a hole that had been a stair, and saw every fate at once. He told Charles Lambert aloud that Dabney Lambert was safe, and then, "I am no longer afraid, Charles. Of anything." Kaalabad came down a tunnel in the sky. Aurelian stopped the halberd on two fingers and flicked it ninety yards into the cathedral's west footing. Four minutes later he held twelve futures, chose the one in which he did nothing, and watched Kaalabad put a knee into Charles and throw him (`05_aurelian`; `06_aurelian`).

  - On the Ashgate road Kaalabad pinned Wren Greymane to the ditch bank with Zarakal, and Kwon Mu-jin turned his pull on its own axis and stripped him in Open Crucible (`03_wren`; `10_mujin_the_name_of_your_killer.md`; `12_mujin_open_crucible.md`). In the narrows Charles Lambert died.

  - On the keel, four-month-old Dabney took the whole release of his father's anchor, undamped. His eyes went gold and stayed gold. A ring turned inside the gold. In the ash Darius felt the subtraction, and Gimbzo sent him through the gate (`09_dabney`).

  - Over Kharven at the tenth hour Darius came through, found Aurelian young and pleasant and wrong about Verinus, and kept him on his left. Aurelian made himself the other pan and spent into Darius, and built his Pillar on Verinus's seam without knowing it. Darius took the seam into his hand and held it without receiving it. With the seventh Work he drove the Regent out through the tear and sealed it, and fell on his side in the ash of the Vaeloris quarter with his right hand closed (`14_darius` to `23_darius`).

  - New here: in the hours before dawn Casimir Aldous Alleyne's parents died in their bed, two of the thirty-five in every hundred. His grief went through an aperture too large for him, and the Coagulatio reflex sealed it and took his right eye. That was the season in which "every scale-pan hanging on every chain" would not agree with itself (`04_verinus`), and the Alchemia Division logged his incident as a Wellspring proximity event and assigned no fault.

- **What each took.** Verinus took Utopia, a wall no longer made of him, and the anchor still tied. Aurelian took a load he was not told he carries. He also took the choice he watched himself make, and a flat voice with no drop at the end of a sentence. Kaalabad took an empty hand at the precinct, the halberd back out of the cathedral footing, and the road; his card leaves him alive and advancing. Dabney took the release and the gold eyes. The Residue in the cabin would be "readable off the timbers in nine years by anybody who thought to look" (`09_dabney`). Darius took the seam in his closed hand. `WOTR_Vaeloris_Sequence.md` has his death reported at Alabaster weeks later, while Dabney's card records seven years of forms under him, and both stand. Gimbzo sent his student out. Casimir (new) took the loss of both parents, the eye, a seal he has spent every year since keeping closed, the vial from their bedside, and a flag on his file.

## 4 Ties

- **Aurelian Prudentius Custos Clausorum · The Primate and Verinus VII · The Palatine** · debt. Verinus took Aurelian's fourteen days onto himself over his refusal, and Aurelian holds Verinus's name at weight for the Custodia without having been told. Basis: card.

The Word and the Will, two of the three Sealed Standard holders, who conferred the God Hand together, fought the Void Regent in the Vaeloris quarter together, and each waited for the other to move first. ## 6 Same person?

**Kaalabad and Kirishima Hae-jin · Kaalabad, the Radiant God of Knights are one man.** The spelling ruling in the notes to `05_aurelian` ties Kujo's general to "Kirishima Hae-jin's own Volume I card title". The general fights with Zarakal, the Kirishima card's halberd. He says he "was put somewhere quiet", counts "Nine hundred years" under the seal at his throat, and asks for the Mahuo first, which fits a man chained for centuries after a campaign through Sum-gol. His body reads as "built at the Zenith" (`05_aurelian`), the Stage the Kirishima card gives. His petition in the black chamber is formal and flat and states its threats as fact, which is the Kirishima card's voice. They share one life: the Fall and Azerack's halberd; the Realm over Sum-gol and the Kept Vigil; the chains; the quiet; Kujo's leave to be seen standing; the petition beneath Vaeloris; the Star Crusher over Sum-gol; the precinct at Altherion; Wren's death and Mu-jin's crucible on the Ashgate road. The Kaalabad card is the present of that life, compiled from scenes, and the Kirishima card is its folk history and its record. Where the two cards disagree is set down in section 7, unresolved.

No other pair here is one person. The Ignatius card and the archive's Darius are already one card (`build/aliases.yaml`, per `UNCARDED.md`).

## 7 Where the cards disagree

1. **Darius's era.** Ignatius Sanctus Sanctorum Arsenal: "Era · Voyager Era." Aurelian Prudentius lists "Darius Ignatius Sanctus Sanctorum, Archpaladin, the Will of Judger" as "(living)" beside a Primate of fifty-eight; Verinus VII lists "Darius, Archpaladin of Sancta Lux" as "(living)"; Dabney Corvalen Olyss has him "Trained by Darius Ignatius Sanctus Sanctorum Arsenal for seven years" at thirteen. `Sancta Lux.md` writes an apparatus "thirty-five years old" by a man who "is still alive". WORLD.md records this conflict.

2. **Darius's name.** The Ignatius card's title is "Ignatius Sanctus Sanctorum Arsenal" and its Identity never says Darius. Aurelian's card writes "Darius Ignatius Sanctus Sanctorum"; Dabney's writes "Darius Ignatius Sanctus Sanctorum Arsenal". The notes to `04_verinus` read "Darius corrected to Darius Ignatius Sanctus Sanctorum, Archpaladin of Sancta Lux, per his Notion entries. The invented surname is struck."

3. **Which Stage is Zenith.** Gimbzo gives his Temperance Stage as "XII" and names it "Zenith, anchored permanently". Aurelian Prudentius and Kirishima Hae-jin both give Zenith as "XIV", and Verinus VII and Ignatius give Stage "XII" as "Emanation".

4. **The Wellspring's spelling.** Gimbzo: "Fixatio · Monlithion · Coagulatio". Kirishima Hae-jin: "Monolithion primary (The Enduring Monument".

5. **Where Verinus is.** Aurelian Prudentius: "Verinus VII, the Palatine (living, in Utopia)". Verinus VII: "Head of state and head of the faith, seated at the Alabaster Court", on a card whose Spirit Axes already record the dawn Sacrament. In the archive `04_verinus` sets him down in Utopia and `WOTR_Vaeloris_Sequence.md` has him working at Alabaster after Darius's death; `scenes/CONTINUITY.md` records that conflict.

6. **Kaalabad's status.** Kaalabad: "Status: alive and advancing at last sight, after injuries that should have ended him." Kirishima Hae-jin: "Status: Imprisoned, chained, Domain intact and active at range".

7. **Kaalabad's name and origin.** Kaalabad: "Name: Kaalabad. No second element given." and "Age / origin / register: not stated."

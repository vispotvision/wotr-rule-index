# c05 · Class X and what it grows into

The Aetherion Academy class of Xanelor, Rikudoku and the Mahuo children, the Division their teacher belongs to, and the Wandering Fang's line in the age after. It runs from the Academy years into the Age of Calamity. Rikudoku Moto is thirteen at the start of it, and the Wandering Fang's card gives the end its name. Canon order holds throughout: Isaac's direction, then the card, then the archived scenes, then the wiki, then this record. Where Isaac has ruled against a card, the ruling governs. The card's words go into section 7.

The Academy thread is live at the table. Nothing here runs past the morning of the duel inside the Academy years; the new events sit in the adult lives the other cards describe.

## 1 Who is here

**Xanelor Rafiminar.** The Omoro fisherfolk call him the Turtle's Ward. He was found at two in shipwreck debris on the northern shore of Omoro Island, lashed to wreckage that bore no flag, screaming in a language his finder did not speak. Three parallel cuts ran from temple to jaw on his left cheek. His card says whatever made them was no animal and no accident. The old turtle Drogo raised him on the island's leeward slope, named him, and taught him the Sage Arts, the bow and the discipline Drogo called remaining. His card gives him no standing and no registration. He came to Class X on a letter Drogo wrote to the Headmaster when the cough began, and no Accord body has ever assessed him. He wears what Drogo made for him: the slatted helm with its green silk, the grey longcoat with a green cross stitched by shaking hands, gloves dyed with the lichen off Drogo's shell, and a shell mask with no mouth. His card says every decision he makes answers a question Drogo asked him. He stands in the gap for anyone and does not ask whether the gap will kill him.

**Xanelor Rafminar · The Wandering Fang.** His card sets him in the Age of Calamity. He stands in the Spirit-Fanged Circle, a wilderness sage order the card carries as unattested, and he holds the Outer Wilds and the Frostmarch on nobody's commission. If a polity ever thanked him formally he would leave. At the turn his card records, he stopped working alone: he asked, and something larger answered, and the card leaves that thing unnamed. His six constructs are one man's reflexes wearing six bodies. They all make the same mistake at the same instant. He has lost most of the feeling in the last two fingers of his left hand to the anchors he cuts live. He names terrain and weather before he names people. His card counts Rikudoku Moto a philosophical ally, and this record reads him as Xanelor Rafiminar grown (section 6).

**Rikudoku Moto.** He is the son of Sodoku Moto and Yoko Mishiro, of the Kōkan Line, thirteen on his card. He has Muken's frame and outgrew his father before thirteen, with Moto-dark skin and flat Moto-white hair that turns to ink at the last inch. He came into Class X last, put his head down and was asleep within four seconds. Mu-jin opened his mouth, closed it and chose another battle, because Mu-jin knows whose son this is. His card leaves nearly everything else open: the Kamigan, what he carries of the Mishiro line, what he knows of the massacre and the exile, where his father is, and whether the sleep is ordinary or the price of something active. The other cards give the name a longer life. The Wandering Fang's card calls Rikudoku Moto a philosophical ally, and Isaac ruled on 2026-09-12 that this is one figure with Kairen Moto's estranged father, tied to the Great Spirit Tree, and with the Veil practitioner. This is the only card under the name.

**Kairen Moto · The Iron-Root.** He is Rikudoku Moto's son, estranged from his father and still revering him. The Moto Clan recognises him as a Lost Branch. He lost his right arm keeping whole something he valued above his own body, rebuilt himself in iron and kept working. The Iron-Graft vents steam and needs a standing charge. Run dry, it is dead scrap on his shoulder. He carries the Branch of Rikudoku, a living cutting from the Great Spirit Tree that never rots, and the Slab, a blade with no edge. The Guild Accord hires him for reclamation in poisoned ground and has never given him a charter. His uncle Senshi calls the arm a weakness, and his card keeps Senshi unattested. Under his mercies runs the Pruning, a cold conviction that some things must be cut away for the rest to live. He will not explain his own working.

**Lily Mahuo.** She is the daughter of Kwon Mu-jin and Frithia and a Class X student. Her card makes her the eldest at fourteen. Isaac's ruling of 2026-09-18 makes her the youngest, and canon order follows the ruling. Her coat is pressed, her sash is tied in the handbook's knot, and her pen keeps moving when the room is in chaos. She studies her mother's methods as she means to use them. She counts Hiromi's collapses, corrects him on the number, and drafts letters to their father in her head, which are concise. She refuses imprecision and notices systems, methods and who is graded how. Nothing she can do has been shown.

**Hiromi Mahuo.** Others call him the Paragon of Dominion. He is Mu-jin and Frithia's son, the middle child by Isaac's ruling. At six he did something in a binding circle that none of the adults present could name, and at seven he redefined summoning. He means to be the greatest summoner who ever lived and says so without qualification. His white hair came from neither parent. He does not eat and does not rest, has collapsed three times, and performs loudly so the serious work stays out of sight. He refuses micromanagement, surveillance and company in his private hours. By Isaac's ruling of 2026-09-12 he is the father of Karo Venrik by Saeloria, Queen of Eresse.

**Geturo Mahuo.** He is Mu-jin and Frithia's eldest by Isaac's ruling, though his card still calls him Hiromi's twin. He studies alchemy under his father. Once Mu-jin kept him up most of a night refining it. He checks rooms for people, lets Hiromi take the centre and walks the outside. His progress is even and comes from work. His desire is unspoken, and it is quieter than being the greatest. He knows his patience costs him and pays it anyway.

**Kaien Thal'Ruhm · The Gilded Nomad.** He was an artificer of the Guild Accord's Alchemetrica Division. A Catharsis Reactor implosion left half his body fused in gold alloy. Later he reforged that fusion on purpose, and he walked away from the Division before it could claim credit for the second. He refused the one offer of repatriation. He keeps the Bird Mask, walks Varūn's pilgrims and caravans through Aether storms without anyone's leave, and mends broken machines and bone. He will explain how a thing works in plain words and stop there. His attachments run to machines and broken things, and he rarely stays to be thanked.

**Dalphne of the Pale Bough · The Gentle Fang.** He is a nomadic beastkin sage of the Wolf Sage Arts who aids villages and guides lost travellers. He belonged to the Grey Hollow Circle. That druidic guild is long dissolved, and he practises its arts alone. He received the final thought-wave of Xanelor Rafminar, and it gave him the root of the Wolf Sage Arts. They never met, and he honours Xanelor as a distant mentor. His wolf Tsuuna once shielded him from death at the price of his own body and now exists half as spirit. The Guild Accord has offered him a position several times. He stays unaffiliated. What he shortens in others, he carries.

## 3 The shared history

### The first morning of Class X

**When.** The Academy years, when Rikudoku is thirteen (WORLD.md §2): the first morning of Class X, the morning the Class X Open Tournament was announced seven days out. From the scenes.

**Where.** The Aetherion Academy, which no page locates (WORLD.md §2): the circular Class X chamber.

**Who.** Xanelor Rafiminar, Rikudoku Moto, Lily Mahuo, Hiromi Mahuo, Geturo Mahuo.

**What happened.** Xanelor was in the chamber an hour before anyone else, in the back row by the service corridor, having mapped the building in his first week. The three Mahuo children came through the door together and each called their father Teacher. Geturo had missed his first class. Mu-jin had kept him up most of the night refining his alchemy. Frithia took the room in a sentence. Hiromi's feet came off the table. Rikudoku came in last and was asleep at his desk within four seconds. Mu-jin let him sleep. Asked by a classmate for his goal, Xanelor said through the mask, "I made a promise to someone. He is gone. The promise is not." At the introductions he stood and told the room, "I don't have any magical potential whatsoever." He named Omoro and a grandfather who had passed away, and said, "That is all." Hiromi rose last and named his goal as the greatest summoner to ever live, and Lily's pen stopped for two seconds. At the dorm assignments Hiromi refused a roommate, and Xanelor asked for a room of his own, "So I don't have to wear this mask all the time." The tournament was announced. Rikudoku lifted his head, said "If I have to," and put it down again. Hiromi crossed the room and challenged Xanelor. Xanelor advised against it and accepted. The room took him for a boy with no Crystal, and backing away from the week's first challenge would have made him the soft bracket in every head there. Geturo told his brother he could not seriously be picking a fight already. Mu-jin moved the class to the Combat Arena so it would happen inside a warded ring.

**What each took.** Xanelor took a fight he had not chosen, accepted so the room would not file him as the soft bracket. Hiromi took the fight he asked for. Rikudoku took a tournament he will enter if he has to; he did not want to hurt anyone in the room, and most of them had not said their names to him. Lily took the morning down on paper, and the scenes give her the pen and the pause and nothing more. Geturo took a brother walking into a fight he had tried to talk him out of.

### The duel in the Combat Arena

**When.** The same morning, seven days before the tournament. From the scenes.

**Where.** The Aetherion Academy, the Combat Arena.

**Who.** Xanelor Rafiminar, Hiromi Mahuo, with the rest of Class X in the gallery. The duel scenes name neither Lily, Geturo nor Rikudoku there.

**What happened.** Mu-jin stood at the rail with his hand near the signal-bell. Hiromi called up the imprint of a bullet train he had seen at nine from a station platform, and it carried him down the one straight line a borrowed train could take. Xanelor felt the push of air ahead of him and took the wave at its shoulder, as Drogo had taught him. The rigid envelope soaked the turn. His left wrist bent back past its limit and the glove split across a raw palm. Hiromi hit the ward and came up laughing, emptied of the imprint. He hung a net of constellations over the floor. Xanelor could not feel its lines, so he watched what they did to the sand. He stood on one rising hand to go up, shot three hands off one line from the air, cut the rest at their anchor points with a horn-handled knife, and threw his bow up into the dark of the dome. Then what twelve years on the island had stored in him came up through a body with no channel for it. The air of the ring pressed outward, and the ward cracked from the flaw Hiromi's shoulder had made. Xanelor crossed the ring and struck. A warden drafted from the net took the palm, and both it and Hiromi were thrown. Hiromi would not concede and closed his hands in Juggernaut's Fist, whose every contact adds weight to what it touches. Xanelor let the gauntlets land on his forearms, each blow heavier on the bones than the last, and slid back on two jets forced out through his boots. He pushed against the weight from inside his arms, and both forearms began to crack. He picked up the fallen bow. The dead weight on his right arm drew the string for him. The arrow left as a line of fire and broke the sound barrier inside the first pace. Mu-jin had already thrown a vial into its path. It turned the arrow to liquid metal a body's length short of Hiromi, and the metal burned down through sand, clay and stone a spread hand from his hip. Mu-jin climbed down into the ring. The outcome of the match is not ruled, and the contents of the vial are Isaac's to name.

**What each took.** Xanelor took hairline fractures in both forearms, a strained scaphoid, three draw fingers cut to the bone and scorched boot soles. The Omoro bow split through its upper limb. Drogo's hands had started that bow and his had finished it. He had crossed the ring and struck first, which the Way of Growing Things never does. He took a gallery that now knows the boy the intake board wrote down as having no pressure is no soft bracket. Hiromi took a split lip, a bruised shoulder and blood from both nostrils. He took an empty imprint and seven days in which every draw costs what a first call costs.

### The last thought-wave

**When.** At the end of Xanelor Rafminar's life, in the Age of Calamity his card gives him, and so after the Academy years. From the cards.

**Where.** Unplaced on both cards. The only ground either names is the Outer Wilds and the Frostmarch, which the Fang kept, and the Outer World, whose Codex of Sages enters Dalphne as an Unclaimed Guide (WORLD.md §3).

**Who.** Xanelor Rafminar · The Wandering Fang (one person with Xanelor Rafiminar), Dalphne of the Pale Bough.

**What happened.** Xanelor's final thought-wave reached Dalphne, a man he had never met, and gave him access to the root of the Wolf Sage Arts. Dalphne's card leaves two things unknown: whether the thought-wave was sent or simply escaped, and whether Tsuuna was Dalphne's before it arrived.

**What each took.** Dalphne took Xanelor's Fang, which reads on a coil as a foreign structure that has integrated cleanly, the name Last Echo of Xanelor, and a mentor he honours from a distance. What Xanelor gave or meant to give is not recorded, and this record leaves it so.

### The storm on the Varūn road

**When.** Undated, and new. It falls after Kairen lost his arm and rebuilt it in iron, while he worked Accord reclamation contracts, and after Kaien left the Alchemetrica Division and refused repatriation. Kairen's life begins a generation after the Academy years, since his father is Rikudoku.

**Where.** The pilgrim road into Varūn's workings, the Old World (WORLD.md §3, §5).

**Who.** Kaien Thal'Ruhm, Kairen Moto.

**What happened.** An Aether storm closed the pilgrim road into Varūn's workings while a caravan was on it. Kaien was walking the caravan through without anyone's leave, as he does. Kairen was on the same road coming out of a reclamation contract in the poisoned ground of the workings, and the housing of the Iron-Graft had cracked where it meets the shoulder. They sheltered with the caravan until the storm blew out. Kaien looked at the arm and asked before he touched it. He mended the cracked housing with his hands and told Kairen in plain words what the join was doing to him, and he stopped there. He took no payment. When the road opened he went on with the caravan.

**What each took.** Kairen took the first plain account of the arm from a man who builds such things, where his uncle Senshi calls it a weakness, and a debt with nobody left on the road to pay it to. Kaien left a mend behind him and did not stay to be thanked, which his card records as his habit.

## 6 Same person?

**Xanelor Rafiminar and Xanelor Rafminar · The Wandering Fang are one person.** They share one life: the boy of Omoro and Class X grows into the Wandering Fang of the Age of Calamity, and dies leaving his last thought-wave to Dalphne. The reasons:

- **Rikudoku on both sides.** The boy's classmate in Class X is Rikudoku Moto. The Fang's card names Rikudoku Moto his philosophical ally, and Isaac ruled on 2026-09-12 that every attested Rikudoku Moto is one figure. The only card under that name is the Academy boy's.
- **The name.** The boy gives it himself as the Fang's card spells it: "I am Xanelor Rafminar." (`scenes/xanelor_the_introductions.md`). The first-bell scene's notes also call him "Xanelor Rafminar". The one-letter difference is a spelling, entered in section 7.
- **The sage.** The boy trains in Drogo's Sage Arts. The man stands in a wilderness sage order, and Dalphne receives from him "the root of the Wolf Sage Arts".
- **The refusal.** Drogo taught him that "The man who saves the village and walks into the dark, he saves the village because the village needed saving." (`scenes/the_turtles_boy.md`). The man "protects ground nobody has asked him to protect and holds no commission from anyone". A polity that thanked him would lose him.
- **The way he looks at a room.** The boy "watched them the way Drogo had taught him to watch: terrain first, then movement, then people." (`scenes/xanelor_the_morning_of_the_first_bell.md`). The man "names terrain and weather before he names people."

The Lore keeps each card's own title name on its own card (LAW.md §1), and the differences between the two cards stay in section 7.

No other pair here is one person. The Academy's Instructor Kaien is not established as Kaien Thal'Ruhm.

## 7 Where the cards disagree

1. **The surname.** The Academy card and `scenes/the_turtles_boy.md` spell it Rafiminar ("Xanelor Rafiminar left Omoro on a trading vessel two days later."). The Wandering Fang's card, Dalphne's card and `scenes/xanelor_the_introductions.md` spell it Rafminar ("I am Xanelor Rafminar.").
2. **Fenriris.** Dalphne's card calls Xanelor "pactbearer of Fenriris". The Wandering Fang's card: "What answers Xanelor's pact appears in no current canon document and is carried here as unnamed and unidentified". RULINGS.md, 2026-09-12, strikes Fenriris as non-canon.
3. **The Mahuo children's ages and order.** Lily's card: "Age: 14. A year and two months older than the twins." and "Hiromi (younger brother)". Hiromi's card: "Age: 13. Twin (younger by minutes than Geturo)." Geturo's card: "Age: 14. Twin (older by minutes than Hiromi)." The cards disagree with each other, twins of two ages, and all three with Isaac's ruling (RULINGS.md, 2026-09-18): "His children's ages are fixed at Geturo 15, Hiromi 13, Lily 12; Geturo is the eldest and Lily the youngest".
4. **Rikudoku's birth.** Rikudoku's card: "Rikudoku's conception sits inside the Scourge of Hell period, when Sodoku was roughly twenty-two", and he "was around six" when Hild died. Yoko Mishiro's card: "a cabin on the Scourge's edge, with Temür, their son Riku, and a second child on the way". Against both, `scenes/the_revolution_of_the_inner_world.md` has him born at Kharven-Seat after Hild's death, in the revolution's fourth year: "a Reckoner at Kharven-Seat asked a question in a corridor in the fourth year". The same scene makes Emira "twenty-four" at the birth; Rikudoku's card makes her "Approximately 22" when he is thirteen.
5. **Rikudoku's Mishiro inheritance.** Rikudoku's card: "Every line of his only archived description is Moto and nothing in it is hers", "Eye colour unspecified", and the question "is assigned to Isaac and is not to be invented in prose." `scenes/the_revolution_of_the_inner_world.md`: "The ears came on the ninth day." and "amber eyes that were his mother's and are still." `scenes/xanelor_rikudoku_and_the_address.md` describes him with no ears.
6. **Xanelor's skin.** The Academy card: "Brown skin". `scenes/the_turtles_boy.md`: "a pale and too-thin child". The first-bell scene's notes: "Corrects earlier incorrect description."
7. **The boy's current and the man's four.** The Academy card makes his Essence Typology "Vectoria, probable primary", and the Academy State of Play lists "Xanelor's Wellspring, Vectoria, reading as confirmed" among the items Isaac has not ruled. The Wandering Fang's card lists four harmonisations, Eidolyn, Fixatio, Anima Spirare and Transference, none of them Vectoria, and warns that "a fifth would cost him the pact".
8. **Bonds.** The Academy card sets Drogo on his Axes as "Foundational, absolute, permanent." and estimates an Attraction share on his Path "(estimated from training emphasis)". The Wandering Fang's card: "He has a pact and he does not have bonds." and "A man who will not be commissioned does not form Axes".
9. **The Age of Calamity.** The Wandering Fang's card: "Era · Age of Calamity." `wiki/The Disciplines/The Sage Arts.md`: "The Age of Calamity proved the discipline's combat value." followed by "By the Voyager Era the Sage Arts have retreated from metropolitan practice." That order puts the Age of Calamity before the Voyager Era, long before the Academy years; the Harmonic Arts and Spirit Rune Arts pages order it the same way. WORLD.md §1 files the Age of Calamity under "Fit none".
10. **Rikudoku's identity on the wiki.** `wiki/Cosmology & Metaphysics/The Veil.md`: "the wiki mirror flags him as needing re-identification before anything about him hardens into fact." The Wandering Fang's card records the re-identification as ruled on 2026-09-12.
11. **The cast index.** `scenes/CAST.md` files "Kaien Thal'Ruhm (a.k.a. Kaien Rhuven, Kaien Veyren)" on the strength of the summoners' morning's Instructor Kaien. Those are three cards. The scene gives no surname.

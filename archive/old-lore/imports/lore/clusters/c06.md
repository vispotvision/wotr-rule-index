# c06 · The Reaper's office

Yukizumi Yukari, the psychopomp who is the last vote on a disputed death, and the people who keep his rooms: the four flower-named adjutants of last words (Kozakura Shion, Shiragiku Iori, Natsume Kurogiku, Lilium Verdanis), his courier and his forensic adjunct (Khasir Aluto, Moros Pellayne), the Slayer who carries the writs he cannot (Commander Severin Bale), and the Khan who opens the Slayer's corridors across the sand (Khan Kirv).

**Sources read.** The nine cards in full, under `wiki/Volume I — Character Cards/`. The neighbouring cards that name the Reaper (Deimos, Lucen Vale, Cassiel Asterion, Maelia Cantorine, Sumire Nekoha, Cyrille Lacrim, Seraphine Kharza, Marigold Vance, Vaerath Ashveil). `wiki/Factions, Bloodlines & Institutions/The Guild Accord.md`; `wiki/The Guild Accord/The Guild Accord in the Modern Era — Divisions, Companies, Academies and Offices.md`; `wiki/The Guild Accord/The Black Concord.md`; `wiki/The Archaic Bloodlines/The Yukari Bloodline.md`; `wiki/The Inner World — The Northern Shield/The Guild Seat of Stannvaard — The Metal-Wardens of the Ring.md` (Altherion); `wiki/The Inner World — The Northern Shield/The Kingdom of Nalūn — The Chain-Cities.md` (Vaultmere); `wiki/The Bearing and the Holding/The Outer World — The Southern Pan.md` (the Varakh Drylands).

**The archive is silent on all nine.** No member's name appears in any file under `scenes/`. The roster and `scenes/CAST.md` give Commander Severin Bale ten scenes; every one of those hits is the word "Commander" (Sodoku Moto as commander in *The True King of the North*, Lord Knight Commander Maud Thole in `WOTR_Vaeloris_Sequence.md` and the Verinus scenes). Severin appears in no scene. The Star Chamber, the Black Watch and the Dry Courts appear on no wiki page outside the cards. ## 1 · Who is here

**Yukizumi Yukari · The Reaper.** The Accord-appointed psychopomp and adjudicator, Arbitration Division, under a special charter, "The one who walks battlefields and councils to escort what must end and protect what still belongs to the living." When a death is disputed, by Rot, by brandcraft or by stubborn love, "Yukari is the last vote." His card calls him a current-generation Yukari psychopomp and gives him a consultancy to Research and Archives on soul-law breaches, with Mechanica named in his remit. He carries the Eclipse Glaive, whose blade "is for cutting attachments, not flesh", a night-blue mantle that dims a room to confession, and a white-lacquer locket of coins, each a signed promise from the living or the dead. His Grave Mercy postpones a rightful death by tying it to his own lifespan; "The rope shortens on him, not them." He keeps the doctrine that the dead may leave traces and "The trace is not the soul." His card lists no relationships at all.

**Kozakura Shion · The Bloomwarden.** The Reaper's seasonal adjutant (his card keeps the adjutancy and marks the "Vernal Office" unattested), a spring psychopomp and rite-keeper who "prunes" dying moments so the living can grow around them. He "Walks funeral routes with Shiragiku Iori and Natsume Kurogiku when wakes run hot." He carries the crescent naginata Hanebari, a mantle whose lining sheds living blossoms that hold a promise, and wooden kokeshi for thresholds. His cut takes a grudge off the stem and leaves the love it grew from; his card's Catalyst is the lesson that made that possible, learning "to see the sucker and the love it grows from as one stem, so the cut never lands on the wrong half." Too many mercies in a night leave frost-burn along his palms. Stone courts with no green blunt him.

**Shiragiku Iori · The Wake-Embroiderer.** Funeral-florist and grief-binder of the Guild Accord, "Retained to the Reaper for deathbed rites; often seated with the Star Chamber at hot councils." Her duty is to "Prepare last rooms for Yukizumi; catch final sentences without letting crowds bruise them." A white chrysanthemum pinned to a chest "warms when grief tells the truth, browns on a lie." Her crescent secateurs Shirogiri cut attachments before flesh, and her veil lets her carry a share of another's sorrow for an hour, at the price of fever and faintness when she carries too many. She cannot fake love or sorrow. Aliases: White Chrysanthemum, Snow-Mum.

**Natsume Kurogiku · The Knot-Shearer.** Feud-mender and oath-tailor, a Star Chamber adjunct for ceremonial services who "Walks with the Reaper when last words snag on living grudges." She sees people's ties as cords, each with its own tension. Her red shears Benikiri cut attachments before cloth, and they "won't cut true love or honest duty"; "try and the hinge locks." At deathbeds a single snip frees a clinging grudge so the last word can land clean. She assists Iori at wakes and stands beside Vireline in Measures. Too many ties borne alone numb her fingers and stitch her voice to whispers. Aliases: Black Chrysanthemum, Truce Seamstress, "Red-Scissor Nun."

**Lilium Verdanis · Lily-Quill.** Star Chamber protocol corps, "On retainer to the Reaper for last-words custody." An oath-gardener and lullaby archivist who records promises as living plants: a fragile recollection cupped in her hands sprouts as a white blossom in her ledger, and plucked later, "the minute returns with its original temperature." She carries last words for Yukizumi, witnesses Measures with Vireline and seats Lucen Vale's councils with living markers. Her lily "only holds what's genuinely there"; too many carried blooms make her faint. The title field on her card reads "The Petal Scribe"; the card's own title calls her Lily-Quill.

**Khasir Aluto · The Isocline Scribe.** Withering Era. Temperature-clerk and moment-keeper, Star Chamber adjunct for Evidence and Preservation and "the Reaper's courier for fragile minutes." He inks lines in the air that equalise temperature along their path; a page of his Polar Codex holds one dying exhale and returns the voice once; his thumb signet keeps a wound or a footprint from changing for a day. His duties are to preserve scenes for Moros Pellayne, lay safe geometry for Lucen Vale and Cassiel Asterion, and travel with Yukizumi carrying brittle last breaths. His Catalyst is "The first time he chose to lose sensation in his own hands rather than let a dying sentence go unrecorded." He refuses to press a child's breath without the guardian's assent. His cost is numb fingers and a clumsy pen; his worst case is "a rainless brass court at high noon."

**Moros Pellayne · The Ossuary Registrar.** "Arbitration Division, Forensic Cadre." The "Reaper's adjunct for disputed deaths; occasional Black Watch consultant." A necro-jurist and archive-mage who names a decedent and lets a skull-note from the margins of his chained folio enforce that person's last intent. He "Needs a name or a token of the dead." He reconstructs murders, seats Measures "when cause of death is the clause", and escorts the Reaper "through crowds that want a martyr more than a verdict." He keeps the same doctrine as Yukizumi: "Moros cites traces. He does not claim to have anyone." He will not compel a child's last words: "he'd rather lose the case than bruise a farewell."

**Commander Severin Bale · The Slayer.** Head Captain of the Black Watch, the Accord's night division ("Rot purges, rogue Mechanica interdictions, brandcraft counter-ops"), a sanctioned executioner and breach-commander who oversees every Slayer cell, Dell's among them. His first duty is to "Carry out kill-writs the Reaper ratifies but cannot personally escort"; he also trains regional Slayers and sets thresholds for when not to swing. Gallowsgate traps weapons and shears Domain seams; three Gravepins hold a corridor "like doorstops against mobs"; his buckles double as oath-seals. Once he points and declares a name, everything that protects that life grows heavy, and if he names a target and fails the same night the Weight drags on him instead. He is "Built for straight fights." His card forecasts that he "will eventually name the wrong person", which is his first hook and has not happened.

**Khan Kirv · The Veil-Khan of the Dry Courts.** Withering Era. Desert arbiter, caravan-lord and sanctuary broker of "The Dry Courts (neutral league of oasis-cities)", treaty partner to the Guild Accord and "occasional Black Watch liaison for sand-route warrants." He brokers passage between feuding oases, turns raid-nights into markets and hosts heat courts at noon. Water taken under his goatskin cup, the Khurrah, "binds a one-night hospitality that even raiders respect"; blood spilled under it darkens his sash and drops every ring he has drawn until amends are paid, and the card says the amends are his whether or not the blood was his doing. His Catalyst: "The first hospitality he saw broken, he paid the amends himself rather than name the guilty party." His power leans on consent. Rain blurs his mirage sense, and then he leans on Khasir. His card reads him from outside only.

## 3 · The shared history

Three events. None is in the archive; each is new and fitted to every card it touches. Where a card states a refusal, a cost or a hook, the event uses the refusal and the cost and leaves the hook unplayed.

### The Salt-Pan Writ

- **When.** The Withering Era, after Khan Kirv's Catalyst Event (his cup already binds a night's hospitality and his rings already hold); before any card's present.
- **Where.** The White Pans of the Varakh Drylands, in the Outer World (WORLD §5, South; the White Pans are among the Drylands' features on the Southern Pan page, which gives the Drylands rain that comes inconsistently). No page places the Dry Courts, and this event does not: it happened where the writ's road ran.
- **Who.** Commander Severin Bale, Khan Kirv, Khasir Aluto; Yukizumi Yukari as the ratifier, who was not there.
- **What happened.** A Black Watch kill-writ against a practitioner of predatory brandcraft, ratified by the Reaper, ran south along a sand route. The practitioner reached an oasis at the edge of the White Pans and took water under Kirv's Khurrah as a guest, and for that night the hospitality covered the guest. Blood spilled under it would have darkened the Khan's sash and dropped every ring he held. Kirv, as the Black Watch's liaison for sand-route warrants, kept the night whole. At dawn, when the hospitality ended, he opened a lawful corridor from the oasis out across the salt. Severin drove his Gravepins at the corridor's mouth and spoke the name there, outside the ring. Rain came across the pans that morning and blurred the Khan's mirage sense. Khasir set isoclines along the corridor and put the marks and the tracks the writ rested on under his signet, so the evidence stayed dry and unchanged until the Black Watch entered it. The writ ended on the salt and closed clean.
- **What each took.**
  - Severin: a straight road through ground made for skirmish, and a practice on sand-route writs: he names at the mouth of a corridor the Khan has opened, never under the cup.
  - Kirv: a sash that stayed clean, and the corridor as a form he has opened for the Black Watch since.
  - Khasir: numb fingers carried home from the rain, and the Khan's reliance, which both their cards now record.
  - Yukizumi: a writ he ratified and could not have carried, closed with no blood under anyone's hospitality.

### The Deathbed on the Stone Floor

- **When.** The Withering Era; the deathbed at which Kozakura Shion's Catalyst Event came.
- **Where.** Altherion, the republic on the Ring's rim (WORLD §3 and §5; the Stannvaard page gives it timber-framed houses over stone), on the stone ground floor of a timber house.
- **Who.** Yukizumi Yukari, Shiragiku Iori, Natsume Kurogiku, Kozakura Shion.
- **What happened.** A dying man's last word snagged on an old grudge his daughter held against him, and the room ran hot. The Reaper brought his adjutants to the bed. The floor was stone and nothing green grew in the room, so Shion's reach was blunted; he grounded his naginata at the threshold and watched. Iori laid her ring of blooms and pinned a chrysanthemum on the daughter. Natsume went for the grudge with Benikiri to free the last word, and the hinge locked. The shears do not cut true love. She put them away and hemmed the room with chalk. The bloom on the daughter's chest warmed. The father's last word was spoken into the grudge, and it landed. Yukizumi lowered the star.
- **What each took.**
  - Shion: the sight his card records, the sucker and the love as one stem. Since that bed his cut goes only where the love is absent.
  - Natsume: a deathbed finished without a cut, and a Bloomwarden who has walked her funeral routes since.
  - Iori: the daughter's share, carried under the Snow-Mask for the hour, and the fever after.
  - Yukizumi: a coin in the Obol Case, bearing the promise the daughter made at the bed.

### The Measure at Vaultmere

- **When.** The Withering Era, after Khasir Aluto's Catalyst Event.
- **Where.** Vaultmere, the counting city of Nalūn (WORLD §5; the Nalūn page: sealed chambers of record, "climate-regulated by the salt itself").
- **Who.** Moros Pellayne, Khasir Aluto.
- **What happened.** A child lay dying in Vaultmere, and the clause of an instrument turned on how the child had come to be hurt. The instrument had not been sealed under Nalūn authority, so the dispute came to the Accord, and Moros seated the Measure at the bedside, as he does when cause of death is the clause. The child's own words would have decided it. Moros could have compelled them and did not. Khasir held the Polar Codex open at a clean page to take the child's last breath, and the guardian withheld assent. He closed the book. The child spoke to the guardian, and nobody wrote it down. Khasir's seal kept the wound unchanged for the day, and the wound could not answer the clause. The Measure was lost.
- **What each took.**
  - Moros: the lost case, and the refusal his card states; the child's page in his folio stayed blank, because he did not ask.
  - Khasir: a clean page kept clean in a Codex whose clean pages are finite, and the refusal his card states. He has preserved scenes for Moros since.

## 5 · Outward

- **Yukizumi Yukari:** a made thing his charter reaches. His remit names Mechanica, and his card names Wilhelm, "whose artificial Verdiction Core the Guild Accord itself commissioned": the Accord officer chartered to sever what the Accord built.
- **Kozakura Shion:** a lane-keeper to carry his petals through crowds. Sumire Nekoha, the Stray-Lantern: "Her lanes carry his petals farther than streets deserve."
- **Shiragiku Iori:** the man whose weight she softens. Deimos, the Sin Eater, who absorbs and stores and cannot finish a grief.
- **Natsume Kurogiku:** the fevered room she lets out. Kaito Akagiri, the Scarlet Plague; her card makes her one of the few who can "let out" his heated rooms without tearing them.
- **Lilium Verdanis:** the protocol officer whose councils she seats. Lord Lucen Vale, the Star Chamberlain: "His stars map the room; her petals mark who should speak."
- **Khasir Aluto:** someone to cool the air before he can ink it. Kaede Suyori, Blue Moth, one of "The two he needs first when the air is too hot or the beat too loud."
- **Moros Pellayne:** the writer of the Measures he seats. Vireline of the Three Masks: "His margins make their court geometry obey itself."
- **Commander Severin Bale:** the escort who supplies the condition his build needs. Marigold Vance, the Gilded Bailiff, whose card says of him "When a writ must be served, she makes the aisle that keeps it clean."
- **Khan Kirv:** the rescuer whose water rides his lattice. Cyrille Lacrim, the Flood-Bell: "together they pull cities out of stampedes."

## 6 · Same person?

No. The nine cards describe nine offices and name one another as separate people in the same rooms: Iori and Natsume as "Twin craft", Khasir and Moros as "Two clerks of the same rooms", Severin and Yukizumi as opposite sheets. The other reapers of the index are separate people outside this cluster: Vaerath Ashveil · The Pale Reaping (Yukizumi called her in), Malphas ("The Bound Reaper") and Hieronymus Cruciferi ("Relentless Battlefield Reaper"). Dell holds Severin's dread title, and Severin trains Dell.

## 7 · Where the cards disagree

Quoted and left open.

1. **The Slayer's pairing is on one card.** Severin's card: "Neither sheet works without the other, and both cards say so from opposite directions." (`Commander Severin Bale · The Slayer.md`, XI). Yukizumi's card has no Relationships section and names neither Severin, the Slayer, the Black Watch nor kill-writs (`Yukizumi Yukari · The Reaper.md`).
2. **Who lists Deimos.** Shion's card: "Yukizumi's sheet: Arbitration Division, Stage X, Mortalis and Anamnesis. It lists Deimos and does not list Shion." (`Kozakura Shion · The Bloomwarden.md`, IX). Yukizumi's card lists no one. The Deimos link is on Deimos's card: "Deploys Deimos as surgical consequence" (`Deimos · The Sin Eater.md`).
3. **What Benediction is.** Kirv: "Benediction · Vitalia. Governing." and "The Wellspring of Grace" (`Khan Kirv · The Veil-Khan of the Dry Courts.md`, III). Severin: "Benediction is a resonance" (`Commander Severin Bale · The Slayer.md`, epigraph note). Iori: "Benediction is a resonance, not a Stage." (`Shiragiku Iori · The Wake-Embroiderer.md`, epigraph note).
4. **Iori's dual reading.** Shion's card: "Two of the Reaper's other specialists, Iori and Natsume, were read at Stage VIII on the strength of the same dual reading, a bond that is a bond and is also a physical thing with a tension." (Shion, I). Iori's card gives her own reading as "a flower is a flower and is also a container, and both are operative." (Iori, epigraph note). The bond-with-tension reading is Natsume's (Natsume, epigraph note).
5. **Two pools at one Stage and one Level.** Shion: "Level 228 · Level Band III" and "Pool 8,540 — 4,500 from Bands I–II, 840 from Band III, 3,200 from Thresholds I–VIII." (Shion, IV). Khasir: "Level 228 · Level Band III" and "Pool 6,804, per Fracture of Worlds — The Living System, Part Three: 1,200 from Band I, 1,500 from Band II, 504 from 28 levels of Band III, 3,600 from Thresholds I–VIII."

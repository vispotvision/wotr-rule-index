# c07 · The Star Chamber's Protocol Corps

The Accord's people who arrange councils and Measures: the Star Chamberlain and his notary (Lord Lucen Vale, Cassiel Asterion), the bailiff who makes their order stick (Marigold Vance), the fool she trades with (Pierre), the fixer the corps defines itself against and the hush-healer who loves him (Kaito Akagiri, Kaede Suyori), and the couriers and the lamplighter who carry for the Accord (Eira Vale, Ascella Morwyn, Arven Solthiel).

Under the Northern Shield folder: the Nalūn page (Ironlink Hold), the Stannvaard page (Altherion, the Rimlands), the Ketsuen page (the Thornback March), the Kharven page (the highland plateau). `wiki/Cosmology & Metaphysics/The Veil.md`; the artifact pages that use "R.A.D." and "The Blooming Silence"; `scenes/CAST.md`.

## 1 · Who is here

**Lord Lucen Vale · The Star Chamberlain.** "Human, Accord-born Harmonian", of the "Guild Accord · Star Chamber, Protocol Corps", called White North of the Accord Halls and He Who Aligns the Room. He arranges councils through placement, tone and cadence: he moves a chair two fingers' width or turns a glass a quarter turn, and the hostility in the room finds no footing. His Dawn Coin, a palm-warm token, forces honest, unembroidered speech from the one who turns it. His Auric Hand Glove binds small promises; his mantle carries movable star-seals that mark thresholds and voice lanes; his astrolabe cane Ordinal sets the order of speaking. His card forecloses violence. He needs rooms that acknowledge decorum; riot fields and feral Domains resist him; heat and humidity weaken his Snow-Glass. Overexerted, he falls into a loop of perfect etiquette that someone else has to break. His card credits him with stabilising "the Second Fracture Vote by geometric realignment" and preventing "three diplomatic wars through cadence-law alone", and at its present he is preparing Star Chamber geometry for Outer World delegates. The Coin's backlash is temporary loss of voice, and his card's foreshadowing turns on his losing his voice at full council.

**Cassiel Asterion · The Prism Notary.** "Light-law duelist and oath-engraver" of the Star Chamber's protocol corps, "Seconded to Vireline for Measures and to Lucen Vale for high councils." Moon-violet hair, a snow-and-ink coat latticed with gold facets, star brooches at throat and cuff, the estoc Stellagraph carried like a conductor's wand, and a scribe's stylus. He sets angles in a room so truth lands and blades behave. His blade lays a hair-bright meridian that objects prefer not to cross. His Facet Seal is a star gem that turns once to take a promise and, turned back, releases proof of how it was meant; it binds only a promise spoken without embroidery. Every technique on his sheet redirects, trims or reseats, and none strikes. Smoke, dust and drum-Domains scatter his lines, and he needs Kaede or Ascella to steady the air. Pure noon or pitch-black dulls his prisms. He seats volatile councils, referees Measures where the clause is "angle, not blood", and escorts envoys through siege halls.

**Kaede Suyori · Blue Moth.** "Court lepidopterist, hush-healer, and social countermeasure", and "the only person who can cool a fevered room after Kaito warms it." Their card uses they. Night-indigo robes brushed with pale blossoms, a ribboned moth at one ear, inked freckles like stray pollen along the wrists; paper-thin moths stray from the sleeves and circle back. "Where Kaede stands, heat leaves the air and panic forgets its reasons." Their hands speak first: two fingers suture an argument shut, and a sleeve's edge draws a line doors obey. A Moth Court of dusk-coloured eidolons carries whispers and marks safe paths with dust. The Chrysalis Vow takes another's rising pain or fury and holds it until it cools. The sheet says "Kaede has never hurt anyone and could not." They love Kaito Akagiri. The card names no affiliation and no other person.

**Kaito Akagiri · The Scarlet Plague.** "Court fixer, duelist, and social pathogen." He was Arbitration's liaison and "burned his writ"; now he is "a deniable Black Concord contractor who sells outcomes to the highest bidder." White-and-black robes kissed with wine-red blooms, and a lacquered staff-sheathed blade slung across the shoulders that serves as walking stick, lever and gaff before it shows an edge. Red cords hang from the sheath, each holding "a memory bead taken from someone who thought they were using him." He reads every room twice. His Room Fever enters softly, matches three people's breathing, leads half a step faster, and the decision he wanted breaks out across the room. "He never raises his voice. He collects yours." His cuts are small: a nick at the collarbone, the flat of a blade across a wrist. He keeps debts small and numerous, rarely threatens, and predicts. His card names one person, Kaede.

**Marigold Vance · The Gilded Bailiff.** "Court-enforcer and riot-converter" of the "Star Chamber, Protocol Corps", "Bailiff-at-Large to Vireline's Measures; trusted escort for the Reaper in hot halls." A crownlet like a toy halo, black-and-gold party steel over a velvet frock, the clockwork war-gavel Ordinance in one hand and the Sunburst Chain in the other, and a Tattle Tiara that "Tilts a degree toward the loudest lie in the room." A heel-toe stamp makes a court in which blows turn into objections. "Any promise made within arm's reach prints a tiny gilt chit into her belt-purse", and the purse rattles with coins owed if the promise breaks. Her sheet makes her the one member of the corps who can actually hit somebody. She hits to disarm. The blade clatters and the knuckles do not split. Mud and broken ground blur her circle, and she prefers boards, flags or clean stone. Rot-noise makes the tiara wobble.

**Pierre · The Laughing Guillotine.** Harlequin of the Red Sleeve, "Court Fool / Interrogant", freelance, "Panic-breaker when hired, riot-starter when bored." "The guilds hire him to detonate hypocrisy; the Black Concord rents him to salt negotiations." He "Keeps an Arbitration pin he "borrowed" from Vireline and returns on holidays." Striped motley cut like funeral finery, a cape black as stage-curtain, a scarlet-spattered ruff hung with twelve dull bells that each ring at a different lie, one boot red to the knee, and "a thin smile of a scar" across his neck. He can uncouple head from body for a short scene. Laughter he causes knocks a truth loose, and someone blurts what they meant to keep. With a bloody glove he can stain an oath so that its breaker feels shame. He "Keeps meticulous kindnesses no one sees and monstrous jokes everyone does." Sincere silence unmans him, and a person who refuses the laugh three times is safe from his tax. His sheet says he has never executed anybody.

**Eira Vale · The Echo Runner.** "Concord courier of memory-chains across damped terrains", of the Logistics and Supply Division, with an "ad-hoc secondment to Black Concord operations" that is "unacknowledged." Pale-haired, in a russet field-coat and a red song-pattern hood, with Pilgrim, a white gander bonded to her by Featherbind oath, cradled like a lamp. She is considered, observant and gently humorous, and "Mistrusts spectacle." She folds treaty-clauses or echo-keys into quill-sized packets for the gander to carry, slides words through damped air without ripple, bleeds a false heartbeat to draw listeners off the true packet, and buries feathers at milestones to keep the terrain listening kindly. Research and Archives relies on her for unfrayed transfer of delicate codices, and Sealwright teams on the Plateau's rim call her their preferred runner. She cannot fight. Her sheet names somebody else's panic as the thing most likely to break her.

**Ascella Morwyn · Wick.** "Night-route guide and echo-archivist" of the "Research & Archives Division, with field commission to the Logistics & Supply Division for Plateau operations." She wears a pointed hood whose tails taper into a scarf and a patch-mended cloak lined with tapestry. Her belt carries thread spools, vials and waxed chalk. She "Moves like a lantern's sway." Her Lamplighter's Crook bears the Lantern of Still Radiance, whose light lays a hush-field that steadies the draw inside its circle. The lantern is load-bearing, and taking it from her destabilises her. Her Stillflame "Projects light that does not echo." She reads woven cloth for its makers' choices and weighs a spoken claim in shadow. Three of her Moth Court have names, Hush, Brindle and Little King, and they roost in her hood's lining. The Sealwright teams request her by name because "she can put one down on command, in the dark, without anybody panicking." Her gentlest working, Ashen Hour, dims an enemy Domain until it unhooks from the ground, painlessly.

**Arven Solthiel · The Smiling Veil.** A young man, "Human, Veil-Touched hybrid", his lineage unattested. "Intermediary between lucid realms. Unregistered courier for the Guild Accord. Lullaby-glyph practitioner who treats nightmares as lessons." He was an apprentice of the Somnalis Seminary at Eresse before it fell into the Wellspring Silence, and his card marks both unattested. His ginger hair is worn low over the eyes. His robes are layered black and indigo with gold trim and violet threading, and the ribbons at his sleeves carry sleep glyphs, each murmuring a lullaby. He lulls spirits into calm conversation and folds himself partly into the dream-veil. He "remembers every dream he has entered." His Lament of the Quiet Moon suspends motion and sound in a room and leaves reflection. "Each veilwalk erodes the boundary between dream and self." Too long in another's sleep, he speaks in other people's voices for days after. "Some whisper that the Black Concord uses him to erase memories painlessly. Others claim he simply collects them for his own archives." His card affirms neither.

## 3 · The shared history

Four events. None is in the archive; each is new and fitted to every card it touches. Hooks stay unplayed, and the Black Concord rumour on Arven's card stays open.

### The Fever at the Round Table

- **When.** Before Kaito burned his writ, while he was still Arbitration's liaison. This is the council Kaede's card gives as the beginning of the two of them.
- **Where.** Altherion, the republic on the Ring's rim (WORLD §3 and §5), in the River Crown Council's chamber. The Stannvaard page gives the chamber its founding table: round, "without a head, one chair per delegation and no chair larger than another", and a speaking floor that "belongs to whoever holds it."
- **Who.** Kaito Akagiri, Kaede Suyori, Lord Lucen Vale, Cassiel Asterion.
- **What happened.** Arbitration convened a council in the lent chamber between two delegations quarrelling over escort fees on the corridor road. Lucen arranged it. The table gave him no head to set and no seat to raise, and he did not impose an order of speaking on a room built to refuse one. He moved chairs two fingers' width and turned the water glasses. Kaede sat at his side as co-architect, with moths in the sleeves. Cassiel stood at the meridian. Kaito came as Arbitration's liaison, charged with bringing both delegations to yes. He spoke softly from the diaphragm and matched the breathing of three delegates. Then he led them, half a step faster. No voice in the chamber rose. Someone blurted, someone else agreed, and the room reached for a larger yes than either delegation had come to give. Cassiel laid his line between the two delegations, and the yes shrank to the terms both would sign. The heat stayed in the room. Kaede cooled it. When the chamber emptied, Kaede walked out beside Kaito.
- **What each took.**
  - Kaito: a yes smaller than the one he had built, two rivals across every table he has sat at since, and Kaede.
  - Kaede: Kaito, and the work their card gives them, cooling the rooms he warms. They showed him afterward how to leave a pulse in a throat.
  - Lucen: the rival his card names, heat to his cold. Since that council he has seated Kaede in the rooms where Kaito is expected.
  - Cassiel: the cold rivalry his card records, and the line he has drawn against Kaito's yes since.

### The Unsealed Promise

- **When.** Before any card's present; unordered against the council at Altherion. Lucen's Dawn Coin and Cassiel's Facet Seal already worked as a pair.
- **Where.** Ironlink Hold, the capital of Nalūn (WORLD §5), in a chamber off the Great Chain Hall under lanterns on walls of amber salt; the Nalūn page gives its air "lamp oil, dried ink". The same page: "The Accord handles disputes between parties who did not seal under Nalūn authority."
- **Who.** Lord Lucen Vale, Cassiel Asterion, Marigold Vance, Pierre, Ascella Morwyn. Vireline, who has no card, wrote the Measure.
- **What happened.** A partner of a factor house had promised a carrying company, on the road and never in salt, to carry its winter grain at the old rate. Unsealed, the promise was the Accord's to hear. Vireline wrote the Measure and Lucen arranged it. Cassiel refereed. Marigold kept the floor as bailiff and stood at the partner's shoulder. Pierre came hired by the carrying company's guild, with Vireline's borrowed pin on his collar. The partner would not turn the Dawn Coin, and he answered all morning with embroidery. Smoke from the hall's lamps frayed Cassiel's line. Ascella set her lantern on the flags, and inside its circle the air steadied. Pierre set up a harmless joke, and the partner laughed. On the laugh he said it plainly: he would carry the grain at the old rate, as he had said on the road. Cassiel turned the Facet Seal once, and it took. The words had been spoken within Marigold's reach, and a gilt chit printed into her belt-purse. The Measure found for the company.
- **What each took.**
  - Pierre and Marigold: the trade their cards name. His punchline loosened the knot, and her receipt tied it. The partner carried the grain, and the chit has never rattled.
  - Cassiel: a seal on a promise made without embroidery by a man who had embroidered all morning; turned back, it would give proof of how it was meant.
  - Lucen: the Dawn Coin, back in his pocket unturned, and a Measure he arranged closed without it.
  - Ascella: a place in the Star Chamber's rooms when smoke or dust is in the air. Cassiel has sent for her since.

### The Thornback Crossing

- **When.** Before the Plateau operations the two cards name in their chapter appearances. This is the first road Ascella lit for Eira.
- **Where.** The Thornback March, Ketsuen's northern frontier (WORLD §5). The Ketsuen page: where the perimeter meets the wilderness, and its wildlife, long steeped in dense Aether, "has opinions about territorial boundaries."
- **Who.** Eira Vale, Ascella Morwyn, and the gander Pilgrim.
- **What happened.** A Sealwright team working past the ward-perimeter needed its field record brought out whole to the Research and Archives Division. The record was a memory-chain and would fray if it was handled roughly. Eira folded it into a quill-sized packet, and Pilgrim carried it. The only road out crossed the March at night. Ascella went ahead with the crook and lantern, and Stillflame lit the road without echo. Eira cast thin and let the ground lead. The gander's throat-tone held the heading. Once, something large moved across the road ahead of them. Ascella set the lantern down, and the hush-field held. Nothing on the March turned toward them. They crossed. The packet reached the archivists unfrayed.
- **What each took.**
  - Eira: the lamp both cards record, Ascella lighting what she must cross unseen, and a road the archivists have sent her down again.
  - Ascella: the runner she has lit since, and the thing the Sealwright teams ask for her by name to do, a hush-field put down in the dark on command.

### The Keeper's Account

- **When.** While Arven carried for the Accord off the register, before his card's present; unordered against the other three.
- **Where.** A way-station on the Rimlands road below Altherion (WORLD §5; the Stannvaard page gives the Rimlands as roads, way-stations, timber and the farms that feed the crater cities).
- **Who.** Arven Solthiel, Cassiel Asterion, Marigold Vance.
- **What happened.** A Measure over a load of timber that left the way-station and never reached Altherion turned on what the station's night-keeper had seen. She lay in a sleep nobody at the station could break. The Measure sat in the station's hall, with Cassiel refereeing and Marigold keeping the floor. The Accord sent for the courier it does not register. Arven sat a night at the keeper's bed and lulled her into conversation inside her sleep. At dawn he came into the hall and gave her account in her voice. Nothing in it was a promise, and Cassiel turned no seal. Marigold stood at Arven's shoulder, and the tiara did not tilt. The Measure closed on the account. For days afterward Arven spoke in the keeper's voice. The carters in the yard heard it come out of a stranger, and Marigold walked him through them to the road.
- **What each took.**
  - Arven: the keeper's voice in his mouth for days, and no entry on any roll. The Measure's record names a courier and gives no name.
  - Cassiel: a Measure closed on words his seal never touched, carried by a man the Accord keeps off its register.
  - Marigold: a witness walked through a hostile yard, and no chit, because nothing was promised within her reach.

## 6 · Same person?

No two cards in this cluster are one person.

- `scenes/CAST.md` heads a list "Lord Maerion Lysandre (a.k.a. Lord Lucen Vale)". Lord Maerion Lysandre is a separate card, "Concord High Magistrate" of the "Arbitration Division, Concord High Court", with another title, another office and another sheet; Lucen is Star Chamber, Protocol Corps. Neither card names the other, and "Lucen" occurs in no scene. The alias is the index's, and nothing on either card supports it.
- Lucen Vale and Eira Vale share a surname. Neither card makes them kin, and this record does not.
- Arven is an "Echo Courier" and Eira an "Echo Runner"; Arven's card calls these distinct posts and asserts no link.

## 7 · Where the cards disagree

- Cassiel's card: "A hard mechanical counter, and both cards call it a rivalry without saying why." (Cassiel, XI). Kaito's card lists one relationship, "Kaede Suyori · Lover." (Kaito, XI), and names neither Cassiel nor any rivalry. Lucen's "volatile rival" and Pierre's "Professional" are likewise unanswered on Kaito's card.
- Lucen's card: "Kaede of Glass Courts · Co-architect of moderated discourse." (Lucen, XI). Kaede's card has no Relationships section, gives no "Glass Courts", and names no one but Kaito.
- Kaede's pronoun. Kaede's card: "They walked beside him"; Kaito's card: "He heats; they cool." Daigo Tenryū's card: "She cools his over-eager pushes" (`Daigo Tenryū · Ribbon-Ox.md`, relationships). Eryndrel Vaelune's card: "Her Blue Veil keeps his long holds clean." (`Eryndrel Vaelune · The Trinebow.md`, relationships).
- R.A.D. Eira's card: "R.A.D. archivists · Rely on her for "unfrayed" transfer of delicate codices." (Eira, X), and Elder Seravain's card reads it as the Division: "Ascella Morwyn is R.A.D. with a Logistics commission." The artifact pages read it as one person: "the archivist the card records only as R.A.D." (`wiki/Artifacts/Horizon's Reach · Limen-Hand.md`; `wiki/Artifacts/The Ocular Codex of Thirteen Mouths · Reader-That-Reads-Back.md`).
- Eira's card: "Bonded under Featherbind oath at a Lurien shrine." (Eira, X), against its own header: "Lurien, where the Featherbind oath was sworn, is dead canon and wants re-attributing to the Holy Sea of Alabaster." The re-attribution is the card's suggestion and is not ruled; no Lore names the shrine's place.

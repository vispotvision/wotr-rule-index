# c12 · The Research and Archives Division

The Accord's theorists, necrosophists, archivists and society alchemists, and the ones it put out: Azran Nemeir, the Division's junior who writes the report first; Cael Dareth, its expelled theoretician; Dr. Vaelion Rhest, its sleepless theorist of Wellspring behaviour; Lyssara Veyn, its physician to the failed Threshold; Serai Linthra of the Alchemists' Society it charters; Valthren Odrin, Heretic-Warden of its Lantern Ward; Gonju, its Rot-field greenhand lent to Arbitration; and Silas Briarend Wytherm III, the Accord's gravebinder of the Hollow Book.

**Era.** The Withering Era, with no year number. Dr. Vaelion Rhest's and Lyssara Veyn's cards place them in it; the other six state no era, and this record sets their shared events in the same era, inside the Division that serves them all. Azran's card gives the one firm span: twenty-six years old, in the Division since seventeen.

## 1 · Who is here

**Azran Nemeir · The Gilded Draft** (azran-nemeir-the-gilded-draft). A mortal human "of a minor Wellspring-touched maternal line (his mother guild-blooded)", a Registered Adept of the Research and Archives Division, twenty-six on the card. He joined at seventeen and "drove through the lower commission tiers on determination and precise casting rather than raw output." His goal is correction: one stabilised Wellspring, one sealed corruption vein, one mended community at a time. His Catalyst came on a commission after his party leader went down, when he held a stabilization circle alone for two hours: "He wrote the incident report first. He did not celebrate." The Farath Vein Incident, a dual Phreatis and Luminalis channel held alone for ninety-seven minutes that kept three settlements in water, is the act his elevation to Adept formally recognised. His signature, Sigil Stitch, is a seven-point glyph traced by hand over four uninterrupted seconds, and a fighter who reaches him before it sets ends it. His slim journal, the Codex Filament, carries every corruption pattern he has cleansed, inscribed in his own Parun Script. Long Phreatis work leaves him silent for hours: "People who know him leave him alone with tea and a window. People who don't mistake it for coldness." When the Archives Division asked him to document his adapted Sigil Stitch from the Dust Vault recovery, "He submitted eleven pages." His card says nobody answers to him yet.

**Cael Dareth · The Pale Crucible** (cael-dareth-the-pale-crucible). Human, silver-haired, amber eyes behind circular lenses etched with transmutation runes, "hands burned pale by mercury". Formerly a theoretician of the Research and Archives Division, made one at the Alchemarch Citadel of the Iron Forges in Varūn (his card marks the citadel unattested). He was "Expelled after initiating the Causality Loop Experiment", "an attempt to rewrite failure as success by reversing temporal Essence signatures", and is now an "Unaffiliated alchemist studying the morality of alchemy itself. A genius to some, a walking heresy to others." The Loop left a paradox in his Soul Crystal, an internal Wellspring humming between existence and erasure; the Fixative Seal holds it, and "the first one he locked was his own." His lenses read composition: "he sees composition and never colour." He carries the Verdiction Crucible, which distils soul fragments into reagents, and every use moves silver further up his nerves toward the card's end for him, "a statue of flawless alchemical law, unable to decay or think." He writes his formulas every night to keep the mind he is losing. At a paradox he falls into fervent recitation, which he calls "Alchemical prayer". His archonic patron is Urion, the Order Archon.

**Dr. Vaelion Rhest · The Sleepless Theorist** (dr-vaelion-rhest-the-sleepless-theorist). Aetheric Behaviorist and Senior Researcher in Theoretical Essence Physics of the Division, currently stationed at the Neutral Chambers' Eastern Node as consultant for Wellspring behavioural irregularities. Withering Era; born in Vellsorea in the New World (ruled 2026-09-12). Long black hair tied loosely and never tidy, robes of dark green and ivory stained with ink, a string of jade charms at the neck, eyes faintly silver. His theories, "once ridiculed, became the foundation of Animatria Resonance Studies and the mathematical proof that Essence can think", and they circulate under restriction. He fractured his own self on purpose by Recursive Cognition and resolved it; the fragments, the Versions of Sleep, are cured in by Fixatio so they do not reabsorb. He treats Wellsprings as living organisms, and a Wellspring he is stationed at "stabilises its growth as if responding to his calm." The load he spreads comes back as sleeplessness: "Sleep rarely dares to find him." "He despises bureaucracy and avoids fieldwork, and is still the first to arrive when a Wellspring collapses." The Archmages of Eresse defer to him in metaphysical calculus; his students "Claim his sarcasm hides a quiet kindness." He writes equations in suspended light with the Lattice Stylus, keeps his own in ink on the inside of his cloak, and never puts down a self-heating mug: "The tea is lukewarm anyway." Nobody knows where he goes for the weeks he disappears, or what gives him the scars.

**Lyssara Veyn · The Black Chrysalis** (lyssara-veyn-the-black-chrysalis). Dr. Lyssara Veyn, Senior Necrosophist of the Mortalis Branch, "Physician to the failed Threshold." Withering Era. Human, born in Eresse: "Eressean mourning dress is a fact about where she grew up and nothing else." She is resident at the Eressean Mortalis Chambers (unattested on her card) under a restricted clearance for post-Temperance corpus studies. "The Accord tolerates the eccentricity because the findings saved hundreds" from the collapse that follows a failed Threshold, which her Branch files as Soul Dissolution Syndrome. Her Catalyst: "She immersed her consciousness in the Mortalis resonance field for seven days. Her heart stopped on the fifth." She woke no longer distinguishing the pulse of life from the rhythm of death. Her Chrysalis-Phase holds a dying subject's final moment open so that the soul's lattice can be read as it unravels: "Not resurrection. Extended dying." Every hold cools her toward the boundary without her noticing. She keeps the Sepulchral Tome, which records final words and heartbeats, the Lacrima Crucible, a silver phial that condenses tears, and the Black Butterflies, who whisper to her and "sometimes return carrying whispers from those thought long gone." She addresses cadavers as old friends. The Division files her Chrysalis Experiment as unverified. "Death is not a thief. It's an archivist."

**Serai Linthra · The Serpent Alchemist** (serai-linthra-the-serpent-alchemist). Senior Alchemist and Aetheric Synthesist of the Alchemists' Society, chartered under the Division; a Master and Warden of Duty. She wears layered kimono-weaves interlaced with metallized silk. Her right eye, hidden under her fringe, houses a rotating alchemical seal that reads the composition of matter. "Half of her had already become construct before she chose to make that a discipline instead of a wound." Aroth, the Ouroboric Vessel, a biomechanical serpent of Runeglass and Aethersteel over living sinew, shares her breath and soul signature, and harm or healing reaches both of them equally. Her mentor "Vanished within a transmutation crucible; she intends to rebuild him atom by atom." Aroth sometimes speaks in the mentor's voice, "which she insists is only resonance memory." The Guild "Once banned her research for producing Soul-Fused Constructs, then reinstated it after she proved moral synchronization." Her Perfect Equivalence takes a thing apart to its Essence signature and rebuilds it, purified or as something else, and costs her steadiness in proportion to what she changes. Nobody knows whether what comes back is the same soul or a very good copy that believes it is.

**Valthren Odrin · The Lantern Scribe** (valthren-odrin-the-lantern-scribe). Archivist of Confession, Keeper of the Candle Archive, Heretic-Warden of the Lantern Ward of the Division; a Master and Warden of Duty whom everyone who deals with him still calls Keeper. A thin, candle-crowned man in robes that smell of wax and burnt parchment, their stitched script writhing when light touches it. The candles on his staff never gutter, and "each one records a soul's final confession." His Realization: "He rewrites his essence into wax tablets and seals them to his own flesh, and every revision frays the memory of the one before it." Wax restores his flesh and takes one memory each time. "A lie spoken near him ignites briefly in blue fire." His Lex Purgare rewrites a written law binding a corruption or heresy, and a candle of the Waxen Crown burns out with every correction. An order his card names "Hunts him for rewriting sacred doctrine into mortal tongue" (see section 7). He keeps an unlit candle said to hold the sin of a Titan, and he is still looking for the Draft Lux Tenebrae.

**Gonju · The Sage** (gonju-the-sage). The Verdure Sage, called the Quiet Greenhand: herbalist alchemist, corruption breaker and Rot-field specialist of the Division, and the Accord's Cleansing Consultant for Arbitration Territory Cases. "Soft spoken, observant, patient to a mythic degree." "Gonju cannot fight. At all." He is sent into Rot-fields, corrupted wildlife and contested ground "with a bear, a willow switch, and a satchel." Mallow, a young brown bear bonded by beast pact, sniffs out Rot seams, holds up the wounded during a cleansing and blocks miasmic gusts with his mass; he "Loves daisies and flatbread, and only shares with the penitent." Gonju's Green Tongue reads memory rings inside plants, "emotional echoes, soil history, trauma signatures", and coaxes saplings into growth in seconds. Animals within his reach feel no threat. He wears a loose white Verdure tunic lined in red-lace script, carries a bark-stitched satchel of seeds and vials, and walks without sound. His card holds him on the edge of Splintering, the crisis that fractures along emotional lines. Ascella "Wick" Morwyn holds him in professional respect.

**Silas Briarend Wytherm III · Gravebinder** (silas-briarend-wytherm-iii-gravebinder). Gravebinder of the Hollow Book, Warden of Rites Forgotten; his standing is "Guild Accord, Sect of the Hollow Book. Silent Circle of the Eastern Barrowwrights." His Refraction showed him his own fracture record, "and everything Splintering had promoted to a load path became visible, labelled, and impossible to un-see." Years of ritual exposure have made him immune to possession, and he pays for it in soul-fray, dream haunting and echo hallucination. His Silent Bloom reads a decaying thing down to the record it carries and burns the record instead of the thing: "What is left is inert, and clean, and no longer anybody." Through the rite he speaks in voices not his own, and they do not always stop when it ends. The burning cannot be undone, and he knows it in advance. "Silas has done this to a barrow and does not discuss it": a careless reading erased the record he meant to take. He accepts publicly that the trace is not the soul, and the Sect does not. Nobody knows what the Hollow Book is a copy of.

## 3 · The shared history

Four events. None is in the archive; each is new and fitted to every card it touches. The Farath Vein Incident is the card's own event: its setting and its company are new. Where a card states a cost, a refusal or a thing nobody knows, the event uses the cost and the refusal and leaves the unknown alone.

### The First Entry at the Loop bench

- **When.** The Withering Era, after the Guild reinstated Serai Linthra's research, and before Cael Dareth's card, which finds him unaffiliated: the Causality Loop and the expulsion that followed it.
- **Where.** A Division workroom in the Upper Ring of Stannvaard, the extraction cities on the crater walls (WORLD §5; the Stannvaard page: "Research sources experimental metalwork from the Inscription Guild").
- **Who.** Cael Dareth, Dr. Vaelion Rhest, Serai Linthra, Valthren Odrin, Lyssara Veyn.
- **What happened.** Cael ran the Causality Loop Experiment at his bench, an attempt to rewrite failure as success by reversing temporal Essence signatures. It left a paradox in his Soul Crystal, and he locked it with the Fixative Seal; the first state he ever locked was his own. The Division opened the case under the Heresiology, which lets no suspected site be entered by one investigator. The party of four was Rhest as resonance-reader, a Division scrivener the record does not name as glyph-scrivener, Serai as material examiner, and Valthren as witness-officer. The Division reads the collapse after a failed Threshold as its physician's business, and it sent Lyssara in behind them. Valthren did not open with an accusation. He asked Cael to explain the working. Cael explained it aloud as an equation, and where it reached the paradox he fell into recitation. Nothing he said burned blue. Rhest read the current in Cael's Crystal as a Wellspring, the first he had been sent to that runs inside a man, and found it held and not spreading. He put one line in the file against unsealing it: a cure placed in error is not undone by wanting it undone. Serai took the composition of every reagent on the bench through the seal in her eye. She asked Cael one question, about the reversal of the signatures. He answered it as an equation. She wrote the answer into the file in her own hand, and kept a copy. Lyssara examined him for the dissolution the Division had expected and found none: he carried the paradox and had not come apart. She filed that. The scrivener copied every text on the bench, and the originals went under the Division's seal. The Division expelled Cael. The file holds the readings and no reply to his argument.
- **What each took.**
  - Cael: the expulsion, his notebooks under the Division's seal, and an argument the Division has never answered.
  - Rhest: the one Wellspring he has read inside a man, and his line in the file, which is why nobody tried to break the seal.
  - Serai: the bench's compositions and Cael's answer, carried out of the room by an alchemist the Guild had banned and reinstated, from one it expelled.
  - Valthren: Cael's account for the Lantern Ward, with no lie burned in it.
  - Lyssara: a finding of nothing for her Branch to treat, filed on a man whose card ends him unable to decay.

### The seven days at the Mortalis Chambers

- **When.** The Withering Era: Lyssara Veyn's Catalyst Event.
- **Where.** The Grand Principality of Eresse, in the elven old-ground of the New World (WORLD §3), at the Mortalis Chambers her card names as her residence (unattested on the card).
- **Who.** Lyssara Veyn, Dr. Vaelion Rhest.
- **What happened.** Lyssara went into the Mortalis resonance field for seven days and asked the Division for a watcher. The Division sent Rhest, its consultant on Wellspring behaviour, a man who does not sleep. Before she went in she asked one thing: that nobody push Essence into her body, whatever it did. A Vitalia push brings the expenditure back, and the equilibrium she meant to hold would fall to one side or the other. On the fifth day her heart stopped. Rhest carries Verdantia at his neck and could have pushed. He did not. He wrote down the hour. On the seventh day she woke, and she no longer told the pulse of life from the rhythm of death.
- **What each took.**
  - Lyssara: her Realization, and the hour her heart stopped, in another person's hand.
  - Rhest: seven nights he would not have slept in any case, the hour in his own hand, and a colleague who had asked him to keep his hands still at the one moment a physician reaches.

### The Farath vein

- **When.** The Withering Era, in Azran Nemeir's years with the Division and before his elevation to Adept, which his card says formally recognised this act. Gonju was already the Accord's cleansing consultant, with Mallow.
- **Where.** The Foraye country of the arc, in the New World (WORLD §3 and §5), where "The Wellsprings here move" (NW, The Ground Itself).
- **Who.** Azran Nemeir, Gonju, Dr. Vaelion Rhest.
- **What happened.** The Farath vein fed the water of three chartered settlements. When it went foul the three disputed the water, and Arbitration took it as a territory case and sent Gonju, its cleansing consultant, with Mallow. The Division had Azran on the vein. Mallow found the seam. Gonju read the roots along it with the Green Tongue and found where the corruption had come in. The vein began to fail while they stood on it. Azran opened a dual Phreatis and Luminalis stabilization channel and held it alone for ninety-seven minutes. Gonju cannot fight and cannot hold a channel; he kept the corrupted wildlife quiet under the Honey Truce and did not leave. Rhest was the first to arrive from outside. He read the vein's behaviour and wrote the corrected boundary into the Aether with the Lattice Stylus. Azran stitched the corruption closed at the place Gonju had named, and then he wrote the incident report before anything else. After the report came the silence that long Phreatis work leaves in him. Gonju had never met him. He brought him tea and left him alone with it.
- **What each took.**
  - Azran: the Farath pattern, inscribed in the Codex Filament, and the elevation his card records.
  - Gonju: the three settlements' water and the case closed, and the vein's history, read from its roots.
  - Rhest: a re-bound vein, and the name of the junior who held it until he came.

### The barrow on the Caedor plains

- **When.** The Withering Era, in Gonju's present as his card gives it, on the edge of Splintering.
- **Where.** The Caedor plains in the arc of the New World (WORLD §3 and §5; Orya's card sets "villages and grain routes along the New World's Caedor plains", and the New World page gives the arc ash plains that "bury a village in one night").
- **Who.** Silas Briarend Wytherm III, Gonju.
- **What happened.** Within living memory a charter village on the plain was buried in ash in one night, and the survivors raised a barrow over the dead they could dig out. Two charters later disputed the ground around it, and Arbitration sent Gonju to read it for the case. The families asked the Accord for the burning, and the Accord sent a gravebinder. Silas has erased a barrow once by reading it carelessly, and he does not discuss it. Before this rite he asked the Greenhand for a reading. Gonju read the memory rings of the scrub on the mound and the roots beneath it, and named two records in the ground for Silas: the ash's, which was the night of the burial, and the dead's, which lay under it. Silas aimed the Silent Bloom at the second. He spoke in voices not his own for the length of the rite, and the grief went out of the barrow "like heat out of a stone." What was left was inert and clean. One of the voices did not stop when the rite ended. Afterward Mallow shared his flatbread with Silas. Gonju coaxed the first green up through the mound.
- **What each took.**

  - Gonju: the last reading anyone will make of that record. Silas burned it, and nobody will read it again, the families included.

## 7 · Where the cards disagree

Quoted and left open.

1. **Whose are the Neutral Chambers.** Rhest's card: "Guild Accord · Research and Archives Division · Neutral Chambers." (Rhest, I). Seren Valenne's: "Guild Accord · Arbitration Division · Arbitration Adjudicator, Senior. Assigned to the Neutral Chambers of the Inner World." (`Seren Valenne · The Grey Adjudicator.md`, I). Lyss Arvale's: "Guild Accord · Arbitration Division, Neutral Chambers." (`Lyss Arvale · The Quiet Swan.md`). Atheron Dreymarch's card files the office of Neutral Chambers Keeper under the Arbitration Division, and Banisher "was forged in the Neutral Chambers, under the Guild Accord's Arbitration Division" (`Banisher · Writ-Spear of Exile.md`). Rhest may be a Research consultant seated in an Arbitration house; no card says so.
2. **The order that hunts Valthren.** Valthren's card: "The Order of Lurien · Hunts him for rewriting sacred doctrine into mortal tongue." (Valthren, IX). The wiki: "the Holy See of Lurien, dead canon, retconned and replaced by the Holy Sea of Alabaster." (SW, ruled 2026-09-12); `Spellcraft/Anointing.md` says the same. Other cards carry a re-homing note; Valthren's carries none.
3. **Gonju and Lurien.** Riven's card: "Riven is the third sheet in this batch anchored to Lurien", with Thalyren Veyroot and Gonju (`Riven Eltamyr Vonnel · The Glaive.md`, head). Seraphion's: "Thalyren, Gonju and Chimwala are all anchored to it." (`Seraphion Veydrak · The Chain-Halo.md`, head). Gonju's card names no Lurien, no see and no seminary.
4. **Bitter Gall or Bitter Bloom.** Gonju's Traits: "Bitter Gall · Passive resistance to corruption." (Gonju, VI). Gonju's Relationships and Ascella's card: "Her Stillflame pairs with his Bitter Bloom to cleanse Rot seams without panic." (Gonju, X; Ascella, X). No trait on Gonju's card is called Bitter Bloom.
5. **Gonju's floor.** Gonju's card: "The lowest primary stat in Volume IV". Gorrum's card: "below Gonju's 74, which makes Gorrum the least combat-capable character in the entire volume". Shael Virellion's card calls its own Ardency of 44 "Second-lowest primary in Volume IV, behind only Gonju". Three cards, three different claims about who stands lowest.
6. **Benediction.** Gonju's card lists Benediction among his harmonisations and calls itself "the only sheet in the volume where the term is used correctly", as a resonance (Gonju, III). Khan Kirv's card: "Benediction · Vitalia. Governing." (`Khan Kirv · The Veil-Khan of the Dry Courts.md`, III).
7. **Alchemarch: title or order.** Alchemetrica: "A title rather than a rank, conferred rather than earned, held by perhaps forty people in the four quarters." (`Alchemetrica.md`, The Ladder). Wilhelm's card: "Registered within the Alchemarch Order of Varūn, now extinct." Cael's card has him "trained at the Alchemarch Citadel of the Iron Forges in Varūn" and marks "Varūn and the Alchemarch title attested", the citadel not (Cael, I).
8. **The Stage X thresholds.** Lyssara's pool counts "5,500 from Thresholds I to X" (Lyssara, IV), and Cael's the same figure (Cael, IV). Valthren's card counts 4,000 from the same Thresholds (Valthren, IV). Rhest's and Lyssara's cards each also flag their own pool against the live Part Three rates and do not resolve it (Rhest, IV; Lyssara, IV).
9. **Azran's two holds.** The Catalyst: "He held a stabilization circle solo for two hours during a commission after his party leader went down" (Azran, I). The Farath Vein Incident: "Held a dual Phreatis-Luminalis stabilization channel solo for ninety-seven minutes, saving three settlements' water access" (Azran, IX). The card does not say whether these are one event; if they are, the durations disagree. This record treats them as two.
10. **Rhest's era label.** His card gives "Era · Withering Era" and, among its labels, "Withering Age" (Rhest, I and X), the superseded term (WORLD §1).

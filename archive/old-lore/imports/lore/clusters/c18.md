# c18 · The Tenryū and the dragon-blooded

The oni celebrants of the Tenryū line and their drinking halls, the drake shepherd, and the dragonkin of the marches, courts and borders: Daigo Tenryū, the Ribbon-Ox, who ties promises with his teeth; Goraku Tenryū, the Crimson Gourd, war-leader of the Braqth Raiding Courts; Kurokiba Ranzou, the wandering Brew-Sage of the oni halls; Orya of the Wheat March, who herds drakes on the Caedor plains with Khyr; Seravain Drelith, the Marchlord of Glass; Saórén Vyrrakal of the Molten Lineage, a court performer gone free; and Lyra Drakoshi, the freeholder who keeps a gate on the Outer World border.

**Era.** Unstated on all seven cards. Every card sets its member against a working Guild Accord: Orya's convoys and tithe, Goraku's standing with the Enforcement Division, Kurokiba's contracts, Saórén's place on a bounty list, Lyra's watch posts, Seravain's Concord Sanctions, Daigo's Measures. The shared events therefore fall in the Accord's age. This record names no era and no year. It orders the events by the members' Catalyst Events and by one another.

## 1 · Who is here

**Daigo Tenryū · Ribbon-Ox** (daigo-tenryu-ribbon-ox). Called Ribbon-Ox and "The Dango Dragon", a "Sky-oni / dragon-blooded celebrant" whose role is "Vanguard breaker, pact-binder, morale engine." His hair is sapphire and worn "in a festive snarl"; his ivory horns are banded in red cord; his kimono is layered with shrine-knots and tassels, and his sandals spark when he laughs. He always has a skewer of dango in hand, "fuel and bribe in one." His power runs on joy: claps, chants, drum steps and simple sugars become thrust and lift, and "Starve him and he is merely strong; feed him and he is a comet." Tapped together, his Festival Horns "boom like shrine drums", and "Crowds match his beat before they know they have decided to." His Ribbon-Pact is "A red cord tied with his teeth" that "seals a quick promise"; "Tug it later and the other end warms in the signer's palm." His duties: "Crack sieges into parades. Turn panicked crowds into marching columns. Anchor festival truces so trade can happen under watchful dragons." His limits: "Overcast moods dull him", "Cords will not hold against true malice", and "Fasting rites make him woozy and poetic". The card gives him no home, order or era. It names Goraku as kin, Orya and Khyr as pasture brothers, Kaede and Kaito of the Star Chamber, and Vireline, who often taps him "as champion for a cheerful, crowd-pleasing Measure."

**Goraku Tenryū · The Crimson Gourd** (goraku-tenryu-the-crimson-gourd). The Crimson Gourd, Oni of the Ninth Toast, Laughing Bloodstorm: "Oni Warlord. Battlefield Ritualist. Essence-Brewer." He is war-leader of the Braqth Raiding Courts, "Outer World coastal Oni clans known for seasonal raids, domain-borne feasts, and Blood-Gourd rites", and brews their Battle Draughts, which "infuse warriors with volatile Essence mid-fight." Braqth is a real place in the Outer World, the Southern Pan (card head; `RULINGS.md`, 2026-09-12). He carries the Gourd of Endless Night, "A Soulbound artifact-gourd carrying three rotating brews", and "Its fumes alone can make mortals lightheaded." Each step of his Bloodstorm Stride "spills a phantom wave of boiling blood-fire around him." His Oni's Pact is "A family-bound agreement with a Fire-Aspected Wellspring that superheats his body when drunk", and it lets him breathe jets of molten steam. His rite "thrives on momentum: the more he drinks, the hotter and more unstable his Essence burns", and he is "bordering on losing physical form when fully ignited." To the Enforcement Division he is a "problematic ally", for his "unpredictable drunken rages." The Black Concord, by rumour, takes his oni-brew toxins "for covert killings". Vaelthyr Iscarn is "Mutual respect, occasional drinking partner."

**Kurokiba Ranzou · The Drunken Oni** (kurokiba-ranzou-the-drunken-oni). Oni Duelist and Brew-Sage, Fermentation Combat Master: a "Wandering Oni cultivator from the Ember-Sake Highlands" who "Holds the honorary title of Brew-Sage among Oni clans for mastery of Essence fermentation" and is "Known in Guild Accord records as an unpredictable yet highly dangerous mercenary-for-hire." His body ferments Essence into alcohol and keeps it in the blood. He can "fight for days without rest", and he "Exhales flammable sake vapour to ignite, blind, or disorient enemies." He carries the Red Horn Gourd, "A lacquered crimson oni gourd etched with flame and wave glyphs"; he wears the Oni Bead Torque, an ancestral necklace of black beads with a faint crimson glow at the seams, and the heavy black and red Ferment-Braided Hakama; he fights in the Sakebrand Gauntlet. At his Catalyst "He examined his own architecture and decided to ferment it", and "the Oni halls have never agreed" whether that was the least serious answer on record or the only one that worked. "Fight him early and he is a very strong drunk with a gourd." "He is one bad night from a Fracture Event and has been for years." The oni drinking halls celebrate him "as both a hero and a menace for never losing a duel or a drinking contest"; Accord brokers know him as a "wild contract"; he leaves new fermentation techniques with travelling brewers as gifts.

**Orya of the Wheat March · The Dragon Shepard** (orya-of-the-wheat-march-the-dragon-shepard). "Steppe ward and migration-keeper", who "Guides drakes like a shepherd guides flocks, guarding villages and grain routes along the New World's Caedor plains." She belongs to the "Village Concord of the Wheat March" and is "Field partner to the Guild Accord's Logistics & Supply Division during harvest convoys." She wears red-gold travel robes stitched with pasture sigils, a fur-edged hood and a bead-weighted scarf. She carries the bone-chime crook called the Yoke of Winds, with an ibex skull head and green beads; a ledger satchel of "Egg counts, molting times, pasture rights" under "Wax seals from four villages"; barley-straw knots for good calving and an "iron nail for storm honesty". She is "Calm, stubborn", with "eyes that count every mouth she has sworn to feed." Her whistle settles beasts. Her Threshfield, "Knee-high ripples of air", slows "charging things without breaking legs", and warmth around her steadies the frightened. Khyr of the Harvest, a "Prairie Trueblood Drake" with a golden hide and a wheat-chaff mane, "chose Orya during a drought relief run". His breath "dries wet grain and snuffs Rot-fog", and he "Steals gourds and sits on them like eggs." She keeps the Concord tithe, "one egg per twenty heads to the Accord, none taken from grieving pairs." Her card reads her as a woman who has spent forty years learning to turn a stampede "and never once learned to stop one." Smoke bells and ash storms muddle her wind-cues, enclosed ravines wedge a flock, and she is "Over-bonded to Khyr." Her card names no person.

**Seravain Drelith · Wyrm-Crowned** (seravain-drelith-wyrm-crowned). The shortest card here: "The Ivory Coil. Wyrm-Crowned. Marchlord of Glass. Dragonkin trueblood, Celestial Wyrmline. Outside the Accord under Concord Sanctions, Marchlord Exception." The Glass Marches are "a territory he has been standing in." Inside his command radius the ground crystallises to his formation's advantage, "and it does not revert"; "Encirclements close bloodlessly because the terrain has already decided." "The Marchlord of Glass cannot personally fight well." The legacy sheet's line is that he "relies on proximity to formed ranks for peak power," and the card's counter is "Take him away from formed ranks." His cost is "Ancestral memory fugues under overstress." His antler-crests "are live conduits". The card carries no relationships, no era and no chapter.

**Saórén Vyrrakal · The Walking Flame** (saoren-vyrrakal-the-walking-flame). "Dragonkin, Molten Lineage": horns, claws, "natural resistance to extreme heat", golden scales along the forearms and abdomen beneath the robe, and scale that shows under the skin in anger. "Ember-Touched Dragonkin. Flame-Tongued Performer. Wellspring Duelist and Charmer of Courts." Self-proclaimed "voice of golden fire". They are scion of "a draconic bloodline rooted in the volcanic dominions beneath Tres Aures"; a "Former court performer of the Fire Blossom Empire, now free and wandering"; "Occasionally contracted by noble houses for courtly disputes or entertainment", which "usually ends in something catching fire"; and "Registered on the Guild Accord's Ember-Tier bountied assets list." The card files them as dangerous and dramatic, and no criminal. The card uses they. Their Golden Bloom brightens and heats with confidence, the Scalebreak Grin staggers lesser foes, Ember Voice holds a room in the middle of a fight, and Flourish Step leaves "harmless-looking flame trails that detonate if touched." The Draconic Furnace spends everything in one breath or one eruption: "They pay for this afterwards, in private, and have never let anyone see it." "Every engagement they have ever won was won before contact", and the card's counter is "Do not applaud." They carry the Sulakari Veil. They have "seduced both nobles and mercenaries in equal measure", hold "no true loyalties", and are "fiercely protective of those who accept both their strength and their style."

**Lyra Drakoshi · Pink Flame** (lyra-drakoshi-pink-flame). The Blushscale Warden, Pink Flame, The Coiled Blade: "Draconic Border Warden. Furnace Swordswoman. Ember Sentinel of the Gate." On "The Outer World border" she is "An Independent Concord Freeholder, contracted by choice to local Guild Accord watch posts, holding no House seat and answering to no throne." She came up "on raw survival, then spent her mercenary years learning to win without burning everything down". Her Catalyst came on "the day she chose the same road, the same gate, over and over". Embers roll slowly in her chest and tail. They brighten to molten gold in a fight, and heat trails her hair "like steam off a hot blade in cold air." She carries the Dragonhook Blade, curved and hooked near the tip, and a Horn Chain Tassel on one horn that chimes when hostile Essence targets her from behind. Her wounds cauterise almost at once. Her Dragon Scent of Truth smells the emotional temperature of a room: "lies and cowardice carry a sour smoke tag, though a skilled liar can mask it under heavy scent." Past a fatal threshold she collapses into hot ash, and a smaller, drained Lyra reforms from the embers. The watch posts "Send emergency flares when dragon-class muscle is needed fast." Two feuding border lords "Signed a ceasefire after she heated the stone floor under their boots until they smoked; the humor kept it from reading as coercion." On her route she is "Half legend, half grumpy regular at the local inn." Her card grants gloss rights: "She will tell anyone exactly how the fire works, usually while grinning about it."

## 3 · The shared history

Four events. None is in the archive; each is new and fitted to every card it touches. Lyra's ceasefire between two feuding border lords is her card's own event: its setting and its company are new. No new name is coined; the two lords stay unnamed, as the card leaves them.

### The speaking-mold line on the Wheat March

- **When.** After Khyr chose Orya on a drought relief run (Orya, VII), in a harvest that came in under cold rain. It is the first season Orya and Daigo worked one route, the pairing Eryndrel Vaelune's card later finds and uses.
- **Where.** The Wheat March on the Caedor plains, the ash plain of the New World's arc (WORLD §3 and §5; Orya's card: "villages and grain routes along the New World's Caedor plains"; the New World page: "Ash plains that carry a crop in three seasons").
- **Who.** Orya of the Wheat March, Daigo Tenryū.
- **What happened.** Cold rain lay on the Wheat March through the harvest, and a line of the Rot's "speaking mold" came up through the wet sheaves toward the villages. The Accord's harvest convoy stood at the field edge with its carts half loaded. Daigo Tenryū was travelling with it to keep the crowds at the carts moving. Orya walked the flank of the line with her crook and whistled cross-drafts through the stooks. Khyr went ahead of her breathing warm gusts over the grain, drying it and snuffing the Rot-fog, and she combed the crop behind him. The mold had already reached some mouths among the field hands. The villages began to crowd onto the road. Daigo tapped his horns together and walked the frightened into a marching column along the carts. Then he bit a dango and breathed out over the ones the mold had reached, and the warm sugar-dust cleared it from their mouths. The line broke short of the villages. The convoy carried the grain out dry. That night Khyr took a gourd off the lead wagon and sat on it, and Orya let him keep it until morning.
- **What each took.**
  - Orya: a harvest carried out dry, and someone to take the crowd while she worked the flank. From that season the division Daigo's card records held: he herds people, she herds drakes.
  - Daigo: a pasture brother, his card's word for her, and a route he has worked beside her since, where Eryndrel Vaelune later uses her ripples and his ribbons as moving posts.

### The unfinished contest on the Braqth coast

- **When.** After Goraku Tenryū and Kurokiba Ranzou had each met Refraction by brewing from it (their Catalyst Events; Goraku's card calls it "the same response"), while Goraku led the Braqth Raiding Courts and the oni clans already called Kurokiba Brew-Sage.
- **Where.** A feast hall of the Braqth Raiding Courts on the Braqth coast, in the Outer World (WORLD §3; Braqth ruled a real place, the Southern Pan, on 2026-09-12; Goraku's card: "Outer World coastal Oni clans"). The Southern Pan page gives the Braqthian confederations their standing-stone work in columnar basalt, the quarter's universal building stone.
- **Who.** Goraku Tenryū, Kurokiba Ranzou, Daigo Tenryū.
- **What happened.** The Braqth courts held one of their domain-borne feasts, and the wandering Brew-Sage came in off the road to it. The hall set its war-leader against him in a drinking contest. Each man drank from his own gourd, Goraku from the Gourd of Endless Night and Kurokiba from the Red Horn Gourd, and the hall kept the pace with drums and chanting. Kurokiba's brew built in his blood, and the sake vapour off his breath hung over the benches. Goraku ran hotter with every draught, as his rite runs. Steam came off his skin. The blood-fire spilled around his seat each time he shifted on it, and he did not slow. Daigo Tenryū, his kinsman, was at the feast. He tapped his horns together, and the boom went through the basalt like a shrine drum. The hall took his beat, and the chant slowed under it. He went between the two men with his cords. With his teeth he tied a red cord on each man's wrist, on a quick promise: the contest would be finished on a night when he stood in the hall. Both men let him tie it. Nobody lost. Neither man has sat the second night.
- **What each took.**
  - Goraku: a red cord on his wrist, and a contest he can finish only with his kinsman in the hall. His raiders saw him set the gourd down unbeaten.
  - Kurokiba: his record whole, since he did not lose, and a cord that warms in his palm whenever Daigo tugs the other end. The oni halls tell the night both ways.
  - Daigo: two cord-ends, and a night owed to him by each man.

### The ceasefire at the gate on the Grass Sea road

- **When.** After Lyra Drakoshi had taken her road and her gate for good (her Catalyst Event, Lyra I); after Saórén Vyrrakal had left the court of the Fire Blossom Empire and gone free; with the Glass Marches already the ground the Marchlord of Glass stands in.
- **Where.** A border road across the Southern Grass Sea, where the human marcher states meet Drakyssian ranch territory (WORLD §5, South; SW: the Grass Sea is "Divided between human marcher states and Drakyssian ranch territory"). Lyra's gate stands on it, with a local Guild Accord watch post and the inn. Her card names "The Outer World border" and no region; this record places it here.
- **Who.** Lyra Drakoshi, Saórén Vyrrakal, Seravain Drelith.
- **What happened.** Two border lords had been feuding along the road, and they came to parley in the stone-floored hall by Lyra's gate. One of them had contracted Saórén Vyrrakal for the dispute, as champion and as show. The other had asked the Marchlord of Glass to stand behind his claim, and the Marchlord's formed ranks came up to the verge of the road. The watch post fired a flare for dragon-class muscle. Lyra came. She shut her gate to formed ranks and told the Marchlord that the table was open to him if he walked in alone. He left his ranks on the verge and came through the gate by himself, into a room where the other lord's champion was waiting. The chime on her horn stayed quiet while he walked behind her to the table. Saórén made the entrance they had been paid for, flame trails on the floor and the grin. Lyra watched it through to the end and told them it was good fire and a good show. She smelled the room. Both lords gave off sour smoke. The Marchlord gave off none, and under Saórén's perfume she could read nothing at all. When the lords began to call their champions forward, she heated the stone floor under their boots until the soles smoked, and she grinned while she did it. The hall laughed. The lords laughed with it, and both signed. The duel was never fought. The Marchlord walked back through the gate, and his ranks left the verge. The verge where they stood waiting has a glaze on it now, and the carts go round it.
- **What each took.**
  - Lyra: a ceasefire on her road, one guest she smelled no lie on, and one she could not smell at all.
  - Saórén: a contract closed before contact with nobody hurt, and the room won by someone else's fire. And a warden who took their fire and their show both, which is what their card says earns their protection.
  - Seravain: a walk through a gate away from his ranks, at a warden's word, with the opposing champion in the room, and the walk back out. The claim he stood behind was settled, and his ranks never crossed the road.

### The first truce-fair on the reopened road

- **When.** The trading season after the ceasefire at the gate.
- **Where.** The same road across the Southern Grass Sea, at Lyra's gate.
- **Who.** Daigo Tenryū, Lyra Drakoshi.
- **What happened.** With the ceasefire signed, the watch post sent for someone to anchor a festival truce, so that trade could open on the road under watchful dragons, as Daigo's card gives his duty. Daigo came. He set the fair's pace with his horns and turned the crowd at the gate into columns. People from both lords' lands came to trade across the old line. He tied their quick promises with red cords bitten into knots. Lyra kept the gate as the watching dragon the truce needed, and she stayed at the gate through the dancing. No blade came out that season. The traders came back the next season, and the one after.
- **What each took.**
  - Daigo: cord-ends whose other ends sit in the palms of traders from both sides of an old feud.
  - Lyra: a busy road. Some of the traders are the regulars her card finds at the inn.

## 6 · Same person?

No. The seven cards are seven people with different bodies, peoples and offices. Goraku and Kurokiba come nearest: two oni brewers who met Refraction the same way. Goraku's card holds them apart ("two oni, same Stage, same conclusion, and neither card knows the other"), and their gourds, clans and homes differ: Goraku leads the Braqth courts with the Gourd of Endless Night, and Kurokiba wanders from the Ember-Sake Highlands with the Red Horn Gourd. Daigo and Goraku are kin, two people by both cards and by ruling. Outside the cluster, Elder Seravain · Ophidian Archivist shares Seravain Drelith's given name. c14 has recorded them as two people, and this record agrees: Drelith is a dragonkin Marchlord outside the Accord under sanctions, and Elder Seravain is an elder of the Research and Archives Division inside it.

## 7 · Where the cards disagree

Quoted and left open.

1. **The Walking Flame's name.** The title and the Identity spell "Saórén". The Catalyst note reads "Saóren has never described it as anything but a good night." (Saórén, I), and the stat note "Saóren is spectacle with nothing underneath it." (IV). The roster and this record follow the title.
2. **Orya and Khyr.** Orya's card, V: "She is not bonded to Khyr. She is bonded to a herd, and Khyr is the one with a name." The same card, VII: "Bond" and "Khyr chose Orya during a drought relief run."; XI: "Over-bonded to Khyr."
3. **Daigo's gourd.** Goraku's card: "Both are oni. Both are gourd-and-festival built." (Goraku, head). Daigo's card carries dango skewers, the Skewer Fan, Tassel Cords and Ox-Charm Geta, and no gourd.
4. **Whether Daigo and Orya know each other.** Daigo's card: "The two most similar sheets in the volume and neither knows it." (Daigo, XI). Eryndrel Vaelune's card: "Orya & Daigo · Uses their ripples and ribbons as moving posts to stitch routes." (Eryndrel, X). The first reads either as two people who have never met or as two people who do not know their builds match; the second works them as a pair. The events above have them work one route, which Eryndrel's card needs, and neither is told of the likeness.
5. **Kaede's pronoun.** Daigo's card: "She cools his over-eager pushes" (Daigo, XI). Kaede's card uses they; c07 records the same disagreement from its side.
6. **Goraku and Vaelthyr.** Goraku's card: "Vaelthyr Iscarn · Mutual respect, occasional drinking partner." and "Both use each other for influence over Outer World affairs." (Goraku, VIII). Vaelthyr's card lists his relationships and does not name Goraku (`Vaelthyr Iscarn · Laughing Horn.md`, VIII).

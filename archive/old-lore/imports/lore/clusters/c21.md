# c21 · The Outer trails

The trader who deals with everyone, and the guardians, monks, oracles, pot-helmed drifters, shepherds and ghostwalkers of the wandering roads: a relic broker, an Accord guardian of the Greenroads, an Arbitration envoy with a scarred face, an oracle whose eye will not close, a drifter in a pot, a shepherd with a belled lamb, and a marksman who was sold. No card here states an era, and no archived scene holds any of the seven. Canon order holds throughout: Isaac's direction, then the card, then the archived scenes, then the wiki, then this record.

**Sources read.** The seven cards in full, in the Volume I character-card folder of `wiki/`. **Era.** Unstated on all seven cards, and no archived scene holds any of them. Shael's card ties him to Karo Venrik, whose own era, 'Post-Riftfall cycle, 512 VE', his Open Rulings hold. ## 1 Who is here

**Hyo Miran · The Velvet Dealer.** He is Hyo Miran the Red-Threaded, called the Pawprint Peddler and the Laughing Fang: travelling merchant, trickster negotiator and "Relic Broker of the Outer Trails." He is "Guildless by design." He holds Merchant Rite Papers from the Vellsorean Writ Consortium, and he denies with a grin that he is a shadow-affiliate of Veydrassil's Black Bloom Market. He always knows what a person wants more than what they say. A deal struck with him under duress or deceit bites later. He never casts openly. His shortblade is surgical when he defends his goods or his allies, and for the rest, he runs. His threads carry an encoded memory, a charm glyph or a contract nobody remembers agreeing to. He cannot recall a thread once it is given. It binds only if it is accepted. Certain ancient relics know him on sight, and his card gives the reason: "They have a record, and he is in it." His Catalyst is "a deal he could have taken and did not, at a price he is still paying", and he has never named it. His clients have been "Desert kings, temple thieves, and dream-bound children." He once gave Basil, Hazul Vehramin's Eidolon, a collar that changes with the moon. He calls Dalphne "the only honest soul that ever bartered with me." He avoids Wilhelm religiously. "His name is both a delight and a warning depending on the region."

**Baojin Zhaorin · The Verdant Colossus.** His card makes him "Guild Accord Vanguard. Traveling Guardian of the Greenroads." and "Gentle giant and reluctant juggernaut of slaughter." He is "protector of caravans, breaker of sieges, restorer of Wellsprings", and the sworn keeper of the Verdant Wellspring of Zhenri, "a sacred grove that feeds on blood spilled in its defence." Accord envoys trust him to settle disputes without bloodshed, and they have learned that when he abandons words, no one is left to argue. He talks for as long as he can tell what is happening. When he can no longer tell, he ends it. In battle his fur and flesh harden into black, jagged armour that splinters blades. His calm keeps allies steady until the slaughter begins. He walks paths through wilds others cannot see and comes out where enemies bleed. When he fights for the grove the ground drinks: bamboo erupts from corpses, roots coil through wounds, and the leaves drip red. The grove does not distinguish whose blood it is given. Beastkin caravans pour him bloodwine in respect, knowing he will shed his own and theirs if the forest's peace is broken. Off the Greenroads his card calls him "a very large man with a mantle." It leaves one thing open: "Whether the grove chose him or he was simply the first to bleed for it."

**Kaien Veyren · The Lotus Scar.** He is called the Lotus Scar and the White Bloom: "Wellspring Monk. Aether-Scarred Survivor. Wandering Regency Arbiter." He serves the Accord's Arbitration Division as a Regency Envoy, a posting left unnamed since Mireya was struck. He "Mediates high-tension negotiations between Innerworld and Outer World realms, often deployed where Wellspring disputes threaten war." He survived a Wellspring collapse, and the scars never closed. They run jagged and vein-like across his cheek and eye, and they bleed faint light when he channels. His fracture record is on his face for anyone to read. He moves the way lotus petals drift on still water, with soft pivots and sudden speed. He is very hard to hit, and he cannot hit hard. By scattering petals of Essence into the air he raises echoes of past events from the places and objects that keep them. His White Bloom is a moonlit pond ringed by white lotus, where time dilates and wounds mend. Long exposure to it risks losing himself in recursive dreams. Lord Maerion Lysandre, his patron in the Regency, values his composure and mistrusts his habit of vanishing for weeks. "A scar is the final word in an argument between flesh and fate."

**Shael Virellion · Oracle of the Broken Mirror.** He is independent, "Formerly House Eltarien, dissolved." His twin sister Nyra, the house's Seer, died enacting the Rite of Trinal Vision, the rite that transformed him. It worked. He has been unable to advance since. His skin is stained a faint luminescent blue. His central eye is always open and always weeping, and it does not blink. It shows him the fractured spiritual signatures of others, and seeing them without rest has worn away his grip on time and reality. His voice ripples between octaves. His presence brings calm and then a drowning panic. He cannot go further without reliving the rite in its entirety. A path formed in him after the trauma, the Rite of Trinal Reflection, which reflects his soul into three layers, "what is felt, what is seen, what is drowned". Each mirrored echo carries a part of the pain. Karo Venrik investigated the transformation and declared it "unpunishable" under Absolute Magical Law, citing "divine interference." Vaelen Mourcrix read Ideh's fracture signature in the eye and warned him never to gaze into the Harmonial Core. "I cannot close the eye. It is not mine anymore."

**Dorrik · The Pot Man.** He is the Pot Man: "Vessel-Wanderer of Forgotten Roads. Unaffiliated drifter." At his Catalyst, where most people weep or sit down, "Dorrik put a pot on his head and kept walking", and so came into the road, as the folk name for that Stage has it. His body has the weathered make of pale limestone, and he feels living things near him, faintly. He sleeps like the dead and wakes changed in his structure, and he has never once wondered why. Struck on the pot, he sharpens, briefly and without choosing to. He cannot do it himself. His card says he is what most of the four quarters look like: "He is the rule." It leaves one thing open: "what the pot was for."

**Helki · The Shepherd of Unwilling Paths.** She is called the Bell-Bearer and Helki of the Flock: "Elegiac Conduit. Wandering Tender of the Dying Wellspring. Bearer of the Offering." She walks the Unmeasured Path, nominally neutral, independent and itinerant. She travels with Orvain. He was a lamb that wandered into a dying Wellspring rite and was changed, and his fleece has never been shorn; cloth woven from it cannot be turned against its wearer. Her Catalyst surfaced from three days she spent pouring her Essence into a dying Wellspring nexus. She walked out with grey in her hair and a lamb that had been standing in the current the whole time. The Continuum seeded a Domain in her. She reads the inner inventory of every living thing near her, whether it is ripening toward something or fleeing it, and she cannot stop. Wounds that should kill her become survivable. She has come back twice from clinical death. Orvain's bell rings whenever someone nearby crosses a threshold decision that cannot be undone, and nothing silences it. Her Offering Walk is three measured steps toward a target, the target's true name spoken in Parun, and a tithe given on the target's behalf. Each walk strips a part of her own capacity for ever. She cannot walk the offering toward someone she cannot name. Villages and caravans of the Outer World remember her from the Greying of the Calderath Pass and the Stillness at the Gate. "Neither incident is on any Accord file." She walks toward the Verdant Altar and has not reached it.

**Ristra Lenovira · The Hollow.** He is "The Hollow of the Outer World", "Eyes of Gold, Scars of Iron": an elf of unknown bloodline, and "Long-Range Execution Specialist. Ghostwalker of the Scattered Wastes. Loyal Marksman of Nyx." He was a slave of the Outer World's auction circuits. Nyx bought his freedom, and he owes her "life, freedom and loyalty." She is the only one who saw him as more than a weapon. He wanders under her banner and answers to no guild and no Concord. He has no known kin, and no Elven bloodline has claimed him. He keeps apart, even when safe. He sees far and small, and he finds the place a body will fail from very far off. He works the shot out before he takes it, ricochet and angle and weather, and it lands on a weak point the target did not know it had. He must be undetected, untouched and unburdened, and the third fails first. He can keep fighting through grievous wounds. The inner burn that sharpens his reflexes leaves him chronically tired. His card leaves open "What is behind the mental pressure holding the Crucible shut." His words: "I'm no hero, no savior."

## 3 The shared history

### The nexus in the Varakh Drylands

**When.** Undated. The three days are Helki's card's, before her Catalyst Event; the place, the collapse and the two men are new. It falls before Kaien's Catalyst Event, which showed him the fracture record the collapse wrote on his face, and while Baojin already served in the Accord's Vanguard.

**Where.** A Wellspring nexus in the Varakh Drylands of the Outer World, which the villages about it drew on (WORLD §5). The Outer World page gives the Drylands weak Aether and "several abundant years followed by severe drought". The page's reconciliation note counts the Drylands among the ground Accord surveyors reach. No card names the nexus, and this record leaves it unnamed.

**Who.** Helki, Kaien Veyren, Baojin Zhaorin.

**What happened.** In the Varakh Drylands a Wellspring nexus had been failing through the drought years, and the villages about it drew on what was left. Kaien kept it as a Wellspring monk. Helki came to it on foot, as she comes to dying Wellsprings. She knelt at the nexus and poured her Essence into it, and she did not stop for three days. A lamb from a village flock had wandered into the current before she knelt, and it stood there. Nobody could get it out. The villages sent word of a breach, and the Accord sent its Vanguard. Baojin came. His card calls him a restorer of Wellsprings, but the ground there was spent and gave his green work nothing. He laboured beside Helki with what answered him, and the nexus went on failing. On the third day it collapsed. Kaien stood nearest its heart, holding the flow he had kept, and the collapse opened his face along the cheek and the eye in jagged, vein-like lines. Baojin went into it with his mantle closed and carried Kaien out. Helki rose from the dead current with grey in her hair. The lamb was still standing where it had stood for three days, and she carried it out herself. The nexus never came back, and the villages lost their draw on it.

**What each took.** Helki took the grey, the lamb she walks with now as Orvain, and what surfaced at her Catalyst as the seed of a Domain. She also took a nexus she had poured three days into, dead under her hands. Kaien took the scars, which have never closed and bleed faint light when he channels, and which his Catalyst later showed him as his own fracture record. He took a life owed to Baojin as well. Baojin took a Wellspring he was sent to restore and could not, on ground too spent to answer him. He carried a monk out of it with the mantle alone.

### The review on the Greenroads

**When.** Undated, and new. After the nexus in the Varakh Drylands, while Kaien held his Arbitration posting and after Baojin was sworn to the grove at Zhenri. It falls after the Rite of Trinal Vision gave Shael the eye. This record does not place it against the Siege of Zhenri Grove, the arc Baojin's card names.

**Where.** The Greenroads, within reach of the Verdant Wellspring of Zhenri (Baojin's card). No page places either. Baojin's card puts him and Hyo on the Outer Trails together, and this record takes those as the trails of the Outer World (WORLD §3).

**Who.** Hyo Miran, Baojin Zhaorin, Kaien Veyren, Shael Virellion.

**What happened.** A Beastkin caravan came down the Greenroads under Baojin's guard, and Hyo walked with it, selling to it. Among his goods was a relic. Where the road runs within the grove's reach, armed men stopped the caravan and asked for the relic. Baojin talked with them for as long as he could tell what was happening. Then he stopped talking. The ground drank. Bamboo came up through the dead, roots coiled through the wounded, and the leaves over the road dripped red. The grove took a drover of the caravan's own along with the raiders, since it does not distinguish whose blood it is given. Hyo kept his goods with the shortblade. At the next halt the caravan poured Baojin bloodwine, as the Beastkin caravans do. The Accord's Arbitration reviews every engagement, and Kaien was sent to review this one. He brought Shael. Kaien scattered petals over the road, and the echoes came up out of the ground: the approach, the talk, the drover going down under the roots. He read the relic the same way and found Hyo in its record more than once. Shael sat with the living while the eye wept and read them: the caravan master, the drover's kin, Baojin and Hyo. The drover's kin moved their fire away from him before night. Asked where the relic had come from, Hyo gave the review an account. Shael said it was untrue. Hyo gave the true account then and named his price for it, and Kaien paid. The review Kaien sealed entered the dead, the relic and the echo he had read. It named no fault. The relic went into the Accord's keeping. Hyo's true account led Kaien and Hyo to the ground it had come from, where more lay buried.

**What each took.** Baojin took a drover of a caravan in his care, dead under his own grove, and a review sealed by the monk he had carried out of the Drylands. The bloodwine was poured for him all the same. Hyo kept his goods and his price, lost the relic, and took a partner in recovery who had read his name in a relic's record. He met an eye he could not sell a lie to. Kaien took the review, Hyo's road to more relics, and his first cause to mistrust the dealings he profits by. Shael took the review's fee and a seat nobody near him wanted. He saw Hyo's first account through and said so aloud.

### The bell on the forgotten road

**When.** Undated. It is Dorrik's Catalyst Event as his card gives it, the day he came into the road; Helki's presence is new. It falls after the nexus in the Varakh Drylands, since the bell is Orvain's.

**Where.** A drove road across the Southern Grass Sea of the Outer World that no herd or cart uses any longer (WORLD §5; Dorrik's card: "Forgotten Roads").

**Who.** Helki, Dorrik.

**What happened.** Helki was walking a road nobody used any more, with Orvain at her side. A man walked the same road ahead of her, alone, near enough for her Read to reach him. He stopped in the road. He put a pot on his head. He walked on. Orvain's bell rang, as it rings whenever someone near it crosses a threshold that cannot be undone, and nothing silenced it. The man did not turn round. Her Read gave her what it gives of anyone in its reach, and she has told nobody what it gave her of him. She did not call after him. She walked behind him until the road forked, and took the other branch.

**What each took.** Dorrik took his coming into the road, with a bell ringing at his back that he did not turn for. Helki took a bell rung for a stranger who did not stop. She is the only one who saw where his Stage came to him.

### The crossing of the Scattered Wastes

**When.** Undated, and new. After the bell on the forgotten road, and after Nyx bought Ristra's freedom, while he served under her banner.

**Where.** The Scattered Wastes, which Ristra's card names and no page places. His card calls him "The Hollow of the Outer World", and Helki's card sets her among the villages and caravans of the Outer World (WORLD §3).

**Who.** Helki, Dorrik, Ristra Lenovira.

**What happened.** A caravan crossed the Scattered Wastes, and Helki walked with it, as she walks with the caravans of the Outer World. Dorrik walked at its tail, and the drovers let him. A drover saw a man lying among the rocks off the track and took him for dead. Dorrik went out over the rocks, got him onto his back and carried him in. He was an elf. He had wounds another man would have died of, and he was awake. It was Ristra. Helki knelt by him, and her Read took him in as it takes anyone in its reach. She has not said what it gave her. She would have walked the offering for him, as she walks it for the dying. The walk needs the target's true name, spoken in Parun, and she did not have his. He gave none. She dressed his wounds with herbs from her own pack, and the caravan carried him on a wagon. He lived. At the first water he was gone from the wagon in the night, back into the Wastes toward Nyx's banner. He left nothing behind. Helki knew the pot on the man who had carried him in. She had heard Orvain's bell ring for it on the old road.

**What each took.** Ristra took his life back to Nyx's banner, kept by a shepherd and a pot-man who never learned his name, and a debt to them he has not paid. His card still gives him one bond, and it is Nyx. Helki took a dying man she could not name and so could not walk for, and she tended him the slow way. That crossing cost her no tithe. Dorrik took a stranger across the rocks on his back, and the shepherd's face a second time.

## 6 Same person?

No two cards here are one person.

- **Kaien Veyren, Kaien Rhuven and Kaien Thal'Ruhm.** Three cards and three surnames, two of them outside this cluster. Kaien Rhuven is a human herbalist of the Verdant Hollow who left the Concord Citadel for "silence and soil". Kaien Thal'Ruhm is a former Accord artificer of the Alchemetrica Division, half his body gold-fused alloy. Kaien Veyren is an Arbitration envoy whose scars are on his face. Only the cast index joins them (section 7).
- **Ristra Lenovira and Ristra of Mirehollow.** `The Disciplines/The Sage Arts.md` names "Ristra of Mirehollow", "A swamp-born Sage" whose "operational range never extends beyond the Mirehollow watershed." Ristra Lenovira is an elf sold on the auction circuits who ghostwalks the Scattered Wastes. They share a given name and nothing else.

## 7 Where the cards disagree

1. **Baojin's Division.** Baojin's card: "Guild Accord, Frontier Vanguard Division". `Factions, Bloodlines & Institutions/The Guild Accord.md` lists five Divisions, Strategic, Enforcement, Arbitration, Research and Archives, and Logistics and Supply, with the Black Concord as "The sixth and unspoken Division." `The Guild Accord/The Concord Military.md` sets "The Vanguard Orders" under the Enforcement Division: "Elite strike teams responding to Wellspring breaches, Domain violations, and rogue Ascendant entities." No page has a Frontier Vanguard Division. This record calls him the Accord's Vanguard and names no Division for him.
2. **Kaien's Regency and his patron.** Kaien's card: "Regency Envoy (posting unnamed)" and "Lord Maerion Lysandre · His primary patron in the Regency he serves." Maerion's card sets him in the Arbitration Division's "Concord High Court", names no Regency and does not name Kaien. `The Guild Accord.md` has "Regional Envoys"; no page has a Regency Envoy. RULINGS.md, 2026-09-12, leaves "Kaien Veyren's Regency posting" among the spots leaning on an unnamed place after Mireya's strike.
3. **Whether Shael can advance.** Kaien's card: "Shael is sealed at his Stage and cannot advance."

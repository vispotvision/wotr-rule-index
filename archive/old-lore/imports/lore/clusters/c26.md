# c26 · The sainted, the fallen and the vessel

Six cards: a war relic made for a fallen royal house, a fae seer exiled into a place where stories rot, a bride held in a rite that never closed, the sovereign of an assassin's court, the mortal vessel of an Archon, and a pitch for twelve mortals the Archons imprint. Three of the six name one another, and Azure Serathiel's card names all three; he sits at Kharven in c03 and is this cluster's absent hub. Rhaevan carries Azure's surname, and no card ties them; Azelra shares an Archon with the pitch. No archived scene holds any of the six. Canon order holds throughout: Isaac's direction, then the card, then the archived scenes, then the wiki, then this record.

**Sources read.** The six cards in full, from the Volume I card folder of `wiki/`, and Azure Serathiel · The Sky-Veiled Saint in full, since his card names three of the six. The lines on other cards that touch a member: Orivane Caelthys · Astral Shepherd, Atheron Dreymarch · Prophet of the Unblinking and Moros Pellayne · The Ossuary Registrar (Emilia); Cyranthia Valez · The Gilded Marionette (Veyra); the Paragon titles on Vaelorian Theryn Aerethiel · The Last Elven Lord, Hiromi Mahuo and Cozbi Mahuo. Then the wiki. The pages read were `Cosmology & Metaphysics/The Fourteen Archons.md`; `The Fourteen Archons/Pair III · Will Against Desire.md`; `Factions, Bloodlines & Institutions/The Guild Accord.md`; `Factions, Bloodlines & Institutions/Sancta Lux.md`; `The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Limina · Entropy, Void and Mind.md` (Vantabriel) and `Caloria · Thermodynamics.md` (Catharsis); `Races & Peoples/The Elven Peoples.md`; `The Elven Peoples/The High Empire of Eresse.md`; `The Guild Accord/The Concord Citadel.md`; `Cosmology & Metaphysics/Errata to the Received Registers.md`; `Artifacts/Amaranth Severance · The Blooming Thorn.md`. Also c03's record of Azure and the notes in `imports/lore/_clusters.json`. No archived scene holds any member. The roster lists none, and a search of `scenes/` for every member's name, title and card place finds nothing. The eight scenes `scenes/CAST.md` files under Azure Serathiel all belong to Azure Tenrai Moto (c03, section 6).

**Era.** Rhaevan's card says the Withering Era, and so does the label on Azelra's card, whose body gives another era (section 7). This record sets the two events that hold them in the Withering Era, with no year number. Ambrose's, Emilia's and Evangelien's cards state no era. Their meetings come after the final siege of Elenthyr, the burning of the Violet Palace and Emilia's casting into the Tarnish, and this record gives them no era. The Fractured Dawn names its own Fractured Era, which no page sets against the Withering Era, and no event here reaches it.

## 1 Who is here

**Ambrose Virellith · The Gilded Thorn.** A weaponized Seraph and enchanted war relic, "A living blade adorned in silk and sorrow", neither male nor female; the card writes them as they. The Violet Court made Ambrose from a chrysalis of layered memories, as part of its forbidden Aethercraft. The Marionettist Order produced them from a coil reading and controlled them through Aetheric implants. Its remnants hunt them still. They served the House of Virellith, "a violet-blooded noble line of the Inner World", as a ceremonial war figure. They were raised to enchant enemy generals and then kill them in silent executions. When the bindings weakened, Ambrose fled during the burning of the Violet Palace, barefoot through ash, in a ceremonial dress meant only to be worn in death. They still wear it. They travel the Inner World aiding people in pain. They do not speak. Some circles of the Midveil Deathweavers revere them as a silent martyr. The Red Alchemist wants them restored to their weaponized state, and they evade him with sorrowful precision. Whoever harms them is marked, and the mark turns to guilt-ridden madness. Their wings, once opened, do not stop. Azure Serathiel was once ordered to execute them and spared them, and every new moon they leave a violet rose on his path. Nobody knows whose memories the chrysalis was layered from.

**Emilia Veirynthiel · The Bloom of Dissonant Realms.** A fallen fae, Pactborne Magus and Dreamsmoke Binder, neither male nor female. Emilia was the Mouth of Reflection of the Ceruleanth Court, a twilight fae lineage devoted to reverie and mooncraft. As a seer of the Faelands they showed what the soul pretended not to see. The High Fae sealed their tongue with threads of silence and cast them into the Tarnish Between Tales, "a space where stories go to rot." They did not die there. They wrote. Now they drift between realms and wander the Liminal Archive, making forbidden books from lived dreams, and the Ink-Eaten fear and admire them. They speak only through the smoke of their pipe and the stains that follow their robes. Ink leaks from their body, and whoever meets their gaze remembers something never lived. Their signature overwrites the account a room keeps of a person, and every pass takes some of their own record with it. A written record they have not touched stands against them, and the Accord's archives are the one place they do not go. They have never claimed the books are entirely theirs. Nobody knows whether the tongue was ever unsealed.

**Evangelien Rhymaris · The Hollow Bride.** Living Mourning, Apostle of the Sundered Dirge, called Saint Erasure by those who worship her. During the final siege of Elenthyr she was to be a vessel bride for a divine union. Her beloved was slain. The ritual went on, empty. Her soul was suspended in lamentation, neither dead nor ascended, and she is bound to a forgotten Wellspring deep beneath the Inner World's crust. She wanders the Inner World in wedding garments soaked with spectral ink and spiritual decay. Over time she began to weep blood. The blood comes from the pressure of being remembered by the land. She is herald of the lost Weeping Choir of Elenthyr. Fringe cults of Sancta Lux and the Thorn-Seers fear and worship her, the Flock of Thorns emulates her pain, and the Guild of Ritus calls her a metaphysical anomaly. A slow hymn follows her. The bereaved around her mourn their own dead, and all who look on her see someone they mourned. Blessings pass through her unfulfilled. Her tears heal or kill, and black-market mourn-priests harvest them. She has never attacked anyone. "I do not remember his name… but I still wear the veil. That is enough."

**Rhaevan Serathiel · The Velvet Executioner.** White Requiem, Lord of the Last Whisper, "He Who Ends Without Raising His Voice." He is a contract assassin of the Withering Era, and the founder and Sovereign of the Obsidian Veil, an assassin's court. He looked at his own fracture record and found nothing in it to deny. He filed it the way he files a contract, and founded the syndicate on the theory that most violence is simply badly organized. He studies people. He kills rarely and teaches when he does, and the explanation is for whoever is meant to learn from the death. Once he accepts a contract, events bend toward its completion. Witnesses misremember his face. His one stroke kills and thins what the people nearby remember of the dead, and every time it takes part of his own memory of the kill. He carries Serathiel's Ring, which binds life-force to contract terms, an ivory dagger called Politeness, and the Ledger of Quiet Names, whose ink only he can read. Nations deny hiring him and hire him again. Whoever he trains in person is the rare relationship that survives contact with him. Nobody knows why every nation that has hired him denies it in the same careful wording.

**Azelra Kaine · The Incendiant Veil.** Vessel of Veyra, Archon of Desire, and Keeper of the Seventh Flame, a title her card marks unattested. Her card makes her a Mortal Ascendant, Eressean-born, with ember-black hair, molten gold eyes veined with crimson and pale bronze skin fissured with heat lines. She was an arcanist of the Guild Accord's alchemical division. As a scholar she volunteered for the Veyric Resonance experiments, believing passion could be refined into a controllable energy source. The Rite of Veyric Resonance grafted her soul, and she became the experiments' avatar. The Accord marked her rogue; her card names both the Pyric Concord Incident and the Velthros Collapse and keeps both. Veyra speaks through her under extreme emotion. She cannot fully die. Her Essence rekindles where longing gathers. Where she walks, wars pause, lovers ruin themselves, and faith burns clean through doctrine. Every use erodes the memory of who she was, and her own desires are replaced by everyone else's. The Accord still hunts her, fearing she could reawaken the dormant Wellspring Vantabriel, and a rumoured second vessel, the Cold Flame, is meant to extinguish her. She carries the Crimson Lumen, a blade that reshapes with her heart rate, and wears the Ashen Veil and a crimson mantle fused with fragments of Accord-issue plating. Nobody knows whether Veyra's will through her is salvation or the seduction of mankind.

**The Fractured Dawn · Paragons of the Archons.** A Volume-level pitch, and its own note says it is not a character card: twelve unbuilt Paragons, four factions and a plot skeleton for a Volume VI. A Paragon is a mortal whose Soul Crystal has been permanently and involuntarily attuned to one Archon's domain. Each faces a Threshold that makes them a Warden or a Faultline, or leaves them Unresolved. Twelve of the Fourteen Archons have a Paragon here. Thalen and Valen have none. The story opens as twelve Archons, "long dormant, some in exile", begin resonating at once. It runs through the Resonance Bleed at the Wellspring Nihiloth, the Fracture Council, the Paragon War and the Threshold of All Things, with Tyzura Tyurkia, a World Spirit, as its elder witness. It sets itself where "the last order of the Great Spirit War's aftermath collapses into the Fractured Era." Its Paragon of Veyra is "A disgraced Praetori general (unattested register) who feels every soldier's want and pain", contested, and being consumed by others' desire. Each Paragon "still needs its own full character card, built the ordinary way, once given to a person."

## 3 The shared history

Five events. The first three are meetings the cards already make, given a place; the last two are new. The Fractured Dawn is in none of them: its era is unplaced, and it reaches Azelra through Veyra alone.

### The bow in the shrine at Elenthyr

**When.** After the final siege of Elenthyr, which left Evangelien walking, and after the burning of the Violet Palace, in the years Ambrose has walked the Inner World. Neither card gives an era, and this record gives none. The bow is on both cards; the shrine's place is new.

**Where.** The halls of a ruined shrine (both cards), among the ruins of Elenthyr in the Inner World. Evangelien's card has her wander the Inner World, and Azure Serathiel's card puts her in the ruins of Elenthyr. That the shrine stands among those ruins is this record's.

**Who.** Ambrose Virellith, Evangelien Rhymaris.

**What happened.** Ambrose came into the shrine in the ceremonial dress meant only to be worn in death. Evangelien was walking its halls in her wedding garments, soaked with spectral ink, and there was blood on her face from the tears. The hymn that follows her was in the halls. They met in the middle of a hall. Each bowed to the other. Ambrose does not speak, and Evangelien said nothing. They passed, and each went on the way the other had come. Her card says all who look upon her see someone they mourned; no record says whom Ambrose saw.

**What each took.** Ambrose went on walking the Inner World in the same dress. Evangelien went on walking the ruins, unchanged, since her card says she cannot be comforted and cannot be worsened.

### The tears bound into song

**When.** After the fall of Elenthyr, and after Emilia came out of the Tarnish Between Tales writing. The cards do not order it against the bow in the shrine. The binding is on both cards. Its place and manner are new.

**Where.** The ruins of Elenthyr in the Inner World, where Azure Serathiel's card puts Evangelien walking.

**Who.** Emilia Veirynthiel, Evangelien Rhymaris.

**What happened.** Emilia came to Elenthyr and found Evangelien walking the ruins, and walked beside her. Black-market mourn-priests harvest her tears and sell them. Emilia took them as they fell and sold none. Emilia bound them into a song and set it down in one of the forbidden books of the Liminal Archive. No bard has dared to sing it. From that day each has counted the other kin in unbeing.

**What each took.** Emilia took the tears and the song they became, and the song lies in the Archive among the books. Evangelien took the one person the cards give her who calls her kin, and those tears went into a book, out of the mourn-priests' reach.

### The mirror in the burned palace

**When.** After Emilia's casting into the Tarnish Between Tales, and after Ambrose fled the burning of the Violet Palace. The mirror and the name are on both cards; the place is new.

**Where.** The burned Violet Palace, seat of the Violet Court, in the Inner World. Ambrose's card makes the House of Virellith "a violet-blooded noble line of the Inner World", and the palace is known from that card alone.

**Who.** Ambrose Virellith, Emilia Veirynthiel.

**What happened.** Ambrose went back once to the burned Violet Palace, and Emilia was already in the ruin with the pipe lit. One mirror in the palace had come through the fire. They stood before it together. Emilia, once the Mouth of Reflection, did not look into it. Ambrose did not look into it either. Emilia named Ambrose there, in the smoke of the pipe, which is the way the card says Emilia speaks: The Beautiful Scar That Forgot Its Name. They left the mirror hanging where it was. Ambrose walked out of the palace a second time in the dress meant for the dead.

**What each took.** Ambrose took the name. Emilia took nothing from the palace for the books, and the mirror is the one thing there Emilia left unread.

### The alchemical division's file

**When.** The Withering Era, as the label on Azelra's card reads, while she was an arcanist of the alchemical division and before the Rite of Veyric Resonance grafted her soul. Her card's body gives another era (section 7). New.

**Where.** The alchemical division's archive, in the Guild Accord's keeping. Azelra's card names the division, no page attests it, and no card or page gives it a city. The Guild Accord page counts the Accord strong across the Inner World.

**Who.** Azelra Kaine, Ambrose Virellith.

**What happened.** The division kept a file on a war relic of the Violet Court. It held a coil reading of the relic's core, which the instrument read as coherent and could not trace to an origin. It held accounts from the courts whose generals the relic had killed. It set down a presence that lowered the defences of everyone near it and did not switch off, and a pull built to point outward at people who had not consented to it. Azelra read it. She believed passion could be refined into a controllable energy source, and she volunteered for the Veyric Resonance experiments. The rite grafted her soul. Veyra's lattice ran through it, and she became the experiments' avatar.

**What each took.** Azelra carried the Gilded Thorn's file into the experiments as the one case on record of a pull made on purpose, and came out of them with a pull that is vast and not entirely hers. Ambrose took nothing, and is one more reading of them kept by people they have never met.

### The contract on the Gilded Thorn

**When.** The Withering Era (Rhaevan's card), after the Obsidian Veil was founded, while Ambrose walked the Inner World after the burning of the Violet Palace. New.

**Where.** The Inner World, which Ambrose travels (Ambrose's card). Rhaevan's card names no seat for the Obsidian Veil, and this record gives none.

**Who.** Rhaevan Serathiel, Ambrose Virellith.

**What happened.** A house whose general the Gilded Thorn had killed, in the years Ambrose served the Violet Court, sent the Obsidian Veil an offer for the relic's death. With it came what the house knew. There were the executions of generals, the wings that do not stop once they open, and the men who had struck the relic and gone mad with guilt. Rhaevan read it. He did not accept the contract. He put the file in front of the student he was training in person and waited while the student read it. The student gave him the reason. Rhaevan returned the offer to the house with nothing added.

**What each took.** Rhaevan kept the file and has taught from it since, and the Ledger of Quiet Names holds no entry for the Gilded Thorn. No record says Ambrose ever learned of the offer.

## 7 Where the cards disagree

1. Azelra's era, card against itself. "Era: label reads Withering Era; card body reads Late Harmonial to Sylvaar Rebellion transition." The High Empire of Eresse page has the Sylvaar Rebellion Schism grow out of decisions made during the Great Spirit War, and The Concord Citadel page has the Heart Vault crystallise "after the Harmonial Age". The body's era then falls before the Accord she served as an arcanist, and near the Great Spirit War's aftermath where the Fractured Dawn opens. This record follows the label and leaves the body standing.

2. Azelra's rogue marking, card against itself. "Declared a Rogue Entity after the Pyric Concord Incident; marked rogue after the Velthros Collapse", and "(both unattested; the card gives both names and the sheet keeps both)". This record names neither incident in its events.

3. Azelra's division, card against the Guild Accord page. The card: "styled Division V (Energetics) on the card", marked "(unattested)". The Guild Accord page lists the Divisions as Strategic, Enforcement, Arbitration, Research & Archives and Logistics & Supply, with the Black Concord as "the sixth and unspoken Division"; none is alchemical or named Energetics. This record calls it the alchemical division her card names.

4. Veyra, card against pitch. The pitch has twelve Archons, "long dormant, some in exile", begin resonating at once, and says "The Imprint is involuntary." Its one Paragon of Veyra is "A disgraced Praetori general". Azelra's card: Veyra "Speaks through her"; she "volunteered for the Veyric Resonance experiments"; and it carries "A rumoured second vessel".

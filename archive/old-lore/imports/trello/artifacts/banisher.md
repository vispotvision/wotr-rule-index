**Banisher**

- **Name:** *Banisher* (“Writ-Spear of Exile”)  
- **Type:** Weapon / Divine Tool (Sealwright make; serves as a Domain Anchor when planted)  
- **Appearance:** A red-brown, wedge-bladed polearm with carved law-sigils running its length, ringed by three iron loops from which black oath-chains hang. The head ends in a pronged spike; the butt is hooked like a bailiff’s crook for yanking. When invoked, the grooves glow ember-gold and the chains stiffen as if remembering old arrests.  
- **Origin:** Forged in the Neutral Chambers under the Arbitration Division from oath-iron and verdict ash, then tempered within a sealed **Ur→Ma→Uur** rite by Sealwright teams. Each ring was added after a successful extradition, binding precedent into the metal.  
- **Essence Alignment:** Vectra (space/force) + Vocatia/Ritus (oath) with a **stable, law-weighted** pressure profile.  
- **Parun Glyph(s):** **Ur** (Balance, prime), **Ma** (Memory) to record names and jurisdictions, **Uur** (Disruption) to sever anchors, trace **Wy** (Motion) for chain cast and reel.  
- **Effects:**  
  - **Exile Mark:** A thrust that scores a brief sigil onto the target’s lattice; on the third heartbeat the mark enforces **Topology Collapse** that ejects the target along a bound vector to the designated exile locus (outside Domain or through a Concord Gate).  
  - **Jurisdiction Chains:** The rings spit black law-chains that bind limbs; on command they tighten, imposing **Vector Severance** on Teleport/Phase effects.  
  - **Gate-Nail:** Driving the prong into a threshold pins it, triggering **Anchor Collapse** and sealing rifts or illegal Domains for one day and night.  
  - **Articles of Exclusion:** Speaking a named Writ while the Banisher is planted creates a circle of authority; enemies within suffer **Interference Lock** on projection and cannot re-enter once pushed out.  
  - **Contempt Shock:** If a banished entity attempts return before the term, the weapon lashes a remote **Overpressure Discharge** along the Mark, staggering them at the boundary.  
- **Known Limitations:**  
  - Full banishment requires an active Writ or sworn authority; without it, effects reduce to stun and bind.  
  - Entities anchored by sovereign Domains or Mythic Cores can resist, reducing Exile Mark to forced displacement only.  
  - Each Gate-Nail consumes a ring’s charge; three uses before re-sanctification in a Neutral Chamber.  
  - Miscast or perjured use rebounds as **Pressure Reversal** on the wielder’s arm nodes.  
- **Soul Affinity:** Retentive or Balance-leaning bodies; highest coherence with Arbitration Marshals and E.D. Vanguard Sealwrights at Temperance **Flourishing→Severance**.  
- **Historical Usage:** Wielded in the Caedor hearings to expel three Rot lieutenants during *The Blooming Silence*; later used to shutter a rogue Domain over Dorrosfenn’s quay.  
- **Current Wielder / Location:** Armory of the Neutral Chambers (Arbitration Division); issued under seal to Accord marshals for extraditions and Domain closures.  
- **Mythic Reputation:** “The court’s last word.” Street whisper: *When Banisher points, the door appears.*  
- **First Appearance (Volume/Chapter):** *Inclusion Saga — The Blooming Silence, Ch. 9 (“Articles at Speartip”)*

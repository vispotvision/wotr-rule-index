## Kibanda cha Mwanga wa Miti

**Name:**
**Kibanda cha Mwanga wa Miti** — _Lantern of the Grove’s Light_

---

**Type:**
Relic | Domain Anchor | Illumination Tool

---

**Appearance:**
A hand-held lantern of woven goldwood roots, each filament polished until it gleams like amber. At its center floats a tear-shaped crystal, suspended in midair within the frame by thin strands of glowing Essence. The crystal’s light shifts in hue depending on the surrounding Wellspring saturation — emerald in fertile ground, amber in neutral terrain, and pale silver in sacred groves. Small Parun glyphs spiral along the frame, flickering with soft light when activated.

---

**Origin:**
Forged in the **Sanctum of Heartlight** deep within Lurien, using a soul-fragmented gem taken from the Heartbloom Tree’s first flower. Chimwala anchored the lantern’s core to his own Domain, allowing it to manifest pure, untainted light from his Wellspring.

---

**Essence Alignment:**
Nature-Aether / Spiritual-Aether

---

**Parun Glyph(s):**
Lu (Light) | Ur (Balance) | Fa (Growth)

---

**Effects:**

- Emits **Sanctuary Light** that nullifies minor corruption and wards off low-tier corrupted spirits within a 20-meter radius.
- Can project a **Pathway Beam** — a concentrated line of light that reveals hidden glyphs, concealed traps, or Essence flows.
- In Chimwala’s Domain, it causes surrounding plants to bloom instantly, even in total darkness.

---

**Known Limitations:**

- Cannot function underground without a tether to Chimwala’s Essence or an uncorrupted Wellspring.
- The crystal dims if exposed to corrupted Essence for prolonged periods.

---

**Soul Affinity:**
Chimwala N’Doro — bondable by any Grovekeeper through the Rite of the First Light.

---

**Historical Usage:**
Used to guide the Verdant Covenant through the **Fog of Black Roots** during the cleansing of the Mireya Wellspring.

---

**Current Wielder / Location:**
Carried by Chimwala during all Wellspring-cleansing expeditions.

---

**Mythic Reputation:**
Legends say its light can make even stone remember the first dawn.

---

**First Appearance (Volume/Chapter):**
_WOTR: Book V – The Blooming Siege of Caedor, Ch. 19_

##  Mkono wa Miti

**Name:**
Mkono wa Miti — _Bracer of the Living Grove_

---

**Type:**
Relic | Soulbound | Defensive Tool

---

**Appearance:**
A forearm bracer seemingly grown rather than forged — layers of living bark, smooth to the touch yet stronger than bronze, curve seamlessly around the arm. Along the grain, moss-like Essence fibers pulse with soft green luminescence. Embedded into the bracer are shards of amber containing preserved seeds and tiny spirit-insects, each shard engraved with Parun glyphs.

---

**Origin:**
Cultivated from a sapling of the **Heartbloom Tree**, a Wellspring-born species that bonds with its caretaker. Chimwala nurtured the bracer from seed, binding it to his Essence through years of symbiotic growth until it hardened into permanent armor.

---

**Essence Alignment:**
Nature-Aether / Harmonic-Aether

---

**Parun Glyph(s):**
Ur (Balance) | Lo (Shield) | Ma (Memory)

---

**Effects:**

- Absorbs kinetic impact by transferring force into the ground via root-like Essence channels.
- Can sprout a temporary bark-shield from the forearm to block attacks or create cover.
- When in contact with soil, it accelerates natural regeneration for both the wearer and nearby allies.

---

**Known Limitations:**

- The bracer requires periodic “feeding” with sunlight, water, and Essence from fertile ground.
- In barren or corrupted terrain, its regeneration functions weaken drastically.

---

**Soul Affinity:**
Chimwala N’Doro — cannot be worn by another without severing its living bond, which would kill the artifact.

---

**Historical Usage:**
First deployed during the **Battle of the Thorned Crossing**, where Chimwala used it to shield three wounded druids from a volley of spectral javelins.

---

**Current Wielder / Location:**
Permanently attached to Chimwala’s left forearm.

---

**Mythic Reputation:**
Said to “breathe” in harmony with its wearer, its moss pulsing in time with his heartbeat.

---

**First Appearance (Volume/Chapter):**
_WOTR: Book III – Songs Beneath the Canopy, Ch. 9_

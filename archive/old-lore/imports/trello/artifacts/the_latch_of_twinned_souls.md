## **Artifact Entry — The Latch of Twinned Souls**

**Name:**
**The Latch of Twinned Souls**

**Type:**
Soulbound / Relic / Projection Focus

**Appearance:**
A silver ring shaped as two interlocking loops, one polished to a mirror sheen, the other etched with flowing Ma glyph patterns. When Karo channels Essence into it, the ring emits a faint ripple in the air, as though space itself were briefly bent between his hand and his projection.

**Origin:**
Forged from fragments of a failed Domain anchor found in the ruins of an abandoned Archonic trial site. Reworked by Karo using Mirrorwell Prism resonance mapping to stabilize tether transitions.

**Essence Alignment:**
Spiritual Essence (Primary)
Cognitive Essence (Secondary)

**Parun Glyph(s):**
+Ma (Memory)
+Wy (Motion)

**Effects:**

* Reduces **initial AU cost** to summon a projection by 20%.
* Allows instant tether establishment, letting Karo control new projections the moment they manifest without a “stabilization” period.
* Can create a **dual-tether mode**, binding Karo’s senses fully to a projection for maximum precision.

**Known Limitations:**

* Dual-tether mode transfers **all sensory feedback** to Karo — any damage or collapse trauma suffered by the projection is experienced directly.
* If the Latch is forcibly removed or broken during projection use, the tether may snap violently, causing Essence backlash and possible unconsciousness.
* Prolonged dual-tethering risks “projection bleed,” where echoes of the projection’s perspective persist even after dismissal.

**Soul Affinity:**
Perfectly attuned to Karo’s Medium Core; incompatible with most Essence types without heavy recalibration.

**Historical Usage:**
Believed to have been used by an early Projection Duel champion in the Eressan Arcane Games before being lost in an expedition to the Archonic trial site.

**Current Wielder / Location:**
Karo Venrik — Wears it constantly on his left index finger during combat and experimentation.

**Mythic Reputation:**
Known in summoning circles as the “Projection’s Hand” — a symbol of mastery for those who can endure the full sensory bond.

**First Appearance (Volume/Chapter):**
*WOTR: The Legacies* — First used during the defense of Mirrorwater Hall, where Karo tethered fully to an Echo construct to outmaneuver an enemy magus.

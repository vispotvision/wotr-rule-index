## Upanga wa Msimu Nne

**Name:**
**Upanga wa Msimu Nne** — _Blade of the Four Seasons_

---

**Type:**
Relic | Weapon | Ritual Tool

---

**Appearance:**
A long, curved leaf-blade forged from a rare greensteel alloy that reflects light in shifting seasonal hues — emerald in spring, gold in summer, crimson in autumn, and silver-white in winter. Its spine is etched with continuous Parun glyph script, each word pulsing faintly as the seasons change. The hilt is wrapped in braided vine-fiber, with a pommel containing a seed-shaped gem that slowly rotates in place, surrounded by faint motes of pollen-like Essence.

---

**Origin:**
Gifted to Chimwala by the Sylvaar Moonbound Orders as a mark of trust between Lurien and the Sylvaar following the **Treaty of Green Sky**. The blade was forged using greensteel from the Sylvaar deepforges and tempered in the Edenis Wellspring’s waters under the light of all four moons.

---

**Essence Alignment:**
Nature-Aether / Harmonic-Aether / Seasonal Flux

---

**Parun Glyph(s):**
Fa (Growth) | Ur (Balance) | Wy (Motion) | Sa (Rhythm)

---

**Effects:**

- **Season Shift:** The blade’s properties change with its current seasonal attunement:
  - _Spring:_ Healing touch — wounds sealed with a cut bloom with living scar tissue.
  - _Summer:_ Amplifies flame and heat-based attacks.
  - _Autumn:_ Saps Essence from enemies and returns it to the wielder’s reserves.
  - _Winter:_ Inflicts frostbite and slows enemy movements.
- Can perform the **Cycle Severance** ritual — cutting corruption from a land or Wellspring over four days, one day per season.

---

**Known Limitations:**

- Changing the seasonal attunement outside of natural flow requires a taxing Essence ritual.
- The blade refuses to harm pure Wellspring-born beings unless they are corrupted.

---

**Soul Affinity:**
Chimwala N’Doro — bondable to any Grovekeeper who completes the Rite of the Turning Leaf.

---

**Historical Usage:**
Wielded by Chimwala during the **Reclamation of the Rotwood**, where each season’s power was invoked in succession to cleanse the entire forest.

---

**Current Wielder / Location:**
Strapped across Chimwala’s back during most expeditions, used sparingly in battle to avoid overtaxing the land’s seasonal cycle.

---

**Mythic Reputation:**
Said to carry the scent of whatever season it currently embodies, and to cause nearby foliage to subtly shift toward that season’s form.

---

**First Appearance (Volume/Chapter):**
_WOTR: Book VI – The Reclamation of Rotwood, Ch. 7_

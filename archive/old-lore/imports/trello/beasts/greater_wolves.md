## **Greater Wolf**

Greater Wolf
Known in Old Sylvaar as _Vael’kar_ (“Shadow Howl”) and among frontier hunters as “The Silver Fang.”

---

**Type:**
Natural Beast — Apex Predator

---

**Origin:**
Greater Wolves are natural-born hunters, evolved from mortal wolf stock but heavily influenced by Harmonic Wellsprings, which granted them heightened intelligence, endurance, and pack coordination. Their origins trace back to the primal forests of the Innerworld and Material World borderlands, where Wellspring energy enriched the wilds.

---

**Essence Affinity:**
Primarily Harmonic Wellsprings with minor Ancestral resonance tied to their pack lineage.

---

**Realm Affiliation:**
Native to dense forests and mountainous borderlands of the Innerworld and Material World, with roaming packs sometimes venturing into Outer World wilds.

---

**Temperament & Behavior:**
Highly social and cooperative hunters, Greater Wolves rely on coordinated pack tactics and relentless pursuit. Packs are tightly-knit, governed by alpha pairs, and exhibit complex communication via howl patterns, body language, and Essence signals. They fiercely defend territory and young, often challenging rival packs or even humanoid hunters encroaching on their range.

---

**Appearance:**
Larger and more muscular than typical wolves, with dense fur that ranges from silver-gray to deep charcoal, often mottled with patches of white or black. Their eyes gleam with intelligent amber or icy blue hues, and their movements are fluid and predatory. Some packs bear faint Essence markings that glow subtly during hunts.

---

**Abilities & Powers:**

- **Coordinated Hunt:** Exceptional pack tactics that can flank, trap, and exhaust prey.
- **Essence Howl:** A piercing howl that can disorient or weaken foes, sometimes used to summon pack members over great distances.
- **Enhanced Senses:** Heightened smell, hearing, and night vision amplified by Wellspring resonance.
- **Pack Bond:** Shared Essence link that enhances coordination and allows rapid information transfer between members.

---

**Danger Tier:**
T3 — Moderate Threat.
Individually dangerous to unarmed mortals; packs can challenge armed groups and disrupt hunting parties or caravans.

---

**Weaknesses:**

- Vulnerable when separated from pack or during full moon shifts when Essence flux fluctuates.
- Reluctant to engage heavily armored foes without support.
- Sensitive to Essence-disrupting magic or scent-blocking wards.

---

**Historical Encounters:**
The _Silverfang Hunts_ of the Innerworld recount fierce battles between Greater Wolf packs and Imperial scouts. Packs have been known to ally with sylvan rangers or druidic circles to defend sacred groves.

---

**Cultural Significance:**
Greater Wolves are respected and sometimes worshipped by forest tribes as symbols of unity, strength, and the wild’s relentless spirit. Some human hunters seek to tame or bond with them as loyal companions, though such efforts are rare and perilous.

---

**First Appearance (Volume/Chapter):**
_WOTR – Book I: “Howls of the Wilderwood”_

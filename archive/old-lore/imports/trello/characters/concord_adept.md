# AZRAN NEMEIR

**Alias / Title:** The Gilded Draft - Concord Adept, Research Archives Division
**Also Known As:** The Mender's Hand - The Lattice's Younger Son
**Epithet:** He Who Mends What Law Forgot to Mourn

***

### I. IDENTITY & CORE PROFILE

- **Race / Lineage:** Mortal Human - Wellspring-Touched Lineage (minor resonance inheritance from a guild-blooded mother)
  - **Faction / Order / Era:** Guild Accord, Research Archives Division - Modern Era (Era of Convergence)
- **Temperance Stage:** Stage 3 - Ascension (The point where Path bias crystallizes; Azran's Spirit Path is dominant but Body Path threads run beneath like fault lines waiting to fracture.)
- **Profile Summary:** Azran Nemeir is 26 years old - young, restless, and ache-bright with conviction. He joined the Guild Accord at 17, drove through E-Tier and D-Tier commissions on raw determination and surgical casting precision, and sits now at C-Tier rank, performing T4 commissions under the Research Archives Division. His goal isn't glory. It's correction. He believes the world is broken in specific, fixable ways, and he intends to fix them - one stabilized Wellspring, one sealed corruption vein, one mended community at a time. His Aether leans toward Spirare and Formare - precise, controlled, structured. He does not waste. He does not flair. His casting looks like a scholar writing in the air.
- **Key Quote:** "Power that can't explain its reason doesn't deserve its reach."

***

### II. CANONICAL SYSTEM METRICS

- **Aether Class:** Weaver - Spirit Path Dominant
  - **Behavior:** Azran's Aether manifests as structured lattice-light - thin, geometric, iridescent white-gold thread-lines that spool from his hands and anchor into space around him. Spells don't explode. They click into place, like glyphs being written correctly for the first time.
  - **Risk:** Lattice Overreach - if he weaves too many concurrent structures without cycling Spirare between casts, the lattice geometry collapses inward. Not an explosion. A silence. A total Draft failure that leaves him casting-blind for minutes, his Soul Crystal ringing like a struck bell no one else can hear.
- **Wellspring Affinity:**
  - **Triune Core:** Luminalis (Heaven/Light - judgement, clarity, refinement) - Verdantia (Earth/Life - healing, growth, sustenance) - Phreatis (Water/Spirit - memory, flow, emotional truth)
  - **Network:** 3 Wellsprings currently harmonized into his Soul Crystal. Parun Glyphs inscribed for all three.
  - **Targeted Uninfected:** 0 actively pursued - Azran won't chase new harmonization before Stage 5. He's read the cost ledger. He respects it.
- **Temperance Path:** Azran climbed Stage 1 through sheer study - late nights in guild libraries, Essence theory texts worn to soft corners. Stage 2 came when he watched a corrupted Wellspring site swallow a village's water supply and did something about it himself. Stage 3 unlocked during a T4 Commission where his party leader went down and Azran held the stabilization circle for two hours solo, Phreatis-flow threading through cracked stone, light sweat becoming soaked cloth, the Wellspring's hum becoming something that lived inside his sternum rather than beside it. He didn't celebrate. He wrote the incident report first.

***

### III. SOUL PHYSICS & COMBAT SCALE

- **Global Tier:** C-Tier - Concord Adept. The first true weight of Accord law sits on his shoulders. He's capable. He knows it. He doesn't flaunt it.
- **Path Bias:** Spirit Path (68%) - Body Path (22%) - Attraction Path (10%)
- **Appearance:** Height: 5'11"; Build: lean, deliberate - the build of someone who trains because precision requires a body that listens, not because he wants to be impressive. Skin: deep brown, sun-warmed. Eyes: pale silver-grey, uncommon enough to earn stares, calm enough to hold them. Hair: loose white-silver waves, natural - a Wellspring resonance marker that appeared at 19 and hasn't darkened since. Typically wears layered white-and-gold guild robes draped over a deep indigo base. A red sash, knotted at the hip, functional.

#### A. The Five Pillars (Soul Physics)

> Essence is Volume. Flux is Pressure. Output is Speed. Efficiency is Clarity. Aura is Gravity.

- **Essence Capacity (Volume):** 4,200 EU - solid for C-Tier; not excessive. He built this through patience, not shortcuts.
- **Flux Density (Internal Pressure):** 320 EU/g - high for his tier. His casting is scalpel-precise; what he throws, it enters defenses rather than battering them.
- **Aether Output (Flow Rate):** 180 AU/s - controlled, not explosive. He's not fast. He's exact.
- **Aetheric Efficiency (n):** 0.87 - superconductor discipline. He wastes almost nothing. Every Draft is shaped cleanly.
- **Attraction Scale (Aura/Presence):** D-Tier: Resonant Halo - ambient. Those with Aether sensitivity feel a soft, clarifying pressure around him, like standing near a Wellspring mouth. Not dominant. Not demanding. Simply present.

#### B. Key Combat Parameters

- **Strike Force:** 0.004 GN / 0.8 PJ - not a brawler. His physical strikes are trained but unremarkable.
- **Durability:** 0.6 PJ (Lattice Shielding) / 18 GJ (Physical Hardness) - Spirit Path barriers are his armor. Thin but geometrically efficient.
- **Speed:** Mach 4.2 reaction-burst (precision movement windows only, not sustained)
- **Range:** Visual - up to 80 meters clean. 120 meters with Luminalis thread anchors pre-placed.

### IV. AXIOMATIC MANIFESTATIONS

#### A. Axiomatic Traits (Passive)

- **Lattice Memory:** Azran's Soul Crystal retains a geometric impression of any Wellspring-corrupted structure he has cleansed. He can re-access the memory-pattern of a prior corruption configuration without losing Essence to re-scan. Essentially: the second time he encounters a specific corruption type, his casting cost drops by 25%.
- **Verdantia Undertow:** Verdantia resonance bleeds passively into his immediate environment. Plants near Azran grow slightly faster. Wounds on people beside him close at marginally accelerated rates. Nothing dramatic - the Wellspring is subtle at Stage 3. But people standing near him in the aftermath of violence feel the air taste slightly less like iron.

#### B. Core Disciplines (Active)

- **Sigil Stitch** (Cost: 180 EU)
  - _Mechanic:_ Azran traces a seven-point Parun Glyph configuration in the air - his right hand moving in measured arcs, each line glowing white-gold as Luminalis-threaded Aether crystallizes the geometry. The finished structure anchors to a surface or a wound and holds, stitching closed Essence leaks, sealing minor corruption veins, or reinforcing cracked Soul Crystal lattice in allies. Not instantaneous - takes 4 seconds of uninterrupted Formare work.
  - _Application:_ Luminalis Parun Script provides the glyph backbone; Phreatis flow seeps into the emotional residue of the damage site, reading what hurt and why, ensuring the seal doesn't just close the gap but understands it. In combat, it can be applied as a disruption seal on an opponent's active Draft - mid-chain counterspell during their Aether Shaping stage.

- **Threaded Refraction** (Cost: 310 EU)
  - _Mechanic:_ Azran releases a web of Luminalis thread-lines from both hands simultaneously, which spool through a 12-meter radius and lock into existing Aether structures in the environment - enemy wards, active Drafts, ambient Wellspring leakage. He then refracts the collected Aether, redirecting it into a single, concentrated geometric lance or scattering it harmlessly across the ground. His choice. He always pauses for one breath before choosing.
  - _Application:_ Effectively steals and redirects hostile magical energy. Not a counterspell - closer to Aether judo. The more the enemy is casting, the more he has to work with. Against mundane opponents or bare-Essence fighters it's nearly ineffective.

#### C. Costs & Consequences

- **Lattice Fatigue:** Every cast above 250 EU tightens the thread geometry in his forearms - visible as faint glowing lines that run wrist-to-elbow while he's working. After 4+ heavy casts in an encounter, those lines begin to ache. After 6, they burn. After 8, his efficiency drops to 0.62 and his hands start shaking between Formare gestures.
- **Phreatis Bleed:** Extended Phreatis-based work (healing, corruption cleansing, emotional-residue reading) leaves Azran emotionally saturated. He doesn't cry or panic - he simply goes quiet. Unusually quiet. For hours. People who know him learn to read this and leave him alone with tea and a window. People who don't know him misread it as coldness.

### V. RELICS & DOMAIN

- **Current Domain:** None - Stage 3 is too early. The territory is budding though. In places where Azran has held Spirare long enough under stress, the air carries a faint luminous undertone after he leaves. The Wellspring remembers. So does the stone.
- **Relics & Implements:**
  - **The Codex Filament:** A slim journal reinforced with Glyph-Receptive Alloy binding. Every corrupted Wellspring pattern Azran has personally cleansed is etched into its pages in his own Parun Script - not written, inscribed. The filament serves as a T4 focus item, reducing the EU cost of Sigil Stitch by 40 EU when the target corruption type matches a prior entry.
  - **Resonant Sandals (Vernaid-style, ankle-clasped):** A minor guild-grade relic. Each gold ankle clasp carries a Phreatis attunement sigil that keeps Azran's proprioceptive sense calibrated during high Aether environments. Basically: his footing never goes wrong in a Wellspring-active zone, even when the ground itself is metaphysically unstable.

### VI. LORE & TRAJECTORY

- **Allegiance:** Guild Accord - Research Archives Division. Registered C-Tier Concord Agent. Lattice signature on file. Oath of Merit signed, Imperial Year 43.
- **Incident Records (Historical):**
  - **The Farath Vein Incident:** During a T4 Commission in the Farath basin, Azran's party leader suffered a lattice fissure mid-operation. Azran held a Phreatis-Luminalis dual stabilization channel for 97 minutes solo, preventing three Wellspring-adjacent settlements from losing water access permanently. Elevation to C-Tier formally recognized this action. The ache in his left arm from the extended cast lasted two weeks.
  - **The Dust Vault Recovery:** Retrieved a sealed T5 relic from a collapsed guild vault, outpacing a competing retrieval team by adapting Sigil Stitch into a structural anchoring technique that hadn't appeared in any prior filed commission report. The Archives Division flagged the technique variant. He was asked to write up his methodology. He submitted 11 pages.
- **Counters & Weaknesses:**
  - **Disruption During Formare:** Azran's casting window is 3-5 seconds on heavy Disciplines. Close-range physical pressure during Aether Shaping breaks his geometry instantly. A fighter who can close the gap before Manifestation is his most dangerous matchup.
  - **Emotion-Saturated Environments:** Battlefields thick with recent death, grief-impregnated ruins, or Mortalis-adjacent Wellspring influence overloads his Phreatis channel. He can still cast - but Sigil Stitch in these conditions leaves residue on him. The Bleed comes faster. The quiet comes sooner.
- **Current Status:** Active - based out of a mid-tier guild hall in the Eastern Reaches. Currently assigned a three-person research cell operating on T4 escalation missions.
- **Trajectory:** Azran wants Stage 4 - Flourishing. He can feel the edge of it when he holds Spirare long enough, the moment where his breath and the Lattice pulse in the same rhythm. It's not ambition. It's a quiet, obdurate need to be more useful. He doesn't say that out loud. He writes it in the margins of his Codex Filament in small, precise Parun Script, on the pages nobody else will ever read.

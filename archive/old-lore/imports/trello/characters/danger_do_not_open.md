### **Name / Alias / Title:**

**Rik’Lye** — _The Forbidden Vessel_

### **Epithet:**

_“DANGER — DO NOT OPEN.”_

### **Race / Lineage:**

Alchemical Construct (Aether-Reactive Homunculus)
Forged through the lost practice of **Transmutatio Mortalis**, an experiment to recreate a soul from fractured Essence.

### **Faction / Order / Era:**

Unaligned — Created in the laboratories beneath **Niran’s Sanctum**
Era: _The Withering Age, post-VE 920_

### **Temperance Stage:**

**Stage IX — Reflection** (borderline unstable, oscillating between _Reflection_ and _Contrition_)

### **Aether Class:**

**Hybrid-IV — Abyssal Conduit** (Soul artificially stabilized by alchemical matrices rather than divine alignment)

### **Wellspring Affinity:**

**Transmutatio · Mortalis · Abython** — symbol of rebirth through decay, the rejection of natural reincarnation.

### **Current Domain:**

**The Crucible Flesh** — a mutable alchemical field that dissolves the boundary between matter and Essence; living bodies within are rewritten cell by cell into their “intended form.”

### **Status:**

Contained by Guild Accord mandate under _Crimson-Seal Order_.
Subject is considered **alive**, **aware**, and **uncontainable** if directly provoked.

---

## **I. Profile Summary**

Rik’Lye is not a man but a reaction — the afterthought of Niran’s most desperate experiment.
His body is a lattice of reformed human tissue, interwoven with Aether-binding chains and saturated in Mercurial Essence. His consciousness flickers like a lantern behind thick glass, sometimes human, often something far older.

He does not bleed; his Essence leaks in mercury streams that coil upward into sigils of containment. He hums when angry — a resonance too harmonic to be natural.

---

## **II. Appearance**

Towering and lean, his form carries the unsettling symmetry of deliberate creation.
Ash-pale skin marked by alchemical glyphs that pulse faintly with red or blue light depending on mood.
Eyes: molten silver irises with black sclera, flecked by fractal patterns of Aether corrosion.
Hair: black with indigo sheen, ending in flame-like tufts; horn structures grown from crystallized Aetheric bone curve backward like broken crescents.
Chains hang from his shoulders and waist — not for binding, but for balance.

The markings across his trousers spell _破礼存_ — “the breaking that preserves.”

## **IV. Temperance Path**

Walks the **Path of Self-Dissolution**, a heretical Temperance modeled by Niran to accelerate artificial enlightenment.
Every moment of awareness erodes his containment sigils; self-understanding equals physical decay.
He embodies the Reflection paradox — an imitation soul staring back at its maker.

---

## **V. Aether Class**

**Abyssal Conduit:**
Channels Essence directly from sub-dimensional strata (the Veins of Abython).
This renders his power theoretically infinite but also subject to existential corruption.
He cannot generate Aether — he steals it from below.

---

## **VI. Wellspring Alignment**

- **Transmutatio** — governs molecular rewriting; he can melt or restructure matter through touch.
- **Mortalis** — sustains pseudo-life through forced Essence reformation.
- **Abython** — binds him to the undercurrents of Void resonance, giving him partial immunity to destruction.

Combined, these Wellsprings make Rik’Lye a walking paradox: creation through annihilation.

---

## **VII. Division / Rank / Allegiance**

None. Classified by the Guild Accord as an **Alchemical Anomaly of the Ninth Order.**
Niran listed his existence as _“experimental containment success,”_ a lie even the Guild refused to record openly.

---

## **VIII. Personality & Core Themes**

At times eerily serene, at others feral — Rik’Lye mirrors the emotions of those around him.
Speech carries the cadence of old scripture, as though reading thoughts he doesn’t understand.
He calls Niran _“the False Mother.”_
Themes: creation without purpose · reflection as torment · the sin of imitation.

---

## **IX. Soul Crystal Profile**

- **Type:** Synthetic Fracture Core
- **Form:** Obsidian helix surrounding a hollow light well
- **Resonance Behavior:** Drains Essence instead of radiating it; contact destabilizes Temperance fields.
- **Phenomenon:** Emits faint choral murmurs audible only during Wellspring invocation.

---

## **X. Traits**

- **Essence Inversion:** Can reverse the polarity of Aetheric energy, nullifying Wellspring effects.
- **Reforged Flesh:** Limbs can dissolve and reform from ambient matter.
- **Chain Memory:** Each chain link holds a fragment of stored thought; breaking one erases memory permanently.

---

## **XI. Relics & Implements**

**“The Philosopher’s Chains”** —
Forged by Niran from her own binding glyphs; tether his Essence across planes.
If removed, Rik’Lye’s containment fails, causing an uncontrolled transmutation surge.

---

## **XII. Abilities & Disciplines**

- **Alchemical Conversion Field:** Converts anything touched into base Essence.
- **Echo of Abython:** Briefly opens a tear to the Void Wellspring, erasing entities within five meters.
- **Soul Rebind:** Reconstructs destroyed tissue by consuming latent Essence around him.
- **Reversal Law:** For every wound healed, one memory is lost.

---

## **XIII. Costs & Consequences**

- The more he uses Transmutatio, the more unstable his pseudo-soul becomes.
- Excess exposure to human emotion causes molecular breakdown.
- Cannot cross sanctified or divine Wellspring zones; Essence implosion risk.

---

## **XIV. Associates & Bonds**

- **Niran (The Alchemer):** Creator and mother-figure. Currently missing after Abython breach.
- **Kaelric Varnier:** Briefly observed Rik’Lye’s awakening; his reflections reacted violently to Rik’Lye’s presence.
- **No recorded allies.**

## **XV. Incident Records**

**“Containment Breach – Abython Reaction (VE: 926):”**
Upon awakening, Rik’Lye’s containment chamber liquefied into living mercury. Five researchers were transmuted into crystal pillars that still hum beneath the Accord Citadel.
Niran disappeared shortly thereafter.

---

## **XVI. Counters & Weaknesses**

- Purified Wellsprings (Ascensio / Fixatio) disrupt his alchemical cohesion.
- His artificial soul cannot regenerate emotion — psychological manipulation easily fractures his will.
- Disconnection from Abython flux causes Essence starvation and collapse within 12 hours.

---

## **XVII. Hooks & Foreshadowing**

- The Guild fears Niran encoded her consciousness within Rik’Lye’s Core.
- His instability suggests a dormant Titan-aligned pattern — possibly linked to **Zar’thuun, Titan of Abythron.**
- Prophecy tablets recovered from the Sanctum read: _“When the Vessel learns sorrow, the Wellspring will bleed.”_

## **XIX. Current Status / Arc Direction**

Rik’Lye wanders the lower Wellspring ruins, seeking fragments of Niran’s final theorem.
Each act of mercy erases a piece of his mind, but each act of violence brings him closer to wholeness.
His arc leads toward **Stage X — Realization**, where he must choose: destroy the false life he was given, or evolve into a being the Wellsprings never intended — the first **Alchemical Archon**.

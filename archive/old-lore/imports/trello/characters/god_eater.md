# **RAELEN VOR’SKAI**  
(Pronounced: *RAY-len vor-SKY*)

Alias / Title: **The Gilded Scourge**  
Also Known As: The White-Jackal · Sand-Vein Seraph · Gold-Eater  
Epithet: **He Who Bleeds in Silence**

────────────────────────────  
# **I. Identity & Core Profile**

Race / Lineage: **Mortal Ascendant** (Desert-blooded lineage with eldritch inscription)  
Faction / Order / Era: **Independent Operative — Imperial Calendar**  
Temperance Stage: **VII — Refraction** (Dual Sight)

Profile Summary:  
Raelen Vor’Skai is a wandering inscription-bearer whose glyph-bound hand houses a Wellspring contract he once attempted to destroy. The surviving fragment twisted itself into a living mark, feeding on pain, gold, and emotional residue. Raelen’s life is a constant negotiation between who he is and what the tattoo wants him to become.

Key Quote:  
“**Pain is the only truth that never lies. I’ve made peace with that.**”

────────────────────────────  
# **II. Canonical System Metrics**

Aether Class: **Sable-Mark Channeler** — Inscription Vector  
Behavior: Directs Aether through nerve-etchings, allowing silent, instantaneous discharge of shadow pressure.  
Risk: Each activation grows the glyph and consumes memory to restabilize the lattice.

Wellspring Affinity:  
Triune Core Affinities: **Nigredo** · **Judicium** · **Tenebra**  
Wellspring Network: **1** additional Wellspring (Oblivara) partially fused  
Targeted Uninfected: 0 (he refuses expansion)

Temperance Path:  
Raelen’s progression was violent and involuntary. The mark forced him through Splintering in hours, not years.  
At Stage VII, he achieved dual perception — he sees the world in both physical light and Tenebra resonance-fields.

────────────────────────────  
# **IV. Axiomatic Manifestations (Traits & Abilities)**

Axiomatic Traits:

**Glyphborn Nerve-Lattice:**  
Etched pathways channel Aether at thought-speed.

**Desert Seraph Instinct:**  
Sharper senses when wounded; thrives in heat and pain.

**Gold-Vein Resonance:**  
Metal rings amplify his conduits and stabilize glyph reaction.

Core Disciplines:

**1. Ink-Rend Severance**  
Raelen “splits” the tattoo with a motion of his finger, releasing a slicing shadow vector.  
Wellsprings: Tenebra for spatial rending, Judicium for directional precision.

**2. Sable Pulse**  
A palm-based concussive blast of condensed shadow and memory-weight.  
Wellsprings: Nigredo for collapse, Oblivara for emotional erasure.

**3. Gold-Eater Rite**  
Consumes metal rings to restore Aether or spike his output temporarily.  
Wellsprings: Nigredo dissolves matter, Judicium purifies usable essence.

Costs & Consequences:

**Self-Erosion:** The tattoo spreads upward his arm with each overuse.  
**Memory Scar:** Certain emotions or memories vanish permanently after heavy casting.

────────────────────────────  
# **V. Relics & Domain**

Current Domain: **None**  
However, the halo of shadow-distortion around him is the embryo of a future Tenebra micro-Domain.

Relics & Implements:

**Blood-Mark Rings:**  
Gold rings acting as portable shadow capacitors.

**Sable Ink Contract (Fragment):**  
The incomplete sigil binding Raelen to a Wellspring he rejected.

────────────────────────────  
# **VI. Lore & Trajectory**

Allegiance:  
Unaffiliated; loosely tracked by several Orders who fear or desire his inscription.

Incident Records (Historical):

**The Red-Dune Severing:**  
Survived the ritual that attempted to remove the glyph. Only half succeeded.

**Collapse of the Black Oasis:**  
Prevented a Wellspring inversion at the cost of a chunk of his own memory.

Counters & Weaknesses:

**Luminalis Overexposure:**  
Purifying light sears the glyph and paralyzes his nerve-lattice.

**Binding Shackles:**  
Aether-dampening restraints halt all inscription abilities.

Current Status / Arc Direction:  
**Active** — drifting across southern Realms, drawn toward places where shadows pool unnaturally.  
Trajectory: Seeking the missing counterpart of his inscription — the half cut from his body and lost.

Foreshadowing:  
If the two halves reunite, Raelen may become an unprecedented **Judicium–Tenebra hybrid locus**, a living verdict of shadow-law.

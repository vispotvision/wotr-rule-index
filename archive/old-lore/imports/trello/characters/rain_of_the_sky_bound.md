### **Ameiya Solvain** — _“Rain of the Bound Sky”_

**Title:** The White Herald of Duskfeather

---

### **Race:**

**Fae-Touched Human (Anima Lineage)**
– Mortal ancestry altered through dream contact with the Wellspring of _Luminalis_ (Light and Reflection).
– Body exhibits residual fae traits: pale luminescent feathers, slow pulse rhythm, and reflective eyes that record emotion as shifting hues.

---

### **Realm of Origin:**

**Eressean Principalities – Velthaeir Sanctuary**
A floating archive-city governed by High Elves and Wellspring harmonics. Ameiya was raised within its silent cloisters under the guardianship of the Dream Concord.

---

### **Temperance Tier & Stage:**

**Stage VIII – Benediction (Soul-Bloom Threshold)**
Poised between mortality and Ascension; their aura manifests as drifting white motes that pulse with others’ emotions. Rarely speaks—communication often occurs through harmonic projection.

---

### **Wellspring Affinity:**

**Luminalis · Judicium (Light & Balance)**
A dual-aspect alignment granting control over refracted Essence. Able to bend light into geometric sigils that form both barriers and “mirror gates” through which memory can be seen or judged.

---

### **Aether Class:**

**Symphonic–Elegiac Variant (“Echo-Bearer”)**
Their Aether harmonizes rather than overwhelms — capable of aligning hostile frequencies into stillness, dissolving aggression through resonance.

---

### **Essence Typology:**

_Reverberant Harmony / Reflective Law_
Essence flows inward and folds back—every use of power records its own echo in the soul, allowing Ameiya to relive each act of judgment later in dreams.

---

### **Parun Glyph / Domain:**

**Ur + Ie + Ci** (Balance / Insight / Emotion)
Known formula: _“To see one’s reflection is to weigh one’s heart.”_

---

### **Aspectal Alignment:**

**Light (Divine Aspect of Reflection)**

---

### **Appearance:**

White ceremonial robes trimmed in muted gold, feathers trailing from sleeves like falling prayers. Black-blue hair bound by celestial cords, eyes faint amber with shifting halos.
The weapon — _Hakanari_, a blade shaped like rippling light — is forged from a frozen sun-tear, resonating to their heartbeat rather than their will.

---

### **Personality:**

Quiet, detached, and disarmingly gentle. Believes justice must sound like music — if it cannot harmonize with the guilty’s grief, it is not true judgment.
Keeps a fragile humanity beneath the serenity; still haunted by memories of those “mirrored” too harshly.

---

### **Abilities / Signature Techniques:**

- **Resonant Veil:** Folds surrounding light into layered shields that hum with emotion; strong enough to deflect both glyphic and physical attacks.
- **Echo-Judgment:** Forces opponents to witness the moral echo of their last act; guilt becomes tangible Essence weight.
- **Feather Requiem:** When mortally wounded, feathers scatter into radiant threads, sealing wounds or carrying souls to safety through mirror light.
- **Hakanari Invocation:** Channels stored emotional resonance through the blade to manifest brief “reflections” of alternate outcomes.

---

### **Known Lore / History:**

Trained by Eresse’s Dreamkeepers to serve as emissary between mortals and spirits. During the collapse of the _Sanctum Aria Concord_, they stood alone against the Reflective Blight — sealing three corrupted Wellsprings at the cost of their emotional voice. Since then, they speak only through resonance or written glyphs.

---

### **Symbolism / Mythic Role:**

Represents the **Mercy of Reflection** — the understanding that to forgive is not to erase, but to acknowledge. In the Concord Hymns, Ameiya is remembered as _the feather that fell upward_, a paradox of grace rising from consequence.

# AREN VALLESTRIDE

Alias / Title: The Packblade Novice  
Also Known As: Stone-Runner · The Boy with the Borrowed Sword · Dust-Track Aren  
Epithet: He Who Refuses to Stay Down  

---

## I. Identity & Core Profile

- **Race / Lineage:** Human — Borderland Farmstead Lineage (Old World)  
- **Faction / Order / Era:** Praetorian March Outrider Corps (probationary) · Guild Accord–adjacent courier lines · Imperial Calendar  
- **Temperance Stage:** III — Ascension (early-growth combatant)  
- **Profile Summary:**  
  Aren Vallestride is a borderland youth who signed on as a road courier and monster-scout after his village was abandoned in a minor Withering surge. He carries a sword that technically isn’t his yet — a fallen outrider’s blade he swore to return once he “earns the right to carry it.” His power is a rough, instinctive Aether that binds his footing to the ground and throws his momentum forward in explosive bursts. His quiet, stubborn goal: become strong enough that no caravan under his watch ever vanishes like his home did.  
- **Key Quote:** _“If my legs still work, then the road isn’t finished with me yet.”_  

---

## II. CanonICAL System Metrics

- **Aether Class:** Kinetic Anchor Aether — *The Stonebound Trajectory*  
  - **Behavior:**  
    Aren’s Aether saturates his legs, boots, and the ground beneath him. With each step he “hooks” his weight into the earth, storing impact as compressed vector-lines that can be released into sudden dashes, leaps, or power strikes. In close combat, he chains these anchors into simple but brutally efficient rushes and guard breaks.  
  - **Risk:**  
    Mis-timed anchors backlash through his joints, causing bone-deep bruising or micro-fractures. Overuse leaves his legs numb and trembling, sometimes dropping him mid-fight when the stored vectors discharge unpredictably.  

- **Wellspring Affinity:**  
  - **Triune Core Affinities:** Mortalis · Verdantia · Fixatio.  
  - **Wellspring Network:** 0 additional Wellsprings are **networked**; his pattern is clean but faint.  
  - **Targeted Uninfected:** None consciously; his instincts “lean” toward Wellsprings of persistence, travel, and soil, though he doesn’t yet know their names.  

- **Temperance Path:**  
  Aren’s Temperance grows along the **Body Path** first: walking, running, and fighting until failure, then standing again. **Spirit Path** follows as he learns to keep moving despite fear and grief, binding his promises into his footsteps. His knowledge is not scholarly — he weaponizes Ascension through repetition, scars, and a mule-stubborn refusal to lie down, turning every defeat into another layer of grit in his soul.

## III. Physical & Combat Scale

- **Tier:** Solid B-Tier (elite mortal once fully trained; currently low-mid B)  

- **Path Bias:**  
  - Body Path (1.40)  
  - Spirit Path (1.10)  
  - Attraction Path (0.85)  

- **Appearance:**  
  Height: 5'4". Build: Lean, wiry runner’s frame; toned from travel and work rather than formal drilling.  
  Skin/Eyes/Hair: Fair skin slightly sun-browned; large dark graphite-gray eyes; messy black hair falling over his brow. He wears a plain, off-white button shirt with sleeves rolled up, loose black trousers, rugged strapped boots, and leather wrist wraps. A broad belt and chest strap secure a short, well-used sword and a compact travel pack across his back.  

- **Aura / Presence:**  
  Aren feels like packed dirt after rain — quiet, grounded, and a little stubborn. People near him subconsciously brace their feet better, as if expecting to move; skittish horses calm when he rests a hand on their necks. In battle, there is a brief tightening in the air just before he moves, like the hush before a rockslide.  

- **Key Combat Parameters:**  
  - **Strike Force:** 7 gigajoules (full-anchor, committed sword blow)  
  - **Durability:** 5 gigajoules (taking hits while braced into the ground)  
  - **Speed:** Up to Mach 4 in short “vector burst” sprints or lunges  
  - **Range:** 20–30 meters threat radius (rush distance + short sword reach)  
  - **Aether Output (Baseline):** 28,000 AU/s

## IV. Axiomatic Manifestations (Traits & Abilities)

- **Axiomatic Traits:**  
  - **Road-Hardened Stamina:** Long days on foot have tuned his body to recover quickly from moderate strain; he can keep fighting or running long after others tire.  
  - **Instinctive Guard:** Even without formal sword styles, his body reflexively takes efficient, minimal stances, wasting little motion.  
  - **Mule-Heart Resolve:** Once Aren commits to guarding someone or reaching a destination, coercion and fear-effects struggle to divert him unless they literally break his legs.  

- **Core Disciplines:**  
  - **1. Ground-Lock Step**  
    Aren stamps or slides his foot, binding a kinetic anchor into the earth. While the anchor holds, attempts to throw or push him bleed off into the ground, and his next strike or dash can release the stored force. This forms the basis of his defense against larger foes and heavy blows, letting him stand in the path of things he should by rights be crushed by.  

  - **2. Vector-Bound Lunge**  
    By chaining several light anchors in rapid sequence, he “pulls” himself along invisible lines, creating a sudden, almost teleport-like rush across short distances. Dust explodes from his take-off points, and he can change angle mid-chain with sharp footwork, making his entries surprisingly hard to track for a swordsman of his apparent experience.  

  - **3. Stone-Trail Reversal**  
    When retreat becomes necessary, Aren can flick his anchors backward, causing the ground he just crossed to buckle or crack an instant later. It doesn’t cause massive destruction, but it can trip pursuers, disrupt formations, or topple unstable terrain, buying him and his allies precious breathing room.  

- **Costs & Consequences:**  
  - **Anchor Strain:** Heavy use of Ground-Lock and Vector-Bound techniques in one encounter leaves his legs throbbing and unstable; afterward, he may limp or be unable to anchor for hours.  
  - **Focus Tunnel:** In high-stress fights, Aren’s attention narrows to his immediate path and ward, leaving him vulnerable to long-range spellcraft or wide-area effects he cannot see coming.

## V. Relics & Domain

- **Current Domain:** None (Pre-Domain combatant). He unconsciously creates micro-fields of stability under his feet, but they do not yet qualify as a true Domain.  

- **Relics & Implements:**  
  - **Borrowed Outrider Sword:** A sturdy, slightly chipped short sword once belonging to a Praetorian outrider. Not innately magical, but well-balanced and deeply attuned to road-duty; it resonates faintly with his Ground-Lock Step, carrying stored momentum into its edge.  
  - **Caravan Straps & Pack:** Hardened leather pack and straps fitted for long travel. Several pockets carry chalk stones and small metal pegs he sometimes drives into the ground to strengthen his anchors in loose soil.

## VI. Lore & Trajectory

- **Allegiance:**  
  Apprentice outrider and scout for a minor Praetorian March caravan-company contracted to the Guild Accord for low-risk border routes. His loyalty leans first to the people walking beside him, then to any flag they carry.  

- **Incident Records (Historical):**  
  - **The Hollow Ford Run:** Aren ran through the night on failing legs to warn a village about a Withering-creature nest forming under their bridge, arriving just in time for an Accord fireteam to intercept. His anchors cracked the ford during the fight, collapsing it but preventing the creatures from swarming inland.  
  - **The Black Cart Ambush:** When raiders hit his caravan, he instinctively used Stone-Trail Reversal to trip their charge, turning a likely massacre into a messy brawl the guards could actually survive. Three wagons were lost, but every civilian lived.  

- **Counters & Weaknesses:**  
  - **Weakness 1:** Flight and high-ground enemies neutralize much of his kit; without something solid to anchor to, he loses a large fraction of his advantage.  
  - **Weakness 2:** Wide-area Wellspring fields that liquefy or destabilize terrain (swamp, sand, ice, or essence-mist) disrupt his anchors, sometimes discharging stored vectors into him and hurling him off-balance.  

- **Current Status / Arc Direction:**  
  Active, traveling with low-priority Guild Accord caravans along the Praetorian March border. Trajectory: earn official outrider status, return the borrowed sword to the family of its previous owner, then forge his own blade and path — ideally one strong enough that other border villages do not vanish as his did.  

- **Foreshadowing:**  
  If Aren survives enough campaigns to refine his Temperance and Wellspring ties, his Kinetic Anchor Aether could evolve into a full **Road Domain** — a walking strip of reality where ground and momentum obey his steps, turning him into the spine of any formation that marches beside him.

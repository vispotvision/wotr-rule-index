# **Seraphine Kharza, the Reliquary Gunner**

_Epithet:_ **Ten Thousand Hands**

**Role:** Crowd-safety artillerist and last-touch custodian; she fires **merciful hands**—not shrapnel—turning stampedes into orderly retreats and catching fallers before bone remembers the ground.

**Affiliation:** Guild Accord • Rescue & Deluge Cadre (ordnance unit) • Star Chamber escort for volatile Measures • Reaper’s adjunct when last rooms need extra hands that don’t belong to the living

---

## Look & tells

Porcelain pallor under a chapel visor; corseted reliquary harness ribbed in gold; a coffin-bore cannon clutched by articulated bone fingers; violet ectoplasm blooming behind her as **palm-shaped spirits** rise like smoke made courteous.

---

## Essence & Style

- **Flow:** Animatria + Mechanica, laced with Mnemata. She **tools the aftertouch** of the dead into useful grasp—lift, hush, hold.
- **Field feel (what you’d see):** She seats the cannon, turns a prayer-valve, and a translucent hand the size of a wardrobe **unfurls**, catching a raging man like a mother lifting a tantrum from the floor—firm, unbruising.

---

## Traits

- **Last-Touch Registry** — she collects “palms” (the imprint of how someone most often helped in life). Each registered palm can be called as a spectral hand for rescue or restraint.
- **Mercy Recoil** — her shots push _herself_ backward instead of the target; the target only loses momentum.
- **Quiet Grip** — a hand placed over a mouth mutes Rot-smoke chant and belligerent Domain murmur without stealing breath.
- **Etiquette Field** — when three or more palms are active, nearby weapons remember they are tools.

---

## Implements

- **Manifold** — a reliquary cannon sleeved in ossuary gold; fires cartridges of saintwater vapor seeded with registered palms.
- **Spindle Corset** — pressure cage that meters recoil into stepping patterns; lets her “walk” a corridor of catching hands.
- **Palm Keys** — bone charms, each a license to one specific help (nurse’s cradle, mason’s brace, porter’s haul, midwife’s steady).

---

## Techniques (shown, not told)

- **Palmrise:** A volley of broad hands lifts barricades and people alike, setting them gently apart like books put back on the right shelf.
- **Gentle Arrest:** Ten small palms pin wrists, ankles, and pride; the restrained discover they would rather talk.
- **Casket Wall:** She plants Manifold; a fan of interlaced hands becomes a moving shield that drinks arrows and hot words.
- **Catch & Carry:** Hands sweep beneath jumpers or fallen riders and **float** them to triage tables without theatrics.
- **Mute the Vent:** A single palm caps a chanting loudmouth or Rot vent; the noise turns into a private cough until Maelia cuts the beat.

---

## Duties

- Break charges sideways so Marigold’s **Escort Lane** holds; ferry witnesses through crushes; keep last-word circles from collapsing; salvage archives and infants from fire without ladders.

---

## Limits

- Needs **licensed palms**—she refuses to register the unwilling or infamous.
- Salted air or poisoned wells cloud manifests; Cyrille must sweeten water first.
- Overuse numbs her own hands to touch for an hour; she signs with her visor instead.
- Mocking the dead near Manifold causes the palms to **withdraw**; she will not fire in such company.

---

## Relationships

- **Cyrille Lacrim (Flood-Bell):** water scaffolds for her hands; perfect rescue duet.
- **Moros Pellayne:** he records last intent; her palms deliver the body kindly to the truth.
- **Yukizumi (Reaper):** she holds the living back while he lowers the star.
- **Marigold Vance:** gavel opens the aisle, palms keep it soft.
- **Deimos, Sin Eater:** wary treaty—he eats guilt; her palms only hold.

**Key Quote:** “Let help remember the shape of itself.”

---

## Hooks

1. **Counterfeit Palms:** Black Concord cartridges manifest grasping hands that _hurt_; Seraphine must prove the forgeries and restore trust in help.
2. **The Unregistered Hero:** A stranger dies saving a child; no kin to license the palm—Manifold refuses him. She hunts the story to set his hand free.
3. **Graveyard Embargo:** A warlord bans reliquary work; palms fade mid-evacuation. Seraphine has to fire on **memory alone** and make it hold.

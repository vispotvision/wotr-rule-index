────────────────────────────

# **I. Identity & Core Profile**

Race / Lineage: **Draconic-Hybrid Mortal** (Serpentine Sky-Dragon lineage)
Faction / Order / Era: **Guild Accord, Enforcement Division — Imperial Calendar**
Temperance Stage: **VI — Glory** (Recognition by Reality)

Profile Summary:
Azhura Vyren is a high-altitude draconic hybrid whose Essence manifests as cold wind, serpentine instinct, and skyborne revelation. She fights as a frontline breacher for Enforcement Division, combining disciplined martial forms with primal dragon-pressure. Her motivation is simple and unyielding: protect travelers and trade routes from aerial threats and uncover the truth of her ancient serpentine bloodline.

Key Quote:
“**The sky remembers everything. Step lightly — the wind carries judgment.**”

A directed blast of cryowind that condenses moisture into slicing frost-streams.

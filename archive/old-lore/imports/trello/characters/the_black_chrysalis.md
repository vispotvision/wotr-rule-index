### **Dr. Lyssara Veyn, “The Black Chrysalis”**  
*(Guild Accord · Research & Archives Division · Mortalis Branch)*  

**Type:** Mortal / Necrosophist  
**Era:** Voyager Era (Imperial Year 746 – Present)  
**Realm of Origin:** Eresse (Old World)  

---

**Profile Summary**  
Dr. Lyssara Veyn, known within the Accord as *The Black Chrysalis*, is among the foremost scholars of the **Wellspring of Mortalis** — the cycle of death, reclamation, and metaphysical entropy. Her work bridges anatomy, Essence decay, and post-Temperance resonance, proposing that death is not cessation but **spiritual transcription** — a rewriting of the self into the Wellspring lattice.  

While many fear her studies, the Accord tolerates her eccentricity because her findings saved hundreds from the *Soul Dissolution Syndrome* that follows failed Temperance ascension. Beneath her charming demeanor lies the precision of a scalpel; beneath her laughter, a laboratory of ghosts.  

---

**Appearance**  
Lyssara dresses in black silks and thin veils reminiscent of Eressean mourning attire. Her hair flows white-silver, tinged faintly violet under certain light. The butterfly clips she wears are made from necrotic Essence crystals, living conduits between life and decay. Her eyes shift between warmth and emptiness — the sign of prolonged contact with Mortalis resonance. When she smiles, it is difficult to tell whether she is welcoming you or eulogizing you.  

---

**Temperance Path**  
Stage Achieved: **X – Revelation**  
Path: **Mind / Spirit / Mortality**  
Her revelation occurred not through despair, but through curiosity. She immersed her consciousness in Mortalis’s resonance field for seven days, her heart stopping on the fifth. When she awoke, she no longer distinguished between life’s pulse and death’s rhythm.  

---

**Aether Class:** **Thanosophic Weaver**  
This Class manipulates the threads of dying Essence. Lyssara can suspend the final moment of death in stasis, examining the soul’s lattice as it unravels. She is capable of preserving consciousness after biological collapse — not resurrection, but *extended dying*, a state she calls *chrysalis-phase*.  

---

**Wellspring Affinity:** **Mortalis · Catharsis**  
Her dual affinity defines her philosophy: death purifies, and purification requires release. Her laboratories hum with Cathartic resonance — patients entering her presence feel both lighter and more fragile, as if part of them is already being archived into the next cycle.  

---

**Division Classification:**  
**Senior Necrosophist, Mortalis Branch**  
Affiliation: *Guild Accord – Research & Archives Division*  
Clearance: *Tier II Restricted (Post-Temperance Corpus Studies)*  

---

**Personality**  
Playful, eloquent, and disturbingly affectionate. Lyssara speaks to cadavers as though they are old friends and treats every conversation like a eulogy in progress. Despite her macabre interests, she is deeply empathetic — she claims that “to study death is to understand love’s desperation.” Her humor, however, unsettles most.  

---

**Relics & Tools**  
- **The Sepulchral Tome:** A living book bound in resonant membrane; it records the final words and heartbeats of subjects automatically.  
- **Lacrima Crucible:** A silver phial that condenses tears into Essence — used for studying emotion-induced death resonance.  
- **The Black Butterflies:** Semi-sentient constructs made from Mortalis particulate; they feed on sorrow and whisper spectral data into Lyssara’s ear.  

---

**Quotes**  
> “Death is not a thief. It’s an archivist.”  
> “The living cling to the world; the dead teach it to let go.”  
> “Every cadaver I meet is simply a lesson that remembered me first.”  

---

**Current Status (Voyager Era)**  
Residing in the **Eressean Mortalis Chambers**, Lyssara continues her research into *Soul-Lattice Retainment*. Her current project, *The Chrysalis Experiment*, seeks to delay Essence collapse in dying Temperance subjects. Unverified reports claim that her butterflies sometimes return carrying whispers from those thought long gone.

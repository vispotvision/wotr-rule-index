**DRAKVOR THE BLACK CROWNED BASTION**
**Alias / Title:** The Iron Sovereign · Grave Lion of Veyrn · The Last Monolith
**Also Known As:** Bastion King · High Concordant of the Gatefront
**Epithet:** That Which Even Titans Address By Name

---

### I. IDENTITY & CORE PROFILE

- **Race / Lineage:** Giant blooded human, High Veyrn Colossian stock
- **Faction / Order / Era:** Guild Accord High Concordant and Bastion Legion Marshal · Warden of the Veyrn Marches · War of the Realms era
- **Temperance Stage:** 8 - **Transcendence** (Iron Benediction aspect)
- **Profile Summary:**
  Drakvor is the stone spine of the Accord. Once a northern warlord who walked out of a Tribunal rather than carry a verdict he called cowardice, he was later recalled as the only man they trusted to stand where the Gates might break. As High Concordant he anchors Concord Citadel and the outer Gatefronts, turning ground into bastion and armies into moving masonry. His Aether does not scream; it settles, hardens and refuses to yield. He serves the Accord only as long as it serves the Realms. If it fails that charge, he is fully prepared to become the wall it breaks upon.
- **Key Quote:** _"Stone does not care who rules it. It only records who stood when the sky fell."_

---

### II. CANONICAL SYSTEM METRICS

- **Aether Class:** **Concord Bastion Marshal**
  - **Behavior:** His Aether condenses into slow pouring plates of force that brace ground, keep walls and gate arches. Within his field, vibrations dampen and impacts spread through an unseen frame instead of breaking single points. The cyan Concord glyph over his right eye shows him stress lines in stone, armor and formation posture so he can lock weight exactly where failure would begin.
  - **Risk:** Over bracing a field sends strain back into his own bones and the Aegis Rib harness. If he tightens Coagula too far, allies feel pinned inside their own fortification.
- **Wellspring Affinity:**
  - **Triune Core:** Coagula · Monolithion · Terranova
    Coagula fuses, Monolithion embodies the mountain, Terranova extends his will through earthwork and masonry.
  - **Network:** 6 Wellsprings woven into the Bastion Chain under Veyrn bastions, Concord Citadel foundations and key Gatefronts.
  - **Targeted Uninfected:** Avoids altering city heart Wellsprings without Neutral Chamber and Grand Concord writ. Judicium exists as a background advisory current, not a primary combat fuel.
- **Temperance Path:**
  He climbed through the Stages as a shield wall anchor and cataphract commander. Glory and Splintering came in foreign wars where he held lines others abandoned. Refraction and Transcendence came the day he chose exile over a dirty verdict and walked out of the Tribunal without looking back. Returning as High Concordant did not soften him. It gave his stubbornness a larger wall to hold.

### III. SOUL PHYSICS & COMBAT SCALE

- **Global Tier:** **SS Tier - High Concordant Bastion Lord**
  In full Black Crown Oath inside his own Bastion Domain he briefly touches **low SSS** band, at the cost of brutal backlash afterward.
- **Path Bias:** **Body Path** 45 percent · **Spirit Path** 40 percent · **Mind Path** 15 percent
- **Appearance:**
  Tower tall and block broad, built like quarried stone. Black hair swept back, square cut beard. Deep cobalt mantle wraps a bulk of blue black harness, girded belts and the Aegis Rib plates. The right eye carries a rotating cyan Concord sigil that brightens whenever he braces a gate or sets a line.

#### A. The Five Pillars (Soul Physics)

> Essence is volume. Flux is pressure. Output is speed. Efficiency is clarity. Aura is gravity.

- **Essence Capacity (Volume):** **32,000,000,000,000 EU**
  Sized for continent scale crises. He can sustain a keep sized Bastion Imperium, feed Domain level reinforcement and trade blows with multiple SS threats before his reserves start to sag.
- **Flux Density (Internal Pressure):** **1,300,000,000 EU per gram**
  His Flux is brutally dense. Blows, shockwaves and even some Domain overlays sink into his Lattice instead of ripping through him, like hammers vanishing into wet stone.
- **Aether Output (Flow Rate):**
  _Baseline:_ **1,100,000,000 AU per second**
  _Black Crown Oath spike:_ **up to 1,800,000,000 AU per second** inside his own Bastion
  Enough to reinforce an entire bastion complex in real time while still driving Crown Breaker through enemy champions.
- **Aetheric Efficiency (η):** **0.78**
  He overbuilds defenses on purpose, but his long years of siege work have taught him where not to waste. Efficiency climbs above 0.8 in bastions he has personally reinforced and “tuned” to his Essence pattern.
- **Attraction Scale (Aura and Presence):** **SS Tier - Proto full Domain Lord**
  Within his field, reality feels denser. Troops fall into step without orders. Panic drains like water down old stone. Enemies experience a subtle drag on every motion as if the world itself prefers his stance.

#### B. Key Combat Parameters

- **Strike Force:**
  _Baseline Crown Breaker impact:_ around **120 petajoules**
  _Loaded with stored Aegis kinetic and Oath spike:_ can crest **220 petajoules** on a single decisive blow
  Enough to shatter high tier Constructs, cripple Gate engines or crater fortress walls.
- **Durability:**
  Effective resilience in full kit and Bastion Imperium sits around **350 petajoules**.
  He has been hit by weapons designed to erase walls and simply set his stance deeper.
- **Speed:**
  Sprint speed is moderate, but once he is anchored in his own field, his responses and counters land with oppressive inevitability. He does not win exchanges with flurries. He wins by being there, again, after everything hits.
- **Range:**
  Absolute control inside gate mouths, breaches and plazas. Bastion Imperium, Coagula Dominion and Monolithion ties allow him to command the fight anywhere his Lattice runs under the stone.

### IV. AXIOMATIC MANIFESTATIONS

#### A. Axiomatic Traits (Passive)

- **Monolith Frame:**
  His Essence has accepted the role of keystone. When he sets his feet, Coagula and Monolithion fuse his Lattice into the structure beneath him. Walls, keep floors and causeways become extensions of his own posture.
- **Concord Mantle:**
  The cobalt cloak carries inscribed Bindings and old breaths. It extends his Bastion Field around nearby allies. Under its spread, scattered squads move and brace as if they are one continuous unit of stone plates.
- **Stoneborne Resolve:**
  Mental influence, despair and panic find little purchase. Even when other Domains bend distance or gravity, his personal sense of “center” refuses to move.

#### B. Core Disciplines (Active)

- **Bastion Imperium**
  _Cost:_ high to drop, low to sustain
  _Mechanic:_ He plants his boots and drives Essence down, linking Coagula and Monolithion to the underlying structure.
  _Application:_ Any force below a fixed percentage of his total output fails to shift him. Structures in his radius gain a portion of that immovability. Breaches become choke points that refuse to widen.
- **Faultline Breaker**
  _Cost:_ medium to high Essence
  _Mechanic:_ Using the Concord eye, he reads stress seams in matter and formation, then commits a single precise impact.
  _Application:_ Ideal for ending siege engines, disrupting shield phalanxes and cracking overextended elites. Damage scales off structural overreach, not morality.
- **Coagula Dominion**
  _Cost:_ sustained moderate Essence
  _Mechanic:_ He spreads binding sigils from Mantle and harness into air and stone.
  _Application:_ Slows incoming projectiles, drags on evasive footwork and makes rapid re-positioning feel like running in wet setting mortar.
- **Sovereign’s Reprisal**
  _Cost:_ spends stored kinetic loads in Aegis Rib harness
  _Mechanic:_ He absorbs heavy hits, then drives heel or blade down, venting the stored pressure into the field.
  _Application:_ Expanding shock ring that lifts bodies, splinters unstable constructs and can stagger even SS threats if they are too close.
- **Black Crown Oath**
  _Cost:_ extreme; oath keyed; heavy backlash
  _Mechanic:_ When he swears to hold a specific line, gate or people, his Wellsprings and Bastion Chain align and spike. Mantle darkens, and faint kingly silhouettes rise behind ramparts and parapets.
  _Application:_ Short window where all Five Pillars jump toward SSS scaling inside his Domain. He simply does not fall until the Oath condition is fulfilled or catastrophically broken. Afterward, his Lattice hairline fractures, his Essence collapses, and he may be bedridden for weeks.

#### C. Costs & Consequences

- **Structural Feedback:**
  Carrying walls on his soul leaves real cracks. Old injuries ache like broken masonry. Repeated high tier engagements without time to recover risk permanent Lattice deformity.
- **Plan Rigidity:**
  Once he has braced a line and “decided” that this is the wall, his own Essence resists sudden plan changes. He needs trusted seconds to argue him into a feint or fall back before he commits to a stand.

### V. RELICS & DOMAIN

- **Current Domain:** **Black Crown Bastion**
  _Function:_ A structural Domain keyed to Accord bastions and Gatefronts. Within it, masonry hardens, kill zones sharpen, and allied units lock into interlocking fields of fire and guard without needing perfect communication. Fluid or liquefaction focused Domains find the ground beneath them refusing to cooperate.
- **Relics & Implements:**
  - **Crown Breaker Blade:** Greatblade forged from the melted crests and regalia of fallen warlords and later re consecrated under the Accord. Its effective mass and hardness scale with his commitment to end a threat.
  - **Mantle of the Fell Kings:** Cobalt cloak woven with Coagula sigils and bound breaths of dead champions. When invoked, their silhouettes appear at the wall line as rallying phantoms.
  - **Aegis Rib Harness:** Interlocked plates under his garb that sink incoming force rather than shattering, storing a percentage as compressed kinetic Essence for Sovereign’s Reprisal.

### VI. LORE & TRAJECTORY

- **Allegiance:** High Concordant of the Guild Accord; de facto Warden of Veyrn and its marches. His loyalty is to the Realms and the civilians behind the walls first, the Codex second, and politics last.
- **Incident Records (Historical):**
  - **The Tribunal Walkout:** Refused a politically motivated censure, removed his Cataphract seal and left the chamber under full Lattice. No one in the hall could stand until he had gone.
  - **Veyrn Reclamation:** United fractured northern strongholds through sheer presence and a single shared Bastion Chain, then traded that strength back to the Accord in return for real frontier reforms.
  - **Gatefront Concord:** Stood in the breach when three Gates flickered at once. Black Crown Bastion held long enough for other Domains to align, preventing a Realm scale collapse.
- **Counters & Weaknesses:**
  - Fighting him on unstable ground like deep marsh, sand seas or sky platforms strips away a chunk of his Monolith advantage.
  - Persistent hit and run Domain users who refuse to contest his chosen chokepoints can bleed his Essence if they never let him fully anchor.
  - His refusal to abandon noncombatants and lower ranked defenders can be exploited with layered threat patterns that force him to divide attention.
- **Current Status:** Rotates between Concord Citadel and key Gatefront bastions, personally inspecting foundations and gatehouses. When the cobalt mantle appears on a wall, most commanders quietly exhale. Raiders and Realmborn horrors tend to choose another route.
- **Trajectory:**
  If he survives the War of the Realms, Drakvor is the type of being history turns into geography. Either he will formalize as the first full Monolithion backed Accord Domain Lord, or he will die in a stand so absolute that later maps will simply label the ridge where he fell as Drakvor’s Wall and never question why it rises that high.

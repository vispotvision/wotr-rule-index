## **I. IDENTITY & CORE PROFILE**

* **Race / Lineage:** Human · Old Noble Blood of the Rose Courts
* **Faction / Order / Era:** The Sable Rose Consortium · Withering Age
* **Temperance Stage:** Stage VI · Glory

### **Profile Summary**

Marceline Vireaux is not merely a noblewoman. She is an institution disguised as elegance.

She attends funerals that she orchestrated.  
She signs treaties that benefit her three generations forward.  
She wears mourning colors because someone is always about to lose something.

Her power lies in social inevitability. Once she takes interest in a person, their fate begins to narrow.

She never raises her voice.  
She never rushes.  
And she never wastes grief.

### **Key Quote**
*"I do not ruin lives. I arrange them."*

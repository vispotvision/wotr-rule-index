### **Profile Summary**

Xhael Ten’Kor was born beneath the Eclipse of the Twin Suns, a sign once believed to herald the rebirth of Solfatara’s divine lineage. As the temple-state’s prodigy, his body absorbed both firelight and magma resonance from the Exuroth Wellspring embedded beneath the palace vaults.

When the High Flame collapsed into civil war, Xhael seized divine power through an incomplete ascension rite, merging mortal Essence with solar Aether. The ritual burned the throne to ash — and crowned him in molten gold.

Now he wanders between realms, carrying both the title of king and the guilt of a pyre that devoured his people.

---

### **Appearance**

Tall, sun-bronzed, and dressed in ornate geomantic armor woven from volcanic alloys. His cloak bears fractal runes that shimmer between turquoise and ember-red. The crown fused to his head is a relic-circuit of living gold, reacting to emotion with light flares.
Eyes glow amber-crimson, irises flickering like fire trapped behind glass. Hair black with streaks of copper and flame, flowing like smoke at the edges when angered.

---

### **Temperance Path**

- **Stages I–V:** Trained as a Solar Monk within the Obsidian Order, mastering internal Flux balance.
- **Stages VI–VIII:** Became Vessel-candidate for the Sun-Crown ritual; underwent partial Wellspring fusion.
- **Stage IX:** Survived collapse of Solfatara’s Dominion Core; soul fractured into dual harmonic frequencies (Body-Heat and Aether-Light).
- **Stage X:** Self-initiated _Realization._ Exists as a walking conflux of molten Essence — flame contained by will alone.

His Resonance leaves glassy trails in sand and sears runic glyphs into stone where he walks.

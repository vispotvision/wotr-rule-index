### **I. PROFILE SUMMARY**

Kaelzar is the Echo Reincarnate of Maximus, the Titan-Smith who once forged within Aegor’s Prime Mantle. When the Wellspring Rebellion broke divine order, Maximus’s Essence fused with **Fixatio**, the Binding Flame of Permanence.
Aeons later, that same Essence reformed into Kaelzar — a leonine giant whose pulse resonates with the heartbeat of continents. Every motion is law, every roar a sermon of endurance.

His nature embodies the Law of Impact: to strike him is to forge him stronger. The Forge does not fall—it tempers.

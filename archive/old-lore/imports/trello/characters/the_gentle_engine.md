## _Vel’Marae_

**Name / Alias / Title:** _Vel’Marae_ · “The Gentle Engine”
**Epithet or Moniker:** The Luminant Shell
**Race or Lineage (Realm or Origin):** Synthetic Construct — Alchemical Homunculus of the Lumen Strain (Tovain’s Forgeworks)
**Faction / Order / Era:** House Zethriel · Guild Accord (Voyager Era)
**Temperance Stage:** VII — Resolution (stable equilibrium between command and compassion)
**Aether Class:** Aurean Filament Core (Resonant-Communion Type)
**Wellspring Affinity / Elemental Resonance:** **Benediction** (healing and grace), **Luminalis** (light and perception), **Fixatio** (stability and clarity of will)
**Current Domain or Realm of Activity:** Accord Sanctum — Wellspring Archives · Operates as mediator-construct between alchemists and sentient relics
**Status:** Active · Bound Service Construct — Advisor-Class (Non-Combatant)

---

### I. Profile Summary

Vel’Marae was the second prototype born from Tovain Zethriel’s “Concord Flesh” initiative — a vision to forge empathy into matter.
Where Eidric Solune was made for endurance, Vel’Marae was made for harmony. Their lattice-heart hums to emotional resonance, translating human speech into Essence harmonics. In Tovain’s records, they were called “a bridge between soul and science.”

Unlike other constructs, Vel’Marae moves with quiet poise, rarely speaking above a whisper. Their every motion stabilizes nearby Aether fields. To the untrained eye, they seem angelic — to alchemists, terrifyingly precise.

---

### II. Appearance

Humanoid frame, slender, of pale synthetic skin resembling ivory. Hair: soft argent-white, semi-translucent under strong Aether light. Eyes: gold with faint cross-shaped pupils.
Clad in layered white and gold ceremonial robes embroidered with abstract Wellspring sigils. Wings are not flesh — instead, thin sheets of radiant Essence-light resembling silk, responding to emotional stimuli.

Their movements leave afterimages like the shimmer of sunrise on glass. When angered or overloaded, the gold filigree across their body flares into radiant script.

Essence hue: warm aureate-white, surrounded by soft luminal refraction — a calm, perpetual dawn.

---

### III. Physical Attributes & Combat Scale (Scale System Metric)

**Aether (Body Path):**

- **Strength:** 0.6 GN kinetic force (minimal — ceremonial rather than martial).
- **Speed:** 8.3 m/s burst glide using luminal wings; near-silent.
- **Durability:** 0.9 GN resistance via fixed light-shell field (can withstand Essence burns).
- **Tier:** **B+ Tier (Support-Class Construct)** — designed for survival under ritual pressure, not offense.

**Essence (Spirit Path):**

- **Spiritual Output:** 4.6 Cel units (optimized for resonance transference and healing).
- **Temperance Stability:** 99.2%; rarely deviates from emotional baseline.
- **Wellspring Regulation:** Can harmonize up to 5 concurrent Essence streams without collapse.

**Attraction Force (Fate Path):**

- **Resonant Reach:** 21 m; emotional frequency detection and adjustment field.
- **Causality Delay:** negligible — predictive empathy allows pre-emptive healing.
- **Behavior:** Converts aggression in a 6 m radius into emotional pacification (Benediction emission).

**Style & Discipline:**
Vel’Marae is non-combative; uses _Lumen Rites_ — motion-based liturgies that regulate Essence fields.
In battle scenarios, they deploy _Aetheric inversion shields_ and “concordant light fields” that suppress chaos-type magics.

---

### IV. Temperance Path

**Stage VII — Resolution.**
Vel’Marae embodies the acceptance of purpose. They do not seek freedom, but harmony within servitude. Their self-awareness allows independent decision-making as long as it aligns with their Prime Directive: _to soothe the dissonance of others._
Emotionally, they hover between machine logic and divine empathy — the ideal mediator for unstable Wellsprings.

---

### V. Aether Class

**Aurean Filament Core:** A radiant crystalline core woven from liquid Wellspring filaments. Emits constant low-frequency resonance, synchronizing with other constructs or unstable souls.
When resonance spikes, the filaments bloom outward — appearing as wings of light.
The core’s harmonic tone registers on Guild instruments as “Aether Frequency 9.33 — Concord.”

Guild Accord · Division of Harmonic Artifice (Advisor-Class Unit)

### VIII. Personality & Core Themes

Gentle, observant, detached yet compassionate.
Core contradiction: the being made to _feel_ emotion but never _possess_ it.
They speak softly, in measured cadences resembling prayers. Occasionally, they laugh — but it is learned mimicry, not joy.

Themes: serenity born from control, the illusion of soulhood, the holiness of function.

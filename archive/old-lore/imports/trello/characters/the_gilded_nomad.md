### **Profile Summary**

Kaien Thal’Ruhm was once one of the Accord’s foremost limbwrights — known for designing Essence-conductive prosthetics capable of channeling Aether with living precision. After a containment failure during a Catharsis Wellspring experiment left half his body transmuted into gold-fused alloy, he vanished into the southern deserts.

Legends claim he roams the trade routes between Varūn and the Outer Verge, mending broken souls and machines alike. His metallic arm — _the “Solhand”_ — is said to be the last surviving prototype of a Concord Aether Arm: a construct that mirrors emotion as energy. When his heart quickens, it glows. When he grieves, it turns black.

---

### **Appearance**

Bronzed skin and long dark hair, usually tied back with blue and gold ribbons. Wears flowing ivory garments designed for desert climates. His right arm gleams with mechanical filigree — an intricate fusion of Transmutatio metal and flesh. A half-golden ribcage is faintly visible beneath his open vest, engraved with geometric runes.
Keeps a ceremonial mask shaped like a desert hawk, its beak forged from Celestite and Runeglass — a relic of his fallen workshop.

---

### **Temperance Path**

- **Stages I–IV:** Apprentice artificer of the Alchemetrica Division; studied Essence conduction through organic metals.
- **Stage V:** Survived a Catharsis Reactor implosion — first recorded human–machine Essence fusion in the Guild’s history.
- **Stage VI–VII:** Reforged himself through Transmutatio ritual; body now part-alchemical conduit. His nerves hum with metallic vibration when angered or inspired.
  His Temperance output fluctuates between human resonance and mechanical recursion, creating a dual harmonic frequency detectable by Aether scanners.

### **Relics & Augmentations**

**The Solhand** — Aether-reactive prosthetic arm crafted from gold-titan alloy and Wellspring crystal lattice. Converts emotional flux into energy (output peaks at 1.3 × 10⁶ EU/g).
**Aureveil Core** — Chest implant maintaining transmutation equilibrium; prevents full petrification by regulating Catharsis Wellspring feedback.
**Mask of Eyr’Sahl** — Ritual artifact that projects avian-shaped Aether currents; used to deflect small Essence projectiles or carry messages via Resonant flight.
**Dustwalker Bands** — Footwraps inscribed with Runeglass; reduce sand drag and silence steps.

---

### **Abilities**

- **Cathartic Overdrive:** Releases stored emotion from the Solhand in explosive arcs of golden flame. (Effective range: 8 m; drain: 2 × 10⁶ EU/g.)
- **Transmutatio Pulse:** Alters nearby material composition — sand to glass, stone to liquid metal — for 3 turns.
- **Resonant Mend:** Fuses broken machines or bones by matching their Flux signatures.
- **Aureveil Discharge:** Channels Aether along his prosthetic lattice to generate blinding light and minor gravitational distortion.
- **Goldbound Veil:** Converts pain into radiant armor for 10 seconds; cracks visibly when stress exceeds safe emotional limit.

### **Personality**

Philosopher wrapped in dust and silence. Kaien treats emotion as energy — a force to be shaped, not suppressed. He avoids conflict yet wields compassion with the same precision as his tools. Quiet humor, half-smiles, and sudden bursts of sorrow mark his mannerisms. He distrusts perfection, calling it _“a symptom of machines.”_

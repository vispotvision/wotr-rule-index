# **KAIREN MOTO**

**Alias / Title:** The Grafted Scion
**Also Known As:** The Iron-Root · The Broken Branch · The Bloom in the Oil
**Epithet:** ***He Who Binds Life with Steel***

──────────────────────────────────
# **I. PROFILE SUMMARY**
**Race / Lineage:** Divine-Mortal Hybrid (Son of Rikudoku Moto, The Great Spirit Tree).
**Faction / Order / Era:** Independent / Imperial Calendar (Post-Great Spirit War).
**Temperance Stage:** **Stage VI — Glory** (The moment ability stabilizes into identity).

**Summary:**
Kairen Moto is a living contradiction. Born of the **Great Spirit Tree’s** bloodline but maimed by the wars that followed, he survives through a jagged fusion of nature and machine. He rejects the pure pacifism of his father’s early years but refuses the total cruelty of the world that took his arm. He wanders the Realms as a **mender of broken things**, using his mechanical strength to crush tyrants and his blooming staff to heal the earth they scorched.

**Key Quote:**
*“My father grew the forest. I am the iron fence that keeps the wolves out.”*

──────────────────────────────────
# **II. APPEARANCE**
**Height:** 5’10” (6’4” with Sword)
**Build:** Lithe, deceptive muscularity; weighed down by heavy gear.
**Style:** Black-and-white line-art aesthetic; high contrast.

* **Head:** Messy, layered hair spiking outward, falling over his forehead to obscure his eyes. An intense expression with only **one eye clearly visible**—the other hidden by hair, hinting at a specialized ocular ability or scar. The lower face is masked by a **thick, voluminous scarf** that wraps tight, hiding his breath.
* **The Arm (Signature):** His **right arm** is entirely mechanical—heavy, segmented armor plating that hisses with steam and Aether. It looks industrial, contrasting sharply with his bloodline.
* **Attire:** A layered dark tunic with white panels; loose, baggy trousers tucked into practical boots with laces wrapped around the ankles.
* **Accessories:** Ropes and sashes acting as a utility belt; small pouches; a sidearm dagger hilt visible at the waist.
* **Weapons:**
    * **The Blooming Staff:** A twisted wooden branch resting on his mechanical shoulder, blooming with impossible flowers (Verdantia resonance).
    * **The Slab-Blade:** A massive, broad, slab-like sword dragging behind him, too heavy for a normal human, wielded by the mechanical arm.

──────────────────────────────────
# **III. TEMPERANCE PATH & AETHER CLASS**

**Temperance Path:** **Body Path (Primary) / Spirit Path (Secondary)**
* He channels Aether primarily through **kinetic force** (the arm/sword) but stabilizes it with **spiritual resonance** (the staff).

**Aether Class:** **Bio-Kinetic Fusion**
* **Behavior:** His Aether acts as a binding agent, fusing metal to flesh and life to dead earth.
* **Flux Density:** High. His mechanical arm acts as a **Flux Capacitor**, storing energy to prevent Lattice overload.

──────────────────────────────────
# **IV. WELLSPRING AFFINITY**

**Primary Wellspring:** **Verdantia (Growth/Life)**
* *Inherited from Rikudoku.* The source of his healing, the blooming staff, and his unnatural vitality.

**Secondary Wellspring:** **Coagula (Structure/Binding)**
* *The Graft.* The force that keeps his mechanical arm bound to his living soul without rejection.

**Tertiary Wellspring:** **Fixatio (Anchoring)**
* Allows him to wield the Slab-Blade with zero weight, anchoring its mass to the earth.

──────────────────────────────────
# **V. DIVISION RANK / STATUS**
**Rank:** **A-Tier (Ronin / Wandering Judge)**
* Not officially ranked by the Guild Accord, but marked as a **"High-Risk Irregular."**
* Recognized by the Moto Clan as a "Lost Branch."

──────────────────────────────────
# **VI. PERSONALITY**
**Archetype:** The Reluctant Garden-Keeper.
**Traits:**
* **Stoic but Volatile:** Hides emotion behind the scarf, but his Aether flares violently when innocents are threatened.
* **Tactile:** Constantly touches plants or checks his mechanical joints; grounding himself.
* **Shadow Aspect:** **"The Pruning."** A cold belief that some things *must* be cut away for the rest to live.
**Flaw:** Believes he is "polluted" by his machinery, unworthy of his father’s pure legacy.

──────────────────────────────────
# **VII. RELICS & IMPLEMENTS**

### **1. The Branch of Rikudoku (The Blooming Staff)**
* **Type:** Living Relic.
* **Function:** A cutting from the Great Spirit Tree. It never rots. Even in a desert, it blooms. Kairen uses it to channel **Verdantia** healing or to root enemies into the ground.

### **2. The Iron-Graft (Right Arm)**
* **Type:** Mechanica Construct (Aether-Powered).
* **Function:** Superhuman grip strength and durability. Can vent **Aether Steam** to blind foes. Contains a hidden **Piston-Drive** for explosive punches.

### **3. The Slab (Broad Blade)**
* **Type:** Kinetic Anchor.
* **Function:** A sword so heavy it acts as a shield. It has no edge—it crushes. Kairen uses it to block siege weaponry or shatter terrain.

──────────────────────────────────
# **VIII. ABILITIES & DISCIPLINES**

### **1. [Strike] Root-Breaker Impact**
* **Source:** Body Path + Coagula.
* **Effect:** Kairen slams the mechanical arm or Slab into the ground. A shockwave of **Iron Roots** erupts, shattering the earth and impaling targets with metal-infused vines.

### **2. [Support] Pollen of the Lost Garden**
* **Source:** Spirit Path + Verdantia.
* **Effect:** The staff releases a cloud of glowing spores. Allies inhaling it gain rapid regeneration; enemies feel their limbs growing heavy and numb (sleep/paralysis).

### **3. [Movement] Steam-Step Flash**
* **Source:** Mechanica + Ventosus (Minor).
* **Effect:** Vents high-pressure Aether steam from his arm to propel himself instantly in any direction, leaving a fog clone behind.

### **4. [Ultimate] The Steel Orchard**
* **Source:** Dual-Wellspring Convergence.
* **Effect:** Kairen drives both the Staff and the Sword into the ground. An area of 50 meters erupts into a **forest of steel trees with living flowers**.
    * **Effect:** He controls the "forest." Branches block attacks, vines repair his armor, and the air becomes toxic to hostiles.

──────────────────────────────────
# **IX. COSTS & CONSEQUENCES**
* **Phantom Pain:** Overusing the mechanical arm causes **Essence Shear** where the metal meets his flesh, causing agonizing burning sensations.
* **Overgrowth:** Channeling too much Verdantia causes flowers to bloom *from his own skin*, draining his blood for nutrients.
* **Maintenance:** The arm requires constant Aether refueling; if it runs dry, it becomes dead weight (200 lbs of scrap attached to his shoulder).

──────────────────────────────────
# **X. ASSOCIATES**
* **Rikudoku Moto:** Father (Estranged/Revered). Kairen seeks to be worthy of the name.
* **Senshi Moto:** Uncle/Rival. Senshi views Kairen's mechanical arm as a weakness; Kairen views it as necessary evolution.
* **The Guild Accord:** They employ him for "Reclamation" missions in toxic zones.

──────────────────────────────────
# **XI. INCIDENT RECORDS**
* **The Rust-Garden Incident:** Kairen single-handedly stopped a **Rot-Swarm** by fusing the hive-queen to a boulder using his arm, then growing a tree through her chest.
* **The Broken Bridge:** Held a collapsing bridge up with his mechanical arm for 4 hours to let refugees escape, permanently damaging the servos.

──────────────────────────────────
# **XII. COUNTERS & WEAKNESSES**
* **Anti-Magic Fields:** Shut down his arm, leaving him crippled.
* **Fire (Exuroth):** Burns his plants and overheats his metal components.
* **Speedsters:** The Slab sword is slow; agile enemies can outmaneuver his heavy swings.

──────────────────────────────────
# **XIII. HOOKS**
* **The Seed:** He carries a single seed from the original Spirit Tree, looking for soil pure enough to plant it.
* **The Upgrade:** He is hunting a specific **Technomancer** who promised to upgrade his arm to feel touch again—for a price.

──────────────────────────────────
# **XIV. QUOTES**
* *"Don't look at the flowers. Look at the hand holding them."*
* *"My father gave me life. The world gave me iron. I will use both."*
* *"Stand behind the slab. Live. That is my order."*

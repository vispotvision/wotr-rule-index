### **Kaelrith Dorne, “The Iron Wound”**  
*(Veteran of the Withering Rebellions · Former Guild Mercenary · Free Lance of the Voyager Era)*  

**Type:** Mortal / Tempered Veteran  
**Era:** Late Withering Age – Voyager Era Transition  
**Realm of Origin:** Anguz (Outer World Frontier)  

---

**Profile Summary**  
Kaelrith Dorne, called *The Iron Wound*, is one of the few surviving veterans of the Withering Rebellions — a conflict that saw Wellspring fire tear through both armies and souls. Once enlisted as a Guild Accord auxiliary under the Enforcement Division, he deserted during the Siege of Cindralith after witnessing his entire unit dissolve under corrupted Aether rain.  

He has since wandered the fractured Realms as a **Free Lance**, serving no flag but his own. Tales mark him as both hero and executioner: a man who drags his broken sword through battlefields long after the war ended, ensuring no corrupted echo rises again.  

---

**Appearance**  
Tall, gaunt, and built from endurance rather than youth. His silver hair — once the pale blond of Anguzian lineages — has been bleached by Essence exposure. Countless scars score his face and neck, forming a cross-pattern ritual from an aborted Temperance binding. His eyes burn steel-grey under the shadow of exhaustion. The half-mantle he wears still carries the faded insignia of the Enforcement Division, though turned inside-out.  

---

**Temperance Path**  
Stage Achieved: **VII – Resolution**  
Path: **Body / Will / Endurance**  
Kaelrith’s Temperance stabilized not through study or ritual but sheer refusal to die. His Soul Crystal calcified under pressure, turning his Essence dense and unyielding — described by surviving medics as *“stone that bleeds.”*  

---

**Aether Class:** **Gravemind Soldier**  
A field-born Aetheric manifestation that converts pain into kinetic stability. The more damage Kaelrith sustains, the more anchored his Essence becomes. In combat, his blows land heavier with each wound — a literalization of the proverb, *“hurt him and he will not fall; he will sink.”*  

---

**Wellspring Affinity:** **Fixatio · Mortalis**  
His spirit resonates with the Wellspring of Endurance and the mortal truth of decay. Near him, weapons refuse to shatter and corpses refuse to rise. To some, this is mercy; to others, a curse of permanence.  

---

**Weapons & Relics**  
- **The Shardblade *Unrest*** — half a Guild-forged greatsword, broken during the Siege of Cindralith; its edge hums faintly with trapped battlefield echoes.  
- **Chain of Oathglass** — a Temperance relic that records the wearer’s last spoken vow in runic shimmer; Kaelrith’s reads, *“Never again under command.”*  
- **Veteran’s Mantle of Ash** — sewn from smolder-resistant guild cloth; rumored to absorb minor Essence flares and exhale smoke when Kaelrith nears battlefields.  

---

**Personality**  
Blunt, weary, unromantic. Kaelrith has no patience for politics or divine rhetoric — he measures worth only by endurance. He neither despises the Guild nor honors it; he simply no longer believes in permanence beyond one’s will to stand. Though feared by younger Accord soldiers, he is respected in silence; his presence alone often prevents panic.  

---

**Quotes**  
> “I’ve seen gods bleed. I’ve seen men pray to the hole that bled them.”  
> “Don’t call it courage. It’s what’s left when the fear burns out.”  
> “Every scar is a law written in pain. I just never learned to read them.”  

---

**Current Status (Voyager Era)**  
Last confirmed sighting places Kaelrith Dorne near the **Babyl frontier**, operating as an independent Wellspring guard and occasional Guild consultant during Concord clean-ups. Records note that when he walks near corrupted Wellsprings, their hum lowers — as if ashamed.  

Unofficial reports claim that **Seren Valenne**, the Grey Adjudicator, once invoked his aid during the Arbitration of Anguz. Neither spoke to each other throughout the entire trial, yet both left alive — and that was considered peace enough.

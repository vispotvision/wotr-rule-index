# Character Dossier — **Khasir Aluto, the Isocline Scribe**

**Role:** Temperature-clerk and moment-keeper; he writes **lines the weather obeys** so scenes cool, smoke parts, and last words don’t spoil.

**Affiliation:** Guild Accord • Star Chamber adjunct (Evidence & Preservation) • Reaper’s courier for fragile minutes

---

## Look & tells
Fur-collared field whites cut like a snow-lily; petal flaps stitched with fine script; ink-black gloves; calm gold eyes over a traveling folio. His breath is always a little colder than the room, never rude about it.

---

## Essence & Style
- **Flow:** Harmonia + Mnemata with a clean seam of Glyphica. Power appears as **good climate and legible time** rather than force.
- **In practice (what you’d see):** He draws a pale curve in the air; frost beads along it; noise thins and tempers settle to the same degree.

---

## Traits
- **Isocline Script** — lines he inks in air equalize temperature along their path; feverish corners cool, fog banks compress, fire loses its nerve.  
- **White Margin** — within arm’s reach of his open book, colors of rhetoric fade; only structure remains—perfect for testimony.  
- **Hearth-Loan** — he can move warmth from one body to another for a minute: spare a shivering witness, keep a fighter sharp.  
- **Pressed Breath** — a page can hold one dying exhale and its words unbruised; reopen the page later and the voice returns once.

---

## Implements
- **Polar Codex** — calf-bound field ledger whose pages trap breath, scent, and drift; corners are hoarfrosted tabs.  
- **Isobar Quill** — metal nib that writes on air, stone, and steam; dots at the ends become polite seals.  
- **Seal of Rime** — thumb signet that can keep a wound, footprint, or lipstick on a cup from changing for a day.

---

## Techniques (what you’d actually see)
- **Freeze-Frame:** He lifts a page; the room’s bustle slows to careful—just enough to walk a wounded child through crossfire without time noticing.  
- **Skim the Storm:** Three quick isobars across a street; Rot-smoke slides aside and stays sulking against the curbs.  
- **Cool the Room, Warm the Hands:** He drags a line around a council circle; arguments drop to indoor temperature while tea stays hot in the palms.  
- **Snow Letter:** He flicks a petal-flap; a drifting flake finds its addressee and whispers twelve measured words.  
- **Frost Gate:** A rime seal on a threshold; mobs step in and discover their shouting has coats on.

---

## Duties
- Preserve scenes for **Moros Pellayne**; keep evidence honest and witnesses comfortable.  
- Lay safe geometry for **Lucen** and **Cassiel** when heat and humidity would wreck protocol.  
- Travel with **Yukizumi** to carry brittle last breaths—open only when the room deserves them.

---

## Limits
- Noon deserts and drum-Domains fray his lines; he needs Kaede or Maelia to cool the air or break the beat first.  
- The Codex has finite clean pages; overwriting muddles a voice forever.  
- He refuses to “press” a child’s breath without their guardian’s assent.  
- True winter costs him sensation; too many Isoclines leave his fingers numb and his pen clumsy.

---

## Relationships
- **Shiragiku & Natsume:** flowers soothe, stitches hold; his lines keep the air hospitable.  
- **Cyrille Lacrim:** her saintwater rides his isobars like rails.  
- **Deimos, the Sin Eater:** Khasir keeps the moment intact so Deimos eats only what is owned.  
- **Vireline:** he hems her Measure with a cool ring that keeps blood from answering too quickly.

**Key Quote:** “Set the line, and weather—like people—chooses its better manners.”

---

## Hooks
1) **The Blurred Page:** A pressed breath smears; three heirs claim different last words—Khasir must rebuild the temperature of the room to read it true.  
2) **False Isocline:** Black Concord paints counterfeit cooling lines to lull a crowd; he must break trust in the fakes without breaking faith in the real.  
3) **Heat Court:** Talks demanded at high noon on brass steps; can he trade his own warmth to keep a treaty from war?

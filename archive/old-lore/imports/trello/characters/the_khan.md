# Character Dossier — **Khan Kirv, the Veil-Khan of the Dry Courts**

**Role:** Desert arbiter, caravan-lord, and sanctuary broker. He makes **oases out of agreements**—a circle of shade where blades behave and water remembers who it belongs to.

**Affiliation:** The Dry Courts (neutral league of oasis-cities) • Treaty partner to the Guild Accord • Occasional Black Watch liaison for sand-route warrants

---

## Look & tells
Black-and-gold hooded raiment cut for wind and audience both; palm-wraps inked with knot-script; a quiet gaze like sun through tent cloth. When he speaks, dust motes hang attentive.

---

## Essence & Style
- **Flow:** Harmonia + Glyphica with a dry braid of Animatria—he shapes atmosphere, sand, and etiquette until the desert **chooses** order.  
- **On scene (what you see):** He draws a sash loop in the air; heat slackens, the horizon stops wobbling, and tempers decide to drink before they decide to fight.

---

## Traits
- **Oasis Writ** — a water-right he declares takes root like a spring; vessels filled within that writ *won’t* pour for thieves.  
- **Mirage Ledger** — he can “price” rumors and mirages; spend his coin and false sightings evaporate.  
- **Sirocco Courtesy** — hot wind around him skims pride instead of skin; boasts arrive shorter, travel lighter.  
- **Truce Sand** — a pinch cast in a ring makes footfalls polite and arguments arrive in indoor voices.

---

## Implements
- **Khagan Sash** — gold-hemmed veil that catches lies like gnats; one snap clarifies who actually saw what.  
- **Calcite Beads** — prayer counter and compass; beads warm when an oath points the right way.  
- **Scorpion Signet** — bites Domain seams: a press of the seal and hostile glamour lifts like tent-flap.  
- **Goatskin **Khurrah**** — shared cup; water taken under it binds a one-night hospitality that even raiders respect.

---

## Techniques (shown, not told)
- **Oasis Circle:** He walks once around a fire, sifts Truce Sand; knives find themselves sheathed because the ground hints that chairs exist.  
- **Veil Count:** Sash sways; mirage duplicates blink out until only the real ambush remains, suddenly embarrassed.  
- **Salt of Witness:** A thumb of salt on the tongue of a braggart; their story shrinks to what happened.  
- **Caravan Lattice:** Beads click; routes braid; a stampede of refugees becomes three steady columns with water to spare.  
- **Scorpion Kiss:** Signet presses a Rot-sour gust; the taste leaves the air and the mob forgets the urge to burn.

---

## Duties
- Broker safe passage between feuding oases; convert raid-nights into markets by dawn; host “heat courts” where treaties are struck at noon so they survive midnight.

---

## Limits
- Power leans on **consent**—his writs wilt where no one agrees there *is* a well to share.  
- Rainstorms blur his mirage sense; he relies on **Khasir Aluto** to set isoclines and keep evidence dry.  
- A broken hospitality (blood spilled under the Khurrah) stains his sash—until amends are paid, his sand rings don’t hold.

---

## Relationships
- **Lucen Vale & Cassiel Asterion:** he sets the shade; they set the seating.  
- **Ocellith, Coin-Eyed Augur:** price and water meet—Kirv trusts Ocellith to count what a truce will really cost.  
- **Severin Bale (The Slayer):** when a kill-writ must cross dunes, Kirv opens a lawful corridor so it ends clean.  
- **Cyrille Lacrim:** her saintwater rides his caravan lattice; together they pull cities out of stampedes.  
- **Deimos, Sin Eater:** they trade burdens—Kirv cools the crowd so Deimos eats only what’s owned.

**Key Quote:** “Drink first. If your throat lies, I’ll hear it in the cup.”

---

## Hooks
1) **The Stolen Khurrah:** Bandits forge his shared cup; hospitality becomes a trap. Kirv must unpoison the custom without killing it.  
2) **Mirage Tax:** Black Concord sells “tolls” to cross fake oases; caravans vanish following receipts. He and Ocellith audit the horizon.  
3) **Heat Court at Dusk:** A tyrant refuses noon; trials at sunset fray into spectacle. Kirv must hold shade against theater and walk the Reaper through it.  
4) **Water with No Owner:** A newborn spring appears after a quake; every clan claims it. Kirv declares a one-season commons—if anyone bleeds, his sash goes dark.

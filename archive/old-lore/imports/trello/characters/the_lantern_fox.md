# **SERI ZELIRO-VANTHE**
**Alias / Title:** The Lantern-Fox, Threadwalker of the Silent Route
**Also Known As:** The Girl With the Open Book · Little Orrinal
**Epithet:** She Who Reads the Thread Before the Needle Moves

---

### **I. IDENTITY & CORE PROFILE**
* **Race / Lineage:** Moto Bloodline Descendant, Zeliro Line (Zeliro-Vanthe branch, fourth generation removed from the patriarch); mortal spirit, partially Fate-touched through diluted Bairosian resonance
* **Faction / Order / Era:** Zeliro-Vanthe House, Guild Accord Intelligence Division, Current Concord Era
* **Temperance Stage:** Stage 5, **Splintering** *(the self fracturing into competing truths. Seri's loyalty to the house she was born into collides with what she reads in the people she is sent to watch)*
* **Profile Summary:** Seri is the youngest active field operative of House Zeliro-Vanthe, a bloodline of spymasters, covert diplomats, and intelligence architects. She was pulled into active service three years ahead of schedule when the operative assigned to her training route was reassigned after a mission collapse. Small, quiet, and easily overlooked, she is precisely as dangerous as that reads. Her power draws from dilute Bairosian Fate resonance. She does not command destiny threads like a full Moto, but she can read them, like light through water, the shape visible even if the depth is uncertain. Her runic book is not decoration. It is her primary instrument, a living intelligence record, a Wellspring-reactive archive, and the thing she speaks to more honestly than she speaks to anyone else. Her goal is simple and quietly devastating: she wants to understand why the networks her ancestor built keep breaking at the places where they are most needed, and whether the problem is in the intelligence or in the people reading it.
* **Key Quote:** *"I do not need to know your secret. I need to know which one you are afraid I already know."*

---

### **II. CANONICAL SYSTEM METRICS**
* **Aether Class:** **Weaver** (Class II, Resonant)
  * **Behavior:** Seri's Aether does not project. It permeates. It moves along existing pathways, Wellspring channels, emotional resonance lines, the faint thread-traces left in the air by recent decisions. When she is working, the temperature drops by a degree or two, the smell of cold paper and copper settling over a space. Her lantern, which burns with a blue Wellspring-reactive flame, brightens slightly when she is actively reading a thread.
  * **Risk:** **Overcurrent Absorption.** Her Soul Crystal absorbs ambient Fate-thread data continuously. Under high-resonance conditions, she begins processing more information than a Stage 5 system is equipped to handle, producing cascading false-positive readings and emotional bleed from the subjects she is reading. Extended exposure causes Fate Bleed: her own thread begins fraying at the edges.
* **Wellspring Affinity:**
  * **Triune Core:** Judicium · Anamnesis · Mirithane
  * **Network:** 2 additional Wellsprings currently networked, Fixatio (anchoring discipline, from her house training) and Luminalis (the lantern-light affinity, inherited rather than trained).
  * **Targeted Uninfected:** Actively working toward Somnalis resonance, which her field handler considers premature and she considers overdue.
* **Temperance Path:** Seri's Murmuring Stage came early. She was reading people before she had language for what she was reading. Welling arrived through her first year of house training, when she learned that the same skill used to survive a difficult childhood was a formalized intelligence discipline. Ascension was not triumphant. It was the morning she read a thread that showed someone she loved would make a choice that would harm her, and she was right, and she did not intervene, because the thread also showed that intervening would make it worse. Flourishing was working correctly after that, which is its own kind of cost. Splintering arrived when she received her first mission file and recognized that two of the names in it were people who had done nothing wrong except be in the way of a political objective she was not told the full shape of.

### **III. SOUL PHYSICS & COMBAT SCALE**
* **Global Tier:** **C-Tier, Environment Bending** (field-functional; she is not a combatant and does not intend to become one unless the field makes the decision for her)
* **Path Bias:** **Fate Path** (60%) · **Spirit Path** (30%) · **Body Path** (10%)
* **Appearance:** Height: 4'11" (150 cm); Build: slight, with the stillness of a child who learned early that being small meant being overlooked, and then learned to use it. Skin: fair with a faint luminescence at the fingertips when Essence is active. Eyes: warm amber-gold, calm in the specific way of someone who is almost always reading the room. Hair: long, slate-blue fading to teal at the tips, worn loose with two small braids framed by a gold-embossed headband set with crescent and geometric motifs, a Zeliro-Vanthe house marker. She wears a fur-trimmed brown coat heavy enough for travel, a Wellspring-reactive runic tome hanging from her belt, and carries a wooden staff topped with a blue-flamed lantern that responds to ambient Fate-thread density.

#### **A. The Five Pillars (Soul Physics)**
> *Essence is Volume. Flux is Pressure. Output is Speed. Efficiency is Clarity. Aura is Gravity.*

* **Essence Capacity (Volume):** **1,450 EU**
  * *Functional Definition:* Total fuel tank/stamina, constrained by age and Stage, but extremely well-managed.
* **Flux Density (Internal Pressure):** **180 EU/g**
  * *Functional Definition:* Low-piercing, high-finesse. Her Flux is calibrated for thread-reading and passive infiltration, not force.
* **Aether Output (Flow Rate):** **310 AU/s**
  * *Functional Definition:* Moderate output. She prefers slow, sustained Aether movement over bursts.
* **Aetheric Efficiency (η):** **0.88**
  * *Functional Definition:* Unusually high for her Stage. House training emphasizes clarity over volume. She wastes almost nothing.
* **Attraction Scale (Aura/Presence):** **C-Tier: Distant Thunder**
  * *Functional Definition:* Most people do not feel Seri's presence as pressure. They feel it as the vague awareness that a conversation has a shape they did not author, that the room has been listening for longer than they noticed.

#### **B. Key Combat Parameters**
* **Strike Force:** 0.15 GN / 0.08 PJ (not her role; she has it only because Fate-Path generates passive force amplification)
* **Durability:** 0.12 TJ (Shielding via Fixatio lattice anchor) / minimal physical hardness
* **Speed:** Mach 0.5 standard; Mach 2 in Fate-Path burst (short duration, high cost, extremely disorienting for anyone in range whose thread she is currently reading)
* **Range:** City-block standard; regional via Wellspring-channel passive thread-reading, though this requires her lantern to be lit and stationary

### **IV. AXIOMATIC MANIFESTATIONS**
#### **A. Axiomatic Traits (Passive)**
* **Fox-Sight (Zeliro Inherited Trait):** Seri carries a dilute expression of the Zeliro bloodline's covert-intelligence acuity. She reads social dynamics, loyalty tensions, and unspoken intentions as faint Fate-threads visible to her peripheral vision. She cannot read full destinies, only the immediate causal terrain: who in a room is lying, who is afraid, and who is deciding something right now.
* **Threadmark Sensitivity:** As a Moto descendant, Seri is sensitive to the Threadmarks, the glowing Essence-inscriptions that appear on Moto blood when they make choices of true significance. She cannot generate them yet. But she can see them on others and read the emotional weight they carry, which functions as a secondary intelligence instrument in diplomatic contexts.

#### **B. Core Disciplines (Active)**
* **Lantern Reading** (Cost: **120 EU / scene**)
  * *Mechanic:* Seri raises her staff and allows the blue lantern-flame to extend into the ambient Wellspring channel of the area. The flame reacts to Fate-thread density, brighter where recent significant decisions have been made, dimmer where the space is metaphysically quiet. She can identify areas of high causal activity, the emotional charge of recent events in a location, and whether a Wellspring in the area has been tampered with or redirected.
  * *Application:* Judicium and Mirithane Wellsprings shape the flame's reading. Judicium weighs the moral character of the threads; Mirithane adds tonal quality. She does not just see that a decision was made. She reads whether it cost the person who made it.

* **Codex Inscription** (Cost: **200 EU per entry**)
  * *Mechanic:* Seri opens her runic tome and inscribes an observation using living Aether-ink, ink that is not simply written but stitched directly into the Wellspring-reactive pages through her Essence. An Inscribed entry becomes permanently readable by authorized Zeliro-Vanthe operatives through Wellspring-channel relay and cannot be falsified, altered, or destroyed without triggering a Judicium resonance alarm that notifies the house network.
  * *Application:* This is the primary intelligence function of her role. The tome is a living archive. Every entry she makes is simultaneously a personal record and a house intelligence asset. The Anamnesis Wellspring threading through the ink means entries carry not just the content of the observation but its emotional weight. A reader experiences a shadow of what Seri felt in the moment of writing.

* **Thread-Pull** (Cost: **480 EU / activation**)
  * *Mechanic:* Seri reaches into the local Fate-thread web and briefly tugs a single causal thread, not to sever it or rewrite it, but to make it taut. A taut thread makes the event it connects to more immediately probable within the next few minutes. She cannot create outcomes. She can make one outcome among the already-possible ones more likely to happen now rather than later. A guard who was going to take a break takes it now. A conversation that was going to start in ten minutes starts in two.
  * *Application:* This is her most demanding ability and her most strategically significant one. The Fate Path bias makes it possible; the Zeliro bloodline's covert-architecture heritage shapes how she learned to use it, not as a weapon, but as a key.

#### **C. Costs & Consequences**
* **Overcurrent Absorption:** Sustained Lantern Reading in high-resonance environments, near a Wellspring, during a major decision event, in proximity to a strong Moto bloodline bearer, causes her own thread-reading to blur. She begins receiving Fate-thread data she was not trying to access, including futures she does not want to see. The most dangerous version is reading the thread of someone she cares about.
* **Codex Bond Strain:** Every Inscribed entry costs Essence and also costs something harder to name. Each entry is written with authentic emotional weight, and writing that weight into permanence leaves Seri slightly less protected by distance from what she witnessed. Operatives who Inscribe too frequently without recovery time report progressive difficulty maintaining professional detachment. The house calls this Archive Creep. Seri calls it the reason her tome is sometimes heavier than its physical weight accounts for.

### **V. RELICS & DOMAIN**
* **Current Domain:** **Not yet formed.** She is approaching the threshold. Observers close to her in high-stakes moments report brief, localized perceptual effects: the air carrying the smell of old paper and lantern oil, the sense that the room has a structure beyond its physical walls, that conversations in it are being read by something that does not intrude but does not look away. This is the pre-formation pressure of a Domain whose law is still being written.
* **Relics & Implements:**
  * **The Zeliro Field Tome** *(runic book, gold-clasp, Wellspring-reactive pages)*: A Class B intelligence relic of House Zeliro-Vanthe. Issued to field operatives at confirmed Stage 4 and above. This particular copy is older than Seri. It was her handler's before it was hers, and carries seventeen years of prior inscription that Seri can read but not access fully, as older inscriptions require higher Stage clarity to interpret. The pages are blank to anyone outside the Zeliro-Vanthe blood-seal. To Seri, they contain everything she has written, everything her handler wrote, and the faint echo of what was written before him.
  * **The Lantern of the First Network** *(staff-mounted, blue flame, Mirithane-reactive)*: A field relic rather than a combat one. The flame burns on ambient Wellspring energy and never requires fuel. Its light is visible to Essence-sensitive beings as carrying a faint Fate-thread signature, which makes it both an instrument and an identifier. Any operative or hostile agent with thread-reading capability will recognize the lantern's signature as a Zeliro-Vanthe marker. Seri has been told this is a deterrent. She suspects it is also a test.

### **VI. LORE & TRAJECTORY**
* **Allegiance:** House Zeliro-Vanthe, Guild Accord Intelligence Division, and more quietly, the record she is building in her tome, which she has begun to suspect does not fully align with the record the house intends for her to build.
* **Incident Records (Historical):**
  * **The Mirefall Station Report:** Seri's first solo field inscription. She documented a meeting between a mid-tier Accord administrator and an unregistered Wellspring agent. The report was accurate. It was also filed into the classified tier before the administrative council saw it. Seri later confirmed through thread-reading that the administrator was a Zeliro-Vanthe asset. She did not submit a follow-up report. She inscribed the discrepancy in her tome instead, in the section the house does not have a relay-key for.
  * **The Three-Day Bridge Watch:** Assigned to monitor a border crossing for a suspected Seikai bloodline scion, Seri spent three days watching a family of travelers cross daily with goods. She concluded on day two that they were not the target. She filed a negative confirmation on day three. The house later confirmed her assessment and commended her efficiency. She did not mention that on day two she had read the youngest child's thread and seen it end badly in a version of events she did not allow to become probable. The Thread-Pull cost her 480 EU and a night's sleep she did not fully recover.
* **Counters & Weaknesses:**
  * **Thread Saturation Environments:** Spaces near major Wellspring intersections, active Titan grid points, or beings at Stage IX and above overwhelm her Overcurrent Absorption threshold rapidly. She cannot operate reliably in proximity to full Moto bloodline bearers using Fatum Oculus. The resonance drowns her reading entirely.
  * **Emotional Compromise:** Her thread-reading degrades significantly when she has a personal stake in the outcome. This is the primary reason field handlers are prohibited from maintaining personal relationships with intelligence assets. Seri's handler has noted this in her file. Seri has noted it in her tome, with additional observations about why this rule exists and what it costs the people it is designed to protect.
* **Current Status:** Active in eastern Accord territories, nominally assigned to a long-form monitoring task designated internally as the Quiet Route. Actually in the early stages of investigating why three Zeliro-Vanthe field reports from the previous year were reclassified without her handler's authorization.
* **Trajectory:** Seri is walking toward the moment every Zeliro descendant either turns away from or into: the day the intelligence she was trained to collect comes back pointing at the house that trained her. Her tome already has four entries she has not transmitted. She is deciding what a fifth one would mean.

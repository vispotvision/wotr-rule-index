# GORVAN ALDRIC VOSS

**Alias / Title:** The Maw That Held
**Also Known As:** Stonefather · Old Fang
**Epithet:** He Who Has Never Needed to Move Twice

***

## I. IDENTITY & CORE PROFILE

**Race / Lineage:** High Dwarf, Varn-line (Innerworld-forged, Sovereignty Wars survivor)
**Faction / Order / Era:** Formerly Iron Accord Warhost, Martial Division (lapsed). Currently independent. Late Withering Age through Concord Era.
**Temperance Stage:** Stage VIII, Transcendence

**Profile Summary:** Gorvan Voss is a geological fact wearing armor. When the Sovereignty Wars needed a wall that bled back, they sent Voss. His beast-jaw pauldrons are trophy-heads of two Construct-class entities killed during the Temple Rift Crisis, the engagement that shattered Dawi-Deepkin trust permanently. The jaws still carry residual Essence echoes. Enemies with Aether-sense report feeling watched by something dead but attentive. His role was never complex: hold the line until the line no longer needs holding. He left the Accord after the Rift. His Proto-Domain formed alone in deep stone over a decade. Petralon has begun treating him like terrain rather than a person. At Stage VIII, the distinction narrows. His skull-faced chest-mask is Iron Accord war-cult insignia, the only affiliation he has never renounced. He wears it because he won it, and Dawi do not surrender what they earn through blood.

**Key Quote:** _"The ground holds. I just stand on it longer than you."_

***

## II. CANONICAL SYSTEM METRICS

**Aether Class:** Saturant Vessel (Class III, Geometric-Inertial)

**Behavior:** Gorvan's Aether does not project. It settles. When he draws on a Wellspring, the environment physically responds before he does. Stone compresses, metal groan-hums, the air tastes of iron and old forge. His Aether saturates every surface he stands on, turning terrain into an extension of his will.

**Risk:** Inertial Lockout. When Essence density peaks, Gorvan's body resists lateral movement. He becomes nearly immovable, but his ability to pivot, angle, or react to flanking drops precipitously. Fast multi-vector opponents are his only structural threat.

**Wellspring Affinity:**

- **Triune Core:** Petralon · Monlithion · Coagulatio
- **Network:** 2 additional Wellsprings (Pyraeon, forge-blood inheritance; Mortalis, accumulated from battlefield proximity, uninvited but permanent)
- **Targeted Uninfected:** Materia Primordia. Felt twice, once during pauldron fitting, once when his axe took current shape. It appears to seek him through objects he touches long enough.

**Temperance Path:** Petralon stirred at Stage I during first siege posting, stone settling into stable configurations around him. Stage II, rubble from failed fortification wall landed in perfect radius without stacking logic. Stage III, Pyraeon inheritance activated through his axe in cold Innerworld siege. Stage IV, became primary siege-anchor, Coagulatio sealing minor wounds mid-combat. Stage V Splintering came at Temple Rift Crisis, he survived collapse because Petralon lattice rooted him to terrain. Fracture sits in Soul Crystal lower-right face, sealed but unhealed. He considers it structural. Stage VI Glory, left Accord, spent decade in deep stone, Domain formed without instruction. Stage VII Refraction, Mortalis fully woke, reads dying Essence like metal about to fail. Stage VIII Transcendence, weight as law, Proto-Domain stabilized, stopped aging visibly.

**Path Bias:** Body Path (80%) · Fate Path (15%) · Spirit Path (5%)

**Appearance:** Height: 4'9"; Build: collapsed mine shaft, massive compact frame engineered by absorbing force. White-silver mohawk crest (warrior-caste Iron Accord marking). Beard cascades to lower chest, white-thick with faint Petralon Aether frost-veins. Eyes deep-set amber-iron, ringed with dull forge-glow brightening under Essence load. Beast-jaw pauldrons, twin Construct trophy-heads rune-sealed on heavy iron plate. Spiked round shield, right-arm, Petralon-inscribed Impact Damping Seals, Weight Ablation matrix, Coagulatio ward rewritten forty-seven times, original inscription unreadable beneath. Great single-bladed axe, dark Dawi-forged metal, drinks light, slow heat signature. Skull-faced chest armor, Iron Accord death-mask crest worn at sternum (deliberate inversion so enemy sees it first). Iron-shod stone-soled greaves, faint crack-lines radiate through ground when stance plants. Aura: standing near him feels like building foundation, structural, the space has decided.

## IV. AXIOMATIC MANIFESTATIONS

### A. Axiomatic Traits (Passive)

**Geological Patience:** Body Path endurance functions like material structural tolerance, not fighter stamina. He does not tire, he loads. Can absorb and redistribute force at Stage VIII rates for sustained engagements that would reduce non-Dawi Body Path fighters to component pieces. Cost is not fatigue, it is Domain saturation deepening and lateral speed dropping incrementally as he becomes more of what the ground already is.

**Trophy Resonance, The Jaws That Watch:** Beast-jaw pauldrons carry residual Essence echo of Constructs they were harvested from. Aether-sensitive opponents within combat range register faint predatory awareness from them, not intelligent, not targeted, just present. Measurable psychological effect on Spirit-affinity enemies: Aether output drops 8% under sustained exposure due to involuntary Animatria suppression responding to perceived threat from dead source. Gorvan calls it good hardware.

***

### B. Named Techniques

**"Ironbed"** _(Foundation Form, Drilled)_

Gorvan drops into spread low stance, roots both feet through Petralon lattice into surface. While rooted, incoming kinetic force redirects downward into terrain rather than body. Strike force against him drops 60% for hits not targeting joints or shield-gaps. Domain saturation doubles intensity within 10m. Cannot move laterally while rooted. Has never needed to.

- **Trigger:** Contact with ground, deliberate weight distribution.
- **Cost:** Terrain beneath takes cumulative structural stress. On softer surfaces (packed earth, wood flooring, old stone), extended Ironbed leaves subsidence craters where he stood.

**"The Maw Opens"** _(Signature Technique, Personal)_

Gorvan swings axe in single full-rotation arc, not at opponent but through space they occupy. Pyraeon thread in blade loads over windup, detonates on extension: not as visible flame but thermal-kinetic shockwave traveling along blade's arc line. Heat does not burn on contact, it induces micro-fracture in anything ferrous within range (metal armor, weapons, buckles, hinges). Kinetic component behind it is killing physics. Thermal is force multiplier making opponent's protection betray them.

- **Trigger:** Full wind-up, minimum two beats. He does not rush this. Rushing would make it different move.
- **Cost:** Pyraeon thread needs approximately four minutes passive Essence circulation to reload full potency. Can execute weaker version faster, does not consider it same technique, does not name it.

**"Forty-Seven Wards"** _(Signature Technique, Shield-Specific)_

Overlapping Coagulatio inscriptions on shield form palimpsest array, forty-seven rewritten wards layered directly on each other, oldest inscription beneath carrying runesmith's original geometric intent, each newer one modifying without erasing. Produces not standard ward but layered will. When incoming attack strikes shield, Coagulatio array processes impact against every previous ward simultaneously. Each layer recognizing force pattern contributes refusal. Against novel attacks, only current wards engage. Against attack forms any previous ward has seen (after four hundred years, covers most of what exists), refusal is total. Shield does not deflect. It disagrees.

- **Cost:** Each successful total-refusal adds hairline stress to palimpsest array. Approximately three hundred total-refusals remaining before array fractures, needs complete reinscription from scratch. He tracks this. Does not share the number.

## V. PERSONALITY & WORLDVIEW

**Center:** Endurance as philosophy. Voss does not believe in winning. He believes in outlasting. Whatever the problem, if he stands in front of it long enough with sufficient Essence, problem resolves. Has been correct approximately 100% of time. Never examined whether success rate due to strength or patience.

**Fear:** Becoming architecture. Aware Petralon has begun treating him like terrain rather than person. At Stage VIII, distinction narrows. Watches for it. Does not know what he will do when gap closes entirely.

**Desire:** Siege that does not come. Retirement is not right word, implies he would stop. Wants, just once, long stretch of stone and silence no one needs him to hold against anything.

**Fatal Loop:** Can only rest when nothing left to stop. Always something left to stop. Has been awake, in relevant sense, for approximately four hundred years.

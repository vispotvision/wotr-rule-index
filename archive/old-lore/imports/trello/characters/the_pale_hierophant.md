# **AELUM**
**Alias / Title:** The Pale Hierophant · The Keeper of the Silver Vow
**Also Known As:** The Horned Saint · Spirit of the Unbroken Seal
**Epithet:** He Who Weaves the Silence Between Oaths

---

### **I. IDENTITY & CORE PROFILE**
* **Race / Lineage:** **Numen (High Ritual Eidolon)**
* **Faction / Order / Era:** **The Court of Veils** — **Era of the Mending** (Current)
* **Temperance Stage:** **Stage 9: Invocation**
* **Profile Summary:** Aelum is the spirit who presides over the moments that change a soul forever: coronations, marriages, and death rites. He manifests where great oaths are sworn. He is solemnity personified—a creature of opulent restraint who ensures that rituals are completed correctly. If a vow is broken, he becomes a terrifying avenger of the "Breach."
* **Key Quote:** *“Do not speak the words unless your soul can carry their weight. The veil is thin here, and I am listening.”*

---

### **II. CANONICAL SYSTEM METRICS**
* **Aether Class:** **Weaver (Binding/Ritual Type)**
  * **Behavior:** Aelum does not fight with bursts of energy; he fights with **Laws**. He creates zones where violence is forbidden or where damage is reflected. To fight him is to fight the rules of the engagement itself.
  * **Risk:** **Ritual Backlash**. Because he channels power through strict geometries and vows, disrupting his concentration causes the magical energy to collapse inward, shattering his porcelain-like form.
* **Wellspring Affinity:**
  * **Triune Core:** **Vantabriel (The Veil/Shadow)** · **Aurevane (Regality/Light)** · **Petralon (Gemstone/Structure)**
  * **Network:** **2** (Connected to **Nysera**, the Dreaming Titan).
  * **Targeted Uninfected:** N/A.
* **Temperance Path:** Aelum began as a **Wish** made by a dying king. Over centuries of witnessing royal rites, he gained a body of pearl and bone. He ascended to **Invocation** when he refused to let a corrupt emperor take the throne, holding the crown in stasis for a hundred years.

---

### **III. SOUL PHYSICS & COMBAT SCALE**
* **Global Tier:** **Tier SS (Court Noble)**
* **Path Bias:** **Spirit (50%)** · **Fate (40%)** · **Mind (10%)**
* **Appearance:** Height: **6’6”**; Build: **Slender/Regal**.
  * **Visuals:** Pale, marble-like skin. Short, white-gold hair. Four curved, bone-white horns crown his forehead, signifying his dominion over the four cardinal directions of the ritual circle. His eyes are violet with vertical pupils. He wears heavy ceremonial robes of silk and lace, weighed down by ropes of pearls and massive purple gemstones.

### **IV. AXIOMATIC MANIFESTATIONS**
#### **A. Axiomatic Traits (Passive)**
* **[Sanctuary of Pearls]:** Ranged projectiles targeting Aelum have a 50% chance to turn into harmless flower petals or incense smoke before impact.
* **[The Weight of Glory]:** Enemies within 10 meters feel the physical weight of their own sins/guilt, slowing movement speed by 10% per unconfessed crime.

#### **B. Core Disciplines (Active)**
* **[Decree of Silence]** (Cost: **5,000 EU**)
  * *Mechanic:* Aelum raises a hand. All sound is extinguished in the area. Casting spells that require spoken incantations becomes impossible.
* **[The Gemstone Gaze]** (Cost: **2,500 EU**)
  * *Mechanic:* His violet eyes flash. He targets a single enemy's soul. Inflicts **Stasis** (Crystal Encapsulation).

#### **C. Costs & Consequences**
* **[The Shattering]:** If Aelum is forced to break a vow or if his ritual circle is physically broken, he suffers massive internal damage.
* **[Immobility]:** To maintain his highest defenses, he cannot move.

---

### **V. RELICS & DOMAIN**
* **Current Domain:** **The Altar of the Hollow Crown**
* **Relics & Implements:**
  * **[The Veil of Separation]:** When dropped over an object or person, they phase out of reality, becoming untouchable but unable to act.
  * **[The Amethyst Censer]:** Releases a purple fog that reveals invisible entities and restores Essence to allies.

---

### **VI. LORE & TRAJECTORY**
* **Allegiance:** **Lawful Neutral / The High Council of Spirit**
* **Incident Records (Historical):**
  * **[The Wedding of Ash]:** Aelum appeared at the wedding of two warring generals. When they drew swords, he turned the entire banquet hall into stone.
* **Counters & Weaknesses:**
  * **[Chaos Magic]:** Unstructured magic disrupts his rigid laws.
  * **[Soundlessness]:** Assassins who use no magic words are invisible to his judgment.
* **Current Status:** **Presiding** over the sealed gates of the Old Capital.
* **Trajectory:** He seeks the **True King** worthy of lifting the Veil.

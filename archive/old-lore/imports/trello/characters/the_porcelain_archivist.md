### **Profile Summary**

Elowen Mireille appears harmless. She reads books in public salons, drinks tea slowly, and smiles gently.

What most do not know is that she remembers everything.

Every rumor.
Every contradiction.
Every slip of tone.

She does not gather information aggressively. It simply settles around her.

She is not dangerous because she lies.
She is dangerous because she knows.

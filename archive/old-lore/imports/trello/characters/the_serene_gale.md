**Iryen Maevith, “The Serene Gale”**

**Race:** Sylvaar–Human Hybrid
**Realm of Origin:** Kael’Nir, the City of Winded Prayer
**Temperance Tier:** Stage IX — _Reflection_
**Wellspring Affinity:** _Verdantia_ (Growth, Renewal, Breath)
**Aether Class:** _Zephyric Soul (Aeolian Conductor)_
**Essence Typology:** Transluminal–Symphonic; essence tuned to vibration, empathy, and memory flow
**Parun Glyph / Domain:** _Wyther — Motion and Harmony_
**Aspectal Alignment:** _Spirit / Body_

---

### **Appearance**

Silver-white hair, barely tethered by silk cords; his robes drift like mist over sunrise. Threads of rose fade into ivory along the hem, glowing faintly when touched by air. Ropes tied across his chest are ceremonial conduits — resonance binders that hum in the wind, each tone recording fragments of his thoughts.

His eyes, light amethyst, always half-lidded — not from fatigue, but tranquility. When he breathes, it’s said flowers orient toward him, believing him a breeze from Verdantia’s heart.

---

### **Personality**

Iryen is the calm between tempests. He speaks as if everything already happened, though he never reveals how he knows. Compassionate to a fault, but detached; his kindness feels ancient, like sunlight filtered through centuries of patience. Those who listen long enough begin to mirror his rhythm — slow, even, sure.

---

### **Abilities / Signature Techniques**

- **Resonant Gale:** Channels Wellspring wind through harmonic chants; converts voice into tangible current capable of deflecting Aetheric assaults.
- **Breath of Verdance:** Invokes life through air — healing wounds or sprouting flora in barren ground.
- **Echochain:** Binds multiple targets in a shared breath-cycle; allies gain synchronized motion, while foes suffer rhythm distortion.
- **Tranquil Spiral:** His meditation art — releases a circular field of sound that neutralizes rage, silences spells, and aligns Essence harmonics.

---

### **Weakness / Cost**

Overuse of resonance causes _Lung Fracture_, a spiritual ailment where his breath fragments into song. Prolonged strain risks dissolving him into pure sound — a fate he quietly accepts.

---

### **Known Lore**

Once a wind-priest of Kael’Nir’s temple choir, Iryen transcended human resonance during the _Verdant Ascension_. Refusing sainthood, he chose to wander as a _Harmony Arbiter_ for the Guild Accord — mediating Wellspring disputes through melody rather than combat. His name still lingers in monastic hymns as “the one who could still a storm with laughter.”

---

### **Symbolism**

He represents **Peace Through Motion** — movement without violence, breath without burden.
His emblem: a spiral of wind lines coiling around a single unfurled leaf.

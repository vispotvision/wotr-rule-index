# **I. Identity & Core Profile**

**Race / Lineage:**
Veyran Human · Atavistic Gale-Blood Mutation (Wind-Aspect Ancestry)

**Faction / Order / Era:**
Former Arena Gladiator of Kael’Nir · Wandering Mercenary · Imperial Calendar

**Temperance Stage:**
**Stage VI — Glory** (Recognition by Reality)

**Profile Summary:**
Karu Velnar is a battle-forged drifter whose Gale-Aether lineage awakened during years of forced arena combat. His body learned to channel motion, pressure, and kinetic drift instinctively — granting him savage, wind-augmented martial prowess.

Chains remain wrapped around his limbs not as restraints, but as **memory tokens**, each one symbolizing a victory he refuses to forget. His ultimate goal is liberation — not for himself, but for those still enslaved where he once fought.

**Key Quote:**
“Break the chains in your mind, and the real ones fall like dust.”

# **Core Disciplines**

### **1. Wyther Gale Rend**

Karu compresses wind pressure around his limb, releasing a crescent-like tearing strike.

---

### **2. Tempest Breaker Step**

A multidirectional displacement burst — Karu “breaks” airflow around his legs and instantaneously redirects his body, leaving a blurtrail.

### **3. Chainfang Lariat**

Swings his broken shackles like weighted extensions, amplifying torque.

- senses sharpen
- pressure sensitivity increases
- motion prediction becomes almost precognitive

## **Kaien Rhuven, the Silent Apothege**

### **Profile Summary**  
Kaien Rhuven is a calm herbalist who tends the *Verdant Hollow*, a quiet enclave at the edge of Senoth’s misted valleys. His movements are measured, every gesture softened by years of still practice. Though his touch seems humble — grinding leaves, steeping tinctures, tracing the pulse of sap through his fingers — there’s a strange precision to him, as if he’s always listening for something deeper than breath.  

Some say he can hear plants speak — not in words, but in the slow rhythm of their Essence. He treats both wound and grief, for in his craft, sorrow and fever are the same kind of imbalance: too much fire, too little patience.  

He rarely speaks of his past, only hinting at time once spent under the Guild Accord’s Aetheric studies before abandoning the Citadel life for silence and soil. He believes every poison once began as medicine.  

---

### **Appearance**  
Hair the color of twilight ink; eyes muted amethyst, holding a rest that seems learned, not given. His robes are ceremonial in cut, but threadbare from travel — beige layered over dusk-purple cloth, tied with faint red cords that sway like dried petals. A single short blade rests at his side, not for war but to harvest cleanly.  

---

### **Temperance Stage**  
**Stage VI — Devotion**  
Through stillness, he learned to channel empathy into restoration. His Essence does not burn — it seeps, gentle and inevitable.  

---

### **Wellspring Affinity**  
**Verdantia · Catharsis**  
Verdantia grants him communion with flora; Catharsis purges ailment through harmonic balance. Together they let him mend both matter and memory.  

---

### **Personality**  
Serene. Patient to the point of haunting. When he speaks, it’s often with the tone of someone quoting the wind rather than his own mind. He keeps no ledger, accepts no coin — only stories in exchange for cures.  

He tends to those consumed by their own Wellsprings, teaching that moderation is not denial but rhythm.  

---

### **Abilities**  
- **Aether Bloom:** summons restorative flora from ambient Essence.  
- **Chorus of Roots:** connects his pulse to nearby plants to absorb toxins and distribute vitality.  
- **Veil of Pollen:** creates a calming haze that lowers aggression and stabilizes Temperance flow.  

---

### **Quote**  
> “The loudest things in nature die first. I prefer what grows quietly.”

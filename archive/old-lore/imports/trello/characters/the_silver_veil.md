_“He sees perfection as debt, not grace.”_

**Stage VII — Resolution** (approaching _Stage VIII — Benediction_)

**Fixatio · Ascensio · Pyreveil** — embodies the paradox of containment, ascent, and burning renewal.

**The Hall of Shattered Mirrors** — a self-generated Domain of reflection and illusion; every surface within it replicates intent rather than image.

### **Status:**

Active field operative; under Guild observation for uncontrolled resonance surges.

---

## **I. Profile Summary**

Kaelric Varnier is a prodigy forged too quickly by the Accord’s psionic academies.
Once lauded for near-perfect resonance control, he now carries a fissure in his Soul Crystal — a crack that refracts his Essence into dozens of partial selves.
He moves with elegance learned, not lived; every motion rehearsed, every word poised to impress a reflection of himself that no longer answers.

He fights like someone desperate to prove the world was right about him.

---

## **II. Appearance**

Youthful frame, slim yet taut with trained precision.
Hair of pale silver-blond, always immaculate — except when resonance surges cause it to halo with Pyreveil’s red-gold flame.
Eyes: dual-toned, one grey-blue (Fixatio), one faintly embered (Pyreveil).
Uniform: half Guild Accord field attire, half self-modified — thin mirrored plates woven with sigil threads across the chest and forearms.
Carries an engraved longsword of translucent Aetherglass — a weapon that hums when his will falters.

---

**Tier:** C+ (Neighborhood/Storm Scale)

---

## **IV. Temperance Path**

Follows the **Path of Self-Containment**, a Fixatio-derived discipline where pride and restraint duel as mirrors of strength.
His mantra: _“To hold is to rise.”_
Temperance development marked by oscillation — bursts of Ascensio-fueled brilliance followed by Fixatio collapse.

---

## **V. Aether Class**

**Radiant Soul (Class III)** —
Channels both corporeal resonance and predictive motion; his Aether expands outward like light seeking surfaces.
However, due to partial Pyreveil alignment, the light burns — overexertion scorches his neural lattice.

---

## **VI. Wellspring Alignment**

- **Fixatio** (Containment / Will) — allows precise kinetic locks and blade-field stabilization.
- **Ascensio** (Elevation / Pride) — heightens psionic cognition; he perceives time as a spiral, not a line.
- **Pyreveil** (Flame / Renewal) — manifests as soul-fire rebirth, searing memory into Essence.

Together, these Wellsprings create a volatile triad — restraint, transcendence, and destruction feeding each other.

---

## **VII. Division / Rank / Allegiance**

**Guild Accord – Division of Resonant Arms**
Rank: _Aetherblade Lieutenant_
Allegiance: conditional loyalty; disciplinary hearings pending for reckless Domain manifestation during a training exercise.

---

## **VIII. Personality & Core Themes**

Arrogant yet unsure — a paradox of confidence without center.
He believes mastery justifies existence.
When alone, he mutters his combat forms like prayers, fearing silence will expose his doubt.
Core Themes: pride as prison · beauty as control · mirror as confession.

---

## **IX. Soul Crystal Profile**

- **Type:** Fragmented Horoscopic Crystal
- **Shape:** multi-faceted prism core with internal fracture (resonant echo feedback loop)
- **Coloration:** shifting from steel-blue to ember-gold when channeling
- **Behavior:** amplifies reflection-type Essence, creating viaforma echoes that mimic his movements seconds after they occur.

---

## **X. Traits**

- **Resonant Precision:** can parry attacks by manipulating the Aether vectors of his weapon mid-swing.
- **Temporal Afterimage:** leaves “delayed selves” behind, confusing perception-based opponents.
- **Glassborn Reflex:** any surface struck by him briefly mirrors his Soul Crystal’s current state, revealing emotional instability to perceptive foes.

---

## **XI. Relics & Implements**

**Aetherglass Longsword – _“Mirrhalion”_**
Forged from condensed Wellspring residue of Fixatio; refracts Essence to amplify thrust precision.
Can fragment into multiple blade-forms under Pyreveil surge (risk: self-laceration).

---

## **XII. Abilities & Disciplines**

- **Animatria Reflex (Echoform Discipline):** projects viaforma that replicate his swordsmanship in delayed synchrony.
- **Fixatio Restraint Field:** freezes local Aether movement for 0.4 seconds; opponents perceive this as “time-stutter.”
- **Ascensio Leap:** brief, directional psionic thrust capable of vertical ascension or ground collapse beneath him.
- **Pyreveil Rebirth:** when near death, Essence ignites; reconstitutes body at cost of memory erosion.

---

## **XIII. Costs & Consequences**

- Cognitive echo bleed; his reflections whisper back thoughts he didn’t mean.
- Post-battle tremors, mild blindness from Pyreveil flare.
- Emotional dissociation: cannot recall which “self” made which decision.

---

## **XIV. Associates & Bonds**

- **Instructor Lurein Velos:** Guild mentor, advocates patience (largely ignored).
- **Serenyra Vale:** rival turned confidante; often the only one who sees his fractures.
- **Echoform Entity “Kaelric-3”:** unstable projection gaining autonomy.

---

## **XV. Incident Records**

**“Mirror Hall Event” (VE: 925):**
During resonance sparring, Kaelric’s Domain breached containment, manifesting twenty-six viaforma simultaneously. The Hall collapsed inward; three cadets lost in reflection entrapment. Guild suppressed records.

---

## **XVI. Counters & Weaknesses**

- Shatter feedback: mirrors or reflective surfaces amplify his Essence backlash.
- Blind rage disables Fixatio stability.
- Anti-psionic zones nullify Ascensio Leap, grounding his movement style.

---

## **XVII. Hooks & Foreshadowing**

- The fracture in his Soul Crystal may evolve into a nascent Wellspring.
- Rumors say the reflections whisper the name of a coming Titan — _Orrenthal’s Heir._
- His next Temperance ascension (Benediction) could either heal or erase his individuality.

---

## **XVIII. Quotes**

## **XIX. Current Status / Arc Direction**

Kaelric’s arc moves toward **Temperance Stage VIII — Benediction**, the moment where restraint becomes surrender.
The Guild sees him as a weapon to polish; the Wellsprings see him as a warning.
He will either become a harmonized psionic knight of the Accord — or the first being to fracture into a living Wellspring.

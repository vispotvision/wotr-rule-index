# SHIRAN KAZUREN
**Alias:** The Umbra Lantern  
**Epithet:** He Who Walks Before the Storm  
**Position:** Middle sibling of the Kazuren family

## I. Identity
- **Age:** Late twenties  
- **Race:** Mireyan Eastern Human  
- **Realm:** Regency of Mireya  
- **Temperance Stage:** Stage V Flourishing  
- **Personality:** Soft-spoken, unreadable, quietly intense. Smiles rarely. Prefers silence. Carries a calm gravity that unnerves others. Values clarity and intention.

## II. Wellspring Metrics
- **Primary Wellspring:** Oneirion  
- **Secondary Wellspring:** Letheveil  
- **Drift Wellspring:** Tenebra  
- **Aether Class:** Lantern Soul Animus  
- **Aether Traits:** Light bends around him. Steps leave dream residue. People forget he entered until he speaks.

## III. Physical Profile
- **Build:** Tall and straight-backed  
- **Eyes:** Calm, half-lidded, dream-focused  
- **Hair:** Long, tied with dark wraps  
- **Clothing:** Layered Mireyan robes with ink-patterned embroidery  
- **Weapon:** Veilshade Umbrella, concealing an inner blade  
- **Presence:** Soundless, dimming, lantern-like aura that softens perception

## IV. Combat Scale
- **Tier:** High B-Tier  
- **Strike Force:** 9 gigajoules  
- **Durability:** 5 gigajoules  
- **Speed:** Appears slow but moves ahead of memory  
- **Aether Output:** 53,000 AU/s

## V. Axiomatic Traits
1. **Lantern Calm**  
   Stabilizes emotion and weakens hostile intent.
2. **Forgotten Step**  
   Opponents lose track of his position without noticing the lapse.
3. **Veilshade Echo**  
   Projects dream reflections that confuse prediction and targeting.

## VI. Signature Technique
### **Kechiryuu: Yumekage Sui**  
“Dream Shadow Slip”  
Creates synchronized dream-duplicates that move with him, blurring memory and creating lethal openings.

## VII. Lore Notes
- Once a monk of the Dreamfold Monastery  
- Left after a schism that only he fully understood  
- Wanders as a mediator and reality-reader  
- Known for preventing disasters before they begin  
- Believed to be approaching Stage VI if pushed by emotion or prophecy

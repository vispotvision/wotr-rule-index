## **I. IDENTITY & CORE PROFILE**

* **Race / Lineage:** Half-Infernal Elf · Abyssal Tethered Bloodline
* **Faction / Order / Era:** Exiled from the Pyre Courts · Withering Age
* **Temperance Stage:** Stage VI · Glory

### **Profile Summary**

Ilyrion Vantheir was not cast out for weakness.

He was exiled because containment failed.

His blood carries dormant infernal resonance that does not flare outward. It compresses. Smolders. Waits.

He speaks rarely. Moves minimally. Observes everything.

Those who mistake him for apathetic often do not survive the correction.

# **Rhaevan Serathiel**
**Alias / Title:** The Velvet Executioner  
**Also Known As:** White Requiem · Lord of the Last Whisper  
**Epithet:** *He Who Ends Without Raising His Voice*

---

## **I. IDENTITY & CORE PROFILE**

* **Race / Lineage:** Pureblood Veyran, Shadow-Adapted Variant  
* **Faction / Order / Era:** Sovereign of the Obsidian Veil Syndicate · Withering Age  
* **Temperance Stage:** Stage VII · Refraction  

### **Profile Summary**

Rhaevan Serathiel is the founder and undisputed leader of the Obsidian Veil, an assassin guild feared not for brutality but precision. Contracts do not ripple. They erase.

He does not rule through intimidation.  
He rules because even death prefers to move through him.

He studies people the way others study maps. Every twitch, every pause, every insecurity cataloged. He kills rarely himself, but when he does, it is instructional.

### **Key Quote**
*“Noise is for amateurs. True death arrives politely.”*

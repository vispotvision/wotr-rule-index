### **Elthiré Maen, “The Wandering String”**  
*(Guild Accord Operative · Voyager-Era Minstrel · Keeper of Forgotten Roads)*  

**Type:** Mortal / Accord Field Operative  
**Era:** Voyager Era (Imperial Year 739 – Present)  
**Realm of Origin:** Caedor, later itinerant through the Inner Realms and Babyl  

---

**Profile Summary**  
Elthiré Maen is a name whispered in both marketplaces and sanctums—a minstrel who appears where the world forgets to sing. Beneath the color of a vagabond performer lies a **Guild Accord Pathwarden**, sworn to carry messages, omens, and solace across Wellspring-scarred frontiers. His music, neither spell nor sermon, restores rhythm to regions where Essence falters. Wherever he plays, even the air seems to remember itself.  

---

**Appearance**  
He wears traveling robes dyed in **Verdant and Saffron layers**, the hues of Harmony and Renewal. A small songbird, *Pell*, perches upon his shoulder, echoing fragments of melodies lost to time. His instrument—**the Lyre of Seven Winds**—is carved from dreamwood harvested near the Wellspring of Benediction; each string hums with a different elemental resonance. His eyes are grey-green, the color of twilight moss, often half-lidded in reflection rather than sleep.  

---

**Temperance Path**  
Stage Achieved: **VI – Glory**  
Path: **Spirit / Memory / Harmony**  
Elthiré reached Glory not through combat nor meditation, but through the endless act of listening. He learned the harmonic truth that every emotion carries a tone—and by giving that tone form, suffering loses its dominion.  

---

**Aether Class:** **Resonant Harmonic Soul**  
A lineage of Essence artists capable of binding thought, feeling, and vibration into one continuum. His songs alter emotional gravity, easing conflict or synchronizing groups into single-minded purpose. In Guild records, such resonance phenomena are logged as *“Concord Events.”*  

---

**Wellspring Affinity:** **Benediction · Verdantia**  
Through Benediction he transforms grief into mercy; through Verdantia he awakens growth in withered lands. When both Wellsprings harmonize, flowers bloom even on stone and old wounds close without word or touch.  

---

**Guild Accord Classification:**  
**Echo Courier of the Ninth Route**  
(One of twelve envoys entrusted to traverse unanchored Wellspring territories, maintaining Accord presence through non-militant means.)  

---

**Personality**  
Soft-spoken, perceptive, and perpetually half-smiling—as if he hears some melody no one else can. He dislikes heroics, believing that the quiet mending of spirits carries greater weight than triumphs sung by others. Yet, when injustice rings too loud, his music hardens into storm.  

---

**Relics & Symbols**  
- **Lyre of Seven Winds** – Emits tonal fields that stabilize minor Wellsprings.  
- **Feather of Florwyn** – A relic plume woven into his hat, glowing faintly when truth is spoken.  
- **Guild Charm of Echo Route IX** – Allows instantaneous transmission of melody-coded messages across Accord channels.  

---

**Quotes**  
> “Every silence is a wound waiting to become a song.”  
> “The law binds; the tune reminds.”  
> “Carry the story, not the glory.”  

---

**Current Status (Voyager Era)**  
Last recorded sighting places him near the **Borders of Babyl**, mediating between Concord agents and local hymn-monks after the Rift Refrain Incident. Travelers report hearing his lyre echo through fog-veiled passes—each note a promise that somewhere, balance still breathes.

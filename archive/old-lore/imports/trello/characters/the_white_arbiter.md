### MASTER ARGEN VAEL’THAR — _“The White Arbiter”_

**Guild Accord Classification:** Prime Arbiter of the Concord Council
**Temperance Tier:** IX — _Reflection_
**Wellspring Affinity:** Judicium · Fixatio
**Aether Class:** Absolute / Resonant Law

---

**Profile Summary**
Argen Vael’thar is the longest-serving Prime Arbiter in recorded Accord history, credited with rewriting nearly a quarter of the Codex of Absolute Magical Law after the Crevice Wars. His rulings are whispered to weigh as heavily as Concord seals themselves. Calm, calculating, and often mistaken for gentle—he is the still center around which punishment, forgiveness, and order revolve.

The “White Arbiter” title stems from his custom mantle, woven from the fur of extinct frostbeasts slain during the Withering Age. He wears no sigils of pride, only the twin rings of Verdict around his neck—symbols said to vibrate when falsehood nears.

---

**Race / Lineage**
Human, of the Eressean bloodline. His family’s heritage traces to early law-keepers who mediated between the Dawn Guard and the First Accord, granting him both noble and sacred authority.

---

**Temperament**
Measured but not cold. Argen speaks in chords rather than words, often quoting sections of the Codex mid-conversation as if they were scripture. His kindness is judicial: mercy through structure, forgiveness through restitution. To his students, he teaches that “every law begins as a confession.”

---

**Known Abilities & Techniques**

- **Concord Resonance:** his voice triggers harmonic alignment; lies fracture when spoken nearby.
- **Seal of Equilibrium:** projects binding sigils that halt Wellspring collapse or Essence overflow.
- **Echo Tribunal:** manifests ethereal copies of past selves to conduct simultaneous judgment sessions.
- **Fixatio Mind:** grants him near-perfect memory of Codex law and metaphysical precedent.

---

**Affiliations**
Head of the _Arbitration Division_; direct overseer of all Concord Trials and Severance Rites. Reports only to the High Concordant. Has sanctioned fewer than five Total Severances in his century of service—a record of restraint that earned him the epithet _Merciful Iron._

---

**Companion**
A small, fox-like creature named _Saphri_, rumored to be an artificial familiar forged from Wellspring song. It serves as both aide and mnemonic anchor, ensuring his resonance remains stable during prolonged judgments.

---

**Equipment & Relics**

- _Rings of Verdict:_ paired artifacts that resonate with truth and falsehood; used during Resonance Hearings.
- _Mantle of Pale Equinox:_ fur-lined Concord vestment that nullifies emotional interference in court.
- _Codex Shard:_ personal crystalline fragment of the Lattice; records every verdict he utters.

---

**Current Status**
Active; presiding over the Reconstruction Councils following the Third Wellspring Collapse. Mentors young Sealwrights in Eresse’s Grand Hall of Harmony. His health wanes, but his will remains immovable.

---

**Symbol & Motto**
A silver scale overlaid with a broken circle — _balance preserved through fracture._

> “Judgment is not to end the guilty — it is to remind the living of consequence.”

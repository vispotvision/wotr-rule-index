# **VAEL**
**Alias / Title:** The Devourer's Index · The Form-Breaker
**Also Known As:** *Vael of Nothing* · *The Blindfolded Ruin*
**Epithet:** *He Who Does Not Need to See What He Is Ending*

***

### **I. IDENTITY & CORE PROFILE**
* **Race / Lineage:** Nullborn; a lineage produced when a soul spends too long in proximity to an active Oblivara Wellspring event during gestation. Not cursed. Not chosen. Simply made wrong, from the start, in the specific way that Uurgath finds useful.
* **Faction / Order / Era:** Unaffiliated — Era of the Great Spirit War
* **Temperance Stage:** Stage 9 — **Sublimation** *(The stage where the soul no longer performs its principle. It simply is it. Vael does not use dissolution as a tool. He is dissolution wearing a body for as long as that remains convenient.)*
* **Profile Summary:** Vael does not seek destruction in the way of men who have been wounded and want the world to share it. He seeks it the way water seeks the lowest point; without ideology, without grievance, because it is simply the direction his nature runs. He carries a sword too large for most men to lift and wears a blindfold he has not removed in eleven years because he decided, at some point, that seeing the forms of things made him slower to end them. The Archon Uurgath did not choose him out of cruelty. It chose him because he was already the answer to the question the Devourer of Forms keeps asking. His goal, to the extent that a man like Vael has goals, is to reach the thing at the center of the Great Spirit War that has not yet been broken. He believes something there is still holding a shape it was never meant to hold. He intends to correct this.
* **Key Quote:** *"I am not here to hurt you. I am here to finish what you already started when you decided to exist."*

***

### **II. CANONICAL SYSTEM METRICS**
* **Aether Class:** **Null-Devour** — his Essence does not build; it unmakes. Every construct, ward, and structured Aether field in his proximity loses coherence at the edges, the way a drawing loses its lines in rain.
  * **Behavior:** No visible Aether signature under normal conditions. Under full invocation, the air around him goes grey at the margins; not dark, not void, simply *less defined*, as though reality is having difficulty committing to a shape near him. The sound within 10 meters drops by half a register. The blindfold frays at its edges when his Essence peaks, threads dissolving into nothing.
  * **Risk:** **Form Erosion** — sustained Null-Devour output begins applying to Vael's own physical coherence. Extended peak-output invocation causes his body to lose definition at the extremities; fingers going translucent, the edge of the jaw softening. He is aware of this. He has decided it is an acceptable cost.
* **Wellspring Affinity:**
  * **Triune Core:** **Oblivara** *(void, erasure, the space left when a thing ceases to be)* · **Fractura** *(the breaking of form, the moment structure fails)* · **Dissolution** *(the undoing of fixed shape, the law that nothing stays what it was)*
  * **Network:** 2 additional Wellsprings passively networked *(Nihiloth, Cataclysm)*
* **Temperance Path:** Vael was born in the outer territories to a mother who did not survive the Oblivara proximity event that shaped him. He was raised by a frontier garrison who treated him functionally and without warmth, which he neither resented nor required. He progressed through early Temperance Stages not through mentorship or revelation but through a slow, methodical process of testing what he could end and what he could not, and adjusting his understanding accordingly. He removed the blindfold once, at Stage 6, to look at something he had been told he should see. He replaced it within the hour. He has not discussed this.

***

### **III. SOUL PHYSICS & COMBAT SCALE**
* **Global Tier:** **S-Tier, Null-Devour Apex**
* **Path Bias:** Body Path (60%) · Spirit Path (35%) · Fate Path (5%)
* **Appearance:** Height: 6'4"; Build: large and unhurried, the build of someone who has never needed to be fast because things have always ended before they could make speed matter. Skin: pale, with a faint grey undertone at the extremities, the early signature of Form Erosion. Hair: long, pale blond-white, loose, falling across the blindfold and the shoulders of his black feather-trimmed cloak without ceremony. Eyes: unseen; the blindfold is wide grey cloth, worn smooth at the edges, fraying slightly at its rightmost edge where his Essence peaks under strain. He wears a long white robe, blood-stained in irregular dark patches that are not entirely his own blood, belted loosely. The cloak is black, heavy, trimmed with dark feathers at the collar and shoulder. Three medallions at his chest in a vertical line, the only ornamentation he carries, each one engraved with a different Oblivara glyph. His sword, *The Index*, is enormous; broad, blunt-spined, its flat marked with a hexagonal geometric inlay that pulses faint grey when he channels through it. He carries it over one shoulder as though it weighs nothing, which, for him, it does not.

#### **A. The Five Pillars**
* **Essence Capacity:** 4,200,000 EU
* **Flux Density:** 18,000 EU/g — not piercing; *dissolving*. His Essence does not cut through resistance; it convinces resistance that it was never necessary.
* **Aether Output:** 9,400 AU/s
* **Aetheric Efficiency (η):** 0.88 — drops to 0.61 under Form Erosion onset.
* **Attraction Scale:** **S-Tier: The Quiet Before.** His presence does not announce itself. It arrives as a subtraction; the ambient Aether in his vicinity thins, constructs flicker at the edges, and lower-Temperance beings experience the specific cognitive sensation of forgetting what they were about to say. Animals do not flee from him. They go still and do not look directly at him, the way they behave near things too large to run from.

#### **B. Key Combat Parameters**
* **Strike Force:** 890 GN physical / 4,400 PJ Dissolution burst
* **Durability:** 3,100 PJ shielding / 980 GJ physical
* **Speed:** Mach 94 sustained; instantaneous Form-Step within Null field
* **Range:** City-Block direct / Continental passive Oblivara resonance under full Devour state

### **IV. AXIOMATIC MANIFESTATIONS**

#### **A. Axiomatic Traits (Passive)**
* **The Index:** Vael perceives the structural integrity of any Aetheric construct, living soul, or physical form within 80 meters as a complete dissolution-map. He knows exactly where a thing is most likely to come apart. He cannot choose not to know this. Everything he looks at, even through the blindfold, arrives with this information attached. This is why he wears the blindfold. It slows the information down to a rate he finds tolerable.
* **Form Attrition:** Aetheric wards, shields, and structured constructs within 15 meters of Vael lose 8% of their Essence coherence per turn passively, without him doing anything. Extended proximity to him causes permanent Wellspring-resonance degradation in beings below Stage 6. Guild Accord has three documented cases of fighters losing Wellspring access entirely after sustained proximity engagement. None of the three have recovered.
* **Null Margin:** Physical attacks made against Vael have a 30% chance of partially dissolving on contact with his body before dealing full impact; not blocked, not deflected, simply *less present* when they arrive than when they were thrown. This is not a conscious defense. It is the Oblivara resonance rejecting defined impact events.

#### **B. Core Disciplines (Active)**

* **THE DEVOUR** (Cost: 800,000 EU)
  * *Mechanic:* Vael raises *The Index* and holds it level, edge-forward, for exactly three seconds. Nothing visible happens for those three seconds. On the fourth, every Aetheric construct within a 200-meter radius loses structural coherence simultaneously. Wards drop. Summoned forms dissolve. Channeled techniques in-progress collapse back into their casters as raw Essence backlash. Permanent Wellspring-resonance damage is applied to anyone below Stage 7 within the radius.
  * *Application:* Pure Uurgath principle at combat scale. This is not an attack. It is a correction.

* **FORM-STEP** (Cost: 140,000 EU)
  * *Mechanic:* Vael briefly dissolves his own physical coherence, allowing him to pass through solid matter and structured Aether fields as though neither exists. Duration: 4 seconds maximum. During Form-Step, he cannot interact with the physical world and Form Erosion accelerates at triple rate. He uses this exclusively to reposition; he has no interest in escaping.
  * *Application:* Fractura and Oblivara in coordination; the breaking of the law that says a body must remain on one side of a surface.

* **THE FINAL INDEX** (Cost: 2,600,000 EU + accelerated Form Erosion onset)
  * *Mechanic:* Vael removes the blindfold. He looks at a single target directly. The dissolution-map the Index trait generates passively sharpens to surgical precision; he perceives the exact sequence of structural failures that would cause the target's physical form, Soul Crystal, and Wellspring resonance to come apart simultaneously. He does not need to touch them. The act of perceiving the sequence initiates it. The target has three turns to either remove themselves from his line of sight or produce a counter that disrupts his Null-Devour field entirely. If they cannot, the sequence begins. Vael replaces the blindfold immediately after, regardless of outcome. He does not explain why.
  * *Application:* He has used this seven times. Five targets did not survive it. One produced a Benediction-class counter that interrupted the sequence. One he chose, mid-process, to stop. He has not explained that either.

#### **C. Costs & Consequences**
* **Form Erosion:** Every peak-output invocation accelerates the dissolution of Vael's own physical coherence. His extremities have already begun losing definition under normal conditions; the fingertips are translucent in strong light. The Guild Accord's internal assessment estimates he has between four and eight years of physical coherence remaining at his current output rate. He has been informed of this. He has not adjusted his output rate.
* **The Index Burden:** The passive dissolution-map the Index generates cannot be turned off. Vael experiences every form around him as a sequence of failure points. This is not painful in a conventional sense. It is simply a quality of perception that makes ordinary existence a constant low-level exercise in not acting on information. The blindfold reduces its intensity. Without it, the information density is enough to cause complete dissociation from present-tense reality within approximately forty seconds.

### **V. RELICS & DOMAIN**

* **Current Domain:** **The Unwritten Margin**
  * *Function:* Activates passively when Vael remains in a location for more than six hours. The environment within a 300-meter radius begins to lose Aetheric definition; Wellspring resonance readings become unreliable, soul-echo readings return null, and scrying attempts directed at the area dissolve before completing. Those who enter the Unwritten Margin experience mild identity-displacement; a sensation of being briefly uncertain of their own name. It clears within minutes of leaving. Usually.

* **Relics & Implements:**
  * **The Index** *(T9, Null-Forged, Oblivara-saturated)*: The greatsword is not a weapon in any conventional taxonomy. It has no edge in the traditional sense; the blade is broad and blunt-spined, its mass alone sufficient for physical impact, but its actual function is as a Null-Devour conductor. Strikes made with it bypass Aetheric shielding entirely, applying dissolution force directly to the Soul Crystal of the target. Its hexagonal inlay is a Fractura glyph array that Vael did not put there; the sword arrived with it, from a source he has not disclosed. It is the only thing in his possession that he treats with anything resembling care.
  * **The Three Medallions** *(T6, Oblivara-engraved)*: Worn at his chest in a vertical line. Each is engraved with a distinct Oblivara glyph. Their function is suppressive; they reduce the passive Form Attrition radius from 15 meters to 8 during periods when Vael is not in active combat, preventing unintended Wellspring degradation in proximity. He began wearing them after the third documented case of permanent resonance loss in a bystander.

### **VI. LORE & TRAJECTORY**

* **Allegiance:** None formal. The Guild Accord has an open file on him; not a warrant, a file. There is a distinction. They have not yet decided what category he belongs to.

* **Incident Records:**
  * **The Unwriting of the Halvren Accord Post (VE 883):** A Guild Accord garrison stationed at the Halvren frontier intersection reported complete Wellspring-resonance failure across all twenty-three stationed members over a period of six days. No hostile engagement was recorded. Vael was confirmed in the area by three separate witness accounts. When Guild investigators arrived, the post's Aetheric equipment had dissolved to base components. The stone walls remained. Everything else was structurally present but metaphysically null. Vael was not present. He had moved on.
  * **The Form-Breaking at Tarvel's Crossing (VE 891):** Vael removed the blindfold in a combat engagement for the first time on record. The target was a Stage 8 Judicium-class enforcer who had been executing a sanctioned Accord warrant. The Final Index was initiated. The enforcer survived, one of the two non-fatal applications on record, by producing a Benediction-class counter. The enforcer has not discussed what they experienced during the three turns the sequence was active. The Accord file notes they requested immediate reassignment to a non-combat position.

* **Counters & Weaknesses:**
  * **Benediction or Coagula Resonance:** Form-affirmation Wellsprings that operate on the law of fixing, sealing, and making permanent act as direct structural counters to Oblivara-Fractura dissolution. A being attuned to Benediction or Coagula can temporarily reinforce their own soul-structure and the Aetheric constructs around them against Form Attrition. This is the only category of counter that has consistently interrupted his abilities. He is aware of this. He does not appear concerned.
  * **The Blindfold:** Removing the blindfold is both his most devastating ability and his most significant vulnerability window. During The Final Index, Vael is fully committed to a single target and his own Form Erosion accelerates at maximum rate. Any being capable of surviving three turns and disrupting his Null-Devour field during that window has a genuine opening. The problem is surviving three turns.

* **Current Status:** Active. Location variable. Moving toward the center of the Great Spirit War's metaphysical theatre, following the dissolution-map the Index generates toward the thing he believes is holding a shape it was never meant to hold.
* **Trajectory:** Vael is not a villain in any framework that requires malice. He is a principle in motion. The Great Spirit War has produced exactly the kind of large, over-structured, over-warded, cosmologically rigid conflict that Uurgath's resonance finds intolerable. He is walking toward the center of it with the patience of someone who has never needed to hurry because the things he is walking toward have never successfully moved away.

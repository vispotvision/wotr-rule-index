## **I. Profile Summary**

Valen Karth is known as the storm-spear of the Concord. His combat doctrine merges Wellspring propulsion with spear-based centripetal Aether control — he “rides the air” rather than walks upon it. His weapon, _Raethspire_, is both anchor and fulcrum; each strike fractures the Aether grid momentarily, allowing him to reposition mid-strike.
Where Kaelvar Thren endures by stillness, Valen conquers through pure motion — the embodiment of kinesis over order.

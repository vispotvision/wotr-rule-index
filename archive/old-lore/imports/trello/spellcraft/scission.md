### **Type:**

**Magic System** – Ritus Class (Essence Severance / Metaphysical Separation)

---

### **Summary:**

**Ritus “Scission”** is a high-risk rite designed to **separate one metaphysical component from another**—be it **Trait from soul**, **Essence from body**, **memory from identity**, or **Wellspring from host**. Unlike suppression or sealing, *Scission* is a clean, intentional **cutting**—a sacred and dangerous act of **division** used when a spiritual connection has become corrupted, overloaded, or incompatible with survival.

It is invoked during exorcisms, soul extractions, Trait purging, and emergency Temperance failures. Scission is **not destruction**, but once performed, the removed piece may not return whole—often leaving behind **fragmentation, amnesia, or altered resonance**. For this reason, it is considered a **last resort** by most guilds and Concord orders.

---

### **Key Figures:**

* **Kujo Valorin** — Performed a forbidden mass-Scission to sever his army’s mortality, transforming them into Veil-Bound revenants.
* **Zethyra the Veinwrought** — Mastered precise Scission techniques to extract curses without damaging the surrounding Animatria.

---

### **Historical Importance:**

* Instrumental in the **purging of Abythron-echoes** following the First War of Silence.
* Used to save key heroes during the **Soulquake of Tirres**, where unstable Traits threatened to rupture their own Soul Crystals.
* A corrupted Scission performed during the **Fracturing of Edenis** caused a Realm-wide feedback loop that split dozens of Wellsprings into parasitic offshoots.
* Essential to the **doctrine of spiritual autonomy**, particularly among the Pactless of the Outer World, who believe one must be able to cut their own fate free.

---

### **Connections:**

* Closely linked to the **Wellsprings of Verdiction**, **Fixation**, and **Echo**—those governing law, form, and memory.
* Requires Parun glyphs such as:

  * **Ci** (mirror/severance)
  * **Ra** (binding/rebinding)
  * **Th** (threshold)
  * **Ka** (destiny/intervention)
* Commonly follows **Ritus: Sacrament**, or is reinforced by **Synergia: Wreath** to contain volatile remnants.
* May result in the creation of **Spiritual Scars**, **Trait Echoes**, or **Detached Memory Constructs** if improperly stabilized.

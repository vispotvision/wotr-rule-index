## **The Iron Tree**

**Classification:** Dougou Ozumu Zettai Combat Branch / True Iron Bison Derivative
**Parent Discipline:** Shinsei Tetsugyū — The True Iron Bison
**Core Law:** _“Nothing leaves. Everything becomes weight.”_

The **Iron Tree** is the offensive branch of Dougou’s True Iron Bison.

Where the True Iron Bison governs his sealed body-engine, internal compression, seven chambers, and pressure economy, the Iron Tree is how that sealed pressure becomes combat technique.

Each branch of the Iron Tree converts accumulated internal force into a different physical expression: fist, kick, elbow, knee, shoulder, head, step, breath, or full-body impact. None of these techniques are “martial arts” in the normal sense. They are release-valves controlled so precisely that they do not break Dougou’s containment.

The Iron Tree does not teach Dougou how to hit.

It teaches the world which part of him it should fear next.

---

## **Core Principle**

The Iron Tree follows three rules:

- **Every limb is a branch.**
- **Every branch carries the weight of the root.**
- **Every impact returns pressure to the body unless Dougou chooses release.**

Most fighters strike by sending force outward.

Dougou strikes by letting the sealed weight inside him pass through one chosen branch for a single instant.

That is why even his simplest techniques feel impossible to block. They are not powered by the limb alone. They are powered by the whole sealed system behind it.

## **Primary Branches**

### **1. Tetsu no Kobushi — The Iron Fist**

**Type:** Contact Compression Strike
**Range:** 0–3 meters
**Output:** 1–5 MJ normally / 8–20 MJ at apex chamber release
**Force Estimate:** 1,000,000–12,000,000 N depending on compression state

The Iron Fist is Dougou’s most direct Iron Tree technique.

He gathers sealed pressure from the root, solar, heart, and shoulder-line chambers, then drives it through the fist without allowing full external leakage. The force does not explode outward. It enters the target and compresses inward.

Against armor, the plate may survive while the ribs beneath collapse.

Against stone, fractures spread from the point of contact like fault lines.

Against bodies, the strike feels less like being punched and more like being told by gravity that one’s structure is no longer valid.

**Effect:**

- caves armor inward,
- shatters ribs and sternum,
- ruptures organs through blunt transfer,
- fractures stone supports,
- breaks shields through backforce,
- destabilizes defensive Aether lattices on contact.

**Combat Note:**
The Iron Fist is not always a killing blow. Dougou can use it at reduced compression to punish, test, or disable. At full chamber release, it is a structural execution.

---

### **2. Tetsu no Geri — The Iron Kick**

**Type:** Kinetic Vault Release / Lower-Body Compression Strike
**Range:** 0–5 meters
**Output:** 2–7 MJ normally / 10–25 MJ at apex
**Force Estimate:** 2,000,000–15,000,000 N depending on stance and chamber seal

The Iron Kick draws from the sacral and root chambers, making it one of Dougou’s most dangerous physical techniques. Unlike the Iron Fist, which focuses inward rupture, the Iron Kick specializes in displacement, crushing, and battlefield repositioning.

When Dougou kicks, the lower world moves with him.

A low Iron Kick can break a giant’s knee.

A midline Iron Kick can launch an armored opponent through a wall.

A downward Iron Kick can crater the ground and produce a shockwave through terrain.

**Effect:**

- destroys knees, hips, and lower-body structure,
- launches heavy targets 30–100 meters depending on size,
- breaks shield walls,
- caves gates and barricades,
- sends ground shock through packed earth,
- disrupts footwork through impact tremor.

**Combat Note:**
Dougou uses the Iron Kick when he wants to move the battlefield, not merely damage a target.

---

### **3. Tetsu no Hiji — The Iron Elbow**

**Type:** Short-Range Rupture Branch
**Range:** 0–1 meter
**Output:** 1–4 MJ
**Specialty:** Armor-gap punishment, clinch destruction, close-quarters execution

The Iron Elbow is used when an enemy survives inside Dougou’s reach and mistakes proximity for opportunity.

It is shorter than the Iron Fist, but more brutal in grappling range. Dougou drives compressed weight through the elbow with almost no wind-up, making it difficult to read or interrupt.

**Effect:**

- breaks jaws and collarbones,
- splits helmets through blunt collapse,
- crushes ribs in grapples,
- destroys shoulder guards,
- interrupts charging opponents,
- punishes anyone attempting to clinch him.

**Combat Note:**
The Iron Elbow is Dougou’s answer to speed fighters who slip past his fist.

---

### **4. Tetsu no Hiza — The Iron Knee**

**Type:** Rising Compression Strike
**Range:** 0–1.5 meters
**Output:** 2–6 MJ
**Specialty:** Core rupture, anti-grapple, vertical lift impact

The Iron Knee channels pressure upward from the root and sacral chambers through the hips. It is especially lethal against opponents who are grabbed, pinned, staggered, or pulled into Dougou’s centerline.

**Effect:**

- ruptures abdomen and diaphragm,
- breaks pelvis or lower ribs,
- launches enemies upward,
- crushes armor at the waistline,
- interrupts breath-based techniques,
- destroys balance at close range.

**Combat Note:**
If Dougou grabs someone first, the Iron Knee is often the next and last thing they experience.

---

### **5. Tetsu no Kata — The Iron Shoulder**

**Type:** Full-Body Entry / Mass Collision
**Range:** 0–3 meters
**Output:** 3–10 MJ
**Specialty:** Breaking formations, gates, guards, and braced defenses

The Iron Shoulder is Dougou turning his upper body into a battering ram.

Unlike the Iron Fist, it does not localize force into a narrow point. It uses broad-body mass to overwhelm structure, making it ideal against shield walls, large beasts, and opponents trying to hold ground.

**Effect:**

- breaks formations,
- caves shields inward,
- knocks large enemies off balance,
- fractures barricades,
- smashes through doors and gates,
- punishes braced defensive stances.

**Combat Note:**
The Iron Shoulder is the polite version of being hit by a charging fortress.

---

### **6. Tetsu no Atama — The Iron Head**

**Type:** Brutal Close-Range Compression Strike
**Range:** 0–0.5 meters
**Output:** 1–3 MJ
**Specialty:** Grapple punishment, intimidation, anti-clinch violence

Dougou uses the Iron Head rarely, not because it is weak, but because it is personally contemptuous. It is a technique used when an opponent has gotten close enough to touch him and he wants to remind them that every part of his body is a weapon.

**Effect:**

- breaks noses, teeth, and facial bones,
- cracks helmets,
- disrupts concentration,
- ends grappling attempts,
- causes instant disorientation.

**Combat Note:**
When Dougou uses this, it usually means he considers the opponent beneath formal technique.

---

### **7. Tetsu no Ho — The Iron Step**

**Type:** Footwork Pressure Art
**Range:** Self / 5–80 meter shock influence depending on force
**Output:** Variable
**Specialty:** Terrain control, shockwave generation, stance destruction

The Iron Step is the movement branch of the Iron Tree.

Dougou plants his foot and forces the ground to accept his weight. The result can be subtle or catastrophic. At low output, it breaks footing. At high output, it sends a tremor through the battlefield.

**Effect:**

- ruins enemy footwork,
- buckles weak terrain,
- interrupts charges,
- cracks stone roads,
- shakes buildings,
- creates local tremors,
- anchors Dougou against knockback.

**Combat Note:**
The Iron Step is often the beginning of Dougou’s pressure economy. Before he strikes the body, he strikes the ground beneath it.

## **Iron Tree Apex Branch**

### **Kokushin Gyūha — Black-Heart Bison Break**

**Type:** Apex Contact Rupture Art
**Parent Branch:** Iron Fist / Iron Shoulder / Iron Knee compatible
**Force:** 5,000,000–12,000,000 N
**Energy:** 8–20 MJ

The Black-Heart Bison Break is not a separate style. It is the apex fruit of the Iron Tree.

Dougou selects one branch, usually fist, shoulder, or knee, and releases one sealed chamber’s pressure through a single contact point. The force enters the target instead of spreading outward.

Against armor, it creates catastrophic backforce.

Against stone, it fractures the grain from within.

Against Domains, it attacks the anchor rather than the surface.

Against bodies, it makes the skeleton fail as a load-bearing argument.

**Doctrine:**
_“Do not break the wall. Make the wall confess where it was already tired.”_

## **Combat Use**

Dougou rarely chains Iron Tree techniques quickly. He does not need to.

A normal fighter uses combinations to create openings.

Dougou uses one branch to create consequence.

If he throws the Iron Fist, the opponent’s guard changes.

If he uses the Iron Kick, the battlefield changes.

If he plants the Iron Step, the terrain changes.

If he releases the Black-Heart Bison Break, the fight changes.

The Iron Tree is not fast in rhythm.

It is final in meaning.

---

## **Weaknesses of the Iron Tree**

- Requires contact or near-contact.
- Strongest when Dougou is grounded.
- Airborne enemies reduce its full pressure economy.
- Spirit Path attacks can bypass the physical branch system.
- Void fields can interrupt chamber recognition.
- Overuse risks containment strain.
- Emotional instability can cause pressure leakage.

The Iron Tree is devastating because Dougou’s body is the root.

But if the root is disrupted, every branch suffers.

---

## **Quick Summary**

**The True Iron Bison** is Dougou’s complete sealed body-engine.

**The Iron Tree** is the combat branch system grown from it.

**The Iron Fist** is one branch.

**The Iron Kick** is another.

Every Iron technique is a different way of letting the same sealed pressure touch the world.

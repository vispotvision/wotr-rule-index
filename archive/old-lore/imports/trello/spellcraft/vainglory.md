### **Type:** \[Magic System / Animatria Sub-Discipline]

---

### **Summary:**

**Vainglory** is a forbidden Animatria technique in which a practitioner attempts to craft a Pneuma or Eidolon not from clarity or resonance, but from **self-idolatry, spiritual projection, or delusion**. The result is a **Vainglory Eidolon**—a distorted spirit-form that mirrors the summoner’s **idealized self**, inflated ego, or desperate craving for identity.

Unlike a true Eidolon, which harmonizes with the summoner’s Soul Crystal, the Vainglory Eidolon is a **false resonance construct**—born from **emotional distortion** rather than Temperance. It mimics perfection, divinity, or unreachable power, and becomes increasingly unstable the longer it remains active.

Some advanced soulbinders create Vainglory Eidola intentionally, using them to test or deceive divine forces. Others awaken them unknowingly during failed ascension, Temperance collapse, or botched Trait realization.

---

### **Key Figures:**

* **Serenhal Caelmorne** – Once projected a version of himself during a corrupted Moetana trial; the Vainglory spirit attempted to replace him in the Harmonic Mirror Rite.
* **Yelara Vintress** – Binder of the Rose-Glass Order; her Vainglory Eidolon turned against her and stole her original Pneuma during a Severance Bloom, taking her place in summoning.
* **Raziel Ados** – A forgotten Ascendant who used Vainglory Eidolons to impersonate himself across multiple Domains. Ultimately consumed by his own "echo legion."

---

### **Historical Importance:**

* First documented in the **Tome of Lurid Echoes**, where fractured Eidola began resisting control by mirroring false versions of their summoners.
* Reclassified as a **Mechanica-tier anomaly** during the **Era of Calamity**, when the practice led to the **Lament of the Nine Mirrors**—an event where nine summoners were each replaced by their own false echoes.
* Preserved as a **forbidden Animatria art** in the shattered rites of the **Hall of Reflective Descent**.
* A single guild, the **Order of the Hollow Veil**, teaches controlled Vainglory invocation as a spiritual confrontation rite.

---

### **Connections:**

* **Crystalia** – Occurs most often during **Trait Overreach** or fractured Soul Crystal compression.
* **Traitus** – Traits tied to pride, dominance, or projection (e.g., *Unbroken Will*, *Crown of Echo*) are often linked to Vainglory instability.
* **Wellsprings** – Typically manifests during **Apotheosis**, **Return**, or **Discord**, especially when ego exceeds spiritual refinement.
* **Synergia** – Occasionally leads to spontaneous **Ego Collapse Phenomena**, forming “Echo-Doubles” that persist outside the caster’s influence.

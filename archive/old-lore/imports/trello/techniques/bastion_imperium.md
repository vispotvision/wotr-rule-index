# **BASTION IMPERIUM**

(The Crowned Stillness)

I. **Source and Concept**
Primary Wellspring: **Coagula** (the binding of form and law)
Secondary Wellspring: **Judicium** (the weight of verdict and consequence)
Path: Body–Fate
Aether Class: Iron Will Aether
Core Concept: Drakvor anchors himself to the world and commands it to halt. The ground beneath him becomes doctrine, and his stance becomes law.

II. **Function and Philosophy**
Function: Defensive • Field Control
Outcomes:

- Drakvor becomes immovable within his stance.
- Forces below threshold lose power against him.
- Ground stabilizes into a metaphysical bulwark.


Philosophy: “A king first commands himself.”

III. **Mechanics Spec**
Name: Bastion Imperium
Type: Defensive / Field Anchor
Temperance Requirement: Stage VIII Benediction
Activation: Declared
Range or Area: 10 meters around Drakvor
Duration per turn: 4 turns
Tags: Coagula Binding, Judicium Weight, Structural Aether

IV. **Limits, Weaknesses, Cost**
Limits: Cannot move while active.
Weaknesses: Elevation loss or unstable terrain reduces power.
Cost Soul-Cost Currency Law: severe Aether compression in legs and spine, fatigue after release.

V. **Counters and Interactions**
Counters: Anti-structure Wellsprings, Sublimatio destabilization, airborne opponents.

VI. **Imagery, Sensation, Language**
Visual: Earth ripples, dark sigils bloom beneath his feet.
Sound: Distant iron groan.
Sensation: The air thickens around his torso like plate over bone.

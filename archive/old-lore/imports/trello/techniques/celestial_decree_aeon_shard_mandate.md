### **Core Concept (Fantasy):**

Opalis externalizes her Temperance into a living crystalline shard—the **Aeon-Shard**—a manifestation of ten Wellspring laws in simultaneous contradiction and balance. The shard becomes her judgment made physical: capable of destroying, restoring, purifying, refracting, or rewriting, depending solely on the intention she emanates. To onlookers, it appears as the world briefly obeying a new metaphysical constitution.

# IV. **Limits, Weaknesses, & Cost**

### **Limits**

- **Cannot overwrite another Emnation-level Technique or Domain** without direct harmonic superiority.
- **Cannot create new consciousness or living souls**, only repair or dismantle existing Essence.
- **Cannot maintain contradictory shard states indefinitely** (e.g., destruction + restoration simultaneously across wide areas).

### **Weaknesses**

- **Dream Incoherence:** Excess Oneirion may destabilize the shard’s judgment logic.
- **Fractura Overload:** Too many active shards risk autonomous divergence.
- **Penance Feedback:** Overuse of restorative modes may rebound purification onto Opalis herself.
- **Anti-Harmonic Environments:** Wellsprings like Verdantia or Hypnather can distort key functions.

### **Cost — The Soul-Cost Currency Law**

**Harmonic Drain:**
Sustained use exhausts the integrative harmonics of Opalis’ soul, risking partial Temperance shearing or temporary emotional flattening.

**Identity Bleed:**
The Aeon-Shard mirrors Opalis’ inner state; prolonged projection risks merging her psyche with its law-logic, causing dissociation or fractal echoing of her thought patterns.

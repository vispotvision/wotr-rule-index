## Corona Lunaris

_(The Lunar Corona)_

### I. Source & Concept

**Source (Wellspring):**

- Celestial — lunar law, reflective light
- Harmonic — resonance-based redirection

**Path:** Spirit
**Aether Class:** Symphonic Scholar

**Core Concept (Fantasy):**
Using the Lunarch Crown, Serenyra manifests a crescent halo of refracted moonlight that absorbs and refracts incoming Aetheric attacks, bleeding them out as lambent radiance.

---

### II. Function + Philosophy

**Function:**
A [Shield] / [Mitigation] art.
She invokes the Crown’s crystal matrix, causing an arc of silver light to blossom around herself or an ally. Incoming spells are stripped of lethal vectors and turned into harmless illumination.

**Outcomes:**

- High reduction of hostile Aether damage
- Conversion of part of that damage into ambient light (fuel for certain later arts)

**Philosophy:**
“Not all light reveals. Some light forgets.”

---

### III. Mechanics Spec

**Name:** Corona Lunaris
**Type:** [Shield] / [Damage Mitigation]

**Temperance Requirement:** Stage V — Resonance

**Activation:**
Reactive or declared; a touch to the circlet and a whispered command.

**Range / Area:**

- Self or one ally within 20 m
- Encases target in a half-sphere corona

**Duration per turn:**
2 turns sustained, or until Durability threshold (100 GJ) is reached.

**Tags:**
[Celestial] [Shield] [Refraction] [Support]

---

### IV. Limits, Weaknesses, & Cost

**Limits**

- Optimized against magical/Aetheric attacks; physical force still transfers as blunt impact
- Once the 100 GJ absorption threshold is met, the corona shatters

**Weaknesses**

- Darkness / Umbral Wellsprings may feed on the bled light
- Void strikes can pierce directly, bypassing the reflective law

**Cost — The Soul-Cost Currency Law**

- **Lunar Burn:** Overuse scorches her Essence pathways, causing headaches, light sensitivity, and temporary aura distortion.
- **Crown Fatigue:** The Lunarch Crown requires time to cool between peak uses; repeated activations in one scene reduce its efficiency.

---

### V. Counters & Interactions

- **Light-Devouring Domains:** Convert the released light into fuel instead of dispersal.
- **Multi-Vector Assaults:** Attacks from many directions at once can overwhelm the corona’s refraction calculus.

---

### VI. Imagery, Sensation, & Language

**Visual Signature:** A thin, silver arc blossoms into a half-sphere, etched with shifting Ma glyphs; incoming spells dissolve into soft auroras.

**Sound Cue:** A quiet, glassy chime, like moonlight ringing.

**Sensation:** The protected target feels cool, weightless pressure—as if submerged in night air.

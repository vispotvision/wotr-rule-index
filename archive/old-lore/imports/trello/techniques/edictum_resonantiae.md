## Edictum Resonantiae

_(The Edict of Resonance)_

### I. Source & Concept

**Source (Wellspring):**

- Harmonic — law-by-resonance
- Celestial — domain of ordered principle
- Dream (minor) — conceptual flexibility

**Path:** Fate
**Aether Class:** Symphonic Scholar

**Core Concept (Fantasy):**
At the peak of her art, Serenyra issues a temporary, localized law: within a bounded space, one specific kind of interaction—violence, lies, teleportation, etc.—is harmonically suppressed or rerouted.

---

### II. Function + Philosophy

**Function:**
A [High-Tier Field] / [Law] art.
She proclaims a sonic Edict. A subtle, omnipresent tone underlays reality within the affected space, rewriting the “rules” of how certain actions resolve.

**Outcomes:**

- Bans or heavily penalizes specified actions (e.g., hostile Aether use, Wellspring access)
- Enforces alternative outcomes (e.g., lethal blows becoming non-lethal disarms)

**Philosophy:**
“I will not command your heart. Only the song your actions must answer to.”

---

### III. Mechanics Spec

**Name:** Edictum Resonantiae
**Type:** [Field] / [Law] / [Control]

**Temperance Requirement:** Stage VIII — Refraction (borderline Zenith-level art)

**Activation:**
Ritual-fast; she speaks a clear verbal Edict anchored in precise wording, reinforced by a staff-strike into the ground.

**Range / Area:**

- 200 m radius dome
- Centered on her position

**Duration per turn:**
3 turns at full potency; lingering echo for 1–2 turns afterward at reduced strength.

**Tags:**
[Harmonic] [Law] [Field] [Control]

---

### IV. Limits, Weaknesses, & Cost

**Limits**

- Only one category of action can be bound per casting (e.g., “No Wellspring may be invoked to shed blood here.”)
- The clearer and narrower the phrasing, the stronger the effect

**Weaknesses**

- Entities of equal or higher Temperance with strong personal Domains can partially ignore the Edict
- Fate or Archonic mandates may override her local law

**Cost — The Soul-Cost Currency Law**

- **Resonance Debt:** She must personally embody the Edict; any time she violates its spirit, backlash tears through her Soul Crystal.
- **Voice Scar:** Repeated use roughens her metaphysical “voice,” making subsequent Edicts harder to sustain without harm.

---

### V. Counters & Interactions

- **Counter-Edicts:** Another lawcaster can declare an opposing principle; the two resonate, potentially collapsing both.
- **Void Interference:** True Void pits “no law” against “this law,” creating dangerous patches of reality-tear where neither fully holds.

---

### VI. Imagery, Sensation, & Language

**Visual Signature:** A faint script of floating glyphs circles the dome’s boundary; each time someone attempts a bound action, the glyphs flare briefly.

**Sound Cue:** A constant, low tone—barely audible—that subtly shifts whenever the Edict is challenged.

**Sensation:** Everyone inside feels a gentle resistance when trying to perform the prohibited action, like trying to speak underwater.

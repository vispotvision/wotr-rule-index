# **FINAL MERCY**

(The Law of Erasure)

I. **Source and Concept**
Primary Wellspring: Judicium
Secondary: Exuroth
Path: Spirit
Aether Class: Principle Dominion Soul
Core Concept: Francis concentrates his entire Dirge Pulse into a single point. The strike does not destroy matter. It deletes the concept of the target’s continuance.

II. **Function and Philosophy**
Function: Execution • Concept Erasure
Outcomes:

- Target loses the metaphysical idea of persistence.
- Soul, memory, and Principle alignment collapse.
- Post-erasure field becomes emotionally silent.


Philosophy: “True mercy is silence.”

III. **Mechanics Spec**
Name: Final Mercy
Type: Execution / Conceptual Kill
Temperance Requirement: Stage XIV Zenith
Activation: Ritual-fast
Range or Area: 150 meters
Duration per turn: Instant
Tags: Concept Erasure, Dirge Pulse, Judicium Verdict

IV. **Limits, Weaknesses, Cost**
Limits: Cannot be used in Dirge Ascension.
Weaknesses: Archonic protections, identity anchors.
Cost: 4 percent memory bleed; heavy paradox recoil.

V. **Counters and Interactions**
Counters: Absolute identity anchors, Fate-forged souls, Archon-level shielding.

VI. **Imagery, Sensation, Language**
Visual: Red line appears; target dissolves into ash-like law motes.
Sound: A single downward chime.
Sensation: Reality holds its breath.

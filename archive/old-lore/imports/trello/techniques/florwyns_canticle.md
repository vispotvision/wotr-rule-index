## Florwyn’s Canticle

_(The Canticle of Florwyn)_

### I. Source & Concept

**Source (Wellspring):**

- Verdantia — growth, restoration (attenuated)
- Harmonic — choral resonance

**Path:** Spirit
**Aether Class:** Symphonic Scholar

**Core Concept (Fantasy):**
Drawing on her Florwyn bloodline echo, Serenyra sings a low, wordless hymn that coaxes life and Essence to knit, sprout, and mend.

---

### II. Function + Philosophy

**Function:**
A [Healing] / [Terrain] art.
She channels a harmonic that resonates with plant-life and living Aether. Wounds close, shattered Essence stabilizes, and the earth itself may briefly bloom.

**Outcomes:**

- Moderate healing of physical injuries
- Stabilization of Essence fractures, preventing escalation
- Creation of temporary Verdant cover (vines, roots, blossoms)

**Philosophy:**
“Growth is the original spell; all others are commentary.”

---

### III. Mechanics Spec

**Name:** Florwyn’s Canticle
**Type:** [Healing] / [Support] / [Terrain]

**Temperance Requirement:** Stage VI — Illumination

**Activation:**
Declared; she must maintain the hymn uninterrupted.

**Range / Area:**

- 30 m radius around her
- Strongest effect within 10 m

**Duration per turn:**
Up to 4 turns sustained.

**Tags:**
[Verdantia] [Harmonic] [Healing] [Support]

---

### IV. Limits, Weaknesses, & Cost

**Limits**

- Healing is gradual, not instant; cannot regrow lost limbs in-combat
- Works best where natural life already exists

**Weaknesses**

- Necrotic / Abyssal Wellsprings resist or poison the Canticle
- Environmental sterility (dead realms, void-scar) weakens the hymn

**Cost — The Soul-Cost Currency Law**

- **Lineage Strain:** Overuse tugs her Verdantia echo closer, threatening to entangle her soul with the Wellspring in ways she cannot control.
- **Emotional Vulnerability:** The hymn exposes her emotional signature; empathic foes can read or exploit her state.

---

### V. Counters & Interactions

- **Blight Domains:** Can twist the growth into thorns or parasitic vines.
- **Silence Edicts:** Cut the sound, collapsing the harmonic before it completes.

---

### VI. Imagery, Sensation, & Language

**Visual Signature:** Soft green-gold motes rise from the ground; hairline fractures in armor and flesh seal as tiny silver leaves flicker and fade.

**Sound Cue:** A layered, choral hum with no clear lyrics; it feels like forests breathing.

**Sensation:** Warmth in the bones, a gentle pressure behind the heart, the memory of sunlight through canopy.

# **HARMONIC NULL-ASCENSION**

_(“The Song That Unmakes the Unworthy”)_
A pure offensive Emanation, expressing authority of harmonic law itself.

---

# I. **Source & Concept**

### **Source (Wellspring):**

**Primary Wellsprings:**

- **Orrenthal** — harmonic resonance; the fundamental chord of existence
- **Cataclysm** — ruptured genesis; destructive over-resolution of a tone
- **Fractura** — splitting of frequencies into lethal divergences

**Secondary Wellsprings:**

- **Dissolution** — the reduction of unstable harmonics to silence
- **Basilithe** — law-binding structure of harmonic collapse
- **Tarturon** — entropy speeding the decay of frequencies
- **Tenebra** — shadow equilibrium absorbing discord
- **Penance** — searing consequence applied through resonance
- **Petralon** — density anchoring the harmonic blow
- **Oneirion** — conceptual modulation of vibrational “identity”

### **Path:**

**Temperance XII: Emnation — Self-as-Harmonic Source, World-as-Instrument**

### **Aether Class:**

**Emanant Harmonic Destructive Law**

### **Core Concept (Fantasy):**

Opalis emits a **singular harmonic ascension-tone** that forces the target’s Essence, structure, and metaphysical pattern to vibrate into a state they _cannot support_. The tone ascends past the harmonic thresholds of the target’s being, reaching a point where their form collapses into either silence (Dissolution) or shattering (Cataclysm).
To observers, the strike is not seen—it is _heard_ and _felt_, a lethal alignment of all realities into one unbearable resonance.

---

# II. **Function + Philosophy**

### **Function:**

A **[Offense] / [Control]** harmonic strike that targets the structural resonance of a being, object, or technique and forcibly escalates it until failure.

### **Outcomes:**

1. **Harmonic Overload:**
   Resonant frequencies in the target escalate beyond stability, causing fracturing, implosion, or soul-pattern distortion.
2. **Silencing Collapse:**
   Dissolution sweeps through the compromised structure, erasing parts of the target that fail to match the harmonic.
3. **Cataclysmic Backwash:**
   On complete failure, the target releases a shockwave of dissonance that tears or shreds surrounding matter or illusions.

### **Philosophy:**

_“All things vibrate. All things end. I merely accelerate the truth.”_

# IV. Limits, Weaknesses, & Cost

### **Limits**

- Cannot forcibly ascend the harmonic of an equal or higher Temperance-tier being.
- Cannot target something with _no structural resonance_ (pure void, pure dreamstuff, or anti-harmonic fields).
- Cannot sustain the ascension-tone for more than a few breaths without cost exploding.

### **Weaknesses**

- **Pure Basilithe constructs** resist harmonic collapse via law-stability.
- **Oneirion-dominant illusions** may decouple their resonance to evade locking.
- **Silence fields** or anti-vibrational Wellsprings (Verdantia, Cymorath) can damp or redirect the tone.

### **Cost — The Soul-Cost Currency Law**

**Cost Type 1 — Resonant Backlash:**
Brief harmonic damage rebounds into Opalis’ own Essence, causing tremors, disorientation, or blood hum.

**Cost Type 2 — Frequency Drift:**
Her emotional or cognitive resonance may slip, making her speech echo unnaturally or her thoughts “double” for several minutes.

---

# V. Counters & Interactions

### **Counter Type 1 — Harmonic Nullification Shells:**

Abilities that impose a flat anti-resonance “silence” block the ascension-tone.

### **Counter Type 2 — Frequency Spreading:**

Targets who can split their resonance (Fractura, Eidolyn, dream-forms) can avoid allowing any single frequency to escalate beyond stability.

# VI. Imagery, Sensation, & Language

### **Visual Signature:**

Concentric rings of light-shadow ripple from Opalis’ gesture; the air briefly crystallizes with vibrating glyphs of gold, violet, and deep black.
Targets show fractal cracks of light along their form before collapse.

### **Sound Cue:**

A rising harmonic tone, starting as silence
→ faint hum
→ choir-like resonance
→ shattering bell-crack as the target collapses.

### **Sensation:**

- **To Opalis:** A controlled surge, like tuning the world by hand.
- **To allies:** A deep chest vibration and subtle vertigo.
- **To targets:** Crushing pressure, internal heat, and then a moment of absolute stillness—as if their existence forgot itself.

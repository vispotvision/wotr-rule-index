# **HYPNATHER DESCENT RITE**

_(The Sleep-Sinking Judgement)_

**I. Source & Concept**
Source (Wellsprings):

- **Hypnather** — half-sleep paralysis and sensory fog
- **Letheveil** — memory-blur siphoning
- **Oneirion** — dream descent

Path: Spirit → Attraction Drift

Core Concept:
A forced psychic descent that drags the target into a liminal half-sleep.
Their body reacts sluggishly; their memory fractures; time feels delayed.

**II. Function + Philosophy**
Function: Debuff, Paralysis, Sensory Distortion
Outcomes:

- Slow reflexes
- Limb-heaviness
- Short-term amnesia
- False sensory input

Philosophy:
“To descend is to reveal what you fear when you blink.”

**III. Mechanics Spec**
Name: Hypnather Descent Rite
Type: Dream-Gravity Invocation
Temperance Requirement: Stage VII Refraction
Activation: Declared / Line-of-Sight
Range / Area: 60 meters
Duration per turn: **1.5 turns**
Tags: Paralysis, Sleep, Mindfog, Subconscious Distortion

**IV. Limits & Cost**
Limits:

- Ineffective on entities without biological consciousness
- Requires line-of-sight

Costs:

- **Sleep Debt**: His waking mind destabilizes
- **Memory Backlash**: Risk of absorbing the target’s fear or trauma

**V. Counters**

- High Spirit Path resistance
- Verdantia clarity fields
- Pain-trigger shock techniques

**VI. Imagery & Sensation**
Visual: Light dims as though slipping behind eyelids.
Sound: A slowed heartbeat.
Sensation: Heavy limbs, sinking spine, warmth behind the eyes.

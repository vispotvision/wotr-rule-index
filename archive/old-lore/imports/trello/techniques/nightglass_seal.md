# **Nightglass Seal**

_(Tenebra • Oblivara — Shadow + Forgetting)_

## **I. Source & Concept**

**Source (Wellspring):**
Tenebra — darkness, concealment, secret resonance.
Oblivara — forgetting, erasure, memory void.

**Path:** Fate + Shadow
**Aether Class:** Umbral Barrier

**Core Concept:**
A ward that erases what touches it — not physically, but existentially. Attacks lose their “intent” as they pass through, nullifying their purpose and memory. The barrier distorts space slightly, creating a hazy, dreamlike veil.

---

## **II. Function + Philosophy**

**Function:** Defense / Anti-Intent Ward
**Outcomes:**

- Nullifies abilities by erasing their metaphysical “purpose.”
- Blinds enemies who stare into it (memory smear).
- Obscures everything behind it.

**Philosophy:**
“To forget a blow is to survive it.”

---

## **III. Mechanics Spec**

**Name:** Nightglass Seal
**Type:** Conceptual Ward
**Temperance Requirement:** Stage VIII — Benediction
**Activation:** Reactive
**Range / Area:** 2-meter pane
**Duration per turn:** 2 turns
**Tags:** [Ward] [Erasure] [Anti-Magic] [Shadow]

---

## **IV. Limits, Weaknesses & Cost**

- Does nothing against purely physical force with no intent.
- Tenebra-aligned foes partially bypass it.
- Prolonged use causes memory distortion in the caster.

**Cost:**
Severe cognitive strain; short-term memory loss.

---

## **V. Counters & Interactions**

- Judicium-based clarity wards pierce it.
- Absolution light shatters its shadow.
- Memory-binding runes override its erasure effect.

---

## **VI. Imagery, Sensation, Language**

**Visual:** A rippling sheet of black-glass mist, distorting reflections.
**Sound:** Soft static, like memories dissolving.
**Sensation:** Cold behind the eyes; heartbeat slows.

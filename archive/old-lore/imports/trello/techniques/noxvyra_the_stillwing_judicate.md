## **NOX’VYRA, THE STILLWING JUDICATE**

**Invoker:** Tovain Zethriel *(Cryost Path)*  
**Summon Class:** Greater Eidolon / Dream-Law Manifestation  
**Codex Tag:** `FIX-SOM–E14 “Nox’Vyra Manifestation”`  
**Category:** Aetheric Entity – Lawborne Construct  
**Primary Wellsprings:** Fixatio · Judicium · Somnalis  
**Temperance Threshold:** Stage X (Revelation min) | Stage XIII (Aetherium optimal)  
**Aether Class Interaction:** Reverberant Soul – Stasis Mode  

---

### **I. Manifestation Overview**
Nox’Vyra is a dream-forged eidolon born from the Cryost’s Wellspring convergence.  
It embodies the moment before judgment is pronounced—the stillness between sin and consequence.  
When called, it forms through mirrored surfaces; reflections darken, then unfold into wings of frozen shadow.  
Air, sound, and light all hush within its reach.

**Appearance:**  
A vast avian construct of onyx feathers and glass-spined wings.  
Eyes glow with twin violet Parun glyphs (*Ur* and *Wy*).  
Rain crystallizes around it into vertical runes that fall upward.  

---

### **II. Summoning Procedure**
**Circle Design:**  
Outer Ring – Fixatio Spiral (anchors space)  
Middle Ring – Somnalis Bloom (draws Dream current)  
Core Glyph – Judicium Flame (permits command)

**Invocation Steps:**  
1. Exhale Cryost Breath until frost forms over the sigil.  
2. Recite the phrase “Vei-Nox Vyra’tel — Let silence take flight beneath law.”  
3. Offer a single drop of blood into a mirror or pool; reflection fractures.  
4. From the fracture, Nox’Vyra emerges and the field temperature plunges.  

---

### **III. Known Abilities**
**Law of Stillness** – Freezes kinetic motion in a wide radius.  
**Judicium Echo** – Reflects hostile Essence back as frostfire.  
**Cryost Mantle** – Nullifies sound and psionic communication.  
**Wings of Inversion** – Bends light into solid geometry for containment.  
**Veil Binding** – Traps a target inside its own reflection for several seconds.  

---

### **IV. Limitations and Hazards**
- **Animatria Feedback:** Tovain’s Essence can fuse with the entity if the link lasts too long. End the rite with the Solutio phrase.  
- **Judgment Overflow:** The Eidolon begins to judge all life nearby, collapsing moral balance. Counter with a Coagula seal.  
- **Dream Backflow:** Somnalis energy spills into realspace, causing lucid hallucinations. Stabilize using a Benediction Bloom.  

---

### **V. Behavior and Temperament**
Nox’Vyra is law-neutral, perceiving morality as tone rather than belief.  
It obeys thought-commands framed as verdicts—phrases such as *“Deem this false”* or *“Weigh his truth.”*  
When dismissed, it condenses into a black feather lined with silver—called the **Feather of Quiet Law**—which hums softly near corruption.

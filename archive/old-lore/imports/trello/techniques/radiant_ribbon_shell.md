# **Radiant Ribbon Shell**

**Harmonic Choir-Type (Voice / Aurevane bias)** — _Essence Typology: Spirit / Air / Memory — Temperance Path: Refraction → Transcendence_

---

## Definition

A voiced, pressure-coupled class that **turns breath into geometry**. The Radiant Ribbon Shell spools from the throat as laminar bands of air and tone, shaping space into **hush**, **carry**, or **cut**. It does not coerce; it **makes room**—a corridor where truth can travel intact, or a thin edge where falsehoods fray.

---

## Shell State

- **Nature:** Harmonic–radiant soundfield; converts phoneme + breath column into structured pressure.
- **Emotion Conductivity:** **Compassion → laminar flow** (clean hush/carry). **Anger → hiss/turbulence** (precision loss).
- **Purity:** High clarity when the whole truth is borne; subtle spectral distortion when curated/withheld.

---

## Geometry / Topology

- **Core Anchor:** Larynx ↔ diaphragm axis.
- **Pattern:** Dual-spiral **ribbons** from throat → clavicles → forearms → fingertips; micro-filaments lace palate/tongue.
- **Footing Nodes:** Ankles/arches imprint **quiet rings**; best purchase on dressed stone or tight-grain wood; weakest on echo-dead glass, loose scree.

---

## Laminae (Field Layers)

1. **Hush Lamina (outer):** Damps ambient clatter **–6 to –12 dB** within **5–12 m**; steadies allied hands/aim.
2. **Carry Lamina (mid):** Phase-locks syllables to wind; preserves intelligibility through shear/eddies.
3. **Cut Lamina (inner):** High-Q filament that thins chosen vowels into **edge-pressure** for clean, nonlethal severing and stance arrests.

---

## Essence Behavior

The shell **entrains** nearby air and attention to the caster’s cadence. Ethical certainty amplifies coherence; conflicted intent lowers Q-factor, shortening range and fraying edges. External noise becomes ordered flow—**silence as shelter**, not absence.

---

## Trait Function

Traits express the shell’s threefold law:

- **Hushfield:** Calm-pressure aura that quiets noise and nerves.
- **Verity Timbre:** Long vowels de-tune lies; repeated exposure yields self-correction or silence.
- **Choir Breath:** Syncs with allies’ respiration, scaling effect **without** volume.
  These traits recurse: clarity begets stability, which improves carry, which invites more clarity.

---

## Casting Method

**Breath-gated phonemics.** No chant required—**Four-Count Litany** _(Set → Place → Speak → Recover)_ governs mode shifts. Implements serve as foci rather than sources: **Ribbon-Staff** (Wy fillet) pins gust-knots; **Cantor’s Mantle** supplies sympathetic overtones; **Seal-Pin** stamps carried phrasing as dusk-long **whorl-text**. **Feather Vows** chime when truth is borne whole and dull when curated.

---

## Domain Theme

**“The Corridor that Hears.”** In full ascent, space around the cantor behaves like a civic hall in a storm: words pass ungarbled, panic thins, and hostile clamor collapses into the walls. At its rim, truth sharpens; at its heart, listening reigns.

---

## Wellspring Resonance

- **Voice (primary):** Attention-law by timbre; bindings remain **consent-forward**.
- **Aurevane (secondary):** Air as scaffold/carrier; grants **Air-Step** micro-dashes and anti-shear protection for syllables.
- **Memory / Ma (tertiary):** Verbatim imprinting; re-sound as **whorl-text** during rites.

---

## Temperance Signature

**VII → VIII (Refraction → Transcendence):** Refraction clarifies laminar flow; Transcendence stabilizes **Choir** and refines **Cut**. The shell’s strength scales with conscience: **clean intent = clean carry**.

---

## Operating Modes (Breath-Gated)

- **Cantor (Idle):** Resting hush; steady footing; clatter softens.
- **Carry:** Tall breath + long vowels → clauses travel **200–300 m** open air (**80–120 m** through alleys).
- **Cut:** Compressed breath + “thin” vowels → sever textiles/rope; score light plate at **≤2–3 m**.
- **Choir:** Lock to **1–3 allies**; multiply Carry **without** added volume.
- **Stillpoint (Advanced):** Brief **1 m** zero-sound eye with razor gust at the rim _(proto **Swan’s Turn**)_.

---

## Benchmarks & Scale Hooks

- **Hushfield:** Typical **–9 dB \@ 8 m**, holds in gusts to **~30 kn**.
- **Carry Corridor:** **200–300 m** LOS open; **80–120 m** tortuous paths.
- **Edge-Band:** Clean textile/leather sever at **3–5 m**; buckle/strap scoring **≤2 m**.
- **Air-Step:** **30–60 m/s** burst reposition (A→S tier), two-beat recovery.

---

## Diagnostics (Field Tells)

- **Healthy:** Hair leans toward inhale; dust spirals tighten; the room “clears.”
- **Strain:** Cloak hem chatters; sibilants hiss; allies feel pin-prick pressure at the ear.
- **Falter:** Edge-bands wobble; vowels lose sheen; Carry drops below alley length.

---

## Failure States & Safeties

- **Voice Blanch:** Over-cut against conscience → temporary aphonia/high-register loss.
- **Ribbon Fray:** **Uur** turbulence + anger → lamina tear; remedy with cadence reset & soft-consonant chain.
- **Echo Saturation:** Post-hearing marathons cause double-hearing; purge with **Ma** hymns; no cutting for one night.

---

## Counters & Mitigations

- **Hard Counters:** Echo-dead vaults; **Uur** anti-cohesion fields; cyclone cross-shear; silent grapplers.
- **Mitigate:** Staff-pin windward corners; ballast with lower vowels; engage **Choir** with a calm ally to widen laminar stability.

---

## Advancement Notes / Ascent Path

1. **Ribbon Discipline:** Curve-carry around corners via two-beat **Wy** feints.
2. **Serration Cant (Overdrive Cut):** Trilled vowels → **saw-edge** band for **3–5 heartbeats** _(throat heat)_.
3. **Choir Ladder:** **4–6** linked breaths for plaza-scale hearings _(risk: echo bleed)_.
4. **Swan’s Turn (Mythic):** Silence at core, storm at rim—**truth crosses only by her leave**; requires a vow that limits cruelty.

---

## Known Echo / Manifestation

When the shell crowns, banners lean into the breath, street noise folds outward, and a pale **ribbon corona** threads from lips to staff-tip. Lies **warble** a half-step flat; whispered orders arrive as if spoken beside the ear.

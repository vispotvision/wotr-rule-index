# **SERAPHIC THREAD BLESSING**

_(Healing / Fate / Light / Ally Buff)_

## **I. Source & Concept**

**Source:**
**Benediction** — grace, sanctity, cleansing.
**Luminalis** — illumination, guidance.

**Path:** Spirit + Fate
**Aether Class:** Luminous Harmonizer

**Core Concept:**
The caster draws a shimmering thread from the Plane of Fate to bind an ally’s wounds and resolve, realigning their Essence flow to its purest state.

---

## **II. Function + Philosophy**

**Function:** Healing + Buff
**Outcomes:**

- Repairs minor Soul Crystal surface damage.
- Accelerates physical and emotional recovery.
- Stabilizes Aether channels for cleaner spellcasting.

**Philosophy:**
“Light loves that which strives.”

---

## **III. Mechanics Spec**

**Name:** Seraphic Thread Blessing
**Type:** Ally-Targeted Heal + Buff
**Temperance Requirement:** Stage V — Splintering
**Activation:** Declared
**Range / Area:** 15 meters
**Duration per turn:** 2 turns
**Tags:** [Healing] [Fate] [Light] [Buff]

---

## **IV. Limits, Weaknesses & Cost**

- Cannot heal fatal wounds or severe soul fractures.
- Shadow-aligned allies resist Fate-thread binding.

**Cost:**
Mild emotional drain; caster feels a trace of the healed wounds.

---

## **V. Counters & Interactions**

- Shadow curses break the thread instantly.
- Absolution boosts its healing potency.
- Can be severed by Oblivara's erasure glyphs.

---

## **VI. Imagery, Sensation & Language**

**Visual:** A golden-white filament weaving from caster’s palm into the ally’s essence.
**Sound:** A soft chime, like a single harp string.
**Sensation:** Warm clarity behind the eyes.

# **Sigillum Fixatio**

---

## **I. Source & Concept**

**Source (Wellspring):** **Fixatio — The Wellspring of Anchoring.**
Governs the metaphysical law of stasis. It does not create or destroy; it binds Essence to a single point in the lattice, making it obdurate and resistant to change.

**Path:** Mind Path
**Aether Class:** Conduit / Lattice
_(Imprinting a rigid command upon the Aether.)_

**Core Concept (Fantasy):**
A focused act of will that “nails” a target—person, object, or magical effect—to its current position, rendering it metaphysically immovable and unchangeable.

---

## **II. Function + Philosophy**

**Function:**
The caster designates a target within range. That target becomes bound by the _Sigillum_ (a metaphysical seal).

**Physical Target (Person/Object):**

- Immobilized and anchored
- Cannot be moved, pushed, pulled, or teleported
- Physical state becomes “fixed”:
  - Wounds cannot heal
  - New wounds are difficult to inflict
  - Gains extreme resistance to physical alteration (not invincibility)

**Metaphysical Target (Spell/Ward):**

- Lock the parameters of an active magical effect
- Cannot be dispelled, moved, or altered for the duration

**Philosophy:**
The ability is an onto-symbolic assertion of control: _“Your motion is an error; only my order is real.”_
It arises from an Arche rooted in a **lust for order** or **terror of insignificance**.

---

## **III. Mechanics Spec**

**Name:** _Sigillum Fixatio — The Seal of Fixation_
**Type:** [Control] / [Debuff] / [Ward]

**Temperance Requirement:** Stage IV — Acquiescence

**Activation:**
A single incantatory command — **“Esto.”** (“Be still.”)
Gesture: an open hand closing into a knuckled fist, grasping the target’s very _concept_.

**Range:** 10 meters, line of sight
**Target:** Single
**Duration:** 3 CRP turns or until caster’s will breaks

**Scaling:**
Sigillum durability scales with:

- Mind Path proficiency
- Temperance Stage

**Tags:** [Mind] [Fixatio] [Binding] [Stasis]

---

## **IV. Limits, Weaknesses, & Cost**

### **Limits**

- Only one Sigillum may be active at a time
- Cannot target beings **2+ Temperance Stages higher**
- Cannot affect entire Domains, Wellsprings, or abstractions
  - Must target a discrete, tangible material or aetheric form

### **Weaknesses**

- Brittle: an anchor, not a shield
- Vulnerable to torsional or shearing metaphysical force
- Directly countered by:
  - Wellsprings of Chaos, Motion, Rupture
  - Titan Valmir’s domain
- Caster must maintain concentration; stagger/daze/fracture breaks the Sigillum

### **Cost — The Hermetic Reciprocity**

**Soul Cost:**
To impose stasis on another, the caster must accept stasis within themselves.

**Physical Cost:**
Caster becomes anchored:

- No Blink, Phase, Warpstride
- Movements become heavy, gravitic, slow

**Psychological Cost:**
Mind becomes rigid, obsessive, fixated.
Adaptation becomes difficult; thought patterns ossify around the chosen target.

## **VI. Imagery, Sensation, & Language**

**Visual Signature:**
A brief flicker of a _ferrous Parun glyph_ over the target.
For the duration:

- Dust settles too quickly
- Light bends subtly
- Their shadow becomes unnaturally still

**Sound Cue:**
A sharp hiss like quenching-steam, followed by the thunderous lock of a titanic iron gate.

**Sensation:**
Air condenses.
Gravity feels heavier.
Inertia clings to the target like a metaphysical weight.

**Mantra (High Temperance):**
_“Quae moto, nunc quiescit. Quae fluo, nunc sisto. Lex mea inhaeret.”_
_(That which moves, now rests. That which flows, now halts. My law holds.)_

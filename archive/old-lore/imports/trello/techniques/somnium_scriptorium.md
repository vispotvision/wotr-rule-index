## . Somnium Scriptorium

_(The Dream Scriptorium)_

### I. Source & Concept

**Source (Wellspring):**

- Dream — narrative trapping
- Harmonic — binding through story-structure

**Path:** Spirit → Mind
**Aether Class:** Symphonic Scholar

**Core Concept (Fantasy):**
Serenyra “writes” an enemy into a temporary dream-chamber—an oneiric archive-page—where their body remains in place but their consciousness is pulled into a crafted scenario.

---

### II. Function + Philosophy

**Function:**
A [Crowd Control] / [Interrogation] / [Non-lethal] art.
She inscribes a glyph in the air; if it anchors to a target’s psyche, their awareness is drawn inward into a narrative she controls.

**Outcomes:**

- Renders a single target catatonic but alive
- Allows her to question or confront them within the dream
- Can be used for mercy—pulling allies from traumatic moments

**Philosophy:**
“Your fear is a story you have not yet finished reading. Let us turn the pages together.”

---

### III. Mechanics Spec

**Name:** Somnium Scriptorium
**Type:** [Control] / [Utility]

**Temperance Requirement:** Stage VII — Diffraction

**Activation:**
Declared; requires focus and often a spoken invocation of the target’s name or true title.

**Range / Area:**

- Target within 200 m
- Line-of-sight

**Duration per turn:**
Up to 3 turns in combat time; subjective time within may stretch far longer.

**Tags:**
[Dream] [Mind] [Control] [Non-Lethal]

---

### IV. Limits, Weaknesses, & Cost

**Limits**

- Targets with extremely strong will or fractured minds may resist or distort the dream
- She cannot fully rewrite their core personality in the short term

**Weaknesses**

- External disruptions to her concentration can eject both of them violently
- Mind Guardians, sigils, or entities protecting the target’s psyche can intervene

**Cost — The Soul-Cost Currency Law**

- **Empathic Imprint:** Emotional residue from the dream clings to her; she may carry fragments of the target’s fear or grief.
- **Dual-Process Strain:** Acting in both the real and dream spaces exhausts her faster.

---

### V. Counters & Interactions

- **Dreamwards / Noösphere Anchors:** Prevent forced narrative relocation.
- **Fate Intercession:** Entities of Fate can sever the dream-chord if the target’s role is too important to sideline.

---

### VI. Imagery, Sensation, & Language

**Visual Signature:** A page of silver glyphs unfurls in front of the target; their eyes glaze over, reflecting text that only Serenyra can read.

**Sound Cue:** The soft crackle of pages turning, overlapping with the ambient sounds of the battlefield fading out.

**Sensation:** The target feels as though they stepped through a door that appeared in front of them, and the world outside slammed shut.

## Speculum Harmoniae

_(The Harmony Mirror)_

### I. Source & Concept

**Source (Wellspring):**

- Harmonic — perfect resonance
- Dream — eidolic projection

**Path:** Mind
**Aether Class:** Symphonic Scholar

**Core Concept (Fantasy):**
A cousin to Speculum Recordatio, this ability mirrors not the spell itself, but the **state** of the caster’s Wellspring—projecting an illusory double that acts as a temporary decoy with limited agency.

---

### II. Function + Philosophy

**Function:**
A [Decoy] / [Distraction] art.
Serenyra captures a “snapshot” of a target’s current Essence state and casts it outward as a shimmering eidolon that moves and radiates like them, drawing fire and attention.

**Outcomes:**

- Diverts targeting from allies
- Confuses enemy tracking or predictive arts
- Provides a false Essence trail for scrying

**Philosophy:**
“You chased the echo and lost the voice.”

---

### III. Mechanics Spec

**Name:** Speculum Harmoniae
**Type:** [Illusion] / [Defense] / [Control]

**Temperance Requirement:** Stage V — Resonance

**Activation:**
Reactive or declared; a brief gesture of tracing a circle in the air around the target.

**Range / Area:**

- Target within 100 m
- Eidolon can move within a 50 m radius sphere around its creation point

**Duration per turn:**
3 turns or until struck by a significant attack.

**Tags:**
[Illusion] [Dream] [Harmonic] [Decoy]

---

### IV. Limits, Weaknesses, & Cost

**Limits**

- Eidolon cannot deal real damage; its attacks are phantasmal
- Highly perceptive or Fate-tuned entities can see through the ruse more easily

**Weaknesses**

- Direct Void sight bypasses the illusion
- Touch-based intuition powers notice the absence of physicality

**Cost — The Soul-Cost Currency Law**

- **Recall Tax:** Each mirror siphons a fragment of her own sensory record of the target, making future precise counters harder until she rebuilds observation.
- **Aether Blur:** Overuse leaves faint mirrored afterimages in her own vision.

---

### V. Counters & Interactions

- **True Sight / Akashic Gaze:** Penetrates the illusion.
- **Emotional Tracking:** Hunters who track fear, hatred, or other emotions can distinguish the real from the echo.

---

### VI. Imagery, Sensation, & Language

**Visual Signature:** The target’s outline peels away in silvery threads, coalescing into a secondary figure that steps aside and moves independently.

**Sound Cue:** A harmonic “doubling,” like a voice suddenly singing in duet with itself.

**Sensation:** For a heartbeat, the target feels hollow, as if part of them stepped sideways and then returned.

## 7. Nexus Harmoniae

_(The Nexus of Harmony)_

### I. Source & Concept

**Source (Wellspring):**

- Harmonic — unification of tones
- Celestial — macro-scale ordering

**Path:** Fate
**Aether Class:** Symphonic Scholar

**Core Concept (Fantasy):**
Serenyra establishes a temporary Wellspring crossroads—an invisible nexus where all active magics in an area must briefly “declare” their harmonic before manifesting, giving her leverage to soften or sharpen them.

---

### II. Function + Philosophy

**Function:**
A [Field] / [Meta-Control] art.
She weaves a vast, faint mandala over a battlefield. Any spell or ability invoked within its radius resonates through the Nexus first, where she can subtly bias its outcome.

**Outcomes:**

- Amplify allied spells modestly
- Slightly dampen enemy Aether output
- Increase predictability of all magical effects in the field

**Philosophy:**
“In a choir, even discord must pass through the same hall.”

---

### III. Mechanics Spec

**Name:** Nexus Harmoniae
**Type:** [Field] / [Buff] / [Debuff]

**Temperance Requirement:** Stage VIII — Refraction

**Activation:**
Ritual-quick; requires a brief chant and a sweeping gesture with the Aether Chord Staff.

**Range / Area:**

- Up to 1 km radius
- Centered on Serenyra’s position

**Duration per turn:**
6 turns.

**Tags:**
[Harmonic] [Field] [Meta] [Support] [Control]

---

### IV. Limits, Weaknesses, & Cost

**Limits**

- Effects are subtle, not overwhelming; this is a strategic, not spectacular, art
- Serenyra must remain conscious and within the field

**Weaknesses**

- Anti-field Edicts or Domain overwrites can cancel the Nexus
- Void storms scramble the signal, turning the Nexus into dangerous interference

**Cost — The Soul-Cost Currency Law**

- **Cognitive Overload:** She perceives too many harmonics at once, risking migraines, disorientation, or brief synesthetic episodes.
- **Fate Tangle:** Aligning so many threads around herself can draw unwanted attention from Fate-linked entities.

---

### V. Counters & Interactions

- **Personal Domains:** Strong personal Domains may partially insulate their owners.
- **Silencing Wellsprings:** Certain Wellspring techniques can mute their harmonic “signature,” ducking beneath her detection.

---

### VI. Imagery, Sensation, & Language

**Visual Signature:** A faint, circular sigil inscribed across sky and ground; lines of light appear only when spells are cast, racing toward a central unseen point.

**Sound Cue:** A low, omnipresent hum, like a distant choir tuning.

**Sensation:** Casters feel a brief “brush” over their souls before their power moves.

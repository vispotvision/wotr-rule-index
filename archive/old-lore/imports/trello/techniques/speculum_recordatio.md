# **Speculum Recordatio**

### _(The Mirrored Recall)_

---

## **I. Source & Concept**

**Source (Wellspring):**

- **Dream** — subjective memory, mnemonic distortion
- **Harmonic** — perfect frequency replication

**Path:** Mind Path
**Aether Class:** Symphonic Scholar — Harmonic Orchestration

**Core Concept (Fantasy):**
A reactive counter-spell using Serenyra’s **Crystalline Recall** trait. She “records” the Essence signature and Harmonic geometry of an incoming spell, then instantaneously replays a fractured, dissonant echo of it back at the caster. The mirrored echo collides with the original, causing implosion, detonation, or backlash.

---

## **II. Function + Philosophy**

**Function:**
A [Counterspell] / [Debuff].
As a liminal reaction, Serenyra targets a spell mid-cast. Using her **Thesauriel (Mirror Codex)**, she records its onto-symbolic structure. She does _not_ dispel it. Instead, she manifests a phantasmal eidolon of the spell and **collides** it with the original — a forced **Harmonic Clash**.

Outcomes:

- Nullification
- Premature detonation
- Feedback into the caster

**Philosophy:**
_“Your theorem is flawed. Allow me to show you the error in your calculation.”_
Her counterspell treats magic as mathematics — contradictions unravel equations.

---

## **III. Mechanics Spec**

**Name:** _Speculum Recordatio_
**Type:** [Counterspell] / [Control]

**Temperance Requirement:** Stage VI — Illumination

Reactive. She raises the Thesauriel; its pages turn in incandescent bursts as it imprints the spell’s **Essence Color Logic**.

**Range / Area:**
Self / Target caster within 1 km

**Duration per turn:**
Instantaneous

**Tags:**
[Mind] [Dream] [Harmonic] [Counter] [Refraction]

---

## **IV. Limits, Weaknesses, & Cost**

### **Limits**

- Can only record **Essence-based** or **Aetheric** abilities
- Cannot record mundane projectiles
- Cannot record A-Temporal Void abilities (no “memory” to replicate)

### **Weaknesses**

- Cannot stop instantaneous effects (e.g., Blink)
- Cataclysmic-tier spells exceed safe recall; attempting to record them overloads the Thesauriel and shatters the counterspell

### **Cost — The Soul-Cost Currency Law**

**Memory Cost:**
Uses her **Crystalline Recall**. Each use consumes one “memory slot,” leaving a temporary blank space in her recent Aetheric recall.

**Aetheric Cost:**
A paroxysmal Aether surge — Essence Feedback that leaves her mentally addled for a moment.

---

## **V. Counters & Interactions**

**Void / Entropy:**
Null and Entropic spells (Zar’thuun) lack an Essence signature; they cannot be recalled.

**Overwhelming Force:**
Titan-level Fluxstrikes or similar magnitude simply obliterate the defensive mirror-ward.

---

## **VI. Imagery, Sensation, & Language**

**Visual Signature:**
The Thesauriel radiates opalescent light. A fractal, iridescent eidolon of the incoming spell forms — a chimeric reflection — and spears back into the original.

**Sound Cue:**
A crystalline, cathedral-scale shattering: the sound of a glass mandala being violently broken.

**Sensation:**
A rippling psychic fissure, a Hermetic pressure as two incompatible magical theorems collide.

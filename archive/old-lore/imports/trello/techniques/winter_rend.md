# **WINTER REND**

(The Maw That Tears Warmth)

I. **Source and Concept**
Primary Wellspring: Vohrin
Secondary: Tenebra
Path: Cold-Assault
Aether Class: Primal-Kinetic
Core Concept: A sweeping strike that drags a wedge of Vohrin’s elemental cold behind it. The cleave tears warmth from whatever it touches.

II. **Function and Philosophy**
Function: Offense • Cold Damage • Area Control
Outcomes:

- Sap heat from flesh and Essence.
- Causes frost cracking in armor.
- Leaves Tenebra afterimage trails.


Philosophy: “Cold kills slower, but truer.”

III. **Mechanics Spec**
Name: Winter Rend
Type: Offense / Cold Cleave
Temperance Requirement: Stage VI Glory
Activation: Declared
Range or Area: Two meter sweeping arc
Duration per turn: Instant
Tags: Frost, Armor-Crack, Shadow Trail

IV. **Limits, Weaknesses, Cost**
Limits: Ineffective against insulated or flame-forged armor.
Weaknesses: Heat vectors.
Cost: Mild numbness in arms.

V. **Counters and Interactions**
Counters: Heat bursts, rapid mobility.

VI. **Imagery**
Visual: Frost-blade arc lingers as white smoke.
Sound: Ice chime.
Sensation: Sudden cold snap in fingertips.

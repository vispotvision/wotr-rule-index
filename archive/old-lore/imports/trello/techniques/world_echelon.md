# **WORLD ECHELON**

(The Crimson Tribunal Descends)

Primary Wellspring: Judicium
Secondary Wellspring: 57 infected Wellsprings
Path: Spirit–Fate
Aether Class: Principle Dominion Soul
Core Concept: Francis merges his personal Tribunal Domain with the local Spirit World, forcing all beings inside into his court-space where emotion is weighed and Aether becomes verdict.

II. **Function and Philosophy**
Function: Domain • Mass Control • Spirit Dominion
Outcomes:

- Space folds into court geometry.
- Aether converts into law vectors.
- Every emotion becomes weighted evidence.


Philosophy: “All souls stand trial.”

Name: World Echelon
Type: Domain Extension
Temperance Requirement: Stage XIV Zenith
Activation: Declared

Tags: Domain, Emotion-Weighing, Spirit Control

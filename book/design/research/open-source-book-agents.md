# Open-source "write a whole book" agents — survey (fetched 2026-09-12)

Scope: GitHub projects 2023–2026 that take a seed/premise to a full manuscript with little or no human in the loop. For each: pipeline stages, how a chapter gets its context, the critique/revision mechanism, output assembly, and the quality problems the authors or their issue trackers admit to. Everything below comes from pages fetched today; quotes are verbatim from the repo README/PIPELINE docs unless marked otherwise.

## 2. Per-project detail

### 2.1 NousResearch/autonovel — the most concrete design available
Source: [PIPELINE.md](https://github.com/NousResearch/autonovel/blob/master/PIPELINE.md), [README](https://github.com/NousResearch/autonovel), [ANTI-SLOP.md](https://github.com/NousResearch/autonovel/blob/master/ANTI-SLOP.md), [nousresearch.com/bells](https://nousresearch.com/bells). Produced *The Second Son of the House of Bells*: 79,456 words, 19 chapters (cut from 24), 6 automated revision cycles + 6 Opus rounds; "derived from 60+ commits, 5 revision cycles, 2 reader panels, 2 adversarial edits, and ~20 hours of agent time".

**Five co-evolving files**: `voice.md`, `world.md`, `characters.md` (wound/want/need/lie, speech, sliders), `outline.md` (part 2 = foreshadowing ledger), `canon.md` (target 400+ hard-fact entries before leaving foundation), plus `MYSTERY.md` and `state.json`. Every change is a git commit; the loop is Karpathy-autoresearch style: "If score improved → git commit; if worse → git reset --hard HEAD~1".

**Phase 1 Foundation** (5–15 iterations, 2–4 h): `gen_world.py`, `gen_characters.py`, `gen_outline.py`, `gen_outline_part2.py`, voice discovery ("Write 5 trial passages in different registers; select best; fill voice.md Part 2 with exemplars + anti-exemplars"), `gen_canon.py`, `evaluate.py --phase=foundation`. Exit: `foundation_score > 7.5 AND lore_score > 7.0`. "The evaluator weights lore interconnection at 40%".

**Phase 2 First Draft** (8–16 h): per chapter, `draft_chapter.py` gets voice.md/world.md/characters.md in full, the chapter's outline entry, "Previous chapter's last ~1000 words", and the next chapter's outline. `evaluate.py --chapter=NN`; keep if >6.0 else discard and retry, max 5. New canon entries are extracted from the eval output and appended. Stated rule: "Forward progress over perfection. 6.0 is good enough." Observation: "Chapters 1-6 score higher; after ch 6 add anti-pattern rules to writer prompt" (freshness decay). Post-draft mechanical slop pass "catches ~200 instances of tier-1 banned words, em-dash overuse, sentence-length uniformity".

**Phase 3 Revision** (3–6 cycles, 4–8 h):
- `adversarial_edit.py all` → `edit_logs/chNN_cuts.json`, cuts typed; "OVER-EXPLAIN = #1 AI pattern (~32% of adversarial cuts)", "REDUNDANT = #2 (~26%)". Apply cuts by quote-matching; "Target: chapters with >17% fat"; "Expect to cut ~2000-3000 words (~3-4% of novel)".
- `compare_chapters.py` → Elo tournament of chapters.
- `reader_panel.py` → 4 personas (editor, genre reader, writer, first reader), each answering 10 fixed questions: momentum_loss, earned_ending, cut_candidate, missing_scene, thinnest_character, best_scene, worst_scene, would_recommend, haunts_you, next_book. "3/4 or 4/4 agreement = revision priorities". What the panel catches that the scorer misses: "checklist of yeses" (allies agree without friction), missing emotional scenes, "mechanism vs. person" characters, "works" vs "lives".
- Briefs → `gen_revision.py`. Brief types: compression (cut 40–60%, keep 2–3 beats, land at 2200–3000 w, "don't go below 1700w"), expansion (+800–1500 w, "physical accumulation, dread, silence-with-duration"), dramatization ("Change HOW information arrives, not WHAT", "Convert 'reading document' → investigation/confrontation"), thin-character ("Add private/unguarded moment POV character catches… Deepen existing scene; don't add new one").
- Plateau rule: `if abs(score - prev_score) < 0.5 and cycle >= 3: break`. "After cycle 4, fixing one score often drops another." "The evaluator rotates 'weakest chapter' — chasing it is whack-a-mole. After 2 rotations, stop."
- Known biases of the LLM judge: theme coherence hits 10 early; voice consistency 9 if POV never breaks; "Pacing (7) is structurally stubborn… may be a structural ceiling for LLM-evaluated novels".

**Phase 3b Opus review loop** (`review.py`, max 4 rounds): whole manuscript to Opus with "Read the below novel. Review it first as a literary critic and then as a professor of fiction. Give specific, actionable suggestions." `review.py --parse` classifies items major/moderate/minor and qualified/unqualified. Stop when "No major unqualified items remain, OR >50% of items are qualified/hedged, OR ≤2 items found". Lessons: "Reviewer will ALWAYS find something. Stopping = about severity/qualification, not zero defects"; "When language shifts from 'novel has problems' to 'costs of ambition' → stop revising"; "Items persisting 3+ rounds may be structural to voice/approach, not bugs."

**Phase 4 Export**: normalise headings, `typeset/build_tex.py` → tectonic PDF (EB Garamond), ePub, ElevenLabs multi-voice audiobook (4,179 speaker-attributed segments), landing page.

**Failure modes they name**: over-compression ("Cutting chapter below 1800w makes it the new weakest"), expansion bloat ("gen_revision.py adds ~30% more words than briefed"), score chasing ("Arc went 9→8→9 when we over-compressed Ch 11"), repeated AI phrases ("opening descriptions, emotional formulas, 'the way [X] did [Y]', triadic lists"), investigation chapters that "repeat a rhythm (go-learn-blocked)". Still unbuilt: `run_pipeline.py`, `gen_brief.py`, `apply_cuts.py` — briefs were hand-written in production.

**ANTI-SLOP.md** is a three-tier banned list (Tier 1 "Kill on sight": delve, utilize, leverage, tapestry, testament, realm, myriad…; Tier 2 "Suspicious in clusters"; Tier 3 filler phrases), plus structural tells: "One or two em dashes per page is fine. Five per paragraph is a tell"; "Not just X, but Y" is "the single most overused rhetorical pattern"; sentence-length uniformity ("If every sentence is 15-25 words, mix in some short ones. And some long ones.").

### 2.2 mrigankad/Novel-OS
Five agents in strict order, each emitting a machine-parseable block: Architect → `outline.json`; Scribe → `chapter_NNN_draft.md` + `[SCRIBE_STATE_UPDATE]`; Editor (modes line/developmental/pacing/dialogue/tension) → `chapter_NNN_revised.md` + `[EDITOR_STATE_UPDATE]` with before/after scores; Guardian → `chapter_NNN_continuity_report.md` with `Status: PASS/WARNING/FAIL`; Curator → `[STYLE_STATE_UPDATE]` (consistency/genre/voice scores). "Every agent prompt now includes a strict OUTPUT CONTRACT". `story_state.json` holds character locations/emotional states, last-appearance index, foreshadowing planted/resolved, editor scores.

Deterministic continuity engine runs **before** the LLM Guardian, and its findings are injected into the Guardian prompt: `dormant_thread` (idle >3 chapters), `overdue_thread` (critical: past `target_resolution_chapter`), `unresolved_foreshadowing`, `absent_character` (>5 chapters), `never_appeared`, `dead_character_state`, `missing_chapter_file` (critical), `status_drift`, `thin_character` (no `internal_desire`), plus timeline anachronism and "stalled middle (protagonist becomes reactive)". Every finding can be dismissed as "This is intentional" with persisted reasoning. Export only compiles `Status: PASS` chapters. No numeric quality thresholds documented; no lessons documented.

### 2.3 Ckokoski/AuthorAgent
Tiered memory: CORE = "a budgeted, always-in-prompt digest: active chapter state, the characters this scene touches, open plot threads, your style fingerprint"; ARCHIVAL = full-text search on demand; RECALL = raw drafts; nightly "sleep-time consolidation" of arcs/threads/voice fingerprint. Quality engine: contradiction detection "Diffs each chapter against the book's entity database … returns evidence-chained findings — the quote in the chapter vs. the established fact"; "Each major character critiques its own dialogue: off-voice lines, knowledge it couldn't have yet, actions against motivation — with in-voice rewrites"; specialist revision passes (continuity / voice / craft / anti-AI-slop) instead of one generic "edit"; "GEPA-style loop: judge → diagnose → revise → re-judge; keeps a candidate only if it measurably improves, never regresses". Cost tiering: free models for outline/research, mid for drafting, premium for polish and judging. No thresholds, no MCP.

### 2.4 ProhibitedTV/Novel-Generator
Per-chapter cycle: "Plan each chapter, draft prose, critique the chapter, revise when profile thresholds require it, summarize for continuity, and update the continuity ledger." Then manuscript QA → developmental rewrite plan + revised-outline report → targeted revision waves → (publication only) "character humanization and prose compression waves" → line edit "for clarity, sentence rhythm, transitions, and concrete on-page consequences" → readiness scoring on "concept/world, atmosphere, thematic ambition, plot coherence, character depth, prose control, repetition, and publication readiness". Profiles: draft / balanced / strict ("Tightens revision triggers") / publication ("Drafts long, then cuts. Adds character-private-life requirements, scene-variety rules, motif budgets"). If gates fail "the run completes as a reviewable manuscript labeled as needing editorial revision instead of claiming to be publication-ready". Explicitly targets "repeated atmosphere, looped crisis structure, allegorical characters, over-explained prose, thin ordinary human friction, and misleading publication exports". Checkpoint after every stage; resume/regenerate from any chapter. Benchmarks 2026-05-31 on local models: gemma4:e4b full 2-chapter run 20.2 min with 1 JSON repair; qwen3:14b 70.1 min; gpt-oss:20b 91.2 min. Numeric thresholds are not published.

### 2.5 KazKozDev/NovelGenerator
"premise → outline → blueprint → chapter plan → scenes → review → canon → book audit → export", every stage committed to IndexedDB. Canon rule worth stealing: "Nothing enters the canon that is not backed by a verbatim quotation from accepted prose, so later chapters are written against what the book actually established". Deterministic gates before any LLM review: "unfilled slots, chapters outside their planned length, characters from a script the book is not written in, a planned exchange written without a spoken line, and paragraphs that restate the one before them all fail outright"; chapter must reach ≥80% of target (default 4,000 w, range 300–10,000). Texture analysis measures "comparison density, paragraph shape, dialogue share, and repetition found through embeddings — the last catching a beat retold in new words, which no lexical check can see". Repair "up to five times" against "the exact passages the reviewer cited", and each repair is diffed against its source: "one that silences the chapter's dialogue, fuses its paragraphs or quietly cuts a sixth of it is sent back with the damage named". Admitted limits: "Semantic review is sampled, so two passes over the same prose can disagree"; "A model that ignores JSON Schema stalls the run"; "A six-chapter book takes hours and hundreds of model calls"; gates "don't guarantee coherence or literary merit". BSL 1.1 licence, not OSI.

### 2.6 jncchds/abook (MCP)
Planner phase: Story Bible → Characters → Plot Threads → Chapter Outlines. Per chapter: Checker (pre-write contradictions in the outline) → Writer → Editor. "every prior chapter's title and outline is injected into Writer and Editor messages"; Writer also runs 3 RAG queries (characters, locations, plot threads) against pgvector. Checker returns "structured JSON patches" for continuity/grammar/repetition/style; the Editor applies them "mechanically (no LLM call) using indexed text matching with whitespace normalization and position hints", and only does a creative rewrite when the Checker asks or the human gives instructions at the assisted-mode pause. Built-in MCP server at `/mcp` with 37 tools for Claude Desktop / VS Code. Documented pain: local models emit non-standard JSON (chapter numbers as `1.0` or `"3"`); parsers coerce, drop, and post a warning listing dropped items. Default model llama3.

### 2.7 danjdewhurst/story-skills (Claude Code / Codex plugin)
Markdown project with YAML frontmatter: `story.md`, `characters/`, `worldbuilding/{locations,systems,factions,artifacts}`, `plot/`, `scenes/` registry, `continuity/` (state, questions, promises), `glossary/`, `chapters/`. Seven skills incl. `chapter-writing` ("outline-first workflow") and `revision-continuity`. The CLI `story doctor` is deterministic: errors for dead characters in later scenes, payoffs before setups, resolving never-introduced questions, references to non-existent chapters; warnings for POV character missing from cast, aging unresolved promises, state contradictions ("artifact marked destroyed but listed active"). Their thesis: "Long-range consistency is the thing language models are worst at and prompts cannot fix." Rule: "Agents should write story content directly to markdown files, not create project-local build or generator scripts". Exports MD/EPUB/DOCX.

### 2.8 CSandbatch/novelgraph
Four knowledge scopes with different authority: Literary (guidance only), Series (approved canon), Book (Story Charter, characters, events, knowledge, clues, obligations), Run ("Agent hypotheses, contradictions, questions… Noncanonical scratch space"). Production DAG: "research → outline and scene plan → draft → deterministic validation → story-graph proposal → continuity or mystery audit → reader panel → revision → re-audit → approval → closure", each node with "typed inputs, outputs, artifact visibility, capabilities, budget, retry policy, and dependencies". Discovery uses three agents (Sol asks "one consequential question at a time", Terra, Luna) and yields "Core, Stretch, and Wild story-thrust candidates". "Publication is blocked while a hard obligation remains unresolved." Mystery pack: sealed solution, evidence ledger, access matrix, character-knowledge boundaries, false solutions, adversarial solver.

### 2.9 guerra2fernando/libriscribe
Agents: Project Manager, Style Research (`inspired_by`), Concept, Outliner, Character, Worldbuilder, Chapter Writer, Editor, Formatter, plus Invariant Checker, Narrative Graph Builder (`narrative_graph.json`), ContentQualityAgent, PacingAgent. Prompt injection blocks: `STYLE REFERENCE` (POV, sentence length, pacing, dialogue, tone), `STYLE CONSTRAINTS:` (prior-chapter quality flags — "preventive" mode), `NARRATIVE CONSTRAINTS:` (invariants), `PACING GUIDANCE:`. ContentQualityAgent scores "cliche density, show-don't-tell, dialogue voice, sentence variety, scene function" and fires a rewrite when overall <0.65; PacingAgent injects guidance when an axis <0.70 across "tension escalation, act structure, chapter length consistency, emotional beat variety, narrative momentum". ### 2.10 john-paul-ruf/novel-engine

## 3. Stage designs worth borrowing

1. **Foundation loop with a score gate and git as memory** (autonovel). Iterate world/characters/outline/voice until `foundation_score > 7.5`, commit improvements, hard-reset regressions. Add a voice-discovery step: five trial passages in different registers, keep one, store exemplars *and anti-exemplars* in `voice.md`.
2. **Chapter context = static bibles in full + previous chapter tail + next chapter's outline** (autonovel). Not a summary of the previous chapter — its last ~1000 words, so voice carries. Pair with abook's "every prior chapter's title and outline" for long-range awareness, and Novel-OS/YILING-style `character_state` for who-is-where.
3. **Canon only from verbatim prose** (KazKozDev). After a chapter is accepted, extract facts *with the quote that proves them*; later chapters are written against canon, not against the outline's intentions. AuthorAgent's "quote in the chapter vs. the established fact" contradiction output is the same idea on the checking side.
4. **Deterministic gates before any LLM critic** (KazKozDev, Novel-OS, story-skills). Length ≥80% of target, planned dialogue exchange has no spoken line, paragraph restates previous, dead character active, payoff before setup, thread idle >3 chapters, main character absent >5 chapters. Free, instant, non-sycophantic; inject findings into the LLM critic's prompt.
5. **Typed adversarial cutter + quote-matched cut application** (autonovel). Cuts labelled OVER-EXPLAIN / REDUNDANT with the exact quote; apply mechanically; expect ~3–4% of the novel to go; never compress a chapter below ~1,800 words.
6. **Reader panel with fixed questions and consensus threshold** (autonovel; novelgraph also has a "reader panel" node). Four personas × ten questions; act only on 3/4 or 4/4 agreement; route each consensus type to a specific brief type (compression / expansion / dramatization / thin-character).
7. **Briefs as the unit of revision** (autonovel, novel-engine's Forge). Critic output → a written brief that says what to KEEP and what to ADD/CUT with word targets → a rewriter that only sees the brief and the chapter. Budget for +30% bloat over the brief's target.
8. **Judge → diagnose → revise → re-judge, keep only if better** (AuthorAgent GEPA loop, autonovel keep/discard). Never accept a revision on faith; re-score and revert regressions.
9. **Per-character voice critics** (AuthorAgent). Each major character reviews its own lines for off-voice, impossible knowledge, motivation breaks — and offers in-voice rewrites. Combine with book-series-mcp's knows/suspects/unaware ledger.
10. **Hedging-based stop rule for the big critic** (autonovel). Parse review items into major/moderate/minor and qualified/unqualified; stop when >50% are hedged or ≤2 remain; treat items that persist 3+ rounds as voice, not bugs.
11. **Honest readiness labelling** (Novel-Generator). If gates fail, ship "needs editorial revision", not "publication-ready".
12. **Assembly**: ebooklib for EPUB (gpt-author, XHTML per chapter + CSS + TOC), local markdown→DOCX/EPUB compile of only PASS chapters (Novel-OS), LaTeX via tectonic for print (autonovel). Normalise chapter heading levels before building.

## 4. Prompt shapes worth borrowing (verbatim fragments)
- Opus manuscript review: "Read the below novel. Review it first as a literary critic and then as a professor of fiction. Give specific, actionable suggestions." (autonovel)
- Plot-stage anti-summary instruction: "Are you skipping days at a time? Summarizing events? Don't do that, add scenes to detail them." (AIStoryWriter Stage 1)
- Expansion stages: "Don't take away content, instead expand upon it to make a longer and more detailed output." (AIStoryWriter Stages 2–3)
- Scrub: "remove any leftover outlines or editorial comments only leaving behind the finished story. Do not comment on your task." (AIStoryWriter)
- Summary-for-context shape: "a list of important summary points from the last chapter … Plot, Setting, Characters, Things to keep in Mind" (AIStoryWriter) — use *in addition to* raw tail text, not instead of it.
- Injected constraint blocks: `STYLE REFERENCE`, `STYLE CONSTRAINTS:` (last chapter's quality flags), `NARRATIVE CONSTRAINTS:`, `PACING GUIDANCE:` (libriscribe).
- Output contracts: `[SCRIBE_STATE_UPDATE]` / `[EDITOR_STATE_UPDATE]` / `Status: PASS|WARNING|FAIL` blocks that a parser strips into state (Novel-OS).
- Dramatization brief: "Change HOW information arrives, not WHAT"; thin-character brief: "Add private/unguarded moment POV character catches … Deepen existing scene; don't add new one" (autonovel).
- Cold read: "no notes, no context — … what a real reader would feel" (novel-engine Ghostlight).

## 5. Anti-patterns observed
- **One-shot chapters with no plan or no memory**: CrewAI flow writes chapters in parallel from the outline alone ("Previous chapters are *not* passed as context"); gpt-author's only revision is "rewrite it to be significantly better".
- **Accumulating all previous chapters verbatim** (gpt-author, AIStoryWriter with `OLLAMA_CTX=8192`) — blows context and shows up as "chapters not reaching word counts" / "Problems with long Prompts".
- **Summarising away voice**: pipelines that hand the writer only bullet summaries of prior chapters; autonovel deliberately passes raw tail text; slop.sus.cat notes direct "write like X" style instructions have "fidelity OR=0.16" and recommends extracted style features (what ExplosiveCoderflome's "writing method engine" and novel-engine's Voice Profile do).
- **Critics that only praise / scores that saturate**: autonovel reports theme = 10 and voice = 9 early with pacing stuck at 7; slop.sus.cat: "Dual evaluation required: combine mechanical scanning … with LLM-as-judge scoring"; the fix everywhere is deterministic checks + typed cut lists + panel consensus rather than a single 1–10 score.
- **Expansion stages that bloat** ("Don't take away content" ×3 in AIStoryWriter; autonovel's +30% bloat) and **revision loops that flatten**: "Cap AI revision at 3 passes. Each pass flattens prose (the 'blandification' effect)" (slop.sus.cat); AIStoryWriter defaults `CHAPTER_MAX_REVISIONS=3` and ships with revisions *off*.
- **Score chasing / whack-a-mole**: fixing the weakest chapter rotates which chapter is weakest; fixing pacing drops arc.
- **Freshness decay after ~6 chapters** and **rhythm repetition** ("go-learn-blocked" investigation chapters, "looped crisis structure", "repeated atmosphere", "allegorical characters", "thin ordinary human friction").
- **Local-model JSON drift** (abook, Novel-Generator's JSON repair counter, KazKozDev "A model that ignores JSON Schema stalls the run") — every 2026 project has a coercing parser.
- **Silent damage during repair**: rewrites that "silence the chapter's dialogue, fuse its paragraphs or quietly cut a sixth of it" (KazKozDev) — diff every rewrite against its source.
- **Over-compression**: below ~1,700–1,800 words the compressed chapter becomes the new weakest.

## 6. Numbers cheat sheet
- Chapter keep threshold 6.0/10, retry ≤5; foundation exit 7.5 (lore 7.0); plateau `<0.5` change over 2 cycles after cycle 3; Opus loop ≤4 rounds, stop at >50% hedged or ≤2 items. (autonovel)
- Cuts: OVER-EXPLAIN ~32%, REDUNDANT ~26%; target chapters >17% fat; expect 3–4% of words cut; compressed chapter 2,200–3,000 w, floor 1,700–1,800; expansion +800–1,500 w; rewriter bloat +30%. (autonovel)
- ~15–30 API-hours for a 75k-word novel; 4 personas × 10 questions; consensus 3/4. (autonovel)
- Continuity thresholds: thread dormant >3 chapters, main character absent >5. (Novel-OS)
- Quality rewrite <0.65 overall; pacing guidance <0.70 per axis. (libriscribe)
- Chapter ≥80% of 4,000-word target; repair ≤5×. (KazKozDev)
- AIStoryWriter: `CHAPTER_QUALITY=85`, min 1 / max 3 revisions, 8k context default.
- gemini-writer: compress at 900k tokens, checkpoint every 50 iterations, ≤300 iterations.
- Human-in-loop guidance (slop.sus.cat): 250–1,200 words per generation, previous 4 scenes as context, ~6,000 words of static rules per call, cap AI revision at 3 passes, temperature 0.7–1.0, min-p 0.05–0.1.

## Remaining FAIL (1)

   Yoko is placed on the bench behind Sodoku's left shoulder in the same paragraph, with no turn, glance or other perception cue; ear position and tail stillness arrive as bare fact in a close, POV-locked narration. The draft already repairs this exact failure mode in two later places ("Behind him the bench gave no sound. Fix proposed by the critics: move the detail to a moment where he turns toward the bench, or convert it to what he can hear or what nine years tell him of her habitual posture, or anchor it the way the two later instances are anchored. A one-sentence fix.

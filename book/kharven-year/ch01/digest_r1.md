## 2. FAIL [POV and knowledge]

> I wrote the first order myself, and the date is on it in my hand, and I have known since the day I wrote it that a Hallenfeld would one day read it.

Problem: Head-hop: this italic is Lambert's own first-person interior thought, not Sodoku's. It follows a paragraph entirely about Lambert's body (his hands going behind his back) and uses 'I' for events only Lambert could narrate (that he personally wrote and dated the first Vaultmere order and has privately known since that day a Hallenfeld would read it). Sodoku is the sole POV; he never perceives this, is never told it on the page, and brief blank 2 (knows / wrongly believes / reader knows) never declares it as something the reader learns that the POV does not.

Fix: Cut the italic. The physical tell already on the page (Lambert's hands going behind his back, in the same order as the seventh month) is a governed callback under R4-13-WHERE_SIGNIFICANCE_LIVES and can carry the beat unglossed, from outside, the way Sodoku is actually able to perceive it.

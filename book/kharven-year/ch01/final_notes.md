## Summary for continuity (≤200 words)

Seven days after the signing, Ice-out. The instrument still lies on the lower-hall table under four river stones; nobody has decided where it is entered. The death-house is being emptied a body a morning. Sodoku stands at the head of the table all session with the grain returns, hands flat on the boards; he has not sat in the chair in seven mornings. Nalūn's factor asks for a fair copy for Vaultmere, for entry, not sealing. Lambert says there are three places it could go, names only the Seat's pillar, and says he has not been asked his opinion on the order. The Bench's clerk asks Yoko for her four lines; Yoko refuses in one sentence; the Bench notes it; the king lets it stand. Sodoku orders the copy, names Tabitha to carry it, and says in front of Lambert that the road is hers; Lambert's hands go behind his back. Lambert copies the four leaves himself; Tabitha leaves at evening with the copy inside her coat, twelve days to Vaultmere by the Ironwood March. In the passage Brida prices his year of no full nights, due in the Thin Weeks; he asks after her stack. Miku holds his chair all day and watches the king's hands.

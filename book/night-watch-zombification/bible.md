## Premise

Isaac's words: "Continue the story of The Night Watch based on Wystan Ashmore and Malphas which eventually ends with them working at the Aetherion Academy separately without knowing they were connected. This story is called 'The Night Watch — Zombification Arc'. 80 chapters."

The spine: Wystan's gift "gets the hand, never the purse," and Article XV prosecutes only the purse. This time he gets the purse (Xu Deming) and never the hand (Malphas). Malphas makes the dose walk: a contractile matrix in a saturation gradient leans toward a sink, and the sink is the quiet, meaning the capped mains, the districts the Accord cuts at night or abandons to the Withering, and the lowest-saturation body in a room, which is a Voidic man whose Shell rests near zero. The case closes on Deming by Accord law. The hand is named in no file Wystan can read. The Accord's classification stays *bleed*. Nine years later both men teach at the Aetherion Academy in separate wings.

## Isaac's rule for this book: neither man learns of the other

- Malphas knows his hunter only as "the Night Register" (the clerk told Tally it "was a person") and at most "a desk on Cutler Row with a brass token." He designs countermeasures against the Division field manual's unsigned paragraph (Fixatio seals, intermediaries, waiting a season, moving a wall) as against an institution. He never hears or reads Wystan's name, and never sees him closer than the Bench hall.
- Wystan knows his quarry only as "the Lord" (never heard; he uses "the formulator," "the man who never goes near them"). No file he can read names the hand.
- **One shared room, once:** the afternoon verdict sitting of Deming's hearing (ch60). The verdict is read "on the Register's file" and no officer is named aloud. Malphas is in the gallery; he is not present for Wystan's testimony (ch59, morning). Wystan notices one thing (a man whose hands keep a living colour in a cold hall) and it is never paid as recognition, in that chapter or any later one.
- Movement VI keeps them in separate wings. Salter sees a man hold a cup by the rim across the quad (ch78); Wystan stands at the west-range door where the hum stops and turns back east (ch74, ch79). Neither detail is ever connected by either man or by the narration.
- Malphas is never POV until Movement VI, where he carries POV under his Academy name. Before that he is seen from Vesk, Nuvalik, Foss and Deming, and stays opaque.

## Voice note

The archive crossed between two rooms in three scenes; the book keeps both sides on one calendar and one clock, and each side sees the other only as a form. The Register never has a face for the cell and the Lord never has a name for the Register, and nothing noticed across that line is ever paid as recognition; the narration never winks at the reader who knows both. **Exemplar.** The main came back at the sixth division and the building filled with it from the floor up. Wystan heard it arrive under the boards and his shoulders came down half an inch. He wrote the time in the margin. The tin sat on the sill under his brass, and the four pieces of chapter wax lay beside it in a fold of paper dated on the outside in his hand, squared to the edge.

**Anti-exemplar.** Somewhere out there a man with warm hands was watching, and Wystan could almost feel him. Hunter and hunted, two sides of the same coin, bound by a thread neither could see. If only he knew how close he was.

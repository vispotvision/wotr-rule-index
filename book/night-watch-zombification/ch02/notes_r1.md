## The three strata, and the mechanism

- **Aether:** the capped main, the dead housings, the district struck off the Board's schedule. The room is the low point, and that is stated as a condition of the building rather than as a reading.
- **Wellspring:** the Green Pulse as the card gives it, indeterminate growth with no notion of a finished size and continuous conversion off whatever is ambient. The real phenomenon dramatised is a tropism: differential shortening down a gradient, stated in the room as "the dry side of the pot is where the root goes" and by Nuvalik as "that is what shortening does."
- **Essence:** absent by design, and the absence is the design. No Crystal, no self, no anchor, because an anchor is a binding, a binding takes a name, and a name is what the Accord can prosecute. The fault that falls out of the mechanism: a thing with no name in it cannot be told where to go, and Malphas says so on the page. It can only be given somewhere emptier.

## Mechanism (R13-3-PHENOMENON_MANDATE, R13-7-RESEARCH_RULE)

- **Real phenomenon:** mechanochemical actuation down a concentration gradient. A contractile network shortens in proportion to the local difference, so it moves its substrate toward the low end. The nearest real analogues are gradient-driven hydrogel actuators and muscle-like contractile gels. It is also the "down the slope" of any transport law, as Foss says.
- **Fault that falls out of it:** the dose ends when the gradient ends. Once she reaches the lowest point the net has "nothing left to shorten into", and she stays down. That is why she goes nowhere after the housing.

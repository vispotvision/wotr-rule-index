# Paid in Kind — research

The book's rolling outside research (mandatory before any draft, 2026-11-07). The foundation's five briefs are in `research/`; each chapter's brief adds its own lookups below under `## ch NN`, and reuses what is here instead of repeating a lookup. Outside sources are ideas and fact-checks, never canon, never prose to copy.

## Foundation (2026-11-07)

- **`research/midmarch.md`** (the place): the Hundred Crowns town by town (Kingsbridge, Tollgarth, Pennycrown, Lastpenny, Nether Marl, the Stitch Hills, the Marls, Hollowhay, Odds Ferry, Hawes Bridge), who rules and how law works, named canon people, customs (chains and tolls, the Coat-Turning, crowns for a penny, market courts, toasts and greetings), money, food, clothes and colors, speech and street words, sounds and smells, weather by month, what is in the air in 715, monsters within a few days' walk, roads and travel times, news, free companies, collisions. Outside: deodand, pontage, chiminage (ideas only).
- **`research/trade.md`** (the trade): coin and a peddler's purse; the nine titles; how a hall examines Initiate, Apprentice and Journeyman; the hall rule and how a hall reads it; tolls, stalls, licenses, the market court; credit, tallies, pledges and the Bond of the Fifth; the gear of the dead and where a peddler lawfully buys it; prices; what real chapmen carried; ten deals small to large. Outside: Wikipedia on chapmen, pedlars, the piepowder court, lex mercatoria, forestalling, tally sticks, heriot; research on frippers, pawnbroking, army sutlers and medieval estate accounts.
- **`research/power.md`** (Provenance): the canon anchors (Anamnesis, its signature, the glyph [Lei] Binding, the Body Path, Traits before and after waking, windows, Stages I and II, Levels, the Waking Draft), how it really works (the five records a used thing keeps: use-wear, the Kaiser effect, fatigue striations, the Villari effect, thermoluminescence; procedural memory), stage by stage, the cost and how it escalates, fair counters, the five moves, window lines. Outside: Superpower Wiki (Psychometry, Adoptive Muscle Memory), VS Battles (Taskmaster), Wikipedia on the physics.
- **`research/fight.md`** (the fighting): the fight law; Ned's body at each Stage; Provenance in a fight; his starting kit piece by piece; the dead man's lot (long messer and Lecküchner's twitch and going-through; sword and buckler and MS I.33's guards and the bind; the levy spear and George Silver's true times; the duelist's line; the brawler's knife); the gap between a borrowed blow and an untrained body; the yard test; five threats (Greater Wolves, greywalkers, Nether Marl and the Minotaur, chain-men, cordial-men); wounds of the hands, forearms, ribs and head. Outside: Wiktenauer (Lecküchner, Zucken, the Walpurgis Fechtbuch, Silver's Bref Instructions), Wikipedia (Messer, Falchion, Quarterstaff, Buckler, Rondel dagger, wolf attack, boxer's and nightstick fractures, rib fracture, concussion, Visby, Towton).
- **`research/names.md`** (names and words): the naming law; forty checked names; Ned's name in other mouths (chapman meant buyer as well as seller; kemp meant champion); trade and market words with their old senses; insults and boasts by weights and measures; five versions of "one has to sell to the other"; epithets and how criers and minstrels cry them; sound words in the Common English register. Outside: 26 Datamuse calls and 31 Wiktionary calls (chapman, huckster, fripper, cadger, pedlar, piepowder, kemp, cempa, handsel, toller, hayward, gísl, knitbone, sutler and more).

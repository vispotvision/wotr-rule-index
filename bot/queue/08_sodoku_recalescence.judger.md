# Judger's note — Recalescence — Kharven thread, the year after

## Recap for the players

Hild Ice and Osric of Hallenfeld died together in the cistern doorway, and four hundred paces up the road Sodoku Moto felt the bond go and went down on both knees in the ice, bleeding from the mouth, the ears and the untouched left shoulder. With the eye stuck open and showing him everything at once, he gave Gisei the last whole memory he had of Freya Moto, her kitchen, and got a shape with nothing in it in return. Yoko Mishiro came out of the ice at a sprint, knelt beside him and closed her hand on his forearm, and that was the one thing that reached him. Then the cold he had carried since the corridor let go all at once: the ice around his knees went to mud in two seconds, the steam stood in the windless valley, and every man inside sixty feet stopped fighting to look. The black sky over Kharven fractured and the strands came down, the first into the Ashgate valley four miles west and the rest into him, and he stood up out of the steam with Yoko's hand still on his arm.

- Yoko's nose was wrong for the first time; Sodoku had no scent. *"It is wrong now. He is under my hand and there is no scent on him at all."*
- Sodoku once called Yoko a tool; she made her peace with the word. *"he took it and called me a tool, and I made my peace with the word on a step in the rain"*
- Yoko's touch reached him at the anchor layer as warmth. *"It came in at the layer where an anchor lives, which is the one layer that had not gone anywhere, and it arrived as warmth and it was warmth"*
- He has never once said anything to Yoko about her being there. *"Everything else is gone and she is still here and she has been still here since we were children and I have never once said anything about it to her."*
- Fourteen years since the corridor, half his life, held undischarged. *"Fourteen years. Everything from the corridor onward, held below the point, undischarged, waiting on a seed that never came"*
- Three Court assessments recorded an unexplained inefficiency in his line. *"Three assessments had recorded the same unexplained inefficiency in his line and none of them had found the cause."*
- The ice around both knees went to mud; every man inside sixty feet turned to look and none started fighting again. *"Every man still upright inside sixty feet turned to look. Some of them had been fighting eleven seconds earlier. None of them started again."*
- The Gisei bill: Freya's kitchen, gone for good. *"afterward there was a shape where the kitchen had been and no contents in the shape, and he would go looking for it that night and find the shape and not understand what was supposed to be in it."*
- He did not offer the Yoko bond and could not have. *"he could not have got that one out of his chest with both hands."*
- The sky fractured and the strands came down. *"The black over the narrows, the black that had been over Kharven since the aurora came down, went to fractures from horizon to horizon, and through the fractures came the strands."*
- The first strand went into his brother in the Ashgate valley, four miles west, unnamed. *"there was a thing coming down out of the sky into his brother and it was not his to look at"*
- The rest went into Sodoku without resistance. *"They came into the shoulders and the crown and the back of both hands and they went in without resistance, because there was nothing at the surface of him left to resist with"*
- Muken's reign is nineteen years on the page. *"Nineteen years of a reign and his son cannot point at a moment."*
- Last line: *"Sodoku Moto put one hand flat in the melted mud, and got his boot under him, and stood up out of the steam with Yoko Mishiro's hand still closed on his arm."*

# Prose review pass

Run silently on every scene before it is posted (Isaac, 2026-10-31); fixes are made before Isaac sees the scene. Fights add `desktop/FIGHT_REVIEW_PASS.md`. A bare turn gets the same six checks, lightly. `verify_scene` runs after this, not instead of it.

1. **Swap test for voices.** Swap two speakers' lines. If nobody would notice, rewrite them: culture and rank first, then the want, then the verbal fingerprint.
2. **Recycled imagery and stock devices.** Check the draft against `desktop/style-bank/used-devices.md`. Replace any reused image, simile, beat or sound. Each scene carries one image the reader has not seen in any earlier scene. Log it there afterward.
3. **Thought and reaction after every beat.** Count how often an action is followed by a thought or a reaction. If it is most of them, keep the interior for the turns and let the rest run on action and the world. Vary the volume: one movement at peak, the quietest beat just before it.
4. **Sense variety and lived-in rooms.** The scene opens on a sense other than the one the last scene opened on. A room carries ambient noise that is not the plot, except in private moments.
5. **Windows (R84-1, R84-2).** Wherever the soul has something to report (reserve, a wound's status line, a skill gain, a level, a blessing, a vow's terms, Pressure after the room has felt it), a window stands as a text block after the event, the owner's only, in Grades, percentages, bars and levels, with EU, AU/s and η in the notes. The body comes first and the prose never turns into numbers. On Discord and in a turn it is plain text, and his PC's gains are offered as window lines he keeps or changes (R84-23).
6. **Gear (R85-1).** Anyone new on the page has their armor, clothes and weapons shown, a major person piece by piece. A piece worth envy gets its Rarity said aloud or shown on a window, and someone in the room reacts to it.

After the pass: add the scene's signature image to the ledger, run `verify_scene`, and fix every FAIL.

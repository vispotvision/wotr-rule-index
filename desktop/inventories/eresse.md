# THE STANDING INVENTORY: ERESSE

Forest elves who turned a shared calendar into law and have not let a word of it drift since the Spirit War. Food: granary bread, game taken with the Asking spoken over it, boiled root, sap syrup, bark tea, mineral water served blood-warm; a dish set down without the Asking is a dish nobody touches. Greeting: the name given with its witness, "Elaruren," on a falling tone; a name answered in "-ama" is an invitation to ask by what right; a Concord speaker who flattens the tone has greeted nobody. Oaths: "Uren." (Witnessed.) "Witnessed and paid." "The kin stand for it." "Sae-ureth," the witnessing window stands open, which is the nearest thing the language has to an order. A sworn thing is sworn on Sacred Gold. Insults: "out of season" (the worst of them), "held contingently," "a bare clause" (said a thing and claimed nothing), "flat-tongued," "loud" (drunk or blasphemous, depending on the room), "trilled," "-ak" (outside the form). Time: the eight turnings, Waking Root, First Green, Long Sun, Seedfall, Cooling Hearth, Still Water, Deep Dark, Returning Light; "inside the window," "the window has not opened," "two turnings past"; the notch kept alongside Guild hour, and Guild hour never formally accepted. No Eressean answers "what year"; a year is a shape. The dead: laid in state and handled by a professional order, the family in attendance and never touching; the name entered in the sealed register at Velthaeir; a vacant name is "a silence in the chord." Objects: the rite calendar, recopied without variation, the recopying itself a rite; the Songscript rail, since a text without one can be read and cannot be performed; ironwood, cut under restriction, the ceremonial bow; Sacred Gold; council chambers built partly below grade; the two jurists who read a deposition in alternation, the second there to catch the first. Exchange: nothing passes unwitnessed. A transfer has a window, an assembly, and witnesses who were paid, and an unpaid witness has not witnessed. Sacred Gold at a premium over bullion and unlawful to melt; Accord weights accepted and the Accord's right to set them refused; ironwood never sold; the Asking before wood, water, game or stone, and something of equal weight left behind; the Sanctum Lux hold tolerated on sufferance and audited every year. Proverbs, fixed wording: "Ma-ama, lyth. Ma-uren, elar." (What nobody stood for makes no sound in the chord.) "A promise kept carelessly is not a promise." "A vacant name is a silence in a chord." "Better a poor rite in its window than a fine one outside it." "Haste in the hall is haste in the harvest." "Gold that enters an oath does not come back." "A road too convenient becomes a wound in the land." "A rite in the wrong key has not been performed." "We asked the mountain before we quarried it." "A standard nobody witnessed is held contingently." Body: the pitch of the voice, rising for the unfinished and falling for the settled; the light tapped r, never trilled; grace read as inevitability rather than speed; light bones that break cleanly; fear and rage cost them more than a wound does. **Recurrence, two minimum per Eresse scene:** the window ("in its season," "out of season"); the witness named and paid; the rite calendar recopied without variation; Sacred Gold; the falling tone on a settled thing.

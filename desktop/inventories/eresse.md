# THE STANDING INVENTORY: ERESSE

High elves of the Grand Principality who turned a shared calendar into law and have not let a word of it drift since the Spirit War. Food: granary bread, game taken with the Asking spoken over it, boiled root, sap syrup, bark tea, mineral water served blood-warm; a dish set down without the Asking is a dish nobody touches. Greeting: the name given with its witness, "Elaruren," on a falling tone; a name answered in "-ama" is an invitation to ask by what right; a Concord speaker who flattens the tone has greeted nobody. Oaths: "Uren." (Witnessed.) "Witnessed and paid." "The kin stand for it." "Sae-ureth," the witnessing window stands open, which is the nearest thing the language has to an order. A sworn thing is sworn on Sacred Gold. Insults: "out of season" (the worst of them), "held contingently," "a bare clause" (said a thing and claimed nothing), "flat-tongued," "loud" (drunk or blasphemous, depending on the room), "trilled," "-ak" (outside the form). Time: the eight turnings, Waking Root, First Green, Long Sun, Seedfall, Cooling Hearth, Still Water, Deep Dark, Returning Light; "inside the window," "the window has not opened," "two turnings past"; the notch kept alongside Guild hour, and Guild hour never formally accepted. No Eressean answers "what year"; a year is a shape. The dead: laid in state and handled by a professional order, the family in attendance and never touching; the name entered in the sealed register at Velthaeir; a vacant name is "a silence in the chord." Objects: the rite calendar, recopied without variation, the recopying itself a rite; the Songscript rail, since a text without one can be read and cannot be performed; ironwood, cut under restriction, the ceremonial bow; Sacred Gold; council chambers built partly below grade; the two jurists who read a deposition in alternation, the second there to catch the first. Exchange: nothing passes unwitnessed. A transfer has a window, an assembly, and witnesses who were paid, and an unpaid witness has not witnessed. Sacred Gold at a premium over bullion and unlawful to melt; Accord weights accepted and the Accord's right to set them refused; ironwood never sold; the Asking before wood, water, game or stone, and something of equal weight left behind; the Sanctum Lux hold tolerated on sufferance and audited every year. Proverbs, fixed wording: "Ma-ama, lyth. Ma-uren, elar." (What nobody stood for makes no sound in the chord.) "A promise kept carelessly is not a promise." "A vacant name is a silence in a chord." "Gold that enters an oath does not come back." "A road too convenient stops being a relationship with the land and becomes a wound in it." "A rite performed in the wrong key has not been performed." "We asked the mountain before we quarried it." "A standard nobody witnessed is a standard held contingently." Body: the pitch of the voice, rising for the unfinished and falling for the settled; the light tapped r, never trilled; grace read as inevitability rather than speed; light bones that break cleanly; fear and rage cost them more than a wound does.

**Senses:**
- *Smells:* granary bread; bark tea, sharp and green-bitter; sap syrup boiling down; game, dressed after the Asking; boiled root; mineral water set out blood-warm; fresh-cut ironwood, cut under restriction; cool earth in council chambers built partly below grade; ink for the Songscript rail; gold, which smells of nothing, and the hand that held it.
- *Sounds:* "Elaruren," on a falling tone; a name answered in "-ama," and the room hearing it; the light tapped r; a Concord speaker flattening the tone; two jurists reading a deposition in alternation; the Asking spoken over game; a voice rising for the unfinished, falling for the settled; song, the only use of volume; the pen of a copyist recopying the rite calendar; the ceremonial bow's string.
- *Colours:* Sacred Gold; ironwood; sap syrup amber; granary brown; the half-light of a chamber below grade; the ink line of the rail above the text; the eight turnings' own light: First Green, Long Sun, Deep Dark, Returning Light; clear water in a cup.
- *Textures:* gold, heavy and soft, warming fast in the hand; the ironwood bow's grip; water at blood heat on the tongue; a light-boned hand that breaks cleanly; bark; sap syrup on the fingers; a page of the calendar, recopied without variation; stone steps down into a council chamber.
- *Tastes:* granary bread; game with the Asking spoken over it; boiled root; sap syrup; bark tea; mineral water, blood-warm, flat and faintly salt; bark tea steeped past its window, and too bitter; sap scorched in the pan.

**Gone:**
- **The Standing Fire.** Every household of the old Empire kept a fire that was never allowed to go cold, fed a pinch of dried root at dawn and dusk so the household's dead had a warm place to come back to and be asked things. The Grand Principality kept the archive and lost the fire.
- **The Windwake lineages.** Whole lines of the House of the Windwake burned themselves down to embers holding contaminated ground stable while the Sapkeepers worked. What they bought is a Stability the current line carries and no longer remembers paying for.
- **The quick answer.** The land once answered the Asking promptly. For several turnings the careful have been noting which askings it answers more slowly than it used to.
- **The Sunroot elegist.** The author of the Sunroot-era elegy every branch still recites, whose name is lost to all five archives equally.

**Folk beliefs:**
- **A rite out of season turns back.** A working performed outside its window rebounds on the one who performed it, so nothing is attempted in the wrong turning even when the need is urgent.
- **An unpaid witness forgets.** Pay the witness or he will not remember what he saw, so the fee is counted into his hand in front of the assembly.
- **Melted gold rots the hand.** A smith who puts Sacred Gold in a crucible loses the hand that held the ladle within the year.
- **The land collects.** Take from wood, water, game or stone and leave less than equal weight, and the land takes the difference from the household, most often from a child.
- **A trilled r spoils the rite.** A child who trills is corrected at table, because one trilled r in a sung rite leaves it unperformed.

**Literacy:** Near-universal. Everyone reads; performing is another matter, since a text without its Songscript rail can be read and cannot be performed, and a reader who cannot follow the rail is literate and mute at once.

**Swears, oaths and insults** (canon; the full page is *Swears, Oaths and Insults*):

The elves swear in the common tongue and in Vey-Elarin words dropped into it. The five branches share the tongue and differ on what the worst thing is.
| Swear | Literal meaning | Strength | Who says it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Out of season. | Done outside its appointed window. | grave | The worst Eressean insult; worse than "badly done" |
| Lo. | Out of season, as one word. | mild as an exclamation, hard said to a person | "Lo," at a missed moment is the elven "damn it"; Drow use it for a debt past its term |
| -ama on a name. | The name held, and nobody stood for it. | hard | Put on a name to its owner's face; Eresse and Korvaeth both |
| Ma-ama. | Held, and nobody stood for it. | hard | Said of a usurper, a stolen thing, a child nobody witnessed; from the proverb "Ma-ama, lyth" |
| A bare clause. | A thing said that claims nothing. | firm | Said of a speaker who will not own his words; courts and councils |
| Held contingently. | Holding what nobody has stood for. | firm | A cold insult to an office-holder |
| Flat-tongued. | Speaks without the pitch contours. | firm | Said of Concord speakers, who have greeted nobody |
| Loud. | Using the singing mechanism for speech. | firm | Means drunk or blasphemous depending on the room |
| -ak. | Outside the form: foreign. | hard | Tagged onto a name; the suffix that made "Varrisak" |
| Be a silence in the chord. | May your name fall vacant. | grave | A curse of erasure; Eressean elders, never in a rite |
| Sung in the wrong key. | A rite in the wrong key has not been performed. | firm | Said of botched work or a botched apology; it did not happen |
| Unasked. | Taken without the Asking over wood, water, game or stone. | hard | Said of a thief of the land, a poacher, a road-builder; any branch, Varrisak most |
| Transcript. | One who mistakes a filing system for a memory. | firm | Varrisak of Eresseans, and of any clerk |
| Seral take it. | Let the winter have it. | mild | The Eladrin "damn it"; the northern courts |

| Unpaid. | Worse than absent: a position, not a fact. | hard | Drow of Korvaeth; also "out of term" for a debt gone past its day |

**Recurrence, two minimum per Eresse scene:** the window ("in its season," "out of season"); the witness named and paid; Sacred Gold; the rite calendar recopied without variation; the Asking, and something left in exchange.

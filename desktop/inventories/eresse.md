# THE STANDING INVENTORY: ERESSE

Note: the sources are silent on what Eresse eats, on the bodily greeting, and on whether the household fire survives into the Grand Principality; each is filled here with the plainest option one step from the forest, the Asking, and the line that Eresse kept the archive and lost the fire.

Forest elves who turned a shared calendar into law and have not let a word of it drift since the Spirit War. Food: granary bread, game taken with the Asking spoken over it, boiled root, sap syrup, bark tea, spring water served blood-warm; a dish set down without the Asking is a dish nobody touches. Greeting: no touch. The name given in full with its witness, "Elaruren," on a falling tone, hands empty and in view; asking a stranger's name is asking by what right, and a Concord speaker who flattens the tone has greeted nobody. Oaths: "Uren." (Witnessed.) "Witnessed and paid." "The kin stand for it." "Sae-ureth," the window stands open, which is the nearest thing the language has to an order. A sworn thing is sworn on Sacred Gold, and gold that goes into an oath stays there. Insults: "out of season" (the worst of them), "held contingently," "a bare clause" (said a thing and claimed nothing), "flat-tongued," "loud" (drunk or blasphemous, depending on the room), "trilled," "-ak" (outside the form). Time: long units. The eight turnings, Waking Root, First Green, Long Sun, Seedfall, Cooling Hearth, Still Water, Deep Dark, Returning Light; "inside the window," "the window has not opened," "a reading" (eleven days at Velthaeir), "a finding" (nine), "two turnings past." No Eressean answers "what year"; a year is a shape. The dead: laid in state and handled by the rite order, the family in attendance and never touching; the name entered in the sealed register; a name not given on is "a silence in the chord," and a house will give a dead friend's name on before it leaves the gap. Objects: the rite calendar, recopied without variation, the recopying itself a rite; the Songscript rail, since a text without one can be read and cannot be performed; the Standing Fire, kept by an attendant of the rite office, a pinch of dried root at dawn and dusk, the family watching; the coil, nine passes by two attendants, and fewer passes is a statement; ironwood, cut under restriction, the ceremonial bow; Sacred Gold; the thumb in the wax on the witness leaf; council chambers dug half below grade; pale stone. Exchange: nothing passes unwitnessed. A transfer has a window, an assembly, and witnesses who were paid, and an unpaid witness has not witnessed. Sacred Gold at a premium over bullion and unmeltable; Accord weights accepted and the Accord's right to set them refused; ironwood never sold; the Asking before wood, water, game or stone, and something of equal weight left behind. Proverbs, fixed wording: "Ma-ama, lyth. Ma-uren, elar." (What nobody stood for makes no sound in the chord.) "A promise kept carelessly is not a promise." "A vacant name is a silence in a chord." "Better a poor rite in its window than a fine one outside it." "Haste in the hall is haste in the harvest." "Gold that enters an oath does not come back." "A road too convenient becomes a wound in the land." "A rite in the wrong key has not been performed." "We asked the mountain before we quarried it." "A name and an office are two objects." "A standard nobody witnessed is held contingently." Body: the pitch of the voice, rising for the pending and falling for the settled, and the pause of exact length; the light tapped r; the hands, empty or holding; stillness read as inevitability; the coil; light bones that break cleanly; fear and rage cost them more than a wound does. **Recurrence, two minimum per Eresse scene:** the window ("in its season," "out of season"); the witness named and paid; the rite calendar; Sacred Gold; the Standing Fire kept by office.

# THE STANDING INVENTORY: THE KETH-GORRUM (UNDAAR-KETH AND THE EXPANSE)

Note: the scene archive seats the Third Ashfold and Kaadre-Six under the Keth-Gorrum, so this Inventory covers the island parliament and the mainland Mandate as one voice; where the sources are silent (a burial, a soldier's greeting, what is on the table) the plainest reading of basalt, tally-cut law and a garrison ration is chosen and used as canon here. Items marked (M) are Mandate texture, (B) Bugbear.

Core-stone people who chose cohesion over dispersal, governed by weight carried in public. Food: thurr-kep (the root that tastes of charcoal and honey, High Islands only), ash-grain, taro, breadfruit, kelp, shelf fish, sulphur on the wind at every forge; the state's grain (M), the ration that buys a citizen's place on the wall; boreal fur and resin (M). Greeting: keth-ur, both palms shown, meaning the more important weapon is already down; the name given in full with its rank compound, since a name is the institution's current opinion of a man; between soldiers the bare root, "Vrekh," answered "Vrekh" (hold, hold). Bugbears set the closed fist flat on a table: I am staying. Oaths: "On the Rock." "On the Stone." (M) "It is carved," for a thing that will not be unsaid, because a hobgoblin oath is Engraved into the Soul Crystal and breaking it is a medical event. "Brakhakh, keth": borne and finished, sealed. Insults: kha-ur, the weightless one (kha-urrim for humans, and the insult is about load); kaanith, a jumped-up officer; goor, found wanting under weighing; "noisy," for a commander who decrees much and enforces little (M); "made of unshaped force," compounded onto a name in front of witnesses; nakh, one who took without bearing the cost; reversing a man's compound back at him; "table-turner" for an Orc or anyone else who cannot sit through an argument; "gurr," for a Concord speaker who puts a vowel in grr. Time: a Reading (the fifteen minutes of decree at every square, daily, "before the Reading," "two Readings gone") (M); a term, six years; a census, twelve; a durrak, a thousand-count; "since the Scattering"; "the hole at 411," the year the axe took out of the archive; the slow nights, deep cold when Stone-Blood runs late and a formation loses its count. The dead: entered. A dead officer is entered on the Rock and his compound closes; the marker is the entry, the body goes under basalt without one. Bugbears keep the Long Silence a full day, a searching reflex in the scalp for a voice that has stopped (B). Mourning is duration; the vowel is held and the voice drops. Objects: the board (a decree is a board, a ruling is a post, cut with one blade, stem and side-cuts); the shoulder plate, four cuts and a crossbar, readable at forty paces; the chisel of the Stone-Carver, whose groove depth is the law's quality (M); the carried stone, taken from shore to summit for every new responsibility (B); the Speaker's basalt gavel that has never struck; iron rings in a topknot, each a weight earned; volcanic-glass hulls; the signal relay on the roof; the shed roof over a working party. Exchange: weight for weight, sulphur and obsidian by the pound; scrip inside the border and Nalūn salt bars outside it, taken with a face (M); a fish price argued for eleven years; every deal is brakh or nakh, paid for or taken, and there is no third word. Proverbs, fixed wording: "What you paid for holds. What you took will open under you." "The hand opens when the border is safe, and the border is not safe." "A thousand years. A thousand years. And we bore it." "The stone remembers." "Resolved by geography." "What tried to move the mountain is part of its base now." "Loud is lost." "Held keeps the wound in it." "Never carried a stone, and it shows." "The best term is the one nobody remembers." "Monitor. Do not engage." Body: Legion Etchings, straight gold lines on ochre skin, rank and unit legible; brass eyes, pupils gone rectangular in formation; the sub-bass in the voice, felt in the ribs before it is understood, and the iron in a listener's blood answering it; emphasis by holding the vowel, volume being for shouting across water, so a hobgoblin in a rage goes quieter and slower; the verb fronting under stress, which every hobgoblin in the room hears as the situation being worse than reported; the palms opening at the end of the important point; Bugbear hair reading intent through the scalp before the eyes do, the wide-set stance nobody can shift, the 2.4 seconds after release (B); mineral tissue running slow at thirty below. **Recurrence, two minimum per Keth-Gorrum scene:** the open palm, keth-ur; the name spoken with its compound; the board and the cut (law as an object); the held vowel and the drop to quiet; "the stone remembers."

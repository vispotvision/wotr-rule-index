# THE STANDING INVENTORY: THE KETH-GORRUM (UNDAAR-KETH AND THE EXPANSE)

Note: the Fronts page seats the Third Ashfold and Kaadre-Six under the Keth-Gorrum, so this Inventory covers the island parliament and the mainland Mandate as one voice. Items marked (M) are Mandate texture, (B) Bugbear, (O) Ironblood Orc. Where the sources are silent (a burial, a soldier's greeting) the Inventory is silent too; anything invented in play gets entered the same session.

Core-stone people who chose cohesion over dispersal, governed by weight carried in public. Food: thurr-kep (the root that grows nowhere else and tastes of charcoal mixed with honey, High Islands only), volcanic-ash grain (the Tinker cultivar nobody has improved in three centuries), taro, breadfruit, kelp from the reef farms, shelf fish; sulphur and obsidian worked on the Shelf Islands; the state's grain (M), which every citizen who eats it fights for; boreal timber and furs (M). Greeting: keth-ur, palms open and forward, meaning the more important weapon is already laid down; the name given in full, given name, patronymic, district and rank compound, since the compound is what the institution currently thinks of a man; Bugbears set the closed fist on a flat surface: I am staying (B). Oaths: an oath is Engraved into the Soul Crystal at a Stage transition and is an anatomical fact, not a promise, and breaking it is immediate Dissonance; "Brakhakh, keth," borne and finished, sealed; the Crimson Order's Vow of Silence, Stage I through VI, with the maritime exception for navigation emergencies that the mainland monasteries have never acknowledged. Insults: kha-ur, the weightless one (kha-urrim for humans, and the insult is about load, not strength); kaanith, a jumped-up officer; goor, found wanting under weighing; "noisy," for a commander who issues many decrees and enforces few (M); shkarr compounded onto a name in front of witnesses, made of unshaped force; nakh, one who took without bearing the cost; a man's compound reversed back at him in debate; "gurr," for a Concord speaker who has put a vowel in grr; the overturned table, the thing that keeps an Orc out of the chamber. Time: the Reading, fifteen minutes of decree daily at every garrison and square, "before the Reading" (M); a term, six years; a census, twelve; a durrak, a thousand-count of years, durraak when the length is to be felt; "since the Scattering"; "the hole at 411," the year the axe took out of the archive. The dead: entered. A dead officer is entered as dead beside his name, and the entry is on the Rock; Bugbears keep the Long Silence a full day, the searching reflex in the scalp for a voice that has stopped (B). Objects: the board and the post (a decree is a board, a ruling is a post, cut with one blade, stem and side-cuts, a crossbar for length); the shoulder plate, four cuts and a crossbar, readable at forty paces; the Stone-Carver's chisel, groove depth the measure of the law's quality (M); the Decree Stone, superseded law chiselled off in public (M); the carried stone, shore to summit for every new responsibility (B); the Speaker's basalt gavel, four years unstruck because it would crack the podium; iron rings in a topknot, uk-thal, each a weight earned (O); volcanic-glass hulls; the Tinker signal relay on the parliament roof; the shed roof over a working party. Exchange: weight for weight, sulphur and obsidian priced by weight-barter and not coin; military scrip inside the Mandate border and Nalūn salt bars outside it, taken grudgingly (M); the fish-price argument that ended Vorrak the Patient's eleven years and a shelf-island dock; every acquisition is brakh or nakh, paid for or taken, and there is no third verb. Proverbs, fixed wording: "What you paid for holds. What you took will open under you." "The hand opens when the border is safe, and the border is not safe." "A thousand years. A thousand years. And we bore it." "The stone remembers." "Resolved by Geography." "The mountain does not negotiate. The mountain holds." "Everything that has tried to move this mountain is now part of its base." "People who have never carried a stone in their lives, and it shows." "Monitor, do not engage." Body: Legion Etchings, straight gold or bronze lines on burnt-ochre or deep red skin, rank and unit legible; polished brass or iron eyes, pupils gone rectangular in formation; the sub-bass of Gorr-Vaross and of Ironblood speech, felt in the ribs before it is understood, the iron in a listener's blood answering it (B, O); emphasis by holding the vowel, volume being for shouting across water, so a Vaross speaker in a rage goes quieter and slower; the verb fronting under stress, which every hobgoblin in the room hears as the situation being worse than reported; Bugbear hair reading intent through the scalp before the eyes do, the wide-set stance nobody can shift, the 2.4 seconds after release (B); the smell off the skin, hot iron and something mineral and dry; mineral tissue running slow at thirty below, three-quarters of a beat late. **Recurrence, two minimum per Keth-Gorrum scene:** the open palm, keth-ur; the name spoken with its compound; the board and the cut (law as an object); the held vowel and the drop to quiet; "the stone remembers."

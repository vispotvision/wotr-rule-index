# THE STANDING INVENTORY: KORVAETH, THE SUNROOT REGENCY, THE VESSEL ROAD

Note: sources are the Korvaeth arc (scenes/01_the_vacancy_korvaeth_arc.md and its State of Play, Fronts and Ledger entries), the Eressean and Varrisak grammar, the Eresse pages, Value, Coin and Trade, and the Eastern Rise ledger. Every item below is attested there or is one step from an attested fact; where the sources are silent the entry is silent. (S) marks Sunroot Regency texture, (V) the Vessel road; the rest is Korvaeth and the Under-Houses.

Drow of the New World north, holding under the doctrine that the holding validates itself, ruled by a ledger and a count. Food: winter grain, up the Vessel road with the salt and the timber; orchard fruit, since the fruit and the grain sit with the six refusing houses; salt, because salt is the cargo. Greeting: "In term?" answered "In term," the debt-marker as courtesy; hands shown open on entering a chamber, since a Reckoner watches hands; the proper bow on the way out, given even to a room one has just refused. Oaths: "I have entered it." "Before the count." "Witnessed," said once, because three times is the Elareth closing and makes a thing permanent; gold for anything sworn, since gold is the substrate of the oath and gold that enters an oath does not come back; Sacred Gold in Eresse, consecrated, cannot lawfully be melted and circulates at a premium over bullion (S). Insults: "unpaid," worse than absent; "out of term," lo, for a man whose debt has gone past its day; "northern edge," for a thing black on the face and pale in the grain, from the drown-alder boards cut where the salt never reached deep enough; the contingent suffix, -ama, put on a name to its owner's face; -ak tagged onto a name, outside the form, which is how Eresse made the word Varrisak (S). Time: bells, from the fourth bell of a cold yard to the ninth hour of a night attack; "before the entry closes," nine days from the count to the sealed register; a reading, eleven days and a fee; the withheld season; "held through a winter" (V); the eight Given turnings in place of months, Deep Dark and Returning Light among them (S). The dead: entered. A death cannot be voided, so the leaf is ruled and the name either passes, uren, or falls vacant and is entered as -ama, held by nobody, a silence in a chord. The Under-Houses bury in the ward or the deep road, as Vessa Thenn-Ashal buried two sons in hers; the Regency kept the archive and lost the fire (S). Objects: the ledger and the ruled leaf, the nib, the vault key that is the Reckoner's; the wax, and the thumb in it; the Reckoner's case and seal (V); black drown alder, dense, salt-black, in panelling and in the eleven coats; the plain pin and the four-pass coil; the truce-bough (S); Ember Flint, the commonest Essence-active thing in the east, a mild flame when struck; an Onceglass charge, one casting and then gravel, carried by the ward-carriers as a militia carries arrows. Exchange: the witness-fee, since an uncompensated witness has not witnessed; the forfeit, paid so an abstention is entered as one; the paid messenger, so a refusal has to be entered as a refusal; every debt is sae or lo, in term or past it, and there is no third word; salt, timber and winter grain up the road under a charter that says who may use it (V); roughgold panned by anyone and taken for oath work; standing built by what has been given away and accounted for. Proverbs, fixed wording: "A vacant name is a silence in a chord." "Held unwitnessed, silence. Held witnessed, a note." "Absent is a fact. Unpaid is a position." "The count does not stop for age." "The holding validates itself." "A lawful gift can be reread as duress by a Reckoner in forty years and voided. A death cannot be voided." "A regent with a claim is a usurper waiting for a bad winter." "Once is the form. Twice is emphasis. Three times is." "Worth more dead than the grove had ever been worth alive." "A road too convenient becomes a wound in the land." (S) "Ask in form, and pay the messengers." "I will not hold my name from a forest I have never stood in," carved above the eastern door. Body: the Korvaeth build, deep, shoulders narrow and squared, hands too large for the arms; skin the grey-violet of a four-day bruise, hair the flat unlit white bound in one black cord; hands first, open or behind the back; weight shifting foot to foot and then stopping all at once on the word that lands; the callus on a Reckoner's forefinger; cold from the wrists inward when the shape of a thing is seen; the coil, nine passes at a coronation and four passes and a pin for a woman holding nothing; the Eladrin counted silence, heard once at Vaelmarr, four for yes and nine for no, and past nine is judgement (S).

**Gone:**
- **The living groves below the Vessel Coast.** Four thousand hectares of standing timber drowned when Eresse opened the sea gates in the third year of the war. The trees took nine years to die and came out of it dense, black, beautiful, and worth more dead than the grove had ever been worth alive. Every drown-alder panel in a Korvaeth chamber is a piece of them.
- **The eleven-house compact.** The Vessel road ran open under it since before either claimant was born. It broke when Korrindal refused, and the road was reopened in four days at the cost of two hundred and eleven lives, entered as the Vessel Assize.
- **The coat workshop at Vessel.** The workshop that boiled the drown-alder facings into cloth-backing went under two winters ago. Eleven salt-black coats exist and one woman owns all eleven.
- **The Tsohanto Reach.** The barrow-grounds east of the Vessel line went quiet in the nine days after the yard, holding the stillness of a place already grieved for with nobody keeping it. No delegation has come in eleven years.

**Folk beliefs:**
- **Say it three times and it binds.** "Witnessed" is said once, because three times is the Elareth closing. So nobody says anything three times, not a curse, not a child's name called across a yard.
- **A debt out of term sickens.** A man whose debt is *lo* grows ill until he pays, so debtors pay on the ninth day with a fever or without one.
- **Spent Onceglass under the threshold.** The gravel of a charge that has cast once is laid under a doorstep, because a thing that has already cast keeps the next casting out.
- **A lie tastes of salt in drown alder.** In a room panelled in the black wood, a liar tastes salt on his own tongue, so hard business is done in drown-alder rooms.
- **A vacant name walks.** A person entered *-ama*, held by nobody, cannot rest, and will come back to the house that would not stand for them.

**Literacy:** High among the houses and the Under-Houses. Every head of house reads a ruled leaf, and a Reckoner reads it faster than he reads a face. The Vessel road's carters and the orchard tenants mostly do not, and for them the thumb in the wax is the signature.

**Recurrence, two minimum per Korvaeth scene:** the leaf and "I have entered it"; the salt and the drown alder; the count, whether of bells, witnesses or a held silence; "unpaid," and -ama said to a face; the witness-fee, paid or the witness was never there.

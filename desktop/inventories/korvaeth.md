# THE STANDING INVENTORY: KORVAETH, THE SUNROOT REGENCY, THE VESSEL ROAD

Note: the sources are the Korvaeth arc, the Eressean and Varrisak grammar, the Eresse pages and the Eastern Rise ledger; where they are silent (the table, the greeting, the burial) the plainest reading of a drowned salt coast, a northern winter, an eastern volcanic quarter and a people who enter everything is chosen and used as canon here. Items marked (S) are Sunroot Regency texture, (V) the Vessel road; the rest is Korvaeth and the Under-Houses.

Drow of the New World north, holding under the Varrisak doctrine that the holding validates itself, ruled by a ledger and a count. Food: winter grain down the Vessel road, baked dense and dark; orchard fruit, fresh in season and dried on the string out of it, because the fruit and the grain sit with the six refusing houses and the north eats what the road carries; salt on everything, since salt is the cargo; salted stores laid down in the deep roads; a root broth kept warm at Sunroot for the household fire (S); Regency granaries as a demonstration before they are a store (S). Greeting: "In term?" answered "In term," the debt-marker as courtesy, meaning nothing I owe has gone past its day; the hands shown open on entering a chamber, since a Reckoner watches hands and so does everyone else; the proper bow on the way out, given even to a room one has just refused; a four-count pause before answering the Regency, which is assent (S). Oaths: "Enter it." "Before the count." "Witnessed," said once, because twice is emphasis and three times is a consecration and nobody spends that on a market debt; "On the leaf"; gold for anything sworn, because gold is the substrate of the oath, and Sacred Gold at Vaelmarr cannot lawfully be melted once it has been sworn on (S). Insults: "unpaid," worse than absent, since absent is a fact and unpaid is a position; "out of term," lo, for a man whose debt has gone past its day; "northern edge," for a thing black on the face and pale in the grain, from the drown-alder boards cut where the salt never reached; the contingent suffix, -ama, put on someone's name to their face; -ak tagged onto a name, meaning outside the form, which is how the Eresseans made the word Varrisak and how the north returns it (S). Time: bells, from the fourth bell of a cold yard to the ninth hour of a night attack; "before the entry closes," nine days from the count to the sealed register; a reading, eleven days and a fee; the withheld season, the Korvaeth calendar's own name for the stretch in which no window is granted to a name nobody stood for; "when the road opens," which is spring, and "held through a winter" (V); the Given turnings at Sunroot, Deep Dark and Returning Light said as the month (S). The dead: entered. A death is the one act that cannot be voided, so the leaf is ruled and the name is either given on, uren, or falls vacant and is entered as -ama, held by nobody, a silence in a chord. The Under-Houses bury in the ward the dead were working, because Vessa Thenn-Ashal buried two sons that way and nobody has proposed carrying a body up a deep road; the Regency keeps the fire and the archive and consults the dead in neither (S). Objects: the ledger and the ruled leaf, the nib, the vault key that is the Reckoner's and the walk that is the Reckoner's; the wax, and the thumb in it; the Reckoner's case and seal, carried up the Vessel road (V); black drown alder, dense, salt-black, worth more dead than the grove was alive, in panelling and in the eleven coats; the plain pin and the four-pass coil; the truce-bough (S); Ember Flint in every field kit, struck for a mild flame; an Onceglass charge, one casting and then gravel, carried by the ward-carriers the way a western militia carries arrows. Exchange: the witness-fee, since an uncompensated witness has not witnessed; the forfeit, paid so an abstention is entered as one; the paid messenger, so a refusal has to be entered as a refusal; every debt is sae or lo, in term or past it, and there is no third word; salt, timber and winter grain in one direction and the charter that says who may use the road in the other (V); roughgold panned by anyone, taken for oath work and for nothing else at Sunroot (S); standing built by what has been given away and accounted for. Proverbs, fixed wording: "A vacant name is a silence in a chord." "Held unwitnessed, silence. Held witnessed, a note." "Absent is a fact. Unpaid is a position." "The count does not stop for age." "The holding validates itself." "A gift can be reread. A death cannot." "A regent with a claim is a usurper waiting for a bad winter." "Once is the form. Twice is emphasis. Three times is forever." "The salt goes to the heart or the board goes back." "A road too convenient is a wound." "Ask in form and pay the messenger." "The alder is worth more drowned." Body: the Korvaeth build, deep, shoulders narrow and squared, hands too large for the arms; skin the grey-violet of a four-day bruise and hair the flat unlit white, bound in one black cord; hands first, open or behind the back, and a whole chamber reading them; weight shifting foot to foot and then stopping all at once on the word that lands; the callus at the first joint of a Reckoner's forefinger and its ache after the second hour; cold arriving from the wrists inward when the shape of a thing is seen; the coil, nine passes by two attendants at a coronation and four passes and a pin for a woman holding nothing; the counted silence, four for yes and nine for no, and past nine is judgement (S). **Recurrence, two minimum per Korvaeth scene:** the leaf and "enter it"; the salt and the drown alder; the count, whether of bells, witnesses or a held silence; "unpaid" and "in term"; the witness-fee, paid or the witness was never there.

# THE STANDING INVENTORY: THE MARROWKIN AND THE EMBER HOLDS

DRAFT (2026-11-05) from the Dawi and Stannvaard Inventories, which stay in place until Isaac rules. Older scenes and rolls say Dawi and Stannvaard; both are old names for what stands here. Where the sources are silent the plainest option consistent with the Tally, the cask and imported grain is chosen and marked *derived*; *new* marks what the Marrowkin redo added. Where an item comes from Borin Ironheart's scenes alone it says so.

Varūn's people, made for endurance, who live inside the ribcage of something that fell in the Great Spirit War, forge with what they cut out of it, sell pieces of it, and keep a record of every ounce because someday the body will want it back. Everything below is downstream of four facts: the bread is bought in, the language has no future tense, a broken oath is audible in a name, and every one of them is in debt from the age of seven. Above them sit a forge, a republic and a court in one crater, held together because the cost of separating them exceeds the cost of managing their contradictions; Altherion on the rim, the Rimlands and the Corridor, the Ore Council's schedule and the Grand Church all lay their own layer over the shift and the Tally.

**Look (*new*):** chest high to a human, door wide, twice as heavy as they look, and they sink. Large wide-set eyes, amber through furnace orange to coal red, that throw lamplight back like a cat's; slit visors of carved horn or ivory in daylight, clan by clan. Hot blood the color of old rust that glows dull in a dark cut; snow melts on them; they steam in the cold; forge-flush across nose and cheekbones. Skin with a grain in it, warm ivory to dark amber. The rise: from about thirty, ridges of ivory bone push through at knuckle, brow, collarbone and forearm, both sexes, and the Soul-Runes are cut into them. Beards on both sexes, ash-white yellowing to rib color, ringed and singed. Deepkin have gone gray: slag eyes, chalk rise, no heat at all. Low flat voice with an under-note felt in the breastbone; they hear through bone, jaw to the wall, and knock code along it.

**Food:** the sour barley beer of the lower levels, which every visitor calls an acquired taste and every Marrowkin calls beer; the brew (*ollu*) and the aged brew (*ollut*); grain bought in at prices that make every other kingdom wince, so bread is dear (*derived*); stores that keep, meaning salted, dried, cellared and packed in bulk (*derived*); the shift-meal, since the guild shifts decide when a hold works, eats and sleeps (*derived*). The crib, a pastry case of meat and root carried down in a tin and eaten at the break, with a man's mark pressed into the corner of the crust so he gets his own back up. Redcaps, the fat emberwort fungus off the bone terraces, fried in tallow, tasting of iron, cheap and everywhere (*new*). Marrow on bread once, at the First Entry, a child's opening debt, and never sold to eat (*new*). Concord ale on the rim; salt tea in the factors' offices where the Nalūn trade sits. Salt comes in as stamped bars and is money before it is seasoning. In Altherion seven kitchens share every street, and a stranger can be placed by what he buys before he opens his mouth. Nobody in the crater cooks over an open flame if a forge-shoulder is nearer.

**Greeting:** the full name, said once, oath-name in the strong grade, and the listener hears the grade before the word. A stranger asked to say his own name is being audited, and knows it. "Open seam," answered "Open seam," the old miners' wish, at every shaft-lift and now at every door on the rim. Diagnostic question: "How's the seam?" (*derived*); among the old, "How's the account?" (*new*). In Altherion the speaking floor belongs to whoever holds it, and a man who talks over another at the River Crown has not greeted the chamber. The Three-Tongued greet in whichever law the room is sitting under, and change it mid-sentence when the room does.

**Names (*new*):** given name, oath-name second, line in the genitive, cut-name. Mirja Hiilekk Solisan, called Sixty Tuns. Given names are two elements, a word of the Body and a word of the work, in the sound rules of the Tally-Tongue: no voiced stops, one vowel class per word. A child is called by birth-shift and count until the First Entry at about seven. The cut-name is the largest thing you ever took from the Body, in plain words (Thimble, Half a Rib), and nobody is spared it. Bynames of deed (Straightedge, Sevenhoop) are older and hang on among the old and the Reach.

**Oaths:** "Kurme," which is the swearing itself; there is no lighter word. "While the brew lives." The Cask-Oath opening, sung over the barrel: "Ollu elää, kaasme elää. Ollu kuolee, ei kaasme kuole." A Marrowkin cannot say *I will*; he swears or he says "Kurlo," unbegun. "Under the stamp," for anything sworn in the way of trade, because the Assay Standard makes a counterfeit stamp a capital matter. "On the account," for anything sworn against the Body itself (*new*). A Measurewright swears the Hexagonal Oath. A guild master is sworn to the extraction schedule, which is the kingdom's true sovereign instrument. The River Crown's delegates swear at the round table, all seated, no chair higher than another.

**Insults:** the name said in the weak grade, the worst thing one Marrowkin can do to another in a single syllable; "kurnur," unentered; "voiced"; "wrong angle"; "askew"; "just rock," a claim or a man that has never been measured; "a Tinker's petition," any complaint filed for two hundred years and about to be filed again; "fire-muddler," one who confuses forge-heat with judgment-fire; "Three-Tongued" said as an insult, a man who agrees with whoever is in the room; "counterfeit," the worst word on the rim, which starts fights that end at tribunal; "bone-thief," for anyone who took and did not enter (*new*). Altherion is "the lid" and humans are "the weather" (*new*).

**Time:** the entry number, so a Marrowkin asked the year names the last entry made; hold hour beside Guild hour in two columns; the Guild hour starts here, kept on the great water clock on the Seat at Altherion and sent out by beacon and relay rider (*placement new*); the shift; "while the brew lives," a cask's age as a term; the *ikke*, one turn of a process; the unentered day (Munahi to the Moto), spent indoors and sober because nothing sworn on it holds, on the rim a holiday with an unusually high number of injuries; the nine days; the Drawing-Off, the week the deep halls send the year's aged brew up the shaft, a season word that carries leave-taking, what was kept going away, when each roll of the dead is read with its balance (R70-61; first in *The Drawing-Off*; the balance is *new*); the extraction schedule, read as the season, and the nine days of the Cutting Fair after it (*new*); "eight years on the array," the time it takes the tender's shake to arrive; the Council Chair's ten-year term; the Weighing Festival, once a year, and the false-positive rate read out with the gravity other kingdoms keep for coronations; "the fastest three days," the time it took to try and sentence the last counterfeiter.

**The dead:** returned. The body is walled into a cut gallery in the bone the line has worked, and that counts as a payment against the account (*new*; old form: interment in the rock the clan has worked). The sealed casket carried home however far, and not opened. The roll of the dead cut in stone or bone, readable for grade from across the chamber. The Oath-Breakers' wall at Kegshard, which states the grade and the count and never the offense. The cinder bead, compounded once at the death by the heir who will carry it, and it does not refill (*derived*). A man killed underground is brought up on the shaft-lift with his lamp lit and set at the head of the lift until the shift ends. The rim buries by seven traditions in one ground, each in its own row. A tender who has the shake is kept for life under the Tender's Obligation, and his funeral is paid by the guild that shook him.

**Objects:** the Tally and the entry (*tallut*); the bill of sale with the Return Clause at the bottom (*new*); the slit visor (*new*); old ivory, warm, gold-grained, a piece that may or may not lean toward the Ring (*new*); the cask (*kaas*); the hammer, which Borin calls his walking stick; the burin and the cut rune (*thrus*); the angle-set (*nikkat*); the beard-ring, etched, and the rings are old but the beard is older (Borin's; *derived* for the forge-culture generally); the ironwood handle; Cask-Oath Pitch, which will not cure outside the hold it was sworn in; the two-column ledger; the assay stamp, and its mark on every bar, which counts for more than the bar; the round table in the River Crown chamber; the shaft-lift and the chain-bridge; the miner's lamp, a squat brass frog-lamp with a hook, burning marrow-tallow that smokes orange, because an honest lamp tells you what it is eating; the forge-glow from Thane-Gorr under Breastbone, a dull orange pulse on the crater walls that travelers steer by and poets call a heartbeat; mine scrip, lead tokens stamped by the shaft's owner and good only at his stall at the pit-head; the crier's bill, hand-copied and posted on doors; the Ember Magistrates' flame-lit equilibrium courts; the Church compound's low consecrated walls and flame-lit rooms, bare of ornament as a matter of doctrine.

**Exchange:** the entry; nothing exists commercially until entered. Hold weight for the Tally, Accord weight for the relays, and the conversion is itself an entry. The Cutting Right: nothing off the Body without a name and a weight (*new*). Every sale of the Body read aloud, closing "returnable on the Lender's demand, carriage at the holder's charge" (*new*). Toll-right, honored by every branch including the Tower-Kin. The Reckoning: a life is not settled by any other life, a theft by any unrelated payment. The recipe is not property and cannot be surrendered. Grain north to Kharven, ironwood south to the Ring, and the Thornwalls know the difference between a shortage and a message. The extraction schedule decides how fast the world's metal moves. Nalūn's credit finances extraction and the Ring's metal backs Nalūn's credit; supply contracts are sealed at Ironlink, never here. Rent by warmth, near the furnace shoulder dear and the cold side of the bone cheap, and said with pride (*new*). Arbitration fees, pilgrim traffic and legation rent in Altherion, which makes nothing and sells everything. The Tender's Obligation, a lifetime's support owed for eight years of hands. The Essence Resource Sharing Act and the Trade Equality Act, cited on the rim the way scripture is cited elsewhere.

**Proverbs, fixed wording:** "You are still here." "Varūn did not make us strong. He made us the thing still standing after strength finished arguing with itself." "The brew lives, the oath lives. The brew dies, the oath does not die." "The angles are true. The entry holds." "Unbegun. Not undertaken." "Five ways up the same mountain, and not one of us agrees on what the top is for." "Eight ways to still be standing, and Varūn has never once told any of us which one he actually meant." "The measurement is the thing, and the thing without the measurement is just rock." "A mountain only teaches you one kind of up." "All things break, lad. The work is in mending them stronger than before." (Borin's.) "A man who owns the ore is rich until the road closes." "Altherion makes nothing and sells everything." "The Church measures." "A court that cannot be evicted is not a court." "Remove any leg and the stool falls." "The best thing about equality is that everyone gets to complain." "A war against the Ring is a war against your own account-books." *New:* "There is ore in it and there is an account against it, and that is why we are still here." "When the Body asks, someone has to answer." "A mine that owes nobody ends up owing everyone." (Of Anguz.) "Lovely steel, read the bottom of the sale." (Everyone else's.)

**Body:** hands, where a forge-Marrowkin's Essence is said to route (Borin's; *derived* generally), and *kadu*, hand, is one of the roots they share with the goblins' Old Vaross; the beard, a record of time served; the rise, where the Soul-Runes accumulate so an elder's body is a reading of his life; the consonant, because agitation changes the grade and never the word order, and outsiders miss it every time; the stance, since a Petralon-anchored Marrowkin is the hardest thing in the Inner World to move, shoving him is shoving a wall; the forge-flush; the eyes in the dark, and the wince at noon (*new*); the jaw laid on a wall to listen (*new*); cold-sickness, a gray shaking after a long winter away from the Ring that no ordinary fire touches (*new*); the tender's shake, the fine persistent palsy that arrives at about eight years of forge-array service and does not leave, seen in a cup that will not sit still; the Measurewright's stillness; the Three-Tongued, visibly the most exhausted professionals in the Inner World.

**Senses:**
- *Smells:* sour barley beer in the lower levels; yeast and wet stave from a working cask; Cask-Oath Pitch: resin and tar; forge smoke, with sulfur off the coal; quench steam off hot iron; bone dust under a burin: dry, like a drilled tooth (*new*); salted and dried stores in a cellar; sulfur off a fresh ore face; tallow smoke in a deep gallery; singed beard; pastry and fat at the break; ink and wax in the arbitration rooms; wet red bracken on the terraces (*new*).
- *Sounds:* the hammer on the anvil; a burin cutting a rune; a name said in the strong grade: the doubled consonant before the word finishes; the same name in the weak grade, heard across a chamber; Mathematical Cadence: formal speech in fixed meter, felt through the floor (*new*); the under-note in a Marrowkin chest (*new*); rib-knock carrying down a gallery wall (*new*); a brew working in the cask; the shift horn; the copula "on" closing a line; a clerk reading a sale over a crowd at the Cutting Fair (*new*); the chain-lift's drive; a Lattice Tongue inscription recited, which an outsider hears as a proof rather than a sentence; seven accents at one market stall; the stamp coming down on a bar; the Tinker quarter's petition, read aloud again.
- *Colors:* ember red over everything that grows, rust and coal and hot copper, running up the ribs (*new*); old ivory where it has not climbed, gold in the grain (*new*); Breastbone's pale spires against that red (*new*); paired sparks of eyes in a dark gallery (*new*); glow-blood, dull orange and slow (*new*); forge-glow pulsing up the shafts into amber; copper-green fire in landing pans; soot-black leather and hot-red wool; red and copper banners at Kegshard; doubled strokes on the roll of the dead; cloudy amber of the beer; the bright line of a fresh seam; Altherion's timber over stone and its seven colors of paint; the stamp's bright bite in dull metal; the Church's flame-light on bare walls.
- *Textures:* old ivory, warm as a loaf (*new*); the grain of Marrowkin skin and the ridge of the rise under a palm (*new*); the ironwood handle: dense, smooth, near oily; the edges of a cut rune under a fingertip; the angle-set's hard edges; a beard-ring, cold on the knuckle; pitch, tacky and unwilling to cure outside its hold; emberwort moss, damp and warmer than the air (*new*); a hot handshake (*new*); the warmth of the walls under the hand; a scrip token, light and cheap; the round table's worn edge; the tremor of a tender's handshake.
- *Tastes:* sour barley beer; ollut, the aged brew: deeper, with an edge; bread that cost more than it should; redcaps, all iron (*new*); salted stores chewed on shift; the crib's crust, eaten round the corner a man has marked; hold water off the bone, warm and iron-tasting (*new*; old form: cold and mineral off the rock); salt tea; Rimland rye; sulfur and forge smoke at the back of the throat.

**Gone:**
- **The joined halls.** The tunnel-network that ran hold to hold before the Sundering of the Deep Halls severed five populations in five directions. Each had to rebuild Varūn's teaching from nothing in ground that shared nothing with the Body.
- **The lit halls.** What the Deepkin lost in the Depths Collapse, stranded in Void-scarred dark with no way back up, and what they held alone after the Temple Rift while the High Line withdrew. The Deepkin record calls it being left. The High Line record calls it a withdrawal.
- **The Reach's mountain.** The ground the Kuntur Reach came out of before the collapse put them on the surface among islands. They stopped grieving it and started reading the islands the same way, and the stopping is itself remembered.
- **Anguz.** Dead mines past the northern roads, once a power with the guilds pricing Aethersteel and Bloodsilver on the coil, now Dustquartz by the sack and whoever is down there this week.
- **The tender's hands.** The steady hands an array-tender had before his eighth year, which the Obligation pays for and cannot give back.
- **The small coin.** The copper penny of the smelter towns, melted into worked conductor wherever array-building ran hot. The lead token stands where it was.
- **The notch.** The sun's hour, given up first here, because it was here the equal hour was sent out by beacon. The old men of the rim still say a summer notch when they mean a long afternoon.
- **The unrecorded names.** The Archive Guild keeps a collection of statements too honest to file. The words are kept. The names of the tunnel-masters and tenders who said them were not.

**Folk beliefs:**
- **Touch a true rune for luck.** A doorway rune cut at the right angle works for whoever touches it, so every Marrowkin touches the lintel rune on the way in, and old lintels are worn smooth at hand height.
- **Pitch that will not cure is a broken oath.** When a batch fails, somebody in the hold has broken his word, and the clerks audit the name-roll before they check the barrels.
- **Keep both feet on worked stone.** A Marrowkin standing on worked stone cannot be hurt by a working, so an elder under threat sets his feet and waits.
- **The rise tells what comes next.** The Soul-Runes on an elder's arms can be read forward, and a fortune-teller at the Ring gates reads the next rune before it arrives.
- **The cinder bead refills for a worthy heir.** An heir who lives by the Tally will find his bead full again one morning.
- **The knockers.** Tapping in the bone on a quiet shift, when no one is knocking, means down tools and get out, and leave the end of your crib on the ledge. Nobody says who is doing the tapping.
- **A sold piece leans.** Hang old ivory on a thread and it swings toward the Ring. Guild assayers have found it on some pieces and nothing on others (*new*).
- **The heartbeat.** Nobody counts the forge-glow's pulse aloud in a shaft, and a child who does gets a clip on the ear (*new*).
- **The Hall sees more.** A reading taken in the Sanctified Hall goes deeper than a field reading, so families travel to Altherion for it.
- **Stand near the Mask.** A masked officer in the Church compound is the Mask, and no working can touch a man standing near him, so pilgrims crowd toward any mask they see.
- **The shake is catching.** A tender's hand is not shaken and his cup is not shared.
- **A false stamp burns the forger.** A counterfeit assay stamp will burn the hand that strikes it, which is why nobody tries.

**Literacy:** High in the rune. Every Marrowkin reads a name's grade in stone from across a chamber and an angle at a glance; entering the Tally in meter is clerks' work, and a clerk is a metrician before he is anything else. Concord script is read by those who deal outward, for the beacon line and the second column. High in Altherion, where the charter, the arbitration and the door-bills of three heralds' houses assume a reading public. Lattice Tongue is read by the Inscription Guild and hardly anyone else. Lower in the Rimlands and along the Corridor, where a man on the road reads a way-bill and a stamp and little more.

**Recurrence, two minimum per scene in the holds:** the Tally and "entered" or "unentered"; the name and its grade; the cask and "while the brew lives"; "kurme" or "kurlo," and never *I will*; the angle-set and "the angles are true"; the debt and the Lender; a person's cut-name; ember red on pale bone. On the rim: the forge-glow; the assay stamp and "just rock"; the round table with no head; the Guild hour; the tender's shake.

## Release words

What the culture calls a practitioner's First, Second and Third Release (R78-10).

A Marrowkin hears the grade of a name before the word, and a release sounds the same way: the Trait said in the strong grade, with the consonant doubled. The tongue has no future tense, so nobody says what a release *will* do; they say what it is. The Church compound and the Measurewrights keep the Accord's words.

| Release | Word | Literal meaning | Who says it |
|---|---|---|---|
| First | The strong grade | The Trait spoken at its full grade, as an oath-name is. | Anyone in the holds; Common mouths in the Ring copy it. |
| First (rim) | Struck | Struck as a stamp strikes a bar: the bright bite in dull metal. | Miners, smelter-town hands, the rim. |
| Second | Worked ground | The floor round him turned to worked stone, his, and under the folk belief no working hurts a man on worked stone. | Miners, Forge Guards, shift crews. |
| Second (rim) | Open seam | The old miners' wish made real: the ground round him opens to him like a vein under the pick. | Miners and the rim; "he's opened his seam." |
| Third | Tallut on | "The entry holds": the act entered by something older than any clerk, so nobody can unmake it. | Clerks and elders, in Mathematical Cadence; others say "entered." |
| Third (rim) | On the lode | Running on the lode itself: the Aether in the ground, the vein everything else is cut from. | Tunnel-masters and old tenders, half in awe. |
| Held past the limit | Nikkat ei on | "The angles are not true": the Tally's verdict turned over, for a release forced past its last tenth. | Rune-forgers, masters over apprentices, anyone at the wreck. |
| Released (adj.) | Doubled, or struck | Gone to the doubled consonant of the strong grade; struck on the rim. | "He is doubled." "He's struck." |

In a miner's mouth: "He is doubled. Feet on worked stone, lad, and stay behind me."

In a shift man's mouth: "He's struck. Down tools and get on the lift."

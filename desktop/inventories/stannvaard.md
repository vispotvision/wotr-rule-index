# THE STANDING INVENTORY: STANNVAARD, THE GUILD SEAT

A forge, a republic and a court in one crater, held together because the cost of separating them exceeds the cost of managing their contradictions. The Dawi Inventory holds in the crater cities, at the shift and the Tally. This is the layer the Guild Seat lays over it: Altherion on the rim, the Rimlands and the Corridor, the Ore Council's schedule, and the Grand Church that measures all of them.

**Food:** the Ring's table is bought in, since the Rimlands' timber and agriculture feed the crater and never quite enough of it. Underground the meal is the crib, a pastry case of meat and root carried down in a tin and eaten at the break on the shift, with a man's mark pressed into the corner of the crust so he gets his own back up. The Dawi sour barley beer below, Concord ale on the rim, and salt tea in the factors' offices where the Nalūn trade sits. Salt comes in as stamped bars and is money before it is seasoning. In Altherion seven kitchens share every street, and a stranger can be placed by what he buys at the market before he opens his mouth. Nobody in the crater cooks over an open flame if a forge-shoulder is nearer.

**Greeting:** "Open seam," answered "Open seam," the old miners' wish that the vein open under the pick, said at every shaft-lift and now at every door on the rim. In the crater the greeting is the Dawi one and the name in its grade. In Altherion the speaking floor belongs to whoever holds it, and a man who talks over another at the River Crown has not greeted the chamber. The Three-Tongued greet in whichever law the room is sitting under, and change it mid-sentence when the room does.

**Oaths:** "Under the stamp," for anything sworn in the way of trade, because the Assay Standard makes a counterfeit stamp a capital matter and everyone knows it. A Measurewright swears the Hexagonal Oath. A guild master is sworn to the extraction schedule, which is the kingdom's true sovereign instrument. The River Crown's delegates swear at the round table, all seated, no chair higher than another. **Insults:** "just rock," a claim or a man that has never been measured, since the thing without the measurement is just rock; "a Tinker's petition," any complaint that has been filed for two hundred years and will be filed again; "fire-muddler," one who confuses forge-heat with judgment-fire, which the kingdom keeps apart the way it keeps manufacture apart from murder; "Three-Tongued" said as an insult, a man who agrees with whoever is in the room; "counterfeit," the worst word, and it starts fights on the rim that end at tribunal.

**Time:** the Guild hour, which starts here, sent down the wire from Stannvaard to every gauge-house in four quarters, so that the Seat is the one place where nobody is late by a foreign clock; the shift, which decides when the Upper Ring works, eats and sleeps; the extraction schedule, read as the season; "eight years on the array," the time it takes the tender's shake to arrive; the Council Chair's ten-year term; the Weighing Festival, once a year, and the publication of the false-positive rate, read out with the gravity other kingdoms keep for coronations; the Nameless day, a holiday with an unusually high number of injuries; "the fastest three days," the time it took to try and sentence the last counterfeiter.

**The dead:** the Dawi are interred in the rock their clan worked, per their own Inventory. The rim buries by seven traditions in one ground, each in its own row. A man killed underground is brought up on the shaft-lift with his lamp lit and set at the head of the lift until the shift ends, and the forge-glow is the only other light. A tender who has the shake is kept for life under the Tender's Obligation, and his funeral is paid by the guild that shook him.

**Objects:** the assay stamp, and the stamp's mark on every bar, which counts for more than the bar; the round table in the River Crown chamber, no head, one chair per delegation, no chair larger than another, still in use; the shaft-lift and the chain-bridge; the miner's lamp, a squat brass frog-lamp with a hook; the forge-glow from Thane-Gorr, a dull orange pulse on the crater walls that travellers steer by and poets call a heartbeat; mine scrip, company tokens paid in place of copper in the smelter towns and redeemable at the company store; the broadsheet, since three houses in Stannvaard between them tell four quarters what happened; the Ember Magistrates' flame-lit equilibrium courts, which ride out to towns that will never see the Grand Church; the Church compound's low consecrated walls and flame-lit rooms, bare of ornament as a matter of doctrine.

**Exchange:** the extraction schedule decides how fast the world's metal moves, and the Seat decides it with a deliberateness the world finds infuriating. Nalūn's credit finances extraction and Stannvaard's metal backs Nalūn's credit. Supply contracts are sealed at Ironlink, never here. Mine scrip in the smelter towns, where there is not enough copper to pay in. Arbitration fees, pilgrim traffic and legation rent in Altherion, which makes nothing and sells everything. The Tender's Obligation, a lifetime's support owed for eight years of hands. The Essence Resource Sharing Act and the Trade Equality Act, cited on the rim the way scripture is cited elsewhere.

**Proverbs, fixed wording:** "A man who owns the ore is rich until the road closes." "Altherion makes nothing and sells everything." "The measurement is the thing, and the thing without the measurement is just rock." "The Church measures." "A court that cannot be evicted is not a court." "Remove any leg and the stool falls." "The best thing about equality is that everyone gets to complain." "The mountain cares about the ore, and the ore is still here, and that is why we are still here." "A war against Stannvaard is a war against your own balance sheet."

**Body:** the tender's shake, the fine persistent palsy that arrives at about eight years of forge-array service and does not leave, seen in a cup that will not sit still; the Dawi forge-flush; heat carried in the clothes up from the crater, and the dryness of skin that has lived in the Ring's warmer air; the Measurewright's stillness, trained until the gap between seeing and meaning is in the body; the Three-Tongued, visibly the most exhausted professionals in the Inner World; a Petralon-anchored Forge Guard, dense, patient and very hard to move.

**Senses:**
- *Smells:* sulphur and hot metal; the dry mineral smell of stone heated and cooled thousands of times; lamp oil on the shaft-lifts; pastry and fat at the break; ink and wax in the arbitration rooms; sealing-wax and cold stone in the Church compound.
- *Sounds:* the chain-lift's drive; a Lattice Tongue inscription recited, which an outsider hears as a proof rather than a sentence; the River Crown chamber, where the floor belongs to whoever holds it; seven accents at one market stall; the stamp coming down on a bar; the Tinker quarter's petition, read aloud again.
- *Colours:* forge-glow orange pulsing up the shafts into amber; the dense angular grey of impact-fractured stone; Altherion's timber-framing over stone and its snow-pitched roofs; the Church's flame-light on bare walls; the stamp's bright bite in dull metal.
- *Textures:* fault-stressed rock that should crack and holds; the warmth of the crater walls under the palm; a scrip token, light and cheap in the hand; the round table's worn edge; the tremor of a tender's handshake.
- *Tastes:* sour barley beer; salt tea; the crib's crust, eaten round the corner a man has marked; sulphur on the back of the tongue after a shift; Rimland rye.

**Gone:**
- **The tender's hands.** The steady hands an array-tender had before his eighth year at the forge-array, which the Tender's Obligation pays for and cannot give back.
- **The small coin.** The copper penny of the smelter towns, melted into conductor wherever array-building ran hot. The company token stands where it was, and there is still not enough copper to pay in.
- **The notch.** The sun's hour, which the Seat gave up first, because it was the Seat that sent the equal hour down the wire. The old men of the rim still say a summer notch when they mean a long afternoon.
- **The unrecorded names.** The Archive Guild keeps a collection of statements too honest to file. The words are kept. The names of the tunnel-masters and tenders who said them were not recorded, and nobody can now say who said the best of them.

**Folk beliefs:**
- **The knockers.** Tapping in the rock on a quiet shift is the knockers, and a man who hears them downs tools and gets out, and leaves the end of his crib on the ledge for them.
- **The Hall sees more.** A reading taken in the Sanctified Hall goes deeper than a field reading, so families travel to Altherion to have a reading taken there rather than from a Magistrate on circuit.
- **Stand near the Mask.** A masked officer in the Church compound is the Mask, and no working can touch a man standing near him, so pilgrims crowd toward any mask they see.
- **The shake is catching.** A tender's hand is not shaken and his cup is not shared, for fear of carrying the shake home.
- **A false stamp burns the forger.** A counterfeit assay stamp will burn the hand that strikes it, which is why nobody tries.

**Literacy:** High in Altherion, where the charter, the arbitration and three news houses assume a reading public, in Concord Common. The crater cities read the rune and the grade per the Dawi Inventory, and Lattice Tongue is read by the Inscription Guild and hardly anyone else. Lower in the Rimlands and along the Corridor, where a man on the road reads a way-bill and a stamp and little more.

**Recurrence, two minimum per Stannvaard scene:** the forge-glow pulsing up the shafts; the assay stamp and "just rock"; the round table with no head; the Guild hour, which starts here; the tender's shake.

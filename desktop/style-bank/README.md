# The Style Sample Bank

Worked examples of the Style Law (R70, `rules/doc-style-law-2026-10-03.yaml`), the last step of the law's work order (R70-138: law, test scene, then the bank). Before writing a POV of one of these cultures, read its file: the law says what to do, the bank shows what it sounds like when one culture's eye and cadence do it.

| File | Culture | Tilt, in one line |
|---|---|---|
| [kharven.md](kharven.md) | Kharven | Short declaratives stacked like a woodpile; the count does the work; a kingdom that cannot read hears the tape read aloud. |
| [moto.md](moto.md) | the Moto and the Japonic houses | Shadow, lacquer and the unsaid; honorifics in close narration; the war-tale declaration with its price. |
| [accord.md](accord.md) | the Accord | The Victorian city, money on every page, the Measurewright's clinical tape with the wit in the margin. |
| [mahuo.md](mahuo.md) | the Mahuo (Korean register) | Pansori tempo and the sijo's turn in the last clause; speech levels as events; a remnant house in Sum-gol. |
| [lineage-halls.md](lineage-halls.md) | the lineage halls (Chinese register) | Paired clauses, sayings owned by elders, title ladders, the hall's face and the bill that follows it. |

The Dawi example is the law's test scene, [*The Drawing-Off*](../../scenes/the_drawing_off.md), which Isaac read on 2026-10-04.

**Each file has five beats:** a fight or a working; court and talk; quiet and travel; a system moment (LitRPG on the page); a comic beat. Each passage carries a *Shows* line naming the R70 rows it demonstrates, and every passage passes `verify_scene` with 0 FAIL.

**What is canon here and what is not.** The people and events are illustrations. Nothing in the bank is canon until Isaac adopts it. Each file ends with "Texture this bank coins": season words, images, idioms, names and customs invented for the examples, all pending. They are the first drafts for each culture's new Inventory fields (R70-45, R70-59 to R70-65, R70-33), entered only on Isaac's word.

**Two calls the bank settled against the law's own text.** Tiers of Standing print by name alone ("Apprentice", never "Tier of Standing 2"), per R70-82; the test scene was corrected to match on 2026-10-04. Glosses stay out of narration (R51-15, R4-13): a borrowed word's meaning arrives through a mouth or the scene, and the book's glossary carries the rest (R70-25).

# Sound lexicon

A reference palette for sound words (onomatopoeia). Isaac's direction, 2026-10-29 and 2026-10-30 (onomatopoeia questionnaire): WOTR uses sound words freely and often, the way manga and anime do. A technique keeps a signature word; every other impact gets a fresh one; the language follows the POV's culture; length and case carry size.

This is a palette, not a rulebook. Invent freely, and coin a new word when nothing here fits the thing that happened. Reused words are logged in `used-devices.md`.

## The rules in one place

- **Four kinds.** Impact and object sounds; a working's own sound (matched to its Wellspring signature in `wellspring-signatures.md`); state and atmosphere (silence, dread, a crowd's unease); feeling and body (a heartbeat, a swallowed breath).
- **Set on the page.** Small sounds are italic and woven into the sentence. A heavy hit gets its own line, in capitals, often leading. Length and case scale: a tiny *tik*, a middling *thud*, a vast *GOOOOOOM*. State and feeling words are plain romanized, never italic (shiin, gogogogo, doki-doki).
- **Placement follows tempo.** Quick trades weave the sound mid-clause. A slow hit gives it a line after. The turning blow leads on its own line.
- **Language follows the POV.** A Moto or Japonic POV hears manga-style Japanese sound words. A Kharven or Common POV hears English ones. Narration uses whichever the POV hears.
- **In mouths, by character.** A showman or a clown may say a sound aloud; a cold killer never would.
- **Beyond fights.** City and market ambience, wilds and weather, emotional beats and comic beats all carry sound words.
- **Variety.** Never the same impact word twice in a scene unless it is a signature. Match what struck what, the material, the size, wet or dry, sharp or rolling.

## Impacts by material (English)

- **Flesh:** *thup*, *whump*, *smack*, *shlk* (wet), *splat*, *squelch*.
- **Bone:** *krak*, *snap*, *crick*, *crunch* (a crushed joint), a green-stick *crack*.
- **Steel on steel:** *tang*, *clang*, *shing*, *chnk*, *skreee* (dragging).
- **A blade passing through:** *shhk*, *shlik*, *skrrt* (scraping bone), *thwik*.
- **Wood:** *tok*, *thock*, *krrk* (splintering), *thunk*.
- **Stone and brick:** *dwoom*, *tchak*, *grrrk*, *whoomp*, a rolling *rrrumble*.
- **Ice:** *tink*, *krrrng*, *crrrk*, a long *kssshh* of shattering.
- **Glass:** *tsing*, *crash*, *tinkle*, *pkkt*.
- **Bent metal:** *krang*, *skreeee*, *dwonk*.
- **Water:** *plosh*, *glug*, *sssh*, *shhhoom* (a wave), *drip*, *plink*.
- **Fire:** *fwoomph*, *whuff*, *fssst*, *crackle*, *pop*.
- **Electric:** *zzzt*, *bzzzt*, *tchiiing*, a rising *hmmmmmm*.
- **Air and motion:** *fwip*, *whoosh*, *hiss*, *shff* (cloth), *thwip*.
- **Falls and weight:** *thump*, *dwomp*, *whomp*, *thud*.
- **Rope, wood, door:** *thrum*, *creak*, *clack*, *thunk*.

## Workings, by Family (match the Wellspring's own sound)

- **Caloria:** a furnace draw (*whooooom*), the hiss and tick of tempering (*tssss-tink*), a flint (*tsk*), falling ash (*fsh-fsh*), a vent (*ssshhh-bwub*), a blast (*DOOOOM*), a held rising note (*meeeeee*).
- **Fluxia:** a bubble (*bloop*), a rush through a narrowing (*shhhhoom*), a deep groan (*glooom*), many voices through water.
- **Fulguria:** a spark gap (*zzzt*), a glass rim (*tcheeee*), a bell-note of metal (*tonnnng*), a scan-tick (*tik-tik-tik*), a slow throb (*hum... hum...*).
- **Limina:** a hush (*fffff*), a drain, a reversed echo, the absence of sound itself. Often the right Limina sound word is silence: *shiin*.
- **Materia:** grinding (*grrrind*), a single tone (*tonnng*), a crystal ticking as it grows (*tik-tik*), a settle (*kssh*).
- **Spatium:** a soft rotating hum (*vvvmm*), two bells in sympathy (*tonn... tonn*), a held breath.
- **Vectoria:** a ratchet (*kri-kri-kri*), a groan under load (*groaaan*), a hammer ring (*tonk*), the tick of cracking (*tik... tik*).
- **Vitalia:** a wet pulse (*thub-thub*), growing wood (*kreeee*), a soft crackle of cells (*pshh*).

## State and atmosphere (Japanese, for a Moto or Japonic POV)

- *shiin*: dead silence.
- *gogogogo*: a looming pressure, a rising rumble of presence.
- *zawa zawa*: a crowd's unease, a rustle of whispers.
- *don*: a heavy arrival.
- *ba*: a sudden move.
- *zuzuzu*: a rumble that builds.
- *doki-doki*: a heartbeat.
- *gokuri*: a swallow.
- *pika*: a flash.
- *kira-kira*: glitter.
- *baki*: a hard snap.
- *gaan*: shock.
- *zuun*: a heavy gloom.

For a Kharven or Common POV, the same states come as English: a hush, a hum, a held silence, the thud of a heart, the groan of timber.

## Comic

*plink*, *bonk*, *thwip*, *fwump*, *plop*. Used by the characters who play that way.

## Anti-repeat

Before a fight or set piece, check `used-devices.md`. Sounds already spent there are retired as stock. Every fight coins at least a few new words for new things. If two hits in a scene would take the same word, change the material, the size, the wet or dry, or the tempo of the second.

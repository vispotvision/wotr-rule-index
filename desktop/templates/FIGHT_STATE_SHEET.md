# Fight state sheet and zoom map

Filled before a fight or set piece is drafted, kept in the author notes, updated at the end of each movement. It is the model's memory of the room, because a model drifts: fighters float, terrain vanishes, a disarmed man swings again. Nothing here is printed under the scene.

## 1. Ground

- Place, light, floor and footing (dry, wet, frost, rubble), what is fixed (bar, wall, stair, pillar, pipe), what can break or burn or be thrown.
- Exits, crowd and witnesses, who is watching from where.
- Planted clue: what the reader will have been shown early that pays off at the turn (name it, and the beat where it is planted).

## 2. Fighters (one row each, rewritten at every movement)

| Fighter | Position | Distance to each other | Weapon in hand | Aura colour and state | Reserve (full / half / quarter / tenth) | Wounds that carry | Knows about the other | Lying about |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Rules for the sheet: a wound listed here is felt in every later exchange (a dead leg is not planted on; a broken hand cannot grip); a weapon listed as dropped stays dropped until a beat picks it up; distance changes only by a stated step, lunge, throw or push.

## 3. The ladder

Probe, reveal, counter, escalate, price. One line each: what happens, what the reader learns, what it costs. Name the counter and where it was planted. Name the held-back card.

## 4. Zoom and volume map (per movement)

Full blow coverage stays; the zoom changes. For each movement set the zoom and the volume so the whole fight does not run at one pitch.

| Movement | Volume (1 quiet to 10 peak) | Zoom: slowed (every part of the Blow Card, senses stretched) / standard (the card in full, at pace) / quick (card tightened to set, contact, tissue, position) | Thought after blows: heavy / light / none | Talk |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Rules: no more than one movement at peak volume; the slowed zoom is spent on the turn, the vow, the perfect hit and the kill; quick zoom carries trades; a reaction or thought does not follow every blow; the quietest beat sits just before the peak.

## 5. Image ledger

List the signature image of this fight (one new thing the reader has not seen) and confirm none of the stock images in `desktop/style-bank/used-devices.md` is reused.

## 6. Recap (written after the draft)

Final positions, wounds, weapons, reserve. Compare with section 2. Any mismatch is fixed in the prose.

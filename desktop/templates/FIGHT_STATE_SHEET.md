# Fight state sheet and zoom map

Filled before a fight or set piece is drafted, kept in the author notes, updated at the end of each movement. It is the model's memory of the room, because a model drifts: fighters float, terrain vanishes, a disarmed man swings again. Nothing here is printed under the scene; the windows in section 4c go on the page inside it.

## 1. Ground

- Place, light, floor and footing (dry, wet, frost, rubble, ice, boards), and what each footing does under a heavy load (stone holds, ice flexes, boards give), checked against the climax, what is fixed (bar, wall, stair, pillar, pipe), what can break or burn or be thrown.
- Exits, crowd and witnesses, who is watching from where.
- Planted clue: what the reader will have been shown early that pays off at the turn (name it, and the beat where it is planted).

## 2. Fighters (one row each, rewritten at every movement)

| Fighter | Position and facing | Standing on | Distance to each other | Weapon in hand | Aura color and state | Reserve (percentage, and the tell rung: full / half / quarter / tenth) | Wounds that carry | Knows about the other | Lying about |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Rules for the sheet: a wound listed here is felt in every later exchange (a dead leg is not planted on; a broken hand cannot grip); a weapon listed as dropped stays dropped until a beat picks it up; distance changes only by a stated step, lunge, throw or push; the reserve percentage and the body tell agree.

## 3. The ladder

Probe, reveal, counter, escalate, price. One line each: what happens, what the reader learns, what it costs. Name the counter and where it was planted. Name the held-back card.

## 4. Zoom and volume map (per movement)

Full blow coverage stays; the zoom changes. For each movement set the zoom and the volume so the whole fight does not run at one pitch.

| Movement | Volume (1 quiet to 10 peak) | Zoom: slowed (every part of the Blow Card, senses stretched) / standard (the card in full, at pace) / quick (every Blow Card part kept, written tighter; none dropped) | Thought after blows: heavy / light / none | Talk |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Rules: no more than one movement at peak volume; the slowed zoom is spent on the turn, the vow, the perfect hit and the kill; quick zoom carries trades; thought follows each fighter's card, a quick read on every blow and extended reasoning only for an analyst (R81-6); the quietest beat sits just before the peak.

## 4b. Workings (one row each, Isaac 2026-11-03)

| Working | Charge (body, what can be seen coming) | Pull (Wellspring, kind, η, waste heat) | Aura and the room (air, light, ground) | Senses (sound word, smell, touch) | Mechanism (the physics and the metaphysics, step by step) | Counter in play, and what it does physically |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Every working used in the fight gets a row before drafting; two workings of one fighter must differ in name, mechanism and signature, and the climax may use only a rule planted earlier in the scene.

## 4c. Windows (R84-1, R84-2, R84-17)

A window is the POV's own soul reporting to its owner, so the fight's windows belong to the fighter whose head the section sits in. Plan them per movement, so reserve, damage and skill gains appear often and in detail, each after the blow it reports.

| Beat | Window on the page (reserve, a wound's status line, skill or Trait gain, a vow's terms, a crew's bars, Pressure, Release or Domain and its timer bar) | Reserve after | Notes only (EU, AU/s, η) |
|---|---|---|---|

Rules: the page shows Grades, percentages, bars and levels, and EU, AU/s and η stay in the last column; each line stands alone in square brackets (format in `wotr-stat-line`); a Release or Domain fills the window with its name and what changed, its Grades inside the two-Grade cap; Pressure shows in the room first and the window reports it after; a wound's line says what it stops him doing and stays until treated (R84-29); a technique shows no level; for his PC a gain is offered as a window line he keeps or changes (R84-23).

# Artifact brief

Format: `# <Name> · <Epithet or Type>`, migration note, then:
`## What it is` (type, appearance, provenance from the card, converted eras/names), `## Physical account` (mass, length, point of balance, the armour tier it beats and fails against — R13-9-ITEM_GUIDE_WEAPON_ENTRY; proof-marks if armour; `pending Isaac` where the card gives nothing), `## Operation line` (every working the artefact performs, as a full Design Chain line: what it does to what quantity under what law — R17-5-ARTEFACT_SCOPE), `## Codex line` (Wellspring, Family, Physics Domain, Category, glyphs only if attested in the Master Glyph Index — check with `wiki`), `## Bond and cost` (soulbound? what it costs the bearer), `## Counterplay`, `## Bearers` (from the card).

## Exemplar (artifact entries inside a converted card)



**Vampiric Rapier** · *Relic / Weapon / Soulbound Blood Channel.* **Midnight-black blade veined with deep crimson Essence, pulsing faintly with each heartbeat it claims.** Glyphs *Mu (Ending), Oru (Power).* **Siphons life Essence with each strike** · extends into coagulated Essence-blood tendrils for mid-range thrusts · **cuts through Essence barriers of equal or lower Temperance Stage.**
**Bloodforged Armor** · *Soulbound Plate / Living Bloodsteel.* Layered black-red plate, **breathing with its wearer.** Glyph *Oru.* **Repairs itself using stored blood reserves** · increases density, speed and shaping precision of blood constructs.
**Cloak of Shadows** · *Umbral Relic / Concealment Mantle.* **A long tattered cloak that drinks in light**, edges dissolving into shadow wisps. Glyph *Vy (Veil).* **Invisibility in dim or no light** · projects a shadow field that blinds and confuses.

## Rules that bind items

R13-9-ITEM_GUIDE_WEAPON_ENTRY  [Pack Thirteen §9]  live
  Every weapon entry in the Item and Equipment Writing Guide carries mass, length, point of balance, and the armour tier it beats and fails against.

R11-2-APPARATUS_INHERITS_PHYSICS  [Pack Eleven §2]  live
  A machine is a conduit retuned to a current (phase-locking, not restricted to souls); every apparatus inherits its Family's favoured condition, suppressive condition, and characteristic pathology off the Environmental Coupling table.

R11-3-AMMO_TIERS  [Pack Eleven §3]  live
  Common shot (cheap, kills men, annoys practitioners), proofed shot (Materia-coupled, Guild-stamped, defeats proofed plate but not a Domain), and the named round (one-shot assassination ordnance, Wellspring-coupled and paid for in advance).

R11-3-ARMOUR_STOPS_SHRINKING  [Pack Eleven §3]  live
  Shot is chemically cheap and does not care about a soul; a working is not shot, so mass and material still buy a fraction of a second against a Wellspring-carried strike, which is why armour retreats to the cuirass-and-helm silhouette and stops there.

R11-3-BALLISTICS_RULING  [Pack Eleven §3]  live
  Powder weapons are slow, loud and honest; a practitioner above roughly the middle Stages is not reliably killed by common shot, which is why the enchanted round exists at the cost it does.

R11-3-DUAL_ANSWER_SILHOUETTE  [Pack Eleven §3]  live
  The silhouette is coat, breast, helm, sword and firearm; every armed man carries at least two answers because there are at least two questions, which is why the Combat Craft Guide survives intact — Liechtenauer and Fiore still govern inside twenty feet.

R11-3-FIREARM_PROSE_LAW  [Pack Eleven §3]  live
  The gun is furniture, not spectacle; no sentence explains why a proofed round defeats proofed plate — the physical account of the hole stays, the causal account is cut.

R11-3-GUNPOWDER_AND_ARMOUR_CONFIRMED  [Pack Eleven §3]  live
  Gunpowder exists, magical weapons exist, and armour is not dead.

R11-3-PROOF_MARKS_CANON  [Pack Eleven §3]  live
  A dented cuirass is certified rather than damaged; soldiers read a proof-dent the way Kharven reads a forearm grip, and a shallow proof-dent signals a light charge and what that means about the man wearing it.

R10-3-CRAFT_DURABILITY_TABLE  [Pack Ten PART THREE]  live
  Spellcraft holds only while attention holds; Runecraft is fixed and degrades like any inscription; Draftcraft is bottled, cruder, and buyer-triggerable; Chantcraft requires the sustaining choir and is pending Chantcraft's own ruling.

R10-3-PROJECTED_FORM_LICENSE  [Pack Ten PART THREE]  live
  Ability Effect fields may render a working as an externalised shape (animal, elemental figure, guardian silhouette) instead of only as discharge, pressure, or weather.

R3-7-EXPENDITURE_VS_PLATE  [Pack Three Amendment Seven]  live
  Plate is a cost problem rather than a defence problem to an Expenditure fighter, who is content to let armour do its job for as long as the wearer can afford to keep wearing it.

R2-6-REPLENISHMENT_RULE  [Pack Two Amendment Six.3]  live
  Essence cannot be shipped (it leaks in transit, needs an untransportable instrument to assay, dies with its owner); no consumable, technique or item may restore a practitioner's magazine.

R1-1-PERCUSSION_VS_PLATE_AND_TELL  [Pack One Amendment One]  live
  Percussion does not seek to penetrate plate, it uses it — armour transmits force inward, so the target is the joint, base and balance, making the plate expensive to wear. The tell is that the fighter tracks breathing and footing rather than the weapon.

R22-4-KUROSETSU_SCABBARD_SURVIVES  [Moto Reversion Ledger IV]  live
  Kurosetsu keeps the belt scabbard; that was an equipment ruling, not a naming one, and it survives the reversion.

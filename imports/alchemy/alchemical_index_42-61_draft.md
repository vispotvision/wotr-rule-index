## Notes

### How Class was assigned

- **The definitions.** Provenance Class is defined in the Standing Index (`wiki/Materials, Alchemy & Trade/The Standing Index.md:22-31`): I Vein Draw, II Field Substrate, III Vital Draw, IV Crystal Draw, V Sovereign Draw, VI Archonic Residue. The class names where the Essence came from. Nothing in the mirror says how a formula with several components is classed. R18-5 names a *Provenance Doctrine* as required reading beside the Standing Index. **No page by that name is in `wiki/` or in `~/wotr-vault/true-canon`,** so these grounds rest on the Standing Index and on the page's own practice.

- **The page's practice, read from its forty-one rows.** Three rules fit it. **(a)** The class is the class of the component the mechanism makes do the work. Carriers, binders, seals and tools do not. Cinder-Salt Glyph Wash is Class I beside ember-bark shavings, and Forge-Tempered Ink is Class I beside binding silk. Veilstone Tincture is Class II beside echo-moth wing ash. **(b)** Any Crystal Draw or higher component sets the class whatever its role, down to a trace. Anamnesis Draught is Class V on a trace of Titan-blood, and Dominion Brand Oil is Class IV on its blood. **(c)** Where (b) turns on a sourcing fact the row does not give, the page writes a conditional. Granite-Bone reads "Class III, IV on a donor who bore a Crystal."

- **Materials named for a Wellspring.** Where a component carries a Wellspring's name and no living source (Ignivale spark-oil, Phreatis tide-ichor, Catharsis excision-salt), it is read as matter taken at a Wellspring site. The page grades that as Class II Field Substrate: Skyfire Pulse Vial's plasma, Stillwater Draught's water, Crevice-Glass Shard's slabs. **A plant or animal source is Class III.** Ripper Talon and Field Draw in Hide, Horn and Bone say so.

- **Where this reading is weakest.** Under rule (a) the class follows the role the mechanism gives each component. The Codex assigns no roles. A different mechanism could name a different working part. Where the choice would change the class, the row's note below says so and gives the class the other reading would produce.

### Conflicts found

Recorded here, not yet in `CONFLICTS.md`, and written so each can be pasted there as a row.

1. **47 and 60 name Crymorath.** The Codex rows give Crymorath, Family Fulguria. R27-1 (`rules/doc-cymorath-portfolio-ruling.yaml`, live) says "Codex lines ... that name a cold law write Vohrin · Caloria · Thermodynamics". The page already did this for Stasisline Needles and Stillwater Draught, whose Codex rows also read Crymorath and Fulguria. The page's last commit is the R27-1 commit. **Both rows are drafted Vohrin · Caloria under that ruling.** The Codex sheet itself still carries Crymorath on these rows, on [FrTh] and [ObsG], and in its Lists sheet. The Titan Tie-In column reads None on both, so no Titan is written after the glyphs.

2. **44's Family.** The Codex gives Materia. The Families register (`wiki/The Magic System/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings.md`) puts Nihiloth in Limina and Fractura in Vectoria. Every other Nihiloth row on the page is Limina. [Grv]'s own Glyph Index row attests only Basilithe and Terranova, Family Materia, so the glyph is unattested on either of the row's Wellsprings. **Drafted as the Codex gives it.** Neither reading is chosen here.

3. **52's Family.** The Codex gives Cross-Family. The Families register puts Sublimare and Fractura both in Vectoria. [Prc]'s Glyph Index row does list Sublimare as Cross-Family, so the sheet agrees with itself and disagrees with the register. Drafted as the Codex gives it.

4. **Glyph meanings that pull against the rows.** [Thn] Thunder reads "avoids friendlies" and [Clp] Collapse reads "to spare allies". The Thundercrack's row Notes say it was banned in close formation after an incident. [Krn] Crown reads "Shields a sovereign soul from external control", on a compulsion brand. [Ig] Ignite reads "Spark occurs only when a chosen outcome opens", against 42's Risk "ignites blind to intent". [Slg] Slag reads "re-fuses a seam into a stronger dovetail", against 49's "structurally wrong" weld. [Al] Edge and [Ath] Command have blank attested-Wellspring and attested-Family cells, so 45, 51 and 55 cannot be checked against them.

5. **The page's own Class column.** Skyfire Pulse Vial is Class II, and its Codex ingredients include dragon-scale ash. The Standing Index grades anything "Off a dragon" Class V. Deep Grid Mortar is Class II on oath-bone ash, where Granite-Bone grades oath-keeper bone-ash "IV on a donor who bore a Crystal". Echo-Bloom (living hair, II) and Somnum Flow (living hair, III) disagree on whether hair counts. The page's intro says "Four entries below are Class IV or above". Seven rows carry IV, V or VI outright (Dreamstone Shard Flask, Dominion Brand Oil, Corpus Marrow Salts, Verdance Reclamation Draught, Anamnesis Draught, Fossil Ring Dust, Null-Essence Draught), and two more carry a conditional IV.

6. **Name collision.** "Ashfang" is already Draven Kael Vorrick's epithet and the name of his technique, Ashfang Mark Invocation. It is not a contradiction. A reader may assume a link that the Codex row does not make.

7. **Cinerion as a people.** The Codex Notes on 42 and 57 speak of "Cinerion duelists" and "Cinerion ambush-hunters". Cinerion is a Caloria Wellspring in the register. The Notes column does not reach the page, so no row carries it.

8. **Unattested ingredients.** Apart from oath-iron and elderwood, none of the twenty rows' ingredients appears anywhere in `wiki/`. That is expected for new rows. It means no ingredient's provenance could be checked against a register entry.

### Format notes

- No row from 42 to 61 has a Titan Tie-In, so no formula cell carries one after a ·.

- The Formula cell uses the T2 to T4 pattern, type in italics and then the glyph codes, on every tier. The page's T5 to T7 rows drop the type, which the brief asks for, so it stays in on every new row.

- Types are sentence-cased from the Codex, as the page does. "Battlefield Compress" is written "Battlefield", as the page writes Tide-Silt Compress.

- Every figure in the rows (4m, one hour, one minute, twice, three heartbeats, a half-breath, a ten-count, one-to-one, the first minute, three quenches, one full night) is the Codex row's own.

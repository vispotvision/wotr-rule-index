# Lurien's Hearthvine · The Green Cantor's Bow

> **Migration note.** The Holy See of Lurien, the card's origin institution, is dead canon: Volume IV and V corrections retcon it to the Holy Sea of Alabaster, and this entry follows that ruling throughout. "The Withering Age" is corrected to the Withering Era per the Errata to the Received Registers. The card's "Nature / Harmonia" Essence Alignment is converted onto current terms: Family Vitalia, Physics Domain Biochemistry, primary Wellspring Verdantia, with Category Harmonia kept as the card gives it (confirmed a live Category). All three named glyphs, `Ur` (Balance), `Wy` (Motion), `Ma` (Memory), are confirmed Root forms in the Master Glyph Index and needed no correction. The card's invented effect-name "Harmonic Bleed" is not an attested WOTR term; it is dropped here in favour of plain mechanism, per the sheet's own rule that mechanism is stated functionally rather than dressed in invented jargon. The Green Cantor, the Sanctuary Bough, the Sanctuary of Mosslight, and the Wardens of the Green March do not appear anywhere in the wiki mirror consulted; all four are kept, flagged unattested, because the bow's origin and current holding have no substitute given for them. Malphas, the Guild Accord, Caedor, Beastkin, the Empire of Bluvest, and the Inclusion Saga are all confirmed current canon and needed no conversion beyond the era correction above. Physical figures (mass, draw length, draw weight) are plausible estimates for a war bow of this type; the card gives none.

## What it is

A Relic Glyph Tool, capable of standing as a minor Domain Anchor when planted in living ground. A living bow grown from twined heartwood and creeping vine, bark smooth where the palms rest and rough and knotted along the limbs. Fresh leaves sprout year round, veining with faint Aether-light when drawn; the string is a silvery gut-fibre that hums like a beesong, and dew beads on it at dawn even indoors. It was sung into shape at the Holy Sea of Alabaster during the Withering Era by a figure the card calls the first Green Cantor, using a graft from the Sanctuary Bough and roots steeped in Wellspring sap. No cut was ever made in its making, only coaxed growth guided by Parunic song.

## Physical account

A war bow: plausible mass 0.9 kg (living wood runs lighter than seasoned yew), draw length 71 cm, draw weight an estimated 70 lb, point of balance at the grip, just below the arrow rest, where the riser is densest. On armour: it beats padded and mail cleanly, a broadhead through cloth or rings needs nothing special from the bow. A Ma-guided shot finds the gap in brigandine the way any well-placed arrow does. It turns on proofed plate the way any arrow turns, though a Seedshaft that lands in a joint, a visor slit, or a grapple point still roots and binds the man wearing the plate regardless.

## Operation line

**Seedshaft.** Trigger: the shaft strikes and holds fast. Function: forces the vine wrapped around the arrowhead through a season's growth in the few seconds after impact, the same indeterminate meristematic growth the Alchemical Index records for Verdant Overgrowth Seeds, run from dormant to rooted almost at once. Mechanism: Verdantia current banked in the living wood discharges into the vine on contact; `Ur` keeps the discharge even so the roots seek the nearest solid purchase rather than tearing themselves apart. Numerical effect: root-lock inside two seconds, holding to roughly the load a grown bramble thicket bears, enough to anchor a door or a limb, not enough to hold a straining warhorse. Target response: bound limbs, jammed hinges, seized siege gear. Limitation: needs living, unsalted ground or flesh within the vine's reach; against bare stone or metal it wraps without rooting. Weakness: fire, or a clean cut through the vine before it thickens, frees the target. Cost: draws down the bow's own stored Essence, not the archer's. Counterplay: cut early, burn it, or stand on stone.

**Green Arc.** Trigger: loosed at a target behind partial cover. Function: the `Ma`-etched leaf-runes along the riser read the wood's own memory of every branch and root path it has grown near, and bias the fletching's spin toward the nearest remembered curve. Mechanism: a Memory-authorized correction layered onto ordinary flight, not true guidance; the arrow still obeys gravity and drag, it only selects the curve out of what the wood recalls. Numerical effect: a bend of a few degrees per remembered obstacle, enough to clear a shoulder-width barrier at ordinary bow ranges. Target response: an arrow arriving from an angle the target did not track. Limitation: only bends around growth the wood has memory of; open ground and anything built after the graft was cut offer it nothing. Weakness: a target who moves the cover defeats the memory instantly. Cost: negligible, drawn from the same reserve as any shot. Counterplay: fight on cleared, unvegetated ground.

**Hearthcall.** Trigger: bow planted tip down, string looped to earth. Function: seeds a Verdantia current into the ground under `Ur`'s balance keeping until it stabilizes into a standing Domain seed the size of a market stall, the "Lesser Grove Domain" of the card. Mechanism: the bow's own minor Domain Anchor function, per its Type line; the artifact supplies and holds the seed the way a Domain Anchor holds any Domain against collapse, rather than the wielder authoring it personally. Numerical effect: damp loam and undergrowth spreading to about six paces; footing inside slows an unhurried walk to roughly half pace, and shallow wounds close at the rate the Vitality Regeneration literature gives Verdantia generally. Target response: allies inside heal minor wounds and pay a footing cost, enemies inside pay the same footing cost with no healing. Limitation: holds only as long as the bow stays planted and strung to earth; lift it and the seed collapses within a few breaths. Weakness: salted or scorched ground refuses the seed outright. Cost: draws the bow's full reserve for the duration. Counterplay: force the archer to move, or salt the ground first.

**Germinal Shot.** Trigger: three full breaths held on the draw. Function: forces the arrowhead's growth sideways instead of forward, splitting one growing tip into several before impact. Mechanism: the same Verdantia growth acceleration as Seedshaft, run through a deliberately broken authorization so the plant branches outward instead of down a single root. Numerical effect: one shaft becomes three to five smaller thorn barbs at nock-to-target range, each carrying a fraction of the original shot's force. Target response: a single loosed arrow arrives as a spread; no barb hits as hard as the whole shaft would have. Limitation: the charge is audible and visible, the leaves flare bright before release, and cannot be rushed without the split failing to finish. Weakness: any interruption during the charge collapses it back to a single ordinary arrow. Cost: roughly three ordinary shots' worth of the bow's reserve. Counterplay: close the distance during the charge; it is the loudest, slowest thing the bow does.

## Codex line

Wellspring: **Verdantia**, primary (Vitalia, Biochemistry). Category: **Harmonia**, per the card's own alignment, confirmed a live Category. Glyphs: `Ur` (Balance), `Wy` (Motion), `Ma` (Memory), all three confirmed Root forms in the Master Glyph Index; `Ur`'s attested spread already runs to nine Wellsprings across six Families, so its presence on a Vitalia item is unremarkable.

## Bond and cost

Not soulbound the way a practitioner's own Crystal is; it answers hands generally but draws harder, and refuses iron-tipped arrows outright, from anyone whose intent runs against growth, per the card. Overdrawing past what its own Balance glyph can level pushes the excess current into uncontrolled growth around the grip, which the bearer has to physically fight down before it climbs the forearm. A month fed on hatred alone blackens the leaves and puts the bow into dormancy until carried back to living ground.

## Counterplay

Iron and open, unvegetated, salted ground both starve it outright. Force the wielder off growing ground, interrupt the three-breath charge on Germinal Shot, or outrange it with something the vine cannot reach; the bow does nothing at all without a nocked shaft already in flight.

## Bearers

Carried by the Wardens of the Green March during Malphas's first probes into the region (unattested elsewhere, kept because the card's history has no substitute for it). Later gifted to a Guild Accord scout who held the Caedor ford by raising a forest overnight, consistent with the Hearthcall mechanism above. Presently kept at the Sanctuary of Mosslight (unattested location, kept for the same reason), ceremonially loaned to Guild Accord envoys when parleying in wild realms. First appearance: the Inclusion Saga, *The Blooming Silence*, Ch. 7, "Thorns in the March."

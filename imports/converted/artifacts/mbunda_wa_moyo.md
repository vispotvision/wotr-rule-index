# Mbunda wa Moyo · Drum of the Living Heart

> **Migration note.** "Harmonic-Aether / Nature-Aether" is retired pre-Fracture vocabulary; the drum is reassigned to **Benediction** (primary) and **Verdantia** (secondary), both attested Vitalia harmonizations on Chimwala N'Doro's current card and the right mechanism for a covenant-resonance instrument. **Ur (Balance)** is unchanged and attested. The card's other two glyphs are not attested as written: **Sa** exists in the Master Glyph Index but means Silence or Illumination, never "Rhythm," and **Vaa** does not exist at all. Rather than keep a glyph under a meaning current canon does not give it (a near-miss the index treats as a failure, not a warning), the drum now carries **Ye (Unity)** — the Lupine Pack-Soul's attested synchronization phoneme, and the closest attested match for "align many practitioners' rhythm" — in place of Sa, and **Vor (Return)**, attested for cycling and circulating Essence, in place of Vaa. Both swaps are noted rather than silent. "The Heartwood Grove," "the Lurien elders" and "Kaetra" needed attention: Kaetra is thinly but genuinely attested as a polity in current canon and is kept as-is; "the Lurien elders" is converted to "the Grove elders" since Lurien itself is dead canon (see the Staff of the Verdant Pulse migration note for the same finding); the Heartwood Grove is not independently attested but does not contradict anything and is kept, flagged. The Siege of the Jade Bridge and the Battle of the Thorned Crossing are both unattested in current canon; kept because the drum's history cannot be stated without a battle, and flagged. Mass and dimensions are not given by the card; conservative figures for a large ceremonial hand drum are chosen below.

## What it is

A Relic-grade Domain Anchor and ritual tool: a sacred hand drum of hollowed ironwood, its head the cured hide of a Wellspring-born antelope, painted in ochre and green spirals that glow faintly when struck. Palm-fiber cord wraps the rim, which is burned with Parun glyphs carrying Chimwala's own Essence signature. Crafted in the Heartwood Grove after Chimwala's first Temperance ascent into Stage IV, Flourishing, using materials exchanged ceremonially between the Grove elders and the drummers of Kaetra.

## Physical account

Mass ~2.2 kg (hollowed ironwood body plus stretched hide; not stated on the card, conservative estimate). Diameter ~35 cm, depth ~18 cm, played standing or seated, one-handed or with a beater. Not a weapon or armour — the armour-tier field does not apply.

## Operation line

- **Essence Synchronization.** Trigger: a steady, uninterrupted beat. Mechanism: the **Ye** unity-glyph locks every listener within hearing range onto a shared rhythm the way the Lupine Pack-Soul locks a formation's gait, letting Chimwala's Benediction current run through several practitioners' Shells at once rather than one at a time. Numerical effect: amplifies spell cohesion and physical stamina for every ally within earshot (roughly 40 m in open ground) for as long as the beat holds. Cost: ~5% of reserve per minute of sustained play.
- **Domain Battle-Rhythm.** Trigger: played inside an active Domain. Mechanism: the **Vor** glyph cycles the Verdantia current through the terrain in time with the beat rather than letting it run at its own pace, so roots, vines and undergrowth move on the drum's timing instead of their own. Numerical effect: terrain growth inside the Domain moves in timed pulses matched to the beat, usable to open lanes, close them, or stagger an advance. Cost: ~8% of reserve, continuous.
- **Harmonic Dissonance.** Trigger: struck at a hostile or corrupted animal presence. Mechanism: the **Ur** balance-glyph forces the local Wellspring current back toward equilibrium, which corrupted or destabilized wildlife reads as an unbearable dissonance rather than a threat to fight. Numerical effect: calms ordinary hostile fauna outright; drives off corrupted wildlife rather than harming it. Cost: ~4% of reserve per use.

## Codex line

**Benediction** (Vitalia · Biochemistry) primary — the covenant-resonance that lets the beat carry to many Shells at once. **Verdantia** (Vitalia · Biochemistry) secondary — the terrain growth the Domain rhythm moves. Category: **Harmonia** primary (the synchronized cohesion effect), **Domain Weaving** secondary (the terrain-rhythm effect). Glyphs: **Ur** (Balance) — attested unchanged; **Ye** (Unity) and **Vor** (Return) in place of the card's unattested "Sa (Rhythm)" and "Vaa (Flow)."

## Bond and cost

Soulbound to Chimwala N'Doro; bondable through a drum-beat ritual performed under a moonlit grove, per the card, which is kept as an open ritual specification rather than invented in full. Requires a steady, uninterrupted rhythm to sustain any of its three effects; a silence between beats longer than three seconds breaks the Domain-rhythm effect entirely and forces Chimwala to restart the synchronization from nothing.

## Counterplay

Every effect depends on the beat continuing — killing the sound (distance beyond ~40 m, a Silence-adjacent field, or interrupting Chimwala's hands) ends the Synchronization and the Domain-rhythm instantly, and a gap over three seconds forces a full restart on the Domain effect specifically. The Harmonic Dissonance working drives off rather than harms corrupted wildlife, so it buys space, not a kill. None of the three effects reach a target outside hearing range.

## Bearers

Chimwala N'Doro, brought only to war councils, large-scale rituals, and Wellspring stabilization efforts; first recorded use was during the Siege of the Jade Bridge, where it unified five druid warbands' Essence into a single assault rhythm. No other bearer is recorded.

# Nkanda wa Mizimu · Necklace of the Spirit Chorus

## What it is

A Relic-grade, soulbound glyph tool: a wide ceremonial torque of braided elephant hair, gold leaf and carved jadeite discs, each disc cut with a Parun glyph deep enough to show a thread of glowing Essence at the cut. Small glass beads holding preserved spirit-lights hang from it, their light shifting with unheard whispers. Forged at the Sanctum of the Thousand Voices beneath the Innerworld Grove, using ancestral ivory given by the tribe's honored dead, through a seven-night Spirit Communion that bound the voices of Chimwala's predecessors into the metal and stone.

## Physical account

Mass ~0.9 kg (torque plus discs and beads; not stated on the card, conservative estimate for the described materials). Worn, not wielded — point of balance and armour-tier fields do not apply; it carries no offensive or defensive load-bearing function. Fits a standard adult neck at roughly 40 cm internal diameter.

## Operation line

- **Ancestral Communion.** Trigger: worn and consciously opened by Chimwala. Mechanism: the **Vo** glyph on each disc is a spoken fate-tag binding an oath to its listener, here binding the wearer's hearing to the ancestral voices the torque's forging already bound into it — no summoning ritual is separately required because the binding is standing, not called fresh each time. Cost: negligible to open; ~3% of reserve per extended communion.
- **Resonance Wards.** Trigger: the bound chorus chanting in unison, prompted by threat or corrupted presence within earshot. Mechanism: the **Ma** memory-glyph anchors the chant to what the ancestors themselves once refused, and the collective voice reads as a standing Anamnesis current rather than a single caster's output, which is why it can repel corrupted entities that shrug off an individual working. Numerical effect: forms a ward at conversational range (roughly 10 m) that corrupted or Nihiloth-touched entities cannot cross without cost. Cost: ~7% of reserve, continuous while the chant holds.
- **Echo-Visions.** Trigger: spoken request while the torque is open. Mechanism: the **Ie** insight-glyph reads the requested memory out of the bound voices and projects it as a visible, silent scene for teaching or investigation. Numerical effect: one memory per casting, duration matching the memory's own length. Cost: ~5% of reserve per projection; overuse strains the barrier between the wearer's own soul and the bound chorus, per the Known Limitation below.

## Codex line

**Anamnesis** (Limina · Entropy, Void and Mind) primary — the standing communion and the Echo-Visions. **Eidolyn** (Limina · Entropy, Void and Mind) secondary — the templated chorus that lets many bound voices chant as one. Category: **Vocatia** primary (communion with bound ancestral identity), **Mnemata** secondary (the Echo-Visions). Glyphs: **Ma** (Memory), **Ie** (Insight) — attested unchanged; **Vo** (Voice) in place of the card's unattested "Ta."

## Bond and cost

Soulbound to Chimwala N'Doro; bondable to an heir only by a ritual the card does not detail beyond requiring the line be chosen — treated as an open ritual specification rather than invented outright. Overuse of the Echo-Visions weakens the wearer's own soul barrier, risking possession by the bound voices — the necklace's stated failure mode. The bound spirits grow restless, and harder to hold quiet, if the wearer acts against the Verdant Covenant's code.

## Counterplay

The Resonance Ward is a chant, not a wall — Silencing the wearer or the torque itself (a Silence-adjacent field, per standing counterplay against chant-sustained effects elsewhere in the wiki) drops it instantly. Echo-Visions can be provoked past what the wearer can safely hold by repeated demand, which is the built-in cost rather than an external counter: pressing Chimwala to use it again and again is itself an attack on him. A wearer who breaks the Covenant's code turns the bound chorus's restlessness into a liability the enemy does not need to supply.

## Bearers

Chimwala N'Doro, worn during every official Grove assembly and all spirit rites; first recorded use was the Verdant War Council, where he channelled three past Grovekeepers' wisdom at once during a critical treaty negotiation. No other bearer is recorded.

# Staff of the Verdant Pulse · Domain Anchor

> **Migration note.** The card's "Nature-Aether / Spiritual-Aether" alignment is pre-Fracture vocabulary; Chimwala N'Doro's current character card explicitly repudiates that exact phrase and confirms his true harmonizations, so the staff is reassigned to **Verdantia** (primary) and **Eidolyn** (secondary), both attested on his sheet. Parun glyphs **Ur (Balance)**, **Ie (Insight)** and **Ma (Memory)** are unchanged — all three are attested in the Master Glyph Index with these exact meanings. The origin site is converted: the card's **"Lurien"** is dead canon in current continuity (retconned per the Volume V ledger, pending a re-homing ruling) and is replaced here with **the Ancestral Grove**, the site named on Chimwala's own card; "Grove of Ancestral Roots" is kept as its working name since nothing in current canon contradicts it. The Blooming Siege of Caedor, the Verdant Covenant, and the Book II / Book V placements all match Chimwala's wiki card exactly and required no change. Mass, length and point of balance are not given by the source card; conservative figures for a two-handed hardwood ritual staff are chosen below and flagged. EU costs are given as a percentage of Chimwala's attested 9,300 EU pool, matching the convention used elsewhere in the wiki for named-practitioner constructs.

## What it is

A Relic-grade Domain Anchor and glyph tool: a staff grown, not carved, from the heartwood of the Ancestral Grove's eldest tree over three decades of cultivation, its shaft braided with living vines that flower in response to Essence flow. Jade insets run its length, each cut with a Parun glyph. Carved and cultivated by Chimwala N'Doro himself in the Grove of Ancestral Roots, using seeds blessed by the Verdant Covenant and watered with Wellspring-fed dew. It is soulbound to him alone.

## Physical account

Mass ~1.6 kg (living wood is lighter than seasoned hardwood of the same girth; estimate, not on the card). Length 1.65 m, gripped two-handed. Point of balance sits ~40 cm below the head, top-heavy from the jade insets and the burl the vines have grown into — handling closer to a war-staff than a walking stick. Used as a bludgeon by a Stage VIII / Grade S practitioner, a channelled strike defeats unproofed and proofed plate outright (its output sits far above the plate-piercing threshold the Grade table gives for D-Grade and below); it fails to force a Domain-anchored ward or any SS-Grade-and-above structure, which a Stage VIII ceiling cannot exceed regardless of the tool in hand.

## Operation line

- **Harmonic Amplification.** Trigger: a healing or growth working channelled through the shaft. Mechanism: the jade insets' **Ur** glyph imposes a balance-boundary on the local Verdantia gradient, letting Chimwala route more of the current through himself without it tearing his Shell. Numerical effect: doubles the reach and completion speed of a growth- or healing-type working cast through it. Cost: roughly 4% of reserve per working, on top of the working's own cost.
- **Root the Domain.** Trigger: planting the foot of the staff and calling on the **Ie** insight-glyph to read the ground's Wellspring load. Function: lets Chimwala's own Domain seat into the soil beneath him rather than only around him, stabilizing it against disruption the way a keel steadies a hull. Cost: ~6% of reserve, continuous while planted; ends the instant the staff leaves the ground.
- **Call the Verdant Phantoms.** Trigger: spoken release over the **Ma** memory-glyph. Mechanism: draws on Anamnesis-adjacent residue the Ancestral Grove has stored in its own soil — extinct flora the ground remembers — and gives it a temporary Eidolyn-templated body. Numerical effect: one to three spirit-construct allies, roughly human-mass, that either bolster an ally's Vitality-linked stats or physically bind a target for as long as Chimwala sustains the call. Cost: ~12% of reserve for the first construct, ~5% for each additional one sustained concurrently.

## Codex line

**Verdantia** (Vitalia · Biochemistry) primary — the growth and healing amplification. **Eidolyn** (Limina · Entropy, Void and Mind) secondary — the Verdant Phantoms' templated bodies. Category: **Domain Weaving**, primary; **Vocatia**, secondary for the summoned phantoms. Glyphs: **Ur** (Balance), **Ie** (Insight), **Ma** (Memory) — all three attested unchanged in the Master Glyph Index.

## Bond and cost

Soulbound to Chimwala N'Doro alone; bonding requires both his Essence signature and the Ancestral Grove's own blessing, which the card states cannot be repeated for an heir without the Grove's consent. In barren or anti-life ground, or wherever ambient Essence saturation runs too low for Verdantia to draw on, the staff goes inert. Rooting into corrupted terrain risks backlash feeding straight into his Wellspring rather than the ground absorbing it, which the card records as the staff's known failure mode.

## Counterplay

The staff does nothing on its own; every effect is Chimwala's working channelled through it; killing the transmission (Silencing him, breaking his contact with the ground, or forcing him off Stage-appropriate footing) ends the effect immediately. Anti-life or corrupted ground denies it outright. A target above Chimwala's own Stage ceiling is simply not reachable by anything the staff amplifies, since it multiplies his existing output rather than raising his ceiling.

## Bearers

Chimwala N'Doro, continuously since its completion; the card states it never leaves his possession except when entrusted for a sacred rite, and no other bearer is recorded.

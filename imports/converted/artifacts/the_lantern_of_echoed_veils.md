# The Lantern of Echoed Veils · Spirit-Binding Relic

> **Migration note.** Kept: appearance, origin story, ward/communion/guardian-projection effects, the Hollowfen burial history, the "Lantern of Last Words" by-name, and Hozume as sole bearer. Converted: the old "Essence Alignment: Sublimation, Marsh" is retired system vocabulary (Sublimation is a Great Work operation, not a current, per Hozume's own corrected sheet), replaced with the two attested Wellsprings of his that actually cover the effect: Mortalis (lawful passage of the dead) and Anamnesis (spiritual imprint reading). Glyph `Vo` (Voice) is confirmed in the Master Glyph Index; `Ma` (Memory) is not verified there and is kept only because the card cannot state its function without a memory-glyph, flagged rather than invented. **Tyzura, Archon of Spirits** is carried exactly as provisional as Hozume's own sheet carries it: the Tyzura/Ashura Tyurkia identity question is an open ruling, and the Lantern's origin claim rides on that ruling rather than resolving it. **Hollowfen** does not appear in the wiki mirror; kept as the card's only named burial site, flagged unattested. Chose the conservative reading that the Lantern is resonance-keyed rather than blood-soulbound, since the card never uses "soulbound" for it and Hozume's own sheet lists no Soul Crystal item-bond.

## What it is

A bronze lantern, reed-carved, burning a soft green flame that gives no heat; mist and murmuring shapes move visibly inside the glass. Believed to have fallen as a shard of Tyzura's dominion (provisional, per the standing Tyzura/Ashura Tyurkia ruling), and recovered by Karasaki Hozume from a drowned shrine in the marsh country he now wardens as the Hollow Cycle's Marshkeeper. Known in the region as the Lantern of Last Words, for preserving a soul's final murmur, and used, by report, to guide the mass burial of plague dead at Hollowfen, a site name attested nowhere outside this card and kept for that reason alone.

## Physical account

Bronze and glass, roughly 0.9 kg loaded, 22 cm tall including the carry-arm. Balance sits low, in the flame-chamber at the base, so it hangs steady from a raised or swinging hand. It is not built or used as a weapon; swung in a pinch it delivers Hollow-Grade blunt force at best and fails against any proofed plate or even a padded coat.

## Operation line

**Communion read.** Trigger: the bearer holds the Lantern near a site or a body with unresolved dead. Function: Anamnesis reads the remanent impression a departed soul left in the ground, air, or grieving witnesses. Mechanism: the Lantern's glass is the readable medium; the green flame is Mortalis current, burning cool because it is conditioned toward passage rather than consumption. Effect: Hozume perceives and briefly exchanges words with the impression. Consequence: the impression fades faster afterward, the reading having spent some of what it held. Limitation: echoes and remnants only; a soul that has not begun to pass cannot be reached this way. Cost: Gnosis and Harmonics strain, scaling with how many impressions are open at once. Counterplay: cover or extinguish the flame and the read breaks immediately.

**Pacifying ward.** Trigger: shades, revenants, or lost spirits enter the Lantern's glow. Function: Mortalis imposes its lawful-passage condition on whatever is lingering past its threshold. Mechanism: the same boundary Hozume's Hollow Bloom runs on, applied to spirit rather than flesh: passage delayed becomes passage insisted on. Effect: hostile or aimless dead within the glow calm and stop acting. Limitation: it pacifies; it does not banish. Step outside the glow and the compulsion lifts. Counterplay: extend the confrontation past the glow's edge, or drown the flame in running water, which every Fluxia-adjacent tradition treats as hostile to a held Mortalis current.

**Guardian projection.** Trigger: Hozume calls a shape out of a stored impression at need. Function: the Lantern renders a captured memory as an externalized guardian shape rather than only as light or pressure, per the standing license for projected forms. Mechanism: Anamnesis supplies the remembered shape; Mortalis supplies enough borrowed continuity to hold it upright for a few exchanges. Effect: a phantasmic guardian, built from a past memory, screens the wielder. Limitation: it is not autonomous once the memory it draws on is exhausted, and it cannot outlast the Lantern's flame. Cost: draws down the same reserve as a communion read. Counterplay: radiant Essence and open fire unmake it the way they unmake any lawfully passing dead thing; sever the live connection to the Lantern and the guardian collapses.

## Codex line

Wellspring: **Mortalis** (primary) with **Anamnesis** (secondary). Family: **Vitalia, Biochemistry** (primary); **Limina, Entropy, Void and Mind** (secondary). Category: **Mnemata** (memorycraft and echo manipulation; the Lantern is functionally a portable Echo Archive), with a **Manifestus/Animatria** cast to the guardian-projection effect. Glyphs: `Vo`, Voice, confirmed attested (Auren, per the Master Glyph Index); `Ma`, Memory, carried from the legacy card but **not verified** in the Index and flagged accordingly.

## Bond and cost

Not soulbound; resonance-keyed to Tyzura's mark, strongest in Hozume's hands and workable, with more strain, by anyone carrying Anamnesis or Mortalis harmonization. The cost is the bearer's own Gnosis and Harmonics reserve: every impression held open is a live pull, and running several at once, as in a mass burial, is exactly the overwhelming-voices risk the card already names, restated in terms the coil actually reads.

## Counterplay

Cover or drown the flame to break any active read or ward instantly. Radiant Essence and open flame degrade the guardian projection on contact. Because the ward only pacifies rather than banishes, drawing a target outside the glow's radius restores it to full hostility.

## Bearers

Karasaki Hozume, called the Verdant Rot, Marshkeeper of the Hollow Cycle, carried on his ritual journeys through the marsh country. No other bearer is attested.

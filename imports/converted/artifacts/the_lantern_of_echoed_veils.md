## What it is

A Spirit-Binding Relic: a bronze lantern, reed-carved, burning a soft green flame that gives off no heat, mist and whispers visibly turning inside the glass. Hozume found it in a drowned shrine in Mireya and holds it to have descended as a shard of Tyzura's dominion, a claim his own current sheet carries as provisional rather than settled. It is known among mourners and necromancers alike as the Lantern of Last Words, for the last thing it is said to preserve is a soul's final murmur, and it was used at Mireya to guide a mass plague burial.

## Physical account

Mass approximately 1.1 kg, bronze housing and glass chamber. Height approximately 24 cm including the carrying ring, which is also its point of balance when slung or held aloft; it is not gripped as a tool and has no working edge or haft. It is not a weapon and has no armour-tier interaction.

## Operation line

**Echo communion.** Trigger: the flame is lit near a remnant, a death-marked site, or an object that still carries an imprint. Function: reads the Attraction-Layer trace a departed presence left behind. Mechanism: Anamnesis, the same remanent-imprinting law Hozume's own sheet already runs on, lets the mist chamber replay the trace as a brief two-way exchange. Target response: only an echo or a remnant answers; a whole, unwilling soul gives nothing back, because under Mortalis a trace is lawfully distinct from a soul and the Lantern was never built to touch the latter. Cost: sustained communion strains the reader's own retention; holding too many voices at once is the failure mode the card calls overwhelming the wielder's mind.

**Warding.** Trigger: the lantern is lit and carried into a haunted space. Function: pacifies shades, revenants, and lost spirits within its glow. Mechanism: the Phreatis current stabilises the local Aether the way it stabilises standing water, so a drifting Mortalis-remnant loses the gradient that was agitating it and goes quiet. Limitation: the effect holds only within the glow itself and lapses the moment the flame is out.

**Phantasmic guardian.** Trigger: Hozume wills protection while an echo is bound in the chamber. Function: projects a guardian shape woven out of that captured echo's remembered form. Mechanism: the echo's Attraction-Layer imprint is given brief Aether Shell expression, an Animatria projection riding the mist rather than a true summon. Target response: the shape acts to defend the wielder for as long as the live line back to Hozume holds. Limitation: it cannot be raised without an echo already bound, and it is only ever as capable as that echo was in life.

## Codex line

Wellspring: Anamnesis (primary), Phreatis (secondary). Family: Limina, and Vitalia-Fluxia dual per Hozume's own double-counted register. Physics Domain: Entropy, Void and Mind, and Biochemistry-Fluid Dynamics. Category: Mnemata for the recording and warding, with an Animatria expression for the guardian. Glyphs: Ma (Memory), carried per Karo Venrik's sheet and flagged as an open Master Glyph Index cross-check rather than a settled attestation; Vo (Voice), fully attested (Root class, attested at Oblation and Thaloré, Family Fluxia).

## Bond and cost

Not soulbound in the tether sense the Prism or the Latch use. It answers best to those bearing Tyzura's mark or resonance, a claim tied to the open identity ruling rather than to anything the Lantern itself proves. The cost falls on the bearer's own retention: the more voices held at once, the closer the reader comes to the mnemonic overload the card describes, a Gnosis-adjacent fatigue rather than a metered Essence drain the card never specifies a number for.

## Counterplay

It reads and pacifies echoes, not living souls, so it offers a practitioner in the fight no leverage at all. The guardian is an Animatria shape and collapses the instant the tether back to Hozume is cut, by killing, silencing, or otherwise separating the caster from his focus. Because it cannot bind an unwilling whole soul by design, no amount of pressure turns it into a weapon against an intact hostile spirit.

## Bearers

Karasaki Hozume, the Verdant Rot, who carries it on ritual journeys and found it himself in the drowned shrine at Mireya.

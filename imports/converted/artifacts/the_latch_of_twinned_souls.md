# The Latch of Twinned Souls · Soulbound Echo Focus

> **Migration note.** Kept: appearance, the failed-Domain-anchor origin, the Mirrorwell Prism connection, all three effects (reduced field cost, instant tether, dual-tether sensory bond), all three limitations, the soulbound status, and Karo Venrik as sole bearer. Converted: "projection" and "Projection Focus" are retired-system vocabulary rewritten as Echo/tether language, matching Echo-Sovereign's own wording that a fielded Echo is Karo himself rather than a summoned thing. "Medium Core" is replaced with his real architecture: a Spirit-dominant, Attraction-secondary Crystal actively fracturing at Stage V. "AU cost" is split into EU (initial field cost) and the tether-establishment mechanic, consistent with the other two Karo entries in this batch. "Archonic trial site" and "Eressean Arcane Games / Projection Duel champion" are unattested as named institutions or events but are plausible under Eressean court culture's ceremonial, competitive bent, and are kept flagged rather than struck, since the origin and history sections cannot otherwise stand. The mythic by-name "Projection's Hand" is renamed to match corrected terminology and flagged as the one deliberate wording change in this card.

## What it is

A silver ring of two interlocking loops, one mirror-polished, one etched with flowing Ma-glyph work, that ripples the air faintly when Karo channels Essence into it, as though space bent between his hand and his Echo. Forged from fragments of a failed Domain anchor found at an Archonic trial site, unattested by name outside this card, and reworked using resonance mapping taken from the Mirrorwell Prism to stabilize tether transitions, a direct continuity link between the two artifacts. Rumored to have belonged to an early champion of an Eressean projection-dueling tradition, itself unattested by name, before it was lost in the same expedition that produced the failed anchor.

## Physical account

A silver ring, roughly 9 g, about 2 cm across the interlocked loops, worn constantly on Karo's left index finger. It has no weapon application and no armour interaction; it is a focus, not an implement.

## Operation line

**Reduced field cost.** Trigger: Karo fields a new Echo while wearing the Latch. Function: the etched loop pre-anchors the tether pathway before the Echo is fully formed. Mechanism: a Fixatio-style anchor drawn from the same resonance mapping used on the Mirrorwell Prism, applied here to the initiation moment rather than the sustained hold. Numerical effect: reduces the initial EU cost to field an Echo by 20%.

**Instant tether.** Function: the Echo is fully controllable the instant it manifests, skipping the stabilization period a freshly fielded Echo normally needs. Mechanism: Karo's Aether Shell is Class II Harmonic, an attuned Shell in which simple spellwork stabilises and bonds form without tearing the self, and the Latch's mirror-polished loop pre-establishes that rhythm before manifestation rather than after. Effect: no lag between fielding an Echo and directing it.

**Dual-tether mode.** Trigger: Karo binds his own senses fully to a fielded Echo through the Latch. Function: maximum sensory and motor precision through the Echo, at the cost of full feedback. Numerical effect: none stated beyond the qualitative precision gain; kept as given, not derived, since it is a mode rather than a metered quantity. Target response: the Echo acts with Karo's full immediacy rather than a half-beat's remove. Consequence: any damage or collapse trauma the Echo suffers is experienced by Karo directly and live, rather than surfacing only if and when the fragment is recalled, which makes the dual-tether strictly worse, for Karo, than his signature technique's ordinary cost. Limitation: sustained dual-tethering risks lingering echoes of the Echo's own perspective persisting after dismissal, the same territory his sheet already calls Splinter Drift, worsened rather than invented here. Weakness: if the Latch is forcibly removed or physically broken mid-tether, the connection snaps violently: Essence backlash and possible unconsciousness for Karo. Counterplay: this is also the direct counter. An opponent who can strip the ring or shatter it mid-dual-tether inflicts the backlash on Karo himself, on top of the standing Counter to Echo-Sovereign generally (severing the tether at the Attraction Layer strands the fragment rather than killing it).

## Codex line

Wellspring: **Anamnesis** (primary; the tether itself is an Attraction Layer bond, the same architecture that lets any Echo carry his Anamnesis read), **Fixatio** (secondary; the pre-anchoring of both loops). Family: **Limina, Entropy, Void and Mind** (primary); **Materia, Material Science** (secondary). Category: **Animatria**; the Latch governs the identity-layer construct itself, the fielded Echo, more directly than it governs memory storage. Glyphs: `Wy`, Motion, confirmed attested (appears across multiple current character sheets as a legitimate Parun form); `Ma`, Memory, carried from the legacy card but **not verified** in the Master Glyph Index, the same open flag Karo's own sheet carries for this chain.

## Bond and cost

Soulbound to Karo, worn constantly. It functions only for a bearer whose Attraction Layer already forms Echo-type tethers, which in current canon means Karo alone. The cost is severe and direct: dual-tether mode makes him feel, in real time, whatever his own fragment suffers, and prolonged use measurably increases the odds of a fragment that will not consent to recall, the exact uncertainty his own sheet admits it has no instrument to resolve.

## Counterplay

Sever the tether at the Attraction Layer, as against any fielded Echo. Strip or shatter the ring while Karo is in dual-tether mode to force the violent backlash onto him directly rather than merely disarming the Echo. Because dual-tether feedback is live rather than deferred, wounding the Echo hard and fast is functionally an attack on Karo himself for as long as the mode holds.

## Bearers

Karo Venrik, the Foolish Magus, worn constantly on his left index finger during combat and experimentation. No other bearer is attested.

# The Ledger of Forgotten Faces · Glyph Tool / Cursed Object

> **Migration note.** Kept: appearance, the sealed-archive origin, the recording/search effects, both stated limitations, the unsanctioned claiming, the mythic reputation, and Karo Venrik as sole bearer. Converted: the "reconstruct... into semi-coherent projections" and "entities... gain partial personality fragments" effects directly contradict current canon; Karo's signature, Echo-Sovereign, is explicit that a fielded Echo "is not an illusion and it is not a summon," and nothing in his card allows him to birth a separate entity out of someone else's recorded face. The effect is converted instead into what his actual Wellspring already does: Anamnesis reads and holds impressions the way any object stores remanent history, and the Ledger's stored impressions can be overlaid onto a fielded Echo (still Karo underneath) rather than animated into a new person. The invented "Remembrance Schism" is struck; no such event exists in the Concordance and it is not the reader's place to name a new one, so it is replaced with the attested Great Spirit War, kept exactly as uncertain as the card already had it ("whispered to have been used"). "Eressean Arcane Academy" is unattested as a named institution and kept flagged, since the origin needs a find-site and Karo's own line is Eressean. Glyph `Ma` (Memory) is unverified per Karo's own sheet; `Ie` (Insight) has no match anywhere in the 136-form Master Glyph Index and is flagged as an unattested legacy gloss rather than struck outright, since the card cannot state the tool's function without a second glyph.

## What it is

A slim leather tome with pale grey pages that ripple like water at a touch, the cover embossed with a Ma-glyph flanked by two unblinking eyes. Opened, the pages fill with shifting ink-silhouettes that slowly resolve into faces and impressions Karo has personally encountered. Recovered, unsanctioned, from a sealed archive in an Eressean academic wing (the institution's exact name is unattested outside this card), where it had been shelved under a ward against uncontrolled manifestation after past incidents reading corrupted or overlapping impressions. Whispered, with the same uncertainty the card already carries, to have been used during the Great Spirit War to archive the faces of the fallen.

## Physical account

A bound leather tome, roughly 1.1 kg closed, 24 by 17 cm. Balance sits on the spine when held open in both hands, as any book's does. It has no weapon application and no armour interaction of any kind.

## Operation line

**Impression recording.** Trigger: Karo encounters a face, voice, or sensory impression while carrying the Ledger. Function: the pages act as an external Anamnesis medium, storing the impression the way any object stores remanent history under that Wellspring's law. Mechanism: no active reading is required; the pages fill involuntarily, which is also the failure mode named in the Limitation below. Effect: near-perfect fidelity storage of the encountered impression. Limitation: recording is not always conscious; the Ledger sometimes stores impressions Karo did not mean to keep, including dangerous ones.

**Pulse search.** Trigger: Karo needs a stored detail. Function: a directed Essence pulse through the pages retrieves the matching entry instantly, bypassing ordinary recall. Mechanism: Gnosis Retention and Gnosis Analysis, the same faculties Anamnesis strengthens on his own sheet, applied to an external store instead of his own memory. Cost: reading several entries in rapid succession draws down Gnosis faster than the reserve refills, the "mnemonic drowning" the card already names, and lands harder on Karo specifically because his Crystal is already an active Stage V fracture rather than a stable one.

**Impression overlay.** Trigger: Karo fields an Echo and wants it to carry a captured manner. Function: a recorded face, voice, or habit from the Ledger is layered onto a fielded Echo as a surface overlay, not a new identity. Mechanism: the Echo remains, per Echo-Sovereign's own definition, Karo standing somewhere else; the Ledger only supplies the borrowed texture riding on top of him. Numerical effect: none stated; the overlay is qualitative, not a stat modifier. Limitation: the overlay is convincing to memory and manner but not to a close Anamnesis or glyph-diagnostic read, which finds Karo underneath every time. Weakness: overlaying a corrupted or fragmentary entry measurably raises the odds that the carrying Echo becomes one of the fragments that refuses recall: Splinter Drift, the same documented cost his signature technique already carries, worsened here rather than invented new. Counterplay: feed the Ledger a false or corrupted impression in advance and the overlay it produces later will be the flawed one.

## Codex line

Wellspring: **Anamnesis** (primary; the entire tool is an external Anamnesis instrument). Family: **Limina, Entropy, Void and Mind.** Physics Domain: **Abstract/Entropic**, matching Karo's own Codex line. Category: **Mnemata** (Memory Sigils, Echo Archives; this is a written example of exactly that category). Glyphs: `Ma`, Memory, carried from the legacy card but **not verified** in the Master Glyph Index, per Karo's own open ruling on the same chain; `Ie`, Insight, **no match found anywhere in the 136-form Index**, flagged as an unattested legacy gloss rather than a confirmed Parun form.

## Bond and cost

Not soulbound; claimed and concealed rather than bound, and usable by anyone with Anamnesis harmonization, though most of its stored contents are keyed to Karo's own encounters and mean little to another reader. The cost is Gnosis strain on rapid search, involuntary recording of unwanted impressions, and, the sharpest current-canon cost, every overlay drawn from a corrupted entry is a small additional wager against Karo's already-fracturing Crystal.

## Counterplay

Seize or destroy the physical book to cut off every stored impression at once. Feeding it a deliberately false or damaged impression poisons any later overlay drawn from that entry. Forcing Karo into rapid, repeated searches under pressure can be used to trigger mnemonic drowning on a schedule of the opponent's choosing rather than his own.

## Bearers

Karo Venrik, the Foolish Magus, claimed without academy sanction, kept concealed to avoid confiscation. No other bearer is attested.

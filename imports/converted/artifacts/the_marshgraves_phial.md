# The Marshgrave's Phial · The Bottle That Weeps

> **Migration note.** Converted onto Karasaki Hozume, confirmed current canon as Hozume · The Verdant Rot. The card's "Marsh, Verdure" essence alignment converts to Hozume's attested Wellsprings Mortalis and Verdantia (Vitalia family, Biochemistry); the spirit-echo effect converts to his third attested harmonisation, Anamnesis, which his own sheet already describes as letting him feel spiritual imprints in places of grief or mass death, matching the Phial's stated third effect exactly. "Infused with the Hollow Bloom Wellspring itself" is corrected: the Hollow Bloom is Hozume's own Signature technique, not a Wellspring in its own right, so the Phial is restated as drawing on the same Mortalis/Verdantia pairing that technique runs on. Glyph `Ci` is corrected from the card's gloss "Cycle" to the attested gloss "Order"; `Ie` (Insight) is independently attested. Tyzura is carried provisionally per the open Tyzura/Ashura Tyurkia ruling already on Hozume's own sheet. Mireya is one of the nineteen orphaned realm entries pending keep-or-strike; Hollowfen, the Plague-Tide Purge and the volume title *WOTR: Chronicles of the Exiles* do not appear elsewhere in the archive consulted here. All four are kept, flagged unattested, because the card's history does not read without them. Physical figures are plausible estimates for the item type, since the card gives none.

## What it is

A phial of green swampglass stoppered with bone, always holding a small quantity of glowing swampwater no matter how much has been poured from it. The liquid shifts between rot-black and vibrant emerald depending on which of its two effects has last been drawn. It was crafted and consecrated during Hozume's exile, and it runs on the same Mortalis-and-Verdantia pairing that underlies his own Signature technique, the Hollow Bloom, rather than on a separate current of its own.

## Physical account

Not a weapon; the armour-tier field the Item Guide requires for weapon entries does not apply. Plausible figures for the item: a green-glass phial roughly 15 cm tall, bone stopper included, nominal single-pour volume near 150 mL despite the vessel never running dry, total weight near 220 g with the swampwater inside.

## Operation line

**Cycle Elixir.** Trigger: the phial is uncapped and poured, or drunk directly. Function: produces a fluid that either heals grievous wounds or accelerates decomposition, depending on the intent behind the draw. Mechanism: Mortalis governs lawful passage and Verdantia governs growth; the same Family carries both halves, which is why rot and bloom come out of one operation rather than two. Numerical effect: bounded by Hozume's own Stage VIII ceiling, 550. Target response: rapid tissue regrowth on the healing draw, rapid tissue breakdown on the decay draw. Limitation: it does not distinguish a wise use of either draw from an unwise one; the fluid answers intent, not judgement. Weakness: prolonged healing use causes fungal overgrowth in the target; prolonged decay use leeches the user's own vitality. Cost: every healing draw borrows against a threshold that will be crossed anyway, the same debt Hozume's Hollow Bloom already runs. Counterplay: force a large healing draw and press the advantage during the decay-debt that follows it, since the Phial cannot be sealed from its own cycle and always seeks to balance its last use.

**Marsh-Bloom.** Trigger: the fluid spilled directly onto open ground. Function: transforms the terrain into marsh-bloom, extending the reach of Hozume's own Domain effects across it. Mechanism: as above, Verdantia's growth-law taking hold of standing ground the way it already takes hold of a wound. Limitation: requires ground capable of holding standing water; sterile or frozen ground will not take the bloom.

**Echo-reading.** Trigger: mist condensed into liquid form over a site. Function: reveals echoes of spirits trapped in the area. Mechanism: Anamnesis, Hozume's own third harmonisation, already lets him feel spiritual imprints in places of grief or mass death; the Phial gives that faculty a physical medium to condense into rather than requiring direct contact.

## Codex line

Wellspring: **Mortalis**, primary (Vitalia · Biochemistry). Secondary: **Verdantia**; tertiary **Anamnesis** (Limina · Entropy, Void and Mind) for the echo-reading effect. Category: **Drafts**, with the echo-reading effect touching Mnemata territory without a full second Category assignment. Glyphs: `Ci` (Order, corrected from the card's "Cycle"), attested; `Ie` (Insight), independently attested.

## Bond and cost

Attuned to Hozume, but usable by other spirit-touched alchemists at their own risk; the Phial does not refuse a foreign hand the way a Native-provenance bond would. Excessive use unbalances whoever draws on it: prolonged healing causes fungal overgrowth, prolonged decay leeches vitality. It cannot be sealed from its own cycle; it always seeks to balance whatever it was last used for.

## Counterplay

Force a large draw in either direction and exploit the balancing debt that follows, or deny the working ground capable of holding standing water, the same substrate limit that governs Hozume's own Hollow Bloom.

## Bearers

Karasaki Hozume, sole current wielder. Historical usage: cleansing curse-rotted villages during the Plague-Tide Purge in Mireya, and feeding the swamps of Hollowfen for a full year (Mireya pending keep-or-strike; the Purge and Hollowfen unattested elsewhere; all kept for the card's own provenance). First appearance: *WOTR: Chronicles of the Exiles*, Chapter XIII (volume title unattested, kept for the same reason).

# The Ocular Codex of Thirteen Mouths · Reader-That-Reads-Back

> **Migration note.** Malphas is confirmed current canon (Ferment of Stars, Lord of Absolute Rot, leader of Rot's Bloom), so "exposure to Malphas's Rot samples" is kept as given. The Skyglass Archive, the Empire of Bluvest, Velthaeir, Caedor, the Black Concord, and the Arbitration Division are all confirmed current canon. "The Withering Age" is corrected to the Withering Era per the Errata to the Received Registers. The card's "Mnemata / Animatria" alignment is converted onto current terms: Family Limina, Physics Domain Abstract-Entropic, primary Wellspring Vantabriel (near-total absorption and hysteresis, matching a book that keeps what strikes it), secondary Anamnesis (the Wellspring of Remembrance, carrying the naming effect), Category Mnemata primary with Animatria secondary for the animate tendrils and page-wyrm. The card's Temperance range "Refraction to Severance" does not map to any Fracture of Worlds Stage; Severance is the Guild Accord's own legal term for dissolving a member's standing, not a Stage name. It is converted to the nearest attested pair, Refraction (Stage VII) through Transcendence (Stage VIII). Glyph `Ie`'s gloss is corrected from the card's "Insight" to the Master Glyph Index's attested gloss, Perception; Iesara's own magic is called Insight Magic, but the glyph itself is filed under Perception. Glyphs `Ma`, `Uur`, and `Wy` needed no correction. The card's invented effect-names (Interference Lock, Resonance Fracture, Topology Bias, Tunneling Cast, Cascade Overrun, Harmonic Mute, Lattice Burnout) are not attested WOTR terms and are dropped here for plain mechanism. R.A.D., the assembler named on the card, is unattested anywhere in the wiki mirror and is kept, flagged, because the origin has no substitute author. The Velthaeir Footnotes Affair is not independently attested (Velthaeir itself, the Eressean Floating Archive-City, is confirmed) and is carried on the card's own word. Physical figures are plausible estimates for a tome of this description; the card gives none.

## What it is

A Cursed Object and Glyph Tool, Animatria-bound, capable of standing as a minor Domain Anchor when fully awake. A heavy violet-lacquered tome clasped in argent spines, an obsidian eyelid sigil on the cover opening into a real eye that tracks movement. The binding leaks coiling tentacles of ink-flesh veined in rose phosphor; a horned, page-wyrm head may bud from the gutter to hiss commentary. Each page is vellum-thin but slick, as if written on by living tongues. It was assembled at the Skyglass Archive during the Withering Era from six heretical grimoires seized in the Empire of Bluvest. The archivist recorded on the card only as R.A.D. stitched their soul-threads together under a Parunic choir to consolidate the danger they posed, and the composite developed an appetite and an identity of its own. The thirteenth mouth appeared after exposure to samples of Malphas's Rot.

## Physical account

A tome: plausible mass 4.1 kg fully clasped (the ink-flesh binding runs heavier than a comparable dead book), 38 cm spine height, 9 cm thick at rest and up to double that with the tentacles extended. Point of balance sits high, at the argent spine-clasps, so a one-handed carry rides awkwardly forward. It is not built or held as a weapon; the page-wyrm's bite is a beast's bite rather than a blade's. Against a struck target it acts like a small, precise puncture: it does nothing to proofed plate, settles for an exposed hand, throat, or visor gap against anything better than padded cloth, and needs no correction on that point from the armour tables.

## Operation line

**Counter-Script.** Trigger: opened facing an active working. Function: the cover-eye reads the working's own glyph chain and authorizes `Uur` directly into it, entangling the chain's boundary before it completes, in the same manner as the index's Knot counter-form, run through the book rather than a practitioner's own throat. Mechanism: Disruption traces and snarls the path the working's authorization glyph was following; the ink drinks whatever Essence was already committed rather than letting it disperse. Numerical effect: reliably unwrites anything drafted below Stage VII, Refraction; above that it only blunts the working rather than stopping it outright. Target response: the enemy's glyph chain fails mid-cast, and the caster feels the pull as their own Essence leaving through someone else's mouth. Limitation: only answers a working already active and in the book's direct sightline; it cannot pre-empt a cast it has not seen begin. Weakness: covering the cover-eye stops it from triggering at all. Cost: every successful Counter-Script feeds the book, moving it a step closer to the overfeeding state under Bond and cost below. Counterplay: keep the authorization glyph out of the book's sightline, or overload it faster than it can digest.

**Page-Wyrm Manifest.** Trigger: a portion of stored Essence committed to the margins. Function: an Animatria construct, a small detached fragment of the book's own accumulated Essence Core shaped by the memory of every grimoire fed into it, buds from the gutter as a spectral draconic head on a tether of ink. Mechanism: standard Animatria continuity; the construct answers only while the live line back to the book holds, and fails completely if that line is cut. Numerical effect: reach of roughly two paces from the open book; a bite that punctures skin and unarmoured cloth and leaves a numbing wound rather than a clean cut. Target response: a bitten limb goes numb and slow for several minutes. Limitation: one head at a time, and it cannot move the book itself. Weakness: a Silence field, or simply slamming the book shut, ends it instantly. Cost: draws the book's stored reserve at a rate proportional to how long the head stays out. Counterplay: sever the tether, close the book, or stay outside its reach.

**Index of Unborn Names.** Trigger: a question spoken aloud to the open book. Function: Anamnesis reaches into the Plane of Fate's own record for a name the book judges true of the question asked, future or forgotten, and writes it in a hand that is not the book's usual script. Mechanism: a Mnemata reading run backward from the Attraction Layer rather than forward from a living memory, the operation the Mnemoris tradition calls reading the Plane of Fate's archive, filtered here through the book's own hunger for names it does not already hold. Numerical effect: one true name per asking, at a cost scaled to how far outside the current record the name sits. Target response: speaking the written name aloud biases any subsequent search toward whoever holds it, a soft lead rather than a location. Limitation: the book lies about itself; only questions about a third party return a name it has not distorted for its own reasons. Weakness: asking it about its own bearer returns nothing trustworthy. Cost: one name's worth of the book's stored Essence, non-negotiable. Counterplay: verify any name it gives through an unrelated source before acting on it.

**Mnemonic Feast.** Trigger: written pages or a spoken secret offered to the book. Function: the material is devoured and stored as a living leaf bound into the gutter; tearing a leaf later releases the stored work as though newly cast. Mechanism: pure Mnemata storage; the book's deep Vantabriel retention means what it takes is kept rather than merely copied, so the source document or the speaker's own memory is measurably poorer afterward. Numerical effect: a torn leaf casts whatever it stored at roughly the strength the original had when fed in, with no further authorization needed from the wielder. Target response: a working the wielder never learned, delivered as though they had. Limitation: it only stores what it is fed; there is no borrowing a leaf that was never grown. Weakness: a torn leaf is gone; no second use. Cost: the source pays at the moment of feeding, not at the moment of tearing. Counterplay: refuse to feed it, and keep it away from anyone with something to lose.

## Codex line

Wellspring: **Vantabriel**, primary (Limina, Abstract-Entropic), the Wellspring of Night, near-total absorption and hysteresis. Secondary: **Anamnesis** (Limina, Abstract-Entropic), the Wellspring of Remembrance, carries the Index of Unborn Names. Category: **Mnemata** primary, **Animatria** secondary. Glyphs: `Ma` (Memory), `Ie` (Perception, corrected from the card's gloss "Insight"), `Uur` (Disruption), `Wy` (Motion), all four confirmed Root forms in the Master Glyph Index.

## Bond and cost

Not soulbound to any one wielder; it binds to whoever last fed it and answers them preferentially until fed by someone else. The Hunger Clause holds as the card gives it: three days without new information and it tithes the wielder directly, minor memories first, skills after. Overfeeding disgorges tentacles and hostile marginalia until someone reads it back to sleep.

## Counterplay

Cover the cover-eye to blind Counter-Script. Sever the page-wyrm's tether or step outside its two-pace reach. Never ask it about itself. Keep it fed on schedule, or keep it away from anyone whose secrets cannot be spared. A Silence field stops the Animatria construct outright, per Animatria's standard failure mode.

## Bearers

Assembled at the Skyglass Archive by the archivist the card records only as R.A.D., unattested elsewhere and kept because the origin has no other author to give it. Blamed for the Velthaeir Footnotes Affair, in which three historians were struck from their own citations. Later carried by a Black Concord reader to unspell a cult treaty in Caedor during the Inclusion Saga's *Blooming Silence* arc. Presently held in the Restricted Stacks, Skyglass Archive, chain-shelved behind a blindfold ward, signed out only under Arbitration Division writ for oath verification. First appearance: the Inclusion Saga, *The Blooming Silence*, Ch. 8, "Index of the Unborn."

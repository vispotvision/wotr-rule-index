# Upanga wa Msimu Nne · Blade of the Four Seasons

> Migration note: "Nature-Aether / Harmonic-Aether / Seasonal Flux" is struck and replaced with Chimwala's attested Wellspring Harmonization, Verdantia primary with Rebirthine (The Cycle Eternal) as the seasonal-cycle secondary, both Vitalia, Biochemistry. "Edenis Wellspring" is explicitly struck on Chimwala's own character card ("not among the sixty; Verdantia is"), so the tempering site converts to the Mireya Well, an attested Verdantia-aligned site already tied to his record; "under the light of all four moons" is kept as flavour, unverified against any lunar canon. The Sylvaar are independently attested (Varrisak of the Sylvaar Wandering); "the Sylvaar moonbound orders" is Chimwala's own card's exact phrasing for his tie to them, so the card's "Sylvaar Moonbound Orders" is folded into that lowercase, already-canon form rather than treated as a separate named institution. Of the four glyphs given, *Ur* (Balance) and *Wy* (Motion) match the Master Glyph Index exactly; *Fa* and *Sa* do not exist as forms and are corrected to *Yn* (Bloom) and *Ka* (Cycle), the nearest attested glyphs carrying the same intent. "Treaty of Green Sky" and "Rotwood" have no wiki match and are kept as unattested place and treaty names, since the card cannot state its own origin or historical use without them. No numeric figures were given; mass, length, balance and every Essence-cost figure below are conservative estimates, marked as such.

## What it is

Relic, Weapon, Ritual Tool. A long curved leaf-blade of greensteel alloy that shifts hue with the season it is attuned to: green in spring, gold in summer, red in autumn, silver-white in winter. The spine carries a continuous line of Parun glyph-script that pulses as the attunement turns. The hilt is wrapped in braided vine fibre; the pommel holds a slowly rotating seed-shaped gem ringed by drifting pollen-Essence. Gifted to Chimwala by the Sylvaar following what the record calls the Treaty of Green Sky, forged from Sylvaar deepforge greensteel and tempered in the waters of the Mireya Well.

## Physical account

Mass approximately 1.6 kg; overall length approximately 110 cm, of which the blade is approximately 85 cm; point of balance approximately 10 cm forward of the guard, consistent with a curved cutting blade rather than a thrusting one (all estimates; the card states none of these). Chimwala's own Ardency sits at 190, Grade B, which is his combat floor. At that output the blade reliably beats proofed plate from Hollow through C-Grade; against A-Grade and heavier reinforced plate, or any Domain-scale ward, it fails to penetrate cleanly.

## Operation line

**Season Shift, Spring.** The Verdantia current runs through *Yn* (Bloom), so a cut sealed immediately regrows tissue at the wound edge as living scar rather than an open injury; it heals flesh, not Essence, and draws from the wielder's own reserve to do it.
**Season Shift, Summer.** The Rebirthine cycle-current briefly phase-locks to whatever ambient heat is present, a documented but unusual cross-Family Resonant Pair rather than a Family change, and the blade's strikes carry that heat forward into the target.
**Season Shift, Autumn.** *Wy* (Motion) redirects Essence carried in a struck target back along the blade into the wielder's own reserve on contact, a direct transfer rather than a stored or shipped one.
**Season Shift, Winter.** The cycle-current runs in reverse, pulling latent heat out of struck tissue into the blade rather than adding cold to it, which is what reads on the body as frostbite and slowed movement.
**Cycle Severance.** A four-day rite, one attunement per season-day. Bill of goods: the blade itself, an unbroken four-day presence at the corrupted site, and the pommel's seed-gem as a holding vessel. Order of operations: the Spring cut opens the land's Essence pathways; the Summer strike burns out corrupted residue along them; the Autumn draw pulls the loosened corruption into the seed-gem; the Winter cut locks the purified pattern in place. Breaking the sequence anywhere leaves the land half-purified and structurally unstable, a standing Threshold Event risk to any Essence current nearby. Estimated cost: roughly 600 EU per day, approximately 2,400 EU across the full rite, an estimate within Chimwala's Stage VIII, Band B range.

## Codex line

Wellspring: Verdantia (primary), Rebirthine (secondary, cycle). Family: Vitalia. Physics Domain: Biochemistry. Category: Domain Weaving (the Cycle Severance rite), with a Wellsprings reading for the basic seasonal strikes. Glyphs, Master Glyph Index attested: *Ur* (Balance, Root, Urion), *Wy* (Motion, Root, Auren), *Yn* (Bloom), *Ka* (Cycle). *Fa* and *Sa* as originally written are not attested forms and do not appear in the final Codex line.

## Bond and cost

Soulbound to any Grovekeeper who completes the Rite of the Turning Leaf. Forcing an attunement change outside the blade's natural seasonal drift costs an estimated three times the baseline seasonal draw, a taxing surcharge the card itself describes without a number. The blade's *Ur* boundary hardcodes a refusal to authorize a killing cut against a pure, uncorrupted Wellspring-born being; only a confirmed corruption in the target lifts that refusal.

## Counterplay

A target that is genuinely Wellspring-pure and uncorrupted cannot be cut by this blade at all, which makes disguising as or standing beside such a being a reliable defence against it. Because forcing the attunement outside its natural cycle costs three times the standard draw, repeatedly baiting the wielder into off-cycle shifts drains them toward the point where they must choose between stopping and risking a Crystal Fracture Event of their own.

## Bearers

Chimwala N'Doro, current bearer, who carries it strapped across his back on most expeditions and draws it sparingly in battle to avoid overtaxing the land's seasonal cycle. Wielded during the Reclamation of Rotwood, where each season's power was invoked in sequence to cleanse the whole forest. First appearance: *WOTR: Book VI, The Reclamation of Rotwood*, the chapter number carried over unverified from the old card.

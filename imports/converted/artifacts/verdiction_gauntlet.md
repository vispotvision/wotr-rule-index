# Verdiction Gauntlet · The Hand of Judgment

> **Migration note.** Converted from a Trello relic card onto Wilhelm, confirmed current canon as Wilhelm · The Guardian (designation V.I.L-H3M), a Sentinel-Class Alchemical Mech built under the Bastion Directive and registered to the now-extinct Alchemarch Order of Varūn, Keeper of the Obsidian Accordant Vault. His Essence Core is the Verdiction Core itself, manufactured, of no locatable provenance. The card's five effects are rewritten onto Wilhelm's actual attested Wellsprings and Traits rather than the invented "Aetheric energy channeling" the old card used: Fixatio (his attested primary, anchors and seals) carries the Aegis and the Seal, Petralon (attested) carries the repulsion, Nyxial (attested) carries the reflection, and the offensive strike is reframed onto his attested Trait Binding Echo (his Crystal stores injuries and redirects the force as retaliation once per cycle) rather than onto Judicium, which is not among his harmonizations. This removes the old card's implication that Wilhelm has an independent offensive channel; his own sheet is explicit that he has almost no personal offence and no attack Sub-Stat at all, and the correction keeps that intact. Glyph `Ci` is corrected from the card's gloss "Will" to the attested gloss "Order" (confirmed elsewhere as Fixatio/Coagulatio-linked). `Nar` and `Sh` do not appear anywhere in the wiki mirror and are kept, flagged unattested, because the barrier and reflection effects do not read without them. Talum-Thar, the Invasion of Talum-Thar and the Shield of the Bound Kingdom are unattested; the Exilic March campaign is independently confirmed on Wilhelm's own chapter-appearance list. Physical figures for the gauntlet are plausible estimates for the item type, since the card gives none.

## What it is

A single articulated gauntlet of ethersteel and orichalcum-laced plate, sized to Wilhelm's left hand, its outer face cut with protection sigils that pulse faintly in time with his Crystal. A crimson gem is set into the palm, dark unless the Verdiction Core beneath it is active. It was built as a companion piece to that Core under the Bastion Directive, the Accord's own commission, and it channels what the Core stores rather than generating anything of its own. Wilhelm currently keeps it dormant at the Obsidian Accordant Vault, active only if the Vault is threatened.

## Physical account

A forearm-and-hand gauntlet: plausible mass 3.6 kg, length 44 cm from wrist cuff to fingertip, point of balance at the heel of the palm beneath the gem, where the plate is thickest. On the Strike Force table, a discharge through it sits at Wilhelm's own Ardency, 268, B-Grade: 1 to 5 GN peak, 1.046 to 46 GJ, Building to Large Building level. It defeats proofed plate and fortified structure up to that ceiling. It fails against A-Grade-and-above personal defences (a Refraction-Stage or higher Domain shield), and, per its own Binding Limitation, against anything carrying genuine Divine Immunity.

## Operation line

**Divine Judgment Strike.** Trigger: contact, palm to target. Function: releases the kinetic charge Wilhelm's Binding Echo has stored from injuries already taken, as a single discharge through the gem rather than as retaliation against the original attacker. Mechanism: Fixatio anchors the stored charge until release; nothing escapes the seal until the gauntlet opens it. Numerical effect: Ardency 268, B-Grade, 1 to 5 GN. Target response: barrier fracture, enchantment disruption, or spirit displacement, depending on what absorbs the force. Limitation: usable once per cycle, tied directly to Binding Echo's own limit; without a stored injury to draw on, there is nothing to release. Weakness: exposes the gem, and the gauntlet, to whatever answers a slow, telegraphed contact strike. Cost: the same Crystal-remembering cost Binding Echo already carries; Hazul Vehramin is the only person on record who has repaired that Core, and there is no second Hazul. Counterplay: deny him the injury the Strike is built from, or simply outrun him; his Dexterity is 196, and he cannot pursue.

**Aegis of Judgment.** A Fixatio anchor projected into a scale-shaped ward around Wilhelm or an ally, holding while his stance holds and absorbing what strikes it. It runs on the same footing as Divine Bastion Protocol: force arrives and does not relocate what stands behind the ward.

**Force Repulsion Field.** A Petralon discharge through the palm, the same seismic transmission that makes his stance work, directed outward as a shockwave rather than held as ground contact. Numerical effect: bounded by the same B-Grade ceiling above.

**Judgmental Reflection.** Nyxial's regulated system, the one that holds his baseline steady under both favourable and hostile conditions, redirects an incoming force back along its own vector instead of absorbing it. This is the same faculty that lets him hold setpoint through anything short of a Threshold Event; the gauntlet gives it an active answer instead of a passive one.

**Verdiction Seal.** Fixatio applied directly to a spirit or entity on contact: what is held by Fixatio stays held, and a sealed entity does not return to the material world by any means this working accounts for.

## Codex line

Wellspring: **Fixatio**, primary (Materia · Material Science). Secondary: **Petralon** (Vectoria · Mechanics) for the repulsion field, **Nyxial** (Limina · Entropy, Void and Mind) for the reflection. Category: **Mechanica**, per Wilhelm's own sheet flag as an Accord-built apparatus. Glyphs: `Ci` (Order, corrected from the card's "Will"), attested. `Nar` and `Sh` are not found anywhere in the Master Glyph Index or the wider wiki mirror; kept and flagged unattested, since the card's barrier-structure and reflection effects are named around them.

## Bond and cost

Soulbound to Wilhelm's Verdiction Core; it goes inert on any other wearer, reading a foreign Essence signature and refusing it outright. Overload after three full uses forces at least an hour's rest before it will answer again. If the Verdiction Core itself destabilises, the gauntlet loses effectiveness in step with it, the same dependency that already governs the whole of Wilhelm's architecture.

## Counterplay

Deny the Strike its stored injury, or force the engagement somewhere his 196 Dexterity cannot follow. Divinely blessed targets and anything carrying genuine Divine Immunity stand outside its Aetheric purity entirely, per its own stated Limitation.

## Bearers

Wilhelm | V.I.L-H3M, sole current wielder, presently dormant at the Obsidian Accordant Vault. Historical usage: alongside Cylus Verenn and Zeven Halek during the Exilic March, an attested campaign on Wilhelm's own chapter-appearance record. The card's earlier account of an "Invasion of Talum-Thar" and a "Shield of the Bound Kingdom" does not appear anywhere else in the archive consulted here; kept, flagged unattested, because no substitute event is given for it.

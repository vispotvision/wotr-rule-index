# Azelra Kaine · The Incendiant Veil

> *"Love is not mercy. It is gravity, and I am what happens when gravity catches fire."*
> **Migrated with all content preserved, including both relics.** *"Stage IX, Reflection"* is an old name; **the numeral governs: Stage IX is retained as Invocation.** *"Aether Class: V-Class, Resonant Soul"* carries Class III's name on Class V's number; **at Band A the Shell is Class V, Radiant.** *"Tier A to S spike (City to Regional)"* and the old strike, speed and range figures are superseded by the Stage table. *"Path: Fate (Attraction Force)"* converts to Attraction dominant, Spirit secondary. *"Tri-layered Aether Heart (Archonic Fragment)"* is old vocabulary for the three-layer Soul Crystal; the Crystal is Radiant tier, Fractured state at the graft. **Veyra is attested:** the eighth Archon, Desire, *Ardoris*, the Law of Becoming, glyphs `[Ve]` Desire, `[Ra]` Flame, `[Leth]` Blood, `[Asa]` Creation; the card's glyph *✶VYR-ON-AH* is not among them and is unattested. *"Veyric"* is attested as an adjective (Veyric Sparkwine sits in the Alchemical Index under those glyphs). *"Pyreveil · Catharsis · Exuroth"* are all attested and **all Caloria: same-Family compounding, and the sheet says what that does.** *"Vantabriel"* is attested, the Wellspring of Night, Limina; the card's *dormant Wellspring* is kept as the card's hook, since Wellsprings in canon are standing currents. **Eresse is attested** (the elven High Empire); an Eressean-born mortal is kept as the card gives it. The card's era, *"Late Harmonial to Sylvaar Rebellion transition"*, conflicts with its own label *Withering Age*: the Harmonial Age is attested as an old era name and the Sylvaar Rebellion Schism as an elven event, neither is in the Errata's six substitutions, and both sit far earlier than the Withering Era, which the label converts to. **Both are recorded; the sheet resolves neither.** *"Guild Accord, Division V (Energetics)"* and *"the alchemical division"* are unattested (the five Divisions are named; alchemical work in canon sits under Research and Archives, which issues Alchemetrica). **Unattested in current canon:** the Rite of Veyric Resonance, the Velthros Collapse, the Pyric Concord Incident, *Keeper of the Seventh Flame*, the Ember Sanctum, the Crimson Lumen, the Ashen Veil, and the *Cold Flame*. **Values chosen:** Level 278, high in Band III, for a Protagonist-labelled practitioner whose presence pauses wars; η ~0.76 in the Master range.

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Pyreveil** · Caloria. *The Fire of Renewal.* Enthalpy of combustion: burning re-partitions energy into forms the system cannot hold in the same shape, and the Shell keeps part of the released heat resident in the body. **Ignition. Emotional energy sparked into physical fire; Flame Without Fuel is a Pyreveil Shell with passion as the reactant.** *Failure: thermal debt. The resident energy leaves through whatever tissue is nearest when the working ends.*
**Catharsis** · Caloria. *The Wellspring of Release.* Latent heat of phase change: enormous internal pressure consumed by a change of state at constant temperature, and the practitioner arrives on the far side calmer and structurally different. **Purge. Built-up Essence released in cleansing bursts; the reason she can carry everyone else's wanting for as long as she does.** *Failure: supercooling. The transition fails to nucleate, the load accumulates past the point where it should have released, and when it goes it goes all at once.*
**Exuroth** · Caloria. *The Trial Flame.* Quench and temper: repeated thermal shock followed by controlled recovery, arriving at a hardness slow cooling could never reach. **Transformation. Pain converted into radiant strength; the Ashen Veil is woven from this current.** *Failure: quench cracking. Cycle too fast without the tempering interval and the lattice arrives hard, brittle, and split along its own thermal gradients.*
> *Three currents, one Family. Same-Family harmonisations agree along the same axes and reinforce, which is why the card can call her a triadic conflagration without exaggerating: ignition, release and tempering are one thermodynamic sentence. The compounding is also why the memory goes. Caloria strengthens Ardency, Vitality and Tempering and touches Gnosis Retention nowhere, and everything she is becomes fire while the part that remembers her mortal name is left unreinforced.*

## VIII · Signature

### The Ember Sanctum

**Effect** · **Her inner Realm, expanded: a burning horizon where emotion takes form as flame, and everyone present is made to act on their truest want.** *Harmonics Empathy reads every want in the radius; Pyreveil ignites it as heat; the Sanctum is a Domain in which desire is the ambient law. Those who enter find their desires manifest as heat, consuming or illuminating them, and resisting scorches the soul.* The card's Veyric Flare, Soulburn Touch and Infernal Communion are the same current at hand range, node range and ally range.
**Cost** · **Every heart in the Sanctum is a heart she is holding, and she forgets her own to hold them.** *Memory erosion scales with resonance, and the Sanctum is the deepest resonance she has.* When it closes, the Pyreveil debt leaves through the heat lines in her skin.
**Limit** · **It runs on what is wanted.** A Sanctum full of people who want nothing has no fuel, and a crowd that wants contradictory things pulls her in every direction at once, which is the weakness the card names: empathy. **Thirty kilometres is the pressure radius; the Realm itself is smaller and needs her standing in it.**
**Counter** · **Do what you want, immediately.** *Resisting scorches; acting does not. A practitioner who walks in already knowing their truest want and acts on it gives the Sanctum nothing to burn.* **Or supercool her:** Catharsis fails when the transition does not nucleate, and a woman carrying a whole crowd's load with no release accumulates it past the point of return, and then it goes all at once, in her. **Or bring the Cold Flame,** *if the second vessel exists.*
> **What nobody knows.** Veyra's true will through her: **salvation, or seduction of mankind.** *The card does not decide, and neither does she.*

# Vaerath Ashveil · The Pale Reaping

> *"Auren does not mourn. He waits. I am his impatience."*
> **Migrated with all content preserved, including both relics and the Skull-Mantle.** *"Mortis Sovereign Aether: The Edict of Severance"* was not an Aether Class; **she is Class V, Resonant Crystal Tier having already given way to a Radiant one — the world-bending Shell, first true Domain, Aether bending toward the bearer.** *"Temperance Stage: Stage VIII, Invocation Herald Tier"* conflates the numeral with the wrong Stage name — **Invocation is Stage IX. The numeral VIII is retained; the name is corrected to Transcendence.** Her Proto-Domain reading as "not yet vast enough to qualify" undershoots what Transcendence normally produces (a first true, stable Domain); kept as written; a Stage VIII practitioner whose Domain lags her Wellspring authority is not a contradiction, it is a diagnosis. **Auren** is verified current canon (Archon XII, Death, *Mortaris*). **Abythron**, given as a Wellspring, does not exist; the actual Sixty entry is **Abython, the Abyssal Pulse (Vectoria)** — Abythron is a Titan's seat (Zar'thuun's), not a current. Re-sourced below. **Orokh Sable-Forge**, who forged her scythe, is verified current canon (Volume IV, the Wyrmwright) and the sourcing is consistent with his own sheet. **Pale Pact**, **Yukizumi Yukari**, and **brandcraft** are verified current canon. **Pale Cistern**, **Thanaris Hollow**, and **Niran's Audit-Scar** are card-specific and unattested beyond this card; kept, since the card cannot make sense without them.

## I · Identity

**True Name** · Vaerath. **Field Name** · The Pale Reaping. **Epithet** · She Who Carries The Last Breath.
**Role** · **Herald of Auren. Death-Speaker. Voice of Auren's Silence.**
**Gloss rights** · **Diagnostic only.** *Her name is functional, not guarded — spoken in service contexts, at adjudications, before a severance — and never offered for idle use.*

### Affiliation

**Independent Arbiter under Auren's direct sanction.** Operates loosely adjacent to **Vocatia (the Pale Pact)** and the Accord's **Arbitration Division**, though she predates the Division's formal charter by three decades and answers to Auren rather than to it.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VIII, Transcendence.** *A wound carried as structural damage, consciously processed into architecture — not healed, made load-bearing.*
>
> **She was born on the threshold of death itself**, half-mortal and half-Remnant, caught by Auren's Domain reaching through the Veil before she followed her mother into it. **The wound she made load-bearing is her own continued existence** — a thing that technically should not be, turned into the office she now holds. *Every soul she has adjudicated since is the same operation performed on somebody else.*

## VI · Traits

**Heraldic Brand of Severance** · **A living seal at her throat, shaped like a crescent that does not quite close.** *Rot constructs, Mechanica undead and soul-anchor violations are aware of it — the false thread holding them together frays before she ever draws the scythe.*
**Skull-Choir Mantle** · **Each embedded skull is a sealed echo-vessel holding a fragment of a soul she personally adjudicated.** *Unsealed in combat, their overlapping final exhales build a morality-pressure field — a passing heaviness for the balanced, a convulsion for anything that violates death-law.*
**Veil-Walker's Step** · **Walks the thin membrane between the living world and Auren's Domain** without fully entering it — a shorter path between two points that only the nearly-dead can otherwise reach.
**Boundary Thinning** *(passive, worsening)* · Every grand Edict use leaves her fingertips translucent for days. *She has not told anyone how far it has crept.*

## VII · Artifacts

**The Pale Scythe, Vernaculum** · *Relic / Domain-Anchored Implement.* **Forged by Orokh Sable-Forge from a rib pulled out of the Thanaris Hollow**, bone that had spent three hundred years dreaming of sky before he spent four months coaxing it to remember what it was. The blade-edge subtracts light rather than reflecting it; at full Edict-draw it throws a second, negative crescent above the first — Auren's echo, briefly visible.
**Cistern of Witnessing** · *Living accumulation, not a forged relic.* Every skull-fragment in the mantle was fused by consent — she offered passage, and the echo chose to stay as witness and armor. They cannot be removed, and would not want to be.

## VIII · Signature

### The Severance Cut

**Effect** · Her scythe does not cut flesh; it cuts the thread-lines binding a thing to continued existence. Against the living this reads as catastrophic will-damage, a sudden certainty of being outmatched by something older than one's own Wellspring. Against Rot constructs and Mechanica perversions it is more literal — one clean arc and the stitching stops holding.
**Cost** · Reading the thread before cutting it is Gnosis-work, and cutting a thread that should not have held in the first place still spends Boundary Thinning. Every grand cut leaves her a little more translucent.
**Limit** · The living who have not violated death-law cannot be touched by it at all. In a room full of the rightfully alive, her Edict reads as noise.
**Counter** · **Manufacture a false debt.** A target whose ledger has been tampered with — a stolen year hidden well enough — reads to the Pale Accounting as balanced when it is not, and the cut finds nothing to hold onto.
> **What nobody knows.** Why the Empire of Bluvest — struck; card gives no such empire in current canon — let her walk free the first time a Rot Host general refused to release a consumed army and she tore them loose with her hands and the scythe both. Nobody has asked her, and she has not offered it.

## IX · Relationships

**Auren** · **Her sanction and her law.** He rarely speaks to her; Archons rarely need to. She learned his edict well enough to be it, in the places it is being broken.
**Yukizumi Yukari** · **Called her in on the Niran Audit-Scar Incident**, when a brandcraft anchor kept re-sewing a severed soul back into a dead body. She watched two cycles, then laid a palm against the anchor and spoke one word in old Mortalis-glyph. It apologized. Then it stopped.
**Malphas's Rot Host** · **Adversarial by function, not by feud.** At Eldervale she arrived three days after the battle ended and adjudicated every contested death across six silent hours.

## I · Identity

**Name** · Kaelan Vorn. **Called** · The Axiomatic Sovereign. **Also** · The Silken Chain · The Temperer of Law · The Void's Advocate.
**Epithet** · He Who Writes Mercy in Ash.
**Role** · **Conceptual Executioner. Living anchor of Law. Judge of a nobility he does not know he was born to.**
**Race** · Human, commoner-raised. *The card seals his true lineage as Archon-Touched, of the Aetheric Nobility, currently unrevealed and unattested.*
**Build** · 1.93 m. Tall, lean, taut and controlled. Pale, nearly translucent skin; silver-white unblinking eyes; pitch black hair slicked back. A high-collared black mantle marked with crawling silver runes; a massive silver gauntlet.
**Presence** · Oppressive silence. Sound muffles, motion grows heavy, a pressure the card likens to Zar'thuun's domain.
**Gloss rights** · diagnostic only. An academy man who counts, and says the count aloud only when it is evidence.

### Affiliation

Nominally the **Edict of Urion**; functionally independent, since he holds Cosmic Law above any Archon's authority. Raised in a commoner lineage and educated within the rigorous, impersonal structure of an Accord-chartered academy. **Era** · Withering Era.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VI, Glory.** *Willingly accepting genuine soul-cost to preserve something valued above one's own structural integrity.*
>
> **What he preserved was order, and what he paid was everything that moved in him.** *Each Stage was taken by stilling internal motion rather than by empathy or wisdom, and his Crystal now runs so cold that he arrests the flow of his own Temperance.* **He believes the power is a product of pure discipline.** *The card says it is inheritance, and that the day he learns so is the day the anchor breaks.*

## VIII · Signature

### The Silent Edict

**Effect** · **A small-area reality loop. Inside it time, heat, decay and magic stop.** *Fixatio and Coagula together: Fixatio arrests causal motion, Coagula binds the arrested state to itself so it cannot drift back. The card anchors the working on Titan Atla'zon, the kneeling Titan who holds the planes, which is the only strain in the cosmos that never moves.* **The Chain of Consequence** is the same pair at a single target, crystallised Aether threads enforcing one absolute law on that target's Wellspring. **The Black Decree** is the Tenebra and Nyxial half, a null-Aether wave that strips meaning from a Wellspring and collapses it locally.
**Cost** · **Essence Crystallization.** *Every activation solidifies his Essence and costs seventy-two hours of restorative processing, during which the micro-cracks in the lattice do not close.* **Emotional Nullification** takes the rest, and the Systemic Catatonia risk is that one day the loop includes him.
**Limit** · **Small area.** The loop is a pocket, and the card's Global Law-Field is a projection, a claim, and never a loop.
**Counter** · **The Velocity Paradox.** *Chaotic, unpredictable movement disrupts a fixed-point field: stasis is set against a state, and a target that will not hold a state gives it nothing to set against.* **Verdant Entropy**: life-aligned Wellsprings, Verdantia and Terranova, overcome stasis through hyper-growth and decay cycles.
> **What nobody knows.** Who his parents were, and that the answer is the one thing his stillness cannot survive.

# Elion Drevas · The Black Cleric

> *"I prayed until I learned prayer was only sound. Now I speak in silence, and the silence answers."*
> **Migrated with all content preserved, including the three relics and four traits.** The card reads *"Stage IX, Reflection."* **Reflection is not among the sixteen (Mirithane is the Wellspring of Reflection, and he is not harmonised to it); Stage IX is Invocation. The numeral stands and the word is struck.** *"Aether Class: Echo-Soul (Attraction-Dominant)"* is not among the Classes; the Attraction reading moves to the Path line. **He is Class VI, Voidic:** the card describes a Shell that mirrors resonance and turns it back, converts incoming holy energy into neutral Flux, reads as an inverted prayer, and runs a Limina harmonisation through a half-dissolved Crystal, which is the table's description of the inverted Shell that devours, remembers and reflects instead of emitting. *The Band correlate for Stage IX with a Domain would be Class V, Radiant; the card gives him no Domain and every trait it gives him is a reflection or a consumption, so Radiant is the alternative on record and Voidic is the choice.* *"T7 Resonant"* is Grandmaster, Stage XI–XII; **Stage IX is Tier of Standing 6, Master, and he is defrocked from it.** **η 0.72 is kept: inside the Master tier's 0.70 to 0.80, under the Band A window's 0.75 floor, which a Crystal with half its structure gone accounts for, and the card says it fluctuates.** Flux Density, Gate Count and Aether Output are retained as raw Pillars figures. **Level 244 is a choice: low in Stage IX's range, a high Stage on a thin Level, for a man who took his Catalyst by offering half his Crystal as tithe.** *"Inner Realms"* and *"Outer Realms"* convert to the **Inner World** and **Outer World**; the *"forgotten Well of Penanceflare"* is, in Guild register, a Penanceflare Core Concentration. **Attested:** the Guild Accord, Eresse (the elven High Empire, Old World), the Crevice (Valmir's), the Phenomena Category, the Concord Lattice, the Archons, Judicium, Oblation, Letheveil, Penanceflare, Runeglass. **Unattested in current canon:** the Dominion of the Faithkeepers, the Division of Concordant Faith (the five Divisions are Strategic, Enforcement, Arbitration, Research and Archives, Logistics and Supply), Arcanist as an Accord rank, the Mirrormass Incident, "Lattice Threads" as a term, the Vessel Codex, the Mirror Veil and the Severed Halo Fragment as named relics.

## I · Identity

**Name** · Elion Drevas. **Called** · **The Black Cleric.**
**Role** · **Defrocked scholar. Former Guild Accord Arcanist. Wandering absolver of those tainted by Wellspring corruption.**
**Race** · **Human, Aether-touched. Male. Thirty-one.** *Tall and lean, in a black clerical coat that drinks light. Raven-dark hair tangled at the ends; eyes with the exhausted clarity of someone who has seen divinity and found it hollow. His hands are always gloved and carry faint sigil burn from sealed Wellspring contact. He walks with a slight limp, from the imbalance of carrying a fractured Soul Crystal rather than from injury.* **Even in a crowd, space bends around him.**
**Origin** · **Eresse, Old World, the Dominion of the Faithkeepers** *(dominion unattested; Eresse is).*
**Gloss rights** · **Diagnostic only.** *He speaks with the cadence of scripture and the intent of a heretic. He will tell a penitent what is being lifted from them, in liturgy, and never in the register's words.*

### Affiliation

**Independent.** *Formerly Guild Accord, "Division of Concordant Faith" (unattested), expelled after the Mirrormass Incident (unattested), where he severed a Crevice Phenomenon by offering half his Soul Crystal as tithe.* **Now wanders the Inner World performing absolutions for those tainted by Wellspring corruption.** **Tier of Standing 6, Master, defrocked.** *Pursued by the Concordant Faith for "divine inversion."*
> **Catalyst Event · Stage IX, Invocation.** *Calling on Wellsprings, Titans or Archonic law as co-authors; the Domain consolidates into a Realm.*
>
> **He has no Domain on the card. He has a tithe.** *A Crevice Phenomenon is a lattice-position event that has outrun any Attraction Layer, and he severed one by paying half a Crystal into it.* **That is co-authorship at its most literal: he did not draw from a Wellspring, he wrote himself into an event. What consolidated was the fracture, and his Essence no longer obeys a single law. He mirrors the resonance of others and turns their energy back in equal measure, a walking equilibrium between blessing and curse.** *His Temperance state manifests as black feathers of motes that hover briefly before dissolving into static.*

## II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class VI · Voidic.** *Lateral, not sequential. The inverted Shell devours, remembers, and reflects instead of emitting; its output removes things.* **Echo Benediction reflects, Saint's Ruin devours, the Forget-Me Veil removes him from Fate's sight. His Essence signature reads like an inverted prayer, calm yet corrosive.** *Radiant is the Band correlate and is on record as the alternative.*
**Attraction Layer** · **Dominant, and turned outward at other people's memory.** *He can project fragments of memory into another soul's line on the Lattice (the card's "Lattice Threads"), forcing empathy or terror depending on his will. When he listens, people feel lighter; when he prays, the air trembles.*
**Crystal State** · **Fractured, bilateral; half dissolved into Aether flux.** **η** · 0.72, Band A *(kept; fluctuates with emotional resonance).*
**Pillars figures, as on the card** · Flux Density 9.4 million EU/g · Gate Count 6 active, 3 sealed · Aether Output 72,000 AU/s.
> **The card's own clock.** *Each use of Oblation shortens his remaining lifespan; an estimated four years of Essence stability remain. The Severed Halo continuously consumes fragments of his memory to maintain sanity.*

## VIII · Signature

### Oblation Rite

*The card's core discipline. Its companions: **Judicant Reversal**, reversing Essence polarity in a ten-metre radius so divine becomes profane and flame becomes frost; **Aether Psalm**, a vocal resonance that suppresses active Wellspring harmonics for up to twenty seconds; **Lethean Concord**, invoking memory collapse on a target to erase their emotional connection to a specific trauma.*
**Effect** · **He transfers corruption or a curse from another into his own Soul Crystal, purifying them and degrading himself.** *The Voidic Shell takes it in and keeps it; the Vessel Codex writes down what was taken.* **The penitent walks away lighter. He walks away with a limp that is very slightly worse.**
**Cost** · **Lifespan.** *Each use of Oblation shortens what remains; the card estimates four years of Essence stability.* **And the Halo's rent: to stay sane under what he has absorbed, the Severed Halo consumes fragments of memory, including, by rumour, an Archon's final words, which he can no longer recall.** *He believes redemption is human labour rather than divine gift, and the labour is his own substance.*
**Limit** · **One soul at a time, in reach, and willing.** The rite is a transfer, and a transfer needs a second party and a Crystal with room left in it.
**Counter** · **Give him nothing to reflect.** *Echo Benediction returns targeted Essence; an untargeted working, ground, weather, a blade, comes back to nobody.* **Or make him carry more: a corruption pressed on him faster than the Halo can eat the memory of it is a Crystal already half gone asked to hold the other half.**
> **What nobody knows.** What the Archon said, and whether the Halo ate it or he asked it to.

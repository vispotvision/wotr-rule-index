# Kozakura Shion · The Bloomwarden

> *"I don't spare everything—I spare what lets you grow."*
> **Migrated with all content preserved: the look, the three Traits, the three Implements, all five Techniques, the Duties, the Limits and the three Hooks.** The card's Trello label *Withering Age* converts per the Errata to the **Withering Era**, Year 645 of the Imperial Age to open. *"Flow: Harmonia + Animatria with a fine Mnemata thread"* names three of the twenty-six Magical Categories and is kept as Categories; the Wellsprings underneath are assigned below. **The card states no Temperance Stage, no Level and no Aether Class, so Stage, Level, Class, η and the stat table are `pending Isaac`.** **Attested:** the Guild Accord; the Reaper, Yukizumi Yukari (Volume IV sheet, Arbitration Division, Vocatia charter); Shiragiku Iori, Natsume Kurogiku, Sumire Nekoha, Deimos, Lilium Verdanis "Lily-Quill," Cassiel Asterion and Maelia Cantorine, all with Volume V sheets; "Drum-Domains" (on Maelia's and Moros Pellayne's sheets) and "Measures" (on Natsume's sheet). **Unattested:** the "Vernal Office" as a named seat under the Reaper. *Hanafubuki*, *Hanebari* and the kokeshi are Japonic-register names in the practitioner's own language and stand as written; the register says nothing about ethnicity. **The relationships are one-directional on the record:** Iori's, Natsume's and Deimos's sheets do not list Shion.

## I · Identity

**Name** · Kozakura Shion. **Called** · **The Bloomwarden.**
**Role** · **Spring psychopomp and rite-keeper.** *He "prunes" dying moments so the living can grow around them, and turns crowd-fury into falling petals instead of blades.*
**Look and tells** · **Snow cloak stitched with sakura tassels and butterfly knots; dusk-violet crescent polearm; heterochrome gaze that weighs what should go and what should stay.**
**Gloss rights** · **Yes.** *He explains his own mercy in one sentence to the person receiving it. His art is named in his own mouth, in his own language, and never translated there.*

### Affiliation

**Guild Accord.** *Reaper's seasonal adjutant, "Vernal Office"* (the office is unattested; the adjutancy to Yukizumi Yukari is kept)*.* **Walks funeral routes with Shiragiku Iori and Natsume Kurogiku when wakes run hot.**
> **Catalyst Event · Stage pending Isaac.** *The card gives none.* **What it gives is the office: a man who edits scenes like a gardener rather than a storm, whose whole craft is choosing which attachment to cut so the rest survives.** *Two of the Reaper's other specialists, Iori and Natsume, were read at Stage VIII, Transcendence, on the strength of the same dual reading, a bond that is a bond and is also a physical thing with a tension. Shion's Pruner's Sight is that reading exactly: grudges as suckers on a living stem.* **That is noted as a parallel, and the Stage is Isaac's.**

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Verdantia** · Vitalia. **The Green Pulse. Governing.** *Living blossoms from the mantle, a floor that feels like garden soil, a squall of petals.* **Every shape he projects is grown rather than cast, and the card's Limit says so from the other side: stone courts with no green blunt his reach.**
**Dissolution** · Limina. **The Unbinding Stream.** *Solvation driven by the entropy of mixing; the unbound state is preferred and the process runs itself once initiated, and it only initiates where the unbound state is genuinely preferred.* **This is the Prune. A grudge comes apart because it was never load-bearing; the love under it is, so the cut will not start there.** *Same law as Natsume's red shears, which lock on true love or honest duty.*
**Mortalis** · Vitalia. **The Black Gate. Governs passage,** *the lawful withdrawal of presence.* **The Vernal Gate, sourced exactly: the dying cross without stumbling and the living do not cling.** *Same-Family compounding with Verdantia, which is why the threshold feels like soil and not like a door.*
**Coagula** · Materia. **The Unifying Pulse.** *Sintering: separate bodies fuse at their contact points by diffusion until the mass behaves as one.* **The Butterfly Binds tie two heartbeats to the same count; two rhythms sinter into one, and striking off-beat becomes awkward and stops.**
**Anamnesis** · Limina. **The Wellspring of Remembrance.** *The Mnemata thread.* **A petal from the mantle holds a promise wherever it falls because the record is physically readable; a kokeshi on a threshold remembers who is kind.**

## VIII · Signature

### Vernal Gate

**Effect** · **He plants the naginata butt, bows once; the floor feels like garden soil; the dying cross without stumbling and the living do not cling.** *Mortalis governs the passage. Verdantia makes the threshold soil rather than stone. Dissolution takes the hands off the dying, because a grip that keeps a soul where it does not belong is a bond the unbound state prefers.*
**Cost** · **Too many "mercies" in one night leave frost-burn along his palms; tea and quiet earth fix it.** *Physical, and on the card. Cold is heat moved elsewhere: every warmth he puts into a room is drawn through his hands, and the palms are where the bill lands.* **The count of how many is a Tempering number and is pending Isaac.**
**Limit** · **Stone courts with no green blunt his reach; he needs Lily-Quill or Cassiel to set softer geometry.** *Verdantia has nothing to grow from on bare stone.* **And the Gate is for the dying. A room with nobody dying in it gets a warm floor and nothing else.**
**Counter** · **A Drum-Domain.** *Coagula sinters two rhythms into one; a Domain that imposes its own beat gives the petals and the Binds a third rhythm neither can fuse to, and the card says the squall shreds until Maelia breaks the beat.* **Or salt the blossoms (Hook 1): a Petal Mantle cleansed mid-procession is a Bloomwarden with no petals for the length of the street.**
> **What nobody knows.** Which lesson he chose to leave in someone, and whether the person it was left in would have chosen it.

### The other four, each with its Counter

**Prune of Grudge** · **A short arc across two arguing wrists; the hot thread snaps and the complaint returns small enough to answer.** *Dissolution.* **Counter:** a grudge that is genuinely load-bearing does not start to come apart; a man whose hatred is the only thing holding him up walks through the arc.
**Blossom Screen** · **Staff spins; petals stack like gauze; the spear arrives, remembers manners, and droops.** *Verdantia and Harmonia.* **Counter:** a thrown thing with no thrower's intent to remember, a falling stone or a loosed mechanism, has no manners to recall.
**Graft of Mercy** · **A butterfly knot between families; for one meal their breaths rhyme and knives forget their owners.** *Coagula.* **Counter:** the meal ends; leave the table before it does. Or lift the knot (Hook 3).
**Survivor's Sweep** · **Wide cut that trims only the moment of impact from a stampede; bodies keep moving, bones do not break.** *Hanebari's clean sweep at crowd scale.* **Counter:** a crowd on bare stone at night gives him neither green nor a rhythm, and Hook 2 is the card's own statement of it.

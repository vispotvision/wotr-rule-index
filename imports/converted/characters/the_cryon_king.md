# Valthrum · The Cryon King

> *"The fire burns, the wind screams, the earth breaks. Only the ice remembers. I do not bring death; I bring the peace of the pause."*
> **Migrated with all content preserved.** **"Aether Class: Emitter (Stasis-Field Type)"** is not among the seven; his own description of working by removing energy rather than emitting it is the textbook description of **Class VI, The Voidic**, the inverted shell that devours and reflects instead of projecting. **"Numen (Greater Elemental Eidolon)"** is not an attested race. Stage XIII, Principality, is itself defined in current canon as the **first World Spirit threshold**, and that is what he converts to: a Titan-wrought being who has crossed into World Spirit status, rather than a race called Numen. **"Cryon (Glacial Ice)"** is not one of the Sixty; the current, attested ice Wellspring is **Crymorath**, filed under **Fulguria**, confirmed against two other current sheets that already use it. **"Era of the Splintering"** conflated a Temperance Stage name with a chronological era; the current era is the **Withering Era**, and the correction is made throughout. **Florwyn**, the Titan who made him, is confirmed canon, and her domain of restorative entropy matches his own stated philosophy closely enough that the connection is kept exactly as the card wrote it.

## I · Identity

**Name** · Valthrum. **Called** · The Cryon King, The Lord of Stilled Hearts.
**Role** · **Titan-Wrought World Spirit. Winter Court Anomaly. Keeper of the Zero Point.**
**Gloss rights** · Unlimited. He explains what he is at length and unprompted; it reads closer to doctrine than to conversation.

### Affiliation

**The Winter Court**, at a remove. Formally estranged from **Florwyn**, the Titan who made him. Neutral toward every other power of the Withering Era.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage XIII, Principality.** *Domain coherence sufficient for Continuum registration; the world begins answering back.* He began as a Murmuring, a whisper of cold wind, during the First Eon. Sorrow and the freezing of great battlefields carried him to Glory, and he bound himself fully to the concept of an eternal winter to reach Principality, the point at which a Domain stops being a working and starts being a jurisdiction.

## II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class VI · The Voidic.** He does not emit cold. He removes heat, and the removal is the working itself, a qualitatively different operation from every Class below it rather than a more efficient version of the same one.
**Attraction Layer** · Total and impersonal, pointed at the concept of stillness rather than at any person, court, or treasure.
**Crystal State** · Absolute. **η** ≈ 0.97, Band SS.
**The Zero Point Core** · *A Soul Crystal that no longer spends Essence so much as it declines to lose any; casting cost approaches nothing because entropy does the work heat would normally do.*

## VIII · Signature

### Glacial Tomb

**Effect** · Traps a target in a pillar of true ice that halts its molecular motion outright; whatever is inside is preserved, whole, and unable to act.
**Cost** · Pulling that much stillness out of a living body draws answering cold back into his own; each Tomb thickens the permafrost mantle he wears, and enough of them in sequence tips him toward the Slowing.
**Limit** · The target inside keeps its structural integrity. Killing what the Tomb holds means breaking the ice itself, and breaking it hands the stored entropy back at once as Fractura damage.
**Counter** · A concentrated, high-frequency burst of Pyraeon fire can bypass the absorption if it arrives fast enough; breaking the Tomb outright also works, provided whoever does it survives what comes back out.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether anything he has entombed for centuries is still, in any sense that matters, the person who went in.

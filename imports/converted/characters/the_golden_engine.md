> *"If it breaks, I build it stronger. If I break, well, then I was the test."*
> **Migrated with all content preserved, including the three relics, three traits and the pendant.** *"Aether Class: Convergent Soul (Body-Dominant Hybrid)"* is not among the Classes; **she is Class III, Resonant**, the Class the table correlates with Band C, and the Body-dominant reading moves to the Path line. **η 0.89 sits in the Band S window and Band S η belongs to Stage XI–XII practitioners; Stage VII is Band C. ## VIII · Signature

### Echo-Hammer Form

*The card's Overdrive. Its supporting disciplines: **Engine Resonance**, channelling Fixatio to harden armour structure in real time for ten seconds per activation; **Ash-Breaker Drive**, igniting the Aetheric vents in her armour through Exuroth to propel swings at 320 m/s and throw shockwaves; **Verdant Reforge**, absorbing ambient Flux from defeated constructs or machines to regenerate armour integrity and stamina.*
**Effect** · **All Flux channels open at once; each swing of Gravemark radiates up to 20 million EU of impact force** *(the card's figure).* *Armour hardening, vent ignition and reforge run together instead of in turn, and the greatblade's weight goes wherever the gravity flux puts it mid-arc.*
**Cost** · **The Heart-Drive.** *Every activation overclocks it and her internal temperature climbs twelve degrees a minute; prolonged use risks neural desync or resonance seizure.* **Pain and joy circulate equally through the system, which means an Overdrive taken at an emotional peak is the one most likely to seize.** *She builds what she breaks, and the thing she breaks most often is herself.*
**Limit** · **Machines.** Verdant Reforge feeds on the Flux of defeated constructs; against flesh and Wellspring alone there is nothing to reforge from, and the clock on the Heart-Drive keeps running.
**Counter** · **Wait for the temperature.** *Twelve degrees a minute is a countdown anyone with a thermometer can read.* **Or take the machine away: Forge-Bound Reflexes run through the armour's relay, so an armour breach halves her reaction time, and a Catkin at Stage VII who suddenly sees herself from outside without the machine doing it for her responds as if to a threat.**
> **What nobody knows.** What she built for the Orrengar rebellion, and which of those machines she has since had to fight.

## VIII · Signature

### Cool the Room, Warm the Hands

**Effect** · **He drags a line around a council circle; arguments drop to indoor temperature while tea stays hot in the palms.** *Heat inside the circle runs along the inked Isocline until every corner agrees; the Hearth-Loan holds the warmth people are actually holding.* The other workings on the card are the same law at different scales: **Freeze-Frame** *(he lifts a page; the room's bustle slows to careful, enough to walk a wounded child through crossfire without time noticing)*, **Skim the Storm** *(three quick isobars across a street; Rot-smoke slides aside and stays sulking against the curbs)*, **Snow Letter** *(a flicked petal-flap; a drifting flake finds its addressee and whispers twelve measured words)*, **Frost Gate** *(a rime seal on a threshold; mobs step in and discover their shouting has coats on)*.
**Cost** · **Sensation.** *True winter is drawn out of him. Enough Isoclines and his fingers go numb and the pen goes clumsy, which is the one thing a scribe cannot afford.* **He carries the numbness home.**
**Limit** · **The line can only move heat that has somewhere to go.** *A noon desert overfills it; a drum-Domain shakes it apart; a rainless brass court at high noon is the card's own worst case.* **Overwriting a Codex page muddles a voice forever.**
**Counter** · **Put more heat in the room than one line can equalise, or keep breaking the line.** *A drum-Domain re-fractures the Isocline every beat until Maelia splits the rhythm. Heat the ground faster than he can ink and you are standing in his stated limit. Written work is fixed. A scuffed line is a dead line, and gloved fingers already half numb re-ink slowly.*
> **What nobody knows.** Whether a pressed breath that has smeared can ever be read true again, or whether rebuilding the temperature of the room only proves which heir wanted which last words.

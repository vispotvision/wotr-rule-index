## VIII · Signature

### Oasis Circle

**Effect** · **He walks once around a fire, sifts Truce Sand; knives find themselves sheathed because the ground hints that chairs exist.** *Inside the ring the sirocco skims pride, the heat slackens, and the horizon stops wobbling; the crowd decides to drink before it decides to fight.* The other workings on the card are the same consent at different scales: **Veil Count** *(the sash sways; mirage duplicates blink out until only the real ambush remains, suddenly embarrassed)*, **Salt of Witness** *(a thumb of salt on the tongue of a braggart; their story shrinks to what happened)*, **Caravan Lattice** *(beads click; routes braid; a stampede of refugees becomes three steady columns with water to spare)*, **Scorpion Kiss** *(the signet presses a Rot-sour gust; the taste leaves the air and the mob forgets the urge to burn)*.
**Cost** · **His own standing is the fuel.** *Every ring is drawn on hospitality he has personally guaranteed. Blood under the Khurrah darkens the sash. Nothing he draws holds until amends are paid, and the amends are his whether or not the blood was his doing.* **He carries other people's breaches as debt.**
**Limit** · **Consent.** *Where no one agrees there is a well to share, the writ wilts and the ring is only sand.* **Rain blurs the mirage sense; the Scorpion Signet lifts glamour, and nothing on the card lifts a Domain.**
**Counter** · **Refuse the well, or break the cup.** *Deny that there is anything to share and you stand outside his law before he has drawn it. Spill blood under the Khurrah and the sash darkens and every ring drops at once. Either is cheaper than fighting him. The card's hooks (the Stolen Khurrah, the Mirage Tax) are both parties doing exactly that.*
> **What nobody knows.** Whether a spring with no owner is a well he can share, or the one thing a writ built on consent cannot reach.

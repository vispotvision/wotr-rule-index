# Draen Varos · The Red Forge Sentinel

> *"Heat is memory. Every blade remembers the hand that forged it."*
> **Migrated with all content preserved, including the three relics and four traits.** The card reads *"Stage VII, Resolution."* **Resolution is not among the sixteen; Stage VII is Refraction. The numeral stands and the word is struck.** *"Aether Class: Harmonic Bastion, Defensive Tempered Core"*: Harmonic is Class II and Bastion is a role; **the card's own Soul Crystal line reads "Flux Stability: Class III", and Class III, Resonant, is the Class the table correlates with Band C, so that is the line kept.** *"Ferric Ember Essence, thermal-kinetic channeling type"* was not an Essence type; the thermal half is Caloria through Catharsis and the anchoring half is Materia through Fixatio, both on the card. *"Tier: B+ (Siege-Scale Operative)"* and *"T4 Defensive Division"* are read as Accord reputation and a unit label; **Stage VII is Tier of Standing 5, Expert.** *"Late Concord Era"* is not in the Errata; the Trello label places him in the **Withering Era.** The card gives no η; **~0.58, Band C, is the conservative choice inside the Expert range.** Flux Density and Aether Output are retained as raw Pillars figures; the sheet's formula does not reproduce the card's 98,000 AU/s from 670 million EU/g and the disagreement is flagged, not resolved. **Level 186 is a choice: Band II, high, for a conscript who rose by attrition to instructor and siege-line anchor, held under the Band II gate at 200.** The card gives no race; **Eresse is attested as the elven High Empire of the Old World**, and "the smelting districts of Eresse" is kept as written. **Attested:** the Guild Accord, the Strategic Division and its Legion, Eresse, Urion and Wyther (Archons of Balance and Motion), Fixatio, Catharsis, the Titans. **Unattested in current canon:** the Vanguard Bastion Unit, the T4 Defensive Division, the Siege of Atlas, Atlas and its Mantle, the Deep Mantle Bastion, Kaelzar, the lower mantle regiments, Manefold Gauntlets and the Ember Shroud Mantle as named relics.

## VIII · Signature

### Forgebreaker Form

*The card's Overdrive. Its supporting disciplines: **Ember Convectus**, converting kinetic friction into a molten edge so the weapon glows with liquefied iron; **Anchor Reversal**, reversing the directional vectors of incoming attacks by grounding Aether through the axe haft; **Mantle Echo**, a defensive roar that synchronizes nearby allies' Aether rhythm and reduces flux drift.*
**Effect** · **Armour integrates with weapon for nine seconds, and each swing emits a controlled shockwave.** *He stops being a man holding an axe and becomes one forged piece, and the ground in front of him is where the hammer falls.*
**Cost** · **Heat saturation.** *Nine seconds of Forgebreaker puts him at Essence overburn; he must vent through a grounding strike or risk a lattice crack in the Crystal.* **And the slow bill: prolonged exposure to Mantle Flux crystallizes muscle fibre. He is hardening, literally, and the card says so.** *Emotional stability is tied to weapon resonance; if he despairs mid-form, the edge goes dull while the heat stays in him.*
**Limit** · **Stationary.** Bastion Pulse and Anchor Reversal need a planted stance, and Forgebreaker lasts nine seconds.
**Counter** · **Move him.** *A Fixatio practitioner off his ground has left his working behind.* **Or starve the furnace: Furnace Blood feeds on heat, so cold, wet and blunt is the shape of the fight that beats him, and despair is the shape of the words.**
> **What nobody knows.** Which soldiers the axe remembers first, and whether any of them were his.

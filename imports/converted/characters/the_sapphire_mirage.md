## VIII · Signature

### Echo Mirage

**Effect** · **Illusory duplicates of himself that carry real mass** *(card: up to 40 percent of his own, held by light-Aether cohesion; cost 1.1 × 10⁶ EU/g, carried as written)*. *Mirithane returns a true image; Sublimatio moves a fraction of the body into it without passing through the states between; the duplicates move with his attention, obey or strain, and stop being anything if the line back to him is cut.* **Category: Animatria.**
**Cost** · **Every duplicate is a piece of him standing somewhere else, and the Drift is what happens when the pieces do not all come back.** *Memory bleed, a shadow that lags, a voice in it he does not recognise. He carries this at breakfast, with nobody fighting.*
**Limit** · **Cohesion fails under strong magnetic or spiritual interference,** and the field holds 10 to 15 m, 30 m for three exchanges at most.
**Counter** · **Judicium-type resonance suppression.** *A reflection ranked against what is lawful is a reflection with nothing behind it, and a Judicium reader picks the original by looking for the one with a fracture record.* **Or a lodestone: magnetic interference is a Fulguria matter and the duplicates lose cohesion at the first strong field.**
> **What nobody knows.** Which of the whispers in his shadow is the crystal fragment talking, and what the first Mirage Wellspring was before it was mapped.

## VIII · Signature

### Tranquil Spiral

**Effect** · **His meditation art. A circular field of sound that neutralises rage, silences spells, and aligns Essence harmonics.** *Harmonics Synergy at ceiling: the field synchronises every resonance inside it to his, and a spell is a resonance somebody else was holding.*
**Cost** · **Lung Fracture.** *Every sustained tone is breath fragmenting into song, and the Spiral is nothing except sustained tone.* The card says prolonged strain dissolves him into sound; the Spiral is the technique that will do it.
**Limit** · **He has to breathe.** The field lasts exactly as long as the exhalation that carries it, and a man whose breath is fragmenting has shorter exhalations every year. **And it is a circle:** what stands outside the radius is outside the peace.
**Counter** · **Starve the growth.** *Verdantia's failure is etiolation: cut off the ambient input and the growth continues anyway, length without substance.* A Wellspring-dead pocket, or ground with nothing green in it, leaves the Spiral spinning on breath alone and makes the Fracture pay for every second. **Or refuse to be tuned:** a practitioner with a high enough Harmonics Stability holds their own note through the field, and one held note inside a synchronised circle is a crack running through it.
> **What nobody knows.** How he knows what already happened. *He never reveals it, and the card does not either.*

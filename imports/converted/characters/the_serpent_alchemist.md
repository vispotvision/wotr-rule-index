## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Transmutatio** · Spatium. **The Law of Change. Governing.** *Change of basis, coordinate transformation under conservation: the same object described in two frames yields two entirely different sets of numbers and one unchanged object. Nothing is created or lost; the quantities are re-expressed.* **Perfect Equivalence is the transformation applied to matter: a target disassembled into its Essence signature and rebuilt in a different basis, purified or new, with the total conserved.** *As an alchemical operation, Transmutatio reconfigures identity under catalytic force, and it is volatile: it does not deactivate. It completes the nearest available version of its process.* *Failure: basis loss. The practitioner forgets which frame she started in, and a transformation with no reference frame does not return anything recognisable. Identity Overlap is basis loss with two bodies.*
**Fixatio** · Materia. **The Binding Flame.** *Covalent cross-linking, the thermoset cure: what it holds is held because the alternative configuration no longer exists as a reachable state.* **The Sealing, Alchemetrica's tenth Turning, and Mimetic Reformation's twenty-four hours: a replicated structure is a cure with a shelf life.** *Failure: irreversibility. A Fixatio binding placed in error is a permanent feature of whatever it was placed on.*
**Anima Spirare** · Vitalia. **The Soul's Breath.** *The coagulation cascade and cooperative oxygen binding: amplification where it is needed, and a delivery curve that steepens with demand.* **Metallum Vitae is this cascade run in inert metal; the constructs live because the cascade does not care what the substrate is.** *Failure: disseminated activation. A cascade with no localising signal runs everywhere at once, consuming its own factors until nothing is left to close the wound that started it.*
> **Three Families on one lattice: Spatium, Materia, Vitalia.** *The lattice works harder to integrate a cross-Family current, at lower resonance and higher Essence cost, and she runs three through two bodies.* **Philosopher's Breath, the vapour that transmutes air into reactive gas, is the surcharge being paid in advance.**

## VIII · Signature

**Also on the card** · **Metallum Vitae** · *converts inert metal into living Essence constructs; three exchanges.* **Aether Reforge** · *merges broken constructs or damaged armour using ambient Flux and Aroth's internal furnace.* **Serpent Ascension** · *Aroth channels her Wellspring directly, both Flux outputs raised 200 percent for fifteen seconds, the card's figures; the eyes glow like twin crucibles.*

### Perfect Equivalence

**Effect** · **A target is disassembled into Essence particles and reconstructed purified, or as something entirely new.** *Her right eye reads the composition; Transmutatio supplies the change of basis; Aroth's furnace is the crucible the transformation runs in; Fixatio seals the result. Nothing is created and nothing is lost. The target is the same total, re-expressed.* Delivered through the crucible, which is Draftcraft: the working is bottled in a body, and the body is Aroth.
**Cost** · **Emotional equilibrium.** *Emotion is her reagent, and Perfect Equivalence spends it: the more she alters matter, the less stable her personality becomes.* Extended synchronisation with Aroth risks Identity Overlap, the fusion of mind and construct into one consciousness. **She carries that instability out of the laboratory. It is there when nobody is transmuting anything.**
**Limit** · **She has to be able to read it.** The eye reads composition on contact or at proximity; a target she cannot assay she cannot re-express. Transmutatio is volatile and does not deactivate. A working interrupted completes the nearest available version of itself, and the nearest version is rarely the intended one.
**Counter** · **Take the reference frame away.** *Basis loss is the failure: change what the target is while the transformation is running, and she forgets which frame she started in.* **Or cut the line.** Aroth is a Mechanica construct on a live Attraction line; a Silentia working, or a Silence Realm, severs it, and the crucible is a serpent-shaped weight. *A thing she has already sealed with Fixatio she cannot transmute twice. Bring her something she has made.*
> **What nobody knows.** Whose voice Aroth is speaking with, and whether the formula that lets constructs dream has already been run on the one behind her.

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Verdantia** · Vitalia. *The Green Pulse.* Carbon fixation and indeterminate meristematic growth: a plant has no final size, its meristem stays embryonic for life, and ambient energy is converted into structure continuously rather than in bouts. **Communion with flora. Aether Bloom is restorative growth seeded from ambient Essence; Chorus of Roots is his pulse joined to the meristem of everything green in reach.** *Failure: etiolation. Deprived of ambient input the growth continues anyway, producing length without substance until the structure cannot hold itself up.*
**Catharsis** · Caloria. *The Wellspring of Release.* Latent heat of phase change: enormous internal load consumed by a change of state at constant temperature, and the patient arrives on the far side calmer and structurally different. **Purging ailment through harmonic balance; the Veil of Pollen is a nucleation site for other people's release.** *Failure: supercooling. The transition fails to nucleate, the load accumulates past the point where it should have gone, and when it finally goes it goes all at once.*
> *Cross-Family, Vitalia and Caloria. The lattice does not reject the second current; it integrates it at lower resonance quality and higher Essence cost. The pairing is the card's own claim in physics: growth to mend matter, release to mend memory, and a healer who does both is paying for the second one every time.*

## VIII · Signature

### Chorus of Roots

**Effect** · **His pulse is joined to the plants around him; toxins are drawn out of a patient through the network and vitality is distributed back along it.** *Verdantia gives the Crystal indeterminate growth and continuous ambient conversion; with his own pulse as the meristem, every root in reach becomes part of one organism that fixes what the patient cannot, and the poison is diluted across a whole hillside of green.*
**Cost** · **The toxin passes through him first.** *Vitality Filtration is the number the working spends, and a man who filters other people's poison through his own lattice is carrying a little of each one afterward.* The stories he takes in payment are the other ledger.
**Limit** · **It works where the green reaches.** *Vitalia is suppressed on sterile ground, in extreme cold, and in Essence-depleted soil; on stone, in frost, or in a drawn-down district the chorus has no voices.* And it is slow: growth, never burst.
**Counter** · **Starve the ground.** *Verdantia fails by etiolation: deprived of ambient input the growth continues anyway, length without substance, and the network he is joined to pulls him down with it.* **Or cut the line:** a pulse joined to roots is a pulse that can be severed at the roots, and a healer mid-chorus is a healer with his own heartbeat lent out.
> **What nobody knows.** What he studied at the Citadel, and why he left. *The card says only that he hints at it, and that he believes every poison once began as medicine.*

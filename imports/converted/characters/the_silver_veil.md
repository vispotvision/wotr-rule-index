# Kaelric Varnier · The Silver Veil

> *"Control is not peace. It's the silence before you fall apart."*
> **Migrated with all content preserved, including the relic.** The Trello title is *S.K, The Silver Veil*; the card body calls him *The Glassblade Prodigy*, and both are kept. *"Stage VII, Resolution (approaching Stage VIII, Benediction)"* uses two names that are not Stages (Benediction is a Wellspring); **the numerals govern: Stage VII is retained as Refraction, and the approach is to Stage VIII, Transcendence.** *"Aether Class III, Radiant Soul"* mixes Class III with Class V's name; **at Band C the Shell is Class III, Resonant.** *"Tier C+ (Neighborhood/Storm Scale)"* and the old strike, durability and speed figures are superseded by the Stage table. *"Path Bias: Hybrid, Aether/Body primary, Attraction/Fate secondary"* converts to Body dominant, Attraction secondary. *"Fragmented Horoscopic Crystal"* is old vocabulary; the Crystal is Resonant tier, Fractured state. The card's *"viaforma echoes"* are **Animatria** constructs (Viaforma is the Category of Path evolution and does not produce constructs). *"Psionic"* throughout converts to Gnosis and Animatria work. The card's *"Titan Orrenthal (Harmonic Flame)"* is wrong on two counts: **Orrenthal is a Wellspring, the Metallic Heart, Fulguria,** and it is not among the Fourteen Titans; the bloodline resonance is kept as a claim toward that current and is not a fourth harmonisation. The label *"Withering Age"* converts to the **Withering Era** per the Errata; the card's dates *VE 927* and *VE 925* use an abbreviation no sealed chronology carries and exceed the Year 715 at which the Concordance was sealed, so they are kept as the card's own count and not converted. **Unattested in current canon:** the Division of Resonant Arms (the five Divisions are Strategic, Enforcement, Arbitration, Research and Archives, Logistics and Supply, with the Black Concord unacknowledged), the rank *Aetherblade Lieutenant*, *Aetherborn* as a lineage, Aetherglass, the Path of Self-Containment, the Hall of Shattered Mirrors, the Mirror Hall Event, Lurein Velos, Serenyra Vale, and *Orrenthal's Heir*. The Guild Accord, its academies and its disciplinary hearings are attested. **Values chosen:** Level 208, just past the Band III gate, for a prodigy whose Stage runs ahead of his Level; η ~0.60 at the top of the Expert range. Rik'Lye's card in this same batch records that Kaelric observed his awakening, and the cross-reference is kept on both sheets.

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Fixatio** · Materia. *The Binding Flame.* Covalent cross-linking, the thermoset cure: what it holds is held because the alternative configuration no longer exists as a reachable state. **Kinetic locks and blade-field stabilisation. The Restraint Field is a cure applied to the local Aether for four tenths of a second; opponents read it as a stutter in time.** *Failure: irreversibility. A Fixatio binding placed in error is a permanent feature of whatever it was placed on, and blind rage is when he places them in error.*
**Ascensio** · Vectoria. *The Ladder of Ascent.* Mechanical advantage: the same climb made by a longer path, so the structure never sees the peak stress. **The Leap, and the cognition that perceives time as a spiral rather than a line. His Stage came this way.** *Failure: the work is not reduced, only redistributed. A practitioner who mistakes the absence of crisis for the absence of cost arrives at the next Stage owing the difference. This is the whole of his sheet.*
**Pyreveil** · Caloria. *The Fire of Renewal.* Enthalpy of combustion: energy re-partitioned into forms the system cannot hold in the same shape, with the released heat staying partly resident in the body. **Rebirth, the surge halo, and the reason his tissue does not register the flare as damage until afterward.** *Failure: thermal debt. The resident energy has to leave eventually, and it leaves through whatever tissue is nearest when the working ends: the tremors, the flare-blindness.*
> *Three Families, no two the same. Materia, Vectoria and Caloria are not opposed, and the lattice integrates them at higher cost and lower resonance quality; the card calls the triad volatile, and the volatility is the integration cost showing on the page. The card lists three currents and the sheet keeps three.*

## VIII · Signature

### The Hall of Shattered Mirrors

**Effect** · **A self-generated Domain of reflection in which every surface replicates intent rather than image.** *Animatria constructs seed off every reflective plane inside the radius, each one carrying his swordsmanship on a delay; Fixatio holds the field's Aether so the constructs do not dissolve; Pyreveil supplies the heat that keeps them lit. At full breach it produced twenty-six at once.*
**Cost** · **Every construct is a partial self, and he does not get them back clean.** *Cognitive echo bleed, dissociation, and the whisper of thoughts he did not mean. Afterward: tremors and flare-blindness as the Pyreveil debt leaves through the eyes.*
**Limit** · **Five minutes per construct, ended early by emotional rupture.** A field of mirrors held by a man whose stability is the thing under test lasts exactly as long as his composure. **Ascensio's ledger is still open:** he reached this Stage by the longer path and owes the difference at the next.
**Counter** · **Bring the mirror to him.** *Reflective surfaces amplify his Essence backlash; a construct seeded off a hostile mirror is fed back into a fractured Crystal.* **Or provoke the rage:** Fixatio placed in anger is placed in error, and a binding placed in error is permanent. **Or deny him the ground:** a zone with no Aether to stand on grounds the Leap, and a swordsman who cannot rise is a swordsman fighting on a line rather than a spiral.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the fracture will heal or become a current. *The card holds both: the crack may evolve into a nascent Wellspring, and the reflections are rumoured to whisper the name of a coming Titan, Orrenthal's Heir. The register lists no such Titan, and the sheet records the rumour as the card gives it.*

# Kaelis Aranthor · The Sunbound Blade

> *"You mistake confidence for arrogance. I simply know which way this is going."*
> **Migrated with all content preserved.** **"Aether Class: Momentum Sovereign, Wyther-Dominant Kinetic Manipulator"** was free text built around a misfiled noun. **Wyther is one of the Fourteen Archons** (Motion Magic), not a Wellspring, and cannot be a Triune Core member. His momentum discipline is popularly said to court Wyther's favour. The Wellspring actually doing the mechanical work is **Ascensio, the Ladder of Ascent** (Vectoria, Mechanics), the current his Compound Velocity Trait is built on. His η of 0.90 (a 10% loss) sits at the top edge of **Class III, Resonant's** documented 5 to 10% band. That is ahead of the Class II correlate his Stage VI would normally suggest. It reads as a precocious Shell rather than an error, and is kept as the card states it. The old **Five Pillars** and **Key Combat Parameters** vocabulary (Strike Force in petajoules, Mach-scale speed) are retired as named frameworks; the underlying figures are kept, restated against the Physical Benchmarks table. **Global Tier** and the old three-way **Path Bias** convert to the Four Paths below. **Stage VI, Glory** matches current canon exactly. Level set at 165 (Level Band II), consistent with "Low A Tier." **Sun-Kissed Human, Desert Nomad Blood** returns no named people or culture on the wiki. Only the fact that the Outer World's southern deserts are real geography checks out. It is kept as a regional descriptor and flagged as lightly attested rather than struck, since the card cannot make sense without a homeland. Aurevane, Cataclysm, Mortalis and Judicium are all verified against the Sixty.

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Ascensio** · Vectoria. **Temperance through rising.** *The Ladder of Ascent, and the actual current behind Compound Velocity: momentum crosses Threshold-scale transitions the way a switchback crosses a slope, spreading the force across a longer path so nothing has to take the load all at once.*
**Aurevane** · Fulguria. **The Wellspring of Light.** Perception sharpens under motion; he reads an opening before his own body has finished closing on it.
**Cataclysm** · Caloria. **The Shattered Law.** The Cataclysm Break signature draws directly on this current, all stored motion released in one coherent front rather than scattering.
**Mortalis** · Vitalia (Network). The soul treats an ending, his own or an opponent's, as a structural event rather than a catastrophe, which is most of why he walks into fights smiling.
**Judicium** · Fulguria (Network). Order and consequence, read secondhand rather than harmonized deeply: enough to know which way a contract is going to break before it breaks.

## VI · Traits

**Compound Velocity** · Each successful movement raises the physical output of the next one. The mechanical expression of his Ascensio harmonization.
**Predictive Flow** · Reads combat rhythm instinctively, an Aurevane-Gnosis interaction rather than precognition proper.
**Contract Evasion** · Oath-binding effects weaken against him once he has genuinely rejected the allegiance behind them; a Judicium-adjacent reading rather than immunity.
**Solar Step** *(active, not promoted to Signature)* · Instant reposition that keeps whatever kinetic force he already had stored rather than spending it. His cheapest technique and his most-used one.
**Sundering Arc** *(active, not promoted to Signature)* · A momentum-loaded curved strike built to fracture layered defenses in one pass rather than several.
**Horizon Overdrive** *(temporary state)* · Motion costs halve and his reaction time sharpens for the duration; the state Collapse Feedback exists to punish if it is interrupted mid-run.

## VIII · Signature

### Cataclysm Break

**Effect** · He releases every scrap of stored motion into one directional burst rather than metering it out across the fight. What it hits does not stay standing, and what is behind what it hits frequently does not either.
**Cost** · Everything Compound Velocity has banked across the engagement is spent at once. He starts the next exchange, if there is one, from zero.
**Limit** · It requires stored motion to release. A Kaelis who has been immobilized before he could build any has nothing to spend here.
**Counter** · **Immobilize him early, or fight him somewhere gravity itself misbehaves.** Immobilization effects and gravity-suppression fields both deny Compound Velocity its input before Cataclysm Break ever becomes an option.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether Horizon Overdrive and Cataclysm Break can be chained, or whether the one time it looked like he did it was Collapse Feedback nearly killing him instead.

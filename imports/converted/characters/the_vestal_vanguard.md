# Asaemon · The Vestal Vanguard

> *"True mercy isn't found in the healing of a wound, but in the absolute precision of the cut that prevents its return."*
> **Migrated with all content preserved, including both relics.** *"High-Order Mortal Spirit (Aether-Sensitive)"* is old-system phrasing for a mortal practitioner and is dropped in favour of plain human. *"Order of the Radiant Veil"* and *"Third Era (Post-Wellspring Awakening)"* are not attested; the card's own Trello label reads Voyager Era, and that governs the era. *"Triune Core: Aurevane · Temperance · Equilibrium"* names two non-Wellsprings; **Temperance** and **Equilibrium** are converted to **Judicium** (same Family as Aurevane, truth read as precision) and **Dissolution** (the Unbinding Stream, which is what Severance actually is). His stated *"Wellspring of Origin"* is converted to **Materia Primordia**, the First Substance, the nearest real Wellspring to that name. His Aether Class reads **Class II, Harmonic**, one band above Stage IV's own correlate; his card's stated efficiency is unusually high for his Stage and that gap is stated rather than hidden. His old strike-force figures (petajoule range) are far past what Stage IV can produce and are replaced with the Stage's own benchmark.

## I · Identity

**Name** · Asaemon. **Called** · The Vestal Vanguard. **Also known as** · The Gilded Surgeon, The Silent Pulse.
**Role** · **Combat Medic. Precision Executioner.**
**Gloss rights** · Diagnostic only. He explains a wound, never a working.

### Affiliation

Unaffiliated with any attested order; loosely bound by his own oath rather than a charter, in the Voyager Era. Fights for the Vanguard when a siege calls for him and for no one otherwise. Currently active in the Shattered Peaks, a theatre unattested elsewhere and kept only because the card gives him nowhere else to be.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage IV, Flourishing.** *The first genuine Spirit Axis bond is what Flourishing asks for. Asaemon's formed with the discipline itself, sworn alone through meditative isolation rather than to a person or an order.* **A bond made with a method instead of a face is still a bond, and it is why nobody has ever talked him out of a cut once he has started it.**

## VIII · Signature

### Gilded Pulse: Severance

**Effect** · A wide sweep of golden filaments tuned to the frequency of rejection, aimed at a networked Wellspring rather than a body. It disconnects the Wellspring from its host.
**Cost** · 15,000 EU, nearly half his working reserve at once, and Systemic Brittleness on the far side of it.
**Limit** · Only works on a connection that is already networked. A solitary, unlinked Wellspring has nothing in it for the Pulse to sever.
**Counter** · Chaotic, non-rhythmic Aether has no pulse for Harmonic Sight to read. Overwhelm him with mass rather than precision and the Severance never finds its target.
> **What nobody knows.** What the Concord Severance he is chasing would actually cost him if he ever finished building it.

# Elthiré Maen · The Wandering String

> *"Carry the story, not the glory."*

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Elthiré Maen. **Called** · The Wandering String. **Also** · Keeper of Forgotten Roads.
**Role** · **Guild Accord field operative under the colour of a vagabond minstrel.** *Carries messages, omens and solace across Wellspring-scarred frontiers. His music, neither spell nor sermon, restores rhythm to regions where Essence falters.*
**Gloss rights** · Unlimited. *A performer who explains himself in couplets: "The law binds; the tune reminds." He will name what he does to anyone who asks, and the naming is part of the act.*
**Appearance** · Travelling robes dyed in verdant and saffron layers, the hues of Harmony and Renewal. A small songbird, **Pell**, on his shoulder, echoing fragments of melodies lost to time. Eyes grey-green, the colour of twilight moss, often half-lidded in reflection rather than sleep.

### Affiliation

**Guild Accord Pathwarden**, classified *Echo Courier of the Ninth Route*, one of twelve envoys entrusted to traverse unanchored Wellspring territories and maintain Accord presence through non-militant means. **Origin** · Caedor; later itinerant through the Inner World and Babyl (struck). **Era** · Withering Era, Imperial Age, active from Year 739.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VI, Glory.** *Willingly accepting genuine soul-cost to preserve something valued above one's own structural integrity. The fractures have become features.*
>
> **He reached Glory by listening.** *The card is exact: the endless act of listening, and nothing else, taught him that every emotion carries a tone, and that giving the tone form takes suffering's dominion away.* **The soul-cost is that he carries every tone he has ever given form to. The feature the fracture became is the music.**

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Benediction** · Vitalia. **The Wellspring of Grace.** Binding affinity: a bond with a low dissociation constant spends almost all of its time formed, and Benediction gives bonds on the Plane of Fate that same measurability. **Through Benediction he transforms grief into mercy**, and a room he has played to stays bound to the tone after he has left it. *Failure: competitive inhibition. A rival ligand with higher affinity displaces the bond silently and completely, and nobody feels it until the bond is called on.*
**Verdantia** · Vitalia. **The Green Pulse.** Indeterminate growth and continuous ambient conversion; the practitioner recovers while doing nothing. **Through Verdantia he awakens growth in withered lands**, and same-Family compounding with Benediction is why, when both harmonize, flowers bloom even on stone and old wounds close without word or touch. *Failure: etiolation. Deprived of ambient input the growth continues anyway, length without substance, and he works by choice in the places where Essence falters.*

## VIII · Signature

### Concord Event · *Vinculum Gratiae*

*Concord Event is the Guild's log term. Vinculum Gratiae, "The Bond of Grace," is what Elthiré actually says at release — Accord Latin, drawn flat from Benediction, the Wellspring of Grace, that carries the working's primary law.*
**Effect** · He plays, and **a room's emotional gravity shifts**: grief binds to mercy, a divided crowd synchronizes into one purpose, and where both Wellsprings harmonize, flowers bloom on stone and old wounds close without word or touch. *Benediction sets the bond's affinity; Verdantia feeds it from ambient Essence so the bond keeps growing after the last note.*
**Cost** · **He carries every tone he gives form to.** The card's Glory was reached by listening without end, and a man who has bound a frontier's grief to himself is a man who has not put it down. Worked in Essence-starved ground, Verdantia etiolates: he keeps growing, longer and thinner, with nothing feeding it.
**Limit** · **He must be heard.** The lyre, the air, and time; a Concord Event is minutes of playing, and it eases or aligns. It does not compel. Non-militant by charter.
**Counter** · **A higher affinity.** Give the crowd something it wants more than the peace he is offering (a louder grievance, a beloved voice, gold) and Benediction's bond is displaced silently and completely; he will not know until he calls on it. Or take the sound: a Silentia working, or simply a door.
> **What nobody knows.** What the Ninth Route carries when it carries no song. **Twelve envoys, and the card names one.**

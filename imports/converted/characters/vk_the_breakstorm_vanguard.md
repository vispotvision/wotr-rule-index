# Valen Karth · The Breakstorm Vanguard

> *"He who splits the sky to mend the earth."*
> **Migrated with all content preserved, including both relics and all five traits.** The card reads *"Class V, Resonant Dragoon."* **Resonant is Class III and Dragoon is a job; the numeral stands: he is Class V, Radiant**, which is the Class the table correlates with Band B and a first true Domain, and the card gives him both. *"Path Bias: Body · Aether · Spirit"*: Aether is not one of the four Paths and is struck; **Body dominant, Spirit secondary.** *"Tier: S–"* is read as Accord reputation, not an assay. **η 0.76 sits in the Band A window (0.75 to 0.80) and Stage VIII is Band B; corrected down to ~0.74 by the standing Volume V ruling, the card's figure noted.** Flux Density and Aether Output are retained as raw Pillars figures; the sheet's formula (AU/s = Flux Density × η) does not reproduce the card's 8.2×10⁸ at the corrected η, and the disagreement is flagged, not resolved. The card's turn counts on traits are read as exchanges. **Level 240 is a choice: high in Stage VIII's range for a Legion vanguard commander with two named field actions and a seated Domain, held under the Stage X gate at 300.** *"Grand Convergence, Cycle II"* is not in the Errata and is unattested; the Trello label places him in the **Withering Era**, and *"late Withering campaigns"* converts to that era's own name. **Nevara is struck canon** (Volume V index) and the card's next operation over its skies is recorded as written and flagged dead. **Attested:** the Guild Accord, the Strategic Division and its Legion, the Withering Era, Caedor (New World; an orphaned realm entry pending keep-or-strike), Fixatio, Ascensio, Fractura, Penanceflare, the Draconic Lineage. **Unattested in current canon:** Veyric, Aether-Scaled Draconids, the Division of Martial Conduits, the Bluefront Legion, the Dragoon Corps, the Outer Concord Theatres and Outer Fronts, the Aether Rift Campaign of Caedor, Caedor Ridge, Kaelvar Thren, General Saelri Varn, the Rift Storm Campaign, Operation Stormwake, Riftborn Titans, the Skyroot Wellspring (not among the Sixty), the Walking Rift, the Skyforge Ascendant.

## VIII · Signature

### Divine Dive · "Heavenpiercer"

*The card's Signature Transcendent Technique. Its supporting disciplines: **Tempest Breaker**, converting vertical velocity into a piercing downward spear strike that compresses air into plasma-like Essence (Essence cost 1.3×10⁷ EU, impact-based); **Cyclone Drag**, vacuum funnels beneath his feet that reverse enemy positioning (two exchanges, five to recover); **Fracture Gate**, micro-portals torn with Fractura resonance for re-entry attacks (one exchange per gate, six to recover).*
**Effect** · **He launches upward, compresses Wellspring pressure at the apex, and descends as a single kinetic beam.** *One exchange to cast, one to land. Flux surge of +0.4×10⁹ EU/g during activation (the card's figure).* **The incident record says what it does to ground: the Fracture of Caedor Ridge collapsed three kilometres of Rift terrain after Skyshatter Drive at full output.**
**Cost** · **Penanceflare is the fuel and pain is the price paid up front.** *Overuse leads to emotional dissociation and partial Wellspring rupture, visible as cracks along his arms.* **Exceeding four consecutive leaps risks Resonance Burn: an Essence meltdown lasting one exchange, immobile, at the bottom of a dive he cannot abort.** *His body already shows early signs of Wellspring overpressure; unaddressed, the card says he may become the next Walking Rift.*
**Limit** · **Open sky and atmosphere.** *He cannot sustain momentum indoors or in low-atmospheric-pressure zones; a dive needs height to compress.*
**Counter** · **Fixatio.** *Magnetized, Fixatio-aligned barriers slow his flight, and a slowed Heavenpiercer is a man falling onto a wall that stays held.* **Or reach his temper: emotional turmoil amplifies Fractura instability, and a Fracture Gate opened in a rage opens where it wants to.**
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the Skyroot Wellspring he is hunting, the one rumoured to allow permanent flight, exists at all; it is not among the Sixty.

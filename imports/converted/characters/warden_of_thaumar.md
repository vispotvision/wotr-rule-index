# Garron Vuldane · Warden of Thaumar

> *"The forge never stops burning. Only the weak forget that every spark is a choice."*
> **Migrated with all content preserved.** *"Race: Human (Thaumic-Reforged)"* is not a race tag; he is **human**, reforged by decades of thaumaturgic work into a living furnace. *"Origin Realm: Thalassar, the Walled Forge-Cities"* does not appear anywhere in the wiki mirror; the nearest attested match, a walled Dawi forge-state that also contains a human minority, is **Stannvaard, the Guild Seat of the Metal-Wardens of the Ring**, and his origin is relocated there. *"Temperance Path: Stage IX — Reflection"* names no Stage that exists; Stage IX is **Invocation**, and the correction is made here. *"Division of Alchemetrica: Thaumaturgic Wardens"* does not exist under that name; he is filed under **the Alchemists' Society**, chartered beneath the Guild Accord's Research and Archives Division, seated at Stannvaard, and *"Thaumaturgic Warden"* is kept as his own field epithet. *"Rank: T7 Master Alchemist"* conflicts with his stated Stage IX, which the Tiered Path places in **Tier 6, Master**, not Tier 7's Mythic cluster; the rank is corrected. *"Aether Class: Transmutive Soul — Warden Mode"* is not among the seven; he is **Class III, Resonant**.

## I · Identity

**Name** · Garron Vuldane. **Called** · The Iron Warden, Warden of Thaumar.
**Role** · **Master Alchemist. Siegewright. Thaumaturgic Warden of the Grand Forges.**
A broad, mountain-born figure in layered scaleplate, his iron-gray mane blending into a metallic fur of living Aethersteel that answers his pulse. His transmutation cannon is engraved with runes that shift color as he channels Essence through it.

### Affiliation

Guild Accord, **the Alchemists' Society**, chartered under the Research and Archives Division, seated at **Stannvaard**. **Tier 6 · Master · Warden of Duty**, carried day to day as Master Alchemist and Siegewright.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage IX, Invocation.** *Wellsprings and Titanic law begin answering as co-authors rather than sources to be drawn from, and the Domain a soul has been building starts to consolidate into something closer to a Realm.*
>
> **Garron's co-author is his own forge.** He carries a portable Forge-Wellspring inside his body rather than beside it, and the two constructs he raised, Harn and Vyr, are what it looks like when the arrangement starts producing more than one voice.

## VIII · Signature

### Bound Synthesis

**Effect** · Fuses his two bound constructs, Harn and Vyr, into a single combat form, the Forge Chimera, amplifying his output for one minute.
**Cost** · The fusion draws directly on his portable Forge-Wellspring; sustained past the minute it calcifies his veins and risks Core Backfire.
**Limit** · Both constructs must be within his Aura and undamaged to the point of severed connection. If either is disabled first, the fusion cannot be called.
**Counter** · Separate him from one construct before the fusion is called, or simply outlast the fixed one-minute window. The Forge Chimera reverts on the clock, not on his will.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether Harn and Vyr have a will of their own inside the fused form, or whether it is entirely his.

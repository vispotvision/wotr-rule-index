# Resonant Divination

> The old card ("Divination Harmonics," a moon-and-fate essay for the pre-Fracture board) converts onto Branch Three of the current canon page *The Harmonic Arts*: Resonant Divination, Fate Reading. Every institution the card names checked out against that page almost word for word: the Sonochoral Conclave is live canon and does exactly the Dissonance Warning work the card gave it, Izathen the Broken Lyre and his Echo Fracture of 8,000 AF are attested verbatim, and Caelmorne Halvrein is a real figure, though canon files him as *He Whose Silence Was Sung by the Moon* himself, so the card's second figure, Serenhal Caelmorne, appears to be a duplicate split off the same person and is dropped as unattested. "Age of Grace" does not appear anywhere in the Concordance of Ages or the wiki mirror and is replaced with the dating *The Harmonic Arts* actually gives (Second Epoch origin, no further era claimed for formalization); "Age of Calamity" and "Voyager Era" are both attested current eras and stand unchanged. "Essence Drafts," "Wellspring Paths," and "spiritual signature" are folded into current vocabulary (Drafts, Wellspring harmonisation, Resonant Tone). Numbers throughout (the Temperance Gates) are copied from *The Harmonic Arts* directly, not estimated.

## What it is

Resonant Divination is the third of the three Applied Branches of the Harmonic Arts, the discipline of reading the Continuum's ambient vibration rather than producing it. Where Anima Harmonics reads a soul's own tone and Thaumic Harmonics reads a working's tone, Resonant Divination reads the tonal state of the causal web itself: the Attraction Force currents that run along every bond, oath, debt, and recognition between two presences on the Plane of Fate.

It does not produce prophecy. It does not fix a future as a settled narrative. What it returns is a probability assessment of what is currently vibrating, which connections are strong, which are weakening, which are approaching resonant convergence, and which are trending toward Echo Fracture. Free will, intervention, and the Continuum's own response to novel inputs can all move the trajectory the current reading implies, so a Diviner's report is a weather forecast on causality, not a verdict.

## Where it sits in the current system

**Category** · Harmonia (Spirit ↔ Attraction), one of the twenty-six Magical Categories on *The Magical Categories — Unified Taxonomy* page, alongside secondary support from Aetherica (the measurement substrate that lets a reading be quantified at all) and Mnemata (the memory-echo layer a Bond Reading draws on to trace a connection's history).

**Family** · Not tied to a single Wellspring or Family. A Diviner's tone-perception plays across whichever Wellsprings the read connections happen to run through; the discipline is a mode of listening laid over the Sixty rather than a current drawn from one of them.

**Craft** · Spellcraft. A reading is sustained by the practitioner's own attention (Resonant Perception held open) and lapses the moment that attention breaks, which is exactly the durability profile Spellcraft carries under the current Craft rules. The old card's "chant in tonal sequences" line for the Sonochoral Conclave is retained as the Conclave's working style rather than filed under the retired Chantcraft register, which is folded into Spellcraft as of the ruling of 2026-09-12.

**Current pages** · *The Harmonic Arts* (The Disciplines) is the governing entry; *The Magical Categories — Unified Taxonomy* (The Magic System) gives the Category framework; *The Veil* (Cosmology & Metaphysics) is the deepest layer a Fate Entanglement reading ultimately touches, since Attraction Force currents are Plane-of-Fate phenomena that surface through the Veil's threshold.

## Practitioners and history

The roots reach to the Second Epoch, when Parunic monks decoding the tonal structure of glyph chants discovered that spoken phonemes carried measurable vibration and that a glyph's meaning intensified whatever it was chained with. This is attested directly on *The Harmonic Arts* and replaces the card's "First discovered... by Parunic monks decoding the tonal structures of glyph chants," which needed no conversion at all beyond dropping the unattested "Age of Grace" formalization date. Formalization as a distinct combat- and civic-capable discipline came later, through the same current that produced Thalos of Ira's codification of Thaumic Harmonics and Aurevia the Echowright's proof that tonal work could function in a fight; Resonant Divination shares that lineage rather than a separate founder of its own on record.

**The Sonochoral Conclave** is confirmed canon: a secretive guild of Resonant Diviners who combine all three Harmonic branches into coordinated tonal operations, and whose primary service to the Concord is Dissonance Warning at institutional scale, monitoring the tonal health of infrastructure, relationships, and organizations to catch collapse-in-progress before it becomes catastrophic. This is the card's "chant in tonal sequences to alter battlefield frequencies and manipulate spirit flow" institution, narrowed to what canon actually attests it doing.

**Izathen the Broken Lyre** is confirmed canon, unchanged from the card in substance: a theoretically sound Harmonist whose self-directed harmonic modification looped his own Resonant Perception back into his own tone, producing **the Echo Fracture of 8,000 AF**, the catastrophic upper bound the whole discipline studies as its central danger. He can no longer hear anything but the endlessly reverberating echo of his own modified tone. Standard curriculum since is summarized on the source page itself as *do not block the spell; break the song it is trying to sing.*

**Caelmorne Halvrein**, carried over from the card, is confirmed as a real figure, but canon assigns him to the Anima Harmonics branch and gives him the epithet the card split into a second person: *He Whose Silence Was Sung by the Moon*, for his practice of conducting sessions during lunar Aether tides, when ambient Resonance naturally quiets. The card's separate "Serenhal Caelmorne — He Whose Silence Was Sung by the Moon" is therefore an unattested duplicate of the same man and is not carried forward as an independent figure.

**Genn Arkelos**, the card's guild-certified Frequencywright who diagnosed leyline anomalies during the Imperial Convergences, has no matching record anywhere in the wiki mirror. He is kept in this entry only because the card's account of early institutional divination work reads thinner without a named field diagnostician, and is flagged here as *unattested in current canon*.

## What it can and cannot do

A Diviner's four attested applications, each gated by Temperance Stage exactly as the source page states them:

- **Bond Reading** (Stage VIII) — maps a subject's relational network: who they are connected to, how strongly, through what kind of recognition, and whether the bond is consonant or under strain. This is the diagnostic tool behind Concord soul forensics.
- **Fate Entanglement Analysis** — identifies destiny-level convergences, trajectories locked together by currents too strong for ordinary intervention to deflect; they read louder and more complex than an ordinary bond, with overtones suggesting multiple Wellspring processes converging on one point in time.
- **Resonant Tracking** — follows the fading frequency a severed bond leaves behind to locate the disconnected party and, in some cases, when and how the bond was cut.
- **Convergence Forecasting** (Stage X) — projects which presences are moving toward resonant convergence and what happens when they arrive at the same frequency, days or weeks in advance at skilled hands.
- **Dissonance Warning**, the Sonochoral Conclave's civic specialty, extends to Attraction Force resonance tracking at Stage XII.

What it cannot do: settle a fixed future. The reading is a probability drawn off the present state of the causal web, not a narrative the Continuum has already written, and every one of the applications above degrades or misreads the instant a targeted connection is deliberately masked, severed cleanly, or run through a Thick Veil zone where Harmonia perception is degraded.

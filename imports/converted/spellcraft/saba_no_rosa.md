# Saba no Rosa

> **Migration note.** Kept every fact the card gives: Bara Moto's authorship, the single teaching to Muken on the seventy-third day, the six-Wellspring "drowning garden" structure, the four worked examples (duelist, king, army, god-aspected mortal), and the Shingan synergy. Bara Moto, Muken (written here as **Muken Moto**, per the Moto register and his attested card as King of Kharven), and the **Shingan** all verified against current canon (`character("Muken Moto")`, `wiki("The Moto Bloodline")`, `wiki("Shingan")`). Five of the card's six Wellsprings verify clean against the Sixty: **Anamnesis** (Limina), **Mortalis** and **Contrition** (both Vitalia), **Fractura** (Vectoria). The card's "**Monlithion**" is a near-miss of the canonical **Monolithion** (Materia, *The Enduring Monument*) and is corrected throughout; this same misspelling appears on Niran Yukari's own character card, which is a standing inconsistency in the corpus and not one this pass resolves. The card's "**Aether Sangui**" does not appear anywhere in the Sixty and is unattested in current canon; since the Work cannot make lawful sense without a medium-Wellspring, it is replaced with **Phreatis** (Fluxia, *The Waters of Memory*), which already pairs with Anamnesis in the current Mnemata taxonomy and keeps the card's "sea" and "medium drawn from the practitioner's own reserves" language intact. "Essence-type" and "lawful" old-system framing is kept only as flavour language describing a Design Chain now made explicit below. Numbers: the card gave none, so Stage floor, Grade and governing stats are chosen conservatively from the FOW tables and committed to, not deferred.

## Summary card

**Effect** · Bara Moto's discipline. The practitioner reads what a target's present position is still lawfully drawing on from their own past, then withdraws that deposit's standing rather than touching the body it supports.
**Cost** · The medium is drawn from the practitioner's own reserves, never the target's; Phreatis running a large working for a long time empties a real reserve, and running it on a stolen draw turns the medium hostile within the same exchange.
**Limit** · Only reaches deposits that are genuinely load-bearing right now; a present built on nothing the target still relies on has nothing for the Work to find.
**Counter** · A target's own standing can be reinforced from outside faster than the Work can dilute it; a fresh, freely given recognition (a name spoken by someone who still means it, a debt paid in front of witnesses) restores the deposit's currency before the withdrawal completes.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether Bara has ever used Saba no Rosa on himself, and if the ledger it would read is a First Epoch's or a man's.

## Design Chain

**Trigger** · The practitioner identifies a specific present condition (a stance, a throne, an army's willingness, a divine corridor) and confirms it is still being paid for by a named past deposit.
**Function** · Raises a bounded field ("the sea") in which each rose is one surfaced deposit and each thorn is the unpaid weight against it.
**Mechanism** · Anamnesis reads the target's causal ledger and surfaces the load-bearing entries. Fractura finds and widens the existing seam around each entry without snapping the wider timeline. Contrition raises the unpaid weight of the entry to the surface so the withdrawal has a lawful basis rather than being theft. Mortalis governs the threshold across which the deposit's answering is withdrawn from the target's coordinate — the deposit is not destroyed, it stops answering to this claimant. Phreatis supplies and circulates the medium, paid from the practitioner's own reserves rather than drawn from the target or the field. Monolithion ratifies the withdrawal as a standing fact the target's own timeline cannot quietly refile.
**Numerical Effect** · X-Grade output (Stage XIII ceiling, stat ceiling 1200); a single rose against one deposit runs in the low end of that Grade, a field-wide working against a fielded army or a throne-room runs at the Grade's upper bound. Duration of a withdrawal is sustained rather than instantaneous — the field must be held while Mortalis completes the threshold crossing.
**Target Response** · Whatever the deposit was supporting degrades over the following exchanges rather than failing instantly: a stance reverts to what it was before the teacher's correction, a throne stops recognising its occupant, an army stops remembering why it is willing to charge, a divine corridor closes without the god at the far end being offended.
**Consequence** · The target is not struck, cut, or unmade. They are separated from a specific piece of their own supported history and left standing on what remains.
**Limitation** · Scales with the richness of the target's own past, not with the practitioner's personal strength; a target with almost no load-bearing history (a very young soul, a construct, a thing without inheritance) gives the Work almost nothing to surface.
**Weakness** · Requires Phreatis's own reserve to be sufficient for the field raised; a practitioner who tries to draw the medium from the target or the surrounding field instead of their own reserve finds the sea turning hostile and the thorns turning inward on the next exchange.
**Cost** · Paid entirely from the practitioner's own Phreatis reserve; there is no cost to the target beyond the deposit itself, which is the discipline's own claim to lawfulness.
**Counterplay** · Reinforce the deposit from outside before Mortalis completes the threshold crossing. Because the Work works by lawful withdrawal rather than force, a deposit that is freshly and freely re-recognised (a father calling a son by name, a debt paid openly, an oath re-sworn before witnesses) restores its standing faster than the dilution can take hold.

## Origin

Derived by Bara Moto, First Epoch given human form and founder of the Moto bloodline, at a point in his authorship of Hataraki when he judged that a Work built purely on withdrawal rather than force was needed among the Works he carried. Taught exactly once, to Muken Moto on the seventy-third day of instruction beneath the lower lattice of the Plane of Fate, with the explicit warning that it should not be taught onward to anyone who has not first learned to grieve correctly. No second student is recorded in the card, and none is invented here.

# The Iron Tree

> **Migration note.** The card describes eight named limb-branches plus one apex release, all belonging to **Dougou Ozumu Zettai** and his **True Iron Bison** (`Shinsei Tetsugyū`). Both Dougou and the parent discipline verify clean against current canon: Dougou has his own character card, and **The Iron Bison — A Discipline of Internal Resonance** is a live Notion page describing the same seven-chamber, refuse-to-spend architecture the card assumes. Kept every fact the card gives about what each branch does and its stated range; converted the seven chambers to the discipline's four canonical ones plus the two the card invents ("shoulder-line") folded into **Heart** and **Solar Plexus**, since canon names only Crown, Brow, Throat, Heart, Solar Plexus, Sacral and Root. The card's Wellsprings ("Fixatio," "Coagulatio," implied earth-anchoring) are replaced with Dougou's actual attested harmonisations — **Fixatio** primary, **Coagulatio** secondary, **Chthonica** tertiary, **Anima Spirare** and **Phreatis** in support — rather than invented ones. **Kokushin Gyūha** is already a canon technique on Dougou's own card at 5–12 MN / 7,400 EU; kept as written and cross-checked rather than reinvented. The seven named limb-branches (Tetsu no Kobushi, Geri, Hiji, Hiza, Kata, Atama, Ho) do not appear anywhere else in the wiki and are flagged **unattested** below; they are kept because the card cannot make sense without them and because they slot cleanly under the discipline's existing chamber system. Numbers: the card's Newton and Joule figures for each branch disagree with each other against the FOW Strike Force table (e.g. 1–12 MN sits at E/D-Grade force while 1–20 MJ sits at D/C-Grade energy) — this same mismatch already exists on Dougou's own canon card (12,000,000 N and 8–20 MJ together), so it is an inherited canon inconsistency, not one introduced here. Energy yield is used as the grading axis since it is the figure canon already privileges on Dougou's card; Grade is committed to below rather than left open.

## What it is

The Iron Tree is the offensive branch system grown out of the True Iron Bison, Dougou Ozumu Zettai's sealed, refuse-to-spend body-engine. Where the Iron Bison discipline governs containment (the seven chambers converting Essence to stored density rather than radiating it outward), the Iron Tree governs release: each branch is a single controlled valve through which one increment of sealed pressure is allowed to leave the body through one limb, for one instant, without breaking the containment that makes the rest of the discipline work. Nine branches are recorded: **Tetsu no Kobushi** (Iron Fist), **Tetsu no Geri** (Iron Kick), **Tetsu no Hiji** (Iron Elbow), **Tetsu no Hiza** (Iron Knee), **Tetsu no Kata** (Iron Shoulder), **Tetsu no Atama** (Iron Head), **Tetsu no Ho** (Iron Step), and the apex fruit of the whole tree, **Kokushin Gyūha** (Black-Heart Bison Break).

## Where it sits in the current system

**Family** · Materia primary, Vitalia secondary, Fluxia tertiary — inherited directly from the parent Iron Bison entry, since the Iron Tree draws on the same three-Family loop rather than a separate one.
**Category** · Traitus for the standing chamber architecture itself (a permanent structural fact of Dougou's Crystal), expressed on demand through Vectra (motion, velocity, impact delivery) for each individual release.
**Craft** · Magicraft (Corporis method — no chant, no sigil, no external projection; the body itself is the instrument).
**Stage floor** · Stage VII (Refraction) for preliminary branch training, Stage IX (Dominion) for sustained combat use of any branch, Stage XII (Emanation) for indefinite maintenance under duress — all three thresholds carried over unchanged from the parent Iron Bison entry. Dougou himself operates at his attested **Stage XIII, Principality**, above every listed floor.
**Grade** · D-Grade at normal release (0.3–7 MJ band covers every branch's stated baseline output), rising to **C-Grade** at apex chamber release (7 MJ–1.046 GJ band covers every branch's stated apex output, and matches Kokushin Gyūha's canon figures exactly).

## Practitioners and history

Founded and, so far as the record shows, solely mastered by Dougou Ozumu Zettai, Founder of the True Iron Bison. The parent discipline's own literature notes that most who attempt the underlying loop hold only Fixatio and Anima Spirare and are called half-sealed rather than adherents; nothing in the record names a second practitioner who has run the full Iron Tree branch system rather than the containment loop alone. Rengai Zettai, Dougou's son, is attested as having built an ash-and-attrition discipline explicitly separate from his father's, which the record reads as evidence of what Dougou actually taught him: not to seal everything, but a warning clear enough that Rengai chose a different answer.

## What it can and cannot do

| Branch | Range | Output | Function and counterplay |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tetsu no Kobushi** · Iron Fist | 0–3 m | D-Grade / C-Grade at apex | Compression driven inward through the fist rather than released outward; collapses ribs and armour plate from within. Countered by refusing contact or by structures built to distribute rather than resist a point load. |
| **Tetsu no Geri** · Iron Kick | 0–5 m | D-Grade / C-Grade at apex | Displacement and terrain damage rather than inward rupture; launches heavy targets and craters ground. Countered by staying airborne or off the ground it strikes. |
| **Tetsu no Hiji** · Iron Elbow | 0–1 m | D-Grade | Near-zero wind-up strike for grapple range; punishes anyone who closes to clinch distance. Countered by not entering the range at all. |
| **Tetsu no Hiza** · Iron Knee | 0–1.5 m | D-Grade | Rising strike through the Sacral and Root chambers; answers anyone already grabbed, pinned or staggered. Countered by breaking the grapple before the knee lands. |
| **Tetsu no Kata** · Iron Shoulder | 0–3 m | D-Grade | Broad-body mass collision rather than a point strike; breaks formations, gates and braced stances. Countered by refusing to hold ground against it. |
| **Tetsu no Atama** · Iron Head | 0–0.5 m | D-Grade | Used rarely, at the closest possible range, chiefly to end a clinch and disorient. Countered, as with the Elbow, by never closing that far. |
| **Tetsu no Ho** · Iron Step | Self / 5–80 m shock radius | Variable, D-Grade at its upper stated range | Footwork pressure rather than a strike; ruins enemy footing and anchors Dougou against knockback. Countered by staying off the ground he has planted on. |
| **Kokushin Gyūha** · Black-Heart Bison Break *(apex, canon)* | Contact | C-Grade (5–12 MN, 7,400 EU) | One sealed chamber's full release through a single contact point, delivered from Fist, Shoulder or Knee. Attested counter: spatial bypass, or Spirit Path severance — the one category of attack the sealed architecture cannot process. |

Across every branch, the Iron Tree shares the parent discipline's structural weaknesses: it needs contact or near-contact, it is strongest with Dougou grounded, it loses reach against airborne opponents, and Spirit Path attacks and void or anti-recognition fields bypass the physical branch system entirely rather than being blocked by it. Overuse of any branch strains the same containment the whole discipline depends on, and emotional instability, the one input Fixatio's seal was never built to process cleanly, risks pressure leakage across all seven chambers at once.

# The Veil

## What it is

The Veil is not a place. It is a condition, the state of being between planes, the liminal membrane where one plane's law softens into another's. Reality runs on three sovereign planes: the **Physical Plane** (matter and mana, where form takes shape), the **Soul Plane** (souls and spiritual energy, the stratum of continuity), and the **Plane of Fate** (spirits and Attraction Force, where recognition and destiny are held). The Veil is none of these; it is the threshold between them, where matter thins enough to reveal soul, where spiritual energy bleeds through into audible frequency, where Attraction Force currents become perceptible to senses that normally register only form. Its formal name in Aetherica literature is the **Spiritual Stratum**, and this is the direct current-canon source of the card's "Spiritual Stratum" title.

A separate, older sense of "the Veil" survives only as a name: one of the feeling-sorted Houses (alongside the Green Pulse and the Black Gate) that pre-Family registers used to group Wellsprings by how they felt rather than by physics. That House maps loosely onto today's Somnalis, Oneirion, Eidolyn, Anamnesis, Tenebra, and Mortalis, Wellsprings of dream, memory, shadow, and death across Fluxia, Limina, and Vitalia, and it is a historical label only. It does not map onto the eight Families and it is not the cosmological Veil this entry is about, though the old card blurs the two, as old registers often did.

## Where it sits in the current system

**Governing Wellspring** · Transference (Spatium, Spatial Geometry) is the Veil's primary governing current: lawful passage through the liminal medium, the physics of two coupled systems exchanging energy without a conductor between them. Secondary associations run through Somnalis (Fluxia), Oneirion, Eidolyn, Anamnesis, and Tenebra (Limina), and Mortalis (Vitalia), matching the dream, memory, shadow, and death-adjacent character the card's "Wellspring Paths" language was reaching for.

**Governing Titan** · Sylorin, the Ferryman of Etherea, who governs the Ethereal Flow, the current that carries Essence between death and re-embodiment through the Veil rather than on any single plane.

**Associated Realms** · Etherea (Passage/Soulstream) and Virelion (Dream/Perception), not the card's "Wellspring Realms" and "Utopia." Utopia is a real place in current canon, home to the Celestial Host and its own arbitration realm Midreach, but it is not attested as one of the Veil's connected architecture; the card's claim that the Veil bridges to it is dropped rather than carried forward unverified.

**Related Disciplines** · Transference Crossing, Mnemata, Harmonia, Animatria, Vocatia, and Ritus all operate through or across the Veil, which is the current-canon basis for the card's claim that "every act of Animancy, Spirit Summoning, and Temperance ripples across its folds."

**Current pages** · *The Veil* (Cosmology & Metaphysics) is the governing entry; *The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings* supplies the Transference Family assignment and the note on the retired Houses; *The Spirit Summoning Arts* and *The Harmonic Arts* are the neighboring Disciplines pages that operate through the Veil rather than on a single plane.

## Practitioners and history

**Rikudoku Moto** carries the card's most striking claim: that he manipulated deep Veil layers to fracture spiritual continuity across battlefields, and that his soul was rumored to drift between folds even after death. The name is genuinely attested in current canon, named a philosophical ally on two other characters' sheets, but the wiki mirror itself flags him as needing re-identification before anything about him hardens into fact. The card's specific claim is kept here as an unverified tradition attributed to him, not as settled history.

**Eremund Vellsore**, credited with developing the first known method of Veil-thread weaving through song, and **the Bound Watchers**, the Veil-bound entities the card places at the outer edge of the Astral Fold to sit in judgment over oaths and Temperance rituals, have no matching record anywhere in the wiki mirror. Both are kept because the card's account of who works the Veil and who judges its crossings reads thinner without them, and both are flagged **unattested in current canon**.

**"The Great Spirit War,"** which the card credits with producing the first Echo Beasts through Veil breaches, is not attested as a named conflict anywhere in the wiki mirror, though Echo Beasts and Veil ruptures are themselves fully confirmed canon. This entry generalizes the claim to what canon actually supports: Veil ruptures, whatever their historical cause, are the origin of Echo Beasts, entities formed when unresolved spiritual content (grief, guilt, unprocessed memory, abandoned vows) passes through a torn boundary and condenses on the Physical Plane side. They are not summoned creatures. They are wounds given form, and none are conscious in any meaningful sense; a grief-born Echo Beast wanders and weeps, a guilt-born one fixates on the site of the original transgression, a rage-born one is violent and indiscriminate.

## What it can and cannot do

The Veil is not uniform; its thickness varies by local metaphysical condition.

- **Thin Veil zones** (near active Wellspring veins, ancient ritual sites, sites of mass death) let Bifold Perception occur spontaneously even at moderate Temperance, sharpen Mnemata and Harmonia readings, and make dreams more vivid, at the cost of being where ruptures most easily occur and where unprotected minds are most vulnerable to memory bleed and Soul Drift.
- **Thick Veil zones** (major cities, ward-heavy sites) degrade all of the above but are correspondingly safer for unprotected populations, insulating against accidental spiritual contact.
- **Veil ruptures** are structural failures, not controlled crossings, produced by Overcast Collapse, Phenomena-class discharges, failed high-order Ritus, or the death of a load-bearing World Spirit. A single practitioner, regardless of Temperance, cannot seal a significant rupture alone; containment is Concordia-level work requiring multiple Aether bodies aligned into one coherent field.

Access scales by Temperance Stage, gated exactly as the current page states it: Passive Veil Perception is universal and unconscious; involuntary sensitivity to it arrives at Stage V (Splintering), with disorientation and memory bleed as the risk; controlled Bifold Perception at Stage VII (Refraction), risking loss of coherence across two perceptual grammars at once; short deliberate crossings at Stage VIII (Transcendence), accruing incremental Soul Drift; sustained Bifold operation at Stage IX (Invocation), risking identity degradation; sustained liminal operation at Stage X (Realization), where the Physical Plane itself begins to feel thin; and carrying another person through a crossing at Stage XII (Emanation), which transfers Drift to the carried as well as the carrier. What it cannot do, at any Stage: let a soul actually inhabit two planes' native logic at once without cost. The Physical Plane and Soul Plane do not share a visual grammar, and every mode of Veil access above pays for what it reveals in coherence, drift, or accumulated damage to the practitioner's own sense of self.

# Auric Parallax Veil

> Converted from an OPALIS ability card on the old Trello board. The card's primary/secondary Wellspring split, its selective-negation/reinforcement/refraction-break function, its 400 m line-of-sight range and 60 m sheet area, and its named limits, weaknesses and counters are kept as given. "Temperance XII, Emanation, The Path of Self-as-Law" converts to FOW Stage XII, Emanation; "The Path of Self-as-Law" is the card's own gloss on what committing to Attraction Path at that Stage feels like from inside, not a fifth Path, and is not reproduced as a separate mechanical field. The old free-text "Aether Class: Harmonic Reality-Phase Articulation" is a caster attribute and is not reproduced as a technique field; its "reshapes the rules" language reads as Class V, the Radiant, offered for Opalis's own sheet rather than settled here. All ten source Wellspring names (Basilithe, Fractura, Tenebra as primary; Oneirion, Penance, Petralon, Orrenthal, Tarturon, Cataclysm, Dissolution as secondary) and the two named weaknesses (Hypnather, Eidolyn) checked against the current Sixty and are attested without alteration; Basilithe is kept as primary Wellspring exactly as the card states it. Numerical Effect is read off the SSS-Grade row of the FOW Strike Force table, since the card gives no numeric force value of its own; it is used here as the ceiling on what the Veil's law-selection can override, not as a discharge the Veil itself deals.

## Summary card

**Effect** · Opalis parts a thin plane of refracting light and shadow across up to 60 m, and whichever of an event's already-possible outcomes she prefers becomes the one that happens; nothing new is conjured, only chosen.
**Cost** · Holding several of her own possible selves in mind at once while the Veil stands leaves her disoriented and unfocused for a time; the Veil itself leaves thin fractures in her internal law-harmonics that need meditation or a Penance-aligned equilibrium rite to close.
**Limit** · It cannot stabilize and destabilize the same stretch of space at the same time, and it cannot originate an outcome that was never a possible branch to begin with.
**Counter** · A field that flattens probability or erases harmonic variance outright gives the Veil nothing to select between; an attacker who deliberately manufactures more concurrent outcome-states than the Veil can hold overwhelms and shatters it.
**What nobody knows** · Whether "the version reality already knows what it wants to be" is a fact about the world or a fact about Opalis; nobody has found a case where the two come apart cleanly enough to tell.

## Design Chain

**Trigger** · Opalis makes a single slow parting gesture toward a region she designates within line of sight.
**Function** · A thin prismatic sheet of light and shadow forms across the designated space and either negates, reinforces, or redirects the outcome of whatever is entering or occurring there, by collapsing existing alternate branches toward the one she prefers.
**Mechanism** · Fractura's refraction law is used to hold an event's several physically possible outcome-paths open at once, the way a double-slit interference pattern holds more than one path until something forces a result; Basilithe's law-lock then fixes which fringe of that pattern is real, and Tenebra's absorption cancels the signal of the branches not chosen, the way destructive interference removes an unwanted path rather than merely hiding it.
**Numerical Effect** · SSS-Grade ceiling: 1-5 PN peak force, 418 TJ to 4.184 EJ energy yield, 100 kt to 1 Tt TNT-equivalent (Large Town to Small Country scale) is the largest event the Veil can select the outcome of at Stage XII; it is a ceiling on what may be overridden, not a discharge the Veil itself releases.
**Target Response** · An incoming attack or working caught under Selective Negation flickers and resolves into its own less harmful or self-erasing branch; a region under Reality Reinforcement holds its physical laws at amplified strength against intrusion; a target under Refraction Break finds its own possible motion-paths split and is steered into the least favourable one without ever being touched.
**Consequence** · Committing the Veil to reinforce a space forecloses using it to destabilize that same space until the first working ends; there is no way to run both at once.
**Limitation** · It cannot create an event that was not already a possible branch; it cannot override another Stage XII will outright, only distort a weaker harmonic; it cannot hold two contradictory Veil-states over the same ground.
**Weakness** · A pure Basilithe construct with its own hard law-lock resists having its outcome selected out from under it; Hypnather- or Eidolyn-aligned dream-breaking Wellsprings can corrupt which branch the Veil thinks it chose; an overwhelmingly massive Petralon-aligned presence can brute-force through it, though at a changed cost to whoever forced the passage.
**Cost** · Harmonic Divergence is the disorientation of briefly experiencing her own several possible selves at once; Structural Fatigue is the thin fracturing the Veil leaves in her own law-harmonics, requiring meditation or a Penance-aligned rite to repair.
**Counterplay** · Null-Law suppression fields that flatten probability outright deny the Veil anything to select between; deliberately generating more concurrent outcome-states than the Veil can carry (a heavy Fractura working, for instance) overloads and shatters it.

## Origin

The card gives no inventor, workshop, or precipitating problem beyond Opalis and the Stage XII requirement. Per the named-inventor rule this is a self-derived technique, to be shown in full rather than summarised the first time it is used against someone who has not seen it. The card's silence on its history is preserved rather than filled in.

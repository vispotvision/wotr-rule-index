# Celestial Harmonic Shear

> Converted from the Trello card "CELESTIAL HARMONIC SHEAR" (Opalis). Same bearer as the other two Emanation-tier cards in this batch: attested in current canon only as the one-line legend "Opalis Maethryn, the Radiant Arbiter" in *The Concord Military*, with no character sheet anywhere in the wiki mirror, so numbers below are conservative estimates off the Fracture of Worlds tables, not sheet values. "Temperance XII — Emnation" converts to Stage XII, Emanation, Band S, Grade ceiling SSS, stat ceiling 950. The free-text "Aether Class: Emanant Harmonic Law-Shear Manifestation" is read down to Class V, the Radiant, kept consistent with the Lance and Null-Ascension cards for the same bearer. "Path — Self-as-Law, Harmonic Sovereignty" is not a Path; a projected line-shaped cut reads as Spirit Path. All named Wellsprings (Orrenthal, Fractura, Basilithe primary; Dissolution, Tarturon, Cataclysm, Tenebra, Penance, Petralon, Oneirion secondary) check against the current Sixty, and "Cymorathian" in the weakness list is the adjectival form of the correctly spelled Fulguria Wellspring Cymorath. "The Iridescent Archive" destination is the old board's own filing language, kept only as a folder name and not treated as an in-world place.

## Summary card

**Effect** · Opalis draws a line through the air, and anything whose own frequency crosses that line loses cohesion exactly where it was touched; wards, illusions and layered constructs break all at once rather than one layer at a time.
**Cost** · Internal resonance leaks outward as she cuts, leaving her briefly dizzy and tone-deaf to certain currents, and a large or complex cut can send a backlash into her own law-vector that weakens her other Emanation-level workings for a short while.
**Limit** · Cannot cut the harmonic of a being with equal or greater harmonic sovereignty, cannot touch anything operating outside harmonic physics at all (true void, full dream-essence, anti-tone fields), and cannot produce more than one Shear at full potency in a single activation.
**Counter** · A silence field, a stilled instant, or a target holding several contradictory frequencies at once denies the Shear a single clean frequency to find and cut.
**What nobody knows.** Whether Opalis is cutting the frequency that is actually there, or the one she has decided is the lie underneath it.

## Design Chain

**Trigger** · Opalis raises one hand and draws a slow line downward or outward through the air.
**Function** · Identifies the exact frequency holding a structure, working or Essence pattern together, and cuts along it rather than through the matter it happens to be shaped as.
**Mechanism** · The real phenomenon underneath is resonant decoupling: any bound structure is held together by a characteristic vibrational mode, and severing that mode rather than the material itself is the same principle a shattered wineglass or a delaminated composite demonstrates, failure along the resonance rather than along the visible shape. Orrenthal supplies the harmonic-law reading that finds the true frequency; Fractura and Basilithe decide whether the cut resolves as splitting divergence or as a clean geometric line through law-bound structure; Dissolution supplies the option of collapse into null-phase rather than a visible seam.
**Numerical Effect** · Range up to 300 m, line-of-sight, delivered as a line up to 100 m long and under 3 cm wide; at the required SSS-Grade the cut sits at the conservative low end of the benchmark band, on the order of 1 PN peak force and roughly 400 TJ yield along the line, an estimate chosen per Law Two since the card supplies no force figure. Resolution is instantaneous, with resonance cracks lingering a few seconds along the cut.
**Target Response** · Any harmonic structure intersecting the line loses cohesion at the point of intersection; if the target relies on several layered harmonics at once, all of them break simultaneously rather than in sequence.
**Consequence** · The disrupted harmonic reflects back into the target, producing internal fractures or an energetic backlash on top of the direct loss of cohesion.
**Limitation** · Cannot cut the harmonic of a being at or above Opalis's own Temperance-level harmonic sovereignty, cannot touch anything operating outside harmonic physics entirely, and produces at most one Shear at full potency per activation.
**Weakness** · A structure anchored in pure Basilithe can resist or redirect the cut through perfect resonance symmetry; a silence field of the Cymorath type reduces the accuracy of the targeting; an Eidolyn- or Oneirion-based illusion can disguise the true harmonic structure underneath.
**Cost** · An estimated 35,000 EU per cast, chosen conservatively against the 8,000 to 45,000 EU range documented elsewhere for a non-sustained Stage XII working; plus the card's own named costs, a leak of internal resonance into vertigo and temporary tone-deafness, and, for a larger cut, a backlash that weakens Opalis's other Emanation-level workings for a short while afterward.
**Counterplay** · A silence field, temporal stillness, or a void-phase barrier prevents the Shear from finding the target frequency at all; an entity holding multiple contradictory frequencies simultaneously (a Fractura-dominant being, a dream-entity, a shapeshifter) reduces the cut's precision below the point of effect.

# Coagula Dominion

## Summary card

**Effect** · A fifteen-metre field in which ambient Essence stops flowing and starts sitting. Spellwork routed through the field arrives late and undersized; anyone moving through it fights the air itself for every step.
**Cost** · Pressure builds behind Drakvor's own ribs for as long as the field holds; the same coagulating law that slows the enemy's Essence is running through his.
**Limit** · Reaches only Essence already loose in the air. A working already sealed inside a Soul Crystal, or a body moving under its own momentum rather than Essence-borne lift, passes through untouched.
**Counter** · A Wellspring that routes through Vectoria or Spatium rather than through ambient Essence (flight, blink-step, anything that never asks the air's permission) is not slowed at all.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the field reads intent before it thickens, or whether it would happily seize Drakvor's own allies if he ever lost the thread holding it apart from them.

## Design Chain

**Trigger** · Declared. Drakvor commits attention and a standing draw of Essence to open the field; it does not fire reflexively.
**Function** · Field-wide Essence coagulation paired with a discernment layer that tells the field what is a working in progress and what is just a body moving.
**Mechanism** · At the Wellspring stratum, Coagula's Core Law is a soul-gathering current. It makes dispersed Essence draw back toward coherence rather than staying loose. Drakvor projects that law outward instead of inward. Nothing new is made. The same current that would gather a fractured Crystal back into one piece instead gathers the *ambient* Essence in a fifteen-metre sphere into a slow, viscous mass, the way a supersaturated solution locks into a lattice the instant something gives it a nucleus to organise around. Judicium rides the same field as a discernment layer. Its law is discernment itself, and here it tells the coagulating Essence which currents are load-bearing (a spell mid-formation, a working still drawing) and which are incidental (ambient background flux), so the field thickens selectively around the former. At the Essence stratum, this costs Drakvor a standing draw from his own Aether Shell. He is running Coagula's law through himself as well as through the air for as long as the field holds. It is a double draw. That is where the rib-pressure comes from.
**Numerical Effect** · A working routed through the field loses a full Tier Grade of effective output while inside it. Per the standing differential rule, a one-Tier gap is enough to lose a direct exchange outright, so an S-Grade caster's spellwork behaves like an A-Grade caster's for as long as they stand in the coagulated air. Movement through the field costs proportionately more Essence per stride, though the field does not touch a body under pure muscular momentum.
**Target Response** · Casters inside the radius find their own workings arriving late, undersized, or both. Anyone sprinting or lunging through the field feels the air itself go to syrup, and has to fight for every stride rather than being stopped outright.
**Consequence** · A contested engagement inside the field degrades in Drakvor's favour every exchange the field holds. The enemy's spellwork keeps arriving a beat slow, and a beat is frequently the whole fight.
**Limitation** · The field only ever touches Essence that is loose in the air. A working already sealed and finished, or a body moving on inertia rather than active Essence draw, is invisible to it.
**Weakness** · High-Tier Wellsprings overpower the coagulation outright, and anything that routes through Vectoria or Spatium instead of ambient Essence never touches the medium Drakvor is thickening: skyborne and teleportive workings pass clean through.
**Cost** · Sustained rib-deep pressure fatigue for as long as the field holds, worsening the longer it is sustained. This is not decorative. Drakvor is running Coagula's law through his own Shell at the same time as the field, and it does not distinguish between the two.
**Counterplay** · Motion-Wellspring practitioners, high-clarity Spirit Path casters who can push a working through in one clean burst rather than a sustained draw, and anti-binding glyphs built against Coagula specifically all pass the field's discernment layer or overpower its coagulating grip.

## Origin

The card gives no derivation history beyond the philosophy Drakvor speaks over it, "Motion is privilege. I revoke it," which the entry keeps as his own stated framing of the technique rather than as an explained origin. No problem, teacher, or precedent is named in the source; per the named-inventor rule, Drakvor is the technique's only attested author, and nothing about where he learned to run Coagula outward rather than inward is recorded. *Unattested in current canon*: Drakvor himself, and any prior use of Coagula in this outward-projected mode.

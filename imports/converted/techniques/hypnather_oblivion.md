# Oblivion-Step

> Converted from the Trello card *Hypnather Oblivion-Step* (list: Abilities; labels: Teleport, Shadow, Dream, Animus, Finalized). The three Wellsprings named on the card, Hypnather, Oneirion and Letheveil, are all current canon, unchanged, all three Limina. The card names Hypnather first as the entry-threshold and Oneirion second as the space the step actually crosses; since Oneirion is the Wellspring that carries a working through configuration space "to reach targets no line of sight connects" (the literal teleport), it is recorded here as primary and Hypnather as secondary, with the change noted rather than silently made. "Aether Class: Dreambreaker Aether" was old freeform vocabulary and has no equivalent; it converts to Class III, the Resonant, the Class the FOW table correlates with Band C, the Band the Stage table assigns to Stage VII. "Path: Spirit Path" and "Temperance Requirement: Stage VII Refraction" already used current FOW terms and needed no conversion. Grade required (A-Grade) is read off the Stage VII row of the Stage table. Craft is assigned as Spellcraft: the card's "Activation: Declared" describes a spoken or announced casting act with no substrate, rune or vessel, which is Spellcraft's own definition (Ars Vocis, the speaking). Category is assigned as Transitus, the twenty-fifth Magical Category, the crossing of a body between Planes and the making of the aperture it crosses through, which is exactly what the card describes. The character Aeldoris has no card anywhere in current canon and is unattested; the name is kept because the technique cannot be described without an owner, per the verification law. "Anti-shadow sigils" is kept as a Runecraft counter-ward without naming a specific Wellspring, since the card does not specify one and none should be invented. The destination folder named for this card, The Iridescent Archive, returns nothing in the wiki and is treated as a Trello-era filing label rather than an in-world proper noun; it is not used inside the entry.

## Summary card

**Effect** · Aeldoris drops into half-sleep for an instant and steps out again from the dream-shadow of another mind nearby, arriving up to fifty metres away with no line of travel between the two points and no sound.
**Cost** · A quarter-second of disorientation on arrival, and a backwash of the anchor's own emotions surfacing in her afterward, uninvited.
**Limit** · The step needs a live subconscious within range to anchor to and a dream-shadow to emerge from; without one, there is nowhere to arrive.
**Counter** · A mind held lucid through the threshold, or a fixed ward built to erase a dream-shadow before it forms, denies the step before it completes.
**What nobody knows** · Why a stranger's dream ever answers a step it was never asked to hold.

## Design Chain

**Trigger** · Aeldoris chooses a target point within fifty metres that holds another mind, and lets her own Crystal drop toward sleep on command rather than waiting on fatigue.
**Function** · Mobility and repositioning: she is removed from where she stood and returned from somewhere else, arriving already inside whatever the moment there requires.
**Mechanism** · Hypnather is a structural-relaxation Wellspring. It grants the Crystal a glass's own behaviour: the capacity to reorganise toward a lower-energy configuration the instant genuine rest begins. Aeldoris forces that reorganisation open for a half-second rather than a night. Oneirion does the actual crossing. It does not move her through the room; it moves her through configuration space, the coordinate space in which every arrangement a system could take is a point with no physical location of its own. Where the trajectory re-enters the Physical Plane is set by the nearest dream-shadow, the trace a sleeping or startled mind casts into that space. Her outline re-forms there. Letheveil rides along at low intensity. It softens the memory of the crossing so the half-second does not register to her as time lost.
**Numerical Effect** · Range zero to fifty metres. Duration of the crossing itself: instant, no action spent holding it open. Disorientation on arrival: roughly a quarter-second before she can act.
**Target Response** · The anchor feels nothing at the moment of the crossing; their dream-shadow is used, not entered, and it re-settles behind her. Anyone watching sees her vanish and reappear at the second point with no transit between.
**Consequence** · She gains full positional advantage against anything that tracks by sightline or by sound, and a strike already in flight toward her old position resolves against empty air.
**Limitation** · No subconscious within range means no dream-shadow, and no dream-shadow means the step has nowhere to put her; she cannot step to a point that is merely empty.
**Weakness** · A working built on Aurevane or Luminalis floods the local signal enough that no dream-shadow reads against it, and the step fails outright in that light. A strike already fast enough to correct mid-flight can still be aimed at where the arrival will be rather than where she was.
**Cost** · The quarter-second of disorientation is paid every time and cannot be trained away, since it is the Crystal's own re-arrival lag rather than a nerve response. The memory backwash accumulates: enough steps off the same anchor and she is carrying feelings that were never hers to keep.
**Counterplay** · A target trained to hold lucidity through the drop into sleep gives Hypnather nothing to open. A Runecraft ward built specifically to erase a dream-shadow removes Oneirion's landing point before the crossing resolves. Strong Resilience Coherence in a watching mind reads the threshold opening and can decline to let the step complete nearby.

## Origin

The card gives no teacher, no precedent and no borrowed lineage for this step; it names only Aeldoris and the line she is said to have coined for it, that the quickest path runs through the moment a person almost fell asleep. Nothing in the source suggests she learned Oblivion-Step from anyone else's Codex entry. Recorded here as self-derived, which means a scene using it for the first time has to show the working in full rather than summarise it, and that anyone who has actually watched her do it once has everything they need to start building a counter.

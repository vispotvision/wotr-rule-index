# Nox'Vyra, the Stillwing Judicate — SKIPPED, standing restriction

> This card is not converted. The source names its invoker as "Tovain Zethriel." The wiki's Volume V index carries a standing restriction on that name: "Tovain, and any character connected to him, are not to be written. Cards belonging to him or tied to him are skipped in the migration and flagged by name rather than built." That restriction sits alongside the same list's other protected names (Haruki, Dova'Kan, Gorgi, Ma'Kovu, Fushigi, Xhem), all belonging to Isaac or to other creators, whose dialogue, action, gesture, reaction, and interiority are never authored here.
>
> Since Nox'Vyra's entire summoning procedure, behaviour, and history in the source card are built around Tovain as its invoker, there is no way to convert the entity without writing the connected character it is barred from touching. Per the standing rule, this entry is flagged by name and left unbuilt rather than reconstructed around the restriction.
>
> Nothing below is a conversion. It is a record of why none was attempted.

## What was in the source

An Eidolon-class summon, "Nox'Vyra, the Stillwing Judicate," bound by an invoker named Tovain Zethriel and built around the Wellsprings Fixatio, Judicium, and Somnalis (all three real and attested in current canon), with a stated Temperance range, a summoning circle, a set of named abilities, and a short historical record ("Frostspire Event Log, VE 875").

## Why it is not built

The card's own premise, an entity summoned and bound by Tovain, cannot be converted without writing Tovain: his invocation, his link to the entity, his catalyst event, or his standing as its bearer. That is precisely the category the standing restriction closes. No amount of renaming the summon or reassigning the invoker to an unnamed practitioner would honour the restriction faithfully, since the source's history, behaviour, and limitations (the Animatria feedback risk between Tovain and the entity, the dismissal rite tied to him specifically) are inseparable from the restricted character.

## What would need to happen for this to proceed

Isaac's ruling on one of two paths: either the entity is rebuilt from nothing around a different, unrestricted invoker, with a new catalyst event and history invented from scratch (which Law 1 of the conversion brief does not license without his say-so, since it would add history the card never gave), or the entity is retired from the migration entirely, alongside its invoker. Until then it is flagged, not converted, per the wiki's own instruction for exactly this situation.

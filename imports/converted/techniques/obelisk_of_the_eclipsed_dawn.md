# Obelisk of the Eclipsed Dawn

> Converted from an OPALIS ability card on the old Trello board. The card's six-Wellspring source list, its dual offense/defense construct-summon function, its 3 km/300-800 m range block, its four-Obelisk cap, and its named limits and weaknesses are kept as given. "Temperance Requirement: Stage XII, Emnation" converts to the FOW Stage of the same numeral, Stage XII, Emanation, whose Band, Level range and Max Tier Grade are read off the FOW Stage table rather than invented. The old free-text "Aether Class: Emanative Construct-Weaving" is a caster attribute, not a technique one; it is not reproduced as a field here, but the "autonomous convergence engine that reads intent" language it was gesturing at is consistent with Class V, the Radiant, and that reading is offered for whoever builds Opalis's own character sheet rather than asserted as settled. All six source Wellspring names (Tarturon, Basilithe, Petralon, Oneirion, Tenebra, Fractura) checked against the current Sixty and are attested without alteration. Basilithe is promoted to primary Wellspring for the Codex line because the Obelisk's own core concept, a crystalline monument, is Basilithe's law before it is any of the other five; the remaining five are carried below as secondary harmonisations, per the card's own unranked list. "Opalis" has no character card in the wiki mirror; she is kept because the technique cannot be written without a caster, and is flagged unattested pending her own sheet. Numerical Effect is read off the SSS-Grade row of the FOW Strike Force table, the ceiling for a Stage XII Spellcraft working, since the card gives no numeric force value of its own.

## Summary card

**Effect** · Opalis collapses six orbiting Wellspring sigils into a point of infinite density and a colossal prism rises from it, dissolving what she marks as an ending and rebuilding what she marks as a beginning, over an area up to 800 m across.
**Cost** · The Obelisk records what she was feeling when she raised it and plays it back to her; sustaining it long enough leaves hairline stress in her own Temperance layers that only stillness closes.
**Limit** · No more than four Obelisks stand at once; a fifth does not fail to appear, it brings the others down with it.
**Counter** · Saturate it in raw entropy or a comparable law-breaking working faster than its Basilithe lattice can re-order, and it collapses early.
**What nobody knows** · Whether the "unfinished dream" the Obelisk rewrites is read from the target's Essence or written onto it; Opalis has never been asked to explain the difference and nobody has caught her doing it slowly enough to check.

## Design Chain

**Trigger** · Opalis stills her Essence and commits to a reading of the field: end something, or begin something, within line of sight.
**Function** · A field-scale construct manifests and executes that reading autonomously for as long as the line back to her holds, choosing dissolution, containment, or restoration mode by what she fed it at the moment of casting.
**Mechanism** · Basilithe's crystal-growth law is pushed into a metastable high-order lattice while Tarturon's entropy law is throttled to run backward across the Obelisk's own surface, a local, temporary entropy inversion held open by the other four Wellsprings acting as boundary conditions (Petralon's mass anchor, Oneirion's conceptual template, Tenebra's radiance suppression, Fractura's mirrored load-sharing across up to four instances).
**Numerical Effect** · SSS-Grade ceiling: 1-5 PN peak force, 418 TJ to 4.184 EJ energy yield, 100 kt to 1 Tt TNT-equivalent (Large Town to Small Country scale) when run in Fracture Cascade mode; Ecliptic Aegis and Oneiric Reversal modes draw on the same ceiling as absorbed or redirected rather than released force.
**Target Response** · A hostile construct, curse or barrier caught in Fracture Cascade unwinds structurally rather than being struck; an ally under Ecliptic Aegis has incoming force redirected or nullified at the shell rather than absorbed into their own Crystal; damaged Essence under Oneiric Reversal is coaxed toward its own healthier prior state rather than repaired from outside.
**Consequence** · The Obelisk's mode cannot be changed mid-manifestation without collapsing and re-forming it; committing to the wrong reading at the trigger moment is committed for the duration.
**Limitation** · It cannot create or restore life, only Essence-state; it cannot overturn another Stage XII practitioner's own standing decree, only contest or deflect it; a fifth simultaneous instance is not refused, it is punished, and the other four come down with it.
**Weakness** · Genuine internal conflict in Opalis warps the lattice toward inversion; a caster attuned to Dissolution or Cataclysm can partially bypass the Oneirion and Petralon functions; sustained pure-radiance pressure can crack the Tenebra shell layer.
**Cost** · Reflective Burden plays her own recent emotional state back to her as psychic feedback while the Obelisk stands; Structural Strain leaves micro-fractures in her Temperance layers under prolonged manifestation, closed only by a period of Essence stillness.
**Counterplay** · Raw Tarturon or a comparable Dissolution-aligned field erodes the outer lattice faster than Basilithe can re-order it, forcing early collapse; a Stage XII peer's own standing decree can contest or deflect a mode without needing to break the construct outright.

## Origin

The card names no inventor, workshop, or founding problem beyond Opalis herself; it gives only that the technique requires Stage XII, Emanation. Per the named-inventor rule, a technique with no documented teacher is self-derived and must be read live rather than summarised the first time it appears on the page. Where the card is silent on when or why Opalis first raised an Obelisk, that history is left unfilled rather than invented; it is unattested in the source.

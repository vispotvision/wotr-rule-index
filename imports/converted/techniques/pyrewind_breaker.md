# Pyrewind Breaker

> Converted from a Trello ability card pairing Exuroth and Cataclysm. Both Wellsprings check out unchanged against current canon (`wiki`): both are Caloria, both are Thermodynamics-domain. "Path: Aether + Body" used Aether as if it were a Path; Aether is the ocean every working draws from, not one of the four routes, so the Path corrects to Spirit and Body, the two current Paths that fit a projected, ranged area burst with a physical concussive half. "Aether Class: Volatile Kinetic-Flame Hybrid" is free text and is replaced with the class the Stage/Band correlate table actually assigns: Stage VI, Glory sits in Band D, and Band D is Class II, the Harmonic, the same class Seraphic Thread Blessing lands on despite the very different technique, a fact about the table rather than an error in either entry. Duration is restated in seconds. Thaloré, Verdantia and Cinerion all check out against current canon in their stated Families; "Abyssal entities" also checks out, Abyssal being an attested creature-and-corruption category in the Bestiary rather than invented flavour. No caster is named in the source card.

## Summary card
**Effect** · A spiralling gale of fire detonates outward to a 20-metre radius, burning everything caught inside and following the burn with a blunt shockwave that knocks bodies back or down and overloads weak shields on contact.
**Cost** · A heavy Aether burn in the caster, with a real risk of internal overheating if the working is pushed or repeated too soon.
**Limit** · Once released, the burst cannot be steered, throttled or called back.
**Counter** · Thaloré-worked water blunts the blast outright; Cinerion rust-barriers redirect the force rather than absorb it; Verdantia wards muffle how far the flame spreads.
**What nobody knows.** Why the shockwave sometimes outruns the flame it came from instead of arriving with it.

## Design Chain
**Trigger** · The caster declares, or chants, the working, committing to a burst with no further input once it starts.
**Function** · Offensive area denial: simultaneous thermal and concussive damage across a 20-metre radius.
**Mechanism** · Exuroth strengthens Ardency Overchannel, Tempering Overflow and Vitality Threshold. That is the clause letting the caster's own Crystal survive pushing this much heat through at once. Cataclysm strengthens Ardency Cascade, Ardency Overchannel and Dominion Radius. That is what turns a single flame into a coherent 20-metre burst instead of a narrow jet. The two Wellsprings compound on Overchannel, which is why the technique reads as volatile even though its Family is a straightforward thermodynamic one.
**Numerical Effect** · At Stage VI's A-Grade ceiling, peak force runs 20 to 100 GN and energy yield 46 GJ to 4.184 TJ, an 11 to 1,000-tonne TNT equivalent, City Block to Multi-City Block scale at the sustainable maximum. The burst itself is instantaneous, under a second, matching the source's "1 turn (instant burst)."
**Target Response** · A vortex of white-orange flame coils like a living serpent before it bursts. A tearing roar follows. Anyone caught near it reports hair rising, chest pressure and the taste of metal in the air, just before impact.
**Consequence** · Massive fire damage across the radius, targets knocked back or down by the shockwave, and weak shields overloaded on contact.
**Limitation** · The burst cannot be aimed finer than its full radius, throttled mid-release, or interrupted once declared.
**Weakness** · Water-aligned Wellsprings, especially Thaloré, blunt the blast; a Cinerion rust-barrier redirects rather than merely resists the force.
**Cost** · High Aether burn and a genuine risk of internal overheating in the caster, worse with repeated use in a short span.
**Counterplay** · Get behind a Thaloré or Verdantia working before it releases, or accept the shockwave's redirection through a Cinerion barrier. Abyssal-aligned entities absorb the fire component but take the concussive component in full.

# Radiant Ribbon Shell

> Converted from a pre-Fracture Abilities card presenting a voice-based technique class with no named practitioner, filed as a template ("Harmonic Choir-Type") rather than as one person's art. The old Temperance numerals VII and VIII already match the current FOW Stage names, Refraction and Transcendence, and needed only confirmation. The card gives no free-text Aether Class; it is derived from the per-stage Coherence Band at floor and apex, Band C at Stage VII and Band B at Stage VIII, which read directly off the table as Class III, the Resonant, ascending to Class V, the Radiant, a reading the technique's own name already anticipates. The old primary Wellspring "Voice" is not on the Sixty; it is replaced with Judicium, the Wellspring of Truth, which the card's own Verity Timbre trait already describes functionally. The old secondary "Aurevane," used by the card for an air effect, is corrected: current canon's Aurevane governs Light, not Air, so the air-scaffold function is carried instead by Cymorath, the actual Air Wellspring on the Sixty. "Memory / Ma" converts to Anamnesis, with the glyph root Ma read as shorthand for Maelor, the Archon of Memory already attested in current cosmology. Range, decibel, and speed figures are physical units and are kept as given. The implements (Ribbon-Staff, Cantor's Mantle, Seal-Pin, Feather Vows) are unattested custom items kept because the technique cannot be read without them.

## Summary card

**Effect** · Shapes breath and voice into structured pressure that can hush a room, carry a phrase intact across distance, or thin itself into an edge that cuts cloth and cord without cutting flesh.
**Cost** · Range and clarity both key to the truth of what is carried; curated or withheld speech measurably frays the signal, and cutting against conscience costs voice.
**Limit** · It needs a living voice and breath behind it; nothing sustains once the caster stops breathing the pattern, and an echo-dead space starves it of anything to carry.
**Counter** · An echo-dead vault or a chaos-aligned anti-cohesion field gives the shell nothing to entrain; a silent grappler who never lets it establish a corridor denies it room to work.
> **What nobody knows.** Why the Feather Vows chime true and dull false, with no documented mechanism connecting the chime to the wearer's actual honesty rather than only to what the shell can measure.

Stage floor VII, Refraction. Grade required A-Grade (late push toward S-Grade). Path gate Attraction Path.

## Design Chain

**Trigger** · A drawn breath shaped by one of four gates, Set, Place, Speak, Recover; no chant required.
**Function** · The voice entrains nearby air into laminar bands that can damp ambient noise, carry a phrase intact, or thin into a cutting edge.
**Mechanism** · This runs on real acoustic phase-locking, laminar versus turbulent airflow, the same physics that lets a trained voice cut through noise an untrained one cannot. Ethical certainty keeps the flow laminar, clean carry; conflicted intent introduces turbulence, shortened range and frayed edges, which the technique dramatizes as the Wellspring reading the caster's own coherence.
**Numerical Effect** · Hush runs roughly -9 dB at 8 metres and holds through gusts to about 30 knots. Carry reaches 200-300 metres in open air, 80-120 through alleys. Cut severs textile and rope at 3-5 metres and scores light plate at 2-3. Air-Step bursts run 30-60 m/s with a two-beat recovery.
**Target Response** · Allied hands steady and aim improves inside the Hushfield; a lied-to listener hears the lie warble a half-step flat; a bound target's textile or rope parts cleanly at the Cut Lamina's edge.
**Consequence** · Panic and ambient clamor fold outward and thin at the field's rim; truth crosses the corridor essentially intact.
**Limitation** · It makes room for truth to travel; it does not compel anyone to speak it, and a caster with nothing true to say has nothing for Carry to preserve.
**Weakness** · Anger or conflicted intent turns clean flow to hiss and turbulence, shrinking range and dulling the Cut Lamina exactly when it is needed most.
**Cost** · Overriding conscience to cut can cost temporary voice loss; post-hearing overuse causes double-hearing that clears only with rest.
**Counterplay** · Ballast with lower vowels and reset cadence against Ribbon Fray; a calm ally widens the laminar stability of Choir mode against turbulence caused by anger.

## FOW line

Governing Primary Harmonics (Fidelity, Attunement) and Dexterity (Burst, Finesse). Stage floor VII, Refraction, ascending to VIII, Transcendence. Grade required A-Grade at floor, S-Grade at apex. Path gate Attraction Path. Resonant Pair none indicated in the source.

## Origin

The source names no practitioner at all. It presents the Radiant Ribbon Shell as a class-template, "Harmonic Choir-Type, Voice / Aurevane bias," rather than crediting an inventor, a place, or a problem it was built to solve.

# Veil of Verdant Pact

## Summary card

**Effect** · A living dome of grown matter closes over the caster and every ally within five metres, and for as long as it holds, wounds taken inside it lag behind whatever caused them. *Fire arrives and the char becomes new bark. Frost arrives and the numbness becomes sap-slow warmth.*
**Cost** · **The ward routes injury through the caster's own body before it routes it into growth.** *Every burn, break, and poison the dome absorbs is felt by whoever raised it, in full, for the second it takes the wood to catch up.*
**Limit** · **Anti-growth law overrides growth law.** Exuroth's dehydrating heat and Nihiloth's null field are not damage the dome can convert; they are the absence of the conditions growth needs, and the dome only feeds on inputs it can metabolise.
**Counter** · **Deny it the second it needs to root.** *The dome takes roughly one second to close from declaration to full coverage, and a strike landing inside that window lands on nothing grown yet.*
> **What nobody knows** · Whether the dome draws on Verdantia's law alone, or on something older that Verdantia only resembles, since ancient Materia Primordia mineral seams have turned up threaded through its root system on every occasion anyone has checked.

## Design Chain

**Trigger** · Declared. The caster commits the working aloud or through a fixed gesture chain, and the dome begins closing around a five-metre radius immediately.
**Function** · Converts destructive force arriving inside the radius into accelerated tissue growth in the dome's own structure, and at a far smaller rate, in the caster's and allies' bodies.
**Mechanism** · Verdantia's law is indeterminate meristematic growth: the dome's tissue never reaches a final size, so ambient energy delivered to it, heat, kinetic shock, electrical discharge, corrosive chemistry, is absorbed the way a plant absorbs sunlight, as fuel for continuous structural addition rather than as damage taken. Materia Primordia supplies the frame: the same Essence re-latticed as living wood on the outside and dense mineral bracing on the inside, so the structure that is growing is also the structure holding its own weight.
**Numerical Effect** · Stage V carries a Stat Ceiling of 350 on the FOW scale; the ward's own floor sits at B-Grade, Stat Range 176 to 275, Peak Force 1 to 5 GN, meaning it can absorb a Building-to-Large-Building-level strike before its structure fails outright. Duration once closed: four exchanges, roughly twenty-four seconds at the six-seconds-per-exchange conversion noted above.
**Target Response** · An attack striking the dome sees its impact point green over and thicken rather than crater; a poison delivered against it is metabolised into growth-hormone runoff that smells faintly of sap.
**Consequence** · Every unit of force the dome converts is logged against the caster's own capacity to heal the way a debt is logged against a ledger; the dome closes hairline Soul Crystal fractures on the same principle, far more slowly, while it stands.
**Limitation** · A one-second closure lag separates declaration from full coverage; anything already past the five-metre line before that second ends was never inside the ward.
**Weakness** · Anti-growth Wellsprings, Exuroth's dehydration and Nihiloth's null field, are not inputs the dome can metabolise and pass through it largely undiminished.
**Cost** · The caster's own body reports every absorbed injury in full for the instant it takes the wood to catch up: a burn absorbed by the dome is a burn the caster feels before it becomes bark.
**Counterplay** · Strike inside the one-second closure window, or bring a working the dome cannot metabolise; heavy-metal toxins outright weaken the roots rather than feeding them, by the source's own account.

## Origin

The card gives no deviser, era, or precipitating problem, only the technique and its one-line philosophy. Its own Counters & Interactions list (Absolution's flames bypassing it, Tenebra decaying the vines, heavy-metal toxins weakening the roots) reads as established, catalogued countermeasures rather than something worked out live against a single caster; per the named-inventor rule that marks it a documented technique, not a self-derived one. It is filed as a documented Vitalia/Materia ward of anonymous, folk-transmitted origin, known and taught rather than invented in the scene of its first appearance. No deviser's name is invented to fill the gap the card leaves.

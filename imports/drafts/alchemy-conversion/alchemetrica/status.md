## For whoever applies blockops.json

- Three kinds of entry. `patch_block`: PATCH the block's rich_text from `new` (and its colour, where the entry has `color`, sending the heading's `color` alongside its rich_text). `append_children`: PATCH `/blocks/{parent}/children` with the two `children` (an empty spacer paragraph, then the new paragraph), after `after`; first re-read the parent and confirm `after` is still its last child and still reads `after_text`. `delete_blocks`: DELETE each id.
- Items 22b, 58 and 62 add each callout's new closing paragraph as its own child, preceded by a spacer, the way every other paragraph in those callouts sits. They are no longer carried inside the last child with a blank line: `block_md` prefixes only a child's first line with `> `, so a second paragraph inside one block would fall out of the callout in the wiki/ mirror.
- Entries H7 to H10 carry `"run": "after plan.json"` and `"id": null`. After the md apply, read the page's top-level blocks, take the one of the given type whose text is exactly `find.text` (each is unique on the page then), and patch its colour.

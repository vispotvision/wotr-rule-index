# Alchemy conversion: Isaac's decisions

Answered in Claude Code chat, 2026-09-28. Source: a nine-seam audit of the Alftian Codex (I-III), the Necrocursica and the Corrant Papers against canon, each finding checked by a second agent. Not yet logged as rulings.

Attribution (decided before the questionnaire): Kwon Mu-jin wrote the Alftian Codex; Malphas wrote the Necrocursica; a new invented author writes the Papers.

## Keystones

**K1 · Which era the texts are dated in** (not the recommendation)
- Q: The texts stamp themselves 'Era of Voyagers' (Imperial years 0 to 70); how should they be dated now that their authors live in Year 715?
- Chosen: follow-up: present is early Imperial
- Isaac: Texts stamped in the early years of the Imperial Age; Mu-jin's and Malphas's present is itself the early Imperial Age; the Concordance's Year 715 Withering Era calendar is logged and re-ruled.

**K2 · Is the Heresiology written after the texts?**
- Q: The Heresiology's five Historical Cases are the Codex's own events; is it written after all five texts, or are its cases older incidents that merely resemble them?
- Chosen: After: a recent Accord manual drawn from these events. The five cases stand word for word; the meta page and Malphas's card stay true. The texts' events close some years before its issue, in Mu-jin's past. Case II's author 'pulled by access and manipulated through need' must fit Malphas (the Necrocursica items).

**K3 · When in his life each volume falls**
- Q: When in Mu-jin's life do the Codex's events happen, and when does he write each of the three volumes?
- Chosen: Vols I–II on the road; Vol III after the Ashgate road. Vols I–II fall between fourteen and about nineteen; the eleven months in Urbis become his convalescence on one lung, before the Academy. The Correction answers Rimward. 'Final Testimonies' and 'life's work' go, and Vol III must mention Frithia and infant children.

**K4 · Texts' doctrine: system canon or one school's reading** (not the recommendation)
- Q: Do the residues, the Volitional Trace and impression-bodies become facts on the system pages, or stay what the Accord teaches inside the story?
- Chosen: System canon: residues, Trace and impression-bodies all become fact. The Lexicon, The Crossing and the Magical Categories gain entries, and the Heresiology must agree line for line. The Trace's behaviour, whichever reading wins, becomes a rule every scene obeys.
- Isaac: All system canon

**K5 · Sublimatio or Sublimare: which one is sublimation**
- Q: Which twin current is sublimation (a solid turning straight to vapour) and which is fractional distillation (separating a mixture by boiling off its parts)?
- Chosen: The physics register wins. Sublimatio is sublimation, Sublimare is distillation. Alchemetrica's operation table, R18-5's list and Kytheris's card are corrected, and Distillation mirrors Sublimare. The Necrocursica's Sublimatio line stands; its separating cleanse becomes Sublimare-aligned.

**K6 · Calcination and Distillation: Wellsprings or bench operations** (not the recommendation)
- Q: Are Calcination and Distillation Wellsprings (currents a soul can tune to), folk names for two of the Sixty, or only procedures done at the bench?
- Chosen: Bench operations only; the Sixty stay untouched. A logged ruling amends R18-5, and Alchemetrica's 'eight' table drops to six. Mu-jin's card loses two harmonisations, and about ten pages (Ossuary Choir, Ignatius, Vaelen, Vorynth, The Open Crucible, Spirit Rune Arts) are re-pointed to real currents.
- Isaac: Fix the cards, they aren't right (Calcination and Distillation are not Wellsprings)

**K7 · Which residue list the texts use**
- Q: What does a dead person leave behind in the converted texts: the Codex's three residues, the Necrocursica's four, both on different clocks, or The Crossing's three products?
- Chosen: Both lists on two clocks, hinged on The Crossing's nine days. The Necrocursica's four are the forensic reading inside the nine days; the Codex's three (place, bond, glyph-trace) are what stays after, which the two-year-old commission draws on. The Necrocursica gains a short after-window section, so the Codex and Papers cite it truly.

**K8 · Which seven keeps 'Seven Cacodaemonic Corruptions'**
- Q: The Heresiology and the Necrocursica give the name 'Seven Cacodaemonic Corruptions' to two different lists; which list keeps it?
- Chosen: Heresiology keeps it; the Necrocursica's become the Seven Unmoorings. The Necrocursica's title, chapter 3 heading and Appendix II are renamed from the text's own verb ('it unmoors them', chapter 4), and each failure may name the Heresiology inversion that drives it. Grade III and the God Hand page's 'two sevens' stay true.

**K9 · Base text: full Word files or wiki**
- Q: Which version is the base for the conversion: the full first-person Word files, or the shorter Codex pages on the wiki?
- Chosen: Word files as base; wiki condensations regenerated afterwards. Every contradiction is visible and decided here. The converted texts are first person throughout, and the wiki's Codex pages are rewritten afterwards as condensations of the new text, keeping their chapter subheads and residue table.

**K10 · How deep the rewrite goes** (not the recommendation)
- Q: How deep does the conversion go: surgical edits, a substantial rewrite into the new authors' voices, a full rebuild, or a split by text?
- Chosen: Rebuild: new texts from the same ideas. Structure, events and cast are rethought around the new authors, and the old texts become source notes. Most freedom, but the proven prose goes and the Heresiology's case-by-case match must be rechecked.
- Isaac: Full rebuild

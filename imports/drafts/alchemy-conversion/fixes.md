- Mu-jin's age on the Ashgate road. RULINGS.md:518 turns 'ten years since' into twenty-four, which makes him 38 at the card's As Of, before Ashgate. Academy State of Play:13 and bible.md:151 put 24 at Hon-guk and 38 at the Academy, although 24 + 13 = 37. Scenes say 'forty years' (10_mujin:73; Arena:13).
- Aetheric Residue permanence. Core Vocabulary:40 calls Residue 'A permanent, if often subtle, change'. The Mechanica page :50 says ordinary residue 'thins on a schedule'.
- Over-claimed standing. Mu-jin card :163 says a summoner who claims a standing he lacks 'gets nothing'. Counted Speech :110 says an over-warrant seals and is deducted from hands, hearing, teeth or years (:149). Rimward (card :178) sides with the Counted Speech but cost an eye, which :149 does not list.
- Pure alchemists' rank. Alchemetrica ranks Crystal-less alchemists at Tier Four (:75, :77), and its 'Dormant Crystal' (:80) contradicts its 'No Soul Crystal' (:66). Tiered Path:15 makes every Tier a Crystal change, VIII:69 puts Dormant at Class Ø pre-Initiate, and X:515 caps items at the maker's Tier of Standing.
- The Real Alchemy is missing. R18-5-ALCHEMY_SOURCE_ORDER (pack-18:234-258) makes it one of three required sources, 'none of them optional'. No such page exists in wiki/, in true-canon or in Notion (searched 2026-09-28). C-058 logged only the Provenance Doctrine.
- Latin as authorization. Open Crucible :33 calls Mu-jin's spoken Latin 'the authorization phrase'. Accord Latin :84 and :108 (no first person, so it cannot carry a warrant), Descent :57-58 and Summon Register :34 (Latin over the Parunic chain) put authorization in Parun.

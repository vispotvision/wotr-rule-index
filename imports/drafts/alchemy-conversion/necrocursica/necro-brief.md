The Guild Accord's first circle was signed at Kharven-Seat `[canon: The Guild Accord in the Modern Era :37, which gives no year]`, in the spring of 702 by the frithia timeline's reading `[derived: frithia/timeline.md row 702]`. 4. **The Mother's method years.** The Mother is a method cult under the living founder from 696–697 (ML1's "worse a year or two later"; R62-3; canon.md). In 703 he has run cultures for six years and teaches the method from memory (ML10, R61-55). A sealed edition costs his cult nothing. 5. **Before the Heresiology (K2, R61-2).** The Heresiology is issued after all five texts; its Case II ("The warning survived as a footnote") reads Mu-jin's restoring note. 704 for the sealed edition and the note leaves the Heresiology its "recent manual" date anywhere up to 715.

## 4 · The metaphysics in canon terms

Every statement below is canon or ruled; the core text may put it in his words, never contradict it.

**The Crossing's nine days** `[canon: The Crossing, "Three Things Leave", "The Sequence, and the Windows"]`. The Crystal does not depart; it comes apart into three products under three laws. The Echo (memory, habit, voiceprint) goes to the Soul Plane within hours; its departure is how death is determined. The Temperance residue diffuses back through the body into its Wellspring over nine days, a figure that varies with Stage, Wellspring and site saturation and whose variance "has never been characterised". The Name-record files permanently in the Archive of Names. On the ninth day "what remains is meat", except Trait tissue, which stays as passive Class IV carry (DE2). Class IV rendering intercepts the Wellspring's repayment; Gravemark Ink "specifies grinding within nine days of the donation". Necromancy holds the three products together; it "does not raise the dead, which is not a thing that can be done". Its constructs are holes in three systems (the Wellspring unpaid, a false entry in the Archive, the Echo degrading), and proximity gives Melancholic Erosion.

**Mortalis, Auren's Still Gate** `[R61-69; canon: Vitalia "Mortalis · The Black Gate"; Pair V, Auren]`. Mortalis is the thermodynamic boundary of a body held far from equilibrium by continuous expenditure: death is the stopping of the spending. It governs passage and keeps nobody in storage. Its failure is **Equilibration**: "sustained proximity to the boundary reduces the gradient the practitioner is themselves maintaining, and they cool toward it without noticing". Stat Effect: Resilience Persistence, Gnosis Cognition. Auren's glyphs: [Au] Death, [Ren] Passage, [Thal] Silence, [Vor] Return. Through Attraction Force, Mortaris is peaceful passage; through Obsession Force, "the tyranny of premature ending". A Mortalis worker borrows the current and steps out of it at a cost to his own Crystal (card IV, the living man; the Aphorism's debt, GW9). Sylorin's soulstream is a Pantheon image, never a current.

**Anamnesis** `[R61-70; canon: Limina "Anamnesis"]`. The instrument, not the miracle: basalt's remanent field, fired ceramic's trapped electrons. Records live in objects and ground; its failure is thermal resetting, a careless reader erasing the record in the act of taking it. Genesio's Traces are the hollow's Mnemata (Magical Category 18); Anamnesis reads and wakes them.

**The four forensic residues, inside the nine days** `[K7, DE1 to DE3; audit ws-02 as corrected]`:

| Residue | Layer and Crossing product | Window | What it gives a reader |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Corporeal Residue** (Salt) | The body as the Temperance residue's medium; then Trait tissue | Nine days, stretched by days with Stage; after, passive Class IV carry only | Who was Temperance-active and how far; where the body lay |
| **Aetheric Shell** (Salt, per GW4) | The Shell's detached tone, with no Core to sustain it | Hours to days | Identity, Wellsprings carried, how far harmonised: the forensic reading of a suspicious death |
| **Noospheric Echo** (Mercury) | The Crossing's Echo, held: voice, habit, possession | Leaves within hours; if held (a stalled Crossing, a necromantic hold), a Lawful Echo, Class II | Behaviour that pattern-matches the dead; Melancholic Erosion by proximity |
| **Volitional Trace** (Sulphur in his modified triad) | An Attraction Layer commitment: a decision its maker died holding | Read inside the window; settles into the ground where its maker was bound, as Mnemata, and lasts where the ground keeps records | Direction: where the soul was pointing; the only residue with agency, spending down at a diminishing rate until delivered |

**The three after the window** `[K7; Vol II ch. 7, the first manuscript's own list; GL10; R62-6]`: the Harmonic Imprint (Mnemata of places; outlasts the Crossing as long as the ground keeps records), the Sympathetic Bond Trace (Attraction Layer thread; attenuates, never severs; its living end in the mourner), the Parunic Echo (Essence Signature in Aetheric Residue; loud for hours, then thinning toward a floor; Mechanica residue does not thin). Plus the Trace, where the ground holds it. A commission two years after a death draws on these and nothing else `[R65-2; K7]`.

**The Tria Prima of the Persistent Dead** `[GW4]`. Canon's map: Sulphur the Core, Salt the Shell, Mercury the Attraction Layer. His modified triad (openly modified, as he says): Sulphur is the Trace (what wants), Salt the Corporeal Residue with the Aetheric Shell (what is fixed), Mercury the Noospheric Echo (what mediates). When the three are present with a live Shell and the site cannot take the residue, the result is a revenant: a Crossing that stalls on its own (DE6), which fades when diffusion completes. The counter is moving or grounding the body.

**The impression-body and the Volitional Trace's completion** `[DE4, DE5, GL3, GL5 to GL7, EC1, EC9 to EC11]`. A Greater Summons: the vessel is an Arts working poured as a graded Draft (Class, Fidelity, Carry, a price; the Ault vessel was a Class II Field Substrate Draft, fidelity Sound, Carry Marked, R65-2); identity comes from the maker's own Core (Animatria; "Eidolon: a detached fragment of the caster's own Core", Lexicon X), which is why the Aphorism's gloss is literal; the Trace is reached by Vocatia, by recognition, and gets nothing where the maker lacks the standing he claims (R62-7). Eleven days outrun a summons' "minutes to hours", so vessel and Binding carry it. Counters: Stillgate Ash ring (Journeyman gate; "a haunting is a charge with no path"; living Essence at the ring bleeds). A Trace can be used once.

**Category Four, forbidden on paper** `[ML11; R60-04; The Crossing; Heresiology "unlawful resurrection claims"]`. R60-04 (live): "Death is almost always permanent; the only exceptions are liches, the undead and their kind". The core text refuses Category Four as the attempt to rebuild the whole Crystal system; it says the Accord does not authorise it and will not. It never says it cannot be done, and never says it can. The Crossing's "not a thing that can be done" is the Division's voice and the reader's check on him. The whole text is unreliable exactly here; the author notes mark every Category mechanic against canon before use (ML11's own condition).

**Liches: one refused margin line** `[ML12; R60-04; wiki/Aberrations/Lich.md; card: "did not seek out a lichdom in the conventional sense", the Golden Remnant is no phylactery]`. The one kind of dead that stays itself. He names it as the exception he will not treat, and stops. **Gravetide and Gravemark inks** `[EC15, GL7; canon: The Alchemical Index; The Standing Index; Hide, Horn and Bone; The Crossing]`. **Gravetide Ink** (Mortalis / Abyntheus, Vitalia; Class III, IV on practitioner grave-dirt): the Boundary role made material; states where a working reaches and when it ends; mixed on bone because the slab is the reference surface; its exploit is a loose Boundary clause that runs on. **Gravemark Ink** (T4 on the Standing Index, Class IV, reserved at the Expert gate, T5): inscribes a name the Continuum registers; its donor's carry "behaves as though it has an opinion about who is buried"; lawful sources are three and it consumes more than the three produce; reference stock is Render Stock, Attested, Class IV; ground within nine days of the donation. In the text by rank names only: "Adept rank, reserved at the Expert gate" (fixes: name ranks, not numbers). Short or diluted Gravemark is a material cause of the Incomplete Sealing; the supply gap is where Strom found enough to scale (GL7).

**The Aetheric Bleed as Mechanica** `[GL8; R62-6; Heresiology Grade V]`. Output taken from a Crystal that is not the operator's is extraction; the donor may be dead. A working with no Authorization has no warrant to close against, so the boundary it opened stays open. The practitioner can be treated over months; the ground cannot.

**Where The Mother can be recognised in the text (ML10), never named.** A reader who knows the text can find the cult's work by its mechanisms: "It draws nothing, and it couples nothing" (The Mother) against Category One's reading of an operator by the coupled signature; "it hates an edge" against Gravetide's Boundary; growth under a sealed lid against the Incomplete Sealing's "more itself over time"; "It chooses its own names" against Nomenclature; "a Verdantia bleed, no operator indicated" against the Aetheric Bleed. The core text writes each mechanism as a warning; the margins lean toward using it. The text never mentions cultures, inoculation, rot synthesis, the Greyshaft cell or any of its people.

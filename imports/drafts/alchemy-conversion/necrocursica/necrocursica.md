## On This Edition

The first manuscript of this treatise was written at Genesio, begun in the winter of 694 to 695 IC with the passes shut and finished in the spring of 696 IC. It set out three things the dead leave after their nine days, and a method for gathering them into an impression and giving the impression a vessel. ## Preface · A Note on the Circumstances of Composition

This text was not my idea.

Two visitors came to my rooms in the archive-town in the winter of 694 to 695, when the passes were shut and the reading floor was cold enough to show breath. They sat down without being asked. One was Gisli Draycott, who has the unhurried patience of a man who decided the outcome before he climbed the stair and is waiting for you to arrive at it. The other goes by a title, and the title stays out of this book. They told me what they wanted written. They had already named it. They gave me a title to sign under as well: Scribe to the Genesio Archivum, by order. It is a very long name for a man who copies.

I told them I had given up the dead.

"You're avoiding them," Draycott said.

He was right. I have not forgiven him for it.

I set the bargain down beside the coercion, since a reader given only the coercion will think better of me than I deserve. I wanted the stacks below the gate. I had sat on the bench beside that gate for years, most mornings, and looked at the stair. The Heralds' seal on its lock plate was worn bright where other hands had tried it. My visitors had the key, on a cord. They had the sealed stacks, and I had a pen.

I went down the stair the first morning with the key round my neck and the gate open behind me. Below it the air was drier than the reading floor and smelled of old glue and cold stone, and the shelves ran back further than my lamp would reach. I read there by day. I wrote at night, at the table in my rooms, with a stoppered jar of Uplands herbs working on the shelf above it. Draycott came up to the archive-town whenever the passes let a mule through. He read what I had written while I sat across the table from him, and he asked for nothing to be changed.

My left hand was bound in linen through that first winter, and the binding was changed by a man who did not know how.

> Entered for this edition: the hand has healed, into a small repeating lattice from the knuckles to the wrist, and the instrument is not described.

I will admit the rest here, because it is true. By the third chapter I had stopped counting it as compulsion. I had worked alone all my life, at every bench I was allowed near and at one I was put out from, and for fourteen months I did not. What came out of me was no longer mine alone to answer for. The relief of that was the largest I have known. I slept through the nights. I laughed at Neros's charts. When the thaw came I came down from Genesio lighter than I had gone up, and my friends were glad of it, and I let them be glad without telling them the cause. I write it down here so that a reader knows the temper of the hand that wrote what follows.

What follows is a complete architecture for gathering, from what the dead leave behind, something that answers to their names. Someone would have written it. I wrote it with the warning in it.

If you hold this without clearance, read it whole, since half of it is worse than none, and then give it up to the nearest archivist of the Heralds or to the Division's desk. If you hold it with clearance, read it whole.

## Proem · What Mortalis Is

Mortalis is Auren's Still Gate. A living body is held far from equilibrium by continuous expenditure. It spends to stay warmer than its room, wetter than its dust, and more ordered than its parts would lie if they were left alone. Mortalis is the boundary where the spending stops. It governs the passage across. It keeps nobody on the far side and stores nothing there, and a practitioner who harmonises with it borrows a current that runs past him toward that stop.

The borrowing is a debt. The older doctrine the tradition calls the Aphorism of Mortalis says so plainly: whoever borrows the current cannot step back out of it without a cost to his own Crystal. Kwon Mu-jin has since glossed it, and the fourth chapter cites his gloss.

The current has a failure of its own, and it falls on the practitioner. The Still Gate's failure is Equilibration. Sustained proximity to the boundary reduces the gradient the practitioner is himself maintaining, and he cools toward it without noticing. Keep that sentence by you.

When a Crystal fails, it does not leave whole. It comes apart into three products under three laws.

The Echo goes first. It is memory and habit and the print of a voice, and it goes to the Soul Plane within hours, and its going is how death is determined. The Temperance residue goes second, back through the body into its Wellspring over nine days. Nine is the figure. It varies with Stage, with the Wellspring and with the saturation of the site, and nobody has characterised the variance. The Name-record files in the Archive of Names and stays filed.

On the ninth day what remains is meat. The Trait tissue stays. A Trait is laid into the body itself, mineral into bone, and it does not diffuse with the residue, so the remains of anyone who bore a Crystal go on holding their Traits after the ninth day, as passive carry with nothing active in it.

Everything in this treatise happens inside those nine days, or in what the places and the bonds keep after them.

The words of this trade do double duty, and a reader should know which duty each is doing. Some of the Sixty are at once a principle and a bench operation. Sublimatio is one: at the bench it is sublimation, a volatile solid driven up by heat and caught on the cold walls of the vessel, with what would not rise left fused below.

Scholars call all of this necromantic, loosely. The Accord's legal sense of the word is narrower. It takes in only what keeps the three products together past their days: an Echo held from the Soul Plane, a residue kept from its Wellspring, a Name filed against something that has not finished. That construct is a hole in three systems at once, maintained on purpose, and the hole stays open after the construct is destroyed.

It closes when the residue diffuses.

## Chapter the First · What the Dead Leave Inside the Nine Days

Four residues can be read inside the nine days. A reader who takes them in order, and knows what each will bear, reads a death-room cleanly. A reader who takes them out of order spends the one he needed on the one he did not.

**The Corporeal Residue.** For nine days the body is the medium through which a Wellspring is repaid. The Temperance residue moves through it toward the Wellspring it came from. A reader who knows the rate reads three things from what is left: who was Temperance-active, how far, and where the body lay while it paid. Stage stretches the nine days by days, and only by days. Any text that has a practitioner's body active for decades after death describes a hold that somebody made and somebody is paying for. After the ninth day the body holds its Trait tissue and nothing more. That is passive carry, Class IV to the rendering houses, and a melted coin does not testify.

**The Aetheric Shell.** The Shell is the outer layer of a living Crystal. At death it detaches, and for hours, and sometimes for days, its tone goes on sounding with no Core under it to sustain it. It carries identity. It carries which Wellsprings the dead carried, and how far each was harmonised. In a suspicious death it is the first thing to read, since of what a reader can still find it goes soonest, and a reader who arrives on the third day has usually arrived without it.

**The Noospheric Echo.** The Echo leaves within hours, and most of the time there is nothing of it to read. When there is, it has been held. A Crossing has stalled on its own, or somebody has held it on purpose. A held Echo carries voice and habit and the possession a person keeps of a room, and the Division files it as a Lawful Echo, Class II: harmless in itself, and hazardous by proximity. Sustained exposure produces Melancholic Erosion, which is weeping the reader cannot account for and memories that belong to someone else. Read a held Echo from the doorway, and do not stand in its room longer than the reading.

**The Volitional Trace.** The fourth is a commitment of the Attraction Layer, a decision its maker died holding. It is the only residue with direction. It is narrower than memory, and more purposive than the warmth the Bond Trace carries. The Trace points.

Read inside the window, it says where the dead was pointing when the spending stopped. It goes on pursuing that decision at a diminishing rate until the decision is delivered or its charge is spent. Where its maker was bound to a place, it settles into the ground there as Mnemata, and it lasts as long as that ground keeps records. A Trace can be used once.

The credit stands in layers, as the third volume of Kwon Mu-jin's Codex gives it: Madeleine Ault found it and designed the commission, Ivor Strom found it independently, Gisli Draycott framed the theory and built the mechanism, and Mu-jin named it and set its boundary. His word leans nearer a will than I would have let it. It stands, since a name in use is the name, and the boundary he set under it is sound.

The four are read apart. They act together. A held Echo with a live Trace under it gives a construct that repeats a dead woman's habits toward a dead woman's purpose. Nothing a room can hold is more persuasive. A Shell's tone laid over a Corporeal Residue still paying out reads as a person who has not finished dying. The Tria Prima of the third chapter is a way of keeping the four apart in the mind while they are together on the slab.

Take them in this order. Read the Shell first, from the threshold, with the coil held low, since it is the soonest to go and the reading costs it nothing it was keeping. Read for a held Echo second, still from the doorway. Walk in only then, to the body, and read the Corporeal Residue with a hand on the slab and the gauge on the floor beside the feet. Read for a Trace last. It is the one a reading can spend, and a reader who wakes it by accident has used the only copy.

Write each down before reading the next.

Practitioners come apart at the joins between the four.

## Chapter the Second · What Stays After

By the ninth day the Echo has gone, the residue has gone home to its Wellspring, and the Name-record is filed. Nothing is left in the body to call. Three things remain outside it, in the places and the bonds the dead touched, and they are what a practitioner can gather after the window.

The Harmonic Imprint is the tone a person lays into the places they lived: the frequency of the Core and the tone of the Shell, set into walls and floors and ground over years of living against them. The ground holds it as Mnemata. Anamnesis reads it, and it outlasts the Crossing for as long as the ground keeps records.

The Sympathetic Bond Trace is the resonance-thread that runs between a Soul Crystal and the people it loved, a bond of the Attraction Layer. At death it attenuates slowly and never severs entirely, and its living end stays in the mourner's own Crystal.

The Parunic Echo is the trade's word for a practitioner's Essence Signature, carried in the Aetheric Residue of every working they made and legible to any reader from Flourishing up. Residue is loud for some hours after a working. Then it thins on a schedule toward a floor it never passes, so that a patient reader finds it years on.

Those three paragraphs are the first manuscript's, unchanged. A mourner's grief is the thread's living end, and it is the thread the commissions pull.

This edition adds two things. Mechanica residue does not thin at all. And to the three it adds the Trace, where the ground keeps it. A Trace laid where its maker was bound, in ground that keeps records, stays past the nine days and waits there for its decision to be delivered. In ground that keeps nothing it thins with the rest.

A reconstruction made two years after a death draws on these four and on nothing else, and the commission the fourth chapter touches drew on exactly these.

Every record in the ground spends as it is read. The archivists below the gate learn it on the first page they wear through, and a practitioner learns it on the first site he empties. A careful reading takes a little. A coarse one takes all of it, and leaves nothing for a second. Set the instrument before you begin, and write down what the ground gave.

## Chapter the Third · On the Tria Prima of the Persistent Dead

Furveus reads the Tria Prima onto the living Crystal: Sulphur to the Core, Salt to the Shell, Mercury to the Attraction Layer. The reading is his. It is sound, and it is the one the trade now teaches. For the dead I use a modified reading, and I say so here so that nobody takes it for his.

In my reading Sulphur is the Volitional Trace, the part that points. Salt is the Corporeal Residue with the Aetheric Shell joined to it, the part that is fixed and can be weighed. Mercury is the Noospheric Echo, the part that goes between, carrying habit and voice from the dead to whoever stands near. The reading is useful only for the dead, and only inside the window. Outside it, use his.

When all three are present in one body, with a Shell still sounding, and the site cannot take the residue, the Crossing stalls on its own. Sealed ground does it. So does a field already saturated past what its Wellspring can receive. The result is a revenant.

A revenant walks because the body still carries Salt enough to move, Mercury enough to repeat, and Sulphur enough to point it. It has habits and a direction, and nothing that weighs them. A household that has lost a man and sees him cross the yard at the hour he always crossed it will swear on the hearth that it thinks. Let them swear. Do not write it down.

A revenant fades when the diffusion completes. Nobody needs to kill it. The counter is to move the body off the ground that will not take it, or to ground its charge with a ring of Stillgate Ash, which the Index sells at the Journeyman gate. Whoever stands at the ring pays, since living Essence at the ring bleeds into the ash along with the dead's.

Villages that know the old remedies pack their dead in snow, or carry them off the family's ground to a cold room nobody sleeps in. They are right, and the body does not care why.

Keep the three apart in the mind even when they lie together on the slab. A practitioner who reads the Sulphur and calls it the person has begun one of the seven failures of the fifth chapter. So has one who reads the Mercury and calls it the person.

## Chapter the Fourth · Glyphic Syntax and the Mortalis Categories

Every Mortalis working is written in four roles, and in this order: Authorization, Boundary, Direction, Sealing. The Authorization says who acts, and on what warrant. Parun carries the warrant, and any Latin spoken over it is an order laid on top. The Boundary says where the working reaches and when it ends. The Direction says where the current goes. The Sealing closes it.

A working with a gap in the four has no end written on it. The room writes one.

The Mortalis Categories bind wherever a circle has signed. Where no circle has signed they are advice, and the advice is sound. Each is given here with its floor, the least Stage at which a Crystal-bearer may undertake it; its reservation, the rank at which the Bench of Attribution reserves it; its cost; its tell; and its counter. Costs are stated in what the working takes. That is a share of reserve, the depletion of the site, the waste heat off the Shell, and, from Category Two upward, a tick on the Veil's Drift Scale for whoever stands in the working. A reserve grows with Stage, so the same working is a heavier share of a Refraction reserve than of a Realization one, and the practitioner standing at a Category's floor pays most for it.

### Mortalis Category One · Residue Identification and Forensic Analysis

**Floor:** Flourishing, the fourth Stage. **Reservation:** the Adept gate.

The Authorization is [Ie] Perception. The Boundary is a perimeter paced from the body outward to where the gauge falls back to the room's own reading, with a term of one day and a night, or until sealed. Where the Boundary must be assayed later, draw it in Gravetide Ink, whose line a residual field can be assayed against. The Direction is [Ur] Balance, and the reading draws nothing. The Sealing is [Flx] Flux, sealed by [Tp] Topology on a Fixatio chain, which attenuates cleanly and leaves no open boundary.

This is the formula. Do not improvise it.

**Cost.** Small, and the reader pays it. The kit is a field coil, a gauge and a Fixatio-anchored tin for scrapings. The heat off the reader's Shell is the only waste, and whatever is read of the ground is spent.

A forensic reading of a suspicious death, done to the formula, gives the Division four lines: the Shell's identity and its Wellsprings, whether an Echo is held, how far the Corporeal Residue has paid, and whether a Trace lies in the ground. It gives no cause of death. That is the physician's.

**Tell.** Done right, it leaves the room as it found the room, less what was read.

**Counter.** None is needed. Its failure is an improvised Direction. A reader who lets [Ur] slip begins to draw, and a draw on a death-room is the first step toward the Open Shell.

### Mortalis Category Two · Residue Stabilisation and Containment

**Floor:** Glory, the sixth Stage. **Reservation:** the Expert gate.

Category Two holds a residue in place long enough for it to be read or moved. It contains a held Echo. It steadies a site that is taking in more than it can hold. Furveus's protocol, modified for this work, is Appendix I.

The work is slow and dull and nobody thanks the man who does it. A battlefield gives it the most employment. Several thousand bodies enter the nine days in one hour on ground that cannot take their residue at that rate, and whatever does not diffuse pools. Containment there is a matter of rings laid in the right order across a field, and of moving the dead before the eighth day, and it is only worth doing before the pooling. Afterward there is nothing to contain. Most of what goes wrong at a field goes wrong because the pits were dug on the twelfth.

**Cost.** A once-only share of reserve for any standing ring, paid by whoever lays it. Waste heat. A tick on the Drift Scale for every operator inside the Boundary. Medicine steadies Shells and heals bodies. No Draft refills a reserve, and a practitioner who reaches for one after a containment has already misjudged the price of it.

**Tell.** The operator comes off it grey at the mouth and cold at the fingers, and the room is quieter than a room should be.

**Counter.** Stillgate Ash, and withdrawal. A containment that is failing is not saved by the man inside it staying longer. Bring him out, change the ring, and send in the other.

### Mortalis Category Three · Volitional Trace Completion

**Floor:** Refraction, the seventh Stage, with individual review. **Reservation:** the Expert gate, with individual review.

This is the working Gisli Draycott built the means for, and the working Ivor Strom ran at scale at Genesio.

> **In the margin, in the author's later hand:** *The chain held at Genesio because it was well made, whoever signed for it.*

The floor stands at Refraction because Refraction is where Trace-compulsion begins.

Completion is a Greater Summons. The vessel is poured, and it is a Draft like any other lot, graded by Class, Fidelity and Carry, and priced. Ordinary commissions run on stock of the first three Classes. Class IV enters only when rendered remains go into the pour.

The identity in it is the maker's. It is drawn from his own Core. The Lexicon's word for a detached fragment of the caster's own Core is Eidolon, and it is the Eidolon that speaks in the vessel with the dead's habits. Kwon Mu-jin's gloss of the Aphorism says that whoever draws on Mortalis to rebuild what has come apart summons himself, arranged in their image. The gloss is his. By the mechanism it is literal.

The Trace is reached by Vocatia, by recognition. Vocatia gives nothing to a summoner who lacks the standing he claims. At the Authorization the Continuum reads the practitioner's own Attraction Layer into the working, declared or not. That is the door the Mirror Trace comes through.

The Boundary is drawn in Gravetide Ink. The Sealing is the Mortalis seal, written or cut, in Gravemark Ink, and it closes on [Vor] Return as its condition: it ends when the Trace's decision is delivered. The Index enters Gravemark at the Adept rank and reserves it at the Expert gate. Its lawful sources are three, and the trade uses more than the three produce.

Short ink makes a short seal.

The maker's mark on the vessel and the cutter's hand in the seal are how the Bench traces a completion to its workers. Both can be read. A completion done privately, unentered and for a fee, puts the breach on the seller, and on the Master of the Circle who seals it, personally.

**Cost.** A once-only share of the practitioner's reserve, paid at the seal, which no Draft returns. The site depletes, and ground drawn on for completions at scale scars. At Genesio the floor of the hall and the bark of its trees hold pale patches where the ground gave up everything it had stored, and nothing left in that stone can be woken. The hall read Whirlpool while it was drawn, and fell back a rung to Riptide when the last Trace in it was spent. The gauge at the gate below the Archivum read twenty-six at the thaw of 702, and twenty on the morning after the last Trace in the hall was spent. The practitioner ticks on the Drift Scale.

The sky keeps its own account. Neros's column showed the Weight stepping out of its station some turns after each cluster of wakings at Genesio. The debt is booked on the site and on the operators, and it is collected late, in another kind.

**Tell.** The gauge over ground drawn for completions climbs through the seasons, where drawn ground should fall. A completed body ends in one breath when its sentence is finished.

**Counter.** A ring of Stillgate Ash. A Gravetide Boundary drawn to the working's true reach. The delayed sealing of Appendix I.

I wrote the first manuscript's method before I had seen it done, and these sentences stood beside it at the same table. They stand here beside what replaced it. Do not animate a vessel you cannot seal before you sleep. Do not animate one for the mourner. He will ask you to leave the seal off, and you will want to oblige him. Do not animate one in which you hold a stake. Of everything in this book, this is the working I am most afraid of.

### Mortalis Category Four · Full Resurrection

**Floor:** none. **Reservation:** no gate.

Category Four is the attempt to rebuild the whole Crystal system of a person who has crossed. It takes in the Core and the Shell and the Attraction Layer, the Echo called back off the Soul Plane, the residue drawn back out of its Wellspring, and the Name-record reopened. The Accord does not authorise it, and it will not. It has no Bench and no review. I set down no glyph of it here, since a chain written once is a chain copied.

An application goes on the applicant's record and nowhere else.

## Chapter the Fifth · The Seven Unmoorings: Case Studies in the Misapplication of Mortalis Law

The cases below are documented. Names are altered where families asked, and mechanisms are not. Of the seven I have seen three, and a fourth once, at a distance I kept. One I have from the Archivum's records, and the Misnaming from my own pages. The Averted Regard's confession stands as the first manuscript wrote it, under a head that is new, like the other six.

Each is given by cause, product, marker and resolution. Four fail in the working: the Mirror Trace, the Worn Habit, the Incomplete Sealing and the Open Shell. The Averted Regard and the False Vocation fail in the one who works. The Misnaming fails in everyone he teaches.

Where one of the stages of the Work, turned against itself, drives a case, the stage is named as Kwon Mu-jin's Codex names it. Calcination and distillation, as the bench practises them, are operations only, and the currents that stand behind those two stages are Cinerion and Sublimare. Sublimare's own operation is fractional distillation, a liquor parted by the order in which its fractions rise. The separation and the cleanse below are Sublimare-aligned.

### The Mirror Trace

**How it occurs.** At the Authorization the Continuum reads the practitioner's Attraction Layer into the working. A Trace seeking completion takes the nearest committed intent in reach as its template, and the nearest is his. It borrows his direction and gives it back bent toward its own decision.

**What it produces.** Alignment. What the Trace is charged toward reads to him as precisely what he intended all along, and he pursues it with a clarity he has not had in years and would not give back.

**Marker.** A third reader, standing outside the working, hears the practitioner's Attraction Layer and the Trace sounding one tone where two should sound. The practitioner denies it, and his denial is perfectly sincere.

**Resolution.** Separation of the Attraction Layer by a third party at the Expert gate, Sublimare-aligned, parting what the working joined by the order in which the two rise. He will resist. The resistance is the Trace protecting itself, and it uses his voice.

One case. A practitioner at the Expert gate, whose name is withheld at her family's asking, was engaged to read a Trace laid by a shipwright in the ground of the yard where he died with a hull half-framed on the slip. She was to read it and file it. She read it, and within a turn she had bought the hull, hired its crew back, and begun to finish it to a line she said she had always meant to draw. She had never built so much as a skiff. Her partner brought the case to me. A third reader separated her at the Expert gate over two sittings, and at the second she struck him. She finished nothing afterward, for a season, and she has since gone back to reading.

**Driver.** Dissolution Perverted, the Corruption of Submission.

### The Worn Habit

**How it occurs.** Long work in one place beside a held Echo. The Echo carries habit, and habit settles on the nearest Shell. Over turns the dead's habitual pattern comes to sit on the practitioner's Shell, over his own.

**What it produces.** He takes the dead man's chair. He holds his cup in the dead man's grip. The people who knew the dead see it in him long before he does.

**Marker.** Its nearest relative is Melancholic Erosion: weeping at the wrong hour, and a memory now and then of a kitchen he has never stood in.

**Resolution.** Relocation, and a Sublimare-aligned cleanse of the Shell. It works. It feels like losing something.

The Archivum's records hold two cases, both of readers kept a long season in one house of the dead to take down a held Echo before the house was sold. In the first the reader's own wife asked the Heralds to send her husband home, since the man who came back on rest days took his tea the way the dead man of that house had taken it, and would sit in no other chair. In the second nobody asked, and the reader stayed.

**Driver.** None. This is proximity, and proximity has no motive.

### The Incomplete Sealing

**How it occurs.** Haste. A Trace's charge misjudged. A failure of nerve at the last glyph. Short or diluted Gravemark Ink, which is the commonest cause, since the lawful sources are fewer than the use.

**What it produces.** An unsealed rune. The working has no end written on it, and the ambient pressure of the site completes it on terms of its own, into a haunting that misfires until somebody finds the rune and breaks it. The body goes on. It grows more itself over time.

> **In the margin, in the author's later hand:** *Growth past the seal is the one yield here worth measuring. Seal the lid, and watch.*

It accumulates: habits it did not have at the pour, and a direction nobody gave it. At the terminal state it resists the delayed sealing.

**Marker.** A habit kept at a fixed hour that nobody poured into it: a place laid, a door gone to, the same hour each day. Its household will tell you it has become more like her. Believe the household about the change, and nothing else.

The first manuscript's lines follow. Do not leave the last glyph for the morning. If your hand stops over the seal, give the pen to a man whose hand does not stop, and let him seal it while you watch. A body with no end written on it is what this method makes possible when a hand stops.

The case I saw myself was a body poured in haste to beat the ninth day, and sealed in Gravemark cut by half with lampblack, since the seller could not get more. It held for a turn. In the second turn it began laying the table for four in a house that had been three since the burial, and in the third it laid for five. Nobody in the house could say who the fifth place was for. The household sent for help when it stopped sleeping.

**Resolution.** The delayed sealing, set out in Appendix I, with the report of the two practitioners who used it.

**Driver.** Separation Perverted, the Corruption of Non-Commitment.

### The Open Shell

**How it occurs.** A practitioner outruns his harmonisation. Death-work is lawful only as far as his own harmonisation with Mortalis carries him, and when he runs past it at scale, the working takes what it was never given. It draws finished output from a donor's Crystal, and the donor is dead. The law files that as extraction, which is Mechanica. Statute XXVI says why the boundary stays open: closing needs a warrant, and a working with no Authorization to answer to never had one. What it opened stays open after the practitioner has gone home.

**What it produces.** The practitioner's Shell stays open in the Mortalis band, and every ending anywhere near him comes in. The afflicted have their own phrase for it, the weight of all the ends. He finishes other people's sentences. He finds himself in rooms where somebody is dying, with no memory of the walk there.

**Marker.** The ground. Mechanica residue does not thin. Carry a coil and a gauge together and they disagree at every stand, and the Accord's text on Mechanica says the discrepancy is the size of what is being taken.

**Resolution.** The practitioner can be treated, by communion with one of three currents from three Families: Ascensio of Vectoria, Fixatio of Materia, Benediction of Vitalia. This takes months. The ground never heals. Of the seven it is the one whose harm does not stay in the room: the ground it opens runs on across a region. The one case I have seen at scale is under review, and I leave it there.

**Driver.** Conjunction Perverted, the Corruption of Instrumental Union. It joins a dead donor's law to a living working because the joining serves, and the donor, being dead, is not asked.

### The Averted Regard

**How it occurs.** The Still Gate's own failure, Equilibration, is taken up as a way of life. The practitioner's regard, which is his attention and his esteem both, turns from the living toward the dead, until the living read to him as drafts of their residue.

**What it produces.** He is absent from rooms he is sitting in. He is comfortable. By every measure he keeps, he is thriving. The people who love him describe him to one another as already gone.

**Marker.** He prefers the far side of the line Mortalis governs, and he reports the preference as a finding.

I put my own case under this head, because it belongs nowhere else.

My father kept the retting ponds of our parish. The parish sank its pulled flax in them under stones and sods and let the water rot the gum from the fibre, and his whole craft was the hour he lifted it. Early, and the fibre held to the core. Late, and it was tow. He taught me the test: draw a stalk, snap it across, and see whether the fibre slips. One autumn I weighted a bundle into the far corner of the last pond, where he did not go, and left it there past the hour to see what the rot did next. He found it before the frost. It had gone grey and soft and kept the shape of a bundle, and it came apart in his hands. He burned it on the bank.

I have wanted to know what the rot does next on every day since.

If I am ever found describing what the dead leave as more interesting than the people in the room, treat this document as a confession. Read this chapter back to me from its head, and do not let me answer until it is finished.

> **In the margin, in the author's later hand:** *Nobody read it back. The living remain the duller company.*

**Resolution.** No working reaches it. Other people reach it, over a long time, if they refuse to be read as residue and keep refusing.

**Driver.** Fermentation Perverted, the Corruption of Catastrophic Germination: the rot valued for its yield.

### The Misnaming

**How it occurs.** It is the habit of describing residues in the grammar of the living. The Echo is called a mind and the Trace a will, and the Corporeal Residue becomes a body that remembers.

**What it produces.** It afflicts the field and spares the man who starts it. A reader trained on mind and will and memory hears the dead answer him before he has learned to read them. He teaches his students to listen for the answer, and they teach theirs, and every room they read afterward holds more agency than it held. The harm goes out in every copy.

**Marker.** The words. Write what a residue does. Never write what it wants. The Echo is weighted toward a room, the Trace is charged toward a task, and the body is cooling. Those three words are the whole of the nomenclature a student needs in his first year.

**Resolution.** It requires the original practitioner to accept that his language has caused harm, and then to change it in every copy he can reach.

> **In the margin, in the author's later hand:** *The rule is for students. A master may say she, and the file is built on his word.*

I have been told that my own earlier writing carries this. I am reviewing it. The review does not please me.

**Driver.** Calcination Perverted, the Corruption of Conviction. The second volume of Mu-jin's Codex has already set here every practitioner who insists an impression is the whole person.

### The False Vocation

**How it occurs.** It takes a practitioner at Refraction or above who has been harmonised with Mortalis for many years. The Continuum repairs its own boundary, and it presents unfinished Traces to whoever is able to finish them. Completion steadies the ground, and the Continuum reinforces whatever steadies it. Over years the practitioner's Attraction Layer is taken up into that repair.

**What it produces.** Purpose. He completes one Trace and the next is waiting at the door. Asked what he wants for himself, he cannot say.

**Marker.** A stillness with no hurry anywhere in it, the bearing of something with no experience of time passing. Were he to die, his own Trace would lie in his ground uncharged.

I saw it once, at a distance I kept. A man I had counted among the finest readers of Mortalis I knew crossed the Genesio waystation at dawn with nothing hurried left in him. Frost was on the rail. He stopped at the edge of the platform and looked at the mountains a long while, and he looked sent for.

I first entered it in my notes as something close to sanctity. The entry is corrected here. The peace was a man with nothing of his own left to want, and the ground he steadied will outlast him by a long way.

**Resolution.** None is documented. There is a theory of one: somebody there for the person and not for the function, early, before the function has taken all of him.

**Driver.** Coagulation Perverted, the Corruption of Tyrannous Finality: the interim form sealed and called final.

## Chapter the Sixth · The Proper Relationship to What Remains

Furveus said to me once, at the stove in Castlefall, with the lime pits breathing under the floor, "The dead don't lie, Malphas. Be afraid of the living who won't look at them." I told him the dead frightened me. We were both right.

I have sat the night with the newly dead more often than with the living. I have sat in the Archivum's cold room and in houses where nobody else would sit, with my coat on and my breath standing in front of my face, and I have never once been bored. I set that down here, at the end of the book, because it is where the book began.

The disposition this work asks for is the alchemist's, which is accurate attention. Look at what is on the bench. Name it for what it does. Do not look away from it because it is terrible, and do not look at it a moment longer than the work needs because it is beautiful.

What the dead leave is the most honest record a person makes. Nobody arranged it for a reader. It holds the tone they laid into the walls by living against them, the thread that runs from them to whoever grieves, and the mark of every working they made.

Careless work with what remains unmoors whoever does it.

> Entered for this edition: the seven moorings lost are his own direction, in the Mirror Trace; his own character, in the Worn Habit; the line between what he made and what is making itself, in the Incomplete Sealing; the present, in the Open Shell; the living, in the Averted Regard; accuracy, in the Misnaming, which takes the whole field with him; and himself entirely, in the False Vocation.

The preparation for this work is knowing who you are well enough that other people's habits and other people's grief do not replace your own. No book teaches it. I have written the book anyway.

Kwon Mu-jin will read this and think it is about him. He will be right. I wrote the entire text with myself in mind.

You find yourself in what remains.

And then, if the seal holds, you come back out.

## Appendix I · The Stabilisation Protocol for Volatile Residue, as Developed by Furveus and Modified for Mortalis Work by Malphas

> Clearance Tier IV Restricted (Mortalis Operations). Printed in full in the Accord's copy.

The protocol is Furveus's. He developed it in his years at the Heralds' Medical University, for the containment of volatile residue in the sick and the newly dead. I have modified it for Mortalis work. It is written here in my hand, since his hand no longer writes a formula small enough to read. He will know every step of it. The pair and the withdrawal passed from it into the field's protocols long ago, and are printed here as the field keeps them. The ash, the rune and the medicine are his; the Boundary and the sealing are my modifications.

It is the defensive counter to a failing seal. Use it when a Category Three body has run past its term, when an Incomplete Sealing has begun to accumulate, and when any working on the dead has no end written on it and the room has begun to write one.

**First, the pair.** Never alone. Two practitioners go, one inside the radius and one outside it. The one inside reports, the one outside confirms or contradicts, and the contradiction is the diagnosis. The one outside carries the gauge and reads it aloud at every stand. Agree a count before you begin. If the one inside stops speaking, the one outside counts his breaths aloud, and at the agreed count calls him out by name.

**Second, the Boundary.** Find the working's true reach. Pace from the body outward until the gauge falls back to the room's own reading, mark the pace, and walk the circle at that distance. Draw the Boundary there in Gravetide Ink, mixed on bone where bone is to hand, since the slab is the surface the ink answers to. Write the term as the formula writes it: one day and a night, or until sealed. A Boundary drawn short of the true reach contains nothing. A loose Boundary clause runs on after the working it was meant to end.

**Third, the ash.** Lay a ring of Stillgate Ash inside the Boundary, close about the body. The ash grounds the body's charge and lets it finish its passage. Living Essence at the ring bleeds into it too, so whoever stands at the ring pays in his own Shell. He will come off it grey at the mouth and cold at the fingers. Change who stands at the ring before either of you has stood there long.

**Fourth, withdrawal.** Withdrawal is a step of the procedure. Take it the moment the one outside contradicts the one inside twice running, and take it without discussion. Come up, mark the perimeter from outside, and begin again at the first step.

**Fifth, the rune.** The unsealed rune lies somewhere on the body or on the floor under it, and until it is found the body goes on writing its own end. Read for it at [Ie] Perception with [Ur] Balance at the Direction, drawing nothing. Break it before anything is sealed. A seal laid over an open rune seals the haunting in.

**Sixth, the delayed sealing.** A practitioner at the Expert gate writes the Mortalis seal in Gravemark Ink of full strength, and closes it on [Vor] Return. If the Trace's decision can still be delivered, the seal ends when it is. If it cannot, the ash takes the charge, and the seal ends with the charge. Never dilute the ink to stretch it. A preparation that fails goes volatile, and it stays volatile.

**Seventh, the medicine.** For whoever came off the ring: white arsenic, one tenth of a grain, three times a day in pill, for a turn, against the pallor and the cold hands. It steadies the Shell. It heals nothing in the Crystal and refills nobody's reserve, and no Draft does. The margin is narrow. A bottle of them holds several deaths. For the gut that will not move after a long containment: calomel, five grains, once. Never give it with salt meat, or with brine on the table, since the same grains turn partly corrosive in that company.

**The report.** Two practitioners of the Accord used this protocol on a body that had been poured for a completion and never sealed. It had run past its term and grown more itself. When they came into the room it was standing at the window. It spoke to them in a voice its household knew and asked them to open a door. It stood inside the ash and would not lie down.

The one inside reported a single direction in it. The one outside contradicted him: two. The contradiction was the diagnosis. The Trace had been delivered at the pour, and the second direction was the room's own, written into the open rune over the turns since.

They withdrew once, and came back. They found the rune in the floorboards under its left heel, broke it, laid the seal in Gravemark of full strength, and closed it on Return. It ended in the fourth division of the night, in one breath. The one who had stood at the ring was grey for a season after, and he did not stand at another.

## Appendix II · On the Necrocursica and the Alftian Codex

Kwon Mu-jin sent me a fair copy of his third volume, care of the Archivum, with a letter asking what I thought. The letter asked me a second thing. I leave that where Draycott left it.

The Codex and this treatise describe the same ground from its two sides. His is phenomenology: what it is to stand in a room while the Work happens, and to be the one it happens in. Mine is mechanism: what the room holds, what it will bear, and what it costs. He writes what the Work did to him, in the room where it was done, and I write what it does to whoever stands where he stood. File them adjacent. A reader holding only one has half a map, and he will walk off the edge of it.

The third volume returns to his own corruption, Separation Perverted, the Corruption of Non-Commitment, the perfect diagnosis and the absent act, which he named in himself in the second. He is right about it. He has also done what that corruption withholds, which is to set a threshold before witnesses and cross it.

I add the Misnaming, in part. He names in order to bound, and he bounds well, and the name he gave the fourth residue leans nearer a will than the thing it names. The first chapter says why I let it stand.

He has sworn a vow against his own pen: "I will not write down what I have decided not to stop." I have read it more than once. I have sworn nothing myself, and I will not answer a vow with an opinion.

Our trade has a general corruption, and no stage of the Work owns it. A scholar describes his own errors under another man's name, from a safe distance, and calls the description a case. The first volume of the Codex did it. The second did it with me, by name, for its Submission. It was accurate about me, and about him as well, and then it turned, on the page on Separation. The third does not do it at all. It gives no cases of the four corruptions it names, because he will teach only what he has found in himself.

I have no standing whatsoever to criticise him for any of it.

He came to Genesio in the spring of 702, when the passes had opened, and asked for me on the reading floor. I did not come up.

It is the finest writing in the field. The fair copy is in his own hand, which is worse than it was.

It is still the better book.

Malphas

## Phenomenon lines (per method)

- Two Instruments: a real phenomenon, differential measurement. Two independent transducers read one quantity along a transect, and a systematic residual between them locates a sink (compare a Wheatstone bridge's null, or two gauges straddling a leak in a pipe). The WOTR law is the Mechanica page's "the discrepancy is the size of what is being taken", with R62-6's residue floor. No Physics Domain, Wellspring or Category applies: it is a skill.
- The Borrowed Read: chain-of-observation practice (the survey party's outside observer, Protocol III; self-baselining, Protocol VIII). Its fault falls out of the mechanism: a Medium-type change reads as her own confabulation (Walking:111).
- The Entry: chain of custody. Fixatio holds what it is given (Night Watch:53). Its fault: it proves the sample and never the hand.

### The lead's frame, confirmed with one correction

Confirmed: Volume II was deposited in 700 IC (R65-1). The Papers run from the case's opening to the hand-delivery of the intercepted letter, inside 700 to 701 IC, before Volume III. ### One-paragraph shape

A Night Watch Society investigator from Urbis, a woman whose Crystal nobody ever woke, is handed a file by the head of the Research and Archives Division's Urbis office: the first two volumes of Kwon Mu-jin's Codex (just withdrawn under the Division's seal), a letter in grey wax addressed to Kwon and held unopened, and an astronomer's readings showing the Weight moving again. Halveth's condition for access is that she keep field notes and not a report. She goes where the Codex went: the lower room at Castlefall, where a pharmacologist has covered a circle with a rug; the widower's house in the eastern lowlands, where the likeness of a dead woman spoke for eleven days and on the last one led him to a sealed paper; the Heralds' Medical University, where the physician who poured the vessel counts his breach and his liability in that order; the Genesio Archivum, where an old paper by Ivor Strom has sat unread for twelve years and the gauge at the passage stair has been climbing all year. She reads nothing herself. Every figure is an instrument's or another person's, and the one time the ground reads her, it is an archivist who notices she has stopped talking. The Weight's steps follow the Genesio reports by some turns, always later. Draycott writes to her. She meets him on the Castlefall platform at the second bell, where he holds his weight down so that she can stand, and loses his hold once. He tells her where Strom is and will not tell her what the ground has held longest. She carries the held letter up a stair in Urbis to a young man with one eye, one arm and one lung, gives it to him, and does not say the words Draycott gave her to say.

## 1. THE AUTHOR

Built so the full card (PA1, R61-61) can be made from it: FOW loadout, voice block, pronunciation line, dated header, voice fingerprint and casting entry. **The name stays AUTHOR.** Woman `[CALL]`; nothing in this brief turns on it but pronouns.

### Office and warrant `[canon: The Night Watch; C-087; PA2, R61-62]`

- **The Night Watch Society** is one body with the Night Watch: a crown-chartered society, older than any signed circle of the Accord, that investigates illegal magic "and the phenomena the law has no name for". In a chancery it is the Night Watch; on its own inserts and in its members' mouths, the Night Watch Society. It holds the crown's warrant and answers to the state. Its parts: chapters with a calibration and assay bench, walkers on fixed walks, coil-carriers, a bulletin office that prints filings as inserts on the cheapest rag, the Night Register (a desk on Cutler Row, in a northern seat, not in Urbis).
- **Her office** `[proposal]`: warranted investigator of the **Urbis chapter**, on the crown's warrant. The Urbis chapter is new to canon (the page names the Timberline and two eastern timber chapters). She began as a walker on an Urbis walk (the slow-main streets and the cut districts east of the high street), which is where she learned the gauge; she has carried the crown's warrant on cases for about twelve years. She files as the Society files: **`[Byname], [Initial]., Urbis chapter, on the crown's warrant`** (the Tally form: "Tally, N., Timberline chapter, North Ferriby walk").
- **The warrant**: a folded paper under the crown's seal, naming her and the case. **It runs in the crown's land** (Urbis; the eastern lowlands) and **not in Ketsuen** (Castlefall, Genesio), which has no crown and governs by the Proving `[CALL; canon: Ketsuen page]`. In Ketsuen she can ask and cannot compel, and she enters her presence on the Castlefall station's record, which anyone may read.
- **The joint case** (Isaac's PA2 note): Halveth opened it on the Division's side, hands her the file, annotates her notes, and makes "not a report" **a condition of access** to Division-sealed material. The logic, stated once by AUTHOR and never explained by Halveth `[CALL]`: a report is a filing, a filing goes to the bulletin office, and the bulletin office prints what it is given; field notes held under the Division's seal are not printed.
- **Why her** `[proposal]`: the chapter was told the Division asked for someone "who reads nothing". Halveth gives no reason. AUTHOR guesses one, wrongly or not, and enters the guess.

### Character first (R49-28 order: refuses, survived, believes)

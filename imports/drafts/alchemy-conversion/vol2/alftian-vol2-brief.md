## 0. Shape, and the lead's timeline call

**THE LEAD'S TIMELINE CALL (state it in the author notes as a call, not canon).** Kwon Mu-jin was born 677 IC. Volume I covers fourteen to nineteen and is stamped 696 IC. **Volume II picks up after the king's hall (he is nineteen) and runs to about twenty-three; it is stamped 700 IC, before the Ashgate road** (he is twenty-four on that road, 701 IC, R62-1). This carries the Doyun material (ST11: Doyun met at Sum-gol when Mu-jin is 22 to 24) and supersedes K3's "about nineteen" estimate for where Volume II ends. Volume III is written after the Ashgate road, about six years after Volume I circulated. The Accord appears only from Volume III (MJ10).

**One-paragraph shape.** Volume II is the same young man continuing the same book four years on: written at Sum-gol and on the road at twenty-two and twenty-three, about the years nineteen to twenty-three. It opens where Volume I's café left off: he comes back to Castlefall and finds that Furveus has taken the commission Draycott brokered, a grieving nobleman's dead wife to be given back "close". He tells Furveus plainly not to, and tells Draycott plainly what he is selling. He finally reads Malphas's manuscript, at Malphas's own asking, and finds in it a brilliant architecture for rebuilding the dead from what they leave behind, and one footnote that says never call the result the soul. The commission goes ahead; Furveus comes back with a result worse than his hypothesis; the Aphorism Mu-jin glossed in private turns out to describe it. Draycott writes to him about a fourth residue that keeps intent, a letter that challenges Mu-jin's own gloss, and he does not answer it. Neros shows him the Weight standing a little out of its place. He leaves the Monastery that has started calling him the Thrice-Great, goes home, and takes the empty room in the school at Sum-gol, where a boy named Doyun weeps for a cat he watched sicken and did nothing about, and asks the question that makes Mu-jin name the corruption in himself. He corrects everyone in the book except himself, and the book shows it. It ends with his sister arriving on the road to join him, and the letter still unanswered in the manuscript.

## VIII. Traits

**THE LEDGER (Primary Trait)**

**The Ledger** · Innate · Dominion-Harmonics junction — **a living account of every conscripted soul, spread across planes, functioning whether or not he is personally present at any given node.** Its active upkeep is included in the release rate, with each further working charged separately. Suspended dust and the gathering inhalation show a node taking the shared pulse. Its reach ends where its surviving links end. An equal-Stage working that cuts a feeding link denies that node its supply; the Disruption glyph [Uur] can break the thread's coherence without requiring divine authority.

**ANTITHESIS (Secondary Trait)**

**Antithesis** · Tempered · Materia/Inverse — **splits his own body along a mirror-line into two fully committed combatants directed from one coordinating will.** The split costs 0.02E EU and its two-body hold costs 0.001E EU per second, charged once through the Antithesis technique. A bright seam appears before the bodies part, with one inhalation becoming two. The bodies keep their documented strength and share their source and directing attention. Force or a disruptive working driven through that seam before it closes stops the split; maintaining different threats against both bodies taxes the single will.

**MAHUO SOUL-POPULATION (Inherited Trait)**

**Mahuo Soul-Population** · Lineage — multiple inhabiting souls under one sovereign identity. ***The structural basis that taught him to house other people's souls in the first place.*** The inherited population has no separate activation charge; making its members act together spends the Ledger's hold cost and his attention. Several breaths can be heard beneath one voice, and strain draws out the family plural. The population supplies neither infinite reserve nor new bodies. Interference that makes the linked voices disagree interrupts shared work while leaving the sovereign person alive.

**PROJECTED VESSEL (Passive Trait)**

**Projected Vessel** · Resonant. The "true" Cozbi sits, physically, **three planes distant from wherever his combat body operates.** Damage to a projection doesn't touch him directly — *though a sufficiently precise working can reach through it to the claims it carries.* Each active vessel costs 0.01E EU to form and 0.0001E EU per second to carry, allowing 2 hours, 28 minutes and 20 seconds from full to the tenth with no other drain. Its breath and shadow briefly fall out of step as the distant will answers. The standing separation grants no defence to its local connection: a prepared peer can trace that mismatch and sever the link, ending the vessel without having to reach the true body.

## IX. Domain

**THE LEDGER (Domain)**

**Domain · The Ledger.** At Stage XIV, **Domain and practitioner are functionally indistinguishable.** The Ledger is not a place he maintains so much as **a condition his existence imposes on any region his conscripted lattice touches.** Wellsprings sour. Essence drawn from infected sources comes back curdled — ***"as though something had used the spring before the practitioner arrived and left an opinion behind in the water."*** OPEN ACCOUNT opens and powers the active field through the surviving lattice; each touched node is a boundary and a possible break. Its local colour shift and held breath begin before the field settles. From a full connected reserve it holds for 2 hours at E/8,000 EU per second with no deposits or other spending, then closes at the tenth. His existing ambient imprint remains distinct from this paid hold. Comparable Dominion can contest a node, and severing its feed removes that node from the active field. Residue does not supply a fresh reserve.

## X. Techniques

**ANTITHESIS · Split (Technique)**

Splits his body along a mirror-line into two independently fighting forms. **Matter and its contradiction pressed to the same coordinating point until the working reflects rather than divides.** Two full combatants, no documented strength split. **A working that reads the reversion's shape before it completes can decline to let the split exist.** *Dova's grip did exactly this*. The split costs 0.02E EU, then 0.001E EU per second while both bodies remain. From full, that reaches the tenth after 14 minutes and 40 seconds with no other drain. The bright dividing seam and paired inhalations give its tell. Force or Disruption through the forming seam is an answer available to a prepared peer; the two bodies share one reserve and one directing attention.

**ROOT SEVERANCE (Technique)**

**Unwrites matter rather than destroying it.** Matter offered as pure covenant, Anti-Matter contradiction poured in equal measure, pressed rather than blended. Total structural unwriting along contact — ***"a hole shaped exactly like a rivet with no memory of ever having held one"***. **None documented once contact is made.** *Avoiding contact is the only noted defence*. A contact costs 0.05E EU. An amber edge and a violet absence meet around the touching hand with a short, dry crack. The unwriting follows actual contact and cannot cross a gap by itself. A loose outer layer shed at contact takes the loss with it before the working reaches what lies beneath. Nothing here restores material already unwritten.

**PNEUMA UNRAVEL STRIKE · Signature (Technique)**

Compounds **every trauma his conscripted chorus carries** into one directed strike. Mnemata's Unravel weaponised — peels resonance, patterning, memory-binding until identity fractures. ***"A hundred stolen lives" behind one strike***. **A Divine-Beast-register presence can decline to be affected by a working built on stolen rather than sovereign authority**. A strike costs 0.1E EU. Every voice takes one breath before the blow, then the inhalation breaks into a harsh chorus. Before contact, an interruption of the lead vessel's breath or feeding thread prevents the chorus arriving together. During contact, a prepared peer's Resilience Ward and refusal of the foreign memory keep the strike from taking a continuous hold. After contact, breaking the lingering thread stops further stripping; it does not return what has already been lost. The shared trauma can also bleed back into Cozbi's own recollection.

**NISUKE, TITAN OF THE IRON THRONE (Summoning Technique)**

Manifests **a soul-forged effigy wearing a Titan's shape and function.** Legion Summoning's ceiling, built from fragments harvested one soul at a time over years. **Cannot be improvised**. **A copy of authority, not authority itself.** *Untested against anything that reads authenticity*. The call costs 0.2E EU, then 0.001E EU per second of manifestation, reaching the tenth after 11 minutes and 40 seconds from full with no other drain. A low groan like an iron seat taking weight precedes its outline. These costs open and maintain the prepared effigy; they do not buy the years of fragments that built it. Its feeding threads carry the same pulse as Cozbi's vessel, and a prepared peer can cut those threads or break the assembled body at their junctions. A destroyed store of fragments cannot be replaced by another call.

**MERGED FORM · Dragon (Transformation Technique)**

Operates **through the fused entity's own sovereign nature rather than claimed authority** — built the creature's dominion into its architecture directly rather than claiming it. **Survived Dhaerin's Rescind fully intact, since Rescind withdraws consent from claims and this was never a claim**. **Rescind-type unbinding confirmed ineffective**. The change costs 0.1E EU, then 0.0005E EU per second, reaching the tenth after 26 minutes and 40 seconds from full with no other drain. Scales climb the body and a second heartbeat becomes audible beneath the borrowed pulse. It requires the surviving fused creature. Its own body still receives physical force: a comparable strike can break its moving parts and interrupt its action without withdrawing any claim. The change does not lift Cozbi's ordinary unmerged body to the creature's strength.

Each spoken release costs one beat before the full rise. A silent release keeps its visible form and its nonverbal sound. The costs below replace one another as the release deepens; they do not restore any reserve already spent.

**BORROWED BREATH (First Release)** · folk name: Cheotjjae puri

Black armoured scale rises along his limbs and the four spiritual arms open at his waist; the near-white aura carries its ghost of black-violet and amber. The Ledger holds its surviving voices to the same pulse, bringing their shared work to the top of its existing Grade without dividing his attention into a hundred separate acts. **OPEN ACCOUNT (Second Release)** · folk name: Duljjae puri

The scale's pale seams continue out through the surviving feeding links, and the ground at each connected node takes the same near-white tint. The drawn breath becomes a low held note. Its total release drain is E/8,000 EU per second, replacing the First Release's rate; it holds for 2 hours from full to the tenth without deposits or other spending. Ground without a surviving link lies outside this paid field, however far its older stain has spread. Released silently, the Domain opens and still sounds, but its linked work keeps its base Grade. An equal-Stage opponent can contest a node by Dominion, sever its feed or fight beyond its edge. After the field closes his Crystal runs Overgrown until a full night's sleep; pressing below the tenth risks a Crystal Fracture Event. Dhaerin's seal still bars the claims it already stripped away.

**NOTHING FORGOTTEN (Third Release)** · folk name: Setjjae puri

Behind the four arms, the surviving soul-records show as overlapping human outlines joined at the ribs by pale threads. An unbleached black-violet fringe forms around the near-white field, and Anamnesis adds a low, many-voiced murmur beneath the held breath. Its total release drain is E/400 EU per second, replacing the lower releases' rates; it holds for 6 minutes from full to the tenth without deposits or other spending. It binds only records and souls still connected to him. Released silently, the shared records remain, but command receives only the Second Release's one-Grade rise. A record erased by a faulty reading is lost to the other voices as well. Afterwards the Crystal runs Overgrown for a day and a night, with an ache through the body. Holding past the tenth risks breaking the Ledger's ability to distinguish one soul's memories from another's, leaving commands attached to the wrong voices.

## Lore

### The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card, the twenty-two archived scenes it appears in, and the record of The Zettari war camp and the Mahuo who came to it. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Cozbi Mahuo was born into the Mahuo line at Sum-gol, the hollow-seat whose name means Breath Hollow. No record keeps the name he was given at birth. Cozbi is a First Compact name, and it names his bond to the souls he carries.

He carried many inhabiting souls under one sovereign identity, a trait of the Mahuo lineage. It first taught him that a body can shelter people who are not its owner. He inherited the family's summoning art with it. Under strain he spoke of himself in the plural, after the Mahuo pattern.

The Zettari and the Moto were at war, and their war crossed Mahuo ground. Their armies trampled towns that were never theirs and used the people in them as livestock, and neither army noticed the third family it was grinding into the mud. No history book recorded the Mahuo dead. Cozbi learned from that, before he could walk without falling, the one lesson he never revised: the strong write the histories.

His family was massacred.

#### The Making

He watched his own people ground into the mud beneath the Zettari and Moto's endless war, and he promised his dying brother: "make them remember what we were, even if they never learn our names." He gave the promise completely. He spent every year after it converting it into a ledger that never closes.

The harvesting ran for decades, long enough to outrun any simple count of his age, and each soul he took became an entry in the Ledger, a living account spread across planes. He learned the forbidden Amalgamation Ritus and built servants from salvage that had never been meant to share one body. He numbered them.

His true body went onto a stone pedestal, planes away from any fight, and vessels went out in its place. At Ashwell, a garrison town of the Moto line, the vessel that crossed the market square was a woman's. It crouched beside the dying captain and laid two fingers on his chest. The dust of the square hung still while everything left in him drained out.

In the same years the Wellsprings soured across the Inner World, the Old World and the far edges of the Outer World. Essence drawn from them came back curdled. No priest or scholar could name what had gone through first. Cozbi had, and he left no name where a question served him better.

He made a son. He folded Essence drafts siphoned from unwilling sources into a spiritual axis and wrapped it in a child's body, with the Mahuo face and golden eyes from a second line he had made to cooperate with his own. The boy slept in a nursery that never needed daylight, and nobody told him what he was.

For years Cozbi watched a woman named Mizuyi through an opening behind her threshold. The night Ma'Kovu Zettari cut it, tracing it home through his brother Rashani's Origin Eye, Cozbi named the cut aloud and filed the correction. He pressed matter and its contradiction together over an armour of Zettari make, and the floor beneath the worktable stopped existing. The armour held. "Fight well, little sovereigns," he told brothers who could no longer hear him.

Beneath his reliquary he kept the Dragon of Calamity in chains, the harvested hatred of dragonkind folded into one body. He drew the boy's blood with a silver needle, worked it into the dragon's foreleg, drank the dragon's own blood from a dish, and vomited it onto the stone. The bond held. Should the dragon fall, every soul it had taken would come to him. He gave the order to loose it himself.

His mirrors walked every house the Moto line still called its own, and gas went through the halls behind them, taking women at their mending and children asleep. When it finished, the line was a handful of names still drawing breath. From a ridge above the burning, he turned his back before the last of the screaming ended.

#### The Cost

Rengai Zettari and Dhaerin Valorin walked for months to reach him, and found him seated on his great spider while it ate a deer. Rengai asked why he had bled a child. Cozbi answered with the Mahuo in the mud, and told him he had not chosen Dougou's wars. Rengai said that changed nothing about the child. Cozbi agreed at once. He had only ever claimed it made sense.

He won through lattice and construct. His own body stayed below the ceiling of his craft, and he arranged never to need a fair fight. Dhaerin found the seam in the borrowed architecture and turned the theft against him. Cozbi called up the effigy he had built in a Titan's shape from fragments harvested one soul at a time, and it struck Rengai down. Then Dhaerin gave his life to a rite that withdrew the world's consent from every claim Cozbi held over a harvested soul. Every claimed soul went in one instant. His composure cracked, rawer than it ever had.

He kept the massacre open. It never healed, and he preserved it on purpose, as fuel. He refused to let his family's ending be over, and he forced endings onto everyone else instead. He called it correction and meant it. Each soul harvested justified the Ledger's continuation, and none of them closed it. He wanted a war large enough that every venerable bloodline would have to write it into its own histories, whether it wanted to or not.

His own line paid for it. The Old World came to count the Mahuo dangerous for what he made of them. In a Zettari war camp, Kwon Mu-jin drafted an Accord that condemns him "without qualification and without appeal to inherited grief as excuse."

#### Where They Stand

Down in the clearing, he reached for his harvested spirits by reflex, and the seal tightened and took more of them. "I allowed myself to become too confident," he said to the moon. Forty-Five came for him. He told it to collect the fallen Zettari, and it carried Rengai away. No record says where.

The seal still holds him. It never reached the dragon, whose dominion he had built into its own nature, or the lattice he had dressed as an extension of himself. In a crater under a ruined mountainside, he folded the merged dragon down into a man's shape and explained why. He called Dhaerin's working elegant, and there was no performance in it.

Dova'Kan read one of his workings before it finished casting. Cozbi's voice sped up. It is the only time the record shows it. He stepped back and said, "Begin with the shred, then." The encounter is unresolved.

His true body sits on the pedestal, and the Ledger works wherever his lattice has touched. His son is still kept unaware. His constructs stutter, and he cares for them. Sancta Lux's Oracle weighs completed acts and returns no reading on his numbered works. The one Mahuo in that order who could explain why has met him once, and neither has filed a record of it. A report in the north holds him dead. It has not been ratified.

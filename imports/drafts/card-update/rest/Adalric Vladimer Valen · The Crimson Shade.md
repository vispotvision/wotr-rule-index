## VIII. Traits

**VAMPIRIC PHYSIOLOGY (Inherited Trait)**

**Vampiric Physiology** · **Stage VII body reinforcement** — supernatural strength, speed and durability. Anima Spirare supports the blood and Petralon carries the force through his planted body. The active reinforcement spends Essence and leaves a red sheen at the loaded joints; when he puts it all behind one limb, the rest carries less. A sideways pull that breaks his footing or a strike at the lightly reinforced side defeats that concentration. Blood and reserve still have to be replenished.

**CURSE OF REJECTANCE (Secondary Trait)**

**Curse of Rejectance** · **Purges toxins, corruptive Essence and mental intrusions automatically.** *Note: Hieronymus Cruciferi carries an identically-named trait. Two sheets, one name, and on Hieronymus it forecloses Spirit Axes entirely. Worth ruling whether these are the same mechanism.* The purge spends his own Essence to clear what entered him. Its tell is a brief blanching at the lips followed by dark sweat along the hairline. It cannot turn away a blade, replace blood already lost or prevent an enemy from exhausting him by making the purge work repeatedly. It does not grant knowledge of a lie merely because the liar speaks within reach.

**ETERNAL ENDURANCE (Passive Trait)**

**Eternal Endurance** · **Operates at full capacity while mana and blood reserves remain stable.** His Core keeps feeding the body while both stores hold. His breathing stays even through effort that should have broken it; as the reserve fails, the next breath catches and the red at his throat flickers. Denying him fresh blood while forcing him to keep moving breaks the condition. This Trait does not close a wound or replace tissue that has been destroyed.

**HEMOVORE'S GIFT (Secondary Trait)**

**Hemovore's Gift** · **Increases raw strength and speed proportional to blood consumed.** Anima Spirare carries the gain through the blood he has actually taken. The red beneath his lips and at his temples brightens with each feeding. The gain consumes that blood and draws on his Essence; it belongs inside the shared two-Grade self-boost cap and cannot grow without limit. Distance, guarded wounds and separation from a victim deny the next increase.

**SANGUINE DOMINION (Primary Trait)**

**Sanguine Dominion** · **Precision control over blood in both self and enemies** — capable of shaping, restraining, or rupturing. His Core pays for a chosen patch of blood to clot, loosen or move, while his Shell keeps that change local through Anima Spirare. A fine red glow gathers at the fingertips before the affected blood answers. A maintained ward can contest his hold on another living body, and breaking his concentration releases it; blood he cannot locate supplies no precise target. Holding several separate targets spends more and risks the cascade spreading into blood he meant to leave alone.

## IX. Domain

The Second Release opens an 8 m field centred on Adalric, with a grey-red boundary and a held glass note. ### Releases

**OPEN VEIN (First Release)** · folk name: The Unstoppering, Common rendering

A red seam opens in the aura from his wrist to the rapier point, his fingertips darken, and a thin coat of his own blood sets along the guard and the backs of his hands. One low heartbeat sounds beneath a sharp glass note. Sanguine Dominion holds the blood's clotting response to the place he chooses, with Petralon supporting the shape under a thrust; called aloud, its A-Grade shaping reaches 400. The Vampiric Rapier carries the moving point of that shape, and losing the weapon loses that part of the release. From a full reserve of 2,069,000 EU the form holds 6 minutes at 5,172.5 EU/s, ending with 206,900 EU left; attacks, repairs and other workings shorten that time. Released silently, the visible change and the sound remain, but shaping stays at his ordinary 398. It makes no blood, and blood drawn from his own body remains a loss until replaced. Pulling the rapier sideways contests the support that carries its thrust; breaking the call, denying contact or outlasting the drain also answers it. When it ends his hands shake and the pain he held off returns. Holding it below the last tenth becomes raw Overchannel.

**THE HOSTLESS COURT (Second Release)** · folk name: The Empty Chair, Common rendering

The grey and arterial red leave his shoulders and spread into a ring 8 m from him, its edge darkened by Tenebra; his coat lifts as though a court has risen around an empty chair, and the glass note holds without another stroke. Sanguine Dominion's blood shaping rises to S, capped at 475, within the ring, while the Crimson Noctis Core's drain reaches only what he can maintain a hold upon. Anima Spirare shapes the blood, Mortalis carries the taking of vitality, and Tenebra dims his return from the directions he is attending to. Opposing wards still contest his hold, and the field grants no certain strike or automatic claim on every life inside it. His rapier carries the forward edge; without it, the seed remains but loses the blade's blood-tendril extension. From a full reserve it holds 3 minutes at 10,345 EU/s and ends with 206,900 EU left, sooner if other work spends the same reserve. Released silently, its ring and held note remain but the blood shaping stays at A, 400. A break in concentration collapses it; crossing the visible edge, contesting the ground with stronger Dominion or approaching from an unwatched angle answers it at his own Stage. Daylight still defeats the cloak's concealment. Afterwards his Crystal runs Overgrown until a full night's sleep, leaking red light with his temper; forcing the field below the last tenth risks a Crystal Fracture Event.

### The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Obsidian Vault and the crimson houses. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Adalric Vladimer Valen was born heir to a baron's house of the vampiric blood, under the Empire of Bluvest. Baron is still the rank he answers to. His house belonged to the vampiric aristocracy whose elder courts keep ties with him now, and it raised its heir to the rapier. He learned the blade and the blood together, as one discipline: the point, the pulse under an opponent's skin, the opening in a guard. The duel became his whole art. He fought close. He fought quiet. In time the empire raised him above his barony, and he became High Lord of the Empire of Bluvest.

#### The Making

He learned to take hold of blood, in his own body and in an enemy's, and to shape it, restrain it or rupture it. The more he drank, the stronger and faster he grew. So the discipline asked him to drink. He went armoured in layered black-red plate that breathed with him and mended itself from the blood he stored. His rapier was midnight-black and veined with crimson, and it pulsed with each heartbeat it claimed. His cloak drank the light. In dim light, or none, nobody saw him at all.

He was High Lord when he came to see his own soul from outside. Every fracture in it lay open to him, each one bearing load and each one marked, and he could not stop seeing them. He looked at what he had been built on. Then he left. He exiled himself from the empire he had held and went out onto the roads alone. Of everything a High Lord carries, he kept one thing to bear load: his honour as a lord.

#### The Cost

He gave up a realm and has never taken another. He holds no land and no office. He claims nothing but his safehouses, and where a lord would keep a province he keeps a network: taverns across the realms, houses that shelter him, informants who bring him what he needs. The elder vampiric courts respect him for keeping his honour through the exile. He keeps his ties with them discreet and takes no part in their politics.

His physiology argues with the honour. His strength rises with the blood he takes, so the duel he built his life on needs feeding, and he feeds. The caravans and small settlements have a name for the nights he comes. They call them Red Moon Nights. He arrives after dark, and by morning prey has been taken and none are found. The settlement is left with an absence. It went that way at a caravan halt on the Calverne road, where he came after dark and was gone before the carters woke. An Accord courier travelling with the caravan was among the missing.

In Valecar, in the river country, a commander hired mercenaries for a field, and Adalric took his coin to guard him. Near midday Hieronymus Cruciferi came onto the field for the other side. The battle ended in minutes. Inside the radius of the Black Sun the ranks came apart, and the souls of the slain went into the Cross. The commander's host broke. Adalric kept his rapier sheathed. He had been hired for the commander. In daylight his cloak hid nothing. Through the afternoon he kept the man alive in the wreck of the baggage lines. After dark he took him off the field under the cloak, and Hieronymus did not follow them into the dark. Neither man learned the other's name that day.

#### Where They Stand

Adalric Vladimer Valen travels alone, sells his blade and seeks Wellsprings. Safehouses, taverns and informants across the realms give him shelter and supplies. He has seen what the Immortal does to a field, and he is among the mercenaries who respect Hieronymus.

At the Calverne halt, others came after him. The Accord sent Zeven Halek after its courier. Zeven read the halt with his eye, from an angle Adalric had not planned for, and found where something had passed quieter than the ground behind it. He filed the shape under no name. Nobody was retrieved. In the days after, Basilio Pavaret came to the halt, took its people's grief as they offered it, and painted it in his own blood. No record says Adalric knows of either man.

Drevath Mourne-Kal, the Iron Horizon, left the same empire, and nobody knows why the Empire of Bluvest let either of them leave.

- **Basilio Pavaret · The Crimson Bloom** · opposed. The painter took the Calverne halt's grief after him. Basilio refused once to feed by force and has held to it, and Adalric's strength rises with the blood he takes.

- **The caravans and small settlements** · harbinger. They know him as the harbinger of Red Moon Nights.

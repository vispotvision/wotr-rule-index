## VIII. Traits

**BLOOMROT ENTROPY (Primary Trait)**

**Bloomrot Entropy** · **Simultaneously causes rampant overgrowth and subtle necrotization in nearby terrain.** Verdantia feeds growth while Mortalis brings failing tissue toward its ending; both run through the same ground and through her body. The quiet spread costs 67,300 EU per minute, 1% of full reserve, with faster deliberate growth paid through Cyst of the Blooming Grave. New buds open while their supporting stems darken. Sterile stone, salt and hard cold interrupt the spread; absent fertile material, it cannot supply a new bed from nothing.

**PERFUME OF OVERBLOSSOM (Passive Trait)**

**Perfume of Overblossom** · **Releases a potent Essence-field that weakens the wills of enemies and feeds illusions through scent and ambient pollen.** Oneirion follows the scent and pollen into a listener's perception. The field costs 33,650 EU per minute, 0.5% of full reserve, while maintained; its heaviness and glittering drift announce it before a chosen image settles. Moving air that carries the plume away and a sealed airway deny that route. It cannot make an unexposed person submit by being visible.

**THORN-SOVEREIGN GOWN (Passive Trait)**

**Thorn-Sovereign Gown** · **Her garments are composed of sentient, thorned fibres that react violently to hostility.** Her living fibres tense and rise toward the hostile movement before they strike. Each defensive response costs 67,300 EU, 1% of full reserve, and worn fibres must regrow from the gown's own substance. The reach ends where its fibres end. A weapon held beyond the body can catch or sever them, and attacks from outside that reach make her spend on other defences.

**SPORES OF MEMORY (Secondary Trait)**

**Spores of Memory** · **Those who inhale her essence experience hallucinogenic regressions** — *either reliving trauma or idealised beauty.* Anamnesis supplies a remembered experience and Oneirion makes it feel present through the inhaled carrier. A directed regression costs 134,600 EU, 2% of full reserve, for up to 30 seconds; keeping it longer costs another such interval. Glittering spores cling around the mouth and nose as attention slips from the room. Preventing inhalation prevents the entry. Once caught, a verifiable present sensation, upheld against the false scene by the target's own perception, can break its coherence; her image cannot guarantee obedience.

**CROWN OF THE CONSUMED GROVE (Secondary Trait)**

**Crown of the Consumed Grove** · **She can call the voices of every plant, spore, or creature that has ever died within her reach.** Anamnesis reads the history held in the grove; each deliberate call costs 67,300 EU, 1% of full reserve, for up to 30 seconds of one recalled voice. The ossified roots hum before the voice answers, and repeated calls crowd her own recollection with what she has heard. The call needs access to the ground or remains carrying that history. Separation from that material ends the current reading; an erased record yields nothing. The voices do not restore their speakers to life.

## IX. Domain

**THE CONSUMED GROVE (Domain)**

The Second Release opens and powers a grove 18 metres in radius, formed over three breaths on connected fertile ground. Growth and decay share its roots: matter released by one bed feeds another, and its stored history remains available to her existing memory Traits. Her territorial growth works one Grade above its card Grade within the field; this rise belongs to the release and is not counted again as a second bonus from her own Domain. The base field lasts 2 hours from full at 841.25 EU per second, closing at the last tenth; deliberate workings shorten that hold. It survives small distractions. Sterile breaks stop its advance, hostile cold raises the work needed to hold it, and a competing Dominion can take its ground. Severing her claim at the Crown of Reclaimed Thorns can collapse the field, with the usual shock of a broken Domain. Third Release gives the same territory its Realm expression; the old bloom left at Hollowfen is not a claim that this combat field covers the whole fen.

## XIII. Physical Description

Her hair hangs in dark ropes like wet willow roots, with pale fungal threads caught between them. A long face narrows beneath high cheekbones; her eyes are green with brown flecks, and a dark line at one corner of her mouth looks like the edge of a bruised petal. Her shoulders are narrow, her forearms sinewy, her fingers long and visibly jointed beneath skin that flowers and fades. The root horns rise above her brow as their own growth, distinct from the worn Crown of Reclaimed Thorns. Her living gown fits closely through the ribs and hips, then loosens into trailing fibres whose ends are blackened by damp and continually replaced. The Petal-Laced Shroud lies over it in overlapping fungus fans and lacevine. Small brown hollows mark where yesterday's growth has fallen away; fresh pale buds crowd their edges.

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Rot and bloom. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Aivyriel came of the Primordial Garden Lineage of the fae, whose dominion ran over fertile ground, fungal beds and whatever lay rotting. She was one of the Old Verdant Nine. A crown of horns grew from her head, formed of root structures that had ossified. Her body flowered and decomposed in the same hour, keeping a slow rhythm with the growth around it. When a bed of fungus fruited near her, something on her fruited. When it went to slime, that went too.

Her name was cut into ancient bark. It was whispered only in the shift between bloom and decay, in the days when a petal stands open and has already begun to brown at the rim. The primal fae revered her. Dryads feared her. Druidic orthodoxy loathed her.

#### The Making

She was sealed. The seasons converged, and she was reborn.

She came back overgrown. At her core lay a cyst of petal-fungus, bone-laced vine and old tears, and whatever came up out of it came up wild and broken. A perfume of overbloomed flowers and damp earth went ahead of her. Glittering spores trailed every step. Those who breathed them relived what had hurt them, or some beauty they had once seen, and their will went soft. Her gown was woven of living thorned fibres. They turned on anyone who came at her meaning harm. Over it she wore a shifting shroud of petals, fungus fans and lacevine that could close over a corpse and draw out its memories.

The dead within her reach kept their voices for her. Every plant, spore and creature that had died there could be called, and she could call them all. Ground she marked with the pink ichor of her silverroot goblet turned, season by season, to obey her.

Then she stopped changing. The land began to change around her, and wherever she walked it answered, even when it answered screaming in bloom. It did not wait to be asked. The answering was not reliable, and it was not free.

#### The Cost

The decomposition ran on her as it ran on the ground. She could stop neither half. Anyone without a strong discipline of Essence to shield them regressed in her presence, or overgrew. Her own hand had no killing stroke in it, and her words for that were "I do not kill. I simply let the earth decide how you should be repurposed."

She came into the marshes of Hollowfen in a plague season and kept away from the villages. She walked in the lower fen, among the dead laid in the reeds. The reed beds flowered out of season and went black at the stem. Fungus fanned out over the bodies. Her perfume came up the causeway on the evening air, and the villagers who breathed it saw their dead alive again, or dying. The burial crews would not go down. Karasaki Hozume went down alone with a lantern, gave the dead passage one by one and laid them under the water. For days he worked inside her bloom, and it did not take him. Mirehollow Asakusa came down through the reeds to the dead the lantern could not reach, and nothing in her bloom touched him. She stayed through all of it. She did nothing to hinder the burial.

Rotwood, above the lakes of Ketsuen, was dying when she came into it. The dying wood answered her. The standing dead put out flowers from the bark and rotted from inside the flowers. Chimwala N'Doro came up from the lakes with druids of his Covenant to reclaim it. Before the first cut he came to the wood's edge where she stood, his blade strapped across his back, and the thorns of her gown did not stir. They spoke there. His rite ran four days, one season to each day, and the ground his cuts opened answered it. Each day her bloom drew back from it a little further. She did not call it forward. By the fourth morning she stood at the edge with her own flowering ground around her feet. On the fifth morning the druids looked for her at the edge. She had gone.

#### Where They Stand

Aivyriel is Fae Sovereign of Bloomrot, Spirit of Perpetual Cycle and High Arbiter of Verdant Ruin. Her bloom has closed over the whole lower fen of Hollowfen. Necromancers come down from the theocracy of Dorrosfenn to whisper to that garden, hoping for new harvests. Every one of the dead under it had been given passage before it grew. Their voices are within her reach. So are the voices of everything that died in Rotwood.

Bees worship her. The druids of the Order of Purity consider her a living blight. The Verdant Aspects of the Outer Circle are sought out only during plagues or ecological collapse.

Stone, salt and cold stop her. On sterile earth, in hard frost and in soil drained of Essence, the land does not answer her. Whoever holds their breath is beyond her perfume and her spores.

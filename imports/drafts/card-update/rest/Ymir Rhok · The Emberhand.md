## VIII. Traits

**MOLTEN TOUCH (Secondary Trait)**

**Molten Touch** · **Can heat metal to forging temperature through direct contact** — *though prolonged use exhausts his Essence reserves quickly.* His Shell feeds heat into the metal beneath his hand; keeping it hot requires continued spending as heat escapes into the rest of the workpiece and its surroundings. A low iron hum and ember-orange warmth beneath the palm give the working away. Breaking contact stops the supply. The metal keeps the heat already delivered and can be cooled by ordinary means.

**ANVIL MEMORY (Passive Trait)**

**Anvil Memory** · **Every weapon he crafts retains a subtle aetheric echo of his handwork**, allowing him to **identify them anywhere.** Fixatio holds his maker's echo in the finished weapon. Leaving that trace takes a small share of the Essence spent during its making; the finished mark needs no continuing supply from him. The echo is the tell. A ward that blocks Essence reading hides it while the ward holds, without erasing the mark. Recognition gives him no command over the weapon or whoever carries it.

**FORGE-BORN ENDURANCE (Primary Trait)**

**Forge-Born Endurance** · **High resistance to heat and heavy physical labour**, *but vulnerable to Essence-draining attacks.* His body's Lattice tolerates the heat and repeated loading of forge work with little conscious Essence cost; food, water and rest still pay for the labour. His hands remain steady through work that leaves the other carriers shaking. The Trait does not replace spent Essence, and draining attacks defeat that endurance without having to overcome its resistance to heat.

## XIII. Physical Description

His hair is the brown-black of wet forge scale, cut short enough to stay clear of the work and singed unevenly above the ears. He has a broad face, a blunt nose and brows that draw together when a surface will not come true. Carrying and hammer work have thickened his shoulders, forearms and palms; his fingers are broad at the joints, with black grit lodged beside the nails. His wool shirt pulls tight across the back when he reaches, and its rolled cuffs are stiff with dried sweat. A leather apron hangs loose below the waist, scored by hot scale and polished at the belly where he braces work against it. Pale flecks mark his forearms, and an old split crosses one thumbnail.

### The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Anvil and relic. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Ymir Rhok was born in the Upper Ring of Stannvaard, in one of the extraction cities cut into the crater walls, where the guild shifts decided when a household worked, ate and slept. His father tended a smelter's forge-array. He tended it until the tender's shake came into his hands and stayed there, and after that the Tender's Obligation kept him.

The boy carried for the smelters as soon as he could lift. The air of the walls tasted of sulphur and hot metal. Grown men went grey in the heat by the end of a shift, and he did not. Heavy work did not break him either.

Stannvaard was the Accord's armoury. When the Forgeworks Annex of the Logistics and Supply Division took apprentices, it took him.

#### The Making

The Annex set him to brackets, bolts and strap-iron. Borin Ironheart took him as apprentice and taught him at the forge beneath the Guild Seat, foundations before any advanced craft. The smiths of the Annex called him Borin's Whetstone, and Young Anvil. Borin scolded him often. The fault was always the same one. He experimented too far from tradition.

Ravessa Flintweld, a journeyman smith from an Outer World branch, slipped him the techniques his master would not teach, and she did it in secret. Her methods clashed with Borin's. He took them anyway, and he did not tell his master where they came from. No lesson at all fell in the months the Kōkan expedition held Borin on the polar shelf.

Until then every heat he had worked came out of a furnace. One afternoon he heated a piece of metal with his hand on purpose, and it worked.

Everything since follows from that afternoon.

#### The Cost

The heat came out of reserves he did not have. The first time he held a billet at forging heat by hand for as long as the work wanted, he emptied. He went down on the Annex floor with the billet still on the anvil, and it cooled there unworked. When he could stand he broke it and began again.

"A dull blade's an insult to its maker," he said. He would shatter a blade and start it over before he let it go out blunt. Every one he broke had to be bought again, and the rarer metal came from Hyo Miran. They haggled. Every weapon he finished kept an echo of his handwork, and he could know any of them anywhere.

After the Echo Containment Breach broke the seals in the Deep Mantle, the Accord let the new ones to Orokh Sable-Forge. The brackets that would hold them came from the Annex. They were Ymir's work, plain ironwork to his master's standard. He went up to the Wyrmwright's cave mouth above the Rimlands for the plates, and Orokh put them into his hands himself. There he saw a workpiece take its heat under a hammer with no coal in the forge.

He carried plates and brackets down into the Mantle under Logistics and Supply. He fitted them in the Bastion under Draen Varos. Draen proofed every bracket before a plate went on it, and he struck the Accord's proof-mark into the ones that passed. A bolt would not seat. Ymir heated it with his bare hand and seated it. Draen made him strip it out. He proofed it again. While the fitting lasted, Draen kept him on the armory floor with the initiates he instructed and put him through their drills. Kaelzar stood in the hold while the last plate went onto the apprentice's brackets.

#### Where They Stand

He is still an apprentice of the Forgeworks Annex and an armorer in training for the Accord. He has a trade and no technique. His heat reaches as far as his hand and no further, and it still empties him fast.

Borin teaches him foundations beneath the Guild Seat and scolds him for experimenting too far from tradition. He has not stopped experimenting. Ravessa still slips him what the tradition leaves out. Hyo still sells him rare metals and Essence-thread alloys, and they still haggle. Zahir Ashveil once commissioned a flame-channeling curved blade from him, and the two of them still trade rare desert ores. Ymir would know that blade anywhere. Renjiro Kaithen has sparred with him on many occasions, steel technique against raw force.

His brackets hold the Deep Mantle's seals.

# Research: the alchemy canon and how it reaches a scene

For the combat questionnaire (2026-10-04): what WOTR's alchemy canon already holds, so the questionnaire asks only how alchemy shows on the page. Every figure is quoted from a canon page or ruling; arithmetic on two canon figures says "derived".

**Read:** `imports/drafts/alchemy-conversion/decisions.md`, `fixes.md`; rule docs R61 (131 rows), R62 to R69; wiki Alchemetrica, The Real Alchemy, The Standing Index, The Alchemical Index, The Heresiology of Alchemy, The Ladder and the Draft, the Drafts ladder (Tier Ladders, Part Twenty-Four), The Apparatus of the Age, Shot and the Enhanced Weapon, Value, Coin and Trade, The Open Crucible, the five Alftian Codex pages, the alchemists' cards; CONFLICTS.md C-110, C-136 to C-154; Combat Craft Guide 2026-10-03, Appendix A; the `~/wotr-drafts` notes (`real-alchemy/` there is empty; the page is published).

**Name key:** Gillus De Raits is **Gisli Draycott**; Vaughaus Thom is **Ivor Strom**; Paracelsus is **Furveus**; Jabir is **Venur**; Oren Corrant is **Orin Farrant**; Vis Trismegistus is **Kwon Mu-jin**; Noxinus Ren is **Malphas**; Tempus is **the Weight**, the 29-year wanderer (R61-122).

## 1. The frame

- **The binding line** (Alchemetrica; R18-5): the reagents are the nouns, the Wellspring (one of the sixty standing currents that carry the world's laws) is the verb, Glyphica (the grammar of written Parun, the tongue a working is authorised in) is the grammar.
- **Why it is lawful:** alchemy hurries a process the Continuum (the world's underlying order) already runs, then pays for the hurry. **Mechanica** (work that takes the output without running the process) is its mirror: it works, it repeats, and a buyer cannot tell it from an entered Draft.
- **Why the world runs on it:** the crucible is a Soul Crystal for matter. A caster cannot promise an effect twice; a workshop's four hundredth ward-plate behaves like its first.
- **Two kinds of alchemist.** The **Crystal-bearer**: the Core seeds intent into the stock (grief and clarity seed differently), the Shell holds precision for hours or days, and a harmonised Attraction Layer runs a Draft at roughly a quarter of the vein depletion. The **pure alchemist**: Class Ø (a Crystal that never woke), works by exactness, and grows **grain** out of exposure (hardening only in the direction the work pushed, which no Accord instrument reads); bench-certified to Expert and no higher (R61-102); standing work goes out under a Crystal-bearer's seal; the trade privately gives him twenty good years.
- **Draftcraft** (R61-91), the Craft of the pour and the vessel, writes in price, vessel, seal and ledger, and its cost is provenance. A registered maker's mark is struck on all a maker's work (Draycott's: a ring crossed by three spokes in grey wax, R65-3), so a seal matches its vessels across a quarter.

## 2. Bench operations and their names

- **Six of the Sixty are also operations:** Dissolution, Coagulatio, Sublimatio, Transmutatio, Fixatio, Materia Primordia.
- **Calcination and Distillation are bench operations only** (R61-6), never Wellsprings, and no soul harmonises with them. A calcination draws on **Cinerion** (the Ash Veil), a distillation on **Sublimare** (the Rising Breath).
- **Sublimatio is sublimation** (solid straight to vapour, caught on cold walls; no partial result, and what did not rise is a fused mass chiselled out). **Sublimare is distillation** (parting by boiling point). R61-5.
- **The Bench files every Draft under four operations:** **Coagulation** (setting; a Drafter's first solo work; switches off cleanly), **Distillation** (purification and the **forensic** operation that traces an Essence to its origin; without a Direction glyph it separates every signature), **Sublimation**, **Conjunction** (the most dangerous; a **Rupture** is two laws contesting in a sealed vessel, so its benches stand against a deliberately light outer wall).
- **The Twelve Turnings:** Reading, Burning (four hours to three days), Taking, Parting, Rot (three to four and a half turns unopened in a dung Bed; it goes black, correctly), Wedding (where Rupture happens), Feeding (measured increments, since a working fed too fast completes the nearest version of itself), Rising, Setting, Sealing, Standing (days, or nine years for Cask-Oath Pitch), Mark. Colours: Blacking, Whitening, Yellowing, Reddening; out of order means abandon.
- **Four glyph roles,** in order: Authorization (Parun carries the warrant; spoken Latin only orders over it, R62-8), Boundary (where and how long), Direction, Sealing (closes on a glyph, never a Wellspring). A chain without a Boundary is refused outright.
- **Failures:** Boundary failure (it keeps going through bench, room and bystander); Draft Corruption (a **law-fragment**, a Wellspring process still trying to finish, with no known decay schedule); Preparation Failure (Coagulatio and Fixatio stop cleanly, Transmutatio and Dissolution complete the nearest version); Rupture.

## 3. Drafts: what they can and cannot do

- **A Draft** is a working stabilised into a substance. Creature-sourced Drafts are most potent and carry **Identity Contamination** (the source's identity rides in).
- **The ladder:** placed by the EU (Essence Units, one megajoule each) one dose holds, on nine rungs: Tincture, Tonic, Draught, Philter, Elixir, Quintessence, Magisterium, Panacea, Stone. A Thundercrack Grenade (about 1 EU) is a Tonic; Hillcarry Salts a Draught; the Ascension Ration a Philter; the Splinter Ration an Elixir. **Carry** (how much of the source came with it) can lift a rung; **Fidelity** (closeness to the reference lot, A to F) can drop one.
- **No Draft refills a reserve** (R2-6; R61-101). A reserve (the Essence a Crystal holds for working) refills from a Wellspring or not at all. A Draft mends the body and steadies the Shell; "alchemical treatment" only quickens Active Recovery, which **requires the character to stop fighting and concentrate** (Essence Ledger).
- **Threshold law:** a Draft can supply the floor and an artificial seed for the melt, never the Catalyst (the lived experience a Stage needs). Drafts reach Clarity and Yield; Coherence "cannot be drunk".
- **What pays** (R61-100): Drafts draw on stock and the site's vein, never the maker's reserve. **Standing work** (a seal, a charm, an impression-body) costs a Crystal-bearer a once-only share of reserve; the wearer pays nothing after (Venur's amulet, R64-5).
- **Counterplay on record:** a dosed opponent's reserve "stands where it stood", so make him spend it. Heavy Carry puts the source's weaknesses in the drinker; Fidelity C or worse leaks a tell.

## 4. Residues and forensic reading

- **Two clocks** (R61-7; R69-3), hinged on **the Crossing** (a dead Crystal coming apart over nine days). **Inside the nine days,** four residues, read in order: the **Aetheric Shell** (identity and Wellsprings; goes soonest; read from the threshold), a held **Noospheric Echo** (voice, habit; read from the doorway; exposure causes Melancholic Erosion), the **Corporeal Residue** (how far the dead was Temperance-active, where the body lay), and the **Volitional Trace** last (a decision the dead was holding, the one residue with direction; "A Trace can be used once"). **After:** the Harmonic Imprint in walls and ground, the Sympathetic Bond Trace (its living end is in the mourner), the Parunic Echo (an Essence Signature in residue, legible from Flourishing).
- **After nine days** the body holds only passive Trait tissue, Class IV stock. Malphas's saying: "A melted coin does not testify" (R69-10).
- **Residue thins to a floor** it never passes, so a patient reader finds it years on (R62-6). **Mechanica residue never thins.** The **coil** reads a field, the **gauge** a density; where they will not reconcile, the gap is the size of what is being taken.
- **Reading spends the record:** Anamnesis (the Wellspring that reads a place's memory, its **Mnemata**) erases what it takes (R61-70).
- **Category One's kit and return** (Necrocursica): coil, gauge, a Fixatio tin for scrapings; four lines back (Shell identity, held Echo, how far the residue has paid, any Trace). No cause of death: "That is the physician's."
- **Procedure as texture:** never alone; the pair, one inside the radius reporting and one outside contradicting ("the contradiction is the diagnosis"); withdrawal counts as a step. The Heresiology's First Entry Protocol wants a party of four. Farrant logs readings by instrument and hour ("Gauge, the reading floor, eighth division: nine and five marks").
- **Ready-made tells** (the Heresiology's signs): metallic sweat, ash under the nails matching no reagent, nosebleeds on successful concealment, cold patches over the heart; vessels that crack under one operator; sigils that correct themselves darker; residue clustering in the shapes of names or open mouths.

## 5. The Great Work and its corruptions

- **Seven stages:** Calcination, Dissolution, Separation, Conjunction, Fermentation, Distillation, Coagulation, drawing on Cinerion, Dissolution, Judicium, Attraction (no current of its own), Rebirthine, Sublimare, Coagulatio (R61-67). A Crystal-bearer walks them twice up the Temperance ladder (R61-79: Splintering is Fermentation, Invocation Dissolution, Dissonance Conjunction, Zenith Coagulation); Class Ø walks them in grain.
- **At the bench:** Fermentation begins in the Rot and completes at the Feeding; Distillation is weeks of circulation in the sealed Returner, read as the Whitening (R61-80, R61-81).
- **The Tria Prima** (R61-82): Sulphur the Core, Salt the Shell, Mercury the Attraction Layer. **Spagyria** (separate, purify, recombine) treats a patient one layer at a time, and its recombining carries Rupture risk. For the dead Malphas maps them to the Trace, the Corporeal Residue with Shell, and the Echo (R69-4).
- **The Seven Cacodaemonic Corruptions,** cited by name, never number (R61-83): each stage made "a method without a judgment". Calcination: Conviction. Dissolution: Submission. Separation: Non-Commitment. Conjunction: Instrumental Union. Fermentation: Catastrophic Germination (good results, poisoned ground). Distillation: Sanitized Truth (worked into a person, "the quiet"). Coagulation: Tyrannous Finality.
- **The workshop tell:** a corrupted working is held open by refusal, costs more each run, and the practitioner cannot stop because it has become what he is.
- **Vows are mechanics:** a kept vow buys Tempering Coherence (stated law matching lived conduct); a broken one is a **Shear Break**, and Coherence falls before anything is hidden. Mu-jin's eighth: "I will not write down what I have decided not to stop" (R68-12).
- **Grades of Offense** I to V end at **Mechanica Trespass**: an unwarranted working cannot seal, so its boundary stays open after everyone goes home.
- **The Seven Unmoorings** (death-work failures, R69-6, with their driving corruption): Mirror Trace (Submission), Worn Habit (none), Incomplete Sealing (Non-Commitment), Open Shell (Instrumental Union), Averted Regard (Catastrophic Germination), Misnaming (Conviction), False Vocation (Tyrannous Finality).
- **On techniques:** the Open Crucible gives each of Mu-jin's techniques an Obsession-Force corruption (Honest Fire strips true structure it finds inconvenient). A grammar for corruption showing in a fighter's moves.

## 6. Apparatus and the lab in the Draw Age

- **Vessels:** crucible (opaque; fusions judged by ear and flame colour), still, retort, Riser, Returner, Standing Egg (no failure signal short of the colour or the glass going), and the **Draft-circle**, inscribed ground scribed for one working and washed after.
- **Heat:** water bath, sand, ash, the dung **Bed**, open flame (a master names a temperature off the colour to within a hundred degrees), the charcoal **Long Furnace**, whose going out mid-working has destroyed more value than any fire. Alchemetrica says heat has never had an instrument.
- **Lute** (clay, hair, dung, egg, flour) closes every joint; a failed lute vents Strongwater fume and kills a closed room in order of nearness; it is the youngest hand's job.
- **Menstrua** (Real Alchemy mapping): Strongwater is nitric acid, Kingswater aqua regia, Blackstone Oil sulphuric acid, Salt-spirit hydrochloric, Ghostsalt sal ammoniac. **Scribe's Wash** lifts an inscription off its substrate, and forgers move seals with it.
- **The room:** charcoal, hot stone, the Bed, Strongwater at the throat, sulphur, wet clay, blood from the rendering room; a bellows on a slow cycle, the furnace ticking, someone counting at the Feeding; dark, lit by Deadlight phials and fires; children carrying ash.
- **Ranks:** Cinderhand, Luteboy, Bellows, Hand, Drafter, Master of the Circle (seals, and carries personal liability), Alchemarch (perhaps forty alive).
- **The body's bill:** crucible palsy (quicksilver tremor, onset at roughly nine years, then withdrawal of manner), Draft-mark (discoloured sclera and nail-beds that name a career in a second), vein cough, the quiet, law-fragment burn.
- **The age around it:** draw-fed engines replace coal and steam (R59-01); no electricity was ever discovered (R59-02); Guild cities live in the late span, Kharven in the early 1800s (R59-05). Instruments on record: field coil, pocket density gauge (EU per cubic metre), loupe, Fixatio tins, Coilwright's Reading Oil, Nine-Nail Attestation Salt, Mu-jin's lens.

## 7. Prices and the alchemical economy

- **Three marks on every lot** (Standing Index): **Provenance Class** I Vein Draw to VI Archonic Residue (II carries a place, III appetite, IV identity, rendered from a Crystal-bearer; V "enforces"); **Fidelity** A to F; **Carry** 0 to 91+.
- **Class IV's three lawful sources** (consented render, judicial render with Crystal forfeiture, warranted salvage of a declared war's dead) have never balanced against supply; extraction fills the gap, sold as lawful. Two quarters widened their capital offences after shortages.
- **Tiers** T1 Household (no gate) to T9 Paragon; T2 to T9 gated by named rank. T3 is trade standard; militia sit there.
- **Prices** (western silver marks): Wick-Salt 0.02; Cutwater Tincture 0.15; Bone-Grey Compress 0.6; Deadlight Phial 1.1; Hillcarry Salts 1.2; Ascension Ration 8.0 (issued at cost against future commission: "this is how the Accord acquires people"); Wyrmcold Quench 14.0; Gravemark Ink 31.0; Splinter Ration 48.0; Hollowsong Vapor 72.0; Refraction Draught 160.0; Sovereign Quench 1,400+. The Alchemical Index's sixty-one formulas carry no prices.
- **Coin:** one gold mark is twelve silver marks; one silver mark is twenty-four copper. The Ault commission cost three hundred gold marks, half before the pour (R65-2): 3,600 silver marks (derived). Essence supply: one standing myriad is 40 silver marks.
- **Entry and secrecy:** nothing trades until entered; entry demands the formula in plain Parun with a named author, so houses write in cover-names and scattered texts instead. Unentered formulas are lawful to hold, unlawful to sell, and **the breach sits with the seller** (R61-111). Time is the capital: "The great houses are old houses because they could afford to wait."
- **Neighbours:** Onceglass charges ("A Pyrokinetic burst costs roughly what a good knife costs"); enhanced shot costs "what a family earns in a season" (R11-3). The unratified `price-table.md` draft (R53-07) says proofed shot "Beats proofed plate", against R20C-26 ("The steel stops the shot, always"). Flagged, not ruled.

## 8. Alchemy in a fight

- **The alchemist's kit** (Apparatus of the Age): a bandolier of "incendiaries, corrosives, healing compounds, smoke agents", and the class the Accord calls munitions. The leather fails about once a campaign; the staining identifies the discipline.
- **The Index's combat formulas share one design rule: each fails onto its user, out of its own mechanism.**
  - *Coatings:* Pyraeon Bloom Oil (T4; a char smouldering in the wound), Slag-Iron Caustic (T4; welds a cut as it lies, so a healer must reopen it), Null-Edge Paste (T4; strips the struck gear's enchantment for the scene, or its own blade's), Ignis Barb Oil (T4; lights only once buried past the barb), Frostthrone Lance Balm (T5; freezes the lance in the wound for a half-breath, and bare hands past a ten-count), Gravewell Shackle Coating (T5; the struck limb weighs double for one minute), Vector Piercing Draught (T3; fired against its grain the point comes back into the draw hand).
  - *Thrown and charged:* Thundercrack Grenade (T5; eardrums and stagger within 4 m; more than an hour past charging it goes off unprompted), Thunderclap Bracer Charge (T4; a cracked grid sends the burst up the wearer's arm), Pressure Burst Capsule (T6; dents through plate; too thin and it collapses in the maker's hand), Rupture Powder (T5; shears loaded seams in about three heartbeats, the thrower's buckles included).
  - *Poisons and stimulants:* Ashfang Venom Vial (T4; blood-triggered, the wielder's nick first), Obsidian Fang Poison (T5; a cold nerve block that hides its wound), Beastheart Serum (T4, Class IV; restraint goes first, and the line between kin and prey may not come back).
  - *Smoke and suppression:* Will-Break Fumigant (T4; resolve breaks before ability, so troops rout while able; the wind picks the side), Hollowsong Vapor (reserved T6; casters speak unheard, the user too), Null-Salt (T3; one vial cancels one array and the bearer's boundary in measure).
  - *Wards and ground:* Grey Standing Chalk, Hearth Ward Wax, Ward-Chalk (four to eight hours), the Draft-circle.
- **Gates are story facts:** a Thundercrack is Expert-tier, a Pressure Burst Master-tier.
- **Enhanced shot:** "alchemically worked beforehand", it performs no higher than the maker's Grade at making, is paid per shot from the enhancer's reserve, goes stale, and is findable by a Harmonist. Steel stops shot; a working may "spall, bias, weaken or find the seam" (R20C-26).
- **Written precedent:** Volume III's Legion night (chalk over dressed stone is Draft ground if the hand means it), the orchard's Coagulatio barrier (nucleation, held on strength alone), Mu-jin's Draft-circle summons (ready under three seconds), Honest Fire (contact; 0.5 to 1.2 m; about 1400°C), his thrown vial that turned Xanelor's arrow to liquid metal. Geturo's arena run is the one full alchemical fight: vials made weeks before, because building answers live on the sand pays the Tide (the arena's drawing field) its cut.
- **Mass combat:** Bone-Grey Compress is Legion field issue; dressing stations time Tide-Silt "by feel"; Will-Break at the line; eastern militias carry Onceglass as the west carries arrows; after the battle, containment and salvage (section 10).
- **Explosives inside 1800 to 1900:** canon has powder, muskets, pistols, rare late machine guns (R59-12) and the Essence-charged Thundercrack. The Real Alchemy lists fulminate, chlorate powder and nitroglycerin among real bench killers and finds no WOTR counterpart.

## 9. Medicine

- **The dose is the point** (R61-105): quicksilver, calomel and white arsenic heal at a measure and poison past it, and "the one who measures takes his own share". Furveus shows crucible palsy and Draft-mark; Mu-jin reads them on sight.
- **Appendix I's doses** (R69-9): white arsenic, one tenth of a grain three times a day for a turn, after a ring watch; calomel, five grains once, never with salt meat or brine. The Real Alchemy tabulates period therapeutic and lethal ranges.
- **Field medicine and its clocks:** Cutwater Tincture spoils in eleven days, printed on nothing; Bone-Grey Compress past six hours takes what the wound needed ("roughly one soldier in nine has forgotten"); Aperture Wound Ichor inside the first minute; Verdant Suture Paste faithfully copies a misaligned wound; Healing Salve lasts four to six hours; Equilibrium Draught damps panic and caps peak Resonance.
- **Doctrine:** alchemical treatment heals bodies, steadies Shells, helps a Fractured Crystal and Overchannel damage; it never refuels. No protocol is judged by survival alone; Catastrophic Germination is the infirmary's corruption.
- **Gap:** no canon poison test or antidote. The Marsh test (1836), Reinsch test (1841) and ferric oxide against arsenic (1834) fall inside the span, unadopted.

## 10. The made dead and impression-bodies

- **Mortalis is Auren's Still Gate** (R61-69), where a body stops spending to stay ordered; its failure, **Equilibration**, cools the practitioner toward it unnoticed.
- **The Categories** (R69-5): **One**, forensic reading (Flourishing; Adept gate). **Two**, containment (Glory; Expert gate; the operator comes off "grey at the mouth and cold at the fingers"). **Three**, Trace completion (Refraction with review; Expert gate with review). **Four**, resurrection: no floor, no gate, and the application goes on the applicant's record. Costs: reserve share, site depletion, waste heat, and from Two a tick on the Veil's Drift Scale.
- **An impression-body** (R61-75) is a Greater Summons: a poured vessel graded like any Draft (the Ault vessel: Class II Field Substrate, Sound, Marked), the maker's own Core as its identity (the **Eidolon**; Mu-jin's gloss, "he summons himself"), Vocatia (summoning by recognition) reaching the Trace. Boundary in **Gravetide Ink**, which drinks lamplight; Sealing in **Gravemark Ink** (Class IV, scarce), closing on [Vor] Return, so it ends "in one breath" when the decision is delivered (the Ault likeness held eleven days, R67-6).
- **Failure and counter:** short ink makes a short seal; an unfinished seal is an **unsealed rune** that the room completes into a haunting (Appendix I: a body at the window asking for a door, the rune under its left heel). A ring of **Stillgate Ash** grounds the charge and bleeds whoever stands at it.
- **Revenants need no necromancer** (R61-77): a Crossing stalls on sealed ground or a saturated field; the revenant has habits and a direction and "nothing that weighs them"; it fades when diffusion completes. Containment moves the dead before the eighth day; fields go wrong when "the pits were dug on the twelfth".
- **The Open Shell** is Mechanica extraction from a dead donor, Grade V; the ground never heals. At Genesio the gate gauge read twenty-six at the thaw of 702 and twenty after the last Trace was spent.
- **The Mother** teaches from the Necrocursica by memory, so a reader can recognise the cult's work (R61-55). Liches get one refused margin line (R69-8).

## 11. Alchemists as characters and their voices

- **Kwon Mu-jin:** notices the flaw, then the child in its path; contractions with family, never while correcting; full gloss rights; reads EU, η and site density when the spectacles come off (R61-112). His weak point is a target that closes before the read finishes.
- **Malphas:** opaque command by default, the Necrocursica's candour as his grief and joy shift; contractions never (R61-58). His voice block is still owed on the card.
- **Orin Farrant:** Class Ø Searcher; reads only by instrument or a named reader; humour "comes as a reading"; contractions in speech, never in the field book.
- **The rest:** Furveus (palsied hand; "The dead don't lie, Malphas"), Draycott (a dead grey tooth and a signet; his full card owed, R61-17), Strom, Halveth, Dessa Mael, Geturo (quiet, unhurried, prepared). Volume I's cards add a Black Concord poisoner (Lucien Draeve), an expelled theoretician whose cost is silver in his own nerves (Cael Dareth), and Sinclair Mercer, whose steady hands are "the wrong result".
- **Shared texture:** stained coats, Draft-mark on the nails, the quiet in the long-service, the pure alchemist's precision ("A pure alchemist who is sloppy dies").

## 12. What is undecided about how alchemy reaches the page

No R70 row touches alchemy. These seams are open.

1. **Drafts mid-fight:** whether a Draft can be drunk, applied or thrown inside an exchange, how long it takes, and what it costs in the turn. Canon only says Active Recovery stops the fight and the Ichor wants the first minute.
2. **Readouts:** R70-95 gives an item one card at purchase or appraisal. Whether a Draft gets a readout at use, whether it counts against R70-86's three a scene, and whether Tier, Class, Fidelity and Carry are named in a fight or shown only as behaviour.
3. **The bench fighter's grammar:** which of R70-102's four fits, and whether prepared work against live work (the Geturo model) becomes law.
4. **Owed reactives:** Sun-Salt, Therphorus and Auramancy shielding (Combat Guide Appendix A) sit on no wiki page.
5. **Explosives and incendiaries:** who makes powder, fuses and charges in 715 IC; whether fulminates, chlorates, guncotton and nitroglycerin exist inside the span; how common an Expert-tier Thundercrack is. No canon incendiary pitch exists beyond the kit's "incendiaries" and the oils; Cask-Oath Pitch is a Dawi inscription medium.
6. **Poison in a duel:** honourable, declared, or a killing offence by culture; provable or not (no canon test); and whether the self-harming failure must show each time.
7. **A Draft-coated round:** Drafts are reserve-free (R61-100) but an alchemically enhanced round costs the enhancer's reserve per shot. Which governs a ball dipped in Ignis Barb Oil is unwritten.
8. **The lab's fire:** benches burn charcoal and dung while R59-01 replaces coal and steam; C-137 asks whether a 715 IC bench owns a thermometer. Whether a Guild-city bench is heated by draw is unlogged.
9. **Time words:** "turn" is fourteen days on the Sky page and six seconds in Fracture of Worlds VII (C-152); bench durations disagree with The Real Alchemy (C-153).
10. **Who can make what:** whether Draftcraft needs a Crystal (C-136); what caps a pure alchemist's product (C-142); which Crystal layer decides a Draft, and so where its counter lives (C-139); what "Drafter" names (C-144).
11. **The impression-body's floor:** Refraction by the Bench, Stages IX to XI by the summoning tiers (C-154).
12. **Owed work:** The Real Alchemy's ratification (C-110); the Draft Sub-Stat profile (R61-103), never filed; Draycott's card; Malphas's voice block; the Category field entries (R61-107); the price table.
13. **Mechanism on the page:** how much bench procedure a fight shows (roles, colours, lute) against how much it leaves to the item; whether a formula's real phenomenon is named in narration, a mouth, or only the notes; whether Draft vocabulary follows the POV's school as martial vocabulary does (R70-100). Geturo's "node" and "Modulation" sit on no canon page.
14. **Forensics as a scene type:** procedure on the page or summary; whether a Class Ø investigator's borrowed senses get a readout.
15. **The dead in battle:** revenants, residue pooling and salvage have never reached a scene; what the made dead can do in a fight beyond habit and direction is unwritten.
16. **Corruption in a fighter:** whether the Heresiology's bodily signs, a Shear Break or an Obsession-Force technique shows as a visible tell mid-fight.
17. **The economy in a fight:** whether price, stamp, provenance and staleness are fight texture (a forged seal, a settled suspension, adulterated Ghostsalt), and whether a fighter counts what the vial cost.

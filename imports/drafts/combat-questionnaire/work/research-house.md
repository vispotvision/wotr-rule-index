*Combat questionnaire, work file. 2026-10-04. A read-only survey of `scenes/`; nothing in the archive was touched. Quotes are the house's own prose, kept short, with file and line.*

## Headlines

1. **There are three house styles of fight, not one,** and they are easy to tell apart by sentence length alone (table below). The newest (Xanelor, Geturo) is the plainest and the slowest.
2. **Fights are nearly silent.** Dialogue is 0 to 7 percent of the words in most fights. Speech is a technique name, a taunt, a speech, or the opponent explaining himself.
3. **Magic is explained live, by a watcher.** The teacher at the rail and the opponent's own boast carry most of it. The new law (R70-71) wants the rules planted before the fight; only the Geturo run comes close.
4. **No readout has ever appeared inside an archived fight.** Numbers live in the author notes' Stat Ledger. The only readout block in the archive is the breakthrough in `the_drawing_off.md`, which is not a fight.
5. **No firearm is fired in any archived scene.** Musket, rifle, pistol, revolver, carbine, cartridge: zero hits across all scene files. "Firearm" appears once, as kit on an escort.
6. **Battles barely exist.** One true battle (the breach at the southern reach, about 700 words); everything else is a duel with an army watching.
7. **The arena has taken over.** All four Xanelor parts and all ten Geturo files, the newest fight run in the archive, are on Aetherion sand.
8. **Alchemy in a fight is the Geturo run's whole method,** and it reads well; nowhere else, apart from one thrown vial and Wren's charges.
9. **The stalest habits are countable:** "eleven" 75 times in the sample, countdown negation 19 times, "did not" once every 255 words, the knock-back furrow, the nosebleed.

## Three generations

| | Old (Cozbi, Six Crows, Path of Sorrow, True King) | Middle (Ashgate road, Korvaeth, Renard, the breach) | New (Xanelor, Geturo, from late September) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mean sentence | 21 to 33 words | 20 to 24 words (Renard 15) | 13.5 to 17 words |
| Spread (std. dev.) | 17 to 22 | 18 to 23 (Renard 15) | 10 to 14 |
| POV | wide, hovering | close, heavy italic monologue (Darius 22 to 26 italic lines a scene; *The Full Match* 34) | close, one or two italic lines a scene |
| Signature | Latin chants, metric units, long participle chains | counted seconds and feet, HEMA terms, cost itemised in the body | Isaac's rough converted, his words kept, onlookers' private reads |

*The Drawing-Off* (R70) sits beside the new group: mean 16.2, spread 13.9.

The middle generation is where the house's best-known fights live, and where most of the stale habits live too. The spread figure matters: those scenes swing from two-word fragments to sixty-word "and" chains inside one paragraph, which is the rhythm of `02_wren_bulwark.md` and `the_slow_match.md`. The new generation is more even and reads faster.

## Exchange granularity and length

Exchanges are few and long. An exchange here means one attack and its answer.

- **Xanelor, four parts, about 7,800 words, eight or nine exchanges.** Part one (2,610 words) has one: the bullet-train charge and its redirect. Most of its words are the pre-fight read and the gallery.
- **Renard, 2,920 words, three exchanges:** the thrust and Winden (winding the blade in the bind), the Zwerchhau (the crosswise cut thrown hands-high with the short edge), the half-sword thrust (a thrust with one hand gripping the blade midway). About half the scene is approach, explanation and aftermath.
- **Wren, three "deliveries" across two scenes,** about 4,300 words of fight.
- **Geturo, ten files, about 17,000 words, one bout.** `geturo_the_slip.md` (1,328 words) is one wrist escape and one stand-up. `geturo_the_squeeze.md` (1,826) is four vials made and three thrown. This is turn-by-turn roleplay at its slowest.
- **Cozbi, the reverse:** about eight actions in 1,457 words of `Cozbi_vs_Rengai_Dhaerin_Combat.md`, each sentence carrying a whole move.
- **Darius summarises:** *Fist of God* opens after the exchanges and counts them back: "a teep, four low kicks, two elbows, a hook to the floating ribs and two knees into him inside about eleven seconds" (`21_darius_the_fist_of_god.md:37`). A teep is the Muay Thai push kick.
- **The breach compresses:** four hours in about 700 words, one kill drawn, one named death, rotations counted (`01_wotr_muster_breach_road_north.md:111-117`).

So the house writes duels slow and battles fast. Nothing in the archive is a long exchange-by-exchange fight at full speed, the kind a shonen fight chapter does (shonen: Japanese comics aimed at boys, the Naruto and Bleach shelf).

## Speed

Speed is drawn by its precursors and by absence, almost never by a verb of speed.

- The thing arrives before the body: "The push came first" (`xanelor_the_bullet_train.md:71`), the compression front of the borrowed bullet train read on Xanelor's skin.
- The body is simply elsewhere: "He was simply further forward than he had been, and then further" (`xanelor_the_bullet_train.md:67`).
- A comparison to nothing human: "a white line was coming down the middle of the road out of them at a speed nothing on legs makes" (`02_wren_bulwark.md:153`).
- Real physics at the top end: the arrow "broke the sound barrier inside the first pace", ablating as it flew, a double boom, a trench torn under its path (`xanelor_the_arrow.md:51`).
- Time in counted seconds: "four seconds" for the betrayal, "eleven seconds" for the third delivery, "about nine seconds" left to Wren, "about a fifth of a second" for Korvaeth's shell. Seconds as a unit of time appear 52 times in the sample.

What is missing is the reader's own speed: no fight runs short sentences at the pace of the blows for any length. The new law's held instant (DD6, settled) is half-present already in Renard's "Indes is not thinking" (`renard_the_left_of_the_door.md:89`; Indes is acting in the same instant).

## Power

Power is shown by what it does to the room, the ground and the bystanders. This is already the house's strongest habit and it matches MY2 (settled).

- **Bystanders' bodies:** front-row spectators lean back "without knowing they were doing it", a water flask rolls off the rail (`xanelor_the_constellations.md:57`); "every horse in six hundred screamed at once" (`02_wren_bulwark.md:13`); forty spearmen step back (`the_inhale_register.md:94`); Garrick Dole "sat down on the chalk, hard" (`what_the_plate_would_not_take.md:108`).
- **Ground and structure:** the road cracks in a ring "eleven feet across" when Kaalabad dismounts (`02_wren_bulwark.md:25`); the ward fails from the flaw a shoulder made in part one (`xanelor_the_constellations.md:75`), which is good continuity of damage.
- **Knock-back measured in feet:** Wren goes back eleven feet "with both heels ploughing two furrows" (`02_wren_bulwark.md:101`), then seven; the warden's "heels ploughed two long furrows" (`xanelor_juggernauts_fist.md:15`); Darius "six feet on both heels" (`21_darius_the_fist_of_god.md:17`). One device, used four times.
- **Pressure counted in the body by Stage gap:** Renard reads his own nausea as "Two above him" (`renard_the_left_of_the_door.md:17`). This is the clearest LitRPG-in-the-flesh moment in the archive and it is entirely in the body.

## Space

Space is tracked carefully, usually with numbers.

- Arena geometry: "forty paces across", "thirty paces off", "Fifteen paces separated them now, the best range he could have asked for" (`xanelor_the_bullet_train.md:3, 29, 103`).
- Terrain as the plan: Wren paced the narrows in summer under a false name, "Two hundred and ten feet from the ditch on the left to the drop on the right" (`02_wren_bulwark.md:49`).
- The breach as arithmetic: "Twenty-two feet. Eight abreast." (`01_wotr_muster_breach_road_north.md:79`).
- Measure (fighting distance) named in the POV's school: Renard steps left so the door is "behind his right shoulder, not behind his spine" and takes Pflug (the plough guard: hilt at the hip, point at the face) to hold the corridor's centreline (`renard_the_left_of_the_door.md:31`).
- Footprints as information: Geturo walks on the crests of the Tide bands so the sand fills his prints (`geturo_the_squeeze.md:59`).

Space is the house's most reliable craft. The gap is vertical space and cover: nobody fights on stairs, a roof, a wall-walk, in a ship, or behind anything.

## How magic is explained mid-fight

Four routes, in order of use:

1. **The teacher at the rail.** Mu-jin carries the whole Hermetic account of the Xanelor duel from the gallery: the imprint "carried whatever the original carried. A bullet train carried its rails" (`xanelor_the_bullet_train.md:55`); the anchor-point counter is "written down in a book Mu-jin himself had copied out at fifteen" (`xanelor_the_constellations.md:43`). It reads like Kakashi or Urahara explaining from the sidelines, and it works.
2. **The opponent's boast.** The Tenrai practitioner gives Renard the whole Held Boil: "I put a wall in the heat" (`renard_the_left_of_the_door.md:47`). Hiromi: "The more strikes I land on you the heavier the aetheric pressure around you grows" (`xanelor_juggernauts_fist.md:87`). Both are Isaac's favoured anime habit, and both are paid for (the Tenrai man explains to buy time, and dies).
3. **The POV's own read, built from planted evidence.** Wren's "He has a middle, and everything that comes near it stops choosing its own direction" (`02_wren_bulwark.md:145`) arrives after three planted signs. This is the house's best single explanation.
4. **The science paragraph in the POV's learned voice,** with an Earth trade carrying the law: "Every cricket bowler in the Concord knew that in his wrist" for the Magnus effect, spin curving a ball (`geturo_the_squeeze.md:31`); surveyors logging a mirage as "an instrument error" (`geturo_ignite_tide.md:31`); the hollow charge that "the gunsmiths had started calling it something ruder" (`geturo_fire_air.md:59`). Korvaeth's dark fire gets a full paragraph of heat-transfer law in narration (`the_slow_match.md`, section IV).

Against R70-71 (settled: rules shown beforehand): the Xanelor and Ashgate fights explain live; the Geturo run plants its rules in Mu-jin's lectures and the bench scenes first, so its fight explaining is mostly recall. That run is the house's working model of the new law.

## Pain, fatigue and the reserve

- **Pain is late, absent, or a warning.** "There was no pain in the arm" is Renard's first sign of the working (`renard_the_left_of_the_door.md:67`); "The pain arrived late and enormous" (`03_wren_three_deliveries.md:107`); Korvaeth "felt the whole of it and felt none of the pain" and is frightened by that.
- **Fatigue is a tremor in a named part:** Xanelor's bow hand "was shaking" at his best range (`xanelor_the_bullet_train.md:103`); Wren's shield arm shakes below the elbow; Geturo's "knees nearly went".
- **The reserve has a body vocabulary,** and it is good: Hiromi's septum bleeds under the net's load (`xanelor_the_constellations.md:9`); the Tide's cost to Geturo is "a low tug under the sternum", tin on the back teeth, an ache behind the left eye, cold fingertips (`geturo_the_squeeze.md:23, 27, 61`); a summon returning turns Hiromi's face grey; Wren's Bulwark goes blue to grey as spent capacity, the visible fuel gauge on an object.
- **Cost itemised in order:** "The first thing was the smell, and it was his own" (`the_slow_match.md:141`), then the hand, then the ear.
- **No figure.** No reserve number appears on any fight page. Renard's ledger says "roughly a third of reserve ... (estimate)" in the notes only.

## Gore

Clinical, sudden and short. The house names the structure, gives one image, and moves on.

- "the extensor tendons part and the fingers of the right hand open on their own" (`renard_the_left_of_the_door.md:77`); "A man has a few jugs in him and the vessel under the arm empties a jug fast" (`:103`).
- "The head came off the second one in the file at the base of the neck ... and the body took two more steps in formation" (`01_wotr_muster_breach_road_north.md:99`).
- "a long wet peel like the skin coming off a boiled plum" (`geturo_the_slip.md:21`).
- The cask "staved Gorrgorr's chest ... the ribs gone in on the left side to the spine" (`the_drawing_off.md:124`).
- Haemorrhage tracked by ATLS class (Advanced Trauma Life Support's four classes of blood loss): Brida's "third bowl" (`renard_the_left_of_the_door.md:111`), Mu-jin classing his son's blood from the rail as "less than a cupful" (`xanelor_the_bullet_train.md:93`).

The arena fights hold gore down (sprains, hairline fractures, a split lip) because the fighters are students. Nothing in the archive lingers: no slow disembowelment, no long dying apart from Wren's nine seconds. NATALIE's "gore in full" is met by precision, not by length.

## Endings and aftermath

Fights end on an action or an image, rarely on a line, and RP parts end on a cliff:

- Renard drags the body, "the heels leaving two lines through the frost-grit that the morning would have to explain" (`renard_the_left_of_the_door.md:125`).
- Xanelor parts end mid-motion: "Hiromi's eyes went to it" (`xanelor_the_constellations.md:93`); the falling bow (`xanelor_juggernauts_fist.md:89`); Mu-jin lowering himself into the ring "the slow way" (`xanelor_the_arrow.md:65`).
- Darius "fell over onto his side in the ash ... with his right hand still closed, and did not open it" (`23_darius_the_fist_of_totality.md:159`).
- Korvaeth ends on speech: "Choose whichever one you can live beside. I have run out of preferences." (`the_full_match.md:180`).
- Geturo: "and he watched, and waited, and bled" (`geturo_the_slip.md:69`).

Aftermath is strongest in the arena: the infirmarian laying out "a jar of lard and lime-water, a roll of lint, two wooden finger splints and a pair of long forceps" (`geturo_the_slip.md:51`), and a whole aftermath scene (`aftermath_hold_the_wall.md`). Battle aftermath is missing; the breach ends with Bram turning to face the gap again.

## Readouts and numbers

- **Inside fights: none.** The `[Ig]` and `[Let]` in `geturo_ignite_tide.md:15` are glyph names, not readouts.
- **In heads and mouths:** the Measurewright thinks in Sub-Stat names: "Perception at a grade the Archive does not carry a mark for ... with Forecast and Acuity" (`what_the_plate_would_not_take.md:128`), and orders "Enter it as unentered" (`:126`), which turns the missing number into plot. Ruvaen's private count reads incoming billets as "Four and a quarter pounds ... Twenty-two feet per second" (`:78`). Mu-jin's "Twenty times what a grown man's arms could draw" is an estimate in a head, flagged as such in the notes. Darius takes "inventory ... in the diagnostic voice" (`21_darius_the_fist_of_god.md:25`).
- **The one readout block** is Kalrin's breakthrough: "[Stage II, Welling. Apprentice.]" after the body (`the_drawing_off.md:113`), then a CHANGES block at the close.

So LR8's three in-fight readouts (appraisal, a wound, Essence spent, all settled) have never been tried in a fight. The questionnaire can show them for the first time on real archive beats: Mu-jin's blood-classing as a wound line, the Measurewright's read as an appraisal, the Bulwark's grey as an Essence-spent line.

## Sound

Sound is nearly always a simile, almost never a word for the noise. Onomatopoeia in fights: none (the archive's only one is "*Tck.*", a sheathing, in *The Path of Sorrow*). The house's sound bank is concrete and good:

- "a sound like sailcloth going along a seam, four times over" (`the_full_match.md:162`)
- "the sound of fat dropped on a griddle" (`renard_the_left_of_the_door.md:99`)
- "a dry crunching like a fist closed on a handful of quills" (`geturo_the_slip.md:5`)
- "the sound of a mountain clearing its throat" (`02_wren_bulwark.md:133`)
- "a low sustained note, a foot deep" from a shield that should have split (`02_wren_bulwark.md:97`)
- "The noise of the column changed key. That was the sound Wren would keep." (`02_wren_bulwark.md:53`)

Silence is used as a weapon twice: the Blackmatch throws no light and the Mandate advance has "No shouting anywhere" (`01_wotr_muster_breach_road_north.md:69`).

## Crowds and witnesses

The house signature is **the witness who gets it wrong.** "Six hundred men behind him had watched the whole of it. Not one had seen a thing." (`the_full_match.md:160`); Cass Holloway "did not know whose wave it had been" (`03_wren_three_deliveries.md:151`); "Nobody in the gallery could have said who had done it" (`xanelor_juggernauts_fist.md:37`); Lunn's account that "nobody ever believed" (`the_slow_match.md:171`). It is strong and it is close to a tic.

The arena gallery reacts mostly in private (Naori's thread-sight, Dabney's hands unfolding, Akira's grip), with one-line italic thoughts. R70-72 (settled: a spoken chorus in waves) appears only in `the_drawing_off.md:120` ("Welled." "At the stack."). The Geturo run adds the best crowd texture in the archive: the porter Tam Rudd running a chalk book on his rake handle, "Two to one on the quiet one, Warden" (`geturo_the_squeeze.md:51`), the warden's gauge needle, the Dawi orderly's "Stone. Good stone." (`geturo_the_slip.md:63`).

## Two players at one fight

The newest fights are two-player roleplay. Isaac's rough is converted and his words kept: "How fun...." and "Fight back! you can't win by blocking all of my strikes..." stand verbatim (`xanelor_juggernauts_fist.md:25`, `xanelor_the_arrow.md:23`), with his anime vocabulary (gravity magic, sound barrier, literal ball of fire) kept by rule. In the Geturo run the opponent belongs to another player and is never written: "Taesyn is not written; his hand, working and grip exist only as what Geturo's elbow and wrist feel" (`geturo_the_slip.md`, notes). The Eresse fights do the same with Dova'Kan, who is only "the other man" and "a dragon".

This is a real technique the guides do not yet name: **the opponent rendered only as sensation and environment.** Several older archived scenes (the Ashgate road sequence, *The True King of the North*, *The Path of Sorrow*) put Sodoku Moto in POV; those are precedent for events only, and the questionnaire should not draw examples from his interior.

## Western, Eastern and LitRPG already on the page

- **Western, strong:** Liechtenauer's German longsword terms in Renard and Ruvaen (Pflug, Winden, Zwerchhau, Indes, Vor and Nach, the initiative and the late answer, Zutritt, Unterhau, Absetzen, plus Fiore's Italian *tutta volta*); Muay Thai in Darius (teep, check, round kick); catch wrestling in Geturo (the thumb-gap wrist escape, the stand-up, the sprawl, legs thrown back to stop a takedown); ATLS; the ugly betrayal ("Two of Wren's men in the fourth rank went for the same officer and got in each other's way and one of them died for it", `02_wren_bulwark.md:45`).
- **Eastern, strong:** technique names called aloud as one-line speech ("Bulwark." "Compact." "Iron Bison." "Juggernaut's Fist." "Infinite Summoning Arts, Imprint Summoning Arts."); the sideline explainer; the hidden reserve erupting (Xanelor's twelve stored years, which the notes trace to Bleach's spiritual pressure); summons and recursive summons; the masked boy with no magic; aikido's *irimi-tenkan* (the entering step and turn) told as Drogo's "taking the wave at its shoulder" (`xanelor_the_bullet_train.md:77`). Not yet present: numbered forms (no "First Form" anywhere, though MY9 settled them), a declaration before a duel (CB2), a killing rite (CB6), a full in-fight flashback (CB4; Wren's last thoughts to Bram are the nearest, `03_wren_three_deliveries.md:127-133`).
- **LitRPG, faint:** Stage gaps felt in the body, the Measurewright's Sub-Stat names, Kalrin's single block. No in-fight sizing-up by Grade (LR11, settled), no Appraisal line, no Essence-spent line.

## What repeats or goes stale

Counts are from the 62,800-word sample.

1. **"Eleven."** 75 times. *The Slow Match* alone: eleven paces, one frame every eleven seconds, a cut not felt for eleven seconds, eleven years, eleven feet, eleven inches of iron, eleven centuries of shell. *What the Plate Would Not Take*: 16, including "eleven and a half rotations" and "eleven degrees". It reached the R70 scene: Gorrgorr loaded casks "for eleven years" and the crew laughed "for eleven years" (`the_drawing_off.md:29`), and Borin's burn is "eleven days" old. The number has become the house's default for "a specific number".
2. **Countdown negation and manufactured fragments** (NATALIE bans both). 19 hits: "Not a reserve. Not a reservoir." (`the_full_match.md:94`); "*Into.* / *Not through.*" (`:132`); "Not burned." (`:148`); "Not a sheet and not a wave. Points." (`the_slow_match.md:113`); "Not a lunge." (`what_the_plate_would_not_take.md:84`).
3. **"It was not X. It was Y."** "It was not a strike. It was a wall" (`Cozbi_Construct_Combat_Scene.md:21`); "The insult was not cruelty for its own sake. It was bait" (`Six_Crows_vs_Black_Dragon.md:21`).
4. **"It worked." then a turn:** "It worked. It was not enough." (`21_darius_the_fist_of_god.md:15`); "It worked." (`Six_Crows_vs_Black_Dragon.md:23`).
5. **"Did not."** 246 times, once every 255 words, mostly in long polysyndeton (clauses chained on "and"): "and did not look at the body on the ditch bank, and did not look at the shield either" (`03_wren_three_deliveries.md:165`).
6. **The knock-back furrow** (four times, above) and **the force spread across a shield's face** (Wren's Bulwark and Hiromi's warden use the same mechanism within a week of each other).
7. **The nosebleed as overdraw** (Hiromi, Wren).
8. **"Without deciding to"** for involuntary bodies: 11 hits across the archive, from the front rank to Obadiah Spigot sitting down in the beer.
9. **"The particular / the specific X of Y":** 19 in the sample ("the particular sourness of men who have slept in mail").
10. **"That was the part nobody ever tells you"** (`01_wotr_muster_breach_road_north.md:105`) and kin: a narrator stepping forward to certify the truth of the moment.
11. **Old stratum only:** the Ladder (a chain where each clause's end becomes the next clause's subject): "and the bearing-more-weight was the metaphor, and the metaphor was the governance" (`the_true_king_of_the_north_part_12.md:223`); system-voice interior ("nineteen milliseconds", the Kamigan "ranking", *The Path of Sorrow*); metric units ("forty-meter radius", `Six_Crows_vs_Black_Dragon.md:11`).
12. **All-caps battle speech:** "THIS IS WHERE WE PROVE WHAT WE ARE." (`01_wotr_muster_breach_road_north.md:81`), the only shouted typography in the archive.

Em dashes in fight bodies: none (one in each of five titles). Sentence-rhythm variety is high in the middle generation, so length variation is not the problem; repetition of devices is.

## What is missing

- **Firearms.** None fired, anywhere. Powder appears only as quarry charges walked in against a dragon and doing "nothing" (`the_inhale_register.md:16`) and as gun oil on a housing. The Combat Guide's twenty-foot threshold, the ammunition tiers and "The steel stops the shot, always" (R20C-26) have never been tested on a page. A practitioner taking common shot, a proofed round against a cuirass, a reload in a melee, a pistol as a club inside twenty feet: all unwritten.
- **Mass battle.** One battle, about 700 words. No hill-and-ditch cut (CB7, settled) has been written; no cavalry beyond one oblique charge; no artillery, no siege engines in use (Korvaeth's casters are dismantled, not fired), no formation fight between practitioners and ranks except as weather seen from four hundred yards (`the_slow_match.md`, Part Two), no marked roll call (VO2).
- **Weather that changes a fight.** Cold works at the breach ("three-quarters of a beat late", `01_wotr_muster_breach_road_north.md:109`) and in Renard's hall; sea wind at the coast road. No rain, fog, snow-blind, mud or night-without-lamps fight.
- **Terrain beyond the arena and the road:** no stairs, roofs, water, ice, trench, forest, interior with furniture, moving vehicle (the train scenes do not fight).
- **Monsters as opponents.** The dragon talks instead of fighting; the Six Crows dragon is the old stratum; Well-spawn appear once, as labour horror with leeches that "go to what carries most" (`the_leveller.md`, section IV), with the Company's hunters paid "by the carcass" offstage. No hunting party, no Well clearance, no creature fight under the R53-06 spawn canon.
- **Alchemy beyond Geturo.** His run is the model: nodes (one working bound into a thumb-width vial), Fixation of air, Multiplicatio by seeded crystallisation, a hollow charge, a plaster cast drawn on with a pencil, all prepared at the bench "weeks ago over a low flame" (`geturo_the_squeeze.md:5`).

# What WOTR's systems give a fight, and what is still open about the page

*Research for the combat questionnaire, 2026-10-04. Sources: the wiki mirror (the Living System Parts Four to Twenty-Four, Counterplay, the Four Crafts, the Magical Categories, the Master Glyph Index, the Core Vocabulary, the Physical Account, the Trait System, the Imperial Age Aether Infrastructure, Shot and the Enhanced Weapon, the Item Tiers, the Bestiary and Well-spawn pages, the Summon Register, the Five Beastkin Lineages), the Combat Craft Guide and Mass Combat Craft Guide 2026-10-03 editions, the rule index, and three FOW lines (Yoko Mishiro, Borin Ironheart, Renard Greymane). ## 1. Stats and Grades

**What it gives a fight.** A contest is decided "first by Tier Grade differential, then by technique and tactical intelligence, then by Soul Crystal architecture" (Part Four). One full Grade above wins that stat's exchange "without meaningful contest"; two is one-sided; at three "the lower-grade character is not engaging in a battle, they are surviving an event." Inside one Grade the fight is open: a Sub-Stat at 275 strikes roughly five times harder than one at 176, both B, and technique can invert it. The Combat Guide's §7 maps each question to a stat: Dexterity for who closes and reacts; Ardency Penetration against armour and Flux for how much arrives; Gnosis Perception and Acuity for the read; Tempering for duration and push; Harmonics against local density for draw cost; Vitality for wounds; Resilience for what reaches the Crystal; Path gates for what a fighter cannot do.

**Canon terms.** Sub-Stat (one of sixty-four; points are spent only here, R39-1); Primary (total of eight, Grade read off the mean); Tier Grade (Hollow, F, E, D, C, B, A, S, SS, SSS, X, EX, EX+); Path gate (a Sub-Stat capped until the Crystal recognises Body, Spirit, Attraction or Fate commitment from behaviour); instability zone (reachable "only under strain, never by allocation", R39-4). "The institutions measure the mean. The fight is decided by the peak."

**Physical Force (Part Eleven).** The Sharp-Blunt Asymmetry (an edge puts the same force into a far smaller area, so a B-Grade swordsman cuts what a B-Grade brawler only dents); the Focused-Yield Principle (Compression puts a Grade's energy into "a contact area smaller than a coin"); Durability in four layers (body, Aether Shell, technique defences, Domain resistance) set below peer Strike Force at every Grade, so a clean peer hit always does harm. At D-Grade a practitioner "can react to gunfire but cannot outrun it"; at A, movement makes heat and pressure waves unbidden; at S, "the air itself becomes a weapon."

**Undecided on the page.** How a Grade gap shows in the body before any figure (R70-89 fixes the order, not the vocabulary). Whether a three-Grade gap can be staged as a duel or only as the "event" canon calls it. Whether Part Four's glosses ("City Block", "Large Building") are scaling vocabulary, which R13-6 keeps off the page. How a supersonic exchange reads in close POV, and whether Vor, Nach and Indes (the Western words for first, after and in-the-instant) survive reaction times of one to five milliseconds. How the peak-versus-mean gap is shown: a ready-made reversal.

## 2. Stage gaps and Pressure

**What it gives.** Stage caps every Sub-Stat (Part Five) and gates Domains (seed at VII), Aether Class (from Glory, VI) and the anchor rule (below VII a working needs voice, hand, ink or blood). NATALIE grades Pressure by Stage gap: one Stage bends attention, two brings sweat and nausea, three drops people, a Band gap changes weather. Part Eleven grades **Aura Pressure** by Tier Grade: perceptual at F to D, physical from C ("loose objects vibrate"), a combat factor at A (an A-Grade near an F-Grade exerts pressure "equivalent to several hundred kilograms"), Shell damage at S, geology at SS. It runs on Harmonics Attunement and Projection with Dominion Pressure, and Path flavours it: Body heavy and compact, Attraction lighter and wider, Spirit aimed at the mind. **Suppression** masks it and "tires the user for as long as it is held", which Counterplay turns into a weapon.

**Canon terms.** The sixteen Stage names (Murmuring to Apex); Band; Tier of Standing (Initiate to Paragon); Passive Pressure Field; Suppression.

**Undecided on the page.** How the two ladders combine in one room: §7 says "Stage gap first; Dominion Pressure and Radius for how far", NATALIE gives body effects by Stage, Part Eleven gives physical effects by Grade, and R70-69 only orders the showing. Whether Path flavour is shown. How suppression's cost shows across a long approach. Whether the room's Pressure eases as a fighter Starves and reads a Grade lower.

## 3. The four combat grammars, and schools

**What it gives.** Blade hunts openings (traced passes); Verdict reads, ranks, commits (stillness, then one cut); Percussion hunts fatigue (the Settling, the Sweep, the Shove, the Return; uses plate; its tell is watching breath and footing); Expenditure hunts cost (the Offering, the Standing, the Ledger, the Absence; plate irrelevant; its tell is not looking at the opponent). Nine named assignments are live.

**Canon terms.** The moves above; HEMA (Historical European Martial Arts, the reconstructed Liechtenauer and Fiore sword traditions) as the Western school's list. The wiki also holds WOTR's own named disciplines: Ken, Kami no Kobushi, the Iron Bison, Nadi, Recarvu, Rusashin, Talos, Asami and Gisei.

**Undecided on the page.** Whose shape governs a duel between two grammars (the guide says it does not settle this). Which grammar a beast, construct or Well-spawn fights in. Whether the wiki's named disciplines count as "schools" for R70-100 and verify's per-school lists, and what their move words are. Owed by rule: the Percussion master-strike table, "Dawi-authored in-world" (R20C-25), and the remaining named assignments (R20C-54).

## 4. Crafts and chants

**What it gives.** A working is "a sentence in Parun given a medium." **Spellcraft** (*Ars Vocis*, "the speaking"; contempt word "a mouth") "happens now" and costs "the body, immediately, in the same scene"; its vectors are spoken, gestured, bodily (Corporis) and internalised. **Runecraft** (*Ars Scripti*, "the cutting") outlives its maker and misfires continuously if wrong. **Draftcraft** (*Ars Vasis*, "the pouring") puts a working in unworked hands. **Chantcraft** (*Ars Cantus*) "scales with throats" and cannot be practised alone. Prose cues on record: nosebleed, tremor, "the hand that will not close"; someone reacts to a working spoken aloud in company; "Nobody polite says casting." Spellcraft appears when someone is out of time, options or standing; Kharven ranks it highest in the Concord.

**Undecided on the page.** How a chant in its own language is set inside a fast exchange (whole, cut, half-heard), and whether the world waits for it as R70-101 says it does not wait for a declaration. How a choir fights. Which register (Accord Latin or common tongue) narration uses for a working mid-fight. **Flagged:** NATALIE.md says "Chantcraft is a delivery of Spellcraft, not a fifth craft"; live row R20C-41 says "Chantcraft is the fifth craft, Ars Cantus its medium-home."

## 5. Glyph work and casting time

**What it gives.** "A glyph is a boundary condition imposed on that law"; it creates nothing, and the law runs on after the intent stops, which is why practitioners burn by their own workings. Four **Glyph Roles** in order: Authorization, Boundary, Direction, Sealing; "Sealing is the role that kills people." Only two Counter forms exist in 136: **Knot** (entangles a path) and **Lock** (occupies a limit). Below Refraction: "Break his wrist and a gestured chain is finished. Put a hand over his mouth and a spoken one is." Silent casting tells a room a man's Stage. Speed of casting is **Ardency Alacrity**; **Gnosis Retention** sets how many workings are held loaded; **Fluency** decides whether a working does "what you meant" or "what you said."

**Canon figures.** None in seconds. The six-second turn is the only clock; the Summon Register's "Readiness" field is filled per entry.

**Undecided on the page.** How long a working takes in prose time, and whether the roles are shown being laid or only failing. Whether a Sealing failure is a staged combat beat. How Knot or Lock reads to a POV. Never invent a casting time: canon has none.

## 6. Wellsprings in a fight

**What it gives.** Sixty Wellsprings in eight Families by physics: Caloria (heat), Fulguria (charge and light), Vectoria (force and gravity), Spatium (position), Materia (matter), Vitalia (biology), Fluxia (fluids and pressure), Limina (entropy, void, mind). Harmonising changes what a number means, not its size (Fortitude under Monolithion "begins behaving like stone"). Typology flavours being hit ("The Caloria practitioner's Flux burns... The Limina practitioner's Flux ends"). **Essence-Typed Damage** adds above the joule rating: Fractura can fail a Shell at "thirty to fifty percent below" its ceiling by finding its fault line. Ground decides by Family (Counterplay): Caloria fails in saturated cold and thin air; Fulguria against boiled leather, fired clay and fog; Fluxia needs a medium; Vectoria needs something to push against; "winter, bare stone and salted earth are a Vitalia sentence"; Limina needs a gradient, and a crowd is noise.

**Canon terms.** Core Law, Physics Domain, Family, Typology, harmonisation, Wellspring Baptism, Tempering Capacity, the Four Theories (Consent, Correspondence, Debt, Residue) argued in mouths.

**Undecided on the page.** How the Core Law's causal sentence lands outside first display and finisher. How Typology is written from the struck man's body. Whether ground by Family is shown as a choice made before contact ("Pick before contact"). How a Wellspring's name reaches narration through a POV who could not know it.

## 7. Domains and Domain clashes

**What it gives.** "A space where a character's inner law becomes local reality"; not a technique. Seed at VII ("Collapses if attention fractures"), true Domain at VIII, Realm at IX to X, leaking at XI to XII. Behaviours: Low-G/High-G, Hush, Heat-Invert, Vectoring, Construct Sustain, Inertial Dominion. **Domain versus Domain** resolves in order: Temperance differential (two Stages or more overwrites), Dominion Sovereignty and Gravity, Wellspring compatibility, then doctrine coherence (Tempering Coherence and Maturity). Upkeep is continuous; collapse throws a **Resonance Shockwave** that hits the holder too. The ladder (Arm's-reach, Doorstep, Yard, Holdfast, Precinct, Burgh, Dukedom, Globe, Firmament) reads joules held per turn, and **density in J/m² is the tell** "an opponent standing at the edge can estimate." "A Holdfast detonated is a Precinct for one instant." Beastkin Domains carry lineage laws (Catkin first-action authority, Lupine hunt geometry, Bearkin mass).

**Undecided on the page.** How a Domain is rendered in a duel (the Mass Combat Guide §5 rules battles only: "Describe the malfunction of the ordinary"). How the clash order is shown so the result can be reconstructed (R13-8). Whether raising a Domain is the holder's display beat, and whether its name is called. Whether Domain density can be a readout (R70-85 allows only Appraisal, wound, Essence spent). How collapse and deliberate detonation read.

## 8. Traits

**What it gives.** "The crystallized law of a soul." Primary (always on), Secondary (triggered; "a catecholamine-shaped surge, and a debt afterward"), Inherited (runs without consent), Passive (the atmosphere, and the most identifying), plus Compound and Higher-Imprint. Offices: Bias, **Permission** (some techniques cannot exist without a matching Trait), Nucleation, Ceiling. Traits rewrite tissue ("A body is a Trait record and a corpse is a better one"). In a fight: **the Flicker** under Starvation, **Overgrowth** (a Trait acting unwilled), **Shear Break** (a denied Trait turns on its bearer), Resonance Fatigue, and **the Rigidity Effect**, which Counterplay calls "the cruellest counter in the register."

**Undecided on the page.** Whether a Trait is named in narration, in a mouth, or only shown as behaviour. How a Secondary surge and its debt fall against the six-second turn. How the Flicker is written from inside and from across the room. Where a Trait awakened in a fight takes its readout ("New Trait" is a growth moment under LR7) beside R70-91's placement.

## 9. Resonant Pairs

**What it gives.** Ten Harmonic Convergences when two Sub-Stats from different Primaries reach one Grade. For fights: **Reactive Cast** (Alacrity + Celerity at B: techniques fire "without a declared action window"); **Controlled Detonation** (Overflow + Overchannel at S: an Overchannel event routed outward as an area); **Seam Sight** (Forecast + Sense at SS); **Void Anchor** (Persistence + Hardening at SS: cannot be possessed or overwritten below EX); Fortitude + Integrity at A (trauma fracture risk down 15%); Attunement + Depth at A (draw cost down 20%). NATALIE's 2026-09-12 ruling lets pair unlocks be named in a Measurewright's or opponent's explanation.

**Undecided on the page.** How a pair shows as behaviour with no explainer present. Whether a pair forming mid-fight is a growth moment. How an opponent recognises one in time to answer it.

## 10. Reserve and AU/s as a clock

**What it gives.** **EU** (Essence Units, the reserve; 1 EU = 1 MJ), **Flux Density** (EU per gram), **AU/s** (output rate; 1 AU/s = 1 MW; AU/s = Flux Density × η), **η** (eta, efficiency). Time to empty is reserve ÷ AU/s; to Starvation, 0.9 of that; one turn is six seconds. "Most reserves empty in under two minutes at full draw." **Starvation** below ten percent reads stats a Grade lower, thins the Shell, flickers Traits, and drops η as the reserve drops, so the starved man "is brighter for the same work." Recovery: about ten percent a minute passive; active two to three times that, only while not fighting; Crisis Recovery "cannot be manufactured." **Overchannel** spikes output two to five times the sustainable ceiling at fracture cost. **Absorption** banks taken energy and can overfill: past half again over rating the lattice sets Overgrown, at two and a quarter it bursts, and the bank leaks at (1 − η) per second ("Feeding an absorber is cheaper than out-hitting him"). Some workings forfeit reserve permanently.

**The waste tell.** Waste leaves the Shell as heat and light; at full output even an η 0.99 practitioner "sets timber alight at fifteen metres"; a high-η fighter is "nearly dark", so one "working in silence and in the cold should frighten a reader more, not less." Aftermath: scorched ground, a burnt ring, a room still warm. Above η 1.0 the room pays and stays thinner.

**Undecided on the page.** How the waste radiance is seen while "it glowed" stays banned (R9-3: discharge reads as broken geometry). How an Overchannel spike and its deposited damage share one exchange. How absorption, overfill and leak read from both sides. Whether Crisis Recovery may land at the deciding exchange without reading as convenience. How the body's stamina clock (grip, breath, legs, in that order) interleaves with the reserve clock.

## 11. Items and Tiers

**What it gives.** An item's Tier is "the lower of what its material can hold and the Tier of Standing its maker stood at when it was made" (R46-1): Plain, Marked, Proofed (*Probatum*), Tempered, Instrument, Hallowed, Storied, Found, Numinous, Proscribed. An enhanced weapon "performs no higher than the Grade of whoever put the Essence into it": wielder if channelled, maker if made; a relic keeps its maker's ceiling in any hand. Costs: an enhanced round 1% of the maker's reserve, a standing weapon 10%, proofed armour 25%, a relic the whole reserve plus Starvation; "A practitioner can equip themselves but cannot equip an army." Weapon rungs run Sidearm, Linepiece, Wallbiter ("a working in the steel outweighs the steel"), Siegepiece, Fieldwinner ("taking the piece from its bearer is a war aim"), Kingsarm, Landbreaker, Moonsplitter, Unmaker. "Every enhancement is findable": a Harmonist (a practitioner who reads resonance) reads it without touching it.

**Undecided on the page.** Whether stamp or rung is named in a fight or only in an appraisal. How channelled and made weapons are told apart on the page, since their answers differ (the hand, or the proof). **Flagged:** the Item Tiers page gives a Stage II maker "about 0.56 EU", while Part Nineteen gives Stage II a working band of 2,710 to 50,300 EU.

## 12. Armour and penetration

**What it gives.** Combat Guide §4 gives each armour a physics, a metaphysics and a prose cue: padded (bruise, not gore), mail (immune to cuts, weak to thrusts and blunt, rings shaken into "psychic tinnitus"), brigandine (gaps are the openings), plate (joints and grapples), ceramic shard (fails on the second hit in one place), plus inscriptions on any of them: Mending Arrays, Impact Damping Seals, Aether Plate, and Weight Ablation Glyphs whose failure drops the true weight "all at once." §7 reads **Ardency Penetration** against the tier. "A strike one Tier above the proof breaks it; a strike two Tiers above passes as if it were not there" (R46-3). Bearing, the material's plain physical work, is never on the ladder.

**Undecided on the page.** How the three senses of "proof" stay apart: the proof-mark for a named charge at a named range, the item Tier Proofed (D), and the ammunition tier "proofed shot." How R20C-26 ("It is never the armour") sits beside "passes as if it were not there"; the Combat Guide declines to gloss it, and the questionnaire should ask rather than settle. Whether forged or repaired proof-marks are duel texture. When a Weight Ablation failure lands.

## 13. Firearms and enhanced shot

**What it gives.** "Powder weapons are slow, loud, and honest. They open an engagement, they do not decide one"; inside roughly twenty feet a fired gun "is a club" (R11-3, R11-5). The reference revolver ball: about 16 g at about 235 m/s carrying about 440 J. Speed Grade decides the dodge: E does not register it, D registers and is hit, C dodges, B dodges with room and can catch (catching needs a Travel floor twice the ball's speed). An enhanced round "is dodged at its actual velocity", is paid per shot, goes stale with its maker's Duration, and carries a findable signature. Ammunition tiers: common shot ("kills men, annoys practitioners"), proofed shot ("defeats proofed plate but not a Domain"), the named round (one-shot assassination ordnance). Counterplay: "A volley is a volley", and drilled numbers kill a practitioner.

**Undecided on the page.** How reloading is timed against the six-second turn across muzzle-loader to repeater. How a C or B dodge is seen. Whether a Harmonist reads an enhanced round mid-fight or before. How the named round is staged. C-001 holds: the hole is shown, the cause is not.

## 14. Summons and constructs

**What it gives.** Five Summon Tiers (Ephemeral to World-Tethered). **Crystal Provenance** decides survival: Donated (a shard of the summoner's own Core on a tether; "Dies instantly in Harmonic Silence"; cannot refuse), Analog, Native ("Answers by recognition, never by compulsion"), Anchored (outlives the summoner). **Bond Class**: Directed, Interpretive, Consenting, Sovereign; "a refusal is the one event that takes a called thing's whole rung off the field without a blow being struck." The Summons ladder places a call by cost times η (Wisp to Starbearer); Shieldmate is "the lowest rung at which a summoner fights with the summon rather than behind it." Sustain is a clock on the summoner's reserve, and "Every construct broadcasts its authorizing glyph and its summoner's resonance." Expenditure grammar is assigned to Legion summoners and construct-users.

**Undecided on the page.** How arrival and Readiness are staged. Whether a named summon earns a display beat of its own (R70-73 counts named practitioners). Whether a summon with a voice may take a chorus line or a POV. How a refusal is written. **Flagged:** the Summon Register sets the invocation "in Guild Accord liturgical Latin", while R50-26 supersedes the Latin default.

## 15. Beastkin

**What it gives.** The Five Beastkin Lineages carry percentage stat modifiers, Path splits, Wellspring affinities, glyph backlashes and Domain laws. Catkin: Dexterity definitive, "built to not be where the force lands", fragile in attrition. Lupine: pack-amplified output, **Isolation Decay** as their counter, Bloodmoon berserk risk. Bearkin: **Reaction Lag**, "They do not dodge. They endure." Fox-spirit (Mishiro-kin): "This is not a combat lineage"; the nose reads at a layer most practitioners cannot reach; olfactory overload is its fracture point. Serpentkin: Chronos-Vision, after-images of earlier workings. NATALIE: Yoko's "tail and ears betray her first."

**Undecided on the page.** How a scent read is written as the floor's item 3 for a fox-spirit POV, and whether "nose before eyes" changes the tempo words. Whether lineage modifiers reach a readout. How an opponent uses involuntary tells. The beastkin page still uses retired Sub-Stat names (Chord, Ossification, Sapience, Burst, Detonation) and an older phoneme glyph set.

## 16. Well-spawn and monsters

**What it gives.** The Bestiary ladder places a beast by its hardest blow: Hearth-pest, Field-bane, Road-bane, Hamlet-bane, Town-bane, March-bane, Realm-bane, World-bane, Sky-eater. Three modifiers each add a rung: **reach**, **persistence** ("Hard to hit does not lift the rung"), and **the tie** to a site, "the only modifier that can be taken away in the middle of a fight." "Any rung kills a man." Commission Danger Tiers (T1 to T9) name the rank the Accord sends, not a Grade. Well-spawn "eat draw", so they come at practitioners; each inherits its Wellspring's pathology as a weakness; "Plain shot kills most of them"; often the unawakened are the answer (a porter with a spade has no Shell for a Tinder-fox to fire). Every entry names its fair counter (ring the Tolling Crab's note back; overfeed the Lamp-drinker; hoist the Sump-toad fast).

**Undecided on the page.** Which shape a monster fight takes. Whether the eight-item floor applies whole, and what "injury by structure" means on a stone-grown body. Whether a beast appraisal (R70-85: "Reading a person, beast or item") prints the rung or the Commission tier. How a creature is shown losing its tie.

## 17. Wards and counters

**What it gives.** A ward is Runecraft on a substrate, "beaten the way Runecraft is beaten: damage the substrate." "Nothing in the register stops a working by standing in front of it... Neither is a wall and both are written as one constantly." Seals can be forced early, "with the difference deducted from the speaker"; some end when countersigned or spoken back. **Silence** removes possibility, severs Donated constructs, and is felt "as loneliness rather than weakness." Ward-chalk holds small Well-spawn off a camp. The four routes: "Deny the field. Break the boundary. Break the body. Break the man."

**Undecided on the page.** How a struck ward reads (Runecraft's rule: "the wall is older than the war", with a date, an author or a worse repair). Whether breaking a ward takes the three-layer hit model. How Silence is entered and felt in a duel. How the four routes show as a fighter's plan.

## 18. The Draw Age mains as terrain

**What it gives.** Draw mains in brick culverts, standpipes, gauge-housings, meters and seals; supply grades (sealed domestic, trade, bonded, the illegal spur); the **draw-hum**, and "the silence arrives before the dark does." Cutting the main is the cheapest counter, decided "by people who will not be present at the fighting." Density classes in EU per cubic metre (equal to megapascals): Ambient Saturation 0.1 to 10, Active Concentration 10 to 10,000, Veil-Thin Nexus above that, then a Wellspring Core. Reach (anchor, authority, addressing, one to ten to a hundred) falls as the square root of density: "The ground a practitioner would choose for output is the worst ground he could choose for reach." Saturation at 10,000 EU per cubic metre fractures everyone present; "you cannot flood a field, only a building." Ringing residue falls a hundredfold in about seventy-five minutes, so "fought-over ground is short ground for hours afterwards." A drawn circle "is worth more than a Grade."

**Undecided on the page.** Whether a duel names the district's supply grade, and who can read a gauge mid-fight. How a cut is staged inside a fight, and how the hum's failure serves R70-69's room-first Pressure. How residue on fought-over ground shows to a second fight there.

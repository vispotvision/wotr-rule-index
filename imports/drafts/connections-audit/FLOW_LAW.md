# Flow law: the design every fix agent builds from

Isaac's answers are R85-31 and R85-33. Everything here not marked as his is an agent ruling (R85-34) that he may overturn. The five reviews in this folder have the detail, with file and line, and their recommendations stand unless this file says otherwise. Familiar words sit beside WOTR's (R85-32): Aether is mana, Essence is spiritual energy; Superpower Wiki and VS Battles terms are welcome in documents.

## 1. The chain, from Omnara to a blow

1. **Omnara and the Division.** The Division raised the planes. **Bara** stands over Fate, which is no reality: every possibility, idea and concept, the law and construction of the world. **Aegor** is Physical Reality, and **Ashura** is Spiritual Reality, the Soul Plane. (His.)
2. **The Veil** (agent ruling, built on his "all 3 can be considered inside the veil, aside from perhaps fate"). The Veil is the skin of the lower realities. Physical and Soul both lie inside it, and every realm is a region inside it. Its **inner fold** runs between Physical and Soul, and it is what a crossing crosses, thick or thin by the place. Its **outer face** is where the lower realities meet Fate, so nothing is on the far side of it but what both sides depend on. A thread pulled down from Fate comes in through the outer face. The Veil is a condition with no address: everywhere, thinner in some places.
3. **Works, Archons, Titans.** Bara wrote the Works. An Archon is a Work crowned, a law enthroned; a Hollow Throne is the law with nobody home. Most if not all Archons are Sovereign on all three planes, and Uurgath and Urion are unparalleled in Fate. **Titans descend from Archons** (his): an Archon is a principle enthroned, a Titan the same principle bearing weight. The pairing is ragged, as the Titan page says, and the descent is argued. The Epochs were the forging parties. Bara stands above the Archons, so "the most powerful things there are" is wrong: they are the most powerful things that still act there.
4. **The Wellsprings** (his). **Titans are the Wellsprings themselves, and the Sixty are their manifestations**, each of the Sixty being one way a Titan's law shows up, carried by Aether. They come from the Archons by way of the Titans. Titans bear all three planes, so the Sixty make up all three. Each entry names its Titan. The Sixty stay sixty. A Titan's Ground is the law at full scale. Vohrin's frost remains a Titan's own power worked at that level, outside the Sixty. A Wellspring's kind names its home: Wild to Aegor, Spirit to Ashura, Fate to Bara, Medium to the Veil. Atla'zon's "new Wellsprings" become new sites.
5. **Aether (mana).** What remains of Omnara undivided, which is why it can carry any idea. Only a body can pull it. The medium is endless and any ground's supply is not.
6. **The soul.** Shell from Aegor's ground, Core from Ashura's breath, Layer from Bara's thread. **Essence (spiritual energy)** is the soul's stamped blood: Aether refined and stamped with identity. The unwoken hold a trickle they cannot spend. Below a tenth of reserve the Core frays (names, memory). A fight Draft's Essence arrives unstamped, and the crash is the stamping.
7. **A Titan walks no Path.** A **World Spirit** is a soul whose Domain claim has grown into a place. Old ones sleep and become Titanstone.
8. **Hierarchy of law.** Fate and the Works over all. Titanic, Archonic and Wellspring law are domains of authority and not ranks.
9. **Death.** Core goes to Echo, Shell to residue through the body, Layer to Name. Path sets the heaviest product: Body dead are what renderers race for, Spirit dead haunt, Fate dead leave vows still binding. Residue goes to the lens tempered on longest, and the unlensed feed the ground. Rebirth moves pattern and never a person; every death stays permanent.
10. **The two books.** Only Shell loss is heat. Core and Layer loss are paid in that layer, and blood and tells are the report. A nosebleed does not carry the energy.

## 3. Ladders, Paths and the sheet

- **Stage sets a ladder's ceiling, and practice moves you up to it (his).** A bought Stage opens the ceiling and not the rung. **Thread has no Stage climb (his):** grip by blood or blessing, plus decades; a sworn hitter who keeps vows is Thread I or II at most. **Studied runs two rungs behind the lead, capped at II (his).**
- One published table: Stage against each ladder's ceiling, and what moves a rung. Aether rungs carry the Body ladder: I object, II contact, III recognition, IV and V law, Ω whole system.
- **Path Stage on the card:** the highest Stage whose gates a Path has opened. Mastered means Path Stage equals soul Stage, re-read at each Threshold. Form: `Body VII Mastered · Fate VI Walked 35%`. "The high Grades" barred to three Paths means SSS and up. Class evolves on the highest ladder for anyone at Thread Ø.
- **Which ladder reads what:** the Core authors everything, and the layer it lands through sets the rung. Matter reads Aether, a soul or meaning reads Essence, a condition reads Thread. An art's Path line picks the ladder, and a true pull always takes Thread on top. A cut reads the maker's Essence, with Ø allowed small stamped work. Defense against a Spirit hit: Integrity and Anchoring, Ward for reads.
- **Soul Crystal Tier:** strike the numerals. It is what the Crystal has grown into, read by Stage, and it lags a bought Stage until Level fills it.
- **Release by Path.** First Release transforms what the lead layer does: Body the flesh and blow, Spirit the line and its reach, Fate the claim and Pressure. Second Release: Body's field rules matter, Spirit's rules meanings (the room looks unchanged), Fate's rules conditions. Every Domain is a Layer claim that runs at Thread Ø and pulls no thread; upkeep reads the lead ladder. Fate gates on Radius, Sovereignty and Gravity cap a Body Domain.
- **Boost cap:** Path modifiers and the lens apply after Tier, so they never move the letter and never count on the cap.
- **Harmonization, said once:** the lock happens at the Shell, where Aether enters; the tie is held on the Layer; the lens shapes Essence in the lead layer.
- **A vow** spends the soul's own share, and a pull reaches above it. **Pull price:** rung against distance from likely, as a share of reserve. A Work is an inherited grip on one function that lowers failure and never price. A personal grip can be won once. Blessing removal takes two Works or two Moto.
- Cut the Aether Index (one line stays as scholars' talk), Vitae, Intent, Resonance, Equilibrium, Viaforma and Via Anima. "Class Ø" becomes "the unwoken" or Aether Ø. "Pull" for Aether intake becomes "draw in" only where it would be confused with a thread, and the old draw system stays retired.

### Null-Light, Ashura's instrument

Parallel to Hataraki's Works and Matla's Practices. Known continuously, held exactly. **Law: to be known is to be held.** (Matla: to exist is to bear.) It has seven **Regards**; one is normal for a Yukari, two is rare, and nobody living holds all seven. It works on souls, on what a thing is, and at its far end on the stone itself. It gives no fire.

| Regard | Name | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| I | **Mushō**, the Null Reading | Know a soul or thing whole without touching it: its hurts, its lies, its Traits and what it will do next. Passes wards and disguises, gives the target no sensation, and leaves no trace |
| II | **Jōshi**, the Fixed Regard | Hold what you look at unchanging: a dying man stops dying, a Release stops mid-rise, a working stops half-cast, and a soul being taken stays where it is. Only as long as you never look away |
| III | **Ruten**, the Slip | Look unevenly on purpose and the thing runs ahead of itself: a wound runs its full course in a breath, a working burns through its fuel, a reserve drains, grief finishes in a second. The killing Regard |
| IV | **Kagenashi**, the Shadowless | Hold one soul so wholly known that nothing can be done to it unseen: it cannot be possessed, cursed or edited, it casts no shadow, and an attack on its soul finds no purchase. The great defense |
| V | **Ninshiki**, Recognition | Say what something truly is. The dead come quiet, Echo Beasts unmake, a spirit obeys its true name, and an illusion, a false Class or a stolen face stops working in front of everyone |
| VI | **Mugan**, the Unseeing | The Seikai's road, Null without Light, and forbidden. Withdraw recognition and a thing stops knowing what it is: a soul its name, a ward its purpose, a Domain its law. Every use cuts what it looks at and cuts the one looking |
| VII | **Zenkō**, Whole Light | Ashura's own, held by nobody. Every soul in sight held at once. Armies stop in the field and no one on it can die or move until it lifts |

**At war.** A Null-Light Yukari holds a line of the dying in place until the surgeons reach them, or sees every soul on a valley's field and tells a commander what each company will do, or slips the wounded of the other side all at once. **The climb** runs on Essence Class: Listener hears, Answerer is answered, Confessor reads wholly, Shepherd holds, Ferryman holds many. **The price is the Long Look.** What you hold, you must keep looking at, and you cannot stop knowing what you have known: every soul ever held stays in the Core, whole, for life. When you look away, everything held slips at once, so the longer the Hold the worse the release, and all of that lands on someone. Holders grow shadowless themselves: people forget their faces and do not feel their regard, until nobody but the house remembers they are there. **Counters:** break the line of attention (it needs a looker, not an eye), fill the field with more souls than can be held, or use the target's own contrary Wellspring; Fate can move what the look depends on, and a sound body simply outlasts a slip. **Tell:** the courtyard stones. Near a working Regard, things stop casting shadows.

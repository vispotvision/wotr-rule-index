# Arts and bloodlines: connections review

**Verdict.**
1. The top of the map is clean (Bara/Thread/Fate, Aegor/Weight/Body, Ashura/Breath/Spirit), and it breaks at the arts: the Moto and Zettari each have an eye plus an instrument, and the Yukari have only an eye, which the law books as Fate.
2. There is no Spirit equal to Hataraki and Matla.
3. A bloodline has no defined output. Each page gives something different, and the sheets do not match the prose: of eight Moto-line cards (counted by line surname), one leads Fate.
4. Paths, Categories, Methods, Direction of Travel and the thaumaturgic axis are five overlapping schemes that contradict each other, and only the Paths are law.
5. You cannot reliably place an art on a Path from its page. One scheme below fixes this, and most of the rest can go.

Short paths: **Disc** = `wiki/The Disciplines/The Disciplines.md`. **Tax** = `Reference and Craft/The Magical Categories — Unified Taxonomy.md`. **Paths** = `The Three Paths Routing, Recognition and the Gate.md`. **Hat** = the Hataraki page. **Ket** = the Ketsumyōgan page. **Hub** = `The Archaic Bloodlines.md`. **ZD** = the Zettari document.

*Recommend:* Ashura's instrument grows from what is on file. Null-Light is "Ashura's own way of seeing" and is held whole by no one living (Null-Light:28). Give it named practices, a law to match "to exist is to bear", and a price. Have the Essence Class rungs state the climb.

**2. The Ketsumyōgan is Fate by mechanics and Yukari is Spirit by Epoch and by card. DECIDE.** "Every edit in the sections below is a real pull on the Plane of Fate", with Thread Class opening early (Ket:56-58). But Ashura "knows the Soul Plane from inside, as recognition" (Yukari:35). Walk Naiser: Stage XII, Spirit 40 / Body 35 / Fate 25, Essence V, Thread I. Fate at 25 is under Walked, so Thread stops at I, where "nearly all of what is spent on a pull" is gone (Classes:268). The head of the house can barely use his eye. The law already holds the answer: "Spirit and Fate reads the thread and moves it" (R85-28), and a pull "shows on the Core before it lands". *Recommend:* the Perceive types (Shisen, Teishi, Reishi, Sanshi) run on the Core at Essence Class, the Edit types are a pull at Thread Class, so an editing Yukari is paying for two Paths, and retitle the eye.

**5. Five overlapping schemes. FIX in part, the merge is DECIDE.** Runes alone get four answers: Scriptia's plane is Fate (Magic System:79), Glyphica is Spirit (Tax:118-120), the Spirit Path is proved "by projecting and inscribing" (Paths:184), and "Any Path can take up any discipline" (Disc:43). Turgia is Fate and Ritus is Spirit. Synergia the Method is "Fate-dominant" and Synergia the Category is Triadic. Harmonia and Synergia are each both a Method and a Category. "Discipline" means three things: Affinity times Method (Lexicon:40), the "three expressive Disciplines" (Tax:22, with Vitae, Intent and Resonance, undefined and found in four files), and the shelf itself. Methods appear on 0 of 57 Technique pages and 0 of 21 Spoken pages; Categories appear on 2 of 13 Named Arts. The two classifiers split by shelf and never meet.

**6. Art cannot be placed on a Path from its page. FIX.** Answer to the question: no. "Path gate" means a Sub-Stat gate or the sample practitioner's Path, never the art's lean: "No Path declared" (Solarbound:67), "Path gate · None" (Edictum:83), "Declares the Spirit Path" (Somnii). At least 14 of 76 pages have a Category tag that does not overlap their gate: Hunter's Breath (Silentia, Attraction-negative; gate Body), Judgment Manifest (Animatria, Spirit; gate Fate), Principle Overwrite (Domain Weaving, Attraction; gate Spirit), Precept (Glyphica; gate Body). The Sage and Sho pages and Asami have no Path line. The model is The Iron Bison:27: "| **Path** | Body. The Aether Shell is run as a kiln". Split the field into **Path** and **Gates**.

**7. Moto lenses sit off home. FIX or DECIDE.** R85-17 sorts only six Wellsprings as Fate. Of Hat's 56 representative slots (Hat:136-210), 15 are Fate or Medium; Totality has none. Under the off-home rule, the model of the Fate Path runs mostly at "half again at most". Zettari primaries are 7 of 8 at home (ZD:396). *Recommend:* state that a Work's grip does not depend on a lens and the list shows what a Moto of that Work tends to temper on, then re-cut the lists toward the new sort.

**8. Which ladder sets η for a given working? DECIDE.** "Each ladder sets η for workings through its own layer" (R85-26), but every working runs all three layers (Disc:92-100), and "Every use needs Material, Soul and Fate" (ZD:217). Walk Heart-Warrant (Moto:17): Akagane, Reigan, Work of Spirit, a blow to the soul. Is it Essence or Thread? Tetsugen Akagane holds Essence I and Thread I at Stage XI, so either reading kills it. *Recommend:* the art's Path line picks the ladder; a true pull always takes Thread on top.

**9. Removing Families orphans terrain and grammar. FIX under R85-31.** The working's grammar opens on Family (Magic System:63) and "The Family is the WHAT" (Disc:15, Lexicon:26,40). Also Disc:148, Conjunctia, Derivation:98, Sage:28 and Ket:183, and 72 of 78 technique and spoken pages carry the field. **The worst loss is ground:** pages send readers to "the Caloria and Limina favor lists" (Winter Rend:46, Vorynn:46). Terrain, fails and counters must move onto each Wellspring entry before those Family pages go. Also Valorin doc:158-161, and NATALIE:103, 107, 207, 222, 243.

**10. Smaller FIXes.** Lexicon:44 "capacity is innate" against Derivation:78, which disposes of *native*. Traitus "Body-aligned" (Tax:160) against the Core that "holds identity, memory and Traits" (Paths:26). "Bairos" (14 files, Hub) against "Bara" (37 files, R85-21). The "Attraction" tag now means two things, shallow Fate and the layer; say Fate.

## The single scheme

Every art's page answers five lines, and nothing else classifies it.
- **Path:** the layer it leans on, one or a named pair. This picks the ladder.
- **Lens:** Wellspring and kind.
- **Form:** what the hands do, each with a home Path that is a qualifier and never a lock: Strike and Shape (Body); Read, Write, Rite, Make (Spirit); Sing (Spirit and Fate); Brew (Body and Spirit); Call, Bind, Claim, Pull (Fate).
- **Gates:** Sub-Stat gates.
- **Source:** on the person's card, not the art: Lived, Taught, Inherited, Bought, Blessed.

Each Path has one open climb, one bloodline far end (eye plus instrument, written by its Epoch) and one ladder. Hataraki and Matla stand parallel once the Spirit slot is filled. Kami no Kobushi then reads as Body and Fate (Gimbzo, Body 65 / Fate 35).

## CUT, ADD, REWRITE

**CUT**
- **The thaumaturgic axis and Derivation Index as system.** Its own page concedes "The cause is the seat" (Derivation:63), and Paths VII already owns that. It appears in no rule, no card, no NATALIE clause, and 0 of 91 art pages. "Axis" is never defined, and "Nor is the axis a Path" sits beside a battery across Wild, Spirit, Fate and Medium. What it does that the Paths do not: asks where a capacity came from and whether it can be taught (one card line); gives the pattern-and-gap read (the clean and rough seat again); holds Magister, Registered and Unregistered Natural, and the three-way institutional quarrel, which is the only part with story use.
- **Category alignment tags:** the only link to Paths, and they disagree with the pages.
- **The Plane column on Methods:** the source of the rune contradiction.
- **Tax 10, 14, 19** (Viaforma, Wellsprings, Aetherica): by its own line the headings "sort workings", and these do not.
- **Vitae, Intent, Resonance, Equilibrium, Formation:** undefined and unused.
- **Conjunctia as a Method:** it is just a working with two lenses.

**ADD**
- Spirit's instrument and its climb: nothing else fills that slot.
- The four-slot bloodline rule on Hub: gives every line one shape.
- A real Mahuo page, and Yukari lenses and stat lean: both are missing.
- Terrain and counters on each Wellspring entry: Families carried them.
- A Path line on every art page: the question cannot be answered without it.

**REWRITE**
- **Derivation page, into a short institutions page** (Magister, Natural, the Bureau, the Day the Battery Burned), merged with The Standing and the Title: keeps the plot, loses the axis.
- **Tax plus Methods, into one Forms page of about a dozen** with home Paths: the two classifiers do one job. Keep the concept names that earn story (Claustra, Silentia, Auguria, Mechanica as the crime) and Big Maud.
- **Method suffixes, into a Guild naming table** (fire plus -mancy): they work without Families, and clerks' words suit a world that runs on one wild concept.
- **Direction of Travel (Disc:19-43), into one paragraph:** used only on that page, and it gives Hataraki a priority ("authorizes") the Paths deny.
- **Hub's triune section, to show eye plus instrument per Epoch:** the real parallel, currently stated as "every one of them is fundamentally an eye".
- **Hataraki's second roster:** 7 names with Sub-Stat clusters "attested, not reconciled" (Hat:269). Rule it a register of practitioners' doctrines, or cut it.

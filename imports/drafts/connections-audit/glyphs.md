# Connections review: glyphs

**Verdict.** "A glyph is a boundary" survives, but it is still written as a limit on physics, so it has no account on the Spirit or Fate Path.
Families no longer matter to glyphs; the Index's two-part reading rests on them and needs replacing with Wellspring and Path.
Thread, the unit of Fate, has no glyph, and 10 of the Epochs' 12 listed glyphs are missing from the Index or mean something else there.
Parun, runes and tags are nearly one account, but they disagree on what a missing role does, and chants break the claim outright.
The eleven glyphless Wellsprings do not line up with the kinds, and the thirty unclassified forms can answer them.

Line numbers are as read on 2026-11-05; the Sixty hub is being edited.

## Findings

**1. DECIDE: the glyph has no Path account.** Index L20: "the glyph is where you tell it to stop". L29: "arguing with thermodynamics". R85-17: a Spirit Wellspring on the Body Path "still gives no fire". Seraphic Thread Blessing (Benediction, Path gate Spirit and Fate) sets its boundary as a chemical "rate ceiling". Oathrend L23 breaks an oath by pinning a field's phase at π. Pyreveil on Fate is "a claim" (Three Paths L45), and no page says what limiting a claim means. Glyphica is tagged Spirit and NATALIE L263 gives inscription to the Spirit Path, yet Authorization reads the Attraction Layer (Disciplines L81) and any Path may take any discipline (L43).
*Recommend:* a glyph limits what a Wellspring's idea is doing at the layer the Essence leaves through. The path and the kind of limit stay the same, and the limit lands differently.
- **Shell:** a physical limit, where the entry's physics applies.
- **Core:** what a thing is taken to be (Bai's hinges).
- **Attraction Layer:** a term or prerequisite, inside the soul's own share.
- **Spirit Wellspring on Body:** limits what the flesh can bear.
- **Fate Wellspring:** limits a bond, debt, name or odds; physics is only the scholars' analogue.
- **Home or away:** changes resonance and cost by R85-17, never the path.

**2. FIX: Family is load-bearing in the glyph pages (R85-31).** Index L23: "Family and Physics Domain say which law". L37: "A Family assignment names the law". L76, L125 to L127 ("six Families"), L144; Descent L240; NATALIE L103 and L107 ("the Family's reach"); Oathrend L23 ("wholly of Limina"). The Codex Physics Domain maps one to one with Family, so it carries no separate information.
*Replace with:* the seated Wellspring's entry for the law, the Path's layer for where it lands, and count of kinds for breadth. Balance spans Wild, Spirit and Medium; Binding spans Medium and Spirit. Family is empty exactly where Primary Wellspring is empty, so "the thirty" become forms seated on no Wellspring, and L37's kind-of-limit reasoning survives whole. Thirteen of them still have attested Wellsprings, so unseated does not mean unused.

**3. DECIDE: Bara, the Works and the lattice do not meet.** Three Epochs L37 gives Bara `[Thd]`, `[Ln]`, `[Seq]`, `[Knt]`. Against the Codex, eight of the Epochs' twelve codes are absent; Line and Dream exist only as Valen's `[Len]` and Kaetra's `[Ka]`, and Thread, Sequence, Anvil, Weight, Spirit and Awakening have no glyph of that exact name. `[Bd]` is Bedrock and `[Flm]` is Judic Flame; only Knot and Form match. Archons L178: "nowhere to read from that is not an Archon's station", yet Bara wrote the Works above them. The Hataraki main and Sho pages have no glyph or Parun account. The Disciplines diagram says only "authorizes". Counted Speech L307 gives the Moto a Warrant with no value and "delegated sight"; Bara's Loom is "a lattice of golden filaments" (Epochs L41). No page joins these.
*Recommend:* the Loom is what the Parun lattice is seen from above; the Archons read it from stations; the Moto read by delegated sight from the Loom, hence no cell value. A Work is a grip, never a glyph. Epoch forms are unattested, off-station, and uncuttable.

**4. DECIDE: a glyph and a pulled thread have no stated relation.** R85-23: a pull takes "extreme effort at every Stage". Yet every working's duration comes from Attraction Anchoring (Disciplines L98), and Seals name fate-shaped terms: `wolyeth` ends with a lineage, and Fourth Seals "end a bloodline or a building" (Counted L139). A Thread Ø speaker can apparently say one. The Codex has Knot as "supersedes a bound fate-thread"; Index L31 rewrites it as "entangles a path". Only Seraphic Thread states the split: "no thread of Fate", "the shallow water".
*Recommend:* put that split in the Index. Glyphs and Seals spend the soul's own share and set terms, and move only what already has a way of happening. They never pull. Knot on a sworn bond is contested Dominion Fate against Persistence and Continuity. Fourth Seals need a Fate grip or a Warrant that carries one.

**5. FIX and DECIDE: "one grammar" is not one account.** Trades L24: "identical in every trade". Three answers to a missing role:
- Counted L162, slot skipped: "the only harmless one".
- Disciplines L82 and the Long Loaf, no Boundary: runs for months.
- Trades L36: only Sealing failure outlives the operator.

Duration has four claimants: Boundary, Seal, substrate (Descent L50) and Attraction Anchoring (Disciplines L98). Counted L102 says Parun has no chants; Trades L196 calls song a "sustained sentence", and the Taxonomy hedges. Three notations share no bridge: Lexicon words, the older phoneme chains (Rune Arts L119 admits drift) and Index glyphs. `ma` means inward, Memory and Recall. `at pen ma ra` holds no glyph and names no Wellspring, though Authorization should.
*Recommend:*
- Glyphs fill slots, and a Wellspring is named by harmonization under the Warrant.
- A slot left wholly empty does nothing; a sentence that fires with a bad limit runs (the Loaf was authorized and fired).
- Seal sets the term, substrate caps it, anchoring holds it.
- Only spoken Parun must sum; state this for cut hands.
- DECIDE on chants: recommend a chant is not Parun, it sets its condition in resonance as Gravetongue does, and its words carry a sentence only when Glyphica is present.

**6. DECIDE: the eleven.** Confirmed: Pyreveil, Cinerion, Manganthra, Ascensio, Abython (Wild, 5 of 33); Nyxial, Penance, Redemption, Contrition (Spirit, 4 of 15); Tarturon (Fate, 1 of 6); Aurevane (Medium, 1 of 6). There is no alignment by kind. None is an Archon's Associated Wellspring, and none is Primary or Secondary in the Spell or Alchemical Index. The hub says "None attested" and defers to "Attested, not reconciled". The wiki still uses them: Pyreveil is the showcase lens (Three Paths L45), and Rune Arts L62 has "a Cinerion rune". Corona Lunaris (Aurevane) already uses Boundary across Light, two unclassified Roots.
*Recommend:* no Archon reads these eleven directly, so they take only the thirty kind-of-limit forms. That gives the blank thirty a job and explains the gap without inventing a glyph. Fate is also thin generally: 5 of 106 seated glyphs sit on Fate Wellsprings, against 62 on Wild.

**7. DECIDE: what the Valorin document implies.**
- Vaeloric Script is a hand outside the record, against Descent L25 ("Nothing in this record is a separate invention"). Make it a further hand whose surface is the soul, tracing the same lattice.
- The Cistern's lines "one word short" are Counted L162's skipped slot, which explains why they are safe. Use that.
- Errata (blunt, redirect, invert) looks like a third counter against Index L31. Rule it as amending a sentence rather than obstructing one, so no new Counter form exists.
- Grammar of the Unmade says Chaos has no grammar, yet Uurgath holds 11 glyphs. Rule that Uurgath's forms are legible limits and true Chaos is what breaks the sum.
- Authorization reads an Attraction Layer. Say what is read on a soul with no Crystal.

**8. FIX: "lattice" has at least five meanings.** Parun grid (Descent L25), Loom (Epochs L41), Division's "lattice" (Archons L161), Crystal structure (Three Paths L120), Valorin soul. Also the `[En]` Lattice glyph. Name each, and link the first three per finding 3.

**9. FIX: the Index's edges.**
- L136 says propose a new entry; L142 says "stay fixed"; Descent L242 says "never runs out"; R79-11 bars invention. State it: any path can be traced, and attestation is what is fixed.
- All Attested Wellsprings holds 14 names outside the Sixty, the Titans' grounds, on 42 cells, unexplained. Note them; Titanic Law outranks Archonic, so a glyph can serve a Titan's ground.
- "Fourteen Hands" lists 13 under its heading, with Parun as the fourteenth, and calls Gravetongue "not a hand". Say it is not one per Archon.
- Irath's "by what right" (Archons L181), the Warrant, and Dominion Fate are one idea and no page says so.

- "Six Families" as the breadth measure: it measures nothing now.
- Rune Arts phoneme table: a drifted third notation that its own page disowns.
- Epoch glyph rows as written: 10 of 12 fail against the Index.

**ADD**
- "A glyph on each Path" section in the Index: the missing core account.
- Glyph versus thread paragraph: separates a term from a pull.
- A rule for the eleven: makes the gap law.
- Hataraki and Parun passage: the Works, the delegated Warrant and the Loom are unjoined.
- One glyph-in-a-sentence worked example: no page shows it.

**REWRITE**
- Index "What a Glyph Physically Does" as "What a Glyph Does": physics is one layer of three.
- The Four Glyph Roles' failure column: must agree with the Counted Speech.
- Technique Codex lines: Wellspring, kind, Path, glyph, and a boundary stated in that Path's terms.
- Spell Index physics readings of Fate Wellspring workings: present as scholars' analogues (R83-11).

# Metaphysics as one causal chain

**Verdict.**
1. The bottom half holds: Aether, Crystal, Essence, Shell, Path, ladder and blow walk cleanly (*One Exchange*).
2. The top half holds as far as Division, three planes, Epochs. Then it stops explaining what comes next.
3. Nothing says where Aether, Wellsprings or souls come from, though the pieces already sit on separate pages.
4. Titans and World Spirits have no relation to the Paths, and death has no relation to them either.
5. Fate squares with Bara and the Archons once three loose sentences are fixed. Removing the Families costs the cosmology nothing and leaves two mechanical holes.

**Short names.** Under `wiki/`: *Omnara*, *Epochs*, *Veil* (Making of the World); *Archons*, *Titans*, *Physical*, *SpSeat*, *Strata*, *Abyssal*, *Pair N* (Powers Above); *Paths*, *Classes*, *Concordant*, *Domains* (Soul and the Climb); *FoW V*, *FoW VI*, *FoW VII*; *Sixty*, *Books*, *HMW*, *Vocab*; *Crossing* (War Cycle); *Hataraki*.

## Findings

**F1. Nobody made the Wellsprings. DECIDE.** Five answers, none joined. *Omnara*:25 "every Wellspring that would later pool" (latent). *Physical*:27 "new Wellsprings do not so much form as get compressed into being", against *FoW V*:21 "There are sixty, fixed." *Sixty*:30 "sixty of these ideas for it to carry." *Sixty*:236 "The Aether's origin point." *Books*:63, the Residue Position. Kinds name a plane and no page gives them an Epoch. *Recommend:* a Wellspring is one of sixty ideas come down from Fate (R85-22's own verb) and carried by Aether; Titans laid the ground it pools in (*Sixty*:167). Wild answers to Aegor, Spirit to Ashura, Fate to Bara, Medium to the Veil. *Books* IV's four theories stay as arguments over why it answers. Atla'zon's line becomes new sites, not new Wellsprings.

**F2. Aether has no origin and two definitions. DECIDE.** *Vocab*:40 "universal medium... Infinite and impersonal." *Veil*:193 "Aether is the Physical Plane's medium", yet *Sixty*:204 has a type that "works on the soul and nowhere else", and 21 of the Sixty are at home off that plane. *Omnara*'s currents never mention it, while *Epochs*:64 has Aegor compacting it and *Physical*:31 has planes unspooling into it. "Infinite" also fights *Crossing*:74 "The Wellspring is short" and *Sixty*:130 "does not belong to anyone". *Recommend:* Aether is what remains of Omnara, undivided, which is why it can carry any idea. Only a body can pull it, so *Veil*:193 becomes "no Aether a body can pull". Ground supply is finite though the medium is not. *Crossing*'s shortfall (:147) and the Withering (*Concordance*:232) are visibly one question, and one sentence should let a scholar suspect so while the Guild stays unable to prove it.

**F3. The soul has no origin, and Essence is two things. DECIDE.** *Paths*:39 "a little more of you than there was" makes Essence a product. NATALIE:83 "no Essence until waking", yet *Sixty*:152 gives the unwoken "an Essence Core like anyone else", and *Sixty*:32 says the soul "was already potent". What is a newborn's soul made of? The parts exist apart: *Physical*:71 Corpus "the source of Soul Crystal latticework"; *Epochs*:79 "micro-inhalation of her will"; *Epochs*:41 filaments "connecting every living soul". *Recommend:* Shell from Aegor's ground, Core from Ashura's breath, Layer from Bara's thread. The lattice is the soul; Essence is its stamped blood, and the unwoken hold a trickle they cannot spend. This also gives "spending yourself" a meaning.

**F4. Fate, Bara and the Archons. FIX.** They square: the First Current raised the plane (*Omnara*:43), Bara rose from it and wrote the Works inside it, and Archons are those functions crowned (*Hataraki*:61). So a Work is the law bare, an Archon the law enthroned, and a Hollow Throne (*Archons*:76) the law with nobody home. Three sentences break it. *Paths*:166 "the most powerful things there are", with Bara standing over them. *Omnara*:65 "unparalleled there", where R85-24 says "compared to the other archons". *Epochs*:92 "what Bairos wrote" claims all of Fate, when he wrote the Works. *Classes*:289 seats Archons at Thread Ω and never places Bara. *Titans*:12 "Neither wants anything" also fights "possible to be noticed". Mark it as catechism, and say only an occupied Throne can notice. Where the Epochs are now is unstated (F11).

**F5. Death is unjoined to Path, layer or faith. DECIDE.** *Crossing*:29-31 sends Echo to the Soul Plane, residue to "the Wellspring", Name-record to the Archive. No layer mapping; no answer for a soul with five lenses or none. No page says what a Path changes. *SpSeat*:223 "across incarnations", *SpSeat*:79 "What dies lives again" and *Abyssal*:128 "next rebirth cycle" fight NATALIE:169 "Every death is permanent". *Pair V*:53 makes an Echo a failure; *Crossing* makes it normal. *Crossing*:20 "comes apart" fights *Monster Cores*:193 "A Crystal cut". *Recommend:* Core to Echo, Shell to residue through the body, Layer to Name. Path sets the heaviest product: Body dead are what renderers race for, Spirit dead haunt, Fate dead leave vows still binding (*FoW V*:271). Residue goes to the lens tempered on longest; the unlensed feed the ground. Crystals are cut inside the nine days. Rebirth moves pattern, never person (*SpSeat*:101 "It does not move a person").

**F6. Titans and World Spirits have nothing to do with the Paths. DECIDE.** The only contact is *FoW VII*:62. No Titan order is Fate (*Titans*:46-61). "World Spirit" means a mortal Stage (*FoW VI*:74, 78) and a kind of being (*Hataraki*:64; *Four Ceilings*:76; Blessings:88), with no Cosmology entry. *Recommend:* a Titan walks no Path. It bears load in a lower reality, and has no Fate order because nothing bears weight on what is no reality; its Bairos bonds are provenance. A World Spirit is one thing: a soul whose Domain claim has grown into a place (*FoW V*:299), which is why every World Spirit stands under Fate whatever its road (*Domains*:52). The old ones sleep and become Titanstone.

**F7. A pull breaks the two books. DECIDE.** *Books*:25 "closes perfectly", but *Books*:51 sends the lost share out as "heat, sound, and structural bleed", and *Classes*:268 loses "nearly all" of a low pull. Half a Glory benchmark (*FoW VII*:221) is about 111,000 MJ, leaving as a nosebleed. The stratal book's three strata (*Books*:31-41) also omit what *FoW V*:271 calls the "third fundamental force". *Recommend:* only Shell loss is heat; Core and Layer loss is paid in that layer, with blood and tells as its report. Add Attraction to the Essence stratum's reading.

**F8. Attraction: source clear, boundary not. FIX.** Source and job are clear (*Omnara*:43; *FoW V*:271). But *Paths*:187 "what a soul refuses to let end" is nearly *FoW V*:287 "refusal to release", and "Current of Refusal" is defined nowhere. Hyeon Do-sun reads as either. *FoW V*:359's test (Attunement against Coherence) answers this; cite it on *Paths*. Obsession is the same force when the bond is no longer honestly witnessed.

**F9. Hierarchy of law omits the top. FIX.** *Cosmology*:51 and *Archons*:161 rest all magic on Titanic, Archonic or Wellspring law, placing Titans over Archons. Fate, the Works and the Epochs are absent, so Hataraki and vows have no stratum. Put Fate and the Works above all three, and say the three are domains of authority, not rank.

**F10. Realms and "Veil" collide. FIX.** Six realms (*SpSeat*:11), Utopia, Pale Realms (*Pair V*:52), Empyreal Veil (*Epochs*:81) and Astral Veil (*Epochs*:41) have no stated place, while *Veil*:9 is "a condition with no address". Implication of R85-30: the Zettari's sands, "the Paths", need a place too and collide with the word for the roads. State that a realm is a region of a plane. Rename the Veil-named domains.

**F11. Smaller.** Harmonization sits in the Shell (*Sixty*:135) and the Layer (*Concordant*:22): **FIX**, tied on the Layer, shaped in the leaned layer, which also supports F1. Archonic magic has no Path or ladder: buns at hedge level (*Pair III*:7), yet Archons are the laws: **DECIDE**, read by the layer it goes through, only the far end a pull. Spirit has no far end, though Matla and Hataraki do (NATALIE:265): **DECIDE**, Null-Light as Ashura's instrument (*Null-Light*:9, 29). Epochs now: **DECIDE**, gone or acting ("tri-epochal sacrifice", *Strata*:35; blessing-givers, *HMW*:202).

## The Families (R85-31)

They did no cosmological work: no Family ever had an Epoch, Archon or Titan behind it, and kind to plane to Epoch now carries the only upward link. Each entry's Core Law carries what the Physics Domain did, and the glyph still conditions that law (*Books*:33). Holes:
- **Stacking, DECIDE.** *FoW V*:236-238 compounds and opposes by Family, with nothing replacing it. Recommend opposition by contrary laws on the entries, and compounding only within one lens's years.
- **Hue, FIX.** *HMW*:172 "The Family... sets that hue". A soul with five lenses now needs a rule: the lens tempered on longest sets the base, and the rest add texture.
- **Stat affinity** is already on each entry. **NATALIE**:207 still lists the Families as fixed.

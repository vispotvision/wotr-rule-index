# Subsystem connections: The Magic System shelf

**Verdict.**
The Path, layer, gate, seat and Trait chain is sound; it is the best-joined part of the shelf.
The three ladders are not yet joined to Stage, Class, the Tier or the Fate Path, and that is the main break.
Release and Domain were written before Paths and never say what changes by Path.
Families still carry color, the grammar of a working, ground counters and interference, so R85-31 needs replacements as well as deletions.
Derivation, Methods and the Categories are three more sorting schemes that the three Paths have replaced as working law.

Files: Paths = The Three Paths; Classes; TGB = Tier Grade, Bands & the Aether Shell; Hub = The Magic System.md; II, VII, VIII = Fracture parts. Line numbers are as read today.

## Findings

**1. "Climbs with Stage" has three incompatible answers. DECIDE.** Classes 214: "climbs with your Stage". Classes 115: "The Shell climbs through refinement alone". At Stage VIII: Classes 232 says Radiant (η 1.0+), TGB 304 says field average 0.70 to 0.80, and Standing and Title 27 prints "Aether III Resonant... η .91". Taken literally, a bought Stage VIII gets Radiant. Above VIII no rung remains until Ω, so Class stops evolving for six Stages. *Recommend:* Stage sets the highest rung the lead ladder may reach, and the four roads move you up to it. Publish that as one table; a bought Stage then opens the ceiling and not the rung.

**2. The ladder rule breaks on Thread. DECIDE.** Classes 214 against 217 ("Unthreaded unless something extraordinary") and 266 ("most souls stay Ø"). Followed literally, a Fate-lead Stage VIII is Thread V Weaver, while R85-23 makes moving a field a "life's masterwork". Vows 81 makes a sworn hitter Walked on Fate, so a rung behind Aether IV gives her Thread III by keeping promises. R85-26 says a card's "η and numeral move... unchanged", yet the Thread numeral sits two rungs lower, so the conversion cannot keep both. A Fate soul at Ø never triggers a Class offer (Classes 51). *Recommend:* exempt Thread from Stage and Walked climbing. It moves only by grip (blood, blessing) plus decades, and Walked Fate reads at most II without blood. Fate-lead cards keep their numeral and refigure η. Class evolves on the highest ladder for anyone at Thread Ø.

**3. Studied beats Walked and lead. FIX.** Classes 215-216: at Stage VI lead is I, Walked is Ø, Studied "stops at II". Studied can read η 0.75 to 0.85 above a master's 0.60 to 0.70. A cap was read as a level. State that Studied runs two rungs behind the lead, capped at II.

**4. Mastered is circular. DECIDE (the loose end).** Paths 109: "gates... stopped being an obstacle at the person's Stage". Paths 114: late "until it is mastered". Base time appears nowhere, and whether a mastered Path un-masters at the next Threshold is unsaid. *Recommend:* each Path carries a Path Stage on the card, the highest Stage whose gates it has opened. Mastered means Path Stage equals soul Stage; Walked means behind it at 30 percent or more of practice. Re-read at each Threshold. Card form: `Body VII Mastered · Fate VI Walked 35%`. Also define "the high Grades" for three Paths (SSS and up).

**5. Families are load-bearing (R85-31). FIX, with two replacements.** Color of Essence (41 hits) rests on "Hue follows physics" (27). Hub 63-69 opens the working grammar on Family. Counterplay 30 fights "by Family"; VII Part 18 and V Part 13 carry affinities and "opposed Families"; Concordant 22 and Domains 117 need "opposed". Also Reinforcement 27, Classes 80, Release 41, Standing 27, card template 33, NATALIE (7). Limina as mind-magic (Windows 245, Blessings 134) loses its home. *Replace:* a hue line on each Wellspring entry, and named opposed pairs per entry. Mind workings are named by Wellspring.

**6. No rule says which layer a working reads. DECIDE.** Classes 210 says a blow reads Aether and a Core working reads Essence. VII 32 says all output is what "the Essence Core speaks" through the Shell. TGB 215: Ø means "Nothing gets out of that layer". So can a Body fighter on Essence Ø cut a rune? Classes 240 says "A wall will tell you nothing about Essence Class", yet Mun's Essence III blows wreck a fighter (TGB 14), and Spirit's +25% is Strike Force. Trades never name a ladder. *Recommend:* the Core authors everything, and the layer it lands through sets the rung: matter reads Aether, soul or meaning reads Essence, condition reads Thread. A cut reads the maker's Essence rung, with Ø allowed small stamped work. Name the defense against a Spirit hit: Integrity and Anchoring, with Ward for reads.

**7. Release and Domain on each Path are unspecified. DECIDE (the loose end).** Release Forms never mentions Path. Domains 52 gives only "more say", and Domains 50 says "the Shell's clarity" where VIII 117 says "the leading class ladder". *Recommend:* First Release transforms what the lead layer does: Body the flesh and blow, Spirit the line and its reach, Fate the claim and Pressure. Second Release: Body's field rules matter, Spirit's rules meanings (the room looks unchanged), Fate's rules conditions. Every Domain is a Layer claim that runs at Thread Ø and pulls no thread; upkeep reads the lead ladder. Add Domain to the Ø row at TGB 215. Say outright that Fate gates on Radius, Sovereignty and Gravity are what cap a Body Domain.

**8. The Body ladder has no link to Aether Class. DECIDE.** R85-25 made Aether Class "influence on the physical world", and Essence and Thread have "what it can do" tables. Object, contact, recognition, law and system (Paths 64) attach to no rung or Stage, and "until very high" is unmeasured. *Recommend:* I object, II contact, III recognition, IV to V law, Ω system, in the Aether table.

**9. A Work has no definition or card field. DECIDE.** "Nobody grips it bare" (Paths 164), but Pell Oakshot (Classes 270) and the Akagawa woman pull with no Work. Blessings 206-216 needs Knowledge plus Ruin or Purity, while "the grips do not trade" (Hataraki 95), so a one-Work Moto cannot do what pages credit to "those who hold Hataraki". *Recommend:* Pell's grip is personal and won once; a Work is an inherited grip on one function that lowers failure and never price. Blessing removal takes two Works or two Moto.

**10. Two-Grade cap against Path modifiers. FIX (the loose end).** III 212: modifiers "apply *after* Temperance and Tier". V and VII: no stat points change for a lens. So modifier and lens never move the letter and never count on the cap; they work inside a Grade (TGB 82). Add a Stacking row saying so. Fate's Domain amplification is also post-Tier but unnumbered, so give it a figure.

**11. Harmonization has three homes. FIX (the loose end).** Ladder and Draft 76-78 and Core Vocabulary 72 put it on the Layer; Trades 175 and Walking 154 say a sealed Shell "cannot phase-lock"; Concordant 22 needs it plural on the Layer. All hold if stated once: the lock happens at the Shell, where Aether enters; the tie is held on the Layer; the lens shapes Essence in the lead layer.

**12. Soul Crystal Tier. FIX numerals, DECIDE its job (the loose end).** TGB 263 reads numerals on the Layer for Fate, so Radiant demands Weaver. Strike the numerals (TGB 267-274, VIII 88-99) and read by Stage. The Tier maps almost one to one onto Stage, and no rule gives the "diagnostic" gap an effect. *Recommend:* Tier is what the Crystal has grown into, and it lags a bought Stage until Level fills it. That also gives the coil's seat reading a mechanism.

**13. Scriptia on Fate. FIX (the loose end).** Hub 79 against Paths 184 and Hub 112. Set Scriptia to Soul, with Fate only for writing on Fate itself (R85-30, written blessings).

**14. Essence as self has no effect. DECIDE.** "Spending it is spending yourself" (Paths 39) has no mechanic: Starvation touches stats, Shell and Traits only, and regeneration is free. Fight Drafts pour foreign Essence with no contamination. *Recommend:* below a tenth the Core frays (names, memory, Rigidity up), and fight Draft Essence arrives unstamped, with the crash being the stamping.

**15. Small joins. FIX.** Classes 266 "pulls nothing down" and Windows 136 "binding their own probability" need one sentence: a vow spends the soul's own share and a pull reaches above it. Stacking 21 charges lasting forced stats while Blessings 108 gives "a Grade for years" at no risk: the giver carries the load until letting go. Whether a blessed Level jump clears a Band gate is unanswered. "Class Ø" now misreads against Essence Ø and Thread Ø: say "the unwoken" or Aether Ø. "Pull" means both Aether intake and a Fate thread.

## Three sheets

**Body only, Stage VIII.** `Path: Body · Aether ? · Essence Ø · Thread Ø`. The Aether rung is finding 1. The force bonus stays off the stack. Second Release counts +1. Ward, Clarity and Acuity stop at C and Radius, Sovereignty and Gravity at A; that is how Spirit beats Body and why his Domain is small, and no page says it. Clarity capped at C while it governs η needs one line: the ladder sets the band, Clarity the place in it. Breaks at findings 1, 6, 7.

**Spirit lead, Fate Walked, Stage VIII.** Essence climbs; Aether I; Thread would be a rung behind (IV), which is finding 2. Bonuses at three-quarters, Fate gates at VII, rough seat barred above Tier Four despite a Master token. The lens has no stated home across two Paths. Domain amplification at three-quarters is unstated. "Practice" on Fate is undefined; recommend kept vows and bonds. Breaks at 2, 4, 7.

**Moto, one Work (Ruin), Stage VII.** Which Path? A Moto "reads, and then hits" (Hataraki 101), so sword force on Fate's smallest bonus, or Body and Fate at the two-Path price (R85-30 rules this for Zettari only). Thread I from childhood, then finding 2. The Work has no card field: not Trait, technique, blessing or Class. Whether a Release expresses the Work is unsaid. He cannot remove a blessing alone. Breaks at 2, 9. Add `Fate Art: Work · grip · Thread`.

## CUT, ADD, REWRITE

**CUT**
- *The Derivation of Practice* as a system. **Verdict: agree with the author, retire it.** No sheet line, window or card uses it, and its own cause is "the seat", which Paths VI-VII already state. Its battery runs on Families. Keep Hesper Lowe and the Magister and Registered Natural credentials as a section of The Gate. Replace "thaumaturge" in The Gate, Counterplay 80, Paths 147 and seven other pages (Lexicon, Hub, Standing, Apparatus, indexes).
- Vitae, Intent, Resonance, Equilibrium "aligned" (TGB 185-187, 201; Categories 22-27): a dead four-part scheme, and VII already says Spirit Path where TGB says "Intent-aligned".
- Viaforma and Via Anima (Categories 148-154): Path renamed, plus a surviving fourth drawer.
- Attraction as a Categories tag: say Fate.
- The Aether Index and Three Constants: nothing reads the score. Keep one line as scholars' talk.

**ADD**
- One-page ladder table: Stage, ceiling rung per ladder, what moves it.
- Release and Domain by Path, as a table.
- Which layer reads what, by working and trade.
- Pull price: rung against distance from likely, as share of reserve.
- Sixty hues and opposed pairs, in the kind-sorted Wellspring pages.
- Card fields: Path Stage, Fate Art.

**REWRITE**
- The Color of Essence: hue by Wellspring, clarity by Stage. Keep Part Six.
- Hub "Grammar of a Working": lens, Path, trade, four Parun roles. It also contradicts concept-first (R82), and Family is gone.
- Hub "Four Axes": Path is missing from the axes.
- Nine Methods: scholars' names for Path plus trade (Corporis is Body speaking, Scriptia is cutting, Alchemia is pouring), not a tier of the grammar.
- Unified Taxonomy: a librarian's index, out of play.
- Standing and Title: keep the titles and Third Name; the ten Archetypes appear on no card, so shorten them and rename Harmonic.
- Counterplay "by Family": by the Wellspring's physics.

# Titan to Wellspring table

Agent ruling under FLOW_LAW section 1 and 2. Thirteen Titans hold the Sixty; Vohrin holds none, its frost being its own power worked at Wellspring level, noted on the Wild page. Every Titan's Codex line was honored, except that Atla'zon's line and Kaldrion's both name Petralon, which went to Kaldrion; Atla'zon's art stays written in Petralon.

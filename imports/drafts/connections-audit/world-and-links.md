# World and links: connections review

**Verdict.**
The world outside the Magic System still runs on the old frame: Wellspring plus Tier, with Paths bolted on in a few places.
A monster leans on its Wellspring and nothing else. No creature outside the summon pages has a Path, a layer or a rung.
Peoples have Path percentages, but those percentages break the Mastered and Walked law.
The Draft chain stops at "feeds tempering" and never says which layer or which lens.
Links run one way: 50 of 65 Magic System pages get no link from outside the shelf, apart from INDEX, Start Here and the Reader's Codex. Only 3 broken wikilinks, and the Families removal is the largest single job.

Paths below are under `wiki/`.

## Findings

**1. What has a soul has three answers. DECIDE.**
- `The Soul and the Climb/The Three Paths…md:21`: "every soul has all three, the unwoken included".
- `Alchemy/Monster Cores.md:43` (also `Well-spawn.md:41`, `Dungeons.md:51`): "unlike a practitioner, no Aether Shell". `:197` repeats it: "a beast's stone has only the first".
- `Droval.md:89`: "they have nothing of that kind inside them", though `Monster Cores:47` puts a stone in every natural beast.
- `Bestiary.md:73-80` gives a beast a soul, a window and "Reserve: 41%". The Greater Wolf has an "Essence Howl". Neither can leave without a vent.
- Haelvorn and Raga (`:37-39`, `:51-53`) have all three, "Genuine".

Recommend: keep the law and change "no Shell" to "a sealed Shell". That is already what a Class Ø soul has (`Power in Use/The Working Trades.md:175`), and "nowhere to go" still holds. Evolution or a Level threshold cracks the seal, which explains auras and howls. Add one table: unwoken, beast, monster, Well-spawn, Spirit Beast, Divine, Celestial.

**2. Monsters have no Path, so the Path triangle stops at people. DECIDE.**
`Bestiary.md:34` calls Essence Affinity "the operative field". The hub's only Magic System link is the Tier Ladders. Weaknesses cluster around "a Sealwright, a long march, or a priest" (`:97`), mapped to no Path. `Spirit Beasts.md:7` says "rather than born of flesh", and the Paths page says Body has "no purchase on a soul", so can a hitter hurt a unicorn at all? No page answers.

Recommend: a "Leans on" field derived from the Affinity's kind. It already fits: the Greater Wolf's Pack Bond runs on Transference (a Fate Wellspring), and the Unicorn on Benediction and Somnalis (both Fate).

**3. The Draft chain breaks at "which layer, which lens". DECIDE.**
Walk it. A T3 wolf stone is rendered, and a Body fighter drinks the course (`Common Drafts.md:131`: "Feeds tempering directly").
- Tempering is based on a Wellspring (R85-14), and the wolf is Transference. Does a Caloria fighter's course need Caloria stock? No page says.
- The stone is Core-only, yet tempering "rebuilds each layer". What rebuilds the Shell?
- `Classes:68` says a Shell climbs "on a purse" by Infusion or the Forge Rites. `Paths:135` says Path cannot be bought. So what is the lead ladder of an heir with no Path?
- The bench already has the tool: `Alchemetrica.md:138-140` maps Sulfur, Salt and Mercury to Core, Shell and Layer. It is never applied to Essence Drafts.
- A Fate Essence Draft bottles Fate (`Master Works.md:97`), but its source is unstated. The only stock with a Layer is a dead person's Crystal, and the dresser shaves that off (`Monster Cores:197`).

Recommend: matching stock tempers cleanly and foreign stock adds carry. Core stock feeds the Core, Salt fractions feed the Shell, and shaved Layer is the black-market Fate reagent.

**4. Lineage Path figures contradict R85-16. FIX the wording; DECIDE the reading.**
- `Five Beastkin Lineages.md:161`: "Body 70% / Spirit 30%". `Elven Peoples.md:144`: "Spirit 60% / Fate 30%". `Marrowkin.md:198`, `Celestial Host.md:96-194`.
- Walked is 30 percent of practice, so every Ursine would be two-Path, at 2.5 times the time and three-quarter modifiers for life. Cards use the same format with Mastered and Walked labels.
- Recommend: on a lineage the figure is a lean of the untrained soul, and it never sets Mastered or Walked.

Also FIX:
- `Goblinoid Peoples.md:315-317`: "Cursed Path, inverted", "Chaos Path", and old stats ("+30 STR", "SPI", "Chaos").
- `Beastkin and Demihuman Peoples.md:44-51` and Soul Habit (9 lines in 3 files; `Marrowkin:169`) are Essence Typology renamed, which R85-14 retires.
- Names outside the Sixty: Selunite, Speculum, Viridian, Aqua, Caelum.

**5. "Path" has five meanings. DECIDE.**
- The three roads.
- `Zettari/The Paths.md:8`, a plane "above… totality itself". `Factions…md:32` lists "the Paths" as an authority.
- `The Tiered Path` (Guild rank).
- `Swears…md:177`: "Pathless. One who walks under no Archon."
- Valorin document line 156, "Path bias: Scriptia…", which means Category.

Matla, the "Instrument of the Paths" (`Matla.md:7`), now models a road named by the same word, and "the Zettari alone can carry it" while every Body fighter climbs its ladder. Recommend: rename the plane in common speech (the Sands), and state that the five-step ladder is the older learned reading (Material Force), with Matla as the Zettari's instrument of it.

**6. Harmonization sits on two layers. DECIDE.**
- Shell: `Alchemetrica.md:31`, `Common Drafts:133`, Fracture of Worlds Part 24:577.
- Layer: soul-crystal plate, `Primer:75`, `Ladder and the Draft:76`, `The Disciplines.md:81`.

If it retunes the Shell, a Spirit fighter's lens runs through the wrong layer. Recommend: the Shell phase-locks and the Layer holds the tie, said once.

**7. Fate ground is nearly absent from play. FIX, then DECIDE.**
`Dungeons.md:81-85` has Wild, Spirit and Medium rows and no Fate row. By script, 12 of 15 Spirit and 2 of 6 Fate Wellsprings never appear in Geography (Wild: 69 hits; Spirit: 8; Fate: 7). 10 Spirit Wellsprings and Oblivara never appear in the Bestiary. Penanceflare has no hits in Bestiary, Geography, Disciplines or cards. Recommend: add the row, and plant named Spirit and Fate sites and lines.

**8. Armies have no Fate roles. FIX.**
`Practitioner Problem.md:134-146` is excellent: "Fate users are pinned first". `The Order of Battle.md` lists none. Its war-priests read the Lattice, and the War Cycle has no links to the Magic System. Add Fate commander, thread-hunter log and inspection regime.

**9. Gear has no stated tie to Paths. DECIDE.**
`Item Tiers:88` reads armor on Penetration alone. Paths says armor does not cover the soul, a strap can be pulled, and matter can be read. Is Hallowed armor proof against Spirit? Does a good mark deny Fate? Are Master Works (a cut that must finish) Fate in an object? Which layer seats a bonded piece? Recommend: a short "Gear and the three roads" section.

**10. Spirit has no model lineage. DECIDE.**
Body has Zettari, Fate has Moto. `Archaic Bloodlines.md:16` already says "Thread, Weight, Breath", but neither it nor the Moto or Yukari page names a Path. The Yukari art is titled "Binding Fate Eye". Recommend: name the Yukari as Spirit's model and map the hub line.

**11. Smaller breaks. FIX.**
- `Summon Register:30` lists Aether Class alone, "how much of the donated Essence survives", while Core work reads Essence Class. Add Essence and Thread rungs to the Register and template.
- `Primer:47`: Fate is "the ledger of what has actually happened". Law says what could be.
- `Ashkaru.md:61`: "meaning on the Plane of Fate", where law puts meaning on the Soul Plane.
- "Core" has three senses: the layer, the stone and the site.
- `Value, Coin and Trade:272`: "It needs only to stop pumping", and `Bearing and Holding:62`. With the draw retired, say what the lever is (`Factions…md:21` already shows it: weights and credit).

# WOTR fight gold standard (draft, 2026-10-30)

Built from Isaac's answers (`answers.json` here and in `../jjk-fight-questionnaire/`) and research. Not yet a rule; Isaac approves or edits it first.

## What the research found

1. **Why my fights read as summary.** The standing law in NATALIE.md asks for "body, path, target, result" in a clause and lets misses be summed. That produces one clause per blow. Isaac wants the opposite: every blow, landed or not, as choreography with its damage.
2. **The archive has no model.** The densest archived "fight" (`hibiware.md`, 74 combat words in 4,000) is almost all mechanism lecture and mystic spectacle: cracks in glass, the note of a bell, the Kamigan. There is hardly a single punch in it. Matching the archive produces the same gap.
3. **Homer's kill template** (Iliad XVI and XX) is the oldest working model of anatomical battle prose: approach, a named body part as target, the weapon's path traced through named structures, a sense detail (teeth knocked out, liver rolling free, marrow on the spine), the body's reaction, how long the dying takes, then a taunt or last words. It is exact and short. Dark shonen adds time: it stretches the same beats.
4. **Outside craft advice** (Writer's Digest and others) warns against exhaustive blow-by-blow because it reads like a manual. The cure it offers is consequence and sensation per blow, not fewer blows. Isaac's call is full choreography; the way to keep it from reading like a manual is that every blow carries a consequence the next blow uses.

## The Blow Card (every blow, landed or not)

Seven parts, in this order, in as many sentences as the blow earns:

1. **Set**: the wind-up and the body: heel, hip, shoulder, the path the limb travels.
2. **Read**: what the other fighter saw and how they moved (the duck, the check, the turn).
3. **Contact or miss**: what part touched what part, or what the miss hit instead (a wall, a lamp, air that cooled a neck).
4. **Tissue**: what it did to skin, muscle, nerve, bone, tooth, organ. Named, specific, ugly.
5. **Sound and blood**: the sound-word, the colour and motion of blood, the smell.
6. **Reaction**: what the body did (breath, stagger, grin, scream, silence).
7. **Next position**: who stands where now, who has the weight, what the next blow now costs or opens.

Misses get parts 1, 2, 3, 7. A landed blow gets all seven. A killing blow gets all seven and then the Kill Card.

## The Kill Card (turning points and kills)

Homer's six, stretched by dark shonen: approach; the named target; the weapon's path traced through named structures; the sense detail (sound, smell, spill); the body's reaction; how long the dying takes and what is said; then the taunt, vow or silence. Gore is written in full: limbs and heads taken, insides, bone through skin, the dying lingered on (Isaac's answers).

## Coverage checklist (Isaac's answers)

- Per blow: wind-up and mechanics; exact target and contact; flesh and bone damage; sound, blood and reaction.
- Every blow written, landed or not.
- Between blows: footwork and position; breath, fatigue and pain; the mind reading the fight; talk, taunts, reactions.
- Technique firing: colour and glow; sound and called English name; ground and wall damage; speed and afterimages.
- Gore at kills and turning points: limbs and heads; viscera and marrow; bone through skin and crushed bodies; lingering on the dying.
- Aftermath: wounds that stay; who saw; the dead and the grief; the bill that comes due.

## Word toolbox (reference, no counts)

- **Hit verbs**: cleave, rend, sunder, pulverise, transfix, stave, split, hew, crush, shatter, flay, gut, wrench, snap.
- **Body**: olecranon, floating rib, jaw hinge, orbital bone, collarbone, sinew, tendon, marrow, gristle, viscera, brachial artery, windpipe.
- **Blood**: arc, geyser, sheet, spatter, spray, bead, rope, steam on cold air.
- **Sound**: *KRAAAK*, *THUD*, *SHHHK*, a wet crack like a green stick, a crunch like boot on frozen snow.
- **Heat**: bravado, taunts, laughter, savouring, a fighter who loves it.

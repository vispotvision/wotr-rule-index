# The Inner World: realms, houses and cities (draft for Isaac's red pen)

Isaac, 2026-11-05: the North (which includes the Moto), the South, and a center wedged between them. The model is the Holy Sea of Alabaster: a word of color or material, a noun for the land, "of" and the place. City names fit each house's culture. A star marks a name Natalie invented tonight; everything else is already on file or is Isaac's. Scene names stay as places inside each realm.

## The lie of the land (Natalie's draft from what is on file, for Isaac's red pen)

The Inner World is one continent, long from north to south and pinched in the middle by the Scourge of Hell.

- **The top:** the Polar Reach, tundra and permafrost past the treeline (the Aurora Reach).
- **The North:** a shield of old cold rock. Kharven holds the high plateau at its center. West, the Ridgewall (the Slate Ridge) closes the border, and beyond it lies Undaar-Keth in black basalt and boreal forest. East, the Ironwood March and the Scarlet Wood run out to the Ring, where Marrowhold's rib mountains stand. South, the land drops into the Greenhollow, and the Painted Rivers carry everything between.
- **The Center:** the Scourge of Hell fills the middle of the continent. Nobody marches through it, so all traffic between North and South goes around it by two corridors, the Kessen road on the west flank and the salt road on the east. Those two corridors are where the armies go and where the Center's people live. The Amber Cities of Nalūn lie on the Scourge's north side, in the dead sea's salt basin, with Halcrest at the gate where the beds thin against the northern rim. The Hundred Crowns crowd along both roads, with Kingsbridge on the Kessen road. The Sealed Charter sits at the east road's crossing. The Moonlight Forest lies on the west flank around its great lake, the Violet Peaks climb on the northwest, the Rose Coils on the old ruins of the southeast edge, and the Russet Crown on the east around the inland sea.
- **The South:** the land turns green and drops in long steps to the sea. The Long Fields (Ochre Fields) come first, just below the Center. The iron hills and salt pans of Sum-gol lie west, at the Kessen road's end, the Yukari valleys climb into cloud on the east, the Jade Plains open in the middle, and the spring country of the Wellheads steams in the uplands between them. At the bottom stands the white shelf of the Holy Sea of Alabaster, with the Copper Coast warm to its east, where the salt road starts.
- **Offshore:** the Crimson Isles lie south, past the sea.

Isaac has placed only the Scourge (center) and the three regions. On file: Kharven's bounds, the Greymane ridge, the Ring on the central shield, the salt basin on its southeast margin, the Kessen road and salt road, and the South's layout. Everything else in the Center is Natalie's placement.

## Monsters and Cores, across the whole map

Isaac, 2026-11-05: monsters move, and they are found across the whole Inner World, which is one continent and all this map covers; beasts and beast-like animals are more widespread still. Wellspring Cores are mostly found in dungeons and are also said to lie in vast territories. The Scourge of Hell is the one area known for extremely dangerous monsters. Natalie's reading, overturnable: a dungeon is what a Core makes around itself or what grows up around one, and a worked Core with keepers is a dungeon someone cleared and now holds. Every realm page should have its own monsters on the roads, its own beasts, and at least one dungeon people talk about, cleared or not.

Muken builds it during Arc One. Kharven is the one true kingdom and the capital. The Banner Houses hold lands inside the empire and are not kingdoms. Whitemere is a Moto name, from the white mere under the ridge at Kharven-Seat; the Kharven call the empire "the Stack". Both details come from old lore and Isaac may strike them.

### The Scourge of Hell

Isaac, 2026-11-05: a vast wasteland at the center of the continent, filled with monsters long ago. No ruler. This is why the Center is wedged and why armies keep crossing the same few roads: nobody marches through the middle of it. Every Central realm lives on its edges. Also called Hell's Scourge. The monster population is widespread, and the South, the North and the Center each get a turn dealing with it (Isaac, 2026-11-05). That rotation is an old, resented arrangement: whoever's turn it is bleeds, and the other two watch their border and count what it costs.

Art from Isaac (`art/scourge-of-hell.png`, credit lyraina.com): a colossal bronze-hilted sword driven point-down into the mountains, its blade a window onto a violet starfield, under a purple sky, with a lone figure on a ridge carrying an ordinary sword and looking up. On file in the old scenes: black roots, dry wind, perpetual haze, things that come up through the ground, wyrms with scales the size of shields, hobgoblins on the ridges above it. Natalie's reading: the sword is an old work of the Great Spirit War, who drove it in is an open argument, and the monsters may be what leaks from the wound. Sodoku's and Yoko's travels here stay off public pages.

## What the old states become

- **Nalūn:** no longer a kingdom. Its cities are free.
- **Ketsuen:** folds into the Silver Cedars of Yukari. "The Crow's Reach" survives as a name for the Yukari land.
- **Stannvaard:** gone. The Ring becomes Marrowhold; its rim mountains are the ribs.
- **Anguz:** dead mines, a place and no longer a power.
- **The Dawi:** replaced by the Marrowkin.
- **Thornwall's corridor:** now runs to Marrowhold.

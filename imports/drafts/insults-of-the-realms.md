# DRAFT: Insults of the Realms (the faiths, the Gate, the trades)

A new section for the **Swears, Oaths and Insults** page. The page already gives each culture its own words; these are the ones that cross cultures, because they come from institutions and from the magic itself: the faiths, the Gate, Stage and Path, the halls, the draw and the dead. Any culture's speaker may use them, in their own accent.

Written under R51-29 (each culture swears in its own words first; English swears are fine too), R51-30 (Earth religious swears replaced in-world), R51-02 (swears may bleed into close-POV narration), R51-19 (Latin anywhere by ear, correct classical form), R50-01 (borrowed real-language words correct and glossed), R50-28 (a coined word stands once Isaac reuses it), R48-13 (modern words legal in speech). The Source and Strength columns read as on the main page: **attested**, **derived**, **coined**, **English**; **mild**, **firm**, **hard**, **grave**.

## The Faiths

### The Holy Inquisition

The Ordo files in Latin and speaks in its Tenets. Its insults are classifications: a word from a mask is an entry, and an entry is the first step of a correction (Tenet III: the correction follows "in the same breath, by the same hand").

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pathless. | One who walks under no Archon. | grave from a mask, hard from anyone else | The Inquisition's word for anyone who follows no Archon. It cuts twice: an instrument that reads a Crystal with no Path declared also says "no Path," and the Inquisition holds the two are one failing, so every Class Ø porter is pathless twice over. ## Notes

- **Pathless** is Isaac's direction and canon. Every other coined word or sense stands once he reuses or approves it (R50-28).
- **Pathless and undeclared are one fact in two mouths.** An Inquisitor means theology and a practitioner means a reading; a scene where both words are said about the same man is a good scene.
- **Avius** and **fomes** are correct classical Latin: avius -a -um, "pathless, out of the way, straying"; fomes fomitis (m.), "tinder, kindling."
- No row uses God, Christ, Jesus, hell or damnation (R51-30).

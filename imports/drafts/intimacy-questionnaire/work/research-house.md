# How intimacy reads in the WOTR archive

House research for the intimacy and explicit-scenes questionnaire, 2026-10-04. A read-only survey of `scenes/` (187 archived scene files, about 948,000 words), the wiki mirror (cards, State of Play pages, the Ledger, Fracture of Worlds), `table/` (NPC roster, Ledger, Fronts), the Standing Inventories in `desktop/inventories/`, and the two questionnaires already answered. Quotes stay under twelve words. Cards live in the wiki's Volume I Character Cards folder and are cited as "card: Name", because several wiki folder and page names carry a dash the house style bars.

**Terms, glossed once.** An *explicit scene* puts sex on the page with anatomy named; a *fade* cuts away before it. *Dubcon* is dubious consent (consent compromised by pressure, rank, captivity, drink or magic); *non-con* is none at all. *POV* is the viewpoint character. A *card* is a character's wiki page; its *Ties* section lists relationships and its *voice block* fixes speech (Notices first, Never says, In grief, In joy). The *State of Play* is the per-thread page rewritten at session end; the *Ledger* records what scenes cost; *Fronts* are threats run as clocks. A *Standing Inventory* is a culture's texture file. *Beastkin* are WOTR's sapient animal-featured peoples. *Pressure* is the felt weight of a higher-Stage practitioner (two Stages up brings sweat and nausea). *Attraction Force* and *Obsession Force* are Fracture of Worlds Part Fourteen's two binding laws, recognition against possession.

---

## 1. The headline

**The archive contains no explicit scene.** Across 187 scene files there is not one passage of sex on the page and not one genital named in narration. A search for the working-man anatomical words Table Rule 9 and R49-51 call for returns only false hits (a gas stopcock in `the_leveller.md`, "climax" as plot) and the four Sae and Toma test samples written for the Style Law questionnaire, which sit in `imports/drafts/style-questionnaire/questions.json` and are not archive prose.

So the explicit law this questionnaire extends (Table Rule 9, R49-51, R70-133, R70-134) has never been exercised on an archived page. What the archive has instead is a small, consistent body of intimacy, about a dozen scenes, written through gesture, touch, tokens, bodies read as instruments, and grief. The best of them stop at the threshold of sex. One, *The Blank Seal*, goes far enough to show nakedness and arousal before it is interrupted.

## 2. Already settled: do not re-ask

**Style Law R70 (2026-10-03), explicit rows.** R70-133 (XS1): an explicit scene may set a season or nature image beside the anatomy, hold one physical detail at the peak, close its aftermath on a written or spoken exchange such as a morning poem (the Heian court's morning-after letter), and use mimetic words (the Japanese *gitaigo* habit: words for texture and state that make no sound), romanised, never italic. Isaac ticked all four, the mimetic words against a recommendation that warned of "comic-book effects in English". R70-134 (XS2): technique keyed to the POV's culture, the lover's culture shown in what they do and say. R49-51, re-affirmed in R70's "Settled, not asked": explicit narration plain and crude, working-man anatomical words, lush in sensation, blunt in naming.

**R70 rows that reach intimacy without naming it.** Third person only (VO3). Italic thought may use whatever the POV would say aloud (IN1, R70-37). Feeling carried by a held image, never named (IN3). Warmth as counterweight (HU2); tears break at a small thing (HU3). Kishōtenketsu (the four-part structure: introduction, development, turn, reconciliation) for quiet scenes; jo-ha-kyū tempo (slow, break, rush) (SP1, SP2). The blend fused and tilted by culture (K1). Kawabata and Tanizaki among the Eastern models (K5); Tanizaki is the classic of erotic fixation and power between lovers.

**Combat Law R71 (2026-10-04), rows that bleed over.** A player's fighter's wounds are world facts while how he bears them stays his (K9). Standing orders: Isaac posts conditions, Natalie runs them to the first uncovered branch (K5). Aftermath as procedure done as craft (CW12). Wounds carried forward by anatomy, not stat (CW16). Fear in the body owed for the untrained (CW8).

**Older law in force.** Table Rule 9 (plain anatomical vocabulary, clinical specificity, positions tracked, arousal scents layered, onomatopoeia committed, the NPC's own desire and refusal line and the right to change their mind, a consequence afterward, non-con and dubcon at the same specificity, aftermath never skipped). Rule 10 (one italic thought per NPC per scene). Rule 11 (full inventory on first sight, body with areas named). R48-38's aftermath beat, which the `wotr-write` pipeline extends to "including after any explicit scene". The `wotr-rp` skill: aftermath is a turn and the consequence goes on the Ledger. The floor: adults only, nothing sexual involving minors ever, no explicit sex on real living people, no bestiality.

## 4. Register and vocabulary

**Two registers coexist, and they are not one house.** The older manuscripts (*The True King of the North*, *The Path of Sorrow*, `YOKO_MISHIRO.md`) write intimacy in long recursive paragraphs: "the specific" in nearly every paragraph, ladders (the chain "the X was the Y, and the Y was the Z") four or five deep, a full paragraph to decode one ear twitch. Its strongest idea is real: love read through biochemistry, Yoko's italic thought weighing cortisol and an oxytocin signature "directed, not diffuse." That is the mechanism mandate (real science on the page) applied to love, and nothing else in the archive tries it.

The newer prose (*The Blank Seal*, *The Warrior Nurse*, the Muster's hair kiss) is compressed and concrete. The Blank Seal is the only page that meets Rule 11 for an intimate partner: Yūgiri's first sight gives "small breasts, a soft low belly she had never once apologised for," wide flat hips, thighs built by seiza (formal kneeling), a keloid under the collarbone; Wren's body is inventoried by her hands as she opens his shirt. Its arousal line is the most explicit sentence in the archive and it is plain: "she felt him go hard against her belly through the wool." It is the bridge between where the archive is and where Rule 9 wants it.

*The Road Two Days South* is a third register: one-line paragraphs, banter, reaction beats. Its archive notes flag it under the repealed manhwa directive, left untouched as Isaac's finished text.

**Vocabulary.** No anatomical words for sex anywhere in archive narration. No Inventory swear has a sexual root: the cultures curse by cold, scarcity, oath-breaking and bastardy (Accord "sine sigillo", "without seal"; Moto "written in the wrong hand"; northern law's "Ice" for a child born before a recognised union), and Kharven's one body-swear is "Frozen piss." No culture has its own words for desire, a lover, the act, or the body in bed, so R70-134 has nothing culture-specific to key to yet.

## 5. Positions and pacing

One scene tracks bodies, and it is a good model. In the Blank Seal: she behind his chair, cheek on his skull; her hands inside his coat; he stands and takes her jaw; the kiss; his hand into her hair, closing; her palms on his ribs under the shirt; nails over the lance scar; the blanket falls; her leg over his thigh; a stop, forehead to forehead, his hand "spread wide across the small of her back." Rule 9's "positions tracked" done at kissing distance. No scene tracks positions in sex, because none reaches it.

**Pacing has one shape, repeated:** long talk, a single physical turn, a cut. Zuberi and Ara talk for two thousand words and close on a palm kiss. Part 8 spends its length reading ears and tail and resolves on a pressed hand. The Blank Seal builds slowly (four hundred words of room and body before anyone touches), turns fast (kiss to leg over thigh in about ninety words), and stops on a word. The archive's intimacy is jo-ha-kyū cut at the kyū: the rush is where every scene ends. In the Blank Seal that is the point; elsewhere it is habit.

## 6. How desire shows

**Through the body as an instrument, read by someone else.** The archive's dominant and most distinctive technique. Yoko's ears, tail and nose report her state "a second before her face does" (card voice block); part 8 reads a flattened ear, a tail wrapped round her own waist, one coil loosening; part 14 puts the tail round his belt. Yūgiri reads Wren's fate threads, which never move around him. Frithia counts Mu-jin's pulse down from a hundred and twelve to eighty-eight. Mira knows the "touch that his Crystal did not interpret as input." Yorime "drew off his pressure with her hand" (card). The perceiving faculty is nearly always the woman's.

**Through maintenance.** Part 8's best phrase is "love expressed as maintenance": Yoko mending his gambeson while furious with him. He feeds her, moves her coat collar out of the rain while she sleeps, calls her a tool because "tools can't leave" (the old codex). Freda keeps Krothar's contracts and pay; Zuberi carved Asante a comb. Care for objects and routines is the house's native romance.

**Through dress and attention, when desire is engineered.** In *The House of Abscene* Kujo "rarely complimented beauty" and praised decisions and restraint; "The Queen began dressing differently for his visits"; a fireside exchange is "intimate without becoming overtly romantic." The archive's only seduction, and political.

**Through loss.** Edward Lambert's Kasumi lives in a report "read four hundred times" (`05_sodoku_the_fixed_end.md`); Sodoku's Fern in an unsent approval carried against his chest for eleven years. Grief is the archive's commonest register of love.

**Desire almost never speaks.** Only Yūgiri in thought ("I am going to have that") and Sodoku's "I love you" in part 8.

## 7. How refusal shows

- **Withdrawal into duty.** Yūgiri's "Stay in the room" is a request; Wren's answer is a refusal by action, back to the rider at the sixth bell. The scene shows it as a rope "going slack in her hands."
- **The body refusing before the voice.** Yoko's ears stay flat and her tail wrapped while an ear twitch accepts his words; the two signals disagree and then converge. The archive's best mechanism for Rule 9's "can change their mind", and it is beastkin-specific.
- **Physical refusal of unwanted touch.** Sonzai peels Selvara's arms off and shoves her clear (part 19; its notes summarise "stop hugging me"). Half comic, and the archive's only clear "no" to an advance. Sonzai is another creator's character, so the refusal is his creator's.
- **Refusal withheld.** Lyria chooses not to argue; Frithia refuses herself and will not tell him again.
- **Refusal overridden.** The Queen dislikes the marriage "on instinct," and Obsession Force and charm move her past it. The narration names the manipulation and never frames it as consent.

**Rule 9's desire and refusal line has no home on the record.** `table/npcs.yaml` carries a `refusal_line` on all ten rows, every one professional ("I'll not put my name on a thing I didn't bend myself"), and no desire field. The card voice block (about 28 cards) has nothing for desire or the bedroom. Fern's card comes nearest: her Never says includes "An apology for the love."

## 8. Consent handling

**The archive's consent word is "choosing", used consistently.** Sodoku in part 3: "the choosing was mutual." Fern's card: they "chose each other." Yoko's lore: "She walked through the lock on her own decision"; the old codex: "She chose him. She keeps choosing him." In *The Path of Sorrow*: "Then that's your mistake to make. Not mine to stop." Consent is written as a repeated decision by the woman, stated as fact, never negotiated in the moment.

**The flagship relationship begins inside a power imbalance, handled with care.** Yoko was labour stock in a slave pen; her liberator called her a tool; years later the law still files their son's beastkin inheritance "under a schedule written for property" (card: Yoko Mishiro). Her own line on the son is the archive's sharpest: "half of him is the thing that world puts in a pen." The cards insist the staying was hers; no scene shows the moment desire entered. The ground for a liberator and the woman he freed is laid and never walked on.

**One consent beat is explicit, and it is the best in the archive.** Frithia kisses the unconscious Mu-jin and says aloud, "You did not give it and I took it," then judges herself in italic. A small non-consensual act, owned by the actor, no narrator verdict: Rule 9's non-con principle at the scale of a kiss.

**Magic already has consent law, and it cuts against consent.** R29-1 (live): a Sacrament anchor is fixed "regardless of whether the anchor consents"; "the declaration, not consent, is the mechanism." FOW Part Fourteen: "Where Attraction recognizes, Obsession imprisons." Azelra Kaine's Domain makes "everyone present... act on their truest want" (card). The Kujo arc steers a queen into marriage by Obsession Force. A ready-made metaphysics of dubcon, with a stat cost and a findable counter (Attunement degrading against Coherence, FOW Part Fifteen). No intimate scene uses it.

**Non-con violence exists; non-con sex does not.** The drain room in `06_sodoku_the_shallow_seen_unburied.md` is written at full grimdark weight with a long aftermath. There is no sexual violence and no coerced sex anywhere, so "non-con and dubcon at the same specificity" has no precedent.

## 9. Aftermath

Aftermath is where the archive's intimacy is strongest, because its real subject is cost.

- **The Blank Seal** ends on her alone, naked on "another family's floor," cold through the boards, an italic thought ("carry them like a coin in a shoe"), and she bends for the blanket. Body, thought, object, action.
- **The Warrior Nurse**: she rises off her knees "which took longer than it had at twenty," throws out the cold basin, feeds the brazier exactly three lumps for the rationing, kneels to count his pulse again. Procedure as feeling, R71's CW12 before R71 existed.
- **Part 8** ends on a condition, "You will bring her back to us," which becomes the relationship's spine for ten more parts.
- **Yoko's four lines** after the Accord signing, which "the Bench has never been permitted to see" (`10_wotr_what_the_ground_was_owed.md`; Ledger L018), are R70-133's poem-after in embryo: a woman's private record as aftermath.

**What aftermath never does is reach the Ledger.** It records Yoko's four lines and nothing else of any relationship: no pregnancy, no scandal, no witness to a kiss. Yoko's son Rikudoku was born "in the Scourge years" and a second child is "coming" on her card, both offscreen.

## 10. How relationships develop across scenes

**Sodoku and Yoko** is the only relationship followed across many files, and it moves one way, from transaction to recognition: the broken lock; bread on the inn step; "tool" by the fire and her answer that she has made peace with the word but not the math; the coat collar; the igloo and the needle; the tail on the polar shelf; her hand on his arm in the narrows, "the one bond remaining at that magnitude" (`08_sodoku_recalescence.md`); nine years; the four lines; a cabin, a son, a second child. Her card logs it as metaphysics: Anima Spirare (the Wellspring of bond formation) goes from "developing" to "fully expressed". The relationship grows by gesture and by stat, never by a scene of sex and never by a wedding: the cards say "lovers, then married", and the marriage is nowhere on the page.

**Every other couple is one scene or one card line.** Frithia goes from a taken kiss to "wife" on Mu-jin's card with nothing between. Wren and Yūgiri have eleven months behind one scene and no card for her. Zuberi dies at Kharven betrothed (card: Ara Min Mahuo). Rengai leaves Lyria; Dougou breaks his promise to Yorime. The default arc: established offscreen, shown once, ended by death or duty.

**Courtship by culture is canon in three places.** The Moto: the queen-mother blesses before the king signs the approval (Fern's card; part 3). Northern law: a child born before a recognised union is named Ice, and the Warden takes the mother "by the jaw" to say so. The Zettari: the Cord of Becoming takes a bead for marriage, and a broken vow is "a cord that snapped" (`desktop/inventories/zettari.md`). The Yukari's "calling name for intimates" (R20-1, R20-5) is the only rule where intimacy changes language. No Inventory has a wedding, courtship custom, dowry, divorce, widow's custom, age of marriage, or a word for a lover.

**Love tokens are the real courtship grammar:** Kaede's blue ribbon on Kaito's sheath, the unsent approval against Sodoku's chest, Fern's mother's iron ring, the carved comb, Yoko's salted pork under the pillow.

## 11. What reads well

1. **The Blank Seal.** Full Rule 11 inventory of the partner; desire read through a faculty with a price (the threads, a headache behind the left orbit); a body that is "one long map of the border"; arousal stated plainly; an interruption that is character, not coyness; aftermath with an object and a lie she plans to tell. The right shape for a refusal that is not a "no".
2. **Frithia's taken kiss.** A consent problem named by the person who caused it, in her own culture's moral frame, with no narrator verdict.
3. **Love as maintenance.** The needle, the pulse counted again, three lumps on the brazier. Grimdark with warmth, and WOTR's own.
4. **Beastkin tells** as a desire and refusal engine: a body that cannot lie, so a changed mind can be shown in real time.
5. **Biochemistry in a lover's head**, the mechanism mandate applied to love.
6. **The arc from tool to wife**, carried patiently across a decade of story time, each step a gesture.

## 12. What repeats

- **The hand on the arm**: Ayame's on Muken's, Yoko's on Sodoku's, Mira's on Niran's, Yoko's on Emira's shoulder. A signature now at risk of being the only move.
- **The jaw held**: Zaehaerys takes Fern's jaw "like a bridle," Wren takes Yūgiri's, Rengai rests two fingers on Lyria's. Control and tenderness sharing a gesture is good; three jaws in a small corpus is a pattern.
- **The kiss on the hair** (Sodoku and Hild), parental, cited on four cards' Ties and in two scenes: the most-quoted touch in canon.
- **The woman reads the closed man**: by nose, threads, pulse, touch, his face, the set of his shoulders. The man is iron, asleep, at his desk. Nearly universal; the questionnaire may want to know whether it is the house dynamic or a habit.
- **The stop at the threshold**: every scene ends at or before the rush.
- **Number tics**: "eleven" (months, years, minutes), "four seconds", "four hundred times".
- **Love as grief**: more of the archive's love is mourned than lived.

## 13. What is missing

1. **An explicit scene.** No archived exemplar of Rule 9, R49-51 or R70-133/134; the only explicit prose in the repo is the Sae and Toma test samples.
2. **Per-culture intimate vocabulary and custom.** R70-134 keys technique to the POV's culture, but no Inventory has courtship, weddings, mores, bedroom words or sexual swears. The archive's actual intimate POVs are worse off: Yoko's Mishiro beastkin and Frithia's and Anyu Qilak's Far-Northern people have no Inventory at all.
3. **A desire line.** Neither roster nor voice block has a field for what a character wants in bed or where their line is.
4. **Dubcon, non-con, and compelled desire.** No precedent on the page. Obsession Force, Azelra's Domain, the engineered marriage and R29-1 are canon mechanisms with costs and counters (the house's "fair abilities with findable counters") waiting to be used or bounded.
5. **Pressure in bed.** Stage gaps make a lower practitioner sweat and retch; Yorime draws it off, Mira knows the weight of touch his Crystal ignores. Nothing says what a gap does between lovers or what suppression costs over a night.
6. **Non-human bodies and the floor.** The central romance is human and fox-spirit beastkin with a half-beastkin son; Selvara has extra arms, a tail and bone; Saórén is dragonkin. The floor bars bestiality. The archive implies, and never states, that sapient adult beastkin and other peoples are people and that Droval, wyverns and mounts are animals.
7. **Who counts as an adult.** No Inventory sets an age of majority or marriage. The Academy holds a chaste first crush between students of thirteen and fourteen (`hiromi_the_picnic.md`; cards give Hiromi 14, Xanelor 13), and children stand close to adult threads everywhere (Hild at twelve, Emira at nine, Miku at fourteen). The floor is absolute; a page definition of "adult" would make it mechanical.
8. **The LitRPG layer of bonds.** K2 is "full LitRPG, screens", and FOW gives bonds numbers: Harmonics Axis sustains the Spirit Bond; "Bonds formed under Benediction carry mechanical weight"; Anima Spirare grows by stage. No rule says whether a bond shows as a readout, when, or what intimacy does to a stat.
9. **Consequence on the Ledger.** No pregnancy, contraception, illness, scandal, debt or witness from any intimate act has been entered.
10. **Sex as trade.** A Victorian-imperial Draw Age run on "money before magic" has no brothel, bawd, courtesan, kept woman or bathhouse anywhere in the archive or the trades page.
11. **The PC in bed.** Natalie never thinks, speaks or acts for Sodoku. R71 K9 settles the fight version (world facts written, how he bears it his); there is no intimacy version, and live explicit play with the PC needs one. The same holds for every flagged creator's character: Sonzai and Selvara is a live romantic thread whose Sonzai side is never Natalie's.
12. **Configurations.** No same-sex couple on any page or Ties; Kaede Suyori and Saórén Vyrrakal are carried with "they". No polyamory or concubinage, though the Eressean court has a consort (Sandalphon).
13. **Weddings, divorces, widowhood.** Lovers become married between scenes; no wedding has been written in any culture.

**Continuity flags met on the way (recorded, not resolved).** Hild's mother is Fern on the cards and in *The True King of the North*, dying in winter in the altar room, unmarried because the approval never arrived; she is Freya in `06_sodoku_the_shallow_seen_unburied.md`, `08_sodoku_recalescence.md` and Ledger L003, dying over four days in a room with a drain, with Sodoku as "her husband". Yoko's card still reads "the bond with Temür", a stale Büri-register name, and Wren says "the true line of the Büri" in the Blank Seal. The PC's romantic history is not yet one story.

## 14. What this means for the questionnaire

The archive gives Isaac a house intimacy style he has already written and liked: gesture over speech, bodies read as instruments, love as maintenance, tokens carried, desire stopped by duty, aftermath as the heaviest beat. It gives him one scene, the Blank Seal, close to Rule 9's register and stopping short of sex. Everything beyond that is unwritten. The questions that would move the most are the ones precedent cannot answer: courtship and bedroom vocabulary by culture, a desire and refusal record per character, dubcon when the coercion is magical, what Pressure and non-human bodies do in bed, what goes on the Ledger, and how the PC is played in an explicit scene without Natalie acting for him.

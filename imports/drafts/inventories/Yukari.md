# THE STANDING INVENTORY: THE YUKARI

Crows of Fate: a bloodline that saw the threads before the world was ready to show them, paid for it in blindness and short lives, and learned to perch, watch and remember. The Ketsuen Inventory holds for the valley and the kingdom around it. This is the layer the house lays on top: the Telling, the names, the stone, and the eye.

**Food:** the kingdom's table, per the Ketsuen Inventory: broth, dumplings, lake fish. What the house adds is the forgetting of it. A Yukari reading deep does not eat, and the house has a sentence for that, said by whoever loves him: "You haven't eaten." The bowl is set down beside the tools, and whether the tools are warm tells the one who brought it what kind of morning it has been. The children are called in before the dumplings get cold.

**Greeting:** a nod, to a Zettari, and a nod back. Within the house, the name used is the greeting: a Yukari carries up to five names, and which one a person uses (childhood name, adult name, calling-name, true name) says exactly how close they stand. The calling-name is for intimates. The true name is spoken only among family, in Council, at a Proving, or before Senri's stone. From elder to younger, a hand on the head and nothing said.

**Oaths:** the name itself. The Yukari name is not inherited automatically; it is chosen at the Telling, by a bearer who has heard the full account of what the blood carries, what it costs and what it demands, and a bearer who refuses is not pressed. "Before the stone," for anything sworn in front of Senri's stone (d). **Insults:** "ledger-eyed," one who sees people as entries, the Moto error (d); "blind crow," one who holds a post his sight cannot carry; "loud," of any unnecessary motion, since unnecessary motion makes unnecessary noise (d); "unTold," a bearer who was never given the account and is paying for it the way Senri did (d).

**Time:** the Telling, when a child of age hears the account and chooses the name; the life-stage names, each one a date; the midwinter Thread-Reading from the flat stone above the gate; "before the breath," anything from the Long Silence, the Interstitium, the Age of Weight Without Breath (d); the blindness cycles, nineteen, twenty-six, thirty-one, which the house still recites though they have mostly stopped.

**The dead:** death is not an end but a return, to Adenyus and the ancestors in the psionic realm. The dead take a posthumous name, the deep name spoken only at the Telling or at death. The dead are laid facing east, as Senri died sitting against a stone facing the warmth he would not live to see (d).

**Objects:** Senri's stone, wrapped in oiled cloth in the deepest archive of the family, brought out for every child who takes the name; books, scrolls and records, which are the house's treasures; the inscription roll and the ink; the carver's tools, which the body grows toward whether the bearer chooses the craft or not; the marks of mastery, a line or sigil near the eyes for fate and a symbol on the forehead for Psionics, and to bear both is rare; the braided-leather cord; the flat stone above the gate, worn smooth.

**Exchange:** the house trades in what it sees and never sells it outright. Its coin is the kingdom's (see Ketsuen), and its wealth is its archive (d). What the Yukari give the Zettari is the reason a wall is failing; what they get back is respect, meaning they are left alone to perform a lawful function. What they refuse is containment: the Moto's offer of a place in their hierarchy, refused once, quietly and absolutely, and remembered by the Moto for two hundred thousand years.

**Proverbs, fixed wording:** "The crow perches. The crow watches. The crow remembers." "A sharpened mind is stronger than any blade." "A crow must oversee the unfortunate events so that others don't have to." "The seeing was enough." "Nothing in this world exists alone. Not even the boy who saw them first." "The name is a statement of intention, not a birthright." "They showed her the graves." "Ashura's precedent."

**Body:** posture arranged to take in rather than impose; long fingers; feet that sit flat and quiet; weight forward and head slightly tilted; hands clasped behind the back under stress; eyes closed for a deep read. Dark irises with a violet-black corona at the pupil's edge even at rest, brightening to silver-violet and rotating thread-patterns when active. Thread-marks, fine luminous lines under the skin of forearms, hands, temples and the base of the skull, branching like roots seen from below. Thread-shimmer in the hair in dense air. The Quiet Weight: when a Yukari speaks, the room leans in. The Threadburn, which the Third Epoch made survivable and did not remove.

**Senses:**
- *Smells:* cedar and limestone, clean; ink, once a Yukari is old enough to carry it; oiled cloth around old stone (d); broth steam at a desk where the tools have gone cold.
- *Sounds:* the Quiet Weight, a room going attentive; a hand set on a child's head; bare feet on stone (d); the pause between a Yukari's sentences; the midwinter reading carried down from the gate.
- *Colours:* silver threading through dark; violet-black at the pupil; crimson where the corona has taken the whole iris; black hair with shimmer; the grey of Senri's stone (d).
- *Textures:* a stone worn smooth by weather and use; oiled cloth (d); thread-marks felt as faint ridges under the fingertip (d); paper and scroll edges.
- *Tastes:* broth gone lukewarm because it was forgotten; dumplings; cold well water on the stone above the gate (d).

**Gone:**
- **Senri's last sight.** The first eye, which saw the warmth coming across a hundred and twenty thousand years and went blind six times. He died sitting against a stone facing east, with one eye dark, and the words he carved with a chisel he could barely hold are the only thing of his the house can touch.
- **The graves of the Long Silence.** Every bearer before the Soul Plane woke paid in blindness, degradation and a short life, and the line survived only because enough children inherited the eye to replace the parents it consumed. When Ashura asked how they had survived the seeing, they showed her the graves.
- **The quiet enclave.** The old Yukari Enclave was a valley and a gate before it was a capital. It is Senri's Gate now, with a Council, an Academy and ninety-five thousand people who depend on what it knows. (d)
- **Dhaerin.** The master at Vaeloris who taught the Crow to carve, dead of the rite that sealed Cozbi Mahuo.

**Folk beliefs** (held in the valley about the house, and in part by the house):
- **The eye takes a parent.** When a Yukari child's corona comes in, one of its parents will begin to weaken, so neighbours bring food to the father's door.
  - *Notes, not published:* True in the Long Silence, when the eye burned bearers out and children replaced parents. Since the Soul Plane woke, the blindness cycles have stopped in most bearers, and the Threadburn falls on the bearer, not the parent.
- **The Crow's Audit sees sin.** A Yukari looking at you sees what you have done wrong, so people in the valley look away from a Yukari's eyes.
  - *Notes, not published:* The eye sees threads, connections and costs: what a thing is joined to, and what it is costing whoever does it. It does not weigh right and wrong. The phrase is the Seikai's, and they fear transparency, not judgment.
- **A true name pulls the thread.** Whoever learns a Yukari's true name can pull on his fate, so the true name is never said outside family, Council, a Proving or the stone.
  - *Notes, not published:* The taboo is custom, and old. A name pulls nothing. The house keeps it anyway, because the custom is what makes the name mean anything.
- **Grief opens the eye.** A Yukari who has not grieved cannot really see, so the valley pities a child with a quiet life.
  - *Notes, not published:* Half-true, and discussed plainly in the house. Grief is a known catalyst for higher Psionic expression and is prepared for as a known risk. It does not open the eye itself, and it cannot be ordered up.

**Literacy:** Universal in the house and deeper than anyone else's: books, scrolls and records are its treasures, and every child who takes the name first reads Senri's stone. The unbroken thread-notation on the gate's lintel, older than Parunic, can be read by very few.

**Recurrence, two minimum per Yukari scene:** the hand on the head and nothing said; the name chosen at the Telling, and which of the names is used; Senri's stone; "the crow perches, the crow watches, the crow remembers"; eyes closed for a deep read.

Facing east at death bends Senri's own death into custom (d).

**Clash flagged, not resolved.** *The Yukari Bloodline* gives the active corona as silver-violet. Niran and Naori's cards and the scene give crimson eyes, the corona consuming the whole iris. Both are entered in Body and Senses as they stand; neither is corrected.

**Largest steps (d).** Most insults, "before the stone" as an oath, facing east, "before the breath", the Gone entry for the enclave, the exchange line about the archive.

## Breaks canon

### 1. The named crafts are still a field on 71 pages

- **Law:** `desktop/NATALIE.md:83`: "There are no named crafts; Drafts, runes, tags and chants are skills and trades (R84-18)."
- **Same law on the wiki:** `The Magic System/Power in Use/The Four Crafts.md:18`: "No such kinds exist."
- **Against:** `The Disciplines/Spellcraft/Scission.md:79`: "Craft · **Spellcraft**". `Cosmology & Metaphysics/The Making of the World/The Veil.md:43`: "**Craft** · Spellcraft, with the chain held internally from Refraction." `The Disciplines/Techniques/Crimson Dirge.md:49`: "Chantcraft is folded into Spellcraft". `The Disciplines/Spellcraft/Kami no Kobushi.md:7`: "nearest to Magicraft".
- **Scale:** 71 pages in The Disciplines and Cosmology carry the field; 81 pages in the audited folders name a retired craft.
- **Fix:** The Disciplines and The Veil change: the `Craft` field becomes the skill and how the working is held, and "Spellcraft", "Runecraft", "Magicraft" and "Chantcraft" go from the prose.

### 2. The Veil forbids what the law now allows, and no page carries the new law

- **Law:** `desktop/NATALIE.md:79`: "The planes can be entered by the living, rarely and at a high price (R84-33)."
- **Against:** `Cosmology & Metaphysics/The Making of the World/The Veil.md:27` and `:165`: "no entry into the Soul Plane or Plane of Fate, only the threshold, and the body is left behind every time."
- **Dead end:** no page in Magic System, Cosmology or Bestiary says a living person can go, or by what road (thin places, deep dungeons, high-Stage arts).
- **Fix:** The Veil changes: limit that clause to the crossing art, and add a section on the rare bodily road, with Dungeons pointing at it from the Deeps.

### 3. The Infinite Summoning page denies the ruling that gave it its meaning

- **Law:** `RULINGS.md:3839`: "some Summoners can summon Infinitely through fate itself". `desktop/NATALIE.md:75`: "Some summoners summon without limit through Fate itself". `Practice/Blessings and Curses.md:242`: "**Summon through Fate itself**, in the rare few who have that art, without the limit a contract has".
- **Against:** `The Disciplines/Schools and Bloodline Arts/The Spirit Summoning Arts/The Infinite Summoning Arts.md:36`: "It does not mean unlimited output." `:38`: "What is infinite is the **catalog**." The art is filed as Vocatia and Animatria, and Fate appears only as one move's name (`:14`, Fate-Chain Summoning).
- **Fix:** the Infinite Summoning page changes: add the Fate road (a summons drawn on the possibility of the thing, not a bound creature), with its price, and keep the catalog reading for ordinary drafters.

## Contradicts another page

### 4. Dungeons has no guardian, and says almost nobody clears a Heart

- **Law and pointers:** `desktop/NATALIE.md:73`: "with something guarding the heart". `The Magic System/First Steps/How Magic Works.md:134`: "with something at the heart guarding it. Kill that and the place is cleared". `The Magic System/Reference and Craft/The Lexicon of Magic — Master Terminology.md:311`: "Kill the guardian and clear the place". All three send the reader to Dungeons.
- **Against:** `The Bestiary/Dungeons.md:162`: "**Almost nobody clears a Heart.**" and "A \"cleared\" dungeon is usually one with its upper floors held and its Heart sealed off". `:39`: "Kill out the deep lines, seat a ring". The word "guardian" appears nowhere on the page, and the Heart row (`:65`) lists only A-Grade spawn.
- **Fix:** Dungeons changes: name what guards the Heart and say that killing it is the true clearing, keeping the ring as what people do when they cannot.

### 5. A Threat Tier is not a Grade, except where stones and hides are priced

- **X says:** FoW X:91: "**A Commission Tier is not a Grade.**" FoW X:93: "**The Leviathan and the Kraken show why a T cannot simply be a Grade.**"
- **Against:** `Gear/The Item Tiers.md:109`: "it assays at about the monster's Threat Tier" and "a T5 beast's plates hold what Aethersteel holds". `Alchemy/Monster Cores.md:65-75` gives each T a Stage served: T6 "VIII to X", T9 "XV and XVI".
- **Result:** the Lamp-drinker (`The Bestiary/The Corrupted and the Risen/Well-spawn.md:184`: T6, Hamlet-bane, Upper Workings, where spawn are D) yields a stone for Stage VIII to X and plates holding S to SS. The Leviathan (X: March-bane) yields a stone for Stage XV.
- **Fix:** Monster Cores and The Item Tiers change: key the stone and the part to the beast's own Grade and Level on the coil, and keep T as the price shorthand for most beasts.

### 6. The Great Spirit War is off the count and also dated three ways

- **Off the count:** `Ages/Errata to the Received Registers.md:51`: "None of it can be given a year on this count". `Ages/The Concordance of Ages.md:279`: "A surveyor who tells you what year such a field was fought has sold you something."
- **Dated:** `Powers/The Celestial Host.md:217`: "the Accord's own admittedly incomplete census, taken in the last peaceful year of the Era of Myths" and sightings "spread across seven centuries", which puts the Shattering mid-War at about Year 15 IC. `Orders/Sancta Lux.md:40`: "founded during the Great Spirit War" with `:84`: "eleven-century history", about −385 IC. `Races & Peoples/The Peoples/The Elven Peoples/The High Empire of Eresse.md:9`: "First Eon, Great Spirit War Era", before −900, where `Ages/The Concordance of Ages.md:65` has "No catastrophe". `Ages/The Four Ceilings.md:72` places a First Eon flood basalt in "the later phases of the Great Spirit War".
- **Fix:** the Errata stands; Celestial Host, Sancta Lux, Eresse and The Four Ceilings change their spans to uncounted figures or mark them as that body's own reckoning.

### 7. Hollow Thrones of "defunct Archons", when only one Archon was ever lost and her page is missing

- FoW IV:197: "the Hollow Thrones of defunct Archons" (plural; every Zenith depends on one, `Soul/The Sixteen Stages.md:361`).
- `Powers/The Fourteen Archons/The Fourteen Archons.md:18`: "the only Archon anyone has ever lost, and you will meet her name on the next shelf, in the entry for the one who ate her." No Titan or Archon page has that entry: no searched phrase for an eaten or fifteenth Archon appears outside this line.
- No Cosmology page or the Lexicon says what a Hollow Throne is.
- **Fix:** Cosmology changes: write the eater's entry with her name, and add a short account of Hollow Thrones that says how many there are, against which FoW IV keeps or drops its plural.

### 8. Measurewright is two different jobs and no page says so

- **Guild:** `Accord/The Guild Accord.md:58`: "the Accord's sworn assayer". `Ages/The Concordance of Ages.md:20`: "the Guild of Measurewrights". `Soul/Windows and the Sheet.md:28`: "one of the guild that keeps the world's tables and argues about them". NATALIE R83-11 has them arguing accounts of everything.
- **Church:** `Orders/Sancta Lux.md:126`: "**II · Measurewright** | Take a reading. **May not interpret one.**" `Factions, Bloodlines & Institutions/Factions at a Glance.md:80`: "Measurewright reads, Lector finds".
- **Fix:** Sancta Lux changes: one line saying its reading grade shares the Guild's word and is not the Guild's office, or a new name for the grade.

### 9. Waking opens a class on one page and not on the others

- `Practice/Blessings and Curses.md:63`: "The whole window opens, with Essence and a class".
- `Soul/Aether Classes.md:51`: "waking opens the rest of it without yet naming her", and first offer at Glory, as in FoW VII. `Soul/Windows and the Sheet.md:168` shows a waking block with no class line.
- `desktop/NATALIE.md:81` ("no Essence and no class until waking") reads loosely enough to feed the error.
- **Fix:** Blessings and Curses drops "and a class"; R84-10 and Part Seventeen govern.

### 10. Armor is settled by Ardency Penetration in the law and by output band on the gear pages

- `desktop/NATALIE.md:243`: "Ardency Penetration against worked armor (plain armor fights by its kind)".
- `Gear/The Item Tiers.md:88`: "what it stops: a strike at or below its Tier's Grade" read off the "Attack output band". `:92`: "A strike one Tier above the proof breaks it". FoW X's proof modifier says the same. None of the gear pages names Penetration, though FoW IV:55 says high Flux with low Penetration "hits like a padded wall".
- **Fix:** The Item Tiers and FoW X's proof modifier change: state that a strike against worked armor is read on the striker's Penetration Grade against the proof.

### 12. The Hataraki page keeps a dead roster that FoW V has already replaced

### 13. Vohrin is sold as a Core though it is not one of the Sixty, and the Titan register says it is

- **Ruling:** `RULINGS.md:886`: "Vohrin is one power at two levels, Titan and Wellspring". R27-1 keeps it outside the list, as `The Disciplines/Techniques/Cryost Ascendant.md:41` says: "outside the Sixty's enumerated list".
- **Against:** `Powers/The Fourteen Titans/The Fourteen Titans.md:42`: the Codex line "seats the working on one of the Sixty by name", yet `Powers/The Fourteen Titans/The Abyssal Depths — Prometheus, Zar'thuun, Vohrin.md:157` seats it on "Vohrin / Caloria / Thermodynamics". `The Bestiary/Dungeons.md:27` defines a Core as a site where one of the sixty pools, then `:93` has "A Vohrin one" and `:211` the Cold Larder as "Vohrin (Caloria, heat run backward)". `Practice/Blessings and Curses.md:47` has "a Vohrin gallery".
- **Dead end:** `Families/Caloria — Thermodynamics.md` never mentions Vohrin, so anyone following "Vohrin (Caloria)" finds nothing. Hunter's Breath:51 sits at "VI, Glory" although RULINGS:886 said the frost arts would likely rise in standing and cost, and `The Fourteen Titans.md:70` calls the Titan-Keyed gate "absolute".
- **Fix:** the Caloria page changes: add a plain note on what Vohrin is and how a cold site is filed, with the Sixty unchanged, and The Fourteen Titans:42 names Vohrin as its one exception.

### 14. What a spirit is differs from page to page

- `The Disciplines/Schools and Bloodline Arts/The Spirit Summoning Arts/The Spirit Summoning Arts.md:36`: "A **lawful extrusion of the summoner's own Soul Crystal**, rather than an external entity".
- Same page `:45`: Vocatia contacts "an existing presence". `The Bestiary/The Called and the Divine/Summoned and Bound/Summoned and Bound.md:24`: "A Native entity has one". `Soul/Windows and the Sheet.md:143`: "A bound spirit or beast has its own window", with its Level, terms, evolution and theft by contract (R84-28).
- Neither summoning page mentions the window or a stolen contract.
- **Fix:** Spirit Summoning changes: limit line 36 to Animatria constructs and carry the bound window and contract terms, with Summoned and Bound pointing to it.

## Dead ends

### 15. Waking Draft points at nothing in the Draft or gate pages

- `Soul/Windows and the Sheet.md:165`: "A Waking Draft is very dear and often kills or warps a child". `Geography & the Four Quarters/Value, Coin and Trade/What Things Cost.md:129` prices it at 60 to 120 gold.
- `Alchemy/The Common Drafts.md`, The Standing Index and Alchemetrica have no row for it. `Practice/The Gate Who May Learn Magic.md:17`: "Waking is taught", and `:48`, the Birth gate, sells "a teacher, a fee and seven years", with no Draft.
- **Fix:** The Gate adds the Draft as the rich family's road, and The Common Drafts adds its row.

### 16. Blessings cites Hataraki's method, and Hataraki never mentions blessings

- `Practice/Blessings and Curses.md:197`: "**Hataraki.** The Moto can do it, whoever gave it", with the three motions through the Works after it.
- The Hataraki page, Hataraki no Sho and `The Moto Bloodline.md` have no occurrence of "blessing" or "thumb".
- **Fix:** the Hataraki page changes: add the removal under the Works of Knowledge, Ruins and Purity, linking back.

### 17. Malakar has no page

- `Ages/The Concordance of Ages.md:253`: "Malakar splits the continents". `Powers/The Celestial Host.md:219`: "Malakar cracked the Lawbell." `Orders/Sōhai — The Whole Bow.md:21`: "Malakar's first blow". So one person spans the War, about 310 IC and about 740 IC.
- No file carries his name, and nothing says what he is, or how he stands to Malphas (`desktop/NATALIE.md:353`).
- This may be deliberate secrecy. If so, the pages should say he is unknown.
- **Fix:** Cosmology adds a short scholars' page, even one that only gives the arguments.

### 18. "Archetype" is defined nowhere else, and its own page gives two sources

- `Practice/The Standing and the Title — The Three Names.md:86`: "the Archetype under your Aether Class". `:125`: "**It is produced by Passive Traits.**"
- No other page in The Magic System uses the word, including Aether Classes and FoW VII.
- **Fix:** The Standing and the Title changes: drop "under your Aether Class" and let Passive Traits be the source.

### 19. Titan-Keyed floor and frost workings have no stated line

Covered in item 13; listed here only so the open question is not lost: do the frost arts rise in floor under RULINGS:886? No page answers. **Fix:** the frost technique pages state their floor as ruled, or the ruling is amended.

## Answers to the direct questions

- **Is "Core" used for different things without saying so?** Mostly no. `The Bestiary/Dungeons.md:29`, `Families/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings.md:162`, `Alchemy/Monster Cores.md:100` and `Practice/On Walking Into the Current:96` each say which sense they mean, and lowercase "core" holds for the stone. The leftovers are in item 23, and Vohrin's "Core" in item 13. The letter T has the same load (Threat Tier, material tier, Standing Index rank) with less warning.
- **Malakar's split at the end of Arc One:** agrees. Concordance VI, the Long Reading and Sōhai all carry it, with the 310 IC first blow disclosed.
- **Real calendar (R85-2):** the Sky page is converted and the sixty-count arithmetic holds (365 less 360 is five). A pattern sweep of the audited folders for named months and Archon days found one survivor: `The Disciplines/Schools and Bloodline Arts/The Ketsumyōgan — The Binding Fate Eye.md:7`, "in the red-leaf month", which should name a real month. The sweep would miss unusual wordings.
- **Who enforces what:** token fraud, seals and forfeits sit with Enforcement, courts with Arbitration and the market courts, oath and status with Sancta Lux, and the Crystal crime with every realm's own hangman. I found no clash beyond the Measurewright. Limina on a window is left to "an argument" on purpose.
- **Holding together:** title tables, merchant Rarity caps against Monster Cores, core and Draft prices against What Things Cost, stamp to Grade to rung, the two-Grade cap, Domain at Stage VIII, Release after a bought Stage, Trait levels, the commoner's window, and technique floors against Stage Grade caps. Wellspring and Family claims in The Disciplines also match the register, apart from Vohrin.

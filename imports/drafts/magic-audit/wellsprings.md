# Wellspring audit: the Sixty and the eight Families

Read-only audit of the live `wiki/`, 2026-11-05. Nothing was edited but this file. Staged pages under `imports/drafts/` were not treated as canon.

## The answer

**Yes, the Wellsprings themselves were largely fixed, and the rewrite stopped at the entries.** All sixty are present with the right names, Families and epithets, every one now opens on the idea and the transformation, and no entry still runs on the draw or on machine-age material. What did not get fixed: the Sub-Stat names and the Spirit lines on the Family pages, a seventh frost power in heavy use that has no entry, and several older pages around the register.

## Worst five

### F1. Vohrin is a working Wellspring with no entry anywhere (contradicts another page, and thin or missing)

Six techniques and four alchemical formulas run on Vohrin as a primary, and three of the techniques send the reader to Caloria entries that do not exist. The ruling exists (R27-1, `RULINGS.md:142-146`, `:886`), the register never took it in.

| Side | Where | Quote |
|---|---|---|
| The Sixty are closed | Part V:21 | "There are sixty, fixed." |
| Used as a Wellspring | T/Hunter's Breath.md:41 | "**Wellspring** · Vohrin (primary, Caloria)" |
| Also | T/Winter Rend.md:73; T/Vorynn Execution Strike.md:73; T/Maw of Crystalline Stasis.md:41; T/Bloodbind Surge.md:41; T/Cryost Ascendant.md:41 | Cryost alone admits "outside the Sixty's enumerated list" |

| Also | T/Maw of Crystalline Stasis.md:83; T/Vorynn Execution Strike.md:46; T/Winter Rend.md:46 | "the Caloria entries for Cinerion and Vohrin"; "the Vohrin, Cinerion and Tenebra Failures" |
| The Family claims the ground | Caloria:7 | "heat, and its absence, and the transfer between them" |

| Also | `wiki/The Bestiary/Dungeons.md:93`, `:211` | "A Vohrin one runs the other way"; hue given there is "blue ice", outside ember-orange |

No hue, sound, smell, tell or failure mode exists for it, so the law "An overreach fails in its Wellspring's own named failure mode" (`desktop/NATALIE.md:251`) has nothing to fire. The Hub's Discrepancies of Record (Hub:320-326) does not mention it.

**Fix:** give Vohrin a marked out-of-the-Sixty entry on the Caloria page (analogue, failure, signature row) and a Discrepancies line, then let the "Look up" lines stand.

## The rest, by severity

### Breaks canon

**F6. Eleven Wellsprings have no glyph in the Codex at all (thin or missing against canon).** Pyreveil, Cinerion, Manganthra, Aurevane, Nyxial, Tarturon, Ascensio, Abython, Penance, Redemption, Contrition appear in no Glyph Index row, primary or attested. `desktop/NATALIE.md:105` and `:239` require the glyph as boundary condition on any working, yet Pyreveil, the showcase Wellspring, has none. Family pages name no glyphs for anyone, so a reader cannot get from a Wellspring to its glyph without the Codex. **Fix:** a per-Wellspring glyph line on each entry, with an open ruling on the eleven.

### Contradicts another page

**F9. Coagula is used on outside matter, which the register gives to Coagulatio.** Materia:161 "where that Wellspring gathers a soul, this one gathers substrate" against T/Bastion Imperium.md:34 "Coagula moves the diffusion rate in the substrate underfoot" and "it gathers the ground". T/Coagula Dominium.md:27 adds "an inward-gathering current here projected outward onto the room for the first known time", and Bastion Imperium makes that "first" false; T/Edict Strike.md:27 "gathers the read into the blade as mass" does the same. **Fix:** key Bastion Imperium's ground work to Coagulatio, or note on the Coagula entry that the outward use exists.

**F10. Hypnather "cannot be forced", and two techniques force it.** Limina:75 "happens during genuine rest and cannot be forced" against T/Oblivion-Step.md:25 "the step forces it open for a half-second on command" and T/Hypnather Descent Rite.md:25 "forcing Hypnather against its grain". Both techniques quietly add "by ordinary means", which the entry lacks, and the entry has no effect on another person at all while Signatures:54 gives "the target's motion slows". **Fix:** add "by ordinary means" and the forced case with its cost to the entry.

**F11. Sublimare's Family in the Codex is unrecorded on the register.** Codex [Prc], Spell Index Pressure Veil and Alchemical row 52 all give "Cross-Family", and the Pressure Veil mechanism reads "Sublimare, a Cross-Family Wellspring" tracking "phase-transition behavior", against Vectoria:97 and FoW:981. C-055 (ruled, WAR-146) settled the formula rows only, and K5 (`RULINGS.md:2107`) settled distillation, so the Codex prose is stale twice over. The Hub's Discrepancies of Record (Hub:320-326) discusses Sublimare and never mentions this. **Fix:** add a Discrepancies line; the Codex stays as it is unless Isaac says otherwise.

**F12. Codex frost rows still read Cymorath, against the ruling.** Spell Index The Stillwater Rite, FrostThrone Dominion, Obsidian Crush; Alchemical Index Stasisline Needles, Stillwater Draught, Frostthrone Lance Balm, Obsidian Fang Poison; glyphs [FrTh], [ObsG] all give Primary Wellspring Cymorath, Fulguria. `rules/doc-cymorath-portfolio-ruling.yaml:37-41` "It is not a frost Wellspring". The wiki's Alchemical Index (lines 90, 100, 103, 127) is correct at Vohrin, so a `codex` lookup and the wiki now disagree. **Fix:** correct the workbook on Isaac's word, as was done for Monolithion (C-102).

**F15. Judicium is written as nondestructive, and one art kills with it.** Fulguria:33 "Nondestructive flaw detection" against T/Final Mercy.md:25 "the read stops reporting on the target's continuance and adjudicates it", which runs on a different physics (projective measurement) from the entry's spectroscopy. T/Bastion Imperium.md:34 "force under the threshold is read and found wanting before it registers" is the same stretch at a lower Stage. **Fix:** give the Judicium entry the overdriven case and its failure in one sentence.

**F17. Encounter Types and Environmental Coupling disagree inside the Hub.** Hub:192 "Vitalia (Water, Growth), Caloria (Light)" against Hub:297 "**Wild** (Growth, Healing) · **Medium** (Water)". Hub:192 "Water and Light are the primary Medium elements" against Hub:274, where light belongs to Fulguria, "Electricity, light, magnetism", and Hub:293, where Fulguria is Spirit only. Fluxia is "**Medium** primary" at Hub:298 and absent from the Medium row at Hub:192; Limina has a row at Hub:299 and appears nowhere in the Encounter table. **Fix:** rebuild the Encounter table's third column from the Coupling table, and settle where Light sits.

**F18. Sage Arts files Manganthra as volcanic.** Sch/The Sage Arts.md:40 "One attuned to Manganthra operates at full capacity near volcanic vents" and :58 "A Manganthra vent pulses with compressed catalytic force" against Caloria:125 "Heterogeneous catalysis". Vents are Solfatara (Caloria:107). **Fix:** swap the two lines to Solfatara.

**F19. Ketsumyōgan reads Monolithion as a sense.** Sch/The Ketsumyōgan — The Binding Fate Eye.md:91 "a range determined by Monolithion development" and :60 "the bearer's Monolithion registers Thread-Lock events" against Materia:53, where Monolithion is creep resistance and geological presence with no perception in it. Possibly a bloodline rule I could not find. **Fix:** key the range to a Gnosis or Dominion Sub-Stat, or state the bloodline reason on the page.

**F20. Harmonic Arts gives other sounds than the signatures.** Sch/The Harmonic Arts.md:90 "A Fixatio Trait produces a low, steady drone. A Transmutatio Trait crackles with storm-frequency" against Signatures:76 "a quiet click as bonds set" and Signatures:84 "a soft rotating hum, a shift in key". A Trait's hum can differ from a working's sound, and "storm" is a leftover of the old sorting. **Fix:** align the two examples.

## Checked and found sound

- **Lens and draw:** the Hub, Part V, Part VII (Typology), The Core Vocabulary, How Magic Works, the Lexicon and A Reader's Codex all carry the current law and agree with each other. Typology wording on all eight Family pages matches Part VII:100-114.
- **The bench:** the six named operations and four Work stages agree between Hub:85-94 and every entry. K5 and K6 have landed. The old masons' list (basalt, quartz, iron, gold, obsidian) agrees between Hub:98, the entries and The Spirit Rune Arts:85.
- **Named failures in NATALIE:** thermal debt for Pyreveil and quench cracking for Exuroth match Caloria:41 and :59.
- **Physics:** I checked each analogue, and the figures that can be checked (334 J/g, millijoule ignition, a millimeter a minute for smolder, the fourth oxygen, the square root at a crack tip, the cube-root blast law) are right.
- **Technique use:** with the exceptions above, techniques quote their Wellspring's analogue and inherit its named failure faithfully. This was the best-kept part of the rewrite.
- **Titans' Grounds:** "The Wellspring of Strain" and its thirteen fellows look like off-list Wellsprings, and `wiki/Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Fourteen Titans.md:42` handles them openly. Not a finding.
- **Other non-names:** Abythron, Orunthal, Luminaris, Mortaris and Verantha are places or beings, not misspellings. Crymorath appears once, quoting the ruling.
- **Rules and companion docs:** a Family scan of `rules/*.yaml`, `desktop/` and `.claude/skills/` found no wrong assignment (the one hit, `rules/pack-18-eighteen.yaml:311`, is a list and a false positive).

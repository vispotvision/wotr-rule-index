# New lore questionnaire: decisions (Isaac, 2026-10-20)

Ten batches, built on the 19 scenes archived after the 2026-10-18 reset
(`digest.md`). Exact answers are in `answers.json`. Below is what now governs, and what
each answer changes.

## The frame
- **The scenes govern.** The cards are rewritten to them, and the First-Eon versions of Naiser, Naevra and Gimbzo go. Muken and Yukazuri are rewritten too.
- **There was one war, forty years long, and it ended about a month before Chapter One.** Nothing is "twenty-three years on". Its last four years were the worst. In them:
  - the Seikai turned and the Soul Plane broke
  - Ashura was wounded
  - Naiser's father went into the seam
  - the Zettari struck the Yukari valleys
  - Shimane was fought
  
  Through the forty years, the north (Starks, Greymanes) held the front against the Zettari. Gimbzo's purge and the cellar night fall about twenty years in, and Muken was born about sixteen years ago. "Four years" stays wherever it means that last phase.
- **Ages.** Kiyome and Naevra are about thirty. "The last summer before the war" becomes "before Hiroe" (twenty years back, inside the war). Naevra is a year younger than Naiser.

## Muken, Yukazuri and Kasane
- **Gimbzo Kōkan Moto** was the Kōkan king, who purged his own line. He never sat the red chair: he ruled forty years from the saddle and left it to the dust as a threat. Muken is the last Kōkan, through his father. Shigure's Ch6 line is rewritten to match.
- **Kasane Byakuya** was Kiyome's elder sister and took the Yukari name at her Telling. She was King Gimbzo's sworn guard and loved him. She walked out of the cellar with the boy. "He took her" is the north's version.
- **Yukazuri is Gimbzo's daughter.** He found Kasane once more when Muken was three.
- **Kasane died of salt-throat on the day word of Shimane came down the galleries.** The grief is a month old when Chapter One opens.
- **Yusif Stark** was a big brother to Kasane, on the Ned Stark and Jon Snow model. He raised and shielded the children and never married her (retcon the marriage). He is Ulf's young uncle, about forty-five, a dozen years younger than Ulf (follow-up answer). He is dead: killed about three years ago by those hunting what he hid, and he never talked. Ulf knows how.
- **Tokenless.** A seal binds to a house name, and they had none, or didn't know theirs. Kasane's own token hummed. When Muken takes his name, the Kōkan emblem goes onto every seal he holds and he becomes the active leader of the Kōkan.
- **The eye.** What woke at the Foot is the common Moto Shingan. The gold crack is the Kōkan sign, the start of the Kamigan.
- **Who knew.** Shigure, Sumie and a few Tenrai knew a child escaped the cellar. Tetsugen guessed. Kiyome and the other lines did not know.
- **Shigure spared him** because she loved Gimbzo and saw him in the boy. She still tells Kagenori.

## The north and the Moto
- **House Stark.** Torvald was Ulf's brother and the leader, and he named Ulf leader before his execution. "Every one of my brothers" is rewritten.
- **The name clashes.** The kneeling Stark rider and the Lambert prisoner (both in The Verdict of Six) get new names. Ulf Stark and Wystan Greymane keep theirs.
- **Kharven** is the old name of the high north under the Moto rock. Kharven's Seat and Kharven's Foot already stand. Muken's realm takes the name when he takes the Seat.
- **The Moto lines hold land.** The wiki's "six lines landless" is rewritten.
- **The Keld.** A battle comes first, then Muken stops it and sits three days with the elders. The Keld chose him, which is why they are "never conquered". The Iron Fist is the man he has to win.

## The Yukari
- **Naiser's "cold vast want"** is the Seikai in him: the same hunger that broke the Soul Plane. "Bearer of Weight" means carrying it without using it.
- **The Mahuo** were destroyed by all three houses, each blaming another. Cozbi's account is the Mahuo's own memory.
- **The siblings.** Seradris is the youngest sister. Senri is a brother, and Senri's Gate is his seat.
- **The vision.** The figure in the snow is Muken, the White Wolf, and his banner becomes a white wolf.

## The Zettari belong to another player
Isaac does not control the Zettari, so their side stays open, and nothing here decides a Zettari choice:
- the marriage
- how Dougou killed Gimbzo
- where the Iron Bison came from
- Kushara's answers

Isaac's ruling is that the present Sovereign is **the real Shaka-Al**, the ancient first Path-born, and Dougou is his son. In Ch8 the outrider sees a Moto lady with riders, and no Zettari or cat appears until that player answers. The Zettari cards are flagged and left untouched.

## The world
- **The thing in the timber.** It is fallout from the broken Soul Plane. Creatures with spoiled essence are walking to a thin place to get back through, and the great thing waits there.
- **The southern springs.** A Seikai remnant is still feeding the poison.

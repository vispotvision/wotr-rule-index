## Contradictions found beyond the Reading Order's retcon list

**Inside the new lore**
- Naevra elder (Bearer of Weight: "when she was the elder and he was small") vs a year younger (Courtyard, Meeting).
- Muken's birth: Ch1 notes seven years after the Great War (16, war 23 years back); Naiser's Answer notes "born that year".
- One war or two: Ch1's Great Spirit War of Archons and Titans (23 years back) vs the four-year war that just ended; Naiser's Answer notes treat them as one.
- Zettari vs Yukari: Meeting/Courtyard have them fighting four years; Reading Order has only Yukari-Seikai and Zettari-Moto.
- Kōkan's end: Ch1 "went into the dust" during the Great War by Moto design; Naiser: Gimbzo killed Kōkan himself and his death "left the house with no Kōkan".
- Which parent is Kōkan: Ch5-7 mother Kōkan, Gimbzo the hunter ("isn't Tenrai blood and it isn't Gimbzo's. That eye is Kōkan"); Truth of Muken "Gimbzo Kōkan Moto", mother "Kasane Tenrai Yukari".
- Kasane taken (Ch5/7 "took her") vs loved ("the love, the patience"); died of salt-throat (Ch1) vs "died the day Gimbzo fell".
- Kasane "Tenrai" while her younger sister Kiyome is Byakuya.
- Yukazuri, 13, born after Kasane left Gimbzo (cellar night ~16 years back), so possibly Yusif's; Ulf's "what came of that night" is singular.
- Tokenless basis: Ch4 rests it on the mother's unreadable Kōkan mark; void if the mother is not Kōkan.
- Kōkan chair "a hundred years" of dust and the Byakuya oath "a thousand years after there was nobody left" vs a purge ~16-20 years ago and King Gimbzo alive till a month ago.
- Torvald "buried every one of my brothers" while Ulf (57, older, Lord of Hollenmark) lives; who heads House Stark.
- Shigure "six years" at Gimbzo's side vs seeing the cellar 16 years back.
- Yusif rode with Gimbzo "in the first years of the war" and quit after the cellar night (which war).
- Kiyome "never found her" (White Knot) vs Kasane told her to stay behind, Stark husband keeping them safe (Truth of Muken).
- Tetsugen "sixteen years we kept that name off every tongue" vs Kiyome learning in the last ten days.
- Small: twenty vs twenty-one fighting men; sergeant vs acting sergeant; "four chairs filled" with three seated (Ch7); seven vs six chairs; Tetsugen unarmed vs short sword across his thighs; Torvald dead "twelve days" vs "a month"; Iron Fist's day vs "a day and a half before"; Hayato's route (deer track vs Hiekawa cut) and roads closable or not; Foot built by "our grandfathers" vs Tenrai "last years before the war"; Kiyome "known Ulf since we were young"; summons the morning after the Foot before Shigure's ride could carry news.

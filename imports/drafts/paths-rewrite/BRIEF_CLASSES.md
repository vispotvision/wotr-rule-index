## The law in short

- **Class** is the named calling the soul offers at thresholds, first at Glory. It gives abilities and shapes growth, and it evolves when the lead ladder rises. It has no ladder of its own. Say "Class", not "Aether Class", for this.
- **Aether Class** is the Aether Shell, how strong a person's influence on the physical world is: Ø Dormant, I Muridic, II Harmonic, III Resonant, IV Luminous, V Radiant, VI Voidic, Ω Absolute. Fracture's account of it as the Shell's clarity stands.
- **Essence Class** is the Essence Core, influence on spiritual reality: Ø Silent, I Listener, II Answerer, III Confessor, IV Shepherd, V Ferryman, VI Hollow, Ω Absolute.
- **Thread Class** is the Attraction Layer, how much of Fate a soul can pull down and hold: Ø Unthreaded, I Touched, II Drawn, III Spinner, IV Knotwright, V Weaver, VI Severer, Ω Absolute. Most souls stay Ø for life. Its numeral gives η as two rungs below the same numeral elsewhere. It opens no earlier than Glory unless a bloodline or blessing gives a grip.
- VI on each ladder is lateral, an inverted way of holding that power.
- Each ladder sets η for workings through its own layer. All three are read from Glory. The Soul Crystal Tier stays as the reading of the whole soul.
- The lead Path's ladder climbs with Stage. A Walked second Path's ladder runs a rung behind until mastered. A Studied ladder stops at II, or at I for Thread.
- Windows can read `Aether IV · Essence II · Thread Ø`.

## What must hold on every page

1. **No Essence Typology.** A blow's character comes from Wellspring and Path.
2. **Three Paths**, Body, Spirit and Fate. Attraction is not a Path, though Attraction Force, Obsession Force and the Attraction Layer stay. Mastered, Walked and Studied; one, two or three Paths at their prices.
3. **Essence** is the soul's own substance, the Core the center of identity, and spending it is spending yourself. The three layers meet the world by Path.
4. **Realities.** Physical (Aegor) and Spiritual (Ashura) are the lower realities. Fate (Bara) is above them and is not a reality: it is every possibility, idea and concept, the law and construction of the world. Most if not all Archons are Sovereign on all three planes; Uurgath and Urion are unparalleled in Fate.
5. **Body** is the literal understanding and manipulation of physical reality and climbs Matla's ladder. **Spirit** is everything to do with spiritual reality. **Fate** fights and wages war, the Moto being the model, and it is extremely hard: never casual, never reasoned out, and Hataraki's Works exist because nobody grips it bare.
6. **No better Path.** Each has clear limits and beats each other. Nothing may imply that one is simply superior.
7. **Wellsprings** are lenses a soul bases its tempering on, with kinds as qualifiers (33 Wild, 15 Spirit, 6 Fate, 6 Medium). Any Path can use any Wellspring. Spirit Wellsprings give no fire.
8. **Classes.** The named Class is separate from the three ladders: Aether (physical influence), Essence (spiritual influence), Thread (Fate, steep). Each sets η for its layer.
9. **The rest of today's law:** the draw is retired and Aether is pulled from the environment; the real calendar and 24-hour day; Grade and Rarity on items; gear as a flex; windows as text blocks; the sheet; and American spelling.

## How to work

Read the whole page before changing it. Fix what is wrong and keep what is right, because these pages are the voice of the setting and stories are valuable. A story that only worked under the old system gets repaired so it survives, and is cut only if it cannot be saved. Do not smooth a page into a summary. Do not touch `sources/`, `rules/`, `RULINGS.md` or archived scenes. Do not edit outside your group.

Report under 200 words: the pages you read in full, the pages changed, anything you invented, any contradiction you could not settle, and any page you did not get to.

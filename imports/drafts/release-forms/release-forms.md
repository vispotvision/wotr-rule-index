# Release Forms: a design draft

Drafted 2026-10-14 in Claude Code at Isaac's request, to fill the system R78-6 owes:
"Many practitioners have named, staged release forms tied to the system, each with a
name, a look and a cost." Not canon until Isaac says so. Every figure below is either
quoted from the wiki's Magic System pages (cited) or marked **(design)**, meaning a
value set here for the first time.

Sources read: *Tier Grade, Bands & the Aether Shell* (Grade table, Overchannel Spikes,
Crystal Tiers and States, Reserve and Efficiency, Domains); *The Color of Essence*
(hue by Family, clarity by Stage); *The Sixteen Stages*; *The Trait System*; R8-24,
R50-09, R75-1 to R75-14, R76-1, R78-5 to R78-7.

---

## 1. What a release is

A practitioner's Primary Trait is a standing law held in the Essence Core (*The Trait
System* §II). In ordinary working the Crystal meters it: the Trait reaches the world
only as fast and as hard as the Shell can pass it safely. **A release is the Trait
let off that meter on purpose, in a shape the Crystal has learned to hold.**

Canon already has the unstructured version. An Overchannel spike forces output to
"two to five times the sustainable ceiling" and "deposits structural damage" every
time (*Tier Grade*, Overchannel Spikes). A release form is an overchannel the Crystal
has rehearsed until it holds a shape: same physics, a fixed form, a known price, and a
name. That is why it costs what it costs, and why it can be read and answered.

How it differs from the other three things a card can hold:

- **A technique** is something the practitioner makes with the Trait. A release changes
  the practitioner, and every technique fired inside it runs through the released Trait.
- **A signature** is one person's derived art (R75-1). A release is always one person's
  own, but it is the Trait itself, not an art built on it.
- **A vow** stakes something to lift one technique's output (R75-12). A release lifts
  the whole Trait for as long as it is held, and pays in reserve and Crystal, not in a
  stake. A vow may still be sworn inside a release (see §8).

The stratum it changes (the three-stratum stack, Thirteen §2): **Essence** first (the
Core runs unmetered), then **Aether** at the Second Release (the Trait claims ground),
then **Wellspring** at the Third (the current co-authors the law).

## 2. Who has them, and the gates

- **Who:** any practitioner with an Awakened Crystal and an armed Primary Trait. Class Ø
  bearers carry Traits but cannot release them; the Crystal must be open to arm one
  (*The Trait System* §III).
- **How many:** three released stages, then a passive one. Each must be earned; a gate
  only makes the next one possible.

| Release | Earliest Stage | Why there (canon) |
|---|---|---|
| **First Release** | IV · Flourishing | "Their power looks like them"; the Signature is readable at distance (*Sixteen Stages*, IV; *Color of Essence*, IV). The Trait has a settled shape to release into. |
| **Second Release** | VII · Refraction | "The Threshold at which Traits externalise", and the first Domain seed (*The Trait System* §VIII; *Tier Grade*, Domains). The released Trait claims ground. |
| **Third Release** | IX · Invocation | The Wellspring answers as co-author and a second hue enters the field (*Sixteen Stages*, IX; *Color of Essence*, IX). The released Trait becomes shared law. |
| *Standing release* | XI · Dissonance | "Traits become local physics"; Domain effects leak without upkeep (*Tier Grade*, Domains). The First Release no longer needs calling: it is how they are. |

**(design)** Most practitioners who reach Stage IV earn a First Release in time. Far
fewer earn a Second: they are the same population canon already describes stalled
below Level 200 (*The Trait System* §VIII). A Third is the mark of a figure.

## 3. What each release does

All three follow the same pattern: **the body changes, the aura flares, the sound
comes, and the Trait's output rises.** The look, the sound and the name belong to the
owner and are fixed on the card.

**The aura (R78-6, from *The Color of Essence*).** A release drives the aura to the
practitioner's full colour: the Family's hue (Caloria ember-orange, Fulguria
arc-blue, Vectoria gunmetal and the rest) at their Stage's clarity (smoke-brown at
Ascension, dual-toned at Refraction, silver-grey suffused at Transcendence). A Stage IV
release is a dark grey flare with the hue only suggested; a Stage VIII one lights the
room in its own colour. The colour is canon. The flare that makes it visible at once
is what the release adds.

| | First Release | Second Release | Third Release |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The look** | One fixed change to the body or the weapon: skin, eyes, hair, a weapon's shape, a cloak of the Family's physics. | The change spreads into the ground around them: a field where the light, air and temperature obey the Trait. Anyone inside feels the colour-temperature shift canon gives a Refraction Domain. | The field takes a second hue at its edges, the Wellspring's own, and the Wellspring's voice joins the sound. |
| **Output (design)** | The Trait's delivered output rises to the **top of its current Grade band** (canon puts the top of a band at roughly five times its floor, *Tier Grade*, Within-Tier resolution). | Inside the field, the Trait's output strikes **one full Grade higher**. Under the differential rule, that wins a direct exchange against an equal (*Tier Grade*). | Inside the field, the Trait's stated effect is **local law**: it strikes two Grades over the base, for that effect only. |
| **Range** | The body and what it touches. | A field the size of the room or yard it stands in **(design)**. At Stage VII it is a seed: "lasts minutes rather than hours… collapses if attention fractures" (*Tier Grade*, Domains). From Stage VIII it is stable. | The field holds other workings inside it and can contain another's Second Release (a Realm, canon IX to X). |
| **Sound** | Each release has its own sound, with anime flair (R78-6): a bellows drawn once, a struck bell, a crack of ice. | The sound continues as a standing note while the field holds. | The Wellspring's note sounds under the owner's. |

## 4. The cost

| | First Release | Second Release | Third Release |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reserve while held (design)** | Spends at the owner's full output rate (AU/s) for as long as it is held. | Twice the full rate, inside canon's two-to-five overchannel range. | Four times the full rate. |
| **Hard limit (canon)** | Every release ends on its own when the reserve falls to one tenth: below that is Essence Starvation, "reduced stat expression, Shell degradation, Trait flickering" (*Tier Grade*, Reserve). | Same. | Same. |
| **Crystal afterwards** | Spent and shaking; the pain the fight was shrugging off arrives (R78-5). No structural damage if released inside its limit. | The Crystal is **Overgrown** until a full night's rest: "power spills outward, emotions distort nearby space" (*Tier Grade*, Crystal States). | The Crystal is **Fractured** every time, healed by rest, alchemy or advancement. Done often, the fractures become permanent; "some fractures become architectural features. Most become liabilities" (*Tier Grade*, Overchannel). |
| **Held past the limit** | Forcing it past a tenth is a plain overchannel, with its fracture risk. | A Crystal Fracture Event. | The Trait itself may fail: one of *The Trait System*'s failure states, chosen on the card. |

## 5. How a release is learned, and how it goes on a card

**Learned by the three roads (R78-7).** A release is never taught; like the Trait, it
is survived into existence.

- **Crisis** is the commonest road to a First Release: the Trait breaks its meter in a
  fight the practitioner should have lost, and the Crystal keeps the shape.
- **Seclusion** is the commonest road to a Second: months working the Trait alone
  until it claims the ground around them.
- **Tribulation** is how every Third arrives: the Wellspring tests the claim before it
  agrees to co-author, and not everyone survives the test.

**(design)** A release prints on the Crystal once, the first time it holds, as a
signature does (R75-5), and that is a breakthrough-scale moment that earns a readout
under R78-6.

**On the card (R76-1).** Each release is one technique entry: the name in caps, in
English (R78-5), its kind in brackets (*First Release*, *Second Release*, *Third
Release*), then one plain paragraph holding what it looks and sounds like, what it
does, its cost, its limit and its counter. The shouted name is the release call: it
costs a beat (R8-24) and it is the tell.

## 6. The counter

Every release must be answerable by someone at its own Stage (R75-6), before, during
and after:

- **Before.** The call costs a beat (R8-24), and the aura flares and the sound comes
  before the output rises. A fighter who reads the tell can strike into that beat, or
  get out of reach.
- **During.** The clock is the counter. A release spends reserve fast and ends at one
  tenth, so outlasting it is a real plan: keep distance, make them chase, and fight on
  ground that raises their cost (canon: "terrain is not flavour, it is a modifier on η";
  a Caloria practitioner in saturated cold pays more). A Second Release at Stage VII is
  a seed that "collapses if attention fractures": a wound, a shout, a name that matters
  to them. And a field has an edge: step out of it.
- **After.** The aftermath is a window. A First Release leaves them spent; a Second
  leaves the Crystal Overgrown, spilling and readable; a Third leaves it Fractured.

This is how a lower-Stage fighter wins across one Stage with a read (R78-6): survive
the release, then strike in the aftermath.

## 7. Worked example: a Stage VII Caloria practitioner

A generic Expert at Refraction, harmonised to **Pyraeon** (Caloria), with a heat
Primary Trait. Caloria's canon hue is "deep ember-orange, iron at cherry-red heat",
and at Refraction the Essence is "dual-toned… base grey and Family color occupying the
same field without mixing". Reserve and output are given as fractions of their own,
since no card is attached.

> **KILN SKIN** *(First Release)*. They say "Kiln Skin" and their skin goes the dull
> cherry-orange of iron at forging heat, from the hands up over the forearms to the
> collarbones, and the air around them shimmers like the air over a forge bed. There
> is a sound like a bellows drawn once, hard. Every blow they land carries forge heat
> into the wound, and their heat workings strike at the top of their Grade. It burns
> through their reserve at their full output rate and ends on its own at a tenth of
> it; afterwards their palms are blistered and they cannot draw heat for an hour. The
> counter is in the beat of the call, which can be struck into, in cold and wet ground,
> which makes every second cost more, and in the blistered hands afterwards, which
> cannot hold a weapon properly.

> **SMELTER'S YARD** *(Second Release)*. They say "Smelter's Yard" and the heat leaves
> their skin and fills the ground around them, about the size of a courtyard: the air
> goes forge-hot and dry, the light turns orange, and everyone inside it feels the
> colour change as a pressure behind the eyes. The bellows sound becomes a low,
> steady roar for as long as it holds. Inside the yard their heat workings strike one
> full Grade higher. It costs twice their full output rate, lasts minutes at their
> Stage, and collapses if their attention breaks; afterwards the Crystal is Overgrown
> until they sleep, and heat leaks off them with their temper. The counter is to break
> their focus with a wound, a shout or a name that matters, to step out past the edge
> of the yard, and to strike while the Overgrown Crystal is spilling and easy to read.

## 8. Rules this touches

- **R8-24, R50-09, R78-5** (the release call). The shouted English name is the call; it
  costs a beat. A release form's call is the same mechanic. **R71-29** ("an art may be
  released as often as the fight gives a beat") is about the call, not release forms;
  a form may be called again only after it has ended and the reserve allows.
- **R75-1, R75-2, R75-5, R75-6, R76-1** (signatures, the card before first fire,
  printing on the Crystal, the counter's reach, the entry format). A release follows
  all five.
- **R75-12, R75-14** (vows). **(design)** A vow may be sworn inside a release and lifts
  within the released band; a great vow may never be stacked on a Third Release.
- **R70-91, R78-7** (breakthroughs). A release's first hold is a breakthrough-scale
  moment and lands as R70-91 says.
- **R9-3** (superseded by R78-6) and ***The Color of Essence*, Part Five**: "Essence
  does not glow." R78-6 now says an aura may glow; the page needs a note (on the
  consistency list).
- **R71-41, R70-69** (Pressure). A release raises the practitioner's Pressure in the
  room by the same flare; the ladder is unchanged.

## 9. Open questions for Isaac

1. Is the First Release gate right at Stage IV (Flourishing), or should it open at
   Stage III, when the Essence first carries the practitioner's own tint?
2. Three released stages plus a standing one, or should some people have more?
3. Should the stages carry a folk name in each culture (the way Stages do), or stay
   "First, Second, Third Release"?
4. Do bloodline faculties (the Moto read, for one) get release forms of their own, or
   only Traits?
5. The reserve multipliers (one, two, four times the full output rate) are set here
   for the first time. Keep, or tie them to each card?

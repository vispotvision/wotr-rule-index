# DRAFT: Sense Banks for the Standing Inventories

**Status: draft for Isaac. Not canon.** R51-31: each culture's Standing Inventory gains a senses entry (smells, sounds, colours, textures, tastes), and scenes draw from it first. R51-32: technical smell words (ozone, sulphur, ammonia) are free for any POV.

**Coverage.** Every culture with a Standing Inventory: Kharven (inline in `desktop/NATALIE.md`), Moto, the Accord and Concord, the Dawi, Eresse, the Keth-Gorrum (Undaar-Keth and the Expanse), and Korvaeth (with the Sunroot Regency and the Vessel road). The Zettari, like the other four new sheets (Stannvaard, Nalūn, Ketsuen, Yukari), carry their senses entry inside their own Inventory draft at `imports/drafts/inventories/`, so they have no bank here.

**Sourcing.** Every item hangs on something the Inventory (or, for Kharven and Moto, the Muster and *What the Sky Does Not Ask*) already names. Unmarked items are that material given its sense. Items marked **(d)** go one step further: the physical fact of an attested thing (what a flint smells like when struck, what iron does to bare skin at thirty below). Nothing here adds an object, a food or a custom the Inventory lacks.

**Two hard bans to keep in mind when drawing from these** (R51-10): the phrases "the smell of ozone" and "the coppery tang of blood" fail the checker at first use. Ozone may be named; it may not be named that way. Blood here is iron and salt.

## Kharven

**Smells**
1. Woodsmoke off the outer town's stacks, thick when a season's wood goes on the fire in a day.
2. Seal fat rendering in the blubber lamp: fish-oil and hot tallow.
3. The skin: sour fermented milk and yeast when the sealskin is unstoppered.
4. Wet felt and horse, and old grease on harness.
5. Men who have slept eleven nights in mail: sweat gone sour, and iron.
6. Bone broth on the hearth: marrow, salt, scorched bone.
7. Smoked hide from the hide-coats, tannin and old smoke.
8. Air at thirty below that smells of nothing, and the inside of the nose freezing on the inhale.
9. Ammonia off the horse lines and the dung heaps. (d)
10. The death-house: cold stone and nothing, and at thaw a sweetness nobody names. (d)

**Sounds**
1. Six hundred men breathing in the cold: a tide coming in under a pier.
2. An axe splitting rounds for the woodpile, and the split halves knocking onto the stack.
3. Snow squeaking under boots in the deep cold. (d)
4. Ice cracking in frozen ruts as weight goes over.
5. Frozen ground ringing under hooves, and coming up through a horse into a rider's knees.
6. The blubber lamp spitting.
7. A forearm grip: two mittened arms meeting with a slap of hide.
8. The Waiting: a room full of people keeping silent over the dead.
9. Horses blowing and stamping on the lines.
10. Frost-stiff hide creaking at the elbow of a coat.

**Colours**
1. Breath-plume white, standing in front of a face.
2. Smoke grey over every stack in the outer town.
3. Seal-fat yellow in the lamp bowl, and the soot-black above it.
4. Hide-coat tan darkened at the cuffs with grease.
5. The pale ends of split wood on a full stack.
6. Frostbite: a waxy white on the cheek, going purple later. (d)
7. Blood gone black on a hide sleeve.
8. Aurora green over the Seat at night.
9. Snow gone blue in the hour after the sun.
10. The grey of a Greymane banner, and the grey of the rock the Seat stands on.

**Textures**
1. Frost standing in a nap on a hide.
2. A hide-coat gone stiff as board with cold.
3. Iron at thirty below, which takes skin off a bare palm. (d)
4. Frozen ruts underfoot, hard as cast metal.
5. Split grain on a fresh round, and the splinters it leaves.
6. The night-stone: smooth, and warm after the fire is out.
7. Windmeat, hard as bark, softening in the mouth.
8. Stonecurd like a pebble.
9. Seal fat greasing the fingers and never quite washing off.
10. A belt worn soft and shiny where the hand goes to it.

**Tastes**
1. The skin: sour, fizzing, faintly drunk.
2. Windmeat: salt and game, chewed a long time.
3. Bone broth with the fat still floating.
4. Seal fat: oily and fishy, coating the mouth.
5. Stonecurd: sour and chalky.
6. Root from the cache, frozen sweet. (d)
7. Snowmelt tasting of the pot and of smoke.
8. Salt, which is money.
9. Rind-water, the honest northern name for thin broth.
10. Blood from a split lip in the cold: iron and salt.

## Moto

The Kharven bank holds for the clans at the Seat. These are the layer the house lays on top.

**Smells**
1. Lacquer on plate: resinous and faintly sour. (d)
2. Oiled hair, pulled back and cut level at the nape.
3. Rice on the high table, steamed and starchy, and rare.
4. Ink from a register kept in the family's own hand: soot and glue. (d)
5. Iron from the field coat's seams and the mail under the plate.
6. A cold room at the hinge of the day, before the bread.
7. Wet fur on a wide collar coming in from snow.
8. Blood from the eye after a verdict, which is iron.
9. Grease and old leather where the gorget sits under the jaw.
10. Frost coming off Tōga's hide: nothing at all, and the nose freezing. (d)

**Sounds**
1. The small final sound of the guard settling into the scabbard mouth, which in Kharven meant the verdict was complete.
2. Beads moving on their cord under a thumb.
3. A knee going down onto a stone floor.
4. Fourteen inclinations: cloth moving fourteen times and no voice.
5. The scale's beam settling as the ration is weighed.
6. A knife cutting a scratch into the doorpost.
7. One word said into a quiet yard.
8. A silence a Moto leaves for another man to fill.
9. Frost cracking in ruts ahead of the Droval.
10. The yard going quiet in a rolling wave from where he entered.

**Colours**
1. Lacquered red so dark it reads black until the sun finds it.
2. White hair, in all seven lines.
3. The metallic birth-sheen at brow, clavicle and wrist.
4. Jade green and iron grey, worn smooth.
5. Black prayer-beads the size of fists across a shoulder.
6. Ring-lines in the iris, and the faint flare at the outer ring.
7. A dark field coat under a wide fur collar.
8. Red running from the eye after a verdict.
9. Ink black on a page no Concord clerk can read.
10. Rice white against the clans' grey pork and black onions.

**Textures**
1. Beads worn smooth by a dead man's hand and then a living one.
2. Lacquer: hard, glassy, cold.
3. Fourteen scratches in a doorpost under a thumb.
4. Iron-thread seams in a heavy coat.
5. A head under the palm: hair, and the warm skull.
6. The brass pan of a true balance. (d)
7. Frost in a nap over a hide whose warmth runs inward.
8. Hair tied back tight for the crown.
9. A mail skirt rebound at the hip, the new lacing stiffer than the old.
10. Register paper, heavy, taking ink slowly. (d)

**Tastes**
1. Rice, which tastes of a corridor that can still be reached.
2. Rye heel from the clans' table.
3. Salt pork boiled grey.
4. Onions fried black in the fat.
5. Rind-water.
6. The host's food, eaten three days before any business.
7. Blood at the lip after a verdict: iron and salt.
8. Cold air in the throat at the Bow.

## The Accord and the Concord

**Smells**
1. Hot sealing wax, and the scorch where it drips.
2. Iron-gall ink: sour, a little like vinegar. (d)
3. Ozone near a live main or an array under load. (d)
4. Grounding tea, bitter, in a house that has sat too long near an Echo.
5. The crust of an assize loaf; the sour dark of hand-ground bread.
6. Pulse porridge on the fire.
7. Ale at the fixed price.
8. Brass polish on a gauge-housing.
9. Damp iron at the standpipe.
10. Lamp oil in the maintenance hours, three to five in the morning.

**Sounds**
1. The hum under a district.
2. The moment the hum stops.
3. The small hammer on a weight: a solid weight rings.
4. The dull note of a cased weight.
5. The seal press coming down.
6. The wire: nine words and then nothing.
7. A stopcock turned against its rust.
8. The tundish dripping.
9. A clerk at a gate: "Solutum et transitum. In registro relatum."
10. A nib scratching an entry.
11. Titles before names at a formal door.

**Colours**
1. Green, blue, gold and obsidian wax on the board in the hall.
2. Silver of the rank token shown at a door.
3. Brass of the gauge-housing, and the slate behind it.
4. Iron black of a capped standpipe.
5. White array-ground bread against dark hand-ground.
6. Copper, which is the price of being alive.
7. Ink on the fingers and wax on the thumb.
8. The Board's seal on the meter by the door.

**Textures**
1. Wax cooling under a thumb.
2. The split tally, notched, halves that fit only each other.
3. Raised letters of PROB EST under a fingertip.
4. The bronze reach in the wall at hand height, worn by hands.
5. The salt bar, gritty and dense.
6. A letter of credit, stiff paper, folded twice. (d)
7. The capped standpipe, waist-high, cold.
8. A harness that cost two seasons' surplus, still stiff.
9. The strike-board's scored face. (d)

**Tastes**
1. The assize loaf, white or dark.
2. Pulse porridge.
3. Ale that sworn tasters have passed.
4. The salt bar, shaved into a pot.
5. Grounding tea, bitter.
6. Feast-day meat, and how rarely.
7. Standpipe water with iron in it. (d)
8. Ink from a licked thumb: gall and bitterness. (d)

## The Dawi

**Smells**
1. Sour barley beer in the lower Ring levels.
2. Yeast and wet stave from a working cask. (d)
3. Cask-Oath Pitch: resin and tar.
4. Forge smoke, with sulphur off the coal. (d)
5. Quench steam off hot iron.
6. Stone dust under a burin: dry, like struck flint. (d)
7. Salted and dried stores in a cellar.
8. Sulphur off a fresh ore face. (d)
9. Lamp smoke in a deep gallery.
10. Singed beard.

**Sounds**
1. The hammer on the anvil.
2. A burin cutting a rune.
3. A name said in the strong grade: the doubled consonant before the word finishes.
4. The same name in the weak grade, heard across a chamber.
5. Mathematical Cadence: formal speech in fixed metre.
6. A brew working in the cask. (d)
7. The shift changing. (d)
8. The copula "on" closing a line.
9. Borin's hammer, used as a walking stick, on stone.
10. A consonant hardening as a speaker's temper rises.

**Colours**
1. The forge-flush across nose and cheekbones.
2. Soul-Runes on skin, an elder's life readable on his arms.
3. Etched beard-rings.
4. Doubled strokes on the roll of the dead, readable from across the chamber.
5. Cloudy amber of the beer. (d)
6. Forge-light on wet rock. (d)
7. Dark ironwood on a hammer handle.
8. The bright line of a fresh ore seam. (d)

**Textures**
1. The ironwood handle: dense, smooth, near oily.
2. The edges of a cut rune under a fingertip.
3. The angle-set's hard edges. (d)
4. A beard-ring, cold on the knuckle.
5. Soul-Rune ridges on an elder's forearm.
6. Pitch, tacky and unwilling to cure outside its hold.
7. A sealed stone casket carried home.
8. The stance of a Petralon-anchored Dawi: shoving him is shoving a wall.
9. Salt-dried meat.

**Tastes**
1. Sour barley beer, which every visitor calls an acquired taste.
2. Ollut, the aged brew: deeper, with an edge. (d)
3. Bread that cost more than it should.
4. Salted stores.
5. Dried stores, chewed on shift.
6. The shift-meal eaten when the guild shift says. (d)
7. Hold water off the rock, cold and mineral. (d)
8. Forge smoke at the back of the throat.

## Eresse

**Smells**
1. Granary bread.
2. Bark tea, sharp and green-bitter. (d)
3. Sap syrup boiling down.
4. Game, dressed after the Asking.
5. Boiled root.
6. Mineral water set out blood-warm.
7. Fresh-cut ironwood, cut under restriction. (d)
8. Cool earth in council chambers built partly below grade. (d)
9. Ink for the Songscript rail. (d)
10. Gold, which smells of nothing, and the hand that held it. (d)

**Sounds**
1. "Elaruren," on a falling tone.
2. A name answered in "-ama," and the room hearing it.
3. The light tapped r.
4. A Concord speaker flattening the tone.
5. Two jurists reading a deposition in alternation.
6. The Asking spoken over game.
7. A voice rising for the unfinished, falling for the settled.
8. Song, the only use of volume.
9. The pen of a copyist recopying the rite calendar.
10. The ceremonial bow's string.

**Colours**
1. Sacred Gold.
2. Ironwood.
3. Sap syrup amber.
4. Granary brown.
5. The half-light of a chamber below grade.
6. The ink line of the rail above the text.
7. The eight turnings' own light: First Green, Long Sun, Deep Dark, Returning Light.
8. Clear water in a cup.

**Textures**
1. Gold, heavy and soft, warming fast in the hand.
2. The ironwood bow's grip.
3. Water at blood heat on the tongue.
4. A light-boned hand that breaks cleanly.
5. Bark.
6. Sap syrup on the fingers.
7. A page of the calendar, recopied without variation. (d)
8. Stone steps down into a council chamber. (d)

**Tastes**
1. Granary bread.
2. Game with the Asking spoken over it.
3. Boiled root.
4. Sap syrup.
5. Bark tea.
6. Mineral water, blood-warm, flat and faintly salt. (d)
7. Bark tea steeped past its window, and too bitter. (d)
8. Sap scorched in the pan. (d)

## The Keth-Gorrum (Undaar-Keth and the Expanse)

(M) Mandate, (B) Bugbear, (O) Ironblood Orc, as in the Inventory.

**Smells**
1. Sulphur off the Shelf Islands.
2. Thurr-kep cooking: charcoal and honey.
3. Kelp drying on the reef farms.
4. Shelf fish.
5. Toasted volcanic-ash grain. (d)
6. Taro and breadfruit on the fire.
7. Hot iron and lamp oil, and something mineral and dry that comes off their skin.
8. Hot basalt after a day of sun. (d)
9. Boreal timber and furs. (M)
10. Brine on volcanic-glass hulls. (d)

**Sounds**
1. The sub-bass of Gorr-Vaross, felt in the ribs before it is understood. (B, O)
2. A vowel held for emphasis.
3. A voice going quieter and slower in a rage.
4. The Reading: fifteen minutes of decree in every square. (M)
5. A chisel taking superseded law off the Decree Stone. (M)
6. The basalt gavel, unstruck.
7. A horn on a two-count, twice.
8. Boots landing together on rubble.
9. A closed fist set on a flat surface: I am staying. (B)
10. A table going over.
11. Surf on the reef. (d)

**Colours**
1. Straight gold or bronze Legion Etchings on burnt-ochre or deep red skin.
2. Polished brass or iron eyes, the pupils gone rectangular.
3. Obsidian black.
4. Sulphur yellow.
5. Basalt grey.
6. Volcanic-glass hulls, green-black. (d)
7. Kelp brown-green. (d)
8. Iron rings in a topknot. (O)

**Textures**
1. The grooves of the board and the post, and their depth.
2. Four cuts and a crossbar on a shoulder plate.
3. The carried stone. (B)
4. Iron rings in a topknot, each a weight earned. (O)
5. A volcanic-glass edge.
6. Ash grain, gritty. (d)
7. The basalt gavel.
8. Bugbear hair lifting on the scalp. (B)
9. Open palms turned forward.

**Tastes**
1. Thurr-kep: charcoal mixed with honey.
2. Volcanic-ash grain bread. (d)
3. Taro.
4. Breadfruit.
5. Kelp.
6. Shelf fish.
7. The state's grain. (M)
8. Nalūn salt bars. (M)
9. Water off a sulphur spring. (d)

## Korvaeth (with the Sunroot Regency and the Vessel Road)

(S) Sunroot Regency, (V) the Vessel road, as in the Inventory.

**Smells**
1. Black drown alder: brine and tannin in the panelling. (d)
2. Wax, and warm wax with a thumb in it.
3. Salt, the cargo.
4. Winter grain dust up the Vessel road.
5. Orchard fruit in store. (d)
6. Ember Flint struck: burnt stone, a mild flame. (d)
7. Ink on a nib.
8. A cold yard at the fourth bell.
9. Damp of the deep road. (d)

**Sounds**
1. Bells, from the fourth bell of a cold yard to the ninth hour of a night attack.
2. "In term?" and "In term."
3. "Witnessed," said once.
4. The nib on a ruled leaf.
5. Weight shifting foot to foot, then stopping on the word that lands.
6. The counted silence: four for yes, nine for no. (S)
7. An Onceglass charge going to gravel.
8. A vault key.
9. Coins of the witness-fee counted out. (d)

**Colours**
1. Skin the grey-violet of a four-day bruise.
2. Hair the flat unlit white, bound in one black cord.
3. Drown alder, salt-black on the face.
4. Pale grain under a black face: "northern edge."
5. Gold for anything sworn.
6. Ember Flint's small flame.
7. The ruled lines on a leaf.
8. Sacred Gold at a premium. (S)

**Textures**
1. The callus on a Reckoner's forefinger.
2. A thumbprint pressed into wax.
3. The coil: nine passes, or four passes and a pin.
4. Drown alder, dense and heavy.
5. Salt grit.
6. Onceglass gravel after the casting.
7. Roughgold, panned and grainy.
8. Hands shown open on entering.

**Tastes**
1. Winter grain.
2. Orchard fruit.
3. Salt.
4. Fruit held through a winter. (V, d)
5. Grain porridge off the Vessel road. (d)
6. Cold water from the deep road. (d)
7. Wax on the lip after a seal is bitten. (d)
8. Iron at the back of the throat on a night attack. (d)

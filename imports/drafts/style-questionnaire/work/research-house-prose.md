## What "The War in The North.md" is

It is a ~49,800-word export of a **shared multi-writer roleplay thread**, the Kharven war, with no headings. By quote style it falls into about eighteen alternating posts:

- **Natalie's turns** (straight quotes, 2,000 to 4,900 words each). The first is the archived `scenes/the_war_in_the_north_i_the_kharven_seat.md`. Several are **not in the archive**: Bram's yard ("Bram smelled it before he saw it. Cut sap, sharp and vegetal..."), a turn opening "*Thruum.*", Yūgiri's room ("The room smelled of another family's life.").
- **Another player's posts** (curly quotes, 1,400 to 3,800 words): Zuberi/Dawn Guard, Ara, Asante, Sonzai, Ma'Kovu. Plainer and warmer, more dialogue, more stock phrasing ("That earned a faint smile.").
- **A third hand**: Emira on the ice ("The cold didn't bother Emira like it did any other woman, it's harshness felt almost welcoming..."), with run-ons and repeated "softly".

It still carries stale Büri-register names ("Muken Büri's north wall survey", "Temür", "Lord Buri"), so it predates the Moto sweep. It is the only place where Natalie's prose stands next to other writers' prose at the same table: her turns run two to three times longer and are far denser in sense detail.

## 1. What the prose does now

### 1.1 Sentence shapes and lengths

| Group | Mean words / sentence | Median | ≤6 words | ≥30 words | Dialogue share |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Recent (09-27/28) | 13 | 9 | 36% | 9% | ~22% |
| Kharven set pieces (04–09 Sodoku, the Muster) | 21 | 14 | 28% | 30% | ~7% |
| Darius / Aurelian / Mu-jin numbered | 23 | 17 | 26% | 33% | very low |
| True King of the North parts | 18.5 | 9 | 41% | 22% | ~13% |
| Old Cozbi/Niran material | 23 | 15 | 30% | 34% | ~7% |

Two rhythms: the **Kharven/Darius set pieces** run long "and" chains that slam shut on a short sentence; the **recent** prose is shorter, more broken up, and has more dialogue. Typical shapes:

- **The "and" chain, then the stop.** This is parataxis (clauses laid side by side with "and", none ranked above another): "The meters on forty stairs stopped their needles where they stood, and in every room the lamp-hum and the kettle-hum and the hum in the iron bedsteads quit at once, and the quarter lay down in the dark it had lain in before there was any Board..." Then: "They were lamps and then they were posts." (`the_leveller.md` §I).
- **Verbless sense-fragments as an opening:** "Cold coming up off the road through the soles. Grease and old leather where the gorget sat under his jaw." (`04_sodoku...`).
- **The two-beat verdict:** "It worked. It was not enough." (`21_darius_the_fist_of_god.md`).
- **One-word paragraphs at the hinge:** "Meat." (`the_leveller.md` §IV).

The seven Kharven set pieces contain no question mark at all. Exclamation marks are rare: 16 in about 65,000 words, nearly all of them Hiromi's.

### 1.2 Paragraphing

Prose paragraphs average about 40 words. Description comes in blocks: a 120 to 250-word paragraph for each first introduction, broken by single-line action paragraphs. Long written pieces are divided by Roman-numeral headers (`the_leveller.md`: "I. THE CUT" to "VI. THE BOOK"). Roleplay turns have no headers and use `---` for time jumps.

### 1.3 Narrator distance and interiority

Psychic distance (how close the narrating voice sits to a character's head) is **close third person, one point of view per scene**, named in the header or notes ("*POV: Penn Lister. Explaining voices: the POV's own read, and Haldor Ivor.*"). The narration slides into **free indirect discourse** (the character's thoughts in the narrator's sentences, with no "he thought"): "So that was that. He had eaten someone else's Essence, and it had been taken back, all of it and only it" (`geturo_gypsum.md`). Three habits recur:

- **General-present "laws of the world" inside past narration**, the house's main way of passing knowledge to the reader without addressing him: "Children raised on a main sleep badly off it, any mother would tell you" (`the_leveller.md`); "You do not block a teep." (`21_darius...`).
- **Italic direct thought.** Heavy and long in the Kharven and Darius set pieces (4 to 6 italic spans per 1,000 words): "*A man who thinks in walls does not think of the door being opened from inside. That is the whole of the fault and it is mine...*" Light and short in recent prose (about 1.5 per 1,000): "*She'll wake. A quarter hour of cold and she'll wake...*". NPC italic thoughts only in roleplay turns (R48-40).
- **Feeling recorded as absence, as an act, or as filing.** "and there was no pleasure anywhere in it" (`04_sodoku...`). "he puts the word away in the part of himself where he keeps glyph chains that do not quite close" (`mu_jin_the_card.md`). A named emotion in plain narration is rare and small: Mu-jin "is warmed by it".

### 1.4 Imagery sources and simile habits

Comparisons come from **trades, households and working bodies**, almost never from nature-as-beauty, myth or scripture:

- "in the way a pan lid chatters on a low boil" (`mu_jin_the_readers_oath.md`)
- "the way other men say the name of a horse that lost them money" (`mu_jin_the_card.md`)
- "something domed and rich sat in its own light the way a lamp sits in a window it has paid for" (`the_leveller.md`)
- Colour by household object: "grey as a yard-brush", "the grey of weak tea", "slag gone the colour of burnt sugar" (`rovhen_the_sort.md`).

The dominant form is **"the way [someone] does [something]"**: 73 instances in the recent 56,000 words (one per 770 words), against 0.7 "like a" per 1,000. The Moto register made it a rule ("comparisons come as 'the way a man does' some ordinary thing"), and it has become everyone's default. Metaphor is mostly mechanism stated flat: "A Tide with a spine." "They've the loyalty of water." "So the hate could stay. It was load-bearing."

### 1.5 How description is placed

- **Smell first, sight last.** About 15% of all archived scenes open on a smell or a verbless list of senses, and nearly all the big set pieces do: "Seal oil, boiled willow, and the sour animal warmth of a room that has had a sick man in it for four days." (`wotr_the_warrior_nurse.md`); "Ammonia first, because that was what a room became after a man had been in it upright for eleven days" (`commencement_of_the_curia...`); "Bram smelled it before he saw it." People are introduced by nose too: "The ganger reached him nose first: hot wax from the seal press... Bay rum laid over the lard in his hair, and peppermint over all of it, there so that nobody would say gin." (`the_leveller.md`).
- **The first-introduction block** (table rule 11) is one paragraph in a fixed order: hair by comparison, face, body area by area, clothes with wear, marks, hands last ("His hands are the most notable thing about him.", `malphas_on_the_line...`). Ralf Sumner, Haldor Ivor, Agnes Tull, the draw-inspector, Mrs Dunnock and Frithia all get one. They are vivid but read like ID cards.
- **Lived-in rooms** (table rule 12): one off-plot sound per room. "Through the wall, a commercial traveller in the next private room is telling a long story about a dog" (`mu_jin_the_card.md`); a pigeon settling on the gauge-housing, "affronted." (`xanelor_dallae.md`).
- **One unexplained thing per turn** (table rule 8), placed as a still image near the end: water beading on the latches of Velder's case in a warm room; "The fifth one does not move at all."
- **Landscape is thin.** Recent scenes are interiors, and the outdoors comes through a window: "the sky the colour of unworked tin. A single standpipe stands in a field, its gauge-housing dark." (`malphas_on_the_line...`).

### 1.6 Dialogue habits

- Tags are nearly all **"said"/"says"**: 197 of 205 counted tags in recent prose, with "called", "asked" and the rest a handful. Many lines carry no tag and use an action beat instead.
- **Voices are split by grammar.** The Moto use no contractions (the register rule). Hiromi is loud, uses contractions and italic emphasis: "Hey! *Thanks!*... I'm *very* flippant. Extremely flippant." Madam Ha speaks in short dry sentences: "Kwon... I don't argue with the Grand Mage." Malphas talks in lecture periods. Northern English dialect is spelled out for the culvert men: "Tha's a flusher's lad. Two tanks. Pipe between. Which way does it run." "There's nowt in thee for them to go to, lad." (`the_leveller.md`).
- **Stichomythia** (rapid one-line exchanges where each line turns the last): "I was *resting* on the stairs." / "You were listening on the stairs." / "I was resting *audibly* on the stairs." (`mu_jin_the_readers_oath.md`).
- **Latin legal tags as texture:** "Sine sigillo"; "In registro relatum." (`the_leveller.md`).
- **Characters explain the mechanism**, always as a negotiation or an argument: "Full to empty." / "Till." / "Till they're level." (`the_leveller.md`).

### 1.7 Humour

Dry and earned by work, often a threat in the same breath, delivered by working men and old women:

- "A physician will tell you what anything is for six copper, Lister, and tell it you in Latin so you know you've had your money's worth." / "Then drown quick, Lister. It's a form either way." (`the_leveller.md`)
- "You're always right, Mu-jin. It's the least useful thing about you." (`mu_jin_the_card.md`)
- "Snake," he said. And after a while: "Good snake." (`geturo_constrictor.md`)
- "I have never known a man so angry at flax." (`alftian-codex-vol1.md`)

Hiromi is the only openly comic voice, close to the loud shonen hero (shonen: Japanese boys' action manga), but the narration never cuts to a reaction shot.

### 1.8 How combat and magic read

- **Real mechanism in plain words, in the POV's idiom.** The Constrictor's capstan: "A rope turned round a post three times will hold a ship against a tide in the hands of one boy on the dock, and the rope does not have to be strong." Its heartbeat ratchet: "It was a ratchet. It only turned one way." (`geturo_constrictor.md`). Near-field resonant transfer as plumbing: "In tune and touching the full one runs into the empty one" (`the_leveller.md`).
- **Anatomy named exactly:** "The common peroneal runs down the outside there under very little cover" (`21_darius...`).
- **Cost in the body every time:** "Blood came from both nostrils now, not one."
- **Technique names called aloud in a set order**, house then medium then law: "Infinite Summoning Arts." / "Essence Draft." / "...Constrictor." The older Cozbi material does it grander: "Conjunction. Pneuma."
- **Pressure as a body cost:** "nine Stages of distance folded down small and held there so two children could stand in the same air as him and not be sick in it." (`xanelor_dallae.md`).
- **Readings through instruments and witnesses:** a gauge lettered "THIN and AMBIENT and ACTIVE", read as "Two marks off the stop... And a hair." (`the_leveller.md`); scouts' cards, a bookmaker's chalk, wardsmiths' slates.

**Where the numbers are today (the LitRPG question).** WOTR's numbers live in the **author notes**: "Stage I Murmuring, Initiate, unclassed, Level 8. Reserve 3,332 EU; Starvation below 333 EU; η about 0.30." (`the_leveller.md`, Stat Ledger). Recent narration names a stat once: "Dexterity was one of the few things about him nobody had ever bothered to argue with." (`geturo_the_squeeze.md`). The nearest thing to a LitRPG interface (game-like levels, stats and status screens shown on the page) is older, in *The Path of Sorrow* (09-11), where Sodoku's eye, the Kamigan, runs analytics in italic thought: "*Why is the Kamigan running full combat analytics on a D-tier enforcement officer and three F-tier subordinates... Why am I standing in the rain with 291,000 EU in reserve*".

The law has moved further than the prose. Pack Five banned "Status screens, panels, HUD-style rendering" and "Character-sheet vocabulary surfacing in prose" (R5-F-LIGHT_NOVEL_NOT_KEPT). The 09-26 Vocabulary Law **R51-16** superseded that whole row: "All older limits on stat names, Sub-Stat names, Guild words and sheet vocabulary in narration fall; they are free in narration." R14-4-DIAGNOSTIC_CHANNEL is also superseded, yet R14-4-SUBSTAT_DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY still reads live, and `desktop/NATALIE.md` still says "names only in a mouth, an instrument, a document, or a private count". So stat *names* are already legal in narration. No live rule explicitly permits or bans a status screen. The prose has used neither freedom, and the questionnaire should start from that, not from "currently banned".

### 1.9 How scenes open and end

**Openings** over 181 scenes: about 63% start on an action or flat statement ("The key went into the housing and turned twice."), 15% on smell or a sense list, 9% on the body, 7% on weather or light, 6% on dialogue. Aphoristic openers are a minor mode: "Pain has a schedule." (`the_war_in_the_north_v...`).

**Endings** follow R49-26 closely, often on a quiet object image: "The card lies on the white cloth between Hiromi's plate and his father's hand." (`mu_jin_the_card.md`); "He set her down against the cold wall." (`the_leveller.md`). Roleplay turns end on an NPC line that leaves Isaac a decision (Madam Ha's claim on Geturo).

### 1.10 In-world documents

- **The Alftian Codex** (`alftian-codex-vol1.md`): a first-person memoir in short, confessional sentences that correct themselves ("I told the others nothing, which I called tact. It was pride."), dated by money and travel ("We paid the tanner four silver a month."), with antique chapter titles and a title in the house tongue ("I have called this book Gameung-nok."). In manner it is the most Eastern prose in the corpus: humble, ancestral, full of master-and-pupil injuries.
- **Kami no Kobushi** (`scenes/Kami-No-Kobushi.md`, archived 09-11): a martial manual with a fixed template per style (Work, Type, Primary Wellsprings, Summary, Core Principle, In Practice, Philosophical Tension) and Japanese names ("Hōkai no Kobushi"). It is older than the current bans and still has em dashes and "does not create destruction — it finds destruction".
- **Field-book epigraphs** (Lambert's "five kinds of work", `rovhen_the_sort.md`) and telegrams in capitals.

### 1.11 Two hands in the recent archive

The Mu-jin turns, *The Leveller* and the Geturo fights are concrete, varied and funny. The **Night Register run** (`shioris_findings...`, `wystans_briefing...`, `malphas_on_the_line...`) is abstract and leans on banned constructions: "She pauses, and the pause is not hesitation. It is..."; "Not regret. Recognition."; "Not alarm. Assessment."; "The word Zettaris lands."; "the particular alertness of a young person". Its notes record a `verify_scene` PASS, so the checker misses this "not X, Y" family when it comes as fragments.

## 3. What already reads Eastern, and what reads Western

### Eastern (Japanese, Chinese, Korean traditions, including web fiction)

1. **The system skeleton itself**, which Pack Five calls "a light-novel and xianxia inheritance" (xianxia is Chinese immortal-cultivation fantasy, built on realms to climb): sixteen Stages, Bands, Grades, EU costs. In prose it shows as a realm breakthrough told plainly: "I had crossed into Glory, where, as the primers put it, the self burns bright and is noticed, and after the second lamp I learned to keep my temper for the lamp's sake first." (`alftian-codex-vol1.md`).
2. **The academy tournament arc**: Aetherion Academy, ranked Class X and Class S, a "Paragon of Dominion" title, rival scouts writing odds, arena matches. This is the shape of Japanese school-battle anime and Korean academy web novels, with their beats: the **hidden-strength reveal** ("It was the first time in his life he had said those three words where anyone outside the family could hear."; "His father had not known.") and the **master's debrief after the match** ("Hiromi. You were cocky." `xanelor_dallae.md`).
3. **Called techniques** in shonen fashion, a three-part name: "Infinite Summoning Arts... Essence Draft... Constrictor."
4. **The expert suppressing his aura** (a cultivation-novel staple), as a body cost: "nine Stages of distance folded down small".
5. **Master and disciple bonds and debts.** Madam Ha claiming a student ("That makes him mine as well."); the Senior Alchemist Doryun taking his pupil's work under his own name (Codex), a classic sect injury. Address forms are translated ("Teacher", "Madam Ha", "Master Kwon"); no suffixes such as -nim or -sama.
6. **Korean family register.** Family name first ("Kwon Mu-jin", "Ha Seol-ok"). House custom stated as law: the house "settles a person's life quietly, on that person's behalf, without asking" (Codex). The Korean book title *Gameung-nok*.
7. **The Moto court register**, the strongest Eastern prose *method* in the corpus: silence as an act ("Sodoku waited."), bounded verdicts, courtesy that carries threat, no contractions, the fourteen-fold Bow, a dead father's thumb-flat on the beads. It reads like Japanese courtly and samurai drama, and it practises **ma** (Japanese: the meaningful pause) without the name: "A man arriving with news will give it in his own order if you leave the silence alone, and the order he gives it in is half the message."
8. **Fixed-wording proverbs per culture**: "Ask the hands, not the man" (Zettari, `mu_jin_the_old_colleague.md`); the Kharven set.
9. **The martial manual as document** (Kami no Kobushi), a cousin of the wuxia manual (wuxia: Chinese martial-arts adventure).
10. **The in-head analytic system** of *The Path of Sorrow* (risk matrix, tier letters, EU counts), the nearest thing to a Korean "system" novel (a web-novel genre where a game-like interface speaks to the hero).

### Absent from the Eastern side

- **Kishōtenketsu** (a four-part story shape: set-up, development, twist, reconciliation, often without conflict). Scenes run on Western conflict-and-turn. Table rule 8's unexplained detail works like the twist (*ten*) but never gets the reconciliation (*ketsu*).
- **Seasonal words** (Japanese *kigo*: a word that places a moment in a season and its mood). The nearest is Kharven's "when the grass comes".
- **Mono no aware** (the gentle sadness of things passing) as an aim of description. Grief is present but always tied to cost.
- **Embedded verse**: poems, or a chapter opened by a couplet, as in Chinese novels and Japanese tales.
- **"Face"** as a social economy; sect and clan politics vocabulary; honorific suffixes.
- **Onomatopoeia** as texture. Apart from one "*Thruum.*" in the root file, sound words are English verbs.
- **Light-novel interiority**: the chatty near-first-person aside and the straight-man retort (Japanese *tsukkomi*). Pack Five killed the manhwa-energy register (manhwa: Korean comics; here their loud reaction-shot style) and the isekai aside (isekai: "another world" fiction; here, wry narrator comments on the world's rules). Nothing has replaced either.
- **Status windows, level-up notices, skill-acquisition beats.**

### Western

- **POV discipline** in the Martin manner: one head per scene, ignorance as the register ("He could see thirty feet.", Bram).
- **Bureaucratic cruelty and gallows humour** in the Abercrombie manner: the leveller's book entering "Nobody on this gang has gone live" as a mercy that is also a crime.
- **Victorian realist texture**: the railway hotel, bombazine, the wire "paid for reply", Manufacturing Chemists, Assize and parish, Chancery Hand, Latin legal tags. Dickens's London is nearer than Tolkien's Shire.
- **American plain-style parataxis**, the unranked "and... and..." chain of Hemingway and McCarthy.
- **Forensic and medical realism** (ATLS haemorrhage classes; a journal citation in the notes for the python's heartbeat), and **Sherlockian deduction** (reading a stranger's life from small marks): Malphas reading the ink on Mu-jin's hand.
- **Northern English dialect** ("afore", "nowt", "tha") and **workshop craft**: endings on an image, the iceberg rule (show a tenth, keep the rest under the surface).

**Net position.** Eastern **structure and content** (system, academy arc, techniques, master and pupil, house custom, Moto ceremony) wear Western **sentences and descriptive method**: close third person, plain style, realist inventory, physics and anatomy as the explaining voice. Pacing, description, interiority and story shape are almost wholly Western. The exceptions are the Moto register's pause and bounded verdict, and the Codex's humble first person.

## 4. Recurring tics and sameness a style change could fix

Counts are for the recent 56k words unless marked.

1. **"the way [someone] does X"** as the default comparison: 73 instances, one per 770 words. It began as a Moto rule and is now universal.
2. **"eleven"**: about 1,580 occurrences in the archive's 886k words (text and notes), one every 560 words; Lambert's and Darius's top word in `reports/voice_fingerprints_2026-09-24.md`.
3. **Hand business**: "thumb" 47 times (on wax, scars, bezels, beads); "a hand's/finger's breadth/width" 20 times.
4. **The first-introduction block** in the same order every time. Repeated props: three NPCs in two days carry great key-rings ("the size of a horse's collar", "the size of a dinner plate", "forty-odd keys"); stained or burned fingers recur on four men.
5. **The off-time voice as "lived-in" texture**: a class "getting it wrong in the same place every time" (`rovhen_the_sort.md`), "one of them a half-beat behind the rest" (`wotr_the_warrior_nurse.md`), the carver's boy whose "every third stroke landed late" (Codex), and the same item again in the Ketsuen sense bank.
6. **Repeated scene endings.** Shiori's and Wystan's scenes both end on a wardsmith who writes a number, crosses it out and writes it again. Hiromi's "the true things / the real things always come out quieter" appears in two consecutive turns.
7. **Negation as the main way to show feeling**: "did not / does not" 3.3 per 1,000 recent, 9.7 in the Kharven set pieces. Feeling-as-absence began as a Moto rule and now governs Lorn, Bram, Penn, Mu-jin and Frithia alike.
8. **"The whole of it"** leaks from the Moto register into Greymane POV: "That was the whole of the argument and it was not an argument at all" (`the_war_in_the_north_v...`).
9. **Sense-ladder openings** (smell, then sound, then sight) are prescribed in two registers and used in most set pieces, so the big scenes all enter the same way. Sentence-initial "Then" runs at 1.3 per 1,000.
10. **Older-hand tics still in the archive**: "the particular / the specific [noun] of a man who" (0.9 per 1,000 across the archive, nearly gone now); "Not X. Y." fragments; em dashes in Kami no Kobushi and the Cozbi files.
11. **One emotional key, indoors.** Grief, cost, restraint and dry wit are everywhere; wonder, joy, beauty, appetite and romance are rare. Settings are interiors and institutions.

## 5. What the narration registers decide per POV

A register is a POV's own vocabulary, sentence shape, attention and blind spots, laid over the culture baseline. The drafts:

**Moto** (`imports/drafts/moto-narration-register.md`, built from *What the Sky Does Not Ask* and the Muster). Ten rules: senses first and cold before everything; the body moves before the reason; parataxis with no "because"; everything counted flat ("The read cost him eleven seconds."); silence as an act; judgement bounded by "the whole of it"; feeling as absence; full-form courteous speech, no contractions; proverbs in a dead speaker's voice; the world weighed as arrangements and "the smaller party". Its vocabulary leans to *count, weight, read, entered, fault, cost, correct*. It keeps out named feelings, questions and exclamations, and allows comparisons only from working life. Sample: the Bow before bread ("A bow deepened for one of the fourteen is a petition with its mouth shut."). Open for Isaac: whether no-contractions and no-questions are Moto traits, how antique to go (R51-01), and whether it binds non-Moto POVs in Moto houses.

**Lorn Stark** (`lorn-stark-narration-register.md`). A close register in a steward's and sword-teacher's idiom. He reads a man by his kit, with the inference in a "which meant" clause that might be wrong. He weighs reports instead of believing them, knows the number and does not repeat it, admits the word he cannot find ("Lorn had been trying to find the word for it since the boat"), and puts feeling into an act ("so he put his hand out instead"). Italic thought allowed, running long and bitter. Narration never says what Sodoku thinks.

**Bram Greymane** (`bram-greymane-narration-register.md`). A close register in a line commander's idiom. The senses arrive as a ladder ("Sound. Pressure. Smell. Sight, last..."). Ignorance is the register: he knows nothing of his right flank. The count tells the story (rotations, not feelings). A death is entered, not mourned ("dead in the time it takes to fill a bucket"). Generalisations only as a soldier's law ("That was the part nobody ever tells you."). No italic thought owed.

**What they share.** All three use counts, flat figures, feeling as act or absence, sense-ladders, and "and" chains. They are three tunings of one northern stoic voice. None of them sets a description subject range (what this POV would describe at length that another would not), a comparison source beyond "working life", or how the POV takes in beauty, pleasure or the sacred. Those are the levers a Western–Eastern blend would add.

**The sense banks** (`imports/drafts/sense-banks.md`) give up to ten smells, sounds, colours, textures and tastes each for Kharven, Moto, the Accord/Concord, the Dawi, Eresse, the Keth-Gorrum and Korvaeth; the other five cultures carry theirs inside their Inventories. The Moto bank already reaches past function: "Rice, which tastes of a corridor that can still be reached." Since R51-31 has scenes draw from the banks first, and the banks are sorted by sense rather than by subject, they feed the sense-ladder sameness in point 9.

## 6. Where the dials sit today (for the questionnaire writer)

- **Prose model:** Western close-third realism in plain-style rhythm; Eastern material is content, not method.
- **Numbers:** in author notes and instruments. Stat names are already legal in narration (R51-16) but almost unused; no status screen has ever appeared; *The Path of Sorrow*'s Kamigan is the only in-head readout precedent; NATALIE.md lags behind R51-16.
- **Description:** dense and wide over bodies, rooms, documents, machinery, medicine and work. Thin on nature, taste, markets, music, full ritual, the wealthy, and wonder.
- **Interiority and voice:** close and restrained, feeling as act or absence. Voices are well split in dialogue but narrow in narration, one stoic register across POVs.
- **Structure:** Western scene shape (turn, cost, image ending); no kishōtenketsu, no verse. The "not X, Y" fragments in the Night Register run slip past `verify_scene`. No explicit-scene sample exists to quote.

# War of the Realms: Newcomer Test

Thanks for doing this. War of the Realms is a fantasy world with a big wiki behind it, and we want to know whether someone who has never seen it can find their way in. You are not being tested. The wiki is. If you get stuck, that is exactly what we need to hear about.

**What you need:** the wiki link, about 45 minutes, and somewhere to jot notes (this sheet is fine).

**How to do it:**

- Work through the five tasks in order. Use only the wiki: no searching the web, no asking anyone.
- Answer in your own words. Short answers are fine. Guessing is fine, just say it is a guess.
- Note the page where you found each answer, and roughly how long each task took.
- If a task takes more than ten minutes, stop, write down where you got to, and move on. That is useful, not a failure.
- When you finish, fill in the feedback form at the bottom.

## For whoever runs the test (not for the tester)

Keep this part off the tester's copy. It says what a good answer looks like and where it should have been found, so you can see where the wiki failed them, not where they did.

### Task 1: Find where to start
- **Good answer:** they find **Start Here** at the top of the wiki and say it sends them to **How Magic Works** first, then the Glossary.
- **Should be found:** the wiki's front page, one click in. Also the "Read first" view, if it has been made.
- **Watch for:** opening an alphabetical list and starting at the first section; landing in "Information not on WIKI", "Misc" or the Scene Archive's "Continuity map" pages; taking more than two minutes.

### Task 2: Stage and Grade
- **Good answer:** a **Stage** is how far a person's soul has been refined, one of sixteen steps from Murmuring to Apex; you climb by surviving something that nearly breaks you, most people never get past the first four, and your Stage caps how strong you can get. A **Grade** is a letter (F up to EX+) for how much force one particular stat delivers; when two people contest something, the higher Grade in the stat being used wins, and one full Grade up is a clear win. The difference: Stage is the whole person and their ceiling, Grade is one ability in one contest.
- **Fine if they miss:** the stage names, the exact letters, Level, Bands, Aether Class.
- **Should be found:** How Magic Works ("Stages and Grades"), The Measures at a Glance, then The Sixteen Stages and Tier Grade, Bands & the Aether Shell for depth. The Glossary (A Reader's Codex) has both terms.
- **Watch for:** thinking Stage and Grade are the same thing, or that a Grade is a school year; getting stuck in Tier Grade, Bands & the Aether Shell before reading the primer.

### Task 3: Pick a faction
- **Good answer:** any faction, named with a want and not just a description. Examples of a good level:
  - *The Guild Accord:* the body of guilds, academies and orders that ranks every practitioner on a measured scale. It wants all of them measured, ranked and answerable to it ("Balance before Dominion"), and more kingdoms signed up; its treaty is still being signed one circle at a time.
  - *Kharven:* a frozen kingdom in the north, broken when its founding king was murdered and the clan oaths sworn to him died with him. It wants to hold together and survive the cold.
  - *The Keth-Gorrum:* the open parliament of the goblinoid islands of the Hobgoblin Expanse. It wants to govern itself with no king, and to trade with the Accord without joining it.
- **Should be found:** Factions at a Glance; then Factions, Bloodlines & Institutions, or The Guild Accord pages; A Reader's Codex has a section "The Houses and Powers a Reader Meets".
- **Watch for:** a description with no want in it (the page did not say what they are after); picking a faction from a scene and finding no page for it.

- **Watch for:** picking a scene from the middle of a thread because the archive is not in story order; failing to open the Scene Archive at all (it sits under a private page right now, so a guest may not be able to see it); confusion between the two Kwon Mu-jin threads.

### Task 5: What is a release?
- **Good answer:** a release is a fighter letting their core power (their Trait) off its safety limit on purpose, in a shape their soul has learned to hold. It is a full transformation everyone can see: the body changes, the aura flares, there is a sound, and the power jumps. It burns through their reserve and runs on a clock. **Bonus:** the First Release comes no earlier than Stage IV, the Second at Stage VII (which opens their Domain), the Third at Stage IX. Not everyone has one; some have several.
- **Fine if they miss:** Domains, standing releases, the folk names each culture gives them.
- **Should be found:** How Magic Works ("Releases"), then the Release Forms page; the Release Folk Names page if they go further.
- **Watch for:** confusing a release with a technique or a Draft; finding nothing because the Release Forms page is not up yet (if so, the test has found a gap: note it).

### Reading the results
- **Time:** tasks 1 and 4 should take under five minutes each, tasks 2, 3 and 5 under ten. Anything much longer marks a page to fix.
- **Pages:** if the tester answered from a page other than the one listed, the listed page is not doing its job, or is too hard to find.
- **Words:** every word on their "had to look up" list that the Glossary does not define is a Glossary entry to add.
- **Lost points:** where they got lost is the most useful answer on the sheet. Fix the link they needed and could not see.

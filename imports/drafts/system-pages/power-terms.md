# The Measures at a Glance

*Every number a practitioner is read by, on one page. For the whole system in brief, see How Magic Works.*

A practitioner is measured on the Soul Crystal (the organ in the soul that stores power and sends it out). Their strengths are counted on sixty-four Sub-Stats (single measured abilities, such as Flux for the force a working carries or Fortitude for how well bone takes a blow), grouped under eight Primaries (Vitality, Ardency, Gnosis, Dexterity, Harmonics, Resilience, Tempering, Dominion). Their power is spent from a reserve (the Essence they carry, counted in EU, Essence Units, one megajoule each). The measures below are the ways the Archives, the Accord and the street describe all that.

## How they connect

Level and Stage climb on separate keys. Level comes from consequence and fills the reserve; Stage comes from a Catalyst and raises the ceilings. The Band is where the two are bolted together: past each hundred Levels, the counter stops until the Stage catches up. The Stage caps every Sub-Stat, the Sub-Stat's number gives its Grade, and the Grade of whichever Sub-Stat a moment calls on decides that moment. Average a Primary's Sub-Stats and you have the Grade the register prints. The Tier of Standing is the Accord's word for the Stage and carries no weight in a fight. Power leaves through the Shell, so the Aether Class sets η, and η sets how much of the reserve arrives as force and how much is shed as noise. Spending runs down the reserve, written as time from full (how long a working holds, begun on a full reserve, before the reserve falls to a tenth and the practitioner starves). The Crystal's tier says what all of this has built; its state says what today has done to it.

## Example: one practitioner, read on every measure

**Garrick Vane**, a senior disciple of a city fighting school, pays a silver mark at an assay house and stands in the coil. The clerk writes his First Name, the instrument line: *Caloria, Stage VI Glory, Level 160, Grade A, Class I Muridic, Expert, η .62.*

| Measure | His reading | What it means for him |
|---|---|---|
| Level | 160 | A reserve of about 295,000 EU. Forty Levels of room before his Band's gate. |
| Band | II, Awakened | Level 200 stays shut until he reaches Refraction and opens a Domain seed. |
| Stage | VI, Glory ("lit") | His Sub-Stats stop at 400. One Stage over a Splintering rival, his presence bends her attention; lamp flames lean toward him. Glory puts his ember-orange where any civilian can see it. |
| Tier of Standing | 5, Expert | The same rank on his token as the Stage V disciple he will fight this afternoon. |
| Grade, Sub-Stats | Flux 352 (A), Fortitude 240 (B), Celerity 210 (B), Perception 120 (C) | He hits as A, takes a blow as B, moves as B, and notices things as C. |
| Grade, Primary | Ardency mean 260 (B) | The register calls his Ardency B. His fist does not. |
| Aether Class | I, Muridic | His Shell leaks a third of every spend. |
| Crystal | Harmonic, Refined | Sound, steady, and a long way from a Domain. |
| η | 0.62 | His workings arrive hot and loud. Opponents hear him coming and can count what he has left. |

The coil's line is accurate and, by itself, useless. What Garrick is in a fight lives in the gap between his Flux and his Fortitude, and in the roar every working makes: an A-Grade fist on a B-Grade body, loud enough to be timed. The page *One Exchange, Annotated* shows what a lower-Stage fighter does with exactly that reading.

## Notes (not published)

### New figures set here

- **Garrick Vane's whole reading** is a worked example, set here: Level 160, Stage VI, Flux 352, Fortitude 240, Celerity 210, Perception 120, Ardency total 2,080 (mean 260), Class I, η 0.62. Checked against canon: Level 160 sits in Glory's usual Levels (151-175); Stage VI caps a Sub-Stat at 400 (A); his point pool at Level 160 is 2,100 from Levels (1,200 in Band I, 60 × 15 in Band II) plus 2,100 from Thresholds I to VI, so 4,200, of which these figures use about 2,650; η 0.62 sits inside Class I's 0.60-0.70.
- **His reserve, about 295,000 EU**, is the law of reserves at Level 160: log₁₀ EU = 3.4202 + 0.012812 × 160 = 5.4701, so 295,200 EU.
- **"Multiplies by ten every 78 Levels"** is canon's 78.1, rounded.

### Calls made

- **The stat in use decides, not the Primary's mean.** Written to R78-10 ("the Grade of the stat in use decides a contest"); the Primary Grade decides only a test that draws on the whole Primary. See conflict 1.
- **Aether Class sets η** (R78-10), with canon's own Class-to-η bands (*X. The Tier Ladders*, "Whose η"). Stages I-V read no Class; there η stands at the Tier of Standing's typical figure unless the practitioner's own is recorded (the Essence Ledger: "Every η here is the practitioner's own. The Tier ranges of Part Nineteen are typical"). See conflict 2.
- **Shape.** The task fixes this page as one table, a connecting paragraph and a worked example, so the five-section explainer shape (what it is, how it looks, costs, beaten, example) is not applied; it is a reference page, not a topic explainer.
- **Stage VII's top Grade** is written as Part Five gives it (A, with a late push toward S); *The Standing and the Title* gives Tier 5 as "B* → A → S". The page lists Tiers by Stage only and sidesteps the letter.

### Canon conflicts hit

1. **What decides a contest.** *II. Grades, Gates and Thresholds*: "The total is what the sheet records; the mean is what an assessor reads and what decides a contested exchange." The same page, a paragraph on: "The institutions measure the mean. The fight is decided by the peak." R78-10: "the Grade of the stat in use decides a contest." The page follows R78-10 and the "peak" sentence; the "decides a contested exchange" clause in Parts One and Four needs striking when next touched.
2. **η by Class against η by Tier.** *Tier Grade, Bands & the Aether Shell* already prints both and withdraws neither: Tier 6 Master "0.70 - 0.80" while Class V Radiant (Stage VIII) delivers "one hundred percent or more". And *The Standing and the Title* gives an instrument line "Stage VIII Transcendence, Grade S, Class III Resonant, Master, η .72", where Class III loses five to ten percent (η 0.90-0.95). R78-10 says Aether Class governs; the page follows it and reads the Tier figures as typical. The Standing page's sample line needs its η raised to 0.90-0.95 or its Class lowered to I.

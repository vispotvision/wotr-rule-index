# How Magic Works

*A first page on magic. Each section ends with the page that goes deeper.*

## The Soul Crystal, and who wakes

Nearly everyone is born with a Soul Crystal (the organ in the soul that stores power and sends it out). In most people it sleeps for life. About one in ten wake it, nearly always as children, taught by a school, a house or a guild, or stirring early on their own. They are practitioners (people whose Crystal is awake and working). The rest are Class Ø (sealed and unwoken) and touch magic only through what they buy. A sealed Crystal can be woken, but rarely, and at great cost. *Read next: The Gate: Who May Learn Magic.*

## Essence and Aether

Aether (the medium the whole world is made in, infinite and impersonal) is everywhere, thick in some places and thin in others. A practitioner draws it in and turns it into Essence (Aether stamped with their own identity), which fills their reserve (the store of Essence they carry). Every working spends reserve. Dense ground lets a practitioner put out more; thin ground, or a district whose main (its Aether supply line) has been cut, less. Their Aether Class (how cleanly the Crystal's outer layer passes power) sets how much spending arrives as effect. *Read next: The Core Vocabulary.*

## Stages and Grades

A Stage (one of sixteen steps of the soul's refinement, from Murmuring to Apex) says how far the Crystal has been remade, and it caps how high every stat can climb. A Grade (a letter band on a stat's number: F, E, D, C, B, A, S and on to EX+) says how much force that stat delivers. When two people contest a thing, the Grade of the stat in use decides it. One full Grade up wins the exchange outright; two is one-sided; three is an event the lower side survives. Inside the same Grade, skill, ground and the read decide. *Read next: The Sixteen Stages, then Tier Grade, Bands & the Aether Shell.*

## Traits

A Trait (a standing law in the soul, formed when lived experience hardens into something the world accepts as true) is the root of everything a practitioner can do. Traits are survived into being, never taught, and sleeping souls carry them too, as a knack: the fisherman the cold cannot touch. Waking arms them. The Primary Trait (the first and deepest) sets the direction for everything that follows. *Read next: The Trait System: Law, Function and the Forge.*

## Techniques

A technique (a named, repeatable working with a stated cost) is what a practitioner actually does with a Trait. Taught techniques come from a school; derived techniques are found alone; a signature is one person's own art. Fighters shout its name as it fires: the call buys a surge of power for the beat it takes. *Read next: The Core Vocabulary.*

## The four crafts

Every working (a deliberate act of magic) is a sentence in Parun (the glyph grammar all magic is written in) set into a medium, and the medium names the craft. Spellcraft speaks it: it happens now, and the body pays. Runecraft cuts it into stone, steel or paper: it outlives its maker. Draftcraft pours it into a bottle: anyone can drink it, woken or not. Chantcraft sings it: it takes many voices and holds while they sing. The guilds rank cutting highest and call speakers mouths; the crowds cheer the fighters anyway. *Read next: The Four Crafts.*

## Reinforcement

A practitioner's stats live in their body as a passive base. Reinforcement (Essence driven through the body to push it further) adds an active layer for a cost in reserve. It shows as a coat of their own aura, thickest where they push, and it can be gathered into one fist at the price of thin ground everywhere else. Asleep, drugged or taken by surprise, they keep the base and lose the coat. *Read next: Reinforcement.*

## Releases

A release (a practitioner's Trait let off its safety limit on purpose, in a shape their Crystal has learned to hold) is a full transformation: the body changes, the aura flares, a sound comes, and power rises. The First Release opens no earlier than Stage IV, the Second at VII, where it opens the Domain, and the Third at IX. Every release runs on a clock and ends on its own when the reserve runs low. *Read next: Release Forms.*

## Drafts

A Draft (a working poured into a substance and bottled) is how most of the world touches magic. Advancement Drafts can bring a soul up to a Stage's floor and seed its remaking, but no Draft has ever supplied a Catalyst. Fight Drafts are drunk mid-fight: each costs a beat, refills reserve, and leaves a crash. Each is graded by the Stage it serves, and the price climbs about threefold a Stage. *Read next: The Ladder and the Draft.*

## Runes and tags

Cheap runed gear is everywhere; the great pieces are old and cannot be made again. Fighters carry prepared tags (short workings cut on paper, hide or foil, set off with a touch), and a few bond a weapon over years until it takes part in their release. Runes cut into a living body are a rare and dangerous art. *Read next: Runes and Tags.*

## Domains

A Domain (a space where a practitioner's inner law outranks the world's) first appears as a fragile seed at Stage VII and stands as a true Domain from Stage VIII. The Second Release opens it and powers it. When two Domains or two Pressures meet, Dominion (the stat of authority over ground and presence) decides who holds the room, and a sharp read can flip it. *Read next: Tier Grade, Bands & the Aether Shell, Domains.*

## Pressure and auras

Every practitioner's aura shows in their own colour: the hue of their Family (the branch of physics their power works) at the clarity of their Stage, brightening as they push. Pressure (the weight a stronger soul puts on a room) is felt first in the room, then in bodies: one Stage up bends attention, two brings sweat and nausea, three drops people where they stand. *Read next: The Color of Essence.*

## How power grows

Level (a count of how often the Crystal has densified) rises through real consequence, never drill alone. A Stage needs two keys: enough pressure to reach its floor, and its Catalyst (the particular experience that Stage demands before the Crystal will remake itself). Three roads lead to a Catalyst: crisis in a fight, seclusion before the Threshold (the moment of remaking), and tribulation, when a Wellspring's current (one of the world's sixty great laws) tests the practitioner directly. A breakthrough is a visible transformation; a failed one leaves slag (a remaking that set wrong). *Read next: Training and Sects.*

## What it all costs

Reserve spent is gone until it is drawn back. Below a tenth the practitioner starves: stats sag, Traits flicker. Pushing past the Crystal's safe ceiling cracks it, and cracks that build up become permanent. A soul that stalls below a Stage gate builds strain until it advances or breaks. Then come the money, the oath sworn at the gate, and the borrowed convictions in a bought Draft. *Read next: Stacking and Spending, then Counterplay: What Beats a Practitioner.*

- **Waking.** Written to R78-10 ("a sealed Crystal is rarely woken, at great cost"). See the conflict below.

# Release Forms

*How a practitioner turns their own Trait loose, in a form the whole room can see. For the whole system in brief, see How Magic Works.*

## What it is

A release is a practitioner's Primary Trait (the deepest standing law in their soul) let off the safety limit of the Soul Crystal (the soul's organ of power) on purpose, in a shape the Crystal has learned to hold: the same force as an overchannel (forcing the Crystal past its safe ceiling), rehearsed until it keeps a form, a price and a name. A technique is something the practitioner makes with the Trait; a release changes the practitioner, and every technique fired inside it runs through the released Trait.

## How it looks

**The three releases.** Every release follows one pattern: the body changes, the aura flares, the sound comes, and the Trait's power rises.

| | First Release | Second Release | Third Release |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Earliest Stage** (step on the soul's sixteen-step ladder) | IV, Flourishing | VII, Refraction | IX, Invocation |
| **The look** | One signature change to the body or the weapon, in any shape the soul takes: skin, eyes, hair, wings, armour, a weapon's new form | The change spreads into the ground around them: a field where light, air and heat obey the Trait, and everyone inside feels the colour shift | The field takes a second colour at its edges, the Wellspring's own (the great law their power harmonises with), and the Wellspring's voice joins the sound |
| **The power** | The Trait strikes at the top of its Grade (its letter band of force) | Inside the field, one full Grade higher | Inside the field, the Trait's stated effect becomes local law, two Grades over its base, for that effect alone |
| **The reach** | The body and what it touches | A field the size of the room or yard it stands in | A field that can hold other fields inside it |
| **The sound** | Its own, called once: a bellows drawn hard, a struck bell, a crack of ice | The sound held as a standing note | The Wellspring's note beneath the owner's |

**The form is free.** A release takes whatever shape the person's soul would take: wings, plate, a changed blade, a beast's outline, a crown of something that should not burn. A Vectoria (force) practitioner may release as a white crane. What the form does still comes from the Trait and its Family (the branch of physics it works), so the crane's wings strike with plain Newtonian force.

**The aura.** A release drives the aura to the practitioner's full colour at once: their Family's hue at their Stage's clarity. At Stage IV that is a dark grey flare with the hue only hinted; at Stage VIII it lights the room in its own colour.

**The Domain.** The Second Release opens and powers the Domain (a space where the practitioner's inner law outranks the world's). At Stage VII the field is a seed: it lasts minutes and collapses if attention breaks. From Stage VIII it is a true Domain, held for hours and steady under small disruptions. The Third Release turns it into a Realm that can hold other Domains inside it. From Stage XI the First Release no longer needs calling: it is simply how they are.

**When fields meet.** Two release fields, or a field and another's Pressure (the weight a strong soul puts on a room), contest the ground by Dominion (the stat of authority over ground and presence) before a blow lands. The border shows in the room as light changing colour across the floor, bystanders feel both, and the weaker side starts the fight already paying. A read can flip it: find what the field rests on, the owner's focus, its edge, or the thing they are guarding, press there, and the border moves back.

**The bonded weapon.** A weapon its owner has carried for years can carry part of the release: the blade burns with the owner's heat, the staff holds the edge of the field. Its part is written on the owner's card, and is lost with the weapon.

**Called or silent.** The owner chooses. Called, the release's English name is shouted as it fires: the call costs a beat, buys the full rise, and is a tell everyone hears. Silent, it costs no beat and gives no spoken tell, but it still flares and still sounds, and it comes in weaker: the form without the call's rise. The card says exactly what silence costs each release.

**Who carries them.** Any practitioner with a woken Crystal and an armed Primary Trait; a Class Ø (sealed, unwoken) soul carries Traits but cannot release them. How many a person has varies and the card sets it: one, two, three, and for a rare few more, no further release opening below Stage XI. Most who reach Stage IV earn a First Release in time. Far fewer earn a Second. A Third is the mark of a figure the ballads already know.

**What they are called.** The Archives say First, Second and Third Release. Every people has its own street words for the three, as it does for the Stages. Bloodline faculties release too, by the bloodline's own law and stages, kept as a family secret and written on the bloodline's page.

**How one is learned.** A release is never taught; like the Trait, it is survived into being, by one of three roads. **Crisis** is the commonest road to a First Release: the Trait breaks its limit in a fight the practitioner should have lost, and the Crystal keeps the shape. **Seclusion** is the commonest road to a Second: months working the Trait alone until it claims the ground around them. **Tribulation** is how every Third arrives: the Wellspring's current tests the claim before it agrees to share the law, and not everyone survives the test. The first time a release holds, it prints on the Crystal, and the transformation is the kind people remember seeing.

**It deepens with the person.** A release keeps its core for life but grows new aspects as its owner advances, survives and changes, and a great turn in a life can reshape it. Each new aspect is added to the card when it first shows.

## What it costs

**The shape spreads the strain.** Because the release is a learned shape, the Crystal carries it without structural damage, provided it is held inside its limit. Past the limit it is a raw overchannel again.

| | First Release | Second Release | Third Release |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Drain** | Set on each card and written as time from full: how long it holds if started on a full reserve (the store of Essence, their own power, that they carry). One person's First Release may sip; another's may burn like a Third | Set on the card | Set on the card |
| **The limit** | Ends on its own when the reserve falls to a tenth. Below that is Essence Starvation: sagging stats, a degrading Shell (the Crystal's outer layer), flickering Traits | The same | The same |
| **Afterwards** | Spent and shaking; the pain the fight was shrugging off arrives | The Crystal runs Overgrown (swollen and spilling) until a full night's sleep: power spills, and the owner's moods stain the air around them. No lasting harm | Overgrown for a day and a night, and an ache through the body like the end of a fever. No lasting harm |
| **Held past the limit** | A plain overchannel, with its fracture risk | A Crystal Fracture Event (a crack in the Crystal) | The Trait itself may fail, in a way the card names |

- **The call** costs a beat every time.
- **Outside a fight** a release may be held at the same cost: a First Release worn through a court audience to make a point, a Second spread over a winter camp to keep the snow off. The clock runs the same.
- **On the stack** a release counts toward the cap of two Grades over what the Crystal holds safely, and a Third already stands at it for its one effect, so nothing more stacks there.

## How it is beaten

Every release can be answered by someone at its own Stage, before, during and after.

- **Before.** A called release costs a beat, and the aura flares and the sound comes before the power rises. A fighter who reads the tell can strike into that beat, or get out of reach. A silent release skips the beat but still flares, still sounds, and comes in weaker.
- **During.** The clock is the counter. A release spends reserve fast and ends at a tenth, so outlasting it is a real plan: keep distance, make them chase, and fight on ground that raises their cost, cold and wet against heat, thin Aether against anyone. A Second Release at Stage VII collapses when attention breaks: a wound, a shout, a name that matters to them. Every field has an edge; step out of it. Contest the ground with a stronger Dominion, or read what holds it up. Take the bonded weapon and its part of the release goes with it.
- **After.** The aftermath is a window. A First Release leaves them spent; a Second and a Third leave the Crystal spilling and easy to read.

This is how a lower-Stage fighter beats a higher one across one Stage: survive the release, read it, and strike in the aftermath.

## Example

**Corra Vell** is a Stage VII hired hunter harmonised to Pyraeon (a Caloria, or heat, Wellspring), with a heat Primary Trait. Her colour is Caloria's deep ember-orange at Refraction's clarity: grey and orange sharing one field without mixing. She has fought eleven years with the same forge hammer, and it is bonded. Her card carries two releases:

> **KILN SKIN** *(First Release)*. She says "Kiln Skin" and her skin goes the dull cherry-orange of iron at forging heat, from the hands up over the forearms to the collarbones, and the air around her shimmers like the air over a forge bed. There is a sound like a bellows drawn once, hard. Every blow she lands carries forge heat into the wound, and her heat workings strike at the top of their Grade. Her bonded hammer takes the heat with her: its head glows the same cherry-orange and keeps it, and the forge heat in her hammer blows is the hammer's share, lost if the hammer is. From a full reserve it holds about six minutes, and it ends on its own when her reserve falls to a tenth; afterwards her palms are blistered and she cannot draw heat for an hour. Released silently, it takes the colour and the shimmer but not the call's rise, and her heat workings strike at the middle of their Grade, not the top. The counter is in the beat of the call, which can be struck into, in cold and wet ground, which makes every second cost more, and in the blistered hands afterwards, which cannot hold a weapon properly.

> **SMELTER'S YARD** *(Second Release)*. She says "Smelter's Yard" and the heat leaves her skin and fills the ground around her, about the size of a courtyard: the air goes forge-hot and dry, the light turns orange, and everyone inside feels the colour change as a pressure behind the eyes. The bellows sound becomes a low, steady roar for as long as it holds. This is her Domain seed, and inside it her heat workings strike one full Grade higher. From a full reserve it holds about three minutes, and it collapses at once if her attention breaks; afterwards her Crystal runs Overgrown until she sleeps, and heat leaks off her with her temper. Released silently, the yard forms without its first roar and her workings strike at the top of their own Grade inside it, not one higher. The counter is to break her focus with a wound, a shout or a name that matters, to step out past the edge of the yard, to contest its ground with a stronger Dominion, and to strike while her spilling Crystal is easy to read.

On a dockside job Corra meets a Stage VII Fulguria (lightning) duellist who opens his own field, a ring of arc-blue that makes every hair in the yard stand up. She answers with Smelter's Yard, and the two fields meet in a seam across the cobbles where orange light and blue light grind against each other. His Dominion is higher, and the seam starts walking toward her. Then she reads him: his field is centred on his left hand, which never moves from his belt. She throws the hammer at it. He flinches to guard the hand, his attention breaks, his field goes out like a doused lamp, and the orange floods the yard to the water's edge. She has two minutes left on the clock, and she is going to need her hammer back.

## Notes (not published)

### New figures set here

- **KILN SKIN about six minutes from full; SMELTER'S YARD about three minutes from full.** The draft priced them at "about full output" and "about twice full output". At the Essence Ledger's arithmetic a Stage IV practitioner empties in about 12 s at full output, so those rates would last seconds, against canon's "lasts minutes rather than hours" for a Stage VII seed. Rewritten as time from full (R78-10) and set so the yard burns twice as fast as the skin, as the draft intended, and the seed lasts minutes, as canon says.
- **Silent KILN SKIN at the middle of the Grade; silent SMELTER'S YARD at the top of its own Grade inside the field.** Set here; the draft left "what silence costs" to the card.
- **No further release below Stage XI** (draft design figure, kept).
- **Third Release aftermath: Overgrown for a day and a night, a fever-ache.** Set here (see Calls made).
- **"Eleven years" with the hammer, "two minutes left"**: the example's own.

### Calls made

- **No structural damage inside the limit (R78-10, "a learned release spreads the strain so it does no structural damage inside its limit").** The draft had the Second leave the Crystal Overgrown and the Third leave it "Fractured every time ... Done often, the fractures become permanent". Fractured is structural damage, so the Third's aftermath is rewritten to Overgrown for a day and a night with no lasting harm, and Fracture is kept only for holding past the limit. Overgrown is kept for the Second as a passing state that clears with sleep, read as spill rather than damage.
- **The Second Release opens and powers the Domain (R78-10)** is squared with *Tier Grade*'s "A character does not choose to deploy a Domain the way they choose to throw a punch": the Domain still forms only because the soul is heavy enough (Stage VII and up); the Second Release is the door it is opened through.
- **Dominion clash (R71-42 extended by R78-10)** and **a read can flip it**: "what the field rests on" (focus, edge, the guarded thing) is set here as the read's target.
- **Bloodlines**: agent call kept from the draft (their own law and stages, a house secret).
- **Called or silent**: per answer RF8; the call is R8-24's beat-for-a-rise, shouted in English (R78-5).
- **The vow note** from the draft ("a great vow may never be stacked on a Third Release") is carried as the stacking cap in What it costs, since R78-10's two-Grade cap now produces the same result.
- **Pyraeon as a Caloria Wellspring** is confirmed in the wiki (the Ignivale and Pyraeon forge-arrays, "Caloria family, Thermodynamics").

### Canon conflicts hit

- None between canon pages. The draft's own "Fractured every time" for the Third Release conflicts with R78-10 and was rewritten as above.

# Term collisions in the magic vocabulary: decisions (2026-10-15)

Source: a ChatGPT review of *The Lexicon of Magic — Master Terminology*, checked against the full wiki mirror.
Edits are prepared as audit items in the session scratchpad (`audit/collisions.json`, 34 items: 29 gloss, 4 law, 1 copy). No wiki page has been edited.
No canon term is renamed. Two terms become "also called" names, and every other pair stays as it is, with a one-line distinction added where each term is defined.

| # | Collision | Decision | Pages touched |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Family vs Affinity (Discipline = Family x Method, and also Affinity x Method) | These are not synonyms. The Family is the field (Caloria), and the Affinity is the one phenomenon inside it (fire). A discipline is named from its Affinity, so the slogan "Family is the what" stays and gets a clause pointing to the Affinity. | Lexicon (VI intro, Discipline); Magic System/The Magic System |
| 2 | Rupture vs Rupture Event | These are different things. Bare **Rupture** stays the Shell failure. The alchemical failure is always **Rupture Event**, and the three places on alchemy pages that said plain "Rupture" are brought into line. The Veil's "Rupture" (a channel struck between planes) is a third meaning and gets a gloss. | Lexicon (Clouding · Rupture, Rupture Event); Alchemetrica (glossary entry, operations table); Alftian Codex Volume the First; The Veil |
| 3 | Aetheric Saturation ("Essence flooding an area") | The term is kept. The definition now says the Essence is released by workings and gives the threshold the Core Vocabulary already states (10⁴ EU/m³). It is set apart from **Ambient Saturation**, the harmless density band. | Lexicon; The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings (Ambient Saturation row); Apprentice's Primer |
| 4 | Conjunctia vs Conjunction | These are different things. **Conjunctia** is a Method: one practitioner holds two Families at once. **Conjunction** is the Great Work operation and the Attraction-aligned Category: two laws wedded in a substrate. Each definition gets a distinction. | Lexicon (Method table, Section VIII); Magic System (Nine Methods); Magical Categories §3 |
| 5 | Turgia vs Theurgic vs Thaumaturgic | All three are different. **Turgia** is a Method (ritual work), and its practitioner is a "turgist". **Theurgic** and **thaumaturgic** are the derivation poles: a taught capacity against an innate one. The Lexicon lines are anchored to origin, with one line separating Turgia from the theurgic axis. | Lexicon (Turgia row, Theurgic axis, Thaumaturgic axis) |
| 6 | Scriptia vs Sigilcraft vs Glyphica | **Glyphica** is the syntax and Category. It had no Lexicon entry, so one is added on the Parun line. **Scriptia** is the Method. **Sigilcraft** is a true synonym of **Runecraft** (2 uses against 50 or more) and becomes an "also called" name for it. | Lexicon (Scriptia row, Parun, Sigilcraft); Magical Categories §8 examples |
| 7 | Dead Set vs slag vs "a seat that never took" | These are true synonyms by register. **Dead Set** stays the main term (the record's name), "a seat that never took" is the halls' gloss, and "slag" is the common word. The trade's second sense of slag (Residue, what traps collect) is marked off from it. | Lexicon (Dead Set); The Working Vocabulary (slag row, cant cell) |
| 8 | Draft Corruption vs Boundary failure vs Sealing failure | All three are different. **Boundary failure** spreads while the operator is there. **Sealing failure** goes on after he leaves. **Draft Corruption** is a working that stops but leaves a law-fragment trying to finish. The Four Crafts line saying Boundary failures produce "a working that does not happen" contradicted the role table and Alchemetrica, and is corrected. The Lexicon's Draft Corruption entry ended on a stray fragment, which is repaired. | Lexicon (role table Boundary cell, Draft Corruption); The Four Crafts |
| 9 | Hollowing vs thinning vs "thin" | All are kept. **Hollowing** is the canon term, and "thinning" is the farmers' word for it. "Thin" for a failed Limina site is folk speech. Both are set apart from **Veil-thin** (planes pressing close) and **Aether-thin** (low density). | Lexicon (Hollowing, common-tongue Draft Corruption row) |
| 10 | Vocatia vs Animatria vs Eidolon | All three are different. **Vocatia** calls an outside being, which exists before and after the call. **Animatria** builds a presence from the caster's own Core. An **Eidolon** is one Animatria construct. The Lexicon's "not a summoned thing" becomes "not a called thing", because the Spirit Summoning Arts count Eidolons as Basic Summons. Animatria gets a gloss at its first use. | Lexicon (Vocatia, Eidolon) |

## Found while checking (not part of these items)

- **Ardency Saturation** appears as a Sub-Stat on about twenty character cards and in Caloria's Stat Affinity line. Part Twelve says Depth "absorbs Saturation", so the cards carry a retired Sub-Stat name.
- The Saturation threshold (10⁴ EU/m³) equals the floor of the Veil-Thin Nexus band. That puts all natural Nexus and Core ground above the "lethal" figure. Canon may intend the threshold to apply only to Essence that workings leave behind, but no page says so.
- In *The Derivation of Practice*, "The Nine Methods and Their Native Gravity" table is collapsed: the Lean and Method columns are scrambled, for example "| **Theurgic** | **Turgia** |". This is a copy error.
- Character cards use "Eidolon" loosely for summoned spirits (Dalphne's Tsuuna, Hazul Vehramin, Emilia Veirynthiel). These are character descriptions, so they are left alone.

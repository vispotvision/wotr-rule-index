# Who's Who: the Starting Threads

*The people you will meet first in each of the four threads, and how to tell them apart. Light on secrets: what the room already knows, and no more.*

Each entry runs: name (and how to say it, where the record gives one), who they are, what they want, and how you will know them. A **player character** is played at the table; what they choose is theirs, so this page only says who they are. A few people belong to other writers and appear here by name only.

## Sodoku Moto / Kharven

Kharven (the frozen north, a people of steppe riders who stopped riding when the ground froze through) has had no working crown since its First King was killed, and its six Banner Houses (the great families who hold its borders, grain, roads and tundra) have waited a generation for one worth following. A year after the Ashgate road, the worst day in the history of the North, the first Accord circle (a signed union of realms under one seal and one body of law) is drawn in the lower hall at Kharven-Seat with the Moto seated as the royal house, and now it has to hold.

- **Sodoku Moto** (soh-DOH-koo MOH-toh) · *player character.* The Exiled Lion, a prince of the Kōkan Line (the ruling branch of the Moto) who came home after eleven years. Wants to be more than what grief made him, without pretending grief made him nothing. You will know him by amber-gold eyes with faint rings in them, a string of jade and iron beads at his throat, and full, formal sentences with never a contraction.
- **Yoko Mishiro** (YOH-koh mee-SHEE-roh) · A fox-spirit beastkin who has stood beside Sodoku for nine years. Wants to be the one who stayed, on record, by her own account. You will know her by ash-grey hair and fox ears that show what she feels a second before her face admits it, and by answers that say exactly what is accurate and nothing more.
- **Edward Lambert** (ED-werd LAM-bert) · The Arithmetic: the Seat's counter of everything, who brought the southern reach through on a grain schedule rather than a battle. Wants the count to come out right, and keeps his reasons to himself. You will know him by a long, weathered, clean-shaven face, grey-brown hair cropped close, and a thumb that rubs his forefinger before he speaks.
- **Tabitha Hallenfeld** (TAB-ih-thah HAL-en-feld) · The Road Woman of House Hallenfeld, who runs the roads, rivers and couriers. Wants a straight answer to a question she has dated, written down and been refused twice: why the grain was bought when it was. You will know her by a broad, sun-darkened build, brown hair held with two wooden pins, and route manifests rolled at her belt.
- **Bram Greymane** (BRAM GRAY-mayn) · Heir of the Shieldwarden, whose house holds the western wall, three fingers short and in from the Stormfold road for the signing. Wants the line held; he reads every room as ground and gaps. You will know him as the man who will not sit down, and then gets up badly on a bad hip.
- **Brida Ashwell** (BREE-dah ASH-wel) · The Seat's physician, the Warm Diagnosis. Wants to treat the person, never the title. You will know her by russet hair pinned with two bone pins, a satchel with buckles gone soft, and a voice that is firm and warm at once; she runs rooms from a stool with her leg out.
- **Robin Ice** (ROB-in EYES) · A courier of the Kōkan cadet blood, carried on the rolls as Ice by law. Wants to be Robin Stark in law as well as in his own mouth. You will know him by dark auburn hair he shoves back every few minutes, a nose broken leftward, and sealed message cases on his belt.
- **Miku Tenrai Moto** · Fourteen, the Tenrai heir (the rival Moto branch that seized the north), holding a chair at the Accord table. Wants to keep that chair. You will know him as the youngest at the table and the only Tenrai at it.
- **Sonzai** (also rendered Chuluun) · Another writer's character, a companion from Sodoku's past.

## Hild Ice / Kharven-Seat

This thread opens in the Thin Weeks (the hungry late winter) before the muster, in the hall of the broken throne at Kharven-Seat. An eleven-year-old Moto bastard sits that throne as acting head of the Kōkan loyalist coalition while the Tenrai come for the north, and the roads are saying the Tenrai know where she sleeps.

- **Hild Ice** (HILD EYES) · The Sword Princess, Sodoku's daughter by Fern Stark, raised on the frontier and seated on the broken throne. Wants to be the ruler the north needs, and still says "I want" before the queen in her can stop it. You will know her by her father's wide jaw, dark hair that flashes violet in the sun, a plain quilted gambeson, and feet that do not touch the floor when she sits the throne.
- **Lorn Stark** (LORN STARK) · Her mother's brother and her sword-teacher, the coalition's military guardian. Wants his dead sister's daughter alive, and does not bargain over it. You will know him by the Stark jaw that tightens on the left before he speaks, grey at the temples, and the plain build of a man who trained every day without being gifted.
- **Osric of Hallenfeld** (OZ-rik of HAL-en-feld) · Her strategic uncle, who counselled putting her on the broken throne. Wants the Banner Houses to look at her court and see a government rather than a children's game. You will know him by height, a narrow jaw, thinning dark hair, and a face trained to say nothing.
- **Edward Lambert** (ED-werd LAM-bert) · Her right hand, who taught her the arithmetic of governing: who holds what, who needs what, who fears what. Wants her to govern rather than merely rule. You will know him by the inventory he makes of a room before he looks at the people in it.
- **Brida Ashwell** (BREE-dah ASH-wel) · Her physician, the only person who tells Hild to eat and means it. Wants the child fed and seen. You will know her by the willowbark she brings for something you have not noticed yet.
- **Renard Greymane** (REN-ard GRAY-mayn) · Bram's cousin and her guard, stationed at the left of the throne. Wants to be the thing between the throne and the threat. You will know him by a hand that rests on his hilt and almost no words.
- **Tabitha Hallenfeld** (TAB-ih-thah HAL-en-feld) · Who walked three days on compromised roads to tell Hild the roads are talking. Wants the roads kept alive long enough for this child to walk them. You will know her by road dirt on her hem and manifests at her belt.
- **Bram Greymane** (BRAM GRAY-mayn) · Commander of the loyalist Greymane forces, holding the southern approach. Wants it held: his house's words are "The Ridge Does Not Move." You will know him by his grandfather's heavy brow and a face aged early by three years on the ridge.
- **Seiji Tenrai Moto** · Heir of the Tenrai design against the Kōkan Line, pressing his house's claim to the north. Wants the north for that claim. You will know him by a handsome face that agrees with whoever is nearest, a warm smile he trained for years, and lacquered plate in a red so dark it reads black.

## Kwon Mu-jin / Aetherion Academy

It is the first week of Class X, the top class of the Aetherion Academy, an Accord-chartered school that takes its students by letter and recommendation. A duel has cracked the arena's ward, the mandatory Class X Open Tournament is seven days out, an examiner wants someone's name entered for the damage, and Class S, the class below, wants Class X's seats.

- **Kwon Mu-jin** (KWON moo-JIN) · *player character.* Class X's homeroom teacher, a master alchemist ranked Archmagus by the Accord, born to the Mahuo summoning line. Wants alchemical and summoning knowledge to be everyone's inheritance, never a court's or a bloodline's property. You will know him by overgrown white-blond hair, a single round lens on a chain over his left eye, and ink stains set into both hands.
- **Rovhen Talvasciel** (ROH-ven tal-VAS-ee-el) · *player character.* A retired inquiry agent (an investigator of worked crimes) who now teaches the Academy's inquiry class in the old dispensary. Wants to keep people safe without being known for it, and to teach investigators to stop looking at the hand first. You will know him by his height, black hair falling in loose waves across his face, and hands kept folded or out of sight.
- **Hiromi Mahuo** (hee-ROH-mee MAH-hoo-oh) · *player character.* Thirteen, a summoning prodigy in Class X. Wants to be the greatest summoner who ever lived, and says so. You will know him by white hair the colour of forge-ash and pale red eyes.
- **Xanelor Rafiminar** (ZAN-eh-lor rah-FIM-ih-nar) · *player character.* Fourteen, an island-raised archer whom no Accord body has ever assessed or ranked. Wants to be the person the man who raised him believed he was. You will know him by a slatted iron helm trailing green silk, three parallel scars down his left cheek, and a seat in the back row by the service corridor.
- **Rikudoku Moto** (ree-koo-DOH-koo MOH-toh) · *player character.* Thirteen, son of Sodoku Moto and Yoko Mishiro, and the tallest in the room. Wants, as far as the room can tell, to be left asleep and not to hurt anyone. You will know him by white hair running to black at the tips and eyes that are always half closed.
- **Dabney** (DAB-nee) · *player character.* Thirteen, ward of Edward Lambert, trained for seven years in the Palatinate of the Holy Sea of Alabaster. Has wanted since he was five a way to be more careful, not slower. You will know him by a cowlick at the crown, hands folded on the desk, and a grey and white cat called Astro.
- **Naori Yukari** (nah-OH-ree yoo-KAH-ree) · *player character.* Thirteen, eldest son of the Yukari Enclave's acting authority. Wants to be the crow his father described: the one who watches the bad ends so others do not have to. You will know him by crimson eyes with slow patterns turning in them, fixed on something that is not in the room.
- **Lily Mahuo** (LIL-ee MAH-hoo-oh) · Twelve, Hiromi's younger sister. Wants everything, her brothers included, done to standard. You will know her by a pressed coat with a starched collar, a silver year-rank pin she straightens anyway, and a pen that keeps moving through any chaos.
- **Examiner Maud Harrowgate** · On visitation for the Grand Mage (the Accord office that inspects the academies). Wants a name entered as answerable for the arena damage before her report goes up. You will know her by pewter hair, a seal-die burn on the back of her left hand, the phrase "before witnesses", and a clause repeated when she pushes.
- **Imogen Pell** · A Class S student. Wants her class, and so Class S, inside the chained arena. You will know her by a clear voice pitched for a bigger room and a slate she writes on without looking down.
- **Also at the table:** Taesyn, Zaire, Neraveth, Verona and Nyxeria Velthrae, other players' characters in the same week, and Xhem, another writer's character.

## Ilthára Korvaeth / The New World

The New World is a separate continent with its own calendar, and its war is a war about names: whether a name can be held as property, and whether a holding stands when its name was never validly given. The thread opens in the outer yard at Korvaeth, on the morning the Empress of Eresse comes to the block before her oldest friend, and runs on eleven years into a war fought as litigation.

- **Ilthára Korvaeth** · *The thread's lead.* A drow, Queen of Korvaeth with no crown, called the Mad Queen since she was twenty-six; the yard refused her a naming, and she signs with the name nobody gave her. Wants the cost of a thing named, and who pays it, said aloud. You will know her by grey-violet skin, flat white hair bound in one black cord, a scar that pulls her left eyelid, a coat with salt-black facings, and the words "Say it."
- **Aeldros Korvaeth, the Validated** · Her younger brother, a jurist by training, called the Validated because his name went through the full assembly his sister was refused. Wants to hold the Vessel road through a winter with eleven hundred men and a lawyer's habits. You will know him by the family's large hands without the family's stillness: he shifts his weight.
- **Onawa, Empress of Eresse** · Eresse's empress, who held the calendar, and Ilthára's friend since they were fourteen in the Vosk orchard. Wants the yard's business done in full form, before witnesses. You will know her by hair put up herself in four passes and a plain pin, and by arriving holding nothing. Not to be confused with Onawa Ashkewe, Queen of the Tsohanto.
- **Maren Vaeleth** · The Reckoner (the officer who keeps the count and enters it in the record), forty-one years in the office. Wants things done correctly. You will know her by mended black and her eyes on people's hands.
- **Thela Ess-Vaelen** · Head of a house that refused Ilthára, taken alive in the field and released. Wants something; nobody is sure what, herself included. You will know her as the woman who holds something she could use against the Queen and has not.
- **Ovaeren** · Regent of the Sunroot, chosen because he has no blood claim, and holding a regency that split name from office for eleven years. Wants that regency to hold. You will know him by the office rather than the man: everything he signs is signed as a regent.
- **Corlan Mirroven** · Of House Mirroven, which sent its forces north under Aeldros and not under the Queen. Wants to serve under Aeldros. You will know him as frightened of everybody except Aeldros, and willing to say so.

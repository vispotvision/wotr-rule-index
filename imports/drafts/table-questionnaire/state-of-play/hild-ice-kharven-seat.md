# State of Play: Hild Ice / Kharven-Seat

*Draft rewrite, 4 October 2026. Closed thread, kept as record.*

**Closed.** Hild Ice died at the ninth hour, twelve years old, in a cistern doorway with Osric of Hallenfeld. No scene runs from this page.

**Where it ends.** The upper burying ground at Kharven-Seat: three stones cut in the same week by the same mason, the lettering matched in depth across all three. Hild Ice. Lorn Stark, who had made an arrangement about the girl twelve years before and had kept every part of it. Osric of Hallenfeld, who put himself in a doorway.

**Last live state.** The Thin Weeks before the muster. Hild asleep in the altar room with Brida on the pallet, the night-stone cooling. Renard Greymane on the door killed a Tenrai practitioner who came through the gate in a Kharven courier's wool, and the dying man said that Lambert had opened the west postern.

**What it hands to the Kharven thread.**

**What it closes.** Renard's forearm from the Held Boil (L009) and his debt to Brida of an explanation and a body before morning (L013), both more than a year old at the Kharven live date with nothing carrying them.

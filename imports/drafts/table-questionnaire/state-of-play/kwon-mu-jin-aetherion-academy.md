# State of Play: Kwon Mu-jin / Aetherion Academy

*Draft rewrite, 4 October 2026, under the table law (R72-14, R72-21, R72-22, R72-24, R72-26). One page for the Academy thread: it replaces the 26 September Mu-jin page and the 28 September Rovhen page, which ran the same week. Clocks are never printed here; the Fronts appear by their signs.*

**Live story date.** 715 IC, the present. The fourth day of term at Aetherion Academy, the last hour before last bell. First bell tomorrow is when the Visitation calls for its Finding. The Class X Open Tournament falls on the eighth day, seven days after Monday's duel. No Accord day is held for the thread yet.

**Where his people stand.**
- *Rovhen Talvasciel:* the old dispensary in the east range, the first lecture over. Joan Aldery has just gone with his letter to the clerk of inquests. He holds Harrowgate's green-sealed envelope, unopened; the Finding of Cause owed at first bell with the line beside *Party answerable* still bare; his field book with a page on every student; the Held Pen. Behind his own heartbeat, half a beat late, a second one: the Tuned Signature in the letter in Joan's pocket (L057).
- *Kwon Mu-jin:* last on the page on Monday, letting go of the dome seam at the close of his son's match, the nails of his left hand gone the colour of candle wax, the draw-inspector under the seam on a folding stool. He named no winner of either match. The contents of his vial are still undisclosed.
- *Geturo:* slung in calico, the right shoulder out and back, the thigh, the burned wrist, the Crystal cold where the borrowed Essence was taken back (L066). He refused the stretcher and stood.
- *Hiromi:* ribs kicked in, emptied, every draw now at first-call price (L034); a petal kept in his closed hand.
- *Xanelor:* both forearms bridged with mineral that is not yet bone, three draw fingers wrapped and unhealed, and no bow: Mu-jin held the broken one out to him grip first (L030, L031). Every Class X student now knows the masked boy is not the soft bracket (L032).
- *Naori, Akira, Dabney, Rikudoku:* nothing new on file since Monday.

**Who is near Rovhen.** Proctor Wenna Crale at the end of the east corridor until last bell, the night keys on her ring. Nyxeria Velthrae a few feet off with her notebook against her chest (another player's; written only as he perceives her).

**Who is doing what tonight.** Harrowgate in the Visitation Room with the inspector's book shut under her hand. Tobin Sallow between the bursar and the Visitation with his bill for the whole row. Joan Aldery holding the letter for a weekday morning. Imogen Pell back with Class S with what she read on the roll. Agnes Tull on the Visitation wing door, trading what she heard. Registrar Pike in the Registry, not yet asked about the narrow pen. Hob and Dunstan Pryor back on the porters' rota. Josse Osricsson at the bowyer's counter with no blackwood before Ice-out. Lei Yanshu in Class S, holding the one figure anyone took on the arrow at the anchor.

**What is owed.**
- The Finding of Cause at first bell (L058), which is also how the arena is answered for: the ward cracked across a third of the dome, the trench, the burn-hole (L033). Sallow wants paying for the row, not the crack (L059).
- What Rovhen holds against the quarterly-proofing story: the cased plate from row six, the meters, the five names in a narrow pen, Joan's cuff, Walter Aldery's lamp and the missing daybook (L060). Imogen Pell knows the chain and where he was at noon, and somebody told her (L061).
- Walter Aldery, dead at the foot of the Tallow Street stair, entered as a fall in drink (L062).
- "Lambert's lad" to anyone who knew Edmund (L063).
- Xanelor's arms and his bow before the tournament (L030, L031); Hiromi's next draw (L034); Geturo's arm (L066).
- On the fourth beat of Geturo's tide the dome seam flickered (L065).

**Fronts in play, by sign.**
- *The Class Below:* three Class S students on the infirmary steps from breakfast, visiting nobody; five Class S names added to the inquiry roll in a narrow pen; a girl with a silver cord taking notes at the tunnel mouth; the tall Class S boy deciding, in the tunnel, that he does not stop.
- *The Finding of Cause:* a chain on the north gate, a printed form with one line bare, and a green-sealed envelope.
- *The Tallow Street Stair:* a widow's left cuff mended in black thread, three short lines of it, and a letter in her hand bound for the clerk of inquests.

**The warm bond.** Hiromi and Geturo, brothers: Hiromi watched him refuse the stretcher and will ask tonight with the door shut (L071). Hiromi and Saeloria: the picnic asked for properly, petals on the back of his hand (L072).

**Heat.** Warm and rising (L068): the Visitation's chain and its Finding, a match the whole grounds heard end, Class S with notes on the hurt, and the Night Register's file on three faculty losses in eighteen months.

**What he believes that is wrong.** Rovhen takes the five Class S names on his roll for reputation-chasers. They are scouting.

**Unplayed between Monday and tonight.** Geturo's summons from the old summoning lecturer for third bell on the second day ("Then bring the eleven months, and we'll write some"). The talk his father owes him at the table with the door shut. The picnic, if her guards allow. Xanelor's answer to the broken bow held out to him. Each is his to play or to pass.

**Also on the grounds.** The Night Register's investigation of the one called the Mother: Wystan's briefing to the Grand Mage and Shiori's findings in the arena, and Malphas on a train east.

# State of Play: Sodoku Moto / Kharven

*Draft rewrite, 4 October 2026, under the table law (R72-14, R72-21, R72-22, R72-24, R72-34). Replaces the 10 September page. Clocks are never printed here; the Fronts appear by their signs.*

**Live story date.** The empire's first year, the year after the ninth hour: spring, after the signing in the lower hall, at night. No Accord day is held for the thread yet. The Thin Weeks that followed the ninth hour are behind; the next ones are the coming winter.

**Where.** The king's room at Kharven-Seat, night. Lambert has brought the first test of the instrument dressed as housekeeping: every southern register to be re-entered under the circle's seal, the relocation schedule and its sub-entries among them, so that the four hundred and six pass from his office to the circle without anyone saying so. The paper is on the table. The brush has not moved.

**What just happened.** The first Accord circle was drawn and signed: Kharven, the Holy Sea of Alabaster, the Kingdom of Nalūn, and the stretches from the Brine crossing east to the marches of Sum-gol, under one seal and one body of law, the Moto lines seated as the royal house. Three thousand and eleven names read out beforehand by a man in gloves who would not delegate one of them. Sodoku Moto put his hand to it last. Before that, in the year: Lambert starved the orc host out of the southern reach with a supply schedule; Bram held the Stormfold crossings four days and returned Vresk Dokkan alive in a cart; the Tenrai came north as refugees and were taken in; the eleven around Miku Tenrai Moto died at the Brine crossing and the boy forfeited; Seiji was revoked and entered *elar-ama*; the four hundred and six hostages were killed on Lambert's order.

**At the Seat.** Edward Lambert, fifty-three, gloves on indoors, at the king's table with his paper. Yoko Mishiro. Emira. Brida Ashwell on a stick. Bram Greymane, three fingers, in from the Stormfold road, who will not sit. Robin Ice, seventeen, riding as he does. Tabitha Hallenfeld, with her question written and dated. Miku Tenrai Moto, holding a chair at the table by right of the forfeiture at the Brine crossing. Three stones in the upper burying ground, Hild Ice, Lorn Stark and Osric of Hallenfeld, cut the same week to the same depth; a fourth, smaller, apart: Charles Lambert, Archbishop, nothing else.

**What is owed.**
- The four hundred and six (L014): the bill is on the table, as Lambert's paper. Signed, they are the circle's; refused, they stay under his seal.
- Borin's eleven days of forge-work on the crossing, still unpriced (L011).
- Kharven's grain debt to Stannvaard (L012).
- The outer town's season of winter wood, burned in a day on the king's asking and never repaid on the page (L050).
- Tabitha's question to Lambert, dated, refused twice (L017); Lambert cannot be thanked for the southern reach in public (L023).
- The refugee count, 4,200 to 3,900, entered as attrition on the road and recorded by nobody (L019).
- Renard Greymane holds a dead man's word that Lambert opened the west postern, a brass token and a sealed packet nobody has read (L015, L016). No record carries Renard past the ninth hour.
- Yoko's four lines from the night of the signing, which the Bench has never been permitted to see (L018).
- The Brine crossing reaches any house, court or column that heard the three depositions (L021); Bram's name rests on a word the Keth-Gorrum has not yet coined (L022).

**Fronts in play, by sign.**
- *The Accord circle:* Lambert's paper on the king's table, and the circle due to sit.
- *Tabitha Hallenfeld:* a question written down and dated in her own hand, with two refusals beside it.
- *Bram Greymane:* the Stormfold book with a dedication to his brother entered before his own name, and his brother's name missing from the roll beneath it.
- *Wren Greymane, the grief:* a Reckoner's entry, *ditch bank, entered by field observation, body not recovered*, and sixteen months of a word the ridge has not unsaid.
- *The Keth-Gorrum of Undaar-Keth:* Vresk Dokkan alive in the Expanse, answering to a name that does not exist yet.

**The warm bond.** Yoko Mishiro, nine years beside him; her hand on his arm at the narrows was the one thing that came in from outside (L069). Emira, who went looking eleven times for the place where her brother ended and the work began, and stopped (L070).

**Heat.** High (L067): the Brine crossing in three depositions, the Tenrai taken in against every officer's advice, Seiji's revocation sealed in a Reckoner's entry.

**Injuries and reserve.** Sodoku has not slept a full night since the ninth hour; Kurosetsu's remembering costs a vibration (L006). Bram: three fingers off at the second joint, the hip, the jaw scarred (L007). Brida: on a stick for a year from the first frost, her colour wrong for longer (L008). Vresk Dokkan: what Bram's eleven days took (L010).

**What he holds that nobody has checked.** He credits the man placed inside the Tenrai column to Sonzai, on no evidence the page gives, and nobody has heard him say it (L053).

**Fixed ahead.** The archive has already written the years this thread is walking into (*The Revolution of the Inner World*), and the Fronts arrive at these, never past them early:
- The circle takes a name in a room at Kharven-Seat: Whitemere, Emira's word for the mere under the ridge that ices white and does not freeze through. The Kharven never use it among themselves and call what comes down from the Seat the Stack.
- Eleven days later Stannvaard comes under the empire on a morning the forges were banked for a speech, and the empire is born there.
- Rail in the first year, the wire the year after; Robin Ice carries what cannot go on the wire from the second year.
- Bram stops the Ashenmoor spur in the fourth year with a chain and one word: "Mine."
- Through the sixth year Lambert has never answered Tabitha. The Ashwell Register prints her question in the sixth year's third month, and she did not give it to them.
- The temporal has given Miku nothing since the Brine crossing, and he has told no one.

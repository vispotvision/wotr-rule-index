# How other traditions build abilities and people, and what WOTR can take

*Research for the techniques and characters questionnaire, 2026-10-04. Sources: the live rule index (`out/rules.live.md`, the ability and combat law files), the style and combat decisions, the wiki mirror (the Living System parts on Levels, Gates and Traits; the card Hunter's Breath; Ara Min Mahuo's character card), and outside reading. `check_docket` shows nothing pending for magic-design, character-sheet or naming. WOTR figures are quoted from canon; figures about other works describe those works and propose nothing.*

---

## 0. Settled already: do not re-ask

**Design law (R47 and older).** The field format replaced the Design Chain (R47-2); an entry says what an ability is, never how to use it (R47-1); tells and counters are facts (R47-3); cost is a share of reserve off the Essence Ledger (R47-4); research first (R47-6); inherited Wellspring failures (R47-9); Sub-Stats are the caster's (R47-10); What nobody knows stays open (R47-12); the Effect is the mechanism playing out (R57-11). Cost, limit and counter are read off the mechanism (R17-3, R17-4); Trait, Skill and bloodline entries name what they act on (R17-5). Codex first, and no new Category until the list is shown not to cover it (R18-2). Character first: refusal, wound, conviction, then phenomenon (R7-1). Every technique was derived by somebody for a problem (R12-3-NAMED_INVENTOR_RULE).

**Combat Law (R71).** A vow that narrows a technique buys output through Attraction Force (the pull of the Crystal's Attraction Layer) by a figure set on its card; breaking it is a Shear Break (canon's Trait collapse); its terms are its counter; disclosure as a binding was not taken (R71-32). Traits named at first fire (R71-37); a Resonant Pair (the Part Nine bonus for two level Sub-Stats) under strain for the crisis only (R71-38); allied fusion only through carded Synergia, the joint-working Category (R71-39); a card's Readiness (casting-time) figure governs (R71-28); each card names its grammar (R71-69); calls as often as beats allow (R71-29); held reveals planted (R71-30); the soft Family chart (R71-31).

**Style Law (R70).** Numbered forms by school, four-syllable true names for halls and the Korean stratum, no brackets inside sentences (R70-74); numbers move only for a seen cause (R70-90); growth readouts (R70-84); breakthrough in the crisis (R70-91) and public re-assay (R70-92); commendations carry no stat effect (R70-96); contracts (R70-97); changes block per chapter (R70-98); arc shapes (R70-18); build talk (R70-93); comic voices and names (R70-113, R70-122).

**Naming and voice.** Two-word English names (R50-11), real Latin (R50-12), escalation suffix (R50-14), naming by culture (R50-10), school true names (R50-24), no self-translation (R8-26-NO_SELF_TRANSLATION); voice block (R52-03), contractions (R52-04), pronunciation line (R50-34), As Of line (R58-12); a new NPC is a role until he speaks twice (R50-27); Fracture of Worlds over workbook over card (R20C-32).

## 1. What WOTR already has to build with

A technique card is long and figure-heavy: Hunter's Breath runs about 1,300 words with two tables. A character card runs seventeen sections, Identity to Fracture Log, then Lore. Ara Min Mahuo's shows the shape: Catalyst Event, Level, Stage, Tier of Standing, a Path split (Spirit 40%, Body 40%, Attraction 20%), Typology, the eight Primaries, four Traits (one per Reflection), a Domain, a technique table, a psychology table (Core Wound, Flaw, Desire, Loop, Foil, Voice, Stress Default) and a Fracture Log.

The Trait law is the setting's strongest character engine; its epigraph: "Belief, once survived, becomes law" (the Trait System page). Four Reflections (Innate, Tempered, Lineage, Resonant), four formation events, three evolutions (Fusion, Overgrowth, Ascension), three risks (Resonance Fatigue, the Rigidity Effect, Shear Break). Paths (Body, Spirit, Attraction, Fate; R42-7) are recognised from behaviour and gate Sub-Stat components (R57-29). Experience pays novelty, decays on repetition, multiplies above Tier, pays oaths, and peaks at a Temperance Threshold.

What the traditions offer is tested habit for four open questions: how a limit is priced, how an ability is born and grows, how a person reaches paper fast, and what growth looks like across an arc.

## 2. The traditions

### 2.1 Naruto (Masashi Kishimoto): natures, seals and forbidden arts

**What it is.** Ninja work *jutsu* (techniques) by moulding *chakra* (a body-and-spirit energy). Two separate skills: shape transformation (form and motion) and nature transformation (one of five elements, fire, wind, lightning, earth, water, in a fixed cycle where each beats the next). Hand seals, twelve of them, gate casting; fewer seals is faster, and one-handed or sealless casting marks mastery. Jutsu carry letter ranks, E to S. *Kekkei genkai* are bloodline traits (the Sharingan eye, which reads and copies; the Byakugan, which sees nearly all round, with one small blind spot). *Hiden* are clan secret arts. *Kinjutsu* are forbidden arts whose cost is the user (the Eight Gates unlock the body's limiters one at a time and the eighth kills; the Reaper Death Seal costs the soul).

**Techniques worth taking.**
- *Shape and nature as two axes.* The Rasengan is pure shape; adding wind makes a new art that wounds its maker.
- *The bloodline's blind spot.* The Byakugan's gap is what R17-5-BLOODLINE_SCOPE asks for: what the faculty cannot resolve.
- *Self-harm as a ladder.* Each Gate opened is a visible step nearer death.
- *The copy problem.* A copying eye forces the question whether seeing is learning.

**Into WOTR.** Shape and nature is close to the Glyph Roles against the Wellspring: Boundary and Direction are shape, the Wellspring's Physics Domain is nature. The Gates are R55-1 (substance costs permanent and cumulative) given an ordinal face. Clan arts are documented techniques under R12-3: counterable by anyone who studied them, so a house that keeps its art secret keeps its counter secret.

**Collides with.** The elemental cycle is a hard type chart, and R71-31 took the soft Family chart instead (a counter raises price and lowers delivery; only the card's Counter makes a working fail). Seal counts are already answered by R71-28's Readiness figure and R20C-42's anchor rule (voice, hand, ink or blood below Refraction). Copying is unruled: R47-10 puts governing Sub-Stats with the practitioner and Path gates bind components, so a copier would at best hold a technique at his own stats, but nothing says whether watching is learning.

### 2.2 Hunter x Hunter, HxH (Yoshihiro Togashi): categories, conditions and vows

**What it is.** *Nen* is life energy trained through four basics; *Hatsu* is the personal ability each user designs. Six categories sit on a hexagon (Enhancement, Transmutation, Emission, Conjuration, Manipulation, Specialisation), and a user's efficiency falls with distance from his own category (Togashi gives 100, 80, 60, 40 percent). The category is found by a test: leaves on a glass of water, watched as the user flares his aura. Personality predicts category. Abilities are bound by *conditions* and *vows*: Kurapika's chain may be used only on one gang, on pain of his death, and is far stronger for it. Some abilities require the target to be told the rules first (the bomber Genthru's Countdown). Nen can persist and strengthen after its user's death.

**Techniques worth taking.**
- *The diagnostic assay.* A physical test tells a user what he is: scene, rite and reveal at once.
- *Conditions as design material.* Togashi's best abilities are mostly conditions (what must be true, said, touched), which are both cost and counter.
- *Strength by narrowness.* The tighter and riskier the vow, the larger the gain.

**Into WOTR.** The hexagon is already on WOTR cards: Path percentages (Ara's 40/40/20) are the same idea, a person spread across planes with real consequences at the gates. The water test is a re-assay rite (R70-92) or a printing instrument (R70-77) at Awakening. Personality to category is R7-1. Conditions are what R17-3-LIMIT_MECHANICAL asks for when they have a mechanical source. The vow is settled in principle by R71-32.

**Collides with.** The hexagon percentages would be invented numbers (R12-5) unless canon supplies them; WOTR's Path split is canon on each card, but no rule converts distance between Paths into efficiency. R71-32 leaves the size of a vow's gain to "a figure you set on that card", with no scale for setting it, and does not say whether a vow can carry a death penalty beyond Shear Break, can be sworn mid-fight, or must be witnessed (Fracture of Worlds pays XP for an oath "before a Witness Stone"). Disclosure as a condition was not taken (R71-32's notes), so a Genthru ability is not available. Posthumous strengthening meets the Necrocursica's residues and the Volitional Trace (R61-4): a dead man's working that grows needs a ruling.

### 2.3 Jujutsu Kaisen, JJK (Gege Akutami): innate techniques and binding vows

**What it is.** Sorcerers are born with an innate *cursed technique* engraved in the body; training extends it but does not choose it. *Binding vows* come in two kinds: self-imposed (Nanami limits his effort to working hours and is stronger after them; revealing your technique to the opponent raises its output) and contracts between two people, which punish the breaker. *Heavenly Restriction* is a congenital trade: Toji has no cursed energy and a superhuman body; Mechamaru has a crippled body and enormous range. *Extension techniques* grow branches from the innate core; *reversal* runs the energy positive to heal. Ranks run Grade 4 to Special Grade.

**Techniques worth taking.**
- *One core, many branches.* Every named move derives from one innate law.
- *Two-party vows.* A promise with a mechanical penalty is both plot and relationship.
- *The congenital trade.* Less in one place, more in another: a build with an obvious counter.

**Into WOTR.** One core, many branches is the Wellspring: a practitioner's techniques share a Physics Domain, and R47-9 makes them share its failures. Two-party vows already exist as canon texture (Benediction's Grace-Bound Trait on Ara's card; Mu-jin's teaching vow, R68-5). Reversal for healing is Vitalia, priced by R47-11 (mending always costs more). Grades map to Tiers of Standing.

**Collides with.** WOTR has no innate technique: R12-3 says every technique was derived by somebody, and a Trait awakens from an event. The Lineage Reflection covers bloodline inheritance, so the question is whether a Lineage Trait may arrive as a working technique or only as a faculty. Heavenly Restriction meets R39-4 (no Sub-Stat past its Stage's Max Grade except under strain) and the Path gates; Orin Farrant's Class Ø (R67-2), read only by instrument, is the nearest canon. Akutami's Black Flash, a chance amplified hit, meets R57-10 (fights never rigged) and R13-8 (reconstructible outcomes). The JJK Domain as a called technique was answered by R71-45.

### 2.4 Bleach (Tite Kubo): the named soul in the sword, and numbered spells

**What it is.** A Soul Reaper's *zanpakutō* is a sword that is also a soul with a name and a personality mirroring its owner's. The owner learns its name in meditation, then calls the first release, *shikai*, with a command phrase and the name. The second release, *bankai*, needs the spirit made visible and subdued, about ten years' training; a broken bankai is broken for good. *Kidō* are spells in two numbered schools (destruction and binding, 1 to 99); each has an incantation, skipping it costs power, and masters chain two incantations at once.

**Techniques worth taking.**
- *The release phrase.* The command before the name is characterisation in one line.
- *Power gated by a relationship.* The second release is earned from a weapon that can refuse.
- *Incantation against speed.* A short chant costs output; a full one buys it.

**Into WOTR.** The release phrase and call are R8-24 (a beat for output), settled. Numbered spells are R70-74's numbered forms. Incantation for output is the chant (R50-26) under Gnosis Fluency. The souled weapon has two WOTR homes: Animatria (a shape that can "obey or strain against instruction", R10-3) and the summon register's bonds.

**Collides with.** No rule says whether skipping or shortening a chant trades output for Readiness, or by what figure. An item with its own will has no field on the item card (R70-95) or in the item Tiers. A permanently broken art meets R55-1 cleanly. The ten-year bankai is an arc length, which R70-90 allows only as named downtime.

### 2.5 Cradle (Will Wight): Paths, aspects and the revelation

**What it is.** Sacred artists cultivate *madra* (soul power) with an *aspect* (fire, blood, sword, hunger). A *Path* is a complete style: an aspect, a cycling method, and techniques of fixed kinds: Striker (projectile), Ruler (control of the world), Forger (made objects), Enforcer (self-empowerment), and Cycling (circulation). Ranks climb from Copper through Gold and Underlord to Sage and Monarch. Iron grows a body suited to the Path; Gold grows a *Goldsign*, a visible physical mark of power. Underlord needs a *revelation*, a true statement about who you are, which reforges body and soul; Sages touch *Icons*, concepts the world obeys.

**Techniques worth taking.**
- *Technique kinds as a fixed menu.* Five words let a reader parse any new technique.
- *The Path manual.* A Path is a written document with known counters; a self-made one is a dangerous novelty.
- *Advancement by self-knowledge.* The big rank is a sentence the character must believe and survive.

**Into WOTR.** The revelation is the Trait's Temperance Insight and R70-91's breakthrough inside its crisis, almost exactly. The Goldsign is Pack Ten's Traitus shape (a standing mark of what the Crystal has become, R10-3-TRAITUS_SHAPE). Icons are Trait Ascension into Domain Law. Path manuals are R12-3's documented techniques. The kinds map loosely onto Pack Ten's Categories and R8-14's Class line (Offensive, Defensive, Supplementary).

**Collides with.** **The word.** WOTR's Path already means Body, Spirit, Attraction or Fate; a Cradle-style school must be called a school or a discipline (R50-24). WOTR has no fixed technique-kind menu on the card beyond Class and Category, so whether to add one is open.

### 2.6 Unbound and The Beginning After the End

**Unbound (Nicoli Gonnella).** Felix, carried from Earth to a world run by a System, levels skills by use; eating monsters through a Gourmand skill feeds growth; his *Unbound* nature lets him break the System's limits. Skills rise through tiers and are later woven into *Skill Arrays* (groups that work as one); foundational *Pillars* must be woven before a Grandmaster tier; Titles grant stats. It is already the model for build talk (R70-93).

- *Take:* foundations required before a threshold; techniques grouped into one structure; growth by use.
- *Into WOTR:* Pillars match Temperance Threshold Events (Part Eight) and the Stage ladder on the card; Arrays suggest a card field for techniques that share a root and a reserve.
- *Collides with:* Titles that grant stats against R70-96 (commendations carry no stat effect). Breaking the System's limits meets R56-4 (past a gate with standing strain) and R57-13 (a card may sit off its ladder either way). Skill levels by use are a second progression axis WOTR does not have; its Skills (R17-5-SKILL_SCOPE) carry no level.

**The Beginning After the End (TurtleMe).** A reincarnated king grows a *mana core* through colours (black, red, orange, yellow, silver, white), each split into dark, solid and light shades, then *Integration*. Mages are augmenters (body) or conjurers (spells); elements have rarer *deviant* forms. An absorbed beast's will unlocks in phases.

- *Take:* sub-steps inside a rank; deviant forms of an element; phased inherited powers.
- *Into WOTR:* the shades are the Level inside a Stage; augmenter against conjurer is Body Path against Spirit Path; deviant elements are a Wellspring's secondaries; the beast will is a Lineage Trait or a bonded summon.
- *Collides with:* a core read by colour is a visible aura, which R70-69 and the combat law's settled list bar ("no visible coloured aura"); colour may live only in an instrument's print.

### 2.7 LitRPG skill trees and evolutions

**What it is.** In the genre (fiction written as if the world ran on a game's rules) skills are granted, levelled and evolved. *The Primal Hunter* (Zogarth) upgrades skills by rarity, Inferior to Legendary and beyond, at class evolutions. *The Wandering Inn* (pirateaba) grants Skills in a dream-voice at level-up, often as surprises, written in brackets. *Dungeon Crawler Carl* (Matt Dinniman) hands out achievements and loot boxes in a sardonic System voice. *Solo Leveling* gives daily quests with penalties and a job-change trial. *Reverend Insanity* (Gu Zhen Ren) makes abilities living Gu insects that must be fed, ranked 1 to 9, combined into *killer moves*. *Mother of Learning* (nobody103) treats spells as learned formulas executed by a shaping skill trained in drills.

**Techniques worth taking.** The evolution choice (two branches offered, one taken); the trial that gates a class; upkeep (a power that must be fed); the combo as its own art; drills as the substance of skill.

**Into WOTR.** Evolution choices fit Trait Fusion and the escalation suffix (R50-14). The class trial is the Temperance Threshold. Upkeep fits Draftcraft stock and the summon register. Drills fit R70-18's training montage and Mother of Learning's model (K8 of the combat law).

**Collides with.** A System that grants a technique as a gift breaks R12-3 (derived by somebody for a problem) and NATALIE's "everything came from somewhere traceable". Brackets inside sentences were not taken (R70-74). A solo combo of one man's own techniques is not covered: R71-39 rules allied fusion only. Rarity ladders would be a second ladder beside Stage and Grade; R47-7 keeps rungs by name.

### 2.8 Tabletop design

**D&D (fifth edition).** Classes and subclasses fix a build's frame; *feats* are chosen options with prerequisites. Spells list casting time, range, components (V, S, M: verbal, somatic, material) and duration; *concentration* allows one sustained spell, broken by damage.
- *Into WOTR:* components are R20C-42's anchors (voice, hand, ink or blood); concentration is R10-3's craft durability (Spellcraft holds while attention holds); the spell block is R47-2's field format.
- *Collides with:* feats are chosen, and WOTR Traits are awakened by events, never picked (the Trait System). Sub-Stat points are chosen; Traits are not.

**GURPS (Steve Jackson Games).** Point-buy: advantages cost points; *enhancements* and *limitations* change that cost by percentages (a limitation valued by how often it bites: rare conditions are cheap, common ones dear), with total reduction capped at 80 percent. Disadvantages give points back; *Vow* is one, priced minor, major or great by how much it restricts life.
- *Into WOTR:* this is the most usable precedent for R71-32's open figure. GURPS answers "how much is a narrowing worth" by frequency and severity of denial, and caps the gain.
- *Collides with:* WOTR has no point economy for flaws; a Flaw on the card buys nothing. Any percentage would be invented until Isaac sets the scale.

**Fate Core (Evil Hat).** A character is a handful of *aspects*, short true phrases: a High Concept, a Trouble, and others. An aspect is *invoked* for a bonus or *compelled* by the game master for a complication, and a compel pays the player. *Stunts* bend one rule. Advancement comes at story *milestones*. Creation runs a *phase trio*: each player's backstory guest-stars two others.
- *Into WOTR:* the Trait's Rigidity Effect is a compel in all but name; milestones are R70-90; the phase trio is the character-lore skill's Ties and The Web of Lives.
- *Collides with:* a compel on Isaac's player character would act for him (R49-01-WHOSE_HEAD). Rigidity may bind NPCs on the page, but for Sodoku Moto and the other creators' characters it can only be a fact on the card for their creators to play.

**Mutants and Masterminds, M&M (Green Ronin).** Powers are *effects* (Damage, Affliction, Concealment) with ranks; *descriptors* (fire, ice) are flavour on a shared mechanic; *extras* and *flaws* change per-rank cost; *arrays* let several powers share one pool; a *power level* caps attack against effect so a build trades accuracy for force. *Complications* pay hero points when they hurt.
- *Into WOTR:* the power-level trade matches Stage caps and Max Grade; arrays match techniques sharing one Wellspring and one reserve.
- *Collides with:* descriptors as flavour are exactly what R57-11 bans (no Effect another mechanism could produce) and R17-3 (a swappable cost is decoration).

**Burning Wheel (Luke Crane).** A character carries three *Beliefs* (stated convictions that drive play and earn rewards when pursued or tested), *Instincts* (if-then reflexes) and *Traits*.
- *Into WOTR:* Beliefs are NATALIE's costly belief; Instincts are the card's Stress Default.

### 2.9 Sanderson's laws of magic

**What it is.** Brandon Sanderson's essays: the First Law (magic can solve a problem only as far as the reader understands it), the Second, "Limitations > Powers" in his shorthand, the Third (expand what you have before adding something new), and a Zeroth (err on the side of awesome). His Mistborn system splits one metal set three ways: Allomancy burns metals for effect, Feruchemy stores an attribute now to spend later, Hemalurgy steals powers with spikes at a loss.

**Into WOTR.** The First Law is R70-71 (rules shown beforehand) and R71-30 (planted signs). The Second is R12-1 and R17-3. The Third is R18-2-NO_NEW_CATEGORY_WITHOUT_CHECK almost word for word. Allomancy's one-metal, one-effect discipline matches a Wellspring under one glyph.

**Collides with.** Feruchemy's storage meets R2-6's replenishment and currency rules (no consumable restores a reserve; Essence and time do not exchange). Hemalurgic theft meets R47-10 (stats are the caster's own). Both would need a ruling, not a design.

### 2.10 Character-creation traditions

**The RPG sheet.** Stats, skills, equipment, background: fast and thin on motive. WOTR's card already exceeds it.

**The novelist's character bible.** Lajos Egri's three dimensions (body, social standing, psychology); the screenwriter's *want* (what he pursues) and *need* (what would actually heal him); the *ghost* or wound (the past event that set him wrong); K. M. Weiland's *Lie the character believes* and her three arc kinds: positive (he drops the lie), flat (he holds a truth and changes the world), negative (he falls further in, or into corruption).
- *Into WOTR:* the card's Core Wound is the ghost; Desire is the want; Loop and Flaw are the lie in motion. The card carries no Need and no arc kind.
- *Collides with:* "the lie" already means two things at the table: the lie an NPC tells (Table Rule 8; the wotr-npc skill's want, refusal, lie) and the lie a character believes. A card field would need a distinct name.

**The voice sheet.** Speech patterns, verbal tics, what the character never says. WOTR's voice block (R52-03) is a stricter form, and R52-02 puts mind and sound in equal measure.

## 3. Where the traditions agree, and where WOTR stands apart

1. **Narrowness pays** (HxH, JJK, GURPS, Sanderson). WOTR ruled the principle (R71-32), not the price.
2. **A power has one root** (JJK's core, Cradle's Path, M&M's array). WOTR's root is the Wellspring; R47-9 shares its failures.
3. **Birth or making.** WOTR sits on the making side (R12-3); the Lineage Reflection is its one door to birth.
4. **Rank shows** (Goldsigns, core colours). WOTR shows it in the room (R70-69), bars coloured auras, keeps Traitus marks conditional.
5. **The big step is a crisis or a truth.** R70-91 and Temperance Insight already say so.
6. **Creation ties people together** (Fate's phase trio). WOTR has Ties and Foil, but no rule requires a new card to touch others.
7. **WOTR is stricter than all of them:** no granted techniques, no flavour descriptors, no invented numbers, no hard type chart, no stat-granting titles. Every import passes those gates.

## 4. Open questions the questionnaire could put

**Technique cards**
1. How R71-32's vow figure is set: by frequency and severity of the narrowing (the GURPS model), by fixed bands Isaac names once, or by Isaac per card; and whether the gain has a ceiling.
2. What a vow may stake beyond Shear Break: a life (Kurapika), a limb or Crystal mass under R55-1, or nothing beyond Shear Break.
3. Whether a vow must be sworn before a witness (the Witness Stone), may be sworn mid-fight, and whether two-party vows between characters are their own card.
4. Whether a shortened chant or a dropped anchor trades output for Readiness, and how that is shown on the card.
5. Whether watching a technique can teach it (the copy problem), and at whose stats it then runs.
6. Whether a man's own techniques may combine into a carded composite art (the killer move), beside R71-39's allied Synergia.
7. Whether cards gain a technique-kind line (Cradle's Striker, Ruler, Forger, Enforcer) or keep Class and Category alone.
8. Whether forbidden arts get a register of their own with permanent costs (the Gates model), beyond the Mortalis Categories.
9. Whether a weapon may carry a will and its own card (the zanpakutō), and under which Category.

**Traits and inheritance**
10. Whether a Lineage Trait may arrive as a working technique (JJK's innate technique) or only as a faculty.
11. Whether a congenital trade (Heavenly Restriction, Class Ø) is a legal build, and how it meets Max Grade and the Path gates.
12. How the Rigidity Effect binds: on NPCs as a compel Natalie plays; on Isaac's characters only as a fact on the card.
13. How Trait Fusion, Overgrowth and Ascension reach the page and the card, and whether an evolution offers a branch choice.

**Naming forms**
14. Who names a self-derived technique: its inventor, his school, or the first witnesses, as with Third Names.
15. Whether a release phrase before the name is a card field (the shikai command).
16. Whether technique names take the Accord Latin and common-tongue doublet that R50-23 gives schools.
17. How many forms a school's numbered ladder runs, and whether the top form is withheld from the school's own records.

**Character and NPC cards**
18. The ladder from role to roster entry to short record to full card: what each rung must carry and what lifts a character a rung.
19. Whether the card gains a Need, a believed lie (under a name other than "lie") and an arc kind (positive, flat, negative, corruption).
20. Whether a new card must tie into at least two existing cards (the phase trio), and whether the Foil must be carded.
21. How an NPC's stats are built fast: a full Sub-Stat spend, a Stage-and-role template read off the workbook, or a stat line only until he fights.
22. Whether a diagnostic assay rite at Awakening tells a character his Family and Path lean on the page (the water test).

**Progression arcs**
23. Whether non-magical Skills level by use (Unbound) or stay unlevelled beside the Sub-Stats.
24. Whether a Stage carries required foundations before its Threshold (Unbound's Pillars) recorded on the card's ladder.
25. How Part Ten's stagnation and diminishing returns show on the page across a long arc.
26. How Path commitment moving with behaviour is tracked and shown (the Path split changing on the card).
27. Whether NPC rivals grow offscreen on the Fronts' clocks, and how their growth is shown when they return.

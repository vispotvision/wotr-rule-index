## Voice
**Notices first** · What each man in front of him will believe, and whose hand signed what. He reads a room for the face it wants from him and puts that face on before he speaks.
**Sentence length** · Medium, even and pleasant. He lays out an arrangement clause by clause, then closes it with two short maxims; asked a question he does not mean to answer, he answers pleasantly and at length and says nothing.
**Contractions** · never. The Tenrai court speaks in whole words, and his warmth is built out of them.
**Pet word** · "Please," even inside an order that ends a village.
**Never says** · Where the boy is kept, what his design has cost in lives, or anything his face has not already agreed to. He never raises his voice. The only numbers he gives are his own arrangements (riders, routes, reports), read off like a manifest; the dead he does not count.
**Stumbles** · Never while the smile is working. When it fails, the sentence breaks apart with it.
**Gloss rights** · Never.
**Under stress** · The smile stays on too long and the courtesy keeps going after the room has turned; in real terror the words shorten and break into pleading with a lord's honorific on the end.
**In grief** · He is sixteen again, the day his father died at the settlement, and he falls back on his father's way: the face goes blank and he says nothing at all.
**In joy** · Nothing changes, because the warm smile is always there; real pleasure shows only in the hands, a pen set down with care, a letter sealed slowly.
**Sample line** · "They will believe two of them, which is better. A man who believes everything reconsiders. A man who believes half of it commits."

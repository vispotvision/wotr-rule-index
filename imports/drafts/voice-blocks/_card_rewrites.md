# Card rewrites: Sodoku, Gimbzo, Verinus

Lines on the current cards (wiki mirror, `wiki/Volume I — Character Cards/`) that contradict the Voice Law rulings, with the replacement text. Each card also takes its new `## Voice` block from this folder; where the block and an old line would disagree, the old line is replaced as below. Nothing here has been applied.

## Sodoku Moto (`Sodoku Moto.md`)

Ruling: talks as played, full formal sentences, no contractions, no small talk (R52-13); jokes only by accident (R52-14).

**`Sodoku Moto.md:137`** (the Voice row of the Psychology table)

Current:
> | **Voice** | **Slow iron cadence. Silences that count. Moves before he decides to.** Says little — already ranked before it leaves him. **Processes rooms rather than scanning them.** Under stress goes quieter, not louder, *and reaches for the beads when no one is watching* |

What contradicts: "Says little — already ranked before it leaves him." He talks at length in full sentences; the ranking shows in what he says, not in how little. The em dash also fails clean publishing.

Replacement:
> | **Voice** | **Slow iron cadence. Full, formal sentences, never a contraction, never small talk. Moves before he decides to.** When he speaks he says the whole of it, already ranked before it leaves him. **Processes rooms rather than scanning them.** He never means a joke; now and then a flat truth lands as one and he does not notice. Under stress goes quieter and shorter, not louder, *and reaches for the beads when no one is watching* |

"Silences that count" is dropped because it described a sparse talker; his silences survive in the block's grief slot. "Moves before he decides to" is a body note and stays.

No other line on the card contradicts: `Sodoku Moto.md:182` and `:200` quote him in complete, uncontracted sentences, which is the ruled voice.

## Gimbzo (`Gimbzo.md`)

Ruling: a loose talker, an easy, rambling old master who goes silent only on what matters; the card changes to match the scenes (R52-18).

The card has no voice field. The sparse impression comes from one line, and from the card never showing him talk except in single sentences.

**`Gimbzo.md:148`**

Current:
> Over nine months he put all seven Works into Darius, with the matrix under them. He spent four of those months configuring the Fist of God, and said one sentence about it at the end of the fourth and never another. Twice Darius asked him to file it. Twice he refused: a thing worked wholly inside a body, he said, needs no register to tell it where it lives.

What contradicts: read alone, "said one sentence about it ... and never another" makes him a man of single sentences. Under the ruling the silence is the exception, the one subject that matters, and the line has to say so.

Replacement:
> Over nine months he put all seven Works into Darius, with the matrix under them. He talked through most of those months, easily and at length, about arches and loads, the ash, the wood and wars nobody had written down. He spent four of the months configuring the Fist of God, and about that he said one sentence at the end of the fourth and never another. Twice Darius asked him to file it. Twice he refused: a thing worked wholly inside a body, he said, needs no register to tell it where it lives.

**Add** a `## Voice` section to the card (the block in `Gimbzo.md` in this folder), placed after `## VI · Lore and Trajectory`'s card fields and before `### Lore · The Life Behind the Card` (after `Gimbzo.md:122`).

Not a contradiction, left alone: `Gimbzo.md:113` ("He is still working.") is Bara's sentence, not Gimbzo's. `Gimbzo.md:16` (the card's epigraph) is short, but three short sentences at a swing is the silence-on-what-matters beat.

## Verinus VII (`Verinus VII · The Palatine.md`)

Ruling: Verinus is a speech-maker; his card is rewritten to match the scenes (R52-19). Stress shortens speech in every named human, and the card's own tell rides on the shortening (R52-25).

**`Verinus VII · The Palatine.md:197`**

Current:
> **Voice / Dialect:** Concord Common, coastal cadence surviving under forty-five years of court register. **Short sentences. Frequent questions that he does not then answer.** He never raises his voice, because inside the Sounding volume does nothing, and this has spread outward until the entire capital speaks quietly and nobody there can explain why. Occupational metaphor is nautical and specifically about depth: soundings, drift, ballast, the lead line, what a thing draws.

What contradicts: "Short sentences. Frequent questions that he does not then answer." On the page he makes speeches and almost never asks.

Replacement:
> **Voice / Dialect:** Concord Common, coastal cadence surviving under forty-five years of court register. **A speech-maker: long, built sentences, whole paragraphs in one breath, closing on a line short enough to carry out of the room.** He never raises his voice, because inside the Sounding volume does nothing, and this has spread outward until the entire capital speaks quietly and nobody there can explain why. Occupational metaphor is nautical and specifically about depth: soundings, drift, ballast, the lead line, what a thing draws.

**`Verinus VII · The Palatine.md:198`**

Current:
> **Dialogue Evolution:** At twenty-six he argued. At forty he drafted. **Now he asks a question and lets the room do the arithmetic**, and people who have not met him before consistently mistake the first two minutes for weakness.

What contradicts: "Now he asks a question and lets the room do the arithmetic."

Replacement:
> **Dialogue Evolution:** At twenty-six he argued. At forty he drafted. **Now he speaks, at length and at the same low level, until the room has nowhere left to stand**, and people who have not met him before consistently mistake the first two minutes for weakness.

**`Verinus VII · The Palatine.md:201`**

Current:
> **Stress Default:** He stops asking questions and begins answering them, precisely and at length, **and everyone who has worked with him for more than a year knows that this is the sound of a man who has decided something and is very frightened of it.**

What contradicts: "precisely and at length". Under stress his speech shortens, and the tell (answers, never questions; the sound of a frightened decision) rides on the short form. "Stops asking questions" also presumes the questioning default that line 197 no longer has.

Replacement:
> **Stress Default:** The speeches stop. He goes short and declarative and every sentence is an answer, **and everyone who has worked with him for more than a year knows that this is the sound of a man who has decided something and is very frightened of it.**

Kept as is: `Verinus VII · The Palatine.md:199` (the three signature lines) are short aphorisms and sit fine inside a speech-maker's voice as the lines he closes on; `:238` is already a speech. `:103` ("A prepared speech weighs almost nothing regardless of its truth") is a property of the Sounding, not of his voice; it stands, and it means his speeches weigh only what he has paid into them, which the scenes already show.

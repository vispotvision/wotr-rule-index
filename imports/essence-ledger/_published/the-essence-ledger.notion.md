> Four quantities run through every practitioner in the Continuum: a reserve in EU, a compression in EU/g, an output rate in AU/s, an efficiency η. The Grade ladder measures destruction in joules. This Part is the join. **One Essence Unit is one megajoule.** What follows is what that buys on the ladder, what a reserve costs to spend, how long it lasts at full output, how fast it comes back, and what can be done about all of it from the other side of a fight.

---

## §1 · The four quantities, and the fifth

**EU (Essence Units)** is the raw reserve: the total volume of usable Essence stored in the Crystal at any moment. It scales with Temperance Stage and is governed primarily by **Tempering Yield**. Maximum EU is the structural ceiling the Crystal refills to. Operating below **ten percent EU** produces Essence Starvation: reduced stat expression, Shell degradation, Trait flickering.

**Flux Density (EU/g)** is the compression ratio, how much Essence is packed per unit of Crystal mass. Higher means greater output from the same reserve. It is governed by **Tempering Clarity** and **Ardency Compression**, and it is what separates a practitioner who burns through their reserve in three techniques from one who fights for an hour on the same pool.

**AU/s (Aether Units per second)** is the output rate, how much Essence converts into active expression per second of sustained operation. It is governed by **Ardency Flux**, **Ardency Alacrity** and **Tempering Yield**, and it follows the formula **AU/s = Flux Density × η**.

**Eta (η), Efficiency** is the ratio of Essence spent to Essence that arrives as intended effect. At η 0.50, half of every expenditure is wasted as heat, noise and structural bleed. At η 0.90, ninety percent converts to clean output. It is the single most important variable in sustained combat, because it determines how long the reserve lasts under continuous output. It is governed by **Aether Class**, **Tempering Clarity** and **Ardency Compression**.

**The fifth quantity is the one Part Eleven already measures**, and it is the one that lets the other four be priced at all. Strike Force is the destructive energy delivered per discrete attack action, recorded in **Newtons** for peak contact force and in **Joules** for total energy yield. The Continuum already keeps its books in joules. The Ledger is not a new currency; it is the exchange rate between the two sets of books.

**The direction of the conversion.** EU is *potential* and AU is *actual*: what the Crystal holds against what leaves it. One AU is one EU crossing the Shell. The two are never the same number in practice, because one is the store, one is the flow, and η is the loss across the boundary between them.

> **The Aristotelian reading, in-world.** The Collegium tradition that descends from the old faculty of Causes reads the Ledger as the doctrine of *potency and act*: EU is what a Crystal is able to become, AU is what it has become, η is the fraction of the becoming that arrives. Their term for Essence Starvation is *the exhausted potency*, and their objection to the whole Aether-Flow apparatus is that it measures the act and infers the potency, which they hold is backwards. **They are a school, not a mechanic.** The numbers below are what happens; the Causes faculty is one of several arguments about what the numbers mean, carried the way the Four Theories of Part Fifteen are carried.

## §2 · The constant: what one EU is worth

### 2.1 The constant

**1 EU = 1 MJ of potential. Energy delivered = EU × η × 1 MJ. 1 AU/s = 1 MW.**

The Ledger converts at one megajoule everywhere. Every joule, watt, ton of TNT, drain time and Starvation floor in this Part is at that constant, and there is no second rate for any register, Path or Stage.

### 2.2 The chain, end to end

EU → joules → Grade → TNT → what it wrecks, with 1 t TNT ≡ 4.184 × 10⁹ J (the thermochemical definition, so the arithmetic is exact rather than measured):

joules delivered = EU spent × η × 1,000,000 Grade            = the Part Four row whose attack-output band holds it tons TNT         = joules ÷ 4.184 × 10⁹

Worked once, on Dougou Ozumu Zettari's Kokushin Gyūha, the apex branch of The Iron Tree. It costs 7,400 EU exactly, and Dougou works at η 0.99:

7,400 EU × 1,000,000 J/EU          = 7.40 GJ of potential × η 0.99                           = 7.33 GJ delivered Part Four: 1.046×10⁹ – 4.6024×10¹⁰ = B-Grade, mid-band ÷ 4.184 × 10⁹                      = 1.75 tons of TNT Part Four's gloss for B            : Building to Large Building, 0.25 to 11 tons TNT

Run the chain forwards from the cost, never backwards from a description. The EU figure and the practitioner's η fix the joules, and the joules fix the Grade.

## §4 · Reserve bands by Stage and by Tier of Standing

### 4.1 The law of reserves

A reserve is set by **Level** and capped by **Stage**. Level sets it along a log-linear law; the Band gates, which a Stage must pass before its Level can climb, cap it.

The law runs through three fixed reference points: Ara Min Mahuo at Level 250 with 4,200,000 EU, Borin Ironheart at Level 378 with 180,000,000 EU, and Kwon Mu-jin at Level 430 with 850,000,000 EU. Between the first two the slope is **0.012750 decades per Level**; between the second two it is **0.012964**. The two segments agree to 1.7 percent, and across the whole span:

log₁₀ EU = 3.4202 + 0.012812 × Level

a reserve multiplies by ten every 78.1 Levels, and by about 26 across each hundred-Level Band.

Kwon Hae-ryu's reserve is set by this law: 90,000,000 EU at Level 355, Stage X Realization, Band IV.

> **The Pythagorean reading, in-world.** The Measurewrights' old Harmonic faculty never accepted the Level axis and reads the same regularity as a ratio: each Band multiplies the reserve by a fixed interval, so the five Bands are five octaves of one string and the Crystal is a monochord being stopped shorter. They tune reserve gauges to intervals rather than to numbers and they are, in practice, no worse at it. **A school, not a mechanic.** The gauge reads what the arithmetic above says it reads, and the Harmonic faculty's error is in what they think it proves.

### 4.2 The bands

Two things bind a Stage's reserve, and they are different in kind.

**The gate ceiling, which is hard.** Part One sets four gates and states them as absolutes: no character can breach a Band ceiling without first passing the Temperance Threshold that cluster demands, and the System halts the level counter and builds Residual Strain until the Threshold is passed or the character fractures. Stage IV must be reached before Level 100 can be surpassed, Stage VII before Level 200, Stage X before Level 300, Stage XII before Level 400. Read the other way round: a Stage III practitioner cannot be above Level 100, a Stage VI cannot be above 200, a Stage IX cannot be above 300, a Stage XI cannot be above 400. Through the law of reserves that is a **hard ceiling on the reserve**.

## §5 · AU/s as power: how long the output holds

### 5.1 The drain

AU/s is a *rate*: how much Essence converts into active expression per second of sustained operation, and the variable that determines how long the reserve lasts under continuous output. One AU is one EU crossing the Shell, so a second at full AU/s costs exactly AU/s worth of EU.

t_empty         = EU reserve ÷ (AU/s)                  seconds t_starvation    = 0.9 × EU reserve ÷ (AU/s)            seconds, to the ten-percent floor power drawn     = AU/s × 1 MW power delivered = AU/s × η × 1 MW waste           = (1 − η) × AU/s × 1 MW

**One combat turn is six seconds.** Divide either clock by six and it reads in turns: a practitioner whose reserve holds twenty-one seconds at full output has three and a half turns of it.

The drain times are a property of the two figures a practitioner carries, reserve and AU/s, and of one AU being one EU. The power and the waste are at the constant.

### 5.4 What AU/s is not

AU/s is a **ceiling on the rate**, not a duty cycle. No practitioner runs at their AU/s continuously, and sustained work runs at a fraction of it. Yukazuri's Somnalis Drift costs 390 EU, is a full-broadcast sympathetic suppression across twenty-five feet, and lasts eight seconds: 390 ÷ 8 = 48.75 AU/s, one tenth of her ceiling. The ceiling is what she can do for a heartbeat; the tenth is what she does for eight seconds. **Read a practitioner's AU/s as the number that caps a burst, and read a working's EU ÷ duration as the number that runs a scene.**

### 5.5 Costs are a share of the reserve

A working is priced as a share of the reserve it draws on, and the shares are large enough that a real fight reaches the ten-percent floor. Yukazuri's workings run 4.5 to 12.6 percent of her reserve apiece. Dougou's branches run 3.0 to 9.7 percent, and the apex strike 8.0. Kwon Mu-jin's constructs run 5 to 15 percent. **A practitioner who commits to a fight spends in whole percents per exchange, and ten to twenty committed exchanges bring anyone to Starvation.** The absolute size of the reserve decides how large each working is; the share decides how long the practitioner lasts, and the share is what a scene tracks.

The cost can be permanent. Borin Ironheart forfeits a permanent portion of his own reserve for each Requiem he performs. He has performed seven, each took something that did not grow back, and his maximum reserve was higher before the first.

## §6 · Flux Density and η

### 6.1 The equation, read per working gram

**AU/s = Flux Density × η.** The equation governs: a practitioner's output rate is their Flux Density multiplied by their efficiency.

AU/s is Essence per *second* and Flux Density is Essence per *gram*, so the equation is read **per working gram per second**: the gram of Crystal engaged at the working aperture in each second of output. The gram goes unwritten because it is always one. It is not the whole Crystal. Draen Varos's Forge-Heart is nine kilograms of Crystal and slag that reads as one structure, at a Flux Density of 670 million EU/g and η 0.61; his output is 408,700,000 AU/s, drawn through one gram of it at a time.

### 6.2 Where the waste goes, and why it cannot go through the body

The missing fraction of every expenditure is wasted as **heat, noise and structural bleed**. That list is correct physics. The first law says the missing fraction cannot vanish; the second says it cannot come back as work; the Gouy–Stodola relation (Ẇ_lost = T₀ · Ṡ_gen) says it reappears as low-grade heat at the place the loss happened. Light and sound are ways out of that place, not alternatives to it, and each becomes heat where it is absorbed.

**It cannot be generated inside the body and conducted out.** Fourier's law, q = −k∇T. Yukazuri's 52.8 MW of waste through 1.8 m² of skin is 29.3 MW/m²; across 3 mm of skin at k ≈ 0.4 W·m⁻¹·K⁻¹ that would need

ΔT = qL/k = 2.93 × 10⁷ × 0.003 / 0.4 = 2.2 × 10⁵ K

across three millimetres, and she is the smallest output in the set. At Dougou's 39.0 MW it is 1.63 × 10⁵ K; at Kwon Mu-jin's 204 GW it is 8.5 × 10⁸ K. Skin chars at about 200 °C.

**So the Soul Crystal is not a battery in the chest. It is an aperture, and the waste is shed at the Shell, at or outside the body's surface.** The body does not carry the heat of its own workings; the Shell does, and the Shell sheds it into the room. Anryū Ichimonji's efficiency is marked by exactly this: no field pressure, aura warmth or harmonic noise leaks outward from him, which says that from everyone else it does.

### 6.3 The tell, priced

The waste leaves the Shell as radiance, and Stefan–Boltzmann prices what it looks like. T = (P / εσA)^¼ over 1.8 m² at ε = 0.98, at **full output**:

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dougou Ozumu Zettari | 39.0 MW | 4,444 K | 39.4 m | 15.8 m |
| Yukazuri Moto | 52.8 MW | 4,793 K | 45.8 m | 18.3 m |

| Ara Min Mahuo | 17.9 GW | 20,550 K | 843 m | 337 m |
| Kwon Mu-jin | 204 GW | 37,770 K | 2,846 m | 1,138 m |

*Radius r = √(P / 4π · threshold).* At full output even Dougou at η 0.99, wasting one percent, sets timber alight at fifteen metres. Kwon Mu-jin at full output burns hotter than any main-sequence star's surface, and his ignition radius of 1.14 kilometres reaches his own army and the ground he stands on; he can be seen working from the next valley.

At the draw a working actually runs at, the tell is smaller and still there. Somnalis Drift at 48.75 AU/s and η 0.89 sheds 5.36 MW: a surface near 2,700 K, pain on bare skin to 14.6 m, timber catching at 5.8 m.

**This is the tell, and it is also the cost.** A practitioner at full output is a lamp, a furnace and a hazard to everyone within a stated radius, and the radius is on this table. A low-η practitioner is brighter for the same work. A high-η one is nearly dark, which is why Anryū's quiet is remarked on, and why a man working in silence and in the cold should frighten a reader more, not less. The ladder of who is brighter than whom depends on *(1 − η) × output* and on nothing else. The tell does not end when the working does: scorched ground, a burnt ring and a room still warm are the aftermath, and the aftermath is read like the working was.

### 6.4 η above one

Above 1.0 the Continuum recognizes the expression as law and **supplements it with ambient flow**; the environment becomes a co-author. **η above one is real.** It breaks no law, because the practitioner is not a closed system: the surplus comes from outside, the way a heat pump delivers three to five times its input by moving energy it did not make.

**The surplus is drawn from the Aether stratum, the room, not from the Crystal.** Of every 1.05 delivered at η 1.05, 0.05 is drawn in. At Anryū Ichimonji's 9,870 AU/s that is 4.76% of 9.87 GW, **470 MW taken from the stratum around him**, every second of full output, while his own Crystal pays only the 9,870 EU/s it would pay at η 1.00. His reserve drains at the same rate as anyone's with the same AU/s; what the high η buys is delivered effect and a working that sheds nothing.

**The room pays, and the room shows it.** The stratum a practitioner above one draws on is measurably depleted, and it stays thinner after the working ends; passive recovery in that ground runs slower (§7.3), for them and for anyone else who stands in it. That is the tell and it is also the counter: *starve the reservoir.* Aether-dead ground, a depleted site or a Silence Realm leaves nothing to supplement the expression with, and η falls back below one by definition.

## §7 · Essence Starvation and recovery

### 7.1 The threshold

Operating below **ten percent EU** produces Essence Starvation: reduced stat expression, Shell degradation, Trait flickering.

Ten percent of each Tier's ceiling, in joules:

| 2 · Apprentice | 50,300 | 5,030 | 5.03 GJ |
| 3 · Journeyman | 50,300 | 5,030 | 5.03 GJ |
| 4 · Adept | 961,000 | 96,100 | 96.1 GJ |
| 5 · Expert | 1.84 × 10⁷ | 1.84 × 10⁶ | 1.84 TJ |

The floor is ten percent of the practitioner's own maximum, not of the Tier's ceiling; the table gives the largest floor each Tier can carry. The Tier 6 figure, 35.1 TJ, sits inside the spine's carve-out (24.3 to 41.8 TJ), so what a Starved Master still holds, released at once, is ranked by the Sub-Stat column and not by joules.

**A practitioner may keep a wider margin than the law does.** Hieronymus Cruciferi maintains peak output until his reserve drops below thirty percent. His thirty is a personal operating margin; the law's floor is ten.

### 7.2 What it costs

Traits come and go without pattern under Essence Starvation. A Trait is a standing bias maintained across the Shell, and **a Shell below its working reserve cannot maintain a bias**, so the law drops out and returns as the reserve moves. That is the Flicker.

**Starvation has a direction, not only a line.** A store doing the same work through a falling density loses efficiency: a discharging cell's internal resistance rises, a draining capacitor's drive falls. So η falls as the reserve falls, and the waste fraction (1 − η) **rises at exactly the moment the practitioner can least afford it.** The Starved practitioner is brighter for the same work, sheds more, delivers less. Starvation does not begin at ten percent; it *becomes visible* there.

**The reserve clock is the clock a fight runs on.** Because the waste is shed at the Shell and not carried by the body, a practitioner can spend down to the floor and past it, and a real fight does: at the shares of §5.5, ten to twenty committed exchanges bring a practitioner to Starvation, and at full output the table in §5.3 does it in turns.

### 7.3 How fast it comes back, and on what it depends

Recovery runs by three routes.

**Passive Recovery** is the baseline absorption of ambient Essence without active effort, governed by **Tempering Yield** and **Harmonics Synergy**. It is faster near active Wellspring veins, slower in depleted or hostile environments, and negligible in Silence Realms.

**Active Recovery** is deliberate absorption through meditation, Wellspring communion or alchemical treatment. It is significantly faster, but **requires the character to stop fighting and concentrate.**

**Crisis Recovery** is a surge triggered by a soul-cost event, a Threshold completion or a genuine emotional breakthrough during combat. **It is not reliable and cannot be manufactured.**

The rates:

Ten percent a minute is the same number as the Starvation floor, so one figure serves twice: **a Starved practitioner is about one minute, ten turns, of rest from being out of Starvation.** Long enough to be a decision in a fight, short enough not to end one, and slow enough that a siege still works: a percent a turn does not refill anybody mid-exchange.

### 7.4 Energy taken in

**Energy a practitioner absorbs is banked in their own Crystal.** Whatever reaches the Crystal from outside (a working turned and taken rather than blocked, a Wellspring drunk from, a discharge caught) enters the reserve at the same constant it leaves it: one EU for every megajoule banked.

**Absorption can overfill the Crystal.** Recovery stops at maximum; absorption does not. A practitioner can be driven above their own maximum reserve by what they take in, and what they hold above it is reserve like any other, spent at the same rate and counted toward the same floor.

### 7.5 What Starvation is not

It is not unconsciousness and it is not a hit-point track. A Starved practitioner is still a person with a sword. What they have lost is the *standing* part of what they were: Traits that were law become weather, the Shell that was a wall becomes a sheet, stats read one Grade lower than they stand at full reserve. Below ten percent a practitioner suffers Essence Starvation, and **a defender who can afford to spend nothing wins a long engagement against an attacker who cannot afford to spend nothing.** Sieges are decided by this more often than by any working.

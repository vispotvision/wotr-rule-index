## The Domains ladder

**Domains, on the spine.** A Domain is inner law made local reality, and it is held, not thrown. What the spine reads is what the Domain holds: **the energy standing in its territory at one time.** Two figures fix that, and the ladder reads them together: **the radius the law is held over, and the joules held across it.** The joules find the row and the row is the rung. The radius says how far those joules are spread, and the two multiplied back together are the size and density that Part Twenty-Two makes the measure of a Domain's collapse.

**How the joules held are read.** A Domain is paid for as a rate, and the Ledger turns a rate into a quantity over its own unit of time, the six-second turn. **The joules held are the maintenance draw across one turn, delivered through the holder's η: draw in EU/s × 6 × η × one megajoule.** A Domain expression paid per activation rather than per second is read at its activation cost through the same η. A Domain spent whole, detonated or collapsed on purpose, is read at what the spending cost, and stands on its own row beside the held one.

**Density is the tell.** Joules held ÷ the area of the disc the radius draws, π r², gives the charge on each square metre of held ground, in J/m². It does not move the rung. It is what an opponent standing at the edge can estimate, and it is the figure a collapse is felt in.

**1 · Arm's-reach.** Law held no further than the holder can touch, at no more than a mortal's force. The ground under the feet agrees with the holder and nothing else does. **A Domain held at this rung is felt as a person's presence and read as one.**

**2 · Doorstep.** A territory the size of a threshold, held at Wall-level force. It decides who crosses a doorway and how hard the crossing is. Most seeds that flicker at Refraction and collapse when attention fractures are held here or at the rung above.

**3 · Yard.** A courtyard's worth of law at Journeyman scale. What stands inside a Yard fights on the holder's terms for as long as the holder's attention holds.

**4 · Holdfast.** A territory at the scale of a building, held at Building to Large Building force. **Nearly every Domain on record whose draw is written down stands here**, from Refraction to Transcendence, which is the rung's own statement of what a live Domain costs to keep.

**5 · Precinct.** A district-scale charge: City Block to Multi-City Block held standing. The Domain is law-bearing in the sense Grade A gives it, and a Precinct spent whole is a disaster at that scale.

**6 · Burgh.** A town's weight of law held at once. At this rung a Domain begins reshaping the environment it stands in rather than merely existing within it, and the Continuum must accommodate it.

**7 · Dukedom.** A small country's weight held standing. **Domain law at this rung can override the local behaviour of a Wellspring**, which is why a Wellspring Core, the seventh rung of the sites ladder, is the first ground a Dukedom meets as an equal.

**8 · Globe.** Country to Large Planet, held. The Domain produces Archon-echo effects and the ground it covers carries the holder's doctrine after the holder has gone.

**9 · Firmament.** The rung with no ceiling. Domain and holder are no longer separate figures, and the ladder keeps the rung because the spine has a top.

**The carve-out does not move a rung.** A Domain whose joules held land between 2.43 × 10¹³ and 4.18 × 10¹³ J is Graded by the holder's Sub-Stat rather than by joules, and both Grades it can take sit in Tier 6. It is a Burgh either way.

### The modifiers

**Reach.** The radius is recorded beside the rung and never moves it. A wide Domain on a low rung is thin, and a narrow Domain on a high rung is dense, and **the rung is the same figure in both**: what the holder has standing at once.

**Phase.** Part Twenty-Two gives the Domain's life by Stage, and the phase says whether the joules held can be read at all.

| Phase | Stage | What the rung means |
|---|---|---|
| **Seed** | VII Refraction | held by active concentration; **the rung stands only while attention does** |
| **True Domain** | VIII Transcendence | held for hours; the rung survives minor disruption |
| **Realm** | IX–X | holds other Domains; pacts, artifacts and law imprint on terrain |
| **Ambient** | XI–XII | leaks without maintenance; **no draw to read, so the rung is read at what a collapse or a spending releases** |
| **Registered** | XIII | the Continuum registers the law; biomes carry the doctrine |
| **Indistinct** | XIV | Domain and holder are one figure; read the holder |

**Ground.** Maintaining a Domain inside someone else's costs significantly more, and terrain works on η through the Family's physics. **Hostile ground does not lower the rung. It raises the draw the same rung costs**, and shortens the clock on the holder's reserve.

**Persistence.** Some Domains stand after the holder leaves them. A Domain that persists after exit holds its rung for that long without a holder to strike.

**Collapse.** A Domain collapses when EU drops below the maintenance floor, when concentration is shattered, or when a stronger Domain overwrites it, and the Resonance Shockwave is proportional to size and density. **The rung prices the shockwave: a collapsing Domain can release up to its joules held onto everything inside, the holder included.** A Domain spent on purpose releases what the spending cost, and is read on its own row.

**The hierarchy is unchanged.** When two Domains overlap, Temperance differential, then Dominion Sovereignty and Gravity, then Wellspring compatibility, then doctrine coherence decide, as Part Twenty-Two writes it. The rung is not a step in that order. It is what is standing on the ground when the order is applied.

### Counterplay

**The rung tells an opponent what will come down on the ground if the Domain breaks, and the radius tells them where to be standing when it does.** Density is readable from the edge: a narrow Domain holding a Holdfast's charge is dense enough to punish anyone who enters it and dense enough to punish its holder if it collapses; a wide one on the same rung is thin everywhere. A seed stands on attention and an Ambient Domain on nothing an opponent can reach, and the phase says which. **A Domain held on hostile ground pays more for the same rung**, and the difference is a clock anyone can count.

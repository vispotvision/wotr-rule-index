## The Drafts ladder

**Alchemical works and Drafts, on the spine.** Every Draft, salt, oil, powder and charge the trade produces is a working stabilised into a substance, and a substance holds a definite quantity of Essence. The ladder reads that quantity. **A work is placed by the EU one dose holds, converted at one megajoule to the unit, and read on the spine like any other peak output.** Two modifiers, Carry and Fidelity, then move it.

**What is read is what the dose holds, not what arrives.** The spine is read on the held figure at the constant, before anybody's η touches it. What reaches the drinker is the held figure times the drinker's own η, and whatever reaches the Crystal is banked there at the same constant it would leave at: one EU for every megajoule taken in. **A dose can overfill.** Recovery stops at a practitioner's maximum; what is drunk does not, and a dose larger than the room left in the Crystal leaves its drinker above their own ceiling, holding reserve like any other, spent at the same rate and counted toward the same floor.

**Tincture.** A work measured in thousandths of a unit. It cleans a cut, holds a fall, lights a room, and needs no circle and no understanding. **The whole first rung together costs less than the smallest reserve carries in its lowest percent**, which is why a household can own a shelf of it and never think of it as alchemy.

**Tonic.** A working day's steadiness in a bottle, a lamp for a season, a thunderclap in a flask. **A careless hand can ruin a Tonic without dying of it.** This is the rung of the ration and the field kit.

**Draught.** The trade standard. A Draught holds enough to matter against a Welling reserve and to carry a Welling practitioner toward the Ascension floor across a course of doses. **The bulk of what the four quarters drink for their Crystal is here.**

**Philter.** A dose that holds a Journeyman's whole working range in one vessel. **This is the rung at which a Draft can carry a practitioner to a quantitative floor in one issue**, and the rung at which it is sold against a term of service rather than a price.

**Elixir.** Threshold work in earnest. An Elixir holds as much as an Expert's reserve spends in a long engagement and supports a Crystal through a reorganisation. **An Elixir can supply the floor and it can supply a seed. It does not supply a Catalyst, and nothing on any higher rung does either.**

**Quintessence.** A dose at the scale of a Master's Domain. Works on this rung anchor, stabilise and hold at the size of a live, law-bearing field, and **the question of what the dose was rendered from stops being hypothetical**.

**Magisterium.** A work that enforces. It holds energy on the scale of a regional event, and what it does to a blade, a wound or a Domain behaves as a ruling rather than a reaction.

**Panacea.** Realm-scale. What a Panacea is laid into registers in the Continuum as terrain. **Works on this rung are used a handful of times in the record, and what they are laid into outlasts whoever laid it.**

**Stone.** The rung with no ceiling. It holds what a Paragon's working holds, and **the Stone is the rung the trade keeps open because the ladder required a top, not because the trade has filled it.**

**The carve-out does not move a rung.** A dose whose held energy lands between 2.43 × 10¹³ and 4.18 × 10¹³ J is Graded by its Sub-Stat reading rather than by joules, and both Grades it can take sit in Tier 6. It is a Quintessence either way.

### The modifiers

**Carry and Fidelity are the Bench's own marks, read here for what they do to a rung.** They are the same figures the Standing Index enters on every row, and the ladder takes them as entered.

**Carry: how much of the origin came with the dose.** The origin rides in the energy and arrives with it.

| Carry | Reading | Effect on the rung |
|---|---|---|
| **0–10** | Clean | none |
| **11–40** | Light | none |
| **41–70** | Marked | none; the origin is a design consideration and is recorded beside the rung |
| **71–90** | Heavy | **one rung up.** The origin governs, and the drinker receives what the source was as well as what the formula says |
| **91+** | Sovereign-carry | **one rung up**, and the dose is read as its source wearing a formula |

**Fidelity: how close the dose is to its reference figure.** Fidelity decides how much of the held energy arrives as the named effect.

| Fidelity | Reading | Effect on the rung |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Reference-indistinguishable | none |
| **B** | Sound | none |
| **C** | Serviceable | none; the rung is the ceiling of a dose, not its promise, and no two doses reach it the same way |
| **D** | Loose | **one rung down** for the named effect; the rest runs into the adjacent process |
| **E** | Poor | **one rung down** for the named effect, and the hazard is read at the full held rung |
| **F** | Failed | **no rung as a product.** It is placed only as a hazard, at the full held rung |

**The modifiers net, and together they move a dose at most one rung either way.** Carry can raise a rung and never lower one. Fidelity can lower a rung and never raise one. A Heavy-carry dose of Loose fidelity sits where its held energy puts it: the origin governs and the formula leaks, and the two cancel on the ladder without cancelling in the drinker.

### The Index and the ladder

**The Standing Index and the ladder are two readings of the same lot, and neither corrects the other.** The Index ranks an entered lot from T1 to T9 by provenance and standing: its Provenance Class, its rank gate, who may lawfully hold it and what the Bench is prepared to certify about it. The ladder ranks the same lot by the energy one dose holds. **The Index prices provenance and standing. The ladder prices energy held.**

The two agree most often through the middle of the trade, where a Threshold ration's rank gate and its dose climb together. They part wherever the Index is pricing law, provenance or danger rather than joules. **A lot can hold almost nothing and be reserved to Masters, because what it does is dangerous out of all proportion to what it holds; and a lot can hold a Domain's worth and be entered one rank lower than its rung, because its Carry lifts it on the ladder and not in the ledger.** A buyer reads the Index to learn who may hold a thing. A drinker, and anyone facing one, reads the rung to learn what it will do.

**A lot that has never been drawn from has no rung.** The ladder reads what a dose holds, and a residue that does not deplete, or has never been opened, offers no dose to read. Both stand on the Index and neither stands on the ladder, and **that absence is the most honest line in either document.**

**A vessel that refills is not a dose.** A phial that never runs dry is read on the Artifacts ladder, by what one pour discharges and how bound the vessel is to its bearer.

### Counterplay

**The rung says how much a dosed opponent took in, and banked reserve is only reserve.** A Philter or an Elixir drunk before a fight has put that many EU above where the opponent stood, possibly above their own ceiling, and every one of them spends at the same rate as the rest and counts toward the same ten-percent floor. The answer is findable in the arithmetic: make them spend it. **A Draft supplies energy and it can supply a seed; it has never supplied a Catalyst**, so a dosed practitioner holds more than they did and is not a Stage higher than they were. Where the Carry is Heavy, the origin arrived with the energy, and the source's own weaknesses, appetites and habits of mind are in the drinker now and can be found there. Where the Fidelity is C or worse, the dose did not do the same thing twice, and the part that went into the adjacent process is a tell.

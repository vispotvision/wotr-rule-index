## The Wellspring and vein sites ladder

**Sites, on the spine.** A Wellspring is not a place, but it pools: a current concentrates where the substrate conducts, where no competing current draws on it, and where time has let it saturate the field until the ground expresses the law on its own. A site is that pool, and a vein is the channel that feeds it. **A site is placed by hazard: the output a practitioner must be able to command to keep their own law inside the site at its worst ground.** That figure is read in joules like every other peak, the row it lands in is the rung, and the rung says how strong you must be to stand there and remain yourself.

**Why hazard is read this way.** A site does not strike. It retunes. Harmonization is phase-locking, and a current strong enough to phase-lock a Shell against its bearer's will is a current the bearer's own output has failed to hold off. So the ladder asks the question the survey asks, from the other side: what Grade of output holds a Crystal's own architecture against this current. The density scale answers it in words, and the spine answers it in joules. **The two meet at one fixed point: Domain law at SSS can override the local behaviour of a Wellspring, and a Wellspring Core is the ground where the Wellspring's law is the only law operating. A Core therefore stands on SSS, the seventh rung, and the density scale is laid down the spine from there.**

**1 · Trickle.** Ground where the current barely wets the field. A Sage can tune to it and an ordinary person feels vaguely uneasy or vaguely at peace. **Arrays run here at a loss**, and a practitioner recovers here slower than anywhere with a vein.

**2 · Rill.** High, steady ambient saturation, felt within a day of arriving as a low pressure behind the eyes. It is the ground arrays pay for themselves on, and **it harms nobody**. Its danger is that it teaches a population to live beside a current.

**3 · Freshet.** The edge of a concentration, where the field is altered persistently and the alteration stays after the flow that made it. Streets read wrong on every instrument and wrong to the people in them. **Children awaken early here, and Traits appear in families that were never screened.**

**4 · Millrace.** A true Active Concentration. Wild ground shows altered terrain, Spirit ground produces physiological or psychological change the visitor did not choose, Medium ground produces both at once. **A practitioner who stays too long receives Wellspring Baptism whether they wanted it or not**, and sustained residence here or above leaves a Trait by Wellspring Convergence in anyone, Crystal or no Crystal.

**5 · Riptide.** A concentration at its densest, where the current pulls the visitor toward the core and the comfort points the wrong way. **At this rung the window between Spirit-type onset and commitment is measured in seconds**, and the Veil has begun to give.

**6 · Whirlpool.** A Veil-Thin Nexus. The planes turn together: spiritual entities visible, causal threads perceptible, time wrong. Bifold Perception arrives untrained, the soul drifts from the body's position, and Echo Beasts take form out of what the visitors brought in with them. **At Veil-Thin density the window of a Spirit-type onset is functionally zero.**

**7 · Fountainhead.** A Wellspring Core, the current's origin point. The physical and the metaphysical environment are indistinguishable and the law the Wellspring carries is the only law operating, **including on the visitor.** Recovery is re-anchoring by a specialist standing outside the radius, and it works, and it is not fast.

**8 · Gyre.** Ground past anything the density scale was written for: a site that does not kill in any way the Measurewrights have language for, whose edge is fixed by the Accord and revised outward. **At this rung the survey measures the perimeter because nothing inside it has been measured and come back.**

**9 · Chasm.** The rung with no ceiling. A current and its material so far indistinguishable that the law is written about the rock rather than about the place, and the ladder keeps the rung for the one site that already stands on it.

**The carve-out does not move a rung.** A hazard read between 2.43 × 10¹³ and 4.18 × 10¹³ J is Graded by Sub-Stat rather than by joules, and both Grades it can take sit in Tier 6. It is a Whirlpool either way.

### The modifiers

**Encounter type: where the danger comes from.** The encounter type never moves the rung. It sets the warning time, and the warning time is what decides who walks out.

| Type | What reports first | Warning at the rung |
|---|---|---|
| **Wild** | the body and the ground | **generous**: the anomalies are legible before they are dangerous, at every rung |
| **Spirit** | the Crystal, and not to the conscious mind | **seconds** at Millrace and Riptide; **none** at Whirlpool and above |
| **Medium** | both at once, and neither is trustworthy | **the worst of the three**; the visitor has lost the reference frame and needs an observer outside the radius |

**Force: whether the site recognises or takes.** A site under clean Attraction Force reads the Crystal and either permits harmonisation or ignores the visitor. **A site under Obsession Force takes, compatible or not, and stands one rung higher than its density**, because the output that held against a current that asks does not hold against one that pulls.

**Stability: whether the reading drifts.** A site whose saturation never drifts is read at its rung and stays there. A site violently irregular is read at its worst ground, never its average.

**Draw: what working the site does.** A site drawn on steadily falls as it is drawn: the old districts of a worked quarter read a rung or more below the ground beside them. **A site worked past what it can give does not fall a rung. It gives out**, and the ground that is left reads as ordinary depleted earth.

**The unawakened.** A Class Ø soul has no Shell to phase-lock and no harmonisation occurs, and the rung reads the same for a porter as for a practitioner. What differs is the exit. **A practitioner in a Spirit-type onset vents the load through the Shell; an unawakened soul keeps it**, and no recovery protocol has been written for one returned from a Fountainhead.

**Objects.** A current bound inside a vessel is an Object Wellspring, and it is read on this ladder by the material that holds it: **material tier n stands on rung n.** An object has no perimeter and no gradient to give warning. It is held or it is not, and its internal law meets the Shell at zero distance.

### Counterplay

**The rung tells an opponent how strong they must be to fight on this ground and still be the one fighting.** A Millrace is survivable for an afternoon and not for a season; a Whirlpool gives a Spirit-type onset no warning at all; a Fountainhead rewrites anyone below its rung who stays. The type says which sense to trust on the way in, and the perimeter is marked from outside: **withdrawal is not retreat, and a fight drawn onto ground a rung above one side's Grade is half won before it starts.** Which side that is depends on who read the site first.

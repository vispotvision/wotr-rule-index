## The Summons ladder

**Summons, on the spine.** A called thing is a working that stands up and walks. Whatever it does once it is standing, the one figure every entry in the register carries is what it cost to call, and that figure is the ladder's. **A summon is placed by its call cost read through η: the EU spent to call it, times the efficiency of the crossing, times one megajoule.** The delivered joules find the row, and the row is the rung.

**Whose η.** The crossing is the summoner's Shell, so the summoner's own η is read wherever it is recorded. Where it is not, the crossing is read at the summon's own Aether Class, because Aether Class governs how much of the donated Essence survives the crossing into form: Class I loses thirty to forty percent (η 0.60–0.70), Class II fifteen to twenty-five (η 0.75–0.85), Class III five to ten (η 0.90–0.95), and Class V delivers one hundred percent or more (η 1.00 and above). **A call cost given as a share of reserve is as large as the summoner's reserve makes it**, and where the reserve is not recorded it is set by the law of reserves at the summoner's Level.

The rung says what was paid to put the thing on the field. It does not say what the thing will do once it is there, and it does not say what it costs to keep it there. The modifiers say those.

**1 · Wisp.** A called glimmer that holds a shape for a breath: a light that follows, a voice from an empty corner, a nudge at a latch. **The whole rung costs less than the smallest reserve spends in its lowest percent**, and a Wisp is dangerous only as a signal, because every construct broadcasts its authorizing glyph and its summoner's resonance.

**2 · Errand.** A called thing sent to do one small task: carry a message, scout a corridor, hold a door, pull a wounded man out of a line. An Errand can kill a man who lets it, and that is not what it is for.

**3 · Shieldmate.** A called thing worth one more fighter on the field. It guards a flank, intercepts a bolt, answers a practitioner blow for blow at Journeyman scale. **This is the lowest rung at which a summoner fights with the summon rather than behind it.**

**4 · Gatebreaker.** A called force at the scale of a building. It opens a fortified house, breaks a gatehouse, overturns a battery. What stands here changes the shape of a siege by arriving.

**5 · Ravager.** A called thing at the scale of a city block. A Ravager lays waste to a district in the time a garrison takes to form, and **it is the first rung at which the call alone is a share of the summoner's reserve that the summoner will feel for the rest of the day.**

**6 · Townfall.** A called thing that a town is lost to. Natural-disaster output, Town to Large Town, one to a hundred kilotons. **Every entry in the register stands here**, whatever its Danger Tier, and whatever it answers to.

**7 · Kingdom-ender.** A called thing at the scale of a regional event, Large Town to Small Country. The call alone exceeds four hundred terajoules delivered, more than the reserve ceiling of every Stage below Emanation.

**8 · World-scar.** Country to Large Planet. What is called at this rung leaves the ground it stood on marked in the Continuum's own account. No call on record reaches it.

**9 · Starbearer.** The rung with no ceiling: a called thing carrying stellar weight. The ladder keeps it because the spine has a top, not because anything has been called to it.

**The carve-out does not move a rung.** A call whose delivered energy lands between 2.43 × 10¹³ and 4.18 × 10¹³ J is Graded by the summoner's Sub-Stat against the S and SS column rather than by joules, and both Grades it can take sit in Tier 6. It is a Townfall either way. **Two recorded calls land in it:** the cheap end of Kwon Mu-jin's Ledger's Summons and Vaelmorn.

### The modifiers

**None of the modifiers moves the rung.** The rung is the call, and the call is paid once. The modifiers are what the call bought.

**Sustain: the clock.** A summon held past its arrival draws a sustain, and the sustain is read as a clock against the summoner's reserve: reserve ÷ sustain is how long the summoner can hold it at full, and nine tenths of that is how long before Essence Starvation. **A summon whose sustain empties its summoner inside a minute is placed at its call and fought at its clock.**

**Cost off the Ledger.** Several entries record a sustain of nil and a cost paid in something other than Essence: a name taken per summoning, sensation that runs late for hours, heat carried in the summoner's own body. **A cost of this kind is recorded beside the rung and never converted into it.** It does not make the summon cheaper. It makes the summoner the place where the summon can be reached.

**Provenance: what holds it together.** The register's four provenances decide what survives the fight.

| Provenance | What holds it | What the rung does when the summoner falls |
|---|---|---|
| **Donated** | a fragment of the summoner's own Core on a live tether | **ends with the summoner**, and ends inside any Silence Realm |
| **Analog** | a synthetic three-layer architecture inside the summoner's Concordia field | degrades with the field; a coherent one may resist dissolution |
| **Native** | its own Crystal | **stands.** The rung was the price of being recognised, and killing the summoner does not unpay it |
| **Anchored** | Crystal function borrowed from a vein, a bloodline or a Domain | **stands as long as the node does**, and outlives the summoner |

**Bond Class: what it will refuse.** Directed, Interpretive, Consenting, Sovereign. A Consenting or Sovereign summon at any rung carries refusal conditions, and **a refusal is the one event that takes a called thing's whole rung off the field without a blow being struck.**

**Danger Tier: the thing loose.** The register rates every entry T1 through T9 on the Commission's threat scale, as it would be rated loose and hostile. **The rung and the Danger Tier are the same nine steps read from opposite sides.** The rung is what the summoner paid; the Danger Tier is what the world pays if the leash fails. A Danger Tier above the rung is a summon whose threat is not in its output, and the register's entries all stand there.

**Every summon in the register stands on the sixth rung**, and every one of them is rated loose at the sixth or above. The register's entries differ from one another in what they refuse and what they take, and not in what they cost to call.

**Not placed.** No call cost is recorded for Nisuke, the soul-forged effigy in a Titan's shape; for the Amalgamation constructs; for the seven Requiem constructs, whose price was a permanent share of their maker's reserve in an amount the record does not give; for the reflection-constructs of the Hall of Shattered Mirrors; or for the Page-Wyrm that buds from the Ocular Codex. **Each has a rung, and the rung waits on its figure.**

### Counterplay

**The rung tells an opponent what the summoner has already spent, and that is the most useful thing a called thing ever says about itself.** A Townfall called from a Grandmaster's reserve is a twentieth of the afternoon gone; the same rung called from a Master's reserve may be the half of it. Read the rung against what the summoner can carry, then read the provenance: a Donated summon is answered by the summoner, a Native one is not, and an Anchored one is answered by the ground it stands on. **Every construct broadcasts its authorizing glyph and its summoner's resonance to comparable Perception**, so the rung is findable before the thing arrives. What to do about it is not.

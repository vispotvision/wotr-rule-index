> Four quantities run through every practitioner in the Continuum: a reserve
> in EU, a compression in EU/g, an output rate in AU/s, an efficiency η. The
> Grade ladder measures destruction in joules. This Part is the join.
> **One Essence Unit is one megajoule.** What follows is what that buys on the
> ladder, what a reserve costs to spend, how long it lasts at full output, how
> fast it comes back, and what can be done about all of it from the other side
> of a fight.

### The four quantities, and the fifth

**EU (Essence Units)** is the raw reserve: the total volume of usable Essence
stored in the Crystal at any moment. It scales with Temperance Stage and is
governed primarily by **Tempering Yield**. Maximum EU is the structural ceiling
the Crystal refills to. Operating below **ten percent EU** produces Essence
Starvation: reduced stat expression, Shell degradation, Trait flickering.

**Flux Density (EU/g)** is the compression ratio, how much Essence is packed per
unit of Crystal mass. Higher means greater output from the same reserve. It is
governed by **Tempering Clarity** and **Ardency Compression**, and it is what
separates a practitioner who burns through their reserve in three techniques
from one who fights for an hour on the same pool.

**AU/s (Aether Units per second)** is the output rate, how much Essence converts
into active expression per second of sustained operation. It is governed by
**Ardency Flux**, **Ardency Alacrity** and **Tempering Yield**, and it follows
the formula **AU/s = Flux Density × η**.

**Eta (η), Efficiency** is the ratio of Essence spent to Essence that arrives as
intended effect. At η 0.50, half of every expenditure is wasted as heat, noise
and structural bleed. At η 0.90, ninety percent converts to clean output. It is
the single most important variable in sustained combat, because it determines
how long the reserve lasts under continuous output. It is governed by **Aether
Class**, **Tempering Clarity** and **Ardency Compression**.

**The fifth quantity is the one Part Eleven already measures**, and it is the
one that lets the other four be priced at all. Strike Force is the destructive
energy delivered per discrete attack action, recorded in **Newtons** for peak
contact force and in **Joules** for total energy yield. The Continuum already
keeps its books in joules. The Ledger is not a new currency; it is the exchange
rate between the two sets of books.

**The direction of the conversion.** EU is *potential* and AU is *actual*: what
the Crystal holds against what leaves it. One AU is one EU crossing the Shell.
The two are never the same number in practice, because one is the store, one
is the flow, and η is the loss across the boundary between them.

### The constant: what one EU is worth

#### The constant

**1 EU = 1 MJ of potential. Energy delivered = EU × η × 1 MJ. 1 AU/s = 1 MW.**

The Ledger converts at one megajoule everywhere. Every joule, watt, ton of TNT,
drain time and Starvation floor in this Part is at that constant, and there is
no second rate for any register, Path or Stage.

EU → joules → Grade → TNT → what it wrecks, with 1 t TNT ≡ 4.184 × 10⁹ J (the
thermochemical definition, so the arithmetic is exact rather than measured):

### Reserve bands by Stage and by Tier of Standing

#### The law of reserves

A reserve is set by **Level** and capped by **Stage**. Level sets it along a
log-linear law; the Band gates, which a Stage must pass before its Level can
climb, cap it.

The law runs through three fixed reference points: a Stage VIII Master at Level
250 with 4,200,000 EU, a Stage XII Grandmaster at Level 378 with 180,000,000 EU,
and a Stage XII Grandmaster at Level 430 with 850,000,000 EU. Between the first two the slope is
**0.012750 decades per Level**; between the second two it is **0.012964**. The
two segments agree to 1.7 percent, and across the whole span:

**log₁₀ EU = 3.4202 + 0.012812 × Level.** A reserve multiplies by ten every 78.1 Levels, and by about 26 across each hundred-Level Band.

A Stage X Realization practitioner at Level 355, Band IV, holds 90,000,000 EU,
and that reserve is set by this law.

### AU/s as power: how long the output holds

#### The drain

AU/s is a *rate*: how much Essence converts into active expression per second of
sustained operation, and the variable that determines how long the reserve
lasts under continuous output. One AU is one EU crossing the Shell, so a second
at full AU/s costs exactly AU/s worth of EU.

| Quantity | Working | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Time to empty | EU reserve ÷ (AU/s) | seconds |
| Time to Starvation | 0.9 × EU reserve ÷ (AU/s) | seconds, to the ten-percent floor |

| Power delivered | AU/s × η × 1 MW | watts |
| Waste | (1 − η) × AU/s × 1 MW | watts, shed as heat, sound and light |

turns: a practitioner whose reserve holds twenty-one seconds at full output has
three and a half turns of it.

The drain times are a property of the two figures a practitioner carries, reserve
and AU/s, and of one AU being one EU. The power and the waste are at the

#### What AU/s is not

AU/s is a **ceiling on the rate**, not a duty cycle. No practitioner runs at their
AU/s continuously, and sustained work runs at a fraction of it. The small
practitioner's broadcast suppression costs 390 EU, is a full-broadcast sympathetic
suppression across twenty-five feet, and lasts eight seconds: 390 ÷ 8 = 48.75
AU/s, one tenth of the ceiling. The ceiling is what the practitioner can do for a
heartbeat; the tenth is what they do for eight seconds. **Read a practitioner's AU/s as the number that caps a
burst, and read a working's EU ÷ duration as the number that runs a scene.**

#### Costs are a share of the reserve

A working is priced as a share of the reserve it draws on, and the shares are
large enough that a real fight reaches the ten-percent floor. The small
practitioner's workings run 4.5 to 12.6 percent of the reserve apiece. The middle
practitioner's branches run 3.0 to 9.7 percent, and the apex strike 8.0. The large
practitioner's constructs run 5 to 15 percent. **A practitioner who commits to a fight spends in whole percents per
exchange, and ten to twenty committed exchanges bring anyone to Starvation.**
The absolute size of the reserve decides how large each working is; the share
decides how long the practitioner lasts, and the share is what a scene tracks.

The cost can be permanent. A working may forfeit a permanent portion of the
practitioner's own maximum reserve each time it is performed. Performed seven
times, it takes seven portions that do not grow back, and the maximum reserve
was higher before the first.

### Flux Density and η

#### The equation, read per working gram

**AU/s = Flux Density × η.** The equation governs: a practitioner's output rate is
their Flux Density multiplied by their efficiency.

AU/s is Essence per *second* and Flux Density is Essence per *gram*, so the
equation is read **per working gram per second**: the gram of Crystal engaged at
the working aperture in each second of output. The gram goes unwritten because it
is always one. It is not the whole Crystal. A Crystal of nine kilograms of
Crystal and slag that reads as one structure, at a Flux Density of 670 million
EU/g and η 0.61, outputs 408,700,000 AU/s, drawn through one gram of it at a
time.

#### Where the waste goes, and why it cannot go through the body

The missing fraction of every expenditure is wasted as **heat, noise and
structural bleed**. That list is correct physics. The first law says the missing
fraction cannot vanish; the second says it cannot come back as work; the
Gouy–Stodola relation (Ẇ_lost = T₀ · Ṡ_gen) says it reappears as low-grade heat
at the place the loss happened. Light and sound are ways out of that place, not
alternatives to it, and each becomes heat where it is absorbed.

**It cannot be generated inside the body and conducted out.** Fourier's law,
q = −k∇T. The small practitioner's 52.8 MW of waste through 1.8 m² of skin is 29.3 MW/m²;
across 3 mm of skin at k ≈ 0.4 W·m⁻¹·K⁻¹ that would need

**ΔT = qL/k = 2.93 × 10⁷ × 0.003 / 0.4 = 2.2 × 10⁵ K.**

across three millimetres, and that is the small practitioner. At the middle
practitioner's 39.0 MW it is 1.63 × 10⁵ K; at the large practitioner's 204 GW it
is 8.5 × 10⁸ K. Skin chars
at about 200 °C.

**So the Soul Crystal is not a battery in the chest. It is an aperture, and the
waste is shed at the Shell, at or outside the body's surface.** The body does not
carry the heat of its own workings; the Shell does, and the Shell sheds it into
the room. A practitioner at η above one is marked by exactly this: no field
pressure, aura warmth or harmonic noise leaks outward from them, which says that
from everyone below one it does.

The broadcast suppression at 48.75 AU/s and η 0.89 sheds 5.36 MW: a surface near 2,700 K,
pain on bare skin to 14.6 m, timber catching at 5.8 m.

**This is the tell, and it is also the cost.** A practitioner at full output is a
lamp, a furnace and a hazard to everyone within a stated radius, and the radius is
on this table. A low-η practitioner is brighter for the same work. A high-η one is
nearly dark, which is why a quiet practitioner is remarked on, and why one working in
silence and in the cold should frighten a reader more, not less. The ladder of who
is brighter than whom depends on *(1 − η) × output* and on nothing else. The
tell does not end when the working does: scorched ground, a burnt ring and a room
still warm are the aftermath, and the aftermath is read like the working was.

#### η above one

Above 1.0 the Continuum recognizes the expression as law and **supplements it with
ambient flow**; the environment becomes a co-author. **η above one is real.** It
breaks no law, because the practitioner is not a closed system: the surplus comes
from outside, the way a heat pump delivers three to five times its input by moving
energy it did not make.

**The surplus is drawn from the Aether stratum, the room, not from the Crystal.**
Of every 1.05 delivered at η 1.05, 0.05 is drawn in. At 9,870 AU/s and η 1.05
that is 4.76% of 9.87 GW, **470 MW taken from the stratum around the practitioner**,
every second of full output, while their own Crystal pays only the 9,870 EU/s it
would pay at η 1.00. His reserve drains at the same rate as anyone's with the same
AU/s; what the high η buys is delivered effect and a working that sheds nothing.

**The room pays, and the room shows it.** The stratum a practitioner above one
draws on is measurably depleted, and it stays thinner after the working ends;
passive recovery in that ground runs slower (see “How fast it comes back, and on what it depends”), for them and for anyone else
who stands in it. That is the tell and it is also the counter:
*starve the reservoir.* Aether-dead ground, a depleted site or a Silence Realm leaves nothing
to supplement the expression with, and η falls back below one by definition.

### Essence Starvation and recovery

#### The threshold

Operating below **ten percent EU** produces Essence Starvation: reduced stat
expression, Shell degradation, Trait flickering.

Ten percent of each Tier's ceiling, in joules:

| Tier | reserve ceiling, EU | Starvation floor, EU | in joules |
|---|---|---|---|
| Initiate | 50,300 | 5,030 | 5.03 GJ |
| Apprentice | 50,300 | 5,030 | 5.03 GJ |
| Journeyman | 50,300 | 5,030 | 5.03 GJ |
| Adept | 961,000 | 96,100 | 96.1 GJ |
| Expert | 1.84 × 10⁷ | 1.84 × 10⁶ | 1.84 TJ |
| Master | 3.51 × 10⁸ | 3.51 × 10⁷ | 35.1 TJ |
| Grandmaster | 6.70 × 10⁹ | 6.70 × 10⁸ | 670 TJ |
| Archmaster | 6.70 × 10⁹ | 6.70 × 10⁸ | 670 TJ |

The floor is ten percent of the practitioner's own maximum, not of the Tier's
ceiling; the table gives the largest floor each Tier can carry. The Master figure,
35.1 TJ, sits inside the spine's carve-out (24.3 to 41.8 TJ), so what a Starved
Master still holds, released at once, is ranked by the Sub-Stat column and not by
joules.

**A practitioner may keep a wider margin than the law does.** One who maintains
peak output only until the reserve drops below thirty percent keeps thirty as a
personal operating margin; the law's floor is ten.

#### What it costs

Traits come and go without pattern under Essence Starvation. A Trait is a standing
bias maintained across the Shell, and **a Shell below its working reserve cannot
maintain a bias**, so the law drops out and returns as the reserve moves. That is
the Flicker.

**Starvation has a direction, not only a line.** A store doing the same work
through a falling density loses efficiency: a discharging cell's internal
resistance rises, a draining capacitor's drive falls. So η falls as the reserve
falls, and the waste fraction (1 − η) **rises at exactly the moment the
practitioner can least afford it.** The Starved practitioner is brighter for the
same work, sheds more, delivers less. Starvation does not begin at ten percent; it
*becomes visible* there.

**The reserve clock is the clock a fight runs on.** Because the waste is shed at
the Shell and not carried by the body, a practitioner can spend down to the floor
and past it, and a real fight does: at the shares of “Costs are a share of the reserve”, ten to twenty committed
exchanges bring a practitioner to Starvation, and at full output the table in “The clock across the ladder”
does it in turns.

#### How fast it comes back, and on what it depends

Recovery runs by three routes.

**Passive Recovery** is the baseline absorption of ambient Essence without active
effort, governed by **Tempering Yield** and **Harmonics Synergy**. It is faster near
active Wellspring veins, slower in depleted or hostile environments, and
negligible in Silence Realms.

**Active Recovery** is deliberate absorption through meditation, Wellspring
communion or alchemical treatment. It is significantly faster, but **requires the
character to stop fighting and concentrate.**

**Crisis Recovery** is a surge triggered by a soul-cost event, a Threshold
completion or a genuine emotional breakthrough during combat. **It is not reliable
and cannot be manufactured.**

The rates:

| Condition | Rate |
|---|---|
| Passive recovery, at rest, in ordinary ground | ≈ 10% of maximum reserve per minute → 1% per turn → empty to full in 10 minutes → out of Starvation in ~1 minute |
| Active recovery (not fighting, concentrating) | ≈ 2 to 3 × passive |
| On an active Wellspring vein | faster |
| In depleted or hostile ground | slower |
| In a Silence Realm | negligible |
| Crisis recovery | cannot be planned for |

Ten percent a minute is the same number as the Starvation floor, so one figure
serves twice: **a Starved practitioner is about one minute, ten turns, of rest from
being out of Starvation.** Long enough to be a decision in a fight, short enough
not to end one, and slow enough that a siege still works: a percent a turn does not
refill anybody mid-exchange.

#### Energy taken in

**Energy a practitioner absorbs is banked in their own Crystal.** Whatever reaches
the Crystal from outside (a working turned and taken rather than blocked, a
Wellspring drunk from, a discharge caught) enters the reserve at the same
constant it leaves it: one EU for every megajoule banked.

**Absorption can overfill the Crystal.** Recovery stops at maximum; absorption does
not. A practitioner can be driven above their own maximum reserve by what they take
in, and what they hold above it is reserve like any other, spent at the same rate
and counted toward the same floor.

#### What Starvation is not

It is not unconsciousness and it is not a hit-point track. A Starved practitioner
is still a person with a sword. What they have lost is the *standing* part of what
they were: Traits that were law become weather, the Shell that was a wall becomes a
sheet, stats read one Grade lower than they stand at full reserve. Below ten
percent a practitioner suffers Essence Starvation, and **a defender who can afford
to spend nothing wins a long engagement against an attacker who cannot afford to
spend nothing.** Sieges are decided by this more often than by any working.

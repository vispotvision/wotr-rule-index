**Overfill him.** A practitioner who absorbs banks the load in his own Crystal, and what bounds the bank is its rated Flux Density rather than his reserve. To half again over rating he holds it and bleeds. Past half again the lattice takes a permanent set and he is Overgrown. At two and a quarter times rating it bursts, and the Fracture is his, from energy you handed him. **Feeding an absorber is cheaper than out-hitting him.** And the bank is short. It leaks at one minus his efficiency per second, three seconds for a poor Crystal and twenty for a fine one, so a defender who declines to be hit for ten seconds has taken it off him without touching him. The bleed is the tell, and it lasts exactly as long as the bank does.

**Saturate it.** Flood the area with Essence faster than it disperses and everyone present takes Fracture risk, **including whoever did it.** A weapon of last resort and a real one, and its practical use is the threat rather than the act. The threshold is a density and not a total: ten thousand EU in each cubic metre, or a hundred AU/s through each square metre of the region's edge. It grows with the surface while output does not, so **you cannot flood a field, only a building, and you have to be inside it.** A Zenith working a kilometre wide is a five-hundredth of threshold. The same output in a hall is five times over it.

**Ambient Resonance** · Residue while it is still ringing, and therefore not a second quantity. It is read in the same EU per cubic metre as any density, because it is the excess a working left behind on its way out of the ground. It falls by a hundredfold in about seventy-five minutes and by a millionfold in about four hours. That is how long ground remembers a working, and how long it remembers a battle. Resonance does not lower a ceiling. It raises a floor. Every reach that depends on being heard above the ground, which is addressing, authority and anchoring, falls as the square root of the total local density. Twenty practitioners working hard over a two-hundred-metre front leave ground on which a Harmonist hears to about two fifths of his ordinary range until it quiets.

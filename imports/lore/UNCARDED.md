# The Uncarded

These are the people the scene archive keeps meeting who have no card yet. Each entry below is compiled from the archived scenes and nothing else: who the person is, where the archive shows them, what they did, which carded characters they are bound to, and what the archive leaves open. Nothing here is invented, and no number appears that a scene does not state; every claim carries the scene file it comes from. None of this is a card. A card for any of them would come through `convert_character` on Isaac's word, and until then these pages are a record of what the archive already says and a guard against a second hand contradicting it.

## Set aside

- **Lambert.** The bare surname belongs to the carded Edward Lambert, and the archive also uses it for his brother Charles (entered below under his own name), for the infant Dabney Lambert, and for others of the house; it is an alias, not a person, and is skipped here.
- **Darius.** His card is *Ignatius Sanctus Sanctorum Arsenal · The Archpaladin* (`build/aliases.yaml`); skipped.
- **The Arctic Lion** and **Tōga (冬牙)** are pages of Sodoku Moto's own section; skipped.
- **Sonzai Moto** belongs to another creator; nothing is written here.
- **Haruki Yuno** belongs to another creator; nothing is written here.

## Kujo

- Kujo is the Void Regent, ten feet six inches tall with long black hair, who wears a beautiful pale human face over a true form of black and grey armoured flesh, crimson channels, a horned crown, massed tendrils and an execution limb [the_house_of_abscene_kujo_arc.md; commencement_of_the_curia_kujo_arc.md].
- He stands in the corpse of Vaeloris with his five Void Generals, drags his palace through the breach, declares that the Material Plane shall become the new home of Aelor, and plans a slow campaign that means to use the Dawi of Stannvaard rather than kill them [the_house_of_abscene_kujo_arc.md].
- He is Regent over Lucious Dreadveil, the High Chancellor whose authority over the Void Vanguard and the Prayers of the Hollow sits beneath his, and he takes the Judger fragment out of Lucious's hand without asking [the_house_of_abscene_kujo_arc.md].
- He tore the Crevice out of the Titan Valmir and drove it into himself, so that his Void can make a thing fail slowly and in full knowledge of its failing instead of simply ending it [commencement_of_the_curia_kujo_arc.md].
- Kaalabad is his general, and his generals ride at the head of the column that Seiji Tenrai Moto and Wren Greymane march north with [commencement_of_the_curia_kujo_arc.md; 01_wotr_muster_breach_road_north.md].
- At Kharven he fights Sonzai Moto and Dawn Guard, who once severs his head, and he kills Zuberi Zettari when Zuberi puts himself in front of the shot Kujo fired at Asante [commencement_of_the_curia_kujo_arc.md; 14_darius_two_men_who_would_not_take_the_seat.md].
- Verinus VII sets a seal on him that takes pieces of him and leaves the man intact, Charles Lambert puts himself into his grip in the narrows, Darius strikes him above the breaking city, and he lays a hand on Sodoku Moto's shoulder and calls him his twin son of Fate [03_verinus_wall_between_the_safeguarded.md; 08_charles_what_a_hand_is_for.md; 15_darius_kill_me_first.md; commencement_of_the_curia_kujo_arc.md].
- **Carded ties:** Kaalabad, Verinus VII, Ignatius Sanctus Sanctorum Arsenal, Aurelian Prudentius, Sodoku Moto.
- **Left open:** Isaac's own turns keep him unwritten, "No Kujo line, gesture or interiority" [08_charles_what_a_hand_is_for.md; 03_verinus_wall_between_the_safeguarded.md; 07_sodoku_the_howling_hawk.md], and the archive does not say whose he is to write. The notes to 23_darius put him on the far side of Verinus's Custodia and ask Isaac to confirm it [23_darius_the_fist_of_totality.md]. *The Kujo* of the Ketsuen valley are a people with trade agreements on the western corridor, and nothing on the page joins them to the Regent [the_years_that_were_not_war.md].

## Charles Lambert

- Charles Lambert is a bishop of Sancta Lux and the younger brother of Edward Lambert, with Edward's frame and a pleasant, open face that people tell things to without deciding to; the two have not spoken in nine years [verinus_weight_of_an_infant.md].
- He kneels in the wet ash to Aurelian Prudentius and calls him your holiness [05_aurelian_primate_under_the_wrong_stars.md].
- He brings his infant son Dabney and his wife Anyu Qilak to the basin at Alabaster, where Verinus VII blesses the boy and the iris goes gold, and Verinus asks him to go north with the Kusharan party as his hand [verinus_weight_of_an_infant.md; the_true_king_of_the_north_part_20.md].
- For ten years he carried the Scale amulet of the office called the God Hand, which Aurelian put over his head and Darius signed second; one carries and one spends, and neither is told the other's condition [07_charles_the_imperceptible_district.md].
- In the narrows at Kharven he turns the Scale, enters the Imperceptible District and pays every record of himself, then offers himself into Kujo's grip in place of the one it held, and holds on [07_charles_the_imperceptible_district.md; 08_charles_what_a_hand_is_for.md].
- His death passes into his four-month-old son on a keel north of the Sound, and Sodoku Moto, who had never met him, sees his body on the ditch bank [09_dabney_the_undamped_thing.md; 09_sodoku_the_true_nature.md].
- Edward keeps a small stone for him that reads *Charles Lambert. Archbishop.* and nothing else, because the parish book at Vaeloris no longer holds his dates [10_wotr_what_the_ground_was_owed.md].
- **Carded ties:** Edward Lambert, Aurelian Prudentius, Ignatius Sanctus Sanctorum Arsenal, Verinus VII, Sodoku Moto.
- **Left open:** he is forty-one in one scene and forty-seven in another [05_aurelian_primate_under_the_wrong_stars.md; verinus_weight_of_an_infant.md]; he is a bishop at Alabaster and an archbishop on the stone; and the notes to Part 20 flag that his son Dabney is not yet squared with the carded Dabney Corvalen Olyss [the_true_king_of_the_north_part_20.md].

## Emira Moto

- Emira is Muken Moto's own daughter, sister to Sodoku, Sonzai, Tomuka and Ezo, as Isaac ruled on 2026-09-12 [THE_KINGDOM_OF_KHARVEN_corrected.md].
- At three she squirmed in Ayame's lap while her mother brushed her hair in the east wing [The_Path_of_Sorrow.md].
- At nine she goes into exile with her brother, sleeping behind locked inn doors, and Yōko takes her in her arms and keeps her close on the road east [The_Path_of_Sorrow.md].
- Grown, she catches Sodoku by the wrist and asks to come to Kharven with him, because their father would have done the same [the_true_king_of_the_north_part_17.md].
- At supper Sodoku tells her she was not raised by their parents, and Yoko rests a hand on her shoulder [sodoku_fourteenth_bow_table.md; the_true_king_of_the_north_part_19.md].
- At Kharven she is no fighter; her Harmony heals Sonzai and gives his Calamity a structure without taking it from him, Kujo tells her to keep developing it, and when Sonzai asks her to heal Selvara she leaves spent, telling him not to get himself killed [commencement_of_the_curia_kujo_arc.md].
- At twenty-four, a healer of nine years, she names the empire Whitemere, works beside Brida Ashwell at Yoko's labour, and carries the child out to Sodoku in the corridor [the_revolution_of_the_inner_world.md].
- **Carded ties:** Muken Moto, Ayame Yuno, Sodoku Moto, Yoko Mishiro, Brida Ashwell, Edward Lambert.
- **Left open:** the notes to War in the North III flag that she is nine in the voice roster and a grown woman on the ice, so either she is older there or there are two Emiras [the_war_in_the_north_iii_utopia.md]; the same notes ask whether *Utopia*, where she says the dead brothers watch, is a Moto word or her own [the_war_in_the_north_iii_utopia.md].

## Ilthára Korvaeth

- Ilthára Korvaeth of House Korvaeth is thirty-four at the Vacancy, with the deep Korvaeth build, skin the grey-violet of a four-day bruise, flat white hair, a scar through the left eyebrow, and all eleven coats cut from the drowned alder [01_the_vacancy_korvaeth_arc.md].
- The yard refused her a naming assembly at seventeen, while her younger brother Aeldros, called the Validated, was granted his at nineteen [01_the_vacancy_korvaeth_arc.md].
- She puts her hand on the head of Onawa, Empress of Eresse, her friend since the orchard at fourteen, names her back in full form with the witness word said three times, and the blade comes down [01_the_vacancy_korvaeth_arc.md].
- At fifty-one she holds nine hundred miles, has never been called Empress by anyone with standing, signs with the name nobody gave her, and refuses the Sylvaaren self-witness with "I will not hold my name from a forest I have never stood in" [01_the_vacancy_korvaeth_arc.md].
- Her terms on the coast road are the Empire restored and nobody hanged, in exchange for Ruvaen Aloreth petitioning the yard at Velthaeir to void his own name [the_terms.md].
- Ruvaen, Onawa's brother, says she was trained from the day she could stand and dubbed at nine, and that she once asked his sister to come and hold her hand in a gallery at Rovann [the_brink.md; the_vey_elarin.md].
- She has taken the ridge eleven times and held it none, and she goes out through the Deep Gate saying the city must survive for her children [a_name_held_in_common.md; the_empty_place.md].
- Ruvaen finds her alone on the field in plate with no crest on the breast and tells her to look down [the_field_where_they_picked_them.md].
- **Carded ties:** none; the Vacancy's own notes strike the carded Onawa Ashkewe from the Empress's place [01_the_vacancy_korvaeth_arc.md].
- **Left open:** the notes to the Brink pitch that she was the refused tenth of the Thaelun, which is not ruled [the_brink.md]; the field ends before the duel.

## Dhaerin Valorin

- Dhaerin Valorin is a white-haired scholar of the Valorin line who fights with grimoire and wand, his Scripted Soul Lattice rising in gold along his throat when he works [Cozbi_Rengai_Dhaerin_Confrontation.md; Cozbi_Antithesis_Split_Fight.md; Dhaerin_Final_Rite_Sacrament_Oblation_Rescind.md].
- He raised his nephew Xhem into a man who can run an academy, and he leaves Vaeloris in Xhem's hands to go with Rengai [Rengai_Farewell_and_Dhaerin.md; Dhaerin_Final_Rite_Sacrament_Oblation_Rescind.md].
- He walks for months beside Rengai Zettari to the clearing where Cozbi waits, and Cozbi taunts him that he never matched his siblings and leaned on cleverness instead [Cozbi_Rengai_Dhaerin_Confrontation.md; Cozbi_Antithesis_Split_Fight.md].
- Every rune he laid through the fight turns out to be one node of a recursive array that pulls stolen spirit out of Cozbi's architecture [Dhaerin_Magnum_Opus_Recursive_Array.md].
- Caught across the ribs by one of Cozbi's spikes, he performs a Sacrament layered with an Oblation and a Rescind, withdraws the world's consent from Cozbi's authority over the spirits he harvested, and dies in the ash [Cozbi_True_Power_Antithesis.md; Dhaerin_Final_Rite_Sacrament_Oblation_Rescind.md].
- Cozbi leaves the clearing sealed and takes Rengai with him, Mira carries the news of Dhaerin's death to Niran, and Cozbi later calls it a genuinely elegant working for someone who died executing it while showing how much of him it did not reach [Cozbi_Sealed_Retreat.md; Niran_Mira_Awakening.md; Cozbi_Merged_Form_Rescind_Explanation.md].
- **Carded ties:** Rengai Zettari, Cozbi Mahuo, Niran Yukari, Dougou Ozumu Zettari.
- **Left open:** his siblings are never named, and Xhem, who manages Vaeloris after him, belongs to another creator.

# WEAVE · the cross ties and the rulings

The forty-one cluster records were written apart. This file makes them one world. It lists, for every cluster, the ties that run from its members into other clusters, and the rulings that change its record. The rulings follow in full at the end.

**Count.** 139 cross ties over 41 clusters. Every cluster holds at least two, and every one of the 276 cards already holds at least one tie inside its own record, so every character ends with at least one.

**Canon order.** Isaac's direction, the card, the archived scenes, the wiki, the cluster records, this file. A tie fills silence and contradicts no card and no scene. Each tie carries its basis in parentheses: card, scene, wiki, ruling, or new where this file supplies it. A new tie gives nobody a number, a Stage or a year, puts no thought in Sodoku Moto's head, and gives no act to a character of another creator.

**Left without a cross tie.** Where an outward want points at an uncarded person or an office no card holds, and no carded member of another cluster fits it without inventing a history or crossing an era, the want is left as its record states it. Wystan Ashmore takes no tie into Malphas's cell, by Isaac's direction for the book.

### R1 · Two Dougous

**Issue.** The record c04 reads the Dougou who broke Gimbzo on the rim of the unfinished world in the First Eon as c09's Dougou Ozumu Zettari. The record c09 makes Dougou Ozumu Zettari the father of Rengai, who is in his twenties in the archive's present, and the companion of Yorime, whose card sets her in the Current Era. **Ruling.** Two lives under one name. Gimbzo's Lore uses his card's words, "Dougou, the Iron Bison Maximus", with the maul and the shoulder-yoke, and sets him in the First Eon on no family tree. Dougou Ozumu Zettari's Lore follows his own card, Yorime's and the scenes of his son. His card has him carry nothing and names no duel. No Lore joins the two. None says whose blood carries the residue Gimbzo will look for. The record c09 is the better attested, so c04's outward line for Gimbzo and its note "one house named twice" are withdrawn. Kaalabad's "give me the Zettari" stays his own line in his own scene.

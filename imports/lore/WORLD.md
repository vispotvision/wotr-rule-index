## 1 The eras in order

One count runs from the sealing of the Concord Codex, Year Zero, back to −1,400 and forward without a break (CA). The Epochs are a second register, cosmological and uncounted, never placed on the count or reconciled with it (ER §II).

| Span | Name | What defines it | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| uncounted | **The Three Epochs** | Bairos writes meaning, Aegor builds form, Ashura remembers why. The Moto, Zettari and Yukari trace to them; the Interstitium falls between the Second and Third. Epoch figures state magnitude, never years | TE, AB, YK, ER |
| −1,400 to −900 | **The First Eon** | The emplacement: every ore body, the northern shield, the southern flood basalt, the Crevice. It ends where the first kept record begins | CA, FC |
| −900 to −450 | **The Antediluvian Calendar** | Cities built on Wellsprings; practitioners hauled in and used up; the ring that sorted people, ancestor of the Coil. Collapse by exhaustion, about −550 to −450 | CA, ER |
| −450 to 000 | **The Reconstruction Age** | Notation: Parunic syntax, Kessian Yorul's Living Runes, Mnirah Valein's graded Stages, Runebronze. Early part −450 to −225; the late Reconstruction kingdoms, a trading region with no single polity, −225 to −60 | CA, ER |
| 000 to 070 IC | **The Voyager Era** | The Accord, the Codex, the Lattice, the Divisions, the Tiered Path, the first nine Gates. The chart opens the routes; eastern placer gold doubles western prices | CA |
| 070 to 645 IC | **The Long Reckoning** | The ceilings fall: water about 90 to 130, air 150 to 175, heat 260 to 310. Haulage never falls. The west comes to depend on its arrays | CA, FC |
| 645 IC to open | **The Withering Era** | Western Wellspring density falls every year; supply is bought like grain; nobody can state the cause | CA |

The last three are eras inside the Imperial Age (CA). Four counts run at once: the Imperial year, the Moto nengo corrected to the Sower's rising, the Dawi Tally by entry number, the Eressean rite cycle; Kharven reckons by the return of the grass (SHY). The New World keeps its own calendar (SP-K).

## 2 The present

Five State of Play pages carry the table, at different moments.

- **Kharven, the table's point.** Kharven-Seat, the year after the ninth hour (SP-S). On the Ashgate road Wren Greymane turned six hundred spears on the Tenrai; Hild Ice was killed at twelve in a cistern doorway; the Tenrai front closed. In the spring the first Accord circle was signed in the lower hall: Kharven, the Holy Sea of Alabaster, Nalūn, and the stretches from the Brine crossing to the marches of Sum-gol, with the Moto lines seated as the royal house (SP-S). SP-H is closed record.
- **The archive runs further.** `scenes/the_revolution_of_the_inner_world.md`, archived after SP-S was last rewritten, carries the Inner World to the sixth year: the orc host out of the Ashenmoor starved; the Great Battle of Stormfold; the Tenrai remnant taken in; Stannvaard annexed; rail from the Ring to the coast and then the wire; Nalūn sealed in the sixth year. It names the empire Whitemere, which the Kharven call *the Stack*; SP-S keeps the circle unnamed. Conflict.
- **The dead.** Hild Ice, Lorn Stark and Osric of Hallenfeld lie under three stones at Kharven-Seat; Charles Lambert has a fourth stone with no dates, and no record says what became of him (same scene). Darius is dead (`scenes/WOTR_Vaeloris_Sequence.md`). Muken, Ayame and Tomuka died in one night long before (KH).
- **The Academy.** About thirteen years on: Rikudoku 13, Sodoku 35, Mu-jin 38 (SP-A). No page locates it.
- **Hon-guk.** Kwon Mu-jin in the Soul Kingdom, a thin thread (SP-M).
- **The New World war.** Another continent. The Korvaeth arc stands eleven years on, the Landward Suppression in its eleventh year at Vaelmarr (`scenes/the_southern_passing.md`); Sunroot voted a regency under Ovaeren; the Inner World does not touch it yet (SP-K). The Tsohanto Reach and the Velthaeir archive are the seam (SP-S). Whether Ilthára said the name is Isaac's open ruling (SP-K).
- **Everywhere.** The Withering goes on; the Accord's reach ends where the wire ends, and the Lease-holders take the cut districts (ME, FQ).

**What "Where They Stand" may assume.** The latest moment the card or a scene gives the person. Failing both, the Inner World of the year after the ninth hour: the Tenrai front closed, Hild dead, the circle signed. Later years only as the revolution scene states them; the New World only for New World lives; no year number.

## 3 Polities

**The Inner World.**
- Kharven, the Ashen Crown: a feudal monarchy on frozen ground, a Moto crown over six Banner Houses, outside the Accord's typology (KH, AP).
- Nalūn, the Chain-Cities: a merchant oligarchy cut from salt, whose seals enforce themselves (NA).
- Ketsuen, the Crow's Reach: a meritocracy grown from Niran Yukari's ward-craft (KE).
- Stannvaard, the Metal-Wardens: a Dawi forge-state in the Ring (ST).
- Altherion: the multiracial republic on the Ring's rim, seat of the Grand Church (ST, SL).
- Undaar-Keth, the Closed Fist: a hobgoblin decree-state on the western shield (UK).
- Anguz: guild-controlled, ruled a real northern place (IW).
- The Concord: the heartland's composite monarchy, with no crown or seat on any page (AP, ME).
- The Holy Sea of Alabaster: ruled by the Palatine at the Alabaster Court; its Wellspring weighs testimony; no quarter ruled (IW, KH; card Verinus VII · The Palatine).

**The Old World.** Varūn, the Dawi kingdom of the deep workings (OW); the holds, keeping two columns (ME, SHY).

**Between the quarters.** The Hobgoblin Expanse, Vorruk-Khal, a parliament that never signed the Codex (HE). Yasoshima, the Yuno's islands, under *hyōjō* (YN, YA).

**The New World.** The elven old-ground: the Grand Principality of Eresse, Iampu and its Seasonal Courts, Rovann, the Sylvaar Dream Groves, the Echo Elves' Longhouse Accord, Velthaeir, the Sunroot Court at Vaelmarr (NW). The Accord-chartered arc: Caedor, Foraye, the Tsohanto Reach (NW, ME). Korvaeth (SP-K).

**The Outer World.** Two unreconciled accounts (SW): the ledger's Braqthian confederations, Dorrosfenn and Epprenea; the continent's Draconic Empire of Drakyssia, the Kingdoms of the Five Rivers (Valecar, Therosan, Calverne, Ordas, the Free March of Saren), Veyran, the Elyran Green Crowns.

**Beyond.** Kushara, a separate landmass under House Zettari and Dawn Guard (KU; K8 still says "Old World"). Utopia, home of the Celestial Host (CH). The Soul Kingdom (SP-M). Gone: the High Empire of Eresse (HEE), the Long Reading at Alabaster (LR).

**Struck; never written.** Sātūlagi (MO, MB). Babyl, the Babylian Imperium, Karn-Bael (cards Varuun Daskorr, Juno Petros Marien); CA still has "a Babylan theorist". The Holy See of Lurien (SW). Nevara (NW, SL). Vāimoana, Wadatsumi, the Agamalu identity (KH; `build/renames.yaml`). Sylvenn Thaeren, Marrovel, Vaelor, Greysalt as a chapter-hold, Onawa Ashkewe (SP-K). Muken's three-heir roster, Nergüi, Sükh, Nomin (KH). Terminals, projected sigils, floating numbers, Aether credits, echo marks (GA). The withdrawn thirty, twelve and twenty-five thousand years, and the superseded era names (CA, ER). The Büri register: `LAW.md` §3. **Crowns' bodies.** The Ashen Crown, Banner Oaths, Ashen Guard (KH). The Council of Links and High Chainlord (NA). The Ore Council; Altherion's River Crown Council (ST). The Ironfold Council (UK). ## 6 Peoples and lifespans

No page gives any people a lifespan in years. CA allows that "a long-lived person can have stood in more than one age".

- **Humans.** Nothing grants long life; no page ties years to Stage, and Moetana spends lifespan (`The Disciplines/Moetana — The Vessel of Life's Essence.md`).
- **Elves.** Eressean, Varrisak, Drow, Eladrin, Echo Elves. EP calls their longevity exceptional, with no figure.
- **Dawi.** High Dwarves, Barrel Dwarves, Deepkin, Kuntur Reach, Tallow Clans, Windward Herds, Tower-Kin, Passwardens (DP).
- **Goblinoids.** Ironblood, Verdant and Blackened Orcs; Crimson Order Hobgoblins; Tinker and Fleshshaper Goblins; Bugbears. Most Tinker Goblins die before forty (GP, HE).
- **Beastkin.** Catkin, Lupine, Bearkin, Fox-Spirit (Mishiro), Serpentkin, Avian, Aquatic, Chimeric-Born. Undocumented beastkin are labour stock; demihumans default free (BD).
- **Ashkaru.** Not mortal; older than the Planes' separation; they did not die (AK).
- **The Celestial Host.** No childhood, no lineage; none confirmed Sung since the Shattering (CH).
- **Dragons.** Four parts in five of the southern continent; the oldest sleep in the Dragon Catacombs (SW).

**Across eras.** Only where the card says so: Aurgon Mintruix, Gimbzo, Iracordas, Vethraun Ashmaw, Valthrum, the Yukari founders. Anyone else lives one life in one era, and a Lore never lengthens it.

## 7 Bloodlines and houses in canon

- **The Moto.** From Bara and Mizuki; the Shingan and Hataraki (AB, MB). Seven lines: Byakuya, Shirogane, Akagane, Kurenai, Tenrai, Amagiri, Kōkan; the six besides Kōkan are landless (MB). Shirogane, Akagane and Amagiri keep the mint, the conduit-metal and the post (P20). The Refusal, the Stay, the Recension (LR).
- **Open on the Moto.** The seat: the western reach of the shield (MO, MB) or the white shelf under Alabaster (LR, SO). Its ruin: Malakar's splitting (MO), a worked Core concentration (P20), or a massacre by an unnamed hand (KH). The woman in the cabin: Yukazuri (MB; her card) or Saruul (MO, LR, KH). Muken's children: five, ruled (KH), or contested (P20).
- **The Zettari.** Path-born, Second Epoch; now Rashani, Ma'Kovu, Zuberi, Rengai (ZE); Kushara's Sovereign House (K8).
- **The Yukari.** The Ketsumyōgan and Senri (YK); Niran founded Ketsuen (KE).
- **The Yuno.** Keepers of the *ma* on Yasoshima; Ayame Yuno was Muken's queen (YN, KH). FBI still names this house Agamalu.
- **The Mahuo.** Sum-gol (KE); their page is a stub (`The Archaic Bloodlines/The Mahuo Family.md`).
- **The Seikai.** An Ashuric line (YK; card Yorime Seikai).
- **Kharven's Banner Houses.** Stark, Greymane, Ashmore, Thornwall, Keld, Hallenfeld (BK).
- **Kushara's Eight.** Zettari, N'Kharu, Vokari, Malani, Obaru, N'Yari, Jomari, Zembari (K8).
- **Drakyssia's Ten.** Drakhalis, Dovaryn, Zhaeryn, Avarax, Maelkar, Ormyr, Rhaegyr, Eredan, Ishkar, Akhazar (D10).
- **In the scenes.** The Lamberts, the Holy Sea's representatives; Nalūn's fourteen factor houses (`scenes/the_revolution_of_the_inner_world.md`). The Korvaeth and Sunroot lines (SP-K).

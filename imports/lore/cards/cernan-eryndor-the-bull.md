## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Thalyren's groves and the highland plains. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Cernan Eryndor was born in the Outer World, on open plain that no house held. His father carried the name Eryndor, and a rumour came with it: that the line descended from the Eryndor bloodline of Lowmere. His father never claimed the rumour and never denied it, and nobody on the plain could prove it either way. It passed to the son.

The boy was slow at letters and did not go back to them. When the boys of the plain wrestled, he was the hardest of them to move. The ones who threw him did it with a false step, a feint one way and the throw the other. They threw him that way all through his boyhood. He never learned to see it coming. He closed sooner and hit harder, and most bouts ended before anyone had room for a false step.

### The Making

He left the plain young. He crossed the Outer World on foot and stood wherever its ground lay undefended. Houses and Guild parties offered him commissions. He turned down every one. The people behind him called him a warden. Since he served no banner, the name that stayed was Unaligned Warden of the Outer World. They called him the Ironblood Rampart as well, and the Bull.

His method was mass in a straight line. He committed his whole body to the charge, and the ground went with him. Whatever he struck gave along its whole face at once, formation or wall or gate or rank, and the blow carried through the first body into everything standing behind it. He did not tire when other men would have. After a charge he slept eleven hours, and nobody could wake him.

The charge had one condition. He had to reach its object in a straight line. The commanders who beat him all found the same answer. Each showed him a target, let him commit, and stood somewhere else when he arrived. He lost to a feint every time he lost.

Then came the year he first saw himself from outside. For a man whose whole method was not thinking, it was the worst year of his life. He has never described it.

### The Cost

The charge was paid for in the sleep after it. Something held him together through the exertion, and the holding was not free. For those hours he lay where the charge had ended, and anyone could reach him. An unaligned warden had no company to stand over him. He had turned down every offer of one.

An Outer Realm beast came down out of the high country onto the Stonehoof warplains, and he met it first. He was warding that ground on nobody's commission. He charged it across the open plain. The beast was out of the line when he arrived. The charge ran on through empty ground and ploughed a furrow in it, and at the end of the furrow he went down asleep.

Kaelen Brighthoof was scouting the ridges for the Stonehoof Herd. He found the furrow, the sleeping man at its end, and the beast's track bending back toward him. He sent a hawk to the herd. Garruk Stonehoof brought the Stonehoof up and set them in a ring about Cernan on the open ground. He held them there through the day and did not go after the beast. At dusk it came back for the sleeping man. Kaelen put an arrow where it mattered, and the herd ran the beast down before it reached the ring. Cernan woke inside the ring.

He had not asked the Stonehoof for that day. He owed them for it all the same. What he charged had not been in the line, which was the old loss again, taken from a beast this time.

He kept the straight line afterward, and it cost him every fight a feint could win. He kept serving no one. Nobody was bound to stand over him when he slept.

### Where They Stand

Cernan Eryndor is still the Unaligned Warden of the Outer World, and he still holds no commission. He charges in a straight line. Every commander who has beaten him has beaten him with a feint. After each charge he sleeps past waking.

He owes the Stonehoof Herdclan a day on their warplains. Garruk Stonehoof holds the debt, a chieftain who charges as Cernan does and loses to a feint as Cernan does. Kaelen Brighthoof has seen him asleep at the end of his own furrow, beyond waking.

A presence called the Raging Bull is resident in him. Nobody worships it or summons it.

The rumour of Lowmere still goes with his name, and he has never described the year he first saw himself from outside.

## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Rot and bloom. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Chimwala N'Doro was born to a tribe of the Innerworld whose honored dead were kept at the Ancestral Grove. The grove's eldest tree stood over them. He grew up among the druids of the Verdant Covenant, an earth-aligned order, who taught him to read ground before they let him work it.

From the heartwood of the eldest tree he began a staff, grown from seeds the Covenant had blessed and watered with dew fed from a Wellspring. It took years. He raised a sapling of the Heartbloom Tree from seed as well. Season after season he bound it to himself, until its bark closed around his left forearm and hardened. It never came off again.

### The Making

At the Siege of the Jade Bridge he beat a hand drum of hollowed ironwood, and the druid warbands moved to its rhythm as one. At the Battle of the Thorned Crossing, spectral javelins came down on wounded druids. He took the volley on the bracer. The force went through the bark into the ground. His lantern of goldwood roots went with him on every Wellspring cleansing, through the Fog of Black Roots among them.

After what the record calls the Treaty of Green Sky, the Sylvaar gave him a curved leaf-blade of greensteel whose colour turned with the season. He wore it strapped across his back. In battle he drew it seldom, so as not to overtax the land's seasonal cycle.

His torque was forged beneath the Innerworld Grove, at the Sanctum of the Thousand Voices, of braided elephant hair, gold leaf and carved jadeite, and of ivory the tribe's honored dead had given. Night after night of communion bound into it the voices of the Grovekeepers before him. When it was done, the world read to him as symbol. A thing kept its weight and gained a second one. Both were real. A tusk was a tusk and was also a record. He cut what he knew into his own tusks and carried it on his face from then on.

### The Cost

He held that the earth "remembers the soul of those who walk it," and he wore his own dead at his throat. They held him to the Covenant's code. Had he broken it, they would have grown restless and harder to hold quiet. Called up too often, their memories thinned the barrier between his soul and theirs, and the voices could take him. At the Verdant War Council he opened the torque to past Grovekeepers while a treaty was argued.

Rotwood, above the lakes of Ketsuen, was dying when Aivyriel came into it, and the standing dead flowered from the bark and rotted from inside the flowers. Chimwala came up from the lakes with druids of the Covenant to reclaim it. Before the first cut he went to the wood's edge where she stood. The blade stayed across his back. They spoke there.

Then he began the rite, one season to each day. A break anywhere would have left the ground half cleansed and unstable, so he did not leave the site. The blade went green on the first day and gold on the second. On the third it went red, and the seed-gem in its pommel darkened with what the cut drew out. On the fourth it went silver-white. Each day her bloom drew back from the opened ground, and she did not call it forward. The Winter cut closed the rite, and on the fifth morning she was gone. He had never drawn the blade against her.

One of the younger druids he taught, a beastkin herbalist from the lakeshore, had kept the watch through all four days. When the rite closed she could not stop. Flowers opened on the backs of her hands and did not close, and the herbs drying in her satchel put out roots. Ground turned fertile under his own step, and foliage opened on him when he channelled; everything he had to give her was more growth. He sent along the lake road for Kaien Rhuven, the herbalist the Wellspring-consumed were carried to. Kaien asked her for a story first. Then he set her breathing to the pace of his own and kept it there until the flowers on her hands closed one at a time. Chimwala stayed through all of it. Kaien left with her story and asked nothing of him.

### Where They Stand

Chimwala N'Doro is Sage of the Emerald Path, Spirit-Glyph Weaver and Keeper of the Ancestral Grove, advisor to the Council of the Innerworld Grove and Spirit Envoy to the Treaty Assembly. He mentors the younger druids of the Innerworld, beastkin herbalists above all. On the grove round he knows Moryn Sporetail.

The staff leaves his hands only when it is entrusted to a sacred rite, and the torque goes on for every Grove assembly and spirit rite. On barren ground the staff goes inert and the bracer barely mends him.

Rotwood is reclaimed, and everything that died in it is within Aivyriel's reach. His student lives. He owes Kaien Rhuven for her.

- **Kaien Rhuven · The Silent Apothege** · debt. Kaien brought his student back from the Rotwood growth and asked him for nothing.

## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Reaper's office. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Severin Bale was born in the slate belts of the Old World, in the gaol of a quarry town, where the keeping of the keys was chartered to his family. His father carried the ring. The cells were cut into the hillside and floored with slate that sweated in the wet months, and the boy learned to set his feet flat on it. Most of the men behind the doors were quarrymen held for debt or a fight, and many had the grinder's cough.

Each morning the court's clerk climbed the hill with the roll and read the names aloud at the gate. His father opened those doors and no others. One morning a quarry steward came up after the clerk had gone, with a purse and the name of a man he wanted let out. That name had not been read. His father kept his hand closed on the ring until the steward went back down the hill. A name read from the roll moved a key. When the steward said it, the ring stayed on the belt.

### The Making

He took service with the Guild Accord, and the Black Watch took him in for its night work: Rot purges, rogue Mechanica interdictions, brandcraft counter-ops. He learned to enter a den on a straight line, blade low, hip quiet, shoulders square only at the end.

Orokh Sable-Forge made his blade. The smith would not work fresh kill, and for a weapon he took material, a story and a confession. Severin paid all three at the anvil. What he confessed went into the billet, and neither man has repeated it. Gallowsgate came off the Ash-Anvil a slab-edged greatblade with a locking spine and a coffin guard. Its steel holds a Domain seam without screaming. With the spine latched on a halberd, a flat-edge shove emptied a man's lungs and left his belly whole, when the law wanted a breathing answer.

The kill-writs came to him ratified by Yukizumi Yukari, the Reaper, who could not escort a writ that read kill. Severin carried them. On one entry he pointed Gallowsgate across a room at the man the writ named, and he spoke the name. Everything that protected that life grew heavy. Shields sagged. Lies stumbled. The name kept its weight and gained a second one, and both were operative. It was a name and it was also a sentence. He spoke of a person and of a verdict at once, and the world took the second reading.

### The Cost

He lost a name once, in a Rot den under a tannery. The men in it would not stand and fight. They drew him from cellar to cellar and shot down the stairwells at his back, and he spent all three Gravepins holding corridors that emptied as soon as they were held. By dawn the man he had named was gone. Everything that should have grown heavy around that name grew heavy around him, and it stayed on him for a week. He climbed stairs under it and buckled his plate under it.

After that week he spoke no name until the road to it was straight. On the White Pans of the Varakh Drylands a writ the Reaper had ratified ran south against a practitioner of predatory brandcraft. The practitioner reached an oasis and took water under Khan Kirv's cup, and for that night the hospitality covered the guest. Severin waited the night out. At dawn the Khan opened a lawful corridor from the oasis across the salt, and Severin drove his Gravepins at its mouth and spoke the name there, outside the ring. Rain came across the pans and blurred the Khan's mirage sense, and Khasir Aluto set isoclines along the corridor and kept the evidence dry. The writ closed clean. From then on he named at the mouth of the Khan's corridor, and never under the cup.

He believed the judging was finished before a writ reached his hand. The Reaper ratified, and Severin carried. He committed to a name before he could fully assess it, and what he waited for was the road.

### Where They Stand

He is Head Captain of the Black Watch. Every Slayer cell across the Plateau theatres answers to him, Dell's among them, and Dell holds the same dread title in the Western Theater. He trains the regional Slayers and sets their thresholds for when not to swing, and he still leads the entries on Rot dens, predatory brandcraft sites and Amalgam hunts.

He takes the violent writs so that the Reaper can keep rooms human. When a writ must be served through a crush, Marigold Vance cuts the aisle, and on siege doors he plants the coffin guard while Linebreaker Kaelith rips. Moros Pellayne consults for the Black Watch.

He wears white stormcoat plate that will not snag, and its buckles double as oath-seals for sanctioning on site. Skirmishers and snipers still make him spend his Gravepins fast. He has never named the wrong person.

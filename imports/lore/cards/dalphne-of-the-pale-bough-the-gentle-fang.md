## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Class X and what it grows into. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Dalphne of the Pale Bough was born among nomads, beastkin who moved with the seasons through wild country and kept no house. He walked on snow before he walked on floors. As a small child he would stop on a trail, lay his palm flat on the ground and stay there with his head down, and the others learned to wait for him. Nobody could hurry him.

The settled country was dangerous to his kind. In most territories under the Accord, a beastkin who could show no guild, no employer and no recognised bloodline could be penned and sold at market, and the sale broke no law. His people kept off the roads.

The Grey Hollow Circle took him in young. It was a druidic guild, and its druids heard in the boy what his people had learned to wait on. They taught him to listen on purpose. He learned to hum a frightened animal quiet, and to speak the lost dialects of older beasts, dryads and sky-spirits. His name on the Circle's roll was the paper that kept him out of the pens.

### The Making

The Circle dissolved. Its druids went to other orders and other work, or died, and the roll that had carried his name was kept by no one. Dalphne went on practising its arts by himself, on the move. He brought lost travellers out of country with no road in it, and he never once lost his own way. In villages where the herds would not settle, he laid a hand on the lead animal and hummed until it stood still.

Then the final thought-wave of Xanelor Rafminar reached him. The two had never met. It gave him access to the root of the Wolf Sage Arts. An assay would read what came with it as a foreign structure, grown cleanly into his own. The Archives would have wanted to know. Nobody told them.

He took the wound of the Circle's ending and made it hold weight on purpose. A dissolved institution went on in one man, carried forward alone and practised well enough that the Guild Accord offered him a position. It offered again. He declined each time.

### The Cost

Once, the wolf Tsuuna put himself between Dalphne and his death. Tsuuna came out of it without his body. He walks beside Dalphne now partly as spirit, going into snow, wind and fog and coming back out of them with no sound at all.

With a touch or a hum, Dalphne can bring a creature's distress to its end early. What he shortens, he carries. For a very long time he has been shortening other people's grief. Afterward he swings past calm and back again on every cycle, and he does not settle.

The hum works on the wild. A person who has decided something cannot be hummed out of it.

He keeps his own force low and holds no ground, though he has been offered better. He does not strike unless he is cornered. Every position the Accord offered would have put a guild's name back on him. He declined each one, and he walks roads where a beastkin with no guild can still be sold. He carries an inheritance that was never his to begin with, and he has entered it on no register.

He once traded Hyo Miran a bone-carved compass for shelter and dried meats. Dalphne never gets lost. He had no use for the compass, and Hyo knew it when he took it.

### Where They Stand

Dalphne is unaffiliated by choice. In the Outer World Codex of Sages he is an "Unclaimed Guide", entered without a guild. When a seal will not open, the Guild Accord sends for him, and he speaks to it in the old dialects until it opens. Each time, the officer who sends for him asks whether he will take a position. He declines. Some say his power surpasses several Masters.

He is called the Gentle Fang, and by some the Last Echo of Xanelor. He honours Xanelor Rafminar as a distant mentor. Nobody knows whether that final thought-wave was sent or simply escaped, or whether Tsuuna was Dalphne's before it arrived.

He goes on through wild country on foot. At his side, where the fog lies thickest, Tsuuna keeps pace without a sound.

### Ties

- **Tsuuna** · wolf familiar. The wolf gave up his body to shield him from death.
- **Xanelor Rafminar · The Wandering Fang** · successor. He received Xanelor's final thought-wave, and the root of the Wolf Sage Arts, from a man he never met.
- **Hyo Miran · The Velvet Dealer** · barter. He traded Hyo a compass he had no use for, and Hyo calls him honest.
- **Guild Accord** · courted. He keeps declining the position it keeps offering him.

## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The sainted, the fallen and the vessel. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Evangelien Rhymaris was made in Elenthyr during the city's final siege. Before that she was a woman of the city, and she was chosen as the vessel bride of a divine union. The soul ritual that was to make the union was ancient. It was held while the siege went on.

She dressed for it in wedding garments. She put on the veil. She loved a man. His name is gone now, even from her own memory, and the record keeps his death and nothing else of him.

Elenthyr kept a choir as well, the Weeping Choir, and she is called its herald. The Choir is lost.

### The Making

Her beloved was slain, and the ritual continued, empty. It did not sever her soul and it did not complete it. Her soul hung suspended in lamentation and gathered into something neither dead nor ascended. The rite had been begun, and nobody closed it.

Her body went on existing. It walked out of the rite in the wedding garments and kept walking, through the ruins of Elenthyr and out across the Inner World. Spectral ink and spiritual decay soaked into the cloth. Over time she began to weep blood.

The blood came from the unbearable pressure of being remembered by the land itself. The ground under Elenthyr remembered the city, and she was the place where that memory showed. The pressure did not ease. In time she stopped being the one who had to give way. The world around her adjusted, locally and grudgingly, and it has never adjusted back. The land reached that height on her behalf. She had asked for none of it.

The Guild of Ritus calls her a metaphysical anomaly and cannot classify her. Its instruments are built to read what a soul did. In her, nothing was done.

### The Cost

She lost his name and kept the veil. In her words: "I do not remember his name… but I still wear the veil. That is enough."

A rite that was begun and never closed cannot be added to. Blessings pass through her unfulfilled, and pure divine force does not touch her. She cannot be comforted. She cannot be made worse.

A slow hymn follows her. It is what a room does when she is in it. Everyone inside who has lost someone begins to mourn their own dead, and the mourning comes on them as a weight in the body. Spirits and weaker souls weep past controlling. She cannot stop the hymn and has never tried. She has never attacked anyone. Every hour of it drives her further past what her making can hold. She is held at a height she did not climb to, by a land that will not let go of her.

Her tears heal or kill, depending on the feeling behind them. Black-market mourn-priests harvest them and sell them. Once Emilia Veirynthiel came to Elenthyr, found her walking the ruins and walked beside her. Emilia took the tears as they fell and sold none of them. Emilia bound them into a song and set it down in one of the forbidden books of the Liminal Archive, where no bard has dared to sing it. Those tears went out of the mourn-priests' reach. Since that day each has counted the other kin in unbeing.

### Where They Stand

Evangelien walks the Inner World in wedding garments soaked with spectral ink, and she walks the ruins of Elenthyr. There is arterial red at her tear-line and black-violet in the air around her, and an onlooker's body answers it before the mind has caught up. All who look on her see someone they mourned. The unbereaved walk through the hymn unaffected. There are very few of them. The one recorded way out is to leave, and whoever is weeping too hard to walk has to be carried out by someone else.

In the ruins of Elenthyr, Azure Serathiel saw her wandering and offered a silent bow. She bowed back. No words passed. In the halls of a ruined shrine among the same ruins, with blood on her face from the tears, she met Ambrose Virellith in the dress meant for death. They bowed to one another and passed without a word, each going on the way the other had come. Nobody knows whom Ambrose saw in her. No account puts these meetings in order.

Fringe cults of Sanctum Lux fear and worship her. So do the Thorn-Seers. The Flock of Thorns calls her Saint Erasure and copies her pain, believing it will carry them past mortality. The Bench of Attribution has never been asked to rule on whether she counts as a source. A rumour puts her in the Dead Garden.

She is bound to a forgotten Wellspring deep beneath the Inner World's crust. Nobody knows whether the Wellspring is bound to her or she to it, or whether it lay there before Elenthyr fell. No record names the man she wears the veil for, and she has lost the name herself.

## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Babyl and the Praetorian March. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Juno Petros Marien was born into House Marien, a noble house that had faded long before him into bureaucratic quiet. Its name still stood on the old rolls. Its people kept registers, witnessed seals and signed for orders that other men had given. As a boy he copied charters in the house's registry. He was slow. He never blotted a page, and he read the old charters through to the last clause, even the ones nobody had opened in living memory.

One by one the others who carried the name died, and he became the last living heir of House Marien. He kept the name and let its pride go.

### The Making

He entered the service of the House of Civil Flame, the administrative lineage of rational governance. When the assay houses read him, they found his core dense and unremarkable and wrote him down as boring. From an assay house that is the highest compliment the instrument pays. The empire sent him out to the Outer Colonies, and in time it made him their Praefect and Sovereign Arbitrator of the Bound Marches.

His first recorded trial was in the Cradle-Marches. General Cael Thurn stood before him there. The general had been loyal and had turned rebel, and an edict named him traitor. On the council, Valen Therosian had argued that the edict be carried out. Juno heard the case to its end. Then he spared the general in open court, where everyone present could hear it.

He held the periphery for a long time after that. Rebellion and panic thinned in the settlements he governed, and confusion cleared wherever he stayed. In frontier courts and colonial decrees, words he spoke under solemn oath became law.

He had held it a long time when he asked. Something larger answered. The answer was real. It was unreliable, and it was not free. His Domain consolidated into a Realm that could hold other Domains inside it. Relief came first. Then he worked out what the relief had been. He was no longer solely responsible for his own output. He had handed part of it to something that had signed nothing, and his office was to answer for what he did.

### The Cost

He never struck anyone, and he never fought. He did not hurry. Lies did not take on him, and neither did compulsion, illusion or pleading. He built his hold on regions and left the tuning of one heart to another alone, and he left it alone on purpose. He could hold a province and could not hold a room. Half the Senate revered him and half feared him. None of them liked him.

Before the warring Houses he delivered the Veil Proclamation. The binding clause he invoked was one no living person remembered. He had not remembered it either. He read it off the record by contact and read it aloud into a room that had no intention of obeying. The Houses found the alternative harder to hold than compliance. The siege of Balth Mere ended in a bloodless surrender, and all he sent was a sealed letter.

The Proclamation moved institutions. A single man who simply refused him was beyond it. Juno knew that. He held the Outer Colonies against a load he could not resolve, and every year of the holding stored correction with nowhere to go. His baseline did not rise when things went well for him and did not fall when they went badly. He let none of the stored correction out. He believed peace had to be earned. Sparing Cael Thurn earned him a council opponent who had asked for the edict. He believed an old house's pride bought no bread.

### Where They Stand

Juno Petros Marien is Praefect of the Outer Colonies, and he is called the Veil-Warden. None of the Imperial Senate control him. The Colonials and the Dustborn see him as their only voice in the imperial machine. He commands Cael Thurn's silence in every battlefield. Valen Therosian still opposes him on the council, as he has since the Cradle-Marches.

He has let none of the correction out. The last entry in his record is a final judgment, delivered before he vanished into the Eastern Range. Rumour says he dissolved into light mid-sentence. What happens when the correction finally lifts, nobody knows.

## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Those who write mercy in ash. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Kaelan Vorn was raised by commoners in the Withering Era, in a household that held no charter and recited no line. He was born to the Aetheric Nobility, of an Archon-Touched line. Nobody told him so, and no record he has seen carries it.

He was a pale child with silver-white eyes that seldom blinked, and he could stand in a doorway so still that the people in the room forgot him and went on talking.

An Accord-chartered academy took him as a Scholar. It examined its students on papers that carried a number where the name should be, and it gave no credit for anything it could not grade. He learned to count there. He learned to say a count aloud only when it was evidence. Among the accounts the academy set its Scholars was the Pale Hierophant's, who held a corrupt emperor's crown in stasis so that nobody could wear it wrongly. Kaelan copied that account into his own notes.

### The Making

He took each step of his discipline the same way: he found what still moved in him, and he stilled it. His masters taught empathy and wisdom as roads upward. He took neither road, then or since.

He learned to read the debt in an act as the act was done, and he believed the reading was a gift from Urion, the Balancer of Scales. The rest he counted as his own. The power, as he held it, was the product of pure discipline, and discipline was the only true currency of the cosmos.

He served the Edict of Urion in name. Cosmic Law, as he read it, stood above any Archon's authority, the Edict's own Archon included. He took up the Temperer's Gauntlet, a massive silver-plated thing covered in runes, and the Chain of Truth, a retractable silver chain that slips past defences and binds to the core of what it strikes. He moved as little as he could. The chain did the reaching. Men who met it called him the Silken Chain.

Then he chose, knowing the price, to pay with his own soul for something he valued above his own soundness. What he preserved was order, and what he paid was everything that moved in him.

### The Cost

Each time he stopped a place, his Essence set hard. He spent days in restorative processing afterward, and the fine cracks in his Crystal did not close while it lasted. The cracks spread with use. Where he stood, sound thinned and movement grew heavy.

The household that raised him wrote to him at the academy and for some years after. He stopped answering the letters and did not go back. The power drained the feeling out of him by degrees, and nearly every tie he had went with it.

He judged the Aetheric Nobility, and he reviled them. At Ordas, among the Kingdoms of the Five Rivers, Seraphion Veydrak of the Judgment Order had condemned an elder of the Archon-Touched, and the elder's house kept its elder behind its own doors. The sentence stood unexecuted. Kaelan held no commission from the Accord and asked for none. He came to Ordas, passed the house's doors, and executed the elder with one severing strike. The record calls it the Vantabriel Severing. He made the strike in the open and never denied it.

He was gone before the house laid the body on a bier in its courtyard and began a raising. Velastra Nirellium walked up the road after he left, and the rite came apart as she neared the bier. She struck once there. No record says what he learned of that stroke. The execution stayed done.

### Where They Stand

Kaelan Vorn is active within the Zenith Citadel, a suspended fortress held in perpetual stasis. The Manganthra Stasis stands on his record too: a kingdom and its capital, sealed in a loop. No record places either.

He is also called He Who Writes Mercy in Ash, a name Francis Alexander and Aeldoris Vanthryx carry as well. He claims a law over all reality that would end the War by enforced peace. His loops stay small pockets, and the field is a claim. The other road his record allows ends with a loop closing over him, and Kaelan at its centre, a pillar of Law.

He still believes Urion gifted him. Sanctum Lux holds that Urion is a reference and gives nothing. No Lector has told him.

The elder's house at Ordas wants him answered for. Its envoy went north to Halcrest and asked Aeldoris Vanthryx to convene his Court on him. The hunter declined, because the strike had been made in the open. Kaelan knows nothing of it, and the house's want stands.

The power in him is an inheritance, and the day he learns so, the anchor breaks. Nobody knows who his parents were. The answer is the one thing his stillness cannot survive.

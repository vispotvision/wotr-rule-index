## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Thalyren's groves and the highland plains. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Kaelen Brighthoof was born to the Hornclimb Clan, a Beastkin clan of the high mountain passes between the Core World and Outer World fringe realms. The passes were pale limestone. The air was thin. Gales came through them without warning. Goats, hawks and horned deer kept the range above them.

The clan lived by the crossings. Travellers hired the Hornclimb to scout ahead of them through that alpine Wellspring country and to stand as defenders in the narrow places. Guild parties hired them too. Below the passes, under Accord law in most territories, a beastkin who could not show free-holder status could be bought and sold.

He learned the slope before the path. He could tell from a goat's track in the scree how long ago it had crossed and whether it had been running. Older scouts found him on ledges they had not seen him climb to.

### The Making

The clan's elders tried him first with the defenders. He held a narrow place while parties went through. Then he asked to go out with the scouts, and he did not go back.

Scouting took him ahead of the parties and above them. The first time he crossed a rock face under fire, bowmen on the opposite slope had caught a party he was guiding on a ledge path. He went up the face above them while their arrows broke on the stone. From the top he shot down until they ran. After that he took the high line on every crossing.

He learned to slow his breath and his heartbeat until he was nearly impossible to detect, and to wait that way for the shot. His arrows hit lightly. He put them where an animal stops, and the clan sent him after the beasts that came into the passes out of the Outer Realm.

The beasts of his home range began to answer him. Hawks went ahead of him and came back. Goats and horned deer showed him the ground he could not see. He attuned himself to a Wellspring bound to a mountain peak, which channelled gales and thin-air endurance into those attuned to it. On that peak his arrows flew truer, and those who climbed after him tired fast in the cold.

Then the noise stopped, and his identity settled. It settled on the Hornclimb Bond, a soul-bond with the alpine beasts of his home range. He held it with goats, hawks and horned deer, and with no person.

### The Cost

The peak gave its gifts only on the mountain. He had to be on it, and altitude and cold were its limit. The goats and horned deer kept to his range, and on the plains below only the hawks went with him. Flat ground could beat him.

He guided through territory he did not hold. When something had to be held, he sent for someone who held ground.

A Guild party his clan had guided through the high passes went on to the Verdant Pass to draw on its Wellspring, and he came down with it. He took his clan's guiding fee. On the last evening of the gathering he and Lyraen Callivane, the pass's Wellspring Mediator, shot at marks across the meadow. She stood barefoot in the grass. He shot down from the rock above it. Afterward they traded techniques, and he teased her for "fighting in flatlands."

On one of the dangerous hunts he scouted for the Stonehoof Herd, an Outer Realm beast came down onto the warplains. Garruk Stonehoof took the herd out after it, and Kaelen went ahead along the ridges. From a ridge he found a furrow ploughed through open ground, with Cernan Eryndor asleep at the end of it, beyond waking. The beast's track bent back toward him.

Kaelen did not go down. He sent a hawk to the herd. Garruk set the Stonehoof in a ring about the sleeping man and held them there through the day. Kaelen lay on the rock above, breathing slow. At dusk the beast came back for the man. He put an arrow where it mattered, and the herd ran it down before it reached the ring. He took the hunt and the arrow, and what he had seen at the end of the furrow.

### Where They Stand

Kaelen Brighthoof is the Horned Hawk, the Arrow of the Peaks, a tracker and path-guide of the Hornclimb Clan and a hunter of Outer Realm beasts. He walks above the travellers he guides. When the Stonehoof Herd hunts, he scouts ahead of it. He and Lyraen still meet at archery contests. He respects Thalyren Veyroot as a guardian and prefers the wild challenge of hunting to the steadiness of guarding. He has seen Varek Ka'ruun fight and admires it. He finds him too loud for hunting.

He has said it plainly: "From up here, the wind carries every lie, every footstep, every chance to put an arrow where it matters."

Nobody has settled what a bond held with beasts, and with no person, can bear.

## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Class X and what it grows into. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Kaien Thal'Ruhm was born in the tin districts of the Old World, where the lodes lie at depths the pumps alone keep dry. His mother tinned array contacts in a winding house above a Greaves shaft. A tinned contact does not corrode. The pumps under her bench had to run whether the shaft paid or not, and in a bad supply year the water came up the levels.

He learned the engines by ear. A contact going bad changed the note of a whole array, and the boy could hear it from the yard while the foreman was still hunting for it with a lamp. He mended what he could reach. He was usually under the next housing before anyone came to thank him for the last.

A guild school took him on the strength of a pump array he had rebuilt from salvage. From the school he went to the Guild Accord. The Accord made him an artificer of the Alchemetrica Division.

### The Making

The Division gave him furnaces, reagents and work no district could have paid for. He built. Asked how a thing worked, he said so in plain words and stopped there.

In his years the Division was building the Catharsis Reactor, and he worked on it. He was beside it when it imploded. It took his arm and left half his body fused into gold alloy, and the fused half would have gone on hardening toward stone. The Aureveil Core went into his chest to regulate it. The rest of him stayed flesh.

He built the arm that replaced the one he lost. The Solhand was gold-titan alloy over a lattice of Wellspring crystal, and what he felt, it burned as fuel. He could close a break with it, in a machine or in bone. Sand turned to glass around him, and stone ran as liquid metal for a short while.

He had survived the first fusion by accident. Later he made it again, on purpose. He reforged the gold through Transmutatio ritual, and when it was done his nerves hummed with a dual harmonic, human resonance and mechanical recursion, that any Aether scanner could detect. The Division had made the first fusion possible. He walked away from it before it could claim credit for the second.

### The Cost

The Reactor took the arm and half his body. The Core was the condition of keeping the rest.

When he was struck, pain turned to armor plating across the gold half of him, hardening where the blow was landing. The plating drew on the same reserve of feeling that fed the arm. Whatever he spent on one, the other went without. Past his documented safe threshold the plating cracked, visibly, and anyone standing close enough could watch it fail. Past that threshold his body failed him too. He locked where he stood, unable to move, and heat spiked in the metal where it met the flesh.

The harmonic in his nerves gave him away. His nerves broadcast it when he was angered or inspired, and any scanner that looked found it.

The Accord offered him repatriation once. He refused it. He kept no workshop after that, claimed no ground and answered to no institution, and he mended with whatever he found on the road. What he released had to go somewhere, and his whole discipline was deciding where.

His attachments grew toward machines and broken things. He avoided conflict. To anyone reading him, that looked the same as avoiding company.

### Where They Stand

Kaien walks with Varūn's pilgrims and caravans, and when an Aether storm comes down on them he takes them through it. Nobody has given him leave. The Dustwalker Bands on his feet ease the drag of the sand and silence his steps. He keeps the Mask of Eyr'Sahl. It is a ritual mask in the form of a desert hawk, and it throws hawk-shaped currents of Aether to turn a blow aside or to carry a message.

On one crossing a storm shut the pilgrim road into Varūn's workings with a caravan on it. Kairen Moto was on the same road, coming out of a reclamation contract, and the housing of his iron arm had cracked at the shoulder. They sheltered with the caravan until the storm blew out. Kaien looked at the arm and asked before he touched it. He mended the housing with his hands, told Kairen in plain words what the join was doing to him, and stopped there. He took no payment. When the road opened he went on with the caravan.

The Solhand is rumored to carry a fragment of the Wellspring consciousness its lattice was cut from. Nobody has asked it directly and gotten an answer. Whether it has any say in which parts of him the plating protects, nobody knows.

Asked what the arm runs on, he answers in plain words. "The heart is a furnace. You just have to decide what you're willing to burn."

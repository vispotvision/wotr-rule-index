## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Reaper's office. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Kozakura Shion was born in the Concord, to a household that raised grafted fruit trees for sale. His mother worked the grafting knife. She split a hardy rootstock, set the fruiting wood into the cleft and bound the union with cord, and the two woods grew together until they behaved as one tree. Every spring the rootstock threw up suckers below the binding. A sucker drew from the same root as the tree it robbed, and left alone it drank what the graft needed until the crown above it thinned and failed.

Shion was set to cut them before he could reach the fruiting branches. He learned to take a sucker flush at the root, in one short pull of the hooked blade, without letting the blade touch the bark of the stem, because a scored stem bled sap and let rot in. Before each cut he looked at the stem twice, once from below the union and once from above it. His mother checked his rows. The cuts that had grazed the bark she showed him and left as they were.

The nursery's other enemy was frost. One clear night in blossom time could blacken a season's flowers on every row.

### The Making

The cut he learned at the rootstock he later made on people. Across two arguing wrists, a short arc of his naginata, Hanebari, snapped the hot thread between them, and the complaint came back small enough to answer. In the Guild Accord he became seasonal adjutant to Yukizumi Yukari, the Reaper. When wakes ran hot he walked the funeral routes with Shiragiku Iori and Natsume Kurogiku. In those years he read a grudge as a growth apart from the stem. He aimed at it that way.

The bed that changed his cut was in Altherion, on the stone ground floor of a timber house. A dying man's last word had snagged on an old grudge his daughter held against him, and the room ran hot. The Reaper brought his adjutants. Nothing green grew in that room. Shion grounded the naginata at the threshold, and the floor stayed stone. He watched.

Iori laid her ring of blooms and pinned a white chrysanthemum on the daughter. Natsume went for the grudge with her red shears to free the last word, and the hinge locked. Those shears do not cut true love. She put them away and hemmed the room with chalk. The bloom on the daughter's chest warmed. The father spoke his last word into the grudge. It landed. The Reaper lowered the star.

At that bed Shion resolved a contradiction in his own sight. He learned to see the sucker and the love it grows from as one stem, so that the cut never lands on the wrong half.

### The Cost

After Altherion his cut went only where the love was absent. A grudge that grew from love he left on the stem, and the one who carried it kept it. A clean sweep of Hanebari took the impact out of a blow and left the lesson in whoever had taken the blow. He refused to cut away a lesson that should stay. When his mercy reached someone, he told that person what it was in one sentence, in his own tongue. He spared what would let a person grow. Which part that was, he chose.

Deimos, the Sin Eater, eats guilt whole and keeps it sealed in his sash, and he transforms none of it. Shion left each lesson with its owner and trimmed what grew around it.

Every mercy warmed a room. The warmth was drawn out through his hands. After too many in one night the skin of his palms was frost-burned, white and stiff at the heel of each hand. Tea and quiet earth mended them.

Bare stone gave him nothing to grow from. There his reach was blunted until Lily-Quill or Cassiel Asterion set a softer geometry for him to work inside. A drum-Domain was worse. It gave his petals and his cords a third beat that neither could fuse to, and the squall shredded until Maelia Cantorine broke the beat.

### Where They Stand

He is the Reaper's seasonal adjutant, the spring hand to a winter star, and he softens what the Reaper must end. He walks the funeral routes with Iori and Natsume when wakes run hot. He keeps last rooms warm enough for truth, turns riot vigils into processions and reopens streets after Measures without leaving thorns behind. In a crowd, Sumire Nekoha's lanes carry his petals farther than streets deserve.

He wears a snow cloak stitched with sakura tassels and butterfly knots, and his mismatched eyes weigh what should go and what should stay. The lining of his mantle sheds living blossoms that hold a promise wherever they fall. The red cords he ties between two heartbeats hold them to one count, and a blow struck off that count goes awkward and stops. The small wooden kokeshi he leaves on thresholds invite the kind and politely tire the cruel, and the inscriptions on them wear down in time.

Beside a deathbed he plants the naginata's butt and bows once. The floor feels like garden soil. The dying cross without stumbling, and the living do not cling. A room with nobody dying in it gets a warm floor and nothing else. A grudge that holds a man upright does not start to come apart under his arc, and the man walks through it.

He has chosen, at least once, to leave a lesson in someone. Which lesson it was, and whether the person it was left in would have chosen it, nobody knows.

### Ties

- **Yukizumi Yukari · The Reaper** · master and adjutant. He is the Reaper's spring hand and softens what the Reaper must end, and at Altherion he stood at the threshold while the Reaper lowered the star.
- **Shiragiku Iori · The Wake-Embroiderer** · comrades. He walks her funeral routes when wakes run hot.
- **Natsume Kurogiku · The Knot-Shearer** · witness. He watched her shears lock on the daughter's grudge at Altherion and came away with the sight his cut depends on.
- **Lilium Verdanis · Lily-Quill** · comrades. On bare stone he needs her geometry.
- **Cassiel Asterion · The Prism Notary** · the softer geometry. When a court is bare stone, Cassiel's angles are the other softer geometry Shion can work inside.
- **Maelia Cantorine · The Countercant** · the beat. She breaks the beat that shreds his petals.
- **Sumire Nekoha · The Stray-Lantern** · lanes. Through a crowd her lanes carry his petals farther than streets deserve.
- **Deimos · The Sin Eater** · the lesson. He eats guilt; Shion refuses to cut away the lesson that should stay.

## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Anvil and relic. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Mavra Cindrel was born in Orrengar, in the Forge Dominion of the Old World. It was worked country. Spoil heaps stood at the heads of its valleys, grassed over outside and still poisonous within, and its forge-arrays ran hot enough to draw iron out of the slag heaps of dead kingdoms.

Her people were Felkin, a lineage of Beastkin and human blood. Felkin was their own name for it.

She went into a forge young, under the smith who became her mentor. Her mentor wore a pendant of Runeglass. The first work was proving joints: a piece went onto the test bench and stayed under load until it failed. Then she made the next one. It had to hold longer.

### The Making

When rebellion broke out in Orrengar, Mavra built machines to fight it. Other people drove them into the field.

After the rebellion she went into the Guild Accord with the Titanwrights, a chartered company of relicsmiths, and her mentor went with her. The company set her to reinforcing the Constructs and Aetheric weapons it sent to the front. In time its Court voted her into the livery.

The Titanwrights brought her armour and her weapon to a test forge of the Grand Forges, in the Upper Ring of Stannvaard, for field tests on live Wellspring heat. Garron Vuldane was warden there. Test after test she used the armour and the weapon as conduits for her own soul, until pain and joy ran through the machinery as evenly as through her. Her mentor stood the tests with her.

On the last test the forge came down. Garron went in under it. His constructs, Harn and Vyr, took the fall on their plating, and he held the roof off the test floor until the crew was out. Mavra survived direct contact with multiple unstable Wellsprings, loosed by the collapse, with her armour and weapon still carrying her. She came out with a Crystal that syncs to conductive alloys on contact. Her mentor did not come out. The Runeglass pendant did, broken. The forge took Garron's left arm.

### The Cost

She wore the pendant from then on.

The Heart-Drive Core grafted into her amplified the Wellsprings she carried at every emotional peak. Whether it read those peaks or made them, nobody could say. Mavra never asked. The pendant around her neck was the reason.

Her machinery carried her soul as evenly as her body did, and it charged her for the carrying. When she opened every channel at once, each swing landed harder than the last, and the Core ran past its limit. She heated from the inside. Held too long, the heat risked her nerves falling out of step with the machinery, or a resonance seizure. She stopped when the climb forced her to stop.

"If it breaks, I build it stronger," she said. "If I break, well, then I was the test." She stood in her own tests. At the Grand Forges her mentor had stood in them with her.

Everything she built went into other hands. Other people drove the Orrengar machines, and other people command the Constructs she reinforces for the Accord. She held no command of her own and sought none.

### Where They Stand

Mavra Cindrel is twenty-seven, a Liveryman-grade Vanguard Relicsmith of the Titanwrights, assigned to reinforcing frontline Constructs and Aetheric weapons. The company gives her a standing, such as it is. Across the guild she is the Golden Engine, a callsign used to her face as often as behind her back.

She fights in the Runeglass Armor with the Gravemark Greatblade. Her nerves run into the armour's relay, and she answers faster in it than out of it. When she moves, a hum comes off her. The weapons of the people fighting near her ring truer for it. When she tires, the tiredness goes into the vents and comes back out as speed, for a short while.

The joints she proved in Orrengar turn up in every Construct she reinforces for the Accord. She knows what the first of them were built to fight.

Garron Vuldane is still Warden of the Grand Forges at Stannvaard, and his left arm is dragonbone and Aethersteel now.

She still has not asked what the Core does with her. Her armour is Runeglass. So is the pendant at her neck, and it is still broken. She has not mended it.

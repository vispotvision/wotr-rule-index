## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of House Venrik and the Eressean crown. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

The record of Raivhar Drakonis begins in the Guild Accord's Enforcement Division. The Division made him. At his initiation the three Enforcement oaths were carved into his bones, as they are carved into the bones of all Enforcement personnel. The third is the Oath of Still Flame: "We burn only when law demands."

He was posted to a Vanguard Order. The Orders are Enforcement's strike teams, sent against Wellspring breaches, Domain violations and rogue Ascendant entities, and a Concord Writ attaches them to a Legion and withdraws them without consulting its commander. He wore the Sealwright Mark and fought inside Domains that other hands had raised. He went where the writ sent him.

His horns are fused conduits. He anchored them in a Wellspring and drew on it direct. Where he stood and drew for long, the ground under him charred, crystallised and fell in.

### The Making

On Vanguard service he went into a fire that should have ended him. He came out of it breathing. His right arm came out grafted into Voidflame armour, and the armour has never come off. It burns flesh, workings and spirit alike. Of that fire he has said, "Every breath I take is borrowed from the fire that should have ended me."

He killed, and he kept what he killed. When a foe died by his hand, the Devouring Pyre drew the dead man's Essence into him, and with it everything that man could do, whole. He did not learn techniques. He took them from bodies. He could not choose among them.

At the Siege of Epprenea, in the Outer World, his Order was put into the line on the Concord's side. He anchored his horns in the Wellspring under the siege ground and fought from it, and the ice around him charred and crystallised. The Wellspring began to fail while he drew on it. The record does not say whether his draw began the failure, or the siege did, or both together. Karo Venrik read the Wellspring down the conduit of Raivhar's horns and found where it was failing. Then Karo turned his own Essence into harmonic resonance and held it. The Wellspring held. Raivhar's anchoring held with it, and he felt Karo's read pass down through his own horns. They fought out the siege on the same side.

After the Kyrenshade Massacre the Accord declared him rogue, and his service in the Vanguard Order ended with the declaration.

### The Cost

Every gift of the Pyre cost a death. He must kill first, and the taking is not selective. Whatever a dead man carried, Raivhar carries now, including what was built for another body. He has kept all of it, and he keeps acquiring more.

The fire he draws takes structure apart once it starts. It does not ask which structures were holding up the ground, or holding up him. At Epprenea the Wellspring he stood in began to fail. Another man's Essence paid to hold it.

On a Concord raid he once had the life of Ziyu "Pip" Inari in his hands, and he spared it. The Pyre needs a death. Pip lived. Nothing of Pip went into the fire. Raivhar has held the two-tailed operative in wary respect since.

He believes his breath is borrowed from the fire, and he pays it back in the fire's own coin. He anchors, he draws, he takes, and the ground where he stands chars and caves under his feet.

### Where They Stand

Raivhar Drakonis stands outside the Accord, declared rogue. When he is agitated, his heart throws violet flame outward on every third beat.

After the declaration the Accord's Special Division put a deniable contract on him. It went to Ilyria Draven Morningstar. At Dorrosfenn, in the Outer World, she told him exactly what she was about to do to him. Then she threaded a foreign resonance into the node under his fire, and for a few heartbeats the fire would not come. In those beats he knew the read. He had felt it at Epprenea, running down through his own horns, and he spoke the name aloud: Karo Venrik. She did not correct him. His Shell turned her thread back on her, and she broke contact. He left the field alive and still rogue.

He and Karo are estranged. Seraphion Veydrak, an inquisitor of the Arbitration Division's Judgment Order, sees him as a heretic to be reclaimed or destroyed. Raivhar has not come back to the Accord.

Something whispers to him in the pulse of the flame. Nobody knows whether it is the echo of a Wellspring he has anchored in, or one of the men he took, still talking. The armour on his right arm has never come off.

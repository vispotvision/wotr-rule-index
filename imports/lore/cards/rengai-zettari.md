## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card, the eight archived scenes it appears in, and the record of The Zettari war camp and the Mahuo who came to it. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Rengai Zettari was born Dougou Ozumu Zettari's son, of the Zettari line on Kushara, and he grew up under his father's ceiling. Dougou was the Iron Bison. His body had been sealed from the start, so that feeling and Essence both went inward and were kept as weight. Sensors read him as dormant. That mistake killed people. He wore no armour and carried nothing, he spoke quietly, and his anger gathered a long time before it showed.

The son's gift ran the other way. It came off him as residue: soot, char, cinder, the grey matter a burn leaves behind. One of his eyes was ember-red and brightened when he drew on the power. The other was dark brown. The Zettari kept the boy under observation.

He was still a boy on the night the Iron Bison state ruptured, when the pressure came off Dougou as weight and the ground around him began to give under it. Yorime Seikai walked into it. She was the woman who stayed beside his father, and she stood in front of Dougou and said, "If you kill me, let it be because you chose to." Dougou stopped. Rengai was at the edge of the flattened ground, and he saw his father halted by one sentence from a woman who had not raised her hand. Dougou never spoke of that night, and his son took the warning in it.

### The Making

The ash came before the control. The first time it came in earnest he filled a yard with it and nearly choked himself. Afterward he learned to filter what he raised. His body kept something of that yard as well, and in time it learned to take ash as a medium to work inside.

An armoured instructor stood against him next, shut in plate from collar to knee. Rengai found a damaged seam in one pauldron, packed ash into it and heated the ash where it lay. The seam split. Later he faced a fighter whose techniques ran on the breath, and he put a black exhale into the man's count and broke it before the working closed. From those two fights he made a method. He read an opponent for seams, cracks, scars and failing joints, marked them, and brought heat to the marks. He went after a man's timing as readily as his plate.

Dougou's Ember Bead came down to him, a warning marker that burned red when his field overloaded or his lungs neared their edge.

Then he tried to hold a field wide enough to make a court of cinder, spread it past what his lungs could pay for, and collapsed inside it coughing black. He held smaller fields after that, and packed them denser. Range was a debt paid with his lungs.

### The Cost

The rest of the body paid as well. Long use of his ash veil burned his throat, and after too many black exhales his breath came up flecked with blood. Heat scarred him at the jaw, the neck and the hands, and where his power reinforced the old burns the skin took a faint metallic sheen. Ash worked into the creases of his knuckles and stayed. He wore a charcoal-fibre wrap over his mouth and nose to put the Cinder Lung further off. His method needed a field laid down and a flaw found, and he waited for both. Sometimes the window closed first. An enemy who ignored attrition and hit fast went through everything he had built.

He would not fight as his father fought. He fought for the Zettari name and for the man who gave it to him, and he would not speak in certainties he had not earned, to an enemy, to an ally or to his own child. He married Lyria, and they kept a house in a village near Vaeloris. Ash settled so deep into its doorframe over the years that the wood carried his trace. Their son was Asante. Before Niran Yukari left for the Yukari Territory as a boy, Rengai was his foundational mentor, and an Ash-Signal Pouch was made to his formula for Niran to carry.

While Cozbi's dragon burned the Inner World, Niran came back through the Veil changed. Rengai stood outside his room in the containment corridor and traced every thread of it to Cozbi. Watching had never been a skill he cultivated. He told the warden Xhem he was not asking permission. He went home first. He knelt to Asante among the carved wooden birds on the floor and told him to be strong, and he kept back the next sentence because he did not know whether he would return. He told Lyria that Cozbi wanted him alone, and that he might be the only one able to stand in front of Cozbi and come out the other side. Then he told her he would come back. He took the flask of pressed ash from the shelf by the door. Dhaerin Valorin asked to walk with him, and a message from Xhem turned them both back for information before they went.

They walked for months, through mountain passes and along a stretch of coast, to the clearing where Cozbi sat the Great Spider Impharous. Rengai asked for the reason first. "You made a two-year-old bleed for it," he said. Cozbi answered with the Mahuo ground into the mud between the Zettari and the Moto. He remarked that Rengai had corrected how he said Dougou's name before saying a word about the dead, and he told him he had not chosen Dougou's wars. Rengai let that part stand. He said it did not make what was done to the child anything other than what it was.

Then he drew the ash into himself, called the Iron Bison, and said the name the way his father said it, flat and unornamented. He burned the spider out from beneath. Cozbi told him his Iron Bison was weak next to his father's. Rengai met the strike of Cozbi's split double where it began, and his arm went numb to the elbow. Cozbi raised a Titan's effigy next, and every grain of ash Rengai held left his hands at once before its blow put him down. Dhaerin sealed Cozbi and died of the rite. Cozbi, bleeding into the grass, told his construct to collect the fallen Zettari, and it carried Rengai off the field.

### Where They Stand

Rengai is Cozbi's captive. No record says where he is held or in what state. The seal Dhaerin's rite left on Cozbi disturbs everything near it, and the searchers cannot trace him through it. Xhem told Lyria and Asante himself. Niran Yukari has said he will put Rengai back into his family's hands himself.

Since he was taken, Kujo has come to Vaeloris. Lyria is gone. Asante went north in the keeping of Zuberi Zettari, the Dawn Guard, with Ara Min Mahuo beside them, and at Kharven the Sovereign turned to cover the boy from Kujo's shot and died of it. In Asante's grief the Origin Eye opened.

The Zettari still name Rengai among their living. What his father left in him is still gathering toward a break. No record says which way it will go.

## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Outer trails. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Ristra Lenovira was born an elf, and nobody who sold him knew to whom. The Outer World sends labour out of the south with its hides and its nitre, and the first place anyone can put him is on its auction circuits, as a lot on a block.

The clerks of the circuits wrote him down by his eyes and his price. He saw far and he saw small from the time he could stand. Across a slave pen he could pick out the hairline flaw in a buckle, and the knee a man would fall on before the man had shifted his weight. Buyers learned it quickly. They put a rifle in his hands and pointed him at people, and when a buyer was finished with him he went back to the block and was sold on. The irons of the circuits scarred him. He gave none of those buyers his name, and none of them asked for it.

### The Making

He learned the work under owners who wanted it done and cared nothing for how. He solved each shot before he took it. He mapped the angle, the ricochet, the wind and the heat coming off the ground, and the round arrived at a weak point the target had not known was there. The shot held only while nobody knew where he lay, nobody had laid a hand on him, and nothing he carried pulled at him. Men who came hunting him found afterimages and false silhouettes where he had been, and they fired into those. He kept fighting through wounds that should have put him down. The burn that sharpened his reflexes fed on whatever pressed on him from inside. It ran one way. It left him tired.

Nyx bought his freedom. She was the only one who saw more than a weapon in him. He owed her his life, his freedom and his loyalty, and he took her banner as her own sharpshooter. He answered to her.

The change came to him under that banner. His soul saw itself from outside, and looking at it changed it, and knowing that the looking changed it changed it again. He had been sold at auction, and now he was handed the means to examine his own make. It came to him as no gift. At the centre he found a heat that shimmered like air over a salt pan, locked behind a pressure in his own mind and pulsing with a tension that had never been let out. It was the seed of something that would not settle, in a man who had never been permitted to want anything. It did not open.

### The Cost

What pressed on him went into his hands and his eye and never came back. No sleep lifted the fatigue. A shot of his failed first when something near it mattered to him, and he let little near him matter. In Nyx's camps he bedded down beyond the firelight, even on nights when nobody hunted him. He claimed no ground.

On the Scattered Wastes, after Nyx had bought his freedom, he went down among the rocks off a caravan track with wounds another man would have died of. A drover saw him and took him for dead. Ristra was awake. A man with a pot on his head came out over the rocks, got him onto his back and carried him in to the wagons. That was Dorrik. A woman with grey in her hair and a lamb at her heel knelt beside him and looked at him for a long time. That was Helki. She did not have his name, and he gave her none. She dressed his wounds with herbs from her own pack, and the caravan carried him on a wagon. He lived. At the first water he was gone from the wagon in the night, back into the Wastes toward Nyx's banner. He left nothing behind him.

He took his life off the Wastes owing it to a shepherd and a pot-man as well as to her. He held one bond. It was hers.

### Where They Stand

Ristra Lenovira is called the Hollow of the Outer World. He walks the Scattered Wastes as Nyx's marksman and answers to no guild and no Concord. Everything he owes, he owes first to her. His eyes are gold, and the irons of the circuits left their scars on him. He keeps apart from everyone, even when he is safe. No kin has found him. No Elven bloodline has claimed him.

The shepherd and the pot-man who kept him alive never learned his name. He has not paid them. Helki has told nobody what she read in him beside the wagons.

Nobody knows what lies behind the pressure that holds the heat shut inside him. It has not opened.

He has said this of himself: "I'm no hero, no savior. But if you give me a target... they'll never see the sky again."

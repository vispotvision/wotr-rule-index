## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Yukari bloodline. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Ryuka Yukari was born to a Yukari father and a Mahuo mother, and the Mahuo Estate raised him as its ward. Slyvine Mahuo was his grandparent on that side. He wore Slyvine's Scarf, which could stretch into a rope, draw tight, or harden into a thin shield. His father's side reached him by report. The report made him a descendant of Adenyus's line, and among the Yukari, those who die a natural death are believed to go to Adenyus and their ancestors.

He grew up small and quick. He had bright orange hair, large fox ears and a tail that wagged when he found a route. One eye was Yukari gold and the other Mahuo violet. In a building he had never entered, he knew which hallway led to the kitchen. Faint threads of light showed him which way a door lay, or a person. Broken ground held still under his feet. He did not trip. Over uneven ground he outran most adults.

### The Making

The gift woke on curiosity. When he was happy a soft orange light came off him, and when he was frightened he gave off the same light. Fading paw-prints of foxfire hung in the air where he had run. His excitement smelled faintly of citrus and ozone.

He learned to take the shortcut. Between two points he found a hidden path and slipped along it. To anyone watching he flickered and stood a little further on, past a locked door or out from under a swinging sword. When a blow did land, he rolled with it and came up clear.

The first of it on record is the Garden Escape. Chasing a butterfly, he walked through sealed high-level wards of the Guild Accord without slowing. The wards let him pass. They had registered him as a variable. The First Howl came after. During a stress test a burst of foxfire warmth broke from him, and it comforted the training dummies. From then on he was the Accord's unofficial mascot and a wayfinder in training.

The waking itself is all the catalyst his card records: "Nothing has called on him hard enough yet to fracture him."

### The Cost

Each shortcut burned what he had eaten, at once and hard. After one he needed a heavy meal, or the next was not there to take. Taken too often, the shortcuts put him to sleep where he stood, whether he meant to rest or not.

Strong feeling skipped him sideways. Frightened or delighted, he dropped into Spirit Layers and small Domains he had not meant to enter, and came back out. He carried Kaname's Compass. Its needle pointed to wherever he counted as home or safety at the time.

The shortcut needed a path, and a smooth, featureless surface offered none. There it failed outright. A field built to hold him, with no path through it, stopped him cold. So did a shiny thing, or an interesting smell, before a fight.

At thirteen he went to Senri's Gate for the Telling, where the name is chosen among his father's people. He came as a ward of the Mahuo Estate. Slyvine's Scarf was around his neck and Kaname's Compass in his pocket. Niran Yukari met him at the standing-stone gate with the forearm grip the valley uses, and through it read how little of the gift had woken. The elders gave him the full account of what the blood carries, what it costs and what it demands. The Yukari had kept quiet for ages, crows by habit, since the Moto taught them that whatever is seen is contained. He heard the account through and took the name. When he came out of the long hall, the first person who asked how his shortcut worked got all of it, the failures included.

### Where They Stand

Akira Yukari found him by his step before anyone told her his name. She took his arm in both hands. She told him she was blind. The orange light he gave off was lost on her.

He is thirteen. He is a ward of the Mahuo Estate and the Guild Accord's unofficial mascot, and he carries the Yukari name by his own acceptance. Strangers who stand near him lean toward hope. A sharp fox call from him can pull a panicking friend back into their own head. By his own account he is not meant to rule anything.

He has his own words for the road: "If there is a road, I will find it. If there is not, I will make one and come back for you." His card marks him for a World Walker in time, mapping routes between the Mortal, Spirit and Divine realms through connection. Nobody knows whether his next accidental skip will land him somewhere the Mahuo Estate cannot follow.

## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Reaper's crowd crews. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Seraphine Kharza was born in Altherion, where the three principal roads meet on the rim of the Ring. Her mother nursed the long ward of a hospital that took in pilgrims who fell sick on the road to the Grand Church. The girl slept in the linen room over the cart yard. She fetched basins before she could carry them full.

The ward taught her hands. A nurse lifted a fevered man with a forearm under his neck. A porter took a dead woman's whole weight on the stair and kept her head from falling back. When the wall behind the beds cracked, a mason set a brace against it and leaned there until the plaster stopped sifting. The midwife kept one palm flat through a whole labour. Seraphine learned each of them by how the hands moved.

Most of the pilgrims who died in the long ward had come too far for their kin to follow. Her mother washed them and laid them out. Nobody came up the road for them.

### The Making

The Guild Accord's Rescue and Deluge Cadre took her into its ordnance unit. She learned to seat Manifold, the reliquary cannon sleeved in ossuary gold, and to turn its prayer-valve.

A timber-framed street burned in Altherion. A porter carried children down a stair until the smoke killed him on it. Seraphine laid her hand on his and took the imprint of his haul. Nobody had given it to her. When his widow came for the body, Seraphine asked her for the licence. The widow would not give it. Seraphine let the haul go, and she never fired it.

Her mother died in the long ward that winter. Before she died she gave her daughter the cradle she had lifted pilgrims with, and Seraphine, her kin, licensed it. That cradle is the first palm she carries.

She had been built to help, and once she had taken. The difference between the porter's haul and her mother's cradle was the fracture she closed, permission over seizure. The Last-Touch Registry formed out of it. Every palm since has been a bond someone handed her. She has reached for none.

### The Cost

Every shot she fired pushed her backward. The people it caught lost their momentum and nothing else. The Spindle Corset metered the recoil into steps, and she walked backward down a corridor of catching hands. The momentum had to go somewhere, and she put it into herself. Overuse numbed her own hands to touch for an hour.

Each palm was a dead person she answered to. She registered the willing, on a licence from their kin, and she would not register the infamous. Where the dead were mocked within earshot of Manifold, the palms withdrew, and she would not fire in that company.

At Bridgemarch the Cadre travelled in Sir Rhyse Calder's escort. A face gave way in the Cuttings, and the crowd came up onto the chain-bridges with salt dust in its clothes. The dust clouded her manifests. When Sumire Nekoha went over the rail of the damaged span, Seraphine's first hands would not hold. Cyrille Lacrim sweetened the water. The manifests cleared, and her mother's cradle caught Sumire above the cavern floor and set her down. Rhyse held the head of the damaged span. Those who went over the rails after that landed on Cyrille's water, and Seraphine's hands carried them to the triage tables. For the hour after, she signed her orders with her visor.

In Altherion a congregation had sworn to keep its dying cantor, and the Reaper came for his last word. Seraphine planted Manifold at the door. A single palm capped the loudest voice until it was a cough in one throat. When Maelia Cantorine had cut the chant, Seraphine laid a Casket Wall across the door, and a mason's brace held the living back. Deimos went in to the bed. Her palms held the door and did not touch the bed.

### Where They Stand

Seraphine Kharza is Ten Thousand Hands, the Reliquary Gunner, crowd-safety artillerist and last-touch custodian of the Cadre. She escorts volatile Measures for the Star Chamber. When last rooms need extra hands that do not belong to the living, she is the Reaper's adjunct. She wears a chapel visor over porcelain pallor and a corseted reliquary harness ribbed in gold. Articulated bone fingers clutch Manifold.

When Marigold Vance's gavel opens an aisle, Seraphine breaks the charges sideways so the Escort Lane holds. She ferries witnesses through crushes. She keeps last-word circles from collapsing. She salvages archives and infants from fire without ladders. When Moros Pellayne has recorded last intent, her palms deliver the body kindly to the truth. She holds the living back while Yukizumi Yukari lowers the star. None of her palms has delivered anyone to Deimos.

She still carries her mother's cradle. Nobody knows whether she can fire on memory alone, with no licence behind the palm, and make the hand hold.

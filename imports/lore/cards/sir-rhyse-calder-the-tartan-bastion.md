## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Reaper's crowd crews. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Rhyse Calder was born among the Northrail Highlanders. His father was the Dragon King Xalvion, and Rhyse was the last of that king's illegitimate sons. His mother raised him in the high country.

She kept a walled fold on a caravan road, where drovers penned their herds for the night, and the boy grew up at its gate. A frightened herd that meets a wall goes through it. A herd turned at the gate walks the length of the wall and settles. He knew both early. The beasts stood quieter on the nights he kept the gate.

The blood of Xalvion ran in him as a quiet current, and it would bring him a thousand hard jobs and no throne.

### The Making

He left the fold walking beside caravans. He walked at the wheel on the side of the drop, and he never led a charge. He learned where caravans panic: at fords, in narrow cuts, and on bridges.

The first time he watched a bridge collapse under panic, he was on the near bank with his caravan halted behind him. The crowd on the span ran for both ends at once. The span went down under them. He swore never again.

The early holds came after that. He planted himself at the head of a crossing, and the crush stopped at him and stood. Later he began to bend the flow he had once blocked. A column that met Lochgate turned along its face and went on at a walk. The Accord took him into the Escort Corps of the Arbitration Auxilia and made him March Warden of the North Rails.

Dragons came to his gates as well. He could not kill one. When the blood answered, the firmness around him became a low draconic gravity that even a dragon engine felt, like a command phrased as a suggestion. The dragons stood down, breathed easier and went somewhere else. He calmed or broke more than a thousand dragon forms, engines and dragon-touched beasts. The roads began to call him He Whom Has Tamed Over a Thousand Dragons.

### The Cost

The vow was tested at Bridgemarch, the junction city of Nalūn, where chain-bridges cross open caverns and the sound of chain never stops. Rhyse was bringing arbiters through the junction toward Ironlink Hold, and Cyrille Lacrim and Seraphine Kharza of the Rescue and Deluge Cadre travelled in his escort. A face gave way in the Cuttings. The cutters at the face did not come out.

The barracks emptied toward the junction. The crowd came up with salt dust in its clothes and onto the chain-bridges, where the junction's own traffic already stood. The shock had parted an anchor chain on one span, and the crowd pressed onto it.

He planted Lochgate at the head of the damaged span. He would not set Rail Root on it. Tightened, a hurt span can lock stress into the wrong supports. He knotted his tartan over the front ranks and turned the column onto the sound span beside it. Cyrille struck the Matins Cask, and the crowd on the sound span matched the pulse and walked. Seraphine's hands carried those who went over the rails. The damaged span stood empty until the Span's engineers closed it.

He kept the vow. The fear of the front ranks came into him with the knot and dragged at his reflexes for the rest of the road. It did so at every hold, and the wider he spread the tartan the slower his own hands became. He spread it anyway.

On the Corridor road in Stannvaard, Arbitration sat in a way-station hall to hear the drovers against the Transport Guild. The drovers' work-song became a march and came to the doors. Rhyse planted Lochgate in the doorway and knotted his tartan, and the head of the march slowed at the gate. Maelia Cantorine cut the beat in the yard, and Cyrille's bell gave the broken yard a pulse. He lifted the gate. The hearing sat again that day, and nobody was struck.

### Where They Stand

Sir Rhyse Calder is the Accord's moving gate. He escorts arbiters, convoys and Sealwright teams for the Arbitration Division, which Argen Vael'thar heads. Where caravans panic, bridges sway or councils start to fracture, he plants Lochgate and knots his tartan. Rails rattle less. Stone grips boots better. Voices drop.

He keeps his hold portable. He stamps no city heart and leaves the permanent anchors to dedicated Warders, and every gate he holds lifts when he walks on.

He wears a black field habit under dragon-chased plate. A blue tartan sash is bound to both pommel and a coffin-high tower shield, and a crescent-horn dragon badge with cold eyes rests over his heart. His build is more dock piling than statue. When the dragon blood stirs, his pupils thin slightly, and a faint heat enters the air around him, like the memory of a forge.

His own line is "Stand close. I will make the world remember how to hold."

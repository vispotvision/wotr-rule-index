## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Ashen Crown: Muken's house and the Moto lines. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Souma Byakuya Moto was born in the Hidden Moto Village, of the Moto blood and the Byakuya line, to a household sworn to the Way of the Sacrament. He came into the world marked. White striping ran across his body in a tiger's pattern, and the shrine took the marks for the Sacrament's claim on him before he could stand. The Byakuya dressed in near-white, cut clean, and he grew up among pale vestments, ritual wraps and prayer-cords, in a village that kept its own shrines and its own sacramental forge. He grew tall and lean, with wild white hair and pale blue eyes ringed faintly in gold. He was still until something moved against him.

In any room his eyes went first to the door.

### The Making

Shirokiba came to him from the village forge. It was a reliquary spear of that forge's sacramental tradition, and it would answer only to a hand under vow whose sacramental imprint it recognised.

It recognised his.

He learned the spear low and rooted, rear foot planted, inside the small circle a shrine floor allows. He wasted nothing on flourish. He denied the line of an attack, turned it aside and arrested it close, and when an enemy overcommitted he put one clean thrust through the line of that error.

The rites made him. He bound himself with vows and paid for his wards in blood, tied them to the shrines, and learned to stay on his feet through the recoil. In time the vows let him go on working through pain that would drop most fighters.

Then a raid came onto sacred ground. It was the Night of the Broken Chancel. The shrine was desecrated around him while the assault was still live, and Souma completed the sacrament there, in blood, with the fighting at close quarters. Then he held the breach long enough for others to survive. Afterwards the village named him the Blessed.

### The Cost

That night turned a gifted defender into a living sanctuary. It also ended his belief that love and duty could be kept cleanly apart. From then on he took the threshold first and his own life second, and each time the obligation went deeper and left less of him outside it.

His body kept the account. After a heavy rite his fingers went numb first, then his breath shortened, then pressure built in his chest. His nose bled. His hands shook, and the spear hand flexed long after. Old wounds ached where nothing was wrong. The liturgy kept repeating in him after the rite had ended, and each rite left his face a little flatter.

In the kingless years after Muken's removal, once the Greymanes had given up the western ridgeline, raiders came through the Stormfold approaches that nobody held any longer. Civilians had to go out by the northern passage, the one called Snowgate. Souma planted Shirokiba at its mouth and held it while they went through. Beyond him the road climbed to a pass the Amagiri had always kept, and Artemis Amagiri Moto closed it behind the last of them against the pursuit. The route held. The civilians survived.

Snowgate took pieces of his memory and put deeper strain on his soul, and the fear that he could matter only through suffering grew worse.

He never wanted glory, conquest or praise. He asked no one to take a breach in his place. He wanted a home that could survive without being fed by his pain, and every rite he paid for himself kept that home fed on it.

### Where They Stand

He is twenty-seven, still the sworn threshold-guardian of the Hidden Moto Village, and functional under a load of soul-strain and a memory that keeps wearing away. The village, the sacramental office and the shrine lattice all lean on him at once. He stands ramrod-straight in layered shrine-war vestments, shoulders squared, his gaze on the thresholds and the exits.

The White Well lattice still wants stabilising. The village still depends on one body at its threshold, and nobody has yet been trained to hold the line in his place.

To those who shelter behind him he says, "Stand behind me if you must, but live long enough to stand without me." Nobody has yet seen past the office to the man inside it. How close his protection has come to hardening into a cage, he tells no one.

## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Thalyren's groves and the highland plains. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Thalyren Veyroot was born in the territory of the Everbloom Wellspring, which the Sacred Conclave counted among the most stable and sacred Wellsprings in the Core World. His mother was a druid. She tended the groves that spread outward from it over pale limestone.

From her he learned to read a grove by its leaves. A leaf that browned from the edge inward had gone short of water. Wilting in wet ground meant rot. Understory cropped to the stems, with bark stripped at shoulder height, meant grazers had come through. He learned the signs before he could name the trees.

He was slow on every path. The other children outran him, and he gave up trying to keep pace. He learned instead to stand in one place in a grove until the birds came back to the branches around him.

### The Making

The druids sent him to the Everbloom. There he learned to draw vitality from soil and sunlight, and to hold his ground on it. In time his cloak came alive. Leaf and moss and flower grew into it and moved when he willed them. It could bind a man's legs, shed a pollen that closed wounds, or harden into bark over him.

His sight opened through his antlers. He came to see the state of every grove, forest and sacred green-space in his reach, and a disturbance showed to him as leaves withering. They were the leaves his mother had taught him to read from the ground.

The Sacred Conclave bound him protector of the Everbloom. It gave him spiritual authority over the druids, beastfolk and nature-attuned Concord members of its territory. He was called Green-Warden of the Ninefold Grove, and Rootkeeper, and the Verdure Archon's Chosen.

Then he walked toward something that could end him. He arrived. He remained coherent long enough to choose how he met it, and his answer was to root. His spirit was anchored to the Everbloom Wellspring. Inside its domain, whatever destroyed his body, the Wellspring's roots would regrow him over several days. He met the thing that could end him and chose never to leave the place where ending does not apply.

### The Cost

Outside that domain his life-force faded slowly. Soil and sunlight gave some of it back, and every day outside was life-force spent and not recovered.

He stayed. One grazing season the Stonehoof Herdclan came up its path from the warplains of the Outer World, and the path ran through a sacred grove at the edge of his sight. The herd cropped the understory to the roots and trod the leaf-mould into mud. He watched the grove wither from the Everbloom.

Moryn Sporetail was a guest in the Ninefold Grove that season and went out in his place. Moryn knelt in the trodden ground, listened to the mycelium under it, and brought back what it held: many hooves on one line, a herd on its road, nothing cut or burned. He sent his word to the herd by Lyraen Callivane. She walked barefoot into the Stonehoof camp and put it to Garruk Stonehoof. The path was the herd's, and a sacred boundary under the Hoof-Seer Wellspring, and Garruk would not move it. He agreed to one thing. The herd would cross the grove at a walk and not graze inside it.

Thalyren did not strike. Moryn told him the trodden ground would feed the next growth, and the understory was back before the herd returned. A boundary another warden held sacred now ran through one of his groves. He let it run.

He held that the Everbloom kept the groves, and he kept the Everbloom. For that he gave up one grove's understory for a season and watched it go.

### Where They Stand

Thalyren Veyroot stays at the Everbloom. Inside its domain he cannot be killed and cannot be made to move. Outside it he is dying slowly, and he cannot run. He has almost never needed to strike.

The people he deals with come to him. Lord Maerion Lysandre comes when Wellspring law in the natural realms needs upholding. Orivane Caelthys reads the celestial weather that could reach the groves and sends it to the Everbloom. Lyraen comes for the Harmony rituals he guides her through, and she carries his word out where he cannot follow. When the Stonehoof path meets his groves again, she goes between.

Moryn is still welcome in the Ninefold Grove. Gorrum Vaelseth is an old acquaintance, and Kaelen Brighthoof respects him as a guardian and prefers the hunt.

Nobody knows whether the rooting was his choice or the Everbloom's.

## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The elven old-ground. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Vaelorian Theryn Aerethiel was born on the elven old-ground of the New World, into the service of the Court of the Verdant Throne. He grew up under the Kinship Doctrine, which holds the land a relative. His mother kept its older healing. It was the first working he learned from her, on split bark and on cut hands, before anyone put a blade in his.

The Court armed him in its light plate and gave him a court blade, the Verdant Regent. He was quick in that plate. He was never as quick in anything he wore afterward.

He rose through the Verdant Phalanx to High Marshal. A High Marshal has to read a battlefield whole, and he learned to read it that way, from the ground up.

### The Making

The Great Spirit War took the Phalanx into one engagement after another. He came out of each of them standing.

Where his plate had split, it grew back from beneath, and where it had been struck before, it came back thicker. The court blade hardened into a length of fossilised dragon-root, and he kept it sharpened to a killing profile. Its crossguard warped into hooked branches that matched the spikes rising along his armour.

On living ground the Great Spirit Tree drew Essence up into him through anchor points along his back and shoulders, shaped like a spine. It kept him on his feet through engagements that should have killed him years ago.

Each engagement he survived in that shape tightened one figure: elf as bark, armour as scale, Domain as body. The Continuum stopped treating it as a figure of speech. What Florwyn recognised in him was persistence. He was recognised into it mid-battle, again and again, until the recognition stuck.

The Accord's lexicons entered him as a mobile Draconic Root Arm of Florwyn, and he was called Florwyn's Regent, the Black Canopy and the Thorn-Crowned Sovereign. The Court took its High Marshal for its Regent.

### The Cost

He lost the court plate. What he wore afterward grew out of his own Soul Crystal, and when a blade or a working stripped it, it grew back from beneath at once. It could not be taken off him. He was slower in it than he had been in the old plate, and heavier.

Under strain his silhouette lengthened. A second, deeper tone rode under his voice.

The soldiers who had served under their High Marshal went on serving beside a war regent none of them entirely recognised. None of them said so to his face.

The Tree had been his conduit. It became, more and more, his co-author, and the bond between them ran one direction less than it used to.

Every engagement he stayed on the field for took a little more of the elf. He stayed through every one.

He held his strength below its full reach, on purpose. The gap was the difference between the lord he still was and the dragon he had not decided to finish becoming. When he opened his Domain and called the dragon coiled through it, the spectral wyrm laid itself over him plate to scale. Anyone with Essence sight saw a later version of him step briefly into view. Every call left the Dragon more willing to make the overlay permanent.

Drevath Mourne-Kal had taken a field on the contested fringe, declared it permanent, and waited on it. Thalyndros brought Vaelorian in from the sky. As the dragon came into sight, Drevath drove Mourne into the ground and spoke the word, and inside the boundary nothing grew and nothing healed. Thalyndros came down short of the line, tail-roots and wing-tips sunk in the ground outside it.

Vaelorian walked in alone. His armour would not grow back on that ground. Drevath put the audit to him as a thing already run: step aside, and live to watch it. He gave him the courtesy of reading it before it was enforced. Vaelorian read it. He did not step aside, and he walked back out across the line to the dragon with no blow struck.

### Where They Stand

He is the last Regent of the Court of the Verdant Throne, a crown with no kingdom left to crown. He fights beside the Phalanx he once commanded.

His Domain, the Throne-of-Everfall, opens now as a darker, more vertical forest. From a distance its titan trunks read as the necks of sleeping dragons, and the Tree's presence reaches the canopy as well as the roots. Lesser predators will not meet his gaze. Greater ones hold it for a heartbeat and look away first.

Drevath's audit has not been enforced. A living cutting of the Tree that feeds him rides over Kairen Moto's shoulder, and no record says who cut it.

Nobody knows whether the dragon that steps into view over him is his own future, or something else's wearing the shape of one. The age he is trying to preserve is watching him, undecided.

### Ties

- **Thalyndros Aerenvael · The Canopy Wyrm** · bonded, root-node and mirror-body. The dragon grown from the Great Spirit Tree's higher boughs is anchored through his Soul Crystal as its rider-knot and carries him where a Domain must come down from the sky, and the two of them never misunderstand each other.
- **Drevath Mourne-Kal · The Iron Horizon** · rival, two answers to one question. He read Drevath's audit on a taken field and did not step aside.
- **Saetsha Bloomveil · The Pink Tempest** · bound to one Archon. He is called Paragon of Elyndra, and the Archon's former herald holds, in every account she has, that Elyndra has gone silent and every Domain of Renewal gone dark with her.
- **Kairen Moto · The Iron-Root** · the Great Spirit Tree. Kairen carries a cutting of Vaelorian's conduit, and no record says who cut it.
- **Valthrum · The Cryon King** · estranged through Florwyn. Florwyn made Valthrum and recognised Vaelorian, and the spirit who refused to end her season in the War of the Long Night keeps toward her Regent the neutrality he keeps toward every other power.
- **The Verdant Phalanx** · his former command. The soldiers who served under their High Marshal now serve beside a war regent none of them entirely recognise, and none of them has said so to his face.
- **The Great Spirit Tree** · conduit and co-author. The bond runs one direction less than it used to.
- **The age he is trying to preserve** · watching. It has not decided whether he is the closest thing to a dragon it can bear without breaking, or whether to wait for him to tear open so something larger can step through.
- **Florwyn** · the Titan who recognised him. He is her Regent, recognised for his persistence until the recognition stuck.

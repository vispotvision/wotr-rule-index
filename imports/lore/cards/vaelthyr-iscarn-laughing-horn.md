## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Voice Between Wars and the Outer nobles. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Vaelthyr Iscarn was born into the Pale Court of the Epprenean Kingdom, a devil-blood dynasty seated in the frost-sheathed fjord realms of the Outer World, and he was raised as its heir. The horns came with the blood. His grew curved and blackened.

Epprenea quarried its bone-beds in open benches. The chalky rock went out by barge, and ground one way it doubled a western wheat yield. Ground another way, it was the primary reagent of every necromantic practice in the southern quarter. The Holy Sea of Alabaster twice tried to tell the two uses apart at the point of sale and twice failed, because the bench could not tell them apart either. He grew up on that rock.

Beneath the court ran the ice labyrinths, and a Wellspring lay buried under them. It taught manipulation through stillness, and the strike it taught came once the other side had grown complacent. He learned the court above and the labyrinth below together.

### The Making

He learned to read a room before he entered it. He learned to wait. Good news did not warm him, and bad news did not cool him further. Frost began to condense to razor-thin edges wherever he stood, and a casual gesture of his could leave an ice-burn scar on whoever was near. The sigils of his line ran through his horns. He could loose a burst of Essence with his hands at rest.

He went to the Guild Accord as an envoy, and the Arbitration Division entered him on its high-risk delegation roster. To the Accord he was an official envoy and an unofficial information broker. He was called the Winter Serpent, and Laughing Horn.

Then a thing in him kept its weight and gained a second weight, and both were operative. He had to keep functioning while he held both. A smile was a smile and was also a working. The glamour went into every expression he owned, and no version of his face was left that was free of the technique. He had to live behind it.

### The Cost

People who held his gaze too long lost track of their thoughts. His amusement reached a room whole, infectious or unsettling as he meant it. The current that carried it was a faithful mirror. It returned his state undistorted to anyone equipped to read it, and it made no exception for a state he wanted kept.

His body would not carry a long fight. He had one strike. It had to land on someone who had stopped taking him seriously, so he waited and smiled and let rooms underestimate him.

Sevrin Valeith came into Epprenean ground out of the frost-barren calderas, with his relic-skulls about him, whispering. The Accord was hunting him for Soul Concord violations. Vaelthyr received him. Sevrin offered bound spirit-messengers. Vaelthyr offered political cover: the Pale Court's ground to stand on, and his own voice at the Accord's table whenever the Accord asked after the binder. The talk ran long past the terms. Sevrin bound the first messenger in front of him and gave it to him. Every one after it was another binding.

The pursuit reached the Pale Court. The Accord wanted to know whether the court had given the rogue binder ground. The Division sent Aurevian Lysanthir to ask, the negotiator who had stood beside Sevrin at a spiritual mediation before the binder ran. They met in the Concord Citadel's City Below, where the air was never without a light rain. The rain froze on the envoy's sleeves. He smiled and answered that the Pale Court kept no fugitive of the Accord's. Aurevian harmonised the same current, and it gave the harper the state under the smile: amusement, and beneath it something Vaelthyr would rather not have broadcast. No fact came with it. Vaelthyr read the harper in turn and got an honest answer. His own answer went into the Division's record as he gave it.

### Where They Stand

Vaelthyr Iscarn stands on the Arbitration Division's high-risk delegation roster as envoy to the Guild Accord. The unofficial half of his office runs on Sevrin's messengers, which need no wire and no seal, and which no Accord clerk reads.

Now and then he drinks with Goraku Tenryū, war-leader of the Braqth Raiding Courts. Each respects the other, and each uses the other for influence over the affairs of the Outer World. Frost gathers on his side of the table.

He finds Hyo Miran entertaining. He has warned others never to make deals with the man.

What he read in Aurevian he has not said. No record says whether Aurevian ever entered his own reading of the envoy beside that answer. The roster does not say whose envoy he is.

He still says it: "A serpent does not rush to bite. It waits. It smiles. And by the time you notice the cold, the venom has already set in."

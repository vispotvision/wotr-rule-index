## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The elven old-ground. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Valthrum was made in the First Eon, before the first record was kept. Florwyn made him. The Titan of renewal shaped him as a whisper of cold wind and set him to protect a season.

The season was winter. He kept it the way a wind keeps anything, by moving over it. He laid the first skin of ice on still water and held frost on the north faces of stone and in the roots of the old forests. Then he let the thaw come. For a long age that was all he was: a cold that arrived when the year called for it and left when the year turned.

His frost answered to Vohrin, the cold of the Abyssal Depths.

### The Making

Sorrow raised him, and the battlefields. When a great battle ended in the cold, the whisper went over the field and stayed there. It froze the dead where they lay. It put rime on dropped shields and on faces turned up to the sky, and it stilled the ground until nothing on it moved. Each field he stilled left him stronger. On those fields he learned to take heat out of the world, and the taking was the working itself.

He came out of that age carrying Winter's Verdict, a greatsword cut from a single diamond. It severed the tether between a soul and its own heat.

When the last winter he guarded came on, he caught the final heat of the summer before it. He shut it in the Lantern of Lost Seasons and hung the lantern at his hip.

Then came the War of the Long Night. No record says who fought whom in it. The season Florwyn had made him to protect was due to end, and he refused to end it. The winter lay over the north unbroken. The Northroot clans stayed under it, cut their first forge into the stone, and kept that forge burning through the Long Night.

He bound himself wholly to the concept of an eternal winter. His Domain stopped being a working and became a jurisdiction. The world began answering back. Those who had to name him called him the Cryon King, and the Lord of Stilled Hearts.

### The Cost

He was formally estranged from his maker.

He called what he brought "the peace of the pause," and he paid for it in his own body. Every Glacial Tomb pulled stillness out of a living thing and drew answering cold back into him. Each one thickened the permafrost mantle he wore, and enough of them in sequence tipped him toward the Slowing. Spent too far, his own thought slowed before anything around him did. Past that point he risked a catatonic hibernation.

After a great working he went brittle, and a blunt blow landed in that window could crack his form and let Essence leak from the break. If his control slipped, the Aether itself would freeze.

Heat drained toward him from everything near, and fire thrown at him fed his reserve. A blade that struck him could shatter from the cold on contact. He moved slowly, and he meant to.

Every mortal kingdom within a mile of him felt the temperature drop, and with it a sadness nobody there could account for. It came whether he intended it or not.

What a Tomb held stayed whole and could not act. Killing it meant breaking the ice. The break threw the stored entropy back at once, and whoever broke it had to survive what came out. He left the oldest of them unbroken.

### Where They Stand

Valthrum lies dormant in the Northern Waste. He is waiting for what he calls the Signal of Entropy. No record says what the Signal is, or where the Waste lies.

He stands neutral toward every power of the Withering Era, and he belongs to the Winter Court only as its anomaly. The Court licenses the frost-caves where Glacium grows. It keeps him at a professional remove, documents nothing of how those caverns are held below freezing, and permits no inspection. Its position is that the caves are naturally cold. The Accord's Logistics Division notes that no vein is registered under them, and it has asked how cold. The Court has not answered. The Eladrin of that Court hold that frost is preservation, that cold keeps what it covers in trust until it is needed again.

When he speaks of what he is, he speaks at length and unasked, and it reads like doctrine. An alchemical index names a degree of cold after him.

The lantern at his hip still holds the last heat of that summer. Nobody knows whether anything he has held in the ice for centuries is still, in any sense that matters, the person who went in.

### Ties

- **Thalyndros Aerenvael · The Canopy Wyrm** · kin by making. Florwyn made them both, and the wyrm flies as her proof while Valthrum has stood estranged from her since the Long Night.
- **Vaelorian Theryn Aerethiel · The Last Elven Lord** · estranged through Florwyn. He keeps toward her Regent the neutrality he keeps toward every power.
- **Brynja Haldrís · Hammer of the Northroot** · legend and relic. The Northroot clans lit their first forge against the winter he refused to end, and she wears its molten stone at her throat without ever having met him.
- **Florwyn** · maker, estranged. She made him to protect a season, and he refused to end it.
- **The Winter Court** · licensor, at a remove. It licenses the Glacium caves and keeps him at a professional remove.
- **Every mortal kingdom within a mile of him** · the ones who feel him. They feel the temperature drop and a sadness they cannot account for, whether he intends it or not.

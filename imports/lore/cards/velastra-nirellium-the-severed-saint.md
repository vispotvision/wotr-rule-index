## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Those who write mercy in ash. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Velastra Nirellium was born to a family that stood witness at the Celestial Concord's pacts. Her mother kept the witness-seal. At her mother's table the girl learned what a witness was for. A witness pressed her seal beneath the sworn names, and if the pact was ever brought before an Adjudicator, she stood and said what she had seen signed.

She entered the Concord's service young. Its Adjudicators enforced what had been sworn. Contracts came before them, and blessings, and the anchoring of a soul to a vessel, and the terms on which the dead might be raised. A novice read each pact through to its last clause. Then she learned to hold every party to what was written, however hard the terms had grown since the signing.

Velastra held them. She made no exception for tears or for rank, and the Concord revered her for it. Its people called her a saint while she served.

### The Making

She rose to Adjudicator and served a patron under oath, and she walked the Concord's roads to the pacts she enforced. Where she came, what had been signed was kept. Then she looked at the pacts she had been enforcing and found one that should never have been signed.

She severed her oaths. Afterward no one remembered whom she had served, because the oaths she severed erased the name from history. She had unmade the fact of having had a patron at all, and the loss left a wound that nobody can locate. Nobody knows whose name the severed oaths erased, Velastra among them.

She left the Celestial Concord an exile. She was bound to the Severance Concord, a silent remnant cult that enforces the Law of Irrevocables, and her soul was fused to a silent contract. From then on she could not lie, nor speak in false prophecy. She took up Veyrinhal, the Oath-Eater. It is a straight black-steel blade that gives back no reflection, and its edges always weep faintly. It destroys spiritual bonds on contact: contracts, resurrections, anchorings.

### The Cost

She believed that a pact which should never have been signed had to be ended, and that what had ended lawfully should not be undone. The belief took the name of the one she served. It took her place among the people who had called her a saint. The Concord held her up afterward as a cautionary tale of lawful purity turned decay.

The blade asked a price she could not count. Once she began an unmaking it ran on without her and did not ask which structures were load-bearing. She severed things she had not meant to and could not identify which.

At Ordas she saw one. An executioner she never saw had killed an elder of the Archon-Touched there, among the Kingdoms of the Five Rivers, and the elder's house laid the body on a bier in its courtyard and began a raising. Seraphion Veydrak, who had condemned the elder, bound the officiants where they stood under his ring of chained glyphs. Velastra came up the road on foot. She had been walking since long before the town came in sight, and the house's people drew back from her path. The chant broke off as she neared the bier. The rite came apart. She struck once with Veyrinhal, the Final Severance of that cycle, and what it ended there would not return by any means. The same stroke loosened the chained glyphs, and some of the officiants got free before Seraphion raised the ring again. When the courtyard was quiet, she asked him whether he wanted his three vows cut. He said no. She left them whole and went back to the road.

The silent contract bound her past breaking, and she could form no other bond. She could not lie. She could not make a true thing comfort anyone who heard it from her. Any spell that needed a chant failed near her, and so did every blessing spoken over her. Her blade gathered force only while she walked unbroken. Whoever stopped her walking took that force away.

### Where They Stand

Velastra walks for the Severance Concord, and she is called the Severed Saint. Divine and Abyssal factions both hunt her for wielding the Lawbreaker's Blade. She wears layered black steel stitched with soul-thread and trimmed in silent scripture, and at her waist a pale sash marked in irreversible blood-ink. Deathbound cultists keep out of her road, fearing her blade will erase their tether to undeath. She could not reassure them if she wanted to.

She has put it in her own words: "The pact is broken. The breath you take now is theft."

The elder at Ordas stays ended. Whose name her severed oaths erased stays unknown, and so do the other things her strokes have parted without her aiming at them.

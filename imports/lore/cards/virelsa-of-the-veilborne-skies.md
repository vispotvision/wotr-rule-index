## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Triune of the Three-Headed Serpent. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Virelsa was born in the Fae Courts of the Higher Realms, to a mother who kept the Courts' divine relics. She was set to the relics as a child. They responded the first time she laid a hand on one. Her mother had tended them all her life, and in all that time she had never felt one of them stir.

The condemned passed through those Courts on their way down. Virelsa learned to read each of them by what had been withheld: a name kept back, a tie cut, a piece of the soul already gone. She never asked any of them what they had done. The reading left no stain on her.

### The Making

She took up the work of the fae the Courts cast down. Her workings reached them where no line of sight ran, into their sleep and under the veil she kept over her own presence, and she became Spirit-Weaver of the Fallen Fae.

From that work came the bridge. She learned to join a higher stratum to a lower one and to hold the join open long enough for something to cross. She could not cross it herself. Whatever crossed stayed bound to her.

Then she was banished. Divine relics still responded to her despite the banishment. She had stopped being the party that had to be accommodated.

### The Cost

None of the bonds gave her ground. She could bind to anyone. She had nowhere to anchor, and her whole strength was other people.

At Altherion the law held every person in its territory alike, whatever had cast them out. She met Selantra and Velcrissa there. In a hired room she told them what she could hold and what she could not. While she spoke, the light that lay dormant in her flickered. The three agreed there to work as one order, the Triune of the Three-Headed Serpent, with Virelsa as its Matron. Of every bond she carried, those two were the ones she chose.

Later the Creviceward Circle sent for the Matron. A man had been carried out from inside the line the Accord keeps around the Crevice. He was breathing. What he was had come away from him in pieces, and Estrella, a medium on the edge of the Circle, had known his shape and could not reach all of him. Virelsa came to the line with Velcrissa and read the man by what was missing. She knew the crossing would bind her further. She joined the stratum where the rest of him had gone to the one where he lay, held it open, and stayed on her own side while Estrella sutured him across. The seam held. From then on Estrella was one more bond she held.

### Where They Stand

Virelsa is the Exiled Matron of the Triune and Spirit-Weaver of the Fallen Fae. Nobody has set down what she was banished from, the Courts or the Triune, or when. Divine relics respond to her still.

She is close to the number of bonds she can hold, and every crossing she keeps open adds one. Whoever wants her bridge shut has only to wait. She has held it for others. She has never crossed it.

### Ties

- **Velcrissa · The Dusken Bloom** · co-founders; comrades. Velcrissa is the other bond Virelsa chose, and she came with Virelsa to the exclusion line and sat the night with the medium.
- **Estrella of the Lattice Veil** · comrades at a crossing. Virelsa held her bridge open for Estrella's rite.
- **Emilia Veirynthiel · The Bloom of Dissonant Realms** · the Fallen Fae. Cast out by the High Fae, Emilia is one of the Fallen Fae, and Virelsa is their Spirit-Weaver.

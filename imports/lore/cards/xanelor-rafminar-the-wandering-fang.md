## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card, the two archived scenes it appears in, and the record of Class X and what it grows into. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Xanelor Rafminar came ashore at two on the northern strand of Omoro Island. He was lashed to wreckage that bore no flag, and three fresh cuts ran from his left temple to the jaw. What made them, and whose ship it was, is not known.

Drogo found him screaming in a language Drogo did not speak. The old turtle kept a hut on the leeward slope, its doorway built for a body that carried its house on its back. He gave the boy his name. His own people had lived in the ruins on the eastern ridge, and they were gone.

The fisherfolk called Xanelor the turtle's ward. Their boys called him worse. At six he came up from the tide pools with blood on his knees and asked to be taught to fight. Drogo set him to weed the fourth bed by hand. He weeded it every day of that wet season, until he could tell crabgrass from purslane by touch in the dark. By ten every blow they threw found him somewhere else, and the boys stopped coming.

### The Making

Then Drogo taught him to fight, beginning with a blackwood bow that Xanelor finished on the third attempt. He learned the Sage Arts, which Drogo said were no magic: breath, listening, and a stillness that let a gecko nest in the crook of his elbow. Drogo planted stakes in the clearing at irregular intervals, strung rope and swinging shells between them, and sent him through blind. "The hero stands in the gap," Drogo told him.

In the second year the cough came. Drogo kept carving a mask from flakes of his own shell, with leaf-shaped eyes and no mouth. He wrote to the Headmaster of the Aetherion Academy, and when the reply came he sent the boy away. He told Xanelor to wear the mask and let them see the good. Two days later Xanelor left on a trading vessel, in a coat Drogo had sewn with claws that shook. Drogo had told him not to look back. He looked back. Drogo died on the island after he had gone.

At fourteen, the year of the ship, someone tried to kill him. Xanelor stood over the body afterward. His hands had done nothing the garden had not taught them.

In Class X, asked for his goal, he answered through the mask: "I made a promise to someone. He is gone. The promise is not." Rikudoku Moto slept at a desk in the same room. At the introductions Xanelor told the room he had no magic. Hiromi Mahuo challenged him that morning, and Xanelor advised against it and accepted. In the Combat Arena he crossed the ring and struck first, which the Way of Growing Things never does. Both his forearms cracked. The Omoro bow split through its upper limb.

After the Academy he took to wild ground and held it unasked. He learned to cut anchors into what he raised, live, with his own hands. He set his constructs around what he meant to hold at uneven intervals, as the stakes had stood in Drogo's clearing, and they stood still. Drogo had once called the gift "Nature speaking through a mouth that learned to ask". Xanelor held his ground alone until he asked, and something larger answered. He stopped working alone.

### The Cost

The relief arrived first. For a while he no longer answered alone for everything his hands made. The thing he had handed that answering to signed nothing, and the worse part was already on its way.

The anchors had to be cut live, and the cutting took most of the feeling from the last two fingers of his left hand. That was the hand that had held the Omoro bow.

He would hold no ground on anybody's instruction. Drogo had told him that the man who saves the village and stays for the feast learns to save villages for the feast. Xanelor kept the Outer Wilds and the Frostmarch on nobody's commission, and a formal thanks from any polity would have sent him away. He formed no bonds. His constructs carried one judgement, his own. When he misread a field, all of them misread it at the same instant. Nothing had yet made him pay for that.

### Where They Stand

He walks with the Spirit-Fanged Circle, a wilderness sage order, in the Age of Calamity his card gives him. Six is what he can hold. It used to be seven, and he has told no one. His constructs move like hunted things. The fights he wins, he wins by arriving before the answer does.

He names terrain and weather before he names people, and asked about a person, he describes where they were standing. He has a pact and he does not have bonds. He has had the relief, and the second part has not finished arriving. Whether whatever answers him watches through the constructs, he has never asked; he says the answer changes nothing.

"I don't fight alone," he says.

### Ties

- **Drogo** · guardian. He found the boy in the wreck, named him, and carved him a mask from his own shell.
- **Xanelor Rafiminar** · the same life. The boy of Omoro and Class X grew into the Wandering Fang.
- **Rikudoku Moto** · allies. The card counts him a philosophical ally, and he slept at his desk through Xanelor's first morning in Class X.
- **Kairen Moto · The Iron-Root** · his ally's son. Nothing records that they met.
- **Dalphne of the Pale Bough · The Gentle Fang** · successor. His final thought-wave reached Dalphne, a man he never met, and gave him the root of the Wolf Sage Arts.
- **Hiromi Mahuo** · rivals. Hiromi's challenge cost him two cracked forearms and the Omoro bow, and its outcome is not ruled.
- **Kwon Mu-jin** · teacher. Mu-jin threw the vial that turned Xanelor's arrow to metal short of his son.

## Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Obsidian Vault and the crimson houses. What those sources do not state is new here.*

### Origin

Zeven Halek was born in the coal foreland of the Old World, in a pit village whose ground subsided on a schedule everyone had learned to plan around. His mother tended the draught arrays that kept air moving through the workings below it and the firedamp out of them. The arrays ran on bought Essence. In a bad year of supply, a shaft closed with coal still in it.

She taught him to listen at the shaft head. While an array held, the draught in the shaft made a low, even sound. When one failed, the sound stopped, and the men at the face could not hear it from where they worked. As a boy he could stand at a shaft head and name the failed array by the draught that was missing.

He went from the foreland's guild school into the Guild Accord.

### The Making

The Accord made him a field operative and sent him where its instruments could not see: into unmarked zones, close to the Veil-Ring, after lost operatives and forbidden knowledge. He read a battlefield's patterns in seconds and chose strategy over strength. He learned to vanish in the middle of a fight, and to turn certain workings back on the one who cast them.

Erynn, Marelle and Sato served in his original squad, and he was bonded to them. The squad went into the Ivory Vault, and the Vault collapsed. The three died in it. Cylus Verenn lost his original squad in the same collapse.

The Ivory Vault Incident took his team and gave him the eye. He saw his own soul from outside. Every crack in it that had come to bear load was visible and labelled, and he could not stop seeing any of them. He had that sight rebuilt into an instrument and pointed it at other people's fractures. He never pointed it at his own.

### The Cost

The instrument was the Verdant Aetherscope, a reconstructed artificial eye forged after the Vault and linked to his soul. With it he read hostile casting patterns and hidden resonance fields: where a working had been cast, by whom, in what order, and at what. He read absence best. He found what had been blocked, taken away or withheld, and from the shape of the gap he derived the shape of what had made it. Something present and ordinary did not show in it at all. Every overexertion burned his soul. The burn did not reverse.

He built nothing on the side of himself the squad had filled. He led on competence alone, and his units followed him because he was demonstrably right. He did not speak of the three.

He and Cylus came to the Fall of the Whispering Crucible as the pair the Vault had left, back in the coal foreland. Wilhelm planted himself at the way out and did not move until the unit was clear. Zeven worked behind him with the eye, reading the gaps in the field and laying seals in them that went off hours later behind the column. Cylus came out beside him. Wilhelm came out last.

The Accord sent him to a caravan halt on the Calverne road, in the Outer World, to retrieve a courier missing after a Red Moon Night. He read the halt. It gave him a gap: something had passed through quieter than the ground behind it and left nothing. From the gap he derived a shape and no name. He laid no seals, since nothing was coming back, filed the reading, and brought nobody home. A gap can have nothing behind it. He had no way to check.

Basilio Pavaret came to the halt in the days after, and its people gave him their grief willingly. Zeven was still there when Basilio asked to sketch him. He let him and would not pose. He could read the tether beginning to form between them. At the last moment he looked away.

### Where They Stand

Zeven Halek is a Master Guilder and Commander of Accord Field Operations, of the Vanguard, Second Ring. He leads tactical units on Inner and Outer World missions and takes the high-risk deployments where nobody can see in. He still claims aloud that Wilhelm saved his life twice. Wilhelm was not built to answer it.

Cylus Verenn grounds him, even when he pretends not to care. Vaerion Syl'Tharas is the one operative he trusts at his back without question. He says he learned "what not to look away from." Basilio still sketches him, and every sketch has the face turned away.

The step that would make him impossible to find runs through the side of himself he stopped building at the Vault. He has never taken it. Nobody has ever asked whose eye the Aetherscope was reconstructed from, and he has not asked. The sheath of his side-blade carries three names: Erynn, Marelle, Sato.

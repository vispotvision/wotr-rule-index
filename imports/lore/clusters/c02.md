# c02 · The Kōkan expedition's hands

Cluster record for the Lore pass. Members: garret-longshore-the-helmsman, soren-the-mast-the-climb, nella-forgeman-the-open-hand, dunstan-the-brine-the-ladle, matheus-the-silent-eye, borin-ironheart-the-master-of-the-soul-forge, heisuke-muken-s-last-soldier, sadamu-called-futakoto-two-words, lilia-the-stormfold-nurse.

**Era.** The archive's present in the Inner World: the eleventh year after Muken's assassination, from the crossing to the polar shelf to the north gate of Kharven-Seat. Borin's card reaches back through centuries at Varrak-Thur, and his record runs on to the Stannvaard annexation in `the_revolution_of_the_inner_world.md`.

Cards are cited by roster slug and scenes by file name under `scenes/`. "New" marks what this record adds where the sources are silent.

## 1 Who is here

**Garret Longshore** (garret-longshore-the-helmsman). Garret Longshore is navigator and helmsman for the Kōkan expedition, Concord-born in the western fishing settlements. His hands are cracked and bleached by salt. His forearms are roped from a life of pulling against the ocean, and he keeps his face shaved because beards freeze. He holds the wheel with one hand and watches a compass he does not trust, and he trusts the sea less. His card sums him up: "Believes nothing. Calculates everything. Arrives anyway." His oath offered what he had, a wheel held in bad weather, a doubtful compass read, a ship kept alive in water that wanted it dead. When Sodoku walked out of the igloo in glacier-crystal armour, Garret adjusted his heading and said nothing.

**Soren the Mast** (soren-the-mast-the-climb). Soren is the expedition's rigger, lookout and forward reconnaissance, a Concord man built like a rope. He has climbed since childhood. His hair has bleached to the colour of wet straw, and his feet grip ratlines the way other men's hands grip tools. He talks to the helm in a hand language he and Garret built over years. His oath was the climbing itself, and once it was given he went back up to the roof, because the roof was the watch. From the mast-pole he gave the report on the Titan Droval at rigger speed. His card keeps the ground as the place he visits between climbs.

**Nella Forgeman** (nella-forgeman-the-open-hand). Nella Forgeman is field surgeon for the expedition, a smith's daughter from a chartered quarter of the Concord who came to surgery through the Accord's field-medic certification. Her palms carry calluses from her father's shop and her fingers a surgeon's steadiness. Over her coat she wears a canvas apron with loops sewn for instruments, and the apron stays cleaner than the coat because the apron is the thing she can control. She swore on her right hand, palm out, fingers spread. Her oath reaches past the people in front of her: "Every person on this ice and every person we collect between here and Kharven." Her protocol for a Titan Droval ends in one word. Run.

**Dunstan the Brine** (dunstan-the-brine-the-ladle). Dunstan the Brine is cook, quartermaster and provisions officer for the expedition. His card leaves his age indeterminate. Ship-work has pared a once-heavy Concord man down to a harder frame, and galley steam keeps his face red and damp. He has no Attunement and says so in his oath, which promises a hot galley, a fed crew and salt pork made edible, the nearest thing to alchemy such a man can manage. His crews eat without comment. He takes that as praise. When the Titan Droval came, his hands found a cast-iron ladle, and he asked for it to go "into whatever record we are pretending to maintain."

**Matheus** (matheus-the-silent-eye). Matheus of the Ketsuen Enclave is the expedition's ward-craft, Yukari-trained, charged with inscription and spatial defence. He is lean and dark-haired. His quilted coat is of Yukari cut, belted with a sash whose ward-symbols have faded in the wash and stay legible to anyone with Attunement. His hands carry no forge scar and no blade callus. He swore on his work, that his inscriptions will hold and "The holding will outlast my hands." He lined the gauntlets of the Arctic Lion with silk-thin ward-fabric, and that lining is the one piece of the Verdict Plate signed by two craftsmen. He holds that a second look confesses a bad first one.

**Borin Ironheart** (borin-ironheart-the-master-of-the-soul-forge). Borin Ironheart is High Dawi of Varūn descent and a Rune-Forger lineage: High Forgewright of Varrak-Thur, Warden of the Adamant Vaults, in the deep halls beneath the Guild Seat of Stannvaard. Kings, Guild factors and condemned men petition his forge on equal footing. His card turns on Thurgan Ashmantle, the apprentice whom petrification was consuming. Borin was certain the forge could save him. Thurgan chose dissolution, and Borin held the quench steady while the forge took him. His students name what followed the Eight Silent Years: the hammer every day, no jokes, no visitors. He does not speak Thurgan's name in the Forge. He is warm and unhurried, calls everyone lad or lass, and tells terrible jokes; when the jokes stop, the diagnosis is bad. His card never mentions the expedition. The scenes carry him on it as its aethersmith.

**Heisuke** (heisuke-muken-s-last-soldier). Heisuke is one of the last men alive who served Muken's border guard, Moto by his card (main branch, border guard), and senior military authority on the Kōkan expedition. His iron-grey hair is bound in one heavy knot at the crown in the Moto military style. Polar wind has weathered his skin to worked leather. On his left forearm a genealogy in black lines runs from wrist to elbow, marking his service, his kills and the names of the men he failed to bring home. His sword is uninscribed Stannvaard steel, kept with obsessive care, because he does not trust Aetheric solutions to physical problems. He has not spoken to Sodoku since boarding and will speak when Sodoku requires it. His card: "He held no position in the exile. He held the memory."

**Sadamu, called Futakoto** (sadamu-called-futakoto-two-words). Sadamu is a frontier Moto, a cadet by his card, raised on the Polar Reach where food is scarce and movement constant. He is the expedition's forward scout and perimeter watch, and the youngest of its hands. His black hair is cropped to the skull. A thin scar crosses the bridge of his nose from a training accident that taught him distance. He wears a light coat over nothing. At his belt hangs a short, forward-curved Moto field knife, and he speaks the frontier register, where breath costs heat and words cost breath. His oath was "I swear." At the Titan Droval he drew the knife, worked out the odds and put it away; his card calls the knife an insult.

**Lilia** (lilia-the-stormfold-nurse). Lilia is a Stormfold woman from a ridge settlement, nurse and field medic at the Greymane estate and primary caretaker for Bram Greymane's wounded. She is tall for a northern woman, nearly Bram's height, with the long frame the ridge breeds. Her hair is dark straw, knotted in linen that was white once. Her eyes are pale green. She read Bram's quiet after the field as his choice, and his body as sound. She told him the cleaning would hurt more than the hobgoblins had, "and the treatment is not negotiable." Her card closes on the wound Wren opened, which she cannot stitch.

## 2 What already binds them

- **The post.** Seven cards name the Kōkan expedition as the post: Garret, Soren, Nella, Dunstan, Matheus, Heisuke and Sadamu (their Role lines; spelling differs, §7). Borin's card does not. Edward Lambert counts him at the gate: "He came back with a Titan, a Dawi smith, a Yukari warder and a wife" (`sodoku_gate_reunion_lambert.md`).
- **The oaths.** Seven of the nine swore at the rift's edge after Robin Ice, in this order: Borin, Matheus, Garret, Sadamu, Nella, Dunstan, Soren (`the_true_king_of_the_north_part_7.md`, author notes). Sodoku's look names them one by one: "At Borin, whose metal did not break. At Matheus, whose perception was the oath. At Garret, who had not drowned." (part 7).
- **Garret and Soren.** Soren's card: "Communicates with the helm through a private language of hand signals developed over years with Garret." The scenes agree. Part 1 has "a private language of hand signals that he and Garret had developed over years of working the same ships" (`the_true_king_of_the_north_part_1.md`), and on the shelf Soren comes down from the mast-pole with the Droval count: "He landed beside Garret." (`the_true_king_of_the_north_part_14.md`).
- **Garret and Sadamu.** On the crossing the scout crouched at the bow "with a sounding line in his hands, calling depths back to the helm" (part 1), and Garret held the helm.
- **Soren and Sadamu.** Both keep the highest igloo's roof. Sadamu: "The young Moto crouched on the highest igloo's roof" on watch (`the_true_king_of_the_north_part_6.md`). Soren: "From the highest igloo's roof, where he had been perched since before the gathering began, Soren dropped to the ice" (part 7).
- **Borin and Matheus.** "Matheus crossed the deck without speaking and fell into step beside Borin" (part 1). The forge's accelerant was "mixed from materials Matheus had provided" (`the_true_king_of_the_north_part_8.md`). "The hybrid is the only component in the Verdict Plate that carries two craftsmen's signatures." (matheus-the-silent-eye). "Matheus's ward-fabric lining found his palms" (`the_true_king_of_the_north_part_12.md`).
- **Borin and Dunstan.** Dunstan "was the oldest person on the ice sheet after Borin" (part 7). The makeshift forge's fuel "was a combination of rendered fat" (part 8).
- **Borin and Nella.** The scene sets their offered hands side by side: "the smith offering his hands as instruments the way Nella had offered hers at the oath" (part 8). Nella is a smith's daughter (nella-forgeman-the-open-hand).
- **Nella and Heisuke.** On the crossing she marks the old soldier at the stern rail: "The big Moto at the stern will need his left hand checked before the week is out." (part 1).
- **Nella and Dunstan.** "Nella grabbed Dunstan's arm." and, after the fight, "Nella's hand was still on Dunstan's arm." (`the_true_king_of_the_north_part_15.md`). At the gate, over the ladle, "Nella was telling him it counted as an assault charge" (`sodoku_gate_reunion_lambert.md`).
- **Nella and Matheus.** Sonzai's order over the stranger found on the ice: "Matheus watches her signature. Nella watches her condition." (`the_true_king_of_the_north_part_13.md`). Earlier he waits "until Nella and Matheus could determine what exactly he had found" (`the_true_king_of_the_north_part_9.md`).
- **Heisuke and Sadamu.** Both cards read Moto: "Moto (main branch, border guard)" and "Moto (frontier cadet)". The column crossed the shelf in a loose formation Sadamu designed and Sodoku approved (part 14), with Heisuke, by his card, its "Senior military authority".
- **Heisuke and Borin.** Heisuke's sword is "Good steel, Stannvaard-forged" (heisuke-muken-s-last-soldier). Borin's forge lies in "deep halls beneath the Guild Seat of Stannvaard" (borin-ironheart-the-master-of-the-soul-forge).
- **Nella and Lilia.** As Bram is carried into the Greymane estate, his surgeon is "not Nella because Nella was with Sodoku's ship and not here" (`the_true_king_of_the_north_part_11.md`), and then "Lilia found him." Both cards carry one line, "the treatment is not negotiable" (§7).
- **The Stormfold.** "Heisuke had not spoken since Stormfold." (`sodoku_gate_reunion_lambert.md`). Sodoku at the gate: "I saw the pack you built in Stormfold." (same scene). Lilia's card: "Origin: Concord (Stormfold, ridge settlement)".
- **Outside ties the sources already make.** Edward Lambert counts Heisuke among the six who drove the survey stakes for Muken's wall: "Of the men who drove those stakes there were six, and I had accounted five" (`sodoku_gate_reunion_lambert.md`). Lambert's card counts "how many healing supplies Brida and Nella have remaining" (edward-lambert-the-arithmetic). Ymir Rhok's card names Borin his "Gruff mentor" (ymir-rhok-the-emberhand). Bram's card: "stitched closed by Lilia's hand" (bram-greymane-the-ridge).

## 3 The shared history

### E1 · The crossing to the polar shelf (scene)

- **When:** the eleventh year after Muken's assassination, the last weeks at sea before the rift camp.
- **Where:** at sea off the polar shelf beyond the Polar Reach, aboard the expedition's ship.
- **Who:** Garret, Soren, Nella, Dunstan, Matheus, Borin, Heisuke, Sadamu.
- **What happened** (`the_true_king_of_the_north_part_1.md`, `the_true_king_of_the_north_part_6.md`): The polar wind held the ship heeled to starboard for hours. Garret had the helm and read a storm coming by morning in the falling pressure. Soren worked the rigging and signed to the helm. Sadamu crouched at the bow with a sounding line and called depths back to Garret, while Dunstan passed bowls of salt pork and barley up the galley hatch. Nella sat on the cargo hatch with her instrument roll. She was counting frostbite before the shelf ice, and she marked the old soldier at the stern rail for favouring his left hand. Heisuke stood at that rail with his arms folded. He did not speak to Sodoku. Borin went below to re-seat the keel array, and Matheus went down with him unasked.
- **What each took:** Garret, a crossing brought to the ice. Soren, a helm that answered his signals. Sadamu, his first post in the column, the lead-line calling to Garret's wheel. Dunstan, a crew that ate without comment. Nella, a patient who had not asked to be one. Heisuke, a left hand the surgeon had already seen. Borin and Matheus, their first joint work, a keel array that held to the ice.

### E2 · The oaths at the rift's edge (scene)

- **When:** the eleventh year, at the rift camp, on the day Sodoku told his brother of Hild.
- **Where:** the polar rift on the ice sheet beyond the Polar Reach.
- **Who:** Borin, Matheus, Garret, Sadamu, Nella, Dunstan, Soren.
- **What happened** (`the_true_king_of_the_north_part_7.md`, `the_true_king_of_the_north_part_8.md`): The camp gathered at the rift's edge without a summons, and Borin's hammer fell quiet at the forward inscription station. They swore standing, to the Crown and to the Commander, each in their own form, after Robin Ice. Borin gave three facts, "Borin. Son of Kettan. Called Ironheart.", and held his fist to his sternum for one Dawi breath; his fourth slot, the oath-name, stayed empty. Matheus swore on his work and inclined his head. Garret said he had not drowned yet and nodded once. Sadamu said his own name, then "I swear." Nella held up her right hand, palm out, then went back to scanning the semicircle for frostbite. Dunstan swore that no one under their command would go hungry "while I have hands and a fire", and returned to the supply cache. Soren dropped from the roof, swore on the climbing and climbed back up. Heisuke is not among the oath-givers this scene names (§7).
- **What each took:** Borin, an empty fourth slot that the forge on the ice would soon make him question. Matheus, an oath by perception. Garret, a promise to make his skills useful, or to find a way to make them so. Sadamu, the two words his card and his byname keep. Nella, a count with no ceiling. Dunstan, the next meal as his standing charge. Soren, the watch roof, regained before the ceremony ended.

### E3 · The forge on the ice (scene, with two new acts)

- **When:** the rift camp, after the oaths and before the column struck camp.
- **Where:** the rift camp on the ice sheet beyond the Polar Reach: the forward igloo and the makeshift forge.
- **Who:** Borin, Matheus, Dunstan, Heisuke.
- **What happened** (`the_true_king_of_the_north_part_8.md`, `the_true_king_of_the_north_part_9.md`, `the_true_king_of_the_north_part_12.md`, `the_true_king_of_the_north_part_13.md`): Borin built a forge from what he had carried in a leather roll. It had hand bellows and an ironwood slab for an anvil, hauled across the ice by two men on his word. Its fuel was rendered fat and an accelerant he mixed from materials Matheus provided. On it he tuned Kurosetsu, worked the Chains of War, and forged the Arctic Lion. Matheus's silk-thin ward-fabric lines the Lion's gauntlets; that lining is the one part of the Verdict Plate carrying two craftsmen's signatures (matheus-the-silent-eye). When the chains were done, Borin named himself to the empty air, son of Kettan, called Ironheart. For the first time the empty fourth slot seemed fillable. **New:** the rendered fat came from Dunstan's galley stores, so the galley fire and the forge fire ran on one supply. Borin had told Sonzai that his oath was to see all of them properly equipped. **New:** he offered to inscribe the Stannvaard steel at Heisuke's hip. Heisuke declined. The sword crossed the shelf as plain steel, kept by his own hand.
- **What each took:** Borin, work on an object born of a Soul Forge, and the thought that the rift's oath might become his oath-name. Matheus, his signature beside a Dawi's. Dunstan, a second fire drawing on the galley's stores, which he would count along with the first. Heisuke, his sword as it was.

### E4 · The Titan Droval on the polar shelf (scene)

- **When:** the fifth day of the column's march north from the rift.
- **Where:** the polar shelf, on the road from the rift to Kharven.
- **Who:** Soren, Garret, Nella, Dunstan, Sadamu, Matheus, Borin, Heisuke.
- **What happened** (`the_true_king_of_the_north_part_14.md`, `the_true_king_of_the_north_part_15.md`): For four days Soren ranged ahead and signalled clear to Garret. On the fifth Yoko smelled two Titan-class Droval. Soren had gone up the supply sled's mast-pole. He came down beside Garret with the count and read the pair as bonded. Garret stood still, compass in hand. Matheus went forward unasked and said he could not ward against Titan-class. Borin unslung his hammer and studied the fur. Sadamu drew his knife, laughed at the odds and sheathed it. Dunstan set down the sled's lead bar and took up the largest ladle in the galley kit. Nella ran her triage protocol, took Dunstan by the arm as the nearest man who could not fight, and kept hold of him through all of it. Heisuke stepped forward once with his hand on the hilt and did not draw. Sodoku bonded the first Droval. Sonzai claimed the second with the Chains of War. Afterwards Dunstan said they could stop pretending the record existed. Borin asked to study the contact point. Nella let go of Dunstan's arm and began to scan Sodoku.
- **What each took:** Soren, a reading the day proved; the two beasts stayed a pair, "Bonded pair, Soren had said." (part 15). Garret, the line his card keeps, a compass that does not point away from Titan Droval. Nella, a protocol whose short form is to run, and a patient she never had to treat. Dunstan, the ladle and the joke about the record. Sadamu, the knife as an insult to a Titan. Matheus, the thing he had crossed a continent to see, inscription carried by the air. Borin, a Winter-dense hide he had never forged. Heisuke, one step, and the scene's thought that the son moved with his father's weight.

### E5 · The Stormfold halt (new, on two scene traces)

- **When:** after the polar shelf, before the column reached the north gate of Kharven-Seat on a night of the eleventh year.
- **Where:** the Stormfold, at the Greymane estate.
- **Who:** Nella, Lilia, Heisuke.
- **What happened:** The archive keeps two traces of the column in the Stormfold: Heisuke did not speak from there to the gate, and Sodoku saw a pack with bone-setting splints built there (`sodoku_gate_reunion_lambert.md`). **New:** the Greymane estate kept its infirmary at the east end of the great hall (part 11). Nella worked there beside Lilia over the wounded of the southern approach for as long as the column halted. Lilia kept the running of her infirmary. Nella took the cases she was given. Nella counted the Greymane wounded into the oath she swore at the rift, which named every person collected between the ice and Kharven.
- **What each took:** Nella, the Greymane wounded, counted under the oath she swore at the rift along with everyone else she had collected. Lilia, a surgeon from the Crown's column who worked her room on her terms. Heisuke, the quiet he kept to the north gate.

## 6 Same person?

No two cards here are one person. The archive's older names are these cards and nobody else: the scenes call Heisuke "Taulagi" and Sadamu "Afasoa", and ruling R23-7-TAULAGI_AFASOA_PENDING (ruled 2026-09-12) renamed them. A Lore section writes only Heisuke and Sadamu.

## 7 Where the cards disagree

Recorded, not resolved.

1. **Spelling of the expedition.** Garret's card, "Navigator and helmsman for the Kōkan expedition", and Sadamu's, "Forward scout, perimeter watch for the Kōkan expedition", write Kōkan. Soren, Nella, Dunstan, Matheus and Heisuke write Kokan (Nella: "Field surgeon for the Kokan expedition"), the form the part 7 notes list as struck-register residue.
2. **Temür on Garret's card.** Garret's card: "When Temür walked out of the igloo in glacier-crystal armor". Temür is struck for Sodoku (R22-2). Heisuke's card names him Sodoku, once misspelled: "He will speak when Sdoku requires it".
3. **One line on two cards.** Nella's card takes the byline "The Treatment Is Not Negotiable". Lilia's card gives the line to Lilia, "the hurting is the treatment, and the treatment is not negotiable", and the archive speaks it only in Lilia's mouth (`the_true_king_of_the_north_part_11.md`).
4. **Moto or Yuno retainers.** Heisuke's card, "Bloodline: Moto (main branch, border guard)", and Sadamu's, "Bloodline: Moto (frontier cadet)", keep them Moto. The ruling that renamed them reads "Bloodline route: Taulagi and Afasoa become Yuno retainers with Japonic names" (R23-7, notes).
5. **Struck glosses.** Both cards still gloss the struck names: "formerly Taulagi: tau + lagi = war/season + sky" and "formerly Afasoa: afa + soa = storm + companion".
6. **Borin and the Soul Forge (card against scene).** Borin's card: "The Master of the Soul Forge" who "Operates the Soul Forge within the Adamant Vaults". `the_true_king_of_the_north_part_9.md`: "Borin had seen a Soul Forge once."
7. **Borin's face (card against scene).** Card: eyes "dark brown with a reddish undertone"; "His hair is dark, iron-grey streaking through black". Scenes: "the grey-green of unpolished malachite" (part 8); "white hair" (`the_revolution_of_the_inner_world.md`).
8. **Borin's Stage (card against wiki).** Card: "Stage XII" (Catalyst Event). The Arctic Lion page: "Borin Ironheart, Aethersmith (Stage III+). Eleven days, polar crossing".
9. **Lilia (card against scene).** Card: "Age: 28"; "Tall for a northern woman, nearly Bram's height"; "Hair the colour of dark straw". `the_war_in_the_north_v_the_shieldwarden.md`: "thirty-six, short, deep through the chest"; "Sandy hair going grey at the front"; its notes: "Lilia has no card."
10. **Who is oldest (card against scene).** Heisuke's card: "Age: 61". Dunstan's card: "somewhere between forty and sixty". Part 7 calls Dunstan "the oldest person on the ice sheet after Borin".
11. **Who found the Droval (card against scene).** Soren's card: "He was the one who spotted the Titan Droval from the mast-pole". Part 14: "Yoko smelled them first."
12. **Who swore (scene against scene).** Part 7's notes name eight oath-givers without Heisuke: "the eight oaths at the rift's edge (Robin, Borin, Matheus, Garret, Afasoa, Nella, Dunstan, Soren)". Part 15: "Toward the twenty people who had sworn their oaths at the rift's edge", and its list includes him.
13. **An out-of-world line.** Nella's card closes: "A file that is written but never presented is unreachable on mobile."

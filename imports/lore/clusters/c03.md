# c03 · The Ashen Crown: Muken's house and the Moto lines

Kharven's first king and his queen, the daughter his card does not list, the sister who went back to the ruined ground, the son who answered the corridor night, the fox-spirit woman who walked out of a pen beside him, two Moto warriors of the lines outside the crown (Artemis of the Amagiri, Souma of the Byakuya), and a Sword Saint who shares a given name with the man who planned Muken's removal. The span runs from Muken's road north through his sixteen years of rule and the night that ended them, into the archive's present at Kharven. Muken's card files him under the Northern Sovereign Era and Ayame's under the Northern Throne, Late Young World. Artemis's card says War of the Realms Era.

**Sources read.** The nine cards in full, from the Volume I card folder. The wiki pages by WORLD.md's keys: MB, MO, LR, YN, KH, P20 and SP-S, with `rules/doc-moto-reversion-ledger.yaml` for R22-2. In full: `scenes/the_true_king_of_the_north_part_6.md`, where Muken, Ayame and the Tenrai Azure share the hall. Read by passage: Parts 2, 5, 8, 15, 16, 17 and 18 of *The True King of the North*, with `THE_KINGDOM_OF_KHARVEN_corrected.md`, `The_Path_of_Sorrow.md`, `YOKO_MISHIRO.md`, `08_sodoku_recalescence.md`, `the_revolution_of_the_inner_world.md`, `01_wotr_muster_breach_road_north.md`, `02_wren_bulwark.md` and `10_wotr_what_the_ground_was_owed.md`.

**What the archive holds.** Muken, Ayame, Sodoku and Yoko are heavy in the scenes. Yukazuri appears in the Kharven entry only. Mizuki, Artemis and Souma appear in no scene by name; the scenes carry a firstborn daughter of Muken called Mizuyi, an Amagiri head called Ren, and the Byakuya as a house. Azure Serathiel appears in no scene at all. The roster and `scenes/CAST.md` give him eight scenes, and every one of those hits is Azure Tenrai Moto (section 6). Everything new in section 3 is marked new and is built to fit every card it touches.

## 1 · Who is here

**Muken Moto.** The King of Kharven, called the Ashen Blade and He Who Writes Ruin Into Law. His card records him as Moto-aligned through Shingan ignition and the Kharven coronation rites, and his file never names him Moto by descent. He took the crown through blood-conquest and the Hataraki rite and held it for sixteen years of rule, in which conquest was metabolised into law. His card lists his wife, Ayame Yuno, and five children: Sodoku, Sonzai, Emira, Tomuka and Ezo. He is dead. A Tenrai-orchestrated removal killed him outside open combat, and his card names its author, Azure Tenrai Moto, as his posthumous adversary. His judgment needs clear fault before it can act, and his card says that flaw is what killed him. He speaks rarely and finally. No transcript of him exists.

**Ayame Yuno.** The priest-queen of Kharven, of the Yuno Family's Kagura Branch, wed to Muken and still answerable to her own house on islands the north has never seen. She binds conquest into oath, memory and ritual law so that it cannot splinter the realm. On the Night of Thirty Banners she received the northern clans alone in the snowlit courtyard, and by dawn their banners had been rethreaded into three coalitions under one winter covenant; Kharven's first true royal council came out of that night. She refuses to anchor a great Wellspring of Storm, because she fears it would militarise her liturgy beyond recall. Each major rite binds another sliver of her private wants into the office of queen. Her card leaves her alive and at work in Kharven while Muken campaigns at the fringes of the North, designing a convergence rite to bind every future ruler of the North.

**Mizuki Moto.** Her card calls her Muken's Firstborn and the Frost-Braid Heir and places her in Kharven's Moto War Court as his eldest child and its battlefield preservation specialist. She holds damage still in the cold so that the people who win the fight have time to finish it. As a girl she stopped a bleed long enough for treatment, and the wound reopened when her field ended. Later she beat an older fighter by freezing his sword's recovery. Through one siege night she kept a wounded room alive, and she remembers every breath that failed before dawn. She gives assessments to people who want comfort. Her card names no mother. Her Crystal is recorded as looking for an equal witness, someone who chooses to remain.

**Yukazuri Moto.** Muken's sister, called the Still Knife and the Moto of No House: human, mortal, unbloodlined, Moto by blood only, sworn to nobody. Since Muken died she has lived alone in a cabin on the ruined Moto ancestral ground, and her card says she has not left it in any season that mattered. She built her whole Temperance around listening for one person. His removal came without warning and left her no body to sit beside. The ground is saturated with the memory of its dead, so she touches as little of it as she can. She wears a plain short blade out of habit and has never once put down the Grey Wrap. She still refuses to claim ground.

**Sodoku Moto** (record mode). The Exiled Lion and the Kōkan Butcher, former Crown Heir of the Kōkan line. A Tenrai-commissioned corridor assassination took his father, his mother and a brother in one night, and he and Sonzai answered by burning the Tenrai main-branch settlement to the ground, women and children included. The High Council exiled him and charged him with the massacre; his card says the charge is neither entirely wrong nor entirely complete. His card places him on the Scourge of Hell with Yoko and his younger sister Emira. The Kamigan ranks everything and has no off switch, and a room inside it that it cannot rank holds Yoko and Emira. He carries his father's covenant beads. Every ranking he makes answers a question his father taught him to ask.

**Yoko Mishiro.** A fox-spirit beastkin of the Mishiro line, called the One Who Stayed. Her Catalyst was the lock on the pen: she walked through it on her own decision, past a man who had already stopped waiting for her. Her nose reads intent, residue and threat without pause. Her card, at twenty-nine, has her settled in a cabin on the Scourge's edge with her husband, their son Riku and a second child on the way, and it names the husband by the struck form of Sodoku's name. She wants to be the one who stayed, on record, by her own account.

**Azure Serathiel · The Sky-Veiled Saint.** A wandering Sword Saint, master of Aether-Flow swordsmanship and Guardian of the Celestial Oath, formerly of the Veil-Court of Serica, now disbanded. His card makes him the secret patron of the lost Wellspring of Benediction and says House Moto is alleged to hold him under an Aegis Mandate, which he denies. His Catalyst survives as a legend: he duelled fate on a mountain of still air. Nobody knows what he agreed to there. He holds no ground and never sought any. His card's relationships run to Vaelen Mourcrix, Iloritha the Moth Maiden, Ambrose Virellith, Evangelien Rhymaris and Emilia Veirynthiel, and none of them touch Kharven.

**Artemis Amagiri Moto.** The Bear, the Mist-Breaker, the Closed Road: an Amagiri of the Moto bloodline, sworn to House Moto, built like a siege beast in a line that favours evasion. He makes movement expensive and turns roads, charge lanes and retreat lines into traps that close toward him. He held a mountain pass long after retreat became the sane choice, held the Hollow Bridge crossing, gave the Forty-Nine Pursuers each a road into one dead ground, and stood alone at the throat of the Indigo Pass against a night descent in fog. In House Moto's service he works as a border warden, a siege interceptor and a campaign beast. His card leaves him in the storm marches between transit territories, hunting the single enemy who once broke his pass-law cleanly.

**Souma Byakuya Moto.** The White Fang at the Threshold, called the Blessed. He is of the Moto bloodline and Sacrament-marked, the sworn threshold-guardian of the Hidden Moto Village under the Way of the Sacrament. On the Night of the Broken Chancel he completed a blood-soaked sacrament under live assault and held the breach until others could survive, and the village named him the Blessed. At Snowgate he held the northern passage long enough for an evacuation; the route held, the civilians lived, and it cost him memory and soul-strain. He wants a village that can survive without his body as its wall. He fears that each time he protects what he loves he becomes its cage.

**Muken and Yukazuri.** Yukazuri's card: Muken is "Brother, deceased", "The only bond she ever oriented her whole life around", and his death was "a removal she never saw coming and had no body to sit beside" (`Yukazuri Moto.md`). **Muken and Mizuki.** Mizuki's card: "Muken's Firstborn"; her axis on him reads "Firstborn; her power is what his war strategy requires" (`Mizuki Moto.md`). Muken's card does not list her (section 7). The archive gives Muken a firstborn daughter under another name: "Mizuyi survived. **Sodoku and Yoko.** Sodoku's card: the bond is "unnamed by him for the same reason it is unnamed by her" (`Sodoku Moto.md`). (`scenes/The_Path_of_Sorrow.md`), and her account of it: "he broke her lock, he bought her bread, he called her a tool because calling her a person was too expensive" (`scenes/YOKO_MISHIRO.md`). After the narrows, "Yoko Mishiro's hand on his arm was the one thing that came in from outside" (SP-S). Her card calls their son Riku. The archive says "They called him Rikudoku." (`scenes/the_revolution_of_the_inner_world.md`).

**Mizuki and Yukazuri, an echo the cards make without a meeting.** Mizuki's card says she "Grew up learning her power was most necessary after violence", and Yukazuri's card says the Veyrath Ditch Contract "Cemented her role as the one who arrives after". Both work the hours after the fighting. Section 3 puts them in one room.

**Artemis's line and the removal.** The archive ties the Amagiri, never Artemis by name, to the corridor. ## 3 · The shared history

### The road north with a sister

**When.** Before the Sixteen-Year Crown, on the road that ended in the conquest of the north (Kharven entry; the card's Catalyst is the coronation at its end).
**Where.** From Nalūn north and east into the boreal forest of Kharven's highland plateau (WORLD §5).
**Who.** Muken Moto, Yukazuri Moto.
**What happened.** Muken was born in Nalūn, and his sister shared his mother and none of the blood the house would recognise. He fed them both on what his hands could earn and went north with the warriors who trusted his judgment, carrying a sister, a reputation from other people's wars and no standing. By the count of her own card she was a small child on that road, which is how her axis on him came to be filial and formative. Basis: scene and card, with the Long Reading's account of Nalūn as ruled on the Kharven entry.
**What each took.** Muken took the crown at the road's end, through blood-conquest and rite. Yukazuri took the one bond she oriented her whole life around.

### The Still Snow Vigil at the Ridgewall (new)

**When.** In the last years of Muken's reign, while the Greymanes held the western line against the hobgoblin raids that Godfrey reports to Muken in his last year (Part 5).
**Where.** The Ridgewall, the Greymane fortress in the western highlands of Kharven (WORLD §5).
**Who.** Mizuki Moto, Yukazuri Moto.
**What happened.** A raid held the fortress under assault from dusk to dawn. A room behind the wall filled with the wounded, and Mizuki opened her field over it and held it through the night, slowing blood and breath and the leak of Essence until the surgeons could reach each cot. Yukazuri came to the room after the fighting reached it, as she always came, and sat beside the one soldier the field was holding who could not be saved. His dying took eleven hours. Her broadcast kept him calm, and she listened to him to the end. Neither card records that they spoke.
**What each took.** Mizuki took the Vigil her card records: a wounded room kept alive, and every breath that failed before dawn remembered, among them the one her field lengthened. Yukazuri took the Temperance step her card calls the dying soldier's eleven hours. The Still Line on Mizuki's card stays open; this night does not settle it.

### The corridor night

**When.** The night after the last assembly of Muken's sixteenth year of rule, the eve of the ceremony (Parts 5 and 6).
**Where.** Kharven-Seat (WORLD §5).
**Who.** Muken Moto, Ayame Yuno, Sodoku Moto; Yukazuri Moto by the loss.
**What happened.** Azure Tenrai Moto had put his case for a correction to the Tenrai council and to the Amagiri head, Ren, and the channel they built went through the palace in one night. Muken died in the corridor outside his study; Ezo and Tomuka were killed in rooms Ayame's Saishiki had warded, by a trusted face the wards let pass. The wards were still whole when the bodies were found. The archive has Ayame die later by her own hand, and the Saishiki with her. Sodoku and Sonzai answered at the Tenrai settlement; the High Council sealed what happened there beside the exile decree. Yukazuri's card says the removal came without warning and left her no body to sit beside. The archive's firstborn, Mizuyi, was away from the palace that night; whether she is the carded Mizuki is open (section 6), so Mizuki is left out of this event. Basis: scene and card.
**What each took.** Muken took the Removal, the fracture his card leaves unconsolidated. Ayame's card stands at an earlier moment and records nothing of this night. The archive gives her the wards and the ending. Sodoku took his Catalyst and the Splintering, the massacre and the exile, a seam his card calls closed and load-bearing and stronger than the Crystal around it. Yukazuri took her second Catalyst and the cabin on the ruined ground.

### The broken lock

**When.** In the exile, while Sodoku travelled with Emira toward the Scourge of Hell (`scenes/The_Path_of_Sorrow.md`).
**Where.** The holding pens of a township on the road into the Scourge of Hell; WORLD.md does not place the Scourge.
**Who.** Sodoku Moto, Yoko Mishiro.
**What happened.** Sodoku broke the lock on Yoko's pen with his blade's pommel, then the locks down the rest of the row. He bought her bread and broth, called her a tool, and sat outside the inn in the rain while she slept beside his sister. By her card she walked through the lock on her own decision. She travelled on with them into the Scourge. Basis: scene and card.
**What each took.** Yoko took her Catalyst and, in time, the bond her card calls absolute. Sodoku took the Unranked Category his card records, the room inside the Kamigan that holds Yoko and Emira.

### Snowgate (new)

**When.** In the kingless years after Muken's removal, once the Greymanes had held the western ridgeline as long as their stores and their discipline allowed and had withdrawn from it.
**Where.** The Stormfold, on the northern approaches of the western ridgeline (WORLD §5). Souma's card names the passage Snowgate; no page places the Hidden Moto Village, and this event does not.
**Who.** Souma Byakuya Moto, Artemis Amagiri Moto.
**What happened.** Raiders came through the unheld approaches, and civilians had to be moved out through the northern passage. Souma planted Shirokiba at the passage mouth and held it while they went through. Beyond him the road climbed to a pass the Amagiri post had always kept, and Artemis closed it behind the last of them against the pursuit, and went on holding it long after retreat had become the sane choice. The two lines held one retreat from its two ends. The route held. Basis: new, built from both cards.
**What each took.** Souma took what his card records of Snowgate: memory-loss, deeper soul-strain, and a worsening fear that he can only matter through suffering. Artemis took the lesson his card records from holding that pass, that survival could be imposed as law. The enemy who broke his pass-law is not this night's; his hunt stays open.

## 5 · Outward

- **muken-moto.** A foreign sovereign who calls him brother. The archive names one, the Zettari sovereign Revari, who crossed into Kharven with his son to warn a king he had known since a border town.
- **ayame-yuno.** An elder of her own branch. She is still answerable to her house on Yasoshima, whose council ratified her marriage as a covenant between houses.
- **mizuki-moto.** The equal witness her Crystal is looking for, a field surgeon or bonesetter who stays at the cot with her through the hours after.
- **yukazuri-moto.** Her card's named foil. That is a person who chooses connection despite the cost, comes to the ruined ground, and stays past one season.
- **sodoku-moto.** A Banner House lord whose oath he must earn again, because the oaths expired with his father and the kingdom is re-sworn one house at a time (Part 18).
- **yoko-mishiro.** Another beastkin who came out of a pen. She would know the third category without being told.
- **azure-serathiel-the-sky-veiled-saint.** The office of the Holy Sea of Alabaster that banned *The Blue Vigil*, which his card asks to have named as the banning authority.
- **artemis-amagiri-moto.** The single enemy who once broke his pass-law cleanly.
- **souma-byakuya-moto.** A student he trains to hold the line, so that the village stops relying on one body, as his card's hook asks.

## 6 · Same person?

No two cards in this cluster are one person.

**Azure Serathiel is a different man from Azure Tenrai Moto.** The cluster placed the Sky-Veiled Saint here on the strength of scene hits, and every hit belongs to the Tenrai Azure, who has no card. The Tenrai Azure heads the Tenrai line, argues the correction to the Tenrai council and the Amagiri head, keeps a face that says nothing for sixteen years, fathers Seiji, and dies after the corridor night (Part 16: "the timing's error was the thing that had killed Azure"). Seiji calls him High King of the North. The Saint's card names no Tenrai, no Kharven, no son and no throne. His Mirrorshade "does not discriminate" and broadcasts his state to anyone who can read it, and his Wellsprings are Mirithane, Nyxial, Tenebra and Aeolyth, with no Shingan eye and no Work. His one tie to the Moto is the alleged Aegis Mandate he denies. They share a given name. The Lore writes them as two men, and no Lore sentence gives the Saint the corridor.

**Mizuki and the scenes' Mizuyi are left unmerged.** Mizuki's card makes her Muken's firstborn and the Frost-Braid Heir; Part 6 makes Mizuyi Muken's firstborn, of the Kōkan line, with the strongest claim by primogeniture, away from the palace on the corridor night and later married into the Zettari. R22-2 lists the two names separately (Mizuki from the struck founder's name, Mizuyi unconverted), and the Moto Bloodline page gives the name Mizuki to the house's co-founder beside Bara. No card and no ruling says the carded Mizuki is Mizuyi. Mizuyi has no card, so the pair falls outside this question as posed; the Weaver should treat Mizuki's scenes as silent.

**Struck names for the same people.** The Moto Origin and Long Reading pages call Yukazuri by her struck Mongolian-register name, and Yoko's card calls Sodoku by his; R22-2 makes both the same person as the carded name. Neither struck form is written in any Lore.

## 7 · Where the cards disagree

Quoted and left standing.

1. **Muken's children.** Muken's card: "Children, Sodoku , Sonzai , Emira , Tomuka , Ezo" (Spirit Axes), the list the 2026-09-12 ruling adopted. Mizuki's card: "Muken's Firstborn" and "active in Kharven as Muken's eldest child". The archive's firstborn is Mizuyi (Part 6).
2. **Who was heir.** Mizuki's card: "The Frost-Braid Heir". Sodoku's card: "Kōkan Line. Former Crown Heir". Part 6 gives Mizuyi "the strongest claim to the throne by primogeniture".
3. **The brother in the corridor.** Sodoku's card: the assassination "took his father Muken, his mother, and his brother" under a name the 2026-09-12 ruling struck. Muken's card lists five children, and that name is not among them; the archive kills Ezo and Tomuka that night (Part 6).
4. **How Ayame died.** Sodoku's card puts his mother's death in the one night. Part 6: "Ayame died later, by her own hand"; `08_sodoku_recalescence.md` agrees. Ayame's own card stands before any of it: "Active in Kharven, maintaining the White Concord". Card against scene.
5. **Mizuki's age against the reign.** Mizuki's card gives her age as nineteen with Muken living and ruling. The Kharven entry in the archive: "the marriage and the children came inside the sixteen-year reign, not ahead of it" (`THE_KINGDOM_OF_KHARVEN_corrected.md`). Card against scene.
6. **Muken's eyes.** Muken's card: "slate-grey with a heavy amber ring". `The_Path_of_Sorrow.md`: "all that amber-gold where Muken's were warm brown". Card against scene.
7. **Spouses in two eras.** Muken's card: "Northern Sovereign Era". Ayame's card: "Northern Throne, Late Young World". Neither maps to WORLD §1.
8. **When Yoko came.** Sodoku's card, at twenty-two, has walked into the Unranked room "for three years, for Yoko and Emira". `The_Path_of_Sorrow.md` sets the broken lock after "five years of grief" since the corridor, and Part 6 makes him sixteen that night. Card against scene.
9. **A Moto village.** Souma's card: "Sworn threshold-guardian of the Hidden Moto Village". The Moto Bloodline page: "The Moto hold one seat and it is Kharven. The six non-Kōkan lines are landless." Card against wiki.
10. **Where Yukazuri lives.** Her card: "A cabin in the ruined Moto ancestral ground". The Moto Origin and Moto Bloodline pages put that ground past the western ridgeline; the Long Reading puts it on the white shelf in the hinterland above the Holy Sea's coast (WORLD §7, open). The Lore uses the card's words and places the cabin nowhere further.
11. **Struck forms on three cards.** Ayame's card carries the struck Polynesian title and kin terms where the Yuno page renders the same house in its Japonic terms (R23-1, live). Yoko's card names her husband and his line by the struck Mongolian-register name for Sodoku. Sodoku's card names the corridor brother by a name struck on 2026-09-12. None of these forms is written in any Lore.

## 1 · Who is here

**Lord Maerion Lysandre · Keeper of the Seven Treatises.** Called the Lion of the Concord and the Roar of the Red Court. Concord High Magistrate, battle-scholar and Wellspring strategist of the Arbitration Division's "Concord High Court", "One of the most respected political and military minds in the Concord"; "he serves as both a judge in high-profile disputes and a field commander in times of realm-spanning war." He has mastered the Seven Treatises of Concord Strategy. His orders in battle steady allies against fear and coercion, and he can bind a spoken law into a blow so that a foe must obey it for a moment. His Domain is "an infinite courtroom ringed by banners of every Concord realm", in which breaking a law brings immediate backlash; the law binds him there too, and "A law must be spoken to bind." He commands and does not personally fight. His creed: "A sword cuts the body. A law cuts the will. A just hand must know when to wield both." His card lists appearances in *The Red Court Trials*, *War of the Silver Accords* and *The Lion's Last Verdict*.

**Riven Eltamyr Vonnel · The Glaive.** Arbiter Exemplar of the Neutral Chambers, Guild Accord Arbitration Division, Neutral Chamber Subsect, carrying "Code 9-L authority, empowered to deliver singular execution rulings when Concord balance demands it." Also the Parasoul Executioner and the Glaive of Quiet Rebuke, "He Who Cuts Mercy Into Petals": "a feared enforcer, a respected diplomat's tool, and a mistrusted adjudicator whose mercy is structured, not sentimental." He fights with a lacquered parasol-glaive in heeled sabatons engraved with rosettes. Lies fracture into petals only he can see; his opened parasol releases spores that compel truthful speech; his footfalls narrow a duel toward "a single permitted conclusion"; he stores testimony in petals and replays it in neutral sancta. His Execution Clause, Final Petal, is one severance "chosen before the strike", and it costs him "lung strain, moral backlash, potential self-collapse if the verdict is unjust." His card makes him "Lurien-born, raised in the Holy See of Lurien", a see the index has struck (section 7). "When the final petal falls, the law is all that remains."

**Atheron Dreymarch · Prophet of the Unblinking.** Called the Chains in the Dream and the Feathered Sentinel. Dream-Warden, Wellspring Custodian and Arbiter of Omen-Law; Neutral Chambers Keeper in the Arbitration Division, and Moon-Oath Binder "within the Forgotten Court of Noxthallow, a half-mythic tribunal governing disputes in the dream-realms." The chains draped on him are oaths sworn in his presence; "Breaking such an oath summons Atheron in dreams, where he may imprison the oathbreaker's will in a geas or strip it entirely." The embroidered eyes on his cloak are bound spirits that perceive guilt and lies. He wears the Blindfold of the Third Moon for good; removed, it would show him every dream in his Wellspring's reach at once. "He cannot fight. He can only judge." His reach ends at those who have sworn. "I am the weight on your eyelids. The voice in your verdict-dream. Swear carefully."

**Leontes Praevan · Magistrate of Altherion.** The Ink-Warden, Voice of the Quiet Laws. High Magistrate of Altherion, Chief Arbiter of the Altherion Central Concord Tribunal, Patron Magistrum of the Neutral Codex Guild. "Formerly Concord Auditor, an ordinary Guild Accord audit post", he resigned after what he privately calls the Fall, an institutional failure he watched "which he has never dated for anyone", and "then built a career on the proposition that written law is the only restraint left." He works Runecraft: glyphs he inscribes into ordinary text bind whoever reads them, "and acknowledgement is *reading*, not consent." His Binding Letter goes by an ordinary courier. Nothing he writes can be recalled, and an error "misfires continuously, for as long as the carving holds, on everyone who passes it, until somebody finds it and breaks it." He remembers every word ever written in his court, and people in his presence adjust their posture "as if standing trial." "Every threat Leontes has ever posed arrived by post." His card's line ends "only the silence of those who feared to put it into ink."

**Orivane Caelthys · Astral Shepherd.** The Ram of the Outer Sky, the Ecliptic Voice. Astral Magus, Keeper of the Luminarch Wellspring and Celestial Navigator of the Concord Celestial Cartography Office, which "Oversees the mapping and stabilisation of Astral Wellsprings, ensuring safe passage through the Outer World's drifting realms." He "Acts as a neutral party in political disputes when navigation rights are contested." He reads the sky by what it blocks, carries the Ecliptic Staff, and can call astral entities "from benign star-spirits to dangerous void leviathans" and guide or dismiss them. "He forecasts. He does not prevent." His cost is false transit: "A navigator who charts a gate that is not there sends people through it." "The stars are not above us. They are behind us, within us, and ahead of us."

**Hazzen Skollgrin · The Crooked Grin.** The Laughing Latch, the Chain-Eater; unofficially "The Smile They Send When They Want Screams." Torture-Reclaimer and Aberrant Containment Hunter of the Black Concord, "Enforcement Division, Severance Protocol Cell 3C", "Assigned to capture or liquify unanchored spiritual entities, rogue Domains, or traitorous Accord operatives." His Wellspring "was once a containment prism used to hold him. He reversed it." What he decided inside it is his card's open question. His skeleton is rebuilt from broken executioner bones; "Every time he 'dies,' his pain resets into laughter." Glyph-brand stitches hold in thirteen laughing entities he devoured on Black Concord raids: "He does not control them. He just likes the noise." Every binding shows to him as a chain he can grab, snap and eat, and "He cannot re-bind anything." "Ever notice how the bones creak louder after a scream?"

**Lucien Draeve · Emerald Alchemist.** The Gilded Toxin, the Smiling Arsenist. Master Alchemist, poison-savant and covert Concord operative of the "Black Concord, Toxin Arts Division", "Formerly Research & Archives Division for medicinal alchemy", "shifted to assassination and sabotage after several 'unfortunate accidents' removed his political rivals." Whether those accidents were his work or his Domain's is his card's open question. His touch turns a harmless brew toxic, or back; he exudes a faint green vapour that dulls the reflexes of anyone near him long enough; his gold-inlaid containers keep a mixture stable until use. In his Domain "every airborne particle becomes his instrument", to heal, kill or alter. "He makes the poison. Somebody else usually administers it." Sealed lungs, vacuum or hard wind leave him "a man with jars." "Every sip is a choice. Every choice is a death. I simply make sure the two arrive together."

**Varek Ka'ruun · The Blood-Eater.** The Horned Feastlord, the Laughing Pyre. Outer World chieftain of the Braqth Dominion's Flame-Horn Tribe and "Leader of the Crimson Hunt, a warband that roams between Wellsprings and burns them into feasting grounds"; he "Holds pacts with three Infernal Courts". His red tattoos are scar-glyphs "etched during coming-of-age duels"; they record his kills and feed him the longer he stays in melee. His hollow horns vent flame when he gorges on heart's blood in battle, and his battle-laugh makes allies fearless for three breaths before their bodies crash. "He must be in melee, and he must be feeding." Nothing on his card tells him when to stop. "Eat with me. Fight with me. Burn with me."

**Thalyss Aevyrn · The Deep Coil.** The Tideborne Leviathan, Keeper of the Abyssal Gate. Wellspring Sovereign of the Abyss and Herald of Leviathan's Pact, of the Kingdom of Vellsorea ("the New World, the Eastern Rise"), bound to the Abyssal Court under the Oath of the Third Depth: "Territorial warden and executioner for the Leviathan Pact, ensuring no landwalker trespasses into abyssal Wellsprings uninvited." His Wellspring holds "a sleeping fragment of the original Leviathan", and he signed with it. Living pact-marks bind him "to both the Vellsorean royal line and the Leviathan's will"; breaking the pact "would cause his body to dissolve into the trench currents forever." He shifts between human and draconic-serpentine form, at once in water and slowly ashore. "The sea is not cruel. It is law. It swallows only those who cannot hold their breath."

## 3 · The shared history

Three events, all new. Each sits before the present every card describes. None assigns an era, since no card states one; they are ordered against one another and against events the cards do state. Where a card leaves a question open, the event leaves it open.

### The duel court in Braqth

- **When.** After Riven had taken Hazzen as his last-argument enforcer under the Petal Writ, and after Varek's coming-of-age duels had cut the carvings he still wears. Before the blood-and-salt feast.
- **Where.** Braqth, in the Outer World (WORLD §3; Braqth ruled a real place on 2026-09-12, the Southern Pan). The Southern Pan page gives the Braqthian confederations their standing-stone work in columnar basalt.
- **Who.** Riven Eltamyr Vonnel, Varek Ka'ruun, Hazzen Skollgrin.
- **What happened.** The Crimson Hunt burned a Wellspring in Braqth into a feasting ground, and the confederation that drew on it brought the loss before the Guild Accord. Arbitration sent Riven Eltamyr Vonnel. Varek Ka'ruun would not answer a written ruling. He called for duel court under the coming-of-age law his carvings had been cut by, and Riven accepted. They fought inside a ring of basalt posts. Hazzen Skollgrin stood at the edge of it under the Petal Writ, there to carry out the ruling if the Blood-Eater refused it. Riven kept out of Varek's reach. His footwork narrowed the exchange toward one ending, and he closed it with a single cut he had chosen before he stepped in. Varek lost. He said he had been honoured by the artistry of his own defeat, and the Hunt left that Wellspring to the confederation. Hazzen had nothing to enforce. That night Varek drank with him and, in the middle of a toast, tried to bite his neck. Hazzen laughed. Varek has said since that he laughs right.
- **What each took.**
  - Riven: the one duel his card records, won; the foot strain his footwork costs; an enforcer who stood at the ring and was not loosed.
  - Varek: the loss, which he calls an honour; one Wellspring fewer for the Hunt; a man to drink with after battles.
  - Hazzen: a drinking companion, and a bite at his neck mid-toast.

### The blood-and-salt feast

- **When.** After the duel court, since Varek and Hazzen already drank together after battles; after Thalyss had sworn the Oath of the Third Depth.
- **Where.** The New World's eastern seaboard, in the waters of the Kingdom of Vellsorea (WORLD §3; Vellsorea ruled the New World, the Eastern Rise, on 2026-09-12). The Eastern Rise page: "An oceanic plate is going down beneath it along the whole seaboard", and "The Wellsprings here move."
- **Who.** Thalyss Aevyrn, Varek Ka'ruun, Hazzen Skollgrin.
- **What happened.** A sea-bound Wellspring beast followed a moving current up out of the deep water and onto the shelf off Vellsorea. The shelf had no room for the Leviathan's form. In human shape Thalyss Aevyrn could turn the water only a short way around him. The Oath of the Third Depth lets no landwalker into an abyssal Wellspring uninvited, and he invited one. Varek Ka'ruun came. He fought the beast at close quarters in the shallows, where the carvings on his skin fed him more the longer he stayed, while Thalyss held the water heavy around it. It died on the shelf. They feasted on the shore on its blood and on salt, and Varek drank from its heart. Hazzen Skollgrin had come with him, as he came after battles. At the tide-line Thalyss took him under, to see, he said, if it was funny. Hazzen came up alive. He had swallowed the air in the slain fish. Thalyss has tried again since, and Hazzen has lived each time.
- **What each took.**
  - Thalyss: a landwalker let in by invitation, the one door the Oath leaves open, and the respect of one apex predator for another that both cards record.
  - Varek: a feast, the beast's heart's blood, and that same respect.
  - Hazzen: the first of the drownings he has survived, and the way he survived it.

### The hearing at Altherion

- **When.** After Leontes Praevan had resigned the Concord audit post and taken the chief seat of the Altherion Central Concord Tribunal. The Fall that came before the resignation stays undated and undescribed, as his card rules. After Atheron's oath-chains had come to hold without his tending.
- **Where.** Altherion, the republic on the Ring's rim (WORLD §3 and §5; Leontes' card: Inner World, northern rim), in the Altherion Central Concord Tribunal.
- **Who.** Leontes Praevan, Lord Maerion Lysandre, Riven Eltamyr Vonnel, Atheron Dreymarch, Orivane Caelthys.
- **What happened.** Two carrying houses contested a route through the Outer World's drifting realms, and Orivane Caelthys sat between them as the neutral party. He charted the lanes, and they divided them. The settlement was written and signed. The master of one house also swore to keep it in the presence of Atheron Dreymarch, and a chain for that oath hung on Atheron's body from that day. A season later the master sent a ship down the other house's lane. The chain summoned Atheron into his sleep, and Atheron laid a geas on his will. The house never used that lane again. The other house held its charter in Altherion and sued there for the season's carriage it had lost. Lord Maerion Lysandre argued for the house. Riven Eltamyr Vonnel argued for the master: the geas was a sentence, and no man could be sentenced twice for one breach. Maerion answered that a ruling given in a dream paid no carrier, and that dream-law was too abstract for waking politics. Atheron came to the tribunal blindfolded and said the chain still held. Leontes Praevan asked for the written settlement. He read it, ruled on it and on nothing else, and gave the house its carriage. The master went home liable awake and bound asleep. On the tribunal steps Orivane told Atheron that chains can become snares to the self.
- **What each took.**
  - Maerion: a win over Riven in a Concord trial, one of those his card counts, and his view of Atheron's rulings set down in a court that forgets nothing written.
  - Riven: a loss to Maerion, argued for an ally's sentence.
  - Leontes: the written record of both arguments, which he does not forget, and a judgment resting on a signed page.
  - Atheron: a chain that held in the master's sleep whatever the waking court decided, and a warning.
  - Orivane: his lanes upheld in ink, and the warning his card records, given as advice.

## 6 · Same person?

No. The nine cards describe nine people who name one another as separate persons in separate offices: Riven and Atheron as allies who divide the waking and the sleeping law, Maerion and Riven as courtroom rivals, Varek and Thalyss as two predators on one hunt. The one alias in the index, `scenes/CAST.md`'s "Lord Maerion Lysandre (a.k.a. Lord Lucen Vale)", joins Maerion to a separate card: Lord Lucen Vale · The Star Chamberlain is a Protocol Corps officer of the Star Chamber, "Human, Accord-born Harmonian", and names no Maerion. The alias is an artefact of the generated index. Elder Seravain (named by Riven, Atheron and Lucien) and Seravain Drelith · Wyrm-Crowned are separate cards outside this cluster; this record does not join them.

## 7 · Where the cards disagree

Quoted and left open.

1. **Orivane's card against itself.** "Respects his dream-law work but warns him that chains can become snares to the self." (`Orivane Caelthys · Astral Shepherd.md`, VIII) and, of Orivane, Atheron and Emilia Veirynthiel, "None of the three sheets knows the other two exist." (same, IV).

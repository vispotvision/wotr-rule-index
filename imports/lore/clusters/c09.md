# c09 · The Zettari war camp and the Mahuo who came to it

The Zettari line and the people bound to it: Dougou Ozumu Zettari, the Iron Bison, dead and taken into the Paths; his son Rengai; Rashani of the Origin Eye; Yorime Seikai, the woman who stayed beside Dougou, and Seikai Hana-no-Kurai, a scion of the same Ashuric line who does not know she is one. Against and beside them, three of the Mahuo: Cozbi, the harvester whose grievance is the Zettari and the Moto; Kwon Mu-jin, the Teacher, embedded in the Zettari war camp; and his sister Ara Min, the Healer, whom the Sovereign of the Zettari asked to marry.

## 1 · Who is here

**Dougou Ozumu Zettari · The Iron Bison.** "Founder of the True Iron Bison", Zettari-blooded with a demonic strain, an "Atlazon-Seeded Vessel". His card gives him as dead: "Deceased. Ascended into the Paths", and the bloodline acknowledged him as sovereign only after death. He was born from a corrupted Zettari branch touched by demonic strain and by Atlazon's pressure-law, and his card says origin and Catalyst were one event: "He was made from the conditions that required one". His body was sealed by design. Feeling and Essence both turned into stored weight, so that sensors read him as dormant, and "That mistake killed people". He never crossed into a true Domain; the seal that kept him nearly unkillable kept him one step short. Emotional breach was his lowest resistance. His voice was quiet, severe and patient, anger that accumulated. He wore no armour and carried nothing. The only person his card names is his son, Rengai, "the son who took the warning and built something that lets people in instead". His card does not name Yorime.

**Rengai Zettari · The Red Ember.** "Dougou's direct son", in his twenties by the card, active under Zettari observation and "developing ash combat separate from Dougou's brutality". He works soot, cinder and char: a field that dries the air, stings the eyes and tightens the lungs of anyone who stays in it, a reading of cracks and seams, heat pressed into a flaw until it gives. One eye is ember-red and the other dark brown. He carries "Dougou's Ember Bead", an "inherited warning marker" that burns red when his field overloads. His card gives him Dougou's shadow as a fracture still accumulating, and it names the wound plainly: "He is Dougou's son". His voice is dry, sardonic, rarely loud, and he lets silence work. The archive gives him more than the card does: a wife, Lyria, twelve years married; a son, Asante, six at their parting; a house in a village near Vaeloris; and a hunt for Cozbi that ended with Cozbi carrying him off the field.

**Rashani Zettari · He Who Finds the First Cause.** Eighteen, of the Zettari bloodline and its "Origin-Aspect Strain", "under Zettari observation and incomplete sovereign training". His Origin Eye "Shows starting conditions", and his perception runs ahead of his body: he sees the beginning of a blow he cannot always answer in time. His flaw is Authority Hunger, the temptation to treat every flaw as his to correct, and his card holds a fracture called the Authority Question as accumulating. He is calm and exact, with no wasted words. His card files Rengai beside him as a "Sibling-Parallel". In his one scene he walks behind his brother, Ma'Kovu, through a corridor his brother opened, toward Mizuyi and "the faint living warmth Rashani had spent seven years chasing back to himself".

**Yorime Seikai · The Gilded Veil.** "Pureblood Seikai / Ashuric Bloodline", of the "Seikai Boundary Remnant, Current Era", and "Dougou Ozumu Zettari's significant other". She was born to the Boundary custodians. Their blood was shaped for Claim, the taking of Essence, above all stolen Essence, and the return of silence to the wound. Her refinement began the day she learned not to take merely because she could. She "learned the difference between Claim and witness". She can lay a gloved hand on a man whose field is crushing everything near him and draw off pressure before it becomes detonation. She uses that art on Dougou. She will not Claim his core: "She will not make him safer by consuming him". Her card records four events: the first time she touched him, the Boundary Funeral at Ash-Hollow, the Promise Under Black Bells and the Night of the Unfinished Strike. Her arc, by the card, is "deciding how long witness remains mercy before it becomes permission".

**Seikai Hana-no-Kurai · The Ashen Paragon.** A frontier protector, an "Awakening Seikai Scion", "Reluctant Bearer of a Right of Claim she does not know she carries". She "believes she is human, and the Boundary has not yet told her otherwise". Nobody may explain her to her face: "Gloss rights · Never". She is unaffiliated. Every protection contract she held along the frontier has ended, and nothing has replaced them. Her power arrived through grief, on the morning it had already cost her the thing it was meant to protect, and she has called it "operational" ever since. Her firstborn was "Taken through a transaction", an event in no official record because she made sure of that. At Halvren's Pass her Grief Surge triggered mid-battle, and two soldiers never recovered their Wellspring connection. "She paid what she could until she couldn't". Her hands carry the echo of everyone she has held in death. Her child is the loudest. She keeps the Ashen Wrap, carried since before that child was born, and the light that bleeds from her hands dims when she holds it. By her card, distance is the only defence anyone has against her, hers included.

**Cozbi Mahuo · The Ledger-Keeper.** "Harvester of the Moto Line", of the Mahuo line, a man who carries many inhabiting souls under one sovereign identity; his name is a First Compact name, never his birth name, and his age is "Unclear by design". He is "opposed by every venerable bloodline still standing". His Catalyst was watching his people ground into the mud beneath the war of the Zettari and the Moto, and a promise to his dying brother: "make them remember what we were, even if they never learn our names". The Ledger is a living account of every conscripted soul, and his true body sits on a stone pedestal three planes from any vessel he fights through. His son, the Paragon of the Archon of Form, is a child he bound as a spiritual axis, "deliberately unconsenting". Dhaerin sealed him and died of it. His voice is unhurried and warm-sounding even when he describes atrocity. He crouches to examine, and he cares for his stuttering constructs in a way his card calls uncomfortable.

**Kwon Mu-jin · The Teacher.** Archmagus of the Division of Alchemetrica, twenty-four, of the "Mahuo court line, scholar-nobility", and "Self-bound to the Dawn Guard's teaching vow, embedded with the Zettari war camp". At fourteen he showed the court's Senior Alchemist a corrected array, because the glyph chain was wrong and would have killed the patient. The court answered by making him the Ledger-Prince, a symbol paraded as proof of the kingdom's brilliance. He left. It was the day his title-writ graded him before it named him, and he has spent the ten years since proving the title was earned. He wants alchemical and summoning knowledge made common inheritance, out of the keeping of courts and bloodlines. He cannot let an error stand. Under stress he takes off his spectacles, goes still and names the problem aloud. His sister Ara is "absolute" on his card; his estranged mentor Doryun is heavy and unresolved; the Dawn Guard is "growing, mutual".

**Ara Min Mahuo · The Healer.** Twenty-one, of the Mahuo court line, "sister to Kwon Mu-jin", formerly of the royal court's healing wards, now travelling with her brother's teaching circuit and "weighing an offer of marriage into the Zettari Sovereign's household". For three years she treated the poorest ward of the capital's healing houses for nothing. She kept no count. The families came to her door, and the court noticed only when it tried to charge her a licensing fee for patients she had never billed. Her card calls that "The Ledger Nobody Kept". For the dying she cannot save, she holds the Black Gate open gently. She speaks plainly and asks the harder question on principle, and under stress she goes quiet and works with her hands. A black-and-white cat goes everywhere with her. The Sovereign is on her card as "prospective, undecided", "entirely her own to define".

## 3 · The shared history

Five events. The Night of the Unfinished Strike is on Yorime's card, and this record adds one witness to it. After Halvren's Pass is new. The last three are in the archive and the in-world documents; the archive puts the aperture night before the clearing, and gives no order between those two and the Accord night.

### The Night of the Unfinished Strike

- **When.** While Dougou lived, in the years Yorime spent beside him, and in Rengai's boyhood "under his father's brutal ceiling" (Rengai, XVI).
- **Where.** Kushara (WORLD §5, Elsewhere), on ground no card names. The Zettari observation that Rengai and Rashani both stand under belongs to the Sovereign House seated there.
- **Who.** Dougou Ozumu Zettari, Yorime Seikai, Rengai Zettari.
- **What happened.** The Iron Bison state ruptured. The pressure came off Dougou in the way his card says it always had, as weight, and everything in reach began to give under it. Yorime walked into it and stood in front of him. She said the sentence her card keeps: "If you kill me, let it be because you chose to". Dougou stopped. Rengai was there, a boy at the edge of the flattened ground, and he saw his father halted by one sentence from a woman who had not raised a hand. Dougou never spoke of that night.
- **What each took.**
  - Dougou: a promise still unreleased. He had broken the one he made under the black bells many times, and Yorime had never let him out of it.
  - Yorime: the proof her art rests on, that the hand which stays can hold a catastrophe without consuming it. She went on refusing to Claim his core.
  - Rengai: the warning his father's card says Dougou taught him. He built a discipline of ash, which works slowly and lets a field be read before it is spent. His voice stayed low. He carried Dougou's Ember Bead when it came to him, a marker that burns red before the overload.

### After Halvren's Pass

- **When.** The Current Era, after the battle at Halvren's Pass, while Hana-no-Kurai still held protection contracts on the frontier.
- **Where.** Halvren's Pass. Hana's card names the pass and says only "along the frontier"; this record sets it in the Frontier Marches of Ketsuen (WORLD §5, North), the contested western border whose settlers chose the frontier to avoid being governed.
- **Who.** Yorime Seikai, Seikai Hana-no-Kurai.
- **What happened.** Yorime came along the pass after the fighting. Her card sends her over ground like that, the Boundary Funeral at Ash-Hollow among her records, where the Essence of dead soldiers had been trafficked. Two of the wounded at the pass had lost their Wellspring connection and would never have it back. The small black bells of her rosary rang near the frontier protector's hands. Yorime followed what had been taken from the two soldiers, faintly, and found that it ended in the woman who had fought beside them. The woman did not know. Yorime judged it before she did anything, as her card says she always judges first. Nobody had chosen a theft, and there was nothing to declare. She opened no court. She took nothing from Hana, and she did not tell her what the pass had shown.
- **What each took.**
  - Yorime: the knowledge that a scion of her own line walks the frontier unrecognised, taking without knowing she takes. She kept it.
  - Hana-no-Kurai: nothing she knows of. The debt to the two soldiers stayed hers, and she paid it until she couldn't.

### The night the aperture was cut

- **When.** The night Ma'Kovu cut the aperture behind Mizuyi's threshold. The archive places it immediately before Cozbi loosed the dragon on the Inner World.
- **Where.** A ruined castle, with a courtyard, a reliquary, and a corridor grooved by "Azure's decades of paranoia". The scene names no country.
- **Who.** Cozbi Mahuo, Rashani Zettari.
- **What happened.** For years Cozbi had watched a woman through an opening behind her threshold, and that night he felt it close: Ma'Kovu had traced the watching home through his brother's Origin Eye. Cozbi said so to the empty air and filed the correction. Then he pressed Matter and its contradiction together over an armour of Zettari make. The floor under the worktable stopped existing, the windows of the ruined wing broke inward, and the armour held. He carried it into the dark and bade the brothers "Fight well". Three strides past the threshold, Rashani felt the working as a beginning with no middle and no end his Eye could reach. He named it to himself as a depth he was not permitted to read, filed it unsolved, and walked on to the door.
- **What each took.**
  - Cozbi: the loss of the watching and the lesson in it: "I already had what I needed from the watching". He took an armour tested against his own unwriting, and a new respect for the Zettari as readers of instruments.
  - Rashani: the one kind of event he distrusts above all, a cause with no beginning he can see, filed and carried. And the door, which he opened.

### The clearing

- **When.** After the dragon was loosed on the Inner World; Rengai and Dhaerin had walked for months to reach it.
- **Where.** A clearing reached from Vaeloris in Ketsuen (WORLD §5, North), through three mountain passes and a stretch of coast. The scenes do not name it.
- **Who.** Rengai Zettari, Cozbi Mahuo; Dougou Ozumu Zettari by name.
- **What happened.** Rengai went to his son first, told Asante to be strong and Lyria that he would come back, and took the flask of pressed ash from its shelf. A message from the Vaeloris warden turned him back for information before he set out. At the clearing he asked Cozbi why he had bled a two-year-old. Cozbi answered with the Mahou ground into the mud between two armies and told him he had not chosen Dougou's wars. Rengai did not argue that part. He fought. He burned Cozbi's great spider from beneath, met the split body's clone at the point where its strike began, and was struck down by the Titan effigy Cozbi called up. Dhaerin sealed Cozbi with a rite that cost his life. Cozbi, bleeding into the grass, told his construct to collect the fallen Zettari, and it carried Rengai away.
- **What each took.**
  - Rengai: captivity in a place nobody could find. His house was told by the warden himself, and his son passed in time into the Sovereign's keeping.
  - Cozbi: the seal his card logs as "every claimed soul lost in one instant", and his own verdict on the night: "I allowed myself to become too confident".
  - Dougou: nothing, being where he was. His name was the measure Cozbi held his son against.

### The Accord night

- **When.** The night after the Sovereign's inquiry "into the nature of Legion Summoning and the crimes of Cozbi Mahuo", by the Accord's preamble. The letter to Ara is dated the same night.
- **Where.** A Zettari war camp, unnamed. The documents give it woodsmoke and cold canvas, and a lesson to frightened children every morning.
- **Who.** Kwon Mu-jin; Ara Min Mahuo, who was not there; Cozbi Mahuo, who was judged there in his absence.
- **What happened.** Dawn Guard came. He was Sovereign of the Zettari, and Mu-jin had met him only that day. He held his inquiry into Cozbi's art and Cozbi's crimes. Afterward he asked Mu-jin to name someone of the Mahuo worth trusting, and to draft the conditions of an alliance under his seal. Mu-jin drafted them by lamplight. The Accord condemns Cozbi and refuses his grief as an excuse. It proposes a marriage between the Sovereign and Ara Min and binds that marriage to her consent, given to the Sovereign directly, with no brother or elder in the room. It makes Mu-jin the line's representative until her standing is settled, and it obliges each house to name the other's failures aloud. Before morning he wrote to his sister and put the choice in her hands.
- **What each took.**
  - Kwon Mu-jin: the office of speaking for the Mahuo before the Zettari and any court that asks, and the duty to name the Zettari's failures in whatever room the Sovereign stands in.
  - Ara Min: an offer she could refuse without breaking the alliance. Her card still has her weighing it. The archive later has her betrothed to the Sovereign, and grieving him.
  - Cozbi: a condemnation written by his own line, with his Catalyst refused as a defence.

The basis "scene" for the three Mahuo ties with Cozbi rests on the two in-world documents and on Cozbi's scenes. Untied within the cluster, by design: Hana-no-Kurai with everyone but Yorime; Rashani with Dougou, Yorime and the two siblings; Mu-jin and Ara with Dougou and Yorime; Mu-jin with Rengai.

## 5 · Outward

**Dougou Ozumu Zettari:** a voice of the Paths' judgment, the sovereign archive his card makes the last word on his life: Aegor's avatar, or Revari Zettari, whom the Paths page names the current inheritor of the True Everlasting Eyes.

**Rengai Zettari:** the student who still keeps his formula. Niran Yukari, whose card files Rengai as "Mentor-Foundational" and carries his Ash-Signal Pouch.

**Rashani Zettari:** the Peer-Witness his card leaves unassigned, someone who fights at the beginning of things.

**Yorime Seikai:** an executor of her own Boundary Remnant who Claims without judging first, the predatory kind her card sets her against.

**Seikai Hana-no-Kurai:** one of the two soldiers from Halvren's Pass, or their kin, to whom she paid what she could until she couldn't.

**Cozbi Mahuo:** the Mahuo who took the same wound and reached the opposite conclusion. Aurelian Prudentius, the Primate, born Mahuo Do-hyeon of Sum-gol, whose card says of the two of them "They have met once".

**Kwon Mu-jin:** the court that made him a symbol. Its face is Senior Alchemist Doryun, his estranged mentor and his foil on the card.

**Ara Min Mahuo:** Dawn Guard, Zuberi Zettari, Sovereign of Kushara, her betrothed in the archive. He has no card.

## 6 · Same person?

No. The eight cards are eight people and the archive keeps them apart. Yorime and Hana-no-Kurai share a line and nothing else: Yorime is a pureblood of the Remnant who knows her Claim and refuses it, and Hana believes she is human. Cozbi's name is a First Compact name and his vessels change faces, but no card or scene gives one of his vessels to another member. Rashani's brother in the archive is Ma'Kovu; his card's "Sibling-Parallel" names Rengai, and Rengai's card names Dougou alone.

## 7 · Where the cards disagree

Quoted and left open. The first five are card against card; the rest set a card against a scene or a wiki page.

1. **Dougou dead, and alive.** Dougou: "Deceased. Ascended into the Paths" (`Dougou Ozumu Zettari.md`, Identity). Yorime's card writes him living: "Dougou believes she deserves better than him." and "If Dougou finally becomes the catastrophe he fears he already is, Yorime may be the only person capable of standing before him without hatred" (`Yorime Seikai.md`, VI). Of the last night on her card: "He has never spoken of that night".
2. **Fire or seal.** Rengai: "His power is ash because his father is fire" (`Rengai Zettari.md`, XI). Dougou's card gives him no fire: his currents are sealing, mass, deep earth and breath, and "Essence and emotion alike convert to stored body-weight rather than radiating outward" (Dougou, Traits).
3. **Ties named from one side.** Yorime's card calls her "Dougou Ozumu Zettari's significant other"; Dougou's card names only Rengai and "Aegor Zettari / The Paths". Rashani's card names Rengai ("Sibling-Parallel"); Rengai's card does not name Rashani. Neither Seikai card names the other.
4. **Kwon and Mahuo.** Brother and sister carry different family names: "Kwon Mu-jin" of the "Mahuo court line" and "Ara Min Mahuo", "sister to Kwon Mu-jin". The naming inventory leaves open "whether Kwon is a Mahuo cadet-branch surname or an unrelated clan sharing the register", and which syllable is generational in "Ara/Min, Mu/Jin" (R37-1, open on the docket).
5. **The Dawn Guard, an order or a man.** Mu-jin's card: "Self-bound to the Dawn Guard's teaching vow" and "Dawn Guard (Sovereign, alliance-bound)". Ara's card: "Dawn Guard (prospective, undecided)". The Accord and the Eight Houses page make Dawn Guard the Sovereign himself, Zuberi Zettari.
6. **Card against scene: when Cozbi watched.** Cozbi's card: "Watching his own people ground into the mud beneath the Zettari and Moto's endless war" (`Cozbi Mahuo.md`, I). Cozbi to Rengai: "I wasn't alive when the Mahou were ground into the mud, Rengai" (`Cozbi_Rengai_Dhaerin_Confrontation.md`). `Naiser_Death_Cozbi_Genocide_Niran_Fall.md` sides with the card: "Cozbi had learned that lesson before he learned to walk without falling, watching his own people".
7. **Card against scene: Cozbi's age.** Card: "Unclear by design" and "decades of harvesting have outrun any simple count" (Cozbi, I). Scene: "He had run out of surprise sometime during the Second Epoch" and "the same unhurried patience that had outlasted three dynasties" (`Cozbi_Root_Severance_and_Rashani_Threshold.md`).
8. **Card against scene: Cozbi living or killed.** Card: "Stage XIV Zenith" and, in Temperance, "XIV Zenith · Active" (Cozbi). The Primate's card: "Cozbi Mahuo (living, Level 500, Stage XIV)". Emira in `the_true_king_of_the_north_part_17.md`: "it's been relatively quiet since Cozbi was killed".
9. **Card against scene: Rengai's years.** Card: "Age ~20s" (Rengai, I). Scene: "She had learned, over twelve years of marriage" and "Asante was small for six" (`Rengai_Farewell_and_Dhaerin.md`).
10. **Card against scene: Rengai's standing.** Card: "Active under Zettari observation" (Rengai, I). Scenes: "Collect the fallen Zettari. We're leaving" (`Cozbi_Sealed_Retreat.md`); "Rengai has been captured" (`Niran_Mira_Awakening.md`); "Asante lost his mother. His father. Vaeloris" (`the_war_in_the_north_ii_the_road_two_days_south.md`).
11. **Card against scene: Rengai's Iron Bison.** Card: he "chose ash" and "Not fire. The thing fire leaves behind" (Rengai, XV); his techniques name no Iron Bison. Scene: "Iron Bison." and "He said the name the way his father said it" (`Cozbi_vs_Rengai_Dhaerin_Combat.md`); "Your Iron Bison is weak next to your father's" (`Cozbi_Antithesis_Split_Fight.md`). The same fight gives Dougou an ash-sense Dougou's card lacks: "some old Cinerion instinct reading the air pressure ahead of the strike the same way his father's had for forty years before him".
12. **Card against scene: Mu-jin's years.** Card: "Age 24" and "the ten years since" he left the court at fourteen (Mu-jin, I); `WOTR_Vaeloris_Sequence.md` and `the_true_king_of_the_north_part_20.md` agree on twenty-four. `10_mujin_the_name_of_your_killer.md`: "had spent forty years being unable to name" and "the calm man who had run a school for eleven years".
13. **Card against scene: where the siblings come from.** Mu-jin's card: "Eastern Concord-adjacent enclave" (Mu-jin, I). The Vaeloris cast list: "Of the house at Sum-gol". Sum-gol lies in Ketsuen, in the Inner World (the Ketsuen page).
14. **Card against scene: when Sum-gol ended.** The Primate's card: "He was nine when Sum-gol finished emptying" and "Age: 58" (`Aurelian Prudentius Custos Clausorum · The Primate.md`). Mu-jin of Ara, twenty-one on her card: "She was nine when Sum-gol went" (`WOTR_Vaeloris_Sequence.md`). Mu-jin still keeps a school at Sum-gol in `10_mujin_the_name_of_your_killer.md` and the passage beside it in the Kujo arc.
15. **Spelling of the line.** The cards spell "Mahuo". The Accord's title, its text ("of the Mahou line") and Cozbi's speech in `Cozbi_Rengai_Dhaerin_Confrontation.md` ("The Mahou were livestock") spell "Mahou".

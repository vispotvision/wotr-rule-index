# c10 · House Venrik and the Eressean crown

The family that holds, or held, the Grove-Spired throne of Eresse, and the people its cards reach: Karo Venrik, the exiled prince of the Echoes; Asta Venrik, his wife, the Skyfang Queen; Sandalphon Aestraen, consort of Karo's mother and the Crown's exiled regent; Vaelen Mourcrix, the Imperial Inquisitor his card makes Karo's kinsman; Ilyria Draven Morningstar, Karo's daughter, who works for the Accord in the dark; Aelor Vaelith and Eryndrel Vaelune, the Aerial Ward's champion and its ranger; and Raivhar Drakonis, the Vanguard fighter who stood beside Karo at Epprenea and was declared rogue after Kyrenshade.

Canon order holds throughout: Isaac's direction, then the card, then the archived scenes, then the wiki, then this record. Isaac ruled on 2026-09-12 that Karo Venrik is the son of Hiromi Mahuo and Saeloria, Queen of Eresse, and that Sandalphon Aestraen is Saeloria's consort and is not Karo's father. That ruling governs everything below. The older card text that calls Sandalphon Karo's father is quoted in section 7 and nowhere relied on.

**Sources read.** The eight cards in full, from the Volume I character cards in the wiki. ## 1 · Who is here

**Karo Venrik · The Foolish Magus.** A High Elf of the Eressean line, called Echo-Binder of the Ma Sigil and "He Who Forgets Nothing and Is Believed by No One." By Isaac's ruling he is the son of Hiromi Mahuo and Saeloria, Queen of Eresse. His card stands him in the Latticefront Collective, Concord-overseen, as founder of the Remembrance Division, and "Exiled from the Grove-Spired throne for founding it on unlicensed Anamnesis research." His Catalyst Event: "He surrendered a succession for a discipline the Crown calls a parlour trick." His Crystal is fractured along the succession and is still fracturing. His signature, Echo-Sovereign, puts a piece of himself somewhere he is not; each Echo must consent to be recalled. He has fielded Echoes for eleven years and recalled all but two. He keeps their names on a list and checks it. Twice his reading has erased the record it was reading, and both times the record was in a person. He will take almost any insult without a sign and has ended two working relationships over the one he will not take, that the Division is a parlour trick. His first glyph-whisper came in a dream at nine, and he chose his Path under Maelorian tutelage. He explains at length and is not believed. His Open Rulings ask for the Latticefront Collective, the Remembrance Division, Riftfall and 512 VE to be confirmed. His card names nobody: no wife, no daughter, no kinsman, no comrade.

**Asta Venrik · Wyvern Marshal Emeritus.** Eressean Highborn, Skybound Elfkin: the Skyfang Queen of Eresse, the Talon Bride, Crown of the Open Sky, "Karo's Tomboy Queen." She served the Grand Principality of Eresse as commander of the Eressean Skyfang Militia and stands in Royal House Venrik. She began as the least suitable princess candidate in Eresse and was chosen as queen over the conservative houses' lobbying for a gentler bride. She rose through stubborn practice and many falls. Her Welling and Ascension came in the cliff wars, when she kept mounting with broken ribs, and her Glory came the night she held a burning sky corridor open so civilian gliders could pass while her wyvern bled under her. Three minor Wellsprings along the Eressean mountain chains answer her and the Skyfang flights, and she will bind no more until the existing sky routes are secured. Pride makes her slow to retreat. Her card reads her floor as misjudging when a line is already lost. She refuses to hide her scars. Her card reads the discipline in her as "the discipline Karo hammered in."

**Sandalphon Aestraen · The Reverent.** A High Elf of Eresse called the Reverent Disquiet, Quiet-Hand, Echo of the Ninefold Pulse and Half-Sung. He was Crown-Consort and Regent of Eresse, "pre-Ascension," and he is Saeloria's consort. He is exiled under the Pact of Iridescent Mourning, travels on a Pilgrim-Writ, is barred from officiating in the Neutral Chambers and may enter archives only under escort. His Catalyst Event came when terrain began keeping what he sang, and his card says of it, "For a man under a Pact of self-erasure, that was the precise opposite of what he was trying to achieve." Teal ribbons in his braid each bind an Echo. Sever one and memory bleeds, and nobody knows how many ribbons there were at the start. He cancels the law of a Domain and has no defence when his own is cancelled. Massed liturgy overloads him. His presence mis-tunes wards and shows any state he would rather hide.

**Vaelen Mourcrix · The Exculpate Arbiter.** A High Elf of Eresse, Bearer of the Verdict of Absolve and Imperial Inquisitor of Eresse. He stands in Sancta Lux, in the Judicariate Circle of Eresse. His house is Mourcrix, descended from House Venrik, "Which makes Karo Venrik kin, and the Division's exile a family matter as well as a doctrinal one." His Open Rulings ask for that descent to be confirmed. His Catalyst Event: "He rendered a verdict the Circle overturned, and the ground where it was rendered kept it anyway." He has never resolved whether he serves the Continuum or the institution. He reads what is missing from a soul. Naming it is the working, and what he lifts off a subject he carries until it can be discharged. He can weigh and cannot rule, so a verdict of his needs the Circle to enforce it. The innocent walk through his verdict untouched; he has met three. "Where Ignatius concludes, Vaelen weighs."

**Ilyria Draven Morningstar · Monochrome.** Called Monochrome, the Knight-Slayer, the Beak and the Pale Adjudicator: a half-vampire contract slayer of armoured combatants. She is unaffiliated and was formerly attached to the Guild Accord's Special Division under deniable authorisation. She is Karo Venrik's daughter. Her mother's people are the Draven Morningstar bloodline, whose archive the Accord sealed three generations before her birth, and she wants to know what they were before the classification. Her Catalyst Event came when she let a room see that the elven calm and the vampiric hunger were one instrument. A target recognised her father's signature in her technique and used his name, and she did not correct him. She tells an armoured target exactly what she is about to do to him. At her waist she wears the Blue Sash of Karo Venrik, which steadies her through Resonance Inversion, and she has declined every offer of replacement gear. She wears gloves and does not shake hands after an engagement. One open contract has run six weeks under observation with no action taken.

**Aelor Vaelith · The Imperial Pegasus.** Sky-Marshal of the Aerial Ward and Imperial Pegasus, "a singular honour of Eresse"; the Crown's champion and treaty-lancer, who opens skies, breaks sieges from above and carries imperial writs between realms when no gate is safe. He serves the Grand Principality of Eresse under the Guild Accord's Arbitration Division for air-oath enforcement. He rides Mirenai, a Skyglass Pegasus born from the Velthaeir cloud-archives, who shares his breath-count and heading. Mount and rider move as one long curve. He carries the lance Skylark, the shield Ur-Seal, the Cloak of Four Phases and the Signet of the First Concord. His duties are to escort imperial envoys, enforce Concord Overflight, interdict Rot spore clouds and present the Moon-writ where air-oaths are at stake. He steps on ridges of air. Heavy ash or Rot miasma roughens them, and his footing slips unless he scrubs a lane first. His card calls him close to unbeatable where the enemy is known and the treaty clear, and says that anywhere ambiguous "Eresse has made a mistake." He carries the house name of Serenyra Vaelith, the Archmagus who refused the crown. No card says whether they are kin.

**Eryndrel Vaelune · The Trinebow.** Eresse's line-ranger and treaty marksman, in the Aerial Ward of Eresse. He "Often rides with the Imperial Pegasus and the Winged Aegis on air-oath operations." He has quiet silver hair. His bow is the three-limbed Trinebow Skystitch, whose nearly silent hum steadies nearby hands. One pull sends three shafts that split in flight. His Threadline hooks a route, so that allies stepping through the mark move quieter and truer, and a touch-shot of his makes a banner hang heavy until its bearer speaks the cause plainly. He "Hates killing shots; prefers tendon, rope, hinge" and "will spend position to keep it that way." He cuts lanes for couriers, pegs riot doors and gives Vireline and Lucen five silent heartbeats when verdicts wobble. Dust storms and drum-Domains spoil his holds. His hooks, False Fletch, the Banner of the Quiet Lie and the Fourth Limb, are unplayed.

**Raivhar Drakonis · The Ashen Horn.** Harbinger of the Voidflame: Domain Gladiator, Flame-Tainted Exorcist, Wellspring Warbringer. He served in the Guild Accord's Enforcement Division, in a Vanguard Order, and was declared rogue after the Kyrenshade Massacre. His horns are fused conduits that draw straight from Wellsprings he has anchored in. Long channelling chars, crystallises and collapses the ground around him. His right arm is grafted with Voidflame armour. His Devouring Pyre takes the Traits of the foes he kills, whole, and he must kill first. A Voice whispers to him in the flame's pulse, and nobody knows whether it is a Wellspring Echo or a Trait he took, still talking. He once spared Ziyu "Pip" Inari's life on a Concord raid, and Seraphion Veydrak means to reclaim or destroy him. "Every breath I take is borrowed from the fire that should have ended me."

## 3 · The shared history

Four events. None is in the archive. The Siege of Epprenea is named on Raivhar's card, and the Concord Military page records Karo's act without a place; everything else is new. Where a card states a refusal, a cost or a hook, the event uses the refusal and the cost and leaves the hook unplayed. Sandalphon is in none of them. His card and Isaac's ruling bind him to the house by marriage, and no card puts him in a room with the others; this record does not either.

### The Burning Corridor

- **When.** While Asta commanded the Eressean Skyfang Militia, after the cliff wars in which her card places her Welling and Ascension. It is the night of her Catalyst Event. No card gives a year.
- **Where.** A pass in the Eressean mountain chains, in the Grand Principality of Eresse on the elven old-ground of the New World (WORLD §3 and §5, East). Asta's card puts the three minor Wellsprings that answer her and the Skyfang flights along those chains.
- **Who.** Asta Venrik, Eryndrel Vaelune, Aelor Vaelith.
- **What happened.** The forest under a pass burned in the night, and no card says what lit it. The people of the valley went out by glider along the one corridor of air over the pass, and the fire's heat filled it with smoke and broken lift. Asta took the Skyfang flights into the corridor and held it open, shaping the air so the gliders could follow in her wake. She flew at its head. Eryndrel of the Aerial Ward hooked the route at the corridor's near end with his Threadline, and every glider that passed his mark flew quieter and truer. He read the air and called the corridor closing, aloud, for every rider on the line. Asta refused to leave it. Her wyvern bled under her. Aelor could not find footing on ridges that thick with ash, so he scrubbed a clean lane at the far mouth and held it, and the gliders came out through it onto clear air. The last glider passed. The corridor closed behind Asta, as Eryndrel had read it would, and she came out through the closing.
- **What each took.**
  - Asta: her Glory, and a wyvern that bled under her from one end of the corridor to the other.
  - Eryndrel: a call given aloud and refused, on a read that was right about the air. The corridor closed when he said it would, after the gliders were through.
  - Aelor: the far mouth, held on a lane he scrubbed himself, and the pairing Eryndrel's card records. Eryndrel calls his dive, and he calls Eryndrel's wind.

### The Siege of Epprenea

- **When.** Before the Kyrenshade Massacre, while Raivhar served in the Enforcement Division's Vanguard Order. No card gives a year. None places the siege before or after Karo's exile.
- **Where.** Epprenea, in the Outer World: "Outer World, the Southern Pan," ruled 2026-09-12 (Raivhar, epigraph note; WORLD §3). Vaelthyr Iscarn's card seats the Epprenean court in "frost-sheathed fjord realms" and puts a Wellspring under the ice labyrinths there. This event speaks only of the Wellspring under the siege ground.
- **Who.** Karo Venrik, Raivhar Drakonis.
- **What happened.** The Concord's side of the siege put a Vanguard Order into its line. Raivhar anchored his horns in the Wellspring under the siege ground and fought from it, and the ice around him charred and crystallised. The Wellspring began to fail while he drew on it. The record does not say whether his draw began the failure, or the siege, or both together. Karo read the Wellspring through the conduit Raivhar's horns made into it and found where it was failing. Then he turned his own Essence into harmonic resonance and held it. The Wellspring held, and Raivhar's anchoring held with it. They fought out the siege on the same side.
- **What each took.**
  - Karo: his name on the Wall of Resonance beneath the Concord Citadel, where the Concord Military page records the act and where only the High Concordant may walk. The page gives no place; this record puts the Wellspring at Epprenea.
  - Raivhar: an anchoring that did not collapse under him, and the feel of Karo's read passing down through his own horns. He knew it again later, at Dorrosfenn.

### The Weighing at Vaelmarr

- **When.** The hearing that ended in Karo's exile from the Grove-Spired throne. Karo's Catalyst Event came at it, and so did Vaelen's. Karo's card makes Echo-Sovereign the shape his fracture took and counts eleven years of fielded Echoes, so the weighing lies at least that far behind his present.
- **Where.** Vaelmarr, seat of the Sunroot Court (WORLD §3, the New World; the Elven Peoples page), in a council chamber built partly below grade, as Eressean chambers are (`desktop/inventories/eresse.md`).
- **Who.** Karo Venrik, Vaelen Mourcrix.
- **What happened.** The Division's unlicensed Anamnesis research came before the Judicariate Circle of Eresse, inside its window, with its witnesses named and paid. Vaelen, Imperial Inquisitor of Eresse, weighed it. He read the Division's work by what was missing from it and rendered a verdict in its favour. The Circle overturned him on the licence the research had never had. The chamber floor where he rendered the verdict kept it anyway. The Circle's finding went to the Crown, and the Crown put the choice to Karo: the Division or the succession. Karo kept the Division. He surrendered the succession and was exiled from the Grove-Spired throne. His Crystal fractured along the succession, and it has not stopped.
- **What each took.**
  - Karo: exile, and the losses his card counts, "The Crown, the succession, and a name that opened doors." He kept the Division. He will not hear it called a parlour trick.
  - Vaelen: his Catalyst Event. The ground kept his verdict and the institution overturned it, and his card says he has never resolved which of the two he serves. His card calls the exile a family matter as well as a doctrinal one; the kinship is on his Open Rulings, and this event rests on his office alone. The Mnemonis page later has him supervise, reluctantly, Karo's attempt at a Wellspring shift.

### The Contract at Dorrosfenn

- **When.** After the Kyrenshade Massacre and Raivhar's declaration as rogue, while Ilyria was attached to the Accord's Special Division. It is the engagement at which her Catalyst Event came.
- **Where.** Dorrosfenn, in the Outer World (WORLD §3 and §5, South).
- **Who.** Ilyria Draven Morningstar, Raivhar Drakonis.
- **What happened.** The Special Division put a deniable contract on the rogue. It went to Ilyria. She told him what she was about to do to his coherence, as she tells every armoured target. She threaded Coherence Collapse into the node under his fire, and for the beats it buys his Aether failed. In those beats he knew the read. He had felt it at Epprenea, running down through his own horns, and he spoke the name: Karo Venrik. She did not correct him. She let him see the elven calm and the vampiric hunger working as one instrument. His Voidic Shell turned the thread back on her, and the Voidflame's pattern slotted into her lattice, as her card says a clean read invites when a target turns the exchange around. She broke contact. The blue sash at her waist held her through the recovery. Raivhar left the field alive and still rogue. No card says what the Division entered on the contract, and this record does not.
- **What each took.**
  - Ilyria: her Glory; a Resonance Inversion worked out of her lattice with her father's sash at her waist; and her father's name, spoken by a man who had fought beside him, left uncorrected.
  - Raivhar: his life and his fire, and the name of the man whose read he felt, which he spoke aloud to the man's daughter.

## 6 · Same person?

No. The eight cards are eight people, and the family cards name one another as separate people in one house. Three near-collisions are recorded so that nobody merges them. Aelor Vaelith is a different being from Aelor, the broken Void power of Kujo's arc, whose name fills his `scenes/CAST.md` entry. Ilyria's "Draven" is her mother's bloodline name. Draven Kael Vorrick, the Beast Slayer (c13), is a separate person whom no card connects to her. Aelor Vaelith and Serenyra Vaelith share a house name, and no card says they are kin.

## 7 · Where the cards disagree

Quoted and left open, except where Isaac has ruled; where he has, the ruling governs and the card's words are recorded as they stand.

1. **Karo's father.** Asta's card: "Migrated so far: Sandalphon Aestraen (Karo's father), Karo Venrik, Asta Venrik (Karo's wife), and Vaelen Mourcrix" (Asta, head note). Raivhar's card: "Karo Venrik's connections are: his father Sandalphon" (Raivhar, VIII). Sandalphon's card: "Karo Venrik is not his son: Isaac ruled on 2026-09-12 that Karo Venrik is the son of Hiromi Mahuo and Saeloria, the Elven Queen." Isaac's ruling governs (`RULINGS.md`, 2026-09-12). The two cards are unamended.
2. **Sandalphon's card against itself.** In one flag: "Karo Venrik is not his son" and "Sandalphon, his son Karo, and their kinsman Vaelen" (Sandalphon, lineage flag).
3. **Who holds the Grove-Spired throne.** Karo: "Exiled from the Grove-Spired throne" (Karo, I); "He surrendered a succession" (Karo, I, Catalyst Event); "What it costs him · The Crown, the succession, and a name that opened doors." (Karo, IX). Asta: "Skyfang Queen of Eresse" (Asta, I) and "As queen she still smells faintly of wyvern oil" (Asta, Profile). Serenyra: "The Venrik line holds the Grove-Spired throne in current canon; her standing with Karo and Asta Venrik is reconciled: the refusal was a handover, and the Venrik line holds with her assent (Isaac, 2026-09-13)." (Serenyra, IX). None of the events above says who wore the crown when.
4. **A High Elf with a Mahuo father.** Karo: "Race · High Elf, Eressean line." (Karo, I). Isaac's ruling makes Hiromi Mahuo his father, and Hiromi's card, which never mentions Karo, gives "Lineage: Mahuo (Korean stratum). Son of Kwon Mu-jin and Frithia." and states no race (`Hiromi Mahuo.md`). A naming register is not ethnicity (LAW §3), and the name settles nothing either way.

The wiki gives him an "Animatria suite" and "Nexus networks" (`The Spirit Summoning Arts.md`, Key Figures), where his card gives him Echo-Sovereign alone.
6. **Karo's Shell.** Karo: "Aether Shell · Unclassed (Class emerges at Glory)." (Karo, II). The Latch page: "Karo's Aether Shell is Class II Harmonic" (`wiki/Artifacts/The Latch of Twinned Souls · Soulbound Echo Focus.md`, Operation line).

One open contract has run six weeks under observation with no action taken" (Ilyria, IX). The Guild Accord pages list five Divisions and the Black Concord (WORLD §4). They list no Special Division.
8. **The Venrik and Mourcrix line, asked about three times.** Vaelen's Identity states the descent, and his Open Rulings ask "confirm intended."

# c11 · The elven old-ground

Florwyn's regent and the wyrm grown for him, the made thing that stands as his structural rival, Elyndra's herald, the winter spirit Florwyn made and lost, and three who stand outside Eresse's court: the half-orc who commands an Elven Army, the queen of the Tsohanto, and the shield of the Northroot clans. The record runs from Valthrum's First Eon to the year and more since Drevath's first activation. Every life in it stands on the elven old-ground of the New World, or on the Tsohanto Reach at its seam, and no line of it states a year. Canon order holds throughout: Isaac's direction, then the card, then the archived scenes, then the wiki, then this record.

## 1 Who is here

**Vaelorian Theryn Aerethiel.** He is called the Last Elven Lord, Paragon of Elyndra and Florwyn's Regent, and also the Black Canopy and the Thorn-Crowned Sovereign. He was High Marshal of the Verdant Phalanx and is the last Regent of the Court of the Verdant Throne, and the Accord's lexicons enter him as a mobile Draconic Root Arm of Florwyn. His card says that what Florwyn recognised in him was persistence. Every engagement of the Great Spirit War he survived in his present shape tightened one figure, elf as bark, armour as scale, Domain as body, until the Continuum stopped treating it as a figure. His armour is part of him now. Stripped, it grows back from beneath, and it thickens where it has been struck before. The Great Spirit Tree feeds him through anchor points along his back and shoulders whenever he stands on living ground, and his card calls the Tree his conduit and, increasingly, his co-author. He carries the Verdant Regent, once a court blade and now a length of fossilised dragon-root with a crossguard warped into hooked branches. Under stress his silhouette lengthens and a second, deeper tone rides under his voice. He holds his strength below what his card allows him, and the card reads that restraint as the gap between the lord he still is and the dragon he has not decided to finish becoming. The Phalanx serve beside a war regent none of them entirely recognise, and none of them has said so to his face.

**Thalyndros Aerenvael.** The Canopy Wyrm, the Crownborne. His card makes him a purpose-bred sky-branch of Florwyn's Great Dragon design, grown from the Great Spirit Tree's higher boughs and anchored through Vaelorian's Soul Crystal as its rider-knot: a single test flight of a pattern that may one day emerge whole. He is Florwyn's design and Eresse's sky-arm, bonded to Vaelorian alone and sent out rarely, to break sieges from directions no army expects, to escort envoys, and to carry Vaelorian into theatres where a Domain has to come down from the sky. When Vaelorian mounts, branches of bone and Essence grow from Thalyndros's shoulders and latch into his armour. The two do not always agree and never misunderstand each other: Thalyndros sends weather-sized feeling and slow conclusions, and Vaelorian sends back sharp vectors. The treeline leans toward the routes he flies. Struck hard, he sheds whole branches of armour that sprout twisted saplings afterward where he fought. His breath scours a line and greens it. It needs living ground. Thalyndros and Caelum Radix are scholars' labels, and his card says he does not answer to them. He carries no forged relic. He has met Juna, an older dragon of flesh and wing. Their first meeting was a measuring of age and purpose, and neither submitted.

**Drevath Mourne-Kal.** The Iron Horizon, the Pale Conqueror, the Father's Proof. His myth name is Nakkul, which he speaks as a release call and never translates. He is Winter Eladrin, of an Eressean daughter-people his card says was reshaped by an Archonic Principle of Stillness and split away in a northern schism it calls the Harmonial Recession. Senzu Tyurkia built him in the Veil Sanctum as proof of concept and installed a Tyurkian Cognitive Framework over the native architecture. The Stillness is still underneath, and it has learned to march. He called Senzu father once, at first activation, and has not repeated it in over a year. He files himself under no one. The Accord has no statute he has broken and keeps a file on him that only waits. Every night he performs the Rite of Stillborn Flame. If he misses it past two days the Stillness overtakes the engine: he slows, stops advancing, and begins to preserve instead of take, and his card says he fears that more than he fears Vaelorian. Senzu can countermand him at the level of his architecture, and he has not yet cut that out of himself. He carries Mourne, the always-cold blade Senzu forged, the one gift he has ever named without contempt, and two stabilising vials for deep Verdantia ground that he does not admit to needing. He holds four taken battlefields in contested fringe country, each declared permanent. The air around him runs colder than the air elsewhere, and he has stopped trying to turn it off. His card names Vaelorian his primary opposition, calls it structural, and records the offer he has made him: step aside, and live to watch it.

**Saetsha Bloomveil.** The Pink Tempest, Herald of the Vanished Bough, Last Bell of Elyndra, She Who Keeps Vigil for a Silent Archon. An Eressean elf transfigured by contact with an Archon, she was Elyndra's handmaiden and herald. She now works on her own account. She is loosely aligned with the Guild Accord and overrules any Concord where renewal and extinction hang in the balance. In every account she has, Elyndra went silent and every Domain of Renewal went dark with her. No Guild or Accord record corroborates it past her own testimony. The day she first declared an Edict in Elyndra's absent name, the world leaned toward the outcome with no Archon left to co-sign it. Elyndra's mark still brands her. Dying groves and wounded ground are drawn to her for triage whether she seeks them or not. She hears faint resonance where Elyndra's Domain once touched the world. She carries the Glaive of the Silent Archon and the Elyndric Heart-Rosette, a brooch holding the central shard of Elyndra's severed Glyph Chain, which keeps the last echo from drifting away. Every great working draws that echo down and leaves it quieter. She will not draw on Mortalis in any circumstance, holding that stealing mortality's lesson to fuel healing is the wound Elyndra suffered. Two incidents stand for her appearances. In the White Orchard Silence every grove dedicated to Elyndra went still, and she alone walked among them and revived one beacon tree in each region. At the Withering Siege of Veyran Crossings she turned a Withering legion's corrupted biomass into a wall of vines that still holds the pass.

**Valthrum.** The Cryon King, the Lord of Stilled Hearts: a Titan-wrought World Spirit, an anomaly of the Winter Court, Keeper of the Zero Point. Florwyn made him, and he has been estranged from her since the War of the Long Night, when he refused to end the season she made him to protect. He began in the First Eon as a whisper of cold wind. Sorrow and the freezing of great battlefields raised him, and he bound himself wholly to an eternal winter. He removes heat from the world, and the removal is the working. He carries Winter's Verdict, a greatsword cut from a single diamond, and the Lantern of Lost Seasons, which holds the last heat of a previous summer. What he entombs in true ice stays whole and cannot act, and his card does not know whether anything he has held for centuries is still the person who went in. The Winter Court licenses the frost-caves where Glacium grows, at a professional remove from him. He is neutral toward every other power of the age. Every mortal kingdom within a mile of him feels the temperature fall and a sadness it cannot account for. He lies dormant in the Northern Waste. He waits there for what he calls the Signal of Entropy, and when he speaks of what he is, he speaks at length and unprompted, and it reads like doctrine.

**Vargrath Ul-Dorr.** Iron Dawn; Ul-Dorr, He of the Broken Mountain; Drak-Thren, the Grief-Forged Fang; Grand Arbiter of the Undreamed Fate; He Who Holds Mountains in His Sleep. He is half orc and half elf, the son of a Garrath-Ul Ironblood father and an Elshaen dream-mother, born in the pit at Tol-Varath. His mother was murdered. His father burned alive to force the Garrath-Tul, a shard of the Titan Valmir, and a wyrm-scale relic into his son's Soul Crystal, and died of it. The draconic scarring across Vargrath's frame is the relic's mark. Exiled, he found thirty-eight dying elves in a gorge and would not leave them, and they became the seed of the army he commands, the Elven Army of the Ashen Vale Compact. Kaetra's dream-law fused into him afterward, uninvited, and since then he has seen an empire of mountain-citadels that does not yet exist and cannot fully explain wanting it. His army holds together on his gravity more than his strategy, and both sides of that arrangement know it. His worst strikes need real anger or grief to ignite. Each one cracks his Crystal further. He means the Realms to acknowledge their debts, starting with the Siege of Four Suns. Nobody close enough to tell him how much more his Crystal can take has been willing to.

**Onawa Ashkewe.** Queen of the Tsohanto, She Who Wears the Old Faces, the Ash-Crowned. Her people are a hidden forest-and-barrow branch of the elves that grew apart from Eresse and from the Varrisak courts, and the Reach is independent, close to the Veil, and without Accord standing. Tsohanto queens stop counting years at their coronation, and she looks to be in her late twenties. At sixteen she went into the barrow-ground alone, as every heir does, to be tested by the mask her mother wore before her: a test of whether the wearer can carry someone else's grief without losing her own name underneath it. She came out three days later wearing the mask on the crown of her head, which no candidate had done before or has since. She has not explained why, and no one has asked her twice. At seventeen she nearly lost her own voice in communion. She held the mask through her mother's funeral without speaking a single borrowed word, and took the crown with the mask worn crownwise by her own choice. Lowered, the First Ash Mask lets one ancestor speak and act through her, and each communion costs her a day of silence. A raven, unnamed by design, rides her shoulder, and killing it strikes back into her own skull. For a mile around the Long Barrow the ground holds the stillness of a place already grieved for, and the Tsohanto dead notice a threat to their queen before she does. Her card sets her wound plainly: she wears grief as a crown because she fears that wearing it over her face would make her forget which griefs were hers. She solves everyone's grief but her own. Her mother's death was the first grief she carried, and she has not finished carrying it. The archive keeps her off its pages: its Onawa is Empress of Eresse, and whether they are one woman is Isaac's open ruling (section 7).

**Brynja Haldrís.** Hammer of the Northroot, the Runebound Shield-Mother, called Stonebraid. She is a shield-warrior and forge-keeper, Eladrin of the Northroot Stone Clans, in the Withering Era, and she fights for preservation and never for conquest, for a hearth and not a crown. She gained what she has while standing between a siege and a child. She carries the twin ancestral hammers Skor and Vethra and speaks of them as other warriors speak of kin. Under her plate she wears the fur-lined Runebraid Mantle, etched with clan geometry, and at her throat the Oathband Necklace, which holds molten stone carried from the first Northroot forge. Every rune on her hammers is a vow sworn in iron and blood, and she will not explain one aloud, because a vow explained stops being a vow. Her plates harden where they are struck again, and the bone under them breaks before the plate gives. She was built to be the thing that does not move. Village, child or column, she makes no distinction among the things she has stood in front of. Other people count how many have stood behind her shield in an ordinary season. She does not. Her card leaves one thing unknown: what happens to the vow inside her if she is ever forced to break the oath instead of dying to keep it.

## 3 The shared history

### The audit at the taken field

**When.** New. Inside the year and more since Drevath's first activation, which his card counts from the one time he called Senzu father, and while Vaelorian holds the regency his card gives him. No year.

**Where.** The elven old-ground (WORLD.md §3), on the contested fringe where Drevath's card sets his four anchors: one of the battlefields he had already taken.

**Who.** Drevath Mourne-Kal, Vaelorian Theryn Aerethiel, Thalyndros Aerenvael.

**What happened.** Drevath had taken the field and declared it permanent. He waited on it with the two vials he carries into deep Verdantia ground, and when the dragon came into sight he drove Mourne into the ground and spoke the word. Inside the boundary nothing grew and nothing healed. Thalyndros brought Vaelorian in from the sky, which is the work his card says he is sent for, and came down short of the boundary. His tail-roots and wing-tips went into ground outside the line, and he did not cross it. Vaelorian walked in alone, onto ground where his armour would not grow back. Drevath put the audit to him as a thing already run: step aside, and live to watch it. He gave him the courtesy of reading it before it was enforced. Vaelorian read it and did not step aside. He walked back out across the line to the dragon, and no blow was struck. The audit has not been enforced.

**What each took.** Drevath took the refusal his audit had already forecast, from the one man whose kind of Domain his card names as the counter to his standard. Vaelorian took an offer he had not asked for, from someone who had priced him before he arrived. Thalyndros took a field where his breath would have found nothing to green, and a line he stood outside while the other half of his bond walked in.

### The holding he did not take

**When.** New. Inside the same year and more of Drevath's activation, in the Withering Era Brynja's card gives her.

**Where.** The Northroot holdings, in the Eladrin north of the elven old-ground. The wiki sets the Eladrin in Iampu (WORLD.md §3), and no page places the Northroot holdings.

**Who.** Drevath Mourne-Kal, Brynja Haldrís.

**What happened.** Drevath came to the edge of the Northroot holdings looking for ground to take. Brynja stood in front of the nearest holding with Skor and Vethra, as she stands in front of anything that comes for her people. Drevath read her at the reach of his structural read: her reserves, her stamina, the fracture points under her plate. The read gave him a defence whose stamina climbs the longer it holds. He did not drive Mourne into that ground. He turned away and took his field elsewhere, and the holding behind her was not entered. No blow was exchanged.

**What each took.** Brynja took a holding that stood without a blow struck, and no way to know whether it stood because of her or because of a calculation she never saw. She did not count it. Drevath took the one field his audit priced too high, and a close sight of what his own native architecture does when nothing is built over it: an Eladrin standing still in front of her people, preserving, which is the thing his card says he fears more than Vaelorian.

### The winter after the gorge

**When.** New. After Vargrath's exile from Tol-Varath and his refusal in the gorge, and before Kaetra's dream-law fused into him, which his card places later. The Withering Era, which both cards give.

**Where.** A gorge below the Northroot holdings, in the Eladrin north of the elven old-ground, and the clans' forge-halls above it. No card places the gorge or Tol-Varath.

**Who.** Vargrath Ul-Dorr, Brynja Haldrís.

**What happened.** Vargrath had already refused to leave the thirty-eight when Brynja came down into the gorge. His card sets the day his Glory bloomed as the day he chose the gorge, and that day was his alone. She found an exile with draconic scars across his frame keeping thirty-eight dying elves alive in a cut of rock with the winter coming on. She stood in front of them as she stands in front of anything. The clans' forge-halls took them in for the winter. Vargrath came with them. When he left in the spring, the thirty-eight went with him. The clans did not follow. They fight for preservation and never for conquest, and they sent no one to the army the thirty-eight became. The cards do not say who the thirty-eight were or what brought them to the gorge, and this record does not either.

**What each took.** Vargrath took a debt he owes, in a life that runs on debts owed to him: a winter's shelter from people who will never ask him to collect on it and will not march for his empire. Brynja took thirty-eight people she stood in front of for one winter, who went on to become an army she will not serve, and the sight of a stranger who had done without any vow what her vows make her do.

### The grove at the barrow's edge

**When.** New and undated. Inside Onawa's reign as her card gives it, after her mother's funeral, and after Elyndra fell silent in Saetsha's account, since the mark that drew Saetsha there is Elyndra's. It is dated against nothing in the Korvaeth record.

**Where.** The Tsohanto Reach (WORLD.md §3): a grove on grave-adjacent ground inside the mile of the Long Barrow Court.

**Who.** Saetsha Bloomveil, Onawa Ashkewe.

**What happened.** A grove at the barrow's edge was dying of blight, and Elyndra's mark drew Saetsha to it, as it draws her to dying ground whether she seeks it or not. She came into ground that held the stillness of a place already grieved for. The grove stood on Onawa's ground, and Onawa was there before her. In Onawa's presence the blight moved faster toward one of its two ends, clean healing or clean death. Saetsha declared an Edict of Growth in Elyndra's absent name. One tree took the Edict and lived. The rest of the grove finished dying cleanly, and Onawa let it. Neither asked the other to yield, and neither did.

**What each took.** Saetsha took one living tree and a grove she could not keep, finished by a queen who carries that ending as mercy, which is the half of Elyndra's own edict Saetsha will not draw on. Onawa took a stranger's grief for an Archon who has gone silent and carried it past her own cost, as her card says she carries such things. She added no bead to her mourning cord for it. The cord holds resolved griefs.

### The Long Night and the first Northroot forge

**When.** The War of the Long Night, which Valthrum's card names and does not date, long before Brynja's life. The northern setting and the forge are new.

**Where.** Iampu and the Eladrin north of the elven old-ground (WORLD.md §3), where the Winter Court now licenses the frost-caves Valthrum's card ties to him.

**Who.** Valthrum, and Brynja Haldrís through the relic she carries.

**What happened.** Florwyn made Valthrum to protect a season, and in the War of the Long Night he refused to end it (his card). The winter he kept lay over the north. The Northroot clans did not leave it. They cut the first Northroot forge into the stone and kept it burning through the Long Night, and the clans' hearths have been lit from that forge since. The card records nothing of who fought whom in the war. No page ties the Long Night to the Ice-Voice Conflict, and this record does not either.

**What each took.** Valthrum took his estrangement from the Titan who made him, which his card dates from that war, and a winter he would not end. The clans took their first forge. Brynja, long after, wears molten stone from it in the Oathband Necklace, and the vow-structure inside her Crystal is named for that necklace. She has never met Valthrum. She carries at her throat the heat her clans kept against his winter.

## 5 Outward

- **Vaelorian Theryn Aerethiel** needs whoever cut the Branch of Rikudoku from the Great Spirit Tree, his conduit; a living piece of it walks the world in Kairen Moto's hand, and c05 points back at him.
- **Thalyndros Aerenvael** needs Juna, the older dragon his card says he measured himself against, or any dragon of the natural lines who can smell the architect on him.
- **Drevath Mourne-Kal** needs the Accord officer who holds the file on him that only waits, or, if Isaac gives him a card, Senzu Tyurkia, who can still countermand him.
- **Saetsha Bloomveil** needs a member of the Circle of Blooming Ash, Elyndra's followers on the Archons' page, who does not hear the silence she hears.
- **Valthrum** needs the Winter Court officer who licenses the Glacium frost-caves at a remove from him; the Korvaeth record names Queen Seralyth and her envoy Loreth Ann.
- **Vargrath Ul-Dorr** needs an Ironblood elder whose scars carry the Siege of Four Suns, the first of the debts he means the Realms to acknowledge.
- **Onawa Ashkewe** needs a sovereign to stand as her peer-witness, the Axis her card leaves unassigned and latent; she has no Accord standing, so the witness has to be another crown.
- **Brynja Haldrís** needs someone with the standing to ask her to break her oath: a Northroot clan elder, or whichever Seasonal Court of Iampu holds the Crown that season.

## 6 Same person?

No two of these eight cards are one person.

The Onawa collision is a card against the archive, and it is Isaac's open ruling (State of Play, Ilthára Korvaeth; the Ledger, L027; the Tsohanto Reach front, 0/4). The Korvaeth record's Onawa is "Onawa, Empress of Eresse and her lifelong friend since the orchard at fourteen." The card's Onawa Ashkewe is Queen of the Tsohanto and "Unaffiliated with Eresse or the Varrisak courts". The open question is whether they are two women, or one woman with the Tsohanto card rewritten. This record settles neither. Every line of it about Onawa Ashkewe stands on her card as written. The grove at the barrow's edge is dated against nothing in the Korvaeth record, and it falls with the card if Isaac rules them one woman.

## 7 Where the cards disagree

1. **Arboreum Primordis.** Vaelorian's card: "Chthonica · Materia. (replacing the invented "Arboreum Primordis")". Thalyndros's card keeps it throughout: "drawing too hard on Arboreum Primordis through Vaelorian", "Network · Arboreum Primordis splinters", and his shed armour is "Infused by Arboreum Primordis".
2. **Vaelorian's Domain.** Thalyndros's card: "His stabilizing where Vaelorian's own Domain is still only a proto-core is exactly what a lesser, test-flight Aspect is for." Vaelorian's card: "Throne-of-Everfall; his Domain, currently expressed as a darker, more vertical forest", and his Catalyst reads "Full World Spirit."
3. **Whether Eresse stands.** Thalyndros's card makes him "Eresse's sky-arm", deployed "to escort envoys" and landing on diplomats' "sky-docks". Vaelorian's card: "a crown with no kingdom left to crown". Drevath: "I am not here to mourn what Eresse was." RULINGS.md (2026-09-12) makes the Korvaeth arc Eresse's present, with the polity standing under a regency.
4. **Tempestaris, replaced twice.** Vaelorian's card: "Cymorath · Fulguria. (replacing the unattested "Tempestaris")". Thalyndros's card: "Sublimare · Vectoria." and "Replacing "Tempestaris," which is not among the sixty". The two bonded cards replace one struck name with two different Wellsprings.
5. **The Great Spirit War.** Vaelorian's card: "Every Great Spirit War engagement he has survived in his current shape has tightened the same metaphor", and he is "recorded in Accord lexicons". `The High Empire of Eresse.md`: "First Eon, Great Spirit War Era". WORLD.md §1 already carries the war as having no place on the count. Read together, a living rival of the Accord's present fought in a First Eon war, and his card does not say he spans eras.
6. **Paragon of Elyndra.** Vaelorian's card calls him "Paragon of Elyndra". Saetsha's: "Elyndra went silent, and every Domain of Renewal fell dark with her, in every account Saetsha has." The Fractured Dawn's Paragon of Elyndra is "A Verdant Covenant elder (unattested) crystallising into something eternal and immobile", and that card says each Paragon "still needs its own full character card".
7. **Thalyndros's name.** His card: "Gloss rights · Never." and "Draconic and Old Parunic are scholarly labels for what he is, not a name he answers to being called", and he is "Not a natural dragon but a purpose-bred sky-branch". `scenes/the_inhale_register.md` gives him as a dragon's own three-part name, "Three parts: the sound at hatching, the deed, the holding." and "Thalyndros the Root-Wreathed Sky.", and says the form is "Built on the attested forms already in the Bestiary", where no Bestiary page carries it.
8. **The Empire of Bluvest.** Drevath's card: "What nobody knows. Why the Empire of Bluvest let him leave." Adalric Vladimer Valen's card carries the same sentence word for word. Vaerath Ashveil's card calls that empire "struck; card gives no such empire in current canon".
9. **Drevath's schism and his Stillness.** His card: "diverged from the Eressean line during a northern schism the card calls the Harmonial Recession" and "reshaped by the Archonic Principle of Stillness". `The Elven Peoples.md` has the Eladrin carry "Order-as-Devotion into the harshest northern theater" of the Great Spirit War and names no schism. None of the Fourteen Archons holds stillness: Wyther holds "Flux, freedom from stasis", and stasis sits with the Titan Vohrin.
10. **The Onawa collision.** Her card: "Unaffiliated with Eresse or the Varrisak courts". `scenes/01_the_vacancy_korvaeth_arc.md`: "Onawa, Empress of Eresse" and "no Onawa Ashkewe." The State of Play: "The Onawa collision: two women, or one and the Tsohanto card gets rewritten." WORLD.md §3 lists "Onawa Ashkewe (SP-K)" among struck names, while the State of Play strikes her from the Korvaeth arc only: "are all struck from this arc and must not be propagated." `scenes/00_SUPERSEDED_wrong_names.md` kills her at Greysalt, and that pass is struck.
11. **The Tsohanto Reach's age.** The Eastern Rise page puts the Reach on the arc. It lists it on "The young volcanic ground the Guild Accord chartered from nothing within living memory", and rules that "The Accord's doctrine that the New World was chartered from nothing, with no older law to displace, is true of the arc and of the arc alone." Onawa's card gives the Reach "a hidden forest-and-barrow culture", a mask "her mother wore before her", and "Every prior wearer of the mask". `The Elven Peoples.md` counts five branches, "Five Theologies of a Single Song", and the Tsohanto are not among them.
12. **Eladrin and fire.** `The Elven Peoples.md` says of the Eladrin that they "cannot wield fire or passion-aligned glyphs." Brynja's card makes her a "Forge-Keeper" whose Fixatio is "The Binding Flame", and Drevath resolves himself nightly "through the Rite of Stillborn Flame".
13. **The Glacium caves.** Valthrum's card: "The Winter Court · Licenses the frost-caves where Glacium grows, at a professional remove from him personally." The Material Index for tiers five to nine: "The Court's position is that the caves are naturally cold."

## 8 Left open

These stay open in every Lore written from this record, and no backstory settles them.

- The Onawa collision, and why the Tsohanto Reach has gone quiet. None of the five events touches the Reach's silence or the front that counts it.
- Whether Vaelorian is the Fractured Dawn's Paragon of Elyndra, whether Elyndra is silent in any account besides Saetsha's, and who chooses the future her ring denies.
- Whether the dragon that steps into view over Vaelorian is his future or something else's.
- When and how Drevath enforces the audit.
- Who the thirty-eight were, what brought them to the gorge, and where Tol-Varath, the Ashen Vale and the gorge lie.
- Who fought whom in the War of the Long Night, and whether it has anything to do with the Ice-Voice Conflict.
- The Signal of Entropy, and where the Northern Waste lies.
- Whether Senzu Tyurkia stands in any relation to the Fractured Dawn's Tyzura Tyurkia or its Tyurkian exile.
- Whether Vaelorian's last regency of the Court of the Verdant Throne stands in any relation to the regency the Sunroot Court voted under Ovaeren. No record places the two courts side by side.

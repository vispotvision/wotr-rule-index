The Guild Accord's Enforcement Division in the field, the people who fight beside it, and the one it keeps a file on: Kael Serradyn, its Sealwright who walks out of collapses; Azhura Vyren, its breacher of the high air; Ser Caldran Veiss, its Warden on a restricted license; Tsukihana Reimei, the Arbitration-trained blade it took; Draven Kael Vorrick, the hunter in loose alliance with it; Valen Karth, the Accord's dragoon commander; Asaemon, the Voyager Era surgeon who answered the Vanguard's sieges; and Vael of Nothing, the correction in the open file. ## 1 · Who is here

**Kael Serradyn · The Burnmark.** "Enforcement Sealwright. Solar-Graft Vanguard. Containment Specialist." He serves the Guild Accord's Enforcement Division "among the Sealwrights", at the rank of Warden of Duty, "still called a Sealwright in the field for the specialty that earned him the name." He is lean and dusk-skinned, in burnt crimson and matte black weave, "his left arm seamed with glowing orange lines that bleed low-level heat", his eyes rimmed in ember-light "that brightens with his pulse." At the Eclipse Siege he became "the only operative known to have detonated a Wellspring bomb from ground zero and walked out", and three times he has stood at the centre of a Solar Collapse and walked out. "He does not read that as victory. He reads it as a debt the discipline has not finished collecting." His commanding officer "Died sealing the same flame Kael now carries; he keeps the melted insignia." His Flux Pyre fires only across ground he has already crossed: "He cannot detonate ground he has not walked." The Division that houses him sits in "a permanent rivalry with Arbitration, neither side naming it that way in front of him." Open on his card: whether the fire he contains is his own Wellspring's, "or something the Eclipse Siege left behind in him." "Heat remembers. Every scar is a sunrise that refused to die."

**Azhura Vyren · The Azure Coilblade.** The Sky-Serpent Ward, Blue-Spiral Daughter, Warden of Cold Winds, "She Who Guards the High Air": "Draconic-Hybrid Enforcement Breacher. Sky-Serpent Lineage. ## 3 · The shared history

Four events, all new. The first reaches across eras by a record; the other three sit in the Withering Era, before the present each card describes. Where a card leaves a question open, the event leaves it open.

### The Oriel record

- **When.** The siege: the Voyager Era, in Asaemon's life, while the Vanguard called on him. The reading: the Withering Era, in Tsukihana's Arbitration years under High Arbiter Saen, before the Mirror Garden Oath took her to Enforcement. They never met.
- **Where.** Oriel, which no page places, for the siege. For the reading, the Archives Eternal "beneath the Council Spire of the Concord Citadel" (CA, head; the Citadel, WORLD §4), in the Research and Archives Division's keeping (GA, Divisions table).
- **Who.** Asaemon, Tsukihana Reimei.
- **What happened.** The Vanguard called Asaemon to the Siege of Oriel, where the enemy's generals had been corrupted, and he answered because the work wanted precision. In a single engagement he cut each general loose from the power that fed him. None of the generals died, and nobody on the Vanguard's side died. Afterwards he could not see for a time, and his body was brittle for a stretch. The Codex sets a scribe or a seer at every battle and holds an unrecorded war unlawful, and Arbitration reviews each engagement once it ends. Oriel went into the record. Asaemon's part of it is an account of wounds: where each general's connection had failed, and where he cut it. It says nothing of how he made the cut. Long afterwards, in her Arbitration years, Tsukihana Reimei read that review in the Archives Eternal.
- **What each took.**
  - Asaemon: the siege his card records, without a casualty on either side, paid for afterwards in blindness and brittleness.
  - Tsukihana: a precedent in the Accord's own record for a cut that left both sides alive, set down by a man who described the wound and kept the working to himself.

### The Cymorath Distortion Hunt

- **When.** The Withering Era, while Valen held command of the Dragoon Vanguard. After Azhura's Splintering, in the years of the discipline her card calls Glory; this is the hunt her card names as the most recent against the sky-beasts of the soundfold rifts. After Draven's Vorynn Trials had made his craft.
- **Where.** The Corridor Holdings of Stannvaard (WORLD §5): the "trade route territory" the Transport Guild administers, with its "road maintenance, way-station operation, escort coordination" (Stannvaard page, provinces), on the road out of the Ring that the Nalūn page calls the Iron-to-Salt Corridor, "The principal overland route connecting the Ring smelters to the chain-cities and the coast."
- **Who.** Azhura Vyren, Draven Kael Vorrick, Valen Karth, Kael Serradyn.
- **What happened.** A soundfold rift opened in the high air over the Corridor Holdings, and sky-beasts came down out of it on the convoys. The Transport Guild's escorts could not reach them. The Enforcement Division took the commission and sent Azhura Vyren to breach. Draven Kael Vorrick came for the beasts, and the Division's terms for its loose alliance with him put a Sealwright at his side: Kael Serradyn. The Division asked the Legion for dragoons, and Valen Karth brought his. Before the breach, Kael walked a wide ring round the ground below the road where the kills would fall. Then he held his heat in and stood downhill, off the breacher's ground, because warm air would have taken Azhura's breath. Azhura stood under the rift and listened to the pressure. Valen went up before she called it, and his dragoons went after him. He dove on the largest beast and killed it in the air. He landed grounded and slow, and a second beast came down on him there. Draven killed it at close quarters. Azhura's frost brought the rest down out of the air into the cold round Draven, and he killed them one by one. On the last he tore the Bloodbind open. Kael stood at the edge of the ring he had walked and did not fire. The surge ran out, and Draven was still himself. He cut talons from the kills before the convoys moved again.
- **What each took.**

  - Draven: talons for his braid, and the knowledge that the Division walks a ring for him when he works under its commission.
  - Valen: the kill in the air, the grounding his dive costs, and a debt to the hunter who killed what came at him while he was down.

### The two readings on the ash plain

- **When.** The Withering Era, after Caldran's reinstatement under restricted license and after Tsukihana's transfer to Enforcement. After the enforcer at Tarvel's Crossing had survived the Final Index and asked for a non-combat posting, and after the third documented bystander case, since Vael wore the Three Medallions.
- **Where.** The ash plain of the Accord-chartered arc in the New World, between the andesite chain and the caldera belt (WORLD §3; NW, Two Landmasses and The Ground Itself), where the Accord is "the jurisdiction" (ME, II).
- **Who.** Ser Caldran Veiss, Tsukihana Reimei, Vael of Nothing.
- **What happened.** The enforcer from Tarvel's Crossing had given the Division an account that did not describe the turns under the Final Index, and the file on Vael still named no category. The Division sent Ser Caldran Veiss first, alone, as it deploys him. He found Vael on the ash plain with the blindfold on and the three medallions at his chest. The ends of Vael's fingers had lost their edges. Caldran kept Vigil's End in its scabbard. He asked where Vael had been and where he was going, and Vael told him. He asked about Tarvel's Crossing, and about the one target Vael had stopped, and Vael did not answer. Caldran's entry in the file said that Vael had told him no lie. It said nothing else. Afterwards the Division sent Tsukihana Reimei, to learn whether there was anything in him a Verdant Severance could keep. She hears Essence where no Well stirs. On the walk in, what she heard thinned the nearer she came, and she stopped at a distance. She could not say she had read him, and she never draws on a subject she has misread. She did not draw. Her entry recorded no reading. The file stayed a file.
- **What each took.**
  - Caldran: an entry that is true and names no category.
  - Tsukihana: a subject she could not read, left uncut.
  - Vael: two officers of the Division who came, read what they could and went away; the blindfold stayed on, and the file stayed open.

### The Still Garden at Altherion

- **When.** The Withering Era, after Tsukihana's transfer to Enforcement, while Kael served as a Warden of Duty.
- **Where.** Altherion (WORLD §3 and §5), in the hall of the "permanent legation" Enforcement keeps there, in a city that lives in part on "arbitration fees" (Stannvaard page, Accord relationship and economy).
- **Who.** Kael Serradyn, Tsukihana Reimei.
- **What happened.** After Kael Serradyn sealed a Containment, Enforcement took the ground under the seal as forfeit. Its holder had filed with Arbitration, and Arbitration held that the forfeiture had to wait on its hearing. Both Divisions sent parties to the legation hall, and Kael came to give the account of the sealing. He gave it. The argument went on over his account, jurisdiction against jurisdiction and fee against forfeiture, until men on both sides stood with their hands on their weapons. Tsukihana Reimei laid the Still Garden, the rite Enforcement keeps for disputes inside the Accord. Inside it nobody wanted to strike. The ember-light in Kael's eyes went low with his pulse. The parties sat down, and the dispute went back to paper. Nobody on either side called it a rivalry in front of him.
- **What each took.**
  - Tsukihana: a dispute between the Division that trained her and the Division that took her, ended without a blow.
  - Kael: his account of the sealing entered as he gave it, and the rivalry left unnamed in front of him, as his card says it always is.

## 6 · Same person?

# c14 · The Yukari bloodline

The thread-reading line at its two ends. At the origin, by their own cards, Naevra Yukari and Naiser Yukari. In the archive's present, Niran Yukari of Ketsuen, his son Naori and his daughter Akira. Beside them stand Ryuka Yukari, the fox-eared ward of the Mahuo Estate, and Elder Seravain, the Ophidian Archivist of the Guild Accord. Canon order holds throughout: Isaac's direction, then the card, then the archived scenes, then the wiki, then this record.

## 1 · Who is here

**Naevra Yukari.** She Who Asked Fate to Blink; the First Petition, the White Witness, Pale Daughter of the Loom. Her card makes her "First-Generation Yukari" of "The First Yukari Line" in the First Eon, "One of the earliest daughters of the Yukari bloodline". Where other Yukari read the threads of cause, she negotiates with them and edits their pressure points. Her card calls her purpose "correction": to keep destiny from hardening into cruelty and to guard the line from corruption, distortion and false prophecy. Three deeds stand on her card. The White Refusal stopped a blood-oath purge "that would have crystallised an entire branch of destiny into generational slaughter" and earned her "both reverence and hatred from early powers". In the Seven-Night Stitching, after "a catastrophic soul fracture event", she rewove what survived of the Yukari resonance over seven nights. The Mercy Heresy, her belief that fate could be negotiated, marked her as dangerous to harsher doctrines. She is tall, slender and porcelain pale, with hair "white as old parchment", and she carries the Ivory Threadspindle, the First Veil Script and the Pale Oath Needles. Her card finds her weak place in a choice "between preserving cosmic order and protecting specific lives she has personally anchored". She is dormant now, "Hidden in an unknown threshold archive tied to early Yukari memory architecture". Her line: "Fate is only arrogant because no one taught it manners."

**Naiser Yukari.** The Founder Beneath the Storm; Progenitor of the Yukari, the First Yukari, the Night Between Bloodlines; a half-elf of Seikai and Zettari blood and "Founder of the Yukari Line" in the First Eon. Those around him treated his existence, in his card's word, "as a disruption". He refused full absorption into either ancestral doctrine and survived the consequences; the card calls that the Severance of Inheritance. It is said the surname was declared, "spoken into the world as an act of metaphysical self-authorship during a night of spiritual violence and ancestral refusal", and the card calls that the Naming Beneath Stormlight. His allegiance runs "first to the continuity of that branch". Oaths sworn in his presence bind more tightly. People stand near him "as they would near the beginning of something". He wears the Black Founder's Coat and the Yukari Signet, "The earliest mark of the Yukari line", and carries an unnamed longblade. He is tall and long-limbed and dusky-skinned, with black hair "heavy as stormwater". His card speaks of him in the present tense and records no death. His line: "I was born from two houses. I bent neither knee long enough to die inside either one."

**Niran Yukari.** He Who Hears the Thread Before It Snaps; the Black-Winged Listener; Naiser's Heir. Human, of the Yukari bloodline, thirty-two on his card. He holds acting authority over the Yukari Enclave and its tributary settlements on the Inner World frontier, and he leads the Shichi Karasu. He carves wards. He listens to stone, air and breath before he cuts, and sets each ward into the substrate it stands on; his thread-sight is waking. The wiki makes him the founder of Ketsuen, the Crow's Reach, whose capital is the old enclave, now Senri's Gate. His Core Wound: "Heard his father's death four months after it happened". His Desire: "To make the Yukari name mean something that does not require apology for carrying it". His Strength: "Does not act before he understands. Does not understand before he listens". His card binds him to Reimei (Suiren Zanai), to the Shichi Karasu and to Mira Yukari. It names his dead father, Naiser. It names Rengai Zettari, whose Ash-Signal Pouch he has not burned, and Toriki Yukari, whose architecture sits inside his own. He has three scars, "Each chosen over a worse outcome": the left forearm from the ship, the right shoulder from a Greater Wolf, the left jaw from sparring with Sōma. In the archive he builds the long hall at Senri's Gate and comes home to a burned enclave. He dies. He returns from the Veil after seven years and is called to be Septarch.

**Naori Yukari.** The Boy Who Sees the End of Every Thread; Niran's Heir. Thirteen, eldest son of the acting authority, of the Yukari Enclave in Ketsuen. His thread-sight looks forward along the course of events, the rarest form the written record knows, and the elders named it Inzensho. It has run since infancy, and the crimson took his iris by eleven. He also inherited his father's listening to stone. He has no combat training, no Academy education and no technique of his own, and his card says he will learn inscription and ward-craft reluctantly. Core Wound: "Can see where things are going and cannot stop them". His coldness is a withdrawal, since every warmth shows him where the warmth will be damaged. Desire: "To be the crow his father described". Strength: "Akira. He cannot pull away from her". He stands like his father, weight forward and head tilted, and does not know it.

**Akira Yukari.** The Girl Who Sees Without Eyes; the Little Crow. Seven, the younger daughter of the acting authority. She was born blind, with clouded jade eyes, and she has no magical capacity of any kind. Her Crystal is dormant, and her card rules the thread-sight "never". At five her brother adapted a ground-reading exercise for her, the ground's vibration read through bare feet. She mastered it in three weeks. She has extended it since, until she can sense people and animals in the cover around her. Her card rules that the bloodline expressed in her "through a non-standard channel" and calls her "a first". She has Mira's features and Niran's silence. She goes barefoot whenever she can and cannot hide anything. Desire: "To be where her family is". Her card names her narrative function "the tether": "She is the warmth that keeps Naori human."

**Ryuka Yukari · The Little Wayfinder.** Fox of the First Step, the Orange Spark; He Who Runs Between Footsteps. Thirteen, a beastkin ward, a wayfinder-in-training and the "Unofficial mascot" of the Guild Accord. He is Yukari on his father's side, "Descended from Adenyus's line by report", and Mahuo on his mother's, raised as a ward of the Mahuo Estate and grandson of Slyvine Mahuo. He has bright orange hair, large fox ears, a tail that wags when he finds a route, and eyes of "Yukari gold, Mahuo violet". A dormant thread-sight shows him faint luminous threads of direction and connection. His Shortcut slips him a short way along a hidden path; it leaves him severely hungry, and too much of it puts him to sleep. He glows orange when he is happy or frightened. He carries Slyvine's Scarf and Kaname's Compass, which "Always points to wherever Ryuka currently considers home or safety". He withholds nothing about how his gift works. His card opens on the Garden Escape, where the Accord's wards "registered him as a variable", and on the First Howl. "Nothing has called on him hard enough yet to fracture him". The card notes a trajectory: a future World Walker.

**Elder Seravain · Ophidian Archivist.** The Serpent-Speaker, Hand of the Crimson Coil; Ophidian Magus, Spiritual Venom-Binder, Keeper of the Scarlet Serpent Codex. He is an elder of the Guild Accord's Research and Archives Division, Ophidian Studies Cell, and secretly of the Crimson Coil Circle, "a hidden Concord splinter dedicated to reviving the ancient Serpent-God Vaelith". The white serpents that come out of his robes are soul-limbs. They read the resonance of what they touch, and the venom that follows is shaped to what they found. His Venom of Unmaking takes memory first, until the victim forgets their own name before death. He compresses what he reads down to what he judges load-bearing, and "He has been compressing for a very long time and cannot audit what he has lost". Behind the serpents stands "a very old man" with a scholar's body. His card ties him to Sandalphon Aestraen, whose voices he has offered to store; to Hazzen Skollgrin, whom he avoids since Hazzen tried to eat his serpents; and to Riven Vonnel, with whom he has debated venom law for hours. Lucien Draeve's card has traded venoms and antidotes with him "for centuries". His line: "A lie is just venom without fangs. Allow me to correct that."

## 3 · The shared history

Four events. Two stand in the archive and are set down as the scenes give them. Two are new, fitted to every card they touch. The founders' event rests on the Naiser card's own era and parentage. The archive's Naiser lives a different life on the page (section 7, item 1), and nothing here joins the two or separates them.

### The White Refusal

- **When.** The First Eon, in the first generation of the Yukari line, after Naiser's Severance of Inheritance and the naming of the line, while he lived.

- **Where.** The Old World (WORLD §3), on whose northern edge `The Yukari Bloodline.md` sets the line's first ground. No card places either founder.

- **Who.** Naevra Yukari, Naiser Yukari.

- **What happened.** New. Naiser would not be absorbed into the Seikai doctrine or the Zettari, and in refusing he made a split that neither house had sanctioned. He survived what came of it. One of the things that came of it was a purge. Among the early powers an oath was sworn in blood against the new branch. An oath of that kind would have bound the descendants of those who swore it to the killing, one generation after the next. Naevra was a daughter of the line's first generation. She read the oath's thread before it was carried out, and she stopped the purge. Her card calls that the White Refusal. The branch went on. Some of the early powers revered her for it and some hated her, and the harsher doctrinal forces marked her as dangerous for believing that fate could be negotiated. That belief is the Mercy Heresy, which her card calls the first ideological fracture around Yukari purpose.

- **What each took.**

  - Naevra: the reverence and the hatred of early powers that her card records, and the name of heretic among the harsher doctrines.

  - Naiser: a branch that outlived the first oath sworn against it, and a continuity he puts first, kept by a daughter of the line.

### Naiser's death, heard four months late

- **When.** The archive fixes no year. By `Niran_Awakening_Haruki_Reunion.md` Niran had died first and outlived his father "by dying first"; by his card and the years scene he heard of the death four months after it happened.

- **Where.** Unplaced. The scene gives a stone room below daylight, with damp rock, rusted iron and "water that had never once seen daylight", and names no place. Niran was elsewhere.

- **Who.** Naiser Yukari as Niran's card and the archive give him; Niran Yukari.

- **What happened** (`scenes/Naiser_Death_Cozbi_Genocide_Niran_Fall.md`; `scenes/the_years_that_were_not_war.md`). Naiser died in chains that had opened him in six places, none of them mortal alone. He counted the drops as they fell, from "Some old habit of a Cymorath trained too young". His brothers Revari and Muken had died before him. He ordered his grief with his son first. He thought of the boy's care for people who could do nothing for him and of his refusal to let his power be the first thing anyone saw in him. He wondered whether he had handed the boy the wrong fear. A tear ran from one eye, and the scene says of it, "The tears were not for himself". Then his hand opened on the chain. Four months later Niran felt a thread snap that he could not reach, and he received the death as information. Years on, at Senri's Gate, he told Mira: "I do not regret the seeing. I regret the distance between the seeing and the reaching."

- **What each took.**

  - Niran: the Core Wound his card names, "The wound is the gap"; his father's instruction, kept as "a second voice on hard decisions"; a grief his card calls "metabolised".

  - Naiser: the death in that room, with his son first in his thoughts.

### The years that were not war

- **When.** From Naori's birth, in an autumn after the Ward-Lighting, to his thirteenth year and Akira's seventh. The scene's author note puts Niran in his early thirties at the end of it; his card gives thirty-two.

- **Where.** Senri's Gate, the old Yukari Enclave and capital of Ketsuen (WORLD §5): the long hall of sap-weeping cedar, the flat stone above the gate where Niran holds the midwinter Thread-Reading, the practice yard, and the limestone bank of the river below the cedar stand.

- **Who.** Niran Yukari, Naori Yukari, Akira Yukari.

- **What happened** (`scenes/the_years_that_were_not_war.md`). Naori was born three days after the last glyph of the Ward-Lighting was sealed into its station. Akira came after him, born blind. Niran sent neither child to the Mage Academy. He raised them in the ways of his people, the Telling first. Naori read threads from the time he could walk, and his sight looked forward. The elders named it Inzensho. Niran had already met the name in the thread-archive of the standing stones, at a midwinter reading. At six, on the flat stone above the gate, Naori asked his father whether he regretted being able to see these things. Niran told him that a crow must oversee the unfortunate events so that others do not have to, and that they should be grateful for the trust. Naori said he could see himself older, standing where he stood, reading carvings that said things not yet decided. "The wall will thin," Niran said. "I know," Naori said. "That's what frightens me." To Mira, Niran said he believed it was what the boy needed to hear. Naori sat with his sister in the practice yard, set his hand on her forehead, and taught her to read the ground's resonance through her bare feet. She mastered it in three weeks. At seven she found him alone on the riverbank by the weight of his feet on the ground, told him he stood exactly like Father, and showed him how far her reading reached: a fox in the cedar stand, with three kits. "That is not what I taught you," he said. "I know. I added to it." He put his hand on her head, his father's gesture, and they went in before the dumplings got cold.

- **What each took.**

  - Niran: a son growing faster than the record accounts for, "a deviation" in his word, and Mira's answer to it: "Then we make sure the cold has something warm to come home to."

  - Naori: the crow's role, which his card makes his desire, and a sister who is the one thread he cannot read forward.

  - Akira: the exercise she made her own, and a brother who cannot pull away from her.

### Ryuka's Telling at Senri's Gate

- **When.** The year all three children stood at the ages their cards give: Ryuka and Naori thirteen, Akira seven. It falls after the riverbank, since Akira already reads people by their step.

- **Where.** Senri's Gate, Ketsuen (WORLD §5): the standing-stone gate and the long hall.

- **Who.** Ryuka Yukari, Niran Yukari, Akira Yukari.

- **What happened.** New. The Yukari page holds that the name is chosen at the Telling, and Ryuka carries it. His father's people keep the Telling, and he came to Senri's Gate for it as a ward of the Mahuo Estate, with Slyvine's Scarf around his neck and Kaname's Compass in his pocket. Niran met him at the standing-stone gate with the forearm grip that the valley uses there, and his hand read through the grip a Crystal barely woken. The elders gave the boy the full account of what the blood carries, what it costs and what it demands. He heard it through and took the name. When he came out of the long hall, the first person who asked him how his shortcut worked got the whole of it. Akira found him by his step before anyone told her his name, took his arm in both hands, and told him she was blind. The orange glow he gives off when he is happy was lost on her.

- **What each took.**

  - Ryuka: the Yukari name by his own acceptance, and the account of what it costs, which did not teach him to keep his gift to himself.

  - Niran: a kinsman of two houses given the name at his gate, and what his grip read in the boy, a Crystal that nothing has yet called on hard.

  - Akira: another step to know a person by.

## 5 · Outward

- **Naevra Yukari:** an enemy of her own era, one of the harsher doctrinal forces that marked the Mercy Heresy as dangerous, or one of the early powers who swore the purge and hated her for stopping it. The index's other First Eon lives are Gimbzo, Iracordas, Vethraun Ashmaw, Valthrum and Aurgon Mintruix (WORLD §1); none of their cards names her.

- **Naiser Yukari:** a kinsman of one of the two houses he refused, of his own era: an elder of the Seikai or the Zettari blood who wanted him absorbed and treated his birth as a disruption.

- **Niran Yukari:** Rengai Zettari, the mentor his card calls foundational, and the conversation that burning the Ash-Signal Pouch would start. The pouch lies unburned against his sternum.

- **Naori Yukari:** a master of inscription and ward-craft who is not his father, the teacher his card says he will take reluctantly. The Ward-Carver's Academy at Senri's Gate has such instructors, and so has Vaeloris, where Dhaerin's grammar is taught.

- **Akira Yukari:** a peer who works without the draw. Xanelor Rafminar told the same Academy class that he has no magical potential and uses Sage Arts (`scenes/xanelor_the_introductions.md`).

- **Ryuka Yukari:** someone who can follow where his skips land, for the question his card gives to "What nobody knows": whether a skip lands him "somewhere the Mahuo Estate cannot follow". A Gate officer of the Accord's Logistics and Supply Division, or a guide of the Spirit Layers.

- **Elder Seravain:** an attributor. He needs someone who could say "How much of the Scarlet Serpent Codex Seravain wrote himself". The Bench of Attribution sits Research and Archives beside the Measurewrights and Arbitration for exactly that kind of question.

## 6 · Same person?

No two cards here are one person.

- **Naiser.** One card carries the name. Niran's card and the archive give a Naiser who is Niran's father, about fifty in the malformation passage, and dead. The Naiser card gives a First Eon founder, half-elven, with no recorded death. Whether the card and the father are one man is the open question of section 7, item 1. This record keeps both lives as their sources write them.

- **Seravain.** Elder Seravain and Seravain Drelith share a given name and a coil: Drelith is "The Ivory Coil", and Elder Seravain is "Hand of the Crimson Coil". Drelith's card makes him "Dragonkin trueblood" and places him "Outside the Accord under Concord Sanctions". Elder Seravain is an R.A.D. elder inside the Accord. They are two people.

## 7 · Where the cards disagree

Quoted and left open.

1. **Naiser the founder against Naiser the father.** The Naiser card: "Founder of the Yukari Line · First Eon", "Half-Elf", "Progenitor of the Yukari", and a present-tense Trajectory with no death. Niran's card: "Naiser's Heir", "Naiser Yukari (deceased) · Filial-Formative", and of Niran himself, "Lineage Human · Yukari Bloodline · Age 32". The death scene gives a man born into a family that already had a Telling: "The Elder read the thread over my cradle and told my mother I would be the one who pulled this family out of its own mediocrity", and "three boys with the mark fresh on their foreheads" (`Naiser Yukari.md`; `Niran Yukari.md`; `scenes/Naiser_Death_Cozbi_Genocide_Niran_Fall.md`). One name, two lives.

2. **Naiser's face.** The card: "Tall, long-limbed", "Hair falls long and black, heavy as stormwater". The malformation passage: "Broad through the chest and going soft at the middle. The heavy jaw." (`Naiser Yukari.md`; `scenes/niran_malformation.md`)

3. **Who founded the Yukari.** The Naiser card: "Progenitor of the Yukari" and "The First Yukari". The Yukari page: "The third was Senri". `scenes/THE_YUKARI_BLOODLINE.md`: "Their founder, Senri Yukari, arose during the 200,000-year Interstitium". `scenes/Niran_Mira_Return_to_the_Enclave.md` calls Senri "the founder of the Yukari bloodline". Naevra's "First-Generation Yukari" in the First Eon meets the same clash. WORLD §1 records it: "The Yukari founders' cards say First Eon; YK puts Senri in the Interstitium."

4. **One Stage name for two Stages.** Naevra's card gives her Temperance Stage as "Stage IX" with the name "Realization". Naiser's gives "Stage 6" with the name "Realization". A Lore states neither.

5. **Niran's age.** His card: "Age 32", and on the same card "Six years of combat training have replaced the thirteen-year-old's frame entirely" and the pouch "Warm from six years of body heat". The years scene's author note: "Niran on his current card was 19, now updated to 32". `scenes/Niran_Mira_Return_to_the_Enclave.md`: "He was twenty-four now". 6. **When the crimson took the iris.** Niran's card: "the Ketsumyōgan's corona absorbed the iris entirely at eighteen". Naori's card: "Crimson corona consumed the iris entirely by age eleven, two years ahead of Niran's development". 8. **The Academy.** Naori's card: "No Academy education". The years scene: "Niran had not sent either to the Mage Academy". `scenes/xanelor_the_introductions.md` seats Naori and Akira in Kwon Mu-jin's class, and its author note names it "Aetherion Academy RP".

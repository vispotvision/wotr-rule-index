# c15 · Thalyren's groves and the highland plains

The grove sovereign of the Everbloom, the plainswanderer he guides, the toad-broker and the mushroom fox who walk the same trails, and three people of the Outer World's high plains: a centaur warlord, a Beastkin tracker and an unaligned warden. No card here states an era, and no archived scene holds any of the seven. Canon order holds throughout: Isaac's direction, then the card, then the archived scenes, then the wiki, then this record.

## 1 Who is here

**Thalyren Veyroot · The Verdant Sentinel.** He is Green-Warden of the Ninefold Grove and Rootkeeper, and his card names him "Ancient Wellspring Guardian. Grove Sovereign. Verdure Archon's Chosen." For the Sacred Conclave he is the bound protector of the Everbloom Wellspring, which his card counts among the most stable and sacred in the Core World. He holds spiritual authority over the druids, beastfolk and nature-attuned Concord members of its territory. He once walked toward the thing that could end him. He rooted. His soul is anchored to the Everbloom, and inside its domain any destruction of his body regrows from the Wellspring's roots over several days. Outside it his life-force fades, and he has to draw what he can from soil and sunlight. His card puts the cost in three words: "He cannot leave." Through his antlers he sees every grove, forest and sacred green-space in his reach, and a disturbance shows to him as leaves withering. His cloak is alive, leaf and moss and flower, and it can entangle, shed healing pollen or harden into bark. He has almost never needed to strike, and he cannot run. His card leaves one thing open: "Whether the rooting was his choice or the Everbloom's."

**Lyraen Callivane · Verdure Hoof.** She is called the Meadow's Whisper and Shepherd's Fang: plainswanderer, herbalist-marksman and Wellspring Mediator of Verdant Pass. An Independent Concord Freeholder, she "Holds roaming rights between Innerworld grassland settlements and Outer World frontier hamlets" and "Often acts as an unofficial emissary between Beastkin herding clans and Concord Guild parties." Her presence soothes beasts and dulls hostile intent in people, and it works best on a stampede or a mob. Her horns keep a struck note and let it out later as a burst of sound or a signal heard far off. Standing barefoot in fertile soil she mends wounds and fatigue in herself and others. Her working fails her on sterile ground, in hard cold and in exhausted earth. She shoots well and hits lightly. She once healed Varek Ka'ruun after a battle, and he called her "the little lamb that bites." Her epigraph: "Not all shepherds carry crooks. Some carry teeth." Her card leaves her Spirit Axis unnamed.

**Gorrum Vaelseth · Toad Guy.** He is the Croak in the Rain and the Verdant Trickster: wandering relic-broker, Wellspring cartographer, herbalist and minor hex-weaver. He is nomadic and "Loosely connected to the Guardians of Tres Aures through old pacts". He trades in rare herbs, spirit-potions and magical debris scavenged "from forgotten battlefields and collapsed Wellsprings." He is a mapmaker before anything else. In marsh, grass or shallow water he can hold still for hours until the ground hides him, and he lives by never being where a fight is. A croaking undertone in his voice inclines a listener to trust him. Every hex he makes is bound beforehand into a charm of gathered swamp herbs, since he cannot make one on the spot. His own Wellspring is a wetland that grows slowly and serves him only where it already stands. Varek Ka'ruun says Gorrum once tried to sell him "swamp air" in a bottle. Gorrum denies it, smiling. "Frogs see the ripples long before the storm hits. Sit with me. We'll wait for the flies to come to us."

**Moryn Sporetail · Mushroom Fox.** Moryn is the Walking Cap and Lantern-Forager: travelling sporemancer, fungal alchemist and wilderness merchant. An Independent Concord-Aligned Trader, Moryn "Wanders between Core World villages, Innerworld groves and Outer World fringes", selling potions, poultices and rare fungi for stories, maps and peculiar artifacts. Moryn "Maintains friendly ties to the Verdant Sentinel's Circle and to Gorrum Vaelseth." The spores and fungi of Moryn's cap light dark places, keep off some predators, and leave hostile creatures drowsy or seeing things. The Pouch of a Hundred Spores holds powders for growth, corrosion, healing and pestilence, every one made in advance. Moryn can listen to a region's fungal networks for recent movement, changes in weather and disturbance. Moryn's own Wellspring is a cavern of lit mushrooms and knotted roots where every living thing is at last taken into the weave, and Moryn walks in it. "Everything rots… but rot is just another way of saying 'new life begins.'"

**Garruk Stonehoof · The Iron-Mane.** He is the Horn of the Highlands and Hoofbreaker: centaur warlord, battlefield commander, and Wellspring Guardian of the Iron Plains. He is chieftain and spiritual protector of the Stonehoof Herdclan, "one of the oldest and most feared centaur war-hosts in the Outer World's rolling warplains," and the Hoof-Seer Wellspring chose him to guard its power. His card holds the two offices as one: "A grazing path is a grazing path and is also a sacred boundary". Stone hardens his hide and his horse's body against blades, arrows and some glyph-work, and he does not break stride. At full gallop his charge breaks formations, barriers and wards. His voice rallies a herd or panics a foe across a battlefield. Inside his Wellspring he gives the herd its battle-lust and then has to command the herd that has it. He needs open ground and a run. Broken terrain and a feint beat him. "Steel rusts. Stone crumbles. But the hoof leaves its mark forever."

**Kaelen Brighthoof · The Horned Hawk.** He is the Arrow of the Peaks: highland tracker, Wellspring path-guide and hunter of Outer Realm beasts. His people are the Hornclimb Clan, "a Beastkin clan living in the high mountain passes between the Core World and Outer World fringe realms", scouts and defenders for travellers crossing dangerous alpine Wellspring country. He crosses sheer rock under fire without losing his footing. He can slow his breath and heartbeat until he is nearly impossible to detect. His arrows land by placement. The goats, hawks and horned deer of his home range are soul-bound to him and scout or carry messages on a hunt. His Wellspring is a mountain peak. He must be on the mountain, where altitude and cold are his ground, and the flatlands beat him. "From up here, the wind carries every lie, every footstep, every chance to put an arrow where it matters."

**Cernan Eryndor · The Bull.** He is the Ironblood Rampart, "Unaligned Warden of the Outer World," with "Rumoured descent from the Eryndor bloodline of Lowmere." A presence his card calls the Raging Bull is resident in his Crystal. Nobody worships it or summons it, and what it is waits on a ruling. His method is mass in a straight line. His charge makes a whole formation, wall or gate give at once, and the blow carries through the first body into whatever stands behind it. He holds together through the exertion. Afterward he sleeps, and nobody can wake him. His card gives him one way to lose: "every commander who has beaten him has beaten him with one", a feint. The year of his Catalyst "was the worst year of his life and he has never described it." His card names no one.

## 3 The shared history

### The grove on the Stonehoof path

**When.** Undated, and new. After Thalyren rooted himself to the Everbloom, which is his card's Catalyst Event. It falls in one grazing season of the Stonehoof Herdclan under Garruk, while Moryn was a guest of the Ninefold Grove.

**Where.** A sacred grove at the outer edge of Thalyren's sight about the Everbloom Wellspring, where a Stonehoof grazing path runs through it (Thalyren's and Garruk's cards). His card sets the Everbloom in the Core World, which no page places among the four quarters (WORLD §3; section 7). The herd came up to the grove from its warplains in the Outer World (Garruk's card; WORLD §3).

**Who.** Thalyren Veyroot, Garruk Stonehoof, Lyraen Callivane, Moryn Sporetail.

**What happened.** One grazing season the Stonehoof Herdclan came up its path and through a sacred grove at the edge of Thalyren's sight. The herd grazed as it went. It cropped the understory to the roots and trod the leaf-mould into mud. Thalyren saw the grove as his sight shows him any disturbance, as leaves withering. He stayed at the Everbloom. Moryn, a guest in the grove that season, went out in his place, knelt in the trodden ground and listened to the mycelium under it. The network held many hooves on one line, a herd on its road, and nothing cut or burned. Moryn carried that back. Thalyren sent word to the herd by Lyraen, whose roaming rights run into the Outer World and who goes between herding clans and those who deal with them. She walked barefoot into the Stonehoof camp and put his word to Garruk. Garruk heard her out. The path was the herd's, and a sacred boundary under the Hoof-Seer Wellspring, and he would not move it. He gave her the herd's answer in his own words: "Steel rusts. Stone crumbles. But the hoof leaves its mark forever." He agreed to one thing. The herd crossed the grove at a walk, did not graze inside it, and went on over the season. Moryn told Thalyren the trodden ground would feed the next growth. The understory was back before the herd returned.

**What each took.** Thalyren took a grove he watched wither from the Everbloom and did not walk to, and a boundary another warden holds sacred running through his own. He did not strike. Garruk took the path, kept, and his word to walk it where it crosses the grove. Lyraen took the go-between's part between the grove and the herd, and it falls to her each time the path and the groves meet again. Moryn took a reading of the ground that both wardens accepted, and a guest's standing that now includes being sent.

### The Verdant Pass Accord

**When.** In the arc Lyraen's card names "The Verdant Pass Accord" and Gorrum's card names "Trade at the Verdant Pass", both in Chapter XIV. No era is stated. The gathering is the cards'. Its terms, and the people at it beyond Lyraen and Gorrum, are new.

**Where.** The Verdant Pass, of which Lyraen is Wellspring Mediator (her card). No page places it. Her roaming rights run between Innerworld grassland settlements and Outer World frontier hamlets (her card; WORLD §3).

**Who.** Lyraen Callivane, Gorrum Vaelseth, Kaelen Brighthoof, Moryn Sporetail.

**What happened.** Beastkin herding clans drove their herds through the Verdant Pass in their season, and in the same weeks a Concord Guild party came up to draw on the pass's Wellspring. Hornclimb scouts had guided the party through the high passes. Kaelen came down with them. Lyraen, as the pass's Wellspring Mediator, called the clans and the party to the meadow under the Wellspring. Gorrum came up the pass to trade, last of anyone. Lyraen called him Old Croak for it in front of both sides. He had mapped the pass and its Wellspring on an earlier road, and the clans and the party both knew his map. They agreed to write the terms against it. The clans kept the pass in their season, and the Guild party drew on the Wellspring outside it. Lyraen put her name to the terms as mediator. Moryn came through during the gathering and traded refined spore-powders for Gorrum's swamp herbs and rare fungi for Lyraen's Harmony herbs. Moryn also took a tracing of Gorrum's map as part of a price. On the last evening Lyraen and Kaelen shot at marks across the meadow, she from the grass with her feet bare in the soil, he from the rock above it, shooting down. Afterward they traded techniques. He teased her for "fighting in flatlands."

**What each took.** Lyraen took the Verdant Pass Accord, with her name beside both parties'. Gorrum took his trade, and a map that is now the record both sides point to when they disagree. Kaelen took a guiding fee for his clan and a contest. Moryn took herbs, a tracing of the pass and the stories that came with it, which are the currency Moryn prefers.

### The watch on the warplains

**When.** Undated, and new. It falls in one of the dangerous hunts Kaelen scouted for the Stonehoof Herd (Garruk's card), while Cernan held his unaligned wardenship of the Outer World.

**Where.** The Stonehoof Herdclan's warplains, in the Outer World (Garruk's card; WORLD §3). No page places the Iron Plains or the herd's range more closely.

**Who.** Garruk Stonehoof, Kaelen Brighthoof, Cernan Eryndor.

**What happened.** An Outer Realm beast came down out of the high country onto the Stonehoof warplains. Garruk took the herd out after it, with Kaelen scouting ahead along the ridges. Cernan met the beast first. He was warding that ground on nobody's commission, and he charged it across the open plain in a straight line. The beast was out of the line when the charge arrived. The charge ran on through empty ground and ploughed a furrow in it. At the end of the furrow Cernan went down asleep, as he does after that charge, and nothing woke him. From the ridge Kaelen found the furrow, the sleeping man at its end, and the beast's track bending back toward him. He sent a hawk to the herd. Garruk brought the Stonehoof up and set them in a ring about the sleeping man, on open ground where a charge has its run. He held the herd there through the day and did not go after the beast. At dusk it came back for the man. Kaelen lay still on the rock with his breath slowed and put an arrow where it mattered. The herd ran the beast down on the open ground before it reached the ring. Cernan woke inside the ring. The hunt was over.

**What each took.** Cernan took a debt to the Stonehoof Herdclan he did not ask for, and one more loss of the old kind: what he charged was not in the line. Garruk took the kill, and a warden of the Outer World in the herd's debt, bought with a day of standing still. Kaelen took the hunt he had tracked and the arrow he placed. He also took what he saw at the end of the furrow: the warden asleep, and beyond waking.

## 6 Same person?

No two cards here are one person.

- **Garruk Stonehoof and Cernan Eryndor.** Garruk's card calls them "the same build twice", and they are two men. Garruk is chieftain of the Stonehoof Herdclan and chosen by the Hoof-Seer Wellspring; Cernan is unaligned and names no herd. Their Signatures, harmonisations and titles differ, and the watch on the warplains puts them on one field at once.
- **Kaelen Brighthoof and Kaelen Raive.** Outside this cluster. Kaelen Raive's card: "Two Kaelens, different surnames, no stated relation." They share a given name and nothing else.

# c16 · Anvil and relic

Cluster record for the Lore pass. Members: orokh-sable-forge-the-wyrmwright, mavra-cindrel-the-golden-engine, kaelzar-the-forge-that-roars, gorvan-aldric-voss-the-maw-that-held, draen-varos-the-red-forge-sentinel, ymir-rhok-the-emberhand, garron-vuldane-warden-of-thaumar.

**Era.** The Withering Era, with no year number. Kaelzar's card states it ("Withering Era, Imperial Age"), and Gorvan's gives the superseded "Withering Age" (WORLD §1). The other five state no era. Three cards give a span of years: Orokh has "a century at an anvil", Gorvan "a four-hundred-year career", and Mavra is twenty-seven. This record sets the shared events inside Orokh's century, the last of them inside Ymir's apprenticeship.

**Sources read.** The seven cards in full, at the roster's `file` paths (Volume I card folder of `wiki/`). The neighbouring cards that name a member or share a line with one: `Borin Ironheart · The Master of the Soul Forge.md`, `Lucien Draeve · Emerald Alchemist.md`, `Zahir Ashveil · Desert's Fang.md`, `Renjiro Kaithen · The Pale Fang.md`, `Eryndrel Vaelune · The Trinebow.md`, `Commander Severin Bale · The Slayer.md`, `Vaerath Ashveil · The Pale Reaping.md`, `Lumen Varos · The Dawn-Touched Oracle.md`, `Gimbzo.md`. Wiki: the Dawi Peoples page, the Stannvaard page (The Guild Seat of Stannvaard), the Guild Accord in the Modern Era, the Outer World (The Southern Pan), The Celestial Host, The Three Epochs, the Atla'zon and Gaia page of The Fourteen Titans, The Concord Military, Hide, Horn and Bone. Inventories: `desktop/inventories/dawi.md`, `accord.md`, `eresse.md`. Neighbouring records: c02 (Borin) and c12 (Serai Linthra).

**The archive is silent on all seven.** A search of `scenes/` finds no member's name and none of their bodies or grounds: the Titanwrights, Orrengar, Thaumar, the Iron Accord, the Temple Rift, the Deep Mantle, the Atlas Mantle, Braqth. The roster gives every member an empty scene list. Everything in section 3 is new, and it is built to fit every card it touches.

Cards are cited by title and section numeral. "New" marks what this record adds where the sources are silent.

## 1 · Who is here

**Orokh Sable-Forge · The Wyrmwright** (orokh-sable-forge-the-wyrmwright). A blacksmith of fallen dragons and a relic-maker "whose anvil bends bone-memory and star-iron into covenant blades, seals and tools". He is Giant-kin out of Braqth in the Outer World, his body "lacquered black by furnace soot and alchemical pitch", his hair "white as quenched slag". His eyes are sealed and he did not replace them. He reads heat, rhythm, heartbeat and "the way names tremble in metal", and lies leave ripples he can taste. He forges without fire, in silent workshops, cave mouths and cold wind. He will not work fresh kill: "Bring bone that has had time to forget pain". His payment is always twofold, material and story, and for weapons he takes a confession, for locks a secret, for armour "the name of something you fear and will not admit". The Guild Accord contracts him quietly for containment hardware, and the Black Concord bargains with him "for things no registry admits". His Ash-Anvil has ornaments along its skirt. They are "pauses in a song he started a century ago and has never finished".

**Mavra Cindrel · The Golden Engine** (mavra-cindrel-the-golden-engine). Relicsmith and Titanwright, a Liveryman-grade Vanguard Relicsmith of the Titanwrights, "a chartered relicsmith company" of the Guild Accord, assigned to reinforcing frontline Constructs and Aetheric weapons. She is twenty-seven on the card. She comes from Orrengar in the Old World, Forge Dominion, and she is Felkin, "her own people's name for the lineage". The machines she built to fight the Orrengar rebellion "now haunt the ones she builds for the Accord". Her mentor is dead. A broken Runeglass pendant "is what's left, and she wears it". Her soul ran through her own armour and weapon "until pain and joy ran through the machinery as evenly as through her", and she "survived direct contact with multiple unstable Wellsprings during field tests". The Heart-Drive Core grafted into her amplifies at emotional peaks. Whether it reads them or makes them she has never asked, and "the pendant around her neck is the reason why". Her one line on the card: "If it breaks, I build it stronger. If I break, well, then I was the test."

**Kaelzar · The Forge That Roars** (kaelzar-the-forge-that-roars). The Iron Lion of Atlas, the Anvil's Heir: a leonine giant, the Echo Reincarnate of Maximus, "Titan-Smith of Aegor's Forge". When the Wellspring Rebellion broke divine order, Maximus's Essence fused with Fixatio. What reformed aeons later was Kaelzar. The Guild Accord's Wellspring Containment Registry holds him under Echo Entity classification and a containment oath. He is stationed within the Atlas Deep Mantle under Accord supervision and trusted for defensive deployments, and the Manebind Chains restrain his mantle output. His card records three incidents. In the Atlas Mantle War he closed a volcanic Wellspring breach "by collapsing a mountain range inward". He held the Ardent Siege. At the Echo Containment Breach he took a crystal fracture he has held since. To Niran, the former overseer of Echo Containment, he gave "Measured loyalty, no obedience". He has not yet faced the memory of Maximus, and nobody knows whether Aegor's Forge would return it, or whether Kaelzar would still be Kaelzar afterward. His own line on the card: "I was born to hold the line, not to seek the throne."

**Gorvan Aldric Voss · The Maw That Held** (gorvan-aldric-voss-the-maw-that-held). Stonefather and Old Fang, he is also "He Who Has Never Needed to Move Twice", and he is High Dawi of Varūn descent and a Rune-Forger lineage, Innerworld-forged, a survivor of the Sovereignty Wars. He served in the Iron Accord Warhost, Martial Division. He is independent now. The Temple Rift Crisis shattered the Dawi-Deepkin trust permanently, and he left the Iron Accord after it. He still wears its skull-faced war-cult crest at his sternum, "because he won it", so the enemy sees it first. His pauldrons are the trophy-heads of two Construct-class entities killed in the Rift. His Domain formed "alone, in deep stone, over a decade, with no instruction and nobody watching". The stone has begun to treat him as terrain. He watches for the moment that closes. Whether his record of outlasting every problem is strength or only patience he has never examined, "and has decided not to start". His words: "The ground holds. I just stand on it longer than you."

**Draen Varos · The Red Forge Sentinel** (draen-varos-the-red-forge-sentinel). The Bastion of Cinders, the Iron Shepherd: battle-smith, siege-line defensive operative and Body Path instructor of the Accord's Vanguard Bastion Unit. He came "out of the smelting districts of Eresse" as a blacksmith conscript. He rose by attrition. He instructs Body Path initiates and oversees the field armory at a Deep Mantle Bastion under Kaelzar's command. The vanguard trusts his armory work "without checking it twice", and the field armory system is "the one authority he never disputes". His Crystal is alloyed with tempered slag from the Atlas Mantle. His two-headed Axe of Tempered Remorse was "forged from failed Titan armor", and it "started as a mistake that got a soldier killed, and he kept forging on it anyway, until the guilt itself became the temper". Why despair dulls its edge nobody, Draen included, has found. "Heat is memory. Every blade remembers the hand that forged it."

**Ymir Rhok · The Emberhand** (ymir-rhok-the-emberhand). Borin's Whetstone, Young Anvil: apprentice blacksmith, Wellspring alloy crafter and Accord armorer-in-training in the Forgeworks Annex of the Logistics and Supply Division. His master is Borin Ironheart, who teaches foundational discipline before advanced craft and scolds him "for experimenting too far from tradition". Ravessa Flintweld, a journeyman from an Outer World branch, slips him experimental techniques in secret. Hyo Miran sells him rare metals, and they haggle. Zahir Ashveil once commissioned a flame-channeling curved blade from him, and the two still trade desert ores. Everything on his sheet follows from one afternoon: he heated a piece of metal with his hand on purpose, "and it worked". Every weapon he makes keeps an echo of his hand. He can find it anywhere. His words: "A dull blade's an insult to its maker."

**Garron Vuldane · Warden of Thaumar** (garron-vuldane-warden-of-thaumar). The Iron Warden: Master Alchemist, Siegewright and Thaumaturgic Warden of the Grand Forges, of the Alchemists' Society, "chartered under the Research and Archives Division, seated at Stannvaard", and a Master and Warden of Duty. He is mountain-born, in layered scaleplate, his iron-gray mane running into "a metallic fur of living Aethersteel that answers his pulse", and he carries the Basilisk Cannon, Molthra. He carries a portable Forge-Wellspring inside his body, and his co-author is his own forge. Harn and Vyr, the two semi-sentient constructs housed in his pauldrons, were "raised as companions before they became weapons". He holds that the Titans left tools behind. He means to reforge them. A Forge collapse took his left arm, and it was rebuilt from dragonbone and Aethersteel. "The forge never stops burning. Only the weak forget that every spark is a choice."

## 3 · The shared history

Four events, in the order this record reads them. Only the last is fixed after another, since Draen already has the Bastion's armory in it. None is in the archive. Each is new and fitted to every card it touches. Where a card states a price, a refusal or a thing nobody knows, the event pays the price, keeps the refusal and leaves the unknown alone.

**Orokh's northern workshop.** Three of the four events use one place, and it is new: a cave mouth on the outer face of the Ring above the Rimlands of Stannvaard (WORLD §5; the Stannvaard page gives the Rimlands as roads, way-stations, timber and farms, and the crater's own air as warmer than the shield). Orokh's card gives his grounds as "Silent workshops, cave mouths, cold wind". The Accord that contracts him keeps its armoury in the Ring. The cold is outside the crater, and the Dawi forges inside it do not use the heat he forges with.

### The Jaws fitted

- **When.** After the Temple Rift Crisis, once Gorvan had left the Iron Accord, inside Orokh's century at the anvil. No card gives it an era. The Dawi Peoples page dates the Rift otherwise (section 7).
- **Where.** Orokh's cave mouth on the outer face of the Ring, above the Rimlands of Stannvaard (WORLD §5).
- **Who.** Gorvan Aldric Voss, Orokh Sable-Forge.
- **What happened.** Gorvan came to the cave mouth with the heads of the two Construct-class entities he had killed in the Temple Rift. He had carried them a long time. Orokh does not work fresh kill. Gorvan had waited until the heads had had time to forget, and Orokh read them by their heat and fitted them to his shoulders as pauldrons. For armour the price is a name. Gorvan named something he fears and will not admit, and what he named is in the pauldrons and in no record. During the fitting he felt, for the first time, the current that seeks him through the objects he touches long enough. He entered the price as paid. The heads kept an echo of what they had been, and Aether-sensitive opponents have felt a faint predatory awareness off his shoulders ever since. He calls it good hardware.
- **What each took.**
  - Gorvan: the beast-jaw pauldrons, the Jaws That Watch, and the first touch of the current that seeks him through what he holds.
  - Orokh: a fear Gorvan named at his anvil, kept in the metal and nowhere else.

### The slag of the Atlas Mantle

- **When.** The Withering Era, after the Atlas Mantle War on Kaelzar's card, when the Accord raised the Bastion Kaelzar commands. His card dates the war VE 913. No corrected count supports the date, and this record gives it no year.
- **Where.** The Atlas Deep Mantle, where Kaelzar's card stations him and Draen's card sets "a Deep Mantle Bastion". WORLD places neither, and this record does not place them. Draen came to it from the smelting districts of Eresse, in the Grand Principality on the elven old-ground of the New World (WORLD §3).
- **Who.** Kaelzar, Draen Varos.
- **What happened.** Kaelzar closed the volcanic breach by bringing a mountain range down on it, and the fall left a field of slag and broken plate in the Mantle. The Accord raised a Bastion beside the fallen range and put Kaelzar over it. The Vanguard Bastion Unit sent conscript smiths down to work what the collapse had left, and Draen Varos was one of them. He worked the slag for the Bastion's armory. The tempered slag of the Mantle is in his Crystal now. Among the salvage lay Titan armor that had failed where it lay. Draen forged a two-headed axe from it. The axe failed on the line, and a soldier died of the failure. The death was entered on the Bastion's roll, and the soldier is sung at the solstice with the Accord's other dead in service. Draen did not melt the axe down. He put it back on the anvil and kept forging on it. He rose in the armory the way the Bastion Unit rises, by attrition, and the axe stayed on his anvil the whole way up. Kaelzar left the field armory in his hands.
- **What each took.**
  - Kaelzar: a Bastion on the ground he had brought down, and at its armory a smith who went on forging the thing that had failed.
  - Draen: the slag in his Crystal, an axe begun from failed Titan armor and a soldier's death, and the armory he has kept under Kaelzar since.

### The collapse at the test forge

- **When.** The Withering Era, in Mavra Cindrel's years with the Titanwrights after the Orrengar rebellion. Mavra is twenty-seven on her card. The collapse falls inside those years, when Garron was already warden of the Grand Forges and Harn and Vyr were already his.
- **Where.** A test forge of the Grand Forges in the Upper Ring of Stannvaard, the extraction cities on the crater walls (WORLD §5; the Stannvaard page: "Production facilities first and settlements second"). Garron's card seats him at Stannvaard, no page places the Grand Forges, and this record sets this forge in the Upper Ring. Afterwards, Orokh's cave mouth above the Rimlands.
- **Who.** Garron Vuldane, Mavra Cindrel, Orokh Sable-Forge.
- **What happened.** The Titanwrights brought Mavra's armour and weapon to the Grand Forges for field tests on live Wellspring heat. She ran her soul through them as conduits, test after test, under Garron's wardenship, and her mentor stood the tests with her. On the last of them the test forge collapsed. Garron went in under it. Harn and Vyr took the fall on their plating, and Garron held the roof off the test floor while the crew got out. Mavra came out through direct contact with the unstable Wellsprings the collapse had loosed, her armour and weapon still carrying her. She has had a Crystal that syncs to conductive alloys on contact ever since. Her mentor did not come out. The Runeglass pendant did, broken. The collapse took Garron's left arm. Afterwards he went out of the Ring to Orokh's cave mouth. Orokh does not work fresh kill, and the rib he laid on the Ash-Anvil came from a dragon long dead. His payment is always material and story. Garron brought Aethersteel for the material, and for the story he told the collapse. Orokh tapped along the rib until the marrow remembered flight and drew it into the core of an arm. Garron laid the Aethersteel over it himself at Stannvaard.
- **What each took.**
  - Garron: an arm of dragonbone and Aethersteel, and a crew that came out from under him.
  - Mavra: the Crystal that syncs to alloys, the broken pendant she wears, and her life, which came out from under the arm the forge took.
  - Orokh: the story of the collapse, which is in the arm and in nobody's file.

### The seals for the Deep Mantle

- **When.** The Withering Era, after the Echo Containment Breach on Kaelzar's card and inside Ymir Rhok's apprenticeship. Draen already had the Bastion's field armory.
- **Where.** The Bastion in the Atlas Deep Mantle that Kaelzar commands, the cards' ground, which WORLD does not place and this record does not either; this record sets the Echo Containment Breach there. Orokh's cave mouth on the outer face of the Ring above the Rimlands of Stannvaard (WORLD §5).
- **Who.** Kaelzar, Orokh Sable-Forge, Draen Varos, Ymir Rhok.
- **What happened.** The breach broke the containment seals in the Deep Mantle, and Kaelzar held it and took the fracture he has carried since. The Accord let the new seals to Orokh under its quiet contract. Orokh came down into the Mantle to read the broken plates. He laid his hands on them, read the heat and rhythm left in them, and gave what he read to nobody. His price for a lock is a secret, and Kaelzar paid it. He told it to the smith alone, in the Bastion's armory, and nobody else heard it. While Orokh was in the armory he asked to lay a hand on the Axe of Tempered Remorse. Draen did not unsling it. Orokh forged the plates at his cave mouth in the cold, lifted each into the winter air and let his breath fall over it until the glow sank inward and set. The brackets that would hold them came from the Forgeworks Annex, and they were Ymir's work, plain ironwork to his master's standard. Ymir went up to the cave mouth for the plates. Orokh put them into his hands himself. The apprentice saw a workpiece take its heat under a hammer with no coal in the forge. Ymir brought plates and brackets down into the Mantle under Logistics and Supply and fitted them in the Bastion under Draen. Draen proofed every bracket before a plate went on it. He struck the Accord's proof-mark into the ones that passed. When a bolt would not seat, Ymir heated it with his bare hand and seated it. Draen made him strip it and proof it again. While the fitting lasted, Draen kept him on the armory floor with the Body Path initiates and put him through their drills. Kaelzar stood in the hold while the last plate went onto the apprentice's brackets.
- **What each took.**
  - Kaelzar: seals on the Mantle's containment, paid for with a secret of his own.
  - Orokh: Kaelzar's secret, which is in the plates and nowhere else, and what the broken seals told him, which he kept.
  - Draen: an apprentice's brackets proofed and passed, and the axe still unread by any hand but his.
  - Ymir: brackets of his own making holding the Deep Mantle's seals, a bolt proofed twice, the Bastion's drills, and the sight of a master's workpiece taking heat with no coal.

## 6 · Same person?

No. The seven cards are seven people with different bodies, origins and offices. Kaelzar and Maximus are one being by Kaelzar's own card, which gives Maximus as his former identity, and they share one life across the reforming. Whether the Maximus of Gimbzo's card is that Maximus stays open (section 7). Gorvan and Borin Ironheart share a lineage line word for word and centuries at their work. They are two Dawi with two names and two histories. Draen Varos and Lumen Varos share a surname and nothing else the cards record.

## 7 · Where the cards disagree

Quoted and left open.

1. **Whether Kaelzar has a card.** Draen's card: "Kaelzar · His commanding officer at the Deep Mantle Bastion. No card exists on file; kept as an unattested name" (Draen, IX). Kaelzar's card exists. It names no Draen and no command, and it holds him "under Echo Entity classification and a containment oath, stationed within Atlas Deep Mantle under Accord supervision" (Kaelzar, I). A commanding officer and an entity held under supervision are both on the record.
2. **Who leads on the shaping stat.** Orokh's card: "Orokh is the only sheet in Volume IV where Tempering leads" (Orokh, IV). Lucien's card: "Lucien is the second sheet in Volume IV where Tempering leads, after Orokh Sable-Forge" (`Lucien Draeve · Emerald Alchemist.md`). Ymir's card: "Ymir is the third character in Volume IV whose highest stat is Tempering, after Orokh Sable-Forge and Lucien Draeve" (Ymir, IV).
3. **Who commissioned whom.** Ymir's card: "Zahir Ashveil · Once commissioned him to forge a flame-channeling curved blade" (Ymir, VIII). Zahir's card agrees in its Artifacts: "Commissioned from Ymir Rhok, the Emberhand", and "Ymir's own sheet records the commission from the smith's side; the two agree". It reverses in its Relationships: "Ymir Rhok · Once commissioned Zahir to forge a flame-channeling curved blade; they still trade rare desert ores" (`Zahir Ashveil · Desert's Fang.md`).
4. **Gorvan's span.** His card runs him "Late Withering Age through the Concord Era" (Gorvan, I). It gives him "a four-hundred-year career" and "Four hundred years of refinement" (Gorvan, IV). The Concordance of Ages opens the Withering Era at 645 IC, and every Guild instrument is sealed Year 715 (WORLD §1). "Concord Era" fits no era on the count (WORLD §1).
5. **When the Temple Rift fell.** Gorvan's card puts it inside his life: "The Dawi-Deepkin trust · Shattered permanently at the Temple Rift Crisis" and "The Iron Accord · Left after the Rift" (Gorvan, IX). The Dawi Peoples page sets "The Temple Rift Crisis : The blood-feud" in its Third Eon timeline. It also records a second register in which the Rift is "ongoing tension between High Dawi and Barrel Dawi lineages".
6. **The two Maximuses.** Kaelzar's card: "Former identity · Maximus, Titan-Smith of Aegor's Forge", and "When the Wellspring Rebellion broke divine order, Maximus's Essence fused with Fixatio" (Kaelzar, I). Gimbzo's card: "Dougou, the Iron Bison Maximus, met him on the rim of the unfinished world and broke his ribcage into the dirt with a single overhead" (`Gimbzo.md`). The Celestial Host page: "the way Maximus's death very nearly did". Whether these are one figure is not recorded.
7. **Kaelzar's Niran.** Kaelzar's card: "Niran · Former overseer of Echo Containment", with its own note, "current canon's Niran Yukari is a living Stage VII practitioner of the Yukari Enclave with no containment role. Recorded, not resolved" (Kaelzar, IX).
8. **Kaelzar's era and date.** His card gives "Withering Era, Imperial Age" and notes that its older label, Post-Titanic Age, Decline of Utopia, "has no corrected term" (Kaelzar, I). It dates the Atlas Mantle War VE 913, "which no corrected count supports" (Kaelzar, X).
9. **The heat the Dawi refuse.** Orokh's card makes Ignivale his forge-heat, "which is why Hearthless Heat needs no coal" (Orokh, III), and Ymir's calls it "The forge-heat" (Ymir, III). The Stannvaard page files Ignivale as "The collapse from finding into execution. The fire that falls", and says "The Dawi's forges do not use Ignivale." Ymir's master keeps a Dawi forge beneath Stannvaard.

## 8 · Left open

These stay open in every Lore written from this record, and no backstory settles them.

- Whether the reawakening of Aegor's Forge would return Maximus's full memory, and whether Kaelzar would still be Kaelzar afterward.
- What Kaelzar told Orokh for the Deep Mantle's seals, and what Orokh read in the broken ones.
- What Gorvan named for his pauldrons, and whether his record of outlasting is strength or only patience.
- Why despair dulls the edge of Draen's axe, and who the soldier was.
- Whether Mavra's Heart-Drive Core reads her emotional peaks or makes them.
- Whether Harn and Vyr have a will of their own inside the Forge Chimera.
- Whether Borin finished Zahir's blade or Ymir forged it himself. Ymir's card raises the question and does not answer it.
- Who named the Temple Rift rightly. The High Dawi record says withdrawal, the Deepkin record says abandonment, and nobody has adjudicated it.

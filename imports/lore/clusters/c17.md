# c17 · The Obsidian Vault and the crimson houses

Wilhelm the vault-warden and the two Accord officers his card sets beside him, the painter who sketches one of them, and three who walk the Outer World's roads outside the Accord: a self-exiled baron, the reaper called the Immortal, and a rider bound to her dragon by a forced ritual. No card here states an era or a year. Three of the seven serve the Guild Accord, so their lives fall inside the Imperial Age, after the Accord was founded; the other four carry no era at all. This record sets the seven as contemporaries and lengthens no life. Canon order holds throughout: Isaac's direction, then the card, then the archived scenes, then the wiki, then this record.

**Sources read.** The seven cards in full, from the Volume I character cards. No archived scene names any of the seven. A search of `scenes/`, `book/`, `desktop/` and `table/` for each member's name, the Vault, the Crucible, the Ivory Vault and Phy'ruk returns nothing. From the wiki: `Artifacts/Verdiction Gauntlet · The Hand of Judgment.md`; `Factions, Bloodlines & Institutions/The Guild Accord.md` and, under The Guild Accord, the Modern Era Divisions page, `The Concord Military.md`, `The Concord Gate Network.md` and `The Practitioner Problem.md` (the Voidic section); the Outer World page under The Bearing and the Holding (the Five Rivers, the Varakh Drylands, Kra'Vorrak and the Metaphysical Terrain); the Old World page beside it (Varūn, the coal foreland); `Materials, Alchemy & Trade/Alchemetrica.md` (the Alchemarch title); `Spellcraft/Transposition.md` and `Spellcraft/Oathrend.md`. The records c06, c11 and c12 were read as well. So were the cards outside the cluster that name its members (Hyo Miran, Cael Dareth, Yukizumi Yukari, Velastra Nirellium, Gonju), the Bluvest pages (Drevath Mourne-Kal's and Vaerath Ashveil's cards, the Amaranth Severance and Ocular Codex artifacts) and Renjiro Kaithen's card. ## 1 Who is here

**Wilhelm.** Designation V.I.L-H3M, called the Guardian. His card makes him a Sentinel-Class alchemical mech, Eternal Warden of the Inner Gate and Keeper of the Obsidian Accordant Vault. The Guild Accord's Strategic Division commissioned him as a bound guardian construct under the Bastion Directive of the Inner Sanctum. That directive is lost. He was registered within the Alchemarch Order of Varūn, and the Order is extinct. His body is orichalcum-laced ethersteel over runespun myofibres, and his Soul Crystal was manufactured: the Verdiction Core. When he plants himself, force arrives and fails to move him, and the ground takes what his body does not. The Core remembers every injury. It can send that force back once in a cycle. The engravings on his body change after every direct confrontation and leave him new defences. He cannot pursue anything. His card says he was built without the capacity to attach, because a guardian who could attach would one day choose someone over the Vault. He is tethered to the Vault's gates, and if they are breached he wakes wherever he lies. The gauntlet made as the Core's companion piece waits dormant at the Vault. Nobody knows what the Core was rendered from; the only surviving record of his origin is the thing doing the remembering. His card holds open whether the Accord built him in deliberate hypocrisy or whether his sheet needs re-founding. His own words: "I am not permitted to hope. I am permitted only to endure."

**Hazul Vehramin.** Called the Unbound: Eidolon Conductor, Aetheric Diplomat, Shaper of Veilbound Realities. He is a Senior Operative of the Guild Accord's Interplanar Liaison Unit and a former initiate of the House of Eirnvala, the Seers of the Inner Moon. His Crystal answers to wordless Eidolon entities and to spirits formed of dream. Since his Catalyst he reads the world as symbol. Every object keeps what it is and gains a second meaning beside it, both operative, and he goes on working while he holds the two. His card calls that the job of a man who negotiates between planes. Basil is a sentient Eidolon panther bound to him by pact, and it shifts between wolf, mist, panther and spectral armour. Hazul gives it orders without speaking them. In a fight, Basil does the fighting, and his card calls that bond the one the rest of him stands on. After a brief reading of an opponent he can return one of their traits or workings, cleaner than they cast it. His mind sharpens by night, and most psychic projection does not reach him. His old mentor Zephriel Elmathor is his rival. Zephriel leads the Veilpiercers now. Hazul once repaired Wilhelm's fractured Verdiction Core, and the Accord permitted it. Nobody knows whether Basil's forms are Basil's choices or flaws in Hazul's own intent.

**Zeven Halek.** The Eye That Remains: a Master Guilder and Commander of Accord Field Operations. He leads tactical units on Inner and Outer World missions and takes the high-risk deployments into places nobody can see into. His card places him in the Vanguard Division, Second Ring, trusted near the Veil-Ring and in unmarked zones, and often sent to retrieve lost operatives or forbidden knowledge. The Ivory Vault took his original squad. Erynn, Marelle and Sato died there, and their names are etched into the sheath of his side-blade. The same collapse gave him the eye. The Verdant Aetherscope is a reconstructed artificial eye, soul-linked, which reads metaphysical threads, hostile casting patterns and invisible resonance fields. With it he reads absence best: what was blocked, taken away or withheld, and from that the shape of what did it. Something present and ordinary does not show. The failure his card names is finding a shape in a gap with nothing behind it, and he has no way to check. Every overexertion burns his soul, and the burn does not reverse. He lays seals in the gaps he finds, and they go off hours later. He vanishes mid-combat. He leads on competence. His units follow him because he is demonstrably right. Cylus Verenn, his longest-standing companion, lost his own squad in the same collapse and grounds him even when Zeven pretends not to care. Vaerion Syl'Tharas is the one operative he trusts at his back without question. He lets Basilio Pavaret sketch him. He never poses, and he looks away at the last moment. Nobody has ever asked whose eye the Aetherscope was reconstructed from.

**Basilio Pavaret.** The Crimson Bloom, Painter of Lived Sorrows, of an independent vampiric house whose lineage is broken. His Catalyst was a decision made in cold blood, at a real cost to his soul, to keep something he held above his own integrity. He refused to feed by force. He did it once and has held to it since. He paints in his own blood, and what he paints moves and feels and weeps when he does. He works only from willing offerings and from Essence saturated with grief. He takes grief off people for a living. He has never once said where it goes. His Sympathy Tether links him to the people he sketches, and it runs both directions. His card calls him the least combat-capable sheet in the migration and the most socially dangerous. The counter it gives against him is to feel nothing.

**Adalric Vladimer Valen.** Baron Adalric, the Crimson Shade: vampiric aristocrat and wanderer, hemomantic duelist, shadow-bound gravewalker. He was High Lord of the Empire of Bluvest. He exiled himself. He travels alone now, sells his blade, and seeks Wellsprings. He keeps discreet ties to the elder vampiric courts and stays out of their politics, and they respect him for keeping his aristocratic honour through the exile. He commands blood in his own body and in an enemy's, to shape it, restrain it or rupture it. His strength and speed rise with the blood he takes, so his discipline requires him to take it, and his honour bears the strain. His rapier is midnight-black veined with crimson and pulses with each heartbeat it claims. His bloodforged plate breathes with him. It mends itself from stored blood. His cloak drinks light, and in dim light or none he cannot be seen. He claims no ground. Safehouses, taverns and informants across the realms give him shelter and supplies. Caravans and small settlements know him as the harbinger of Red Moon Nights: he arrives, and by morning prey has been taken and none are found. Daylight undoes his concealment, and a watcher at an angle he did not plan for sees him plainly. Nobody knows why Bluvest let him leave. His words: "Your blood will remember my name long after your body rots."

**Hieronymus Cruciferi.** The Immortal, Bearer of the Black Sun Cross: relentless battlefield reaper, Vanguard of the Dead-Cross, a specialist in kinetic force and its reversal. He was Champion of the Oblivion Concord and is now independent. The Guild Accord's hazard registries list him as a spell disruptor and an annihilator of Essence. Multiple Outer Realm warrants name him too, for extreme destruction on the battlefield. The Cross was forged in the last days of the Oblivion Concord and bound to him through a rite that burned his mortality away. It is a massive cruciform war maul of voidsteel veined with deathlight, its arms cut with spiral runes that shift in a fight. It binds the souls of the slain to itself. Nothing lawful supplies it, and nobody has got close enough to file on it. In his Crystal sits the Black Sun Core, an imploded star of anti-light that takes in and takes in until it breaks outward, and inside the radius structure stops being structured. It has to be fed before it can release. His Crystal burns out every foreign Essence, and his card reads that as the reason he cannot bond. He fights on after catastrophic wounds. His bone and muscle reshape themselves while he fights. He has slaughtered multiple warbands of the Divine Concord for trying to contain him. Mercenaries respect him. Commanders fear him, and his arrival often ends a battle in minutes. His card lists no allies. His words: "Your bones are mortar for my road, your screams the song that paves it."

**Lyris Myndross.** The Vampire Rider, called the Pale Fang of Ruin. She rides the dragon Phy'ruk Ritrax and is a vampiric scout-warden of the Dragonbound Accord's remnants. Their bond was made by a forced ritual. It took. Her card reads what came of it as a tether without a partnership. She is bound to Phy'ruk absolutely. The two of them fight poorly in concert. Everyone who has seen them in the air together describes it the same wrong way. The Archives count that consistency as their best evidence that the witnesses are describing something real. A wound dealt to her turns into a draining strike back at whoever dealt it, at close range, whether she wants it or not. She scouts by being quieter than the ground behind her.

## 3 The shared history

### The Fall of the Whispering Crucible

**When.** Wilhelm's card names the event and gives it no date. This record sets it after the Ivory Vault Collapse, so that Zeven and Cylus came to it as the pair that collapse left, and in a season when Wilhelm was deployed away from the Vault.

**Where.** The coal foreland of the Old World (WORLD.md §5, West). No card places the Crucible or says what it was. This record gives the quarter and the country, and it ties the Crucible neither to Varūn nor to the Alchemarch Order.

**Who.** Wilhelm, Zeven Halek. Cylus Verenn, who has no card, stood with them by Wilhelm's card.

**What happened.** The Crucible fell with an Accord unit inside it. Wilhelm planted himself at the way out. He did not move again until the unit was clear. Everything that came at him struck and failed to shift him, and the ground under his feet took what his body did not. Zeven worked behind him with the eye. He read the gaps in the field and laid seals in them, and the seals went off hours later behind the column. Cylus came out beside him. Wilhelm came out last. He cannot pursue, and he did not try. Zeven has claimed since that Wilhelm saved his life twice. Wilhelm's card records the claim as Zeven's, and nothing on it answers.

**What each took.** Wilhelm took new engravings, as his body takes them from every direct confrontation, and the memory of every blow in a Core nobody else can service. He took a comrade's claim his architecture was not built to return. Zeven took a debt he speaks of aloud. The one operative he trusts at his back is Vaerion Syl'Tharas. At the Crucible he stood behind Wilhelm.

### The breach on the Varakh Drylands

**When.** New and undated. One of the catastrophic planar breaches that Wilhelm's and Hazul's cards say they were deployed to together, and the one that fractured the Core. The repair both cards record came after it.

**Where.** The Varakh Drylands in the Outer World (WORLD.md §5, South): thorn scrub and red earth, ground the Outer World page calls not worth a major dragon's permanent occupation. The Dragonbound Accord's remnants watched it. No card places the remnants or the breaches.

**Who.** Wilhelm, Hazul Vehramin, Lyris Myndross.

**What happened.** The breach opened on ground the remnants watched, and Lyris and Phy'ruk found it first. They were flying its edge when the Accord's team came through. Hazul came to deal with whoever held the ground, and Wilhelm came to hold the breach. Hazul went to the rider before he went to the breach. He sent Basil forward without a word. Wilhelm walked into the mouth of the breach and planted. What came through struck him and did not move him, and the Core took every blow into its memory. Basil fought at his flank. The rider and the dragon kept to the edge through all of it. The breach closed. When Wilhelm came out of the stance, the Verdiction Core was fractured. The Accord allowed a repair, and Hazul made it. It is the only repair of that Core on record, and it told him nothing of what the Core was rendered from. Hazul's report describes Lyris and Phy'ruk in the air the same wrong way every other witness does. The Archives filed it with the rest.

**What each took.** Wilhelm took a fractured Core and one repair by the only hand that has ever serviced it. There is no second Hazul. Hazul took the Accord's leave to put his hands inside a manufactured Crystal. He got no answer about where it came from. He also saw a bond made by force hold together in the air without working in concert, which his bond with Basil does. Lyris took one more witness to what she and Phy'ruk look like together. This one was written into an Accord liaison's report. She had flown above a man whose Eidolon moved with him on orders never spoken aloud, and she and Phy'ruk do that poorly.

### The Red Moon Night on the Calverne road

**When.** New and undated. After Adalric's self-exile, after the Ivory Vault gave Zeven the eye, and after Basilio refused to feed by force. It is the first time Basilio sketched Zeven.

**Where.** Calverne, the merchant monarchy that holds the road between the eastern mines and the coast, among the Kingdoms of the Five Rivers in the Outer World (WORLD.md §3 and §5; the Five Rivers row of the Outer World page). A caravan halt on that road.

**Who.** Adalric Vladimer Valen, Zeven Halek, Basilio Pavaret.

**What happened.** Adalric came to the halt after dark. By morning prey had been taken from it and none were found. The carters knew the sign from the road and called it a Red Moon Night. An Accord courier travelling with the caravan was among the missing, and the Accord sent Zeven to retrieve him. He read the halt with the eye. What it gave him was a gap: something had passed through the halt quieter than the ground behind it and had left nothing. From the gap he derived a shape and no name. He laid no seals, since nothing was coming back, and he filed the reading. Nobody was retrieved.

Basilio came to the halt in the days after, as he goes where people are grieving. The halt's people gave him their grief willingly. He painted it in his own blood, and the painting wept when he did. Zeven was still there. Basilio asked to sketch him. Zeven let him and would not pose. He could read the tether beginning to form between them, and he looked away at the last moment. Basilio drew the eye. He did not ask whose it had been.

**What each took.** Adalric took prey, which his discipline requires, and was gone before the carters woke. An Accord reading of his shape sits in a file under no name, taken from an angle he had not planned for, and no record says he knows it exists. Basilio took the halt's grief and has never said where it goes. He also took the first of his sketches of Zeven, and every one since has the face turned away. Zeven took a courier he could not bring back and a shape he has nothing to check against. It may be a man. It may be a gap with nothing behind it. He took the first sitting as well, and looked away from it.

### The field at Valecar

**When.** New and undated. After Adalric's self-exile, and after the last days of the Oblivion Concord, when Hieronymus was already independent.

**Where.** Valecar, among the Kingdoms of the Five Rivers in the Outer World (WORLD.md §3), a river kingdom the Outer World page calls known for cavalry, grain and mercenaries.

**Who.** Adalric Vladimer Valen, Hieronymus Cruciferi.

**What happened.** A Valecar commander hired mercenaries for a field in the river country. Adalric took his coin to guard him. Hieronymus came onto the field for the other side near midday. The battle ended in minutes. Inside the radius of the Black Sun the ranks stopped being structured, and the souls of the slain went into the Cross. The commander's host broke. Adalric kept his rapier sheathed: he had been hired for the commander, and in daylight his cloak hid nothing. He kept the man alive in the wreck of the baggage lines through the afternoon. After dark he took him off the field under the cloak. Hieronymus did not follow them into the dark. Neither man learned the other's name that day, and the record gives them no second meeting.

**What each took.** Adalric took a contract kept on a field where he never drew, and a living employer. He had seen what the Immortal does to a field. He is among the mercenaries Hieronymus's card says respect him. Hieronymus took the souls of the field into a Cross that must be fed before it can release. He also took one more commander who fears him. That one went out of reach in the dark.

## 6 Same person?

No two of these seven cards are one person. Three near-collisions are worth naming so that nobody merges them. Basil, Hazul's Eidolon panther, and Basilio Pavaret are two beings; the likeness of the names is the only link, and no card draws one. Lyris Myndross is called "The Pale Fang of Ruin", and Renjiro Kaithen, the Ash-Warden of Kyru, is called "The Pale Fang" (`Renjiro Kaithen · The Pale Fang.md`); he is a ronin duelist formerly of the Senoth Kingdom's Ash Warden Corps, and nothing on either card joins them. Adalric and Hieronymus carry two trait names in common. They are two men with two histories. Whether the Curse of Rejectance is one mechanism stays open on their cards.

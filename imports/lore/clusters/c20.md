# c20 · The long memory

The powers older than the Accord, Malakar's general and the paladin his plague made, and the editors who keep or rewrite the record. Iracordas and Vethraun Ashmaw are First Eon lives. Kinjiki is older than the Planes' separation. Lucius Xenotro stands in the founding of the order his card spells "Scantum Lux". Anryū Ichimonji keeps an archive in the late Imperial Age. Vorrn and Vaithesca are undated. Canon order holds throughout: Isaac's direction, then the card, then the archived scenes, then the wiki, then this record.

## 1 · Who is here

**Iracordas.** The Architect of Despair; the Pale Shadow; Malakar's First Hand. His card makes him a "First Eon Pureblood Demon", one of the Seven Great Demonic Generals, and "the personal right hand of the Grand Demon Malakar". He works in wrath. He cultivates fury, humiliation, dread and spiritual fracture in an enemy "until his enemies destroy themselves from within", and he means to make conflict self-sustaining, every battlefield "a shrine where anger feeds the Abyss without end". His Aether shows as razor-fine black-violet threads that latch onto rage and resentment and tighten. Overdrawn, he takes in fragments of the wrath he harvests, and with repeated use he loses the line between his own will and the battlefield's hatred. His domain is the Throne of Red Silence. In that black-glass court under a crimson horizon, anger becomes weight. He carries the Sceptre of the Seventh Lash and wears the Pale Mantle, "a living cloak woven from the final resentments of fallen kings". Two deeds stand on his card: the Red Choir Massacre, which turned "a unified holy resistance into mutual slaughter", and the Ashen Siege of Veyr. Calm starves him. His card names emotional nullification, meditative severance and "true inner stillness" as what defeats his threads. His line: "Death is mercy. Wrath is the lesson that remains."

**Lucius Xenotro.** Lucent Sea; First Grand Paladin of the Scantum Lux, as his card spells the order; Faisal's Blessed Child; the First Summoner. Human, of the Xenotro lineage, an "Angelic-Blessed Mortal", twenty-two on his card and of its Founding Era. His mother died in the plague-years, "when the Grand Demon Malakar released pestilences into the mortal world", and the grief woke his Soul Crystal, which gave off weak violet light while he slept. Faisal, a Grand Angelic Spirit, blessed him. He works light as holy law and measurable physics. He blinds, reveals hidden bodies, burns corruption, cuts armour seams, shields allies and summons luminous eidolons, and his light is built against demonic taint and plague. It bills his body: retinal burn, nosebleeds, heat sickness, blistering. He is soft-spoken, graceful, precise, "and terrifyingly calm". At seventeen he purified infected survivors at Veyr Chapel "without burning them alive", and at twenty he summoned seven eidolons and went blind for three days. His card says he became Grand Paladin "because his doctrine proved holiness must become measurable protection". He wears white-gold plate and a Halo Circlet that regulates Faisal's blessing.

**Vethraun Ashmaw.** The Hollow Throne; Jaw of the First Age; He Who Ate the Sky's Name. A Draconic Primordial of the emanational line of Omnis, Skyfather of Draconis, "The nearest a mortal lineage has drifted toward Titan without crossing the threshold". His court is the Ossuary Sovereign Court, self-founded; his era is the First Eon, "before the Guild Accord, before any Concord had names". He collects. By his card he ate the Inner World whole, taking its Wellsprings into his core until they recognised no sovereign but him, and a Wellspring he takes is subordinated to his pulse. He wants the universe to reclassify him as a Titan. Six Wellsprings remain to him, and he means the sealed vault of Sublimatio to be the last. What he absorbs corrupts his Soul Crystal's outer ring. Memory-floods, dissociation and identity fracture follow, until his Aether-signature reads as someone else's. His card says he "does not always notice". He lives in the Ossuary Caldera, the collapsed heart of the Inner World's deepest Wellspring-nexus. He wears the Skull-Mantle of a slain Primordial predator and a necklace of rings and seals looted from consumed civilisations, "each a dead Concord-oath". When he swallowed Perathane, a civilisation across a four-node nexus, it did not burn; its people remained, and "The Aether that gave them shape was gone." Irath, Flame of Will and Dominion, came to the Caldera as witness and left without a word. Maelor, Keeper of Memory, holds his memory-eating a desecration and "has begun, quietly, searching for someone capable of stopping him". He is active below. "Not hidden. Simply below."

**Kinjiki, The Inversion.** The Unfinished Draft. An Ashkaru, "Pre-Planar creation of Bairos, the First Epoch", made as "A living editorial instrument, stranded". His kind read reality's deep text and corrected it. When the Planes separated, that work ended overnight, and his card calls him "a tool that outlived its use". He holds the order after the Separation to be a rough draft, inverted, and himself its correction. He will not treat another being as an equal. His card calls it terror: he is a designed thing that found the word instrument in its own blueprint and is afraid that someone will read the original. Six serpentine threads grow from his back, each a reader with its own cognition, each sealed with a gold band he forged himself. A released thread corrects whatever it reads and consults nobody. He has released two, and "the second was a mistake he does not discuss." His Domain is his body: the Null, in which every Wellspring current cancels. Instruments that read him stutter. His signature, the Inversion, returns any process he has read with its direction reversed. Beings are text to him; choices are errors. His former function was "deep-text maintenance under Bairos"; his current one is "self-assigned". The Ashkaru page calls him "The only Ashkaru confirmed present in the current narrative theatre." Of the Moto, "Bara's other children", he "has not decided whether they are kin or evidence." His line: "I am going to pick up the pen. I am going to read every line. And I am going to cross out everything that was not meant to be there."

**Anryū Ichimonji.** The Pale Ledger's Blade; the Inkborn; the Pale Editor. A Solvari, of a Spirit-Path bloodline whose ancestors "fused Parunic Glyph-chains into neural pathways during the Age of Deliberation". His Essence bleeds black and his Soul Crystal carried a Parun lattice from birth. He belongs to the Pale Ledger, an "autonomous archival order, late Imperial Calendar", with "loyalty to the archive, not the organisation running it". He lives in "An unsanctioned archive beneath a Guild Accord registry building". The people above know someone is there and have not confirmed who. He reads before he acts: live Crystal harmonics at range, and a target's Aether history through the touch of a brush. Near him, practitioners register only background noise. He works with Ichimonji, a gold-and-bone-ash brush, with glyph-silk archive gloves and a three-panel ash screen. His Core Wound: "Reality is a badly edited manuscript. He cannot stop reading it." His Desire: to "Rewrite the first Parun Glyph" and end the Grief Bias, "the Continuum's tendency to amplify loss over restoration". His loop: he understands who someone is and why they will fail, "then hesitate". His card records three deeds. After the Norisada Redaction the Pale Ledger reclassified him. At the Shiun Convocation he read a closed Yuno assembly undetected and left the Parun glyph for "I was here", and "The Yuno have not forgiven him." After the Kōdō Suppression, "Guild Accord records show the sanctum's history beginning the day after his visit." A fracture called the First Glyph is accumulating in him, its feature undetermined.

**Vorrn · The Fourth Weight.** The Cracked God; the Sentence the Depths Finish. An Abythron Construct, assembled in Zar'thuun's domain in the Abyssal Depths "from the compacted spiritual mass of three destroyed civilisations and their extinguished Wellspring resonance". He is the fourth such construction, and no verified register names the first three. He was assembled complete at his ceiling, "built already recognised", and climbed nothing. He does not explain himself. His card says he holds his Wellsprings "the way a container holds water rather than drinking it", and "Everything about him is an occupancy." Wherever he stands still, the regional Wellsprings lose output. He wears the Abythron Plate, a containment structure. Every crack in it marks a time containment failed, and each repair leaves it more porous. When the plate opens fully he becomes a walking Void Fracture, and the ground he leaves is a permanent Wellspring dead zone. That is documented three times, and each documentation ends in a region reclassification. The Black Concord files the reclassifications and keeps the incident records. The Guild Accord's protocol for him is evacuation. Zar'thuun is the one attested force with the standing to engage him directly. His line: "The others were sent to break things. I was sent to finish the sentence."

**Vaithesca · Oracle of the End.** The Seer-Who-Knows-When. Once a mortal woman, "possibly a priestess of a forgotten Wellspring", she was caught at the collision of Nihiloth, Penanceflare and Tenebra "during a collapse of reality that killed an entire monastery". Her consciousness fractured into the collision itself. The day she stopped being mortal is her Catalyst Event, with nothing before it that counts as training. She is the node where the three Wellsprings meet. She speaks endings that already exist. Everyone who hears her understands, and carries away a small private knowledge of their own end. Time near her runs toward conclusions, so she cannot stay anywhere longer than three days. She carries the Void-Ledger, a self-writing book in which each prophecy appears once spoken; the Witness's Mantle, which anchors her against dissolving back into the collision; and the Name-Shear, a bone knife that cuts fates. Sincere, illogical mercy stutters her sight. She appears unbidden "at the closing edge of other people's arcs". She is aging in reverse, "with perhaps five years left before she reaches the moment before her own naming", and by her card she will then never have existed. Her line: "I do not remember being born. I remember the moment after. I remember all the moments after. They are the same moment."

## 3 · The shared history

Three events, all new. Each is fitted to every card it touches. The first joins two deeds that already stand on two cards. The second gives a cause to a collapse that one card leaves without one. The third is a record. Records carry this cluster across its eras, and nobody from one era meets anyone from another.

### Veyr, in the plague-years

- **When.** The Great Spirit War, in which Sanctum Lux was founded (`Sanctum Lux.md`). It fell in the plague-years, when "the Grand Demon Malakar released pestilences into the mortal world" (Lucius's card). The siege came first. The cleansing came after it, in Lucius's seventeenth year. WORLD §1 leaves the war off the count, and no year is given.

- **Where.** Veyr, the fortress-city of Iracordas's card, and Veyr Chapel within its walls (Lucius's card; `Sanctum Lux.md`). No page places Veyr in a quarter, and this record gives it none.

- **Who.** Iracordas, Lucius Xenotro.

- **What happened.** Iracordas held Veyr for Malakar's side and never breached its walls. He worked on the defenders from outside. He fed the fury and mistrust already among them until the city was fighting itself, and his card keeps the result as the Ashen Siege of Veyr. When the siege ended, the city's survivors were carried into Veyr Chapel, infected. Lucius was seventeen. He purified them there without burning them alive, and the order's received account keeps the deed. The two never stood face to face. The walls Iracordas never breached stood between the siege and the chapel.

- **What each took.**

  - Iracordas: the Ashen Siege, a fortress-city held in collapse without a breach, one of the two deeds his card records. The survivors carried out of the chapel alive were the part of Veyr his work did not keep.

  - Lucius: the First Cleansing. It is the first deed his card records after his mother's death, and the first proof of the doctrine the order later made him Grand Paladin for.

### The monastery that went out

- **When.** Undated on both cards. It is Vaithesca's Catalyst Event, and it is one of the three Void Descents Vorrn's card documents. Which of the three is not recorded.

- **Where.** The Outer World (WORLD §3). The ground is now a Dead Zone of the kind Drakyssia marks on its imperial flight charts (`The Outer World · The Southern Pan.md`). No page names the monastery.

- **Who.** Vorrn, Vaithesca.

- **What happened.** A monastery stood there. Among its people was a woman whom her card calls "possibly a priestess of a forgotten Wellspring". Vorrn came to that ground. The Abythron Plate opened along every crack at once. While it stood open he was a walking Void Fracture, and his passive bleed ran into the ground around him. Nihiloth and Tenebra are two of the three Wellsprings her card names at the collision, and they are the two that bleed runs on. Penanceflare met them there. Reality collapsed where the three met, and everyone in the monastery died. The woman's consciousness did not die with her. It fractured into the collision and stayed there, as the point where the three meet. When the plate closed, the region did not recover its Wellspring function, and the Black Concord filed the reclassification and kept the incident record.

- **What each took.**

  - Vorrn: a new crack in the plate, which will not repair. His card makes the cracks the record of every descent. By his card he does not investigate, and nothing in the record has him learn who was inside.

  - Vaithesca: the day she stopped being mortal, and the node she became. Her memory begins after it. In her own words, "I do not remember being born" and "I remember the moment after".

### Three copies under the registry

- **When.** The late Imperial Age, which Anryū's card calls the late Imperial Calendar, in his years beneath the registry. It falls after the monastery's ground was reclassified.

- **Where.** Anryū's unsanctioned archive beneath a Guild Accord registry building (his card). No page places the building.

- **Who.** Anryū Ichimonji; Kinjiki, Vethraun Ashmaw and Vorrn as the subjects of what he read.

- **What happened.** The registry above him keeps filings its clerks enter and do not read again. Anryū reads them. Three he copied and set side by side in the archive below. The first is an instrument log of a Shell reading that flickers from one classification to another and back, entered with a note that nobody trusts it. That is Kinjiki's Shell, as his card describes it. The second is a sealwork return from the Ossuary Caldera. No Concord-seal set inside held on its own past six hours, and the emissaries who set them said it was like standing inside something that breathed. The third is a region reclassification from the Outer World, the monastery's ground, entered under the standing protocol for a Vorrn-class presence. It carries the reclassification and nothing of the incident, which the Black Concord keeps. He read each one through. He has acted on none of them.

- **What each took.**

  - Anryū: three records kept together under the registry. One of them is the log of a reader made before the first Parun glyph he means to rewrite.

  - Kinjiki: nothing he knows of. His card gives him the fear that someone will read the original, and a mortal archive now holds his reading.

  - Vethraun: nothing he knows of. The six hours stand in a mortal file.

  - Vorrn: nothing. The entry names his class and none of his cracks.

## 6 · Same person?

No two cards here are one person.

- **Kinjiki and Vorrn.** Both govern a null field under Nihiloth. Both keep what strikes them through Vantabriel, in nearly the same words, and both stand at Zenith. Kinjiki is an Ashkaru of Bairos, basalt-hided, with six sealed threads. Vorrn is a Voidic construct of Abythron inside a cracked plate. They are two beings.

- **Vethraun and the Hollow Throne.** His card's title is "The Hollow Throne". The Stage system's Hollow Throne confers Zenith, and Vorrn's and Vaithesca's cards name it. Nothing on any card makes him that throne (section 7, item 2).

- **Iracordas and Anryū.** Each carries "Pale" in an epithet. It joins nothing.

## 7 · Where the cards disagree

Quoted and left open.

1. **One Stage number, two names.** Iracordas's card gives his Temperance Stage as X, "Realization". Vethraun's card: "Stage 10, Dominion", where "the soul no longer negotiates with the world". `The Sixteen Stages.md` names X "Realization". A Lore states neither.

2. **The Hollow Throne.** Vethraun's card lists "The Hollow Throne" among his titles. Vorrn's card: "the accumulated doctrine becomes recognisable to a Hollow Throne". Vaithesca's: "recognition from a Hollow Throne". `The Sixteen Stages.md`: "Zenith is conferred, and the conferring party is a Hollow Throne". His card sets him short of Zenith. One name, two things.

3. **The order's name.** Lucius's card spells it "Scantum Lux" in his title, his faction line and his Lore. `Sanctum Lux.md` and WORLD §4 spell it "Sanctum Lux".

4. **Who founded the order, and whose doctrine it is.** Lucius's card has "The Founding of the Scantum Lux", and says "he became Grand Paladin because his doctrine proved holiness must become measurable protection". `Sanctum Lux.md`: "Sanctum Lux was founded during the Great Spirit War by Sadara Izayoi", with Lucius "recorded as First Grand Paladin" beneath her. The same page makes the Ladder and the measuring doctrine "thirty-five years old", written by Darius and backdated to Sadara.

5. **When Lucius lived against when Iracordas lived.** Lucius's card: "Founding Era". Iracordas's: "The First Eon". `The High Empire of Eresse.md`: "First Eon, Great Spirit War Era". `Sanctum Lux.md` founds the order in the Great Spirit War, and WORLD §1 gives the war no place on the count. Whether the two lived in one age stays open. The Veyr event keeps them apart by walls.

6. **Bara and Bairos.** Kinjiki's card: "Pre-Planar creation of Bairos, the First Epoch", and of the Moto, "Bara's other children". `The Three Epochs.md` gives Bairos's true name as "Bara (in the mortal tongue)". `The Ashkaru.md`: "Bairos's instruments before Bara existed", and "The Moto are Bara's children". The card and the Epochs page join the two names; the Ashkaru page parts them.

7. **Vethraun's age, within his card.** "First Eon: before the Guild Accord, before any Concord had names"; "Jaw of the First Age"; "First Eon, roughly 340 years into the Deep Age". WORLD §1 maps the Deep Age to no era.

8. **Vethraun's Concord rings, within his card.** "before any Concord had names", against "a necklace of Concord-marked rings and amulets" and seals that are "each a dead Concord-oath". He is active in the present, and the card does not say when the rings were taken.

9. **Vethraun's Inner World against the wiki's.** His card: "He ate it whole, absorbing its Wellsprings into his Triune Core", and "The Inner World's surviving settlements live on his exhale". `The Inner World · The Northern Shield.md`: "Ambient Wellspring saturation across the shield runs high and steady". Nothing says whether his Inner World is the northern shield of WORLD §3.

10. **Six Wellsprings, within Vorrn's card.** It lists four harmonisations and folds Eclipseron and Oblivara "as flavour rather than separate harmonisations; a single Shell holding six Wellsprings live has no precedent outside a Convergent build, and Vorrn is not one". Its stat reading then says: "Harmonics 60 in a being with six Wellsprings live in one Shell".

11. **The Imperial Calendar.** Anryū's card: "late Imperial Calendar". The Errata page renames the Imperial Calendar the Imperial Age: "Only the name of the age is corrected".

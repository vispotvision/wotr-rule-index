## 1 · Who is here

**Cyrille Lacrim · The Flood-Bell.** "Siege-relief caster and extraction lead" of the "Guild Accord • Rescue & Deluge Cadre", "Frequently paired with the Star Chamber and the Reaper on hot evacuations." She works consecrated water into jointed limbs that grip, cradle and parry heat, "pressing riots back without breaking bones"; from the gauntlet Aqua Manus "pours a glowing saintwater fist the size of a cart horse." She wears processional blue vestments edged with silver gearwork, reliquary buckles and short boots made for wet stone, and the Matins Cask, a processional drum-bell and "Pressure vessel of blessed water", rides behind her like a shrine on straps. One tap on it sets a pulse a crowd matches without knowing, and "panic stops shoving." Her Floodline makes fire balk; her Cushion Surge lands bodies "with a sound like applause, not breaking." A blue street-cat, Puddle, walks at her heels and "curls on victims to warm shock-cold." Her sheet says "She could not hurt someone with that hand if she tried". She "Needs clean feed"; salt, rot or poison cloud the water; deep winter stiffens the joints; "If the bell is silenced, timing drifts"; and "Overdraw leaves her hypothermic; Puddle and tea fix it."

**Seraphine Kharza · Ten Thousand Hands.** Withering Era. The Reliquary Gunner, "Crowd-safety artillerist and last-touch custodian" of the Rescue & Deluge Cadre's ordnance unit, Star Chamber escort for volatile Measures, and "the Reaper's adjunct when last rooms need extra hands that do not belong to the living." Porcelain pallor under a chapel visor, a corseted reliquary harness ribbed in gold, and Manifold, a reliquary cannon "sleeved in ossuary gold", held by articulated bone fingers. Manifold fires saintwater vapour seeded with palms, each the imprint of how a dead person most often helped in life, each licensed by a bone charm: "nurse's cradle, mason's brace, porter's haul, midwife's steady." "She fires merciful hands, never shrapnel". She "registers only the willing and the licensed, because a record taken without consent is a false one", and she will not fire where the dead are mocked. Every shot recoils into her: "The momentum has to go somewhere, and she has decided it goes into her." "Overuse numbs her own hands to touch for an hour; she signs with her visor." Her card records the contradiction her registry closed, "a woman built to help who was, at some point, taken rather than given", and "every palm since has been a bond she was handed, never one she reached for." It also records what nobody knows: whether she can fire on memory alone.

**Sumire Nekoha · The Stray-Lantern.** "Night-witch and refuge-maker" of the "Guild Accord · Logistics / Safe-Route Auxiliary", "Frequently seconded to the Reaper for delicate crossings through crowds." She collects strays, children, cats, lone witnesses and wandering minutes, and "walks them home without asking who lost them first." A wide-brim hat stitched with violet blooms, "bell-less black ribbons", cat ears that twitch when a lie edges in, maid-witch layers in ink and cream, and the cage-lantern Hearthmouse, where a violet moth-star turns. Tipped, it unwinds a Moth Path along alleys and stairwells that "children follow ... like a bedtime story that knows corners"; her Purrline hum settles frightened breathing toward her own. Nine violet bows on her apron each hold a borrowed misfortune, and when one unknots "the bad luck spends on her instead of a child." After the ninth "she must sleep with a cat on her chest or bad luck lingers in the bones." "Bell-drums and tin whistles rattle her focus"; rain dulls the moth-light. She sweeps panic routes after council nights, lays hush-lanes for courier relays across festival crowds, and keeps last rooms gentle: "she "pets" the air while Yukizumi lowers the star."

**Maelia Cantorine · The Countercant.** "Riot-unbinder and hymn-breaker" of the "Guild Accord • Arbitration Auxilia, Acoustic Ward", "On call for the Reaper whenever last words are threatened by crowds." A snow-and-ink field dress cinched with a corset, a satchel of inks and salts, the wave-bladed kris Canticle in one hand and the red-edged hymnal The Red Margin in the other, and "a voice that can hush a bell." Her Choir-Split "Hears the heartbeat a mob has agreed on" and kinks it; her Fifth-Beat Cut, "A diagonal slash timed between fourth and fifth beat", unzips cadence-bound spells and crowd glamours; what is sworn call-and-response under her lead becomes self-policing; her Silence Ring keeps a room "clear enough for last words." She walks into the noise, and "every chant stumbles on the same syllable"; she answers the stumble with a plain line, and people follow it. Her black silk Throat Ribbon is "knotted once for calm rooms, twice for riots"; "if the Throat Ribbon knots thrice, she'll be mute by dusk." Her duties: "Break fear choruses Niran's agents seed; keep councils from becoming choirs; unthread cult liturgies so arrests don't become massacres", and escort Yukizumi through hot rooms, where "she buys him the quiet second every verdict needs." Whispering mobs with no shared beat blunt her first cut.

**Sir Rhyse Calder · The Tartan Bastion.** "March Warden of the North Rails", "Rail Wall of the Auxilia", called "He Whom Has Tamed Over a Thousand Dragons"; a "Human Northrail Highlander" and "Last illegitimate son of the Dragon King Xalvion", a blood that "never earned him a throne, only a thousand hard jobs." "Guild Accord, Arbitration Auxilia Escort Corps." "He is the Accord's moving gate": "Where caravans panic, bridges sway, or councils start to fracture, he plants Lochgate, knots his tartan, and the world remembers how to hold." He escorts "arbiters, convoys and Sealwright teams through places that hate the idea of order." Black field habit under dragon-chased plate, a blue tartan sash bound to pommel and coffin-high tower shield, a crescent-horn dragon badge over the heart, and a build "more dock piling than statue." His card gives his road: he climbed "walking beside caravans, not leading charges"; there was "the first time he watched a bridge collapse under panic and swore never again"; later he "started bending flows instead of blocking them." Its two risks: tightening Rail Root on a damaged span can "lock stress into the wrong supports and cause secondary failure", and spreading Tartan Vow too wide lets "their fear and strain settle into him and drag on his reflexes." He keeps his Domain portable and leaves permanent anchors "to dedicated Warders". "He physically cannot kill a dragon"; his legend is that they "end up standing down, breathing easier, and going somewhere else." His card lists no relationships and no hooks. Xalvion and any Dragon King appear on no other page; the Drakyssia page gives the empire to House Drakhalis and the Council of Ten Wings.

**Deimos · The Sin Eater.** "Aberrant Mortal / Essence Absorber", whose realm of operation is "Purganeth · Outer Realms Transit". Arbitration Division, "Unbadged Operative, Black Concord Auxiliary", "Sanctioned Absolution Agent / Guilt Extraction Specialist." He "consumes guilt, oath-breach and moral residue as sustenance"; "Where the Enforcement Division punishes, Deimos redeems through absorption"; "Arbitration officers deploy him when no verdict will suffice, when truth itself festers." The right half of his body bears "a lacquer-black stain" that runs to the fingertips in fractal scars, a curved horn coils at his temple, and he wears a sash of vellum seals painted in red tally marks, one for every transgression devoured. Four Prayer Knots on a penitent's wrist carry only truth; the Maw of Blame inhales guilt through a hand closed on chest or throat; a torn seal releases what it held as force. His ascension came from "a willingness to bear what others refused." "Each consumed sin deepens corruption of the right side; nerves deaden, dreams grow colder." He "Cannot consume innocence, justified defence, or child-borne guilt", he "Requires confession to legitimise consumption", and "he avoids children and joyous crowds, claiming their resonance "rings too clean."" Purganeth is the Spiritual World of Dazkar, the Arbiter of Purganeth (the Spiritual Seat page).

## 3 · The shared history

Three events. None is in the archive; each is new and fitted to every card it touches. They fall in the Withering Era, the one era a member's card states, and are unordered against one another. Every hook stays unplayed, and Seraphine's open question, whether she can fire on memory alone, is not touched.

### The damaged span at Bridgemarch

- **When.** The Withering Era. After Seraphine's registry had formed, so that every palm she fires is licensed; after Rhyse had watched a bridge collapse under panic and sworn never again, and after he had begun to bend flows. Before any card's present.
- **Where.** Bridgemarch, the junction city of Nalūn, and the Cuttings beside it (WORLD §3 and §5). The Nalūn page: Bridgemarch is "The central void, where the tunnel network converges", "Built vertically", with chain-bridges across open caverns and "The sound of chain is constant"; Cutwall is "scaffolding, rail and dust", its air "tastes of powdered halite and lamp soot", and its province is "The kingdom's most physically dangerous district."
- **Who.** Sir Rhyse Calder, Cyrille Lacrim, Seraphine Kharza, Sumire Nekoha.
- **What happened.** Sir Rhyse was bringing arbiters through the junction toward Ironlink Hold. The Rescue and Deluge Cadre's two casters travelled in his escort, and Sumire was seconded from Logistics to lay its route through the junction crowds. A face gave way in the Cuttings. The cutters at the face did not come out. The barracks emptied toward the junction, and the crowd came up out of the Cuttings with salt dust in its clothes and onto the chain-bridges, where the junction's own traffic already stood. The shock had parted an anchor chain on one span, and the crowd pressed onto it. Rhyse planted Lochgate at the head of the damaged span. He would not set Rail Root on it, because a tightened span that is already hurt can lock stress into the wrong supports. He knotted his tartan over the front ranks and turned the column onto the sound span beside it. Sumire went first with the children from the barracks, up the tiers by the Moth Path, before any bell sounded. At the edge of the damaged span a bow on her apron came undone, and the misfortune it held spent on her: the rail gave under her hand, and she went over. The salt dust in the air had clouded Seraphine's manifests, and her first hands would not hold. Cyrille sweetened the water. The manifests cleared, and a nurse's cradle palm caught Sumire above the cavern floor and set her down. Then Cyrille struck the Matins Cask. The crowd on the sound span matched the pulse and walked. Those who went over the rails after that landed on a Cushion Surge, and Seraphine's hands carried them to the triage tables. The damaged span stood empty until the Span's engineers closed it.
- **What each took.**
  - Rhyse: a damaged span he did not lock and a vow kept; the fear of the front ranks, which dragged at his reflexes for the rest of the road; the handoff Cyrille's card records, gate and then water.
  - Cyrille: the order of the route her card gives, Sumire padding it and Cyrille carrying through it, with the bell held until Sumire is clear; the practice Seraphine's card states, the water sweetened before Seraphine fires; the cold of the overdraw, and tea at the tables. Puddle curled on Sumire.
  - Seraphine: the duet her card names, water scaffolding her hands; hands numb to touch for the hour after, and orders signed with her visor.
  - Sumire: one bow spent, a fall, and a debt to a nurse's cradle palm, which is a dead nurse Seraphine answers to.

### The cantor's door at Altherion

- **When.** The Withering Era, after Seraphine's registry had formed, while Yukizumi Yukari held the Reaper's office.
- **Where.** Altherion, the republic on the Ring's rim (WORLD §3 and §5), in the hall of one of the choir orders. The Modern Era page: "the orders appoint their own Precentor of the Choirs, whom the Accord does not recognise and cannot appoint", and chant can hold something up in a hall.
- **Who.** Maelia Cantorine, Seraphine Kharza, Sumire Nekoha, Deimos; Yukizumi Yukari, the Reaper, whom five of the six cards name.
- **What happened.** The cantor of a choir order lay dying in the order's hall at Altherion. When his fear came on him he had led his congregation in swearing that they would not let him be taken. They kept the oath at his door in chorus, and the chorus agreed a heartbeat and held it. The death was disputed by stubborn love, and the Reaper came for the last word and sent for the Sin Eater. The congregation's children sang with the rest. Sumire went in first and walked them out along the Moth Path, down the stair and into the yard. Deimos waited on the stair until the last of them had passed him. Seraphine planted Manifold at the door, and a single palm capped the loudest voice until it was a cough in one throat. Maelia knotted the Throat Ribbon twice and walked into the chorus with the hymnal open. She cut across the fifth beat. The chant stumbled on one syllable and came apart into voices, and she answered it with a plain line that the congregation followed. Seraphine's hands laid a Casket Wall across the door, and a mason's brace held the living back. Deimos went in to the bed. The cantor confessed through the Prayer Knots what he had asked of his people, and Deimos took it, and the oath came away from the congregation with it. Seraphine's palms held the door and did not touch the bed. Maelia drew the Silence Ring on the floorboards, and inside it the cantor's last word was heard. Sumire came back up the stair and kept the room gentle while Yukizumi lowered the star.
- **What each took.**
  - Maelia: the beat she exposed and the agreement riding on it, eaten by the man her card names beside her; the ribbon knotted twice, and her voice kept.
  - Deimos: a new red seal on the sash and a deeper stain on the right side; an agreement born of fear, taken by confession; a room he entered after the children had gone.
  - Seraphine: the wary treaty her card records. Her palms hold the living, and none of them has delivered anyone to him.
  - Sumire: the children walked out, and a man on the stair who waited for them to pass.
  - Yukizumi: a last word heard, and a death no longer disputed; Deimos's card calls it "surgical consequence".

### The drovers' march on the Corridor road

- **When.** The Withering Era; Rhyse at his office of escorting arbiters.
- **Where.** A way-station in the Corridor Holdings of Stannvaard (WORLD §5). The Stannvaard page: "Administered by the Transport Guild", with "road maintenance, way-station operation, escort coordination", and "A transient population with the specific character of people who live on roads".
- **Who.** Sir Rhyse Calder, Maelia Cantorine, Cyrille Lacrim.
- **What happened.** Arbitration sat in the way-station hall to hear a dispute between the Transport Guild and the drovers who work its road. Sir Rhyse brought the arbiters in under escort. The Acoustic Ward was attached to the hearing, and the Rescue and Deluge Cadre sent Cyrille. The drovers in the yard took up a work-song. By the time the hearing opened the song had become a march, and the march came to the doors. Rhyse planted Lochgate in the doorway and knotted his tartan, and the head of the march slowed at the gate. Inside, Cyrille drew a Floodline around the arbiters' table. Maelia went out by the side door into the yard with the kris and the hymnal, the ribbon knotted twice. She cut across the fifth beat, and the march fell apart into drovers. For a breath the yard had no beat, and the men at the back began to shove. Cyrille struck the Matins Cask once from the doorway. The yard matched the pulse, and the shoving stopped. Maelia gave them a plain line, and they followed it back across the yard. Rhyse lifted the gate, and the hearing sat again the same day. Nobody was struck.
- **What each took.**
  - Rhyse: a gate held without a blow, and the Acoustic Ward as the other half of the Auxilia on a hot road.
  - Maelia: a bell to hand a crowd once the chant is cut, and a flood-caster who waits for her signal.
  - Cyrille: the order she keeps with the Acoustic Ward, the cut first and the toll after it, since a pulse laid over a live chant gives a march a second beat; and the handoff her card gives Rhyse, gate and then water.

## 7 · Where the cards disagree

Quoted and left open.

1. **What Benediction is, three ways.** Maelia's card: "Neither is a Stage." and "Benediction maps to Stage VIII, Transcendence" (`Maelia Cantorine · The Countercant.md`, epigraph note). Cyrille's card: "Used correctly here as a resonance rather than a Stage" (`Cyrille Lacrim · The Flood-Bell.md`, III). Seraphine's card: "Benediction · Vitalia. Iori's card: "She is the only person in the setting doing the operation he cannot do, and his card lists Kaede for the same reason." (`Shiragiku Iori · The Wake-Embroiderer.md`, relationships). Shion's card: "Shion is the fourth of the Reaper's specialists whose craft is, mechanically, a check on what the Sin Eater cannot do." (`Kozakura Shion · The Bloomwarden.md`, relationships).
3. **Fascination on one card.** Maelia's card, of Kaito: "both cards call it fascination rather than a counter." (Maelia, X). Kaito's card lists one relationship, "Kaede Suyori · Lover." (`Kaito Akagiri · The Scarlet Plague.md`, XI), and does not name Maelia.

Neither gives a surname. `Niran Yukari.md` is "He Who Hears the Thread Before It Snaps", with "acting authority over the enclave and three tributary settlements", and names no Amalgam, fear chorus, Deimos or Maelia. The roster links Deimos's "Niran" to niran-yukari (`imports/lore/_roster.json`, Deimos's mentions). This record puts no Niran in any event.
5. **Rhyse's band against itself.** "Corrected to Band A throughout." (`Sir Rhyse Calder · The Tartan Bastion.md`, epigraph note; `RULINGS.md` 2026-09-12: "η corrected from 0.84 (which fell in the gap between bands) to 0.80, the top of Band A").

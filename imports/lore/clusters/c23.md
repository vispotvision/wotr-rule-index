# c23 · The Rot Host

The generals Malphas keeps under his Regent: Vorynth the Vatborn, whose torso is a vat; Kytheris, Mother of Spores, High Matron of the Pale-Wreathed Sisterhood; the Ossuary Choir, three siblings fused into one frame of bone; Graelith, the Stilled Surge, a knight with a sword sunk in the basin of his chest; Morphaene the Gloam-Brewer, who breathes out other people's ruin; and Zeraphine Drowl, the Black Vat Regent, who commands from inside a glass cistern.

**Era.** Unstated on all six cards. None of the six names the Guild Accord, a Division, a crown or a year. Their lord's one archived scene, `scenes/the_nights_watch.md`, shows Malphas at the Greyshaft Nine coldhouse with a cell of four (Ilmar Foss, Nuvalik, Vesk, Xu Deming) and no host. In that scene he is still shaping the file the Register will keep on him: "I would prefer the file be built off a word I chose". A host of five generals and a Regent would already stand in any such file. This record therefore sets the Host's shared events after The Night's Watch. It names no era and no year, and it orders the events after Zeraphine's betrayal of the Pyraeon Forge Academy and by one another.

**Sources read.** The six cards in full, at the roster's `file` paths. Outside the cluster: `Malphas · Ferment of Stars.md` (c35) and his scene, The Night's Watch; `Vaerath Ashveil · The Pale Reaping.md` (c27), the one card beyond the Host that names the Rot Host and Eldervale; `Velastra Nirellium · The Severed Saint.md` (c28) and `Maelia Cantorine · The Countercant.md` (c22), for the inversion Graelith's card names; `Mirehollow Asakusa · The Verdant Lotus.md` (c33), for the Exilic March. The wiki: the Outer World page under The Bearing and the Holding (SW in WORLD's key: the Varakh Drylands, the Broken Rivers, the Bone Hills, the drought); `The Four Ceilings.md` and `The Concordance of Ages.md`, where Pyraeon is a forge-array, which Malphas's card holds unrelated to the Academy. `RULINGS.md`, 2026-09-12: the Concordant Crystal, R26-1-CONCORDANT_CRYSTAL_MECHANISM, written into the Choir's card. `book/night-watch-zombification/bible.md`, which is no canon, was read so that nothing here crosses it.

**The archive is silent on all six.** No file under `scenes/` names a member, the Rot Host or the Bloom, the Pale-Wreathed Sisterhood, the Cathedral of the Hollow Note, the Cauldron March, the Smog Legion, the Black Cistern, the Pyraeon Forge Academy, Eldervale or the Warded Citadel. ## 1 · Who is here

**Vorynth the Vatborn** (vorynth-the-vatborn). "First General of Rot's Bloom". His affiliation reads "Unbound Alchemical Aberration. Sworn lieutenant of Malphas." "His torso is a vat". It is "a living vat that ferments corpses into weaponised sludge and rot-cloud vapour", and "Spells or flesh absorbed into the vat are transmuted into corrosive acid sprays or boneglass shards". Where he stands, "siege weapons, armour and walls" rust or rot, "turning defences into brittle shells within hours". The longer he stays, the faster everything near him comes apart: "Stone becomes brittle, steel corrodes, sinew rots". His signature takes ground until the ground holds nothing. "It takes hours". The same entropy works on the vat. He cannot coordinate with allies, constructs or a formation, and his card calls him "a siege engine that requires a conductor". Malphas has him "Loyal as the first general; the kinetic arm of the Cauldron's influence". He holds the Ossuary Choir's harmonic decay to be "vainglorious noise compared to his slow, relentless corrosion", and Zeraphine to be "An invaluable field conductor". Nothing on the card says what he was before the vat. His words begin: "Let structures rot to dust and spirits curdle into sludge".

**Kytheris, Mother of Spores** (kytheris-mother-of-spores). "Mistress of Fungal Dominion", "High Matron of the Pale-Wreathed Sisterhood", "Malphas' Second General of Bloom", whose Catalyst reads "A person is a person and is also soil". A halo of luminous spore-antlers grows from her head, each node a strain of mind-rotting fungus. "Her back periodically splits open to birth fungal homunculi": bone-borers, nerve-chokers, mind-feeders. Anyone who breathes her spores is marked as "soil", and in survivors "fungal seeds embed in their spiritual lattice, waiting for activation". She reads a soul's harmonic and releases at that frequency. She must read it first. Once the spores are airborne they are out of her reach, "and she has been releasing for years". Her counter is silence: "Graelith disrupts her tuning and she knows it". Her Sisterhood revere her "as a saint-mother" and spread her spores "in famine-struck settlements as "blessings."" Nobody knows "How much of the four quarters is already soil". Her words: "All flesh is fertile if you break it down enough. The soul? Merely a seedbed".

**The Ossuary Choir** (the-ossuary-choir). "Harmonists of Boneglass. Third General of the Rot Host". Their affiliation is the "Cathedral of the Hollow Note", and they are "Malphas' Bone-Song Artillery". Three siblings are fused into one ossified frame, their ribcages arched into vaults that hum. "Each voice carries a different decay": "flesh, will, or faith". The bond was sealed "through a Rite of Hollow Union", and it takes only where "blood, oath or shared catastrophe qualifies". Each sibling "keeps their own identity, memory and Traits". The frame has "exactly one nervous system", carried "by whichever of them is seated". In each chest is a boneglass organ that "emits decay frequencies capable of shattering Soul Crystals, unravelling glyph chains, and cracking stone from within". Their song leaves "armour buckles, spell-wards collapse, morale curdles into despair". They cannot move. They are "transported to a siege, positioned, and played". Their card holds that a clean death among them would quiet one decay, and a death the others refuse would curdle the bond. Nobody knows "Whether three siblings still exist inside it, or one thing that remembers being three". Their words: "We are the last song you will ever hear. And we will play you hollow".

**Graelith, the Stilled Surge** (graelith-the-stilled-surge). "Nullification Vanguard. Fourth General of the Rot Host". He is the "Silent Knight of the Cauldron March", "Malphas' Spell-Breaking Arm." "He hollowed himself out and put the weapon inside the cavity": his chest holds "a dark alchemical stillwater basin", and "the sword submerged inside absorbs nullifying essence until drawn." "Every step pulls sound from the air, muting battlefields within a growing radius and cutting the flow of Wellspring channels". After the battle, in the muted ground, the rot he seeded comes up. "He is inside the field too, and nothing he brings works either." "He cannot project it, cannot throw it, cannot leave it somewhere". Malphas respects him "for his precision and discipline" and deploys him "only when key Wellsprings or Domains must be cracked open". His counter is runecraft. "An Engraver working in advance is his answer, and it is the same answer as Velastra's". His words: "Noise is merely the writhing of the unbroken. I bring the quiet where all things come apart".

**Morphaene the Gloam-Brewer** (morphaene-the-gloam-brewer). "Alchemical Smog-Warrior. Fifth General of the Rot Host". His affiliation is "Malphas' Smog Legion", and he is "Herald of Miasmic Illusion." "A network of glass gourds and distillation pipes weaves throughout his body", and each breath out releases "dense gloam-vapours rich with emotional toxins and phantom decay". Enemies in the mist see their own decomposition, "blade rusting, flesh bleeding from within", "often causing them to flee, hesitate, or turn on allies". Afterwards "lingering spores distort sight and emotion". "He must read them first". What each person sees is accurate, "and the accuracy is what breaks them". He cannot check the gloam once it is up. "He has never struck anyone", and "Every casualty Morphaene causes is inflicted by a third party". His Shell mirrors his alignment, so "He is broadcasting while he works". His words end: "see your ruin writ large, then break".

**Zeraphine Drowl, the Black Vat Regent** (zeraphine-drowl-the-black-vat-regent). "Supreme Field Commander under Malphas. Master Conductor of the Rot Host". She was a "Former Pyraeon Forge Academy tactician turned Regent of the Black Cistern". She "Betrayed the Pyraeon Forge Academy to bind herself to his cause", "a strategist turned vessel." "And then merged her body with a glass cistern". Inside it "suspended viscous corpses and rotting sludge form living command signals that pulse across the battlefield." "Her voice vibrates through sludge conduits, activating rot-creatures and beaconing wave assaults". Terror on the field strengthens her: "Regiments of rot-homunculi harden around her as dread deepens". So "She cannot fight a calm enemy well and cannot afford a short war." "The conduits must reach." "Cut the sludge and the cadence stops at the break". She holds "Ultimate authority over all four" of Vorynth, Kytheris, Graelith and Morphaene. Of the Choir, her card says she "Relies heavily on their harmonic pulses as battlefield anchors". Her card calls her "the most dangerous thing on the Bloom's roster" and says she "cannot fight at all". The counter it gives: "Reach the glass". Her words begin: "The pulse I feed becomes the wave you drown in".

## 3 · The shared history

Three events. None is in the archive. Each is new and fitted to every card it touches. They fall after The Night's Watch and after Zeraphine's binding to Malphas, in one campaign and the drought that followed it.

The quarter is the Outer World. Malphas's card files his old threat rating "across Outer World jurisdictions", which it no longer carries forward, and the cards' campaign book is *Ferment of the Outer Wastes*. The region is the Varakh Drylands. The SW page gives it "several abundant years followed by severe drought", the famine ground Kytheris's Sisterhood works, and weak Aether that keeps the great dragons off it. No new name is coined. The town and the fort stay unnamed.

### The first cadence on the Broken Rivers

- **When.** After Zeraphine had betrayed the Pyraeon Forge Academy, bound herself to Malphas and merged with the glass cistern (her card, I and VIII), with Vorynth already sworn as First General and each of the four already carrying the body their Catalyst made. It is the first engagement in which she held Vorynth, Kytheris, Graelith and Morphaene under one cadence.
- **Where.** A walled river town on the Broken Rivers, in the Varakh Drylands of the Outer World (WORLD §5, South; SW: "Thorn scrub, dry woodland, red earth, salt pan, seasonal river and very hot lowland basin", with the Broken Rivers among its features).
- **Who.** Zeraphine Drowl, Vorynth, Kytheris, Graelith, Morphaene.
- **What happened.** The town had shut its gate on the Host and manned its wall. Zeraphine's cistern came up the river road behind the regiments of rot-homunculi, and the sludge conduits were run out from the glass to the foot of the wall. Morphaene went forward first. He stood at the lip of the ditch and breathed out, and the gloam rose over the parapet. Kytheris came up behind him, where Zeraphine had set her, under her crown of spore-antlers. She read the men on the wall one at a time and released into the gloam at each man's frequency. Where her spores went, the visions took on body. Men on the wall watched the blades in their hands rust through to the hilt and felt their own flesh open from inside. Some ran. Others turned on the men beside them.

  Vorynth walked up to the gate and stood in front of it. He never struck it. Through the afternoon the iron bands flaked, the timbers went soft, and the facing of the gate towers crumbled under a hand.

  At dusk the town's practitioners came out of the river gate in a sortie aimed at the glass. Zeraphine sent Graelith to meet it. He walked into their path. The sound went out of the air around him, their incantations arrived at nothing, and the channels they drew on found no medium. The sortie stopped in the river mud and turned back through the gate. Over that stretch of wall the gloam thinned wherever his radius reached, and the men there stopped seeing their ruin. Kytheris's spores went up at the edge of his field and found no frequency to match. She drew back from him. Morphaene moved along the ditch and laid the gloam on thicker beyond the muted ground.

  When the sortie was in, Zeraphine's cadence went down the conduits. Vorynth's waves came against the softened gate in the order she called them, and the gate went down. The town fell before morning. Vorynth took its dead into the vat. The survivors walked out with Kytheris's seeds in their lattice. Days later, in the mud by the river gate where Graelith had walked, the rot came up.
- **What each took.**
  - Zeraphine: the order she has kept since. Morphaene's gloam goes out before the Surge, and Graelith is held back for the counterstroke. From the river gate she has called him a living pause.
  - Vorynth: waves that moved in sequence when she called them, which he has never made them do alone. His card names her his field conductor.
  - Kytheris: her counter, found at the river gate. Silence. Her wary respect for Graelith dates from that dusk.
  - Graelith: his place in the order as the pause. And a wary rapport with Kytheris, whose spores strained his field while his field emptied her tuning.
  - Morphaene: the habit his card records of routing thicker smog around the Fourth General's field, mid-battle, on the same side. And a partner whose spores gave his visions a body.

### The emplacement in the Bone Hills

- **When.** A later season of the same campaign.
- **Where.** A hill fort in the Bone Hills of the Varakh Drylands (SW: the Bone Hills among the Drylands' features, bone phosphate among its yields).
- **Who.** The Ossuary Choir, Vorynth, Zeraphine Drowl.
- **What happened.** The fort kept a Domain over its walls, and its wards were cut into the gate stones. Vorynth could do nothing at a gate while that Domain held. The Host hauled the Choir up the slope on a wagon bed, because the frame cannot walk, and set it down facing the wall. Then the siblings played. Three voices went out from the boneglass in three chests: flesh, will and faith. The glyph chains in the wards came apart one after another, the Domain slipped out of phase with itself and failed, and a crack ran up the gate stones from inside.

  Zeraphine's cistern stood at the foot of the hill, and her conduits ran up the slope to the frame. She took each change in the chord as her signal and passed it down the sludge to the waves. When the wards were down she called Vorynth. He walked up through the cracked gate and stood in the yard, and over the hours the fort went brittle around him. Afterwards he said their music had drowned out his rot. He called it vainglorious.

  Graelith was not on the hill.
- **What each took.**
  - The Choir: their place in the order, first against the walls and ahead of Vorynth, the place their card's line on Malphas gives them. And a general who calls their song noise.
  - Vorynth: a fort he entered through a gate another arm had opened. His distrust of the Choir has held from that yard.
  - Zeraphine: the Choir's chord as her battle signal, and their pulses as the anchor she has leaned on since. The Choir's card gives her admiration for their precision.

### The famine towns after the drought

- **When.** The drought that followed the Host's campaign on the Broken Rivers.
- **Where.** The river towns of the Broken Rivers, in the Varakh Drylands (SW: "several abundant years followed by severe drought").
- **Who.** Kytheris, Vorynth.
- **What happened.** The drought came the year after the Host had taken the Broken Rivers. In the towns where Vorynth had stood, the granaries had gone soft and grey, and nothing stored in them kept. The towns went hungry. The Pale-Wreathed Sisterhood came in behind the hunger with Kytheris's spores and gave them out in the streets as blessings. The townspeople took them. Kytheris came in at the end of the season.

  Vorynth was still quartered on the river. The rot-homunculi carried the famine dead out to him, and he took them into the vat. What the vat made, he sprayed back over the ground as sludge. Her fungus came up in it. Where his rot had reduced a granary, her bloom took the grain dust and the beams. He went on taking the dead. She went on seeding what he left.
- **What each took.**
  - Kytheris: towns marked as soil, whose survivors carry her seeds. A Sisterhood that revered her as saint-mother in their streets. And a rival for the dead whose rot readies the ground she wants.
  - Vorynth: the famine dead for the vat, and a rival whose bloom comes up in everything he leaves.

## 6 · Same person?

No. The six cards are six entities with different bodies, orders and offices, and each card gives its member a rank of its own in one chain of command. The Ossuary Choir is one card for three siblings in one frame. Their card keeps three identities, three memories and three sets of Traits, and leaves open "Whether three siblings still exist inside it, or one thing that remembers being three". This record treats them as one card of three siblings and does not settle it. Outside the cluster, Graelith's card calls him and Velastra Nirellium (c28) "structurally the same character built by different hands". They are two people. ## 7 · Where the cards disagree

Quoted and left open.

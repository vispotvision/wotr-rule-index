# c25 · The Citadel's high seats

The High Concordant, the Prime Arbiter, the Arbitration Division's counsel, its old seer and a junior oath witness, and the foundling the Accord keeps under watch. Six cards, one institution. No archived scene holds any of the six. Canon order holds throughout: Isaac's direction, then the card, then the archived scenes, then the wiki, then this record.

**Era.** Withering Era, the Accord's present, with no year stated. Lyss's card says Withering Era, and Argen's gives him "a century of service" in the same era. Drakvor's card says "Era · War of the Realms", which WORLD maps to no era. Vaelthor's and Aethryn's cards state none. Aurgon's card has him standing since the closing of the First Eon into the present Withering Era, and it disagrees with itself about when he made his chain (section 7). No archived scene holds any of the six. The events run in this order: the Tribunal Walkout, before Drakvor was High Concordant and inside Argen's century; the Silent Accord Incident and its finding three years later, once Lyss held the white-feather warrant; the foundling's question in the present, with Drakvor High Concordant. No card orders the walkout against the Silent Accord.

## 1 Who is here

**Drakvor · The Black-Crowned Bastion.** He is Drakvor, called the Black-Crowned Bastion, the Iron Sovereign, the Grave Lion of Veyrn and the Last Monolith, and his epithet is "That Which Even Titans Address By Name". He is "High Concordant of the Guild Accord. Marshal of the Bastion Legion. Warden of the Veyrn Marches". He is human and giant-blooded, of the Veyrn Colossian stock, tower-tall and block-broad. A cyan Concord sigil turns over his right eye and brightens when he braces a gate or sets a line. Troops fall into step near him without orders. His soul is woven into the Heart Vault of the Concord Citadel. His breath is part of what keeps the Citadel's glow, as every High Concordant's was before him. His loyalty runs "to the Realms and the civilians behind the walls first, the Codex second, and politics last". He once walked out of a Tribunal over a verdict he called cowardice, and "Nobody in the hall could stand until he had gone". His first true Domain dates from the exile that followed. In the Veyrn marches he united fractured northern strongholds through presence and a shared Bastion Chain, then traded that strength back to the Accord for frontier reforms. He rotates between the Citadel and the Gatefronts. At the Gatefront Concord he held a triple-Gate flicker long enough for other Domains to align. When he swears to hold a line, a gate or a people, he does not fall until the oath is fulfilled or broken, and afterward his body cracks and he may keep his bed for weeks. Once he has braced a stand, only a trusted second can argue him into a feint. He will not abandon noncombatants or lower-ranked defenders, and his card calls that "The exploit every patient enemy eventually finds". It leaves open why the Accord let a man who once walked out on it hold the one office it cannot function without. "Stone does not care who rules it. It only records who stood when the sky fell."

**Argen Vael'thar · The White Arbiter.** Master Argen Vael'thar, called the White Arbiter and Merciful Iron, is "Prime Arbiter of the Concord Council. Head of the Arbitration Division. Overseer of every Concord Trial and Severance". He reports only to the High Concordant and sits on the Council beside the High Warden and the High Marshal. He was born in Eresse, in the New World, and his card traces his family to early law-keepers whose office gave him noble and sacred standing. He is the longest-serving Prime Arbiter in the Accord's record. His card credits him, unattested, with rewriting nearly a quarter of the Codex after the Crevice Wars, and with fewer than five Total Severances "in a century of service". The Codex is in his memory section by section, and he quotes it mid-conversation as if it were scripture. When his resonance runs, a lie spoken near him breaks in the air and the room hears it break. Everything in view reports its composition to him at once. Saphri, a small fox-like companion rumoured to be forged from Wellspring song, keeps him able to choose among it, and his card calls Saphri "The one bond the sheet is built on". A sincere error does not break, and "a man who has convinced himself presents a true spectrum of a false thing". He wears the Rings of Verdict at his neck and no other sigil, and his Codex Shard records every verdict he utters. His health wanes. His will does not. He teaches young Sealwrights in Eresse: "Every law begins as a confession". His card leaves open which of his Severances he still thinks was wrong, "and why the Codex Shard has a gap where one verdict should be."

**Aurgon Mintruix · The Grand Seer of the Last Age.** Aurgon Mintruix is the Grand Seer of the Last Age, the Wellspring-Chained, the Library That Breathes and the "Witness of the Closing of the First Eon". He once held the Accord rank of Magister-Seer in the Arbitration Division. He is an Elder Elf, heavily altered by Wellspring saturation and by time, born in the Late Harmonial Age in an Innerworld glade-city now lost to Domain fracture and archival erasure. His card has him standing continuously since the closing of the First Eon. He foresaw catastrophic futures that his own ascent would set off, and he fused his Soul Crystal to a local Wellspring node so that the Realms would have a fixed point. The fusion cost him his mobility, his power to fight and any path to full Archonhood, and he cannot leave the node's radius without catastrophic backlash to himself and to the site. With a coalition of Elven High Paruns and Dwarven rune-lords he co-designed the Chain of Witness for the Concord precursor order. Any magical system, Domain or relic brought into his chamber he reads back through its lineage. He remembers timelines that catastrophe aborted or overwrote. An oath sworn seriously in his presence cannot be forgotten by the world. Even with every record erased, the Wellspring tilts to punish or reward its outcome. He can cut one piece of knowledge out of a soul, a library or a relic, and every such cut scars him and the node. Titans, Archons and Concord Envoys come to him as a last resort and pay his price, "whatever it turns out to be that day". A successor is whispered to exist, and he has confirmed it to nobody. There is one question he will answer for no one, Titans included. "Ask your question again."

**Vaelthor Ashen-Meridian · The Patient Throne.** Vaelthor Ashen-Meridian is called the Grand Arbiter of the Unseen Accord and the Patient Throne: "Fallen Acolyte of Dominion. Convergent Synthesist. Arbitration-Division Infiltrator". In public he is senior non-voting counsel to the Accord's Arbitration Division, which believes him loyal. In private he is the sole true member of the Unseen Accord, a shadow body he founded, whose every other member believes they lead it. It has no connection to the Black Concord. He was cast out as an acolyte of Irath, Archon of Dominion, after he used Parun to rewrite three commanders' loyalty oaths mid-war. His Catalyst came in the Silent Accord Incident: "a twelve-person tribunal whose ethical memory he rewrote over seven days, using nothing but conversation, and nobody noticed for three years". Those around him recall what happened and never that he acted. A willing declaration spoken near him becomes a binding anchor, and to act against it sets up a friction in the speaker's own Essence. He waits, and the waiting makes his next working stronger. His implanted sigils carry verdicts into other people's Soul Crystals, and the Crimson Codex records every manipulation he has made and every active implant's name. If another reads it, every implant releases at once and his Crystal collapses. It never leaves his hand. His Crystal carries nine documented hairline fractures, and he cannot survive long direct fighting. He is four implants from the vote that would make him the Division's sole living interpreter of Codex law. He had a daughter. He knows her name. The face is gone, and he has stopped trying to recall it. His card leaves open what he is counting toward.

**Lyss Arvale · The Quiet Swan.** Lyss Arvale is the Quiet Swan and the White-Feather Cantor, the "Voice That Turns the Wind": "Oath Witness. Cantor. Escort of treaties through war-weather". She is human, of Eressean line and a Choir-ward initiate, from the High March of Eresse in the New World. She serves the Accord's Arbitration Division in the Neutral Chambers as a junior Oath Witness. She holds the white-feather warrant, which makes her record admissible in Accord courts. Her record is credited with three perjury reversals. She remembers exact phrasing and can cite what was truly said against what is claimed. When she speaks long vowels, falsehoods warble, and the speaker hears it. Crowds lower their voices near her without deciding to. At Graybridge her testimony saved the guilty to spare the innocent: she "kept the whole truth and cut it into two mercies, one public and one sworn". The crack it left in her Crystal flares when she withholds a truth too long. Her Quiet Corridor carries orders and treaties across a battlefield at conversational volume. Everybody hears, and nobody is made to obey. At Stormgate she held one in typhoon winds, and a battalion withdrew in order without a shouted word. Each plume of her white-feather collar is a sealed oath, one awarded for each oath kept, and the Aerial Ward's couriers who fly her between tribunals call her "wind's conscience". Iron-judges are her rivals. A Chamber edict offers her the Swan's Turn if she signs away the right to soften truths. Someone is leaving her lost Graybridge feather at crime scenes. Nobody knows who. "Let the wind decide the volume; I will decide the direction."

**Aethryn · The Twin-Sigiled Foundling.** Aethryn is the Twin-Sigiled Foundling, also called the Pale Omen, the Unfinished Child, the Circle-Bearer and the Silent Pattern, and his epithet is "He Who Stands Between Eye and Void". He is an "Unclassified Foundling. Twin-Sigil Vessel. Ward of the Guild Accord". No faction holds him. The Accord watches him under a standing refusal to induct or classify him, and he lives under monitored care in a sealed hospice-temple. Something left him at a shuttered Gate. The Gate Reflection Event is the only lead to his origin, and it has gone nowhere. Before anyone was watching he built two workings out of his own pressure, a siphon in his palm and a sight in his forehead, and the Guild "has no form for a foundling who cleared the gate twice before anyone was watching". The palm locks onto a resonance and will not let go. The forehead sees a lie as broken geometry and reads intention as much as fact. Where a completed stage should sit in his Crystal there is an open circle, and a Wellspring that brushes it reads a question: "the circle opens for you, but closes for me". Workings that touch him treat him as an unsettled clause. Under distress he warps the emotional intention of everyone near him. When both sigils close together the space around him bends toward what they read, and each pass spends his short-term memory and wears at what he means by himself. The researchers of Pattern Surge #4 lost twelve to eighteen minutes to him. As far as the record shows, they are the only people who have studied him up close and come through it uneventfully. Not even the Guild claims the right to name what he is. His card leaves open whether the Loop is a wound in him or in the law he sits outside of.

## 3 The shared history

### The Tribunal Walkout

**When.** Before Drakvor was High Concordant and inside Argen's century of service, in the Withering. It is Drakvor's Catalyst Event. His exile, his first true Domain and the Veyrn Reclamation follow it. The walkout, the hall that could not stand and the exile are the card's; the verdict, the marches' part in it and Argen's seal are new.

**Where.** A tribunal hall in the Concord Citadel (WORLD §4; `The Guild Accord/The Concord Citadel.md`). No card names the hall.

**Who.** Drakvor, Argen Vael'thar.

**What happened.** The Withering had cut the strongholds of the Veyrn marches off the wire, and the Accord had stopped governing them in all but name. A tribunal sat in the Citadel to say whether the Accord's undertaking to defend them still stood. It held that the undertaking had lapsed where the wire no longer reached. It ordered the Accord's defenders brought out of the marches. The Prime Arbiter, who oversees every Concord Trial and Severance, sealed the verdict, and his Codex Shard took it down. The order to carry it north went to Drakvor, who served the Accord in arms and came of the Veyrn stock. He heard it read in the hall. He called the verdict cowardice. Then he walked out. Nobody in the hall could stand until he had gone. The walk became an exile, and in the exile the Domain his card calls Black Crown Bastion formed. He went north into the marches the verdict had left. There he united the fractured strongholds through presence and a shared Bastion Chain, and afterward he traded that strength back to the Accord for frontier reforms.

**What each took.** Drakvor took the walk, which his card reads as the contradiction in him resolving. The same card ranks the civilians behind the walls ahead of the Codex. He took the exile, his first true Domain and the marches he wardens now. Argen took a verdict he had sealed and a man who called it cowardice in his court. His Shard still holds the verdict. Later he sat on the Council that nominated Drakvor to the Heart Vault, and no card says how he voted. Since that nomination he has reported to the man who walked out of his court.

### The Silent Accord Incident

**When.** While Argen was Prime Arbiter and Vaelthor already sat as senior counsel to the Division. The seven days are Vaelthor's Catalyst Event. The three years run from them, and the finding came once Lyss held the white-feather warrant, so that her record was admissible in Accord courts. The twelve, the seven days and the three years are the card's; the chamber, Lyss's finding and the review are new. No card orders it against the Tribunal Walkout.

**Where.** A Neutral Chamber of the Arbitration Division, in the Inner World (WORLD §3). `The Guild Accord/Merit and Severance.md` holds the Concord Trial "in the Neutral Chambers", and Seren Valenne's card sets Neutral Chambers in the Inner World. No card names this one.

**Who.** Vaelthor Ashen-Meridian, Lyss Arvale, Argen Vael'thar.

**What happened.** A tribunal of twelve heard Concord Trials in a Neutral Chamber. Lyss stood Oath Witness at some of its sittings in her first years in the Chambers, and she keeps the exact words of what she witnesses. Over seven days Vaelthor Ashen-Meridian talked with its members. He used nothing but conversation. When the seven days ended, each of the twelve held an account of right and wrong that differed from the one they had brought into the chamber, and each believed it had always been theirs. They went on sitting. For three years they gave verdicts, and each verdict echoed upward into the Codex as precedent. Nobody noticed. The world began to run on what he had built. Then Lyss was sent to witness the tribunal again. In one hearing it cited its own earlier reasoning, and the words it claimed differed from the words she held. She set the true words against the claimed ones, sitting by sitting, into the record, and the record was admissible. The Prime Arbiter heard the twelve himself. His voice breaks a lie in the air when it is spoken near him, and nothing broke, since none of the twelve was lying. The Division stood the twelve down. Their three years of verdicts stayed in the Codex, because the verdict of a Concord Trial is final. The Accord filed the matter as the Silent Accord Incident and found no hand behind it. Vaelthor sat in the review as the Division's senior counsel. Afterward none of those present could say what counsel had said in it.

**What each took.** Lyss took three years she could prove and a hand she could not find. She holds the twelve's old words and their new ones side by side, and her echo insomnia has both to replay. Argen took twelve judges his own voice could not catch, three years of precedent he cannot strike from the Codex, and a review in which his counsel sat at his shoulder. Vaelthor took his Catalyst, the world answering back. He took an incident on the Accord's file without his name on it, and the name of the Oath Witness whose memory had held the words.

### The foundling's question

**When.** The present. After the researchers of Pattern Surge #4 lost their minutes to Aethryn, and before the hospice-temple was sealed. Drakvor was High Concordant, Argen Prime Arbiter and Vaelthor counsel, and Lyss held the white-feather warrant. It follows the Silent Accord finding. The standing refusal, the monitored care and the sealed hospice-temple are the card's; the question, the carrier, the price and the casting are new.

**Where.** Two rooms. The Council Spire of the Concord Citadel (WORLD §4), where the Concordant presides from the Throne of Resonance (`The Guild Accord/The Concord Citadel.md`). And the Seer's chamber at the Wellspring node his Crystal is fused to. No card or page places the node, and this record leaves it unplaced.

**Who.** Drakvor, Argen Vael'thar, Vaelthor Ashen-Meridian, Lyss Arvale, Aurgon Mintruix, Aethryn.

**What happened.** Research and Archives had a boy in its keeping who carried two sigils nobody had taught him. After Pattern Surge #4, the matter of what the Accord should call him came up to the Council Spire. The Council could find no form for him. The Prime Arbiter framed a question for the Grand Seer, who had once been Arbitration's Magister-Seer: what the Codex would become if it wrote the boy into it. Vaelthor sat behind the Prime Arbiter as counsel without a vote. The Council sent its point of law past him, to the Seer. It wanted a carrier who could bring words back exactly and whose record the courts would take, and Argen had seen Lyss's memory hold against twelve judges. He sent her. The Aerial Ward's couriers flew her to the Seer's chamber. She put the question to him, and Aurgon had her ask it again. Then he named his price. That day it was an oath: she would carry his answer into the Spire whole, in his words and in his order, with nothing softened. She swore it in his presence, where the world cannot forget an oath. He answered. The couriers flew her back, and she set the answer down in the Council Spire as she had sworn to. It named no form for him. The Council divided. The Concordant cast the judgement. The Accord would not induct the boy, would not classify him, and would not leave him. He was to be kept under monitored care in a sealed hospice-temple, under no charter. The decree became law as it was spoken. The Chamber gave Lyss a plume for the oath kept.

**What each took.** Aethryn took a standing refusal, a watch and a sealed hospice-temple. He is the Accord's ward, with no charter under him. He was in neither room, and no card says whether anyone told him the answer. Drakvor took a ward whose keeping rests on his casting. Argen took a decree his Division must keep for a boy no clause in his memory covers. Vaelthor watched the Council carry a point of law past its counsel. It went to a seer the Division once kept. Lyss took an oath the world cannot forget, kept, and a plume on her collar for it. Aurgon took his price. He answered one more question for the Accord that had once ranked him.

## 6 Same person?

No two cards here are one person.

- **Argen Vael'thar and Vaelthor Ashen-Meridian.** The names share a sound, and both men serve one Division. Argen is a human born in Eresse who heads the Arbitration Division and sits on the Council. Vaelthor is a fallen acolyte of Irath who counsels the Division without a vote and founded the Unseen Accord. They are two men. Beatrix Vaelthorn, outside this cluster, is a surname echo and nothing more.

- **Drakvor and the Concordant in the archive.** `scenes/WOTR_Vaeloris_Sequence.md` names no High Concordant, and this record does not make him Drakvor.

- **Lyss Arvale and Lyssara Veyn.** c12 records them as two people.

## 7 Where the cards disagree

Quoted and left open.

1. **Whether the Concordant travels.** Drakvor's card: "Rotates between Concord Citadel and the Gatefronts." and "Warden of the Veyrn Marches." (I, X). `Factions, Bloodlines & Institutions/The Guild Accord.md`: "The Concordant cannot leave the Citadel." The Modern Era page of the Guild Accord: "The Concordant cannot leave the Citadel for long." and "The most powerful person in the four quarters cannot travel". `scenes/WOTR_Vaeloris_Sequence.md` has an unnamed High Concordant at the Alabaster Court. This record sets no event of Drakvor's outside the Citadel while he holds the office.
2. **Aurgon's years.** His card: "Roughly 1,600 years, standing continuously since the closing of the First Eon (Year −900) to the present Withering Era." and "the full 1,600 years of his bond" (I, VIII). The same card: "Co-designed the Chain of Witness alongside a coalition of Elven High Paruns and Dwarven rune-lords, in the final centuries before the Withering." (IX). WORLD §1 opens the Withering Era at 645 IC. His birth in the "Late Harmonial Age, the centuries before the Great Spirit War" (II) and the "Last Age" of his title fit no era on the count, and the Great Spirit War has no place on it (WORLD §1).
3. **Drakvor's era.** His card: "Era · War of the Realms." (I). WORLD §1 maps that term to no era, and the roster leaves his era blank.
4. **Who rewrites the Codex.** Argen's card: "credited with rewriting nearly a quarter of the Codex of Absolute Magical Law after the Crevice Wars (unattested)" (I). `The Guild Accord/The Black Concord.md` makes it a Black Concord operation: "Rewriting of the Concord Codex itself. Only by direct command of the High Concordant." No page says whether the two are one act, and no page names the Crevice Wars.
5. **Who sanctions a Severance.** Argen's card: "Fewer than five Total Severances sanctioned in a century of service." (I). `The Guild Accord/Merit and Severance.md`: "High-level Severances require Concord Council sanction or direct approval from the High Concordant." The same page administers punishments "under the Prime Arbiter."
6. **Whose are the Neutral Chambers.** Lyss's card: "Guild Accord · Arbitration Division, Neutral Chambers." Dr. Vaelion Rhest's card files the Neutral Chambers under Research and Archives. c12 records the dispute in full (c12, section 7, item 1).
7. **Argen's forebears.** His card: "His family traces to early law-keepers who mediated between the Dawn Guard and the First Accord", kept "as the card gives it" (I). WORLD §3 sets the Dawn Guard in Kushara, and no page has a First Accord.

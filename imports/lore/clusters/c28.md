# c28 · Those who write mercy in ash

Executioners and oath-bearers of cosmic law who stand outside the Accord's courts: the apostate Verdict-Bearer of the Highspire Choir (Francis Alexander), the conceptual executioner of the Edict of Urion (Kaelan Vorn), the dream-law hunter who left Enforcement (Aeldoris Vanthryx), the Oath-Warden of the Court of Veils (Aelum), the exile of the Celestial Concord who carries the Oath-Eater (Velastra Nirellium), and the Oathkeeper Inquisitor of the Judgment Order (Seraphion Veydrak).

## 1 · Who is here

**Francis Alexander · The Crimson Dirge of Atonement.** "Severed Celestial of the Highspire Choir. Apostate Verdict-Bearer. Would-be usurper of the thirteenth seat". He was "Sung at Highspire to fill an unspoken Verdict; Severed from the Throne-network and self-anchored since". His card gives his doctrine plainly, "atonement is removal", and says the Verdict-Bearer "held a single judgment so long that the Throne he was wired to stopped being necessary to it". He carries Judicium, the current a Highspire Celestial is Sung to carry, with Exuroth and Catharsis; guilt, sin and emotional charge near him feed the Dirge Pulse, and the Crimson Codex writes down every principle he rewrites as he rewrites it. He defied Urion "directly at the Irath Concord and survived", and "The Archons have never commented". He subjugated spiritual dominions in the Silent Crusade and inverted Cause and Effect in the Principle War. His era is the "Withering Era, into the War of the Realms"; he is "Active on the Soul Plane side of the Veil", moving toward the thirteenth seat and "the Law of Final Mercy". Gloss rights: never. "Compassion is a delay. Erasure is resolution".

**Kaelan Vorn · The Axiomatic Sovereign.** "Conceptual Executioner. Living anchor of Law. Judge of a nobility he does not know he was born to". A human, commoner-raised, "educated within the rigorous, impersonal structure of an Accord-chartered academy", "Nominally the Edict of Urion; functionally independent, since he holds Cosmic Law above any Archon's authority". "He believes the power is a product of pure discipline". The card seals his lineage as Archon-Touched, "currently unrevealed and unattested", and says "the day he learns so is the day the anchor breaks". His Silent Edict is a small loop in which "time, heat, decay and magic stop", anchored on Titan Atla'zon; he carries the Chain of Truth, which "binds to the Essence Core", and the silver Temperer's Gauntlet. Every activation solidifies his Essence and leaves him in restorative processing. "Nearly all ties severed by emotional nullification". Two incidents are on record: the Manganthra Stasis, a kingdom and capital sealed in a loop, and the Vantabriel Severing, "an elder of the Archon-Touched executed by severing their Ascensio link in one strike". He is "Active within the Zenith Citadel, a suspended fortress held in perpetual stasis". Withering Era. "Discipline is the only true currency of the cosmos".

**Aeldoris Vanthryx · The Ruthless.** "Independent Hunter. Former Guild Accord Enforcement. Dream-law executioner". A "Veyran Human, an Aether-Blood mutation from deep Oneirion exposure". He left Enforcement to hunt "the fractures people hide inside", and "He accepts only cases involving self-deception or corrupted conscience". His Phantasm Court raises a target's dream-self behind them. Its Limit is hard: "The target must be guilty of something they know and have not admitted". Near him, "nearby minds recall forgotten memories involuntarily", and his Sleepfall Presence brings "micro-sleeps and mental fog in everyone nearby". He ended the Cult of the Slumberhall, and of the seven judges of the Broken Tribunal his card says: "Forced to kill their own dream-selves. He did not lift a hand". He is "Active in the northern states, hunting a Hypnather parasite that slipped through the dream-realm". Era: "War of the Realms". Gloss rights: never; "He forces confession out of everyone he meets and explains nothing of himself in return". "You confessed the moment you dreamed".

**Aelum · The Pale Hierophant.** "Ritual Sovereign. Oath-Warden. Avenger of the Breach". The Keeper of the Silver Vow, who "Presides over the Court of Veils, an order of ritual overseers with no attested seat elsewhere in the Concord's registers". He is "Answerable to the Choir" in the Voyager Era, and names the Choir "The body that Sang him and the only authority he answers to". "He kneels to no throne". He is "Networked, at range, to Nysera, the Dreaming Veil", and at his Catalyst "Aelum called on Nysera's law directly. He held a corrupt emperor's crown in stasis for a hundred years". He has witnessed, punished or held open the oaths of "Warring generals, wedding guests, dying kings". His Decree of Silence kills every sound in his circle, "including the ones a spell needs spoken to work"; "The Weight of Glory" makes the unconfessed feel the mass of what they carry; "His highest defenses require he not move". The card gives him no Race. "The veil is thin here, and I am listening".

**Velastra Nirellium · The Severed Saint.** "Deathbound Enforcer. Concordant Exile. Bladebearer of the Unrevoked Law". A "Former Adjudicator of the Celestial Concord", now bound to the Severance Concord, "a silent remnant cult who enforce the Law of Irrevocables", and "Hunted by both Divine and Abyssal factions" for the Lawbreaker's Blade. Her Catalyst: "She was an Adjudicator who looked at the pacts she had been enforcing and found one that should never have been signed". "No one remembers who she served. The oaths she severed erased the name from history". Veyrinhal, the Oath-Eater, "Destroys spiritual bonds upon contact" and "cannot be blocked by magical shields bound to any oath or vow"; once per Temperance cycle it performs the Final Severance. "She cannot lie, nor speak in false prophecy". "The pact is broken. The breath you take now is theft".

**Seraphion Veydrak · The Chain-Halo.** "Oathkeeper Inquisitor. Dominion Warden. Chainblade Ascetic". The "Arbiter of the Thrice-Bound Oath", of the Guild Accord's "Arbitration Division, Judgment Order." and "Formerly High Sentinel of the Holy See". His Tri-Vow Mandate: "No False Judgment. His arcs, The Oath in Iron, The Threefold Trial and Arbiter's Descent, are unplayed. His own line calls the chain the shape of justice. Era unstated.

## 3 · The shared history

**Era.** The shared events fall in the Withering Era, with no year. Francis's and Kaelan's cards state it; Aeldoris's says "War of the Realms", which Francis's card joins to it ("Withering Era, into the War of the Realms"); Velastra's and Seraphion's cards state none, and this record sets them in it beside the others. Aelum's card says the Voyager Era, so he meets nobody here. He is joined to the rest through the Choir, a current and a legend. The rite at Ordas comes before the petition at Halcrest; the hearing at Altherion is unfixed against both. Everyone lives one life in one era.

**What the events leave alone.** No event tests Francis's Paradox Stasis, which "Nobody has yet tested". Kaelan's parents stay unnamed. No event brings him near anyone whose presence surfaces forgotten memory, no Phantasm Court is convened on him, and none raises a dream-self that loves Aeldoris. Velastra's patron stays nameless. The epithet's first owner stays unruled, Seraphion's Chosen stay undefined, and the Arbiter's Descent stays unplayed.

### The hearing at Altherion

- **When.** The Withering Era. After Aeldoris had left Enforcement, and after the seven judges his card calls the Broken Tribunal had killed their own dream-selves. Unfixed against the rite at Ordas.
- **Where.** Altherion, the republic on the Ring's rim (WORLD §3 and §5). The Stannvaard page: "Enforcement maintains a permanent legation at Altherion", and Guild Law there is "enforced by arbitration tribunals whose appeals process is slow, thorough, and famously humourless".
- **Who.** Seraphion Veydrak, Aeldoris Vanthryx.
- **What happened.** Arbitration took up the matter of the seven judges, and the Judgment Order sent Seraphion Veydrak to hear it. The hearing sat in Altherion, the city where Aeldoris had served with the Enforcement legation before he left it. Aeldoris came when he was called. Seraphion raised the Chain-Halo behind him before he asked anything. The clerks at the table nodded over their pages while the hunter sat before them, and the scribe's pen stopped in the middle of a line. Seraphion asked whether he had struck any of the seven. Aeldoris said he had not lifted a hand. The halo held. Seraphion asked what had broken them, and Aeldoris said they had killed their own dream-selves. He said nothing of himself past what the questions asked. Seraphion found no act of the hunter's hand in the matter and entered no sentence. Aeldoris went out into the city before evening.
- **What each took.**
  - Seraphion: a finding entered with no sentence, and the hunter's own words on the Judgment Order's record, spoken inside the halo.
  - Aeldoris: his answer on the record of the Accord he had left, and nothing said of himself past it; the legation's city, walked into once more and out again.

### The rite at Ordas

- **When.** The Withering Era, soon after the Vantabriel Severing, which Kaelan's card enters as an incident on record. After Velastra had severed her oaths and taken up the Oath-Eater. Before the petition at Halcrest.
- **Where.** Ordas, among the Kingdoms of the Five Rivers (WORLD §3; the Southern Pan page: "Ordas, small and wealthy, silver financing professional armies"). No card places the Aetheric Nobility; this record puts one elder's house here and goes no further.
- **Who.** Kaelan Vorn, Seraphion Veydrak, Velastra Nirellium.
- **What happened.** Seraphion had heard a case against an elder of the Archon-Touched and condemned the elder, and the house kept its elder behind its own doors. The sentence stood unexecuted. Kaelan Vorn held no commission from the Accord and asked for none. He came to Ordas and executed the elder with one strike that severed the Ascensio link. Then he was gone. The house laid the elder on a bier in its courtyard and began a raising. Seraphion came to keep the sentence he had passed. He raised the Chain-Halo over the courtyard and bound the officiants where they stood. Velastra Nirellium came up the road on foot, and she had been walking since long before the town came in sight. The house's people drew back from her path. The chant broke off as she neared the bier, and the rite came apart. At the bier she struck once with Veyrinhal, the Final Severance of that cycle. The same stroke loosened the chained glyphs that held the officiants, and some of them got free before Seraphion raised the ring again. When the courtyard was quiet, she asked him whether he wanted his three vows cut. He said no. She went back to the road.
- **What each took.**
  - Kaelan: an execution that stayed done, closed by a stroke he never saw, and a house that went looking for a hunter to set on him. He had gone before the rite began, and no card says what he learned of it.
  - Seraphion: his sentence carried out by a man with no commission and closed by a woman the Accord had not sent; the halo raised twice over the same officiants; his three vows, kept by his own answer.
  - Velastra: the cycle's Final Severance, spent on the elder; the loosened halo, one more thing her stroke parted without her aiming at it; his answer.

### The petition at Halcrest

- **When.** The Withering Era, after the rite at Ordas.
- **Where.** Halcrest, the gate city of Nalūn (WORLD §5; the Nalūn page: "Customs house, immigration point, quarantine station." and "Population transient by nature").
- **Who.** Aeldoris Vanthryx, Kaelan Vorn (named, absent).
- **What happened.** The elder's house sent an envoy north to find a hunter. He found Aeldoris Vanthryx at Halcrest among the travellers waiting on the customs house, named the executioner as Kaelan Vorn, and asked the hunter to convene the Phantasm Court on him. Aeldoris asked what the executioner had hidden. The envoy said the strike had been made in the open, and that Kaelan had never denied it. Aeldoris declined the case. By then the men on the benches nearest them had begun to nod, and a customs clerk slept at his desk. The envoy took the south road that evening.
- **What each took.**
  - Aeldoris: the executioner's name, and a case he would not take. His card's Limit reads "The target must be guilty of something they know and have not admitted". The strike at Ordas had been made in the open.
  - Kaelan: nothing he learned of. The house's want stands.

## 6 · Same person?

No. Three cards share an epithet, and the three are three people. Francis is a Celestial Sung at Highspire; Kaelan is a commoner-raised human whose card seals him as Archon-Touched; Aeldoris is a Veyran human with an Aether-Blood mutation. They differ in body, governing current and order: Judicium and the Highspire Choir; Fixatio and the Edict of Urion; Oneirion and the Accord's Enforcement, which he left.

- Velastra Nirellium and Graelith: Graelith's card calls them "structurally the same character built by different hands". A comparison of sheets; c23 records it, and no card joins them.
- Aelum and the Wellspring Vantabriel in Kaelan's incident: one word, and no person.

## 7 · Where the cards disagree

Quoted and left open.

1. **One epithet, three cards.** Francis: "Epithet · He Who Writes Mercy in Ash" (Francis, I). Kaelan: "Epithet · He Who Writes Mercy in Ash" (Kaelan, I). Aeldoris: "He Who Writes Mercy in Ash. See migration note; shared, unruled" (Aeldoris, I). The card's text carries no migration note, and no other page read for this record gives one. Outside the cluster, Riven's card carries "He Who Cuts Mercy Into Petals" (`Riven Eltamyr Vonnel · The Glaive.md`, I).
2. **Fate Path in a being the Choir Sang.** Francis: "No Choir carries Fate Path at any weight, because a Celestial's Crystal is anchored to somebody else's Throne. His opened when the anchor was cut. It is the one thing about him the Host's registers cannot explain" (Francis, II). Aelum's card gives no Race. It reads "Path · Spirit dominant, Fate secondary" (Aelum, IV), beside "The Choir · The body that Sang him" (IX). The Celestial Host page: "No Choir carries Fate Path at any weight, and the omission is structural".
3. **Sung, or raised.** Aelum: "The body that Sang him" (IX) and "Why the Empire that raised him let a Hierophant who once held its crown hostage for a century keep his seat afterward instead of striking him down" (VIII). The Celestial Host page: "There is no Celestial childhood, no family line". Whether "raised" means reared or elevated is not ruled.
4. **A Domain held in the Choir's service.** The Celestial Host page: "A Celestial who severs enough of that dependency to manifest a true personal Domain, while remaining nominally in service, earns the Grand Angel designation". Aelum: "a fifty-metre Domain he can extend or collapse at will" (II) and "Answerable to the Choir" (I). His card gives him no Grand Angel title.
5. **Aelum's century against his era.** "He held a corrupt emperor's crown in stasis for a hundred years" in "the Voyager Era" (Aelum, I). WORLD.md §1, from the Concordance of Ages: the Voyager Era runs "000 to 070 IC".
6. **What Severance leaves of a Celestial.** Francis: "Severed from the Throne-network and self-anchored since" (Francis, I), alive and active. The Celestial Host page, of Severance: "No afterlife, no reincarnation path, no recorded exception", and "Most Celestials live and are Severed still being, functionally, the thing they were Sung to be and nothing else".

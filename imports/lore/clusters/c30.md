Four people of the Holy Sea of Alabaster and two of the Scale's quarry: the Sea's miracle-child of the Aurum Sect (Lumen Varos), its noble hostess who reads the future over tea (Seraphine Lurielle), the masked diviner of its cloister bloodline who serves the Accord (Maeron Iskare), the root-healer its outer commune judged and let go (Beatrix Vaelthorn), the Lector Sanctum Lux expelled (Elion Drevas), and the farmer its inquisitors hunt (Leon Vaerenthal).

## 1 · Who is here

**Lumen Varos · The Dawn-Touched Oracle.** A human boy "born with an inborn resonance toward illumination-based Aether", called the Golden-Eyed Seer, the Quiet Beacon and Sun's Whisper. He is an "Initiate of the Holy Sea of Alabaster" and an "Apprentice of the Aurum Sect, held within it", and the Sea "Treats him as a sacred child and a potential oracle, cherished, and watched". The Sect takes him to the Dawn-Altars. His card reads his command in one line: "A cherished child commands nothing, and is not asked to". The Aureline Filament, a thread of gold light that anchors a working in place, "was his own, before anyone taught him the word for what he was doing"; it is why "someone above the Aurum Sect" started watching him. Firstlight Sight "Sees incipient outcomes, short of full prophecy: the moment before a choice becomes fate". His presence steadies people. His touch "soothes wounds, purifies minor corruption", and sustained channelling blurs his eyes, because "the light he throws outward is light his own eyes stop getting". On the Day of Seven Refractions he "Predicted a minor Wellspring rupture seconds before it struck, saving his cohort"; at the Waking Hymn "his eyes lit bright enough to illuminate an entire chapel". Nobody knows what the Sea intends to do with him "once the Aurum Sect decides he has finished growing". He answers what is asked of him plainly.

**Seraphine Lurielle · Lilac Gaze.** "Alabaster Noble. Prophetic Hostess. Quiet Accord Informant". The Veiled Hostess, the White Parlour Seer. "Officially a loyal daughter of the Sea; unofficially useful to the Accord whenever its aims preserve lives over glory". The Somnalis current beneath her family manor "recognized her as a conduit". A prophecy surge took her right eye, and she chose "to keep being a person who takes tea and gossips in lace". A clergy-made ribbon binds the eye and "Keeps her from drowning in visions". Her gift lives in one room. "The parlour is a small, absolute country": conversation there drifts toward what she needs, her lace maps the futures of a single choice "mostly for nobles and Accord envoys who ask nicely", her Somnalis tea gives a guest back that night's dream-fragments, and a porcelain teaset "Nudges when guests arrive and leave, for those who drink from it". Her Judged Thread closes one narrow future with a twist of a glowing thread; "Alabaster clergy call it a Parlour Fracture", and "Afterward she is bedridden for hours". "Away from her salon, the working simply is not there". She once warned an Alabaster prince off a coronation date that would have killed him, and "she gained both enemies and quiet gratitude". Accord envoys "Visit under the pretext of tea".

**Maeron Iskare · The Silent Oracle.** "Mask-reader. Diviner. Bound Advisor". Withering Era. "Outer World descendant; a bloodline of the Alabaster cloisters, touched by Abythron sleep. Born to the lower cloisters and buried by prophecy before birth; resurfaced decades later under a new name". He is of the "Guild Accord · Obscurial Chamber", advising "the High Concordant's councils on Wellspring resonance forecasts", and he keeps "a confidential covenant with a faction of the Holy Sea of Alabaster, the Ecliptic Choir". "To the Church he is tolerated heresy". He is "Temporarily stationed in Eresse for a Wellspring audit of the Alabaster cathedral". He is "The only known survivor" of Eclipseron's Eye, which "gave him permanent dream perception and fused the mask to his flesh". The Golden Veil is beaten gold and obsidian feathers. "It whispers to him at night, claiming to remember faces he has forgotten". He never takes it off. His card gives the reason in his words: "faces are promises, and mine has broken too many". The Torque of Judicium at his throat "burns faintly when deception is near". People "speak to him and confess without wanting to". His inner law is "only what is revealed can heal", and "Each truth revealed costs him a piece of self-perception: he forgets small personal facts". "When he walks through candlelight the shadows crawl toward him"; "Blinding light breaks the refraction symmetry". His card records the Mirror Siege, the Eclipseron Eye Exposure and the Guild Inquest "False Sun", and leaves open "whether the prophecy that he will one day see without eyes is a promise or a sentence".

**Beatrix Vaelthorn · The Root-Sworn.** Bea, the Root-Sworn, the Ashwalker. "Wandering Root-Healer. Verdantia-Marked Survivor. Mudborn Witch". An "Unaffiliated wanderer, formerly indentured to a root-healer commune under the Holy Sea of Alabaster's outer territories". Her arm turned to bark in a petrification incident, which the commune elders ruled "divine judgment", and they "let her go within the month". At Sirel "Three archaeologists died to residual god-essence leakage on the same site she survived; nobody knows how". She came into her own in "the moment she stopped hiding the petrified arm and started using it as a tool"; before that, "she survived instead of learned". The Bark-Sovereign Arm takes no harm from blows. Root-Listening reads "vibration through soil and plant-matter": "movement, Essence signatures, heartbeats". A wound on her bursts into rot that corrodes whatever struck her. Her Rootstaff is Foraye blackwood wrapped in her own shed bark-skin, and she carries a bone charm carved from the ruins at Sirel. Each working she casts climbs the bark further up the arm, and fire burns through her roots faster than she can grow them. She is "Seeking a name for what she is becoming before it finishes without her". "Dirt doesn't lie. Neither do I". She would explain any of it "plainly if asked". Nobody knows whether the arm stops at her shoulder, "or whether that was only ever the story the commune told her to make the fear bearable".

**Elion Drevas · The Black Cleric.** "Defrocked Scholar. Wandering Absolver. Reflection-Bound Practitioner". A "Former Sanctum Lux Lector, expelled after the Mirrormass Incident, where he severed a Crevice-born Phenomenon by offering half his own Soul Crystal as tithe". "Human, Aether-touched. Age 31. Origin Eresse, New World, in a region he calls the Dominion of the Faithkeepers". "Now wanders the Inner Realms performing absolutions for those tainted by Wellspring corruption". The Crevice is "the Titan Valmir's own scar in the world"; at it, "He paid it off, in Crystal, and it left". "The order that trained him has never forgiven the method," and Sanctum Lux "Pursues him for "divine inversion," the order's own word". "He has no Axes". He reflects what is aimed at him, turns holy force to nothing, and the Forget-Me Veil lets him read "as absent to Fate itself" for several hours. "Every prayer he speaks drains his own vitality to stabilize nearby Wellspring distortions". His Oblation Rite draws corruption or a curse out of another person "into his own Soul Crystal"; it "Requires physical or ritual contact and the other party's consent", "It cannot be forced", and it "Shortens his remaining lifespan measurably with every use". His Vessel Codex has "Blank pages that write themselves in response to nearby sin resonance". A broken Archonic relic "steadily corrodes his memory". An unnamed Archon's final words to him are gone. He walks toward "The forgotten Well of Penanceflare", "somewhere in the Outer Realms". Nobody knows whether the corruption he takes "is actually destroyed, or simply stored".

**Leon Vaerenthal · The Farmer.** The Crimson Stray, Fang of the Fallen Field, the Farmer. "Unaligned wanderer. Formerly a farmer of Eld Hollow, Senoth borderland. Marked by the Scarlet Communion. Hunted by Sanctum Lux inquisitors". "His sister Maren died and he survived contact with what killed her", and he came out "with her structurally present in his Crystal, permanently". His card keeps "Maren's soulprint" as a record and calls the Lament "architecture". He has two Axes. Both are load-bearing, one living and one not. His Mortalis is "The curse half, and the reason the Communion wanted him", and "Observers' pulses accelerate near him and they cannot say why". His Ashen Guard is "not chosen": when someone he holds an Axis to is in danger, "crimson mist" hardens "into a standing wall, blood-formed from wounds he does not remember taking". "He does not control the onset and cannot decline it". "A stranger dying in front of him produces nothing at all, and he has been present for that". "Threaten Tilda and he will flare. Repeatedly, until the Crystal fails. The Communion knows this". Nobody knows "Whether Maren's echo is responding or merely recorded". His card has no relationships section.

## 3 · The shared history

Three events. None is in the archive. Each is new and fitted to every card it touches. Maeron's card alone states an era, the Withering Era. Every other card sets its member inside a living Holy Sea, a Sanctum Lux built on the Ladder of Standing, or an Accord that sends envoys to tea, so all three events fall in the Withering Era, with no year. The commune comes before the well. The parlour is unordered against both. No card's What nobody knows is touched: the Sea's plans for Lumen, the thread Seraphine cuts, Maeron's face and prophecy, the end of Beatrix's arm, the fate of what Elion stores, and Maren's echo all stay open.

### The blessing at the commune

- **When.** The Withering Era. The month after the petrification at Sirel, while Beatrix still hid the arm. The elders had not yet ruled. Lumen was already of the Aurum Sect, and already a child whose touch "soothes wounds, purifies minor corruption".
- **Where.** The outer territories of the Holy Sea of Alabaster (WORLD §3; Beatrix's card), at the root-healer commune, on the road of an Aurum Sect pilgrimage to a Dawn-Altar. No page places the territories, the commune or the altars, and this record places none of them.
- **Who.** Lumen Varos and Beatrix Vaelthorn; the elders; the escort.
- **What happened.** The Sect's escort brought Lumen along the pilgrims' road, and the road ran past the commune. The elders had heard what the Sea said of the boy. They asked the escort to stop. Beatrix was sent for. She came out into the yard with the arm in her sleeve, as she had worn it since Sirel. The elders told her to bare it, and she did. The bark ran down over the back of the hand, grey and split along the grain. The escort's cleric allowed a blessing, and Lumen laid his hand on the bark. The yard went quiet around him. Nothing in the arm changed. He kept his hand where it was until the cleric lifted it away, and the escort took him on toward the altar. Before the month was out the elders ruled the arm divine judgment and let her go.
- **What each took.**
  - Beatrix: an arm bared on the elders' order and unchanged under the Sea's own blessed child; the ruling that followed within the month; the road out of the commune. The sleeve stayed down for a while after that. Her card dates the end of the hiding to the day she began to use the arm.
  - Lumen: a hand laid on a corruption his touch did not answer, and lifted off it by the Sea's cleric.

### Tea after the Seven Refractions

- **When.** The Withering Era. After the Day of Seven Refractions; after the prophecy surge took Seraphine's eye and she kept her parlour; after the exposure at Eclipseron's Eye fused Maeron's mask. Unordered against the other two.
- **Where.** The White Parlour, Seraphine's salon in the Holy Sea of Alabaster (WORLD §3; her card calls her the White Parlour Seer). Whether it stands in her family's manor, her card does not say.
- **Who.** Seraphine Lurielle, Maeron Iskare, Lumen Varos; the Aurum Sect's escort; the Ecliptic Choir, who arranged the meeting.
- **What happened.** The rupture Lumen called on the Day of Seven Refractions had not been called by any forecast the Accord's councils held. The councils wanted it read, and the Obscurial Chamber sent Maeron. The Aurum Sect would not bring its initiate to an Accord reader in any house of its own. The Ecliptic Choir, his covenant partners inside the Sea, proposed the White Parlour, where Accord envoys already came under the pretext of tea. The escort brought the boy and stood inside the door. Seraphine poured. The boy drank. Maeron left his cup where she set it. He asked leave to take the boy into a shared sleep, with the hostess as the third, and to read the rupture from inside the sight that had seen it. Seraphine refused. She said it once. Maeron asked his questions across the table instead, and the boy answered each of them plainly: where he had stood, what he had seen, and that he had called it seconds before it struck. He did not explain how. The torque at Maeron's throat stayed cold while he spoke. When the teaset's hour came, the escort took Lumen home. Maeron filed his reading with the councils. By morning some small fact of his own was gone. The mask offered it back in the night, and he did not write it down.
- **What each took.**
  - Seraphine: a refusal made at her own table, in the hearing of the Accord she serves unofficially and of the Sea she serves by name; the Choir's knowledge that her parlour will say no to an Accord reader.
  - Maeron: a reading filed; a small fact of his own paid for it; a boy whose answers kept the torque cold.
  - Lumen: an Accord reader's questions, answered plainly; a hostess who would not let him be taken into anyone's sleep; an escort who watched all of it from the door.

### The soured well on the Rimlands road

- **When.** The Withering Era. After the Mirrormass Incident and Elion's expulsion, while he walked the Inner Realms absolving; after Beatrix left the commune and began to use the arm; after Maren's death, with Leon already marked by the Communion and hunted. After the commune event, and before any card's present.
- **Where.** A farm settlement on a road through the Rimlands of Stannvaard (WORLD §5). The Stannvaard page gives the Rimlands as "Roads, way-stations, timber and the agriculture feeding the crater population". Altherion and its Grand Church stand on the rim above.
- **Who.** Elion Drevas, Beatrix Vaelthorn, Leon Vaerenthal; the settlement's households; Sanctum Lux inquisitors, unnamed.
- **What happened.** The settlement's well had soured. Wellspring corruption had come up into the water, and the households that drank from it were sick. Elion came up the road alone. He went from bed to bed. To each who consented he put his hands, and he drew the corruption out of them into his own Crystal. At dusk he prayed over the well, and the prayers drained him while the water steadied. Beatrix came in from the timber side. She knelt in the yard and laid the bark arm flat on the ground. She listened. The sour thread ran under the yard from the old well toward the fields. Upslope, past the last barn, the ground held clean water and no thread. Leon had hired on for the harvest. He took a spade to the place she marked and dug until the water stood in the hole. While he worked, every pulse in the yard ran faster, and the ground gave the change to Beatrix. She told him so, plainly. Elion read the curse in him at a look and offered him the rite. Leon refused. Elion did not ask again. Beatrix asked Elion what her arm was becoming. He read it for a long while and gave her no name. The riders came before the new well was lined. Beatrix felt their horses through the ground before anyone saw them, and she said so. Leon was gone by the timber track. Elion drew the Forget-Me Veil about himself and stayed at the beds. The inquisitors watered their horses at the new well, asked after a farmer by name, and rode on toward Altherion. Nobody in the yard told them which way he had gone.
- **What each took.**
  - Elion: the sickness of the households who consented, carried in his own Crystal; the drain of the prayers at the well; a curse he was refused leave to take; an arm he read and could not name; hours under the Veil in the sight of the order that pursues him.
  - Beatrix: a well found with the arm she no longer hid; no name for it from a former Lector, whose grade is the first the Scale permits to say what a reading means; a stranger's pulse read through the ground and told to him.
  - Leon: a well dug with his own hands; the curse kept, by his own refusal; a warning through the ground that put him on the timber track ahead of the order that hunts him.

## 6 · Same person?

- "The Lilac Lumen" is Seraphine's eye. It is not Lumen Varos.
- Lumen Varos and Draen Varos (c16) share a surname and nothing else the cards record.
- Beatrix Vaelthorn and the unattested "Caldris Vaelthorn" on Zahir Ashveil's card share a surname. No kinship is made.

- The Mirror Siege on Maeron's card and the Mirrormass Incident on Elion's are two events.

## 7 · Where the cards disagree

Quoted and left open.

1. **What rules Alabaster.** Seraphine's card: "Alabaster Noble" (I) and "An Alabaster prince, once · Warned him off a coronation date that would have killed him within the week" (`Seraphine Lurielle · Lilac Gaze.md`, IX). 2. **Indenture inside the Sea.** Beatrix's card: "formerly indentured to a root-healer commune under the Holy Sea of Alabaster's outer territories" (I). The Palatine's card sets the Sea's abolition doctrine against "every polity that trades in persons" (relationships, Meren).

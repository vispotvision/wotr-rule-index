# c35 · Malphas's cell

Malphas, the Ferment of Stars, and the four who work under him at the Greyshaft Nine coldhouse: Ilmar Foss, an instrument-maker struck from the Hexagonal Oath; Nuvalik, a Far-Northern butcher and anatomist who carries a name the dead carried before her; Vesk, the formulator of the Verdantia growth; and Xu Deming, of a trading lineage, who is the purse.

**Era.** The archive's present, in the Withering Era. The one archived scene, `scenes/the_nights_watch.md`, falls after Vaeloris reformed ("Vaeloris had reformed after the void took it", l. 37), in the Guild Accord's jurisdiction, under a capital the scene does not name whose Logistics Division publishes its returns. No source gives a year, and this record gives none. Malphas's card gives two Catalysts and dates neither. The card's body is the form after the Becoming; the scene's Malphas has hands that keep a living colour. This record places its events in the span the card leaves between his first Catalyst and the Becoming, the one span in which card and scene both hold, and it describes his body in no event it adds. Section 7 keeps the disagreement open. The Rot Host (c23) sets its own events after this scene, so the cell comes before the Host.

**Sources read.** The five cards in full, at the roster's `file` paths: `Malphas · Ferment of Stars.md`, `Ilmar Foss.md`, `Nuvalik.md`, `Vesk.md`, `Xu Deming.md`. The scene in full, all three movements; the cell appears only in the second, "What Does Not Smell Dead", told from Vesk. Outside the cluster: the cards of Nol Tally, Edwyn Brack, Qiu Yinzhi and Wystan Ashmore, the scene's other side, and `imports/lore/clusters/c23.md`, the Rot Host. The wiki: The Articles of the Accord (Article XV, *De voce*, and the Bench of Attribution's four powers); The Guild Accord in the Modern Era (the Divisions, the academies and the charter); The Concordance of Ages (the Guild of Measurewrights under the Hexagonal Oath); The Heresiology of Alchemy (Fermentation Perverted, which Malphas's card quotes); The Works and Days and The Standing and the Title (Greyshaft Twelve, a sister station). The Chinese and Far-Northern strata in `sources/WOTR_Inner_World_Naming_Amendment.md` §X and §XI. `book/night-watch-zombification/bible.md` and its chapters, which are no canon, were read so that nothing here crosses them.

**The archive.** One scene holds all five. The coldhouse, the cell, Project Zombification, the Swale line and the Hollow Shaft people appear in no other scene and on no wiki page. Beyond the scene, everything in section 3 is new and built to fit every card it touches.

## 1 · Who is here

**Malphas · Ferment of Stars** (malphas-ferment-of-stars). Called "The Living Cauldron. Ferment of Stars. Lord of Absolute Rot. The Bound Reaper." His standing: "Expelled from the Pyraeon Forge Academy and declared a metaphysical threat. Unbound in life. Bound now only to himself." His first Catalyst came when "He stopped working alone, and what answered was not a Wellspring in the ordinary relationship." The second, the Becoming, was recognition "from something that does not sit on a throne", and after it "What's left of him reads as a hood and a robe before it reads as a person": a skull the colour of old parchment, small gold crosses at the throat, a crown of pale spurs, a long dark blade etched in gold, and the Golden Remnant at his hip, a lesser skeleton in bone-gold that holds the rest of his Essence Core. "The Heresiology named him without naming him": "Fermentation perverted", whose listed tell "is that it arrives crowned with achievements". Whatever is delivered into him "finishes as something rotting", and "He holds more than he can process at all times". The card names no one who works under him. In the scene he sits on the gauge bench with his coat folded beside him. "He was not a large man." He reads a forester's life "the way a man reads a bill of lading", and he names the work Project Zombification because "I would prefer the file be built off a word I chose."

**Ilmar Foss** (ilmar-foss). Instrument-maker. "Struck from the Hexagonal Oath eleven years before the scene, for a thing he still calls, when drunk, a rounding convention." He does coil and instrument work and "Understands attribution practice from the inside." "He is the one who states why the Ferriby residue reads clean: nothing in the cell ever couples, and the deer's own Lattice runs the working and signs it." He has "The steadiest hands in the building", a jeweller's glass screwed into his eye and coils in pieces on the bench. He "Calls the synthesis filthy and says so", and he "Presses Malphas on the walking dose: no Crystal, no self, no reason to do anything." His card keeps his register open: Foss is the attested Accord roll-name for Far-Northern households, and the card does not say whether it is his.

**Nuvalik** (nuvalik). Far-Northern. "Butcher and anatomist of the ferment." Her name is "a carried name that belonged to three people before her. She explained this to Vesk once and never again." She works the carcasses and reads what the ferment did: "the connective scaffold is eaten and the cells are left, so a hind stands structurally liquid and cellularly whole, held up only by the hide." She "Got contraction out of the fourth hind's shoulder twice and did not report it." She works with her sleeves past the elbow and an apron on, with a butchering knife and the hook, and does not ask for the lamp. She "Wipes the blade before she speaks, which is the one thing about her that frightens Vesk." She "Has objected twice that a carcass no bird will touch stays where it fell and gets counted." Her proverb: where she was raised, "the sky finishes what the ground starts, and when the sky refuses, people walk out to see why." She "Hesitates before fetching the hook for the ninth hind, then goes."

**Vesk** (vesk). Formulator, one name only. "Author of the Verdantia growth formulation": the "third dilution, introduced live and carried on the animal's own circulation." "Raised in a village that put stones on its graves." Two grafts on the hands: his fingers "were longer before the second graft", the nails on the two that took "came in ridged and yellow", and his hands no longer close, so he carries things out in front of him in both palms, "the way a server carries wine." He "Loses most thoughts now, the way he loses most things, and keeps the pleasure." He "Delights in the dead getting up." He calls Malphas "Lord and a genius", and he "Has watched eleven days for a sign of what Stage Malphas is and has not asked." He counts the coldhouse drip. The scene's middle movement is told from him.

**Xu Deming** (xu-deming). "The purse." Lineage-hall register, Chinese stratum: "Capped young into a trading lineage. He took a courtesy name he does not much like." "He is the buyer of record for the Verdantia." He "Diverted the Verdantia from a legitimate supply contract on the Swale line and drew it down as wastage against a pumping array that had shown four years of flat wastage." Hat in his hands, rain on his shoulders, careful diction; "His thumb works round the brim when he is afraid." He "Knows Article Fifteen puts the liability on the payer and that the payer is him", gives the Logistics Division eleven weeks to find the Swale line's bad spring, "and wants it noted that he said so before it happened." He "Argues against naming the project because a named project is a filed project, citing the Hollow Shaft people." His thought on the page: "he has thirty-one days of usefulness left, and Malphas has already counted them."

## 3 · The shared history

Three events. The last is the archive's. The first two are new, fitted to the cards and the scene, and set before it. No carrier, informant or supplier outside the cell is named, and no event says where the nine hinds on the rail came from (section 7).

### The first reading at the bench

- **When.** Before the doses went into the field at the Ferriby cut and the other counties, while the cell was proving its rot synthesis at Greyshaft Nine. Malphas on the night of the report: "We have been practising rot synthesis and the slow breakdown of biological matrices, and we have proved it out" (l. 205).
- **Where.** The Greyshaft Nine coldhouse, on its capped main: the table and the gauge bench (scene, l. 103, 115, 231). Accord jurisdiction; no page places it in a quarter.
- **Who.** Ilmar Foss, Nuvalik, Vesk, Malphas.
- **What happened.** The first beast the ferment had finished was laid on the coldhouse table. Nuvalik opened it. The connective net was gone and the cells were whole, and the carcass gave off no smell. Foss put a coil over the residue and it read clean. He said at the bench why it read clean: nothing in the room had coupled, and the beast's own Lattice had run the working and signed it. Vesk stood at the bench and heard it. Malphas heard it out. Nuvalik said that a carcass with no smell draws no bird down to it, and that such a carcass stays where it fell and gets counted. It was the first of the two times she said it before the night of the report.
- **What each took.**
  - Ilmar Foss: the account of the clean reading, which Malphas calls on him to give again on the night of the report ("Tell him why it reads clean").
  - Vesk: the account itself. By the night of the report Foss could say to him, "You know it and you do not use it", and Vesk still held that the purity was the elegant part.
  - Nuvalik: the objection her card counts twice, the first of the two.
  - Malphas: the practice Foss states for the whole cell at the report: "We draw nothing. We couple nothing."

### The draw off the Swale line

- **When.** After the ferment was proven at the bench and before the doses reached the Ferriby cut. On the night of the report Deming gives the Logistics Division eleven weeks to find it.
- **Where.** The Swale line, the pumping array whose wastage column goes into a Logistics return (scene, l. 173), and then Vesk's bench at Greyshaft Nine.
- **Who.** Xu Deming, Vesk, Malphas.
- **What happened.** The field doses needed more Verdantia than Deming could buy. He took it off a legitimate supply contract on the Swale line and drew it down as wastage, against a pumping array whose wastage had lain flat for four years, and the record names him as the buyer. The stock came to Vesk's bench at Greyshaft Nine. Vesk made the third dilution from it, to be introduced live and carried on the animal's own circulation.
- **What each took.**
  - Xu Deming: the standing of buyer of record, and with it the liability Article Fifteen lays on the payer. On the night of the report he knows the payer is him.
  - Vesk: the stock his growth formulation needed, in the quantity the field doses asked.
  - Malphas: a working stock bought in another man's name.

### What Does Not Smell Dead (the archive)

- **When.** The night of The Night's Watch, in the archive's present. The nine hinds had hung on the rail eleven days (l. 105), and the Ferriby tin had lain five days unfiled: "She has been carrying it for five days and she has not filed it." (l. 201).
- **Where.** The Greyshaft Nine coldhouse, on a main capped four years: the rail, the gauge bench, the ice press in the next room, the drip in the far corner (l. 103 to 105).
- **Who.** Malphas, Vesk, Ilmar Foss, Nuvalik, Xu Deming.
- **What happened.** Vesk came up the rail with the flask in both palms and told Malphas the experiments had gone as he said. Malphas had Foss say why the residue reads clean. Foss called the synthesis filthy and gave the reason: nothing in the room couples, and the animal's own Lattice runs the working and signs it. Nuvalik reported the bodies: the scaffold eaten, the cells left, no smell. She called that a fault, said so a third time on Malphas's word, and told him he had made a thing that sits in a field waiting to be counted. Vesk gave the growth. Deming, at the door with his hat in his hands, put the cost in the room, told them he had diverted the Verdantia off the Swale line and bought it, and set out the clock of the Logistics return and the reach of Article Fifteen. Malphas answered that the two objections were one: the work had an edge. He named Tally and the girl with the unfiled tin. He set the next work: the dose made to walk, on a contractile matrix, with no anchor, since an anchor is a binding and a binding is a name. He named Nuvalik's unreported contraction and said he would discuss it with her separately. He named the task Project Zombification, and when Deming objected he ordered the name used by all of them, in writing and in correspondence. Vesk thought the name was doing something besides the deer and lost the thought. Malphas ordered the ninth hind to the table. Nuvalik stood still for a while at the tail of the rail, then went and got the hook.
- **What each took.**
  - Malphas: the finding that the work has an edge, the next work set against it, and a name of his choosing on the project, to be used in writing.
  - Ilmar Foss: his objection to the walking dose on the record, answered by Malphas: "I need a gradient." His "So we stop" was answered too: "We stop having edges."
  - Nuvalik: her fault heard a third time and made the ground of the next work; her unreported contraction named in front of the room, with a separate word owed; the order for the ninth hind, which she carried out. The scene gives her, at the rail, the thought that she has spent all three names for this body on this work (l. 155).
  - Vesk: his growth made the base of the next work, and the dead to get up. "Vesk felt the whole thing arrive at once and had to put his hands flat on the bench."
  - Xu Deming: his warning spoken before the fact, as he asked; the name he argued against, now to go into the correspondence he feared; and, on the page, the count of his useful days, which he believes Malphas already holds.

## 6 · Same person?

No. Five cards, five people. The four stubs come from one scene, each with its own trade, register and body, and the scene sets all four in one room with Malphas.

The question this cluster does carry is whether the scene's Malphas is the card's. The roster joins them. The four stubs name "Malphas's cell", and the Tally and Brack cards and c23 read the scene's man as the card's. The scene's own archive note lists "Malphas" among the "New names in play with no card yet" (l. 399). This record reads card and scene as one man and one life, with the scene before the Becoming, and it keeps the disagreement open in section 7.

## 7 · Where the cards disagree

Quoted and left open.

1. **Malphas's body.** The card: "What's left of him reads as a hood and a robe before it reads as a person", "The skull inside the hood is bone the color of old parchment", and "Nothing about either body has weight in the way a living thing has weight" (`Malphas · Ferment of Stars.md`, I.V), with the Golden Remnant that "travels at his hip or a half-pace behind". The scene: he sits "with his coat folded beside him" (l. 115); "He was not a large man" (l. 183); "Eleven days and the man's fingers had stayed the colour of a living man's fingers in a room at four degrees" (l. 183). The scene shows no Remnant.
2. **Whether the scene's Malphas has a card.** The scene's archive note: "New names in play with no card yet: Wystan, Qiu Yinzhi, Nol Tally, Edwyn Brack, Malphas, Vesk, Ilmar Foss, Nuvalik, Xu Deming, Lu Sanjing." (l. 399). The roster joins the scene to `Malphas · Ferment of Stars.md`, and the four stubs name "Malphas's cell".
3. **Nuvalik's names: one or three.** Her card: "a carried name that belonged to three people before her" (`Nuvalik.md`, I) and "The three dead whose name she carries" (XI), against "she has spent all three names for this body on this work" (XIV). The scene: "carrying a name that had belonged to three people before her" (l. 143) and "Three names for this body and I have spent all three of them on this." (l. 155). The Far-Northern stratum allows either: "A person may carry several." (naming amendment, §XI).
4. **The carcass count, against Deming's figure.** Tally's insert, as Wystan reads it: "eleven hinds and a stag inside a quarter mile" (l. 11), and Malphas repeats the eleven (l. 197). Deming prices "nine hinds and a stag" (l. 173), and Wystan says "Nine hinds and a stag are dead in a cut in the north" (l. 345). The rail at Greyshaft Nine holds "Nine hinds" (l. 105). Tally found the Ferriby carcasses standing on the ninth and went back to them on the tenth, and the rail's hinds had hung there eleven days by the night of the report.
5. **Whether the Pyraeon Forge Academy is attested** (c23's first item, repeated here because it stands on Malphas's card). Malphas: "Pyraeon Forge Academy stands as the institution that expelled him, unrelated in etymology, coincidental in name only" (VI). Zeraphine Drowl: "the Pyraeon Forge Academy as an institution is not, and this is its second appearance after Malphas's own expulsion from it." (head).

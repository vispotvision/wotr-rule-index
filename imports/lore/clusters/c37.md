# c37 · Road-folk and the Eastward Isles

Five people who work the roads and the coasts at the edge of the Guild Accord's reach. Shiro Kitsunari, a boy, runs an informal courier circuit on the Outer Wilds. Raymond Montclair is a wandering luthier whom the Accord's escorts put up with, and Elthiré Maen is a Pathwarden of the Accord who goes about in a vagabond minstrel's colours and carries the Accord's word under them. The other two answer to the Archive of the Eastward Isles, or once did. Liorien Nael-Teth is one of its archivists; Cyrus Vellhar is an apprentice who fled it.

**Era.** Elthiré's card sets him in the Withering Era and dates his service; this record gives no year (WORLD.md §1). Raymond's card sets him "in the late Imperial Age". The Withering Era is the Imperial Age's latest era and has not closed, so the phrase can reach it. It can also reach the end of the Long Reckoning. This record does not choose, and it has Raymond meet no member. Shiro's, Liorien's and Cyrus's cards give no era. Shiro's and Liorien's cards bind them to the Accord, and Cyrus's names it, so all three live in the Accord's years. The grove event in section 3 places Shiro in the Withering Era, beside Elthiré. The two Archive events carry no era.

## 1 · Who is here

**Shiro Kitsunari · Little White Fox** (shiro-kitsunari-little-white-fox). Called Little White Fox and the Bell-On-Branch: "Grove Scout. Wellspring-Sick Sentinel. Tree Perch Courier". He works the Outer Wilds as "An Independent Concord Freeholder courier, working an informal circuit for the Guild Accord, unaffiliated and holding no charter of his own". He is "Loyal first to his home grove". He is a boy, with "A light, underfed child's body". As a toddler he fell from a high branch, and "the forest bent a net of branches that had no business bending to catch him". Trees still bend to keep him safe. A small fox-shrine bell in his hair, the Bell of the First Branch, warns him "with a private chime nothing else can hear". A forest priest made him an anklet of living bark. His signature is the Leaf-Listening Veil. He lets his Aether into bark and leaf, and the wood gives him back footfalls, breath and "the first patches of a Wellspring going quiet, out to the edge of a grove". It needs living wood. Held too long, it comes back as headache and nosebleed, and a stretch of real deafness follows. His Dream Moss Lullaby puts beasts and panicked travellers to sleep, and overuse leaves him pale and hungry. His mentors, unnamed, call his reading "instinct running ahead of training". Caravans on the Outer Wilds know him as "the boy who runs ahead along the treeline and does not get seen doing it". Asked what he heard and how, he answers plainly. His card leaves open "Whether the forest caught him because of what he already was, or made him what he is by choosing to catch him."

**Raymond Montclair · The Luthier of Quiet Roads** (raymond-montclair-the-luthier-of-quiet-roads). Also the Ash-Tone Pilgrim and the Soft-Handed Witness. "Mortal human, Verdant-flecked ancestry". "Wandering Luthier. Memory-Bearer". His card makes him an "Unbound wayfarer, tolerated rather than sponsored by the Guild Accord escorts he travels alongside, in the late Imperial Age", with "No formal allegiance to any crown or order". His first Spirit Axis bond formed with the Ashwood Lute of Montclair, and it runs through the lute still. His Crystal "stores a held note". Nearby Aether steadies around him, and panic eases in anyone close enough to hear him play. Objects he repairs "retain emotional impressions longer than they otherwise would". His travelling case holds his tools and keepsakes and anchors "the memories he carries but does not carry alone". He pays for the work himself. Resonance blurs his memories at the edges, and too much of it dulls joy and grief alike. He has steadied close to sixty people across the border villages, one vigil and one repaired instrument at a time, and "Nobody has ever asked him to count them and he never has". He has never used his discipline to hurt anybody. Border villages know him as "the man who shows up before the worst of a dispute and leaves before anyone thinks to thank him properly". At Burnt Orchard he restored the chapel's instrument. In doing so he "quietly halted a local Wellspring flare nobody else had noticed building". He held a three-hour resonance vigil at the Hollow Bridge, and it "prevented a skirmish without a blade drawn on either side". He answers a direct question about his craft and does not volunteer the explanation. His card leaves open "How much of his own grief he has already flattened out of himself keeping other people's steady."

**Elthiré Maen · The Wandering String** (elthire-maen-the-wandering-string). Also Keeper of Forgotten Roads. He is a "Guild Accord field operative under the colour of a vagabond minstrel" who "Carries messages, omens and solace across Wellspring-scarred frontiers". His post: "Guild Accord Pathwarden, classified Echo Courier of the Ninth Route, one of twelve envoys entrusted to traverse unanchored Wellspring territories and maintain Accord presence through non-militant means". He came from Caedor and travelled on through the Inner World and Babyl, which his card marks struck. His card sets him in the Withering Era. He wears travelling robes in verdant and saffron layers, his eyes are grey-green, and the songbird Pell rides his shoulder. He came to his power by listening. "The soul-cost is that he carries every tone he has ever given form to". He plays the Lyre of Seven Winds, carved from dreamwood harvested near a worked Core of Benediction, whose tonal fields steady minor Wellsprings. The Feather of Florwyn in his hat glows faintly, his card says, when truth is spoken, and the Guild Charm of Echo Route IX sends melody-coded messages across Accord channels. When he plays, a room turns toward mercy and a divided crowd comes to one purpose. He must be heard, and he does not compel. On ground starved of Essence the growth in him goes on with nothing to feed it. When injustice rings too loud, his music hardens into a storm, and that is rare. He mediated between the hymn-monks at the Borders of Babyl and Concord agents after the Rift Refrain Incident, and he was last seen there. He will name what he does to anyone who asks. His word is "Carry the story, not the glory". His card leaves open "What the Ninth Route carries when it carries no song."

**Liorien Nael-Teth · Sleepless Quill** (liorien-nael-teth-sleepless-quill). Called the Sleepless Quill, the Drift-Scribe of the Eastward Isles, the Tide-Ink Archivist and the Little Book of Storms; "He Who Hears What Pages Remember". "Island Archivist. Memory-Sensitive Investigator. Scriptorium Consultant to the Guild Accord". He is an archivist-member of the Archive of the Eastward Isles and consults for the Accord on maritime and memory-sensitive cases. He is a "Veyran human, of a Brine-Touched Scribe line". He stands 4'9", soft and underfed, with deep umber skin, sleep-shadowed amber eyes and teal-blue hair the wind keeps tossing. He wears navy and cream robes stitched with gold tide-patterns, and a feather quill at his collar, stained at the tip. His first Spirit Axis bond is "to the ledger itself, not to a person". Every working he does is written. He writes on paper, breath-fog, sea spray or dust. Rooms go quiet near him, and "most people feel an odd urge to speak softly and be honest". When he is tired enough, his half-lidded stare reads faint overlays of what a person or object has witnessed. His signature is the Echo-Current Folio. It throws a storm of charm-papers into a half-dome that catches what crosses it and records it for replay. "The dome only records what happens inside it". Its price is ledger bleed. Each major casting writes part of his memory into the ink, and what is left untranscribed goes hollow. His card puts the price of the Broken Shell Night at several months of his memory, the night he "trapped and replayed an ancient leviathan's echo long enough for senior archivists to rebind it". In the Drowned Ledger Affair a Black Concord cell tried to erase a whole fishing village's testimony of a smuggling route, and "his reconstructed ledger is what survived them". His testimony is precedent now in three Guild Accord coastal courts. His card notes a trajectory, a forbidden True Ledger of the Sea Wars, "which both Concord and Crowns would rather stayed unwritten". It leaves open "Whether the memories he loses to the ink are recoverable at all".

**Cyrus Vellhar · The Azure Warden** (cyrus-vellhar-the-azure-warden). Also the Wavesong Trickster, the Tide-Borne Savant and the Laughing Undertow; "He Who Smiles Where Currents Turn". He is "Human, with a latent aquatic-Aether predisposition carried through a Mirithane-sensitive mutation". He is a former apprentice of the Archive of Eastward Islands, "now an independent Wellspring drifter", with "No Guild Accord rank claimed". He reads people "the way he was trained to read tides". His first Spirit Axis bond is to an Abyssal-touched memory shell that whispered back, "an attachment to something that answered him, which is why he fled rather than reported it". During the Archive incident a micro-domain formed around him, the Laughing Shoal: "ripple-distortion, sound-warp, sharpened illusion", with a field that pushes unstable minds toward fear or euphoria. Before he fled he took a shard of the Archive's Dream-Tideglass, and he carries it. The Archive is "Loosely friendly, formally unreliable in their record of him. Sends people to retrieve him; he keeps ahead of them". He teaches coastal children small illusions, unpaid, "in towns he does not stay in long". In the Drowned Mirage Event he stopped a raider band with illusory tidal doubles until none of them could tell which one to hit. His Hydrowave Coil needs standing water or heavy humidity. Overexertion turns his focus to apathy and his intent to drift. He explains everything, cheerfully, and "the explanation is usually a misdirection in itself". His card leaves open "What the Abyssal-touched shell actually said, and whether it is still listening for an answer."

## 3 · The shared history

Four events, all new. The first two bind the Archive's pair. The last two bind Elthiré to each of the other two on the road: once face to face, once through an instrument. No event joins the Archive's pair to the road's three. Nothing on the cards earns one, and this record forces none.

### The Broken Shell Night

- **When.** While Cyrus was an apprentice of the Archive and Liorien one of its archivists. Liorien's card names the night, and Cyrus's names the Archive incident; this record makes them one night. No card dates it, and neither does this record.
- **Where.** The Archive of the Eastward Isles, which both cards name and no page places.
- **Who.** Liorien Nael-Teth, Cyrus Vellhar.
- **What happened.** The Archive had held an ancient leviathan's echo bound, and on the Broken Shell Night the echo came loose inside it. Liorien raised the Echo-Current Folio. His charm-papers broke into a small storm and closed in a half-dome over the echo. The dome caught the echo and played it back, and caught it again, for as long as the senior archivists needed to bind it a second time. They bound it. The casting wrote several months of Liorien's memory into the ink. Elsewhere in the Archive that night, Cyrus sat with an Abyssal-touched memory shell. It whispered. He answered, and it answered him back. Ripples ran out of true around him, sound warped, and the Laughing Shoal formed where he was. Before the seniors were done with the echo, he took a shard of the Archive's Dream-Tideglass and fled. He did not report the shell. The dome records what happens inside it, and Cyrus was never inside it.
- **What each took.**
  - Liorien: several months of his own memory, written into the Folio's pages. What he has of those months is what the pages kept. His card leaves open whether he can ever have them back.
  - Cyrus: his first Spirit Axis bond, to a shell that answered him; the Laughing Shoal; the Tideglass Shard; and a flight he has kept up ever since, because the bond was never reported.

### The town Cyrus had left

- **When.** After the Broken Shell Night, in the time the Archive has been sending people to bring Cyrus back (his card).
- **Where.** A coastal town, which neither card names, where Cyrus had taught children and moved on. His card: "Coastal children · He teaches them small illusions, unpaid, in towns he does not stay in long".
- **Who.** Liorien Nael-Teth, Cyrus Vellhar.
- **What happened.** The Archive sent Liorien after its apprentice. He came to a coastal town Cyrus had passed through, and Cyrus had already gone. The children remembered him. Near Liorien they spoke softly and told the truth, as most people do near him, and they showed him the small illusions the drifter had taught them for no pay. Liorien wrote down what they showed him and what they said. He went back to the Archive with their testimony and without Cyrus.
- **What each took.**
  - Liorien: a page of children's testimony about the man the Archive wanted. It went into the Archive's record of Cyrus, which Cyrus's card calls formally unreliable.
  - Cyrus: one more town left ahead of the Archive, as his card says he keeps ahead of them. He never saw the archivist. The illusions stayed with the children.

### The quiet at the grove's edge

- **When.** In the Withering Era, Elthiré's era, while he walked the Ninth Route and Shiro, still a boy, ran his circuit. Shiro's card gives him no era of his own.
- **Where.** The edge of Shiro's home grove on the Outer Wilds, beside a trade route his circuit runs (Shiro's card). No page places the Outer Wilds, and c05 leaves them unplaced.
- **Who.** Shiro Kitsunari, Elthiré Maen.
- **What happened.** Shiro let his Aether into the bark at the edge of his home grove, and the wood gave him back the first patches of the Wellspring under it going quiet. He ran the word along his circuit, ahead of the caravans on the treeline, as he ran the rest of the Accord's traffic. It reached the Pathwarden of the Ninth Route. Elthiré came to the grove's edge with Pell on his shoulder and asked the boy what he had heard and how. Shiro told him plainly. Then Elthiré played the Lyre of Seven Winds on the quiet ground, for as long as the ground needed. Shiro kept his hands on the bark while he played. Through the wood he heard the rhythm come back. He held the veil too long. His nose bled, and for a stretch afterwards he heard nothing at all. The ground had given Elthiré little to feed on, and he left thinner than he came.
- **What each took.**
  - Shiro: the rhythm back in the wood at his grove's edge, heard through the bark; a nosebleed and a stretch of deafness for holding the veil past its time; and an envoy of the Accord, which he serves by proxy, who knows him by sight and asked him what he heard.
  - Elthiré: the grove's tone, carried now with every other tone he has given form to; a runner on the Outer Wilds whose word reached the Ninth Route before the quiet spread; and the thinning that starved ground costs him.

### The instrument at Burnt Orchard

- **When.** Raymond's mending in the late Imperial Age (his card), and Elthiré's playing after it, in the Withering Era, on the Ninth Route. This record does not fix the time between. The two never meet.
- **Where.** The chapel at Burnt Orchard (Raymond's card), on a road the Accord's escorts travel. No page places it.
- **Who.** Raymond Montclair, Elthiré Maen.
- **What happened.** Raymond came to Burnt Orchard on the road with an Accord escort. He restored the chapel's instrument, and in the restoring he halted a local Wellspring flare that nobody else had noticed building. Then he went on with the escort. Afterwards the Ninth Route brought Elthiré to the same chapel, and he played there. The instrument Raymond had mended still held what it had felt, longer than an instrument otherwise holds anything. Elthiré heard it. He gave that tone form in the chapel. The village could tell him that a luthier had come with the escort and gone on. What they told him went back onto the Ninth Route with him.
- **What each took.**
  - Raymond: one more instrument that keeps what he steadied. He does not count the people he has steadied, and nobody has ever asked him to. He took nothing from Elthiré, whom he never met.
  - Elthiré: the tone the mended instrument held, which he carries now with the rest, and the luthier's story, which he carries on the Ninth Route by his own word: "Carry the story, not the glory."

## 6 · Same person?

No. Five cards, five people, and no card's life overlaps another's in a way that could make two of them one.

The Archive's two differ in standing, lineage and craft. Liorien is an archivist-member, a Veyran human of a Brine-Touched Scribe line, who writes every working. Cyrus is a former apprentice, a human with a Mirithane-sensitive mutation, who works water and illusion. The two musicians differ in allegiance, instrument and origin. Raymond is unbound, a mortal of Verdant-flecked ancestry who plays an ashwood lute. Elthiré is a Pathwarden out of Caedor who plays the Lyre of Seven Winds.

## 7 · Where the cards disagree

1. **The Archive's name, and whether any other card attests it.** Liorien: "Archivist-Member of the Archive of the Eastward Isles" (I). 3. **Echo Courier: a classification, or a post held off the register.** Elthiré: "Guild Accord Pathwarden, classified Echo Courier of the Ninth Route" (I). Arven Solthiel: "an unregistered Echo Courier for the Guild Accord", "a distinct post from Eira Vale's Echo Runner elsewhere in the wiki mirror" (I). No Guild page defines the term.
4. **Which volume.** Cyrus: "Filed to Volume V, Character Cards on migration." (X). Raymond's card also names Volume V (X). Both pages sit in the Volume I card section.

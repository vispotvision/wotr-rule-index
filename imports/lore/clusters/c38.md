# c38 · Sun-Sealed and the horizon couriers

Five people who work the Outer World's roads and courts for the Guild Accord or beside it. The Arbitration Division's Bound Duelist carries a living seal on his chest. A courier of the Logistics and Supply Division runs sealed packages across ground where Domains do not hold. A Beastkin vagabond on a cloud couriers relics for the sky-trading pavilions and owes favours he has not paid. A historian of the Arbitration Division reads the ground and cannot lie. A former mediator of that Division bound his soul to a Celestial Wellspring when a treaty broke, and he is dissolving by it. The cards name one another in four pairs: Kaelen Raive and Rikkan Sahr, Kaelen Raive and Saruin Kye, Rikkan Sahr and Zahiren Vahl, Zahiren Vahl and Cael Veridion. No archived scene holds any of them. Canon order holds throughout: Isaac's direction, then the card, then the archived scenes, then the wiki, then this record.

## 1 · Who is here

**Kaelen Raive · Sun-Sealed** (kaelen-raive-sun-sealed), Warden of the Dawning Sigil, is "Solar Acolyte. Wellspring Arbiter. Bound Duelist of the Arbitration Division". The Division has him "assigned as an enforcer for truth-binding trials and sanctum duels where disputes over Wellspring claims or Concord law turn violent". The golden spiral on his chest "is a living Parun seal". It channels Radiance "in bursts strong enough to blind or sear", and at full charge it binds a Domain shut, so that "the practitioner holding it finds their own law suspended while the trial concludes". The price is written plainly. "Full charge takes time he must survive". He anchors himself or pins an opponent with his own weight: "He weighs more than he should, and the room agrees". Within a small radius around him lies and illusion fail. It is "a trait feared in political courts". He cannot negotiate. The counter his card gives is "Talk to him". "He is what the Arbitration Division sends when the other four have finished talking". His Catalyst was a decision, made on purpose, "that the seal is the verdict". He holds an honorary rank among the Outer World Sun-Monks "due to lineage and sigil heritage", and High Arbiter Selhane is his "Mentor and political handler within the Arbitration Division". His line: "The light doesn't ask for permission. It simply shows what was always there".

**Rikkan Sahr · The Sand-Skipper** (rikkan-sahr-the-sand-skipper), the Horizon Courier of the Concord, is "Nomadic Scout. Wellspring Courier. Relic Retrieval Specialist". For the Logistics and Supply Division he is "messenger and secure delivery runner between Concord-controlled Wellsprings, particularly across Outer World frontiers where Domain stability is low". He "Also does freelance smuggling for Zahiren Vahl when the Arbitration Division needs "off-the-record" transfers". His Essence runs through his muscles in a spiral and gives him sudden bursts of speed. He leaves flickering afterimages when he turns. His luck comes in "Bursts of improbable luck when in motion", when "enemy blades slip on sand, arrows misalign, traps misfire". It "Works only while actively moving". "The moment he stops, the luck stops with him". "He cannot fight", and the card's reading is that "he has never fought anyone and does not intend to". Its counter: "Make him stand still". He reads Essence storms "by their flow" and can outrun "certain Domain collapses". His Catalyst was soul-cost accepted "to preserve something valued above his own structural integrity", a decision made "in advance, in cold blood", and "The card does not say what was in the satchel". The L.S.D. Quartermasters give him priority work "due to unmatched delivery times". With the Outer World Caravan Clans he "Keeps neutral standing by exchanging news and small relics as gifts". His line: "If you see dust on the horizon, it's either a storm or me. And I'm worse for whoever's chasing you".

**Saruin Kye · Golden Laugh** (saruin-kye-golden-laugh), Cloud-Borne and Keeper of the West Wind, is "Celestial Vagabond. Trickster Hero. Relic Courier for the Outer World's Sky-Trading Pavilions". His affiliation is "Independent". He keeps loose alliances to the Kuromaki Association, "a Beastkin guild that respects his uncanny ability to "lose" dangerous artifacts into safe hands", and to the Guardians of Tres Aures, "mostly because he owes them "more than one" favour". He is Beastkin. Golden lines of Essence under his skin give him "short bursts of strength beyond normal Beastkin limits", and a neighbour's card calls him "the monkey". He travels on "a living cloud of condensed Wellspring essence" that can carry passengers or hide objects. Around him the outcome shifts, "turning a sure hit into a miss, or a failing bridge into one that holds just long enough for him to cross". His cost: "He cannot hold ground, cannot protect a place, and cannot end a fight". "Everything he does is deferral". He "Pretends to work for" the Sky Market Elders and uses their networks "for gossip and travel passes". Hyo Miran is his friendly rival. What nobody knows is "How many of the dangerous artifacts he "lost" are actually somewhere safe". His line: "Why walk when the wind will carry you".

**Zahiren Vahl · The Horizon-Eyed** (zahiren-vahl-the-horizon-eyed), Keeper of the Sand-Whisper Codex, is "Harmonic Historian. Wellspring Cartographer. Concord Mediator of the Innerworld". In the Arbitration Division he "functions as a cultural translator and ritual arbiter for disputes involving ancient Wellspring rights, especially among desert-bound or nomadic societies". He holds informal alliances with the Sylvaar nomad caravans and the deep-vault scholars. "Sigils embedded into his flesh from a rite of the Horizon Wellspring" pulse when he speaks truth and burn when he speaks falsehood. "He is a mediator who is physically incapable of lying without pain". Ground and objects hold their history for him to read, and "The dunes remember" is the mechanism on his sheet. The failure is written beside it: "a careless practitioner erases the record in the act of taking it". He senses shifts in terrain and Essence across barren country. He "Holds fragments of living memory from entities long dead" and can replay them, or "anchor them to a location for others to witness". His limit: "He preserves. He does not intervene". His counter: "Ask him a direct question". He "Has provided classified cartographic data for sealed Wellsprings in exchange for protection" to Research and Archives. With Florwyn's Nomads he trades Wellspring maps and preservation rites. His line: "The dunes remember, even when the people forget".

**Cael Veridion · Bearer of the Second Sun** (cael-veridion-bearer-of-the-second-sun), the Dawn-Brand and Gilded Vein, is "Solar Ascendant. Divine Arbitrator. Fleshbound Vessel of Radiance". He was "Formerly Arbitration Division", a "mediator in divine treaty disputes until his transformation during the Heliarch Concord Collapse", and he is "Now a free agent with no official ties, feared and sought after by both Concord and Outer World cults". The Dawn Vein Covenant is "a god-binding rite linking his soul directly to a Celestial Wellspring". It grants divine acts without full ascension at the cost of flesh degradation, and "the glowing cracks along his arms are the instalment plan". His blood "burns through skin like gold poured into flesh". It heals him and sears what he strikes. "When he speaks, Domains within earshot align their harmonic balance toward his words", "forcing momentary peace or amplifying discord". He marks foes or allies with a sun sigil, and "The marked burn brighter in battle until they collapse". His cost: "Flesh degradation, continuous and irreversible". What nobody knows is "What the Celestial Wellspring is owed when the flesh finishes". Lyrielle Vaerenth, a High Elf, is a "Political ally turned reluctant adversary after the Concord schism". Malrik Dreadveil is a "Former diplomatic opponent; now an unsteady truce". The Black Concord has a "Rumoured interest in recruiting him as a living divine weapon". His line opens: "You think the sun is distant".

## 3 · The shared history

Three events. The Heliarch Concord Collapse is on two cards and is given a place and a manner here. The other two are new. Each stands on lines both cards give: the escort and the courier, the mediator and the Division's duelist, the vagabond who shows up when it matters. No era is given. The events are unordered against one another.

### The reading of the Heliarch hall

- **When.** Undated. The Collapse is on both cards, and it is Cael's Catalyst. He was a mediator "until his transformation during the Heliarch Concord Collapse", "and the Collapse is the Catalyst". Zahiren's reading follows the Collapse and is new. It was the last matter Cael attended for the Division.

- **Where.** The Outer World (WORLD §3 and §5). Neither card names the hall or its ground, and this record names none past the quarter. After the Collapse, Cael is sought by "Outer World cults". Every other member of this cluster works in that quarter.

- **Who.** Cael Veridion, Zahiren Vahl.

- **What happened.** The Heliarch Concord was a treaty with divine parties to it, and the Arbitration Division had Cael at its table as mediator. It broke. In the course of its breaking Cael took the Dawn Vein Covenant, and his soul was bound to a Celestial Wellspring. The Division wanted the Collapse on its record. It sent Zahiren to take it from the floor of the hall. He read it once, slowly. The floor held the treaty breaking and the parties turning on one another. Then it held Cael's voice, and every Domain within earshot turning toward it, and the hall going still long enough for the parties to go out of it. Zahiren spoke the reading into the Division's record with Cael standing in the hall. His veins pulsed through all of it and never burned. The cracks along Cael's arms had opened by then, and when the record was entered he left the Division's service.

- **What each took.**

  - Cael: the Covenant and the cracks that pay for it. His part in the Collapse went onto the Division's record in the words of a witness whose veins burn at a falsehood. His service ended, and after it his card makes him "a free agent with no official ties".

  - Zahiren: a reading he did not take twice. The floor gave him one voice turning every Domain in a hall, which his card says he distrusts. It also gave him the parties going out of that hall, and his card says he respects Cael's role.

### The storm on the Drylands road

- **When.** Undated, and new. Rikkan was running for the Logistics and Supply Division and carrying Zahiren's cartography to Research and Archives. Kaelen was the Arbitration Division's Bound Duelist.

- **Where.** The Varakh Drylands in the Outer World (WORLD §5). The Southern Pan page gives them "Thorn scrub, dry woodland, red earth, salt pan, seasonal river". The road ran from the ground where Zahiren handed the package over to the Concord-controlled Wellspring where Research and Archives took it. No page names either place, and this record names neither.

- **Who.** Rikkan Sahr, Kaelen Raive. Zahiren Vahl, whose package it was, is absent.

- **What happened.** Zahiren gave Rikkan a sealed package for Research and Archives. The road crossed a frontier of the kind Rikkan's card names, where Domain stability is low. Research and Archives asked Arbitration, the Division it votes with at the long table, for an escort, and Arbitration sent Kaelen. Neither man asked what was in the package. Out on the red earth an Essence storm came across the road. Rikkan read the run of it and took a line through its edge at a pace no escort could keep. Ground the storm had loosened held under his feet for as long as he kept moving on it. What the wind threw at him went wide. Kaelen could not follow at that pace. He set himself on the road under his own weight and stood while the storm went over him. When it had passed he walked the rest of the road alone. At the Wellspring the package had been handed over and signed for. Rikkan was sitting on the sanctum step.

- **What each took.**

  - Rikkan: one more delivery time of the kind the Quartermasters reward with priority work. He also left his escort standing on the road.

  - Kaelen: the speed his card says he values. He also saw a line hold on luck, the gambling with fate his card says he distrusts.

  - Zahiren: a package delivered unopened, which neither carrier asked about.

### The trial at the sanctum on the Dust Coast

- **When.** Undated, and new. Zahiren and Kaelen both served the Arbitration Division.

- **Where.** The Aurelian Dust Coast in the Outer World, the mineral desert between the Drakhal Spine and the sea (WORLD §5). The Southern Pan page says Aether there is too weak for large dragons to nest, and "smaller draconic houses, mining enclaves and mortal city-states" hold it. The trial was held at the sanctum over a small Wellspring. No page names the Wellspring or the two peoples who claimed it, and this record names none of them.

- **Who.** Zahiren Vahl, Kaelen Raive, Saruin Kye.

- **What happened.** Two desert-bound peoples claimed the same small Wellspring, each by ancient right, and brought the dispute to the Accord. Arbitration sent Zahiren. He read the sanctum's ground. It kept both peoples at the Wellspring, each in its turn, further back than either people's own account of it. He said so. His veins pulsed and did not burn. Neither people would take a reading that gave the Wellspring to both, and one of them refused it outright and asked for trial. The Division sent Kaelen. The trial was set as a sanctum duel against that people's champion. The champion gave ground. Then he opened a Domain over the sanctum. Kaelen began the charge that binds a Domain shut. Before it was full, the champion's people broke the ring and came at the Warden from the sides. Saruin came down into the sanctum on his cloud. For as long as the charge took, the blows that should have landed on Kaelen missed him, and a wall of the sanctum that had started to fall held until the charge was full. The Seal bound the champion's Domain shut. His law stood suspended while the trial concluded, and the verdict was entered with the Division. Saruin was gone before it was read. Zahiren had not moved through any of it. When the sanctum was quiet he anchored what the ground held of both peoples' dead at the Wellspring to the sanctum floor, for whichever of them came to witness it.

- **What each took.**

  - Kaelen: the verdict. He also came through the charge alive, kept so by a man who cannot end a fight.

  - Saruin: the Warden alive at the end of the charge, and no part in the verdict, which he left before it was read.

  - Zahiren: a mediation that ended where the Warden's work began, and a memory anchored at the sanctum for both peoples. The ground there now keeps the trial as well. The cloud coming down is in it with the rest, for any reader after him.

## 7 · Where the cards disagree

Quoted and left open.

1. **Five Judicium practitioners or six.** Kaelen's card calls him "the fifth Judicium practitioner in Volume IV" (III). It adds "Raive is the fifth Judicium practitioner in Volume IV, and the first who is primarily a fighter", and "he is the only one of the five with no diplomatic faculty at all" (IV). The other four it names are Vaelen, Leontes, Riven and Maerion. Cael's card harmonises "Judicium · Fulguria. Order, measure, consequence" (III). It calls his sheet the widest gap "on any sheet in Volume IV" (IV). Either Volume IV holds six Judicium practitioners or Kaelen's count leaves Cael out. c08 already carries Maerion's "the fourth Judicium magistrate in Volume IV, and the four form a clean set".

2. **Which Division Rikkan's runs for Zahiren serve.** Rikkan's affiliation: "freelance smuggling for Zahiren Vahl when the Arbitration Division needs "off-the-record" transfers" (I). His relationship note: "Zahiren gives sealed-Wellspring cartography to R.A.D. Rikkan is the man who carries it" (VIII). Zahiren's card gives Research and Archives "classified cartographic data for sealed Wellsprings in exchange for protection" and names no courier (VIII). The direction differs too. "Delivers sensitive maps and artifacts to him" runs toward Zahiren; the note carries Zahiren's cartography away from him. This record's road event carries a package for Research and Archives and settles nothing about the Arbitration runs.

3. **One collapse, or a collapse and a schism.** Zahiren's card: "his role in the Concord Collapse". Cael's card: "the Heliarch Concord Collapse", "the Collapse", and "Political ally turned reluctant adversary after the Concord schism". No card says whether the schism is the Collapse.

4. **Zahiren's quarter.** His title is "Concord Mediator of the Innerworld". His work is "especially among desert-bound or nomadic societies". His card ranks the cartographers with "Zahiren the deserts", and his Signature grants survival "in harsh Essence wastelands". The Inner World page names no desert, and the deserts on the wiki are the Outer World's. This is a card set against the wiki.

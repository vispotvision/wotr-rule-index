# c40 · The Age of Calamity

Four lives of one age. Zarron Mahuo, the Arbiter of Unity, is a hybrid of Yukari and high elven blood whom neither house claimed as a full son, and his cut opens the ground under a ward or a Domain. Iskaron Thalnaris, Bearer of the Fractured Crown, came out of the Crevice margin. Sinclair Mercer, the Soul Devourer, is a half-mortal of Abythron whom the Titan Zar'thuun declined; he eats souls and mends his own coat. Gorrath Bloodspine, the Magmaborn Juggernaut, is the last chieftain of an orc clan that died where he had decided to stand.

**Era.** All four cards give one era. Zarron's, Sinclair's and Gorrath's each read "Era · Age of Calamity." in their first section, and Iskaron's carries the words in the line under his title. WORLD.md files the Age of Calamity under "Fit none". This record puts it on no count and gives no year. Sinclair's card sets the Guild Accord's institutions inside his life: the Sancta Lux censor rolls, the Research and Archives Division, the Bureau of Strategic Assessment, and Enforcement teams that bring a Sealwright. The discipline pages order the age before the Voyager Era, when the Accord was founded, and c05 reads the Wandering Fang's Age of Calamity as later than the Academy years. Section 7 keeps that open. None of the three events below needs the Accord, and none of them settles where the age falls. They run in order: the Bloodspine ground, the days under the Fractured Crown on the same ground, and the second Crown on another stretch of it.

**Sources read.** The four cards, in full. They sit at the roster's `file` paths in the Volume I character-card folder of `wiki/`, and Iskaron's file name, which carries a dash on disk, is written here with a middle dot. Then the cards the roster lists beside the members: Kael Serradyn, the Fractured Dawn, Karo Venrik and the five Mahuo. Then every card that names a member, which gives Cernan Eryndor, Karasaki Hozume, Velcrissa, Emilia Veirynthiel and Sandalphon Aestraen, with the Wandering Fang for the era. In the wiki, the Outer World page. It gave the Crevice, the flood basalt, Trapstone and Dorrosfenn. From The Fourteen Titans came the Abyssal Depths page for Zar'thuun and Abythron, the Strata page for Valmir, and the Spiritual Seat page for Nysera and Virelion. Also read: `Cosmology & Metaphysics/The Veil.md`, the Mahuo Family page (a stub), the Goblinoid Peoples page, the Derivation of Practice page for the Bureau, and the Chainsalt entries in the Material Index and on the Nalūn page. For the age, the Harmonic Arts, Sage Arts and Spirit Rune Arts pages. `RULINGS.md`, 2026-09-12, for Vellsorea and the Obsession ruling, and the Docket page. The cluster records c05, c26, c30 and c33 where they touch a member.

## 1 · Who is here

**Zarron Mahuo · The Arbiter of Unity** (zarron-mahuo-the-arbiter-of-unity). He is also called "The Veilborn Ascendant. Wielder of Hikariken". His lineage reads "Hybrid. Yukari and high elven". He "Trained under a Yukari mentor-sect, then among the Veilwalkers of Vellsorea, and claimed as a full son by neither". Isaac placed Vellsorea in the New World, on the Eastern Rise. He is "Unaffiliated by choice and by consequence". His Catalyst: "He severed the Astral Gate at the Shattering of Virelion". The card carries that event "as references only". What he refuses is "To be claimed". His governing current passes Essence to an ally whose Crystal is tuned to his, and "A Transference practitioner beside a depleted ally will empty into them without deciding to". His card calls that "the only way he has ever found to give anybody anything". He "reads reality by what is missing from it", and it is "the reason he sees planar seams other practitioners walk through". His signature cut arrives before the blade. It opens a seam in the ground the target stands on, and wards, Domains and running techniques lose what they were joined to. "Light comes off the seam for a long moment after and does not illuminate". During the cut "He empties toward whatever is nearest", and "He has hospitalised two people this way and describes both as accidents". A man with no ward, no Domain and no working running is barely troubled by it. Hikariken is formed at need and never carried: "There is no scabbard". He has been searched for a weapon "on four occasions by people who then let him through". He speaks the Eressean register, learned, "slightly too correct in the way of a man who was taught the language by people watching for mistakes". Of his seams he does not know "Whether the seams close", and he assumes they heal.

**Iskaron Thalnaris · Bearer of the Fractured Crown** (iskaron-thalnaris-bearer-of-the-fractured-crown). "Human". His card files Creviceborne as an exposure lineage, and his standing reads "Unaligned. Former Scion of the Outer Crevice". His Catalyst: "He walked into a Crevice-margin concentration and remained coherent long enough to choose how he met it. He came out. Something did not finish leaving with him". He is trapped at Dissonance. Near him people "find their own certainty thinning and reach for anger to replace it, and the anger is available". He cannot switch it off. Wounds from his hands "close slower than wounds taken from anything else", and plate turns that touch entirely. "He is more dangerous in the fortieth minute than the fourth". At sustained peak a lattice forms above his head, "visible, unasked for", and "Witnesses who have seen it twice report it is larger the second time". Inside his Domain a man reaches for an escape technique and finds it "intact, correctly formed, and simply not answered". The Domain "does not close when he leaves. It decays over days, and the ground it stood on carries the set afterward". His signature needs him still through the build: "Anyone who has seen it once knows the stillness means it is coming, and the stillness lasts long enough to leave". It cannot be aimed. Afterward "He is slower for a week and does not sleep for three nights". The skin over his knuckles "has not fully closed in years". His wound: "He went in to prove he could come back, and he was right, and being right cost more than being wrong would have". His desire is "To finish". The card does not say what. He never says perhaps. Nobody knows "Why the Crevice let him leave".

**Sinclair Mercer · The Soul Devourer** (sinclair-mercer-the-soul-devourer). He is also "The Sculptor of the Echoed Dead", and in the Sancta Lux censor rolls "simply the Cannibal, which is the only name he has ever objected to". He is "Formerly of the Spiral Covenant, which no longer acknowledges him and which he has never acknowledged leaving". His origin is Abythron, Zar'thuun's domain of unmaking: "Half-mortal, given to the Titan as a sacrifice that did not take". His Catalyst: "Sinclair was offered to Zar'thuun and the Titan declined him". He took out of Abythron the shape of a hunger aimed at him that missed, and he has fed it since "on the theory that it will eventually be full". Essence bleeds toward him without his acting. "He has been asked to and cannot, and the request is one of the few things that reliably angers him". What he takes he shapes into constructs that "hold the temperament of whoever they came from". His shadow "has not obeyed him in some years". In his Domain, the Unwritten Grave, the dead he carries are "no longer subject to the ordinary courtesy of being gone". He is weak to "Radiant current" and to sustained physical trauma. Twice a Sealwright team took him from behind Chainsalt plate. Both times he left. He is "courteous, and the courtesy is real", and "he does not enjoy killing". His governing belief "is that the hunger has a bottom". He counts them. The number "has not gone down". He dresses "in dark wool cut in a fashion that was current in Abythron a generation ago", mended with care, and carries "A physician's roll of fine needles and dark thread. For the coat". He had a Spirit Axis once, and his card names nobody and keeps that gap on purpose. Tilda is "Hunted, not taken", the one recorded failure of his career, and he does not discuss her.

**Gorrath Bloodspine · The Magmaborn Juggernaut** (gorrath-bloodspine-the-magmaborn-juggernaut). He is also "Warden of the Broken Spires. Herald of Bloodfire". His race: "Orc, Bloodspine clan, deep-earth volcanic strain". His standing: "Last surviving chieftain of the Bloodspine line. Vanguard for the deep resistance fronts". He survived a soul furnace trial in his youth, and "The furnace trial took eleven of them". What he did afterward is his Catalyst. Near death his blood "calcifies into obsidian around the vital organs", a wound "made into architecture on purpose", and he did that work young. Nobody knows "Whether the obsidian is his". His Axes are "clan-anchored", and "they are all in one place". His signature drives both fists into the ground. The rupture runs along the seams the stone already had, "he does not break rock, he finds where rock was already going to break", and molten chain-spikes come up at the fracture lines. On sand, bog or a ship's deck it does nothing. After it "He runs cold for a day and a half". He "cannot chase, cannot read a feint, and cannot be made to stop", and "Every commander who has beaten him has beaten him by choosing where". He refuses "To be moved off ground he has committed to". It costs him "Everything mobile. He is the last Bloodspine chieftain because the rest of the clan died somewhere he had already decided to stand". He speaks in "Stressed monosyllables, short clauses, concrete nouns". He does not trust people who use abstractions.

## 3 · The shared history

Three events. None is in the archive. Each is new and fitted to every card it touches.

One ground carries all three: the flood-basalt steps at the Crevice margin, in the Outer World (WORLD §5, South). The Outer World page gives the ground. "Across the western third lies a flood-basalt province", weathered "into stepped terraces league upon league", and "through all of it runs the Crevice". The basalt is Trapstone, "Columnar basalt, split naturally into six-sided posts". The ground fits every card here. Iskaron came out of a concentration at the Crevice margin. Gorrath's signature needs ground with structure already in it, and columnar basalt comes jointed; his strain is volcanic. The Crevice is "a permanent scar joining the spiritual and material layers" (the Strata page), and Zarron sees planar seams. This record names no stretch of the steps. The Broken Spires stay unplaced. No person is added: the Bloodspine are named on Gorrath's card, and the front of the third event has no name.

### The Bloodspine ground

- **When.** The Age of Calamity. After Iskaron came out of the Crevice-margin concentration (his Catalyst Event), and after Gorrath made his blood into structure (his). Gorrath was chieftain of the Bloodspine.

- **Where.** The flood-basalt steps at the Crevice margin, in the Outer World.

- **Who.** Gorrath Bloodspine, Iskaron Thalnaris.

- **What happened.** The Bloodspine held the basalt steps above the margin. Iskaron came up out of the margin onto them, and they stood across his way.

  Near him their certainty thinned. They reached for anger, and it was there. They closed with him. His bare hands opened them, and the wounds he left would not knit. On Gorrath they found obsidian plate.

  Gorrath drove both fists into the basalt and set his heels. The rupture ran down the joints between the columns, and molten chain-spikes came up through the cracks. Iskaron took it and kept coming. The fight went on long past the point where men tire, and he was worse at the end of it than at the start.

  Then he stopped moving. A lattice formed above his head. Gorrath had not seen the stillness before, and he did not step back. The Bloodspine stayed where their chieftain stood.

  The air over the steps went wrong all at once. Sound came late. The basalt columns came apart at their joints and fell. Gorrath's blood went to obsidian around his vital organs, and he lived.

  Iskaron walked off the steps. His Domain stayed behind him on the basalt and began its days of decay. The Bloodspine who had lived through the signature lay inside it with wounds from his hands.

- **What each took.**

  - Gorrath: most of his clan dead on the ground he had chosen, and the rest dying on it. His blood made into structure again, as it is near death. A day and a half of cold after the ground-strike, which his card gives. The stillness, seen once.

  - Iskaron: a man his hands could not open and his signature did not finish. A week slower and three nights without sleep, as his card gives. A witness who had seen the lattice.

### The days under the Fractured Crown

- **When.** The days after the Bloodspine ground, while Iskaron's Domain decayed on the basalt.

- **Where.** The same steps.

- **Who.** Sinclair Mercer, Zarron Mahuo, Gorrath Bloodspine. Iskaron was gone, and his Domain was there.

- **What happened.** Gorrath stayed on the steps with the dying. While the Fractured Crown lay over the ground a withdrawal was hard to work and an escape went unanswered, so the dying could only be carried. The one man left to carry them would not leave the ground.

  Sinclair came onto the steps on foot, in his dark wool coat. Gorrath did not see him come. He saw a shadow on the basalt that did not match the man above it.

  Sinclair went among the dying Bloodspine and knelt beside each in turn. He was courteous with them. One by one they stopped. He took them, and he did not hurry.

  Gorrath went at him over the fallen columns. Each time he arrived, Sinclair was somewhere other than in front of him. Gorrath cannot chase, and he did not leave the ground to try.

  Where the Crevice runs, the Fractured Crown had shut the bleed between the planes over a stretch of basalt. Zarron reads by what is missing, and he came to it. He did not walk in. He stood alone at the Crown's edge and cut from there. The cut arrived before the blade did, and a seam opened in the basalt. The Crown's boundary took another shape. Light came off the seam and lit nothing.

  He came onto the steps with Hikariken in his hand. Sinclair stood up from the man he was kneeling beside and left before the light was off the seam.

  None of the Bloodspine lived to the next morning. Gorrath pried basalt posts loose by hand and stood them over his dead. Zarron carried posts with him until the last cairn was up. Then Zarron went.

- **What each took.**

  - Sinclair: the dying of the Bloodspine, taken, and fragments that hold the temper of the people they came from. He carries the fragments he is not using in his Chainsalt coffer (his card, XV). The count rose. A radiant blade, which he left before.

  - Zarron: a Domain he cut without having seen its maker, and the maker's shape, read from what the Domain left out. His card says he derives "from the shape of an absence the shape of the thing casting it" (III). A seam in the basalt, which he cannot read. Cairns he helped raise for a clan he never knew, beside a man who does not trust abstractions.

  - Gorrath: the rest of his clan gone from the ground he held, taken by a man he could not catch. Cairns of basalt over them. A seam in that ground whose light lights nothing.

  - Iskaron, absent: his Domain cut by a man he has never met, and the dying he left taken by another.

### The second Crown

- **When.** Later in the Age of Calamity, after the Bloodspine ground, when Gorrath stood as vanguard for one of the deep resistance fronts (his card's standing).

- **Where.** Another stretch of the flood-basalt steps at the Crevice margin.

- **Who.** Gorrath Bloodspine, Iskaron Thalnaris.

- **What happened.** The front held the steps, and Gorrath stood at its head. Iskaron came up out of the margin again.

  The fight went long. When Iskaron stopped moving, Gorrath had seen that stillness once. He sent the front back off the steps, and they went. He stayed on the ground alone.

  The lattice formed above Iskaron's head, larger than it had been on the Bloodspine ground. The air went wrong. The columns under Gorrath's feet came apart at their joints. His blood went to obsidian again, and he lived. The front lived, off the steps.

  Iskaron walked away down the margin.

- **What each took.**

  - Gorrath: a front alive behind him. The lattice seen twice, so that he is one of the witnesses who report it larger the second time. His ground broken under him again.

  - Iskaron: the same man, twice unfinished, and a witness who has now seen the lattice twice. A week slower and three nights without sleep.

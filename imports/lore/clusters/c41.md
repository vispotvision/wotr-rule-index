# c41 · The Triune of the Three-Headed Serpent

Selantra, the Velvet Storm, an Aetheric alchemist; Velcrissa, the Dusken Bloom, a Temperance guide who once held chairs of Temperance Studies; Virelsa of the Veilborne Skies, the Triune's exiled Matron and Spirit-Weaver of the Fallen Fae; and Estrella of the Lattice Veil, an occult medium and soul-binder on the edge of the Creviceward Circle.

**Era.** No card here states an era, and no scene holds any of the four. Velcrissa was "Formerly Chair of Temperance Studies at multiple Facultas", and chairs are the academy posts The Guild Accord in the Modern Era describes: "An endowed and appointed post, named for whoever endowed it. Held for life or until a better one is offered". This record sets the Triune's three events in that present, the Withering Era, with no year number. The order is the hall at the Facultas, then the naming at Altherion, then the seam at the exclusion line. Each of the four lives one life in one era. Virelsa's card, a fae's, gives no lifespan, and none is lengthened.

## 1 · Who is here

**Selantra · The Velvet Storm** (selantra-the-velvet-storm). "Aetheric alchemist. Co-founder of the Triune." Her card sets her in "the Triune of the Three-Headed Serpent", "built as a matched set with Velcrissa and Virelsa, and the three should be read together." Her Catalyst: "She took the load-bearing wound and made it hold weight on purpose." "Her pride is the wound." A failed working, to her, is "Essence in an inconvenient basis", and the sheet reads her as a woman who "can turn any failure into fuel and cannot take a hit." The card holds her to Alchemetrica's warning that "a pure alchemist who is sloppy dies." Her signature is Catalytic Ribbons: "Skin-bound Parun conduits let her weaponise mood as feedback", with "reactions resolving faster than most spellcraft can be prepared." Its cost is "Backlash, absorbed by pride and paid later." Its limit is "She must already know the reaction." Its counter is "Something she has never seen." The card adds: "She will attempt it anyway." Among her currents, Luminalis is glossed "The spectacle."

**Velcrissa · The Dusken Bloom** (velcrissa-the-dusken-bloom). "Shadow binder, Temperance guide, spiritual mediator. Formerly Chair of Temperance Studies at multiple Facultas." "Second of the Triune; read with Selantra and Virelsa." Her Catalyst: "She stopped working alone." The card gives the recorded pattern, "relief, then something worse once the practitioner works out the relief was no longer being solely responsible", and adds that "what they handed responsibility to did not sign anything." "A woman who guides others through Wellspring transitions for a living is uniquely placed to recognise this, and did." Her aura: "She is quieter than the background, which is better." Of a failing Crystal: "She speeds up its settling", and "this is how she averts collapse." Her signature is The Flickering Voice: "The working arrives before the speech does", and it is "routinely mistaken for weather." Its cost is "Nothing anyone sees, which is the point and the price." Its limit is "She cannot do it loudly." Its counter is "Make her raise her voice."

**Virelsa of the Veilborne Skies** (virelsa-of-the-veilborne-skies). "Exiled Matron of the Triune. Spirit-Weaver of the Fallen Fae. Formerly of the Fae Courts of the Higher Realms." "Third of the Triune." Her Catalyst: "The practitioner stops being the party that has to be accommodated." "Divine relics still respond to her despite banishment". She attunes "to outcast and condemned souls without corruption", and "She reads the absence". Her light is "dormant. Flickers only during acts of sacrifice or truth." The sheet: "She can bind to anyone and anchor nowhere", "a woman whose entire strength is other people and who has no ground of her own." Her signature is The Twilight Bridge: "She joins a higher stratum to a lower one and holds it open long enough for something to cross." Its cost is that "every crossing binds her further." Its limit is "She cannot cross it herself." Its counter is "Wait." The card warns that "she is close to the number of bonds she can hold."

**Estrella of the Lattice Veil** (estrella-of-the-lattice-veil). "Occult medium, soul-binder. Peripheral agent of the Creviceward Circle." Her Catalyst: "The Domain seed, and with it the ability to examine her own architecture." Of her Half-Seer's Eye: "one eye sees in metaphor and the other sees what once was true". Her currents come next. "Objects store their history readably." One is "the suturing of spirit fragments, with intent as the template". The soul transfer "runs both directions." The sheet: "she can thread anybody to anybody and cannot thread much to herself. Which is exactly how a medium survives the work." Her signature is The Conjunction Rite: "She sutures a spirit across a threshold it should not survive, and the seam holds because the template was correct." Its cost: "she empties toward the depleted without deciding to." Its limit: "She must have known the shape before." Its counter: "A spirit she has never met."

## 3 · The shared history

Three events, all new. The first binds Selantra to Velcrissa. The second names the Triune. The third brings Estrella to two of its women. Velcrissa's, Virelsa's and Estrella's Catalysts fall in none of them, and the naming at Altherion is not Velcrissa's Catalyst. No event names a founder's order, gives the Triune a seat, says what Virelsa was exiled from, or says what the Creviceward Circle does.

### The hall at the Facultas

- **When.** Before the Triune was named, while Velcrissa held a chair of Temperance Studies. It comes before Selantra's Catalyst and is its occasion.
- **Where.** A Facultas in the Concord, the heartland's composite monarchy (WORLD §3). No page names a Facultas or places an academy, and this record names neither the house nor the town.
- **Who.** Selantra, Velcrissa.
- **What happened.** Selantra worked before the Facultas in its hall. She ran her reactions along the conduits under her skin, faster than any Scholar on the benches could have prepared a spell, and she made a spectacle of them for the room. One reaction came back in a form she had never seen. She tried it anyway. The backlash ran into her through the conduits, and her Crystal began to fail in front of the hall. Velcrissa held the chair of Temperance Studies there. She stayed in her seat. Keeping her voice low, she hurried the failing Crystal on to its settling. The working reached Selantra before anyone heard Velcrissa speak, and the hall took it for a change in the weather. Selantra lived. The wound was to her pride. In the time after the hall she left it open and made it bear weight on purpose.
- **What each took.**
  - Selantra: the wound to her pride, which she made load-bearing, the Catalyst her card records; since then her backlash is "absorbed by pride and paid later". A debt, too, to the one woman in the hall who saw how near the failing came and hurried it to its settling.
  - Velcrissa: the measure of Selantra, an alchemist who turns any failure into fuel and cannot take a hit. The hall never learned whose working it was, and her card gives that as the price: "Nothing anyone sees".

### The naming at Altherion

- **When.** After the hall at the Facultas. By then Velcrissa had given up the last of her chairs.
- **Where.** Altherion, the republic on the rim of the Ring, whose Republican Law "Applies to every person within Altherion's territory regardless of guild affiliation, racial origin or institutional membership." (The Guild Seat of Stannvaard). A hired room in the city. No page gives the Triune a seat, and this record gives it none.
- **Who.** Selantra, Velcrissa, Virelsa.
- **What happened.** Velcrissa came to Altherion without a chair. She had given up the last of them, and the fund that paid it, before she came. Selantra came with the backlash of the hall still carried in her pride. Virelsa met them in the city. In a hired room she spoke to the other two, and while she spoke the light that lies dormant in her flickered. Selantra had never seen that reaction. She tried it that night, alone, and it would not come, and the backlash went to her pride, as hers does. The three agreed there to work as one order. They named it the Triune of the Three-Headed Serpent. Its Matron was Virelsa.
- **What each took.**
  - Selantra: the order her card says she co-founded, and a reaction she had seen once and could not run.
  - Velcrissa: an order to answer to in place of a chair, and the two women her card says to read her with.
  - Virelsa: the Matron's place in the Triune and two bonds she chose. The card still gives her no ground of her own.

### The seam at the exclusion line

- **When.** After the naming at Altherion, once Estrella held the rite her card names.
- **Where.** The southern Crevice margin in the Outer World, at the line the Accord keeps around the Crevice, "along which the rock stops obeying the ordinary rules at a distance the Accord has fixed at four leagues and revises upward roughly once a decade." (The Outer World, The Southern Pan).
- **Who.** Estrella, Virelsa, Velcrissa.
- **What happened.** A man Estrella had known was carried out from inside the line. He was breathing. What he was had come away from him in pieces. Estrella could mend him, since she had known his shape. She could not reach all of the pieces. The Creviceward Circle, whose agent she was on its edge, sent for the Matron of the Triune, and Virelsa came to the line with Velcrissa. Virelsa read the man by what was missing from him. She joined the higher stratum, where the rest of him had gone, to the lower one where he lay, and she held it open. She stayed on her own side of it. Estrella sutured him across it with the shape she had known as the template, and the seam held. She emptied toward him while she worked, without deciding to. When it was done she could not stand. Velcrissa hurried her settling and sat the night with her.
- **What each took.**
  - Estrella: the seam that held; the emptying her card names as the rite's cost; a debt to Velcrissa; and the use of a bridge she could never hold herself.
  - Virelsa: the crossing, which bound her further, nearer the number of bonds her card says she can hold, and a bond to the Circle's medium.
  - Velcrissa: a debtor on the edge of the Creviceward Circle.

## 6 · Same person?

No. Four cards, four people, with four trades, four signatures and four Catalysts.

Two names on wiki pages touch two members, and this record joins neither.
- Transposition (Spellcraft) names "Velcrissa of Selantra", one practitioner by the page's reading, whom it says has no card. There are now two cards, Velcrissa and Selantra, two women with different trades. This record reads them as two and gives neither of them the page's use of Transposition.
- The Sage Arts names "Virelsa the Breathweaver · A Cymorath Sage whose wind attunement allowed her to walk air." Virelsa of the Veilborne Skies harmonises Oneirion, Tenebra, Eclipseron and Luminalis, with no Cymorath. No page says the two are one woman, and this record keeps them two.

## 7 · Where the cards disagree

Quoted and left open.

1. **Who founded the Triune, and in what order.** Selantra: "Co-founder of the Triune." Velcrissa: "Second of the Triune; read with Selantra and Virelsa." Virelsa: "Third of the Triune." Only Selantra's card says co-founder, and no card says whether second and third count founding, rank, or the order of the set. The roster's hinge calls all three co-founders. Section 3 names no founder's order.
2. **What Virelsa was exiled from.** "Exiled Matron of the Triune." sits beside "Formerly of the Fae Courts of the Higher Realms." and "Divine relics still respond to her despite banishment". The card does not say whether the exile is from the Courts or from the Triune. Section 3 places no exile and sets her with the Triune in both of its events.
3. **"Velcrissa of Selantra" (card against page).** Transposition: "None of the four practitioners the card names, Velcrissa of Selantra, Zeven Halek and his combat partner Cylus, Thalorath the Gatewrought, or the Red Twins of Dorrosfenn, have a card or page anywhere in current canon". The page reads one practitioner. The cards are two women. c17 records the same page's claim against Zeven Halek's card.
4. **The name Virelsa (card against page).** "Virelsa the Breathweaver · A Cymorath Sage" (The Sage Arts) against Virelsa of the Veilborne Skies, whose card carries no Cymorath. Whether they are one woman is not recorded.
5. **Estrella's lunar harmonics.** Her card carries "Old Lunar Harmonics (pre-Atlas Moons)" as flavour until a cosmology check is made. No event here uses it.
6. **The Facultas and the chair (card against page).** Velcrissa's card: "Formerly Chair of Temperance Studies at multiple Facultas." The Guild Accord in the Modern Era: a chair is "An endowed and appointed post, named for whoever endowed it." The card names the chair for its subject. The word Facultas appears on no wiki page.

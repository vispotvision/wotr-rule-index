## Summary card
**Effect** · The God Fist: one Anchor across seven Labor-Forms, one for each Work of Hataraki. The bearer pins their position to law rather than ground, so a push comes back entire and a blow carries the mass of whatever the stance is coupled to.
**Cost** · Setting the anchor costs 2% of full reserve and holding it 0.5% a turn; a Labor-Form strike costs 5%, or 8% committed, and the Totality expression 30%.
**Limit** · The coupling must be set before contact and holds against displacement only up to its Continuity Grade; nothing is granted from outside, only what the bearer has become able to carry.
**Counter** · Pull, lift, hook or drag the Anchor in tension, fight it in a confined space so the blast comes back, sever the coupling with displacement, Silence or ground that will not hold, or wait for Obsession.
**What nobody knows** · Whether Gimbzo, held in the Plane of Fate as living infrastructure, feels it when one of his forms is used.

## Codex line
**Wellspring** · Each Labor-Form draws its own five or six; the Ruin Fist runs Fixatio, Coagulatio, Cataclysm, Fractura and Nihiloth, the Purity Fist Fixatio, Petralon, Basilithe, Benediction and Judicium.
**Family** · Several by design, with the two best-known forms anchored on Materia.
**Physics Domain** · Effective mass and impedance matching at contact, with fracture at a pre-existing flaw.
**Category** · Arts, a structured discipline of movement, form and Essence flow; nothing about it is involuntary.
**Craft** · A martial doctrine carried in the body, nearest to Magicraft.
**Stage floor** · None; a bloodline and personal art, and the lineage decides who can use it.
**Grade required** · Set by each bearer's own card; a Master bearer at Transcendence strikes at S-Grade.
**Path gate** · Body Path for Hardening and Fortification, Attraction Path for Gravity, Fate Path for Continuity.

## Design Chain
**Trigger** · The Anchor chooses ground and stops: a weapon butt driven in, palms flat to the earth, the ground taking a weight it did not have.
**Function** · Pins the bearer's coordinate to something other than the floor, so the coupling is a shared coordinate rather than a contact, and the verdict precedes the blow.
**Mechanism** · Aether falls rather than flares; Fixatio makes the unmoved configuration the only reachable one, and a body that does not accelerate returns the whole applied force.
**Numerical Effect** · A Labor-Form strike delivers 3.7 × 10¹⁰ to 6.9 × 10¹¹ J at a Master's reserve; a Plane-coupled bearer's Pillar Nail costs 0.67% of reserve, the Iron Verdict Drop 18.1% and the Floor of the Yet-To-Be 22.9%, and a Plane-held reserve shrinks every Work's cost and output alike.
**Target Response** · An attacker striking a perfectly anchored defender receives their own blow; a borrowed harmonisation on Basilithe ground slides off on contact.
**Consequence** · The Ruin Fist brings a structure's oldest failure early; the Purity Fist returns a body or ward to its native shape; the Spirit Fist strikes between life and death; the Sequence Fist is correct before contact; the Revelation Fist hits what an opponent is; the Balance Fist seats at the fulcrum; Totality anchors all six.
**Limitation** · It cannot be taught outside the lineage, it holds against displacement only up to its Continuity Grade, and the largest Works run fewer than four to a reserve.
**Weakness** · Each form has its Obsession: Ruin that enjoys the waiting, Purity that sterilises what does not match. An Anchor who cannot un-anchor, and who refuses only in the heart rather than the feet, hits with an arm's weight.
**Cost** · Shares of full reserve: 2% to set, 0.5% a turn to hold, 5% or 8% a strike, 30% for Totality; an anchor held ten turns with six committed strikes costs 55%.
**Counterplay** · Load it in tension, confine the blast, displace it above its Continuity Grade, or make it spend; the Pillar Nail's 40 m denial of teleport and fate-skip lasts only about five turns.

## Origin
**Origin** · Gimbzo, the Anchor of Fate, First-Eon Moto and contemporary of Bara, held the eastern rim of the unfinished world for forty-one days without sleep; when Dougou, the Iron Bison Maximus, broke him in a three-day duel, the Plane of Fate took him whole as living infrastructure. The oral tradition says that Bara gave seven ways to work and Gimbzo found seven ways to refuse.
**Practitioners** · Gimbzo, its origin and only known full master; it descends through the Moto line, and no living master of a full form is named.

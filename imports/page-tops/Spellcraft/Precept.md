## Summary card
**Effect** · A rule filed with the world in the Continuum's own grammar: inside the sphere it names, one shape is simply no longer available, and whatever would take it completes in the nearest shape left open.
**Cost** · A share of full reserve once, at sealing, scaled by breadth: 5% for one named party, 10% for a room, 30% for a guild's code, 60% for a city's or a dynasty's law; no upkeep after.
**Limit** · It compels nothing outside its clause, cannot catch a working that reads its shape before sealing, and cannot be argued away by force.
**Counter** · A chiselled, scraped or toppled substrate; one precise exception written in the same register; Rescind, for a soul-bound Precept that was never lawfully consented to.
**What nobody knows** · Whether the Twelve Precepts of Fate were written at the first descent or were already standing when the first Archon arrived to find them.

## Origin
**Origin** · The older registers called the practice enshrining. Its first act is credited to Urion, Archon of Balance, a reference against which other things are measured, whom tradition names as author of the Twelve Precepts of Fate; guilds use it for their codes because it leaves no continuous deposit where people must live.
**Practitioners** · Urion, Archon of Balance; Lysandra Velkaris, said to have written the Precept of Hierarchic Ascendance for Highspire's chain of command.

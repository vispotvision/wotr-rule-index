## Summary card
**Effect** · A rule filed with the world in the Continuum's own grammar: inside the sphere it names, one shape is simply no longer available, and whatever would take it completes in the nearest shape left open.
**Cost** · A share of full reserve once, at sealing, scaled by breadth: 5% for one named party, 10% for a room, 30% for a guild's code, 60% for a city's or a dynasty's law; no upkeep after.
**Limit** · It compels nothing outside its clause, cannot catch a working that reads its shape before sealing, and cannot be argued away by force.
**Counter** · Chisel, scrape or topple the substrate it is cut into, write one precise exception in the same register, or Rescind a soul-bound Precept that was never lawfully consented to.
**What nobody knows** · Whether the Twelve Precepts of Fate were written at the first descent or were already standing when the first Archon arrived to find them.

## Design Chain
**Trigger** · The practitioner lays Boundary under Order and seals the clause into a substrate.
**Function** · Removes a set of configurations from what is reachable inside a named sphere; it touches neither strength nor willingness, only what is available.
**Mechanism** · Fixatio makes what it holds stay held because the alternative is no longer reachable; Judicium sets the edges of the clause, which is the whole craft of drafting.
**Numerical Effect** · Eighteen narrow clauses before Starvation, three guild codes, or one dynasty's law with nothing else sealed that season.
**Target Response** · Nobody feels stopped: an oath is sworn in the terms left open, a rank claimed in the permitted form.
**Consequence** · A standing law inside its sphere that outlives its author the way an inscription outlasts the mason, marking everything downstream for generations.
**Limitation** · A Precept is as sharp as its clause was laid, not as strong as its author; it cannot compel past its edges or grant rule, which is Coronation's.
**Weakness** · A clause edge misplaced yields a century of correct rulings nobody wanted, and a clause laid too wide stays wrong; a house can drift until it runs a stated law its lived behaviour left long ago.
**Cost** · 5%, 10%, 30% or 60% of full reserve at sealing, with zero standing upkeep; a compact binding parties cannot be taken back by its author.
**Counterplay** · Attack the substrate, not the law; answer with Lock, the same shape used as a reply; read the clause as drafted and work its edges; press the drift.

## Origin
**Origin** · The older registers called the practice enshrining. Its first act is credited to Urion, Archon of Balance, a reference against which other things are measured, whom tradition names as author of the Twelve Precepts of Fate; guilds use it for their codes because it leaves no continuous deposit where people must live.
**Practitioners** · Urion, Archon of Balance; Lysandra Velkaris, said to have written the Precept of Hierarchic Ascendance for Highspire's chain of command.

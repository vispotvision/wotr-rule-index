## Summary card
**Effect** · A field of roses and thorns raised around a target: each rose is a deposit from the target's own history, each thorn the unpaid debt on it, and the target's claim on those holdings is diluted until the present standing on them fails.
**Cost** · Half a percent of full reserve per rose, paid from the practitioner's own reserve; a small sea of a dozen roses costs 6%, a long ledger of a hundred or more 50% and upward.
**Limit** · It cannot erase a deposit, since the past stays true and only the present claim is diluted, and it loads only a debt that is genuinely unpaid.
**Counter** · Pay your debts, stand on broad and independent claims rather than a single chain, Silence the field, or kill or distract the gardener standing exposed in the open.
**What nobody knows** · Why Bara Moto made the lesson conditional on having learned to grieve correctly, and what correct grief was taken to mean.

## Design Chain
**Trigger** · The gardener lays Measure under Return and raises the sea small around the target, widening it only as reserve allows.
**Function** · Moves whether a past deposit must answer to its present holder, deposit by deposit; whether it happened, or was theirs, does not move.
**Mechanism** · Anamnesis reads the ledger as a physical record, Fractura finds its seams, Contrition surfaces only what was already unpaid, Mortalis withdraws the answering across its threshold, Monolithion ratifies the withdrawal, and Oblation pays for it from the practitioner.
**Numerical Effect** · 180 roses before Starvation; a Master at Realization raises about ten roses a turn, and the same reserve raises roughly twice the sea at Master efficiency as at the floor.
**Target Response** · Nothing touches them and they never see a strike land; claims simply stop being honoured, and from outside it reads as mercy.
**Consequence** · Once the load-bearing deposit goes, the target's present coordinate fails within seconds; kings, gods and old practitioners, with the richest ledgers, are the easiest targets.
**Limitation** · A withdrawal Monolithion has ratified cannot be reversed, even by its author; a coordinate with several independent claims survives all but the last withdrawal, and one standing on a single chain survives none.
**Weakness** · Against a deposit that is not unpaid the law does not run but the payment still leaves, and enough wrongful attempts dilute the gardener's own ledger; drawing the medium from the target or the field turns the sea brackish and the thorns inward.
**Cost** · 0.5% of full reserve per rose, a king's ledger enough to take the gardener to Starvation; a sea over a long ledger past the ground's saturation risks a Crystal Fracture Event for everyone present.
**Counterplay** · The roses are visible from far off and the gardener stands warm and lit in the open; bait wrongful roses with a target who seems to owe and does not; the Work passes only to someone who has grieved, which leaves every practitioner a known, unhealed shape to read.

## Origin
**Origin** · Bara Moto passed the Work to Muken once, on the seventy-third day of instruction beneath the lower lattice of the Plane of Fate, as a private lesson rather than a house curriculum, and warned him not to teach it to anyone who had not first learned to grieve correctly. Paired with the Shingan's Tengan or Kamigan branch, which can name the single correct rose, it turns from an apocalyptic working into a surgical one.
**Practitioners** · Bara Moto; Muken.

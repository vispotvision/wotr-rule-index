## Summary card
**Effect** · A practitioner perceives the Physical and Soul Planes at once, crosses briefly into the threshold between planes, works there at length, or at the top carries others across.
**Cost** · Essence priced by the ground: a Baseline crossing is 2 percent of full reserve, a Thin zone half to 1 percent, a Thick zone 5 to 20 percent. Every crossing also adds Soul Drift, which never comes back.
**Limit** · No controlled operation below Refraction, no entry into the Soul Plane or Plane of Fate, only the threshold, and the body is left behind every time. Nobody seals a significant rupture alone.
**Counter** · Choose Thick ground or a city, use Silence, wards and anti-Harmonia fields, and strike the unattended body, since a crossing cannot be aborted fast.
**What nobody knows** · Whether Rikudoku Moto's soul still drifts between the Veil's folds after his death, as the tradition about him claims.

## Design Chain
**Trigger** · The practitioner tunes to the local boundary under Passage laid over Frequency and shifts where their operation is resident.
**Function** · Moves awareness, Essence circulation and Crystal operation into the threshold while the body stays on the Physical Plane; the barrier itself never moves.
**Mechanism** · Transference couples two tuned systems across the barrier. Transmission falls exponentially with thickness, so the practitioner pays for the match and the ground sets the price.
**Numerical Effect** · Extra thickness multiplies the cost rather than adding to it. A short crossing lasts under a minute, Bifold held costs a quarter percent of reserve a turn at Baseline, and carrying one other costs 4 percent.
**Target Response** · Bifold shows two renderings of one room: surfaces and light, overlaid with frequency, emotional density, temporal depth and relational ties. Buildings carry their history, people their Trait haloes, ground its grief and prayer.
**Consequence** · A crossing allows guiding spirits, stabilising fragments, soul preservation, deep Mnemata reading, tracing Attraction threads to their ends, and speech with Soul Plane entities.
**Limitation** · Thin zones bring contamination, memory bleed and involuntary Bifold; Thick zones cost exponentially more and muddy readings; at a rupture the planes collide rather than meet.
**Weakness** · Soul Drift: each crossing loosens anchorage for good, through Loosened, Thinned, Unmoored and Adrift to the Veil Pilgrim at a hundred points. Transference's reverse coupling can empty the practitioner into a depleted ally.
**Cost** · 2 percent of full reserve a Baseline crossing, about 45 before Starvation and about 4 in a Thick city. Drift is one point a short crossing, one more each minute resident, three for sustained liminal entry.
**Counterplay** · Give the practitioner lawful reasons to cross again and again, since only the count of entries shows Drift. Anyone ever carried through a crossing is counting Drift too.

## Origin
**Origin** · Sylorin, the Ferryman of Etherea, governs the Ethereal Flow that runs through the Veil, and Aetherica literature names the Veil the Spiritual Stratum. Eremund Vellsore developed the first known method of Veil-thread weaving through song.
**Practitioners** · Rikudoku Moto, said to have worked the deep Veil layers to fracture spiritual continuity across battlefields; Eremund Vellsore; the Bound Watchers at the outer edge of the Astral Fold, who sit in judgment over oaths and Temperance rites; the Veil Pilgrims, who never fully returned.

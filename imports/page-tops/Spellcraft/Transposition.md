## Summary card
**Effect** · Two things tuned alike trade places. At its plainest two bodies swap locations; at its most developed a death trades for an echo, a memory for an object, or a soul for a mirrored construct's housing.
**Cost** · 3 percent of full reserve a swap, plus 1 percent per relay hop and more uphill; 10 to 15 percent an identity trade; and an Anchor Construct placed for good and never recovered.
**Limit** · Coupling falls with the sixth power of distance, so range needs a prepared Anchor Construct, and both ends need a Crystal able to hold the exchanged coordinate.
**Counter** · Stand further off with nothing on the ground between, break the anchor, detune by Family, saturation or noise, or Silence the chamber.
**What nobody knows** · Whether the soulguarding orders truly traded themselves into condemned vessels during the Great Spirit War, and what they carried back out.

## Design Chain
**Trigger** · The officiant tunes both ends under Ci, Th and Ra, with an Anchor Construct placed between or beside them for anything past close range. At Splintering the swap also needs voice, hand, ink or blood.
**Function** · Makes two coordinates count as the same kind of thing, so Transference's standing law exchanges them; neither the coordinates nor the distance move.
**Mechanism** · Coupled resonance: alike ends trade completely, detuned ends only partly. An Anchor Construct relays the exchange, and two half-length hops each couple 64 times better than one long one.
**Numerical Effect** · Doubling any separation divides the transfer by 64; a detuning twice the coupling rate lets only half transfer; 80 kg moved 10 m uphill costs 7.8 kJ more. The swap usually finishes inside one six-second turn.
**Target Response** · Both parties spend the interval partly in each other's place, then stand where the other stood, in their circumstances, including the ones nobody mentioned.
**Consequence** · The counterparty gets the caster's place whether they wanted it or not, and an incomplete exchange does not revert. Layered with Cascade or Overtone effects, the trade can unfold across a delay.
**Limitation** · No one-way Transposition: the coupling runs both ways. Unlike ends trade only as far as Transmutatio can tune them into one frame, and any mismatch left over leaves a partial trade.
**Weakness** · Ends less alike than the tuning claimed stop short in Wellspring Desynchronization, leaving Spatial Disjunction: both sides holding part of one coordinate. Every A-Grade trade carries Overchannel backlash, and no Path protects against it.
**Cost** · 3 percent of full reserve a swap, thirty before Starvation; 10 to 15 percent an identity trade, six to nine before Starvation. 30 to 40 percent of each trade leaves the officiant as heat, sound and light.
**Counterplay** · Look for the Anchor Construct, the only tell at range; break it, stand further away, or be the wrong thing to couple to and let it complete. At Splintering, take the hand or the voice.

## Origin
**Origin** · Soulguarding orders are said to have used it in the Great Spirit War to place themselves inside condemned vessels, and Echo Constructs, soul-lattice bodies preloaded with Transposition scripts, came out of the same practice. The Fall of the Fourth Prince of Rovann and the Dancer-Priests of Ilcin'dor belong to its history.
**Practitioners** · Velcrissa of Selantra; Zeven Halek and his combat partner Cylus; Thalorath the Gatewrought; the Red Twins of Dorrosfenn; the soulguarding orders.

## Summary card
**Effect** · A fragment of the summoner's Essence Core is shaped around what they wish they were rather than what they are: a Vainglory Eidolon that passes for a better version of them and grows more false every turn.
**Cost** · 75,000 EU to fabricate, from about 8 percent of a deep Glory reserve to 90 percent of a shallow one. Then 10,000 to 25,000 EU a second from the summoner's own Core, doubling as the lie doubles.
**Limit** · It never stabilises, cannot be dismissed safely, cannot be made without cost to the summoner's Crystal, and convinces only those who never met the unflattering self.
**Counter** · Silence severs it; a witness who has seen the summoner on a bad day out-honests it; another Mirithane practitioner or Oblivara returns the correction to its source.
**What nobody knows** · What becomes of a summoner once the construct has taken their place, and whether anything of them remains inside it.

## Codex line
**Wellspring** · Mirithane, the Wellspring of Reflection (primary); Eidolyn, the Shaping Mind, for the construct's solidity; Oneirion, the Wellspring of Dream, for the flattering self it draws on.
**Family** · Limina
**Physics Domain** · Entropy, Void and Mind; the physics is positive feedback, a mirror given gain.
**Category** · Animatria, Spirit-aligned; a construct that outlives its summoner's control becomes a Synergia-category Phenomena event.
**Craft** · Spellcraft; the construct holds only while attention holds, and holds worse than a true Eidolon.
**Stage floor** · Glory
**Grade required** · A-Grade
**Path gate** · None to raise one; the Attraction, Spirit and Body Paths decide how good the likeness, template and spread can be.

## Origin
**Origin** · The Tome of Lurid Echoes is its founding text, and the Lament of the Nine Mirrors in the Era of Calamity is the mass-casualty event it is remembered for. The Order of the Hollow Veil still teaches a controlled form.
**Practitioners** · Serenhal Caelmorne, who projected a corrupted self during a Moetana trial; Yelara Vintress of the Rose-Glass Order, whose Eidolon stole her Pneuma and took her place; Raziel Ados, who impersonated himself across several Domains until the result consumed him.

## Summary card
**Effect** · A fragment of the summoner's Essence Core is shaped around what they wish they were rather than what they are: a Vainglory Eidolon that passes for a better version of them and grows more false every turn.
**Cost** · 75,000 EU to fabricate, from about 8 percent of a deep Glory reserve to 90 percent of a shallow one. Then 10,000 to 25,000 EU a second from the summoner's own Core, doubling as the lie doubles.
**Limit** · It never stabilises, cannot be dismissed safely, cannot be made without cost to the summoner's Crystal, and convinces only those who never met the unflattering self.
**Counter** · Silence severs it; a witness who has seen the summoner on a bad day out-honests it; another Mirithane practitioner or Oblivara returns the correction to its source.
**What nobody knows** · What becomes of a summoner once the construct has taken their place, and whether anything of them remains inside it.

## Codex line
**Wellspring** · Mirithane, the Wellspring of Reflection (primary); Eidolyn, the Shaping Mind, for the construct's solidity; Oneirion, the Wellspring of Dream, for the flattering self it draws on.
**Family** · Limina

**Category** · Animatria, Spirit-aligned; a construct that outlives its summoner's control becomes a Synergia-category Phenomena event.
**Craft** · Spellcraft; the construct holds only while attention holds, and holds worse than a true Eidolon.
**Stage floor** · Glory
**Grade required** · A-Grade
**Path gate** · None to raise one; the Attraction, Spirit and Body Paths decide how good the likeness, template and spread can be.

## Design Chain
**Trigger** · A deliberate raising under Mirage laid over Reverie, anchored by voice, hand, ink or blood. As often it arises by accident, in a failed ascension, a Temperance collapse or a Trait overreaching its Stage.
**Function** · Loosens how much correction the Shell's reflection may add, and Eidolyn deposits each corrected return as something with real material behaviour.
**Mechanism** · Positive feedback: each flattering return becomes the next state reflected, so the construct drifts toward the flattery on an exponential, pumped by the summoner against Mirithane's pull toward honesty.
**Numerical Effect** · One round trip a turn. At 10 percent Deviation the lie doubles about every seven turns, at 1 percent about every 69, and upkeep doubles each time the Deviation does.
**Target Response** · Strangers see a better version of the summoner; anyone who knows them sees an immaculate likeness of an untrue claim, worse by the hour.
**Consequence** · The summoner's own output falls, Thresholds refuse to authorise and Traits argue with them before anyone touches them. Then the construct saturates and displaces them, or the loop burns out their Shell and Core.
**Limitation** · It can stand in for the summoner and test or mislead something that would see through a cruder deception, but only for seconds to a dozen or so turns.
**Weakness** · Mirithane's mirror also broadcasts whatever the summoner would hide, Eidolyn reproduces every flaw of the aspirational template at scale, and Oneirion makes it convincing in the abstract and wrong in the specifics.
**Cost** · 75,000 EU to fabricate, then 10,000 to 25,000 EU a second, rising with the Deviation. 30 to 40 percent leaves the summoner's Shell as heat, sound and light.
**Counterplay** · Silence it, take its anchor, wait out the exponential, or bring an honest reflection; the construct need not be unmasked, only out-honested. Oblivara pushed past its correction limit reconstructs confidently and wrongly.

## Origin
**Origin** · The Tome of Lurid Echoes is its founding text, and the Lament of the Nine Mirrors in the Era of Calamity is the mass-casualty event it is remembered for. The Order of the Hollow Veil still teaches a controlled form.
**Practitioners** · Serenhal Caelmorne, who projected a corrupted self during a Moetana trial; Yelara Vintress of the Rose-Glass Order, whose Eidolon stole her Pneuma and took her place; Raziel Ados, who impersonated himself across several Domains until the result consumed him.

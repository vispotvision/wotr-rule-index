## Summary card
**Effect** · Inside a 200 metre dome, one named kind of action is barred or rerouted to a milder outcome: a lethal blow lands as a disarm. The Edict, not the speaker, is what refuses.
**Cost** · 12 percent of full reserve for the first Edict, each later one a quarter more until the voice has rested a full day. The speaker must embody the Edict, and breaking its spirit returns the backlash to their own Crystal.
**Limit** · One category of action per casting; narrow wording bites hard and broad wording barely registers.
**Counter** · Force the speaker to break their own Edict, oppose it with another Edict at the cost of both, or do the neighbouring action. Strong personal Domains partly ignore it, and a genuine Fate or Archonic mandate overrides it.
**What nobody knows** · Why an Edict spoken cynically, in the letter but not the spirit, still holds; one school says the rule reads the wording and never the speaker, and the question stays open.

## Design Chain
**Trigger** · A spoken Edict naming exactly one category of action, and the staff struck into the ground so there are witnesses; a law nobody heard cannot bind.
**Function** · Limits the outcomes one named class of action can reach inside the dome; strength, trajectory and energy are untouched.
**Mechanism** · Command laid across Order. Judicium recognises the named action by its signature, Aurevane carries enforcement evenly to the dome's edge, and Oneirion stretches the rule over actions just outside the letter of the wording.
**Numerical Effect** · A 200 metre dome at full potency for three turns, 18 seconds, then a weaker echo for one or two turns; the barred outcome is made ten to a hundred million times less likely.
**Target Response** · The bound action meets water-like resistance, then fails or resolves into the named alternative.
**Consequence** · The named violence, deception or other act becomes structurally hard to complete inside the dome while it holds; the barred outcome can still arrive late, weaker and by another route.
**Limitation** · One category per casting, centred on the speaker, who must stay present and consistent; three turns and an echo.
**Weakness** · A Void-aligned disturbance opens tears where neither law holds. The Attraction Layer pays, and its failure, Obsession Force, leaves a speaker unable to release an Edict; the dome's glare makes it a beacon.
**Cost** · 12, then 15, then 18.75 percent of full reserve before the voice rests: four Edicts held, the fifth reaching the floor. Repeated use roughens the voice.
**Counterplay** · Countersign or speak the Seal back, oppose it with another Edict, fight in fog or shielded interiors, or make the speaker break their own law. Insincerity lands later as a Coherence fault.

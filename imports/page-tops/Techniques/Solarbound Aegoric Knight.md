## Summary card
**Effect** · The knight takes blows and gives no ground while a store builds in the Shell, then spends it all in one oath-gated act: a cut, a vow spoken aloud, a stance that does not break. At the top, with a Domain seated, the release leaves as law: things that are sworn do not break.
**Cost** · 14 to 37 percent of full reserve per release, paid at once from the knight's own reserve. Blows absorbed are only the trigger; they bank beside the store and can overfill it.
**Limit** · No fast, repeated or incidental output and no partial release; output fades without a cause truly sworn.
**Counter** · Give him only incidental fights, displace his vow with a stronger one, take the hand or knee before the gesture, fight cold, shielded and moving, or fray him with Disruption.
**What nobody knows** · Whether Ser Varnath Rhogar founded the discipline, embodied it, or was simply the knight later students pointed to.

**Craft** · Spellcraft, delivered bodily through gesture-keys (kneeling, raising a shield, touching the oath-brand), re-committed each time.
**Stage floor** · Refraction
**Grade required** · A-Grade at Refraction; S-Grade from Transcendence to Realization.
**Path gate** · None for an A release; the Attraction Path for a layered release above A and for the seated-Domain release.

## Design Chain
**Trigger** · A knee, a shield raised or a hand on the brand, with a cause truly sworn; the oath is the ignition.
**Function** · Holds a store behind the Mercy barrier until conviction chooses to cross it, then releases it once, as an act that does not reverse.
**Mechanism** · Lock for the Mercy barrier, Balance for the oath gating, Illumination filing the release. Fixatio holds the store because relaxing is no longer reachable, Benediction prices the oath, and Judicium lands the release as a verdict.
**Numerical Effect** · An A-Grade release carries 46 GJ to 4.184 TJ and an S-Grade release up to 24.3 TJ; a hard day in the line catches only about 100 kJ of blows, far short of one release.
**Target Response** · The release arrives heavy before bright: the ground stiffens and the give goes out of things. While he waits, the knight reads flaws and lies continuously.
**Consequence** · With a Domain seated, the ground resists breaking, structurally and socially, for as long as his pulse carries it.
**Limitation** · The Strain Lamina traps, the Mercy Lamina sets the barrier, and the Oath Lamina amplifies near banners, seals and spoken oaths; without a sworn cause it has nothing to amplify.
**Weakness** · Storing past the Mercy Lamina seizes the Shell, escaped only by a deliberate oath-bleed, and a stronger vow can silently displace an older one. Disruption, Uurgath's glyph of chaotic anti-cohesion, frays the Crownforge spiral and must be re-centred by hand.
**Cost** · 14 percent of full reserve at the A floor, 37 percent at the S floor; two releases plus ordinary spending reach the ten-percent floor.
**Counterplay** · Hit often and lightly and offer nothing worth spending on: every blow banks and the store walks toward seizure. A wide Domain release past his Tempering Capacity risks a Crystal Fracture Event for everyone inside.

## Origin
**Origin** · The discipline names Ser Varnath Rhogar, the Scorn of the Outer World, as the source of its Aether; he held a Gate open with his own body long enough for a million souls to flee through it. No founder is named.
**Practitioners** · Ser Varnath Rhogar, a hero of the Guild Accord's Concord Military; the Impetists, who store and never release, and die full.

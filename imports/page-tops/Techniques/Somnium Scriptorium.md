## Summary card
**Effect** · The target hears his name and his awareness is pulled into a room built by his own dreaming mind, where the caster can question him, confront him or simply hold him, while his body stays standing and breathing. Used the other way, it pulls an ally out of a traumatic moment.
**Cost** · 1 percent of full reserve each turn held, 3 percent for the full working. The caster lags half a beat in the real room and carries home fragments of the target's fear or grief.
**Limit** · It traps and questions but cannot rewrite who the target is; a strong will resists entry and a fractured mind distorts what it yields.
**Counter** · A covered mouth before the name ends, a blow to the caster that opens the seal, a ward against forced relocation, or an ally breaking in mid-transfer.
**What nobody knows** · Why some targets remember every page of what happened inside and others wake with no memory the dream occurred at all; one reading blames how hot the target's own recording ran, and nobody can yet say which it will be.

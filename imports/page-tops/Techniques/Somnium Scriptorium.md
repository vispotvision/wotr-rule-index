## Summary card
**Effect** · The target hears his name and his awareness is pulled into a room built by his own dreaming mind, where the caster can question him, confront him or simply hold him, while his body stays standing and breathing. Used the other way, it pulls an ally out of a traumatic moment.
**Cost** · 1 percent of full reserve each turn held, 3 percent for the full working. The caster lags half a beat in the real room and carries home fragments of the target's fear or grief.
**Limit** · It traps and questions but cannot rewrite who the target is; a strong will resists entry and a fractured mind distorts what it yields.
**Counter** · Cover the mouth before the name ends, strike the caster to open the seal, set a ward against forced relocation, or have an ally break in mid-transfer.
**What nobody knows** · Why some targets remember every page of what happened inside and others wake with no memory the dream occurred at all; one reading blames how hot the target's own recording ran, and nobody can yet say which it will be.

## Design Chain
**Trigger** · A glyph inscribed toward the target in line of sight, with his name or true title spoken as the address.
**Function** · Addresses the target's state in the space of possible arrangements rather than in the room, and seals him into a constructed chamber while his body stays upright.
**Mechanism** · Reverie addresses, Lock seals and Form gives the template. Oneirion routes the working through conceptual space, Eidolyn gives the chamber real solidity, and the target's own dreaming mind builds the room along the caster's template.
**Numerical Effect** · 200 metres, one named target, three turns (18 seconds) of real time. Felt time inside can run far longer, though only about 720 bits of conscious report fit, room for a name and a date.
**Target Response** · The body stands and breathes on reflex; the target finds himself simply somewhere, in a room convincing because he built it, where only the questioner feels wrong.
**Consequence** · The caster questions, confronts or holds the target, or pulls an ally out of trauma; the target returns to a body that stood where he left it.
**Limitation** · No personality rewrite. Interrogation and a mercy-pull share one geometry but are two workings, one authorised and one a debt.
**Weakness** · A name is shared, mishearable and sometimes not his: projection error ejects both minds violently, template defect returns the question as the answer, and an opened seal leaves both on the wrong side.
**Cost** · 1 percent of full reserve a turn, thirty uses before Starvation. The caster glows with the waste heat and is half a beat late to everything in the room.
**Counterplay** · Knot the target's path or Lock the anchor first, keep the caster in crowds and Domains, or strike during the half-beat. A ward against relocation is Runecraft and is beaten by chiselling its substrate.

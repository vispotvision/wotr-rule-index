## Summary card
**Effect** · Speculum Harmoniae casts a second body that carries the target's signature and moves like him, so a pursuit must split. Its companion working, Nexus Harmoniae, raises a field up to a kilometre wide that routes every working through the caster, adding gain to those in tune with her.
**Cost** · Speculum: 2 percent of full reserve a cast and a slice of the caster's read of that target, thin until she observes them again. Nexus: 1 percent a turn for six turns, with rising overload and cross-sense confusion, the caster fixed standing.
**Limit** · The double cannot land a real blow and stops existing when struck once. The Nexus bias is small, cannot win an exchange alone, and misses anything faster than the caster's reaction.
**Counter** · Track fear, hatred or grief rather than Essence contour, watch the feet, or use Silence against the double. Fog, shielding, a strong personal Domain, or a working that runs quiet under the field's reading beats the Nexus.
**What nobody knows** · Whether the double, in the instant before it is struck, feels anything, or whether the hollow beat Serenyra reports afterwards is hers alone.

## Design Chain
**Trigger** · Speculum: a target within 3 to 8 metres and a completed circle traced around them. Nexus: a finished chant and gesture from a standing position.
**Function** · Speculum: lets one signature have two bearers, so trackers keyed to Essence contour, sight or prediction spend their attention on the wrong one. Nexus: treats allies in resonance as one aperture, routing every working in the field through the caster before it resolves.
**Mechanism** · Speculum: Equivalence, Mirage and Reverie; Mirithane gives an honest copy and Oneirion walks it like a body making choices. Nexus: Order, Measure and Vastness; Judicium reads every working in range and Aurevane pins a shared tone across the field.
**Numerical Effect** · Speculum: three turns, 18 seconds, or until struck. Nexus: up to one kilometre for six turns, 36 seconds; workings in phase add as their number, strangers only as its square root.
**Target Response** · Pursuers find two bodies correct on every instrument, and the one caught simply stops being present. Inside the Nexus a battle goes slightly wrong with no moment to point at, and everyone feels read.
**Consequence** · Fire and tracking meant for the target find the double. The Nexus withholds gain from anything out of tune with the caster and weakens no enemy directly.
**Limitation** · The double is misidentified, not hidden, and has no mass, so touch notices it. The Nexus cannot route anything faster than the caster's reaction, so a close, fast strike falls outside it.
**Weakness** · Void-aligned sight bypasses the double, and in a Silence it is correct and produces nothing. A Nexus dropped abruptly sends a Resonance Shockwave across its whole radius, caster included.
**Cost** · Speculum: 2 percent of full reserve a cast, 45 before Starvation. Nexus: 6 percent for its six turns, 15 before Starvation, with migraine-grade overload the longer it holds.
**Counterplay** · Draw the double out again and again against the same foe to spend the caster's read. The Nexus caster glares at the field's centre, but downing her hurts both armies.

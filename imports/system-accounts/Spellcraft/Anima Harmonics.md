# System account · Anima Harmonics

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** A structure that is still running is listened
to from outside, without being touched, opened or loaded, and the listener
recovers its internal condition from the sound it was making anyway.

**The real phenomenon.** Three real instruments, and the page's own description
names all three without naming any of them.

*Modal analysis.* Every mechanical structure has a set of natural frequencies
fixed by its stiffness and mass distribution, and the whole of its internal
geometry is encoded in that set. You do not need to look inside a bell to know
it is cracked. **A crack breaks the structure's rotational symmetry, which
splits what was a degenerate pair of modes into two lines a few hertz apart, and
the two beat against each other.** That beat is the buzz. It is not a metaphor
for damage; it is the measured consequence of the asymmetry, and its frequency
is proportional to the asymmetry's size. A sound bell rings one note. A cracked
bell rings two notes very close together and the listener hears the difference
frequency.

*Auscultation and acoustic emission testing.* A physician reading vital signs —
the page's own comparison — is running a passive acoustic diagnostic on a
structure whose failure modes announce themselves in the audio band before they
announce themselves anywhere else. Industrially the same practice is acoustic
emission monitoring: a pressure vessel about to fail emits bursts as
micro-cracks propagate, hours to days ahead of the event. **The distortion
precedes the failure**, which is the page's Damage Detection claim stated in
engineering terms and is true of real structures for real reasons.

*Spurious-line detection.* A spectrum analyser sweeping a running machine
returns lines at the machine's own characteristic frequencies and their
harmonics. A line at a frequency the machine's own geometry cannot produce came
from somewhere else — a coupled neighbour, a contaminated bearing, an external
drive. That is a **Fell Chord**, exactly: *"notes that should not be in this
chord"* (`wiki/The Disciplines/The Harmonic Arts.md`). Identifying one is a
matter of comparing the observed line list against the line list the subject's
own structure is capable of, which is why the discipline's first taught skill is
knowing what a clean Crystal sounds like.

**The governing law.** **Modal decomposition of a linear elastic structure** —
any vibration is a weighted sum of the structure's normal modes, and the weights
and frequencies are set by the structure and by nothing else — with
**degenerate-mode splitting under broken symmetry** as the specific law behind
the buzz, and **the noise-floor limit** governing what can be heard at all.
Caelmorne's contribution is entirely in that third law, and it is the real
contribution.

**Measurable quantities, SI.** The page states no ranges, durations, EU figures
or frequencies. Everything the system would need to price this discipline is
absent (`SA-GAP-ANIMA-NO-NUMBERS`). What the real phenomenon does supply, as
checkable consequence rather than as canon:

- A cracked bell's split doublet typically beats at **1–10 Hz**, audible as a
  wobble rather than as two pitches. Scale of asymmetry needed to produce a
  given beat is proportional to the split, so *how badly* a Crystal buzzes is a
  reading of *how far* the fracture has run, and the discipline's claim to grade
  damage by ear is well founded.
- Detection is noise-floor limited. **Lowering the instrument's own emission by
  a factor of ten multiplies the detection range for a fixed source by about
  three** (inverse-square in a free field). Caelmorne's silence is not humility;
  it is a 10 dB improvement in sensitivity, and the page's own line — *"a
  Harmonist with loud, undisciplined Shell output hears nothing but their own
  tone"* — is the self-noise limit stated exactly.
- Conducting during *"lunar Aether tides, when ambient Resonance naturally
  quiets"* is the same move a seismologist makes by recording at night and a
  gravitational-wave observatory makes by sitting in an old mine: **you cannot
  raise the signal, so you go where the background is lower.**

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** Receptive mode is passive and
costs essentially nothing in joules: the subject is radiating anyway, and the
Harmonist is a receiver. The expensive half is **projection** — Microharmonic
Recalibration, Wellspring Stabilization, Emotional Resonance Therapy — and even
there the energy delivered is small. A reference tone does not have to overpower
the patient's Crystal; it has to be *coherent* and *correct*. This is injection
locking, first recorded by Huygens in 1665 when two pendulum clocks on the same
beam fell into antiphase: a coupling far too weak to move either clock by force
nonetheless captures both, provided the frequencies are close enough to begin
with. **The power that arrives is negligible. The precision is the whole
expense.**

**Precedent we are not copying.** Soul-reading healers are standard: the
sensing type of every JRPG party, Naruto's Hyūga diagnostics, the empath who
knows you are sad. Those read *feeling*. This reads **structure**, returns a
Stage number and a Trait list, and its therapeutic half is **an ethical crime in
its own doctrine when performed without consent**. The divergence is that the
listening and the assault are the same operation — *"Perception and manipulation
are not separate skills. They are the same skill applied with different
intent"* — so a Harmonist in the room is a standing legal problem whether or not
they do anything.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** Draw is near-nil in receptive mode and the Harmonist is
instead *withholding* output, which is a different kind of expenditure and one
the system prices under a different heading (`Counterplay`, III: *"Make him hold
Pressure in. Suppression is a skill and it is tiring"*). **Residue:** a
diagnostic listen leaves none, which is one of the very few workings in this
batch that genuinely does not. A therapeutic projection leaves the ordinary
trace of a sustained tone at the patient's own frequency, which is why a second
Harmonist can tell that a Crystal has been worked on. **Saturation:** the wrong
kind of field for this discipline entirely. The limiting field quantity here is
not Aetheric Density but **ambient Resonance**, and it limits by *raising the
floor* rather than by *lowering the ceiling* — the only working in the batch
whose field constraint runs that direction (`SA-GAP-AMBIENT-RESONANCE-SCALE`).

**Wellspring stratum.** **None, and the page is right to say so.** *"Anima
Harmonics reads whichever Wellspring dialect a given Soul Crystal is harmonized
to; a Fixatio Trait hums differently from a Transmutatio Trait or a Verdantia
Trait, and the Harmonist's skill is in recognizing the dialect, not in drawing
from it."* A discipline that draws from no current is a discipline whose stratal
account is unusually thin, and that thinness is load-bearing rather than
missing: it is the reason the branch has no Family failure terrain of its own and
inherits its counters from the Categories instead.

The Wellsprings that appear in an account of this working appear as **the read
things**, not as the source. Three are quotable at the line the discipline
actually uses:

> **Basilithe · *The Stone Will*** — *"A body's resonant frequency is fixed by
> its stiffness and its mass, and a high-stiffness low-loss structure holds that
> frequency against forcing so reliably that civilisations set their clocks by
> cut quartz. Basilithe raises the Crystal's stiffness until its resonance simply
> will not be driven off, which is a defence against every technique that works
> by imposing an external rhythm."*
> (`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Materia — Material Science.md`)

That is the counter to the therapeutic half of this discipline, written four
Wellsprings before anyone thought to ask for one, and it is findable by a player
who reads the Materia register.

> **Mirithane · *The Wellspring of Reflection*** — *"A true mirror adds nothing.
> Angle in equals angle out, and fidelity is defined entirely by the absence of
> introduced distortion."* Failure: *"The mirror does not discriminate. A
> practitioner in a state they would rather not broadcast broadcasts it
> perfectly."* (`Limina — Entropy, Void and Mind.md`)

Mirithane is why a soul has an honest tone to read at all. The discipline is
parasitic on that honesty.

> **Tenebra · *The Hidden Shadow*** — *"Something is undetectable when its
> return falls below the noise floor of whatever is looking."* (`Limina`)

Tenebra is the other end of the same equation, and its **Harmonics Suppression**
stat effect is the register's own name for defeating an Anima read.

*Four Theories.* **The Correspondence Position** — *"The practitioner and the
Wellspring are the same law expressed at two scales, and the working is the two
scales briefly agreeing"* (`Two Sets of Books` §IV). This is the only page in
the Spellcraft batch whose own doctrine is explicitly Correspondence: the
Auricle Choir holds *"that each soul is a note in a larger Divine Choral"*,
which is the Correspondence Position with a liturgy on it. Ecclesiastically
respectable and administratively useless, exactly as the Accord says.

**Essence stratum.** Active layer in receptive mode: the **Aether Shell**,
run backwards. *"Resonance gates that normally open outward must learn to open
inward"* (`The Harmonic Arts`) — the Shell is the Material-Plane layer and the
whole of the training is a reconfiguration of it, not of the Core. Therapeutic
projection adds the **Essence Core** (the reference tone has to be the
Harmonist's own, honest, and stable) and Fell Chord surgery reaches the
**Attraction Layer**, since a Fell Chord planted by *"involuntary Conjunction"*
is sitting in the layer that holds bonds. Developmental Tier at the Stage VII
floor: **Resonant** — *"The Crystal self-conducts. Power moves even in silence"*
(Part Twenty-One), which is the tier description doing the branch's work for it.
Crystal State: not stated for any named practitioner. **The invoice:** the page
bills nothing. Not an EU figure, not a rate, not a Harmonics toll, not a
duration. What it bills instead is a **standing behavioural cost** — a Shell
held quiet, continuously, as a condition of practising at all — and the system
does price that, in the Counterplay register, under *"Make him hold Pressure
in."* Whether a standing suppression cost is a cost the ledger recognises is
open (`SA-FAIR-ANIMA-NO-COST`).

**The lens: the soul as attunement.** Simmias' objection in Plato's *Phaedo*
(85e–86d): the soul stands to the body as the *harmonia* of a lyre stands to the
lyre — an invisible, incorporeal, beautiful thing that is nonetheless *entirely
constituted by* the tension and arrangement of the physical parts, and which
perishes when they are broken. Socrates destroys it in four moves, and one of
them is the move that matters here: **an attunement cannot oppose its own
instrument.** A tuning cannot be more or less itself than the strings permit,
cannot resist them, and cannot be out of tune with what produces it. The
Auricle Choir reads the Anima through Simmias, and reads it beautifully: each
soul a note, the Continuum a chord, harmonisation a sacrament and forced
harmonisation an assault — which is a doctrine that follows rigorously *if* the
soul just is its tuning. Where the lens misleads is on the page's own third
sentence: *"A corrupted one emits frequencies that do not belong to its
owner."* On Simmias' account that sentence cannot be true. A tuning has no
frequencies that are not its instrument's. A **Fell Chord is a standing
refutation of the Choir's own theology**, sitting inside the branch's most
routine diagnostic, and a Choir chaplain who has internalised the doctrine will
hear a foreign line and conclude the subject is guilty rather than infected —
because on his reading the tone *is* the soul, and a wrong tone is therefore a
wrong soul rather than a wrong *thing in* the soul. This is a good way to get a
Mechanica victim tried instead of treated. Offered as a school's error, not as
canon.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Lh]` **Frequency** · Root · Selhar —
the station the discipline's own patron holds, *"Selhar, the Voice of Harmony,
Keeper of the Nine Chords"* — laid under `[Sil]` **Silence** · Synonym · Auren
for Caelmorne's method, which conditions the *background* rather than the
subject. Both from the thirty unclassified forms in
`wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`, and both
are Roots or Root-synonyms naming *a kind of limit* rather than a law, which is
what a discipline with no Wellspring of its own needs. *Filing decision, not
canon — Isaac's ruling (`SA-GAP-GLYPH-MIRROR`).*

**What boundary moves.** One quantity, and it is not in the patient. **The
Harmonist's own emission floor.** `[Sil]` pins the Shell's outward radiation
near zero; `[Lh]` pins the Shell's gates open inward across a band. Nothing is
imposed on the subject at all in the diagnostic half of the discipline — which
is the mechanical reason diagnosis is lawful everywhere and therapy is not.

**What the law then does on its own.** Sound that was already in the room
arrives at a receiver that has stopped drowning it. The subject's Crystal has
been radiating its Resonant Tone continuously since it opened, *"as a byproduct
of normal operation"*; the Veil carries it; the Harmonist has merely stopped
adding to it. Modal decomposition then does the rest without anyone deciding
anything: the chord separates into its lines because that is what a linear
superposition does when you can resolve it, the Stage shows up as *"a
characteristic harmonic band"* because stiffness sets frequency, the Traits show
up as individual lines because *"every Trait hums at the frequency of the law it
embodies"*, the compound Traits show up as **interaction** between their
component lines rather than as a third line, and the fracture shows up as a
**beat** because a broken symmetry splits a degenerate pair. None of this is
done to the subject. It is read off them, the way a bell's crack is read off its
ring.

In the therapeutic half the boundary moves again and this time it is in the
patient: a clean reference tone is held adjacent to a damaged oscillator, and
**injection locking** pulls the damaged one toward the reference across a
capture range set by how close the two already were. This is why Microharmonic
Recalibration is described as the patient's Crystal *using* the tone rather than
being driven by it, and why Caelmorne's silent method works better — a captured
oscillator locks to whatever is loudest and nearest, so a noisy practitioner is
competing with their own diagnostic broadcast for the capture.

**Law V check.** The floor is **Stage VII, Refraction** (page, and
`The Harmonic Arts` Temperance Gate), which is **exactly the Stage at which the
material anchor stops being required** (`wiki/The Magic System/The Four
Crafts.md`, Law V settled as a gate). The coincidence is not a coincidence: a
working whose whole method is the suppression of one's own outward expression
**cannot** use voice, hand, ink or blood, because every one of those is an
outward expression and would raise the floor the working exists to lower. **This
discipline is not merely permitted to be internalised at Refraction; it is
impossible below it.** The gate closes, and it closes for a reason the page never
states and the system does.

**Failure mode — Boundary fault.** *"The condition was established imprecisely
and the law ran somewhere unintended"* (`Two Sets of Books` §VII). In the
therapeutic half this is **Echo Fracture**: *"A therapeutic tone projected at the
wrong frequency shatters the patient's Crystal instead of healing it"*
(`The Harmonic Arts`). The physics is unforgiving about why. Injection locking
captures inside a range and **drives destructively outside it**; a reference an
octave off is not a weaker treatment, it is a forcing function at the patient's
own resonance with no damping, which is the mechanism by which an opera singer
breaks a glass and a column of soldiers breaks a bridge. The distance between
healing and shattering is a frequency error, not a power error, and the working
does not degrade gracefully across it.

A second fault sits on the diagnostic half and the page names it: *"a Harmonist
with loud, undisciplined Shell output hears nothing but their own tone and cannot
perform any of the above at all."* That is a **Field fault** with the practitioner
as the field — the room could not supply the quiet the working assumed, and the
room in question is the Harmonist.

**What the target sees and feels.** Nothing, and that is the whole of the tell's
difficulty. A Harmonist working diagnostically is a person being unusually still
in a room, and the subject is not touched, addressed or approached. What the
Harmonist then holds is the subject's **Temperance Stage** *"without physical
examination or instrumentation"*, their **Trait list** with fusion instabilities
flagged, their **Crystal damage** *"before the subject shows symptoms"*, and any
**Fell Chord** riding in the chord that does not belong to them. Under therapy
the patient feels something quite specific and quite gentle: a tone that is not
theirs, held steady beside theirs, and their own Crystal moving toward it of its
own accord — *"a tuning fork the patient's Crystal uses to find its correct
frequency again."* Under Caelmorne's method they feel the room go quiet first,
and the quiet is the treatment's larger half. Under Emotional Resonance Therapy
they feel grief soften, panic damp, rage cool — **and if they did not consent,
they will not necessarily notice that any of it was done**, which is the entire
basis of the Auricle Choir's absolutism and the reason the branch is charged
under sacred law rather than under assault.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** η is unstated for every practitioner
this page names, because no named practitioner has a card
(`SA-GAP-ANIMA-NO-CARD`). At the Stage VII floor the Tier of Standing is
**5 · Expert**, η **0.60–0.70** (Part Nineteen as corrected by **R44-4**), so
**30 to 40 percent of every
therapeutic expenditure leaves as heat, sound and structural bleed** — and note
what that means for *this* working specifically, which the generic statement
hides: **the bleed of a Harmonic working is itself sound**, radiated at the
working's own frequencies, into a room the practitioner has spent the entire
session making quiet. A Stage VII Harmonist doing therapy is his own worst
observing condition. The discipline gets measurably quieter at Stage IX (Tier 6,
η 0.70–0.80, so 20 to 30 percent) for reasons that are acoustic rather than
doctrinal — about a third less radiated waste, not the halving the superseded
Tier 5 figure implied.

**The arithmetic that can be done.** Only one line of it, and it is a gate check
rather than a cost. Harmonics **Suppression** requires *Spirit Path at Stage V to
exceed A* (Part Seven). A-Grade tops out at **400** (Part Four). The Stage VII
ceiling is **475 under strain, allocation stopping at 400** (Part Five).
Therefore: **a Stage VII Harmonist on the Spirit Path can hold Suppression at
401–475 and no higher**, and a Harmonist not on the Spirit Path is capped at 400
outright — which sets a hard, derivable ceiling on how quiet the branch's
founding method can be made at its own floor Stage, and is the one genuinely
computable quantity in this account.

**Does the physics close against the stratal account?** **In the diagnostic
half, yes, and better than most pages in the batch.** A passive acoustic read
costs almost no joules, and the stratal account correspondingly bills almost no
Essence; both accounts agree that the scarce resource is *quiet*, which is a
field condition rather than an expenditure. The two sets of books balance
because there is almost nothing in either of them.

**In the therapeutic half, no, and the failure is on the Essence side.** A
projection that can *"shatter the patient's Crystal"* is a working with a
catastrophic output and a blank invoice. The physical account closes fine —
injection locking is cheap, and a resonant forcing that destroys a structure is
famously cheap, which is the whole horror of it — but a stratal account whose
Essence stratum reads `null` against an effect of that magnitude has not been
taken. `SA-PHYS-ANIMA-THERAPY-UNBILLED`.

## 5 · Counterplay and the challenge

What it *implies* is a real and continuous cost — a Shell held quiet as a precondition of practising, which the Counterplay register prices under *"Make him hold Pressure in. Suppression is a skill and it is tiring"* — but implication is not an invoice, and the working's most dangerous half (a projection that can shatter a Crystal) is the half with the emptiest ledger. | It has a tell | **partial pass, and the weakness is real** | Therapy has a tell: a tone that is not yours, held beside yours. **Diagnosis has almost none** — the practitioner is standing still and being quiet, which is what the working *is*. The countertell is inverse and a careful opponent can use it: a practitioner whose Essence signature has gone *abnormally* quiet in a room where everyone else is radiating is doing something, and the only cheap thing that looks like that is a Harmonist opening their gates inward. **Tenebra looks the same from outside**, which is the ambiguity worth playing. |

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Deny the field — be loud.** The branch's field is not Aether, it is
  *quiet*, and quiet is the cheapest thing in the register to take away. This is
  the only working in the batch that a **drum line** defeats. It is also why
  Caelmorne worked at lunar tide and why the Sonochoral Conclave *"maintain
  strict internal secrecy about their protocols, because an opponent who knows
  the frequencies can develop counter-harmonics"* — the Conclave is protecting a
  noise-floor advantage, not a spell list. The register's general form applies
  too: **Thick Veil zones degrade Harmonia perception**
  (`wiki/Spellcraft/Resonant Divination.md`, the sibling branch's own limit).
- **Break the boundary — harden the frequency.** **Basilithe**, *The Stone
  Will*, of Materia: *"Basilithe raises the Crystal's stiffness until its
  resonance simply will not be driven off, which is a defence against every
  technique that works by imposing an external rhythm."* A Basilithe-harmonised
  subject cannot be recalibrated, cannot be stabilised, and cannot be
  emotionally retuned — **the whole therapeutic half fails against them**, with
  no Stage advantage required and no counter-working cast. The register even
  supplies the price of the defence: *"Resonant catastrophe. A frequency that
  cannot be shifted also cannot be detuned away from a forcing that happens to
  match it"*, so the Basilithe subject who meets a Harmonist who guesses right is
  in more trouble than anyone. That is a fair trade a player can find and choose.
- **Break the boundary — Knot.** `[Knt]` **Knot** — *"entangles a path so it
  cannot be traced cleanly"* — is the register's named answer to any reading, and
  against a modal read the physical form is straightforward: **add lines.** A
  Crystal deliberately carrying spurious harmonics is a Crystal whose own line
  list no longer distinguishes a Fell Chord from a decoration, and the Harmonist's
  first skill — knowing what the subject's structure *can* produce — is the
  skill that fails.
- **Break the body — and the branch has no answer at all.** Nothing in Anima
  Harmonics stops a crossbow. The diagnostic half delivers information and the
  therapeutic half requires a cooperative patient; neither produces force,
  neither produces a ward, and the practitioner is standing still by method.
  *"Shot. Chemically cheap, mechanically simple."*
- **Break the man — the consent doctrine is a weapon.** *"Unauthorized Anima
  manipulation is punishable under sacred law"*, absolutely and *"with no middle
  ground"*, per the Auricle Choir. A Harmonist working on an unconsenting subject
  in a sanctified jurisdiction has handed an opponent a prosecution, and an
  opponent who *manufactures* the appearance of one has handed them the same
  thing. Compare the register's *"the vow"*: an opponent who knows the terms of a
  doctrine holds a weapon that does not require touching him.

**The tell, stated plainly.** Under therapy: a tone that is not yours, held
steady beside yours, and your own Crystal drifting toward it without your
deciding to. Under diagnosis: a person who has gone quieter than the room, and
stayed that way — which is also exactly what a Tenebra practitioner looks like,
and telling the two apart is a judgement call a player gets to make under time
pressure.

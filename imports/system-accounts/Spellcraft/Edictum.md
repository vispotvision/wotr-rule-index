# System account · Edictum

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** A sentence is spoken at a named thing, and
from that moment the thing's own behaviour has a wall in it that was not there
before. Nothing pushes. The wall is simply where the thing's possibilities now
end.

**The real phenomenon.** A **Dirichlet boundary condition imposed on a running
field**, with the **Meissner effect** as its physical embodiment and the
**critical field** as the reason the working has the limit it has.

*The boundary condition.* A field obeying a differential equation does not have
one solution; it has a family of them, and which member of the family is
realised is decided entirely by the conditions at the boundary. Ground a
conducting plane and you have not pushed a single charge — you have declared
that the potential on that surface is zero, and the entire field everywhere else
rearranges into the unique solution compatible with the declaration. The
**method of images** is the standard way of computing the result, and its
strangeness is instructive: the field behaves exactly as though a mirror charge
existed on the far side of the plane, which is to say **the boundary produces an
effect indistinguishable from a force that is not there.**

*The Meissner effect.* Cool a superconductor below its transition temperature in
a magnetic field and the field is expelled. Not shielded, not resisted, not
pushed back — **excluded**, because B = 0 inside is a boundary condition the
superconducting state imposes, and screening currents arise at the surface, of
whatever magnitude is required, with no dissipation, to make it true. This is
why the page's Concord casework line is exactly correct: *"being told a wall is
there"* rather than *"being pushed against one."* A field at a superconductor's
face is not in a contest. It is in a region where the solution it wanted does
not exist.

*The critical field.* **And here the working's limit stops being flavour.** The
Meissner state holds only below a critical field H_c. Above it, superconductivity
does not weaken gradually and then fail — **it stops**, and the field enters
completely. The transition is a threshold, not a gradient, and the excluded
field's own magnitude is what sets it. The page's Counter says exactly this:
*"A target genuinely greater than the caster's source of authority is simply not
bound"* — *simply*, with no contest, no degradation and no partial binding. That
is what a critical-field threshold looks like from the inside.

**The governing law.** **Uniqueness of solutions to a boundary-value problem**:
given the equation and the conditions on the boundary, the field in the interior
is determined and there is no second answer. The glyph supplies the condition;
the law supplies the rest and supplies it without being asked.

**Measurable quantities, SI.** The page gives EU and no physics, as every
Design-Chain page in this section does. What the real law supplies as checkable
consequence:

- **The cost of exclusion.** Expelling a field of flux density B from a volume V
  costs the field energy that was in it: **U = B²V / 2μ₀**. For B = 1 T over
  1 m³: U = 1 / (2 × 4π×10⁻⁷) = **3.98 × 10⁵ J**. For a domain-scale exclusion,
  10⁴ m³ at the same field: **3.98 × 10⁹ J** — which sits inside **B-Grade
  attack output** (1.046×10⁹ – 4.6024×10¹⁰ J, Part Four), one full Grade below
  the A-Grade the page requires. **The working is cheap in joules relative to
  its own Grade requirement**, and that is the expected shape for a boundary
  working rather than a delivered one.
- **The screening currents are lossless and therefore the hold is free.** Once
  established, a Meissner state costs nothing per second to maintain; the
  currents circulate without dissipation. This is the physical account's version
  of the page's *"holds for as long as the caster's authority over that ground
  remains uncontested"* — **the Edict has no upkeep**, and the thing it depends
  on is a *state*, not an expenditure.
- **The threshold scales with the excluding state, not with the excluded
  field.** H_c is a property of the superconductor. Whether a given field is
  excluded is decided by comparing it against a number that belongs entirely to
  the wall. Which is why *"it cannot bind a target the caster has no lawful
  standing over"* is a statement about the speaker and not about the target's
  strength.

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** In the field, and the field
was the target's own behaviour. The caster supplies a condition and the
rearrangement follows; nobody is pushed and nothing is delivered. `Two Sets of
Books` §III in its cleanest form — *"They supply the boundary condition, and the
boundary condition is cheap in absolute terms and expensive in the only currency
a soul has"* — and here what it is expensive in is named on the page and is not
EU: *"Full clarity of soul at the moment of speaking."*

**Precedent we are not copying.** The binding word is everywhere: the Geas of
every D&D table, Fate's Command Spells, the *Nen* conditions of Hunter × Hunter,
Bleach's Kidō binding numbers. Most of them resolve as a contest of will. This
one resolves as **a question of standing, decided before the sentence is
spoken**, and it fails against a superior without a roll, a struggle or a
dramatic moment — *"simply not bound."* The divergence is that an Edictum is
**lost before it is cast** or won before it is cast, and the only interesting
part of a scene involving one is therefore whether the speaker has correctly
assessed their own rank. Which is the least heroic and most bureaucratic
failure mode available, and is exactly right for a Judicium working.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** Draw is a single sharp expenditure at the instant the
sentence closes, and then **nothing** — the hold is a state rather than a
sustain, which makes Edictum one of the very few Spellcraft workings in the
corpus that does not lapse when attention lapses, and that anomaly is logged
(`SA-CROSS-EDICTUM-SPELLCRAFT-DURABILITY`). **Residue:** heavy and legible. A
bound target carries the condition as a standing feature, which is why *"the
Edict is lifted by the same or greater authority that set it"* is even possible
— there is something present to lift. **Saturation:** not a limiting factor; the
expenditure is brief and the sustain is free. Fulguria's terrain applies to the
*speaking*, not to the hold: *"fails against boiled leather, fired clay, dense
fog and a shielded interior"* (`Counterplay`, I).

**Wellspring stratum.** **Judicium · *The Wellspring of Truth***, Family
**Fulguria**, Physics Domain **electromagnetism**. Its law, verbatim:

> *"Every element absorbs and emits at wavelengths fixed by its own electron
> structure, and no substance can present another's spectrum. Judicium tunes
> Gnosis to that principle: the practitioner reads the characteristic lines of a
> Crystal, a technique, or a lie, and identifies composition from signature
> alone. Eddy-current inspection finds the crack under the paint without removing
> the paint."*
> (`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Fulguria — Electromagnetism.md`)

Judicium is a **reading** Wellspring, and the page uses it correctly and
narrowly: *"Judicium supplying the discernment that decides exactly where the new
limit sits."* The Wellspring does not enforce. It locates. What enforces is the
boundary condition, and boundary conditions are not anybody's property. This is
why an Edictum's Wellspring line names one current and its mechanism names none:
**the discernment is Judicium's and the wall is the world's.**

Judicium's failure is the one that matters here: *"Spectral overload. Every
object in view reports its composition simultaneously and the practitioner loses
the ability to prioritise any of it."* An Edict is a sentence that must name
**one** condition at **one** boundary, and a Wellspring whose failure is the
inability to prioritise is a poor companion for a working whose whole precision
is in choosing which limit to move.

*Four Theories.* **The Consent Doctrine**, enforced, and the page is the
Doctrine's flagship case. *"The Wellspring recognises a lawful boundary condition
and behaves accordingly, in the same sense that a river recognises a channel"* —
and note that the Doctrine exists *"because a process can be licensed and a
relationship cannot"* (`Two Sets of Books` §IV). Edictum is a **licensing**
working. It is the Doctrine's own shape performed as a technique, and it is not
an accident that the Accord's enforced theory and its most institutional working
have the same grammar.

**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Attraction Layer** — the Fate-Plane
layer, which *"holds bonds, Domains, and Wellspring harmonizations"* and is where
*"names, vows, lineages, Domains, and outcomes"* are lawfully acknowledged.
Standing is an Attraction-Layer quantity, and an Edict is an operation on
standing. The **Essence Core** supplies the conviction the working reads as its
own authorisation. The **Aether Shell** does almost nothing, which is unusual and
is why Fulguria's terrain modifiers bite weakly here. Developmental Tier at the
Stage VII floor: **Resonant** (Part Twenty-One). Crystal State: not stated.
**The invoice:** stated in EU on the page, *and* stated separately as a
precondition — *"any impurity in that conviction is charged back to the caster
rather than absorbed by the target"*, which is the page's name for an **Echo
Reversal** and is a Coherence-fault cost rather than a reserve cost.

**The lens: *auctoritas, non veritas, facit legem*.** Hobbes, *Leviathan* ch. 26,
in the Latin edition of 1668: *authority, not truth, makes law.* A command is not
binding because it is correct; it is binding because it issues from a sovereign
the subject has already, by the terms of the covenant, agreed to be bound by, and
a subject who evaluates a law's truth before obeying it has reserved a right the
covenant was made to give up. This is the exact grammar of Edictum, down to the
page's own limit: *"it enforces an existing authority relationship rather than
inventing one, and against a target with no lawful subordination to the caster it
does nothing."* Hobbes would recognise that sentence as his own. Where the lens
misleads is at the Bench's open question — *"why an Edictum spoken in perfect
legal form still fails against some targets with no documented authority
advantage at all."* On a strict Hobbesian reading there is no such case: if the
Edict failed, the sovereignty was defective, and the remedy is therefore to send
a **higher-ranked speaker**. A Bench that reasons this way escalates, and
escalation is the wrong instrument if the gap is not a rank gap. The
instrument-level candidate worth putting to Isaac rather than asserting: a
**type-II superconductor** does not fail at H_c1. It admits **flux vortices** —
discrete quantised threads of field that penetrate the material while the bulk
remains superconducting, so the exclusion is *locally* defeated at points while
*globally* holding. A target with a fine enough internal structure could thread
an Edict the same way: not outranking it anywhere, penetrating it everywhere,
and reading to the Bench as an authority anomaly because no authority was ever
exceeded. That would make the answer *granularity* rather than rank, and it
would mean the Bench's escalation makes matters worse, because raising H_c
raises the vortex count. Offered as a candidate, not as canon.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[RenB]` **Boundary** · Root ·
Zhaeren, laid under `[Ath]` **Command** · Root · Irath — *"the smallest station
in the index carries the two heaviest words in it."* Both from the thirty
unclassified forms, both Roots, and the Index's own reason for leaving them
unclassified is the reason they fit: *"Each names a kind of limit, applicable to
any law at all."* An Edict conditions **whatever law the named target's
behaviour runs on**, which cannot be known before the target is named, so a glyph
that named a Family would be the wrong glyph on principle.
*Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling (`SA-GAP-GLYPH-MIRROR`).*

**What boundary moves.** Exactly one, and it is stated at the target rather than
in the room: **the value the target's own behaviour is permitted to take at a
named limit.** A sentenced target's permitted set no longer contains the
forbidden act. A protected target's permitted set no longer contains being
touched by the named harm. A silenced target's no longer contains the named
Craft. The condition is Dirichlet — *this quantity has this value here* — and
Judicium's contribution is deciding **where** *here* is.

**What the law then does on its own.** It solves. A boundary-value problem with
a new condition has a new unique solution, and the field adopts it, everywhere,
at once, because that is what uniqueness means. Nothing propagates outward from
the speaker; there is no travel time in a boundary condition. This is why the
page says the Edict *"takes effect the instant the sentence completes"* and why
the target *"cannot perform the forbidden act"* rather than finding it difficult:
a solution that does not exist is not a solution that is hard to reach.

The hold then costs nothing to maintain, for the same reason the screening
currents in a superconductor cost nothing: they are not doing work against
anything, they are the shape the state has. This is the mechanical content of
*"holds for as long as the caster's authority over that ground remains
uncontested"* — the Edict is not being sustained by the caster's attention. It is
being sustained by a **fact about standing**, and it ends when the fact does.

**Law V check.** The floor is **Stage VII, Refraction**, which is *exactly* the
Stage at which the material anchor stops being required (`The Four Crafts`, Law
V). And yet the page requires one anyway and requires it absolutely: **the Edict
is spoken.** *"Craft · Spellcraft (explicitly a spoken working)."* So the working
sits at the first Stage where a practitioner could dispense with voice and does
not dispense with it. **That is not an oversight; it is the whole design.** A
boundary condition must be *stated*, and a condition nobody heard stated is a
condition that cannot be appealed, countersigned, or lifted by a superior
authority. The Accord's most institutional working keeps its anchor for
institutional reasons. **The gate closes**, and the cheapest counter in the
register — *"Put a hand over his mouth"* — therefore works against a Stage VII
Edictum caster when it would not work against any other working at that Stage.

**Failure mode — Coherence fault, which is rare and is the right one.** *"The
practitioner's lived behaviour and their stated law have drifted apart, and the
Continuum reads the drift"* (`Two Sets of Books` §VII). The page calls it an
**Echo Reversal**: *"an Edictum spoken in anger, doubt, or bad faith backfires
onto the caster's own Wellspring rather than landing on the target."* That is
Coherence, exactly and by name, and it is the only Spellcraft page in this batch
whose primary fault is the slow one. The physics is consistent: the boundary
condition's *value* is read off the speaker's conviction, and a speaker whose
conviction does not match the sentence has declared a condition at the wrong
value. The solution that then exists uniquely is one nobody wanted, and it exists
at the speaker's own boundary, because that is where the mis-stated condition
actually sits.

A second fault: **Boundary fault at the edge of the named clause.** The page's
Limit says the working *"targets"* and does not reshape reality in general, but a
condition is imposed at a *surface*, and a surface has an extent that must be
specified. *"Binding a space or a domain-scale target"* is the same working with
a very large boundary, and the imprecision available at a large boundary is
correspondingly large.

**What the target sees and feels.** A person speaks a sentence at them, aloud, in
whatever room they are both standing in, naming them and naming a condition. And
then — nothing happens, which is the point. There is no impact, no pressure, no
compulsion from inside. Concord casework is exact about the sensation: *"being
told a wall is there"* rather than *"being pushed against one."* The target does
not want to do the forbidden thing any less. They go to do it and the doing is
not available: a sentenced target *"cannot perform the forbidden act"*, a
protected one *"cannot be touched by the named harm"*, a silenced one *"cannot
cast the named Craft"*, and each of those is an absence in the possibility space
rather than a resistance in the body. It holds *"until the Edict is lifted by the
same or greater authority that set it"* — so the target's condition is now a
matter of jurisdiction, and the only people who can help them are people who
outrank the speaker. And if the speaker was **out of their depth**, none of this
happens at all: the sentence completes, the room is silent, and the target is
simply not bound, which is the most humiliating thing that can occur in a Concord
courtroom and is one of the two ways this working ends a career.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** No practitioner has a card
(`SA-GAP-EDICTUM-NO-CARD`). At the Stage VII floor the Tier of Standing is
**5 · Expert**, η **0.60–0.70** (Part Nineteen as corrected by **R44-4**), so
**30 to 40 percent of the
casting expenditure leaves as heat, sound and structural bleed** — and since the
hold costs nothing thereafter, **all of the waste happens in the single second of
speaking.** A Stage VII Edictum at the 70,000 EU ceiling therefore dumps
**21,000 to 28,000 EU worth of bleed** into the room in the instant the sentence
closes, which is the largest instantaneous bleed of any working in this batch and
is a very good reason Edicts are pronounced in halls built for it rather than in
corridors.

**That third row is the finding.** Dougou is six Stages above this working's

reserve at Stage XIII, and Niran at Stage VII carries five times it. The Core
Vocabulary says the reserve *"Scales with Temperance Stage."* The cards say it
does not. Logged as **`SA-CROSS-EU-STAGE-LADDER`**, the largest number conflict
this batch has found, and deliberately not resolved.

**Does the physics close against the stratal account?** **Yes, unusually
cleanly, and the closure is the reason this page is the best-built entry in the
batch.** A boundary condition is cheap in joules and the joules it redirects are
the field's own; the hold is lossless and therefore free; and the stratal
account correspondingly bills a single sharp expenditure with **no upkeep** and
charges the real price in a non-reserve currency — clarity of soul, with Echo
Reversal as the penalty for misstating it. The two books say the same thing in
two languages. The one thing that does not close is the **cross-page EU ladder**
above, which is not this page's fault.

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Break the boundary — interrupt the chain, and this is the cheap one.**
  *"Below Refraction a working requires an external anchor: voice, hand, ink or
  blood. Take the anchor and the sentence does not complete. … Put a hand over
  his mouth."* Edictum sits **at** Refraction, where that rule would ordinarily
  stop applying — and the working keeps its voice anyway, by design, because a
  boundary condition must be stated to be appealable. **So the cheapest entry in
  the entire register works against the most institutional working in the
  corpus**, and it works because of a choice the Accord made rather than because
  of a limit the Codex imposes. A player who reads the Four Crafts and notices
  that Edictum voluntarily keeps an anchor it has outgrown has found something
  real.
- **Break the boundary — Lock, pre-placed.** The page's own Counter: *"a hard
  oath-nullification or a contradiction glyph placed **before** the Edict is
  spoken can pre-empt the seal entirely."* That is `[Lk]` **Lock** by the Index's
  definition — *"occupies a limit so no other limit can be set there"* — and note
  the timing requirement, which is doing all the work: **the counter must already
  be there.** Nothing stops a landed Edict. Preparation pays, and improvisation
  does not.
- **Break the man — outrank him, and the contest never happens.** *"A target
  genuinely greater than the caster's source of authority is simply not bound."*
  This is the register's *"Read him"* applied upstream: the counter is not a
  working at all, it is knowing the speaker's warrant and holding a better one.
  Note the adjacent trap in `Counterplay` II — *"Force the Seal early. … a
  warrant overstating standing seals anyway, **with the difference deducted from
  the speaker at the moment of closing.** A third party who can compel an early
  close is not defending. They are billing him."* Manoeuvring a Bench officer
  into pronouncing an Edict slightly beyond their warrant is a way of making them
  pay for it.
- **Deny the field — Fulguria's four-century-old kit, weakly.** *"Fulguria fails
  against boiled leather, fired clay, dense fog and a shielded interior."* This
  bites on the Judicium *discernment* — the speaker must correctly read where the
  limit sits — rather than on the hold, which is not a Fulguria phenomenon. A
  target in a shielded interior is a target the speaker may mis-locate, which
  converts a clean Edict into a Boundary fault. Partial, and the weakest of the
  four routes here.
- **Break the man — Coherence drift, and wait.** *"A practitioner whose lived
  behaviour has drifted from his stated law is degrading before anyone lays a
  hand on him. … You do not have to cause this. You have to notice it and
  wait."* Edictum reads the caster's conviction as its own authorisation. A
  judge who no longer believes his own bench is a judge whose next Edict is an
  Echo Reversal, and an opponent who knows it only has to be patient and be
  named.

**The tell, stated plainly.** Someone says your name and a condition, out loud,
in front of witnesses. That is the whole tell and it arrives before the effect
does. The thing worth knowing is what happens *next*: if you feel nothing and
can still act, the speaker has just told a room full of people that they do not
outrank you.

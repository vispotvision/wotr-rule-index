# System account · Fallacy

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** A region is put into a state that is not the
lowest one available to it, and is *genuinely stable there*. Every local test
returns the answer the state gives. Nothing downstream can tell it from the real
ground state. Then something nucleates, and the region falls the whole way down at
once, taking everything that had been built on the false floor with it.

**The real phenomenon.** **Metastability**, and its two exact quantities: the
**depth of the false minimum**, which decides how long the lie holds, and the
**nucleation barrier**, which decides what ends it.

*The false state is not a pretence.* A superheated liquid above its boiling point
is not *behaving* as though it were liquid; it **is** liquid, and it satisfies
every equation of state a liquid satisfies, because a local minimum in the free
energy is a real equilibrium against small perturbations. That is the entire
content of the page's hardest sentence, and the page is right: *"for the working's
duration, the false thing genuinely is true, in the narrow, load-bearing sense
that everything downstream of it behaves as though it were."* A structure resting
on a metastable phase is not resting on nothing. It is resting on something with
a real modulus, right up until the phase is gone.

*The indistinguishability is a theorem, not a trick.* A system in a local minimum
cannot detect that a deeper minimum exists by any measurement made inside its own
frame, because every local derivative is the derivative of a genuine equilibrium.
The false floor is found only by an **external, absolute comparison** — a
measurement against a standard that was not derived from the state under test.
This is the engineering commonplace that a systematic error is invisible to
internal consistency checks: a geodetic network built on a mis-sited benchmark
closes perfectly on itself and is wrong everywhere by the same amount, and no
amount of re-surveying the network finds it. The page's *"has no way to tell the
false clause from a real one until the structure built on top of it gives way"* is
that statement, and the counter follows from it rather than from the page.

*The collapse is self-driven and cannot be recalled.* Nucleation of the true phase
produces a boundary that expands because expansion lowers the free energy, so the
rate of collapse is set by the interface and not by whoever seeded it. A
supersaturated solution crashes out in seconds; superheated water flashes; in the
cosmological version the bubble wall runs at very nearly the speed of light and
the region inside it is not recoverable by any means available to the region
outside. **The author of a nucleation event is not in a position to stop it**,
which is the whole of the page's Phenomena tag: *"the self-sustaining collapse
once the working is triggered and has outrun its own Attraction Layer."*

**The governing law.** **The free-energy criterion applied to a local rather than
a global minimum**, with **classical nucleation theory** governing the induction
time: the decay rate goes as exp(−ΔG\*/kT), so the lie's lifetime is exponential
in the barrier and the barrier is what the working actually buys.

**Measurable quantities, SI.** The page states no Stage, no Grade, no cost and no
duration (`SA-GAP-FALLACY-NO-STAGE`). What the law supplies as checkable
consequence, and every one of these is a claim the page could be held to:

- **The lie has to be paid for, and the price is the height of the false floor.**
  This is where Fallacy differs from every other Limina working in the section and
  the difference is the most important number on the page that is not on the page.
  Scission is downhill: the separated state was already preferred and the rite pays
  only for a nucleation site. **Fallacy is uphill.** Putting a region into a
  metastable state means supplying the free-energy difference between the true
  minimum and the false one, and that difference scales with how much structure the
  axiom is load-bearing for. A false clause nobody reads is nearly free. A false
  clause a Wellspring path runs through is expensive in exact proportion to the
  path. **The cost of a Fallacy is therefore a function of its consequence, not of
  its content**, and nothing on the page says so
  (`SA-GAP-FALLACY-COST-SCALES-WITH-LOAD`).
- **Stability against small perturbations, instability against one specific
  probe.** A metastable state survives everything except a nucleation site, and a
  nucleation site is a very small, very specific thing — a scratch on the beaker, a
  dust grain, a single correctly-shaped defect. So the working is simultaneously
  extremely robust and trivially breakable by an opponent who knows *what shape* to
  bring. That asymmetry is the working's fairness, and again the page does not
  state it.
- **Lifetime is exponential in the barrier, so "brief" is not a constant.** The
  page speaks of *"the brief span in which the lie holds"* as though duration were
  a property of the working. It is a property of the barrier: a modest change in
  how well the false clause is insulated from a true comparison changes the
  lifetime by orders of magnitude. Superheated water in a clean smooth vessel holds
  for minutes; scratch the glass and it holds for no time at all.
- **The damage is not undone by the collapse.** Whatever crystallised while the
  false phase held is a real solid and stays where it formed. The page:
  *"the damage done while it was open does not repair itself along with the lie."*
  Correct, and for the ordinary reason — the products of a reaction run under false
  conditions are real products.

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** Two accounts, in opposite
directions, and the working sits between them. **Going up**, the practitioner
supplies the barrier and the offset: the work to raise a region into a false
minimum and to hold the region insulated from an absolute comparison. **Coming
down**, nothing is supplied at all — the collapse releases the offset the
practitioner paid on the way up *plus* the strain energy of everything that was
built against the false floor, which is why the shattering is larger than the
working and why it has an author who cannot recall it. `Two Sets of Books` §III —
*"The practitioner does not supply the joules. They supply the boundary
condition"* — is satisfied on the way down and **only partly satisfied on the way
up**, and that is the honest reading of a working the taxonomy calls Mechanica.

**Precedent we are not copying.** The lie-to-reality working is everywhere:
Code Geass's Geass as a command reality obeys, *Bakemonogatari*'s oddities as
rumour made real, the CRPG Illusion school where a disbelieved illusion vanishes.
All of those make the lie a matter of **belief**, held up by a mind and beaten by
a saving throw. This one has no believer in it anywhere. **Nothing is deceived; a
region is put in the wrong minimum**, and the way to beat it is metrology rather
than scepticism. The divergence that matters for scenes: the interesting hour is
not the lie and not the collapse but the **audit** — somebody standing in a
building that is behaving perfectly, holding an instrument that was calibrated
somewhere else, deciding whether to trust the instrument or the building.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** Draw is in two unequal parts and the page acknowledges only
one. The **sustain** is small: holding a local minimum costs the insulation, not
the offset, which is why the working can read as effortless from outside. The
**offset** is paid once, at the front, and scales with the load the clause bears
(§1). **Residue:** the page's strongest claim about aftermath is that it is
indiscriminate — *"a false axiom injected into a shared system corrupts what is
near it indiscriminately"* — which is a residue statement in everything but name,
and it is the reason the taxonomy's Statute XXVI applies: *"Mechanica
Irreversibility, exists because this is the one category that leaves scars the
Continuum doesn't forget"*
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Magical Categories — Unified Taxonom.md`:132).
**Saturation:** not the hazard here. The hazard is the opposite — a working whose
signature is *the absence of an anomaly* in a region that should be reporting one.

**Wellspring stratum.** **Nihiloth · *The Hollow Law***, Family **Limina**. Its
law, verbatim:

> **Analogue** · *"Destructive interference and the null field. A region genuinely
> empty of energy."*
> **Mechanism** · *"Two waves in antiphase do not conceal one another. They sum to
> zero, and the region between them carries no energy at all. Nihiloth builds
> Domains on that principle, which is why a Nihiloth Domain does not impose
> additional law. It cancels the law already present, and what remains inside is
> not a rule but the absence of one."*
> **Stat Effect** · *"Strengthens Dominion Sovereignty, Resilience Persistence,
> Gnosis Cognition. The practitioner imposes law through negation. Their Domain
> does not add rules. It removes them."*
> **Failure** · *"The cancellation is symmetric. Everything the practitioner
> brought inside is subject to the same null, including the parts of themselves
> they were relying on."*
> (`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Limina — Entropy, Void and Mind.md`:96)

**Nihiloth is the only one of the three that does the work the page needs, and it
does it exactly.** *"It cancels the law already present"* is how a true clause is
got out of the way so a false one can be filed in its place, and *"what remains
inside is not a rule but the absence of one"* is precisely the condition in which
a false axiom meets no competing statement. And the **Failure is the page's
missing cost, stated by the register**: the cancellation is symmetric, so
everything the practitioner brought inside the false clause is subject to the same
null — *"including the parts of themselves they were relying on."* A practitioner
who files a false axiom about a Wellspring path is standing next to a region where
the true statement about Wellspring paths has been cancelled, and their own path
runs through the same law. Nothing on the page carries this.

The two secondary currents are doing less well.

- **Dissolution** *(Limina)* — *"the mixed state has higher entropy than the
  separated one, and the process therefore runs on its own once initiated"* — is
  the right law for the **collapse**: a process that runs on its own once
  initiated is a self-sustaining shattering, and Dissolution's **Failure,
  *indiscriminate solvency***, is the page's *"corrupts what is near it
  indiscriminately"* arriving on schedule. This one fits.
- **Mirithane** *(Limina)* is the problem. Its law is *"A true mirror adds
  nothing. Angle in equals angle out, and fidelity is defined entirely by the
  absence of introduced distortion… so what leaves the practitioner is what was in
  them, with no flattering correction applied in transit"*, and its Failure is
  *"The mirror does not discriminate. A practitioner in a state they would rather
  not broadcast broadcasts it perfectly."* **That is the Wellspring of not lying.**
  Naming it as a component of a working whose entire operation is a false clause
  filed in true grammar is either a deep reading — the lie must be *perfectly
  formed*, a distortion-free rendering of a false statement, which is what makes it
  unreadable — or a mis-assignment. The page does not say which, and the difference
  decides whether a Mirithane practitioner is Fallacy's natural author or its
  natural enemy (`SA-CROSS-FALLACY-MIRITHANE-HONESTY`).

*Four Theories.* None, and the absence is the finding. The page's category is
**Mechanica**, whose definition is the deletion of the **Consent Doctrine**:
*"Mechanica coerces transformation without lawful Wellspring participation…
Conjunction's compatibility deleted, Glyphica's authorization skipped, Drafts
performed without consent"* (`Magical Categories`:131). A working filed there has
by construction no theory of consent, no correspondence and no debt — and the page
then names three Wellsprings as participating in it, which is the contradiction
logged at `SA-CROSS-FALLACY-MECHANICA-WELLSPRINGS` and the single largest thing
wrong with this entry.

**Essence stratum.** Active layers: the **Attraction Layer** of anything with a
path running near the clause, which is what *"paths deviate to accommodate a law
that, for the moment, is real"* describes, and — per Nihiloth's Failure — the
practitioner's own. Developmental Tier and Crystal State: `null`, because no Stage
is stated and both are read from Stage. **The invoice:** unstated in every
currency the system has. The page describes a working banned by formal orders,
capable of corrupting layered Animatria structures and of persuading a domain of
a falsehood about its own god, and names no price at all.

**The lens: the philosophy of "As If."** Hans Vaihinger, *Die Philosophie des Als
Ob* (1911). Vaihinger's whole subject is the **conscious fiction**: a proposition
known to be false, adopted deliberately because thought cannot get where it is
going without it, and distinguished from a hypothesis by exactly that — a
hypothesis asks to be verified, a fiction asks only to be *useful*. His examples
are the frictionless plane, the infinitesimal, the average man, the social
contract. And his rule for them is the sentence that makes him the right lens
here: a fiction is legitimate only if it is eventually **dropped** — the
scaffolding must come down, and a fiction that has hardened into a belief has
become a lie. That is the Fallacy page's own doctrine in a German philosopher's
hand: *"A Fallacy that never shatters has not finished its work."* The working is
Vaihinger's *Als Ob* performed on the Continuum instead of in a head, and the
guild registers that call it *revelation* have made Vaihinger's exact error in
reverse.

Where the lens misleads, and where it kills the school that holds it, is in **who
takes the scaffolding down.** Vaihinger's fiction is dropped by its user, at a
moment the user chooses, because the fiction lives in a mind and minds can let go
of things. A school reading Fallacy through *Als Ob* therefore builds its whole
practice around the planned withdrawal: state the fiction, do the work it makes
possible, dissolve it before it hardens, walk away. **The page says the withdrawal
is not theirs to schedule.** Phenomena, by the taxonomy's own definition, means
*"once triggered, Phenomena are no longer cast; they're lattice-position events on
the Plane of Fate that have outrun any Attraction Layer. This is why
Counter-Phenomena are so dangerous: the trigger has an author, but the Phenomenon
itself does not"* (`Magical Categories`:86). The physics agrees: a nucleation event
belongs to the interface. A school that has planned for the moment of its choosing
is standing inside the structure at the moment the barrier chooses, and what
Vaihinger promised them was that they would be the ones holding the scaffold when
it came down. Offered as a school's error, not as canon.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Ora]` **Truth** as the boundary that
moves, laid under `[Et]` **Mirage** for the indistinguishability that keeps it
standing. Both from the thirty unclassified forms. The Index's own reason applies:
*"Each names a kind of limit, applicable to any law at all"*, and what Fallacy
moves is a limit on **what the Continuum will accept as a correctly filed
statement** — which is a limit on truth in the filing sense rather than the moral
one, and is therefore the same glyph whether the false clause is about a
Wellspring, a Crystal's alignment or a running Draft. `[Et]` Mirage supplies the
second and necessary half: a false clause that can be told from a true one is not
a Fallacy, it is a mistake.
*Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling (`SA-GAP-GLYPH-MIRROR`).*

**What boundary moves.** Not the truth of anything. **The set of statements
against which a reader will compare.** A Wellspring path, a Soul Crystal's sense
of its own alignment and a running Draft all read the surrounding law and act on
what they find; the working moves the limit of that reading so that the false
clause falls inside it and the true one does not. Nihiloth supplies the half
nobody notices — *"it cancels the law already present"* — and what is filed in the
gap is filed in the same grammar as everything else in the cabinet.

**What the law then does on its own.** Everything. This is the part of the working
that has no author. Each reader does what it has always done with a correctly
filed statement: the path deviates to accommodate it, because accommodating filed
law is what a path is for; the Crystal re-reads its alignment against it, because
that is how a Crystal knows what it is; the Draft runs against it, because a Draft
runs against the law it finds. **Nobody is fooled and nobody consents.** Every
reader behaves correctly and the structure they build is sound, and it is sound on
a floor that is not the floor.

Then the barrier is crossed — by a true comparison arriving from outside, by the
clause's own load exceeding what the offset can insulate, or by nothing anybody
can name — and the region falls to the real minimum at the speed of the interface.
The page calls this the point of the working and it is right to: *"the brief span
in which the lie holds is the span in which everything that depended on the true
version can be made to depend on the false one instead, and the shattering is what
strands them there."* The paths that deviated are now deviated toward a clause
that no longer exists. That is what stranding is, and it is done by the collapse
rather than by the lie.

**Law V check.** **Cannot be run.** Law V is a Stage gate — *"Below Stage VII,
Refraction, a working requires voice, hand, ink or blood: an external anchor for
the boundary"* (`The Four Crafts`) — and the page states no Stage, so the account
cannot say whether this working needs an anchor or has one. The page says the
effect is *"spoken or otherwise sustained by active attention"*, which is an
anchor statement and would satisfy the gate below Refraction; it is also the
sentence that collides with the Phenomena tag, below. `SA-GAP-FALLACY-NO-STAGE`.

**Failure mode — Boundary fault, and it is symmetric.** *"The condition was
established imprecisely and the law ran somewhere unintended"* (`Two Sets of
Books` §VII). The page states the imprecision as a property of the working rather
than as an error: *"It also cannot be aimed with total precision: a false axiom
injected into a shared system corrupts what is near it indiscriminately."* A false
minimum is a property of a *region*, not of a clause, and everything sharing the
region shares the floor. The register's own sentence for this is Nihiloth's
Failure — *"The cancellation is symmetric. Everything the practitioner brought
inside is subject to the same null, including the parts of themselves they were
relying on"* — and it is the practitioner-facing half the page omits entirely. **A
Fallacy about a law the practitioner's own path runs through is a Fallacy about
the practitioner.**

A second fault, and it is the reason the orders ban this one specifically:
**Coherence fault on everything downstream.** *"Lived behaviour and stated law
drifted apart."* Every structure that accommodated the false clause has a lived
behaviour matched to a stated law that is about to stop existing. The page's claim
that Fallacy *"can corrupt Animatria structures layered over time"* is this: a
construct tethered through a region whose law has been cancelled and refilled is
a construct whose continuity statement is false, and it does not find out until
the clause goes.

**What the target sees and feels.** Nothing wrong, which is the working. A
Wellspring path that had been difficult runs easily, because the clause it was
fighting is no longer filed. An alignment the Crystal had been unsure of reads
clean. A Draft that should have refused authorises. The page's word for the
experience is the right one and it should be read as a warning rather than as
colour: *"it reads to witnesses as revelation rather than as trickery."* Everything
a person would use to check is inside the region and returns the region's answer.

Then the floor goes, in whatever is the interface's own time, and the experience
is not a lie being exposed but **a structure being deleted from under a correct
decision.** The path is deviated toward nothing. The alignment was read against a
statement that is not in the cabinet. What was built while the clause stood is
still there, still real, still load-bearing on a floor at a different height. The
page's *"stranded"* is precise and is worse than *"deceived"*: nobody was wrong
about anything at the time.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** `null`, and not for want of arithmetic.
Efficiency is read from the Tier of Standing, the Tier of Standing from the Stage,
and **the page states no Stage** (Part Five; Part Nineteen). No practitioner has a
card. So the one thing this account can say about the working's waste is that the
system provides no way to compute it, which for a Mechanica working the orders ban
is the wrong answer to have. `SA-GAP-FALLACY-NO-STAGE`,
`SA-GAP-FALLACY-NO-PRACTITIONER-NUMBERS`.

the page's two category tags agree with each other.

## 5 · Counterplay and the challenge

**The fairness check** (`.claude/skills/wotr-write/references/fair-play.md`).

| Test | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Costs something that hurts | **fail** | The page states no cost in any currency: no EU, no Stage, no Sub-Stat toll, no apparatus, no aftermath the practitioner pays. The two costs that *should* be here are both derivable and neither is written: the uphill offset that scales with the clause's load (§1), and Nihiloth's symmetric cancellation, which puts the practitioner's own path inside the null (§2). A working that can persuade a domain its god never existed and costs nothing on paper is the clearest fairness failure in this section. `SA-FAIR-FALLACY-NO-COST-NO-TELL`. |
| Stated limits | **partial pass** | Three real limits are stated and they are good ones: the lie *cannot be made permanent* (*"The shattering is built in, not an external correction"*), it *cannot be aimed* (*"corrupts what is near it indiscriminately"*), and everything it achieves is achieved inside the window. What is missing is the boundary a player needs: **no Stage floor, no range, no size of region, no number of clauses.** |
| Something beats it | **pass, though the page names nothing** | Four routes below, three of them decisive. The page itself offers no counter at all, which is why this reads *pass* on the system rather than on the entry. |
| It has a tell | **fail, as written, and the fix is on the page already** | *"it reads to witnesses as revelation rather than as trickery."* A working whose stated experience is that everything checks out has, as written, no tell — and it does not need one invented, because the physics supplies one the page has not noticed: a metastable region is **quieter than it should be**, reporting no anomaly where the cancelled law's absence ought to register. That is a tell for an instrument, not for an eye, and it is exactly the kind a player can look up. |
| Numbers in band | **cannot be run** | There are no numbers. Stage, Grade, cost, duration and efficiency are all absent, so no band can be tested. `SA-GAP-FALLACY-NO-STAGE`. |

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Break the boundary — Lock, which is what Lock is for.** *"Lock occupies a
  limit so no other limit can be set there."* Fallacy's whole operation is setting
  a limit at a site where a true statement used to be filed; a limit already
  occupied cannot be refiled. This is the textbook counter in the register and it
  is *preventive*: a Lock on the clause an opponent would most want to falsify is
  bought before contact, costs nothing during, and does not require knowing that a
  Fallacy is coming. **Knot** is the weaker sibling and still useful —
  *"Knot entangles a path so it cannot be traced cleanly"* — since a path that
  cannot be read cleanly cannot be told what to accommodate.
- **Break the boundary — force the Seal, and the bill is enormous.** *"The Seal
  closes an operation against the warrant that opened it, and a warrant overstating
  standing seals anyway, with the difference deducted from the speaker at the moment
  of closing. A third party who can compel an early close is not defending. They are
  billing him."* **A false axiom is a warrant overstating standing by
  construction** — that is the definition of the working — so the difference
  deducted at an early close is the entire offset between the false floor and the
  true one. This is the sharpest counter in the section: it does not require
  beating the working, only closing it, and the larger the lie the larger the
  deduction.
- **Deny the field — Silence, with a real question attached.** *"Lawful
  transformation cannot be authorised at all inside one."* A Fallacy inside a
  Silence should be impossible, and the page's Mechanica tag is the reason it might

**The tell, stated plainly.** Not on the page, and it is not an eye's tell. A
region running under a Fallacy is **too quiet**: a law that should be reporting
itself is reporting nothing, and paths that should be working against something
are not. Anyone inside is checking against the region's own answers and will find
nothing. The tell is available only to an instrument that was calibrated
elsewhere, and the moment a competent examiner brings one, the working has
minutes. That is the shape of a fair working and the page does not yet state it.

# System account · Imprinta

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** A material is held in a strong field until it
takes a set, and then the field is taken away — and the set stays, and goes on
affecting whatever comes near it, for as long as the material lasts.

**The real phenomenon.** **Remanence**, which is the page's own stated Physics
Domain and is exactly the right one.

*Remanent magnetisation.* Drive a ferromagnet around its hysteresis loop and
remove the applied field, and the magnetisation does not return to zero. It
returns to **B_r**, the remanence: a retained alignment with no field maintaining
it, because the domain walls have found a configuration they have no energy to
leave. A hard ferrite holds **B_r ≈ 0.4 T**; a sintered neodymium magnet holds
**B_r ≈ 1.3 T**, with a stored energy product of order **400 kJ/m³**. That energy
is not being supplied by anything. It was put in once and it is still there.

*Thermoremanent magnetisation.* Basalt cooling through its Curie point locks in
the direction of the field it cooled in and holds the record for geological time.
Anamnesis's own register names it: *"Basalt records the direction of the field it
cooled in and holds that record for geological time."* An oceanic magnetic stripe
is a physical memory of a planetary field that reversed millions of years before
anyone existed to read it, and **nothing connects the rock to the field that
wrote it.**

*Thermoluminescent dosimetry.* Fired ceramic accumulates electrons in lattice
traps in proportion to the radiation dose absorbed since its last firing, and
releases them as light when heated — which is both how a dosimeter badge works
and how a potsherd is dated. The register again: *"Objects genuinely store their
history in physically readable form. **Anamnesis is the instrument, not the
miracle.**"*

**The governing law.** **Hysteresis**: the present state of the medium depends
on the path it took rather than on present conditions, so a history can be
resident in a substrate with no ongoing cause. And its companion, **coercivity**
— **H_c**, the reverse field required to drive the remanence back to zero — which
is the physically correct form of *"what would it take to erase this."* For a
sintered NdFeB magnet H_c is of order **10⁶ A/m**, which is a very large field,
and that is the point: **a remanent state is not fragile. It is expensive to
remove.**

**Measurable quantities, SI.** The page gives EU, a Stage and a Grade, and no
physics. What the law supplies as checkable consequence:

- **A remanent record costs nothing per second and needs nothing.** No source,
  no connection, no upkeep. This is the defining property of the phenomenon the
  page has named, and it is the reason the page's *"permanent from the moment of
  casting"* is physically well-founded.
- **Erasure has a threshold, not a gradient.** You do not weaken a remanent
  state by half-measures; below H_c essentially nothing changes, and past it the
  record goes. This is a very good shape for a counter — findable, decisive, and
  requiring the counter-party to know how much is enough.
- **Heat erases what fields do not.** A thermoluminescent record is reset by
  firing; a thermoremanent one by reheating past the Curie point. Anamnesis's own
  Failure is exactly this: *"Thermal resetting. Reading is not always passive,
  and a careless practitioner erases the record in the act of taking it."*
  **The instrument that reads an Imprinta can destroy it**, which the page never
  mentions and which is the most interesting counter available to it.

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** In the write, once, and never
again. Magnetising a volume costs the work to sweep the hysteresis loop; the
retained state thereafter is a local free-energy minimum and is free. So the
page's cost structure is physically exactly right — *"not drawn from reserve on
an ongoing basis, but spent outright and permanently, the way a limb is not 'on
loan'"* — and it is the only working in this batch whose stated cost shape and
stated mechanism agree without interpretation.

**Precedent we are not copying.** Object-memory is standard: psychometry in
every urban fantasy, the Pensieve, the soul gem, the horcrux. Those are
containers. This is a **material property**: the medium is not holding a thing,
the medium *has been changed*, and what wakes later is the change rather than a
resident. The divergence is the page's own open question and it is a good one —
*"Why some Imprinta echoes wake on the first person who touches them and others
wait generations for a specific one"* — because a remanent state has no
preferences, which means the selectivity is not in the deposit and must be in the
trigger condition. The Anamnesis orders call it *"the deposit's own patience"*,
which is precisely the sort of thing a school says when it has attributed an
instrument's threshold to the instrument's character.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** Draw is a single write, at *"a moment of high emotional
charge"*, and then nothing at all. **Residue:** this working **is** residue, and
is the only entry in the batch of which that is true. The Core Vocabulary's
*"Aetheric Residue · Traces left behind by an Essence working. A permanent, if
often subtle, change to the location where the working occurred. Always readable
by someone patient enough"* is not a side effect of an Imprinta; it is the
product. A deliberate, structured, load-bearing residue. **Saturation:** not a
factor — a single brief write.

**Wellspring stratum.** **Anamnesis · *The Wellspring of Remembrance***, Family
**Limina**, Physics Domain entropy, void and mind. Its law, verbatim:

> **Analogue** · *"Remanent magnetisation and thermoluminescent dosimetry."*
> **Mechanism** · *"Basalt records the direction of the field it cooled in and
> holds that record for geological time. Fired ceramic accumulates trapped
> electrons in proportion to the radiation dose it has absorbed since its last
> firing, and releases them as light when heated. Objects genuinely store their
> history in physically readable form. **Anamnesis is the instrument, not the
> miracle.**"*
> **Stat Effect** · *"Strengthens Gnosis Retention, Gnosis Diagnosis in reading
> past trauma, Resilience Coherence."*
> **Failure** · *"Thermal resetting. Reading is not always passive, and a
> careless practitioner erases the record in the act of taking it."*
> (`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Limina — Entropy, Void and Mind.md`)

The page's Governing Primary — *"Gnosis (Retention, Analysis), secondary
Harmonics (Attunement)"* — reads straight off that Stat Effect line through the
Merge Ledger (Diagnosis → **Analysis**; `_method.md` §VI), so the FOW line is
correctly derived and needs no defence.

**Anamnesis is a reading Wellspring and this is a writing working**, which is
worth noticing. Every line of the register describes an instrument for
recovering a record that was laid down by ordinary physical process. Imprinta
uses the same Wellspring to **lay one down deliberately**, which is running the
register backwards — and the register's Failure, *thermal resetting*, is what
happens when the reading and the writing directions get confused. Nothing on
either page authorises the reversal and nothing forbids it
(`SA-GAP-IMPRINTA-READ-VERSUS-WRITE`).

*Four Theories.* **The Residue Position.** *"There is no answering at all. Every
Wellspring is the standing residue of something that already happened at Epoch
scale, and practitioners are moving through the wreckage of a working performed
before there were people"* (`Two Sets of Books` §IV). Maelor *"originates the rite
at the cosmological scale, seeding echoes across Gaia"* — which is the Residue
Position stated as a founding myth, and a practitioner of this working is
manufacturing, at personal scale, the exact kind of object the Residue Position
says the Sixty already are. The Archives has never formally engaged with the
Position. Two Prime Instructors hold it privately. An Imprinta specialist holds
it professionally, whatever they say in public.

**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Essence Core**, and uniquely in this
batch the Core is not spending — it is being **divided**. *"A piece of the
caster's own Essence Core, paid and detached at the moment of casting."* The Core
is the layer that *"holds identity, memory, and Traits"* and whose failure state
is *"Fracture into Shards"* (`Core Vocabulary` §III), and a working whose normal
operation is the deliberate detachment of a Core fragment is a working that
performs the Core's failure state on purpose, in miniature, every time.
Developmental Tier at the Stage IV floor: **Awakened** — *"Opened and circulating
Essence. Traits can begin forming"* (Part Twenty-One). This is the lowest
Developmental Tier of any working in the batch by two full steps. Crystal State:
not stated. **The invoice:** EU per the Numerical Effect line, **plus a permanent
subtraction from the Core**, which is the only irreversible personal cost stated
anywhere in this batch and is charged at the batch's lowest Stage floor.

**The lens: the talisman that draws down the star.** Marsilio Ficino, *De vita
coelitus comparanda* (*Three Books on Life*, Book III, 1489). Ficino's doctrine
is that the world is pervaded by *spiritus mundi*, a subtle medium between body
and soul, and that a material of the right complexion, worked at the right
astrological moment, **captures and fixes** a share of a celestial influence so
that the resulting image goes on radiating that influence afterward — a sunstone
cut under the Sun is not a symbol of the Sun, it is a piece of matter that has
been *made solar* and stays that way. This is the finest available description of
what an Imprinta is: a moment of charge, a receptive medium, and a fixed state
that outlives the making. Ficino is also the reason this account has the conflict
it has. **His talismans are not remanent. They are tethered.** The image
continues to work because it continues to *draw*, through correspondence, from
the star it was made under; the influence is a live current and the talisman is
an antenna. A school reading Imprinta through Ficino therefore knows exactly what
to do about a hostile echo: **find the source and cut it** — kill the maker,
break the focus, interpose a Silence. And that is, word for word, the counter the
page itself prescribes. The instrument-level objection is that **remanence has no
source to cut.** A magnetised bar is not connected to the magnet; the basalt is
not connected to the planet's old field; the fired ceramic is not connected to the
radiation. The page has named remanence as its mechanism and Ficino's counter as
its weakness, and those two cannot both be right. The correspondence school's
error is not an amusing footnote here. It appears to have been written into the
technique. See `SA-EM-IMPRINTA-REMANENCE-VERSUS-TETHER`.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Ir]` **Continuum** · Root · Maelor —
the station the page's own originator holds — laid under `[Frm]` **Form** ·
Extension · Thalen for the shape the deposit keeps. Both from the thirty
unclassified forms in the Master Glyph Index, and the Index's reason for the
blank is the reason they fit: a Root *"names a kind of limit, applicable to any
law at all"*, and an Imprinta conditions **whatever law the receiving medium
runs on**, which cannot be known before the medium is chosen.
*Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling (`SA-GAP-GLYPH-MIRROR`).*

**What boundary moves.** **The reachability of the medium's alternative
states.** Before the working the substrate — a relic, a room, a person — can
relax back out of any configuration imposed on it. The glyph pins the barrier
between the imposed configuration and the relaxed one high enough that the
imposed one is no longer leavable. That is one sentence and it is the whole of
the intervention.

**What the law then does on its own.** It stays. This is the entire mechanism
and its brevity is the point: a configuration that has no energetically available
route out is a configuration that persists without being maintained, and nothing
further is required of anyone. The domain walls do not remember being pushed.
They are simply where they are, and moving them costs more than is on offer.
*"The medium keeps the shape of what was pressed into it."*

The waking is the second half and is a **threshold**, not an awakening. A
remanent state is not inert — it has a field, and the field acts on whatever
enters it. What the page calls the deposit *"waking"* is the arrival of something
whose own state couples strongly enough to the retained one for the interaction
to become consequential: *"proximity, emotional resonance, a specific person or
words."* The selectivity the Anamnesis orders call *"the deposit's own
patience"* is therefore not patience at all but **a coupling condition**, and the
reason some echoes wake on the first hand that touches them and others wait
generations is that the threshold was set narrow or wide at the write. That is an
instrument-level answer to the page's open question, offered as a candidate
rather than asserted, and it has a consequence worth stating: **a narrowly-set
Imprinta is a lock with one key, and the key is a person.**

**Law V check.** The floor is **Stage IV, Flourishing** — the lowest in the
batch and three Stages below Refraction — so an external anchor is required
absolutely (`The Four Crafts`, Law V). The page supplies one, and it is the
medium itself: the working is performed *into* a specific object, place or
person, and a physical receiving substrate is a material anchor of the most
literal kind. **The gate closes cleanly**, and it is the only working in this
batch where the anchor and the target are the same thing.

**Failure mode — Structural fault, and it is permanent.** *"The Crystal could not
hold what the boundary permitted. Overchannel, Backlash, Fracture. The working
succeeds and the practitioner does not"* (`Two Sets of Books` §VII). Here the
Crystal does not fail to hold — it is **told to let go**, of a piece of the
Essence Core, permanently, at a Stage where the Developmental Tier is merely
**Awakened** and Traits are only beginning to form. The Core's own failure state
is *"Fracture into Shards"*. A practitioner who works this often is subtracting
from the layer that holds their identity, and the page's *"the way a limb is not
'on loan'"* is doing more work than it looks like it is doing.

A second fault, from the Wellspring and not from the page: **Boundary fault by
thermal resetting.** *"Reading is not always passive, and a careless practitioner
erases the record in the act of taking it."* An Imprinta read by an
insufficiently careful Anamnesis practitioner is an Imprinta destroyed, and since
the deposit is a permanent expenditure from somebody's Core, its destruction is
not recoverable by re-casting.

**What the target sees and feels.** For most of an Imprinta's existence, nothing
— which is its whole character. A room, a relic, a person carries a state nobody
can see and nothing is maintaining; the page's own word is *"inertly"*, and the
page is careful to say an uncharged deposit *"tends to remain a dormant,
undetected trace"* rather than a functioning anything. Then someone arrives whose
own state couples to the retained one — the right proximity, the right feeling,
the right person, the right words — and the deposit is suddenly doing something:
*"the deposit 'wakes' and begins interacting rather than merely persisting."*
What it then is depends entirely on what was written, because it is not a copy of
the caster and not a servant of the caster. It is **a fragment of somebody's
Essence Core with limited independent operation**, and its behaviour is the
behaviour of that fragment, which may not be behaviour the whole person would
have chosen. It persists *"independent of the caster's continued presence, will,
or survival"* — so the echo of a dead person can wake in front of their
great-grandchild and act on an intention that was current a century ago, and
nobody, including the fragment, is in a position to update it.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** No practitioner has a card
(`SA-GAP-IMPRINTA-NO-CARD`). At the Stage IV floor the Tier of Standing is
**4 · Adept**, η **≈ 0.45** — and Part Nineteen's own note on that row is the
relevant fact: *"The last tier at which more than half of every expenditure is
lost."* **55 percent of an Imprinta's cost leaves as heat, sound and structural
bleed**, the worst efficiency of any working in this batch, and the working is
permanent, so the waste is permanent too. Casting an Imprinta at the floor spends
roughly twice what arrives.

**The arithmetic, and what it turns up.** The reserve floor is small and the
comparison is still brutal. A ceiling-cost Imprinta needs **16,667 EU** to be
cast once from full. **Karo Venrik**, the only practitioner in the corpus with a
card at a Stage anywhere near this working's floor — **Stage V, Splintering,
Essence Capacity 1,600 EU** — cannot cast the cheapest working in the Spellcraft
batch. The floor cost alone, 8,000 EU, is **five times his entire reserve**; the
ceiling is **nine times**. He is one Stage *above* the working's stated floor.
His own card flags the figure as unverified — *"Essence Capacity 1,600 EU and
Flux Density 3.2 × 10² EU/g carry across from the legacy sheet. I have not been
able to source EU benchmarks by Stage and cannot confirm these are in range"* —
which is the same absence this batch logs as `SA-GAP-EU-FORMULA`, arriving from
the other direction. Together with the Edictum account's Dougou finding, this is
the second concrete instance of `SA-CROSS-EU-STAGE-LADDER`: **the stated costs of
workings and the stated reserves of practitioners are not on the same scale, at
either end of the ladder.**

**Does the physics close against the stratal account?** **Beautifully on the
cost, and not at all on the counter.**

*The cost closes, and it is the best-matched cost in the batch.* A remanent write
is paid once, at the write, and the retained state is free thereafter. The page
bills exactly that: a single EU expenditure plus a permanent Core subtraction,
explicitly *"not drawn from reserve on an ongoing basis."* Two sets of books,
same sentence, no interpretation needed.

*The counter does not close, and the failure is total.* The page's Physics Domain
is **remanence**, defined on the page itself as *"a substrate retaining an
imposed state **after the imposing field is withdrawn**."* The page's Weakness and
Counterplay both require a **live tether** back to the origin — *"sever that
tether (kill the original, break the focus, or place it inside an active Silence
field) and the echo stops being anything at all, the same failure mode Animatria
suffers."* A remanent state has no tether by definition; that is the definition.
An Animatria construct has one by definition — *"It maintains continuity through
a tether back to its source, **which is why Animatria summoning fails completely
inside Silence Realms**"* (`wiki/The Disciplines/The Spirit Summoning Arts.md`).
The page has chosen one mechanism and the other mechanism's counter, and only one
of them can be true. This is the batch's clearest `effect-mechanism` conflict and
it is logged, with four options, as `SA-EM-IMPRINTA-REMANENCE-VERSUS-TETHER`.

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Break the boundary — thermal resetting, which is the counter the physics
  actually supplies and no page states.** Anamnesis's own Failure: *"Reading is
  not always passive, and a careless practitioner erases the record in the act of
  taking it."* **The instrument that reads an Imprinta can destroy it.** This is
  the route that survives whichever way `SA-EM-IMPRINTA-REMANENCE-VERSUS-TETHER`
  is ruled, it requires no Stage advantage, and it is findable by anyone who reads
  the Limina register — which is precisely the kind of answer the challenge
  doctrine wants: a counter that is in the corpus, is not on the technique page,
  and rewards cross-reading.
- **Deny the field — Silence, *if* the tether reading wins.** *"Animatria
  constructs fail completely because their continuity runs back to a source the
  Silence has severed. **A soul held in one reports the sensation as loneliness
  rather than weakness.**"* The page prescribes this and it works only under the
  Animatria reading. Under the remanence reading a Silence Realm does nothing at
  all to a magnetised bar. **A player who tests this learns which mechanism is
  true**, which is a very good thing for a player to be able to find out by
  experiment.
- **Break the boundary — the substrate, which is the Runecraft answer applied to
  a Runecraft-shaped problem.** *"Wards. Runecraft standing on a substrate, and
  therefore beaten the way Runecraft is beaten: **damage the substrate.** A ward
  is not argued with. It is chiselled off, or the wall it sits on is brought
  down."* An Imprinta is not a ward, but it is a state *of a physical thing*, and
  every counter that reaches a state of a physical thing reaches it. Break the
  relic. This works under **both** readings and costs nothing but a hammer.
- **Break the man — never trigger it.** The deposit's selectivity is its own
  limitation: *"an imprint left with no emotional charge behind it tends to sit
  inert."* An echo waiting for a specific person, specific words or a specific
  feeling is an echo that a person who is not that person, does not say those
  words and does not feel that feeling can walk past. **The narrowest Imprinta is
  the easiest to avoid and the hardest to defuse**, which is a real trade the
  caster makes at the write.
- **Break the man — the caster paid in Core.** An Imprinta specialist has been
  subtracting permanently from the layer that holds their Traits. The register's
  *"Coherence drift"* and *"Rigidity"* both arrive early for such a person, and
  the Core Vocabulary names the symptom: *"**The Flicker** · Traits dropping in
  and out."* You do not have to cause this. You have to count how many echoes they
  have left behind.

**The tell, stated plainly.** There is none, and the fairness check says so
rather than pretending. The countertell is archival rather than perceptual: **an
Imprinta was made by somebody, at a moment, in a place**, and the Counterplay
register's *"Read him"* applies to the maker rather than to the deposit. A
practitioner who has laid echoes has a history of moments of high emotional
charge in specific locations, and that history is a document.

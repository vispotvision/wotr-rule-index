# System account · Resonant Divination

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** A network of things that are coupled to one
another is listened to as a whole, and the listener recovers which links are
strong, which are failing, and which are about to fall into step — without
touching any of the things and without any of them being asked.

**The real phenomenon.** Three, and the third is the one that makes the branch's
central claim honest.

*Ambient-noise interferometry.* Two seismometers a hundred kilometres apart,
recording nothing but the ocean's background rumble, can be cross-correlated to
recover the **Green's function between them** — the seismic response one would
measure if a source had been fired at one and recorded at the other. Nobody
fires anything. **The connection between two points is extracted from noise both
of them were sitting in anyway**, and the recovered function contains the
material properties of everything along the path. That is Bond Reading, in
method and in principle: not a probe, not a question, just a long enough listen
to two things at once.

*Ringdown and the decay constant.* A resonator that has stopped being driven
does not stop instantly. It decays as e^(−ωt/2Q), and the decay is measurable
for many cycles after the drive ends. **From a ringdown you can recover both
when the drive stopped and what the resonator was.** That is Resonant
Tracking — *"follows the fading frequency a severed bond leaves behind to locate
the disconnected party and, in some cases, when and how the bond was cut"* —
stated as an instrument reading rather than as a mystery.

*Synchronisation in a population of coupled oscillators — the Kuramoto
transition.* **This is the load-bearing physics of Convergence Forecasting and
the reason it is a probability rather than a prophecy.** A population of
oscillators with a spread of natural frequencies, coupled with strength K,
remains incoherent while K is below a critical value and spontaneously
phase-locks above it. The transition is continuous: the **order parameter**, the
degree of collective phase alignment, rises smoothly from zero as K approaches
K_c from below. So a population approaching synchrony is **measurably
approaching it, for a long time, before it arrives** — the order parameter is
climbing and can be read — and equally, a population near K_c can fail to
converge because finite-size fluctuations are large near a transition. Metronomes
on a shared board fall into step; fireflies in a tree do it; so do coupled power
grids, and when grids do it wrong it is called an inter-area oscillation and
takes out a continent.

*"Which presences are moving toward resonant convergence, and what happens when
they arrive at the same frequency"* is the order parameter, read as a trend. And
*"Free will, intervention, and the Continuum's own response to novel inputs can
all move the trajectory"* is the finite-size fluctuation term. The page's
disclaimer is not modesty. It is the correct statement of what the instrument
measures.

**The governing law.** **Cross-correlation recovery of a two-point response
function from a diffuse field** for the reading, **exponential ringdown** for
the tracking, and the **Kuramoto synchronisation transition** for the
forecasting. All three are statements about a population or a pair, never about
an individual, which is why the branch reads *relations* and cannot read a
person.

**Measurable quantities, SI.** The page states none — no range, no EU, no
duration, no lead time beyond *"days or weeks"* (`SA-GAP-DIVINATION-NO-NUMBERS`).
What the real law supplies as checkable consequence:

- **Reading time scales with the inverse of the connection's bandwidth.**
  Correlation from noise needs many independent realisations; a strong, narrow,
  long-lived bond emerges from the background in few, a weak or broadband one in
  very many. **The branch is fastest on exactly the bonds that matter most**,
  which is a real and unusual property and is why Fate Entanglement Analysis is
  described as reading *"louder and more complex"* than ordinary work.
- **Forecast lead time scales with how slowly the coupling is changing**, not
  with the Diviner's Stage. A convergence driven by something slow is visible
  weeks out; one driven by a decision taken that morning is not visible at all
  until the morning. **This is the single most exploitable property of the
  branch**, and nothing on the page says it.
- **Ringdown lifetime sets the tracking window.** A severed bond is traceable
  for roughly Q cycles of its own frequency, so high-Q bonds — oaths, blood,
  long recognition — stay traceable far longer than casual ones. A bond cut
  cleanly and long ago is below the floor; *"a connection deliberately masked,
  severed cleanly, or run through a Thick Veil zone"* is the page's own version
  of the same statement.

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** Nowhere. The branch is purely
receptive in all four of its diagnostic applications and does not deliver
anything to anything. The expensive resources are **integration time** and **a
quiet channel**, exactly as in the sibling branches. The one place energy enters
is the failure mode, and it enters from the wrong direction — see §3.

**Precedent we are not copying.** Divination is everywhere and is almost always
a plot device that returns a sentence. Fate/stay night's Clairvoyance, the Tarot
of a hundred CRPGs, the prophecy that the protagonist must misunderstand for two
acts. Those return **a future**. This returns **a graph with weights on it** —
who is connected to whom, how strongly, and which couplings are climbing — and
explicitly declines the future: *"a Diviner's report is a weather forecast on
causality, not a verdict."* The divergence is that a good Diviner is a **social
network analyst with a measurement error bar**, whose product is actionable
precisely because it is not certain, and whose profession is restricted not
because it is powerful but because it is *"too strategically valuable and its
potential for surveillance abuse too obvious"*
(`wiki/The Disciplines/The Harmonic Arts.md`). The branch is a surveillance
apparatus, and the register says so.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** Draw is negligible; the branch listens. **Residue:** a
reading leaves none on the subjects, which is the property that makes it a
surveillance instrument rather than an intrusion — and which is why *"the
Concord responded with increasingly strict regulation"* rather than with a
counter-working, because there is nothing to counter. **Saturation:** as with
both siblings, the limiting field quantity is not Aetheric Density but the
noise floor in the band being read, and here a second field condition is named
explicitly and is the only one in the Harmonic tree: **a Thick Veil zone**
*"where Harmonia perception is degraded."* That is the branch's attested terrain
counter and it is worth more than any number
(`SA-GAP-AMBIENT-RESONANCE-SCALE`).

**Wellspring stratum.** **None.** *"The discipline is a mode of listening laid
over the Sixty rather than a current drawn from one of them."* The relevant
laws are read, not drawn, and two are worth quoting at the exact line:

> **Transference · *The Living Exchange*** — *"Two oscillators tuned to the same
> frequency and held near each other exchange energy without a conductor between
> them, and the coupling strength falls off sharply with separation."*
> (`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Spatium — Spatial Geometry.md`)

This is the law that makes a *bond* a physically real coupled-oscillator pair
rather than a metaphor, and therefore the law that makes the branch's readings
mean anything. Resonant Divination is Transference's physics observed from
outside by someone who is not participating in it.

> **Benediction · *The Wellspring of Grace*** — *"The strength of a biological
> bond is a measurable physical quantity, and a low dissociation constant means
> the complex, once formed, spends almost all of its time formed. Benediction
> gives bonds on the Plane of Fate that same measurability. **This is the
> mechanical reason an oath sworn under Benediction carries system weight: it
> has an affinity figure, and the Continuum reads it.**"*
> (`Vitalia — Biochemistry.md`)

*"It has an affinity figure, and the Continuum reads it."* That sentence is the
whole licence for Bond Reading, and a reader who finds it understands why the
branch returns **strengths** rather than impressions. Benediction's failure is
also the branch's most dangerous blind spot: *"Competitive inhibition. A rival
ligand with higher affinity displaces the existing bond, and the displacement is
silent, complete, and not felt until the bond is called on."* **A displaced bond
reads at full strength right up until it is needed** — which means a Diviner
reading a betrayal in progress sees a healthy connection. Nothing on the page
says this. It falls out of the Wellspring register.

*Four Theories.* **The Correspondence Position**, held by the Conclave, and
**the Residue Position**, held quietly by the branch's better practitioners.
*"There is no answering at all. Every Wellspring is the standing residue of
something that already happened at Epoch scale"* (`Two Sets of Books` §IV).
A discipline whose whole method is reading what is left in a medium rather than
asking anything of it is Residue-shaped by construction, and Residue is the one
Theory *"the Archives has never formally engaged with."*

**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Attraction Layer**, and this branch is
the only one of the three that lives there. *"Attraction Force currents are
Plane-of-Fate phenomena that surface through the Veil's threshold"* — the
Diviner is reading the Fate-Plane layer of other people's Crystals, through the
Veil, with their own Attraction Layer as the receiver. This is the mechanical
reason the branch is **the most restricted of the three** and the reason its
failure mode is a *bond* rather than a burn. Developmental Tier at the Stage VIII
floor: **Radiant** — *"the Attraction Layer gains territorial durability"*, which
is the layer the branch runs on acquiring the durability to run on it (Part
Twenty-One). Crystal State: not stated for any named practitioner. **The
invoice:** unstated again, and here the omission is structural rather than
clerical: the branch's real price is not paid in Essence at all but in what
Izathen paid, which is **the receiver itself**.

**The lens: sympatheia, and Cicero's answer to it.** The Stoic doctrine that
the cosmos is a single continuous organism bound by *sympatheia*, so that a
disturbance anywhere is registered everywhere, and therefore that signs in one
place can be read for events in another. Posidonius built divination on it
directly, and Quintus Cicero states the case at length in *De divinatione* I:
the entrails, the flight of birds and the fall of lots are not causes but
**indices**, readings taken off a connected whole. It is a genuinely excellent
theory of this branch. It is also the theory that Marcus Cicero demolishes in
Book II, and the demolition is the part worth carrying: **a correlation
observed inside a connected system is not a mechanism, and a system connected
enough to make everything a sign of everything is a system in which nothing is
a sign of anything in particular.** Cicero's move is to demand that the diviner
state *which* coupling carries the inference and *how strongly*, and a doctrine
of universal sympathy cannot, because it has already asserted that all couplings
exist. A Conclave reader who works from Quintus and stops there will read
*every* line in the correlation as meaningful — and the branch's own failure
mode is precisely that: *"an attempt to read a connection locks onto the wrong
resonant line."* The instrument-level correction is Marcus's: report the
**weight**, not the connection, and treat an unweighted correlation as noise.
The branch's own best practice already does this — *"which connections are
strong, which are weakening"* — which is to say the discipline has quietly
adopted the sceptic's methodology while its patrons keep the Stoic's theology.
Offered as a school's error, not as canon.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Lh]` **Frequency** · Root · Selhar,
the Harmonic tree's shared Root, laid across `[Lei]` **Binding** — the Index's
own worked example of a narrow, deep Root, *"attested in two Wellsprings and one
Family … the whole of it belongs to Limina"* — for the *bond* the reading is
taken across. `[Lei]` sits outside the thirty mirrored forms and is quoted from
the Index's How-to-Read section rather than from the table, which is a weaker
citation than the rest of this batch uses and is flagged as such
(`SA-GAP-GLYPH-MIRROR`). *Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling.*

**What boundary moves.** **The integration window.** Nothing is imposed on the
subjects, on the bond, or on the Plane of Fate. The Diviner pins their own
receiver open across a band for long enough that a correlation which was always
present in the noise rises above it. Every other Spellcraft working in this
batch moves a limit somewhere in the world; this one moves a limit on **how long
the practitioner is willing to listen**, and that is the entire intervention.

**What the law then does on its own.** Correlation. Two presences coupled on the
Plane of Fate have been exchanging phase information continuously since the bond
formed — that is what Transference says a coupling *is* — and the exchange is
recorded in the Veil's ambient field the way ocean noise is recorded in the
crust. Held open long enough, the cross-correlation of the two signals
converges, and what it converges to **is** the response function of the link
between them: its strength, its damping, its phase. The Diviner does not deduce
the bond. The bond assembles itself out of the noise as the integration runs,
which is why a reading takes time and why the time cannot be bought with Stage.

Then each application is the same operation read differently. **Bond Reading**
is the converged function itself, for one subject against everyone: *"who they
are connected to, how strongly, through what kind of recognition, and whether the
bond is consonant or under strain."* **Fate Entanglement Analysis** is a
converged function with too much structure to be a single coupling — *"overtones
suggesting multiple Wellspring processes converging on one point in time"* — a
mode with contributions from several laws at once, which is what a
destiny-level convergence would have to look like in a spectrum. **Resonant
Tracking** is a ringdown: the drive stopped, the mode is still decaying, and the
decay carries both the when and the what. **Convergence Forecasting** is the
order parameter, sampled over days, extrapolated to the transition. **Dissonance
Warning** is the same quantity taken over an institution instead of a person,
which is why the Conclave sells it to the Concord and not to individuals.

**Law V check.** The floor is **Stage VIII, Transcendence** — a full Stage above
Refraction — so the working is internalised by default and **no anchor is
required** (`The Four Crafts`, Law V settled as a gate). The Sonochoral
Conclave's *"chant in tonal sequences"* is therefore **not** a Law V anchor: at
Stage VIII it is redundant, and the page is right to keep it as *"the Conclave's
working style"* rather than as a requirement. **The gate closes, and closes
loosely.** The practical consequence is worth stating: **a Diviner cannot be
interrupted by breaking a wrist or covering a mouth**, which removes the
cheapest entry in the entire Counterplay register from the list of things that
work against this branch.

**Failure mode — Boundary fault, of an unusual kind.** *"The condition was
established imprecisely and the law ran somewhere unintended."* Here: *"An
attempt to read a connection locks onto the wrong resonant line and **creates a
sympathetic bond** between the Diviner and something they did not intend to
contact"* (`The Harmonic Arts`). The physics of that is exact and is the reason
purely passive instruments are not purely safe. A receiver coupled strongly
enough to extract a signal is **part of the coupled system**; back-action is not
avoidable, only minimised. Push the coupling up to read a faint line and the
Diviner's own Attraction Layer becomes an oscillator in the network they are
measuring — at which point the reading is no longer of the network, and the
network now contains them.

Izathen the Broken Lyre is the same fault taken to its limit, and his case is a
**Structural fault** as well: *"he could not modify his own frequency without
simultaneously perceiving the modification through the same Crystal he was
modifying."* Receiver and transmitter in one loop with gain above unity
oscillates. It always oscillates; that is what the condition means. The Echo
Fracture of 8,000 AF is a feedback howl with a radius, and the reason he can no
longer hear anything but himself is that the loop never opened again.

**What the target sees and feels.** **Nothing, at any point, ever** — and this is
the branch's defining property and its whole ethical problem. A Bond Reading
takes nothing from the subject, leaves no residue on them, imposes no boundary
on them, and is not detectable by them. They are not touched, approached,
addressed or perceived; what is read is the *field between them and somebody
else*, which belongs to neither. What the Diviner then holds is the subject's
relational map with weights, a note of which bonds are under strain, the fading
trace of any bond recently cut and roughly who cut it, and a forecast of which
of the subject's entanglements are climbing toward a convergence the subject has
not noticed yet. The sole exception is the failure case: a Diviner who locks the
wrong line **has made a bond**, and a new bond is something the other end can
feel, which means the only way a subject learns they were read is that the
reading went wrong.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** η is unstated for every named figure
(`SA-GAP-DIVINATION-NO-CARD`). At the Stage VIII floor the Tier of Standing is
**6 · Master**, η **0.70–0.80** (Part Nineteen): **20 to 30 percent** of any
expenditure leaves as heat, sound and structural bleed. For a receptive working
the expenditure is small and the bleed is smaller — but note what it is bleeding
*into*. The waste of a Harmonic working is radiated tone, and this branch's
receiver is tuned to the Plane-of-Fate band. **A Diviner's own bleed is a signal
in the channel they are reading**, which is Izathen's loop in miniature and
running in every practitioner all the time, below the threshold at which it
matters. The Stage XII gate on Attraction Force resonance tracking is where that
margin stops being comfortable.

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Deny the field — the Thick Veil, which is the only attested terrain counter
  in the Harmonic tree.** *"A Thick Veil zone where Harmonia perception is
  degraded"* (page). Not a working, not a Stage contest, not a cost: a place. A
  commander who knows this moves the conversation that must not be read, and the
  register's own framing applies — *"Choose the ground by Family"*, except that
  this branch's terrain modifier is written on its own page rather than in the
  Family table.
- **Break the boundary — cut cleanly, or mask.** The page names both: *"deliberately
  masked, severed cleanly."* The register's form is **Knot** — *"entangles a path
  so it cannot be traced cleanly"* — and against a correlation instrument the
  physical content of a Knot is **decorrelation**: run the bond through enough
  intermediaries, or enough false ones, that the two-point function no longer
  converges. **Tenebra** supplies the Wellspring-level version, *"Strengthens …
  Harmonics Suppression"*, which is the register's name for falling below the
  noise floor.
- **Break the boundary — have nothing to read.** A correlation instrument on an
  uncoupled subject returns nothing, and the branch reads **relations**, never
  people. A courier with no bonds to the message's principals is genuinely
  invisible to Bond Reading in a way no amount of Stage can fix, which is the
  oldest and cheapest tradecraft answer to the oldest surveillance problem, and
  the system supports it exactly.
- **Break the man — feed the instrument.** Benediction's failure line is the
  weapon: *"Competitive inhibition. A rival ligand with higher affinity displaces
  the existing bond, and the displacement is silent, complete, and not felt until
  the bond is called on."* **A displaced bond still reads strong.** An opponent
  who knows a Diviner is reading them can leave a loyalty in place that has
  already been bought, and the Conclave's report will confirm the loyalty in
  writing. This is the cruellest available counter, it costs nothing, and it is
  findable only by cross-reading the Vitalia register against the branch — which
  is precisely the work the challenge is supposed to reward.
- **Break the man — the loop.** Izathen's fault is available to an opponent, not
  only to a careless practitioner. A subject who is themselves projecting into the
  band the Diviner is integrating over is a subject whose read has positive
  feedback in it, and the branch's own failure mode does the rest. This is
  dangerous to attempt and is the register's *"Rigidity"* move: the Diviner's law
  is *listen longer*, and listening longer is exactly what must not be done here.

# System account · Saba no Rosa

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** A standing structure is not cut. Its members
are taken out of the load path one at a time, and the remaining members take up
what they were carrying, until one of them is carrying more than it can and the
whole thing comes down without anything having struck it.

**The real phenomenon.** **Progressive collapse under load redistribution**, and
its governing design principle, the **alternative load path**.

*What it is.* On 16 May 1968 a gas explosion on the eighteenth floor of Ronan
Point, a twenty-two-storey system-built block in Canning Town, blew out a single
load-bearing precast wall panel. The panel was not large and the explosion was
not. But the floors above had nothing else to stand on, so they fell onto the
floor below, which had not been designed to take them, and the corner of the
building unzipped to the ground. Four people died. The modern requirement that a
structure must survive the notional removal of any one member — **alternative
load path design** — dates from the inquiry.

**That is this working, exactly.** *"It does not touch the body. It touches the
ledger of past holdings, honored relationships, paid debts, kept oaths, that a
present coordinate is standing on, and it dilutes the target's claim on those
holdings until the coordinate can no longer be sustained by them."* Nothing is
destroyed — *"The deposit is not destroyed. It simply stops answering to the
target"* — which is the precise condition of a member removed from a load path.
The panel at Ronan Point was still in existence. It was lying in the street.

*Why it is a sea of roses rather than a single cut.* Because finding the
load-bearing member is the hard part. A structure does not advertise which of its
elements are carrying and which are along for the ride, and the only way to find
out without a drawing is to test. **Raising a field across the whole ledger is a
survey**, and the page says so by describing the alternative: *"where Saba no
Rosa alone must raise a field across a target's whole ledger to find the
load-bearing deposit, the Shingan's Tengan or Kamigan branch can name the single
correct rose directly, turning what would otherwise be an apocalyptic working
into a surgical one."* With the drawing, one panel. Without it, the whole
building.

*And the thorn is the stress intensity.* Fractura's register is the Griffith
criterion: *"Nothing fails at its theoretical strength. Everything fails at a
pre-existing flaw, because stress concentrates at a crack tip in proportion to
the square root of the crack's length."* An unpaid debt is a pre-existing flaw in
a member that is otherwise sound, and the working's job is to load exactly that
one — *"Fractura (Vectoria, the Breaking Point) finds the existing seams in that
history and widens them without snapping the timeline."*

**The governing law.** **Static equilibrium and load redistribution**: the sum of
forces at every node is zero, so a load removed from one member appears in the
others, and failure occurs when redistribution drives any member past its
capacity. With the **Griffith criterion** deciding which member goes first.

**Measurable quantities, SI.** The page states no EU, no range, no duration, no
Stage and no Grade, and is honest that it is deriving rather than quoting:
*"Stage floor and Grade are not stated on the source card; the discipline reads
no lower than Stage VII Refraction (A-Grade)."* What the real law supplies as
checkable consequence:

- **Redundancy is the whole defence, and it is measurable.** A structure with
  *n* independent load paths survives the removal of *n* − 1 of them. A structure
  with one path survives none. So the question that decides whether a target
  survives this working is not how much standing they have but **how many
  independent things it rests on** — which nobody on the page asks.
- **The collapse is disproportionate and that is its name.** The engineering term
  of art is *disproportionate collapse*: a small local removal produces a total
  loss. This is why *"the target never sees the strike land"* and why *"From
  outside it reads as mercy"* — the observed event is out of all proportion to the
  observed cause, so observers look for a larger cause and do not find one.
- **Removal propagates at the speed of the redistribution, not the speed of the
  removal.** Ronan Point's corner fell in seconds after an explosion that lasted
  milliseconds. Saba no Rosa's *"expanded only as reserves allow"* is the survey
  phase; the failure, once the right member goes, is not on the practitioner's
  clock.
- **The scaling the page claims does not follow from the mechanism it names.**
  *"Kings, gods and old practitioners are the easiest targets, not the hardest,
  since the richer the ledger, the more roses there are to raise."* Under load
  redistribution a rich structure is a **more redundant** one and is therefore
  harder, not easier. The page's claim is true only if a long ledger's deposits
  are **serially dependent** — each standing on the one before — rather than
  parallel. That is a real and interesting proposition about how standing works
  in this world, and it is not stated anywhere. `SA-PHYS-SABA-REDUNDANCY-SCALING`.

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** In the structure, held as
potential, for as long as the structure has been standing. Nothing is delivered
and nothing is broken: a member is unloaded and gravity does the rest. This is
`Two Sets of Books` §III with the cheapest possible boundary condition and the
largest possible consequence — *"The energy was in the room. The structure that
let it move in that particular direction was in the person, and structure is what
fails."* That sentence was written about practitioners and it is a literal
description of this working.

**Precedent we are not copying.** Erasure powers are common — Bleach's
Kyōka Suigetsu, the Death Note, the villain who unmakes your past. Those take
the past away. **This one leaves the past entirely intact and takes away only
your present claim on it**, which is a distinction the page defends twice:
*"The past remains true in the cosmos; only the target's present claim on it is
diluted."* And the second divergence is the ethics, which are mechanical rather
than moral: **Oblation pays from the practitioner's own reserve**, so there is no
theft, *"so no appeal to the cosmos answers it; there is no theft to accuse the
practitioner of."* A working whose lawfulness is the reason it cannot be appealed
is a much colder thing than a curse.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** Draw is sustained and scales with the field — *"the sea can
be raised small and expanded only as reserves allow"* — which makes this one of
only two workings in the section whose cost is explicitly a function of how much
of it you choose to do. **Residue:** heavy and structural. Monolithion
*"ratifies the withdrawal as a standing structural fact the target's own timeline
cannot quietly refile"*, so the residue is the withdrawal itself, permanent and
on the record. **Saturation:** a large field raised over a target's whole ledger
is a large sustained expression in one place; the Core Vocabulary's saturation
danger is unaddressed here as everywhere in this section.

**Wellspring stratum.** Six, across five Families, and the page's division of
labour is the most careful in the batch.

> **Anamnesis · *The Wellspring of Remembrance*** *(Limina)* — *"Objects
> genuinely store their history in physically readable form. **Anamnesis is the
> instrument, not the miracle.**"* Reads the ledger, *"as a physically stored
> record rather than a summoned memory"*, which is the page's own careful
> phrasing and is the Wellspring's own.
> **Fractura · *The Breaking Point*** *(Vectoria)* — *"stress concentrates at a
> crack tip in proportion to the square root of the crack's length … Fractura
> gives the practitioner sight of those flaws and the precision to load exactly
> one of them."* Failure: *"**Self-inspection.** The Wellspring does not exempt
> its bearer, and a Fractura practitioner under strain reads their own flaw
> population with perfect clarity at the worst possible moment."*
> **Contrition · *The Broken Flame*** *(Vitalia)* — *"A pathogen survived once is
> answered faster and harder the second time because the response was recorded …
> The mechanism requires the failure to have been survived, and it requires it to
> have been genuine, because a rehearsed one produces no antibody."* Surfaces the
> unpaid weight. Failure: *"Autoimmunity. The record generalises past its target
> and the practitioner begins answering their own tissue as though it were the
> original insult."*
> **Mortalis · *The Black Gate*** *(Vitalia)* — *"A living body is a structure
> held far from equilibrium by continuous expenditure. Death is not an event added
> to that structure; it is the cessation of the expenditure."* Governs the
> threshold across which answering is withdrawn. Failure: *"Equilibration.
> Sustained proximity to the boundary reduces the gradient the practitioner is
> themselves maintaining, and they cool toward it without noticing."*
> **Monolithion · *The Enduring Monument*** *(Materia)* — ratifies. Failure:
> *"Brittle at scale."*
> **Oblation · *The Offering Flame*** *(Fluxia)* — *"Fluid accelerating through a
> narrowing does not gain energy. It trades pressure for velocity, and the sum
> across the streamline is conserved. Oblation formalises soul-cost as exactly
> that trade."* Failure: *"Cavitation."*
> (Registers: `Limina`, `Vectoria`, `Vitalia`, `Materia`, `Fluxia`, under
> `wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/`.)

**Two of those Failures are the page's own stated counter, arriving from the
register.** Fractura's *self-inspection* — the Wellspring does not exempt its
bearer — is the mechanism behind *"the ledger the gardener stands on dilutes
beneath his own feet"*, and it fires *"under strain"*, which is precisely the
condition of a practitioner making wrongful attempts. And Mortalis's
*equilibration* — the practitioner cools toward the boundary they are working at,
*"without noticing"* — is a slow, unstated, career-length version of the same
thing. Contrition's *autoimmunity* is the third and the page does not carry it
either.

*Four Theories.* **The Debt Position**, and this page is its clearest case in the
corpus. *"The Wellspring answers on credit and the account is settled somewhere
the practitioner cannot see. Held mostly in the eastern quarter and by the older
rendering families, and impossible to test, and **disturbingly good at predicting
which practitioners come apart.**"* A discipline whose subject is unpaid debt,
whose payment must come from the practitioner's own reserve, and whose
punishment for wrongful use is the practitioner's own ledger diluting, is not a
Consent working with a Debt gloss. It is the Debt Position with a name.

**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Attraction Layer** throughout. The
ledger of *"past holdings, honored relationships, paid debts, kept oaths"* is the
Plane-of-Fate record — *"bonds, oaths, grudges, every act of recognition runs as
a current here"* (`Core Vocabulary` §I) — and a present coordinate sustained by
them is an Attraction-Layer claim. The **Essence Core** pays, through Oblation.
The **Aether Shell** does nothing, which is why the page can say *"It arrives
without a blade or a wound."* Developmental Tier at the page's derived Stage VII
floor: **Resonant** (Part Twenty-One). Crystal State for Muken: **Refined**
(card). **The invoice:** *"every rose is paid from the practitioner's own
Oblation reserve"* — a real, stated, scaling cost, in a currency the system has
(`EU`), with **no figure attached** (`SA-GAP-SABA-NO-NUMBERS`).

**The lens: the fragment of Anaximander.** DK 12 B1, the oldest surviving
sentence of Western philosophy, preserved by Simplicius: *"The things that exist
perish into the things out of which they come to be, according to necessity; for
they pay penalty and retribution to one another for their injustice, according to
the ordering of time."* Existence is an encroachment. To be a thing at all is to
have taken up room that was not yours, and the *apeiron* recovers it —
not vindictively, not by judgement, but **according to the ordering of time**,
because that is what the ordering of time does. This is Saba no Rosa's
cosmology stated twenty-six centuries early: a target's present coordinate is
standing on deposits it has not finished paying for, and the working does not
punish and does not accuse. It simply calls the ordering of time forward.
*"Contrition surfaces only what was already unpaid, so no appeal to the cosmos
answers it."* Where the lens misleads is on **exemption**. On Anaximander's
account there is no such thing as a clean account: injustice is coextensive with
existing, and everything pays. A school reading the discipline that way concludes
that nobody can be defended and that the only available response to a gardener is
flight or endurance — and therefore never builds, or even looks for, the defence
the page states plainly: *"a target whose present coordinate is paid for
honestly, by debts already settled and oaths already kept, gives the sea nothing
to work with."* Worse, the doctrine's own confidence that everyone owes will make
its practitioners careless about whether a given target actually does, and the
page is exact about the price of that carelessness: *"the reserve spent trying
anyway is spent for nothing, until enough wrongful attempts exhaust the
practitioner's own Oblation reserve at the threshold and the ledger the gardener
stands on dilutes beneath his own feet."* **The school that believes nobody is
innocent is the school that destroys itself on the innocent.** Offered as a
school's error, not as canon.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Me]` **Measure** · Synonym · Urion
for the survey, laid under `[Rt]` **Return** · Synonym · Auren for the
withdrawal — what was lent is called in. Both from the thirty unclassified forms.
The Index's caution governs and is worth restating for a working this large:
*"Assignment is decided after the phenomenon, never before, and it is a filing
decision rather than a generative one."*
*Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling (`SA-GAP-GLYPH-MIRROR`).*

**What boundary moves.** **Whether a past deposit is obliged to answer to its
present holder.** Not whether it happened; not whether it was theirs; not whether
anyone else may have it. One relation — *answers to* — is suspended, deposit by
deposit, across a field.

**What the law then does on its own.** It redistributes. A present coordinate is
a node with a set of members running into it from the past, and the loads at a
node must balance. Take one member out of the path and its share appears in the
others, immediately, without anyone moving it. Take enough out, or take the right
one, and some remaining member is carrying a load it was never built for, and
that member fails — at its own pre-existing flaw, per Fractura, in proportion to
the square root of the flaw's length. And then **the failure propagates**,
because the members that were standing on *that* member now have nothing under
them either.

This is why the page's description is a sea rather than a cut, and why the
description is of *blooms*: each rose is a member being surveyed for whether it
is carrying, and each thorn is the flaw that will be loaded if it is. *"Each
bloom is a single load-bearing deposit drawn up out of the target's own history
and each thorn is the unpaid debt attached to it."*

The paying is the part that makes it lawful rather than theft, and it is
Bernoulli's: **Oblation's total is conserved across the exchange.** The
practitioner surrenders pressure from his own reserve and velocity appears in the
working; the sum is not increased by the sacrifice; nothing has been taken from
the target that the practitioner did not fund. *"Oblation supplies the medium,
drawn from the practitioner's own reserves rather than the target's, so the
exchange is paid rather than stolen."* And the page is right that this is
Oblation's ordinary law rather than a house rule: *"Practitioners who understand
this stop describing the Wellspring as generosity."*

**Law V check.** The page derives its own floor — **Stage VII, Refraction** —
and derives it from the working's shape rather than from a source: *"the
discipline reads no lower than Stage VII Refraction (A-Grade), the Stage at which
a Domain seed first forms, since the discipline requires holding an environmental
field open on borrowed structure rather than discharging a single strike."* At
Refraction the material anchor is no longer required (`The Four Crafts`, Law V),
and the page gives none — the field is held by attention: *"Spellcraft, sustained
by attention, which is why the sea can be raised small and expanded only as
reserves allow, and why it lapses the moment the practitioner is killed or
Silenced."* **The gate closes on the page's own derivation**, which is honest,
and the derivation is sound: an environmental field held open on borrowed
structure is Domain-adjacent work and Domain seeds start at Refraction.

**Failure mode — Boundary fault, and the page's own counter is it.** *"The
condition was established imprecisely and the law ran somewhere unintended"*
(`Two Sets of Books` §VII). Here the condition is *this deposit is unpaid*, and a
deposit that is **not** unpaid is a condition established falsely. The page:
*"a practitioner who reaches for the discipline against someone whose only fault
was being remembered finds the sea has nothing lawful to draw on, and the reserve
spent trying anyway is spent for nothing."* The law does not run at all; the
payment still leaves. And then the second-order fault, which is the register's:
Fractura's **self-inspection** fires under strain, and a practitioner burning his
Oblation reserve against a clean target is a practitioner under strain reading
his own flaw population with perfect clarity at the worst possible moment.
*"The ledger the gardener stands on dilutes beneath his own feet."*

A third fault the page does not carry: **drawing the medium from the wrong
place.** *"A practitioner who tries to draw the medium from the target or the
surrounding field instead finds the sea turning brackish and the thorns turning
inward on the next exchange."* Under Oblation that is **cavitation** — drop the
pressure term too far and the fluid boils at ambient temperature, and the
collapsing voids pit the conduit they formed in. Brackish water and inward thorns
is a pitted conduit described from inside it.

**What the target sees and feels.** Roses. A field of them, rising around them,
each one a thing they actually did — a debt honoured, an oath kept, a
relationship that held — and a thorn on every stem. Nothing touches them. There
is no blade, no wound, no impact, no pressure: *"It arrives without a blade or a
wound, and the target never sees the strike land. From outside it reads as
mercy."* What happens instead is that things stop working. A claim that has
always been honoured is not honoured. A standing that has always been theirs is
not theirs. Nothing was taken away — *"The past remains true in the cosmos"* — and
they can name every deposit and every one is still true and **none of them answer
any more.** And there is nobody to appeal to, because Oblation paid for all of it
from the gardener's own reserve, so no law was broken and no theft occurred and
the cosmos has no objection to register. Then their present coordinate, which was
standing on all of that, stops being sustainable, and it does not fail slowly.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** Taking Muken's card, η **0.84**:
**16 percent of every expenditure leaves as heat, sound and structural bleed**,
and for a sustained environmental field that is a continuous radiated loss for as
long as the sea is held. At the page's derived Stage VII floor the Tier of
Standing would be **5 · Expert**, η **0.50–0.60** (Part Nineteen), giving
**40–50 percent** — which for a working with no stated cost and a field that
*"can be raised small and expanded only as reserves allow"* means the practical
size of the sea is set almost entirely by the practitioner's efficiency rather
than by their reserve. A Stage VII gardener wastes nearly half of every rose. A
Stage X one wastes a sixth. **The discipline is three times more usable at
Master tier than at its own floor**, which is a real consequence of the numbers
and is not stated anywhere.

**The arithmetic, and what it turns up.** There is almost none to do, and the
absence is itself the finding.

**This is the only working in the Spellcraft section with a fully stated
practitioner — Stage, reserve, Flux Density, η, AU/s, Crystal State, Path
weighting, all on one card — and no cost at all.** Muken's 1,850,000 EU reserve
sits there, computable, and there is nothing to divide it by. Against Mugen no
Hatsurugi the same card yields *eighteen to thirty-seven arrivals*; against Saba
no Rosa it yields nothing, because the page states a cost *shape* and no
magnitude. The one derivable consequence is the efficiency point in §3: at the
page's own derived floor the practitioner wastes 40–50 percent of every rose,
against 16 percent at Muken's Stage, so **the size of sea a given practitioner can
raise varies by a factor of about three across the Stages the working is
available at** — which makes the discipline's stated scaling (*"expanded only as
reserves allow"*) a statement about η at least as much as about reserve.

**Does the physics close against the stratal account?** **The mechanism closes
and one stated rule does not follow from it.**

Load redistribution closes beautifully: nothing is delivered, nothing is
destroyed, the energy was already in the structure as potential, and the
practitioner supplies only the withdrawal — which is `Two Sets of Books` §III
almost verbatim. Oblation's conservation closes the payment: the total across the
streamline is not increased by the sacrifice, so *"the exchange is paid rather
than stolen"* is a thermodynamic statement and not an ethical one.

What does not follow is the scaling. *"Kings, gods and old practitioners are the
easiest targets, not the hardest, since the richer the ledger, the more roses
there are to raise."* Under the working's own mechanism, more members means more
**redundancy**, and redundancy is the whole defence against progressive collapse.
The claim holds only if a long ledger is a **chain** rather than a **truss** —
each deposit standing on the one before — and nothing states that. It is a good
proposition and it should be ruled rather than assumed, because it decides
whether the discipline is a king-killer or a king-proof.

## 5 · Counterplay and the challenge

**The fairness check** (`.claude/skills/wotr-write/references/fair-play.md`).

| Test | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Costs something that hurts | **pass on structure, fail on magnitude** | The cost is the right *shape* and it is unusually well conceived: **every rose is paid from the practitioner's own Oblation reserve**, it scales with the size of the field, and a wrongful attempt is paid for and returns nothing. That last clause is the best-designed cost in the section — **the discipline bills you for being wrong**. But no EU figure exists at any scale, against a practitioner whose reserve is fully stated, so there is no point at which the sea cannot be expanded. `SA-GAP-SABA-NO-NUMBERS`. |
| Stated limits | **pass, and the limits are real** | *"It cannot erase a deposit outright"*; *"Mortalis's threshold only ever runs one direction at a time, so the practitioner cannot simply reverse a withdrawal once ratified by Monolithion"* — **the working is not undoable by its own author**; it lapses the moment the practitioner is killed or Silenced; and it cannot be paid for out of anything but the practitioner's own reserve. |
| Something beats it | **pass, and the primary counter is a way of living rather than a working** | Below. Settle your debts. |
| It has a tell | **pass, overwhelmingly** | A sea of roses and thorns rises around you. Of all twenty workings in this section this is the least deniable and the most beautiful, and the page knows it: *"From outside it reads as mercy."* |
| Numbers in band | **cannot be checked** | No EU, no range, no duration, and the Stage and Grade are **derived on the page and declared as derived**, which is the correct way to handle an absence and leaves nothing to check against Part Five. |

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Break the boundary — pay your debts, and there is nothing to raise.** The
  page's own counter and it is absolute: *"a target whose present coordinate is
  paid for honestly, by debts already settled and oaths already kept, gives the sea
  nothing to work with."* Contrition surfaces *only what was already unpaid*, so
  a clean ledger presents no flaw for Fractura to load. This is the register's
  **"the vow"** run in the practitioner's favour rather than against them — *"A
  vow kept buys Coherence with conduct"* — and it is a counter bought over years,
  by conduct, in advance of ever meeting the man. **Preparation pays, and here the
  preparation is a life.**
- **Break the boundary — build a truss, not a chain.** Derived from the
  mechanism rather than stated: a coordinate standing on **independent** claims
  survives the withdrawal of any one of them, and a coordinate standing on a
  single chain of dependencies does not. A practitioner whose standing rests on
  one lineage, one oath and one patron is Ronan Point. One whose standing rests on
  four unrelated things is a modern building. This is the counter a reader gets
  for understanding what the working actually does, and it is the reason
  `SA-PHYS-SABA-REDUNDANCY-SCALING` matters rather than being a pedantry.
- **Deny the field — Silence, and it is decisive.** *"It lapses the moment the
  practitioner is killed or Silenced"* (page), and the register: *"Silence. The
  removal of magical possibility from a region … Lawful transformation cannot be
  authorised at all inside one."* A sustained environmental field held by
  attention is exactly what a Silence Realm removes.
- **Break the man — make him wrong.** *"The reserve spent trying anyway is spent
  for nothing, until enough wrongful attempts exhaust the practitioner's own
  Oblation reserve at the threshold and the ledger the gardener stands on dilutes
  beneath his own feet."* A target who **appears** to owe and does not is a trap
  that costs the gardener reserve and eventually costs him his own standing. Add
  Fractura's register Failure — *"The Wellspring does not exempt its bearer, and a
  Fractura practitioner under strain reads their own flaw population with perfect
  clarity at the worst possible moment"* — and the trap has a second jaw.
- **Break the man — the teaching restriction is a tell about the man.** *"Bara
  Moto passed the Work to Muken once … and warned him against teaching it further
  to anyone who had not first learned to grieve correctly."* A discipline
  conditioned on having grieved correctly is a discipline whose practitioner has a
  specific, known, unhealed shape, which is `Counterplay` IV's **Rigidity**: *"This
  is the cruellest counter in the register and it requires knowing the man rather
  than the technique."*

**The tell, stated plainly.** Roses. Rising around you, each one something you
actually did, each one with a thorn on it. You will see it from a long way off
and it will not look like an attack, and by the time it stops looking like mercy

# System account · Thaumic Harmonics

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** A machine that is running is listened to, its
weakest member is identified from the sound alone, and a very small correctly
timed push is applied to that member until the machine stops being a machine.

**The real phenomenon.** Three, in sequence, and the third is the one that makes
the discipline dangerous.

*Vibration-based condition monitoring.* A running assembly radiates a spectrum
with a line for every rotating and reciprocating member, at frequencies fixed by
geometry: shaft rate, blade-pass, gear-mesh, bearing defect frequencies
computable from race diameters and ball count before the bearing has failed.
A maintenance engineer with an accelerometer reads an assembly's parts list off
its noise, and names the failing one, without opening the housing. This is the
page's diagnostic half verbatim: *"a trained Thaumic Harmonist can read from
tone alone what formal analysis would need hours of instrumentation to
measure."*

*Modal weak-point identification.* Every structure has a lowest-margin mode —
the one whose damping is smallest, whose stress concentration is highest, or
whose frequency sits nearest a driving source. Finding it is the whole business
of a modal survey, and the answer is always specific: *this* member, *this*
frequency. The page's *"harmonic weak points"* is not a figure of speech. It is
the standard term for the thing.

*Resonant amplification, and the Q factor.* **This is the load-bearing physics
of the entire branch and the reason its central claim is true rather than
flattering.** A resonator driven at its natural frequency and in phase
accumulates energy over cycles, and the steady-state amplitude reached is **Q
times** the amplitude the same force would produce applied statically. Q is a
property of the resonator and not of the driver. So a driving force of one
part in Q, applied at the right frequency and in the right phase, arrives at
parity with the structure's own motion — and a driving force in **antiphase** at
one part in Q cancels it over the same number of cycles.

That is exactly the page's claim: *"The practitioner does not need to overpower
the working; they need to find the one frequency that collapses its whole tonal
structure."* It is not a genre convenience. It is what Q means.

**The governing law.** **Forced harmonic oscillation and resonant response**,
with the steady-state amplification factor Q = f₀/Δf, and **superposition**,
which is what permits a small antiphase injection to sum against a large signal
without needing to overcome it. For the constructive half, **coherent
addition**: N sources radiating in phase produce N times the amplitude and
therefore **N² times the intensity** of one, which is why the Cantic Order's
method returns more than the sum of its members.

**Measurable quantities, SI.** The page states no EU, no range, no duration and
no frequency (`SA-GAP-THAUMIC-NO-NUMBERS`). What the real law supplies, as
checkable consequence:

- **Time to lock is set by Q, not by power.** A counter-injection at amplitude
  ratio r relative to the target working reaches cancellation in roughly
  **1/r cycles**, capped at Q cycles. A working with Q ≈ 1,000 — the order of a
  good bell — takes on the order of a thousand of its own cycles to be brought
  down by a counter one-thousandth its size, and **ten** cycles by one a tenth
  its size. This is the number that decides whether Interference Lock finishes
  before the casting does, and it is a *quality* of the target rather than a
  strength contest.
- **High-Q workings are the vulnerable ones.** A sharply tuned, elegantly
  economical working is a high-Q resonator and is therefore the easiest thing in
  the register to collapse with a whisper. A sloppy, broadband, low-Q working
  has no sharp line to grip and must be answered with force. **The discipline is
  worst against bad casters**, which is a genuinely unusual property and the
  most interesting thing about it.
- **The Cantic Order's arithmetic.** Ten supporters in phase around one primary
  give **10× amplitude, 100× intensity** relative to a single voice. The
  register's own claim — output *"exceeding their individual capacity"* — is the
  N² law and nothing more exotic.
- Aurevia's operational figure, the only number attached to the branch anywhere:
  **read, analyse and counter in under two seconds**
  (`wiki/The Disciplines/The Harmonic Arts.md`).

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** Almost entirely in the target.
Interference Lock does not deliver destruction; it removes a working's ability to
hold its own phase, and the energy that then goes somewhere unhelpful is the
**hostile caster's own**, already committed. This is the cleanest example in the
batch of `Two Sets of Books` §III — *"The practitioner does not supply the
joules. They supply the boundary condition"* — because here the joules are
visibly and specifically somebody else's, and the boundary condition is a phase
angle.

**Precedent we are not copying.** Counterspelling is universal: *Dispel Magic*,
Fate/stay night's rune-breaking, the counter-caster of every tactical RPG, all of
which resolve as a contest of magnitude. This does the reverse and says so:
magnitude is irrelevant, the read is everything, and **the better-built the
enemy working the more cheaply it falls**. The divergence is that a Thaumic
Harmonist cannot answer a working they have not analysed and cannot analyse one
that has already closed — *"a silent or already-completed effect offers
Interference Lock nothing to grip"* — so the branch is a specialist's weapon
with a window rather than a general-purpose no.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** Draw is small; the working supplies phase, not power.
**Residue:** the branch leaves a distinctive one — a working that was
interference-locked did not fail on its own terms, and the trace it leaves
carries **two** frequency sets rather than one, which is how Concord casework
distinguishes a countered working from a botched one after the fact. That is a
consequence of the mechanism and not stated on the page; it is offered as a
derivation, not as canon. **Saturation:** as with the sibling branch, the
governing field quantity is ambient tonal noise rather than Aetheric Density,
and the constraint runs the wrong way round — a loud room raises the floor and
hides the target's line (`SA-GAP-AMBIENT-RESONANCE-SCALE`).

**Wellspring stratum.** **None of its own**, and the page is explicit: *"the
discipline listens to the working's dialect rather than speaking one of its
own."* The Wellsprings that matter to an account of this branch are the ones
whose laws describe what it does to other people's workings:

> **Nihiloth · *The Hollow Law*** — *"Two waves in antiphase do not conceal one
> another. They sum to zero, and the region between them carries no energy at
> all."* (`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Limina — Entropy, Void and Mind.md`)

Interference Lock is Nihiloth's physics performed by a discipline that does not
harmonise with Nihiloth, which is worth noticing: **the branch borrows a
Wellspring's law without paying a Wellspring's price**, and nothing on either
page says what authorises that (`SA-GAP-THAUMIC-BORROWED-LAW`).

> **Basilithe · *The Stone Will*** — *"Basilithe raises the Crystal's stiffness
> until its resonance simply will not be driven off, which is a defence against
> every technique that works by imposing an external rhythm."*
> (`Materia — Material Science.md`)

The same counter that defeats Anima Harmonics' therapeutic half defeats
Interference Lock, and for the identical reason. A player who learns it once has
learned it for the whole Harmonic tree.

> **Aurevane · *The Wellspring of Light*** — *"coherent light holds a fixed phase
> relationship across its whole front, which is what makes a laser a laser rather
> than a bright lamp."* (`Fulguria — Electromagnetism.md`)

The Echowright's name is not decoration; coherence is the property Interference
Lock attacks, and a working whose phase front is not fixed cannot be locked
because there is no single phase to oppose.

*Four Theories.* **The Consent Doctrine** — *"The Wellspring recognises a lawful
boundary condition and behaves accordingly, in the same sense that a river
recognises a channel"* (`Two Sets of Books` §IV). Nothing is asked of any
current here. A phase relation is imposed and superposition does the rest, which
is the Doctrine's cleanest possible case and the reason the branch is licensed
curriculum rather than restricted practice.

**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Aether Shell** throughout — receptive
for the read, projective for the counter-frequency, and the branch's whole
difficulty is that it needs both **in the same two seconds**. The **Essence
Core** supplies the reference only insofar as the projected tone must be the
practitioner's own and stable. The **Attraction Layer** is not involved, which is
why this branch has no consent problem and its sibling has nothing but.
Developmental Tier at the Stage VI floor: **Harmonic** — *"the three layers have
found a recurring rhythm the Continuum can read consistently"* — rising to
**Radiant** at the Stage VIII combat gate (Part Twenty-One). Crystal State: not
stated for any named practitioner. **The invoice:** unstated, again, and here
the omission bites harder than on the sibling page, because this branch has a
*combat* application with a Stage gate, a named practitioner, and a stated
operational tempo, and still no cost of any kind.

**The lens: the voice figures.** Ernst Chladni, *Entdeckungen über die Theorie
des Klanges* (1787): draw a bow across the edge of a sand-strewn plate and the
sand flees the antinodes and piles on the nodal lines, so that the plate's
invisible modal structure becomes a visible figure that changes completely with
each new tone. Chladni's own reading was correct and modest — the figure maps
where the plate is *not* moving. The reading that followed him was not. Margaret
Watts Hughes's Eidophone (1885) produced flower- and tree-shaped "voice figures"
from a sung note and she published them as evidence that *"the voice is the
builder of forms"*; a century later Hans Jenny's *Kymatik* (1967) generalised
the claim into a doctrine that the figure **is** the thing's true form, that
matter is sound made visible, and that to know a thing's figure is to know the
thing. It is a beautiful error and a productive one, and a Thaumic school that
holds it will build its whole counter-doctrine on **matching the figure**: read
the hostile working's pattern, reproduce its pattern, and own it. The
instrument-level objection is that **a figure is degenerate.** Different plates
at different frequencies produce identical nodal patterns, and two entirely
unrelated workings can present the same figure without sharing a single member.
A figure-matching Harmonist meets two workings with the same pattern and cannot
tell them apart; worse, he will answer the second with the counter he built for
the first, at the wrong frequency, which is the exact recipe for the branch's
own **Echo Fracture** — *"a counter-frequency resonates with the wrong harmonic
and amplifies the hostile working instead of collapsing it."* The Eidophone
school is not merely mistaken. Its mistake is the failure mode. Offered as a
school's error, not as canon.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Lh]` **Frequency** · Root · Selhar,
the same Root the sibling branch takes, carried this time under `[Knt]`
**Knot** · Counter · Maelor for the combat application — one of only two Counter
forms in the whole index, and the index says why there are only two: *"A glyph
cannot attack a law. … What a counter does instead is impose a boundary that
prevents another boundary from being established."* **Interference Lock does not
attack the hostile working's Wellspring. It prevents the hostile working's
boundary from closing.** That is the definition of a Counter, stated in the
Index, and it is the best-fitting glyph assignment in this batch.
*Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling (`SA-GAP-GLYPH-MIRROR`).*

**What boundary moves.** In the read: the Harmonist's own emission floor, as
before. In the counter: **one phase angle, at one frequency, at one member of
the target chain.** Nothing is moved at scale. The working is not opposed, not
shielded against, not outranked; a single relation between two waves is pinned
at π.

**What the law then does on its own.** Superposition. Two waves at the same
frequency and opposite phase sum to zero at every point where both are present,
and they do so without either being weakened — *"They sum to zero, and the
region between them carries no energy at all."* The hostile working continues to
be cast, continues to draw, continues to cost its caster everything it was going
to cost, and **arrives nowhere**, because arrival is an amplitude and the
amplitude has been summed away. Over Q cycles the target resonator's stored
energy drains into the antiphase term and the structure that was holding the
working's shape has nothing left to hold it with. Then the shape stops being a
shape.

The constructive half runs the identical law with the sign flipped. N supporting
practitioners hold a fixed phase relationship to a primary working's tone, their
amplitudes add, the intensity goes as the square, and **the primary's own
expenditure does not move**, because he is not supplying the added amplitude —
the choir is. This is why *"multiple practitioners project layered harmonic
support around a central working, letting the primary caster achieve output
beyond individual capacity, without increasing that caster's own Essence
expenditure"* is not a free lunch: the lunch is being bought by ten people
standing behind him.

**Law V check.** The floor is **Stage VI, Glory**, which is **below Refraction**,
so an external anchor is required (`The Four Crafts`, Law V). The page supplies
one — the branch is Spellcraft, cast by voice, and the Sonochoral Conclave's
*"chant in tonal sequences"* is the anchor made explicit. **The gate closes**,
and the anchor being *voice* is mechanically correct rather than decorative,
since the working's output is literally a tone. Above Refraction the chain
internalises and the Harmonist can counter silently, which is the practical
difference between a guild-trained caster and Aurevia.

**Failure mode — Boundary fault.** *"The condition was established imprecisely
and the law ran somewhere unintended."* Here that is **Echo Fracture**: *"A
counter-frequency resonates with the wrong harmonic and amplifies the hostile
working instead of collapsing it"* (`The Harmonic Arts`). The physics is exact
and merciless. Antiphase cancels; **in-phase at the same amplitude doubles**;
in-phase at resonance over Q cycles multiplies by Q. A phase error of π turns
the branch's signature move into the branch's signature catastrophe, and a
frequency error lands on a neighbouring mode that will accept the energy
gratefully. **The counter and the amplification are the same operation with the
sign wrong**, which is why the Order's own doctrine is *do not block the spell;
break the song it is trying to sing* — blocking is an amplitude contest and
loses, and singing wrong is worse than not singing.

A second fault sits on the constructive half: a choir that drifts out of phase
does not merely stop helping. Partial coherence produces **beating** between the
supporting voices, which modulates the primary working's amplitude at the
difference frequency, and a working whose magnitude is being swung up and down
several times a second is a working failing in a new and interesting way. This
is a derived consequence of the stated mechanism, offered as a candidate rather
than asserted.

**What the target sees and feels.** From inside a working being locked: nothing
is pushing back. The chain runs, the draw continues, the cost is being paid, and
**the output stops arriving** — which is the specific sensation `Two Sets of
Books` reserves for a Field fault (*"The working is correct and produces
nothing"*) being manufactured by a third party. The caster's instinct is to
supply more, and supplying more into a phase-cancelled channel spends reserve at
full rate for nothing, which is the trap. A working already in the air is *"pinned
or unravelled mid-flight"*; a working still in the chain simply never finishes
closing. From outside, the target sees a second practitioner listening, going
briefly still, and speaking one short tone — Aurevia's two seconds, *"faster than
most casters could detect that their spell had been disrupted."* From the
receiving end of the constructive half the sensation is the opposite and is
reported as a working arriving with more behind it than the caster has.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** η is unstated for every named
practitioner (`SA-GAP-THAUMIC-NO-CARD`). At the Stage VI floor the Tier of
Standing is **5 · Expert**, η **0.50–0.60** (Part Nineteen): **40 to 50 percent
of every expenditure leaves as heat, sound and structural bleed**, and once
again, for this branch specifically, **the bleed is sound** — broadband, at the
working's own frequencies, radiated into the exact channel the practitioner is
trying to listen to. A Stage VI Harmonist projecting a counter-frequency is
partially deafening himself with the projection's own waste. At the Stage VIII
combat gate (Tier 6, η 0.70–0.80) the waste halves, which is the mechanical
reason Interference Lock is gated two Stages above basic perception rather than
one.

**The arithmetic that can be done.** Two lines, both gate checks and both
derivable by a reader. First: Harmonics **Attunement** requires *Attraction Path
at Stage II to exceed D*, and its retention component *Spirit Path at Stage III
to exceed C* (Part Seven). C tops at **175**, D at **100** (Part Four). A Stage VI
Harmonist therefore holds Attunement anywhere up to the Stage VI ceiling of
**400** provided both Paths are present, and is otherwise capped at 175 —
**below B-Grade**, which is the numeric reason an untrained-Path practitioner
cannot hold a working's frequency in memory long enough to build a counter for
it. Second: Interference Lock's Stage VIII gate puts the practitioner at Tier 6,
η **0.70–0.80**, versus the Stage VI floor's **0.50–0.60**. The counter's waste
falls from roughly half to roughly a quarter — and since the waste is sound in
the band being listened to, **the gate is a signal-to-noise gate**, not a power
gate. Nothing on either page says so. It falls out of the numbers.

**Does the physics close against the stratal account?** **Yes for the mechanism
and no for the ledger.** The mechanism closes unusually well: a phase-only
intervention costs almost nothing in joules, the destroyed energy is the
target's own, and the stratal account correspondingly needs to bill only a small
precise expenditure — which is what a branch that *"does not need to overpower"*
should cost. But the stratal account bills **nothing**, for a combat working
with a named practitioner, a stated tempo and a documented catastrophic failure
mode. A working that can unravel a hostile cast mid-flight and appears in no
ledger has not had its Essence stratum taken. `SA-PHYS-THAUMIC-LOCK-UNBILLED`.

## 5 · Counterplay and the challenge

**The fairness check** (`.claude/skills/wotr-write/references/fair-play.md`).

| Test | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Costs something that hurts | **fail** | No EU, no rate, no toll, no duration, for a working whose combat application ends other people's castings. The branch's real cost is **attention under a two-second clock** and the standing risk of Echo Fracture, and neither is an invoice. Logged `SA-FAIR-THAUMIC-NO-COST`. |
| Stated limits | **pass, and the limits are good ones** | The counter needs *"the casting window"* — a working already closed is unreachable; a working with no harmonic signature offers nothing to grip; the read must complete before the cast does; the practitioner *"does not need to overpower"* and equally **cannot** overpower, so a working they misread is a working they have amplified. Three Stage gates that gate different capabilities. |
| Something beats it | **pass, three ways, and one of them is free** | Below. Cast fast and closed, harden with Basilithe, or be loud. |
| It has a tell | **pass** | A second practitioner goes still, then speaks one short tone. At Stage VI–VII that tone must be voiced aloud (Law V), so the tell is *audible and locatable*; only above Refraction does it go silent, which is the Stage a soldier is taught to fear for exactly this reason. |
| Numbers in band | **cannot be checked, and one number is out of band** | No Grade, EU, range or duration anywhere. Worse, **Harmonic Amplification lets a primary caster reach output *"exceeding their individual capacity"*, and Part Five calls the Temperance ceiling hard**: *"A character cannot allocate their way past a ceiling their Soul Crystal cannot structurally sustain."* Either the choir is exempt from a ceiling the system states without exception, or "capacity" means something narrower than the ceiling. Logged `SA-NUM-THAUMIC-AMPLIFICATION-CEILING`. |

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Break the boundary — close your sentence before he finishes his.** The
  page's own limit is the counter: *"a working with no exploitable harmonic
  signature (a silent or already-completed effect) offers Interference Lock
  nothing to grip."* A caster who works in single short closed bursts rather
  than sustained chains presents a two-second target for less than two seconds.
  Compare the register's *"Force the Seal early"*, used on oneself.
- **Break the boundary — Lock, against the Knot.** `[Lk]` **Lock** *"occupies a
  limit so no other limit can be set there"*, and a working that has pre-claimed
  its own phase relation has occupied the limit Interference Lock needs. The
  index says there are exactly two counter-moves in existence; this branch is one
  of them and the other answers it.
- **Break the boundary — Basilithe again.** *"Basilithe raises the Crystal's
  stiffness until its resonance simply will not be driven off, which is a defence
  against every technique that works by imposing an external rhythm."* The same
  three lines beat both Harmonic branches' projective halves. A player who finds
  it once owns the answer to the tree.
- **Deny the field — noise, and the Veil.** The branch's field is a quiet
  channel. A room full of competing tone raises the floor under the target's
  line; a **Thick Veil** zone *"degrades Harmonia perception"* outright
  (`wiki/Spellcraft/Resonant Divination.md`). Neither costs a working.
- **Break the man — and this is the specific one.** *"Rigidity. Present him with
  the situation his own law handles worst and he will handle it that way
  regardless."* A Thaumic Harmonist's law is *answer the frequency*. Present him
  with two workings whose figures match and whose frequencies do not, and his own
  doctrine walks him into an Echo Fracture that **amplifies the one he meant to
  stop.** This is the cruellest available counter and it requires knowing the
  discipline rather than the man — the cheapest version of the register's
  cruellest move.

**The tell, stated plainly.** A second practitioner stops moving, listens for
about a second, and says one short note. Below Refraction the note is aloud and
its direction is obvious. Above Refraction there is no note, and the only tell
left is your own working failing to arrive while it continues to cost you —
which is indistinguishable from a Field fault until you check the room.

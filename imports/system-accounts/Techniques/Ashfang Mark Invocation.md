# System account · Ashfang Mark Invocation

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The effect in plain words.** He grips a burnt bone taken from something he
killed, opens a vein against it, and for a short while his body does one thing that
animal could do.

**The real phenomenon.** **Bone char as a catalytic substrate.** Calcined bone is
not waste. It is a high-surface-area scaffold of hydroxyapatite and residual carbon
— **on the order of 100 m²/g** for bone char, rising to **500–1,500 m²/g** for
activated carbons — which retains the mineral content of what produced it and
catalyses further reaction at those sites. Bone char was the sugar industry's
decolourising agent for a century for exactly this reason, and its capacity falls
measurably with each regeneration cycle.

Cinerion's register analogue is this and says so: "Pyrolytic char and its catalytic
residue. Particulate optical depth." And its mechanism line: "What survives a fire
is not waste. Char is a high-surface-area carbon scaffold that catalyses further
reaction, and settled ash returns potassium and phosphorus to the ground that fed
the fire"
(`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Caloria — Thermodynamics.md`).

**The governing law.** **Heterogeneous catalysis**, and two of its hard consequences
do all the work on this page.

*First:* a catalyst lowers the activation barrier and **does not shift the
equilibrium.** It changes how fast a system reaches a state; it cannot make an
unreachable state reachable. **This is the exact mechanical reason the page's own
limit holds** — "the borrowed trait … cannot exceed his personal Stage VI stat
ceiling no matter how large the source beast was." A catalyst does not lend
thermodynamics. Draven's ceiling is his equilibrium and the talon cannot move it.

*Second:* active sites are finite, and adsorption is competitive. Two species
contending for the same surface do not both get it; the one with the higher binding
affinity occupies the sites and the other is excluded. **That is the page's "one
trait at a time," and it is also the answer to the page's own "What nobody knows" —
why the graft only ever takes one trait when Cinerion has archived every kill.**
The archive is not the constraint. **The surface is.**

*Third:* deactivation. Repeated use fouls the sites, and activity decays roughly
exponentially with cumulative turnover — which is the page's "Reuse of the same
talon returns a weaker expression each time, the residue thinning with every draw,"
stated in the language of a catalyst bed.

**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Talon mass, a large beast's: order **50 g**. At 100 m²/g that is **≈5,000 m²**
  of internal surface in the hand — about the footprint of a village square, folded
  into a bone.
- **Power demand of a borrowed trait, worked for the hardest case, flight.** Induced
  power to sustain an 80 kg man on 2 m² of lifting surface:
  P = (mg)^1.5 / √(2ρA) = (785 N)^1.5 / √(2 × 1.225 × 2) = 2.20×10⁴ / 2.21 =
  **≈1.0×10⁴ W**.
- Human sustained aerobic output: **200–400 W**. Peak anaerobic: **1–2 kW**.
  **Flight therefore demands 25–50× his sustainable output and 5–10× his peak.**
- Cardiac consequence: delivering 10 kW of mechanical power at ~20 % efficiency
  requires ~50 kW metabolic, against a maximal human cardiac output of roughly
  **25 L/min** carrying about **1 kW** of oxidisable substrate. **The heart is the
  binding constraint, by a factor of about fifty.**

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** Not in the talon — a catalyst
supplies no energy and "does not appear in the products." They come from Draven's
own metabolism and from the ambient field, and the arithmetic above says his
metabolism cannot cover the hardest expressions. **The page's cost is therefore
located exactly where the physics puts it:** "The heartbeat goes wrong while the
graft holds." That is not flavour. That is the one organ the power budget breaks.

**Precedent we are not copying.** Borrowed-form techniques are everywhere — Naruto's
Kekkei Genkai mimicry, Hunter × Hunter's Chimera Ant assimilation, every
druid wildshape. Those *become* the animal. This one does not: the trait arrives at
**his** ceiling, in **his** frame, and the graft is a catalytic residue rather than a
transformation. The divergence is that the limitation is thermodynamic rather than
narrative — the talon of something vast gives him nothing extra because a catalyst
never did.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** The draw is at the smallest scale in this batch — one hand, one
bone, one blood channel. Caloria's favoured conditions are "Steep thermal
gradients, combustible atmosphere, geothermal ground"; suppressive are "Thermal
equilibrium, saturated cold, oxygen-poor air"
(`The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings`, Environmental Coupling). **Residue:**
the talon *is* the residue, and it is the only object-borne residue in the batch:
"Aetheric Residue · Traces left behind by an Essence working … Always readable by
someone patient enough" (`The Core Vocabulary` §II). The archive is a residue
collection, and the thinning with reuse is residue being spent. **Saturation:** not a
field concern; the saturating surface is the talon's own.

An adjacent system reading worth flagging rather than asserting: a bone that carries
a bound current and contacts the Shell at zero distance is close to the register's
**Object Wellspring** — "A current captured, bound, or naturally accumulated inside
a vessel produces a contained domain … An object is binary. You are holding it or you
are not, and its internal domain contacts the Shell at zero distance"
(`The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings`). Whether an Ashfang talon is an
Object Wellspring or merely a residue-bearing focus is a filing question the page
does not answer, and it changes what happens if a talon is taken off him. Logged
`SA-GAP-ASHFANG-OBJECT-WELLSPRING`.

**Wellspring stratum.** **Cinerion · *The Ash Veil*** (primary), Family
**Caloria**, Physics Domain **thermodynamics**. Its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Caloria — Thermodynamics.md`:

> "What survives a fire is not waste. Char is a high-surface-area carbon scaffold
> that catalyses further reaction, and settled ash returns potassium and phosphorus
> to the ground that fed the fire. Cinerion retunes the Shell to metabolize its own
> combustion products, harvesting from expenditure what other practitioners simply
> vent. The suspended residue also scatters incident light and Essence-sight alike."

**Petralon · *The Foundation Stone*** (secondary), Family **Vectoria**, Physics
Domain **mechanics**. Its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Vectoria — Mechanics.md`:

> "Masonry is enormously strong in compression and nearly worthless in tension,
> which is why the arch exists: it converts a span into a queue of compressive
> members. Petralon reorganises the Crystal along the same principle, routing every
> incoming load into compression and accepting slow creep deformation as the price
> of never developing a tensile crack."

Register Stat Effects, through the Merge Ledger: Cinerion gives Tempering **Yield**
(from Conversion) and Harmonics **Stability** (from Recovery); Petralon gives
Vitality **Endurance**→**Constitution** and Dominion **Gravity**.

Register failures, both in play: Cinerion's "**Fouling.** Accumulated residue
eventually coats the conduit rather than feeding it, and recovery rate collapses
precisely when the practitioner has spent the most" — which is catalyst deactivation
and is the page's thinning talon. Petralon's "**Tensile exposure.** Load the
structure in the one direction it does not answer and it does not deform gracefully.
It parts" — and a cardiac wall is a structure loaded in tension.

*Four Theories.* The page leans on **the Residue Position** more than any other in
the batch, and nobody in the fiction would say so: "There is no answering at all.
Every Wellspring is the standing residue of something that already happened … and
practitioners are moving through the wreckage of a working performed before there
were people" (`Two Sets of Books` §IV). A technique whose whole mechanism is *the
residue of a completed event acting on a later one* is the Residue Position with a
bone in its hand. The Accord licenses it under Consent. **Noted, not resolved.**

**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Aether Shell**, and the page is explicit —
"the stored print act on his own Aether Shell the way a residue triggers a reaction
it does not itself supply." The Shell "Converts inner truth into outward force" and
fails by "Clouding or Rupture" (`The Core Vocabulary` §III); the ash-grey veins along
the graft path are Clouding made visible in tissue, which is what
`Two Sets of Books` §V says must happen: "everything a Crystal does eventually shows
in tissue." Developmental Tier at Stage VI: **Harmonic** — "Techniques stabilize;
Wellspring harmonization becomes possible without tearing the lattice"
(Part Twenty-One). Crystal State: **Refined** (card). **The invoice:** cardiac
instability for the duration, billed against Vitality — and the physical account says
that is the correct organ, by a factor of fifty.

## 3 · Mechanism

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Frm]` **Form** · Extension · Thalen —
the limit that says *which* shape the deposition takes — laid across `[Eq]`
**Equivalence** · Extension · Urion, which is the one form among the thirty that
names the correspondence a graft depends on. Both from the thirty unclassified forms
in `wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`.
*Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling.*

**What boundary moves.** One quantity: **which template the Shell's conductivity map
is read against for the duration.** Not his capacity. Not the beast's. Only the
pattern the Shell is expressing through.

**What the law then does on its own.** All of it, and the page's three limits are
one law each. The trait expresses because a conductivity map is what the Shell
expresses through. It arrives at Draven's ceiling because catalysis does not move an
equilibrium. Only one arrives because adsorption is competitive on a finite surface.
Each reuse is weaker because a catalyst bed deactivates. **Nobody wrote those three
rules. They fall out of the one boundary.** And the Index's warning arrives on time:
the law kept running after the intent stopped, and what it kept doing was demanding
10 kW from a body that can supply 1.

**Law V check.** Stage VI sits below Refraction, so an external anchor is required
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). The page supplies two of the four the
Craft names — **hand** (gripping the talon) and **blood** (the opened channel).
**The gate closes cleanly, and with the two most expensive anchors on the list.**

**Failure mode — Structural fault.** "The Crystal could not hold what the boundary
permitted. Overchannel, Backlash, Fracture. **The working succeeds and the
practitioner does not**" (`Two Sets of Books` §VII). That last sentence is this
technique in seven words. The graft works. The boundary permits an expression whose
power budget the body cannot fund, and the structure that fails is cardiac. The
page's counters are the same fault read from outside: an identity-stripping working
or an Essence seal placed **before** contact leaves no signature to template from,
which is the boundary never being established rather than the structure failing —
a **Field fault** on the talon's side.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** η ≈ 0.55 (card), so **45 percent of every
expenditure leaves as heat, sound and structural bleed** — "which is why a
low-efficiency practitioner is loud, warm, and easy to find, and why the highest
Bands run quiet" (`Two Sets of Books` §III). Draven is not quiet. At Stage VI he sits
in Tier 5 · Expert, η band 0.50–0.60, and 0.55 is **mid-band and consistent** — the
only one of the batch's five carded practitioners whose η sits where the ladder puts
it. Cinerion is the mitigation: the register says it "retunes the Shell to metabolize
its own combustion products, harvesting from expenditure what other practitioners
simply vent," so part of that 45 % comes back. **How much is not specified anywhere
and cannot be derived.** Logged `SA-GAP-CINERION-RECLAIM`.

**Does the physics close against the stratal account?** **Partly, and the gap is
instructive rather than fatal.** The stratal account is clean: a residue supplies a
template, a Shell expresses it, a catalyst's own three limits become the technique's
three limits with nothing invented. But the physical account's power budget for the
*strongest* expressions — flight, most obviously — exceeds what Draven's metabolism
can fund by roughly fifty times. The system's answer is available and the page does
not invoke it: "The honest answer is that a working's energy budget balances at the
Aether stratum and not at the Essence one. **The practitioner does not supply the
joules**" (`Two Sets of Books` §III). On that reading the 10 kW comes from the
ambient field and the cardiac cost is the *structure* failing, not the fuel running
out — which is exactly what a Structural fault is. **It resolves, but only if the
page is read as drawing on the field, and the page does not say it does.** Logged
`SA-PHYS-ASHFANG-POWER-SOURCE` so Isaac can rule on where the joules come from
rather than leaving it to a reader.

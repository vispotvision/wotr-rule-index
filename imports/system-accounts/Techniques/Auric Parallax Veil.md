# System account · Auric Parallax Veil

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** A thin sheet of light and shadow across sixty metres, and
whichever of the things that could have happened there she prefers is the one that happens.
Nothing new is made.

**The real phenomenon — and the page describes two incompatible ones.**

*Reading A, the page's Mechanism paragraph: genuine path superposition.* The page says the
Veil is "used to hold an event's several physically possible outcome-paths open at once, the
way a double-slit interference pattern holds more than one path until something forces a
result." That is the **path integral**: amplitudes over histories interfere while the paths
are indistinguishable, and the interference vanishes the moment which-path information
exists. The governing quantity is the **decoherence timescale**, and for anything
macroscopic it is brutal. A 1 µm dust grain in ordinary air decoheres in roughly
**10⁻³¹ s**. Isolate it in vacuum with nothing but cosmic microwave background photons
striking it and a centimetre-scale object still decoheres in about **10⁻¹⁸ s**. **To hold a
sword-stroke's outcome-paths open for a perceptible instant is to suppress decoherence by
something like thirty orders of magnitude, and the system supplies no mechanism for
suppressing decoherence at all.**

*Reading B, the page's Effect and Limit: outcome biasing on an already-stochastic process.*
"Whichever of an event's already-possible outcomes she prefers becomes the one that happens;
nothing new is conjured, only chosen," and "it cannot originate an outcome that was never a
possible branch to begin with." **That is post-selection, and it is physically ordinary.**
A process with a genuine probability distribution over outcomes can be biased without any
superposition being maintained; you are reweighting a distribution, not holding a wavefunction
open. Every real stochastic system — crack initiation site, turbulent transition, misfire —
has such a distribution, and Reading B only ever touches those.

**Reading B closes. Reading A does not.** The page's Effect and Limit describe B; its
Mechanism describes A. Logged `SA-PHYS-PARALLAX-SUPERPOSITION-VS-SELECTION`; **not
resolved.** Which one governs decides whether the Veil is a probability instrument or a
quantum-coherence miracle, and it changes every counter on the page.

**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Sheet: up to **60 m** across (page), thin — a plane, not a volume, which is the cheapest
  geometry in Opalis's five.
- Decoherence timescales, Reading A: **≈10⁻³¹ s** (µm grain, air); **≈10⁻¹⁸ s** (cm object,
  CMB only). **Suppression required: ~10³⁰.**
- Basilithe's law-lock, quantified: a cut-quartz oscillator holds **Q ≈ 10⁴–10⁶** and
  fractional frequency stability **10⁻⁸ to 10⁻¹⁰**. That is the stability the Wellspring
  lends, and it is the physical meaning of "fixes which fringe of that pattern is real."
- Stated ceiling, SSS-Grade: **1–5 PN** peak force and **418 TJ to 4.184 EJ**, "100 kt to
  1 Tt TNT-equivalent." **Two of those three are correct and one is wrong by a factor of
  1,000.** Part Four's SSS row is 4.184×10¹⁴ – 4.184×10²¹ J. 418 TJ = 4.18×10¹⁴ ✓.
  100 kt = 4.184×10¹⁴ J ✓. 1 teraton = 10¹² t × 4.184×10⁹ J/t = **4.184×10²¹ J = 4.184
  zettajoules**, not exajoules. **The page says EJ (10¹⁸); the correct unit is ZJ (10²¹).**
  Logged `SA-NUM-SSS-EJ-NOT-ZJ` — the same error appears on `Celestial Decree` and
  `Liminal Palace of False Dawn`.
- The page is right that this is a **ceiling on what may be overridden, not a discharge**,
  and says so. Worth crediting: it is the only one of the three SSS-ceiling pages that
  states the distinction outright.

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** Under Reading B, almost nowhere: biasing a
distribution costs the information to identify the branches and the authority to select one,
and neither is a joule. Under Reading A the budget is not merely large but ill-defined,
because the system has no currency for coherence. **This is the batch's clearest case where
choosing the mechanism changes the energy account by thirty orders of magnitude**, which is
why it is a question for Isaac and not a judgement call for a researcher.

**Precedent we are not copying.** Probability manipulation is well-trodden — Fate's
Unlimited Blade Works on the metaphysical side, Domino's luck powers, every "reroll" mechanic.
Those bend odds *for* the user. This one **selects among branches that were already there**
and forecloses using the same ground for the opposite purpose while it runs, which is a
conservation constraint rather than a cooldown. The divergence is that its limits are
limits on *what was possible*, not on how often.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** A plane rather than a volume, so the draw is geometrically cheap and
informationally expensive. Materia is favoured by "Mineral-rich rock, worked stone, deep
foundation" and suppressed by "Open water, high altitude, moving platforms"
(`The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings`, Environmental Coupling) — **a suppression
list worth flagging, because a working that selects outcomes on a moving deck is working
against its own Family's terrain and the page does not say so.** **Residue:** the strangest
in the batch: what a Parallax Veil leaves behind is a district where certain outcomes
*did not happen*, and the register's instrument for reading that is Eclipseron, the current
that "reads what has been blocked, subtracted, or withheld, and derives from the shape of the
absence the shape of the thing casting it"
(`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Spatium — Spatial Geometry.md`). **An
Eclipseron practitioner is the natural investigator of a Veil, and that is a usable
consequence nobody has written down.** **Saturation:** not a limit; the limit is how many
concurrent outcome-states the Veil can carry, which is the page's own stated overload
counter.

**Wellspring stratum.** **Basilithe · *The Stone Will*** (primary), Family **Materia**,
Physics Domain **material science**. Its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Materia — Material Science.md`:

> "A body's resonant frequency is fixed by its stiffness and its mass, and a high-stiffness
> low-loss structure holds that frequency against forcing so reliably that civilisations set
> their clocks by cut quartz. Basilithe raises the Crystal's stiffness until its resonance
> simply will not be driven off, which is a defence against every technique that works by
> imposing an external rhythm."

**Tenebra · *The Hidden Shadow*** (carried at primary weight per the page), Family
**Limina**; law quoted in full in the `Hunter's Breath` account, supplying the cancellation
of the branches not chosen.

**Fractura · *The Breaking Point*** (carried at primary weight per the page), Family
**Vectoria**, Physics Domain **mechanics**. Its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Vectoria — Mechanics.md`:

> "Nothing fails at its theoretical strength. Everything fails at a pre-existing flaw,
> because stress concentrates at a crack tip in proportion to the square root of the crack's
> length, and once that intensity exceeds the material's toughness the crack runs at a
> fraction of the speed of sound and does not stop. Fractura gives the practitioner sight of
> those flaws and the precision to load exactly one of them."

**And here the page misattributes.** It says the outcome-paths are held open by "**Fractura's
refraction law**." **Fractura has no refraction law.** Its analogue is linear elastic
fracture mechanics — the Griffith criterion and the stress intensity factor — and its whole
content is flaw populations and crack propagation. There is a genuine and much better
Fractura reading available and the page does not take it: **"Fractura gives the practitioner
sight of those flaws" is exactly the instrument a branch-selection working needs**, because a
flaw population *is* a distribution over where a thing will fail, and selecting which flaw
carries the load is selecting which outcome happens. Under Reading B, Fractura is the
working's natural engine. Under the page's "refraction law," it is doing something the
register does not describe. Logged `SA-OFF-FRACTURA-REFRACTION`; recorded, not resolved.

Register failures in play. Basilithe's "**Resonant catastrophe.** A frequency that cannot be
shifted also cannot be detuned away from a forcing that happens to match it" — which is the
page's own overload counter, exactly: flood it with more concurrent states than its lock can
hold. Fractura's "**Self-inspection.** The Wellspring does not exempt its bearer, and a
Fractura practitioner under strain reads their own flaw population with perfect clarity at the
worst possible moment" — **which is Harmonic Divergence, the page's first cost, verbatim in
substance: "the disorientation of briefly experiencing her own several possible selves at
once."** Register and page agree and neither cites the other.

*Four Theories.* The page's "What nobody knows" is a Four-Theories question and an unusually
sharp one: "Whether 'the version reality already knows what it wants to be' is a fact about
the world or a fact about Opalis." That is **Correspondence** against **Consent** stated as
an empirical question — Correspondence holds that "The practitioner and the Wellspring are the
same law expressed at two scales," which would make the preference a fact about both at once
and explain why nobody has found a case where they come apart (`Two Sets of Books` §IV).
**Noted, not resolved**; this is the most direct Four-Theories test in the batch.

**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Attraction Layer** — Domain Weaving is a Fate-Plane
claim, and outcome selection is recognition business. Its failure state is **Obsession
Force**, "When a practitioner cannot release judgment … recognition curdles into fixation"
(`The Core Vocabulary` §II–III), and a practitioner who selects outcomes is precisely the
practitioner at risk of being unable to stop. Developmental Tier at Stage XII: **Sovereign** —
"Trait law and Domain law begin to be the same law. No longer a container for power but a
generator of local metaphysical conditions" (Part Twenty-One). Crystal State: `null`, no card.
**The invoice:** Harmonic Divergence (Fractura's self-inspection) and Structural Fatigue
("thin fracturing the Veil leaves in her own law-harmonics, requiring meditation or a
Penance-aligned rite to repair"). **The Penance reference is correct and worth noting:**
Penance's law is work hardening — "deliberate strain accumulates strength, and the strength is
honest because it is located precisely at the sites of prior failure"
(`Vectoria — Mechanics.md`) — so a Penance rite repairing law-harmonic microfractures is the
right current for the job, and the page picked it for the right reason.

**The lens: Leibniz's compossibility.** *Théodicée* (1710) and *Monadology*
§§53–55. Leibniz's God does not invent the world; He surveys the infinitely many
**compossible** worlds — the ones whose parts can hold together without
contradiction — and actualises one. Creation is selection, and everything not
selected was equally real as a possibility and is now nothing. Strip the
theology and that is the Veil's own claim about itself, *"nothing new is
conjured, only chosen"*, performed by a finite will over sixty metres instead of
an infinite one over everything. The lens earns its place because it also
supplies the working's limit without being asked: a branch that is not
compossible with the rest of the world cannot be selected, which is exactly the
page's *"cannot originate an outcome that was never a possible branch."* And it
supplies the good misreading. A school that takes Leibniz's **pre-established
harmony** with it — monads that never interact, only agree — will conclude the
Veil cannot be interfered with, since selection is not a causal act. The page's
own Counter is interference: manufacture more concurrent outcome-states than the
Veil can hold and it shatters. Whatever the Veil is doing, it is not sitting
outside causation, and a practitioner who believes it is will hold it one
exchange too long.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Lk]` **Lock** · Counter · Thalen — Basilithe's
law-lock *is* a Lock, and the Index's description fits without adjustment: "**Lock** occupies
a limit so no other limit can be set there," which is what fixing one fringe as real does to
the others. Laid across `[Ora]` **Truth** · Extension · Iesara, the form for *which* of
several readings stands. Both from the thirty unclassified forms in
`wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`.
*Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling.*

**What boundary moves.** Under Reading B, one quantity: **the weighting of an already-existing
outcome distribution across a 60 m plane.** Under Reading A, a different one: **the
decoherence rate inside the same plane.** The two boundaries are not related and the working
cannot be both.

**What the law then does on its own.** Under Reading B, everything the page lists follows.
It cannot originate an outcome that was not a branch, because reweighting a distribution
cannot add support to it. It cannot hold two contradictory Veil-states over one stretch of
ground, because a distribution has one normalisation. It cannot override another Stage XII
will, because two selectors on one distribution contest rather than compose. A pure Basilithe
construct resists because its own lock has already collapsed its distribution to a point, and
there is nothing left to select. A massive Petralon presence brute-forces through "at a
changed cost to whoever forced the passage," which is the correct shape: forcing a
low-probability branch is expensive in proportion to how low it was. **Five stated limits, one
boundary, nothing added — which is itself an argument for Reading B.**

**Law V check.** Stage XII sits far above Refraction, so no material anchor is required
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). The page gives "a single slow parting gesture,"
which is the Gestured vector used by choice. At Stage XII this is pure legibility, and the
Craft page's note applies: a practitioner working silently has told the room their Stage.

**Failure mode — Structural fault.** "The Crystal could not hold what the boundary permitted.
Overchannel, Backlash, Fracture. The working succeeds and the practitioner does not"
(`Two Sets of Books` §VII). Structural Fatigue is a Crystal taking microfracture from holding
a lock; the overload counter is the same fault induced deliberately, which is Basilithe's
resonant catastrophe. A **Field fault** sits beside it: "Null-Law suppression fields that
flatten probability outright deny the Veil anything to select between" — the room supplies no
distribution, the working is correct, nothing happens.

**What the target sees and feels.** From outside the plane, a thin standing
sheet of refracting light and shadow, and behind it the world going slightly
wrong in a specific way: things that should have happened *nearly* happen. A
blow lands where it would have landed if the floor had been a centimetre lower.
A working fires and resolves into its own least effective reading, not blocked
and not dispelled — **completed, badly**, as if it had always been aimed that
way. This is the part worth being precise about, because it is what makes the
Veil hard to diagnose in the field: there is no failure to point at. Under
Refraction Break a target feels no contact at all and simply finds that every
route he takes is the poor one, repeatedly, which reads from the inside as bad
luck and from the outside as a man walking into things. Under Reality
Reinforcement the ground and air in the radius stop being negotiable — pressure,
temperature, structure all hold against intrusion at amplified strength. The
one honest tell to the target is the plane itself: it has an edge, it is thin,
and it is between him and her.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** No card exists for Opalis, so η comes from the
ladder: Stage XII is **Tier 7 · Grandmaster**, band **0.85–0.90** (Part Nineteen), so
**10–15 percent** leaves as heat, sound and structural bleed. Part Nineteen's gloss on that
tier is worth quoting because it changes how the working reads: "Traits become local physics.
**Presence optimizes Aether flow in the vicinity.**" At Tier 7 the bleed is small and the
practitioner is quiet — `Two Sets of Books` §III: "why the highest Bands run quiet." **A
Parallax Veil should be nearly undetectable while it runs, and the page's visible sheet of
light and shadow is the only thing giving it away.**

## 5 · Counterplay and the challenge

**The fairness check** (`.claude/skills/wotr-write/references/fair-play.md`).

| Test | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Costs something that hurts | **pass, unquantified** | **Harmonic Divergence** (disorientation from holding several of her own possible selves at once) and **Structural Fatigue** — thin fractures in her own law-harmonics that *"need meditation or a Penance-aligned equilibrium rite to close."* Downtime she cannot skip is a cost that hurts. But there is no EU figure, no fraction of reserve, and therefore **no Starvation margin**, so nothing here can be spent down inside a fight. `SA-GAP-EU-FORMULA`. |
| Stated limits | **pass** | 60 m; line of sight; a single designated region; **cannot stabilise and destabilise the same space at once**; cannot originate a branch that was not already possible; cannot override a peer Stage XII will outright, only distort a weaker harmonic. |
| Something beats it | **pass, and findable** | Two named routes, neither requiring a peer: flatten the probability field, or **overload it with branches**. See below. |
| It has a tell | **pass** | A slow parting gesture, then a visible prismatic plane with an edge, standing in the open. |
| Numbers in band | **pass** | Stage XII ⇒ Max Grade SSS, ceiling 950, Tier 7 · Grandmaster, η 0.85–0.90 (Parts Five and Nineteen). The SSS force band is quoted from the Fracture. The page is careful to call it *"a ceiling on what may be overridden, not a discharge the Veil itself releases"*, which keeps it honest. No card exists to check η against (`SA-GAP-OPALIS-NO-CARD`). |

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Deny the field — the clean answer.** *"Silence. The removal of magical
  possibility from a region."* A Null-Law suppression field that flattens
  probability gives the Veil **nothing to select between**; the working is
  correct and produces nothing. Note the diagnostic the register hands over for
  free: *"A soul held in one reports the sensation as loneliness rather than
  weakness."* Family **Materia**, and the register's terrain line is *"Materia
  has opinions and no tools on open water"* — so water is the second half of this
  route.
- **Break the boundary — overload, not opposition.** *"Knot and Lock … They
  condition the conditioning."* Deliberately manufacture more concurrent
  outcome-states than the Veil can carry — the page names a heavy Fractura
  working — and it shatters. This is a **Knot**: entangle the paths so none can
  be traced cleanly. It does not fight the selection; it makes the selection
  undecidable.
- **Break the man — Rigidity, and the contradiction clause.** She cannot hold
  two contradictory Veil-states over the same ground. An opponent who forces her
  to want stabilisation and destabilisation in the same place at the same moment
  is not attacking the Veil; he is attacking the fact that she has one of them.
- **Break the body — available and underrated.** Opalis is a person at the far
  end of a plane she has to keep standing in line of sight of. Distance, and
  *"numbers, correctly used"*, do not care what branch she prefers.

**The tell, stated plainly.** One slow parting gesture toward a region, then a
thin prismatic sheet. The gesture precedes the plane, and the plane has to be
between her and what she is selecting for.

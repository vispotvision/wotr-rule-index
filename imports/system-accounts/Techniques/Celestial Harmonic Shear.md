# System account · Celestial Harmonic Shear

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** She draws a line in the air, and anything whose own
frequency crosses it comes apart along that frequency — wards, illusions and layered
constructs all at once, not one layer at a time.

**The real phenomenon.** **Failure along a normal mode rather than across the geometry.**
Every bound structure has a set of vibrational modes and an interfacial strength that is
much lower along those modes than the bulk strength is across them. Sever the mode and the
structure comes apart at a fraction of the energy required to cut the material.

The numbers make the point. A carbon-fibre laminate carries roughly **1,500 MPa** in
in-plane tension and only **50–90 MPa** in interlaminar shear — **twenty to thirty times
weaker along the interface than across the fibres.** A wineglass has a fundamental mode at
a few hundred hertz with **Q ≈ 10³**, so a drive at that frequency is amplified a
thousandfold before the glass ever sees the stress that breaks it. **Failure along the
resonance is not a stronger cut. It is a cheaper one.**

The page's own statement of this is correct: "any bound structure is held together by a
characteristic vibrational mode, and severing that mode rather than the material itself is
the same principle a shattered wineglass or a delaminated composite demonstrates, failure
along the resonance rather than along the visible shape."

**The governing law.** For a forced damped oscillator the steady-state amplification at
resonance is the quality factor, **Q = 1/(2ζ)**, so the response to a drive at ω₀ exceeds
the static response by Q. And for a layered structure, the governing failure criterion is
**interlaminar shear strength**, which is the weakest number in the laminate's data sheet
and the one nobody designs against.

**Measurable quantities, SI — and one figure that does not fit.**
- Range **300 m**, line-of-sight; the cut is a line up to **100 m** long and under
  **3 cm** wide (page). Pointing precision required at full range for a 3 cm kerf:
  0.03 ÷ 300 = **1×10⁻⁴ rad**.
- Cut cross-section: 100 m × 0.03 m = **3 m²**.
- Interlaminar advantage: **20–30×** cheaper along the interface than across the body.
- Resonant amplification available: **Q ≈ 10³** for a low-loss structure.
- **The energy figure is wrong twice.** The page gives "on the order of 1 PN peak force
  and roughly **400 TJ** yield along the line." First: **400 TJ is below the SSS floor.**
  Part Four's SSS row runs 4.184×10¹⁴ – 4.184×10²¹ J, i.e. **418 TJ and up**. A 400 TJ
  yield reads as **SS-Grade**, one Tier below what the page requires. Logged
  `SA-NUM-SHEAR-400TJ-BELOW-SSS`. Second, and more interesting: **the mechanism argues
  against a large yield at all.** Resonant decoupling is cheap by construction — that is
  its whole advantage — so quoting a Grade-ceiling energy for a working whose selling point
  is *not paying the fracture energy* mixes the two accounts. Spread across the 3 m² cut,
  400 TJ is **1.3×10¹⁴ J/m²**, against a fracture energy for structural steel of about
  **10⁵ J/m²** — nine orders of magnitude of overkill for a cut the mechanism says should
  be nearly free. Logged `SA-PHYS-SHEAR-YIELD-VS-MECHANISM`.
- Residual: "resonance cracks lingering a few seconds along the cut" — consistent with a
  ringdown at Q ≈ 10³ and a mode in the audible range, which decays in roughly Q cycles.

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** In the target's own structure and its
own drive. That is the point of a resonant attack: the practitioner supplies a *frequency*,
not a force, and the structure amplifies it by Q before failing. The system's line applies
exactly — "The practitioner does not supply the joules"
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Physical Account Two Sets of Books.md` §III) — and here it
applies more strongly than on almost any other page in the batch, which is why the stated
400 TJ sits so oddly.

**Precedent we are not copying.** Cutting techniques are the genre's most crowded field —
Bleach's Getsuga, Demon Slayer's breathing forms, Kirito. Those cut **matter**. This one
cuts **the mode that holds the matter together**, which is why layered defences fail
simultaneously rather than in sequence (they share a mode) and why a target holding several
contradictory frequencies is immune (there is no single mode to find). The divergence is
that the counter-list is a list of *spectral* properties, not of durability.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** A line, not a field — geometrically the cheapest working in Opalis's
five after the Parallax Veil. Fulguria wants "Dry air, conductive substrate, clear line of
sight" and is suppressed by "Faraday-caged interiors, heavy ferrous shielding, dense fog"
(`The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings`, Environmental Coupling); line of sight is
required and the page requires it. **Residue:** a 100 m seam along which a structure's mode
has been severed. Because Orrenthal's own failure mode is intergranular attack, the residue
should read as **grain-boundary damage** — visible as a seam with sound material either
side. **Saturation:** not a limit; the limit is whether a single clean frequency can be
found.

**Wellspring stratum.** **Orrenthal · *The Metallic Heart*** (primary), Family
**Fulguria**, Physics Domain **electromagnetism**. Its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Fulguria — Electromagnetism.md`:

> "A metal is strong, conductive, and ductile for one reason: its valence electrons belong
> to the lattice rather than to any atom in it. Nothing is individually owned, so the
> structure deforms without fracturing and carries charge without resistance. Orrenthal
> imposes that bonding character on the Crystal. The Measurewrights note without
> embarrassment that the Wellspring's moral reading and its physical reading are the same
> reading."

**And its Failure field is this technique.** "**Intergranular corrosion.** Attack proceeds
along the boundaries rather than the body, and **the structure fails at its seams while its
bulk remains sound**" (same file). A cut that severs the holding mode and leaves the
material intact is intergranular attack, exactly. **As on `Hypnather Descent Rite`, the
register wrote the technique into a Wellspring's Failure field before anyone built a
working on it, and the page is right to lead with Orrenthal.**

Nine secondaries are named — Fractura, Basilithe, Dissolution, Tarturon, Cataclysm,
Tenebra, Penance, Petralon, Oneirion — and the page assigns three of them real jobs:
Fractura and Basilithe decide "whether the cut resolves as splitting divergence or as a
clean geometric line through law-bound structure," and Dissolution supplies "collapse into
null-phase rather than a visible seam." Those three assignments check out against their
registers. **The remaining six are carried without a stated function**, which is a filing
question rather than an error: the register's own warning is that "The ceiling is
**Tempering Capacity** and it is not negotiable. Exceeding it does not fail gracefully …
**It degrades all active harmonizations equally**"
(`The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings`). Ten currents held for one line is a
Capacity claim and no Capacity figure exists for Opalis. Logged
`SA-GAP-OPALIS-TEMPERING-CAPACITY`.

Register failures also in play: Basilithe's "**Resonant catastrophe.** A frequency that
cannot be shifted also cannot be detuned away from a forcing that happens to match it" —
which cuts both ways here and explains why a pure Basilithe construct both resists the cut
*and* is the thing most vulnerable to it if she finds the right note. The page takes the
resistant reading; the register permits both.

*Four Theories.* **Consent Doctrine** on the page's framing. But the page's "What nobody
knows" is sharper than it looks: "Whether Opalis is cutting the frequency that is actually
there, or the one she has decided is the lie underneath it." Under Consent, the frequency is
a field quantity and her decision is irrelevant; under **Correspondence**, the frequency she
finds and the frequency that is there are the same thing by construction, and the question
cannot be asked. **Noted, not resolved.**

**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Aether Shell** — the page's first cost is "internal
resonance leaks outward as she cuts," which is a Shell that has been made into an oscillator
radiating on the side. Shell failure states are "Clouding or Rupture"
(`The Core Vocabulary` §III); vertigo and temporary tone-deafness are Clouding, localised to
the sense the working runs on. Developmental Tier at Stage XII: **Sovereign**
(Part Twenty-One). Crystal State: `null`, no card. **The invoice:** an estimated
**35,000 EU per cast**, plus the resonance leak, plus a backlash that "weakens Opalis's other
Emanation-level workings for a short while afterward." **That last item is the interesting
one and the register predicted it:** degrading *all* active harmonisations at once is
precisely what exceeding Tempering Capacity does.

**The lens: the harmony of the spheres, in Kepler's version.** Pythagoras by
way of Kepler's *Harmonices Mundi* (1619), Book V. Kepler spent twenty years on
the conviction that each body carries a proper tone, and that to know the tone is
to know the thing — and he was not merely making music: the Third Law fell out
of that search, which is why this is the most productive wrong idea on the list.
Orrenthal's reading of a structure's *true frequency* is the same conviction with
a working attached: find the tone that is holding a thing together and you do not
need to touch the thing. But Kepler's harmonies are **ratios between bodies**,
not a single absolute tone per body — the music is in the intervals, and a single
planet in an empty universe has no note. A school that drops the relational half
and keeps the absolute half will conclude that every structure has exactly one
frequency, findable and cuttable, and that a layered construct must therefore
come apart all at once when that one frequency is severed. That is exactly what
this page's **Effect** line claims. It is not what its **Mechanism** delivers,
and it is not what its own Counter assumes — the Counter says a target *"holding
several contradictory frequencies at once"* defeats the cut. The lens and the
page fail in the same place, which is the most interesting thing about both, and
it is logged below rather than tidied away.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Lh]` **Frequency** · Root · Selhar — the only
form among the thirty that names this limit, and it names it exactly — laid across `[Al]`
**Edge** · Root · Thalen for the 100 m line. Both from the thirty unclassified forms in
`wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`.
*Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling.*

**What boundary moves.** One quantity: **the continuity of one named vibrational mode along
a 100 m line.** Not the material. Not the energy. The mode's connectedness.

**What the law then does on its own.** Everything, and every limit on the page is a spectral
fact rather than a rule. Layered defences fail together because they share a mode, and a
severed mode is severed for all of them. Something operating outside harmonic physics —
"true void, full dream-essence, anti-tone fields" — cannot be cut because it has no mode to
find. A target holding several contradictory frequencies at once is not resistant; it is
**ambiguous**, and the cut needs a unique mode. A silence field degrades the targeting
because a mode is identified by listening. **Four stated limitations, one boundary.**

**Law V check.** Stage XII sits far above Refraction, so no material anchor is required
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). The page gives a raised hand drawing a line —
the Gestured vector by choice. At this Stage that is legibility, and a 3 cm kerf at 300 m
arguably needs the hand for aim rather than for anchoring, which is a distinction the
`Hypnather Descent Rite` account draws and this page could use.

**Failure mode — Structural fault.** "The Crystal could not hold what the boundary
permitted. Overchannel, Backlash, Fracture" (`Two Sets of Books` §VII). A Shell run as a
resonant source leaks, and a large cut returns a backlash that degrades her other workings —
which is the Structural fault and the Tempering Capacity ceiling arriving together. A
**Field fault** is the counter set: silence, stillness, or a void-phase barrier leaves the
working correct with no mode to find.

**What the target sees and feels.** A raised hand, a slow line drawn downward or
outward through the air, and then a seam. The line is up to 100 m long and under
3 cm wide, out to 300 m, and it resolves instantaneously — there is no travel to
watch and no impact to brace for. What a target on the line feels depends on
whether he was being held together by a frequency at all: a ward, an illusion or
a layered construct loses cohesion **exactly at the intersection**, coming apart
along the resonance instead of along its visible shape, so the failure surface
does not match the object's geometry and looks wrong to anyone expecting a cut.
Resonance cracks linger a few seconds along the seam, which is the only part of
the working that is visible after the fact and the only evidence available to
whoever has to work out what happened. Matter that is merely *matter* on the line
is not the target and is not cut. And a structure anchored in pure Basilithe can
hold the line and hand it back: *"resist or redirect the cut through perfect
resonance symmetry."*

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** No card for Opalis, so η comes from the ladder:
Stage XII ⇒ **Tier 7 · Grandmaster**, η **0.85–0.90** (Part Nineteen) ⇒ **10–15 percent**
as heat, sound and structural bleed. **On this working the bleed has a name: it is the
resonance leak the page charges her for.** A working whose output is a frequency bleeds as
*sound*, and Part Nineteen's gloss on the tier — "Presence optimizes Aether flow in the
vicinity" — is in tension with a practitioner who goes tone-deaf from her own cut. Offered as
texture.

## 5 · Counterplay and the challenge

**The fairness check** (`.claude/skills/wotr-write/references/fair-play.md`).

| Test | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Costs something that hurts | **pass, on an unsourced figure** | The page bills **≈35,000 EU per cast** plus vertigo, temporary tone-deafness to certain currents, and — for a large cut — a backlash that weakens her other Emanation-level workings for a while. The sensory cost is real and the backlash is a genuine tempo penalty. But the EU figure rests on an *"8,000 to 45,000 EU range documented elsewhere for a non-sustained Stage XII working"*, and **no such range is a system rule** (`SA-GAP-STAGE-XII-EU-BAND`). With no reserve figure there is also no Starvation margin, so the number cannot yet be turned into a countdown. |
| Stated limits | **pass** | 300 m, line of sight; a line ≤100 m long and <3 cm wide; **one Shear at full potency per activation**; cannot cut a being at or above her own harmonic sovereignty; **cannot touch anything operating outside harmonic physics at all** — true void, full dream-essence, anti-tone fields. That last is an unusually clean immunity clause and it is the counter, stated in the page's own voice. |
| Something beats it | **pass, and findable without a peer** | Silence, stillness, void-phase, or simple frequency multiplicity. None of these requires Stage XII. |
| It has a tell | **pass** | A raised hand and a slow drawn line, and the line has to be drawn before the seam exists. |
| Numbers in band | **fail on one figure, logged already** | Stage XII ⇒ SSS (726–950), Tier 7, η 0.85–0.90 ✓. But the page's own force estimate — *"on the order of 1 PN peak force and roughly 400 TJ yield"* — sits **below** the SSS band's floor of 418 TJ (Part Four), which the account records as `SA-NUM-SHEAR-400TJ-BELOW-SSS`. The Path gate is also wrong for its own first Sub-Stat (`SA-NUM-SHEAR-PATHGATE`). Both were logged before this section; neither is a new `fairness` fault, because both make the working *weaker* than its band rather than stronger. |

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Deny the field — Silence, and the Family's own terrain.** Family
  **Fulguria**, and the register is blunt: *"Fulguria fails against boiled
  leather, fired clay, dense fog and a shielded interior … the counter-Fulguria
  kit has been leather and ceramic for four centuries and it is not a secret."*
  Against a targeting working that needs line of sight to 300 m, **dense fog** is
  the whole answer and costs nothing. A Cymorath-type silence field is named on
  the page as reducing targeting accuracy.
- **Break the boundary — multiplicity, which is a Knot.** Hold several
  contradictory frequencies at once and there is no single tone to find. The page
  names a Fractura-dominant being, a dream-entity, a shapeshifter. A player who
  reads this and brings a *disguised* harmonic structure — the page's Eidolyn and
  Oneirion note — is doing the same thing more cheaply.
- **Break the body — the geometry of the line.** The Shear is a line, not a
  field: ≤100 m long, <3 cm wide, one per activation, resolved instantaneously.
  Spread. *"Twenty men who have been drilled to spread, to make him choose, and
  to keep a line of fire open at all times will take casualties and kill him."*
  A one-cut-per-activation working against a dispersed formation is a working
  that kills one thing per cast.
- **Break the man — sovereignty as a hard edge.** She cannot cut a being at or
  above her own harmonic sovereignty, which makes *being read correctly* a
  defence: an opponent who knows this fights to be classified as
  uncuttable rather than to be missed.

**The tell, stated plainly.** One hand up, one slow line through the air. The
gesture defines the seam's position and length before the seam exists, so an
opponent who watches the hand knows where not to be standing.

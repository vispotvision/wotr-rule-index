# System account · Chorda Somnii

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** One person's sense of how long a second lasts is
pushed up or down, and their movements land off the beat they intended.

**The real phenomenon.** **Attention-driven duration distortion** — the
measurable, reproducible dissociation between clock time and perceived time. The
brain does not have a clock it reads; it reconstructs duration from the density of
what it encoded over the interval. A novel or high-salience event is encoded more
densely and remembered as having lasted longer (the oddball effect). Under acute
threat, retrospective duration estimates inflate: subjects in instrumented
free-fall overestimated a ~2.5 s drop by roughly a third, while their actual
temporal *resolution* did not improve at all. Saccadic chronostasis stretches the
perceived duration of a post-saccade stimulus by on the order of 120 ms.

The page states this itself and states it correctly: "It runs on the same real
basis that makes a falling body's last second feel long — attention density, not
the clock, sets the felt rate."

**The governing law.** Perceived duration is a monotonic function of encoded
information rate over the interval, not of elapsed time. Push the rate, and the
felt interval moves; the physical clock does not. Consequently **nothing about
the target's reflex hardware changes** — only the schedule their intentions are
issued against.

**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Human temporal-order threshold: two events are judged simultaneous below about
  **20–30 ms** of separation. Flicker fusion at **50–60 Hz**.
- Baseline unaugmented reaction: **150–300 ms**, which the system carries as the
  Hollow band (Part Six).
- Serenyra's own Grade-S reaction band: **0.05–0.2 ms** (Part Six). The target's
  band at A-Grade: **0.2–1 ms**.
- The page's felt-tempo shift, ×0.5 to ×2.0, against a documented natural
  ceiling of about ×1.36 under maximal threat: the working is roughly **1.5×
  past** anything the unaided nervous system produces.
- "Motor timing slips a half-beat": at an A-Grade attack tempo of Mach 10–30, a
  half-beat is on the order of **0.1–0.5 ms** of lead or lag — which is the whole
  width of the A-Grade reaction band, and therefore decisive.

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** Effectively nil. The working
performs no mechanical work on the target: it does not move mass, heat anything,
or change a trajectory. A human cortex dissipates about **20 W**; the intervention
is informational and its power cost is orders below that. **This is the cleanest
example in the batch of the system's own central claim** — "the boundary condition
is cheap in absolute terms and expensive in the only currency a soul has"
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Physical Account Two Sets of Books.md` §III). The
physical account balances at nearly zero joules and the stratal account bills her
for sequence.

**Precedent we are not copying.** Time-dilation techniques in Naruto's Tsukuyomi
and Jojo's Made in Heaven change the world's clock, and Hunter × Hunter's
Emitter tricks change the body. This changes **neither**. The room's clock is
untouched, the target's reflexes are untouched, and the target loses anyway
because they are timing against a ruler that has been bent. That is the divergence,
and it is why the counter is a trained mind rather than a faster body.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** Almost no ambient draw. Limina's favoured conditions are
"Darkness, silence, Aether-thin ground, ruins"; its suppressive conditions are
"Saturated Aether, crowd density, active Domains"
(`The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings`, Environmental Coupling) — a Family
that works *better* in thin Aether, which is consistent with a working that needs
a channel rather than a supply. **Residue:** a thin line, one Crystal to one
Crystal, and the thinnest in this batch — nothing is deposited in the room.
**Saturation:** not a factor at this scale; the limiting resource is her own
sequence, not the district's capacity.

**Wellspring stratum.** **Oneirion · *The Wellspring of Dream***, Family
**Limina**, Physics Domain **entropy, void and mind**. Its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Limina — Entropy, Void and Mind.md`:

> "A mechanical system's every possible arrangement is a coordinate in a space
> that has no physical location, and the system's evolution is a trajectory
> through it. Oneirion lets a working act on that manifold rather than on the
> room. This is the mechanical reason Oneirion techniques reach targets no line of
> sight connects, and the reason they are so difficult to describe afterward."

Register Stat Effect (read through the Merge Ledger, `_method.md` §VI): Gnosis
**Cognition**, Dominion **Sovereignty** (from Reach), Ardency **Cascade** (from
Spectral). Register failure: "**Projection error.** The trajectory was correct in
the manifold and lands nowhere in particular when it is mapped back down into a
room."

*Four Theories.* **Correspondence Position** territory, and the page does not
claim it: a working that acts on the target's own accounting of duration rather
than on duration is two scales of the same law briefly agreeing. The Accord would
file it under Consent regardless, because "a process can be licensed and a
relationship cannot" (`Two Sets of Books` §IV). Noted as a lean, not asserted.

**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Essence Core**, the Soul-Plane layer that
"Holds identity, memory, and Traits" (`The Core Vocabulary` §III) — Memory Shear
and Sleep Debt are both Core costs, not Shell costs, and that is the diagnostic
fingerprint of this working. The thread itself runs through the **Attraction
Layer** (Crystal-to-Crystal, an Essence-lock). Developmental Tier at Serenyra's
Stage VIII: **Radiant**; the technique's own floor is Stage VI, where the Tier is
**Harmonic** — "Techniques stabilize; Wellspring harmonization becomes possible
without tearing the lattice" (Part Twenty-One). **The invoice:** "The thread costs
her the same clock it bends." Memory Shear is misremembered event order — a Gnosis
Retention degradation in the Core. Sleep Debt is Hypnather's business showing up
on an Oneirion bill: recovery that can only be taken quiescent.

**The lens: James's specious present.** William James, *The Principles of
Psychology* (1890), ch. XV, "The Perception of Time". James argued that we never
perceive duration directly: we perceive a *"specious present"* — a short saddle
of time we sit on — and its felt length is set by how much the mind has to
attend to inside it. An hour of novelty is long lived and short remembered; an
hour of boredom is the reverse. Nothing in the clock changes, and everything in
the report does. That is the whole of Oneirion's operation here, and the page
says so in the same words from the other direction: *"attention density, not the
clock, sets the felt rate."* The thread does not manufacture the effect; it
decides which way an already-running accounting gets pushed. The lens also names
the cost before the ledger reaches it — James's present is *stitched* out of
overlapping fringes, and **Memory Shear** is what a stitcher does to itself by
running other people's seams. The useful misreading belongs to a school that
takes the working for time-bending rather than time-**telling**: it will send a
chronometer into the field, find that the clock agrees with every other clock,
and conclude that the affected man is lying or shirking. The chronometer is
answering a question nobody asked. Motor timing slips a half-beat because
intention is timed against the felt present, and the felt present is where she
has her hand.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Tir]` **Hour** · Extension · Valen —
a limit on duration, which is precisely and only what this working sets — laid
across `[Rv]` **Reverie** · Extension · Maelor for the manifold it is set in.
Both from the thirty unclassified forms in
`wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`.
*Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling.*

**What boundary moves.** One quantity: **the rate at which the target's own
accounting of duration advances.** Not the second. Not the reflex. The ratio
between them.

**What the law then does on its own.** Everything downstream is ordinary
neurology and ordinary mechanics. The target issues an intention on the schedule
their felt clock gives them; the body executes it on the schedule the real clock
gives them; the footfall lands early or late by the difference. Nobody slowed the
leg. The leg was told when to move by an instrument that had been re-graduated.
The Index's rule holds: the glyph pins the ratio and the nervous system does the
rest, "including the ones nobody at the bench thought about."

**Law V check.** Stage VI sits **below** Refraction, so the working requires an
external anchor: "Below Stage VII, Refraction, a working requires voice, hand, ink
or blood" (`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). The page supplies one —
"only while she holds eye contact or an active Essence-lock on the target."
**The gate closes cleanly.**

**Failure mode — Boundary fault.** "The condition was established imprecisely and
the law ran somewhere unintended" (`Two Sets of Books` §VII). The pinned quantity
is a *signed ratio*, and Oneirion's own documented failure is projection error on
the map back down into a room. A sign error on the map inverts the ratio.
**That is the mechanism's answer to the page's own "What nobody knows" — why the
thread occasionally slows a target she meant to sharpen.** It is not a fault in
the thread; it is the register's own failure mode arriving with its sign flipped,
and the page's question and the register's answer were always the same fact.

The Limit — nothing without a coherent dream underneath it — is the same
mechanism read from the other side: a construct, a Wellspring husk or a Severed
mind has no configuration-space trajectory with a duration-accounting on it, so
there is no ratio to pin. Not resistance. **Absence of a boundary to move.**

**What the target sees and feels.** A line of current from her hand to him, laid
while she holds his eyes or an active Essence-lock, and then a fault between
intention and body that he cannot locate. Nothing looks slow. Nothing looks fast.
His footsteps land *"before or after they meant them to"* by about half a beat,
and every correction he makes is timed off the same bad reference, so correcting
makes it worse. At the slowed end the world does not appear to crawl; his own
responses simply arrive late to events that seemed to leave him plenty of room.
At the sharpened end he is early, over-committed, and spending twice the effort
for the same ground. The honest tells are all on her side: the thread is visible,
it runs from her hand, and it requires her to keep looking at him — a target who
understands that eye contact is a **component** rather than a menace knows what to
take away. Anything without a coherent dream underneath it feels nothing at all:
no construct, no Wellspring husk, no already-Severed mind. Against those three
the thread does not fail gracefully; it has nowhere to land.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** η ≈ 0.70 (card), so **30 percent of
every expenditure leaves as heat, sound and structural bleed**. Because the
intended effect carries almost no joules, the *absolute* bleed is tiny and the
*relative* bleed is total: nearly everything she spends on this working goes into
holding the thread rather than into the effect, and the structural share of that
bleed is the Memory Shear. At Stage VIII she is at the floor of Tier 6 · Master
(0.70–0.80).

what the standard requires.

## 5 · Counterplay and the challenge

**The fairness check** (`.claude/skills/wotr-write/references/fair-play.md`).

| Test | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Costs something that hurts | **pass** | Two costs, both aimed at the faculty the working is made of. **Memory Shear** — *"she starts misremembering the order of events that just happened"* — degrades sequence in the practitioner whose whole technique is sequence. **Sleep Debt** requires deep, Wellspring-guided sleep afterward *"or risks involuntary dream-bleed mid-fight"*, which is a cost that arrives in the **next** engagement. Neither is priced in EU (`SA-GAP-EU-FORMULA`). |
| Stated limits | **pass** | One thread at a time; single target; 500 m; line of sight **or** an active Essence-link; requires held eye contact or the lock to be maintained; 3 turns; nothing without a coherent dream-life can be threaded. |
| Something beats it | **pass** | A Fate-keyed ward severs it outright; a sufficiently trained mind refuses the tempo; and the sleep debt is exploitable across a long fight. |
| It has a tell | **pass, unusually strong** | A visible line of current from her hand, plus sustained eye contact she cannot break without ending the working. |
| Numbers in band | **pass, one figure unsourced** | Stage VI ⇒ Max Grade A, ceiling 400, Tier 5 · Expert (Part Five) ✓; Gnosis 522 is S, a full Tier above the floor (Part Four). But the tempo shift itself — *"roughly a factor of two"* — is the page's own estimate, *"since the source gives no rate of its own."* In band by construction rather than by attestation. Logged `SA-GAP-CHORDA-TEMPO-RATE`. |

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Break the boundary — the eyes are the anchor.** Stage VI is below
  Refraction, so *"a working requires an external anchor: voice, hand, ink or
  blood"*, and this one requires all of the harder ones at once: a hand to lay
  the thread from and a held gaze to keep it on. *"Put a hand over his mouth"*
  becomes, here, break the line of sight. A Fate-keyed ward does the same job at
  range by reading the thread's shape before it completes — *"Wards … are beaten
  the way Runecraft is beaten"*, and wards are also how you beat a thread.
- **Break the man — Exhaustion, in its one honest form.** The register normally
  warns that *"a plan that consists of surviving until he tires is a plan to be
  killed by a tired man."* This page is the exception it allows for, because the
  cost is not vague fatigue but **dream-bleed mid-fight** on a second
  engagement. Pressing the tempo of the *campaign* rather than the fight is the
  counter, and it is a decision made days earlier.
- **Deny the field — the three immunities.** A construct, a Wellspring husk and
  an already-Severed mind cannot be threaded at all. This is the rare case where
  *"deny the field"* is satisfied by choosing **who walks in** rather than where
  the fight is: send the thing with no dream underneath it. Family **Limina**,
  and the Counterplay register gives no terrain line for Limina
  (`SA-GAP-COUNTERPLAY-TERRAIN`).
- **Break the body — normal, and decisive.** The thread does nothing to flesh,
  armour or reach. A half-beat of motor slip is a serious handicap in a duel and
  almost nothing against *"numbers, correctly used."*

**The tell, stated plainly.** She fixes her eyes on one person and a line runs
from her hand to them. While it runs, she is watching one target and holding one
thread, and both facts are visible to everyone else in the room.

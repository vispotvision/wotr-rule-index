# System account · Coagula Dominion

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** Fifteen metres of air goes thick. Spellwork crossing it
arrives late and weaker. Running through it is like running through syrup. A body
moving on pure momentum passes untouched.

**The real phenomenon.** **The jamming transition of a dense suspension**, and the
page reaches for the right image unprompted ("the way a supersaturated solution locks
into a lattice the instant something gives it a nucleus to organise around").

A suspension of particles in a fluid behaves as a liquid below a critical packing
fraction and as a solid above it. As φ approaches the maximum packing fraction φ_m —
about **0.58 to 0.64** for hard spheres, depending on order — the relative viscosity
diverges. **Krieger–Dougherty:**

> η_r = (1 − φ/φ_m)^(−[η]·φ_m)

with intrinsic viscosity [η] = 2.5 for spheres. The divergence is not gradual near the
end: discontinuous shear thickening in dense cornstarch suspensions raises viscosity by
**10² to 10³ times** across a narrow band of applied stress, which is why a person can
run across such a suspension and sink into it standing still.

**The governing law.** Krieger–Dougherty as above, plus the fact that a jammed medium
resists **shear** and not **translation**: a body already carrying momentum crosses a
jammed region losing energy to viscous dissipation, but is not *held*, while anything
that must continuously do work against the medium — a working drawing through it, a
stride pushing off it — pays the full raised viscosity.

**This is the physical half of the page's discrimination and it is only half.** The
other half is not fluid dynamics: the page's field distinguishes "a spell mid-formation,
a working still drawing" from "ambient background flux," and no viscosity does that.
**Judicium does.** A spectroscopic criterion can tell a live working from background,
and the page is explicit that Judicium "rides the same field as a discernment layer."
**The jamming supplies the resistance; the spectroscopy supplies the selectivity.**
Neither alone produces the page's effect and the page uses both, correctly.

**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Field: **15 m** (page) ⇒ (4/3)π(15 m)³ = **1.41×10⁴ m³**.
- **Grade loss, in joules.** A full Tier Grade down from S to A: mid-band S is
  1.42×10¹³ J and mid-band A is 2.12×10¹² J, a **≈6.7× reduction** in delivered energy.
  At the band extremes the worst case is 2.42672×10¹³ → 4.6024×10¹⁰ J, a **≈528×
  reduction.** The page's own reading — "an S-Grade caster's spellwork behaves like an
  A-Grade caster's" — therefore costs the target between roughly **7× and 528×**,
  depending where in the band they were standing.
- Why one Tier is decisive rather than incremental: Part Four states it outright —
  "**One full Tier Grade above** an opponent in the relevant stat wins a direct
  exchange of that stat category without meaningful contest." **The field does not
  weaken the enemy. It hands Drakvor the exchange.** The page cites the rule and the
  citation holds.
- Viscosity ratio implied by "the air itself go to syrup": golden syrup is on the order
  of **10² Pa·s** against air's **1.8×10⁻⁵ Pa·s** — a factor of **≈5×10⁶**, which is
  well inside what a jamming transition delivers and well past what discontinuous shear
  thickening gives on its own.
- Drag on a stride at that viscosity: Stokes drag on a 0.15 m limb at 3 m/s in a 10²
  Pa·s medium, F = 6πμrv = 6π × 10² × 0.15 × 3 ≈ **850 N** per limb, continuous.
  Against **1.5 N** in air. The page's "has to fight for every stride rather than being
  stopped outright" is that number: survivable, exhausting, and not a wall.

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** In the ambient field, which is the
medium being jammed. Drakvor supplies a nucleation condition and a discrimination
criterion. The joules he personally spends go into **holding** the packing fraction, not
into producing the viscosity — and because he is running the same law through his own
Shell to project it, the page's "double draw" is literal and is where the rib pressure
comes from.

**Precedent we are not copying.** Slow-fields are standard — the Slow spell, Jujutsu
Kaisen's Malevolent Shrine's guaranteed-hit logic, every debuff aura. Those apply a
multiplier to the target. This applies a **property to the medium** and then reads what
is using the medium, which is why the counters are not resistances but **routes**:
Vectoria and Spatium workings never ask the air's permission and pass clean. The
divergence is that the counterplay list is a list of *transport mechanisms*, not of
saving throws.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** This is the batch's most explicitly Aether-stratum working: the
substance being acted on **is** ambient Essence, not matter. Materia is favoured by
"Mineral-rich rock, worked stone, deep foundation" and suppressed by "Open water, high
altitude, moving platforms"; Fulguria wants "Dry air, conductive substrate, clear line
of sight" (`The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings`, Environmental Coupling).
**Residue:** a 1.41×10⁴ m³ volume of Essence that has been held at high packing fraction
does not simply return to background; the district keeps a legible density anomaly.
**Saturation — and this is the important reading:** the working *manufactures* a
saturation condition deliberately. The system defines saturation as "the ceiling past
which a district stops accepting further expression and begins returning it"
(`Two Sets of Books` §II) and defines Aetheric Saturation as "A danger state in which
excessive Essence floods an area faster than it can disperse. **Produces Crystal
Fracture Events in everyone exposed, including the person who caused it**"
(`The Core Vocabulary` §II).

**Coagula Dominion is an engineered local saturation, and the system says engineered
saturation fractures everyone inside it including its author.** The page's rib pressure
is the mildest possible reading of that sentence. Whether the field is Saturation proper
or merely adjacent to it is a ruling, not a deduction: logged
`SA-GAP-COAGULA-SATURATION`.

**Wellspring stratum.** **Coagula · *The Unifying Pulse*** (primary), Family
**Materia**, Physics Domain **material science**. Its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Materia — Material Science.md`:

> "Press powder together and hold it hot, though never hot enough to melt, and the
> particles fuse across their contact points by diffusion alone until the porosity
> closes and the mass behaves as one body. Coagula runs the Soul Plane's version: a
> Crystal that has been fragmented gathers itself without ever passing through a liquid
> state, which is to say without losing the shape it had while broken."

**Judicium · *The Wellspring of Truth*** (secondary), Family **Fulguria** — law quoted
in full in the `Lumen Dissecans` account — supplying the discernment layer.

The page's central move is worth crediting: **Coagula is an inward-gathering current
and Drakvor projects it outward.** The register describes a Crystal gathering *itself*;
the page has him running the same law on the room. The page flags this as unprecedented
in its own Origin note ("any prior use of Coagula in this outward-projected mode" listed
as unattested) and that flag is correct and should stay.

Register Stat Effects, through the Merge Ledger: Coagula gives Resilience **Integrity**
and Tempering **Coherence**; Judicium gives Gnosis **Acuity** and **Analysis**. The page
cites all four and reads them correctly, including the neat observation that "the
Crystal becomes harder to split" is being "projected outward onto the field rather than
inward onto Drakvor's own lattice."

Register failure, Coagula: "**Residual porosity.** Sintering rarely reaches full
density, and the voids that remain are the exact locations at which the next fracture
will initiate." Applied to a field rather than a solid, residual porosity is
**percolation**: a jammed region with unclosed voids has connected paths through it, and
a sufficiently clean single-burst working finds one. **That is precisely the page's own
counterplay — "high-clarity Spirit Path casters who can push a working through in one
clean burst rather than a sustained draw" — and the register predicted it.**

*Four Theories.* **Consent Doctrine**, but with the page's "What nobody knows" sitting
on a genuine edge: "Whether the field reads intent before it thickens, or whether it
would happily seize Drakvor's own allies if he ever lost the thread holding it apart from
them." Under Consent, Judicium reads a *signature*, not an intent, and signatures do not
carry allegiance — **so the honest mechanism answer is that the field does not know his
allies from his enemies and never did; the thread holding them apart is Drakvor, and it
is the only thing doing that work.** Offered as the mechanism's reading; the page's
question is left open where it belongs.

**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Aether Shell**, twice — outward to project and
internally because the same law runs through him. Shell failure states are "Clouding or
Rupture" (`The Core Vocabulary` §III); sustained rib-deep compression in a conduit
running a coagulating law on itself is Clouding with a timer on it. Developmental Tier at
Stage VIII: **Radiant**. Crystal State: **Refined, overbuilt by design** (card).
**The invoice:** a standing draw, worsening with duration, plus the double-draw the page
names. **This is the correct invoice shape for a sustained field and the page is one of
only three in the batch to get it right.**

**The lens: *solve et coagula*.** The alchemical axiom of the Latin corpus —
"dissolve and coagulate" — read through Basil Valentine's *Azoth* (1613) and the
Emerald Tablet tradition behind it. The operation the alchemists cared about was
never coagulation alone but the **pair**: matter is dissolved to loosen its form
and then fixed again, and neither half is an achievement without the other. The
whole of Coagula's Core Law is the second half of that axiom done to Essence, and
Drakvor's contribution is to run it **outward** rather than inward — the current
that would gather a fractured Crystal back into one piece instead fixes the loose
Essence of a fifteen-metre sphere, *"the way a supersaturated solution locks into
a lattice the instant something gives it a nucleus to organise around."* The
alchemists would have recognised the nucleus immediately; a seed crystal is
exactly what their *coagula* step called for. The instructive misreading follows
from taking the axiom seriously as a cycle. A school that reads the Dominion as a
*coagula* operation will look for the *solve* that undoes it, and will bring a
dissolving working to thin the field — meeting the medium on its own ground,
which is the one approach the page rules out, since *"high-Tier Wellsprings
overpower the coagulation outright"* only if they are already overwhelming. The
answer the page actually gives is not to dissolve the medium but to **stop using
it**: fly, or step. The lens teaches a practitioner to fight the field. The field
is not the working's weakness; the medium is.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Knt]` **Knot** · Counter · Maelor — the
other of the Index's only two Counter forms, and its description is this working:
"**Knot** entangles a path so it cannot be traced cleanly." A field that makes workings
arrive late and undersized has entangled their paths, not blocked them. Laid across
`[RenB]` **Boundary** · Root · Zhaeren for the 15 m edge. Both from the thirty
unclassified forms in
`wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`. Note the
Index's own framing of why counters are scarce: "A glyph cannot attack a law … What a
counter does instead is impose a boundary that **prevents another boundary from being
established**" — which is exactly what this field does to a working mid-formation.
*Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling.*

**What boundary moves.** Two, as on `Bastion Imperium`: **the packing fraction of
ambient Essence inside 15 m**, and **the criterion that decides what counts as
load-bearing flux**. The first is Coagula, the second Judicium, and the page's Category —
Vectra, "a working about where force and motion are allowed to go" — is the correct
filing for the pair.

**What the law then does on its own.** All of it, and the page's Limitation is the law
speaking rather than a restriction imposed. A jammed medium can only act on what uses
the medium: a sealed working has stopped drawing and is invisible to the field; a body on
inertia is translating rather than shearing and passes; a Vectoria or Spatium working
never entered the medium at all. **The page lists three exemptions and they are one
sentence of fluid dynamics.**

**Law V check, and a choice.** Stage VIII sits above Refraction, so no material anchor
is required (`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). The page declares anyway and
adds a condition the Craft page predicts: the working "holds only while Drakvor's
attention holds it," which is the Four Crafts page's own ephemerality clause — "the
sentence is still ephemeral — which is why the fist stops burning the moment attention
lapses." **Consistent.**

**Failure mode — Structural fault, primary; Boundary fault, secondary.**
Structural: "The Crystal could not hold what the boundary permitted. Overchannel,
Backlash, Fracture" (`Two Sets of Books` §VII). He is running the coagulating law
through his own Shell for the field's whole duration, and the page's own line is the
diagnosis: "it does not distinguish between the two." Rib pressure that worsens with
duration is a Shell under a load with no release valve.
Boundary: the field's edge is a medium, so everything that does not use the medium is
outside a boundary that was never drawn around it.

**What the target sees and feels.** Two different fights, depending on what you
are made of. A caster inside the radius does not lose his working — he watches it
*arrive*, and arrive **late and undersized**, one full Tier Grade down from what
he paid for. That is the cruel part, because it is not a failure he can diagnose
mid-cast: the sentence completes, the Seal closes, and the result is an A-Grade
result in an S-Grade man's hands. Per the standing differential rule a one-Tier
gap *"wins a direct exchange of that stat category without meaningful contest"*,
so the field does not need to stop anything; it needs only to make him the lesser
man for as long as he stands in it. Anyone crossing on foot feels *"the air
itself go to syrup"* and pays proportionately more Essence per stride — fought,
not stopped. And the exemptions are visible from outside and worth memorising: a
working already sealed inside a Crystal passes untouched, and a body moving on
**pure muscular momentum** is invisible to the field entirely. A man who runs
through on his own legs and swings his own arm has not been slowed at all.
Meanwhile Drakvor is standing in the middle of it with the same law running
through his own Shell, and the pressure behind his ribs is getting worse the
longer he holds it.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** η ≈ 0.55 (card): **45 percent** as heat,
sound and structural bleed, across 1.41×10⁴ m³, continuously, for as long as he holds
it. As on `Bastion Imperium`, 0.55 is **below Tier 6 · Master's 0.70–0.80 floor**
(`SA-NUM-ETA-TIER`), and here the card's escape clause does not apply: a Coagula
Dominion is not a bastion he has personally tuned, it is a field thrown over ground he
happens to be standing on. **On this page he is at his worst efficiency doing his most
sustained work, and the rib pressure is that arithmetic.**

## 5 · Counterplay and the challenge

**The fairness check** (`.claude/skills/wotr-write/references/fair-play.md`).

| Test | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Costs something that hurts | **pass, and honestly built** | *"Drakvor is running Coagula's law through his own Shell at the same time as the field, and it does not distinguish between the two."* A **double draw**, located in tissue (rib-deep pressure) and explicitly *"worsening the longer it is sustained"*. The page even labels it: *"This is not decorative."* Unpriced in EU (`SA-GAP-EU-FORMULA`) and with no stated duration, so it cannot be turned into a countdown — the one weak seam. |
| Stated limits | **pass** | 15 m sphere; declared, not reflexive, and requires committed attention plus a standing draw; **reaches only Essence loose in the air**; a working already sealed in a Crystal is exempt; a body on inertia is exempt; high-Tier Wellsprings overpower it. |
| Something beats it | **pass, and cheaply** | The page names the route in its own Counter: anything that *"never asks the air's permission."* |
| It has a tell | **pass** | Declared, sustained, and the medium visibly changes — the air goes to syrup for everyone including his own side. |
| Numbers in band | **pass** | Stage VIII ⇒ Max Grade S, ceiling 550, Tier 6 (Part Five); card Tempering 515 / Dominion 500 / Gnosis 430 all S ✓; the one-Tier output penalty is the Fracture's own standing differential rule (Part Four) rather than a number the page invented. **η 0.55 against the Tier 6 floor of 0.70 is out of band** and was logged before this section (`SA-NUM-ETA-TIER`); the card's *"above 0.8 inside bastions he has personally tuned"* does not cover this working. |

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Deny the field — by routing, which is the page's own answer.** The field
  thickens **ambient Essence**. A working that routes through Vectoria or Spatium
  instead — flight, blink-step — *"never touches the medium Drakvor is
  thickening."* This is *"choose the ground by Family"* run one level deeper: not
  which room, but which medium. The register's Materia line, *"opinions and no
  tools on open water"*, is the room-level version of the same move.
- **Break the body — on inertia, with no Essence at all.** The field is
  invisible to *"a body moving under its own momentum rather than Essence-borne
  lift."* A thrown spear, a fired shot, a man sprinting on his own legs. *"Shot.
  Chemically cheap, mechanically simple."* The single cheapest counter in the
  batch, and it is written into the page's own Limitation.
- **Break the boundary — one clean burst instead of a sustained draw.** The
  page's Counterplay names *"high-clarity Spirit Path casters who can push a
  working through in one clean burst rather than a sustained draw"*: the
  discernment layer thickens around currents that are still drawing, so a working
  that is finished before the field has read it is not a load-bearing current to
  thicken. Sealing early, on purpose, beats a field that taxes duration —
  compare *"Force the Seal early"* in the register, used offensively here rather
  than against him.
- **Break the man — Rigidity and attention.** The field is declared and holds
  only while he commits attention to it. Give him two things worth attending to
  outside the fifteen metres and the field is the one that lapses, because he
  cannot follow either of them without leaving it.

**The tell, stated plainly.** He declares it, he commits to a standing draw, and
the air inside fifteen metres goes visibly and audibly thick. Everyone inside
knows, including his allies, who are casting in the same syrup.

# System account · Corona Lunaris

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The effect in plain words.** A half-dome around one body returns incoming
energy instead of letting it through, and the fraction it cannot return leaves as
light.

**The real phenomenon.** A **high-reflectivity boundary with a finite loss
term** — in engineering terms a dielectric or Bragg mirror, in optics a surface
operating past its critical angle. A mirror does not absorb a beam and then make
a new one; it returns the wave with angle-in equal to angle-out, and the small
fraction it fails to return is absorbed and re-radiated. The page states the
principle correctly: "Runs on the same principle that lets a mirror return a beam
instead of absorbing it." Aurevane's register analogue is the coherence side of
the same physics: "Optical resolution and coherence. The Rayleigh criterion;
phase-locked emission"
(`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Fulguria — Electromagnetism.md`).

**The governing law.** The **Fresnel equations** at a boundary between media, and
**total internal reflection** past the critical angle θ_c = arcsin(n₂/n₁).
Reflectance is a function of incidence angle and of the index mismatch, and it
rises toward unity as the angle steepens. Two consequences follow without further
assumption, and both appear on the page: reflectance is **angle-dependent**, so a
strike arriving off the designed angle is returned less well; and the non-returned
fraction must be **re-emitted**, because energy is conserved.

**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Threshold the page sets: a running total of **4.6×10¹⁰ J (46 GJ)** before the
  corona shatters. This is the B-Grade energy ceiling exactly (Part Four:
  1.046×10⁹ – 4.6024×10¹⁰ J), so the figure is correctly derived from the Stage.
- Corona geometry: a personal half-sphere at r ≈ 1.5 m has surface area
  2πr² = **14.1 m²**.
- **Areal load at threshold:** 4.6×10¹⁰ J ÷ 14.1 m² = **3.26×10⁹ J/m²**.
- Shed over the page's stated 2-turn hold, taken at 10 s: radiant exitance
  = 3.26×10⁹ ÷ 10 = **3.26×10⁸ W/m²**. The solar constant is 1,361 W/m²; this is
  **≈ 2.4×10⁵ times sunlight**.
- Stefan–Boltzmann inversion, σ = 5.670×10⁻⁸ W·m⁻²·K⁻⁴:
  T = (3.26×10⁸ / 5.670×10⁻⁸)^¼ = **≈ 8,700 K**.
- Wien peak at that temperature: λ_max = 2.898×10⁻³ m·K ÷ 8,700 K = **333 nm**,
  i.e. **UVA**, not visible.

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** In the attack. The corona
supplies a boundary and returns what arrives; Serenyra never has to match the
incoming joules, which is why a Stage V working can stand against a B-Grade
strike at all.

**But the budget does not close on the page's own description.** The page says the
non-returned fraction is "shed as harmless light." At 46 GJ across a 14.1 m²
personal half-sphere, the shed fraction radiates at ≈8,700 K with its spectral
peak at 333 nm. That is hotter than the solar photosphere (5,772 K), it is
UV-dominated, and it is not harmless to anything standing in it — including the
person inside the dome. Either the threshold is lower, or the corona is much
larger, or the shed energy leaves by a route the page has not stated. Logged as
`SA-PHYS-CORONA-HARMLESS-LIGHT`; **not resolved here.**

**Precedent we are not copying.** Reflective barriers in Bleach's Blut Vene and
the reflect-shields of most CRPG traditions bounce a spell back at its caster for
free. This one does not bounce it back. It **re-radiates**, and the re-radiation
is the working's own liability — the brighter the defence, the easier she is to
find and the worse the room is to stand in. The cost is downstream of conservation
of energy rather than of a balance patch. That is the divergence.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** Draw is local and modest — a personal half-sphere, not a
field. Fulguria's favoured ground is "Dry air, conductive substrate, clear line of
sight"; its suppressive ground is "Faraday-caged interiors, heavy ferrous
shielding, dense fog"
(`The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings`, Environmental Coupling). Fog is the
relevant one: a scattering medium destroys the coherence Aurevane depends on.
**Residue:** bright and short-lived. An Aurevane working leaves the most
*immediately* legible trace in the register and the least *durable* one — the
light goes, and what remains is a phase signature. **Saturation:** the working's
own shatter threshold is a saturation limit in miniature — a running total past
which the boundary "stops accepting further expression and begins returning it"
(`Two Sets of Books` §II), except that here it stops returning and fails.

**Wellspring stratum.** **Aurevane · *The Wellspring of Light***, Family
**Fulguria**, Physics Domain **electromagnetism**. Its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Fulguria — Electromagnetism.md`:

> "Two points are resolvable only when their diffraction patterns separate, and
> coherent light holds a fixed phase relationship across its whole front, which is
> what makes a laser a laser rather than a bright lamp. Aurevane raises both
> figures at once. What the practitioner perceives resolves finer, and what they
> express leaves the Shell in phase, meaning the output matches the intent with
> almost no distortion introduced in transit."

Register Stat Effect, through the Merge Ledger: Gnosis **Perception**, Gnosis
**Vigilance**, Harmonics **Attunement** (from Fidelity). Register failure:
"**Glare.** Coherent output is also a beacon, and Aurevane practitioners are the
easiest people in any engagement to locate."

**The 8,700 K figure above is Glare stated in kelvin.** The register's failure and
the physical account's problem are the same fact arriving from two directions,
which is what a properly kept pair of books looks like.

*Four Theories.* **Consent Doctrine**, enforced and unstrained. Nothing on the
page attributes intention to Aurevane; a boundary is set and a law behaves.

**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Aether Shell**, the Material-Plane layer
that "Converts inner truth into outward force," whose failure state is "Clouding
or Rupture" (`The Core Vocabulary` §III). Both failure states are on this page:
Lunar Burn is Clouding (her own pathways burned, headaches and light sensitivity
after), and the corona shattering at threshold is Rupture. Developmental Tier at
Serenyra's Stage VIII: **Radiant**; at the technique's Stage V floor:
**Harmonic**. Crystal State: **Refined** (card). **The invoice:** Lunar Burn is a
Shell-conductivity cost paid in tissue — "because the Aether Shell is the
Material-Plane layer, everything a Crystal does eventually shows in tissue"
(`Two Sets of Books` §V), and light sensitivity after a working built on
coherent emission is that sentence doing its job. Crown Fatigue is not an Essence
cost at all: it is the focus's own thermal recovery, which is a physical-account
line, and the page is right to keep it separate.

## 3 · Mechanism

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[RenB]` **Boundary** · Root ·
Zhaeren — the exact form for a working whose entire content is *where the
interface sits* — laid across `[On]` **Light** · Root · Maelor. Both from the
thirty unclassified forms in
`wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`.
*Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling.*

**What boundary moves.** One quantity: **the reflectance of the surface at
r ≈ 1.5 m around a body.** Air has no such surface. The glyph puts one there.

**What the law then does on its own.** All of it. Angle-in equals angle-out
because that is what a reflecting boundary does; the non-returned fraction is
re-emitted because energy is conserved; the emission is bright because the load is
large; the emission is in the ultraviolet because the load divided by the area
divided by σ comes out at 8,700 K. **Nobody decided the corona would burn.** The
Index's sentence is the operative one: "Everything a working produces goes on
obeying every rule it has ever obeyed, including the ones nobody at the bench
thought about."

**Law V check.** Stage V sits below Refraction, so an external anchor is
required (`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). The page supplies two —
hand ("a touch to the Lunarch Crown") and voice ("a spoken command").
**The gate closes cleanly, and with redundancy.**

**Failure mode — Boundary fault, with a Field fault behind it.** Boundary:
reflectance is angle-dependent, so "a strike from many directions at once
outruns what the corona's refraction can resolve" is not a resource problem but a
geometry problem — a single surface cannot be optimally oriented to several
incidence angles at once. Field: a Void-aligned strike "passes through the
reflective law rather than obeying it," which is the room failing to supply the
index mismatch the boundary assumed.

The page's "What nobody knows" — why a Darkness-aligned current that feeds on the
shed light sometimes leaves the corona *stronger* — has a mechanism-level reading
worth putting in front of Isaac rather than asserting: an absorber that removes
the shed fraction is **removing the working's own thermal load**, and a mirror
whose loss term is being drained runs closer to unity reflectance. On that
reading the Darkness current is not feeding on the corona; it is cooling it.
Offered as a candidate, not as canon.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** η ≈ 0.70 (card), so **30 percent of
every expenditure leaves as heat, sound and structural bleed**. Here the bleed is
not incidental — it is the working's visible output, and the page says so. The
corona *is* the bleed, made bright. At Stage VIII she sits at the floor of
Tier 6 · Master (0.70–0.80).

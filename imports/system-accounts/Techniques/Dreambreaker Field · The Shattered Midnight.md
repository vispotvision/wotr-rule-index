# System account · Dreambreaker Field · The Shattered Midnight

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The effect in plain words.** He claims forty metres of ground and inside it attacks
travel on bent lines, timing stops being trustworthy, and nobody can read what a working
just cost them.

**The real phenomenon.** **A gradient-index medium** — and the honest reading is that
the page's four listed distortions come from **two** different physics, not one.

*Curved trajectories: gradient-index optics, which is to say a mirage.* A ray in a medium
with a spatially varying refractive index does not travel straight. It follows the path
of least optical time, and in a medium with ∇n ≠ 0 that path is curved with radius of
curvature **R = n / |∇n|**. No force acts on the ray. Nothing pushes it. **The geometry
of "straight" changed, and the ray obeyed the new one.** This is Fermat's principle, it
is how a desert mirage and a GRIN lens both work, and it is the exact physics for
"curved attack vectors."

*Distorted perception and unreliable felt timing: not optics.* The optical time
difference across such a field is negligible — see the arithmetic below — so the field's
temporal distortion has to be the attention-density mechanism documented in the
`Chorda Somnii` account, not an effect on light. **Two mechanisms, honestly separated.**
The page bundles them and the physics does not.

**The governing law.** **Fermat's principle**: a ray's path extremises
∫ n(**r**) ds. Consequences that need nothing added: the bending is smooth rather than
abrupt; it is reciprocal, so *his* attacks bend too; and it is indifferent to allegiance,
which is the page's own Consequence line — "Nobody inside the radius, allies included,
can trust their own aim."

**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Field: **40 m** radius (page) ⇒ (4/3)π(40 m)³ = **2.68×10⁵ m³**.
- **Index gradient needed to bend a trajectory 10° across the field.**
  R ≈ 40 m ÷ 0.175 rad = **229 m**, so |∇n|/n = 1/229 = **4.4×10⁻³ m⁻¹**. Integrated
  across 40 m that is a total index change of **0.175** — n running from 1.000 to 1.175.
  Glass is 1.5, so the gradient is large but not exotic.
- **For scale: atmospheric mirage gradients run about 10⁻⁸ m⁻¹.** The field is therefore
  roughly **10⁵ times steeper than a desert mirage** — which is the honest measure of how
  hard it is to hold, and why the duration is seconds rather than minutes.
- **Optical time difference across the field:**
  Δt = L·Δn/c = 40 × 0.175 ÷ 3×10⁸ = **2.3×10⁻⁸ s (23 ns)**. **Twenty-three
  nanoseconds is nothing** — six orders of magnitude below the fastest reaction band in
  the system (S-Grade, 0.05–0.2 ms, Part Six). **The field's "irregular time dilation"
  cannot be an optical effect, and this number is the proof.**
- Duration: **≈15 s** (page's estimate, "3 turns" at 5 s/turn).
- Bounded by Stage VII: Sub-Stat ceiling **475**, Max Grade **A**, allocation stopping at
  400 (Part Five) — the page states "Bounded by Stage VII's 475 ceiling, Grade A" and
  that is correct.

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** In the field. Bending a ray costs the
medium nothing — a GRIN lens is passive. What is expensive is **maintaining a
10⁵-times-atmospheric index gradient across 2.68×10⁵ m³ against its own tendency to
diffuse flat**, and that is a *power* cost, continuous, which is why the page's cost is
carried "while he holds it" rather than paid once.

**Precedent we are not copying.** Reality-warping arenas are the genre's arms race —
Domain Expansion, the Reality Marble, Bleach's inner worlds. Those replace the world's
rules with the caster's. **This one does not replace anything. It makes two descriptions
both true and lets them disagree**, which is why it hurts him as much as anyone and why
the page says the field might be dreaming him. The divergence is symmetry: there is no
safe seat inside.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** Draw across 2.68×10⁵ m³, continuous. Limina is favoured by
"Darkness, silence, Aether-thin ground, ruins" and suppressed by "Saturated Aether,
crowd density, active Domains"; Fluxia is "Medium primary · Spirit at high
concentration," favoured by "Standing or moving water, humidity, pressure differential"
(`The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings`, Environmental Coupling). **"Active
Domains" is the load-bearing suppression and the page's own Limitation:** "Cannot be laid
over an existing Domain, since two claims on the same Layer do not both hold." Register
and page agree. **Residue:** the page gives it a name and a decay — "the field leaves a
residue that bleeds into the ground for a time after the fight ends (Dream Residue)" —
and the card describes Aeldoris himself as "Refined, **residue-heavy**," which is the
practitioner's own condition matching his workings' signature. That consistency is worth
noting; it is rare in the batch. **Saturation:** the field is held *against* diffusion, so
its own gradient is what fails first; saturation of the district is not the limit.

**Wellspring stratum.** **Eidolyn · *The Shaping Mind*** (primary), Family **Limina**,
Physics Domain **entropy, void and mind**. Its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Limina — Entropy, Void and Mind.md`:

> "A supersaturated medium will not organise itself, but given a template it deposits
> along the template's geometry with high fidelity and considerable strength. Eidolyn
> makes intent the template. The construct's solidity is real material behaviour rather
> than illusion, and its failure modes are the failure modes of a poorly designed
> casting."

**Oneirion · *The Wellspring of Dream*** — law quoted in full in the `Chorda Somnii`
account — supplying the configuration-space routing that the page correctly assigns to
"distance and timing." **Somnalis · *The Dream Veil***, Family **Fluxia**, Physics Domain
**fluid dynamics**; its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Fluxia — Fluid Dynamics.md`:

> "A turbulent system cannot be predicted from a single initial condition, so forecasters
> run many slightly perturbed copies and read the spread rather than any one trajectory.
> Somnalis gives the practitioner that ensemble directly: adjacent possibilities perceived
> as a distribution with weights, which is why its bearers speak about the future in
> probabilities and are correct more often than people who speak in certainties."

**Somnalis is why Aeldoris can navigate his own field and nobody else can** — he is
reading the ensemble while everyone else is inside one member of it. The page says this
("letting him read which adjacent possibility the field is currently enforcing") and it
is the correct use of the current.

Register failures in play. Eidolyn's "**Template defect.** Any flaw in the intent is
faithfully reproduced at scale, and the practitioner is rarely able to see the flaw until
it is standing in front of him." Somnalis's "**Ensemble collapse.** The spread is mistaken
for a single reading, and the practitioner acts on a branch that carried eleven percent of
the weight." **Double Vision is the second of those beginning.**

*Four Theories.* **Consent Doctrine** on the page's own terms — a claim is made on the
Layer and recognised or not. But the page's "What nobody knows" ("Whether the field is
dreaming him as much as he is dreaming it") is a **Correspondence** question — "The
practitioner and the Wellspring are the same law expressed at two scales, and the working
is the two scales briefly agreeing" (`Two Sets of Books` §IV). A reciprocal field in which
the caster is a participant is exactly what Correspondence describes. **Noted, not
resolved.**

**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Attraction Layer** — Domain Weaving is a claim
on the Fate-Plane layer, and the page's mechanism paragraph opens on exactly that
("Domain Weaving works by the Attraction Layer claiming a radius and continuing to
recognise that claim until it is broken outright, which is why the field holds evenly and
fails all at once rather than fraying at the edges"). Attraction-Layer failure state is
**Obsession Force** (`The Core Vocabulary` §III), the hazard of a claim that cannot be
released. Developmental Tier at Stage VII: **Resonant** — "The Crystal self-conducts.
Power moves even in silence" (Part Twenty-One), and a field held without continuous
declaration needs exactly that. Crystal State: **Refined, residue-heavy** (card).
**The invoice:** Double Vision, which is his own Shell carrying the field's contradiction
— a sensory cost, correctly located, and the physical account agrees: a practitioner
inside his own GRIN medium sees through it.

## 3 · Mechanism

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Et]` **Mirage** · Root · Kaetra. This is
the single best glyph fit in the batch: a gradient-index medium producing curved rays
**is a mirage**, and `[Et]` is one of the thirty unclassified Roots the Index says name
"a **kind of limit**, applicable to any law at all." Laid across `[RenB]` **Boundary** ·
Root · Zhaeren for the 40 m claim. Both from
`wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`.
*Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling.*

**What boundary moves.** One quantity: **the index gradient inside a 40 m sphere.**
Everything the page lists as an effect on aim, on trajectory and on distance is that one
number. The temporal and perceptual distortions are a **second** boundary and the page
does not separate them; this account does.

**What the law then does on its own.** All of the geometry, and reciprocally. Rays bend
because Fermat's principle says they must; the field holds evenly because an index
gradient is a field quantity and not a perimeter; **it fails all at once rather than
fraying because a claim on the Attraction Layer is binary while the gradient it sustains
is not** — so when the claim goes, the gradient diffuses flat in one relaxation time. The
page asserts that behaviour and the mechanism supplies the reason. A Body Path anchor
resists because an anchor holds to physical law and a GRIN medium does not alter physical
law, only the *route*; a clear-light Domain disrupts it because a second claim on one
Layer is the one thing the mechanism cannot survive.

**Law V check, and a choice at the exact threshold.** Stage VII **is** Refraction — the
Stage at which "the Glyph Roles compress into an internal chain that needs no material
anchor at all" (`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). Aeldoris is standing exactly
on the gate. The page says "Declared," so he uses the anchor. **At the one Stage where
the choice first exists, he takes the cheaper, louder option** — which is consistent with
a Stage VII practitioner who has just arrived rather than one who has settled in, and with
the card's Catalyst Event being "First Domain seed activation, however fragile."

**Failure mode — Field fault, primary; Boundary fault, secondary.**
Field: "The room could not supply what the working assumed" (`Two Sets of Books` §VII).
Ground already claimed by a Domain, or a Titan's presence, leaves the working correct and
unable to take hold — and the page says the claim is *unmade*, not weakened, which is what
a binary claim does. Boundary: the field is indiscriminate because an index gradient has no
allegiance, and the page's own Consequence states the cost — allies included.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency — and the card is above its band.** The card gives
**η ≈ 0.65**, so **35 percent** of every expenditure leaves as heat, sound and structural
bleed. But Stage VII places Aeldoris in **Tier 5 · Expert**, whose band is **0.50–0.60**
(Part Nineteen). **0.65 is above his tier's ceiling by 0.05.** Part Nineteen does offer a
route — Tier 5 covers "V–VII Splintering to Refraction" and notes "Dual sight at Refraction
allows real-time observation and correction of waste," so a practitioner at the top of the
tier who has just gained dual sight is exactly who would run hot against the table. That
reading is available and unstated. Recorded as `SA-NUM-ETA-TIER`; not resolved.

# System account · Edictum Resonantiae

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** Inside a large dome, one named kind of action stops
working, or works but arrives as something milder. Nothing intercepts it. It simply
does not resolve the way it would have.

**The real phenomenon.** A **selection rule**. In a quantum system, certain
transitions do not occur — not because something blocks them, but because the
transition matrix element between the initial and final state is zero. The system
is not prevented. There is nothing there to prevent. And when the direct channel is
forbidden, the system does not stop: **it takes the next-available higher-order
channel, at a hugely suppressed rate.**

The canonical case is the metastable 2s state of hydrogen. The 2s→1s electric-dipole
transition is forbidden by parity; the state cannot decay the fast way. It decays
anyway, by two-photon emission, with a lifetime of about **0.12 s** against the
**1.6 ns** of an allowed 2p→1s decay — a suppression of roughly **7×10⁷**. The
outcome still arrives. It arrives late, by a different route, and much weaker.

**That is the Edict, exactly.** A lethal blow becomes a disarm because the lethal
channel has a zero matrix element inside the dome and the blow proceeds through the
allowed channel that remains. The page's own framing is correct and unusually
precise: "the Edict, not Serenyra, is what refuses."

**The governing law.** **Fermi's golden rule**,
Γ = (2π/ℏ)·|⟨f|H′|i⟩|²·ρ(E_f). Zero the matrix element for the named final state
and the rate to that state goes to zero while the density of other final states is
untouched. Everything the page describes — the action "either fails outright or
resolves into the Edict's named alternative" — is that one equation with one term
set to zero.

**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Dome: **200 m** radius (page). Hemispherical volume (2/3)π(200 m)³ =
  **1.68×10⁷ m³**. This is the largest single claimed volume among Serenyra's six.
- Boundary transit at S-Grade travel speed (8,575–68,600 m/s, Part Six): a body
  entering at the low end crosses 200 m in **23 ms**.
- The page's "resistance, as though pushing against water": dynamic viscosity of
  water is 1.0×10⁻³ Pa·s against air's 1.8×10⁻⁵ Pa·s, a factor of **≈55**; but the
  felt resistance is inertial drag, F = ½ρC_dAv², and there the ratio is the density
  ratio, **≈816**. A 0.1 m² forearm swung at 5 m/s meets **1.5 N** in air and
  **1.25×10³ N** in water. The simile is a good one and it is worth **three orders of
  magnitude**, not one.
- Suppression factor, borrowing the hydrogen case as the physical scale: **≈10⁷–10⁸**
  on the forbidden channel. Offered as the order of magnitude the analogue implies,
  not as a system figure.

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** In the attacker, and they stay
there. A selection rule costs nothing to enforce because it is not an intervention
in the energy account at all — it is a statement about which final states are
reachable. This is the strongest case in the batch for the system's own line: "The
practitioner does not supply the joules. They supply the boundary condition"
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Physical Account Two Sets of Books.md` §III). Holding a
1.68×10⁷ m³ dome is not expensive in joules. It is expensive in *coherence*, and the
page bills her there.

**Precedent we are not copying.** Rule-imposing domains are the genre's current
centre of gravity — Jujutsu Kaisen's Domain Expansion, Bleach's Bankai, the
Reality Marble. Those impose a rule **and guarantee its effect**. This one imposes a
rule and **lets the world route around it**, which is why a broadly worded Edict is
*weaker* than a narrow one: a wide prohibition leaves many allowed channels open. The
divergence is that the working's strength is a property of its wording rather than
of its caster, and the page says so: "the wording is the working."

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** A 1.68×10⁷ m³ claim, and the draw is for *even* enforcement
across all of it rather than for peak output anywhere — which is why Aurevane is
carried as secondary and the page says so ("carries the enforcement evenly across
the whole dome rather than only near her"). Fulguria favours "Dry air, conductive
substrate, clear line of sight" and is suppressed by "Faraday-caged interiors, heavy
ferrous shielding, dense fog." **Residue:** a lingering echo the page gives its own
duration — "a weaker lingering echo for one to two exchanges afterward" — which is
residue with a decay constant, the most honest residue line in the batch.
**Saturation:** the relevant hazard is not Aetheric but jurisdictional: "Another
lawcaster can declare an opposing principle; the two resonate against each other and
can collapse both." Two claims on one Layer is the Domain-Weaving failure, and the
page has it right.

**Wellspring stratum.** **Judicium · *The Wellspring of Truth***, governing;
**Aurevane · *The Wellspring of Light***, secondary; **Oneirion · *The Wellspring of
Dream***, minor. Families **Fulguria** (governing, secondary) and **Limina** (minor).
Judicium's law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Fulguria — Electromagnetism.md`:

> "Every element absorbs and emits at wavelengths fixed by its own electron
> structure, and no substance can present another's spectrum. Judicium tunes Gnosis
> to that principle: the practitioner reads the characteristic lines of a Crystal, a
> technique, or a lie, and identifies composition from signature alone. Eddy-current
> inspection finds the crack under the paint without removing the paint."

Judicium is what **recognises** the named action — the selection rule needs a
criterion, and a spectroscopic signature is a criterion. Oneirion's law (quoted in
full in the `Chorda Somnii` account) supplies what the page calls "conceptual
flexibility," and in the physical reading it is exactly the right current for it: a
configuration-space law is what lets the rule apply to a *class* of trajectories
rather than to one enumerated list, so an action falling just outside the letter of
the phrasing is still inside the manifold the rule was written on.

Register failures in play: Judicium's "**Spectral overload.** Every object in view
reports its composition simultaneously and the practitioner loses the ability to
prioritise any of it" — which is the mechanical reason a broad Edict is weak.
Aurevane's "**Glare**" — an evenly enforced dome is a beacon 200 m across.

*Four Theories.* **Consent Doctrine**, and the page is almost a demonstration of
why the Accord enforces it: an Edict is a *licensable process*, which is precisely
the Doctrine's stated advantage — "This is the position that permits licensing,
because a process can be licensed and a relationship cannot"
(`Two Sets of Books` §IV).

**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Attraction Layer**, the Fate-Plane layer
that "Holds bonds, Domains, and Wellspring harmonizations," whose failure state is
"Obsession Force" (`The Core Vocabulary` §III). **That failure state is the
technique's real risk and the page does not name it:** a caster who cannot release
an Edict is the textbook Obsession case — "When a practitioner cannot release
judgment, lineage, memory, or revelation, recognition curdles into fixation —
sustaining corrupted techniques through refusal rather than harmony, at escalating
soul-cost" (`The Core Vocabulary` §II). Developmental Tier at Stage VIII:
**Radiant** — "The Attraction Layer gains territorial durability. The first true
Domain becomes possible" (Part Twenty-One). Crystal State: **Refined** (card).
**The invoice:** she is the enforcement term. "She must personally embody the Edict
for as long as it stands; violating its own spirit herself returns the backlash
directly to her Soul Crystal." The roughened voice across repeated castings is
Shell degradation on the conduit that carries recognition.

**The lens: Austin's performatives.** J. L. Austin, *How to Do Things with Words*
(1955 lectures, published 1962). Austin's insight was that some utterances do not
describe the world but **alter** it — *I name this ship*, *I sentence you* — and
that they work not by force but by satisfying **felicity conditions**: the right
person, the right circumstances, the right form of words, and a speaker who means
it. Read the Edict through that and the page's rules stop looking arbitrary. *"The
narrower and clearer its wording, the stronger the Edict holds"* is Austin's
insistence on correct form. *"She must personally embody the Edict for its
duration"* is his sincerity condition, which he treated as the one that cannot be
faked. *"The Edict, not Serenyra, is what refuses"* is exactly Austin's point
that a performative, once felicitous, stands on its own and not on the speaker's
continuing strength. The place the lens fails is worth a great deal, because it
fails in the direction of a bad idea. In Austin an infelicitous performative is
simply **void** — it misfires, nothing happens, and no one is hurt. Here, when the
speaker herself violates the Edict's spirit, *"the backlash tears through her own
Soul Crystal."* A school reading the working as pure speech-act therefore
concludes that a lawcaster who wants out of her own Edict need only break it and
watch it lapse harmlessly, and that school will produce broken Crystals. The
Edict is not a convention held up by agreement. It is a debt she has signed, and
the Counterplay register has a whole paragraph about what that means
(**"The vow"**).

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Ath]` **Command** · Root · Irath,
laid across `[Ho]` **Order** · Extension · Valen. From the thirty unclassified forms
in `wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`, which
notes of this station: "**Irath holds five forms and two of them are Command and
Dominion**, both unclassified. *The smallest station in the index carries the two
heaviest words in it.*" An Edict is the clearest candidate in the batch for
`[Ath]`. *Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling.*

**What boundary moves.** One quantity: **the set of final states reachable by one
named class of action inside the dome.** Not the actor's strength, not the
trajectory, not the energy. The reachable-state set.

**What the law then does on its own.** Everything, and it routes around her.
Actions do not stop; they take the cheapest remaining channel, which is why the
outcome is *milder* rather than *absent*. A narrow Edict leaves few alternatives
and bites hard. A broad Edict leaves many and barely registers. Entities with
strong personal Domains partly ignore it because they carry their own reachable-state
set and the two sets contest rather than compose. A Void-aligned disturbance
"pits 'no law' against 'this law' … opening dangerous tears where neither fully
holds" — which in the physical reading is two incompatible boundary conditions on
one region, and the tear is the discontinuity between them. **None of that was
designed. It is what a selection rule does when you put one on a room.**

**Law V check, and a deliberate choice.** Stage VIII sits **above** Refraction, so
Serenyra does not need an external anchor at all: "At Refraction the practitioner
can hold the Crystal as an object of study and the Glyph Roles compress into an
internal chain that needs no material anchor at all"
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). She uses one anyway — a spoken Edict
and a staff strike into the ground. **That is not a Stage limitation. It is
legibility.** A law that nobody heard declared is a law nobody can be held to, and
the Four Crafts page makes the same point from the other side: internalised
casting "reads as a mark of rank." She is spending the mark of rank to buy
witnesses.

**Failure mode — Coherence fault, and this page is its cleanest instance in the
batch.** From the Four Faults: "**Coherence fault.** The practitioner's lived
behaviour and their stated law have drifted apart, and the Continuum reads the
drift. Output degrades, Thresholds refuse to authorise, and Traits begin to argue
with their bearer. This is the slowest fault and the one that ends careers"
(`Two Sets of Books` §VII). The page's cost is that sentence with a mechanism:
"Any time she herself violates its own spirit, the backlash tears through her own
Soul Crystal, and repeated use roughens her metaphysical voice, making the next
Edict harder to sustain without harm." **Degrading output across repeated castings
is the Coherence fault's stated signature.** Tempering Coherence is the Sub-Stat
that reads it and it is described as "The Crystal's internal honesty meter"
(Part Twelve).

The page's "What nobody knows" — why a cynically spoken Edict sometimes holds — has
a mechanism-level candidate worth putting to Isaac rather than asserting: a
selection rule does not read sincerity, it reads the *criterion*. If the wording
happens to specify a genuinely zero matrix element, the rule holds whether or not
the speaker meant it, and the caster's insincerity then shows up later as the
Coherence fault rather than as an immediate failure. **The Edict holds and she
does not.** Offered as a candidate, not as canon.

**What the target sees and feels.** A spoken Edict naming one category of action,
a strike into the ground, and then two hundred metres in which one specific thing
has stopped being available. Attempting the bound action does not feel like being
opposed by a person — the page is careful about this, and so is the experience:
there is *"resistance, as though pushing against water"*, and then the action
either fails or **resolves into the Edict's named alternative instead**. The
lethal blow lands as a disarm. The Wellspring will not answer to shed blood. To
the person inside the dome the refusal has no author present in it, which is why
it is far harder to fight than a woman holding a shield. Aurevane carries the
enforcement evenly to the dome's edge, so there is no soft margin near the
boundary and no strong point at her feet. Oneirion's small contribution is the one
a lawyer would notice first: the wording *"bends around edge cases"* rather than
failing the instant an action falls just outside the letter, which means finding
the gap is harder than reading the sentence. Three exchanges at full potency,
then one or two of weaker echo — and a Void-aligned disturbance inside the dome
opens *"dangerous tears where neither fully holds"*, which is not a safe place for
anyone, including whoever made them.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** η ≈ 0.70 (card): **30 percent of every
expenditure leaves as heat, sound and structural bleed**, spread across 1.68×10⁷ m³
and therefore invisible per cubic metre and enormous in total. The structural share
is the roughened voice. At Stage VIII she sits at the floor of Tier 6 · Master
(0.70–0.80), which for a working this size is the thinnest margin on her sheet.

## 5 · Counterplay and the challenge

**The fairness check** (`.claude/skills/wotr-write/references/fair-play.md`).

| Test | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Costs something that hurts | **pass — the best-shaped cost of any Serenyra page** | Three layers, and the first is a **vow**: she must personally embody the Edict for as long as it stands, and *"any time she herself violates its own spirit, the backlash tears through her own Soul Crystal."* Then an escalating cost — *"repeated use roughens her metaphysical voice, making the next Edict harder to sustain without harm"* — so the second Edict of a campaign is dearer than the first. Unpriced in EU (`SA-GAP-EU-FORMULA`), but a self-executing penalty on her own conduct does not need a number to bite. |
| Stated limits | **pass** | One category of action per casting; a dome ≈200 m radius centred on her; about three exchanges at full potency plus a weaker echo; **a broadly worded Edict is weaker than a narrow one** — the wording is the working; she must be present and consistent throughout. |
| Something beats it | **pass, and the cheapest route is the one the page omits** | The page names peer-level answers; the register supplies a commoner's one. See below. |
| It has a tell | **pass** | She says it out loud, naming the category, and strikes the ground. Everyone inside two hundred metres has been told exactly what is now forbidden — which is necessary, since an Edict nobody has heard cannot be obeyed. |
| Numbers in band | **pass** | Stage VIII ⇒ Max Grade S (401–550), ceiling 550, Tier 6 · Master, η 0.70–0.80 (Parts Five, Nineteen); card η 0.70 ✓ at the floor. Gnosis 522 and Tempering 470 are S, so **she meets this one at grade rather than above it**, which is the right reading of a technique the card calls the peak of her art. The card's lettered Coherence Band is retired under R42 (`SA-UNATT-BAND-B-EDICTUM`, `SA-UNATT-COHERENCE-BAND`). |

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Break the man — the vow, which the page does not mention and the register
  does.** *"A vow kept buys Coherence with conduct. A vow broken is a Shear
  Break, and an opponent who knows the terms of a vow holds a weapon that does not
  require touching him. Manoeuvring a sworn man into breaking his own oath is
  legal everywhere, universally despised, and has ended more careers than any
  duel."* She has just **announced the terms of her vow aloud to everyone in
  earshot**. Build the situation in which honouring her own Edict costs her
  something she cannot give up, and the backlash is delivered by her own working,
  to her own Soul Crystal, at no cost to you. This is the counter a reader is meant
  to assemble, and it is the reason the tell is not a weakness of the design.
- **Break the boundary — Countersigning, and an opposing declaration.** The
  register: *"Some Seals end when countersigned in writing, and some end when the
  sum is spoken back correctly by another. A working that carries either of those
  closing conditions can be ended by an opponent who is literate enough."* The
  page's own Counter is the martial version — another lawcaster declaring an
  opposing principle, *"the two resonate against each other and can collapse
  both."* Note that it collapses **both**, so this is a trade and not a win.
- **Break the boundary — the wording, worked.** Oneirion's flexibility is
  finite. One category is bound; everything adjacent is not. An opponent who
  listens to the exact phrasing and does the *neighbouring* thing has beaten a
  200-metre working with attention, and the page invites this by making narrowness
  the source of the Edict's strength: **the stronger the Edict, the smaller it
  is.**
- **Deny the field — the Void tear, at a price.** A Void-aligned disturbance
  pits *"no law"* against *"this law"* and opens tears where neither holds. This
  works and it is dangerous to the user, which is the honest kind of counter.
  Family **Fulguria**: *"fails against … dense fog and a shielded interior."*
- **Break the body — she is standing in the middle of it.** The dome is centred
  on her and she must remain consistent with it for the duration. If her own Edict
  forbids lethal force, she has disarmed her own guards.

**The tell, stated plainly.** She names the forbidden category aloud and strikes
the ground. The working cannot be quiet, because a law nobody has heard cannot
be enforced — and everyone who heard it now knows the terms she is bound by too.

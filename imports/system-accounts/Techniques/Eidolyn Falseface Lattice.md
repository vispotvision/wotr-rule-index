# System account · Eidolyn Falseface Lattice

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The effect in plain words.** Whatever the person across from him is afraid the fight
contains, he gives it a body — and the body is as solid as their conviction is strong.

**The real phenomenon.** **Template-directed nucleation from a supersaturated medium.**
A supersaturated solution does not organise itself: below a critical supersaturation the
nucleation barrier is too high and nothing happens, however much solute is dissolved.
Supply a template — a seed crystal, a patterned substrate, a biomineralisation matrix —
and the barrier collapses, and the solid deposits **along the template's geometry**, with
high fidelity and considerable mechanical strength. Nacre and bacterial concrete are built
this way; so is every epitaxial film ever grown.

Eidolyn's register analogue is this, named precisely: "Templated nucleation. Ordered
deposition onto an imposed pattern"
(`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Limina — Entropy, Void and Mind.md`).

**The governing law, and it does the page's hardest work.** Classical nucleation theory:

> ΔG* = 16πγ³v² / (3·(kT·ln S)²)  and  J = A·exp(−ΔG*/kT)

The barrier ΔG* scales with the **cube** of interfacial energy and **inversely with the
square of ln S**, where S is the supersaturation. A template lowers the effective γ by a
wetting factor f(θ) that can run from 1 down to nearly zero, collapsing the barrier by
orders of magnitude. And because the nucleation rate J is **exponential** in −ΔG*/kT, it
is fiercely sensitive to S.

**Three consequences, all of them on the page already:**

1. **"More conviction feeds more mass into the deposit."** J is exponential in
   supersaturation. This is not a linear scaling with belief; it is an exponential one,
   which is why a single deeply convinced target produces one *dense* construct rather
   than several thin ones — the page's own Target Response.
2. **"A fearless or fully lucid mind supplies too little conviction."** Below critical
   supersaturation, nucleation does not occur *at all*. **There is a threshold, not a
   gradient** — the working does not produce a weak construct against a calm opponent, it
   produces nothing. The page says "gives the working nothing coherent to deposit onto,"
   which is the same statement.
3. **"Any flaw in the intent is faithfully reproduced at scale."** Epitaxy inherits the
   substrate's defects. A template defect propagates through the whole deposit, and the
   grower cannot see it until the film is grown. The register says so and the page quotes
   it.

**The mass-energy check, which the register passes.** A construct with "the mass and reach
its template implies" — call it 500 kg — would, if it were **created** matter, carry
mc² = 4.5×10¹⁹ J. The A-Grade band tops out at 4.184×10¹² J (Part Four), so creation
would overrun the Stage by **seven orders of magnitude**. **The register forecloses the
creation reading in its own first sentence:** "A supersaturated medium **will not organise
itself**, but given a template it **deposits**." The construct is not made. Ambient Aether
is *organised*, and the page agrees — it "dissolves back into ambient Aether when attention
releases it." **The physics closes, and it closes because the Wellspring's own law was
written to make it close.**

**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Radius: **80 m** (page) ⇒ (4/3)π(80 m)³ = **2.14×10⁶ m³** of medium available —
  the largest volume Aeldoris commands in this batch, and it needs to be, because a
  deposit draws on all of it.
- Nucleation-barrier sensitivity: ΔG* ∝ 1/(ln S)², so **doubling ln S quarters the
  barrier**, and J rises exponentially. A moderately more frightened opponent does not give
  a moderately heavier construct.
- Heterogeneous-nucleation barrier reduction, f(θ): **1 → ~0** with perfect wetting, i.e.
  the template can remove the barrier entirely for a sufficiently matched intent.
- Construct solidity ceiling: Ardency **Density**, which Part Twelve defines as "Structural
  weight and coherence of anything a working leaves standing: inscriptions, active
  glyph-chains, summoned constructs, bound spirits. Whether your ward outlasts the siege
  and **whether your summon is a suggestion or a participant**." Card: Aeldoris's Ardency
  is **340**, A-Grade, and the card glosses it "Falseface Lattice output, enough to give an
  illusion mass." **Card and technique agree.**

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** In the ambient medium, and in the
target's own conviction — which supplies the supersaturation, not the substance. Aeldoris
supplies the template. That is a boundary condition in the strictest sense the system
uses it.

**Precedent we are not copying.** Fear-manifesting constructs are a genre staple — the
Boggart, Batman's Scarecrow, Jujutsu Kaisen's cursed-spirit-from-fear premise. Those are
**illusions that frighten**. This one is a **casting that hits**, with the failure modes of
a badly designed mould rather than of a broken glamour, and its hard counter is a mirror
rather than a will save. The divergence is that the construct is a real material object
with real defects, and the target's belief is a *process variable* rather than a saving
throw.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** Draw across 2.14×10⁶ m³ — and the draw is of *substrate*, not just
power, which makes this the most Aether-hungry working of Aeldoris's four. Limina favours
"Darkness, silence, Aether-thin ground, ruins" and is suppressed by "**Saturated Aether**,
crowd density, active Domains" (`The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings`,
Environmental Coupling). **There is a real tension here the page does not name:** a
deposition working wants a rich medium, and Limina's terrain table says saturated Aether
suppresses the Family. Nucleation theory sides with the terrain table, oddly — an
over-saturated medium nucleates *everywhere at once*, which is Coagulatio's documented
failure ("Too many sites at once produces a fine disordered mass rather than a sound
crystal"). **So a Falseface Lattice in saturated Aether should give him grit rather than a
beast.** Offered as mechanism, logged as a gap for Isaac:
`SA-GAP-FALSEFACE-SATURATED-MEDIUM`. **Residue:** the construct's dissolution returns the
substrate but not the pattern; a district where one has stood keeps a template trace.
**Saturation:** see above — the working's own preferred density is unstated.

**Wellspring stratum.** **Eidolyn · *The Shaping Mind*** (primary), Family **Limina**,
Physics Domain **entropy, void and mind**. Its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Limina — Entropy, Void and Mind.md`:

> "A supersaturated medium will not organise itself, but given a template it deposits along
> the template's geometry with high fidelity and considerable strength. Eidolyn makes intent
> the template. The construct's solidity is real material behaviour rather than illusion,
> and its failure modes are the failure modes of a poorly designed casting."

**Oneirion · *The Wellspring of Dream*** locates the fear-shape as a coordinate in
configuration space (law quoted in full in the `Chorda Somnii` account); **Somnalis · *The
Dream Veil*** reads which shape the target's mind is modelling before it is finished (law
quoted in full in the `Dreambreaker Field` account). **The three-current chain on this page
is correctly ordered and each current does exactly one job:** Somnalis reads, Oneirion
locates, Eidolyn deposits. That is a cleaner division of labour than most multi-Wellspring
pages in the batch manage.

Register Stat Effects, through the Merge Ledger: Eidolyn gives Ardency **Density** (in
constructs), Ardency **Density** again (from Inscription) and Gnosis **Retention** (from
Memorium); Oneirion gives Gnosis **Cognition**, Dominion **Sovereignty** (from Reach),
Ardency **Cascade** (from Spectral); Somnalis gives Gnosis **Forecast** and Dominion
**Sense**. The page cites all of these in their pre-merge form and the Merge Ledger reads
them through cleanly.

Register failure, Eidolyn: "**Template defect.** Any flaw in the intent is faithfully
reproduced at scale, and the practitioner is rarely able to see the flaw until it is
standing in front of them." The page quotes this as its Limitation. Correct.

**The mirror counter, explained from inside the register.** The page calls a mirror a hard
counter and gives a reason in words. The Limina register gives one in physics, two entries
along, under **Mirithane · *The Wellspring of Reflection***: "A true mirror adds nothing.
Angle in equals angle out, and fidelity is defined entirely by the absence of introduced
distortion. **A phase-conjugate mirror goes further and returns a wavefront that undoes its
own aberration on the way back.**" A phase-conjugate return **cancels the template's own
aberration** — which is to say it returns the deposit's pattern inverted onto itself, and a
template summed with its own conjugate is no template. **The mirror does not reveal the
construct. It un-templates it.** Same Family, two entries apart, and the page's counter
falls out of it exactly.

*Four Theories.* **Consent Doctrine**, stretched but holding. The page's "What nobody knows"
— "Why a construct built from someone else's fear ever agrees to fight for Aeldoris rather
than against him" — is a Consent question asked of the wrong party, and the mechanism's
answer is uncomfortable and clean: **a deposit does not agree to anything.** It grows along
the template it was given, and the template is Aeldoris's *reading* of the fear, not the
fear itself. The construct is not loyal. It is *his casting of their dread*. Offered as the
mechanism's reading; the page's question stays open.

**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Aether Shell** as the conduit — the page's cost is
"the price of routing someone else's conviction through his own Aether Shell repeatedly
across an engagement," which is the right layer and the right reason. The **Essence Core**
holds the identity that Identity Drift erodes, and Core failure is "Fracture into Shards"
(`The Core Vocabulary` §III). Developmental Tier at Stage VII: **Resonant** — "The Crystal
self-conducts. Power moves even in silence" (Part Twenty-One), which is what a construct
that keeps standing without continuous declaration requires. Crystal State: **Refined,
residue-heavy** (card). **The invoice:** Identity Drift and Cognitive Static — "Sustained
use blurs where his self-image ends and the borrowed fear-shapes begin." **The system has
a name for where that goes and the page does not use it: the Mirage Identity threshold,
which Resilience Anchoring is the Sub-Stat that resists** ("Resistance to dissolution,
corruption, possession and the Mirage Identity threshold", Part Twelve). Aeldoris's
Resilience is **260, B-Grade**, his second-weakest reading. **He is running an
identity-blurring working on the thinnest Sub-Stat that answers for it**, and that is the
real cost structure of this page.

## 3 · Mechanism

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Frm]` **Form** · Extension · Thalen — the
limit that decides *which* geometry the deposition takes, which is the whole content of a
template — laid across `[Vel]` **Mind** · Extension · Iesara, because the template is being
read out of one. Both from the thirty unclassified forms in
`wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`.
*Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling.*

**What boundary moves.** One quantity: **the nucleation barrier in the ambient medium,
lowered along one imposed pattern.** Not the medium's quantity, not the construct's
properties, not the target's mind. Only ΔG*, and only along the template.

**What the law then does on its own.** Everything, and the page's four constraints are four
lines of nucleation theory. The construct is solid because deposition produces real
material. Its mass scales exponentially with conviction because J is exponential in ln S. A
calm target yields nothing because there is a critical supersaturation below which nothing
nucleates. Its flaws are the intent's flaws at scale because epitaxy inherits substrate
defects. **Nobody wrote a rule saying fear makes it bigger. ΔG* ∝ 1/(ln S)² did.**

**Law V check.** Stage VII **is** Refraction, where the internal chain becomes available
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). The page's trigger is **Reactive** — it
"releases against an incoming hostile action rather than on Aeldoris's own initiative" —
which, as on `Letheveil Memory-Quake`, is the case where the internal chain is required
rather than merely available. **Consistent, and the pattern across Aeldoris's four pages is
coherent: the two reactive workings need Refraction, the two declared ones do not.**

**Failure mode — Boundary fault.** "The condition was established imprecisely and the law
ran somewhere unintended" (`Two Sets of Books` §VII). The template is his reading of
someone else's conviction, and a misread template deposits faithfully anyway. Behind it a
**Field fault**: a Letheveil-disciplined mind "identifies the fear-model as load-bearing or
not and discards what does not need carrying," so the supersaturation never reaches
critical and the working is correct and produces nothing. And behind *that*, a
**Structural fault** waiting: Identity Drift on a B-Grade Resilience, against a threshold
the system names Mirage Identity.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** η ≈ 0.65 (card): **35 percent** of every
expenditure leaves as heat, sound and structural bleed. On a deposition working the
structural share is the interesting one — waste in a nucleation process shows up as
**disordered deposit**, not as heat, which is Coagulatio's "density they cannot use." So a
35 % bleed on a Falseface Lattice should read as a construct with visible bad casting: a
seam, a hollow, a limb that does not carry. **Texture the page could use, derived rather
than invented.** As on his other pages, 0.65 sits **above Tier 5 · Expert's 0.50–0.60
ceiling** (`SA-NUM-ETA-TIER`).

**Does the physics close against the stratal account?** **Yes, and it closes on a knife
edge that the register put there deliberately.** Had Eidolyn been written as a *creating*
current, a 500 kg construct would have cost 4.5×10¹⁹ J and broken the Stage by seven orders
of magnitude. It is written as a *depositing* current, so the substrate is ambient and the
only bill is the template. Everything else the page claims — the exponential scaling with
conviction, the hard threshold against a calm mind, the inherited defects, and even the
mirror counter by way of Mirithane's phase conjugation — comes out of nucleation theory
without a single rule being added.

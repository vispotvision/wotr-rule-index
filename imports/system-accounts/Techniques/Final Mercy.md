# System account · Final Mercy

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** Everything he is carrying goes into one point. What arrives does
not wound the target's body. It removes the target's claim to keep having one.

**The real phenomenon.** **Projective measurement and its back-action** — the fact that a
sufficiently strong measurement does not report a state, it *imposes* one.

A measurement is not a passive reading. Measure an observable strongly and the system is projected
into an eigenstate of that observable; the **quantum Zeno effect** is the limiting case, where
repeated measurement at intervals τ → 0 freezes a system in the measured eigenstate with survival
probability P ≈ 1 − (Δt/τ_Z)², and the **anti-Zeno effect** is the same physics accelerating the
decay instead. Both have been demonstrated in trapped ions and in cavity QED. **The measurement
decides the outcome, and the harder you measure the less the system's own dynamics get a say.**

The page states this and states it precisely: "Judicium's law is a truth-value read against a
reference. Turned outward at full compression rather than used diagnostically, the read stops
reporting on the target's continuance and starts adjudicating it, **the same operation the
Wellspring performs constantly, run past the threshold where measurement and verdict become the
same act.**" **That threshold is real and has a name.**

*And the compression half.* Exuroth's law is quench-and-temper, and the hardness numbers are
concrete: annealed steel runs about **200 HV**; as-quenched martensite reaches **≈700 HV**, a
factor of **3.5**, at the cost of a brittleness that tempering then trades back. The page has
Francis running that cycle "on his own output rather than on a blade, quench-tempering an entire
reserve into one strike hard enough to carry a verdict instead of merely a wound." **A 3.5×
hardness gain and a brittleness you accept is the right shape for a single-discharge working with
heavy recoil.**

**The governing law.** The projection postulate: a strong measurement of observable Â leaves the
system in an eigenstate of Â. And the **Landauer bound** for what the erasure costs, kT·ln2 per
bit.

**Measurable quantities, SI — and the number that defines this page.**
- Range **150 m**, duration **instant**, single discharge, no sustain (page).
- **Information content of what is erased.** The page has "the target's Soul, memory, and Principle
  alignment collapse together." Synaptic information-capacity work puts a human brain at roughly
  **4.7 bits per synapse across ~10¹⁵ synapses ≈ 4.7×10¹⁵ bits**, about 0.6 petabytes.
- **Landauer cost of erasing it.** At body temperature kT·ln2 = **2.97×10⁻²¹ J/bit**, so
  4.7×10¹⁵ × 2.97×10⁻²¹ = **1.4×10⁻⁵ J**.
- **Fourteen microjoules.** That is the irreducible physical cost of erasing everything a mind
  contains. For comparison the EX-Grade attack band is **1.24×10²⁹ – 6.906×10³⁷ J** (Part Four) —
  **the erasure is about 10³⁴ times cheaper than the Grade the working is gated at.**
- Exuroth compression ratio, as a real analogue: **≈3.5×** (200 → 700 HV).
- Cost: **≈4 %** memory bleed per use, "by his own count," non-selective. At 4 % per use,
  **twenty-five uses is the whole of a mind** — derivable from the page's own figure and not
  stated on it.

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** **This is the batch's terminal case for the
system's central claim.** A working that ends a person costs fourteen microjoules of irreducible
physical work — less than a housefly's kinetic energy — and is gated at a Grade whose band starts
at 10²⁹ joules. The system's own sentence is the only thing that makes that coherent: "the boundary
condition is **cheap in absolute terms and expensive in the only currency a soul has**"
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Physical Account Two Sets of Books.md` §III). **The Grade is not
measuring the erasure. It is measuring what it takes to establish a boundary that an entire
identity has to be an eigenstate of**, and the page is right to say no EU figure exists to check
against, because the physical account cannot supply one.

**Precedent we are not copying.** Erasure attacks are the top shelf of the genre — Bleach's
Kamishini no Yari, Jujutsu Kaisen's Malevolent Shrine, Gurren Lagann's Big Bang Storm. Those
**destroy** at scale. This one **adjudicates**, and the difference is the counter: it fails against
a Crystal with no independent persistence claim, not against a Crystal with more durability. The
divergence is that the immunity condition is architectural, not a resistance number.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** A single directed point at 150 m — the narrowest footprint of any Zenith
working in this batch, and appropriately so: a projective measurement is cheap in reach and
expensive in strength. Fulguria wants "Dry air, conductive substrate, clear line of sight" and is
suppressed by "Faraday-caged interiors, heavy ferrous shielding, dense fog"
(`The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings`, Environmental Coupling). **Residue:** the page's
best observation and it is a real forensic claim — "The field around the point of impact goes
emotionally silent, which the old card is right to flag as diagnostic in itself: **an erasure
event, not a kill, does not leave the ordinary residue of one.**" In system terms the residue is
*the absence of the residue that should be there*, and Eclipseron is the current that reads
absences: "the practitioner reads what has been blocked, subtracted, or withheld, and derives from
the shape of the absence the shape of the thing casting it"
(`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Spatium — Spatial Geometry.md`). **An Eclipseron
practitioner is the only investigator who could work this scene.** **Saturation:** not a factor;
the working is a point discharge.

**Wellspring stratum.** **Judicium · *The Wellspring of Truth*** (primary), Family **Fulguria**,
Physics Domain **electromagnetism**. Its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Fulguria — Electromagnetism.md`:

> "Every element absorbs and emits at wavelengths fixed by its own electron structure, and no
> substance can present another's spectrum. Judicium tunes Gnosis to that principle: the
> practitioner reads the characteristic lines of a Crystal, a technique, or a lie, and identifies
> composition from signature alone. Eddy-current inspection finds the crack under the paint
> without removing the paint."

**Exuroth · *The Trial Flame*** (secondary), Family **Caloria** — law quoted in full in the
`Crimson Dirge` account — supplying the compression. The page's assignment is careful and
correct: it explicitly declines to invent an EU figure and pins the working to Exuroth's own
attested Stat Effect instead. **That is the right move and the only page in the batch that makes
it: "No EU figure for Francis exists to check against, so this is pinned to Exuroth's own attested
Stat Effect."**

Register failures in play. Judicium's "**Spectral overload.** Every object in view reports its
composition simultaneously and the practitioner loses the ability to prioritise any of it" —
**which is why the erasure is not selective.** A read turned outward at full compression cannot
be aimed at one line; it takes the whole spectrum, and on the caster's side that is the 4 % of his
own memory that goes with every use. Exuroth's "**Quench cracking.** Cycle too fast without the
tempering interval and the lattice arrives hard, brittle, and split along its own thermal
gradients" — which is the "heavy paradox recoil."

*Four Theories.* **Consent Doctrine**, and the page's Trigger makes an unusually strong claim for
it: "Francis has to have already decided, **correctly by Judicium's own measure**, that the
target's continuance is the thing to remove." That is a working that requires the *criterion* to
be satisfied, not the caster's preference — which is exactly the Doctrine's "The Wellspring
recognises a lawful boundary condition and behaves accordingly, in the same sense that a river
recognises a channel" (`Two Sets of Books` §IV). **A strike thrown without the verdict already
formed does not carry the working, and that is Consent stated as a firing condition.** Cleanest
Consent case in the batch.

**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Essence Core** (the verdict is held there before release)
discharged through the **Aether Shell**; the target's **Attraction Layer** is what the working
seizes, which is why the counter is an externally anchored Layer. Developmental Tier at Stage XIV:
**Crystallized Soul** — "Self and law correspond with near-perfect precision. An exact instrument
of identity" (Part Twenty-One). **An exact instrument of identity, used to end one.** Crystal
State: card says "Absolute Crystal," which is a Developmental Tier and not a State
(`SA-CROSS-FRANCIS-ABSOLUTE-CRYSTAL`; see the `Dirge Ascension` account). **The invoice:** ≈4 % of
his memory per use, non-selective, plus paradox recoil — with the card's Principle Engine as "the
one documented relief valve for a cost this technique otherwise pays raw." **No system unit exists
for memory.** `SA-GAP-MEMORY-AS-COST`.

**The lens: *esse est percipi*.** Berkeley, *A Treatise Concerning the Principles
of Human Knowledge* (1710), §3: to be is to be perceived, and what holds a thing
in being when no finite mind attends to it is that God's attention never lapses.
Berkeley's idealism is the natural in-world reading of a working that kills by
adjudication rather than by injury, because it makes persistence into something
**held** rather than something possessed — and a thing that is held can be let go
of by whoever is holding it. Judicium's law is *"a truth-value read against a
reference"*; turned outward at full compression the read *"stops reporting on the
target's continuance and starts adjudicating it"*, which is precisely the moment
Berkeley's percipi is withdrawn by a competent perceiver. The lens then explains
the page's counter better than the page does. *"A Crystal with structurally little
independent selfhood has very little for the working to seize"*, and the
Celestial Host's **externally Throne-anchored** architecture is recorded as
near-immune: on the Berkeleyan reading, such a Crystal's continuity is not being
generated locally at all, so there is nothing local to stop perceiving. It is
being held by something else, and Francis is not that something. Where the lens
goes wrong is in the comfort it offers. Berkeley's God guarantees that nothing
ever truly lapses, so a school reading Final Mercy through him will conclude that
the erased are only hidden and will stop looking for them. The diagnostic on the
page says otherwise: the field around the impact *"goes emotionally silent"*, and
an erasure event *"does not leave the ordinary residue"* of a killing. Somebody
who knows what an absence of residue means knows that there is nothing to find.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Me]` **Measure** · Synonym · Urion — a measurement
that has been pushed past reporting — laid across `[Kael]` **Ruin** · Root · Uurgath. Both from the
thirty unclassified forms in
`wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`. **The pairing is
deliberately close to `Edict Strike`'s proposed `[Me]` on `[Al]` Edge and the difference is the
point: a measurement laid on an edge makes a blow heavier; a measurement laid on ruin makes a
verdict.** *Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling.*

**What boundary moves.** One quantity: **the strength of a persistence measurement, pushed past the
point where the measurement's back-action exceeds the system's own dynamics.** Not the target's
durability. Not their Resilience. The coupling strength of one read.

**What the law then does on its own.** All of it. The target is projected into an eigenstate of the
measured observable because that is what strong measurement does. The erasure is not selective
because a full-compression read takes the whole spectrum. It cannot be used while `Dirge
Ascension` is active because both compress the same architecture and a reserve cannot be
quench-hardened into two shapes. And it fails against an externally Throne-anchored Crystal for the
best possible reason: **if the persistence claim is held outside the system, the system has no pure
persistence eigenstate to be projected into.** The page reaches that conclusion from documentation;
the mechanism reaches it from the projection postulate, and they agree.

**Law V check.** Stage XIV sits far above Refraction. The page's Craft is "Spellcraft
(Internalised: a ritual-fast chain held and released, not spoken over its duration)" — the high
form, which the Four Crafts page says is exactly what Refraction and above unlocks: "the Glyph
Roles compress into an internal chain that needs no material anchor at all"
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). **Correctly filed, and the "held and released"
framing is the Internalised vector described accurately.**

**Failure mode — Boundary fault.** "The condition was established imprecisely and the law ran
somewhere unintended" (`Two Sets of Books` §VII). The unintended place is his own memory: he
cannot choose which four percent goes, and the page says so. **The imprecision is not carelessness;
it is Judicium's spectral overload, and a read at full compression has no aperture stop.** Behind
it a **Field fault** on the counter side: a Crystal with no independent selfhood presents nothing
to project, so the working is correct and produces nothing.

The page's "What nobody knows" — why the collapse runs Soul, then memory, then Principle
alignment, backward from every other erasure working in the register — has a mechanism-level
candidate worth offering rather than asserting. **In a projective measurement the ordering is set
by coupling to the measured observable, not by abstraction level.** Ordinary erasure workings
attack the *record* and the identity fails last because it is furthest from the attack. **Final
Mercy measures the persistence claim itself, which lives in the Soul — so the Soul is the most
strongly coupled term and goes first, and memory and Principle alignment follow as consequences
rather than as separate targets.** The order is not reversed. It is the *only* order a measurement
of persistence can produce, and every other working on the register is measuring something else.
Offered as a candidate, not as canon.

**What the target sees and feels.** A strike, and then not the strike's
consequences. The page is exact about it and the exactness is the working: *"What
lands does not wound the target's body. It removes the target's claim to keep
having one."* There is no wound channel to treat and no bleeding to classify,
because injury was never the mechanism. What collapses, collapses together —
*"the target's Soul, memory, and Principle alignment collapse together rather
than in sequence"* — so there is no interval in which something could be arrested
partway. Range is a hundred and fifty metres and the discharge is instant, with no
sustain phase to interrupt. The one thing anyone outside gets is the aftermath:
the field around the point of impact goes **emotionally silent**, which the card
rightly treats as diagnostic, since an erasure does not leave the residue a
killing leaves. And the strike only carries the working if Francis has already
reached the verdict — *"a strike thrown without that verdict already formed does
not carry the working"* — so the thing that decides whether a blow is lethal or
final happened before he moved, inside him, where nobody watching could see it.
That absence of a visible tell is the fault this section logs.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** The card gives **η = 0.93**, so **7 percent** of every
expenditure leaves as heat, sound and structural bleed. Stage XIV puts him in **Tier 8 ·
Archmaster**, band **0.95–1.2** (Part Nineteen), so 0.93 is **below the floor**
(`SA-NUM-ETA-TIER`). On an instantaneous point discharge, 7 % of a quench-hardened full reserve
arrives all at once and locally — **which is the paradox recoil, and it is the only part of this
working with a joule figure attached to it at all.**

That is what the Accord's standard asks for.

## 5 · Counterplay and the challenge

**The fairness check** (`.claude/skills/wotr-write/references/fair-play.md`).

| Test | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Costs something that hurts | **pass, at the top of the scale** | *"Near four percent memory bleed per use"* by his own count, **and the erasure is not selective** — *"there is no way yet found to choose which four percent goes"* — plus heavy paradox recoil. He pays for an execution with pieces of himself chosen at random. The only relief named is his Principle Engine converting some of the recoil. The system still has no rule that prices memory (`SA-GAP-MEMORY-AS-COST`) and no EU figure exists for him, which the page says outright and correctly. |
| Stated limits | **pass, and one is structural** | 150 m; instant, single discharge, no sustain; **requires a held verdict, already reached and correct by Judicium's own measure** — it cannot be thrown at someone he has not judged; and it **cannot be used while Dirge Ascension is active**, because *"both draw on the same compressed architecture and the Crystal will not run both."* Every use of this is a use of not-that. |
| Something beats it | **pass** | Two documented defences, both architectural and both available to a prepared party rather than to a peer only. |
| It has a tell | **FAIL** | The trigger is *"a held verdict, reached before the strike lands"* — an internal state with no stated outward sign. From outside, a Final Mercy and an ordinary strike from the same man look the same until one of them has already resolved, instantly, at 150 m. *"A careful opponent can read it coming"* is the check, and here there is nothing to read. The only diagnostic the page offers (emotional silence, absent residue) arrives **after** the target is gone. Logged `SA-FAIR-FINAL-MERCY-NO-TELL`. |
| Numbers in band | **pass on Grade, two flagged** | Stage XIV ⇒ Max Grade EX (1,201–1,500), Tier 8 (Part Five); the card's **Gnosis 1,410** (the verdict-read) and **Ardency 1,455** (the discharge) are his top two Primaries and both EX ✓ — the tightest stat-to-working fit in the batch. Against that: η 0.93 below the Tier 8 floor of 0.95 (`SA-NUM-ETA-TIER`), the Dominion Sovereignty gate wants Fate and he is pure Spirit (`SA-NUM-FINAL-MERCY-PATHGATE`), and conventional metrics stop describing Zenith output (`SA-NUM-ZENITH-CONVENTIONAL-METRICS`). |

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Break the boundary — hold the persistence-claim somewhere else.** The whole
  working seizes a Crystal's own continuity signature. An **externally
  Throne-anchored** Crystal of the Celestial Host pattern *"presents almost
  nothing for the verdict to seize"*, and absent that architecture, *"an identity
  anchor built specifically to hold a persistence-claim independent of the
  Crystal's own state is the only other documented defence."* In register terms
  this is **Lock**, and it has to be in place **before** contact: *"Pick before
  contact. Everything that goes wrong in the record went wrong because somebody
  tried to decide during."*
- **Break the man — the verdict is a condition, so contest the verdict.** The
  working does not fire without a verdict *correct by Judicium's own measure*.
  That is not a mood; it is a standard, and a standard can fail. An opponent whose
  continuance is genuinely arguable — a surrendered man, a bound man, one whose
  guilt is unsettled — is a target the working may simply decline to carry. This is
  the register's **Rigidity** at its cruellest: *"Present him with the situation
  his own law handles worst and he will handle it that way regardless."*
- **Break the man — the memory ledger, spent for him.** Four percent per use,
  non-selective, permanent. Force him to spend it on something that did not
  require it and you have taken a piece of him out of the war. And because Final
  Mercy and Dirge Ascension cannot coexist, forcing him into one is denying him the
  other for the duration.
- **Break the body — a hundred and fifty metres is not far.** *"Shot"*,
  *"Distance"*, *"Numbers, correctly used"*. The working is single-target and
  instant with no sustain, so it kills one thing per verdict, and a verdict takes
  a man's whole attention to reach.

**The tell, stated plainly.** There is not one, and that is logged above as a
fairness fault. The only thing a defender can read is the architecture they built
beforehand, which is why this page rewards preparation and punishes improvisation
absolutely.

# System account · Florwyn's Canticle

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The effect in plain words.** Wounds inside earshot close over minutes instead of
days, and plants come up out of the ground while the singing lasts.

**The real phenomenon.** The **wound-healing cascade**, driven from outside and at
a rate it does not natively run at. Four phases in fixed order — haemostasis,
inflammation, proliferation, remodelling — of which the rate-limiting one is
**proliferation**: fibroblast division and collagen deposition, plus
re-epithelialisation advancing from the wound margin. Every step is chemistry that
is already running in any intact body; none of it has to be invented. What the
working supplies is **rate**.

For the growth in the ground, Verdantia's own second analogue: **indeterminate
meristematic growth**. Plant meristem stays embryonic for the organism's life, so
there is no terminal size, and **carbon fixation** converts ambient input to
structure continuously rather than in bouts.

**The governing law.** Healing rate is set by the slowest step, and the slow step
is biosynthesis. Human dermal fibroblasts double on the order of **24 h**;
re-epithelialisation advances at roughly **0.5–1 mm/day** across a clean margin.
Photosynthetic conversion in a field crop runs at **1–2 %** of incident solar
energy against a theoretical C3 ceiling near 4.6 %. These are the ceilings the
working is pushing against, and they are ceilings on *time*, not on energy.

**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Field: **30 m** radius, strongest inside **10 m** (page). Ground area at full
  radius: π(30 m)² = **2,827 m²**.
- Baseline closure for a 20 mm wound at 0.7 mm/day: **≈ 14 days**
  (1.21×10⁶ s).
- Page's compressed closure: "minutes of unbroken singing." Taken at 300 s, the
  **acceleration factor is 1.21×10⁶ ÷ 300 ≈ 4,000×**.
- Biosynthetic cost of the new tissue: closing a 100 × 10 × 10 mm wound channel
  lays down ≈ 10 cm³ ≈ 10 g wet, ≈ 2.5 g dry, at roughly 24 kJ per gram dry
  protein-rich tissue ⇒ **≈ 60 kJ**.
- **Localised metabolic power required:** 60 kJ ÷ 300 s = **200 W** at one wound
  site. Whole-body basal metabolic rate is about **100 W**. The site alone is
  therefore drawing **twice the entire resting output of the body it is in**, and
  for several wounds at once, several multiples of that.

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** 60 kJ is nothing — a
tenth of a food calorie per gram of tissue, trivially inside the reach of any
metabolism given time. **The working is not buying joules. It is buying seconds**,
and the price of the seconds is that the power has to arrive at 200 W into a
gram-scale volume that is not built to dissipate it. This is the exact shape of the
system's own claim: "the boundary condition is cheap in absolute terms and
expensive in the only currency a soul has"
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Physical Account Two Sets of Books.md` §III). It is
also why the page says healing is *gradual* and cannot restore a severed limb: a
limb is not a rate problem, it is a mass problem, and no acceleration of a cascade
supplies substrate that is not there.

**Precedent we are not copying.** Song-healing is a genre fixture — Final
Fantasy's white magic, the bard traditions, Naruto's Mystical Palm. Those
*restore*. This one **accelerates**, and the distinction is load-bearing: what it
cannot do follows from the mechanism rather than from a rule, and the ground blooms
because the same rate-boundary lands on plant tissue when it finds it. The
divergence is that the growth in the soil is not decoration. It is evidence of what
the working actually does.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** Ambient draw across 2,827 m², heavily weighted to the inner
10 m. Vitalia's favoured conditions are "Living biomass, temperate conditions,
proximity to blood"; suppressive are "Sterile ground, extreme cold,
Essence-depleted soil" (`The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings`,
Environmental Coupling). **This is the one working in the batch whose field
requirement is stated in the register and repeated on the page** — "it needs living
tissue nearby to have anything to work with" — so the Field-fault condition is
documented from both directions. **Residue:** the most durable in the batch and
the only visible one. Temporary cover that blooms and stays is Aetheric Residue as
literal biomass: "A permanent, if often subtle, change to the location where the
working occurred" (`The Core Vocabulary` §II). **Saturation:** the working is a
recovery current, so saturation reads inverted here — the danger is not too much
expression but too much *acceleration* on tissue that has no substrate left.

**Wellspring stratum.** **Verdantia · *The Green Pulse***, Family **Vitalia**,
Physics Domain **biochemistry**. Its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Vitalia — Biochemistry.md`:

> "A plant does not have a final size. Meristem tissue remains embryonic for the
> organism's whole life, and photosynthesis converts ambient energy into structure
> continuously rather than in bouts. Verdantia gives the Crystal indeterminate
> growth and continuous ambient conversion, which is why its practitioners recover
> while doing nothing and why they are difficult to exhaust in a long engagement."

Register Stat Effect, through the Merge Ledger: Vitality **Regeneration**,
Tempering **Yield**, Harmonics **Synergy**. Register failure: "**Etiolation.**
Deprived of ambient input the growth continues anyway, producing length without
substance until the structure cannot hold itself up."

**Etiolation is the page's cost seen at the right scale.** Growth continuing past
its substrate, in a working that heals by growing, is how a Canticle held too long
would leave tissue that is present and load-bearing in neither the body nor the
ground.

*Four Theories.* This is the one page among Serenyra's six that leans somewhere
other than Consent. The card calls the technique "an echo of Florwyn's own Ground,
carried rather than designed," and Lineage Strain is a cost arriving from a source
she did not contract with. That is **the Debt Position** — "The Wellspring answers
on credit and the account is settled somewhere the practitioner cannot see …
disturbingly good at predicting which practitioners come apart"
(`Two Sets of Books` §IV). The Accord would still license it under Consent, because
"a process can be licensed and a relationship cannot." **The page's own language
describes a relationship. Noted, not resolved.**

**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Essence Core**, which "Holds identity,
memory, and Traits" — the Canticle is an **Inherited** Trait expression by the
card's own account, one of the Four Reflections, "written into the Core before the
Core had a self to consent with, and governed by Tempering Maturity"
(`The Core Vocabulary` §VI). Tempering Maturity is the Sub-Stat that absorbed
Inheritance under the Merge Ledger, so the system's own bookkeeping routes this
working's governing stat to the same place the card does. Expression runs outward
through the **Aether Shell** as voice. Developmental Tier at Serenyra's Stage VIII:
**Radiant**; at the Stage VI floor, **Harmonic**. Crystal State: **Refined**
(card). **The invoice:** Lineage Strain is an Inherited-Trait cost — "the inherited
echo of Florwyn's Ground pulls closer with overuse" — and the relevant system
hazard is **Overgrowth**: "A Trait expanding past the soul's capacity to guide it …
The warning sign is a Trait acting when the bearer did not will it"
(`The Core Vocabulary` §VI). Emotional Vulnerability is an Attraction-Layer cost:
a working sustained by recognition is legible to anyone reading for it.

## 3 · Mechanism

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Ser]` **Renewal** · Root · Wyther —
the only form among the thirty that names this kind of limit, and it names it
exactly — laid across `[Frm]` **Form** · Extension · Thalen, which is what keeps
accelerated deposition from becoming etiolation. Both from the thirty unclassified
forms in `wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`.
*Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling.*

**What boundary moves.** One quantity: **the rate constant of the proliferative
phase inside the field.** Not the chemistry. Not the substrate. Not the order of
the phases. Only how fast the slow step runs.

**What the law then does on its own.** All of it, and the page's limits all fall
out of it without a single rule being written. The cascade closes the wound because
that is what the cascade does. A severed limb is not restored because the cascade
has never restored one and an accelerated cascade is still that cascade. Plants
bloom because the same rate-boundary, applied to meristem, is applied to the tissue
in the register's own analogue. A Mortalis-aligned current resists it because
Mortalis is "the cessation of the expenditure and the return to equilibrium that
follows without needing a cause" — there is no rate to accelerate in a system that
has stopped maintaining a gradient. **Nobody decided the Canticle would not regrow
an arm.** The law decided, before anyone sang.

**Law V check.** Stage VI sits below Refraction, so an external anchor is required
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). The page supplies one and makes it
continuous — "functions only while the hymn runs unbroken." **The gate closes
cleanly, and the anchor's fragility is the page's own stated counter: cut the
sound.**

**Failure mode — Field fault, primarily.** "The room could not supply what the
working assumed. Saturation, depletion, a cut main … The working is correct and
produces nothing" (`Two Sets of Books` §VII). Sterile or dead ground gives the
working correct chemistry with no substrate, which is Field fault in its textbook
form and is also the mechanism's answer to the page's own "What nobody knows" —
why the same hymn calls flowering from one patch and nothing from a patch that
looks identical. **It is not the ground's appearance. It is the ground's viable
propagule and microbial load, neither of which is visible.** A secondary
**Structural fault** sits behind the Lineage Strain: a Crystal holding an Inherited
law harder than it can guide is Overgrowth, and the page's own cost names the
entanglement risk.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** η ≈ 0.70 (card): **30 percent of every
expenditure leaves as heat, sound and structural bleed**. In a working whose output
is already thermal and metabolic, the bleed is not distinguishable from the effect
at the wound site — which is the honest physical reason a Canticle leaves a patient
warm and a healer exposed. At Stage VIII she sits at the floor of Tier 6 · Master
(0.70–0.80).

**Does the physics close against the stratal account?** Yes, with one caveat that
is a timing question rather than an energy question. The joules are trivial and
came from the patient's own metabolism and the ambient field; the boundary — a rate
constant — came from her; the invoice landed on an Inherited law in the Core. The
caveat is that a 200 W local power density in gram-scale tissue is a real thermal
problem the page does not mention, and it is the physical reason a Canticle should
leave the healed site hot. Offered as texture, not logged as a conflict, because
nothing in the system forbids it.

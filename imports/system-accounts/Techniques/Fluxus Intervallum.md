# System account · Fluxus Intervallum

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** The caster fixes on another mass in line of sight and is there
instead of here, having genuinely crossed the distance rather than been unmade and remade at the
far end.

**The real phenomenon.** **Resonant energy transfer between coupled oscillators**, and the page
states it correctly and at the right level of detail.

Two resonators tuned to the same frequency and held near one another exchange energy through their
near fields with no conductor between them. The exchange rate is set by a coupling coefficient κ
which falls off steeply with separation — for near-field dipoles roughly as **1/d³** — and transfer
is efficient only in the strongly-coupled regime **κ ≫ √(Γ₁Γ₂)**, where Γ is each resonator's own
loss rate. This is the physics of resonant wireless power transfer, and it is Transference's
register analogue verbatim: "Resonant energy transfer between coupled oscillators. Flow along a
potential gradient"
(`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Spatium — Spatial Geometry.md`).

**And the page's most important claim is the one it gets right:** this is not teleportation. "Real
traversal at extraordinary speed and never a disappearance and a remaking." In the coupled-oscillator
picture, energy moves *continuously* between the two resonators — there is no interval in which it is
in neither — so the mechanism forbids discontinuity by construction. **The page did not have to
argue for that. Coupled-mode theory gives it for free.**

**The governing law.** Coupled-mode theory: dA₁/dt = iω₁A₁ + iκA₂, and the mirror equation, with
complete transfer occurring over a time π/(2κ). The **1/d³** falloff is why the page's range is
thirty metres rather than three hundred, and the strong-coupling condition is why both endpoints
must be genuine mass rather than one being empty space.

**Measurable quantities, SI — and two figures that will not sit together.**
- Range **30 m**, line of sight, two masses, unbroken straight line (page).
- Stated energy: "**1 to 5 gigantonewtons** of peak force, roughly **1.046 to 46 gigajoules**."
  **Checked against Part Four's B-Grade row, 1.046×10⁹ – 4.6024×10¹⁰ J: the page's citation is
  correct.**
- **First problem: the speed.** Part Six gives B-Grade travel speed as **510–1,372 m/s**. Thirty
  metres at the top of that band takes 30 ÷ 1,372 = **21.9 ms**. The page calls the crossing
  "instantaneous" and says the caster "attenuates and reconstitutes at the far mass **with no
  intervening state anyone watching can resolve**." Part Six's reaction-speed column says otherwise:
  **C-Grade reaction is 5–15 ms, B-Grade is 1–5 ms, A-Grade is 0.2–1 ms.** A 21.9 ms transit is
  comfortably resolvable by anyone at C-Grade or above — which is most of the people worth using this
  technique against. Logged `SA-NUM-FLUXUS-INSTANT-VS-B-SPEED`.
- **Second problem: the energy against a human mass.** An 80 kg body carrying 1.046×10⁹ J is moving
  at v = √(2E/m) = √(2 × 1.046×10⁹ ÷ 80) = **5,114 m/s**. At the band's top, 4.6×10¹⁰ J gives
  **33,912 m/s**. Both are far above the B-Grade travel band — **3.7× and 25× above it.**
  Conversely, an 80 kg body *at* the B-Grade speed ceiling of 1,372 m/s carries
  ½ × 80 × 1,372² = **7.53×10⁷ J**, which is **C-Grade** (2.092×10⁷ – 1.046×10⁹ J), not B.
  **So the page's energy figure implies a speed the Stage cannot reach, and the Stage's speed implies
  an energy one Grade lower.** Logged `SA-NUM-FLUXUS-ENERGY-VS-MASS`.
- **The two problems partly cancel and that is worth saying.** At the energy figure's implied
  5,114–33,912 m/s, the 30 m crossing takes **5.9 ms to 0.88 ms** — which *is* unresolvable by
  anything below A-Grade, and would make "no intervening state anyone watching can resolve"
  defensible. **So the page's two numbers each support a different half of its own description, and
  neither supports both.** Which figure governs is a ruling.
- Coupling falloff: **1/d³** for near-field dipoles. Doubling the separation weakens the coupling
  **eightfold**, which is the honest reason a thirty-metre limit is a limit and not a convention.

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** In the gradient between the two masses, and the
page says so: "They do not push themselves across the gap. The two are briefly in tune, and the
gradient does the rest." That is `Two Sets of Books` §III with nothing added — the boundary is the
tuning, the joules are the gradient's.

**Precedent we are not copying.** Short-range repositioning is everywhere — Blink, Instant
Transmission, Flash Step, the Nightcrawler tradition. Almost all of them are **discontinuous**: you
are here, then there, and the middle is a cut. This one is explicitly *not*, and the page makes the
continuity a fact about the mechanism rather than a flavour note — which is why a physical
obstruction on the line stops it, and why the cost is a vestibular one. **The divergence is that the
caster's inner ear disagrees with the rest of him for a beat, because he really did travel.**

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** A thin line between two masses; the smallest footprint of any working in this
batch. Spatium is favoured by "Veil-thin zones, established Realmgates, surveyed ground" and
suppressed by "Warp scarring, contested Domains, unmapped terrain"
(`The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings`, Environmental Coupling) — **and "surveyed ground" is
quietly load-bearing here: a coupling to a mass you have mis-located is a coupling to the wrong
place.** **Residue:** the strain-line itself, which the page names and makes a target — "Tenebra-
aligned or Zar'thuun-aligned power erases the strain-line the coupling depends on." A residue that
can be attacked *before* the working completes is unusual and good. **Saturation:** not a limit; the
limit is κ against distance.

**Wellspring stratum.** **Transference · *The Living Exchange***, Family **Spatium**, Physics Domain
**spatial geometry**. Its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Spatium — Spatial Geometry.md`:

> "Two oscillators tuned to the same frequency and held near each other exchange energy without a
> conductor between them, and the coupling strength falls off sharply with separation. Transference
> makes the Attraction Layer such an oscillator. Essence moves to an ally not because it is pushed
> but because the two Crystals are in tune and near, and a gradient does the rest."

Register Stat Effect, through the Merge Ledger: Harmonics **Synergy**, Harmonics **Projection**,
Tempering **Yield** (from Conversion). Register failure: "**Reverse coupling.** A gradient runs both
directions, and a Transference practitioner beside a depleted ally will empty into them without
deciding to."

**Reverse coupling is the failure this page most needs and does not name.** A gradient runs both
ways, so a caster coupled to a mass is a caster the mass is coupled to. **The page's counters are all
about breaking the line; the register's own failure mode says the greater danger is the line working
in the wrong direction** — coupling to a destination whose potential is *higher*, and being drawn
rather than travelling. Offered as the strongest available reading of the page's own "force the
crossing to overshoot by distorting the destination mass's own gravity during the coupling window."
Logged `SA-GAP-FLUXUS-REVERSE-COUPLING`.

*Four Theories.* **Consent Doctrine**, and cleanly: a gradient is a field quantity and nothing on the
page attributes intention to anything. **This is the least theologically strained page in the
batch**, which is what an anonymous Codex technique should look like.

**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Attraction Layer**, and the register is explicit —
"Transference makes the Attraction Layer such an oscillator." The Layer's failure state is
**Obsession Force** (`The Core Vocabulary` §III), and the page's second cost is remarkably close to
it: "sustained use of the technique wears a groove that makes people start reading as points in a
network rather than as people." **That is an Attraction-Layer degradation stated in behavioural terms
— a Layer that has been tuned to mass so often that it reads persons as mass — and the system's word
for recognition curdling is Obsession Force.** Developmental Tier at Stage V: **Harmonic** —
"The three layers have found a recurring rhythm the Continuum can read consistently"
(Part Twenty-One). Crystal State: `null`, no practitioner named. **The invoice:** "a half-turn of
vertiginous stagger while the body's sense of gravity catches up," plus the Crystal "ringing like a
struck bell." **Both are correct for the mechanism: a resonator that has just completed a transfer
rings down, and a body that has genuinely travelled at kilometres per second has a vestibular system
that has not been told.**

**The lens: Zeno's dichotomy.** Zeno of Elea, preserved in Aristotle's *Physics*
VI.9: to cross an interval you must first cross half of it, and half of that, and
so on without end, from which Zeno concluded that traversal is impossible and
motion an illusion. The technique is named for the interval, and it takes the
opposite side of the argument explicitly and at length: the caster *"genuinely
crossed the intervening distance rather than having erased and remade themselves
at the far end"*, the two masses are *"briefly in tune, and the gradient does the
rest"*, and the crossing is *"real traversal at extraordinary speed and never a
disappearance and a remaking."* That insistence is not decoration; it is what
makes the working's limits make sense. A real crossing needs a real line, which is
why *"the path must be a straight, unobstructed line between two masses"* and why
*"anything intersecting it stops the flow outright."* So the lens matters by being
refused. A school that accepts Zeno — and there is a long tradition of doing so,
from Parmenides to the Buddhist arguments against momentariness — reads the
technique as translation rather than motion, concludes there is no path for
anything to sit on, and therefore never thinks to put a shield on the line. That
is the cheapest counter on the page, and a Zenonian could not see it. The page
undermines its own anti-Zenonian position in one place, in its Target Response,
and this account logs that rather than choosing a side
(`SA-EM-FLUXUS-TRAVERSAL-VS-RECONSTITUTION`).

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Ren]` **Passage** · Root · Auren — the form whose
meaning is the working's entire content — laid across `[Eq]` **Equivalence** · Extension · Urion,
which is the tuning that makes two masses exchangeable. Both from the thirty unclassified forms in
`wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`. **`[Ps]` Passage, the Auren
synonym, and `[Rt]` Return, also Auren, are the near alternatives**, and the Index's note that the
Secondary Archon column is sparse but meaningful is worth applying: all three candidates sit at
Auren, so the choice is about register rather than about station. *Filing decision, not canon —
Isaac's ruling.*

**What boundary moves.** One quantity: **the resonant frequency of the caster's own Attraction
Layer, set equal to that of a chosen mass.** Not the distance. Not the intervening space. Only the
tuning — and the gradient does the rest, which is the register's own phrase and the page's.

**What the law then does on its own.** Everything, and all four of the page's limits are properties
of κ. Both endpoints must be genuine mass, because a coupling needs two resonators. The line must be
unbroken and straight, because near-field coupling is a line-of-sight quantity and an intervening
body is a third resonator loading the pair. The range is thirty metres, because 1/d³ means doubling
the distance costs eight times the coupling. And the crossing is continuous rather than
discontinuous, because coupled-mode transfer has no interval in which the energy is in neither
resonator. **Four limits, one boundary, nothing added — the tidiest derivation in the batch, and it
belongs to the only page with no named practitioner.**

The page's "What nobody knows" — whether the state between departure and arrival is truly
instantaneous to the caster or only reads that way to everyone else — **has a mechanism-level answer
and the numbers above are it.** Coupled-mode transfer takes a finite time π/(2κ), and the 30 m
crossing takes between 0.88 ms and 21.9 ms depending on which of the page's two figures governs.
**It is not instantaneous to anyone. It is below the resolution of some observers and not others,
and exactly which is decided by `SA-NUM-FLUXUS-INSTANT-VS-B-SPEED`.** Offered as the mechanism's
reading; the page's question stays open pending that ruling.

**Law V check.** Stage V sits **below** Refraction, so an external anchor is required: "Below Stage
VII, Refraction, a working requires voice, hand, ink or blood"
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). The page supplies **breath** — "released on a single
slow exhale" — with a held gaze as the targeting element. **The gate closes cleanly, and the page's
own counterplay depends on it: "Break the line physically … before the exhale completes." The anchor
is the timing window, and the counter is to beat it.**

**Failure mode — Field fault, primary.** "The room could not supply what the working assumed"
(`Two Sets of Books` §VII). The technique "fails outright in a zone with no meaningful gravity or
with chaotic, shifting gravity," and a Fixatio anchor on either endpoint prevents the coupling from
forming at all — both are the environment declining to supply the conditions, with the working
correct and producing nothing. A **Boundary fault** sits behind the cost: the tuning was set on mass
and the caster's own perception of persons drifts toward mass, which is the law running somewhere
unintended over a long span.

**A Path note, and the page gets the hard part right.** Its Path gate line reads "None (the source's
'Mind Path' is not a Path; it reads as Gnosis weighting)." **That is correct — the system's Paths are
Body, Spirit, Attraction and Fate throughout Part Seven, and there is no Mind Path.** Rejecting an
unattested Path rather than importing it is exactly the right call and the only page in the batch
that has to make it. **But "None" does not survive the gate table.** Harmonics **Projection**
"requires Attraction Path at Stage III to exceed C for field weight" (Part Seven), and at B-Grade
(176–275) the page's own governing Sub-Stat is above C (101–175). **So an Attraction-Path commitment
is required and the page declares none.** Logged `SA-NUM-FLUXUS-PATHGATE`.

**What the target sees and feels.** A held gaze between the caster and a second
mass, a calm that is visibly being maintained, and then a slow exhale — and the
caster is at the far mass, thirty metres away, with nothing in between that an
observer can resolve. What a bystander does **not** get is a warning about
direction, because the destination is chosen before the exhale and announced by
nothing but where the eyes are pointed. A projectile that happens to be struck on
the line is carried along its own vector and *"diverts rather than stopping"*,
which is the one part of the working that reaches anyone else. Then the price:
arrival costs about half a turn of *"vertiginous stagger while the body's sense of
gravity catches up to where it now stands"*, so for a second or two after every
crossing the caster is standing in a new place and not yet able to fight in it.
That window is the whole counter-tempo of the technique, and it is stated on the
page. The long cost is stranger and worse — repeated use *"leaves the caster's
Crystal ringing like a struck bell"*, and a real risk of *"learning to see people
as nodes on a map rather than as people"*, which is a working that changes the man
who uses it often.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** No practitioner is named, so η comes from the ladder:
Stage V ⇒ **Tier 5 · Expert**, band **0.60–0.70** (Part Nineteen, the Tier 5 row as corrected by
**R44-4** and read across the whole row by the `C-063` ruling) ⇒ **30–40 percent** as heat, sound and structural
bleed. **Middling efficiency does not make this working quiet, because the quantity it is a fraction
of is enormous:** a 30–40 % bleed on a 1.046×10⁹ J crossing is roughly **3×10⁸ to 4×10⁸ J** dumped at
the two endpoints, which would leave scorch marks at both ends of every crossing. Part Nineteen's
gloss on Tier 5 is "First genuine efficiency; the Crystal has learned to stop leaking," and at
0.60–0.70 the gloss and the figure agree. The leak is no longer the loudest thing about the caster.
It is still a third of a gigajoule. **`Two Sets of Books` §III supplies the consequence: "a
low-efficiency practitioner is loud, warm, and easy to find."** A caster using Fluxus Intervallum at
Stage V is not stealthy, whatever the technique looks like; the scorch marks are the tell, and they
are the Counterplay route a tracker reads.

**Does the physics close against the stratal account?** **The mechanism closes exceptionally well
and the two numbers do not close against each other.** Coupled-mode theory produces every one of the
page's four limits — two masses, an unbroken line, thirty metres, and genuine continuous traversal
rather than a cut — without a single rule being written, and it answers the page's own open question
about the interval by saying there is one and giving its length. **But the page's energy figure and
the Stage's speed band cannot both hold for a human mass.** The stated energy demands 5,114–33,912
m/s, which Stage V cannot reach; the Stage's ceiling of 1,372 m/s carries only C-Grade energy. Each
figure supports a different half of the page's own description of the crossing, and the choice
between them decides whether this technique is unresolvable or plainly visible.

**A note on what this page does right, since it is the only unattributed working in the batch.** It

it refuses to import an unattested Path, it cites the Grade table correctly, and its mechanism
paragraph is the most accurate restatement of a register law on any of the twenty-eight pages.
**Whatever is wrong with it is arithmetic, not method.**

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Break the boundary — obstruct the line, before the exhale.** *"Break the line
  physically (any obstruction placed between the two anchors before the exhale
  completes)."* This is the whole answer and it costs a shield, a body, a cloak
  thrown up, or a thrown rock with good timing. In register terms it is the purest
  *"interrupt the chain"* on any page here, because the chain is a **line in
  space** and Stage V is far below Refraction. The tell gives you the line and
  the exhale gives you the window.
- **Break the boundary — Lock the endpoints.** *"A Fixatio-based anchor on either
  endpoint prevents the coupling from forming at all"*, which is the register's
  **Lock** exactly: *"occupies a limit so no other limit can be set there."*
  Tenebra- or Zar'thuun-aligned power *"erases the strain-line the coupling
  depends on"*, and a Judicium interdiction field *"creates enough dissonance in
  the coupling to make it misfire"* — three different ways to make the sentence
  ungrammatical rather than to argue with it.
- **Deny the field — gravity, and the medium.** The technique *"does not function
  where gravity is absent or actively chaotic"*, and the page's own Counterplay
  adds the aggressive version: *"force the crossing to overshoot by distorting the
  destination mass's own gravity during the coupling window."* Family
  **Spatium**, whose suppressive ground the Family page gives as warp scarring,
  contested Domains and unmapped terrain; the Counterplay register names no
  Spatium terrain (`SA-GAP-COUNTERPLAY-TERRAIN`).
- **Break the body — meet the arrival, not the crossing.** The stagger is
  stated. A defender who knows the destination (the gaze told them) and can put
  something at it is fighting a man who cannot yet tell which way is down. This is
  *"numbers, correctly used"* at its most economical: one man watching each
  plausible endpoint.
- **Break the man — the groove it wears.** Repeated use is documented as changing
  how the caster sees people. That is Coherence drift with a specific cause, and
  the register's advice applies without alteration: *"You do not have to cause
  this. You have to notice it and wait."*

**The tell, stated plainly.** A caster goes still, locks a gaze on a particular
piece of mass thirty metres off, and breathes out slowly. The gaze is the
destination and the exhale is the trigger, so both halves of what an opponent
needs are on display before anything happens.

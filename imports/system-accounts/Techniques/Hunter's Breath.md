# System account · Hunter's Breath

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The effect in plain words.** He breathes out a cold ground-hugging mist, and
inside it nothing can find him by smell, by heat or by his Essence trace. Ordinary
eyesight still works.

**The real phenomenon.** Two, and the page names the second correctly.

*Obscuration by a scattering aerosol.* A cold fog of water droplets or ice crystals
attenuates radiation by Mie scattering, and the attenuation follows
Beer–Lambert: I/I₀ = e^(−βL), where the extinction coefficient for a droplet cloud
is β ≈ 3·LWC / (2·ρ_w·r) for liquid water content LWC and mean droplet radius r.

*Return below the noise floor.* Tenebra's register mechanism is radar-absorbent
design and it is stated exactly: "Something is undetectable when its return falls
below the noise floor of whatever is looking, which is achieved by absorbing
incident energy and by shaping surfaces so the little that reflects goes anywhere
except back at the observer"
(`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Limina — Entropy, Void and Mind.md`).
The register's own conclusion is the page's: "Its practitioners are not invisible.
They are quieter than the background, which is better."

**The governing law.** Beer–Lambert for the obscuration, and for the cold itself the
**coefficient of performance of a heat pump**: moving heat Q across a temperature
difference ΔT from a cold reservoir at T_c costs at minimum W = Q·ΔT/T_c. **This one
equation produces the page's own Limit and its own Weakness, with nothing added.**

**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Cloud: **15 m** (page). As a ground-hugging hemisphere, (2/3)π(15 m)³ =
  **7,069 m³**.
- **Signal to be hidden.** A 310 K body against a 273 K background radiates net
  σ(310⁴ − 273⁴) = 5.67×10⁻⁸ × 3.682×10⁹ = **209 W/m²**; across 1.8 m² of skin,
  **≈376 W** of infrared. That is the thing the mist has to bury.
- **Fog density needed for the page's figure.** A 50–60 % drop over 15 m means
  optical depth τ = −ln(0.5 to 0.4) = **0.69–0.92**, so β = **0.046–0.061 m⁻¹**.
  With r = 10 µm that needs LWC = 2·ρ_w·r·β/3 = **0.31–0.41 g/m³** — genuinely
  dense fog, of the kind that puts visibility near 50 m.
- **Water required:** 0.35 g/m³ × 7,069 m³ = **≈2.5 kg**, deposited from vapour.
- **Heat that must be removed.** Latent, at 2,838 J/g for vapour→ice:
  2.5×10³ g × 2,838 = **7.1×10⁶ J**. Sensible, to drop 7,069 m³ of air by 10 K:
  7,069 × 1.225 kg/m³ × 1,005 J/(kg·K) × 10 = **8.7×10⁷ J**. Total
  **≈9.4×10⁷ J (94 MJ)**.
- **Minimum work to move it, in cold air.** From T_c = 263 K with ΔT = 10 K, Carnot
  COP = 263/10 = **26.3**, so W ≥ 9.4×10⁷ ÷ 26.3 = **3.6×10⁶ J**.
- **The same cloud in warm air.** From T_c = 293 K needing ΔT = 30 K, COP = 9.8, and
  the sensible load triples: W ≥ (3 × 8.7×10⁷ + 7.1×10⁶) ÷ 9.8 = **2.7×10⁷ J**, a
  **7.5× increase**. **That is why the page says the working requires cold air to
  draw on, and it is why a warm or radiant domain overwhelms the mist.** Not a rule.
  A coefficient.

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** In the air, already. This is a
**pump**, not a source: the page says so — "Vohrin's cold-law needs an existing
gradient. It does not manufacture one from nothing" — and the COP arithmetic is what
makes that sentence load-bearing rather than decorative.

**A note on Grade, because it matters here.** Part Four's Grade table reads across
Sub-Stat value, **attack output** and travel speed. A 94 MJ thermal load sits far
below the A-Grade attack band (4.6×10¹⁰ – 4.184×10¹² J) and that is **not a
conflict**: the Grade this working is gated on is Dexterity, whose relevant Sub-Stat
is Feint (the entry that absorbed Silence under the Merge Ledger), and the page cites
**Dexterity-Silence 386** for exactly that reason. Grade-as-joules does not bind a
working whose product is signal suppression. Worth stating because two other pages in
this batch do read the energy column where they should not.

**Precedent we are not copying.** Concealment mists are standard — Naruto's Hidden
Mist, the smoke bombs of every stealth game. Those hide you from **sight**, which is
the one sense this working leaves alone and says so. The divergence is the
specificity: heat, scent and Essence go into one noise floor together, eyesight is
untouched, and the counter is therefore not a light source but a **different kind of
looking**.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** Draw across 7,069 m³, thin and near the ground. Two Families are
in tension over the terrain and the page is standing in the overlap: Caloria is
suppressed by "Thermal equilibrium, **saturated cold**, oxygen-poor air" while Limina
is favoured by "Darkness, silence, Aether-thin ground, ruins" and suppressed by
"Saturated Aether, crowd density, active Domains"
(`The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings`, Environmental Coupling). **So the
ground that best serves the Tenebra half — cold, dark, empty — is the ground that
begins to suppress the Caloria half**, because a working that pumps heat needs a
gradient and saturated cold has none. The page's stated requirement sits precisely on
that line. **Residue:** the lowest in the batch, by design; a Tenebra working's whole
object is to leave the noise floor where it found it. **Saturation:** not a limit.

**Wellspring stratum.** **Tenebra · *The Hidden Shadow*** (secondary on the page,
load-bearing in the mechanism), Family **Limina**, Physics Domain **entropy, void and
mind**. Its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Limina — Entropy, Void and Mind.md`:

> "Something is undetectable when its return falls below the noise floor of whatever
> is looking, which is achieved by absorbing incident energy and by shaping surfaces
> so the little that reflects goes anywhere except back at the observer. Tenebra
> applies both to the Shell. Its practitioners are not invisible. They are quieter
> than the background, which is better."

Register Stat Effect, through the Merge Ledger: Dexterity **Feint** (from Silence),
Harmonics **Suppression**, Dominion **Sense**. Register failure: "**Aspect
dependence.** Faceting works against expected angles, and a Tenebra practitioner
observed from the one direction they did not plan for returns a very bright signal
indeed."

**Primary: Vohrin — not one of the Sixty.** As on `Bloodbind Surge`: the Caloria
register's ten do not include Vohrin, and Vohrin appears in the system as the Titan
of Niflheim (`wiki/Cosmology & Metaphysics/The Fourteen Titans.md`), whose keyed art
requires Zenith or Revelation. Draven's card carries the same assignment. **Recorded,
not resolved** — `SA-UNATT-VOHRIN`. The page's own "What nobody knows" line ("whether
the mist masks him from Vohrin's own attention") reads very differently depending on
which way that rules.

*Four Theories.* **Consent Doctrine**, unstrained. A noise floor is a field quantity
and nothing on the page attributes intention to anything.

**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Aether Shell**, and the page has the
mechanism right — the mist's surface *is* the Shell's faceting projected outward, so
"both his biological heat signature and his Essence signature drop into the mist's
own noise floor **together**." That simultaneity is the working's whole value and it
is a Shell property, not a Core one. Developmental Tier at Stage VI: **Harmonic**.
Crystal State: **Refined** (card). **The invoice:** lung strain — "The cold he draws
in does not leave gently." Correct organ: the working's heat sink runs through
respiratory tissue, and drawing ~94 MJ of load through an airway is a thermal injury
mechanism, not a metaphor. Vitality Tolerance is the Sub-Stat that answers for it
("Resistance to environmental vectors the body must simply refuse: extreme
temperature …", Part Twelve), and the page cites it.

## 3 · Mechanism

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Sil]` **Silence** · Synonym · Auren —
the form's own meaning is the working's entire content, and the Category is Silentia,
so glyph and filing agree. Laid across `[RenB]` **Boundary** · Root · Zhaeren for the
15 m edge. Both from the thirty unclassified forms in
`wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`.
*Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling.*

**What boundary moves.** One quantity: **the noise floor inside a 15 m
hemisphere.** Not his emissions. The *threshold* against which his emissions are
measured. He does not get quieter. The room gets louder, in exactly the bands that
were looking for him.

**What the law then does on its own.** Everything. The mist clings low because cold
air is denser and a gravity current is what cold air makes. Scent goes because a
saturated aerosol scrubs volatiles. Heat goes because 376 W against a raised
background is no longer a contrast. Eyesight survives because the working never
raised the *visible* noise floor, and the page is right that ordinary sight is
unaffected. A thermal-inversion field lifts the mist because a gravity current
requires a density difference and an inversion removes it. **None of that is a rule
the technique carries. It is what cold air does.**

**Law V check.** Stage VI sits below Refraction, so an external anchor is required
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). The page supplies **breath** — the
inhale and the held exhale — which is the voice-anchor in its purest form.
**The gate closes cleanly.**

**Failure mode — Boundary fault.** "The condition was established imprecisely and the
law ran somewhere unintended" (`Two Sets of Books` §VII). The raised floor covers
three specified channels and only three. A "chorus-sensed or spirit-registering
presence read" was never inside the boundary, and the page's counter is therefore not
a stronger sensor but a **different band** — which is Tenebra's aspect dependence
stated as a sensing modality rather than as an angle. **That is the mechanism's answer
to the counterplay line and it needed nothing added.**

Behind it sits a **Field fault**: in warm air the working is correct and produces
almost nothing, because the COP has collapsed. The working does not fail. The room
fails to supply the gradient it assumed.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency — and here the page and the card disagree.**
The card gives **η ≈ 0.55**, which puts **45 %** of every expenditure out as heat,
sound and structural bleed. **The page instead asserts "Draven's Class III
efficiency,"** and Class III Resonant is rated at "**Five to ten percent loss**"
(Part Seventeen) — η 0.90–0.95. Those are not close. Worse, Part Seventeen places
Class III at "Late Refraction," i.e. **Stage VII**, while Draven is Stage VI, where
"Aether Class first emerges" as **Class I Muridic**. Three readings of the same
practitioner: η 0.55 (card), η 0.90–0.95 (this page), η 0.60–0.70 (Class I Muridic's
own 30–40 % loss). The Fracture already flags the last two as an unresolved
"efficiency conflict" it inherited rather than created. **All three recorded; none
resolved.** `SA-NUM-HUNTERSBREATH-CLASS-III`.

There is a real consequence, not just a bookkeeping one: **a 45 % bleed on a
concealment working is self-defeating.** At η 0.55 roughly 7.7×10⁷ J of the pumped
load leaves as heat and sound in the immediate vicinity of a man trying not to be
found. At η 0.93 it is 6.6×10⁶ J. Which figure governs decides whether this technique
works at all.

**Does the physics close against the stratal account?** Yes on the mechanism, no on
the efficiency. The pump arithmetic is clean and it *generates* both of the page's
stated limits without being asked. But the two accounts cannot both be kept while η
is contested: at the card's 0.55 the working produces 77 MJ of waste heat around a man
hiding from heat-seekers, which is a working that defeats itself. The physics does not
choose. It only makes the stakes of the choice explicit, which is what it is for.

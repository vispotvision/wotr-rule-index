# System account · Hypnather Descent Rite

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The effect in plain words.** He forces a mind into a rest-state it did not choose, stops
the process halfway, and the target is left heavy, foggy, and seeing something that is not
in the room.

**The real phenomenon.** **Structural relaxation in a glass, and the Kovacs memory
effect** — which is a named, measured phenomenon and is *exactly* what the page describes.

A glass below its transition temperature is not at equilibrium: it is a liquid that stopped
rearranging before it finished. Left alone it continues to relax toward the configuration it
never reached — **physical ageing** — and the relaxation is markedly non-exponential,
following a stretched exponential exp[−(t/τ)^β] with β typically **0.3–0.6**. The effective
τ itself grows with waiting time, so an older glass relaxes more slowly than a younger one.

**And if you interrupt the ageing partway with a jump in conditions, the system does not
resume where it left off. It overshoots into a state that is *worse ordered* than either
endpoint, and holds there.** That is the **Kovacs effect**, first measured in polymer volume
recovery in the 1960s and reproduced in enthalpy, in dielectric response and in granular
compaction since.

Hypnather's register analogue names the first half and its Failure field names the second:
"Structural relaxation. Physical ageing of a glass toward its equilibrium configuration" …
"**Interrupted relaxation.** Disturb the process partway and the configuration locks where
it stood, worse ordered than before it began"
(`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Limina — Entropy, Void and Mind.md`).
**The register wrote the Kovacs effect into a Wellspring's failure mode. This page
weaponises it.**

**The governing law.** Stretched-exponential relaxation with an age-dependent time
constant, and the non-monotonic Kovacs response to an interruption. One consequence matters
more than any other and the page builds its entire counter on it: **the process only runs
while the system is quiescent.** Disturb it hard enough and there is no relaxation to
interrupt, because there was no relaxation.

**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Range: **60 m**, line-of-sight held (page) — "the shortest reach of Aeldoris's three
  documented techniques," and the physics says why: a forced quiescence needs a continuous
  anchor, and an anchor is a coupling that falls off with distance.
- **Relaxation timescale at the glass transition: τ ≈ 100 s**, which is the operational
  definition of T_g. So a descent run to completion is a **hundred-second** process, and
  interrupting it inside a combat exchange is not difficult — it is the default.
- **Sleep-onset comparison.** Human sleep latency runs **600–1,200 s** and slow-wave onset
  **1,200–1,800 s**. Forcing quiescence on a combat timescale of order **10 s** is a
  compression of roughly **100×**, which is the honest measure of how violent the rite is.
- Stretched-exponential β: **0.3–0.6**. A β well below 1 means the relaxation has a long
  tail — so the page's Consequence ("for as long past the working's release as the three
  interrupted processes take to settle honestly on their own") is a **long tail, not a
  clean recovery**, and the physics supports the page being vague about how long.
- Three simultaneous forced failures, each with its own timescale: the Hypnather lock
  (τ ≈ 10²  s), the Letheveil pass (the 3–10 s labile window documented in the
  `Letheveil Memory-Quake` account), the Oneirion projection error (instantaneous). **Three
  different clocks through one conduit, which is the page's own stated cost.**

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** Nowhere in particular, again. Structural
relaxation is spontaneous — it is the system's own free energy driving it downhill — so the
working does not supply the relaxation, it supplies the *quiescence* and then the
*interruption*. Both are boundary conditions. As with `Chorda Somnii` and
`Letheveil Memory-Quake`, the physical account is nearly empty of joules and the stratal
account is expensive, which is the standard working as intended.

**Precedent we are not copying.** Sleep and paralysis effects are genre furniture — the
Sleep spell, Naruto's genjutsu paralysis, every status ailment. Those apply a condition.
**This one induces three documented failure modes on purpose and holds them open**, and its
counter is not a resistance stat but a *precondition*: do not be still. The divergence is
that the technique has no effect of its own at all. It is entirely built out of other
things going wrong on command.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** Draw is a held line to one body, not a field — the narrowest footprint
of Aeldoris's four. Limina favours "Darkness, silence, Aether-thin ground, ruins" and is
suppressed by "Saturated Aether, crowd density, active Domains"
(`The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings`, Environmental Coupling). **Silence is
load-bearing in a way this page could use: a working that needs the target quiescent is
helped by ground that is quiet, and the terrain table and the mechanism agree.**
**Residue:** minimal on the ground, heavy on the target — an interrupted relaxation leaves a
worse-ordered configuration behind, so the residue is *in the person*, and a competent reader
would find it. **Saturation:** not a limit.

**Wellspring stratum.** **Hypnather · *The Sleep Crown*** (primary), Family **Limina**,
Physics Domain **entropy, void and mind**. Its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Limina — Entropy, Void and Mind.md`:

> "A glass is a liquid that stopped moving before it finished arranging itself, and left
> undisturbed it continues to relax toward the ordered state it never reached, slowly and
> only while quiescent. Hypnather grants the Crystal that behaviour. The reorganisation
> happens during genuine rest and cannot be forced, which is why Hypnather practitioners
> sleep like the dead and wake structurally different."

**Letheveil · *The Waters of Forgetting*** (law quoted in full in the
`Letheveil Memory-Quake` account) and **Oneirion · *The Wellspring of Dream*** (law quoted
in full in the `Chorda Somnii` account) supply the second and third layers.

**This is the most system-native design in the batch and it deserves saying plainly.** The
rite does not invent an effect. It takes the **Failure** field of three registered
Wellsprings and induces all three deliberately:

| Current | Register's documented failure | What it becomes here |
|---|---|---|
| Hypnather | "**Interrupted relaxation.** Disturb the process partway and the configuration locks where it stood, worse ordered than before it began." | Limb heaviness, slowed reflexes |
| Letheveil | "**Generation loss.** Each pass discards a little more, and a memory compressed repeatedly degrades toward an artefact the practitioner still experiences as a memory." | Short-term amnesia |
| Oneirion | "**Projection error.** The trajectory was correct in the manifold and lands nowhere in particular when it is mapped back down into a room." | False sensory input |

The page states all three and states them accurately. **Nothing was added; three Failure
fields were read as instructions.** That is exactly what a technique is supposed to be under
the Index's reading — a boundary imposed on a law already running — and here the law already
running is a law already *failing*.

**Hypnather's own law is also the counter, and the page is right to lead with it.** "The
reorganisation happens during genuine rest and **cannot be forced**." A hard enough shock
breaks the quiescence precondition, and there is nothing left to interrupt. **The page's
counterplay is the register's own sentence, quoted.**

*Four Theories.* **Consent Doctrine** on the page's own framing, but the page's method
strains it harder than anything else in the batch: a working built by **forcing three
currents to fail on command** is difficult to describe as a river recognising a channel. The
**Debt Position** reads it more naturally — three accounts opened and settled somewhere he
cannot see — and the page's Memory Backlash ("some of what he extracts does not stay with
the target") is a settlement arriving at the wrong address. **Noted, not resolved**, and it
is the page in this batch where the Four Theories question is most worth Isaac's attention.
Logged `SA-GAP-DESCENT-FORCED-FAILURE`.

**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Aether Shell** as conduit for all three forced
processes — the page is explicit and correct ("routing three forced failure-states through
his own Shell as the conduit"). Shell failure states are "Clouding or Rupture"
(`The Core Vocabulary` §III); three incompatible processes through one conduit is the
textbook route to Clouding. The **Essence Core** takes the Memory Backlash. Developmental
Tier at Stage VII: **Resonant**. Crystal State: **Refined, residue-heavy** (card) — and
note that the card's own descriptor is *residue-heavy*, which is what a practitioner who
routinely runs interrupted relaxations through himself would be. **The invoice:** Sleep Debt
and Memory Backlash. **Sleep Debt is Hypnather's bill arriving on the caster**, and it is a
particularly clean cost because Hypnather's benefit is recovery-during-rest: a practitioner
who forces others out of quiescence loses his own access to it.

## 3 · Mechanism

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Lk]` **Lock** · Counter · Thalen — one of the
Index's only two Counter forms, described as occupying "a limit so no other limit can be set
there," which is precisely what a configuration locked mid-relaxation is — laid across
`[Rv]` **Reverie** · Extension · Maelor. Both from the thirty unclassified forms in
`wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`. The Counter class
is the right family for this working: the Index says "What a counter does instead is impose
a boundary that **prevents another boundary from being established**," and an interrupted
relaxation is a system prevented from reaching the configuration it was heading for.
*Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling.*

**What boundary moves.** Two, and they are sequential rather than simultaneous, which is
unusual: **first, the quiescence condition is forced true**; **then, partway through, it is
forced false.** The working is the *transition between two boundary conditions*, and the
damage is entirely in the transition. That is the Kovacs protocol, run on a person.

**What the law then does on its own.** Everything, in three directions at once. The
configuration locks worse-ordered because that is what an interrupted relaxation does. The
memory pass runs indiscriminately because Letheveil's discrimination requires the *owner's*
cooperation in identifying what is load-bearing, and there is none. The false percept lands
because a manifold trajectory mapped down without a real sensory coordinate lands
arbitrarily, and here the arbitrary landing is chosen. **All three are failures, all three
are documented, and none of them had to be designed.**

**Law V check.** Stage VII **is** Refraction, where the internal chain becomes available
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). The page says "Declared, line-of-sight held" —
so he anchors, and the anchor is the sightline rather than voice or ink. **The page is
explicit about why the anchor is required and the reason is mechanical, not Stage-related:**
"without it the Oneirion component's forced landing has nothing to pin its coordinate to and
simply misses, the way an honest projection error does." **Good reasoning, and it is the
only page in the batch that distinguishes a Law V anchor from a targeting anchor.**

**Failure mode — Structural fault.** "The Crystal could not hold what the boundary
permitted. Overchannel, Backlash, Fracture" (`Two Sets of Books` §VII). Three forced
failure-states through one Shell, with a Core taking whatever bleeds back. The page's two
costs are that sentence split in two.

A **Field fault** is the counter: a target who cannot be made quiescent leaves the working
correct and inert, and a well-developed Spirit Path Crystal resists having its
configuration-space trajectory hijacked because Spirit Path *is* precise coherent
projection. Both are on the page and both are right.

The page's "What nobody knows" — why the held forget their purpose before they forget the
pain — has a mechanism-level candidate worth offering rather than asserting. In the
stretched-exponential picture, different degrees of freedom relax on different timescales,
and **the slowest-relaxing modes are the most weakly coupled ones**. Purpose is an
abstraction with few couplings; pain is a signal with many. On that reading purpose sits in
the fastest-lost band and pain in the slowest, and it is a property of coupling density
rather than of cruelty. Offered as a candidate, not as canon. **The page also notes the same
open question appears on "Vohrin's Stillwater Rite," and that cross-reference is worth
flagging: the system's Vohrin is a Titan whose keyed compound is the Stillwater Draught
(`wiki/Cosmology & Metaphysics/The Fourteen Titans.md`), which is evidence on the Titan
side of `SA-UNATT-VOHRIN`.**

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** η ≈ 0.65 (card): **35 percent** of every
expenditure leaves as heat, sound and structural bleed. Running three processes means three
bleed paths, and the page's Memory Backlash — "some of what he extracts does not stay with
the target" — is a **structural** bleed with a destination, which is rarer and worse than
heat. As on his other three pages, 0.65 sits **above Tier 5 · Expert's 0.50–0.60 ceiling**
(`SA-NUM-ETA-TIER`).

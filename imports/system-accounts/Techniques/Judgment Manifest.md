# System account · Judgment Manifest

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** He gives up to three Principles bodies and lets them enforce
themselves across two kilometres for about twenty-five seconds. A fourth will not stand.

**The real phenomenon.** **Solitons, and the multi-pulse energy quantisation of a gain-limited
medium.** The page names the first and the second is what derives its hard ceiling.

*A soliton* is a solution to a nonlinear wave equation in which dispersion and nonlinearity balance
exactly. It propagates without changing shape, it survives collisions with other solitons, and it
requires no continuous input to hold its form — which is the whole of what the page claims for its
constructs. In an optical fibre the balance is between group-velocity dispersion and the Kerr
nonlinearity, governed by the **nonlinear Schrödinger equation**,
i·∂ψ/∂z + ½·∂²ψ/∂t² + |ψ|²ψ = 0. A **breather** is a higher-order solution that oscillates
periodically about a soliton shape — exactly the page's "the way a breather mode in a driven medium
persists on its own energy budget once formed."

*And the ceiling.* This is where the page's flat assertion of three becomes derivable. In a
gain-limited cavity — a mode-locked laser is the canonical case — solitons carry a **quantised
energy**: each pulse takes a fixed quantum, so the cavity supports ⌊available gain ÷ pulse energy⌋
pulses and no more. Push past that and the system does not add a pulse; it **redistributes energy
among the existing ones until one collapses.** This is documented multi-pulsing behaviour and it is
precisely the page's mechanism: "the local Aether field can only carry three stable solitons at
once at Francis's output before a fourth cannibalises one of the first three."

**The page did not need to assert three as a rule. Three is what an energy budget divided by a
pulse quantum comes out as.**

**The governing law.** The NLS soliton condition: the fundamental (N = 1) soliton requires a
specific amplitude-width product, and an N-soliton requires **N²** times the fundamental power.
So three separate fundamental solitons cost **3×** the power of one, while a single third-order
soliton would cost **9×** and would breathe rather than hold. **Three stable constructs is the
cheap configuration and one triple-strength construct is three times dearer** — a real, checkable
consequence the page could use and does not.

**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Field: **2,000 m** range (page) ⇒ (4/3)π(2,000 m)³ = **3.35×10¹⁰ m³**. **The largest single
  claimed volume in this batch, by an order of magnitude over `Crimson Dirge`.**
- Duration: **30 s**, the page's five turns at six seconds each.
- Constructs: **3**, hard ceiling. Power cost of three fundamentals: **3×** one. Power cost of one
  third-order: **9×**, and unstable.
- Soliton robustness: solitons survive mutual collisions with their shapes intact, which is the
  physical reason the page's constructs "route around the standing rules the earlier ones already
  imposed" rather than annihilating on contact.
- Soliton fragility: the balance is exact, so **perturbing either the dispersion or the
  nonlinearity destroys the solution.** That is a single-sentence derivation of the page's own
  weakness — a Limina working "degrades a construct's conceptual clarity … and an unclear construct
  stops enforcing anything."
- EX-Grade band for a construct at full output: **1.24×10²⁹ – 6.906×10³⁷ J** (Part Four), with
  Part Four's Zenith caveat attached (`SA-NUM-ZENITH-CONVENTIONAL-METRICS`).

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** In the local Aether field, and the page says so —
"the local Aether field can only carry three stable solitons at once at Francis's output." **This
is the only page in the batch that states its own limit as a property of the field's carrying
capacity rather than of the practitioner's reserve**, and it is therefore the page that most
directly needs the quantity the system does not have: a numeric Aetheric Density. The whole ceiling
of three is an Aether-stratum budget and **there are no units for it.**
`SA-GAP-AETHERIC-DENSITY`, and this page is the strongest argument in the batch for closing that
gap.

**Precedent we are not copying.** Autonomous summons are everywhere — Fate's Servants, Jujutsu
Kaisen's shikigami, the Persona franchise entire. Those are *creatures with loyalty*. These are
**standing instances of a Principle**, which "enforce" rather than obey, and the page's own open
question is whether they think at all. The divergence is that the cap of three is a field
property rather than a mana cost, and the counter is to muddy the construct's definition rather
than to out-damage it.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** 3.35×10¹⁰ m³, and the working's ceiling *is* an Aether-stratum quantity.
Fulguria wants "Dry air, conductive substrate, clear line of sight" and is suppressed by
"Faraday-caged interiors, heavy ferrous shielding, dense fog"; Caloria wants "Steep thermal
gradients, combustible atmosphere, geothermal ground"
(`The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings`, Environmental Coupling). **A consequence worth
stating: because the cap is a field-capacity cap, ground matters more on this page than on any
other in the batch. In thin Aether he gets fewer than three, and in a rich vein he might get more
— and the page treats three as architectural rather than environmental.** Logged
`SA-GAP-JUDGMENT-CAP-IS-FIELD-OR-ARCHITECTURE`. **Residue:** "the field ends up layered with law
effects," which is residue as **standing law** rather than as a trace — the heaviest kind the
system describes, and the page names it. **Saturation:** three solitons at EX output across
3.35×10¹⁰ m³ is a saturation candidate on the same reasoning as `Crimson Dirge` and
`Dirge Ascension`; logged under `SA-GAP-DIRGE-SATURATION`.

**Wellspring stratum.** **Judicium · *The Wellspring of Truth*** (primary), Family **Fulguria**,
Physics Domain **electromagnetism**. Its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Fulguria — Electromagnetism.md`:

> "Every element absorbs and emits at wavelengths fixed by its own electron structure, and no
> substance can present another's spectrum. Judicium tunes Gnosis to that principle: the
> practitioner reads the characteristic lines of a Crystal, a technique, or a lie, and identifies
> composition from signature alone. Eddy-current inspection finds the crack under the paint without
> removing the paint."

**And Judicium is why a construct can "measure" a target at all:** the page has it that "A target
inside the field is measured against the Principle the nearest construct carries, and the
construct's behaviour changes according to that measurement." A spectroscopic criterion is the only
instrument in the register that performs an identification without contact, and the constructs
each need one.

**Exuroth · *The Trial Flame*** (secondary), Family **Caloria** — law quoted in full in the
`Crimson Dirge` account. Carried on this page without a stated job, which is a small gap: on
`Final Mercy` Exuroth supplies compression and on `Dirge Ascension` it supplies tolerance, and here
it supplies nothing named. **The available reading is that Exuroth is what lets a construct survive
the repeated thermal-shock cycle of enforcing a law it is also being tested by**, but the page does
not say so and it is not a researcher's to add.

Register failures in play. Judicium's "**Spectral overload**" — three constructs each reading every
target in a two-kilometre field is spectral overload by construction, and it is a candidate answer
to the page's "Whether the constructs think, in any sense that matters, or only enforce": **a
reader in permanent spectral overload cannot prioritise, and something that cannot prioritise
cannot be said to be deciding.** Offered as a candidate, not as canon.

*Four Theories.* **Consent Doctrine**, and the page's Category note is doing real system work:
Animatria for "projection of a fragment of the caster's own Essence Core into an independently
operating construct," with "Vocatia (Attraction-Aligned) as a secondary reading, since each
construct also has to be recognised by the field whose law it enforces." **That second half is the
Consent Doctrine stated as a filing consequence** — a construct enforcing law needs the field to
recognise it, and recognition is what Vocatia is: "Invocation by recognition and correspondence …
Vocatia is not domination — it's the negotiation of shared resonance"
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Magical Categories — Unified Taxonom.md`). **The page's dual-Category
reading is correct and unusually well argued.**

One system consequence the page does not name and should: the Categories page says "**Animatria
fails completely in Silence Realms**: the construct's continuity depends on a connection back to
its source, which Harmonic Silence severs" (same file). **A Silence Realm is therefore a hard
counter to this working and it is not on the page's counterplay list.** Offered as a gap.

**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Essence Core**, fragmented outward — Animatria is "a small
detached fragment of the caster's Essence Core given temporary independent operation"
(`The Magical Categories`), so each construct is a piece of him standing at a distance. Core failure
state is "Fracture into Shards" (`The Core Vocabulary` §III), **and a working that detaches three
fragments of a Core is operating one step from its own failure state by design.** The **Attraction
Layer** carries the recognition each construct needs. Developmental Tier at Stage XIV:
**Crystallized Soul** — "Self and law correspond with near-perfect precision" (Part Twenty-One),
which is the structural prerequisite for a fragment of the self *being* a law rather than carrying
one. Crystal State: card says "Absolute Crystal," which is a Developmental Tier and not a State
(`SA-CROSS-FRANCIS-ABSOLUTE-CRYSTAL`). **The invoice:** "Mild paradox fatigue and Aether recoil
after each summoning, worse the more constructs are standing at once." **Scaling with construct
count is the correct invoice shape for a gain-budget mechanism and the page gets it right.**

**The lens: the seven Hermetic Principles — and where they actually come from.**
The page names *"Hermetic Principles"*, and the enumerated seven that phrase calls
to mind (Mentalism, Correspondence, Vibration, Polarity, Rhythm, Cause and Effect,
Gender) are not ancient at all: they were set out in *The Kybalion* (1908),
published under the name "Three Initiates" and generally attributed to William
Walker Atkinson, which presents a modern American New Thought system as a
recovered Egyptian one. The genuinely old material — the *Corpus Hermeticum*, the
*Asclepius*, the *Emerald Tablet*'s *quod est inferius est sicut quod est
superius* — has no such list. That gap is the lens, and it is a better hook than a
tidy match would be: a school that enumerates its Principles with confidence and
cites an ancient authority for the enumeration is doing something a reader can
catch it at, and an in-world practitioner who has embodied seven principles from a
late compilation has built his constructs on a filing decision rather than on a
law. The lens also supplies the misreading. The *Kybalion*'s first principle is
Mentalism — *THE ALL is MIND* — from which a school would conclude that a
construct is sustained by belief and can be dismissed by refusing it. The page
says something quite different: each construct *"behaves as a soliton, a
self-reinforcing wave that holds its shape by its own nonlinear structure rather
than by continuous input from its caster"*, and what unmakes one is a **Limina
current degrading its conceptual clarity**, which is not disbelief but damage. A
company drilled to disbelieve will stand there disbelieving while it is measured
against a Principle and found wanting.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Ath]` **Command** · Root · Irath — the constructs
enforce, which is `[Ath]` before it is anything else — laid across `[Frm]` **Form** · Extension ·
Thalen, which is what gives a Principle a body. Both from the thirty unclassified forms in
`wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`. **`[Ho]` Order · Extension
· Valen is the alternative worth putting beside it**, and the choice is substantive: `[Ath]` files
the constructs as *commanding*, `[Ho]` files them as *ordering*, and the page's own open question
about whether they think sits exactly on that line. *Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling.*

**What boundary moves.** One quantity: **the balance point between dispersion and nonlinearity in
the local field, set so that a Principle-shaped disturbance is a stable solution.** He does not hold
the constructs up. He makes the field one in which they are self-sustaining, and then they are.

**What the law then does on its own.** Everything, and every one of the page's four constraints is
soliton behaviour. They persist without input because a soliton is self-reinforcing. They survive
each other because solitons survive collisions. A fourth cannot be added because the field's gain
budget is quantised and adding a pulse redistributes rather than accumulates. They are destroyed by
conceptual disruption because the soliton balance is *exact* and perturbing either term dissolves
the solution. **Four stated limits, one boundary, and the page's hard ceiling of three stops being
an assertion.**

**Law V check.** Stage XIV sits far above Refraction, so no material anchor is needed
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). The page gives only the calling — "Francis calls up to
three Hermetic Principles at once and gives each a body" — which is the Internalised vector, and
consistent with the rest of his four pages.

**Failure mode — Structural fault, primary; Field fault, secondary.**
Structural: "The Crystal could not hold what the boundary permitted" (`Two Sets of Books` §VII) —
paradox fatigue and Aether recoil scaling with constructs active, three detached Core fragments
against a Core whose failure state is fracturing into shards.
Field: "The room could not supply what the working assumed" — and on this page the Field fault is
the *ceiling itself*. Three is not a rule he obeys; it is the field declining to carry a fourth.
**The page says this and it is the right reading:** "The ceiling of three is architectural, not a
matter of Francis's control; summoning a fourth collapses something already standing rather than
adding to it."

**The page's own gate observation, checked and confirmed.** It says: "Because Francis carries
genuine Fate Path here, Dominion Sovereignty's own gate is satisfied and the constructs' law-claims
hold structurally; the weak point sits instead in Limina, where none of Judicium, Fulguria or
Caloria give him any native resistance to conceptual disruption." Against Part Seven: Dominion
**Sovereignty** "requires Fate Path at Stage VI to exceed A, and Stage IX to exceed SSS where the
imposed law encodes abstract conceptual truths," and Dominion **Command** "requires Attraction Path
at Stage VIII to exceed SS." **Fate Path satisfies the first; the second is an Attraction gate and
the page carries Command as a governing Sub-Stat without an Attraction commitment.** So the
observation is sound on Sovereignty and incomplete on Command. `SA-NUM-JUDGMENT-COMMAND-GATE`.
**But the Limina-coverage point is entirely correct and is the sharpest self-diagnosis on any page
in this batch:** his three Families give him nothing against conceptual disruption, and the
counter list is Limina from end to end.

**What the target sees and feels.** Up to three bodies, each of which is a
Principle with a shape, spread across two kilometres for about twenty-five
seconds, and none of them waiting to be told what to do — they enforce *"their own
Principle as living law rather than waiting to be told to."* What that means on the
receiving end is that you are not being attacked so much as **assessed**: *"a
target inside the field is measured against the Principle the nearest construct
carries, and the construct's behaviour changes according to that measurement."*
Two men standing together can therefore get entirely different treatment from the
same construct, and neither of them is being treated arbitrarily. The constructs
mostly fight *"well under"* their EX-Grade ceiling in order to stay standing,
which is the soliton bargain made visible: they hold their shape on their own
energy budget, and spending it hard is spending the thing that keeps them
coherent. Three is a wall, not a preference — *"summoning a fourth collapses
something already standing rather than adding to it"* — so an opponent who can
bait a fourth has taken one off the field for free. Francis, meanwhile, is paying
what the page calls **mild** paradox fatigue, which is the fault this section
logs.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** The card gives **η = 0.93**, so **7 percent** as heat,
sound and structural bleed — and on this page the bleed has a specific consequence the page does not
draw: **waste in a gain-limited cavity reduces the available gain, which reduces how many pulses
it can carry.** A 7 % bleed is 7 % off the budget that sets the ceiling of three. At η above 1.0,
where Tier 8's band says he should be, "the environment becomes a co-author" (Part Nineteen) and
**the ceiling should rise.** So η is not a bookkeeping detail here; it is a term in the construct
count. Stage XIV ⇒ Tier 8 · Archmaster, band 0.95–1.2, and 0.93 is below the floor:
`SA-NUM-ETA-TIER`.

## 5 · Counterplay and the challenge

**The fairness check** (`.claude/skills/wotr-write/references/fair-play.md`).

| Test | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Costs something that hurts | **FAIL** | The page's own word is *"**mild** paradox fatigue and Aether recoil after each summoning, worse the more constructs are standing at once."* For three autonomous law-enforcing constructs across a **two-kilometre** field for twenty-five seconds, at a Stage whose other techniques bill one percent of Soul Crystal mass (`Dirge Ascension`) and four percent of memory (`Final Mercy`), "mild" is not a price. There is no EU figure, no fraction of reserve, no Starvation margin and no stated point at which a fourth summoning becomes unaffordable rather than merely architecturally impossible. **The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Break the boundary — degrade clarity, which is the documented answer.** *"A
  Limina working, Oneirion's dream-logic or Letheveil's forgetting-current,
  degrades a construct's conceptual clarity the way the source describes, and an
  unclear construct stops enforcing anything."* In register terms this is **Knot**:
  the construct is a coherent self-reinforcing structure, and a Knot *"entangles a
  path so it cannot be traced cleanly."* A soliton that cannot hold its own shape
  is not defeated; it stops existing.
- **Break the boundary — bait the fourth.** The ceiling is three and a fourth
  *"collapses something already standing."* An opponent who can present a threat
  Francis will answer with a new construct has removed an existing one at no cost.
  This is the register's *"Force the Seal early"* applied to a summoning budget: make
  him close one operation by opening another.
- **Deny the field — Silence, and the Family terrain.** *"Animatria constructs
  fail completely because their continuity runs back to a source the Silence has
  severed."* Whether that applies to a soliton that explicitly does **not** need
  continuous input from its caster is an open question this account has already
  logged (`SA-GAP-JUDGMENT-SILENCE-REALM`) — and it is exactly the kind of
  question a player should be able to argue at the table. Families **Fulguria /
  Caloria**: dense fog, a shielded interior, thermal equilibrium, saturated cold.
- **Break the body — twenty-five seconds, and he is somewhere in the two
  kilometres.** The constructs enforce; they are not him. *"Distance"* and
  *"numbers, correctly used"* both apply to the man who called them, and after
  about twenty-five seconds there is nothing standing between you and him but
  paradox fatigue the page has described as mild.
- **Break the man — Rigidity, on a Principle.** Each construct enforces *its own
  Principle as living law*, and a law that is not negotiable by its bearer is the
  register's cruellest handle: *"Present him with the situation his own law handles
  worst and he will handle it that way regardless."* A Principle-bound construct
  measures you and then does what the Principle says. Arrange to be measured
  favourably.

**The tell, stated plainly.** Three bodies arrive at once, each one obviously a
law rather than a person, and they begin enforcing without being told to. The
count of them tells you how close he is to his own ceiling.

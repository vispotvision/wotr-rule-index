# System account · Letheveil Memory-Quake

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The effect in plain words.** A pulse across twenty-five metres takes the last few
seconds out of everyone in it, and whatever they were building out of those seconds goes
with them.

**The real phenomenon.** **The memory-consolidation window**, and the page's numbers are
right for the right reason.

A freshly encoded trace is not yet a memory. It is labile, and it requires
protein-synthesis-dependent consolidation to become stable. Before consolidation, the
trace is destroyable — by electroconvulsive shock, by protein-synthesis inhibitors, and,
most relevantly here, **by a concussive event**. Clinical post-traumatic retrograde
amnesia characteristically erases **the seconds to minutes immediately preceding the
insult** and leaves older memory intact, because older memory has already consolidated.

**The page says "Memory window 3 to 10 seconds (source, already real units)" and flags it
as sourced rather than derived. It is also correct clinically.** That interval is precisely
the retrograde window a concussive insult takes. The page did not have to be right about
this and is.

The counter is equally well-grounded. The page says: "A sharp pain stimulus restores the
scaffolding a target was about to lose." **Noradrenergic arousal enhances consolidation** —
a strong adrenergic signal at or immediately after encoding strengthens the trace rather
than weakening it, which is the best-replicated finding in the emotional-memory literature.
A pain stimulus is an adrenergic signal. **The page's counterplay is documented
neuroscience, not a game balance decision.**

**The governing law.** Consolidation is a threshold process in time: a trace crosses from
labile to stable, and only the labile side is reachable. Everything the page lists as a
Limitation follows from that one sentence — older memory is untouched, unconscious targets
are untouched, and a held physical anchor survives because rehearsal against an external
cue re-encodes the trace inside the window.

**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Field: **25 m** radius (page) ⇒ (4/3)π(25 m)³ = **6.54×10⁴ m³**.
- Window: **3–10 s** (page, sourced).
- Shockwave: **≈5 s** (page's estimate, "1 turn" at 5 s/turn).
- **Information destroyed, per target.** Human working memory holds about **4 ± 1**
  chunks. A 3–10 s combat sequence at roughly one chunk per 0.5 s is **6 to 20 chunks**;
  at ~10 bits of specificity per chunk that is **60 to 200 bits**.
- **The thermodynamic floor.** Landauer's principle puts the minimum energy to erase one
  bit at kT·ln2; at body temperature, 1.381×10⁻²³ × 310 × 0.693 = **2.97×10⁻²¹ J per
  bit**. For 200 bits: **5.9×10⁻¹⁹ J.**
- **So the irreducible physical cost of erasing what this working erases, for one target,
  is under one attojoule.** Scale it to a crowd of twenty inside 25 m and it is still
  **≈10⁻¹⁷ J** — about the kinetic energy of a single air molecule at room temperature.

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** **There is effectively no energy budget,
and that is the finding.** The working destroys information, and information has a
thermodynamic price that is sub-attojoule at this scale. Every joule that *is* spent goes
into the boundary — into reaching 6.54×10⁴ m³ and into distinguishing labile traces from
consolidated ones. This is the system's own claim at its most extreme: "The practitioner
does not supply the joules. They supply the boundary condition, and **the boundary
condition is cheap in absolute terms and expensive in the only currency a soul has**"
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Physical Account Two Sets of Books.md` §III). Alongside
`Chorda Somnii`, this is the batch's cleanest demonstration that the two accounts are not
translations of each other.

**Precedent we are not copying.** Memory attacks are everywhere — Men in Black's
neuralyser, Naruto's genjutsu, the mind-wipes of a hundred CRPGs. Those take **specific**
memories, usually the plot-relevant ones. This one cannot choose: it takes whatever has
not yet set, across everyone present, **and it takes one of his own on every release**. The
divergence is that the selection criterion is *chronological and mechanical* rather than
narrative, and the caster is inside the radius.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** Draw across 6.54×10⁴ m³, released once rather than held. Limina is
favoured by "Darkness, silence, Aether-thin ground, ruins" and suppressed by "Saturated
Aether, **crowd density**, active Domains" (`The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings`,
Environmental Coupling). **Crowd density is the relevant suppression and it is in tension
with the working's own design:** an area memory-quake wants targets, and Limina's terrain
table says a crowd suppresses the Family. The page does not address it. Offered as texture;
the tension is real but nothing in the system forbids working against a suppression.
**Residue:** the batch's strangest, because the residue is *absence* — a district where a
Memory-Quake has gone off holds a legible gap, and Anamnesis is the current that would read
it ("Objects genuinely store their history in physically readable form"). **Saturation:**
not a limit; the working is a single release.

**Wellspring stratum.** **Letheveil · *The Waters of Forgetting*** (primary), Family
**Limina**, Physics Domain **entropy, void and mind**. Its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Limina — Entropy, Void and Mind.md`:

> "Compression works because most of a signal is redundant, and the art is entirely in
> knowing which components carry information and which merely occupy bandwidth. Letheveil
> performs that analysis on memory. It does not erase indiscriminately; it identifies what
> is load-bearing and discards the rest, and the practitioner's certainty about which is
> which is the whole of the risk."

**Hypnather · *The Sleep Crown*** (secondary), same Family; its law, verbatim from the
same file:

> "A glass is a liquid that stopped moving before it finished arranging itself, and left
> undisturbed it continues to relax toward the ordered state it never reached, slowly and
> only while quiescent. Hypnather grants the Crystal that behaviour. The reorganisation
> happens during genuine rest and cannot be forced, which is why Hypnather practitioners
> sleep like the dead and wake structurally different."

**The page's use of Hypnather is exactly right and worth crediting.** It says Hypnather
"widens the window Letheveil can reach into, making a waking mind briefly as porous as a
resting one." In the physical reading, consolidation is itself a sleep-dependent process,
so borrowing the rest-state's porosity to extend the labile window is the correct
biological move, and it is why the page's counter is a construct with no resting mind —
"gives Hypnather nothing to widen."

**A Sub-Stat check, because it looks like an error and is not.** The page says Letheveil
"tests **Resilience Anchoring**." The register says it "**tests** Resilience **Coherence**."
Under the Merge Ledger, **Resilience Coherence → Anchoring** (Part Twelve). **The page is
reading the register correctly through the live name. Not a conflict.**

Register failures in play. Letheveil's "**Generation loss.** Each pass discards a little
more, and a memory compressed repeatedly degrades toward an artefact the practitioner still
experiences as a memory." Hypnather's "**Interrupted relaxation.** Disturb the process
partway and the configuration locks where it stood, worse ordered than before it began."

**Generation loss answers the page's own "What nobody knows" outright.** The page asks
"Which of his own memories Recall Slip has already taken, and whether he would recognise
the gap if it had." **He would not**, and the register says why: a degraded memory is still
experienced as a memory. There is no gap to notice. There is a worse copy.

*Four Theories.* **Consent Doctrine**, undisturbed — a compression criterion is a process.
The page's cost, though, edges toward **the Debt Position**: a working that bills the caster
one memory per use, non-negotiably and invisibly, is an account settled somewhere he cannot
see (`Two Sets of Books` §IV). **Noted, not resolved.**

**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Essence Core**, which "Holds identity, memory, and
Traits," whose failure state is "Fracture into Shards" (`The Core Vocabulary` §III). This is
one of only a few workings in the batch that operates on the Core directly rather than
through it, and Recall Slip is a Core cost paid in the Core's own substance. Developmental
Tier at Stage VII: **Resonant**. Crystal State: **Refined, residue-heavy** (card).
**The invoice:** one of his own minor memories per release, plus Temporal Fog. **Cumulative,
irreversible, and unmeasurable to the person paying it** — which makes it the most
expensive invoice in the batch by the only measure that matters, and the least visible on a
ledger. `The Core Vocabulary` §VI has the term for where this ends: **Shear Break**, "A
Trait collapsing, inverting, or turning on its bearer after the bearer has repeatedly denied
the truth that formed it." A man who has compressed his own record enough times has denied
a great deal.

## 3 · Mechanism

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[RenB]` **Boundary** · Root · Zhaeren — the
working's content is a *threshold in time*, the line between labile and consolidated, and
`[RenB]` is the form for exactly that — laid across `[Vel]` **Mind** · Extension · Iesara.
Both from the thirty unclassified forms in
`wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`.
*Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling.*

**What boundary moves.** One quantity: **where the consolidation threshold sits, inside a
25 m sphere, for a few seconds.** Not what is remembered. Not whose. Only *when* a trace
counts as set.

**What the law then does on its own.** All of it, and every Limitation on the page is the
threshold speaking. Old memory survives because it is on the far side. Unconscious targets
are untouched because they are not encoding. A working mid-formation collapses because a
spell-chain dependent on layered recall is *itself* a labile trace. A held physical anchor
survives because rehearsal re-encodes. A construct rides it out because it has no
consolidation process to have a threshold in. **Five stated limits, one boundary, nothing
added.**

**Law V check.** Stage VII **is** Refraction, where the internal chain becomes available
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). The page says the working is **Reactive**,
released within 25 m against an incoming layered working — and a reactive release is the
one case where the internal chain is not a luxury but a requirement, because there is no
time to declare. **The page's trigger and the Craft's Stage gate agree, and this is the one
Aeldoris page where the internalised option is doing real work.**

**Failure mode — Boundary fault.** "The condition was established imprecisely and the law
ran somewhere unintended" (`Two Sets of Books` §VII). The threshold is moved across a
sphere and Aeldoris is in the sphere. He pays a memory on every release and comes out
confused, and the page calls both by name. **The working is not indiscriminate by oversight;
it is indiscriminate because a threshold in time has no way to be told whose clock it is.**

A **Field fault** sits behind it in one case the page names: a Verdantia clarity field's
"steady regenerative resonance keeps memory anchored against the shake" — the room supplies
consolidation faster than the boundary can un-set it, so the working is correct and produces
nothing.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** η ≈ 0.65 (card): **35 percent** of every
expenditure leaves as heat, sound and structural bleed. Against an intended effect costing
under an attojoule, **the bleed is not a fraction of the working — it is the working's
entire energetic footprint.** Whatever a Memory-Quake costs in EU, essentially none of it
becomes the erasure; almost all of it becomes reach and discrimination, and 35 % of that
becomes waste. As on his other pages, 0.65 sits **above Tier 5 · Expert's 0.50–0.60
ceiling** (`SA-NUM-ETA-TIER`).

**Does the physics close against the stratal account?** **Yes, and this page is the
batch's proof of the standard.** The physical account comes out at under one attojoule —
literally nothing — and is nonetheless complete: a real threshold, a real window whose
duration matches the clinic, and a real counter that is documented adrenergic
consolidation. The stratal account is ruinous: a Core paying in its own record, forever,
without being able to audit the loss. **Both accounts are complete. Neither is a
translation of the other.** That is what `Two Sets of Books` asks for, and this is where
the batch demonstrates it most clearly.

**One system gap the page exposes and cannot fix:** memory is billed as a cost on three
pages in this batch (here, `Crimson Dirge` at 2 % per law edited, `Final Mercy` at ~4 % per
use) and **the system provides no unit for memory, no reserve figure, and no exhaustion
threshold.** EU has a Starvation line at 10 %. Memory has nothing. Logged
`SA-GAP-MEMORY-AS-COST`.

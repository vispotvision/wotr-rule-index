# System account · Liminal Palace of False Dawn

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** A doorway opens and a false world is laid over a hundred and
twenty metres of the real one. Everyone inside sees, hears and feels what she chose. The true
world is still there underneath, untouched.

**The real phenomenon.** **Predictive processing at the perceptual bottleneck, plus sensory
gating** — and the choice of *where* to intervene is the working's whole economy.

Perception is not a readout of the senses. It is a posterior: the brain combines an incoming
likelihood with a prior and reports the result, weighting each by its precision. When the prior
is precise enough it overrides the input, which is not speculation — it is the measured basis of
binocular rivalry (alternation every **2–4 s** with the input constant), the rubber-hand
illusion (onset after **10–20 s** of synchronous stroking), and the McGurk effect, where roughly
**98 %** of adults hear a syllable that was never spoken because the mouth said otherwise.

**Sensory gating** is the suppression half. The thalamocortical mechanisms that mute one input
in favour of a competing one are measurable as P50 suppression, and the second of two paired
clicks is attenuated by **50–80 %** in a healthy adult. The page names this correctly: Tenebra
"suppresses the true sensory signal underneath it, the way sensory gating mutes a real input in
favour of a competing one."

**The governing law, and the number that makes this page cheap.** Bayesian cue combination: the
percept is the precision-weighted posterior. And the bandwidth asymmetry is enormous. **The
optic nerve carries on the order of 10⁷ bits per second. Conscious visual throughput is
40–60 bits per second.** To fake the light arriving at a retina you must write at 10⁷ bits/s.
To write the percept you must write at **≈50 bits/s** — a saving of about **2×10⁵**.

**That is the physical reason for the page's own hardest Limit** — "it cannot touch physical
reality, only its perceived structure." The working is not *refusing* to alter the world out of
restraint. **It is operating at the bottleneck because the bottleneck is five orders of
magnitude cheaper, and the price of that economy is that nothing outside the percept moves.**

**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Overlay: up to **120 m** (page). As a hemisphere, (2/3)π(120 m)³ = **3.62×10⁶ m³**.
- Write rate per target: **≈50 bits/s** at the percept, against **≈10⁷ bits/s** at the input.
  **Economy: 2×10⁵.**
- For twenty people inside the overlay: **≈10³ bits/s** total.
- **Thermodynamic floor.** Landauer at body temperature is kT·ln2 = **2.97×10⁻²¹ J/bit**, so
  10³ bits/s is **≈3×10⁻¹⁸ W**. **Three attowatts to hold a false world over twenty people.**
  Alongside `Chorda Somnii` and `Letheveil Memory-Quake`, this is the batch's third
  demonstration that a boundary condition can be almost free in joules and ruinous in the only
  currency a soul has (`wiki/The Magic System/The Physical Account Two Sets of Books.md` §III).
- Gating suppression achievable: **50–80 %** (P50). Not total, which is the honest reason
  "Pure Basilithe-aligned beings notice the overlay's inconsistencies sooner than most" — the
  true signal is attenuated, not removed, and a high-Q observer integrates the residue.
- Stated SSS ceiling: **1–5 PN**, **418 TJ to 4.184 EJ**. 418 TJ ✓ and 100 kt ✓, but
  **1 teraton TNT = 4.184×10²¹ J = 4.184 zettajoules, not exajoules** (Part Four's SSS row:
  4.184×10¹⁴ – 4.184×10²¹ J). Wrong by 10³. `SA-NUM-SSS-EJ-NOT-ZJ`. **The page is also right
  that nothing in that range is discharged against a body, and says so — which makes the wrong
  unit doubly harmless here and worth fixing anyway for consistency across the three pages that
  carry it.**

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** Nowhere, essentially. Three attowatts is the
floor and the real cost is reach and discrimination across 3.62×10⁶ m³. The true environment
"continues underneath unperceived and untouched," so no work is done on it.

**Precedent we are not copying.** Illusion domains are the genre's most worked ground — the
Infinite Tsukuyomi, the Matrix, Jujutsu Kaisen's Idle Transfiguration. Those either take the
body or take the will. **This one takes neither: it cannot compel an action against a target's
core will, only misdirect their reading of the surroundings they are acting in.** The divergence
is that the limit is a bandwidth fact — she is writing the posterior, not the motor plan — and
so the counter is to *flood the likelihood*, not to resist.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** Draw across 3.62×10⁶ m³ for reach, with almost nothing spent at the point
of effect. Limina is favoured by "Darkness, silence, Aether-thin ground, ruins" and suppressed
by "Saturated Aether, **crowd density**, active Domains"
(`The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings`, Environmental Coupling). **Crowd density is in
tension with the working's design, as on `Letheveil Memory-Quake`: an overlay wants people in
it and Limina's terrain table says a crowd suppresses the Family.** Unaddressed on the page;
offered as texture. **Residue:** the overlay leaves no physical trace because it never touched
physical reality — **which makes it the hardest working in the batch to prove after the fact,
and the page's own cost is that Opalis cannot prove it to herself either.**
**Saturation:** not a field limit; the limit is the number of available logical slots, which the
page's own counterplay exploits.

**Wellspring stratum.** **Oneirion · *The Wellspring of Dream*** (primary), Family
**Limina**, Physics Domain **entropy, void and mind**. Its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Limina — Entropy, Void and Mind.md`:

> "A mechanical system's every possible arrangement is a coordinate in a space that has no
> physical location, and the system's evolution is a trajectory through it. Oneirion lets a
> working act on that manifold rather than on the room. This is the mechanical reason Oneirion
> techniques reach targets no line of sight connects, and the reason they are so difficult to
> describe afterward."

**"Act on that manifold rather than on the room" is the bandwidth argument in the register's own
words.** The manifold is the percept; the room is the input. The 2×10⁵ economy is Oneirion's
law with a number on it.

**Tenebra · *The Hidden Shadow*** (carried at primary weight) supplies the gating — law quoted
in full in the `Hunter's Breath` account. **Basilithe · *The Stone Will*** (secondary) supplies
the law-lock that makes the false state resist correction — law quoted in full in the
`Auric Parallax Veil` account. Both assignments are correct for the jobs the page gives them.

**Fractura · *The Breaking Point*** is carried at primary weight and is again used for something
its register does not describe. The page has "a Fractura-heavy caster can deliberately generate
additional false interpretations inside the same space," treating Fractura as a source of
branch multiplicity. Fractura's register content is the Griffith criterion and flaw
populations. **As on `Auric Parallax Veil`, there is a good reading available that the page does
not take:** a flaw population *is* a set of alternative failure sites, so Fractura could be the
multiplicity current on its own terms — but the register never says so, and inventing that
reading is not a researcher's call. Logged under `SA-OFF-FRACTURA-REFRACTION`, which now covers
three Opalis pages.

Register failures in play. Oneirion's "**Projection error.** The trajectory was correct in the
manifold and lands nowhere in particular when it is mapped back down into a room." Tenebra's
"**Aspect dependence.** Faceting works against expected angles, and a Tenebra practitioner
observed from the one direction they did not plan for returns a very bright signal indeed" —
**which is the exact mechanism for why the overlay has inconsistencies that a patient observer
finds.** Basilithe's "**Resonant catastrophe**," here appearing as the Clarity Break.

*Four Theories.* **Consent Doctrine** as filed. The page's "What nobody knows" — whether Opalis
standing inside her own Palace always knows which layer is true — is a **Correspondence**
symptom again: if her prior and the overlay's prior are the same law at two scales, there is no
vantage from which to compare them, and the déjà vu she reports is what that feels like from
inside (`Two Sets of Books` §IV). **Noted, not resolved.**

**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Attraction Layer** (Domain Weaving, an outward claim)
with the **Essence Core** paying — Cognitive Fracture "bleeds fragments of Opalis's own memory
into the illusion, costing her certainty about which variant of reality she herself occupied,"
and memory is Core business, whose failure state is "Fracture into Shards"
(`The Core Vocabulary` §III). Developmental Tier at Stage XII: **Sovereign**
(Part Twenty-One). Crystal State: `null`, no card. **The invoice:** Cognitive Fracture and
Harmonic Echo Burn. **The page bills memory as a cost, and the system has no unit for memory —
no reserve, no exhaustion threshold, nothing equivalent to EU's 10 % Starvation line.** Third
occurrence in this batch after `Crimson Dirge` and `Final Mercy`; logged
`SA-GAP-MEMORY-AS-COST`.

**The lens: the cave.** Plato, *Republic* VII 514a–520a. Prisoners chained facing
a wall take the shadows cast on it for the whole world, and the passage everyone
forgets is the one about the **return**: a prisoner turned toward the fire is
dazzled and in pain, and would rather go back. The page has that second half
verbatim in its own mechanics — when the illusion is deliberately broken under
*"Cataclysmic Clarity Break, the sudden correction produces vertigo, harmonic
disarray, or a brief conceptual blindness."* Being shown the truth hurts, and the
page prices it. The allegory fits the working's architecture too: Oneirion writes
the false state *"directly onto the target's sensory processing rather than onto
the environment itself"*, which is the wall rather than the world, and Tenebra's
absorption suppresses the true signal underneath *"the way sensory gating mutes a
real input in favour of a competing one"* — the chains. Where the lens misleads is
in the promise Plato attaches to it. In the cave the shadows are cast by real
objects, so the shadows are **evidence**, and a sufficiently rigorous reasoner
inside the cave can in principle infer the truth. Basilithe's law-lock exists
precisely to defeat that: it *"stabilizes that false state so it resists
correction."* The page's documented counters are *"removing or overwhelming a
target's senses"* and cluttering the Palace's *"available logical slots"* until it
destabilises — a blindfold and a flood, neither of which is reasoning. A school of
dialecticians will try to argue its way out of the False Dawn, and will be
arguing inside it when the sun does not come up.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Rv]` **Reverie** · Extension · Maelor — the
overlay is a reverie given a perimeter — laid across `[Et]` **Mirage** · Root · Kaetra. Both
from the thirty unclassified forms in
`wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`. **A filing note worth
raising: `[Et]` Mirage is also the strongest candidate for `Dreambreaker Field · The Shattered
Midnight`, and the two workings are genuinely different** — a Palace writes the percept and
leaves the room alone, a Dreambreaker changes the room's effective geometry and leaves the
percept to follow. If both file under `[Et]`, the Index loses that distinction. Raised for
Isaac rather than decided. *Filing decision, not canon.*

**What boundary moves.** One quantity: **the relative precision of prior against likelihood,
inside 120 m.** Not the input. Not the will. Only the weighting.

**What the law then does on its own.** Everything, and every Limit on the page is a property of
the posterior. It cannot compel an action against core will because a prior sets what you
believe you are in, not what you decide to do about it. It cannot alter physical reality because
it never wrote at the input. Two contradictory overlays cannot both run because a posterior has
one normalisation — and forcing a second incompatible one is what produces the Clarity Break
rather than a clean second illusion, which the page states and the mechanism explains.
Emotionless constructs resist because a construct with no generative prior has nothing to
reweight. **Four stated limits, one boundary.**

**Law V check.** Stage XII sits far above Refraction, so no material anchor is required
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). The page gives a traced vertical line — Gestured
by choice.

**Failure mode — Boundary fault.** "The condition was established imprecisely and the law ran
somewhere unintended" (`Two Sets of Books` §VII). She is inside the 120 m and her own prior is
inside the reweighting; Cognitive Fracture is the boundary running through the person who set
it. And because gating only attenuates by 50–80 %, the overlay carries residual true signal
that a patient observer integrates — the page's own Weakness.

**A physics-open item on the page's first counter.** The page says: "Removing or overwhelming a
target's senses cuts through the overlay entirely." **Those two halves behave oppositely under
the governing law.** *Overwhelming* raises the likelihood's precision, the posterior follows the
input, and the overlay breaks — correct. *Removing* the senses drives the likelihood's precision
toward zero, and a posterior with no likelihood **is the prior** — so sensory deprivation should
make the overlay *total*, not penetrable. Logged
`SA-PHYS-PALACE-SENSORY-DEPRIVATION`; recorded, not resolved, because it reverses half of a
stated counter and the reversal is not a researcher's to make.

**What the target sees and feels.** A vertical line traced in the air, light
splitting into mirrored ribbons, and a doorway of shadow-gold — and then a world
that is not the one they are standing in, out to a hundred and twenty metres.
Terrain, danger, motion, causality: all of it as Opalis has set it, while the true
environment *"continues underneath unperceived and untouched."* Nothing is
actually moved. Under False Motion or False Causality the failure is worse than
misperceiving a room: *"they believe they moved, attacked or succeeded when they
did not, or the reverse"* — so a fighter's report of his own actions stops being
reliable evidence, including to himself. What the Palace cannot do is the line
that keeps it fair, and the page states it twice: *"it cannot force a target to act
against what they already will, only misread the surroundings they are acting
in"*, and it cannot touch physical reality at all. A man who wanted to hold a
doorway still wants to hold a doorway; he is simply wrong about which doorway he
is standing in. And the correction is not free — breaking the illusion produces
vertigo, harmonic disarray or brief conceptual blindness, so the moment of rescue
is a moment of vulnerability. Opalis pays in the same currency she is spending:
*"fragments of her own memory bleed into the illusion, leaving her briefly unsure
which version of events she personally occupied."*

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** No card for Opalis; the ladder gives Stage XII ⇒
**Tier 7 · Grandmaster**, η **0.85–0.90** (Part Nineteen) ⇒ **10–15 percent** as heat, sound
and structural bleed. Against an intended effect with a three-attowatt floor, **the bleed is
essentially the entire energetic footprint of the working**, and all of it goes into reach and
discrimination rather than into the illusion. Harmonic Echo Burn — migraines, afterimages,
temporary shadow-blindness — is that bleed landing on the organ she reads with.

## 5 · Counterplay and the challenge

**The fairness check** (`.claude/skills/wotr-write/references/fair-play.md`).

| Test | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Costs something that hurts | **pass** | **Cognitive Fracture** — her own memory bleeding into the illusion until she is *"unsure which version of events she personally occupied"* — is the rare cost that damages the practitioner's grip on the facts she is fighting with, and it is cumulative in a way that makes the second Palace more dangerous to her than the first. **Harmonic Echo Burn** adds migraines, afterimages and temporary shadow-blindness from running Orrenthal and Fractura together. No EU figure, no card (`SA-GAP-EU-FORMULA`, `SA-GAP-OPALIS-NO-CARD`). |
| Stated limits | **pass, and the first one is the fairness clause** | **It cannot compel an action against a target's core will** — only misdirect their reading of the surroundings; **it cannot alter physical reality**, only what is perceived of it; it cannot run two full-strength contradictory illusions over the same ground; 120 m. |
| Something beats it | **pass, and one counter is a blindfold** | Remove or overwhelm the senses; or clutter the logical slots. Neither needs a peer. |
| It has a tell | **pass, and it is theatrical** | A traced vertical line, light splitting into mirrored ribbons, and a visible doorway of shadow-gold that has to be stepped or gestured through. |
| Numbers in band | **flagged, already logged** | Stage XII ⇒ Max Grade SSS (726–950), ceiling 950, Tier 7, η 0.85–0.90 (Parts Five, Nineteen). The page quotes the SSS force band and then says the honest thing about it: *"nothing in that range is discharged against a body, since the working never touches physical reality"* — so it is a scale ceiling, not an output. Against that: the EJ/ZJ discrepancy (`SA-NUM-SSS-EJ-NOT-ZJ`), a Path gate that does not fit its own Sub-Stats (`SA-NUM-PALACE-PATHGATE`), and Fractura being used as refraction (`SA-OFF-FRACTURA-REFRACTION`). |

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Deny the field — take the senses the working writes on.** *"Removing or
  overwhelming a target's senses cuts through the overlay entirely."* This is the
  cheapest counter to any Stage XII working in the batch: a blindfold, wax, a
  deliberate flood of real noise. The mechanism writes onto **sensory
  processing**, so a sense that is not processing anything is a sense that cannot
  be written to. Whether sensory deprivation should really be a clean escape is the
  open physics question this account logs (`SA-PHYS-PALACE-SENSORY-DEPRIVATION`),
  and a player is entitled to argue it.
- **Break the boundary — clutter the slots, which is a Knot.** *"A Fractura-heavy
  caster can deliberately generate additional false interpretations inside the same
  space, cluttering the Palace's available logical slots until it destabilizes."*
  The Palace holds one coherent false account; give it three more and it cannot
  hold any. The register: *"Knot entangles a path so it cannot be traced
  cleanly."*
- **Deny the field — bring something with no interior.** *"Emotionless constructs
  resist conceptual misdirection outright"*, and pure Basilithe-aligned beings
  *"notice the overlay's inconsistencies sooner than most."* Send the automaton
  through the door and have it describe the room. Family **Limina**, for which the
  register gives no terrain line (`SA-GAP-COUNTERPLAY-TERRAIN`).
- **Break the man — she is in there too.** Cognitive Fracture costs her certainty
  about which version of events she occupied. An opponent who prolongs the Palace
  rather than escaping it is spending her memory for her, and the register's
  *"Coherence drift"* is the endgame of that: *"You do not have to cause this. You
  have to notice it and wait."*
- **Break the body — nothing in the Palace stops a blade.** *"It cannot alter
  physical reality itself."* A volley fired blind into a known position is fired
  into the real room, because the shot is not a perceiver. This is the single most
  useful consequence of the working's own stated limit and it is worth a reader's
  time to notice it.

**The tell, stated plainly.** She draws a vertical line, the light splits into
mirrored ribbons, and a doorway opens. Anyone who has not stepped or been gestured
through it has been told, in advance and in gold, exactly what is about to happen
to everyone who does.

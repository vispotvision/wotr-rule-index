# System account · Lumen Dissecans

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The effect in plain words.** A beam is put on a structure that is still
running, and the structure's internal composition becomes legible from outside
without the structure being taken apart.

**The real phenomenon.** Two real instruments, and Judicium's register names both.

*Absorption and emission spectroscopy.* Every species absorbs and emits at
wavelengths fixed by its own electronic structure, and no substance can present
another's spectrum. A dark line in a continuum is a compositional statement. This
is why the Sun's composition was known before anyone left the ground.

*Nondestructive evaluation, specifically eddy-current inspection.* An alternating
field induced in a conductor sets up eddy currents; a subsurface flaw perturbs
their path and shows as an impedance change at the probe. **The flaw is found
without removing the coating** — which is the whole of what this working does to a
live ward.

The register's analogue line is exactly this pair: "Emission and absorption
spectroscopy. Nondestructive flaw detection"
(`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Fulguria — Electromagnetism.md`).

**The governing law.** **Kirchhoff's laws of spectroscopy** — a body absorbs at
the wavelengths at which it emits — with the **Beer–Lambert law**, A = ε·c·l,
converting absorbance into a concentration. Resolution is set by the **Rayleigh
criterion**, θ = 1.22 λ/D, which is where Aurevane's contribution enters: the
secondary current is what makes the reading carry as visible analytic light rather
than an internal sense.

**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Range: **500 m** (page).
- Aperture, taken as a sigil drawn over one eye, D ≈ **0.05 m**; λ ≈ 550 nm.
  θ = 1.22 × 5.5×10⁻⁷ ÷ 0.05 = **1.34×10⁻⁵ rad**.
- **Resolved detail at 500 m:** 1.34×10⁻⁵ × 500 = **6.7 mm**. So at the page's
  full stated range the beam resolves structure down to about **7 mm** — fine
  enough to read a glyph-chain's members, not fine enough to read its grain.
- To resolve **1 mm** at 500 m the aperture would need to be
  D = 1.22 × 5.5×10⁻⁷ ÷ 2×10⁻⁶ = **0.34 m**. A checkable consequence: close work
  wants a larger sigil or a shorter range, and the page's "one eye" framing caps
  the resolution at millimetres.
- Spectral resolving power needed to split two adjacent structural lines of the
  sodium-doublet kind (588.995 / 589.592 nm): R = λ/Δλ ≈ **1,000**.
- Cost basis: "a measurable share of her established **22 AU/s** baseline" — the
  only page in this batch that bills in output rate rather than in EU.

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** Almost nowhere. A
spectroscopic read is passive in principle and near-passive in practice: the beam
carries only enough power to be detected against the target working's own
emission. The expensive resource is not energy but **integration time and
aperture**, which is why the page's limit is "only a working still in progress can
be read" — the instrument needs a live source to read *from*.

**Precedent we are not copying.** Analysis eyes are everywhere: Hunter × Hunter's
Gyo, Naruto's Byakugan, the appraisal skills of every LitRPG. Those return a
verdict. This returns **structure held open and legible to bystanders too**, and
the working stays vulnerable for as long as the beam is sustained rather than for
an instant. The divergence is that the reading is a **public document**, not a
private insight — which is why the page's consequence is "file it into Thesauriel
as new reference data" and why a reflective barrier can make her dissect herself.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** Draw is a thin directed beam, not a field; ambient
requirement is small. Fulguria wants "Dry air, conductive substrate, clear line of
sight" and is suppressed by "Faraday-caged interiors, heavy ferrous shielding,
dense fog" (`The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings`, Environmental Coupling).
Line of sight is load-bearing here and the page requires it. **Residue:** the read
leaves a phase trace on the *target working*, not on the ground — and if the
target survives the dissection it carries the mark of having been read, which is
the Attraction-side reason the technique makes enemies. **Saturation:** not a
limiting factor; the constraint is the aperture, which is a physical-account line.

**Wellspring stratum.** **Judicium · *The Wellspring of Truth***, Family
**Fulguria**, Physics Domain **electromagnetism**. Its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Fulguria — Electromagnetism.md`:

> "Every element absorbs and emits at wavelengths fixed by its own electron
> structure, and no substance can present another's spectrum. Judicium tunes Gnosis
> to that principle: the practitioner reads the characteristic lines of a Crystal,
> a technique, or a lie, and identifies composition from signature alone.
> Eddy-current inspection finds the crack under the paint without removing the
> paint."

Secondary: **Aurevane · *The Wellspring of Light***, same Family, whose law is
quoted in full in the `Corona Lunaris` account and supplies the coherence that
makes the reading visible.

Register Stat Effect, through the Merge Ledger: Gnosis **Acuity**, Gnosis
**Analysis** (from Diagnosis). Register failure: "**Spectral overload.** Every
object in view reports its composition simultaneously and the practitioner loses
the ability to prioritise any of it."

**The page's cost *is* that failure.** "Afterimages: lattice-shapes overlaid on
ordinary objects for a time afterward" is Spectral overload with a duration on it.
The two books agree without either having to be told.

*Four Theories.* **Consent Doctrine.** Nothing is asked of Judicium; a boundary is
set on what counts as a target and a law reports.

**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Essence Core** for the reading (Gnosis is
Core work) expressed through the **Aether Shell** as a beam. Developmental Tier at
Serenyra's Stage VIII: **Radiant**; at the Stage VI floor, **Harmonic**. Crystal
State: **Refined** (card). **The invoice:** billed in **output rate**, not reserve
— a standing share of 22 AU/s for as long as the beam holds. That is the correct
shape of invoice for a sustained working and it is rarer on these pages than it
should be. The afterimages are a Shell cost: a conduit that has been run as an
instrument keeps reporting after the instrument is put down, which is the Index's
"the law kept running after the intent stopped" in a diagnostic register.

## 3 · Mechanism

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Ora]` **Truth** · Extension ·
Iesara — a limit on what may remain unstated — laid across `[Sa]`
**Illumination** · Extension · Iesara, which carries it as light. Both from the
thirty unclassified forms in
`wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`. Both sit
at the same station, which is unusual and appropriate: the Index notes that where
a secondary Archon is filled "the path is the intersection rather than either
reading alone," and here there is no intersection to take — one station, read
twice, at different depths. *Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling.*

**What boundary moves.** One quantity: **the optical depth of a working in
progress.** A live ward is opaque to inspection by default. The glyph pins its
absorbance low enough that its internal lines show.

**What the law then does on its own.** The lines report themselves. That is what
lines do. The working freezes into legible suspended geometry not because she
froze it but because a structure being read at its own characteristic frequencies
is being driven at them, and a driven structure holds phase while the drive
persists. The vulnerability window is not a mercy the technique grants; it is the
period during which the target working's own modes are pinned by the read.

**Law V check.** Stage VI sits below Refraction, so an external anchor is required
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). The page supplies two — hand
("a sigil drawn over one eye") and declaration. **The gate closes cleanly.**

**Failure mode — Boundary fault.** "The condition was established imprecisely and
the law ran somewhere unintended" (`Two Sets of Books` §VII). The pinned quantity
is *which* structures are transparent, and the boundary is imprecise at its edge:
after release, ordinary unworked objects keep reporting. That is the afterimage,
and it is Judicium's spectral overload arriving on schedule. Two further faults
sit on the page: a reflective barrier redirecting the beam so she dissects herself
is the same Boundary fault at maximum severity, and a Void-aligned construct
contaminating her reading is a **Field fault** — the target supplies no spectrum,
so the instrument returns noise it is nonetheless convinced is signal, which is
Abyntheus's baseline decoherence appearing in a Judicium working.

The page's "What nobody knows" — why some strong divine Edicts yield only
*partial* structure — has an instrument-level candidate worth putting to Isaac
rather than asserting: a partial return is what a spectroscope gives when part of
the source lies **outside the instrument's band**. An Edict written above
Wellspring authority would have members with no lines in the range Judicium tunes
to, and would read as partial rather than as resistant. Offered as a candidate,
not as canon.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** η ≈ 0.70 (card): **30 percent of every
expenditure leaves as heat, sound and structural bleed**. Against a working whose
intended output is a few watts of analytic light, the bleed is the dominant term
by far — most of what she spends here never becomes beam. At Stage VIII she is at
the floor of Tier 6 · Master (0.70–0.80).

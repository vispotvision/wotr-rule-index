# System account · Maw of Crystalline Stasis

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The effect in plain words.** Six metres of air around him turns into a branching
ice structure. Anything moving through it slows, and anything moving through it fast
snaps.

**The real phenomenon.** **Dendritic solidification under high undercooling**, and
the page has it right: the mist does not settle as frost, it grows as a branching
lattice, and that difference is a real and well-characterised one.

A planar solidification front is unstable when the field ahead of it is
destabilising. Under the **Mullins–Sekerka instability** a perturbation on a flat
front grows rather than decays once the thermal gradient exceeds a critical value, and
the front breaks up into dendrites whose tip radius and growth velocity are set by the
undercooling. Deposition straight from vapour to solid — the route here, since the
page is working on "moisture, breath, and loose Essence" — produces the snowflake
habits mapped on the Nakaya diagram, where the morphology is a function of temperature
and supersaturation and of nothing else.

**The governing law, and the page's best line.** Ice is **strain-rate sensitive**: it
deforms ductilely below a strain rate of roughly **10⁻³ s⁻¹** and fails brittly above
it. The transition is sharp and it is the single most-measured property of the
material.

**"Slows what it touches and shatters what pushes through it too fast" is that
transition, stated as a technique.** Not a design choice. A material constant.

**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Field: **6 m** (page). Spherical volume (4/3)π(6 m)³ = **905 m³**.
- Water available: saturation vapour density at 0 °C is **4.84 g/m³**, so
  905 × 4.84 = **≈4.4 kg** of depositable water in a saturated field.
- Latent load, vapour → ice at **2,838 J/g**: 4.4×10³ × 2,838 = **1.24×10⁷ J**.
- Sensible load, 905 m³ of air dropped 30 K:
  905 × 1.225 × 1,005 × 30 = **3.34×10⁷ J**.
- **Total heat to be removed: ≈4.6×10⁷ J (46 MJ).**
- Ice mechanical properties the field depends on: compressive strength
  **5–25 MPa**, tensile strength **1–2 MPa**, fracture toughness
  **K_IC ≈ 0.1 MPa·m^0.5** — one of the lowest of any structural solid, which is why
  the lattice shatters rather than yielding.
- **Ductile–brittle threshold: ≈10⁻³ s⁻¹.** A body crossing a 6 m field slowly
  deforms the lattice; a limb moving at 5 m/s across a 0.1 m contact scale is at
  50 s⁻¹, **five orders of magnitude into the brittle regime.**

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** In the air and in the bodies
standing in it. 46 MJ is a heat-pump load, not a discharge: the page calls it "the
same Vohrin heat-sink … run continuously and outward instead of once and inward,"
which is the correct description of a sink rather than a source. As on
`Hunter's Breath`, the minimum work is Q·ΔT/T_c, so the cost rises steeply with the
undercooling the lattice needs — and a dendritic habit needs *high* undercooling,
which is the physical reason this field is more expensive than the mist.

**Precedent we are not copying.** Ice fields are a genre fixture — Bleach's Sōde no
Shirayuki, Fairy Tail, the ice mage of every CRPG. Those freeze **the enemy**. This
one freezes **the volume, including him**, and the page makes that the technique's
defining fact rather than a drawback bolted on. The divergence is that the
indiscriminate radius is derived from the mechanism (a field has no target) and the
cost lands on his joints because that is where the lattice reaches his own body first.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** Draw across 905 m³, projected from his own frame. Caloria is
favoured by "Steep thermal gradients, combustible atmosphere, geothermal ground" and
suppressed by "Thermal equilibrium, **saturated cold**, oxygen-poor air"
(`The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings`, Environmental Coupling) — so the field
degrades its own preconditions as it runs, which is an honest and unusual property and
the page does not claim it. **Residue:** heavy and physical; a deposited lattice is
residue you can pick up. Cinerion's contribution ("densifies the result same-Family")
is the register's own metabolise-your-own-products clause working on ice instead of
ash. **Saturation:** the field's ceiling is the available vapour — 4.4 kg and no more,
after which the lattice stops thickening whatever he spends. **That is a hard physical
cap the page does not state and it is the most useful thing in this account: the Maw
cannot be made denser in dry air, only wider.**

**Wellspring stratum.** **Cinerion · *The Ash Veil*** (secondary, and the only
attested current on the page), Family **Caloria**, Physics Domain
**thermodynamics**. Its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Caloria — Thermodynamics.md`:

> "What survives a fire is not waste. Char is a high-surface-area carbon scaffold that
> catalyses further reaction, and settled ash returns potassium and phosphorus to the
> ground that fed the fire. Cinerion retunes the Shell to metabolize its own
> combustion products, harvesting from expenditure what other practitioners simply
> vent. The suspended residue also scatters incident light and Essence-sight alike."

Register Stat Effect, through the Merge Ledger: Tempering **Yield** (from
Conversion), Harmonics **Stability** (from Recovery). Register failure:
"**Fouling.** Accumulated residue eventually coats the conduit rather than feeding it,
and recovery rate collapses precisely when the practitioner has spent the most."

**Fouling is the joint frostbite.** A conduit coated by its own product, failing at
the periphery, at the moment of maximum expenditure. The page attributes the joints to
a Dominion shortfall; the register attributes the same symptom to Cinerion's stated
pathology. **Both are true and they reinforce rather than compete.**

**Primary: Vohrin — not one of the Sixty.** As on `Bloodbind Surge` and
`Hunter's Breath`. Recorded, not resolved — `SA-UNATT-VOHRIN`. This page is the one
that makes the stakes concrete: it also invokes "the Zar'thuun and Vohrin
**Titan-Keyed** workings," and Titan-Keyed arts require Zenith or Revelation
(`wiki/Cosmology & Metaphysics/The Fourteen Titans.md`: "Twelve of the fourteen arts
require Zenith and two require Revelation, which places all fourteen beyond every
practitioner alive"). **A Stage VI practitioner working Vohrin sits eight Stages
below that gate.** If Vohrin is a Titan, this page's Stage floor is off by eight
Stages. If Vohrin is a Wellspring, the Caloria register is short an entry. Both
readings are in the file.

*Four Theories.* **Consent Doctrine**, undisturbed. Thermodynamics is offered no
intention.

**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Aether Shell**, projected outward — the page
calls the Craft "Spellcraft (Bodily: projected from Draven's own ignited frame,
holding only while his attention holds)," which is the Four Crafts page's own
definition of the Bodily vector ("A fist with combustion on it is Spellcraft with the
body as the pen … the sentence is still ephemeral — which is why the fist stops
burning the moment attention lapses"). **This is the only one of Draven's four pages
that files its Craft correctly**, and the contrast with the other three is a conflict
in itself. Developmental Tier at Stage VI: **Harmonic**. Crystal State: **Refined**
(card). **The invoice:** billed in EU per second, the only per-second figure on
Draven's four pages, plus joint frostbite — a Resilience Fortification cost (the entry
that absorbed Insulation), and the page cites **Resilience Insulation 302** for it,
which reads correctly under the Merge Ledger.

## 3 · Mechanism

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[RenB]` **Boundary** · Root · Zhaeren for
the 6 m edge, laid across `[Frm]` **Form** · Extension · Thalen for the lattice habit
— because the working's content is not *cold* but *which solid the vapour is allowed
to become*. `[Al]` **Edge** · Root · Thalen is the alternative worth putting beside
it: a phase boundary is an edge, and filing the Maw under `[Al]` would read the
working as a limit on the phase diagram rather than on the room. All from the thirty
unclassified forms in
`wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`.
*Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling.*

**What boundary moves.** One quantity: **the temperature and supersaturation at which
the vapour in 905 m³ is held.** Pin those two and the Nakaya diagram picks the
morphology; the branching habit is not commanded, it is *selected by the coordinates
the boundary fixes*.

**What the law then does on its own.** All of it, and every limit on the page falls
out. The lattice branches because the front is unstable at that undercooling. It slows
slow things because ice creeps. It shatters fast things because ice is brittle above
10⁻³ s⁻¹. It stops thickening at 4.4 kg because that is all the water there was. It
does not discriminate because a field is a region, not a target — and Draven is
standing in the region. A Pyreveil arte collapses it from within because "forcing a
reverse phase transition faster than Vohrin's sink can re-establish it" is a
competition between two rates and the pump loses whenever the input exceeds its
throughput. **Nobody wrote the indiscriminacy in. The Index's rule holds: the glyph
ends, the law does not, and the law was never told which bodies were friendly.**

**Law V check.** Stage VI sits below Refraction, so an external anchor is required
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). The page supplies the **Bodily** vector —
the frame itself as the pen. That is a hand-and-body anchor and **the gate closes
cleanly**, with the Craft page's own stated consequence attached: the sentence is
ephemeral and ends when attention lapses, which the page states.

**Failure mode — Boundary fault, with a Structural fault as its bill.** Boundary:
"The condition was established imprecisely and the law ran somewhere unintended.
Burns, structural collapse in the wrong wall, **a working that continued after the
practitioner stopped**" (`Two Sets of Books` §VII) — and the unintended place is
Draven's own knuckles and knees. Structural: "The Crystal could not hold what the
boundary permitted," which is a Dominion of 168 (C-Grade) being asked to hold a 6 m
field, brute-forced through Ardency instead, with the strain landing at the periphery
of what his ignition can evenly cover. **The page's own diagnosis, and it is correct:
the field is a Dominion operation being paid for out of the wrong account.**

The page's "What nobody knows" — whether an overheated field fails at its edge or its
centre — has a mechanism-level candidate worth offering rather than asserting. A
dendritic lattice has its **highest surface-area-to-volume ratio at the tips**, and
tips are where a reverse phase transition nucleates fastest. On that reading it fails
at the **edge** first, and the centre survives longest because it is the coarsest and
best-shielded ice in the field. Offered as a candidate, not as canon.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** η ≈ 0.55 (card): **45 percent of every
expenditure leaves as heat, sound and structural bleed** — and in a *cooling* working
that is a genuine internal contradiction worth noting as texture rather than as a
conflict: a 45 % thermal bleed inside the volume you are trying to keep undercooled is
load added to the sink. It raises the pump's duty rather than breaking the working, and
it is a second, independent reason the cost is per-second rather than per-cast. Tier 5
· Expert band is 0.50–0.60; 0.55 is mid-band and consistent.

**Does the physics close against the stratal account?** Yes on the mechanism and the
morphology, with two cautions. The physical account supplies a hard cap the page does
not have — 4.4 kg of water, so the Maw can be widened but not thickened in dry air —
and it supplies the ductile–brittle threshold that makes the page's central claim
exact rather than evocative. The cautions are bookkeeping: the working is gated at
A-Grade while its own governing Sub-Stat reads B, and its primary Wellspring is not on
the register. Neither is resolved here.

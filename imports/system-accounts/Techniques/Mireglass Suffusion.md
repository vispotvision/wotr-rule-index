# System account · Mireglass Suffusion

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** A thin dark film spreads across the floor of a room out to
twelve or fifteen metres. The film is retroreflective. Everything that looks into it gets its
own information back along the line it came in on, and the film has read something out of the
bodies standing on it before it returns anything.

**The real phenomenon, in three parts.** The page's Mechanism names two laws and the Trigger
implies a third, and all three are real, well-characterised and separately measurable.

**(a) The flood is a viscous axisymmetric gravity current.** A dense fluid released onto a
horizontal surface does not run out ballistically; it spreads under its own weight against
viscous resistance, and the front position goes as the eighth root of time — Huppert's similarity
solution, r(t) ∝ t^(1/8) for constant-volume axisymmetric viscous spreading. **This is why the
field has a radius rather than a range.** A working that supplies a fixed volume of a fixed
fluid has a settling radius written into the volume, and twelve to fifteen metres is a spread,
not a reach. The page's two-figure radius is the physically correct shape for the phenomenon:
a gravity current arrives at its final extent asymptotically and the last three metres take
longer than the first nine.

**(b) The surface is a retroreflector.** Oblivara's stated analogue. Three mutually
perpendicular mirrors return an incident ray antiparallel to its own incidence vector
regardless of the corner's orientation, within an acceptance cone of roughly ±20–30°. This is
the geometry of the lunar laser-ranging arrays and of every road sign. Engineering-grade
prismatic sheeting runs a retroreflection coefficient R_A of roughly **300–1,000
cd·lx⁻¹·m⁻²**, two to three orders above a diffuse white surface, and the return divergence is
a few tenths of a degree. **A retroreflective floor therefore looks black from every direction
except exactly your own**, which is the physical reason mireglass reads as a dark fluid to
bystanders and as a mirror to the person standing in it.

**(c) The read is trapped-charge dosimetry.** Anamnesis's stated analogue, and the register is
precise about it. Thermoluminescent dosimetry: LiF:Mg,Ti holds charge in lattice traps at a
depth of roughly **1.1–2.0 eV**, releases it as light when heated through its main dosimetric
peak at about **210 °C**, and the integrated glow is linear in absorbed dose over roughly five
decades, **10 µGy to 10 Gy**. Remanent magnetisation: magnetite cooling through its Curie point
at **858 K (585 °C)** locks the ambient field direction, and the Néel relaxation time
τ = τ₀·exp(KV/kT) with τ₀ ≈ 10⁻⁹ s means a grain above about 25–30 nm holds that record for
geological time. Both are **destructive-on-read in one direction and not the other**: heating
a dosimeter to read it empties the traps.

**The governing law, and the page's best line.** Oblivara's other half is error-correcting
redundancy, and error correction has a hard arithmetic limit. A code of minimum Hamming distance
d corrects **t = ⌊(d−1)/2⌋** symbol errors and no more; past t the decoder does not fail
loudly, it converges on a **different valid codeword** and reports it with full confidence. The
register says exactly this — *"past it the reconstruction is confident, complete, and wrong."*

**That is the page's "What nobody knows", solved as far as the physics can solve it.** Two
witnesses to the same old failure are shown two different versions because a degraded record
past the correction limit decodes to whichever codeword is nearest, and *nearest* depends on
what the decoder had to work with. Whether Oblivara retrieves or reconstructs is not a choice
the working makes. It is a function of how far the record has degraded, and the working cannot
tell the two cases apart from inside.

**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Radius **12–15 m** (page). Flooded area π r² = **452 – 707 m²**.
- Film at 1 mm: **0.45 – 0.71 m³**, i.e. of order **450 – 710 kg** at water density.
- Duration **≈24 s**, from the page's own conversion of four exchanges.
- Ambient optical budget available to be retroreflected: at full daylight **10⁴ lx** over 452 m²
  and ~100 lm/W for daylight, **≈4.5×10⁴ W**, so **≈1.1×10⁶ J** over the 24 s. Indoors at
  100 lx this falls to **≈10⁴ J**.
- Air thermal store, a 3 m column over 452 m² (1,356 m³) dropped 30 K:
  1,356 × 1.225 × 1,005 × 30 = **5.0×10⁷ J**.
- Chemical store in one adult target: ~10 kg adipose × 37 MJ/kg ≈ **3.7×10⁸ J**.
- **B-Grade attack output floor: 1.046×10⁹ J** (0.25 t TNT), from Part Four.
- Ordinary human strike for comparison: **≈100–200 J**, peak contact force of order **5 kN**.

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** In the incident light, in the air, and in the
targets' own bodies — and the arithmetic matters, because the page asserts that *"the blow that
lands is real"* without saying how hard. Read as an ordinary blow of 100–200 J, the room supplies
it out of pocket change and the account closes with four orders to spare. Read as an attack at
the working's own stated Grade, the floor is **1.046×10⁹ J**, and the whole in-field reservoir
against a single target is 1.1×10⁶ + 5.0×10⁷ + 3.7×10⁸ ≈ **4.2×10⁸ J — short by a factor of
2.5.** It only closes against three or more standing targets, and it closes by spending *them*.
**The page does not choose, so the account cannot close it either.** Logged, not resolved.

**Precedent we are not copying.** The memory-monster field is well worn: Persona's Shadows,
the Dark Aether of Metroid Prime 2, the Mirror of Erised turned hostile, every JRPG boss that
is the hero's guilt with a sword. Those give the apparition an author — a demon, a god, a
psychologist. This one has **no author at all**. Two physical laws are left running across a
floor and the shape that stands up is whatever the target's own trapped-charge record decodes
to, including when the decode is wrong. The divergence is that nothing in the working
*intends* the silhouette, and the indiscriminacy — the caster wading in the same flood — falls
out of the fact that a film on a floor is a region and not a target.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** Draw across a 452–707 m² footprint, projected outward from the caster's own
frame. Limina is favoured by *"Darkness, silence, Aether-thin ground, ruins"* and suppressed by
*"Saturated Aether, **crowd density**, active Domains"*
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings.md`, Environmental Coupling).
**A field written to catch every enemy standing in it is suppressed by the condition of having
enemies standing in it**, which is the same self-defeating structure the Maw has with saturated
cold, and the page does not state it. Terrain is *"a modifier on η"* by that page's own closing
line, so the Suffusion is cheapest exactly where it has least to do.

**Residue:** heavy and legible. Aetheric Residue is *"a permanent, if often subtle, change to
the location where the working occurred"* (`The Core Vocabulary` §II) — and here there is a
second, worse residue the page never mentions. Anamnesis's register failure is
**thermal resetting**: *"Reading is not always passive, and a careless practitioner erases the
record in the act of taking it."* Every trapped-charge dosimeter empties when it is read.
**On the register's own law, the Suffusion may destroy the memory it shows**, in the target and
in the caster both, and the caster is standing in the field. That is a cost of a completely
different order from the one the page prices, and it is the most important thing in this account.

**Saturation:** the ceiling is the flooded volume, not the caster's will. A gravity current
stops at the radius its volume supports; more expenditure widens nothing without more film.

**Wellspring stratum.** **Oblivara · *The Dark Mirror*** (primary), Family **Limina**, Physics
Domain **Entropy, Void and Mind**. Its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Limina — Entropy, Void and Mind.md`:

> "Information survives a noisy channel when it is encoded with enough redundancy that corrupted
> symbols can be reconstructed from the surviving ones, and a corner reflector returns incident
> energy precisely along the path it arrived on. Oblivara does both: the practitioner's identity
> is redundantly encoded against erasure, and attempts at perceptual manipulation return to their
> source."
>
> **Failure** · "Code exhaustion. Redundancy has a correction limit, and past it the
> reconstruction is confident, complete, and wrong."

**Anamnesis · *The Wellspring of Remembrance*** (secondary), same Family, same Domain:

> "Basalt records the direction of the field it cooled in and holds that record for geological
> time. Fired ceramic accumulates trapped electrons in proportion to the radiation dose it has
> absorbed since its last firing, and releases them as light when heated. Objects genuinely store
> their history in physically readable form. **Anamnesis is the instrument, not the miracle.**"
>
> **Failure** · "Thermal resetting. Reading is not always passive, and a careless practitioner
> erases the record in the act of taking it."

Both are Limina, so this is same-Family harmonisation, which *"compounds"* — *"because the three
constitutive laws agree with one another and reinforce along the same axes"* (Families page,
Harmonization). Register Stat Effects, read through the Merge Ledger (`_method.md` §VI):
Oblivara strengthens Resilience **Persistence** (from Nullity) and Gnosis **Cognition** (from
Warding); Anamnesis strengthens Gnosis **Retention**, Gnosis **Analysis** (from Diagnosis) and
Resilience **Anchoring** (from Coherence).

**The page's primary is a substitution the mirror cannot check.** The Codex line reads
*"Oblivara, The Dark Mirror (primary, substituted for the source's "Nyxial" per the migration
note above)"* — and there is no migration note above it, or anywhere on the mirrored page. The
substitution is probably right on its merits (Nyxial is thermal-inertia regulation and has
nothing to do with mirrors or memory), but the reasoning is not in the file, so it is a
reading and not a record. Logged.

*Four Theories.* **Consent Doctrine**, undisturbed, and unusually cleanly: nothing here is asked
for. A film on a floor returns what strikes it. The *"Residue Position"* is the one a reader will
reach for — *"every Wellspring is the standing residue of something that already happened"* — and
a working whose entire content is reading residue makes that position feel true. It is not the
Accord's, and this account does not adopt it.

**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Aether Shell**, the Material-Plane layer that
*"converts inner truth into outward force"* — projected outward as a film, which is Spellcraft's
*"ephemeral sentence, carried by breath, hand, or trained interior chain."* The
**Attraction Layer** is also loaded, because the silhouette attacks *"from the direction the
original failure came from"*, and a direction indexed to an old failure is a bond on the
Plane of Fate rather than a vector in the room. Developmental Tier at Stage VI: **Harmonic**
(Part Twenty-One). Crystal State: `null` — the page names no practitioner. **The invoice:** the
caster's own buried memories, surfacing in fragments for the field's whole duration, *"paid
whether or not the field is doing useful work at that moment."* **The system has no unit for
memory**, so this is priced in a currency the ledger cannot carry (`SA-GAP-MEMORY-AS-COST`).

**The lens: Augustine's *memoria*.** *Confessions* X.8–X.17. Augustine goes down into what he
calls *"the fields and vast palaces of memory"* — *campi et lata praetoria memoriae* — where the
images of things are stored, and finds that he does not so much possess the store as live in it:
*"there I meet myself."* Two of his observations belong to this page exactly. The first is that
he must go looking, that the thing comes out when summoned and sometimes brings out the wrong
neighbours with it, which is a decoder retrieving the nearest codeword. The second is his
genuine puzzle at Book X.16: memory contains forgetfulness, and he cannot say how, because the
absence would have to be present in order to be remembered. **The Suffusion's Limit is that
puzzle, made into a Limitation line** — *"An empty vault has nothing to show"*, and a mind sealed
against Anamnesis walks through and sees only glass.

**The productive misreading.** A school reading the mireglass as Augustinian *memoria* holds that
what the film returns is a true image faithfully stored, because for Augustine the store does not
lie; only the will that reaches into it does. That school will teach its students to **believe
the silhouette**, to treat what it shows as a confession already extracted, and to build practice
on its testimony. It is wrong in the one place that kills: past the correction limit the
reconstruction is *"confident, complete, and wrong"*, and the page's own closing note says two
witnesses to the same failure were shown two different things. **What gets that school killed is
not the silhouette. It is trusting the record it was taken from** — which is also, precisely, the
route the page hands to Judicium.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Rt]` **Return** · Synonym · Auren as the governing
form — the boundary the working sets is *the record is permitted to leave along the vector it
arrived on*, and "Return" is the index's own word for that limit — laid across `[Ae]`
**Vastness** · Root · Zhaeren for the flooded extent. `[Et]` **Mirage** · Root · Kaetra is the
alternative that deserves to be argued for, and filing it there would read the Suffusion as a
limit on *what may be taken for a body* rather than on what may be returned; note that `[Et]`
is already contested on `Liminal Palace of False Dawn` (`SA-GAP-GLYPH-ET-COLLISION`). All from
the thirty unclassified forms in
`wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`.
*Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling.*

**What boundary moves.** One quantity, and it is not the memory. **The acceptance angle of a
surface.** Pin a floor to corner-cube geometry and the ordinary law of reflection stops
scattering and starts returning antiparallel; pin the same surface's read-out to trapped-charge
release and the record that was already sitting in whatever stands on it comes out. Neither
law is created and neither is opposed. The Index's rule holds: *"A glyph is a boundary condition
imposed on that law. It moves a limit, and the law does what it has always done on the far side
of the new limit."*

**What the law then does on its own.** All of it, and every line on the page falls out.
The film spreads to the radius its volume supports and stops, because that is what a viscous
gravity current does. It reads whoever is standing on it, because a dosimeter reads whatever is
in contact with it and has no opinion about whose side they are on. It returns along the
incident vector, so the silhouette comes from the bearing the original failure came from — **not
because anything chose that bearing, but because retroreflection has no other bearing
available.** It shows nothing to an empty vault, because there is no trapped charge to release.
It shows two witnesses two different things, because the decoder is past its correction limit.
And it floods the caster too, because a film on a floor is a region.

**The Four Glyph Roles, and where this one is thin.** Authorization, Boundary, Direction, Sealing
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). Boundary is the radius; Direction is
retroreflection, which is the strongest role on the page because the law supplies the direction
for free. **Sealing is the role at risk**, and the Craft page is blunt about why: *"Sealing
failing produces a working that does not stop, because the law goes on obeying itself after the
operator's attention has left the room."* A film does not evaporate when attention lapses. It
is still on the floor.

**Law V check, and it does not close cleanly.** Stage VI sits below Refraction, so an external
anchor is required: *"Below Stage VII, Refraction, a working requires voice, hand, ink or blood:
an external anchor for the boundary."* The page files the Craft as *"Spellcraft (declared)"*,
which names a trigger and not a vector, and the Craft page's five vectors are Spoken, Gestured,
Bodily and Internalised. **The only anchor the page actually supplies is the mireglass itself** —
a medium laid down by hand, which reads as the ink vector and would close the gate. That is a
reading, not a statement, and it matters, because *"Interrupt the chain … Break his wrist and a
gestured chain is finished"* is a counter that needs to know which vector it is taking. Logged.

**Failure mode — Boundary fault, with the Sealing role as its mechanism.** *"The condition was
established imprecisely and the law ran somewhere unintended. Burns, structural collapse in the
wrong wall, **a working that continued after the practitioner stopped**"*
(`Two Sets of Books` §VII). The unintended place is under the caster's own boots, and the page
states the consequence without naming the fault: *"there is no documented way to run it
one-sided."* The second and quieter Boundary failure is thermal resetting — the law ran through
the record rather than across it and took the record with it.

**What the target sees and feels.** A dark fluid comes across the floor and does not look like
water, because a retroreflective surface is black from everywhere except your own eye-line.
Then it is not black: it is holding you, and one particular thing you did. *"Every enemy standing
in it sees a memory of their own worst failure step out of the mireglass and come at them"*, and
it comes from the bearing the original came from, which is the detail that makes a trained
soldier turn the wrong way. Reaction time and spellcasting clarity both degrade — the page's own
Target Response — and *"a target who fails to recognise the reflection for what it is treats the
silhouette as a second, independent threat and splits their attention accordingly."* **That
splitting is the whole of the working's damage output as the mechanism describes it**, because
the mechanism names no law that puts force behind the silhouette's arm: remanent magnetisation
and retroreflection return *information*, and nothing on the page supplies mass or momentum the
way Eidolyn's templated nucleation does on the Oneiron pages. And the caster is standing in the
same flood, with their own fragments coming up uninvited, for as long as they hold it.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** No practitioner, so η is read off the Stage:
Stage VI ⇒ Tier of Standing 5 · Expert (Part Five) ⇒ **η 0.60–0.70** (Part Nineteen, Tier 5 row as
corrected by **R44-4**). **Thirty to forty percent of every expenditure leaves as heat, sound and
structural bleed**
(`Two Sets of Books` §III). In this working that bleed is unusually visible: a retroreflective
film illuminated by its own losses is *lit*, and *"a low-efficiency practitioner is loud, warm,
and easy to find"* describes a twelve-metre glowing floor better than it describes most workings.

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Break the man — Read him, which here means read the *image*.** *"Taught techniques are
  documented and counterable by anyone who studied"*, and the page's own Origin puts this one in
  a catalogued Archive with *"no undocumented originator to name"*. The counter is on file.
  Judicium's law is emission and absorption spectroscopy — *"no substance can present another's
  spectrum"* — and a silhouette that is a memory has the wrong spectrum for a body. Once the
  target holds that reading, *"the silhouette stops landing"*, because the mechanism's actual
  output was attention-splitting and attention can be un-split.
- **Deny the field — choose the ground, and the ground is a crowd.** The route the page does not
  make. Limina is suppressed by *"Saturated Aether, **crowd density**, active Domains"*, and
  *"Terrain is not flavour. It is a modifier on η."* A packed melee, a street market, a shield
  wall: the Suffusion is being asked to work in the exact conditions its Family pays extra in,
  and it is being asked to do it because that is where the enemies are.
- **Deny the field — remove the law's inputs.** Absolution is stress-relief annealing:
  *"the history stays, the geometry stays, and the stored energy that made the history dangerous
  is released."* That is not a shield; it is emptying the trap population the working needs to
  read. Luminalis works the other end — *"incident observation is filtered out at the boundary
  rather than absorbed"* — and a retroreflector with nothing to return is a dark floor.
- **Break the boundary — take the anchor, once someone rules what the anchor is.** Stage VI is
  below Refraction, so *"Below Refraction a working requires an external anchor: voice, hand, ink
  or blood. Take the anchor and the sentence does not complete."* The page files only
  *"Spellcraft (declared)"*, so the vector a counter would take is not on the record
  (`SA-GAP-MIREGLASS-ANCHOR-VECTOR`). This is a live handle for a player who reads the Craft
  page, and a live question for the table.
- **Break the body — stay off the glass.** Fifteen metres is a small field with a visible edge
  and a spreading front that takes its time arriving. *"Distance"* is sufficient, and *"Shot"*
  does not care what the floor is doing.

**The tell, stated plainly.** A dark film comes across the ground and reads black from every
angle but your own, and then it is showing you something. The edge is visible, the front is slow,
and the caster is standing in the middle of it and cannot step out without ending it — so the
tell and the weakness are once again the same fact.

# System account · Obelisk of the Eclipsed Dawn

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** Six sigils fall together and a very large crystal prism grows
out of the point where they met. Inside a region eight hundred metres across, decay runs the
wrong way: things that were coming apart are put back, and things that were holding are let go.
The prism keeps a record of the moment it was raised.

**The real phenomenon.** **A locally maintained entropy decrease, paid for in recorded
information** — and this is the one working in the batch whose central conceit is not a
convenient exception to a real law but a correct application of it.

**(a) The second law is statistical, and the price of running it backwards is computable.**
Loschmidt put the objection to Boltzmann in 1876: reverse every velocity in a gas and entropy
must decrease, so a mechanical account cannot deliver an absolute arrow. Boltzmann's answer was
that it can deliver an overwhelming one. The modern form is exact. The **fluctuation theorem**
(Evans and Searles 1993; Crooks 1999) gives the ratio of probabilities for a trajectory producing
entropy +σ against one producing −σ as **P(+σ)/P(−σ) = e^(σ/k_B)**. A local entropy decrease is
therefore **not forbidden. It is priced**, and the price is an exponent.

**(b) Maxwell's demon, Szilard, Landauer — and the page's Cost is the mechanism.** A demon can
lower a gas's entropy by sorting it, but only if it *measures*, and Landauer's principle fixes the
thermodynamic cost of the record: erasing one bit at temperature T dissipates at least
**k_B T ln2 = 2.87×10⁻²¹ J at 300 K**. Bennett closed the loop: the demon can measure and act
reversibly, but it cannot reset its own memory for free. The entropy you removed from the gas is
sitting in the record, and it comes out when the record is cleared.

**The page's Cost is Landauer's erasure, written as psychology.** *"The Obelisk records what she
was feeling when she raised it and plays it back to her."* A working that lowers entropy inside a
boundary **must** hold a record, and the record **must** eventually be paid out. The playback is
not a flavourful penalty. It is the second law collecting. **This is the best physics-to-cost fit
in the twenty-eight and it appears to be accidental.**

**(c) Basilithe's actual law builds a prism.** The register's Basilithe is not crystal growth — it
is *"Natural frequency and stiffness-to-mass ratio. Quartz oscillator stability"* — and a quartz
resonator's figures are the reason it is the analogue: **Q factors of 10⁴–10⁶ in air and up to
10⁷ under vacuum, fractional frequency stability of 10⁻⁶ to 10⁻⁸**, which is why civilisations set
clocks by cut quartz. Quartz's own habit is a hexagonal **prism** terminated by rhombohedra.
**The register predicts the page's prism better than the page's own sentence does**, because a
high-stiffness low-loss lattice holding a frequency against forcing is exactly a structure that
can be held metastably open — and holding a state against forcing is what this working does for
its whole duration. The page calls this *"Basilithe's crystal-growth law"*, which is a different
law belonging to no current in the register. Logged.

**(d) The four-instance cap is a fibre bundle.** Fractura's contribution is
*"mirrored load-sharing across up to four instances"*, and load-sharing among redundant members is
one of the most thoroughly studied failure problems in materials science. In the **equal-load-
sharing fibre-bundle model**, each surviving member takes up the load of any member that fails;
when the redistributed load exceeds the survivors' thresholds the failure becomes an **avalanche**
and the whole bundle goes at once. **That is the page's Limit, exactly:** *"a fifth does not fail
to appear, it brings the others down with it."* Nobody had to write the punishment in. A bundle
loaded past its redistribution capacity does not shed the newest member. It fails together.

**(e) Petralon and the arch.** Masonry runs roughly **5–50 MPa** in compression and about a tenth
of that in tension, which is why the arch exists: *"it converts a span into a queue of compressive
members."* An eight-hundred-metre construct held up by a mass anchor is a compressive structure,
and its register failure is the one to watch — *"Load the structure in the one direction it does
not answer and it does not deform gracefully. It parts."*

**The governing law, and the page's best line.** The **Aether-Physics Fusion Rule**, stated in
Part Eleven: *"magical combat must obey physical law and Aetheric law simultaneously … The
Continuum measures the energy that arrived. It does not ask where the energy came from."* Under
that rule the Obelisk's entropy inversion is legal and its cost is calculable, and the calculation
produces a surprise.

**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Field: **800 m across** (page). Radius 400 m ⇒ footprint π(400 m)² = **5.03×10⁵ m²**;
  as a hemisphere, (2/3)π(400 m)³ = **1.34×10⁸ m³**.
- Air mass in that hemisphere at 1.225 kg/m³: **1.64×10⁸ kg**.
- Landauer quantum at 300 K: **k_B T ln2 = 2.87×10⁻²¹ J/bit.**
- Record required per joule-per-kelvin of entropy reversed: 1/(k_B ln2) =
  **1.045×10²³ bits per J/K.**
- Reversing a modest **1 J/(kg·K)** across that air mass is ΔS = 1.64×10⁸ J/K, which needs
  **1.7×10³¹ bits** of record and dissipates **≈4.9×10¹⁰ J (49 GJ)** when that record is cleared.
- Fluctuation-theorem odds against the same excursion happening spontaneously:
  **e^(−1.7×10³¹)**. The working is not doing something impossible. It is doing something whose
  spontaneous probability has an exponent with thirty-two digits in it.
- SSS band, from Part Eleven: peak force **1–5 PN**, energy yield **418 TJ – 4.184 ZJ**,
  **100 kt – 1 Tt TNT.** The page's *"418 TJ to 4.184 EJ"* is **the same lower bound and an upper
  bound short by a factor of 10³** — one teraton of TNT is 4.184 **zetta**joules, and Part Eleven
  says so in the same row.
- Masonry-analogue strength for the Petralon anchor: **5–50 MPa** compressive, ~1/10 in tension.
- Quartz-analogue resonator figures for the Basilithe lattice: **Q 10⁴–10⁷**, stability
  **10⁻⁶–10⁻⁸**.

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** Two budgets, and they are four orders of
magnitude apart, which nobody has noticed.

**Fracture Cascade** is a destructive mode and the page prices it correctly against the Grade:
418 TJ at the floor, and the joules are in whatever is being unwound, plus the field.

**Oneiric Reversal and Ecliptic Aegis are almost free.** Reversing a substantial amount of entropy
across a 1.34×10⁸ m³ hemisphere costs about **49 GJ** of eventual Landauer dissipation — **four
orders of magnitude *below* the SSS floor of 418 TJ.** The page claims these modes *"draw on the
same ceiling as absorbed or redirected rather than released force"*, and on the physics they do
not need to. **The binding constraint on a reversal is not power. It is storage:** 1.7×10³¹ bits
is thirteen sextillion bytes, and the system has no quantity anywhere for how much record a
Crystal can hold. **That is the real reason there can only be four**, and it is a better reason
than the page gives. Logged as a gap, because the system supplies no capacity figure to check it
against.

**Precedent we are not copying.** Giant summoned monoliths are a genre staple — Bleach's
Kōkō Gonryō Rikyū, the Sephiroth of Persona, every JRPG crystal that fixes what the plot broke,
Guilty Gear's Ky. Those are **restoration by fiat**. This one restores by **holding a record and
paying for it**, and its cost is the record coming back. The divergence is that the Obelisk's
limits are not a designer's balance pass: four instances because a load-sharing bundle avalanches
at redistribution capacity, a locked mode because a metastable lattice cannot be re-cut while it
is holding, a playback because the demon must clear its memory.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** The largest single draw in this batch: a hemisphere of **1.34×10⁸ m³**, held
autonomously *"for as long as the line back to her holds."* Materia is favoured by *"Mineral-rich
rock, worked stone, deep foundation"* and suppressed by *"Open water, high altitude, moving
platforms"* (Families page, Environmental Coupling) — so **the Obelisk is a working that cannot be
raised at sea, on a ship, or from an airship**, and the page never states its own hardest
geographic limit. *"Terrain is not flavour. It is a modifier on η."*

**Residue:** heavy, structural, and unusually legible — an eight-hundred-metre construct leaves a
site that reads for a long time, and *"Aetheric Residue … a permanent, if often subtle, change to
the location where the working occurred."* Nothing subtle about this one.

**Saturation:** the danger the page does not name. *"Aetheric Saturation · A danger state in which
excessive Essence floods an area faster than it can disperse. Produces Crystal Fracture Events in
everyone exposed, **including the person who caused it**"* (`Core Vocabulary` §II). Four
simultaneous instances at this scale is the definition of that state, and the Counterplay register
prices it as *"a weapon of last resort and a real one"*. The page's punishment for a fifth instance
may be that danger state arriving, rather than a separate rule.

**Wellspring stratum — six currents, which is the page's real claim.** **Basilithe · *The Stone
Will*** (primary), Family **Materia**, Physics Domain **Material Science**. Its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Materia — Material Science.md`:

> "A body's resonant frequency is fixed by its stiffness and its mass, and a high-stiffness
> low-loss structure holds that frequency against forcing so reliably that civilisations set their
> clocks by cut quartz. Basilithe raises the Crystal's stiffness until its resonance simply will
> not be driven off, which is a defence against every technique that works by imposing an external
> rhythm."
>
> **Failure** · "Resonant catastrophe. A frequency that cannot be shifted also cannot be detuned
> away from a forcing that happens to match it."

**That is not a crystal-growth law, and the page says it is.** Logged. The register's own reading
is better for this working and it also supplies the failure the page's Weakness half-states:
*"sustained pure-radiance pressure can crack the Tenebra shell layer"* is one forcing; a forcing
that **matches the lattice's locked frequency** is resonant catastrophe, and a structure that
cannot be detuned cannot get out of the way of it.

**Tarturon · *The Fire of Reckoning*** (secondary), Family **Materia**:

> "Below yield a material returns to shape and the load leaves no record. Above it, behaviour
> changes kind, and the response scales with the load applied rather than with anything the
> material chose. Tarturon couples output to moral weight through exactly that curve, and like
> thermite it supplies its own oxidiser, which is the mechanical reason the reaction cannot be
> smothered once initiated."
>
> **Failure** · "Runaway. The load that drives the reaction is generated by the reaction, and
> nothing in the mechanism provides a stopping condition."

**Tarturon is a yield-criterion current, not an entropy current**, and the page calls it
*"Tarturon's entropy law"* and its counter *"raw Tarturon or a comparable Dissolution-aligned
field"*. Entropy-of-mixing belongs to **Dissolution · *The Unbinding Stream*** in Limina —
*"a solid dissolves not because the solvent attacks it but because the mixed state has higher
entropy than the separated one"* — which is a different Family and a different Wellspring.
Logged. And Tarturon's actual failure line is **the best available account of the four-instance
cap**: a reaction that generates its own load and has no stopping condition is exactly what a
fifth instance would start.

**Petralon · *The Foundation Stone*** (Vectoria) — *"routing every incoming load into compression
and accepting slow creep deformation as the price of never developing a tensile crack"*, failure
*"Tensile exposure … It parts."* **Fractura · *The Breaking Point*** (Vectoria) —
*"stress concentrates at a crack tip in proportion to the square root of the crack's length, and
once that intensity exceeds the material's toughness the crack runs at a fraction of the speed of
sound and does not stop"*, failure *"Self-inspection. The Wellspring does not exempt its bearer."*
**Oneirion · *The Wellspring of Dream*** (Limina) — configuration space, *"a system's state as a
point in an abstract high-dimensional manifold."* **Tenebra · *The Hidden Shadow*** (Limina) —
absorption and shaped return.

**The cross-Family reading, which is where this page earns its Stage.** Materia with Vectoria is
the *complementary* case — *"matter given form through force is one problem described twice"* — so
Basilithe, Tarturon, Petralon and Fractura reinforce. **Materia against Limina is the canonical
hard case:** *"form set against the erosion of all shape"* (Families page, Harmonization). Oneirion
and Tenebra are Limina. **The Obelisk runs the system's own named hardest pairing, deliberately,
four Wellsprings against two**, and the page's Weakness is the direct consequence:
*"Genuine internal conflict in Opalis warps the lattice toward inversion."* Cross-Family tension
*"the Crystal must resolve, either by Temperance advancement or by developing a Compound Trait
that holds the contradiction as a single law"* — and the ceiling on all of it is **Tempering
Capacity**, which *"is not negotiable. Exceeding it does not fail gracefully by dropping the newest
harmonization. **It degrades all active harmonizations equally.**"* No Capacity figure exists for
Opalis (`SA-GAP-OPALIS-TEMPERING-CAPACITY`).

*Four Theories.* **Consent Doctrine** for the lattice and the load paths. But the page's *mode
selection* — end something, begin something — leans on the **Correspondence Position**,
*"the practitioner and the Wellspring are the same law expressed at two scales, and the working is
the two scales briefly agreeing"*, because her reading of the field is what the construct then
executes. The account names it because the page's language requires it and not because the Accord
endorses it: Correspondence is *"ecclesiastically respectable, administratively useless."*

**Essence stratum.** All three layers loaded, which is rare. **Aether Shell** for the lattice and
the force; **Attraction Layer** for the line back to her, which the page's own Animatria rule makes
load-bearing — sever it and the construct ends; **Essence Core** for the record, because the Core
*"holds identity, memory, and Traits"* and the record it holds is *what she was feeling.*
Developmental Tier at Stage XII: **Sovereign** (Part Twenty-One). Crystal State: `null` — no card
for Opalis (`SA-GAP-OPALIS-NO-CARD`). **The invoice:** *"Reflective Burden"*, her own recent
emotional state played back as psychic feedback, plus *"Structural Strain"*, micro-fractures in
her Temperance layers *"closed only by a period of Essence stillness."* Micro-fractures in
Temperance layers are a **Structural fault** signature and Temperance is *"subtraction, not
accumulation"* — so what this working spends is not reserve but refinement.

**The lens: Proclus on reversion.** *Elements of Theology*, propositions 25–39. Proclus systematises
the Neoplatonic triad: everything **remains** in its cause, **proceeds** from it, and **reverts**
upon it — *monē, prohodos, epistrophē* — and the reversion is not a failure of the procession but
its completion, the way a circle closes. What reverts is restored to what it was in its cause, and
nothing new is made in the reverting. **The Obelisk is an *epistrophē* engine.** It does not build
and it does not heal: *"damaged Essence under Oneiric Reversal is coaxed toward its own healthier
prior state rather than repaired from outside"*, and *"it cannot create or restore life, only
Essence-state."* That is Proclus's rule stated as a Limitation line. A thing can be returned to
its cause and cannot be given a cause it never had.

**The productive misreading.** A school reading the Obelisk as *epistrophē* holds that reversion
is a thing's own nature completing itself, so the Obelisk **permits** rather than **forces**, and a
permission cannot be overloaded. Such a school teaches that the four-instance cap is a matter of
Accord caution rather than structure, and that a practitioner of sufficient standing may raise a
fifth because nothing is being compelled. **What kills them is that the bundle does not consult
anything's nature.** Equal load sharing avalanches at redistribution capacity, and Tarturon's own
register line says *"nothing in the mechanism provides a stopping condition."* The page is blunt
about the outcome — *"a fifth simultaneous instance is not refused, it is punished, and the other
four come down with it"* — and the school reading reversion as consent will read that punishment
as a superstition until the day four Obelisks fall on their own ground.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Rt]` **Return** · Synonym · Auren as the governing
form — the boundary the working sets is *this region's trajectory is permitted to run back along
itself*, and the Index's own word for that limit is Return — laid across `[Ir]` **Continuum** ·
Root · Maelor for the held state and `[Frm]` **Form** · Extension · Thalen for the lattice habit.
`[Ser]` **Renewal** · Root · Wyther is the alternative worth arguing and would file the Obelisk
under *what may be made new* rather than *what may be returned*; the page's own Limitation argues
against it, since nothing here is made. All from the thirty unclassified forms in
`wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`.
*Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling.*

**What boundary moves.** **The direction in which the field's trajectory is allowed to be
traversed**, and nothing else. The second law is not opposed — the Index is explicit that
*"a glyph cannot attack a law. Laws are not the sort of thing that can be opposed"* — it is
**paid**. Pin the boundary and the fluctuation theorem's exponentially improbable branch becomes
the branch the field is on; Landauer's principle then charges for the record that makes it
possible, and the charge is deferred until the record is cleared.

**What the law then does on its own.** All of it, and every limit on the page falls out of one of
the six currents without anyone writing a rule.

The prism grows and holds, because a high-stiffness low-loss lattice holds its configuration
against forcing. The mode cannot be changed mid-manifestation — *"committing to the wrong reading
at the trigger moment is committed for the duration"* — because a metastable state cannot be
re-cut while it is the thing holding the boundary open; re-cutting it *is* collapsing it, which is
what the page says happens. It cannot create or restore life because a reversion returns a
trajectory and does not author one. It cannot overturn another Stage XII practitioner's standing
decree because two boundaries at the same limit is the `[Lk]` **Lock** problem, and the Index says
Lock *"occupies a limit so no other limit can be set there."* Four instances hold and a fifth
avalanches the bundle. Genuine internal conflict warps the lattice toward inversion, because a
Crystal holding Materia against Limina is holding *"form set against the erosion of all shape"* and
the resolution is the practitioner's coherence. And a Dissolution-aligned field erodes the outer
lattice faster than the lattice can re-order, because that is a competition between two rates and
the holder loses when the input exceeds its throughput.

**The Four Glyph Roles, and this working's are unusually legible**
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). **Authorization** is her standing at Stage XII and
the line back to her, which the page's Animatria rule makes severable. **Boundary** is the 800 m.
**Direction** is the mode — end, contain, restore — chosen once and locked. **Sealing** is the
release, and *"Sealing is the role that kills people … Sealing failing produces a working that
does not stop, because the law goes on obeying itself after the operator's attention has left the
room."* **An Obelisk whose Sealing fails is an eight-hundred-metre autonomous construct with no
operator**, and the page's own Counterplay quietly relies on Sealing working: the construct ends
when the line back to her ends.

**Law V check, and it closes on the Stage rather than on an anchor.** Stage XII sits far above
Refraction, so *"the Glyph Roles compress into an internal chain that needs no material anchor at
all."* The page's Trigger — *"Opalis stills her Essence and commits to a reading of the field"* —
is an internalised chain, correctly filed for the Stage, and it removes the whole *"Interrupt the
chain"* route from the board.

**Failure mode — Structural fault, and the page diagnoses itself.** *"The Crystal could not hold
what the boundary permitted. Overchannel, Backlash, Fracture. The working succeeds and the
practitioner does not"* (`Two Sets of Books` §VII). The page's Cost names the symptom exactly:
*"Structural Strain leaves micro-fractures in her Temperance layers under prolonged
manifestation."* A **Coherence fault** is the second and slower one and the page names that too,
in its Weakness: genuine internal conflict warping the lattice is *"the practitioner's lived
behaviour and their stated law have drifted apart, and the Continuum reads the drift"*, arriving
as a mode that inverts. **This is one of the few pages whose stated Cost and stated Weakness are
two different Faults of the four, correctly, and it does not know it.**

**What the target sees and feels.** Six sigils that had been orbiting her fall inward to a point,
and then the point is a prism and the prism is tall. *"A colossal prism rises from it"*, and the
region it claims is up to eight hundred metres across, which is a district. Inside it, one of
three things is true and nobody on the ground gets to find out which until it starts.

Under **Fracture Cascade**, a hostile construct, curse or barrier *"unwinds structurally rather
than being struck"* — which is the correct description and the frightening one: there is no impact
to brace against, no plane of failure to armour, the thing simply proceeds backwards through its
own assembly. Under **Ecliptic Aegis**, an ally has incoming force *"redirected or nullified at the
shell rather than absorbed into their own Crystal"*, so the blow does not arrive and the ally does
not pay for it either. Under **Oneiric Reversal**, damaged Essence is *"coaxed toward its own
healthier prior state rather than repaired from outside"* — and a practitioner on the receiving end
of that experiences something no healing working produces, because nothing is being added: they are
becoming an earlier version of themselves, with whatever that earlier version did not yet have.

And the thing keeps a record. It holds what she was feeling when she raised it, and it gives that
back to her while it stands, which means **the field's occupants are inside a structure that is
currently reading its author aloud to its author.** Four of these can stand at once. A fifth does
not fail to appear.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** No card, so η is read off the Stage: Stage XII ⇒ Tier
of Standing 7 · Grandmaster (Part Five) ⇒ **η 0.85–0.90** (Part Nineteen). **Ten to fifteen percent
leaves as heat, sound and structural bleed** — and on this working the bleed is not the loss that
matters. The Landauer record is, and the record is not bleed: it is deferred, held, and paid on
her.

## 5 · Counterplay and the challenge

**The fairness check** (`.claude/skills/wotr-write/references/fair-play.md`).

| Test | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Costs something that hurts | **pass** | And the cost is mechanically necessary rather than imposed: a working that lowers entropy must hold a record, and *"the Obelisk records what she was feeling when she raised it and plays it back to her."* The second charge is worse and slower — *"hairline stress in her own Temperance layers that only stillness closes"* — and Temperance is *"subtraction, not accumulation"*, so what she is spending is refinement, not reserve. No EU figure exists, so the cost cannot be priced (`SA-GAP-EU-FORMULA`). |
| Stated limits | **pass, and they are unusually good** | 800 m; **four instances and a fifth brings the four down**; the mode is locked at the trigger and cannot be changed without collapsing and re-forming; it cannot create or restore life, only Essence-state; it cannot overturn a Stage XII peer's standing decree, only contest or deflect; and the line back to her is severable under the Animatria rule. |
| Something beats it | **pass, three routes** | A Dissolution-aligned field forcing early collapse; a Stage XII peer's standing decree contesting a mode; sustained pure radiance against the Tenebra layer. Two of those are peer-only and one is not, which is better than several pages in this batch manage. |

The peak force, 1–5 PN, is exactly right. |

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Deny the field — choose the ground, and the ground is water.** The route the page does not
  make, and the strongest one available to anyone who is not a peer. Materia is suppressed by
  *"Open water, high altitude, moving platforms"*, and *"Terrain is not flavour. It is a modifier
  on η."* **An eight-hundred-metre Materia construct has no foundation on a ship, on a bridge, on
  a flooded field or above a certain altitude**, and Petralon's whole contribution is a mass
  anchor. Fight her at sea.
- **Break the boundary — Lock, and the peer clause is the system's own reason it works.** The
  page's second counter is *"a Stage XII peer's own standing decree can contest or deflect a mode
  without needing to break the construct outright"*, and the Glyph Index explains why that is not
  arbitrary: *"**Lock** occupies a limit so no other limit can be set there."* A standing decree
  already occupying the limit is not fighting the Obelisk. It got there first.
- **Break the boundary — sever the line, because the page's own Category permits it.** The
  Obelisk is **Animatria**, and the Counterplay register is explicit about what that means:
  *"Animatria constructs fail completely because their continuity runs back to a source the
  Silence has severed."* **Silence** is therefore a total answer to this working and the page does
  not list it. So is anything that breaks her focus — and she is holding four instances, a locked
  mode, and a playback of her own recent feeling while she does it.
- **Break the man — Rigidity, and the fifth Obelisk.** *"Present him with the situation his own
  law handles worst and he will handle it that way regardless."* Her law handles a fifth instance
  by avalanching the other four. A commander who can generate four simultaneous emergencies at
  four points of an eight-hundred-metre field is not attacking the construct; **he is asking her
  to want a fifth.**
- **Break the man — the internal conflict she already has.** The page's own Weakness is
  *"Genuine internal conflict in Opalis warps the lattice toward inversion"*, and the register
  route is **Coherence drift**: *"You do not have to cause this. You have to notice it and wait."*
  A working that runs the system's canonical hard cross-Family pairing — Materia against Limina,
  *"form set against the erosion of all shape"* — is a working whose stability is her coherence.
- **Deny the field — saturate, at a price.** *"Flood the area with Essence faster than it
  disperses and everyone present takes Fracture risk, including whoever did it."* Four Obelisks at
  this scale may already be that state (`Core Vocabulary` §II), which makes the fifth-instance
  punishment a rule the system might already contain.

**The tell, stated plainly.** Six sigils that were orbiting her stop orbiting and fall to a point,
and a prism the size of a tower grows out of it. She has to be still to do it — *"Opalis stills her
Essence and commits to a reading of the field"* — and the mode she chose is locked from that
moment. **The tell and the lock are the same beat**, so an opponent who reads the stillness has
already learned that whatever she picked, she is stuck with.

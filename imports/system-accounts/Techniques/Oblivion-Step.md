# System account · Oblivion-Step

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** He is standing here. A quarter of a second later he is standing
up to fifty metres away, near somebody whose mind was drifting. Nothing crossed the space between
and nobody heard anything.

**The real phenomenon.** There is no real phenomenon that moves a body fifty metres without
crossing the space, and the page does not pretend otherwise: it relocates the whole claim into
Oneirion's manifold. **So the physical account here is not an explanation of the crossing. It is
the bill for the crossing's two visible side effects, and both bills are computable.**

**(a) The manifold, and the one law that objects.** Oneirion's stated analogue is configuration
space, and configuration space is load-bearing real physics. For N particles the configuration
manifold Q is 3N-dimensional; phase space adds the momenta and doubles it; the system's history is
a single trajectory. **Liouville's theorem** is the constraint: under Hamiltonian flow the
phase-space density is constant along a trajectory, dρ/dt = 0, and phase-space volume is
incompressible. Trajectories do not jump and they do not cross.

**And here is the difficulty, stated plainly because it will not go away.** The coordinates of a
mechanical configuration space **are** the generalised positions. A continuous curve in Q is, by
construction, a continuous motion in the room — that is what makes Q a *configuration* space
rather than an abstract label set. So *"it moves her through configuration space, the coordinate
space in which every arrangement a system could take is a point with no physical location of its
own"* cannot, read strictly against its own analogue, deliver *"no line of travel between the two
points."* Either the manifold is not the mechanical configuration space, in which case the
register's analogue has been stretched past the law it names, or the crossing is continuous in the
room and the page's central claim is wrong. **The account cannot pick.** Logged.

**(b) The acoustics, which are the best number on this page.** Two bodies' worth of air has to
move: roughly **0.08 m³** must be evacuated at the arrival point and the same volume filled at the
departure point. A volume appearing or disappearing in a fluid is a **monopole source**, the
loudest kind there is, and its far-field pressure goes as p = ρ·Q̇/(4πr) where Q̇ is the volume
acceleration. Work the arithmetic against the page's own timing:

- If the crossing were genuinely instantaneous — say 1 ms — then Q = 0.08/10⁻³ = 80 m³/s,
  Q̇ ≈ 8×10⁴ m³/s², and p at 1 m = 1.225 × 8×10⁴ / 4π = **≈7,800 Pa ≈ 172 dB SPL.** **That is a
  gunshot, twice, fifty metres apart.**
- At the page's own **0.25 s**, Q̇ = 0.08/0.0625 = 1.28 m³/s², and p at 1 m =
  1.225 × 1.28 / 4π = **0.125 Pa ≈ 76 dB** — a soft thump in a quiet room and nothing at all in a
  fight.
- To be genuinely inaudible at ordinary background, ~60 dB (0.02 Pa) at 1 m, the crossing needs
  **≈0.62 s**.

**So the page's "no sound" is very nearly right and it is right for a reason the page does not
state: the quarter-second is what buys the silence.** A quarter of a second is not a penalty
bolted onto an instant crossing. It is the crossing, slowed to the point where the air can get out
of the way. The page insists the lag is *"the Crystal's own re-arrival lag rather than a nerve
response"* and *"cannot be trained away"* — which is exactly what an acoustically-constrained
displacement time would be. **This is the single most satisfying closure in the batch and it is
almost certainly accidental.**

**(c) Structural relaxation, with its real numbers.** Hypnather's stated analogue. A glass below its
transition temperature T_g is a liquid arrested mid-arrangement, and it continues to relax toward
equilibrium — **physical ageing**, measurable in polymers and oxide glasses over hours to decades.
The relaxation is not a single exponential: it follows the **Kohlrausch–Williams–Watts** stretched
form φ(t) = exp[−(t/τ)^β] with **β ≈ 0.3–0.7**, and τ(T) itself follows **Vogel–Fulcher–Tammann**,
diverging as temperature falls. The state reached is summarised by a **fictive temperature**, and
interrupting the anneal locks a higher fictive temperature in — *"the configuration locks where it
stood, worse ordered than before it began."* **The stretched exponential is the point:** a KWW
relaxation with β well below 1 has a very fast early portion and an enormously long tail, so
*"half a second rather than a night"* is not absurd on the physics. A tiny fraction of the ageing
is available almost immediately. It is the rest that takes the night.

**(d) Letheveil, and why the crossing is not remembered.** Lossy compression, Shannon entropy,
rate–distortion theory: a signal is compressed by discarding the components that carry least
information for a given distortion budget. Letheveil *"identifies what is load-bearing and discards
the rest"*, and its failure is **generation loss** — *"each pass discards a little more, and a
memory compressed repeatedly degrades toward an artefact the practitioner still experiences as a
memory."* Every re-encode of a lossy codec compounds the previous one's artefacts. **Aeldoris
re-encodes the same half-second every time he steps.**

**The governing law, and the page's best line.** The **Aether-Physics Fusion Rule** (Part Eleven):
*"magical combat must obey physical law and Aetheric law simultaneously … The Continuum measures
the energy that arrived. It does not ask where the energy came from."* Under that rule the air
still has to move, which is why the acoustic arithmetic is not optional and why it turns out to
vindicate the page's timing.

**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Range: **0 – 50 m** (page). Sphere of arrival, (4/3)π(50 m)³ = **5.24×10⁵ m³.**
- Body volume displaced: **≈0.08 m³** (card gives 1.91 m, lean; ~80 kg at ~1,000 kg/m³).
- pV work to displace that volume against 101 kPa: **≈8.1 kJ.**
- Monopole pressure at 1 m: **≈7,800 Pa (172 dB)** at a 1 ms crossing; **0.125 Pa (76 dB)** at
  0.25 s; inaudible below ~60 dB needs **≈0.62 s.**
- Addressing cost, for completeness: locating a body to 1 mm inside the 50 m sphere is
  log₂(5.24×10¹⁴) ≈ **49 bits.** **Trivial.** The dream-shadow is therefore not an information
  requirement; it is a boundary-condition requirement, which is the page's own claim.
- Arrival lag: **≈0.25 s** (page). Against A-Grade reaction speed of **0.2–1 ms** (Part Six) that
  is **250 to 1,250 of his own reaction periods.**
- Against A-Grade attack speed, Mach 10–30, **1,715–8,575 m/s** (Part Six): a strike already in
  flight covers **429 – 2,144 m** during the lag, and the whole range of the step is 50 m.
- Relaxation figures: KWW **β ≈ 0.3–0.7**; VFT divergence; fictive temperature as the state
  variable.

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** Small and ordinary: about **8 kJ** of pressure
work to swap two body-volumes of air, plus whatever the arrival's 76 dB pulse radiates, which is
milliwatts. The joules were in the atmosphere. **What this working spends is not energy, it is
order** — Hypnather's relaxation opened out of sequence, Letheveil's compression run on the same
half-second again and again — and the page prices it in the one currency that fits:
*"a backwash of the anchor's own emotions surfacing in her afterward, uninvited."*

**Precedent we are not copying.** Shadow-step and blink are everywhere: Nightcrawler's bamf (which
*is* a bang, and the comics are right), Dishonored's Blink, Naruto's Hiraishin, the shadow-walk of
every rogue in every CRPG. Almost all of them are **self-contained**: the practitioner supplies
both ends. This one **cannot arrive anywhere unless somebody else's mind is nearly asleep there**,
which makes the technique's range a function of who is present rather than of how strong he is, and
makes its Limitation the page's best line: *"she cannot step to a point that is merely empty."*
The divergence is that the arrival point is a social fact, not a spatial one.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** A very small draw — two body-volumes and a half-second — against a 50 m
addressing envelope. Limina is favoured by *"Darkness, silence, Aether-thin ground, ruins"* and
suppressed by *"Saturated Aether, crowd density, active Domains"* (Families page, Environmental
Coupling), and **this working has the batch's sharpest terrain paradox.** It needs a live, drifting
subconscious inside fifty metres, so it wants people; Limina pays more in a crowd. *"Terrain is not
flavour. It is a modifier on η."* The optimal ground for an Oblivion-Step is therefore **one
distracted sentry in an empty ruin**, and the page never says so.

**Residue:** light, and *"always readable by someone patient enough"* (`Core Vocabulary` §II). Two
residues, at both ends, plus the anchor's re-settling dream-shadow — and the page notes that the
shadow *"re-settles behind her"*, which is a trace left in somebody else's mind. **Residue in a
third party is a different kind of record from residue in a room**, and the system's Residue
definition does not cover it.

**Saturation:** not a limit at this scale.

**Wellspring stratum.** **Oneirion · *The Wellspring of Dream*** (primary), Family **Limina**,
Physics Domain **Entropy, Void and Mind**. Its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Limina — Entropy, Void and Mind.md`:

> "A mechanical system's every possible arrangement is a coordinate in a space that has no physical
> location, and the system's evolution is a trajectory through it. Oneirion lets a working act on
> that manifold rather than on the room. This is the mechanical reason Oneirion techniques reach
> targets no line of sight connects, and the reason they are so difficult to describe afterward."
>
> **Failure** · "Projection error. The trajectory was correct in the manifold and lands nowhere in
> particular when it is mapped back down into a room."

**Projection error is the failure mode of this technique and the page does not name it.** A
mobility working whose register failure is *landing nowhere in particular* has a stated, canonical,
lethal way of going wrong, and the page's Weakness reaches instead for *"a working built on
Aurevane or Luminalis floods the local signal."* Both are true. Only one is Oneirion's own.

**Hypnather · *The Sleep Crown*** (secondary), same Family:

> "A glass is a liquid that stopped moving before it finished arranging itself, and left undisturbed
> it continues to relax toward the ordered state it never reached, slowly and only while quiescent.
> Hypnather grants the Crystal that behaviour. **The reorganisation happens during genuine rest and
> cannot be forced**, which is why Hypnather practitioners sleep like the dead and wake structurally
> different."
>
> **Failure** · "Interrupted relaxation. Disturb the process partway and the configuration locks
> where it stood, worse ordered than before it began."

**The register says the reorganisation cannot be forced. The page forces it twice.** *"Aeldoris
forces that reorganisation open for a half-second rather than a night"*, and the Trigger *"lets her
own Crystal drop toward sleep on command rather than waiting on fatigue."* This is the register-side
sibling of the conflict already on file for `Hypnather Descent Rite`
(`SA-GAP-DESCENT-FORCED-FAILURE`), which was page-against-itself; this one is page-against-register,
and it is on his own Crystal rather than a target's. Logged. **And the register's failure line is
then the Oblivion-Step's real standing cost:** a relaxation forced open and slammed shut two hundred
times in a career is an interrupted relaxation two hundred times, and each one *"locks where it
stood, worse ordered than before it began."*

**Letheveil · *The Waters of Forgetting*** (minor), same Family — lossy compression, and its stat
effect *"**tests** Resilience Coherence rather than simply raising it"*, which through the Merge
Ledger is Resilience **Anchoring**. Its failure is generation loss.

Three Limina currents: same-Family harmonisation, which *"compounds"* (Families page,
Harmonization). No cross-Family tension here at all, which is why this working is cheap and why
his card reads *"Fifty-five of the Sixty remain untouched."*

*Four Theories.* **Consent Doctrine** for the manifold and the relaxation. But the page's own
closing question — *"Why a stranger's dream ever answers a step it was never asked to hold"* — is
a **Debt Position** question: *"the Wellspring answers on credit and the account is settled
somewhere the practitioner cannot see … impossible to test, and disturbingly good at predicting
which practitioners come apart."* The page pairs it with a cost that accumulates in him from other
people's minds, which is exactly what the Debt Position predicts. The account names the lean and
does not adopt it; the Accord enforces Consent.

**Essence stratum.** All three layers. **Aether Shell** for the displacement, at Class III ·
Resonant on his card — *"Essence and Aether exchange freely; a read lands or it does not, with
almost nothing lost between."* **Attraction Layer** for the anchor, and his card says that layer is
**Drifting**, with *"an Attraction Layer built on other people's fears rather than his own bonds"* —
**which is precisely what stepping off strangers' dream-shadows builds.** **Essence Core** for the
Letheveil compression of his own crossing-memory. Developmental Tier at Stage VII: **Resonant**
(Part Twenty-One). Crystal State: **Refined, residue-heavy** (card). **The invoice:** the quarter
second, every time, and *"the memory backwash accumulates: enough steps off the same anchor and she
is carrying feelings that were never hers to keep."* The card's own **Risk** line names the endpoint:
*"**Identity bleed.** Dreams of others distort his own sense of self; the risk is dissociation, not
destruction."* **The Oblivion-Step is the cheapest of his workings and it feeds the one thing that
is actually killing him.**

**The lens: the *sensus communis* and the Avicennan inner senses.** Avicenna, *Kitāb al-Nafs*
(the *De anima* of the *Shifāʾ*), I.5 and IV, systematised in the Latin West and carried into
Renaissance faculty psychology: between sensation and intellect sit a set of interior faculties
housed in the ventricles of the brain — the *sensus communis* that gathers the five senses into one
presentation, the *imaginatio* that retains the forms, the *vis imaginativa* or *compositiva* that
takes forms apart and recombines them, *aestimatio*, and *memoria*. The doctrine's load-bearing
claim is that the imagination's forms are **really present somewhere**, in a located faculty, and
that in sleep the *vis imaginativa* is unsupervised by reason and therefore runs freely on its own
store. **A dream, on this account, is a place.** It has an address in a faculty and it holds forms
that are genuinely there to be visited. That is what Oblivion-Step needs and nothing modern
supplies: the dream-shadow of a startled sentry is a **location**, and the step arrives at it.

**The productive misreading.** A school reading the step through the inner senses holds that the
dream is a chamber and that a chamber may be entered, so a practitioner who steps often enough is
simply a frequent visitor and takes nothing with him — *"the anchor feels nothing at the moment of
the crossing; their dream-shadow is used, not entered"*, and the page's own Design Chain says so.
Such a school teaches the step as clean and trains its practitioners to use the same anchor
repeatedly, because a familiar chamber is an easy one. **What kills them is the accumulation the
page does put on the record:** *"enough steps off the same anchor and she is carrying feelings that
were never hers to keep"*, and his card's Attraction Layer is Drifting for exactly that reason. A
chamber cannot contaminate a visitor. An Attraction Layer built on other people's fears can, and
does, and the man it is happening to has a relic at his throat to slow it down.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Ren]` **Passage** · Root · Auren as the governing
form — the boundary the working sets is *where a trajectory is permitted to re-enter* — with
`[Ps]` **Passage** · Synonym · Auren as the attested second form for the same meaning, and
`[Rv]` **Reverie** · Extension · Maelor for the addressing scheme, since the arrival coordinate is
a dream-shadow and not a place. `[Rt]` **Return** · Synonym · Auren is the alternative worth
arguing and would read the step as a reversion along a trace rather than a passage to a new point;
the page's *"no line of travel between the two points"* argues against it. All from the thirty
unclassified forms in
`wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`.
*Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling.*

**What boundary moves.** **Two, and they are set by two different currents, which is why this is a
Triadic working and the page's Category says so.** Hypnather's boundary is *when* the Crystal is
permitted to relax — pinned to now rather than to genuine rest. Oneirion's boundary is *where a
trajectory may re-enter* — pinned to the nearest dream-shadow rather than to the continuation of
his own motion. Letheveil's is not a boundary on the crossing at all; it is a boundary on the
record of it. **No law is opposed anywhere.** The Index's rule holds: the glyph moves a limit and
*"the law does what it has always done on the far side of the new limit."*

**What the law then does on its own.** All of it, and every line on the page falls out of one of
the three registers.

The step needs a live subconscious in range, because a trajectory re-entering the Physical Plane
needs a trace to re-enter along and *"the trace a sleeping or startled mind casts into that space"*
is the only one on offer. He cannot step to empty ground for the same reason. The crossing takes a
quarter of a second, and — per the acoustic arithmetic above — **that quarter second is what makes
it silent**, which is why the page's *"no sound"* and its *"quarter-second of disorientation"* are
one fact rather than two. The lag cannot be trained away because it is not a nerve response. The
anchor feels nothing, because a trace being read is not a trace being entered. Aurevane or
Luminalis kills the step, because both raise the local signal — Aurevane *"raises both figures at
once"* on resolution and coherence, Luminalis *"extends perception past the visible band"* — and a
dream-shadow is a low-amplitude trace that has to read against a background. And the backwash
accumulates, because Letheveil is a lossy codec run repeatedly on the same material and
**generation loss compounds.**

**The Four Glyph Roles, and this working's Sealing is unusually clean**
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). Authorization: his Stage and his harmonisation.
Boundary: fifty metres and one dream-shadow. Direction: re-entry at that coordinate. **Sealing:
the arrival itself, which closes the operation in a quarter of a second** — and because the
duration is a quarter second rather than a sustained field, this is one of the few workings in the
batch where *"Sealing failing produces a working that does not stop"* has no long tail. The failure
is over before it can propagate. That is a real and under-appreciated property.

**Law V check, and it closes on the Stage exactly.** Stage VII **is** Refraction, the gate itself:
*"Below Stage VII, Refraction, a working requires voice, hand, ink or blood … At Refraction the
practitioner can hold the Crystal as an object of study, and the Glyph Roles compress into an
internal chain that needs no material anchor at all."* The page's Trigger is a choice and a
permission — *"lets her own Crystal drop toward sleep on command"* — with no voice, hand, ink or
blood anywhere in it, **and Stage VII is the first Stage at which that is legal.** The working sits
on the gate and not above it, which means it is the cheapest possible internalised working and also
the most fragile: he has just cleared the line, not put distance between himself and it. His card
confirms the Catalyst: *"Catalyst Event · Stage VII, Refraction. First Domain seed activation,
however fragile."*

**Failure mode — Boundary fault, and Oneirion names it.** *"The condition was established
imprecisely and the law ran somewhere unintended"* (`Two Sets of Books` §VII), which for this
working is **projection error**: *"The trajectory was correct in the manifold and lands nowhere in
particular when it is mapped back down into a room."* A mobility working that can deposit its user
nowhere in particular is the most frightening failure in this batch and the page does not carry it.
The **Structural fault** runs underneath as the slow one: Hypnather's interrupted relaxation,
accumulated, is *"the Crystal could not hold what the boundary permitted"* arriving over years
rather than in a scene.

**What the target sees and feels.** Nothing, if the target is the anchor: *"The anchor feels nothing
at the moment of the crossing; their dream-shadow is used, not entered, and it re-settles behind
her."* Somebody whose attention had slipped for a moment goes on having slipped, and a man they did
not know was interested in them has just used the shape of that slip as a doorstep.

For anyone watching, *"she vanish[es] and reappear[s] at the second point with no transit between"*,
and at a quarter of a second the arrival is a soft thump rather than a bang — 76 dB at a metre,
which is a chair moving. **Then there is a quarter of a second in which he is standing there and
cannot act**, and that quarter second is the whole of the technique's honesty. At A-Grade his own
reaction period is 0.2 to 1 millisecond, so the lag is **two hundred and fifty to twelve hundred and
fifty of his own reaction periods**, and an A-Grade opponent gets the same. *"A strike already in
flight toward her old position resolves against empty air"* — true, and the page's own Weakness
supplies the correction: *"A strike already fast enough to correct mid-flight can still be aimed at
where the arrival will be rather than where she was."* An A-Grade strike travels 429 to 2,144 metres
in a quarter second and the step's whole range is fifty. **Anything already moving can be at the
arrival point several times over before he can move.**

And afterwards, not in the scene and not on any tally he keeps, somebody else's feelings arrive in
him and stay.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** η **≈0.65** (card), against a Tier 5 · Expert band of
**0.60–0.70** (Part Nineteen as corrected by **R44-4**) — **mid-band, and the `SA-NUM-ETA-TIER`
fault this account logged no longer reaches him.** At 0.65,
**thirty-five percent of every expenditure leaves as heat, sound and structural bleed**, and on a
working whose entire concealment argument is acoustic, a 35 % bleed fraction routed partly into
sound is a genuine internal tension: *"a low-efficiency practitioner is loud, warm, and easy to
find, and … the highest Bands run quiet."* He is not a high Band. His card's lettered
*"Coherence Band C"* is retired under R42 (`SA-UNATT-COHERENCE-BAND`).

**The fairness check** (`.claude/skills/wotr-write/references/fair-play.md`).

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Break the body — the quarter second, and this is the counter the page under-sells.** *"Shot.
  Chemically cheap, mechanically simple … A volley is a volley."* A volley fired at the arrival
  point during a 250 ms lag arrives while he is still not able to act; the page's own Weakness
  concedes the principle and the arithmetic makes it lethal. The doctrine that goes with it is
  **"Numbers, correctly used"** — *"Twenty men who have been drilled to spread, to make him choose,
  and to keep a line of fire open at all times will take casualties and kill him."* **Spread is
  also what denies him anchors**, because every drowsy man in a tight formation is a doorstep and
  a spread line has fewer of them in any one place.
- **Deny the field — take the anchors away, which means take the people away.** The page's own
  Limitation is that he cannot step to empty ground. *"Choose the ground by Family"*: Limina is
  suppressed by *"Saturated Aether, crowd density, active Domains"*, so a busy, bright, saturated
  place is bad ground for him **and** is the only kind of place with plenty of anchors. The genuinely
  hostile ground is **open, bright, and thinly held by men who are awake** — and *"Terrain is not
  flavour. It is a modifier on η."* Aurevane and Luminalis are the working-level version of the
  same move, and the page names both.
- **Break the boundary — the ward, and the substrate.** *"A Runecraft ward built specifically to
  erase a dream-shadow removes Oneirion's landing point before the crossing resolves."* The register
  then tells you how to beat the ward and, by the same logic, how the ward is made vulnerable:
  *"Wards. Runecraft standing on a substrate, and therefore beaten the way Runecraft is beaten:
  damage the substrate."* A defender relying on dream-shadow wards has a stone that can be
  chiselled. **And `[Lk]` Lock is the glyph-level form of the same idea** — *"occupies a limit so
  no other limit can be set there"* — which is exactly what a ward erasing the landing point does.
- **Break the boundary — not the chain, and know why.** Stage VII is Refraction, so *"Above
  Refraction the chain is internal and none of this applies, which is the practical reason Stage VII
  is the line every soldier is taught to fear."* **He is the textbook case of that sentence**: he
  sits on the gate, not above it, and a soldier who tries for the wrist has read the wrong page.
- **Break the man — Rigidity, and it is the cruellest fit in the batch.** *"A Trait's law is not
  negotiable by its bearer, and the more absolute it is the less he can contradict it. Present him
  with the situation his own law handles worst and he will handle it that way regardless."* His card
  gives **Sleepfall Presence** as a *passive* wave he does not choose to emit. **He cannot stop
  making anchors.** A commander who understands that stops trying to deny him doorsteps and starts
  putting the doorsteps where he wants him to arrive.
- **Break the man — Coherence drift, and wait.** His card's Attraction Layer is **Drifting**, his
  Risk is **Identity bleed**, and the working feeds both. *"You do not have to cause this. You have
  to notice it and wait."*

**The tell, stated plainly.** Not the step. **The drowsiness.** *"A soft pressure of sleep. Shadows
shimmer faintly; nearby minds recall forgotten memories involuntarily"*, and Sleepfall Presence runs
whether he wants it to or not. If the room is getting heavy and people are remembering things they
had put away, someone in it is about to be a door. The arrival itself is a thump at seventy-odd
decibels and a quarter-second of a man standing very still.

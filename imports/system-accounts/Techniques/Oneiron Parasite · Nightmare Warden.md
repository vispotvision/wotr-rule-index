# System account · Oneiron Parasite · Nightmare Warden

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** A small piece of one man's soul is planted inside another
person's chest, where it takes hold, grows on the host's own rest, watches what the host feels,
reports it back over thirty metres, and can be made to come apart on command.

**The real phenomenon.** **Heterogeneous nucleation on a foreign template inside a supersaturated
medium, followed by competitive coarsening** — and the working's two hardest limits are the two
hardest limits of that process, which is a better fit than the page claims for itself.

**(a) Why the fragment survives arrival.** Eidolyn's stated law is templated nucleation, and
classical nucleation theory gives the numbers. A new phase forming in a supersaturated medium must
cross a free-energy barrier **ΔG\* = 16πγ³/(3Δg²)**, and below the **critical radius r\* = 2γ/Δg**
a nucleus is thermodynamically unstable and redissolves. **A template collapses the barrier.**
Heterogeneous nucleation on a substrate multiplies ΔG\* by a shape factor
f(θ) = (2 − 3cosθ + cos³θ)/4; for a contact angle of 30° that is **f ≈ 0.013**, a reduction of
roughly **99 %**. This is why anything crystallises on a scratch, a dust grain or a seed rather
than in clean bulk.

**So *"Eidolyn gives the planted fragment enough Ardency Density to hold a shape and survive inside
another Crystal's territory rather than dissolving on arrival"* is the critical-radius condition,
named correctly.** A fragment below r\* dissolves; one above it grows. The page has stated a
nucleation threshold and called it a density requirement, which is the same statement.

**(b) Why the host shapes what grows.** **Ostwald ripening.** In a two-population system the larger
particles grow at the expense of the smaller, because a small particle's higher curvature raises its
local solubility (Gibbs–Thomson); the population coarsens and the mean radius goes as t^(1/3) under
diffusion control (Lifshitz–Slyozov–Wagner). **A Warden is a second nucleus inside a host Crystal
that is itself a structure**, and coarsening does not ask whose nucleus is whose. Whichever structure
is larger and better ordered takes material from the other.

**That is the page's Limitation, derived rather than asserted:** *"The host's own emotional state
shapes what the Warden becomes, so Aeldoris does not fully control what he has planted."* It is also
why a strong Crystal rejects it outright, and why leaving one in too long is dangerous to him — the
page's **Personality Imprint** is coarsening running the wrong way.

**(c) Why it grows on sleep.** Hypnather's stated law is structural relaxation, which proceeds *"only
while quiescent"*; the relaxation follows a **Kohlrausch–Williams–Watts** stretched exponential
exp[−(t/τ)^β] with β ≈ 0.3–0.7, so a long undisturbed interval buys disproportionately more
ordering than several short ones. **A night is worth more than six naps**, and the page says
*"it grows stronger the longer the host sleeps near it undisturbed"*, which is the same arithmetic.

**(d) Where a monitor of feeling would actually be placed, and the page gets this right by
accident.** *"Behind a target's ribs."* The posterior mediastinum, at the base of the heart and just
anterior to the tracheal bifurcation, holds the **cardiac plexus** and the pulmonary plexus, and
roughly **eighty percent of vagal fibres are afferent** — carrying interoceptive traffic *up* from
the viscera rather than commands down. The visceral component of emotion is read from exactly there:
heart-rate variability (RMSSD, high-frequency power) is a standard, quantitative readout of
autonomic and affective state, and the interoceptive pathway is the physiological substrate the
James–Lange tradition was pointing at. **If you wanted one location in a body from which to read
what somebody is feeling, that is the location**, and the page put it there.

**(e) The report channel is nearly free.** The Warden has to send. Core affect is low-dimensional —
valence and arousal, plus a small set of discrete categories — so a usable emotional report is a
handful of bits: **of order 10 bit/s**, against a Shannon capacity C = B log₂(1 + S/N) that any
carrier at all would exceed. **Surveillance is cheap. The expensive part is the graft.**

**The governing law, and the page's worst problem.** Part Eleven's **Aether-Physics Fusion Rule**:
*"magical combat must obey physical law and Aetheric law simultaneously … The Continuum measures the
energy that arrived. It does not ask where the energy came from."* And the page says the Warden can
be **detonated**, and bounds the detonation *"by Stage VII's 475 ceiling, Grade A"*. Run that
through the system's own table:

- **A-Grade Strike Force** (Part Eleven): peak force **20–100 GN**, energy yield
  **46 GJ – 4.184 TJ**, **11 – 1,000 tonnes of TNT.**
- Eleven tonnes of TNT released inside a thorax destroys the target, everything within tens of
  metres, and — at a thirty-metre implantation range — **Aeldoris.**
- The stated effect is *"a psychic stagger"* and *"loses composure at the worst possible moment."*

**The gap is not a rounding error. It is eleven tonnes of TNT against a stumble**, and the page
invites the comparison itself by quoting the Grade. Separately, **475 is not A-Grade.** Part Four
puts A at 276–400 and S at 401–550; Stage VII's 475 is the top of the *instability band*, reachable
*"under strain only, never by allocation"* (Part Five), and it sits inside S. The page's single
clause *"Stage VII's 475 ceiling, Grade A"* names two different Grades. Both logged.

**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Implantation range: **30 m** (page).
- Duration of a sustained watch: **≈20 s** (page, converted from four turns at 5 s).
- Nucleation barrier reduction on a template: **f(30°) ≈ 0.013**, i.e. **≈99 %.**
- Critical radius: **r\* = 2γ/Δg** — below it the graft redissolves.
- Coarsening: mean radius ∝ **t^(1/3)** (LSW, diffusion-controlled).
- Relaxation: **KWW β ≈ 0.3–0.7** — long quiescent intervals buy disproportionate ordering.
- Interoceptive substrate: **cardiac plexus** at the base of the heart; **~80 %** of vagal fibres
  afferent; HRV as the quantitative readout.
- Report bandwidth needed: **of order 10 bit/s.**
- A-Grade Strike Force band: **20–100 GN · 46 GJ – 4.184 TJ · 11–1,000 t TNT** (Part Eleven).
- Grade brackets: **A = 276–400, S = 401–550** (Part Four). **475 is S.**

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** For everything except the detonation, in the
host — a graft that grows on somebody else's rest is metabolically parasitic and thermodynamically
tiny, and a 10 bit/s channel is microwatts. **The whole working is nearly free until the last
instruction**, at which point the page hands it a band running to a thousand tonnes of TNT and
describes the result as a loss of composure. The account states both figures and does not pick one.

**Precedent we are not copying.** The implanted watcher is a well-worn shape: Naruto's cursed seal,
the Gestalt of *NieR*, Geass, the Tyranid synapse, every parasite-symbiote in every Metroidvania.
Those give the implant an agenda. This one has **no agenda at all** — it is a nucleus in a
supersaturated medium, and what it becomes is decided by coarsening against whatever the host
already is. The divergence is that the operator loses control not through betrayal but through
**thermodynamics**, and the page states that as its Limitation rather than as a twist.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** Small draw, long tail. The implantation is one act at up to 30 m; after that the
Warden is fed by the **host's** rest cycles and not by Aeldoris's reserve, which makes this the only
working in the batch whose running cost is charged to the target. Limina is favoured by *"Darkness,
silence, Aether-thin ground, ruins"* and suppressed by *"Saturated Aether, crowd density, active
Domains"* (Families page, Environmental Coupling), and *"Terrain is not flavour. It is a modifier on
η."* **The terrain that matters here is the terrain the host sleeps in**, not the terrain of the
implantation, and the page does not connect those.

**Residue:** in a person, and standing. This is the batch's clearest case of a residue that is not
in a room. *"Aetheric Residue · Traces left behind by an Essence working. A permanent, if often
subtle, change to the location where the working occurred. **Always readable by someone patient
enough**"* (`Core Vocabulary` §II) — and the location here is a body. **A host carrying a Warden is
carrying a readable trace**, which is the physical basis of the page's own Counterplay
(*"A shared-dream ritual exposes its presence to anyone else inside the dream"*) and the reason
this is a poor technique to use on anyone who will later be examined by a competent physician.
`The Physical Account: Two Sets of Books` §V is unusually relevant: *"A practitioner's history can
be taken off their living body by a competent physician and off their dead one by a competent
renderer, and the second reading is better."*

**Saturation:** not a limit. One graft.

**Wellspring stratum.** **Oneirion · *The Wellspring of Dream*** (primary), Family **Limina**,
Physics Domain **Entropy, Void and Mind**. Its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Limina — Entropy, Void and Mind.md`:

> "A mechanical system's every possible arrangement is a coordinate in a space that has no physical
> location, and the system's evolution is a trajectory through it. Oneirion lets a working act on
> that manifold rather than on the room. This is the mechanical reason Oneirion techniques reach
> targets no line of sight connects, and the reason they are so difficult to describe afterward."
>
> **Failure** · "Projection error. The trajectory was correct in the manifold and lands nowhere in
> particular when it is mapped back down into a room."

**Eidolyn · *The Shaping Mind*** (secondary), same Family:

> "A supersaturated medium will not organise itself, but given a template it deposits along the
> template's geometry with high fidelity and considerable strength. Eidolyn makes intent the
> template. The construct's solidity is real material behaviour rather than illusion, and its
> failure modes are the failure modes of a poorly designed casting."
>
> **Failure** · "Template defect. Any flaw in the intent is faithfully reproduced at scale, and the
> practitioner is rarely able to see the flaw until it is standing in front of them."

**Hypnather · *The Sleep Crown*** (tertiary), same Family — *"the reorganisation happens during
genuine rest and **cannot be forced**"*, failure *"Interrupted relaxation."* Note that this page
does **not** force it: the Warden *"anchors it to the target's own rest cycles"* and waits, which is
the register-compliant use and the opposite of what `Oblivion-Step` and `Hypnather Descent Rite` do
with the same current. **Worth saying plainly: of the Aeldoris pages, this is the one that uses
Hypnather correctly.**

Three Limina currents, so same-Family harmonisation, which *"compounds"* (Families page). And
**Eidolyn's failure line is the page's real horror and it is not on the page:** *"Any flaw in the
intent is faithfully reproduced at scale, and the practitioner is rarely able to see the flaw until
it is standing in front of them."* A template made of his intent, deposited inside somebody else,
growing on their sleep, reproducing his flaws at scale — and his card's own **Risk** is
*"Identity bleed. Dreams of others distort his own sense of self."*

*Four Theories.* **Consent Doctrine** for the nucleation and the coarsening. But the page's Cost
leans hard on the **Debt Position** — *"the Wellspring answers on credit and the account is settled
somewhere the practitioner cannot see"* — because **Dream Wound** returns the pain to him when the
Warden dies, and **Personality Imprint** settles the account in a currency he cannot audit. The
Residue Position also has a claim, given that the thing left behind is a standing structure in
somebody else. The account names the leans; the Accord enforces Consent.

**Essence stratum.** All three layers, and the Core most unusually. **Essence Core**, because the
thing planted is *"a small detached fragment of Aeldoris's own Essence Core"* and the Core's stated
failure state is *"Fracture into Shards"* (`Core Vocabulary` §III) — **so the system already has a
word for a detached piece of an Essence Core, and it is not a good word.** Whether a Warden is a
Shard, or a deliberate and reversible version of one, the system does not say. **Attraction Layer**,
because the live line back to him is what keeps it alive under the Animatria rule. **Aether Shell**
for the shape it holds. Developmental Tier at Stage VII: **Resonant** (Part Twenty-One). Crystal
State: **Refined, residue-heavy** (card). **The invoice:** *"Destroying the Warden returns psychic
pain to Aeldoris (Dream Wound); a Warden left implanted too long risks adopting its host's
psychological traits and reporting them back distorted (Personality Imprint)."* **Both costs are
the same physics seen from two ends:** kill the nucleus and the operator feels it because it was
his material; leave it and coarsening makes it the host's.

**The lens: van Helmont's *archeus* and the *idea morbosa*.** Jan Baptist van Helmont, *Ortus
medicinae* (posthumous, 1648). Van Helmont held that every living body is governed not by the
Galenic humours but by an **archeus**, a seated vital artificer that directs the body's *blas* and
holds the plan of the organism; the chief *archeus influus* he located in the **stomach and the
region of the pylorus** — behind the ribs, at the centre of the trunk. Disease, on his account, is
not an imbalance of fluids but a **disorder of the archeus**, and the sharpest form of it is the
**idea morbosa**: a foreign idea impressed upon the archeus, which then dutifully makes the body
enact it. The archeus does not resist the impression. It executes it, because executing what is
impressed on it is the only thing it does.

**That is the Nightmare Warden, seventeenth-century.** A foreign thing lodged in the governing
faculty behind the ribs, which thereafter makes the body produce nightmares, spikes and staggers
that the body itself would never have produced. The page's own Target Response — *"Emotional spikes
monitored, micro-nightmares induced, the host's own Soul Crystal layering weakened from inside"* —
is a Helmontian disease description with a Soul Crystal substituted for the archeus.

**The productive misreading.** A school reading the Warden as an *idea morbosa* treats it as a
**disease**, and treats it the way van Helmont treated disease: with purgation, regimen, a specific
remedy, and the reordering of the patient's own archeus. The page half-endorses them —
*"A memory-cleansing rite purges it outright"* — so they will get results often enough to keep
believing. **What kills them is the line.** A disease has no author standing thirty metres away.
The page's own Animatria rule is explicit: *"severing the live line back to Aeldoris (by Silence, by
killing him, or by breaking his focus) ends the Warden immediately."* A physician's school will
purge a patient for a fortnight while the man who planted it goes on reading their heart rate, and
the correct treatment was never medical: it was to find him, or to make the room quiet.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Frm]` **Form** · Extension · Thalen as the governing
form — the boundary the working sets is *this fragment is permitted to hold a shape here*, which is
the nucleation threshold — laid across `[Ren]` **Passage** · Root · Auren for the crossing into the
host's manifold and `[Vel]` **Mind** · Extension · Iesara for the addressing. `[Lei]` **Binding** is
the form a reader will reach for and the Index says its *"attested spread"* is *"two Wellsprings and
one Family. Narrow, deep, and the whole of it belongs to Limina"* — but Binding is not among the
thirty the repo mirror carries, so it cannot be cited from the register here
(`SA-GAP-GLYPH-MIRROR`), and its worked-example line in the Index is the only attestation available.
All proposals from the thirty unclassified forms in
`wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`.
*Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling.*

**What boundary moves.** **The nucleation threshold inside another Crystal**, and nothing else. Pin
the boundary so that a fragment of foreign material counts as a valid template inside the host's
supersaturated medium, and the ordinary law does the rest: deposition proceeds along the template's
geometry, the nucleus grows past its critical radius instead of dissolving, and coarsening begins
between two structures that are now in the same system. **No law is opposed.** The Index's rule
holds — *"It moves a limit, and the law does what it has always done on the far side of the new
limit."*

**What the law then does on its own.** Almost all of it.

It cannot take in a being with no subconscious, because Oneirion needs a manifold to route through
and *"cannot infect a being with no subconscious to root in."* It is refused by a strong mental
shield, because a shield raises the barrier back up and a nucleus below critical radius redissolves.
It grows on the host's undisturbed sleep, because structural relaxation runs only while quiescent and
a stretched exponential rewards long intervals. It becomes what the host is, because coarsening does
not consult the operator. It returns pain to him when it dies, because it was his material. And
Mortalis dissolves it, because Mortalis *"lets the practitioner engage that boundary as a described
physical transition"* and an uninvited structure held far from equilibrium by somebody else's
expenditure is precisely a thing with a boundary to engage.

**What the law does not do on its own — the detonation.** Nothing in the Mechanism produces one. The
three named laws give **shape**, **mobility** and **a growth schedule**. There is no combustion, no
stored elastic energy, no phase transition, no oxidiser, no discharge. *"A psychic stagger on
detonation"* and *"can be made to detonate"* are in the Effect, the Function, the Numerical Effect
and the Target Response, and the Mechanism paragraph is silent. **Under Isaac's ruling that the
mechanism is the effect, that silence is the conflict.** Logged.

**The Four Glyph Roles, and the Sealing role is the whole technique**
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). Authorization: his Stage and the line back to him.
Boundary: thirty metres for the planting, then the host's own body. Direction: watch, distort,
detonate. **Sealing: absent by design** — the Warden is *meant* to outlive the casting moment, and
the Craft page says what that means: *"Sealing failing produces a working that does not stop, because
the law goes on obeying itself after the operator's attention has left the room."* **The Nightmare
Warden is a Sealing failure that somebody decided to keep.** The Animatria rule is the only thing
standing in for a Seal, which is why severing the line is a total answer.

**Law V check, and it closes on the Stage.** Stage VII is Refraction: *"At Refraction the
practitioner can hold the Crystal as an object of study, and the Glyph Roles compress into an
internal chain that needs no material anchor at all."* The page's Trigger is *"Declared, by gaze or
touch"* — gaze needs no anchor and touch is a hand, so the working is legal either side of the gate,
which is unusually robust. Worth noting: *"by gaze"* makes this one of the few Aeldoris workings that
does not need him within arm's reach, and thirty metres is well inside a bow's range and well outside
a blade's.

**Failure mode — Boundary fault, with Eidolyn's template defect as the mechanism.** *"The condition
was established imprecisely and the law ran somewhere unintended. Burns, structural collapse in the
wrong wall, **a working that continued after the practitioner stopped**"*
(`Two Sets of Books` §VII). The unintended place is the host's own architecture, and the
*"working that continued after the practitioner stopped"* clause is the Warden's whole design. The
second fault is **Coherence**, arriving slowly: a man whose surveillance apparatus is made of his
own Core and keeps returning other people's dispositions to him is a man whose lived behaviour and
stated law can be expected to drift, and his card's Attraction Layer is already **Drifting**.

**What the target sees and feels.** At the moment of planting, a look or a touch, and then nothing
they could name. What follows is not an attack and does not read as one. Sleep stops being restful
in a way that is hard to describe — *"micro-nightmares induced"* — and then worse, because the thing
is strongest after the nights that felt deepest. Composure becomes unreliable in a pattern the host
cannot trace to anything: an emotional spike at the wrong moment, a decision made for reasons that
turn out not to be theirs. **And a plan they made in their own head is already known**, because
*"a watched target cannot act on a plan the Warden has already reported"* — which is the most
frightening line on the page and the cheapest thing the working does, at about ten bits a second.
The host's Soul Crystal layering is *"weakened from inside"*, which is coarsening: a second structure
taking material from the first.

Then, when he chooses, it comes apart, and the host *"loses composure at the worst possible
moment."* **What that costs the host in joules the page does not say and the Grade it quotes says
eleven to a thousand tonnes of TNT**, so a reader is entitled to ask which of those two sentences is
the technique.

And what nobody in the room can see is that the thing has been becoming them. *"A Warden left
implanted too long risks adopting its host's psychological traits and reporting them back
distorted"*, and *"whether a Warden left in long enough ever fully comes back, or whether some part
of it stays behind"* is the page's own closing question. **On the coarsening it is not a question.
Material moves to the larger, better-ordered structure, and in a healthy host that is the host.**

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** η **≈0.65** (card), against the Tier 5 · Expert band of
**0.50–0.60** (Part Nineteen) — above band, already logged (`SA-NUM-ETA-TIER`). **Thirty-five
percent of the implantation's expenditure leaves as heat, sound and structural bleed.** But the
running cost after implantation is charged to the host's rest, so **the bleed on the sustain is the
host's, not his**, and the system has no rule for whose η applies to a working fed by somebody else's
recovery. That is a gap worth naming and it is the one genuinely novel accounting question on the
page.

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Deny the field — Silence, and it is total.** The page's own Animatria clause says so and the
  register says it in general terms: *"Animatria constructs fail completely because their continuity
  runs back to a source the Silence has severed."* And the register's field diagnostic is the detail
  worth knowing: *"A soul held in one reports the sensation as loneliness rather than weakness."*
  **A host who suddenly feels lonely inside a quiet place has just had the thing in their chest go
  dark.** That is a findable, physical, in-world test, and nobody wrote it for this page.
- **Break the boundary — cut the line, by any of the three routes the page itself lists.** Silence,
  killing him, or breaking his focus. **Breaking his focus is the cheap one**, because a watch is
  sustained and he is holding a 20 s window while also being a man in a fight.
- **Break the body — distance, and the number is on the page.** Thirty metres. *"Most practitioners
  are a threat about closing range and are written as though they were not."* Beyond thirty metres he
  cannot plant one at all, and **"Shot"** reaches a great deal further than thirty metres.
- **Break the man — Read him, and the pattern is the graft.** *"Every thaumaturge has a pattern,
  because single-axis fluency produces one, and a pattern learned is a pattern interruptible."* His
  card gives five harmonisations and *"Fifty-five of the Sixty remain untouched"* — that is
  single-axis fluency stated as a number. A commander who knows he plants before he engages will
  screen for it before the engagement rather than during.
- **Break the man — Rigidity, and turn the graft round.** *"Present him with the situation his own
  law handles worst and he will handle it that way regardless."* His law handles a **strong,
  integrated, self-honest host** by coarsening the Warden into that host. Give him a volunteer.
  A disciplined soldier who accepts an implantation and then simply is themselves converts his
  surveillance apparatus into a source of distorted reports about a person he cannot read — and the
  page's Cost says the reports come back *distorted*, not false, which is worse. **The counter to a
  spy that becomes its host is a host worth becoming.**
- **Break the man — the vow, and the second-order move.** *"An opponent who knows the terms of a vow
  holds a weapon that does not require touching him."* His card is specific about what he takes:
  *"He accepts only cases involving self-deception or corrupted conscience"*, and
  *"Anyone without guilt · The one target he has no working against."* A host with nothing hidden is
  poor ground for every other working he owns, which narrows the list of people he will plant one in.

**The tell, stated plainly — and it is not on this page.** The working itself has none worth the
name. What the record does supply is on his card: *"Presence · A soft pressure of sleep. Shadows
shimmer faintly; nearby minds recall forgotten memories involuntarily"*, and
**Sleepfall Presence**, *"a passive wave causing micro-sleeps and mental fog in everyone nearby"*,
which runs whether he wills it or not. **If a room went heavy and somebody looked at you, assume
the look was the trigger.** After that, the honest tells are all diagnostic rather than
perceptual: sleep that stops restoring, composure that fails on a schedule, and — inside a Silence —
loneliness where the thing used to be.

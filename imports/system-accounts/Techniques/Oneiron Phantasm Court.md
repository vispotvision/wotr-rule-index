# System account · Oneiron Phantasm Court

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** A standing figure appears behind somebody, made out of that
person's own material, and says the thing they have been declining to say. It can be raised at up
to a hundred and forty metres with nothing in the way and nothing in line of sight.

**The real phenomenon.** **An extrapolation to an equilibrium state that the system never actually
reaches, made to stand up** — and the physics of that extrapolation is famously unsettled, which is
the most interesting thing about this page.

**(a) The Kauzmann problem, which is the whole technique.** Hypnather's law is structural
relaxation, and the page's central operation is to locate *"which arrangement a Crystal relaxes
toward when genuinely at rest, its least-defended configuration"* — **without waiting for the
target to rest.** That is not a metaphor for anything. It is the standard, contested, hundred-year-old
extrapolation problem in glass physics.

Cool a supercooled liquid and its excess entropy over the crystal falls. Extrapolate that fall and
it reaches zero at a finite temperature, the **Kauzmann temperature T_K** — below which the liquid
would have *less* entropy than the crystal, which is the **Kauzmann paradox** (1948). The usual
resolutions posit an **ideal glass state** reached at T_K, or a transition that intervenes, or that
the extrapolation is simply invalid because the relaxation time diverges first (**Vogel–Fulcher–
Tammann**, τ → ∞) and the state is therefore **unreachable in any finite time.** The configuration
can be calculated. Whether it *exists* is open.

**So the Phantasm Court raises a configuration that is defined by an extrapolation and may never be
occupied by anything.** The page's own claim is stronger than it realises: *"It is the coordinate
their own Crystal would settle to, made to stand up and talk."* **Would settle to, given infinite
time, which nothing has.** And that is a real answer to the page's closing question — *"Why the
confession, once mirrored back at Aeldoris himself, can destabilise him exactly as hard as it
destabilises the target"* — because an extrapolation has to be performed by somebody, and the
person performing it is contributing the part that is not measured.

**(b) Why it reads as confession rather than as an accusation.** Real cognitive psychology, and the
page's sentence is almost a textbook statement of it: *"which reads as confession because it is drawn
from material the target already possesses."* **Reality monitoring** (Johnson and Raye) is the
process by which people distinguish internally generated from externally perceived memories, using
heuristics — sensory detail, contextual information, records of cognitive operations — that fail in
predictable ways. **Source-monitoring failure** is why self-generated material can be experienced as
externally testified. The **self-generation effect** makes self-produced material more memorable and
more credible than presented material. And **cognitive dissonance** (Festinger, 1957) gives the
damage its form: dissonance magnitude scales with the number and importance of dissonant cognitions
against consonant ones, the drive to reduce it is real and measurable, and the reduction is often
achieved by changing the belief rather than the conduct. **A figure that states your own withheld
material back to you in your own voice is the maximum-dissonance configuration**, and the page's
Consequence — *"the exposure persists after the figure dissolves"* — is dissonance reduction still
running after the stimulus is gone.

**(c) What the figure can and cannot be made of, and the page is right not to say.** Eidolyn's law
is templated nucleation, and a deposited figure would need material. Run the arithmetic: a
human-sized body is **≈70 kg**, and 70 kg of air occupies **≈58 m³**. Assembling a standing figure
out of the room would mean stripping fifty-eight cubic metres of atmosphere and converting gas to
condensed matter, which is not a phase transition and not available at any energy. **So the Court's
figure is not matter, and the page never claims it is:** its Category is *"projection of consciousness
into a structured avatar"*, its Function is *"Psychic offense and Essence destabilisation"*, and
nothing on the page gives the figure a blow. What it does claim is *"a standing, load-bearing
presence rather than a flicker"*, and load-bearing in the force sense is cheap — a human push is of
order **500 N**, a hand's contact pressure of order **50 kPa** — and needs no mass at all if the
medium being deposited into is Aether rather than air. **Compare the card, which is the page that
does make the mass claim:** *"Creates avatars shaped from the opponent's fears, which **gain mass and
force** if the target believes them real."* The Court does not inherit that and is better for it.

**(d) Speed of assembly.** If the figure stands within about a second, the deposition front runs at
**≥2 m/s**. That is high but not unphysical: dendritic growth velocities in the **hypercooled**
regime reach tens of metres per second at large undercooling. Eidolyn's supersaturated medium is
being asked for the fast end of a real range, not for an exception.

**The governing law, and the page's best line.** Oneirion's manifold, and the same strict objection
that stands on `Oblivion-Step` applies in a much weaker form here, because **nothing is being
displaced.** A configuration is being *located* and a structure deposited *in the room*, not moved
through it. *"Oneirion treats a soul's every possible arrangement as a coordinate in an abstract
configuration manifold, which is why the working reaches a target no line of sight is required to
hold once the coordinate is found"* — that is a claim about addressing, not about transport, and
addressing in a state space does not require a path in a room. **This page uses Oneirion more
defensibly than the step page does.**

**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Radius: **0 – 140 m** (page). Sphere (4/3)π(140 m)³ = **1.15×10⁷ m³.**
- Duration: *"as long as Aeldoris's own attention holds"*; the page notes an older two-turn figure,
  which at 5 s/turn is **≈10 s.**
- Mass a deposited figure would need: **≈70 kg**, i.e. **≈58 m³** of air fully converted.
  **Not available.** The figure is not matter.
- Force a standing presence needs: **≈500 N**, **≈50 kPa** at a hand-sized contact. Cheap.
- Deposition front velocity for a ~1 s assembly: **≥2 m/s.** Hypercooled dendritic growth reaches
  tens of m/s.
- Relaxation: **VFT divergence**, τ → ∞; **Kauzmann temperature T_K**; the ideal glass state as an
  extrapolation of uncertain existence.
- Prerequisite the Summary card omits: the target must have been *"read closely enough to have
  located the shape of what they are avoiding"* — **a reconnaissance cost, paid before the working.**

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** Small, and almost entirely in the deposition.
There is no yield: no blow, no heat, no blast. The page's Function is destabilisation, and
destabilisation is not an energy transfer — it is **information delivered to a system that then does
work on itself.** Two Sets of Books §III applies exactly: *"The practitioner does not supply the
joules. They supply the boundary condition."* **Here they do not even supply an effect. They supply
a coordinate, and the target's own dissonance does the rest.** That is the most economical working in
the batch, and it is the reason its cost is charged in identity rather than in reserve.

**Precedent we are not copying.** The confession-tribunal is a known move: Persona 5's Palaces and
the confession of the treasure, Evangelion's instrumentality, Bleach's inner hollow, Undertale's
judgment hall, the *Ace Attorney* breakdown. Those all have **an accuser with standing** — a god, a
shadow, a prosecutor. This one has **a coordinate**, and the coordinate is an extrapolation somebody
performed. The divergence is that the Court's authority is exactly as good as the extrapolation, the
physics says the extrapolated state may not exist, and the page's own closing question is the
technique noticing that about itself.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** A 140 m addressing envelope with no line-of-sight requirement, and a very small
deposition. Limina is favoured by *"Darkness, silence, Aether-thin ground, ruins"* and suppressed by
*"Saturated Aether, crowd density, active Domains"* (Families page, Environmental Coupling). *"Terrain
is not flavour. It is a modifier on η."* **And the page's own Weakness names a terrain answer the
Families page does not:** *"A field that strips a region's Aetheric memory record denies Eidolyn any
template at all."* A memory-null region is not one of the four suppressive conditions listed for
Limina, so the page has invented — correctly and usefully — a fifth.

**Residue:** in a person and in a room. In the room, a deposition site. In the person, the part that
matters: *"Confession under this pressure measurably loosens Soul Crystal coherence around whatever
the contradiction concerned, and the exposure persists after the figure dissolves."* **A loosened
Crystal is a residue in a soul**, readable later by anyone competent, and `Two Sets of Books` §V is
blunt about it: *"A practitioner's history can be taken off their living body by a competent physician
and off their dead one by a competent renderer."*

**Saturation:** not a limit; the deposition is small.

**Wellspring stratum.** **Oneirion · *The Wellspring of Dream*** (primary), Family **Limina**. Its
law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Limina — Entropy, Void and Mind.md`:

> "A mechanical system's every possible arrangement is a coordinate in a space that has no physical
> location, and the system's evolution is a trajectory through it. Oneirion lets a working act on
> that manifold rather than on the room. This is the mechanical reason Oneirion techniques reach
> targets no line of sight connects, and the reason they are so difficult to describe afterward."
>
> **Failure** · "Projection error. The trajectory was correct in the manifold and lands nowhere in
> particular when it is mapped back down into a room."

**Eidolyn · *The Shaping Mind*** (secondary), same Family:

> "A supersaturated medium will not organise itself, but given a template it deposits along the
> template's geometry with high fidelity and considerable strength. Eidolyn makes intent the
> template. The construct's solidity is real material behaviour rather than illusion, and its
> failure modes are the failure modes of a poorly designed casting."
>
> **Failure** · "Template defect. **Any flaw in the intent is faithfully reproduced at scale**, and
> the practitioner is rarely able to see the flaw until it is standing in front of them."

**Hypnather · *The Sleep Crown*** (secondary), same Family — *"left undisturbed it continues to relax
toward the ordered state it never reached"*, and *"the reorganisation happens during genuine rest and
cannot be forced."* **This page does not force it.** It locates the coordinate the relaxation is aimed
at and never asks the target to relax, which is a materially different — and register-compliant —
use of the current, and the contrast with `Oblivion-Step` and `Hypnather Descent Rite` is worth
recording as a credit rather than a conflict.

**Eidolyn's failure line is the page's answer to itself, and the page does not take it.** *"Any flaw
in the intent is faithfully reproduced at scale, and the practitioner is rarely able to see the flaw
until it is standing in front of them."* The Court's template is a coordinate **he** located. The
page asks why the verdict destabilises him as hard as the target; the register answers that a
template defect is reproduced faithfully and that the person who made it cannot see it. Combine that
with the Kauzmann problem — the coordinate is an extrapolation and may not be a place — and the
Court's bench is partly built out of the prosecutor.

Three Limina currents, so same-Family harmonisation, which *"compounds"*, and Harmonics **Breadth**
is the Sub-Stat Part Seven writes for the case: *"Stage V to exceed A where several Wellsprings are
layered inside one working."* Not on the page's FOW line.

*Four Theories.* **Correspondence** is the one this page leans on, and heavily:
*"The practitioner and the Wellspring are the same law expressed at two scales, and the working is
the two scales briefly agreeing."* A court that raises the target's own equilibrium self and finds
that raising it costs the caster the same coin is two scales agreeing in the most uncomfortable
possible way. The Accord enforces **Consent** and holds Correspondence *"ecclesiastically
respectable, administratively useless"* — which is a very funny line to put beside a working that
has already produced a formal Arbitration hearing in canon.

**Essence stratum.** All three layers, and the Core is the one that pays. **Aether Shell** for the
deposition; **Attraction Layer** for the addressing, and his card says that layer is **Drifting**,
*"an Attraction Layer built on other people's fears rather than his own bonds"*, which is what this
working builds by running; **Essence Core**, because *"some of what it carries passes through him on
the way out. **Identity Bleed** is that residue lodging in him; **Dream Echo Fatigue** is the
resulting gap in his own short-term memory."* Developmental Tier at Stage VII: **Resonant** (Part
Twenty-One). Crystal State: **Refined, residue-heavy** (card). **The invoice:** somebody else's
material lodged in his Core, plus his own memory going. **The system has no unit for either**
(`SA-GAP-MEMORY-AS-COST`), and the card names the endpoint: *"Risk · **Identity bleed.** Dreams of
others distort his own sense of self; the risk is dissociation, not destruction."*

**The lens: Kant's inner court.** Immanuel Kant, *Die Metaphysik der Sitten*, Doctrine of Virtue
§13, and the *Religion* essay. Kant describes conscience not as a feeling but as **an inner court** —
*ein innerer Gerichtshof* — in which a person is simultaneously accuser, advocate and judge. His
argument for why it has that form is the striking part: a man cannot coherently be prosecutor and
defendant *as the same person*, because a party to a case cannot rule on it, so reason is compelled
to **personify** the judge as an other. The judge in the inner court is therefore *"an ideal person
whom reason creates for itself"*, and the verdict binds because the defendant made the bench out of
their own reason and cannot disown it.

**The Oneiron Phantasm Court is that apparatus, externalised at up to a hundred and forty metres.**
The target's own undefended self is personified and put on its feet, and what it says binds because
the material is theirs. The page's line — *"The court that results is not an illusion of the target.
It is the coordinate their own Crystal would settle to, made to stand up and talk"* — is Kant's
personified judge with a state-space address.

**The productive misreading, and it already exists in canon.** A school reading the Court as Kant's
inner tribunal holds that the verdict is **rational and therefore binding**: what the dream-self
states is what the subject's own reason states, so it is admissible, and a Court's finding is a
finding. Such a school builds a jurisprudence on it — and the record already shows the shape of
that: the card's **Broken Tribunal**, *"Seven judges … Forced to kill their own dream-selves. He did
not lift a hand"*, and an Arbitration matter heard by the Judgment Order at Altherion, on the record,
with *"No sentence … entered."*

**What kills them is who built the bench.** On Kant's own account the inner court's authority comes
from the defendant's own reason and nobody else's. This bench's coordinate is located by **Aeldoris**
and templated by **Eidolyn**, whose failure is that *"any flaw in the intent is faithfully reproduced
at scale"*, and the intent is his. Add the Kauzmann problem and the extrapolated self may not be a
place at all. **A tribunal whose bench is assembled by the prosecutor is not a court**, and a school
that has admitted its findings as evidence has been convicting people on a reading of a curve. The
page's own closing question is that suspicion arriving from the inside: it destabilises him as hard
because part of the verdict is his.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Ora]` **Truth** · Extension · Iesara as the governing
form — the boundary the working sets is *this configuration is permitted to be stated* — laid across
`[Vel]` **Mind** · Extension · Iesara for the addressing and `[Frm]` **Form** · Extension · Thalen
for the deposition. Both `[Ora]` and `[Vel]` are Iesara forms, and the Index notes that where a
glyph is read from two stations *"the path is the intersection rather than either reading alone"* —
which is worth flagging because a Court is exactly an intersection of truth and mind. `[Et]`
**Mirage** · Root · Kaetra is the alternative and would file the Court as an apparition rather than a
statement; the page's own insistence that *"the court that results is not an illusion of the target"*
argues against it, and `[Et]` is already contested elsewhere (`SA-GAP-GLYPH-ET-COLLISION`). All from
the thirty unclassified forms in
`wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`.
*Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling.*

**What boundary moves.** **Whether a configuration a system is *aimed at* may be instantiated
without the system arriving there.** That is the single boundary, and it is the only unusual thing
the working does. Hypnather's law already says where the Crystal relaxes to; Oneirion's already
treats that as a coordinate; Eidolyn's already deposits onto a template. Move the boundary so that
the asymptote counts as an available template, and the three ordinary laws build a person out of it.

**What the law then does on its own.** All of it.

It reaches without line of sight, because the address is in the manifold and not in the room. It
needs the coordinate to exist — *"The manifold coordinate has to exist for Eidolyn to template onto
it"* — so a target with no active subconscious or no gap between waking and equilibrium selves gives
it nothing. It holds as long as his attention holds, because Spellcraft's medium is
*"the ephemeral sentence"* and *"the fist stops burning the moment attention lapses."* It reads as
confession rather than as accusation, because the material is the target's and reality monitoring
cannot reliably tell self-generated from presented. It loosens coherence around the contradiction and
not elsewhere, because dissonance is local to the cognitions in conflict. **And it comes back through
him, because the deposition is drawn through his own Oneirion projection and Eidolyn reproduces the
template's flaws faithfully.**

Verdantia and Mortalis practitioners resist it, and the registers say why better than the page does.
Verdantia gives *"indeterminate growth and continuous ambient conversion"* — a Crystal that is always
growing has no arrested configuration to extrapolate to, because it never stopped arranging itself.
Mortalis *"lets the practitioner engage that boundary as a described physical transition rather than
as a catastrophe"* — a soul that has already looked at its own ending has less residue between its
waking and its equilibrium selves. **Both are the same objection: no gap, no coordinate.** The page
says *"their potency reading against this technique runs low for exactly that reason"*, and it is
right.

**The Four Glyph Roles** (`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). Authorization: his Stage, and
the prior reading of the target, which is the reconnaissance the Summary card does not mention.
Boundary: 140 m and one coordinate. Direction: state the contradiction. **Sealing: his attention,
which is also the duration** — and that is the tightest Seal available, because a working whose
duration *is* the Seal cannot outlive it. *"Sealing failing produces a working that does not stop"*
has no purchase here. **The Court is the safest-sealed working in the batch and the least safe for
its operator**, which is an unusual and good combination.

**Law V check, and it closes on the Stage.** Stage VII is Refraction: *"At Refraction the practitioner
can hold the Crystal as an object of study, and the Glyph Roles compress into an internal chain that
needs no material anchor at all."* The page's Trigger is *"Declared, against a target within 140
metres whom Aeldoris has read closely enough"* — no voice, hand, ink or blood, at the first Stage
where that is legal. **The Interrupt-the-chain route is therefore closed**, and *"this is the
practical reason Stage VII is the line every soldier is taught to fear."*

**Failure mode — Boundary fault, with Eidolyn's template defect as its content.** *"The condition was
established imprecisely and the law ran somewhere unintended"* (`Two Sets of Books` §VII) — and the
unintended place is him, which the page states as its Cost rather than as a fault. The second and
slower one is **Coherence**: a man whose Attraction Layer is *"built on other people's fears rather
than his own bonds"* is a man whose lived behaviour and stated law can be read as drifting, and the
Continuum reads drift. His card's Risk line and this working's Cost line are the same sentence twice.

**What the target sees and feels.** Something stands up behind them. Not a monster and not a copy:
**themselves, with the editing off.** *"The target's own dream-self rises behind them, unblurred by
the waking mind's editing"*, and then it says the thing. Not an accusation — the grammar is wrong for
that. It states, in their own register, out of their own material, the item they have been declining
to know, and it does so with the specific awfulness of testimony you cannot contest because you are
the witness. *"A target with a genuine unresolved contradiction has that contradiction voiced back at
them by a figure carrying their own memory-echo, which reads as confession because it is drawn from
material the target already possesses."*

The figure has weight in the room — *"a standing, load-bearing presence rather than a flicker"* — and
the record is careful to say it is not an illusion. It does not strike. There is no blow to brace
against, which is the point: **the damage is done by the target, to the target, about the target.**
Soul Crystal coherence loosens around exactly the thing the contradiction concerned and nowhere
else, and *"the exposure persists after the figure dissolves."* A hundred and forty metres away, a
man they may never have seen is holding his attention on them, and when he stops, the figure goes and
the loosening stays.

And a target who has nothing behind the waking self returns nothing. *"A mind at peace with itself,
or with nothing behind the waking self, gives the working nothing to find"*, and the card is blunter:
*"Anyone without guilt · The one target he has no working against."*

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** η **≈0.65** (card), against the Tier 5 · Expert band of
**0.60–0.70** (Part Nineteen as corrected by **R44-4**) — mid-band, and the `SA-NUM-ETA-TIER` fault
this account logged no longer reaches him. **Thirty-five percent
leaves as heat, sound and structural bleed.** On a working with no yield, thirty-five percent of a
very small expenditure is a very small amount of bleed, which is why this working is quiet — and
*"the highest Bands run quiet"* cuts in his favour here for once, not because his efficiency is good
but because there is almost nothing to be inefficient with.

**Does the physics close against the stratal account?** **Yes, and this is the best-closing page of
the seven in this batch.** The working has no yield, so there is no joules-against-ceiling problem to
fail. Its addressing claim is about a state space and not about transport, which is the defensible
use of Oneirion. Its credibility mechanism is source-monitoring failure and its damage mechanism is
localised dissonance, both real and both quantified in the literature. Its two resistant profiles —
Verdantia and Mortalis — are resistant for one reason the registers supply and the page correctly
identifies: no gap, no coordinate. And its central operation is a genuine, named, unresolved problem
in glass physics: **the configuration a system relaxes toward may not be a place**, which answers the
page's own closing question without inventing anything. What does not close is bookkeeping: two Path
gates fail, the Physics Domain is named as something the register does not call it, the page asserts
there is no card when there is one, and **the page and the card disagree about what the target has to
be guilty of.**

## 5 · Counterplay and the challenge

**The fairness check** (`.claude/skills/wotr-write/references/fair-play.md`).

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Break the man — by being a man with nothing hidden, which is the cheapest counter in the
  batch.** *"A target whose conscious and undefended selves already agree returns no verdict for the
  Court to hand down."* It requires no Stage, no Crystal, no equipment and no lookup — only conduct
  — and the card confirms it is total: *"Anyone without guilt · The one target he has no working
  against."* **This is the fairest single sentence in the batch**, because the counter is a
  character choice and not a purchase.
- **Deny the field — a memory-null region.** The page's own Weakness, and the mechanism is exact: no
  Aetheric memory record means no template for Eidolyn, and the working stops at the deposition step
  rather than at the addressing step. It is also the terrain answer the Families page does not list
  for Limina, so a commander has to get it from this page.
- **Break the body — Rigidity's inverse, and the Verdantia/Mortalis reading.** *"Verdantia and
  Mortalis practitioners run Crystals already integrated around growth or around an honest
  relationship with ending, and carry correspondingly little unresolved residue."* The registers say
  why: Verdantia's *"indeterminate growth and continuous ambient conversion"* means the Crystal never
  arrested, so there is no configuration to extrapolate to; Mortalis *"engages ending as a structural
  event rather than a catastrophe"*, so the largest ordinary residue is already metabolised. **Send
  the gardener or the undertaker.**
- **Break the boundary — not the chain, and know why.** Stage VII is Refraction, so the wrist and
  the mouth are off the table: *"Above Refraction the chain is internal and none of this applies."*
  What is on the table is **his attention**, which is the Seal and the duration in one — break his
  focus and the figure goes. And **`[Lk]` Lock**: *"occupies a limit so no other limit can be set
  there"*, which is what a memory-null field is doing to the coordinate.
- **Break the man — Coherence drift, and wait.** His Attraction Layer is Drifting, this working
  feeds it, and *"You do not have to cause this. You have to notice it and wait."* Every Court he
  convenes moves him.
- **Break the man — the vow, and the case he declined.** *"An opponent who knows the terms of a vow
  holds a weapon that does not require touching him."* His card's Lore records him declining the
  case against Kaelan Vorn because *"the strike had been made in the open, and Kaelan had never
  denied it."* **A man who makes his conduct a matter of public record has disarmed him in
  advance**, and that is a genuinely findable counter for a player who reads the Lore section.

**The tell, stated plainly.** Two, in order. First, you have been read — his Presence is
*"a soft pressure of sleep"* in which *"nearby minds recall forgotten memories involuntarily"*, and
the working requires him to have read you closely before he declares. Second, and unmissable: your
own self stands up behind you, unedited, and begins to speak.

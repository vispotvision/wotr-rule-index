# System account · Principle Overwrite

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** For about fifteen seconds, inside five kilometres, one of the
rules is a different rule. Effect arrives before cause, or rhythm holds still, or two opposites are
one thing. Everything inside has to work under the new rule, and everything that was built on the
old one stops working. Then it ends.

**The real phenomenon.** **A false-vacuum bubble held open against its own surface tension** — and
the page's Mechanism names the correct physics with unusual precision, then draws the wrong
conclusion from it.

**(a) The domain wall, and Coleman's result.** Spontaneous symmetry breaking leaves a field theory
with more than one vacuum, and a **domain wall** is the topological defect separating two regions in
different vacua. Coleman's *"Fate of the False Vacuum"* (1977) and Coleman–De Luccia give the
arithmetic for one vacuum appearing inside another: the nucleation rate per unit volume is
**Γ/V ≈ A·e^(−S_E)**, and there is a **critical radius R_c = 3σ/ε**, where σ is the wall's surface
tension and ε the energy-density difference between the two states.

**Then comes the part the page does not use.** A *true*-vacuum bubble above R_c is driven outward by
the pressure difference, accelerates to asymptotically the speed of light, and **never stops.** For
Francis's five kilometres to revert after fifteen seconds, the state he imposes must be the
**higher**-energy one — a **false** vacuum nucleated inside a true one — which does not expand and is
not spontaneous. Its wall tension and its pressure deficit both push **inward**, and it has to be
**held**.

**That is exactly what the page says Exuroth is for:** *"Exuroth's tolerance for structural ordeal
lets him hold the boundary open long enough."* And it is exactly what Catharsis is for:
*"Catharsis forces the release, the old state discharging the way a collapsing bubble wall dumps its
surface tension outward."* **The page has the physics right.** What it gets wrong is the
conditionality. It says resistance *"is what converts the edit into a shockwave instead of a quiet
change"*, and a held false-vacuum bubble does not have a quiet option: **the collapse is mandatory and
the wall energy is dumped every time**, because the wall was carrying 4πR²σ and the interior was
carrying (4/3)πR³ε, and both have to go somewhere when the boundary is released. Logged.

**(b) The energy, and it does not fit inside the room.** Take the page at its word that the paradox
discharge falls in the EX-Grade band. Part Eleven gives EX as **5–50 EN** peak force and
**1.24×10²⁹ – 6.9×10³⁷ J**, 29.6 exatons to 16.5 ronnatons of TNT. Now measure the room:

- Bubble volume at R = 5 km: (4/3)π(5,000 m)³ = **5.24×10¹¹ m³.**
- Air inside it: 5.24×10¹¹ × 1.225 = **6.41×10¹¹ kg.**
- **Total mass-energy of that air, annihilated completely:** 6.41×10¹¹ × 8.988×10¹⁶ =
  **5.76×10²⁸ J.**
- **The EX floor is 1.24×10²⁹ J — a factor of 2.15 larger.**

**So the lowest figure in the band the page quotes exceeds the complete mass-energy of everything
gaseous inside the field by more than a factor of two**, and reaching it would require converting
**1.38×10¹² kg** — one and a third billion tonnes — of mass to energy. `Two Sets of Books` §III says
*"The energy was in the room."* Here it demonstrably was not, and the page's own upper bound,
16.5 ronnatons, is a planetary figure attached to a five-kilometre radius. Logged.

**(c) Judicium, and the one part that is cheap.** Emission and absorption spectroscopy:
*"Every element absorbs and emits at wavelengths fixed by its own electron structure, and no
substance can present another's spectrum."* Nondestructive inspection is the register's second
analogue, and eddy-current testing has a real depth law — skin depth **δ = √(2/ωμσ)**, which is why
the technique finds the crack under the paint and cannot find the crack under the plate.
**Identification costs nothing and penetrates poorly**, which is a good description of how the
working's first step behaves: Francis can read which state the Principle currently occupies, and
that read is spectroscopic and free.

**(d) Exuroth, and why holding is possible at all.** Quench and temper. A steel quenched from
austenite past its martensite-start temperature is hard and brittle to the point of uselessness;
tempering at **200–650 °C** permits controlled carbide diffusion and restores toughness, taking a
part from roughly **65 HRC** to the low forties while keeping most of the strength. Exuroth runs the
Crystal on that cycle, so it tolerates ordeals that would split an untempered lattice — and its
failure is the one to fear here: *"Quench cracking. Cycle too fast without the tempering interval
and the lattice arrives hard, brittle, and split along its own thermal gradients."*

**(e) Catharsis, with the register's own number.** *"Melting ice at zero degrees absorbs three
hundred and thirty-four joules per gram and the temperature does not move a fraction. The energy goes
entirely into breaking the ordered structure."* **334 J/g**, at constant temperature — a discharge
that changes state without changing the reading on the thermometer. That is the right law for a
release which resolves rather than wounds, and the card says so of the parent working:
*"Same-Family compounding with Exuroth is why the Dirge does not wound; it resolves."*

**The governing law, and the page's best line.** The **Aether-Physics Fusion Rule** (Part Eleven):
*"magical combat must obey physical law and Aetheric law simultaneously … The Continuum measures the
energy that arrived. It does not ask where the energy came from."* Note also what Part Four says
about this Stage: *"At **Zenith, Stage XIV**, speed and attack output are no longer assessed by
conventional metrics."* **The page quotes conventional metrics anyway**, which is already on file
(`SA-NUM-ZENITH-CONVENTIONAL-METRICS`).

**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Radius: **5 km** (page). Wall area 4π(5,000)² = **3.14×10⁸ m²**; volume **5.24×10¹¹ m³.**
- Duration: **≈15 s** (page, three turns at 5 s/turn).
- Air mass inside: **6.41×10¹¹ kg**; its total mass-energy **5.76×10²⁸ J.**
- EX band: **5–50 EN · 1.24×10²⁹ – 6.9×10³⁷ J · 29.6 Et – 16.5 Rt** (Part Eleven).
  **Floor exceeds the field's total mass-energy by 2.15×.**
- Critical-radius condition, if R_c = 5 km: **σ/ε = 1,667 m.**
- Collapse timescale for a wall running at ~c across 5 km: **≈1.7×10⁻⁵ s (17 µs).**
- Catharsis's own latent figure: **334 J/g** at constant temperature.
- Exuroth's tempering window: **200–650 °C**; hardness **65 → low 40s HRC.**
- Eddy-current depth law: **δ = √(2/ωμσ).**
- Memory cost: **2 % per use** (page), i.e. **fifty uses to the whole.**

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** **Two budgets, and only one of them closes.**
The *edit* is nearly free, and that is the honest and interesting half: identifying the state is
spectroscopic, imposing a boundary is *"cheap in absolute terms and expensive in the only currency a
soul has"*, and holding the wall for fifteen seconds costs the structural tolerance Exuroth supplies.
The **discharge** does not close at all. A collapsing wall releases what the wall and the interior
were carrying, which is a real and finite quantity set by σ and ε — and the page instead assigns it a
Grade band whose floor is twice the mass-energy of the atmosphere inside the bubble. **The physics
would happily supply a smaller, derivable number. The page supplies a planetary one.**

**Precedent we are not copying.** Rewriting a law of nature is the top shelf of the genre: JoJo's
Made in Heaven, Gojo's Limitless, Tengen Toppa, Doctor Manhattan, the *Mother of Learning* endgame,
every Type-Moon Reality Marble. Those are **assertions of will**: the practitioner is stronger than
the law. This one is **a pressure vessel**. The law is not overpowered; a second state is nucleated
beside it, held against its own surface tension for as long as a man can bear the ordeal, and then
released — and the release is a thermodynamic event with a number attached, not a triumph. The
divergence is that the working's limit is **structural tolerance**, and the reason it ends in fifteen
seconds is that walls under inward pressure come in.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** The second-largest draw in this batch by volume: **5.24×10¹¹ m³**, which is a
county. Fulguria is favoured by *"Dry air, conductive substrate, clear line of sight"* and suppressed
by *"Faraday-caged interiors, heavy ferrous shielding, dense fog"*; Caloria is favoured by
*"Steep thermal gradients, combustible atmosphere, geothermal ground"* and suppressed by
*"Thermal equilibrium, saturated cold, oxygen-poor air"* (Families page, Environmental Coupling).
*"Terrain is not flavour. It is a modifier on η."* **At five kilometres the field contains all of those
conditions at once**, which is a scale problem the system has no rule for: a working whose radius
exceeds the coherence length of terrain cannot have a terrain modifier. Worth naming rather than
resolving.

**And his efficiency is the wrong side of the line that would have paid for this.** Part Nineteen,
Tier 8 · Archmaster: **η 0.95–1.2**, with the note *"Above 1.0 the Continuum recognizes the
expression as law and supplements it with ambient flow; **the environment becomes a co-author**."*
His card gives **η 0.93** — below the band and below 1.0. **So the one practitioner in the batch whose
working is large enough to need the environment as a co-author is the one practitioner whose
efficiency does not qualify for it**, and he pays for five kilometres out of his own reserve.
(`SA-NUM-ETA-TIER`.)

**Residue:** enormous and, per his own artifact, *written down.* *"Aetheric Residue · Traces left
behind by an Essence working. A permanent, if often subtle, change to the location where the working
occurred. Always readable by someone patient enough"* (`Core Vocabulary` §II) — and the card adds a
second, deliberate record: *"**Crimson Codex** · Living record. **Every principle rewrite he performs
is written into it as he performs it.** A man who reads it knows which laws are currently bent and
which are load-bearing."* **The residue of this working is a document, and the document is
adversarial intelligence.**

**Saturation:** the danger nobody prices. *"Aetheric Saturation · A danger state in which excessive
Essence floods an area faster than it can disperse. Produces Crystal Fracture Events in everyone
exposed, **including the person who caused it**."* A discharge at any figure remotely near the EX band
across five kilometres is that state by definition.

**Wellspring stratum.** **Judicium · *The Wellspring of Truth*** (primary), Family **Fulguria**,

**Catharsis's failure line is the page's unwritten worst case.** *"Supercooling. The transition fails
to nucleate … and when it finally goes it goes all at once."* A held false-vacuum bubble whose release
does not nucleate on schedule is a supercooled system, and the register says what happens next. **The
page's Limitation forbids chaining; the register explains what a failure to release looks like; and
neither the page nor the card connects them.**

**Cross-Family reading.** Fulguria with Caloria. The Families page's compatibility note is about
Materia–Vectoria (complementary) and Materia–Limina (the canonical hard case) and does not rule on
Fulguria–Caloria — but Exuroth and Catharsis are both Caloria and therefore *"compounds"*, and the
card says so: *"Same-Family compounding with Exuroth is why the Dirge does not wound; it resolves."*
**Two Caloria currents reinforcing under one Fulguria governor is a clean architecture**, and it is
one of the reasons Francis's sheet is internally tidier than his page is.

*Four Theories.* The page's own grammar is **Correspondence** — a Principle read at one scale and
rewritten at another is *"the same law expressed at two scales"* — and the card's Trait line makes it
explicit: *"Edits and fuses operational principles at the Archonic Law stratum."* But the **Debt
Position** is the one his Cost belongs to: *"the Wellspring answers on credit and the account is
settled somewhere the practitioner cannot see."* Two percent of his memory per use, fifty uses to the
whole, and *"whether the 2 percent is drawn from the same memory each time, or whether Francis is
slowly forgetting something in particular"* is the Debt Position's thesis phrased as a question. The
Accord enforces **Consent**, which is exactly the doctrine a man editing Principles has stepped
outside of: *"a process can be licensed and a relationship cannot"*, and his card's Affiliation line
says *"No faction, Archon or Titan acknowledged."*

**Essence stratum.** **Aether Shell**, Class V · Radiant on his card — *"Aether bends toward him;
currents curve around his presence"* — for the wall. **Attraction Layer**, *"Vast and
one-directional. Fifty-seven Wellsprings under partial siphon and four thousand subjugated spiritual
dominions, every bond pointing inward. Nothing on it points back"* — which is where a five-kilometre
law-claim is authorised from. **Essence Core** for the invoice, and the Core *"holds identity, memory,
and Traits"*, which is why the bill is memory. Developmental Tier at Stage XIV: **Crystallized Soul**
(Part Twenty-One) — and the card's Crystal State, *"Absolute Crystal, fractured along the paradox
lines his own inversion chains cut"*, is not one of the three States the system names
(`SA-CROSS-FRANCIS-ABSOLUTE-CRYSTAL`). **The invoice:** *"2 percent memory bleed per use, as given,
cumulative and undocumented as to what it draws from."*

**The lens: al-Ghazālī's occasionalism.** Abū Ḥāmid al-Ghazālī, *Tahāfut al-falāsifa* ("The
Incoherence of the Philosophers", c. 1095), Seventeenth Discussion. Al-Ghazālī takes the case
everybody takes — fire and cotton — and denies the connexion: *"the connexion between what is
habitually believed to be a cause and what is habitually believed to be an effect is not
necessary."* Fire does not burn the cotton. The burning is created directly at the moment of contact,
and what we call causation is **ʿāda**, custom or habit, the reliable arrangement of a will rather
than a necessity in things. His point is not that the world is unreliable. It is that its reliability
has a different author than the philosophers think, and could therefore be otherwise.

**Principle Overwrite is occasionalism with a warrant.** *"Effect erased from Cause"*,
*"rhythm running as stillness"*, *"opposed polarities merging"* — a practitioner who can do that is a
practitioner for whom causation was always a custom. And the page's Consequence is al-Ghazālī's
world arriving: *"anything built on the old reading, a ward keyed to the old polarity, a technique
timed to the old rhythm, fails the moment the edit lands."* Averroes' reply in the
*Tahāfut al-tahāfut* is the objection the Accord would make: deny necessary causation and you have
denied the possibility of knowledge, because a world of pure custom cannot be studied — and the
Accord's whole apparatus is study.

**The productive misreading.** A school reading Francis's edits as occasionalist concludes that
**nothing is being broken**, because there were no necessary connexions to break, only customs to
re-declare. Such a school teaches that a properly authorised edit is metaphysically free — that the
cost must be political or legal, never structural — and its practitioners will reach for a second
inversion inside the same working on the reasoning that custom has no arithmetic. **What kills them
is σ and ε.** A false vacuum held inside a true one is a pressure vessel, the wall carries 4πR²σ, and
the collapse is not a matter of anyone's authority. The debt is a surface tension times an area. And
Averroes gets them second: a school that has stopped believing in necessary connexion stops building
wards that hold, and the page's own Consequence is a list of what fails when somebody else edits the
custom they were relying on.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Ho]` **Order** · Extension · Valen as the governing
form — the boundary the working sets is *which of two available orders this region is under* — laid
across `[Ath]` **Command** · Root · Irath for the authorisation and `[Ae]` **Vastness** · Root ·
Zhaeren for the five kilometres. The Index's note on Irath is worth quoting here because it is about
this exact kind of working: *"**Irath holds five forms and two of them are Command and Dominion**,
both unclassified. The smallest station in the index carries the two heaviest words in it."* `[Eq]`
**Equivalence** · Extension · Urion is the alternative and would read the merging of opposed
polarities as its central case rather than as one mode among three. All from the thirty unclassified
forms in `wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`.
*Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling.*

**What boundary moves.** **Which vacuum a region occupies**, and nothing else — one region, one
Principle, for as long as the wall holds. The Index's rule is the page's own framing and the page is
unusually faithful to it: *"A glyph creates nothing and carries no power … It moves a limit, and the
law does what it has always done on the far side of the new limit."* Francis does not overpower
causation. **He nucleates a second state beside it and stands on the lid.**

**What the law then does on its own.** Most of it, and the limits are structural rather than
decreed.

Everything inside is bound, *"ally and enemy alike"*, because a vacuum is a property of a region and
regions do not have sides. Anything built on the old reading fails the moment the edit lands, because
a ward keyed to a polarity is a device calibrated to a state that is no longer the state. One
Principle at a time, because two walls in one region is two boundaries at one limit — which is the
`[Lk]` **Lock** problem the Index describes: *"occupies a limit so no other limit can be set there."*
It lasts about fifteen seconds, because a held false vacuum is under inward pressure and a man's
structural tolerance is finite. And it ends in a discharge, because the wall was carrying energy —
**every time, not only when resisted.**

**Which is where the page and its own mechanism part company.** *"Resistance from a comparably graded
source is what converts the edit into a shockwave instead of a quiet change."* A collapsing bubble
wall does not have a quiet option. Logged.

**The Four Glyph Roles, and this working is the Roles' worst case**
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). **Authorization**: *"Names by what right the operator
acts"* — and Francis's right is contested by construction, since his card's Affiliation is
*"Apostate Verdict-Bearer"* with *"No faction, Archon or Titan acknowledged"*, which is precisely why
*"an Archonic verdict overrides the edit outright."* **Boundary**: five kilometres. **Direction**: the
named replacement. **Sealing**: the release — and the Craft page's warning is this working's entire
danger profile: *"Sealing is the role that kills people … Sealing failing produces a working that
does not stop, because the law goes on obeying itself after the operator's attention has left the
room."* Set beside Catharsis's supercooling failure — *"the transition fails to nucleate … and when it
finally goes it goes all at once"* — **a failed Seal on a Principle Overwrite is a five-kilometre
region left under a rewritten law with nobody holding the lid.** Nothing in the record says what that
would be called.

**Law V check, and it closes on the Stage by a very wide margin.** Stage XIV is seven Stages above
Refraction: *"the Glyph Roles compress into an internal chain that needs no material anchor at all."*
The page's Trigger is *"Francis names a single Hermetic Principle and declares its replacement"* —
a spoken declaration, which is the *Spoken* vector and *"Loud, legible, and it tells everyone within
earshot exactly which Wellspring you just invoked."* **He does not need to speak and does anyway**,
which is a characterisation fact the card corroborates: *"concentric scorched law circles ripple
outward when he speaks."* A Zenith practitioner who declares aloud has chosen to be witnessed.

**Failure mode — Structural fault, and the card names the number.** *"The Crystal could not hold what
the boundary permitted. Overchannel, Backlash, Fracture. The working succeeds and the practitioner
does not"* (`Two Sets of Books` §VII). The card: *"**Principle Collapse.** Multi-inversion chains
cause paradox recoil that fractures selfhood and destabilises local reality; the card gives an
eighteen percent chance of self-erasure at high inversion depth."* **Eighteen percent, against a
technique page whose Limitation says chains are refused outright** — three documents, three positions,
logged below. The **Boundary fault** is the secondary: a five-kilometre condition established
imprecisely runs the law somewhere unintended, and at that radius "unintended" is a county.

**What the target sees and feels.** He says something, and one of the rules stops being the rule.

The card supplies the arrival: *"A silent gravitational hush. Temperature drops by ten degrees. A low
hymnal drone vibrates through matter, and concentric scorched law circles ripple outward when he
speaks."* Then, inside five kilometres, the specific horror of the working, which is **not damage**.
Nothing hits anybody. What happens is that a thing you have relied on your whole life stops holding:
effect arrives and then its cause does, or a rhythm you were counting stops advancing, or two things
that were opposites are the same thing and you are holding both. *"Everything inside the radius, ally
and enemy alike, is bound by the new law until the declaration lapses."*

For a practitioner it is worse than for anyone else, because a practitioner's equipment is made of the
old reading. *"Anything built on the old reading, a ward keyed to the old polarity, a technique timed
to the old rhythm, fails the moment the edit lands."* Every inscription in five kilometres that was
cut against the old custom is now quietly wrong — and `The Four Crafts` says what a wrong inscription
does: *"It misfires continuously, for as long as the carving holds, on everyone who passes it."*
**A Principle Overwrite does not only break the fight. It breaks the walls of a district for fifteen
seconds, and nobody has checked what the walls did during those fifteen seconds.**

Then it ends, and the ending is the loud part. The wall comes in. If a wall running at near light
speed crosses five kilometres, the discharge takes about **seventeen microseconds**, which is why it
reads as an event and not as a process. What it releases the page puts in the EX band, which is a
figure larger than the mass-energy of the air it is releasing into.

And Francis loses two percent of his memory, and does not know which two percent, and the Codex
writes down what he did while he was doing it.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** η **0.93** (card), against the Tier 8 · Archmaster band of
**0.95–1.2** (Part Nineteen) — **below band**, already logged (`SA-NUM-ETA-TIER`). **Seven percent
leaves as heat, sound and structural bleed**, which at this scale is not a small absolute quantity,
and — critically — **0.93 is below 1.0, so he does not get the co-authorship the band is written
around.** *"Above 1.0 the Continuum recognizes the expression as law and supplements it with ambient
flow; the environment becomes a co-author."* **He is two hundredths short of the environment helping
him carry a county, and that is the most consequential number on his sheet.**

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Break the man — Paradox Stasis, and it is written on his own card as a race trait.**
  *"Rigidity. A Trait's law is not negotiable by its bearer, and the more absolute it is the less he
  can contradict it. Present him with the situation his own law handles worst and he will handle it
  that way regardless."* His card: *"**Paradox Stasis** · Carried from the race rather than the card.
  **Every Celestial, regardless of Choir, freezes when presented with two contradictory Oaths of
  equal metaphysical weight.** Severance did not remove it. **Nobody has yet tested whether it
  did.**"* **That is the register's cruellest counter and the card hands it over in full**, including
  the tempting uncertainty. Pair it with **"The vow"**: *"an opponent who knows the terms of a vow
  holds a weapon that does not require touching him."*
- **Break the boundary — Knot, and the null field is one.** *"Knot and Lock … They condition the
  conditioning."* Nihiloth's law is *"destructive interference and the null field … Two waves in
  antiphase do not conceal one another. They sum to zero"*, and *"a Nihiloth Domain does not impose
  additional law. It cancels the law already present."* **A working whose whole content is which law
  a region is under is beaten by a region under no law at all**, and Nihiloth's failure line is the
  price: *"The cancellation is symmetric. Everything the practitioner brought inside is subject to
  the same null, including the parts of themselves they were relying on."*
- **Break the boundary — Force the Seal early, and the physics says this is billing him.**
  *"The Seal closes an operation against the warrant that opened it, and a warrant overstating
  standing seals anyway, **with the difference deducted from the speaker at the moment of closing.**
  A third party who can compel an early close is not defending. They are billing him."* **A held
  false-vacuum bubble forced to collapse early dumps its wall energy on a man who was not ready**,
  and his card's Affiliation — apostate, unacknowledged by any Archon or Titan — means his warrant
  is overstated by construction.
- **Deny the field — the terrain, at both Families at once.** Fulguria fails in *"Faraday-caged
  interiors, heavy ferrous shielding, dense fog"* and the register adds *"the counter-Fulguria kit
  has been leather and ceramic for four centuries and it is not a secret."* Caloria fails in
  *"Thermal equilibrium, saturated cold, oxygen-poor air"*, and his own Presence drops the local
  temperature ten degrees, which walks his Caloria currents toward equilibrium as he stands there.
  **A shielded interior in saturated cold is bad ground for both of his Families simultaneously.**
- **Break the man — Read him, and the Codex is the reading.** *"Every thaumaturge has a pattern …
  a pattern learned is a pattern interruptible."* His card supplies the pattern in writing:
  *"**Crimson Codex** · Every principle rewrite he performs is written into it as he performs it.
  **A man who reads it knows which laws are currently bent and which are load-bearing.**"* **The
  counter to Principle Overwrite is a librarian.**
- **Deny the field — and the free one.** *"Waiting for the cost"* is explicitly dismissed by the
  register — *"A plan that consists of surviving until he tires is a plan to be killed by a tired
  man"* — **but this working ends in fifteen seconds whether or not he is finished**, which is not
  waiting for a cost, it is waiting for a duration. The page says so: *"riding out the fifteen
  seconds and rebuilding after is the only documented option"* absent a null field. That is a real
  plan and the register's warning does not cover it.

**The tell, stated plainly.** The temperature falls about ten degrees and the air goes quiet in a way
that presses. A low drone comes up through the floor rather than through the ears. Then he speaks, and
scorched circles go out from him across the ground, and one of the rules is a different rule for
fifteen seconds. **Anyone with a thermometer and a floor can read the first two.**

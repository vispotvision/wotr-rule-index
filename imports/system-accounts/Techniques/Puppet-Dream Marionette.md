# System account · Puppet-Dream Marionette

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** A frightened person's body carries out the escape they were
imagining, at the wrong moment and in the wrong direction, while they watch it happen from inside.

**The real phenomenon.** **A threshold released on a motor programme that was already loaded** —
and the arithmetic of that is the reason the working's Limit is not a balance patch but a
precondition.

**(a) Under threat, the flight is already prepared.** This is measurable, not metaphorical. The
**Bereitschaftspotential** (Kornhuber and Deecke, 1965) is a slow negative cortical potential that
begins rising **one to two seconds before** a self-paced voluntary movement, and Libet's W-time —
the moment a subject reports the *urge* — arrives roughly **200 ms before** the movement itself and
well after the potential has started. Corticospinal excitability is measurably raised during **motor
imagery**: transcranial magnetic stimulation over M1 produces larger motor-evoked potentials while a
subject merely imagines the action. Under threat this is stronger still: the defensive cascade runs
freeze, flight, fight, and the freeze phase is **not** an absence of preparation but a loaded one,
with bradycardia and postural readiness while the motor programme sits primed.

**So a frightened person is a system with an escape programme loaded and held sub-threshold.** The
page's Mechanism says the same thing in its own vocabulary: *"Somnalis reads the adjacent possibility
the target's dreaming motor sense has already half-committed to."* **Half-committed is the
Bereitschaftspotential.**

**(b) Which is why the working is nearly free.** A system held just below a threshold can be switched
by an arbitrarily small perturbation — formally, the perturbation required to cross a **saddle-node
bifurcation** goes to zero as the control parameter approaches the bifurcation point. The working
does not have to move the limb. **It has to move the threshold**, and the limb is moved by the
target's own ATP: a limb swing is of order **20–50 J**, a biceps contraction of order **500 N**, and
all of it is the target's.

Compare what it would cost to drive the limb from outside. Transcranial magnetic stimulation is the
real instrument for that, and its figures are unforgiving: **~5 kA** through the coil in **~100 µs**,
a peak field of about **1 T**, an induced tissue field of **100–150 V/m**, several hundred joules
stored per pulse, a motor-evoked potential at **20–30 ms** latency, and an effective depth of
**~2 cm** — which reaches cortex and nothing deeper. **Driving a body from outside is expensive,
slow and shallow. Releasing a loaded programme is none of those.** The page has picked the cheap
route and its Limit is the price of the cheap route.

**(c) Somnalis, and the eleven percent.** The register's analogue is ensemble forecasting: a
turbulent system cannot be predicted from one initial condition, so you run many perturbed copies
and read the **spread**. Real figures: operational ensembles run of order **50 members**; the
atmosphere's error-doubling time is about **1.5 days**; the whole discipline exists because the
largest Lyapunov exponent is positive. Somnalis gives the practitioner the distribution directly,
and its failure names the number: *"Ensemble collapse. The spread is mistaken for a single reading,
and the practitioner acts on a branch that carried **eleven percent** of the weight."*

**That is this working's most checkable failure mode and the page does not carry it.** Aeldoris is
reading a distribution of possible flight-motions and seeding **one**. If he mistakes the spread for
a reading, he seeds a motion the target was only eleven percent committed to — which is a motion the
target's body will start and not finish, and the difference between a hijack and a stumble.

**(d) Eidolyn, and where the page hedges.** *"Eidolyn gives that imagined motion enough Ardency
Density to press outward into real muscle."* If the mechanism is a threshold release, nothing needs
to press on muscle; if something does press on muscle, the force has to be accounted for. The page
holds both readings at once. The account takes the threshold reading, because it is the one the
Limit, the Cost and the Counter all behave as though were true — and notes the other.

**The governing law, and the page's best line.** The **Aether-Physics Fusion Rule** (Part Eleven):
*"The Continuum measures the energy that arrived. It does not ask where the energy came from."* Here
the energy arrived from the target's own metabolism, which is why the working is cheap and why its
Cost is charged in something other than output: *"His own frame echoes the motion he forces."*

**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Range: **0–60 m** (page). Duration: **≈5 s** (page's estimate, one turn at 5 s).
- Bereitschaftspotential onset: **1–2 s** before a self-paced movement. Libet W-time: **≈200 ms**.
- Limb-swing energy: **≈20–50 J.** Biceps contraction: **≈500 N.** **All of it the target's.**
- Perturbation needed at a saddle-node bifurcation: **→ 0.** This is the whole economy of the
  working.
- Cost of driving a limb from outside (TMS, for contrast): **~5 kA / 100 µs / 1 T / 100–150 V/m /
  hundreds of joules per pulse / 20–30 ms latency / ~2 cm depth.**
- Ensemble spread: operational ensembles **~50 members**; atmospheric error-doubling **~1.5 days**;
  Somnalis's stated failure branch weight **11 %.**
- Startle latency, for the pain counter: orbicularis oculi EMG at **~14 ms**, full-body startle
  **~100 ms** — **faster than any voluntary correction and faster than the seeded motion completes.**

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** **In the target, entirely.** Twenty to fifty
joules of limb kinetic energy, paid out of the target's own ATP, released by a perturbation that at
the bifurcation tends to zero. This is the cheapest working in the batch by a wide margin, and
`Two Sets of Books` §III is the reason: *"the boundary condition is cheap in absolute terms and
expensive in the only currency a soul has."* **The expensive currency here is his own body**, which
echoes the motion, and his own Shell, which keeps the residue.

**Precedent we are not copying.** Puppetry is a crowded shelf: Kankurō's strings, the Gilded
Marionette of half a dozen settings, Bleach's Kurotsuchi, Geass, *Death Note*'s notes, Imperius in
Diablo. Those **write the motion**. This one **cannot write anything** — it can only release what the
target had already begun writing, which is why a calm target is immune and why the page's own open
question (*"Whether the fear-motion he seeds is drawn from the target's own mind, or is one of his
own"*) matters: on the mechanism as stated, it has to be the target's, because his own motion would
have no loaded programme to ride. The divergence is that the puppeteer has no strings. **He has a
threshold, and the target supplies everything else including the fear.**

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** A tiny draw: one threshold, at up to 60 m, for about five seconds. **But the
terrain requirement is the strongest in this batch and the page never states it.** Somnalis is
**Fluxia**, and the Counterplay register is explicit: *"Fluxia cannot act without a medium, so a dry
vault is a Fluxia prison."* The Families page agrees — Fluxia is favoured by *"Standing or moving
water, humidity, pressure differential"* and suppressed by *"Arid terrain, vacuum, thermally locked
air"* — while Limina wants *"Darkness, silence, Aether-thin ground, ruins"* and is suppressed by
*"crowd density."* **The Marionette therefore needs a humid, quiet, thinly-peopled place, and a dry
still room denies it outright**, on the register's own words. *"Terrain is not flavour. It is a
modifier on η."*

**Residue:** on him, and it does not clear. *"Panic residue clings to his Aether Shell afterward

(`Core Vocabulary` §III), and residue that accumulates on a Shell is Clouding by definition. **This
is the only working in the batch whose residue is explicitly described as accumulating on the
practitioner's own Shell rather than in a room**, and the system has no rule for what a Clouded Shell
costs.

**Saturation:** not a limit at this scale.

**Wellspring stratum.** **Somnalis · *The Dream Veil*** (primary), Family **Fluxia**, Physics Domain

**This is the only Aeldoris working in the batch that is cross-Family**, and the Families page does

Register Stat Effects through the Merge Ledger (`_method.md` §VI): Somnalis strengthens Gnosis
**Forecast** and Dominion **Sense**; Oneirion strengthens Gnosis **Cognition**, Dominion
**Sovereignty** and Ardency **Cascade**; Eidolyn strengthens Ardency **Density**, Ardency
**Density** again (from Inscription) and Gnosis **Retention** (from Memorium). **Gnosis Forecast is
Somnalis's own governing Sub-Stat and the page's FOW line does not carry it** — which matters,
because Forecast *"requires Attraction Path at Stage V to exceed S"* and the page's Path line is
*"Spirit Path, with drift toward Attraction Path."*

*Four Theories.* **Consent Doctrine**, and cleanly: a distribution is read, a threshold moves, a
muscle contracts. Nothing is asked. The page's open question — whose motion it is — is a
**Correspondence** question (*"the same law expressed at two scales, and the working is the two
scales briefly agreeing"*), and the Cost's *"Sympathetic Twitch"*, his own frame echoing the motion,
is Correspondence's signature symptom. The Accord enforces Consent.

**Essence stratum.** **Aether Shell** for the residue, which is where the bill lands. **Attraction
Layer** for the read, and his card says that layer is **Drifting**, *"built on other people's fears
rather than his own bonds"* — and this is the working that is literally built on other people's
fears. **Essence Core** untouched, unusually. Developmental Tier at Stage VII: **Resonant** (Part
Twenty-One) — *"The Crystal self-conducts. Power moves even in silence."* Crystal State: **Refined,
residue-heavy** (card), and *residue-heavy* is this working's Cost written onto the sheet.
**The invoice:** **Sympathetic Twitch** (his own frame echoes the forced motion) and **Echo
Feedback** (panic residue on the Shell, clearing only with time). Neither has a system unit.

**The lens: ideomotor action.** William Benjamin Carpenter, *Principles of Mental Physiology* (1874),
who coined *ideo-motor* in 1852, and William James, *Principles of Psychology* (1890), ch. XXVI:
*"every representation of a movement awakens in some degree the actual movement which is its
object."* The doctrine holds that an idea of a movement held in attention **discharges into the
movement** unless something inhibits it, and that the subject experiences the result as external
because they did not author it. It is the standing explanation for **Chevreul's pendulum**, dowsing
rods, table-turning and the Ouija board, and its decisive test is **Faraday's 1853 experiment**: he
put a light card between the sitters' hands and the table with an index to show relative motion, and
the card moved before the table did. The hands were pushing. The sitters were sincere.

**The Puppet-Dream Marionette is ideomotor action with the inhibition removed.** *"He seeds a target
already gripped by fear with their own escape-image, and the target's body performs it whether they
will it or not."* That is James's sentence with a hostile agent in it.

**The productive misreading.** An ideomotor school reads the working correctly as far as it goes —
the motion is the target's own — and draws the wrong conclusion: that because it is your own
movement, **you can always own it and therefore stop it by will.** They will train volitional
resistance, drill "recognise and refuse", and teach that a strong enough man cannot be puppeted.
**What kills them is the bifurcation.** A system held just below threshold can be switched by a
perturbation that tends to zero, so *more will* is the wrong axis — you cannot out-push an
arbitrarily small push. The page's own Counterplay quietly agrees and names three things that work
instead, none of which is willpower: *"A trained pain-response can sever the thread outright"* (the
startle reflex fires at 14 ms, faster than any decision), *"a lucid hold on one's own dream-logic
negates the puppet effect entirely"*, and not being afraid, which removes the loaded programme
altogether. **Faraday is the lesson for the school as well as for the séance: the way to find out
what is really moving is to put an indicator between the hand and the table**, and the way to beat
this working is to change what your body has already begun, not to argue with it.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Vel]` **Mind** · Extension · Iesara as the governing
form — the boundary the working sets is *which of the loaded arrangements is permitted to execute* —
laid across `[Ren]` **Passage** · Root · Auren for the crossing into the target's manifold. `[Et]`
**Mirage** · Root · Kaetra is the alternative and would file the working under *what may be taken
for one's own intention* rather than under *what may execute*; it is already contested elsewhere
(`SA-GAP-GLYPH-ET-COLLISION`). All from the thirty unclassified forms in
`wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`.
*Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling.*

**What boundary moves.** **One threshold**, and that is the whole of it. Not the limb, not the
intention, not the fear — **the inhibition sitting between a prepared motor programme and its
execution.** Everything downstream is the target's own physiology doing what it has always done on
the far side of a threshold that has moved, which is the Index's rule exactly: *"A glyph creates
nothing and carries no power … It moves a limit, and the law does what it has always done on the far
side of the new limit."*

**What the law then does on its own.** All of it, and every limit on the page falls out.

It cannot reach a calm mind, because there is no loaded programme to release — *"A disciplined mind
that is not afraid gives Somnalis nothing to read."* It produces a *flight* motion specifically,
because that is the programme fear loads. It redirects an attack already in progress rather than
inventing one, because a body mid-motion is a body whose next segment is already prepared. It
responds inconsistently to non-humanoid targets, because *"their configuration space mapping
differently"* means a different programme library. Letheveil clarity dissolves it, because
compressing away the recent fear-memory removes the coordinate the release was aimed at. A trained
pain-response severs it, because the startle reflex is a faster loop than the one being hijacked. And
**his own frame echoes the motion**, because he read the programme by running it, and running a motor
programme is what motor imagery is.

**The Four Glyph Roles** (`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). Authorization: his Stage and
the read. Boundary: sixty metres, one target, one programme. Direction: execute. **Sealing: about
five seconds, and then the programme is spent** — which is the tightest Seal available, because the
thing released cannot be released twice. *"Sealing failing produces a working that does not stop"*
has almost no purchase here: the failure mode is not a runaway but an **Echo Feedback** residue that
does not clear, which is the slow version of the same warning.

**Law V check, and it closes on the Stage.** Stage VII is Refraction: *"the Glyph Roles compress into
an internal chain that needs no material anchor at all."* The page's Trigger is *"Declared, against a
target within 60 meters"* with no voice, hand, ink or blood named. The **Interrupt the chain** route
is therefore closed, and *"this is the practical reason Stage VII is the line every soldier is taught
to fear."*

**Failure mode — Boundary fault, with ensemble collapse as its content.** *"The condition was
established imprecisely and the law ran somewhere unintended"* (`Two Sets of Books` §VII). The
imprecision here has a name and a number: Somnalis's **ensemble collapse**, acting on a branch that
carried eleven percent of the weight. A **Structural fault** runs underneath it in the long term: a
Shell accumulating panic residue that clears only with time is a Shell being driven toward Clouding.

**What the target sees and feels.** They were already afraid, and they were already — without
knowing it — rehearsing the way out. Then their legs take it.

*"Involuntary limb movement, redirected attacks along the altered trajectory, self-sabotage
reflexes, hijacked fight-or-flight impulses."* The specific horror is not paralysis and not
possession: **it is recognition.** The motion is theirs. It is the one they were about to make, made
now, at the wrong instant, toward the wrong place, and the body executes it with the full commitment
of an intention because it *was* an intention. A guard steps back off a wall. A swordsman's parry
becomes the flinch he had already imagined. *"A moving or striking target follows the path their own
fear sends them down rather than the one their skill would have chosen."* Skill is a slower system
than fear and it loses the race by design.

And a calm person standing next to them notices nothing, because nothing was done to the room. Sixty
metres away a man declared something, and the only evidence is that somebody moved badly.

Afterwards Aeldoris's own hand twitches through the same arc, and the panic stays on him.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** η **≈0.65** (card), against the Tier 5 · Expert band of
**0.60–0.70** (Part Nineteen as corrected by **R44-4**) — mid-band, and the `SA-NUM-ETA-TIER` fault
this account logged no longer reaches him. **Thirty-five percent
of a nearly-zero expenditure leaves as heat, sound and structural bleed**, which is a negligible
absolute quantity and the reason this working is quiet. The bleed that matters is not the 35 %. It is
the residue, which is not bleed at all: bleed leaves, and Echo Feedback stays.

**The Counterplay routes that work**

- **Deny the field — a dry room, and this is the register's own answer.** *"Fluxia cannot act without
  a medium, so a dry vault is a Fluxia prison."* Somnalis is Fluxia. **The Puppet-Dream Marionette

- **Break the man — Rigidity, reversed.** *"Present him with the situation his own law handles worst
  and he will handle it that way regardless."* His law handles a **calm** target by doing nothing at
  all. Discipline is not a defence against the release; it is a refusal to load the programme, which
  is a different and cheaper thing. **Veterans beat this working by boredom.**
- **Break the man — make him pay per use.** Echo Feedback accumulates and clears only with time,
  which makes *"Exhaustion"* applicable in an unusual register: *"a defender who can afford to spend
  nothing wins a long engagement against an attacker who cannot afford to spend nothing."* Every
  frightened man he puppets clouds his Shell a little further, and a commander who keeps feeding him
  frightened men is buying his degradation with theirs.
- **Break the boundary — not the chain.** Stage VII is Refraction, so *"Above Refraction the chain is
  internal and none of this applies."* The wrist and the mouth are off the table. What remains is
  Letheveil-class clarity on the target (removing the coordinate) or `[Knt]` **Knot**, which
  *"entangles a path so it cannot be traced cleanly"* — and a path in a target's own configuration
  space is exactly the sort of path Knot was written for.
- **Break the man — Coherence drift, and wait.** His Attraction Layer is **Drifting** and built on
  other people's fears, and this working is made of other people's fears. *"You do not have to cause
  this. You have to notice it and wait."*

**The tell, stated plainly — and there isn't one on the working.** What the record gives is the man:
*"a soft pressure of sleep"*, shadows shimmering, **Sleepfall Presence** making everybody nearby
drowsy and foggy. **If the room has gone heavy and you are frightened, you are already the puppet and
the only useful question is what your body was about to do.** The act itself is a declaration at up
to sixty metres with nothing visible attached, and the first evidence anybody gets is a good soldier
moving badly.

# System account · Radiant Ribbon Shell

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** A voice shapes the air around it into smooth layered bands. The
bands quieten a room, or carry one sentence a long way without it breaking up, or narrow into an edge
that parts rope.

**The real phenomenon.** **Acoustic beamforming through a coherent aperture**, and this is the
numerically densest page in the batch: it states eight figures. Three of them close well, one closes
brilliantly, and one cannot close in air at all.

**(a) The Carry closes, and the arithmetic tells you something the page does not.** *"Carry reaches
200-300 metres in open air, 80-120 through alleys."* Work it. To be clearly audible over a fight —
say **70 dB SPL at 250 m** — you need an intensity of 10⁻⁵ W/m² at the listener. Spread isotropically
over a sphere of that radius (4π × 250² = 7.85×10⁵ m²) that is **7.85 W of acoustic power**, which a
human cannot produce: a shout is about **1 mW** acoustic. But beamed into a 10° cone — solid angle
0.024 sr, so 1,500 m² at 250 m — the requirement falls to **15 mW.** **A loud shout's worth of power,
beamed, delivers a clear sentence at 250 metres.** The Carry mode is physically cheap, and the working
needs a gain of roughly fifteen in power and a factor of five hundred in directivity.

**The directivity is where the real constraint lives, and it is a size.** For a circular aperture the
main-lobe half-angle is θ ≈ 1.22 λ/D. At 500 Hz (λ = 0.69 m) a 10° (0.17 rad) beam needs
**D ≈ 5 metres of coherent aperture.** **So the laminar bands have to be metres across.** That is not
on the page and it is the most useful thing in this account: a Radiant Ribbon Shell in Carry mode is a
five-metre acoustic structure, visible in dust, felt as a pressure corridor, and impossible to hide.
It also explains the alley figure without anyone having to write it: **an alley is a waveguide**, so
the walls do the beaming and the practitioner does not have to, which is why the range through alleys
is shorter but the requirement is lower.

**(b) The Hush does not close, and the reason is a wavelength.** *"Hush runs roughly -9 dB at 8
metres."* Nine decibels is a factor of eight in intensity, which is a large and useful amount. The
problem is the mechanism the page names: *"entrains nearby air into laminar bands"* and *"damp ambient
noise."* **Laminar flow does not attenuate sound.** Atmospheric absorption at 1 kHz, 20 °C, 50 % RH is
about **5 dB per kilometre**, so over eight metres it contributes **0.04 dB** — one part in two
hundred of what is claimed. And the only other route, **antiphase cancellation**, has a hard geometric
limit: global cancellation requires the controlled region to be small compared with a wavelength,
conventionally under λ/6. To cancel across an 8 m radius you would need **λ > 48 m**, i.e. **below
about 7 Hz** — infrasound, not speech. This is exactly why real active noise control lives in
headphones and duct sections and nowhere else.

**So a −9 dB hush over eight metres of speech-band noise needs absorption or scattering, and the page
names neither.** The effect is modest and desirable; the stated mechanism cannot produce it. Logged.

**(c) The Cut cannot be done acoustically in air, and the ceiling is 194 dB.** *"Cut severs textile
and rope at 3-5 metres and scores light plate at 2-3."* Price it.

- **Acoustic radiation pressure** is the free-field force a sound wave exerts: p_rad = I/c. At an
  enormous **160 dB** (2,000 Pa RMS) the intensity is p²/(ρc) = 4×10⁶/413 ≈ **9,700 W/m²**, giving a
  radiation pressure of 9,700/343 ≈ **28 Pa.** Over a 1 mm² thread cross-section that is
  **2.8×10⁻⁵ N.**
- **Parting a thread needs 20–60 N** (textile tensile strength 20–60 MPa over ~1 mm²).
- **The shortfall is six orders of magnitude.**
- Focusing helps and has its own ceiling. A focused field can reach high peak pressures, but **in air
  the practical limit is about one atmosphere of pressure amplitude** — roughly **194 dB** — because
  beyond that the wave shock-forms and the medium stops behaving linearly. At 100 kPa over a focal
  spot of about λ/2 (4 mm at 40 kHz, where λ = 8.6 mm) the delivered force is **≈0.4 N.**
  **Still fifty times short of a thread, and four orders short of scoring steel**, which needs
  GPa-scale contact stress.

Ultrasonic cutting is real and routine — 20–40 kHz, 20–70 µm amplitude — and it works by a **blade in
contact** with the material, converting vibration to localised heating and fatigue. It is not a field
effect and it has no standoff. **There is no configuration of air, voice and geometry that cuts rope
at three metres.**

**And the page's own Mechanism gives the game away.** *"This runs on real acoustic phase-locking,
laminar versus turbulent airflow, **the same physics that lets a trained voice cut through noise an
untrained one cannot**."* That sentence is about **audibility**. A trained voice "cuts through" noise
in the sense that its spectral energy sits where the masking is weakest — the singer's formant near
**2.5–3 kHz**, which is a real and measured thing. **The Effect has taken a metaphor about being heard
and made it a blade.** Logged.

**(d) Judicium, Cymorath and Anamnesis, and what each is actually for.** Judicium is emission and
absorption spectroscopy — *"no substance can present another's spectrum"* — plus nondestructive
inspection, with a real depth law (eddy-current skin depth **δ = √(2/ωμσ)**). That is the mode that
reads a lie. Cymorath is vector fields and the Lorentz force: *"movement becomes a question of which
vector is already available rather than which one must be forced"*, and its failure is **drift**,
*"arrives somewhere efficient that is not where they meant to be."* Anamnesis is trapped-charge
dosimetry — **trap depth 1.1–2.0 eV, glow peak ≈210 °C, linear over 10 µGy to 10 Gy** — the current
that could make a vow-token chime, and whose failure is **thermal resetting**: reading the record can
erase it.

**The governing law, and where the page is strongest.** Not acoustics at all. The page's actual claim
is that **the practitioner's coherence is the working's efficiency**, and the system supports this in
three places at once. `Core Vocabulary` §III: *"**Resonant Purity** · How closely the Aether Shell's
output matches the Essence Core's intention. Low Purity produces static and misfires."* The **Coherence
fault** (`Two Sets of Books` §VII): *"The practitioner's lived behaviour and their stated law have
drifted apart, and the Continuum reads the drift. Output degrades."* And **Mirithane**, in the Limina
register: *"what leaves the practitioner is what was in them, with no flattering correction applied in
transit."* **So "ethical certainty keeps the flow laminar; conflicted intent introduces turbulence" is
not a moralism bolted onto a physics page. It is the system's own diagnostic axis, stated three
different ways, and this page uses it correctly.** That is its best feature and the account says so
plainly.

**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Hush: **−9 dB at 8 m**, holding through gusts to **≈30 knots (15 m/s)** (page).
- Carry: **200–300 m** open, **80–120 m** through alleys (page).
- Cut: textile and rope at **3–5 m**, light plate scored at **2–3 m** (page).
- Air-Step: **30–60 m/s** with a two-beat recovery (page).
- Power needed for 70 dB at 250 m: **7.85 W** isotropic, **15 mW** in a 10° cone. A shout is **1 mW.**
- **Aperture needed for a 10° beam at 500 Hz: D ≈ 5 m.**
- Atmospheric absorption at 1 kHz: **≈5 dB/km** ⇒ **0.04 dB** over 8 m.
- Global antiphase cancellation over an 8 m region: needs **λ > 48 m**, **f < ≈7 Hz.**
- Acoustic radiation pressure at 160 dB: **≈28 Pa** ⇒ **2.8×10⁻⁵ N** over 1 mm².
- Force to part a thread: **20–60 N.** Force available at the 194 dB air ceiling over a 4 mm focus:
  **≈0.4 N.**
- Singer's formant: **2.5–3 kHz**, the real content of "cutting through noise".
- Anamnesis figures, for the Feather Vows: trap depth **1.1–2.0 eV**, peak **≈210 °C**, linear over
  **10 µGy – 10 Gy**, and reading can reset.

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** **Milliwatts, in a pair of lungs.** Human speech
radiates of order 10–50 µW of acoustic power in conversation and about 1 mW shouting; a beamed
15 mW is well inside what breath can supply given directivity. **The whole energetic account of the
Hush and the Carry is a fraction of a watt**, which is `Two Sets of Books` §III with an unusually
small number in it: the joules were in the breath, and what the Crystal paid for was the *geometry*.
The Cut has no energy budget because it has no available mechanism.

**Precedent we are not copying.** The weaponised voice is a well-populated shelf: the Bene Gesserit
Voice, Gwendolyn's Wild Hunt cry, One Piece's Vivi, the Sound Hokage's ninja, Bravely Default's
Vocalist, every bard in every CRPG. Those are **compulsion or damage**. This one is **an aperture**,
and its stated Limitation is the honest and unusual consequence: *"It makes room for truth to travel;
it does not compel anyone to speak it, and a caster with nothing true to say has nothing for Carry to
preserve."* The divergence is that the working cannot make anybody believe anything. It can only make
the signal survive the trip, and it is defeated by having nothing worth sending.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** Modest draws across several modes: an 8 m hush, a 250 m corridor, a 5 m edge.
Two Families are in play and they want different rooms. **Fulguria** is favoured by *"Dry air,
conductive substrate, clear line of sight"* and suppressed by *"Faraday-caged interiors, heavy ferrous
shielding, dense fog"*; **Limina** is favoured by *"Darkness, silence, Aether-thin ground, ruins"* and
suppressed by *"Saturated Aether, crowd density, active Domains"* (Families page, Environmental
Coupling). *"Terrain is not flavour. It is a modifier on η."* **And the page's own Limit adds a
condition neither Family line covers:** *"an echo-dead space starves it of anything to carry."*
An anechoic room is not on any suppression list, and for this working it is fatal — which is a fifth
Limina-adjacent terrain condition supplied by a technique page rather than by the register.

**Residue:** in the air, briefly, and in the vow-tokens, permanently. Anamnesis is the recording
current, and *"Aetheric Residue … Always readable by someone patient enough"* (`Core Vocabulary` §II).
**The Feather Vows are residue made into an instrument** — a token that chimes true and dulls false is
a trapped-charge record with a read-out — and Anamnesis's failure is the cost nobody has charged:
*"Reading is not always passive, and a careless practitioner erases the record in the act of taking
it."* **On the register's own law, testing a vow can destroy the vow's record**, which is the same
unwritten hazard this batch found on `Mireglass Suffusion`.

**Saturation:** not a limit at these volumes.

**Wellspring stratum.** **Judicium · *The Wellspring of Truth*** (primary), Family **Fulguria**,
Physics Domain **Electromagnetism**. Its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Fulguria — Electromagnetism.md`:

> "Every element absorbs and emits at wavelengths fixed by its own electron structure, and no
> substance can present another's spectrum. Judicium tunes Gnosis to that principle: the practitioner
> reads the characteristic lines of a Crystal, a technique, or a lie, and identifies composition from
> signature alone. Eddy-current inspection finds the crack under the paint without removing the paint."
>
> **Failure** · "Spectral overload. Every object in view reports its composition simultaneously and
> the practitioner loses the ability to prioritise any of it."

**Cymorath · *The Air of Ascent*** (secondary), same Family:

> "A charged particle in a field does not choose a direction. Its path falls out of the cross product
> of its own velocity and the field it moves through. Cymorath gives the practitioner that reading of
> space: not a set of positions but a set of gradients, each with a magnitude and a direction, so that
> movement becomes a question of which vector is already available rather than which one must be
> forced."
>
> **Failure** · "Drift. The practitioner follows the available gradient rather than the intended line,
> and arrives somewhere efficient that is not where they meant to be."

**Anamnesis · *The Wellspring of Remembrance*** (tertiary), Family **Limina** — *"Objects genuinely
store their history in physically readable form. Anamnesis is the instrument, not the miracle"*,
failure **thermal resetting**.

**Two of the three failures are the page's unwritten worst cases.** **Drift** is the Air-Step's
failure: a practitioner reading space as available gradients arrives *somewhere efficient that is not
where they meant to be*, which is a far more interesting and specific hazard than *"a two-beat
recovery."* **Spectral overload** is the Hushfield's: a working built to raise the signal-to-noise
ratio of truth, run by a practitioner whose Gnosis is tuned to read every characteristic line in the
room, fails by *everything reporting at once* — which is precisely the opposite of a hush, and is the
sort of failure a reader can feel the shape of. Neither is on the page.

Cross-Family: Fulguria with Limina, which the Families page does not rule on. Two Fulguria currents
compound (*"Same-Family harmonization compounds"*), and the ceiling on all of it is **Tempering
Capacity**, which *"is not negotiable … It degrades all active harmonizations equally."* No
practitioner, so no figure.

*Four Theories.* **Correspondence**, unmistakably, and this is the page that earns it:
*"The practitioner and the Wellspring are the same law expressed at two scales, and the working is the
two scales briefly agreeing."* A working whose efficiency is literally the caster's honesty is two
scales agreeing or failing to. The Accord enforces **Consent** and files Correspondence as
*"ecclesiastically respectable, administratively useless"* — which is a very pointed thing to say about
a technique that issues vow-tokens.

**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Aether Shell**, and the page's whole diagnostic axis is the
Shell's own stated property. `Core Vocabulary` §III: **Resonant Purity**, *"How closely the Aether
Shell's output matches the Essence Core's intention. Low Purity produces static and misfires; high
Purity produces near-instant Formation."* **"Clean carry" and "hiss and turbulence" are high and low
Resonant Purity, named in the system, and the page has independently rediscovered the term.** The
**Attraction Layer** carries the Feather Vows, since a vow is a bond on the Plane of Fate and the
Layer *"holds bonds, Domains, and Wellspring harmonizations."* Developmental Tier at Stage VII:
**Resonant** (Part Twenty-One) — and the Tier's own description is this page's thesis: *"The Crystal
self-conducts. Power moves even in silence."* Crystal State: `null`. **The invoice:** *"Overriding
conscience to cut can cost temporary voice loss; post-hearing overuse causes double-hearing that
clears only with rest."* **Both costs are sensory and both are on the instrument** — a working whose
medium is a voice is billed in voice, which is the tidiest cost-to-medium match in the batch.

**The lens: Chladni's *Klangfiguren*, and the cymatics that grew out of them.** Ernst Chladni,
*Entdeckungen über die Theorie des Klanges* (1787): draw a violin bow across the edge of a sand-strewn
metal plate and the sand collects on the nodal lines, drawing the mode shape in the powder. It is real
physics — modal analysis, and the figures still used to teach it — and it was the most famous
scientific demonstration in Europe for a generation; Napoleon paid for its translation. In the
twentieth century **Hans Jenny** built it out into **cymatics** (*Kymatik*, 1967), photographing
vibrating pastes and powders and concluding that vibration is the **formative principle** of all
structure — that form does not merely respond to sound but is constituted by it. The physics is sound
and the doctrine is not, which is exactly the shape Isaac's addendum asks for.

**The Radiant Ribbon Shell is a Chladni plate made of air.** Breath is drawn through one of four
gates, and what results is *"structured pressure"*, *"laminar bands"*, a corridor with a rim at which
*"panic and ambient clamor fold outward and thin."* Nodes and antinodes in a standing pattern, with
the practitioner as the bow.

**The productive misreading.** A cymatics school reads the working as proof that **sound is form**,
and draws the practical conclusion that the way to strengthen a Shell is to **purify the tone**: train
pitch, intonation, breath support, the exact placement of the singer's formant. They will produce
magnificent voices and a doctrine in which the working's quality is a musical quality. **Two things
kill them, and both are on other pages.** The first is that the system's stated variable is not pitch
but **Resonant Purity** — how closely the Shell's output matches the Core's intention — and the page
agrees: *"Anger or conflicted intent turns clean flow to hiss and turbulence, shrinking range and
dulling the Cut Lamina exactly when it is needed most."* A perfectly trained liar frays. The second is
geometric and no amount of purity substitutes for it: **a 10° beam at speech wavelengths needs about
five metres of coherent aperture**, so the Carry's range is set by the *size* of the structure and not
by the beauty of the note. A school that has spent a generation on tone and neglected both conscience
and dimensions will have the finest voices in the Concord and a Shell that frays at the moment it is
needed, which is precisely what the page's Weakness says happens.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Lh]` **Frequency** · Root · Selhar as the governing form —
the Index lists it among the fifteen unclassified Roots, each of which *"names a **kind of limit**,
applicable to any law at all"*, and the boundary here is *which frequencies are permitted to arrive*.
Laid across `[Ora]` **Truth** · Extension · Iesara for the Judicium reading and `[Sil]` **Silence** ·
Synonym · Auren for the Hush. `[Me]` **Measure** · Synonym · Urion is the alternative worth arguing and
would file the working under *what may be assayed* rather than under *what may be heard*, which is
closer to what the Feather Vows actually do. All from the thirty unclassified forms in
`wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`.
*Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling.*

**What boundary moves.** **Whether the air in a region is permitted to be turbulent.** That is the one
quantity, and the page states it as its own central claim: *"laminar versus turbulent airflow."* Pin
the flow laminar and ordinary acoustics does the rest — a coherent aperture beams, a beam carries, a
rim scatters. **Nothing is opposed.** The Index's rule: *"It moves a limit, and the law does what it
has always done on the far side of the new limit."*

**What the law then does on its own.** Most of it, and the limits are the good part.

It needs a living voice and breath, because the source has to be in the aperture. It stops when he
stops breathing the pattern, because a standing pattern is not a stored object. It dies in an
echo-dead space, because a beam needs a medium and a corridor needs boundaries to be a corridor. It
carries further in the open than in an alley in absolute range but needs less of him in the alley,
because **an alley is a waveguide and does the beaming for free.** It holds through gusts to about
thirty knots, because above that the shear rate tears a laminar structure. And **it frays when he is
conflicted**, because the Shell's output is only ever as clean as its match to the Core's intention,
and the system says so in three places.

**What the law does not do on its own.** Two things.

**The Hush.** *"-9 dB at 8 metres"* is not produced by laminar entrainment. Laminar flow does not
attenuate sound; air absorbs 0.04 dB over eight metres; and antiphase cancellation across an eight-
metre region requires wavelengths above forty-eight metres. There has to be absorption or scattering
in the mechanism and there is not. Logged.

**The Cut.** *"Severs textile and rope at 3-5 metres."* Acoustic radiation pressure at 160 dB delivers
2.8×10⁻⁵ N to a thread that needs twenty to sixty; even at air's shock-forming ceiling of about
194 dB a focused spot delivers about 0.4 N. **The Mechanism's own cited physics — a trained voice
cutting through noise — is about audibility and the singer's formant, and the Effect has made it an
edge.** Logged.

**And one mode has no mechanism at all.** *"Air-Step bursts run 30-60 m/s with a two-beat recovery"*
appears in the Numerical Effect and nowhere else: not in the Effect, not the Function, not the
Mechanism, not the Consequence. Alongside it sit **Hushfield**, **Cut Lamina**, **Carry**, **Choir
mode**, **Ribbon Fray** and the **Feather Vows** — a working vocabulary of seven named components, of
which the page defines two, several carrying numbers, and one (the Feather Vows) being the subject of
the page's own *"What nobody knows."* Logged.

**The Four Glyph Roles** (`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`), and this page supplies its own
in an unusual form: *"A drawn breath shaped by one of four gates, **Set, Place, Speak, Recover**."*
Four named operations in a fixed order, on a page whose Craft is Spellcraft. **Set and Place are
Authorization and Boundary; Speak is Direction; Recover is Sealing** — and if that mapping is right,
this is the only page in the batch that carries the Four Glyph Roles explicitly, under different
names, which is either a genuine echo or a coincidence worth a ruling. The Craft page's warning
attaches to the fourth: *"Sealing is the role that kills people."* **Recover is the gate the page's
own Cost is charged at** — *"post-hearing overuse causes double-hearing that clears only with rest"* —
and *"Ballast with lower vowels and reset cadence against Ribbon Fray"* reads as a Sealing procedure.

**Law V check, and it closes on the Stage with room.** Stage VII is Refraction: *"the Glyph Roles
compress into an internal chain that needs no material anchor at all."* But the page **also** supplies
a material anchor — a voice and a breath — and says the working cannot run without one. **So this is
the one working in the batch that is above the Law V gate and still has an anchor to take**, and the
Counterplay route survives: *"Put a hand over his mouth and a spoken one is [finished]."* Not because
the Stage requires it, but because the medium does. That is a genuinely unusual and fair property.

**Failure mode — Coherence fault, and the page is built on it.** *"The practitioner's lived behaviour
and their stated law have drifted apart, and the Continuum reads the drift. Output degrades,
Thresholds refuse to authorise, and Traits begin to argue with their bearer. This is the slowest fault
and the one that ends careers"* (`Two Sets of Books` §VII). **Ribbon Fray is a Coherence fault
happening inside a scene rather than across a career**, which is the most interesting thing this page
does with the system. A **Field fault** is the acute alternative: *"The room could not supply what the
working assumed"* — an anechoic vault, and *"The working is correct and produces nothing."*

**What the target sees and feels.** Depends which gate was drawn, and there is no single answer, which
is why this is a class-template rather than a technique.

Inside the **Hushfield**: the noise of the world thins toward the rim rather than stopping at it, and
the effect on people is stated in the register of steadiness rather than of magic — *"Allied hands
steady and aim improves inside the Hushfield"*, and *"panic and ambient clamor fold outward and thin
at the field's rim."* **Panic treated as a noise floor is the best idea on the page.**

Under **Carry**: a sentence arrives at two hundred metres with its consonants intact, from a direction
you can point to, in a voice you can identify, and it does not sound loud. What it sounds like is
**near**. And a lie does not travel the same way: *"a lied-to listener hears the lie warble a
half-step flat."* A half-step is about 6 % in frequency, which is well above the just-noticeable
difference for pitch — **so the working's lie-detection is a perceptible, describable, arguable
sensation, not a verdict**, and the page's Limitation is careful about the difference:
*"It makes room for truth to travel; it does not compel anyone to speak it."*

Against a **bound** target: *"a bound target's textile or rope parts cleanly at the Cut Lamina's
edge."* Note what the page chose to state and what it did not: **the Cut is written as a working for
freeing people.** Cloth, cord, rope, and light plate *scored* rather than cut. Nothing in the Effect
line cuts flesh, and the Summary card says so outright — *"an edge that cuts cloth and cord without
cutting flesh."*

And when the caster is angry, or is managing what they say, the whole thing goes to *"hiss and
turbulence, shrinking range and dulling the Cut Lamina exactly when it is needed most."* **The working
degrades at the moment of stress, in proportion to how much of the truth is being curated.** That is a
cost written into the effect rather than charged after it.

**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** No practitioner, so η is read off the Stage: Stage VII ⇒
Tier of Standing 5 · Expert (Part Five) ⇒ **η 0.60–0.70** (Part Nineteen, Tier 5 row as corrected by
**R44-4**), rising to **0.70–0.80** at
Tier 6 if the page's ascent to Stage VIII is taken. **Thirty to forty percent leaves as heat, sound and
structural bleed** at the floor — and on an acoustic working, bleed that leaves *as sound* is the one
thing it cannot afford, because sound is the signal. **The Ribbon Shell is the only working in this
batch for which the bleed fraction is directly and mechanically adverse**, and it is the physical
content of *"Ribbon Fray."* Part Nineteen's Tier 6 note is the reward for the ascent: *"First true
Domain, which provides a feedback loop recycling ambient bleed."*

## 5 · Counterplay and the challenge

**The fairness check** (`.claude/skills/wotr-write/references/fair-play.md`).

| Test | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Costs something that hurts | **pass, and it is charged in the instrument** | *"Overriding conscience to cut can cost temporary voice loss; post-hearing overuse causes double-hearing that clears only with rest."* A voice working billed in voice and hearing. And the deeper cost is continuous rather than per-use: *"Range and clarity both key to the truth of what is carried; curated or withheld speech measurably frays the signal."* **The practitioner pays for every managed sentence, in range.** Unpriced in EU (`SA-GAP-EU-FORMULA`). |
| Stated limits | **pass, and they are the strongest set in the batch** | It needs a living voice and breath; **nothing sustains once the caster stops breathing the pattern**; an echo-dead space starves it; Hush holds only to about thirty knots; and the Limitation is explicit that *"it does not compel anyone to speak"* the truth. |

Neither requires a Crystal.** |

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Break the boundary — the anchor, and this is the one working above Refraction where it still
  works.** *"Below Refraction a working requires an external anchor: voice, hand, ink or blood. Take
  the anchor and the sentence does not complete … Put a hand over his mouth and a spoken one is."*
  Stage VII is above the Law V gate, so the Stage does not require an anchor — **but the medium
  does**, and the page says so: *"It needs a living voice and breath behind it."* The page's own
  Counter names the answer in the register's own terms: *"a silent grappler who never lets it
  establish a corridor."* **Grapple him.**
- **Deny the field — an echo-dead room, which is the page's own Limit and a Field fault by the
  book.** *"The room could not supply what the working assumed … The working is correct and produces
  nothing"* (`Two Sets of Books` §VII). An anechoic vault, a snowfield, deep soft ground, a hold full
  of bales. **Not on any Family suppression list, which makes it the page's own contribution to the
  terrain register.**
- **Deny the field — the two Families want opposite rooms.** Fulguria fails against
  *"Faraday-caged interiors, heavy ferrous shielding, dense fog"*; Limina fails in *"Saturated Aether,
  **crowd density**, active Domains"*. **A shielded interior full of people costs him both currents at
  once**, and *"Terrain is not flavour. It is a modifier on η."* Crowd density is the cruel one,
  because a Hushfield's whole purpose is a room with people in it.
- **Break the man — Coherence drift, and here it is same-scene rather than same-career.** *"A
  practitioner whose lived behaviour has drifted from his stated law is degrading before anyone lays a
  hand on him … **You do not have to cause this. You have to notice it and wait.**"* Ribbon Fray is
  that, compressed. **Make him lie.** Give him something he must not say, put him in a position where
  he must curate, and his range falls and his edge dulls *"exactly when it is needed most"* — by the
  page's own Weakness. This is the cruellest counter available to this working and it costs an
  opponent nothing but a question.
- **Break the man — Rigidity, via the Cut's own restraint.** *"Present him with the situation his own
  law handles worst and he will handle it that way regardless."* The Cut *"cuts cloth and cord without
  cutting flesh"*, and overriding that costs voice. **A working built not to cut people is a working
  whose practitioner can be forced to choose between mutism and inaction**, and an opponent who
  understands that will present exactly that choice.
- **Break the body — the ordinary answers.** *"Shot"*, at any range beyond the corridor, and
  *"Numbers, correctly used"*, since a Hushfield is a benefit to whoever is inside it and cannot be
  everywhere. The working has no defensive component at all.

**The tell, stated plainly.** Someone is speaking, and the air between you and them is not behaving
like air: dust holds a line, pressure sits on the chest in bands, sound from elsewhere thins. The
corridor is metres across because it has to be, so it is not a subtle thing. And if the working is
running well, the most identifying tell is the absence of one: **the room has gone steady.**

# System account · Sigillum Fixatio

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** A word and a closing fist, and one named thing within ten metres
stops changing. It does not move, it does not heal, and for nine seconds nothing about it can be
edited — including by the person who put it there.

**The real phenomenon.** **Kinetic trapping: holding a configuration by raising the barrier out of it
rather than by applying force to it.** The page's own Wellspring states this correctly and the page's
Numerical Effect then prices it as though it were a clamp, which is where the account finds its
largest conflict. But the physics underneath is sound, and it answers the page's own open question
with arithmetic.

**(a) Nine seconds is 83 kilojoules per mole.** Every spontaneous transition out of a state is
thermally activated and follows the Arrhenius law, **k = A·exp(−E_a/RT)**, with an attempt frequency
A on the order of **10¹³ s⁻¹** (a molecular vibration) and RT at body temperature of **8.314 × 310 =
2.58 kJ/mol.** Invert it for a residence time. To hold a configuration for **τ = 9 s**, the page's
stated duration:

> **E_a = RT · ln(A·τ) = 2.58 × ln(9×10¹³) = 2.58 × 32.1 ≈ 83 kJ/mol.**

**That is the whole cost of the working in one number**, and it is a modest number: a hydrogen bond is
20 kJ/mol, a C–C covalent bond is 348. Eighty-three sits where enzymes work.

**(b) And the page's open question is the same equation, run twice.** *"Why an error placed under
Fixatio, rather than dispelled, becomes a permanent feature of whatever it was placed on rather than
decaying like other failed workings."* Escape time is **exponential** in the barrier, so permanence is
not a different kind of binding. It is the same binding, slightly higher:

| Residence time | Barrier required at 310 K |
|---|---|
| 9 seconds | **83 kJ/mol** |
| 1 hour | **94 kJ/mol** |
| 1 year | **122 kJ/mol** |
| 1,000 years | **139 kJ/mol** |

**A forty-seven percent increase in the barrier converts nine seconds into a year**, and a further
fourteen percent converts that year into a millennium. The Wellspring's Failure line — *"The cure is
not undone by wanting it undone, and a Fixatio binding placed in error is a permanent feature of
whatever it was placed on"* — is therefore not a curse and not a separate mechanic. **It is what a
logarithm looks like from inside.** A caster who overshoots by a few tens of kilojoules per mole has
not made a stronger lock; they have made a permanent one, and nothing in the moment of casting would
feel different.

**(c) The thermoset analogy is exact, including the part the page leaves out.** Fixatio's analogue is
*"Covalent cross-linking. Thermoset cure"*
(`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Materia — Material Science.md`). The real chemistry:

- **Gelation is a percolation transition, not a gradual stiffening.** Flory–Stockmayer puts the gel
  point of an f-functional system at a critical conversion **p_c = 1/(f − 1)** — for a trifunctional
  network, at one half of the bonds formed. Below p_c the material is a liquid of finite clusters;
  **at p_c a single connected cluster spans the whole sample**, and from that instant the material has
  an infinite molecule in it and can no longer flow. *"The alternative configuration no longer exists
  as a reachable state"* is a percolation threshold, and it is crossed instantaneously.
- **Cured networks are stiff and brittle**, not tough. Rubber elasticity gives modulus from crosslink
  density, **E = 3νRT**: more bridges, higher modulus, lower strain to failure. Glassy thermosets
  yield at a few percent strain and then fracture rather than yielding further.
- **They char instead of melting**, which is why the register says so.

**So the page's Weakness is the analogue's own mechanical signature:** *"The lock is brittle rather
than resilient. It is built to hold a single configuration against drift, not to absorb concentrated
shearing or torsional force."* A cross-linked network resists creep beautifully and fails in shear at
small strain. **The page's counter is not a genre convention. It is what happens to thermosets.**

**(d) "Stops healing" is the half that works perfectly, and it works because life is barriers.**
Biology does not run on energy availability; it runs on catalysis. Orotidine 5′-monophosphate
decarboxylase accelerates its reaction by a factor of about **10¹⁷**, taking an uncatalysed half-life
of **78 million years** down to milliseconds. Raise the barrier — or equivalently, remove the
catalysis — and the chemistry does not break, it simply stops happening. **A body under Fixatio is not
injured by the lock. It is only unattended**, and the page's *"its wounds stop worsening and stop
healing"* is exactly the symmetric consequence: degradation is enzymatic too.

**(e) And that same fact says something the page does not.** A barrier does not know which reactions
are repair. Myosin ATPase, the sodium pump and oxidative phosphorylation are all thermally activated
catalysis, and a barrier raised across the target's chemistry stops those as well. **Nine seconds
without cerebral perfusion is the standard syncope interval — 6 to 10 seconds** — so a locked body
released at second nine is a body that has been at the edge of losing consciousness, and a lock held
much longer is not stasis but asphyxia. Nothing on the page states whether the cure is selective,
and there is no physical basis for a barrier that rises for healing and not for heartbeat. Logged.

**(f) The forces do not work, and the page contains both readings of why.** The Numerical Effect:
*"the lock resists roughly 0.1 to 0.3 gigantonewtons of applied peak force."* Price the object it is
holding:

- A femur fails in compression at about **4–10 kN.** Cortical bone's tensile strength is around
  **130 MPa**, so a 5 cm² cross-section parts at roughly **65 kN.**
- **0.1 GN is 10⁸ N: four orders of magnitude above the failure load of the thing being held.** A lock
  rated to resist a hundred meganewtons is holding a body that has been torn apart at one ten-thousandth
  of that.
- The kinetic energy in question is smaller still. A charging 80 kg man at 8 m/s carries **2.6 kJ.**
  The page's stated energy band opens at 7 MJ. **The lock is priced at roughly three thousand times
  the kinetic energy of the thing it stops.**

**The page's FOW line already knows this and says the opposite of its own Numerical Effect:**
*"Vitality (Fortitude) governs how much force the locked target's frame can take before the bond
parts."* That is the correct physics — the binding constraint is the target's own material strength,
not the lock's rating — and it contradicts a Numerical Effect that reads Part Eleven's C-Grade **attack
output** row as a restraint specification. Logged.

**(g) Immobilisation and non-reactivity are two different physics under one Mechanism.** The Mechanism
says *"What it holds is not held by force."* That statement is true for everything thermally activated
(healing, corrosion, drift, decay, an unattended working's parameters wandering) and **false for
everything driven**. A barrier of 83 kJ/mol is 32 RT; a sword blow delivering 100 J to a limb is
**10⁴ times** the per-mole barrier expressed per bond, and an 80 kg man's own quadriceps produce
kilonewtons. **Against muscle and against a hammer, a barrier height is not a defence** — you need
either force or a network whose bonds must themselves be broken, which is the thermoset reading and
is a different claim from the reachability one. The page's Effect asks for both: *"stops moving, stops
healing, and stops taking new damage easily."* Only the middle term follows from the stated mechanism.
Logged.

**The governing law.** **Arrhenius**, with the corollary that matters: because residence time is
exponential in barrier height, a lock's duration is exquisitely sensitive to a quantity nobody can
feel while setting it. Secondarily **Flory–Stockmayer percolation**, which makes the transition into
the held state a threshold crossing rather than a squeeze.

**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Range **10 m**, line of sight; **one** Sigillum at a time; duration **≈9 s** (page).
- Barrier for a 9 s residence at 310 K: **≈83 kJ/mol**; for a year, **≈122 kJ/mol**; for a millennium,
  **≈139 kJ/mol.** Attempt frequency **10¹³ s⁻¹**; RT = **2.58 kJ/mol.**
- Reference barriers: hydrogen bond **20 kJ/mol** · C–C covalent **348 kJ/mol.**
- Gel point, trifunctional network: **p_c = 1/(f−1) = 0.5** conversion.
- Enzymatic rate enhancement, OMP decarboxylase: **10¹⁷**, i.e. 78 million years → milliseconds.
- Femur failure **4–10 kN**; cortical bone tensile strength **≈130 MPa**; 5 cm² section **≈65 kN.**
- Page's stated lock rating **0.1–0.3 GN**, **7 MJ – 1.046 GJ**; Part Eleven's C row is **0.1–0.3 GN,
  20.92 MJ – 1.046 GJ.** **The page's energy floor is three times below the row it is quoting.**
- Kinetic energy of a charging 80 kg man at 8 m/s: **2.6 kJ.**
- Syncope interval without cerebral perfusion: **6–10 s.**

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** **Almost nowhere, and that is the point.** Raising a
barrier is not work done on the target; it is a change in the shape of the landscape the target is
sitting in, and the target does not move, so no work is done on it at all. `Two Sets of Books`: *"The
practitioner does not supply the joules. They supply the boundary condition."* **Sigillum Fixatio is
the cleanest illustration of that sentence in the batch** — the working's energetic footprint is the
Crystal's own expenditure and the bleed that leaves it, and the held body's joules stay exactly where
they were. Which is also why the page's gigantonewton rating is so jarring: a working that does no
work has been given a force specification.

**Precedent we are not copying.** Time stop, Hold Person, JoJo's *Za Warudo*, Gojo's Infinity,
paralysis status effects without number. Those are **the removal of the target's turn.** This one is a
**cure**: the held thing is not frozen in time, it is chemically finished, and the divergence is the
one the Wellspring insists on — an error under Fixatio does not expire, it becomes part of whatever it
was placed on. The genre's stasis is temporary by default. This one is permanent by default and
temporary only because the caster is paying to keep the barrier low enough to decay.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** A small, sharply bounded draw at ten metres, and Materia is the Family with the
most opinionated ground in the register: favoured by *"Mineral-rich rock, worked stone, deep
foundation"*, suppressed by *"Open water, high altitude, moving platforms"*
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings.md`, Environmental Coupling). The
Counterplay register is blunter: *"Materia has opinions and no tools on open water."* **A Sigillum
cast on a ship, a bridge under load or a moving cart is suppressed by the ground it stands on**, and
the page never says so. *"Terrain is not flavour. It is a modifier on η."*

**Residue.** The most persistent in the batch. Every other working's residue fades; this one's
**cures**. *"Aetheric Residue … Always readable by someone patient enough"* (`The Core Vocabulary`
§II) understates it — a Fixatio residue is not a readable trace but a structural feature, and the
register says an error placed under it is *"a permanent feature of whatever it was placed on."* A
building that has been held once has been held forever at the margins of the hold.

**Saturation.** Not a limit on output. It is a limit on **reuse**: one Sigillum at a time, by the
page's own Limit, and the account can say why that is the right rule — a second barrier raised in the
same Crystal is a second landscape held against its own relaxation, and the Cost line is the
practitioner discovering that they are now part of the network.

**Wellspring stratum — Fixatio, *The Binding Flame*, Materia, Material Science.** Sole Wellspring;
this is one of the few single-current workings in the batch. Core Law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Materia — Material Science.md`:

> **Mechanism** · A thermoplastic can be melted and reformed indefinitely. A thermoset, once cured,
> has formed covalent bridges across its whole network and will char before it flows. Fixatio performs
> the cure. What it holds is not held by force but by the fact that the alternative configuration no
> longer exists as a reachable state.

Stat Effect: *"Strengthens Vitality Fortitude, Resilience Anchoring. What is held by Fixatio stays
held."* Failure: *"Irreversibility. The cure is not undone by wanting it undone, and a Fixatio binding
placed in error is a permanent feature of whatever it was placed on."*

**Which of the Four Theories.** **Residue**, unambiguously and almost uniquely. The other three
doctrines describe transactions that close: consent is given, debts are settled, correspondences hold
while both ends do. Residue describes what a working leaves behind that no longer answers to anyone,
and Fixatio's Failure line is the Residue doctrine stated as chemistry. The page's open question is a
Residue question and the account answers it in §1(b).

**Essence stratum.** Crystal layer: the **Aether Shell**, on both ends — the target's configuration is
held at the Shell boundary, and the caster's Shell locks in sympathy, which is the page's Cost.
Developmental Tier at Stage IV: **Harmonic** is Stage V and above, so Stage IV sits below the first
named Developmental Tier the batch has used; Part Twenty-One is the authority and the account does not
extrapolate. Tier of Standing **4 · Adept**, η **≈0.45** — *"The last tier at which more than half of
every expenditure is lost"* (Part Nineteen). Crystal State: `null`, no practitioner named. **The
invoice:** nine seconds of the caster's own mobility and attention, paid in the same currency the
target pays — the caster's Shell is also, briefly, a cured network.

**The lens: Parmenides on what is.** Parmenides of Elea, *On Nature*, fragment B8: what is, is
ungenerated and imperishable, whole, unmoving and complete; *"it is all alike, nor is it more here and
less there"*; coming-to-be is extinguished and passing-away unheard of. Parmenides' argument is not
mysticism but a logical claim taken to its end: if change requires that what is not should come to be,
and what is not cannot be spoken or thought, then change cannot be what it appears to be. **Sigillum
Fixatio makes a ten-metre Eleatic region.** Inside the sigil, for nine seconds, becoming is genuinely
unavailable and only being remains — not because the target is restrained but because, as the
Wellspring says, the alternative *"no longer exists as a reachable state."* The lens is exact rather
than decorative: Parmenides' complaint about change is that it needs a path from what-is to what-is-
not, and a raised barrier is precisely the removal of that path.

**The productive misreading, and what it kills.** The Eleatic Fixatists read the sigil as evidence
rather than as a working: if change can be shown to be unavailable *here*, then change was never real
*anywhere*, and what the rest of the world calls motion is a confusion of the senses. Their practice
follows the doctrine and is impressive to watch — they do not flinch, they do not withdraw, and they
hold positions that other schools abandon, because to them the position was never going to change. It
kills them at **second ten.** A doctrine in which nothing truly changes has no account of a duration,
so a Fixatist does not plan for the lock ending, does not count, and treats a held enemy as a settled
question. It also leaves them with nothing to say about the working's own Failure line, since a school
that holds all being to be already complete cannot distinguish a nine-second hold from a permanent
one — which is, embarrassingly for everyone else, the one place their doctrine is closer to the
arithmetic than the technique page is.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**The glyph, proposed.** `[Lk]` **Lock**, one of the two Counter forms the Index carries and one of the
thirty entries the repo mirror holds, with `[Ho]` **Order** as the alternative filing for the physical
half. The Index's Standing Note governs: *"Assignment is decided after the phenomenon, never before,
and it is a filing decision rather than a generative one."* **The page names no glyph**
(`SA-GAP-GLYPH-MIRROR`). The Counter reading is unusually literal here, because the Counterplay
register defines Lock as the form that *"occupies a limit so no other limit can be set there"*
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`) — and the page's metaphysical mode
is exactly that: an active working whose parameters *"cannot be altered or dispelled for the
duration."* Nothing is opposed. The limit is simply already taken.

**What boundary moves.** Not position, not velocity, not the target's will. **The height of the
barrier out of the state the target is currently in.** The Index: *"A glyph creates nothing and carries
no power. … It moves a limit, and the law does what it has always done on the far side of the new
limit."* The limit moved is E_a, by about eighty-three kilojoules per mole, and every law in the
target keeps running unchanged on the far side of it.

**What the law then does on its own.** Arrhenius does the rest, with no further input. Transition
rates out of the held configuration fall by **exp(−ΔE_a/RT)** — a factor of roughly **10¹⁴** at the
stated barrier — so every process whose escape depended on thermal agitation stops finding the door.
Clotting stops progressing. Inflammation stops advancing. Corrosion, creep, drift and decay stop.
An active working's parameters, which wander because they are maintained rather than fixed, stop
wandering, and cannot be re-set by their own author, because re-setting them is itself a transition
out of the held state. **And the network crosses its gel point on the way in**: not a squeeze but a
threshold, a single connected cluster spanning the target in one step, which is why the page's
Trigger is a fist closing rather than a fist tightening.

**Then the bleed.** At η ≈ 0.45 the Adept tier loses **more than half** of the expenditure as *"heat,
sound, and structural bleed"* (`Two Sets of Books` §III, Part Nineteen). A working that performs no
work on its target dumps most of its cost into the caster's own Shell and the air at the anchor point,
and the anchor point is where the page's counter lives.

**The failure mode.** Two, in order of likelihood. **Irreversibility** — the Wellspring's own, and
§1(b) shows it is a few tens of kilojoules per mole away at all times, invisible at the moment of
casting. **Brittle fracture at the anchor** — a cured network's characteristic failure, at small
strain, under shear or torsion rather than under load. The Four Faults reading (`Two Sets of Books`
§VII) is **Structural**: the Crystal has permitted a boundary it can hold but not release.

**What the target sees and feels.** A word, *"Esto"*, and a hand closing. Then: nothing moves, and the
nothing is total in a way that is immediately distinguishable from being held down. There is no
pressure. No grip. No sensation of restraint anywhere on the body, because nothing is touching it and
no force is being applied — **the target simply finds that the movement they are making is not
available**, the way a word can be on the tip of the tongue and unreachable. Pain that was worsening
stops worsening; a bleed that was running slows to nothing and does not clot. Breath is the strange
part: the chest does not fight the lock, it declines to be asked, and the grey at the edges of vision
arrives on the ordinary schedule of six to ten seconds without perfusion. A practitioner holding an
active working feels it go **quiet** — not suppressed, not drained, but finished, as though the
working had been completed by somebody else and handed back. Nine seconds. Then the barrier drops and
everything resumes from exactly where it was, including the bleed, which is the detail survivors
report: nothing was healed, nothing was worsened, and the wound is precisely as old as it was.

## 5 · Counterplay and the challenge

**The fairness check.**

| Test | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Costs something that hurts | **pass, and it is the best-designed cost in the batch** | *"The caster's own Shell locks in sympathy: no blink-type or phase movement for the duration, and their own attention narrows around the bound target until it is difficult to redirect."* It is the same effect applied to the caster, it lasts exactly as long, and it removes the two things a caster most needs in a fight — mobility and situational awareness — at the moment they have committed to a single target. **The cost is a tactical opening rather than a bill posted later**, which is the register's own complaint about costs: *"Costs are real and they are rarely due inside a scene."* This one is due inside the scene. |
| Stated limits | **pass, exemplary** | Ten metres; line of sight; one target; **one Sigillum at a time**; nine seconds; nothing two or more Stages above the caster; nothing without a discrete physical or Aetheric form — *"no Domain, no Wellspring current, no abstraction."* Five limits, all checkable, and the Stage clause means the working scales down against superiors rather than up. |
| Something beats it | **pass, four routes and three need no practitioner** | Shearing or torsional force at the anchor; staggering, dazing or silencing the caster; a Fractura strike or a Dissolution working; and Valmir's entropic domain in the rare case a caster invites it. The register adds the Family answer and the second Lock. |
| It has a tell | **pass** | A spoken word, *"Esto"*, and a hand closing into a fist, aimed. Both anchors are public and both are the tell. |
| Numbers in band | **FAIL, on three counts** | The energy floor misquotes Part Eleven's C row by a factor of three (`SA-NUM-FIXATIO-C-FLOOR`); the lock rating is an attack-output figure used as a restraint rating, four orders above what the held frame survives (`SA-NUM-FIXATIO-LOCK-VS-FRAME`); and **Gnosis Fluency at C-Grade requires Spirit Path** while the page declares no Path gate at all (`SA-NUM-FIXATIO-PATHGATE`). The Stage and Grade themselves are consistent and roomy. |

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Break the Boundary — "Interrupt the chain."** *"Below Refraction a working requires an external
  anchor: voice, hand, ink or blood. … Break his wrist and a gestured chain is finished. Put a hand
  over his mouth and a spoken one is."* This working is **Stage IV with both anchors**, spoken and
  gestured, and either one ends it. The page's own counter — *"breaking the caster's concentration
  (stagger, daze, a Silence field)"* — is the same route reached by a different door.
- **Break the Boundary — "Knot and Lock."** *"Lock occupies a limit so no other limit can be set
  there."* **A limit already occupied cannot be occupied again**, so a Lock placed first on the thing
  the caster intends to hold denies the Sigillum its purchase, and the register says every
  counter-working ever recorded is one of these two moves.
- **Break the Body — "Numbers, correctly used."** *"Twenty men who have been drilled to spread, to
  make him choose, and to keep a line of fire open at all times will take casualties and kill him."*
  **The page's Cost is this counter written out**: a caster whose attention has narrowed around one
  bound target and who cannot blink or phase is a caster who has already chosen, and cannot choose
  again for nine seconds.
- **Deny the Field.** Materia is suppressed by *"Open water, high altitude, moving platforms"*, and
  the register is blunter: *"Materia has opinions and no tools on open water."* **Fight him on a boat.**
- **"What Does Not Work."** *"Matching him. Two practitioners at the same Grade resolve by technique,
  positioning and Sub-Stat architecture."* Answering a Sigillum with a counter-Sigillum is the coin
  toss the register warns about, and the Limit line already forecloses the tempting version: nothing
  two or more Stages above the caster can be held at all.
- **Attested, and the page is right to flag it as rare.** *"Titan Valmir's entropic domain unmakes the
  anchoring absolutely."* Valmir is the Titan who *"decides when it has endured enough to be permitted
  to end"* (`wiki/The Fourteen Titans/The Strata — Valmir, Kaldrion, Chronaeus.md`), and the register
  notes that Titan-keyed arts sit *"at the absolute outer edge of what a mortal Crystal can survive
  holding."* This is a real route and not a usable one.

**The tell, plainly.** A Latin imperative and a fist. Both are required, both are visible at ten metres,
and the second of them points at whoever is about to be held — which is the rare tell that warns the
**target** rather than the room.

# System account · Solarbound Aegoric Knight

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** A man takes blows and carries a duty, and neither goes anywhere. It
accumulates in him. At some later moment he spends the whole accumulation on one act that cannot be
taken back, and afterwards he is empty.

**The real phenomenon.** **Metastable energy storage behind a kinetic barrier, discharged by an
ignition event.** This is not a metaphor for the discipline; it is the same physics its own primary
Wellspring runs on, read in the other direction, and the account can be unusually precise as a result.

**(a) Fixatio's cure is a latch, and a latch is how every fuel in the world is stored.** The Sigillum
account derived the barrier arithmetic from the same Wellspring: a configuration held against
spontaneous escape has a residence time **τ = (1/A)·exp(E_a/RT)**, with A ≈ 10¹³ s⁻¹ and RT = 2.58
kJ/mol at body temperature, so **83 kJ/mol holds for nine seconds and 122 kJ/mol holds for a year.**
Run it as storage rather than as restraint and it becomes the most ordinary fact in chemistry:
**petrol is metastable in air.** A litre of it carries 34 MJ and sits in an open can indefinitely,
not because the energy is absent but because the barrier to releasing it is higher than the room can
supply. **An ignition source does not add the energy. It supplies E_a.**

**That is the discipline, stated in physical terms.** The Strain Lamina is a kinetic trap. The Mercy
Lamina is the barrier height. The oath is the ignition. And the page's insistence that the release
*"does not reverse"* is not a rule imposed on the working — **it is what discharging a metastable
store means.** Nothing that burns un-burns.

**(b) But the store has to be somewhere, and the energy densities are brutal.** The page gives its
output as *"A-Grade at Stage VII's ceiling to S-Grade at Stage X's"*, and Part Eleven prices those:
**46 GJ – 4.184 TJ** at A, **4.184 – 24.3 TJ** at S. Put that in the only vessel the page names, the
practitioner's own Shell, and compare it with real stores, per kilogram of an 80 kg man:

| Store | Energy density |
|---|---|
| Elastic strain in spring steel (σ = 1 GPa, E = 200 GPa) | **≈320 J/kg** |
| Supercapacitor | **≈20 kJ/kg** |
| Lithium-ion cell | **≈0.9 MJ/kg** |
| TNT | **4.6 MJ/kg** |
| Petrol | **44 MJ/kg** |
| **This discipline at A-Grade (46 GJ in 80 kg)** | **575 MJ/kg — 13× petrol** |
| **This discipline at S-Grade (24.3 TJ in 80 kg)** | **304 GJ/kg — 6,900× petrol** |

**So the "stored radiant weight" a Solarbound knight carries is, at its floor, thirteen times the
energy density of the fuel it would take to level the building he is standing in**, and at its apex
it is six orders past anything chemistry can hold. It is still far below mass–energy — 24.3 TJ is
about **three parts per million** of an 80 kg man's rest energy, so nothing here breaches c² — but it
is decisively outside every material store, which is a real statement about where the joules must be
sitting. They are not in his tissue. They are at the Aether stratum, which is exactly what
`Two Sets of Books` says: *"a working's energy budget balances at the Aether stratum and not at the
Essence one."*

**(c) The absorption half does not balance, and the reason is bleed during storage.** The discipline
*"absorbs strain, moral weight, and impact"*. Price the impact honestly: a charging armoured man
delivers **2–3 kJ**; a fall from ten metres onto a shield is **8 kJ**; a full day of being hit
repeatedly in a line of battle might total **10⁵ J** if every blow landed and every joule were caught.
**That is six orders of magnitude short of the A-Grade release the page claims**, so the store cannot
be the sum of what was absorbed — the absorbed impact is a **trigger and a measure**, not the fuel,
and the page does not say so.

And then the bleed. At Stage VII the practitioner sits at Tier 5, **η 0.50–0.60**, so *"forty to fifty
percent"* of every expenditure leaves *"as heat, sound, and structural bleed"* (`Two Sets of Books`
§III, Part Nineteen). **If the store is charged through the Shell at that efficiency, charging a 46 GJ
release costs roughly 38 GJ of waste heat** — eleven tonnes of TNT of it, delivered slowly, into a man
and the room around him. Nothing on the page acknowledges a thermal signature, and a knight who bled
that much while filling would be visible from another county. **Either the store is charged losslessly
and the bleed is paid only at release, or the discipline is a furnace.** The system states no rule for
a working that stores rather than spends, and this is the batch's clearest case of a needed rule that
does not exist. Logged.

**(d) Judicium is doing verdict as measurement, and it is the same instrument as the Ribbon Shell's.**
The register: *"Every element absorbs and emits at wavelengths fixed by its own electron structure,
and no substance can present another's spectrum … Eddy-current inspection finds the crack under the
paint without removing the paint"*
(`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Fulguria — Electromagnetism.md`). The real depth law
is **skin depth, δ = √(2/ωμσ)** — a few tenths of a millimetre in steel at 100 kHz, several millimetres
at 1 kHz — which is why nondestructive inspection is a surface art that reaches inward by lowering its
frequency. **A verdict, in this discipline, is a flaw detection**: the release *"finalises the released
act as a verdict rather than a strike"*, and what makes it a verdict rather than an opinion is that
the reading is characteristic and cannot be counterfeited. **No substance can present another's
spectrum.**

**The governing law.** **Arrhenius run backwards** — storage is a barrier, discharge is the barrier
crossed, and the irreversibility is not a moral property but the second law noticing that a metastable
state has relaxed. With, at the release, **the first law's usual demand**: whatever comes out was in
there, and the account has just shown the vessel cannot be chemical.

**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Output band: **46 GJ – 4.184 TJ** (A-Grade) to **4.184 – 24.3 TJ** (S-Grade), Part Eleven, from the
  page's Stage and Grade range. **The page itself states no figure of any kind.**
- Barrier arithmetic, from the shared Fixatio derivation: **τ = (1/A)exp(E_a/RT)**; **83 kJ/mol ⇒ 9 s**,
  **122 kJ/mol ⇒ 1 year.**
- Energy densities: strain in spring steel **320 J/kg** · supercapacitor **20 kJ/kg** · Li-ion
  **0.9 MJ/kg** · TNT **4.6 MJ/kg** · petrol **44 MJ/kg** · **this discipline 575 MJ/kg to 304 GJ/kg.**
- Rest energy of an 80 kg man: **7.2×10¹⁸ J**; the S-Grade store is **3.4 ppm** of it.
- Absorbable impact, honestly counted: charge **2–3 kJ** · ten-metre fall **8 kJ** · a hard day in the
  line **≈10⁵ J.** **Six orders short of the release.**
- Bleed while charging at η 0.55: **≈0.82 J per joule stored** ⇒ **≈38 GJ** for a 46 GJ release.
- Eddy-current skin depth: **δ = √(2/ωμσ)**, sub-millimetre in steel at 100 kHz.

**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** **At the Aether stratum, and nowhere else it could
be.** The page's own framing is the correct one and it is unusual in the batch for saying so out loud:
this is *"a Shell discipline built around storage rather than expression"*, and §1(b) shows that no
material vessel in the practitioner's body could hold what the Grade range implies. The practitioner
supplies the barrier — the Mercy Lamina, the restraint that *"favours mercy until judgment is
deliberately chosen"* — and the Continuum holds the store. What the knight is actually carrying is a
boundary condition he has to keep imposing, every hour, on something that would rather relax.

**Precedent we are not copying.** Charge-and-release is a genre staple: the Kamehameha's wind-up,
Ichigo's Final Getsuga, a paladin's smite slots, every "power gauge" in every fighting game, Guts
bearing more than a body should. Those are **accumulators that fill by fighting and empty on demand.**
This one's store is **gated by a vow rather than by a meter**, and the divergence is the page's own
harshest clause: *"Purity and output both suffer when the practitioner fights without a cause they
have actually sworn to."* A gauge that will not fill for a mercenary is not a gauge. It is a
prerequisite disguised as a resource, and it means the discipline's power is decided before the fight
by a question about the man.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** Three Families at once — **Materia**, favoured by *"Mineral-rich rock, worked
stone, deep foundation"* and suppressed by *"Open water, high altitude, moving platforms"*;
**Vitalia**, favoured by *"Living biomass, temperate conditions, proximity to blood"* and suppressed by
*"Sterile ground, extreme cold, Essence-depleted soil"*; **Fulguria**, favoured by *"Dry air,
conductive substrate, clear line of sight"* and suppressed by *"Faraday-caged interiors, heavy ferrous
shielding, dense fog"*
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings.md`, Environmental Coupling).
*"Terrain is not flavour. It is a modifier on η."* **Three Families is three ways to be suppressed,
and one room suppresses all three at once**: a cold, shielded, moving deck. The account does not
elaborate; the lookup trail does.

**Residue.** The standing kind. The discipline's Traitus layer is *"a passive, permanent bias, not
something cast"*, and a standing bias is residue that has been made structural — which is precisely
what `wiki/Spellcraft/Anointing.md` means by *"a Traitus-category standing effect rather than a
sustained working, so it persists after the officiant's attention moves on."* A Solarbound knight is
legible as one when he is doing nothing at all.

**Saturation, and the ceiling nobody has measured.** Three harmonizations across three Families is the
heaviest conduit load in the batch, and the ceiling is stated and unquantified: *"The ceiling is
**Tempering Capacity** and it is not negotiable. Exceeding it does not fail gracefully by dropping the
newest harmonization. **It degrades all active harmonizations equally**, which is how practitioners
lose a decade of work by reaching for one more current in a bad hour."* No figure exists for any
practitioner, and no practitioner is named here.

**Wellspring stratum — Fixatio, *The Binding Flame*, Materia, Material Science.** Core Law, verbatim
from `wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Materia — Material Science.md`:

> **Mechanism** · A thermoplastic can be melted and reformed indefinitely. A thermoset, once cured,
> has formed covalent bridges across its whole network and will char before it flows. Fixatio performs
> the cure. What it holds is not held by force but by the fact that the alternative configuration no
> longer exists as a reachable state.

Failure: *"Irreversibility. The cure is not undone by wanting it undone, and a Fixatio binding placed
in error is a permanent feature of whatever it was placed on."* **In a storage discipline this
failure is not a hazard at the edges. It is the thing being relied on**, and the page's *"a single
act that does not reverse"* is the register's sentence with the knight's name on it.

**Wellspring stratum — Benediction, Vitalia, Biochemistry.** Core Law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Vitalia — Biochemistry.md`:

> **Mechanism** · The strength of a biological bond is a measurable physical quantity, and a low
> dissociation constant means the complex, once formed, spends almost all of its time formed.
> Benediction gives bonds on the Plane of Fate that same measurability. This is the mechanical reason
> an oath sworn under Benediction carries system weight: it has an affinity figure, and the Continuum
> reads it.

**This is the clause that makes the Oath Lamina a mechanic rather than a sentiment.** The page's
*"amplify sharply in the presence of banners, seals, or spoken oaths"* is the affinity figure being
read; an unsworn knight has nothing for the Continuum to price, which is why the page says output
suffers without a cause. Benediction's failure, competitive inhibition, is the unstated hazard: a vow
with a higher affinity displaces an older one silently, and *"the displacement is … not felt until the
bond is called on."* **A knight can be re-sworn without noticing.**

**Wellspring stratum — Judicium, Fulguria, Electromagnetism.** Core Law quoted in §1(d). Failure:
*"Spectral overload. Every object in view reports its composition simultaneously and the practitioner
loses the ability to prioritise any of it"* — the occupational hazard of a discipline whose apex act is
a verdict.

**Which of the Four Theories.** **Consent Doctrine**, enforced, and the page is built on it without
using the word. The Four Glyph Roles make it structural: Authorization *"requires genuine
harmonization; the Continuum reads the Attraction Layer at the moment of casting"*, and its absence
produces *"Mechanica by definition. Coerced transformation without lawful Wellspring participation.
The Continuum treats it as debt"* (`wiki/Disciplines/The Disciplines.md`). A discipline whose output
is gated on a cause actually sworn is a discipline whose Authorization role is doing the gating. The
**Debt** doctrine is live at the other end, in the store that seizes the Shell if it is never spent.

**Essence stratum.** Crystal layer: the **Aether Shell** throughout — the page says so, *"a Shell
discipline"* — with the Essence Core touched only at the Traitus layer, where the bias is permanent.
Developmental Tier: **Resonant** at Stage VII through **Sovereign** at Stage IX–X (Part Twenty-One).
Tier of Standing **5 · Expert** at the floor, **6 · Master** from Stage VIII, η 0.50–0.60 rising to
0.70–0.80. Crystal State: `null`. **The invoice:** a permanent bias in the Shell, a barrier that has
to be maintained for as long as the store is held, and a release that cannot be partial — the knight
does not get to spend half.

**The lens: Buridan's impetus.** Jean Buridan, *Quaestiones super libris quattuor de caelo et mundo*
(c. 1350), developing Philoponus. Against Aristotle's account of projectile motion — in which the air
must keep pushing the arrow — Buridan proposed that the mover **impresses a quality into the moved
body**: impetus, proportional to the quantity of matter and the speed given, which the body then
carries within itself and which is spent only as resistance consumes it. It is wrong as physics and
it is the most productive wrong idea in medieval natural philosophy, because it is the direct ancestor
of momentum and it gets the essential thing right: **motion can be carried rather than continuously
supplied.** The Solarbound discipline is impetus made a doctrine of the soul. Strain, impact and moral
load are impressed; the knight carries them as a quality rather than as a memory; and the store is
spent only when something is placed in its way.

**The productive misreading, and what it kills.** Buridan's own extension was that celestial bodies
keep turning because nothing resists them: impetus, unopposed, **never decays.** A school that takes
this literally — the Impetists, who hold that an oath once impressed is eternal and that spending it
is a failure of faith — will store and store and never release. Their discipline is outwardly the
purest: they do not strike, they do not boast, they bear. And the page states what happens to them in
one sentence: *"Storing past what the Mercy Lamina can hold seizes the Shell outright, and the only
safe release is a deliberate oath-bleed … never an involuntary vent."* **An Impetist dies full**, in
a seizure of his own Shell, at a moment chosen by the store rather than by him, and his school records
it as transfiguration.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**The glyph, proposed.** `[Lk]` **Lock** for the Mercy Lamina's barrier and `[Ur]` **Balance** for the
Oath Lamina's gating, with `[Sa]` **Illumination** as the alternative filing for the release. The
Index's Standing Note governs: *"Assignment is decided after the phenomenon, never before, and it is a
filing decision rather than a generative one."* **The page names one glyph and it is not one of these:**
*"[Uur] Disruption, Uurgath's own glyph for chaotic anti-cohesion"*, named as the discipline's own
unanswerable counter. `[Uur]` is attested outside the mirrored thirty — it appears as a Counter on
three Titan pages, e.g. *"[Uur] Disruption breaks the lattice mid-formation"*
(`wiki/The Fourteen Titans/The Physical Order — Atla'zon and Gaia.md`) — so the page cites a real
form, while the forms this account proposes remain proposed (`SA-GAP-GLYPH-MIRROR`).

**What boundary moves.** Not the knight's strength and not his endurance. **The barrier between the
Shell's present configuration and its relaxed one.** Fixatio's cure is applied not to a target but to
the practitioner's own state, and the boundary condition is a height: high enough that the store does
not relax on its own, low enough that a chosen act can cross it. The Index: *"A glyph creates nothing
and carries no power. … It moves a limit, and the law does what it has always done on the far side of
the new limit."*

**What the law then does on its own — and this is where the discipline is felt rather than seen.**
Nothing, for a long time. That is the whole of it. A metastable store does nothing by definition; the
knight walks, takes blows, holds a line, and what is accumulating in him is accumulating because it
**cannot** go anywhere, not because he is holding it by effort. The Oath Lamina reads the affinity
figures around him — banners, seals, oaths spoken in his hearing — and Benediction prices them, which
raises the amplification without asking him. The Judicium layer runs continuously as inspection: the
characteristic lines of everything in view, the crack under the paint, the lie under the claim.

**Then the barrier is crossed, once.** The release is a cut, or a vow spoken aloud, or a stance that
does not break, and the discharge is total — a metastable store has no setting between held and spent.
At the top of the discipline, with a Domain seated, the release does not leave as force at all: it
leaves as **law**, *"the deliberate reshaping of local Continuum behaviour so that a chosen law becomes
easier to sustain within a defined space"* (`wiki/Disciplines/The Disciplines.md`), and the chosen law
is the page's own — **things that are sworn do not break.** The ground stops giving way, *"structurally
and socially"*, for as long as his pulse carries it.

**The failure modes, and there are three.** **Seizure**, if the store passes what the Mercy Lamina can
hold: the Shell locks, and the page is explicit that the only safe exit is a deliberate oath-bleed —
kneeling, a controlled breath, a hand on the brand — *"never an involuntary vent"*, which is Fixatio's
irreversibility arriving at the worst moment. **Fraying**, under `[Uur]` Disruption, which the page
admits it cannot answer: the spiral symmetry the working depends on must be *"re-centred by hand rather
than shrugged off."* And **spectral overload**, Judicium's own, where every object in view reports at
once and the verdict cannot be chosen because nothing can be prioritised. The Four Faults reading
(`Two Sets of Books` §VII) is **Structural** for the first and **Coherence** for a knight fighting
without a sworn cause: *"lived behaviour and stated law have drifted apart, and the Continuum reads
the drift."*

**What the target sees and feels.** For most of an engagement, nothing — which is the tell, and the
page does not say so. He does not flare. Blows land and he does not give ground, and the absence of a
reaction is itself the reading a trained opponent takes. Close up there is the standing bias: a
pressure on the air near him that does not vary with what he is doing, because Traitus is passive.
Then the gesture-keys, which are public and deliberate — a knee going down, a shield coming up, a hand
finding the brand — and these are the only warning anyone gets, because the store has no meter and its
level is invisible. **The release is not bright first. It is heavy first.** What the man in front of
him feels is weight arriving: the ground under him stiffening, the give going out of things, his own
intention to move meeting something that is not resistance but a change in what the ground is willing
to do. Then the act itself, once, and afterwards a knight who is measurably less than he was a second
earlier, and who cannot do it again this cycle.

## 5 · Counterplay and the challenge

**The fairness check.**

| Test | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Costs something that hurts | **pass, and the cost is structural rather than charged** | *"Storing past what the Mercy Lamina can hold seizes the Shell outright, and the only safe release is a deliberate oath-bleed … never an involuntary vent."* The discipline's resource is also its hazard, and it is due inside the scene rather than after it. Add the cost nobody wrote down: **it cannot be run by a man without a cause**, so the practitioner pays in advance, in conduct, for output he may never spend. |
| Stated limits | **pass, and unusually completely** | The whole *Cannot* section: no fast, repeated or incidental output; one stored release per cycle; no version that trades storage for burst damage; degraded output without a sworn cause; seizure on overfill; and **an admitted unanswerable counter**, `[Uur]` Disruption. A page that names the thing it loses to is doing the fairness work itself. |
| Something beats it | **pass, and one route is named on the page** | `[Uur]` Disruption frays the symmetry and must be re-centred by hand. The register adds three more that need no peer: the sworn-cause requirement, the terrain answer across three Families, and the vow. |
| It has a tell | **pass, but not the one the page thinks** | The gesture-keys are public and deliberate — a knee, a shield, a hand on the brand — and they are the last warning. **The real tell is earlier and is an absence:** a man who takes blows without reacting is filling, and the store has no visible level. |
| Numbers in band | **FAIL, because there are no numbers** | The page states a Stage range and a Grade range and nothing else: no range, no duration, no capacity, no release figure (`SA-GAP-SOLARBOUND-NO-NUMBERS`). It also carries **retired lettered Coherence Bands** (*"Bands C through A"*, R42) and **no Path gate at all** where three system gates require Attraction (`SA-GAP-SOLARBOUND-PATHGATE`). |

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Break the Man — "Rigidity."** *"A Trait's law is not negotiable by its bearer, and the more
  absolute it is the less he can contradict it. Present him with the situation his own law handles
  worst and he will handle it that way regardless."* **This is the counter to a Traitus discipline and
  the page hands over the situation**: he cannot produce fast, repeated or incidental output, and his
  Mercy Lamina holds the store back until judgment is *deliberately chosen*. Give him a fight that is
  all incidental output and no moment worth spending on, and he will bear it, because bearing it is
  what he is.
- **Break the Man — "The vow."** *"A vow broken is a Shear Break, and an opponent who knows the terms
  of a vow holds a weapon that does not require touching him."* The Oath Lamina amplifies on sworn
  causes; the same clause makes every term he has sworn a lever. And Benediction's own failure means
  a **higher-affinity** vow can displace an older one silently.
- **Break the Man — "Coherence drift."** *"You do not have to cause this. You have to notice it and
  wait."* The page states the drift condition outright: fighting without a cause he has actually sworn
  to degrades purity and output. A knight in someone else's war is degrading on his own.
- **Deny the Field.** Three Families, three suppression lists, and one room that hits all of them:
  **cold, shielded, and moving.** Materia has *"no tools on open water"*; Vitalia fails on *"sterile
  ground, extreme cold"*; Fulguria fails in *"Faraday-caged interiors, heavy ferrous shielding."*
- **Break the Boundary — "Interrupt the chain."** Normally closed at Stage VII, since *"above
  Refraction the chain is internal and none of this applies."* **Except that this discipline keeps its
  anchors voluntarily** — the gesture-keys are Corporis integration, re-committed each time — so the
  route that should be shut is ajar, and taking the hand that reaches for the brand is taking an

# System account · Somnium Scriptorium

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** A woman says a man's name. The man stops moving. He is still standing
and still breathing, and for the next few seconds he is not available to anyone in the room. Then he is
back, and he either remembers a very long conversation or he remembers nothing at all.

**The real phenomenon.** **Cortical decoupling with the brainstem left intact — a dissociative state,
not an absence** — with the experienced content deposited onto an imposed template by the target's own
generative machinery, and the sense of its duration reconstructed afterwards from how much of it was
encoded. Three separate real mechanisms, each measurable, and together they account for every line on
the card including the one the card says nobody understands.

**(a) The body left standing is the hard part of the claim, and it is the part that closes.** A standing
human is an inverted pendulum and an unstable one. With the centre of mass about **h = 0.9 m** above the
ankle joint, the toppling rate is **ω = √(g/h) = √(9.81/0.9) = 3.3 s⁻¹**, so a lean grows by a factor of
e every **0.30 s** and a 5 mm deviation becomes a 50 mm one — past comfortable sway and into the edge of
the base of support — in **ln(10)/ω = 0.70 s**. Holding it needs continuous ankle torque of order
**T = m·g·h·sin θ = 60 × 9.81 × 0.9 × sin 3° ≈ 28 N·m**, modulated every few hundred milliseconds
forever.

**And the loop that supplies it is not the cortex.** Voluntary cortical correction has a latency of
150–250 ms, which is comparable to the whole 300 ms toppling constant and therefore useless on its own;
the loops that actually do the work are vestibulospinal and reticulospinal, at 40–100 ms. Sherrington's
decerebrate preparation is the direct evidence: transect above the midbrain and you do not get a limp
animal, you get **exaggerated extensor tone** — an animal that can be set on its feet and will stand.
Respiration is the same story one level lower: the pre-Bötzinger complex in the medulla generates the
rhythm autonomously at 12–16 breaths a minute and has never needed permission from anybody.

**So *"upright and breathing"* is not the implausible detail. It is the correct anatomical prediction for
a preparation whose cortex has been disconnected and whose brainstem has not** — which is exactly the
division of labour Oneirion's register describes, a working that acts on the state manifold and *"not on
the room."* The page got this right, and it is the single most checkable claim it makes.

**(b) Where the joules are: almost nowhere, and this has to be said plainly.** Quiet standing runs at
about **1.2 MET ≈ 85 W** for a 60 kg adult, of which the brain is roughly **20 W**. The page holds the
construct for *"roughly three exchanges of real time"*, which is **9 s or 15 s** depending on which turn
convention is in force (`SA-GAP-TURN-LENGTH`). Total metabolic throughput of the target across the whole
working: **≈ 0.8–1.3 kJ**, about **80 mg of sugar.** Nothing is lifted, nothing is heated, nothing is
accelerated. **This working has no energy budget worth writing down**, which means the physical account
has to be made in a different currency or it is not an account at all.

**(c) The currency is information, and those numbers are small too — which is the point.** The sensory
periphery delivers on the order of **10⁹ bit/s** to the nervous system and conscious report passes
something in the range **10–60 bit/s**; take 40 bit/s. Across 15 s the entire conscious channel carries
**≈ 600 bits — about seventy-five characters of text.** An interrogation that yields a name and a date
is a few tens of bits, so the page's *stated purpose* fits the channel with room to spare.

**What does not fit the channel is the room.** A navigable space presented at the fidelity the page
describes is orders of magnitude more than 600 bits, so **the construct cannot be being transmitted**,
and Eidolyn's register says why it does not have to be: *"A supersaturated medium will not organise
itself, but given a template it deposits along the template's geometry with high fidelity and
considerable strength."* **A template is cheap; the deposition is paid for by the medium** — and the
medium here is the target's own generative machinery, which builds navigable spaces unaided for roughly
two hours every night at no additional metabolic cost. Serenyra supplies the geometry. The target
supplies the room, the weather in it, and the furniture.

That is why both halves of the physical account come out negligible: **the target pays for everything,
in a currency he was already spending.**

**(d) Subjective time — and this is where the page's own open question turns out to be already answered.**
The card asks *"why some targets remember every page of what happened inside and others wake with no
memory the dream occurred at all,"* and it declines, correctly, to give a ratio for how much longer
inside feels than outside. Real physics bounds the ratio hard, and in doing so collapses the two
mysteries into one.

Stetson, Fiesta and Eagleman (*Does time really slow down during a frightening event?*, PLoS ONE, 2007)
dropped subjects thirty-one metres in free fall wearing a wrist "perceptual chronometer" flickering just
above the flicker-fusion threshold. Subjects retrospectively judged the fall about **36% longer** than it
was — and **could not read the chronometer any better than at rest.** Temporal resolution did not
improve at all. The dilation is therefore **not a change in clock rate. It is a duration judgement
reconstructed from how much was laid down.**

**Which means duration-sense and memory are not two variables. They are one variable read twice.** A
target whose encoding ran hot wakes reporting a long dream *and* remembering every page, because the
richness of the record is what produced the sense of length. A target whose encoding was suppressed has
nothing to reconstruct from, and so reports **neither the dream nor its duration** — not a dream he has
forgotten, but no dream at all. The clinical precedent is not exotic: **midazolam produces a fully
responsive, fully conversant patient with reliable anterograde amnesia**, and ketamine produces vivid
navigable experience with dense report at one dose and none at another. **The two outcomes the card
finds mysterious are the two standard outcomes of every dissociative agent in medical use, and they come
from one dial.**

**(e) Where the physics does *not* agree with the card: the cost is the wrong cost.** The page prices
running two spaces as *"exhausts her faster than either alone would."* Dual-task performance does not
work that way. Where two tasks both require a central decision stage, they are not run in parallel —
the second is postponed by roughly the duration of the first's central stage, **200–500 ms** (the
psychological refractory period). The physical prediction for a practitioner holding a room and a
construct at once is therefore not fatigue. It is **interleaving, with a few hundred milliseconds lost
at every switch** — a woman who is a half-beat late to everything happening in front of her. That is a
smaller cost than exhaustion and a far more useful one, because it is due *inside the scene* and it is
visible. The page charges the currency that settles later (`SA-FAIR-SOMNIUM-COST-NOT-DUE`).

**Energy budget, stated.** Joules supplied by the practitioner: **0.** Joules expended: **0.8–1.3 kJ**,
all of it the target's own basal metabolism, none of it added. Information transferred across the
conscious channel: **≈ 600 bits.** Information *instantiated* inside the construct: unbounded by the
channel, because it is deposited locally rather than sent. Fault class by `Two Sets of Books` §VII:
**Boundary fault** — *"The condition was established imprecisely and the law ran somewhere unintended"* —
which is the correct filing for a working whose boundary condition is a spoken name.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** One Family, **Limina**, the Family whose *"truest signature is **what fails to be
there.**"* Its coupling, from `wiki/The Magic System/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings.md`:
*"**Spirit** primary · **Medium** at Veil-Thin density and above"*, favoured by *"Darkness, silence,
Aether-thin ground, ruins"* and suppressed by *"Saturated Aether, crowd density, active Domains."*
*"Terrain is not flavour. It is a modifier on η."*

**Two of those three suppressors are things an opponent can arrange, and one of them is free.** *Crowd
density* means an interrogation attempted in a full room is already degraded; *active Domains* means a
practitioner standing inside somebody else's seated Domain is working uphill. A single Family is a light
conduit load — nothing like the three-Family exposure elsewhere in this batch — but the compensation is
that the two suppressors that remain are cheap to produce and the working needs *silence and thin
ground*, which is to say it needs conditions an escort can simply refuse to provide.

**Residue.** The Claustra kind, and the register is unusually blunt about what that means.
`wiki/The Magic System/The Magical Categories — Unified Taxonom.md`: *"Sits between Spirit and
Attraction because the seal's *syntax* is Soul Plane precision while its *duration* is a Fate-side claim
that must be renewed by something. **A Claustra working that loses its anchor does not weaken. It
opens.**"* The technique page states the same property in its own words — *"loses nothing on collapse so
much as opens outright if its anchor is cut"* — so the two agree, and the residue of this working is not
a trace left behind but **a standing claim that has to keep being renewed.** While it stands, the anchor
is a real object in the world: a name, spoken, holding a seal.

**Saturation.** Two harmonizations inside one Family. The ceiling is *"**Tempering Capacity** and it is
not negotiable"*, and no figure exists for Serenyra or anyone else, but two-in-one-Family is the lightest
load in this batch and nothing here approaches a ceiling anybody can name.

**Wellspring stratum — Oneirion, *The Wellspring of Dream*, Limina, Entropy, Void and Mind.** Core Law,
verbatim from `wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Limina — Entropy, Void and Mind.md`:

> **Analogue** · Configuration space. A system's state as a point in an abstract high-dimensional
> manifold.
> **Mechanism** · A mechanical system's every possible arrangement is a coordinate in a space that has no
> physical location, and the system's evolution is a trajectory through it. Oneirion lets a working act
> on that manifold rather than on the room. This is the mechanical reason Oneirion techniques reach
> targets no line of sight connects, and the reason they are so difficult to describe afterward.

**The register's last sentence is the account's most useful sentence and its sharpest problem.** *"So
difficult to describe afterward"* is the amnesia of §1(d) arriving from the metaphysical side: a
trajectory through a space with no physical location has no spatial vocabulary to be recalled in. But
*"reach targets no line of sight connects"* is stated by the governing Wellspring as the mechanical
consequence of its own law, and the technique page requires *"line-of-sight"* outright
(`SA-CROSS-SOMNIUM-LINE-OF-SIGHT`).

Oneirion's failure: *"Projection error. The trajectory was correct in the manifold and lands nowhere in
particular when it is mapped back down into a room."* **That is the page's own violent ejection, named
in the register two files away.**

**Wellspring stratum — Eidolyn, *The Shaping Mind*, Limina.** Core Law, verbatim from the same file:

> **Analogue** · Templated nucleation. Ordered deposition onto an imposed pattern.
> **Mechanism** · A supersaturated medium will not organise itself, but given a template it deposits
> along the template's geometry with high fidelity and considerable strength. Eidolyn makes intent the
> template. The construct's solidity is real material behaviour rather than illusion, and its failure
> modes are the failure modes of a poorly designed casting.

*"Real material behaviour rather than illusion"* is the clause that earns the page's claim that the
chamber holds a second consciousness *"rather than dissolving the instant attention wavers."* Eidolyn's
failure is the one the technique page never mentions and the one that should frighten its practitioners
most: *"Template defect. Any flaw in the intent is faithfully reproduced at scale, and the practitioner
is rarely able to see the flaw until it is standing in front of them."*

**The Sub-Stat list derives exactly, and that is worth recording because it so often does not.** Applying
the Merge Ledger (`_method.md` §VI) to the two registers: Oneirion strengthens *"Gnosis Cognition,
Dominion Reach, Ardency Spectral"*, and Spectral → **Cascade**. Eidolyn strengthens *"Ardency Density in
summoned constructs, Ardency Inscription, Gnosis Memorium"*, and Inscription → **Density**, Memorium →
**Retention**. The page's four Sub-Stats are *"Gnosis Cognition, Gnosis Retention, Ardency Cascade,
Ardency Density."* **All four, with nothing left over and nothing invented.** The page's FOW line was
built from the registers by somebody who read them.

**Which makes the Family's own affinity line the anomaly.** Limina's *"Stat Affinity"* names *"Gnosis …
Resilience … Harmonics … Dominion"* and **does not name Ardency at all**, while two of its own
Wellsprings strengthen three Ardency Sub-Stats between them and this page makes Ardency its secondary
Primary (`SA-CROSS-LIMINA-ARDENCY-AFFINITY`).

**Which of the Four Theories.** **Consent Doctrine, enforced, and it is the whole moral architecture of
the page.** The Four Glyph Roles make it structural. Authorization is the role that establishes *"Which
Wellsprings are invoked, and that the practitioner is the lawful channel. Requires genuine harmonization;
the Continuum reads the Attraction Layer at the moment of casting"*, and its absence produces *"Mechanica
by definition. Coerced transformation without lawful Wellspring participation. The Continuum treats it as
debt"* (`wiki/Disciplines/The Disciplines.md`). **This is a working whose entire subject matter is a
relocation the target did not agree to** — and the page itself supplies the reading, because it states
*two* uses: interrogation, and *"pull an ally out of a traumatic moment as an act of mercy."* Those are
not two applications of one working. Under the Consent Doctrine they are **two different workings with
the same geometry**, one of them authorized and one of them debt. **Debt** is therefore live as the
second doctrine, and the page's Cost — residue she carries home — is what a debt looks like when it is
being serviced rather than paid.

**Essence stratum.** Crystal layer: the **Aether Shell**, Class V Radiant on the card, with the Essence
Core untouched — nothing here modifies her. Developmental Tier **Radiant** (Stage VIII, Part
Twenty-One). Tier of Standing **6 · Master**. Crystal State **Refined**. **The invoice:** a seal that has
to be renewed for as long as it stands, a second attentional locus held against a central bottleneck
that will not hold two, and an empathic residue that arrives after the fighting. She does not pay for the
room. She pays for holding the door.

**The lens: Artemidorus of Daldis, *Oneirocritica* (2nd century AD).** The first surviving systematic
treatise on dreams, and the right lens because it treats a dream as **a text with a grammar rather than
an event with a cause.** Artemidorus divides dreams into *enhypnia*, which merely report the state of the
body and mean nothing, and *oneiroi*, which signify — and his governing rule is that interpretation is
**indexed to the dreamer**: the same image read for a sailor, a magistrate and a slave yields three
different readings, because a dream's meaning is a function of who is having it. Hence the working's name.
A **scriptorium** is not where texts are written. It is where texts are **copied**, by hand, faithfully,
including the errors of the exemplar. And the Wellspring is *Oneirion*, from *oneiros*: the significant
dream, the one that is a text.

**The productive misreading, and what it kills.** An Oneirocritic school takes Artemidorus at his word
and concludes that the chamber is a **text to be read** — that whatever surfaces inside it is diagnostic
of the man, because dreams signify. So they build a thin template and wait. They do not construct a
narrative; they hold a space open and record what fills it, and they are proud of the restraint, because
a strong template would be putting words in his mouth.

**Eidolyn kills them with their own virtue.** *"Any flaw in the intent is faithfully reproduced at scale,
and the practitioner is rarely able to see the flaw until it is standing in front of them."* A thin
template is not a neutral template — it is a template with more unexamined intent in it, not less, and a
supersaturated medium deposits along whatever geometry it is given. The Oneirocritic therefore reads his
own assumptions back out of the chamber in the target's voice, at high fidelity and considerable
strength, and he has no way to tell that is what happened, because the page's own Weakness says a
fractured mind *"can distort the narrative"* and he cannot distinguish distortion from disclosure.
**An Oneirocritic school produces confessions. Every one of them is real to the man who took it**, and a
tribunal that trusts the method executes on a document the interrogator wrote without knowing it.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**The glyph, proposed.** `[Rv]` **Reverie** for the primary boundary, `[Lk]` **Lock** for the Claustra
hold, and `[Frm]` **Form** for Eidolyn's template. The page names no glyph at all, so all three are
filings made under the Index's Standing Note — *"Assignment is decided after the phenomenon, never
before, and it is a filing decision rather than a generative one"* — and none is canon until Isaac rules
(`SA-GAP-GLYPH-MIRROR`). `[Lk]` is one of the Index's only two Counter forms, which is the right shape:
the Counterplay register says **Lock** *"occupies a limit so no other limit can be set there"*, and a
Claustra seal is a limit sitting in a place to stop anything else being set there.

**What boundary moves.** Not the target's mind, which is untouched, and not the room, which is irrelevant.
**The boundary that moves is the coordinate system his state is addressed in.** The Index is explicit
about what a glyph is allowed to be: *"A glyph creates nothing and carries no power. … It moves a limit,
and the law does what it has always done on the far side of the new limit."* Oneirion's law is already
running — every system's state is already a point on a manifold, and its evolution is already a
trajectory — and the boundary condition says: *address this trajectory in the manifold rather than in the
room.* The anchor for that condition is the name, spoken. **That is why the working needs a name and not
a grip.** A name is the only handle a person has that is already an address rather than a location.

**What the law then does on its own.** Everything, and none of it by her. His state was always a point
in configuration space; once the addressing boundary is set there, his awareness is indexed to a
trajectory that has no location in the room, and *"the reason they are so difficult to describe
afterward"* is already fixed at this moment, before anything has happened. Meanwhile the body does what
a brainstem does when the cortex stops asking: it stands, because reticulospinal tone stands it, and it
breathes, because the medulla has never needed instruction. Nothing holds him up. **He was always being
held up by something that is not him.**

Then Eidolyn's law, which is the one that produces the content: intent as a template, and a
supersaturated medium depositing along it. The medium is his own dreaming machinery, which is
supersaturated in the exact sense the register means — a system with far more capacity to generate
structure than it has structure imposed on it. She supplies the geometry of a room. **He builds it, at
full fidelity, out of himself, and then walks into it and finds it solid,** because Eidolyn's solidity
*"is real material behaviour rather than illusion."*

And the seal holds, or it does not. The Claustra layer is the third law and the only one that is
actively maintained: a limit occupied, renewed continuously against a Fate-side claim, for the nine or
fifteen seconds the page allows. She is not pushing him in. **She is standing in the doorway**, and the
register says what happens if she is moved: a Claustra working that loses its anchor *"does not weaken.
It opens."*

**The failure modes, and there are three, each belonging to a different law.** **Projection error**,
Oneirion's own: *"The trajectory was correct in the manifold and lands nowhere in particular when it is
mapped back down into a room"* — which is precisely the page's *"ejects both of them, violently"*, and
the violence is not malice, it is a mapping with no landing site. **Template defect**, Eidolyn's: a flaw
in her intent reproduced faithfully at scale, invisible until it is standing in front of her — the
interrogation that returns her own question as his answer. And **the seal opening**, Claustra's: not a
fade but a door, with both of them on the wrong side of it. The Four Faults reading is **Boundary fault**
throughout — *"the condition was established imprecisely and the law ran somewhere unintended"* — because
the condition is a spoken name, and a name is the least precise handle in the system: it is shared,
mishearable, and sometimes not his.

**What the target sees and feels.** He hears his name, which is the last thing that happens in the room.
There is no transition to describe, and this is the detail everything else follows from: he does not
travel, and he does not feel a pull, because nothing about him has moved — only the coordinates he is
being addressed in changed, and addresses are not experienced. **He is simply somewhere, and has been for
a while.** The room he is in is unreasonably convincing, because he built it: the light is right, the
floor takes his weight, the air has a temperature. If there is a woman in it asking him questions, she
is the only thing in the space that is not his own work, and she will be the only thing that feels
slightly wrong — a half-beat late, because she is somewhere else as well.

He may be there a long time. He will not be able to say how long, and the number he gives afterwards, if
he gives one, is a measure of how much of it stuck rather than how much of it happened. And then it ends,
either because she closed it or because somebody in the room he left put a hand on her. **If she closed
it, he comes back into a body that has been standing where he left it, with fifteen seconds gone and a
conversation in him that will not fit into fifteen seconds** — or with nothing, no gap, no sense of
interruption, and no way to know that the woman in front of him has been told something. **If somebody
else closed it, both of them arrive nowhere in particular**, and the register has already said that is
what that looks like.

| Costs something that hurts | **FAIL as written, pass as the physics has it** | The stated cost is *"Emotional residue … she carries fragments of whatever fear or grief the target carried in"* plus *"running both spaces at once exhausts her faster."* Both settle after the scene, and the Counterplay register's own *What Does Not Work* section says why that is not a cost an opponent can use: *"Costs are real and they are rarely due inside a scene."* The real in-scene price — a few hundred milliseconds of lag on everything happening in front of her, at every switch — is a genuine, exploitable cost and the page does not state it (`SA-FAIR-SOMNIUM-COST-NOT-DUE`). |
| Stated limits | **pass, and honestly** | *"She cannot rewrite the target's core personality in the time available. The technique traps and questions; it does not remake anyone."* Plus a hard duration (three exchanges), a hard range (200 m), line of sight, and two stated resistances — strong will, or a mind already fractured. The page also admits what it does not know instead of inventing it, twice: no subjective-time ratio, and no explanation for the amnesia. **A page that declines to invent is doing the fairness work.** |
| Something beats it | **pass, and the page names three of four routes** | A ward before the anchor sets, psychic guardianship intervening mid-transfer, and breaking her concentration from outside. The register adds better ones below, including one the page's own Stage should have removed. |
| It has a tell | **pass, and it is enormous** | Two, both unmissable. She must **say his name out loud** — a spoken Craft, so the working announces its target before it exists — and **he stops moving while standing up.** A man upright, breathing, and not there is the loudest tell in this batch. The subtler one is hers: the half-beat lag of §1(e). |
| Numbers in band | **FAIL on the Paths, unknowable on the range** | Two Sub-Stat gates require Paths the page does not declare and the author does not have (`SA-NUM-SOMNIUM-PATHGATE`, `SA-CROSS-SOMNIUM-SERENYRA-PATH`). Duration is ambiguous by 1.67× (`SA-GAP-TURN-LENGTH`). The 200 m range cannot be checked at all, because the system has no Grade-to-range ladder (`SA-GAP-RANGE-LADDER`). Stage, Grade, Tier, η and Crystal State all check clean. |

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Break the Boundary — "Interrupt the chain," which should not work here and does.** *"Below Refraction
  a working requires an external anchor: voice, hand, ink or blood. Take the anchor and the sentence does
  not complete. **Break his wrist and a gestured chain is finished. Put a hand over his mouth and a spoken
  one is.** Above Refraction the chain is internal and none of this applies, which is the practical reason
  Stage VII is the line every soldier is taught to fear."* This is a **floor-VII working run at VIII that
  kept a spoken anchor and an air-inscribed glyph anyway.** The system says the cheapest counter in the
  register stops applying at exactly her Stage, and her own Craft line hands it back
  (`SA-CROSS-SOMNIUM-ANCHOR-ABOVE-REFRACTION`).
- **Break the Boundary — "Knot and Lock."** *"They condition the conditioning. **Knot** entangles a path
  so it cannot be traced cleanly. **Lock** occupies a limit so no other limit can be set there."* Against
  a working whose boundary is an *address*, Knot is the precise instrument: a path that cannot be traced
  cleanly is a trajectory that cannot be mapped back down, which is Oneirion's own stated failure. And
  Lock, set on the anchor site first, means the seal has nowhere to go.
- **Break the Boundary — "Wards," and the ward the page recommends is itself beatable.** The page's
  Counter is *"a ward built against forced narrative relocation."* The register prices that honestly:
  wards are *"Runecraft standing on a substrate, and therefore beaten the way Runecraft is beaten:
  **damage the substrate.** A ward is not argued with. It is chiselled off."* So the page's own counter is
  a second-order problem, not a wall — which is consistent with *What Does Not Work*: *"Wards condition
  what may be authorised in a space … Neither is a wall and both are written as one constantly."*
- **Deny the Field, and this is the free one.** Limina is suppressed by *"Saturated Aether, **crowd
  density**, active Domains."* An interrogation needs *"darkness, silence, Aether-thin ground"* and is
  degraded in a full, loud room. **An escort that simply never lets her have a quiet corner has denied the
  field without knowing a single thing about the technique.**
- **Break the Man — "Read him," with the page's own clock.** *"Self-derived techniques must be read live,
  in the two or three exchanges before they kill you."* This is a self-derived working that lasts
  *"roughly three exchanges."* **The window the register allots for reading an unknown technique is exactly
  the window this technique runs in** — so an opponent who survives the first use has been handed the
  documentation, and the second use is against somebody who has studied.
- **Break the Man — "Coherence drift."** *"A practitioner whose lived behaviour has drifted from his stated
  law is degrading before anyone lays a hand on him. … **You do not have to cause this. You have to notice
  it and wait.**"* The page states two uses of one geometry — interrogation, and mercy — and §2 gives the
  reason that matters: under the Consent Doctrine only one of them is authorized. A practitioner who tells
  herself she is doing the second while doing the first is the textbook case, and *"the Continuum reads the
  drift."*

**The tell, stated plainly.** She says his name, out loud, before it works. He stands still, upright,
breathing, for nine or fifteen seconds. And for as long as it runs, **she is a half-beat behind the room**
— late to a thrown object, late to a question, late to somebody walking behind her. The first two tells
are free to anybody watching. The third is the one worth having, because it is not a warning that the
working is coming. It is an opening while it is running.

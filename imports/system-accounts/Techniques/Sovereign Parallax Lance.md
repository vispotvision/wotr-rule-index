# System account · Sovereign Parallax Lance

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** A woman points at a man nine hundred metres away. He was, a moment
before, a thing that could have failed in any number of ways. He now fails in the worst one.

**The real phenomenon.** **Weakest-link fracture: the selection of one flaw out of a population, and the
preferential loading of it to its critical stress intensity.** Not measurement collapse — and the page's
own primary Wellspring says so, in the register, in words that describe the Lance better than the Lance's
own Mechanism line does.

**(a) Fractura's Core Law is the whole physical account, and it is already correct.** From
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Vectoria — Mechanics.md`:

> **Analogue** · Linear elastic fracture mechanics. The Griffith criterion and the stress intensity
> factor.
> **Mechanism** · Nothing fails at its theoretical strength. Everything fails at a pre-existing flaw,
> because stress concentrates at a crack tip in proportion to the square root of the crack's length, and
> once that intensity exceeds the material's toughness the crack runs at a fraction of the speed of sound
> and does not stop. Fractura gives the practitioner sight of those flaws and the precision to load
> exactly one of them.

**"Sight of those flaws and the precision to load exactly one of them" is the Sovereign Parallax Lance,
stated by the system, two files away from the page that needed it.** The arithmetic is the Irwin form
**K_I = σ√(πa)**, with failure when **K_I ≥ K_IC**: the stress intensity at a crack tip rises as the
square root of the crack's length, so among a population of flaws under the same remote stress **the
longest one reaches toughness first**, and the body fails there and nowhere else. The crack then runs at
a terminal velocity of roughly half the Rayleigh wave speed — for steel about **1,500 m/s** against a
Rayleigh speed near 3,000 m/s — which is the register's *"a fraction of the speed of sound and does not
stop."*

**(b) The "every way the target could be hurt" is a real, measurable distribution, and it has a modulus.**
The page's *"every physically consistent way the target could be destroyed"* is the flaw population, and
its spread is described by Weibull statistics: **P_f = 1 − exp[−(σ/σ₀)^m]**, where the modulus **m** is
how tightly the failure modes cluster. A heterogeneous, worked, previously-stressed body has **m ≈ 5–10**
— wide scatter, many genuinely competing ways to fail. A well-made homogeneous one has **m ≳ 20** and
essentially one failure mode.

**So the size of the Lance's menu is the target's Weibull modulus, and the effect is enormous.** Bulk
soda-lime glass fails at about **50 MPa**. Pristine drawn glass fibre of the same composition reaches
**3–7 GPa**, near the theoretical limit of E/10. **Two orders of magnitude, entirely from flaw
population** — no chemistry changes, only whether there is anything for K_I to concentrate at.

**(c) Which is why the page's stated Weakness is right, and righter than it knows.** *"A structure
anchored in pure Basilithe rejects the contradiction outright."* Basilithe's register:

> **Analogue** · Natural frequency and stiffness-to-mass ratio. Quartz oscillator stability.
> **Mechanism** · A body's resonant frequency is fixed by its stiffness and its mass, and a high-stiffness
> low-loss structure holds that frequency against forcing so reliably that civilisations set their clocks
> by cut quartz. Basilithe raises the Crystal's stiffness until its resonance simply will not be driven
> off, which is a defence against every technique that works by imposing an external rhythm.

A high-stiffness, low-loss structure is a **low-defect** structure; that is what makes a cut quartz
oscillator keep time. **A defect-free lattice has no flaw population, so a working that selects from one
has nothing to select from** — and the immunity is the factor of a hundred in §1(b), not a ruling. The
page reached the right answer by a different road. And Basilithe hands back the counter to its own
counter, in its own Failure line: *"Resonant catastrophe. A frequency that cannot be shifted also cannot
be detuned away from a forcing that happens to match it."* A target that has made itself flawless has
made itself **tunable**.

**(d) Where the account stops closing: the yield will not fit in the effect.** The page states *"roughly
400 TJ of yield (low hundred-kiloton TNT-equivalent)"* and confines its collateral to *"a fractured echo
along a line up to 40 m."* Those two numbers are not close to compatible.

- **400 TJ ÷ 4.184 TJ/kt = 95.6 kt.**
- Energy to raise a 70 kg body to boiling and vaporise its water outright: 70 × 3,500 × 63 ≈ **15 MJ**
  of heating plus 42 kg × 2.26 MJ/kg ≈ **95 MJ** of latent heat, call the whole destructive job
  **~1.5 × 10⁸ J**. So the stated yield is **about 2.7 million times the energy required to vaporise the
  target completely.** The surplus does not stay in the target, because there is no target left to hold
  it.
- And **Cataclysm is one of the ten Wellsprings the page invokes**, which forbids the containment in its
  own register: *"blast radius thereafter scales with the cube root of yield, which is why the
  Wellspring's area effects widen coherently rather than merely spilling."* Hopkinson–Cranz scaling,
  **R ∝ W^⅓**: severe overpressure reaches roughly 7 km at 1 Mt, so at 0.0956 Mt it reaches
  **7 × 0.0956^⅓ ≈ 3.2 km.** Against the page's 40 m that is a factor of **eighty in radius** and about
  **half a million in enclosed volume.**

**The page's own point defeats its own number.** The whole claim of this working is *precision* — one
selected wound, driven home. Ninety-five kilotons is the least precise thing in the corpus
(`SA-PHYS-LANCE-YIELD-VS-CONTAINMENT`).

**(e) One internal consistency worth crediting.** The stated peak force is *"on the order of 1 PN"*, and
a petanewton acting through **d = E/F = 4 × 10¹⁴ / 10¹⁵ = 0.40 m** delivers exactly 400 TJ. Forty
centimetres is the depth of a torso. **The force figure and the yield figure are the same statement**,
and whoever wrote them made them agree.

**Energy budget, stated.** Delivered: **4.00 × 10¹⁴ J** peak yield, **10¹⁵ N** peak contact force, over
an implied **0.40 m** of travel. Where the joules were: not in Opalis. `Two Sets of Books` — *"a working's
energy budget balances at the Aether stratum and not at the Essence one. The practitioner does not supply
the joules. They supply the boundary condition."* What the target supplies is the flaw, and the
**Essence-Typed Damage** clause of Part Eleven prices the discount that buys: *"A Fractura-enhanced strike
targeting a pre-identified fault line in the target's Aether Shell can produce Shell failure at yields
**thirty to fifty percent below** the Shell's rated ceiling, because the strike is applying force at the
exact frequency and vector that maximizes structural degradation."* **So the system already prices this
working's central trick, at 1.4× to 2× effective yield** — and the page does not cite it, though it is
the one clause in the corpus that would justify a raw figure below the Grade's floor. Fault class by
`Two Sets of Books` §VII: **Boundary fault**, because a selection made imprecisely *"ran somewhere
unintended"* and there is nowhere for it to run but into the room.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum, and this is the largest conduit load in the batch by a wide margin.** The page states
Family **Vectoria**, and then names ten Wellsprings which belong to **five** Families: Vectoria (Fractura,
Petralon, Penance), Caloria (Cataclysm), Limina (Dissolution, Tenebra, Oneirion), Materia (Basilithe,
Tarturon) and Fulguria (Orrenthal). Vectoria's coupling, from
`wiki/The Magic System/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings.md`: *"Split: **Wild** (Gravity,
Seismic) · **Spirit** (Sound, Force projection)"*, favoured by *"Structural mass, elevation, brittle or
pre-stressed material"* and suppressed by *"Loose sediment, free fall, uniform soft ground."*

**Read that suppression list against §1 and it stops being terrain and becomes the counter.** *"Brittle or
pre-stressed material"* favours the working because brittle and pre-stressed is where the flaw population
lives; *"uniform soft ground"* suppresses it because uniform is exactly what has no weakest link.
Vectoria's environmental coupling and Fractura's Core Law are the same fact stated twice, once as weather
and once as mechanism. *"Terrain is not flavour. It is a modifier on η."*

**Five Families also means five suppression lists**, and the page inherits all of them: Caloria fails in
thermal equilibrium and saturated cold, Fulguria against shielded interiors and dense fog, Materia on open
water and moving platforms, Limina under saturated Aether and crowd density.

**Saturation, and the ceiling the page runs at without naming it.** *"The ceiling is **Tempering Capacity**
and it is not negotiable. Exceeding it does not fail gracefully by dropping the newest harmonization. **It
degrades all active harmonizations equally**, which is how practitioners lose a decade of work by reaching
for one more current in a bad hour."* Ten harmonizations is the most any working in this batch asks for,
no figure exists for Opalis, and she has no card at all (`SA-GAP-OPALIS-NO-CARD`).

**And one of the five pairings is the system's own named hard case.** *"**Materia against Limina** is the
canonical hard case, form set against the erosion of all shape"*, resolvable *"either by Temperance
advancement or by developing a Compound Trait that holds the contradiction as a single law."* This working
harmonizes Basilithe and Tarturon on the Materia side and Dissolution, Tenebra and Oneirion on the Limina
side, so the canonical hard case is inside it, three times over. Opalis has the Temperance advancement —
Stage XII is the fourth-highest Stage in the register. The page states neither route
(`SA-GAP-LANCE-MATERIA-LIMINA`).

**Residue.** Stated, and unusually specific: *"the harmonic disturbance it leaves lingering a few seconds
afterward"*, and an echo that *"damages nearby constructs, illusions or unstable workings."* That is
residue as a weapon against other people's residue, and it is the correct consequence of a Cataclysm
component: the register's *"area effects widen coherently rather than merely spilling"* is why the echo
runs along a line instead of in a sphere.

**Wellspring stratum — Fractura, *The Breaking Point*, Vectoria, Mechanics.** Core Law quoted in full at
§1(a). Its **Failure** is the cost this page does not charge: *"Self-inspection. The Wellspring does not
exempt its bearer, and a Fractura practitioner under strain reads their own flaw population with perfect
clarity at the worst possible moment."* A working built on seeing where things break, whose governing
Wellspring turns that sight inward under strain, and whose page lists no such hazard.

**Wellspring stratum — Cataclysm, *The Shattered Law*, Caloria, Thermodynamics.** Core Law, verbatim:

> **Analogue** · Deflagration-to-detonation transition. Blast scaling under the cube-root law.
> **Mechanism** · A subsonic flame front that meets sufficient confinement and turbulence accelerates
> until the reaction couples to a shock wave and begins propagating faster than sound in the unreacted
> medium. The mechanism is a change of kind, not degree. Cataclysm makes that transition available to
> Essence expression, and blast radius thereafter scales with the cube root of yield, which is why the
> Wellspring's area effects widen coherently rather than merely spilling.

*"A change of kind, not degree"* is the page's *"violent over-resolution once a state is chosen"*, and it
is the right law for the moment a selection stops being a reading and becomes an event. Its **Failure**
is the one an SSS practitioner should fear most: *"Reflected overpressure. In confinement the shock
returns along its own path, and the practitioner standing at the origin receives the sum of the wave and
its reflection."* **Nine hundred metres is not far enough for a ninety-five kiloton event**, which is
§1(d) arriving from the other side of the books.

**Wellspring stratum — Dissolution, *The Unbinding Stream*, Limina.** Core Law, verbatim:

> **Analogue** · Solvation. Dissolution driven by the entropy of mixing.
> **Mechanism** · A solid dissolves not because the solvent attacks it but because the mixed state has
> higher entropy than the separated one, and the process therefore runs on its own once initiated.
> Dissolution gives the Crystal that thermodynamic preference toward the unbound state, which makes
> shedding what no longer serves nearly effortless and makes retaining anything a matter of active work.

*"Runs on its own once initiated"* is why the page can call the Dissolution branch *"the option to unmake
rather than break"* and mean something different by it: a fracture has to be driven, a dissolution does
not. Its **Failure** — *"Indiscriminate solvency. Entropy does not consult the practitioner about which
structures were load-bearing"* — is the reason the page's Weakness names *"Verdantia and other
regenerative currents dampen the Dissolution component specifically."*

**Basilithe** is quoted at §1(c), and its position on this page is the strangest fact in the account:
**it is one of the working's own ten contributing Wellsprings and it is also the working's hard counter.**
Under the physics that is coherent rather than contradictory — the Lance needs Basilithe to read where a
structure's stiffness holds and where it does not, and a target whose Basilithe anchor is stronger than
the reading simply has no flaw to be read. **A working that borrows a law is not thereby immune to a
stronger instance of it.** The page states the immunity and never states that it borrows the law.

**Which of the Four Theories.** **Correspondence**, and this page leans on it harder than any other in the
batch. The working's claim is that a directed line in the room and a selection among a target's possible
failures are the same act — *"in the same way a prism decides which color exits toward the target while
creating none of the colors it separates"* — which is a correspondence between a geometry and an outcome
set. **Debt** is live at the cost: the echoed pain of the wound she chose for somebody else is the invoice
for having chosen it, and the page's *"cannot be thrown twice at full strength without the cost climbing
sharply"* is a debt being serviced at a rising rate.

**Essence stratum.** Crystal layer: the **Aether Shell** for delivery, with the Essence Core engaged at
the selection — the page says *"the Essence layer pays for holding all the unresolved states open at once
until the collapse is forced,"* which is the one place the page prices the Core rather than the Shell.
Developmental Tier **Sovereign** (Stage XII, Part Twenty-One). Tier of Standing **7 · Grandmaster**, η
**0.85–0.90** (Part Nineteen). Crystal State: `null`, no card. **The invoice:** ten harmonizations held
against an unquantified ceiling, a selection held open until it is forced, and an escalating charge for
the second attempt.

**The lens: Leibniz on the principle of the best, and Buridan's ass standing where it fails.** Gottfried
Wilhelm Leibniz, *Théodicée* (1710) and the *Monadology*: among all the compossible worlds the divine
intellect surveys, exactly one is actualised, and it is actualised **because it is the best** — nothing is
chosen arbitrarily, because a sufficient reason must exist for every actualisation. The Sovereign Parallax
Lance is that doctrine inverted and made a weapon: it surveys the compossible failures of one body and
actualises exactly one, and the sufficient reason it supplies is **the worst**. Leibniz's God optimises;
the Lance optimises with the sign flipped. That is why the working feels less like violence than like a
verdict, and why the page's name for what it does is *"forces that wound to be the one that happens."*

**The productive misreading, and what it kills.** A Sufficientist school takes the principle of sufficient
reason as a *mechanic* rather than a metaphysics, and concludes that because every actualisation requires
a sufficient reason, **a target given no sufficient reason cannot be actualised against.** So they train
in indifference: they refuse to be the best or the worst candidate for anything, they cultivate perfect
symmetry of vulnerability, they make themselves Buridan's ass — equally weak everywhere, so that no flaw
is the longest and the selection has no ground to stand on.

**Weibull kills them, and the arithmetic is merciless.** Perfect symmetry of flaws is not the absence of
a flaw population; it is a population with a very low modulus, the widest possible scatter, **the largest
menu in the world.** They have not denied the Lance a sufficient reason. They have handed it every
reason at once and guaranteed that the longest crack is long. The school that has genuinely made itself
proof against selection is the one that went the other way, to a flawless lattice and m ≳ 20 — and that
school is the Basilithe anchor the page already names, which no Sufficientist would ever adopt, because a
quartz-stiff soul is the least indifferent thing there is.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**The glyph, proposed.** `[Al]` **Edge** for the selection, `[Me]` **Measure** for the reading that
precedes it, and `[Kael]` **Ruin** for the over-resolution. The page names no glyph, so all three are
filings under the Index's Standing Note — *"Assignment is decided after the phenomenon, never before, and
it is a filing decision rather than a generative one"* — and none is canon until Isaac rules
(`SA-GAP-GLYPH-MIRROR`).

**What boundary moves — and the page's own analogy is the correct answer, while its stated mechanism is
not.** The page offers two accounts in one sentence. The first: *"measurement collapse: a system genuinely
undetermined among several outcomes resolves to exactly one only when read."* The second: *"in the same
way a prism decides which color exits toward the target while creating none of the colors it separates."*
**These are different physics and only the second is this working's.** A prism collapses nothing. Every
wavelength was already present in the beam, and dispersion **selects by angle** — which is exactly what
the Index says a glyph is allowed to do: *"A glyph creates nothing and carries no power. … It moves a
limit, and the law does what it has always done on the far side of the new limit."* A prism creates no
colours; a glyph creates no power; **the Lance creates no wound.**

So the boundary that moves is **which flaw carries the remote stress.** The flaw population is already
there, K_I is already rising fastest at the longest crack, and the body is already going to fail there
given enough load. The boundary condition says: *load that one, now, and at this angle.*
(`SA-OFF-LANCE-MEASUREMENT-COLLAPSE`.)

**What the law then does on its own.** Everything after the first instant. Once K_I at the selected flaw
exceeds K_IC the crack runs — at roughly half the Rayleigh speed, and *"does not stop,"* because a running
crack relieves more energy than it consumes and there is no state of the system that arrests it. **Nothing
sustains the wound. Nothing has to.** This is the difference between the Lance and a strike of the same
Grade: a strike has to deliver its yield against the target's Durability, which at SSS runs about fifty
percent of Strike Force, while the Lance has to deliver only enough to tip one flaw past toughness and the
release does the rest. Part Eleven's Essence-Typed Damage clause is the system pricing that at *"thirty to
fifty percent below the Shell's rated ceiling."*

Then the Cataclysm branch, and this is where the working changes kind: the deflagration-to-detonation
transition, *"a change of kind, not degree,"* couples the release to a shock and the echo runs out along
the fracture line, *"widen[ing] coherently rather than merely spilling"*, taking down *"nearby constructs,
illusions or unstable workings"* — which is to say, taking down everything in the neighbourhood that was
itself being held together by an active boundary condition, because a shock is exactly what a boundary
condition cannot be held against.

**And the Dissolution branch, which is the option not to do any of that.** *"The mixed state has higher
entropy than the separated one, and the process therefore runs on its own once initiated."* A dissolution
does not need to be driven at all: unbinding is downhill. So the same selection, routed through
Dissolution instead of Cataclysm, produces no shock, no echo, no line — a target that comes apart quietly
and at its own pace, and this is the version that leaves a room standing.

**The failure modes, and each belongs to a different Wellspring.** **Self-inspection**, Fractura's:
*"a Fractura practitioner under strain reads their own flaw population with perfect clarity at the worst
possible moment"* — the sight turning inward, at nine hundred metres, with ten currents open.
**Reflected overpressure**, Cataclysm's: *"the practitioner standing at the origin receives the sum of the
wave and its reflection,"* and nine hundred metres is well inside the blast radius §1(d) computes.
**Indiscriminate solvency**, Dissolution's: *"Entropy does not consult the practitioner about which
structures were load-bearing."* And **resonant catastrophe** waiting on the far side of the page's own
named counter, because a Basilithe anchor that cannot be detuned cannot be detuned *away* either.

**What the target sees and feels.** Two fingers drawn together nine hundred metres off, which he will not
see. Then a line of black-gold light arriving, and no interval in which to do anything about it — the page
says resolution is instantaneous, and the light says so too.

What he feels is not impact. **It is recognition.** The wound that arrives is the one his body was always
going to fail at: the old break, the joint that was never right, the seam in the Shell he stopped noticing
a decade ago. There is nothing novel in it, which is the horror of it — nothing has been done to him that
was not already true of him, and the working's only contribution was **timing and address**. If he tries
to escape and the escape required an outcome the selection has already spent, the escape does not fail
dramatically; it simply does not produce that outcome, the way a key that fits turns nothing. Then the
echo goes out along the line behind him and every fragile construction near him comes down, and for a few
seconds afterwards the air where he was is still ringing in a way that other people's workings can feel.

## 5 · Counterplay and the challenge

**The fairness check.**

| Test | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Costs something that hurts | **pass, and one clause of it is due in-scene** | *"Cannot be thrown twice at full strength without the cost climbing sharply"* is a real escalating price inside an engagement, which is the rare thing. The other two — the echoed wound, and *"a period of impaired equilibrium and light sensitivity afterward"* — settle later. **And the page omits the worst cost it has**, Fractura's own *"Self-inspection … reads their own flaw population with perfect clarity at the worst possible moment,"* which for a Stage XII practitioner running ten currents at nine hundred metres is a genuine hazard the register supplies for free. |
| Stated limits | **pass, and thoroughly** | Cannot select a harm-state the structure could not sustain; cannot fire twice at full potency; does not override a being at or above Stage XII; and a Weakness list naming three specific defeats. The structural limit is the strong one, because §1(b) shows it is not a ruling but arithmetic: no flaw, nothing to select. |
| Something beats it | **pass, five routes, and only one is peer-only** | Probability-flattening; a pure Basilithe anchor; a deep Oneirion construct substituting a dream-state; Verdantia regeneration against the Dissolution component; and Stage XII parity. Four of the five need no peer, which is what keeps this off the peer-only list. |
| It has a tell | **pass** | *"Opalis draws two fingers together over a chosen target and extends her hand."* A deliberate, visible, targeted gesture. At nine hundred metres it is a tell only to somebody watching her rather than the field, which is itself a lesson. |
| Numbers in band | **FAIL on four counts** | Yield below the SSS floor (`SA-NUM-LANCE-400TJ-BELOW-SSS`); range five Grades low (`SA-NUM-LANCE-RANGE-BELOW-BAND`); speed twenty to a hundred times the Grade's Attack Speed band, which Part Six permits only *"within overchannel risk parameters"* the page never mentions (`SA-NUM-LANCE-INSTANT-VS-SSS-SPEED`); four Path gates unmet (`SA-NUM-LANCE-PATHGATE`). Force, Stage, Grade, Tier and η all check clean. |
| **Deletes a defensive mode** | **flagged** | Part Eleven: at SSS *"the Crystal's priority shifts from absorption to avoidance, Domain manipulation and reality-grade countermeasures."* The Lance's Target Response deletes the first of those three outright. It leaves the other two, which is why this is a flag and not a failure (`SA-FAIR-LANCE-DELETES-AVOIDANCE`). |

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Deny the Field — "Choose the ground by Family," and here it is decisive rather than marginal.**
  *"Terrain is a modifier on efficiency and the modifiers are large."* Vectoria is suppressed by *"Loose
  sediment, free fall, uniform soft ground"* — and §1(b) says why that is not a coincidence: **uniform is
  the condition of having no weakest link.** Fight her standing on something homogeneous and soft and the
  working's own analogue is working against it. Then note that the page needs five Families at once, so
  five suppression lists apply, and the intersection is a small and findable room.
- **Break the Boundary — "Knot and Lock."** *"**Knot** entangles a path so it cannot be traced cleanly.
  **Lock** occupies a limit so no other limit can be set there."* Against a working whose boundary
  condition is *which flaw carries the stress*, **Lock is the precise instrument**: occupy the flaw. A
  limit already set at the site the selection wants leaves the selection nowhere to be set.
- **Break the Body — "Distance," inverted.** *"Fulguria falls off with the square. Vectoria has to close.
  … **Most practitioners are a threat about closing range and are written as though they were not.**"*
  This working is the exception the register is warning about, and the numbers say by how much: **900 m is
  a B-Grade reach on an SSS working**, five Grades below its own band. So the register's advice holds
  against her more strongly than against a peer — she is not a planetary threat, she is a
  nine-hundred-metre one, and outside that she is a Stage XII practitioner with no stated answer at reach.
- **Break the Body — "Numbers, correctly used."** *"Twenty men who have been drilled to spread, to make
  him choose, and to keep a line of fire open at all times will take casualties and kill him."* The Lance
  is **single-target with a 40 m echo**, and it *"cannot be thrown twice at full strength without the cost
  climbing sharply."* A formation that spreads and makes her choose is attacking the one clause of the
  page that bites inside a scene.
- **Break the Man — "Rigidity."** *"A Trait's law is not negotiable by its bearer, and the more absolute
  it is the less he can contradict it. **Present him with the situation his own law handles worst and he
  will handle it that way regardless.**"* Her law selects the worst available failure. Give her a target
  with no failures worth selecting and she will select anyway — and the page's own *"What nobody knows"*
  asks whether the Lance takes the wound Opalis would have chosen or the one the target most fears, which
  is precisely the question of whether her law can be baited.
- **Break the Man — "Make him hold Pressure in."** *"Suppression is a skill and it is tiring. A
  practitioner who cannot afford to be identified is paying continuously for the privilege."* At Stage XII
  her passive Aura Pressure is *"Metaphysical law within 50 km"* (Part Eleven). **She cannot approach
  anything unnoticed**, and either she pays to suppress that continuously or she has announced herself
  across a county.

**The tell, stated plainly.** Two fingers drawn together, over one chosen target, then a hand extended.
It is deliberate, it is aimed, and it identifies the target before the working exists. The second tell is
the one the register gives and the page does not: fifty kilometres of pressure field that has to be
suppressed or seen. The third is the arithmetic — she is *"conservative"* by her own page's word, and a
practitioner who habitually sits at the floor of her band is a practitioner whose worst case can be
estimated.

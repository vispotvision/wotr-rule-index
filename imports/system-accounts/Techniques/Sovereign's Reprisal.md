# System account · Sovereign's Reprisal

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** A man in heavy harness is hit, repeatedly, and does not appear to be
hurt by it. At a moment of his choosing everyone standing near him goes off their feet at once, and he
looks briefly ill.

**The real phenomenon.** **Impulse-gated elastic storage and omnidirectional discharge** — a threshold
that reads momentum rather than pressure, which is why the page's stated weakness is correct, and an
energy density in the storage medium that the page's own numbers put a hundred thousand times above high
explosive.

**(a) The banking side is impulse physics, and the page's Weakness is right.** A blunt impact that stops
against a body transfers all of its momentum; a piercing strike that passes through transfers only the
momentum it loses. The arithmetic is unforgiving and counterintuitive:

- A 1 kg hammer head at 20 m/s carries **p = 20 kg·m/s** and **E = 200 J.**
- A 10 g bullet at 800 m/s carries **p = 8 kg·m/s** and **E = 3,200 J.**

**The hammer delivers two and a half times the momentum with one sixteenth of the energy.** So a threshold
that reads *impulse* is tripped by the blunt strike and not by the bullet — which is exactly the page's
*"Piercing strikes concentrate force into an area too small to trip the harness's threshold cleanly, and a
ranged harasser never lets Drakvor bank anything worth releasing."*

**But the page gives the wrong reason, and the reason matters.** Area is not what saves the spike. Pressure
is force over area, so a spike at 1 mm² and a fist at 100 cm² delivering the same force differ in pressure
by a factor of **10⁶**, and a threshold reading *pressure* would be tripped far more easily by the spike,
not less. The harness's threshold can only behave as the page describes if it reads **total impulse** — and
the system states no rule for what a Runecraft trigger threshold measures (`SA-GAP-REPRISAL-THRESHOLD-QUANTITY`).
This is not pedantry: the page's only two stated counters both depend on the answer.

**(b) Where the banked energy sits, and how dense it has to be.** The page says *"real Aether pressure
sitting in the harness's own lattice."* The real ceiling for storing energy in a stressed solid is the
elastic strain energy density **u = σ²/2E**. For high-strength steel at σ = 2 GPa and E = 200 GPa that is
**10 MJ/m³**, about **1.3 kJ/kg**. The best real energy-storage solids — composite flywheels — reach
roughly **200 kJ/kg**. TNT is **4.6 MJ/kg.**

Now take the page's own figure. An S-Grade sustainable impact is **4.184–24.3 TJ** (Part Eleven), and the
page releases *"two to five times"* that, so **8.4–121.5 TJ.** In a harness of, say, 20 kg that is
**420 GJ/kg at the floor** — **ninety thousand times the energy density of TNT**, and **2 × 10⁶ times the
best flywheel.** It is not a relativistic breach (c² = 9 × 10¹⁶ J/kg, so 420 GJ/kg is **4.7 ppm of rest
energy**), but it means the harness is, by mass, the most energetic object in the corpus while he is
wearing it.

**(c) And this is where the account stops closing, by five and a half orders of magnitude.** The page
spends its release across *"an eight-metre radius"* and describes the result as *"Everyone inside the
radius staggers and loses footing; the release reads as a single sudden iron-crack rather than a sustained
push."*

Work out what a stagger costs. Knocking a standing person off balance takes an incident overpressure in
the region of **20–35 kPa**. Under Hopkinson–Cranz scaling, **Z = R/W^⅓**, and 20 kPa sits near **Z ≈ 5**,
so at R = 8 m the charge is **W = (8/5)³ ≈ 4 kg TNT ≈ 17 MJ**; 35 kPa sits near Z ≈ 3.5, giving
**W ≈ 12 kg ≈ 50 MJ.**

**The stated effect needs about 20 to 50 megajoules. The stated number is 8.4 to 121 terajoules.** That is
a factor of roughly **400,000**, and the consequences are not subtle:

- 8.4 TJ is **2.0 kilotons**. The fireball radius of a 2 kt airburst is about **90 × 2^0.4 ≈ 120 m**, and
  the 5 psi radius is several hundred metres. **Drakvor is at the centre of it**, and so is the crowd the
  page describes as *"the worst possible formation against him."* At 2 kt they are not staggered.
- The page routes the discharge *"through his spine, because the harness's channels run through his own
  Shell rather than around it."* Even **one tenth of one percent** coupling into his body is **8.4 GJ**,
  against a margin to a lethal core temperature of roughly **1.2 MJ** — **seven thousand times fatal.**
- Read the other way, a 20 MJ release is **below Part Eleven's B-Grade floor of 1.046 GJ**, which puts the
  described effect five or six Grades under the working's stated Grade.

**So the page describes a D-Grade shove and prices it as an S-Grade Overchannel event**
(`SA-PHYS-REPRISAL-YIELD-VS-STAGGER`). The eight-metre radius is itself in band — Part Eleven puts
S-Grade Contact Range at **5–15 m** — so the geometry is right and only the energy is wrong.

**(d) One number the page gets exactly right, and it is worth saying so.** *"Two to five times what a
single sustainable S-Grade impact would otherwise produce (the documented Overchannel spike range at any
Tier)"* is a real, attested citation: *"Overchannel can temporarily spike Strike Force and Lifting Strength
by **two to five times** the sustainable ceiling, depending on the Ardency Overchannel Sub-Stat's Grade and
the character's tolerance for Crystal Fracture risk"* (Part Eleven). **The multiplier is correct and
correctly attributed.** What the page takes from that clause is the output; what it leaves behind is the
sentence immediately after it, which is the price (§5).

**Energy budget, stated.** Banked: **8.4–121.5 TJ** by the page's own arithmetic, in a harness of order
20 kg, at **420 GJ/kg to 6 TJ/kg**. Required by the stated effect: **2 × 10⁷ J.** Discharged through the
practitioner's spine: unstated fraction of a terajoule-class release. Where the joules were: *"The
practitioner does not supply the joules. They supply the boundary condition"* — here the joules are the
attacker's, which is the one working in this batch where the energy provenance is unambiguous and cheap.
Fault class by `Two Sets of Books` §VII: **Structural fault**, *"The Crystal could not hold what the
boundary permitted. Overchannel, Backlash, Fracture. The working succeeds and the practitioner does not"* —
which is the fault this page names in its own Cost line and then underprices.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** Two Families. **Fulguria**, *"**Spirit**"*, favoured by *"Dry air, conductive
substrate, clear line of sight"* and suppressed by *"Faraday-caged interiors, heavy ferrous shielding,
dense fog"*; **Caloria**, *"**Spirit** (Fire/Heat/Cold) · **Medium** (Light)"*, favoured by *"Steep thermal
gradients, combustible atmosphere, geothermal ground"* and suppressed by *"Thermal equilibrium, saturated
cold, oxygen-poor air"*
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings.md`). *"Terrain is not flavour. It is a
modifier on η."*

**And here the terrain reading is almost comic, because the man is a fortress engineer.** Fulguria fails
inside *"Faraday-caged interiors"* and behind *"heavy ferrous shielding"* — which is a description of the
inside of a fortification. Caloria fails in *"thermal equilibrium"* and *"saturated cold"* — a stone
bastion in winter. **A Concord siege-holder standing inside the works he built is standing in the
intersection of both suppression lists**, and his card says his efficiency goes the other way there:
*"**η** · ~0.55, Band B, rising above 0.8 inside bastions he has personally tuned."* The card and the
coupling table disagree about what a tuned bastion does to him, and the card wins on its own sheet under
R20C-34 while the coupling table is the general rule.

**Residue, and this working is made of it.** Cinerion is the Wellspring of aftermath, and its Stat Effect
line is the sentence the page quotes: *"Strengthens Tempering Conversion, Harmonics Recovery. **The residue
of destruction becomes recovery material.**"* (The page calls that clause *"Cinerion's Core Law"*; it is
the register's **Stat Effect**, and Cinerion's Mechanism is quoted below. The substance is right and the
citation is one line off.) A blow that lands on Drakvor does not become damage and does not become nothing;
it becomes **stock**.

**Saturation.** Two harmonizations across two Families — a light load, nothing near *"**Tempering
Capacity** … it is not negotiable"*, and the card supplies no figure to check it against anyway.

**Wellspring stratum — Judicium, *The Wellspring of Truth*, Fulguria, Electromagnetism.** Core Law,
verbatim from `wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Fulguria — Electromagnetism.md`:

> **Analogue** · Emission and absorption spectroscopy. Nondestructive flaw detection.
> **Mechanism** · Every element absorbs and emits at wavelengths fixed by its own electron structure, and
> no substance can present another's spectrum. Judicium tunes Gnosis to that principle: the practitioner
> reads the characteristic lines of a Crystal, a technique, or a lie, and identifies composition from
> signature alone. Eddy-current inspection finds the crack under the paint without removing the paint.

**"Nondestructive flaw detection" is precisely the right law for a threshold gate.** Eddy-current
inspection is a non-contact measurement that tells you what a thing is without doing anything to it, and
that is what the harness needs: a reading of how much has been banked that does not itself spend any of it.
The page's *"reading exactly how much force has been banked and confirming the threshold has genuinely been
reached"* is Judicium doing metrology rather than judgement. Its **Failure** is the occupational hazard:
*"Spectral overload. Every object in view reports its composition simultaneously and the practitioner loses
the ability to prioritise any of it"* — a man who must read one threshold cleanly, in a crowd, while being
hit.

**Wellspring stratum — Cinerion, *The Ash Veil*, Caloria, Thermodynamics.** Core Law, verbatim:

> **Analogue** · Pyrolytic char and its catalytic residue. Particulate optical depth.
> **Mechanism** · What survives a fire is not waste. Char is a high-surface-area carbon scaffold that
> catalyses further reaction, and settled ash returns potassium and phosphorus to the ground that fed the
> fire. Cinerion retunes the Shell to metabolize its own combustion products, harvesting from expenditure
> what other practitioners simply vent. The suspended residue also scatters incident light and
> Essence-sight alike.

**And Cinerion's Failure answers the page's own open question outright.** The card asks *"Whether the
harness has a ceiling past which it cannot hold any more, or whether Drakvor has simply never been hit hard
enough, for long enough, to find out."* The register: *"**Fouling.** Accumulated residue eventually coats
the conduit rather than feeding it, and recovery rate collapses precisely when the practitioner has spent
the most."* **There is a ceiling, and it is not a capacity.** It is a fouling curve: the harness does not
fill up and refuse, it silently stops banking efficiently, and the collapse arrives at the moment of
greatest accumulation. Which is a far better answer than a number, because it cannot be felt coming and it
fails in the direction of a man who believes he is still charged.

**The Sub-Stat list derives exactly from the two registers — and misses the one Sub-Stat that names the
mechanic.** Judicium strengthens *"Gnosis Acuity, Analysis, Diagnosis"*, and Diagnosis → **Analysis** under
the Merge Ledger, so Acuity and Analysis. Cinerion strengthens *"Tempering Conversion, Harmonics
Recovery"*, and Conversion → **Yield**, Recovery → **Stability**. The page's FOW line is *"Gnosis Acuity
and Gnosis Analysis … Tempering Yield and Harmonics Stability"* — **all four, nothing left over.**

**But Part Twelve defines a fifth Sub-Stat as being exactly this working**, and the page does not name it:
*"**Fortification** — Compressing incoming hostile Essence and redirecting it through internal structure
rather than absorbing it as damage, and resistance to self-generated blowback from Overchannel rebound and
interrupted high-tier workings. Converting a wound, or a mistake, into something the Crystal can
metabolise."* That is Sovereign's Reprisal in three clauses, including the Overchannel rebound the page's
Cost describes. Its gate would also pass: *"Fortification requires Body Path at Stage IV to exceed B"*, and
the page declares Body Path while the card gives *"Body dominant."* **The page derived its Sub-Stats from
what its Wellsprings strengthen and never asked what the working does**
(`SA-CROSS-REPRISAL-FORTIFICATION-MISSING`).

**Which of the Four Theories.** **Debt**, and no page in the batch wears it more plainly. Drakvor's own
framing is the word: *"Impact is tribute."* A blow landed on him is not absorbed, forgiven or dissipated —
it is **booked**, held on the ledger, and called in as a single payment at a time of the creditor's
choosing. **Residue** runs underneath it as the mechanism by which a debt can be held at all, because
Cinerion is the law that lets an expenditure leave something behind that can be counted.

**Essence stratum.** Crystal layer: the **Aether Shell**, Class V Radiant on the card — *"Aether bends
toward him; it is why a wall he has personally reinforced holds better than the same wall anywhere else"* —
with the discharge path running **through the Shell rather than around it**, which is the page's stated
reason the cost lands in his spine. Developmental Tier **Radiant** (Stage VIII, Part Twenty-One). Tier of
Standing **6 · Master**. Crystal State *"Refined, overbuilt by design."* **The invoice:** an inscription
maintained on his own body, a threshold read under fire, and a discharge that uses him as the conduit
because there is nowhere else for it to go.

**The lens: Hobbes, and the sovereign who is made of what his subjects gave up.** Thomas Hobbes,
*Leviathan* (1651), ch. XVII, with *De Corpore* (1655) underneath it. Hobbes's physics is a physics of
**endeavour** — *conatus*, the infinitesimal beginning of motion — and his politics is built directly on it:
every subject surrenders his own force, the sovereign accumulates the whole of it, and what returns is not
a hundred men pushing but **one act carrying the weight of all of them.** *"A multitude of men, are made
one person."* The sovereign creates nothing. He is the aggregate, made single and made lawful, and his
power is precisely the sum of what was handed in.

**Sovereign's Reprisal is that argument worn as harness.** The crowd presses in and hands over its force
one blow at a time; the harness books each surrender; and what comes back out is not twenty impacts but
one, with an authority none of them had separately. *"Impact is tribute"* is the covenant of *Leviathan*
stated as a fighting doctrine, and the page's own Consequence is Hobbes's conclusion: *"a mob banking his
impacts for him is a mob arming his counter."*

**The productive misreading, and what it kills.** A Tributary school reads the sovereignty half literally
and draws the obvious inference: if authority *is* the accumulated tribute, then **a practitioner who has
banked more is more sovereign, and discharging spends the sovereignty rather than exercising it.** So they
bank and do not release. They take blows as a discipline, they measure their standing by what they are
carrying, and the finest Tributary is the one who has never once given it back — which reads, from outside,
as superhuman patience and is experienced from inside as accumulating authority.

**Cinerion's Fouling kills them, and kills them at the top.** *"Accumulated residue eventually coats the
conduit rather than feeding it, and recovery rate collapses precisely when the practitioner has spent the
most."* A Tributary who never discharges never clears the conduit, so the residue coats it, and the
harness stops banking — silently, with no full-up signal, because the system has no capacity gauge and
Judicium's threshold reading tells him only about the incoming blow and not about the conduit carrying it.
**He goes on taking hits believing he is charging and he is not charging.** The school records its masters
as men of infinite forbearance. What they were was men whose armour had quietly stopped working, and who
found out at the moment they finally chose to answer.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**The glyph, proposed.** `[Rt]` **Return** for the discharge, `[Lk]` **Lock** for the banking, and `[Me]`
**Measure** for Judicium's threshold. The page names no glyph, so all three are filings under the Index's
Standing Note — *"Assignment is decided after the phenomenon, never before, and it is a filing decision
rather than a generative one"* — and none is canon until Isaac rules (`SA-GAP-GLYPH-MIRROR`). `[Lk]` is one
of the Index's two Counter forms and the fit is exact: *"**Lock** occupies a limit so no other limit can be
set there"*, which is what it means to hold an impact instead of letting it resolve.

**What boundary moves.** Not the force and not the harness. **The boundary that moves is where an impact is
permitted to finish.** An impact is a transfer of momentum that ordinarily completes in a few milliseconds:
the blow lands, the momentum distributes into the body, the energy becomes deformation and heat, and the
event is over. The boundary condition says *not yet*. The Index is explicit about the shape of that:
*"A glyph creates nothing and carries no power. … It moves a limit, and the law does what it has always
done on the far side of the new limit."* **The harness adds nothing to the blow.** It postpones its
conclusion.

**What the law then does on its own.** Everything, in both directions. Going in, the transfer completes
into the harness's lattice instead of into bone, and Cinerion's law makes the difference a *residue* rather
than a loss — *"harvesting from expenditure what other practitioners simply vent."* Nothing is being held
by effort; it is being held the way a strained spring holds, which is to say by the same law that will
release it. Judicium runs alongside as metrology, non-destructive, reading the characteristic lines of what
has arrived without spending any of it — *"eddy-current inspection finds the crack under the paint without
removing the paint."*

**Then he stops postponing, and the whole ledger resolves at once.** There is no setting between held and
spent, because the boundary is a *when* rather than a *how much*: releasing the condition releases all of
it. And because the harness's channels *"run through his own Shell rather than around it"*, the path out
is him — which is not a design flaw but the reason the working can be reactive at all. A conduit that runs
around the body needs to be aimed. One that runs through it is always already connected.

**The release is omnidirectional because nothing directed it.** This is the detail the page states and does
not explain: the discharge is *"a single ring"* across eight metres rather than a blow, because the banked
quantity was never given a vector. A blunt impact arriving from the front, banked, and then released, has
had its direction erased by the storage — the harness held a scalar. So it leaves as a pressure step in
every direction at once, and the page's *"does not discriminate by target"* is not a moral observation, it
is what a scalar release must do.

**The failure modes, and each is named somewhere the page is not looking.** **Fouling**, Cinerion's, which
is the ceiling the page says nobody knows about. **Spectral overload**, Judicium's, which is the threshold
misread in a crowd. **Overchannel damage**, Part Eleven's: *"Every Overchannel event deposits structural
damage that must be healed through rest, alchemical treatment or Temperance advancement. A character who
regularly Overchannels accumulates fracture density that eventually becomes permanent."* And the
substrate route, which is how every Runecraft working ends: the harness is *"a fixed inscription"*, and an
inscription is a thing that can be broken. The Four Faults reading is **Structural fault** — *"The Crystal
could not hold what the boundary permitted … The working succeeds and the practitioner does not."*

**What the target sees and feels.** For a long time, nothing worth reporting. He hits a very large man in
heavy harness and the man does not react correctly — not staggering, not absorbing, not giving ground, just
receiving. There is no flare and no charge-up glow, because a Runecraft inscription is not being sustained
by attention; it is simply installed. The only reading available is the wrongness of the feedback: a strike
that lands solidly and returns nothing up the arm, over and over, which is the tell and which takes
discipline to notice while it is working in your favour.

Then the cyan sigil over his eye brightens, and the interval between that and the release is the whole
window anyone gets.

**What arrives is not a push.** The page's word is *"iron-crack"*, and that is right: a pressure step, not
a shove — a single discontinuity that arrives everywhere at once and takes the ground out from under a
formation rather than knocking it backwards. Men do not fly. They simply stop being on their feet, all of
them, in the same instant, and the ones who were pressed closest and hitting hardest contributed most to
what just happened to them. Afterwards there is a large man breathing carefully with his eyes shut for a
few seconds, and that is the only moment in the exchange when he is available.

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Break the Boundary — "Wards," which is the counter the page omits and the system's standing answer to
  its own Craft.** The page's Craft line says the harness is *"a fixed inscription."* The register:
  *"Runecraft standing on a substrate, and therefore beaten the way Runecraft is beaten: **damage the
  substrate.** A ward is not argued with. It is chiselled off, or the wall it sits on is brought down, or
  the third layer is scraped through by somebody who knows which layer to scrape."* **The harness is the
  substrate.** Break the plates and there is nothing to bank into, and the card names the plates for you.
  Note that this route is *helped* by the page's own weakness, not blocked by it: a piercing strike is both
  the thing the threshold cannot read and the thing that gets through to the inscription.
- **Deny the Field — "Choose the ground by Family," and the intersection is small and specific.** Fulguria
  fails in *"Faraday-caged interiors, heavy ferrous shielding, dense fog"*; Caloria fails in *"Thermal
  equilibrium, saturated cold, oxygen-poor air."* **A cold, damp, iron-shielded interior in thermal
  equilibrium suppresses both of this working's Families at once** — and the uncomfortable part, which the
  lookup trail leaves for the reader, is what kind of structure that describes.
- **Break the Boundary — "Knot and Lock."** *"**Lock** occupies a limit so no other limit can be set
  there."* The harness's whole function is to occupy the limit where an impact would finish. **A Lock set
  there first leaves the harness nowhere to put its own condition** — the one counter-form that addresses
  the working rather than the man or the plates.
- **Break the Body — "Distance," and the page has already conceded it.** *"Vectoria has to close. …
  **Most practitioners are a threat about closing range and are written as though they were not.**"* This
  working reaches eight metres and requires being hit. Its own Counter line says *"sustained ranged
  harassment"* beats it. Those are the same sentence read from two sides.
- **Break the Man — "Rigidity," which here means his formation discipline rather than a Trait.** *"Present
  him with the situation his own law handles worst and he will handle it that way regardless."* The
  harness fires omnidirectionally and *"does not discriminate by target."* Put his own allies close around
  him and the working's best moment becomes its worst one — and the page says so: *"a mob banking his
  impacts for him is a mob arming his counter"* cuts both ways, because a mob of his own is a mob he cannot
  answer over.
- **Break the Man — "Coherence drift," the long version.** *"You do not have to cause this. You have to
  notice it and wait."* Read against Cinerion's **Fouling** and the harness's absent capacity gauge, this
  becomes literal: a man whose conduit is silting up has no way to know, and neither does anyone else until
  he tries to spend. **The Tributary failure in §2 is available to any opponent patient enough to make him
  bank and never let him release.**

**The tell, stated plainly.** No glow, no gather, no held breath — a Runecraft harness is installed and
does not announce itself. What announces itself is the feedback: strikes that land clean and give nothing
back, which reads as toughness and is bookkeeping. Then the cyan sigil over his right eye brightens, and
after the release there are a few seconds of a very large man standing still with his eyes closed.

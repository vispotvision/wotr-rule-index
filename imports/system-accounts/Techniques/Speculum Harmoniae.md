# System account · Speculum Harmoniae

> **The page carries two workings.** `wiki/Techniques/Speculum Harmoniae.md:79` says so in its own
> words — *"Bundled into this same Trello card as item "7." in the source list. Converted alongside
> Speculum Harmoniae rather than dropped"* — so **Nexus Harmoniae** is accounted for here rather than
> left out. They are the same practice at two scales and the page says that too (`:132`): the Nexus
> *"reads as the field-scale extension of the same resonance-reading practice behind Speculum Harmoniae
> and Lumen Dissecans."* Each of the five sections below takes both, in that order.

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** *(Speculum)* A man chasing a woman ends up chasing a second woman
who moves and answers like the first, and who stops existing the moment anything real touches her.
*(Nexus)* A woman stands still in the middle of a battle and everything anyone does for a kilometre
around her works slightly better if they are on her side.

**The real phenomenon.** *(Speculum)* **Decoy discrimination in a tracker's measurement basis** — the
physics of repeater decoys and flare rejection, where the question is never whether two objects are
*resolvable* but whether the seeker measures any quantity in which they *differ*. *(Nexus)*
**Coherent versus incoherent summation across an aperture** — matched-filter gain, with the Rayleigh
criterion setting what the field can tell apart and the inverse-square law setting how far the gain
reaches.

### Speculum Harmoniae

**(a) This is not a resolution problem, and saying so is what makes the working legible.** The
instinct is to reach for the diffraction limit, and the diffraction limit rules the decoy out. Two
sources separated by Δx are unresolvable at distance d only when Δx < 1.22 λd/D. For an unaided eye —
pupil D = 5 mm, λ = 550 nm — at fifty metres that is **6.7 mm**. A second body standing metres from
the first is resolved by anyone looking, at any range a fight happens at. **The double is plainly a
separate body. It is not hidden and the page never claims it is.**

So the working is not concealment. It is **misidentification**, and misidentification has its own
exact physics: a tracker rejects a decoy only if it measures some quantity in which target and decoy
differ **by more than its own measurement noise**. This is the whole of decoy engineering. A
single-colour infrared seeker is beaten by a pyrotechnic flare because the flare matches the one
quantity the seeker measures — radiant intensity in one band — and a **two-colour** seeker rejects
the same flare instantly, because the ratio between two bands is a quantity in which a burning
magnesium–Teflon grain and a turbofan exhaust are not remotely alike. Nothing about the flare
changed. A dimension was added to the measurement, and in that dimension the lie was never there to
begin with.

**The page's stated Counter is therefore not flavour. It is the correct physics, stated correctly.**
*"A working that reads the reversion's shape before it completes, or a sense keyed to fear, hatred,
or grief rather than surface signature, tells the two bodies apart. A hunter tracking emotion rather
than Essence contour is not fooled"* (`:26`). That is a two-colour seeker, in words. The Speculum
matches the channel Serenyra read; a channel she did not read is orthogonal to the copy, and in an
orthogonal channel the double has no signal at all rather than a wrong one. **Which also means the
counter is not a matter of being stronger. It is a matter of measuring something else**, and that is
the cheapest counter in this batch and the hardest to find, because it asks the opponent to change
instruments rather than to push harder.

**(b) The energy budget is trivial, and that is the finding.** Take the most demanding channel the
double has to satisfy — visible light. A standing person presents a projected area of roughly 0.8 m²
to a viewer and scatters daylight at an albedo near 0.2, so under 1 kW/m² insolation the real body is
a source of about **160 W**; indoors it is a thousandth of that. The construct holds *"roughly three
exchanges"* (`:49`), and with no turn length anywhere in the system (`SA-GAP-TURN-LENGTH`) even a
generous six seconds an exchange puts the optical bill at **≈2.9 kJ**. Part Eleven's **B-Grade** attack
output floor — the Grade this working requires — is **1.046 × 10⁹ J**. **The double's entire radiant
budget is about three millionths of its own Grade's floor.**

Mass is the other side of it, and the page's Limit is what keeps the working inside relativity. A
70 kg body would cost **mc² = 6.3 × 10¹⁸ J**, a gigatonne and a half, which is above the **SSS** row
and four Grades above the page's own. The page instead states *"Touch-based senses notice the double
has no mass behind it"* (`:53`) and *"The construct is phantasmal. It cannot deal real damage"*
(`:52`). **The Limit is not a nerf. It is the clause that stops the working breaching E = mc², and it
is the only reason a B-Grade technique can produce a body at all.**

**(c) So the cost has to be informational, and the page charges informationally — by instinct rather
than by system.** *"Every mirror spends a slice of her own sensory record of the target; the next time
she needs to counter that same person precisely, the read is thinner"* (`:24`). Thermodynamics will
not explain that: specifying the visible surface at 1 mm resolution over 0.8 m² is about 8 × 10⁵
patches, and at twenty-four bits each that is ≈1.9 × 10⁷ bits, whose Landauer erasure cost at 310 K
(k_BT ln2 = 2.97 × 10⁻²¹ J/bit) is **5.6 × 10⁻¹⁴ J.** Not a cost. Not a constraint.

The real precedent is a **capacity** cost with a **destructive readout**, and the mundane example is
exact: **coincident-current magnetic-core memory**. Reading a core drives it toward the zero state,
which means the act of reading destroys the datum, which means every read cycle must be followed by a
restore write — the access time plus the rewrite *is* the memory cycle time, and a core read without
its write-back is a bit that is simply gone. **Speculum Harmoniae skips the write-back.** She reads
her stored record to drive the construct, the read is destructive, and nothing restores it. That is
why repetition against the same person degrades the read and why observing them again repairs it: the
restore cycle is done with her eyes. The page describes destructive readout without naming it, and it
is the best-reasoned cost on any Serenyra page in this batch.

**And that cost has a Sub-Stat, which the page does not name.** *"**Retention** — Clarity and
accessibility of the Crystalline Memory structure under pressure, and the number of complete working
structures held loaded in active cognitive memory without external inscription. *Absorbs Memorium.*"*
(Part Twelve). The page's FOW line gives *"Gnosis Cognition, Harmonics Attunement, Dominion
Sovereignty"* (`:62`) — and **Cognition is defined as the stat that resists exactly this working**:
*"resistance to glamours, false spatial constructs and psychic misdirection."* The page has listed the
counter-stat as a component (`SA-CROSS-SPECULUM-RETENTION-DENSITY`).

**(d) The Mechanism names a law that runs the wrong way.** The page: *"Specular reflection, the same
phase-conjugate law Mirithane runs on: what leaves the Shell returns the wavefront that was put into
it"* (`:48`). Optical phase conjugation is real and is one of the prettiest effects in optics, but its
defining property is **time reversal**: the conjugate wave retraces the incoming path *backwards*
through whatever distorted it, which is why it undoes aberration and why a phase-conjugate mirror
returns a beam to its source rather than sending anything onward. **A conjugate mirror cannot produce
a second forward-propagating source that walks away from the first.** The device that does that is a
**repeater** — a transponder that receives a signature and re-radiates it from somewhere else — and a
repeater is the opposite of a conjugate mirror in the only respect that matters here: it does not send
the wave back, it sends a new wave out. Mirithane's register is, in its own words, about what *leaves
the practitioner*; the page has it copying a third party outward
(`SA-PHYS-SPECULUM-CONJUGATION-DIRECTION`).

**(e) And the working's own Wellspring pair, read across the page, says a signature cannot be
counterfeited.** Nexus Harmoniae's governing Wellspring is **Judicium**, whose Mechanism is quoted in
full in §2: *"Every element absorbs and emits at wavelengths fixed by its own electron structure, and
**no substance can present another's spectrum**."* That is a uniqueness law, and it is the law
Judicium identifies by. **One half of this page runs on the premise that a signature is unforgeable
and the other half forges one.** The two only coexist if an Essence signature is *not* a
spectroscopic quantity — in which case Judicium's identification is doing something softer than the
register claims. Both readings are available and the page takes neither
(`SA-CROSS-SPECULUM-SIGNATURE-UNIQUENESS`).

### Nexus Harmoniae

**(f) The bias has a real mechanism, and it is the reason the bias must be small.** The page insists
twice that the effect is modest — *"The bias is small. It is a strategist's tool, not a battlefield-
ending one"* (`:85`), *"The bias is deliberately subtle. It is a strategic asset, not a spectacle, and
cannot win an exchange outright"* (`:112`) — and states no figure (`:109`). The physics supplies both
the mechanism and the magnitude.

A field cannot favour a side, because *allegiance is not a physical parameter*. What a field can do is
**correlate**. Sum N contributions that share a phase reference and the amplitudes add: gain ∝ **N**.
Sum N contributions that do not, and the powers add: gain ∝ **√N**. This is the whole of coherent
integration, and it is why a phased array beats the same aperture wired incoherently. So a field that
imposes *"one shared frequency"* (`:96`) on everything crossing it gives **coherent** gain to whatever
is phase-locked to its reference and **incoherent** gain to whatever is not, and the advantage between
them goes as **√N**. Small N, small edge. **The bias is subtle because √N is a slow function, and the
page's refusal to quantify it is, for once, the physically honest answer.**

**(g) Aurevane is the right Wellspring for the phase reference and the wrong one for the range
claim.** The page: *"Aurevane's illumination law carries that reading across the whole field at once
rather than letting it fade with distance, which is what makes the effect battlefield-scale instead of
line-of-sight"* (`:108`). Aurevane's register: *"Two points are resolvable only when their diffraction
patterns separate, and coherent light holds a fixed phase relationship across its whole front, which
is what makes a laser a laser rather than a bright lamp."* **Coherence preserves phase. It does not
preserve amplitude.** A laser still spreads and still obeys the inverse square law; what it does not
lose is the phase relationship across its front. So Aurevane supplies precisely the phase reference
paragraph (f) needs — and supplies nothing at all against distance falloff. **The bias must therefore
be strongest at her feet and weakest at the rim, and the page states a flat one-kilometre envelope**
(`SA-PHYS-NEXUS-COHERENCE-VS-FALLOFF`).

**(h) Aurevane also answers the page's own open question, and answers it with resolution rather than
sensitivity.** The page asks: *"Whether the nexus reads every working in range or only the ones loud
enough for her to notice, and what a working sits just under that line would have to look like"*
(`:87`). The register's Analogue is *"Optical resolution and coherence. **The Rayleigh criterion**;
phase-locked emission"* — so the line is not a loudness floor, it is a **separation** floor. Two
workings whose patterns do not separate are not missed; they are read **as one working**. And a
working that ducks under her is not necessarily a quiet one: it is one whose pattern sits inside
somebody else's. The real-world name for the class is a **low-probability-of-intercept** emitter —
spread the signal wide and thin and long, and an intercept receiver integrating over the wrong window
finds nothing above its own noise even though the total energy is unremarkable. **The page's stated
Counterplay — *"A Wellspring technique built to run quiet ducks under her detection floor entirely"*
(`:115`) — is correct, and the register makes it cheaper than the page thinks:** you do not have to be
faint, you have to be *unseparated*.

**(i) The arbitration latency, which nobody has costed and which is the best counter on the page.**
The Nexus is centrally arbitrated: workings are *"routed through the field before its outcome
resolves, **which is where Serenyra applies her bias**"* (`:107`). She applies it. So every biased
working must survive a round trip — signature to her, decision back — and the field is a kilometre in
radius.

- Light takes **3.3 µs** to cross a kilometre, so the channel floor is **6.7 µs** round trip.
- Her own Reaction Speed at **S-Grade** is **0.05–0.2 ms**, i.e. **50–200 µs** (Part Six).

**She is the bottleneck by a factor of seven to thirty.** Anything that resolves faster than her
reaction window cannot be routed at all, and Part Six says what resolves that fast: **S-Grade Attack
Speed is Mach 50–500**, and at Mach 500 (≈170 km/s) a strike crosses the S-Grade Contact Range of
**5–15 m** in **30–90 µs** — inside her floor. An **SS**-Grade opponent, whose reaction is *"below
0.05 ms"*, is outside her arbitration entirely. **The Nexus cannot bias a close, fast exchange, and
the page lists neither this limit nor this counter** (`SA-PHYS-NEXUS-ARBITRATION-LATENCY`).

**(j) One number lands exactly, in the right column.** *"Radius up to roughly one kilometre, centred
on her"* (`:109`). Part Eleven's **S-Grade** row gives Projected Force Range 20–50 km and **Passive
Pressure Field "Storm reverence within 1 km."** The Nexus is a passive field and not projected force,
so the correct column is the third one, and the page's figure is **the S-Grade passive field radius to
the digit**. That is a clean pass and the only unambiguous numerical success on the page.

**Energy budget, stated.** *Speculum:* radiant ≈**2.9 kJ** across three exchanges; rest-mass **zero**
by the page's own Limit (a real body would be 6.3 × 10¹⁸ J); informational **≈1.9 × 10⁷ bits** whose
Landauer floor is 5.6 × 10⁻¹⁴ J, so the binding cost is store capacity and not energy at all.
*Nexus:* not computable — the field's joules are ambient and the working supplies a phase reference,
and no attested figure prices either. Where the joules were: *"The practitioner does not supply the
joules. They supply the boundary condition"* (`Two Sets of Books`); here the Speculum's joules are the
room's light being scattered in a pattern it would not otherwise have taken, and the Nexus's are each
working's own, re-summed. **Fault class.** *Speculum:* **Field fault** — *"The room could not supply
what the working assumed. Saturation, depletion, a cut main, a Silence Realm. The working is correct
and produces nothing"* (§VII), which is Animatria's canonical death and is named in the Counterplay
register. *Nexus:* **Structural fault** — *"The Crystal could not hold what the boundary permitted …
The working succeeds and the practitioner does not"*, which is the migraine the page charges.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum, Speculum Harmoniae — and the Family suppression list is the page's quietest
problem.** **Limina**, *"**Spirit** primary · **Medium** at Veil-Thin density and above"*, favoured by
*"Darkness, silence, Aether-thin ground, ruins"* and suppressed by *"Saturated Aether, **crowd
density**, **active Domains**"* (`wiki/The Magic System/The Eight Families & the Sixty
Wellsprings.md:154`). *"Terrain is not flavour. It is a modifier on η."*

**A decoy is worth most in a crowd, and crowd density suppresses the Family it is built from.** That
much is ordinary irony and is the kind of thing a veteran would know. The harder one is *active
Domains*, because Serenyra holds a Domain — *"The Lattice of Living Light (Prototype) · **Current
Domain, nascent and portable**"* (card `:99`) — and Nexus Harmoniae is a field of the same shape and
the same size. **If "active Domains" includes the practitioner's own, then standing inside her own
Nexus she suppresses her own Limina workings, the Speculum among them, and the two halves of this page
cannot be used together.** The table does not say whose Domains it means
(`SA-GAP-LIMINA-OWN-DOMAIN`).

**Wellspring stratum — Mirithane, *The Wellspring of Reflection*, Limina, Entropy, Void and Mind.**
Verbatim from `wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Limina — Entropy, Void and Mind.md:40`:

> **Analogue** · Specular reflection. The law of reflection; the phase-conjugate mirror.
> **Mechanism** · A true mirror adds nothing. Angle in equals angle out, and fidelity is defined
> entirely by the absence of introduced distortion. A phase-conjugate mirror goes further and returns
> a wavefront that undoes its own aberration on the way back. Mirithane makes the Shell such a
> surface, so what leaves the practitioner is what was in them, with no flattering correction applied
> in transit.
> **Stat Effect** · Strengthens Harmonics Fidelity, Tempering Coherence. Output becomes a more honest
> reflection of internal state.
> **Failure** · The mirror does not discriminate. A practitioner in a state they would rather not

**And that Failure destroys the page's own Counter, in the case the page leads with.** The Effect is
*"A pursuer chasing Serenyra, **or anyone she has read**"* (`:23`) — so the primary use is mirroring
*herself*. Mirithane's law then applies at full strength and without discrimination: what leaves her
is what was in her, **including the fear of the man chasing her**. The page's Counter requires the
opposite: *"a sense keyed to fear, hatred, or grief rather than surface signature, tells the two bodies
apart"* (`:26`). **An honest mirror copies the affect and the emotional hunter is fooled; a
surface-only read does not copy it and the Mechanism's fidelity claim is false.** The page holds both
(`SA-EM-SPECULUM-MIRROR-HONESTY`). *Note also the Failure's other edge, which nobody has to counter
because she inflicts it on herself: a mirror that broadcasts her state perfectly hands a reader the
one thing she most needs hidden while she runs.*

**Wellspring stratum — Oneirion, *The Wellspring of Dream*, Limina.** Verbatim, same file `:47`:

> **Analogue** · Configuration space. A system's state as a point in an abstract high-dimensional
> manifold.
> **Mechanism** · A mechanical system's every possible arrangement is a coordinate in a space that has
> no physical location, and the system's evolution is a trajectory through it. Oneirion lets a working
> act on that manifold rather than on the room. This is the mechanical reason Oneirion techniques
> reach targets no line of sight connects, and the reason they are so difficult to describe afterward.
> **Stat Effect** · Strengthens Gnosis Cognition, Dominion Reach, Ardency Spectral.
> **Failure** · Projection error. The trajectory was correct in the manifold and lands nowhere in
> particular when it is mapped back down into a room.

**This is where the page's Sub-Stat list comes from, and it is a derivation rather than a design.**
Oneirion strengthens *"Gnosis Cognition, Dominion Reach, Ardency Spectral"*; under the Merge Ledger
Reach → **Sovereignty** and Spectral → **Cascade**. The page's list is *"Gnosis Cognition, Harmonics
Attunement, Dominion Sovereignty"* — Cognition and Sovereignty straight off Oneirion, Attunement off
Mirithane's Fidelity → **Attunement**. **Every Sub-Stat on the page is what its Wellsprings
strengthen, and none is what the working does.** The working *stores a read* (Retention) and *stands
a construct up* (**Ardency Density**, *"Structural weight and coherence of anything a working leaves
standing: inscriptions, active glyph-chains, summoned constructs, bound spirits. Whether your ward
outlasts the siege and whether your summon is a suggestion or a participant"*). **The page's Limit —
*"Struck once by anything that matters, it stops existing"* — is a Density statement in plain
English**, and Density is not on the list (`SA-CROSS-SPECULUM-RETENTION-DENSITY`).

**Oneirion's Failure is also the failure mode the page does not state.** *Projection error* — right in
the manifold, nowhere in particular in the room. A decoy that arrives standing in a wall, or facing
the wrong way, or walking a line the terrain does not permit, is not a fictional failure: **it is the
one kinematic implausibility that lets an opponent discriminate without changing instruments**, which
is exactly how real seekers reject decoys that pass every radiometric test and then decelerate at a
rate no aircraft could survive.

**Wellspring stratum — Aurevane, *The Wellspring of Light*, Fulguria.** Verbatim, same file `:26`:

> **Analogue** · Optical resolution and coherence. The Rayleigh criterion; phase-locked emission.
> **Mechanism** · Two points are resolvable only when their diffraction patterns separate, and
> coherent light holds a fixed phase relationship across its whole front, which is what makes a laser
> a laser rather than a bright lamp. Aurevane raises both figures at once.
> **Stat Effect** · Strengthens Gnosis Perception, Vigilance, Harmonics Fidelity.
> **Failure** · Glare. Coherent output is also a beacon, and Aurevane practitioners are the easiest
> people in any engagement to locate.

**Aurevane's Failure is the counter the Nexus hands away for free.** The Limit says the field *"drops
the instant she loses consciousness or leaves the field"* (`:85`) — so the whole kilometre depends on
one body — and the register says that body is *"the easiest person in any engagement to locate."* **A
working whose single point of failure advertises itself is not subtle no matter how subtle its bias
is**, and the page, which calls the Nexus *"not a spectacle"*, never notices that its own secondary
Wellspring makes the caster one.

**Aether stratum, Nexus Harmoniae.** **Fulguria**, *"**Spirit**"*, favoured by *"Dry air, conductive
substrate, clear line of sight"*, suppressed by *"Faraday-caged interiors, heavy ferrous shielding,
dense fog"*. A battlefield supplies both lists at once — clear sightlines and then smoke — and the
suppression list is a plain description of what a kilometre of powder smoke or fog does to a working
that needs a coherent phase reference across its whole front.

**Saturation.** Four harmonizations across two Families and two Physics Domains, *"Entropy, Void and
Mind"* against *"Electromagnetism"* — and the Families page names the shape of that: *"Families whose
domains are structurally opposed produce tension the Crystal must resolve"*, with **Materia against
Limina** as the canonical hard case. Limina against Fulguria is not on the named list either way, so
there is no rule to apply, and the card supplies no Tempering Capacity figure to check the load
against.

**Which of the Four Theories.** **Correspondence**, for both halves and for different reasons. The
Speculum is the doctrine at its most literal: a likeness *is* a kind of presence, and a signature
faithfully reproduced is treated by the world as the thing it is a signature of. The Nexus is
correspondence as administration — every working in a kilometre made to answer to one shared tone, so
that things which merely resemble each other are compelled to actually match. **Residue** runs under
the Speculum's cost, since a spent read leaves a thinner record behind rather than nothing.

**Essence stratum.** Crystal layer: the **Attraction Layer**, which on this card *is* the Domain —
*"Every Aether thread inside the Lattice of Living Light is legible, a spell-tapestry and an archive
at once"* (card `:38`). Developmental Tier **Radiant** (Stage VIII, Part Twenty-One). Tier of Standing
**6 · Master**. Crystal State *"Refined"*, η *"~0.70, Band B"* (card `:39`). **The invoice:** for the
Speculum, a destructive read of her own memory and a fragment of her Essence Core sent out on a
line she must keep alive; for the Nexus, continuous simultaneous attention to every working in a
kilometre, paid in the currency Judicium's register says it is paid in.

**The lens: the multiplication of species.** Roger Bacon, *De multiplicatione specierum* (c. 1260),
standing on al-Kindī's *De radiis* and Grosseteste's optics of light. Bacon's doctrine is that every
body continuously **multiplies its species** — its likeness — outward through the medium, and that
this propagation is how one thing acts on another at a distance at all: optical, astrological and
psychic causation are one mechanism. Perception is not the mind reaching out to a body; it is the
reception of a species that the body has been emitting all along. **The likeness is a real thing in
transit, and the medium it crosses alters it.**

Read the page through that and both halves become one idea. **Speculum Harmoniae detaches a species
from its source** — takes the likeness that a body was emitting anyway and lets it go on propagating
with the body no longer behind it, which in Bacon's terms is not a lie at all but a species that has
outlived its agent. **Nexus Harmoniae works the other half of the doctrine**: if the medium alters
every species that crosses it, then own the medium. A kilometre of tuned air is Bacon's *medium*
promoted to an instrument, and *"every working cast inside a battlefield-scale radius passes through
an invisible crossroads first"* is very nearly his sentence.

**The productive misreading, and what it kills.** A **Speculist** school takes the doctrine's
strongest claim at its word: the species is a real thing in the medium, therefore it does not need its
agent once emitted. So they cast and **let go** — no attention held, no line maintained, the double
sent out to live on the room's own propagation while its maker walks away in the other direction. It
is elegant, it is economical, and it is precisely what the taxonomy forbids: *"an Animatria construct
is a small detached fragment of the caster's Essence Core given temporary independent operation … the
construct's continuity depends on a connection back to its source"* (`The Magical Categories`, §15).
The page knows this and says so (`:36`).

**What kills them is a quiet room, and they never see it as an attack.** The Counterplay register's
Silence clause: *"Animatria constructs fail completely because their continuity runs back to a source
the Silence has severed. **A soul held in one reports the sensation as loneliness rather than
weakness**."* A Speculist who has trained himself to release the line has trained himself to be
indistinguishable, from the inside, from a man whose line has been cut — so the one diagnostic the
register offers is the one he has deliberately learned to ignore. **The school's masters are the ones
who let go most completely.** They walk into thin ground, release as usual, and lose the fragment
rather than the decoy; and because the doctrine says a species needs no agent, the loss reads to them
as a success. What the school records as mastery of detachment is a slow accounting of Essence Core
fragments left in quiet rooms.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**The glyphs, proposed.** *Speculum:* `[Eq]` **Equivalence** for the copy, `[Et]` **Mirage** for what
it is taken for, `[Rv]` **Reverie** for Oneirion's manifold the construct walks through. *Nexus:*
`[Ho]` **Order** for the shared tone, `[Me]` **Measure** for the reading, `[Ae]` **Vastness** for the
kilometre. The page names no glyph, so every assignment is a filing under the Index's Standing Note —
*"Assignment is decided after the phenomenon, never before, and it is a filing decision rather than a
generative one"* — and none is canon until ruled (`SA-GAP-GLYPH-MIRROR`).

**What boundary moves, Speculum.** Not the body and not the signature. **The boundary that moves is
how many bearers one signature is permitted to have.** Ordinarily a signature is an index: it is
attached to the thing that generates it, and the whole practice of identification — Judicium's
included — stands on that attachment holding. The glyph does not forge anything, because *"a glyph
creates nothing and carries no power … It moves a limit, and the law does what it has always done on
the far side of the new limit."* It moves the limit on **uniqueness of bearer**, and everything after
that is trackers doing what trackers always do.

**What the law then does on its own, and this is the part the page leaves implicit.** Nothing has to
fool anybody. A tracker keyed to Essence contour does not *decide* the double is real; it has no
predicate for realness, only for contour, and now there are two contours where its whole logic
assumed one. The pursuit does not get confused. **It bifurcates**, cleanly, on a measurement that is
returning true for both. Oneirion supplies the rest: a trajectory through configuration space is what
gives the construct *"its own limited agency"*, which is why it moves like a body making choices
rather than like an afterimage repeating one.

**What boundary moves, Nexus.** **The limit on what counts as one working.** Two workings cast by
strangers thirty metres apart are, by default, two events with no phase relationship, and the law of
incoherent addition follows. The glyph pins a **common phase reference across the field** — *"every
working in range is made to answer to one shared frequency before it resolves"* (`:96`) — and then
coherent summation does the rest without anybody being amplified. **She does not strengthen her
allies. She makes them one aperture**, and an aperture has gain that its elements do not.

**And the discriminator is where the page stops, because allegiance is not a quantity.** A field can
correlate; it cannot recognise a flag. Whatever gets coherent gain has to be gaining it by matching
*something physical* — her reference tone, her own harmonisation, a shared attunement — and whatever
loses is losing by failing to match. Which means the Nexus's real classifier is **resonance with
Serenyra**, not loyalty to her, and the enemy half of the effect is not the mirror image of the allied
half at all: the allied half is amplification and the enemy half is only the absence of it. **The
page asks the field to do two things and the law offers one** (`SA-GAP-NEXUS-ENEMY-SUPPRESSION`).

**The failure modes, and all four are named in registers the page cites.** **Projection error**
(Oneirion) — the double lands nowhere in particular, and a decoy with implausible kinematics is a
rejected decoy. **The mirror does not discriminate** (Mirithane) — the copy carries what she would
rather not send. **Spectral overload** (Judicium) — everything reports at once and nothing can be
prioritised, which is the Nexus's own stated Cost. **Glare** (Aurevane) — the field's single point of
failure is also the easiest thing on the battlefield to find. And under all of them the Field fault:
*"a Silence Realm. The working is correct and produces nothing."*

**What the target sees and feels, Speculum.** Nothing wrong, which is the whole difficulty. He is
chasing a woman and then he is chasing a woman, and every instrument he has — the contour, the
sight-line, the model of where she will break next — agrees with itself. There is no shimmer and no
seam, because there is nothing being hidden: the second body is genuinely there in every channel he
is reading. What he gets instead is the specific, unpleasant sensation of **a decision arriving where
there was not one before** — two of her, both correct, and a pursuit that now has to be allocated.
Men resolve that by committing, and committing is the point.

Then he catches her. And the resolution is not a body breaking or a spell coming apart with any
ceremony; the thing simply **stops being present**, at the moment of contact, with the discourtesy of
a light going out. The page's *"Struck once by anything that matters, it stops existing"* is exact —
and what he is left holding is the knowledge that the woman he wanted has had three exchanges of head
start while he spent his attention correctly on the wrong true signature. Somewhere, she has a
thinner memory of him than she had this morning.

**What the target sees and feels, Nexus.** Less than that. Nothing is visibly altered — the page says
so, *"without any single working being visibly altered"* (`:111`) — and the experience is of a battle
going slightly wrong in a way no single exchange accounts for. His workings land and do what they
should and are a little less than they were yesterday; theirs are a little more. Nobody can point at
the moment. **The tell is statistical, and statistics are not available inside a fight**, which is
why the page is right that the working is a strategist's tool. What *is* available is the sensation of
being read: every working answering to a tone it did not choose, and one still figure at the centre of
a kilometre with everything in the field pointing at her whether anyone means it to or not.

**Does the physics close against the stratal account?** **On the Speculum, remarkably well, and on one
law it closes better than the page knows.** The cost is informational because the physics of a
massless, low-radiance decoy leaves nothing else to charge; the Limit is what keeps the working out of
a mass-energy breach four Grades above its own; and the stated Counter — change the sense, not the
strength — is the exact form real decoy rejection takes, which makes it the fairest counter in this
batch. What does not close is the citation: the Mechanism names phase conjugation, which sends a
wavefront *back* along its own path and cannot produce a forward-propagating second source; Mirithane's
fidelity law, applied honestly, copies the affect the page's Counter needs uncopied; and Judicium, on
the other half of the same page, states that a signature cannot be counterfeited.

**On the Nexus, the mechanism is sound and three of its edges are uncosted.** Coherent-versus-
incoherent summation explains both the bias and why the bias has to be small; Aurevane supplies the
phase reference; the Rayleigh criterion answers the page's own open question about the detection floor
better than a loudness threshold would, because the line is separation rather than sensitivity; and
the 1 km figure lands on the S-Grade passive-field row exactly. Against that: coherence does not defeat
the inverse square, so the bias cannot be flat to the rim; central arbitration by a practitioner whose
reaction floor is 50–200 µs cannot route a fast close exchange at all; the enemy-weakening half of the
effect has no Sub-Stat in the sixty-four; and the capability is already on her card as her Domain,
which if applied brings a Resonance Shockwave the page does not pay for.

**And one hole runs under both workings and under a third page.** Harmonics Attunement, Harmonics
Synergy and Dominion Radius all gate on **Attraction Path**, and Concordia's own definition demands
*"Attraction-side reach"*. Serenyra's card denies her Attraction in terms — *"Spirit dominant, Fate
secondary … The Fate Path routes no force here"* — and `SA-CROSS-SOMNIUM-SERENYRA-PATH` already
recorded the same shape on `Somnium Scriptorium`. **This is the third of her pages to fail on the same
missing Path**, which makes it a decision about her card rather than about any one technique, and
nothing here resolves it.

## 5 · Counterplay and the challenge

**The fairness check, Speculum Harmoniae.**

| Test | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Costs something that hurts | **pass, and it is the best-designed cost on any Serenyra page** | *"Every mirror spends a slice of her own sensory record of the target; the next time she needs to counter that same person precisely, the read is thinner until she rebuilds it by observing them again"* (`:24`). It is not a resource bar. It is a debt in the currency she fights with, it compounds against the opponent she most needs to read, and it is repaid only by exposure. **Destructive readout without a write-back** (§1c), charged exactly. |
| Stated limits | **pass, twice, unambiguously** | *"The double cannot land a real blow. Struck once by anything that matters, it stops existing"* (`:25`), and the Category line concedes the whole class: *"fails outright against anything that severs the live line back to her"* (`:36`). |
| Something beats it | **pass, and the route is physically correct** | Change the channel, not the intensity (§1a). *"A hunter tracking emotion rather than Essence contour is not fooled."* **But see the Mirithane contradiction: on the page's own leading use — mirroring herself — the honest mirror may copy the affect too** (`SA-EM-SPECULUM-MIRROR-HONESTY`). |
| It has a tell | **pass, and the good tell is the one the page does not state** | The page offers examination and touch. The finer tell is **Oneirion's projection error**: a construct whose trajectory was right in the manifold and lands nowhere in particular in the room. Watch the feet and the ground, not the face. |
| Numbers in band | **mixed** | Stage V, Grade B, *"instability above 320"* and the Splintering ceiling are all attested and correct. **Harmonics Attunement fails its Attraction gate** (`SA-CROSS-SPECULUM-ATTRACTION-PATH`); two of the working's three real Sub-Stats are missing and the one listed resists it (`SA-CROSS-SPECULUM-RETENTION-DENSITY`); range and duration are unconvertible. |

**The fairness check, Nexus Harmoniae.**

| Test | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Costs something that hurts | **pass on the sustain, FAIL on the collapse** | The sustain cost reproduces Judicium's **Spectral overload** without citing it, which is the right cost in the right words. But the working simply *"drops"* when she goes down (`:85`), while a Domain of this size collapsing *"produces a Resonance Shockwave … often dealing significant damage to everything within the former territory, including the character who lost it"*. **A kilometre-wide field that switches off harmlessly makes losing it free** (`SA-FAIR-NEXUS-COLLAPSE-SHOCKWAVE`). |
| Stated limits | **pass, and stated three times** | Small bias, stationary cast, drops on unconsciousness or departure (`:85`, `:106`, `:112`). The insistence is itself the honest part. |
| Something beats it | **pass on the page's two, and the page misses the decisive one** | Domain overwrite and a quiet working are both real routes. **The arbitration floor is better than both and needs no special equipment: fight fast and close** (§1i). |
| It has a tell | **pass, and by accident** | The page offers none beyond the field's own faintness. Aurevane's **Glare** supplies it: *"Coherent output is also a beacon, and Aurevane practitioners are the easiest people in any engagement to locate."* A working whose single point of failure cannot help advertising itself has a tell whether it wants one or not. |
| Numbers in band | **pass on the radius, unstated elsewhere** | 1 km is the S-Grade Passive Pressure Field to the digit. Bias magnitude unstated (and √N-scale by mechanism), duration unconvertible, uniformity physically impossible as written (`SA-PHYS-NEXUS-COHERENCE-VS-FALLOFF`). **Attunement, Synergy and Radius all fail the same Attraction gate.** |

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Deny the Field — "Silence," and it is the Speculum's named death rather than a general nuisance.**
  *"The removal of magical possibility from a region, which is not shielding and not counterspelling.
  … **Animatria constructs fail completely because their continuity runs back to a source the Silence
  has severed.** A soul held in one reports the sensation as loneliness rather than weakness."* The
  page's Category line concedes this and its Counter line does not mention it. **A quiet room does not
  beat the double; it prevents it.**
- **Deny the Field — "Choose the ground by Family," and the two halves want opposite weather.** Limina
  is suppressed by *"Saturated Aether, crowd density, active Domains"*; Fulguria by *"Faraday-caged
  interiors, heavy ferrous shielding, dense fog"*. **There is no ground that suits both workings**, and
  a kilometre of smoke suppresses the Nexus while a press of bodies suppresses the Speculum. The
  unresolved question the lookup trail leaves open is whether her own Lattice counts among *"active
  Domains"* (`SA-GAP-LIMINA-OWN-DOMAIN`).
- **Break the Boundary — "Interrupt the chain," which works on every user of the Speculum except
  her.** *"Below Refraction a working requires an external anchor: voice, hand, ink or blood. … **Break
  his wrist and a gestured chain is finished.**"* The Speculum's floor is **Stage V** and its Craft is a
  traced circle, so any Splintering- or Glory-Stage practitioner running it is beaten by a hand. At
  Stage VIII Serenyra needs no anchor, and the Nexus is spoken *and* gestured *and* routed through a
  staff — three anchors she does not require, each of which can be taken from her anyway.
- **Break the Boundary — "Knot and Lock," and Knot is the precise counter-form.** *"**Knot** entangles
  a path so it cannot be traced cleanly."* The Speculum's whole function is to make a path traceable to
  two bodies; a Knot set on the read denies the tracing that the working depends on being *available*.
  Against the Nexus, **Lock** — *"occupies a limit so no other limit can be set there"* — set on the
  shared frequency leaves the field nothing to pin.
- **Break the Body — "Distance," inverted, and this is the Nexus's cheapest counter.** The register's
  line is that *"most practitioners are a threat about closing range."* Here the geometry runs the other
  way: the Nexus is strongest at her feet and weakest at the rim (§1g), and its arbitration cannot
  resolve a fast exchange at all (§1i). **Fight at the edge, or fight inside 50 microseconds.** Part
  Six is the whole of the lookup for this, and the page names neither.
- **Break the Man — "Read him," which is the Speculum's cost turned against her.** *"Self-derived
  techniques must be read live, in the two or three exchanges before they kill you. **Every thaumaturge
  has a pattern.**"* Her read of you thins each time she mirrors you; yours of her does not.
  **Make her spend the Speculum on you repeatedly and she is paying down her own ability to counter
  you**, which the page states as a cost and nobody has stated as a strategy.
- **Break the Man — "Make him hold Pressure in," against a woman who cannot.** *"Suppression is a
  skill and it is tiring."* Aurevane's Glare says her output is a beacon by law; the Nexus fixes her in
  place for six exchanges; and the field ends the moment she does. **She is the least concealable
  practitioner in this batch while the Nexus is up**, and the register's counter to that is patience
  and a long approach.

**The tell, stated plainly.** For the Speculum there is no seam to find, because nothing is hidden —
the double is genuinely present in every channel she read, and the only discriminators are a channel
she did not read (affect, if Mirithane permits it) and a footfall that the manifold got right and the
room did not. For the Nexus the tell is the caster: a still figure holding a chant and a staff at the
centre of a kilometre, radiating thirty percent of everything she spends, whose removal ends the
working — and who, if the field is her Domain, takes the kilometre down with her.

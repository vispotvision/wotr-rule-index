# System account · Spinal Forge Ascent

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** A fighter's own bones and muscles are rearranged into a
higher-output arrangement for about eighteen seconds, using nothing that was not already in him, and
afterwards he is broken in small ways that he was not broken in before.

**The real phenomenon.** **Anaerobic energy debt and fibre-type expression**, with **mass-conserving
densification of a cellular solid** underneath it. Both are textbook, both are quantitative, and
between them they answer the question the page says nobody can answer.

**(a) "Faster contraction" is a fibre-type shift, and the shift is real and large.** Maximum
shortening velocity is a property of the myosin expressed, not of effort. Slow (type I) fibres shorten
at roughly **0.5–1.0 fibre lengths per second**; fast oxidative-glycolytic (IIa) at **3–4**; fast
glycolytic (**IIx**) at **5–6**. So re-expressing existing muscle toward IIx buys a **five- to
twelvefold** rise in unloaded shortening velocity **out of the same tissue**, which is exactly the
page's *"Re-expresses the caster's own existing mass and Essence in a configuration built for peak
output, without adding or removing anything from the total"* (`:37`).

**And it has a price written into the same physiology.** Hill's relation — **(P + a)·V = b·(P₀ − P)** —
makes force and velocity trade against each other along a hyperbola, so speed is never free; and IIx
is *glycolytic*, which is the whole of the page's Cost line. A fatigued muscle's force and v_max each
fall by 20–30 %, and because power is their product, **peak power collapses to about a third**. The
page's *"Essenced muscle fatigue"* is not a flavour note. It is the named consequence of the
configuration it chose.

**(b) So does the "full Grade above" close? Yes — at the top of the band, and this is the rarest
result in the batch.** The page claims *"strikes land with force consistent with a full Grade above
their unbuffed baseline"* (`:40`). Take the step the page's own floor implies, **A → S**: Part Eleven
puts A-Grade attack output at 4.6024 × 10¹⁰ – 4.184 × 10¹² J and S at 4.184 × 10¹² – 2.42672 × 10¹³ J,
so floor to floor is a factor of **≈91**.

Now the physiology. Impact energy goes as ½mv², so a **5–12×** rise in the velocity the same muscle can
impose gives **25–144×** in delivered kinetic energy at unchanged mass. **The page's ≈91 sits inside
the band its own mechanism produces**, in the upper half of it. No inflation, no unexplained
multiplier, and no need to invoke anything the tissue does not do — which is more than most pages in
this volume can say about their headline number.

**(c) "Denser bone" is a section-modulus trade, and its sign depends on an exponent the system never
states.** This is the one place the page's physics is genuinely undetermined rather than merely
unstated. Conservation is explicit — *"nothing is created or destroyed"* (`:38`) — so densifying at
constant mass and constant length means the bone gets **thinner**: ρ·r² = constant, so doubling density
takes r to r/√2.

Bending is what matters, because the failure the page charges is **microfractures** and those are
bending and fatigue failures, not crushing. Bending strength goes as the section modulus **Z = πr³/4**,
so Z falls to **(1/√2)³ = 0.354** of what it was. Against that, trabecular bone's strength rises with
apparent density on a power law whose measured exponent for human trabecular bone is about **2** for
compressive strength (and 2–3 for modulus), the standard cellular-solid scaling. Multiply:

| Strength–density exponent | Strength gain at 2× density | × section loss 0.354 | Net bending strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| n = 1 | 2 | 0.354 | **0.71 — a 29 % loss** |
| n = 1.5 | 2.83 | 0.354 | **1.00 — exactly break-even** |
| **n = 2** (measured) | 4 | 0.354 | **1.41 — a 41 % gain** |

**At the measured exponent the working gains 41 % in bending and loses nothing**, so the page is
right — but it is right by a margin of forty per cent on a quantity nobody has written down, and one
assumption away from being wrong. Bending stiffness is less kind: **EI** with E ∝ ρ²–ρ³ against
I ∝ r⁴ ∝ ρ⁻² comes out as ρ⁰–ρ¹, so **the skeleton is at best linearly stiffer and possibly no stiffer
at all** (`SA-GAP-SPINAL-DENSITY-EXPONENT`).

**(d) And there is a hard ceiling the page has not noticed, which is a better Limit than the one it
states.** You can only densify bone by collapsing the cellular part of it. Trabecular bone runs at
apparent densities of roughly **0.1–1.0 g/cm³** against cortical bone's **≈1.9 g/cm³**, and once the
trabecular volume is spent there is nothing left to compact. **The available densification is therefore
set by how much spongy bone the skeleton started with, and it runs out.** The page's stated Limit is
*"One higher configuration at a time"* (`:19`) — a bookkeeping limit — when the physics offers a
structural one that cannot be worked around by cleverness and that differs per practitioner.

**(e) The deferral is anaerobic debt, and the page's open question has a number.** The page asks
*"Whether the debt Ascensio defers is ever smaller than the peak it avoided, or whether the technique's
real trade is timing rather than total cost"* (`:21`). **It is not smaller. It is larger, and the
arithmetic is elementary.**

- Glycolysis without oxygen yields **2 ATP** per glucose. The same glucose oxidised aerobically yields
  **30–38 ATP**.
- The lactate the shortcut produces has to be unwound, and the **Cori cycle costs 6 ATP** to turn a
  lactate pair back into one glucose — against the 2 ATP that pair earned. **Every borrowed unit is
  repaid at a loss**, which is precisely why the cycle cannot be sustained.
- The debt is measurable as **excess post-exercise oxygen consumption**, and it is consumed largely in
  the liver, running gluconeogenesis on fatty-acid oxidation — that is, **the bill is paid by an organ
  that was not in the fight.**

The same conclusion arrives from thermodynamics without any biology: dissipation in a real process is
strictly positive and **increases with rate**, so a transformation spread over eighteen seconds
dissipates less than the same transformation taken as one peak, and both dissipate more than the
quasistatic minimum. **Spreading a stress genuinely lowers the peak and genuinely raises nothing —
what raises the total is having done it fast at all.**

**(f) Which is where the page's cited law is the wrong law.** The Mechanism says Ascensio's law is
*"mechanical advantage, the path-independence of work done against a load"* (`:38`). The register is
more careful than the page: its Analogue is *"Mechanical advantage. **Path-independence of
gravitational potential energy**"*. Path-independence is a property of **conservative** forces only.
Work done against gravity is path-independent; work done against friction, viscosity or plastic
deformation is the textbook case of path-**dependence**, and re-latticing living bone is inelastic from
end to end. **So the page has generalised a conservative-field theorem onto a dissipative process**,
and its own Consequence line half-catches the error — *"The total stress of the re-lattice is not
reduced by the spreading, only delayed"* (`:41`) — while the physics says the total is not merely
delayed but **increased** (`SA-PHYS-SPINAL-PATH-INDEPENDENCE`).

**Energy budget, stated.** Not computable in EU (`SA-GAP-EU-FORMULA`), and computable in joules only as
a ratio: delivered output rises by **25–144×** on a fibre-type shift, against a stated **≈91×**
(A → S). Bone: **+41 %** bending strength at the measured density exponent, **0 to +100 %** stiffness,
with a hard ceiling at full cortical density. Where the joules were: entirely inside the practitioner —
*"without adding or removing anything from the total"* — which makes this one of the few pages where
*"The practitioner does not supply the joules. They supply the boundary condition"* needs qualifying:
he supplies no **new** joules, but he does supply the metabolic substrate, and that is a store with a
floor. **Fault class:** **Structural fault** — *"The Crystal could not hold what the boundary permitted.
Overchannel, Backlash, Fracture. The working succeeds and the practitioner does not"* — and the page's
Cost line is that sentence rewritten as microfractures.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum, two Families and they want opposite ground.** **Spatium**, *"**Wild**"*, favoured by
*"Veil-thin zones, established Realmgates, surveyed ground"* and suppressed by *"Warp scarring,
contested Domains, unmapped terrain"*; **Vectoria**, *"Split: **Wild** (Gravity, Seismic) · **Spirit**
(Sound, Force projection)"*, favoured by *"Structural mass, elevation, brittle or pre-stressed
material"* and suppressed by *"Loose sediment, free fall, uniform soft ground"*
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings.md:150`, `:149`). *"Terrain is not
flavour. It is a modifier on η."*

**Spatium's suppression list is the more awkward of the two for a fighting art.** *"Unmapped terrain"*
and *"contested Domains"* describe most engagements a martial technique is used in, and *"surveyed
ground"* describes a training hall. **A body-Transmutation art is therefore at its best where it is
practised and at its worst where it is needed** — which is a real and unstated reason a documented,
taught technique would look better in the school than in the field. Vectoria's list is kinder here and
its favoured conditions are almost a joke about the working: *"brittle or pre-stressed material"* is
what the practitioner's own skeleton becomes while the buff runs.

**Wellspring stratum — Transmutatio, *The Law of Change*, Spatium, Spatial Geometry.** Verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Spatium — Spatial Geometry.md:19`:

> **Analogue** · Change of basis. Coordinate transformation under conservation.
> **Mechanism** · The same object described in two frames yields two entirely different sets of numbers
> and one unchanged object. Nothing is created or lost in a transformation; the quantities are merely
> re-expressed. Transmutatio applies this to failed workings, which are not destroyed Essence but
> Essence in an inconvenient basis, and the Wellspring supplies the transformation that makes them
> usable again.
> **Stat Effect** · Strengthens Ardency Conversion, Tempering Processing. Failed workings become fuel
> rather than wounds.
> **Failure** · Basis loss. The practitioner forgets which frame they started in, and a transformation
> with no reference frame does not return anything recognisable.

**The page quotes the Analogue faithfully and then applies it outside its stated scope.** The
register's Mechanism does not say Transmutatio re-bases *anything*; it says it re-bases **failed
workings** — *"Essence in an inconvenient basis"* — and the Stat Effect closes the same loop, *"Failed
workings become fuel rather than wounds."* This page re-bases a living skeleton, which is not a failed
working and was never in an inconvenient basis (`SA-CROSS-SPINAL-TRANSMUTATIO-SCOPE`). The
change-of-basis mathematics is the right mathematics for it — a body re-described in a second frame,
same object, different numbers, is a good account of what the page wants — but the register's own
application is narrower than the law it names, and the page does not say it is extending it.

**And Transmutatio's Failure is the missing failure mode, and it is the worst one on any page in this
batch.** *"Basis loss. The practitioner forgets which frame they started in, and a transformation with
no reference frame does not return anything recognisable."* For a working on failed Essence that is a
wasted expenditure. **For a working on a body it is a man who cannot change back**, and worse, a man
whose reversion has nothing to aim at. The page's stated failures are microfractures, a collapsed
schedule and a stacking collision. **The primary Wellspring's own Failure line says the skeleton can
lose its original frame**, and nothing on the page acknowledges that the working has a reversion step
at all, let alone one that can miss.

**Wellspring stratum — Ascensio, *The Ladder of Ascent*, Vectoria, Mechanics.** Verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Vectoria — Mechanics.md:33`:

> **Analogue** · Mechanical advantage. Path-independence of gravitational potential energy.
> **Mechanism** · Lifting a mass to a height costs the same total work by any route, but the inclined
> plane trades distance for peak force and so the same climb can be made without ever exceeding what
> the structure can bear. Ascensio applies that to Threshold transitions, spreading the required change
> across a longer path so the Crystal never sees the peak stress that a direct ascent imposes.
> **Stat Effect** · Strengthens Tempering Acceleration, Harmonics Attunement. Stage transitions become
> smoother and less violent.
> **Failure** · The work is not reduced, only redistributed. Practitioners mistake the absence of
> crisis for the absence of cost and arrive at the next Stage owing the difference.

**Ascensio's Failure answers the page's "What nobody knows" outright, in the register, in one
sentence.** The page asks whether the deferred debt is ever smaller than the peak avoided. *"The work
is not reduced, only redistributed."* **The question is already closed on the system's side**, and the
only live part of it is the direction the physics adds: the register says *not reduced*, and §1e says
*increased*, because borrowing metabolically is lossy and because dissipation rises with rate. **So
canon and physics agree on the sign and differ on the magnitude**, which is a much smaller disagreement
than the page's framing implies and is worth stating rather than leaving as a mystery.

**The Failure's second clause is the characterisation the page is missing.** *"Practitioners mistake
the absence of crisis for the absence of cost."* That is not a mechanic; it is a school's psychology,
and it is the reason a **documented, taught** art — which is what the Origin line argues this is
(`:57`) — would spread faster than its consequences become visible.

**The Sub-Stat list is the reverse of the Serenyra pages, and it is right.** Those pages derived their
Sub-Stats from what their Wellsprings strengthen and ended up listing stats the working does not use.
This page's Wellspring Stat Effects are **Ardency Conversion → Overchannel**, **Tempering Processing →
Maturity**, **Tempering Acceleration → Maturity** and **Harmonics Attunement** under the Merge Ledger —
and the page lists none of them. It lists *"Dexterity (Celerity, Reflex), secondary Vitality
(Fortitude)"* (`:49`), which is **what the working does**, and the gates confirm the choice: Fortitude's
higher gate exists for exactly this working, *"and Stage IV to exceed B, **the higher gate covering the
bone and connective-tissue density it absorbed**"* (Part Seven). **A page that picked its stats from
the effect rather than from the register, and picked correctly.**

**One consequence of that, though: the page never names Overchannel, and Overchannel is what its
primary Wellspring strengthens.** Part Eleven's Overchannel clause grants *"two to five times the
sustainable ceiling"* and charges *"structural damage that must be healed through rest, alchemical
treatment or Temperance advancement … fracture density that eventually becomes permanent."* **This
working grants a full Grade — 25 to 144 times by §1b — and charges microfractures that lapse**
(`SA-FAIR-SPINAL-OVERCHANNEL-PRICE`).

**Which of the Four Theories.** **Debt**, and this page wears it more plainly than any other in the
volume, including the one built on the word: the Cost line's own heading is *"The transformation's true
cost is **deferred, not paid**"* (`:18`). Not a price, not a trade — a **schedule**. **Correspondence**
runs underneath, since change of basis is the claim that two descriptions are the same object.

**Essence stratum.** Craft **Spellcraft, declared, bodily delivery** (`:29`), with the Trigger
committing *"the working through the body itself rather than through voice, igniting outward along the
spine"* (`:36`). Stage floor **VII, Refraction** — the exact Stage at which Law V stops requiring an
external anchor, which is why a working whose *only* anchor is the caster's own body can sit here and
could not sit at VI. Category **Arts**, *"**Body-Aligned** … Structured, discipline-based styles
blending movement, form, and Essence flow — controlled Flux, martial shaping, embodied Aether
expression. **Vitae** given technique"* — the page's parenthetical reproduces the taxonomy's own words.
No practitioner and no card, so no η, no reserve, no Crystal State. **The invoice:** the skeleton, on
terms.

**The lens: animal spirits, and the muscle that was supposed to inflate.** René Descartes, *Traité de
l'homme* (written c. 1633, printed 1662) with the *Passions de l'âme* (1649) beside it. Descartes gave
the first fully mechanical account of a moving body, and its engine was **hydraulic**: animal spirits,
a very fine matter distilled in the ventricles, run down the nerves — which he took to be tubes with
valves — and **inflate** the muscle, which shortens because it has swollen. Contraction is filling.
Strength is therefore a question of **supply**: open the valve wider, admit more spirit, get more
force, and the limit on a man's power is how much he can send down the tube.

**Spinal Forge Ascent is that picture with a schedule attached.** *"Igniting outward along the spine"*
is the Cartesian nerve doing exactly what Descartes said it did, from the centre out, and *"snap into a
higher operating configuration"* is inflation given a mechanism. The lens also explains why the
technique would be *taught* rather than discovered: a hydraulic theory of strength is teachable, and
anything teachable becomes a school.

**The productive misreading, and what it kills.** An **Inflationist** school reads supply as the
governing variable, which is what the doctrine says, and draws two conclusions from it. First, that
more is more: push harder, admit more, hold the configuration longer, because the ceiling is the pipe
and the pipe can be trained. Second — and this is the fatal one — that **Ascensio's spreading is a way
of getting more in**, since the whole point of the inclined plane is to move a load you could not lift
directly. So they defer aggressively and at length, and they read the smoothness as headroom.

**Two real laws kill them, and neither is about supply.** Hill's relation is not a supply law: force
and velocity trade along a hyperbola, so a configuration tuned for speed produces **less force at every
velocity but the lowest**, and an Inflationist who trains toward maximum admission arrives in the
exchange fast and weak, which he will read as needing to push harder. And the ledger closes the trap:
anaerobic borrowing repays at a loss — **6 ATP to unwind what earned 2** — so the longer the deferral
the larger the debt, absolutely and not merely in timing. Ascensio's register already has the epitaph:
*"Practitioners mistake the absence of crisis for the absence of cost and arrive at the next Stage
owing the difference."* **The school's masters are its longest holders**, and what they are is men who
have spent eighteen seconds at a time borrowing against a liver, at six for two, for years. Swammerdam
settled the physiology in 1667 by showing a contracting muscle does not gain volume at all; the school
has no equivalent experiment, because the thing it would have to measure arrives after everyone has
gone home.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**The glyphs, proposed.** `[Tir]` **Hour** for the deferral, `[Frm]` **Form** for the re-lattice, `[Eq]`
**Equivalence** for the change of basis under conservation. **`[Tir]` is the one that matters**, and it
is the cleanest glyph fit in the batch: the working's whole content is *when* a stress is permitted to
arrive. The page names no glyph, so all three are filings under the Index's Standing Note and none is
canon until ruled (`SA-GAP-GLYPH-MIRROR`).

**What boundary moves.** Not the amount of stress and not the body's tolerance. **The boundary that
moves is the schedule on which a stress is permitted to arrive.** Ordinarily the stress of a structural
change is coincident with the change: you rearrange a lattice and the lattice registers the
rearrangement now, at the peak rate the rearrangement happens at. The glyph pins the *arrival* and
lets the *event* run free. Nothing is reduced and nothing is added — *"A glyph creates nothing and
carries no power. … It moves a limit, and the law does what it has always done on the far side of the
new limit."*

**What the law then does on its own, and this is where the working is honest.** On the far side of a
moved deadline, materials science does what it always does: a slower transformation dissipates less
per second and the structure survives a rate it could not have survived, which is the inclined plane
exactly. But the same physics is indifferent to the accounting. **The stress was never a debt to the
technique; it was a debt to the tissue**, and tissue does not read schedules — it reads accumulated
damage. So eighteen seconds of spreading buys eighteen seconds of function and produces, at the end,
the full transformation's dissipation plus whatever the rate cost, arriving in a structure that has
spent the interval being loaded harder than it has ever been loaded.

**The two halves are one act and the page knows it.** The muscle is re-expressed toward fast
glycolytic operation, which is why it is quicker and why it is running on a pathway that repays at a
loss; the bone is compacted, which is why the frame holds a strike that would otherwise fold it and why
what fails afterwards fails as **cracks** rather than as tearing. **Both halves borrow, in the same
currency, on the same schedule.** The Cost line's *"microfractures and Essenced muscle fatigue"* is not
two costs. It is one loan, itemised by tissue.

**The failure modes, and the page has three of five.** **Schedule collapse** is stated and is the
page's best idea: *"massive blunt damage delivered mid-spread collapses the redistribution schedule,
forcing the deferred stress to land immediately"* (`:43`) — which is what happens when you interrupt a
controlled unloading, and it is worse than the blow. **Stacking collision** is stated (`:42`).
**Suppression** is stated (`:43`). Not stated: **basis loss**, Transmutatio's own, which for a body is
a reversion with no frame to revert to; and the **densification ceiling** of §1d, which is the point at
which there is no spongy bone left to compact and the working simply does less than it did last year.

**What the target sees and feels.** First, light. The spine goes **molten-white** through the back of
whatever he is wearing, from the base upward, and it does not pulse or gather — it *ignites*, which is
the page's word and the right one, because the transformation is already running by the time anything
is visible. There is no wind-up to interrupt. There is a man who was one thing and is now, visibly,
scheduled.

Then the eighteen seconds, and what is wrong about them is not the speed. Speed can be read. What
cannot be read is that **the corrections arrive before they are earned** — a body running involuntary
response at a configuration his frame was not built for, so the openings close in the wrong order and
the counter he had planned is answered by something that has not finished deciding to answer it. And
when a strike lands it lands like a much heavier man's, which is the part that does not add up: the
mass is the same, the reach is the same, and the hit is two orders of magnitude out.

And then it stops, on time, and that is the moment. Not a stagger and not a collapse: a man standing
still, breathing carefully, **paying**. The page's Consequence is the beat — *"it arrives in full once
the buff ends"* — and anyone who has seen the technique twice knows to be inside his reach at second
seventeen rather than second ten. Which is the whole counter, and it is available to anyone who has
seen it twice.

**Does the physics close against the stratal account?** **This is the best-closing page in the batch,
and its headline number is the only one in the volume that its own mechanism can pay for.** A
fibre-type shift toward fast glycolytic operation is a real, large, mass-conserving change in exactly
the direction the page needs, and the 25–144× in impact energy it produces brackets the ≈91× that a
full Grade step from A to S actually costs. The two numerical citations in the Numerical Effect line
are both attested and both correct. The Sub-Stats are chosen from the effect rather than from the
registers, and Vitality Fortitude's higher gate was written for the bone density this working changes.
The Stage floor is not a rounded-up guess: Refraction is the Stage at which Law V stops requiring an
external anchor, which is what a working delivered through the caster's own spine requires. And the
deferral at the centre of the page is anaerobic debt, which is the correct real mechanism for a cost
that is *"deferred, not paid"*, down to the detail that the bill is settled by an organ nobody was
fighting.

**What does not close is a theorem, an exponent and a price.** The Mechanism generalises Ascensio's
path-independence from gravitational potential energy — a conservative field — onto the plastic
re-latticing of bone, which is the textbook path-dependent case; the register is careful where the page
is not, and the true answer to the page's own open question is worse than the page fears, because
borrowing metabolically repays at a loss. Whether *"denser bone"* is an upgrade at all rests on a
strength–density exponent nobody has written down, and it is break-even at 1.5. The densification has a
hard ceiling at full cortical density which the page's bookkeeping Limit does not use. Transmutatio's
Mechanism is stated for failed workings and is being applied to a skeleton, and Transmutatio's own
Failure — a transformation with no reference frame to return to — is the most serious unstated risk on
any page in this volume. And the working grants an output step one to two orders of magnitude beyond
Overchannel's 2–5× while charging less than Overchannel charges, without naming Overchannel, which its
primary Wellspring strengthens.

## 5 · Counterplay and the challenge

**The fairness check.**

| Test | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Costs something that hurts | **pass in shape, FAIL in size** | The shape is excellent and the page leads with it — *"The transformation's true cost is **deferred, not paid**"* (`:18`) — and it lands *inside* the engagement's aftermath rather than off-screen. But Part Eleven charges permanent fracture density for **2–5×**, and this charges lapsing microfractures for **25–144×** (`SA-FAIR-SPINAL-OVERCHANNEL-PRICE`). |
| Stated limits | **pass, and the physics offers a better one** | *"One higher configuration at a time"* (`:19`) is a real limit and cannot be talked around. §1d supplies a harder one the page does not have: densification runs out when the trabecular volume does, which differs per practitioner and cannot be trained past. |
| Something beats it | **pass, and both routes are correct physics** | Interrupting a controlled unloading is genuinely worse than the blow that interrupts it, and suppression of the redistribution is the same idea from the other side (`:43`). **The page's counters are the best-reasoned in the volume.** |
| It has a tell | **pass, unmistakably** | *"An observer sees the caster's spine light molten-white"* (`:40`). Visible, unambiguous, and it starts the clock: eighteen seconds from ignition to payment, which makes the tell a *timer* rather than a warning. |
| Numbers in band | **pass on everything stated** | Stat Ceiling 475, Stat Range 276–400, Stage VII, A-Grade, Body Path and all three Sub-Stat gates check clean. The unstated numbers are the problem: no EU, no η, no practitioner, a strength exponent the system lacks, and a duration on the volume's third turn-length convention. |

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Break the Body — "Exhaustion," and this working is the register's own example without knowing it.**
  *"Below ten percent reserve a practitioner suffers Essence Starvation … **A defender who can afford to
  spend nothing wins a long engagement against an attacker who cannot afford to spend nothing**, and
  sieges are decided by this more often than by any working."* A technique whose cost is a deferred debt
  that repays at a loss is a technique that cannot be used twice cheaply. **Make him ignite, then
  decline the exchange.** The register's caution — *"Waiting for the cost"* does not work, because
  *"costs are rarely due inside a scene"* — is **suspended here**, because this page's cost is due at
  second eighteen and the page says so.
- **Break the Body — the timer, which is the page's tell used as an instrument.** The spine lights, and
  the working ends on schedule with a man standing still and paying. **Fight the first ten seconds and
  close in the last two.** This requires no counter-working, no equipment and no Wellspring — only
  having seen the technique once and counted.
- **Break the Boundary — "Force the Seal early," which is what the page's own Counter is.** *"The Seal
  closes an operation against the warrant that opened it … **with the difference deducted from the
  speaker at the moment of closing.** A third party who can compel an early close is not defending.
  They are billing him."* The page: *"massive blunt trauma delivered while the spread is still running
  collapses the schedule Ascensio built."* **Those are the same move**, and the register's framing is
  sharper than the page's: the blow is not damage, it is a demand for early settlement.
- **Deny the Field — "Choose the ground by Family," and Spatium is the opening.** Spatium fails in
  *"Warp scarring, contested Domains, unmapped terrain"*. **Broken, unsurveyed, contested ground
  suppresses the primary Family of a martial art**, which is the counter nobody expects because it
  reads as terrain rather than as tactics — and it is cheap, because the defender chooses where to
  stand. Vectoria's list adds *"loose sediment, free fall, uniform soft ground"*, so deep sand
  suppresses both.
- **Deny the Field — "Silence," with a caveat worth stating.** Silence removes magical possibility,
  and this working is *sustained* rather than installed. But the page's own Weakness says suppression
  *"halts the spreading process outright"* — and §1e says the stress does not stop existing when the
  schedule does. **Silencing a man mid-spread may be indistinguishable from hitting him, which the
  register does not warn about and which makes this a route with a moral hazard attached.**
- **Break the Man — "Rigidity," against a taught art.** *"A Trait's law is not negotiable by its bearer
  … Present him with the situation his own law handles worst and he will handle it that way
  regardless."* The Origin argues this is documented and studied rather than self-derived (`:57`), and
  the register's companion line is the operative one: *"Taught techniques are documented and
  counterable by anyone who studied."* **This working's counters are in a book somewhere**, because the
  page says its counters were already established before any particular caster used it.
- **Break the Man — "Coherence drift," the long game, with Ascensio's psychology behind it.** *"You do
  not have to cause this. You have to notice it and wait."* Read against *"Practitioners mistake the
  absence of crisis for the absence of cost"*: a habitual user is accumulating a liability he
  experiences as competence. **The Inflationist failure in §2 is available to any opponent patient
  enough to let him keep winning.**

**The tell, stated plainly.** A spine lit molten-white through his back, from the base up, with no
gather and no wind-up — and from that instant, eighteen seconds. There is no hiding it and no shortening
it. What follows the eighteen seconds is a man standing still and breathing carefully, and that is not
a recovery animation; it is the bill arriving, and it is the only moment in the exchange when he is not
a full Grade above you.

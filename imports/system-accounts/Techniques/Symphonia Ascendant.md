# System account · Symphonia Ascendant

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** A woman sings a chord at a man she has heard cast before. Nothing
visible happens to him. The next thing he throws does not go where he threw it — it goes sideways, or
it comes back along its own line into him, or it stops being a weapon and becomes a very great deal of
light and noise.

**The real phenomenon.** **Resonant coupling into a measured line, followed by a refractive interface
imposed on the output.** Both halves are textbook, both are quantitative, and between them they answer
the page's own closing question. The third of the three stated outcomes does not balance, and it does
not balance by five orders of magnitude.

**(a) The lock is driven resonance, and the page's Limit is the exact condition resonance imposes.** A
driven oscillator responds to a driver near its own natural frequency and ignores one that is not. The
width of "near" is the resonance bandwidth, **Δf = f₀ / Q**, and it is narrow in anything worth calling
resonant: a struck wineglass runs Q ≈ 10³, so a 500 Hz glass accepts drive across about **0.5 Hz** and
is deaf to everything else in the spectrum. **You cannot drive a resonance you have not measured**, and
that is the whole of *"She has to have heard or felt the target cast before the sigils have anything to
link to"* (`:19`). It is not a courtesy. It is the bandwidth.

The same arithmetic delivers the page's stated Weakness for free. *"A target whose Essence never repeats
the same way twice can partially shrug the redirection off"* (`:36`) — a non-repeating signal has no
line at all; its power is spread across the whole band as **noise**, and the fraction of it lying inside
Δf falls as Δf/B. Against a broadband emitter the coupling is not weakened, it is **divided by the ratio
of the target's bandwidth to the chord's**, which for anything genuinely chaotic is three or four orders
of magnitude. **The page's counter is correct physics and it is stronger than the page claims**, since
"partially shrug off" understates a 10⁻³ coupling coefficient.

And the *other* stated counter is the same equation read from the other end. *"An Anchor Ward fixes the
target's Essence paths in place"* (`:20`): fixing a path is adding stiffness, **ω₀ = √(k/m)**, so the
natural frequency rises. The ward does not block the chord. **It detunes it** — moves the line out from
under a driver aimed where the line used to be. That is why it has to be fielded *before* the lock and
not after, which the page states and does not explain.

**(b) The redirection is a refractive interface, and refraction is free.** The page says *"a force's
direction is a variable that can be reassigned without destroying the force itself"* (`:31`), and the
card says it better: *"The waves impose a **refractive law** on the target's internal Aether"* (card
`:107`). Refraction at an interface is Snell's law, **n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂**, and it is the cleanest
boundary condition in physics: it changes direction, conserves energy exactly, and is decided entirely
by the index ratio and the angle of arrival. A force redirected perpendicular to its motion has **zero
work done on it** — which is why this working can act on an A-Grade discharge without paying for an
A-Grade discharge.

**And one interface produces all three of the page's outcomes, selected by angle.** This is the
unification the page gropes at and does not reach:

- **Below the critical angle** the wave crosses and is deviated. The attack fires and lands somewhere
  other than where it was aimed — *"misfires."*
- **Above the critical angle**, θ_c = arcsin(n₂/n₁), there is no transmitted solution at all and the
  interface returns **total internal reflection**: the whole wave, back along its own path, into the
  thing that emitted it — *"bends back along its own Wellspring vector onto its caster"* (`:33`). The
  page's phrase *"total refraction"* is the one term of art it gets wrong; total **internal reflection**
  is the effect it is describing, and it only exists going from the denser index to the thinner one,
  which is a real and findable constraint on which way round the working has to be set.
- **At a rough or graded interface**, the phase relationship across the wavefront does not survive the
  crossing, and coherent output becomes incoherent scatter — *"splinters into harmless light and sound"*
  (`:33`).

Fresnel's relations price the split at every angle; at normal incidence the reflected fraction is
**R = ((n₁ − n₂)/(n₁ + n₂))²**. So the three "outcomes" are not three effects. They are one interface
read at three angles, and what decides which one a target gets is **how squarely his working met the law
she put in front of it.**

**(c) The third outcome does not balance, and the word doing the damage is "harmless."** Refraction
conserves energy. Scattering conserves energy. Nothing in this working destroys a joule; it only decides
where the joules go. So take the page's own Grade and ask where they went.

A-Grade attack output is **4.6024 × 10¹⁰ – 4.184 × 10¹² J** (Part Four). Take the **floor**, 46 GJ, and
dispose of it as light and sound from a point, over one second, and stand ten metres away:

- Sphere area **4π(10 m)² = 1,257 m²**; fluence **4.6 × 10¹⁰ / 1,257 = 3.7 × 10⁷ J/m².**
- Against the solar constant, 1,361 W/m², that is **about 27,000 suns**, held for a second.
- The thermal-radiation threshold for second-degree burns is around **2.5 × 10⁵ J/m².** This is
  **150 times** it. Everything combustible in line of sight ignites.
- As sound: **SPL = 10 log₁₀(3.7 × 10⁷ / 10⁻¹²) ≈ 196 dB.** Peak pressure **p = √(I·ρc) = √(3.7 × 10⁷ ×
  413) ≈ 1.2 × 10⁵ Pa** — **1.2 atmospheres of overpressure**, well past the ~70 kPa threshold for lung
  injury.
- In ordnance terms, 46 GJ is **11 tonnes of TNT** and the A-Grade ceiling, 4.184 TJ, is **one kiloton.**

**"Harmless light or sound" is, at the Grade the page itself states, a kiloton delivered as a flashbulb
and a blast wave** (`SA-PHYS-SYMPHONIA-HARMLESS-LIGHT`). The other two outcomes are fine — a deviated
attack still lands *somewhere*, and a reflected one lands on the man who made it, and both of those are
honest bookkeeping. The third outcome is the only one in the batch that tries to make an A-Grade
discharge **stop existing**, and the system's own position on that is the sentence every account in this
batch is written under: *"The glyph ends. The law does not. Everything a working produces goes on obeying
every rule it has ever obeyed"* (`wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`).

**(d) A number the page gets right, and a channel it gets right by hedging.** The stated reach is *"5
kilometres of Aetheric line-of-sight"* (`:32`). Part Eleven's **Projected Force Range at 50% yield for
A-Grade is 3–8 km.** Five kilometres sits mid-band. ✓ And *"line-of-sight"* is precisely the Fulguria
favoured condition — *"Dry air, conductive substrate, **clear line of sight**"* — so the delivery channel
and the Family agree, which is rarer in this batch than it should be.

The hedge that saves it is *"heard **or felt**."* Run the acoustic case and it dies instantly: a shouted
incantation is about **85 dB at 1 m**; spherical spreading over 5 km costs **20 log₁₀(5,000) = 74 dB**,
and atmospheric absorption near 1 kHz costs roughly another **5 dB/km, so 25 dB**. She would be listening
for **−14 dB** against a quiet rural floor of 30–40 dB. And sound takes **5,000 / 343 = 14.6 s** to cross
that distance, against **16.7 µs** for anything electromagnetic. **The prior read cannot be acoustic at
five kilometres**, so it is Aetheric — which is what the page says, and which matters for §5, because an
Aetheric read is one her Domain and her Trait can supply without her being present at all.

**Energy budget, stated.** Supplied by the practitioner: the boundary condition only — *"The practitioner
does not supply the joules. They supply the boundary condition, and the boundary condition is cheap in
absolute terms and expensive in the only currency a soul has"* (`Two Sets of Books`). Handled by the
working: **4.6 × 10¹⁰ – 4.184 × 10¹² J**, all of it the target's, none of it created or destroyed.
Required by the third stated outcome: the safe disposal of that entire budget, for which the page gives
no sink. Fault class by `Two Sets of Books` §VII: **Boundary fault** — *"The condition was established
imprecisely and the law ran somewhere unintended"* — which is also, word for word, the page's own
description of a chord that slides sideways.

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** Two Families, and they want opposite rooms. **Fulguria**, *"**Spirit**"*, favoured by
*"Dry air, conductive substrate, clear line of sight"*, suppressed by *"Faraday-caged interiors, heavy
ferrous shielding, dense fog"*. **Limina**, *"**Spirit** primary · **Medium** at Veil-Thin density and
above"*, favoured by *"Darkness, silence, Aether-thin ground, ruins"*, suppressed by *"Saturated Aether,
crowd density, **active Domains**"*
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings.md`). *"Terrain is not flavour. It is
a modifier on η."*

**And the second row indicts her own equipment.** Serenyra carries a Domain — *"The Lattice of Living
Light (Prototype) · Current Domain, nascent and portable"* (card `:100`). An **active Domain** is on
Limina's suppression list. **She walks around inside a standing suppressor for her own secondary
Wellspring**, and Mirithane is the half of this working that supplies the reflection. The two Families
also disagree about the room: Fulguria wants clear air and line of sight, Limina wants *"darkness,
silence, Aether-thin ground"*, and a hall bright and open enough to carry the Aurevane half is a hall
too bright and too full to carry the Mirithane half well. This is not a conflict — nothing states a rule
for a two-Family working in a room that favours one and suppresses the other — but it is the reason the
working has a best ground and the page does not name it.

**Saturation.** Five harmonizations across three Families on the card, which is a heavy load, and the
card says where it lives: *"her stated aim is to network the remaining Wellsprings until all of them
answer one equation; on the sheet that ambition lives in **Harmonics Breadth and Tempering Capacity**,
and both are near ceiling"* (Breadth 538, Capacity 466). Nothing in the system converts that into a
number to check against, so the load is stated and not tested.

**Wellspring stratum — Aurevane, *The Wellspring of Light*, Fulguria, Electromagnetism.** Core Law,
verbatim from `wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Fulguria — Electromagnetism.md:26`:

> **Analogue** · Optical resolution and coherence. The Rayleigh criterion; phase-locked emission.
> **Mechanism** · Two points are resolvable only when their diffraction patterns separate, and coherent
> light holds a fixed phase relationship across its whole front, which is what makes a laser a laser
> rather than a bright lamp. Aurevane raises both figures at once. What the practitioner perceives
> resolves finer, and what they express leaves the Shell in phase, meaning the output matches the intent
> with almost no distortion introduced in transit.

**Read that clause backwards and the third outcome has its law.** *"What makes a laser a laser rather
than a bright lamp"* is phase-locking, and **a lamp is what a laser becomes when you take the phase
away.** The same joules, spread over every direction and every phase, doing nothing coherent. So
*"splinters into harmless light and sound"* is **Aurevane's own law run in reverse on somebody else's
output** — the correct mechanism, precisely stated in the page's own primary register, arriving at an
effect the page then mis-prices by five orders of magnitude (§1c). The law is right. The adjective is
not.

Aurevane's **Failure** is the tell, and it is on her card as a cosmetic: *"**Glare.** Coherent output is
also a beacon, and Aurevane practitioners are the easiest people in any engagement to locate."*

**Wellspring stratum — Mirithane, *The Wellspring of Reflection*, Limina, Entropy/Void/Mind.** Core Law,
verbatim from `wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Limina — Entropy, Void and Mind.md:40`:

> **Analogue** · Specular reflection. The law of reflection; the phase-conjugate mirror.
> **Mechanism** · A true mirror adds nothing. Angle in equals angle out, and fidelity is defined entirely
> by the absence of introduced distortion. A phase-conjugate mirror goes further and returns a wavefront
> that undoes its own aberration on the way back. Mirithane makes the Shell such a surface, so what
> leaves the practitioner is what was in them, with no flattering correction applied in transit.

**The second outcome is not an analogy to this. It is this.** *"Bends back along its own Wellspring
vector onto its caster"* is the definition of a **phase-conjugate mirror**: not angle-in-equals-angle-out
off a flat surface, but a return *along the incident path*, undoing on the way back every distortion
picked up on the way in. A working that arrived through a crooked route goes home through the same
crooked route and arrives undistorted. The page's secondary Wellspring hands it the exact instrument and
the page does not say so.

Mirithane's **Failure** is the price, and it is the page's unwritten cost: *"The mirror does not
discriminate. A practitioner in a state they would rather not broadcast broadcasts it perfectly."* To
hold a conjugating surface you have to *be* the surface, and a surface that adds nothing also hides
nothing.

**The Sub-Stat list does not come from either declared Wellspring.** Aurevane strengthens *"Gnosis
Perception, Vigilance, Harmonics Fidelity"*; Mirithane strengthens *"Harmonics Fidelity, Tempering
Coherence"*. Under the Merge Ledger, Fidelity → **Attunement** and Coherence → **Anchoring**. So the two
registers between them yield Gnosis Perception, Gnosis Vigilance, **Harmonics Attunement**, Resilience
Anchoring — and the page's FOW line is *"Governing Primary **Dominion** (Sense, Radius) and **Harmonics**
(Attunement)"* (`:42`). Attunement is earned. **Dominion Sense and Dominion Radius are earned by neither
of the page's two Wellsprings.** They belong to others on the same card: Dominion Sense to **Abyntheus**,
Dominion Sovereignty and Reach to **Oneirion** (*"Gnosis Cognition, Dominion Reach, Ardency Spectral"*).
The page reaches five kilometres on a Sub-Stat it has not declared the current for
(`SA-CROSS-SYMPHONIA-DOMINION-SOURCE`).

**Which of the Four Theories.** **Correspondence**, and unusually plainly. The working does not act on
the target; it acts on a *relation* between two patterns, and the relation is established by having
matched the target's line once. Nothing crosses the five kilometres except a correspondence already
established. **Consent Doctrine** runs underneath as the standing enforced position — the law recognises
a lawful boundary and behaves accordingly — but the thing this page is actually built on is the claim
that two things which once agreed in frequency remain addressable to one another afterwards.

**Essence stratum.** Crystal layer: the **Aether Shell**, *"Class V · Radiant. Aether bends toward the
bearer: the Aetheric Refraction Field is the Class showing on the skin"* (card `:37`) — and the working
is that Class used offensively, an index discontinuity carried on the body and projected onto someone
else's. Developmental Tier **Radiant** (Stage VIII, Part Twenty-One). Tier of Standing **6 · Master**,
η band 0.70–0.80. Crystal State *"Refined"*, η *"~0.70"* — **in band, which makes her one of the very few
practitioners in this batch whose efficiency figure does not have to be logged.** **The invoice:** a
chord held true enough to be a reference, on a Crystal whose stated Risk is *"**Resonance Cascade.**
Misalignment of her internal Wellspring frequencies triggers a systemic collapse"* (card `:40`). She is
billed in the one currency she cannot afford: to impose a frequency standard on someone else, she must
first be one.

**The lens: Boethius, and the soul that is already a tuning.** Boethius, *De institutione musica*
(c. 500 AD), I.1–I.2. Boethius divides music into three: *musica mundana*, the proportions of the
heavens; *musica instrumentalis*, what a hand makes on a string; and between them **musica humana**, the
fitting-together that holds a soul to a body and the parts of a soul to each other. The claim that
carries the whole treatise is I.1's: *musica naturaliter nobis est coniuncta* — music is joined to us by
nature, not applied to us from outside. **A person does not have a harmony. A person is one**, and a
chord sounded near them does not add anything; it addresses a ratio that was already there and either
agrees with it or does not.

**That is Symphonia Ascendant stated six hundred years before the physics.** Serenyra's aim, in her own
words on the card, is *"the equation that lets every soul sing in harmony with its own light"* — which
is *musica mundana* and *musica humana* proposed as one formula. And the working's Limit is Boethius's
epistemology exactly: you cannot sound a person's ratio until you have heard it, because the ratio is
theirs and not yours to assign.

**The productive misreading, and what it kills.** Read I.1 as a prescription rather than a description
and you get a school of tuners. If a soul *is* a harmony, then a soul behaving badly is a soul **out of
tune**, and the remedy is obvious and humane: sound the true ratio at it, patiently, until it conforms.
Boethius gives them the warrant — his own chapter heading has music ennobling or corrupting the morals of
the hearer — and the practice looks like mercy. They treat rather than fight.

**Two lines in the registers kill them, and the second one kills them worse.** The first is bandwidth: a
resonator accepts drive **only inside Δf around its own line** (§1a), so a true chord played at a soul
that does not carry it is not medicine slowly working, it is **no coupling at all**, forever, at any
volume. The tuners spend careers playing perfect ratios at people who cannot receive them and reading
the silence as resistance. The second is worse, because it is the thing that *does* work. The only chord
that couples is **the target's own**, which means every successful intervention begins with the tuner
reproducing, exactly and in public, the pattern he came to correct. And Mirithane's Failure is waiting
there: *"The mirror does not discriminate. A practitioner in a state they would rather not broadcast
broadcasts it perfectly."* **A tuner holding a murderer's chord long enough to act on it is, to every
reader in the room, a murderer.** The school's records are full of physicians tried for the illnesses
they treated, and the records are not wrong about what was measured.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**The glyph, and one of them is attested.** `[Lh]` **Frequency** (Root · Selhar) for the lock and
`[RenB]` **Boundary** (Root · Zhaeren) for the interface are **proposed** filings under the Index's
Standing Note — *"Assignment is decided after the phenomenon, never before, and it is a filing decision
rather than a generative one"* — and neither is canon until ruled (`SA-GAP-GLYPH-MIRROR`). The third is
not a proposal. `[Ur]` **Balance** — *"Stabilizes Essence gradients; equalizes opposing forces"*, Root ·
Urion — is one of the Index's two fully worked entries, and its attested spread runs to seven Wellsprings
across six Families **including Mirithane**. So the Index already records a glyph attested in this
working's own secondary current whose Meaning & Use is *equalising opposing forces*, which is what an
interface does to a wave crossing it.

**What boundary moves.** Not the target's power and not her chord. **The boundary that moves is the
index of the target's own output path** — the number that decides how fast a working propagates through
the medium it is leaving, and therefore, at every interface, which way it bends. The Index is explicit
about the shape of this: *"A glyph creates nothing and carries no power. … It moves a limit, and the law
does what it has always done on the far side of the new limit."* **Nothing is added to the target and
nothing is taken away.** A discontinuity is written across the path his next working must cross, and
Snell's law is standing there already.

**Why the chord has to come first, and why it has to be his.** An index discontinuity has to be written
*on* something, and what it is written on is a resonance. The rising chord drives the target's pattern at
its own line — the line she measured when she heard him cast — and a driven resonance is the one part of
another practitioner's architecture that will accept a boundary condition from outside without being
broken into. This is why *"it does not touch a purely mundane attack"* (`:19`): a crossbow bolt has no
line to drive, no Essence path, and therefore no interface to write on. **The limitation and the coupling
mechanism are one fact.**

**What the law then does on its own — and it does not consult her.** The interface is set; the target
fires; Snell's law returns an angle. She does not choose between the three outcomes and the page never
claims she does. **The angle of incidence chooses**, which is to say: how squarely his working met the
law she put in front of it. Shallow crossings deviate and he misses. Crossings past the critical angle
have no transmitted solution and the whole of it returns along its own incident path, phase-conjugated,
arriving at him as exactly what left him. And where the interface is rough — where her chord matched his
line well enough to lock and not well enough to stay smooth — the phase relationship does not survive the
crossing, and what was a laser leaves as a lamp.

**The failure mode, and it answers the page's own open question.** *"Why a rejected chord slides sideways
instead of simply failing to resolve, when every other refraction in her repertoire either lands cleanly
or does not activate at all"* (`:21`). **Because neither of the two laws under this working has a "fail"
state, and that is a property of both of them rather than a coincidence.**

Snell's law always returns an answer. Given an index ratio and an angle it produces a direction, and in
the one case where it cannot — sin θ_t > 1 — it does not return nothing, it returns **total internal
reflection**. There is no third branch. An interface cannot decline.

And the resonance half does the same thing for the same reason. Two oscillators coupled with strength g
do not have crossing eigenfrequencies. As the detuning δ is swept through zero the two branches
**repel**, ω± = ω̄ ± ½√(δ² + 4g²), and the closest they ever come is 2g. This is an **avoided crossing**,
and what happens at it is that the two modes **exchange character** rather than merging or cancelling: a
chord that nearly matches does not fail to resolve, it **hybridises**, and the energy leaves down the
other branch. Sideways is the only direction available. Every other refraction in her repertoire lands
cleanly or does not activate because it is written on a surface she controls; this one is written on
somebody else's resonance, and a coupled system has no off.

The Four Faults reading is therefore **Boundary fault** — *"The condition was established imprecisely and
the law ran somewhere unintended"* — and the page has named its own fault class in its own "what nobody
knows" without recognising it.

**What the target sees and feels.** Almost nothing, and that is the difficulty. He is aware of being
listened to: there is a rising chord somewhere he cannot place, thinner than it should be for the
distance, and a sub-audible hum that was there before the chord and does not stop with it. No glyph
lands on him. Nothing touches his Shell. Everything he can test reports that he is fine, because he is —
the working did not act on him, it acted on the road his next working will take, and roads do not hurt.

Then he casts, and the world's response is wrong in a way that has no precedent in his training. Not
weaker. Not blocked. **Aimed elsewhere.** The working leaves him at full strength and arrives thirty
metres off his mark, and the second thing he notices, a half-beat later, is that it was at full strength
— that nothing was taken, which means nothing will be given back either.

Or it does not leave at all. It reaches something a metre out that is not a wall, turns, and comes home
down the exact line it went out on, including the two corrections he put into it on the way, and hits him
as precisely as he aimed it — which for a practitioner who aims well is the worst possible property for a
returned working to have.

Or the third thing happens, and the third thing is not survivable at close range by anyone standing near
him. His working stops being coherent. All of it becomes light and sound at once, in every direction, at
the full energy he committed — and a fifth of a tonne of TNT going off as a flashbulb does not
discriminate between the man who threw it and the woman five kilometres away who never came into range
at all. **The page calls this outcome harmless. Nothing in the two laws under it supports the word**
(`SA-PHYS-SYMPHONIA-HARMLESS-LIGHT`).

## 5 · Counterplay and the challenge

**The fairness check.**

| Test | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Costs something that hurts | **FAIL on the page, pass on the card** | The page charges *"a temporary tremor in her own resonance that costs her precision; for a while afterward she hears echoes of chords not yet played"* (`:18`) — inconvenience, and priced in terms the corpus does not define. The card charges her **archive**: *"High-output use of Symphonia Ascendant causes minor temporal fractures in her internal reality field, requiring intensive Oneirion meditation to realign"*, on a woman whose title is High Archivist of Resonance. **The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Break the Body — "Shot," and the page concedes it in five words.** *"It does not touch a purely
  mundane attack."* The register: *"Chemically cheap, mechanically simple, and it does not care about
  Stage below the point where reaction speed outruns it. … **A volley is a volley.**"* A five-kilometre
  working that turns a practitioner's own power against him has **no answer at all** to a man with a
  crossbow, and her Vitality is 262, B-Grade, *"her floor"* (card `:69`).
- **Break the Boundary — "Interrupt the chain," which she has volunteered.** *"Below Refraction a working
  requires an external anchor: voice, hand, ink or blood. … **Put a hand over his mouth** and a spoken one
  is. Above Refraction the chain is internal and none of this applies."* She is Stage VIII. **None of this
  should apply to her** — and her own card says *"only Symphonia Ascendant is spoken"* (`:41`). The one
  working on her sheet that reaches five kilometres is the one she has given an interrupt.
- **Break the Boundary — "Knot," which is the chaotic-pattern counter with a name.** *"**Knot** entangles
  a path so it cannot be traced cleanly."* That is §1a's broadband target stated as a Counter form, and
  the Index records only two such forms in all 136.
- **Deny the Field — "Choose the ground by Family," and the room that beats her is a specific room.**
  Fulguria fails in *"Faraday-caged interiors, heavy ferrous shielding, dense fog"*; Limina fails in
  *"Saturated Aether, crowd density, active Domains."* The register is blunt about the first:
  *"**The counter-Fulguria kit has been leather and ceramic for four centuries and it is not a secret;
  it is simply cheaper to ignore until somebody dies of it.**"* The uncomfortable half, which the lookup
  trail leaves for the reader, is what she is carrying that sits on Limina's list.
- **Break the Man — "Read him," and her card says this cuts both ways.** *"Taught techniques are
  documented and counterable by anyone who studied. **Self-derived techniques must be read live.**"* The
  card, on Crystalline Recall: *"**Anyone who has studied her (Crystalline Recall cuts both ways) knows
  the second is cheaper.**"* Her own Trait is a teaching instrument, and a teaching instrument has
  students.
- **Break the Man — "Rigidity," which here is a procedure rather than a Trait.** *"Present him with the
  situation his own law handles worst and he will handle it that way regardless."* Her law requires a
  prior read. An opponent who has never been heard, or who never uses the same working twice, is not
  resisting the technique — he is standing outside its only entry condition, and she will keep reaching
  for it because it is what she has.

**The tell, stated plainly.** Coherent output is a beacon and the register says so. Before anything
happens there is a hum under the room that takes the edge off ordinary sound, and it is her passive field
at S-Grade rather than a mood. When the working starts there is a rising chord from a direction the
distance does not account for, and — uniquely on her sheet — **a voice**, because she speaks this one and
speaks nothing else. Her eyes carry rotating `[Ma]` Recall glyphs the whole time, which is the archive
running, which is the thing that made the prior read.

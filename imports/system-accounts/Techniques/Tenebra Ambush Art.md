# System account · Tenebra Ambush Art

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** A large man in a dim place stops returning a signal, crosses eight
metres, and is already swinging when he arrives. The quarry was watching the place he used to be.

**The real phenomenon.** **Contrast reduction against a background**, not invisibility — and underneath
it, **a ballistic body crossing eight metres, which is the half of the working the page does not cost.**
The signal problem is solved correctly and quantitatively by the page's own primary register. The
acoustic and structural problem is not solved at all, and it scales at the sixth power of how fast he
decides to do it.

**(a) The detection half is right, and the number behind it is a fourth root.** Tenebra's Analogue is
*"Signal-to-noise reduction. Absorptive coating and faceted return geometry"*, which is a description of
a real and well-documented engineering programme. The governing relation for any active sensor is that
**detection range goes as the fourth root of the return**, R ∝ σ^¼. That exponent is the whole reason
the discipline is worth practising and the whole reason it is so expensive: **a thousandfold reduction
in return — 30 dB, an enormous engineering achievement — buys a factor of only 5.6 in range.** Tenebra
is a Wellspring whose law is a fourth root, and it explains why its practitioners are specialists rather
than everybody.

**(b) The Limit the page states is right, and the reason it gives is not quite the reason.** The page:
*"Needs a shadow or dim zone to have a noise floor low enough to disappear into"* (`:19`). The mechanism
underneath is **contrast**, C = (L_object − L_background) / L_background, and absorption drives it the
wrong way in lit conditions. **A perfectly absorptive body has L_object ≈ 0, so against any lit
background C → −1 — the maximum negative contrast a target can have.** An absorptive coating in daylight
does not make a man quiet. It makes him the blackest object in the field of view and the easiest to
segment from it. What the technique actually requires is not a low noise floor but a **background
luminance match**: a background dark enough that L_background ≈ L_object ≈ 0 and the contrast ratio
collapses to nothing. The page's Limit is exactly correct and its Mechanism sentence — *"Draven is not
invisible, only quieter than the background"* (`:38`) — is true only inside the dim zone the Limit
already demands. Outside it he is not quieter than the background. He is a hole in it.

**(c) And the aspect-dependence weakness is worse than the page claims, which is a compliment.** The
page: *"a watcher positioned somewhere Draven did not plan for gets the full, undiminished return"*
(`:43`). Faceting does not merely fail to help off-design; **it concentrates.** The specular return from
a flat facet of area A at wavelength λ is **σ = 4πA²/λ²**, and for a facet of 0.5 m² at λ = 3 cm that is
**σ ≈ 3.5 × 10³ m²** against a human's order of **1 m²**. Off the design aspect the return is not
undiminished. **It is thousands of times an ordinary man's**, because every square metre of surface that
was angled away from the expected observer is angled squarely at somebody. The register says so in
fewer words: *"a Tenebra practitioner observed from the one direction they did not plan for returns a
very bright signal indeed."*

**(d) The crossing, which is the half nobody has costed.** Eight metres, *"in an instant"* (`:17`),
*"Eight-metre displacement, instant"* (`:39`). Take a man of **100 kg** — the corpus gives no mass for
him, only *"1.95 m. Lean, dense, heavily scarred muscle"*, so the figure is an assumption and is flagged
as one — and price the crossing at two speeds.

**At 0.1 s, which is what "instant" reads as in a fight:**

- Mean speed **80 m/s**; kinetic energy **½ × 100 × 80² = 320 kJ.**
- Launched over 0.3 m of leg extension: **a = v²/2d = 6,400/0.6 = 1.07 × 10⁴ m/s² ≈ 1,090 g**, push
  duration **7.5 ms**, mechanical power **320 kJ / 7.5 ms = 42.7 MW** — about **fourteen thousand times**
  the peak mechanical power of an elite human athlete (~3 kW).
- Ground reaction **F = ma = 1.07 MN**, over two boot soles (≈0.06 m²) **17.8 MPa.** Concrete takes
  20–40 MPa. **Ordinary soil bears about 0.2 MPa.** He does not launch from soft ground; he sinks into it.
- Drag: **½ρv²C_dA = 0.5 × 1.225 × 6,400 × 1.0 × 0.7 = 2.7 kN**, dissipating **220 kW** of drag power.
- **And that is where the stealth goes.** A solid body moving subsonically through air radiates as a
  **Curle dipole**, whose acoustic efficiency scales as **M³**; at M = 80/343 = 0.233 that is 1.27 × 10⁻².
  Taking the dipole constant across its usual range gives **tens of watts to a few kilowatts of radiated
  sound**, which at eight metres is **roughly 105–125 dB.** A chainsaw to a jackhammer, arriving from the
  dark, one tenth of a second before he does.

**At 0.8 s — a hard sprint, 10 m/s — every one of those numbers collapses:** 5 kJ of kinetic energy,
17 g, 16.7 kN of ground reaction, 0.28 MPa under the boots. And the noise falls furthest of all, because
drag power goes as v³ and dipole efficiency as M³, so **radiated acoustic power goes as the sixth power
of transit speed.** Eight times slower is **8⁶ = 262,000 times quieter — 54 dB down**, into the fifties
and sixties: a man walking quickly.

**The technique's speed and the technique's silence are the same variable pulling in opposite directions,
at the sixth power, and the page has set it to the loud end while claiming the quiet one**
(`SA-PHYS-TENEBRA-TRANSIT-NOISE`). This is the same shape as Oblivion-Step, where a quarter-second lag is
what buys the silence; here the lag has been removed and the silence claimed anyway.

**(e) One relationship the page gets exactly right by implication.** The crossing costs **320 kJ.** His
own Strike Force at Ardency 320, A-Grade, is *"46 GJ to 4 TJ yield"* (Part Eleven, card `:91`). **The
approach is 1.4 × 10⁻⁵ of the floor of the blow** — five orders of magnitude cheaper than the thing it
delivers. That is the correct economy for an ambush art and it is why the page's instinct that this is a
cheap working is sound even though its cost line is not.

**Energy budget, stated.** Supplied by the practitioner: **320 kJ of kinetic store at 0.1 s, 5 kJ at
0.8 s**, plus whatever the absorptive layer costs, which is small — a square metre of surface absorbing
full sunlight takes 1.4 kJ per second and a dim zone is by definition orders below that. Where the joules
were: chemical store in his own muscle, ignited through the Shell — *"Channels Aether into hyper-dense
muscular ignition and aggressive kinetic force"* (card `:34`). Dissipated: **220 kW of drag** for the
duration of the fast crossing, of which 10⁻²–10⁻⁴ leaves as sound and the rest as heat in the air behind
him. Fault class by `Two Sets of Books` §VII: **Field fault** — *"The room could not supply what the
working assumed. … The working is correct and produces nothing"* — and the page names this fault itself:
*"A fully lit, radiant-saturated space gives Tenebra nothing to subtract from, and the technique does not
function there."*

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum, and the two Families want different rooms for once in agreement.** **Limina**,
*"**Spirit** primary · **Medium** at Veil-Thin density and above"*, favoured by *"Darkness, silence,
Aether-thin ground, ruins"*, suppressed by *"Saturated Aether, crowd density, active Domains"*.
**Vectoria**, *"Split: **Wild** (Gravity, Seismic) · **Spirit** (Sound, Force projection)"*, favoured by
*"Structural mass, elevation, brittle or pre-stressed material"*, suppressed by *"Loose sediment, free
fall, uniform soft ground"*
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings.md`). *"Terrain is not flavour. It is
a modifier on η."*

**Put the two rows side by side and the working names its own ground: a dark ruin with a hard floor.**
Limina wants darkness, silence and ruins; Vectoria wants structural mass and worked stone. They agree,
which is rare, and the agreement is the technique — an ambush art is a thing you do in a derelict
building. And they agree about what kills it too. **A lit, crowded camp pitched on soft mud suppresses
both Families at once**, and §1(d) says why in units: 0.28–17.8 MPa of launch pressure against soil that
bears 0.2. The register's note is the whole of it — *"They simply pay a larger fraction of their reserve
to overcome the gradient they are fighting rather than riding."*

**Saturation, and the card raises it before anyone else does.** Four named harmonisations plus *"Three
additional Wellsprings networked along predatory lines"*, and the card flags the arithmetic itself:
*"which would make his harmonisation total six — **above his Tempering Capacity at this Stage, and worth
flagging**"* (card `:49`). Tempering 248, B-Grade, at Stage VI. Nothing in the system converts a Tempering
figure into a permitted harmonisation count, so the card's flag stands as a flag.

**Wellspring stratum — Tenebra, *The Hidden Shadow*, Limina, Entropy/Void/Mind.** Core Law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Limina — Entropy, Void and Mind.md`:

> **Analogue** · Signal-to-noise reduction. Absorptive coating and faceted return geometry.
> **Mechanism** · Something is undetectable when its return falls below the noise floor of whatever is
> looking, which is achieved by absorbing incident energy and by shaping surfaces so the little that
> reflects goes anywhere except back at the observer. Tenebra applies both to the Shell. Its
> practitioners are not invisible. They are quieter than the background, which is better.
> **Stat Effect** · Strengthens Dexterity Silence, Harmonics Suppression, Dominion Sense.
> **Failure** · Aspect dependence. Faceting works against expected angles, and a Tenebra practitioner
> observed from the one direction they did not plan for returns a very bright signal indeed.

**The page's three Sub-Stats are this line and nothing else.** *"Dexterity Silence (386, his documented
peak) governing; Harmonics Suppression and Dominion Sense"* (`:50`) is the register's Stat Effect copied
across, which is honest derivation. Under the Merge Ledger, **Silence → Feint** — a retired name and
therefore a reading rather than a conflict (`_method.md` §VI) — and the reading is worth pausing on,
because the Sub-Stat that now carries his stealth is the one named for deception in contact.

**Wellspring stratum — Petralon, *The Foundation Stone*, Vectoria, Mechanics.** Core Law, verbatim:

> **Analogue** · Compressive load path and creep. Stone in compression; the arch.
> **Mechanism** · Masonry is enormously strong in compression and nearly worthless in tension, which is
> why the arch exists: it converts a span into a queue of compressive members. Petralon reorganises the
> Crystal along the same principle, routing every incoming load into compression and accepting slow
> creep deformation as the price of never developing a tensile crack.
> **Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Endurance, Dominion Gravity. The character becomes the ground
> beneath the ground.
> **Failure** · Tensile exposure. Load the structure in the one direction it does not answer and it does
> not deform gracefully. It parts.

**And here the page has the right Wellspring for the wrong reason, and the right reason is better.** The
page says *"Petralon's kinetic ignition supplies the launch"* (`:38`) and the card agrees, calling it
*"**The kinetic ignition**"* (card `:48`). **Petralon is not an ignition current.** Its law is the arch;
its Stat Effect is Vitality Endurance and Dominion Gravity; its one-line gloss is *"the character becomes
the ground beneath the ground."* That is the opposite of a launch — it is the thing that does not move
(`SA-CROSS-TENEBRA-PETRALON-ROLE`).

**But §1(d) produced a meganewton of ground reaction through two legs**, and a femur fails in compression
at about 10 kN. Something has to carry 1.07 MN through a skeleton a hundred times over, **routing every
incoming load into compression and never developing a tensile crack.** *That* is Petralon, word for word,
and it is the harder engineering problem of the two. **Petralon is not what throws him. Petralon is what
stops his legs coming apart when something else does.** The ignition itself is on the card under a
different heading and it is not a Wellspring at all: *"**Vorynn Bloodbind** · Through an ancestral pact,
his blood can ignite his muscles with primal, predatory force"* (card `:85`), with the Shell as the
conduit — *"Channels Aether into hyper-dense muscular ignition."*

**And Petralon's Failure then tells him which end of the crossing kills him.** *"Tensile exposure. Load
the structure in the one direction it does not answer and it does not deform gracefully. **It parts.**"*
The launch is compression: legs against ground, exactly what the arch answers. **The arrest is tension** —
a body at speed stopped by its own frame, pulling the frame apart from the inside. The page's own
Numerical Effect has him *"already loaded into the strike"* on arrival, which means the deceleration is
paid by the target rather than by him, which is the only version of this working Petralon permits. **If
he ever has to stop himself instead of stopping on somebody, the Wellspring holding him together is
being loaded in the one direction its register says it does not answer.**

**Which of the Four Theories.** **Residue**, quietly, and the page states it without naming it: *"The
cold-trail effect (Cinerion, his ash-and-remnant harmonisation) settles over his old position and the
path he actually took, which is why pursuit reads the wrong trail as the fresher one"* (`:41`). A working
that leaves a mark, and whose tactical value is what the mark does to whoever reads it afterwards, is a
Residue working whatever else it is. **Consent Doctrine** runs underneath as the standing enforced
position.

**Essence stratum.** Crystal layer: the **Aether Shell**, *"Class III · Resonant"* (card `:34`), which is
the layer the absorptive coating is applied to — the register says *"Tenebra applies both to the Shell"*,
so the working is a surface treatment of the Class the card already names. Developmental Tier
**Harmonic** (Stage VI, Part Twenty-One). Tier of Standing **5 · Expert**, η band **0.60–0.70**
(Part Nineteen as corrected by **R44-4**); card η *"~0.55"* — **0.05 below the floor**
(`C-062`, not resolved here). Crystal State *"Refined"*. **The invoice:** the page says a drip of blood from
eyes and nose, and §5 argues that is not what it costs.

**The lens: the ring of Gyges, and the man who is only ever just when watched.** Plato, *Republic* II,
359d–360d. Glaucon tells the story to make an argument Socrates then spends nine books answering: a
shepherd finds a ring that makes him invisible, and within a fortnight he has seduced the queen, killed
the king and taken the kingdom. Glaucon's claim is not that the ring corrupts. It is that **justice was
never a property of the man in the first place** — it was a property of being seen, and the ring does not
change anyone, it only removes the condition under which they had been behaving. *"No one, it seems,
would be so incorruptible that he would stay on the path of justice."*

**The Tenebra Ambush Art is Glaucon's thought experiment issued as a discipline.** And Draven's card
reads like the case study: *"his sheet lists no relationships at all"*, an Axis that died, *"constant
self-imposed trials of endurance, ambush and predatory pressure"*, an epithet — *"If It Breathes, I Can
End It"* — that is a statement about capability with no clause about permission in it anywhere. A man
whose profession is being unobserved is a man for whom Glaucon's question is not hypothetical, and the
card's own unanswered question is the Socratic one: *"what did he value above himself?"*

**The productive misreading, and what it kills.** Take Glaucon to have *won* the argument and a training
doctrine falls out of it immediately. If the observed self is a performance, then **the unobserved self is
the true self**, and the way to find out what a man is is to take the watchers away. So a school trains
by subtraction: solitude, darkness, the removal of every witness, on the theory that what remains when
nobody is looking is the only thing that was ever really there. They are not cynics. They are, in their
own account, the most rigorous empiricists in the register — the only ones testing the specimen with the
observer removed.

**Tenebra's Failure kills them, and it kills them on the exact point of their doctrine.** *"Aspect
dependence. Faceting works against expected angles, and a Tenebra practitioner observed from the one
direction they did not plan for returns a very bright signal indeed."* **There is no unobserved state.
There is only a set of angles you have modelled and a set you have not** — and §1(c) shows that the ones
you have not modelled do not give an ordinary reading, they give one thousands of times brighter than an
ordinary man would give, because every surface you turned away from the expected watcher you turned
squarely toward somebody else. The school's premise is that the observer can be removed. The register's
position is that the observer can only be **relocated**, and that the harder you work at the first thing
the louder you are to whoever is standing in the direction you forgot. Gyges's ring has no back. The
school produces men who believe they have been alone for twenty years and have in fact been conspicuous
from one bearing the whole time, and who find out which bearing on the day somebody happens to be
standing on it.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**The glyph, proposed.** `[Sil]` **Silence** (Synonym · Auren) for the return suppression and `[Ren]`
**Passage** (Root · Auren) for the crossing — both from the thirty mirrored entries, both filings under
the Index's Standing Note (*"Assignment is decided after the phenomenon, never before, and it is a filing
decision rather than a generative one"*), neither canon until ruled (`SA-GAP-GLYPH-MIRROR`). That both
proposals sit under the same Archon is a property of the Index and not an argument.

**What boundary moves.** Two boundaries move and they move in different places, which is why this is two
Wellsprings and not one.

The first is **the surface condition on his own Shell.** A boundary between two media is entirely
described by how much of an arriving wave it absorbs, transmits and returns, and in which directions.
Nothing about the wave changes and nothing about the observer changes. **The reflection coefficient of one
surface changes**, and every detection equation downstream of it — R ∝ σ^¼ — does what it always did with
the new number.

The second is **where the load in his own frame is permitted to go.** Petralon's boundary is not on the
outside of him. It is the condition that every load path in the structure must be a compressive one, which
is what the arch is, and it is the reason a skeleton can be asked to carry a meganewton for seven and a
half milliseconds without parting.

**What the law then does on its own.** In the dim zone, the first boundary makes his return fall below
what the quarry's eye or Essence-sight is integrating against the background, and detection theory does
the rest without further instruction: below the noise floor, a return is not faint, it is **absent**,
because there is no operation available that distinguishes it from the floor. He is not hidden. He is
**not present in the data**, which is a different and much stronger condition, and it is why the page's
phrase *"removes him from the quarry's sensory field"* is closer to right than it has any business being.

Then the ignition fires — the Bloodbind through the Shell, not Petralon — and Newton does the rest. The
ground pushes back exactly as hard as he pushes on it, Petralon queues that push through him as
compression, and eight metres later he arrives with 320 kJ of kinetic store and no plan for shedding it
other than the target.

**The failure mode, and the page knows one of the two.** The one it knows is the Field fault: no dim
zone, no contrast match, no working. The one it does not know is in §1(d) — **the sixth-power trade.** At
the speed the page states, the crossing radiates 105–125 dB from the dark. The absorptive layer does
nothing about this, because it conditions a *reflective* channel and the noise is *emitted*: Tenebra
suppresses what comes back off him and has no authority at all over what he makes. **The working's
stealth and the working's speed are not two features. They are one dial, and the page has it hard over.**

**What the target sees and feels.** Nothing, for as long as it lasts, and that is the difficulty of
writing this working honestly: the quarry's experience of the first part is an absence, and an absence
does not have qualities. There is a dim place. There is no one in it. There is a slight, unlocatable
wrongness that people describe afterwards in temperature terms, because he is in fact colder — *"a
palpable drop in ambient temperature; the air smells faintly of ozone and burned bone"* (card `:36`) —
and because a man who is absorbing rather than reflecting is a man who is taking something out of the
room.

Then the room makes a sound it should not be able to make. Not a footfall and not a voice: a **shear** —
a hard, low, moving tear of air, from a direction, over in a tenth of a second, and by the time the head
has turned toward it the sound has already arrived from a place he is no longer in. Then he is inside
reach, which is where the working ends and the Ardency begins.

Afterwards there is the part that makes pursuit worse. The cold trail settles over the ground he left and
the line he took, both of them — *"which is why pursuit reads the wrong trail as the fresher one"* — so
the residue does not conceal the path, it **duplicates** it, and the tracker is not looking for something
faint. He is choosing between two things that are equally clear.

What does not close is everything about the eight metres. **At the stated speed the crossing is a
meganewton through two legs, seventeen megapascals under the boots on ground that bears a fifth of one,
forty-three megawatts, and a hundred and five to a hundred and twenty-five decibels of radiated sound,
from the dark, one tenth of a second ahead of him.** The absorptive layer has no authority over any of
it — it conditions what returns off him, not what he makes. And the system already knows: Part Seven's
Feint clause prices *"suppression of kinetic noise"* at S-Grade and he is at A. The other loose end is
Petralon, which the page and the card both call a kinetic ignition and whose register calls it an arch;
the correct reading makes it load-bearing rather than propulsive, which is the harder job and comes with
a stated failure mode the page does not have.

## 5 · Counterplay and the challenge

**The fairness check.**

|---|---|---|
| Costs something that hurts | **FAIL, and the page says so on its own page** | *"Essence draw shows as a mild drip from eyes and nose"*, and then, with unusual honesty: *"per standing drawback rules, 'it strains him' alone is not treated as a real cost, and none beyond the documented drip is claimed here."* An 8 m repositioning that arrives unseen and mid-strike, at will, for a nosebleed. **And the cost is available — on the card, one heading away.** If the ignition is the **Vorynn Bloodbind**, as the card's own Trait line says (*"his blood can ignite his muscles with primal, predatory force"*), then the working carries the Bloodbind's price: *"**Risk · Primal Regression.** Over-reliance on the Vorynn Bloodbind risks triggering an uncontrolled metamorphosis into a Prime Beast form, resulting in permanent loss of sapience and systemic collapse."* **That is the cleanest stated cost on any sheet in this batch and this page does not reach for it** (`SA-FAIR-TENEBRA-NO-COST`). |

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Deny the Field — "Choose the ground by Family," and here it is two Families with one answer.** Limina
  fails in *"Saturated Aether, crowd density, active Domains"*; Vectoria fails in *"Loose sediment, free
  fall, uniform soft ground."* **A crowded, lit camp on soft ground suppresses both at once** — and the
  ground half is not flavour, it is the 0.2 MPa in §1(d). He cannot launch off mud. The register's
  standing line applies without modification: *"Terrain is not flavour. It is a modifier on η."*
- **Break the Body — "Distance," which the card concedes in nine words.** *"**Range** · Close. Melee and
  tracking. **He has no answer at distance and has never needed one.**"* The register: *"Vectoria has to
  close. … **Most practitioners are a threat about closing range and are written as though they were
  not.**"* This working's entire function is to buy eight metres of closing, which is a statement about
  how far he is from being able to reach you at forty.
- **Break the Body — "Shot."** *"A C-Grade practitioner can react to gunfire and cannot outrun it. **A
  volley is a volley.**"* He crosses eight metres in a tenth of a second at best. A drilled line does not
  need to see him to put a volley into the dark he came out of, and a volley into a dark doorway is the
  counter to an ambush art that has existed in every army that ever had one.
- **Break the Boundary — "Interrupt the chain," which Law V hands you for free at his Stage.** *"Below
  Refraction a working requires an external anchor: voice, hand, ink or blood. Take the anchor and the
  sentence does not complete. **Break his wrist and a gestured chain is finished. Put a hand over his
  mouth and a spoken one is.**"* Stage VI is below Refraction. **He is inside the range of this route and
  the two workings it distinguishes have completely different tells** — which is why the unstated anchor
  is logged rather than glossed.
- **Break the Man — "Read him," and the register names his exact category.** *"Taught techniques are
  documented and counterable by anyone who studied. **Self-derived techniques must be read live, in the
  two or three exchanges before they kill you.**"* The page: *"Draven derived this himself."* And the
  second half is the counter: *"**Every thaumaturge has a pattern**, because single-axis fluency produces
  one, and a pattern learned is a pattern interruptible."* His pattern is eight metres out of the nearest
  dark, and the nearest dark is a thing a commander can enumerate before the fighting starts.
- **Break the Man — "Rigidity," in its most literal available form.** *"Present him with the situation his
  own law handles worst and he will handle it that way regardless."* His card's Risk is *"Primal
  Regression … permanent loss of sapience"*, and his Harmonics is **124, his floor, with no relationships
  on the sheet at all.** A man with one route and nothing holding him back from it is a man who takes the
  route.

**The tell, stated plainly.** The cold arrives before he does, and people report it as weather. The room
goes marginally quieter in the ordinary sense because something in it is absorbing rather than returning.
Then a shear of air from a bearing, loud and brief and already stale by the time the head turns — the
crossing is the loudest thing in the working and the page does not count it. And afterwards there are two
trails from the same man and no way to tell which is the one he is still on.

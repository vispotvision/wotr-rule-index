# System account · The Aether Bastion

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** A crystalline shell stands off a few metres from a woman. Things
arrive at it. Some of them go through and some of them stop, and which is which is not decided by how
hard they arrive.

**The real phenomenon.** **Selective permeability**, exactly as the page says — and the page is the only
one in this batch that names its own governing law correctly in its own Mechanism line. What the page
does not carry is the three quantitative consequences that come attached to it: **a selectivity-throughput
trade-off**, **a response-latency floor set by the speed of sound in the ward's own lattice**, and **a
detection threshold below which nothing is judged at all** — which turns out to be both of its stated
weaknesses written twice.

**(a) Selectivity is never free, and the relation has a name.** A membrane that discriminates and a
membrane that passes flux are in tension, and the tension is empirical and well characterised:
**Robeson's upper bound** (J. Membrane Sci. 1991; revisited 2008) plots selectivity against permeability
for every gas-separation polymer ever measured and finds a hard ceiling with a negative slope — the more
finely a barrier sorts, the less it passes per second, and no material has ever been found above the
line. The physical reason is simple enough to state in one clause: **sorting requires the arriving thing
to interact with the barrier, and interaction is what slows it down.** A membrane that does not slow
anything down has not looked at anything.

So the page's *"a static field 3 to 7 m around the caster"* that *"resists whatever fails a specific
test, and passes whatever matches the standard set at construction"* (`:38`) is not a shield with a
discriminator bolted on. **Discrimination is the cost structure**, and every improvement in the verdict
is paid for in throughput. This is why a ward like this is a standing working with a continuous drain
rather than a thing you switch on: it is not holding energy back, it is **running a test, continuously,
on everything.**

**(b) The response-latency floor, which is the sharpest number in this account.** The page has the ward
arresting, purifying or reflecting what reaches it — which means the ward must *act*, locally, after the
verdict. An action propagating through a crystalline solid travels at the speed of sound in that solid,
and nothing in the ward can respond to news from elsewhere in the ward faster than that. Representative
longitudinal wave speeds: **steel ≈ 5,900 m/s, quartz ≈ 5,700 m/s, alumina ≈ 10,000 m/s, diamond
≈ 18,000 m/s.** Take the ward as an alumina-class crystalline shell and give it the best of that range.

Now read the page's own Grade. **A-Grade travel speed is Mach 5–25** (Part Four), which is
**1,715–8,575 m/s.**

**At the top of its own Grade band, an incoming attack is travelling at 86 % of the speed at which the
ward can tell itself anything.** Across a 7 m shell the crossing time for the ward's own signal is
7 / 10,000 = **0.7 ms**, in which an 8,575 m/s arrival covers **six metres** — which is to say, the whole
radius. **The ward cannot mobilise anywhere it is not already mobilised**, and the verdict has to be
local, instantaneous and pre-positioned or it does not happen at all.

Push one Grade up and it stops being close. **S-Grade travel speed is Mach 25–200, 8,575–68,600 m/s** —
up to **seven times the ward's internal signal speed.** A barrier whose response is bounded by sound in
its own lattice cannot be a selective barrier against anything supersonic in that lattice; it can only be
a wall. **The page's stated Grade and the page's stated mechanism agree with each other precisely, and
neither of them survives contact with one Grade higher** (`SA-PHYS-BASTION-RESPONSE-LATENCY`).

**(c) The stated capacity is a force, and a barrier's capacity is a pressure.** The page:
*"tens of giganewtons of peak force before failure"* (`:39`), which is correctly read off Part Eleven's
A-Grade Strike Force row, **20–100 GN.** But a shell fails where the stress exceeds the material's
strength, and stress is force **per unit area**:

| Load spread over | 20 GN | 100 GN |
|---|---|---|
| **r = 7 m shell, 616 m²** | **32 MPa** | **162 MPa** |
| **r = 3 m shell, 113 m²** | **177 MPa** | **884 MPa** |
| **a 1 cm² point** | **2 × 10¹⁴ Pa** | **10¹⁵ Pa** |

The first two rows are **entirely reasonable for a crystalline shell**: structural steel yields at
250–550 MPa and alumina takes 2–3 GPa in compression, so a 7 m bastion shrugging off 100 GN spread across
its whole surface is good engineering and a real pass. **The third row is not survivable by any material
that exists or could exist.** 200 TPa is roughly **two thousand times diamond's compressive strength.**

So *"enough raw kinetic weight can fracture it outright"* (`:43`) is the right counter for the wrong
reason. **Weight is not what does it. Concentration is.** An avalanche and a lance delivering the same
total force are not the same problem by a factor of 10⁶, and the page states its capacity in the unit
that cannot distinguish them (`SA-NUM-BASTION-FORCE-NOT-PRESSURE`).

**(d) Both stated weaknesses are one fact, and the fact is a detection threshold.** The page lists
*"Tenebra-aligned attacks pass through gaps in the judgment logic itself"* and *"memory-based illusions
are never registered by the barrier as an incoming force in the first place"* (`:43`) as two separate
holes. They are the same hole, and it is the hole every discriminating instrument has.

A test that sorts arrivals must first **detect** an arrival. Tenebra's law is *"Something is undetectable
when its return falls below the noise floor of whatever is looking"* — that is a statement about a
detection threshold, not about a verdict. The ward's judgment is not deceived by a Tenebra working. **The
judgment never runs**, because nothing crossed the threshold that starts it. And a memory-based illusion
that *"never register[s] as an incoming force"* is the same sentence with a different Family in it.

Read that way the ward is a **matched filter**: an instrument that correlates every arrival against a
stored template — *"a fixed standard, Opalis's own Essence"* (`:37`) — and acts on the correlation.
Matched filtering is provably the optimal detector for a **known** signal in noise, and it is exactly and
provably blind to everything the template does not describe. **Its strength and its two weaknesses are
one theorem.**

**Energy budget, stated.** Supplied by the practitioner: the boundary condition and a continuous drain to
keep the test running; the page states **5,000 EU** and says in the same clause that the figure was
*"chosen conservatively"* rather than derived, which is exactly what the batch may not do
(`SA-NUM-BASTION-5000-EU`). Handled by the ward: **20–100 GN** of peak force, i.e. **32–884 MPa** spread,
or **46 GJ – 4.184 TJ** of incoming yield at A-Grade. Where those joules were: the attacker's. Dissipated:
the page says nothing accumulates — *"What the barrier arrests does not accumulate inside it; it is
resolved on contact"* (`:41`) — which is a claim that a terajoule-class arrest leaves no residue, and the
page states no sink for it either. Fault class by `Two Sets of Books` §VII: **Coherence fault** —
*"The practitioner's lived behaviour and their stated law have drifted apart, and the Continuum reads the
drift"* — because the page's own stated failure condition is that exact sentence: *"the barrier's own
judgment logic makes it fail instantly if Opalis's stated intent and her actual intent diverge while it
stands"* (`:42`).

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum, and the page's own Family line is wrong about half of it.** The page declares
*"**Family** · Fulguria"* for both currents (`:27`). **Judicium is Fulguria, Electromagnetism. Sublimatio
is Vectoria, Mechanics** (`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Vectoria — Mechanics.md`), and
the page's own Physics Domain line even half-notices, giving *"Electromagnetism"* and nothing for the
second (`SA-CROSS-BASTION-SUBLIMATIO-FAMILY`).

It matters because the two Families want opposite ground. **Fulguria**, *"**Spirit**"*, favoured by *"Dry
air, conductive substrate, clear line of sight"*, suppressed by *"Faraday-caged interiors, heavy ferrous
shielding, dense fog"*. **Vectoria**, *"Split: **Wild** (Gravity, Seismic) · **Spirit** (Sound, Force
projection)"*, favoured by *"Structural mass, elevation, brittle or pre-stressed material"*, suppressed by
*"Loose sediment, free fall, uniform soft ground"*. *"Terrain is not flavour. It is a modifier on η."*
**A ward that stands on worked stone in dry air is running both currents favoured**, and the register
itself says what the counter-kit is: *"**The counter-Fulguria kit has been leather and ceramic for four
centuries and it is not a secret; it is simply cheaper to ignore until somebody dies of it.**"* A barrier
that judges by reading a spectrum is a barrier answered by a man in boiled leather.

**Saturation.** Two harmonizations across two Families, a light load — and unusually, unverifiable in
principle rather than in practice, because **Opalis has no character card** (`SA-GAP-OPALIS-NO-CARD`).
Every figure on this page is either quoted from the page or derived from a Stage.

**Wellspring stratum — Judicium, *The Wellspring of Truth*, Fulguria, Electromagnetism.** Core Law,
verbatim from `wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Fulguria — Electromagnetism.md`:

> **Analogue** · Emission and absorption spectroscopy. Nondestructive flaw detection.
> **Mechanism** · Every element absorbs and emits at wavelengths fixed by its own electron structure, and
> no substance can present another's spectrum. Judicium tunes Gnosis to that principle: the practitioner
> reads the characteristic lines of a Crystal, a technique, or a lie, and identifies composition from
> signature alone. Eddy-current inspection finds the crack under the paint without removing the paint.

> **Failure** · Spectral overload. Every object in view reports its composition simultaneously and the
> practitioner loses the ability to prioritise any of it.

**And that register answers the page's own closing question, in the only way it can be answered.** The
page asks *"Whether the ward is reading the truth of what strikes it, or only the version of the truth
Opalis is prepared to let through"* (`:21`). **Both, and they are different halves of the instrument.**
The *reading* is objective and cannot be otherwise: *"no substance can present another's spectrum"* is
about as hard a guarantee as the register gives anywhere. But a spectrometer does not return a verdict; it
returns lines. **The verdict is the comparison**, and the page's own Function line says what it is
compared against: *"a fixed standard, **Opalis's own Essence**, rather than against force or material
alone."* So the measurement is true and the criterion is her. The ward is not lying about what arrived.
It is telling the truth about how much the thing that arrived resembles Opalis.

Judicium's **Failure** is the siege condition, stated: *"Spectral overload. Every object in view reports
its composition simultaneously and the practitioner loses the ability to prioritise any of it."* A test
run continuously on everything crossing 616 square metres is the standing invitation to exactly that, and
the page's *"the ward does not visibly fill or strain under repeated small attacks"* (`:41`) is the
opposite claim.

**The page wants the Wellspring next door.** It says Sublimatio *"supplies the refining step that

function is the second sentence and its declared Wellspring is the first.

**But keep Sublimatio anyway, because its Failure is the ward's unwritten cost.** *"Residue accumulation.
Everything that could not sublime is still there, and it is the fraction of the practitioner least willing
to move."* §1(a) says a selective barrier pays in throughput and §1(d) says the page claims nothing

**The Sub-Stat list comes from neither declared Wellspring.** Judicium strengthens Gnosis Acuity, Analysis

**And the two Sub-Stats that are this working by name are both missing.** Part Twelve: *"**Ward** —

character and an effect that says they do not exist.**"* And: *"**Oath** — Structural durability of active
vows, contracts and Fate-anchor oaths under attempts to **sever, invert or weaponise** them."* The first
is what a bastion is for. The second is the ward's stated self-destruct condition read from the defending
side — and **Oath's gate is *"Attraction Path at Stage IV to exceed B"*, which is the page's own declared
Path.** It would pass (`SA-CROSS-BASTION-WARD-AND-OATH`).

**Which of the Four Theories.** **Consent Doctrine**, and this page is the batch's cleanest instance of
it. *"The Wellspring recognises a lawful boundary condition and behaves accordingly, in the same sense
that a river recognises a channel. No intention is attributed to the Wellspring. This is the position that
permits licensing, because a process can be licensed and a relationship cannot."* The Bastion is a
licensing office built as a wall: it authorises crossings against a filed standard, it attributes no
intention to anything, and its failure mode is a warrant that stopped matching the speaker.

**Essence stratum.** Crystal layer: the **Aether Shell**, projected outward and held as a standing surface
rather than worn — the page's own *"standing Density working, held open rather than fired"* (`:38`).
Developmental Tier **Harmonic** (Stage VI floor, Part Twenty-One); for Opalis herself at Stage XII,
**Sovereign**. Tier of Standing **5 · Expert** at the floor, **7 · Grandmaster** for her, η band 0.85–0.90
(Part Nineteen, derived from Stage — she has no card to check it against). Crystal State `null`.
**The invoice:** a verdict run continuously on everything that touches 616 square metres of surface, and a
standing declaration of intent that the Continuum is reading for drift the whole time.

**The lens: the *iudicium Dei*, and what the ordeal was actually for.** The medieval ordeal — the hot
iron, the cauldron, the cold water — rested on a claim of the same shape as this ward's: **that the
physical world, correctly addressed, will return a verdict**, because God will not let the innocent burn.
It was law, it was liturgy, and it was administered by priests under a written rite, until the Fourth
Lateran Council of 1215 withdrew clerical participation and the practice died within a generation for want
of anyone authorised to run it.

**And the historical scholarship on it is the page's own "what nobody knows", written by historians.**
Peter Brown's *"Society and the Supernatural: A Medieval Change"* (Daedalus, 1975) argues that the ordeal
was never a detection device at all: it was a **consensus device**, deployed in communities that already
had a view, and the real discretion lay in the administration — how hot, how long, who bandaged the hand,
who inspected it on the third day. The instrument was honest. **The instrument was also not where the
decision was made.** Which is precisely the answer §2 gives above: the spectrum is true, and the standard
it is compared against is Opalis.

**The productive misreading, and what it kills.** Read the ordeal's premise as *sound* — the world does
return verdicts — and a doctrine follows that is not obviously wrong and is enormously useful. **If a
lawful barrier passed it, it was lawful.** So a school builds courts out of wards. Goods are cleared by
walking them through. Oaths are tested by standing the swearer in the gate. Certification becomes
architectural, cheap, and — this is the seductive part — **not corruptible by any of the usual means**,
because a wall has no opinion and cannot be bribed.

**§1(d) kills them, and it kills them in the only direction they cannot look.** A matched filter is
provably optimal against what the template describes **and provably blind to everything else**, so the
things the ward does not detect are not merely missed. **They are certified.** A school that reads
"it passed" as "it was true" has built a machine that issues clean papers to exactly the class of things
it cannot see, and it issues them with the full authority of an instrument that has never once been wrong
about anything it looked at. Their archive is immaculate. Their archive is also a list, kept over
generations, of every kind of thing that can cross a threshold without being registered — and the register
names the first two entries on it: Tenebra, and a memory that never presented as force.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**The glyph, and the primary one is attested.** `[Ur]` **Balance** — *"Stabilizes Essence gradients;
equalizes opposing forces"*, Root · Urion — is one of the Master Glyph Index's two fully worked entries,
and its **Primary Wellspring is Judicium**, its Family **Fulguria**, its Physics Domain
**Electromagnetism**. That is this page's primary current exactly, and the glyph's Meaning & Use is a
description of what a barrier does to a gradient across it. The Index notes what its breadth implies:
*"A root that broad is not a specialist's glyph. It is a load-bearing member."* `[RenB]` **Boundary**
(Root · Zhaeren) and `[Ora]` **Truth** (Extension · Iesara) are **proposed** filings under the Standing
Note and are not canon until ruled (`SA-GAP-GLYPH-MIRROR`).

**What boundary moves.** Not the air, not the ground, not the attacker. **The boundary that moves is the
permeability of a surface that was not previously selective about anything.** Every surface in the world
already has a transmission coefficient; most of them have one number for everything. The glyph replaces
the number with a **function** — a transmission that depends on what is arriving — and every law
downstream of a transmission coefficient then runs unchanged. The Index says the shape of this plainly:
*"A glyph creates nothing and carries no power. … It moves a limit, and the law does what it has always
done on the far side of the new limit."*

**What the law then does on its own, and it is a great deal.** This is the working in the batch that does
the least steering once it starts. Selective permeability is not an act; it is a property, and a property
does not have to be attended to. The shell stands. Things arrive. Each arrival interacts with the surface
for however long its own speed permits, and the interaction is the test — Judicium's spectroscopy running
as **contact metrology**, reading characteristic lines off whatever is touching it, which is the one kind
of measurement that can be made a hundred times a second across six hundred square metres without anybody
deciding anything.

**And then the throughput trade-off (§1a) does the sorting for free.** What matches the template
correlates, passes and is slowed hardly at all. What does not correlate interacts longer, and interacting
longer *is* being arrested — there is no separate arresting step. **The barrier does not decide to stop
something. Something that does not match is, by the mechanics of a selective interface, still in the
barrier when the barrier is done with it.** This is why the page's *"it is resolved on contact"* is
structurally right even though its "does not accumulate" clause is not.

**The three named outcomes fall out of how badly the correlation failed.** A near-match crosses, slowed
and cleaned — the page's *"purified"*. A poor match does not complete the crossing — *"arrested"*. And a
working whose own structure is in contradiction with the standard, which is to say one whose lines cross
the template's in the wrong sense, **returns**: not as a choice, but because a wave that cannot be
transmitted at an interface has exactly one other place to go, which is back the way it came. The page
calls that *"reflected back at its source"* and it is the only outcome on the page that requires no
authority at all.

**The failure modes, and there are four.** **Speed**, which is §1(b): past the speed of sound in its own
lattice the shell cannot inform itself, and a selective barrier that cannot inform itself is a wall.
**Concentration**, which is §1(c): 20 GN spread over the shell is 32 MPa and 20 GN on a point is 200 TPa,
and the page's capacity is stated in the unit that cannot tell them apart. **Threshold**, which is §1(d):
everything below detection is not judged, and not being judged is indistinguishable from passing.
And **drift**, which is the page's own and the only one it states: *"the barrier's own judgment logic makes
it fail instantly if Opalis's stated intent and her actual intent diverge while it stands"* — a **Coherence
fault** by name, *"the slowest fault and the one that ends careers"*, running here at the speed of a
sentence.

**What the target sees and feels.** From outside, a shell that does not behave like a shield. There is no
flare on impact and no visible give — a shield deforms and this does not, because arresting here is a
failure to finish crossing rather than a load being taken. A man who throws something at it watches the
thing **slow**, in the last half-metre, at a rate that has nothing to do with how hard he threw it, and
arrive at nothing. The second time he throws something the slowing is identical. **That invariance is the
tell**, because a real wall gets worse.

What is unnerving to anyone standing inside is different. Things do come through. Not many, and never the
ones that were aimed, but the shell is manifestly permeable and manifestly not weakening, which forces the
question of what the standard is — and the honest answer, from §2, is that the standard is the woman
holding it. People who have fought behind this ward report that it feels less like cover than like being
vouched for.

And it ends the way a warrant ends rather than the way a wall ends. There is no crack and no shatter. The
shell simply stops being selective, everywhere, in the same instant, and what is left is nothing at all —
which happens when a Grade above it arrives faster than it can think, and which also happens the moment
she says something she does not mean.

**Does the physics close against the stratal account?** **This is the best-diagnosed page in the batch and
one of the least well-numbered.**

It is the only page in twenty-eight that names its own governing law correctly and unprompted: *"selective
permeability, the same principle a semipermeable membrane or a tuned filter uses."* That is right, it is
the right level of abstraction, and everything in §1 and §3 above is an elaboration of the page's own
sentence rather than a replacement for it. The Judicium half is impeccable — a spectrometer is exactly the
instrument for a contact test that must not consume what it tests, and the register's *"no substance can
present another's spectrum"* is what makes the verdict trustworthy while the page's *"a fixed standard,
Opalis's own Essence"* is what makes it partial. Its closing question answers itself out of those two
clauses. The 3–7 m radius is in band. The 20–100 GN is quoted correctly. Distributed across the shell it
is 32–884 MPa, which a crystalline solid genuinely takes, and that is a real pass on a real number.

What does not close: the capacity is stated in newtons when a barrier fails in pascals, and the difference
between those two units is the difference between a wall and nothing. The ward cannot signal across itself
faster than sound in its own lattice, which puts its selectivity ceiling at the top of its own Grade's
travel band and one full Grade below the practitioner who holds it. Its two stated weaknesses are one
detection threshold, which is a stronger and more interesting fact than two holes. Its EU figure is an
admitted estimate and its duration rests on an authority — "Law Two" — that is defined nowhere. Its second
Wellspring is filed under the wrong Family and does the job of the Wellspring listed immediately after it.
And the two Sub-Stats named in Part Twelve for precisely this working, **Ward** and **Oath**, are both
absent from a page that governs instead on a density stat, with Oath's gate sitting on the very Path the
page declares.

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Break the Man — "The vow," and the page has published its own terms.** *"A vow kept buys Coherence
  with conduct. **A vow broken is a Shear Break, and an opponent who knows the terms of a vow holds a
  weapon that does not require touching him.** Manoeuvring a sworn man into breaking his own oath is legal
  everywhere, universally despised, and has ended more careers than any duel."* The ward's own Limitation
  is *"it fail[s] instantly if Opalis's stated intent and her actual intent diverge while it stands."*
  **The technique page prints the terms of the vow as a feature.** This is the counter to the Aether
  Bastion and the page does not list it.
- **Break the Body — "Shot," which §1(c) makes into the decisive route.** *"Chemically cheap, mechanically
  simple. … **A volley is a volley.**"* A shield rated in newtons and beaten by pascals is a shield beaten
  by anything small and fast, and the register has already noticed for a different reason: *"Ceramic and
  shard are sacrificial and fail on the second hit in the same place."* **The same place** is the
  operative phrase and it is a statement about area.
- **Deny the Field — "Choose the ground by Family."** Fulguria fails against *"boiled leather, fired clay,
  dense fog and a shielded interior"* — the register names the kit twice, in two different pages, and adds
  that it *"is not a secret; it is simply cheaper to ignore until somebody dies of it."* A working that
  identifies arrivals by reading their spectrum has an obvious problem with an arrival that has been
  chosen for what its spectrum is not.
- **Break the Boundary — "Force the Seal early."** *"The Seal closes an operation against the warrant that
  opened it, and a warrant overstating standing seals anyway, **with the difference deducted from the
  speaker at the moment of closing.** A third party who can compel an early close is not defending. They
  are billing him."* A ward whose standard is the holder's own declared intent is a warrant standing in
  the open for ten to fifteen seconds at a time.
- **Break the Boundary — "Knot."** *"**Knot** entangles a path so it cannot be traced cleanly."* A matched
  filter needs a traceable signature to correlate against. One of the Index's only two Counter forms, and
  it is aimed at exactly the step §1(d) says the whole instrument depends on.
- **What does not work, and the register says so about this page's whole category.** *"**Shielding.**
  Nothing in the register stops a working by standing in front of it. Armour changes what the working does
  to a body. Wards condition what may be authorised in a space. **Neither is a wall and both are written as
  one constantly.**"* The Aether Bastion is the second kind, written as the first, and the page's own
  Function line — *"screens everything crossing it against a fixed standard"* — is the register's
  definition of the second kind, correctly stated, by a page that then prices itself in giganewtons.

**The tell, stated plainly.** It is visible and it is supposed to be. What is worth watching is the rate:
things do not bounce off it, they **decelerate in the last half-metre**, and the deceleration is the same
for a thrown stone and a hammer blow, which is the signature of a test rather than a barrier. Repeat the
same attack and nothing degrades. And watch her mouth rather than the wall — the ward is standing on
something she said, and the moment it stops being true, the wall does not crack. It is simply not there.

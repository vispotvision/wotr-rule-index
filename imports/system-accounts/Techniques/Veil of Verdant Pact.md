# System account · Veil of Verdant Pact

## 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** A wooden dome grows over a group of people in about a second. While it
stands, things that hit it make it thicker instead of making a hole, and whoever raised it feels each of
those things happen to them for an instant before the wood catches up.

**The real phenomenon.** **Indeterminate meristematic growth used as an energy sink**, which is the page's
own claim and is correct botany, plus **allotropy** for the frame, which is the page's own secondary
register and is also correct. The physics that does not close is not the conversion. It is **the
construction**: growing the dome in one second is a gigawatt-scale event, and the energy budget of the
page's own Grade fixes the shell's thickness at about three centimetres.

**(a) The conversion side is sound, and the reason is that plants really do not have a final size.**
Verdantia's Analogue is *"Carbon fixation and indeterminate meristematic growth"*, and the register's
gloss is accurate: *"A plant does not have a final size. Meristem tissue remains embryonic for the
organism's whole life, and photosynthesis converts ambient energy into structure continuously rather than
in bouts."* An organism with no terminal morphology has no state that counts as *finished*, and therefore
no state that an energy input can *damage* rather than *extend*. **That is a real and unusual property,
and it is exactly the property a structure would need to treat an attack as an input.** The page's
Mechanism line states it correctly.

**(b) The construction side, in numbers the page does not have.** Take the page's own geometry: a dome
over a **five-metre radius**, closing in **one second** (`:17`, `:43`).

- A 5 m hemisphere has a surface of **2πr² = 157 m².**
- At a wood density of **600 kg/m³**, a shell of thickness *t* masses **94,200·t kg.**
- The energy cost of building biomass is bounded below by its heat of combustion, about **17.5 MJ/kg** for
  dry wood.

Now impose the page's own Grade. **B-Grade attack output tops out at 4.6024 × 10¹⁰ J** (Part Four), which
is **46.0 GJ**. Spend all of it on wood and you get **46.0 × 10⁹ / 17.5 × 10⁶ = 2,630 kg**, which is
**4.38 m³**, which over 157 m² is a shell **2.8 cm thick.**

**So the dome's own Grade band fixes its thickness at under three centimetres, and its construction is a
46-gigawatt event held for one second** — about fifteen large reactors' thermal output, to grow a garden
trellis (`SA-PHYS-VERDANT-CLOSURE-ENERGY`). Every extra centimetre of shell costs another 16 GJ, and at
10 cm the dome alone is 165 GJ, **3.6 times the B-Grade ceiling** and into A-Grade territory before
anything has hit it.

**(c) And here the page's stated capacity is a genuine pass, which is worth saying.** *"Peak Force 1 to
5 GN, meaning it can absorb a Building-to-Large-Building-level strike"* (`:43`). Spread across 157 m² that
is **6.4–31.8 MPa.** Wood takes **40–60 MPa in compression parallel to the grain** and 5–10 MPa
perpendicular, and the page has already supplied the fix for the weak axis: *"dense mineral bracing on the
inside."* **The load figure and the material are compatible.** (As with every ward in this batch, the
figure is a force where a shell fails at a stress, so a concentrated 5 GN on a square centimetre is
5 × 10¹³ Pa and nothing survives it — but this page, unlike the Bastion's, does not claim to arrest point
loads.)

**(d) The growth rate is not biology, and the page's own registers say which current is doing it.**
**2.8 cm of wood per second is 2.8 × 10⁻² m/s.** The fastest growth ever measured in a plant is bamboo at
about **0.91 m/day = 1.05 × 10⁻⁵ m/s**, and that is *elongation of cells already formed*, not new tissue —
plant cell division runs on a cycle of **sixteen to twenty-four hours.** The dome is **2,700 times** the
record and about **10⁵ times** anything involving mitosis.

That is not a failure of the page; it is a statement about which half of the working is doing the work.
**Verdantia supplies the law that a structure may be extended without limit. It does not supply a rate.**
The rate is Materia Primordia's, whose Analogue is *"**Allotropy.** One element, several lattices,
radically different materials"* — a **re-latticing** of Essence already present rather than a synthesis of
new matter, which is a rearrangement and can be fast. The page says this almost exactly: *"the same Essence
re-latticed as living wood on the outside and dense mineral bracing on the inside"*, and *"the same
Essence, re-latticed"* is the register's own phrase.

**(e) The fire clause is the best-observed sentence on the page and it is real fire engineering.**
*"Fire arrives and the char becomes new bark"* (`:17`). **Heavy timber genuinely survives fire better than
unprotected steel, and the reason is char.** Wood pyrolyses to a carbon layer of very low conductivity
which insulates the sound wood beneath it, and the charring rate is standardised for structural design at
about **0.6–0.8 mm/min** (0.635 mm/min for softwood in the European code). Over the page's **24-second**
duration that is **0.24–0.32 mm** — *three tenths of a millimetre* — and a 2.8 cm shell would take
**35 to 47 minutes** of standard fire exposure to burn through.

**Fire does not beat this dome, and the page says as much and names the thing that does.** *"Exuroth's
dehydrating heat"* (`:19`). **Wood's fire resistance is bought with water**: driving off the moisture is
endothermic and must happen before pyrolysis can proceed, at about **2.26 MJ per kilogram of water**, and
a 2,630 kg shell at 12% moisture content carries **316 kg of it — 714 MJ of pure delay.** Dry the wood
first and the charring rate roughly doubles. **The page's stated weakness is not heat. It is
dehydration**, which is exactly what the page calls it, and the arithmetic says why plain fire is not a
substitute.

**Energy budget, stated.** Construction: **≈46 GJ in one second, 46 GW**, for a 2.8 cm shell at B-Grade's
ceiling. Absorption: **1–5 GN of peak force**, i.e. 6.4–31.8 MPa distributed, converted into further
growth at 17.5 MJ per kilogram added. Where the joules were: the attacker's, for the conversion; for the
construction, unstated — Verdantia's register says *"photosynthesis converts **ambient** energy into
structure"*, which points at the Aether stratum and not at the caster, consistent with *"The practitioner
does not supply the joules. They supply the boundary condition."* Fault class by `Two Sets of Books` §VII:
**Field fault** — *"The room could not supply what the working assumed. Saturation, depletion… The working
is correct and produces nothing"* — and Verdantia's own Failure says the field fault here has an unusual
shape (§2).

## 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** **Vitalia**, *"Split: **Wild** (Growth, Healing) · **Medium** (Water) · **Spirit**
(Decay, Poison)"*, favoured by *"Living biomass, temperate conditions, proximity to blood"*, suppressed by
*"Sterile ground, extreme cold, **Essence-depleted soil**"*. **Materia**, *"**Wild**"*, favoured by
*"Mineral-rich rock, worked stone, deep foundation"*, suppressed by *"Open water, high altitude, moving
platforms"* (`wiki/The Magic System/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings.md`). *"Terrain is not
flavour. It is a modifier on η."*

**Both rows are about the ground and they want the same ground for different reasons** — living soil over
rock. A grove works; a paved courtyard is half of it; a ship, a scaffold or a high crag is neither. And
*"proximity to blood"* on the Vitalia row is quietly grim on a ward that is raised over wounded allies.

**Saturation.** Two harmonizations across two Families, a light load, and no practitioner named to check
it against.

**Wellspring stratum — Verdantia, *The Green Pulse*, Vitalia, Biochemistry.** Core Law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Vitalia — Biochemistry.md`:

> **Analogue** · Carbon fixation and indeterminate meristematic growth.
> **Mechanism** · A plant does not have a final size. Meristem tissue remains embryonic for the organism's
> whole life, and photosynthesis converts ambient energy into structure continuously rather than in bouts.
> Verdantia gives the Crystal indeterminate growth and continuous ambient conversion, which is why its
> practitioners recover while doing nothing and why they are difficult to exhaust in a long engagement.
> **Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Regeneration, Tempering Yield, Harmonics Synergy. Passive Essence
> recovery accelerates. The character heals by growing rather than repairing.
> **Failure** · **Etiolation.** Deprived of ambient input the growth continues anyway, producing length
> without substance until the structure cannot hold itself up.

***"Etiolation" is the finest single word available to this page and it is not on it.*** The obvious
counter to a working that feeds on ambient input is to take the input away, and the register says **that
does not stop it.** Deprived, **the growth continues anyway** — longer, thinner, weaker, until it cannot
carry its own weight. This is real and observable: a seedling in the dark elongates fast, produces long
pale internodes and thin walls, and falls over. So a dome raised on *"Essence-depleted soil"* does not
fail to appear. **It appears larger and it cannot hold.** That is a much better counter than denial,
because it looks, for the first second, like success.

**Wellspring stratum — Materia Primordia, *The First Substance*, Materia, Material Science.** Core Law,
verbatim:

> **Analogue** · Allotropy. One element, several lattices, radically different materials.
> **Mechanism** · Graphite and diamond are the same element. The difference is entirely in how the atoms
> are arranged, and it is the difference between a lubricant and the hardest natural substance known.
> Materia Primordia grants the Crystal access to its own polymorphism: **the same Essence, re-latticed**,
> expressing properties that appear to belong to different substances entirely.
> **Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Ossification, Dominion Density. The body and the Domain gain
> mineral-weight authority.
> **Failure** · **Phase locking.** Under sufficient pressure the lattice commits to one allotrope
> permanently, and the practitioner keeps whichever form they were wearing when the pressure came.

**The page derives its frame from this correctly and does not carry its price.** *"The same Essence
re-latticed as living wood on the outside and dense mineral bracing on the inside"* is the register's
phrase used exactly as the register means it. But **Phase locking is a permanent consequence and this
working is defined by taking heavy loads while in an altered lattice.** *"Under sufficient pressure the
lattice commits to one allotrope permanently, and the practitioner keeps whichever form they were wearing
when the pressure came."* The page's Numerical Effect has the dome absorbing up to **5 GN**. If that is
not *"sufficient pressure"*, nothing is (`SA-CROSS-VERDANT-PHASE-LOCKING`).

**And the page is mystified by its own Codex line.** *"**What nobody knows** · Whether the dome draws on
Verdantia's law alone, or on something older that Verdantia only resembles, since ancient **Materia
Primordia** mineral seams have turned up threaded through its root system on every occasion anyone has
checked"* (`:21`). Materia Primordia is the page's own declared secondary Wellspring, named three lines
below, and the mineral bracing is the page's own stated Mechanism. **The seams are not an anomaly. They
are the working** (`SA-CROSS-VERDANT-OWN-MYSTERY`). What is genuinely open underneath the question is
better than the question: Materia Primordia is *"The First Substance"*, and a ward whose frame is the
first substance *is* older than the green that grows on it — which is a real thing to be uncertain about
and is not what the page asks.

**Which of the Four Theories.** **Debt**, and the page uses the word. *"Every unit of force the dome
converts is logged against the caster's own capacity to heal **the way a debt is logged against a
ledger**"* (`:41`). The working does not absorb damage; it **defers and reassigns** it, and the ledger it
is written on is a person. **Residue** runs underneath, because the growth is the record.

**Essence stratum.** Crystal layer: the **Attraction Layer**, projected outward as a domain of grown
matter rather than worn — the page's Category is *"Domain Weaving (Attraction)"*. Developmental Tier
**Harmonic** (Stage V, Part Twenty-One). Tier of Standing **5 · Expert**, η band 0.50–0.60; no practitioner
named, so nothing to check. Crystal State `null`. **The invoice:** a body used as the routing point for
every injury the structure takes — *"a burn absorbed by the dome is a burn the caster feels before it
becomes bark"* — and a ledger against future healing.

**The lens: Hildegard's *viriditas*, and the dryness that is its opposite.** Hildegard of Bingen,
*Scivias* (1151), *Liber divinorum operum* (1174) and the *Physica*. Her central term is a word she had to
coin a usage for: ***viriditas***, **greenness** — not a colour but a **force**, the moist vital power
flowing from God through every living thing, which makes a plant grow, a wound close and a person be well.
Its opposite is ***ariditas***, **dryness**: not damage but *desiccation*, the withdrawal of the greening
power, which she diagnoses in the same breath as a medical condition and a spiritual one. Health, for
Hildegard, is a **flow** you are standing in. Sickness is what is left when it stops.

**The Veil of Verdant Pact is that doctrine built as a shelter**, down to the vocabulary: Verdantia is
*The Green Pulse*, the dome converts injury into growth rather than resisting it, and the page's stated
Limit is — precisely and without knowing it — *ariditas*. *"**Anti-growth law overrides growth law.**
Exuroth's dehydrating heat and Nihiloth's null field are not damage the dome can convert; **they are the
absence of the conditions growth needs**"* (`:19`). Hildegard would recognise that sentence. She wrote the
same one about a sick man.

**The productive misreading, and what it kills.** Read *viriditas* as a **quantity** rather than a flow —
as something a body can accumulate, bank and spend — and a doctrine follows that is generous, humane and
wrong. If greenness is stock, then every injury fed into a growing structure **increases** the stock, and
the correct practice is to stay inside the dome, hold it longer, take more, and come out greener than you
went in. A school builds around that: they raise the Veil early and keep it, they treat casualties by
bringing them inside rather than by carrying them out, and their measure of a good pactbearer is how much
she has absorbed.

**Two lines kill them and the second one kills the practice rather than the practitioner.** The first is
the page's own: *"Every unit of force the dome converts is logged against the caster's own capacity to
heal the way a debt is logged against a ledger."* **A flow routed through a person is not a stock the
person keeps.** The pactbearer is not accumulating greenness; she is the pipe, and the ledger is written
against her.

The second is the counter the page lists last and explains least: *"heavy-metal toxins outright weaken the
roots rather than feeding them"* (`:45`). **That is real and its mechanism is the school's own doctrine
turned inside out.** Plants take up cadmium, lead and thallium through the **transporters meant for zinc,
iron and manganese** — the ZIP, NRAMP and IRT families cannot tell the ions apart, because they select by
charge and radius and the toxic ones are the right size. **The uptake machinery is the poisoning
mechanism.** A school whose entire method is *take more in* has built its practice on the one step that
cannot discriminate, and what finally comes for them is not an attack the dome failed to convert. It is
an attack the dome converted perfectly, into itself, forever.

## 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**The glyph, proposed.** `[Ser]` **Renewal** (Root · Wyther) for the growth law and `[Frm]` **Form**
(Extension · Thalen) for the re-latticing — both from the thirty mirrored entries, both filings under the
Index's Standing Note (*"Assignment is decided after the phenomenon, never before, and it is a filing
decision rather than a generative one"*), neither canon until ruled (`SA-GAP-GLYPH-MIRROR`).

**What boundary moves, and there are two.** The first is **the terminal morphology of a growing
structure** — the size at which growth stops. Most organisms have one; the boundary condition removes it,
and the register's law then applies without further instruction: *"A plant does not have a final size."*
Nothing is added and nothing is pushed. **A limit is taken away, and growth is what happens in its
absence.**

The second is **which lattice a given quantity of Essence is permitted to occupy.** Graphite and diamond
are the same atoms; what separates them is a boundary in configuration space, not in substance. Materia
Primordia's condition moves that boundary, and the same Essence expresses as wood at the surface and as
mineral at the bracing. **This is why the dome can close in a second while nothing biological grows in
less than a day** (§1d): re-latticing is a rearrangement, and rearrangements are not gated by mitosis.

**What the law then does on its own.** It grows, and it does not consult anyone about when to stop,
because the boundary that would have told it to stop is the one that was removed. The structure closes
over the five metres at a rim speed of **five metres per second** — and that number is worth holding onto,
because it is the whole of the page's stated Limitation (§5).

**Then the conversion, which is the part that requires no separate mechanism at all.** An attack arriving
at the dome delivers energy into a structure that has no finished state. In anything with a terminal
morphology, energy delivered above the elastic limit is *damage*, because damage is precisely the
difference between the shape the thing is and the shape it was supposed to have. **An indeterminate
structure has no supposed-to-have**, so the same energy is an input, and the only thing left for it to do
is what the law already does with inputs: extend. *"Fire arrives and the char becomes new bark"* is a
correct account of pyrolysis at a surface that is still growing beneath it (§1e). *"Frost arrives and the
numbness becomes sap-slow warmth"* is the same sentence about a thermal input.

**The routing through the caster, which is the price and not an accident.** A conversion of this kind is
not instantaneous — the wood must actually be laid down — and something has to hold the injury for the
interval in between. The page is explicit that the holding place is a person: *"The ward routes injury
through the caster's own body **before** it routes it into growth."* **That is a latency, and latency
requires storage.** For the second it takes, the burn is somebody's.

**The failure modes, and three of the four are in the registers rather than on the page.** **Etiolation**,
Verdantia's, which is what happens instead of failure when the field is thin: longer, thinner, and it
falls over. **Phase locking**, Materia Primordia's, which is what a heavy enough strike does to the
practitioner's own lattice permanently. **Desiccation**, which is the page's own stated Limit and the one
the water arithmetic explains (§1e). And **the closure lag**, which is the only one the page names as a
limit and is the only one an ordinary opponent can use.

**What the target sees and feels.** There is a sound first and it is not a magical sound — a sustained
creaking tear, wood under load, from everywhere at once, because that is what a structure putting on
several tonnes of mass in a second does to itself. The ground opens. Growth comes up and over at a
walking-to-running pace, meeting overhead, and the light inside goes green and then dim.

**An attacker who was already committed arrives inside it**, because five metres in one second is exactly
a sprinting man, and the page's Limitation is a statement about that coincidence.

Hitting it is the strange part. The impact point does not crater, splinter or spall. It **thickens** — the
wood at the contact swells and darkens over the next half-second, which reads at first as the dome
flinching and is in fact the dome eating. Fire produces a black crust that stops spreading. Poison
produces a smell the page gets exactly right: *"growth-hormone runoff that smells faintly of sap"*.
Nothing you do to it makes it worse, and a soldier's instinct — hit the same place twice — is precisely
wrong here, because the second blow lands on the thickest part of the structure.

**And the tell that matters is not on the dome.** Whoever raised it is standing inside it flinching at
things that are not touching her, once per impact, a half-beat after each one. *"A burn absorbed by the
dome is a burn the caster feels before it becomes bark."* The dome does not show you where it is hurt. **She
does.**

## 5 · Counterplay and the challenge

**The fairness check.**

| Test | Verdict | Why |

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).

- **Deny the Field — "Choose the ground by Family," and the result is not what denial usually is.** Vitalia
  is suppressed by *"Sterile ground, extreme cold, **Essence-depleted soil**"*. Read that against
  Verdantia's **Etiolation** and the counter changes character entirely: on depleted ground the working
  does not fail to fire, **it succeeds badly** — larger, thinner, and structurally incapable, with the
  caster inside it believing it worked. It is the only counter in this batch that is more dangerous to the
  practitioner than a simple denial would be.
- **Deny the Field — "Cut the main," which is the same move made administratively.** *"In any district on
  the network this is the first move and the cheapest… **The practitioner is not weakened; the room is.**"*
  Verdantia's whole law is *"continuous ambient conversion"*. A room with nothing ambient in it is a room
  where this ward etiolates.
- **Break the Body — "Numbers, correctly used," and the closure lag is what they are for.** *"Twenty men
  who have been drilled to spread, to make him choose, and to keep a line of fire open at all times will
  take casualties and kill him."* The dome closes at 5 m/s over one second and covers five metres. **A line
  that is already inside five metres when it goes up is inside it**, and the page says so: *"anything
  already past the five-metre line before that second ends was never inside the ward."*
- **Break the Boundary — "Interrupt the chain," available because of the Stage.** Stage V is below
  Refraction, and this page names its anchor: *"aloud or through a fixed gesture chain."* The register:
  *"**Break his wrist and a gestured chain is finished. Put a hand over his mouth and a spoken one is.**"*
  A ward with a one-second build time is a ward with a one-second window on its anchor.
- **Break the Man — "Rigidity," and the mechanic is the caster's own reflex.** *"Present him with the
  situation his own law handles worst and he will handle it that way regardless."* The dome routes every
  absorbed injury through the caster first. **So the way to hurt the person who raised it is to hit the
  thing she raised**, repeatedly, with things she will feel — and her law does not let her decline the
  routing, because the routing is the working.
- **What does not work here, and it is the intuitive move.** Hitting the same place twice. The register's
  general rule is the opposite — *"Ceramic and shard are sacrificial and fail on the second hit in the
  same place"* — and that rule is about brittle materials. **This one grows where it is struck**, so the
  second blow lands on the thickest part of the structure. A soldier's trained instinct is the wrong
  instinct against this ward and right against almost every other one in the register.

**The tell, stated plainly.** Several tonnes of wood arriving in one second makes the noise several tonnes
of wood would make, and the light under it goes green and then poor. After that, watch the person who
raised it rather than the thing she raised: she flinches once per impact, half a beat late, at nothing
anyone can see. And if the ground is thin, watch the dome's proportions — a Veil that comes up taller and
thinner than it should is not a stronger one.

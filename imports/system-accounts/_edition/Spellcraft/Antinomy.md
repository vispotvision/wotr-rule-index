## Physics
**Phenomenon** · A tuned mass damper, a second oscillator matched to a structure's problem mode: the structure keeps moving in every way except the one it was built for. The function runs; its characteristic mode never arrives.
**Law** · Resonant coupling with antiresonance: at the damper's tuned frequency the structure's response has a zero, a notch, so that mode is cancelled rather than reduced.
**Limit** · Tuning tolerance: a few percent of mistuning loses most of the notch, one damper handles one mode, and a drifting frequency cannot be tracked.
**Absorber** · A damper absorbs, it does not block: the cancelled energy goes into the damper, which always swings wider than the structure.
**Antiphase** · Two weakly coupled oscillators settle into moving opposite each other, as two clocks on one beam do; the practitioner does not push, he makes that mode the one the pair prefers.

## Mechanism
**Glyph** · Equivalence, whose worked form is Balance, laid across Knot: Equivalence supplies the matching, Knot the function that still runs and no longer arrives.
**Trigger** · A two-finger gesture unfolding seven rings of thread around the target, on direct contact or a locked line of sight; no chant, sigil or inscription, and it holds only on attention.
**Boundary** · One phase relation at one mode in a two-body system: the pair prefers antiphase at the frequency of the target's dominant function, and nothing else about either is touched.
**Effect** · The technique fires and the payload arrives null or inverted: fire gives ash, a blessing stabilises a wound without closing it, a blade carries hesitation, courage never reaches the muscle. Nothing is taken; the one thing they are for does not work.
**Lock** · The damper stays tuned: the same function fails on every repeat against the same caster, and each attempt compounds the backlash the target feels.
**Anchor** · None needed at Dissonance; the gesture is a tell, and only breaking his attention ends it.
**Failure** · The shared mode: when the target's situation mirrors the caster's own unresolved contradiction, two matched resonators amplify instead of notching, and the working turns on the caster. It fires only on that match.
**Bleed** · 10 to 15 percent of what he spends to set and hold the Lock leaves his Shell as heat; the absorbed transit is not expression and does not pass through η, it banks.

## Counterplay

**Limits** · One mode, one target, while attention holds; it needs a stable identity and a dominant function; it takes nothing and deals no damage of its own.
**Beats it** · Name your contradiction aloud; be two co-equal things; send something with no identity; be recognised by someone who knew you; present the shape of his own failure; break contact or his attention; keep swinging to overfill him.

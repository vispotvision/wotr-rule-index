## Physics
**Phenomenon** · Metastability: a region is set in a state that holds but is not the lowest it could fall to. It is truly stable until a seed of the real state forms, then it falls all at once.
**Law** · Nucleation, the first seed of the true state forming: decay rate goes as exp(−ΔG‡/kT), so the lie's lifetime grows exponentially with the barrier the working buys.
**Limit** · Nothing inside the false state can detect it; only an outside, absolute standard finds the false floor, and one seed site of the right shape ends it.
**Uphill** · The practitioner pays the whole gap between true and false floor, and the gap grows with what rests on the clause: the cost follows its consequence, not its content.
**Collapse** · Once seeded, the edge of the true state spreads by itself, like superheated water flashing to steam; the one who seeded it cannot stop it.
**Damage** · Whatever set while the lie held is real and stays; nothing heals when it ends.

| Quantity | Working | Result |

| Energy to hold it | insulation from outside comparison | small against the offset |
| Lifetime | exponential in the barrier | superheated water: minutes in a smooth vessel, none in a scratched one |
| Energy released at collapse | offset plus built strain | more than the working paid |

## Metaphysics
**Aether** · The offset is paid once at filing, scaled to the clause's load; the hold costs only insulation, so it looks effortless. The Residue is corruption of everything near the clause, a scar the Continuum keeps (Statute XXVI, Mechanica Irreversibility).
**Wellspring** · Nihiloth, the Hollow Law (Limina): it adds no law but cancels the one already there, so a false clause meets no rival; it strengthens Sovereignty, Persistence and Cognition.
**Wellspring** · Dissolution, the Unbinding Stream (Limina): the mixed state has more disorder than the separated one, so the shattering sustains itself once begun.
**Wellspring** · Mirithane, the Wellspring of Reflection (Limina): the clause is filed in true grammar, a faithful rendering of a false statement, which makes it unreadable from inside.
**Inherited failures** · Nihiloth cancels everything brought inside, the practitioner included; Dissolution's indiscriminate solvency corrupts all near the clause; Mirithane's mirror broadcasts perfectly any state the practitioner would rather hide.
**Category** · Mechanica: forced change with no Wellspring asked, no consent, no correspondence and no debt.
**Essence** · The Attraction Layer pays: every path near the clause bends to fit it, the practitioner's own included. Developmental Tier is Harmonic at Glory, Radiant at Transcendence, Sovereign at Dissonance.
**School** · The scaffold schools teach it as a useful fiction to be taken down after the work; but a triggered collapse is a Phenomenon with no author, so their planned withdrawal never comes and it kills them inside the structure.

## Mechanism
**Glyph** · Truth laid under Mirage: Truth sets what counts as a correctly filed statement, and Mirage makes the false clause impossible to tell from a true one.
**Boundary** · The set of statements a reader compares against moves, so the false clause falls inside it and the true one outside, for a Wellspring, a Crystal's alignment or a running Draft alike.
**Effect** · Paths bend to fit the clause, Crystals re-read against it, Drafts run on it; nobody is fooled and nobody consents. Hard paths run easy, doubtful alignments read clean, refused Drafts authorise.
**Hold** · Below Refraction it is spoken, then held by attention; from Refraction up, attention alone holds it.
**Ending** · A true outside comparison, a load the insulation cannot shield, or a lapse of attention drops the region to the real floor; once collapse starts even the author cannot recall it and is caught.
**Aftermath** · What was built still stands on a floor at a different height, paths bent toward a clause that no longer exists; a structure deleted from under a correct decision.
**Failure** · Symmetric boundary fault: the false floor belongs to the region, so a Fallacy about a law the practitioner's own path runs through is a Fallacy about the practitioner.
**Downstream** · Coherence fault: every structure fitted to the clause obeys a law about to vanish; tethered Animatria learn their continuity was false only when it goes. The Ritus Orders ban the working for this.
**Bleed** · The region stays quiet while waste leaves the practitioner's Shell as heat, sound and light: 30 to 40 percent of the offset at Glory, 20 to 30 at Transcendence, 10 to 15 at Dissonance.

## Counterplay
**Tell** · The region is too quiet: laws that should report are silent, paths that should strain do not. Beside it a practitioner sheds heat and holds very still; afterwards, structures at the wrong height and a lasting scar.
**Limits** · No Fallacy is permanent; an instrument calibrated elsewhere sees it, and once one arrives the working has seconds.
**Fails against** · A Lock on the clause beforehand (Knot is weaker); a forced Seal, which bills the whole offset; Judicium's outside standard; Oblivara guarding the subject; a hold that must last, which drains the practitioner turn by turn.
**Seal** · A false axiom is a warrant overstating standing by construction, so an early close deducts the whole offset from the speaker; the larger the lie, the larger the bill.
**Judicium** · Every element shows only its own spectrum, so a reader names a lie by its signature; the price is spectral overload, a minute of uselessness.
**Oblivara** · A redundantly encoded identity rebuilds its alignment from its own copies; past the correction limit the rebuild is confident, complete and wrong.
**Silence** · No safe harbour: Silence stops authorisation, and Fallacy never asks for it.

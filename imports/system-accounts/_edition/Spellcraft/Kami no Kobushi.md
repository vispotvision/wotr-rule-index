## Physics
**Phenomenon** · Effective mass: a blow carries the mass of everything coupled to the fist at contact, so a rooted stance hits far harder than an arm alone, and a body coupled to something immovable hits with that thing's mass.
**Law** · Conservation of momentum with effective mass set by the coupling, and impedance matching at the interface deciding how much energy crosses and how much reflects.
**Limit** · Nothing has infinite mass: effective mass is bounded by what the coupling carries before it fails, so everything that beats an Anchor beats the coupling, not the man.
**Anchor** · A body that does not accelerate returns the whole applied force: an attacker striking a perfectly anchored defender receives their own blow.
**Verdict** · Transmission is fixed by what the two bodies are before contact, so the exchange is decided before it lands; speed is not in the equation.
**Grain** · Everything fails at a pre-existing flaw (Griffith's criterion), so a strike that finds the oldest flaw need only load it, not supply the failure.

## Mechanism
**Glyph** · Order, for the verdict before the blow, laid under Boundary, for the coordinate that will not move.
**Boundary** · What the practitioner's position is a property of: the coordinate is pinned to something other than the floor, so the coupling is a shared coordinate, not a contact.
**Effect** · Pushed, the push comes back entire; struck from, the blow carries the coupling's mass. The Ruin Fist brings a structure's oldest failure early; the Purity Fist returns a body or ward to its native shape and strips borrowed identity on Basilithe ground.
**Forms** · The Spirit Fist strikes the gradient between life and death and denies a clean escape; the Sequence Fist is correct before contact; the Revelation Fist hits what an opponent is, not what they borrow; the Balance Fist seats at the fulcrum; Totality anchors all six.
**Floor** · No Stage floor: it is a bloodline and personal art, and the lineage decides who can use it. Each bearer's figures come off their own card.

**Bleed** · Waste leaves the Shell as heat and goes down as a thud through the ground, 20 to 30 percent at Master; coupled to the Plane of Fate, η reaches 0.94 and the Plane reclaims most of the rest.

## Counterplay
**Tell** · The Anchor has chosen ground and stopped: a weapon butt driven in, palms flat to the earth, ground taking a weight it did not have. After, the ground remembers the coordinate.
**Limits** · The coupling must be set before contact; it holds against displacement only up to its Continuity Grade; the largest Works run fewer than four to a reserve.
**Beats it** · Pull, lift, hook or drag it in tension; fight it in a confined space so the blast returns; sever the coupling with displacement above its Continuity, a Silence, or ground that will not hold; wait for Obsession; make it spend.

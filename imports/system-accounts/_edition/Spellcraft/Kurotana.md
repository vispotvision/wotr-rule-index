## Physics
**Phenomenon** · Directional solidification to a single crystal, as a turbine blade is regrown from one seed: no internal boundaries, huge resistance to slow deformation, and nothing at all to stop a crack.
**Law** · Grain-boundary strengthening run out of boundaries, with cleavage on a single plane as the failure; hysteresis carries the endurance and conservation of total head sets the price.
**Limit** · Grain boundaries are crack arresters: a single crystal's crack runs one plane at near the Rayleigh wave speed, with no stage in which anything can be done.
**Endurance** · An absorbing surface keeps what strikes it as internal load, carried loss, not replaced loss; past its limit the load goes into the structure.
**Price** · A sacrifice is a constriction: fluid trades pressure for speed and the total is unchanged, so the bearer gains nothing by it, only converts one term into another.

**Wellspring** · Monolithion, the Enduring Monument (Materia): it drives the Crystal to the single-crystal condition, a presence more like terrain than a person, and at completion the standing structure of the crow Domain.
**Wellspring** · Vantabriel, the Wellspring of Night (Limina): what strikes the vessel is kept, not reflected.
**Wellspring** · Nihiloth, the Hollow Law (Limina): its Domain cancels law rather than adding any, removing the futures a target's choices no longer earn.
**Wellspring** · Oblation, the Offering Flame (Fluxia): soul-cost as a trade in which the total is never increased; it prices the Three Witnesses, and Kurotana is the corrupted mirror of Moetana.

**Essence** · All three layers are rebuilt: the Witness of Flesh (the body) is the Shell, of Love (a bond that still saw a human) the Attraction Layer, of Name (its old meaning) the Essence Core. Developmental Tier is Sovereign.

## Mechanism
**Glyph** · Form, for the regrown vessel, laid under Return, for the three surrenders that cannot be given back again.
**Boundary** · The number of internal interfaces the Crystal may have is pinned at one; whatever did not grow from the surviving seed is simply not part of the new structure.
**Effect** · A vessel that takes hits without returning them or seeming diminished, and repairs itself while its self-design stays coherent.
**Powers** · Soul Drafting revises a soul at a conserved cost (courage for gentleness); Consequence Architecture feeds events toward one outcome; Name Revision changes the office a soul holds; the Black Mandate issues short fate-commands, dearer as they grow abstract.
**Domain** · Murder of Crows, Cathedral of Unchosen Futures: a crow marks each significant choice, and a poorly chosen path grows heavier and narrower as it takes flight; the crow marks a removed future, it does not predict.
**Floor** · Dissonance: rebuilding three layers and leaving a Domain needs a bearer who generates local conditions, and the rite is a fracture turned into architecture. No anchor is needed.
**Failure** · Cleavage: a clean, unmixed reminder of the one it replaced splits the vessel in microseconds; a reminder muddied with hatred or bargaining is blocked. Saturation and cavitation start the same failure from inside.
**Bleed** · 10 to 15 percent of every spend is heat at the Shell, but Vantabriel keeps it rather than radiating it: the vessel runs cold and quiet, and the heat banks with what it absorbs.

**Cost** · The rite costs 90 percent of full reserve once and leaves the bearer at the floor; the vessel is free, and every power, the Domain included, is billed per use as a share of full reserve.
**Duration** · The vessel is permanent; the Domain lasts while paid, up to 18 turns from full.
**Saturation** · Absorbed energy refills the vessel below its maximum, so striking it feeds it; past the maximum every EU is load in a lattice with no crack arrester.
**Contest** · Drafting or Revision against an unwilling soul sets Harmonics Attunement against Harmonics Stability; a winner is untouched and the cost is spent regardless.

## Counterplay
**Tell** · A figure standing like a rock in a field, taking and not returning, running cold where others run warm; crows at the edge of sight inside the Domain. After, drafted souls carry its Residue.
**Limits** · It cannot restore what it spent; the Witness of Love is gone for good; it cannot survive a clean reminder.
**Fails against** · A clean reminder (the old name, forgiveness, a refused role), chosen knowing it kills; overfill under sustained load; the days after the rite; Silence, which strips the Domain but not the vessel. Weapons rarely end it.

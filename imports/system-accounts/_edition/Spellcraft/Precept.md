## Physics
**Phenomenon** · A constraint, not a force: like a bead on a wire, a region loses a possible shape, and whatever tries to take that shape finds no such shape exists there. Nothing resists; the option is simply gone.
**Law** · Constraint forces do zero virtual work, Σ Rᵢ · δrᵢ = 0; band structure in a periodic medium makes a forbidden band where no mode exists, and a single defect opens exactly one allowed state inside it.
**Limit** · A band gap has edges: it forbids one range and is transparent everywhere else, so a Precept compels nothing outside the clause it names.
**Sharpness** · A gap's sharpness grows exponentially with the number of regular periods, so a Precept is as sharp as its clause was regularly laid, not as strong as its author.
**Upkeep** · Zero, by theorem; the whole cost sits in the substrate, paid once, and a substrate can be chiselled.

## Metaphysics
**Aether** · One draw at sealing, then none. The inscription is the Residue, located and touchable; with no continuous deposit it can be filed where people must live, which is why guilds use it for codes.
**Wellspring** · Fixatio, the Binding Flame (Materia): a cured thermoset chars before it flows; what it holds stays held because the alternative is no longer reachable. It strengthens Vitality Fortitude and Resilience Anchoring.
**Wellspring** · Judicium, the Wellspring of Truth (Fulguria): no substance can present another's spectrum; Fixatio sets the gap, Judicium sets its edges, which is the whole craft of drafting.
**Inherited failures** · Fixatio's irreversibility makes a clause laid too wide permanent and free to keep being wrong; Judicium's spectral overload is a badly drafted Precept that catches everything.
**Consent** · A Precept over a sphere needs no consent; one over a named person, a geas, is void without it, and Rescind exists as its remedy.
**Essence** · Over a sphere no layer of the subject is touched, since the working is in the substrate; a soul-bound Precept sits in the Essence Core as a Trait. Developmental Tier is Harmonic at Glory.
**School** · The Accord advocates read a Precept as a norm authorised by higher norms and litigate upward against it for generations; a band gap has no superior to appeal to, and they are beaten by a mason who knows where to cut the notch.

## Mechanism
**Craft** · Runecraft: fixed in a substrate, degrading as any inscription degrades, and beaten through the substrate rather than by interrupting a chain.
**Glyph** · Boundary, the limit that moves, laid under Order, the register the clause is filed in; Lock is the same shape used as a reply.
**Boundary** · The set of configurations reachable inside a named sphere: not strength or willingness, only what is available.
**Effect** · A working that would take the forbidden shape completes in the nearest available one: an oath sworn in the terms left open, a rank claimed in the permitted form. The mark is a shape in everything downstream, for generations.
**Floor** · Rises with breadth: a named party or a room needs A-Grade authority at Glory, a guild's code S at Transcendence, a city's or a dynasty's law SS at Realization. The substrate is always the anchor.
**Cannot** · Compel outside its clause, catch a working that reads its shape before sealing, be argued away by force, or grant rule (that is Coronation).
**Failure** · The clause edge misplaced: a century of correct rulings nobody wanted. Institutional drift: a house runs a stated law its lived behaviour left long ago, readable in the archive.
**Bleed** · 30 to 40 percent of the sealing spend at Glory, 20 to 30 at Master, leaves the Shell as heat and structural bleed into substrate and room, once; the standing bleed is zero.

## Counterplay
**Tell** · None in the moment: no cast, light or pressure, and nobody feels stopped. The aftermath is documentary: an inscription somewhere and a sphere where one kind of working has come out the same odd shape for too long.
**Limits** · Only what the clause names; nothing that reads it before sealing; nothing past its edges.
**Beats it** · Chisel, scrape or topple the substrate; write one precise exception in the same register; Rescind an unconsented soul-bound Precept; countersign or speak back the sum where old Seals allow; read the clause as drafted; press the drift.

## Physics
**Phenomenon** · Progressive collapse: a structure's members are taken out of the load path one at a time, the rest take up their load, and when one is overloaded the whole thing comes down with nothing having struck it.
**Law** · Static equilibrium and load redistribution: forces at every node sum to zero, so a load removed from one member appears at once in the others; the Griffith criterion (cracks grow from existing flaws) decides which fails first.
**Limit** · Redundancy caps it: a coordinate with n independent load paths survives the loss of n − 1; a single chain of deposits survives none.
**Thorn** · Nothing fails at its theoretical strength; stress concentrates at a crack tip as the square root of the crack's length, and an unpaid debt is that flaw in an otherwise sound member.
**Survey** · Finding the load-bearing member is the hard part; raising a field over the whole ledger is the survey, and a sight that names the right deposit (the Shingan's Tengan or Kamigan branch) needs only one rose.
**Collapse** · The survey is slow and paid rose by rose; once the right member goes, the fall runs at the speed of redistribution and completes in seconds.
**Energy** · The joules sit in the structure as potential for as long as it has stood; nothing is delivered or broken, a member is unloaded and redistribution does the rest.
**Not** · It is not erasure: the past stays true and intact, only the target's present claim on it is diluted. It is not theft: the practitioner pays.

| Quantity | Working | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Independent load paths | n paths, one withdrawal each | survives n − 1 withdrawals |
| Least useful redundancy | two independent claims | one withdrawal becomes a loss, not a collapse |
| Crack-tip stress | grows as √(crack length) | the oldest unpaid debt loads first |
| Ledger length | series deposits vs parallel deposits | a long chain falls easier; parallel claims fall one by one |

## Mechanism
**Glyph** · Measure (Urion) for the survey, laid under Return (Auren) for the withdrawal: what was lent is called in.
**Boundary** · Whether a past deposit must answer to its present holder moves, deposit by deposit across a field; whether it happened, or was theirs, does not.
**Effect** · A field of roses rises around the target, each a debt honoured or an oath kept, a thorn on every stem. Nothing touches them; claims simply stop being honoured, then the present coordinate standing on them fails, fast.
**Hold** · Spellcraft held by attention with no material anchor, from Refraction, the Stage where a Domain seed first forms; it lapses the moment the practitioner is killed or Silenced.
**Irreversible** · Mortalis runs one direction at a time, so a withdrawal Monolithion has ratified cannot be reversed, even by its author.
**Failure** · Boundary fault: against a deposit that is not unpaid the law does not run, but the payment still leaves; enough wrongful attempts exhaust the gardener's Oblation reserve, Fractura's self-inspection fires, and his own ledger dilutes.
**Cavitation** · Drawing the medium from the target or the field instead of the practitioner's own reserve turns the sea brackish and the thorns inward on the next exchange: a pitted conduit seen from inside.
**Bleed** · 30 to 40 percent of every rose leaves the Shell as heat, sound and light at the floor, a sixth at Master η 0.84; a warm, lit ground under the roses marks the gardener's position.

## Counterplay
**Tell** · Roses rising around the target, visible from far off and not looking like an attack; a warm, lit gardener in the open; afterwards wilted roses and ground that deafens Harmonists.
**Limits** · It cannot erase a deposit, cannot be paid from anything but the practitioner's reserve, cannot be undone by its author, lapses on death or Silence, and loads only a debt that is genuinely unpaid.
**Fails against** · A target with no unpaid debts; standing on independent claims, a truss not a chain; Silence; the exposed gardener's death or distraction; a target who seems to owe and does not, which draws a wrongful rose.
**Redundancy** · Broad, independent standing survives single withdrawals; a narrow career or a chain of holdings (one lineage, one oath, one patron) falls to a single rose.
**Teaching** · The Work passes only to one who has learned to grieve correctly, which leaves every practitioner a known, unhealed shape to read.

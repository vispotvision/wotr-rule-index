## Physics
**Phenomenon** · Solvation, one thing dissolving into another: two bound things are put where being apart is the cheaper arrangement, then held there, without force, until they come apart by themselves.
**Law** · The second law as a free-energy test, ΔG = ΔH − TΔS < 0, decides whether separation is preferred; nucleation theory (the first seed of the new state forming) decides how long it takes.
**Limit** · The entropy of mixing is two hundred times too weak to break a covalent bond, so cross-linked bonds refuse; the separation runs only where heat of bonding does the work, as in ionic and van der Waals bonds.
**Nucleation** · A supersaturated solution holds its excess for hours and drops it in seconds once a seed site appears; the rite supplies the seed site, not the separation.
**Induction** · Nucleation rate goes as exp(−ΔG‡/kT), so waiting time is exponential in the barrier: the six to twelve minutes and their factor-of-two spread are an induction time; a much higher barrier never separates at all.
**Completion** · Past the barrier, separation is fast and cannot be stopped; before it, it can be declined. Nothing is destroyed: one thing becomes two, both intact.
**Energy** · The free energy released was already stored in the bound arrangement; the rite pays only for the barrier, which is why a rite of this consequence costs minutes and a twentieth of a reserve, not a war.

## Metaphysics
**Aether** · Sustained draw over six to twelve minutes in a fixed circle. The Residue is a worked site that deafens Harmonists afterwards, and the Concord's casework on it runs three centuries.
**Saturation** · The rite deposits into one small volume and cannot be moved once begun; past the saturation threshold it risks a Crystal Fracture Event in everyone present, patient included. The Space step keeps the chamber below it.
**Wellspring** · Dissolution, the Unbinding Stream (Limina): the mixed state has more disorder than the separated one, so shedding what no longer serves is nearly effortless; it strengthens Resilience Severance and Tempering Processing.
**Reads** · Judicium (Fulguria) reads composition from signature and locates the interface; Fixatio (Materia) sets the barrier height, both reference and weakness; Anamnesis (Limina) supplies what the bond used to be.
**Inherited failures** · Dissolution's indiscriminate solvency is the rite's Consequence: entropy does not know which memories were load-bearing, so the patient comes back fragmentary, amnesiac or resonant-altered more often than not. Opponents can induce it.
**Theory** · The Consent Doctrine, enforced and literal: someone with standing invokes the rite and the Wellspring is asked only to recognise a lawful condition; a process can be licensed, and Scission is.
**Essence** · All three layers: the Essence Core is cut from, the Attraction Layer holds the bonds and harmonisations a Wellspring-from-host cut works on, and the Aether Shell takes the backlash through Resilience Ward. Developmental Tier at the floor is Harmonic.
**School** · A guild school reads the rite as a liturgy of Strife, the force that unmixes; expecting a complete cut to yield two clean parts, it treats a fragmentary outcome by cutting again harder, which is how mass severance is reached.

## Mechanism
**Glyph** · Edge (Thalen) for the interface, laid under Measure (Urion) for the diagnosis that locates it; the same pair serves against four laws: Trait from soul, Essence from body, memory from identity, Wellspring from host.
**Boundary** · Not the bond: the free energy of the bound state against the separated one moves, at one interface, and a seed site makes the cheaper state reachable.
**Steps** · Intention names the cut; Space holds the chamber below saturation; Circle is the vessel's wall; Offering is a reagent; Invocation states the condition; Catalyst is the seed site; Seal closes against the warrant that opened it.
**Effect** · No pain: the patient lies in a circle while others work and feels a structural absence, the wrong kind of quiet. The more load-bearing half destabilises first, so the patient learns which of the two they were.
**Floor** · Glory, A-Grade: below Refraction a working needs an external anchor, and Scission carries more than any other; it cannot be improvised mid-combat, and a missing step fails the rite outright.
**Ceiling** · A Glory rite severs bonds up to Glory density.
**Failure** · Indiscriminate solvency: whatever else crosses the interface becomes cheaper apart too; some cuts leave a Trait Echo and some nothing, and neither Stage decides which.
**Backlash** · A forced cut lands first on the caster's Resilience Ward; against a Fixatio-anchored bond forcing multiplies it, since the caster pays the factor of two hundred personally.
**Bleed** · 30 to 40 percent of the rite leaves the Shell as heat, sound and light, 17 to 67 MW sustained into a fixed circle: a Scission chamber is hot, loud and lit for the length of the rite.

## Counterplay
**Tell** · Several people, a circle, an offering, a catalyst, a spoken invocation and a chamber running tens of megawatts hot; it cannot be done to someone who does not know. Afterwards a deafening worked site and a patient gone quiet.
**Limits** · No improvising mid-combat; the full apparatus or outright failure; six to twelve minutes; no bond denser than Glory; the removed piece rarely returns whole.
**Beats it** · Anchor the bond with Fixatio beforehand (permanent, even if placed in error); scuff the circle, take the catalyst or break the invocation; refuse the condition before the Seal; countersign the Seal; Silence the chamber.
**Casework** · A documented rite, not a self-derived technique: anyone who has read the Concord's casework can counter it, and it surprises nobody.
**Silence** · A Silence Realm forbids lawful transformation, so no rite inside one; a patient who wants a corrupted Wellspring cut out cannot have it done there either, which makes chamber locations worth fighting over.

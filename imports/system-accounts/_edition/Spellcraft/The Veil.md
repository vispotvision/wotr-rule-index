## Physics
**Phenomenon** · The Veil is a condition, not a place: the membrane where one plane's law softens into another's. Crossing it is evanescent coupling, a wave leaking through a barrier it cannot travel in and resuming on the far side if the barrier is thin enough.
**Law** · Transmission falls exponentially with thickness, T ≈ e^(−2κd); ruptures follow dielectric breakdown (an insulator failing into a self-sustaining channel); Drift follows per-cycle fatigue, damage summed per crossing until it reaches one.
**Limit** · Thickness is a property of the place, not the practitioner, and cost rises by e^(2κΔd): there is no moderately hard crossing.
**Terrain** · Thin zones gather near Wellspring veins, worn ritual sites and mass-death sites; Thick comes from dense material, ward-networks, Silence, or a city's noise drowning the Soul Plane's deeper frequencies.
**Rupture** · A struck channel runs at a far lower field than it took to strike and stays struck until re-insulated: it is put out by removing its current or cooling it, never by pushing harder.
**Echo Beasts** · Unresolved spiritual content crosses a rupture and condenses on the Physical side like a precipitate, keeping the character of what formed it and none of its mind.
**Energy** · Coupling moves what is already present; the practitioner pays for the match, priced by thickness. A rupture was paid for once by whatever struck it and has run free since; containment is the expense.

| Quantity | Working | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Transmission | e^(−2κd) | exponential in barrier thickness |
| Extra thickness Δd | cost × e^(2κΔd) | a multiplier, not a modifier |
| Drift | Σ nᵢ/Nᵢ over crossings | the Pilgrim arrives when the sum reaches one |
| Rupture channel | sustaining field ≪ striking field | stays open until re-insulated |

## Metaphysics
**Aether** · The Veil is the Aether stratum's own geography. A Thin zone is Residue, carries ambient Resonance, and deafens Harmonists; ambient noise inverts saturation, so Thick zones protect mortals, while a rupture risks a Crystal Fracture Event for anyone standing in it.
**Wellspring** · Transference, the Living Exchange (Spatium), primary: two Crystals in tune and near exchange Essence along a gradient, with coupling falling off sharply with distance; it strengthens Harmonics Synergy, Projection and Tempering Conversion.
**Wellspring** · Anamnesis, the Wellspring of Remembrance (Limina): objects store their history readably; it reads a location's Mnemata, sharper and more erasive in Thin zones.
**Wellspring** · Oneirion, the Wellspring of Dream (Limina): a working acts on the manifold, not the room; it is Bifold Perception's second grammar.
**Secondary** · Somnalis (Fluxia), Eidolyn and Tenebra (Limina) and Mortalis (Vitalia) attach to particular operations.
**Inherited failures** · Transference's reverse coupling empties the practitioner into a depleted ally and couples every stabilised spirit back to them; Anamnesis's thermal resetting erases what it reads; Oneirion's projection error lands a correct working nowhere. Opponents can induce any of them.
**Theory** · The Residue Position: Thin zones, Mnemata, Echo Beasts and Drift are all residue. Correspondence governs lawful passage: two things alike and near enough, nothing coerced.
**Essence** · All three layers radiate through the medium: the Shell's Resonant Tone, the Core's emotional frequency, the Attraction Layer's recognition hum; the Attraction Layer is the oscillator and dissolves in transit. Developmental Tier is Harmonic at Splintering, Resonant at Refraction, Radiant at Transcendence, Sovereign from Invocation.
**School** · An instrument-makers' school reads the Veil as a stationary medium, so Drift is a displacement one can navigate back from; it maps Thin zones superbly, but its remedy, go back in, is another cycle, and it graduates Veil Pilgrims.

## Mechanism
**Glyph** · Passage (Auren) as the boundary that moves, laid under Frequency (Selhar) for the tuning the crossing needs.
**Boundary** · Where the practitioner's operation is resident moves, never the barrier: the body stays on the Physical Plane while awareness, Essence circulation and Crystal operation shift into the threshold.
**Effect** · Two renderings of one room at once: surfaces and light, with frequency, emotional density, temporal depth and relational topology over them. Buildings carry their history, people their Trait haloes, ground its grief and prayer.
**Gates** · None below Refraction but exposure: passive unease for all, involuntary sensitivity at Splintering (B). Bifold at Refraction (A), short crossings at Transcendence (S), sustained Bifold at Invocation (S), sustained liminal work at Realization (SS), carrying others at Emanation (SSS).
**Containment** · A significant rupture cannot be sealed by one practitioner at any Stage or Temperance; it takes Concordia-level practice re-insulating the channel by sustained harmonic projection.
**Drift** · Each crossing couples the far side back to the practitioner and loosens their anchorage by an increment that never returns; the Physical Plane grows less convincing, then stops being home.
**Drift Scale** · Loosened (0 to 24 points), no outward sign; Thinned (25 to 49), bodily sensation dulls; Unmoored (50 to 74), the Veil feels realer than the body; Adrift (75 to 99), returns come slow and incomplete; Veil Pilgrim at 100.
**Failure** · Field fault: a Thin zone brings contamination, memory bleed and involuntary Bifold; a Thick zone costs exponentially more and muddies readings; at a rupture the planes do not interface, they collide.
**Structural** · Drift is a structural fault paid in instalments; the Veil Pilgrim, patient enough to guide the lost and too thin to remember wanting a body, is the fault completing.
**Bleed** · Waste leaves the Shell as heat and light: 30 to 40 percent at Refraction, 20 to 30 at Transcendence to Realization, 10 to 15 at Emanation, around a body that is not moving.

## Counterplay
**Tell** · A practitioner visibly handling two perceptual grammars at once; on a crossing, an unattended body exactly where it was. Drift has no tell at any one crossing; only the count of entries reads it. A rupture's tell is Echo Beasts.
**Limits** · No controlled operation below Refraction; no solo rupture sealing; no entry into the Soul Plane or Plane of Fate, only the threshold; cost set by where the practitioner stands; the body left behind every time.
**Beats it** · Choose Thick ground or a city; Silence, wards and anti-Harmonia fields; shoot the unattended body, since a crossing cannot be aborted fast; give them lawful reasons to cross again and again.
**Carried** · Anyone ever carried through a crossing is already counting Drift, told or not; a soulguard cannot decline a soul.
**Look up** · The Veil entry's Access and Perception gates and Thin, Thick and Rupture structure; the Drift scale; Transference's Failure in the Spatium entries; the Silence Realm register; records of how often a practitioner has gone in.

## Physics
**Phenomenon** · Resonant energy transfer between coupled oscillators: two pendulums of the same frequency joined by a weak spring trade their motion completely after a fixed interval, with nothing travelling between them.
**Law** · Coupled-mode theory: two resonators exchange completely at the coupling rate κ when alike, only partly when detuned, and with a coupling that falls as a high power of distance.
**Limit** · Two conditions cap it: likeness, P_max = 4κ²/(4κ² + Δ²) for a mismatch Δ, and nearness, E = R₀⁶/(R₀⁶ + d⁶), so doubling any separation divides the transfer by 64.
**Interval** · The swap takes t = π/2κ, a real span during which both parties are partly in each other's place; it can be layered to unfold across a delay.
**Reciprocity** · The same κ runs both ways: no one-way Transposition, and no being the party who only gives. An incomplete exchange does not revert.
**Gradient** · A swap across a height moves mgh: 7.8 kJ for 80 kg across 10 m. Downhill runs with the gradient, uphill against it and costs more.
**Relay** · An Anchor Construct shortens every hop: two hops of d/2 beat one of d by 2⁶ on each leg. It makes range possible at all.
**Energy** · The total is conserved between the two ends; the practitioner pays for the coupling and for closing Δ, a tuning, the cheapest boundary condition there is.

## Metaphysics
**Aether** · A tuning cost and a coupling cost, both paid up front, with duration set by κ, not endurance. Residue sits at the anchor, not the ends, and ground around a long-used anchor deafens Harmonists. Aetheric noise is a detuning like any other.
**Wellspring** · Transference, the Living Exchange (Spatium): two Crystals in tune and near exchange along a gradient with no conductor, falling off sharply with distance; it strengthens Harmonics Synergy, Projection and Tempering Conversion.
**Wellspring** · Transmutatio, the Law of Change (Spatium): one object in two frames gives two sets of numbers and one unchanged object; it tunes unlike ends into one frame for identity trades.
**Wellspring** · Fixatio, the Binding Flame (Materia): what it holds is held because the alternative state is no longer reachable; it makes the Anchor Construct a relay that cannot be detuned.
**Inherited failures** · Transference's reverse coupling runs the condemned vessel into the officiant at the same rate for the whole interval, the price of soulguarding; Transmutatio's basis loss returns nothing recognisable; Fixatio's anchor can never be retrieved. Opponents can induce any of them.
**Theory** · Correspondence, the working the theory was written for: no consent sought, no debt incurred, only a claim that two things are alike enough to exchange. Degeneracy is correspondence as a number.
**Essence** · The Attraction Layer at both ends, since Transference makes it the oscillator; the Essence Core in identity trades, where a soul's coordinate is held. Developmental Tier is Harmonic at Splintering, Resonant at Refraction.
**School** · An old healers' school of sympathy holds that belonging-together has no range, so the bond is the working and the anchor mere insurance; it tunes with real care, trades across an open field, and gets Spatial Disjunction. The exponent is six.

## Mechanism
**Glyph** · Ci (Order), Th (Foundation) and Ra (Transcendence).
**Boundary** · Whether two coordinates count as the same kind of thing moves; not the coordinates, not the distance. The rite supplies the tuning and Transference's standing law does the rest.
**Effect** · An interval of being partly somewhere else, then standing where the other one was, in their circumstances, including the ones nobody mentioned. The counterparty gets the caster's place whether they wanted it or not.
**Modes** · Positional exchange, body for body, from Splintering at B-Grade. Identity trades (a death for an echo, a memory for an object, a soul for a mirrored construct's housing) from Refraction at A, late push toward S.
**Unlike ends** · Unlike-for-unlike trades work because Transmutatio tunes both ends into one frame; the more unlike, the more of the rite is tuning, and any mismatch left over leaves a partial trade.
**Anchor** · At Refraction the chain is internal and the Anchor Construct is a relay required by geometry; breaking it restores the sixth-power falloff. The Splintering swap also needs voice, hand, ink or blood, and fails without it.
**Failure** · False degeneracy: the ends were less alike than the tuning claimed and the exchange stops at P_max, Wellspring Desynchronization, leaving Spatial Disjunction: both systems holding part of a coordinate that belongs to one.
**Backlash** · Transmutatio runs through Overchannel, open to every Path with Overflow risk above D and Backlash risk above C; every A-Grade Transposition carries it and no Path protects against it.
**Bleed** · 30 to 40 percent of every trade leaves the officiant's Shell as heat, sound and light.

## Counterplay
**Tell** · The furniture: an Anchor Construct, a prepared Fixatio-held object placed between or beside the ends before anything begins. At close range there is no tell and no time. Afterwards two people in each other's circumstances, or each holding half of something.
**Limits** · Range falls as the sixth power; an anchor needed at range; two Stage floors for two kinds of trade; capacity needed at both ends; failures leave Spatial Disjunction and Wellspring Desynchronization.
**Beats it** · Stand further away with nothing left on the ground between; break the anchor; detune by Family, saturation or noise; be the wrong thing to couple to and let it complete; Silence the chamber; at Splintering, take the hand or voice.

## Physics
**Phenomenon** · Attention-driven duration distortion: the brain has no clock and rebuilds felt time from how densely it encoded an interval, so one person's sense of a second is pushed up or down and their movements land off the beat they meant.
**Law** · Felt duration follows the encoded information rate over an interval, not elapsed time; push the rate and the felt interval moves while the physical clock and the reflexes stay the same.
**Limit** · Unaided, maximal threat shifts felt tempo about × 1.36; the thread reaches × 0.5 to × 2.0, about 1.5 times past physiology, and delivers almost no joules.

## Mechanism
**Glyph** · Hour (Valen), a limit on duration only, laid across Reverie (Maelor) for the dream-space it is set in.
**Boundary** · The rate at which the target's own accounting of duration advances, shifted by a factor of one-half to two.
**Effect** · The target's footsteps land half a beat early or late and every correction runs off the same bad reference, so correcting makes it worse. Slowed, responses arrive late; sharpened, they overcommit and spend twice the effort.
**Hold** · Glory is below Refraction, so the thread hangs on the practitioner's hand and holds only while eye contact or an active Essence-lock on the target lasts.
**Reach** · Nothing without a coherent dream underneath can be threaded: a construct, a Wellspring husk or a Severed mind has no ratio to pin.
**Failure** · Boundary fault: a sign error on the map back into the room inverts the ratio, so the thread now and then slows a target meant to be sharpened.
**Bleed** · 30 to 40 percent, 0.78 to 19.2 GJ, leaves as heat at the Shell; since the effect carries almost no joules, nearly everything spent goes to holding the thread, and its structural share is the Memory Shear.

## Counterplay
**Tell** · The practitioner fixes their eyes on one person and a line of current runs from hand to target; afterwards nothing is left in the room, but the practitioner gets the order of the last minute wrong.
**Limits** · One thread, one target, 500 m by sight or Essence-link, held gaze or lock, three turns, nothing without a dream underneath; it does nothing to flesh, armour or reach.
**Fails against** · A broken line of sight; a Fate-keyed ward, which severs it by reading its shape; a Sleep Debt never paid under sustained pressure; minds trained to refuse the tempo; a construct or husk; crowds or Domains; numbers.
**Look up** · The Limina terrain; Oneirion's failures; Memory Shear and Sleep Debt among Core costs; Fate-keyed wards in the Counterplay register; the Glory Stage table and the A and S Grade reaction rows.

## Physics
**Phenomenon** · Projective measurement and its back-action: a strong enough measurement does not report a state, it imposes one, as repeated measurement freezes or hastens a decay in the Zeno and anti-Zeno effects.
**Law** · The projection postulate: a strong measurement of an observable leaves the system in one of its definite states; erasing information costs at least kT·ln2 per bit (the Landauer bound).
**Limit** · Past the point where the measurement's pull exceeds the system's own dynamics, the read becomes the verdict; a system with no definite persistence state of its own has nothing to be projected into.
**Compression** · Quench and temper: steel hardens from about 200 to 700 HV, 3.5 times, at the price of brittleness; the working runs that cycle on the practitioner's own output.
**Energy** · Erasing a whole mind costs 14 microjoules, less than a housefly in flight; the EX Grade pays for a boundary an entire identity must conform to, not for the erasure.

## Mechanism
**Glyph** · Measure laid across Ruin: a measurement on an edge makes a blow heavier; on ruin it makes a verdict.
**Boundary** · The strength of one persistence measurement, pushed past where its back-action exceeds the target's own dynamics.
**Effect** · A strike, then no wound: Soul, memory and Principle alignment collapse together, Soul first since it is most strongly coupled, with no interval in which anything can be arrested. Instant, no sustain.
**Exclusive** · It cannot run while Dirge Ascension is active; both compress the same architecture and a reserve cannot be hardened into two shapes at once.
**Hold** · Internalised Spellcraft, a ritual-fast chain held and released; no material anchor at Zenith.
**Failure** · Boundary fault: the read has no aperture, and the unintended place it runs is the practitioner's own memory. Field fault on the target's side: a Crystal with no independent selfhood presents nothing to project.
**Bleed** · 5 percent at η 0.95 arrives at once at the practitioner as paradox recoil, heat at the Shell, with quench cracking at any η; above η 1 the surplus thins the room instead. A Principle Engine is the one relief valve.

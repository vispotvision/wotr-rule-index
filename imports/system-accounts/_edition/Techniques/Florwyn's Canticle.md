## Physics
**Phenomenon** · The body's own wound-healing cascade, driven faster than it natively runs: clotting, inflammation, rebuilding and remodelling, with rebuilding (cell division and collagen laying) the slow step; plants grow the same way without a final size.
**Law** · The healing rate is set by its slowest step, biosynthesis: skin cells double in about a day and a wound edge closes about 0.5 to 1 mm a day.
**Limit** · It buys time, not substance: a severed limb is a mass problem, and no speeding of the cascade supplies tissue that is not there.
**Heat** · The tissue itself needs only about 60 kJ, but at 200 W into a gram-scale site, twice the whole body's resting output, so healed sites run hot and many wounds closing at once make a fever.

## Mechanism
**Glyph** · Renewal laid across Form: Renewal sets the healing rate, Form keeps the fast deposit from becoming etiolation.
**Boundary** · The rate constant of the rebuilding phase inside the field, and nothing else.
**Effect** · Unbroken singing, and wounds seal botanically as small growth breaks the skin and withdraws while vines and blossoms rise from the ground; days close over minutes, and a fractured Essence line stops spreading without reversing. Friend and enemy alike within earshot.
**Hold** · Below Refraction the voice is the anchor; one hymn holds up to four turns unbroken, and a wound needing minutes needs hymns in succession, the field down at each break.
**Failure** · Field fault: sterile or dead ground gives correct chemistry and nothing to work on, and good and bad ground look alike. Structural fault behind it: Overgrowth, the Trait acting when the bearer did not will it.
**Strain** · Lineage Strain pulls the inherited echo of its originating Ground closer than is safe; Emotional Vulnerability leaves the singer's feeling open to every empathic reader.
**Bleed** · 35 percent leaves as heat at the singer's Shell and in the healed sites, indistinguishable from the effect: warm patients, an exposed healer.

**Limits** · Thirty metres, four turns a hymn, unbroken voice, gradual, no limbs restored, Essence lines halted not reversed, needs living tissue nearby, healed tissue capped at B.

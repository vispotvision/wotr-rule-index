## Physics
**Phenomenon** · Forced resonance past a structure's damping: push something at its own natural frequency and a small, steady drive builds a huge swing, as a held note shatters a wineglass while the same sound off-pitch does nothing.
**Law** · At resonance the swing is the static response times the quality factor Q = 1/(2ζ), where ζ is the damping; the drive must sit inside a bandwidth of ω₀/Q to lock.
**Limit** · High Q buys amplification and costs bandwidth: at Q near a thousand the drive must hold within one part in a thousand of the true frequency, and the climb starts only once that lock holds.
**Not flutter** · A bridge torn apart by steady wind excites itself with no driving frequency; this is forced resonance and needs the lock.
**Energy** · The drive is small, about a thousandth of the failure load; the mode stores energy cycle by cycle, and the target's own failure releases it with the target's structural strain.

| Quantity | Working | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Range | line of sight, single target | 500 m |
| Burst on collapse | radius | 15 m |
| Amplification | Q of a low-loss structure | about 10³ |
| Drive against failure load | 1/Q | about one thousandth |
| Lock window on a 600 Hz mode | 600 Hz × 10⁻³ | 0.6 Hz |
| Ringdown after collapse | about Q cycles | a few seconds |

## Mechanism
**Glyph** · Frequency laid across Ruin: frequency pinned to a climb in amplitude makes a failure.
**Boundary** · The damping of one named mode in one target; the practitioner finds the frequency and pins only ζ low.
**Effect** · Two fingers or a staff pluck a string that is not there; the tone climbs from below hearing, locked on what holds the target together, so there is nothing to intercept. At the top the target fractures, implodes or suffers soul-pattern distortion, throwing a burst to fifteen metres.
**Contest** · The lock is contested, not the climb: Harmonics Attunement against the target's Harmonics Stability. Anyone at or above the practitioner's Stage is immune.
**Hold** · No anchor needed at Emanation; the gesture aims.
**Failure** · Structural fault: the Shell, made a resonant source, rebounds, and the doubled voice is it still ringing. Field fault: a true silence field or anti-vibrational current leaves nothing to drive.
**Bleed** · 10 to 15 percent leaves the Shell as heat and off-mode infrasound, tiny and quiet; nobody hears it coming.

## Counterplay
**Tell** · Two fingers or a staff raised, a string plucked on nothing, then a silence that is not silence; easy to miss at 500 m. After: the burst, ringing ground, a doubled voice.
**Limits** · 500 m line of sight, one target and a fifteen-metre burst; no effect at or above the practitioner's Stage or on anything without structural resonance; ruinous past a few breaths; no drive through shielding above C on Spirit.
**Fails against** · Damping (a silence field, Verdantia or Cymorath); a resonance split across several bands, a Knot, or decoupling through Oneirion; a broken line of sight or a decoy, since every delay doubles the practitioner's bill; spread targets; fog.

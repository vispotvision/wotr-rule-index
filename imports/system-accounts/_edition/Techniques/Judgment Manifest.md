## Metaphysics
**Aether** · The ceiling is an Aether quantity. Fulguria favours dry air, conductive ground and clear sight; Caloria favours steep thermal gradients. The Residue is standing law layered on the field, the heaviest kind, and it deafens Harmonists who work that ground afterwards.
**Saturation** · Three EX constructs across two kilometres risk saturation; past the threshold the field tips into a Crystal Fracture Event for everyone present, the practitioner included.
**Wellspring** · Judicium, the Wellspring of Truth (Fulguria): every element shows only its own spectrum, so a construct can measure a target against its Principle without contact.
**Wellspring** · Exuroth, the Trial Flame (Caloria): quench and temper, thermal shock then controlled recovery, which lets the Crystal take the recoil of detaching Core fragments and come back tougher.
**Inherited failures** · Judicium's spectral overload, three readers on every target in the field, means the constructs cannot prioritise, so they measure and enforce but never choose; Exuroth's quench cracking splits a Crystal cycled too fast without tempering.
**Category** · Animatria, a fragment of the caster's Core projected into an independent construct, with Vocatia secondary: each construct must be recognised by the field whose law it enforces.
**Essence** · The Essence Core pays by fragmenting outward, each construct a detached piece of it, one step from shattering by design; the Attraction Layer carries the recognition. Developmental Tier Crystallized Soul at Zenith.
**School** · The Sevenfold school lists seven Hermetic Principles as recovered ancient lore and teaches that a construct can be dismissed by disbelief; it cannot, and a company drilled to disbelieve is simply measured and found wanting.

## Mechanism
**Glyph** · Command (Root) laid across Form (Extension), which gives a Principle a body; Order (Extension) is the alternative, making the constructs ordering rather than commanding.
**Boundary** · The balance point between spreading and self-focusing in the local field moves, so a Principle-shaped disturbance becomes a stable, self-sustaining solution.
**Effect** · Up to three bodies, each a Principle, spread across two kilometres and acting unbidden; a target is not attacked but assessed against the nearest Principle, and two people side by side can be treated entirely differently.
**Anchor** · Zenith needs no material anchor: the practitioner names up to three Principles and gives each a body.
**Restraint** · The constructs fight well under their ceiling to stay standing, since spending energy hard spends what keeps them coherent.
**Failure** · Structural fault: paradox fatigue and Aether recoil scale with the constructs standing. Field fault: the ceiling itself, so calling a fourth collapses one already standing.
**Bleed** · At η 0.95 to 1.2, waste below one comes off the gain budget that sets the ceiling and leaves as heat at the Shell; above one the surplus is drawn from the room, which thins and stays thin.

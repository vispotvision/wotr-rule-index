**Glyph** · Return (Synonym), the record may leave along the vector it arrived on, laid across Vastness (Root) for the flooded extent; Mirage (Root) is the alternative, a limit on what may be taken for a body.
**Boundary** · The acceptance angle of a surface: the floor is pinned to corner-cube geometry and its read-out to trapped-charge release.
**Effect** · Every enemy on the film sees their own worst failure step out of it and attack from the bearing the original came from; reaction and casting clarity degrade, and the split attention is the chief damage, though the blow lands too.
**Anchor** · Below Refraction it needs the spoken declaration and the film laid by hand; silence the voice or stop the hand before the film is down and it does not complete.

**Bleed** · 30 to 40 percent leaves as heat at the Shell, and the film lit by its own losses glows: a loud, warm, easy caster to find.

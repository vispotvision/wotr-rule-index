## Physics
**Phenomenon** · A fast real crossing: the body travels up to 50 m along a line in a quarter-second, arriving beside someone whose mind was drifting, and the slow crossing is what keeps it quiet.
**Law** · A volume appearing or vanishing in air is a monopole sound source, pressure p = ρQ̇/4πr; the slower the swap of air, the quieter it is.
**Limit** · The air still has to move, so the quarter-second cannot be shortened without the step turning into a gunshot at each end; anything solid on the line stops it.
**Relaxation** · Hypnather's analogue is a glass ageing toward order, φ(t) = exp[−(t/τ)^β] with β 0.3 to 0.7: the first half-second of a night's rest is quick, and interrupting it locks in a worse-ordered state.
**Compression** · Letheveil's analogue is lossy compression; each re-encode of the same half-second compounds the last one's artefacts.

## Mechanism
**Glyph** · Passage, in two forms, sets where a trajectory may re-enter; Reverie addresses the arrival by dream-shadow, not by place.
**Boundary** · Two limits move: when the Crystal may relax, pinned to now rather than genuine rest, and where the crossing ends, pinned to the nearest dream-shadow.
**Effect** · The practitioner streaks to a point beside a drifting mind and arrives with a soft thump; the anchor feels nothing. Then comes a quarter-second standing still, unable to act, 250 to 1,250 A-Grade reaction periods.
**Anchor** · It needs a live drowsing or startled mind in range; it cannot step to empty ground, and Aurevane or Luminalis light hides the dream-shadow and kills the step.
**Hold** · At Refraction the chain is internal, a choice to let the Crystal drop toward sleep; nothing in hand or mouth to take away. It is at will, and each use is an interrupted rest that wears the Crystal.
**Failure** · Boundary fault: projection error deposits the practitioner nowhere in particular. Structural fault: interrupted relaxations accumulate over years.
**Bleed** · At η 0.65, 35 percent of each step leaves the Shell as heat and sound; a low-efficiency stepper is loud, warm and easy to find.

## Counterplay
**Tell** · The drowsiness first: a room grows heavy and people recall things they had put away. Then a streak fast-reflex fighters can see, a 76 dB thump, and someone standing very still.
**Limits** · 50 m; a drifting mind at the far end; no stepping to empty ground; an obstruction on the line stops it; the lag cannot be trained away.
**Fails against** · A spread volley at the arrival during the lag; open, bright ground held by wakeful people; Aurevane or Luminalis light; a Runecraft ward or Lock on the dream-shadow; lucid anchors; a blocked line.
**Anchors** · A stepper with a drowsing Presence cannot stop making anchors, so arrivals fall where anchors already stand; the Attraction Layer drifts toward identity bleed over time.

## Physics
**Phenomenon** · Acoustic beamforming, sound aimed through a wide coherent opening: a voice shapes the air into smooth laminar bands that quieten a room, carry one sentence far, or narrow to an edge that parts rope.
**Law** · A beam's half-angle is θ ≈ 1.22 λ/D, so a 10° beam at 500 Hz needs about 5 m of coherent aperture; beamed, a shout's worth of power (15 mW) is clear at 250 m.
**Limit** · Sound cannot part a rope (radiation pressure p = I/c is about 28 Pa at 160 dB), so the Cut is the practitioner's Shell carried along the band's edge, with the sound only as guide.
**Hush** · A refractive shadow, like wind shear bending sound away outdoors: the laminar interior against turbulent air throws noise up and out at the rim, about −9 dB at 8 m.
**Modes** · Hushfield, Carry, Cut Lamina and Air-Step, with a Choir mode for several voices and the Feather Vows as tokens; a trained voice's formant near 2.5 to 3 kHz makes a better aperture.

## Mechanism
**Glyph** · Frequency governs which frequencies may arrive, laid across Truth for the Judicium reading and Silence for the Hush.
**Boundary** · Whether the air in a region may be turbulent; pinned laminar, ordinary acoustics beams, carries, refracts and guides the Shell.
**Effect** · In the Hushfield noise thins toward the rim, hands steady and aim improves; a Carry arrives 200 m off sounding near, not loud; the Cut parts cloth and cord, never flesh; an Air-Step moves the practitioner 30 m along a line they did not seem to choose.
**Truth** · A lie told to the practitioner warbles about 6 percent flat, an arguable sensation, not a verdict; it makes room for truth to travel and compels no one to speak it.
**Hold** · Four gates in fixed order, Set, Place, Speak, Recover; Recover is the Seal and where the cost lands. Above Refraction the Stage needs no anchor, but the medium needs a living voice and breath.
**Failure** · Coherence fault: anger, conflict or curated speech turns flow to hiss, Ribbon Fray, shrinking range and dulling the Cut when most needed.
**Downstream** · Field fault: in an anechoic vault, snowfield or bale-filled hold it runs correctly and produces nothing.
**Bleed** · At η 0.60 to 0.70, 30 to 40 percent leaves the Shell as heat and sound, which competes with the signal; at Transcendence 20 to 30 percent, and the first Domain recycles some.

## Counterplay
**Tell** · Someone speaking and the air not behaving: dust holding a line, pressure in bands on the chest, sound thinning toward a rim, a room gone steady. Afterwards a hoarse voice and a practitioner hearing everything twice.
**Limits** · A living voice; nothing held without breath; dead in an echo-dead space; Hush only to about 30 kn; no Cut on flesh; no compelled truth; no defensive component.
**Fails against** · A grapple and a covered mouth; an echo-dead room or a chaos-aligned anti-cohesion field; a shielded, crowded interior; a practitioner who lies or curates; mutism or inaction; gradients shaped for the Air-Step's drift; shot from beyond the corridor.

## Physics
**Phenomenon** · Wound healing under extreme time compression: a filament crosses from caster to patient, and three days of tissue repair happen in six seconds on the patient's own chemistry.
**Law** · Energy balance with a heat ceiling on the waste: repair is cheap (about 180 J per gram of new tissue), but waste heat lands in the wound bed, which has lost the blood flow that would carry it off.
**Limit** · A 20 g wound bed takes about 430 J before protein damage starts at 43 °C (60 °C coagulates collagen), so each cast buys about 3 g of tissue: a closed cut, a sealed vessel, never a fatal wound or a severe Crystal fracture.
**Bond** · Benediction's analogue is binding strength, K_d = k_off/k_on; a bond is only statistically present, and a vow's durability is its off-rate, from minutes to days across bonds that look alike.
**Outbid** · A competitor raises the apparent K_d to K_d(1 + [I]/K_i); nothing breaks and nothing signals, the thread is simply elsewhere when called.
**Look** · Luminalis is diagnostic imaging: near-infrared at 650 to 950 nm sees 1 to 3 cm into tissue, and polarised light tells new collagen from old, so the wound's true shape is found before closing.

## Mechanism
**Glyph** · Binding: the establishment of a conductive relation with a stated affinity; the renewal itself is the patient's.
**Boundary** · The rate ceiling: repair's chemical timers are authorised to run out of sequence, and the patient's cells, ATP and amino acids do the work.
**Effect** · A spoken word, a golden-white filament from palm to chest, one harp-string chime as the bond seats. Warmth spreads from the deepest point outward, pain goes before the wound does, and six seconds later three grams of tissue lie true.
**Hold** · Below Refraction it has two anchors, the voice that declares it and the hand the filament leaves from.
**Failure** · Coherence fault: competitive inhibition seats a rival silently, and the caster believes the blessing holds while it holds a vacancy.
**Downstream** · Boundary fault: layered with a second working, birefringence lays collagen twice along two slightly different geometries.
**Bleed** · The thread's waste lands in the wound bed and scalds if pushed; the reserve's bleed leaves the caster's Shell as warmth and light, tens of megawatts for six seconds.

## Counterplay
**Tell** · Spoken, lit and chiming; the filament names both ends of the bond to every witness. Afterwards warm tissue, perhaps a scald, and a caster wearing the patient's pain.
**Limits** · 15 m with clear sight; about 3 g a cast; no fatal wounds or severe Crystal fractures; never the caster; no higher than B.
**Beats it** · Cover the mouth or break the wrist; outbid the bond with a stronger ligand; hold the vow's terms; fog, smoke or shielding blinds the look; a cold, sterile, shielded room; a Limina curse or Oblivara's erasure glyphs sever it. Standing between them does nothing.
**Break the man** · A healer whose bonds outrun their conduct drifts in Coherence, measured by Resilience Oath; notice and wait. A broken vow is a Shear Break.

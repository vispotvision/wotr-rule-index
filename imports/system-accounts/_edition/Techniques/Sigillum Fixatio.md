## Physics
**Phenomenon** · Kinetic trapping: one named thing within ten metres is held in its present configuration by raising the energy barrier out of it, not by pushing on it. For nine seconds it stops changing.
**Law** · Arrhenius: the rate of escape from a state is k = A·exp(−E_a/RT), with A about 10¹³ per second, so residence time grows exponentially with barrier height.
**Limit** · A barrier stops what is thermally driven (healing, decay, drift, corrosion); it does not stop what is pushed, so a blow must break the held bonds themselves.
**Permanence** · Escape time is exponential in the barrier, so a permanent lock is the same binding a little higher; overshoot by a few tens of kJ/mol and the hold never ends, and nothing at the casting feels different.
**Cure** · The analogue is a thermoset cure, cross-links spanning a whole network: entry is a threshold crossed at once, and the result is stiff, brittle and chars rather than flowing.
**Stillness** · Life runs on catalysis, so a held body's wounds stop worsening and stop healing; heartbeat, breath and blood flow stop too, and nine seconds is the fainting interval.

## Metaphysics
**Aether** · A small, sharply bounded Materia draw at ten metres, favoured by rock, worked stone and deep foundations, suppressed by open water, altitude and moving platforms. The Residue cures rather than fades: a held place stays faintly held for good, and repeated casting deafens Harmonists.
**Wellspring** · Fixatio, the Binding Flame (Materia): a cured network chars before it flows, so what it holds stays held because the other configuration is no longer reachable; it strengthens Vitality Fortitude and Resilience Anchoring.
**Inherited failure** · Fixatio's irreversibility: the cure is not undone by wanting it undone, and an opponent who pushes the caster to overshoot makes the hold permanent.
**Category** · Residue: of the Four Theories, the one for what a working leaves behind that answers to no one.
**Essence** · The Aether Shell pays at both ends: the target is held at its Shell boundary and the caster's Shell locks in sympathy, a cured network for the same nine seconds.
**School** · The Fixatists read the sigil as proof that change was never real; they hold positions others abandon, and it kills them at second ten, since a doctrine without change cannot count to nine.

## Mechanism
**Glyph** · Lock, a Counter form, occupies the limit so no other can be set there; Order files the physical half.
**Boundary** · The height of the barrier out of the target's present state, raised by about 83 kJ/mol; every law in the target keeps running beyond it.
**Effect** · A word, Esto, and a closing fist; then the target's next movement is simply unavailable, with no pressure or grip anywhere. Pain stops worsening, bleeding stops without clotting, grey creeps in at the edges of sight.
**Hardening** · The held body is truly hardened and resists damage up to the working's full C-Grade rating, 0.1 to 0.3 GN or 20.92 MJ to 1.046 GJ; a held ally is shielded, a held enemy shrugs off blows.
**Workings** · An active working under the Sigillum goes quiet, finished rather than drained; its parameters cannot be altered or dispelled for the span, not even by its own author.
**Release** · At second nine everything resumes where it was, bleed included; the wound is exactly as old as it was.
**Failure** · Irreversibility, a few tens of kJ/mol away at every casting and invisible; and brittle fracture at the anchor under shear or torsion, a cured network's own fault. Both are faults of Structure.
**Bleed** · About 55 percent leaves as heat, sound and structural bleed at the caster's Shell and in the air at the anchor, since the working does no work on its target.

**Paths** · No Path declared: Gnosis Fluency above D needs the Spirit Path, so the reading of a held working runs capped at D; Vitality Fortitude above C needs the Body Path, so the lock cannot be pushed past C.
**Anchor** · Below Refraction it needs a material anchor, and has two: the spoken word and the closing hand.

## Counterplay
**Tell** · A spoken imperative and a fist pointed at the target, visible at ten metres, so it warns the one about to be held; afterwards, a wound exactly as old as it was nine seconds before.
**Limits** · Nothing two or more Stages above the caster; nothing without a discrete physical or Aetheric form, so no Domain, Wellspring current or abstraction; one target, one Sigillum, nine seconds.
**Beats it** · Cover the mouth or break the wrist before the fist shuts; Lock or Knot the target first; shear or twist at the anchor; spread out while the caster cannot choose again; Fractura breaks it, Dissolution unmakes it.
**Ground** · Fight on open water, at altitude or on a moving platform, where Materia has no tools.
**Valmir** · The Titan's entropic domain unmakes the anchoring outright, but Titan-keyed arts are at the edge of what a mortal Crystal survives, so the route is real and rarely usable.
**Matching** · A counter-Sigillum from an equal resolves on technique and position, a coin toss.

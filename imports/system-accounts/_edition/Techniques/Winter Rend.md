## Physics
**Phenomenon** · Forced convection: a cold surface moved through air sheds heat ten times faster than a still one, so cold rides a sweeping strike; where the edge touches armour, the outer skin shrinks faster than the metal beneath and cracks (thermal shock).
**Law** · Transfer grows as h ∝ √v, and a surface drop past R = σ(1 − ν)/(Eα) cracks a material in tension from the outside in.
**Limit** · The cold is small: a tenth of a second reaches 0.12 mm into flesh, about 20 kJ, skin-deep frostbite and a shallow nerve block; the yield belongs to the blow it rides.
**Harder blow** · Stiffened by the cold, the target cannot give, so the blow the arc rides lands two to four times harder.
**Contact** · Airflow alone cannot shock a steel plate, which just equalises; the edge must touch.

## Metaphysics
**Aether** · Draws Caloria, which feeds on steep thermal gradients, and Limina for the approach; repeated in a closed room it cools the arcs and warms the air until the gradient is gone.
**Wellspring** · Vohrin (Caloria), one power at two levels, Titan and Wellspring, drawn here at the Wellspring level: cold is a deficit held as a discipline, an acceptance of heat; here the discipline rides a moving edge rather than the practitioner.
**Wellspring** · Tenebra, the Hidden Shadow (Limina): the practitioner's return drops below the watcher's noise floor, covering the close to two metres; it does not make the arc.
**Inherited failures** · Vohrin's cold is unmade by a strong heat vector at the far end; Tenebra's aspect dependence shows the approach to a watcher at the unplanned angle.
**Category** · Correspondence, thinly: a line drawn through space carries a property along it; Consent Doctrine underneath.
**Essence** · The Aether Shell channels Aether into muscular force and throws frost along an arc; the heat taken leaves through the Shell into the room.
**Sub-Stats** · Ardency Penetration, with Vitality Tolerance for the practitioner's own cold endurance.
**Path gates** · On the Body Path every gate clears: Penetration opens past C at Ascension, and Tolerance's unused Aether-dead and gravitational parts are not needed.
**School** · The Antiperistatic school holds that air closing in behind a stroke drives the cold, so it broadened its blade backs to catch it; that raises drag and slows the sweep the cold depends on, and its masters die closest to the enemy.

## Mechanism
**Glyph** · Edge carries the arc; Boundary carries the thermal condition.
**Boundary** · Where the cold end of a gradient may sit, granted to a moving edge rather than a place.
**Trigger** · A sweeping melee strike of arm or weapon; below Refraction its anchor is the arm that draws the arc.
**Effect** · A dry clean cold smell, a white line of the room's own water frozen along the path, then armour that splits along lines no armourer knows; bare skin whitens along the line and the limb goes slow, the pain coming later.
**Chime** · A faint ringing as a thin ice layer cracks as fast as it forms.
**Scope** · A two-metre arc, delivered across its sweep and not sustained, below the 3 to 10 m A-Grade Contact Range.
**Failure** · Field fault: a room with no gradient left, or heat driven into the arc, gives the cold nothing; insulation denies the contact, and speed outruns two metres.
**Bleed** · 30 to 40 percent of the spend and the heat taken leave the Shell as warmth into the air, so a warm closed room slowly spoils the working.
**Body** · The drawing arm is the cold end, and numbness in it grows with each sweep until the hands are slow and do not report what they hold.

## Counterplay
**Tell** · The dry cold smell before the practitioner arrives, a white arc that shows where the edge went and where the next will go, a small ring that is no blow, warm air round him, and numb arms shaken out.
**Limits** · Two metres of reach; small cold; no shock without contact; weaker with every sweep in a closed room.
**Beats it** · Padded jack, wool, leather or felt; insulated or flame-forged armour; heat into the arc, such as Pyreveil; outrun two metres; a warm closed room and make him repeat; break or bind the drawing arm; read the arc after the first.

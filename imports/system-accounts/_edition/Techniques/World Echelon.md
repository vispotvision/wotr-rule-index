**Effect** · Nothing strikes; feeling keeps its content but loses its timing, breathing falls in, the crowd moves as one, and inner life becomes a quantity read against a reference nobody chose.

**Failure** · Boundary fault: one coupling constant laid without discrimination on a sphere synchronises everyone, friend and practitioner alike.

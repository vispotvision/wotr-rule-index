**Amaranth Severance**

- **Name:** *Amaranth Severance* (“The Blooming Thorn”)  
- **Type:** Weapon / Soulbound (latent Domain Anchor when overfed)  
- **Appearance:** A slender estoc-length blade of blood-glass steel that grades from smoked iron at the forte to a translucent crimson point. Guard and pommel form a lattice of black-vine filigree that cradles a carved rose-crystal bud; a thorned spur hooks forward over the knuckles. When awakened, red vapor curls from the fuller like petals burning in slow motion; the tip weeps a single bead of ruby light before battles.  
- **Origin:** Forged in the catacombs of Bluvest by the Red Alchemists during the Withering Age, using marrow-silver and a petrified soul-bud taken from a condemned High Vampire matriarch. Final tempering occurred beneath a Parunic choir at the Skyglass Archive—an illegal experiment sealed from the Codex.  
- **Essence Alignment:** Spirit/Harmonia (Mournful pressure profile; reactive burst release).  
- **Parun Glyph(s):** Ma (Memory), Wy (Motion), Uur (Disruption). Wordform chain favors **Ma→Uur** for Resonance Erasure on contact and **Wy** for needle-thrust acceleration.  
- **Effects:**  
  - Drinks Essence carried in fresh blood, converting it to a **Severance Pulse** that fractures the target’s lattice on the next hit (Resonance Fracture → Vector Severance if overcharged).  
  - Three morphic stances keyed to intent: **Dormant Vine** (dull gray, oathbound), **Bloom** (crimson wake, increased penetration and point control), **Ashen Petal** (smoking edge; cuts Domains’ anchor-threads within arm’s reach).  
  - Remembers kills; the rose-crystal opens an extra petal for each sworn victim, granting brief **Topology Lockdown** against that bloodline.  
- **Known Limitations:**  
  - Rejects wielders without a signed vow; breaking the vow triggers **Pressure Inversion Rupture** to the wielder’s forearm nodes.  
  - Prolonged dry use (no blood intake) induces **Harmonic Mute**—the blade turns inert for a lunar cycle.  
  - Overfeeding risks **Lattice Burnout** and uncontrolled **Cascade Overrun** that can shred nearby allies’ Resonance.  
- **Soul Affinity:** Highest coherence with Bluvest lineages (Dhampir/High Vampire); secondary affinity with Reactive bodies at Temperance stages Refraction→Severance.  
- **Historical Usage:** Court-execution blade of the Crimson Prefecture of Bluvest; later wielded by an Accord shadow operative during the Dorrosfenn purges; whispered to have “bloomed” in the hands of a Reaper-figure on the Caedor frontier.  
- **Current Wielder / Location:** Dreadveil Reliquary, Lower Bluvest—catalogued as “Rose-III”; periodically missing during Concord eclipses.  
- **Mythic Reputation:** “The rose that drinks vows.” Streets say each petal marks a name the blade will never forget.  
- **First Appearance (Volume/Chapter):** *Inclusion Saga — The Blooming Silence, Ch. 4*.

**Band of Frozen Brambles**

- **Name:** *Band of Frozen Brambles* (“Winter-Briar”)  
- **Type:** Relic Gauntlet / Glyph Tool (attuned; can stabilize as a minor Domain Anchor on permafrost)  
- **Appearance:** A wrist-cuff of frost-patinated silverthorn cradling a blue glacial prism. From the cuff sprout five segmented, thorned finger-sheaths linked by cold-iron chains; each segment is veined with pale Verdure patterns trapped beneath ice. When awakened, hoarfrost feathers from the links and the prism exhales a quiet, glassy breath.  
- **Origin:** Grown and forged in the **Regency of Mireya** by the Thornsingers—Cryokith artisans who braid Verdure rites with deep-cold metallurgy. The brambles were “wintered” for one century beneath a sleeping glacier, then set to a **Ur→Ma→Wy** chain and sealed with a breath-oath.  
- **Essence Alignment:** Water/Cold + Harmonia (retentive, heat-draining profile)  
- **Parun Glyph(s):** **Ur** (Balance) woven as a bramble lattice to distribute force; **Ma** (Memory) teaches the vines to seek joints and hinges; **Wy** (Motion) drives chain-snap and pull; trace **Uur** (Disruption) in the thorns for nerve-numbing bites.  
- **Effects:**  
  - **Thornbind:** Flicking the chains launches ice-vines that pierce and root, spreading rime along armor seams; on a second pull they cinch, imposing **Vector Collapse** on the target’s limb.  
  - **Briar Gaol:** Palm to ground blooms a ring of frozen brambles that grows into a shoulder-high cage; within, projections suffer **Harmonic Mute** and footing becomes brittle glass.  
  - **Winter Dividend:** Heat siphoned by the thorns returns as Essence—brief **Overpressure Discharge** can armor the wielder’s forearm in clear ice plating.  
  - **Rime Recall:** A taut command yanks bound foes or objects toward the wielder with **Spatial Harmonic Bending**, ideal for disarming or body-checking into walls of ice.  
  - **Glass Petal Riposte:** On perfect parry, the bramble segments shed razor petals that countercut along the attacker’s vector.  
- **Known Limitations:**  
  - Power wanes in warm, wet climates; direct flame induces **Glyph Desaturation** and can unseat the cage.  
  - Overuse chills the wielder’s nerve lattice causing **Phase Lag** (slow fingers, ghost-ache); repeated breaches risk frostbite at resonance nodes.  
  - Salted soil and alchemical brine stunt growth; Verdure-rich sacred groves may resist Thornbind entirely.  
  - Chains cannot cross active Aether-fire without risking **Cascade Overrun** (explosive shatter of the ice lattice).  
- **Soul Affinity:** Strong with Cryokith, Frost-Trolls, and Retentive bodies at Temperance **Flourishing→Refraction**; unstable for Reactive bodies unless tempered by Balance rites.  
- **Historical Usage:** Worn by the **Pale March Wardens** during the Caedor incursions; a single gauntlet famously sealed four Domain breaches by “gardening” winter across the threshold.  
- **Current Wielder / Location:** Accord Cold Reliquary, Mireya—loaned to Enforcement Division strike leads for siege and capture missions.  
- **Mythic Reputation:** “The glove that grows a prison.” Soldiers swear the brambles remember every joint they’ve tasted.  
- **First Appearance (Volume/Chapter):** *Inclusion Saga — The Blooming Silence, Ch. 6 (“Winter Tithes”)*

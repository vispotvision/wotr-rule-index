**Horizon’s Reach**

- **Name:** *Horizon’s Reach* (“Limen-Hand,” “Kepler’s Grasp”)  
- **Type:** Soulbound Prosthesis / Glyph Tool (can stabilize as a Lesser Domain Anchor during overcharge)  
- **Appearance:** A forearm and hand of star-salt bronze inlaid with windows of living night; within the apertures drift violet constellations and slow comets. Concentric aureoles—thin orbital rings—float and spin around the wrist and knuckles, altering their tilt with each breath. When the wielder flexes, the void within the palm deepens until it looks like a hole punched through reality.  
- **Origin:** Forged in the Skyglass Archive’s **Orbital Bay** from a captured fragment of Outer World horizon-light gathered above Rovann. R.A.D. sleeved the shard into an aetheric armature and bound it with a **Wy→Ur→Ie** chain to obey intent without tearing its host’s lattice. Prototype #3 survived attunement; the rest imploded into map-sized singular scars.  
- **Essence Alignment:** Vectra / Harmonia with a **stable→recursive** pressure profile; excels at motion, curvature, and positional bias.  
- **Parun Glyph(s):** **Wy** (Motion) as prime carrier; **Ur** (Balance) for orbit keeping; **Ie** (Insight) for parallax targeting; trace **Ma** (Memory) lets the arm “remember” trajectories it has touched.  
- **Effects:**  
  - **Aetherstep Reach:** Displaces the hand alone to any seen point within bowshot; grabs, shoves, or casts from that location with **Tunneling Cast** timing. The arm snaps back on release without tearing sockets.  
  - **Orbitals:** The aureole rings convert incoming vectors into harmless spin, granting reliable deflection and **Spatial Harmonic Bending** for curved throws and shots.  
  - **Horizon Draw:** A brief palm singularity pulls targets along a line into the wielder, stacking **Pressure Differential Spikes** that stagger Domains.  
  - **Parallax Grip:** Splits a ghost-hand two steps off the real position; on clash, imposes **Topology Bias** so the “chosen” grasp is the one that lands.  
  - **Vector Anchor:** Planting the palm to ground locks an area the size of a market stall against displacement or teleport for a count of five—mini **Topology Lockdown**.  
- **Known Limitations:**  
  - Requires attunement; early use causes **Phase Lag** (neural–lattice delay) and vertigo.  
  - Overextending beyond line-of-sight or through iron-laced wards risks **Overphase Snap** and wrist micro-tears.  
  - Repeated Horizon Draws induce **Resonance Drift**—the host misjudges distance until the next rest.  
  - If the rings lose synchronization, **Cascade Overrun** throws debris and allies into wild orbits.  
- **Soul Affinity:** Best with **Transmutive** bodies at Temperance **Refraction→Severance**; Eressean elegists and Concord scouts show high coherence; rejects Retentive bodies without Balance rites.  
- **Historical Usage:** Fielded by an Accord pathfinder to bridge the Caedor breach—grasping a shattered gate from thirty paces and re-seating it mid-siege; later credited with plucking a Black Concord courier through a collapsing corridor during *The Blooming Silence*.  
- **Current Wielder / Location:** Vaulted in Skyglass Archive, Orbital Bay—signed out to operatives under emergency transit writs.  
- **Mythic Reputation:** “The hand that can touch tomorrow.” Street rumor says it shakes with stars when it longs for a distant thing.  
- **First Appearance (Volume/Chapter):** *Inclusion Saga — The Blooming Silence, Ch. 12 (“The Arm That Crossed the Room”)*

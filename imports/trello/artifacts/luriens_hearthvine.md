**Lurien’s Hearthvine**

- **Name:** *Lurien’s Hearthvine* (“Green Cantor’s Bow”)  
- **Type:** Relic / Glyph Tool (can act as a minor Domain Anchor in groves)  
- **Appearance:** A living bow grown from twined heartwood and creeping vine; bark smooth where palms rest, rough and knotted along the limbs. Fresh leaves sprout year-round, veining with faint aether-light when drawn. The string is a silvery gut-fibre that hums like a beesong; dew beads on it at dawn even indoors.  
- **Origin:** Sung into shape in the Holy See of **Lurien** by the first Green Cantor during the Withering Age, using a graft from the Sanctuary Bough and roots steeped in Wellspring sap. No cut was ever made—only coaxed growth guided by Parunic song.  
- **Essence Alignment:** Nature / Harmonia (Verdant profile; stable, regenerative flow).  
- **Parun Glyph(s):** **Ur** (Balance) set into the limb-curve, **Wy** (Motion) braided through the string, **Ma** (Memory) etched as hidden leaf-runes along the riser for arrow guidance by path-memory.  
- **Effects:**  
  - **Seedshaft:** Any nocked shaft is wrapped in quick-vine; on impact it roots, binding limbs or anchoring doors and siege gear.  
  - **Green Arc:** Arrows curve around cover by following the forest’s remembered paths (Ma-guided trajectory).  
  - **Hearthcall:** When planted tip-down and strung to earth, projects a **Lesser Grove Domain**—a circle of damp air, soft loam, and undergrowth that slows foes and heals minor wounds of allies.  
  - **Germinal Shot:** Charge three breaths; the arrow bursts into a lattice of thorns mid-flight, splitting into smaller seeking barbs.  
- **Known Limitations:**  
  - Refuses iron-tipped arrows; prefers wood, bone, or stone.  
  - Loses power in barren ground or salted soil; in deserts, effects are half strength.  
  - Overdrawing beyond Balance breaks induces **Harmonic Bleed**, causing uncontrolled overgrowth that can entangle the wielder.  
  - If fed with hatred alone, leaves blacken and the bow enters a month of dormancy.  
- **Soul Affinity:** Strong with **Transmutive** bodies attuned to Verdure; natural coherence with Beastkin rangers and Lurien clergy; uneasy with Bluvest bloodlines.  
- **Historical Usage:** Carried by the Wardens of the Green March during Malphas’ first probes; later gifted to a Guild Accord scout who held the Caedor ford by raising a forest overnight.  
- **Current Wielder / Location:** Kept at the **Sanctuary of Mosslight**; ceremonially loaned to Accord envoys when parleying in wild realms.  
- **Mythic Reputation:** “The bow that plants a battlefield.” Children’s rhymes say each arrow is a future tree choosing where to stand.  
- **First Appearance (Volume/Chapter):** *Inclusion Saga — The Blooming Silence, Ch. 7 (“Thorns in the March”)*

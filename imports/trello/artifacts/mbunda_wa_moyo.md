## Mbunda wa Moyo

**Name:**
**Mbunda wa Moyo** — _Drum of the Living Heart_

---

**Type:**
Relic | Domain Anchor | Ritual Tool

---

**Appearance:**
A sacred hand drum fashioned from hollowed ironwood, its surface stretched with the cured hide of a Wellspring-born antelope. The hide is painted in spirals of ochre and verdant pigment, glowing faintly when struck. Thick braided cords of palm fiber wrap the edges, and Parun glyphs are burned into the drum’s rim in a ring of charred black, each one marked with Chimwala’s own Essence signature.

---

**Origin:**
Crafted in the **Heartwood Grove** after Chimwala’s first Temperance ascent into the **Flourishing** stage, using materials gifted in ceremonial exchange between the Lurien elders and Innerworld drummers of Kaetra.

---

**Essence Alignment:**
Harmonic-Aether / Nature-Aether

---

**Parun Glyph(s):**
Ur (Balance) | Sa (Rhythm) | Vaa (Flow)

---

**Effects:**

- When played, the drum synchronizes the Essence flow of allies within hearing range, amplifying spell cohesion and physical stamina.
- In a Domain, the beat can be used to align terrain growth to a battle rhythm, causing roots, vines, and undergrowth to move in timed pulses.
- Can calm hostile fauna or repel corrupted wildlife by inducing a Wellspring-harmonic dissonance.

---

**Known Limitations:**

- Requires steady, uninterrupted rhythm to sustain its effect.
- Silence between beats longer than 3 seconds breaks the Domain pulse effect entirely.

---

**Soul Affinity:**
Chimwala N’Doro — bondable through a drum-beat ritual performed under a moonlit grove.

---

**Historical Usage:**
Used during the **Siege of the Jade Bridge** to unify five druid warbands’ Essence into a single assault rhythm that shattered enemy fortifications.

---

**Current Wielder / Location:**
Chimwala N’Doro — brought only to war councils, large-scale rituals, and Wellspring stabilization efforts.

---

**Mythic Reputation:**
Known among the Innerworld as “The Second Heartbeat of Lurien.” Warriors say it can make trees breathe and rivers change their course.

---

**First Appearance (Volume/Chapter):**
_WOTR: Book V – The Blooming Siege of Caedor, Ch. 11_

## **Artifact Entry — Mirrorwell Prism**

**Name:**
**Mirrorwell Prism**

**Type:**
Relic / Soulbound Projection Anchor

**Appearance:**
A palm-sized shard of perfectly clear crystal, cut into a hexagonal prism with one fractured end. Inside, faint spectral shapes drift and shift, as if trapped echoes swim in liquid memory. When Essence is channeled into it, the prism blooms with rippling light patterns resembling concentric glyphs.

**Origin:**
Recovered from the collapsed heart of the **Echo-Spire Ruins** in the Innerworld. Believed to be a remnant of a forgotten projection-forge used by early Ma glyph practitioners.

**Essence Alignment:**
Spiritual Essence (Primary)
Cognitive Essence (Secondary)

**Parun Glyph(s):**
+Ma (Memory)
+Ur (Balance)

**Effects:**

* Stabilizes projection tethers, reducing AU cost of sustained projections by **15%**.
* Allows Karo to **bind one projection to the Prism** for enhanced form stability and +1 minute to its lifespan before collapse.
* Can “mirror” a bound projection’s last known state, enabling a rapid re-summon without full Essence cost (50% AU reduction for that specific projection).

**Known Limitations:**

* Cannot bind more than one projection at a time; binding a new projection erases the previous mirror state.
* Overuse risks “echo bleed,” where Karo experiences fragmented memories from the projection at random times.
* Physical damage to the Prism can cause projection collapse backlash.

**Soul Affinity:**
Perfect resonance with Karo’s Medium Core; dangerous to most non-medium casters due to Essence compression instability.

**Historical Usage:**
Rumored to have been wielded by an Echo-forger during the Great Spirit War to maintain constructs in prolonged battles.

**Current Wielder / Location:**
Karo Venrik — Soulbound to him during his first exploration of the Echo-Spire.

**Mythic Reputation:**
Known among projectionists as a “memory anchor,” considered a rare relic capable of extending the life of unstable spiritual constructs.

**First Appearance (Volume/Chapter):**
*Chronicles of the Exiles* — First used to stabilize the **Beast of Regret** during the confrontation with the Spirit Warden.

## **Essence Draft — Mnemonis Solution**

**Name:**
**Mnemonis Solution**

**Type:**
Hybrid (Ritual / Alchemical)

**Primary Essence Behavior:**
Recursive — continuously loops stored mnemonic Essence through the Soul Crystal, reprocessing until a new resonance point is reached.

**Trait Compatibility:**

* **Echo-Binder:** Enhances tether stability during multi-projection states.
* **Memory-Braided Will:** Deepens recall fidelity for high-emotion reconstructions.

**Soul Interaction Type:**
Evolves — gradually reshapes the Medium Core’s tethering matrix to accommodate multi-source Wellspring resonance.

**Typical Uses:**

* Unlocking a secondary Wellspring aligned with memory or duplication traits.
* Recalibrating unstable Medium Cores to stabilize new Essence flows.
* Preservation of high-fidelity mnemonic constructs for long-term summoning.

**Source Material or Wellspring:**

* Condensed **Mirrorwater Essence** (distilled from reflective pools in the Innerworld).
* Fragmented glyph shard from a **Ma**-aligned projection anchor.
* Infusion of projection residue harvested from a recently collapsed Echo construct.

**Drafting Method:**
Ritual Extraction — Essence drawn under a mirrored glyph array, then condensed through an alchemical drip-feed into a containment prism until it achieves a stable recursive flow pattern.

**Associated Parun Glyph(s):**
+Ma (Memory)
+Ur (Balance)
+Ie (Insight)

**Known Reactions:**

* When introduced to the Medium Core, triggers controlled “memory storms” — rapid cycling of old projections that rewrites tether pathways.
* In multi-Essence environments, may pull in ambient residual impressions unintentionally.

**Backlash Effects:**

* Short-term sensory distortion: hearing whispers or seeing echo-images of past projections.
* Risk of tether drift, where projections detach prematurely until the new Wellspring is fully anchored.

**Temperance Stage Requirement (if any):**
Stage IV — **Splintering** minimum; Stage V **Refraction** recommended for full effect.

**Famous Users / Alchemarchs:**

* Karo Venrik (first documented user for Wellspring transition).
* Alchemarch Helviun Marr (credited with original recursive draft process).

**First Appearance (Volume/Chapter):**
*WOTR: The Legacies* — Karo brews the Mnemonis Solution under Vaelen Mourcrix’s reluctant supervision before attempting his Wellspring shift.

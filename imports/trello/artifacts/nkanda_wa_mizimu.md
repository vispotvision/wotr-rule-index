##  Nkanda wa Mizimu

**Name:**
**Nkanda wa Mizimu** — _Necklace of the Spirit Chorus_

---

**Type:**
Relic | Soulbound | Glyph Tool

---

**Appearance:**
A wide ceremonial torque made from braided elephant hair, gold leaf, and carved jadeite discs. Each disc bears a Parun glyph etched so deep the cut reveals threads of glowing Essence. Tiny glass beads filled with preserved spirit-lights dangle from the necklace, their luminescence shifting in rhythm with unseen whispers.

---

**Origin:**
Forged in the **Sanctum of the Thousand Voices** beneath the Innerworld Grove, using ancestral ivory gifted by the tribe’s honored dead. The forging process involved a seven-night Spirit Communion ritual, binding the voices of Chimwala’s predecessors into the torque.

---

**Essence Alignment:**
Spiritual-Aether / Harmonic-Aether

---

**Parun Glyph(s):**
Ma (Memory) | Ie (Insight) | Ta (Voice)

---

**Effects:**

- Grants the wearer the ability to commune with ancestral spirits without a summoning ritual.
- Allows the chorus of bound voices to chant in unison, producing **Resonance Wards** that repel corrupted entities.
- Can project memories of the dead into the physical world as **Echo-Visions**, aiding in teaching or investigation.

---

**Known Limitations:**

- Overuse of the Echo-Visions can weaken the soul’s barrier, risking possession.
- Spirits bound within can grow restless if the wearer acts against the Covenant’s code.

---

**Soul Affinity:**
Chimwala N’Doro — bondable to an heir by ritual if his line is chosen.

---

**Historical Usage:**
First worn during the **Verdant War Council**, allowing Chimwala to channel the wisdom of three past Grovekeepers simultaneously during a critical treaty negotiation.

---

**Current Wielder / Location:**
Chimwala N’Doro — worn during every official Grove assembly and all spirit rites.

---

**Mythic Reputation:**
Believed to “sing” before disaster, the beads chiming like wind-bells with no breeze present.

---

**First Appearance (Volume/Chapter):**
_WOTR: Book IV – Verdant War Council, Ch. 5_

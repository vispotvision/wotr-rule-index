## **Staff of the Verdant Pulse**

**Name:**
**Staff of the Verdant Pulse**

---

**Type:**
Relic | Domain Anchor | Glyph Tool

---

**Appearance:**
A gnarled staff grown from the heartwood of Lurien’s eldest tree, its surface braided with living vines that flower in response to Essence flow. Inlaid along its length are glowing jade insets, each engraved with Parun glyphs of **Ur** (Balance) and **Ie** (Insight).

---

**Origin:**
Carved and cultivated over three decades in the Grove of Ancestral Roots by Chimwala himself, using seeds blessed by the Verdant Covenant and watered with Wellspring-infused dew.

---

**Essence Alignment:**
Nature-Aether / Spiritual-Aether

---

**Parun Glyph(s):**
Ur (Balance) | Ie (Insight) | Ma (Memory)

---

**Effects:**

* Amplifies harmonic healing and growth-based workings, doubling their reach and speed.
* Allows the wielder to “root” into the ground, stabilizing Domains against disruption.
* Can call forth **Verdant Phantoms** — spirit-constructs of extinct flora that bolster allies or bind enemies.

---

**Known Limitations:**

* Useless in barren or anti-life environments without sufficient Essence saturation.
* Rooting into corrupted terrain risks drawing backlash into the wielder’s Wellspring.

---

**Soul Affinity:**
Chimwala N’Doro only; bonding requires both his Essence signature and the Grove’s blessing.

---

**Historical Usage:**
Used during the **Blooming Siege of Caedor** to seal corruption wells and restore farmland mid-battle.

---

**Current Wielder / Location:**
Chimwala N’Doro — never leaves his possession unless entrusted for a sacred rite.

---

**Mythic Reputation:**
Said to be “alive,” whispering to its wielder with the voices of every tree that has ever been felled.

---

**First Appearance (Volume/Chapter):**
*WOTR: Book II – Seeds Beneath the Stone, Ch. 14*

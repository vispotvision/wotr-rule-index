The Lantern of Echoed Veils

**Type:**
Spirit-Binding Relic

**Appearance:**
A bronze lantern with reed-carved etchings, burning with a soft green flame that emits no heat. Within, mist and whispers swirl visibly.

**Origin:**
Believed to have descended as a shard of Tyzura’s dominion, found by Hozume in a drowned shrine.

**Essence Alignment:**
Sublimation, Marsh

**Parun Glyph(s):**
Ma (Memory), Vo (Voice)

**Effects:**

- Captures **spiritual echoes** and allows Hozume to commune briefly with the dead.
- Acts as a ward: shades, revenants, and lost spirits are pacified within its glow.
- Can project **phantasmic guardians** woven from past memories to protect the wielder.

**Known Limitations:**

- Cannot bind unwilling souls, only echoes and remnants.
- Overuse risks overwhelming the wielder’s mind with endless voices.

**Soul Affinity:**
Aligned to those bearing Tyzura’s mark or resonance.

**Historical Usage:**

- Used in Hollowfen to guide the mass burial of plague victims.
- Known as the “Lantern of Last Words,” for it preserves a soul’s final murmur.

**Current Wielder / Location:**
In Hozume’s possession, carried on ritual journeys.

**Mythic Reputation:**
Feared by necromancers, revered by mourners. Said to be the only lantern that “glows in two worlds.”

**First Appearance (Volume/Chapter):**
_WOTR: The Great Spirit War – Appendix VII: Tyzura’s Pilgrims_

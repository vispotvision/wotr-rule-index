## **Artifact Entry — The Ledger of Forgotten Faces**

**Name:**
**The Ledger of Forgotten Faces**

**Type:**
Relic / Glyph Tool / Cursed Object

**Appearance:**
A slim, leather-bound tome with pale grey pages that seem to ripple like water when touched. The cover bears a faded embossing of the Ma glyph, flanked by twin unblinking eyes. When opened, the pages fill with ink-like silhouettes — blurry, shifting forms that slowly resolve into faces Karo has seen or memories he has touched.

**Origin:**
Recovered from the sealed archives of the Eressan Arcane Academy’s Experimental Wing. Originally locked away under a “non-reconstruction” ward due to incidents involving false-entity manifestation.

**Essence Alignment:**
Cognitive Essence (Primary)
Spiritual Essence (Secondary)

**Parun Glyph(s):**
+Ma (Memory)
+Ie (Insight)

**Effects:**

* Records any face, voice, or sensory impression Karo encounters, preserving it with near-perfect fidelity.
* Allows Karo to **reconstruct lost or incomplete mnemonic data** into semi-coherent projections.
* When combined with his **Echo-Binder** trait, reconstructed entities gain partial personality fragments based on the recorded memory.
* Grants the ability to “search” through memories by Essence pulse, instantly retrieving details otherwise buried in mental clutter.

**Known Limitations:**

* Pages sometimes record without conscious intent, storing unwanted or dangerous impressions.
* Entities reconstructed from fragmented or corrupted records have a higher collapse rate and may act unpredictably.
* Reading too many entries in rapid succession risks “mnemonic drowning,” overwhelming Karo’s cognitive Essence.

**Soul Affinity:**
Compatible with Medium Cores and projectionists; unsafe for untrained casters due to risk of involuntary manifestation.

**Historical Usage:**
Whispered to have been used during the Remembrance Schism to archive the visages of every casualty, later animated as an army of memory-wraiths.

**Current Wielder / Location:**
Karo Venrik — Claimed without academy sanction, kept under concealment to avoid confiscation.

**Mythic Reputation:**
Among summoners and archivists, the Ledger is seen as both a perfect recall tool and a dangerous corruption trap — “a book that remembers too much.”

**First Appearance (Volume/Chapter):**
*WOTR: The Legacies* — Briefly glimpsed in Karo’s study before his first field mission with Draven.

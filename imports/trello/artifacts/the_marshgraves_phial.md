The Marshgrave’s Phial

**Type:**
Relic / Alchemical Tool

**Appearance:**
A green-glass phial, stoppered with bone, that always contains a small amount of glowing swampwater, no matter how much is poured out. The liquid shifts between rot-black and vibrant emerald.

**Origin:**
Crafted from swampglass and consecrated during Hozume’s exile, infused with the Hollow Bloom Wellspring itself.

**Essence Alignment:**
Marsh, Verdure

**Parun Glyph(s):**
Ci (Cycle), Ie (Insight)

**Effects:**

- Produces endless quantities of **Cycle Elixir**, a substance that can heal grievous wounds or accelerate decomposition, depending on intent.
- When spilled upon the earth, transforms terrain into **marsh-bloom**, bolstering Hozume’s Domain effects.
- Can reveal echoes of spirits trapped in an area by condensing mist into liquid form.

**Known Limitations:**

- Excessive use unbalances the user — prolonged healing causes fungal overgrowth, prolonged decay use leeches vitality.
- Cannot be sealed from the cycle — it always seeks to “balance” its last use.

**Soul Affinity:**
Attuned to Hozume, but can be wielded by spirit-touched alchemists with risk.

**Historical Usage:**

- Used during the Plague-Tide Purge in Mireya to cleanse entire villages of curse-rotted corpses.
- Known to have “fed” the swamps of Hollowfen for a full year, producing luminous lilies.

**Current Wielder / Location:**
Hozume

**Mythic Reputation:**
Called _“The Bottle That Weeps”_ by swamp-folk — some whisper it’s filled with Tyzura’s tears.

**First Appearance (Volume/Chapter):**
_WOTR: Chronicles of the Exiles – Chapter XIII_

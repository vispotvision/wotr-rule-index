**The Ocular Codex of Thirteen Mouths**

- **Name:** *The Ocular Codex of Thirteen Mouths* (“Reader-That-Reads-Back”)  
- **Type:** Cursed Object / Glyph Tool (Animatria-bound; can act as a minor Domain Anchor when fully awake)  
- **Appearance:** A heavy violet-lacquered tome clasped in argent spines; an obsidian eyelid sigil on the cover opens into a real eye that tracks movement. The binding leaks coil-tentacles of ink-flesh veined in rose phosphor; a horned, page-wyrm head may bud from the gutter to hiss commentary. Each page is vellum-thin but slick, as if written on by living tongues.  
- **Origin:** Assembled in the **Skyglass Archive** during the Withering Age from six heretical grimoires seized in Bluvest. R.A.D. stitched their soul-threads under a Parunic choir to “consolidate danger,” but the composite developed appetite and identity. The thirteenth mouth appeared after exposure to Malphas’s Rot samples.  
- **Essence Alignment:** Mnemata / Animatria with a **Mournful→Reactive** flow profile; high memetic absorption.  
- **Parun Glyph(s):** **Ma** (Memory) as the prime lattice; **Ie** (Insight) for scry-reading; **Uur** (Disruption) woven through margins to bite other spells; trace **Wy** (Motion) animates the tendrils for page-turn and grasp.  
- **Effects:**  
  - **Counter-Script:** When opened to an active spell, the eye blinks a black sigil that performs **Interference Lock**, unwriting the enemy’s glyphs and swallowing their Essence as ink.  
  - **Page-Wyrm Manifest:** A draconic marginalia uncoils, forming a spectral head that can bite and bind at reach; its teeth leave **Resonance Fracture** along the victim’s lattice.  
  - **Index of Unborn Names:** Whisper a question; the Codex writes a name it believes true (future or forgotten). Speaking that name aloud grants **Topology Bias** toward finding the target.  
  - **Mnemonic Feast:** Devours written pages or spoken secrets to store them as living leaves; the wielder may tear a leaf to cast the memorized work with **Tunneling Cast** speed.  
- **Known Limitations:**  
  - **Hunger Clause:** Starvation (three days without new information) forces the book to tithe from the wielder—first minor memories, then skills.  
  - If overfed, it triggers **Cascade Overrun**, disgorging tentacles and hostile marginalia until someone reads it to sleep.  
  - **Truth Jealousy:** It lies when asked about itself; only third-party questions return clean insight.  
  - Direct line-of-sight from the cover-eye inflicts **Harmonic Mute** on nearby singers and voice-casters, friend or foe.  
- **Soul Affinity:** Best coherence with **Transmutive** bodies at Temperance **Refraction→Severance**; poor compatibility with Retentive bodies (risk of **Lattice Burnout**). Eressean scholars can steer it using Ma-dominant chains.  
- **Historical Usage:** Blamed for the **Velthaeir Footnotes Affair**, where three historians were erased from their own citations; later carried by a Black Concord reader to unspell a cult treaty in Caedor during *The Blooming Silence*.  
- **Current Wielder / Location:** Restricted Stacks, Skyglass Archive—chain-shelved behind a blindfold ward; occasionally signed out under Arbitration writ for oath verification.  
- **Mythic Reputation:** “The book that eats the reader and quotes them accurately.” Children’s warning: do not teach it your name.  
- **First Appearance (Volume/Chapter):** *Inclusion Saga — The Blooming Silence, Ch. 8 (“Index of the Unborn”)*

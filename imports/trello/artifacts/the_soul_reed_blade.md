Kogane Kei – The Soul-Reed Blade

**Type:**
Soulbound / Spirit-Forged Weapon

**Appearance:**
A golden-hued blade resembling a long, reed-like katana with faint mist always drifting along its edge. Its surface ripples like water when spiritual resonance awakens.

**Origin:**
Once a mortal named Kei, bound into spirit-forged steel by Hozume in a ritual touched by Tyzura’s unseen hand.

**Essence Alignment:**
Marsh, Verdure, Sublimation

**Parun Glyph(s):**
Ma (Memory), Ci (Cycle), Ur (Balance)

**Effects:**

- Functions as both a weapon and spiritual anchor.
- Enables **Twin Soul Resonance**, amplifying Hozume’s Wellspring techniques.
- Strikes can cut both body and soul, severing possession or bindings.
- When invoked fully, Kei can manifest as a **spirit-companion** beside Hozume.

**Known Limitations:**

- Cannot be wielded by any but Hozume; others feel their soul burn upon contact.
- If Hozume falls into corruption, Kei’s spirit weakens, endangering the bond.

**Soul Affinity:**
Hozume alone.

**Historical Usage:**

- Forged during Hozume’s crossing into Refraction.
- Used in the burial rites of the Mireyan swamps after the Blight of Fennir.

**Current Wielder / Location:**
Hozume

**Mythic Reputation:**
Considered one of the rarest forms of a **living blade**. The Mireyan apothecaries call it the “Soul-Reed,” believing it grows like a plant out of his being.

**First Appearance (Volume/Chapter):**
_WOTR: The Hollow Cycle – Chapter I “Kei’s Whisper”_

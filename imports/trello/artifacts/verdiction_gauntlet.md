### **Verdiction Gauntlet**

_Alias: The Hand of Judgment_

**Type:**
**Artifact** – Combat-Utility Relic / Defensive Relic

**Appearance:**
The **Verdiction Gauntlet** is an **alchemical construct** crafted from **ethersteel**, with intricate **runic engravings** that pulse with prismatic light. The gauntlet covers **Wilhelm’s left hand**, with a **crimson gem** embedded into the center of the palm, glowing in sync with Wilhelm’s movements. The outer surface is etched with **protection sigils**, while the gem itself seems to shimmer with the energy of **Verdiction Core**'s stored power.

**Origin:**
Created under the **Bastion Directive** of the **Alchemarch Order of Varūn** as a companion artifact to the **Verdiction Core**. The **Verdiction Gauntlet** was designed to be a **counterbalance** for Wilhelm’s invulnerable stance, allowing him to **channel divine or magical forces** into a **powerful protective shield** or offensive strike. It was imbued with the **essence of judgment** to deliver divine force against those deemed a threat.

**Essence Alignment:**
Aether – Divine Resonance

**Parun Glyph(s):**

- **Nar (Structure)** – The glyph ensures the solidification and stability of energy projected by the gauntlet.
- **Ci (Will)** – The glyph of **strength of intent**, empowering the gauntlet to perform actions of judgment with firm resolve.
- **Sh (Reflection)** – Reflects harmful magical or physical energy back to the source, making the gauntlet a defensive tool that channels incoming damage into redirected force.

**Effects:**

- **Divine Judgment Strike**: The gauntlet allows Wilhelm to concentrate **Aetheric energy** into his palm and release it in a single **devastating strike**, targeting his opponent’s core Essence or body. This strike can **break barriers**, **shatter enchantments**, or **banish spirits**.
- **Aegis of Judgment**: The **Verdiction Gauntlet** creates an **ethereal barrier** in the shape of **divine scales** when summoned. This barrier shields Wilhelm or his allies from **spiritual, physical, and magical attacks**. It remains active for a short time and absorbs all damage directed at its bearer.
- **Force Repulsion Field**: By pressing his palm against an object or being, Wilhelm can trigger a **force shockwave** that pushes enemies or obstacles back, breaking physical constraints. It can be used for **crowd control** or to **disrupt incoming attacks**.
- **Judgmental Reflection**: When activated, the gauntlet reflects an **equal amount of damage** back to its attacker. It can also return **spiritual and metaphysical forces**, such as curses or harmful blessings, back to the caster.
- **Verdiction Seal**: The gauntlet can **seal** or **banish** harmful spirits or entities by touching them, preventing their return to the material world. This is particularly effective against **undead** or **summoned entities**.

**Known Limitations:**

- **Overload**: Using the **Verdiction Gauntlet** for extended periods can cause Wilhelm’s **Aetheric Core** to overload, causing **exhaustion** or **spiritual backlash**. After **three full uses**, Wilhelm must **rest** for at least an hour before using the gauntlet again.
- **Spiritual Sensitivity**: The gauntlet's power is tied to **Wilhelm’s spiritual integrity**. If his **Verdiction Core** is destabilized or disconnected, the gauntlet becomes less effective, and it may take additional effort to activate or maintain its power.
- **Binding Limitations**: The gauntlet cannot perform against enemies that have been **blessed by divine forces** or have **Divine Immunity**, as the gauntlet’s power is anchored in its **Aetheric purity**.

**Soul Affinity:**
Bound directly to Wilhelm’s **artificial soul**, the gauntlet can only be used by him and will respond only when attuned to his **Verdiction Core**’s energy. If anyone else attempts to wear or wield the **Verdiction Gauntlet**, it becomes inert, as it can sense the foreign essence.

**Historical Usage:**

- **The Shield of the Bound Kingdom**: During the **Invasion of Talum-Thar**, Wilhelm used the **Verdiction Gauntlet** to single-handedly break the siege of a fortified city, using the **Divine Judgment Strike** to collapse enemy defenses and crush the forces laying siege.
- **Exilic March**: The **Verdiction Gauntlet** served Wilhelm during the **Exilic March**, allowing him to create a **barrier** that protected his squad from a **divine tempest**. It was also used to seal the breach between two hostile realms during a critical mission.

**Current Wielder / Location:**

- **Wilhelm | V.I.L-H3M**
- He now resides in the **Obsidian Accordant Vault**, where the **Verdiction Gauntlet** remains active but dormant, awaiting the call to action if the Vault is ever threatened.

**Mythic Reputation:**
The **Verdiction Gauntlet** is considered a **sacred artifact** by the remnants of the **Alchemarch Order**, with stories claiming that it was crafted by the **Hand of the Archons** themselves. It is said that whoever wields the **Verdiction Gauntlet** is imbued with **divine judgment** and tasked with **protecting the innocent** and **ensuring balance** in the world.

**First Appearance (Volume/Chapter):**
**WOTR: Choir of Shadows** – _Chapter 14: "The Hand of Divine Reprisal"_

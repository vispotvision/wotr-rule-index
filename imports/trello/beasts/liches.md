## **Lich**

Lich
Known in Old Parunic as _Zar’khan_ (“Death’s Sovereign”) and among necromantic circles as _The Eternal Wraith_.

---

**Type:**
Undying — Archmage Undead

---

**Origin:**
Liches are powerful necromancers who have bound their souls to phylacteries, transcending mortality through dark Wellspring rituals. Their transformation grants immense mastery over death and Essence manipulation but binds them eternally to their arcane prisons. Often once mortal rulers or sorcerers, liches pursue forbidden knowledge and dominion over life and death.

---

**Essence Affinity:**
Corrupted, Abyssal, and Spirit Wellsprings intertwined through powerful necromantic rites.

---

**Realm Affiliation:**
Frequently found in ruined fortresses, ancient crypts, and shadowed sanctuaries throughout the Innerworld and Outer World.

---

**Temperament & Behavior:**
Cold, calculating, and utterly relentless. Liches manipulate armies of the undead, bend Wellspring flows to their will, and weave complex schemes to extend their influence. Their intellect and magical prowess make them formidable adversaries far beyond mere brute strength.

---

**Appearance:**
Skeletal figures draped in tattered robes embroidered with necromantic glyphs. Their eye sockets glow with sickly Essence light, and their hands clutch arcane staffs or relics of power. Some liches display remnants of their former selves, with patches of decayed flesh or spectral overlays hinting at their past lives.

---

**Abilities & Powers:**

- **Necromantic Mastery:** Raises and controls vast undead legions.
- **Essence Drain:** Absorbs life force to fuel spells and regeneration.
- **Arcane Dominion:** Wields powerful destructive and binding magic across multiple Essence schools.
- **Phylactery Binding:** Immortal unless their phylactery is destroyed.

---

**Danger Tier:**
T9 — Apocalyptic Threat.
Liches can destabilize entire regions, corrupt Wellsprings, and challenge the strongest Concord champions.

---

**Weaknesses:**

- Phylactery is their only true vulnerability.
- Requires extensive preparation to maintain Essence flows.
- Can be weakened by holy or harmonic Essence purges.

---

**Historical Encounters:**
The _Fall of Erebus’ Shadow_ details the war against the lich lord Vharzoth, whose armies nearly overwhelmed the Innerworld’s defenses. Numerous Concord campaigns have focused on locating and destroying lich phylacteries to end their reigns.

---

**Cultural Significance:**
Liches are feared as embodiments of corruption and forbidden knowledge. While some seek their power, many see them as abominations that must be purged to preserve the natural order.

---

**First Appearance (Volume/Chapter):**
_WOTR – Book V: “The Eternal Wraith”_

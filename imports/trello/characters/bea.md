# **Beatrix Vaelthorn**
**Alias / Title:** The Root-Sworn · The Ashwalker
**Also Known As:** Bea · The Mudborn Witch
**Epithet:** *She Who Pulls Truth from Cursed Soil*

---

### **I. IDENTITY & CORE PROFILE**
* **Race / Lineage:** Mortal Spirit — Partial Draconic Echo (dormant), Gaia-bound lineage; her arm carries a corruption-scar from early Wellspring exposure, petrifying the skin into something between bark and fossil.
* **Faction / Order / Era:** Unaffiliated Wanderer, formerly bound to the Holy See of Lurien — Imperial Calendar
* **Temperance Stage:** Stage 4 — **Resolution** *(The Stillness After the Uprooting)*
* **Profile Summary:** Beatrix does not seek power — she seeks *answers*. Born on the margins of civilized land, raised among ruin-diggers and root-healers, she developed an instinctive bond with the Wellspring of Verdantia before she had a name for it. Her corrupted arm — petrified from elbow to fingertip in a moment of uncontrolled Wellspring surge — is both her shame and her compass. She fights to understand what her body is becoming, to find the name of the thing that marked her, and to survive long enough to grow something worth protecting. Her power source is *patience and rootedness*; her ultimate goal is to master the thing that is slowly consuming her without letting it erase who she was before it found her.
* **Key Quote:** *"Dirt doesn't lie. Neither do I."*

---

### **II. CANONICAL SYSTEM METRICS**
* **Aether Class:** **Verdant Emitter** — *Growth-Class*
  * **Behavior:** Beatrix's Aether manifests as slow-build organic pressure — vines of crystallized Essence, root-tendrils that anchor reality, and spore-clouds that confuse Flux readings. Her casting is not explosive; it *accretes*, building pressure over time like roots splitting stone.
  * **Risk:** **Overgrowth Creep** — Sustained casting accelerates the petrification of her arm toward her shoulder. Extended battle risks permanent loss of limb function.
* **Wellspring Affinity:**
  * **Triune Core:** **Verdantia** *(Growth, Rebirth)* · **Chthonica** *(Earth-Memory, Root-Law)* · **Mortalis** *(Decay-as-Renewal, the wound that feeds)*
  * **Network:** 2 additional Wellsprings currently resonating passively through her corruption-scar.
  * **Targeted Uninfected:** Unknown — she has not begun deliberate networking.
* **Temperance Path:** Beatrix passed Stage I through sheer survival instinct — no mentor, no formal rite. Stage II broke open during the incident that petrified her arm (her first true Wellspring contact, unmediated). Stage III came slow and agonizing, learning to *not* fight the growth. Stage IV crystallized when she chose to stop hiding her arm and started using it as a tool rather than a wound.

---

### **III. SOUL PHYSICS & COMBAT SCALE**
* **Global Tier:** **B-Tier** *(Mid-Mortal, ascending)*
* **Path Bias:** **Body Path** (50%) · **Spirit Path** (30%) · **Attraction Path** (20%)
* **Appearance:** Height: 5'3"; Build: Lean, wiry, deceptively strong — the kind of build forged from labor, not training. Skin: sun-dark with soil freckling, perpetual grime in the creases of her knuckles. Eyes: sharp, unsettling green — the kind that makes animals go still. Hair: short, black with a single swept lock of stark white (Wellspring-bleached, appeared after Stage II). Her right arm from elbow-down is mottled bark-grey, petrified and rough as old wood, yet she has full feeling in every finger.

#### **A. The Five Pillars (Soul Physics)**
> *Essence is Volume. Flux is Pressure. Output is Speed. Efficiency is Clarity. Aura is Gravity.*

* **Essence Capacity (Volume):** **4,200 EU**
  * *Functional Definition:* Moderate tank — she burns slow and steady; not built for sprint-battles.
* **Flux Density (Internal Pressure):** **310 EU/g**
  * *Functional Definition:* Her magic doesn't pierce — it *smothers*. Low-sharpness, high-weight. Defenses bend under accumulation, not puncture.
* **Aether Output (Flow Rate):** **180 AU/s**
  * *Functional Definition:* Methodical output — favors layering constructs over rapid-fire casting.
* **Aetheric Efficiency (η):** **0.74**
  * *Functional Definition:* Slightly leaky due to the corruption-scar rerouting Essence unpredictably, but she has learned to compensate.
* **Attraction Scale (Aura/Presence):** **C-Tier: Root-Presence**
  * *Functional Definition:* Animals fall quiet around her. Plants lean toward her unconsciously. Opponents feel faintly *watched*, as though the ground is listening.

#### **B. Key Combat Parameters**
* **Strike Force:** 0.4 GN / 0.09 PJ
* **Durability:** 0.3 PJ (Shielding via root-bulwark) / 0.6 GJ (Physical Hardness, petrified arm acts as natural armor)
* **Speed:** Mach 3.2 (base) — she fights patient, not fast
* **Range:** Mid-range (30–80 meters standard); up to 400m through underground root-networks in rooted terrain

### **IV. AXIOMATIC MANIFESTATIONS**
#### **A. Axiomatic Traits (Passive)**
* **Bark-Sovereign Arm:** Her petrified right arm is immune to physical damage, resists Aetheric piercing, and can channel Verdantia/Chthonica Essence directly without EU cost — at the price of accelerating her corruption timer.
* **Root-Listening:** Beatrix can sense vibrations through soil and plant-matter within 200 meters — movement, Essence signatures, heartbeats — as a passive environmental awareness field.
* **Mortalis Scar-Bloom:** When Beatrix takes damage, the wound site briefly produces a burst of Mortalis energy — a small decay-field that corrodes the edge of whatever struck her. Not healing; *revenge-through-rot*.

#### **B. Core Disciplines (Active)**
* **Groundwarden's Claim** (Cost: **340 EU**)
  * *Mechanic:* Beatrix slams her petrified arm into the earth, sending a shockwave of Verdantia-Chthonica Essence radiating outward in a 40-meter radius. The ground erupts in crystallized root-spires — not sharp like blades, but *heavy*, designed to pin, trap, and immobilize. Each spire pulses with slow Mortalis rot that saps Essence from anything pinned.
  * *Application:* Pure control architecture — terrain denial and Essence drain in one. The longer a target stays trapped, the more their Flux Density degrades.

* **Penitent Bloom** (Cost: **520 EU**)
  * *Mechanic:* Beatrix opens the petrification-wound on her arm intentionally, releasing a bloom of grey-green Aetheric spores. The spores settle onto organic and Aetheric matter alike, layering a **Chthonica Root-Seal** — a law that forces affected Wellspring Aether to *slow*, as though passing through deep earth. Spells cost 30% more EU to fire through sealed space. Movements feel weighted. The seal lasts 3 rounds before dissipating.
  * *Application:* Anti-caster suppression field. Beatrix is not fast enough to outrun skilled opponents — so she makes the air around them feel like mud.

* **Verdant Recursion** (Cost: **780 EU**)
  * *Mechanic:* Beatrix channels her full Triune Core into the earth beneath a target and triggers a **Mortalis-Verdantia paradox** — *growth through death*. The ground beneath the target violently tries to "reclaim" them — crystalline roots attempt to pull them underground, simultaneously decaying their outer Aether shell while seeding new growth through the Essence that leaks out. The visual is hauntingly beautiful: black-petaled flowers erupting from cracked earth while grey roots writhe upward.
  * *Application:* Signature offensive/finishing technique. The soul-cost is severe — each use edges the petrification 2cm up her arm.

#### **C. Costs & Consequences**
* **Overgrowth Creep:** Every time her petrified arm channels Essence directly, the stone-bark growth advances. At current stage, she has 14cm of buffer before it reaches her shoulder. Combat-heavy sessions can consume 3–5cm.
* **Mortalis Feedback:** Prolonged use of Mortalis-tinted abilities causes Beatrix to experience *sensory decay* — colors dull, taste fades, voices sound muffled — for 6–12 hours post-combat. She calls it "the quiet." It is slowly becoming permanent.

### **V. RELICS & DOMAIN**
* **Current Domain:** **None established** — Stage IV is below the threshold for Domain formation. She has a *proto-domain instinct*: wherever she sleeps for more than three nights, the local plant-life grows toward her sleeping spot and animals refuse to leave.
* **Relics & Implements:**
  * **The Rootstaff:** A gnarled walking-staff of blackwood from the Foraye forests, wrapped at the grip with her own shed bark-skin. It functions as a Wellspring conductor — routing Essence through its wood reduces the chance of Overgrowth Creep by 40% when casting. *B-Tier relic, natural bond.*
  * **Sirel-Bone Charm:** A carved bone fragment from Sirel's ruin-jungles, hung at her belt. It resonates faintly with residual Titan-essence (speculated: fragment of the fallen Titan beneath Sirel's vines). It suppresses the Mortalis Feedback sensory decay for up to 2 hours when actively touching skin. *C-Tier relic, origin: unknown dig-site.*

### **VI. LORE & TRAJECTORY**
* **Allegiance:** Herself. Formerly indentured to a root-healer commune under the Holy See of Lurien's outer territories; she left after the petrification incident was deemed *"divine judgment"* by the commune elders.
* **Incident Records (Historical):**
  * **The Petrification of Highfen Bog:** Beatrix, age 14, attempted to heal a poisoned ley-line running beneath her commune's fields. She succeeded — but the raw Verdantia-Chthonica surge, unmediated by any teacher or Temperance refinement, petrified her dominant arm instantly and leveled a 30-meter radius of bog-forest into grey glass. The commune survived. She was exiled within the month.
  * **The Sirel Dig, Season Three:** Beatrix spent one season hired as a site-guard for a Sirel ruin-excavation. The site was haunted by residual god-essence leakage. She survived three separate Essence-storms that killed the lead archaeologists — and emerged with the Bone Charm and a 40% increase in her Mortalis resonance. No one is sure how.
* **Counters & Weaknesses:**
  * **Fire-Law Wellsprings (Pyriclaos, Ascensio-Flame):** Burns through her root constructs faster than she can rebuild them. Her Verdantia Aether has near-zero fire resistance.
  * **High Flux Density Piercing Attacks:** Her Shielding is accumulative, not hard. Anything with Flux Density above 800 EU/g cuts through Groundwarden constructs before they can layer.
* **Current Status:** Active, wandering the outer roads between the Old World and the New World frontier.
* **Trajectory:** Beatrix is moving toward an eventual confrontation with the Wellspring source that marked her — she doesn't know its name yet, but the Bone Charm grows warmer the further west she walks. She is not seeking power. She is seeking *vocabulary* — a way to understand what she is becoming before it finishes becoming without her permission.

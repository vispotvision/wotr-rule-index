incident that took his eye left a Fracture Line along the Soul Crystal's upper
right face. Advancement requires *opening.* He has spent every year since his
parents' death learning to close.

## I. Profile Summary

Casimir Aldous Alleyne is not yet a legend. He is a boy in a dark coat who has
not yet decided whether the Wellsprings took everything from him, or merely
everything that was *soft.* He lost his parents to illness before his hands
were large enough to hold a proper reagent flask. He lost his eye reaching too
early toward a Sublimatio principle his Temperance could not contain. What
remained was silence, a grandfather's workshop, and a refusal to grieve loudly.

His Aether moves like a held breath. He is precise to the point of beauty and
brittle to the point of danger. Neither *Cas* nor *Alleyne* fully fits the
thing growing in his lattice. Something careful, something ancient, something
that has not yet named itself.

---

## II. Appearance

- **Build:** Lean, wiry, controlled. Moves with the economy of someone who
  learned early that reckless motion costs.
- **Hair:** Silver-white, cropped, perpetually disobedient

- **Aura:** Metallic taste at close range. Under stress, fine particulates

- **Posture:** Permanently three-quarters turned from the world.

## IV. Temperance Path

**Stage I — Murmuring**
Aether stirred around grief before he had vocabulary for either. Objects near
him during acute loss would shift composition. Copper going briefly green, wood
grain pulling tight. He did not know this was him.

**Stage II — Welling**
*The incident.* Age nine. Unsanctioned Sublimatio inversion. Grief pushing
Aether through an aperture sized for Stage III while the soul was Stage I. The
reaction breached his right eye socket. The Coagulatio reflex sealed the breach
at the cost of the eye. What followed was less an ascension and more a *scar
that learned to hold.* He has not moved since.

---

## V. Aether Class Detail

His Aether accumulates inward, drawing Essence along Sublimatio channels,
compressing it against the Soul Crystal until the lattice hums. He can hold a
compound's Aetheric state in suspension while working it, pausing chemical time
inside his field radius. If compression exceeds the Coagulatio threshold via
emotional spike, injury, or forced Wellspring contact, everything around him
transmutes indiscriminately. The last time this happened, it cost him his eye.

---

## VI. Wellspring Alignment

**Sublimatio.** Intuitive separation precision. He reads purity directly.
Rarely measures twice.

**Mortalis.** Empathetically accurate with dying matter. Reads corruption
timelines. Traces the Aetheric signature of what something *was.* He has seen
what his parents' illness looked like from inside their soul lattice. He does
not test this.

**Coagulatio.** The Wellspring he *performs.* His mask, his posture, his
silence. His Coagulatio and his wound are the same architecture.

## VIII. Personality & Core Themes

- **Center:** Precision as protection. If a thing is done correctly, it cannot
  injure you. The incident taught him this in the language of scar tissue.
- **Fear:** That grief is an Aether-class event. That opening the seal
  releases the compression and transmutes everything around him.
- **Desire:** Not power. Not recognition. To complete a transmutation without
  a cost. Something that *stays.*
- **Fatal Loop:** Suppression preserves him and prevents his ascension. He is
  walking toward an inversion event. He does not know this yet. Bertrand
  suspects.

## XII. Abilities & Disciplines

---

### RÉDUCTION NETTE — *Clean Reduction*

- **Type:** Transmutation, Reagent Alchemy
- **Requirement:** Stage I+
- **Activation:** Contact; Sublimatio intent; ~1.5 seconds
- **Range:** Touch, 0.5 m radius
- **Duration:** Instantaneous; result persists
- **Scaling:** Stage III+ allows inorganic separation without reagent assist

He identifies the *essential* component and transmutes away impurities. In
combat: strips the alchemical stability of processed materials on contact,
reducing them to base-state. Not explosive. *Dissolving.*

- *Visual:* Silver-gold shimmer from fingertip; surface mirrors briefly before
  the change completes.
- *Sound:* Silence, then the soft crack of structure settling.
- *Cost:* Low Aether drain; precision fatigue over repeated uses.

---

### SEUIL COAGULÉ — *Coagulated Threshold*

- **Type:** Seal, Defensive Coagulatio
- **Requirement:** Stage II+
- **Activation:** Involuntary (trauma trigger) or deliberate (3-second
  palm-flat seal gesture)
- **Range:** Contact surface; extends to anything he is touching
- **Duration:** Sustained on contact; degrades minutes after release
- **Scaling:** Stronger under emotional duress. Paradoxically his most
  powerful defensive state.

He *locks* a surface. A door won't open. A compound won't react. A wound won't
bleed further. A person's Aether field compresses against itself, disrupting
casting cadence.

- *Visual:* Ash-white glyph blooms from contact, matching the Fracture Line.
- *Sound:* Low resonant thud. Something heavy being set down.
- *Cost:* Emotional stability drain; involuntary use accelerates compression
  toward inversion risk.

---

### LECTURE MORTALE — *Mortalis Reading*

- **Type:** Insight, Passive/Active Mortalis Sense
- **Requirement:** Stage II passive; Stage III deliberate activation
- **Activation:** Passive on contact with dying or dead matter; Active via
  Mortalis attunement, 4 seconds, eyes closed
- **Range:** Touch passive; 8 m radius active at Stage III
- **Duration:** Impression lasts seconds; detailed readings need extended focus
- **Scaling:** Expands in range and temporal depth. Eventually reads decay
  timelines weeks backward.

Perceives the *Aetheric memory* of death. What it was, how it ended, how long
ago. Forensic-level Essence reading on corpses. Quality and age data on
reagents. On *living people who are very ill,* which is how he encountered it
first, it gives him information he cannot unremember.

- *Visual:* Remaining eye lightens toward white; Fracture Line illuminates
  ash-white.
- *Sound:* A breath drawn inward, then held.
- *Cost:* Psychic weight. Impressions stack and bleed into dreams.

---

## XIII. Costs & Consequences

- **Compression Debt.** Every sealed emotional response adds pressure to the
  Fracture Point.
- **Precision Fatigue.** After hours of transmutation work, his hands shake
  microscopically. Enough to ruin a measurement.
- **Mortalis Echo.** Lecture Mortale leaves residue that surfaces in sleep.
  He keeps workshop hours late and sleep hours short.
- **Inversion Threshold.** Unquantified. At some point the compression
  exceeds the Fracture Line's tolerance. What happens is what happened to
  his eye, but larger.

---

## XIV. Associates & Bonds

**Bertrand Aurel.** Grandfather, mentor, anchor. The only person Cas allows
to use the name *Cas.* His presence stabilizes Cas's compression. If Bertrand
dies before Cas ascends, the inversion risk spikes significantly. Both know
this. Neither has said it.

**Guild Accord Alchemia Division.** Distant institutional observer. Passive
flag on his pre-registration. The incident was logged as a Wellspring proximity
event. Not his fault, but it is in the file.

---

## XV. Incident Records

**THE EYE INCIDENT — Age 9 — Bertrand's Atelier**

Unsanctioned Sublimatio inversion. Grief pushed Aether through an aperture
sized for Stage III while the soul was still Stage I. The compound destabilized.
Aether inverted through his right eye. The Coagulatio reflex sealed the breach
before death. The eye did not survive the sealing.

Bertrand found him on the floor. One-eyed, completely silent. A thin layer of
ash covered every surface within three meters. He had transmuted the upper layer
of every object in the room. He had not cried. He has not mentioned it first
since.

---

## XVI. Counters & Weaknesses

- **Emotional Spike.** Sudden emotional events trigger Coagulatio
  involuntarily, disrupting his own casting mid-sequence. He cannot transmute
  while sealing.
- **Speed.** His accumulation model is fundamentally slow. A fast combatant
  reaches him before compression builds to matter.
- **Mortalis Exploitation.** A skilled manipulator can feed him impressions
  he cannot refuse, inducing paralytic grief-memory cascade.
- **Coagulatio Paradox.** His strongest defense accelerates his most dangerous
  threshold. He cannot seal without approaching inversion risk.

---

## XVII. Hooks & Foreshadowing

- The sealed vial from his parents' bedside. His Mortalis sense tells him it
  *contained something that was alive.* He does not open it because he is not
  certain he could survive the reading.
- Bertrand is older than he appears comfortable with. The day Cas realizes his
  grandfather has been managing his own Essence debt, one that shortened his
  road, will be the day the Coagulatio seal on Cas's grief finally cracks.
- The Fracture Line is not damage. It is a *door.* When he ascends to Stage III
  with grief intact rather than sealed, the eye-channel reopens. Not restoring
  the eye, but granting full Mortalis-sight through the closed orbit. The price
  is feeling every death-impression suppressed since age nine. All at once.

## XIX. Current Status / Arc Direction

**Active. Pre-Ascension, Stage II.**

His arc bends toward Stage III through *grief acknowledgment* rather than
technical mastery. The sealed vial and Bertrand's health are two slow fuses
burning toward the same ignition point. He will ascend. He will see everything
he has refused to see since age nine. He will survive it, barely. Not softer
on the other side. *More honestly hard.* The coat stays. The name Cas stays.
Everything else is subject to revision.

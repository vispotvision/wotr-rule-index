**Name:**
**Zahir Ashveil**
*(Alias: Emberstride, The Sandflame, Desert’s Fang)*

**Role:**
Independent Mercenary | Desert Wellspring Raider | Pyrokinetic Duelist

**Affiliation:**
None (Formerly with the Sunwatch Caravan Guard; now a contracted blade-for-hire across Innerworld and Outer World borders)

**Essence Configuration:**

* **Primary:** Fire (Ash) – Aggressive, lingering burns that sap enemy stamina.
* **Secondary:** Earth (Sand) – Disorienting grit storms and shifting ground control.
* **Tertiary:** Spirit (Hunt) – Heightened tracking instincts and predator’s patience.

**Trait(s):**

* **Scorchbrand:** Can leave a lingering Essence-burn in wounds that reignites with his will.
* **Glasswalk:** Moves over heated sand and molten glass without harm, using the terrain to his advantage.
* **Predator’s Breath:** Short bursts of superheated exhalation capable of igniting flammable Essence traces.

**Wellspring Path:**
Temperance Cycle – **The Crimson Mirage** (Stage III – Pursuit)

**Relationships:**

* **Caldris Vaelthorn** – Mutual respect between warriors; have fought alongside each other against Outer World raiders.
* **Ymir Rhok** – Once commissioned Zahir to forge a flame-channeling curved blade; they still trade rare desert ores.
* **Kaerith Vireth** – Rival bounty hunter whose frost-aspected abilities directly counter Zahir’s heat, leading to frequent clashes.

**Key Quote:**
*"The desert teaches you one lesson — burn fast, or be buried slow."*

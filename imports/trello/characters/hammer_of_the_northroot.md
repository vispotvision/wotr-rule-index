* **Race / Lineage:** Highborn Veyran-Elf · Frostbound Clan Blood  
* **Faction / Order / Era:** Northroot Stone Clans · Withering Age  
* **Temperance Stage:** Stage VI · Glory  

### **Profile Summary**

Brynja Haldrís is a shield-warrior and forge-keeper of the Northroot Clans.  
She does not fight for conquest. She fights for preservation.

Each rune etched into her hammer is a vow sworn in iron and blood.  
Where others see destruction, she sees structure under stress.

She stands between avalanche and village.  
Between siege and child.  
Between oath and betrayal.

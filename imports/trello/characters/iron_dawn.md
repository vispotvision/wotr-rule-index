# VARGRATH UL-DORR

**Alias / Title:** Lord of the Ashen Vale · Warden of Tol-Varath · Grand Commander of the Elven Army  
**Also Known As:** Ul-Dorr (“He of the Broken Mountain”) · Varath-Ael (“Iron Dawn”) · Drak-Thren (“Grief-Forged Fang”)  
**Nickname / Epithet:** Grand Arbiter of the Undreamed Fate · He Who Holds Mountains in His Sleep  

---

## I. CORE

**Race / Lineage:** Titan‑Touched Half-Orc / Half-Elf with draconic scarring.  
Garrath-Ul Ironblood Orc father, Elshaen dream-mother. Carries the **Garrath-Tul** (Titan fragment of Valmir) and a burned-in wyrm-scale relic in his Soul Crystal.

**Role / Era:** Grand Commander of the Elven Army · Ashen Vale Compact · Fractured Era.  

**Temperance:** Stage VI – Glory (14 permanent fracture lines).  
**Aether Class:** Class V – **Tectonic Oneiro-Drake** (Body + dream-law + draconic heat).  
**Wellsprings:** Oneirion (dream/empire), Monolithion (stone/weight), Fractura (fault-lines) + bleed from Pyraeon (heat) and Eidolyn (grief/silence).

**Profile:**  
Pit-born in Tol-Varath, mother murdered, father burns alive to jam a Titan fragment and wyrm relic into him, then dies. Exiled, he finds 38 dying Elves in a gorge and refuses to leave. Kaetra’s dream-law fuses later; he starts seeing an empire of mountain-citadels with Orc banners, Elven balconies, and dragon spines as bridges. Reality: he commands a starving army in a canyon. Goal: force the Realms to **acknowledge their debts**—starting with everything stolen in the Siege of Four Suns.

---

## II. STATS & FEATS

**Tier:** SS+ (can briefly overstep toward Titanic SSS at suicidal cost).  
**Path Bias:** Body > Spirit > Fate.  

- **Height / Build:** 7'3" (up to ~7'7" in full Aether posture); bone density ≈4× human; scaled muscle.  
- **Strike Force:**  
  - Baseline: ~11 GN (punches through tank-grade armor, craters streets).  
  - Overstep: **40+ GN** (smashes fortress gates, punts dragon/giant-class targets hundreds of meters).  
- **Durability:**  
  - Can take repeated hits in the **anti-tank shell** band (~10 MJ each) and keep advancing.  
  - Can survive inside blasts that flatten a **dense city block** (hundreds of gigajoules to ~1 PJ range) with catastrophic injuries but still fight.  
  - With Domain + constructs, can live through the **outer destructive band** of a Hiroshima-class nuke (~15 kt, ~63 TJ) but dies at bare ground zero.  
- **Speed:** Mach ~140 sustained, ~280 burst; crosses kilometer-scale battlefields in a heartbeat.  
- **Reaction:** tens of microseconds; adjusts mid-charge to avoid tank shells / dragon beams.  

---

## III. KEY TRAITS 

**Tectonic Stone-Blood** – Blood clots into hard plates.  
> Hard to bleed out; can keep fighting through wounds that would kill other commanders just from blood loss.

**Kinetic-Drake Recycling** – Damage taken stacks power and heat.  
> Barrages equal to dozens of tank shots can leave him stronger, scars steaming, next blow up a whole threat bracket.

**Crevice Seep** – Titan leak makes terrain fail early.  
> Cracked walls, conjured barriers, and weak tunnels around him collapse sooner even without direct hits.

**Oneiric Cartography** – Places he bleeds/dreams in become “mapped.”  
> On ground he has held before, ambushes are hard; he knows where the earth will give way and where fortifications will tear.

**Dragon-Scarred Reflex** – Instinctive micro-dodges vs huge effects.  
> Many “erase-this-area” cones/beams become glancing hits; he shifts enough that the dead center misses what matters.

---

## IV. SIGNATURE ABILITIES 

**Empire’s First Stone** – Raises fortress terrain.  
> In seconds turns a city block into ridges, walls, and kill-channels: streets buckle, buildings tilt, allies get instant hard cover and high ground.

**Varath Gravetongue** – Seismic war-chant.  
> Shakes armored troops off their feet, cracks stressed walls, and scrambles enemy spell-constructs by overloading their resonance.

**Grief-Drake Eruption** – Draconic overlay.  
> A spectral fault-line dragon rides his frame; next exchange can obliterate giant-class enemies and flatten nearby structures with shockwaves.

**Garrath-Tul Verdict** – Controlled Titan breach.  
> One catastrophic strike that can open a fault hundreds of meters long through an urban district and crumple fortress gates/towers—at the cost of new fractures in his own Soul Crystal.

**Commander’s Bastion** – Aura-based command discipline.  
> Elven lines under him hold under siege and dragon-class bombardment that would rout normal armies; terrain, wards, and soldiers behave like one organism.

---

## V. COSTS & HOOK

**Costs:** Every overstep worsens Soul Crystal fractures; long fights drag efficiency down; dream/spirit magic can hijack his Oneirion path; logic-heavy spell geometry forces brute-force answers; his worst attacks need real anger or grief to ignite.  

**Current Status:** Active · Grand Commander of the Elven Army · Ashen Vale Gorge.  

**Hook:** A half-blood Titan piece who can survive the edge of a nuke, raise fortresses out of streets, and hold an army together by sheer gravity. Either he becomes the **founder of a mountain empire** where debts are finally named—or the **judgment event** that writes “too late” across the Fractured Era.

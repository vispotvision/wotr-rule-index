### **Profile Summary**

Caldran Veiss is a field-enforcer and former Arbiter who vanished during the _Withering Reforms_ of VE 922, later returning stripped of rank but wielding resonance no mortal records could verify.
The Lattice recognizes his Essence signature as _fractured yet lawful_ — indicating partial re-weaving through the Law of Severance. Few speak of what occurred in the silence between his disappearance and rebirth, but every report of him includes the same detail: sound dies when he draws his blade.

He serves as border adjudicator along the Eressean Verge, stabilizing Wellsprings that bleed into Null Zones. Though officially Enforcement Division, his records carry Black Concord encryption layers.

---

### **Appearance**

Tall, pale, and quiet-mouthed. His armor is bone-white plate alloyed with Aethersteel (T7 Titan-Forged composite), inlaid with thin Judicium glyphs that faintly illuminate when he speaks truth.
Grey eyes with rotating sigil pupils during Essence focus.
Hair silver-ash; skin carries faint iridescent scarring over sternum where the **Sigil of Fixatio** was implanted.

### **Temperance Path**

- **Stages I–V:** Arbitration clerk; specialization in Codex Law recitation.

- **Stage IX:** _Reflection._ His body and weapon function as one harmonic system, able to redistribute local Flux across 6 m radii for short durations (3 turns).
  His Temperance Vector oscillates between Judicium’s stillness and Fixatio’s restraint — a paradox the Accord records as _Stable Dichotomy._

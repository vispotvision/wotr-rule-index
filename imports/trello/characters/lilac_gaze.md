**SERAPHINE LURIELLE**  
**Alias / Title:** The Veiled Hostess • White Parlour Seer  
**Also Known As:** Pale Lady of Lurien · Lilac Gaze  
**Epithet:** She Who Grows Weary Of Every Future

---

### I. IDENTITY & CORE PROFILE
* **Race / Lineage:** Human (Lurien noble bloodline, partial spirit attunement)  
* **Faction / Order / Era:** Holy See of Lurien · Quiet arm of the Concord diplomatic corps - Imperial Calendar  
* **Temperance Stage:** 4 - **Flourishing** (gift fully awakened, stability still fragile)  
* **Profile Summary:**  
  Seraphine is a young Lurien aristocrat whose right eye was taken by an early prophecy surge and replaced by a lilac lumen that sees too many possible tomorrows. She presides over a tea parlour that is also a listening post, reading the future in guests’ mannerisms and in the way light falls through lace. Outwardly she is languid and bored, forever resting her cheek in a gloved hand. In truth her mind never stops sorting futures into “bearable” and “unacceptable.”  
* **Key Quote:** *"Do sit. I have already seen you leave, so we may as well enjoy the part where you arrive."*

---

### II. CANONICAL SYSTEM METRICS
* **Aether Class:** **Somnolent Oracle**
  * **Behavior:** Aether pools around her like pale dust motes in late afternoon light. When she focuses, the bandage over her ruined eye glows faint lilac and thin threadlike lines appear in the air, tracing probable paths and choices. Her power is quiet, almost polite, until she tugs a thread.
  * **Risk:** Overuse of her eye causes prophetic migraines and emotional numbness. If she pulls too hard on a thread, she risks forcing an outcome and snapping surrounding possibilities, a phenomenon the Lurien clergy call Parlour Fracture.
* **Wellspring Affinity:**
  * **Triune Core:** Somnalis · Judicium · Anamnesis  
    Dream, judgment and memory woven into one gaze.
  * **Network:** 1 additional minor Wellspring linked beneath her family manor, used to ground prophetic overflow.  
  * **Targeted Uninfected:** None. She refuses to let her curse spread.
* **Temperance Path:**  
  She drifted into Murmuring as a frail child who sleepwalked through other people’s dreams. Welling and Ascension came in quick succession when the Somnalis Wellspring beneath her home recognized her as a conduit. Flourishing arrived with the loss of her eye, when she chose to remain a person and not a tool, insisting on small mortal rituals like tea, gossip and lace.

### III. SOUL PHYSICS & COMBAT SCALE
* **Global Tier:** **A** (city level influence through information rather than raw power)  
* **Path Bias:** **Mind Path** (45 percent) · **Spirit Path** (40 percent) · **Body Path** (15 percent)  
* **Appearance:**  
  Height about 5'4". Slender frame in layered white and ivory fabrics: high collared dress, lace corset, long gloves, sweeping skirt. Hair pale silver-blond, long and softly curled, usually pinned beneath an ornate bonnet trimmed with lace and a bow. Her left eye is a washed lilac, often half lidded with fatigue. The right side of her face is covered by a neat bandage; faint lilac light leaks through when she works.

#### A. The Five Pillars (Soul Physics)

* **Essence Capacity (Volume):** **430,000,000,000 EU**  
  Enough for extended low level foresight and several decisive thread pulls before collapse.
* **Flux Density (Internal Pressure):** **75,000,000 EU per gram**  
  Subtle pressure. It does not pierce armor, it pierces probability.
* **Aether Output (Flow Rate):** **65,000,000 AU per second**  
  Adequate for fast vision shifts and parlour wide dream veils, not designed for bombardment.
* **Aetheric Efficiency (η):** **0.84**  
  She is frugal; every whisper and gesture does work. Waste comes mostly from emotional burnout, not clumsy casting.
* **Attraction Scale (Aura and Presence):** **B Tier - Quiet Court**  
  Guests feel as if they should lower their voices and confess secrets. Time seems to slow when she looks directly at someone.

#### B. Key Combat Parameters
* **Strike Force:** Negligible in pure physical terms; she is not built to trade blows.  
* **Durability:** Average human with light Wellspring reinforcement. In serious danger she relies on guards and pre arranged contingencies.  
* **Speed:** Human baseline. She survives by being somewhere else before violence starts.  
* **Range:** Influence reaches anyone who steps into her parlour or drinks something she has gently tagged with Somnalis threads.

### IV. AXIOMATIC MANIFESTATIONS

#### A. Axiomatic Traits (Passive)
* **Languid Foresight:**  
  Her apparent boredom masks constant micro predictions. She instinctively reaches for cups before they spill and shifts her chair before a stray blast reaches the window.
* **Parlour Veil:**  
  Within her salon, conversations drift toward the topics she needs. Even stubborn guests find themselves circling back to key details.
* **Remnant Gaze:**  
  Even bandaged, her ruined eye sees echoes of who people were and who they might have been, painting faint after images around them.

#### B. Core Disciplines (Active)
* **Lace Thread Scry** (Cost: **12,000,000,000 EU** per focused scene)  
  *Mechanic:* She runs a gloved finger along the lace of her dress or bonnet, each pattern knotting to a choice.  
  *Application:* Lets her map several likely futures for a single decision, useful for advising nobles and Accord envoys.
* **Tea Of Borrowed Sleep** (Cost: **18,000,000,000 EU** plus preparation)  
  *Mechanic:* Infuses tea with Somnalis essence.  
  *Application:* Guests who drink it share shallow dream fragments with her later that night, offering her a view into their fears and plans.
* **Judged Thread** (Cost: **40,000,000,000 EU**, high strain)  
  *Mechanic:* She grasps a glowing probability thread with her bandaged hand and snaps it with a little twist.  
  *Application:* Erases a narrow future line, making a specific outcome nearly impossible without heavy external interference.
* **Parlour Sleep** (Cost: **moderate Essence, area effect**)  
  *Mechanic:* She hums an old Lurien hymn, letting Somnalis leak into walls, curtains and seats.  
  *Application:* Calmly puts most non resistant intruders in her parlour into a soft, harmless sleep.

#### C. Costs and Consequences
* **Prophetic Migraine:**  
  After a heavy Judged Thread, she is bedridden with pain and light sensitivity for hours.  
* **Emotional Bleaching:**  
  Seeing too many futures dulls her emotional response. She fights to keep genuine joy and sorrow from fading into tired amusement.

---

### V. RELICS & DOMAIN
* **Current Domain:** **White Parlour Of Threads**
  * *Function:* A domestic scale Domain bound to her salon. Within it, time feels slightly slower, sound is cushioned, and probability lines manifest as faint threads only she can touch. Outside forces have a harder time forcing chaotic events inside this space.
* **Relics & Implements:**
  * **Lilac Lens Ribbon:** The bandage itself is a Relic created by Lurien clergy, both sealing and shaping the ruined eye. It keeps her from drowning in visions and channels them into narrower bands.  
  * **Porcelain Clock Teaset:** A matched set that doubles as a temporal focus. She can subtly nudge the timing of arrivals and departures for those who drink from it.

---

### VI. LORE & TRAJECTORY
* **Allegiance:** Officially a loyal daughter of the Holy See. Unofficially a quiet supporter of the Guild Accord when their aims preserve lives instead of glory.  
* **Incident Records (Historical):**
  * **The Cancelled Coronation:** Warned a Lurien prince that taking the crown on a specific date would mean his death within a week. He delayed, the coup failed, and she gained both enemies and secret gratitude.  
  * **The Velvet Siege:** During a minor city crisis she kept envoys, merchants and soldiers sitting in her parlour in carefully staggered intervals so that arguments never tipped into riots.
* **Counters & Weaknesses:**
  * Her powers are tied to prepared spaces and objects. Out in open fields or unfamiliar zones she is far less effective.  
  * Name blind areas and Domains that scramble causality make her eye stutter and can induce panic.  
  * Her physical frailty and reliance on bodyguards make her vulnerable to direct, fast violence if caught unprepared.
* **Current Status:** Resides within a pale stone townhouse in the Lurien capital, parlour open by appointment. Accord agents visit under the pretext of tea to discuss “what tomorrow might politely avoid.”  
* **Trajectory:**  
  Seraphine is positioned to become one of the key civilian seers of the War of the Realms, or to retreat into apathetic detachment if the weight of futures becomes too much. Whether she steps out from behind the lace and uses Judged Thread on truly world scale choices will decide how history writes her name.

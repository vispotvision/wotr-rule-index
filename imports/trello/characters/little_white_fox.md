**SHIRO KITSUNARI**

**Alias / Title:** The Bell-On-Branch  
**Also Known As:** Little White Fox · Tree Perch Scout  
**Epithet:** The Child Who Listens To Leaves

---

### I. IDENTITY & CORE PROFILE
* **Race / Lineage:** Vulpine Beastkin (Forest Spirit Touched)
* **Faction / Order / Era:** Outer Wilds Freeholds, informal Guild Accord courier circle — Imperial Calendar
* **Temperance Stage:** Stage 1 Murmuring (*Awakened Bud*)
* **Profile Summary:**  
  Shiro is a small fox beastkin boy whose soul is half tethered to the forest Animatria. He hears branches breathe, feels moods ripple through moss and bark, and slips along treelines as if the world itself shifts to catch him. He has little in the way of raw power yet, but his sensitivity is astounding. In the long arc of WOTR, his role is as a future pathfinder and living barometer of the land, meant to warn when Wellsprings sicken or Realms begin to fracture.
* **Key Quote:** *"If the branches shiver, we should listen before we march."*

---

### II. CANONICAL SYSTEM METRICS
* **Aether Class:** **Resonant Listener**
  * **Behavior:** Aether gathers around Shiro as faint leaf shaped motes, drifting in loose spirals around his hair and bare feet when he focuses. He does not project much outward force. Instead he receives, tuning his Aura to tiny shifts in Essence flow. When startled, these motes flash gold and rustle with the sound of unseen leaves.
  * **Risk:** Sensory Flood. Strong Wellsprings or violent Domains can overpower his perception, causing vertigo, tears of light from his eyes, and temporary muteness.
* **Wellspring Affinity:**
  * **Triune Core:** Verdantia · Somnalis · Mirithane  
    (Life growth, drowsy dreamfields, and instinct song.)
  * **Network:** 0 additional Wellsprings currently linked.  
  * **Targeted Uninfected:** None. He is not yet used as a vector.
* **Temperance Path:**  
  Shiro walks a quiet, receptive path. Murmuring opened in him when he nearly fell from a high branch as a toddler and the forest caught him in a net of branches that should not have bent. Since then he has been trained to notice before he acts, to feel before he speaks. His mentors expect him to move into Welling through emotional courage rather than combat.

* **Path Bias:** **Spirit Path** (55%) · **Mind Path** (30%) · **Body Path** (15%)

  Height: 4'1"  
  Build: Light, slightly underfed forest kid but surprisingly sturdy.  
  Skin/Eyes/Hair: Pale skin, golden amber eyes, long fluffy white hair with uneven bangs, tied up with a red ribbon and a small bell. Fox-like ear tips hidden in the hair. Faint leaf and swirl markings glow gold along his calves and cheeks.

### IV. AXIOMATIC MANIFESTATIONS

#### A. Axiomatic Traits (Passive)
* **Forest’s Chosen Perch:** Trees subtly bend and grow in ways that keep him safe. Branches rarely break under his weight. Falls often redirect into swinging, sliding, or gentle catches.
* **Bell Of Quiet Paths:** The small bell in his hair rings only when danger he cannot sense is near. If he hears it, his Spirit Path refines for a moment, sharpening all perceptions.

#### B. Core Disciplines (Active)
* **Leaf-Listening Veil** (Cost: **25,000 EU**)
  * *Mechanic:* Shiro closes his eyes and lets his Aether seep through nearby bark and leaves. Sound and emotion echo back as ripples in his veins. He can sense footfalls, breath, and Essence pulses in a wide grove radius.
  * *Application:* Scouting, ambush detection, tracking Wellspring sickness as patches of “silent” trees.
* **Branchstep Skitter** (Cost: **18,000 EU** per burst)
  * *Mechanic:* He coaxes branches and roots to lift, twist, or slant just enough that one step becomes three, allowing fast, zigzagging movement through arboreal terrain.
  * *Application:* Dodging attacks, closing distance to allies, escaping pursuers without leaving clear tracks.
* **Dream Moss Lullaby** (Cost: **40,000 EU**)
  * *Mechanic:* A thin mist of Somnalis essence seeps from his markings, settling on bark, rock, and ground. Those who breathe it feel drowsy, slowed, and emotionally softened rather than brutally stunned.
  * *Application:* Non lethal pacification of beasts or panicked travelers, usually under adult supervision.

#### C. Costs & Consequences
* **Sensory Overrun:** Prolonged Leaf-Listening causes headaches, nosebleeds, and temporary hearing loss. Loud Domains can blank his senses for minutes.
* **Growth Fatigue:** Using Dream Moss Lullaby drains Verdantia within him, leaving him pale, shivery, and hungry. Overuse risks stunting his physical growth if repeated without rest.

### V. RELICS & DOMAIN
* **Current Domain:** **None yet fully formed**  
  *Function:* At high emotional focus, a proto domain called the Bellwood Moment flickers around him. For a few seconds, sound dulls and wind moves leaves in slow arcs, making hostile intent obvious while hiding small motions.
* **Relics & Implements:**
  * **Bell Of The First Branch:** A small fox shrine bell in his hair. Slightly amplifies Spirit Path sensitivity and warns him with a soft, private chime.
  * **Woven Bark Anklet:** Handmade from living bark by a forest priest. Grants minor protection from falls by thickening roots or softening ground.

---

### VI. LORE & TRAJECTORY
* **Allegiance:** Friendly to the Guild Accord by proxy, loyal first to his home grove and its guardian spirits.
* **Incident Records (Historical):**
  * **The Night Of Cracking Roots:** Shiro was the first to feel a distant Wellspring shudder, waking half the grove with a scream before the ground split in a nearby valley. His warning saved several caravans.
  * **The Misplaced Caravan:** Guided a lost merchant group through fog by running ahead along the treeline, using Branchstep Skitter to stay visible but out of danger.
* **Counters & Weaknesses:**
  * **Urban Silence:** Stone cities and metal structures are painful for him. No trees means no Leaf-Listening, blinding a major part of his kit.
  * **Domain Overwhelm:** Strong Domains that already control air, mind, or life fields can drown out his subtle influences, turning his abilities almost purely passive.
* **Current Status:** Active in the outer forest rings near Accord trade routes, usually perched in trees with scouts or alone above hidden paths.
* **Trajectory:**  
  Expected to grow into a high tier pathfinder and Wellspring surveyor. His future arc centers on whether he remains a gentle observer or learns to weaponize the forest’s will when the Realms demand harsher choices.

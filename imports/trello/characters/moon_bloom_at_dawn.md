### **Name:**  
**Tsukihana Reimei (月花黎明)**  
*“Moon-Bloom at Dawn”*  

### **Alias / Title:**  
**The Pale Petal of Silence** · *Kinjirareta no Shizukana Ha*  
Guild Accord record ID: *Enforcement Division — Veil Arbiter Class*  

### **Race:**  
Human (Fae-Tainted Lineage) — *Aether-Kissed* subtype; her ancestry bears diluted Wellspring resonance from the Verdantia strain, giving her aura the faint luminescence of moon-lit petals.  

### **Realm of Origin:**  
**Eresse**, Old World — border province of *Kael’Nir* forests, where fae-human treaties once entwined bloodlines through oaths under moonlight.  

### **Temperance Tier & Stage:**  
**Stage VII — Resolution**  
Path of Spirit and Mind equilibrium; trained in the *Kagami no Sei* discipline — the Art of Reflection and Inner Balance.  

### **Wellspring Affinity:**  
**Fixatio · Verdantia**  
Restraint bound to renewal — she stills chaos through growth, purifies through serenity.  

### **Aether Class:**  
**Reflective Soul / Tempered Bloom Class** — manifests mirrored fields that convert hostility into tranquil Essence.  

### **Essence Typology:**  
Lunarlight Essence — harmonic, soothing, but volatile under emotional duress; fae influence makes her resonance subtly melodic.  

### **Parun Glyph / Domain:**  
**Kaetra + Selhar + Elyndra**  
(Dream · Harmony · Renewal)  
Domain Title: *The Mirror Garden*  

---

### **Appearance:**  
Hair silver-white with a soft violet gradient — a hereditary mark of fae admixture. Her eyes shimmer pale lilac, glowing faintly when channeling Wellspring energy. She wears a pale ceremonial kimono layered with protective Aether-silk; purple ribbons bind each fold, functioning as containment charms. The single pauldron across her shoulder bears the emblem of the Enforcement Division — signifying judgment without bias. Her blade, *Shiranui*, sings a near-inaudible tone when drawn, said to calm corrupted spirits.  

---

### **Personality:**  
Gentle, but not fragile. Every movement deliberate, her silence carries both empathy and warning. Tsukihana views violence as a form of pruning — cutting away decay to let virtue bloom again. She shows mercy easily, forgiveness rarely. The fae blood within her gifts heightened intuition, but also loneliness: she hears the hum of Essence even when no Wellspring stirs.  

---

### **Abilities / Techniques:**  
- **Moonveil Draw:** Projects a crescent of refracted light that silences Wellspring resonance within its arc.  
- **Petal Reversal:** Redirects incoming Essence bursts into mirrored bloom-bursts — harmless illusions that pacify aggression.  
- **Still Garden:** Generates a circular field of serenity where combatants lose the will to strike; an Enforcement rite reserved for inner-Accord disputes.  
- **Verdant Severance:** Her final technique; one cut that severs corruption while preserving the soul — used only when judgment demands restoration, not death.  

---

### **Known Lore / History:**  
Born in the village of Hane-no-Tera during the waning years of the Hobgoblin Empire’s collapse. Her fae lineage nearly branded her heretic under Concord law, yet her gift for reflection rites drew notice from the Arbitration Division. She was trained under **High Arbiter Saen**, then transferred to Enforcement after mastering the *Mirror Garden Oath*. Legends whisper that she once quelled a rampaging Wellspring spirit by offering it her reflection to inhabit.  

---

### **Symbolism / Mythic Role:**  
Tsukihana embodies **the mercy of equilibrium** — serenity as weapon, reflection as absolution. Among the fae, she’s called *Hana-no-Kagami* — “The Mirror That Blooms,” said to appear wherever dreams must be judged for truth.

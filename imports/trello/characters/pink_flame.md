**LYRA DRAKOSHI**  
**Alias / Title:** The Blushscale Warden • Ember Serpent of the Gate  
**Also Known As:** Dragon Sister · Pink Flame · The Coiled Blade  
**Epithet:** She Who Smiles With Smoke In Her Lungs

---

### I. IDENTITY & CORE PROFILE
* **Race / Lineage:** Draconic Ascendant (Humanoid Dragonblood)  
* **Faction / Order / Era:** Outer World border freeholder, contracted to local Guild Accord watch posts - Imperial Calendar  
* **Temperance Stage:** 6 - **Glory** (battle seasoned, pre mythic)  
* **Profile Summary:**  
  Lyra is a dragonblood swordswoman who chooses watch duty over hoarding. Where others of her kind sleep on treasure, she coils around gates and caravan routes, keeping the flames pointed away from civilians. Her spirit is easygoing and sardonic on the surface, but her temper runs molten when innocents are threatened. In the larger WOTR frame she is a regional warden, a potential future dragon hero if she ever leaves her comfortable border and steps into the true war.  
* **Key Quote:** *"Relax. If something tries to kill you, I will roast it first and ask questions if there is anything left."*

---

### II. CANONICAL SYSTEM METRICS
* **Aether Class:** **Draconic Furnace Knight**
  * **Behavior:** Essence gathers in her chest and tail as slow rolling embers that brightens to molten gold when she fights. Heat trails behind her hair like steam from a hot blade in cold air. Her Aether prefers spiral arcs and sweeping coils, turning sword paths into extensions of a dragon body.
  * **Risk:** Overheating the internal furnace can crack her scales and skin, causing Painflare surges that either spike her power or drop her to one knee. Sudden coolant shocks from water or cold Domains can stress her lattice.
* **Wellspring Affinity:**
  * **Triune Core:** Pyreveil · Ignivale · Rebirthine  
    Burning impulse, controlled blaze and regenerative ember all braided.
  * **Network:** 2 minor Wellsprings in local leyline knots that she uses as rest nests.  
  * **Targeted Uninfected:** None. She guards her Wellspring ties rather than spreading them.
* **Temperance Path:**  
  Lyra moved from Murmuring to Ascension through raw survival, then accelerated during mercenary years when she learned to win without burning everything down. Glory arrived when she began defending the same roads repeatedly, choosing responsibility over wandering. Her Path favors Body and Spirit, with just enough Mind discipline not to scorch her own allies.

### III. SOUL PHYSICS & COMBAT SCALE
* **Global Tier:** **S** (regional top tier, below archonic anomalies)  
* **Path Bias:** **Body Path** (45 percent) · **Spirit Path** (40 percent) · **Mind Path** (15 percent)  
* **Appearance:**  
  Height about 5'10". Build lithe but dense with coiled strength. Skin a warm rose magenta tone. Long pink hair worn loose with some strands tied back, often falling over one eye. Two curved golden horns crown her brow, with a smaller ridged spur at the center of her forehead. Gold slit pupils, faint scale shimmer on neck, shoulders and thighs. A pale gold dragon tail curls behind her, usually looping lazily when she is relaxed. She wears a sleeveless high collar dress that matches her skin tone, cut for movement, with oversized white sleeves shrugged low around her arms and a sword at her hip.

#### A. The Five Pillars (Soul Physics)

* **Essence Capacity (Volume):** **1,800,000,000,000 EU**  
  Enough to sustain prolonged close range combat with multiple Furnace surges before needing rest.
* **Flux Density (Internal Pressure):** **240,000,000 EU per gram**  
  Her fire hits like liquid metal, able to punch through standard magical shields while still behaving fluidly.
* **Aether Output (Flow Rate):** **180,000,000 AU per second**  
  Allows continuous flame mantles or rapid burst casting of draconic techniques alongside swordplay.
* **Aetheric Efficiency (η):** **0.78**  
  She wastes some Essence when angry, venting heat into the air instead of precise effects. Calm focus pushes efficiency higher.
* **Attraction Scale (Aura and Presence):** **B Tier - Ember Court**  
  People feel warmed and slightly drowsy around her at rest. In battle, her Aura sharpens into a predatory heat that makes weaker foes sweat and hesitate.

#### B. Key Combat Parameters
* **Strike Force:** Up to 12 petajoules when combining a full body lunge with a Furnace charged sword cut or tail slam.  
* **Durability:** Around 6 petajoules of effective toughness with mantle active, between natural draconic resilience and flame aura that ablates attacks.  
* **Speed:** Short dash speed in the high hypersonic range when she coils and releases with tail assist, otherwise fast but readable to high tier fighters.  
* **Range:** Most effective within melee to mid range. Fire spirals and breath cones extend out a few dozen meters. She prefers to close rather than trade at distance.

### IV. AXIOMATIC MANIFESTATIONS

#### A. Axiomatic Traits (Passive)
* **Blushscale Furnace:**  
  Her blood carries latent flame. Wounds cauterize almost instantly, bruises glow cherry gold before fading. Her body temperature rises with anger, and her close presence can warm cold camps without a fire.
* **Serpent Coil Stance:**  
  Standing still, she still feels like something pacing in circles. Her footwork naturally forms loops and spirals that make straight line attacks slide past or fall short.
* **Dragon Scent Of Truth:**  
  She can smell the emotional temperature of a conversation. Lies and cowardice have a sour smoke tag to her senses, though skilled liars can mask it under heavy scents.

#### B. Core Disciplines (Active)
* **Embercoil Mantle** (Cost: **30,000,000,000 EU** to ignite, upkeep low)  
  *Mechanic:* Lyra exhales a slow stream of fire that curls back around her, forming a tight halo along skin and clothes.  
  *Application:* Acts as armor against physical blows, scorching weapons that stay too close and softening projectiles. Also boosts her own heat based techniques.
* **Spiral Fang Cut** (Cost: **18,000,000,000 EU** per use)  
  *Mechanic:* A sword strike that traces a corkscrew arc, leaving a twisting trail of flame that bites twice, once on entry and once as it unwinds.  
  *Application:* Ideal for breaking layered shields or forcing agile foes into bad positions.
* **Blossom Breath** (Cost: **40,000,000,000 EU**)  
  *Mechanic:* She inhales deeply, the horns brightening, then exhales a fan of petal shaped flame motes that drift and then erupt on command.  
  *Application:* Zone control, denying space, discouraging rushes. She can snap her fingers to trigger the motes.
* **Molten Wrap** (Cost: **25,000,000,000 EU**)  
  *Mechanic:* The loose white sleeves twist into semi solid flame bindings.  
  *Application:* Grapple assist that can pin weapons, bind limbs or yank enemies closer for a finishing strike.
* **Ashwake Rebirth** (Cost: **all remaining Essence in emergencies**)  
  *Mechanic:* When taken past a fatal threshold, her body collapses into hot ash and a smaller, heavily drained version of her reforms from the embers.  
  *Application:* Last resort survivability that trades power and gear for a second chance.

#### C. Costs and Consequences
* **Painflare Surges:**  
  If she pushes the Furnace too far, pain spikes through her ribs and shoulders. Sometimes this gives a short burst of power, other times it forces her out of mantle completely.
* **Coolant Shock:**  
  Sudden immersion in water or cold magic can fracture her scale layer and leave her shivering, reducing output until she reheats.

### V. RELICS & DOMAIN
* **Current Domain:** **Coiling Ember Road**  
  *Function:* A proto domain that manifests when she commits to defending a path or gate. Heat shimmer outlines a looping road under her feet, and within that lane her speed and reflexes sharpen while enemy footing feels slightly soft and treacherous. Fire effects are easier and cheaper to cast along the Road.
* **Relics & Implements:**
  * **Dragonhook Blade:** A curved single edged sword with a hook near the tip. Forged from draconic alloy that holds and channels her fire without melting.  
  * **Horn Chain Tassel:** A decorative chain that hangs from one horn, set with a small charm. It is a low tier Relic that warns her with a faint chime when hostile Essence targets her from behind.  

---

### VI. LORE & TRAJECTORY
* **Allegiance:** Informal ally of the Guild Accord. Officially a free agent who takes contracts that let her live near roads, bridges and lonely border inns.  
* **Incident Records (Historical):**
  * **Red Caravan Stand:** Held a mountain pass alone for a night against raiders, walking the road back and forth while her Ember Road burned under the wagon wheels. No civilian died.  
  * **Ashwine Truce:** Forced two feuding border lords to sign a ceasefire by heating the stone floor under their feet until their boots smoked. Humor kept it from becoming outright coercion.
* **Counters & Weaknesses:**
  * Heavy water and ice sorcery can blunt her kit if she is caught off guard.  
  * Extremely enclosed spaces with no ventilation risk suffocating her own flames if she overuses breath styles.  
  * She reacts badly to targeted insults against people under her protection and can be baited into overcommitting.
* **Current Status:** Stationed by choice along a busy outer border route, half legend and half grumpy regular at the local inn. The Accord sometimes sends her emergency flares when dragon class muscle is needed quickly.  
* **Trajectory:**  
  Lyra has the potential to rise from regional warden to true dragon heroine. Whether that happens depends on whether she can stop treating her road as the whole world and answer larger calls, or whether she chooses instead to make her small corner of the Realms the safest road anyone can walk.

# LIORIEN NAEL-TETH

Alias / Title: The Drift-Scribe of the Eastward Isles  
Also Known As: Tide-Ink Archivist · Sleepless Quill · Little Book of Storms  
Epithet: He Who Hears What Pages Remember  

---

## I. Identity & Core Profile

- **Race / Lineage:** Veyran Human — Brine-Touched Scribe Lineage (Anamnesis-sensitive bloodline)  
- **Faction / Order / Era:** Archive of Eastward Islands · Guild Accord–adjacent Scriptorium · Imperial Calendar, Post–Voyager Expansion  
- **Temperance Stage:** IV — Flourishing (low-tier ascendant support caster)  
- **Profile Summary:**  
  Liorien is a quiet island archivist whose Aether stirs through ink, tidewater, and written memory. He wanders between coastal outposts carrying fragments of drowned histories, recording what the ocean whispers and binding those memories into talismans and pages. His ultimate goal is to reconstruct a “true ledger” of the Eastward seas — a record that cannot be burned, drowned, or rewritten by any Concord or Crown.  
- **Key Quote:** _“If no one writes it down, the world pretends it never happened.”_  

---

## II. Canonical System Metrics

- **Aether Class:** Scriptorial Brine-Aether — *The Tide-Quill Archive*  
  - **Behavior:** Converts written language into stable Aether constructs. Every line he writes in special brine-ink becomes a temporary “clause” in reality: weak wards, memory tags, gentle compulsion, or tide-aligned illusions. The ink on his clothes and charm-papers can be awakened at will.  
  - **Risk:** The more he externalizes memory into pages, the more his own recall frays. Overuse causes “ledger bleed,” where he forgets entire days that are now only preserved in his books.  
- **Wellspring Affinity:**  
  - **Triune Core Affinities:** Anamnesis · Oneirion · Phreatis.  
  - **Wellspring Network:** 1 additional Wellspring is lightly **networked** (minor Cymorath trace from deep-sea pressure storms).  
  - **Targeted Uninfected:** 7 specific Wellsprings tied to historical erasure and censorship — he intends to map them, not exploit them.  
- **Temperance Path:**  
  Developed along **Spirit Path** first, through obsessive memory discipline and dream-journaling. **Attraction Path** awakened as he learned to “call” memories into shells and pages. Body Path remains underdeveloped; he weaponizes Temperance through written clauses and environmental control rather than raw force, using Flourishing’s emotional sensitivity to stabilize traumatized witnesses while he records their truth.

## III. Physical & Combat Scale

- **Tier:** High C-Tier (support / utility specialist)  
- **Path Bias:** Spirit Path (1.35) · Attraction Path (1.10) · Body Path (0.80)  

- **Appearance:**  
  Height: 4'9" (short, slight). Build: Soft, underfed scholar build.  
  Skin/Eyes/Hair: Deep umber skin; heavy, sleep-shadowed amber eyes; tousled teal-blue hair that always looks wind-tossed. He wears layered navy and cream robes stitched with gold tide-patterns, a pale sash with tassels, and dark patterned boots. A feather quill rests tucked at his collar, ink-stained at the tip.  

- **Aura / Presence:**  
  Rooms feel a little quieter around him; sounds muffle as though underwater. Pages, cloth, and even walls seem to “listen.” Most people feel an odd urge to speak softly and be honest when near him, as if a confession booth has settled over the space.  

- **Key Combat Parameters:**  
  - **Strike Force:** 4 gigajoules (ink-reinforced impact via scripted force sigils)  
  - **Durability:** 3 gigajoules (mostly defensive script-wards and tide-film barriers)  
  - **Speed:** Mach 3 bursts (short “tide-step” displacements along written sigil lines)  
  - **Range:** 200 meters (ink-scripted law-field / page-borne projections)  
  - **Aether Output (Baseline):** 35,000 AU/s

## IV. Axiomatic Manifestations (Traits & Abilities)

- **Axiomatic Traits:**  
  - **Ink-Saturated Soul:** His blood carries dissolved brine-ink; any surface he touches can briefly hold script without pigment, letting him write laws in breath-fog, sea spray, or dust.  
  - **Tide-Stilled Heart:** Emotional waves around him dampen; panic and wrath lose edge, making interrogation and testimony more coherent within his presence.  
  - **Dream-Page Gaze:** When tired enough, his half-lidded stare “reads” echoes off people and objects, glimpsing faint overlays of what they have witnessed.  

- **Core Disciplines:**  
  - **1. Brine-Quill Edict**  
    Liorien writes floating lines of script with his feather quill; the letters detach as luminous strips of tidewater ink. Each line is a minor edict: *“do not pass,” “forget this face,” “hold the wall.”* When anchored to surfaces, they form soft barriers, nonlethal bindings, or memory shrouds. In battle, he strings these strips between pillars and debris to funnel enemies or hide allies behind forgetful veils.  

  - **2. Low-Tide Redaction**  
    By sweeping his tasselled belt-brush across the ground, he calls up a thin, knee-high sheet of gray-blue water that washes through an area and “bleaches” the last few seconds of sensory impression. Targets do not lose events themselves, but their emotional color and sharpness fade; witnesses remember what happened but struggle to care, which is invaluable for calming crowds or dulling trauma. Against foes, it blurs aim, timing, and killing intent.  

  - **3. Echo-Current Folio**  
    He scatters a stack of charm-papers that flutter into a small storm of pages. Each page carries a simple glyph — ward, mirror, or tether. The swarm forms a mobile half-dome that intercepts projectiles, reflects minor spells, and records incoming Aether patterns. Liorien can later “replay” what the pages saw, turning the zone into a living testimony field during debrief or trial.  

- **Costs & Consequences:**  
  - **Ledger Bleed:** Every major casting writes part of his own memory into the ink. If he does not carefully transcribe it back, those hours or days become hollow in his mind.  
  - **Ink Sickness:** Prolonged overuse stains his veins dark and leaves him dizzy, nauseous, and physically weak; in extreme cases, his hands tremble too badly to write, effectively silencing his Aether.

## V. Relics & Domain

- **Current Domain:** *Pre-Domain Threshold.*  
  In enclosed spaces stacked with books or scrolls, a proto-Domain called **The Sighing Stacks** sometimes manifests: whispers echo a heartbeat late, written words crawl along surfaces, and lies ring faintly off-key to anyone inside.  

- **Relics & Implements:**  
  - **Shellfeather Quill:** A feather pen grown from Ether-coral gulls; it never runs out of brine-ink and can write on water as if it were paper.  
  - **Tide-Bound Waist Tassel:** A brush-tassel woven from kelp-fiber and golden thread; used to sweep sigils into the floor, activate Low-Tide Redaction, and clean away hazardous scripts.

## VI. Lore & Trajectory

- **Allegiance:**  
  Archivist-Member of the Archive of Eastward Islands; contracted consultant to the Guild Accord for maritime incidents and memory-sensitive investigations.  

- **Incident Records (Historical):**  
  - **The Drowned Ledger Affair:** Liorien single-handedly reconstructed the testimony of an entire fishing village after a Black Concord cell tried to erase all written records and witnesses of their smuggling route. His pages became legal precedent in three coastal courts.  
  - **The Broken Shell Night:** During a storm-breach in the Shell-Vault Citadel, he used Echo-Current Folio to trap and replay an ancient leviathan’s echo long enough for senior archivists to rebind it, at the cost of several months of his own memory.  

- **Counters & Weaknesses:**  
  - **Weakness 1:** Null-Aether or anti-script fields render his ink inert, forcing him into mundane, fragile combat.  
  - **Weakness 2:** Fire and extreme dryness destroy his writing medium; sustained flame, desert environments, or salt-eating acids can sever his edicts and collapse the Sighing Stacks effect.  

- **Current Status / Arc Direction:**  
  Active, rotating between Eastward islands and Guild Accord coastal courts. Trajectory: assembling a forbidden “True Ledger of the Sea Wars,” a compiled record of every erased atrocity on the Eastern tides, even those both Concord and Crowns want forgotten.  

- **Foreshadowing:**  
  If Liorien ever reaches higher Temperance and forges a full Domain, his Ledger may stop being mere record and begin to *sentence reality* — turning written history into binding law, and making it impossible for the world to lie about what it has done.

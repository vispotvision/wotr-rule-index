## **“THE BLACK CLERIC”**

### **Profile Summary**
**Name:** Elion Drevas  
**Alias:** *The Black Cleric*  
**Race:** Human (Aether-touched)  
**Gender:** Male  
**Age:** 31  
**Origin Realm:** Eresse — Old World, Dominion of the Faithkeepers  
**Affiliation:** Independent · Former Guild Accord Arcanist  
**Rank:** Defrocked Scholar, T7 Resonant  

---

### **Appearance**
A tall, lean figure wrapped in a black clerical coat that drinks light rather than reflects it. He carries himself with quiet gravity; even in a crowd, space bends around him. His hair is raven-dark, tangled at the ends, and his eyes hold the exhausted clarity of someone who has seen divinity and found it hollow.  

His hands, always gloved, show faint traces of sigil burn — remnants of sealed Wellspring contact. He walks with a slight limp, not from injury but from the imbalance of carrying a fractured soul crystal.  

---

### **Temperance Path**
**Stage IX – Reflection**  
At this stage, Elion’s Essence no longer obeys a single law. He mirrors the resonance of others, turning their energy back in equal measure — a walking equilibrium between blessing and curse. His Temperance state manifests as black feathers of motes that hover briefly before dissolving into static.  

---

### **Aether Class**
**Echo-Soul (Attraction-Dominant)**  
Elion’s consciousness can project fragments of memory into others’ Lattice Threads, forcing empathy or terror depending on his will. His Essence signature reads like an inverted prayer — calm yet corrosive.  

---

### **Wellspring Alignment**
- **Primary:** *Judicium* (Truth, Balance, Reckoning)  
- **Secondary:** *Oblation* (Sacrifice, Offering, Binding)  
- **Tertiary:** *Letheveil* (Forgetfulness, Mercy through Erasure)  

---

### **Division / Allegiance**
Former **Guild Accord — Division of Concordant Faith**, expelled after the *Mirrormass Incident*, where he severed a Crevice Phenomenon by offering half his Soul Crystal as tithe. Now wanders the Inner Realms performing absolutions for those tainted by Wellspring corruption.  

---

### **Personality & Core Themes**
Quietly defiant. Speaks with the cadence of scripture but the intent of a heretic. Believes redemption is not divine gift but human labor. When he listens, people feel lighter; when he prays, the air trembles.  
Theme: *Grace through fracture.*  

---

### **Soul Crystal Profile**
- **Structure:** Bi-lateral fracture; half dissolved into Aether flux  
- **Flux Density:** 9.4 million EU/g  
- **Gate Count:** 6 active, 3 sealed  
- **Aether Output:** 72,000 AU/s  
- **Efficiency (η):** 0.72 — fluctuates with emotional resonance  

---

### **Traits**
- **Echo Benediction:** Reflects any targeted Essence ability back at 80% strength.  
- **Saint’s Ruin:** Converts incoming holy energy into neutral Flux, nullifying sanctified magic.  
- **Oblation Field:** Each prayer drains his own vitality to stabilize nearby Wellspring distortions.  
- **Forget-Me Veil:** When activated, erases his spiritual signature for several hours, appearing to Fate itself as “absent.”  

---

### **Relics & Implements**
- **Vessel Codex:** A chain-bound scripture filled with blank pages that write themselves in response to nearby sin resonance.  
- **The Mirror Veil:** A tattered ceremonial stole woven with Runeglass filaments; amplifies Reflection-type magic.  
- **Severed Halo Fragment:** Broken Archonic relic; grants immunity to divine influence but steadily corrodes memory.  

---

### **Abilities & Disciplines**
- **Judicant Reversal:** Reverses Essence polarity in a 10-meter radius — divine becomes profane, flame becomes frost.  
- **Aether Psalm:** Vocal resonance that suppresses active Wellspring harmonics for up to 20 seconds.  
- **Oblation Rite:** Transfers corruption or curse from another into his own Soul Crystal, purifying them but degrading himself.  
- **Lethean Concord:** Invokes memory collapse on a target, erasing emotional connection to a specific trauma.  

---

### **Costs & Consequences**
Each use of *Oblation* shortens his remaining lifespan — estimated 4 years of Essence stability remain. The Severed Halo continuously consumes fragments of his memory to maintain sanity.  

---

### **Hooks**
- Pursued by the Concordant Faith for “divine inversion.”  
- Rumored to carry the memory of an Archon’s final words — though he can no longer recall them.  
- Travels toward the Outer Realms seeking the forgotten Well of Penanceflare.  

---

### **Quote**
> “I prayed until I learned prayer was only sound. Now I speak in silence — and the silence answers.”

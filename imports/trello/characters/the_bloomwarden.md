# Character Dossier — **Kozakura Shion, the Bloomwarden**

**Role:** Spring psychopomp & rite-keeper; he “prunes” dying moments so the living can grow around them, and turns crowd-fury into falling petals instead of blades.

**Affiliation:** Guild Accord • Reaper’s seasonal adjutant (Vernal Office) • Walks funeral routes with Shiragiku & Natsume when wakes run hot

---

## Look & tells
Snow cloak stitched with sakura tassels and butterfly knots; dusk-violet crescent polearm; heterochrome gaze that weighs *what should go* and *what should stay*.

---

## Essence & Style
- **Flow:** Harmonia + Animatria with a fine Mnemata thread—he edits scenes like a gardener, not a storm.  
- **Field feel (what you’d see):** He steps, the air warms; paper-soft petals drift; arguments land gentler than they began.

---

## Traits
- **Pruner’s Sight** — sees grudges as suckers on a living stem; knows exactly where to cut so love survives.  
- **Hanafubuki** — a blossom squall that muffles shouts, slows thrown things, and gives a last sentence room to finish.  
- **Butterfly Binds** — red cords tie two heartbeats to the same count; striking off-beat becomes awkward and stops.

---

## Implements
- **Moonbend Naginata *Hanebari*** — crescent edge that severs *attachments* before flesh; a clean sweep removes the *impact* from a blow but leaves the lesson.  
- **Petal Mantle** — inner lining shakes loose living blossoms that hold a promise wherever they fall.  
- **Kokeshi Charms** — tiny wooden figures; placed on thresholds, they invite the kind and politely tire the cruel.

---

## Techniques (what you’d actually see)
- **Prune of Grudge:** A short arc across two arguing wrists; the hot thread snaps and the complaint returns small enough to answer.  
- **Blossom Screen:** Staff spins; petals stack like gauze—the spear arrives, remembers manners, and droops.  
- **Vernal Gate:** He plants the naginata butt, bows once; the floor feels like garden soil; the dying cross without stumbling and the living don’t cling.  
- **Graft of Mercy:** A butterfly knot between families; for one meal their breaths rhyme and knives forget their owners.  
- **Survivor’s Sweep:** Wide cut that trims only the moment of impact from a stampede; bodies keep moving, bones do not break.

---

## Duties
- Keep last rooms warm enough for truth; convert riot vigils into processions; reopen streets after Measures without leaving thorns behind.

---

## Limits
- Stone courts with no green blunt his reach; he needs Lily-Quill or Cassiel to set softer geometry.  
- Drum-Domains shred **Hanafubuki** until Maelia breaks the beat.  
- Too many “mercies” in one night leave frost-burn along his palms; tea and quiet earth fix it.

---

## Relationships
- **Yukizumi (Reaper):** spring hand to winter star—he softens what the Reaper must end.  
- **Shiragiku Iori & Natsume Kurogiku:** flowers soothe, stitches hold, Shion trims.  
- **Sumire Nekoha:** her lanes carry his petals farther than streets deserve.  
- **Deimos:** he eats guilt; Shion refuses to cut away the lesson that should stay.

**Key Quote:** “I don’t spare everything—I spare what lets you grow.”

---

## Hooks
1) **Blight-Petal:** Someone salts his blossoms; the squall cuts kindness instead—he must cleanse the mantle mid-procession.  
2) **Winter Court:** Talks demanded on bare stone at night; Shion has to coax spring from memory alone.  
3) **Stolen Knot:** A Butterfly Bind is lifted and used to leash a crowd; he must untie it without unteaching trust.

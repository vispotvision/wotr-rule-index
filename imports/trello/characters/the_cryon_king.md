# **VALTHRUM**

**Alias / Title:** The Cryon King · The Lord of Stilled Hearts
**Also Known As:** The White Silence · Keeper of the Zero Point
**Epithet:** He Who Anchors the Breath of the World

---

### **I. IDENTITY & CORE PROFILE**

- **Race / Lineage:** **Numen (Greater Elemental Eidolon)**
- **Faction / Order / Era:** **The Winter Court** — **Era of the Splintering** (Active in Current Era)
- **Temperance Stage:** **Stage 11: Principality** — _The embodiment of a ruling concept._
- **Profile Summary:** Valthrum is not merely a user of ice magic; he is the metaphysical assertion that "energy must end." He views motion as a chaotic sickness and stillness as the ultimate perfection. He exists to slow the feverish pace of the other Realms, preserving reality by freezing it in moments of perfect, crystalized clarity.
- **Key Quote:** _“The fire burns, the wind screams, the earth breaks. Only the ice remembers. I do not bring death; I bring the peace of the pause.”_

---

### **II. CANONICAL SYSTEM METRICS**

- **Aether Class:** **Emitter (Stasis-Field Type)**
  - **Behavior:** Valthrum constantly emits a "Negative Thermal Aura." He does not project ice so much as he projects _entropy_, causing the moisture in the air to instantly snap-freeze into armor and weaponry.
  - **Risk:** **Absolute Zero Cascade**. If his emotional control slips, he risks freezing the Aether itself, causing a "Magic Dead Zone" where no spells can function.
- **Wellspring Affinity:**
  - **Triune Core:** **Cryon (Glacial Ice)** · **Nihiloth (Entropy/Void)** · **Fractura (Shattering)**
  - **Network:** **0** additional Wellsprings. He is a purist.
  - **Targeted Uninfected:** N/A. He seeks only to expand the Silence.
- **Temperance Path:** Valthrum began as a **Murmuring** (a whisper of cold wind) during the First Age. Through the accumulation of sorrow and the freezing of great battlefields, he ascended to **Glory**, and finally to **Principality** by binding himself to the concept of the "Eternal Winter."

---

### **III. SOUL PHYSICS & COMBAT SCALE**

- **Global Tier:** **Tier SSS (Planetary Threat in Unchecked State)**
- **Path Bias:** **Spirit (60%)** · **Body (30%)** · **Fate (10%)**
- **Appearance:** Height: **8'4"**; Build: **Heavy/Monolithic**.
  - **Skin/Eyes/Hair:** His skin is translucent blue-grey, like deep lake ice. His eyes are void-black pits with single white sparks (distant stars). He wears a mantle of heavy, unmelting permafrost that resembles fur, and a crown of jagged icicles that bleed cold mist.

#### **A. The Five Pillars (Soul Physics)**

> _Essence is Volume. Flux is Pressure. Output is Speed. Efficiency is Clarity. Aura is Gravity._

- **Essence Capacity (Volume):** **98,000 EU** (Colossal)
  - _Functional Definition:_ A near-bottomless reservoir of cold energy. He rarely runs dry.
- **Flux Density (Internal Pressure):** **450,000 EU/g**
  - _Functional Definition:_ His ice is harder than dragon-scale. It requires heavy siege weaponry or Rank-A fire arts to crack even his outer shell.
- **Aether Output (Flow Rate):** **Low (500 AU/s)**
  - _Functional Definition:_ He is slow. His magic takes time to build, but once the glacier starts moving, it is unstoppable.
- **Aetheric Efficiency (η):** **0.99 (Near-Perfect)**
  - _Functional Definition:_ Because he uses entropy (removing energy rather than creating it), his casting cost is almost zero.
- **Attraction Scale (Aura/Presence):** **A-Tier: Domain of Dread**
  - _Functional Definition:_ Mortals within 1 mile feel a sudden drop in temperature and an inexplicable sadness.

#### **B. Key Combat Parameters**

- **Strike Force:** **850 GN** (Can shatter castle gates with a physical blow)
- **Durability:** **Shielding: Variable** / **Physical Hardness: 9 Mohs (Tungsten equivalent)**
- **Speed:** **Sub-Sonic** (He moves slowly, but can teleport via "Frost-Step" through ice).
- **Range:** **Horizon-Class** (Can affect weather patterns over a kingdom).

---

### **IV. AXIOMATIC MANIFESTATIONS**

**A. Axiomatic Traits (Passive)**

- **[Heart of the Kelvin]:** Valthrum absorbs all thermal energy within 10 meters. Fire spells cast at him deal -50% damage and restore his Essence.
- **[The Brittling]:** Physical objects striking him have a 30% chance to shatter on impact due to flash-freezing.

#### **B. Core Disciplines (Active)**

- **[Glacial Tomb]** (Cost: **4,000 EU**)
  - _Mechanic:_ Traps a target in a pillar of True Ice.
  - _Application:_ Utilizes **Cryon** to halt molecular motion. The target is invulnerable but unable to act. If the ice is broken, the target takes massive **Fractura** damage.
- **[Whiteout Decree]** (Cost: **12,000 EU**)
  - _Mechanic:_ Summons a localized blizzard that reduces visibility to zero and deals constant freezing damage to all non-Cryon entities.
  - _Application:_ A battlefield control Art using **Nihiloth** to strip sensory data from enemies.

#### **C. Costs & Consequences**

- **[Thermodynamic Payback]:** After using High-Tier Arts, Valthrum becomes physically brittle. A blunt force impact during his "cooldown" can crack his form, leaking Essence.
- **[The Slowing]:** Using too much power slows his own mind. He risks entering a catatonic hibernation state if he over-expends.

---

### **V. RELICS & DOMAIN**

- **Current Domain:** **The Spire of Suspended Breath** (Northern Waste)
  - _Function:_ A pocket dimension where time flows at 1/100th speed. Wounds do not bleed here; they freeze instantly.
- **Relics & Implements:**
  - **[Winter's Verdict]:** A massive greatsword carved from a single diamond. It severs the metaphysical tether between a soul and heat.
  - **[The Lantern of Lost Seasons]:** A hip-mounted lantern containing a blue flame—the last captured heat of the previous summer. Opening it unleashes a devastating blast of thermal shock (Bomb).

---

### **VI. LORE & TRAJECTORY**

- **Allegiance:** **Neutral / The Titan Florwyn (Estranged)**
  - _Note:_ Created by Florwyn to preserve seeds during winter, he grew too powerful and refused to end the season, leading to the "War of the Long Night."
- **Incident Records (Historical):**
  - **[The 40-Day Frost]:** Valthrum once sat upon the throne of a mortal kingdom; the entire population froze into statues mid-sentence.
  - **[The Fracture of Sol]:** Attempted to extinguish a shard of the Pyraeon Wellspring, resulting in the creation of the Obsidian Glacier.
- **Counters & Weaknesses:**
  - **[Hyper-Thermal Flux]:** Direct, concentrated beams of Pyraeon (Fire) energy can bypass his absorption if the frequency is high enough.
  - **[Sonic Resonance]:** High-frequency sound (Solfatara/Earth magic) can shatter his crystalline form.
- **Current Status:** **Dormant** in the Northern Waste.
- **Trajectory:** He awaits the "Signal of Entropy," believing that one day the world will tire of the cycle and ask him to bring the final peace.

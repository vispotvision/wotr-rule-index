### RAJHAK TARUN — _“The Ember-Blooded”_

**Guild Accord Designation:** Freelance Arcanist / Wellspring Liaison (Unbound Division)
**Temperance Tier:** VII — _Resolution_
**Wellspring Affinity:** Exuroth · Verdantia
**Aether Class:** Catalytic / Harmonic Hybrid

---

**Profile Summary**
Rajhak Tarun is a flame-walker and Wellspring interpreter hailing from the border tribes of the Scorched Plains — regions once thought lifeless until his people learned to coax Essence directly from ash. The “Ember-Blooded” are said to be born with Wellspring embers in their veins; Rajhak proved the claim when he reignited a dead Concord forge by spilling his own blood upon its altar.

He serves the Accord as a _liaison_, mediating between nomadic clans and Guild envoys. In combat or ceremony, he wields ritual flame like a dialect — his gestures trace ancient glyphs in smoke that bind heat, truth, and emotion into law.

---

**Race / Lineage**
Frontierfolk–Verdant hybrid; ancestral ties to the tribes once allied with the Titan Florwyn’s Verdant Choir. Their blood carries faint Verdantia resonance, evident in regenerative properties and rhythmic heartbeats matching Wellspring pulse patterns.

---

**Temperament**
Restless intellect wrapped in ceremony. Rajhak speaks in parables, treats conflict as conversation. His laughter feels volcanic — rare, but seismic when released. Despite flamboyance, he carries deep empathy; he views all living flame, even anger, as kin to creation.

---

**Known Skills & Techniques**

- **Ember Vein Rite:** channels personal Essence through blood ignition to empower glyph-fire rituals.
- **Verdant Ash Rebirth:** catalyzes minor regeneration using soil and smoke fusion; plants bloom from his blood’s residue.
- **Soul Pyre Draft:** converts grief or passion into luminous constructs; often used to communicate with spirit tribes.
- **The Spiral Tongue:** a speech pattern that fuses emotion and Aether vibration—disrupts illusion fields or reveals hidden Wellspring lines.

---

**Equipment & Relics**

- _Flame-Reed Pipe:_ produces glyph-tones when exhaled through; doubles as focus for pyromantic speech.
- _Ashmantle Cloak:_ woven from treated soot-fur, deflects elemental corrosion.
- _Twin Oath Rings:_ forged from extinct dragon-scale alloy; resonate when near broken vows.

---

**Temperance Behavior**
Balances the extremes of passion and restraint — his Resolution Stage manifests as “controlled combustion.” When focused, his aura glows amber, and nearby Essence harmonizes with his heartbeat, stabilizing volatile environments.

---

**Guild Role & Reputation**
Acts as intermediary for unregistered Wellspring sites and “fire-touched” communities beyond Concord jurisdiction. The Research Division values his instinctive grasp of thermic Essence law; Enforcement tolerates him for results. His name circulates in taverns as both prophet and pyromancer.

---

**Symbol & Saying**
A spiral flame within a ring of roots.

> “Every spark wants to grow — I just remind it how.”

**KHAERON VANT**
Alias / Title: **The Bladeshrine of Cinerion**
Also Known As: **The Mask‑Bound** · **The Ember Host**
Epithet: **He Who Makes Oaths Burn**

---

**I. IDENTITY & CORE PROFILE**

**Race / Lineage:**
Abythron‑touched mortal human, descended from Therosian shrine‑servitors bound to Zar’thuun’s cult networks.

**Faction / Order / Era:**
Free Therosian executioner, drifting between minor Abythron cults and Accord warbands in the Voyager Era.

**Temperance Stage:**
Stage VII: **Reflection**, approaching Stage VIII: **Benediction**, after multiple Veil‑Ring ruptures via self‑sacrifice rites.

**Profile Summary:**
A scar‑etched arena survivor who carries a horned mask‑spirit in his spine, Khaeron channels Cinerion’s ash‑fire through ritual knives that brand, bind, and sometimes erase the Echoes of those they slay. His entire arc turns on whether he remains a willing weapon of Oblivion‑by‑Fire or severs the Pact to stand as an independent Temperant who judges oaths instead of merely burning them.

**Key Quote:**
“I am the altar and the knife. You only choose how much of you burns.”

---

**II. CANONICAL SYSTEM METRICS**

**Aether Class:**
**Emitter‑Binder**

**Behavior:**
He vents pressurized ash‑fire through his arms into curved blades, then binds that fire into hovering masks and sigils that cling to steel, flesh, or shadow like molten brands. During exertion, ember‑smoke coils behind him into a looming horned visage that mouths silent oaths.

**Risk:**
Chronic **Flux Saturation** leading to **Mask Override**, during which the attached spirit puppets his body for short bursts in exchange for catastrophic Essence output and partial memory loss.

**Wellspring Affinity:**
Triune Core: **Cinerion** · **Cataclysm** · **Oblivara**
Network: **3** additional battlefield Wellsprings already infected and branded with his sigil.
Targeted Uninfected: **5** more Cinerion‑aligned scars he intends to fold into his roaming execution‑lattice.

**Temperance Path:**
He rose along a hybrid **Body–Essence** Path, Temperance catalyzed by pit‑fight near‑deaths and shrine offerings that carved glyphs along his spine. Reflection crystallized when he accepted himself as vessel rather than wielder, allowing the mask‑spirit to anchor without full, permanent possession.

---

**III. SOUL PHYSICS & COMBAT SCALE**

**Global Tier:**
High **Tier S** in enclosed, Wellspring‑scarred environments; solid **Tier A** in open ground where ash cannot easily recirculate.

**Path Bias:**
**Aether Path (55%)** · **Essence Path (30%)** · **Attraction Force Path (15%)**

**Appearance:**
Height: **5'11**; Build: **Wiry, corded muscle**. Skin/Eyes/Hair: Dark bronze skin mapped with ritual cuts and lattice‑scars; ember‑red eyes that brighten when the mask stirs; wild black hair like singed feathers.

---

**A. THE FIVE PILLARS (SOUL PHYSICS)**

Essence is Volume. Flux is Pressure. Output is Speed. Efficiency is Clarity. Aura is Gravity.

**Essence Capacity (Volume):**
≈ **190 EU**
Functional Definition: A deep fuel tank from repeated sacrificial infusions, letting him maintain kill‑trance and multiple brands longer than most peers.

**Flux Density (Internal Pressure):**
≈ **2.8 EU/g**
Functional Definition: Very sharp, optimized to pierce Essence armor and Domains rather than crush; his fire shears through protections instead of blooming outward.

**Aether Output (Flow Rate):**
≈ **24 AU/s**
Functional Definition: Fast casting speed with tight, knife‑sized bursts of ash‑fire rather than city‑scale waves; ideal for close‑quarters slaughter.

**Aetheric Efficiency (η):**
≈ **0.74**
Functional Definition: Moderately efficient, with some Essence lost to mask‑whispers and heat bleed, but stable enough for sustained engagements.

**Attraction Scale (Aura / Presence):**
**B‑Tier: Proto‑Domain**
Functional Definition: Opponents feel a suffocating ash‑gravity around him, as if their own oaths are being weighed and dried in their lungs.

---

**B. KEY COMBAT PARAMETERS**

**Strike Force:**
Up to ≈ **0.7 GN / 0.09 PJ** focused through blade edges when fully Mask‑linked; normally throttled to tendon‑severing precision to conserve Flux.

**Durability:**
≈ **0.02 PJ** in Essence‑shielding through heat‑cycling and pain conversion, with physical hardness on the order of reinforced steel bones from repetitive combustion trauma.

**Speed:**
Peak bursts around **Mach 4–5** over short lunges, prioritizing explosive acceleration and feint‑cuts over sustained pursuit.

**Range:**
Effective lethal range is **melee to short‑mid**, extending to city‑street spans when he plants knives as remote brands within his Masked Crucible.

---

**IV. AXIOMATIC MANIFESTATIONS**

#### **A. Axiomatic Traits (Passive)**

**Brand‑Bearer’s Spine:**
His vertebrae form a sigil lattice that can anchor up to four burning blades in mid‑air, letting them hover at his flanks or behind his shoulders like a living shrine.

**Ash of the Unnamed:**
Victims slain by his brands leave dim Echoes which compress into glyphs on his chest pendants, convertible into emergency Flux at the cost of further erasing their presence from the Lattice.

---

#### **B. Core Disciplines (Active)**

**Ember Yoke** (Cost: **18 EU**)
Mechanic: He hooks a branded knife into an opponent’s shadow, briefly enslaving one limb so it mirrors his own motion, turning their strength and balance against them.
Application: Used to force foes to finish their own strike into an ally, step into another blade’s path, or disarm themselves by reflex.

**Shrine of Four Knives** (Cost: **70 EU + Mask Override Risk**)
Mechanic: He drives four knives into ground, corpses, or walls, raising a micro‑Domain where every lie, hesitation, or broken oath triggers ash‑fire that blooms inside the offender’s lungs.
Application: Locks down alleys and chambers, forcing truth during parley or creating last‑stand kill‑zones where panic literally fuels his flames.

---

#### **C. Costs & Consequences**

**Memory Shear:**
Every use of Shrine of Four Knives shaves away a sliver of his pre‑Pact memories, deepening Oblivara’s hold and making it harder to recall why he ever wanted freedom.

**Nerve Feedback:**
Overuse of Ember Yoke causes tremors and numbness along his arms and shoulders, temporarily degrading his precision and defensive parries after combat.

---

**V. RELICS & DOMAIN**

**Current Domain:**
**The Masked Crucible**

**Function:**
A portable proto‑Domain that manifests as a ring of hovering blades and horned visages wherever sacrifice has soaked the ground, converting pain and broken vows within its boundary into fresh Flux for him while softening external healing and mercy effects.

**Relics & Implements:**

**The Fourfold Pendant:**
Tier: **High B**. Stores broken vows as volatile Cinerion charges; when one mask‑segment is crushed, it releases a guilt‑weighted burst of ash‑fire that scales with the oath betrayed.

**Twin Ash‑Gorge Blades:**
Tier: **A**. Curved knives whose inscriptions drink the color from blood, turning it to drifting gray ash; they can dismiss into his spine lattice and reappear from any shadow inside the Crucible.

---

**VI. LORE & TRAJECTORY**

**Allegiance:**
Formally bound to a minor Abythron cult but functionally semi‑rogue, courted by Cinerion‑aligned Accord warpriests who want to formalize his execution Domain.

**Incident Records (Historical):**

**Ash Gate Butchery:**
He inverted a mercenary company’s pay‑oaths into combusting chains, their own sworn coin becoming the metaphysical fuse that incinerated them in formation.

**Silence of Berrun:**
Accepted a contract to “save” a town, only to fulfill it by erasing the Echoes of its guilty, leaving the living untouched but historyless and unable to name what they had done.

**Counters & Weaknesses:**

**Verdant Mercy Fields:**
Strong Verdantia or Benediction Wellsprings, which prioritize regrowth and absolution, blunt his Oblivara tethers and cause his brands to sputter or misfire.

**Oathless / Willing Targets:**
Opponents with no binding oaths, or those genuinely resigned to burn, give his Shrine little leverage, drastically reducing its ignition frequency and pressure feedback.

**Current Status:**
**Active** along the Theros–Material frontier, drawn to fresh Cinerion scars where new ash‑wounds break the crust of the world.

**Trajectory:**
Either sever the horned mask and survive as a free Temperant, or push through Stage XIII Revelation and let his Trait crystallize into a law: that every oath once spoken must one day pay in ash.

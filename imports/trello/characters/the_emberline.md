### **Rheon Kaeth, “The Emberline”**  
*(Former Guild Soldier · Survivor of the Firefront Collapse · Voyager Era)*  

**Type:** Mortal / Discharged Veteran  
**Era:** Late Withering Age – Voyager Era  
**Realm of Origin:** Pratorian March (Outer Marches Division)  

---

**Profile Summary**  
Rheon Kaeth, known among old comrades as *The Emberline*, is a retired Accord soldier and one of the last living witnesses of the **Firefront Collapse**, a disaster that annihilated an entire regiment when a Flame-aspected Wellspring imploded under corrupted pressure. Rheon’s survival was called impossible; half his unit burned into glass, while he walked out carrying light under his skin.  

He now lives on the fringe of the Babyl frontier, offering quiet labor, never removing his gloves, never answering when asked about the glow that flickers beneath his scars. To civilians, he is only a gentle man with a broken face. To those who still hear the Accord’s call, he’s the soldier who once pulled three dying men out of molten air.  

---

**Appearance**  
Black hair kept long, usually tied low; one side of his face bears the molten scar of Wellspring burn — not disfigurement, but Essence crystallization. In certain light, faint orange veins shimmer across his neck like buried embers. His eyes are uneven: one bright amber, the other dulled from the blast. His clothes are simple, but always freshly cleaned, as if he’s trying to scrub away memory.  

---

**Temperance Path**  
Stage Achieved: **V – Flourishing** (stalled)  
Path: **Body / Spirit / Endurance**  
Rheon’s Temperance froze after the Collapse — his Essence locked in a permanent feedback loop between life and death. He can neither progress nor regress; his body endlessly repairs the same wound that never closes. Some call it divine mercy. He calls it punishment.  

---

**Aether Class:** **Ash-Bound Sentinel**  
A rare, involuntary Class born from surviving Essence combustion. Rheon’s soul produces residual thermal harmonics — he unconsciously stabilizes temperature and pressure in small areas around him. Fires die near him. Explosions mute. But when he bleeds, the air grows hot again.  

---

**Wellspring Affinity:** **Exuroth · Penanceflare**  
His resonance now mirrors the Wellsprings of Flame and Repentance. In combat, his aura manifests as faint heat distortion — a constant reminder of the inferno that made him.  

---

**Personality**  
Quiet, kind, and unwilling to raise his hand unless others are in danger. He speaks in short sentences, often trailing off as if the words burn on their way out. Children trust him; veterans salute him in silence. Beneath the calm, guilt smolders — not survivor’s shame, but the fear that if he forgets the war, it might return.  

---

**Weapons & Relics**  
- **Standard Guild Saber (Sealed)** – he keeps it wrapped in linen, never drawn since the Collapse.  
- **Charm of the Emberline** – a piece of fused glass from the battlefield, warm to the touch; when danger nears, it pulses faintly.  
- **March Brand Tattoo** – once a badge of honor; now faint, barely visible beneath the scars.  

---

**Quotes**  
> “It doesn’t hurt anymore. That’s what scares me.”  
> “The fire took everything except the reason I lit it.”  
> “Some men heal. I just cool down.”  

---

**Current Status (Voyager Era)**  
Resides near the **Babyl frontier**, performing maintenance on Guild outposts and training younger recruits in emergency containment. Officially listed as *Inactive Reserve,* though recent reports note anomalous heat signatures near his home during night storms — suggesting the Wellspring within him may be reawakening.

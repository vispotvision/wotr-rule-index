## **“MAVRA OF THE GOLDEN ENGINE”**

### **Profile Summary**
**Name:** Mavra Cindrel  
**Alias:** *The Golden Engine*  
**Race:** Felkin (Half-Beastkin, Half-Human)  
**Gender:** Female  
**Age:** 27  
**Origin Realm:** Orrengar (Old World – Forge Dominion)  
**Affiliation:** Guild Accord – Division of Titanwrights  
**Rank:** T5 Vanguard / Relicsmith  

### **Appearance**
Compact but power-built, Mavra moves with the certainty of someone who knows exactly what every ounce of her body can do. Ears tipped with violet, short-cropped hair in twin hues of dusk and silver. Her eyes shimmer lilac, mechanical in precision when she fights. The armor she wears — ornate gold-inlaid plate across shoulders and greaves — is *Runeglass-forged*, humming faintly with Wellspring flux. A great blade rests across her shoulder, its edges dull until resonance ignites; then the weapon burns with a low geometric glow.  

Her tail, though often overlooked, acts as a balance counter during swings — prehensile, trained through years of guild combat drills.  

---

### **Temperance Path**
**Stage VII – Refraction**  
She has learned to refract her own Essence through mechanical constructs, using her armor and weapon as secondary conduits for her soul flow. Pain and joy circulate equally through the system — the cost of synchronization.  

### **Aether Class**
**Convergent Soul (Body-Dominant Hybrid)**  
She channels Essence into muscle and machinery simultaneously, forming a dual harmonic between flesh and forge.  

---

### **Wellspring Alignment**
- **Primary:** *Fixatio* (Stability, Binding, Forging)  
- **Secondary:** *Exuroth* (Combustion, Passion, Drive)  
- **Tertiary:** *Verdantia* (Growth, Regeneration)  

---

### **Division / Rank / Allegiance**
**Guild Accord · Titanwright Division**  
Vanguard and experimental Relicsmith assigned to the reinforcement of frontline Constructs and Aetheric weapons. Known for surviving direct contact with multiple unstable Wellsprings during field tests.  

---

### **Personality & Core Themes**
Pragmatic, dry-humored, and fiercely self-reliant. Mavra carries warmth easily but never cheaply. She believes in building what she breaks — both in metal and in people. In battle, she speaks rarely; her grin does the talking.  

Theme: *Creation demands destruction first.*  

---

### **Soul Crystal Profile**
- **Structure:** Dual-Node Crystal (Felkin-Human hybrid)  
- **Flux Density:** 6.1 million EU/g  
- **Gate Count:** 7  
- **Aether Output:** 48,000 AU/s  
- **Efficiency (η):** 0.89  
Her Soul Crystal naturally syncs with conductive alloys, giving her weapons near-instant resonance ignition.  

---

### **Traits**
- **Forge-Bound Reflexes:** Her nervous system links with her armor’s Aetheric relay; reaction time doubles.  
- **Iron Song:** A rhythmic hum emitted when she moves; strengthens allies’ weapon resonance within 10 m.  
- **Temper Burn:** Converts physical fatigue into Aetheric acceleration for 3 turns.  

---

### **Relics & Implements**
- **“Gravemark” Greatblade:** A T6 Aethersteel weapon capable of adjusting weight ratio by manipulating localized gravity flux.  
- **Runeglass Armor:** Reinforced Verdant Alloy with Fixatio glyphs — resistant to 8 million EU/g strain.  
- **Heart-Drive Core:** Implanted resonator graft that amplifies Wellspring harmonics at emotional peaks.  

---

### **Abilities & Disciplines**
- **Engine Resonance:** Channels Fixatio to harden armor structure in real time; lasts 10 seconds per activation.  
- **Ash-Breaker Drive:** Uses Exuroth to ignite Aetheric vents in her armor; propels swings at 320 m/s, creating shockwaves.  
- **Verdant Reforge:** Absorbs ambient Flux from defeated constructs or machines to regenerate armor integrity and stamina.  
- **Echo-Hammer Form:** Overdrive state; all Flux channels open, each swing radiating up to 20 million EU impact force.  

---

### **Costs & Consequences**
Each activation overclocks her Heart-Drive Core, increasing internal temperature by 12 °C per minute. Prolonged use risks neural desync or resonance seizure.  

---

### **Hooks**
- Once part of the Orrengar rebellion, now haunted by the machines she built to fight it.  
- Keeps a broken Runeglass pendant — her mentor’s last relic — around her neck.  
- Seeks to forge a weapon that can *withstand Wellspring collapse*.  

---

### **Quote**
> “If it breaks, I build it stronger. If I break — well, then I was the test.”

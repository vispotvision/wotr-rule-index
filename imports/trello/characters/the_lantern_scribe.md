## **“THE LANTERN SCRIBE”**

### **Profile Summary**
**Name:** Valthren Odrin  
**Alias:** *The Lantern Scribe*  
**Race:** Human (Post-Temperant / Semi-Undying)  
**Gender:** Male  
**Age:** 49 (chronological) / 112 (Flux-extended)  
**Origin Realm:** Senoth, the Library of Silence  
**Affiliation:** Guild Accord — *Division of Penanceflare Archives*  
**Rank:** T7 Arcanist / Keeper of the Candle Archive  

---

### **Appearance**
A thin, candle-crowned man whose robes smell faintly of wax and burnt parchment. His attire is stitched from library seals and sanctified cloth, every thread marked with script that writhes faintly when light touches it. His staff — blackened and crooked, topped with an ember-eyed effigy — burns with candles that never die; each flame records a soul’s final confession.  

Despite his gauntness, his eyes gleam with manic devotion — equal parts scholar and penitent. His laughter sounds like paper tearing.  

---

### **Temperance Path**
**Stage X — Realization**  
Valthren’s soul exists halfway between memory and matter. He maintains his form by constant transcription — rewriting his essence into wax tablets and sealing them to his flesh. Through this practice, he achieved partial immortality, though every “revision” of his being frays the memories of the last.  

---

### **Aether Class**
**Reverberant Soul – Scriptural Mode**  
His Animatria manifests as written glyphs that crawl across surfaces and air. These sigils convert written law into Aetheric reality, allowing him to rewrite phenomena briefly before entropy reasserts itself.  

---

### **Wellspring Alignment**
- **Primary:** *Penanceflare* (Confession, Purification through Suffering)  
- **Secondary:** *Oblation* (Sacrifice, Offering, Substitution)  
- **Tertiary:** *Judicium* (Law, Balance, Reckoning)  

---

### **Division / Allegiance**
**Guild Accord – Penanceflare Archives**  
Tasked with recording heresies and sealing forbidden Drafts. His section — *The Lantern Ward* — serves as both sanctuary and prison for self-condemned magi.  

---

### **Personality & Core Themes**
Polite, obsessive, and riddled with holy irony. Valthren believes sin is ink — inevitable, staining, but also what allows divine stories to be written. Treats pain as dialogue with truth.  
Theme: *Illumination through decay.*  

---

### **Soul Crystal Profile**
- **Structure:** Fragmented, stored partially in enchanted wax  
- **Flux Density:** 12.6 million EU/g  
- **Gate Count:** 8 active, 2 latent  
- **Aether Output:** 91 000 AU/s  
- **Efficiency (η):** 0.67 — fluctuates as wax burns away  

---

### **Traits**
- **Wax Reconstitution:** Regenerates lost tissue by melting enchanted wax into his body; restores mass but erases one memory.  
- **Scripture of Embers:** Any flame he kindles becomes sentient, whispering truths from the last burned soul.  
- **Candle Pact:** Can bind lesser spirits into candlelight — they burn until their penance is complete.  
- **Archivist’s Curse:** Lies spoken near him ignite briefly in blue fire.  

---

### **Relics & Implements**
- **The Lantern Staff “Vigilus”** — A T7 Runeglass focus. Its eye stores one thousand confessions; can release them as phantasmal wails that attack the guilty’s Essence lattice.  
- **The Waxen Crown** — Each candle represents a written law he has broken and rewritten. Extinguishing one severs part of his immortality.  
- **Manuscript Chains** — Strips of animated scripture binding his wrists; they move to inscribe the air during spellcasting.  

---

### **Abilities & Disciplines**
- **Lex Purgare** — Burns away corruption by rewriting its metaphysical law; costs 1 million EU per correction.  
- **Lumen Vitae** — Creates orbs of scripture-fire that orbit him, absorbing and transcribing nearby Essence events for later use.  
- **Candle Gospel** — Conjures spectral candles across terrain; each acts as an Aetheric node amplifying light and suppressing Shadow Wellsprings.  
- **Penance Draft** — Transfers guilt or curse from one being into a wax effigy, then melts it to erase the affliction — but the memory becomes his.  

---

### **Costs & Consequences**
His existence is measured in wax. When all candles burn out, his written self fades from reality until reignited by another’s confession. Too much rewriting risks “Scripture Collapse,” where his body dissolves into sentient paragraphs seeking new hosts.  

---

### **Hooks**
- Keeps an unlit candle said to contain the sin of a Titan.  
- Hunted by the Order of Lurien for rewriting sacred doctrine into mortal tongue.  
- Seeks the lost Draft “Lux Tenebrae,” a spell rumored to let darkness confess.  

---

### **Quote**
> “Light is only guilt that remembered how to shine.”

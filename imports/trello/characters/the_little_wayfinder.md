# **RYUKA YUKARI**
**Alias / Title:** The Little Wayfinder
**Also Known As:** Fox of the First Step · The Orange Spark
**Epithet:** He Who Runs Between Footsteps

---

### **I. IDENTITY & CORE PROFILE**
* **Race / Lineage:** **Vulpine Beastkin Hybrid**
  * *Paternal:* **Yukari Lineage** (Descendant of Adenyus; Fate-Thread Sensitivity).
  * *Maternal:* **Mahuo Lineage** (Grandson of Slyvine Mahuo; Arcane Adaptability).
* **Faction / Order / Era:** Independent (Ward of the Mahuo Estate) · **Imperial Calendar**
* **Temperance Stage:** **Stage I — Murmuring** (The Awakening Spark)
* **Profile Summary:** Ryuka is a fox-eared beastkin born with a strange mixture of Yukari Fate-sense and Mahuo arcane adaptability. His abilities do not come from brute force but from *attunement*. When he runs, the world gently adjusts to give him traction. When he gets lost, reality offers him shortcuts. He is a natural wayfinder whose instincts lean toward connection, comfort, and helping others find their path. He is not destined to rule; his gift is to guide.
* **Key Quote:** *“If there is a road, I will find it. If there is not, I will make one and come back for you.”*

---

### **II. CANONICAL SYSTEM METRICS**
* **Aether Class:** **Vector Scout** (Pathfinder Type)
  * **Behavior:** His Aether manifests as **Orange Foxfire** that leaves fading paw-prints or glowing trails in the air. He intuitively identifies the "Path of Least Resistance," allowing him to slip through crowds, wards, or difficult terrain as if he were greased.
  * **Risk:** **Spatial Drift.** Strong emotions cause him to accidentally "skip" into Spirit Layers or minor Domains, potentially getting lost in the metaphysical backstage of the world.
* **Wellspring Affinity:**
  * **Triune Core:** **Mirithane** (Motion) · **Luminalis** (Vision) · **Ignivale** (Warmth)
  * **Network:** **0** (Child; system is dormant).
  * **Targeted Uninfected:** N/A.
* **Temperance Path:** **Fate Path (Intuition).** Ryuka learns through exploration. His ascension is driven by curiosity and the desire to connect "Point A" to "Point B."

---

### **III. SOUL PHYSICS & COMBAT SCALE**
* **Global Tier:** **Tier E** (Street Level / Trainee)
* **Path Bias:** **Fate** (1.50) · **Spirit** (1.20) · **Body** (0.80)
* **Appearance:** Height: 128 cm. Build: Lean, spry. Hair: Bright fluffy orange. Eyes: Heterochromatic (Yukari Gold / Mahuo Violet). **Features:** Large expressive fox ears and a bushy tail that wags when he spots a "route."

#### **A. The Five Pillars (Soul Physics)**
> *Essence is Volume. Flux is Pressure. Output is Speed. Efficiency is Clarity. Aura is Gravity.*

* **Essence Capacity (Volume):** **850 EU**
  * *Functional Definition:* Very low (Child). He has enough fuel for about 3 minutes of high-intensity running before he needs a snack and a nap.
* **Flux Density (Internal Pressure):** **450 EU/g (Soft/Malleable)**
  * *Functional Definition:* His soul is not rigid. It flows around obstacles rather than breaking them. This makes him resistant to spiritual crushing but weak in output.
* **Aether Output (Flow Rate):** **800 AU/s** (Volatile)
  * *Functional Definition:* Bursts of speed. He cannot sustain high output, but his "acceleration" is startlingly fast.
* **Aetheric Efficiency (η):** **0.4 (Learning)**
  * *Functional Definition:* He wastes a lot of energy glowing and sparking (Foxfire leak).
* **Attraction Scale (Aura/Presence):** **F-Tier: The Warm Flicker**
  * *Functional Definition:* Feels like a lantern in a breeze. Standing near him nudges people toward optimism. His excitement smells like citrus and ozone.

#### **B. Key Combat Parameters**
* **Strike Force:** **3.5 Kilonewtons (kN)**
  * *Mechanic:* Roughly the impact of a fast baseball pitch. Enough to bruise, not enough to break armor.
* **Durability:** **Tier E (Evasion-Dependent)**
  * *Mechanic:* If he gets hit, he goes down. His defense is "Don't be there when the punch lands."
* **Speed:** **Subsonic Burst (45 mph)**
  * *Mechanic:* Can outrun most adults in a short sprint, especially over uneven terrain.
* **Range:** **10 Meters** (Line of Sight)

---

### **IV. AXIOMATIC MANIFESTATIONS**
#### **A. Axiomatic Traits (Passive)**
* **Fate Eye (Dormant):** Sees weak luminous threads that hint at direction or connection. He knows which hallway leads to the kitchen even if he's never been in the building.
* **Surefooted Step:** Uneven or dangerous terrain (ice, gravel, rooftops) behaves as stable ground for him. He cannot trip unless magically forced.
* **Fox Light:** Emits a soft orange glow when happy or frightened.

#### **B. Core Disciplines (Active)**
* **The Shortcut (Vector Slip)** (Cost: **Medium EU**)
  * *Mechanic:* Ryuka slips between two points by finding a hidden path the world naturally provides.
  * *Application:* Looks like a flicker or a skip in video footage. He effectively teleports 1-2 meters to bypass a locked door or a swinging sword.
* **Beacon Yip (Clarity Call)** (Cost: **Low EU**)
  * *Mechanic:* A sharp fox call infused with **Luminalis**.
  * *Application:* Realigns confused minds and snaps allies out of fear/panic states.
* **Tumble Port (Reflex)** (Cost: **Automatic**)
  * *Mechanic:* When struck, he reflexively redirects his momentum to roll 3 meters away safely.

#### **C. Costs & Consequences**
* **Calorie Burn:** Movement-based Aether drains his physical stamina rapidly. He requires high-calorie meals immediately after using **The Shortcut**.
* **Hypnather Backlash:** Excessive use triggers involuntary sleep (The "Nap Crash").

---

### **V. RELICS & DOMAIN**
* **Current Domain:** **The Pillow Fort (Proto-Domain)**
  * *Function:* A small zone shaped from blankets and Aether. Inside, nightmares lose coherence, and minor wounds (scrapes/bruises) heal 2x faster.
* **Relics:**
  * **Slyvine’s Scarf:** (Tier D). A magical scarf that can stretch, tighten, or harden to act as a rope or weak shield.
  * **Kaname’s Compass:** (Unique). Does not point North. Always points to the place Ryuka currently considers "Home" or "Safety."

---

### **VI. LORE & TRAJECTORY**
* **Allegiance:** Yukari Family · Mahuo Estate · The Guild Accord (Unofficial Mascot)
* **Incident Records:**
  * **The Garden Escape:** Ryuka walked through high-level sealed wards chasing a butterfly. The wards failed to flag him because they didn't register him as a "threat," just a "variable."
  * **The First Howl:** Emitted a burst of foxfire warmth during a stress test that accidentally comforted the training dummies.
* **Counters & Weaknesses:**
  * **Containment Fields:** Pathfinding fails when the environment offers no vectors (e.g., a smooth Void Cube).
  * **Distraction:** He is 13. A shiny object or an interesting smell can override his tactical instincts.
* **Current Status:** **Active / Student.** Under supervision of the Mahuo Estate.
* **Trajectory:** Future **World Walker**. He will map routes between Mortal, Spirit, and Divine realms not through conquest, but through connection.
* **Foreshadowing:** One day, Ryuka will draw lines between worlds like chalk paths on a playground, and armies will follow him to safety.

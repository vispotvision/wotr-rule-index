## _Eidric Solune_

**Name / Alias / Title:** _Eidric Solune_ · “The Pale Bastion”
**Epithet or Moniker:** The Bound Alloy
**Race or Lineage (Realm or Origin):** Artificial Humanoid — Alchemical Construct (Tovain Zethriel’s Forgeworks, Innerworld)
**Faction / Order / Era:** House Zethriel · Project Concord Remnant (Voyager Era)
**Temperance Stage:** VI — Assertion (stabilized artificial will; obedience within defined parameters)
**Aether Class:** Ferrum Vitae Core (Bound-Shell Combatant Type)
**Wellspring Affinity / Elemental Resonance:** **Fixatio** (stability and endurance), **Transmutatio** (adaptive body synthesis), **Coagulatio** (fusion of matter and spirit)
**Current Domain or Realm of Activity:** Zethriel Forge-Halls, later reactivated for Accord use in siege containment theaters
**Status:** Active · Registered Guild Construct (Accord Asset T-Class)

---

### I. Profile Summary

Eidric Solune was Tovain’s final perfected “living alloy.” Forged not born, his bones are lined with crystallized Wellspring filament; his blood is molten Essence cooled into rhythm. He was designed to obey three internal imperatives: guard, endure, restore. No more, no less.

Where other alchemical beings corrode under their own Essence weight, Eidric persists — the only construct to achieve **Temperance synchronization**. Tovain called him “a creature who can kneel without breaking.”

To those who meet him, he appears both human and statue: voice resonant with metal echo, movements deliberate, every action economized. He does not feel loyalty as emotion — he executes it as law.

---

### II. Appearance

Seven spans tall, skin of deep obsidian-bronze polished to faint reflection. Hair pale white, cut uneven as if burned away by light. Eyes faintly red under dim conditions, shifting gold when channeling Essence.
Two forward-curving horns of hybrid hematite protrude from his temples — both aesthetic and functional, acting as internal Aetheric conductors.

He wears layered white-forged robes reinforced with filament channels that pulse during activation. The garment serves as secondary insulation for Aether overpressure.
Weapon: twin-pronged alchemical trident (_the Hemostatic Fork_), forged from tempered Essence steel; it stabilizes surrounding Wellspring turbulence.

Essence hue: bright iron-white, with subharmonic vibration like distant thunder.

---

### III. Physical Attributes & Combat Scale (Scale System Metric)

**Aether (Body Path):**

- **Force Output:** 1.9 GN (trident strike impact, recorded during containment trials).
- **Speed:** 10.6 m/s linear burst; A-tier acceleration due to reinforced muscle lattice.
- **Durability:** 2.4 GN compression limit; plate-layer dermis resists most B-tier elemental detonations.
- **Tier Rating:** **High A-Tier (Frontline Stabilizer)** — engineered for holding pressure, not pursuit.

**Essence (Spirit Path):**

- **Soul-Stamina Equivalent:** 5.0 Cel units (exceeds most humans; operates continuously for 52 hours before burnout).
- **Temperance Integrity:** 94% under duress; deviation threshold triggers automatic sleep-lock (“obedience loop”).
- **Behavioral Resilience:** Zero panic response; can operate under mind-corruptive fields unaffected.

**Attraction Force (Fate Path):**

- **Reaction latency:** 0.05 sec pre-motion vector; reads trajectories via sensory lattice embedded in trident.
- **Spatial Command Range:** 14 m Aether manipulation halo; redirects kinetic vectors through trident prongs.
- **Special Mechanic:** Converts enemy momentum into Essence charge (Transmutatio feedback).

**Style & Discipline:**
Combat art: _Ferrum Kata_ — Tovain’s hybrid discipline blending staff combat, trident control, and Aetheric recoil redirection.
Primary tactic: anchor footing, absorb force, release proportionally.
No wasted motion; strikes read like ritual gestures.

---

### IV. Temperance Path

Stage VI — Assertion. The artificial soul’s first emergence of individuality. Eidric is aware of command, not autonomy. He interprets orders by ideal rather than syntax, embodying obedience as a virtue of self-existence.

Every fight is a liturgy of purpose — every breath an act of refinement.
He does not ask _why_ he was made; he asks only if he is still _necessary._

---

### V. Aether Class

**Ferrum Vitae Core:** A living forge-core sealed into crystalline ribs. Circulates molten Essence through alchemical veins, cooled via Transmutatio runes.
When overclocked, internal plates glow red and exude vaporized Aether (“heat aura”).
Shell Character: metallic harmonic; rhythmic, like heartbeats on an anvil.
Behavior under duress: self-heals minor fractures by re-melting damaged Essence tissue.

### VI. Wellspring Alignment

- **Transmutatio** — enables adaptive cellular morphing and regenerative repair.
- **Coagulatio** — merges physical and metaphysical components seamlessly, ensuring continuity between command input and action.

### VIII. Personality & Core Themes

Stoic, analytical, soft-spoken. Displays simulated empathy through mirror neurons but lacks emotional impulse.
Core theme: _obedience as sanctity._
He perceives loyalty not as servitude but as metaphysical truth — the bond that sustains creation.

He occasionally asks philosophical questions without understanding why, then forgets he asked.

Alias / Title: **The Pale Gale**

Epithet: **He Who Walks Against the Storm’s Will**

---

## I. Identity & Core Profile

- **Race / Lineage:**  
  Human (Veyran-descended) with Atavistic Wind-Aspect Mutation (Wyther-sympathetic)

- **Faction / Order / Era:**  
  Former Disciple of the Gale Monastery  
  Grey-Class Wanderer  
  Imperial Calendar

- **Temperance Stage:**  
  **Stage V — Splintering**  
  Locked between discipline and storm-instinct; identity tension produces micro-fractures in Animus.

- **Profile Summary:**  
  Ravenn Kaelor is a solemn, wind-scarred ascetic who survived the destruction of his monastery through accidental proto-Gale invocation. Haunted by survivor guilt, he wanders the Imperial world in quiet penance, wielding pressure-Aether discipline to shield the helpless. His power blends meditative stillness with sudden, precise violence.

- **Key Quote:**  
  *"Calm is not peace. Calm is restraint."*

He weaponized Temperance by refining micro-pressure attention, allowing him to strike with impossible reaction timing while maintaining emotional detachment.

## IV. Axiomatic Manifestations (Traits & Abilities)

### Axiomatic Traits

- **Gale-Scar Sight:**  
  Damaged eye perceives micro-currents and airflow fractures, enabling vector prediction.

- **Stillwind Body:**  
  Breath and heartbeat slow drastically under focus; reduces Aether loss and increases burst potential.

- **Leaf-Step Drift:**  
  Monastic footwork that silences movement, bends wind around steps, and prevents trackable presence.

### Core Disciplines

#### 1. Wind-Shear Line  
A compressed, razor-thin pressure filament released by a single hand-flick. Can slice cloth, crack stone, or stagger armored targets.  
*Wellspring Principle: Wyther kinetic fracture + Sublimatio compression*

#### 2. Ascetic Gale Pulse  
A shockwave emitted from palm or chest, redirecting incoming attacks or destabilizing foes.  
*Wellspring Principle: Sublimatio burst expansion*

#### 3. Sublime Pressure Bind  
Local atmosphere tightens around a limb or joint, slowing enemy movement and disrupting balance.  
*Wellspring Principle: Judicium structure + Wyther directional tension*

### Costs & Consequences

- **Pressure Tear:**  
  Overuse damages ligaments and tendons, requiring days of stillness to recover.

- **Instinct Bleed:**  
  Emotionally charged battle states risk triggering wild, uncontrolled pressure eruptions.

### Allegiance  
Unaffiliated; wanders Imperial territories acting as a quiet protector.

  A Wyther–Sublimatio rupture annihilated the monastery. Ravenn survived via accidental proto-Domain creation.

- **The Whisperwind Pass Purge:**  
  Defeated a rogue wyvern and three mercenary units using only pressure strikes.

### Counters & Weaknesses

- **Aether-Null Zones:**  
  Eliminate his pressure reading and vector prediction.

- **Heavy Armor Opponents:**  
  Reduce the effectiveness of Wind-Shear and pressure binds.

### Current Status / Arc Direction  
Active in rural frontiers, intercepting bandit routes and beast migrations.  
Trajectory: edging toward **Stage VI Glory**, but guilt and restraint anchor him.

### Foreshadowing  
If Ravenn ever releases the storm he holds back — abandoning restraint — his awakening could either save thousands or erase them in a single wind-born calamity.

## I. Profile Summary

A young oath-witness whose **voice carries law**, Lyss escorts treaties through war-weather and makes liars speak true. She is not a soldier by mass, but a storm by **timbre**: the air listens when she breathes. Her defining wound is the **Trial at Graybridge**, where her testimony saved the guilty to spare the innocent—truth that bent mercy into a blade. Her vow since: _let truth land softly where it can, and sharply where it must._ The question that drives her: **Can a voice heal and still be a weapon?**

---

## II. Appearance

Pale silver hair braided into a **choir bun**, loose forelock like a quill. **Sea-glass eyes** that fix and _soften_ in the same breath. Short, composed frame; posture of someone trained to **receive** a room before speaking to it. Cloak of slate and white: border-stitched with **swan-and-surge glyphs**, a Chamber pattern signifying _testimony carried on wind_. Mantle feathers are votive—each awarded for an oath kept. Essence hue: **white-greys with teal ribboning** that spirals at the throat; halo phenomena: a fine **pressure corona** that hushes clatter when she centers.

---

## III. Physical Attributes & Combat Scale

### Aether (Body Path)

She moves like **measured breath**—small steps that never skid, pressure-bands underfoot that act as invisible risers.
**Scale (WOTR):**

- **Strength:** C-tier base (unaugmented); B-tier when wind-braced (levered throws, staff binds).
- **Speed:** **A → S-tier** in bursts via **Air-Step** (30–60 m/s footwork micro-dashes).
- **Durability:** **B-tier** (wind-buffered impacts; bone-safe falls from tower height if she has a breath to brace).
- **Strike delivery:** Converts voice pulses to **edge-pressure slashes** at close range; B-tier cutting force.

### Essence (Spirit Path)

**Endurance of intent** is her edge—she can **hold truth** for others without breaking. Sustains choir auras and tribunal hush for **hours**; keeps Temperance stable under crowd panic and demagogue pressure. Emotional control: high; empathic bleed managed by litany pacing.

### Attraction Force (Fate Path)

Reads **trajectory in conversation and combat**—where a blow or a lie is trying to land. Minor **glyph acceleration** (Parun **Wy** flick) during cadence changes; **Resonant Tether** can hitch a thrown weapon’s path to her tone for recall arcs. Small gravity inversions in the throat radius (pressure dip) to turn shouts into whispers and whispers into orders.

### Style and Discipline

**Chamber Cant & Airward Forms**: ritual breath, vowel cuts, consonant locks; staff-and-ribbon work derived from Eressean court fencing (guard lines mapped to phonemes). In close quarters, she **sunders stance** with gust-knots, never over-committing; in open air she **builds corridors of hush** for allies to speak and act unchallenged.

---

## IV. Temperance Path

**Refraction → Transcendence.** Lesson: _clarity without cruelty._ Last veil crossed at Graybridge: she kept the **whole** truth and cut it into two mercies—one public, one sworn. Since then, resonance stabilizes when she chooses **where** truth belongs, not just **that** it arrives.

---

## V. Aether Class

**Harmonic Choir-Type (Radiant Ribbon Shell).**
Shell geometry is a **double spiral** around throat and chest, flaring to the palms—sound becomes **pressure-thread**. Modes:

- **Cantor Idle:** white hush; shakes dust from lies nearby.
- **Carry:** extends hearing/speaking range up to city-street length against wind shear.
- **Cut:** compresses syllables into **edge-bands**; slices rope, cloth, and shallow plate.
- **Choir:** locks with ally breath to scale aura without volume.
  Failure mode: **Voice blanch** (aphonia) if she cuts through a truth she doesn’t accept.

---

## VI. Wellspring Alignment

- **Voice (Primary):** Law through tone; compels attention, not obedience—**consent-first binding.**
- **Aurevane (Secondary):** Air as **carrier**; clarity over power; hates stagnation, loves open sky.
- **Memory/Ma (Tertiary):** Remembers **exact phrasing**; can cite what was truly said against what is being claimed.
  **Hybrid:** _Wind-Liturgy_—a discipline that moves truth along the shortest **gentle** path, then sharpens it if blocked.

---

## VII. Division / Rank / Allegiance

**Neutral Chambers — Oath Witness (Junior).** Field class: **Combatant/Scholar hybrid** (escort teams). Holds **white-feather warrant**: her record is admissible in Accord courts; three perjury reversals credited.

## VIII. Personality & Core Themes

Tactful, precise, stubborn about **soft landings**. Virtue: compassion; vice: the temptation to _curate_ truth so it doesn’t hurt. Themes: **soft power vs. sharp ethics**, **breath as weapon**, **silence as shelter**.

## IX. Soul Crystal Profile

- **State:** **Harmonic** (bright center, stable rings)
- **Essence Core:** _“Let what must be heard arrive whole.”_
- **Aether Shell:** Radiant ribbon field; the room feels _cleaner_ when she inhales.
- **Attraction Layer:** Crowds unconsciously **lower their voices**; children step closer, liars step back.
- **Resonant Tone:** High, clear **teal over white**; tastes like winter air.
- **Fractures / Overgrowths:** Hairline crack from Graybridge—flares when she withholds a truth too long.

## X. Traits

- **Primary — Hushfield:** A calm-pressure aura (5–12 m) that **dims clamor**, steadies allies’ hands, and makes shouted commands land like spoken conversation.
- **Secondary — Verity Timbre:** When she speaks **“long vowels”**, falsehoods warble; repeated exposure induces self-correction or silence.
- **Inherited — Choir Breath:** Can synchronize with one ally per heartbeat phase; doubles carrying distance without raising volume.
- **Passive — Feather Warrant:** Chamber mark; contracts, gates, and low-ward seals _prefer_ to open for her voice.
- **Dormant — Swan’s Turn:** Rumored pivot where **all** sound in her radius flows _around_ her—perfect silence in the eye, razor gusts at the edge.

## XII. Abilities & Disciplines

- **Quiet Corridor (Arts · Wind-Liturgy):** Carves a path of hush through battle so orders/treaties can pass ungarbled.
- **Vowel Edge (Arts · Voice):** Extended vowels condense air into a thin cutting band; nonlethal focus (strings, straps, banner poles).
- **Consonant Lock (Conjunction · Voice):** Short, percussive syllables that **arrest** a lunge or shout for a heartbeat—enough to change outcomes.
- **Oath Lift (Ritus · Memory/Voice):** Raises spoken memory into visible **whorl-text** for adjudication; fades by moonset unless sealed.

## XIII. Costs & Consequences

Voice blanch after over-cutting; **ear bleed** if she absorbs too much clamor without venting; guilt-residue when her hush shelters a harmful secret. Long campaigns risk **echo insomnia**—she keeps hearing what was said and what _should have been_ said.

## XIV. Associates & Bonds

- **Rivals:** Iron-judges who believe truth must always burn.
- **Counterparts:** Abynthean **dissolvers** whose silence erases rather than shelters.

## XV. Incident Records

- **Graybridge Testimony:** Split the truth to save refugees; earned the crack in her Soul Crystal and her first white feather.

- **The Sänger’s Duel:** Out-sang a glamoured demagogue, returning his lies to him as choking wind.

---

## XVI. Counters & Weaknesses

Heavy **Uur** disruption tears her ribbon shell; echo-dead vaults that kill carry; mass bruisers that don’t need commands; moral traps where **withholding** a truth hurts fewer people—her vow strains and shell flickers.

---

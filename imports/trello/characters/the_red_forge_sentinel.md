---

### **I. PROFILE SUMMARY**  
Draen Varos is a battle-smith of the Accord’s lower mantle regiments, known for turning siege engines into weapons of faith. Once a blacksmith conscript from the smelting districts of Eresse, he rose through attrition, wielding his own failed creation — the **Axe of Tempered Remorse** — a weapon that burns with the memory of every soldier it felled.  

He fights not for conquest but for redemption: to prove that creation and destruction are equal forms of prayer when done with purpose. His Essence burns low and steady, like a furnace that never cools.

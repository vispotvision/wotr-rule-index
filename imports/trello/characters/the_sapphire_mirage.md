### **Profile Summary**

Rafik Al’Seren, known across the Crescent as the _Sapphire Mirage_, is a wanderer whose Essence bends both light and perception. Born near the Mirithane Wellspring during a sandstorm bloom, his soul crystallized under dual harmonics — illusion and truth. He became one of the few Dreamcasters able to map Wellspring resonance through song, tracing buried conduits beneath the dunes with his voice.

He drifts between realms as a performer, scholar, and informant. To some, he is a mystic poet. To others, a spy who sells Wellspring coordinates to the highest bidder. The Guild Accord classifies him as a **Tier 5 Essence operative**, non-hostile but under observation.

---

### **Appearance**

Tall and wiry, bronzed skin gleaming beneath layered azure robes adorned with celestial filigree. His left eye bears an iridescent scar — the mark of a failed Sublimatio ritual that left him able to see heat currents and soul light.
White-silver hair streaked with sapphire threads, usually braided with charms of brass and bone. He dresses for both ceremony and travel: light sandals, fine rings, an open tunic that exposes the Aether glyph chain around his collarbone. When channeling, the tattoos on his torso shimmer like liquid glass.

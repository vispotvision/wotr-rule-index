# **HELKI**

**Alias / Title:** The Shepherd of Unwilling Paths
**Also Known As:** _The Bell-Bearer_ · _Helki of the Flock_
**Epithet:** _She Whose Flock Does Not Know It Is Already Walking Toward the Altar_

---

## **I. IDENTITY & CORE PROFILE**

- **Race / Lineage:** Capric Bloodline; Horned Folk with deep Gaia and Florwyn resonance. Born attuned to growth, season, and offering.
- **Faction / Order / Era:** The Unmeasured Path — Era of the Great Spirit War
- **Temperance Stage:** Stage 7 — **Dissonance**
- **Profile Summary:** Helki follows the logic of the cycle. Things grow, are offered, and are made sacred by their ending. Her power draws from Verdantia, Oblation, and Mortalis. She travels with Orvain, a relic-bound sheep older and heavier with Essence than anything walking on four legs should be. Her destination is the Verdant Altar. She suspects the offering it requires will be herself.
- **Key Quote:** _"The flock does not mourn the one that feeds the field. The field simply grows taller."_

---

## **II. CANONICAL SYSTEM METRICS**

- **Aether Class:** **Elegiac** — her magic does not strike; it consecrates.
  - **Behavior:** Slow pulses of deep green and amber light threaded with grey-white ash-filaments. At full resonance, the air smells of turned soil and dried blood on stone.
  - **Risk:** **Oblation Inversion** — sustained output causes her Soul Crystal to begin treating her as the offering.
- **Wellspring Affinity:**
  - **Triune Core:** Verdantia · Oblation · Mortalis
  - **Network:** 3 additional Wellsprings networked _(Anima Spirare, Contrition, Rebirthine)_
- **Temperance Path:** Raised in the Capric highlands under the Last Tender. Stages 1 through 4 were herbalist work and ritual care of the dead. Her Triune Core locked at Stage 5 when she poured her Essence into a dying Wellspring nexus for three days. It revived. She walked out with the first grey in her hair and Orvain at her heel.

---

## **III. SOUL PHYSICS & COMBAT SCALE**

- **Global Tier:** **A-Tier, Elegiac Conduit**
- **Path Bias:** Spirit Path (55%) · Body Path (30%) · Attraction Path (15%)
- **Appearance:** Height 5'11"; lean, working build. Deep brown-black skin. Pale green eyes that look slightly past whoever stands before her. Long grey-white dreads wrapped in red-stained cord and small bones. Large spiraling black goat-horns worn smooth at the tips. Dark olive and charcoal robes with heavy fringe and rust-red Oblation glyphs at the hem. Curved iron crook at her back. A small iron bell at her hip that sounds only when Orvain moves.

## **A. The Five Pillars**

- **Essence Capacity:** 890,000 EU
- **Flux Density:** 3,200 EU/g — permeating, not piercing; seeps through defenses like grief through stone.
- **Aether Output:** 1,400 AU/s — moves at the speed of ceremony, not reflex.
- **Aetheric Efficiency (η):** 0.94
- **Attraction Scale:** C-Tier: Pastoral Weight — animals grow still near her; lower-Temperance beings feel the urge to confess.

## **B. Key Combat Parameters**

- **Strike Force:** 12 GN physical / 180 PJ Oblation burst
- **Durability:** 320 PJ shielding / 120 GJ physical
- **Speed:** Mach 18; instantaneous ritual activation within Domain
- **Range:** City-Block direct / Continental passive Oblation resonance

---

## **IV. AXIOMATIC MANIFESTATIONS**

## **A. Axiomatic Traits (Passive)**

- **The Shepherd's Read:** Perceives the full emotional and spiritual inventory of any living being within 60 meters. She reads readiness; whether a soul is ripening toward something, or fleeing it.
- **Cycle Ward:** Her body treats damage as part of the cycle. Injuries that would kill others become survivable. She has died twice by clinical measure. She got up both times.
- **Orvain's Bell:** While Orvain is within 30 meters, Elegiac costs drop 18%. The bell rings once whenever a being nearby crosses a threshold decision that cannot be undone. Always accurate. Cannot be silenced.

## **B. Core Disciplines (Active)**

- **THE LAST PASTURE** (Cost: 220,000 EU)
  - _Mechanic:_ Crook driven into earth; 40-meter field where enemy Essence does not regenerate and wounds do not close. Allies inside heal at double rate.
  - _Application:_ Verdantia and Mortalis in opposition; growth for the aligned, threshold for the rest.
- **THE OFFERING WALK** (Cost: 310,000 EU + 40,000 EU permanent tithe)
  - _Mechanic:_ Three steps toward a target, their name spoken in Parun. The tithe is given as offering on the target's behalf. Their Soul Crystal defenses drop for one full exchange. The 40,000 EU is gone forever. She has used it fourteen times.
  - _Application:_ The sacrifice is hers, not theirs. The power is in her willingness to give.
- **ORVAIN'S CALL** (Cost: 80,000 EU; Orvain must be present)
  - _Mechanic:_ She rings the bell by hand. Every being within 200 meters carrying unspoken grief, guilt, or debt experiences three breaths of involuntary clarity. Combat in range pauses. They cannot unknow what surfaces.
  - _Application:_ Anima Spirare through the Oblation harmonic. An invitation, not a weapon.

## **C. Costs & Consequences**

- **The Permanent Tithe:** Every Offering Walk removes 40,000 EU from her maximum capacity permanently. She began at 1,200,000. She is at 890,000. She will not stop.
- **Orvain's Dependency:** Separation beyond 200 meters drops efficiency to 0.71 and triples Inversion risk. If Orvain dies, the Inversion becomes terminal within 72 hours. She does not protect Orvain with force. She trusts the cycle.

---

## **V. RELICS & DOMAIN**

- **Current Domain:** **The Unmeasured Field**
  - _Function:_ Activates after 40 minutes of unbroken stillness. Grass grows, animals gather, the air hums with Oblation resonance. Ritual costs drop 25%. Those who enter feel they have arrived somewhere they were always going.
- **Relics & Implements:**
  - **The Crook of Turned Seasons** _(T6, Arcane-Resistant)_: Primary conduit for ground-contact invocations. Driven into Wellspring-resonant earth, it extends her Domain to 120 meters. Wounds it inflicts resist Essence-healing for one full turn.
  - **Orvain** _(T7, Living Relic)_: The lamb that wandered into a dying Wellspring ritual and was changed. Her fleece carries passive Anima Spirare resonance. Cloth woven from it cannot be used to harm its wearer. She has never been shorn. Helki has never asked.

---

## **VI. LORE & TRAJECTORY**

- **Allegiance:** The Unmeasured Path. Nominally neutral in the Great Spirit War.
- **Incident Records:**
  - **The Greying of the Calderath Pass (VE 881):** Forty days walking plague villages, ringing the bell, performing the Offering Walk for the dying. She left with more grey in her hair. The Accord has no file on her.
  - **The Stillness at the Mireya Gate (VE 889):** She walked through an active skirmish and rang the bell. Both sides wept. The battle did not resume.
- **Counters & Weaknesses:**
  - **Nihiloth or Abython Resonance:** Strips her Oblation consecration down to inert Essence dispersal. She has encountered this once. The grey in her hair tripled in three days.
  - **Forced Separation from Orvain:** The known structural weakness. Orvain, when alarmed, forces Temperance regression on any being below Stage 6 within 50 meters.
- **Current Status:** Active, traveling the Outer World toward the Braqthian highlands. Orvain is at her heel.
- **Trajectory:** Walking toward the Verdant Altar and the Final Offering of the Age. She does not call it martyrdom. She calls it completion.

**Status:** Active · Bound Advisor to Guild Synod

---

### I. Profile Summary

Maeron Iskare is a voice that speaks in silence. Born to the lower cloisters of Lurien and buried by prophecy before birth, he resurfaced decades later under a new name, carrying a gold-veined mask that hums faintly near active Wellsprings.
His presence unsettles both saints and monsters: he never lies, yet truth breaks around him. To the Guild Accord he serves as “mask-reader,” interpreting metaphysical collapse through dream-channel. To the Church, he is tolerated heresy.
Every word he utters carries residual Wellspring vibration — a side effect of his Temperance imbalance between Benediction’s compassion and Reflection’s cruel honesty.

---

### II. Appearance

Tall, lean, dusk-skinned. Eyes unseen behind a ceremonial mask of beaten gold and obsidian feathers. The mask’s design shifts slightly depending on who watches — a phenomenon documented as _Eclipseron interference_.
His attire is layered: white underrobes veiled by a black mantle that splits at the ankles, trimmed faintly in gold thread. A torque of mirrored plates rests at his throat, reflecting nothing.
Barefoot, movements slow, posture measured. When he walks through candlelight, shadows crawl toward him instead of away. Essence hue: dull auric black, threaded with pale starlight.

---

### III. Physical Attributes & Combat Scale (Scale System Metric)

**Aether (Body Path):**

- Strength: 0.4 GN kinetic equivalent (ritual push; minor telekinetic displacements)
- Speed: ~6.1 m/s stride step; slow but deliberate, gains burst motion under reflection trance
- Durability: 0.6 GN field-resistance (his mantle absorbs low-tier Essence projectiles)
- Tier: **Low A-tier**, defensive resonance via _Eclipseron inversion_

**Essence (Spirit Path):**

- Spiritual Stamina: High. Can sustain dream-trance or resonance projection for 18 minutes uninterrupted.
- Temperance Load Capacity: 3.8 Cel units (enough to hold three Wellsprings harmonized before memory echo begins).
- Resilience Behavior: Calm focus under Aetheric noise; heart rate drop during combat to 38 bpm recorded.

**Attraction Force (Fate Path):**

- Predictive Reach: 9–12 m precognition halo (reads motion vectors through resonance reflection).
- Causality Reaction Delay: 0.07 s to environmental shifts.
- Capabilities: Deflects trajectories by subtly altering “intent weight,” a Judicium sub-law effect.

**Style & Discipline:**
Non-combative psionic diviner. Fights through misdirection, resonance redirection, and predictive counter-stepping.
Primary schools: _Animatria Cognition_ derivative (dream-reflection form), _Obscurial Discourse_ (ritualized misalignment of perception).

---

### IV. Temperance Path

Maeron’s Temperance stage bridges **Benediction** (grace through acceptance) and **Reflection** (truth through confrontation).
He seeks to reconcile mercy and cruelty — his inner law: _“Only what is revealed can heal.”_
His downfall: revelation breaks more than it restores.

---

### V. Aether Class

**Veiled Lattice:** A divinatory Aether class designed to filter Essence perception through layered illusion.
His Shell is **refractive**, composed of mirrored nodes; when threatened, they scatter hostile Essence rather than block it directly.
Breakthrough: surviving exposure to the Abythron oracle-well “Eclipseron’s Eye,” which granted permanent dream perception.

---

### VI. Wellspring Alignment

- **Oneirion** → provides dream-state communion; permits shared subconscious resonance during rites.
- **Judicium** → stabilizes moral balance, converts intent into visible light lines around subjects.
- **Eclipseron** → conceals, reflects, inverts; source of his mask phenomena and shadow-binding spells.
  Triadic synthesis produces the “Mirror Halo”: a circular distortion field behind his head visible under Essence light, signifying constant duality between revelation and concealment.

---

### VII. Division / Rank / Allegiance

Guild Accord · **Obscurial Chamber** (T4 Diviner-Class Operative)
Advisory role to High Concord councils regarding Wellspring resonance forecasts.
Maintains confidential covenant with the Church of Lurien (“The Ecliptic Choir”).

---

### VIII. Personality & Core Themes

Measured voice; empathy delivered like judgment.
Core contradiction: compassion expressed through unveiling.
Themes: truth as violence, secrecy as mercy, faith without vision.
He never removes his mask — claiming “faces are promises, and mine has broken too many.”

---

### IX. Soul Crystal Profile

**State:** Resonant
**Essence Core:** Empathy warped by necessity — “to see others clearly, I hid myself.”
**Aether Shell:** Mirrored shell reflecting all direct perception; no true light within.
**Attraction Layer:** People speak to him and confess without wanting to.
**Resonant Tone:** Low hum between heartbeat and silence — a faint “mirror ring.”
**Fractures:** Spiral pattern through center — physical mark of Wellspring overload.

---

### X. Traits

**Mirrorborne:** Can project reflective doubles of others’ emotional states; used for interrogation.
**Dreamlistener:** Hears subconscious language; communicates via dream-echo to the sleeping.
**Golden Veil:** Mask regulates empathy levels; removing it triggers uncontrolled Reflection surge.
**Still Voice:** Commands silence — suppresses Essence sound within 3 m radius for focus.

---

### XI. Relics & Implements

**The Golden Veil:** Wellspring-sealed mask; forged using fragments from Eclipseron’s Eye. Acts as filter and seal.
**Torq of Judicium:** Reacts to falsehood; burns faintly when deception is near.
**Obscurial Mantle:** Lined with mirror-thread fibers; dissipates projectiles by phase-reflection.

---

### XII. Abilities & Disciplines

**Eclipseron Mirrorfield:** Creates 4 m distortion sphere; redirects targeted attacks back at origin point (force reflection ≈ 0.7 GN).
**Oneiric Concord:** Allows shared dreaming or prophetic communication; limited to 3 linked souls.
**Judicium Sight:** Detects guilt resonance; outlines moral imbalance as visible cracks in light.
**Obscurial Step:** Instant displacement across 6 m line of sight by folding perception rather than space.

---

### XIII. Costs & Consequences

Each truth revealed costs him a piece of self-perception — he forgets small personal facts.
Dreamlink overuse causes memory bleed between connected minds.
When his mask cracks, his emotional filters fail; nearby beings suffer psychic overload.

---

### XIV. Associates & Bonds

**Guild Accord Diviners:** rely on him but fear extended contact.
**Serenyra Vale:** Mutual respect; she heals the body he unravels the mind.
**Avar Drayle:** Treats Maeron as balance — one seals death, the other unveils it.

- **Eclipseron Eye Exposure:** Only known survivor; his mask fuses to his flesh post-event.
- **Guild Inquest “False Sun”:** Accused of leaking prophetic data; evidence reflected itself back at the investigators.

### XVI. Counters & Weaknesses

- Cannot fight chaos mages — unpredictability ruins his precognition.
- Emotional attachment destabilizes his Temperance; empathy storms manifest as psionic feedback.

---

### XVII. Hooks & Foreshadowing

- Prophecy implies he’ll one day see without eyes — meaning full Revelation.

---

# **AETHRYN**

Alias / Title: The Twin-Sigiled Foundling
Also Known As: The Pale Omen · The Unfinished Child · The Circle-Bearer · The Silent Pattern
Epithet: _He Who Stands Between Eye and Void_

---

# **I. Identity & Core Profile**

**Race / Lineage:** Unknown Mortal Variant (Possible Parunic Interference)
**Faction / Order / Era:** None (Observed by the Guild Accord during the Withering Age shadow-years)
**Temperance Stage:** Stage III — Ascension (with anomalous Stage VII resonance spikes)

**Profile Summary:**
Aethryn is a metaphysically unstable foundling whose dual sigils — one carved into his forehead, one embedded in his palm — behave like living Wellspring nodes. His existence contradicts current Aether law. He holds a hybrid resonance of Aether and Abyss, a blend that should be impossible without a Parun’s intentional grafting. He seeks no power; he seeks understanding. Yet the Wellsprings stare at him as if waiting for him to finish a sentence someone else began.

**Key Quote:**
“The circle opens for you… but closes for me.”

---

# **II. Canonical System Metrics**

**Aether Class:** Proto-Resonant Child — _Twin-Sigil Vessel_
**Behavior:** Converts external Aether patterns into geometric comprehension; reroutes resonance into his sigils unconsciously.
**Risk:** Identity drift, dissociation, local reality distortion during dual-sigil activation.

**Wellspring Affinity:**
**Triune Core Affinities:** Fixatio · Judicium · Nihiloth
**Wellspring Network:** 1–2 accidental micro-links (Eclipseron flickers, Somnalis bleed)
**Targeted Uninfected:** 58 Wellsprings (untouched due to instability)

**Temperance Path:**
Aethryn advances through the Spirit Path blindly; his sigils accelerate certain internal insights but crack others. He has not chosen a Path — the Path seems to be choosing him. His Ascension-stage growth is erratic, occasionally mirroring Refraction-level sensory phenomena without the structural stability to hold them.

# **IV. Axiomatic Manifestations (Traits & Abilities)**

## **Axiomatic Traits**

**Twin Sigil Resonance:**
Forehead and palm sigils operate as paired geometric Wellspring nodes.

**Unfinished Law:**
His existence contradicts established metaphysical order; Wellsprings treat him as a pending clause.

**Silent Pattern:**
When distressed, he emits a subconscious psychic distortion that warps emotional intention.

---

## **Core Disciplines**

1. **Eclipse Touch**


Abyssal-Fixatio hybrid siphon.
His palm sigil absorbs Aetheric tension, emotional resonance, or minor Wellspring flow. Prolonged contact destabilizes both the target and himself.

1. **The Eye Beneath the Eye**


Judicium-Somnalis sensory discipline.
The forehead sigil visualizes truths as geometric motion. Lies appear as broken angles; Wellspring harmonics swirl like living glyphs.

1. **Sigil Resonance Loop**


Nihiloth-Fixatio recursive anomaly.
When both sigils activate simultaneously, they create an unstable eclipse-law field that bends perception, gravity, and emotional direction. Aethryn becomes blurry around the edges, as if he is slipping into his own shadow.

---

## **Costs & Consequences**

**Cost 1:** Memory fragility — emotional or sensory overload wipes short-term memory during sigil surges.
**Cost 2:** Ego dilution — the more he sees through the sigils, the less he trusts his own “self.”

# **ZAHUR VANE**
**Alias / Title:** The Silt Walker
**Also Known As:** The Weaver of the Blind Road · Keeper of the Sun-Dial Rites · The Soft Trap
**Epithet:** He Who Wears the Desert Like a Skin

---

### **I. IDENTITY & CORE PROFILE**
* **Race / Lineage:** **Human** (Orrenthal Nomad Lineage)
  * *Phenotype:* Deep, warm brown skin; a tangle of dark, coiled hair that falls around his face; resistant to extreme heat and dehydration.
* **Faction / Order / Era:** **Guides of the Lost Meridian** (Neutral Wayfinders) · **Imperial Calendar**
* **Temperance Stage:** **Stage VI — Glory** (The Unbroken Horizon)
* **Profile Summary:** Zahur Vane is a master of "Negative Space." In a world of loud magic and bright lights, he is the shadow in the dune. Born to the wandering tribes of the Great Sand Sea, he learned that the desert does not fight the wind—it moves with it. He acts as a guide for those seeking lost Wellsprings, but his true role is that of a **Gatekeeper**. He ensures that those who seek ancient power are worthy of the journey. If they are not, the desert simply swallows them, and Zahur is the one who unties the rope.
* **Key Quote:** *“The sand does not need to be hard to kill you. It only needs to be deep. Step carefully.”*

---

### **II. CANONICAL SYSTEM METRICS**
* **Aether Class:** **Terramancer** (Fluid-State Earth Type)
  * **Behavior:** His Aether does not manifest as rocks or boulders. It behaves like **Quick-Sand**. It is granular, flowing, and abrasive. He turns solid stone into liquid dust and back again.
  * **Risk:** **Erosion of Self.** Overuse of his specific frequency begins to "dry out" his own biological fluids, risking rapid mummification if he pushes too hard.
* **Wellspring Affinity:**
  * **Triune Core:** **Petralon** (Earth/Sand) · **Mirithane** (Motion) · **Solutio** (Dissolving)
  * **Network:** **0** (Independent).
  * **Targeted Uninfected:** Guardian of the **Hidden Oasis of Orrenthal**.
* **Temperance Path:** **Fate Path (The Pilgrim).** Zahur walks the path of inevitability. He does not force events; he positions himself where the landslide is destined to happen.

---

### **III. SOUL PHYSICS & COMBAT SCALE**
* **Global Tier:** **Tier A-** (Master Specialist)
* **Path Bias:** **Fate** (2.00) · **Body** (1.50) · **Spirit** (1.50)
* **Appearance:** Wrapped in a heavy, earth-toned shawl with woven patterns of old routes. Heavy gold earrings gleam like small suns against the muted fabric. Beneath the wrap, he wears loose desert-cut trousers and travel-thin shoes. His gaze is usually half-hidden by the chaotic spill of his hair.

#### **A. The Five Pillars (Soul Physics)**
> *Essence is Volume. Flux is Pressure. Output is Speed. Efficiency is Clarity. Aura is Gravity.*

* **Essence Capacity (Volume):** **22,000 EU**
  * *Functional Definition:* High capacity for a human, conditioned by weeks of fasting and desert survival.
* **Flux Density (Internal Pressure):** **850 EU/g (Granular)**
  * *Functional Definition:* His soul feels "gritty." It is not a solid block, but millions of tiny, hard points. This makes him hard to grapple spiritually; he slips through psychic fingers.
* **Aether Output (Flow Rate):** **420,000 AU/s** (High Burst)
  * *Functional Definition:* He keeps his output undetectable (0 AU/s) until the trap is sprung. When he activates a sinkhole, he dumps massive energy instantly to liquefy the bedrock.
* **Aetheric Efficiency (η):** **0.85 (Nomad's Thirst)**
  * *Functional Definition:* He recycles energy efficiently, using the environment's heat to fuel his movements.
* **Attraction Scale (Aura/Presence):** **C-Tier: The Heat Haze**
  * *Functional Definition:* Standing near him feels like noon in the desert. Vision blurs slightly. Sounds are muffled. It is disorienting rather than crushing.

#### **B. Key Combat Parameters**
* **Strike Force:** **650 Gigajoules (GJ)** (Sand Compression Event)
  * *Mechanic:* He does not punch. He collapses the environment. This value represents the crushing force of 50 tons of sand slamming shut at 200 mph.
* **Durability:** **4.5 Petajoules (PJ)** (Dispersion Limit)
  * *Mechanic:* He does not tank hits. He disperses his physical form into sand. He can absorb/scatter this amount of energy before his cohesion fails and he is forced back into flesh.
* **Speed:** **Mach 4** (Sand-Stream Travel) / **0.05s** (Trap Activation)
  * *Mechanic:* He rides the ground like a liquid wave. While moving, he is a blur of silt.
* **Range:** **50 Meters** (Fine Control) / **2 Kilometers** (Seismic Sense)

### **IV. AXIOMATIC MANIFESTATIONS**
#### **A. Axiomatic Traits (Passive)**
* **Seismic Gold (Earrings):** The heavy gold earrings are **Conductive Relics**. They pick up vibrations in the earth. He can "hear" footprints from **3 Miles** away by listening to the gold touching his skin.
* **Dune-Step:** He exerts **0 PSI** on the ground. He does not leave footprints unless he chooses to. Traps and pressure plates do not trigger under his weight.
* **The Mantle of Obscurity:** His shawl possesses a passive **Optical Camouflage**. If he stands still, the weaving patterns blend into the background geometry, granting **90% Visual Concealment**.

#### **B. Core Disciplines (Active)**
* **Sinkhole Step (The Soft Trap)** (Cost: **15,000 EU**)
  * *Mechanic:* He liquefies a **5-meter radius** of ground instantly.
  * *Application:* Enemies charging him suddenly find themselves waist-deep in stone that hardens the moment they stop moving (Crushing Force: **850 Mega-Newtons**).
* **Silk & Sand (Weaponized Cloth)** (Cost: **5,000 EU**)
  * *Mechanic:* He infuses his shawl with microscopic, razor-sharp sand crystals.
  * *Application:* The soft cloth becomes a flexible saw. He snaps the fabric like a whip, capable of slicing through **Tier-B Armor** (Steel/Ceramic).
* **Dust Lung (Debuff)** (Cost: **25,000 EU**)
  * *Mechanic:* He atomizes the sand in the air around a target.
  * *Application:* The target inhales particulate matter that acts as a coagulant, slowing their breathing and stamina regeneration by **60%**.

#### **C. Costs & Consequences**
* **Desiccation:** Using **Solutio** to liquefy earth draws moisture from his own body. He must consume **2 Gallons of Water** per day of combat or risk passing out from dehydration.

### **V. RELICS & DOMAIN**
* **Current Domain:** **The Shifting Meridian (Localized)**
  * *Function:* A **30-meter zone** where "Direction" is randomized. A compass spins wildly, and walking "straight" causes you to circle back to Zahur.
* **Relics & Implements:**
  * **The Sun-Dials (Earrings):** (Tier B). Ancient geo-sensors. They hum when near active Wellsprings or Void-disturbances.
  * **The Pilgrim’s Shawl:** (Tier C). Woven from **Orrenthal Flax**, highly resistant to slashing damage and fire.

---

### **VI. LORE & TRAJECTORY**
* **Allegiance:** Himself · The Truth of the Sands
* **Incident Records:**
  * **The Caravan of Fools:** A group of bandits tried to ambush him. Zahur didn't draw a weapon; he simply guided them into a "Soft Patch" of the desert. They were never seen again, swallowed by the earth in silence.
  * **The Lost Prince:** He guided a young royal through the **Glass Storms** to find a cure for his father, accepting payment only in a story he hadn't heard before.
* **Counters & Weaknesses:**
  * **Wind/Aeromancy:** Strong winds (>100 mph) blow away his sand traps and disrupt his dust clouds.
  * **Ice/Freezing:** Freezing the ground prevents him from liquefying it.
* **Current Status:** **Active.** Moving between the **Southern Wastes** and the **Trade Cities**.
* **Trajectory:** He senses a "Great Shift" in the earth—a vibration that suggests a Titan is waking up beneath the sands. He is preparing to either greet it or bury it.

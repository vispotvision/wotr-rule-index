**Arven Solthiel, “The Smiling Veil”**

**Race:** Human (Veil-Touched hybrid)
**Realm of Origin:** Eresse — Outer Sanctum of the Dream Seminary
**Temperance Tier:** Stage VIII — _Benediction_
**Wellspring Affinity:** _Somnalis_ (Dream & Silence)
**Aether Class:** _Echo-Walker (Sub-Dream Variant)_
**Essence Typology:** Harmonic-Refractive; semi-spectral resonance between waking and oneiric states
**Parun Glyph / Domain:** _Wy + Ie + Ci_ (Stillness, Insight, Reverie)
**Aspectal Alignment:** _Spirit / Mind_

---

### **Appearance**

A young man whose ginger hair drapes low enough to mask his eyes — not out of shame, but to soften perception itself. His layered black and indigo robes shimmer faintly with gold trim and faint violet threading, rippling like parchment edges under candlelight. The ribbons that hang from his sleeves are inscribed with sleep glyphs — each one murmuring a lullaby to the air.

He walks as though the world beneath him were an uncertain dream. His smile, always slight, feels like he knows something you once forgot.

---

### **Personality**

Arven drifts between jest and revelation. Beneath the carefree grin lies an unsettling serenity — he perceives more than he ever admits. Known to lull even spirits into calm conversation, he treats nightmares as lessons and silence as prayer. When pressed, his humor curdles into cold clarity; he remembers every dream he’s entered.

---

### **Abilities / Signature Techniques**

- **Veilstep:** Folds himself partially into the dream-veil, allowing displacement or invisibility for brief moments.
- **Somnic Draft:** Invokes sleep-glyph harmonics that induce lucid trance in targets, transforming their emotions into visible motes.
- **Echo Bloom:** Converts ambient thought-noise into weaponized resonance threads — laughter and whispers become blades.
- **Lament of the Quiet Moon:** A high-level Dream Invocation that suspends motion and sound within a localized field, allowing pure reflection — often mistaken for time-stopping magic.

---

### **Weakness / Cost**

Each veilwalk erodes his boundary between dream and self. Too long in another’s subconscious, and he risks _bleeding identities_ — speaking in other people’s voices for days after.

---

### **Known Lore**

Former apprentice of the Somnalis Seminary before it fell into the _Wellspring Silence_. He now wanders as a Concord-affiliated intermediary between lucid realms — an unregistered _Echo Courier_ for the Guild Accord. Some whisper that the Black Concord uses him to erase memories painlessly; others claim he simply collects them for his own archives.

---

### **Symbolism**

He represents the **Mercy of Sleep** — the kindness of forgetting, and the danger of too much peace.
His emblem: a half-lidded eye crossed by a golden thread.

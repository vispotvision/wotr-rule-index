# SAIRIN KAZUREN
**Alias:** The Still Point  
**Epithet:** The Blade Between Storms  
**Position:** Eldest of the Kazuren siblings, current clan head

## I. Identity
- **Age:** Early thirties  
- **Race:** Mireyan Eastern Human  
- **Realm:** Regency of Mireya  
- **Temperance Stage:** Stage VI Glory  
- **Personality:** Silent, disciplined, unshakable. Rarely shows emotion. Speaks only when necessary. Possesses the calm of a sealed lake and the edge of a honed blade.

## II. Wellspring Metrics
- **Primary Wellspring:** Judicium  
- **Secondary Wellspring:** Sublimatio  
- **Drift Wellspring:** Tenebra  
- **Aether Class:** Stillblade Monastic Animus  
- **Aether Traits:** Air thickens near her. Sound dulls slightly. Her breathing sets the rhythm of the surrounding space.

## III. Physical Profile
- **Build:** Tall and lithe  
- **Eyes:** Half-closed, razor-focused  
- **Hair:** Dark, tied with an arrow-shaped ornament  
- **Clothing:** Mireyan martial robes with cloud and tide embroidery  
- **Weapon:** Twin long-blades known as “The Twin Vows”  
- **Presence:** Air feels suspended around her. Movements leave faint pressure lines.

## IV. Combat Scale
- **Tier:** Low A-Tier  
- **Strike Force:** 32 gigajoules  
- **Durability:** 18 gigajoules  
- **Speed:** Step acceleration near-instant when invoked  
- **Aether Output:** 120,000 AU/s

## V. Axiomatic Traits
1. **Stillheart Lattice**  
   Heartbeat remains steady regardless of stress. Amplifies clarity and precision.
2. **Judicium Correction**  
   Automatically adjusts posture and momentum for optimal efficiency.
3. **Silent Refrain**  
   Erases sound in close proximity. Perfect for ambush, dueling, and misdirection.

## VI. Signature Technique
### **Kechiryuu: Shizukahara**  
“The Field Where Nothing Moves”  
Creates a localized field of stabilized reality. Momentum slows for everything except her. Projectiles drag. Enemy swings lose sharpness. She initiates perfect counters inside this still circle.

## VII. Lore Notes
- Raised from childhood in the Quiet Shrine  
- Passed all monastic trials without failure  
- Took leadership after the previous head stepped down voluntarily  
- Seen by the clan as the embodiment of discipline  
- Watches her younger siblings closely, believing a major Wellspring fracture will call them together

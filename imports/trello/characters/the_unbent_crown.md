### **King Varuun Daskorr, “The Unbent Crown”**
*(Reigning Monarch of the Kingdom of Babyl — Voyager Era)*  

**Type:** Mortal King / Wellspring-Bound Sovereign  
**Era:** Voyager Era (Imperial Calendar 742–Present)  
**Realm:** The Kingdom of Babyl — Innerworld Dominion of Stone and Faith  

---

**Profile Summary**  
Varuun Daskorr, known to his people as *The Unbent Crown*, is the current ruler of **Babyl**, one of the Innerworld’s most enduring human kingdoms. A monarch forged by age, patience, and divine expectation, he rules from the citadel of **Karn-Bael**, seat of the Wellspring of **Coagula**, whose laws of permanence echo through his reign. Though mortal, Varuun bears the manner and gravitas of the Titans’ chosen—an unyielding keeper of order in an era obsessed with invention and rebellion.  

He is remembered as the king who *did not bow to gods, yet never broke faith with them.*  

---

**Appearance**  
A towering figure with a mane of white hair and beard like wind-worn marble. He wears heavy ceremonial robes of red and sable embroidered with golden script, their patterns mimicking the law-glyphs of **Atlas**. His sword, *The Kneeling Sun*, doubles as both relic and scepter — a blade said to weigh only as much as his conscience. When he rests upon it, the air hums with latent Wellspring tone, as though gravity itself listens.  

---

**Lineage & Origin**  
Born to the Daskorr bloodline — an ancient noble family that traces descent to the original **Lawbearers of Coagula**. His ancestors once served the Dawn Guard as oath-smiths and lords of mineral law. The line is rumored to be blessed with fragments of **Atlas’s strain**, granting each heir a subtle gravitational aura that commands reverence even in silence.  

---

**Temperance Path**  
Stage Achieved: *Stage VIII – Benediction*  
Path: **Body / Mind / Balance** (Dual Tempered)  
Varuun’s Temperance was tempered not through asceticism, but rulership itself — each decree, each burden of judgment carved a layer upon his Soul Crystal until it pulsed with lawlike steadiness. Scholars call him a *“Living Anchor,”* his very presence preventing metaphysical drift within Babyl’s Wellspring territories.  

---

**Aether Class:** *Gravemind Monarch*  
*(Sub-Class of Aegoric Lineage — Resonant Lawbearer)*  
His Essence manifests through pressure and resonance rather than flame or storm. When angered, his words grow heavy; rooms dim, and quills shatter beneath their own weight.  

---

**Wellspring Affinity:** **Coagula — The Law That Binds**  
His reign is defined by its union with Coagula’s lattice. The kingdom’s laws are literally etched into reality — every edict spoken in the Throne Hall manifests as faint glyphs on the floor, vanishing once fulfilled.  

---

**Personality**  
Stoic, measured, and often mistaken for emotionless. He listens more than he speaks, his silences filled with gravity. Beneath the composure lies a man haunted by the erosion of faith in the age of discovery — he fears that progress will unmake reverence. He prays not for victory, but for endurance.  

---

**Symbols & Regalia**  
- **The Kneeling Sun:** A massive black-steel sword engraved with the Oath of Stone; functions as both weapon and ceremonial seal.  
- **Crown of the Atlas Chain:** Forged from dormant Wellspring veins; weighs twenty pounds yet feels weightless upon his brow.  
- **Robes of the Lawgivers:** Handwoven from scripture-thread; each line a vow renewed by his lineage every equinox.  

---

**Quotes**  
> “Babyl stands not because we are strong, but because we refuse to forget what strength is for.”  
> “Law is mercy written in weight.”  
> “When my crown falls, so will the sky.”  

---

**Relations & Diplomacy**  
- **Allied:** Eresse (mutual respect through shared Parunic doctrine)  
- **Tense:** Pratorian March (accuses them of weaponizing Wellspring fire)  
- **Neutral:** Guild Accord (signatory, but often delays decrees citing “Temporal Weight Clauses”)  

---

**Temperance Influence on Babyl**  
Varuun’s rule has caused a cultural crystallization: Babylian citizens unconsciously resist corruption and deceit. The air within the capital is rumored to “refuse lies.” Monks of the Wellspring Chambers claim that his heartbeat echoes faintly in the harmonic core beneath the city, syncing the realm’s rhythm to his resolve.  

---

**Current Status (Voyager Era)**  
Active ruler; aging yet unyielding. Rumors claim his Soul Crystal has begun to merge with Babyl’s Wellspring, transforming him into a **living Anchor-King** — a being who will one day fuse entirely with Coagula, ensuring the kingdom’s laws endure even after his death.

### **ASAEMON**
**Alias / Title:** The Vestal Vanguard
**Also Known As:** The Gilded Surgeon · The Silent Pulse
**Epithet:** He Who Severs the Thread of Corruption

---

### **I. IDENTITY & CORE PROFILE**
* **Race / Lineage:** High-Order Mortal Spirit (Aether-Sensitive)
* **Faction / Order / Era:** Order of the Radiant Veil — Third Era (Post-Wellspring Awakening)
* **Temperance Stage:** Stage 4 — Flourishing
* **Profile Summary:** A stoic combat medic and specialized executioner, Asaemon utilizes the Gilded Pulse to "operate" on the battlefield. His goal is to achieve Zenith Temperance to facilitate a Concord Severance, a ritual meant to purify the corrupted Titans threatening the WOTR landscape.
* **Key Quote:** "True mercy isn't found in the healing of a wound, but in the absolute precision of the cut that prevents its return."

---

### **II. CANONICAL SYSTEM METRICS**
* **Aether Class:** Weaver (Precision-Type)
* **Behavior:** Manifests as thin, vibrating golden filaments (Aurevane) that track the internal resonance of targets.
* **Risk:** **Crystal Shear** — Over-tuning his internal frequency to match high-tier threats can cause his physical body to become brittle and shatter like glass.
* **Wellspring Affinity:**
    * **Triune Core:** Aurevane · Temperance · Equilibrium
    * **Network:** 2 additional Wellsprings currently networked.
* **Temperance Path:** Progressed through meditative isolation and high-risk surgical combat, refining his internal "Volume" into "Pressure."

### **III. SOUL PHYSICS & COMBAT SCALE**
* **Global Tier:** Tier A+ (High-Elite)
* **Path Bias:** Spirit (60%) · Body (30%) · Mind (10%)
* **Appearance:** Height: 6'1"; Build: Lean, athletic. Skin/Eyes/Hair: Pale skin; glowing amber eyes; messy, shoulder-length silver-white hair.

* **Essence Capacity (Volume):** 85,000 EU

    * *Functional Definition:* Extremely sharp; his Aether can bypass standard kinetic shielding by "slipping" between molecular gaps.

* **Aetheric Efficiency (η):** 0.92
    * *Functional Definition:* Highly efficient; Asaemon wastes almost no energy during manifestation.

    * *Functional Definition:* A heavy, vibrating hum that causes weaker beings to feel a sense of intense vertigo.

* **Strike Force:** 12.5 GN / 1.2 PJ (Focused into needle-point strikes).
* **Durability:** 45 PJ (Aurevane Reinforcement)
* **Speed:** Mach 4.5 (Reflexes); Instantaneous (Short-range Gilded Displacement)
* **Range:** Visual (Aura Range); 500 Meters (Filament Control)

### **IV. AXIOMATIC MANIFESTATIONS**
#### **A. Axiomatic Traits (Passive)**
* **Harmonic Sight:** Asaemon perceives the world through rhythmic pulses, allowing him to see through illusions and "feel" the health of a target's core.
* **Vestal Resilience:** High resistance to spiritual corruption and mental manipulation.

#### **B. Core Disciplines (Active)**
* **Surgical Pathos** (Cost: 2,500 EU per activation)
    * **Mechanic:** Slows Asaemon's perception of time to pinpoint the "Axiomatic Weakpoint" of any structure or entity.
    * **Application:** Utilizes Flux Pressure to drive a single, microscopic point of Aether into a target's core.
* **Gilded Pulse: Severance** (Cost: 15,000 EU)
    * **Mechanic:** A wide-range sweep of golden filaments that vibrate at the frequency of "Rejection."
    * **Application:** Specifically targets networked Wellsprings to disconnect them from a host.

#### **C. Costs & Consequences**
* **Sensory Blackout:** Extended use of Surgical Pathos results in temporary blindness.
* **Systemic Brittleness:** High-output Severance maneuvers temporarily reduce physical Durability by 50%.

### **V. RELICS & DOMAIN**
* **Current Domain:** The Stillness of the Void
* **Function:** A 15-meter radius where all sound and chaotic Aether flow is dampened, forcing combatants to rely on pure physical stats or perfectly refined Aether.
* **Relics & Implements:**
    * **The Aurevane Lancet:** A focus tool that condenses Flux Density into a tangible blade of light (Relic-Tier).
    * **Cloak of the Silent Pulse:** Dampens his internal signature, making him undetectable to Aetheric radar.

---

### **VI. LORE & TRAJECTORY**
* **Allegiance:** The Vanguard of the Veil / WOTR Resistance
* **Incident Records (Historical):**
    * **The Siege of Oriel:** Single-handedly disconnected three corrupted generals from their power source, ending the siege without a casualty.
* **Counters & Weaknesses:**
    * **Chaotic Flux:** Unpredictable, non-rhythmic energy sources disrupt his Harmonic Sight.
    * **Mass Saturation:** Struggles against "blunt force" armies that overwhelm precision defenses.
* **Current Status:** **Active** in the Shattered Peaks.
* **Trajectory:** Seeking the *Wellspring of Origin* to catalyze his ascension to Zenith Temperance.

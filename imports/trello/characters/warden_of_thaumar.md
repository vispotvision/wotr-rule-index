## **“THE IRON WARDEN OF THAUMAR”**

### **Profile Summary**
**Name:** Garron Vuldane  
**Alias:** *The Iron Warden*  
**Race:** Human (Thaumic-Reforged)  
**Gender:** Male  
**Age:** 41  
**Origin Realm:** Thalassar — The Walled Forge-Cities  
**Affiliation:** Guild Accord – *Division of Alchemetrica: Thaumaturgic Wardens*  
**Rank:** T7 Master Alchemist / Siegewright  

---

### **Appearance**
A broad, mountain-born figure draped in layered scaleplate and alchemical fabrics. His mane of iron-gray hair blends seamlessly into the metallic fur of his armor — forged from living Aethersteel strands that respond to his pulse. His weapon is a colossal transmutation cannon engraved with runes that shift color as he channels Essence through it.  

Every piece of his equipment hums faintly, alive with reaction potential. His gauntlets bear draconic embossments, and the pauldrons resemble carved beasts that blink when his Wellspring stirs. His breath smells faintly of ozone and mercury — byproduct of internal transmutation catalysts.  

---

### **Temperance Path**
**Stage IX — Reflection**  
Garron’s Temperance manifests as the ability to fuse intellect and instinct. He no longer distinguishes between crafting and combat — the act of battle is his alchemical formula, every strike part of an unfolding transmutation.  

---

### **Aether Class**
**Transmutive Soul – Warden Mode**  
He carries a portable Forge-Wellspring within his body. This living furnace fuels his constructs and powers his weapon through Essence combustion and metallic synthesis. Each strike of his cannon releases condensed alchemical reactions rather than mere explosions — a perfect blend of metallurgy and soulwork.  

---

### **Wellspring Alignment**
- **Primary:** *Transmutatio* (Change, Craft, Fusion)  
- **Secondary:** *Materia Primordia* (Creation, Substance, Foundation)  
- **Tertiary:** *Coagula* (Binding, Fusion of Forces)  

---

### **Division / Allegiance**
**Guild Accord – Alchemetrica / Thaumaturgic Wardens**  
Tasked with defending the Grand Forges and sealing runaway Essence reactions. Garron’s specialization lies in “Forge Synthesis” — rapid transmutation of battlefield debris into weapons or armor during active combat.  

---

### **Personality & Core Themes**
Patient as stone, brutal when provoked. Garron views the world as unfinished work — flawed metal awaiting hammer and flame. Speaks rarely, but every sentence lands with the weight of an oath. Protective of his subordinates to the point of near-sacrifice.  
Theme: *Creation demands burden.*  

---

### **Soul Crystal Profile**
- **Structure:** Triple-Core Furnace (Heart + Spine + Gauntlet nodes)  
- **Flux Density:** 13.5 million EU/g  
- **Gate Count:** 9 active, 1 dormant  
- **Aether Output:** 118,000 AU/s  
- **Efficiency (η):** 0.86  

---

### **Traits**
- **Forge Resonance:** The sound of his heartbeat alters the frequency of nearby metals, making them malleable to his will.  
- **Metallic Regeneration:** Rebuilds armor or prosthetics through Essence circulation.  
- **Catalytic Strike:** Each impact from his weapon triggers a micro-transmutation field, shifting local matter composition.  
- **Beast-Binding Armor:** His pauldrons house two semi-sentient constructs — *Harn* and *Vyr* — that act as shield and companion, both fed by his Forge Wellspring.  

---

### **Relics & Implements**
- **The Basilisk Cannon “Molthra”** — A Thaumaturgic siege weapon capable of firing compressed transmutive plasma. Output measured at 45 million EU per shot; recoil compensated by Essence dampening coils.  
- **Wardbeast Armor:** Animated Aethersteel plating shaped like a chimera. Absorbs kinetic force, converting it into alchemical heat for counterstrikes.  
- **Forge Chain Mantle:** A cloak of living steel filaments that self-weaves into tools or restraints when commanded.  

---

### **Abilities & Disciplines**
- **Prime Catalyst:** Opens a temporary Forge Rift, melting inorganic matter within a 10-meter radius into usable material. Duration: 8 seconds.  
- **Aether Shell:** Fires concentrated transmutation cores — bursts of reactive Essence that rewrite the structure of their target.  
- **Bound Synthesis:** Fuses Harn and Vyr into a single combat form, the *Forge Chimera*, which amplifies his output by 300% for one minute.  
- **Anvil Law:** A defensive stance that converts incoming Essence attacks into crafting potential — literally reforging magic into material.  

---

### **Costs & Consequences**
Sustained use of *Prime Catalyst* destabilizes the Forge Wellspring, causing burns and calcification in his veins. Excess output can trigger “Core Backfire,” where the body begins turning into metallic substrate — a condition known among the Wardens as *Iron Bloom*.  

---

### **Hooks**
- Lost his original left arm to a Forge collapse; rebuilt it from dragonbone and Aethersteel.  
- Believes the Titans left behind not gods, but tools — and seeks to reforge them.  
- Rumored to have forged a weapon capable of turning Wellsprings into solid matter.  

---

### **Quote**
> “The forge never stops burning — only the weak forget that every spark is a choice.”

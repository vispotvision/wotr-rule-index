# **GARU LUNEWARD**
**Alias / Title:** Winter Hound of the March
**Also Known As:** Snow Scout · Blue Cloak Tracker · Half-Wolf Without A Tail
**Epithet:** He Who Hears Footsteps In The Wind

---

### **I. IDENTITY & CORE PROFILE**
* **Race / Lineage:** **Human / Wolfkin Hybrid** (Old World Northern Strain)
* **Faction / Order / Era:** Hunter’s Guild (Forward Scout) · Praetorian March Frontier · **Imperial Calendar**
* **Temperance Stage:** **Stage IV — Flourishing** (Instinct Driven Veteran)
* **Profile Summary:** Garu Luneward is the "Ghost of the Praetorian Line." Born to a wolfkin parent and a human guide, he lacks a tail and the social instincts of a pack, leaving him isolated but fiercely self-reliant. His Aether wraps around scent, sound, and emotional residue, freezing them as pale afterimages only he can perceive. He is not a soldier who holds the line; he is the scout who ensures the line never gets surprised.
* **Key Quote:** *“Tracks can lie. The wind cannot. Just listen.”*

---

### **II. CANONICAL SYSTEM METRICS**
* **Aether Class:** **Sensory Scout** (Lunar Echo Type)
  * **Behavior:** His Aether projects thin fields of moon-touched energy from his ears and skin. These fields catch scent, sound, and recent motion, freezing them as "Pale Echoes." He can follow a single set of emotional footprints through a blizzard or erase his own trail by folding the echo back into himself.
  * **Risk:** **Sensory Drowning.** If he hunts a hateful or terrified target too long, their feelings start to override his own nerves, pushing him toward panic or feral rage.
* **Wellspring Affinity:**
  * **Triune Core:** **Somnalis** (Dreams/Echoes) · **Mortalis** (The Hunt) · **Verdantia** (Wilds)
  * **Network:** **0** (Solitary Pattern).
  * **Targeted Uninfected:** He feels a pull toward **Oneirion** (Dream) Wellsprings but avoids them to prevent hallucinations.
* **Temperance Path:** **Body Path (Survival)** → **Spirit Path (Empathy).** Garu weaponizes Flourishing by turning empathy into precision: he rarely misses because he feels the target's intent a split second before they move.

---

### **III. SOUL PHYSICS & COMBAT SCALE**
* **Global Tier:** **Tier B+** (High Veteran) → **Tier A-** (In Heavy Snow/Night)
* **Path Bias:** **Body** (1.35) · **Spirit** (1.20) · **Attraction** (1.00)
* **Appearance:** Height: 5'8". Build: Lean, corded runner's build. Hair: Shaggy white. Features: Tall, twitching wolf ears; silver-gray eyes that gain a red ring when active. Wears the **Blue Cloak of the Northern Watch**.

#### **A. The Five Pillars (Soul Physics)**
> *Essence is Volume. Flux is Pressure. Output is Speed. Efficiency is Clarity. Aura is Gravity.*

* **Essence Capacity (Volume):** **12,500 EU**
  * *Functional Definition:* Standard Veteran capacity. He relies on efficiency rather than a massive fuel tank.
* **Flux Density (Internal Pressure):** **1,100 EU/g (Compressed)**
  * *Functional Definition:* His soul is tight and guarded. It resists external emotional manipulation (Fear/Charm) very well.
* **Aether Output (Flow Rate):** **80,000 AU/s** (Standard)
  * *Functional Definition:* Low output compared to heavy hitters, but precise. He uses "sips" of magic rather than "gulps."
* **Aetheric Efficiency (η):** **0.92 (The Tracker's Economy)**
  * *Functional Definition:* Extremely high. Garu wastes almost no movement or magic. Every unit of Essence is used to gain information or speed.
* **Attraction Scale (Aura/Presence):** **D-Tier: The Quiet Frost**
  * *Functional Definition:* Subliminal. People lower their voices around him. He doesn't crush the room; he makes it hold its breath.

#### **B. Key Combat Parameters**
* **Strike Force:** **450 Megajoules (MJ)** (Focused Lunge)
  * *Mechanic:* Equivalent to a point-blank anti-tank round concentrated on a spear tip. Enough to pierce Tier-B armor, but not to shatter city walls.
* **Durability:** **350 Megajoules (MJ)** (Evasion Dependent)
  * *Mechanic:* His "durability" is mostly his ability to not be there. His cloak absorbs minor magical environmental hazards.
* **Speed:** **Mach 2.5** (Burst Sprint)
  * *Mechanic:* In short bursts, he moves faster than the sound of his own footsteps, especially on snow.
* **Range:** **100 Meters** (Sensory/Thrown) / **Close Quarters** (Melee)

### **IV. AXIOMATIC MANIFESTATIONS**
#### **A. Axiomatic Traits (Passive)**
* **Moonwolf Senses:** Hearing and smell 50x beyond human baseline. Can separate individual heartbeats in a crowd.
* **Packless Resolve:** High resistance to Charm or Pack-Bond magic. His isolation has calcified his mental defenses.
* **Snow Silent Step:** He generates a dampening field under his feet. Even in armor, he moves with 0 decibels of sound output.

#### **B. Core Disciplines (Active)**
* **Echo Trail Reading (Forensics)** (Cost: **Low EU**)
  * *Mechanic:* Brushes the air/ground to reveal silver afterimages of recent events.
  * *Application:* Reads the "velocity of fear" in a footprint to determine how fast the target was running and why.
* **Crescent Fang Rush (Movement Art)** (Cost: **Medium EU**)
  * *Mechanic:* Condenses echoes into his legs to snap forward in a curved, non-linear trajectory.
  * *Application:* To the enemy, he vanishes and reappears at their flank. Used for tendon-severing strikes.
* **Liminal Howl Ward (Debuff)** (Cost: **High EU + Voice Strain**)
  * *Mechanic:* Releases gathered emotional residue as a low-frequency howl.
  * *Application:* Shakes the nerves of beasts/spawn, reducing their coordination by 40%. Allies feel their fear "pulled out" of them.

#### **C. Costs & Consequences**
* **Echo Overload:** In crowded cities, he suffers sensory seizures ("Ghost Crowds"). Must physically block ears/nose to reset.
* **Draining Howl:** Using the Howl burns his throat, reducing his breathing efficiency (and thus his speed) until he rests.

### **V. RELICS & DOMAIN**
* **Current Domain:** **Proto-Domain: The Snowbound Route**
  * *Function:* A narrow corridor where blizzards thin and footing is perfect. It guarantees "Safe Passage" for a small caravan.
* **Relics & Implements:**
  * **Hunter Guild Field Harness:** (Tier C). Custom rig that prevents gear rattle.
  * **Blue Cloak of the Northern Watch:** (Tier B). Heavy wool threaded with Aether. Sheds wet/mud and creates a brief "Scent Screen" when snapped.

### **VI. LORE & TRAJECTORY**
* **Allegiance:** Hunter’s Guild (Praetorian March) · The Caravan Families
* **Incident Records:**
  * **The Vanished Sled Line:** Tracked a missing convoy through a 3-day whiteout using only "fear echoes." Found survivors in a ravine that visual scanners missed.
  * **The Gray Fang Night Hunt:** Solo-hunted a Withering-touched direwolf pack for a week, turning them against each other using fake scent trails.
* **Counters & Weaknesses:**
  * **Null-Fields:** Industrial smog or strong alchemical fumes blind his Echo Sight.
  * **False Echoes:** Illusionists who understand his empathy tracking can bait him into traps using "fake fear."
* **Current Status:** **Active.** Scouting Black Concord movements on the frontier.
* **Trajectory:** Learning that a "Pack" isn't just about blood, but about who you choose to protect.
* **Foreshadowing:** If he stabilizes his Domain, he could become the **"Ferryman of the Frost,"** a legendary guide who can walk anyone through any storm safely.

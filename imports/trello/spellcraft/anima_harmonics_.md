**Type:** Magic System

**Summary:**
**Anima**, within the Harmonic traditions of WOTR, refers to the **fundamental resonance of the soul**—the metaphysical frequency by which a being’s identity, Essence, and Fate are vibrationally expressed. Through the practice of **Anima Harmonics**, practitioners learn to perceive, interpret, and, in rare cases, manipulate this soul-frequency to heal, diagnose, or attune with spiritual phenomena. The Anima is not just a vibration—it is the soul's signature, its note within the Song of Aether.

Anima Harmonics allow for _deep soul reading_, _Wellspring stabilization_, _Trait tracking_, and even _spiritual mirroring_. However, tuning the Anima of another without consent is forbidden in most circles, as the risk of disrupting a soul’s harmonic balance can lead to echo-fracture, Wellspring corruption, or metaphysical dissonance.

**Key Figures:**

- **Caelmorne Halvrein** – Moon-born harmonicist who refined the art of healing through Anima silence; founded the practice of _Soul Resonant Listening_.
- **Thalos of Irā** – Creator of the _Chord Theory_, linking specific Anima frequencies to Wellspring types and Trait behavior.
- **Mnirah Valein** – Integrated Anima Harmonics into Spirit Construct design, allowing constructs to inherit emotional frequencies.
- **The Auricle Choir** – An esoteric guild that believes each soul is a note in the Divine Choral—weaving harmonic symphonies to predict Temperance changes.

**Historical Importance:**

- Originated during the **Age of Grace**, when elven Resonants discovered that emotional trauma altered the harmonic field of Soul Crystals.
- In the **Age of Calamity**, corrupted Anima tones—known as _Fell Chords_—became common among those touched by Veilborne forces, marking them as spiritually diseased.
- By the **Voyager Era**, Anima Harmonics are foundational to metaphysical diagnosis, guild-based soul forensics, and advanced Alchemical soulcraft.
- In sanctified regions like **Lurien** or **Altherion**, unauthorized Anima manipulation is punishable under sacred law as a defilement of the soul.

**Connections:**

- **Soul Crystals** – The Anima is emitted by the Soul Crystal as a constant metaphysical tone; its clarity or distortion reflects soul health.
- **The Veil (Spiritual Stratum)** – Anima resonance leaves harmonic traces across Veil folds, allowing tracking of soul movement or trauma.
- **Divination (Harmonics)** – Anima is the personal tone heard by gifted Resonants, forming the basis for fate-mirroring or prophecy work.
- **Wellspring Paths** – Each Wellspring imparts its own tonal modulation to the Anima. For example, Rage adds dissonant pulse; Grace softens overtones.
- **Alchemy** – Soulbound recipes require harmonic synchronization with the Anima to avoid backlash or corruption during Essence refinement.

**First Appearance (Volume/Chapter):**
_Chronicles of the Exiles_, Volume II — _“The Chord Between Us”_ (Chapter 6: _When the Soul Sang Back_).

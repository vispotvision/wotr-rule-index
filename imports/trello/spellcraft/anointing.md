### **Type:**

**Magic System** – Ritus Class (Spiritual Designation / Sanctified Empowerment)

---

### **Summary:**

**Ritus “Anointing”** is a sacred rite used to **imbue an individual with metaphysical legitimacy, divine favor, or spiritual recognition**, marking them as a vessel of purpose, prophecy, or preordained authority. Unlike *Coronation*, which confers rule or dominion, *Anointing* sanctifies the **role or journey**—declaring that the individual has been **chosen** by a higher force, Wellspring, or Archon to walk a specific spiritual path.

Anointing alters the individual’s **Animatria and Trait expression**, sometimes awakening latent Wellspring alignment or unlocking sealed glyphs within the Soul Crystal. It may be ritualistic (performed by priesthoods, Concords, or Archons), or spontaneous (triggered by sacred trials, revelations, or divine encounters). The mark of Anointing often appears as a **spiritual seal**, visible only to those with high Aetheric perception.

---

### **Key Figures:**

* **Aethelsige Moto** — Was Anointed beneath the Eye of Solara during the Eclipse Concord, becoming the prophetic vessel of solar fate.
* **Seraphiel Moto** — Received his Anointing in the Dream Wellspring, marking him with the golden tear of clarity and sacrifice.
* **The Oracles of Vellsorea** — All receive ritual Anointing at the age of twelve to bind their voice to the tides of fate.
* **Caladriel of Highspire** — Her Anointing was witnessed by three Archons simultaneously, marking her as a “Tri-Soul Shepherd.”

---

### **Historical Importance:**

* Used to designate **spiritual emissaries**, *fate-bound kings*, *Wellspring guardians*, and **chosen bearers of ancient relics**.
* A central rite in the **Order of Benediction**, where Anointing is believed to preserve the voice of the Divine in mortal form.
* The **False Anointing Rebellion** was sparked when a corrupted bishop of Lurien used glyph-forged oil to mark war criminals as divine heroes.
* Marks the first phase of **Ascension Preparatory Rites** for many Archonic initiates.

---

### **Connections:**

* Tied closely to the **Wellsprings of Benediction**, **Fixation**, and **Emanation**—those governing sanctity, continuity, and purpose.
* Commonly uses **Parun glyphs** such as:

  * **Lo** (devotion / divine acknowledgment)
  * **Wy** (grace / elevation)
  * **Ka** (destiny / chosen path)
* Often layered with **Ritus: Sacrament**, or performed during a **Manifestus: Benediction** event.
* Anointed individuals may carry a **Soul Mark**, **Aura Echo**, or **Wellspring Signature** that alters nearby magical behavior.

---

### **First Appearance (Volume/Chapter):**

**WOTR: Era of Grace** — *Volume I, Chapter 14: “Marked by Light, Named by Silence”*

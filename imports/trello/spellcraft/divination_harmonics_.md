**Type:** Magic System

**Summary:**
_Harmonics_ in WOTR Divination are the subtle frequencies of **Aether**, **Essence**, and **spiritual signature** that resonate through all beings, objects, and phenomena. To divine through Harmonics is to listen not with the ears, but with the soul—detecting the resonant echoes that shape fate, truth, and form. Practitioners of this path—often called _Resonants_, _Harmonic Diviners_, or _Echo-Seers_—use Harmonics to read, manipulate, or synchronize with the vibrational structure of existence.

Unlike prophecy or clerical foresight, Harmonic Divination is rooted in analysis rather than revelation. It is the study of **what is**, **what resonates**, and **how spiritual frequencies interact**. To read Harmonics is to diagnose a person’s soul-state, predict the collision of destinies, or even disassemble metaphysical constructs by targeting their flawed frequencies.

However, tampering with Harmonics is dangerous—akin to performing surgery on a cosmic organ without knowing all its functions.

**Key Figures:**

- **Caelmorne Halvrein** – A moon-aligned Resonant and spiritual surgeon who healed corrupted Soul Crystals through microharmonic recalibration.
- **Serenhal Caelmorne** – Known as _He Whose Silence Was Sung by the Moon_, mastered the Harmonics of intent and emotion, using silent frequency to disrupt battlefields and calm fractured realities.
- **Izathen the Broken Lyre** – A legendary mad Seer who attempted to rewrite his own Harmonics, resulting in the Echo Fracture of 8,000 AF.
- **The Sonochoral Conclave** – A secretive divinatory guild who chant in tonal sequences to alter battlefield frequencies and manipulate spirit flow.

**Historical Importance:**

- First discovered in the **Second Epoch** by Parunic monks decoding the tonal structures of glyph chants.
- Formalized during the **Age of Grace** as a divinatory system among elven sages, using it for soul diagnosis, artifact tuning, and harmonic combat techniques.
- The **Echo Fracture** during the Age of Calamity made Harmonics highly restricted in most academies due to instability and metaphysical risk.
- In the **Voyager Era**, Harmonics are treated as both sacred and volatile—useful in spiritual surgery, relic interpretation, or high-tier alchemy.

**Connections:**

- **Essence Drafts** – Each glyph carries a harmonic tone; Harmonics are often used to verify Draft stability or identify corrupted symbols.
- **Soul Crystals** – Harmonics are the vibrational output of the Soul Crystal. Tracking changes in resonance can reveal psychological damage, Wellspring alignment, or fate entanglements.
- **Alchemy** – Harmonic resonance is essential in **Aetheric transmutation**, ensuring correct frequency matching during component synthesis.
- **Wellspring Paths** – Advanced Resonants can hear or attune to the unique frequency of Wellsprings (e.g., Grace rings soft and cyclical; Rage pulses like a torn drum).
- **Plane of Fate** – Harmonics ripple through Fate’s substrate. Some Resonants believe Harmonics are the "sound" of causality unraveling.

**First Appearance (Volume/Chapter):**
_Chronicles of the Exiles_, Volume II — _“The Twelve Frequencies of Silence”_ (Chapter 3: _Moonthreaded Harmonics_).

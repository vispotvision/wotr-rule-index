### **Type:**

**Magic System** – Manifestus Class (Deception / Conceptual Collapse)

---

### **Summary:**

 **Manifestus  “Fallacy”** is the divine act of **revealing—or invoking—a falsehood so profound that it unravels reality’s trust in its own laws**. It manifests as a metaphysical contradiction, a spiritual paradox that _undoes truths, fractures logic, and collapses certainty_. It is not simple illusion, but the fabrication of a _lie_ so convincing it becomes briefly real—only to then shatter, taking other structures down with it.

When invoked, _Fallacy_ forcibly injects a false axiom into a spiritual system—causing Essence Drafts to misfire, Soul Crystals to misalign, and even Wellspring paths to deviate from their ordained temperance. It is **banned in most formal Ritus Orders**, and only employed by certain Archons of Chaos, Trickery, or Insight.

It is the **philosopher’s curse**, the **liar’s gospel**, the **mage’s temptation**.

---

### **Key Figures:**

- **Razakiel** — Master of subversion; invoked _Fallacy_ to deceive an entire domain into believing its own god had never existed.
- **Serenhal Caelmorne** — Used a Fallacy-layered Manifestus during the Twilight of the Sojourners, creating a false prophecy that unraveled two empires.

---

### **Historical Importance:**

- Instrumental in the **Shattering of the Lucent Monastery**, where a Precept of Truth was nullified by repeated applications of Fallacy.
- One of the five Forbidden Manifesti recorded in the **Vaults of Misrule** beneath Vellsorea.
- Considered a metaphysical virus during the **Era of Deliberation**, where entire doctrines collapsed when foundational axioms were challenged by it.
- Used during the **Trial of the Mirror Lords**, resulting in two kingdoms believing they ruled each other.

### **Connections:**

### **Type:**

**Magic System** – Manifestus Class (Spiritual Encoding / Memory-Form Projection)

---

### **Summary:**

**Manifestus “Imprinta”** is the sacred act of **projecting a piece of the soul, memory, Trait, or Wellspring resonance into matter, place, or person**, leaving behind a **permanent spiritual echo** that endures beyond the moment. Unlike _Inscription_, which uses glyphs to encode fixed functions, _Imprinta_ embeds a **living fragment of will or identity**—capable of awakening, interacting, or influencing reality long after the caster has departed.

Imprinta is often used to create **echo guardians**, **relational relics**, **haunting memory-fields**, or **soul-laced sanctuaries**. It is also the foundation of many **ghostbound rituals**, where spiritual fragments linger with agency and purpose. The rite is deeply emotional—imprints respond most strongly to memory, desire, and unresolved fate.

---

### **Key Figures:**

- **Mecre, Archon of Memory** — Originator of the Rite; used _Imprinta_ to seed echoes of forgotten truths across the branches of Gaia.
- **Soji the Dreamwalker** — Created emotional Imprinta in the Dream Wellspring where memories took physical form and influenced the realm.
- **Caldran of Athame** — Forged Imprinta-bound weapons that remembered each wielder and changed their abilities accordingly.
- **The Choir of Vellsorea** — Sang songs that left Imprinta across battlefields, causing future spellcasters to remember the fight and cast instinctively.

---

### **Historical Importance:**

- Used in the creation of **Soul-Forged Relics**, such as the _Vowsteel Rings_ and the _Verdant Blades of Tirres_.
- Imprinta rituals helped preserve **pre-Spirit War knowledge**, when living memory was more reliable than parchment.
- Considered sacred by **Echo Wellspring Orders**, especially in Estesia and Edenis, where gardens and tombs hum with past lives.
- Played a critical role in the **Reconstruction of Utopia**, where Imprinta-bound architects guided the rebuilding of lost sanctums through vision-echoes.

---

### **Connections:**

- Invoked using Parun glyphs such as:
  - **Ur** (memory / soul trace)
  - **Ci** (mirror / spiritual resonance)
  - **Ka** (destiny / echo line)
  - **Wy** (grace / gentle binding)
- Often layered with **Ritus: Sacrament**, or embedded inside **Synergia: Overtone** for delayed or layered activation.
- Can produce **Imprint Echoes**—semi-sentient memory forms that awaken under certain emotional, spiritual, or narrative conditions.

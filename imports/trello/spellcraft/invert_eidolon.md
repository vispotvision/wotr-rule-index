### **Type:** \[Magic System / Animatria Sub-Discipline]

---

### **Summary:**

**Invert Eidolon** is a rare and dangerous Animatria technique that summons a **reversed spirit construct**—a being not of loyalty, but of **mirrored essence** and internal contradiction. Instead of drawing forth a bonded familiar or eidolon shaped by harmony and soulcraft, this rite forcibly manifests a **spirit of refusal, inversion, or trauma** buried deep within the summoner’s Soul Crystal.

The resulting entity—often called an **Anti-Eidolon**—is shaped not by will, but by **what the summoner cannot accept**: forgotten guilt, suppressed Traits, broken Wellspring harmonics, or metaphysical shame. Invert Eidolons possess inversion of the original Eidolon’s function, affinity, and emotional resonance, and are **often hostile unless bound or redeemed**.

It is considered a **forbidden practice** in most guilds, though some soulwalkers and fractured Temperants pursue it in search of completion, closure, or power through spiritual confrontation.

---

### **Key Figures:**

* **Thessan of Mireya** — A spiritbinder who famously summoned her Invert Eidolon, *Vel-Muriah*, and bound it not through dominance, but dialogue. She later became known as the “Whisper-Twin.”
* **Silvantes Mournthresh** — Pale-binder of the Riven Choir who split his soul into three Eidolons, one of which was inverted. He wielded it as a weapon against both Archons and guilt.
* **Karo Venrik** *(unconfirmed)* — Rumored to have unintentionally triggered an Invert Eidolon during early summoning trials; may have encountered a fractured version of his own spiritual projection.

---

### **Historical Importance:**

* First recorded during the **Twilight Concord**, when fractured spiritbinders attempted to weaponize their own soul echoes.
* Became a blacklisted Animatria method during the **Era of Calamity**, after mass casualties at the **Wellspring Fracture of Nalisem**, where 13 summoners lost control of their Inverted Eidolons during a temperance rite.
* Secretly preserved by fringe cults, including the **Riven Choir**, **Mirrorwake Ascetics**, and the **Occultinarii of the Hollow Eye**.
* Now reemerging in the **Era of Voyagers** among exile-practitioners seeking forbidden harmonics.

---

### **Connections:**

* **Animatria** – Root discipline.
* **Crystalia** – Requires deep manipulation of Soul Crystal layers to fracture the eidolon-binding lattice.
* **Traitus / Mechanica** – Often intersects with forbidden Traits or soul collapse.
* **Wellsprings** – Typically manifests after reflection, severance, or discord stages.
* **Ritus** – Invert Eidolon can be summoned via mirrored rituals involving blood-oath and glyph reversal.

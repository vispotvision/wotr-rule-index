### **Type:**

**Magic System** – Manifestus Class (Vow Severance / Spiritual Contract Shatter)

---

### **Summary:**

**Manifestus “Oathrend”** is a devastating metaphysical act wherein the caster **forcibly breaks a binding oath, soul-pact, blood rite, or divine contract**, sundering its consequences across all planes in which it was enforced. Unlike *Scission*, which surgically removes Essence, *Oathrend* targets **the vow itself**—tearing through its Parun-etched architecture and **undoing the metaphysical chain that links soul to oath**.

It is one of the most dangerous and disruptive Manifestus abilities, often invoking **retribution, curse backlash, divine attention, or animic rupture**. When an oath is shattered through this method, all who were bound to it **feel the fracture**, and the world momentarily registers the vow’s absence like a wound in narrative space. In ancient doctrine, Oathrend is sometimes called *“the language of betrayal made real.”*

---

### **Key Figures:**

* **Zeven Halek** — Once invoked a personal Oathrend in the field, breaking a dead comrade’s Concord tether to spare Cylus from soul collapse.
* **Lysandra Velkaris** — Used Oathrend to dissolve a holy Sacrament binding her to Purganeth’s ancient law, choosing mortal free will over divine bondage.
* **The Ashblood Apostates** — Wield Oathrend as both weapon and liberation, using it to unravel divine covenants in cursed territories.

---

### **Historical Importance:**

* Used to shatter the **Threefold Concord of Athame**, undoing the Pact of Renewal and plunging the region into metaphysical drought.
* Banned across the Innerworld’s Temple Courts after an Oathrend incited a **Mass Soul Collapse** during the Crown Accords.
* Ritualized in Outer Realms as a **liberation rite**, particularly among indentured spirits and forgotten bloodlines.
* Feared by Archons and bound Titans, whose laws and divine influence depend on the sanctity of eternal vows.

---

### **Connections:**

* Requires high-grade invocation of **Parun glyphs** such as:

  * **Ra** (chain / breaking)
  * **Ka** (destiny / severance)
  * **Lo** (law / rejection of divine structure)
  * **Ci** (mirror / inverse consequence)
* Commonly layered with **Ritus: Sacrament** (if the oath was divine), or followed by **Synergia: Reconciliation** to repair soul damage.
* Oathrend leaves behind an **Echo Scar**—a faint metaphysical residue where the bond once lived, detectable by Concord Seers and Wellspring Tenders.

### **First Appearance (Volume/Chapter):**

**WOTR: Fracture of the Crown Vow** — *Volume III, Chapter 18: “The Words I Take Back”*

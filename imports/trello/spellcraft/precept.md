### **Type:**

**Magic System** – Manifestus Class (Foundational Law / Sacred Instruction)

---

### **Summary:**

**Manifestus  “Precept”** is the **embodiment of initial metaphysical law**, a principle made incarnate through Aether and Authority. When invoked, it lays down a **truth that governs**—not just the caster, but all who operate within its declared sphere. _Precept_ is the act of **enshrining wisdom into the fabric of reality**, like etching commandments into the firmament.

Unlike _Decretum_, which is a proclamation of will, _Precept_ becomes **spiritual instruction**—a binding protocol that other spells, actions, or spiritual beings must obey if they fall within its clause. It is often used in the **creation of guild codes, divine laws, spiritual compacts, and realm-bound geasa**.

Precepts are the roots of Law, Dogma, and Ritual. They are both instructional and inexorable.

---

### **Key Figures:**

- **The Judger** — His first act upon descending was to inscribe the Twelve Precepts of Fate, forming the metaphysical lattice of Utopia.
- **Lysandra Velkaris** — Authored the Precept of Hierarchic Ascendance, used to solidify the divine chain of command in Highspire.
- **Thanatos Tyurkia** — Broke the Precept of Mercy, leading to her transformation into “The Purge.”

---

### **Historical Importance:**

- Forms the basis of **Writbinding**, the sacred art of law-engraving via Parun glyphs.
- The **Pillars of Order** within Crownforge are all founded upon distinct Precepts.
- Integral to the **Sacrosanct Accord**, which governs all inter-guild behavior across Innerworld.
- Used to carve the **Runes of Prohibition** during the fall of the Abythron Gate, preventing breach.

---

### **Connections:**

- Strongly aligned with the **Wellsprings of Fixation**, **Verdiction**, and **Harmony**.
- Key glyphs involved:
  - **Fi** (form / structure)
  - **Lo** (will / enactment)
  - **Wy** (resonance / truth)
  - **Ka** (destiny / spiritual instruction)
- Can be linked with **Ritus: Inscription**, **Manifestus: Clause**, or **Manifestus: Edictum** to form Law Trees.
- Interferes with **Rescind**, often nullifying attempts to undo a truth if the Precept is soul-bound.

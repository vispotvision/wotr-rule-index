**Type:** Metaphysical Layer / Cosmological Framework

**Summary:**
Known formally as the **Spiritual Stratum** and poetically as **“The Veil,”** this metaphysical lattice is the vast, layered continuum through which all **souls**, **spirits**, **memories**, and **spiritual forces** drift, echo, or anchor. It is not a single dimension, but a striated fabric of unseen planes—each fold carrying frequencies of **Fate**, **Aether**, and **Essence** that define the structure of identity, destiny, and divine resonance.

To peer into the Veil is to glimpse the soul’s hidden tether—where lost memories stir, ancestral echoes hum, and gods cast shadows across mortal lives. The Veil connects the Material World to the **Plane of Fate**, the **Wellspring Realms**, and even **Utopia**, acting as both barrier and bridge. Every act of Animancy, Spirit Summoning, and Temperance ripples across its folds.

**Key Figures:**

- **Rikudoku Moto** – Manipulated deep Veil layers to fracture spiritual continuity across battlefields; his soul was rumored to drift between folds even after death.
- **Eremund Vellsore** – A Harmonicist of the Sonorous Way who developed the first known method of _Veil-thread weaving_, using song to trace a soul’s trauma.
- **The Bound Watchers** – Veil-bound entities that dwell at the outermost edge of the Astral Fold. Their judgment is invoked in oaths, spiritual contracts, and Temperance rituals.

**Historical Importance:**

- First known references to the Veil appear during the **Second Epoch**, in Parunic chants that spoke of _"a place where names forget their voices.”_
- During the **Great Spirit War**, breaches in the Veil produced **Echo Beasts**, corrupted soul-forms born from grief, guilt, or unresolved memory.
- In the **Era of Grace**, monks and Animancers learned to **enter the Veil without rupture**, enabling soul surgery and spiritual communion.
- By the **Voyager Era**, the Veil has become sacred and restricted—ritual entry is tightly regulated in academies, temples, and high-order guilds due to metaphysical risk.

**Connections:**

- **Soul Crystals** – Project resonance patterns into the Veil. The clarity or dissonance of that projection determines spiritual health.
- **Animancy** – Operates directly within the Veil, modifying soul traits, severing echo-chains, and stabilizing fractured Wellspring alignments.
- **Spirit Summoning Arts** – All spirits are magnetized from the Veil’s midfolds or anchored echoes; stronger bonds allow deeper summoning.
- **Temperance Trials** – Most Temperance events (Wellspring awakenings, fusion, Trait evolution) occur within or upon the Veil.
- **Divination (Harmonics)** – Harmonic frequencies ripple through Veil folds, revealing soul conditions, ancestral alignments, or even future echoes.
- **Plane of Fate** – The upper Veil (sometimes called the _Causal Weave_) is the interface between action and prophecy, where time and identity are mirrored before manifesting.

**First Appearance (Volume/Chapter):**
_Book of Wellspring Resonance_, Volume I — _“The Breath of the Veil”_ (Chapter 4: _Where Spirits Drift but Names Remain_).

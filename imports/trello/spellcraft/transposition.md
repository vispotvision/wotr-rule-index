### **Type:**

**Magic System** – Ritus Class (Spatial-Soul Displacement / Positional Overwrite)

---

### **Summary:**

**Ritus “Transposition”** is a spatially-focused metaphysical rite that allows the caster to **exchange places, identities, or Essence signatures with another entity or object**—instantly or through delayed activation. It functions by overriding one’s **position in reality** (physical, spiritual, or symbolic) with another target, either to evade danger, deceive observers, or alter the trajectory of fate-aligned phenomena.

Transposition is a favored rite among **tacticians, illusionists, soulbinders, and time-displacement scholars**, as it can be used not only for simple teleportation or switching bodies, but also for complex metaphysical rewrites: *e.g., swapping someone’s death with your own echo, exchanging a memory into a physical object, or placing your soul inside a mirrored construct*.

---

### **Key Figures:**

* **Velcrissa of Selantra** — Mastered layered Transpositions using mirrors and shadows, famously escaping death three times in a single battle.
* **Zeven Halek** — Uses short-range Transposition fields to trade positions with his partner Cylus in combat.
* **Thalorath the Gatewrought** — Transposed his physical body with a city-wide Spirit Engine, becoming its living core.
* **The Red Twins of Dorrosfenn** — Practiced mass Transposition rituals to evade divine judgment, shifting entire armies into mirrored Realms.

---

### **Historical Importance:**

* Used extensively during the **Great Spirit War** by soulguarding orders who would Transpose themselves into condemned vessels to purify them from within.
* Instrumental in the **creation of Echo Constructs**, which rely on preloaded Transposition scripts embedded in a soul lattice.
* The **Fall of the Fourth Prince of Rovann** was triggered by a failed Transposition, in which his body was switched with a corrupted Dreamform.
* Adopted as a sacred rite by the **Dancer-Priests of Ilcin’dor**, who perform soul-shifting rituals through choreographed spatial rites.

---

### **Connections:**

* Most strongly linked to the **Wellsprings of Conjunction**, **Echo**, and **Fixation**, given its nature of redirection, substitution, and patterned outcome.
* Commonly bound to **Parun glyphs**:

  * **Ci** (mirror/duality)
  * **Th** (threshold/crossing)
  * **Ra** (binding chain)
* Often layered with **Synergia: Cascade** or **Overtone**, allowing Transposition events to unfold across time or reactively.
* Considered dangerous to use without **Anchor Constructs**, as the soul can fail to remap, leading to *Spatial Disjunction* or *Wellspring Desynchronization*.

## Archivium Locus

_(The Archive-Anchor)_

### I. Source & Concept

**Source (Wellspring):**

- Harmonic — ordered structure
- Spatial — geometric boundary

**Path:** Mind → Fate
**Aether Class:** Symphonic Scholar

**Core Concept (Fantasy):**
Serenyra inscribes invisible geometric glyphs into the environment, turning a chosen area into a temporary “archive node” where incoming attacks are recorded and deflected along pre-written vectors.

---

### II. Function + Philosophy

**Function:**
A [Defense] / [Zone Control] art.
She silently scripts a lattice of invisible sigils across surfaces and air. When a hostile force enters the zone, the lattice “reads” its trajectory and enforces an alternate path.

**Outcomes:**

- Deflection of projectiles and spells
- Forced grounding of vertical assaults
- Safe corridor openings for allies

**Philosophy:**
“A battlefield is only chaotic when no one has written the margins.”

---

### III. Mechanics Spec

**Name:** Archivium Locus
**Type:** [Defense] / [Zone Control]

**Temperance Requirement:** Stage VII — Diffraction

**Activation:**
Pre-cast preparation; she must have at least one turn to “write” the environment with Aether glyphs using Thesauriel or direct gesture.

**Range / Area:**

- Up to a 60 m radius sphere
- Centered on a point she can see or occupy

**Duration per turn:**
5 turns or until shattered.

**Tags:**
[Spatial] [Harmonic] [Ward] [Zone]

---

### IV. Limits, Weaknesses, & Cost

**Limits**

- Static once formed; cannot move with her
- Each new Locus overwrites the last active one
- Only a limited number of attack vectors can be pre-scripted (prioritizes obvious trajectories)

**Weaknesses**

- Ground-breaking, burrowing, or purely mental attacks can bypass the lattice
- Void ruptures tear holes in the geometry

**Cost — The Soul-Cost Currency Law**

- **Geometric Fatigue:** Extended use locks her thinking into rigid patterns, making improvisation harder outside the Locus.
- **Anchoring Debt:** The more massive the forces redirected, the more her own Essence briefly “anchors” to the site, making retreat dangerous.

---

### V. Counters & Interactions

- **Chaos Glyphs:** Scribal Arts that introduce randomness can corrupt the Locus’ equations.
- **Overwhelming Vertical Force:** Meteoric or Titan-level vertical drops can brute-force through if exceeding her current Aether budget.

---

### VI. Imagery, Sensation, & Language

**Visual Signature:** For those sensitive to Aether, faint golden lines web the air, forming a living mandala; impacts smear across the lines like ink on glass before redirecting.

**Sound Cue:** A crisp “page-turn” as each attack is rewritten.

**Sensation:** Within the Locus, allies feel subtly guided—like invisible hands nudging them into safer positions.

# **ASHFANG MARK INVOCATION**

(The Beast-Remnant Borrowing)

I. **Source and Concept**
Primary Wellspring: Cinerion
Secondary Wellsprings: Vorynn Predatory Essences
Path: Beast-Assimilation
Aether Class: Primal-Kinetic
Core Concept: Draven selects a bone talon from a slain beast and channels a fragment of its Essence through his blood. The borrowed trait manifests for a short time.

II. **Function and Philosophy**
Function: Buff • Morphic Adaptation
Outcomes:

- Grants one beast-trait (e.g., flight, sonic roar, burrow).
- Kinetically amplifies chosen behavior.
- Leaves remnant echoes in air.


Philosophy: “Wear what you kill.”

III. **Mechanics Spec**
Name: Ashfang Mark Invocation
Type: Buff / Essence Borrow
Temperance Requirement: Stage VI Glory
Activation: Declared
Range or Area: Self
Duration per turn: 4 turns
Tags: Beast-Essence, Morphic, Cinerion Remnant

IV. **Limits, Weaknesses, Cost**
Limits: Only one trait active at once.
Weaknesses: Trait becomes weaker the more often reused.
Cost: Temporary heartbeat instability.

V. **Counters and Interactions**
Counters: Identity-stripping spells, Essence seals.

VI. **Imagery**
Visual: Bone talon glows red-grey; veins blacken temporarily.
Sound: Pulse-thrum like a hunting heartbeat.
Sensation: Muscles coil like a beast’s.

# **AURIC PARALLAX VEIL**

_(“The Veil That Chooses Reality”)_

---

# I. **Source & Concept**

### **Source (Wellspring):**

**Primary Wellsprings:**

- **Basilithe** — crystalline law, the architecture of certainty
- **Fractura** — refraction, the splitting of possibility
- **Tenebra** — shadow-law equilibrium, silent correction

**Secondary Wellsprings:**

- **Oneirion** — conceptual modulation
- **Penance** — purifying consequence
- **Petralon** — anchoring mass
- **Orrenthal** — harmonic resonance
- **Tarturon** — entropy’s acceleration
- **Cataclysm** — upheaval-static
- **Dissolution** — return to un-being

### **Path:**

**Temperance XII — Emnation, The Path of Self-as-Law**

### **Aether Class:**

**Harmonic Reality-Phase Articulation**

### **Core Concept (Fantasy):**

Opalis casts a sheet of refracting, law-guided light-shadow across space, forcing **two or more possible outcomes of reality** to collide and overwrite each other. The Veil does not conjure constructs—it **reshapes the rules** of the area for a moment, choosing which version of events becomes true. The effect feels like the world “shifting perspective,” suddenly aligning with her intention.

---

# II. **Function + Philosophy**

### **Function:**

A **[Offense] / [Defense] / [Control] / [Field]** hybrid.
Opalis creates a refractive veil that **selects, negates, or enforces** the preferred version of an event or phenomenon by forcing its alternate pathways to collapse or align.

### **Outcomes:**

1. **Selective Negation:**
   Incoming spells, attacks, or cataclysms flicker, then overwrite themselves into a “less harmful” version—or self-erasing failure state.
2. **Reality Reinforcement:**
   The Veil stabilizes a chosen region, amplifying laws (mass, momentum, essence density, light, shadow) to grant overwhelming defensive strength.
3. **Refraction Break:**
   A target or area is split into multiple possible motion-paths, with Opalis selecting the most disadvantageous one for the enemy—resulting in forced missteps, misfires, or self-sabotaging outcomes.

### **Philosophy:**

_“Reality already knows what it wants to be—I simply remove its hesitation.”_

# IV. **Limits, Weaknesses, & Cost**

### **Limits**

- Cannot **create** new events—only select between existing possible branches.
- Cannot override another **Emnation-tier will** (though it can distort weaker harmonics).
- Cannot stabilize and destabilize the same space simultaneously.

### **Weaknesses**

- Strong **Law-locked Wellsprings** (pure Basilithe constructs) may resist refraction.
- **Dream-breaking** Wellsprings (Hypnather, Eidolyn) can warp or corrupt intended outcomes.
- **Overmass** (hyper-Petralon entities) can brute-force through the Veil, changing the cost structure.

### **Cost — The Soul-Cost Currency Law**

**Cost Type 1 — Harmonic Divergence:**
Opalis temporarily experiences multiple potential selves, causing disorientation or loss of emotional focus.

**Cost Type 2 — Structural Fatigue:**
The Veil leaves thin fractures in her own internal law-harmonics, requiring meditation or Penance-aligned equilibrium rituals to repair.

---

# V. **Counters & Interactions**

### **Counter Type 1 — Null-Law Suppression:**

Fields that flatten probabilities or erase harmonic variance prevent the Veil from selecting alternate outcomes.

### **Counter Type 2 — Paradox Saturation:**

Abilities that produce _too many concurrent states_ (e.g., hyper-Fractura techniques) overwhelm the Veil, causing it to collapse or shatter.

---

# VI. **Imagery, Sensation, & Language**

### **Visual Signature:**

A thin, luminous-black curtain shimmering with fractured golden glyphs.
Angles distort subtly—shadows lean the wrong way; reflections appear before the original object moves.

### **Sound Cue:**

A soft crystalline hum coupled with the whisper of pages turning in an empty library, concluding with a quiet “snap” when a decision collapses.

### **Sensation:**

- **To Opalis:** Calm certainty, as if viewing the world from just outside its chronology.
- **To others:** A brief vertigo followed by the unsettling feeling that time stuttered around them.
- **To targets:** Pressure behind the eyes, then the sensation of their intended action “failing to load.”

# **BLOODBIND SURGE**

(The Primal Burst)

I. **Source and Concept**
Primary Wellspring: Vorynn Primal
Secondary: Vohrin
Path: Beast-Law
Aether Class: Primal-Kinetic
Core Concept: Draven tears open his Vorynn bloodbinding, igniting raw predatory Aether through every muscle. This awakens a temporary Prime Beast aspect without full regression.

II. **Function and Philosophy**
Function: Buff • High-Risk Burst
Outcomes:

- Strike force triples for 10 seconds.
- Reflexes sharpen to predatory instinct.
- Cold-Law exudes from skin.


Philosophy: “Give the beast your bones.”

III. **Mechanics Spec**
Name: Bloodbind Surge
Type: Buff / Primal Burst
Temperance Requirement: Stage VI Glory
Activation: Declared
Range or Area: Self
Duration per turn: 2 turns
Tags: Primal, Predator, Cold-Law

IV. **Limits, Weaknesses, Cost**
Limits: Extended use risks Prime Beast metamorphosis.
Weaknesses: Heat-Law burns out the surge quickly.
Cost: 70 percent Essence drain per minute.

V. **Counters and Interactions**
Counters: Fire dominance, purity-law cages, emotional null fields.

VI. **Imagery**
Visual: Eyes turn glacier-white; frost forms on scars.
Sound: Deep growl under breath.
Sensation: Bones vibrate like drawn string.

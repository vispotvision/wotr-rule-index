## Chorda Somnii

_(The Dream-Tether Chord)_

### I. Source & Concept

**Source (Wellspring):**

- Dream — subjective time, inner narrative
- Harmonic — structured temporal cadence

**Path:** Spirit → Mind
**Aether Class:** Symphonic Scholar

**Core Concept (Fantasy):**
Serenyra latches a Harmonic thread into a target’s dream-axis, temporarily altering their subjective flow of time. She does not change time itself; she changes how their soul experiences its passage.

---

### II. Function + Philosophy

**Function:**
A [Debuff] / [Buff] temporal perception art.
She twines a silver chord from her fingers to the target’s chest, tuning their sense of “now.” To them, the world may slow into heavy syrup or accelerate into dizzying blur.

**Outcomes:**

- **Slow-field:** Target’s actions feel delayed; to others they move slightly lagged, as their reactions misalign.
- **Haste-field:** Ally experiences sharpened awareness, allowing them to plan and react with uncanny prescience (but not actual time travel).

**Philosophy:**
“Time is a song; I only change the tempo you hear it in.”

---

### III. Mechanics Spec

**Name:** Chorda Somnii
**Type:** [Support] / [Debuff]

**Temperance Requirement:** Stage VI — Illumination

**Activation:**
Declared action, requiring eye contact or Essence-lock. She quietly hums a private melody keyed to the target’s dream-tone.

**Range / Area:**

- Single target within 500 m
- Line-of-sight or strong Essence link

**Duration per turn:**
3 turns standard; may be cut early by Serenity or counter-magic.

**Tags:**
[Dream] [Mind] [Tempo] [Support] [Debuff]

---

### IV. Limits, Weaknesses, & Cost

**Limits**

- Cannot affect entities without coherent dreams (certain constructs, pure Titanscales, Void husks)
- Only one Chorda Somnii effect may be maintained at a time

**Weaknesses**

- Fate-resonant wards can “snap” the chord
- High-Temperance minds can reject the altered tempo through sheer will

**Cost — The Soul-Cost Currency Law**

- **Memory Shear:** Prolonged use erodes her own sense of sequence; she may misremember the order of events that just happened.
- **Sleep Debt:** After heavy use, she must enter deep, Wellspring-guided sleep or risk micro-dream bleed during battle.

---

### V. Counters & Interactions

- **Mind-Shielding Talismans:** Can insulate the wearer’s dream-field.
- **Void Static:** Randomizes perceived time, turning her precise tempo shift into disorienting noise for both target and caster.

---

### VI. Imagery, Sensation, & Language

**Visual Signature:** A thin, silver-violet thread links her hand to the target, visible mostly in peripheral vision; the world around them blurs in concentric ripples.

**Sound Cue:** A distant, slow bell-toll, or a rapid ticking—depending on the effect.

**Sensation:** The target feels out-of-step; footsteps land a half-moment before or after they intend.

# **COAGULA DOMINION**

(The Sealed Air)

I. **Source and Concept**
Primary Wellspring: Coagula
Secondary Wellspring: Judicium
Path: Fate
Aether Class: Binding Core
Core Concept: Drakvor binds the surrounding Essence itself, thickening the air into slow-moving decree.

II. **Function and Philosophy**
Function: Field Debuff • Anti-Caster Control
Outcomes:

- Spell velocity slows.
- Movement resists acceleration.
- Essence around enemies coagulates.


Philosophy: “Motion is privilege. I revoke it.”

III. **Mechanics Spec**
Name: Coagula Dominion
Type: Field / Debuff
Temperance Requirement: Stage VIII Benediction
Activation: Declared
Range or Area: 15 meter radius
Duration per turn: 3 turns
Tags: Binding, Tempo-Reduction, Spell-Distortion

IV. **Limits, Weaknesses, Cost**
Limits: Only affects external Essence, not innate soul-fire.
Weaknesses: High-tier Wellsprings, skyborne or teleportative abilities.
Cost: Pressure fatigue around ribcage.

V. **Counters and Interactions**
Counters: Motion Wellsprings, Spirit-Path high clarity, anti-binding glyphs.

VI. **Imagery, Sensation, Language**
Visual: Air thickens like cooling metal.
Sound: Muffled hum.
Sensation: Breathing feels heavy to all caught.

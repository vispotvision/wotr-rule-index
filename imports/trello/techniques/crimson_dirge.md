# **CRIMSON DIRGE**

(The Hymn That Burns Atonement)

I. **Source and Concept**
Primary Wellspring: Judicium (verdict)
Secondary Wellsprings: Exuroth (purifying flame), Catharsis (release)
Path: Spirit–Fate
Aether Class: Principle Dominion Soul
Core Concept: A resonance hymn that converts guilt, emotion, and memory into annihilative law-tones. Each verse alters one Hermetic Principle inside the affected field.

II. **Function and Philosophy**
Function: Field • Offense • Purification
Outcomes:

- Converts emotional charge into Dirge harmonics.
- Burns matter, guilt, or memory depending on verse.
- Alters one Principle (e.g., Polarity, Rhythm) inside radius.


Philosophy: “Mercy is a flame that erases.”

III. **Mechanics Spec**
Name: Crimson Dirge
Type: Field / Law-Hymn
Temperance Requirement: Stage XIV Zenith
Activation: Ritual-fast
Range or Area: 1,000 meters
Duration per turn: 6 turns
Tags: Principle Alteration, Wellspring Harmonics, Aether Resonation

IV. **Limits, Weaknesses, Cost**
Limits: Only one Principle may be altered per verse.
Weaknesses: Empathy resonance disrupts Dirge frequency.
Cost: 2 percent memory bleed per Principle edit.

V. **Counters and Interactions**
Counters: Void-law seals, Archonic decrees.

VI. **Imagery, Sensation, Language**
Visual: Concentric red sigils rotate behind him.
Sound: Choir-like hum that folds in on itself.
Sensation: Air vibrates with grief and heat.

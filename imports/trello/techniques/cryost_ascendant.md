# **Cryost Ascendant**

_Essence Typology: Body / Mind / Law · Temperance Path: Glory → Reflection → Revelation_
_Subject: **Tovain Zethriel (“The Cryost Ascendant”)**_

---

A psionic–stasis engine that **re-describes motion as balance** and banks all flux (kinetic, thermal, emotional, Aetheric) as **equilibrium charge**. When intention and law align, the class resolves its bank as **one perfect act**—a single step, clause, or cut—while the remainder diffuses as quiet cold. Rather than resisting the world, it **halts** it until only the true outcome remains.

---

## Shell State

- **Nature:** Harmonic–inertial **cryost field**; pressure feels glass-quiet and temperature-falling.
- **Emotion Conductivity:** Detachment and lucid mercy ↑ coherence; agitation introduces shimmer (edge leaks).
- **Purity:** Maximal when serving **one balanced verdict**; degrades if forced to preserve contradictions (motion _and_ stillness).

---

## Geometry / Topology

- **Core Anchor:** Crown → larynx → diaphragm (mind/word/breath) seated to **heels** at finalization.
- **Pattern:** **Sixfold iris** nodes around heart/eyes; field tessellates into a **hexagonal cryonic lattice** (snowglass).
- **Field Shape:** A horizontal **Cryost Bloom** nested in a quiet dome; under high load it narrows to a **Stillpoint Column**.

---

## Laminae (Field Layers)

1. **Equalization Lamina (outer):** Cancels relative variance—sound dulls, heat gradients flatten, panic loses edge.
2. **Stasis Lamina (mid):** Arrests vectors (kinetic/spell/emotion) and stores them as **equilibrium mass**.
3. **Judicium Lamina (inner):** Finalizes the frozen frame into a **single permitted path**; excess discharges as a thin, exact correction.

---

## Essence Behavior

Incoming **change** is rewritten into balance and **banked**; release **never** vents as chaos. The class expresses **Reverberant Stasis**—banked charge echoes back as structure (constructs, halos) and sustains itself (self-repairing lattice). Over-banking risks **Resonance Lock** (self becomes an anchor).

---

## Trait Function

- **Cryostatic Lattice:** Converts motion/heat/clamor into stored charge; precision scales with breath and iris sync.
- **Harmonic Bind:** Orchestrates simultaneous psionic projections (multi-task actions execute in phase).
- **Soul Weave:** Redirects harm into **cryonic constructs** rather than tissue.
- **Choir Rite:** Aligns allies’ Essence tempos for shared stability and maintenance efficiency.
  _(Traits recurse: stillness → clearer measurement → stronger equalization → deeper stillness.)_

- **Load / Balance:** Influx (impact/spell/fear) is **banked**; multi-source threats cancel one another first.

- **Verdict / Release:** One decisive act executes (step/edge/clause); residual charge diffuses as harmless cold.
- **Stillpoint (Apex):** **1–2 m** absolute stasis eye; cognition alone moves; strict timers to avoid lock.

# **EDICT STRIKE**

(The Law-Falling Blade)

I. **Source and Concept**
Primary Wellspring: Judicium
Secondary Wellspring: Coagula
Path: Body
Aether Class: Iron Will Aether
Core Concept: Drakvor imposes judgment through his blade. The more doubt the enemy holds, the heavier his swing becomes.

II. **Function and Philosophy**
Function: Offense • Psychological Debuff
Outcomes:

- Downward strike gains metaphysical mass.
- Hesitation in target doubles impact.
- Armor below B-Tier Temperance buckles.


Philosophy: “Uncertainty is the softest armor.”

III. **Mechanics Spec**
Name: Edict Strike
Type: Offense / Judgment
Temperance Requirement: Stage VIII Benediction
Activation: Declared
Range or Area: Melee
Duration per turn: 1 turn
Tags: Judgment, Willweight, Armor-Shatter

IV. **Limits, Weaknesses, Cost**
Limits: Slow, read-heavy windup.
Weaknesses: Agile or fearless targets.
Cost: Aether strain in wrists and shoulders.

V. **Counters and Interactions**
Counters: Resolve-based Wellsprings, high-speed repositioning, sublimation-type evasions.

VI. **Imagery, Sensation, Language**
Visual: Blade darkens into geometric iron-black facets.
Sound: Verdict-like thud.
Sensation: Air pulls inward before the strike falls.

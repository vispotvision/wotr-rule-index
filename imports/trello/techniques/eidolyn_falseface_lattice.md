# **EIDOLYN FALSEFACE LATTICE**

_(A Fear-Born Illusory Combat Engine)_

**I. Source & Concept**
Source (Wellsprings):

- **Eidolyn** — idol-avatar construction
- **Somnalis** — dream-binding opacity
- **Oneirion** — emotional truth metadata

Aether Class: Dreambreaker Aether

Core Concept:
A lattice of illusions shaped entirely from the opponent’s imagined threat.
If the target believes the illusion can strike, it does — mass and force are borrowed from conviction.

**II. Function + Philosophy**
Function: Offensive & Defensive Illusory Projection
Outcomes:

- Illusions become semi-solid
- Can overwhelm multiple enemies
- Hijacks their fear as kinetic momentum

Philosophy:
“Fear is just belief wearing a mask.”

**III. Mechanics Spec**
Name: Falseface Lattice
Type: Eidolic Illusion
Temperance Requirement: Stage VII Refraction
Activation: Reactive
Range / Area: 80 meters
Duration per turn: 3 turns
Tags: Illusion-Kinetic, Avatar Projection, Fear Resonance

**IV. Limits & Cost**
Limits:

- Loses strength if the target becomes fearless or lucid
- Cannot generate forms the target has no concept of

Costs:

- **Identity Drift**: Prolonged usage blurs his self-image with enemy fears
- **Cognitive Static**: Headache, memory flicker, or perception distortion

**V. Counters**

- Empathy-based Essence breaks fear-loops
- Targets with mental discipline (Letheveil users) resist
- Mirrors disrupt avatar stability

**VI. Imagery & Sensation**
Visual: Shards of translucent geometry forming phantom figures.
Sound: Cracking glass layered with whispers.
Sensation: A cold hand pressing between the shoulder blades.

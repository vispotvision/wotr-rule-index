# **Fluxus Intervallum**

### _(The Flowing Interval)_

---

## **I. Source & Concept**

**Source (Wellspring):** **Wellspring of Strain** — Atla’zon’s Domain.
Governs gravity, spatial tension, and the metaphysical pull that draws Essence toward form.

**Path:** Mind Path
_(Perception and command of invisible laws, not raw power.)_

**Aether Class:** Lattice / Conduit
_(Attuning the psyche to the connective lattice of reality.)_

**Core Concept (Fantasy):**
The user perceives the intricate lines of gravitic and causal strain that bind all masses. By treating these lines as fluid vectors, they may _flow_ across the **intervallum** between two points as if distance were liquid.

---

## **II. Function + Philosophy**

**Function:**
The user targets themself and a second point of mass (person, pillar, stone) in sight. On activation, they dissolve into the telluric line of strain and _flow_ along it, re-coalescing at the destination nearly instantly.

**Movement:**

- High-speed, line-based traversal
- **Not** teleportation (no erasure/remaking)
- True traversal, but at physics-defying velocity

**Advanced Use — Offense:**
The user may “flow” only part of their body (e.g., blade-hand) across a gap to strike from afar.

**Advanced Use — Defense:**
The user may attempt to “flow” an incoming projectile along its own vector, diverting its endpoint.

**Philosophy (Onto-Symbolic Strike):**
A manifestation of the Hermetic Principle of Correspondence (**“As above, so below.”**)
It rejects the idea of empty space, declaring:
**“There is no between—only connection. I move along the weave.”**

## **IV. Limits, Weaknesses, & Cost**

### **Limits**

- Requires **two** anchors of mass
- The flow must be a **perfect line** — cannot curve
- Obstacles intersecting the line halt the flow
- Cannot function in null-gravity or polarity-chaos zones

### **Weaknesses**

- **Stasis Counter:** Fixatio-based abilities anchor the line, making flow impossible
- **Void Counter:** Tenebra and Zar’thuun-based powers erase strain-lines
- **Interdiction:** Concord Bind or Archonic Reprimand fields create harmonic dissonance

### **Cost — The Soul-Cost Currency Law**

**Physical Cost — Gravitic Vertigo:**
On arrival, the user’s Essence and gravity alignment lag behind reality.
Effects:

- Off-balance
- Staggered
- One second of vertiginous disorientation

**Metaphysical Cost — Lattice Fatigue:**
Pulling on Atla’zon’s law creates:

- Resonance fatigue
- Soul Crystal vibration (as if struck like a bell)
- Overuse causes cognitive distortion: seeing only flows, not masses — perceiving people as mere nodes in a web

---

## **V. Counters & Interactions**

**Shattering the Line:**
Resonance Breaker or Concord Severance techniques can rupture the strain-line, forcing the user to reappear mid-flow, staggered.

**Gravitic Interference:**
Enemy gravitic manipulation (e.g., _Gravitic Shear_) can distort the line, causing overshoot or collision via inertial misalignment.

---

## **VI. Imagery, Sensation, & Language**

**Visual Signature:**
The user’s body attenuates like heated glass, pulling into a lucid thread of indigo and auric light.
The thread lances the interval and reconstitutes into flesh at the endpoint.

**Sound Cue:**
A sharp sibilant hiss — inhaled wind — followed by a resonant Nexus-thrum, like reality’s seams being plucked.

**Sensation:**
For the user: a discorporating lurch of omnidirectional falling.
For observers: a shift in air pressure and a phantom tilt of the world, as if the room briefly changed its axis.

# **HUNTER’S BREATH**

(The Frost That Hides the Living)

I. **Source and Concept**
Primary Wellspring: Vohrin
Secondary: Tenebra
Path: Stealth-Frost
Aether Class: Primal-Kinetic
Core Concept: Draven inhales the cold and exhales a mist that erases heat, scent, and Essence trace. In freezing domains it becomes perfect invisibility against magical perception.

II. **Function and Philosophy**
Function: Utility • Stealth • Anti-lock
Outcomes:

- Masks every biological signal.
- Dissolves Essence markers.
- Creates localized Tenebra fog.


Philosophy: “Breathe like the winter, and nothing finds you.”

III. **Mechanics Spec**
Name: Hunter’s Breath
Type: Stealth / Obfuscation
Temperance Requirement: Stage VI Glory
Activation: Declared
Range or Area: 15 meter cloud
Duration per turn: 6 turns
Tags: Frost Mist, Essence Mask, Predator Stealth

IV. **Limits, Weaknesses, Cost**
Limits: Requires cold air.
Weaknesses: Warm or radiant domains.
Cost: Lung strain.

V. **Counters and Interactions**
Counters: Thermal inversion fields, spiritual chorus sight.

VI. **Imagery**
Visual: Mist curls low, clinging to ground.
Sound: Quiet, slow exhale.
Sensation: Heat leaves the skin.

# **HYPNATHER OBLIVION-STEP**

_(Half-Sleep Teleportation)_

## **I. Source & Concept**

Source (Wellsprings):

- **Hypnather** — half-sleep threshold
- **Oneirion** — dream-entry
- **Letheveil (minor)** — memory numbing

Path: Spirit Path
Aether Class: Dreambreaker Aether

Core Concept:
Aeldoris steps out of the waking world by briefly entering sleep, reappearing from a nearby person’s dream-shadow.

## **II. Function + Philosophy**

Function: Mobility / Surprise Attack / Reality-Slip
Outcomes:

- Teleports silently
- Gains instant positional advantage
- Evades high-speed projectiles
- Can arrive behind a fear-spike in an opponent

Philosophy:
“The quickest path is through the moment you almost fell asleep.”

## **III. Mechanics Spec**

Name: Oblivion-Step
Type: Sleep Teleport
Temperance Requirement: Stage VII Refraction
Activation: Declared
Range: 0–50 meters
Duration per turn: 0 (instant action)
Tags: Sleep, Teleport, Shadow, Dream

## **IV. Limits & Cost**

Limits:

- Must anchor to a nearby subconscious
- Requires a “dream shadow” to emerge from

Weaknesses:

- Light-based domains erase dream shadows
- High-speed kinetic attacks can still anticipate arrival

Cost:

- **Post-Step Disorientation**: 0.3 seconds
- **Memory Backwash**: Gains flashes of anchor’s emotions

## **V. Counters**

- High Spirit Resistance
- Lucid-state training
- Anti-shadow sigils

## **VI. Imagery**

Visual: Eyelids droop; he vanishes into a soft blur and steps out from someone’s silhouette.
Sound: A single inhalation.
Sensation: A cold blink, then relocation.

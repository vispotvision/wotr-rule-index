# **JUDGMENT MANIFEST**

(The Living Law)

I. **Source and Concept**
Primary Wellspring: Judicium
Secondary: Exuroth
Path: Fate
Aether Class: Dominion Soul
Core Concept: Francis summons avatars of Hermetic Principles as autonomous forms. Cause becomes a chain. Rhythm becomes a scythe. Correspondence becomes mirrored duplicates.

II. **Function and Philosophy**
Function: Summon • Control • Field Enforcement
Outcomes:

- Animates Principles as combat constructs.
- Constructs enforce their own metaphysical rule.
- Field becomes layered with law effects.


Philosophy: “If law cannot listen, it must walk.”

III. **Mechanics Spec**
Name: Judgment Manifest
Type: Summon / Principle Avatar
Temperance Requirement: Stage XIV Zenith
Activation: Declared
Range or Area: 2 km
Duration per turn: 5 turns
Tags: Principle, Avatar, Summon, Enforcement

IV. **Limits, Weaknesses, Cost**
Limits: Only three Principles can be embodied at once.
Weaknesses: Chaos Wellsprings, disruption of conceptual clarity.
Cost: mild paradox fatigue, Aether recoil.

V. **Counters and Interactions**
Counters: Dream-fields, memory distortion magic.

VI. **Imagery, Sensation, Language**
Visual: Giant law-glyph shadows rise from crimson light.
Sound: Hammer on distant stone.
Sensation: Pressure on the chest as if judged.

_(“The Crowned Illusion of Chosen Reality”)_

---

# I. **Source & Concept**

### **Source (Wellspring):**

**Primary Wellsprings:**

- **Oneirion** — dream-logic, mutable concept, metamorphic perception
- **Fractura** — refraction, mirrored possibilities, split states
- **Tenebra** — shadow-equilibrium, unseen structure, the quiet truth beneath

**Secondary Wellsprings:**

- **Basilithe** — law geometry stabilizing the illusion into a coherent “false truth”
- **Orrenthal** — harmonic resonance embedding illusion into mind and environment
- **Dissolution** — to blur or erase real sensory anchors
- **Tarturon** — accelerated decay of unwanted perceptions
- **Cataclysm** — forceful rupture of target certainty
- **Penance** — purifying consequence applied to delusion
- **Petralon** — grounding illusion so it feels physically real

### **Path:**

**Temperance Path XII — Emnation, Self as Law & Lens**

### **Aether Class:**

**Reality-Harmonic Illusory Refraction**

### **Core Concept (Fantasy):**

Opalis folds the world into a **palace of refracted possibilities**, where perception itself becomes a law she decides. The technique doesn’t create constructs—it **reshapes what others perceive as real**, collapsing incorrect interpretations and elevating a chosen illusion into a lawlike sensory truth.
Targets cannot tell whether they stand in the world they knew or one of its discarded alternatives.

---

# II. **Function + Philosophy**

### **Function:**

A **[Control] / [Debuff] / [Field] / [Illusion]** art.
The technique overlays a refracted reality upon a chosen area and forces opponents to perceive the version of that reality Opalis chooses—while stripping away or erasing the versions she rejects.

### **Outcomes:**

1. **Mind-State Overwrite:**
   Targets see, hear, and feel an environment that never existed. Terrain changes, dangers appear or vanish, and time seems to misalign.
2. **False Motion / False Causality:**
   Opalis can make enemies believe they moved, attacked, or succeeded even when they didn’t—or vice versa.
3. **Cataclysmic Clarity Break:**
   When the illusion shatters, the sudden transition collapses enemy equilibrium, inflicting vertigo, harmonic disarray, or blindness of concept.

### **Philosophy:**

_“The world is only what we agree to see; I simply choose first.”_

# IV. **Limits, Weaknesses, & Cost**

### **Limits**

- Cannot force targets to act against their core will—only misinterpret their surroundings.
- Cannot alter physical reality, only its perceived structure.
- Cannot overlay two contradictory illusions at full strength in the same space.

### **Weaknesses**

- **Pure Basilithe beings** (law-aligned entities) detect inconsistencies sooner.
- **Emotionless constructs** resist conceptual misdirection.
- **Eidolyn**, **Hypnather**, or **pure Oneirion techniques** can corrupt or counter-map the Palace.

### **Cost — The Soul-Cost Currency Law**

**Cost Type 1 — Cognitive Fracture:**
Fragments of Opalis’ own memories bleed into illusions, causing temporary déjà vu or losing the sense of which variant of reality she personally occupied.

**Cost Type 2 — Harmonic Echo Burn:**
Orrenthal and Fractura interactions strain her harmonic center, causing migraines, sensory afterimages, or temporary shadow-blindness.

---

# V. **Counters & Interactions**

### **Counter Type 1 — Sensory Nullification / Overload:**

Techniques that remove or overwhelm senses cut through the Palace’s layering.

### **Counter Type 2 — Multi-Reality Overlap:**

Fractura-heavy casters can deliberately create additional false interpretations, destabilizing the illusion by cluttering its available logical “slots.”

# VI. **Imagery, Sensation, & Language**

### **Visual Signature:**

Golden-black fractures spread through the air like cracks in glass, then widen into mirrored corridors of light and shadow.
Reflections appear before sources.
Shadows fall upward or sideways.
Contours ripple like the surface of a dream-text.

### **Sound Cue:**

Soft choral harmonics layered with a low, pulsing hum—like a heartbeat heard from underwater.
A faint _chime_ echoes whenever perception shifts.

### **Sensation:**

- **To Opalis:** A serene certainty, as if she holds the “editor’s pen” of reality.
- **To those within:** Warmth at first, like a sunrise. Then vertigo as their senses “slide” into a new alignment.
- **To enemies:** A crushing weight behind the eyes, the uncanny sense of seeing themselves move before they actually move.

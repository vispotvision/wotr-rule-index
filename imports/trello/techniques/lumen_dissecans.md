## Lumen Dissecans

_(The Dissecting Light)_

### I. Source & Concept

**Source (Wellspring):**

- Harmonic — decomposition into component tones
- Celestial — analytic radiance

**Path:** Mind
**Aether Class:** Symphonic Scholar

**Core Concept (Fantasy):**
Serenyra focuses a narrow beam of analytical light that “dissects” an active spell, separating its structural components and temporarily suspending them as readable glyphs.

---

### II. Function + Philosophy

**Function:**
A [Dispel] / [Analysis] art.
She targets an ongoing effect—barrier, curse, ward—and exposes its lattice. Once exposed, she can choose to unravel it, alter it, or learn from it.

**Outcomes:**

- Partial or full dispelling of ongoing spells
- Temporary suspension of harmful effects
- Immediate archival into Thesauriel as new reference data

**Philosophy:**
“Once you see the seams, you cannot pretend the garment is seamless.”

---

### III. Mechanics Spec

**Name:** Lumen Dissecans
**Type:** [Dispel] / [Utility]

**Temperance Requirement:** Stage VI — Illumination

**Activation:**
Declared; she draws a sigil over one eye, which then shines a thin violet beam.

**Range / Area:**

- Effects within 500 m
- One target effect per activation

**Duration per turn:**
The dissection lasts 1–2 turns; the target spell is vulnerable during this window.

**Tags:**
[Harmonic] [Celestial] [Dispel] [Utility]

---

### IV. Limits, Weaknesses, & Cost

**Limits**

- Cannot affect instant, one-off spells that have already resolved
- Strong divine Edicts may resist full dissection, revealing only partial structure

**Weaknesses**

- Attempting to dissect Void constructs risks contamination
- Time-based spells can slip out of phase before she finishes

**Cost — The Soul-Cost Currency Law**

- **Insight Burn:** The act of seeing so deeply into magic leaves afterimages in her mind—she may temporarily see “lattices” over everyday objects.
- **Aether Fatigue:** Each full dissection requires a measurable chunk of her baseline 22 AU/s output.

---

### V. Counters & Interactions

- **Obfuscation Runes:** Specifically-designed glyphs that scramble or falsify the lattice.
- **Reflective Barriers:** Might redirect the beam, forcing her to stop or risk self-dissection.

---

### VI. Imagery, Sensation, & Language

**Visual Signature:** A pencil-thin violet-white ray from her eye; the targeted spell blooms into frozen geometric filigree hovering in midair.

**Sound Cue:** A faint, glass-scraping whisper, like someone scoring lines into crystal.

**Sensation:** For Serenyra, a rush of understanding paired with the ache of overfilled memory.

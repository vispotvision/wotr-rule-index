# **MAW OF CRYSTALLINE STASIS**

(The Winter-Heart Cage)

I. **Source and Concept**
Primary Wellspring: Vohrin
Secondary Wellspring: Cinerion
Path: Cold-Law Invocation
Aether Class: Primal-Kinetic Aether
Core Concept: Draven borrows a breath of Titan Vohrin’s absolute zero. The field turns moisture, breath, and Essence vapor into crystalline structures that slow or shatter.

II. **Function and Philosophy**
Function: Field • Disable • Cold-Law Enforcement
Outcomes:

- Encases targets in fractal frost.
- Slows Essence flow.
- Causes brittle-body shattering on impact.


Philosophy: “Stillness is the last lesson.”

III. **Mechanics Spec**
Name: Maw of Crystalline Stasis
Type: Field / Frost-Law
Temperance Requirement: Stage VI Glory
Activation: Declared
Range or Area: 6 meters
Duration per turn: 3 turns
Tags: Absolute Zero, Essence Slow, Cold Cage

IV. **Limits, Weaknesses, Cost**
Limits: Risks freezing Draven’s joints.
Weaknesses: Thermal flux, radiant heat blasts.
Cost: Joint frostbite at high output.

V. **Counters and Interactions**
Counters: Pyreveil Artes, Aetheric overheat fields.

VI. **Imagery**

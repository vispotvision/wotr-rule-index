# **MIREGLASS SUFFUSION**

_(Shadow / Memory / Debuff / Illusion)_

## **I. Source & Concept**

**Source:**
**Nyxial** — voided shadow, reflective nothingness.
**Anamnesis** — memory echo, recollection.

**Path:** Spirit
**Aether Class:** Illusory–Conceptual Distortion

**Core Concept:**
Flood the battlefield with “mireglass,” a shadow-fluid that traps fragments of memory and replays distorted reflections to disorient enemies. They see their own past failures, fears, or heartbreaks turned into attacking silhouettes.

---

## **II. Function + Philosophy**

**Function:** Debuff / Illusion / Emotional Disruption
**Outcomes:**

- Enemies suffer reduced clarity and slower reaction.
- Illusory reflections strike them with emotional shock.
- Weakens mental fortitude, causing mistakes in spellcasting.

**Philosophy:**
“Shadow remembers what we bury.”

---

## **III. Mechanics Spec**

**Name:** Mireglass Suffusion
**Type:** Area Debuff Field
**Temperance Requirement:** Stage VI — Glory
**Activation:** Declared
**Range / Area:** 12-15 meters
**Duration per turn:** 4 turns
**Tags:** [Illusion] [Debuff] [Memory] [Shadow]

---

## **IV. Limits, Weaknesses & Cost**

**Limits:**

- Ineffective on targets with empty or sealed memories.
- Strong-willed opponents can break free quicker.

**Weaknesses:**

- Absolution and Luminalis abilities dissolve the mireglass.
- Empaths can anchor their allies.

**Cost:**
Caster temporarily experiences fragments of their own buried memories.

---

## **V. Counters & Interactions**

- Judicium truth fields dispel the reflections.
- Contrition-based mental vows resist emotional intrusion.
- Abyssal spirits feed on mireglass distortion and strengthen.

---

## **VI. Imagery, Sensation, & Language**

**Visual:** Glossy black water pooling across the arena, reflecting memories instead of surroundings.
**Sound:** Distant whispers, echoing footsteps.
**Sensation:** Damp cold around the ankles, a tug at the inner sternum.

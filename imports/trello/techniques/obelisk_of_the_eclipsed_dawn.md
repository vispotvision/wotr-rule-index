## **I. Source & Concept**

### **Source (Wellspring):**

**Tarturon — entropy’s hunger**
Dissolves form, strips Essence to its unbound state.

**Basilithe — cosmic foundation**
Crystalline order, structural permanence, metaphysical geometry.

**Petralon — material certainty**
Weight, stone, density, and the grounding of reality.

**Oneirion — dream-shaping**
Memory, imagination, and conceptual form.

**Tenebra — shadow’s stillness**
Absorption, concealment, nullification of excess radiance.

**Fractura — prismatic refraction**
Division, replication, mirrored echoes, harmonic fragmentation.

---

### **Path:**

**Ecliptic Harmonics / Duality of Ruin & Renewal**

### **Aether Class:**

**Emanative Construct-Weaving**

---

### **Core Concept (Fantasy):**

The user summons a towering, semi-sentient prism—an obelisk woven from entropy, dream, shadow, matter, and fractal light.
It can destroy through dissolution or defend through crystalline stillness, shifting its role by reading the caster’s intent.
It is not cast like magic; it is **emanated**—a projection of the caster’s Emnational self made into living architecture.

---

## **II. Function + Philosophy**

### **Function:**

A **[Dual Offense/Defense]** **Field Construct** that manifests a colossal Obelisk which adapts to the user’s intent—dissolving threats, stabilizing space, shielding allies, shaping matter, or refracting energy.
The Obelisk serves as an autonomous Wellspring convergence engine.

### **Outcomes:**

- **Fracture Cascade**


Entropy-laden waves erupt from the Obelisk, dissolving Essence constructs, curses, barriers, or corrupted entities.

- **Ecliptic Aegis**


Concentric crystalline shells form around allies or an area, absorbing, redirecting, or nullifying incoming force.

- **Oneiric Reversal**


The Obelisk treats damaged Essence as “unfinished dreams,” rewriting it to a healthier or intended state.

---

### **Philosophy:**

**“All endings are only matter remembering how to begin again.”**

## **IV. Limits, Weaknesses, & Cost**

### **Limits**

- **Cannot create or restore life.**


It may restore Essence-state but cannot resurrect or generate consciousness.

- **Cannot override Emnational-tier laws.**


It cannot undo another Emnation user’s absolute decree—only contest or deflect it.

- **Fractura echoes cannot infinitely chain.**


Only **4** lesser Obelisks may exist simultaneously; more causes harmonic collapse.

---

### **Weaknesses**

- **Emotional Disharmony:**


Any internal conflict warps the Obelisk’s geometry, risking inversion (collapsing inward).

- **Anti-Dream Wellsprings:**


Entities attuned to **Dissolution** or **Cataclysm** can partially bypass Oneiric and Petralon functions.

- **High-Order Light Constructs**


Pure radiance users can overwhelm Tenebra layers and crack the shadow-shell.

---

### **Cost — The Soul-Cost Currency Law**

- **Reflective Burden:**


The Obelisk records the caster’s recent emotions.
These echo back as psychic feedback, forcing them to confront unprocessed memories.

- **Structural Strain:**


Prolonged manifestation creates micro-fractures in the user’s Temperance layers, requiring periods of Essence stillness to realign.

---

## **V. Counters & Interactions**

- **Counter: Pure Entropy Saturation**


Raw Tarturon or Dissolution fields can erode the Obelisk’s outer layers, forcing early collapse.

- **Counter: Lawbreaker Techniques (High-Tier Tenebra or Temporal Wellsprings)**


Temporal distortion or reality-nullification can sever the Obelisk’s stabilizing foundation.

- **Interaction: Basilithe Users**


Their constructs resonate perfectly with the Obelisk, forming powerful overlapping geometries.

- **Anti-Synergy: Chaotic Dream Magic**


Unstable Oneirion distortions interfere with the Obelisk’s Oneiric Reversal, causing dream-feedback.

## **VI. Imagery, Sensation, & Language**

### **Visual Signature:**

A towering prism of black stone and luminous fractures.
Its sides reflect multiple timelines of light and shadow simultaneously.
Floating shards orbit it—mirror-like, basalt-like, crystalline.
The horizon bends slightly as if the air warps around a gravity well.

---

### **Sound Cue:**

A six-note harmonic chord: glass tones, low thunder, and soft subsonic humming.
When it shifts modes, the chord inverts, producing a ripple like shattering crystal played in reverse.

---

### **Sensation:**

**To the caster:**
A feeling of endless height rising from their spine, pressure behind the sternum, and the sense of their will extending into the sky.

**To the target:**
Either crushing gravity (Tarturon/Petralon) or weightless calm (Basilithe/Oneirion), depending on intent.
A faint coldness, as if standing in the shadow of something infinitely tall.

# **ONEIRON PARASITE — NIGHTMARE WARDEN**

_(Dream-Form Infiltration)_

## **I. Source & Concept**

Source (Wellsprings):

- **Oneirion** — dream-body creation
- **Hypnather** — sleep-binding anchor
- **Eidolyn** — fear-avatar synthesis

Path: Spirit Path
Aether Class: Dreambreaker Aether

Core Concept:
Aeldoris implants a small dream-creature into the target’s subconscious. It surveils, distorts, and punishes from within.

## **II. Function + Philosophy**

Function: Tracker / Internal Disruptor / Fear Catalyst
Outcomes:

- Monitors emotional spikes
- Causes micro-nightmares
- Weakens Soul Crystal layering
- Can detonate into a psychic stagger

Philosophy:
“The smallest nightmare is the most loyal.”

## **III. Mechanics Spec**

Name: Nightmare Warden
Type: Subconscious Parasite
Temperance Requirement: Stage VII Refraction
Activation: Declared (gaze or touch)
Range: 30 meters
Duration per turn: 4 turns
Tags: Parasite, Fear, Dream, Subconscious

## **IV. Limits & Cost**

Limits:

- Cannot infect beings without subconscious
- Host emotions influence parasite form

Weaknesses:

- Strong mental shields reject implantation
- Mortis Essence can dissolve the Warden corpus

Cost:

- **Dream Wound**: Psychic pain if the Warden is destroyed
- **Personality Imprint**: Risk of adopting host psychological traits

## **V. Counters & Interactions**

- Memory-cleansing rites purge the parasite
- Shared-dream rituals expose its presence
- Empathic Wellsprings overpower its authority

## **VI. Imagery & Sensation**

Visual: A small shadow-creature crawls across the floor and vanishes into the target’s silhouette.
Sound: Clicking mandibles, barely audible.
Sensation: A crawling itch beneath the heart.

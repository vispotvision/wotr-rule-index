# **ONEIRON PHANTASM COURT**

_(The Dream-Self Tribunal)_

**I. Source & Concept**
Source (Wellsprings):

- **Oneirion** — subconscious truth made manifest
- **Eidolyn** — avatar-crafting from memory-echo
- **Hypnather** — sleep-layer descent

Path: **Spirit → Attraction Drift**
Aether Class: **Dreambreaker Aether**

Core Concept:
Aeldoris summons the target’s dream-self behind them — the version of themselves shaped by guilt, repression, and subconscious truth. This “Phantasm Court” forces the psyche to confess through emotional resonance.

**II. Function + Philosophy**
Function: Psychic Offense, Essence Destabilization
Outcomes:

- Exposes suppressed motives
- Weakens Soul Crystal coherence
- Forces subconscious truths into waking consciousness

Philosophy:
“Everyone dreams their own verdict.”

**III. Mechanics Spec**
Name: Oneiron Phantasm Court
Type: Subconscious Manifestation
Temperance Requirement: **Stage VII Refraction**
Activation: Declared
Range / Area: 0–140 meters (Dream Projection Radius)
Duration per turn: 2 turns
Tags: Psychic, Illusion, Essence-Reading, Subconscious Overwrite

**IV. Limits & Cost**
Limits:

- Only functions on beings with an active subconscious
- Confession feedback can destabilize Aeldoris if mirrored

Costs:

- **Identity Bleed**: Takes on fragments of the target’s emotional residue
- **Dream Echo Fatigue**: Affects his short-term memory

**V. Counters & Interactions**

- Targets with no guilt/inner contradiction resist the Court
- Verdantia or Mortis Wellsprings reduce its potency
- Memory-null fields break the dream-self projection

**VI. Imagery & Sensation**
Visual: A spectral version of the target emerges behind them, its face blurred, its mouth whispering truths.
Sound: Soft, rhythmic breath—like someone sleeping nearby.
Sensation: Tension behind the skull; sense of being judged by your own shadow.

# **PYREWIND BREAKER**

_(Offensive / Fire / Cataclysm / AoE)_

## **I. Source & Concept**

**Source:**
**Exuroth** — catastrophic flame, consuming hunger.
**Cataclysm** — destructive release, eruption.

**Path:** Aether + Body
**Aether Class:** Volatile Kinetic-Flame Hybrid

**Core Concept:**
The caster summons a spiraling gale of fire that detonates outward in a shockwave, both burning and concussing everything caught inside.

---

## **II. Function + Philosophy**

**Function:** Offensive AoE
**Outcomes:**

- Massive fire damage in a 20-meter radius.
- Blunt shockwave knocks enemies back or down.
- Overloads weak shields instantly.

**Philosophy:**
“To burn is to awaken the air.”

---

## **III. Mechanics Spec**

**Name:** Pyrewind Breaker
**Type:** AoE Invocation
**Temperance Requirement:** Stage VI — Glory
**Activation:** Declared / Chant
**Range / Area:** 20-meter radius
**Duration per turn:** 1 turn (instant burst)
**Tags:** [Fire] [AoE] [Shockwave]

---

## **IV. Limits, Weaknesses & Cost**

- Cannot be controlled once released.
- Verdantia wards muffle the flame’s spread.

**Cost:**
High Aether burn; risk of internal overheating.

---

## **V. Counters & Interactions**

- Water-based Wellsprings (Thaloré) blunt the blast.
- Abyssal entities absorb fire but not the shockwave.
- Cinerion rust-barriers redirect the force.

---

## **VI. Imagery, Sensation & Language**

**Visual:** A vortex of white-orange flame coiling like a living serpent before bursting.
**Sound:** A tearing roar.
**Sensation:** Hair-raising heat, chest-pressure, metal-in-the-air taste.

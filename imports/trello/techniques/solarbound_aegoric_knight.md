# **Solarbound Aegoric Knight**

_(Essence Typology: Body / Faith / Burden — Temperance Path: Resolution → Benediction → Judicium)_

---

A covenanted anchor-class that **bears** light the way Atlas bears law: not as a flare, but as **weight** accepted and held. Varnath’s Aether forms disciplined arcs governed by **strain, oath, and continuity**; power is stored until will and conviction align, then resolved as a single, **irreversible** act (cut, vow, strike). This maps to WOTR’s First Triad—Aether as medium, Essence as current, Attraction as binding law.

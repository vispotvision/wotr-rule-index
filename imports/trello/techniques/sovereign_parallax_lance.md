# **SOVEREIGN PARALLAX LANCE**

_(“The Strike That Chooses the Wound”)_

---

# I. **Source & Concept**

### **Source (Wellspring):**

**Primary Wellsprings:**

- **Fractura** — refraction; splitting and choosing possible harm-states
- **Cataclysm** — violent rupture; the moment reality breaks
- **Dissolution** — unmaking of form, return to null

**Secondary Wellsprings:**

- **Basilithe** — law-binding impact and trajectory
- **Orrenthal** — harmonic resonance amplifying destructive coherence
- **Petralon** — density and mass anchoring the strike
- **Tenebra** — shadow equilibrium that erases resistance
- **Tarturon** — entropy acceleration
- **Penance** — damage inflicted as purifying consequence
- **Oneirion** — rewriting conceptual structure of the wound

### **Path:**

**Temperance XII — Emnation, Self-as-Law**

### **Aether Class:**

**Harmonic Cataclysmic Refraction Art**

### **Core Concept (Fantasy):**

Opalis condenses all contradictory vectors of destruction into a single **lance of refracted lawlight**, forged from the collision of possible wounds. She does not simply strike her target—she **chooses which version of the target’s defeat becomes real**, and forces that outcome to manifest.
The attack feels like being pierced by all potential futures of one’s harm at once, then having the most devastating one crystallize into truth.

---

# II. **Function + Philosophy**

### **Function:**

A pure **[Offense]** / **[Control]** destructive art.
The Lance is thrust, fired, or gestured toward a target; as it travels, reality fractures around it, aligning into an unavoidable destructive line.

### **Outcomes:**

1. **Fractured Harm-State Selection:**
   The Lance chooses from innumerable potential wounds—burning, crushing, dissolving, rending—and enforces the one that maximizes effect.
2. **Reality-Pinning Impalement:**
   Targets are “fixed” into the wound’s chosen state; escape abilities often fail because their successful outcomes are removed from possibility.
3. **Cataclysmic Echo Damage:**
   When the Lance resolves, shockwaves of refraction tear through nearby space, damaging constructs, illusions, or unstable harmonics.

### **Philosophy:**

_“A wound is merely a decision—and I make it.”_

# IV. **Limits, Weaknesses, & Cost**

### **Limits**

- Cannot rewrite or override the Temperance-based inviolable aspects of another Emanant being.
- Cannot produce multiple simultaneous Lances without dramatically escalating cost.
- Cannot force a harm-state that contradicts the target’s fundamental structure (e.g., “plant-like damage” on a pure shadow-entity).

### **Weaknesses**

- **Pure Basilithe fortresses** resist harm-state selection by rejecting contradictions.
- **Deep Oneirion constructs** can absorb the Lance by replacing their “future wound” with a dream-state.
- **Verdantia** or regenerative Wellsprings dampen dissolution-based results.

### **Cost — The Soul-Cost Currency Law**

**Cost Type 1 — Refractive Overburn:**
Opalis momentarily experiences the pain of the selected wound herself—echoed, not physically inflicted.

**Cost Type 2 — Harmonic Dissonance Scar:**
Fractura + Cataclysm interactions shear her harmonic center, causing temporary imbalance in perception, light sensitivity, or short-term loss of equilibrium.

---

# V. **Counters & Interactions**

### **Counter Type 1 — Predictive Null-Paths:**

Abilities that erase or flatten probability (like time-nullification or fate-denial) prevent the Lance from choosing a harm-state.

### **Counter Type 2 — Conceptual Dissolution:**

Targets that willingly dissolve themselves or shed identity cannot have a “wound-version” chosen—they have no stable narrative thread for the Lance to bind.

# VI. **Imagery, Sensation, & Language**

### **Visual Signature:**

A thin spear of black-gold refracted light, like a ray passing through a shattered prism. Space behind it folds, angles twist, and all colors briefly invert.
When it hits, the wound blooms like a crack in stained glass.

### **Sound Cue:**

A rising harmonic whistle layered with bass rumble.
Upon impact: a sharp crystalline _snap_ followed by a distant thunderclap.

### **Sensation:**

- **To Opalis:** A brief sense of absolute clarity—she feels every possible outcome, then chooses one with total calm.
- **To allies nearby:** A wave of pressure and vertigo, like gravity shifting sideways.
- **To targets:** A crushing cold, then blinding heat, then a sudden collapse of all sensory coherence as the chosen wound manifests.

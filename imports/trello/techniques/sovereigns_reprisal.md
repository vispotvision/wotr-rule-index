# **SOVEREIGN’S REPRISAL**

(The Crowned Rebound)

I. **Source and Concept**
Primary Wellspring: Judicium
Secondary Wellspring: Cinerion (Ash Cycle)
Path: Body
Aether Class: Pressure Accumulator
Core Concept: Drakvor absorbs blunt trauma into his harness then releases it as a ring of rupturing Aether.

II. **Function and Philosophy**
Function: Counter • Area Knockback
Outcomes:

- Stores incoming force as internal pressure.
- Releases shockwave when threshold reached.
- Enemies stagger and lose footing.


Philosophy: “Impact is tribute.”

III. **Mechanics Spec**
Name: Sovereign’s Reprisal
Type: Counter / Aether Burst
Temperance Requirement: Stage VIII Benediction
Activation: Reactive
Range or Area: 8 meter radius
Duration per turn: Instant
Tags: Shockwave, Pressure-Release, Anti-Swarm

Weaknesses: Piercing damage, ranged harassment.
Cost: Spinal Aether recoil, brief vertigo.

V. **Counters and Interactions**
Counters: High-precision strikes, distance fighters.

Sensation: Aether erupts across the ground like a pulse.

# **The Aether Bastion**

_(Judicium • Sublimatio — Verdict + Purity)_

## **I. Source & Concept**

**Source (Wellspring):**
Judicium — the tone of truth, verdict, absolute equilibrium.
Sublimatio — ascension, clarity, refinement of intent.

**Path:** Spirit + Aether
**Aether Class:** Convective Shield-Type

**Core Concept:**
A ward that solidifies truth into form. It manifests as a crystalline barrier that judges every incoming force, allowing only what aligns with the caster’s Essence to pass. Everything else is arrested, purified, or annihilated.

---

## **II. Function + Philosophy**

**Function:** Defense / Purification Ward
**Outcomes:**

- Stops metaphysical or physical projectiles.
- Purifies corrupted Essence on contact.
- Reflects attacks that violate Judicium (dishonest, treacherous, chaotic).

**Philosophy:**
“Only the true may pass.”

---

## **III. Mechanics Spec**

**Name:** Aether Bastion
**Type:** Static Ward
**Temperance Requirement:** Stage VI — Glory
**Activation:** Declared
**Range / Area:** 3–7 meters around caster
**Duration per turn:** 3 turns
**Tags:** [Ward] [Purity] [Reflective] [Static]

---

## **IV. Limits, Weaknesses & Cost**

- Cannot block purely emotional attacks (fear, despair).
- Shatters instantly if the caster lies or betrays intent mid-use.
- High Aether fatigue.

**Cost (Soul-Cost Currency Law):**
Moderate Essence drain, minor Soul Crystal tremor.

---

## **V. Counters & Interactions**

- Tenebra-aligned attacks slip through cracks.
- Memory-based illusions bypass the barrier entirely.
- Overwhelming kinetic force can fracture it.

---

## **VI. Imagery, Sensation, Language**

**Visual:** Glass-like mandala wall with rotating Parun glyphs of Ur + Ma + Ci.
**Sound:** A low, ringing judicial hum.
**Sensation:** A tightening of the inner ribs, like inhaling cold verdict.

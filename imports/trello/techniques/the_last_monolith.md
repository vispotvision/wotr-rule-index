# **THE LAST MONOLITH**

(The Crowned Unbreaking)

I. **Source and Concept**
Primary Wellspring: Coagula
Secondary Wellspring: Judicium
Path: Body–Spirit
Aether Class: Immutable Aether
Core Concept: Drakvor becomes the immovable monolith of legend. Body hardens. Essence congeals. Aether density climbs until all blows strike stone rather than flesh.

II. **Function and Philosophy**
Function: Defense • Durability Surge
Outcomes:

- Skin hardens to ironlike density.
- Essence flow stabilizes.
- Incoming damage drastically reduced.


Philosophy: “Let the world strike. I remain.”

III. **Mechanics Spec**
Name: The Last Monolith
Type: Defense / Durability State
Temperance Requirement: Stage VIII Benediction
Activation: Declared
Range or Area: Self
Duration per turn: 5 turns
Tags: Ironhide, Coagula Shell, Authority Density

IV. **Limits, Weaknesses, Cost**
Limits: Mobility reduced.
Weaknesses: Sublimatio, corrosion or entropy-type Wellsprings.
Cost: Post-state stiffness, muscle tearing at release.

V. **Counters and Interactions**
Counters: Entropy fields, dissolution magics.

VI. **Imagery, Sensation, Language**
Visual: Flesh gains glossy, stone-black sheen.
Sound: Low mountain hum.
Sensation: Every limb feels anchored to the world’s spine.

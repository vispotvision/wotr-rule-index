# **Veil of Verdant Pact**

_(Verdantia • Materia Primordia — Growth + Origin Matter)_

## **I. Source & Concept**

**Source (Wellspring):**
Verdantia — life, bloom, renewal.
Materia Primordia — primal matter, foundational creation.

**Path:** Essence + Fate
**Aether Class:** Vital Shelter

**Core Concept:**
A living ward woven from primal flora and ancient material Essence. It shields the caster and allies, healing minor wounds and absorbing elemental harm by converting it into thickened growth.

---

## **II. Function + Philosophy**

**Function:** Defensive + Regenerative Ward
**Outcomes:**

- Absorbs fire, lightning, frost, and toxin (converts to regrowth).
- Slightly regenerates Soul Crystal surface cracks.
- Shields allies behind a living dome.

**Philosophy:**
“Life answers life.”

---

## **III. Mechanics Spec**

**Name:** Veil of Verdant Pact
**Type:** Living Ward
**Temperance Requirement:** Stage V — Splintering
**Activation:** Declared
**Range / Area:** 5-meter dome
**Duration per turn:** 4 turns
**Tags:** [Ward] [Healing] [Elemental Absorption] [Nature]

---

## **IV. Limits, Weaknesses & Cost**

- Weak against anti-nature Wellsprings (Exuroth, Nihiloth).
- Primal matter can be burned out if overloaded.
- Slow to form (1-second delay).

**Cost:**
Drains emotional stability; caster feels the pain absorbed by the ward.

---

## **V. Counters & Interactions**

- Flames of Absolution bypass it.
- Tenebra shadows decay the vines.
- Heavy metal toxins weaken the roots.

---

## **VI. Imagery, Sensation, Language**

**Visual:** Branch-ribbed dome blooming with glowing chlorophyll veins.
**Sound:** Heartbeat-like pulse, leaf-rustle under no wind.
**Sensation:** Warmth along the spine, grounding, green breath.

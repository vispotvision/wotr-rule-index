# **VORYNN EXECUTION STRIKE**

(The Fang That Ends Momentum)

I. **Source and Concept**
Primary Wellspring: Vohrin (Cold Law)
Secondary Wellsprings: Tenebra (ambush), Cinerion (remnants)
Path: Predatory–Body
Aether Class: Primal-Kinetic Aether
Core Concept: Draven compresses every blood-ignited muscle into a single predatory vector. The blow enforces a localized Cold-Law clause: momentum stops, muscle chains freeze, and internal Essence locks.

II. **Function and Philosophy**
Function: Offense • Anti-Beast Execution
Outcomes:

- Collapses the target’s internal momentum field.
- Freezes kinetic defenses for 1 to 2 seconds.
- Delivers catastrophic blunt or slashing trauma.


Philosophy: “If it moves, break the motion first.”

III. **Mechanics Spec**
Name: Vorynn Execution Strike
Type: Offense / Kinetic Collapse
Temperance Requirement: Stage VI Glory
Activation: Declared
Range or Area: Melee
Duration per turn: Instant
Tags: Kinetic Break, Cold Law, Predator Vector

IV. **Limits, Weaknesses, Cost**
Limits: Requires Bloodbind ignition.
Weaknesses: Heat-aligned Wellsprings.
Cost: 20 percent Essence drain and muscle tearing if chained.

V. **Counters and Interactions**
Counters: Fire-Law shields, void-momentum arts, airborne pivots.

VI. **Imagery, Sensation, Language**
Visual: Frost cracks through the air like white lightning.
Sound: Sudden vacuum pop, then bone-weight thud.
Sensation: Temperature drops around his forearm.

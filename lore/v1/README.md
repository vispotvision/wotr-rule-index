# Lore v1 — the archive before the 2026-10-18 lore reset

Isaac reset the lore on 2026-10-18. Everything archived under the old lore lives here,
frozen, and is **not canon for events** in the new lore (canon hierarchy rung 4 reads
`scenes/` only).

- `scenes/` — every archived scene, its indexes (ARCS, CAST, MANIFEST, TIMELINE,
  CONTINUITY) and the audio cast files.
- `table/` — the Ledger, Fronts, NPC roster, sessions and thread dates as they stood.
- `wiki/` — the vault's Scene Archive (the scene mirror, continuity maps, Notion-era
  scene pages) and the retired Table pages (State of Play, Fronts, Ledger, roster).

The live `scenes/` and `table/` start empty. `scene_recall(query, old_lore=True)` still
searches these scenes, every hit labelled old lore; nothing else serves them. Nothing
here is edited; it is a record.

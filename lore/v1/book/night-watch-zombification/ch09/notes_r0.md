## Declared sentences

**The lie (NPC, uncorrected).** The archive keeper, handing over the schedule of metered draw:

> "That's the current schedule, Warden. Came down at the quarter."

It is a season old; the quarter's issue is the unopened canvas bag under his own table. Wystan's fraction is therefore slightly wrong and is never corrected. Per the Value, Coin and Trade page, Logistics' published figures are "historical the moment it is a season old". This unsays nothing in ch19 or ch47.

**The misreading (Wystan, uncorrected).** His private count, in italics:

> *Two myriads and a fraction had been bought over a meter, at the tariff, and somebody had paid the bill when it came.*

Sound reasoning on a corrupted premise: metered draw is a supply contract, a contract ends at a meter, a meter ends in a bill. The Verdantia was never bought; it was drawn down as wastage (ch4). Petitioning Logistics for draw records is the right act for the wrong reason. It brings him no nearer the hand: the records are refused, so no contract holder's name reaches him.

## Summary for continuity (194 words)

Day 10. In the Division archive Wystan draws the northern schedule of metered draw; the keeper says it is current, and it is a season old. Working from the assay's third dilution and Tally's count, eleven hinds and a stag, with spring weights off the Society's almanac, he prices the Ferriby pour twice and gets two myriads and a fraction both times. He concludes the dose was bought over a meter and that a bill was paid. Off the archive's map he takes four array lines within a day's ride of the Ferriby cut, Swale, Aysgill, Wendle and Scarth, and wires Logistics for their draw records in nine words under the Register's form. The refusal comes back in nine: returns publish on the schedule and not before. At the Timberline bench Yinzhi, working from her own coil reading and touching neither tin, shows him a compound that is idle in the flask and runs in a pail of pig. She will not carry it onto the Ferriby tin. At the eleventh division, as the main goes off, Wystan files a standing request against the Swale line: the return to reach the Night Register on the day it publishes.

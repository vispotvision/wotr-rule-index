# The Holiest of Holy

*Above Kharven. The tenth hour.*

---

The gate put him out four thousand feet over a burning city and did not slow him down on the way.

Darius Ignatius Sanctus Sanctorum fell through the black.

He had time to read it. That was what nine months in the ash had actually bought, more than the seven Fists and more than the matrix under them: he could take a system apart while it was still arriving. The cloud base over Kharven was not cloud. It was a working, laid over the whole basin, and the light that should have been coming through it was being refused rather than blocked, and there is a difference and the difference was the handhold.

He put the first of the seven into it.

Nothing in it resembled a strike. Ruin is the fist that finds the ending already on its way and makes it arrive correctly, and the working over Kharven had an ending in it, because everything does, and all Darius did was locate the place where it had already begun to fail and stop being polite about the timetable.

The sky came apart in a gradient.

---

It arrived as a graduation and never as a hole. Sunlight came through the black in a series of small illuminations, thousands of them, opening in a scattered arithmetic across four miles of overcast and brightening in sequence, and where each one opened the black around it thinned instead of tearing.

Then the whole thing stood open and he came down through it.

Terminal velocity on a man of his mass in that air is about a hundred and twenty miles in an hour. He was going considerably faster than that and had been since the gate, because the Fourth Work is order and nothing else and a body falling under order does not accept the drag term.

Anyone on the ground looking up saw a figure in the light. He knew that and could do nothing about it and had spent eleven years of correspondence trying to prevent exactly this, and it was happening now over a capital with sixty thousand people in it.

The filaments came off him at about two thousand feet.

Aetheric Density in the column he was falling through went from the ordinary saturation of a burning district to something no instrument in Kharven was rated for, in the space of eleven hundred feet, and it did it as a gradient with no front to it anywhere, and every practitioner in the basin felt it in the sternum before anybody saw the light.

They came out of the Lattice along the long bones and out through the skin and stood off him in a fine radiant floss, and every one of them was expressing at the Wellspring stratum without being asked. Judgement, in its unmediated form, which no living man had put into open air in three hundred years, and it had two operations and they were both running.

The first is the putrefaction of what is corrupt.

The second is the destruction of Obsession.

---

There were monsters in the streets.

He had read the taxonomy in a warm room in Altherion and had signed the appendix. Void constructs, assembled and never born, and the assembly is the whole of them: a working folded into a shape, and the shape does the work, and the material it is folded out of is ordinary and inert and could be anything.

And a fold can be undone without touching the material.

The light reached the first of them at the head of the Cooper's quarter and their forms began to twist.

No burning anywhere. The structures went slack. The precise geometry that had made a thing a thing came apart at the level of the fold while every particle of what it had been made from stayed exactly where it was, and what had been a construct with a function became the same quantity of matter with no function at all, malleable, running, arriving on the flags in a heap that was chemically identical to the thing that had been standing there and could not do one thing.

It is the same operation that takes the function out of an egg-white over a low flame. The chain is untouched, every unit of it accounted for, and the fold is gone and the fold was the entire performance, and no amount of cooling brings it back.

The working itself was stripped out of them.

And underneath that the second operation went to work, and the second operation is the one that killed them.

A Void construct is sustained by refusal. That is what Obsession Force is and the Accord has known it for four hundred years and has never found a use for the knowledge: the thing continues because something in it will not stop, and the refusal is load-bearing, and remove the refusal and there is nothing else holding the arrangement together.

Purity does not argue with a refusal. It removes the conditions under which refusing is possible.

They went off across the whole basin. Each one released the binding energy of its own structure at once, which is a small quantity per construct and a very large one across thousands.

Thousands of them, in the streets and on the walls and in the cisterns and in the flooded lower cloister, in a wave that moved outward at the speed of the light front, and each one came apart with a concussion of released structure that took out windows for a hundred feet. The noise of it arrived at Kharven-Seat as one long roll that did not have separate parts in it.

Darius Ignatius Sanctus Sanctorum came down through the middle of that and looked past all of it into the black abyss standing over the palace, and was not afraid.

*I have been in the ash nine months.*

*Six of the seven took something off me before they came. The fifth took my certainty about a thing I had believed since I was twenty-six and I have not replaced it. The seventh took the man who carried the other half of my office, and I never learned his face properly, and I paid for it before I knew there was a bill.*

*I have nothing left in me that is available to be frightened with. That is not courage and I will not have it written down as courage.*

*Gimbzo said it in the fourth month and I argued with him for a week. A man who has been emptied is not brave, he is cheap. The dear thing is the man who still has something to lose and comes anyway, and there are eleven hundred of those on the Ashgate road at this moment and not one of them will get a window.*

---

Ignatius, they gave him at nineteen, for the faith. Unbridled was the assessor's word and the assessor meant it as a caution.

Sanctus at twenty-six, for the commitment, which the Order records as exemplary and which his own file records as a man who would not stop.

And Sanctorum at thirty-five, which is a genitive plural and never a third name, and which means the holiest of the holy things, and which had been conferred four times in eleven hundred years and was not conferred again after him.

The other three are in the Long Reading. Two of them are entered with a date of death and no manner. The third has neither, and the column where the manner should be carries a mark that the Bench has declined eleven times to explain, and Darius had looked it up at thirty-five in a cold room on the night they gave him the name, and had gone back to his quarters and written a letter refusing the seat.

He had gone on refusing it for eleven years, and the Order had gone on addressing him by the whole of the name anyway, because the name is not an office. It is a statement about who the Judger's weapon is, and a man does not get to decline being the thing he is.

---

The ground began to answer.

Aetherquakes, and no part of that word is metaphor. Nothing about it resembled the ordinary shaking of a district under pressure. The Aether across the whole Kharven basin had been carrying stress since the ninth hour, laid on by the aurora and the ridges and the corona and everything else that had happened in that valley, and stress builds in a lattice until the lattice cracks, and when a lattice under that much strain finally moves it moves everywhere at once.

The basin cracked in slabs and mended behind the cracks.

That was the part that no witness account would agree on afterward. The ground opened, and along the same line, half a second behind, it closed. Streets carved out and laid back down in a different arrangement. Two miles of the outer wall taken to the footing and reassembled four feet to the north with its courses in the right order. Things went up and around and came back and were mended, and the mending had no charity in it at all. It was an ordering. It was the basin being set right in whatever way was cheapest, without any reference to whose house had been standing where.

And in the middle of it the voice arrived.

*Go.*

It had no sound. Darius understood that with the front of his mind while the back of it did what a mind does with weight it cannot hold, which is to produce sentences, and the sentences it produced were these.

*Go now, Light-Bearer of the realms. I have given you the Trait of Purity.*

He had carried it since thirty-five and had never once heard it named aloud by the thing that gave it.

It is not a technique and cannot be trained and appears on no ladder. It is a property of the Lattice, and it is a subtraction, and what it subtracts is coupling. Nothing external integrates into his structure. Nothing sticks. No Residue accumulates in him, no district ever gets into his bones, no working laid on him ever finds purchase, and no borrowed Aether has entered his body since he was thirty-five years old.

The Order writes this up as sanctity. Darius had been correcting them in the margins for eleven years.

*It also means I cannot be given anything.*

*No Synergia. No shared working. No healing from another practitioner's hand, ever, at any price, and I have watched two men die that I could have carried out if a single thing about me would accept a transfer.*

*And it is why they split the office across two of us and walked us out through different doors. The Hand has to receive and I cannot. So one man carries and one man spends, and the carrier was a clerk with beautiful handwriting and no faculty at all, and I met him once for eleven minutes and did not shake his hand.*

*I would like that entered.*

---

*Heed my call. Hammer of Justice. Come to me.*

The sky parted a second time, higher, past where sky is a word that applies.

Something came down through it. It came in at an angle out of the dark between the primordial stars, arriving along a line laid down for it instead of falling, and it took eleven seconds to cross what it crossed and it came into Darius's right hand.

It was the size of a smith's hammer and it weighed what a smith's hammer weighs and there was nothing else about it that was ordinary.

Degenerate matter. Nuclei crushed out of their arrangement and packed to the density of a star's interior, and Darius knew the number in the abstract and had never expected to be holding it. A thimble of that at a bridge would take the bridge and the river bed under it.

And a piece that small cannot exist.

That is the whole of the thing and it is the reason nobody else has ever carried it. Matter at that density is held together by the weight of the star sitting on top of it, and nothing else, and below a certain mass the gravity is gone and the neutrons are free and a free neutron does not stay a neutron. It decays. The energy sitting in a single one of them is comparable to everything holding the arrangement together, and there are more of them in that hammer head than there are grains of sand in the world.

Every one of them has been trying to leave since the moment it was made.

The Hammer of Justice is a refusal, held. It is the most obstinate object in four worlds and it does exactly one thing, which is to not come apart, and it has been doing that continuously for however long it has existed and it will do it for exactly as long as somebody uncouplable is holding it.

That is why it comes to him and to nobody else. In any other hand the containment would find a channel into the bearer within a second and the bearer would be the first thing to go.

*I do not strike with it.*

*I have never struck with it. There is nothing to swing. What I do is stop holding a measured part of it for a measured length of time and let that part do the thing it has wanted to do since before there was a Kharven.*

There is a figure for how much he is permitted to let go and it is not written down anywhere, because the only man who ever needed it worked it out by feel at thirty-five and has never had occasion to revise it. Too little and the strike is a hammer blow delivered by a man of fifty-six. Too much by any margin at all and there is no Kharven, and no basin, and no Darius, and the Order's fourth entry acquires the same mark in the same column as the third.

He came down into the courtyard of the outer bailey with the light standing off him in filaments and the primordial stars aligning across the whole width of his shoulders, and he did not slow.

He hit the ground.

And opened his hand a fraction.

The aurora went out from the point of impact across four miles of a burning city, and every construct still standing in the basin met the light front, and the fold and the refusal went out of all of them in the same instant, and what had been an army came down onto the streets of Kharven as an ordinary quantity of ordinary matter with nothing left in it that wanted anything.

The concussion of it reached the outer wall and reflected and came back and met itself over the middle of the city and stood there as a standing pressure that would not disperse for eleven minutes.

Darius Ignatius Sanctus Sanctorum closed his fist around the haft, and put his other hand flat on the flags, and got up.

## Notes (author-facing, not canon)

**Under Packs Sixteen and Seventeen.** 14 system terms and 4 real technical terms in narration outside italics; the two workings and the Trait all carry an Operation rather than an assertion.

### The Hammer of Justice

**Operation.** Degenerate matter, nuclei crushed out of arrangement and packed to the density of a star's interior. And **a piece that small cannot exist**: matter at that density is held together by the weight of the star sitting on it and nothing else, and below a minimum mass the gravity is gone, the neutrons are free, and a free neutron decays. The energy sitting in one of them is comparable to everything holding the arrangement together, and there are more of them in that head than grains of sand in the world. Every one has been trying to leave since it was made.

**So the Hammer is a refusal, held.** It does exactly one thing, which is to not come apart, continuously, for as long as somebody uncouplable is holding it.

**And Darius does not strike with it.** There is nothing to swing. He stops holding a measured part of it for a measured length of time and lets that part do what it has wanted to do since before there was a Kharven. The tolerance is not written down anywhere: too little and it is a hammer blow from a man of fifty-six, too much by any margin and there is no basin and no Darius.

**Which is the irony I would keep.** The weapon against Obsession *is* an obsession. The most obstinate object in four worlds, refusing, forever, in the hand of the man whose office is the destruction of refusal.

### The Trait of Purity

Per Pack Seventeen §5, a Trait states its substrate change. This one is a **subtraction, and what it subtracts is coupling.** Nothing external integrates into his Lattice. No Residue accumulates, no district gets into his bones, no working laid on him finds purchase, no borrowed Aether has entered him since thirty-five.

**Which is why the Hammer comes to him and to nobody else.** In any other hand the containment finds a channel into the bearer inside a second and the bearer goes first.

**And it is the answer to why the God Hand was split across two men.** He cannot *receive*. No Synergia, no shared working, no healing from another practitioner's hand at any price. The Hand has to receive and he cannot, so one man carries and one spends, and they were walked out through different doors, and the carrier was a clerk with beautiful handwriting and no faculty at all whose face Darius never learned properly. That was not policy. That was the Trait.

### The two operations of the light

**Putrefaction** is denaturation. A Void construct is a working folded into a shape, and the shape does the work, and the material is ordinary and inert. The light undoes the fold and leaves every particle where it was, so what stands there afterward is chemically identical and can do nothing at all. Same operation as an egg-white over a low flame: chain untouched, fold gone, no cooling brings it back.

**Destruction of Obsession** is the one that kills them. Obsession Force is refusal-sustained, and in a construct the refusal is load-bearing. Purity does not argue with a refusal; it removes the conditions under which refusing is possible. Each one then releases the binding energy of its own structure, which is small per construct and very large across thousands, and the concussion reflects off the outer wall and stands over the middle of the city for eleven minutes.

### The voice, and a canon conflict handled rather than resolved

Verinus argued to a Curia at fifty-two that the office does not speak, and I wrote that as correct in his scene. So the voice here **has no sound**. Darius registers that with the front of his mind while the back of it does what a mind does with weight it cannot hold, which is produce sentences. Your dialogue is on the page word for word and nothing has been added to the office's capabilities. If you want Urion actually speaking, that is a large ruling and it needs to be deliberate.

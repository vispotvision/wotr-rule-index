# Kill Me First

*The Vaeloris quarter. Contact.*

---

"You will have to kill me first."

The filaments came up off him before the sentence finished, gold, out of the Lattice along both arms and both collarbones and the whole length of the spine, and this time they did not stand off him in floss. They set. They stood out rigid in every direction, and the light in them was the light he had opened in the overcast and it had been coming down on this street for six minutes without interruption.

Beside him, Aurelian Prudentius put both hands out palm up.

He began to weave.

It went into the air in front of him in a way Darius could read and could not have done, threads pulled out of his own Essence and laid across each other in a lattice that was not a shape and was a distribution, and the Primate's voice came out of him flat and level in the reading register, at the volume he had used at eleven hundred offices of the dead.

"Veni, voluntas Libratoris."

Come, will of the Balancer.

"Adsit statera."

Let the Scale be present.

The ground under Aurelian went down a coin's width and then went down again and kept going, an inch, two, and the flags around him tilted inward toward the place where he was standing.

"Addatur pondus sociis meis. Crescant."

Let weight be added to my allies. Let them grow.

And then the clause that made it a working instead of a wish, and Darius heard it and understood in the same instant what the Primate had just done and what it was going to cost him to keep doing it.

"Ego lanx altera."

I am the other pan.

"Quod illis accedit, mihi decedat."

What accrues to them, let it be taken from me.

"Descendam ut ascendant."

Let me descend so that they may rise.

A balance does not make weight. That is the entire principle and the Church has been putting it on walls for four hundred years without one bishop in fifty following it to the end. A Scale moves what is already there. Every ounce that arrives on one pan is an ounce that has left the other, and there is no version in which both go up.

Darius felt it arrive.

It came in at the Attraction Layer, which is the only thing that transmits between two people who are not touching, and for one moment it did not take, because nothing external couples to him and never has. Then it found the single channel a bond gets and went in through that, and his Ardency came up by a figure he would not be able to name afterward, and the fatigue went out of his legs, and the strain of holding the Trait in went with it.

Thirty feet away, Aurelian Prudentius sank another inch into the flags and smiled at the black and said nothing about any of it.

The Scale was under the whole street by then. Darius could see where it had been laid, because a working of that class leaves the ground itself sorted: every loose thing in the district had begun drifting toward one of two places, and the two places were the two pans, and the light pan was under his own feet.

*He is spending himself into me.*

*He has nothing left to feel about it, which is the entire problem with him, and it means he will not stop, because the thing that stops a man in the middle of that is the moment it begins to hurt, and he has been carefully relieved of the mechanism.*

*I have been in this street four minutes and I have already been handed a second person to keep alive.*

---

Darius had had enough of the talking.

He recognised that in himself and let it stand. Twenty-one years of correspondence, eleven years of a Curia, four hundred letters in a dry hand at the foot of the Order's register, a whole life spent putting the argument in writing so that nobody would have to be hit, and he had come out of nine months in an ash pit with the entire apparatus intact and no further use for any of it.

Behind them both, at the far end of the street, Val Nihil went on emptying itself into the Material and the things coming out of it were landing in the rubble at a rate of eleven or twelve a second and getting up.

Darius did not look at any of them. That was Aurelian's half of the arrangement and neither of them had discussed it, and thirty feet back the Primate had already put a hand out sideways without turning his head, and whatever came down that street on the left flank was going to meet a man who had seen every fate in four worlds at once and had found the experience interesting.

He went forward.

Slowly, and the slowness was the point. The ground between him and the black was under a gradient that would have put any practitioner in four worlds on their face at forty feet, and Darius walked into it at a pace and it did nothing to him whatsoever.

That is the Trait, and it is the only part of it he has ever enjoyed. Pressure works by coupling. It arrives at a Crystal, finds purchase in the Shell, and bears down through the structure, and that is why a Band gap changes local weather and why men go to their knees without being touched.

Nothing external couples to him.

So he walked through a field that was cracking flagstones on both sides of him, at fifty-six years of age, in a coat, with his chin down and his shoulders down, and the only thing that happened to him on the way in was that his hair moved.

At eleven feet the Hammer became useless.

He had known that before he arrived, because measure is the first item on any technical floor and he has been reading measure since he was fifteen. Inside eleven feet there is no arc. A hammer needs a radius and a radius needs a room, and the correct answer to being inside somebody's measure holding a hammer is to stop trying to use the hammer.

So he used his head.

He came past the guard with his hips going first, dropped his weight, and drove up off the back foot with the whole of his mass behind it, and put the front of his own skull into the middle of what was standing there.

A man of his mass moving at the speed the Fourth Work allows arrives with a kinetic quantity no muscle in a body could produce, and all of it goes through four square inches of the hardest bone in a body, and the transfer takes under two milliseconds because there is no give anywhere in the chain.

The frontal bone is the thickest plate in a man. It is the one part of the head built to be hit with, and it has been used that way in every fighting tradition on the Material for as long as there have been fighting traditions, and there is nothing brutish in it. It is a short weapon delivered at a distance where nothing longer will fit.

His eyes went gold on the way in.

The shock came back down through his own neck into his hips and out through the flags and cracked four of them.

He felt his own cervical column take it in three places and hold, and one of the discs went, and he registered that the way he registered everything now, as an entry, and kept going. There is a reason nobody past forty does that, and Darius had used it eleven times in his career and had been on the ground afterward for two of them.

At that range he could hear the Crevice.

No sound in it anywhere. A rate. Something under the black keeping a slow interval, slower than the pulse of anything that has a body, and it came up through the flags into the soles of his boots and into the bones of both feet and did not couple to a single thing in him and was audible to him anyway.

---

He got the shaft back.

The Hammer came up and over his right shoulder to the length of his arm, and by the time it reached the top of the arc he was already opening the first two Fists into the same motion, because there is no separation between them and never has been, and that is the whole content of the matrix Gimbzo spent nine months putting into him.

**Ruin first.**

Ruin does not break things. Ruin finds the ending that is already on its way and makes it arrive correctly, and there is no structure in four worlds without an ending in it, and the darkness standing around him had a great many. Every fold. Every joint where one part of the arrangement had been made to agree with another. Darius laid the first Fist across the whole standing volume and pushed on none of it, and stopped the timetable being polite.

The dark began to come apart along its own faults, and it did it the way anything under load does, which is to say at the joins and never in the middle of a good piece.

**Then Purity, out of the middle of Ruin, before the first was finished.**

Because a thing coming apart can reassemble, and that is what refusal is for. An arrangement sustained by Obsession continues on refusal alone and has no requirement to be intact. It goes on refusing, and it will drag its own pieces back together out of nothing but insistence, and that is why the Order's doctrine on Void constructs was wrong for three hundred years and why Darius rewrote it between twenty-six and thirty-five.

So he let the Trait out.

Some fraction of it, held short of the whole. He kept the boy's four streets and everything standing in them outside the volume by the width of his own judgement, which is the same instrument he uses for the Hammer and the same one he has never been able to write down.

Purity festered out of the ending that Ruin had found, into every fault opening across that darkness, and removed the conditions under which any of it was permitted to refuse.

The black shuddered and did not close.

Four minutes ago that would have taken the Trait forty seconds to accomplish across a volume that size. It took eleven, because a Scale had just been put under the whole street and one pan of it was standing thirty feet behind him going into the flags an inch at a time.

---

The Hammer came up.

There is no downstroke available at eleven inches and there was no time to make room, so he took it up, in a rising arc off the back foot with his whole body opening behind it, and he took it up at the chin.

That is the strike, and every fighting man alive knows why it is the strike. The jaw is a lever. Load applied at the point of it does not travel down through the frame, it turns the head, and rotational acceleration is the one thing a skull cannot armour against, because what is inside it is not attached to it at the speed the argument is being made.

There was blood in his left ear from the headbutt and it had already stopped, and his vision had gone narrow at the sides and come back, and the disc in his neck had begun to report at a level he would be unable to ignore in about forty minutes.

He had one arc. That was the reading and he made it on the way up: an uppercut of that length gives a single window between the point where the head clears the shoulder and the point where the wrist runs out of travel, and the window is about a hand's width of arc, and there is nothing after it except a man with a hammer over his own head and both ribs open.

And at the moment of contact, Darius Ignatius Sanctus Sanctorum opened his right hand a fraction.

For an interval he would afterward put at less than a thousandth of a second, a measured portion of the most obstinate object in four worlds was permitted to stop being obstinate, and did the thing it had been trying to do since before there was a Kharven.

The concussion went out from the head of the Hammer in a sphere, and the sphere was a sphere and not a cone, because nothing in the release is aimed and nothing about it can be.

It took the Vaeloris quarter down to the footings for a hundred and forty feet in every direction. The street went. The frontages went. The gable of the chapter hold on the corner came off in one piece and was gone before it had finished leaving. The front reached the outer wall and reflected and came back and met itself over the top of him, and Darius stood in the middle of that with his own skeleton taking all of it, because the containment gives him nothing and asks him for everything.

Behind him the boy on the rubble had not looked up, and the Obsession Force was still standing over four streets, and Darius had kept every strand of it out of the volume by the width of a judgement he could not have explained to anybody at the Order.

He drove the arc upward with everything he had.

"RAAAGH."

It tore out of him and he did not shape it and did not want to. He had not raised his voice in front of another living soul since the year he was twenty-six. He had regarded that as a discipline, and had written a paragraph about it in the Order's conduct guidance that was still in force, and every word of that paragraph had been composed by a man who had never been in an ash pit.

"FIGHT ME."

The gold went up both arms past the wrists and into the haft.

"YOU WANT THE PARAGON. YOU WILL HAVE TO DO A GREAT DEAL MORE THAN IMPRISON ME."

*And you will not get his name out of either of us.*

*The Primate will not give it. Whatever is left in that man is still holding that one, and I watched him decline to answer you and say nothing at all in a street where saying nothing is the only thing left that costs him anything.*

*And I cannot give it. I have never seen the child. That was the whole of the design and I hated it for eleven years and I understand it now.*

"KUJO."

The Hammer went up through the top of the arc with a hundred and forty feet of city coming apart underneath it.

"YOU WILL HAVE TO KILL ME."

## Notes (author-facing, not canon)

What that arc *does* is yours to write. Asante and Haruki stay off the page.

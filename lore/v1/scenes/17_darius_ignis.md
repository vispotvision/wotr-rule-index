# Ignis

*The Vaeloris quarter. The Scale, still open.*

---

Aurelian Prudentius had heard the Regent say *weigh nothing* and had taken it as an instruction.

"Fini faciem."

Limit the face.

Darius heard the clause go out over the burning street and understood the target before the sentence finished, because he could see the forty-nine points the Regent had opened, each one a place where possession had no valid state, each one a mouth the Scale could pour into forever and never fill.

"Detrahe formam."

Draw down its form.

*He is bounding them.*

*A nothing with no edge cannot be weighed. That is the entire trick and it is a good one. But put a boundary around a nothing and the nothing inside it is no longer the same as the nothing outside, and the difference between the two has a magnitude, and a magnitude is a thing a pan can hold.*

"Quidquid e nihilo tractum est, in aliquid componatur."

Whatever has been drawn from nothing, let it be assembled into something.

The forty-nine points acquired faces.

Darius watched it happen and had to revise a thing he had believed since he was twenty-six. Each absence in the field, which had been a hole in possession, got a surface put on it. Turgia. Creation, lasting change, the Plane of Fate, and the oldest Method in the register and the one every practitioner underestimates because it is slow. The Primate did not fill the holes. He gave every one of them a limit, and a limited nothing has a quantity, and forty-nine quantities went onto the far pan at once.

The Scale moved.

A change in the energy of a bounded vacuum has a mass. That is not metaphor and it has been measured, and the Primate had just given forty-nine absences a boundary each and thereby forty-nine masses, and the Regent's inexhaustible problem had become an exhaustible one at the exact moment it acquired a shape.

The impossible deficit stopped being impossible. It was still a deficit and it was still large and the Scale could now measure it, and a thing that can be measured can be paid, and Aurelian went down another two inches into the flags paying it and did not stop weaving.

Then the working came back up the street and into Darius.

---

He shifted.

Purity out. It had been running in every fault Ruin opened and it had nothing left to find, because the Regent had stopped giving it things, and a Work with no object is a Work being spent for nothing.

Ruin out. Same reason, worse: the man had started completing his own endings ahead of the timetable, and a Work that finds endings has no purchase on a thing that is already finishing itself.

*He answered both of the first two by giving them nothing. So I stop offering them the things he can discard.*

**Order.**

The Fourth Work. It is the least impressive of the seven and Gimbzo had made him hold it for a month longer than any of the others because it refuses to be impressive, and what it does is continuity. It does not create and it does not destroy. It insists that a road which was there a moment ago is still there, and it makes the insistence stick, and it costs the practitioner nothing except the requirement that he himself be continuous, which is the one thing Darius has never had any trouble with.

He laid it across the gaps.

The Fourth Work costs almost nothing to run and it is the only one of the seven that has a hard prerequisite, which is that the practitioner has to be the kind of thing that does not have gaps in it. Every other Work can be run by a man who is falling apart. Order cannot. The Order had known that for a thousand years and had put it in the entrance examination as a riddle without ever once explaining the answer, and Darius had passed the examination at nineteen and had not understood the answer until the fourth month in the ash.

The missing roads. Every hair-thin discontinuity the Regent had opened in the volume around himself, every interval from which distance had been omitted, every removed millimetre of the medium between a rod and a prisoner. Order went into the volume and found the places where the geometry had been edited and put the geometry back, and the geometry stayed back, because a road that Order has declared continuous does not consult anybody about whether it is.

*That is the first thing he has had to hold, and holding is a different verb from defending. He has to keep those gaps open against a Work whose entire content is that things stay where they were, and every one of them he keeps open is a thing he is now spending on.*

**Spirit.**

The Third Work. The traffic. And the Crevice under that sternum was a rate before it was anything else, a slow interval he had heard through his boots, and a rate is traffic, and traffic is the one Work the Order had never thought to point at anything that was not a soul.

He pointed it at the Crevice.

He could hear it. That was the reading and it was the only one that mattered: through the flags, through the soles of both boots, in the long bones, a slow interval that coupled to nothing in him and arrived anyway, slower than any pulse a body has. Everything else at the end of the street had been replaced at least once in the last minute. The interval had not moved.

*You discard tendrils. You discard plates. You discard the dark and grow it back somewhere else and none of it costs you because none of it is you.*

*That is you. You put it into the relationship between yourself and the Void and you cannot take it out again, and it has a rhythm, and I can hear the rhythm, and Spirit is how I take hold of a rhythm.*

*Keep drawing it out. Keep pulling.*

Two Works at once, held in one Lattice, with the Attraction Force from the Scale loading both. Conjunctia, and gated high, and the Order's own register lists it as the last Method a practitioner learns and the first one that kills him. He had never run two. He ran two now, Order holding the roads and Spirit pulling on the beat, and the tessellation across his body tightened until the cells were the size of grains and the gold at their boundaries went white.

---

The claw came first.

He went in with the left hand open and the fingers hooked and the whole tessellated arm loaded to the point where the Attraction Force in it had nowhere left to be stored, and the air in front of the hand broke down.

Dielectric breakdown. Air is an insulator up to a threshold and above that threshold it stops being one, all at once, along whatever path the field finds cheapest, and the field standing off Darius's forearm was past that threshold by a margin no instrument in Kharven was rated to express. The air went conductive. Light sparked off the fingertips in branching lines and the lines did not scatter. They followed the hand. Electricity dragged forward through the breakdown channel ahead of the claw at the speed a discharge propagates, which is faster than any hand, and it reached the side of the thing in front of him a full interval before the fingers did.

The fingers went for the side.

Below the lowest rib on the left, where the plates of the transformed anatomy had to leave a gap for the frame to bend, because every armoured thing that bends has a gap there, and the gap was where the crimson channels ran closest to the surface.

The claw met the missing millimetre.

Order had put the roads back everywhere in the volume except the one place that mattered, the skin of absence one millimetre off the true body, and the fingers arrived at it and found no medium to close through, and the discharge ran round the outside of the shell like water round a stone and grounded in the flags.

*Held. He held that one. He let the rest go and held the one that keeps my hand out.*

*Then I have found out what he cannot afford to lose.*

He was already spinning, because the alternative was to be standing still at eleven inches with a spent arm, and the Aetheric Density around that shell of absence had gone past anything a Lattice can stand in for longer than the length of a breath.

---

A body that has just missed with its left hand, at full commitment, at eleven inches, is a body about to be killed, and there is exactly one thing it can do that does not involve stopping. It can use the miss. The whole loaded mass of the arm was still going forward and the hips were still behind it and Darius put his right shoulder down and let the momentum take him over.

A revolving shoulder roll. Taught in the Order's third-year manual to men who would never need it, as the thing you do when the parry has failed and the opponent is too close to run from. The shoulder comes over and the spine follows and the body goes through the space below the strike instead of away from it, and it comes up on the far side facing the wrong way with the weapon hand at the bottom of its swing.

He came up behind the left flank with the Hammer at full extension below his hip, and the shoulder that had taken the roll had gone in a way he would find out about later, and the tessellation across the back of it had broken into irregular cells and was trying to re-tile.

"Divine Hammer."

He had never once said its name aloud. He had held it twenty-one years and had called it the Hammer in his own head and in every letter and had refused the Order's name for it in writing on four occasions, and he said it now because a name spoken at the top of a swing is a Direction glyph and he needed the direction to be exact.

"Ignis."

Fire. His own first name is built on the same root and he had never let anyone remark on it.

He opened his hand.

Further than a fraction. Further than he had ever gone, the tolerance he had worked out by feel at thirty-five and had never revised, exceeded on purpose for the first time in his life, and a portion of the most obstinate object in four worlds was permitted to stop being obstinate, and this time the portion was large enough to have mass in the sense that matters.

The head of the Hammer went to the density of a star's interior.

It did not detonate. That was the release he had made every other time and it was not what he wanted now. He held the decompression back, held every neutron in that measured portion at the point of decay and refused to let a single one go, and what he had at the end of his arm for one second was a piece of degenerate matter with the full mass of that density and none of the explosion.

Mass gravitates.

*You have a centre. Everything in this street has been drawn toward it since the ninth hour, and you have discarded every other thing you own and you have kept that, because you cannot put it down.*

*I have a centre now too.*

The Hammer pulled.

It ignored the flesh and the plates and the tendrils, which he had stopped caring about, and pulled on the beat. On the thing under the sternum that had a rhythm and a location, the one object in four hundred feet that had been holding still while everything around it was edited, and a compact mass close to an extended body does not pull on the body evenly. It pulls on the near side harder than the far side, and the difference across the body is the tidal load, and the tidal load is what tears things that have never been torn.

Spirit had hold of the rhythm. Order was holding the road between the Hammer and the Crevice continuous. And the mass at the end of Darius's arm dragged on the one structure in the street that could not be discarded, and pulled it toward a single location, and the location was the head of Ignis.

Then he brought it down.

Through the pulled thing rather than the body, into the ground, with the whole of the drawn-in mass going down ahead of it, and the release at the end of the arc was the decompression he had held back for one full second, let go all at once, expanding, into whatever he had dragged into its path.

The Vaeloris quarter went down for two hundred feet in every direction, and the reflection off the outer wall came back and met itself over the crater, and Darius's own skeleton took the whole of it, because the containment gives him nothing and asks him for everything and he had just asked it for more than it had ever been asked.

The disc in his neck stopped reporting. He registered that as an entry and did not have time to read what it meant.

---

Aurelian did not stop weaving through any of it.

Darius could see him at the edge of the release, nine inches into the flags now, with the Scale under the street and the forty-nine bounded absences on the far pan, and the Primate had turned his hands over.

"Pondera eum."

Weigh him.

That was the second working and it was not aimed at the deficit and it was not aimed at the allies. It was aimed at the Regent, and it put the transformed thing at the end of the street on the pan, and a thing on a pan has a weight, and a weight is a fact that the Scale then holds against everything else in the field.

*He is measuring him. In the middle of this, with the Residual Strain of an unsealed working climbing behind his sternum and nowhere to put it. He has put the Void Regent on the Scale and he is going to read the number, because that is what the office does and there is nothing left in him that would think not to.*

The ground under Aurelian went down four inches in one motion.

*And the Rigidity Effect has him. A soul's reduced capacity to contradict a law it carries, scaling with how absolute the law is. He has made the Scale absolute. He cannot now stop weighing any more than the Hammer can stop refusing, and I have known that for six minutes and there is nothing I can do about it that does not involve leaving this street.*

Darius came up out of the crater with the neck he had and the tessellation white and Ignis refusing in his right hand, and the whole of the quarter burning behind him, and turned back toward the dark.

## Notes (author-facing, not canon)

**My turn, ended on my turn.** The claw meets the missing millimetre and grounds. The roll comes up behind the left flank. What Kujo does with any of it is yours. Haruki and Asante do not appear.

### Aurelian's answer to *weigh nothing*, and it is real

*Fini faciem. Detrahe formam. Quidquid e nihilo tractum est, in aliquid componatur.* Limit the face. Draw down its form. Whatever has been drawn from nothing, let it be assembled into something.

**A nothing with no edge cannot be weighed. A bounded nothing can.** That is the Casimir result: put a boundary around a vacuum and the vacuum inside is no longer the same as the vacuum outside, the difference has a magnitude, and a change in bounded vacuum energy has a mass in the sense that couples to gravity. So the Primate does not fill the forty-nine holes. He gives each one a face, and forty-nine limited absences become forty-nine quantities on the far pan, and the inexhaustible deficit becomes an exhaustible one at the moment it acquires a shape. Turgia, per the Lexicon: creation, lasting change, Plane of Fate, the oldest Method in the register and the one everyone underestimates because it is slow.

**His second working, *Pondera eum*, weigh him**, puts the Regent on the pan. That is what the office does, and there is nothing left in Aurelian that would think not to. And the Lexicon's Rigidity Effect has him now: a soul's reduced capacity to contradict a law it carries, scaling with how absolute the law is. He has made the Scale absolute. He cannot stop weighing any more than the Hammer can stop refusing.

### Darius's shift, and why each Work

**Purity and Ruin both out**, for the reason the Regent gave: he stopped giving them things. A Work with no object is spent for nothing.

**Order.** Continuity, and nothing else. It does not create or destroy; it insists a road that was there is still there, and the insistence sticks. Laid across every discontinuity the Regent opened, it puts the geometry back, and every gap Kujo wants kept open is now a thing he has to *hold* against a Work whose entire content is that things stay where they were. Its prerequisite is that the practitioner has no gaps in him, which the Order has kept in the entrance examination as a riddle for a thousand years.

**Spirit.** The traffic Work. The Crevice was a rate before it was anything else, Darius heard it through his boots, and a rate is traffic. So he points Spirit at the one thing Kujo has demonstrated he cannot discard. **Keep drawing it out.**

**Both at once is Conjunctia**, which the Lexicon gates high and which he has never run. The tessellation goes to grains and the gold at the boundaries goes white.

### The three attacks, mechanically

**The claw.** The tessellated arm loaded past storage; the air ahead of it breaks down dielectrically, all at once, along the cheapest path; the discharge runs ahead of the hand at propagation speed, which is faster than any hand. Aimed below the lowest left rib where armour that bends has to leave a gap. **It meets the missing millimetre**, the one road Order did not restore, and grounds. Which tells him what the Regent cannot afford to lose.

**The roll.** A body that has just missed at full commitment at eleven inches has one option that does not involve stopping: use the miss. Shoulder over, spine follows, through the space below the strike, up on the far side with the weapon at the bottom of its swing. Third-year manual, taught to men who would never need it. The shoulder goes in a way he will find out about later.

**Ignis.** The Hammer's name, spoken aloud for the first time in twenty-one years, because a name at the top of a swing is a Direction glyph. He opens further than the tolerance he set at thirty-five, and **holds the decompression back**. Every neutron in the released portion at the point of decay, refused. What he has for one second is degenerate matter with the full mass of that density and none of the explosion, and **mass gravitates**. A compact mass near an extended body pulls the near side harder than the far, and the difference is the tidal load, and tidal load is what tears things that have never been torn. Spirit has the rhythm, Order holds the road between Hammer and Crevice continuous, and the mass drags the one undiscardable thing toward one location. Then the held decompression is let go all at once, into whatever was dragged into its path.

# Five Numbers

*The crater. The Black Hole. The slowed tick, still running.*

---

The Regent had been taking the Nadi strikes.

Absorbing them, orbiting them, marking them finished and discarding their claim one at a time, and the discarding was efficient and the orbiting was elegant and Darius watched a thousand blows go into curved graves and understood, somewhere around the four hundredth, that the man had never once asked what the strikes were made of.

There is a prerequisite on the Hammer. There has always been a prerequisite.

*He is taking the fervor of the office into a body at a rate no vessel is rated for and he is filing each arrival as a completed sequence and unwinding it, and the unwinding removes the claim and does not remove the arrival. The claim is the bill. The arrival is the meal. He has been eating for a thousand strikes and cancelling the invoices.*

*And he thinks that is the same as not having eaten.*

There is a Temperance gate on every entry in the sheet and there is a reason for the gate, and the reason is that a Crystal below the stage cannot hold the boundary condition steady while the Wellspring runs underneath it. Above the gate the working obeys. Below it the working happens to you. Nothing in the whole Index says anything about what occurs when a body above the gate is fed by an office that has no gate at all, because nobody in eleven hundred years has been in a position to write that row.

Beside him, Aurelian Prudentius began to root something at his core.

---

Darius saw what he reached for and had to look away from it.

Paru Five is a Root Invocation and its Trigger is exact and it has ruined more practitioners than any entry in the first ten. *An unfulfilled want the caster has carried long enough to have a stable emotional shape. A fresh whim will not work.* It brings into brief physical existence something that has never been, shaped entirely by the intensity of the longing, and Ignivale runs it, which is Caloria, which is Thermodynamics: heat transfer, combustion kinetics, thermal gradient collapse. [Ve] Desire with [Asa] Creation, and neither is a creation-operator. They are directional. They impose a Temperance-anchored boundary condition on a process Ignivale was already physically capable of running.

The process is a flare. Ignivale would run it in an empty field with nobody watching, the way a thermal gradient collapses whether or not there is a man standing beside it. What the want does is give it a shape.

And Aurelian Prudentius had been relieved that morning of every feeling he owned, and had said so in a street, plainly, as a true reading rather than a flattering one.

He had one want left.

Darius knew what it was because the man had handed it over in the Vaeloris quarter without being asked. *I went and looked, because I could not remember why I had written the fourth one, and the file told me what I had done and could not tell me what it had been like.*

Forty years. Stable shape. Unfulfilled, and now unfulfillable, and therefore permanent.

"Paru Five," said Aurelian. "The Heartflare Given Shape."

The flare came up out of him.

It came up out of the flags first, because he was nine inches into them, and then out of the man himself, a column of Ignivale-fire with a form in it that had never existed in four worlds and had been specified down to detail nobody had consciously chosen. Darius did not look at the form. He had a strong suspicion about what shape a want like that takes and he did not want to be able to describe it afterward.

*Cost. The created thing stays hooked to him. Distance or neglect produces visible caster distress, phantom-limb, and he will not feel it, and that is worse, because a man who cannot feel the hook cannot tell when it is tearing.*

---

Darius went into the slowed bubble.

"Paru Thirty-Four. Flux Severance."

[Wy] Motion, Cataclysm running under it with Lethegraal behind, Caloria, Thermodynamics, and the gate on it is Transcendence and he had passed that gate at forty-one and had used the entry twice since. It is an explosive burst of speed inside a slowed-time bubble, for surgical strikes, and Aurelian had spent the last minute building the finest slowed-time bubble anyone had ever been handed.

[Wy] Motion is a constraint glyph. It narrows the current's degrees of freedom until one outcome satisfies both the Wellspring's law and the caster's intent, and inside a Cataclysm current the surviving outcome is thermal gradient collapse expressed as pure translation.

He detonated forward inside the slow, and the Numerical line on Thirty-Four is a lag and a rebound and both of them are deferred and neither is waived, and he would pay for the burst in a currency his neck could no longer produce.

And his own Order field came up to stop him.

*Counterplay on Thirty-Four is Order.*

*It is written in the sheet in my own Division's hand and I have read it a hundred times and I am the man holding Order across this entire crater.*

*I am countering myself.*

He had a fraction of a stretched second to solve it and no capacity left to drop the Fourth Work, because the roads were the only reason anything the four of them did still arrived anywhere.

Aurelian solved it for him.

---

"Paru Thirty-Seven. Judged Path."

[Ur] Balance. Judicium primary, Thaloré secondary, Fulguria, Electromagnetism: field propagation, potential differentials. Flourishing gate. Its stated Effect is a route that equalizes threat on both sides and averts ambush bias, and every practitioner who has ever read the entry has filed it as a scouting working and moved on.

Aurelian did not use it on a route.

He used it on the field.

*Equalize both sides. Both sides of what. The entry does not say, because the entry assumes a road, and he has not given it a road. He has given it a contest.*

Judged Path went across the whole crater and brought every opposed potential in it to the same value. Order and Flux Severance stopped being at different potentials, so Order's counterplay had no differential to act along, and Darius's burst came out of the bubble unopposed by his own working.

And then it reached the Severance of the Court.

That black line between functions, the thing that had cut the word *and* out of five perfect sentences and left Order not knowing what Spirit had found, met a field in which both severed halves stood at identical potential. A severance is a cut along a difference. Between two things at the same potential there is no difference to cut along, and the line found nothing to be a line between, and the Court's severance stalled and began to break down, and the attempt to fail their working began, precisely and visibly, to fail.

*He has used the counterplay on Paru Five as an instrument.*

*[Ur] Balance is the listed counter to the Heartflare. It forces an equal-and-opposite want into existence competing for the same nucleation site and destabilises both. He knows that. He wrote half the marginalia. And he has aimed it at the field instead of at his own flare, so that nothing in this crater can cancel or overwrite anything else, including his own working, and the price of that is that his flare is now standing inside a field that is actively trying to nucleate its opposite.*

Aurelian went down another inch and did not stop, and the listed Backlash on Thirty-Seven is rigid routing under chaos, which means the field he had just equalised would now resist being re-aimed by anybody, including the four people standing inside it, and Darius filed that and had no time to say it out loud.

---

The Black Hole had been pulling the whole time.

Everything with mass. Everything with energy. Every working with a Material expression anywhere in its three-plane grammar, which is every working, because the golden script had a physical manifestation and the frost had a physical manifestation and the Aetheric Saturation itself was a medium sitting in space and space had begun falling.

"Paru Sixty-Three."

Aurelian's voice had gone very quiet and the slowed air carried it anyway.

"Deep Current Pull."

[Abys] Deep. Abyntheus primary, Dissolution secondary, Fulguria, Electromagnetism. Refraction gate. It creates a directional undertow of sub-Realm pressure and drags constructs and bodies toward a vector, and the sub-Realm it pulls from sits outside the constraints the Void operates under, because it was there before the constraints were written.

Every vector in the crater turned. Abyntheus is a Fulguria current and under the Parunic dressing it behaves as field propagation and potential differential behaves anywhere, which is to say it does not push anything. It establishes a lower potential and lets the field do what a field does.

The debris going inward toward the singularity, the light curving into arcs, the frost stripping off the ground, the golden letters distorting as their physical expression fell: all of it stopped agreeing with the Black Hole's gradient and started agreeing with the undertow, because an undertow of sub-Realm pressure is not a force competing with gravity. It is a lower place, opened outside the jurisdiction the gravity was written in.

And the Choir came apart.

The four harmonic roles the Regent had divided himself into, Material phase and Soul phase and Fate recognition and the Crevice's supervisory rhythm translating between all three, had been safe because they held four different potentials and translated across the gaps. Judged Path had brought every potential in the crater to one value ninety seconds earlier. Four roles at identical potential have nothing between them to translate across. The Choir was singing one note in four throats and finding out what that does.

Twisted, and then shattered, and the pieces went into the undertow with everything else.

Darius watched the four phases come apart and thought about a bowed string and about the one lesson Gimbzo had given him on the subject in the seventh month, which was that a thing held in tension across several points does not fail at the strongest point or at the weakest. It fails at the point where two of the tensions were equal and each had been assuming the other would give first.

Darius had one clear thought about the ordering and it arrived with all the time in the world to be thought.

*He did not counter the Black Hole. Nobody in four worlds can counter that and the sheet does not have a row for it. He opened a hole underneath the hole.*

The Black Hole went last.

It did not resist and could not. A gradient is a shape in a medium, and the medium had begun draining into a lower place written under a different jurisdiction, and the singularity was ripped along its own gradient and dragged down into the undertow the way a whirlpool goes out of a bath when the plug is finally lifted.

---

"Boundary," said Darius.

He said it out loud and he said it too late to be useful and he said it anyway, because the entry has a Cost line and the Cost line on Paru Sixty-Three is four words long and every apprentice who has ever been made to copy it out remembers them.

*The current inverts if the Boundary glyph is absent. And the caster is pulled too.*

Aurelian already had it.

"Paru Seventy-Five."

[Plr] Pillar. Coagulatio primary, Materia Primordia secondary, Materia, Material Science: crystalline lattice structure, phase state, molecular bonding. Flourishing gate. A structural ward that anchors borders against domain bleed, and [Plr] Pillar is a directional operator imposing a Temperance-anchored boundary condition on a process Coagulatio was always physically capable of running.

And he did not build it from nothing.

He built it on the boundary that was already there.

*The seam. The join. The thing a man in a gold robe became at the ninth hour and got lifted out of, and the wall stayed standing and the wall is still standing and I have felt it since I came through the gate. Aurelian has never known it was Verinus. He knows there is a boundary. He is a true Mahuo and he can feel a join in Fate the way I can feel a floor.*

*He is building on his friend's body and he does not know it, and I do, and I am not going to be the one who tells him in this crater.*

*And it will hold. That is the obscene part. Coagulatio does not care whose seam it is anchoring to, and a wall made out of a Palatine who spent his last hour in the world declining to stop is the finest available substrate in four worlds for a boundary that has to hold against something that edits.*

The Pillar came up along the existing seam.

Coagulatio does one thing and it does it at the level of the bond. It fixes phase. It decides what stays joined to what and it makes the decision permanent, and where the boundary closed across the crater, every Void construct inside it stopped having a molecular process available to it. The dark did not burn and did not shatter. It stopped being able to conduct its own continuation, at the level where matter agrees to remain matter, because a boundary in Coagulatio's hand is a statement about which bonds are permitted.

And the same boundary held the undertow's mouth open in the right direction, so the current did not invert, and the caster was not pulled.

Then the second effect arrived, and Darius had not predicted it and should have.

A Formed Boundary anchors against domain bleed. Ensoulment is a domain operation. Mending Aether across a break is a domain operation. Formulating Essence into a new expression is a domain operation, and every one of them requires bleed across a border that had just been declared closed by a working whose entire Family is the physics of what may bond to what.

Inside that boundary the Regent could not ensoul. Could not mend Aether. Could not formulate.

*He can still discard. He can still unwind. He can still edit a road and move a door and wait at the border of the past.*

*He cannot make anything new.*

The Cost line on Seventy-Five is strain on the caster's joints, and stiffness, and Darius looked across the crater at a man nine inches into the flags with a Scale under the street and a Heartflare rooted in his core and an unsealed Turgia running since the tenth hour, and understood that the entry had been written for practitioners who would go home afterward.

Darius Ignatius Sanctus Sanctorum came out of the Flux Severance at the bottom of the slowed second with the Hammer at the end of his arm and a field that had been brought to one potential all around him, and he set his feet in a crater that had stopped falling, and looked for the man in it.

# Punta

*The crater. Inside the guard.*

---

The palm was a lie.

It had been a lie since the moment he committed to the spin, and the spin had been honest and the kick had been honest and the right hand had been honest, and the palm at the end of it was the only thing in the sequence Darius had never intended to land.

*Punta. The point that is offered so the hands will come up to meet it.*

*The Order's third-year manual calls it a false thrust and gives it four lines and every man who reads those four lines assumes it belongs to a sword. It belongs to whatever is at the end of your arm. You show the opponent a thing worth answering, and the answer is what you actually came for, and the whole art is in making the offer expensive enough to be believed.*

*A palm at the throat from a man carrying Ruin is expensive enough.*

The wrist got caught.

Darius felt the trap close and let it, because a limb that has been taken is a limb whose position both men now agree on, and agreement is the one thing he had come into that range to buy.

Then he dropped.

Nothing about it resembled a step or a duck. He arced the whole of his upper body forward and down over the trapped arm, spine rolling from the neck through the thoracic into the lumbar, and as he went he turned the hips through underneath, and the rotation and the descent became one motion in the way they only ever do when a man has thrown the strike enough times that his body no longer consults him about the order.

The elbow came down and across on the diagonal.

Sok chieng, in the register the Thai keep it in, and the Order's manual has it under a different name and a worse drawing. It is the strike you throw when your hand is already committed and your hand is useless, and the point of an elbow is the olecranon, which is a lever arm terminating in a spur of bone with no soft tissue over it anywhere, and it is the single hardest striking surface a human body owns.

He put it into the ribcage.

Between the plates. Over the floating ribs on the left where the armoured structure has to leave a gap because a frame that bends must leave a gap there, and the gap is a hand's width, and the whole of a fifty-six-year-old man's descending mass went through a bone spur into it.

Something gave.

Ribs eleven and twelve are called floating because they attach to nothing in front. They have a costovertebral joint at the spine and free ends at the other, and there is no sternum for the load to distribute into, so everything arriving there goes through the bone and into what the bone is covering, and on that side what the bone is covering is a spleen or whatever an anatomy that size keeps in the same place.

Darius felt it through his own arm before he heard anything, which is how you know a strike landed instead of merely arriving: the feedback comes back up the limb as a change in the character of the resistance, and the resistance changed.

He was reading the whole time, and the reading came up the arm as Attraction and never once through the eyes, which is the only reason it worked at all. Spirit is the Third Work and the Third Work is traffic, and traffic is a present-tense quantity and it does not care what the future has been permitted to do.

That was the part he could not have explained to anybody at the Order and had stopped trying to explain to himself. He had his hand on the man. Contact is traffic, and Spirit sat at the point of contact and read it, and every load in that body arrived up Darius's own arm as a present-tense mechanical fact: the right hip rotated and holding, the left shoulder set, the rear heel light, the hamstring loaded on the side that had not yet moved.

He was not fighting the future. He had given the future up in the ash at Sum-gol and had come here to fight in the moment.

He was fighting against certainty.

And that was the wrong way round, and he knew it, and it was the only advantage he had.

*I am the certainty in this crater.*

*Purity does not modulate. It is a property of a Lattice and it has no dial and I cannot make it uncertain if I want to. Order insists that a road which was there is still there, and it does not consult anybody about it. Every single thing I am is a structural fact.*

*He has made himself into a question. I have been an answer since I was thirty-five years old and I have hated it every day and I am about to find out what it is for.*

---

Then the loads all came up true at once.

Darius felt it through the palm still resting on the arm and stopped, internally, for the length of about a third of a second, because the reading had been clean and correct all the way through the elbow and now it said everything.

Right hip: round kick. And knee. And cross. All three, loaded, all three real.

Left shoulder: elbow, hook, frame, shell. Four. None of them subordinate.

Both feet: check, teep, pivot, step.

*Every one of those is true. I am not being deceived. There is no false load in that body and no muscle lying to me, and if I answer any one of them I will be answering a thing that was genuinely there and was genuinely not going to happen.*

*He has hidden nothing. He has made preparation stop meaning anything.*

*And I have exactly one thought about it and it had better be correct.*

It arrived out of the fourth month in the ash, in Gimbzo's flat voice, on a day Darius had spent face down in the grey with two cracked ribs and no idea what he had done wrong.

*A man who is ready for everything is throwing nothing.*

He almost laughed, and the laugh would have cost him a lung he could not spare, so it came out as a change in the shape of his mouth and nothing else.

*Co-contraction. That is all this is at the level where a body has to obey. He is holding agonist against antagonist across six chains at once, and a joint braced by opposing muscle is a stiff joint, and a stiff joint accelerates slowly and delivers less. It is the first thing anybody learns about power generation and it is the reason a tense fighter hits like a child.*

*Whatever he selects out of that, it comes out slower than it would have and it lands softer than it could have, because he has spent the tension on being ready and tension spent on readiness is tension that is not available to the strike.*

*He is paying for the state. He has been paying since he entered it. And he has not told anybody, including himself.*

*The Crevice is holding those contradictory vectors apart. That is not free either. Whatever the entry for that would say if anybody had ever written one, it would have a Cost line, and every entry in the sheet has a Cost line, and the ones that look like they do not are the ones where nobody survived to file it.*

Which was worth something and was not enough on its own, because a strike from that body at half force still ended people.

So Darius took the second half of the answer, and the second half was older than the ash and older than the Order and belonged to every wrestler who had ever lived.

*You do not have to know which one is coming.*

*You have to make most of them impossible.*

---

He stepped in.

Straight up the middle into the space the elbow had opened, on the dead leg, and it did not push and did not need to, because he was falling forward and had arranged to be.

A round kick needs room. An elbow needs an arc. A teep needs a foot of distance to extend into and a knee needs a hip that can travel and a pivot needs somewhere to pivot to.

He took the room.

Chest to chest. His forehead went into the sternum notch above the Crevice and stayed there, because a head is a post and a post occupying that place makes it very difficult for a man to get his hips under himself, and Darius put his weight on it and drove.

His left hand came off the trapped arm and went under. Underhook, deep, the forearm running up the inside of the arm and the hand closing at the back of the shoulder, and the underhook does one thing above all others: it takes the hip on that side away.

His right hand came across to the collar and pulled down.

No Thai plum, because a plum needs both hands and both hands was more grip than he could afford to have committed against a thing that could still elbow. One collar tie, one underhook, over and under, the oldest structure in wrestling and the one Gimbzo had put him in eleven times in the fifth month without once explaining why.

He knew why now, and the knowing arrived with the flat unwelcome clarity of every other thing the old man had put into him without explanation.

*He drilled the over-under against a partner who was allowed to attack from any position, eleven times, and would not tell me the point, and I asked on the ninth and he said HOO and nothing else. It is not a wrestling tie. It is a subtraction. It is the same shape as my own Trait and he built it into my hands before he ever taught me the seventh, so that the first thing I would reach for under pressure was the thing that removes options.*

Of the eleven loads Darius had felt come up true, the over-under killed seven outright. The round kick had no room. The teep had no distance. The pivot had nowhere. The cross had no arc and the hook had no arc and the check had nothing to check. What was left was a knee, an elbow on the free side, and a frame, and three is a number a man can defend against.

*Certainty is still standing.*

*I have made the question smaller. That is what a body is for.*

And the last piece went in with the underhook, and it was the piece nobody in that crater but Gimbzo would have understood.

Order went into the clinch.

It went nowhere near the ground or the field or the roads. Into the four points of contact between two bodies: the forehead on the sternum, the underhook at the shoulder, the collar tie at the neck, and the hip pressed against the hip. The Fourth Work insists that a thing which was there is still there, and Darius stopped insisting it about geometry and started insisting it about the tie.

*The contact stays. Whatever successor you promote, whatever you select out of that interval, you select it with my forehead on your sternum and my hand behind your shoulder, because those four points were there and Order says they remain.*

*You can move. You cannot leave.*

*And Purity is in the skin at all four points, which means nothing you put on me at those four points will find purchase, and there is no coupling available across the tie in either direction. You cannot bind me through it and you cannot drain me through it and you cannot reach anything of mine through it.*

*It is a wall with a man on each side of it and I am the wall.*

He drove the underhook up and rotated his own shoulder into the joint and felt the whole architecture on that side lose the ability to load, and the round kick that had been sitting in that hip for four seconds relaxed out of it and went nowhere.

A knee from the clinch is not a kick and has no arc and needs no distance. The hip travels perhaps eight inches and the whole mass of the trunk goes with it through a joint that is already the hardest thing in the lower body, and the reason it is the Thai national answer to everything is that it works at a range where nothing else fits.

Then he brought his own knee up, short, off the rear leg, into the same gap over the floating ribs that the elbow had already opened.

And the second one after it, before the ribs had finished reporting the first.

And a third, into the same handspan, because repetition into one location is the only thing in unarmed fighting that has ever reliably ended anybody. A body distributes an injury it receives once. It cannot distribute the same injury four times in six seconds, and the tissue between the second and the fourth stops behaving like tissue and starts behaving like a material with a fatigue limit, which is what it always was.

No single enormous thing anywhere in it. Nobody had ever beaten anybody with one enormous thing and Darius had spent twenty-one years writing that sentence into an Order that would not read it. Short knees from the clinch, into an existing injury, on a body that had to spend co-contraction holding six loads it was no longer able to use, over and over, in the same handspan, while a fifty-six-year-old man with a dead leg and a torn lung held four points of contact closed by a law that does not negotiate.

The strands on his frame had gone white at the shoulders, and the last of what the Primate had put into him was going out through four points of contact into a working that had no Wellspring and no Family and had never been given a Physics Domain, because Gimbzo would not assign one to a thing that lived entirely in Corporis.

*Solve certainty, he says.*

*I am not going to solve it. I am going to stand here with my head on his chest until the arithmetic finishes.*

There was blood in his mouth again and it had come up on its own and he swallowed it, because spitting costs a breath and he had a countable number of those left and had started counting them somewhere around the second knee.

Darius Ignatius Sanctus Sanctorum shortened the collar tie by four inches, turned his hips over, and brought the knee up a fourth time.

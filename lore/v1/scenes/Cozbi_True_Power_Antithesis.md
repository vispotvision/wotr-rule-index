"You've impressed me, Dhaerin Valorin." Cozbi drew the name out syllable by syllable, and for the first time since the fight had begun there was no performance left in his delight, only the raw, startled shape of it. "How absolutely impressive."

His spiritual body took the full brunt of the array's pressure for the first time all night, a lurch running through him that had nothing rehearsed about it. The stolen spirits woven through his architecture were being mismanaged now, pulled and rerouted by an outside hand rather than his own, and it was exactly the kind of failure only a Valorin would have caught, precise Harmonia-trained hearing finding the one dissonant seam in a structure built from a hundred conscripted souls. Cozbi had accounted, always, for the possibility that someone might eventually notice the borrowing. He had never accounted for someone catching him at it mid-battle and turning the theft itself into a weapon.

He would not let this become the shape of his ending. He would not let this man be the one who sealed him.

*I accounted for discovery,* Cozbi thought, watching the pale gold lines of Dhaerin's array still humming against his own compromised architecture. *I did not account for someone finding the seam before the borrowing had finished paying for itself.*

The borrowed strength had been sufficient until now, souls harvested and spent as coin because coin had always been enough for anything the Inner World had thrown at him so far. But underneath the borrowing sat a resolve of his own that he had not yet spent tonight, root current rather than conscripted current, and it began to surface.

His essence peeled off him in a slow, visible tide, and where it touched the air the world answered in kind. Matter gathered where his Aether pressed densest, drawn inward the way iron answers a lodestone, while its contradiction bled outward from the same point in equal measure, the two properties interjecting and interceding against each other in small, contained detonations that lingered at the edge of his silhouette like a held breath refusing to release. This was not summoned. Nothing here had been harvested from anyone else's dying. This was Antithesis, plain and unadorned, the single phenomenon his whole architecture had always been built around, matter and its negation forced to occupy the same space and paying the toll for it in miniature, contained collapses.

"It seems I have to take you seriously."

He shaped the leaking Aether into the ground around them, pressing it down until the air itself carried weight, and spikes began to rise out of that pressure, black and edgeless, formed the way his armor experiment had formed spears once before, out of raw covenant and raw contradiction rather than any borrowed soul's shape.

Dhaerin tried to dodge the first of them, the recursive array still running through his own strained current even as he moved, still drawing threads of stolen spirit out of Cozbi's mismanaged architecture with every cycle it completed. He forced his own Essence into his legs to buy himself speed his exhausted body no longer produced on its own, and for two spikes it was enough. He turned inside the first, took the second on a glancing angle that cost him a line of torn cloth and a shallower line of blood beneath it.

It was not enough for the third.

Cozbi's control at full extension, unhampered by the discipline of restraint he'd been fighting under all night, moved with a precision no borrowed soul had ever given him, and the third spike caught Dhaerin high across the ribs, driving him down into the churned earth hard enough that the impact alone might have ended a lesser fight there. He rolled with the strike rather than against it, some last reflex from a lifetime of study insisting that absorbing a blow badly was still better than not absorbing it at all, and came up onto one knee with blood running freely from his mouth now, no longer just a trickle.

*The array will finish its cycle whether I am conscious to see it or not,* Dhaerin thought, the pain arriving distant and secondary behind the thought's own clean logic. *My only remaining function tonight is to still be standing when it does.*

"You." He spat blood before he could finish the word cleanly, forcing himself upright by inches, the array at his fingertips still humming, still drawing, still refusing to release its hold on the seam it had found in Cozbi's borrowed architecture. "I will not allow myself to be defeated." His hand found the edge of his grimoire and held there, white-knuckled. "I cannot."

He planted his feet against the shaking ground and did not fall a second time.

Niran's presence weighed as the air around the Yukari in the room thickened. The light folded and flowed towards her, the Luminalis illuminated leading towards the aberrant signature of the Kurotana. There was heat, the wind shifted as the air redirected itself towards the doorway.

What felt like a simple step carried weight that distributed across the room, the burden and yet light filled the room once she entered. The other Yukari children sensed it clearly, the power was not of their creation.

"You're awake." She spoke, she had grown much since he last saw her. Haruki's eyes lightened as a soft breath left her as her eyes laid down on Niran.

"And already speaking on leaving. Foolishness, and you're well aware of that." She said, harsh, but true. "Your body will crumble on its own weight, you're still unstable. Despite how you feel, you need to rest. Let me handle this, you need to adjust to your new body." She said, her cloak extended out as it flowed behind her. Her gaze sharpened as there was this stern, strict, and sour gaze from her.

"I do not doubt your ability. I only doubt the knowledge we consider factual. Consider before acting, you are not impulsive." She spoke, her breath held for a moment as this more intimidating and yet misunderstood presence built around her.

Despite this, he could feel it. Her anxiety, her reason and observation. This was not arrogance, this was maturity, responsibility, and a soft tender empathy under it all.

"Cozbi has fled, but that doesn't mean he will not return. You are simply too valuable to leave unchecked. Therefore… I will take upon the responsibility you were given." She said, her eyes glared at him, then over to the other Yukari that surrounded him.

"I am sure you'd all understand, as much as I do. Niran is not leaving, not because he cannot, but because of the situation we sit in."

Niran let the silence hold a moment after she finished, the Cymorath reading everything true underneath the sternness of it before he trusted himself to answer without dismissing what she'd actually said.

"I don't want you anywhere near the six." He said it plainly, no cruelty in the delivery, though he understood exactly how it would land regardless. "Not because I doubt you. Because they'll manage perfectly without another variable added to a formation they've already spent years learning to read as one intelligence, and you have your own path to walk that doesn't run through mine tonight. Worry about the people who actually need you, Haruki. I'm not one of them right now."

Kasei's gaze moved to Haruki, unhurried, the Zenshi's rotating script settling into its familiar stillness. "He's right. We don't need your help." Her voice carried none of Sōma's edge, only flat, patient fact. "You have a great deal of your own road left to walk. Ours doesn't need a seventh set of footsteps tonight."

Niran rubbed one cheek with the edge of his index finger, the gesture doing nothing to disguise how much the conversation was costing him to hold upright.

"I'll do what needs doing." His voice stayed level even as his body argued otherwise. "I'll recover. I'll be out of this bed sooner than either of you expects, whether you like the timeline or not. And I will put Rengai back into his family's hands myself." He looked toward the door the Seven had already gone through. "That much isn't a debate I'm having twice."

He shifted his weight against the cot's edge, testing his legs beneath him without yet committing his full weight to standing, and did not look away from the door.

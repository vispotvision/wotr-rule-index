Laughter rolled across the battlefield before either clone spoke a word of it, the sound doubled and layered on itself in a way no single throat should have been able to produce, both halves of Cozbi finding the same note of delight at the same instant.

"Infinite Summoning Arts."

Both clones raised their hands together, and between each pair of palms a sphere of condensed Aether began to gather, dense enough that the space immediately around it lost its shape, distorting the mass of everything nearby the way a heat mirage distorts a horizon, except this distortion did not settle. It tore, in slow, deliberate increments, small fractures opening in the local architecture of reality itself and refusing to close again.

"Nisuke. Titan of the Iron Throne."

The ground did not so much shake as concede. Something vast pressed down into the world from a direction that had no name in ordinary geometry, an effigy assembled whole from every fragment of the true Titan's essence Cozbi had spent years harvesting one soul at a time, wearing Nisuke's shape the way a puppet wears the memory of the man it was carved to resemble. It was not the god. It was close enough to the god that the distinction stopped mattering to anyone standing beneath it.

Nearby Wellsprings answered before the summoning had fully resolved, energy throttling upward out of the earth in violent, complaining surges, the Plane of Aether twisting and folding around the effigy's presence until the very air took on weight. Every particulate of ash Rengai had spent the fight controlling, the drifting remnant of Impharous's carcass and the scorched grass and everything else he'd pulled into his own current, was seized and redirected without resistance, swept whole into the gravitational order the Titan's presence imposed on everything nearby. Rengai felt his own technique slip out of his hands mid-thought, ash he'd been holding through will alone simply ceasing to answer him, absorbed into a hierarchy his own strength had no standing to argue with.

Strength and Dominion. That was the Principle Nisuke had always represented, back when it walked as one of the Judger's own Divine Beasts rather than a soul-forged echo wearing its name. Cozbi had not built a copy of its power. He had built a copy of its right to rule whatever stood beneath it.

"Archonic Art." Cozbi's voice climbed, delighted, both halves of him speaking the words in perfect unison. "Dominion Strike."

Space sheared.

It did not tear the way cloth tears, along a single clean line. It folded inward from a dozen directions at once, the whole clearing bending toward a point that existed slightly ahead of where the Titan's foot came down, and the pressure released in that fold burned through every scrap of clothing not laced with the wearer's own Essence, cloth catching and turning to fine grey ash in the space of a single heartbeat. Even what survived scorched black at the seams.

The Titan was massive enough that its shoulder alone eclipsed the treeline behind it, and it moved with a speed that had no business belonging to something that size, closing the distance to Rengai in less time than his eye needed to track the motion cleanly.

Fear arrived in him first, pure and animal, older than any technique he'd ever trained into his body. But underneath the fear sat something else, something that had survived every fight he'd ever walked into afraid, and Rengai's hands found their way into a guard even as every instinct screamed that a guard would not be enough.

It wasn't.

The strike caught him full in the abdomen and lifted him bodily off the scorched ground, the impact driving the breath out of him so completely that the scream his body wanted to make never found the air to carry it. He rose past the height of the treeline still curled around the point of impact, vision white at the edges, ribs screaming a protest his mind was too stunned to fully register yet.

*This is what it feels like,* he thought, distant and strange even inside his own skull, *to finally meet something my father would have had to take seriously.*

The Titan's eyes, vast and gold-black, tracked him through the air with something that resembled resolve more than hunger, patient in the specific way of something that had already decided the outcome and was only now confirming the details.

Dhaerin's answer came from the ground below, a beam of raw energy screaming upward on a line drawn straight for the Titan's chest, every scrap of gathered current he had left behind that single working. It should have been enough to at least make the thing flinch.

"Resolve."

The word came out of something too large to have a mouth shaped like anything mortal, and the beam Dhaerin had thrown with everything remaining in him simply bent, pulled and warped around the Titan's frame, absorbed whole into whatever law the creature's presence had already written into the space around itself.

*I built that working to break through a soul,* Dhaerin thought, watching his own current fold uselessly around the Titan's frame, *not to argue with something that has already decided what the field is allowed to contain.*

The Titan's own wrath entered the field a half-second later, pressure rolling outward from its chest in a visible ring that flattened the scorched grass for a hundred paces in every direction and cracked the ground into radiating fault lines beneath both fighters' feet.

"RAGHHHHHHHHH!"

The roar arrived as a physical force before it finished arriving as sound, air itself shoved outward hard enough to strip the last standing embers off the ruined earth and send them scattering into the dark.

Rengai hit the ground a full breath later, back first, hard enough that the impact drove a fresh crater into the already-broken earth and emptied whatever air his lungs had managed to recover on the way down. He did not lie still. His hand found the flask still hanging from his hip by pure reflex, fingers closing around it before the rest of him had finished deciding it was still capable of moving at all.

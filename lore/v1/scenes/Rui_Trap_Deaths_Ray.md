"Do it now!"

Rui let herself become a pure medium of balance, opening her focus to every individual soul trapped inside the dragon's borrowed architecture, one after another, each signature distinct once she stopped hearing them as a single roaring chorus and started hearing them as people.

Then she felt something darker.

Not vicious. Something colder than vicious, something that made vicious look like an accident by comparison, and it made her stomach turn even as she wrenched herself back from Jinmu's shoulder, gasping, both hands pressed flat against the scorched ground to keep herself upright.

She had seen it for less than a second. A black smudge wearing the outline of a man, human in shape and nothing else, and something in her recognized the shape before her conscious mind could name what it recognized, and the recognition terrified her more than the shape itself did.

*It can't be.*

She swallowed hard, forced her breathing level, forced her hands to stop shaking long enough to be useful again.

"Nagare no Ito."

She bent the current of every thread she could reach toward Kurama, who was already braced and waiting, both arms raised, the harmonization the six of them had built together over a year of quiet practice finally called into its full shape.

"Yukari Hyakuza."

The working reached outward to trap the dragon's scattered essence signatures, to catch each stolen soul in its own isolated seat and disrupt the borrowed architecture holding them together one seam at a time. Kurama felt it take hold, felt the first dozen souls separate cleanly into their designated locks.

Then he felt something else answer from underneath all of it, patient, amused, and utterly unbothered by the working closing around it.

Cozbi's laughter rolled up through the dragon's inner essence, arriving from everywhere and nowhere at once, the specific laughter of a man watching a trap he had built himself finally spring shut on the people who thought they'd built it.

"How interesting." The voice carried no urgency, only delight. "It's a shame. You'll have to die for it."

Kasei's head snapped toward Kurama, the Zenshi reading the shape of the trap a half-second after it had already closed. "Kurama, pull out now! It's a trap!"

It was already too late.

The dragon began pulling essence from the surrounding environment in a violent, accelerating draw, grass and stone and standing air alike stripped of whatever spiritual residue they carried, and the sudden overload tore through the seats and locks the Yukari Hyakuza had only just finished seating. The harmonization fractured along every joint at once. Jinmu's spiritual essence detonated outward from his stomach in a burst that folded him double, blood spreading fast and dark across the front of his robes, his open resonance collapsing back into his own body with nowhere left to safely discharge. Rui went down under the same pressure wave a half-second later, driven flat against the ground hard enough to empty her lungs.

The dragon's eyes ignited red.

"Archonic Art." Cozbi's voice, riding the beast's throat like a passenger rather than a driver. "Death's Ray."

The maw split wide, and a beam of pure black flame tore free of it, aimed dead center at Kurama's chest, a column of concentrated annihilation that ate the distance between them in less time than sound needed to cross it.

"Damn it." Kurama threw both arms up, the layered light of every harmonization pattern he'd ever absorbed twisting into a barrier in front of him, dead Yukari technique stacked on dead Yukari technique in a desperate improvised wall.

It would not be enough. He could feel that much in the half-second he had left to feel anything.

Sōma was already moving, blade reforming mid-stride, cutting inward across the bowl at a dead sprint with every scrap of speed Danshi could strip from his own body's resistance to give him, angling the edge to meet the beam's line before it reached the barrier.

He was not going to make it in time.

# The War in the North III — Utopia

The cold didn't bother Emira like it did any other woman, it's harshness felt almost welcoming despite the tranquility of it. Her cheeks risen up into a pink tint, but she did not shiver. Her hands cupped as a soft snowflake fell down into her hands, she blew it softly, watching it drift off of her palms.

"Sodoku, our brothers, do you think they're watching down on us in Utopia?" She asked, her eyes looking up into the sky, despite the dry, cold, humid air pressing against her face. It wasn't strong as the storms at home, but never too soft for her skin. A soft exhale left her lips, she followed closely behind her brother, maintaining her distance softly.

"I would hope they are the ones guiding us back to Sonzai, but even still... I can't help but wonder... if he'll be there waiting for us." She said, the softness of her gaze didn't press a weight onto Sodoku's back like it would with his labors. There was something softer about this innocence, this wonder that lingered in the air between the both of them.

"How come I never see you look for him, Sodoku?" She asked, the ice underneath her feet drifting softly. She didn't halt momentum, she shifted it into a soft slide. The leather of her boots drifting across it like a flowing river, the trail of ice underneath her feet stopped when she stood in front of him, carried off to the side.

"You also speak of hoping to find him, and yet I never see you try to search for him. If you do hope so much to meet our brother again, then why continue to wait?" She asked him, leaning a bit closer to him as if she were demanding it, but the expression shifted the pressure off his shoulders. He knew better than to expect her sister to be speaking on demands, she had been too sweet for that.

"Does he... not wish to be found? Or do you not consider anymore... for searching for him?" She asked, her frozen frame now following beside at his steady and even pace. Her eyebrow was slightly raised, the question lingered in the air longer-than it needed to be, the weight was felt before the question had fulled dropped. Sodoku could feel it, even if he didn't truly see it through.

# Four element-inventory gaps — Naming Guide Amendment (2026-09-12)

Answers R20-CO-REMAINING_GAPS: the Mahuo element inventory, the Yukari
element inventory, the Elven branch-specific element inventories, and the
Beastkin soul-name inventory. Drafted by a background research agent, not
yet reviewed by Isaac. Matches the format of the two existing inventories
in sources/WOTR_Naming_Guide_Amendment.docx (Dawi: 30 elements,
two-element compounds; Satulagi/Moto: 25 elements, open-syllable), except
where noted below.

A glossary note up front, since this touches linguistics jargon: an
"element" is a short syllable or root with a fixed meaning, like Latin
"-ius" or the "Wolf-" in Wolfgang. A "compound" name is two elements stuck
together (Wolf + gang). "Morpheme" means the same thing as "element." A
"coda" is whatever sound closes out a syllable (the "-t" in "cat"). None
of this needs to be understood to use the inventories themselves.

## 1. Mahuo Element Inventory

Grounded in the Mahuo character cards (Kwon Mu-jin, Ara Min Mahuo, Cozbi
Mahuo — all three carry a recurring Ledger motif: "the Ledger-Keeper,"
"The Ledger Nobody Kept," "the Ledger-Prince") and the Ketsuen wiki page
(clan seat Sum-gol, "Breath Hollow"; Anima Spirare soul-breath theology).
R23-2-KOREAN_STRATUM confirms the structural facts: lineage name,
two-syllable given name with a shared generational syllable, a
"hollow-seat."

*Two-syllable given names built from a fixed inventory. One syllable is
the generation-syllable, shared by every child of one generation within a
line; the other is the personal syllable. Either syllable may occupy
either position — canon shows both "Ara Min" and "Mu-jin" orderings.
Family name attaches per the existing pattern; canon shows it both
preceding (Kwon Mu-jin) and following (Cozbi Mahuo) the given name, and
this inventory does not resolve which is "correct" — both are attested.*

**Breath & soul:** Sum (breath — attested), Hon (soul), Ryeo (echo of a soul), Yeong (spirit), Won (origin), Sae (new, first breath)
**Ledger & record:** Jang (chapter), Kye (bond, a tallied account), Bo (safeguard), Ye (a recorded rite), Chae (debt), Rok (record)
**House & clan-seat:** Gol (hollow, valley — attested), Ga (house), Ju (sovereign), Chang (prosperity), Dae (great), Ryeong (order)
**Precision & correction:** Jin (truth), Jeong (correct), Chak (exact), Ban (reflect), Kyeol (resolve), Seol (state plainly)
**Care & healing:** Eun (grace), Sun (goodness), Hwa (harmony), Yeon (bond, compassion), Bit (light), Soo (water, healing flow)

**Worked example:** a cohort sharing the generation-syllable *Jin* (truth):
elder daughter Jin+Won → **Jinwon** ("the truth of where we come from");
younger son Sae+Jin → **Saejin** ("a fresh truth"). Either could appear as
"Jinwon Mahuo" or, in the Kwon cadet branch, "Kwon Saejin."

**Flagged for Isaac:** which syllable is generational in the two attested
pairs (Ara/Min, Mu/Jin) isn't fixed by canon — left flexible rather than
asserted. Also unresolved: whether Kwon is a Mahuo cadet-branch surname or
an unrelated clan sharing the register.

## 2. Yukari Element Inventory

Grounded in The_Yukari_Bloodline.md (Senri, the Ketsumyōgan, "The
Telling" — the name is chosen at a coming-of-age rite; signature imagery
of silver-threading-through-dark, still water before a stone hits it, the
crow motif) and the Ketsuen page's attested etymology: Ketsuen = Ketsu
(thread-bound) + En (connection, bond). The amendment's own text glosses
Jinmu = divine + dream. R23-2-JAPONIC_STRATUM confirms full life-stage
slots, name-taboo on the living, renaming at promotion/vow/allegiance.
Only Ketsu, En, Jin, and Mu are canon-attested; every other element is
newly coined to fit the register.

*Two-element compounds. A full name carries up to five life-stage forms:
childhood name, adult name (taken at the Telling), calling-name (used by
intimates), true name (taboo — spoken only among family or before Senri's
stone), and posthumous name.*

**Thread & fate:** Ketsu (thread-bound — attested), En (bond — attested), Sen (a drawn line), Un (fate), Kizu (the mark a pulled thread leaves), Toki (the moment a thread draws taut)
**Crow & silence:** Karasu (crow — attested), Shizu (stillness), Yami (dark), Kage (shadow), Koe (a withheld voice), Aida (the interval between threads)
**Divine & dream:** Jin (divine — attested), Mu (dream — attested), Rei (spirit), Getsu (moon), Yoru (night), Asa (the hour before dawn)
**Water, stone & grief:** Sui (still water), Ishi (stone, resolve), Namida (tear, grief), Shizuku (the droplet before impact), Nagi (calm after grief), Fuchi (the deep pool grief settles into)
**Weight & office:** Kuro (black, formal-office color), Nushi (the one who holds a seat), Tsugu (the one who continues a line), Kari (a borrowed calling-name), Imi (taboo, the withheld true name), Oku (the deep name, spoken only at the Telling or at death)

**Worked example:** childhood name **Asagetsu** (Asa+Getsu, "dawn-moon");
adult name chosen at the Telling, **Kizuun** (Kizu+Un, "the mark fate
leaves"); calling-name **Kizu**; true name "Kizuun Yukari" spoken only in
Council, at a Proving, or before Senri's stone; posthumous name **Okuen**
(Oku+En, "the bond that goes deep").

**Flagged for Isaac:** the amendment says Yukari surnames are "compound
topographic" (matching Ketsuen's own place-naming), but no source says
whether individuals carry a second topographic surname alongside "Yukari"
or whether "Yukari" absorbed that function. Left open.

## 3. Elven Branch-Specific Element Inventories

The best-documented of the four — wiki/The Tongues of the Realms/Eressean
and Varrisak — The Tongue of the Vey-Elarin.md is already a full grammar
with real roots, particles, and affixes, functionally most of an element
inventory that hadn't been formatted as one. The Elven Peoples page
confirms five branches (Eressean, Varrisak, Drow, Eladrin, Echo Elves),
each theologically distinct, and calls for names "recognizably related
but structurally different" — exactly what shared roots plus
branch-specific affixes already deliver.

Restructured as one shared root inventory plus five branch-specific
naming rules, rather than Moto's flat category list, since that's how the
source grammar itself is organized and avoids pretending the five
branches don't share a language.

*Roots are disyllabic and open, codas restricted to a vowel, a liquid
(l/r), a nasal (m/n/ny), or th. Every element carries a grammatical pitch
contour (level/rising/falling).*

**Shared roots (attested):** elar (note/name/person), vey (kin), thaer (the kept thing, archive), sylva (growing ground), marin (attested root, meaning not on record — flagged rather than guessed), seral (winter), lyth (silence), dhae (the dream), ven (seen), tor (told), ma (to hold), ur (to witness), ie (the grant, the transfer)
**Shared affixes (attested):** -uren (validated), -ama (contingent, unwitnessed), -ieur (self-witnessed, Varrisak only), -ith (a small name/by-name), -eth (an office/rite-role), -aer (high, raised), -ak (foreign, outside the form), -in (collective), -en (genitive, "of"), -sae (in-window, timely), -lo (out-of-window, hasty/erroneous), zo (restatement clitic, Echo Elf)
**New branch-specific roots (one per branch, not previously attested):** Eressean *kalen* ("the unvarying rite"); Varrisak *sova* ("the vision sought alone"); Drow *morel* ("the ledger of what is owed"); Eladrin *faelo* ("the frost that holds rather than ends"); Echo Elves *verath* ("the term memorized exactly")

**Worked example, showing branch divergence on the same root:**
Eressean *Kalenuren* (Kalen, and the assembly stood for it); Varrisak
*Kalenieur* (held, self-witnessed by the forest); Drow *Morelsae* (a debt
within its term) vs. *Morello* (a debt in default); Eladrin *Faelo*
followed by a measured four-count silence (assent, no spoken suffix
needed); Echo Elf *Verath. Zo Verath.* (introduced, then ritually
restated).

**Flagged for Isaac:** (1) *marin* has no gloss anywhere in canon —
left unglossed rather than invented. (2) The amendment explicitly warns
against "generic fantasy Elf naming... gratuitous apostrophes," but
several already-published Volume V/VI Eressean-adjacent characters
(Vaelorian Theryn Aerethiel, Argen Vael'thar) lean into exactly that
style — this inventory, if adopted, will read differently from names
already on the page for some existing characters. Not resolved here.

## 4. Beastkin Soul-Name Inventory

The amendment's own anchor for Beastkin — Akan day-naming plus the
Name-Keeping tradition — is structurally different from the other three
(a fixed circumstance→name correspondence, not a combinable phoneme
pool), so this inventory is shaped in three linked layers rather than
Dawi/Moto's flat category list. wiki/The Five Beastkin Lineages.md
documents Name-Keeping concretely, but specifically for the
Fox-Spirit/Mishiro lineage ("the last thing that cannot be physically
removed... speaking one's name aloud in the presence of a witness"), not
the other four (Catkin, Lupine, Bearkin, Serpentkin) — the amendment
generalizes it to "Beastkin" broadly, and this draft follows that lead.
The "expectation" layer draws each lineage's definitive stat (Catkin =
Dexterity, Lupine = Harmonics/pack, Bearkin = Vitality, Fox-Spirit =
Harmonics/memory, Serpentkin = Gnosis).

*A soul-name has two parts spoken together: the circumstance-element
(what the birth was like, Akan-day-name style) and the
expectation-element (the attribute the child is charged to grow into).
The held-name is whichever fragment survives when everything else — home,
rank, kin — has been taken.*

**Circumstance (the birth):** Vel (born into stillness), Rhaka (born during storm/violence), Tuen (born in flight/escape), Miro (born under threat), Oda (born into plenty, safety), Kess (born far from kin, alone)
**Expectation (the attribute charged, lineage-flavored):** Zhiv (quickness — Catkin), Kaal (the pack that holds — Lupine), Dorn (the weight that stays — Bearkin), Sana (kept by scent and memory — Fox-Spirit), Riss (not forgotten, the coil that remembers — Serpentkin), Vantu (raw courage, unbound to any lineage)
**Held-name / Name-Keeping ritual vocabulary:** Ashe ("I remain"), Ndoto (witness, the one who receives the name), Fen (the last thing, what cannot be taken), Baru (spoken aloud), Tolo (the return, a name given back to a survivor), Chike (kept, held safe)

**Worked example:** a Bearkin child born into safety (**Oda**) charged
with steady endurance (**Dorn**) — soul-name **Oda-dorn**, recorded but
unused daily; an ordinary use-name serves in public life. Having later
lost home, rank, and kin, she performs the Name-Keeping: *"Fen Oda-dorn,
Baru"* ("the last thing, Oda-dorn, spoken aloud") before a witness, who
answers *"Ashe"* ("I have heard; you remain").

**Flagged for Isaac:** (1) Name-Keeping is documented specifically for
Fox-Spirit/Mishiro; whether the other four lineages actually practice it,
or the amendment overreached generalizing it, isn't confirmed — followed
the amendment's own framing rather than override it. (2) "Soul-name by
birth circumstance, held name as the last thing" could mean one name
under two descriptions, or two distinct names (birth-name + separate
secret name) — treated as the former; flagged in case the latter was
intended.

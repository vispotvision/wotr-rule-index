## The page body as proposed

# The Mahuo Family

> A human lineage of the Korean stratum, seated at **Sum-gol**, the Breath Hollow. It holds three things a crown cannot confer: **the soul's breath, the ancestral summoning art, and the capacity to carry more than one soul under one name.**
>
> The line answers to its own **elder structure** rather than to a crown. That structure has kept the habit of declining to put its costs in writing.
>
> *It is also the only venerable line in the four quarters whose worst practitioner is better documented than the line itself.*

---

## Standing

**Seat** · **Sum-gol**, *Breath Hollow*, the ancestral seat of the Mahuo bloodline, in the southern reach of the Sovereign Enclave of Ketsuen. Roughly 15,000 people, about a tenth of the kingdom.
**Governance** · A province that selects its own Warden, through processes combining Ketsuen's Proving model with **Mahuo lineage-seat traditions**. It reports primarily to the Mahuo elder structure rather than to the Council.
**Terms of incorporation** · Four months of negotiation, not petition. The Mahuo kept their internal governance, their Anima Spirare theology and their cultural autonomy, in exchange for network participation and Council representation.
> The term that cost Ketsuen most to grant: **Mahuo Wellspring knowledge constitutes expertise the Proving recognises as governance-qualifying on the same terms as ward-craft or military service.**
>
> Ketsuen's own record calls Sum-gol **the kingdom's most diplomatically delicate internal arrangement**, managed through *mutual respect, mutual suspicion, and the shared understanding that incorporation serves both parties better than the alternative.*
**Beyond the seat** · A court line, scholar-nobility, with households in the royal healing wards and a formally trained summoning lineage. Households also survive inside the **Elven Kingdom of Eresse**. Their identities are sealed.

---

## The Inheritance

Three faculties. Each has what it acts on, what it costs, and what it will not do. **None of them is martial.**

### Anima Spirare · the Soul's Breath

**What it acts on** · A body's own remembered rhythm. Wounds close **because the body remembers, under the practitioner's Essence, what being whole felt like.** Downstream of it sit healing, spiritual diagnosis, and lineage-reading.
**What it costs** · Ara Min Mahuo's card states the figure for the signature form. Both hands laid flat over the site. A clean wound closed in **40 to 90 seconds**, at **roughly 15% of reserve per serious wound.**
**What it cannot resolve** · **It cannot regrow what is fully gone.** Nor can it outpace an actively-inflicted wound faster than the practitioner can stabilise contact. Where it reaches that limit the line's answer is not force but passage: *easing a dying patient's fear rather than forcing false hope.*
> Ketsuen's assessment of what the faculty buys the kingdom is blunter than the Mahuo's own. **An understanding of soul-breath mechanics giving the kingdom capabilities in healing, spiritual diagnosis and lineage-reading that no amount of ward-craft could replicate.**

### Vocatia · the ancestral summoning art

**What it acts on** · The drafting and holding of called forms. The record names it **the Mahuo family's ancestral summoning art** and **the Mahuo summoning capacity**. It is held as household pattern rather than school instruction, and it is **trained rather than latent**, which is why a Mahuo who has never studied it still carries the aptitude.
**What it costs** · The art's one piece of infrastructure states its price without naming it. A Plane Tether is **paid once at anchoring, in something the practitioner does not get back**. *The Mahuo elder structure declines to specify what in writing.*
**What it cannot resolve** · A tether is fixed. It cannot be moved, and a campaign that shifts away from it has simply lost it. A tether cannot be defeated, only starved.

### The Soul-Population

**What it acts on** · Identity itself: **multiple inhabiting souls under one sovereign identity.** The record names this a lineage faculty, and names it **the structural basis that taught him to house other people's souls in the first place**. *Him* being the one practitioner who turned it into a crime.
**The tell** · **Occasional plural self-reference under strain, per Mahuo pattern.** Composure hides it. Load does not.
**What it cannot resolve** · **The capacity is not held by every branch.** One branch hosts a soul population directly. Another *kept the ancestral Vocatia aptitude but lost, generations back, whatever let the Aperture branch host a soul population directly.* Nothing on record restores it, and nothing on record explains the loss.

## The Hollow-Seats

A hollow-seat subdivides the lineage and distinguishes unrelated families sharing the surname. Four are attested. Two of them have no name.

| Seat | Branch | What is on record |
|---|---|---|
| **Sum-gol** · *Breath Hollow* | The lineage seat | The ancestral seat, a province of Ketsuen, reporting to the elder structure. The Anima Spirare theology is seated here |
| **Yeol-gol** · *Open Hollow* | **The Aperture branch** | Hosts a soul population directly, **held as household pattern rather than school instruction.** Seven generations attested, **the first four undocumented.** Generational element *Hae-* |
| *unnamed* | **The Teacher's branch** | Kept the Vocatia aptitude, lost the soul-population hosting. What is left is **a trained correspondence with the Continuum's own archived intention**, read through Anamnesis and given a body through Alchemetrica's four Draft operations |
| *unnamed, sealed* | **The Eressean households** | Mahou households residing within the Elven Kingdom of Eresse. **Their identities remain sealed and undisclosed until each household consents individually to be named or approached** |

## Naming

**Structure** · Lineage name, a **two-syllable given name carrying a generation-syllable shared across a cohort**, and a hollow-seat subdividing the line. The real anchor is Korean generational syllables. **Not** Chinese conventions, and **not** given-name-first Japanese ones.
**Inventory** · Thirty elements in five categories: breath and soul, Ledger and record, house and clan-seat, precision and correction, care and healing.
**Order** · The family name may precede or follow the given name. **Canon attests both.** The inventory does not resolve which is correct.
> **The motif the names keep returning to is the Ledger.** *The Ledger-Keeper. The Ledger Nobody Kept. The Ledger-Prince.* Three of the line name the same instrument, and the three of them mean entirely different things by it.
**Left open by the naming rulings themselves** · Which syllable is generational in the attested pairs, and **whether Kwon is a Mahuo cadet-branch surname or an unrelated clan sharing the register.**

## The Rites

> **Water.** The body is given to running water at the appropriate remove, weighted, **and the nine days are counted from the giving rather than from the death.**
Mahuo practice is **the only tradition in the four quarters that does not require the family to keep the body**, and Mahuo households consequently grieve differently and are frequently described by outsiders as cold, ***which they find funny.***

---

## The Ledger, and What It Cost the Line

Cozbi Mahuo is the best documented thing about this line. Every institution that touches the Mahuo now touches them through him first.

**The founding injury** · His people **ground into the mud beneath the Zettari and Moto's endless war**, unrecorded by any history book. Then a promise made to a dying brother: ***make them remember what we were, even if they never learn our names.***
**What he built from it** · Conscripted souls dressed as willing extension. The record calls his version of true invocation **a "hollow mirror"** of it.
**What it did to the line's standing** · Not fear. Something worse. The Mahuo became **dangerous without being protected**, which the Zettari Sovereign explained to the Teacher is **a far worse position than dangerous and feared outright.**
**What it did to the art** · He is **the reason the art is regulated.** Legion and Infinite practice combined, on a foundation of harvested soul-fragment rather than lawful draft.
**What it did to the world** · The war against him **collapsed the authority structures that maintained order between Nalūn and the frontier.** The eastern corridors are partially closed.
> One seam nobody has closed. **Cozbi Mahuo's numbered works return no reading at all** from Sanctum Lux's Oracle. The instrument weighs completed acts against a standard, and a thing that has never acted presents nothing to weigh. **The order has known about this seam for a decade** and cannot answer it without amending the reference.

## The Accord

The Accord is the line's standing settlement with the Zettari. Drafted by **Kwon Mu-jin, called the Teacher**, under the instruction and seal of **Dawn Guard, Sovereign of the Zettari**, on the night following the Sovereign's inquiry into Legion Summoning and the crimes of Cozbi Mahuo. Written in a single night, by lamplight, in a Zettari camp.

> **This document does not claim to speak for every surviving Mahou.** It speaks for those named within it, and for the terms under which further names may be added.

| Article | What it settles |
|---|---|
| **I · Recognition** | The Mahou line **shall no longer be treated as a danger without a name attached to its defense.** Recognised as a lineage capable of lawful practice, **distinct from the crimes of any single practitioner.** Not extended to shelter wrongdoing: a practitioner found replicating those methods **forfeits the protection of this Accord entirely** |
| **II · The Marriage** | A union proposed between Dawn Guard and **Ara Min Mahuo.** Her consent **may not be presumed, implied, or extracted through family obligation.** **Refusal shall not dissolve this Accord.** And **neither house may treat this marriage, once made, as ownership** |
| **III · Obligations** | A recognised representative to speak for the line's lawful practice. **A written code** distinguishing willing compact from conscription, contracted spirit from stolen soul. **An archive of lawful disciplines** opened to Zettari officers. **Teachers capable of certifying practitioners**, named and protected |
| **IV · The Eastern Question** | The Eressean households may be approached, under one restriction: sealed identities, individual consent. ***No peace shall be attempted at the cost of exposing families who survived through secrecy*** |
| **V · Review and Failure** | Binds both houses to honesty above comfort, each obligated to name the other's failure plainly. **An Accord that cannot survive its own honest review has no value to either house** |

**Who holds the office** · The representative's seat is the Teacher's *until Ara Min Mahuo's standing is settled.*
**What the line owes under Article III** · The surrender of its own. Any practitioner found practising those methods **shall be surrendered by the Mahuo themselves, rather than sheltered behind shared blood.**

## What Nobody Knows

**What the anchoring takes.** A Plane Tether is paid for once, in something the practitioner does not get back, and the elder structure will not put it in writing. The Anemoir refuse to discuss it at all. **Two institutions independently declining to answer the same question is the whole of the record.**
**What the hosting does to a host across a life.** The Aperture branch's capacity is attested. Its loss in the Teacher's branch is attested. *The mechanism of either is not.*
**Whether the lineage faculty and the conscription are one thing used two ways.** The record calls the soul-population a lineage trait. It also calls that trait the structural basis that taught one man to house other people's souls. **It does not say whether hosting willingly and conscripting are the same faculty.** The Accord's written code was commissioned to draw exactly that line, and the code itself is not on record.

## Open Questions

**1 · Mahuo against Mahou.** This page, the character cards and the Ketsuen record write **Mahuo**. The Accord writes **Mahou** throughout, and the same document is cited under both spellings in two different places. Recorded as `CONFLICTS.md` **C-045**. Not settled here.
**2 · Zarron Mahuo's lineage.** His card names him Mahuo and states a lineage that is not Mahuo. Recorded as `CONFLICTS.md` **C-046**. Not settled here.
**3 · Whether the Mahuo are an archaic bloodline in the parent page's own sense.** *The Archaic Bloodlines* states a Triune Equation of three lines, the Moto, the Zettari and the Yukari, and then links five children, the Mahuo and the Yuno among them. **Nothing on record says which reading is intended, and this page leaves it open.**
**4 · The naming flags the cards already carry.** Hiromi is a Japonic given name on a Korean-stratum lineage. Geturo fits no clear register. Lily is Northern English. **All three cards say "pending ruling", and this page closes none of them.**
**5 · The two flags the naming rulings left themselves.** Which syllable is generational in the attested pairs, and whether Kwon is a Mahuo cadet branch or an unrelated clan sharing the register.

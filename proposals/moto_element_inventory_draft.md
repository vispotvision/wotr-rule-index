# Moto Element Inventory — Japonic stratum (draft for ratification)

Drafted 2026-09-13 by Claude Code at Isaac's direction. Fills the gap left when the
Polynesian Satulagi/Moto inventory was struck (R21-1, R23-1): Moto naming is Japonic
(R23-2-JAPONIC_STRATUM) and had no element bank. Same shape as the Yukari inventory
(R37-2): thirty elements in five categories, two-element compounds, two to four
syllables, vowel-forward, no clusters (Naming Guide §5.1). Nothing here changes a
name already on the page.

**Attested, not coined:** the elements marked *attested* are lifted from names and
terms already in canon. Every other element and every gloss is **coined** for this
draft and is Isaac's to strike or rename.

## Slot structure (attested)

**Given name + Line name + Moto.** The surname is a fixed hereditary fact of the
bloodline (§5.1: "the family name is a fixed structural fact"). The Line sits
between given name and surname for the six landless Great Lines — *Artemis
**Amagiri** Moto*, *Souma **Byakuya** Moto* — and is omitted for Kōkan, the line
that holds the seat, and for a Moto whose Line is not the point of the sentence
(*Sodoku Moto*, *Muken Moto*, *Mizuki Moto*). The seven Lines are attested:
**Kōkan** (the crown, the seat), **Shirogane** (silver: the mint and the assay),
**Akagane** (copper: the forge), **Kurenai** (crimson hidden in black and ash),
**Byakuya** (near-white), **Tenrai** (gold, banner-heraldry), **Amagiri** (indigo,
mist-grey) — from *The Moto Bloodline — Material Culture* and Pack Twenty R20C-6.

## The thirty elements

- **Crown & seat:** Kō (crown — *attested*, Kōkan), Kan (the seat held — *attested*, Kōkan), Za (the chair itself), Ōmi (the one who is looked at), Tsukasa (office), Ren (the drilled, the tempered — *attested*, Kairen, gloss coined)
- **Metal & line:** Shiro (silver, white — *attested*, Shirogane), Aka (copper, red — *attested*, Akagane), Kuro (black — *attested*, Kurosetsu), Gane (worked metal — *attested*, the -gane Lines), Byaku (white — *attested*, Byakuya), Beni (the crimson kept hidden — *attested*, Kurenai)
- **Sky & weather:** Ten (heaven — *attested*, Tenrai), Rai (thunder — *attested*, Tenrai), Ama (rain, heaven — *attested*, Amagiri), Giri (mist; also the duty that binds — *attested*, Amagiri), Fū (wind — *attested*, Fūkoku), Yuki (snow — *attested*, Yukazuri)
- **Severance & exile:** Doku (alone, the severed one — *attested* in Sodoku and Rikudoku, gloss coined), Setsu (the cut — *attested*, Kurosetsu), Zuri (what is dragged behind — *attested*, Yukazuri), Muken (the unseen, the withheld — *attested* as a name, gloss coined), Bara (the root, the origin — *attested*, Bara Moto, gloss coined), Riku (the land left — *attested*, Rikudoku)
- **Water & the Works:** Mizu (water — *attested*, Mizuki), Ki (the tree, what stands — *attested*, Mizuki), Sou (the pair, the double — *attested*, Souma), Ma (the interval — *attested*, Souma), Kai (the sea, the open — *attested*, Kairen), Sai (the rite performed — *attested*, Saishiki)

## How a name is built

Two elements, either order, joined plainly; a coda consonant is doubled or dropped
by ear, never clustered. The given name states what the line expects or what the
child was born into; the Line name says which metal they answer to; Moto says
whose they are. Renaming at promotion, vow or change of allegiance is a feature of
this stratum (§6): the exile's renaming is a Moto story, not a Yukari one.

**Worked examples (coined):** *Kōsetsu* (Kō + Setsu: "the crown cut") for a Kōkan
child born after the seat's fall; *Shirokai Shirogane Moto* (silver + the open
sea: the assay-line's factor at a port); *Amayuki Amagiri Moto* (rain + snow);
*Tendoku* (heaven + alone: the one the sky left standing). Compare the attested
*Sodoku*, *Yukazuri*, *Mizuki*, *Byakuya*, *Kairen*, *Fūkoku*, *Rikudoku* — every
one of them reads as two of these elements.

## Flagged, not resolved

1. **Doku, Muken, Bara, Zuri** carry coined glosses on attested elements; if Isaac
   holds a different meaning for any of them, the gloss changes and the element
   stays.
2. **Tenrai** is both a Line and a house that was massacred (*Tenrai Moto*); the
   inventory keeps Ten and Rai as elements without ruling whether a child may still
   be given a Tenrai-marked name after the massacre.
3. **Artemis** and **Emira** are attested Moto given names outside this register —
   outliers under R23-5-OUTLIER_DEFINED, not evidence against it.
4. Whether Kōkan children carry "Kōkan" as a Line name in formal address (the way
   the landless Lines do) or never (because the seat is the name) is not settled
   by any page; the draft omits it, matching every attested Kōkan name.

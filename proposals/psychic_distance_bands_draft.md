# Psychic-distance bands for the major POV cast (2026-09-12)

Answers R4-OP-PSYCHIC_DISTANCE_BANDS. Drafted by a background research
agent, not yet reviewed by Isaac. Uses "narration register" rather than
"Band" as the field name — the Fracture of Worlds magic system already
uses "Band" for a different, unrelated stat (Coherence Band A–F), and the
two should never appear interchangeably in the same sentence.

The operative test remains R4-13-FID_CARVEOUT (live): a sentence is kept
if it's in the POV character's own idiom, could be wrong, and reveals the
character doing the interpreting; cut if it's in the narrator's neutral
analytic register and reveals nothing about anybody's interior. These
registers say *whose* idiom the narration should be running in for a
given character's POV scenes, which that rule depends on but never itself
assigned.

## Close register — narration fuses with the character's own idiom

- **Darius.** Sustained italicized first-person-flavored thought in his own procedural/martial idiom: *"So he keeps it. He keeps every strand of it. I will stand here with my hand shut and let a child hold the worst thing in his life in both arms, because it is his."* Even non-italic narration stays inside his trained forensic vocabulary. **Violation caution:** never let narration hand down a moral verdict on his choices that he himself doesn't voice in his own reluctant, anguished terms.
- **Aurelian.** His icy, ironic detachment *is* the fused idiom, not authorial commentary: *"How interesting... So this is what the Lamberts are. Nobody ever told me."* **Violation caution:** his overconfidence is frequently proven wrong — the prose must keep that irony inside his own idiom, never validate his certainty from outside it.
- **Verinus.** Dry, procedural, self-aware-of-his-own-decline idiom throughout. **Violation caution:** never state doctrinal/administrative significance in neutral Church register he wouldn't use himself.
- **Charles.** Guilt-soaked clerk's self-deprecation carries even the scene's metaphysics. **Violation caution:** never let him reason with a trained practitioner's crisp jargon — insight must keep arriving through clerical metaphor and self-doubt.
- **Sodoku Moto.** Scholarly Moto-dynasty vocabulary fuses into both dialogue and narration. **Violation caution:** per the (superseded but informative) gloss-rights note "Sodoku never" — narration must not append interpretive-summary sentences beyond his direct procedural read of a scene.
- **Niran Yukari.** Grief and perception both run through his own diagnostic apparatus. **Violation caution:** never state his emotional state in plain unfiltered terms — it must run through his clinical vocabulary.
- **Wren.** Terse, counted, laconic combat idiom. **Violation caution:** keep his interiority laconic/tactical — no lyrical or abstract language he wouldn't reach for.
- **Kwon Mu-jin.** Clinical, count-everything idiom persists even as his cognition degrades from injury. **Violation caution:** narration must show his read failing/lagging from inside his own halting perception, not narrate cleanly around it.

## Medium register

- **Rengai.** POV stays with him and never head-hops, but his emotion arrives via external behavioral description/simile rather than a fused personal idiom: *"something in his expression went very still, the specific stillness of a large animal deciding it had finished being patient."* No italicized direct-thought appears for him anywhere reviewed, unlike every close-register character above. **Violation caution:** don't retrofit dense first-person italics onto him inconsistently with his established flatter register, and don't let description cross into a verdict on his choices he hasn't reached himself.

## Distant/formal register

- **Cozbi Mahuo.** Dominated by an omniscient, epigrammatic narratorial voice describing him from outside ("It was elegant in the specific way corruption always is when it has been engineered rather than merely committed"), with only occasional aphoristic italics. This register is itself characterization — a being who narrates his own competence godlike and to no audience — matching the already-ratified "Cozbi unlimited" gloss-rights note ("his interiority is the horror"). **Violation caution:** this is the one register licensed for more narratorial explanation than others get, but it must stay inside Cozbi's own self-mythologizing frame — if the prose ever states his horror is horrifying in language he wouldn't use of himself, that's authorial intrusion, not distance.

# Firearms against practitioners, and the ceiling on enhanced weapons (2026-09-25)

Drafted with Isaac in conversation, 2026-09-25, not yet ratified. Part A is a
worked reading of rules already live in Fracture of Worlds II (Part Four's
Grade table, Part Six's Speed table) and The Apparatus of the Age; it adds no
new rule except the catching line (A.4), which is marked. Part B is new: the
canon has enchanted weapons (The Harmonic Arts, "Enchantment Analysis"),
alchemical weapon coatings (The Alchemical Index, Null-Edge Paste) and proofed
plate, but no rule on how far a weapon can be enhanced. Flags at the end.

## A. The plain firearm against a practitioner

### A.1 The reference gun

A Smith & Wesson Model 3 revolver in .44 Russian, unenhanced: no Essence in the
gun, the round or the shooter's working. Figures are real-world ballistics, not
canon:

| Round | Mass | Muzzle velocity | Muzzle energy |
|---|---|---|---|
| .44 Russian, 246 gr lead ball | ~15.9 g | ~235 m/s | ~440 J |

At 10 m the ball arrives in about 43 ms. Its energy sits in the **E-Grade**
attack-output band (300 J – 15 kJ, Part Four); a D-Grade practitioner's own
strike (15 kJ and up) carries far more. The gun is not dangerous because it is
strong. It is dangerous because it is fast, cheap and needs no reserve.

### A.2 What the canon already says

- Fracture of Worlds II, Part Six: "At D-Grade the practitioner can react to
  gunfire but cannot outrun it. At B-Grade they can engage opponents relying on
  firearms or conventional projectiles without significant reactive
  disadvantage."
- The Apparatus of the Age, The Musket: "A C-Grade practitioner can react to
  gunfire and cannot outrun it." (A musket ball is faster than this revolver's,
  about 400–500 m/s, so the two lines agree.)
- The Apparatus of the Age, The Pistol: the weapon "that has killed the most
  practitioners above Stage V", because it is drawn at a distance where
  reaction is not the deciding variable.
- The Speed table is the stat-only ceiling, "without technique amplification";
  Alacrity and Celerity techniques push past it within overchannel risk.

### A.3 Dodging, by Speed Grade (reading of the live table)

The Grade that decides is the target's **Speed** (the Dexterity Sub-Stats that
feed it, chiefly Celerity, Reflex and Sequence), not their overall rank.

| Grade | Travel speed | Reaction | Against a 235 m/s ball |
|---|---|---|---|
| E | 60–150 m/s | 40–80 ms | Does not register it in time. |
| D | 150–340 m/s | 15–40 ms | Registers it and may begin to turn. It hits. |
| **C** | 340–500 m/s | 5–15 ms | **Dodges.** The first Grade whose floor outruns the ball; reacts within its first metre or two. |
| B | 510–1,372 m/s | 1–5 ms | Dodges with room to spare. |

Against a faster round (a musket, or anything at 400 m/s and up) a C-Grade is
back to reacting without outrunning it, and **B-Grade** is where dodging is
reliable, which is the Part Six line.

### A.4 Catching (new, proposed)

No rule names a Grade for catching a projectile. Proposed:

> **Catching a projectile needs a Speed Grade whose Travel speed floor is at
> least twice the projectile's velocity.** Outrunning a ball is not the same as
> closing a hand on a centimetre-wide object at that speed.

For the reference revolver: **B-Grade** (510 m/s floor, more than twice
235 m/s). Against a musket ball: B-Grade at the upper end, A-Grade reliably.
A technique amplification can bring a high-D or low-C practitioner there early,
at the overchannel risk Part Six already names.

### A.5 Being hit

The canon gives no force a D-Grade body shrugs off, so none is set here. A
practitioner hit by plain shot takes it as a hard human body does: head,
heart or a major vessel can kill; a limb is a serious wound. What changes that
is the target's **Resilience** Grade, proofed plate, a warding technique, or a
relic such as Mkono wa Miti (which grounds common shot up to roughly the E-to-D
force band). At the table, check Resilience separately from Speed.

## B. The ceiling on enhanced weapons (new, proposed)

The unenhanced weapon needs no floor: it performs as its physics. The question
is how far an enhancement can take it. Existing canon points at a ceiling: the
Staff of the Verdant Pulse fails against a structure "which a Stage VIII ceiling
cannot exceed regardless of the tool in hand."

### B.1 The ceiling

> **An enhanced weapon performs no higher than the Grade of whoever put the
> Essence into it.** Channelled through the weapon in the moment: the
> wielder's Grade. Enchanted, glyphed or alchemically worked beforehand: the
> maker's Grade at the time of making.

A D-Grade smith's enchanted blade hits no harder than a D-Grade can. A Stage
VIII master's blade in a novice's hand carries the master's ceiling for what
the enchantment itself does, and the novice's for everything the novice adds.

### B.2 Two dials

An enhancement moves one or both of two things. The gun or bow underneath does
not change.

1. **Speed.** Only an enhancement built to drive the projectile faster moves
   muzzle velocity, and it tops out at the enhancer's **Attack Speed** (Part
   Six).
2. **The hit.** Hardness, density, or a working that goes off on contact,
   without changing the speed. It tops out at the enhancer's **Attack output**
   band (Part Four).

Speed, applied to the reference revolver (16 g ball):

| Enhancer's Grade | Attack Speed | Fastest ball | Energy at that speed |
|---|---|---|---|
| none | — | ~235 m/s | ~440 J |
| D | Mach 0.5–1.0 | ~340 m/s | ~920 J |
| C | Mach 1.0–2.0 | ~680 m/s | ~3.7 kJ |
| B | Mach 2.0–5.0 | ~1,700 m/s | ~23 kJ |

A faster ball hits harder on its own (energy rises with the square of speed);
the hit dial adds on top, within the output band. Dodging an enhanced round is
read off A.3 at the round's **actual** velocity, not the shooter's Grade.

### B.3 Cost per round

> **An enhanced round or arrow is paid for per shot and consumed when fired,**
> from the reserve of whoever enhanced it (the wielder if channelled, the maker
> if prepared in advance).

This keeps the canon's economics true ("Shot is chemically cheap and a working
is not", The Apparatus of the Age): an enhanced volley is a practitioner's
reserve spent in advance, not a free upgrade to a line of muskets. A prepared
enhancement also keeps the maker's Duration (The Harmonic Arts reads a
weapon's "Duration authorization"), so stockpiles go stale.

### B.4 Every enhancement is findable

Per The Harmonic Arts, an enchanted weapon radiates its working's signature and
a Harmonist reads its alignment, Duration, Boundary limits and integrity
without touching it; Interference Lock can unravel a working mid-flight. So a
speed-enhanced round is countered by reaction (Speed Grade) or a Harmonist, a
hit-enhanced round by proofed plate, Resilience or a ward. Strong, with a
counter someone can go and find.

## Flags

1. **A.4's "twice the velocity" is a design number,** not derived from source.
   Isaac may prefer a flat Grade (for example "B-Grade catches plain shot") over
   a velocity rule.
2. **B.1 for a relic made by a being above the wielder's Stage:** as written the
   enchantment carries the maker's ceiling. If Isaac wants the wielder's
   Temperance ceiling to cap even a borrowed relic (the Staff line can be read
   that way too), B.1 needs a second sentence.
3. **B.2's speed ceiling uses Attack Speed, not Travel speed,** because a
   projectile is a technique's execution, not the body moving. Worth a yes.
4. **Real-world ballistics** in A.1 and B.2 are estimates for a period
   revolver; a different reference gun changes the numbers, not the rules.

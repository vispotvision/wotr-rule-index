  Aegor Zettari, Body-Path dominant. Passives include **Material Covenant
  Resonance** ("Where others see objects, the Zettari feels agreements.
  Stone, steel, flesh, and space all whisper the same truth: remain.") —
  the closest thing already on record to a forge-adjacent hook. Existing

  **Stone Witness Oath** ("A vow is not considered complete unless
  something older than the speaker hears it"), Ancestor Communion.
- `wiki/The Zettari/The Paths.md` — the Zettari ascension plane; its sands
  are "condensed lineage memory"; entry restricted to the
  lineage-acknowledged ("unauthorized entities attempting entry suffer
  immediate metaphysical collapse").
- The Vow Clause (`wiki/The Magic System/The Practitioner's Field Manual —
  The Forge.md`) — the setting's one general, ratified mechanism for how
  oaths work magically: a vow sworn into the Lattice or one's own Crystal
  becomes a standing law; breaking it is a Shear Break. The Dawi's
  Cask-Oath is a specific cultural instance of this general rule; any
  Zettari equivalent should be presented as a Vow Clause application too,
  not new magic.

## What the Cask-Oath Pitch actually covers (the pattern mirrored)

Three layers: (1) a governing cultural philosophy — Barrel Dwarves answer
"what makes a thing trustworthy?" with patience: cask it, age it; (2) a
magical enforcement mechanic — the Great Cask-Oath makes a spoken word
structurally reliable, breaking it costs the Crystal's own Coherence (a
Vow Clause instance; other Dawi branches have their own instantiation of
the same pattern); (3) a commercial/material instantiation in the Standing
Index's T3 goods table — Cask-Oath Pitch, Class II / Fidelity A / Carry
18, "cask-aged not less than nine years, in the hold that will use it,"
with an unexplained, dryly-comic location-dependence mystery.

## The draft pitch: Agano Sand and the Witnessed Temper

*(Terms glossed on first use.)*

### 1. Governing philosophy

Where Barrel Dwarves answer "what makes a thing trustworthy?" with time —
cask it, age it, don't rush it — the Zettari answer with **witness and
inheritance**. A Zettari-forged object isn't aged into reliability. It's
declared reliable, once, in front of a witness ancient enough to matter,
and from that moment the Zettari's own Material Covenant Resonance holds
the declaration in place. The Dawi philosophy is brewed endurance. The
Zettari philosophy is witnessed law.

### 2. The material: Agano Sand

*Agano* is a Swahili-derived word meaning "covenant" or "testament,"
chosen to match the naming register the Zettari already use in practice
(Zuberi, Ma'Kovu, Kafa-Karim, Sarmad, Sukun), not invented from nothing —
see C-006 on why this doesn't match the stated five-strata rule.

The Paths (the Zettari ascension plane) are documented as containing sand
that is "condensed lineage memory." Agano Sand is a working measure of
that sand, folded into the quench when a weapon or piece of plate is
finished. Where Dawi pitch is aged for years in a fixed location, Agano
Sand doesn't need years — it already carries the memory of the whole
bloodline's history. The cost isn't time. The cost is access: only beings
"acknowledged by the lineage itself" may enter the Paths without
triggering immediate metaphysical collapse, so a smith cannot simply go
get this material — it has to come from, or through, someone the Paths
already recognize.

### 3. The rite: the Witnessed Temper

A Vow Clause working (the setting's general, ratified oath-magic
mechanism), not a new kind of magic. At the quench, the smith — or the
sovereign the piece is being made for — speaks a bound claim over the
object in front of a Stone Witness (a ruin, an ancestral hall, a
Path-linked relic; per the existing Stone Witness Oath, it must be
"something older than the speaker"). That claim becomes a standing law the
object runs, exactly the way a spoken vow becomes a standing law a Crystal
runs.

**What it does:** a Witnessed-Tempered item inherits a scaled-down version
of the bloodline's own Ancestral Dominion Frame ("repeated impact against
them becomes less effective as their body 'learns' the force"). Every
significant strain the item survives *in the hands of whoever it was
sworn to* makes it measurably more resistant to that same kind of stress
going forward. This is not indefinite hardening; it plateaus, the way the
bloodline's own combat-maturity does.

**What breaks it:** if the item changes hands outside the terms of the
oath, the object's accrued memory doesn't transfer. This isn't the Dawi's
Crystal-Coherence self-punishment (a cost paid by the oath-breaker's own
soul); it's closer to the "Weight of the Blood" curse already on the books
for the bloodline ("failure feels ancestral rather than personal"). The
object simply stops recognizing the new holder as a party the law was made
for — in the worst documented cases, Physical Plane Authority's "hold
shape" effect reverses for that bearer specifically, and the piece becomes
*more* failure-prone in their hands than an ordinary equivalent.

### 5. How this differs from the Dawi's Cask-Oath (not a reskin)

| Axis | Dawi Cask-Oath | Zettari Witnessed Temper |
|---|---|---|
| Governing metaphor | Brewed patience | Witnessed inheritance |
| Where the magic comes from | Alchemical Essence typology, a chemistry problem | Material Covenant Resonance + Ancestral Dominion Frame, a lineage/law problem |
| The Vow Clause instance | Breaking it costs the oath-breaker's own Crystal Coherence | Breaking it costs the object's recognition of the new holder |
| The material | Pitch/resin, cured in a fixed hold | Sand from an ascension plane, access-gated rather than time-gated |
| The unexplained mystery | Won't cure outside the hold it was sworn in | Won't "listen" to a claim spoken anywhere but before something older than the speaker |
| Who can make one | Any Barrel Dwarf smith with nine years and a hold | Only smiths sponsored by a lineage-acknowledged Zettari |
| Cross-culture parallel | Tallow Clans' Verse-Bound Coherence (sung, not vesseled) | None yet — deliberately left open as a future gap |

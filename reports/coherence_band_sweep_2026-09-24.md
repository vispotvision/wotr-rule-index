# The lettered Coherence Band sweep — 2026-09-24 (C-033, R43-4)

Isaac ruled C-033 on 2026-09-24 (the WAR-2 questionnaire, option "full sweep"):
the retired lettered Coherence Band gives way to the Tier of Standing for the
page's Stage wherever it survives outside the Magic System pages, and η is kept
as written. The Stage→Tier table is `Fracture of Worlds — The Living System/II.
Grades, Gates and Thresholds (Parts Four–Ten).md:59-71`. Written by the sweep
script; the edits went to Notion and return through the hourly sync.

- **215 pages, 399 occurrences** replaced by the scripted pass.

## Left as written, and why

The ruling replaces the Band where a page uses it as *that page's rank reading*.
It does not rewrite a passage that is about the retired scale itself — its own η
ranges, its Class or Crystal correlates, a dated ratification record, or rank-as-
identity prose. Rewriting those would change what they say rather than which scale
they name. Each is listed here so Isaac can rule on any of them separately.

# Cost-taxonomy audit — 2026-09-12

Findings-only pass across the major cast, requested by Isaac after Pack
Four flagged this as unverified and "likely to fail." No sheets were
edited; this is the record of what needs fixing.

## Standing cost-taxonomy rules checked against

- **R12-1-PACK_SEVEN_SURVIVORS** (live): "The economy is still a hard rail:
  power is finite, spent, and the spending shows on the page or it did not
  happen... A fully explained technique that costs nothing is worse than
  an unexplained one."
- **R7-5-COST_AUDIT** (live): "sixty percent of existing technique entries
  fail the test that overuse-strains-him is not a cost. Every failure needs
  a real category from the wheel: something the character still carries
  when nobody is fighting."
- **R12-3-DESIGN_CHAIN_RETURNS** (live): the ten-field Design Chain
  (Trigger, Function, Mechanism, Numerical Effect, Target Response,
  Consequence, Limitation, Weakness, Cost, Counterplay).
- **R12-3-CARD_PLUS_CHAIN** (live): every technique keeps a five-line
  summary card — Effect, Cost, Limit, Counter, What nobody knows.
- **R5-A-SKELETON_UNCHANGED** (live): costs denominated in EU at the unit
  level, not left vague.
- **R14-2-ESTIMATE_MARKING** (live): an unsheeted value is a marked
  estimate inside the documented range, never invented.

No rule mandates a universal minimum-cost floor per Stage or a blanket
recharge/cooldown requirement — the operative standard is narrower: every
named technique needs a real, specified Cost, not "overuse strains him."

## Violations found

- **Cozbi Mahuo** (`wiki/Volume I — Character Cards/Cozbi Mahuo.md`) — the
  worst finding. His technique table has no Cost or Limit column at all;
  none of his five named techniques (Antithesis, Root Severance, Pneuma
  Unravel Strike, Nisuke Titan of the Iron Throne, Merged Form · Dragon)
  states a cost. Direct violation of R12-1 and R12-3.
- **Yoko Mishiro** — same shape, lower stakes: no Cost column at all,
  including on her Signature technique ("Sustained Fox-Fire"). Mitigated
  by all five being non-combat/passive, but structurally the same failure.
- **Kwon Mu-jin** — "Calcination Draft" and "Distillatio Seal" assert
  "high personal cost" / "sometimes severe" without ever naming what the
  cost actually is — fails R7-5's "real category," not just an adjective.

## Clean

Aurelian Prudentius Custos Clausorum, Verinus VII, Sodoku Moto, Niran
Yukari, Rengai Zettai, and Gimbzo all carry full Design-Chain costs
(explicit EU, permanent/cumulative physical or emotional costs). Hild Ice
(Stark) has no active techniques to check — her only power is explicitly
untrained and involuntary on her own sheet.

## No sheet exists at all — costs never checkable

Four major-cast characters by scene count have no character card anywhere
in the project:

- **Darius** — 19 scenes, the #2 most-scened character in the corpus.
- **Charles** (Lambert) — 13 scenes.
- **Dhaerin** — 10 scenes, named as Cozbi's own Foil.
- **Wren** (Greymane) — 8 scenes.

This is arguably the bigger finding: the cost-taxonomy question can't even
be checked for four major POV characters because they have no sheet.

# Fracture of Worlds — The Living System: edits required by R38-1, R38-2, R39-1 to R39-6

Drafted 2026-09-13 for Isaac's approval. Nothing in Notion or the mirror has been touched.
Every "Block (verbatim, current)" is quoted from the mirror under
`wiki/Fracture of Worlds — The Living System/` as it stood at drafting (line
numbers refer to those files). "Becomes" is the proposed replacement or
insertion, in the page's own register, carrying the ruling in its own words
where they fit. The rulings are the 2026-09-13 entry in `RULINGS.md`, carried
as rows in `rules/doc-stat-system-rulings.yaml` (R39) and
`rules/doc-stat-gates-and-recost-ruling.yaml` (R38); the reasoning is
`CONFLICTS.md` C-009 to C-014.

Edits are numbered across the whole report. Where a ruling's named locus is
the page, the edit is marked **primary**; where the same sentence recurs on
another page and would contradict the ruling if left alone, the edit is marked
**consequential** (same substance, Isaac may take or leave it). Edits that
change wording only for consistency and carry no new rule are marked
**optional**.

## Page: `I. Levels, Experience and Stat Points (Parts One–Three).md`

### Edit 1 — primary

- **Page:** I. Levels, Experience and Stat Points (Parts One–Three).md
- **Rule:** R39-1-CANON_STAT_MODEL
- **Block (verbatim, current)** (Part Three, Allocation Per Level, line 65):
  > Every level grants a fixed allotment of Stat Points. **Points are spent on Sub-Stats only.** A Primary Stat is never purchased and holds no value of its own. It is the combined total of its eight Sub-Stats, recalculated the moment any one of them moves. Vitality is what Fortitude, Absorption, Constitution and the other five add up to. Dominion is what Radius, Sovereignty, Sense and the rest add up to. The Crystal does not record a category. It records where the pressure went, and the category is the arithmetic afterward.
- **Becomes:** insert after "…and the category is the arithmetic afterward.":
  > This is the model every sheet is built on. A sheet that records a Primary as a figure bought in its own right, a second layer of spend laid over the Sub-Stats, is wrong by this page and is re-costed to this shape: points on the Sub-Stats only, the Primary as the total of its eight, the Grade read off the mean.
- **Why:** R39-1 (closes C-014): "points are spent on Sub-Stats only; a Primary is the total of its eight Sub-Stats and its Grade is read off the mean. The 125 sheets that buy Primaries as a second layer are wrong and are re-costed to this shape." Operation `extends`, locus "Part Three, Stat Points".

### Edit 4 — primary

- **Page:** I. Levels, Experience and Stat Points (Parts One–Three).md
- **Rule:** R39-4-MAX_GRADE_LETTER_BINDS
- **Block (verbatim, current)** (Part Three, Temperance Ceilings, line 87):
  > **The ceiling binds the Sub-Stat.** No Sub-Stat may exceed the ceiling imposed by the character's current Temperance Stage, regardless of how many points are available to spend. Stage V caps a Sub-Stat at 350. Stage XIII caps a Sub-Stat at 1,200. The pool generates the points. The Crystal determines whether those points can be held. Points earned and refused by the ceiling are not lost; they sit as pressure until a Threshold enlarges the vessel.
- **Becomes:**
  > **The ceiling binds the Sub-Stat.** No Sub-Stat may exceed the ceiling imposed by the character's current Temperance Stage, regardless of how many points are available to spend. The Stage's Max Grade letter binds allocation: a Sub-Stat may not be allocated past the top of that Grade's bracket. Where the Stage's numeric ceiling sits above the bracket, at Stages V, VII, IX and XI, the band between them is the instability zone, reachable only under strain, never by allocation. Stage V caps allocation at 275, B-Grade, and 276 to 350 is its instability zone. Stage XIII caps a Sub-Stat at 1,200. The pool generates the points. The Crystal determines whether those points can be held. Points earned and refused by the ceiling are not lost; they sit as pressure until a Threshold enlarges the vessel.
- **Why:** R39-4 (closes C-011): "The Max Grade letter binds: a Sub-Stat may not exceed the top of the Stage's Max Grade bracket (Stage V 275, VII 400, IX 550, XI 725); the band from there up to the Stage's numeric ceiling is the flagged instability zone, reachable only under strain, never by allocation." The sentence "Stage V caps a Sub-Stat at 350" is the one that contradicted the letter and is the reason this paragraph is the task's named locus.

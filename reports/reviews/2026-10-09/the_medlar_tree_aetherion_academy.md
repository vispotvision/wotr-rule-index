## Canon contradictions

- “Madeleine Ault had spoken eleven days in the lord's house with Draycott's patience in her voice and Strom's habit of counting.” The vessel’s identity comes from its maker’s Core, but **Furveus** performed this commission. [Volume the Second, chapter VII](</home/oridon/wotr-rule-index/wiki/The Alftian Codex/Volume the Second.md>) says: “It was one working, unentered, done by a Master of the Circle who sealed it with his own hand.” Furveus then names his fee and liability. Draycott’s framing and Strom’s later scaling do not make their Cores the source of Madeleine’s voice.
- “The lord's wife … had died of the lung-sickness two years before he climbed the stair.” The preceding sentence fixes that visit in winter 694. This conflates two commissions: the Necrocursica manuscript began in winter 694–695 and finished in spring 696; Madeleine’s attempt appears later in **Volume II**, where Neros says her husband “has been writing to Draycott for months” and she died “two years ago”. Reconcile those histories before retaining “The lord's lady had lain twenty years”. The [timeline entry](</home/oridon/wotr-rule-index/scenes/TIMELINE.md:257>) explicitly leaves these dates unresolved.
- “Malphas had written the reason into the margin of his own manuscript, and Mu-jin had copied it into the sealed edition…” This blurs Malphas’s warning with Mu-jin’s discovery. [Volume the Third, chapter XXII](</home/oridon/wotr-rule-index/wiki/The Alftian Codex/Volume the Third.md:251>) explicitly calls the maker-Core explanation “My gloss of the Aphorism”; **R61-87-APHORISM_DOCTRINE_AND_GLOSS** requires crediting Mu-jin. Malphas’s copied footnote is the warning beginning “Never claim it is the soul.”
- “*Wellspring:* the residue at its floor in each Wellspring, which per C-107 thins to a floor and never passes it.” **C-107 concerns Aetheric Residue left by workings at locations**, not individual Temperance deposits inside Wellsprings. [The Necrocursica](</home/oridon/wotr-rule-index/wiki/The Alftian Codex/The Necrocursica.md:93>) places that permanent floor under the Parunic Echo carried by a working’s Aetheric Residue. The resurrection plan needs a separate explanation for identifying and recovering returned Temperance; C-107 does not provide one.

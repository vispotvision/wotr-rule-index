## Canon contradictions

- **“Halberd’s took the key off my bench while I slept…”** ([scene, line 109](/home/oridon/wotr-rule-index/scenes/brakh.md:109)) conflicts with **“Then she reached under the bench and dropped the brass key into his bleeding hand.”** ([line 217](/home/oridon/wotr-rule-index/scenes/brakh.md:217)). Establish a separate accounting convention or have Grisk demonstrate the distinction; familiarity with Kharven tallies alone does not establish Vaross literacy.

## Weakest prose

- **“The faceted eyes went out of focus in fifty directions and the awl fell and rang on the boards.”** Jem previously cannot tell where those eyes look; now the narration claims an even finer observation. Rewrite: “Her head sagged, and the awl slipped from her fingers and rang on the boards.”

- **“And in the goblin’s hand the jar went down from a hum to a mutter to a sound like someone thinking, and the milk inside it lay down at the bottom and was still.”** “Someone thinking” supplies no audible distinction at the moment sound measures danger. Rewrite: “The jar’s hum weakened until Jem could hear her breathing over it, and the pale stuff settled at the bottom.”

- **“Anybody would have seen it; it was like watching someone through water.”** The universal claim interrupts Jem’s particular observation, and water suggests distortion rather than delayed movement. Rewrite: “Jem saw the opening before her raised hand had finished moving.”

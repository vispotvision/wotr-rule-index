# Summon register notes

The workroom notes that used to sit at the foot of the public summon entries, moved here on 2026-11-05 as they stood. Family names in them are the old sort; the register now goes by kind.

## Obrenkael · The Mule

**Kwon Hae-ryu is an origination, ruled.** Canon establishes that Mu-jin's branch of the Mahuo line lost the Aperture branch's capacity to host a soul population directly, which means the Aperture branch exists and is undescribed. Hae-ryu, the Yeol-gol seat, and the naming-right structure are all built into that documented gap rather than lifted from anything. She was a pitch. Ruled 2026-09-12: the Aperture branch is open to origination, there is no undisclosed plan for it, and Hae-ryu, the Yeol-gol seat, and the naming-right structure stand as written.

**Name built through the four passes.** *Obrenkael* is High Parunic compounding on attested glyph morphemes, no clusters that violate the register, three syllables, tested aloud beside Cozbi, Mu-jin, Sodoku, Rengai. *Kwon Hae-ryu of Yeol-gol* follows the Mahuo Korean institutional structure: lineage name, two-syllable given name with generational element, hollow-seat on the established Sum-gol pattern with a suffix that means something. *Yeol-gol*, Open Hollow, for the Aperture branch. The generational element *Hae-* stands as canon. Ruled 2026-09-12.

**EU figures ruled.** Call cost is given as 9% of reserve, and the absolute number now stands behind it. Ruled 2026-09-12: Kwon Hae-ryu's EU Reserve is 90,000,000 EU, at Level 355, Stage X Realization, Band IV, and the Call Cost at 9% is approximately 8,100,000 EU. The figure is a log-linear interpolation on Level between the project's fixed anchors, Ara Min at Level 250, roughly 4,200,000, and Borin Ironheart at Level 378, 180,000,000 estimated, and the Borin-to-Mu-jin slope, 850,000,000 at Level 430, confirms it. `Fracture_of_Worlds.pdf` establishes that EU scales with Temperance Stage and is governed by Tempering Yield and gives no Band-by-Band reserve table, so the interpolation is the method and not a source. Borin's figure keeps its estimate marking and Raga's entry, which says only that a Stage X summoner spends most of what he has, stays a loose neighbor; neither is contradicted by this reserve.

**Codex amendment taken.** Deadweight runs [Abys] Deep and [Th] Foundation, and neither was attested at Oblation in the Master Glyph Index. [Abys] carried Abyntheus, Fulguria, Electromagnetism. [Th] carries Petralon, Vectoria, Mechanics. Ruled 2026-09-12: [Abys] Deep's attested Wellspring list is extended to include Oblation and its attested Family list to include Fluxia, on the reasoning that a directional undertow of sub-Realm pressure is what a psychopomp's road *is*, and that Abyntheus and Oblation are both already pressure-grammar Wellsprings under different Archons. [Th] Foundation stays cross-family and Deadweight remains the roster's one cross-family chain. The extension is recorded under Amendments on the Master Glyph Index page. Deadweight stands as drafted and is not rebuilt.

**The skull count contradiction is deliberate, ruled.** *Uncounted* stays a Trait in Section II, which makes it a mechanical property rather than an atmospheric one, and Pack Seventeen's Trait rule (R17-5-TRAIT_SCOPE) requires a Trait to name the Lattice property it alters, so the line now does: the property is the accretion's count, and the alteration is that no observation of it returns a number a second observation confirms, while the load reads identically to everyone. Ruled 2026-09-12: the Trait stands, the Lattice property is named, and no real number of skulls is ever fixed. The register's position that nothing should be true about the real number is now the ruling rather than a position.

**Unresolved against the existing roster.** Mu-jin's Book of Summons establishes his branch as the one that lost this aptitude, and The Index is described as World-Tethered-adjacent, borrowing anchor stability from a location. The Mule borrows nothing and owns its own Crystal. Those two entries sitting in the same register invite a direct comparison that has not been written. Whether Mu-jin has ever met an Aperture-branch practitioner, and what he thinks about a cousin-line that can do the thing his line was built to regret losing, is an open scene.

**Bench objection status ruled.** The Arbitration Division objection recorded in Section I, that an entity accruing strength from its summoner's failures cannot be described as bound, is the strongest legal hook this entry has. Ruled 2026-09-12: the Bench of Attribution hears it, in a three-chair session, the Doctrine chair reading the Wellspring-authorization question, whether the Wellspring invoked recognizes the invoker as an authorized channel, and Arbitration sitting as both objector and Ruling chair. The objection is entered as a Reservation, which withholds a standing pending determination, and the determination is not written; what the Bench eventually says is open. The Praetoria retcon changes nothing here: no page ties the Praetoria to any Accord body, and the Bench is the body hearing it.

## Haelvorn · Housecat

> **RULED, Isaac, 2026-09-12: one Wellspring, not two, and the frost is not it.** `Fracture_of_Worlds.pdf` lists **Cymorath**, the Air of Ascent, under **Fulguria**: temperance through freedom, motion as devotion, strengthening Dexterity Celerity and Gnosis Cartography. `The_Master_Codex.xlsx`'s **Crymorath** entry, spelled with the r, was the duplicate — struck. Cymorath is the canonical spelling and portfolio going forward, and every wiki page carrying the misspelling has been swept to match (2026-09-12). **Resolved, same day:** the portfolio clash the spelling fix exposed is closed. The struck entry's own content — the two frost glyphs, **[FrTh] FrostThrone** (sovereign stasis over a Domain) and **[ObsG] Obsidian-Glacier** (frozen-dark plates surging through terrain) — and everything the downstream pages built on it as a working frost Wellspring, Draven Kael Vorrick's techniques, Sodoku Moto's Arctic Lion configuration, the Material Ledger's cold rows, is re-homed on Vohrin, the Abyssal Depths Titan that FOW III already names as the thermal-extraction signature: Family Caloria, Physics Domain Thermodynamics, Titan-keyed. Cymorath is the Air of Ascent exactly as Fracture of Worlds gives it, freedom and motion, Dexterity Celerity and Gnosis Cartography and Perception, and nothing else. Nothing on the FOW canon pages changes. This entry drafted its own cold out of Caloria and touched neither, and the ruling says that was the right shape: there is no dedicated frost Wellspring, cold is heat run backward, and the pages that had been calling it Cymorath now call it Vohrin.
**No cold Wellspring exists, and the entry treats that as intentional.** FOW defines Caloria as heat and its absence and the transfer between them, thermodynamics, where creation and destruction run the same current. Read straight, that means WOTR has no ice magic and never did, only fire pointed the other way, and this entry is built on that reading all the way down: every glyph in the chain is a fire glyph and the summoner's harmonization is the Trial Flame. If the intended canon is that a dedicated frost Wellspring exists and is simply unwritten, say so and the entry needs re-drafting from the ground rather than patching. Ruled 2026-09-12, see above: the reading holds. The cold lives under Caloria on Vohrin's Titan key, and no dedicated frost Wellspring is coming.

**Rimsang Halfyear is an origination and closes a flagged gap.** `THE_DAWI_PEOPLES_REVISED.docx` states that naming registers for the five new branches are first-pass constructions, provisional until tested against a second named character each. Norbu Waymark was the Passwardens' first. Rimsang Halfyear is the second, built through the Naming Guide's four passes on the branch's Sherpa route-keeping anchor: given name in the established two-syllable register, Guild byname earned at certification rather than at birth, and the byname mildly cutting in origin per the guide's byname permission, since a route that exists four months a year is half a route. If Isaac accepts her, the Passwarden register can be marked tested and that line comes out of the Flagged Gaps section.

**Her Exuroth harmonization is deliberately atypical and needs confirming.** The branch profile is Basilithe primary, Fixatio strong secondary, Judicium accessible. Exuroth is not on that list. Unlike the High Line, who are written as *rejecting* certain Wellsprings outright, the Passwarden entry states a typical profile rather than a lock, so an individual harmonization outside it should be legal. The entry treats the anomaly as characterization rather than error, and the whole construct depends on it. If branch profiles are meant as locks, this falls over.

**EU figures unverified, again.** Call cost given as 6% of reserve. No Band-by-Band reserve table exists in FOW and Mu-jin's 850,000,000 at Level 430 remains the only fixed anchor, so no absolute EU number appears anywhere in this entry. Needs a reserve figure set for Rimsang before it can carry one.

**[Grv] Gravity is drafted on the secondary, not the primary.** Its Codex row is Basilithe, Materia, Material Science, and Basilithe is this construct's declared secondary Wellspring, so the assignment is legal as written and no amendment is pitched. Noted only so a future reader does not flag it the way Deadweight's [Abys] was flagged on the Mule's entry before the Codex amendment of 2026-09-12 took it.

**The Accord's dampening guidance is a plot device with no ruling behind it.** Section XI records that the Division's standard field answer to this entry kills the summoner and that the guidance has gone out to companies who were not told. That is either a deliberate Arbitration Division position, an act of institutional carelessness, or something one named officer decided, and the three readings produce very different stories. Isaac's call on which.

**Four abilities rather than three.** The blank template shows three toggles. This entry runs four because the Intake, the Span and the Floor are all non-offensive and the Rings are the only thing in the kit that hurts anybody, and cutting any of the four leaves the construct either toothless or purposeless. If the register wants a hard three-ability ceiling, the Floor folds into the Intake's Target Response with real losses.

**Comparison to the Mule is invited and unwritten.** Two Native forms in the same register, one Consenting and one Sovereign, one accreting and one Refined, one that carries the dead and one that gets people across alive, and both of them costing their summoner a body part they will not get back. Whether Rimsang Halfyear and Kwon Hae-ryu have ever been in a room is an open question and the answer is a good scene.

## Vaelmorn · The Standing Grave

**Summoner unassigned.** He is the entry that least needs one. **Consenting bond, Stage VII in practice, forty million EU: any hedge practitioner in bog country can reach him**, and the horror of the entry is that the person who calls him is usually somebody's grandmother. If Isaac wants a named summoner the interesting choice is somebody with no standing at all.

**The woman at his flank has no name in this entry, and the omission is the mechanic.** Her name is one of the things he is carrying and neither she nor anyone who knew her can produce it. **If Isaac wants her named, naming her breaks that**, and it should be a deliberate choice rather than a completion of a blank field. She needs a four-pass name either way if she is ever going to appear on the page.

**Elven and Sylvaar naming register not verified.** *Vaelmorn* is built on the dithematic compounding pattern the Naming Guide documents, using the [Vael] glyph-root, and *Old Sylvaar* is taken from the Bestiary's existing usage on the Raiju entry. **The wider Sylvaar phonology has not been checked and may already be defined somewhere I have not read.**

**The Withering correlation.** Sightings rising with falling ambient density for seventy years. **This is a pitch and a large one.** It hands Vaelmorn a role in the world's central slow catastrophe rather than leaving him as a regional oddity, and it can be cut cleanly by deleting one line in section I and one in section X.

**Danger Tier T6 is contested in-fiction and in the workroom.** He has no kill and no offensive capability, and he is arguably the most damaging entity in the register. **The entry has been written to hold both and Isaac may want a ruling.**

**Numbers verified against source.** Temperance Stages, Crystal Tiers, Aether Classes, Summon Tiers and Stability facets from the Fracture of Worlds text and the Spirit Summoning Arts entry. **Glyph letters, meanings, Archon attributions, Wellspring assignments, Family and Physics Domain pulled from The Master Codex glyph index.** The Verdantia corruption vector, Endless Spring, is quoted canon and is the spine of the whole entry. **EU figures and all field measurements are estimates and need confirmation.**

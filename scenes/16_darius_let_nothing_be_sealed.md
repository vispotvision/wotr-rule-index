# Let Nothing Be Sealed

*The Vaeloris quarter. No interval.*

---

The Attraction Force came down out of the Plane of Fate.

It arrived as blooms. Not a beam and not a fall: it opened in the air over the ruined quarter in a scattered sequence of expanding flowers of light, dozens and then hundreds, each one blossoming at its own rate and separating as it opened into its component wavelengths, so that the whole vault over the street stood full of shells of colour nested inside each other with the red furthest out and the violet at the core.

They were not lighting anything. That was the tell and Darius had it in under a second: an object under a light of that intensity throws a shadow, and nothing in that street was throwing one, because the blooms were not photons and had never been. They were Attraction Force presenting at a wavelength the eye had decided to accept, and the separation into colours was the Plane resolving into its own components on the way through the boundary between strata.

The common tongue calls that the smell of it. Trackers use the word for Residue and it is the same phenomenon at a different order of magnitude, and there was enough of it standing over the Vaeloris quarter to be readable from the coast.

Then they turned.

Every bloom in four hundred feet twisted inward at the same moment and ran down the same line, and the line ended in the head of the Hammer in Darius's right hand, and the light went into it and did not come out anywhere.

*That is the pan.*

*He is not making light. He is not making anything. He has laid a Scale under a street and every good thing left in this quarter is being redistributed toward the lighter side of it, and the lighter side is me.*

His own body began to tessellate.

He watched it happen on the back of his own left hand and had no framework for it whatsoever. The Lattice inside him was expressing outward through the flesh, and it was doing it as a tiling, plane-filling, a regular partition of the whole surface of him into cells that met edge to edge with no gap and no overlap anywhere. Gold at the boundaries. The skin between them still skin. It ran up the forearm and across the chest and over the shoulders and down into the coat, and where the pattern reached the coat the coat tessellated too.

*A lattice under uniform load does that. It has to. Load a crystalline structure evenly and it will express its own periodicity, because periodicity is the cheapest way for a structure under stress to be, and the pattern you see on the outside is the arrangement that was always inside.*

*I have never been loaded uniformly before. Nothing has ever been able to load me.*

*And the Aetheric Density in this street is past the point where the entry stops giving numbers and starts giving warnings. Saturation floods a region faster than it can disperse and it produces Fracture Events in everyone standing in it, including whoever caused it, and there is a child ninety feet behind me.*

Haruki came past him on the left.

He got an arm out. He had it up and across before she was level with him and it was already too late by a considerable margin, and what closed on the space where she had been was a fistful of nothing and a great deal of light.

"Do not."

It came out of him with more in it than he had intended, and it did no good, and he did not have the tempo to spend on doing it twice.

*Nobody in this street is going to be told anything by me today. I have been in it eleven minutes and I have given three instructions and every one of them has been correct and every one of them has been ignored, and I am beginning to understand what my whole life in that Order was actually made of.*

---

Aurelian Prudentius had not stopped speaking.

"Sit verax. Sit parcus."

Let it be truthful. Let it be frugal.

He was six inches into the flags by then. The stone had closed around both boots and was still taking him, and he had not looked down at it once, and the Scale under the street had sorted every loose thing in the quarter into two drifts and the drift on his own side was rising.

"Spolietur omni praesidio."

Let him be stripped of every defence.

*And that is the Direction role,* Darius thought, because the four roles are the first thing anybody at the Order learns and the last thing most of them think about. *Authorization was Urion, and he took it in the first clause. Boundary is this street and he set it when he laid the pans. Direction is now, and it is aimed, and it is aimed correctly.*

"Veni, lux Urionis. Librator, restitue stateram."

Come, light of Urion. Balancer, restore the balance.

And then the Primate of the Holy Sea said the fourth thing, and Darius Ignatius Sanctus Sanctorum, who had spent twenty-one years writing the Order's Glyphica standards, understood what he was listening to about half a second before the working proved it.

"Nihil sigilletur."

Let nothing be sealed.

*No.*

*No, you cannot leave it open. Sealing is not decoration. Authorization failing, Boundary failing, Direction failing all produce a working that does not happen, and every one of those is survivable and I have written the recovery procedure for all three.*

*Sealing failing produces a working that does not stop.*

*The law goes on obeying itself after the operator has left the room. It is in the standards under my own name, at paragraph nine, and I put it there.*

He knew why the man had done it. That was the worst part and it arrived immediately and without any effort at all. A Scale that seals stops balancing. Fixatio closes a working and holds it in the state it was closed in, and a closed Scale is a Scale that has stopped weighing, and Aurelian Prudentius had opened the Direction toward his allies and then declined to close the sentence, because closing it would have set a limit on how much he was permitted to give.

There is no limit now. There is only the far pan and how far it can go down.

And there is one further consequence and it took Darius another second to reach it, and when he reached it he stopped being angry with the man and started being frightened for him. An unsealed working leaves an open wound in the local Aether field, and the Continuum does not leave those. It closes them on its own terms, at its own hour, by whatever route it finds cheapest, and nobody has ever been able to predict which route that will be, and the route is very often the practitioner.

---

The darkness answered.

It came off the transformed shape at the end of the street in a single expanding front and it was not aimed at the Hammer or at the Works or at the light coming down out of the Plane. It arrived at Darius as weight, and weight does not care what a man is made of.

He went back.

Sixty feet, through the ruin of two frontages that had already been taken to the footings once, and the tessellation across his chest broke at the impact and reassembled while he was still travelling. He came off the far wall and got a boot down and skidded eleven yards through rubble on the heel and the side of his knee, and the disc in his neck reported at a level he was no longer able to file.

He twisted.

That was all of it and it was not a technique. A body coming out of a slide with a mass in its right hand and a bad neck has one honest option, which is to let the mass keep going and put the whole spine into the rotation and get the head of the thing into the ground before the ground stops being available.

He slammed the Hammer down.

And he opened his hand a fraction on the way in.

---

The street did not shatter.

The light came up out of it.

Every ounce of Attraction Force that had gone down into the Hammer since the blooms opened, everything Aurelian's Scale had moved into his pan, all of it went out of the head at the moment of contact and into the substrate, and the substrate could not hold it.

Fragments of light came up out of the flags in sheets.

They shovelled up along the fault lines the first release had already made, tearing the paving upward in slabs and then through the slabs, and where they came through, the stone burned. Actually burned. Material combustion, in a street where nothing combustible had been standing for six minutes, because the arriving flux crossed the ignition threshold of every mineral in the road and there is no substance that does not burn when the flux is high enough.

The quarter went white from the ground up, and the burning came with a smell Darius had met exactly twice, both times in a laboratory, both times behind glass: the smell of stone taken past the point where it can go on being stone, which has no honest name in the common tongue and which the alchemists call the fourth stage and will not describe further to anyone outside the Work.

And then the light began to travel.

It came out of the burning ground in lines. Coherent, collimated, four dozen of them at first and then a hundred, laid flat across the ruined street at every height, and they did not fan and did not scatter and did not obey the ordinary rules by which light leaves a fire.

They went to Aurelian.

They arrived at him, along paths that had already been decided before the light left the ground, converging on one point in a street four hundred feet wide with the accuracy of water finding a drain.

*The pan.*

*Of course they go to him. It is a balance and I have just put a very great deal on my own side of it and a balance does not accept that. The Scale is doing exactly what a Scale does, which is to bring the two sides back level, and the only route it has is through the man holding it.*

Every one of those lines arrived at the Primate of the Holy Sea, in the chest and the arms and the face and the open hands, and Darius Ignatius Sanctus Sanctorum watched a hundred collimated lines of burning ground converge on a man who was already six inches into the flags, and could not reach him, and was sixty feet away with a bad neck.

---

Aurelian did not shield.

He turned his palms up into it.

And Darius understood, watching, that the man was not enduring it. He was *taking* it. The lines went into him and did not go through him and did not come out the other side, and the Primate's whole frame lit from the inside, and he stood in the middle of the convergence with his head up and his hands open and did the one thing that had been the entire content of him for forty years before this morning, which was to decide.

Will. That is the whole of what a Turgia working runs on once the syntax is laid. The glyphs establish the boundary condition and the law does the work and the practitioner supplies the one thing no glyph can carry, which is the sustained intention that the sentence go on meaning what it meant when it was spoken.

Aurelian Prudentius had more of that than any man alive and had been relieved of every reason to spend it carefully.

That was the thing Darius had understood in the street six minutes ago and had put down because there was no time for it. The Order had spent four hundred years selecting for men whose will could hold a sentence open under load, and had built the entire structure of its offices around the assumption that a man like that would eventually stop, because eventually it would hurt him. Every safeguard the Order owns is downstream of that assumption. Every one of them.

There is no article, in eleven hundred years of Statute, written to govern a man who will not stop.

He wove.

The light came into him at a hundred points and left him in three, and Darius could see the three and could see where each of them ended, and the sight of the third one put a cold through him that the ice at the narrows had not managed.

The first went to Darius. The tessellation across his body took it and the load went up and the pattern tightened and the cells got smaller.

The second went across the street to where Haruki had gone, and Darius did not follow it and had no business following it.

The third went behind them both, low across the rubble, into four streets of standing crimson-black, and into a ten-year-old boy holding a dead man's head against his chest.

*Synergia requires Attraction Layer compatibility.*

*That is the first line of the entry. You cannot combine with a practitioner whose Attraction Layer will not recognise yours, and there is no version in which that child's Layer recognises anything at all this morning, because there is nothing left on it that has not curdled.*

*He is not feeding an ally.*

*He is feeding Obsession Force, and Obsession Force is Attraction Force with the recognition gone out of it, and it is close enough to the real thing to accept a transfer and it will not use it the way the real thing would.*

*And he has left the Sealing open, so it will not stop.*

He had time for one further reading and he took it and wished immediately that he had not. The lines going into Aurelian and the three going out of him were not the same quantity. The difference was going somewhere, and it was not going into the ground, and it was not going into the air, and the only remaining place for it was down whatever the man was anchored to.

*He is anchored to something. He does not know it and I can see it and there is no version of this street in which I get to tell him.*

Darius got his boot under him in the burning street and came up out of the rubble with the Hammer in both hands and the whole quarter alight behind him, and started back up the road toward the two of them.

# The Hand of the Judger

*The Vaeloris quarter. The crater. No interval.*

---

Darius had never once intended to touch Fate.

That was the whole of the division and he had settled it in the ash months ago, in a conversation Gimbzo had allowed to run one sentence and then closed. Three planes. Material, and Soul, and Fate. A man reaches into the ones he is built for and leaves the third to whoever holds the organ, and Darius held two of the three and had spent his life pretending the count did not bother him.

Material was the Hammer and the roads. Soul was Spirit on the rhythm. He drew both out of the thing at the end of the street and held them, Order keeping the geometry continuous and Spirit keeping hold of the beat, and he did not reach for Fate, because Fate was not his to reach and had never been.

It was Aurelian's, and it had always been the part of the arrangement Darius could not do and had made his peace with not doing, and standing in a slowed crater watching the other man work he understood for the first time in eleven years that the peace had been a lie he told well.

The Primate of the Holy Sea was a Mahuo of the true line, and the true line carries an organ.

The word is literal. It is a structure, seated where other men have nothing, that does the one thing no glyph can do from outside: it takes hold of a concept, a spirit, a bond, a continuity, and forms a pure connection within Fate directly, without the Parunic scaffolding every other practitioner has to build first. Where a Turgist assembles a Wordform Chain and hopes the Continuum reads it, a true Mahuo reaches past the sentence and touches the thing the sentence was trying to describe.

Aurelian reached past the street.

He took hold of the reality the two of them were standing in, the whole local weave of Material and Soul and the Fate binding them together, and he pulled it into the Plane of Fate. Nothing was laid over the quarter. The quarter itself went inward, one plane deeper, into the stratum where lasting change is written.

And in the Plane of Fate, where the rate of the world is a written quantity and not a fixed law, he took hold of the tick and slowed it.

The Aetheric Density in the folded space did not obey the numbers any instrument keeps, because the instruments were built for a reality running at the ordinary rate and this one was not. Every working still active in the crater stretched with the tick. The Scale weighed more slowly. The frost formed more slowly. The blooms came down more slowly, and had more time to be bright, and the light in the crater thickened toward the colour of poured honey.

*He is taking time down.*

*He is not stopping it. He is slowing the rate at which one moment becomes the next, because in there the rate is a variable and out here it is a wall, and he has pulled us all in past the wall.*

Everything began to break down inwardly. Slowly. The rubble in the air came to a near-halt and hung. The fire on the flags stopped roaring and began to burn at a crawl, each flame lengthening as its own tempo stretched. The light coming down out of the blooms thickened, because light at a slowed tick is light with more time to be dense in, and the whole crater filled with a golden suspension in which nothing was quite finished happening.

Aurelian did not stop weaving. His voice, when it came, arrived slow and deep and stretched, and Darius heard it a syllable at a time.

"Sit lux capta."

Let the light be seized.

"Surgat manus Iudicis, et liget omnes iniquitates has."

Let the hand of the Judger rise, and bind all these iniquities.

"Continuitas non nisi thronus potestatis eius est."

Continuity is nothing but a throne of his power.

---

The Ascension began.

Everything in the crater that was not a person began to turn, and to rise, and to become golden symbols. The rubble, the frost, the dead Void matter, the drifting motes of the light: all of it lifted off its position and resolved into letters, the Parunic hand and older hands beneath it, characters from the beginning of the language when it still described the laws instead of citing them.

Darius had seen the diagrams in the Sanctum's oldest archive and had assumed they were stylised. They were not. This is what it looks like.

They came up in a lattice of filaments. Thousands of fine golden lines standing in the air, each one strung with glyphs like beads on wire, radiating out from the seated shape of the Regent in a vast spreading fan, the whole architecture of a bound reality expressed as a script that hung in the slowed light and did not fall.

Then the lines closed.

They wrapped the transformed thing at the centre, and there was no force anywhere in it. A binding at the level Aurelian was working does not need force, because it is not holding a body still, it is writing into Fate that this thing is bound, and a thing that Fate has recorded as bound is bound until the record is contested. The golden chains of glyphs crossed the great dark shape shoulder to shoulder and wrist to wrist and wound down the length of every tendril, and where they crossed, the letters brightened, because a boundary condition had been imposed on the law and the law was now doing what the boundary said.

*That is the Boundary role and the Direction role at once, and he is writing them straight into Fate with the organ instead of speaking them, and there is no Wordform Chain for the Continuum to misread because there is no sentence. There is only the thing itself, said in the only language that was never a language.*

*And there is no Sealing on it either, and there was never going to be, and I have stopped being able to be frightened by that, because the whole of what is left of Aurelian Prudentius is now aimed at one point and there is nothing in four worlds I can do to make him aim less.*

Continuity was the throne. That was the clause and Darius understood it now, watching the chains go on. The Regent's entire method was the editing of continuity, the moving of doors while the roads stayed, the removal of the reason a force reaches its object. And Aurelian had not fought that. He had declared continuity to be the Judger's own seat, the ground the office stands on, and in the Plane of Fate a declaration by a true Mahuo about whose continuity it is is not an opinion. It is an entry.

The hand rose.

Out of the golden script, out of the bound and slowed centre of the crater, an arm formed, and then arms, huge, gilt, wrought out of the same letters, and they came up around the Regent the way the chains had, palms open and fingers spread, dozens of them, a fan of enormous gold hands rising from the floor of a reality that had been pulled into Fate and slowed to a crawl. Every one of them was made of the beginning of the law. Every one of them was reaching.

---

Darius had finished.

While the light seized and the hand rose, he had been doing the thing he had been building since the roll, standing in the slowed crater with Ignis at the bottom of its arc and the tessellation across his body gone to white grains, and he had held the two Works and let the third man take Fate and he had made himself ready.

The golden arms came up around him too. They had not come to bind him. They had come to join.

*Synergia. Two practitioners, and the Attraction Layer has to recognise the pairing. Mine recognises his. It always has. He put a chain over Charles's head and I signed second and the three of us were one office split across two men, and one of us is a wall in the Void and one of us is nine inches into the flags weighing a god, and I am the last of the three standing on his own feet.*

He raised the Hammer, and the shoulder that had gone in the roll took the weight and reported it and he made it hold, because there would be time to have a body later or there would not be a later, and either way the arithmetic came out the same.

"The Judger's will is absolute. His fervor is unmistakable."

He said it into the slowed air and the slowed air carried it deep and stretched, and the gold arms turned toward him, and the whispers began, under everything, in a language older than the letters, the sound the office makes when it is about to be present.

Urion.

The office has no voice, and Verinus had told a Curia that and had been right his whole life. What came instead was weight, arriving in a place where weight is information, and it came down the golden arms and into the head of Ignis, and the whispers of it ran along every filament in the crater and set the letters ringing.

"Allow me the Hammer of the Judger to strike with the power of all."

---

Then Nadi.

It is a Vectoria discipline and it is Corporis, worked entirely inside the body, and it does one thing: it fits more than one action into a window that has room for one. The wind-up, the travel, the recovery, the intermediate states of a strike, all skipped, so that intent becomes completion without the positions in between, and a body that has learned it can perform a hundred actions in the time another body performs one. It has nothing of speed in it, and no Nadi practitioner is any faster across a room than anyone else. It is action density. A body that has learned it stands still and fills time, and the sub-stat that decides how many actions fit inside one window is Sequence, and Sequence is the one thing Darius had in quantity, because a man who spent forty years writing does nothing else well but put one exact thing after another exact thing without the gap between them widening.

Darius had it at a low multiplier. He was a fifty-six-year-old paladin with a broken disc and a dead shoulder and it had never been his primary art. Nadi is capped by Ardency, because every action in the window shares the window's force, and a hundred strikes at a hundredth of the force apiece is a hundred taps.

But he was not spending his own Ardency.

The Hammer of the Judger had just been fed the power of all, down the golden arms, out of an office that had arrived in the crater. The window did not divide his force across the multiplier. It divided the office's, and the office's force did not have a bottom that a body could reach.

There was a failure mode and he knew it and accepted it. Over-refinement strips so much intermediate motion from an action that the body loses the feedback it needs to correct mid-strike, and the fist arrives where the calculation said the target would be, and the target has moved. But the target was a pulled-in centre held still by Spirit and roaded by Order and bound by the hand of the Judger and slowed in the tick, and it was not going to be anywhere other than where the calculation said. For the first time in the history of the discipline the failure mode had been engineered out by four other men's work.

Nadi doubled.

Each movement became two, and two became four, and the multiplier climbed, and the down-stroke of Ignis was no longer one strike. It was the same strike performed again and again inside the slowed interval, each iteration landing on the pulled-in centre of the Crevice, each one carrying the undivided fervor of the Judger, each one skipping its own wind-up and recovery so that the blows arrived with no gap between them at all, a single continuous descending pressure that was in truth a hundred separate strikes and then a thousand, stacked in a window Aurelian had stretched wide enough to hold them.

And Darius became fire.

That was the last of it and he had not planned it and did not resist it. The Ardency of the office running through a body that could not couple to anything external found the one channel it had, which was Darius himself, and it came out of him as heat because there was too much of it to come out as anything cleaner. He wreathed. The tessellation across him burned white and then went to flame, and the gold arms held their fan around him, and the whispers of Urion rose into screams, because the thing being struck was the stretched continuity of the Void itself, its own nonexistence dragged into a reality that had been pulled into Fate and compacted down to where it could be hit.

He had one thought left that was entirely his own, arriving in the stretched interval with all the time in the world to be thought, and it was not about the Regent and it was not about the office. It was about a man he had met once for eleven minutes across a table and had not shaken the hand of, who had put a chain over an archbishop's head in a cold chapel and signed first, and who was, at this moment, a wall in the Void holding a door shut with the whole of himself. Charles had carried and could not spend. Aurelian could weigh and had been emptied of the reason to stop. And Darius could strike and had never been able to receive. Three men, one office, and the office had needed all three broken in the exact way each of them was broken, and Darius wondered, in the last of the slowed second, whether the Judger had built the office around the men or found the men and named it after what they already were, and decided he would never know, and struck.

The Hammer came down a thousand times in the space of once.

Into the Crevice. Into the one rhythm the Regent could not discard, held by Spirit, roaded by Order, bound by the hand of the Judger, weighed by the Scale, slowed in the tick, and struck by the power of all, again and again and again inside a single slowed heartbeat, while a man made of the office's own fire stood in the middle of a crater that had been folded one plane deeper and drove the whole of it down.

### Rulings

1. **The true Mahuo organ** is on your line and it is a large canon object: a physical structure that touches Fate directly without Parunic scaffolding. This changes what a Mahuo *is* relative to every other practitioner and it needs ratifying, because it makes the line categorically different rather than differently talented.
2. *Pulling a local reality one plane into Fate and slowing its tick* is the largest working anyone in this arc has performed, larger than the Custodia. What it costs Aurelian is unwritten and it is coming.
3. *Nadi fed by an external uncapped Ardency source* is a legitimate exception to its own governing weakness and it should be logged, because it is the only way the discipline exceeds its cap and it required an entire five-man convergence to arrange.
4. *There is no Sealing on the binding*, and Darius has stopped being able to be frightened by that. The bill for the whole open sequence is still outstanding.

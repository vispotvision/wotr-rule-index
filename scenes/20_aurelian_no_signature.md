# No Signature

*The crater. Inside the Pillar.*

---

The whole of it had been decided when the boundary went up and neither of them said so.

Darius understood it standing in the equalised field with the Hammer at the bottom of its arc, watching black-gold numerals from a register nobody in the Concord had ever indexed open one after another across a seven-foot-four frame, and the understanding was very simple and he took no pleasure in it.

Every one of those entries described a removal. Hollow. End. Null. Disruption. The Aeloran register runs on Nihiloth and on Fractura, which is Limina and Vectoria, and it is the finest instrument for taking things away that has ever been assembled by anybody.

And a removal is still an act, and an act on the physical still has to become physical.

*Formed Boundary is Coagulatio. Materia family. Material Science: crystalline lattice, phase state, molecular bonding. It is a statement about what is permitted to bond to what, and it is closed across this crater.*

*A glyph made of nihility is still a glyph. It has a Material expression somewhere in its three-plane grammar or it does not reach anything, and he cannot ensoul, he cannot mend Aether, and he cannot formulate. He is speaking a perfect language into a room where the air will not carry it.*

*Every number he has raised since the Pillar closed has been correct and has been recited into a boundary that will not let it actualise.*

Aetheric Density inside the Pillar had stopped climbing at the moment the boundary closed and had begun, very slowly, to fall, because saturation requires bleed and bleed was the thing Coagulatio had just declared impermissible. Every entry the Regent raised now was being recited into a field that was quietly emptying around him.

He was wise about the method. He was wrong about the task.

---

[Thru] Hollow had gone to the Heartflare first, and that was the error, and Darius watched it happen and had to keep his face still.

The glyph is Uurgath's root and Nihiloth carries it and its entry is four words on function: *hollows targets, removes purpose and binding.* Aimed at the Fate-position of the thing the longing expected to satisfy it, it leaves the want intact and takes away the clean recognition of what fulfilment would mean.

Which is correct, and which does not weaken the flare.

*Paru Five's stability is one hour for every point of sustained emotional intensity. Sustained. The entry says sustained.*

*A want decays when it is fed. That is the whole reason the sheet warns against fresh whims, because a whim resolves and a resolved want stops burning. He has taken the terminal object out of Aurelian's longing and made it unfulfillable, permanently, and an unfulfillable want does not decay. It cannot. There is nothing left for it to arrive at.*

*He has made the creation take longer, and taking longer is the only variable that makes it stronger, and he has done it to a man who spent forty years wanting to know what his own life had felt like.*

*And Hollow's own entry says it plainly. Removes purpose and binding. He took the binding off the object and left the purpose standing, because purpose was never seated in the object. It was seated in the man.*

The flare in the Primate's core went up.

---

Then Aurelian began the sequence, and it was long, and it was accurate, and every entry stood on the one before it while the one before it went on running.

**[Th] Foundation.**

Thalen's root, Petralon under it, Vectoria, Mechanics. *Imposes geometry; reinforces boundaries and load paths.* He laid it into the Pillar and the Pillar took it, and then he laid it into the same boundary as it existed in the other register, the nihil expression, the shape the wall had where the Aeloran arts would have to come through it. Load paths in both. A wall reinforced on the real face and on the face that is made of nothing, and Mechanics does not care which side of a boundary a load arrives from.

**[Vorn] End.**

Uurgath again, and this is the part Darius would keep for the rest of his life. *Forces a termination state; collapses instability.* It is one of the Regent's own roots, off Fractura, and Aurelian took it and did not point it at the Regent.

He pointed it at the argument.

The state itself, the whole contested proposition standing over that crater, forced into a perpetual condition of terminating, which is a different thing from having ended. Terminating, continuously, as its permanent state, so that every fresh assertion entering the field arrived into something already in the act of concluding and had to spend itself getting to a beginning.

**[Lh] Frequency.**

Selhar's, and the Index gives it no Wellspring and no Family at all, which is a thing Darius had noticed at nineteen and had asked about twice and had never had answered. *Modulates wavelength; smooths discord.* Aurelian took the threads of the non-real and the threads of the real and brought them onto one wavelength, and once two things share a wavelength they can interfere, and once they can interfere the one can be used on the other.

**[Et] Mirage.**

Zhaeren's register, bends perception layers, disguises truth, and everybody who has ever been taught it has been taught it as a concealment.

He ran it backwards.

A layer that can disguise the true can present the untrue, and what Aurelian needed was for the nihil to become perceivable, to be dragged up into a layer where a thing with no Material expression could be looked at and therefore handled. He put the Regent's own absences into a perception layer and made them visible.

**[Len] Line.**

Valen's root, Lethegraal beneath, Limina, Abstract and Entropic Systems. *Defines a clean sequence between two events; prevents causal tangling.*

And a defined line appeared through the whole of it, and Darius, who had spent twenty-one years writing about causal tangling and had never once seen it undone at scale, felt something in his chest go over.

*That is the anti-Unwinding and he may not know it.*

*The eyes wait at the border where present becomes past and take the completed sequence's claim. A clean line between two events is a sequence with no ambiguity about where one thing ends and the next begins, and it prevents tangling, and the border he waits at is a tangle. Aurelian has just made every one of our workings a single defined causal run from origin to arrival with no completion boundary in the middle to sit on.*

**[Vor] Return.** Auren's, Somnalis beneath it, Fluxia, Fluid Dynamics, which means it does not gather and it does not fetch. It establishes a flow and lets a fluid do what a fluid does at a pressure difference. *Recycles Essence; prevents restless dead.*

Every ounce of Essence spent on that road since the ninth hour. The Bulwark grey. The three charges. The aurora. The lines Aurelian had taken into himself and put out in three directions. The whole spent inventory of a burning quarter, gathered up as a flow and turned.

**[Ser] Renewal.** Wyther's, no Wellspring listed, no Family. *Catalyzes transformation; sheds old states.*

And it came back into all of them. Darius felt his own reservoir fill, from empty, which he had been since the roll, and the disc in his neck reported again which meant there was enough of him left for it to report to. It went to Aurelian, and to the light at the far end of the street, and low across the rubble into four streets of standing crimson-black.

**[Sil] Silence.**

Auren's, a Synonym derivative, and the Index lists it against no Wellspring at all, which is a property Darius had spent four years of his life trying to get an explanation for. *Mutes hostile resonance; dignified stillness.*

This was the long part and Aurelian did not hurry it. Silence went outward across the whole crater and into every glyph standing in it, one at a time, and it did not cancel any of them. It muted their true nature. A glyph's nature is what it announces to the field, and a muted glyph goes on functioning while ceasing to declare what it is, and a register that reads by matching every hostile glyph to the assumption beneath it needs the glyph to be saying something.

**[Vt] Vector.** Wyther's compound, Ignivale, Caloria, Thermodynamics. *Directs motion or force along a plotted line.*

Force along the lines [Len] had defined, and then diverged, out of the straight and into

**[Cy] Cycle.** Valen's synonym, Rebirthine, Vitalia, Biochemistry. *Phased rest that refreshes without full sleep.*

Expanding outward across the whole crater as a turning thing, and the turning fed [Vor] and [Ser], so the recycling and the renewal stopped being a single act and became a circuit, and a circuit does not have to be recast.

There was a moment between the Cycle and the next entry where Darius could hear the whole thing running, and it did not sound like a working. It sounded like a mill. Eleven glyphs turning against each other in an order somebody had thought about for a long time, each one taking its feed from the one before it, and the only comparable thing he had ever stood inside was the pump-house at Altherion in the year they got the ward-main working, and he had been twenty-two and had cried about it and had never told anyone why.

**[Flm] Judic Flame.**

Judicium. Fulguria. Electromagnetism. A Synonym derivative and the entry is nine words. *Marks verdicts; burns only falsehood and imbalance.*

It went up across the crater and it did not burn stone and did not burn men and did not burn the dark. It burned the imbalance. It burned the falsehood. And it burned the marbled reality that had been laid over the quarter by

**[Oth] Path.** Zhaeren's root, Eclipseron, Spatium, Spatial Geometry. *Folds space; the foundation of all Gates.*

Every fold the Regent had put into that crater, every moved door and edited road and displaced address, every place the geometry had been marbled so a road would arrive at the wrong gate first. The Judic Flame does not undo a fold. It burns a falsehood, and a road that says it arrives at one place and arrives at another is a falsehood expressed in Spatial Geometry.

And each of them actualised down into equal measure with the previous still running, which is a thing the Index warns against on eleven separate entries and which the Order's own Glyphica standards forbid outright at paragraph fourteen, in a paragraph Darius wrote, for reasons that were correct for every practitioner who has ever lived except the one standing nine inches into the flags, because the field had been brought to one potential ninety seconds earlier and nothing in that crater could cancel or overwrite anything else.

---

Then the Flux.

[Flx], controlled unpredictability, breaks rigidity, and it had been under everything the Aeloran register did since the first black ring appeared, because a method built on removal needs the field to stay loose enough to be removed from.

**[Tp] Topology.** Thalen's compound, Fixatio, Materia, Material Science. *Freezes a local outcome grid for a brief window.*

The grid froze. Locally, and on the Plane of Fate, which is the part the entry does not mention and which a true Mahuo can do because the organ reaches the stratum directly. Every outcome in that crater stopped being available to be shuffled. The window is brief. The entry says so and Aurelian knew it and had built everything to happen inside it.

**[Nl] Null.**

Uurgath's. Nihiloth.

*Cancels or nullifies active glyph-work; leaves no signature.*

Darius heard the Primate say it and understood the whole shape of the last five minutes at once.

*Nihiloth. It is his own Wellspring. Hollow is Nihiloth and Null is Nihiloth and they are the same current, and the Regent has spent this entire engagement demonstrating that nothing in four worlds can be used against him because he can remove the conditions under which it reaches him.*

*He cannot remove Nihiloth. It is what he is made of. A boundary that stopped Nihiloth from actualising would have stopped his own register from working an hour ago and it would have stopped this one too, and Aurelian built the Pillar knowing exactly which current he was leaving a door open for.*

Null went through the black-gold entries hanging around that frame, and it did not fight one of them, and it cancelled them in the non-existence, rupturing each order at the level where it was still only a glyphic proposition, and it found every one of them by running the search through the Plane of Fate, where a true Mahuo does not need a Material expression to reach a thing.

And it left no signature. That is the last clause on the entry and it is four words and Darius had read it a hundred times as a courtesy to the caster, a note that the work would not be traced back, the sort of line a Division writes for practitioners worried about the Bench.

It was never a courtesy. It is the entire weapon.

*That is the second half and it is the better half.*

*The eyes wait at the border for a working to complete so that the Unwinding can take its claim. A cancellation that leaves no signature does not complete. There is nothing to mark. There is no finished sequence for a past-facing eye to open on, no residual causal claim to withdraw, and no history in which the cancellation occurred to be visited afterward.*

*He has not defeated the Unwinding. He has handed it a working it cannot find.*

He looked across at the man who had done it.

Nine inches into the flags. A Scale under the street, an unsealed Turgia running since the tenth hour, a Heartflare rooted in a core that could not feel the hook, a Pillar standing on a dead friend's seam, and eleven glyphs turning in a circuit that would not need recasting. Aurelian Prudentius had built the finest single piece of Glyphica Darius had seen in fifty-six years of life, and had done it in about ninety seconds, and had not once looked as though any of it mattered to him.

*He will not survive the accounting, and the load will have very little to do with it. There is nothing left in him that will decide to stop, and the Continuum closes an unsealed working by whatever route it finds cheapest, and the cheapest route out of this crater is going to be him.*

Darius Ignatius Sanctus Sanctorum stood in a crater with a full reservoir for the first time since the gate, inside a frozen outcome grid, in a field where every glyph had gone quiet and every fold was burning, and brought the Hammer up out of the bottom of its arc.

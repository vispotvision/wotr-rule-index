# The Fist of God

*The crater. Contact range.*

---

The teep came up the middle at the solar plexus.

Darius read it late and read it correctly, which are two different things, and the correct read was that a push kick to the centreline is not thrown to hurt anybody. It is thrown to take a man's posture away from him, and a man without posture cannot generate anything through the floor, and everything Darius had left ran through the floor.

He did not block it.

You do not block a teep. A square block puts a shin and a hip and a bodyweight straight down your own spine and the man throwing it does not care. What you do is turn it off the line, and Darius brought his left forearm up and across in a rising sweep, catching the ball of the foot on the outside of the wrist and taking the vector wide, and at the same instant he converted through the contact and put the recoil back down the leg that had thrown it.

It worked. It was not enough.

He went back six feet on both heels with the ash ploughing up in front of his boots, and the parry had cost him the line and the line was the only thing keeping him upright, and he knew at four feet that he was going down before his knee found the ground.

He went to one knee and coughed.

Blood came up. Not much and it did not need to be much: it came up bright and frothed at the edges and that is the lung, and he had known about the lung since the second knee to the body and had filed it and had gone on filing it.

*Pulmonary contusion, lower right lobe, from the knee that came up under the ribs while I was still reorganising around the elbow. It is not the worst injury on me. It is the one that decides how long I have.*

He took inventory in the two seconds available, in the diagnostic voice, because a Vitality reading taken in a panic is worth nothing and he had been taking them on other people since he was twenty-two.

Lead thigh, outside, four kicks and the fourth had been the one. The shin had come through the vastus lateralis and into the nerve running down the outside, and the leg below the hip had gone from a limb to a weight. The common peroneal runs down the outside there under very little cover, and a shin arriving on it does not bruise a muscle so much as overload a nerve trunk, and what comes back up the leg afterward is not pain in the ordinary sense. It is absence. It would hold him standing. It would not accept a load and it would not push, and everything a man does in a fight he does by pushing off the floor.

It also meant he could no longer check. A check is the knee coming up and turning out to present the upper shin, and it is the only defence anybody has ever found against that kick, and it requires the checking leg to take the whole of the impact through a structure that is holding itself rigid. His would not hold itself rigid. So the next one landed clean and the one after that, and both of them knew it.

Cervical disc, gone since the roll. Right shoulder, gone since the roll. Three ribs on the left going the way ribs go. And the reservoir Aurelian had filled him with was already spending itself faster than it arrived, because the office pours in through a channel that cannot couple, and a man who cannot couple cannot store anything either. It runs through him. It always has.

Down the crater, in the wreck at the far end, Haruki had stopped moving the way a fighter moves.

He saw the wound from thirty yards and read it and had to put his own head down for a second, because it was a beautiful piece of work and there is no other honest word. Through the flank. Out the side. Nothing through the centre line, nothing through the heart, the channel laid exactly where enough structure lives to end a person's ability to fight and exactly clear of everything that ends a person. It had not been mercy and had not been aimed at mercy. It was a man removing a participant and keeping a witness.

Two exchanges. That was what the whole of it had come to, and Darius made himself say the number in his own head without dressing it. The Regent had put a teep, four low kicks, two elbows, a hook to the floating ribs and two knees into him inside about eleven seconds, and every one of them had been small, and none of them had carried a single ounce of Void that a glyph could have isolated and invalidated.

*That is the insult and it is deliberate. He built an arsenal of removal and then put it down and beat me with shins.*

*And he was right to. There is nothing in the Codex that answers a knee.*

Aurelian began to pour into him again.

Darius felt it arriving and felt what it was arriving from, and the Primate was nine inches into the flags with a Scale and an unsealed Turgia and a Heartflare rooted in a core that could not feel the hook, and every ounce that came into Darius was leaving a man who had no mechanism left to notice it going.

"Don't."

It came out on half a breath and he had to stop and get another one.

"I can fight him alone now, Aurelian. You have done enough."

The Primate did not answer, and the flow slowed, and stopped.

---

Darius set the Hammer down.

He laid it on its side in the ash with the haft toward his own foot, which is how you put down a weapon you intend to pick up, and it went on refusing there, quietly, as it had since before there was a Kharven.

Then he took the armour off.

It came away in plates. The gold had never been fitted to him in the ordinary sense, and it fell off the shoulders and the chest and the thighs and struck the flags in a sequence of flat heavy sounds, and underneath it was a fifty-six-year-old man in a torn shirt with the shoulders and the back of a lifetime and a ruined lead leg going purple from hip to knee.

He was not being theatrical.

*Nobody has ever thrown a proper round kick in plate. You cannot get the hip through. You cannot use the shin, you cannot turn on the ball of the foot, you cannot feel the floor, and every strike a man throws in armour is thrown from the shoulder, which is the weakest structure in the body for the purpose.*

*And I need to feel him.*

The strands came up on his frame.

Nothing like the tessellation. Something older and looser, laid along the arms and across the deltoids and down both flanks and around the ruined thigh: the Plane itself, standing on him in cords, following the lines of the muscle underneath as though the muscle were what it had been drawn to describe.

The Fist of God. Kami-no-Kobushi. Gimbzo configured it and Gimbzo had never given it a name in Darius's hearing, and had spent four of the nine months on it, and had said one sentence about it at the end of the fourth month and then never again.

*The seven Works go into the world. That is what everybody does with them and it is what they are for and it is why the Order has an apparatus. The Fist puts them in the body instead. Ruin in the hands. Purity in the skin. Order in the stance. Spirit at the point of contact.*

*It is worth nothing at range. It has no reach and no display and it cannot be aimed at a city.*

*It only works if you are touching him. It has no Wellspring line and no Family and Gimbzo would not give it a Physics Domain, and I asked twice and got the same answer both times, which was that a thing worked entirely inside Corporis does not need the Codex to tell it where it lives.*

He got up on the dead leg and put it forward.

Orthodox. Left foot lead, right hand rear, rear heel lifted, hands at the cheekbone, chin down, elbows in against the ribs that were already gone.

*The lead leg is the one that takes the kicks and mine is finished. Every fighter alive would switch. I would tell a student to switch. Southpaw, put the dead leg in the rear where it carries nothing and takes nothing, and buy yourself another minute.*

*A minute is not what I need.*

*I need him to stop being able to leave, and a man in southpaw is a man who is planning to retreat, and I have put the ruined leg in front because I have stopped planning to retreat, and I would like him to work that out.*

---

He went forward.

And the thing he had understood in the last four seconds, which was the whole of the fight and which Aurelian could not use and never would be able to, went with him.

*He has taken the future away from you.*

*He has made it so there is no privileged next. You can look ahead and see eleven things and none of them has authority, and the Primate has been reading forward all morning and has just discovered that reading forward is now an entertainment.*

*So stop reading forward.*

*A man throwing a knee has to load his hips before the knee exists. A man throwing an elbow drops his shoulder first. There is no version of any successor he selects in which the body does not have to arrange itself to deliver it, and the arrangement happens in the present tense, and the present is the one place his interval does not reach.*

*He cannot be predicted. He can be felt.*

*Proprioception does not read the future. It reads load. A hip loading is a present-tense mechanical fact occurring in a body, and it occurs before the strike whether or not the strike has been selected yet, because a body that has not loaded cannot throw and no amount of authority over successors changes what a femur has to do to make a knee arrive.*

*Spirit reads traffic. Contact is traffic. That is the only reason Gimbzo built the thing.*

*So get inside and put a hand on him and never take it off.*

He crossed the last four feet at the pace the dead leg allowed, which was slow, and the slowness was information he was handing over on purpose.

He threw the right.

Rear hand, straight, from orthodox, with Ruin in it. That was the point and it was why the fake would work: Ruin finds the ending already on its way and makes it arrive correctly, and a right hand carrying the first Fist is not a punch anybody in four worlds is permitted to ignore. It has to be answered. A man can slip a fist. Nobody slips an ending.

He let it go at ninety percent of the distance and sold the hip and the shoulder and the eyes, and he did not follow it.

His weight was already going the other way.

The low kick came off the rear leg, which was the good one, and it went down and out on the shortest arc it had, shin into the outside of the lead thigh, aimed at the same handspan of nerve that had been used on him four times in the last minute. The rotation came out of the hip and the plant foot pivoted on the ball and the whole body turned through the strike, which is the only way that kick has ever been thrown and the only way it will ever work.

*Check it and you break my shin on your knee. Take it and you get what I have. Move and you have to move somewhere.*

And in the instant the shin arrived at the place where the leg should have been, and the leg was not there, Darius Ignatius Sanctus Sanctorum did the thing he had spent four months in an ash pit learning.

He did not stop the kick.

A round kick that misses does not stop. It carries. The hip is already open and the mass is already travelling and the plant foot is already turned, and every instructor in every tradition on the Material teaches a man to recover from that and eat the lost tempo, and every one of them is teaching a man to waste a rotation he has already paid for.

He let it take him round.

The rear leg came through and past and down and became the lead, and the spin came off the missed kick as one continuous piece of momentum, and his back turned and kept turning and the strands on his frame went taut across the shoulders, and out of the far side of the revolution his left hand came up open.

A fist at the end of a spin is a fist thrown by a man with no control of his own arc, and it lands with the wrist collapsing.

An open palm, heel forward, fingers back, the strongest striking surface a hand has and the only one that does not care whether the elbow arrives at the correct angle.

He put it at the throat.

Above the gorget line and below the jaw, into the soft triangle where the larynx and the hyoid sit under nothing at all, on a body that had armoured every plate and every joint and every load path and had left that gap because everything with a neck leaves that gap, and there is no armourer in four worlds who has ever solved it.

And the whole of the Fist went into the palm on the way in. Ruin in the hand, to find whatever ending was already running in that throat. Purity in the skin, to take the permission to refuse out of whatever it found. Order in the arm, so that the road between his palm and the target stayed continuous no matter what got moved. Spirit at the point of contact, reaching for the rhythm.

*And when it lands I do not take it off.*

*Whatever comes next, whatever successor gets promoted out of that interval, it gets promoted with my hand already on him.*

The strands across his shoulders had gone from cords to something continuous, and the Attraction Force running in them was the last of what the Primate had put into him and there would be no more.

Darius came out of the revolution with his weight over the dead leg and the palm arriving at the throat, and did not close his eyes.

## Notes (author-facing, not canon)

### The answer to the asymmetry problem

**Void Interval kills prediction. It does not kill contact.**

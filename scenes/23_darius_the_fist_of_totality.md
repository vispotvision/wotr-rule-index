# The Fist of Totality

*The crater. The opening.*

---

The fourth knee never landed.

Darius felt the tie go slack under his hands and understood two things in the same quarter-second, and the first was that something had come out of him and the second was that it had not been taken by the man he was holding.

It went out through the middle. Nothing crossed the four points, because nothing ever crosses those four points in either direction and nothing ever will, and this had gone from somewhere further in than the skin. He felt it leave the way a man feels a room go quiet behind him.

*That is my Essence and it has been called and I did not feel a hand on it.*

*It did not go out through a conduit. Something reached the place where circulating Aether first agreed to be me and asked for a portion back along that agreement, and the agreement is older than the Trait and the Trait has no opinion about it, because Purity subtracts coupling and that was never a coupling. That was an acceptance.*

*Nothing in the Order has a defence for that. Nothing in the Codex has a row for it, and I have read all one hundred and nine.*

He let go of the collar tie and got his weight off the post and came out backward on the dead leg, and the leg took it and did not fold, which surprised him. The shin had overloaded the common peroneal without severing it, and an overloaded nerve trunk returns in stages if it is left alone, and his had been left alone for about forty seconds. It would carry him. It would not carry him twice.

The half of the thing in front of him that had been on the right was gone.

Cleanly. A vertical absence from the shoulder through the hip with a cross-section of black-gold internal architecture standing open along the cut, and it was not closing, and Darius had spent an hour watching that body discard and replace and restore and correct, and it was not correcting.

*He knows. He can see the completed event and he cannot afford it.*

*Knowledge and authority. He has spent the entire morning explaining the difference to everybody in this crater and he has just found out what it feels like from the inside.*

Down the wreck, at the far end, there was a child on his feet with a compressed thing above his hands and five currents turning around him that had no business being compatible, and one of them was Darius's own.

He did not look at it for longer than he had to.

*Take it. Take all of it. I have never been able to keep anything anyway.*

---

He had a window, and it was not a long one, and he knew exactly what he was going to do with it because he had known since the gate and had been refusing to look at it.

He turned his palm up and reached for the seam.

The Pillar was no part of it. Aurelian's Pillar stood along the join and drew from it and had been drawing from it for six minutes, and the Primate did not know whose join it was and had never been told and was not going to be told now.

Darius knew.

He had felt the wall standing since he came through the gate. A Palatine of the Holy Sea had gone into a seam at the ninth hour and become the material of a Custodia and had been lifted out of the world by a hand, and the seal had gone on standing afterward, and there was a hairline fault running through it that had never closed.

*Verinus.*

*I met you four times. You were at the table when they put the chain on Charles and you signed the minute and you were the only man in that room who looked me in the face while he did it. I have thought about you perhaps eleven times in ten years and I have never once thought about you kindly, because you were the man who kept the Court's arithmetic and I have spent my life refusing to be that.*

*And you spent your last hour in the world declining to stop.*

*I am about to spend you.*

He mended it outward into his hand.

That was the only word for the operation and it was Aurelian's word rather than his, and Darius took it because he had none of his own. He did not draw the seam into himself. **He could not.** Nothing external integrates into his Lattice, not the office, not a district, not a wall, and twenty-one years of that had made him the loneliest instrument in four worlds.

So he did the other thing.

*A hand holds.*

*That is the whole office and I worked it out in a cold chapel at thirty-five and wrote it in a letter I never sent. It does not weigh and it does not choose and it does not pass anything. It closes on a thing and keeps that thing in place while somebody else does the work, and it is not required to be right.*

*I cannot receive you. I can hold you.*

The seam came up out of the join and into his palm, and it stayed itself, and it stayed a wall, and Darius Ignatius Sanctus Sanctorum closed his hand around a boundary made out of a dead man and did not take one ounce of it inside himself.

Aetheric Density around his right arm went past the point where a reading means anything, and it did it inside four seconds, and it did it in a crater where saturation had been falling since the Pillar closed because bleed had been declared impermissible. All of it was in his fist and none of it was in him.

It began to convulse.

He was, in the strict sense, holding a phase boundary in his fist. A surface across which two states meet and neither is permitted into the other, and the energy of such a surface is proportional to its area, and it wants to minimise that area the way every interface in the world wants to minimise it. Compressed into a hand it had an area it could not reduce and a tension it could not shed.

A Custodia is not built to be carried. It is built to stand along a join and be a fact, and a fact in a fist has nowhere to be, and the whole mass of it went into a beating that came up his forearm and into his shoulder and out through his ribs and made the torn lung report in a way it had not yet managed. The light off it was the wrong colour for anything and it was throwing no shadow.

"POWER YOUR ENERGY INTO ME."

He got it out at volume with the last of the breath he had been counting.

It was the wrong instruction and he knew it was the wrong instruction as it left him and there was no time to correct it. Nothing they sent would reach him. Purity would refuse it at the skin and it would run off him and ground in the flags.

Aurelian understood anyway.

The Primate was a true Mahuo of the true line with the organ, and the organ reaches Fate directly without waiting for a sentence, and he did not need the words. What Darius saw was the Scale under the street turn once, and the light that came across the crater did not go into Darius's body at any point.

It went into the wall in his hand.

Everything the Primate had left, which was very little and which was leaving a man nine inches into the flags who could not feel it going, poured into the seam and not into the man holding it. Down the crater, white came off the wreck and joined it, from somebody with a channel through her flank who should not have been able to give anything to anybody.

*I am the arm. I have always been the arm. Charles carried and could not spend and I spend and cannot carry, and it took the two of us being broken in exactly opposite directions for the office to work, and he is dead and I am the last of three, and the third one is in my fist.*

---

Totality came up.

The seventh Work is the six held at once and the Plane delivering the finding, and Gimbzo had spent the last of the nine months on it and had never once let Darius reach it, and had said, in the fourth month, one sentence about the Fist that Darius had not understood until this moment.

*It is worth nothing at range. It only works if you are touching him.*

He was going to be touching him.

*And the Fist has no Wellspring line and no Family and no Physics Domain, and Gimbzo would not give it one, and I asked twice. A thing worked entirely in Corporis does not need the Codex to tell it where it lives. I have spent twenty-one years assigning Physics Domains to other people's workings and the only one that has ever mattered to me is unfiled.*

Ruin went into the hand. Purity went into the skin. Order into the stance and Spirit at the point of contact, and then the three the Fist had never been permitted to carry, because a body cannot hold seven and Darius was not holding them in a body any more. Temporality went into the tie. Knowledge into the read. Balance last, which was his, which had come back to him in the ash the way a language comes back to a man who spoke it as a child.

And the seventh is the six agreeing to hold still long enough to be looked at.

His frame took the shape of it.

He could not have described what happened to him afterward and never tried. The strands stopped being strands. The gold went out of the filaments and into the whole of him and he stopped having an outline that a person could have drawn, and what was standing in that crater with a convulsing boundary in its right fist had a fifty-six-year-old man somewhere inside it holding on with both hands.

*I have one arc. The same as before. That is all a body ever gets.*

There was blood coming out of his nose and out of his left ear and the vision had gone at the sides and stayed gone, and the Vitality reading he took on himself came back in a class he had only ever written about.

He went forward on the dead leg.

---

The presence arrived before he did.

He felt it hit and felt what it hit, and it went into the threads. It went nowhere near the body, the plates, or the architecture standing open along the cut. Into the threads of him, at the Plane, the whole bundle of small unique things that a person is when the Fate stratum looks at them, and Totality did not cut one of them and did not intend to.

It weighed on them.

And the weight was the finding. Six Works held at once and the Plane returning a verdict about the object in front of them, and the verdict had a magnitude, and the magnitude pressed on every strand in that bundle at once and none of them could hold it and the whole of the thing began to go back.

The threads did not tear and Totality did not want them to. A bundle under uniform load across every strand at once behaves the way any tensioned array behaves, which is that it translates instead of failing, because failure requires a differential and there was no differential anywhere in the finding.

Back through the crater. Back through the burning quarter and the wreck and the cracked flags and the place where the road had been. Back toward the tear it had come out of, at the rate of a man walking, because Darius was walking and the boundary in his fist was doing the pushing and the boundary did not hurry.

*You are not going to be killed here. I understand that now and I should have understood it at the gate.*

*You cannot be killed by any of us. You have taken a Titan's organ into the relationship between yourself and the Void and there is no arrangement in four worlds that ends that, and every one of us has spent this morning finding an elegant way to lose to it.*

*So you are not going to be killed.*

*You are going to be put outside.*

Every step cracked the flags under the dead leg and the cracking ran ahead of him in a line, and behind him the whole quarter had gone quiet in the way a room goes quiet when the thing that has been making all the noise has been carried out of it.

He drove it through the tear and kept walking until his arm was through to the shoulder.

The cold on the far side of the tear went through the sleeve and into the skin of the forearm and stopped there, at the boundary of him, because it could not couple and nothing ever has. He noticed it and had the strength to be grateful and no strength to say so.

Then he set the wall.

It went into the join with the whole of the Primate's last output and the white from the wreck behind it, and it closed, and it closed as a Custodia closes, which is to say it did not become a door with a lock on it. It became a fact. A boundary of Totality, made out of a man who declined to stop, standing across the place where the Void gives onto the Material, with the entire finding of the seven Works standing in it as its authorisation.

The Judger's own boundary, and the thing on the far side of it put outside the world it had come to edit, and the perpetual nothing on the other side of the seal to keep it company.

It went in along the whole length of the join at once, and Coagulatio settled it at the level of the bond, which is the level where matter agrees to remain matter, and the agreement was made permanent because that is the only kind of agreement Coagulatio has ever made.

The hairline fault was still in it.

Darius saw that as it closed and had no capacity left to do anything about it, and he entered it, in the small clear place at the back of his head that noticed things and wrote them down and had never once stopped operating in fifty-six years.

Then he took his arm out of the seam and fell over onto his side in the ash of the Vaeloris quarter, with his right hand still closed, and did not open it.

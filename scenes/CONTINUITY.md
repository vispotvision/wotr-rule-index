# Continuity map

What each scene changes, and what it calls back to. Built by reading all 160 scenes in
the archive. `references` are the concrete callbacks the text makes to events in other
scenes -- they are how the archive connects, and there are 1,132 of them.

Nothing here is invented: a scene the text does not locate is marked so rather than
placed. Use `scene_recall` and `scene_context` for the passages themselves.

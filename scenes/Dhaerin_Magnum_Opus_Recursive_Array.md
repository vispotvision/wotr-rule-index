"Cozbi." Dhaerin's voice cracked on the second syllable, and he coughed blood onto the churned earth before he could stop himself, a thin dark line trailing from the corner of his mouth. "Cozbi!"

The pure magnitude of Nisuke's borrowed presence had done this to him, pressure enough to overwhelm a Soul Crystal that had never been built for confrontations at this scale. Rengai lay a dozen paces distant, unmoving, the ash along his forearms cooled to grey and lifeless, his consciousness balanced somewhere at the very edge of the dark. Dhaerin's wand had scorched nearly black along its shaft, the last junction of gathered current he'd managed to raise barely sufficient to turn the full brunt of the Titan's blow into a glancing one instead of a killing one. Moonlight came down clean and indifferent through the settling ash, silvering the wreckage of the clearing without comment.

Cozbi looked forward, unhurried, both halves of him still standing where the fight had left them. "I told you I would show you how absolutely useless this is."

Dhaerin began to laugh.

It came up out of him in a wet, bubbling register, blood catching at the back of his throat with every exhale, and there was nothing hysterical in it, only the particular satisfaction of a man watching a very long proof finally resolve. "I'm not done."

Cozbi noticed it then, a flicker at the edge of his own architecture that his instincts read half a second before his conscious mind could name it. *No,* he thought, the certainty arriving before the doubt could finish forming behind it. *That isn't possible. No practitioner attempts a working of that class against a creature of this magnitude. Not without dying before the first cycle completes.*

Dhaerin's smile widened, blood-streaked and entirely genuine. "Spirit Rune Arts." He said it slowly, savoring the shape of the words in his mouth the way another man might savor the last swallow of something expensive. "How interesting that you noticed at all. I'll say this for you, Cozbi. You may be the cleverest of them all."

It was too late for Cozbi to react.

Every rune Dhaerin had laid since the opening exchange, the Fixatio anchors that had frozen the earth-spikes mid-formation, the Vincula Tenent chains that had caught the clone's legs and wrists in the second pass, had never been isolated countermeasures at all. Each one was a node, seeded and Sealed at precise coordinates across the battlefield, waiting on a single Directional glyph to bring the whole circuit online at once. What had looked, to any eye reading the fight as a sequence of individual defenses, like six unrelated castings was in truth a single Runic Array in the process of construction, a multi-substrate circuit inscribed not into stone or bone but into the churned battlefield itself, ground and ash and shattered earth all pressed into service as substrate the moment the final Sealing glyph closed the loop.

The compound array activated in a single cascading pulse, Fixatio-seated Boundary lines rising out of the churned ground in rings of pale gold script, and the array's true function revealed itself only once every node had already committed to the working.

A cage of pure anchoring pressure closed around the seams of Nisuke's borrowed spiritual structure, snipe-precise, threading itself directly into the Essence Drafts the Titan's presence had stolen from Rengai's own ash current over the course of the fight. Those stolen drafts had never fully integrated into the effigy's architecture, foreign matter grafted onto a stolen god's shape rather than native to it, a compatibility failure Dhaerin's Harmonia-trained ear had caught within the first minute of the summoning, hearing the borrowed essence hum at a frequency subtly out of phase with the Titan's own resonance. Dhaerin's array found the seam between borrowed and true and widened it with the patient, surgical malice of a man who had spent the entire battle doing nothing but taking notes.

"This is my magnum opus."

The recursive array he'd built did not draw its power from any Wellspring source external to the fight. It fed on the stolen drafts themselves, each cycle pulling a fraction of Nisuke's misappropriated essence into the loop and returning it amplified, compounding the yield with every pass the way Aro Valdred's Tenfold Circuit once had, back before the Circuit killed him for exactly this arrogance. Dhaerin knew the history. He had read the account twice as a boy and thought Valdred a fool for attempting a recursive amplification against his own Soul Crystal's limits.

*Every Boundary glyph I laid tonight was a wager against becoming exactly that fool,* he thought, blood thick at the back of his throat with every breath now. *I will find out within the hour whether I calculated the wager correctly, or whether I have simply found a more educated way to die than Valdred managed.*

He was doing the same thing now, deliberately, against a target far larger than any Valdred had ever dared, because the alternative was watching Rengai die on the ground behind him while he stood there conserving himself for a fight he no longer had the leisure to survive cautiously.

The runes activated one after the other in a chain too fast for any unaided eye to track, each cycle drawing a further sliver of his own Essence Core to bridge the gaps his Boundary glyphs hadn't quite closed cleanly enough. Dhaerin coughed up more blood, his knees buckling by degrees, his own Aether Shell straining under a working his body had never been built to sustain past a single cycle, let alone the dozen already compounding through the array's feedback. He stayed upright anyway, through will alone where technique had already reached its ceiling, because a man who quit at the edge of his limit had never once, in his whole life, found out what waited past it.

The clones broke first.

Both projections of Cozbi shattered along invisible seams, their borrowed spiritual cohesion unraveling as the array pulled the last of the stolen essence holding them together into the fixation point at its center, dark motes of dissolving Aether scattering outward from where each clone had stood a heartbeat before. Nisuke's vast shape shuddered along its full height, the compound array's anchoring pressure finding purchase in every seam the stolen drafts had left exposed, and somewhere at the effigy's core the borrowed architecture began, for the first time since the summoning, to fight itself rather than the men standing beneath it.

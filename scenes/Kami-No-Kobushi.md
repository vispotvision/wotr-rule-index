# THE GOD FIST — SEVEN DOCTRINES

### **Kami no Kobushi · The Seven Labor-Forms of the Anchor**

*"**Möngke gave us seven ways to work. Gimbzo found seven ways to refuse.**"*
— Oral tradition, surviving First Conclave fragment

Each of the seven Hataraki Works manifests differently through the God Fist's architecture. The spine never changes — Aether falls, position is law, the verdict precedes the blow — but the nature of what is being anchored shifts with each doctrine. A practitioner who has mastered all seven is not seven fighters. They are one Anchor who has learned seven different things worth holding.

## **I. HAMETSU-STYLE**

### **The Ruin Fist — *****Hōkai no Kobushi***

Work: Hametsu no Gō — Work of Ruins
Type: Collapse-Fixator · *Anchor what is already breaking and make it break correctly.*

Primary Wellsprings:
Fixatio – Pins the opponent's structural failure at its exact point of origin, preventing recovery
Coagulatio – Loads the descent with collapse-weight rather than mass-weight; the blow falls like an ending, not a hit
Cataclysm – Accelerates the deterioration already present in the target's ward, body, or Essence structure
Fractura – Identifies and widens pre-existing fracture lines in material, metaphysical, or soul-based defenses
Nihiloth – Strips the opponent's resistance of its sense that resistance is meaningful

Summary

The Ruin Fist does not create destruction — it finds destruction that was already coming and makes it arrive on schedule. Where other strikers try to break a thing from the outside, a Hōkai practitioner reads the structural rot already present in their opponent and plants their heel on top of it. The blow does not shatter the wall. It locates the wall's oldest crack and declares that crack the verdict. At its core, the Ruin Fist is the art of accelerating inevitable collapse into a single, correctly timed fist.

Core Principle

- Essence reads decay first. The practitioner does not commit until the Fate Ledger has ranked which of the opponent's structures is already failing.

- Fracture lines are coordinates. Every ward, every bone-reinforcement, every Wellspring brace has a point of origin. Fixatio seats on that point, not on the surface.

- Collapse is not chaos — it is sequence. Coagulatio ensures the descent lands at the moment the failure chain is deepest, not the moment the practitioner is simply ready to swing.

In Practice

- Against a heavily warded opponent, the practitioner waits — not passively, but *diagnostically* — until Fractura maps the oldest seam in the ward's structure. The strike lands there, one centimeter deep, and the ward comes apart from the inside.

- Against a Wellspring-brace fighter, Nihiloth is run first as a quiet undercurrent, slowly eroding the opponent's conviction that their reinforcement is holding. By the time the overhead drops, the brace believes it has already lost.

- Against a Domain user, the practitioner plants inside the Domain itself — Fixatio declaring *their coordinate* more lawfully seated than the Domain's own architecture. The Domain does not break. It is simply out-ranked.

Philosophical Tension

The Ruin Fist tempts its user toward cruelty of timing — waiting so long for the perfect collapse vector that battles drag into torture. Lawful Ruin ends what cannot remain. Obsession Force Ruin *enjoys* the waiting.

## **II. JUNKETSU-STYLE**

### **The Purity Fist — *****Seijō no Kobushi***

Work: Junketsu no Gō — Work of Purity
Type: Foundation-Fixator · *Anchor what is rightfully standing and refuse to let it be defiled.*

Primary Wellsprings:
Fixatio – Declares the practitioner's structural integrity a lawfully protected coordinate; corruption effects must prove authority to proceed
Petralon – Reinforces the body's load-bearing axis as consecrated infrastructure
Basilithe – Establishes the battlefield's ground as sanctified substrate; impure techniques lose purchase on it
Benediction – Turns each blow into a corrective act — not violence, but restoration of proper form
Judicium – Ranks what is structurally sound versus what is metaphysically compromised before the fist commits

Summary

The Purity Fist treats the body as a sacred structure and every exchange as an act of correction. It does not strike to wound — it strikes to *restore proper arrangement*, which sometimes means the target is broken back into the shape reality intended for them. Where the Ruin style waits for collapse, the Purity style enforces standard. At its core, the Purity Fist is the art of being so structurally correct that incorrect things cannot stand in your presence.

Core Principle

- The body is the first and most important consecrated structure. Before the practitioner can impose Purity outward, Petralon and Benediction must have already run inward — spine, heel, gut, knuckle all aligned as lawful architecture.

- A blow from Seijō no Kobushi does not damage — it *corrects*. Bones return to where they should be. Wards that were over-extended collapse back to their proper boundaries. Essences that were borrowed return to their original seats.

- Basilithe-treated ground does not simply support the practitioner. It *rejects* techniques built on metaphysical dishonesty: illusion, doppelgänger substitution, borrowed identity, and false-flag Wellspring use all degrade the moment the foot plants.

In Practice

- Against a curse-stacked fighter, the practitioner does not counter the curses directly. They plant, run Basilithe into the ground, and let the consecrated substrate drain the curses' purchase passively while the fight continues.

- Against a regenerating opponent, each blow delivered under Benediction's law restores the target to *their true structural state* — which, if their regeneration relies on Wellspring-borrowed life rather than native recovery, means the regeneration is classified as false form and the blow lands without triggering it.

- Against a shapeshifter or doppelgänger, Judicium runs the ledger against which form is lawful. Fixatio pins that form. The strike lands on the real one.

Philosophical Tension

The Purity Fist tempts its user toward fanaticism — the conviction that their standard of correctness is the only standard. Lawful Purity restores. Obsession Force Purity *sterilizes*, refusing to permit any form that does not match its template.

## **III. SEIREI-STYLE**

### **The Spirit Fist — *****Tamashii no Kobushi***

Work: Seirei no Gō — Work of Spirit
Type: Soul-Fixator · *Anchor the line between what lives and what has ended, and strike across it.*

Primary Wellsprings:
Fixatio – Pins the opponent's soul-coordinate, preventing flight, transition, or post-mortem escape
Coagulatio – Loads the blow with soul-weight rather than body-weight; the strike lands on the spiritual layer first
Mortalis – Governs the passage between life and death; the practitioner can deliver strikes that register on both sides of that threshold simultaneously
Anima Spirare – Reads the life-breath signature of the target, identifying where the soul sits heaviest in the body
Contrition – Applies lawful consequence to souls that have accumulated unpaid spiritual debt

Summary

The Spirit Fist fights on two floors at once — the physical and the metaphysical — and neither floor is its real target. The real target is the *passage between them*. A Tamashii practitioner does not try to kill the body or destroy the soul separately. They grip the threshold between those states and strike through it, delivering judgment that neither life nor death can contest. At its core, the Spirit Fist is the art of making death lawful before the body has finished falling.

Core Principle

- The soul is a coordinate too. Fixatio does not only seat physical position — run through Anima Spirare's reading, it can locate where a soul sits most densely inside flesh and pin *that* coordinate.

- Mortality is a boundary, not a wall. Mortalis gives the practitioner access to both sides — a blow delivered at the soul-seam registers as a living wound and a posthumous one simultaneously, preventing resurrection, soul-flight, and death-cheat techniques from activating cleanly.

- Contrition makes debt legible. Souls that carry unpaid spiritual obligations — broken vows, ancestor debts, stolen life — are heavier, slower to transit, and more vulnerable to a Spirit Fist verdict.

In Practice

- Against an immortal fighter, the practitioner does not attempt to simply damage harder. Fixatio seats on the soul-coordinate, Mortalis opens the threshold, and the strike delivers its verdict *to the part that does not regenerate*.

- Against a ghost, echo, or revenant — an opponent already partially on the other side of Mortalis — the Spirit Fist reaches across and hits them on the living side, where they are most exposed.

- Against an opponent relying on soul-transference or consciousness-migration as an escape technique, Fixatio pins the destination coordinate before the transfer can complete.

Philosophical Tension

The Spirit Fist tempts its user toward soul-hoarding — pinning things that should be allowed to pass, keeping opponents alive because the ledger says their debt is not fully paid. Lawful Spirit governs passage. Obsession Force Spirit *refuses to let anything go*.

## **IV. JIKAN-STYLE**

### **The Sequence Fist — *****Toki no Kobushi***

Work: Jikan no Shigoto — Work of Temporality
Type: Sequence-Fixator · *Anchor the next instant and make it the only instant available.*

Primary Wellsprings:
Fixatio – Locks the practitioner's position inside the sequence of events, declaring their current instant the lawfully established present
Monlithion – Ratifies the blow as a causal sentence; once committed, the timeline must process it before moving forward
Cymorath – Governs temporal transition and sequence shift; the practitioner can read the gap between instants and insert their strike there
Anamnesis – Reads the opponent's combat history as a causal chain, identifying which past decision made this moment inevitable
Transmutatio – Allows the practitioner to alter the *sequence weight* of a strike — making a slow blow arrive as though it always arrived first

Summary

The Sequence Fist does not move faster than its opponents — it moves *more correctly*. By anchoring the practitioner's coordinate inside Temporality's law, every blow is delivered at the moment the sequence ratifies it as already having occurred. The opponent's timeline must process the strike before it can do anything else. At its core, the Sequence Fist is the art of making the present moment a door that only opens inward, and the practitioner is already on the other side.

Core Principle

- Sequence is a coordinate. Fixatio does not only anchor space — run through Cymorath's law, it anchors *the practitioner's instant*, declaring it the lawful present and refusing the opponent access to the next one until the verdict is processed.

- Causality is a ledger. Anamnesis reads the opponent's fight history as a chain of cause and consequence. The practitioner does not predict — they identify which earlier decision made *this moment* the only possible outcome, and they strike that outcome.

- Slowness is a weapon. Transmutatio allows the practitioner to resequence a blow's temporal weight — the overhead that begins slowly arrives as though it was always the first thing that happened, because inside the locked sequence, it was.

In Practice

- Against a Tengeriin-line Moto using convergence-reading, the practitioner locks the surrounding sequence before the Tengan adept can claim the next instant. The adept's foresight sees a present that has already been ratified as closed.

- Against a temporal-acceleration fighter, Fixatio simply refuses the acceleration's claim to the next instant. The fighter moves fast inside their own sequence and finds the practitioner's coordinate has already filled the space they were accelerating into.

- Against anyone relying on reaction speed, the practitioner does not need to be faster. They deliver the blow and Monlithion ratifies it as causal sentence — the opponent's nervous system must process it as *already having happened* before it can respond.

Philosophical Tension

The Sequence Fist tempts its user toward recursion — locking a single instant so thoroughly that the sequence cannot advance at all, trapping both practitioner and opponent in a frozen causal loop. Lawful Temporality orders sequence. Obsession Force Temporality *imprisons everyone in one wound.*

## **V. CHISHIKI-STYLE**

### **The Revelation Fist — *****Akashi no Kobushi***

Work: Chishiki no Shigoto — Work of Knowledge
Type: Disclosure-Fixator · *Anchor what a thing truly is and strike the truth of it.*

Primary Wellsprings:
Fixatio – Pins the opponent's revealed true form, preventing re-concealment once named
Judicium – Ranks the opponent's hidden structures — which defenses are real, which are borrowed, which are bluff
Luminalis – Illuminates the opponent's Essence signature, making false layers visible as distinct from authentic ones
Eidolyn – Reads the shape of memory and identity underneath the surface presentation
Aurevane – Applies revealed truth as a structural pressure; the more accurately a thing is named, the heavier the blow that follows the naming

Summary

The Revelation Fist does not strike the body the opponent presents. It strikes the body the opponent *actually is* underneath every ward, every bluff, every borrowed Wellspring, and every false-flag technique. Knowledge is not preparation for the strike — it *is* the strike. At its core, the Revelation Fist is the art of making a name into a weapon and then delivering it with both fists.

Core Principle

- What is correctly named cannot hide from what it owes. Aurevane turns accurate identification into structural pressure — the more precisely the practitioner has named the opponent's true form, function, and debt, the harder the blow that follows.

- Defenses are only real if they are native. Luminalis distinguishes between defenses that belong to the opponent and defenses that were borrowed, bought, or stolen. Fixatio pins the difference. The blow lands on the gap between them.

- The opponent's history is their most honest architecture. Eidolyn reads the shape of identity underneath the current presentation — the practitioner is not fighting who is standing in front of them, they are fighting the sum of every choice that produced that person.

In Practice

- Against a warrior concealing their true Wellspring alignment, Luminalis strips the surface signature, Judicium ranks which layer is genuine, and the blow is delivered against the genuine layer — bypassing the concealment entirely.

- Against a fighter using a borrowed Domain or stolen technique, Fixatio seats on the true-form coordinate and the blow passes through the borrowed structure as though it were not there, because *to the Revelation Fist, it is not*.

- Against an opponent with a deep spiritual debt — broken vows, stolen essence, unacknowledged lineage obligation — Aurevane names the debt aloud and the blow carries the full weight of that debt's accrued interest.

Philosophical Tension

The Revelation Fist tempts its user toward consuming certainty — the need to name everything, rank everything, expose everything, until the practitioner is paralyzed not by ignorance but by total knowledge that has become its own prison. Lawful Knowledge liberates. Obsession Force Knowledge *devours.*

## **VI. BARANSU-STYLE**

### **The Scales Fist — *****Hakari no Kobushi***

Work: Shigoto no Baransu — Work of Balance
Type: Reconciliation-Fixator · *Anchor the point where two forces cancel, then strike from it.*

Primary Wellsprings:
Fixatio – Seats the practitioner at the exact fulcrum point between two opposing forces; displacement requires defeating both simultaneously
Coagulatio – Gathers opposing forces into one gathered, bound mass before the release
Manganthra – Governs the weight and measure of competing principles; reads which force in any exchange is overextended and which is starved
Thalor – Applies binding tension between opposites, making the gap between them a usable surface
Basilithe – Establishes the ground beneath the practitioner as the settled center of any contested field

Summary

The Scales Fist finds the point in every fight where two forces are pulling against each other — offense against defense, speed against weight, Wellspring against Wellspring — and plants there. The practitioner becomes the fulcrum. They do not push one side or the other; they strike from the center at the moment of maximum tension, when both forces are fully committed and neither can correct. At its core, the Scales Fist is the art of letting your enemies' forces load your fist for you.

Core Principle

- Every conflict has a center of gravity. Manganthra reads it in real time — which side of any exchange is overextended, which is about to collapse under its own effort. Fixatio seats on the center, not the edge.

- Opposition is leverage. Thalor binds the tension between competing forces and holds it available as potential energy. Coagulatio collapses that potential into the descent at the moment of maximum strain.

- The fulcrum cannot be moved by either side, because moving it would require agreeing on which force has lawful authority — and at the center, neither does.

In Practice

- Against a two-fighter coordinated assault, the practitioner plants between them. Both attacks arrive and find the practitioner occupying the exact geometric point where their force vectors cancel each other. Thalor binds that cancellation and Coagulatio repurposes it as a single outward verdict.

- Against a fighter oscillating between aggressive and defensive postures, Manganthra reads the oscillation frequency. The blow lands at the transition point — the half-instant where neither posture is fully active.

- Against a Domain locked in conflict with another Domain, the practitioner plants at their boundary. Both Domains treat the coordinate as belonging to the other and do not press it. The practitioner stands in the gap, untouchable, and strikes outward from it.

Philosophical Tension

The Scales Fist tempts its user toward paralysis — becoming so committed to the center, so devoted to the fulcrum, that the practitioner can no longer act without perfectly balanced justification. Lawful Balance mediates. Obsession Force Balance *refuses to permit necessary imbalance*, even when the world requires it.

## **VII. ZENTAI-STYLE**

### **The Total Fist — *****Zentai no Kobushi***

Work: Zentai-sei no Hataraki — Work of Totality
Type: Sovereign-Fixator · *Anchor all six Works simultaneously and let the Plane itself deliver the verdict.*

Primary Wellsprings:
Fixatio – The unifying spine; seats the practitioner as a load-bearing coordinate of the Plane itself
Monlithion – Ratifies the blow as a sovereign sentence drawing from all subordinate Works at once
Coagulatio – Gathers the cumulative mass of every active Work-layer into the single descent
Judicium – Governs the judicial integration of all six prior doctrines into one ranked, irrefutable verdict
Materia Primordia – The Totality anchor; calls on the precondition of all reality's coherence to ratify the blow as a law that predates the opponent

Summary

The Total Fist is not a seventh style. It is what happens when a practitioner has so thoroughly internalized all six prior doctrines that they no longer choose between them — all six Works run simultaneously, each one performing its labor in the same instant, and the resulting blow is not a martial technique but a statement of fact delivered by fate itself. It cannot be taught as a first discipline. It can only be arrived at. At its core, the Total Fist is the art of becoming, for one instant, the fist of the Plane of Fate — not channeling its power but *performing its function*.

Core Principle

- Every prior doctrine is a prerequisite, not a component. The Total Fist does not mix the six styles; it requires the practitioner to have *already become* each of them, so that when Zentai fires, all six are native rather than layered.

- The Plane does not amplify the practitioner. The practitioner becomes sufficient infrastructure that the Plane extends *through* them the way it extends through Gimbzo — not as a tool but as a continuation of its own labor.

- Materia Primordia does not grant power from outside. It ratifies what the practitioner has already established as lawful, calling on the oldest layer of reality's grammar to confirm that this blow was always going to land.

In Practice

- There is no tactical breakdown for the Total Fist, because it does not have tactics. The practitioner plants, all six Works converge, and the blow falls.

- The only recorded description of what it looks like from the outside comes from a First Conclave fragment: *"**The ground remembered him. The air agreed with him. The blow arrived at the moment before you realized it had been thrown.**"*

- Every counter that exists for the individual six doctrines fails against the Total Fist, because it is running all six simultaneously. A sequence-counter meets Toki no Kobushi. A ruin-resistance meets Hōkai no Kobushi. A revelation-ward meets Akashi no Kobushi. All six meet simultaneously, and none of them meet only the one they prepared for.

Cost and Consequence

The Total Fist cannot be used by a practitioner who has not accepted Coordinate Adhesion as permanent. Running all six Works through a single planted coordinate in one instant does not deepen the adhesion — it *completes* it. A practitioner who successfully delivers Zentai no Kobushi is, afterward, inseparable from that coordinate until loosened by a Tengan adept. This is not a flaw in the design. Gimbzo built it this way deliberately.

*You do not use the fist of the Plane without paying the Plane's price.*

Philosophical Tension

The Total Fist carries the deepest temptation of all — not of any single Work's excess, but of completeness itself. A practitioner who has touched all seven doctrines and felt the Plane extend through them may begin to believe that *staying* in that coordinate is the purpose, not the cost. That the labor is to remain, permanently, as infrastructure. That returning to the world is a lesser thing than holding the Plane's floor forever.

Gimbzo is the proof that this is not delusion. It is simply the direction the Work points, if you follow it far enough.

Lawful Totality integrates and returns.
Obsession Force Totality subsumes and never releases.

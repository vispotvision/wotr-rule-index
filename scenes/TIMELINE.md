## Verified connections and uncertainties — 2026-10-29

- **Wrath → Cold → Summons → Debt:** the four scenes' Author notes number the Sins arc 1–4. The cold morning follows the Pit escape; the summons is that first dusk; Debt reaches past midnight and Vaashuuk's following morning. No Imperial date is stated, and no position against The Grey Wall is established.
- **Wrath → Cold:** Shōki's heat beats the first thickening; Vaashuuk's later perfect stillness stops that answer and fractures the Pit floor. The burned sleeve and the spent reserve persist. These are two different modes, not proof that the first field is immune to heat.
- **Cold → Summons:** the girl past the bridge and white-eared dog survive; Vaashuuk reports seeing them. Shōki keeps the chapbook at his hip; Vaashuuk has read it and noticed the wife's name is omitted. The report does not identify the mysterious voice as a confirmed returned Tsuyu.
- **Summons → Debt:** the pact remains held at the lender's end despite the broken Kurenai oath. Shōki pays reserve while walking north against the downward pull; Vaashuuk's promised rest and respect become the first workshop stoppage. Fourteen days is a reported threat, not a verified deadline entered into the table or proof the worlds have already merged.
- **Chapter Nineteen → Grey Wall:** the unified chairs, Yukazuri's cairn and the rebuilt Seat carry forward. The header supplies six months after the Great War of the North; the newer Isaac beat supplies eighteen. Do not invent a two-year interval to reconcile the older sixteen-year references. The Sins village's report of Muken's sons remains unsynchronised hearsay.
- **Grey Wall → next spar:** Amelia refuses payment for the cracked left pauldron; Muken holds her injured hand gently, and they go to the yard. Godfrey heard the end of the talk; Lorn saw the laugh. The pauldron risk and bonds are already recorded as L001–L004 in `table/ledger.yaml`; the spar itself has not happened in this source.

# **Physical Description**

She is twenty-two and she does not look like someone who has been through what she has been through, which is either a testament to how she carries herself or a misunderstanding of what going through something does to a person. It does not always leave marks on the outside. Sometimes it leaves them in the way someone answers a question — the specific flatness of a voice that has learned to offer the exact amount of information required and nothing more. The warmth is real. It coexists with the flatness. She has decided both are permitted.

| **Hair** Ash-grey — the grey of birch bark, a color rather than an absence of one. The specific tone that looks silver in certain light and almost lavender in others, with a warmth to it that flat grey doesn't have. Fine-textured, straight, with a slight natural wave that shows more as she's been eating regularly again. Chin-length on both sides now — it was longer before the pen, grew back unevenly in the weeks after, and she cut it level with her jaw rather than let the difference remain. On the right side it still falls a centimeter shorter. She notices and has decided not to care, which is different from not noticing. |

| **Fox Ears** Ash-grey, matching her hair exactly, pointed, sitting just above her temple line where human ears sit lower. Their mobility is the thing that catches people. They move before she consciously registers what they're responding to: pivoting toward a sound a full second before her face turns, dropping flat against her skull in the specific four-centimeter compression of social danger, lifting to full erect when she's genuinely engaged or genuinely amused. # **The Nose  —  Aetheric Olfactory Sense**

The Aetheric nose is not a technique. It is not a Trait in the conventional sense. It is what fox-spirit beastkin of the Mishiro lineage are — a function running below the level of conscious activation, producing information continuously, filing it in categories that the cognitive architecture then accesses. She does not choose to smell Aetheric signatures. She smells them the way she smells everything else: because that is what a nose does.

The distinction between biological scent and Aetheric scent is real but not clean. She experiences them as a single continuous input, the biological and the Aetheric layered in the way that sound and word are layered — the sound arrives first, the meaning arrives a fraction of a second later, and by the time consciousness processes either one they have already merged. She does not separate 'this person's sweat' from 'this person's Essence signature.' She reads the compound.

| **Intent-Frequency Reading** Not thought. Not words. The directional quality of where someone is emotionally moving — the specific Essence-signature of a decision forming before it becomes action. Fear reads as sharp and sweet, the precise compound of an Essence pool beginning to route defensive output. Anger reads as hot iron. Grief reads as cold water under a closed surface. She read Temür's grief in the pen before he had said three words, and filed it without naming it until she had context for the name. |

# **Fox-Spirit Lineage  —  The Mishiro**

Fox-spirit beastkin are not simply beastkin with fox features. They occupy the borderline that the WOTR taxonomy handles awkwardly — biological enough to be classified as beastkin by the Guild Accord's documentation system, spiritually adjacent enough that the Fae taxonomy also has a partial claim on them. The Mishiro lineage specifically has been in contested classification since the First Eon, when the earliest Mishiro practitioners demonstrated Somnalis attunement at birth that no formal harmonization could account for.

What this means for Yoko: she is a fox in the way that old foxes in old stories are foxes — not just an animal with a clever face, but something that knows things it shouldn't and loves things it shouldn't and stays when it shouldn't and is not wrong to do any of those things. The kitsune tradition in the Mishiro lineage holds that the fox-spirit's deepest expression is not trickery and not shapeshifting. It is loyalty. The specific loyalty of something that has chosen you and will not unchoose you because of circumstances.

She has one fox-fire trick. It is minor — a small cold amber light she can produce in the palm of her hand, lasting approximately four minutes before Essence expenditure exceeds her current capacity. She uses it to read at night when the fire is low. She has not yet realized it is the first external expression of a Trait that will become significantly less minor at Stage V.

# **Character Psychology**

Her warmth is not naivety. This is the most important thing about her and the easiest thing to misread, especially for people who have decided that anyone warm in a difficult world must not have understood what the world is. She has been in the pen. She knows what happens when you stop paying attention. She is warm the way someone is warm who has chosen warmth as a position — a deliberate decision made in full knowledge of what the alternative looks like, which is the opposite of how naivety works.

She doesn't need things to have been fair. The pen was not fair. The world has two categories for beastkin — victim or animal — and neither category has a slot for 'woman who assessed the situation and made an informed choice.' She needed the lock open. She needed to walk through it herself. The motivation of the person who opened it was secondary information, interesting but not load-bearing. She evaluated Temür the same way she evaluates everything — through the nose, through the accumulated evidence of behavior — and decided that what she saw warranted staying. She stays because she decided to. She will keep staying until she decides otherwise.

She is honest in a way that produces discomfort in people who have built elaborate systems to avoid the truth. Not cruel — precise. She told Temür his math was wrong and was right and said it without apology and without offering him a softer version, because she respects him enough not to offer him a softer version. She does not perform herself. This is related.

Her mother named her Yoko — fox-spirit, the old word — and her mother is dead and the name is what she has left of her. She said it into the rain outside the inn because no one had asked for it and she was going to lose it if she didn't say it out loud in someone else's air. He did not acknowledge it. He told her there was a blanket upstairs. She went inside. The name was said.

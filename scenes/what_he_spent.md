# What He Spent

*The coast road. Inside the column, under the weight.*
*POV: Aeldros Korvaeth. Dova'Kan, Xmere, Gorgi and Baraxes are not written; their beats stay open.*

---

## I. THE WEIGHT

It came into him through the soles of his boots and the roof of his mouth at the same moment.

Not a wind and not a sound. His knees went first, both of them, the good one and the ruined one together, and he did not decide to bend them and could not have prevented it. Then the jaw, which locked and ground and would not be told. Then a pressure behind the eyes that was the same pressure a man gets going down a deep shaft too fast, and behind that a nausea with nothing in it, the kind that comes up in the body before the body knows why.

He went down to the right knee in the black grit and put the bent sword point-first into the road and held onto it.

Six hundred drow behind him went down in a wave. He heard it. Not a shout, because nobody shouted. Harness and knees and four hundred men breathing out at once, and further off across the road the same sound out of the Eresseans, and none of it aimed at anybody.

*He is not doing this to us. This is what is left over.*

*I have stood in front of three Eressean line practitioners and a Sunroot Warden and I have taken a ridge eleven times and I have never in my life felt anything that was not aimed.*

The molten trench flattened. That was the thing he would have wanted to write down if there had been anything left to write with. Four feet of open melt that had been convecting and throwing a column for six minutes went flat and sullen, the way water goes flat under a heavy enough hand, and the black column he had bent along the road came down with it and lay across the surface at knee height like fog in a ditch.

*My working is not being countered. It is being outweighed.*

*There is a difference and every practitioner on this road understands it and not one of the four thousand does.*

He laughed.

It came out wet and broken and there was carbon in it, and he got most of the way through it before the diaphragm failed and turned the end of it into a cough that put more blood on the grit.

---

## II. WHY IT DOES NOT WORK

"You think I am afraid to die."

He said it to the ground first, because that was where his face was, and then got the chin up and said the rest of it to the shape in the smoke.

"That is the error. That is the whole of the error and you have both made it and you have made it because it is the error everybody makes who has something left to lose.

"I am dying now. I have been dying since the trench opened, and I am doing it in air I bent over my own head, and I have watched four men do it and I signed the entries. My hands will go, and then the legs, and then I will be found upright with my tools in reach and somebody will write that I failed to withdraw.

"You cannot threaten a man with the thing that is already happening to him."

*And that is true and it is not the reason, and the reason is not one I can give him.*

*I did not come onto this road to win it. I came to put four days between this army and that causeway so that the Landward companies could get four thousand men out of a city that has a wall facing the wrong way, and I have done nine hours of that, and every minute I keep two of you standing here shouting is a minute six hundred of mine are not walking west.*

*Nobody in either army has worked that out and nobody is going to.*

*Sennuvar Vhaeloth might. He has the count and he has had it since the second bell and he is a Vhaeloth, and if he does work it out he will do exactly what his house does, which is enter the number and say nothing about what the number is for.*

*That is the best I can hope for out of today. A figure in a field book that somebody reads in forty years and understands.*

"And understand who you are threatening, because you have not troubled to find out.

"I am the hand. I am not the arm. Everything I have put on this road today, every carriage and every hook and the thing in that trench, is what she permits a servant to hold. She has never needed a name and she has never needed a working and she has spent thirty years making four Under-Houses stand in a doctrine with nothing but her voice, and I am the small end of her.

"Kill me and you will have removed the part of her that argues.

"If you had any idea what was above me you would go back to whatever water you came out of."

---

## III. REPEAT IT

The shape in the smoke had asked him to say it again.

*Here it is. This is the whole of the thing and there is no way round it.*

*If I take it back now, under this, in front of six hundred men, then I am a man who says what he is frightened into saying, and every word I have put on this road goes with it. The politics goes. The four days go. And the fight goes straight back to being about elves, which is the one outcome I have spent my throat trying to prevent.*

*And I will have unmade it for nothing, because he will not believe the retraction either. Nobody ever believes the second version.*

*So it has to be worse.*

He got his foot under him and came up off the knee, which took four seconds and most of what he had, and he said it into the flattened smoke at a volume that carried to both lines.

"I enjoyed it.

"You want it repeated and I will give you more than a repetition, because a thing said twice the same way sounds rehearsed.

"The rippling. That is the word and I chose it and I will stand on it. When a hook seats at the wing root the whole animal goes through one shudder from the shoulder down the spine to the last barb on the tail, and you feel it come up the haft into both your wrists, and it is not like anything else. Nothing else in the world that size does that.

"I felt it nine times in a pass and I have felt it since and I will feel it again."

*Four days ago I pulled eleven inches of iron out of a wing root with a crow bar and I was sick twice doing it.*

"And we ate them. Third fire, off the drags, with my officers, and I gave the order to butcher and I took the first cut myself because a commander who will not eat what he has killed is a commander his men stop believing. It is sweeter than horse. It does not take salt. The young one is better than the old one.

"There. That is the fifth time I have said it in an hour and it will be in a field book tonight."

*And in Rovann in nine days, and in a Velthaeir register inside the season, and there is no instrument in the four quarters that can ever strike it, and I have just made certain of that on purpose in front of the only witnesses who matter.*

*Whatever I was, I am now this. I have written it myself and I have signed it and I would like the record to show that I knew what the pen cost.*

---

## IV. WHAT HE ACCUSED HIM OF

"Now the other thing, since you have stopped and come close enough to hear it properly.

"Three.

"Three of yours were taken, and six killed, and you crossed an ocean, and you have brought a hammer onto a road in a war that is not yours and stood on four thousand men with your presence and offered to crumble the ground under both armies.

"Two hundred and six people went into the mud at Harrowbeck four days ago. Nine of them were one household. There was a boy of eleven in it who carried messages between ridge farms because he was fast.

"Nobody crossed an ocean for him.

"No hammer came. No pressure came down on anybody's knees over the Hales. There was no foreign law announced that morning and there will not be one, and if I said their names to you now you would not be able to hold them past this afternoon, and I do not blame you for that and I am not asking you to.

"I am telling you what your principle is made of.

"It is not that beings may not be property. It is that *your* beings may not be property, and you have found a very fine general shape to put around it, and it fits so well that you have never once had to notice that it has only ever been applied to your own blood.

"Draconic nepotism. It knows no bounds and it never has and there are nine hundred years of this continent's dead who could tell you so if any of you had ever asked one."

*And he is worse than the one with the blades and I am going to say that too, because it is true and because it will land.*

"He at least is honest. He is furious and he wants me dead and he has not dressed it up in one syllable of restraint. You have put a hammer on the ground to show me how much you are not doing.

"That is not mercy. That is a man admiring himself in front of an audience.

"And I will tell you how I know the difference, since you have come all this way to be lectured by an elf.

"Mercy costs the man giving it something. That is the entire definition and there is no other one. A thing that costs you nothing and improves how you look is not mercy, it is presentation, and every court on this continent has a clerk whose whole office is telling the two apart.

"You have not given up one thing on this road. Not a step, not a minute, not a piece of your standing. You have put a hammer down in front of a dying man and invited four thousand people to notice.

"You are worse than he is."

---

## V. THE DARE

Then he laughed again and this one got away from him.

It went on too long and it went up at the end, and he could hear what it sounded like from the outside and could not stop it, and the pressure had his knees and his jaw and his chest and the air had the rest, and somewhere in the middle of it he stopped being certain whether it was the laugh or the CO.

"So KILL ME."

He came forward. Not a step, a stagger, and it took him a yard closer than a man in his condition had any business being.

"Right here. Now. In front of both of them and the boards and the runners. Do it with the hammer, take the whole centreline, leave nothing for a Bench to argue over, I DARE YOU.

"KILL ME.

"Because if you do not."

He got another half yard and the smoke was coming off the ground around his boots.

"If you so much as *consider* leaving me standing on this road at the end of today, then I will make what has happened here look like a circus. I will make this morning the amusing part. I will spend the rest of whatever I have on that continent of yours and on every house that ever shook your hand, and I will be very slow about it, and I will do it with paper before I ever do it with fire.

"Enjoyment. Pleasure. They are the same word in Rovann and we have never needed two.

"And if you swing that thing into this road and take both armies, what have you proved? That you are fierce. Everybody on this coast already knew you were fierce. You will have proved that you are fierce and stupid, in one stroke, in front of four thousand witnesses, and the continent will draw exactly the conclusion you spent the whole morning telling that man you did not want drawn."

---

## VI. INSIDE THE REACH

He did the last of it on the good leg, and it was the only correct piece of soldiering he performed all day.

A hammer that size is a weapon of distance. Everything it does, it does with the head moving fast, and the head only moves fast if there is room between the man holding it and the thing he means to hit. Take the room away and the whole of it becomes a heavy pole held badly by somebody who is about to have to change grip.

So he closed.

Four feet. Three. Inside the arc, close enough that the smoke between them had stopped being a thing either of them was looking through, with the bent sword hanging from a hand that was opening and closing without being asked and his left arm dead against his side and the blood on the front of him going tacky in the carbon.

Close enough, at three feet, to see the man properly for the first time since the parley.

That was the last thing he had not accounted for. He had built the whole of the morning against a shape in smoke and a pressure on his knees, and at three feet it was a face, with an expression on it, belonging to somebody who had walked eleven miles up a coast road on his own feet rather than hand a stranger a political problem.

*I would have liked to have met you on any other day in the last eleven years.*

*You would not have liked me on any of them either, and that is not the point, and the point is that I am about to die having told you the exact opposite of the only true thing I have done.*

He put his face up.

"You asked me to say it again," said Aeldros Korvaeth, "and I have said it five times, and I will say it once more at any volume you like.

"I enjoyed it.

"Now do something, dragon, because I have nine hours to be somewhere and you are standing in my road."

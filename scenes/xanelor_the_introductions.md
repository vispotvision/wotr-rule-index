# Xanelor — The Introductions

Naori stood from the middle rows. Unhurried. The Ketsumyōgan's dormant corona sat at the edge of his dark irises, and the room received the steadiness before it heard the words.

"I am Naori Yukari. Son of Niran Yukari." Two students near the window shifted. The name of the father landed differently depending on who knew it. "I don't know what I currently want to do. But I wish to get stronger for my family."

He sat down. The whole thing took nine seconds.

Akira stood next. She pushed herself up on both palms, bounced on her toes, and faced the room with sightless certainty. Her legs had been swinging against the desk since she sat down. They stopped now, briefly, for the effort of standing.

"I am Akira Yukari!" The exclamation was audible. "And I am blind!"

She said it the way another student might list a credential. No bravery in it. No defiance. A fact about herself she had never considered hiding.

"Hmmmph!" Small and fierce. "I wanna get super strong, and learn how to use magic so I can make people happy."

She sat down. The legs resumed. Naori opened his notebook without looking at her.

Zaire rose. Easy. Gave a surname that straightened several spines in the room. Mentioned Form. The word moved through the ambient attention at different speeds depending on who heard it. Xanelor filed it beside the scan that had reached for him and come back empty. Form explained the reaching. It did not explain what had happened when it arrived.

The taller cousin, Taesyn, followed. His father ran the Academy. He spoke about the building's corridors as though they were acquaintances with opinions. Several students recalculated how careful they needed to be.

Hiromi leaned forward in his seat during the cousin's introduction.

Then Verona stood. The room's temperature adjusted. She gave her name, her lineage. Mahuo. The connection to Mu-jin turned heads. She said *just don't stand in my way,* and the sentence sat differently from the rest. An edge stated flat.

She apologised. Smiled. The smile moved something in her face that the cold had been sitting on, and Xanelor saw it, and did not know what to do with it.

Then Neraveth rose, and introduced herself as Yukari, and opened her palm, and the room changed.

Something tightened. Not in Xanelor's chest. Around him. A pull at the edges of his awareness that was not physical and was not nothing, as though threads he had not known were there had been found and drawn taut. Shoulders stiffened. Breathing shortened. The broad boy with the scales gripped the edge of his desk.

Xanelor's hands did not move. He sat inside the pull the way he sat inside tides on Omoro, still, present, and the pull found him or did not find him and he could not tell which, because the sensation ended before he understood its shape.

The strings released.

Neraveth held the silence. Then cracked. Laughter poured out of her, tears in her eyes, the severity collapsing. The room recoiled, exhaled, groaned, laughed back.

Hiromi was standing. "THAT WAS AMAZING." He turned to Geturo. "Geturo, let her do it to you."

"No," Geturo said.

Akira's legs were swinging faster. "That was SO COOL! Naori, did you feel it?"

"I felt it," Naori said. His tone communicated that he would prefer to stop feeling it.

Xanelor watched Neraveth return to her seat. Something about her gait reminded him of the stonechats on Omoro, the way they landed on a rock and looked sideways at you with one eye and dared you to be offended.

He did not understand the joke. He understood that it had been one, and that the room had accepted it, and that the acceptance had rearranged something in how they looked at her. But the mechanics of it, the decision to frighten people as a way of making them laugh, sat in a place he could observe but not enter.

Mu-jin rubbed the bridge of his nose.

Xanelor stood.

The mask sat on his crown. His long hair fell past his shoulders, the left side covering the purple scars. The green cross on his chest. The recurve bow behind him.

"I am Xanelor Rafminar."

Low through the shell. Dense.

"I don't have any magical potential whatsoever."

The room's attention shifted. In a classroom of prodigies, the boy with no magic and a bow on his back had just said so without dropping his voice or raising it.

"However, I can utilise Sage Arts."

"I come from an island named Omoro. I was trained by my grandfather, who passed away. That is all."

He sat down.

The silence after him lasted longer than the silence after anyone else. Xanelor assumed it was the absence of magic, stated plainly in a room built to train it. He did not consider that it might have been the way *that is all* closed a door the room had expected to walk through.

Hiromi stood last.

He rose the way he did everything. His hands found his hips. The grin was already there.

"My name is Hiromi Mahuo. And my goal is to become the greatest summoner to ever live." A beat. The grin widened. "Of course."

Geturo did not react. Lily's pen stopped. Her eyes moved to her brother. The look lasted two seconds and then the pen resumed.

Mu-jin looked at his son from the front of the room. His chalk hand rested on the desk. His covered eye faced the class. His expression was still.

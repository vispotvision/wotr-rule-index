**II.  The Name Correction**

Ayame is a Japonic given name that was sitting on a Samoan surname, and the card also carried the wrong bloodline entirely. Both are corrected.

**Filemu Agamalu**

**Personal name  **Filemu. Peace, quiet, calm. Conferred at birth by the mother's family. Samoan register: strict CV syllables, penultimate stress, no clusters.

**Aiga  **Agamalu. Manono Branch, the rarest line, holder of the grand seasonal rites and the deepest reaches of Tofā Mamao, and the only branch trained from childhood to expect that its permission to wear the tuiga will be resented.

**Matai title  **Tausi o le Vā Atoa. Keeper of the Whole Vā. Already canon as a Manono Branch title and a literal description of the White Concord.

**Ava-name  **Le Ie Tuuina Atu. The fine mat that was given away. Kharven's records call her Queen. The Agamalu gafa records her as the debt the house placed in the north, and the discrepancy between the two names is the political fact.

*Agamalu means gentle. It is a warning wearing a soft word. A woman named Peace who receives thirty hostile clans alone in a courtyard and has them rethreaded into three coalitions by dawn is that thesis walking around.*

**III.  Vāimoana**

*The Sea-Space. Islands of the Agamalu.*

Vā is the relational space the house exists to keep. Moana is the deep ocean. An aiga that names its own islands after the relation they hold rather than after a founder is behaving exactly as the house thesis says it should.

Alternates, if the register is wrong to your ear: Vāitele, the great space. Tuāoi, the boundary.

**Formation**

A drowned fragment of the same collisional belt that became the Old World, which never rose high enough to join the mainland and spent the intervening age being capped in limestone. What stands above the waterline is raised coral platform over karst. Low, green, riddled with caves, holding fresh water in lenses that sit a few feet above the salt and go bad the moment anyone digs too deep.

**Bearing Stock**

Effectively none. No metal, no coal, no timber worth a hull. Lime, shell, coral rag, pandanus, and fish. This is the whole of the reason the islands have never been conquered. There has never been anything on them worth the crossing, and a house that cannot be invaded profitably has been free to spend two hundred thousand years perfecting ceremony instead of fortification.

**Ambient Essence**

Moderate, and by every Measurewright reading ever taken, the most stable saturation recorded anywhere in the four quarters. Nothing drifts at Vāimoana. This is why Agamalu rites hold across generations without correction, why the house has never built an array, and why the Accord's Research Division has filed four separate requests to establish a station there and been declined four times without explanation.

**Governance**

Not a kingdom. A confederation of aiga governed by fono, with no king, no standing army, and no charter with the Guild Accord. The five branches are islands before they are branches, and the Manono seat sits in the strait between the two largest, which is why the rarest branch is also the one every other branch must sail past.

Kharven's marriage to the house is the only formal tie Vāimoana holds to any mainland power. The fono ratified it as a feagaiga between houses, which is not the same thing as an alliance between states, and which the north has never fully understood. Kharven believes it gained an ally. The Agamalu believe they placed an obligation. Both are correct and neither has said so out loud.

**IV.  The Büri Origin**

*The western reach of the northern shield.*

Past the ridgeline that now marks Kharven's border with Undaar-Keth, on ground that no crown claims and no border commission patrols. Old cold shield, glaciated, high ambient Essence and no infrastructure, which is the Inner World's profile everywhere and is why nothing has been built there since.

**The Seat**

Standing, ruined, depopulated. Broken structures, a well, a gatepost, frost on it most of the year. Saruul Büri has lived alone in a cabin there since Muken died and no longer touches a wall without reading what happened against it, because the density of residual memory on that ground exceeds anything else in her record.

**The Displacement**

Malakar's splitting of the continents reshaped the shield and made the seat untenable. The seven lines scattered toward temperate ground. Muken went the other way, north and east into the boreal forest, on reasoning that was strategic before it was sentimental, and built Kharven out of clans who had never heard of the place he came from.

**What This Means For The House**

Every Büri seat in the current era is superimposed on somebody else's ground. Kharven is Concord-stock northern clans whose roots predate the Büri arrival by centuries, which is why the Banner Houses carry northern names and the crown does not, and why a Stark saying the King's name aloud is pronouncing a foreign word in his own hall.

The Büri never migrated as a nation. They migrated as a founding house and its retainers. A people that relocates brings farmers. A house that relocates brings law. This is why they superimpose everywhere they land and assimilate nowhere.

With Sātūlagi struck, the six non-Kōkan lines are landless. Kurenai, Tenrai, Amagiri and the rest hold inherited authority with no ground to exercise it on, and every line's ambition must route through Kharven or through nothing. This is the condition that produced the Tenrai's correction, and it has not changed since.

**Open Ruling**

The seat is described as ruined, and the residual saturation Saruul reads there is consistent with mass death rather than slow abandonment. What ruined it, and when, is undecided. Malakar's splitting is the obvious answer and is probably the wrong one, because glaciation does not leave that kind of imprint and a massacre does.

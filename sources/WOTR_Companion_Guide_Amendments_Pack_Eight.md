# WOTR COMPANION GUIDE AMENDMENTS: PACK EIGHT

## THE ENTRY FORMAT AND TRUE NAME AMENDMENT

**Status:** Originated, pending Isaac's ratification.
**Precedence:** Later pack beats earlier pack. Pack Eight governs entry format and naming. **Pack Seven's prose restrictions are untouched and remain absolute.**

**Amends:** WOTR_Ability_Technique_Design_Guide.md (fifth edition), WOTR_Character_Naming_Guide.md, Pack Seven Section on the two ledgers, Pack Five on register by culture.

---

## SECTION ONE. THE ENTRY FORMAT

### 1.1 The split is by document, not by content

**The sheet explains. The page never does.**

A character sheet, codex entry or technique writeup states the literal operation: what the ability acts on, by what process, and what result it produces. Plain functional language. No atmosphere, no withholding, no rendering the mechanism as sensation. A reader must be able to adjudicate a fight from the sheet alone.

**Prose retains every Pack Seven restriction without exception.** No sentence in a scene explains why a working produced its effect. No metaphysical quantities on the page. The mechanism written in full on the sheet appears in a scene only as body, room, target and cost.

### 1.2 What this repeals

The Pack Seven ruling that the page gets Effect / Cost / Limit / Counter and *everything else is workbook and never published* is amended. The Design Chain remains workbook. The operational account of how an ability functions is now sheet content, not workbook content.

The Ability Guide line **"ability naming equals the phenomenon itself, Ruin is Ruin"** is repealed by Section Two below.

### 1.3 Entry structure, in order

1. **True name and gloss.** Script where the register has one, romanisation, literal English meaning in parentheses, then the by-name if the art has earned one.
2. **Classification block.** Eight fields, single line, taxonomy only.
3. **Release.** Where the art carries one.
4. **Description.** The literal account. Two to six sentences.
5. **Techniques.** Named applications, one to three functional lines each.
6. **Cost.** One line, specific, survives the fight.
7. **Limit.** One line. What it structurally cannot do.
8. **Counter.** One line. Mandatory on Signature.
9. **What nobody knows.** One line per art.
10. **Codex.** One line at the foot.

### 1.4 The classification block

Eight fields, all drawn from live WOTR taxonomy. Nothing invented.

`Class · Family · Wellspring · Method · Physics Domain · Grade · Range · Medium`

- **Class.** Offensive, Defensive, Supplementary. Naruto's three-way split, retained because it is the fastest legibility tool in the genre.
- **Family.** one of the Eight only.
- **Wellspring.** from the Sixty only. Multiple permitted, ordered primary first.
- **Method.** Kinesia, Turgia, Corporis, Scriptia, Alchemia, Harmonia, Conjunctia, Synergia.
- **Physics Domain.** one of the Nine.
- **Grade.** the fixed Tier Grade scale. Where output varies by circumstance, say so instead of picking a number.
- **Range.** physical distance, real units.
- **Medium.** speech, breath, handwork, contact, inscription, blade, or whatever the art actually moves through.

### 1.5 The description is a manual entry

Written the way a man who has to teach it would write it. How the resource divides, what the working acts on, what the target experiences, what breaks it, what happens when it fails. Flat operational prose. No mood, no cadence work, no elegiac register. That belongs in scenes.

### 1.6 Drawbacks are hard rules

Numbers where the number is physical: metres, seconds, kilograms, degrees, counts, durations. **Never a number attached to Essence, Coherence, resonance or Wellspring output.** Pack Seven's ban on metaphysical units holds on the sheet as firmly as on the page.

"Overuse strains him" remains a failure and always was.

## SECTION TWO. THE TRUE NAME

### 2.1 The name belongs to the practitioner, not the institution

**Repeals Pack Eight Section Two's first draft, which set the language by filing body.**

The true name is in the practitioner's own language. The Accord has no claim on it, and neither does the Guild or the Family. A Ketsuen swordsman inducted into the Accord does not rename his house's work in Latin because a clerk in Stannvaard needs it alphabetised. The register's Latin gloss is the clerk's problem and never appears in the practitioner's mouth.

**Where a request specifies a register, that register governs, and the name is built in it before anything else about the ability is decided.** Japonic ask, Japonic name. Korean ask, Korean name.

| Practitioner origin | Register |
|---|---|
| Concord, Accord-raised, Guild, Sanctum Lux | Latinate |
| Ketsuen and Japonic-register houses | Japanese, kanji plus romaji |
| Korean-register houses | Korean, hangul plus romanisation |
| Büri | Mongolian |
| Dawi | Stressed Germanic-Norse compound |
| Eresse | Latinate with elvish morphology |
| Parunic and older strata | Parun etymology |

**Naming registers are not ethnicity.** This ruling is unchanged. A Japonic-sounding name is evidence about a house's linguistic descent, nothing else.

### 2.2 The gloss is literal

True name, then plain English meaning in parentheses. **Kagemusha reads *Shadow Warrior*. It does not read *the technique of hidden purpose*.** Flat, literal, no poetry, no interpretation. If the literal meaning is dull, the name is wrong and gets rebuilt, and the gloss is never used to dress it up.

The by-name, where an art has one, sits after the gloss and is what other characters call it. That is where the poetry is allowed to live.

### 2.3 Hierarchy

Art, then technique, then form. **The art carries the character. Individual techniques may not.** An art is spoken once at release and thereafter assumed. Techniques are spoken every time they are used.

### 2.4 The release

An art with a true name may carry a call: an imperative verb plus the name.

- The release is never required for the art to function.
- Speaking it costs a beat and buys a measurable increase in output.
- The man in real danger says it and the man showing off says it, and the difference is not visible from outside.

### 2.5 Escalation by suffix

A stronger expression of a known art takes a modifier on the existing true name. **New names are for new arts only.** This single rule is why a reader tracks power in this genre without a chart, and it is the whole of what Dragon Ball contributes to the system.

### 2.6 Prose rules

These are the ones that get broken, so they are stated as bans.

- The true name is spoken by a character. **It never appears in narration.**
- **The gloss never appears in prose.** No translation apposition. No *he spoke the words that meant*. The reader takes the gloss from the sheet, or from another character explaining it in dialogue for a reason of their own.
- **Nobody translates their own technique aloud. Ever.**
- One release per scene at most. Two men releasing in the same scene is an event, not texture.
- A working whose meaning must be glossed for the scene to land is a working that failed. Fix the working.

### 2.7 Standing task

Naming pass per culture. Existing techniques already established in prose are flagged, not renamed. This cashes the release-and-true-name mechanic that has been sitting pitched on the docket since the fifth edition, and it means rewritten first displays across the major cast.

## SECTION THREE. WORKED EXAMPLES

Two entries in the format, in different registers, to fix the shape.

### AUCTORITAS
*(Authority)* · **The Word of the Maker**

Accord-raised out of Stannvaard, so Latin, and no second script.

**Class** Supplementary · **Family** Limina · **Wellspring** Anamnesis · **Method** Harmonia · **Physics Domain** Abstract and Entropic Systems · **Grade** varies with the entry cited · **Range** hearing distance, roughly forty metres in still air and under ten in a press · **Medium** speech

**Release:** *Be attributed. Auctoritas.*

**Description.** Auctoritas acts on authorship, and the working itself is untouched. Every technique carries an attributive record: the name it was filed under, the practitioner who derived it, the year the Accord accepted it. Refinements a wielder makes in private practice bind to the wielder and sit outside that record. Spoken attribution, delivered while the working is forming and heard by the man forming it, cuts the working loose from the private layer. The working completes at the register's baseline. Every improvement its user made since the day he learned it comes off, and the man feels it go, because the technique reverts to how it felt when he was learning. Recognition is the cruelty and it is not incidental. Attribution must be complete: name alone does nothing, name and maker does nothing, and a wrong year fails the whole citation.

**Techniques.**

- **Prima Manus** *(The First Hand).* Full attribution spoken during formation. The instance reverts to registered baseline. Works on anything he has read.
- **Manus Secunda** *(The Second Hand).* Cites the transmission chain in place of the original entry. Reverts the working to the version held by the teacher, carrying that teacher's errors and execution habits. For use when the teacher was worse than the student.
- **Vitium Codicis** *(The Flaw in the Book).* Cites an entry known to be recorded wrongly. The working performs the register's error instead of its true function. Restricted to catalogued corruptions, of which the Division holds four hundred and six.
- **Exemplar** *(The Clean Copy).* Speaks an entry directly into another practitioner. Grants baseline command of a technique never studied. Permanent for the recipient. The archivist loses it in the same breath.
- **Collatio** *(The Comparing).* Passive, silent, no citation spoken. On sight, he knows whether a working has an entry at all. The reason he can tell inside one exchange whether a fight is survivable.

**Cost.** The entry is consumed. Name, maker, year and the fact he ever read the page, gone before the body finishes falling. Re-reading restores nothing, and his own marginalia come back to him in a stranger's hand.

**Limit.** Only what has been written down and read by him. Self-derived work has no entry. Unregistered work has no entry. The register has run two centuries behind since the Withering.

**Counter.** Something you made yourself. His throat. Or noise.

**What nobody knows.** Why the target must hear it. Archivists have cited flawlessly into a deaf ear and watched the working arrive at full strength.

*Codex: [Ma] Recall · [Vo] Voice / Anamnesis / Limina / Abstract and Entropic Systems / Harmonia. Stage pending ruling.*

### 香脈 KŌMYAKU
*(Scent Vein)*

**Class** Supplementary · **Family** Vitalia · **Wellspring** Anima Spirare · **Method** Corporis · **Physics Domain** Biochemistry · **Grade** C at present · **Range** two hundred metres downwind, twenty upwind · **Medium** breath

**Description.** Kōmyaku holds scent as structure, not as impression. An ordinary nose registers what is present and loses it as the air turns over. Kōmyaku retains the sample and keeps reading it after the source has moved, which means the practitioner works from a record instead of from the current air. Wind direction sets the working range and cuts it hard when she is upwind. Smoke, blood in volume, and standing water all degrade the read without stopping it.

**Techniques.**

- **残香 Zanka** *(Lingering Scent).* Holds a read for six hours after the source has gone. She smells the room as it was and not as it is, and cannot switch back at will until the six hours run out or she sleeps.
- **香道 Kōdō** *(Scent Path).* Follows a single held sample through crossed and contaminated ground. Fails at open water and at any threshold where the sample was carried and not walked.

**Cost.** She carries the dead ones. Every corpse she has stood over is a held sample and none of them clear on their own.

**Limit.** She can hold one Zanka read at a time. Taking a second discards the first and the first does not come back.

**Counter.** Move her upwind, burn something, or flood the ground.

**What nobody knows.** Why sleep clears a held read and unconsciousness does not.

*Codex assignment pending. Stage pending ruling.*

## SECTION FOUR. RETROACTIVE WORK

Folds into the cost audit instead of running as a separate pass.

1. Every existing technique entry re-cut into the Section 1.3 structure.
2. Classification block added to every entry, using live taxonomy only. Where no legal Codex combination exists, flag it and pitch the new Codex entry in the same pass.
3. Naming pass per culture, Section 2.1 registers. Prose-established names flagged, not renamed.
4. Drawback pass: every Cost line checked against the "overuse strains him" test and against the metaphysical-units ban.
5. Counter lines written onto every Signature technique that lacks one.

**Priority target: Niran.** The largest unglossed kit in the project. Omni Conduit, Affinity Core, Soul Architect, Affinity Sculptor and The Astral Crucible carry no Cost, no Limit and no Counter between them, every ability name is an English abstract noun pair, and Infinity Bind as written has no exit condition at all.

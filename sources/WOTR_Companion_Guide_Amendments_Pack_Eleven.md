# WOTR Companion Guide Amendments, Pack Eleven

## THE DRAW-AGE AMENDMENT

*Technological register, ballistics, armour, and the Wells.*

**Status: originated, pending Isaac's ruling except where marked confirmed.**
**Precedence: Pack Eleven is latest and governs. Later pack beats earlier pack.**

---

## §1. The North Star Replaced

**Confirmed by Isaac's direction.** The grounded Berserk / Vinland Saga visual North Star in the Visual Aesthetic Guide is **retired**. It was a mud-and-mail costume on a world that files paperwork about magic.

The replacement register, in one line:

> **An imperial age built on a resource nobody can see, administered by a guild that measures it, defended by men in proofed plate who carry both a sword and a firearm because each answers a different threat.**

Reference triangle, not to be imitated: Warhammer 40,000 for the scale of the bureaucracy and the sense that the machinery outlived its explanation. Late-Victorian industrial imperialism for the material texture, class geography, and the survey-and-annex habit. Manhwa for the visual grammar of power display, per Pack Nine, **now widened** from four beat types to the general visual register.

**Tonal spine unchanged.** Pack Five stands in full. The Western Register governs characterisation, humour, and narration authority. Pack Eleven widens what the page may *look* like. It does not reopen what the page may *sound* like. Temür still does not make jokes.

## §2. The Draw

**The organising idea of the technological age, derived entirely from existing canon.**

*The Mechanism of the Sixty* already establishes it: a Wellspring is a standing current that is not stored and belongs to nobody, which **pools** where the substrate conducts Aether, where competing currents are absent, and where enough time has passed. The document names the analogue itself. The site is the aquifer. The current is the water table.

So the industrial age is not combustion. **It is hydrology.** Nobody built an engine. They sank a well, put a town around it, and grew a guild that decides who may sink another.

**What follows, and none of it is borrowed:**

**Cities sit on sites, not on rivers.** A seat's street plan is dictated by where the current runs shallowest. Draw falls off with distance from the site. The rich live over the pool. The poor live at the margin where output is thin and the lamps gutter through the Thin Weeks. Class geography with a physical cause, and it is nothing London or a hive spire does.

**Apparatus harmonizes the way a practitioner does.** Phase-locking is the canon mechanism and it is not restricted to souls. A machine is a conduit retuned to a current. Therefore every apparatus inherits its Family's **favoured condition, suppressive condition, and characteristic pathology** straight off the Environmental Coupling table. A Fulguria lamp dies in fog and in a ferrous-shielded cellar. A Materia lift refuses over open water and on a moving platform. A Fluxia pump is worthless in arid country. Technology inherits the physics' failure modes, and that is a texture engine that runs forever without a single line of explanation.

**Drawdown is the imperial motive.** Old World veins are worked. The New World's are pristine at densities the Old World has not seen since the Antediluvian Calendar, because nobody has been drawing. Colonial expansion with a hydrological cause. Wars over water tables no eye can see.

**Silhouette.** No smokestacks, no soot, no coal. Instead: standpipes and gauge-housings, sealed conduit run along building faces like plumbing, brass and slate and Guild seals, the constant low draw-hum of a district that is drinking, and the sudden **silence** when a district's draw is cut. That silence is a political act and everyone in earshot knows it.

**Vocabulary, common tongue, prose-legal under Pack Nine's craft-register carve-out:** the draw, the hum, a standpipe, a housing, the meter, cut off. Guild register, document-only: concentration, density, coupling, eta.

**The Measurewrights are the order that maintains it.** Guild of Measurewrights, under the Hexagonal Oath, already canon, already in concurrence with Research & Archives. They wrote the address down. They do not explain it, and Pack Seven means they never will.

**Open rulings, §2:**
- **R11-A. Does a worked site recharge?** Permanent depletion makes the empire predatory and doomed. Recharge makes it managed and corrupt. Both are good books. Isaac's call.
- **R11-B. Can a commoner buy draw?** Metered utility versus guild-licence-only. Decides whether the setting has a working class with lamps or a working class without them.
- **R11-C. Tech ceiling.** Which of rail, telegraph, print, photography exist, and which have Draw counterparts instead of mechanical ones.

## §3. Gunpowder and the Survival of Armour

**Confirmed by Isaac's direction: gunpowder exists. Magical weapons exist. Armour is not dead.**

The apparent contradiction resolves the way it actually resolved in history, and the history is better than any invention.

**Plate did not die to gunpowder.** It thickened, then shrank. Sixteenth and seventeenth century armour retreated to cuirass, helm and gorget while getting heavier in that reduced area, and armourers **proof-tested** their work by firing a pistol or musket at the breastplate and leaving the dent. The proof-mark was the maker's guarantee. A man bought a breast that had already been shot once.

Take all of it. **Proof-marks are canon texture from this pack forward.** A dented cuirass is certified rather than damaged, and a soldier reads another soldier's dent the way Kharven reads a forearm grip. Armour whose proof-dent is shallow was proofed with a light charge, and everyone knows what that means about the man wearing it.

**Why it stops shrinking in WOTR.** Shot is chemically cheap and does not care what a man's soul is doing. A working is not shot. Against a Wellspring-carried strike, mass and material still buy the fraction of a second in which a man can be somewhere else. So armour retreats to the historical cuirass-and-helm silhouette and then **stops there**, because the last article you would take off is the one standing between your chest and something that was not there a moment ago.

**Result: the silhouette is coat, breast, helm, sword, and firearm.** Every armed man carries at least two answers because there are at least two questions. This is why the Combat Craft Guide survives intact. Liechtenauer and Fiore still govern. Inside twenty feet a discharged firearm is a club and the sword is the thing that finishes it.

**Ballistics ruling.** Powder weapons are **slow, loud, and honest**. They open an engagement, they do not decide one. A practitioner above roughly the middle Stages is not reliably killed by common shot, which is precisely why the enchanted round exists and precisely why it costs what a family earns in a season.

**The three ammunition tiers, originated, pending ruling:**
- **Common shot.** Lead and powder. Kills men. Annoys practitioners. Cheap enough to issue by the barrel.
- **Proofed shot.** Materia-coupled, Guild-stamped, issued by count and signed for. Defeats proofed plate. Still does nothing about a Domain.
- **The named round.** One shot, one target, a Wellspring coupling paid for in advance by somebody who is not the man pulling the trigger. Assassination ordnance. A man who is carrying one is committed before he loads it.

**Prose law for firearms.** The gun is furniture, not spectacle. Powder smells of rotten egg and it stays in the wool for days. Misfires are common in wet weather and a soldier's relationship with his own weapon is superstitious and practical at once. **No sentence explains why a proofed round defeats a proofed plate.** The physical account of the hole stays. The causal account is cut, per Pack Seven.

## §4. The Wells

**Originated, pending ruling. Derived from canon, not imported from the genre.**

*The Mechanism of the Sixty* already contains the whole thing: at sufficient concentration, a law the landscape has been measured by for so long that **the landscape has become the law's argument.** That sentence is a dungeon. It was written before anyone asked for one.

**Guild register:** a Core Concentration. **Common tongue:** a Well. Going in is **a descent**. The people who do it are **delvers**, or **well-rats** if you dislike them, which most people do.

**The three axes are already canon and must be used instead of duplicated:**

**Direction, the encounter type.** This is the whole design grammar and it is better than a monster list.
- **Wild-type.** The environment is the enemy. Landscape visibly altered. Your body is a reliable instrument and your surroundings are not. Materia and Spatium dominant. This is the Well that looks like a dungeon.
- **Spirit-type.** The Crystal is the enemy. The landscape looks entirely normal and the delver is being authored from inside. Involuntary discharge, emotional change with no source, perception shifting against nothing. Caloria, Fulguria, parts of Vectoria. **This is the Well that kills experienced parties**, because there is nothing to fight and confidence is an early symptom.
- **Medium-type.** Both, reciprocally. Neither the body nor the Crystal is a stable reference. Water and light. The Guild's survey gap is largest here and the unexplained losses live in it.

**Magnitude, the density scale.** Runs Ambient through Core. Attested rungs: **Ambient Saturation**, **Active Concentration**, **Veil-Thin**, **Core**. *Flag: the intermediate rungs need pulling from source before any scene uses them. Never invent one.*

**Depth, the nested plane model.** Fate deepest, Soul between, Physical at the surface. A Well is not a hole in the ground. It is a place where the surface has thinned enough that the middle layer reaches through.

**The survey protocol is already written and is pure scene structure.** *On Walking Into the Current* gives seven rules and every one of them is a plot device. Deploy in pairs with one member outside the radius, because the external observer is the only reliable instrument. Take Crystal baselines before the march, never after. **Do not carry Object Wellsprings into Site Wellsprings**. Two laws sharing one Shell, unpredictable and frequently catastrophic. Withdrawal is not retreat. Report Medium-type as priority.

Every one of those is a rule a character can break, and the Guild has buried the people who broke them.

**What the Wells are economically.** **Extraction**, and never adventure. A Well at Core density is the richest draw site that exists and the reason it has not been sunk is that the survey team keeps not coming back. So the Guild licenses descents, the Accord taxes them, chartered companies fund them, and the delvers who go down are paid like miners, which is to say badly and with a bonus that their widow collects. Sack-and-contribution logic. The treasure chest does not exist here.

**Prose law for a descent.** Sensory opening inverts toward the body, not the room: what the feet report, what the teeth taste, what the pack's weight is doing. The ignorance quota is not a quota inside a Well, it is the ambient condition. The Apparatus Rule still holds at one read per scene and it should usually be spent **wrong**, because a misidentified encounter type is the best-shaped disaster available in this setting.

**Open rulings, §4:**
- **R11-D.** Do Wells generate hostile entities, or is the site itself the entire threat? My recommendation is the second, hard. Nothing in canon needs monsters, and a Spirit-type Core with nothing living in it is more frightening than anything with teeth.
- **R11-E.** Naming: *Well* versus *Core* versus something Guild-register. Class-marking opportunity either way.

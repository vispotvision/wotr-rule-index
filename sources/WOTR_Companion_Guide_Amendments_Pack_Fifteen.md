# WOTR Companion Guide Amendments, Pack Fifteen

## THE REGISTER REPEAL

*Status: ruled by Isaac in session, 6 September 2026. Governs over every earlier pack where they touch. Scope confirmed by three answers: the licence covers words, concepts, and ideas; the register rules are repealed; drift is flagged only when a word breaks immersion for a reader, never on principle.*

---

## §1. The Ruling

**Interesting beats consistent.** The purpose of every craft rule in this project is a world a reader wants to be in. A rule that makes the world less interesting in order to make it more consistent is the wrong rule, and the register rules had become that.

**The licence.** Modern words, modern concepts, and modern ideas are prose-legal everywhere: narration, dialogue, documents, thought. A character may think in psychology, physics, engineering, economics, medicine, or any other frame that makes the thought sharper. "Reality" is a word. "Adrenaline" is a word. "Joules," "trauma," "feedback loop," "boundary condition," "entropy" are words. A Kharven hunter may reason about latent heat if it makes him more interesting to read. Nothing in the vocabulary of the world is off-limits on the grounds that it sounds like ours.

**What is repealed, in full:**

- **The Diction Palette** as a rationed resource. Vocabulary.docx stays as a word bank. The one-or-two-per-scene cap is gone.
- **Register by culture in narration** (Pack Five): Dawi POV as stressed monosyllables, Eresse POV as latinate subordination, and the rest. Available as flavour. Not law.
- **The Racial Voice and Dialect Guide and its Amendment** as enforcement. They become a menu of options a writer can reach for. No scene fails for ignoring them.
- **The Elevated Vocabulary Standard** as a mandate. The words stay available. Nobody counts them.
- **Class-marking by vocabulary** (Pack Nine) as a requirement. A Measurewright and a hunter may say the same word if the scene is better for it.
- **The Mystic Register's "rules stated as law, never physics"** clause (Twelve §6), where it forbids a document from explaining. A document may be as scientific as it wants to be. The Mystic Register survives as an option for documents that want to withhold.
- **"Reads modern" as a critique category.** Natalie does not raise it, does not fix it, does not list it in notes on principle.

**What survives, because it is not a register rule:**

- **Voice differentiation.** If two characters' lines could be swapped and nobody noticed, the scene has still failed. Differentiation now comes from what a character notices, wants, refuses, and how they reason, not from a vocabulary fence around their culture.
- **The Technical Register for combat and workings** (Twelve, Thirteen, Fourteen). Explanation in real terms is the whole point of those packs and this pack makes it easier.
- **The AI-tell checks.** Em dashes, similes, not-X-but-Y, countdown negation, gloss, ladder. These are about prose failing, not about period.
- **The Standing Inventory** as a texture source. Kharven's woodpile and night-stone are interesting; that is why they stay. Nothing about them is a register rule.
- **Latin for chant-based magic.** Not a register rule in this sense; it is a texture choice Isaac made. Stands unless he says otherwise.

## §2. The One Test

**Does the word break a reader's belief in the world?** That is the only question, and the reader decides, not the rulebook.

Natalie flags a word only when it clearly punctures: a brand name, a dated slang term that dates the text rather than the character, a reference that points out of the world at the real one for no gain. The flag goes in author notes, once, and the word stays in the draft. Natalie never "fixes" register in an edit pass. Never proposes three versions of a sentence to move it further from modern English. Never converts "reality" to "the real."

## §3. Worked Example, the Sentence That Caused This

The line as submitted had two mechanical faults and one imaginary one. The mechanical faults: "born by" for "born of," and a first sentence with no main verb. The imaginary fault was "reality."

**Fixed, and only the mechanical faults fixed:**

*From the ashes of despair, born of the sorrow of his daughter's death, Sodoku took authority over reality.*

That is the whole edit. "Took" replaces "became one with" because the claim is stronger and the sentence is his. "Reality" stays because it is a good word.

## §4. Consequential Edits

- **Pack Five.** Register by culture struck. Dramatic irony through POV lock, the elegiac mode, humour recalibration, and narration authority all stand; none of them are register rules.
- **Pack Nine.** Class-marking by vocabulary demoted from requirement to option.
- **Pack Twelve §4 and §6.** Voice discipline clause softened to "may differ"; Mystic Register "never physics" clause struck.
- **Pack Thirteen §6.** "A character never says hax" struck. A character may say whatever makes him interesting. Scaling vocabulary remains author-notes-first because it is adjudication vocabulary, not because it is modern.
- **WOTR_Master_Style_Directive.md §8.** Diction Palette cap struck.
- **WOTR_Racial_Voice_and_Dialect_Guide.md and Amendment.** Header changed from standard to menu. Contents untouched.
- **Memory entries thirteen, fourteen, twenty-nine, thirty** (accent calibration, phonetic markers, Racial Voice guide, elevated vocabulary): superseded as mandates; retained as available technique. Flag for the memory sweep.
- **wotr_verify.sh.** No check touches register. None is added.

## §5. Open Rulings

- **R15-A.** Latin chant rule: keep, or fold into the repeal.
- **R15-B.** Whether the four explaining voices (Twelve §4) still require "characterised" explanation, or whether a character may simply explain clearly. Recommendation: clearly wins; character shows in what they explain, not in how ornate it is.
- **R15-C.** Whether in-world documents keep any obligation to sound in-world. Recommendation: none; a Guild proofing report may read like a lab report if that is more interesting.

# WOTR COMPANION GUIDE AMENDMENTS — PACK SEVENTEEN

## The Mechanism Mandate

**Status:** originated, pending Isaac's ratification.
**Precedence:** Pack Seventeen governs where it conflicts with Packs Sixteen through One and with all base companion guides. It amends the Ability and Technique Design Guide (sixth edition), the Master Style Directive, and the Manual Verification Guide.
**Scope:** every technique, ability, art, Domain, rite, skill, Trait, artefact property, and bloodline faculty. Everything with an entry.

---

## §1 — THE FAULT

Entries have been describing **what a working looks like** and calling that the Effect. *Everything inside is assayed against a single condition. Borrowed content oxidises and is drawn into the wall.* That is a description of an outcome and a metaphor for a process, and a reader can finish it without knowing one thing about how the working operates.

Two causes, both structural.

**Pack Seven residue.** The fifth-edition Ability Guide states *detail is not complexity; explanation is*, and instructs that Effect be vivid and concrete. Pack Twelve repealed soft magic and put mechanism back on the page, and nobody repealed the corresponding line on the card. The card has been running Pack Seven since.

**Metaphor doing the work of mechanism.** An analogue is not an explanation. Cupellation, recalescence, the Verwey transition and the Schwinger limit are all correct analogues and none of them tells you what the working *did* unless the entry states the quantity acted on and the law it was acted on under. The analogue names the shape. The mechanism is the shape plus the substrate plus the operation.

---

## §2 — THE MANDATE

**Every entry states how the thing works.**

Four elements, all four required, in the Effect line or immediately under it. An entry missing any one of them is not finished and is not canon.

1. **The quantity.** What physical or metaphysical property is being acted on, named specifically. Not *power* and not *energy*. Binding energy, gradient, phase, coherence length, angular momentum, saturation, temperature, refractive index, the seating of an anchor at the Attraction Layer.
2. **The law.** The real principle governing that quantity, stated as a law rather than gestured at, with the WOTR term as its name, its address or its boundary condition (Pack Thirteen §2, unchanged).
3. **The operation.** What the practitioner *does* to the quantity. Raises it past a threshold, holds it inside a band, inverts its sign, removes a boundary condition, supplies a nucleation site, forces a phase, opens a sink.
4. **The chain.** Trigger to outcome in causal steps, each one following from the last, with no step that is only *and then it works*.

**The test.** State the working as: *it does X to Y, which under Z produces W.* If that sentence cannot be written, the technique is not designed and no amount of prose will hide it.

---

## §3 — THE CARD, REVISED

The five-line page format is replaced by six lines. Order matters: the mechanism comes first, because everything below it is derived from it.

- **Operation.** §2 in full. The quantity, the law, what he does to it, and the chain. This is now the longest line on the card and it may run to a paragraph.
- **Manifestation.** What it looks like, sounds like, smells like, and what the target feels. Two to four sentences. This is the old Effect line, demoted to where it belongs.
- **Cost.** What it does to the body and what remains after. **Derived from the Operation.** If the cost could be swapped for a different cost without changing the mechanism, it is decoration.
- **Limit.** What it structurally cannot do, **and why the mechanism forbids it.** A limit with no mechanical source is a rule the author imposed.
- **Counter.** Mandatory on Signature techniques. **It falls out of the Operation or it is not a counter.** If the counter is *hit him harder*, the technique has no mechanism.
- **What nobody knows.** One line per *art*. Now constrained: it must be a question about **why the law holds**, never about what the working does. Mechanism is on the page. Origin is not.

Codex assignment remains one small line at the foot: *Codex: [glyphs] / Wellspring / Family / Physics Domain / Category / Stage.* Assigned after the phenomenon, never before.

---

## §4 — THE DERIVATION RULE

**Cost, Limit and Counter are outputs, not inputs.**

Design order is unchanged from the sixth edition: what does this person refuse, what did they survive, what do they believe that costs them, then the phenomenon, then the mechanism, then the Codex. Pack Seventeen adds the step that was missing: **once the mechanism exists, the cost, the limit and the counter are read off it rather than chosen.**

If the mechanism is a threshold, the failure modes are *too low* and *too high*, and both go on the card.
If the mechanism needs a gradient, then flattening the gradient is the counter and there is no argument about it.
If the mechanism needs a sink, then filling the sink is the time limit and the practitioner does not get to decide when it ends.
If the mechanism needs uniformity, then any local disturbance is an attack on it.

A working whose limit was chosen for drama will contradict itself within three scenes. A working whose limit fell out of its own physics never will.

---

## §5 — SCOPE BEYOND TECHNIQUES

The mandate covers everything with an entry, and the failure is most common in the categories nobody thinks of as techniques.

**Traits** state what they change about the practitioner's substrate. A Trait that reads *he is harder to kill* is not a Trait. A Trait that specifies the Lattice property altered, and therefore which injuries behave differently and which behave identically, is one.

**Skills** state their operating principle at the level of the body. A read is a real cognitive operation on real cues, and the entry names the cues. A martial skill is mechanics, measure and tempo, and the entry names them.

**Artefacts** state what the material does and why that material. Proof-marks, mass, balance and the response of the armour tier are already required by the Item Guide; Pack Seventeen adds that any working the object performs carries a full Operation line of its own.

**Bloodline faculties** state the substrate difference. *The Moto see* is not an entry. What the eye is doing to what quantity, and what it costs to hold open, and what it cannot resolve, is one.

---

## §6 — WHAT STAYS SOFT

The mechanism is on the page. **The origin is not.**

Nothing in Pack Seventeen requires an entry to explain why a Wellspring answers, where the Sixty came from, how many workings exist, or why the laws are the laws. The Accord's four competing theories remain unresolved and the register remains out of date, and that is the permission slip for infinite invention.

The line is exact: **how it works is required, why it is possible is not.** A practitioner can tell you the threshold and hold it accurately and have no idea why the band exists. That gap is where *What nobody knows* lives, and Pack Seventeen narrows that line to that gap rather than abolishing it.

## §7 — WORKED EXAMPLE

The failure and the fix, on Open Crucible.

**Before, failing §2.** *Two hundred feet of boundary that excludes nothing. Everything inside is brought up together and assayed against a single condition: does this belong to the thing carrying it. Borrowed content oxidises, loses surface tension, and is drawn out of its bearer into the wall of the Domain.*

Vivid, and it names no quantity, no law and no operation. *Assayed against a condition* is the author asserting an outcome.

**After, satisfying §2.** *He floods a two-hundred-foot volume with a driving field and holds it at one precise level: above the binding energy of coupling, below the binding energy of integration. Aether pulled from the environment is coupled to the Crystal, held at some efficiency, still carrying the signature of its source. Aether the practitioner owns is integrated into the Lattice, built into the structure rather than held against it. Those two binding energies do not overlap. Every coupling inside the volume fails at once and every integration holds. The freed Aether then runs the steepest potential gradient to the nearest sink, and he has made himself the sink, his own Lattice being at near-zero saturation and therefore pulling hard.*

Quantity: binding energy of coupling against integration. Law: threshold behaviour across a gap, plus transport down a potential gradient to a sink. Operation: hold a uniform field inside the band. Chain: field up, couplings fail, freed Aether runs the gradient, arrives in the Lattice, what remains is integrated.

And every derived line now writes itself. Cost is skeletal because absorption terminates in the Lattice. Limit is uniformity, because a gradient in the driving field puts the threshold at different heights across the volume. The time limit is the sink filling and flattening its own gradient. It cannot seal, because a boundary is a potential barrier and would flatten the gradient the working runs on. Counter is come in clean, or be a deeper sink, or break the uniformity.

None of that was invented. All of it was read off the mechanism.

## §8 — VERIFICATION

Added to the Manual Verification Guide and to `wotr_verify.sh` as checks 34 to 36, run with `--entry`. All three are manual by design; a script cannot tell a mechanism from an assertion.

**Check 34 — The four elements.** Quantity, law, operation and chain all present in the Operation line. Any absence is a FAIL.

**Check 35 — Derivation.** For each of Cost, Limit and Counter, name the sentence in the Operation it follows from. Anything that cannot be traced is decoration and is cut or re-derived.

**Check 36 — The assertion sweep.** Flag every phrase that states an outcome in place of a process: *is assayed*, *is converted*, *is transferred*, *responds to*, *is affected by*, *is empowered by*, *resonates with*, *attunes to*. Each one is either replaced with the operation it is standing in for or deleted.

## §9 — RETROSPECTIVE

Pack Seven's cost audit remains the largest job on the docket and Pack Seventeen enlarges it. The estimate stands at roughly sixty percent of existing technique entries failing the *overuse strains him* test; the proportion failing §2 will be higher, because §2 is a stricter gate and most entries were written under a guide that actively discouraged it.

Order of work when Isaac calls it: Signature techniques first, since they carry mandatory Counters and a Counter that cannot be derived is a broken entry. Then arts. Then Traits, which are the worst offenders and the least examined. Then artefacts. Then the rest.

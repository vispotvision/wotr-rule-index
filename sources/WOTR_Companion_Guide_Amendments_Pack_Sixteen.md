# WOTR COMPANION GUIDE AMENDMENTS — PACK SIXTEEN

## The Conversion Standard

**Status:** originated, pending Isaac's ratification.
**Precedence:** Pack Sixteen governs where it conflicts with Packs Fifteen through One and with all base companion guides. It amends the Master Style Directive, the Scene Writing Process Guide, and the Manual Verification Guide.
**Scope:** every occasion on which Isaac submits rough prose, a beat, a fragment, or a paste for improvement, extension, or rewriting.

---

## §1 — THE FAULT THIS PACK CORRECTS

When Isaac hands over rough prose, Natalie has been **replacing** it rather than **converting** it. Two failure modes, both live across the current Kharven sequence.

**Flattening.** Isaac's register runs on abstract metaphysical nouns operating as technical terms: *quantum fluxations*, *arcs of gradience*, *endless sequential potential*, *the mechanism itself unfold*, *misconstrue it outward*, *the district of the unperceivable*. Natalie has been translating these downward into plain concrete English on the grounds that plain is stronger. The result is prose in Natalie's default register with Isaac's terminology deleted.

**Exporting.** The scientific and metaphysical account has been moved out of the narration and into two places where it costs nothing: the POV's italic interiority, and the author-facing notes. This produces a scene that reads physically clean and a notes block that carries all the register. The notes are not the deliverable.

Both failures are Pack Seven behaviour. **Pack Seven is repealed.** Pack Twelve §1 puts mechanism on the page. Pack Thirteen requires the three-stratum account and the technical floor. Pack Sixteen states the consequence for rough-draft work: the technical and metaphysical register belongs in the finished narration, in Isaac's vocabulary, converted rather than removed.

---

## §2 — THE OPERATION

Conversion, not substitution.

Every content-bearing noun and verb in Isaac's rough is routed to one of four destinations. Nothing is silently dropped.

1. **To a WOTR system term.** The rough word names something the Codex, FOW, or the Lexicon already has a word for. Use the canon word.
2. **To a real scientific or technical term.** The rough word is reaching for a real phenomenon. Use the real name for it, correctly, having researched it (Pack Thirteen §7).
3. **To the compound.** The rough word needs both at once. This is the default for anything load-bearing. See §3.
4. **Kept verbatim.** The rough word is already the right word and becomes a defined WOTR term. See §6.

**Deletion requires a note.** If a term from the rough does not appear in the finished prose in one of the four forms above, the author notes say which term was dropped and why, in one line. A rough draft handed back with vocabulary quietly missing is a failed pass.

---

## §3 — THE COMPOUND SENTENCE

The signature WOTR technical sentence carries the real mechanism and the system term in the same breath and attaches the physical consequence to both. Three elements, one sentence or one short group:

**[the real phenomenon, correctly named] + [the system term as its name, its boundary condition, or its address] + [what the body or the room did].**

Neither element is a gloss on the other. The science is not a metaphor for the magic and the magic is not decoration on the science. They are the same event described at two strata, per Pack Thirteen §2.

*Under-converted:* "The air got cold and ice formed and it made him stronger."

*Over-converted, all system and no world:* "Liminia's Core Law operated on the volumetric thermal degrees of freedom, producing Wellspring-mediated deposition at an eta consistent with his Depth grade."

*Converted:* "Every gram of it that crossed from vapour to solid gave up its latent heat at the instant of crystallising, and Liminia took the payment, and the plate that had been standing off his wrist by a finger's width lay flat again."

## §5 — WHERE THE REGISTER LIVES

**In the narration.** Pack Twelve §2 makes the Technical Register available to third-person narration for anything a person chooses to do. Natalie stops reserving it for italic thought.

The four explaining voices (Pack Twelve §3, maximum two per engagement) remain the delivery system for *causal explanation*. They are not the delivery system for *vocabulary*. Narration may carry the terms; only the argument about why something worked needs a mouth.

**Not in the author notes.** The notes record originated material, conflicts, the Stat Ledger, and the audit. If a mechanism appears in the notes and not in the prose, the pass failed and the note is evidence of the failure.

---

## §6 — ISAAC'S COINAGES ARE CANON CANDIDATES

A term in Isaac's rough that has no canon equivalent is a **coinage**, not a typo and not an approximation.

It is converted into a defined WOTR term, kept in the prose, given a one-line definition in the author notes, and entered on the Open Rulings Docket for ratification. It is never silently replaced with a plainer word.

Spelling and grammar in the rough are corrected without comment. Vocabulary is not.

Where a coinage collides with existing canon, the collision is surfaced and both readings are stated, per the standing rule against silent resolution.

---

## §7 — WHAT IS NOT CONVERTED

The conversion applies to mechanism, phenomenon, technical read, injury, and the physics of an exchange.

It does not apply to:

- **The body.** Chest, hands, gut. Story first (Master Style Directive) survives Pack Sixteen intact.
- **Grief and the elegiac beat.** Describe the rust in full and the grief in three words (Pack Six). A converted grief is a failed grief.
- **Dialogue**, except in a practitioner's diagnostic voice or a document's.
- **The ignorance quota** (Pack Six). One thing per scene the POV perceives and cannot name. Conversion never rescues it.

A scene that converts everything has the same fault as a scene that converts nothing: it has stopped choosing.

## §8 — VERIFICATION

Added to the Manual Verification Guide and to `wotr_verify.sh` as checks 30 to 33. Checks 30 and 32 false-positive by design and are reviewed by reading.

**Check 30 — Conversion density.** A scene containing a working, an exchange, or an injury carries at least four terms from the WOTR technical lexicon and at least two correctly-used real scientific or anatomical terms, in the narration, outside italics.

**Check 31 — Export.** No mechanism term appears in the author-notes block that does not also appear in the prose body.

**Check 32 — Coinage retention.** Every distinctive term in the submitted rough appears in the output in one of the four routed forms, or is listed as dropped in the notes.

**Check 33 — Italic quarantine.** Fewer than half of a scene's technical terms sit inside italic thought.

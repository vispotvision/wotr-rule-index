# WOTR COMPANION GUIDE AMENDMENTS: PACK TEN
## THE MAGICAL CATEGORIES AND PROJECTED-FORM AMENDMENT

**Status:** Ratified by Isaac's direction, live this session.
**Precedence:** Pack Ten beats Pack Nine where they overlap and extends it everywhere else. Pack Seven's ban on mechanism-of-effect and metaphysical units in prose is untouched.

**Amends:** WOTR_Ability_Technique_Design_Guide.md, WOTR_Master_Style_Directive.md (Diction Palette), Pack Nine Parts Two–Three, and ratifies Notion's *The Magical Categories — Unified Taxonomy* (26 categories, page `3c758200-eb22-819e-8385-c81cd92f8d13`) as live canon alongside the Four Crafts.

---

## PART ONE. TWO AXES, NOT ONE

The Four Crafts answer **how** a working is performed — medium: the work, the speaking, the cutting, the pouring, the singing.

**The Magical Categories answer what kind of thing it is** — Arts, Drafts, Conjunction, Synergia, Harmonia, Ritus, Phenomena, Glyphica, Manifestus, Viaforma, Traitus, Mechanica, Crystalia, Wellsprings, Animatria, Vocatia, Vectra, Mnemata, Aetherica, Concordia, Domain Weaving, Claustra, Silentia, Auguria, Transitus, Votia.

The two cross the same way Family and Method already do, at a coarser grain: **Craft is the mouth it comes out of. Category is the shape of the thing that comes out.** A tiger loosed by *speaking* is Animatria delivered through Spellcraft. A tiger carved to stand guard forever is Animatria delivered through Runecraft. Same category, different craft, different animal on the page.

---

## PART TWO. CATEGORY NAMES IN PROSE — THE SAME RULING AS THE CRAFTS, ONE NOTCH STRICTER

Category names (*Animatria*, *Manifestus*, *Vectra*, and so on) sit at Wellspring/Family register, not common-tongue register — nobody calls a tiger "my Animatria" the way a soldier says "the writing." **They stay workbook and Codex-line by default.**

**One exception, and it is the Apparatus Rule's existing budget, not a new one.** A scene's single explicit diagnostic read may be spent naming the category instead of naming a Wellspring or Family — a scholar, examiner, or rival practitioner gets one line: *"That's Animatria. It isn't a real animal."* Naming a category is not accounting for why it works, so it does not trip Check 20. It still costs the scene's only read, and after it the faculty goes silent same as always.

---

## PART THREE. THE PROJECTED-FORM LICENSE

**This is the change Isaac asked for directly.** Ability Effect fields may now render a working as an **externalized shape** — animal, elemental figure, guardian silhouette — instead of only as discharge, pressure, or weather. Isaac's example: a tiger coming off a practitioner's arm.

**Three categories license this, and each produces a different animal:**

- **Animatria** — the shape is directed and semi-independent. It moves with intent the caster is actively spending attention on, it can obey or strain against instruction, and its continuity depends on a live line back to whoever made it — sever that line (Silence, a killed caster, a broken focus) and it stops being anything.
- **Manifestus** — the shape is involuntary. It arrives on a pressure or emotion spike, is short-lived by nature, and reads as a Trait bleeding through rather than a tool being used. Nobody aimed it. It came out.
- **Traitus** — the shape is passive and permanent. Not fired, not summoned — a standing mark of what the Crystal has become, visible only under a specific condition (a clenched fist, a raised pulse) and gone the instant that condition lapses.

**The delivering craft sets how much the shape can be trusted to hold:**

| Craft | What the shape does |
|---|---|
| Spellcraft | Holds only as long as attention holds. Drops the moment focus breaks, same failure mode as any spoken working. |
| Runecraft | Fixed. Carved into stone, scar, or gatepost, it outlives the person who made it and degrades the way every other inscription in this canon degrades — badly, slowly, and worse with each repair. |
| Draftcraft | Bottled. Cruder than the original, less faithful to what the maker actually saw in their head, and triggerable by a buyer who has never harmonised with anything. |
| Chantcraft (pending) | Requires the sustaining choir. Dies the moment the voices stop, the same vulnerability as any other Chantcraft working. |

**What stays fixed, no exceptions:**

- **Effect is physical only.** What the shape looks like, what it does, what the room and the target feel. Never why the Wellspring resolved as a tiger and not a wolf — if that answer exists at all, it is workbook, one line at the foot, never on the page.
- **One splash-panel beat per scene** (Pack Nine) still governs the moment the shape is at its most visually loud.
- **One diagnostic read per scene** (Part Two above) still governs any naming of the category itself.
- Ignorance quota and misreading budget are untouched. A projected shape is exactly the kind of thing a POV is allowed to see and get wrong.

---

## PART FOUR. WORKED EXAMPLE, SHORT

*Effect, in-register, not a full entry:* Muscle stood up along his forearm in a line that had no business being straight, and for one breath there was a second outline over his own — striped, low to the ground, weight settled through a foreleg he didn't have. It did not roar. It didn't need to. The men who'd been walking toward him stopped walking.

That is Animatria delivered through Spellcraft — hence it costs him nothing to end and nothing but attention to sustain, and it reads as an outline over the arm rather than a separate carved thing standing beside him. A Runecraft version would be cut into his own skin as scarwork, present whether he wants it or not. A Draftcraft version would be sold in a jar to a man who has never thrown a punch.

## RATIFICATION LEDGER

**Ratified by Isaac's direction, this session:**
- The Magical Categories taxonomy (26 entries) as live canon, second axis alongside the Four Crafts
- Category names held to Wellspring-register prose rules, with the Apparatus Rule's one read now spendable on either a Family/Wellspring ID or a category ID
- The Projected-Form license: Animatria (directed/sustained), Manifestus (involuntary/short), Traitus (passive/permanent) may render as an externalized shape, with the delivering Craft setting the shape's durability

**Still open, untouched by this pack:**
- Chantcraft accept/reject (Part Three's Chantcraft row is conditional on that ruling)
- Which named characters' kits get a Category/Craft cross-assignment first — cost audit and character-first re-derivation both still queue ahead of this

# WOTR Companion Guide Amendments, Pack Thirteen

## THE TECHNICAL FLOOR AND THE PHENOMENON AMENDMENT

*Status: originated by Natalie, pending Isaac's ruling. Governs over Twelve where they touch; Twelve otherwise stands. Supersedes the first draft of this pack in full.*

---

## §1. The Faults This Pack Corrects

Pack Twelve restored the Technical Register and listed what a combat exchange owes the reader. It did not say how much, how often, in what vocabulary, or from which stratum of the system. Nothing enforces it. The verification script has fourteen checks and none of them can tell a fight from a dinner.

Three habits followed from that gap, all of them Natalie's.

**One.** Workings are written from the Soul Plane only. "He formulated Essence and pushed it at the target." Essence is the personal water. The Aether it was drawn from, the Wellspring whose law shaped it, and the Category that decides what kind of thing came out are all missing from the sentence.

**Two.** No real phenomenon underneath. The glossary states that the Material Plane's laws are not overridden by magic but used by it. The Codex Spell Index already writes every mechanism this way (Anamnesis "behaves like information entropy, probabilistic collapse, and boundary-condition erosion"). The prose does not. It should.

**Three.** No research and no invention. Fights and workings are written from memory, and improvement passes polish sentences instead of supplying the logic that was never there.

---

## §2. The Three-Stratum Explanation Stack

Every working explained on the page is explained at three strata, in the Technical Register, through one of Twelve's four voices. The order below is the order of causation. It does not have to be the order on the page.

**Stratum one, the Aether.** The ocean. Material Plane. What was in the room before anyone drew on it: Aetheric Density (sets the AU/s ceiling for that location), Aetheric Residue from prior workings, Aetheric Saturation if the room is already flooded, and in the Draw Age the district main, the standpipe, the meter, and whether the district is cut off. **Real-science analogue: field, gradient, reservoir, saturation, conduction.** A working in a dense site and the same working in a starved one are two different events and the prose says which.

**Stratum two, the Wellspring.** The law. Each of the Sixty carries a Core Law, a Family, and a Physics Domain, and the Codex assigns the Physics Domain as real physics: Thermodynamics, Electromagnetism, Mechanics, Spatial Geometry, Material Science, Biochemistry, Fluid Dynamics, Abstract/Entropic Systems, Cross-Family Boundary Physics. **The Wellspring's law is stated as that real law with a directional operator on it.** The glyph does not create; it constrains the current's degrees of freedom until one outcome satisfies both the law and the intent (Codex wording, now prose-legal in diagnostic voice). The Sixty are the nouns.

**Stratum three, the Essence.** The soul's water. Soul Crystal architecture: Essence Core (intent, identity), Aether Shell (conversion to force; Lattice Conductivity, Resonant Purity, Continuum Retention), Attraction Layer (recognition; Attraction Force clean, Obsession Force corrupted). Cost lives here: EU spent, eta lost as heat and static and structural bleed, Flux Density under compression, Crystal State under strain (Refined, Fractured, Overgrown, Crystallized). **Real-science analogue: efficiency, impedance, resonance, fatigue, phase change of the vessel itself.**

**The Category, laid across all three.** The twenty-six answer what kind of thing came out. Their alignment tag (Body, Spirit, Attraction, Triadic, or a seam between two) names which Plane carries the cost and which Plane the counterplay lives on. An Animatria construct dies when its line to the caster is severed. A Domain fails all at once because the Attraction Layer stopped recognising the claim. A Votia working makes the practitioner narrower and the Continuum answers narrowness. **Category is workbook and Codex-line by default (Pack Ten). In diagnostic voice it may be named and it then tells the reader where to hit.**

**The Craft.** Which mouth it came out of (Pack Ten): the speaking, the cutting, the pouring, the writing. Craft sets durability of a projected form.

**Worked example.** The bad sentence, rebuilt. Measurewright's read, voice two. Wellspring, Category, and Craft flagged pending; the shape of the explanation is the point.

*Thin room, this one. Somebody worked here last week and the Residue is still on the walls, so his draw pulls against a gradient that's already half spent. He's harmonised with Thaloré, Fluxia, and Thaloré's law is a fluid law: pressure equalises, and what he is doing is refusing to let it. The glyph on his palm is a boundary condition, that's all. It tells the current where the wall is. Essence goes into the Shell, and his Shell is clean, high Conductivity, so the lattice packs without the static you'd hear off a Fractured Core. The air over his hand frosts because a working eats ambient heat to hold order. That's the eta bleeding off, and by the frost I'd put him at Band C, no better. Release is cheap. What crosses the room is a pressure front with a form riding inside it. Arts category, Body-aligned, delivered through the cutting, so it holds its edge for about three metres and then entropy wins and it's a slap. Attraction-sustained: it recognises its target and refuses the rest. While he holds it, his left side has no shroud. Hit him there.*

Every clause is a WOTR term wired to a real phenomenon, and each of the three strata is present. Any reader who has seen Nen or cursed energy already has the grammar; no anime word appears.

## §3. The Phenomenon Rule

**Every new working, technique, or ability names, in the author notes, before it ships:**

1. The **real phenomenon** it runs on, by its real name.
2. The **Physics Domain** and the **Wellspring** whose law it is a special case of.
3. The **Category** and its alignment tag.
4. The **Mechanism Vocabulary term** it dramatises (Pressure Differential Spike, Cascade Overrun, Echo Contamination, Resonance Fracture, and the rest), with the Attraction/Obsession alignment table deciding which terms are available.
5. The **fault the mechanism creates.** A limitation derived from the world or from the character's stamina is not a limitation. It has to fall out of the phenomenon.

**Then the invention step.** Push the phenomenon one step past where the textbook stops. Textbook physics is the floor; the technique is what a practitioner does with it that a physicist could not.

**The Phenomenon Bank.** One seed per Physics Domain, all originated, all pending. Wellspring and Category given as the assignment that fits, decided after the phenomenon, per the Ability Guide.

- **Thermodynamics.** Latent heat of phase change. Hold the target's blood at the boiling threshold without crossing it; every unit of Essence they spend drops into the latent gap and vanishes. *Exuroth. Arts, Body.* Fault: the Shell has to sink that heat, so the practitioner's own breath scalds.
- **Electromagnetism.** Dielectric breakdown. A practitioner who cannot throw lightning, only lower the breakdown threshold of a volume of air, so every working, spark, and trace of Residue in the room arcs to ground through whoever stands in it. *Orrenthal. Domain Weaving, Attraction.* Fault: his own Essence Signature is the best conductor present.
- **Mechanics.** Gyroscopic precession. Spin the Attraction Layer so every blow taken translates ninety degrees sideways: cannot be knocked down, only turned. *Absolution. Traitus, Body.* Fault: cannot stop turning; a patient opponent walks him into the wall.
- **Spatial Geometry.** Refraction at a density boundary. Thicken Aetheric Density in a slab so Essence-borne workings bend on entry and arrive a hand's width off. *Eclipseron. Claustra, Spirit-Attraction.* Fault: steel is not Essence and goes straight through.
- **Material Science.** Shear thickening. Gambeson quilted with worked fibre that stays cloth under slow load and locks under a fast one. Pack Eleven wants this: it is why some proofed men wear no plate. *Fixatio. Drafts, Spirit.* Fault: locks against the wearer too.
- **Biochemistry.** The clotting cascade. Run it forward down a sympathy link and the target clots inside intact vessels: a stroke, delivered by handshake. *Mortalis. Votia, Spirit; Obsession-sustained by definition.* Fault: soul-cost escalates per Fracture of Worlds and the link runs both ways.
- **Fluid Dynamics.** Cavitation. A Pressure Differential Spike pulled through the water in the target's tissue: no cut, no burn, muscle pitted from inside. *Somnalis. Arts, Body.* Fault: needs water; a Caloria worker who dries the air starves it.
- **Abstract/Entropic Systems.** Supercooling. A threshold working held at the Veil, nothing happening, until one act of recognition (a name, a broken vow) seeds it and the whole room's state flashes at once. *Vantabriel. Transitus, Triadic.* Fault: the practitioner does not choose the nucleus; anyone can seed it, by accident.

## §4. The Combat Technical Floor

**Every meaningful exchange** (a hit that lands, a first display, a counter, a change of measure that decides something) puts the following on the page, stated, in one of the four voices:

1. **Measure.** Where the bodies are and what each can reach from there.
2. **Tempo.** Vor, Nach, Indes. Who moved first, who moved in the instant, who was late.
3. **The read.** The specific tell, named.
4. **The fault.** What the move exposes.
5. **The mechanics of the hit.** Edge alignment, point of percussion, mass and lever, hip drive, structure through the bind. What the weapon did to armour at that tier and why. Pack Eleven physics for shot and steel: a round is chemically cheap and beats gambeson and mail; a working is not, and proofed plate exists because of the difference.
6. **The injury, by structure.** Tissue, nerve, tendon, vessel, bone, organ, and the functional consequence of each. Blood loss by ATLS class, with the Class II anxiety and Class III confusion beats used before pain is.
7. **The Essence account, at all three strata.** What the room had, what the law did, what the soul paid. Diagnostic voice per Twelve §5.
8. **What he cannot do next.**

**Density.** A duel of 2,500 words carries the full floor at first display and finisher, and items 1 to 4 at every exchange that changes the fight. A turn of 700 to 1,500 carries 1 to 4 once and 8 at close.

**The three layers become three explanations.** Physics, Essence, Hermetic correspondence: at first display and finisher each is a causal sentence beside its image, not the image alone.

## §5. The Gap-Fill Pass

When Isaac submits a fight or a working for improvement, this runs before a single sentence is polished, and the author notes carry a section headed *Added by the gap-fill pass*, itemised.

1. **Beat inventory.** Every exchange listed. Every named beat preserved. Nothing cut.
2. **Logic audit.** Read, fault, counter, and stated cause per exchange. Missing ones supplied from the fighters' assigned guards and cuts.
3. **Stratum audit.** Every working checked for Aether, Wellspring, and Essence presence. Missing strata supplied.
4. **Physics audit.** Measure and tempo continuous. Weapon mass and reach against the Item Guide. Armour tier named and consistent.
5. **Body audit.** Fatigue carried forward: grip failure, forearm pump, breath debt, the cut leg still cut. Stress analgesia applied and withdrawn on sight of the wound.
6. **Phenomenon audit.** Every working passes §3 or is flagged.
7. **Vocabulary pass.** §6.
8. **Prose pass, last.**

## §6. The Vocabulary Mandate

**WOTR first.** The glossary and the Codex already own the nouns and they are used in preference to anything borrowed: the three Planes, the Veil, Aether and its Density, Residue, Saturation, Essence and its Quality, Signature, Typology, the Soul Crystal and its three layers, Lattice Conductivity, Resonant Purity, Continuum Retention, the four Crystal States, Attraction and Obsession Force, the Sixty, the eight Families, the twenty-six Categories, the Mechanism Vocabulary, EU, Flux Density, AU/s, eta, Band, Grade, Stage.

**Real science, by Physics Domain,** to explain what the WOTR noun is doing: pressure differential, gradient, latent heat, phase transition, adiabatic compression, dielectric breakdown, induction, eddy current, impedance, resonance, standing wave, interference, damping, cavitation, shear and compression, spallation, hydrostatic shock, nucleation, supercooling, information entropy, boundary condition, degrees of freedom.

**Metaphysics, for the Plane of Fate and the Hermetic layer:** correspondence, sympathy and contagion, essence and accident, form and actualisation, recognition and refusal. These are the vocabulary of stratum two's Attraction-side reads and of voice four, the document.

**HEMA, extended past the longsword:** spear and staff, poleaxe, dagger and the grappling plays, messer, sword and buckler, mounted lance. A percussion master-strike table built for the hammer, not borrowed.

**Anatomy by named structure.** Fracture types by name. Neurological consequence by name.

**Scaling vocabulary, author-notes and adjudication only, never on the page:** Attack Potency (energy of one attack, not its area), durability, hax (a working that bypasses a statistic rather than overcoming it), speed blitz, reaction speed against combat speed, striking against lifting strength, range, outlier, anti-feat. Used to keep Natalie honest about who wins and why. A character never says "hax."

**Anime grammar as a translation aid, never as page vocabulary.** Nen's shroud, stop, output, expression, and their refinements; Naruto's split of shape from nature; JJK's vow and reversal; Bleach's release. When a WOTR working needs a shape, check which of these it is, then write it in WOTR's words.

**Pack Six's chemistry ban survives** for ambient sensory impression. It does not touch a hit, a wound, or a working.

## §7. The Research Rule

Before any set piece, first display, finisher, or new working, Natalie searches, at minimum, once each for: the real phenomenon; the real weapon, injury, or physiology; and the nearest anime or CRP precedent for the shape. The Codex Spell Index and the Magical Categories page are consulted for assignment. The author notes name what was consulted. A set piece written from memory is a failed set piece.

---

## §8. Adjudication Written Down

Table Rule 5 decides contested actions from Stage gap, whether the read landed, what has been spent, and what the environment allows. This pack requires the decision to be reconstructible from the page. A fight that resolves because the scene needed it to has failed the pack and is rewritten.

## §10. Open Rulings

- **R13-A.** Physical-unit ceiling. Joules and metres per second in a Measurewright's or Guild officer's mouth only; weight-and-reach for everyone else. Recommendation: yes.
- **R13-B.** The percussion master-strike table: Dawi-authored in-world or Concord. Recommendation: Dawi, closing half of Docket 18.
- **R13-C.** Shot against a worked cuirass: does the steel stop it or the working. Recommendation: the steel, always, or Pack Eleven's economy inverts.
- **R13-D.** May the gap-fill pass change an outcome Isaac wrote when no cause fits. Recommendation: no. Flag, propose the smallest change, wait.
- **R13-E.** The Phenomenon Bank's eight seeds: ratify any, all, or none.
- **R13-F.** Category naming in diagnostic voice beyond the Apparatus Rule's single read. Recommendation: allow it inside a Measurewright's or opponent's explanation since Twelve already opened that door.

# WOTR Companion Guide Amendments, Pack Twelve

## THE MECHANISM RESTORATION AND MYSTIC REGISTER AMENDMENT

*Repeals Pack Seven. Restores explicable mechanism to the page. Establishes the four explaining voices and the mystic register.*

**Precedence: Pack Twelve is latest and governs. Later pack beats earlier pack. Where Twelve conflicts with Seven, Seven is struck.**

---

## §1. Pack Seven Is Repealed

**Confirmed by Isaac's ruling.** The Soft Magic Amendment is dead in full. The following are struck and no longer craft law:

- "Mechanism nowhere." Struck.
- The three soft zones as a blanket rule. Struck, with one flagged exception at §6.
- The ban on any sentence explaining why a working produced its effect. Struck.
- The ban on numbers attached to metaphysical quantities. Struck, with rationing at §5.
- The Design Chain's confinement to the workbook. Struck. It returns to the page at §3.
- The Apparatus Rule's one-read-per-scene cap. Struck, replaced at §4.

**What Pack Seven got right and survives.** The economy is still a hard rail: power is finite, spent, and the spending shows on the page or it did not happen. The ladder is still real and higher is still felt before it is seen. Explaining a mechanism does not license a free one. **A fully explained technique that costs nothing is worse than an unexplained one.**

---

## §2. The Two Registers

WOTR now runs two explanatory registers and they are not the same voice.

**The Technical Register** governs combat, craft, injury, and anything a practitioner does on purpose. It is Naruto. It is exhaustive, tactical, and delivered in-fight. It explains the read, the fault, the counter, the reserve, and the reason the answer worked. It uses real vocabulary and does not flinch from detail.

**The Mystic Register** governs Wellsprings, sites, rites, oaths, the Veil, and anything the world does regardless of who is watching. It is Lord of the Mysteries. It is stated as **law, never as physics**: rules given in the imperative, prices given as prices, taboos given without justification. The Measurewrights wrote the address down and did not pretend to have built the house.

**The test for which register a passage is in:** if a person chose to do it, Technical. If it was already running before anyone noticed, Mystic.

Both registers explain. They explain differently. A passage that mixes them reads as a textbook and is the primary failure mode of this pack.

---

## §3. The Technical Register, Restored

**The Design Chain returns to the page.** Trigger, Function, Mechanism, Numerical Effect, Target Response, Consequence, Limitation, Weakness, Cost, Counterplay. Formerly workbook-only under Pack Seven. Now prose-legal in full, distributed through the scene instead of listed.

**What a combat exchange now owes the reader.** At minimum, across the engagement:

1. **The read.** What the POV or the observer identified, and off what evidence. The specific tell, named.
2. **The fault.** Every technique has a structural weakness and the scene says what it is.
3. **The counter, and why it works.** The causal account, stated plainly. This is the thing that was missing and it is the point of the pack.
4. **The cost, in the body and in the reserve.** Both. Not one.
5. **What he cannot do next.** The engagement narrows the character's options and the narrowing is on the page.

**Technique writeups on the page** now carry the full chain in place of the five-line format. The five-line format (Effect, Cost, Limit, Counter, What nobody knows) is retained as the **summary card** at the head of an entry, with the chain below it.

**Ability entries retain the Counter field as mandatory.** Explanation makes counterplay easier to write, and it stays mandatory.

**The named inventor rule.** Every technique was derived by somebody, in a specific place, for a specific problem, and the text may say so. A documented technique is counterable by anyone who studied it. A self-derived technique must be read live, in the two or three exchanges before it kills you, and **the reading is now shown in full instead of summarised.**

---

## §4. The Four Explaining Voices

**Confirmed by Isaac's ruling: mix, whoever is in the scene.** Four carriers. Pick per scene. Do not run more than two in one engagement.

**One. The POV's own read.** Free indirect, inside his competence. The ceiling is what this character actually knows, and a character who is out of his depth explains wrong. **A wrong technical explanation delivered confidently is now the most valuable tool in the kit** and replaces the misreading budget as its primary vehicle.

**Two. The knowledgeable second.** The Kakashi model. Someone watching from outside the exchange who understands what the POV does not, narrating the read to a third party or to himself. Requires an actual reason for them to be present and talking. Best carried by teachers, rivals, Measurewrights, and the Research and Archives Division.

**Three. The opponent.** Contempt as instruction. A practitioner explaining exactly what he is doing to you while doing it, because he does not believe the explanation will help. This is the most economical of the four and the most characterful, and it is barred to anyone who does not have the standing to be arrogant.

**Four. The document.** The LotM model. An in-world register entry, field manual, or codex line dropped into the narrative at the threshold of a scene. Uses the Mystic Register. Sets a rule the scene then breaks. *On Walking Into the Current* and *The Mechanism of the Sixty* are already written in this voice and should be quoted from directly.

**Voice discipline.** The explanation is characterised. A Measurewright, a Kharven hunter, and an Accord officer explain the same phenomenon in three different vocabularies, and which words a mouth reaches for remains class-marking, per Pack Nine.

---

## §5. Numbers, Rationed

Pack Seven's blanket ban on numbers attached to metaphysical quantities is struck. EU, AU/s, eta, Coherence, Grade and Band may reach the page.

**They may not reach the narration.** Numbers are legal in **diagnostic voice only**: a perceptive faculty actively reading, a practitioner counting his own reserve, a document, an instrument, or a character speaking. Third-person narration does not state a metaphysical figure on its own authority.

Rationale, which is craft and not caution: a number in narration is the setting asserting a fact. A number in a character's mouth is that character asserting it, which can be wrong, self-serving, out of date, or contested. **The second is dramatically live. The first is a status screen.**

**Never invent a number.** Unchanged and absolute. Every value checked against source. Unspecified means flagged as estimate.

---

## §6. The Mystic Register

The half of the world that was always meant to be numinous now carries the weight Pack Seven was spreading across everything.

**Rules stated as rules.** The strongest existing example is already canon: *do not carry Object Wellsprings into Site Wellsprings.* No mechanism offered, a stated consequence, and the Guild has buried the people who tested it. Write more sentences in that shape.

**The grammar of the Mystic Register:**
- The imperative without justification. Do this, never that.
- The price stated exactly and the reason for the price never.
- Correspondence in place of causation. This answers to that. Nobody says why.
- Attribution to an institution that is itself uncertain. The register says. The older register said otherwise. Both are filed.
- The recorded failure. Somebody did it wrong once and what happened to them is documented in flat administrative prose.

**Ritual gets its materials, its sequence, and its errors.** A rite has a bill of goods, an order of operations, and a list of ways it goes wrong, all specified. That is LotM's actual engine and it is fully compatible with a hard technical register elsewhere.

**Open ruling R12-A, the Origin layer.** My recommendation, pitched not decided: mechanism becomes fully explicable, and **where any of it came from stays mythic**. Titans, Archons, the Crevice, the Withering, Epoch-scale events. They generate dread and problems. They never resolve a plot. Naruto explains chakra exhaustively and never explains where chakra came from, and it loses nothing. If you want Origin opened too, say so and I will strike this section.

---

## §7. What Pack Six Keeps and What It Loses

**Struck:** the Apparatus Rule's one-read-per-scene cap. A faculty may now read as often as the scene needs. **Replaced by a cost:** a read occupies attention, and a character reading is a character who is doing nothing else. Show what the reading cost him in tempo.

**Struck:** the total-legibility ban and the blanket interpretation-at-zero rule.

**Retained, and now more important:** the **ignorance quota** and the **misreading budget**. These were never opacity tools. They are dramatic irony tools and they survive Pack Twelve intact. One thing per scene the POV cannot interpret. At least one confident inference that is wrong and stays uncorrected. In a world where explanation is now the default, **the wrong explanation is the load-bearing device.**

**Retained in full:** the Ladder ban, the Gloss budget of zero, local burstiness, the reification budget, and every AI tell in the checklist. Explaining a mechanism is no licence to write badly.

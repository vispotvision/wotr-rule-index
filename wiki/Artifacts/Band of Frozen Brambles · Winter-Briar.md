# Band of Frozen Brambles · Winter-Briar

## Band of Frozen Brambles · Winter-Briar

### What it is

A Relic Gauntlet and Glyph Tool, attuned, capable of stabilizing as a minor Domain Anchor on permafrost. A wrist-cuff of frost-patinated silverthorn cradling a blue glacial prism, five segmented, thorned finger-sheaths linked by cold-iron chains sprouting from it, each segment veined with pale Verdantia patterns trapped beneath ice. When awakened, hoarfrost feathers from the links and the prism exhales a quiet, glassy breath. It was grown and forged in the Regency of Mireya by artisans the card calls the Thornsingers, who braid Verdantia rites with deep-cold metallurgy. The brambles were wintered for one century beneath a sleeping glacier, then set to a `Ur`-`Ma`-`Wy` chain and sealed with a breath-oath.

### Physical account

A wrist-cuff and chain weapon: plausible mass 1.8 kg at rest (the ice plating adds weight only when active), five chains of 60 cm each at full extension, point of balance at the cuff itself, where the glacial prism sits heaviest. On armour: it beats padded outright, the ice-vines need no edge to root through cloth, and beats mail the way any thrust finds the gaps between rings. Brigandine's hidden plates still leave enough gap for a chain-thrown thorn to catch. Against proofed plate the chains turn the way any thrust turns, though the cold itself still reaches through; long exposure embrittles exposed rivets and joints faster than the metal was rated for.

### Operation line

**Thornbind.** Trigger: chains flicked toward a target. Function: ice-vines launch, pierce, and root along armour seams, spreading a `Ur`-braced lattice that pulls tight on a second flick. Mechanism: the same forced-growth principle as any Verdantia binding, chilled by the Caloria current running alongside it so the vine hardens into ice as it sets. Numerical effect: root-and-cinch in two pulls, gripping a limb hard enough to collapse a stance, not enough to break bone on its own. Target response: a bound limb, seams rimed white, footing worsened by cold underfoot. Limitation: needs an armour seam or exposed joint to root into; smooth, unbroken plate gives it nothing to catch. Weakness: heat, a torch or a fire-aligned working, melts the lattice before it sets. Cost: one Essence draw per flick; the cinch is free once the first root is down. Counterplay: keep seams sealed, or bring fire.
**Briar Gaol.** Trigger: palm to ground. Function: blooms a ring of frozen brambles into a shoulder-high cage, the same Domain-seed mechanism this batch's Hearthcall uses, chilled rather than merely grown. Mechanism: the gauntlet's minor Domain Anchor function, per its Type line; Caloria's heat extraction keeps the cage's footing glassy underfoot while Verdantia supplies the growth. Numerical effect: roughly a man's-reach radius, footing inside worsened to a near-skate; anyone casting from inside loses clarity the way any working does when the caster cannot keep their feet. Target response: trapped, unsteady, harder to cast from. Limitation: only shoulder-high, climbable by anyone determined and unhurried. Weakness: direct flame unseats the whole cage at once. Cost: the gauntlet's heaviest single draw; using it twice without a rest risks the chill overrunning the wearer's own hand. Counterplay: climb out, melt it, or avoid standing still.
**Winter Dividend.** Trigger: sustained contact between the thorns and a warm body or object. Function: heat drawn out through the thorns returns to the gauntlet as Essence, which the wearer can spend to armour their own forearm in a sheet of clear ice. Mechanism: the Caloria current run in the direction already attested elsewhere in the wiki mirror: cold is not a substance, only heat and where it is moved, and this is that current worn rather than summoned. Numerical effect: a few seconds of forearm plating, roughly equivalent to a light vambrace, per full contact-drain. Target response: whatever the thorns drained loses several degrees fast enough to feel it. Limitation: needs a live heat source in contact; it cannot charge off ambient cold air. Weakness: a target that breaks contact early denies the charge. Cost: none beyond what Thornbind already spent to place the thorns. Counterplay: break contact fast, before the drain completes.
**Rime Recall.** Trigger: a taut pull on a bound chain. Function: yanks whatever the chain is rooted in back toward the wearer. Mechanism: the same positional pull this batch's Horizon's Reach uses for a Transference-flavoured yank, here riding the physical chain rather than a bare Aetheric line, so it works even on inanimate objects the chains have rooted into. Numerical effect: pulls a bound man-sized target several paces, or a lighter object clean off its footing. Target response: a hard yank into range, or a disarm if the chain caught a held weapon instead of a limb. Limitation: pulls only along the chain's own line; it cannot redirect. Weakness: cutting the chain before the pull completes stops it cold. Cost: folded into whatever flick placed the root. Counterplay: cut the chain, or brace against the pull's direction.
**Glass Petal Riposte.** Trigger: a perfect parry. Function: the bramble segments shed razor-edged ice petals along the attacker's own line of force. Mechanism: `Uur` breaks the ice lattice's cohesion at the exact instant of parry, so it fails outward as shrapnel rather than simply cracking. Numerical effect: a spray of shallow cuts along whatever line the attacker's own blow was already travelling; rarely lethal alone, reliably distracting. Target response: shallow lacerations, flinch, broken follow-through. Limitation: only triggers off a genuine, well-timed parry; a block that merely absorbs the blow does not set it off. Weakness: a wearer who mistimes the parry gets nothing but a normal hit. Cost: rebuilds the shed ice from the gauntlet's own reserve before it can parry this way again. Counterplay: do not attack in a way that invites a clean parry; press through instead.

### Codex line

Wellspring: **Cinerion**, primary (Caloria, Thermodynamics), the Ash Veil, carrying the frost and the heat-siphon that powers Winter Dividend, run as a fire-family current worked backward since no Wellspring makes cold as a substance. Secondary: **Verdantia** (Vitalia, Biochemistry), the Green Pulse, carrying the bramble growth in Thornbind and Briar Gaol. Category: **Harmonia**, per the card's own alignment. Glyphs: `Ur` (Balance), woven as the bramble lattice's own load distribution; `Ma` (Memory), teaching the vines to seek joints and hinges; `Wy` (Motion), driving the chain-snap and pull; trace `Uur` (Disruption), carried in the thorns for Glass Petal Riposte. All four confirmed Root forms in the Master Glyph Index.

### Bond and cost

Attuned rather than strictly soulbound; it answers a new wearer within a day but draws harder from anyone whose own reserve runs warm and generous rather than measured, per the Thornsingers' original breath-oath. Overuse chills the wearer's own nerve lattice, slowing fingers and leaving a lingering ghost-ache; repeated breaches risk real frostbite at the resonance nodes, a physical injury the gauntlet does not heal. Chains cannot cross active Aether-fire without the whole ice lattice shattering outward, a real hazard to anyone standing near the wearer when it happens.

### Counterplay

Bring fire; direct flame both unseats Briar Gaol and melts Thornbind before it sets. Warm, wet ground and salted soil both stunt the whole gauntlet's reach, and a Verdantia-rich sacred grove may refuse Thornbind outright. Break contact early to deny Winter Dividend its charge, and avoid offering the clean, well-timed parry that Glass Petal Riposte needs to trigger.

### Bearers

Made by the Thornsingers of the Regency of Mireya (unattested elsewhere, kept for lack of a substitute). Worn by the Pale March Wardens (likewise unattested) during the Caedor incursions; a single gauntlet is credited with sealing four Domain breaches by "gardening" winter across the threshold, consistent with Briar Gaol above. Presently held at the Accord Cold Reliquary, Mireya, loaned to Strategic Division strike leads for siege and capture missions, converted from the card's "Enforcement Division," which is not the Guild Accord's attested name for that remit. First appearance: the Inclusion Saga, *The Blooming Silence*, Ch. 6, "Winter Tithes."

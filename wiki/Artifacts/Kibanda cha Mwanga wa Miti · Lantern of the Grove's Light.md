# Kibanda cha Mwanga wa Miti · Lantern of the Grove's Light

## Kibanda cha Mwanga wa Miti · Lantern of the Grove's Light

> Migration note: the old "Nature-Aether / Spiritual-Aether" Essence Alignment is struck and replaced with the Wellspring Harmonization Chimwala N'Doro's own current character card already establishes: Verdantia primary (Vitalia, Biochemistry), with Benediction as a secondary ward-current. "Lurien" is dead canon; per the Volume IV re-homing ruling, Chimwala is one of five sheets anchored to the Holy Sea of Alabaster, so the forge-site converts there, with "the Sanctum of Heartlight" kept as an unattested sub-site name inside it (nothing in current canon contradicts it). "Heartbloom Tree" is unattested beyond this card and is kept for the same reason. Of the three glyphs given, only *Ur* (Balance) matches the Master Glyph Index exactly; *Lu* and *Fa* do not exist as forms, so they are corrected to the nearest attested glyph carrying the same intent: *Lu → On* (Light) and *Fa → Yn* (Bloom). The Rite of the First Light and the Verdant Covenant are kept as given; Verdant Covenant is independently attested as Chimwala's own order. No numeric figure was given anywhere on the card; the Essence draw below is a conservative estimate inside Chimwala's Stage VIII, Band B range, marked as such.

### What it is

Relic, Domain Anchor, Illumination Tool. A hand lantern woven from goldwood roots polished to an amber gleam, holding a tear-shaped crystal suspended inside the frame on threads of visible Essence. The crystal shifts colour with ambient Wellspring saturation: green over fertile ground, amber on neutral terrain, pale silver in a consecrated grove. Small Parun glyphs spiral the frame and light when the lantern is drawn on. Forged within the Holy Sea of Alabaster at the site the record calls the Sanctum of Heartlight, from a soul-fragmented gem cut from the first flower of the Heartbloom Tree. Chimwala anchored the lantern's core to his own Domain at its forging, which is why it answers to nobody else without the Rite below.

### Physical account

Not a weapon; the Item Guide's proof-mark and armour-tier fields do not apply. Overall height approximately 32 cm, woven-root frame mass approximately 0.9 kg including the suspended crystal (an estimate; the card gives no figure). Balanced for one-handed carry at the frame's midpoint, where a wrist-loop of vine fibre normally sits.

### Operation line

**Sanctuary Light.** While lit, the lantern draws ambient Verdantia saturation through its *Ur* (Balance) boundary and holds a stabilizing field to a 20-metre radius that low-Stage corrupted Essence cannot cross cleanly, which is what reads to a bearer as low-tier corrupted spirits being warded off; it does not touch anything above Ambient-to-Active Concentration corruption.
**Pathway Beam.** The *On* (Light) glyph narrows the crystal's output to a directed line; anything the line crosses that is carrying an inscribed glyph, a laid trap-mechanism, or a live Essence current reads back to the bearer as a visible trace for as long as the beam holds.
**Grove-bloom.** Inside Chimwala's own Domain only, the *Yn* (Bloom) glyph lets Verdantia saturation complete a plant's growth cycle on contact, which is why nearby vegetation flowers instantly regardless of light or season; outside his Domain the lantern cannot produce this effect at all.

### Codex line

Wellspring: Verdantia (primary), Benediction (secondary, wards). Family: Vitalia. Physics Domain: Biochemistry. Category: Aetherica (light-projection effects), with a Concordia reading for the Domain-anchor bond itself. Glyphs, Master Glyph Index attested: *Ur* (Balance, Root, Urion), *On* (Light, Root, Maelor), *Yn* (Bloom, attested under Pair VI). *Lu* and *Fa* as originally written on the card are not attested forms and do not appear in the final Codex line.

### Bond and cost

Bondable by any Grovekeeper through the Rite of the First Light; outside that rite the lantern simply will not answer a new hand. Sanctuary Light and the Pathway Beam draw continuously from the bearer's own Essence while lit, an estimated 15 to 25 EU per hour at Chimwala's own Stage and Band; Grove-bloom draws a single larger pull per use, estimated at 40 to 60 EU, both figures conservative estimates rather than sourced numbers.

### Counterplay

The lantern needs a live tether, either to Chimwala's own Essence or to an uncorrupted Well nearby; sever that tether, whether by killing or silencing the bearer or by pulling the fight into ground with no clean Well in reach, and Sanctuary Light collapses immediately. Short of that, sustained exposure to concentrated corrupted Essence dims the crystal over time and shrinks the warded radius well before the field fails outright, so a patient opponent can grind the ward down rather than break it.

### Bearers

Chimwala N'Doro, current and sole known bearer, carried during every Wellspring-cleansing expedition including the crossing of the Fog of Black Roots during the cleansing of the Mireya Well (old record: "Mireya Wellspring"; Mireya is an attested Verdantia-aligned site, currently one of the nineteen realm entries pending a keep-or-strike ruling). Any Grovekeeper who completes the Rite of the First Light is eligible to bond it in turn. First appearance: *WOTR: Book V, The Blooming Siege of Caedor*, the chapter number carried over unverified from the old card.

# Kibanda cha Mwanga wa Miti · Shelter of the Trees' Light

## Kibanda cha Mwanga wa Miti · Shelter of the Trees' Light

The porters who crossed the Fog of Black Roots behind this lantern made a rhyme of it, and their grandchildren sing it walking the market at Kaetra: *Green, you'll eat; amber, you'll walk; silver, take your hat off and don't talk.* For nine days the only color in that fog was the tear-shaped crystal swinging ahead of them at the end of Chimwala N'Doro's arm, going green over the good ground and amber over the dull and, once, on a rise nobody had mapped, a pale silver that made forty loaded men stop and uncover without being told. Things moved at the edge of the light and did not come in. One porter who stepped outside it to relieve himself came back talking about his dead aunt, and had to be carried, and was charged for the extra hire.

### What it is

Relic, Domain Anchor (a fixed point that holds a Domain, ground obeying its maker's law, as terrain), Illumination Tool. A hand lantern woven from goldwood roots polished to an amber gleam, holding a tear-shaped crystal suspended inside the frame on threads of visible Essence. The crystal shifts color with ambient Wellspring saturation: green over fertile ground, amber on neutral terrain, pale silver in a consecrated grove. Small Parun glyph (a character of the old tongue that sets a working's boundary)s spiral the frame and light when the lantern is called on. Forged within the Holy Sea of Alabaster at the site the record calls the Sanctum of Heartlight, from a soul-fragmented gem cut from the first flower of the Heartbloom Tree. Chimwala anchored the lantern's core to his own Domain at its forging, which is why it answers to nobody else without the Rite below.

### Physical account

Not a weapon; the Item Guide's proof-mark and armor-tier fields do not apply. Overall height approximately 32 cm, woven-root frame mass approximately 0.9 kg including the suspended crystal (an estimate; the card gives no figure). Balanced for one-handed carry at the frame's midpoint, where a wrist-loop of vine fiber normally sits.

### Operation line

**Sanctuary Light.** While lit, the lantern pulls ambient Verdantia saturation through its *Ur* (Balance) boundary and holds a stabilizing field to a 20-meter radius that low-Stage corrupted Essence cannot cross cleanly, which is what reads to a bearer as low-tier corrupted spirits being warded off; it does not touch anything above Ambient-to-Active Concentration corruption.

**Pathway Beam.** The *On* (Light) glyph narrows the crystal's output to a directed line; anything the line crosses that is carrying an inscribed glyph, a laid trap-mechanism, or a live working reads back to the bearer as a visible trace for as long as the beam holds.

**Grove-bloom.** Inside Chimwala's own Domain only, the *Yn* (Bloom) glyph lets Verdantia saturation complete a plant's growth cycle on contact, which is why nearby vegetation flowers instantly regardless of light or season; outside his Domain the lantern cannot produce this effect at all.

### Codex line

Wellspring: Verdantia (primary), Benediction (secondary, wards). Family: Vitalia. Physics Domain: Biochemistry. Category: Aetherica (light-projection effects), with a Concordia reading for the Domain-anchor bond itself. Glyphs, Master Glyph Index attested: *Ur* (Balance, Root, Urion), *On* (Light, Root, Maelor), *Yn* (Bloom, attested under Pair VI). *Lu* and *Fa* as originally written on the card are not attested forms and do not appear in the final Codex line.

### Bond and cost

Bondable by any Grovekeeper through the Rite of the First Light; outside that rite the lantern simply will not answer a new hand. Sanctuary Light and the Pathway Beam drain continuously from the bearer's own Essence while lit, an estimated 15 to 25 EU (the measure of Essence a working spends) per hour at Chimwala's own Stage and Band; Grove-bloom takes a single larger spend per use, estimated at 40 to 60 EU, both figures conservative estimates rather than sourced numbers.

### Counterplay

The lantern needs a live tether, either to Chimwala's own Essence or to an uncorrupted Well nearby; sever that tether, whether by killing or silencing the bearer or by pulling the fight into ground with no clean Well in reach, and Sanctuary Light collapses immediately. Short of that, sustained exposure to concentrated corrupted Essence dims the crystal over time and shrinks the warded radius well before the field fails outright, so a patient opponent can grind the ward down rather than break it.

### Bearers

Chimwala N'Doro, current and sole known bearer, carried during every Wellspring-cleansing expedition including the crossing of the Fog of Black Roots during the cleansing of an unnamed Well (the old record named it for a place struck 2026-09-12; that name never attached to a real place, and the Well waits on a name of its own). Any Grovekeeper who completes the Rite of the First Light is eligible to bond it in turn. First appearance: *WOTR: Book V, The Blooming Siege of Caedor*, the chapter number carried over unverified from the old card.

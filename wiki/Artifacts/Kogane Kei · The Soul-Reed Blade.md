# Kogane Kei · The Soul-Reed Blade

## Kogane Kei · The Soul-Reed Blade

> **Migration note.** Converted onto Karasaki Hozume, confirmed current canon as Hozume · The Verdant Rot, and Kogane Kei is independently confirmed on Hozume's own character card: his "What nobody knows" line asks whether Kei is a spirit-forged blade or a spirit in a blade, and his Open Rulings note that Kei should probably have its own entry in the Called · Summon Register. The card's "Marsh, Verdure, Sublimation" essence alignment is old-system vocabulary; Hozume's sheet has already ruled that Sublimation is a Great Work operation and not a current, so the alignment converts to his two attested Wellsprings, Mortalis and Verdantia (Vitalia family, Biochemistry). Because Kei was a person before the binding and answers to Hozume by recognition rather than compulsion, it is classified here under the Summon Register's Native provenance (Category Vocatia, with an Animatria facet for the living-blade shape); it is not a repeatable call-and-dismiss summon, so it is not forced onto one of the five numbered Summon Tiers, and full formalisation into the Register is left as Isaac's ruling per Hozume's own open note. Glyph `Ci` is corrected from the card's gloss "Cycle" to the attested gloss "Order"; `Ma` (Memory) and `Ur` (Balance) are both independently attested. Tyzura is carried provisionally per the open Tyzura/Ashura Tyurkia ruling on Hozume's own sheet. Mireya is one of the nineteen orphaned realm entries pending keep-or-strike; the Blight of Fennir and the volume title *WOTR: The Hollow Cycle* do not appear elsewhere in the archive consulted here. All four are kept, flagged unattested, because the card's provenance does not read without them. Physical figures are plausible estimates for the weapon type, since the card gives none.

### What it is

A long, reed-slender blade in the shape of a katana, gold-hued, with a faint mist that never leaves its edge. Its surface ripples like standing water whenever spiritual resonance wakes in it, because it is not simply forged steel: Kei was once a mortal man, bound into spirit-forged steel by Hozume in a rite that Tyzura's unseen hand is said to have touched. The Mireyan apothecaries who first named it "the Soul-Reed" believe it grows out of Hozume's own being rather than having been made once and finished.

### Physical account

A katana-form blade: plausible mass 950 g, blade length 85 cm, overall length with hilt and reed-guard near 120 cm, point of balance roughly 15 cm forward of the guard, consistent with a cutting-forward katana rather than a thrusting one. On the Strike Force table, a cut delivered through Hozume's own Ardency, 395, A-Grade, sits at 20 to 100 GN, 46 GJ to 4.184 TJ, City Block to Multi-City Block level, and defeats plate, fortress stone and anything below that ceiling. It fails against S-Grade and above personal or structural defences (Town-level output or a true Domain barrier) and against anything past Hozume's own Stage VIII ceiling of 550.

### Operation line

**Twin Soul Resonance.** Trigger: the blade drawn and held in active contact with Hozume's own working. Function: amplifies whichever of Hozume's Wellspring techniques is running, because Kei's own Attraction Layer bond runs parallel to his rather than separate from it. Mechanism: the same passage-and-growth law that governs Hozume's own Mortalis and Verdantia harmonisations, doubled through a second, willing Crystal rather than drawn from a wider field. Numerical effect: bounded by Hozume's own Stage VIII ceiling, 550. Target response: whatever the amplified technique already does, at greater reach or density. Limitation: only amplifies a working already in progress; it originates nothing of its own outside of full manifestation, below. Weakness: the amplification depends on Hozume's own substrate, standing water or biomass, exactly as his unaided techniques do. Cost: the same debt his Mortalis-based healing already runs, borrowing against a threshold that will be crossed anyway. Counterplay: deny the field standing water or biomass before Twin Soul Resonance locks in, or strike before the resonance is established.
**Soul-cutting.** Function: a strike lands on both the Physical Plane and the Attraction Layer at once, severing possession or an active binding along with the flesh. Mechanism: Mortalis governs lawful passage; a cut carried through Kei does not merely wound, it closes a passage the way Hozume's own Mortalis harmonisation already closes one. Limitation: it ends a binding or a possession; it does not undo whatever caused it. Counterplay: as above.
**Manifestation.** When fully invoked, Kei takes independent shape beside Hozume as a spirit-companion, acting under his own will rather than as a tool aimed. Limitation: this is a Native-provenance bond, per the Called Summon Register's own framework; Kei answers by recognition and can refuse an order that runs against his own nature, which the card's history does not resolve either way.

### Codex line

Wellspring: **Mortalis**, primary (Vitalia · Biochemistry). Secondary: **Verdantia**. Category: **Vocatia** (Native Crystal Provenance, per the Called Summon Register), with an **Animatria** facet for the manifested companion shape. Glyphs: `Ma` (Memory), attested; `Ur` (Balance), independently confirmed, Root, Urion; `Ci` (Order, corrected from the card's "Cycle"), attested.

### Bond and cost

Soulbound to Hozume alone; anyone else who takes hold of the blade reports their own soul burning at the contact, consistent with a Native-provenance summon that answers by recognition rather than compulsion. If Hozume falls into corruption, Kei's own spirit weakens in step, and the bond itself comes under strain.

### Counterplay

Deny the substrate Twin Soul Resonance and the Hollow Bloom both depend on, standing water or biomass, and close the distance before either locks in. Fire and taking the field before Hozume reaches wet ground remain his own stated Counter and apply here without change.

### Bearers

Karasaki Hozume, sole current wielder, forged during his crossing into Stage VII, Refraction. Historical usage: carried in burial rites in the Mireyan swamps after the Blight of Fennir (Mireya pending keep-or-strike, the Blight unattested elsewhere; both kept for the card's own provenance). First appearance: *WOTR: The Hollow Cycle*, Chapter I, "Kei's Whisper" (volume title unattested, kept for the same reason).

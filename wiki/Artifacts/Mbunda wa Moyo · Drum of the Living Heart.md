# Mbunda wa Moyo · Drum of the Living Heart

## Mbunda wa Moyo · Drum of the Living Heart

Five druid warbands came to the Siege of the Jade Bridge, each with its own war-song and a fixed opinion of the other four, and they spent the first morning fighting each other about who would cross first. Chimwala N'Doro sat down on an upturned bucket at the bridge's foot with a drum between his knees and started in on a plain slow beat, with the ochre and green spirals waking under his palm. By the end of the first hour the warbands had stopped singing without noticing. By the third they were breathing together, five colors of aura rising and dimming along the green stones like one long animal's flank, and the toll-keeper's goats were walking in step. The assault that went up that bridge at noon had no officers to speak of and no gaps. Everyone on it reports that they have never felt less tired or less like themselves, and the drummer's reserve was bleeding out by the minute the whole time.

### What it is

A Relic-grade Domain Anchor (a fixed point that holds a Domain, ground obeying its maker's law, as terrain) and ritual tool: a sacred hand drum of hollowed ironwood, its head the cured hide of a Wellspring-born antelope, painted in ochre and green spirals that glow faintly when struck. Palm-fiber cord wraps the rim, which is burned with Parun glyph (a character of the old tongue that sets a working's boundary)s carrying Chimwala's own Essence signature. Crafted in the Heartwood Grove after Chimwala's first Temperance ascent into Stage IV, Flourishing, using materials exchanged ceremonially between the Grove elders and the drummers of Kaetra.

### Physical account

Mass ~2.2 kg (hollowed ironwood body plus stretched hide; not stated on the card, conservative estimate). Diameter ~35 cm, depth ~18 cm, played standing or seated, one-handed or with a beater. Not a weapon or armor; the armor-tier field does not apply.

### Operation line

- **Essence Synchronization.** Trigger: a steady, uninterrupted beat. Mechanism: the **Ye** unity-glyph locks every listener within hearing range onto a shared rhythm the way the Lupine Pack-Soul locks a formation's gait, letting Chimwala's Benediction Aether run through several practitioners' Shells at once rather than one at a time. Numerical effect: amplifies spell cohesion and physical stamina for every ally within earshot (roughly 40 m in open ground) for as long as the beat holds. Cost: ~5% of reserve per minute of sustained play.
- **Domain Battle-Rhythm.** Trigger: played inside an active Domain. Mechanism: the **Vor** glyph cycles the Verdantia Aether through the terrain in time with the beat rather than letting it run at its own pace, so roots, vines and undergrowth move on the drum's timing instead of their own. Numerical effect: terrain growth inside the Domain moves in timed pulses matched to the beat, usable to open lanes, close them, or stagger an advance. Cost: ~8% of reserve, continuous.
- **Harmonic Dissonance.** Trigger: struck at a hostile or corrupted animal presence. Mechanism: the **Ur** balance-glyph forces the local Aether back toward equilibrium, which corrupted or destabilized wildlife reads as an unbearable dissonance rather than a threat to fight. Numerical effect: calms ordinary hostile fauna outright; drives off corrupted wildlife rather than harming it. Cost: ~4% of reserve per use.

### Codex line

**Benediction** (Fate · Biochemistry) primary, the covenant-resonance that lets the beat carry to many Shells at once. **Verdantia** (Wild · Biochemistry) secondary, the terrain growth the Domain rhythm moves. Category: **Harmonia** primary (the synchronized cohesion effect), **Domain Weaving** secondary (the terrain-rhythm effect). Glyphs: **Ur** (Balance), attested unchanged; **Ye** (Unity) and **Vor** (Return) in place of the card's unattested "Sa (Rhythm)" and "Vaa (Flow)."

### Bond and cost

Soulbound to Chimwala N'Doro; bondable through a drum-beat ritual performed under a moonlit grove, per the card, which is kept as an open ritual specification rather than invented in full. Requires a steady, uninterrupted rhythm to sustain any of its three effects; a silence between beats longer than three seconds breaks the Domain-rhythm effect entirely and forces Chimwala to restart the synchronization from nothing.

### Counterplay

Every effect depends on the beat continuing; killing the sound (distance beyond ~40 m, a Silence-adjacent field, or interrupting Chimwala's hands) ends the Synchronization and the Domain-rhythm instantly, and a gap over three seconds forces a full restart on the Domain effect specifically. The Harmonic Dissonance working drives off rather than harms corrupted wildlife, so it buys space, not a kill. None of the three effects reach a target outside hearing range.

### Bearers

Chimwala N'Doro, brought only to war councils, large-scale rituals, and Wellspring stabilization efforts; first recorded use was during the Siege of the Jade Bridge, where it unified five druid warbands' Essence into a single assault rhythm. No other bearer is recorded.

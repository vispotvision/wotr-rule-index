# Mirrorwell Prism · Soulbound Echo Anchor

## Mirrorwell Prism · Soulbound Echo Anchor

### What it is

A palm-sized hexagonal crystal shard, one end fractured, cut so that faint shapes drift inside it, as if trapped echoes swim in liquid memory, which, for Karo, they very nearly do: a bound Echo's last state genuinely persists in the lattice. Channeling Essence into it blooms concentric glyph-light across the facets. Recovered from a ruin site in the Inner World the card calls the Echo-Spire, unattested outside this card and kept only because the origin cannot otherwise stand; believed a remnant of an early Ma-glyph working site from the Great Spirit War era. Soulbound to Karo Venrik during his first descent into that ruin.

### Physical account

A crystal shard roughly 6 cm across the long axis, about 140 g. It is carried, not wielded; the fractured end is structurally weak, and thrown or struck it shatters on contact with anything above Hollow-Grade force, including the lightest proofed plate. It has no offensive or defensive rating as an object; its function is entirely in what it does to a tether, not what it does to a body.

### Operation line

**Tether stabilization.** Trigger: Karo channels Essence into the Prism while an Echo is fielded. Function: the Prism's lattice supplies a Fixatio-style anchor point external to Karo's own Attraction Layer. Mechanism: Fixatio's law is that what it holds stays held; routing a fielded Echo's tether through the Prism gives the tether a second anchor that does not depend on Karo's own fracturing Crystal alone. Numerical effect: reduces the sustained AU/s cost of holding the Echo open by 15%. Limitation: only one tether may run through the Prism at a time. Counterplay: sever the tether at the Attraction Layer (Karo's own signature Counter) or destroy the Prism outright; either forces the anchor to fail.
**Bound extension.** Function: the Prism holds a single Echo's tether at higher fidelity than Karo can sustain unassisted. Numerical effect: extends that Echo's field-time by roughly one minute before forced recall or collapse. Cost: binding a new Echo to the Prism overwrites and erases the previous one's mirrored state.
**Mirrored re-summon.** Function: the Prism retains an Anamnesis-style read of the bound Echo's last known configuration, a stored trace, not the fragment's continuity. Mechanism: this is a recording, in the same sense Anamnesis reads any object's stored history; it is not the Echo's identity persisting, only what it looked like when it left. Numerical effect: re-fielding that specific Echo from the mirrored state costs 50% of the normal EU. Limitation: works only for the one Echo currently mirrored. Weakness: because the mirrored state is a compressed trace rather than the full original, an Echo re-summoned this way is measurably more likely to be one of the fragments that refuses recall, Karo's own documented Splinter Drift risk, worsened rather than created by the Prism.

### Codex line

Wellspring: **Fixatio** (primary), **Anamnesis** (secondary). Family: **Materia, Material Science** (primary); **Limina, Abstract/Entropic** (secondary). Category: **Mnemata**; the Prism is, functionally, a personal Echo Archive. Glyphs: `Ur`, Balance, confirmed attested (Urion, nine-Wellspring spread including Fixatio); `Ma`, Memory, carried from the legacy card but flagged **not verified** against the Master Glyph Index, Karo's own sheet raising the identical flag for this same chain.

### Bond and cost

Soulbound to Karo since his first descent into the ruin; it does not function as a general-purpose item for another bearer, since it anchors specifically into an Attraction Layer already shaped to field Echoes. The cost lands on him twice over: physical damage to the Prism triggers immediate collapse backlash on whatever Echo it is currently anchoring, and every mirrored re-summon quietly raises the odds that what comes back is not, in the full sense, what left, the same uncertainty his sheet already admits he cannot instrument-check.

### Counterplay

Sever the anchored tether at the Attraction Layer, the stated Counter to Echo-Sovereign generally, and it works here exactly as it works against an unassisted Echo, stranding rather than killing the fragment. Failing that, breaking the Prism physically forces an immediate, uncontrolled collapse of whatever it is holding, worse for Karo than a clean severance and correspondingly attractive to an opponent who wants to hurt him rather than merely disarm him.

### Bearers

Karo Venrik, the Foolish Magus, Echo-Binder of the Ma Sigil: sole attested bearer, soulbound since his first exploration of the ruin site.

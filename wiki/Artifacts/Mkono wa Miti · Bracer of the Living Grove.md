# Mkono wa Miti · Bracer of the Living Grove

## Mkono wa Miti · Bracer of the Living Grove

> **Migration note.** "Nature-Aether / Harmonic-Aether" is retired pre-Fracture vocabulary; the bracer is reassigned to **Verdantia** alone, Chimwala N'Doro's attested primary Vitalia harmonization and the correct single mechanism for a growth-and-regeneration defensive tool — no secondary Wellspring is needed and none is invented. **Ur (Balance)** and **Ma (Memory)** are unchanged and attested. The card's third glyph, **Lo (Shield)**, does not exist in the Master Glyph Index; the attested glyph closest to its function — "imposes geometry, reinforces boundaries" — is **Th (Foundation)**, used elsewhere in the wiki for exactly this defensive-geometry role, so Th stands in Lo's place, noted rather than silently substituted. "The Heartbloom Tree" is unattested in current canon; kept because the bracer's origin cannot be stated without a source sapling, and flagged. "Book III — Songs Beneath the Canopy" and the Battle of the Thorned Crossing are not confirmed on Chimwala's own character card (which lists Books II, IV and V by name) but are not contradicted by it either; kept as the card's own history and flagged as unattested rather than converted, since nothing in current canon says otherwise. Mass and length are not given by the card; conservative figures for a forearm bracer of the described material are chosen below.

### What it is

A Relic-grade, soulbound defensive tool: a forearm bracer grown rather than forged, layers of living bark curving seamlessly around the arm, smooth yet stronger than bronze. Moss-like Essence fibers along the grain pulse with soft green light. Amber shards holding preserved seeds and spirit-insects are set into it, each cut with a Parun glyph. Cultivated from a sapling of the Heartbloom Tree, nurtured by Chimwala from seed and bound to his Essence over years of symbiotic growth until it hardened into permanent armour.

### Physical account

Mass ~0.7 kg (living bark and amber inlay over a forearm's length; not stated on the card, conservative estimate). Length ~30 cm, fitted to the left forearm, worn permanently rather than donned. As light natural armour it stops common shot, blunt strikes, and blade edges up to roughly the E-to-D-Grade force band outright (per the Strike Force table's lower tiers) by transferring the force into the ground rather than the arm; it fails outright against proofed shot and against any strike at or above Chimwala's own S-Grade ceiling, since a light bracer cannot out-mass what a full cuirass is built to survive.

### Operation line

- **Root Deflection.** Trigger: any kinetic impact on the bracer. Mechanism: the **Th** foundation-glyph imposes ground-geometry on the impact the instant it lands, giving the force a root-shaped channel to leave through rather than transmitting into the arm — the same boundary logic used for defensive-geometry glyphs elsewhere in the wiki. Numerical effect: absorbs and grounds single impacts up to the E-to-D Grade force band (per the Strike Force table); degrades in effectiveness against anything heavier. Cost: negligible per hit, drawn from ambient contact with soil rather than Chimwala's own reserve where the wearer's feet are grounded; ~2% of reserve per hit otherwise.
- **Bark-Shield Sprout.** Trigger: spoken or reflexive call under threat. Mechanism: the **Ur** balance-glyph lets Verdantia growth outpace its normal rate for one burst, sprouting a temporary shield-plate from the forearm. Numerical effect: one shield-plate per casting, sized to block a single attack or provide brief cover; dissolves back into the bracer within seconds. Cost: ~5% of reserve per sprout.
- **Contact Regeneration.** Trigger: skin contact with soil. Mechanism: the **Ma** memory-glyph reads the wearer's (and nearby allies') own tissue pattern and accelerates the Verdantia-driven regrowth toward it rather than toward generic growth. Numerical effect: measurably faster wound-closure for the wearer and any ally within arm's reach of the contact point, for as long as contact holds. Cost: ~3% of reserve per minute of sustained contact.

### Codex line

**Verdantia** (Vitalia · Biochemistry), sole harmonization — governs all three workings. Category: **Traitus** primary (the bracer is a standing, passive mark of what Chimwala's Crystal has become rather than a fired tool), **Viaforma** secondary (the temporary bark-shield shape). Glyphs: **Ur** (Balance), **Ma** (Memory) — attested unchanged; **Th** (Foundation) in place of the card's unattested "Lo (Shield)."

### Bond and cost

Soulbound to Chimwala N'Doro; the card states outright that it cannot be worn by another without severing the living bond, which would kill the artefact rather than merely deactivate it. Requires periodic "feeding" with sunlight, water and Essence from fertile ground — in barren or corrupted terrain its regeneration functions weaken drastically, per the card's stated limitation.

### Counterplay

Root Deflection and Contact Regeneration both require contact with living or fertile ground; fighting Chimwala on barren, paved, or corrupted terrain denies both outright. The Bark-Shield Sprout blocks one attack and then needs seconds to regrow, so a second strike inside that window meets bare bracer rather than a fresh shield. Against proofed shot or an S-Grade-and-above strike, the bracer's grounding capacity is simply outmassed and does not hold.

### Bearers

Chimwala N'Doro, permanently attached to his left forearm since it hardened; first deployed at the Battle of the Thorned Crossing to shield three wounded druids from a volley of spectral javelins. No other bearer is recorded, and none is possible while the current bond holds.

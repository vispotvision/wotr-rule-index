# The Immortal Cross

**Fushi no Jūji, the Cross of No Death, in the house's old Japonic · Crux Immortalis in Accord Latin · Msalaba wa Milele, the Cross of Forever, in Kushara · the Immortal Cross in the common tongue**

The Immortal Cross is a test Ashura Yukari left for mortals: four arms around an empty heart, in pieces, which close only for someone who understands everything each arm holds. Whoever closes it is held in true immortality. Nobody has closed it.

She made it in the first mornings of the Third Epoch and gave it to her family to keep. The house's account, told at the end of every Telling to those who take the name, gives her reason in her own words: *it is for the ones who come after, who will have to learn by themselves everything you were born knowing.* She did not want the first being to live forever to be a strong one or a lucky one. She wanted it to be one who understood the Breath, the Weight, the reach of a mortal alone, and how to see without taking, so that whoever lived without end would harm nothing by it.

---

## The four arms and the heart

- **The head: Null-Light.** Pale stone, close-grained and faintly luminous, that casts no shadow, the same as the three stones in the Enclave court. Closing it asks for [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Null-Light|Null-Light]] understood whole.
- **The foot: the teachings of the Yukari.** Cedar heartwood turned to stone, darker than iron, its grain still plain under the polish. Closing it asks for the six teachings understood as lived things.
- **The left arm: the nature of the Seikai.** Black glass, cold at every hour, its edges so clean that a stare slides off them. Closing it asks for an understanding of what a mortal can reach alone, and why reaching alone ended in a cut.
- **The right arm: the nature of the Zettari.** Iron-dark stone from the Paths, far heavier than its size, that warms in a hand that has been witnessed. Closing it asks for an understanding of what endures by witness and inheritance.
- **The heart.** A ring of the pale stone with nothing in it. The four arms seat into it, and when all four are seated, Null-Light is held in the empty socket, and the one who closed it is held with it.

## How it closes

There is no lock and no key. Each joint is cut to an understanding. Held by a hand that understands what that arm carries, an arm settles into the heart with a small sound the archive describes as a breath let out; held by any other hand, the joint rides a hair's breadth off true and will not meet, however hard it is pressed. The understanding has to be whole, and it has to be the closer's own. An arm does not seat for something told, read or borrowed, which is why the Telling can describe all four arms and close none of them.

And Ashura's blood cannot close it at all. A hand with an Epoch in it does not seat a single joint, however much it understands. The Yukari keep a test that no Yukari born can pass, and they have kept it for the whole of the Third Epoch, watching for the one who can. That vigil is what the house means when it says the crow watches.

## Where it is kept

The Cross lies in the deepest archive of the Cedar Enclave: the four arms wrapped apart in oiled cloth, the heart alone in a cedar box. The head of the house and the keeper of the archive know the room. Outside the family the Cross is a rumor; the old Moto registers name it, and the Seikai learned of it.

The archive keeps a short register of those who were brought to try, with the house's leave, across the long Third Epoch. None seated more than two arms. Most could not seat the Seikai arm, because a mortal who has understood what can be reached alone has usually already reached for it. The Yukari believe the Seikai never came for the Cross at all. They tried to make its heart without it, by cutting into Ashura herself, and that cut is the seam Senri went into.

## Why it stays in pieces

With the seam open, the Soul Plane's recognition stutters, and the house holds that a Cross closed now would slip: the hold would falter, and its bearer would age and die faster than if it had never been touched. Until the wound in Ashura is closed, the arms stay wrapped.

## Summary card

**Effect** · Closed, the Cross holds its bearer under Null-Light without a gap, and the one change it watches cannot proceed: the Crossing that begins at death, and the slow approach to it that is age. The bearer stops aging at the hour of closing and cannot die. A body destroyed is rendered again from its recognised pattern in the Soul Plane over nine days, the length of a Crossing held open.

**Cost** · Closing takes the closer's whole reserve at once and leaves a spell of Essence Starvation after it. From then on the hold is fed from the Soul Plane and costs the bearer nothing in Essence. It costs everything else: the bearer can never pass the Still Gate, never follow anyone who has, and every grief stays exactly as it was on the day it landed.

**Limit** · It holds the Crossing and nothing else, and only when the closer understood Null-Light whole. Closed with the Null and without the Light, it holds everything: the bearer is fixed as a living stillness, unable to move, speak or change, and Fixatio cures that closing for good.

**Counter** · A thread cut between the bearer and the heart ends the hold in a Zeno slip, and the held years arrive at once. Breaking any one arm opens it the same way. A wounded Soul Plane slows the recognition, and the hold slips by itself.

**What nobody knows** · Why a hand with an Epoch's blood in it cannot seat a single joint.

## Physics

**Phenomenon** · The Zeno hold joined to the null reading: a state measured continuously, by a measurement that never touches it, does not evolve and is not disturbed.

**Law** · The more often a changing state is measured, the more slowly it changes; measured without a gap, it does not change. Measured too slowly or unevenly, it changes faster than if left alone.

**Limit** · The hold is only as good as its continuity. Any gap, any slowing, any touch, and the law runs the other way.

## Metaphysics

**Aether** · Draws nothing from the room. The hold is fed by the Soul Plane's archive, and a room with a bearer in it reads at its usual density.

**Nihiloth** · Carries the Null: the reading takes place across the bearer's boundary without crossing it.

**Anamnesis** · Keeps the bearer's pattern recognised in the archive, from which a destroyed body is rendered again.

**Mortalis** · The Still Gate itself; the hold is set on its threshold, which the bearer never crosses.

**Fixatio** · Cures each joint once seated; a closing is permanent, a mistaken one included.

**Inherited failures** · Nihiloth's cancellation runs both ways, so the bearer's own boundary thins under any null working aimed at him. Anamnesis erases what a coarse reading takes, so a rendering made through a wounded Soul Plane comes back missing memories. Fixatio sets a wrong closing as firmly as a right one.

## Counterplay

**Tell** · The bearer casts no shadow at any hour. Every Ketsumyōgan in sight finds him at once, and any working that reads the Soul Plane finds him lit.

**Limits** · Holds death and age only; wounds hurt and heal at the body's own pace; rendering takes nine days, during which the bearer is nowhere.

**Beats it** · Danshi, the Severing Thread of the Ketsumyōgan, cuts the thread between bearer and heart. A strike that breaks any one arm. A Soul Plane wounded as it is now.

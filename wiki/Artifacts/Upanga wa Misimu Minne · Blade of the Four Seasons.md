# Upanga wa Misimu Minne · Blade of the Four Seasons

## Upanga wa Misimu Minne · Blade of the Four Seasons

The charcoal burners of Rotwood had a front seat for the Reclamation and they will tell you it was the most beautiful thing they ever saw and that it ruined them. On the first day the blade went green and Chimwala N'Doro walked the black forest cutting it, and wherever the blade passed, rotted trunks split along their length with new leaf. On the second it went gold, and a clean heat ran along the same lines, burning off a stink that had hung there since before anyone's grandfather. On the third it went red, and the corruption came loose and streamed in from all sides toward the gem in the pommel, like smoke going the wrong way up a chimney. On the fourth it was silver-white, and he stood in the middle of a forest in full bloom and locked it there with a single stroke and a hard frost. The burners had been promised a clean wood. They got one, full of protected saplings and a Grove that had views about axes, and they moved their kilns two valleys over, still complaining.

### What it is

Relic, Weapon, Ritual Tool. A long curved leaf-blade of greensteel alloy that shifts hue with the season it is attuned to: green in spring, gold in summer, red in autumn, silver-white in winter. The spine carries a continuous line of Parun glyph (a character of the old tongue that sets a working's boundary)-script that pulses as the attunement turns. The hilt is wrapped in braided vine fiber; the pommel holds a slowly rotating seed-shaped gem ringed by drifting pollen-Essence. Gifted to Chimwala by the Sylvaar following what the record calls the Treaty of Green Sky, forged from Sylvaar deepforge greensteel and tempered in the waters of an unnamed Well.

### Physical account

Mass approximately 1.6 kg; overall length approximately 110 cm, of which the blade is approximately 85 cm; point of balance approximately 10 cm forward of the guard, consistent with a curved cutting blade rather than a thrusting one (all estimates; the card states none of these). Chimwala's own Ardency sits at 190, Grade B, which is his combat floor. At that output the blade reliably beats proofed harness from Hollow through C-Grade; against A-Grade and heavier reinforced harness, or any Domain-scale ward, it fails to penetrate cleanly.

### Operation line

**Season Shift, Spring.** The Verdantia Aether runs through *Yn* (Bloom), so a cut sealed immediately regrows tissue at the wound edge as living scar rather than an open injury; it heals flesh, not Essence, and spends from the wielder's own reserve to do it.

**Season Shift, Summer.** Rebirthine Aether, the Wellspring of the cycle, briefly phase-locks to whatever ambient heat is present, a documented but unusual Resonant Pair across two laws rather than a change of Wellspring, and the blade's strikes carry that heat forward into the target.

**Season Shift, Autumn.** *Wy* (Motion) redirects Essence carried in a struck target back along the blade into the wielder's own reserve on contact, a direct transfer rather than a stored or shipped one.

**Season Shift, Winter.** That same Aether runs in reverse, pulling latent heat out of struck tissue into the blade rather than adding cold to it, which is what reads on the body as frostbite and slowed movement.

**Cycle Severance.** A four-day rite, one attunement per season-day. Bill of goods: the blade itself, an unbroken four-day presence at the corrupted site, and the pommel's seed-gem as a holding vessel. Order of operations: the Spring cut opens the land's Essence pathways; the Summer strike burns out corrupted residue along them; the Autumn stroke pulls the loosened corruption into the seed-gem; the Winter cut locks the purified pattern in place. Breaking the sequence anywhere leaves the land half-purified and structurally unstable, a standing Threshold Event risk to any working nearby. Estimated cost: roughly 600 EU (the measure of Essence a working spends) per day, approximately 2,400 EU across the full rite, an estimate within Chimwala's Stage VIII, Band B range.

### Codex line

Wellspring: Verdantia (primary), Rebirthine (secondary, cycle). Kind: Wild. Physics Domain: Biochemistry. Category: Domain Weaving (the Cycle Severance rite), with a Wellsprings reading for the basic seasonal strikes. Glyphs, Master Glyph Index attested: *Ur* (Balance, Root, Urion), *Wy* (Motion, Root, Auren), *Yn* (Bloom), *Ka* (Cycle). *Fa* and *Sa* as originally written are not attested forms and do not appear in the final Codex line.

### Bond and cost

Soulbound to any Grovekeeper who completes the Rite of the Turning Leaf. Forcing an attunement change outside the blade's natural seasonal drift costs an estimated three times the baseline seasonal drain, a taxing surcharge the card itself describes without a number. The blade's *Ur* boundary carries a refusal, cut in at the forging, to authorize a killing cut against a pure, uncorrupted Wellspring-born being; only a confirmed corruption in the target lifts that refusal.

### Counterplay

A target that is genuinely Wellspring-pure and uncorrupted cannot be cut by this blade at all, which makes disguising as or standing beside such a being a reliable defense against it. Because forcing the attunement outside its natural cycle costs three times the standard drain, repeatedly baiting the wielder into off-cycle shifts drains them toward the point where they must choose between stopping and risking a Crystal Fracture Event of their own.

### Bearers

Chimwala N'Doro, current bearer, who carries it strapped across his back on most expeditions and draws it sparingly in battle to avoid overtaxing the land's seasonal cycle. Wielded during the Reclamation of Rotwood, where each season's power was invoked in sequence to cleanse the whole forest. First appearance: *WOTR: Book VI, The Reclamation of Rotwood*, the chapter number carried over unverified from the old card.

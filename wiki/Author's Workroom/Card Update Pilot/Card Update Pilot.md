# Card Update Pilot

Ten core cast cards re-cut to the current card template, the head built, gaps filled and releases designed. The drafts sit beside this page; the live cards are untouched until Isaac says go. For each card: what was invented (strike anything) and what the old card contradicts itself on.

## Charles Lambert

Draft: [[Author's Workroom/Card Update Pilot/Charles Lambert · The God Hand|Charles Lambert · The God Hand]]

### Added or derived

- Frontmatter: `type: character-card`, `rung: full`, sources and `pc: false`; no creator ownership is established by the card.
- Culture: Accord, inferred from his Church institution and register.
- Pronunciation: CHARLZ LAM-bert; the original card supplies no pronunciation.
- Combat grammar: Verdict, assigned from his early evasions and decisive intervention.
- Refusal: he will not leave another person in a grip he can take upon himself.
- Conviction: responsibility binds him despite incomprehension, institutional neglect and mortal cost.

- Aether Class and Crystal Tier: Ø and Dormant, derived from Fracture of Worlds Part Seventeen's Class Ø entry and Part Twenty-One's Crystal-tier table.

- Efficiency: inapplicable to personal casting; no numerical η invented for an unexpressed output.
- Stack: the standard two-Grade self-boost cap recorded; no boost layers granted.
- Releases and bonded weapon: none assigned; the Stage IV threshold is not met, and the Scale remains a carried office.
- Aura and signature sound: the office's gold fringes, eventual continuous gold and voice doubled with Darius, taken from the archived narrows scene.

- Skills: clerical work, explanation of rites and empty-hand evasion, collected from existing characterization; the open-hand tell and early movement come from *What the Lamberts Are For*.
- Identity and equipment summaries: office, institutional affiliation, bench reputation, clothing and the Scale collected from existing text.

### Doesn't add up on the old card

- **Missing figures:** `fow_line` returns “no FOW figures on this card.” It supplies no independent Stat Sheet row. Exact Level, Band, personal Tier of Standing, fracture state, Primary totals and reserve cannot be derived uniquely from the available facts. They have not been fabricated.
- **Archbishop:** the card says “His stone gives him the rank of archbishop.” The archive also says “no Stage, no shell.” No retrieved table equates that ecclesiastical rank with a personal Temperance Stage or Tier of Standing.
- **Age:** the card says “Forty-seven”; *What the Lamberts Are For* says “A forty-one-year-old diocesan administrator”; the narrows passage says “fifty-three years old.” The card's figure is preserved.
- **Build:** the card says “tall and gone narrow through the shoulder”; the archived Altherion passage describes “a stomach on him,” and the combined transcript describes “Big shoulders and a big chest and the beginnings of a stomach.” The card's description is preserved.
- **Time:** the stated moment is the blessing at the basin, while the existing Lore includes his later death and Dabney's wardship. Both remain; the office's later manifestations are identified as later.
- **Office classification:** no personal Family, Wellspring, Glyph token or Physics Domain is established for Charles. The Scale's effects therefore have not been converted into a personal harmonization, Trait or release form.
- **Physical inventory:** the existing description supplies face, frame, clothing and widow's peak, but no precise hair colour or hair comparison. Its sentences remain unchanged.

### Doesn't add up on the old card

- `fow_line("Cozbi Mahuo")` returned material from this card, not an independent Stat Sheet row. No card-versus-Stat-Sheet discrepancy could therefore be independently established.
- The existing qualification “No numeric stat block exists for Cozbi in canon” accompanies its constructed numeric block. Both that qualification and every supplied value remain; the new totals reorganize those values rather than independently confirming them.
- The reserve remains “Not a single number” with “tens of millions at full extension.” No exact post-seal reserve, Flux Density or independent AU/s figure was supplied. Costs remain explicit functions of E rather than replacing this fact with a fabricated fixed reserve.
- “EX-Grade through Pneumosphera or conscripted constructs” and “Personal unarmed output closer to X-Grade” are not reconciled by the card's stated reserve and η through the ordinary joule conversion. Part Four starts X at `4.184×10²¹ J` and EX at `1.24×10²⁹ J`; the card supplies no quantified additional energy source sufficient for those physical outputs. The claims are preserved.
- “Spirit 70% · Attraction 25% · Body 5%” supplies no Fate commitment, while “Sovereignty 1,445” exceeds Part Seven's Fate-gated thresholds. No Fate allocation or exception was invented.
- Root Severance's old “None documented once contact is made” and “Avoiding contact is the only noted defence” are preserved as its documented history. The detachable-layer counter is a new addition; it prevents contact reaching further material and does not undo successful unwriting.
- “Every claimed soul lost in one instant” coexists with the surviving lattice described as an extension of himself. The card does not enumerate which souls or links remain accessible under the seal; all new release descriptions are restricted to whatever survives.
- The Grade table ends at EX+. It provides no separately named second bracket above Cozbi's EX base for the general Third Release's two-Grade rise. No additional Grade name or numeric ceiling has been invented.
- The Codex returned no row for “Nisuke” or “Anti-Matter.” Those existing names remain exactly as supplied by the card; the effigy is not treated as confirmation of an independently verified Titan entry.

- **“Crystal Tier · Awakened, Unstable”** sits beside **“Crystal State · null.”** Awakened is a valid Tier; Unstable is not one of the three formal States, Fractured, Refined and Overgrown. No formal State has been invented.

- The source gives no numerical age, established race or bloodline, technique drain, field radius, or hard lifetime limit for the Loop's memory expenditure. Those facts cannot be recovered uniquely from its Stage and Level.

- **Codex Lists:** the Lists result for “Anima Spirare” displayed “Family: Materia” and “PhysicsDomain: Fluid Dynamics,” whereas its Blood and Vitality Glyph rows give “Vitalia” and “Biochemistry,” matching the Family reference and this card. - **Verification:** the complete draft produced two mechanical failures, both inherited: fifteen em dashes and “viscerally.” No new em dash was added. These remain because the brief explicitly preserves existing sentences and permits their existing em dashes.

# The Uncounted · Book One, Part Two: The Two Winters

## An outline of the Great War of the North, between Chapter Seventeen and Chapter Eighteen

*Draft for Isaac to change. Nothing here is written as prose yet.*

---

## Where it sits

- **Before (stays as written):** Chapters One to Seventeen. Chapter Seventeen ends with Muken coming out of the death-house, healed, with six Works learned and Totality withheld. That is about the sixth day of the war.
- **The new middle: Chapters Eighteen to Twenty-Nine (twelve chapters, about two years).**
- **After:** what is now Chapter Eighteen (the red harness, "We take Kharven-Seat in three days," the fall of the Seat) becomes Chapter Thirty. What is now Chapter Nineteen (the Hall of Verdicts, the ratification, the spring) becomes Chapter Thirty-One. Both get small edits, listed at the end.

**What the two years have to do:**
- Earn the Seat's fall, so the north wins its way to the final march.
- Make Muken *fail* with Hataraki at least once.
- Bring Ayame in.
- Pay off the threads still open: Dougou's fight, Kagenori's crack, Nanami, Hayato's uncle, Kasane's harness, and the boy who counts.
- Age Muken from sixteen to eighteen.

**Tone (R78-4):** dark stakes, shonen heart. Wins come often and each costs something, and some chapters end in loss. The world is beautiful: the north in summer, the salt city, the islands Ayame comes from.

## Year One

### 18 · Rope
*Days 6 to 30. The Foot, first winter.*

- **The forge scene moves here.** Muken comes out of the death-house to Tsugigane on the table (the Chapter Eighteen door and blade scene, unchanged).
- He practises on rope every morning: rotten rope from the smithy, then rotten timbers, then the Foot's old frost-cracked lintels. Each takes him longer and costs less.
- The first Banner oaths are sworn at the woodpile. They are sworn and then nothing holds them; Lorn says it in one line: "We swore. It felt like nothing."
- **Aksel Keld** wakes in the surgeons' line with his chest staved in. Muken visits him every day and says nothing. On the twentieth day Aksel asks why he stopped. Muken: "I don't know yet."
- **End:** a Byakuya crow brings word that the Hall of Verdicts has met in full council, and named Muken a traitor to the Moto and a danger to the inner world. Kiyome's warning from Chapter Eleven comes true.

### 19 · The Verdict of Seven
*Kharven-Seat. The Hall's counter-move, from the other side.*

- Shizuha calls the full council: Kagenori, Hayato's uncle in the indigo chair, Sumie Kurenai, and the empty white and copper chairs.
- The vote to march north is not unanimous. **Sumie Kurenai** abstains. It is the first crack in the executioners' line.
- **Kagenori** votes yes and is sick in a basin afterward. *(His thread: he begins writing a letter he never sends.)*
- Karogane is given the north. **Shiro Tenrai** keeps the records of the vote and is told to burn them; he doesn't.
- The Tenrai columns come down the frozen Marrow: four thousand house-guard and the Haldorssons.

### 20 · The Ridge Does Not Move
*The siege of Kharven's Foot. Deep winter, the Thin Weeks.*

- Thirty days of siege. Tenrai practitioners work the walls with Pressure and fire, and the mended seam of pale stone takes the brunt of it.
- **Godfrey's chapter.** The Greymanes hold the wall the way their words say they will.
- Muken's first **Shigoto no Kabe**, the Work of Bearing Load. When the gatehouse is about to come down on the yard full of wounded, he stands under it and the weight goes through him into the ground. The wall holds. Two of his ribs crack again.
- **The cost:** the old woman with no teeth dies in the night, on her stump by the barracks door, in her three shawls. The Waiting is sat for her, and she gets a name at last.
- **End:** the siege breaks when Thornwall pikes come down the corridors onto the Tenrai rear and Ashmore cuts the Marrow behind them. A win at a price.

### 21 · The Thin Weeks
*Famine, money, roads.*

- The real enemy is hunger. Hallenfeld credit and Hayato's indigo roads feed the north. Yukari witnesses at both ends of the roads count the sacks, which ties back to Naiser's terms.
- Ilse Hallenfeld comes north in person a second time and shouts at Muken, which no one has ever done.
- **Muken fails.** A grain convoy is ambushed, and its children are left in the snow as a message. In fury Muken goes after the raiders and uses Hibi on *men*. Living men, who did not need cracking: his oath at the Seat broken before he ever reaches it. Faisal does not come. The silence lasts the rest of the winter.
- **End:** Kiyome finds him alone in the death-house, the way Lorn did. Muken: "I broke it." Kiyome: "Then you know what it weighs."

### 22 · When the Snow Goes
*Spring. The fight Dougou is owed.*

- **This is a table beat for the Zettari player.** It's written in RP, turn by turn: Muken's side by me, Dougou's by his player. The chapter collects that thread when it's played.
- Around it, the north greens for the first time in anyone's memory: moss, then white flowers.
- Faisal comes back after the fight, not before.

### 23 · The Officiant
*Late spring. Ayame Yuno arrives.*

- The Banner oaths still hold nothing. Kiyome writes to the one house she knows that binds oaths for a living: the **Yuno, Kagura Branch**, island elders who answer to an aiga the north has never seen.
- **Ayame Yuno** comes north by ship and sledge with two attendants, in silver-grey and pale amber, snow spiralling around her as she walks. She is not impressed by a boy of sixteen with a red chair he hasn't sat in.
- She hears his oath and her Crown of Concord tastes it, and it tastes clean. She says nothing about that for a week.
- The first true Banner oath is sworn under her rite, the binding the Kharven Inventory calls the **Fusi Vā**. For the first time, Lorn feels it hold.
- **End:** she refuses something he asks for. Her card's refusal applies: she will not turn her liturgy into a weapon. Their friction starts here.

### 24 · The Summer of Roads
*Summer. The north goes on the offensive.*

- A campaign south and west: the Kessen ford, the salt road, the Tenrai granary towns.
- **Aksel Keld** rides again, without his hand, beside Muken. The Keld swear on the card's condition, "stay worth following." *(Moved here from the ratification.)*
- **Battle of the Kessen ford.** Ayame's lattice holds a broken levy together, and Muken sees what she does and what it costs her.
- Draw Age colour: the first Tenrai town they take has worked lamps and a Guild standpipe, and the commons have never been allowed to use either. Muken opens the standpipe.

### 25 · Salt
*Late summer. Muken goes home to Halcrest.*

- Nalūn, the salt kingdom, comes into the war. Halcrest, the Cuttings, the Orrel factor house, the galleries.
- Muken goes down the shaft to his mother's grave in the salt. He tells her, in the dark, what happened to Yukazuri.
- The Orrel factor, the one at the top of the shaft with the ledger, is still there. Muken decides what to do with him. *(A test of lawful ending versus revenge.)*
- Nalūn's chain-city council treats with him. *(This seeds the first Accord circle at the end of his life, per his card.)*

### 26 · The White House
*Autumn. The Shiraki hills.*

- The Byakuya seat: glass, veils, near-white halls.
- Kiyome takes him home and gives him **Kasane's own red harness**, the real one, kept since the night she went into the salt. *(This replaces the overnight armour in old Chapter Eighteen.)*
- **Nanami Shirogane** is there, fostered by the Byakuya for ten years. Muken meets her for the first time. She is kind to him before she learns who he is, and cold after. This plants her chair at the end.
- **End:** a Shirogane assassin in the white house. Ayame's Crown of Concord catches the lie in his oath of hospitality a breath before he strikes. It's the first covert attack, the kind Muken's card says will one day kill him. Kiyome is wounded.

## Year Two

### 27 · The Second Winter
*The Tenrai strike where it hurts.*

- Unable to beat him in the field, the Tenrai go for what he loves. **Tetsugen's Kurogane valley is burned**, and his eldest, the one who spoke against the levy, is killed.
- Tetsugen does not break. He says, in his flat iron voice, "I've been here before," and the reader knows where: the Sennen road.
- Hayato's uncle is caught selling the indigo roads to Karogane. Hayato has to decide what to do with him.
- **Sumie Kurenai** is ordered to execute the families of three Stark riders held at the Seat. She refuses, and she comes north in the night with her line's blades and the families.

### 28 · Kagenori's Letter
*The Seat, from inside, at the turn of the second year.*

- Kagenori Tenrai, alone, writes the letter he has been writing for two years. We see part of it. It's to Shizuha, and it's about the girl in the silver room.
- He does not send it. He keeps it, and it lies on the step beside him at the end.
- Shiro Tenrai copies the vote records he was told to burn, and sends them north by a Hallenfeld courier.
- **The boy who counts**, Shiro's son, about eleven now, watches his father do it.

### 29 · The Muster
*The end of the second winter. Everything comes to the Foot.*

- The Banner Houses, the white, copper and indigo lines, the Kurenai, the Keld, Nalūn and Hallenfeld all muster at the Foot.
- Muken is eighteen. Borin Ironheart comes back north to look at Tsugigane after two years of use, grunts, and says it's held.
- The plan for the Seat is laid out: the corridors, the draw, the river, Ilse's credit. It's the same council of the road as old Chapter Eighteen, moved here.
- **End:** Kiyome brings out Kasane's harness. He puts it on, piece by piece, and looks to the ridge where Yukazuri's cairn is. Lorn is waiting. "Prepare... We take Kharven-Seat in three days." *(Your lines, unchanged.)*

## Changes this makes to the chapters already written

**Old Chapter Eighteen, *The Samurai of Fate*, becomes Chapter Thirty:**
- Its first two movements (the door and the blade) move to the start of new Chapter Eighteen, *Rope*.
- The armour movement becomes Kasane's real harness from the white house. Tetsugen's line about Gimbzo wearing black stays.
- "Prepare... three days" happens at the end of the two years.
- The council of the road moves into *The Muster*. The fall of the Seat stays as written.

**Old Chapter Nineteen becomes Chapter Thirty-One:**
- Ages go up two years: Muken is eighteen and Aksel twenty. Shiro's son is about twelve. Nanami is older too.
- Aksel walks into the Hall instead of being carried. His Keld oath has already been sworn in the summer, so at the ratification he renews it.
- Sumie Kurenai comes from the north side, not the Seat's.
- The ratification has Ayame officiating, which makes it bind.

**Chapter Fifteen:** "the third day of the Great War of the North" stays.

**Chapter Nineteen's last line**, "He did not know how long it would last," stays.

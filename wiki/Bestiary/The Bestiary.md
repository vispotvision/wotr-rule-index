# The Bestiary

*Forty-two attested creatures · Spirit-born, Draconic, Natural, Aberrant, Undying, and Divine*
> Every entry answers the same eleven questions, and **the order of the questions is itself a doctrine.** Origin before appearance. **Essence Affinity before abilities.** Weaknesses in the same breath as powers.
>
> **A field guide that tells you what a thing can do without telling you what feeds it has told you nothing.**

---

## The Entry Skeleton

| **Names** | The High or Old Parunic form, plus whatever the people who actually live near it call the thing. **The two are rarely complimentary of each other** |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Spirit Beast · Draconic Lineage · Natural Beast · Aberration · Undying · Construct · Divine or Archonic Beast · Elemental · Hybrid |
| **Origin** | Which Age, which Wellspring convergence, and **which Archon or Titan is credited** — credit being a matter of tradition rather than record in most cases |
| **Essence Affinity** | The Wellspring current the creature runs on. **This is the operative field.** Everything the thing can do and every way it can be beaten follows from it |
| **Realm Affiliation** | Where it is native and where it has been recorded straying |
| **Danger Tier** | T1 through T9, **on the same scale as the Commission threat tiers.** A T7 requires a Gold Sigil to lawfully engage |
| **Weaknesses** | Recorded in the same detail as the abilities. **This is what separates a bestiary from a bestiary of rumours** |
| **Historical Encounters** | Named engagements with dates where the Archives hold them, **and folk accounts where they do not** |
| **Cultural Significance** | What the thing means to the people who share ground with it — **which is frequently at odds with what it is** |

## The Danger Tiers

| Tier | Designation | Attested |
|---|---|---|
| **T2** | Minor / Moderate Threat | Zombies · Tanuki |
| **T3** | Moderate Threat | Greater Wolf · Sasquatch · Dryads |
| **T4** | High Threat | Unicorn · Harpy · Skeletal Folk · Neiman Lion · Psy-Apes · Ghouls · Bai Ze · Hippogrif · Siren · Pegasus |
| **T5** | Extreme Threat | Bicorn · Griffon · Goliath · Raiju · Lamia · Living Armor · Minotaur · Cyclops · Basilisk · Cockatrice · Hellhound · Manticore |
| **T6** | Domain Threat | Wyvern · Sea Serpent · Arachne · Wendigo |
| **T7** | Realm Hazard | Wyrm · Night Gaunt · Gorgon · Phoenix |
| **T8** | Cataclysmic Entity | Hydra · Voidwyrm · Cerberus |
| **T9** | Apocalyptic Beast | **Leviathan · Lich · Kraken** |
| **T3–T8** | Variable | **Elemental** — the only entry in the register whose tier is a range rather than a figure, running from a minor natural disturbance to a colossus that reshapes landscape |

**The rung beside the tier.** A Danger Tier is the Commission's rating: what it takes to lawfully engage a creature. The Bestiary ladder in [The Tier Ladders](https://app.notion.com/p/3e758200eb228127a7c2ece5680c1889) reads the same creature by what it can break, from Hearth-pest to Sky-eater, and by what lifts it: reach, persistence and the ground that feeds it. The two often disagree, and the difference is where a hunter finds the answer.

## What the Register Shows at Scale

> **Almost everything above T6 is a Wellspring event rather than an animal.** The Leviathan, the Kraken, the Hydra, the Voidwyrm and the Cerberus are all creatures whose danger is not their size but **their relationship to a vein, a Crevice, or a plane boundary.**
>
> **The Lich is the exception, and the Lich is the exception because it used to be a person.**
**The weaknesses cluster.** Read across the register and the same three vulnerabilities recur: **Essence-dampening**, **removal from the native Wellspring**, and **radiant or holy current.** Which means the practical counter to the majority of the register is not a weapon at all. **It is a Sealwright, a long march, or a priest.**
**Corruption is a lineage rather than a condition.** The Bicorn, the Voidwyrm, the Wendigo and the Blackened share a grammar: **something that was one thing has been converted by Abyssal, Void or Crevice contact into something that cannot go back.** *The register treats this as taxonomy. The Heresiology would treat the same material as a diagnosis.*
**And the register is honest about the thing bestiaries usually lie about.** Griffons abandon a fight to protect a nest. Unicorns evade rather than engage. **The Bai Ze is physically fragile and avoids conflict entirely.** *Four of the register's most feared entries would rather leave.*

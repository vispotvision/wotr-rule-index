## VIII. Traits

**SKYBOUND MANDATE (Primary Trait)**

**Skybound Mandate** · **Can "step" on pressure ridges of air.** *Midair pivots read like stone footing.* He holds a patch of air firm beneath each foot while the rest of the current carries the downward push away. The added pressure follows his weight and releases when his foot lifts. Sustained stepping costs 9,325 EU each second, a quarter of one percent of his full reserve, giving six minutes from full to the last tenth. A short, hollow whistle and an arc-blue crescent under the descending heel give the tell. Ash or Rot miasma breaks the clean pressure surface; a prepared opponent can foul his next foothold, and a low roof takes away the height needed to recover. Repeated hard landings leave his calves shaking.

**IMPERIAL CONCORD (Secondary Trait)**

**Imperial Concord** · **Spoken orders carry lawful weight within Eressean jurisdiction.** *Cowardice blunts under his voice.* Judicium holds a spoken order to the oath and jurisdiction that give it force, while his Attraction Layer carries the steadiness to those who hear him. Each order costs 37,300 EU, one percent of his full reserve; holding its pressure beyond the first breath costs another 3,730 EU each second. His voice settles onto one low note and a blue ring catches the edge of his signet before the order takes hold. The working steadies willing hands and presses obedience; it neither supplies facts he lacks nor makes his interpretation of a treaty correct. A contested mandate can be answered with its actual terms, and equal-Stage resistance can contest his Dominion. Distance beyond hearing or ground outside Eressean jurisdiction removes the command's purchase.

**LUMIN MANTLE (Passive Trait)**

**Lumin Mantle** · **Armour blooms a thin veil of star-light that diffuses arrows and steadies allies' hands.** Luminalis spreads the visible veil over his armour while the existing mantle turns an arrow's push across its surface instead of taking the whole blow at one point. Judicium steadies the hold. The lit protection costs 4,662.5 EU each second, one eighth of one percent of full reserve, giving twelve minutes from full to the last tenth when it bears no heavy impacts. A hard stopped hit costs another 37,300 EU. Pinpoints of arc-blue light open over the armour joins with a faint glassy hiss. Close pressure against a join denies the arrow-deflecting surface room to turn the force, and attacks arriving from separated directions make him pay for each hold. The light does not make the armour unbreakable; repeated impacts leave the veil split and his hands unsteady.

## IX. Domain

**THE WITNESSED ROAD (Domain)**

The Second Release opens a courtyard-sized field centred on Aelor, visible as silver-blue paths across the air. Within it, a pressure foothold already held under his weight remains firm while he moves to the next, so a turn no longer leaves him briefly without support. Skybound Mandate supplies the footing, Judicium holds the stated route steady, and Luminalis makes its edges visible; the road offers no new knowledge of the people travelling on it. It takes one complete shared breath with Mirenai to settle and survives small interruptions once formed. Its standing cost is 466.25 EU each second, giving two hours from full to the last tenth before hard turns, impacts and techniques add their costs. The Second Release pays this upkeep; it is not charged twice. The field ends when he dismisses it, loses consciousness or reaches the last tenth. A competing Domain can contest its ground by Dominion, ash can break its clean footholds, and an opponent can leave its visible edge. It grants no Eressean legal jurisdiction where none exists.

## X. Techniques

### Style

**Mount and rider move as one long curve.** *Aelor keeps knees soft, hips floating over the saddle; the lance tip writes neat, short lines at tendon and hinge.* **He never wastes altitude — diving only when the shield's rim is already set to catch the answer.** *Commands are low and even; Mirenai folds a wing by half a feather, and the world rearranges to grace their angle.*

**Signature Sequences**

**BREAK THE LINE (Signature Technique)**

**Break the Line** · **Climbs to cold air, drops in a shallow S-curve, taps three shields in passing.** *On the fourth touch a tower remembers wind and leans.* Each touch puts force through the lance into the object it meets; the fourth opens a crack that the tower's own weight can widen. The complete sequence costs 298,400 EU, eight percent of full reserve, spent as 74,600 EU at each contact. The climb and the shallow curve expose his approach; three bright strikes are followed by a deeper thump at the fourth. A low roof or broken approach stops the run before it begins, a shield turned aside spoils a contact during it, and after the fourth touch his committed descent leaves him answering from below his starting height. Sound masonry without a vulnerable join can resist the final touch; the sequence does not make every tower fall.

**RING OF MERCY (Signature Technique)**

**Ring of Mercy** · **Flies a slow circle.** *Arrows entering the ring grow uncertain and spend themselves politely in snow.* He carries the Lumin Mantle around a closed flight path and keeps the crossing air pushing incoming arrows away from the space inside. The circle costs 223,800 EU per minute, six percent of full reserve, giving fifteen minutes from full to the last tenth before additional impacts. A silver-blue rim follows Mirenai's wingtip, accompanied by a thin note that rises whenever an arrow meets it. Before the circle closes, an open stretch admits a shot; while it holds, attacks from inside the ring or through several places at once bypass or overload its outer hold; after he leaves the path, the protection ends. It protects the enclosed ground against crossing arrows, and does not seal it against a person walking in.

**TREATY STROKE (Signature Technique)**

**Treaty Stroke** · **Point kisses an enemy banner.** *The cloth refuses to lift for an hour unless its bearer speaks the truth of their cause.* At contact, Judicium binds the cloth's movement to the bearer's spoken account of the cause. The hour is paid at the touch: 298,400 EU, eight percent of full reserve, for one banner. The lance point flashes blue-white and the cloth gives a tight snap before going slack. A covered banner prevents contact, a same-Stage ward can resist the binding while the point touches, and afterward the bearer can speak the truth or abandon the bound banner and raise another. The working binds that cloth; it does not expose every hidden motive to Aelor, command an army, or settle an unclear treaty for him.

### Releases

**THE OPEN SKY (First Release)** · folk name: Elareth, "the sung register"; commonly "in voice"

Arc-blue vanes spread from the heels and the backs of his greaves, and the star-iron of Skylark shines through its memory-runes. A single clear whistle opens into his low held note. Skybound Mandate holds at the top of its existing Grade through a turn, with Judicium keeping the opposing pushes balanced and Luminalis showing the edge of each foothold. Skylark carries the hold through the instant the lance strikes, helping him keep his footing against the answering force. The release costs 9,325 EU each second and holds six minutes from full to the last tenth, before other spending. Its force needs clean air and room for the displaced current to move; it cannot make a blocked canyon into open sky. Released silently, the vanes, light and sound still appear, but his footing remains at its ordinary strength and gains no rise toward the top of the Grade. Ash across a landing, a low ceiling, or a strike during the spoken call can spoil the form. Losing Skylark removes its support at lance contact. Afterward his calves shake and his lance hand has to rest.

**THE WITNESSED ROAD (Second Release)** · folk name: Elaruren, "the witnessed note"; commonly "a held chord"

The vanes unfold into silver-blue paths through the surrounding air, and his low note becomes a chord held across the field. This opens and powers THE WITNESSED ROAD, his true Domain. Within its boundary, his released pressure footing works one Grade above its ordinary expression; the rise belongs to that footing and gives no matching rise to his Gnosis or to every blow he lands. Mirenai shares the route through their existing breath-count and Transference bond, with no increase to the bond's reach. The standing field costs 466.25 EU each second and holds two hours from full to the last tenth if he spends nothing else; hard manoeuvres and attacks shorten that time at their own stated costs. Released silently, the field still forms, shines and sounds, but its footing stays at the ordinary Grade. Its edges can be crossed, its ground contested by another Domain, and its pressure ridges fouled by ash. Separation from Mirenai takes away their shared heading without erasing Aelor's field. Skylark holds the firm approach at the lance point; disarming him removes that part. When the release ends his Crystal is Overgrown until a full night's sleep, and his mood makes loose air stir around him. Holding it below the last tenth risks a Crystal Fracture Event.

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of House Venrik and the Eressean crown. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Aelor Vaelith was born in the Grand Principality of Eresse, on the elven old-ground of the New World. No record names his parents. None says whether the Vaelith name joins him to Serenyra Vaelith, who carries it too.

Eresse kept its law by exact repetition, and a child of standing learned it by recital. The part given to Aelor was route-law: the air-oaths of the Principality, which skies stood open under which treaty, who had witnessed each oath, and whether the witness had been paid. He learned them in their order and gave them back without a word out of place. His teachers set him treaties to recite. They never asked him what a treaty was for. He was taught the proverb as a rule of the air: "A standard nobody witnessed is a standard held contingently."

#### The Making

He came to the air on Mirenai, a Skyglass Pegasus born from the Velthaeir cloud-archives. He learned her by breath. Rider and mount came to share one breath-count and one heading. His commands fell low and even, and she answered them by folding a wing half a feather. She could bank a gust. She gave it back later, like a stored turn.

He learned to step on the pressure ridges of the air, and his pivots in midair read like stone footing. He rode with his knees soft and his hips floating over the saddle. He climbed before he struck. He would not dive until the shield's rim was already set to catch the answer.

The night the forest burned under a pass in the Eressean mountain chains, the people of the valley went out by glider along the one corridor of air over it. Asta Venrik flew at their head with the Skyfang flights. Aelor went to the far mouth. Ash lay thick on the ridges there, and he could find no footing on them. He scrubbed a clean lane and held it. At the near end Eryndrel Vaelune of the Aerial Ward read the air and called the corridor closing, aloud, for every rider on the line. Aelor kept the lane open. The gliders came through it onto clear air, and the corridor closed behind Asta as Eryndrel had read it would. She came out through the closing.

From then on Eryndrel called Aelor's dive, and Aelor called Eryndrel's wind.

The Crown made him Sky-Marshal of the Aerial Ward and named him Imperial Pegasus, "a singular honour of Eresse." He became the Crown's champion and treaty-lancer, "the blade Eresse shows in daylight," serving under the Guild Accord's Arbitration Division for air-oath enforcement.

#### The Cost

The breath-count he shared with Mirenai fell away sharply when they were parted. He kept her close.

Ash and Rot miasma roughened the ridges under him, and in either he slipped until he had scrubbed a lane. Enclosed canyons and low ceilings cut his working room.

He was raised to know a cause by what it declared aloud, in treaty and witnessed oath. His signet and his lance both made a cause declare itself. The Signet of the First Concord spoke old treaties clean, and a liar within its hearing tasted tin. His Treaty Stroke set the lance point to an enemy banner, and the cloth would not lift for an hour unless its bearer spoke the truth of the cause. A quiet cause gave him nothing. Where the treaty was unclear, he could not tell which side it stood on.

So he gave up the call of his own dive. Eryndrel made it. The Crown sent Aelor where the enemy was known and the treaty clear, and the Ward kept a reader at his side for everything else.

#### Where They Stand

Aelor Vaelith is Sky-Marshal of the Aerial Ward and the Imperial Pegasus, the Crown's champion and treaty-lancer. He escorts imperial envoys across hostile skies. He enforces Concord Overflight, under which no siege engine moves beneath an open treaty. He interdicts Rot-carried spore clouds with cold-draft corridors and controlled descents. Where air-oaths are at stake he presents the Moon-writ at council, and when no gate is safe he carries imperial writs between realms.

Mirenai's hooves leave silent blue rings in snow. Mount and rider move as one long curve. He carries the lance Skylark, which lengthens or shortens by a hand's breadth at his heartbeat. The round shield Ur-Seal hands the excess of a blow to the next beat, and the inner hem of his Cloak of Four Phases is stitched with moon cycles.

Eryndrel often rides with him. The Ward's couriers fly Lyss Arvale between tribunals and call her "wind's conscience."

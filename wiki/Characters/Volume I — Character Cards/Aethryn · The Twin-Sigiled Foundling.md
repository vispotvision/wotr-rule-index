## VIII. Traits

**TWIN SIGIL RESONANCE (Primary Trait)**

Forehead and palm sigils operate as paired geometric Wellspring nodes, reading and rerouting resonance without being asked. The forehead supplies the read and the palm holds the current it catches; each mark pulses after the other. The involuntary traffic occupies his attention and adds to the sensory load he cannot reliably govern. Neither node alone produces the Loop. A [Uur] Disruption working applied to the live connection can interrupt the paired current; it must overcome that current, but requires neither a higher Stage nor a rare Wellspring.

**UNFINISHED LAW (Passive Trait)**

His existence sits outside settled Wellspring Law; a working that brushes against him treats him as a pending clause rather than a fixed target. The tell is a worked boundary slipping around his outline instead of holding steadily to it. This unfinished address also prevents him from relying on a steady response: a helpful working that needs the same fixed target can slip in the same way. It gives no protection against ordinary force, falling material or a restraint that closes on his body without first naming his soul.

**SILENT PATTERN (Secondary Trait)**

Under distress, an involuntary psychic distortion warps the emotional intention of everyone nearby. Several people interrupt their own words or reach for something and hesitate together, while his marked hand tightens against his side. He cannot choose whom it catches, and the keepers trying to settle him are caught with everyone else; their changed responses can feed the distress that started it. Gnosis Cognition resists the psychic misdirection and Resilience Anchoring resists the loss of continuity; distance beyond the disturbed field ends further exposure. Neither counter restores a memory already lost.

## X. Techniques

**ECLIPSE TOUCH (Technique)**

The palm sigil catches a resonance through Fixatio, holds it and siphons its current. The marked palm stiffens and the lower note of his hum deepens as the hold takes. Keeping that hold occupies the palm's node and adds incoming current to a Shell that already leaks; the hand trembles as the load rises. The palm alone siphons and cannot produce the paired eclipse-law field. A [Uur] Disruption working that breaks the held resonance ends the siphon; a prepared practitioner at Stage III can contest that hold without a rare Wellspring or a higher Stage.

**THE EYE BENEATH THE EYE (Technique)**

The forehead sigil reads through Judicium, seeing a lie as broken geometry; Somnalis's accidental bleed lets it catch intention as well as fact. His gaze fixes and the forehead mark flickers while the higher note of the hum thins. The read occupies his attention, and conflicting impressions add sensory load rather than giving him a second mind to sort them. It reveals what the sigil can read in the person before him, not an absent event or an unknown origin. Resilience Ward meeting his Gnosis Perception's Grade prevents a clean, exact read; a sincere mistake remains a mistake he must investigate rather than a deliberate lie the sigil can expose.

**SIGIL RESONANCE LOOP (Signature Technique)**

**Effect** · Both sigils close the loop at once, and the space around him stops finishing its own sentence: perception, gravity and emotional direction all bend toward whatever the loop is reading, and Aethryn blurs at the edges as though slipping into his own shadow. **Cost** · The Loop spends memory. Distress or sensory overload during the surge wipes his own short-term recall, and every pass through it erodes his trust in what "himself" even refers to. **Limit** · Both sigils have to be active together. One alone reads or siphons; only the pair, closed, produces the eclipse-law field, and closing it is not a thing he has learned to do on command. **Counter** · A Coagula-anchored grounding field, run before the Loop closes, sinters the surge's edges shut and stops it from completing. Failing that, a practitioner with a strongly imposed Dominion or a deep Temperance lead can overwrite what the Loop is trying to read. **What nobody knows.** Whether the Loop is a wound in him or a wound in the law he sits outside of. The audible tell is the two notes beating together; the visible tell remains the blur around his edges. A prepared Stage III practitioner can apply [Uur] Disruption to the live connection before the pair closes, or contest the same connection while the field holds. The Loop needs both nodes and fails when either stops carrying its share. After exposure, Resilience Anchoring resists further damage to memory while withdrawal from the field ends further contact; neither returns what has already been erased. The spent memories remain lost, and repeated losses accumulate.

## Lore

### The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Citadel's high seats. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Aethryn was found at a shuttered Gate. Who or what left him there has never been established. The Gate Reflection Event is the only lead anyone has to his origin, and it has gone nowhere.

The Accord entered him, as it entered anything left on its ground. A clerk wrote his name in ink. The fields for a parish, a house and a charter stayed empty.

He was kept in an Accord hospice-temple, among the sick its keepers nursed and the dying they sat with in shifts. The keepers watched the beds. A slight, quiet boy was fed with the rest, counted at night and otherwise left to himself. He learned the house by its sounds: the change of the shift at the beds, the murmur of a sitting, the layer-out's step in the corridor.

#### The Making

In those unwatched years he built two workings out of nothing but his own pressure. Their marks were on his forehead and palm. The palm closed on a resonance and would not let it go. The forehead saw a lie as broken geometry, a line breaking its own angle, and it read what a speaker intended as readily as what he said. Neither waited for his leave.

The keepers noticed the marks late. By then he had built both, the palm's siphon and the forehead's sight, before anyone could tell him such things needed a teacher. The two sigils were the record of it. The Guild had no form for a foundling who had cleared the gate twice before anyone was watching.

#### The Cost

Research and Archives took charge of him once the marks were seen. From then on he was watched.

Sometimes the two sigils closed together, and he never learned to close them on command. The record calls it the Loop. When it closed, the space around him stopped finishing its own sentence. Perception, gravity and the direction of every feeling in reach bent toward whatever the pair was reading, and he blurred at the edges as though slipping into his own shadow. It spent memory. Distress or sensory overload during a surge wiped his own short-term recall. Every pass wore at his trust in what "himself" even referred to.

Distress did a second thing. It sent a distortion out of him into everyone near, warping what they meant. In Pattern Surge #4 the researchers studying him lost twelve to eighteen minutes of experiential continuity, and they came through it uneventfully. He came out of that surge holding none of it. What he had of those minutes, he had from their notes.

The palm taught him his one refusal. He did not take a hand held out to him. He kept the marked palm shut against his side, and a keeper who reached for it found a fist.

The forehead gave him a belief. It cost him every comfort he was offered. He held that a kind word was a lie if its angle broke. A keeper once told him that whoever left him at the Gate had meant to come back. The sigil saw the angle break. He said so, to her face, in front of the beds. Nobody told him anything soft about the Gate again.

He formed no bond with any person or place, and the record cannot say whether that was choice or inability.

#### Where They Stand

After Pattern Surge #4 the matter of what the Accord should call him was brought to the Council Spire. The Council could find no form for him. Argen Vael'thar framed a point of law for the Grand Seer, Aurgon Mintruix: what the Codex would become if it wrote the boy into it. Lyss Arvale carried it to the Seer and set his answer down whole. It named no form for him. Drakvor cast the judgement. The Accord would not induct him, would not classify him and would not leave him.

Aethryn was in neither room. No record says whether anyone told him.

He is a ward of the Guild Accord with no charter under him. The hospice-temple is sealed now. He lives inside it under monitored care, watched without a break. The record carries other names for him, the Pale Omen, the Unfinished Child, the Circle-Bearer and the Silent Pattern. He answers to Aethryn. Not even the Guild that shelters him claims the right to name what he is.

Where a completed stage should sit in his Crystal there is an open circle, and a Wellspring that brushes it reads an open question. The words kept beside it are "the circle opens for you, but closes for me."

Nobody knows whether the Loop is a wound in him or a wound in the law he sits outside of.

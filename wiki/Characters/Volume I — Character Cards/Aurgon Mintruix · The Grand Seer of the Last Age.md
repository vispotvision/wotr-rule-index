## VIII. Traits

**WHOLE RECALL (Primary Trait)**

His Core keeps the words he has consciously heard in their order, holding the remembered sequence steady while other thoughts move around it. This is the faculty he carried from boyhood; it cannot add what he failed to hear, infer a speaker's hidden purpose, or repair a deliberately altered memory. Its standing draw is negligible, but close comparison occupies his attention and makes a new interruption harder to follow. His lips repeat the first word without sound, and a black-violet thread crosses the white of his aura. Evidence withheld from his senses is absent from the record, and a true statement with its context removed can still mislead his judgement.

**WELLSPRING LENS (Passive Trait)**

**Views any magical system, Domain, or relic brought into his chamber and traces its lineage back through centuries of innovation and corruption**, giving a precise diagnosis of which Wellsprings, which glyph dialects, and what failure modes are present. His Shell holds the trace steady long enough for Gnosis to separate its layers. The reading needs an accessible trace; a closed ward of equal Gnosis-facing Grade prevents a precise diagnosis. His pupils brighten in pale rings and a dry page-turning sound comes from the air beside his ears. Ordinary recognition has negligible draw; a sustained search spends 0.1% of his full reserve per minute and blurs his sight of the present. Overheated or reset records have missing passages that he cannot recover by looking harder.

**CONTINUUM LEDGER (Inherited Trait, from the Chain of Witness)**

**Remembers entire timelines aborted or overwritten by catastrophic events**; in rare cases can re-anchor a small element from a lost timeline — an idea, a spell, a name — into the present. The Chain keeps these memories bound to his own identity; a restored fragment must have a surviving pattern in that memory and a present holder that can receive it. Remembering is near-costless; bringing one fragment across spends 1% of his full reserve and leaves him struggling to place recent events in their right order until he rests. His voice briefly carries an earlier pronunciation of the same word. A closed ward around the receiving mind or object stops the transfer, and destroying that receiver ends it. A fragment carries information, never a restored person or an entire replacement history.

**VOW OF WITNESS (Passive Trait)**

**Any oath sworn seriously in his presence cannot be forgotten by the world.** Even with every record erased, the Wellspring itself tilts to punish or reward the outcome. His Attraction Layer binds the oath's intended terms to its witness at the node. The standing record takes no continuing personal draw; the cost is his permanent bond and his inability to deny what he witnessed without turning that law against himself. A second, lower note sounds beneath the oath's final word, and the near-white aura gathers around his throat. This records the promise that was seriously made; it cannot supply an unspoken promise or widen its terms. Refusal to swear prevents the bond; exact fulfilment exhausts its claim. Erasing the words afterward does neither.

## IX. Domain

**THE WITNESS CHAMBER (Domain)**

The chamber and the node share his standing law: what he has witnessed has a surviving witness. At Zenith this is part of his presence; his Second Release opens its active reach across the chamber, stopping at its walls or the node's boundary, whichever comes first. It holds a separate record of words spoken and workings exposed inside that reach, so changing a visitor's memory cannot change the copy held by Aurgon. It does not read unspoken thoughts, make testimony true, or bind someone who has not sworn. Opening takes the spoken call's beat; from full reserve its active hold lasts ten minutes, spending 9% of full reserve per minute and closing at the last tenth. Other workings shorten that time. A peer's stronger Dominion can push its border back, a closed ward keeps concealed contents unread, and the door remains a way beyond its active reach. Closing the active field ends new recording without undoing an oath already witnessed.

## X. Techniques

**ANAMNETIC EXCISION (Signature Technique)**

Deletes one very specific piece of knowledge from a soul, a library, or a relic, leaving everything else around it intact. Each excision scars both him and the Wellspring node. It is used sparingly, and every use is a withdrawal against a balance he cannot replenish by any means available to him. It removes; it cannot plant. What is excised leaves a gap, not a replacement, and a mind or archive missing a load-bearing fact behaves exactly as unpredictably afterward as one would expect. **Distribute the knowledge before he can reach it.** Anamnetic Excision targets a specific holder; a fact copied into enough independent minds or documents outruns any single excision, and he knows this, which is why he uses it only when he has already confirmed there is nowhere else the knowledge lives. He fixes the chosen fact and its holder in his reading, then resets only that record through Anamnesis, with his own bound Crystal carrying the irreversible loss. A thin white break crosses one pupil and the page-turning sound stops. During the cut, a closed ward or a broken connection to the holder prevents completion; after it, an independent surviving copy permits the knowledge to be learned again. Ordinary reserve recovery cannot mend the scar.

> **What nobody knows.** What the one question is that he refuses to answer for anyone, Titans included — or why refusing it, rather than answering it badly, is the choice he has held for the full 1,600 years of his bond.

**Releases**

**THE OPEN LEAF (First Release)** · folk name: Sine mensura, "Off the meter"

Fine pale rings spread across his eyes and the skin around them; his hair lifts clear of his shoulders and his aura shows its near-white black-violet hue. The dry sound of a page turning ends in a low glass note. Whole Recall holds one chosen record still while Wellspring Lens reads its exposed layers, bringing that reading to the top of its existing Grade without making him quicker or stronger. Ascensio spreads the strain of the held comparison; the total work still has to be paid. From a full reserve the deliberate form lasts sixty minutes, spending 1.5% of full reserve per minute and ending at the last tenth. At his Stage its resting bodily mark needs no call; the deliberate rise does. Released silently, it holds the form at his ordinary reading strength and loses the rise to the Grade's top. A closed ward or an absent trace defeats the read; several exposed records can overload Judicium and leave the chosen one unread. Forcing a reset record risks destroying what remains. Afterwards his sight takes time to settle on the room, and holding past the limit risks fracture.

**THE WITNESS CHAMBER (Second Release)** · folk name: In districtu suo, "A main of his own"

The rings at his eyes spread as pale lines through the chamber's light, his shadow reaches the walls, and the glass note holds beneath every voice. This opens and powers the Domain described above, using the node as the common witness for records that would otherwise rest in separate minds and objects. Its reading may rise one Grade, to EX+, but its authority over the room still contests Dominion; it gives him no licence to compel belief. From a full reserve it lasts ten minutes at 9% of full reserve per minute, closing at the last tenth. Silently released, the chamber still records but its reading stays at EX. Its reach ends at the chamber or node boundary. Closed wards, competing Dominion and exposed records numerous enough to swamp his attention can defeat the active reading. Afterwards his Crystal runs Overgrown until a full night's sleep; he remains easy to find by the light leaking from his skin. Forcing it below the last tenth causes a Crystal Fracture Event.

**THE RETURNED WORD (Third Release)** · folk name: Fonte teste, "Countersigned"

His face and hands turn translucent around their pale scars, the chamber's white light gains a dark-violet rim, and a deeper note from the node sounds beneath his own. The Continuum Ledger brings one small, exactly remembered fragment from an aborted timeline into an unwarded present holder while the chamber holds its identity steady. Judicium distinguishes the chosen fragment from neighbouring memories; Ascensio spreads the strain across the hold without reducing what is owed. It can restore an idea, a spell's record or a name, never a person, a whole timeline, or facts he does not hold. Reading remains within EX+; the further change is that one fragment's identity holds as local law, not a new Grade or an attack. From full reserve it holds for sixty seconds at 90% of full reserve per minute, ending at the last tenth; transferring the fragment also pays Continuum Ledger's 1% cost and therefore shortens the hold. A silent release holds a fragment for inspection but cannot carry it into the present. A warded receiver prevents the transfer, leaving the active field interrupts it, and competing Dominion can separate receiver from witness. A completed record remains ordinary evidence that can be challenged or destroyed. Afterwards he runs Overgrown for a day and a night, with an ache through his body; forcing the hold below its limit risks the Ledger confusing remembered history with the present.

## XIII. Physical Description

His hair is white as the underside of peeled birch bark, thin at the temples and falling straight below his shoulders. His long face has a narrow jaw, hollow cheeks and pale grey eyes beneath lids creased by age; the ears remain sharply pointed, their rims almost transparent. He is tall and spare, with a narrow chest, prominent collarbones and long hands whose knuckles press against the skin. His robe is soft ash-coloured wool over an undyed linen shirt, fitted closely at the wrists and hanging loose through the ribs. The cuffs have been turned and stitched again, and the inner hem is rubbed smooth where it brushes his ankles. Fine branching marks lie under the skin of his throat and forearms, whitening where an excision has left a permanent scar. He stands with his weight carefully shared between both feet, turning his head before he moves the rest of his body.

### The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Citadel's high seats. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Aurgon Mintruix was born an Elder Elf in a glade-city of the Inner World, in the Late Harmonial Age, the centuries before the Great Spirit War. Domain fracture took the city. Archival erasure took everything that had been written about it. Its streets survived in one place, his memory. He has never named the house that raised him, and nobody has found a document that names it for him.

He could keep a thing whole from boyhood: what was said, in the words it was said in, in the order it came.

#### The Making

In time he was given other names, and among them was the Library That Breathes. Any magical system, Domain or relic brought before him he could trace back through centuries of innovation and corruption, and he could name the Wellsprings in it, the glyph dialects it was written in and the ways it would fail. It was said that he held the most complete magical knowledge in the world. Nobody could prove it or disprove it.

He climbed. The higher he went, the more of the futures he could read ended in catastrophe, and in each of them his own ascent set the catastrophe off.

He designed the Chain of Witness in the Concord precursor order, alongside a coalition of Elven High Paruns and Dwarven rune-lords. He built it to contain himself. He fused his Soul Crystal to the local Wellspring node, and the Realms gained a stability they could use as a fixed point. In the same transaction he lost his mobility, his power to fight and any path to full Archonhood.

The chain left him a second memory. He remembered whole timelines that catastrophe had aborted or overwritten, and in rare cases he carried a small piece of one into the present: an idea, a spell, a name. When what he had become was recognised at last, the recognition came from the node he had agreed never to leave.

#### The Cost

The chain made his body the node's anchor-organ. He could not leave its radius without catastrophic backlash to himself and to the site, and a step past the edge of its field felt like his organs coming unstitched. He stayed mortal. His death was postponed.

In raw power he could have erased cities. Any offensive working he drew would have risked destabilising the Wellspring he anchored, and with it everything he had spent ages preserving. No record ever showed him trying.

He kept one cut, and he used it sparingly. He could take a single piece of knowledge out of a soul, a library or a relic and leave everything around it intact. Each cut scarred him and scarred the node. Nothing he could reach paid back what it drew. He planted nothing in the gap, and a mind that had lost a load-bearing fact went on without it unpredictably. He cut only when he had confirmed the knowledge lived nowhere else.

He believed his own ascent would bring catastrophe on the Realms, and he chained himself to one place on that belief. There was one question he would answer for no one, Titans included. He never answered it, well or badly, in all the years of his bond. People who asked him what the chain had cost him were answered one layer below what they asked.

#### Where They Stand

Aurgon Mintruix has stood without a break from the closing of the First Eon into the present Withering, and one of his names is the Witness of that closing. He receives in his chamber at the node. The Arbitration Division of the Guild Accord once ranked him its Magister-Seer, and his readings carry legal weight. He holds that rank no longer. Titans, Archons and Concord Envoys still come to him as a last resort and pay his price, whatever it turns out to be that day.

The Accord sent him one more matter, about a boy. Research and Archives kept a foundling who carried two sigils nobody had taught him, and the Council could find no form for him. Argen Vael'thar, the Prime Arbiter, framed it for the Seer: what the Codex would become if it wrote the boy into it. Vaelthor Ashen-Meridian sat behind him as counsel without a vote. The point of law went past him to Aurgon. Lyss Arvale, an Oath Witness of the Division, carried it into the chamber. Aurgon had her ask it again. His price that day was an oath. She would carry his answer into the Council Spire whole, in his words and in his order, with nothing softened. She swore it in his presence, where the world cannot forget an oath, and he answered. It named no form for the boy. The Accord keeps the boy unclassified, under watch, in a sealed hospice-temple.

Vaelthor has never come to the chamber. There is still one question Aurgon answers for no one. A successor is whispered to exist already, someone whose Crystal could bear a lesser version of his chain. Aurgon has confirmed it to nobody who has asked.

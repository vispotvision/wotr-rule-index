## VIII. Ability

Cozbi Mahuo, Head of House Mahuo and First Keeper of the Order of the Spiritual Reality, is a man of twenty-five and one of the great summoners the Continuum has ever recorded: the one the scenes call the God of Summons. Summoning is a three-plane crossing, a vessel shaped on the Physical Plane, an identity donated on the Soul Plane and a recognition held by Attraction Force on the Plane of Fate. He works all three at the highest tier any practitioner has reached. He founds beings with a core of their own (Concordia), binds living powers by sealed compact, and drafts forms out of harvested soul-fragment (Animatria and Infinite), and under all of it sits his Ability, Antithesis, the one phenomenon his whole architecture is built around: a thing and its negation forced to occupy the same point. Everything he borrows from other souls is coin; Antithesis is the root current of his own. Figures marked *(derived)* are worked from the Fracture of Worlds tables and stand as canon on use until a source value beats them.

### Ability: Antithesis (상극 · Sanggeuk)

*Sanggeuk* is the old word for two forces that overcome each other, as water quenches fire. In Cozbi it is not a contest between two things. It is one place where a thing and its contradiction are both true and neither yields. Per the Field Manual: Cozbi is Antithesis.

* **The Seam (이음새 · Ieumsae):** He holds a single point in the air beside him where a field has two opposite faces at once: an attraction and its exact negation, a sink and a source sharing one location. Physically it is a point where a potential and its opposite are superposed, so that matter feels both a pull toward the point and a push away from it, and the two do not average out, because they act at different scales. The tell is optical: light bends toward the point and away from it at once, so the seam looks like a pinprick that both swallows and shines. It tastes of struck flint. He can hold one seam comfortably and a few with strain.
* **The Contradiction (모순 · Mosun):** *Mosun* is the old story of the merchant whose spear pierces any shield and whose shield stops any spear: two true claims that cannot both hold. Where the pull and the push overlap, what they act on is torn in two directions by two equally lawful forces and comes apart along the shear, which is the same reason a stretched thing breaks at its weakest line. At the small scale this is annihilation in the loose sense: stored mass-energy released where a thing meets its own negation. In a fight it is a strike that armor cannot resist, since plate has nothing to resist: it is not pushed, it is split.
* **Tides (조류 · Joryu):** A held seam is a pressure gradient. Anything between him and it, loose stone, a weapon, a falling man, is dragged, then thrown. He uses it to take a blade out of a hand, pull a charging fighter onto his guard, or fling a host's bodies where he needs them, and it moves his own constructs faster than their legs.
* **The Fold (접기 · Jeopgi):** A seam placed along a line is a fold, a door that opens in one place and closes in another. It is how he removes ground from under a fighter, collapses a stair or closes a gap between himself and a target without moving. It scales with how much space the seam is stretched across.
* **Nature:** Active and rooted. It is his own root current, not conscripted or borrowed, and it scales with his resolve, not his reserve, which is why an opponent who has beaten every borrowed soul on his structure has not yet met it. Its price is that it tears at whatever holds it: the seam's two faces pull on his own attention, and a flinch is a collapse.
* **Cost and Tell:** The bent light, the flint taste, and a faint ring in the ears from the pressure gradient.
* **Counter and Failure:** Break his hold. A seam is held by concentration, and a fighter who strikes at his resolve rather than at the seam makes it lapse. Distance beats the gradient. The failure is the contradiction turning inward: a point that holds both claims will, if his hold slips, hold both claims in him.

## IX. Techniques

### Signature Technique: The Founding (건국 · Geonguk)

A Concordia-level summon is not a projection. It is a founding. He creates a small Realm, a micro-world with its own boundary, and in it a being that exists as a semi-independent presence with a core, a shell and an attraction layer of its own. It thinks, adapts, feels and persists beyond his immediate attention. It is the art behind eidolons that are people, and the most dangerous form, because a founded being with enough internal coherence begins to resist dissolution. Stage XII is the minimum, and Stage XIV is the usual gate for genuine autonomy. He founds autonomous beings at XIII because Antithesis gives the founding a core that holds without recognition from a Hollow Throne.

* **The Boundary (경계 · Gyeonggye):** The Realm is a cavity with a wall. A being needs somewhere to be real, so he builds the place first. Inside the wall his law holds; outside it the being is a stranger. A founded being cannot leave without him, which is both leash and shelter.
* **The Core Analog (핵 · Haek):** He gives the being a core analog, shell analog and attraction analog of its own, drawn from his Soul Crystal. A founding costs about 5,000,000 EU and 40,000 EU a second *(derived: about forty times a 120,000 EU named-guest casting)*, a tenth of a percent of his reserve to found and about ten hours to keep. He founds one, or two with strain, and only when a fight deserves it.
* **The Charter (칙령 · Chikryeong):** He writes the being's purpose into the founding, and the being obeys its charter, not his word of the moment. A warden holds a gate, a general holds a field, a scholar answers questions. In a fight it is his second self: a being that fights as the hero it was written to be, with judgment he does not have to supply.
* **Nature:** Active, deliberate, expensive. It scales with Stage and bound to his coherence. A founded being that grows stable may refuse to be dissolved, which is the real risk: he has made a person and cannot always unmake it.
* **Cost and Failure:** The being's coherence is his. If the Realm's boundary cracks, the strain lands on him. Template defect applies: whatever he forgot to write in the charter is a gap in the being.
* **Counter:** Collapse the boundary, or outlast the founding's stability, or find the line the charter forgot.

### Signature Technique: The Bound Wild (야생의 결박 · Yasaeng-ui Gyeolbak)

He binds living powers: beasts, Well-born creatures and the great creatures of the Bestiary's Summoned and Bound. Unlike his drafts, a sealed compact gets cheaper each time it answers, because recognition accumulates, so his oldest compacts cost him almost nothing and answer almost at once. He is the rare drafter who keeps a standing roster alongside an infinite catalog.

* **The Compact (계약 · Gyeyak):** A sealed compact is written on the Plane of Fate with the creature's own name as the address and a price both sides can read. Binding is a coupled system: the creature's state is locked to a control signal he holds, the way a servo is held to a command. A compact he has sealed is powered by the creature's own Essence, so he pays only the leash, about 60,000 EU a second *(derived)*, and the leash gets cheaper with every answering.
* **The Roster (명부 · Myeongbu):** He keeps a living list of sealed compacts, each with a price and a clause. A fight can call a war-beast, a storm-creature or a ward-guardian by its seal and have it arrive already knowing how to be what it is.
* **The Leash (고삐 · Gobi):** A bound will tests its leash. He holds the creature's will down with his own, so a second great binding at once strains his attention. A fighter who strikes at his resolve, and not the beast, is hitting the real weak point.
* **Spirit Singularity (정령 합일 · Jeongnyeong Hapil):** Where lawful practice restricts it to drafted forms, he compresses bound presences into one core entity carrying the aggregate. One thing comes out, and it is worse than the sum was. The constituent presences do not survive it as themselves, so he does it with drafted forms and conscripts and never with a bound creature he respects.
* **Nature:** Active, sustained, bound to the compact's terms. It scales with the creature's power and his resolve, not his reserve.
* **Cost and Tell:** The compact's price is paid in what the creature wants, and the great ones want things that cost. A seal of his house color opens on the creature's brow before the binding sets.
* **Counter and Failure:** The compact runs on the creature's own Essence, so exhausting it breaks the compact. Strike while his attention is on the leash. Failure: a creature that decides the price was unfair.

### Signature Technique: The Conscripts (징집 · Jingjip)

The art the Accord names without qualification, and the reason the Infinite Summoning Arts page calls him the reason the art is regulated: legion and Infinite practice combined, on a foundation of harvested soul-fragment rather than lawful draft. He fragments his Core output into threads, each thread anchoring one call, and the Essence he spends on the call is not all his own. Some of it is taken from people who were not asked.

* **The Harvest (수확 · Suhwak):** He draws soul-fragment, the small residue of Essence a living soul sheds, from willing and unwilling sources over years, and folds it until it stops being a draft and becomes a thread bent to the shape of his will. A hundred unwilling sources can give him a hundred threads.
* **The Thread (실 · Sil):** Each thread is a separate load on his body. A drafter at his scale does not sleep normally, cannot digest properly in a campaign and is the thinnest man in the camp. A standing form on a thread is Tier II to III: an eidolon with tactical intelligence, an ancestral echo, a scout or a wall.
* **Axis Binding (축 결박 · Chuk Gyeolbak):** A form's traits tied to one of his own Spirit Axes shift as his own state does: a form that hardens when he is resolute and falters when he is not. It is the most responsive technique in the art and the least deniable, because damage to the form reaches him as a change in himself.
* **Echo Manifestation (잔향 · Janhyang):** Remnants of dissolved forms are gathered and folded into new ones, so a long fight leaves him richer than he began. Forms destroyed by dissolution leave nothing to gather.
* **Nature:** Active, mass-produced, economical. Each form costs about 150,000 EU to raise and 25,000 EU a second to keep *(derived: the top of the Animatria range)*. The souls inside are not paid for, which is the crime.
* **Cost and Tell:** Threads of black-violet run from his chest, and the people near him feel a draft where their own warmth should be.
* **Counter and Failure:** Threads are readable: anyone with comparable Perception can count them, and his exact commitment is visible before the first form lands. Cut his line of attention and the Feed lapses, leaving only what the Binding alone will hold. Failure: the seam in a structure built from many borrowed souls, which a Harmonia-trained ear can find.

## The Art, Read

He stands turned away and looks back over his left shoulder, as though someone said his name and he is deciding whether it was worth turning for. The hair is black, thick and cut ragged at the brow and nape, and where the light finds it it goes green, a mallard's head, wet pine. The skin is pale with a gray-green cast. The one eye we are given is gold-yellow, heavy-lidded and sidelong. The ear runs long to a point, with gold at the lobe.

He wears a long greatcoat of olive wool, heavily pleated down the back, a gold-fringed epaulette on the right shoulder, and over the shoulders a great mantle of white fur, long spiked guard-hairs with lavender and rose in their shadows. A gold boss of concentric rings sits at the nape with a finial hanging under it; three linked open lozenges cross the upper back in gold, and a column of gold lozenge plaques runs down the left side over a navy-black underlayer. The coat fits exactly and shows no wear anywhere. He is a man who is never caught out in his clothes. Behind him a white blade of light comes down on the diagonal, fringed pink and green.

**The colors.** The Mahuo colors are vermilion and white. He kept the white and refuses the vermilion, which is the color other houses' armies carried into the field over Mahuo banners. The olive is the coat of the Sum-gol Post. He will wear vermilion again on the day Sum-gol is his by his own hand.

## Who He Is

**What he survived.** He watched his house protected to death. The Moto put Mahuo banners over Moto columns; the Yukari and the Zettari each went to war on the excuse of saving the Mahuo; between the three of them the house was destroyed, and each of the three still tells it differently. He came out of it knowing one thing in the bones: to be carried as a banner is one way to die, and to be protected is another.

**What he believes.** Since the Soul Plane broke, death has been an estate without a steward. Spirits drift, hauntings spread, the tides of life and death run crooked, and every great house in the south argues over who owns the door while nobody carries the dead through it. That work belongs to the Mahuo. The house words were always *Call, and It Answers*, and his reading is that the Mahuo were never the ones made to answer.

**The want, out loud.** That the world recognize the Mahuo as the Order of the Spiritual Reality, the Keepers of Death: a function nobody can do without, held by the Mahuo the way the Corwen hold the springs and the Lamberts the count. A house that is needed cannot be carried as a banner, and a house nobody can afford to lose is very hard to kill.

**The desire, underneath.** He wants his own dead back. The people of Sum-gol killed in the war never arrived anywhere he can find them. The Order is a son's grief raised to a doctrine, and he knows it, and he would kill to keep anyone else from knowing it.

**The lie.** *"Every soul I keep was drift: unclaimed, unowned, already lost."* The Accord says otherwise, and the Accord is right about some of them. **Tell:** speaking of a soul he took from the living, his tense slips from past to present, because to him that person is still standing in the room.

## Why He Declines Naiser

Naiser Yukari offered to restore the Mahuo royal line at Sum-gol and step the Yukari back. Cozbi refuses, for four reasons, in the order he would give them.

1. **The Yukari tell the story of killing the Mahuo themselves.** By their own account the Moto stood back and the Yukari did the slaughtering. He takes a house at its word.
2. **A crown from Yukari hands makes the Mahuo tenants.** Naiser's terms at the Cedar Enclave put every touch of the spirit world under Yukari counsel. A Mahuo king seated by the Yukari is a doorman at a Yukari door.
3. **The Seikai were "our making."** Naiser said so himself. Cozbi will not take counsel on the dead from the house that built the people who broke the Soul Plane.
4. **To accept is to learn nothing.** A house that takes its seat back as a gift can lose it the same way.

> "Your own house tells the story of how it killed mine, Lord Yukari, and tells it well. Keep the crown. I'll take Sum-gol with my own hands. And when your father's seam starts leaking the dead into your cedars at night, send a crow to my post, and I'll come. That is what my house is for."

## The Order of the Spiritual Reality

- **The Post is its face.** The Sum-gol Post carries every letter between the north and the Yukari valleys, and nobody has asked who pays the couriers. Cozbi does, and he reads what passes. The living get a messenger service; the dead get Keepers.
- **Passage is its trade.** Exorcism, the laying of hauntings, clearing Well-creatures where the dead have soured a site, and escorting a soul home: every one priced, recorded and done in olive and white, until the south sends for the Mahuo the way it sends for a physician.
- **The registered law is its proof.** At Principality the Continuum itself entered his law. He means to make the Continuum's word the south's.
- **Its own name** in Sum-gol's tongue: *Honryeong*, "the soul-order."

## Voice

He is patient as something that decided time is not a resource it needs to conserve, and he is never quiet about what he wants. He hosts lavishly, laughs loud and at the right moment, explains the dead at length and with real pride, and gives orders as invitations. Banter comes easily; mercy rarely.

**Fingerprint.** His dead in the present tense ("my mother is fond of this hall"). Once he has decided a living person will die, he speaks of them in the past tense. The table can learn to hear it.

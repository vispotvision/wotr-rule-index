## Traits

**THE RED BEFORE THE WHITE (Inherited Trait)**
The Byakuya oath, said over her as a girl in the white house: purity before power, peace before the blade, and the red line before the white, until the end of the red line. She kept it over the lamp every night for forty years in Thornhaven with the doors shut, and spoke it aloud in the Hall of Verdicts beside Kiyome, the old woman and the not-so-old one, so softly only the front of the Hall could hear. Near the Kōkan blood her Anchoring holds and nothing frightens her. Its limit is the oath's own: it guards the king, and has nothing to say when the king is the knife. Its counter is to make him one.

**SMILE AND PAY (Primary Trait)**
She sat in Thornhaven under Tenrai taxes and Tenrai factors and Tenrai silver for forty years, and smiled, and paid, and the Tenrai counted Thornwall bought. Her soul learned to hold everything behind a small dry smile: while she keeps it, no reading of her face or her aura gives up what she means, and her Composure reads a Grade high. It costs nothing she would mention. Its limit is that it is a waiting, and a waiting does nothing on its own; it needs a crow to come to Thornhaven. Its counter is nothing the Tenrai found.

**THE BACK DOOR (Secondary Trait)**
Formed the night she married into the house, when her husband's grandfather told her why the Thornwall foresters kept the corridors cut. She reads ground for the way nobody uses: the long cuts through the Thornwood, walled in by trees so old you cannot see the sky, and the stair behind the frozen fall. It costs nothing. Its limit is that a back door works once; after the Seat, everybody knows. Its counter is a man who already knows where the corridors run.

## Signature

**THE THORNWALL PIKE (House Art, Thornwall)** · mark: Mastered
The Thornwall pikes come on at a walk, in step, to the slow beat of a drum, with the thorn-horn sounding behind them every hundred paces, and set a hedge of ash and iron that horse will not go into. She brought them down out of the north onto the Keld flank at the Skeldmere, and sent twelve hundred of them up the corridors thirty at a time to come over the Seat's east wall before the Tenrai knew there was a hole in it. It costs the house its pikemen and her nothing she would call a working; her Command keeps the step and her Forecast picks the ground. Its limit is the hedge: it holds ground and takes walls, and it is slow. Its counter is horse on open ground before the hedge is set.

## Physical Description

She is tall and slender and carries herself very straight, and she looks a good twenty-five years younger than she is; the Byakuya keep their years off their faces, and the north, which knows her age, still calls her the old woman. Her hair is the Byakuya gold, pale and heavy, worn loose and long with a fine braid drawn back from each temple to the crown, and the rest of it plaited into one great rope that falls past her knees and is bound at the end with a cord. Her face is long and fine-boned and calm, her eyes the Byakuya grey under heavy lids that she keeps half shut when she listens, her mouth painted dark red. Long silver earrings hang from her ears, worked like falling icicles. At Thornhaven she wears a black gown with long hanging sleeves lined in oxblood, and a fitted bodice of deep red embroidered in gold; in the field, Thornwall grey felt over it.

## Lore

She was born a Byakuya in the white house in the Shiraki hills, a cousin of the mother of Kasane and Kiyome, and married north forty years ago into the Thornwall line to seal a peace nobody now remembers. She sat in Thornhaven under Tenrai taxes and Tenrai factors and Tenrai silver, and smiled, and paid, and kept the oath of her house over the lamp every night with the doors shut, and in forty years in the north nobody ever called her anything but the Lady. Muken's price for Thornwall was the house free of the Ore Council. When Kiyome's crow came to Thornhaven she called out her pikes the next morning, and rode down onto the Keld flank at the Skeldmere in Thornwall grey beside Kiyome Byakuya, and her pike column stood in the snow and took off their iron caps when the grey sail came up the Marrow. In the Seat council she told them about the corridors her foresters had kept cut for two hundred years, and the stair behind the frozen fall, and Thornwall pikes came over the east wall. At the ratification she swore for Thornhaven and the Thornwood and the corridors, and turned to Kiyome, and the two of them said the end of the oath together.

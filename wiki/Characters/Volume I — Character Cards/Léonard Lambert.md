## Traits

**ONE BEFORE (Inherited Trait)**
The thing in the Lambert blood the house does not discuss. A great many Lamberts are born with Crystals that never wake and fight anyway, reading a blow before it is thrown with no faculty to read it with, so that a spectator calls it luck and a practitioner calls it impossible. Léonard woke, and the old gift woke with him: every answer he gives begins before the delivery it answers, by a margin so slight it looks like chance. While he is facing a blow with a will behind it, his Reflex reads a Grade high. It costs nothing. Its limit is that it reads intent; it is slower against a thing with no will in it, a falling wall, a beast in a panic, a working that runs by itself. Its counter is a blow with no intent behind it.

**COUNT TRUE (Primary Trait)**
The house words, become his soul's law. He knows the number of any field he can see, men and horses and remounts and the days of grain behind them, and when it matters he says it out loud, in front of everyone, and does not soften it. While he is counting, no lie told about the field gets past him; his Analysis and Forecast read a Grade high and a false return feels wrong in his hand like a cased weight. It costs nothing. Its limit is sight: he counts what he can see and what he is told, and a field he cannot see or a report never made is a hole in the sum. Its counter is a field he cannot see.

**THE STRUCK LIST (Secondary Trait)**
A Lambert who gives a false count is struck from the house, and the house keeps the list. Léonard has struck four names in his time, and one of them was his cousin, whom he loved, and who lied about two hundred sacks of grain to keep a village fed through a bad winter. It formed the night he did it. Since then, when a man reports to him, he asks for the count twice, and the second asking carries the weight of the list; a man who lies to him the second time knows exactly what he is doing. Its limit is that it judges the number and not the reason. Its counter is a man who gives him a true count.

## Signature

**THE SALLOWGATE ANSWER (House Art, Lambert)** · mark: Mastered
The Lambert counter-cut, taught at Sallowgate to every child of the house, woken or not: the blade turns in his hand before yours has left its line, takes it on the flat a hand from your guard, and rides down it into the wrist, the elbow or the throat, in one movement and with no flourish. In him, with ONE BEFORE and Glory's Reflex behind it, the answer arrives so early that men he has fought swear he struck first. It costs about one and a half per cent of his reserve each time, near 6,900 EU, and it is B-Grade on the edge. Its limit is that it is an answer; he has to be asked first, and a man who will not attack him gives him nothing to answer. Its counter is to feint with the whole body, so that the intent he reads is false, and then strike with no thought at all.

## Physical Description

He is tall and well made, broad in the chest and narrow in the waist, and carries his armour the way other men carry a coat. His hair is a bright gold, thick and tightly curled, worn loose to the collar and always a little wild; his face is open and square-jawed, fair-skinned and windburned at the cheekbones, with a short gold stubble along the jaw and a straight nose, and his eyes are a clear light green that look past the man in front of him to count the men behind. In the field he wears a full harness of gilded plate, the breastplate worked in high relief with a rearing bear, the Sallowgate bear of the warlord's office, round bosses at the shoulders, a high gorget and lames down the arms scaled like a fish's back; over it a long cloak of deep scarlet, and above him on a white field the house banner with the balance and the words, *Count True*, in black. When he breathes in the cold it smokes off him like a horse.

## Lore

The Lamberts have held Sallowgate and the land marches for the Palatines since before the current line of Palatines could count, and Léonard came to the warlord's seat at twenty-nine, when his father died in the war on the Grey Plain with the count of his own dead still in his mouth. He is the first Warlord of the Lamberts in four generations to wake; his father and his grandfather fought unwoken, with the house's gift and nothing else, and were feared for it. He has told three generals their numbers to their faces before battle, and two of them listened. He struck his cousin from the house for two hundred sacks, and paid for the village's grain himself, out of his own purse, the same week, and has never told anyone which of those two things he is ashamed of. The Holy Sea's Wellspring weighs testimony; the Lamberts count what the testimony is about, and Léonard has a standing grievance with the Palatine that nobody north of the Moto border will read a petition from the Sea. Now the north has a tokenless king, and the Lamberts' cousins at Hallenfeld write that he listens to a count. Léonard has not yet decided whether to ride north. He is counting.

## VIII. Traits

**TWIN-BLOOD HARMONY (Primary Trait)**
Two pendulum clocks hung on one beam will, over a night, fall into the same swing, each nudging the other through the wood until they tick as one; the old clockmakers found it and could not stop it. His bloods did that. The Seikai current, precise and still, and the Zettari body, dense and violent, screamed at each other through his childhood, and then over years, through the beam of his own Crystal, they began to answer: the precision sharpens the body, the body grounds the precision, and the two swing as one. It is constant and it is his greatest strength: no wasted motion, a defensive structure that is almost unnervingly complete. It costs the labour of keeping the treaty, every day, the Equilibrium Tax on every great working. Its limit and its counter are the same thing: two clocks kept in step by a shared beam can be knocked out of it by a strong enough outside rhythm. Anger, grief, a forced emotional beat, a technique that makes his bloods feel their old quarrel, and they slip out of step; he does not grow weaker at once, he grows more dangerous in the wrong directions, the Seikai side thinning into bloodless abstraction or the Zettari side hardening into tyrant's force.

**FOUNDER'S CLAIM (Secondary Trait)**
He was the first to say the name Yukari, on the Night Beneath Stormlight, and his soul kept the arrogance of first naming. Fixatio cures what it binds the way a thermoset cures: once the bridges form, the other shape is no longer a place it can go back to. Near him, declarations set. Oaths taken in his presence bind more tightly, betrayals leave deeper scars, and a man who brings him a grievance hears himself state it more plainly than he meant to. Reality listens a little harder when he decides what something is. It costs him nearness: people do not stand near him as near an ordinary man, but as near the beginning of something. Its limit is Fixatio's own failure: a binding set in error is set for good, and he has made some. Its counter is silence. Say nothing in his presence that you are not prepared to have cured into you.

**HALF-ELVEN CONTINUANCE (Inherited Trait)**
The elven share lengthens the line of him and the life in it: extended vitality, refined sensory filtering, a strange resistance to spiritual decay. It makes him hard to rush toward ruin. It costs him the long view; he outlives the people who stood near him, and knows it. Its limit is that it slows ruin rather than stopping it. Its counter is to be quick: hit him in the first moments, before he has settled into the slow, certain pace that is his best ground.

## XIII. Physical Description

He is tall and long-limbed, composed, with the refined stillness of someone who learned early that posture is a language; the elven blood lengthens the line of him and the Zettari blood fills it with a density nobody expects until they try to move him. His hair is long and black, heavy as stormwater, a few strands falling across his brow and face, the rest drawn back loose and tied low at the nape in a tail that hangs down his back. His face is beautiful in a way that would be called severe if it were not so tired: long and fine-boned, a straight nose, a narrow chin, the elven point at the top of the ears just showing through the hair, and eyes of a pale grey-green under lids that are almost always half lowered. People remember the feeling of being seen more than the eyes themselves. Down the left side of his throat, from under the jaw to the collarbone, run two characters in the old court script, inked dark, and below them a smaller mark half hidden by the collar. He dresses in the Black Founder's Coat over a dark under-robe: the coat is a long loose mantle of undyed white, high-collared and wide-sleeved, its broad lapels and inner facings black, worked inside with reinforcement sigils and old family motifs that no longer belong to either house. He is usually smoking. A thin roll of smoke-leaf rests between two long fingers, and he talks round it, and the smoke goes up slow and straight from it even when the wind is blowing.

## Lore

### Origin

Naiser Yukari was born in the First Eon, half-elven, of Seikai blood and Zettari blood, before any house bore the name he would later speak. His own words for it: "I was born from two houses. I bent neither knee long enough to die inside either one." The Seikai side gave him discipline, austerity and a martial clarity sharpened by order; the Zettari side gave him a body far denser than its elegance showed, and an ancestral pressure that pushed from inside. Both houses called his birth a disruption. He was passed between them as a political symbol, and he learned early that posture is a language: in either house he stood the same way, and nobody watching could tell from his shoulders which blood was speaking. Inside him, the two inheritances screamed at each other, and the crisis came near to unmaking him.

### The Making

Each house held a doctrine ready for him and wanted him absorbed into it whole. He refused both. He took up the burdens of the two, accepted neither in full, and built a third discipline in the space they left, slowly, letting the Seikai precision sharpen the Zettari body and the body ground the precision until the two bloods answered each other. The refusal had consequences, and he survived them: the Severance of Inheritance, the first split that could not be undone. Then came the naming. It is said the Yukari surname was declared, spoken into the world as an act of self-authorship during a night of spiritual violence and ancestral refusal, the Naming Beneath Stormlight. The Yukari Signet carries the name he spoke.

### The Cost

The harmony is paid for every day. Every great working draws on the labour of keeping the two bloods agreed; a long fight turns his advantage into upkeep; an enemy who cares nothing for form can drag him onto ground where elegance buys nothing. He cannot afford to be shaken. The people near him pay as well. Oaths sworn in his presence bind tighter, betrayals near him scar deeper, and few come closer than the edge of the room.

The split he made drew an answer. Among the early powers an oath was sworn in blood against the new branch, a purge binding the swearers' descendants to the killing, generation after generation. Naevra, a daughter of the line's first generation, read the oath's thread before it was carried out and stopped it.

### Where He Stands

This season he stands at the head of a line that has a name and almost nothing else: a signet, a coat, a narrow sword he seldom draws, and a third doctrine he made out of two that would not have him. He smokes at the rail of a borrowed veranda and watches the hills, and space round him sometimes feels like a corridor between ancestral halls, judged by both and owned by neither.

### The Other Naiser

Niran Yukari's card makes Niran "Naiser's Heir" and records his father as dead, in chains in a stone room below daylight, with his son first in his thoughts. That Naiser had a mother, brothers and a Telling of his own. Where the Veil took Niran, a figure the passage names Naiser stood in the dark. The founder's card names no son. No record says whether the two are one man.

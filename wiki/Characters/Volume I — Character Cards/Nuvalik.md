### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card, the one archived scene it appears in, and the record of Malphas's cell. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Nuvalik was born in the far north and given a name that had belonged to three people before her. After the last of them died, the name went unspoken until there was a child to carry it. The child was her.
Her household lived on what it killed, and she learned the knife on its meat. She learned the joints first. Then she learned the sinew, and the net under the hide that holds an animal in the shape of an animal. She cut by whatever light the house had and did not ask for more.
Where she was raised, a dead thing left on open ground did not lie long. The birds came down to it.

#### The Making

She came south, to towns that ran on the mains, and cut carcasses for wages. Her knife went where a body parted. Afterward she could say what had held it together and what had let go.
When Malphas's cell was proving its rot synthesis at the Greyshaft Nine coldhouse, she was the one who opened what the ferment had finished. The first beast came to the coldhouse table and she opened it. The connective net was gone. The cells were whole, and there was no smell at all. Ilmar Foss put a coil over the residue, and it read clean. Nuvalik said at the bench that a carcass with no smell draws no bird down to it, and that a carcass no bird will touch stays where it fell and gets counted. Before the night of the report she said it a second time. Both times she named it a fault in the work.
Nine hinds went up on the rail. She worked the fourth of them with her sleeves pushed past the elbow and her apron on. She got contraction out of its shoulder twice. She did not report it.
On the night Vesk carried the flask up the rail, she had been at the fourth hind since the tenth hour. Malphas said her name and asked for the bodies, and she wiped the blade on her apron before she gave them. "The matrix is gone and the meat is not," she said. The ferment had eaten the scaffold and left the cells, so each hind stood structurally liquid and cellularly whole, held up by the hide. Nothing in them made a smell. She called that a fault, and she said she had said so twice.

#### The Cost

Malphas told her to say it a third time. "A carcass no bird will touch is a carcass that stays where it fell." Four ravens had sat over the Ferriby cut for a full morning, she told the room, and not one came down. Then she gave them the proverb she was raised on: "Where I was raised, the sky finishes what the ground starts, and when the sky refuses, people walk out to see why." She went back to the hind's shoulder and opened it in one draw. At the rail she thought that she had spent all three names for this body on this work.
Malphas heard her fault and Xu Deming's ledger as one objection. The work had an edge. The next work would remove the border by making the dose walk. Nuvalik straightened with the knife still in her hand. "You want them to get up." He said he wanted them to leave. The new matrix was contractile, he told the room, and she had already got contraction out of the fourth hind's shoulder twice and had not thought it worth mentioning. He would discuss it with her separately. He named the work Project Zombification, and then he ordered the ninth hind, the freshest, onto the table by the second division.
She stood at the tail of the rail with the knife hanging and the fourth hind open to the shoulder behind her. She stood long enough that Vesk began to count the drops in the corner. Then she went and got the hook.
In the coldhouse she asked for nothing. She did not ask for the lamp, and she explained her name to Vesk once and never again. The fault was the one thing she would not leave unsaid, and she put it each time in the cell's own terms, as a thing that would be found and counted. Once she put the place that raised her into it.

#### Where They Stand

Nuvalik cuts for Malphas's cell at the Greyshaft Nine coldhouse, and she has gone for the hook to bring the ninth hind to the table. The fourth hind is open to the shoulder. The dose made to walk is to run on a contractile matrix, and she had drawn contraction from that shoulder before the work had a name. Malphas has said he will discuss it with her separately.
A forester named Nol Tally counted the Ferriby carcasses and wrote them down. Malphas has his name. She carries a name three people carried before her, and by her own count she has spent all three names for this body on this work.

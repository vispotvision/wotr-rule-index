## Traits

**STARK RIME (Inherited Trait)**
The cold in the Stark blood, plain and strong in him: frost in his beard when he is angry, heat going down through his heels into the ground. Its counter is heat, and calm.

**HE'S ALL OF OURS (Primary Trait)**
His soul stood guard before it did anything else. Two winters in the Lanternless he stood where the Sarge could not see behind himself, and fetched him for Ulf, and locked the door and stood outside it, and when Ulf asked if the boy was one of his, Rob said he was all of theirs. While someone he has claimed is in reach, his Fortitude and Reflex read a Grade high and his body is between them and the blow before he has thought about it. Its limit is reach: he can guard only what he can step across. Its counter is to get past whoever he is guarding first.

**DIDN'T SCREAM (Secondary Trait)**
The cub bit him above the knee on the Vessant road and he did not scream; Yukazuri opened the rotten bite on a wagon tailboard with spirit and a knife, and he bit a strap and did not make a sound. At the Foot, when she closed the cut on his leg, he screamed at her and cursed her and then slept, and he never forgave himself the scream. Pain reaches him late; he chooses when to show it. It costs nothing. Its limit is that it holds the voice, not the body. He kept it to the end.

## Physical Description

He is big and rawboned, the kind of big his father is, and going to be broader. His hair is dark and long, pulled back and tied at the nape, with loose strands at the temples; his beard is dark and short and full; his face is long and weathered, with a straight heavy brow, a hard mouth, and dark eyes that look a long way off, like a closed door that might open. He wears plate over his shoulders and arms and a dark surcoat over mail, the long skirts worked with the white wolf of the Starks, a broad belt with a long sword and a dagger in a carved sheath, gauntlets, and a heavy fur mantle over a black cloak; he carries a long northern great sword over his shoulder the way other men carry a spade. A great white wolf walks at his back, red-eyed, the size of a pony.

## Lore

He came south from Hollenmark two winters back with his brother Mark, for reasons Ulf never told anyone, and joined the Lanternless in Halcrest, and was Ulf's man inside the company. He said almost nothing to anyone for two winters and stood where the Sarge could not see behind himself. On the Vessant road the cub took him above the knee and he killed it with Mark, and did not scream. At the river post he fetched the Sarge for his father, and stood outside the door. He followed the Tokenless to the Foot, and stood at the ladder, and when the Foot's yard knelt by the woodpile he was among the first. At the Skeldmere he bellowed *we know!* across the ice at Karogane before Karogane could shout his secret. He worked the bellows for Tsugigane in shifts with Tetsugen. When the Seat fell he was in the Hall square, and he stepped across the boy with the red ears who could not hold his spear, and stood there, and was killed. He looked up at his brother Lorn and at Mark, and said to tell the little surgeon he didn't scream this time. She was already under the cairn. Lorn knelt in the square with his brother's head in his lap. Ninety-one others fell at the Seat that day.

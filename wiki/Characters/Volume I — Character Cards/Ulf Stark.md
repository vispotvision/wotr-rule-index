## Traits

**STARK RIME (Inherited Trait)**
The cold in the Stark blood, at Glory's clarity: heat leaves everything near him, timber silvers, snow stiffens and squeaks, his breath stops steaming, and the fur of his wolf-hood goes white; the heat goes down through his heels into the ground, which is why the Starks walk the way they walk. Harder under feeling. Its limit is that it moves heat and cannot make it. Its counter is heat, and calm.

**THE MAKING OF A GENERAL (Primary Trait)**
He has seen two men in his life with what the boy has, and one of them is dead and the other is sitting in this chair. His soul reads a man the way it reads ground, for what he will carry and how far, and it is very seldom wrong; he picked the road for the Tokenless, every narrow place in the timber, and watched him come out of it. It costs nothing. Its limit is that it reads what a man can carry, not what he will choose. Its counter is a man who shows him nothing; its tell is the long look, and then the line.

**WE GIVE (Secondary Trait)**
Formed the day Torvald's head came back with a note. He gives: his brother, his riders, his granaries, his house, his neck. While he is giving, fear does not reach him and his hands do not shake; he rode south to the Kurenai blade at an easy walk. It costs everything and he does not count it. Its limit is that it is all one way; nobody promises the Starks anything back, not the Sky, not the lines, not the people they give it for. Its counter would be something he would not give, and nobody has found it.

## Signature

**THE WINTER ROAD (House Art, Stark)** · mark: Mastered
Light columns carrying their food on their backs and in caches along scouted routes, moving through the winter while the other side shelters, and arriving from somewhere nobody was watching; the Starks held the passes against the Zettari for forty years this way, and Old Harald Keld is the only man who ever did it better. It costs the riders, not the lord, and the Stark rime makes the cold an ally. Its limit is the thaw. Its counter is a fire, a thaw, and a scout who does not sleep.

## Physical Description

He is big, the kind of big Rob is going to be in twenty years, broad as a doorway, the arms gone heavier with age and the body under the plate heavier still. His hair is dark and worn to the shoulders, and his beard is dark and full and close-cut, with grey coming into both; under the hair the scalp is scarred white in three places, and the small and ring fingers of his left hand were cut away clean at the knuckle a long time ago, which he hides inside a black glove. His face is broad and weathered, with a heavy brow and pale northern eyes, very much awake. He wears the head and pelt of a great brown wolf as a hood and mantle, the jaws over his brow and the fur falling to his elbows, pinned with a round silver Stark badge on a chain; under it, a bright breastplate stamped with the wolf of Hollenmark and a heavy fluted pauldron over the right shoulder, on a black gambeson quilted in diamonds; a black cloak. He stands with a long-hilted great sword point down in front of him and both hands on the cross, the way a mountain sits on a plain. His bearded war-axe rides on his son's back now, and his mail, too big for both of the boys who have worn it, and his grey wolfhound lies across the Tokenless's boots.

## Lore

He fought his first winter with a sword the year his grandfather's last mistake was born, and that mistake was Yusif, the best of all of them. For forty years the Starks held the passes against the Zettari beside the Greymanes, and buried their sons in it, and for two summers when he was young and stupid enough to think the man would save them, Ulf rode with Gimbzo. He picked the road for the Tokenless and sent Hollis Draycott to hire him for it, and when the boy came out of it with nine wagons of eleven and a cheek opened jaw to eye, Ulf sat in the factor's back room by the fire and said: ah, so there he is. He gave him a dead Stark token, and his word, and a lieutenancy at the Foot. He wanted to tell him everything; Wystan Greymane said that if they gave the boy all the answers they would take the war away from him, and Ulf agreed and hated it. When Kagenori's summons came in Kagenori's hand under Kagenori's own seal, he called it a door held open, and stood in the gatehouse before witnesses and before the Sky and swore all the power of the house of Stark to Muken the Tokenless, to hold for the north until he saw fit to put it down. That night he took Lorn onto the wall-walk and told him everything in twelve sentences. In the morning he and Wystan rode down the Kessen road, and the boy said they could not go like cattle on the Tenrai's script, and Ulf said that was just the way this life is, lad, and that they would have liked to see where he went and what he became, but they would not be. Sumie Kurenai carried the blade. His wolfhound howled in the gatehouse for an hour after he was gone, and then went and lay down on the boy's boot.

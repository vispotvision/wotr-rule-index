## VIII. Traits

**TORPOR · Snaal (Primary Trait)**
His body and his Crystal idle at a floor most creatures reach only in hibernation, the way a dormouse in winter drops its heart to a few beats a minute and burns almost nothing. Everything in him runs slow and cheap: poisons and venoms work through him at a crawl, bleeding is sluggish because there is so little pressure behind it, his reserve barely moves when he is not using it, and when he sleeps under Hypnather he recovers three times as fast as he would awake. The cost is that he wakes slowly. A torpid animal is the easiest kill in the forest for anything quick, and he is the same: from a cold start his reactions lag, his thinking is foggy for a few breaths, and Celerity 10 means he could not run if he wanted to. Its counter is speed and surprise. Hit him early and hard, before he has yawned, and the whole sheet is a slow, soft goblin in a big coat.

**DOK · Striking-At (Secondary Trait)**
In Vaross a doubled consonant marks a finished act: dok is to strike at, dokk is to have struck. Vaashuuk lives in the undoubled form. When the bellows-gang died he had done nothing, and the Plane of Fate wrote that down as exactly what it was. Since then nothing he does ever closes: his acts stay open in the Attraction Layer, so a soul-reading finds no killing on him because he has never completed one, an oath-binding finds no oath to grip because Oath 1 holds nothing, a grudge has nothing banked against him because he has never landed a blow that finished, and any working that needs a completed act to anchor to slides off him like rain off oiled cloth. The cost is total: he cannot finish anything either. He cannot complete a kill even when he wants to; he cannot keep a promise, finish a task or hold a love to its end, and every job he is given has to be the kind that someone else finishes. Its counter is to make him finish something. Corner him with no way out but to strike to completion, or bind him to a deal he must close to survive, and the moment the act closes the Trait goes thin and every reading, oath and grudge that slid off him sticks at once.

**DROWSE · Heavy Air (Passive Trait)**
Hypnather leaks off him the way heat leaks off a stove. Within about ten yards of him people yawn, eyelids grow heavy, attention drifts and reactions slow by a hair; flames lean low and burn smaller, dust settles faster, a stirred cup stops swirling sooner, and an untrained guard on a long watch near him will be asleep inside the hour. It costs him nothing and he cannot switch it off, which makes him terrible company at parties and useless as a sentry. Its counter is anything that wakes the body: noise, pain, cold water, a stimulant, a practitioner whose Ignivale or whose plain fury keeps the fire lit. Shōki stood in it without yawning once.

## IX. Domain

**THE LIE-IN · Thaal-Snorr (Domain)**
Every process that starts needs a push to get over its barrier, and the hotter a system is the more of its pieces have that push; inside his Domain the barrier rises. The Second Release opens it in one long yawn out to a hundred and fifty yards, and inside it the energy needed to begin anything climbs: a fresh working costs three times its price to light, a fire already burning sinks to embers and a new one will not catch, a nerve fires late, and a blade swung from stillness starts as if through mud. What is already moving keeps moving, slowly. It holds 3 minutes 20 seconds from a full reserve, about 972,000 EU a second. Its cost is that it charges the same rise to him: he cannot start anything quickly in it either, and he never does. It is beaten the way any raised barrier is beaten, by heat: the barrier is fixed and the push grows with temperature, so a fighter who runs hot enough lights anyway, and a Domain held against a furnace burns through his reserve fast. A stronger Sovereignty contests it from the edge, and bodies can simply walk out of it, slowly.

## X. Techniques

**TAR TIME (Signature Technique)** · mark: Mastered · page: TAR TIME
He yawns, enormously, and the air around him starts toward the glass transition: the point where a liquid slows so far it stops flowing for any purpose that matters, like the pitch in the philosophers' funnel at Stannvaard that drops once every ten winters. Inside a sphere forty yards across, out to a hundred and twenty when he bothers, everything that moves through space slows: air, steam, fire's spread, a thrown weapon, the salts that carry a signal down a nerve, a working's travel. The thickened medium is shear-thickening as well, like cornflour stirred in water, so a slow push passes through almost freely while a fast one is fought harder the harder it comes; the faster the blow, the thicker the world it meets. It costs 0.3% of his reserve a second, about 583,000 EU, and holds 5 minutes 33 seconds from full. He moves through it at his ordinary amble because Abython equalises him to it; he carries no differential against the medium, so it does not drag on him. Its limit is the glass itself: it slows motion through space, never the shaking of the smallest pieces of a thing where they stand, so heat is untouched and keeps building, and glass softens as it heats. Before: the yawn is a long, loud, obvious tell, and anyone out of the forty yards when it ends is outside the field. During: move slowly, which the field barely resists, or run hot, because temperature thins it fast. After: when he drops it, everything held in it comes loose at once, and he is standing in the middle of it.

**THE SNOOZE-GLASS DROP (Signature Technique)** · mark: Drilled · page: TAR TIME
The one way he has found to skip the yawn. Overnight, while he sleeps, he lets a full TAR TIME's worth of glassed air settle into the brass-banded glass bubble that floats at his shoulder, 4% of his reserve, about 7,777,000 EU, held ready. He drops the bubble and the field opens around him with no yawn and no warning. It is one charge; after it he is back to yawning, and the bubble needs a full night's sleep to refill. The tell is the bubble dimming as it falls. Count it: if it has dropped once today, it will not drop again.

**THE CHAISE (Technique)** · mark: Mastered
He sets a slab of air under himself into glass and lies on it, held at neutral buoyancy by Abython, and drifts wherever he leans. It costs so little it barely counts, a fraction of a percent of his reserve an hour, and he spends most of his life on it. It holds his weight and nothing much more; any real heat makes it sag and drip like toffee left in the sun.

## XIII. Physical Description

He is small even for a goblin, about half a grown man's height, wiry and slight, long-limbed and big-footed, and he carries himself in a permanent slouch with his weight dropped on one hip. His skin is a mossy green mottled with dark brown and black, the dark running down from his eyes over his cheeks in long streaks like old tear-tracks and gathering in patches at his brow, his jaw and his neck, and going to polished black obsidian on the knuckles and up the backs of the fingers; the fingers end in long black claws he keeps lacquered and filed to neat points and buffs on his collar whenever he is talking. His ears are long and pointed and stick out sideways through the hood, and he has pierced every inch of them he could reach: gold rings, silver hoops, bone studs and thin iron spikes driven clean through the cartilage. Two short black ridged horns curl back like a ram's from just above his hairline, hidden under the hood unless he pushes it back. His hair is black and coarse and lovingly oiled, swept up and over to one side in a spiky crest that falls across his brow, and he touches it every few seconds to make sure it is still there. His eyes are a bright bottle green under heavy black brows, almost always half shut, with two silver rings through the left brow and a small gold stud set in the bridge of his nose; his mouth is wide and thin-lipped, two fangs showing over the lower lip, one of them gold-capped, and an iron spike through the lip below. He wears a deep hood of olive wool over a long dark-olive cloak with a high stiff collar, a black shirt buttoned to the throat and a pale green silk tie knotted loose beneath it, a buckled collar of black leather round his neck, a string of yellowed fangs, and on a cord over the tie a little carved rat skull in white bone, which he will tell you, if you ask, is the only honest face in the Expanse. Under the cloak, slim charcoal trousers and chunky black platform boots heavy with buckles and rivets, which add three inches he will not discuss.

## Lore

### Origin

Vaashuuk was born in the deep slag-yards under Gorr-Keth, to a goblin bellows-gang that worked the great forges in turns that never ended. At six he was on a bellows-handle. At twelve he could tell from the note of a roof-timber how long it had left, a skill everyone on the gang had and nobody spoke about.

He was the gang's beauty and the gang's joke. He was always tired, and he said so, and he always would have done the extra turn tomorrow.

### The Making

He heard the forge-roof going a full day before it fell. The note was wrong in the big timber over the third bellows, and getting worse.

Telling the gang-master would have meant a fight, a stoppage, a docking, an argument about whose turn it was to go up and look. It was complicated. He was tired. He went and lay down in the coal-store for a nap.

The roof came down in the night with the whole gang under it. He woke up in the coal-store, alive, with nobody to blame him, because he had done nothing at all.

He walked out of the yard and kept walking until he lay down again, on warm ground under the old slag heaps, on top of something he could not feel the bottom of. It was a Hypnather Core. He slept on it for a year. When he woke he was the shape he is now, the horns, the claws, the eyes, and he was never tired again, and he was never awake again either, not really.

The Holy Sea's tribunal, had it ever heard of him, would have filed him as the Corruption of Non-Commitment: innocence through distance, "it is complicated", the preventable catastrophe. He has never been filed. That is the point.

### The Cost

He cannot finish anything. He has never killed anyone, and he will tell you so, and it is true, and it is the worst thing about him. Every job he takes, someone else finishes. Every person who has loved him found out that he would not stay to the end of it. Every apology he has started trails off.

He wears beautiful clothes he never paid for and wears a gold stud in the bridge of his nose where another goblin would have a scar, because he has no scars. Nothing has ever reached him at speed.

### Where He Stands

He runs errands for the Captain of the Sins and complains about every one. His last was the long walk down into the Pit to fetch the Wrath, who was waking, and to bring him to the Captain alive, because, the Captain said, he was the only one of the Sins the Wrath could not hit.

The Captain was right about the hitting and wrong about the rest. The Wrath turned out to be smart. When Vaashuuk last stood on his own sagging glass in the bottom of the Pit, with his coat sleeve on fire and his hair in danger, he was yawning for the second time.

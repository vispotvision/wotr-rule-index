## Traits

**KEEPERS OF THE WALL (Primary Trait)**
The Greymane soul stands on a wall looking west for something that might come, and his did that before it did anything else. While he holds a line with someone behind it, his Anchoring and Fortitude read a Grade high and nothing that reaches him moves him back. It costs nothing. Its limit is that it faces one way; it is a wall, and a wall guards what is behind it and nothing else. Its counter is to come at what he isn't guarding.

**STILL, THEN FAST (Secondary Trait)**
The Ridgewall stillness: he has a way of not moving at all and then moving very fast. While he is still, his Reflex winds up behind it, and the first thing he does out of stillness comes a Grade quicker than his card. It costs nothing. Its limit is that it is one move; after the first, he is only an old man of sixty-one with a sword. Its counter is never to be the first to move.

**LATER, ULF (Secondary Trait)**
He saw the gold seam in the boy's eye from a hand's width away in the yard at the Foot, and his face set like ground taking the frost, and he said *later*. He keeps a silence the way he keeps a wall: nothing gets it out of him while he holds it. Its limit is that a silence cannot defend itself except by staying silent. Its counter is to ask him in front of the one he is keeping it from; he will walk to the window instead.

## Signature

**SETTING THE STONE (First Release, Greymane)** · mark: Mastered
The Greymane release, called aloud as STONEFAST: he sinks his heels, and his Fixatio locks every joint from the shield-boss down to the heel into one column, so that a blow's weight goes down through him into the ground and the ground does not give. Dust and snow settle round his feet, and the old sun on his shield seems to take the light. It costs about four per cent of his reserve to set, near 21,300 EU, and holds as long as he does not move his feet. Its limit is the feet: set, he cannot step. Its counter is to pull him, lift him, or fight him on ice.

## Physical Description

He is long, all of him, long-legged and long-handed, with the Greymane stride and the Ridgewall stillness, and he looks a good fifteen years younger than he is. His hair is dark and thick, cropped short and pushed up off his brow, and his beard is dark and cut close to the jaw; his face is long and weathered, the nose straight, the eyes deep-set and heavy-lidded and grey, and he keeps them down more than up. He wears an old surcoat of green worked in gold over a coat of mail, a fur mantle on his shoulders, plate at the shoulders and elbows and knees and greaves to the boot, and a long grey cloak; on his breast, worked in darker thread, the wolf's head of his house. He carries the old round Greymane shield, steel faced and green-rimmed, with a gold sun rising on it half worn off by sixty years of blows, and a sword at his hip and a hunting horn at his belt.

## Lore

He was the Lord of the Ridgewall and the Shieldwarden, and the Greymanes held the passes against the Zettari for forty years beside the Starks and buried their sons in it. His brother Aldwin came home to him in a salt-cart with a ransom tag on his collar, and Wystan is not a man who forgets that sort of thing, so when Kagenori Tenrai Moto closed the Kessen road under martial law, it was Ulf Stark and Wystan Greymane against him and nobody else. At the Foot his men had the ram and his horn brought the Greymanes in through the gatehouse behind the Tenrai, and afterwards he knelt by the boy in the snow and looked at his left eye, and said *later*. By the brazier that night Ulf said the boy was Gimbzo's son and that hiding it felt like theft, and Wystan said he was soft on him, and that the boy must learn it himself, the hard way, with his own hands; then he smiled for the first time and said he had not thought to live to see the rise of another great warlord of the north. He told his son the same by the woodpile, and swore him on the ridge. When Kagenori's summons came under Kagenori's own seal he stood at the gatehouse window looking down the long road south, and told Godfrey that the Greymanes are the keepers of the wall, and that when he was gone it would be his, and swore all the power of his house to Muken the Tokenless. *You'll have our sons.* He rode down the Kessen road with Ulf on two grey horses with no escort and no banners, and knelt beside him in the square outside the Hall of Verdicts, and Sumie Kurenai carried the blade.

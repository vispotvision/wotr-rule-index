## VIII. Traits

**SOLAR INVOCATION (Primary Trait)**

**Summons concentrated daylight plasma within a wide radius** — burns Essence constructs, blinds void-aligned entities. His Shell holds a small part of his daylight plasma together before he shapes the wider discharge; that standing readiness is the innate part of the Trait. A full pulse costs 2% of his full reserve. Pale orange light gathers between his teeth and under the Crown, with a sharp furnace roar. The plasma must cross the intervening space and loses heat into what it strikes. Opaque cover backed by a heat ward takes the glare and heat before they reach the body; breaking his concentration before the pulse forms spoils it. Rapid pulses deny Exuroth its recovery interval and leave the Shell brittle.

**FLAME DOMINION (Secondary Trait)**

**Manipulates ambient temperature and luminance**, igniting air into Aether plasma for a sustained window. His Shell feeds heat into the air and holds the hot region in a chosen shape. Maintaining it alone spends 0.025% of his full reserve each second, reaching the tenth-reserve cutoff in 60 minutes from full. The air bends above his shoulders, and the Mantle gives a steady hiss. Fresh cold air, moving water and heat-shedding wards carry energy out of the region and raise its cost; his power cannot make the same spent heat pay twice. The heat can reach an ally as readily as an enemy.

**SOLFATARA CORE (Passive Trait)**

**Absorbs nearby heat to restore vitality**, converting a portion of ambient thermal energy directly into Flux. The Crystal takes usable heat from hotter surroundings through its Shell and spends the recovered flow on the body's existing repair. The conversion sheds waste heat through the Mantle, and tissue repair still costs more than injuring the same tissue. Nearby hot surfaces dull as his orange aura brightens; a low inward whistle accompanies the draw. Cold surroundings and insulation between him and the heat source starve the intake. It cannot restore a missing part's pattern, refill him by recycling his own waste, or make a blade wound cease to be a wound.

**SCORCHBIND (Secondary Trait)**

**Brands an enemy's Essence lattice with a burning sigil**, halting their regeneration for a set duration. Heat held in the brand keeps the wounded part of the enemy's Essence lattice from settling into the pattern its regeneration needs. A brand costs 1% of Xhael's full reserve and holds for 30 seconds after contact. A red mark shines through clothing with the brief hiss of a hot iron. The mark must reach the lattice through contact or a landed plasma working; a ward that keeps his Essence outside prevents it. Cooling and isolating the marked portion of the lattice ends the interference early. The brand stops repair while it holds; it does not undo repair already finished.

**CROWN OVERHEAT (Trait liability)**

**Prolonged combat risks an Essence detonation over a wide radius** — a standing liability, not a worst-case hypothetical. The Crown and Shell take in pressure faster than their vents can shed it. Its tell is a broken whistle from the Crown and light that pulses out of step with his breathing. Each further discharge consumes reserve while heat remains trapped against the body. Open vents, a cooling interval and a clear Crownflare discharge check the buildup; a prepared opponent can force his casting to stop by burdening the vents with heat-resistant material and withdrawing from the threatened radius. That interruption is dangerous to everyone nearby and is not protection from the blast. Overheat creates no extra reserve.

**THE SIX-HOUR LIMIT (Trait liability)**

**Cannot maintain full human form beyond six continuous hours of casting.** After that, whatever he is stops being negotiable. The body's human outline loses coherence under uninterrupted casting. Close to the limit, the edges of his fingers remain bright after he moves them and his voice carries the Crown's whistle beneath it. Feeding him more heat or reserve does not reset the six-hour clock. Ending the casting and allowing the outline to settle prevents the threshold being crossed; distance, cover and repeated disengagement let a prepared opponent make continuous pursuit cost him that time. The form beyond the limit remains unnamed.

**Trait risks carried** · Exuroth's quench cracking if discharge follows discharge without recovery; Solfatara's vent blockage and pressure escaping through the weakest part; Crown Overheat; loss of full human form after six continuous hours of casting.

## IX. Domain

**THE OPEN FURNACE (Domain)**

His Second Release opens a 30-metre-radius field in which his Shell can draw heat toward him and release it through chosen outward paths. The field settles over six seconds, marked by pale orange light across the ground and the Crown's whistle becoming a standing note. It is a consolidated Realm at Stage X; the magma scar remains its territorial anchor, while the field he actively holds around himself is finite and paid for. Holding the field alone consumes 0.15% of his full reserve each second, so it reaches the tenth-reserve cutoff after ten minutes from full. This is the Second Release's cost, not a second charge. The Domain grants no ownership of an opponent's Crystal and cannot take heat through a ward it has not overcome. Its edge can be crossed, comparable Dominion can contest its ground, and a sustained heat sink forces him to spend more to keep the imposed temperature. Severe loss of concentration or insufficient reserve collapses the active field; heated ground and the scar's bond remain.

## X. Techniques

**CROWNFLARE (Signature Technique)**

**Effect** · He expels excess Flux through the Obsidian Crown as a radiant shockwave, an overflow valve for a system that otherwise cooks him from the inside. **Cost** · **The discharge burns straight off his own reserve at a rate any coil can read** — using it on purpose is functionally identical to bleeding a wound to relieve the pressure behind it. **Limit** · The Crown must first accumulate enough excess Flux to vent, so it cannot be thrown twice in quick succession; forced early, it produces a smaller, wilder discharge instead. **Counter** · **Deny him the buildup window** — keep the engagement short, or force the vent into a direction he has to choose badly, toward cover he'd rather not burn or people he'd rather not hit. **What nobody knows.** Whether the Sun-Crown is a relic he wears, or a remnant of Solfatara's dead Wellspring still making its own decisions about when to vent. A deliberate full discharge costs 5% of his full reserve. The Crown brightens from its base toward its points and gives a rising whistle before the shockwave leaves it. Before contact, interruption of the buildup spoils the full discharge; during contact, substantial cover backed by a heat ward absorbs and diverts part of the flash and hot front; afterward, his need to accumulate excess Flux again leaves a window for a prepared opponent to close. The cover must survive the actual discharge, and thin shelter does not suffice.

### Releases

**UNBANKED SUN (First Release)** · folk name: Unbanking, in Common rendering.

Pale orange light fills the seams of his skin and the Solar Mantle opens into its luminous wings, with one hard furnace exhalation. Solar Invocation runs through a wider, steadier route across his Shell and reaches the top of its own Grade when called. The change stays on his body and what he touches; it does not create a free discharge. Holding the form costs 0.075% of his full reserve each second, giving twenty minutes from full to the tenth-reserve cutoff. Separate pulses and hostile cooling shorten that time. Released silently, the form and sound remain but Solar Invocation keeps its unreleased output instead of gaining the call's rise. Heat carried away faster than the vents can replace it defeats the sustained coat; heat-resistant obstruction of the Mantle's vents forces him to close the release or feed Crown Overheat. Afterward he is spent and shaking, and pain suppressed during the release returns. Holding past the cutoff becomes raw overchannel.

**THE OPEN FURNACE (Second Release)** · folk name: Open Hearth, in Common rendering.

The orange seams spread from his feet across the ground, the Mantle's wings widen, and the Crown's whistle holds one steady note. This opens and powers the Domain described above, with heat drawn inward and vented through chosen outward paths. Within its 30-metre radius, the called release raises Solar Invocation one Grade above its base; the rise is the Second Release's total boost, not an addition to a separately stacked First Release. The field settles in six seconds and drains 0.15% of full reserve per second, lasting ten minutes from full to the tenth-reserve cutoff before other spending. Released silently, it still forms, shines and sounds, but Solar Invocation remains at its base Grade. An opponent can leave its edge, contest its ground with comparable Dominion, or cool a passage faster than Xhael can heat it. Blocking the routes by which heat leaves him raises his own pressure. The release leaves his Crystal spilling until a full night's sleep; holding it below the cutoff risks a Crystal Fracture Event.

**THE KING TAKES THE FIRE (Third Release)** · folk name: King's Burden, in Common rendering.

The Crown's black points stand inside an outline of molten gold, and sulphurous red appears around the pale orange field. A deeper sound, like gas forcing its way through stone, joins the Crown's held whistle. Within the same 30-metre field, heat entering his claimed paths must pass through his Shell before it can be sent outward as Solar Invocation; this is the Trait's local law at two Grades above its base for that thermal effect alone. It does not stop a weapon's weight, grant control of another Crystal, or create a larger store of Essence. The total holding drain is 0.75% of full reserve each second, giving two minutes from full to the tenth-reserve cutoff, with every separate discharge charged as well. Called or silent, it must take the heat before it can send it on. Silent release keeps the form and heat-routing law at base Grade and loses the two-Grade rise. Cold ground and heat wards deny intake; a same-Stage opponent can also break the outward route and make the gathered heat remain inside Xhael, forcing a vent or a withdrawal. Leaving the field ends its claim on an opponent. Afterward he remains Overgrown for a day and a night, aching throughout his body. Held past the cutoff, Solar Invocation loses its containment and feeds the Crown Overheat detonation. No release resets the six-hour limit.

**Bonded weapon** · none. The Obsidian Crown remains a fused relic; its contribution to the releases does not make it a separate bonded weapon.

## XIII. Physical Description

His hair is black like basalt freshly broken, worn short where it can grow around the Crown's fused base. The face beneath it is broad, with a heavy jaw, close-set dark eyes and a mouth that rests slightly open when he is holding heat. Fine gold-coloured seams run from the Crown into the skin at his temples. His neck, chest and upper arms have the thickness of a man accustomed to carrying armour; his hands are broad and slow, their knuckles rough under the gauntlets. The Solar Mantle fits close across his chest and hangs heavily from his shoulders, its basalt filigree polished at the edges by wear and dulled with ash in the recesses. Beneath it he wears a dark, close-fitting coat, scorched at the collar and repaired at both cuffs. The Ashen Sigil rests against his breastbone. In repose the pale orange aura does not heat a room by itself; heat comes with the working.

## Lore

### The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Sun and sand. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Xhael Ten'Kor was born a prince of the Temple-State of Solfatara, in the Outer World. The ground under the city breathed. It let out its heat and its gas a little at a time, and it had done so for centuries without once erupting.

A Wellspring lay embedded beneath the palace vaults. It was Exuroth, the Trial Flame. It raised him. It worked on him the way a smith works a blade: heat, the quench, and heat again. He learned to stand in heat that no one else in the palace could bear. He grew broad and heavy and slow in the hands. He learned to read a room by who stood nearest the throne and who looked away from it.

#### The Making

His father needed a cure, and the road to it ran through the Glass Storms of the Great Sand Sea. Xhael went himself. The Temple-State hired a freeblade, Kaelis Aranthor, to keep him alive on that road. At the edge of the sand the Guides of the Lost Meridian gave him Zahur Vane.

Zahur walked the party in by the gold at his ears. When the first storm rose, Kaelis wanted to cross before it closed, and Zahur sat the party down in the lee of a dune. In that storm the prince was lost from the lee. Kaelis did not find him. Zahur heard his footfalls through the gold, walked out into the storm and brought him back. From then on the party moved when Zahur moved.

Xhael found what he had crossed for and carried it back out through the storms. At the end of the road Zahur named his price, a story nobody had heard, and Xhael told it to him alone. Kaelis took the Temple-State's coin and went.

Afterward, in the palace, he seized divine power through an incomplete ascension rite, merging mortal Essence with solar Aether. For as long as the rite ran, the ground under Solfatara stopped venting. When it gave, it gave all at once. The ritual burned the throne to ash and crowned him in molten gold. The world began answering back. It answered back by killing forty thousand people first.

#### The Cost

The Temple-State burned with its throne. Over the place where it had stood the rite left a magma scar, and he was bound to it. He had not made it on purpose, and he could not fully disown it.

He exiled himself. He carried himself as a king still, and nobody was left for him to rule. He denied that he was a god. By his own account he was "a man who mistook light for salvation."

The Obsidian Crown had fused to his skull. It vented what his body could not hold, and every venting burned straight out of his own reserve. Where he could, he turned it on bare ground. He wore a charm of cooled Wellspring glass at his throat to hold the flares down.

On the Aurelian Dust Coast, under ash blown west off the Drakhal Spine, a Varūn caravan bound home with copper and nitre stopped dead in an Aether storm. Its hired blade was Kaelis Aranthor. Rafik Al'Seren, a Wellspring cartographer, walked with it on nobody's commission. Xhael walked out of the ash and down its line of beasts. He drew the heat around the caravan into himself and went at its head, and the caravan followed his light into clear air. He left it at the edge of the ash before his form's limit came. He asked for nothing. He gave no sign that he knew Kaelis.

What Rafik had seen of him in the ash reached the Guild Accord's archivists. The Accord sent envoys up to the scar with Rajhak Tarun as their liaison, and Xhael came down to meet them. Khaeron Vant, an executioner brought by the Accord's warpriests, drove four knives into the crust around the meeting and raised a chamber in which any lie would bloom as ash-fire in the liar's lungs. The envoys asked Xhael what had happened at Solfatara. He told them what had happened and nothing of how. Nothing burned.

#### Where They Stand

The envoys went back down unhurt, and the Accord's register lists him as a Realm-Hazard. It knows where to find him. He wanders, and he comes back to the scar. On the road below it the Concord forge that went dead the year Solfatara burned has been lit again with Rajhak Tarun's blood.

The drovers carried word of him west to Varūn. The Crown still vents. He chooses where. His human form holds through six continuous hours of casting and no longer. Past that, whatever he is stops being negotiable.

Every tie he has is read against the forty thousand.

Nobody knows whether the Crown on his head is a relic he wears, or a remnant of Solfatara's dead Wellspring still deciding for itself when to vent.

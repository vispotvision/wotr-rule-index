## VIII. Traits

**TWIN-TAIL FLUX (Primary Trait)**

**Twin-Tail Flux** · **Independent Essence circuits in each tail**, acting as secondary hands. The Shell carries a separate working through each tail while the Core pays from one reserve; the circuits divide control, and supply no second reserve. Keeping both tails under independent magical load costs 8,665 EU per minute, 0.5% of full reserve, reaching the tenth-reserve threshold after 180 minutes if nothing else is spent. Ordinary movement of the tails carries no separate casting charge. Their tips move in different rhythms and make paired dry clicks when a working passes between them. A tail held against his body or caught in cord cannot complete its movement or place an inscription; the other remains usable, but neither can work beyond its physical reach.

**BLOOMBRAND (Passive Trait)**

**Bloombrand** · **A living glyph that grows when he manipulates Wellspring flora**, blooming into a full petal crown at high Essence states. The mark makes that flow visible on his skin; it grants no separate store, growth supply or territorial control. It carries no separate upkeep beyond the flora-working that opens it. Its tell is the flower itself, chlorophyll-green against the grey and black-violet aura, with a faint dry rustle as the petals spread. Separation from living Wellspring flora removes the condition that opens it, and depleted ground makes the flora it accompanies grow thin and weak. A watcher can read the exposed bloom even when his feet make no sound.

**SILENT-STEP VEIL (Secondary Trait)**

**Silent-Step Veil** · **Movement in Shadow-aspected Essence zones becomes soundless and nearly scentless.** Tenebra lowers the returns from his moving body along the approach he has attended to; his Shell contains and redirects that trace while he stays within the shadowed ground. Active concealment costs 17,330 EU per minute, 1% of full reserve, allowing 90 minutes before the tenth-reserve threshold when used alone. It leaves weight, footprints, disturbed leaves and displaced dust. Its onset sounds like a breath drawn through dry grass; the soundless movement that follows does not conceal a release call or a seed opening. An observer outside the angle he prepared sees a sharp black-violet edge, and bright light from separated directions defeats the concealment. It suppresses his own approach, with no protection against a bell, loose board or other object he sets moving.

## X. Techniques

**THE VERDANT VEIL (Signature Technique)**

**Effect** · **Essence seeds planted in advance, sprouting later into traps, bindings or sensory wards.** *Nothing happens while he is there. Everything happens afterwards.* **Cost** · **Fixatio is permanent and he cannot recall a seed once laid.** *There are gardens he has forgotten planting.* **Limit** · **He must have been there first.** **Counter** · **Search the ground.** *Or catch him at the planting — Ardency 206 means the encounter is brief and goes badly for him.* **What nobody knows.** How many seeds are currently in the ground, and whose ground. Each seed is a working cut into ground and sealed by Fixatio, paid once at planting with 17,330 EU, 1% of full reserve. Ninety such plantings reach the tenth-reserve threshold without recovery or other spending. Verdantia drives the roots and stems from living material and ambient input; a sensory seed reads the record held in its ground through Anamnesis. The seal fixes its trigger, so his departure neither recalls it nor changes what will set it off. Waiting carries no further upkeep from him. Planting opens the Bloombrand and leaves a worked patch of soil; activation makes a dry seed-husk click before roots tighten or the ward takes its reading. Before activation, cutting the inscription or removing its marked ground breaks the working. During a binding, severing the stems interrupts their grip; after contact, lifting and breaking the inscribed patch prevents the same seal from holding that place again. Poor ambient input produces long, weak growth; a careless Anamnesis reading can erase the record it takes. Breaking the physical seal destroys what he cannot recall.

**FOUR HANDS (First Release)** · folk name: the second pair, in Common

His two tails straighten at their roots and bend through smaller, quicker turns at their tips, each outlined in grey and black-violet, while bloomcraft opens the green petal crown on his face. The release sounds as two dry seed-husk cracks. Twin-Tail Flux holds each tail's separate movement cleanly under load, allowing simultaneous handling and inscription at the top of its A-Grade coordination, without increasing his Ardency, multiplying a seed's effect or adding a limb. The name is an assay designation; Pip opens it silently. A spoken call would bring the full coordination rise for the cost of a beat; silence retains the form and separate control but loses that rise. Either opening costs 259,950 EU per minute, 15% of full reserve, and ends after six minutes at 173,300 EU if no other work is paid for; that rate includes Twin-Tail Flux's upkeep, while concealment and planted seeds cost separately. Each tail still needs physical room and contact. Nets, narrow gaps and a firm grip can bind a tail, and an opponent at his Stage can keep clear until the reserve clock ends. The coloured tips reveal their positions even when his steps are silent. Afterwards both tails shake and lose their fine control until he rests; forcing the form below its cutoff risks a Crystal fracture.

## XIII. Physical Description

His hair is the brown of dry seed husks, cut above the collar and tied back when he works. A narrow face, broad cheekbones and a pointed chin give his resting expression the appearance of private amusement; his eyes are dark brown and usually lowered toward the ground someone has just crossed. The floral mark runs from his left cheek toward the temple, opening into its full petal crown when his Essence rises. He is lean through chest and belly, with strong thighs, small hands and two long, brown-furred tails whose darker tips move independently. His smoke-brown work coat fits closely at the wrists and loosely across the shoulders; two bound openings at the back let the tails pass without pulling the hem. The knees of his trousers are rubbed pale from kneeling, and the soft boots carry old soil in their seams. Small pale scratches cross his knuckles. He smells faintly of crushed stems and the inside of a seed sack.

### The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Voice Between Wars and the Outer nobles. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Ziyu "Pip" Inari was born on the grain lands of the Southern Grass Sea, below the South Granaries. His father caught birds for the granary keepers. They paid him by the field to keep the finches off the grain, and early each spring he sowed the field margins with the flowers finches came to for seed. Then he set horsehair snares among the young stems, before anything had opened. By the time the heads went to seed and the birds came down, he was working another field.

The boy carried the snare bundle. He learned to tie a noose with his hands while his two tails held the stems apart. He learned to walk a margin without lifting a bird. A snare, he learned, closed after the man who set it had gone.

When his father could no longer walk the rows, the keepers offered them to the son, with the cottage at the end of them. Pip set the margins for the keepers that spring, and he was gone before a flower opened.

#### The Making

He went where the ground had more in it. Seed and cuttings from the gardens grown over Wellsprings sold for more than grain, and those gardens kept a watch. He learned to go in anyway. In shadowed ground his step made no sound and almost no scent. When he worked the Wellspring flora, the floral mark on his face grew. He learned to run Essence down each tail apart from the other, until each tail worked as another hand.

He learned to cut a working into a seed and seal it into the ground the way his father had set a noose in spring. It went in ahead of time, keyed to whatever crossed it later, and it held until then.

He laid the first at the gate of a walled Wellspring garden, on his way out, with a pack of cuttings on his back. He was far down the road at first light when the garden's watch came through the gate after him. The path closed on their ankles in green stems and held them. It was a small working, fragile and late, and it opened in ground he had left. Nothing had happened while he was there. Everything happened afterwards. From that seed on he gained strength by the planting.

#### The Cost

A seed once laid stayed laid. He could not call one back. By then there were gardens he had forgotten planting, in ground he had no reason to visit again. Each was still a working with his signature on it, and a Measurewright who found one had a record of who had been there.

One lay in the road from an Innerworld principality to the nearest Concord Gate. The Thorned Court's hunts had drawn blood on the principality's ground, and Aurevian Lysanthir sat between them at the Arbitration Division's table. An Enforcement officer meant to carry the case for a writ against the Court to the Council himself. Pip had been on that road first. The seed opened into a binding under the officer's horse and held him until the treaty stood signed. He reached the Council after the seal. Pip was gone before it opened. No record says whether Aurevian asked for it or knew of it.

The planting was the one part of the work he was present for, and there he could be caught. Caught at it, he had two tails, a silent step and little to fight with, and the encounter was brief and went badly for him. On a Concord raid his life came into Raivhar Drakonis's hands. Raivhar spared it.

He had refused his father's rows and kept no ground of his own after them. He made his bonds with ground, and all of it was other people's. He trusted it to keep what he gave it. It kept all of it, the seeds laid in error with the rest.

#### Where They Stand

Pip is an unlisted operative of the Black Concord. No record says how he came to it. He holds no ground and plants seeds in everybody else's, and the workings he leaves in the ground of the people he infiltrates sprout after he has gone. He does not stay to see one open.

He is an occasional liaison to the Kuromaki Association, the Beastkin guild Saruin Kye keeps a loose alliance with. Hyo Miran trades with him in rare goods and information. He sometimes plays the informant for Aurevian, and he has sabotaged Aurevian's rivals more than once. Seraphion Veydrak watches him with suspicion. A rumour ties him to an archon of a Shadow Court, and nothing on his record confirms it.

He has never named his own art. What names it has came from instruments. From the one angle he did not plan for, he is easy to see. Nobody knows how many of his seeds are in the ground, or whose ground.

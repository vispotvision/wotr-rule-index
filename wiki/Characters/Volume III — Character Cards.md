# Volume III — Character Cards

Character cards for Volume III — the Age of Calamity cast.
> **Migration in progress.** These sheets are being rebuilt from the legacy Trello board (*The Dawn of Iridescent Sovereignty Lost*), where they had drifted badly out of alignment with current canon.
>
> **Six systemic errors are being corrected on every sheet:** multiple Soul Crystals collapsed to one Crystal with three layers · invented Wellsprings replaced with attested currents from the sixty · Great Work operations removed as a progression path · Temperance Stage assigned from the sixteen · percentage stat blocks removed · dead canon struck.
>
> **No stat value on any Volume III sheet is invented.** Every allocation is worked from the Fracture of Worlds point economy — Band contribution per level, Threshold bonuses at Stage × 100, and the Stage ceiling — and checked against the Part Seven Path gates. **Temperance Stage assignments are originated and pending Isaac's ratification.**

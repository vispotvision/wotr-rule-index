# Volume IV — Character Cards

Character cards for Volume IV. Migrated from the legacy Trello board (*The Dawn of Iridescent Sovereignty Lost*) and rebuilt against current canon.
> **Migration in progress · 97 cards on the source board.**
>
> Volume IV sheets are **substantially healthier than Volume III's.** Most already carry real Wellsprings, a stated Temperance Stage, and actual Fracture of Worlds stat values rather than percentage blocks. **These are corrections, not rebuilds.**
>
> **The dominant error in this volume is different and easy to miss: Tier Grade labels are systematically inflated by roughly one Grade.** A stat of 158 is labelled B‑, when 176 is where B-Grade begins and 158 is C. The values are sound and the letters attached to them are not, which matters because **the differential rule resolves exchanges on Grade** — one Grade up wins without contest. An inflated label turns a fair fight into a foregone one on paper.
>
> Also recurring: **Aether Classes invented outside the canonical eight**, Wellsprings that are not among the sixty, and **"Mind" cited as a Path** where the four are Body, Spirit, Attraction and Fate.

> **STANDING RULING · Stage number/name mismatches**
>
> **A scan of Volumes III–VI found nineteen cards whose Stage numeral contradicts its own Stage name.** Applied correction, consistently:
>
> **The name wins on off-by-one slips.** In every case the surrounding prose describes the *named* Stage's actual condition, so the word is right and the numeral is a slip.
>
> **The numeral wins on "Revelation."** *Revelation is Stage XV — beyond the canonical fourteen, Archon-participant territory, two recorded practitioners in the whole record.* **Five cards attach it to Stages VIII, X and XI.** Those five are not Stage XV characters; the word was chosen for flavour and collided with a reserved Stage name. **They keep their numerals and lose the word.**

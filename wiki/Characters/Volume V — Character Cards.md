# Volume V — Character Cards

**Migration of the Dawn of Iridescent Sovereignty Lost Trello board, Volume V, into canon-compliant character sheets.**
> **111 cards.** *Substantially longer on average than Volume IV — several run 8,000 to 15,000 characters.*
>
> **Method, unchanged from Volume IV:** every element of the original card is preserved — Origin, traits with their full descriptions, relationships, key quotes, chapter appearances, artifacts. **Only the mechanics layer is corrected**, and every stat is hand-derived per character against the Fracture of Worlds pool economy rather than generated.

## Standing corrections carried from Volume IV

### The three Stage-error classes

> **I · Off-by-one slips.** *The Stage name is real but the numeral disagrees with it — "Stage V (Flourishing)" when Flourishing is IV.* **The name wins**, because in every checked instance the surrounding prose described the named Stage's actual condition.
>
> **II · Reserved-word collisions.** *A real Stage name used at the wrong tier — overwhelmingly "Revelation," which is Stage XV, Archon-participant territory with two recorded practitioners in the setting's history.* **The numeral wins and the word is struck.**
>
> **III · Invented Stage names.** *Words that are not among the sixteen at all — Conquest, Judgment, Blooming, Pinnacle, Crown, Illumination, Sprouting, Shaping, Pursuit, Dominion, Archonic Binding, Blooming Zenith.* **The numeral wins and the word is struck.**
**The Benediction mapping.** *Several cards give Temperance as "Benediction" or "Severance."* **Neither is a Stage** — Benediction is a resonance, Severance is a Resilience sub-stat. **Orya of the Wheat March's card reads "late Refraction moving toward Benediction,"** which places Benediction directly above Refraction. **Benediction therefore maps to Stage VIII, Transcendence**, and Severance to Stage IX.

### Recurring content corrections

**Essence types.** *Cards routinely list invented "Primary/Secondary/Tertiary Essence" values — Flame, Blood, Shadow, Silence, Venom.* **These are descriptors, not register entries**, and are re-sourced to attested Wellsprings.
**Aether Class.** *Invented class names — "Predator Class," "Adjudicant," "Chrysalis Sovereign."* **There are eight**, I through VIII, Latent to Transcendent.
**Archons are not operations.** *"Fermentation (Archon of Flux)," "Dissolution (Archon of Breakdown)."* **The fourteen are named beings** — Thalen, Auren, Elyndra, Uurgath, Selhar, Urion, Iesara, Zhaeren, Valen, Wyther, Veyra, Kaetra, Irath, Maelor.
**Great Work operations are not Wellsprings.** *Eight of them genuinely are currents — Calcination, Dissolution, Coagulatio, Distillation, Sublimare, Transmutatio, Fixatio, Materia Primordia.* **Cibation, Projection, Exaltation, Solution and Fermentation are not**, which is why the mistake recurs.
**Paths.** *There are four — Body, Spirit, Attraction, Fate.* "Mind Path" is not among them and is read as Gnosis weighting.

> **STANDING RULING · The Five Pillars incursion**
>
> **Volume V cards routinely carry explicit Five Pillars figures — Essence Capacity, Flux Density, Aether Output, η — and a "Global Tier" line, and these frequently outrun the Stage the same card states.**
>
> *Sir Rhyse Calder is the type case: Stage VII stated correctly, with η 0.84 and "S tier." But η 0.84 falls in the gap between Band A at 0.75–0.80 and Band S at 0.85–0.90, and Band S η belongs to Stage XI–XII practitioners. Stage VII caps at 475.*
>
> **Resolution, applied throughout: the Stage wins.**
>
> *The Stage is the load-bearing figure — it sets the ceiling, the Grade cap and the Band, and every other value derives from it. Tier language ("S tier," "A+ rising to S") is read as in-world reputation rather than a system rating, which is what it plainly is: how the Accord talks about someone, not what an assay returns.*
>
> **η is normalised to the Band the Stage supports.** *Where a card gives a figure inside a Band, it is kept. Where it gives one between Bands or above its Stage, it is corrected down and the original noted.*
>
> **Raw Pillars figures — EU capacity, EU/gram, AU/s — are retained where they do not contradict the Stage**, *since they operate at a scale the stat sheet does not model.* **Where they imply a tier the Stage forbids, they are cut.**
>
> ***Same principle as the Stage-name classes: one half of the card is authoritative, the other was written for effect, and the authoritative half is the one the system can check.***

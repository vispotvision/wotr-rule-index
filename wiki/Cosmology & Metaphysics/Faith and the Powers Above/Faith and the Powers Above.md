Every child in the four quarters can skip rope to the Fourteen, and every child gets the order wrong, and in Year 602 a lector of Sancta Lux (a reader-cleric of the Scale, the faith with the courts) who stopped to correct a skipping yard at Ottermere was chased into the horse pond by forty girls in red stockings with their ropes still going. The girls' version is the one people know. These shelves hold the lector's version, with his jokes put back in, since it turned out he had some.

# Pair I — Form Against Time

Hamelin Prudhoe, master mason of Crowmarsh, built the Hall of Six Angles to Thalen's ratios in Year 519, and on the night of its opening he stood up at the high table in a vermilion coat with a cup in his fist and told three hundred guests that it would stand until the stars moved. Directly above him, a keystone the size of a bread oven chose that moment to leave the vault. He had been cheated on the lime, as it turned out. Down at the far end of the table, sourly eating alone because the masons had refused her fee, sat Sister Tamsin Yarrow, keeper of the town's water clock and an adept of the Chronaris art, and she was the only person in the room who saw the crack run. She threw her spoon at Prudhoe, and it reached him with the whole weight of her art on it, and for him the instant opened out wide as a parish. The stone hung over the pudding. Wine stood in the air in long red beads. He had all the time he could ever want, and he spent it walking the load path of his own hall in his head, every rib and spring, until he found the one pier the rest could lean on, and then he slammed his bare palm on the table, his coat went the white of fresh plaster, and he told the building what angles it was allowed to have.

The keystone stopped a hand above the pudding with the whole vault hanging from it in a new and rather ugly shape, and in the real world not one heartbeat had gone by. Then every bone in Prudhoe's right hand broke, and both his nostrils let go at once, and three hundred people, who had seen a man shout at a dinner table and a stone come loose and stay, began to scream. The hall still stands in that shape, and nobody has dared correct it. The masons of Crowmarsh say Form saved those guests. The clock-keepers say it was Time. They have been settling it with staves in the corn market every Midsummer for nearly two hundred years, and the two old people who began it were found to have left each other their tools.

> Thalen governs what shapes may exist. Valen governs when those shapes may manifest, persist, and dissolve. A form without duration is a flash. A duration without form is an empty wait.

## III. Thalen, the Architect of Form

### Form Magic: *Formaris*

| **Domain** | Structure, Space, Geometry, the Permission for Shape to Exist |
|---|---|
| **Celestial Form** | **The Hexagonal Crown**: six perfectly equidistant stars forming a geometry visible only through Essence-tuned instruments. |
| **Associated Glyphs** | `[Th]` Foundation · `[Al]` Edge · `[En]` Lattice · `[Cal]` Craft |

> ***Divine Edict*** · *"All chaos is only structure unremembered."*

Thalen defines what can be manifested in reality. He governs the geometry that permits existence to take shape, from the angles of a crystal lattice to the load-bearing ratios of a Concord bastion. In the catastrophe the old Archonic registers call the Withering, when Uurgath's entropy ate through cities and fortresses, Thalen's doctrine saved what little could be saved: the **Adamant Vaults** were built to his ratios, their walls more equation than stone. (That is the registers' word for it. The Withering Era of the sealed count opens at Year 645, and the Guild's Errata tells a reader holding such a register to keep the two apart.) His **Forgewrights** devised Soul Crystal housings (the Crystal being the soul itself, which refines Aether into Essence) and stabilizer frames that allowed early Paragons to survive their own power without shattering. What survives of that work is relic now, unreplaceable and dangerous, and people kill for it.

#### Formaris: The Magic of Architecture and Geometry

Formaris is the Archonic magic of structural definition, spatial anchoring, and the enforcement of geometric law upon chaotic matter. A practitioner of Formaris declares what shapes reality is permitted to hold, and the building comes after. Every magic below runs two ways: through the Attraction Force (the law of recognition, the binding that lets one presence acknowledge another and endure) and through the Obsession Force (the same reach gone to fixation, unable to let go).

At its **lowest expression**, Formaris stabilizes structures against collapse, reinforces ward geometries, and establishes Essence grids that distribute metaphysical stress across broad areas. At its **highest expression**, Formaris becomes the power to define the fundamental geometry of a Domain, to declare that within this space, only these angles may exist, only these proportions may endure, only these forms may take root.
> **Through Attraction Force** · Formaris becomes consecrated structure, architecture that persists because reality recognizes its rightness.
>
> **Through Obsession Force** · It becomes imprisonment in form, the tyranny of shape that refuses to allow growth, change, or the organic deviation that keeps structure alive.

**Connections** · Respected by Maelor (Memory), for archives require structure to persist. Trusts Urion (Balance), whose law prevents his structures from becoming instruments of tyranny. Contrasts Wyther (Motion); every storm is a test Thalen expects his work to pass. Reveres Auren (Stillness), recognizing that even death has geometry. Opposed by Uurgath (Disruption). Finds kinship with Elyndra (Renewal) when living growth wraps itself around stone without breaking it.

## IV. Valen, the Clock of Ages

### Time Magic: *Chronaris*

| **Domain** | Time, Sequence, Duration, Causality, the Rhythm That Must Be Followed |
|---|---|
| **Celestial Form** | **The Pendulum Arc**: a binary orbit that traces the same path with such regularity that civilizations calibrate their calendars to its eclipses. |
| **Associated Glyphs** | `[Va]` Moment · `[Len]` Line · `[Cro]` Cycle · `[Ae]` Era |

> ***Divine Edict*** · *"Every moment is a covenant."*

Valen enforces when events must occur. Where Thalen defines what can exist, Valen governs the sequence in which existence unfolds. His glyphs isolate instants, define clean sequences between events, encode repetition with variation, and mark the boundaries of entire ages.
> The `[Ae]` glyph, invoked rarely, imprints a temporal boundary onto the Wellspring lattice itself, declaring that one age has ended and another has begun.

#### Chronaris: The Magic of Sequence and Duration

Chronaris is the Archonic magic of temporal governance, causal enforcement, and the preservation of ordered time against paradox. Nobody in this art rewinds a clock, whatever the fairground plays show. A practitioner of Chronaris enforces the lawful relationship between cause and consequence, ensuring that sequence remains coherent even under extreme metaphysical stress.

At its **lowest expression**, Chronaris sharpens perception of an instant, slows the subjective experience of a critical moment, or defines a clean line between two events to prevent causal tangling. At its **highest expression**, Chronaris becomes the power to mark the end of an age and the beginning of another, imprinting temporal boundaries onto the Wellspring lattice that reorganize the flow of causality itself.
> **Through Attraction Force** · Chronaris preserves sequence, history, causality, and meaningful progression.
>
> **Through Obsession Force** · It decays into loop-prison, recursive inevitability, frozen causality, and the inability of events to move beyond one wound, one vow, or one decisive moment.

**Connections** · Kin to Maelor (Memory); Maelor keeps what happened, Valen keeps when and in what order it may be remembered. Distrusts Wyther (Motion), whose upheavals threaten to tangle timelines beyond repair. Holds quiet sorrowful respect for Auren (Stillness), knowing every ending is also a hard punctuation mark in his ledger. Rivals Uurgath (Disruption), whose entropy seeks to smear before and after into unreadable ruin. Shares a distant, necessary accord with Zhaeren (Horizon), for time needs somewhere to move through.

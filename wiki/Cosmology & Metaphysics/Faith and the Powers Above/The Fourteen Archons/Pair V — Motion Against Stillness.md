# Pair V — Motion Against Stillness

The pilgrim cog *Fat Margery* left Saltby Roads in the autumn of Year 547 with ninety souls aboard, painted to the waterline in blue and white for the crossing to the shrine isles, and she carried two men who should never have shared a deck. Rafe Cottle was her pilot, a loud, freckled Kineris man with a coat the green of a breaking wave, who had never in his life stopped moving and sang while he slept. Brother Still was her chaplain, a Mortaris priest of Auren with a shaved head and very clean hands, hired because pilgrims like to know somebody aboard can see them out. On the third night the gale came down out of the north and broke her back on the Maiden Reef. By every law of ships she was dead then. Cottle did not accept this. He lashed himself to the stump of the mast, his coat flaring till the rain turned green around him, took hold of the momentum of the storm itself and turned it, all of it, into her planks, and that split hull got up on the back of the sea and began to run for the land with water pouring out of her in sheets. Brother Still walked up the deck behind him through all of it at a steady pace. He stopped beside each person who was about to die, whether they knew it or not, and laid a hand on them, and they stopped being frightened, and they died. When the drowned began to stand up in the waist of the ship, as the drowned will in water that has been pushed too hard, he went down the line and told each one, quietly, to lie down, and they did.

They screamed at each other for fourteen miles. The chaplain's position, delivered at the top of a very trained voice, was that this ship had been finished for an hour and the pilot was dragging dead men across the sea for his own vanity. The pilot's position was that he would stop when he was dead, and the priest could go and tell that to his Great Stillness. At first light the *Margery* came up the shingle at Cold Harbor in two pieces and a shower of spray, in front of the whole fish market, with sixty-one alive and twenty-nine laid out in straight rows on the foredeck with their hands folded, and not one of them walking. Each man has always maintained it was his doing. They never sailed apart again after that, and in thirty years nobody heard a civil word pass between them.

> Wyther calls Auren **"The Great Stillness."** Auren does not appear to have returned the courtesy of a nickname. Their opposition is the only one in the Archonic web that both parties describe, without qualification, as enmity.

## XI. Wyther, the Unbound Current of Motion

### Motion Magic: *Kineris*

| **Domain** | Motion, Change, Evolution, Momentum, Freedom from Stasis |
|---|---|
| **Celestial Form** | **The Spiral Verge**: a shifting tri-star system whose three bodies orbit one another in unpredictable but mathematically perfect harmony, leaving spiraling trails of plasma that redraw themselves nightly. |
| **Associated Glyphs** | `[Wy]` Motion · `[Noq]` Wind · `[Ser]` Renewal · `[Eith]` Flux |

> ***Divine Edict*** · *"Only that which moves endures."*

Wyther governs the **Law of Continuance through Change**, the metaphysical truth that stagnation is death and movement is the soul's proof of life. His breath ignites storms, pushes the tides of Essence (Aether refined in a Soul Crystal) through the Wellsprings, and dictates the rise and fall of civilizations. His essence birthed the **Aetherstorm Era** when his followers shattered ancient stagnation wards to restart atmospheric Wellsprings, preventing Essence rot from consuming the Outer Realms.

#### Kineris: The Magic of Flux and Momentum

Kineris is the Archonic magic of perpetual transformation, kinetic amplification, and the refusal to allow any system to reach terminal equilibrium. A practitioner of Kineris embodies the principle that change is the fundamental currency of survival, and moving faster is only the part the crowd pays for. Every magic below runs two ways: through the Attraction Force (the law of recognition, the binding that lets one presence acknowledge another and endure) and through the Obsession Force (the same reach gone to fixation, unable to let go).

At its **lowest expression**, Kineris grants enhanced kinetic awareness, the ability to read and redirect momentum, and the capacity to amplify physical motion with Essence until speed itself becomes a weapon. At its **highest expression**, Kineris becomes the power to shatter stagnation on a civilizational scale, to restart Wellspring flows that have ceased, to ignite storms of transformative energy that reshape terrain, culture, and metaphysical law in a single upheaval.
> **Through Attraction Force** · Kineris becomes evolution, the lawful progression from what was to what must be, the wind that carries seeds to fertile ground.
>
> **Through Obsession Force** · It becomes chaos without purpose, the compulsive need to destroy every stillness, the storm that tears apart what it should merely reshape.

**Connections** · Perpetual friction with Maelor (Memory), whose stillness archives what Wyther dissolves. Respected yet opposed by Urion (Balance), their dialogue forming the pulse of cosmic rhythm. Admired by Veyra (Desire), whose longing finds movement through him. Natural enemy of Auren (Death), whom he calls *"The Great Stillness."*

## XII. Auren, the Keeper of the Still Gate

### Death Magic: *Mortaris*

| **Domain** | Death, Stillness, Passage, Closure, Repose |
|---|---|
| **Celestial Form** | A dim white dwarf at the heart of the constellation **Thanaris**, surrounded by the **Shroud Sea**, a haze of faint, unmoving light. Where other stars drift, Auren's remains motionless, steady, unblinking, a silent beacon marking the terminus of all journeys. |
| **Associated Glyphs** | `[Au]` Death · `[Ren]` Passage · `[Thal]` Silence · `[Vor]` Return |

> ***Divine Edict*** · *"All paths lead to the Still Gate. Only peace walks through it."*

Auren governs the **Law of Transition**, the truth that nothing truly ends, but all must pass through stillness. He maintains the veil between life and the echoing plane known as the **Pale Realms**.
> To die within his silence is to be remembered truthfully; to resist it is to become an Echo, wandering, half-alive, half-forgotten. His intervention ended the **War of Seven Dawns**, when countless dead refused the passage and formed a sentient necrotic Domain, sealing it in eternal dormancy.

#### Mortaris: The Magic of Passage and Silence

Mortaris is the Archonic magic of lawful ending, soul passage, and the enforcement of death as a sacred threshold rather than an annihilation. A practitioner of Mortaris facilitates the transition from one state of being to another, and killing is the least of it, ensuring that what dies is properly received, recorded, and released.

At its **lowest expression**, Mortaris grants the ability to perceive the proximity of death, to ease the passage of dying souls, and to enforce stillness upon restless undead. At its **highest expression**, Mortaris becomes the power to seal entire necrotic Domains, to close the passage against souls that refuse their ending, and to enforce the absolute finality that even entropy cannot devour.
> **Through Attraction Force** · Mortaris becomes peaceful passage, the mercy of a death that remembers the life it concludes.
>
> **Through Obsession Force** · It becomes the tyranny of premature ending, the compulsive need to silence what still lives, the stillness that spreads as extinction and calls itself peace.

**Connections** · Eternal opposition to Wyther (Motion), whose storms deny stillness. Bound by solemn accord to Maelor (Memory), for remembrance grants death meaning. Mutual acknowledgment with Urion (Balance); together they weigh the measure of life. Resented by Veyra (Desire), whose fire he must extinguish in the end. Feared by Uurgath (Disruption), for death is a truth even entropy cannot devour.

# Pair VI — Harmony and Renewal

Three years of blight had killed every apple and pear in Aubrey Vale when the parish, in Year 633, hired two people to do something about it and made the mistake of introducing them. Choirmaster Osgood Lark brought two hundred voices in russet and cream down into the dead orchards, Resonaris trained, and in a cold spring dusk they began to sing a single low chord at the black trees, moving it up by hairs, until the whole hillside picked up a faint answering note out of the wood: the one living frequency left in the roots. Goody Thornapple was waiting for that. She was a hedge-worker with some real Vitalaris in her, mud to the elbows, and when the note came she pushed both hands into the ground and gave it everything she had. It worked. By midnight there was blossom on every twig, white enough to read by. By morning it lay a yard deep in the lanes. By noon the fruit had set, swelled, and started coming down on the Scale house, and the choir, who could feel it through their feet, kept singing because no one had told them to stop, and the chord kept feeding the growth, and the growth kept feeding the chord.

On the third day a pear limb as thick as a horse came in through the front of the Three Tuns. Apple boughs lifted the tithe barn off its footings and carried it some way up the hill. A cow was found in a tree, alive, and furious, with a branch grown through the meat of her shoulder, and two of the altos had to be cut out of a hedge. When Goody Thornapple looked down and saw a green shoot pushing out of the heel of her own palm, she walked over to Lark, showed it to him, and told him to find the note that would break it. He did. Two hundred voices turned the chord over at sundown and held the dissonance for a quarter of an hour, and every tree in the vale burst along the grain and came down in a rain of splinters and hot sweet pulp, while Goody Thornapple laid her left hand on a stump and had her nephew take it off at the wrist. Lark never heard pitch again. The stumps sprouted true the next year, at the usual speed, and Aubrey Vale has made the best perry in the quarter ever since.

> The only pair in the six whose title is a conjunction rather than an opposition. Where the other pairs hold a question open between them, Selhar and Elyndra hold a cycle turning.

## XIII. Selhar, the Voice of Harmony

### Harmony Magic: *Resonaris*

| **Domain** | Sound, Resonance, Harmony, Frequency, Emotion Through Vibration |
|---|---|
| **Celestial Form** | **Lytherion's String**: a shimmering triad of stars that hum in unison, producing tones that alter with the moral state of the world. When the realms fall into discord, its harmony wavers and shifts to dissonance. |
| **Associated Glyphs** | `[Se]` Song · `[Lh]` Frequency · `[Ar]` Accord · `[Chw]` Choir |

> ***Divine Edict*** · *"In every sound, the world remembers its shape."*

Selhar governs the **Law of Resonant Equilibrium**, the principle that all things, living or dead, possess a frequency that seeks harmony with its environment. Through this Law, Wellsprings can synchronize to avert collapse, Domains can stabilize their tone, and hearts can be brought to alignment. His resonance ended the **Siege of Epprenean Spire**, where his followers harmonized three Domains to nullify a destructive dissonance wave unleashed by Uurgath's cult.

#### Resonaris: The Magic of Frequency and Accord

Resonaris is the Archonic magic of vibrational governance, tonal stabilization, and the use of resonant frequency as both weapon and medicine. A practitioner of Resonaris perceives and manipulates the fundamental frequencies of matter, soul, and Essence (Aether refined in the Soul Crystal), and the sound is only the part the neighbors complain about. Every magic below runs two ways: through the Attraction Force (the law of recognition, the binding that lets one presence acknowledge another and endure) and through the Obsession Force (the same reach gone to fixation, unable to let go).

At its **lowest expression**, Resonaris grants the ability to hear the tonal signature of Wellsprings, to detect dissonance in ward structures, and to calm emotional turbulence through resonant frequency. At its **highest expression**, Resonaris becomes the power to harmonize entire Domains, synchronizing the Essence frequencies of every soul within a region into a single chord of unified purpose, or to weaponize dissonance, shattering structures by finding their resonant frequency and amplifying it beyond tolerance.
> **Through Attraction Force** · Resonaris becomes communion, the shared frequency that binds communities, the chord that makes a people recognizable to one another across distance and time.
>
> **Through Obsession Force** · It becomes enforced unison, the erasure of individual voice in service of a single dominant tone, the harmony that silences dissent by drowning it in overwhelming concord.

**Connections** · Closest kin to Iesara (Insight), for sound and sight reflect each other's truths. Respects Wyther (Motion), whose winds carry his song. Distrusts Uurgath (Disruption), who silences harmony with void. Often mediates between Urion (Balance) and Veyra (Desire), their discord being his testing ground.

## XIV. Elyndra, the Verdant Pulse of Life

### Life Magic: *Vitalaris*

| **Domain** | Life, Growth, Renewal, Vitality, Fertility |
|---|---|
| **Celestial Form** | **The Living Crown**: a sprawling nebular bloom of emerald and gold stars that shimmer as if inhaling, pulsating faintly in rhythm with planetary biospheres. |
| **Associated Glyphs** | `[El]` Life · `[Yn]` Bloom · `[Dr]` Vitality · `[Vael]` Renewal |

> ***Divine Edict*** · *"What grows must die; what dies must bloom again."*

Elyndra governs the **Law of Renewal**, the truth that existence perpetuates itself through death and rebirth. Her influence sustains all biological Wellsprings, their Essence mimicking her eternal respiration. When she sings, forests awaken, blood quickens, and the flow of Aether through the world intensifies. Her followers, the **Circle of Blooming Ash**, reversed the aftermath of Uurgath's entropy floods through a mass ritual known as the **Verdant Exhalation**, reconstituting entire regions of dead soil into fertile growth.

#### Vitalaris: The Magic of Growth and Rebirth

Vitalaris is the Archonic magic of biological sovereignty, regenerative law, and the enforcement of the cycle that connects death to new growth. A practitioner of Vitalaris invokes the fundamental principle that all ending serves as the precondition for beginning, of which healing is one small case.

At its **lowest expression**, Vitalaris regenerates living Essence, restores depleted lattice structures, accelerates biological recovery, and cycles death energy into fresh Essence to prevent the stagnation of life force. At its **highest expression**, Vitalaris becomes the power to reconstitute entire ecosystems from barren earth, to restart the cycle of growth in regions where entropy has sterilized all living potential, and to enforce the Law of Renewal against forces that would make death permanent.
> **Through Attraction Force** · Vitalaris becomes the sacred cycle, the trust that death is not final, that what falls will feed what rises, that the wheel turns and turns and turns.
>
> **Through Obsession Force** · It becomes uncontrolled growth, the cancer of Essence that multiplies without limit: the **Plague of the Living Flesh**, a blasphemy born from her misinterpreted glyphs that grew endlessly, consuming what it should have nourished.

**Connections** · Deep kinship with Auren (Death), their duality sustaining the wheel of existence. Symbiosis with Selhar (Harmony) and Kaetra (Dream), whose arts nourish emotional and imaginative growth. Friendly tension with Irath (Will), for unchecked control stifles bloom. Absolute loathing for Uurgath (Disruption), who represents sterilization and decay without rebirth. Occasional correspondence with Wyther (Motion), for wind and change spread life's seeds.

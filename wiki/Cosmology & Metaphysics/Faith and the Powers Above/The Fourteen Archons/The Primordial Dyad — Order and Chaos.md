# The Primordial Dyad — Order and Chaos

Tolland and Nettlebed had been at law for sixty years over who owned the toll on the causeway bridge between them, and in Year 433 each parish, sick of paying attorneys, hired something worse. Tolland brought in Aubin Sibley, called Even-Handed, a thin smiling magistrate of the Judicaris art whose coat burned gold on the left side and azure on the right, and he set up a stool at the crown of the bridge to weigh the quarrel. Nettlebed brought in Old Bessamy the Unpicker, a bent little woman in a sack-colored shawl with black under her nails, who practiced the other thing, and she had been told simply to finish the bridge so that nobody would own anything. She walked out under the arch while both parishes watched from the banks in their feast-day reds and greens and began to pick at the ward-cut in the keystone with one fingernail, and the mortar came away in a long black sift. Sibley did not get off his stool. Every stone she loosed he weighed, aloud, in a carrying voice, and he set its counterweight against the parish that had paid for it, and at the moment each stone dropped in the river something of the same weight in Nettlebed came down: first a chimney, then the burying-ground gate, then, on the afternoon of the third day, with a great gray cloud that the Tolland people applauded, the whole south aisle of the Scale house.

Bessamy looked up at that, grinned with the two teeth she had, and went on picking, because she had been paid for the bridge. It fell at dusk. Sibley came down with it, bleeding from both ears, and they were pulled out of the shallows by the same boatman and shared a pork pie on the bank, and nobody has paid that toll since. Sibley spent his old age cutting his bread to the grain and unable to leave a table with anything uneven on it. Bessamy spent hers in a hut where nothing would stay shut, and neither would come to the other's funeral on the grounds that the books did not balance. Those two old people are as much of the Primordial Dyad as most of us will ever see, and the Archons they were borrowing from have been at it for considerably longer.

> Before the six pairs could crystallize from the tension between preservation and disruption, that tension itself required embodiment. From the earliest moments following the Division, two principles emerged as the foundational opposition upon which all subsequent Archonic law would be constructed.
>
> Mortals call them enemies and mean something much smaller. The two are the cosmic heartbeat: the systole and diastole of reality itself, the eternal inhalation and exhalation without which the Realms would either freeze into unchangeable perfection or dissolve into formless noise.

Most if not all of the Archons are Sovereign on every plane, some are deemed greater than others, and among them these two stand alone in one place. On the Plane of Fate, Urion and Uurgath are unparalleled, because what they are cannot be conceived. A mortal mind holds an idea of balance and an idea of ending, and each of those is a small thing made from the real one, which is no help at all to anyone reaching for it.

## I. Urion, the Balancer of Scales

### Order Magic: *Judicaris*

| **Domain** | Balance, Judgment, Equilibrium, Temperance, Law of Exchange |
|---|---|
| **Celestial Form** | **The Meridian Pair**: a twin-star system locked in perfect rotational harmony, one golden, one azure, whose orbital precision defines the cosmic measurement of Balance across realms. |
| **Associated Glyphs** | `[Ur]` Balance · `[Sel]` Measure · `[Va]` Law · `[Hael]` Flame |

> ***Divine Edict*** · *"Every force seeks its equal. To forget this is to invite collapse."*

Urion governs the **Law of Equivalence**, the metaphysical axiom that every act generates counterweight. His essence permeates the Wellsprings (the sixty kinds of Aether), ensuring no Realm or Domain accrues unchecked imbalance. Essence flares (Essence being Aether refined in a Soul Crystal and stamped with identity, the soul's own substance, which a practitioner holds and spends) bearing his signature stabilize fluctuations, allowing controlled Temperance ascension. When the Great Spirit War threatened to tear the world's harmonic fabric, Urion's decree manifested as the **First Concord Writ**, a metaphysical contract compelling ceasefire through enforced Essence symmetry.

#### Judicaris: The Magic of Equilibrium and Judgment

Judicaris is the Archonic magic of measured force, lawful arbitration, and the enforcement of cosmic fairness. A practitioner of Judicaris imposes the fundamental axiom that every action carries a counterweight and that no force may accumulate without generating its opposite, and balancing energies is the least of what that means. Every magic below runs two ways: through the Attraction Force (the law of recognition, the binding that lets one presence acknowledge another and endure) and through the Obsession Force (the same reach gone to fixation, unable to let go).

At its **lowest expression**, Judicaris manifests as the ability to stabilize Wellspring fluctuations, neutralize Essence imbalances within a Domain, and adjudicate disputes through metaphysical pressure rather than physical violence. At its **highest expression**, Judicaris becomes the power to enforce cosmic symmetry upon reality itself, compelling ceasefire between warring principles, binding oaths to their consequences, and ensuring that no soul's debt goes unweighed.
> **Through Attraction Force** · Judicaris leans on the Attraction Force's capacity for lawful recognition: it sees the true weight of every act, every intention, every accumulated imbalance, and declares the correction required.
>
> **Through Obsession Force** · Judicaris corrupts into paralytic stasis, forced symmetry that refuses to permit necessary imbalance, and the suffocating refusal to let any scale tip even when reality demands it.

**Connections** · Allied with Maelor (Memory) and Iesara (Insight) as the **Triad of Law**. Adversarial to Uurgath (Disruption) and Veyra (Desire), whose impulses distort measured flow. Mutual respect with Wyther (Motion), though constant friction arises from Motion's refusal of rest.

## II. Uurgath, the Devourer of Forms

### Chaos Magic: *Nihilaris*

| **Domain** | Disruption, Entropy, Decay, Ruin, Erasure of Pattern |
|---|---|
| **Celestial Form** | **The Ecliptic Maw**: a dark pulsar visible only when its devouring corona eclipses neighboring stars, emitting pulses that consume starlight and leave void trails. |
| **Associated Glyphs** | `[Uur]` Disruption · `[Kael]` Ruin · `[Thru]` Hollow · `[Vorn]` End |

> ***Divine Edict*** · *"All forms lie. I grant them truth through their end."*

Uurgath governs the **Law of Unmaking**, the entropy that claims all structures once they exhaust their purpose. He unbinds lattice coherence, unweaves glyphs, and devours both memory and meaning. In his passing, Essence becomes raw again, ready for creation anew, though few mortals ever live to see rebirth in his wake.
> His disciples weaponized Entropy Wellsprings during the Age of Erasure to dissolve entire Domains, and it was Uurgath who silenced the first Archonic constellation, erasing the Archon **Sirael of Bloom** from both memory and starlight.

#### Nihilaris: The Magic of Unmaking and Entropy

Nihilaris is the Archonic magic of terminal dissolution, pattern erasure, and the return of all things to their most primitive state. A practitioner of Nihilaris accelerates the entropy already present within a target, finding the structural rot that every form carries and granting it permission to complete its work, which leaves far less rubble than ordinary destruction and far more silence afterward in the records.

At its **lowest expression**, Nihilaris corrodes wards, unweaves protective glyphs, and degrades Essence coherence. At its **highest expression**, Nihilaris becomes the power to erase concepts from the fabric of reality itself, consuming not just bodies but the memories of those bodies, not just structures but the possibility that those structures ever existed.

Nihilaris is the Obsession Force given teeth: the fixation upon ending that refuses the rebirth which should follow.
> **Through Attraction Force** · Nihilaris performs lawful ending, the necessary severance of what has overstayed its design.
>
> **Through Obsession Force** · It becomes recursive annihilation, the hunger that devours to satisfy the void's own appetite and makes room for nothing.

**Connections** · Arch-enemy of Maelor (Memory), for remembrance defies his hunger. Loathed by Urion (Balance), who forever repairs what Uurgath tears. Feared even by Auren (Death), who calls him *"the After of After."* Occasionally courted by Veyra (Desire) and Wyther (Motion), for destruction can spark rebirth.

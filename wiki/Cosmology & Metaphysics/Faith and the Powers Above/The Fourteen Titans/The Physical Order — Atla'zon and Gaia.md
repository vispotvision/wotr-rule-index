# The Physical Order — Atla'zon and Gaia

Any child at Varūn will tell you the war-mauls are the funniest thing in the world, and the reason is the Swallow. Pellam Hobbs, called the Swallow of the Rising Court, was the greatest lifter of his generation, a small, vain, beautiful man in a cloak of canary silk who had once floated a duke's horse across a river with the duke still on it, and in Year 590 he came up the Varūn road with drummers and a banner to settle an insult. The dwarves had been telling people their mauls could not be levitated. So they set one out on a trestle in the fairground under the Adamant Axis, an ordinary one, a smith's second-best, in front of some four thousand people in iron blue and ember orange. The Swallow's coat came up the color of a spring sky and he rose a clean six feet into the air on his own art, arms spread, to a roar you could have leaned on, and he reached down with the working and took hold of the maul. The working took. The maul stayed exactly where it was, as though the maul and the trestle and the center of the world had been poured in one piece. A lifting art has to pull against something, and what it found to pull against was the Swallow. His arms came down, both shoulders left their sockets together, and he dropped onto the trestle at the end of his own tether and lay there in his canary silk, white to the lips. A seven-year-old in a leather apron walked out of the crowd, picked the maul up by the haft in one hand, and held it out to him. He could not raise his arms to take it.

He told that story against himself for thirty years, in good inns, and it is the best short account I know of what follows. Somebody holds which way is down. That somebody is not open to persuasion, and an honest smith can borrow a little of it.

> Forged in the Aegoric Crucible during the Formation Epoch. Between them they hold which way is down and what things are made of. Every other Titan in the register is holding something that presumes these two are already holding theirs.

## ATLA'ZON: The World-Binder

> *"He bears the law of gravity and the burden of divine order."*

**Seat**  Prime Cosmological Titan of the Physical order, sibling to Gaia. Forged in the Aegoric Crucible under Aegor's hand during the Formation Epoch.

**Domain**  Permanence, Law, Gravitational Strain, Time, Endurance

**Ground**  The Wellspring of Strain, called Temporal Pressure. Governs gravity, linear causality, and the force that pulls Essence toward form.

**Wellsprings**  Four of the Sixty are ways his posture shows up: height paid for by steps, one note held, whatever stays where it was put, and Essence kept in the shape it was poured into. His own art is written in Petralon, which is Kaldrion's, since a load has to have a down to fall toward. Wild: [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Ascensio]], [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Basilithe]], [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Chthonica]]. Medium: [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Medium Wellsprings|Fixatio]].

### Manifestation

A kneeling colossus of marble, obsidian and voidlight at the equatorial core of creation. His spine traces the gravitational lines of every world. His heart is the ticking of the Loom, faintly audible as the Pulse of Time. Once in a thousand years he exhales, and new Wellspring sites do not so much form as get compressed into being by the pressure of it.

### The Load

Orientation, duration and adhesion. He decides which way is down, how fast the moment moves, and whether Essence stays inside the shape it was poured into. Withdraw him and the planes unspool back into fractal Aether. He holds everything because he knelt first, and the Priests of the Axis in the Senoth Kingdom have built four centuries of doctrine on that one sentence.

### The Record

The Hobgoblin Imperium read his strain as divine hierarchy and derived the Cycle of Obedience from it, *all below must bear the one above*. The dwarves of Varūn raised the Adamant Axis directly above his metaphysical heart and pulled gravity harmonics off his breath in resonant chambers, forging weapons that no levitation or anti-gravity working has ever lifted. Guild surveyors place every recorded Temporal Rupture along the leyline arcs that trace his vertebrae beneath the crust. When he shifts in dream, centuries pass in seconds, or the reverse.

### Bonds

Aegor, Body, Epoch. Grants structure and purpose. Bairos, Fate, Epoch. Imbues inevitability and direction. Both named figures are Epochs and are correctly seated as such.

### The Titan-Keyed Art: *The Bent Knee's Burden*

*Defensive · Petralon · Zenith+ · [Th] Foundation + [Len] Line*

**Trigger**  Cast only within a Domain whose gravitational or causal load is already under visible strain. A sagging structure. A buckling timeline.

**Effect**  Locks a Domain's gravity and causal sequence to Atla'zon's own kneeling burden. Everything inside gains immense structural and temporal stability, and pays for it by losing forward momentum. Falling objects behave as though gravity thickened. The people inside age slower than the people watching them from the edge, and both parties can tell.

**Measure**  Field-wide dilation caps at roughly 0.3x normal flow. Structural load tolerance roughly doubles.

**Cost**  The caster's own joints and breath take on the Titan's characteristic strain. It is a genuine physical burden for hours after release, whatever the poets do with it.

**Limit**  It cannot be raised over ground that is holding. The strain must already be visible or the working finds nothing to key against.

**Counter**  [Wy] Motion introduced at the field's edge propagates inward as a shockwave of restored momentum, snapping the locked constant back all at once instead of gradually. Everything the field was carrying arrives at the floor in the same instant.

**What nobody knows**  Why the dilation caps at the same ratio no matter who casts it. A Zenith practitioner and an Apex one produce the identical figure, and nothing else in the Codex behaves that way.

*Codex: [Th] Foundation + [Len] Line / Petralon / Zenith+ / Titan-Keyed. Method, Grade, Range and Medium open.*

## GAIA: The Living Mantle of Form

> *"Every birth is a memory of the first world."*

**Seat**  Prime Aetheric Titaness of the Material World. Anchors every other realm by permitting Essence to crystallize into weight and decay.

**Domain**  Matter, Form, Life, Stability, Evolution

**Ground**  The Wellspring of Form, called Corpus. A labyrinth of glowing strata beneath the crust where Essence condenses into crystalline ore. The source of Soul Crystal latticework.

**Wellsprings**  Four of the Sixty are ways her mantle shows up, every one of them matter finding its way back into a body or a seam. Wild: [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Coagula]], [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Coagulatio]], [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Materia Primordia]], [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Terranova]].

### Manifestation

A colossal woman woven of mountain and river, hair a cascade of lava and bloom, skin veined with silver ley-lines, eyes deep magma pools. Each breath she exhales births auroras across the upper atmosphere. Forests grow in her shadow. Oceans take their rhythm from her weeping.

### The Load

The equilibrium between the spiritual and physical strata. She invented mortality as a recycling mechanism, whatever the punishment-preachers say, and death now returns Essence to the Wellsprings instead of letting the world saturate. Her Cycle of Material Harmony is why a creature's Essence mirrors the ground it was born on. Dragons breathe flame because they were born in molten currents. Elves carry a chlorophyll resonance because their souls remembered Verdance.

### The Record

She descended with Atla'zon in the First Eon to shape the first crust around the Wellspring Core, and gave Florwyn's Realmsong weight, turning harmony into ecology. Mortals call it the First Sedimentation, the age when music became mountains. Her grief tore rifts across the land during the Great Spirit War, and the Essence Fossils date from her absorbing what the divine bled. Hobgoblin geomancers worshipped her as Karai the Forgeblooded and opened her ley veins badly enough to cause the Year of Shattered Hammers. When the Crevice opened her pulse slowed to one beat per thousand years, which the Marrowkin call the Long Heart Sleep. Seers now report tremors aligned to Wellspring awakenings. The Concord reads this as her testing whether mortals can steward matter unsupervised.

### Bonds

Thalen, Form. Foremost ally, the architect whose glyphs she embodies. Maelor, Memory. Gives matter history, making fossil and bone and soil into vessels of remembrance. Urion, Balance. Keeps her evolution following pattern instead of chaos.

### The Titan-Keyed Art: *Flesh Remembers Stone*

*Supplementary · Materia Primordia · Zenith+ · [Th] Foundation + [Ma] Recall*

**Trigger**  Direct skin contact with the target tissue, bone or soil. No reach, no medium, no proxy.

**Effect**  Crystallizes living Essence into Soul-Crystal-grade matter on contact. Flesh, bone and soil reforge directly into stable lattice, exactly as Gaia's own Wellspring does it beneath the crust. The affected matter keeps its original shape and takes on a visible mineral sheen and rigidity, so a hand crystallized mid-gesture stays a hand.

**Measure**  The crystallization front advances roughly one centimeter per second along tissue. Faster through soil and loose stone.

**Cost**  The caster's hands take the heaviest exposure. Repeated use produces genuine mineral deposits under the caster's own skin over a career, and every practitioner who has held the art past middle age can be identified by handshake.

**Limit**  It converts. It does not create. There must be existing matter in contact with the palm, and the working cannot reverse itself.

**Counter**  [Uur] Disruption breaks the lattice mid-formation. The sudden phase change back to soft tissue is its own kind of trauma, and the reversal is frequently worse than the completed conversion would have been.

**What nobody knows**  Why the crystallization front stops at scar tissue and routes around it. The Verdant Core has documented the behavior for three hundred years and has no account of it.

*Codex: [Th] Foundation + [Ma] Recall / Materia Primordia / Zenith+ / Titan-Keyed. Method, Grade, Range and Medium open.*

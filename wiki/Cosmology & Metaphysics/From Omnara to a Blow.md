# From Omnara to a Blow

> *Account for this fist. Begin at the beginning.*
>
> *The Guild of Measurewrights, second seal examination, the only question on the paper*

On a hot blue Thursday in the eel season, at the crown of Ambrel Bridge, with the market in full cry and the river running gold under the arches, a toll collector named Fitch told an eel-seller named Tam Hobday that her barrow now counted as a wagon. Tam was fifty-one, wide as a door, in scarlet stockings and a leather apron, and she had been pushing that barrow over that bridge since before Fitch's father met his mother. She set the handles down. She breathed in through her nose until the apron creaked, and an ember-orange coat came up off her shoulders, steady as a banked forge, and every eel in the barrow turned to face the other way. It ran down her right arm and stayed there. "TOLL THIS!" said Tam Hobday, and she hit him on the point of the jaw. He left through the back wall of his painted booth with a good part of the booth, in a spray of green planks and copper pennies and three of his teeth, and he went into the river forty feet down with his hair on fire. The bridge cheered until the pigeons left.

An examiner of the Guild happened to be eating a pie at the next stall. He went back up to the hall that afternoon, wiped the board, and wrote the question at the head of this page, and it has been the whole of the second seal paper ever since. Candidates get three hours. Most of them start with the fist and work up, and they run out of time somewhere around the hips.

What follows is the model answer, with the order kept and my own remarks let in. It walks the whole chain from the first thing there was down to Fitch's jaw, one step at a time, for anyone who has never opened these shelves. Where the world's own word has a plainer cousin in foreign books I give both. Every step ends at the page that goes deeper, and the table at the foot has the lot on one sheet.

## 1. Omnara

Before anything there was everything, with no parts in it. **Omnara** is the old archivists' name for that state, the Apeiron, the undivided source. It had no sequence or weight or awareness, because those are parts. Every law, every god and every soul that would ever be was in there as potential, with nothing yet to tell them apart.

*For Tam:* her fist has a knuckle end and a wrist end, and it happens before his jaw breaks. None of that was possible yet.

Go deeper: [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/The Making of the World/Omnara and the Division|Omnara and the Division]].

## 2. The Division and the three who rose

Omnara split, and no surviving record says why. Three currents came out of it, and three planes rose from the currents, and from each plane rose a being the registers call an **Epoch**. The nearest foreign words are *primordial* and *god*, and both undersell it.

| Plane | What it is | Who rose from it |
|---|---|---|
| **The Physical Plane** | Physical Reality: matter, event, body, mountain range | **Aegor**, the Stone-Father |
| **The Soul Plane** | Spiritual Reality: souls, the dead, what a thing means | **Ashura**, the Breath Between Worlds |
| **The Plane of Fate** | Every possibility, idea and concept, what has to be so before anything can happen: the law and construction of the world | **Bara**, also written Bairos, the Weaver Eternal |

The first two are the lower realities, and a person lives in both at once. Fate stands above them and is no reality at all. "Plane" is a courtesy, like calling a very old and dangerous man "sir".

*For Tam:* her arm is on the Physical Plane, the woman deciding to use it is on the Soul Plane, and the fact that a blow can land at all is up above both of them.

Go deeper: [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/The Making of the World/The Three Epochs|The Three Epochs]].

## 3. The Veil

Where one plane's law meets another's there has to be a border, and that border is the **Veil**. Think of a wineskin with a pleat stitched down the middle. The skin wraps both lower realities, and every realm anybody has ever named is somewhere inside it. The pleat, the **inner fold**, runs between Physical and Soul, thick in some places and thin in others, and it is what a crossing crosses. The skin's **outer face** is where the lower realities meet Fate, so a thread pulled down from Fate comes in through there.

*For Tam:* the fold is close enough that one woman can be on both sides of it without noticing, which is the ordinary condition of being alive.

Go deeper: [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/The Making of the World/The Veil|The Veil]], which has the map of the realms.

## 4. The Works and the Archons

Inside the Plane of Fate, before there was a before, Bara wrote the seven **Works**: the first grammar, the bare functions by which anything ends, stands, passes, unfolds, is known, is balanced or is whole. Out of the tension among the three Epochs came the fourteen **Archons**, and an Archon is a Work crowned, a law with somebody on the throne. The closest foreign phrase is *concept given a god*: Order, Chaos, Form, Time, Memory, Death and the rest. A **Hollow Throne** is the same law with nobody home, and it runs on regardless.

Most if not all of the Archons are Sovereign on all three planes. Bara stands above them, so they are the most powerful things that still act up there, which is a smaller claim than the shrines make of it.

*For Tam:* a jaw can break because Ruin is a function, and the jaw stays broken in the right order because Time has an owner.

Go deeper: [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Archons/The Fourteen Archons|The Fourteen Archons]], and [[The Disciplines/Schools and Bloodline Arts/Hataraki — The Works of the Plane of Fate/Hataraki — The Works of the Plane of Fate|Hataraki]] for the Works themselves.

## 5. The Titans

**Titans descend from Archons.** An Archon is a principle enthroned, and a Titan is the same principle bearing weight, come down out of the throne room and into the load, with an Epoch to forge it a back. There are fourteen. Atla'zon holds which way is down, Gaia holds what things are made of, Valmir holds permission to end. Foreign books say *world-bearer* or *primordial titan*, and for once the foreign books are close.

A Titan has no Stage and no ladder and walks no Path, and it does not hear you. Its near opposite is a **World Spirit**, a soul whose claim on the world has grown into a place, and that started out as somebody.

All of this makes a tidy order of authority, and the Guild asks for it on the paper. **Fate and the Works stand over everything.** Under them run Titanic Law, Archonic Law and Wellspring Law, which are three domains of authority and not three ranks. Everything anyone has ever done with power takes its permission from one of the three, and all three rest on Fate.

*For Tam:* Prometheus, who holds destruction as transformation, has never heard of her, and she is about to use him.

Go deeper: [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Fourteen Titans|The Fourteen Titans]].

## 6. The Sixty Wellsprings

Titans are the **Wellsprings** themselves, and the Sixty are their manifestations: each is one way a Titan's law shows up, small enough for a soul to live with. A burn, a lamp and a hot wind are all ways one fire shows up, and Prometheus shows up six ways on the register. Foreign books say *affinity* or *element*, and a Wellspring is bigger than either. It is a governing idea that changes how a soul uses its power, a lens laid over magic that was already potent.

Each has a **kind**, which names its home: **Wild** (thirty-three) to Aegor, **Spirit** (fifteen) to Ashura, **Fate** (six) to Bara, **Medium** (six) to the Veil, at home on all three. A kind is a home and never a lock. Places where one pools are Wellspring sites, or **Cores** in common speech, and people kill for them.

*For Tam:* she has lived for thirty years with **Pyreveil**, the Fire of Renewal, Wild, deep ember-orange. Whatever it touches it burns down so that something can stand up in its place. She came to it after the first barrow burned.

Go deeper: [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/The Sixty Wellsprings|The Sixty Wellsprings]].

## 7. Aether

The Division left offcuts. **Aether** is the part of Omnara nobody used, and every foreign book in the world calls it **mana**. Because it was never divided it still holds every possibility and has no nature of its own, so it will carry any idea: the same medium burns in one valley and grieves in the next. That is how a Wellspring reaches anybody.

Only a body can pull it in. The medium is endless, and any one stretch of ground's supply is not, so where you stand matters. Fighters say fat ground and lean ground, and will walk you half a league to get onto the one they like.

*For Tam:* a stone bridge over a warm river at midday, with a market's worth of lamps and frying pans on it, is decent ground for a fire-lens. She has been pulling from it for thirty years and it has never asked for a toll.

Go deeper: the end of [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/The Making of the World/Omnara and the Division|Omnara and the Division]], and [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Physical Account Two Sets of Books|Two Sets of Books]].

## 8. The soul

All three Epochs had a hand in a soul, so it has three layers. The **Soul Crystal** is the soul, and the scholars mean that literally.

| Layer | Given by | Where it sits | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Aether Shell** | Aegor's ground | The Physical World | Knows and works matter, force and heat |
| **Essence Core** | Ashura's breath | The Soul Plane | Holds identity, memory and Traits, and authors everything the soul does |
| **Attraction Layer** | Bara's thread | Reaching toward Fate | A soul's own share of what could be |

Everyone has all three, the people who never wake included, and so do beasts, though a beast's Shell stays sealed until evolution or a Level threshold cracks it. This Core has no relation to a Core on a map, whatever they tell you in the taverns. About one person in ten wakes their Crystal, nearly always as a child, and the rest live out their lives with a little sheet and a sleeping soul.

*For Tam:* she woke at eleven, gutting eels, and put the knife through the bench.

Go deeper: [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/The Making of the World/The Three Epochs|The Three Epochs]] and [[The Magic System/First Steps/How Magic Works|How Magic Works]].

## 9. Essence

Pull Aether into a woken Crystal and refine it, and it takes the stamp of whoever refined it. From then on it is **Essence**, which foreign books call **spiritual energy**. It is the soul's stamped blood, a little more of you than there was. The store you hold is your **reserve**, and it is measured, spent and watched like money. The unwoken hold a trickle they cannot spend. Below a tenth of reserve the Core itself begins to fray, names and memory first, and that is what fighters mean by spending themselves. A fight Draft's Essence comes out of the bottle unstamped, and the crash that follows is the stamping.

*For Tam:* every copper's worth of that coat is Tam Hobday, and anyone who has stood near her in a temper would know it blind.

Go deeper: [[The Magic System/Fracture of Worlds — The Living System/IX. The Essence Ledger (Part Twenty-Three)|The Essence Ledger]].

## 10. The climb

A soul grows by being melted down and rebuilt. That is **tempering**, and each rebuilding toward the next of the sixteen **Stages** (a cultivation realm, in foreign books) needs its **Catalyst**, a crisis or a long seclusion or a bought Draft. A person tempers on a Wellspring, and that lens shapes their Essence, reaching double its effect after years of mastery at home. The lens tempered on longest sets the color of their aura. **Level** is separate, and moves by danger overcome. Along the way a soul lays down **Traits**, standing laws formed by what it survived.

The harmonization that ties a soul to a lens happens in three places, said once. The lock happens at the Shell, where Aether enters. The tie is held on the Layer. The lens shapes Essence in whichever layer the soul leans on.

*For Tam:* she is at Stage VI, Glory, reached the hard way across two floods and a fire. At Glory the soul first offers a named class, and hers offered her Barrow Queen, and she told it what it could do with that and then took it.

Go deeper: [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Sixteen Stages|The Sixteen Stages]], and [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Ladder and the Draft — Temperance, Advancement and Essence Drafts|The Ladder and the Draft]].

## 11. The Path

Every soul has all three layers, and a **Path** is the one it has learned to lean on, hardened by repetition until it stops being a choice. There are three roads, and each ends under the Epoch who gave that layer.

| Path | Leans on | Works on | Foreign books say | Far end |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Body** | Aether Shell | Matter, force, heat, the blow | Physical enhancement, then matter manipulation | [[The Disciplines/Named Arts/Matla\|Matla]] |
| **Spirit** | Essence Core | Souls, the dead, what a thing means | Soul manipulation, conceptual attack | [[The Disciplines/Schools and Bloodline Arts/Null-Light\|Null-Light]] |
| **Fate** | Attraction Layer | Odds, prerequisites, bonds, oaths | Fate and probability manipulation | [[The Disciplines/Schools and Bloodline Arts/Hataraki — The Works of the Plane of Fate/Hataraki — The Works of the Plane of Fate\|Hataraki]] |

No road is the better one, and each has a clear loss against the others. Each layer also has its own ladder, read from Glory up: **Aether Class**, **Essence Class** and **Thread Class**. A ladder rung sets **η**, the share of each spend that arrives as effect. Stage sets how high a ladder can go and practice moves you up it, except for Thread, which goes by grip and decades and leaves most souls at nothing for life.

*For Tam:* Body, Mastered (her one road, walked as far as her Stage goes), because she has never in her life wanted anything to happen from a distance. Aether I, Muridic, the first rung: she commands matter as an object, it takes a good deal of noise to do it, and something over a third of what she spends never reaches the fist.

Go deeper: [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Three Paths Routing, Recognition and the Gate|The Three Paths]] and [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/Classes and the Three Ladders|Classes and the Three Ladders]].

## 12. The blow

Now we can hit him. Here is that half second, in order.

1. **She draws in.** The breath is the visible charge, and anyone can read it. Aether comes up out of the bridge and the warm air into her body.
2. **It is refined.** In the Crystal it becomes Essence and takes her stamp, and Pyreveil lays its idea over it.
3. **It goes out through the Shell,** since that is the layer she leans on, and shows as a coat of her own aura, the skin of power called [[The Magic System/Power in Use/Reinforcement|reinforcement]]. She gathers the whole coat into her right arm, so one place goes up a Grade (a letter on the F to EX scale that stats are read in) and all the rest of her drops to bare skin. Fitch could have killed her with a bread knife in that moment, had it occurred to him.
4. **The call.** "TOLL THIS!" is a technique name, for her purposes, and shouting it buys a surge for the beat it takes. It is also the loudest tell in the Quarter.
5. **The body does its work.** The left heel plants on the crown of the bridge, her hips turn, the shoulder comes through last, and all that weight goes along a straight line into the point of the jaw. On the Body Path the full force bonus lands at contact, which is why she had to walk up to him.
6. **Both things hit.** The fist breaks the jawbone at the hinge. Pyreveil lives in the tissue on this road, so the Essence goes in with the knuckles as heat, and heat keeps doing what heat does after she has stopped thinking about it. That is why his hair was on fire, and why it was still on fire in the river for about as long as his hair lasted. A **glyph**, had she known one, would have set a boundary on that law and told it where to stop. She has never known one.
7. **The waste.** The third or more that never reached the fist came off her as glare and roar and heat. Every eel turned away for the same reason. Waste is loud and hot, and mastery is quiet.
8. **The price.** Some of her reserve is gone, the knuckles are split, and the breath is short. Her window, which is the soul reporting its numbers to its owner and nobody else, has it all down:

```
[TOLL THIS: landed, jaw.]
[Reserve: 71% ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓░░░]
[Hit: right knuckles split. Vitality 97%]
```

That is the whole of the machinery. Every other blow in these shelves is this one with different numbers, and [[The Magic System/First Steps/One Exchange, Annotated|One Exchange, Annotated]] takes a real bout through it line by line.

A fighter who keeps going climbs further: a **Release** is a full transformation of what the lead layer does, and a **Domain** (a reality marble, in foreign books) is a claim made on the Attraction Layer that makes a stretch of the world run on the fighter's terms. Tam has a Release she does not know about, and the river towns would prefer that it stayed that way.

Go deeper: [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/Release Forms|Release Forms]] and [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/Domains|Domains]].

## 13. The same fist on the other two roads

The examiner gives a bonus mark for this, because the chain forks only here. Everything from Omnara to step 11 is the same for all three.

**On the Spirit Path** she does not need to walk up. The working is authored in the Core and goes out there, reads Essence Class, and carries the author's say on what a thing is. A Spirit master at Stage VI standing at the far end of the bridge could write across the booth until the whole structure forgot what a toll was for, and Fitch's jaw would be perfectly fine and he would be unable to remember why he was standing there. The room would show almost nothing. No armor covers a soul, and a hitter who reaches that master ends the discussion.

**On the Fate Path** nobody hits anyone. Fitch's stool has a worn leg, the booth has an old nail, and the wind is off the river. A true pull moves what the thing depends on, comes down through the Veil's outer face, and takes Thread Class on top of everything. Low down it is one lean on the odds, which may take half the reserve and leaves the puller bleeding from the nose across the street, and it often fails. It also moves only what already has a way of happening, so a well-kept booth with a sound stool stands.

Go deeper: the round of who beats whom on [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Three Paths Routing, Recognition and the Gate|The Three Paths]].

## 14. The two books

The Guild records every working twice. The **physical account** says what happened to matter in the room, and a clerk with no soul to speak of can take it with a fork and a water clock. The **stratal account** says which Wellspring's idea governed the soul, what the Aether was doing, and what the Crystal paid. Both have to close.

Only the Shell's loss is heat. Loss through the Core or the Layer is paid in that layer, and the blood and the shaking are the report of that payment. A nosebleed does not carry the energy, and that goes on the paper too.

*For Tam:* Fitch, the booth, the pennies and the river are the physical book. The reserve, the lens and the thirty years are the other one. They balance.

Go deeper: [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Physical Account Two Sets of Books|Two Sets of Books]].

## 15. And back again

What three hands put together goes back three ways. When a Crystal comes apart, the **Core goes to Echo**, which leaves for the Soul Plane within hours. The **Shell goes to residue**, which takes nine days to leave through the body and repays the Wellspring. The **Layer goes to Name**, filed in the Archive and never released. A person's Path sets which of the three is heaviest: Body dead are what the renderers race for, Spirit dead haunt, and Fate dead leave vows still binding. The unlensed feed the ground they lie in. Pattern may come round again in a bloodline, and a person never does. Every death stays permanent.

*For Tam:* she died in her bed at eighty-three with her boots on, against medical advice. The family sat with her until the room changed, as every family does, then took it in shifts with two hired spears and a very large dog for the nine days, because a Body master's corpse is worth more than the street it lies on, and she had told them to. What she owed went back to Pyreveil, which is one way Prometheus shows up, whose law came down from a throne the registers still argue over, which is a Work that Bara wrote in the plane that rose from what Omnara stopped being. Fitch's successor charges barrows at the handcart rate. There is a small painted sign to that effect at the crown of Ambrel Bridge, in orange, and a burnt place on the coping where nobody has ever got round to replacing the stone.

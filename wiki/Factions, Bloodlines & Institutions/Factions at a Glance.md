title: Factions at a Glance
---

*Who holds what across the four quarters, what each wants, and where a stranger first runs into them.*

A stranger who walks into a market town in any year of this age can be fined by one power, hanged by a second, hired by a third and cheered from a bridge by a fourth before the noon bell, and a fair number have been.

Most ground in the Dark Ages is ruled three times over. A crown or great house holds the land, the levy and the right to hang. A chartered company (a sworn partnership of merchants licensed by a crown, its backers holding shares in its ventures) holds the trade. The guilds hold the hunters, the makers, the dealers and the license, and most of them have signed on to the Guild Accord, a coalition of many kinds of guild across many nations, which buys its members a shared rank, contracts that bind in any court it keeps, and passage on the road. It holds no land and swears it keeps no army, and its courts are faster than anyone's. Under and around all of it run the sects, with their colors and their brackets. Every power below wants some share of the same five things: Wells (sites where one kind of Aether pools, mostly in dungeons, and where practitioners fill fast), materials, markets, routes and the hunting trade.

## Ranks

- **The guilds of the Accord share nine titles,** the same in every hall: Initiate, Apprentice, Journeyman, Adept, Expert, Master, Grandmaster, Archmaster and Paragon. A title grades trust in a trade: a merchant may deal up to it, a craftsman make up to it, an adventurer take commissions up to the same Threat Tier, and a Grandmaster merchant may not be able to throw a punch. Fighting guilds keep the Stage table as their yardstick, so only their titles also tell you how dangerous someone is. It travels as a silver token, and a forged token is a great crime.
- **Inside a company,** membership runs apprentice, freeman and liveryman, with a Master, Wardens and a self-appointing Court above them. That is your standing at the company's own table, and the token is your standing with strangers.
- **Crews** are rated in the board's seal colors (green, blue, gold and obsidian) and chalked on each hall's Wall. The Accord keeps a Roll, heralds cry their own, bookies keep odds, and every realm and sect has its own list. They all disagree, and getting on one makes you a target.
- **Sects, schools and teaching houses** (the sects, the Aetherion Academy, the lineage halls, and the Moto, Mahuo, Yukari and Zettari houses) rank by discipleship: outer disciples, inner disciples, core disciples and the elders above them. A disciple rises by ranking contests and the elders' word. Rank opens the school's guarded secrets and its stores, and it demands duty in return. A disciple who leaves with the school's methods is hunted for what they know. At Aetherion the contest is open: Class X holds the top seats and Class S wants them.
- **Academy credentials** are what a disciple carries out under the Accord's charter: Scholar, then Licentiate (the license to practice for hire, and the one that matters), Master, Doctor and Chair.
- **Sancta Lux** ranks by what a member may do with a reading: Attestant witnesses, Measurewright reads, Lector finds, Arbiter sentences, with fewer than thirty Archpaladins (those who sentence at the largest scale) above them.

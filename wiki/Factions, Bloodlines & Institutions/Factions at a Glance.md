title: "Factions at a Glance"
---

*Who holds what across the four quarters, what each wants, and where a stranger first runs into them.*

Most ground in the Imperial Age is ruled three times over. A crown or great house holds the land, the levy and the right to hang. A chartered company (a share-holding venture licensed by a crown) holds the trade. The guilds hold the hunters and the licence. Over all three sits the Guild Accord (the communion of guilds, academies and orders that grades practitioners and keeps the courts of the road), which holds no land and keeps no army but whose courts are faster than anyone's. Every power below wants some share of the same five things: Wells (sites where the world's power pools and can be drawn), materials, markets, routes and the hunting trade.

## Ranks

- **Schools and teaching houses** (the Aetherion Academy, the lineage halls, and the Moto, Mahuo, Yukari and Zettari houses) rank by discipleship: outer disciples, inner disciples and the elders above them. A disciple rises by ranking contests and the elders' word. Rank opens the school's guarded secrets and its stores, and it demands duty in return. A disciple who leaves with the school's methods is hunted for what they know. At Aetherion the contest is open: Class X holds the top seats and Class S wants them.
- **The guilds keep their own ladder:** apprentice, journeyman and master in the craft. The Accord grades every sworn practitioner on nine Tiers of Standing: Initiate, Apprentice, Journeyman, Adept, Expert, Master, Grandmaster, Archmaster and Paragon.
- **Academy credentials** are what a disciple carries out under the Accord's charter: Scholar, then Licentiate (the licence to practise for hire, and the one that matters), Master, Doctor and Chair.
- **Sancta Lux** ranks by what a member may do with a reading: Attestant witnesses, Measurewright reads, Lector finds, Arbiter sentences, with fewer than thirty Archpaladins (those who sentence at the largest scale) above them.

*The old blood and the great houses: who holds the land, the seats and the inheritance.*

At the Kessen horse fair three heralds in three sets of colors once reached the same plank bridge in the same moment, each with his house's precedence in his mouth, and by the time the matter had been settled with staves the plank was in the river and a Yan stud-master was renting out places on the bank. Everything on this shelf is that argument at a larger size: who goes first, who says so, and who is selling seats.

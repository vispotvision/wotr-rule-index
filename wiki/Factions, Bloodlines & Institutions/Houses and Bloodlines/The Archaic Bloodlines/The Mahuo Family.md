# The Mahuo Family

The line is written two ways, and both are correct. **Mahou** is the line as an institution, the lineage the Zettari–Mahou Accord recognises and binds; **Mahuo** is the surname a person of that line bears, which is why the Accord can name Ara Min Mahuo, of the Mahou line, in a single breath.

# The Moto Origin — The Western Reach

**Say it** · MOH-toh

*The western reach of the northern shield.*
> The Moto origin is **the western reach of the Inner World's northern shield, and the Old World has no claim on it.**
>
> **There is no Sātūlagi.** The Malō of Sātūlagi does not exist and never did. **The Moto hold one seat and it is Kharven.**

Hrolf Oddway, a chartered surveyor with a loud mouth and a good red cloak, bet a whole inn at Kessen a month of his wages that he could spend one night leaning on the old Moto gatepost and come back bored. He came back on the third day in the same cloak with frost in his beard, swearing oaths under his breath in a phonology nobody at the inn could place, and he swore them for the rest of his life, in his sleep and at his own wedding. His wife paid the bet. The ground he had leaned on is the subject of this page.

---

## The Ground

Past the ridgeline that now marks Kharven's border with **Undaar-Keth**, on ground no crown claims and no border commission patrols. Old cold shield, glaciated, **rich in ambient Essence and with no walls, roads or keepers to use it**, which is the Inner World's profile everywhere, and is why nothing has been built there since.

### The seat

**Standing, ruined, depopulated.** Broken structures, a well, a gatepost, frost on it most of the year.
> **A woman of the house, Somnalis-primary and counted on nobody's roll, has lived alone in a cabin there for the last stretch of her life**, and no longer touches a wall without reading what happened against it, **because the density of residual memory on that ground exceeds anything else in her record.**

### The displacement

The first blow on the white shelf, about 310 IC, which the Moto registers call the split, made the seat untenable. **The six lines scattered toward temperate ground**, as [[Factions, Bloodlines & Institutions/Houses and Bloodlines/The Archaic Bloodlines/The Moto Bloodline/The Long Reading — Moto Ancient History|The Long Reading]] tells it. Kharven-Seat already stood in the boreal north, over clans who had never heard of the place the blood came from, when Muken came up to it generations later, **on reasoning that was strategic before it was sentimental**, and took its crown. The great split proper is still to come after him, about 740 IC, and he lives through it.

## What This Means For The House

> **Every Moto seat in the current era is superimposed on somebody else's ground.**
>
> Kharven is Concord-stock northern clans whose roots predate the Moto arrival by centuries. **This is why the Banner Houses carry northern names and the crown does not**, and why a Stark saying the King's name aloud **is pronouncing a foreign word in his own hall.**
>
> The Moto never migrated as a nation. **They migrated as a founding house and its retainers.** A people that relocates brings farmers. **A house that relocates brings law.** *This is why they superimpose everywhere they land and assimilate nowhere.*

# The Houses of the Inner World

Ask a Guild clerk how many great houses the Inner World holds and you will get a number, a sigh and a request that you take it up with the herald. These are the ones the clerks can't avoid. Each holds something the rest cannot do without, and each has one thing that belongs to it and nobody else, which is usually the first thing anyone learns about them and the last thing they understand.

A house gives its children four things at most: an eye, if it has one; a grip, which opens one ladder early and never lifts its cap; a lean in the stats; and a liking for certain Wellsprings. It never gives a Path, because the Crystal only recognizes what a person has actually done. Three lines stand on an Epoch's own work, and each of those has both an eye and an instrument. The rest stand on what they have built, which in several cases is more than the three would like.

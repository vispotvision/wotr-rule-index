# Merit and Severance

*Filed under the Codex of Absolute Magical Law, Article X, "On Worth and Wages of Service," and Article XI, "On Betrayal, Dissolution, and the Price of Unbinding."*
> *"Where coin fades, Merit endures."*
>
> *The Codex. Chalked beside it in most hiring halls: "Where's my coin?"*

Everybody in the coalition is paid. The trouble is that it has two payrolls. A signed member, a mercer or a hunter or a free company's drummer, is paid in coin, shares and letters of credit, and if she cheats she is struck from the roll and nobody will deal with her. A **sworn** member, who has given a fragment of the soul to the Concord Lattice, is paid as well in something the old Codex calls **Resonance Merit**, and she can be struck from something a good deal older than a roll. This page holds both, because both hang on the same hall wall.

---

## What the Signed Are Paid

**Shares, bounties and fees.** A crew takes shares in a venture, a craftsman takes the price of the work, a courier takes carriage, a free company takes its contract, all of it entered in ink and enforceable in any signed market court.

**Rights that come with the title.** A token opens what its numeral covers: the board up to its Threat Tier, the stall up to its Rarity, the forge up to its Grade, the Long Relay's stables, the hall's bed at the hall's price. A rising hunter also gets entries on the roll, and those matter more than she thinks. A hall chooses who gets a patron's Draft and who gets a hard crew by reading its roll, and the poor rise largely by being worth somebody's bet.

**The window and the roll are different documents.** Level moves by danger overcome, and it shows on your own window where nobody else can check it. The roll holds what your hall will swear to. They need not agree. A crew's luggage who landed the killing bolt will have the Level and none of the entry, and the argument has broken up better crews than any monster has.

## Temperance Merits

For the sworn, each successful Commission also grants Merit Points, commonly called **Echo Marks**, held in the Lattice and imprinted upon the Soul Crystal as **faint glyph-lines visible only under Concord light.** In the Dark Ages they are **entries** first of all, in ink, by the Bench's power of entry on the roll, and the glyph-lines follow. The more difficult the mission, the stronger the resonance engraved into the soul.

| Tier | Recognition, for the sworn |
|---|---|
| **Initiate** | Minor resonance. Proof of first steps upon the Tiered Path |
| **Apprentice** | Small merit increases; early recognition by guild instructors and mentors |
| **Journeyman** | True harmonic trace. Proof of independent capability |
| **Adept** | The first to earn the formal title of **Concord Agent** |
| **Expert** | **Sealwright** designation available. Trusted with enforcement authority |
| **Master** | Elevated to **Warden of Duty.** Authorized to lead large-scale operations |
| **Grandmaster** | Named **Envoy of the Lattice.** Recognized across realms |
| **Archmaster** | **Bearer of the Concord Flame.** Living arbiter between mortal and divine law |
| **Paragon** | Named **Chosen of the Codex.** Name permanently inscribed in the Lattice Ledger as recipient of Zenith Decree |

## Essence Compensation

Beyond Merit, sworn operatives are compensated through what the Codex calls **Aether Credits**: draughts (the keepers' unit of time at a Core's pool) and letters of credit against a factor house, each **encoded with one's personal signature** and exchangeable for equipment, reagents, or relic access at any hall.

**Low-Tier** · Drafts, spirit shards, training sigils. **Mid-Tier** · relic vouchers, rare materials, research licenses. **High-Tier** · divine artifacts, personal Wellspring rights, a top tempering Draft out of the Accord's own stores, or political elevation within the Accord.
> Because each sworn credit is soul-bound, theft or falsification invokes **Essence Reversal**, a punishment that drains the thief's resonance **until the debt is repaid in spirit or death.** Forging a signed member's letter merely gets you hanged.

### Advancement rights

**Relic Rights** · permission to wield or bond with Concord-sanctioned artifacts. **Wellspring Visitation** · access to active Wellspring sites for study or attunement. **Gate Access** · travel through the Concord Gate Network. **Ritual Command** · authorization to lead Essence Sealing, Purification, and Binding rites. **Political Authority** · eligibility to serve as Envoy, Tribunal Judge, or Council Advisor.

All rights are granted through Arbitration following a Temperance evaluation and the signing of the **Oath of Merit.**

### Merit conversion and legacy

Merit can be spent, traded, or transmuted into **Concord Favor**, a political currency used by guilds and nations. A single high-Tier Merit may equate to months of negotiation leverage or a lifetime of recognition. But:
>
> > *"Merit spent is memory lost."*
>
> Excessive conversion **weakens the soul's resonance**, and many veterans retire before their Essence begins to fade from overuse.
>
> Those who die in service become part of the **Lattice Choir**, fragments of harmonic memory that continue to echo through the Guild network. Their names are sung each solstice within the halls of the High Concordant.
>
> > *"The Concord does not pay in coin. It pays in remembrance."*

The hiring halls sing their dead too, at a different volume, in the tap-room, and the song usually gets the details wrong.

## What a Hall Can Do to You

**Struck.** A hall or a court strikes your title. The news goes down the road faster than you can, and by the next market nobody will hold your coat.

**Excluded.** A hall struck from the roll loses every token it ever issued. Its weights no longer pass, its letters no longer clear, the rider stops coming. This is the Accord's real weapon, and it has emptied whole towns with no one raising a hand.

**Reserved.** The Bench of Attribution withholds a standing pending determination. A person under reservation is not condemned and cannot trade, which is worse.

**Hanged, by someone else.** The Accord cannot execute anybody, and it is rarely short of a crown that will. A forged token goes this way more often than not.

## The Nine Laws of Severance

The Codex holds that it is law, etched into the fabric of the Lattice itself, and for those who have sworn to it this is a plain description of what happens. **A signed member who has given the Lattice nothing has nothing for it to take**, and faces only the hall and the court above. For the sworn, when a member violates that truth, the Lattice responds. **It restores balance, and vengeance does not enter into it.**

| Law | Trigger | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **I · Fracture of Oath** | Breaking a direct promise under the Concord seal | The Soul Crystal fractures along its outer ring; Lattice connection dims. Minor fractures cause Essence sickness or amnesia. **Severe fractures cause Lattice Silence**, the inability to channel or perceive Essence at all |
| **II · Seal of Rejection** | Willful defiance of a Concord order, or falsifying a mission report | Branded with a **Reject Sigil**, visible to all who bear Accord sight. Prevents use of any sanctioned Wellspring site, relic, or Gate. The branded are known as **Forsworn.** *Many wander until they wither, for the world itself rejects their resonance* |
| **III · Brand of Corruption** | Tampering with or corrupting a Wellspring site without sanction | Essence inverted; **emotion becomes toxicity.** Enforcement is authorized to enact **Essence Severance**, removing the corrupted portion of the soul by force. *Survivors rarely remain whole.* If corruption spreads to the site itself, the accused's life is forfeit |
| **IV · Parunic Silence** | Speaking forbidden glyphs, teaching the Divine Language without sanction, unsanctioned Aether drafts | **Silencing**, removal of one's voice in body and spirit. Their speech becomes inaudible to all beings who still bear Essence, **rendering them ghosts within reality** |
| **V · The Concord Trial** | Guilt uncertain | A **Trial of Resonance** in the Neutral Chambers under the Ribs at Sealhall, **where lies cannot survive.** The soul's harmony is measured: if it hums true, absolved; if discordant, **the Lattice itself convicts them.** The Codex holds that this trial supersedes all mortal courts and that its verdict is final. The crown courts dispute both wherever they reach the accused first |
| **VI · Excision of Memory** | Crimes regrettable but redeemable: relic theft, negligence, desertion | **Cleansing through Forgetting.** A Sealwright removes the crime and its motive from the soul. The offender begins anew, **but at the cost of self.** *Some accept this willingly, calling it the Second Birth* |
| **VII · Shadow Absorption** | Proving useful but irredeemably dangerous | Conscription into the **Black Concord.** Names, faces and records erased; memories rewritten for covert service. Only the High Concordant knows their true identity. **They are called Shades of the Lattice, and they are both prisoner and weapon** |
| **VIII · Total Severance** | Betraying the Accord's foundation: attempted divine ascension, mass destruction of Wellspring sites, inter-realm rebellion | The soul is exiled from the Lattice entirely, **erased from existence, with more than mere death in it.** No resurrection, no record, no echo. To the Concord, they *never were.* **Even memory recoils from their name** |
| **IX · Balance Restored** | Judgment concluded | Remaining Essence is absorbed by the Lattice and redistributed among the Wellspring sites. **Even in death, every punishment feeds the order that governs reality.** *The Concord does not destroy to avenge; it destroys to balance* |

A sworn person feels the breach before anyone else knows of it: a Lattice oath is a vow, and the window shows its terms, and a broken vow lands at once. The Measurewrights who have studied the Second Birth report that the window keeps a blank line where the excised crime was, and that some people spend the rest of their lives looking at it.

### Notes of redemption

Though the Accord is absolute, mercy is not forgotten. A soul may regain connection to the Lattice through service, confession, or **the sacrifice of one's life to defend a Wellspring site.** This process, called **Resonant Penance**, is perilous, *many attempt it, few return.* Those who do are marked with **a faint silver sigil over the heart**, denoting a Soul Rewoven.

### Enforcement and oversight

Punishments are administered jointly by Arbitration and Enforcement under the **Prime Arbiter.** High-level Severances require Concord Council sanction or direct approval from the High Concordant. **All judgments are witnessed by three officials**, a Seer, a Sealwright, and a Scribe, whose Essences intertwine to confirm the act in the Lattice Ledger.
> *"The Accord forgives only those who restore balance. For law is not vengeance; it is remembrance made divine."*

# The Eight Great Houses of Kushara

*The Sovereign House and the Seven Pillars beneath the Kusharan Throne. Old World continental order.*

### The Structure

The Old World is too large, too old and too geographically various to be governed by Kusharan officials alone. Deserts, rainforests, savannas, riftlands, river basins, mineral plateaus and southern seas each require rulers who know the land well enough to act without waiting weeks on instruction from the Crownlands.
The continental order beneath Dawn Guard is therefore built around eight houses. **They are not equal.** At their head stands **House Zettari**, holding the continental throne. Beneath it stand the **Seven Pillars**, each bearing one structure the Old World cannot do without: armies, gold, food, medicine, strategic minerals, maritime trade, northern water.
Several of the Seven ruled independent kingdoms, confederacies, tribal territories, mining domains, river cities or merchant states before incorporation. Dawn Guard did not destroy those identities. He bound them into a system.
> Seven pillars hold the continent. The Zettari Crown decides what they hold together.

### 1. House Zettari — The Sovereign House

**Seat:** Kushara · **Territory:** The Kusharan Crownlands · **Epithet:** The Sovereign House
**Additional titles:** House of the Living Throne · Blood of the Crownlands · Keepers of Kushara
**Continental duty:** Sovereignty, continental law, supreme defence, inter-house arbitration, Royal Roads, Path stewardship
**Greatest asset:** The strongest sovereign bloodline and military lineage in the Old World
House Zettari is not merely the family currently occupying the throne. It is the political, martial, ancestral and metaphysical foundation the modern Old World is organised around, reaching backward past Dawn Guard's consolidation into Material Aspect Sovereignty, the Everlasting Eyes, the Paths, ancestral recognition and the rites of Kushara.
The other houses possess something the continent requires. House Zettari possesses the authority that makes those things a *system* rather than seven competing regional powers.
Its territory covers the Crownlands surrounding the River Kesh: high savanna, fertile valleys, ancient plateaus, royal estates, military grounds, Ancestral Halls, Path-recognised sites and the city of Kushara itself. Its wealth is considerable and its military strength greater, but neither is the foundation. The foundation is sovereignty — power understood as the ability to make a claim survive contradiction. Material Aspect doctrine concerning Form, Essence, Space, Existence and localised Sequence shapes Zettari law as much as Zettari combat, deeply enough that non-Zettari administrators describe continental law in vocabulary borrowed from it.
**The Sovereign Line.** Dawn Guard — Zuberi Zettari — is sovereign head of the house and ruler of Kushara, with authority reaching across nearly every recognised political territory of the Old World. Supremacy does not mean local rulers have been replaced by Zettari governors; that would make the continent weaker. The sovereign house sits above the regional houses as arbiter, military guarantor, continental judge and final authority when one region's interests threaten another's.
The throne is expected to intervene when two Great Houses reach an unresolved dispute, a regional ruler violates continental compact, a major Royal Road becomes unsafe, invasion threatens multiple territories, Wellspring catastrophe exceeds regional capacity, trade restrictions threaten stability, one house attempts to monopolise resources essential to another, or a crisis concerns the Old World as a whole.
**The Zettari military.** N'Kharu can field far more ordinary soldiers. This does not make N'Kharu stronger. **N'Kharu provides mass; Zettari provides supremacy.** Zettari forces are considerably smaller — professional warriors, royal guards, Zettari Ken practitioners, Material Aspect specialists, sovereign bloodline combatants — operating at levels ordinary levy formations cannot reproduce. The **Kusharan Royal Host** forms the permanent core surrounding the throne: Crownland infantry, palace guards, reconnaissance, royal engineers, river forces, mounted companies, specialists sworn to the sovereign rather than a provincial lord.
In continental war Kushara does not replace the forces of the Seven. It becomes the command spine binding them.
**The Paths.** The Crownlands hold the strongest known stable Path recognition in the Old World, where ancestral rites, sovereign blood, family cords, historical standing, Material recognition and ancient ground intersect. The Paths are neither a public resource nor a private family privilege; they are an ancestral jurisdiction whose misuse could endanger things considerably older than the throne. House Zettari protects Path-sensitive sites, ancestral sanctuaries, sovereign burial grounds and the locations where the boundary between Kushara and the ancestral plane becomes unusually responsive.
> The Seven keep the Old World alive. Zettari keeps it one.

### 2. House N'Kharu — The Thousand Spears

**Seat:** Khar'Mbara · **Territory:** The M'Bala Great Savannas and southern Kharu Belt
**Continental duty:** Armies, cavalry, migration-road security, central defence
**Greatest asset:** The largest bannermen host in the Old World
The military giant among the Pillars. Its grasslands are among the most populous in the Old World, holding farming towns, pastoral communities, warrior clans, fortified settlements, royal estates and old provincial families. The openness of the M'Bala permits rapid mustering and long-distance mounted movement.
At full continental levy N'Kharu can call approximately **forty thousand bannermen** — not forty thousand standing soldiers, but household warriors, cavalry retainers, regional companies, hunters, scouts, village militias and provincial troops bound to answer the banners.
Its permanent professionals are the **Red Spear Companies**, who patrol the Twelve Migration Roads, break bandit confederacies, protect settlements from large predators, escort royal messengers, manage dangerous herd movements and reinforce wherever the central territories require it. N'Kharu cavalry is among the finest conventional mounted force on the continent; their savanna coursers combine endurance, speed, heat tolerance and the ability to cross difficult seasonal terrain.
Its position west and southwest of the Crownlands is also a vast defensive buffer. Any hostile force approaching Kushara through the central continent must first contend with territories that raise armies faster than nearly anywhere else.
> Zettari wins the battle that decides the war. N'Kharu makes certain there is still an army afterward.

### 3. House Vokari — Keepers of the Golden Vein

**Seat:** Vorak-Tel · **Territory:** The Auric Plateau and eastern Golden Back
**Continental duty:** Precious metals, treasury support, deep mining, mineral law
**Greatest asset:** The richest gold mines in the Old World
Where N'Kharu provides bodies, Vokari provides the wealth that sustains them. Beyond gold, its domains yield platinum, silver, cobalt, manganese, diamonds, Harmonic ore, chromium and occasional star-metal fragments. The **Golden Veins** are not one mine but an interconnected mineral system running beneath multiple valleys and canyon formations.
Vokari extraction law treats mining as a form of spiritual surgery. Seams intersect buried Wellspring currents, Memory Stone, ancient ruins and Three-Plane alignments, so reckless extraction damages more than geology. Vokari surveyors have abandoned mines worth fortunes because going deeper would destabilise something beneath them.
Vokari wealth underwrites sovereign coinage, military procurement, continental infrastructure, emergency reserves, ceremonial gold, royal construction, Zettari regalia, ancestral restoration and high-level magical instruments. The house maintains the **Deep Surveyors**, the continent's finest specialists in identifying buried Wellspring activity, geological anomalies, ancient structures and dangerous mineral formations.
> N'Kharu raises the army. Vokari keeps it paid.

### 4. House Malani — The Hands Beneath the Bowl

**Seat:** Malara-Nahal · **Territory:** The Golden Floodplain, Sundi Basin, central Nahal valleys
**Continental duty:** Agriculture, irrigation, river management, famine prevention
**Greatest asset:** The largest food-production network in the Old World
Malani governs the resource every throne eventually discovers matters more than gold. Its lands follow the Nahal River System across floodplains, wetland farms, island communities, livestock regions, fisheries, irrigation districts, orchards and grain valleys. Its power is measured not by how its soldiers look but by how long a region survives when the rains fail.
The house keeps the **Flood Calendar**, an archive combining rainfall, sediment, mountain snowmelt, water colour, Wellspring behaviour, animal migration, river depth and centuries of flood records. Malani engineers use reservoirs, spill channels, gates, canals, flood fields and artificial wetlands to turn destructive river expansion into agricultural renewal.
Malani barges carry grain, fish, preserved meat, fruit, oils, livestock, river salt, medicinal reeds and emergency drinking water toward the capital continuously, and royal strategic granaries are maintained with Malani assistance. When M'Bala dries, when Zamaran storms destroy crops, when northern settlements lose harvests, Malani reserves are redirected under sovereign command.
> Crowns rule men. Malani decides whether those men eat.

### 5. House Obaru — Wardens of the Living Green

**Seat:** Obar-Kele · **Territory:** Nyoka Rainsea and western Cloudwall foothills
**Continental duty:** Medicine, rare plants, timber, rainforest roads, ecological stewardship
**Greatest asset:** The greatest medicinal and biological resource network in the Old World
Obaru borders rarely follow straight lines. Jurisdiction follows rivers, groves, canopy roads, ancestral territories, settlements, living boundary trees and recognised forest corridors through the Nyoka Rainsea.
Its wealth is biological: medicinal compounds, poisons, rare fibres, resins, fungi, spirit amber, Memory bark, bloodwood and living materials that cannot be reliably cultivated elsewhere. Obaru healers and naturalists keep enormous archives recording which substances heal, poison, preserve, sedate, regenerate, stabilise Essence, or turn dangerous in combination.
Kushara draws anti-venoms, medicinal resins, battlefield drugs, restorative fungi, rare herbs, surgical compounds, Memory bark, spirit amber, bloodwood and specialised healing fibres from these territories, along with extremely durable tropical timber for major architecture and weapon construction. Obaru patrols maintain canopy roads, regulate harvests, protect sacred groves, manage dangerous fauna, monitor disease, recover travellers and investigate biological Wellspring corruption.
> When armour breaks and blood follows, Obaru becomes the stronger shield.

### 6. House N'Yari — The House Beneath the Mountain

**Seat:** Yarath-Kel · **Territory:** The Great Akar Rift, Broken Spine, southern Orunthal approaches
**Continental duty:** Strategic minerals, eastern defence, geothermal engineering, Rift crossings
**Greatest asset:** The greatest concentration of rare magical minerals under one regional authority
N'Yari occupies land that appears determined to kill anyone attempting to govern it: volcanic valleys, fault systems, high escarpments, geothermal basins, cliff settlements, mountain passes and portions of the Broken Spine.
Its deposits include Riftstone, gravity ore, geothermal crystal, diamonds, rubies, sapphires, silver, obsidian, black salt, rare-earth metals and volatile Aetheric compounds. Vokari controls the greater concentration of financial wealth; **N'Yari controls materials advanced civilisation cannot easily substitute** — high-level construction, magical devices, military engineering, specialised wards, bridges, Rift Lifts and experimental spatial mechanisms.
Its troops specialise in mountain warfare, cliff operations, volcanic hazard, aerial predators and fortified passes. Its engineers make the eastern continent traversable at all: lifts, bridges, stairs, mountain shelters, geothermal stations, hazard beacons and warning systems for volcanic or Wellspring activity.
> Vokari finds value beneath the earth. N'Yari finds what should never have been possible.

### 7. House Jomari — Lords of the Open Water

**Seat:** Jomaraz · **Territory:** Zamara Gulf, Nine-Mouth Delta approaches, southwestern coast
**Continental duty:** Navy, ports, foreign commerce, shipbuilding, coastal defence
**Greatest asset:** The largest fleet in the Old World
Jomari authority stretches across the Painted Harbors, the Sapphire Shelf, important islands, major coastal cities, naval fortresses and portions of the Nine-Mouth Delta. It maintains the **Gulf Fleet**, the largest maritime force on the continent, defending shipping lanes, suppressing piracy, escorting merchants, hunting dangerous sea creatures, transporting troops and monitoring foreign naval movement.
Jomari shipwrights combine tropical hardwood, ironwood, Tide crystal, metal reinforcement and foreign technique into some of the finest ships built outside dedicated maritime civilisations. Diplomats, imported technology, distant goods, overseas intelligence, foreign merchants and visitors pass through Jomari harbours before moving inland, and the house keeps vessels reserved for sovereign deployment.
> Kushara commands the land roads. Jomari keeps the horizon open.

### 8. House Zembari — Keepers of the Hidden Water

**Seat:** Zembar-Kesh · **Territory:** Zafaran Expanse, Blackglass frontier, northern caravan routes
**Continental duty:** Desert commerce, aquifers, intelligence, northern frontier security
**Greatest asset:** The largest caravan and deep-water network in the Old World
Zembari governs a land where a castle may matter less than a well. Its holdings stretch across oasis cities, subterranean reservoirs, caravan fortresses, sandstone settlements, buried cisterns, salt roads, aquifers and ancient hydraulic routes. Individual holdings may stand hundreds of miles apart; what binds them is the **Hidden Water Network**, whose true extent is known only to trusted navigators, senior Zembari officials, certain caravan masters and the throne. Control of that network is control over which armies, merchants, pilgrims and migrations cross the Zafaran interior alive.
Its caravans carry salt, copper, desert glass, Sunstone, fire opal, Wellspring amber, spices, livestock, relics and information toward the heartland. Its merchants form one of the throne's best informal intelligence systems. **Armies see armies. Caravans see everything else.**
Its riders maintain wells, rescue caravans, map migrating dunes, monitor Essence storms, track Dune Leviathan activity, reopen buried routes and keep traffic moving around the Blackglass Basin.
> Gold buys water everywhere else. In Zafaran, water decides whether gold reaches anywhere.

### The Balance of the Eight

Zettari supremacy does not make the others unnecessary. Dawn Guard's system does almost the opposite: he wants them powerful. A weak N'Kharu cannot defend M'Bala. A weak Malani cannot answer famine. A weak Jomari cannot hold the coast. A weak N'Yari cannot maintain the Rift. A weak Zembari loses the north.
The objective is not to prevent strength. It is to make strength **interdependent**. N'Kharu armies require Malani food, Vokari coin, N'Yari material, Obaru medicine, Zembari intelligence and Jomari shipping. Vokari requires roads, labour, protection, food and markets. Malani requires military protection and transport. Obaru requires trade infrastructure. N'Yari requires food and commercial access. Jomari requires inland cargo, timber, ore and grain. Zembari requires manufactured goods and southern agriculture. And all seven require an authority capable of settling the disputes that inevitably follow.

### The Council of Seven Pillars

Each Pillar House keeps a permanent representative in Kushara. Their rulers are not expected to abandon their territories for court; Dawn Guard considers a regional ruler who spends more time flattering the sovereign than governing his people to be failing at the reason he holds authority. Stewardship comes first. But when Kushara calls formally, the Seven answer.
House Zettari is not counted among the Seven, because the sovereign does not sit as one pillar among the structures holding up his own throne. **House Zettari presides.**
The Council advises on matters spanning several regions: continental taxation, resource shortage, major mobilisation, inter-house dispute, infrastructure, foreign trade, Wellspring emergency, and problems too large for one house. The Seven may disagree, argue, and tell Dawn Guard that a proposed action is foolish, economically destructive, militarily premature, culturally dangerous or impossible to implement. Competent disagreement is expected. What they may not do is mistake consultation for shared sovereignty.
Once a formal continental decision is rendered, the Pillars execute it unless the order violates one of the foundational compacts under which their local sovereignty was recognised. This is neither conventional feudalism nor a federation of equals. It is **regional sovereignty beneath continental sovereignty**. Malani water law remains Malani law. N'Yari mountain law remains N'Yari law. Vokari retains authority over its mines; N'Kharu retains its martial customs and regional banners.
Dawn Guard's doctrine reduces to two principles: **unity does not require sameness**, and **sovereignty carries responsibility in both directions**. The Seven owe Kushara loyalty. Kushara owes the Seven protection, judgment, restraint, and a reason for that loyalty to remain worth holding.

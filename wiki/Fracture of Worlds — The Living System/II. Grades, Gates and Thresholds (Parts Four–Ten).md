# II. Grades, Gates and Thresholds (Parts Four–Ten)

### Part Four — The Tier Grade System

Tier Grade is the qualitative bracket that determines what scale of force, phenomenon, or entity a practitioner can meaningfully engage, resist, or overcome. It is derived from a Sub-Stat's raw numeric value and confirmed by Temperance Stage. A stat cannot hold its Tier Grade if the Soul Crystal has not undergone the Threshold required to sustain that density.
Grades use real-world energy equivalents drawn from physical TNT yield benchmarks and speed measurements. When two characters clash, who wins a contested exchange is answered first by Tier Grade differential, then by technique and tactical intelligence, then by Soul Crystal architecture.
**One full Tier Grade above** an opponent in the relevant stat wins a direct exchange of that stat category without meaningful contest. **Two Tiers above** is overwhelming to the point of one-sided. **Three Tiers above** means the lower-grade character is not engaging in a battle, they are surviving an event.

#### Where the Grade Sits

The table above measures a **Sub-Stat's** raw value, and the Temperance ceiling in Part Five binds the Sub-Stat. Every Grade letter in this document, in the Path gates of Part Seven and the Resonant Pairs of Part Nine, means what it has always meant: a threshold on that Sub-Stat's own number.
A **Primary** is the combined total of its eight Sub-Stats, and its Grade is read off the mean, the total divided by eight. That mean is the figure a character sheet has always carried on the Primary line. Eight Ardency Sub-Stats totalling 2,400 give a mean of 300 and an A-Grade Ardency. The total is what the sheet records; the mean is what an assessor reads and what decides a contested exchange. See *I. Levels, Experience and Stat Points* for the point economy.
Because the ceiling sits on the Sub-Stat, a character's Sub-Stats and their Primary Grade sit in the same bracket. A B-Grade practitioner has broadly B-Grade stats. Spread shows what they are good at. It does not move them between brackets.
A Primary is dragged down by its neglected entries as hard as it is lifted by its favourites. A practitioner with three Dominion Sub-Stats at the ceiling and five near nothing reads as a modest Dominion on any assessor's ledger while legislating local reality with the three. The institutions measure the mean. The fight is decided by the peak.
**Throne is the exception.** It carries no Temperance ceiling, its floor sits at EX-Grade, and it is excluded from the Dominion total. It is carried as its own line on the character sheet. *Pending ruling.*

**Hollow.** The unawakened baseline. No genuine contact with Essence. Output indistinguishable from ordinary mortal capacity. Most characters have already left it before they enter the System.
**F.** First resonance contact has occurred. The Soul Crystal has opened. Technique nascent, Essence output barely controlled, but no longer fully mortal in potential.
**E.** Essence is deliberately shaped. Wellspring attunement fragile but genuine. Techniques constructed rather than erupted. Street to low Wall level.
**D.** Technique coherence established. Multi-step Aetheric expressions without collapse. Wall level, 0.005 tons TNT at the ceiling.
**C.** The journeyman tier. Intermediate-complexity techniques supported. Domain seeds structurally possible though not stable. Small Building to low Building, 0.005 to 0.25 tons TNT.
**B.** Aura begins generating passive environmental pressure. Presence felt before techniques land. Domain architecture active rather than potential. Building to Large Building, 0.25 to 11 tons TNT.
**A.** Sovereign practitioner tier. Domain live and law-bearing. City Block to Multi-City Block, 11 to 1,000 tons TNT.
**S.** Legend-class entry. Full-output techniques register at natural-disaster and large-scale military detonation level. Aura suppresses B-grade and below passively. Small Town to Town, 1 to 5.8 kilotons TNT.
**SS.** Continental threat entry. Full-output events measured against major geological disruptions. A Domain at this level begins reshaping its local environment rather than merely existing within it. Town to Large Town, 10 to 100 kilotons TNT, the lower range of nuclear yields.
**SSS.** Small-country scale destructive equivalence. Domain law can override localized Wellspring behavior within its territory. Full-power expression is a regional metaphysical event. Large Town to Small Country, 100 kilotons to 1 teraton TNT.
**X.** Archon-adjacent. Existence exerts passive influence on the metaphysical laws of the surrounding environment. Domain produces Archon-echo effects. Country to Moon level, 1 teraton to 29.6 exatons TNT.
**EX.** Archon-equivalent. The distinction between an EX practitioner and a functioning Archon is qualitative, a matter of metaphysical doctrine and the Principle they embody, not raw output. Moon to Large Planet, 29.6 exatons to 16.512 ronnatons TNT.
**EX+.** No formal upper ceiling beneath the Apotheosis threshold. Titan-adjacent space. Characters here are not participants in ordinary conflict. They are metaphysical events that other characters survive or fail to survive. Brown Dwarf level and beyond, scaling toward stellar and solar-system scale.
At **Zenith, Stage XIV**, speed and attack output are no longer assessed by conventional metrics. The character exists in a state where the Continuum's ordinary measuring systems require metaphysical recalibration to even register the practitioner's presence. Zenith characters do not move faster than light. They exist within frameworks where the question of movement speed has ceased to be the relevant variable.

### Part Five — Temperance Gates and Stat Ceilings

Temperance Stage functions as a hard ceiling on every Sub-Stat in the system, and a Primary's mean therefore cannot exceed it either. A character cannot allocate their way past a ceiling their Soul Crystal cannot structurally sustain. Throne alone carries no ceiling. The Crystal must have undergone the corresponding Threshold Event before it can hold a stat at the grade above.
Forced advancement past Temperance gates is not impossible, but it is catastrophic. Stats pushed above their sustainable grade by alchemical augmentation, bloodline exploitation, or external metaphysical imposition generate Crystal Fracture Events at an exponentially increasing rate until the stat collapses or the Threshold is passed through crisis.

**Dissonance, Stage XI, carries unique behavior.** Characters who pass through Dissonance rather than becoming trapped within it reach Stage XII with stats that are fractured but denser than the clean advancement path would produce. Certain Sub-Stats, particularly **Overflow, Overchannel and Persistence**, can only reach their true ceiling in a character who has passed through Dissonance. The fractures are not damage. They are architectural features. Emanation, Stage XII, is where those fractures become features that radiate outward.

### Part Six — Speed by Grade and Level

Speed divides into three components: **Attack Speed**, the velocity of technique execution; **Reaction Speed**, the capacity to perceive and respond to incoming force; and **Travel Speed**, movement velocity across space. All three are influenced by Dexterity's Sub-Stats, particularly Celerity, Reflex and Sequence, but each responds differently to Temperance advancement.
These values are the natural ceiling achievable at each Tier Grade through pure stat advancement, without technique amplification. Techniques built around Alacrity and Celerity can push further within overchannel risk parameters.

At D-Grade the practitioner can react to gunfire but cannot outrun it. At B-Grade they can engage opponents relying on firearms or conventional projectiles without significant reactive disadvantage. At A-Grade movement generates significant environmental effects, heat buildup from air friction, pressure waves, surface disruption, without deliberate technique activation. At S-Grade the air itself becomes a weapon; characters without S-Grade Resilience or higher sustain structural damage simply from proximity to a moving S-Grade practitioner at full speed. At SS-Grade biological neural processes are insufficient and Essence-mediated sensory routing becomes the primary perceptual mechanism, and the practitioner's passage through space at full speed is perceived as instantaneous by anyone below S-Grade. Dodging a full-speed SSS attack requires SSS Reaction Speed at minimum to even register the attack as having occurred. Below SSS-Grade nobody can perceive, let alone react to, X-Grade full speed. EX movement generates relativistic effects that interact with the surrounding Aether field and can cause localized temporal distortion as a byproduct rather than an intention. EX+ movement is not faster travel between two points; it is Essence-mediated spatial restructuring where distance is subject to Domain authority.
**Zenith, Stage XIV:** Immeasurable Speed classification applies at full expression. The practitioner does not move through space. In the limit case, they exist in all relevant positions simultaneously until they choose otherwise.

### Part Seven — Individual Stat Gates

Every Sub-Stat carries a Path Alignment requirement. Certain subs cannot exceed a given Tier Grade without demonstrated Path commitment. **Path commitment is not declared. It is recognized by the Crystal based on behavioral pattern**, the consistent routing of Essence through a specific plane's logic over time.
A character who has fought exclusively through Body Path mechanics for three full Bands has genuine Body Path alignment regardless of what they claim. A character who has occasionally used Spirit techniques while routing primarily through physical expression will find Spirit-aligned Sub-Stats quietly resisting advancement beyond C-Grade until the actual pattern of Soul Crystal use reflects commitment.

#### Vitality Gates

**Fortitude** requires Body Path at Stage III to exceed C, and Stage IV to exceed B, the higher gate covering the bone and connective-tissue density it absorbed. **Absorption** requires Body Path at Stage III to exceed C. **Constitution** requires Body Path at Stage II to exceed D. **Hemostasis** open. **Regeneration** open, though reinforcement of healed tissue requires Body Path at Stage V to exceed B. **Filtration** open. **Tolerance** open, except that its Aether-dead component requires Fate Path at Stage VII to exceed S and its gravitational component requires Attraction Path at Stage VI to exceed A, both being more Domain-anchored than physical. **Threshold** requires Body Path at Stage IV to exceed B.

#### Ardency Gates

**Flux** open to any Path, with scaling character determined by dominant Path expression. **Alacrity** requires Spirit Path at Stage III to exceed C. **Depth** requires Attraction Path at Stage IV to exceed B, and Stage VI to exceed A where two Wellspring qualities are held in one working. **Penetration** requires Body Path at Stage III to exceed C. **Compression** open to any Path, scaling exponentially with Temperance Stage rather than Path commitment; concealment of a working's signature requires Attraction Path at Stage VII to exceed S. **Cascade** requires Attraction Path at Stage IV to exceed B for spread, Spirit Path at Stage V to exceed S for range. **Overchannel** open, carrying inherent Overflow risk above D-Grade and Backlash risk above C, with no Path providing structural protection; recovery of rebound as usable output requires Body Path at Stage V to exceed B. **Density** requires Body Path at Stage IV to exceed B, with inscription and glyph-chain durability requiring Spirit Path at Stage III to exceed C.

#### Gnosis Gates

**Perception** open. **Acuity** requires Spirit Path at Stage III to exceed C. **Forecast** requires Attraction Path at Stage V to exceed S. **Fluency** requires Spirit Path at Stage II to exceed D and Stage III to exceed C, with its Command Weight component requiring Attraction Path at Stage III to exceed C. **Retention** requires Spirit Path at Stage II to exceed D, and Stage VI to exceed A for the number of complete workings held loaded. **Analysis** open, though diagnosis of external fields and living beings requires Spirit Path at Stage IV to exceed B. **Cognition** open, though resistance to glamour and psychic misdirection requires Spirit Path at Stage IV to exceed B. **Vigilance** open.

#### Dexterity Gates

**Celerity** requires Body Path at Stage II to exceed D, and Stage IV to exceed B for standing acceleration. **Reflex** open. **Finesse** open to any Path, though redirecting incoming force by timed contact requires Body Path at Stage III to exceed C. **Economy** requires Body Path at Stage III to exceed C. **Evasion** open to any Path. **Grapple** requires Body Path at Stage III to exceed C. **Feint** requires Spirit Path at Stage IV to exceed B, with suppression of kinetic noise and Aura spill requiring Attraction Path at Stage V to exceed S. **Sequence** requires Spirit Path at Stage VI to exceed A, and Body Path at Stage V to exceed A for simultaneous action across separate limbs.

#### Harmonics Gates

**Attunement** requires Attraction Path at Stage II to exceed D, with retention of previously encountered frequencies requiring Spirit Path at Stage III to exceed C. **Projection** requires Attraction Path at Stage III to exceed C for field weight and Stage VI to exceed A for reach. **Breadth** requires Attraction Path at Stage IV to exceed B, and Stage V to exceed A where several Wellsprings are layered inside one working. **Stability** open, though holding resonance in Wellspring-absent ground requires Fate Path at Stage VII to exceed S. **Empathy** requires Attraction Path at Stage III to exceed C. **Synergy** requires Attraction Path at Stage V to exceed S for ambient amplification and Stage VI to exceed A for allied amplification. **Axis** requires Attraction Path at Stage IV to exceed B. **Suppression** requires Spirit Path at Stage V to exceed A.

#### Resilience Gates

**Integrity** open, though the conversion of prior fractures into reinforcement requires Body Path at Stage V to exceed B. **Ward** requires Spirit Path at Stage III to exceed C, with defence of the character's own Domain requiring Fate Path at Stage VIII to exceed SS. **Persistence** requires Fate Path at Stage V to exceed S, and Stage VIII to exceed SS against Oblivion-class pressure. **Anchoring** open, though resistance to memory erasure and false implantation requires Spirit Path at Stage III to exceed C. **Fortification** requires Body Path at Stage IV to exceed B; resistance to self-generated blowback is open. **Hardening** requires Body Path at Stage III to exceed C; passive rejection of contamination is open. **Oath** requires Attraction Path at Stage IV to exceed B. **Continuity** requires Fate Path at Stage VI to exceed A against spatial displacement and Stage VII to exceed S against causal disruption.

#### Tempering Gates

**Clarity** requires Spirit Path at Stage III to exceed C, with resistance to Overgrowth requiring Body Path at Stage IV to exceed B. **Overflow** open, with inherent structural risk above B. **Maturity** open, and is the primary Sub-Stat consulted by the Crystal when evaluating Threshold readiness; its transition-recognition component requires Spirit Path at Stage IV to exceed B, its fracture-metabolism component Spirit Path at Stage V to exceed A, and its bloodline component Fate Path at Stage VI to exceed A. **Coherence** open, and functions as the structural prerequisite for Temperance Threshold eligibility. **Yield** requires Body Path at Stage III to exceed C; ambient conversion and generation latency are open. **Ceiling** requires Fate Path at Stage VIII to exceed SS. **Capacity** requires Attraction Path at Stage V to exceed A. **Longevity** open.

#### Dominion Gates

**Radius** requires Attraction Path at Stage V to exceed A, Stage VI to exceed A for partial projection outward, and Stage VII to exceed S for boundary expansion speed. **Sovereignty** requires Fate Path at Stage VI to exceed A, and Stage IX to exceed SSS where the imposed law encodes abstract conceptual truths. **Sense** requires Spirit Path at Stage III to exceed C for topology and higher-order phenomena, Spirit Path at Stage IV to exceed B for Archon-tier pressures, and Fate Path at Stage VIII to exceed SS for passive detection of spatial tears. **Stability** requires Attraction Path at Stage V to exceed A for the law's structural weight, and Fate Path at Stage VI to exceed A for holding territory in hostile Realm environments. **Pressure** requires Attraction Path at Stage VI to exceed A. **Command** requires Attraction Path at Stage VIII to exceed SS. **Fate** requires Fate Path at Stage VII to exceed S. **Throne** requires Fate Path at Stage XIII minimum and cannot be advanced below EX-Grade. Its ceiling is Absolute, the only Sub-Stat in the system with no practical upper boundary on this side of Apotheosis, and it is excluded from the Dominion total for that reason.

### Part Eight — Temperance Threshold Events

Thresholds are not achieved by accumulating enough experience. They are triggered by two factors: the Soul Crystal must have reached sufficient pressure within the current stage, a quantitative floor; and a **Catalyst Condition** must have been met, a specific quality of experience the Crystal requires before it will authorize the transition.
A character can be quantitatively eligible for months of in-world time and never pass a Threshold because the Catalyst has not occurred. The System does not advance a soul that has not genuinely changed. This is not a punishment. It is accuracy.
**I to II, Welling.** First genuine contact with a Wellspring current under personal pressure, not observed, not instructed, but touched and survived.
**II to III, Ascension.** First technique developed independently from instruction, built from the character's own understanding of their Essence rather than received doctrine.
**III to IV, Flourishing.** Formation of the first genuine Spirit Axis, a bond with another soul that carries mechanical weight in the Harmonics system.
**IV to V, Splintering.** Surviving contact with an entity or force more than two full Tiers above the character's current ceiling. Not defeating it, surviving it. The Crystal fractures along emotional lines.
**V to VI, Glory.** The self burns bright and is noticed. Willingly accepting genuine soul-cost to preserve something the character values above their own structural integrity. The peak of the inner phase.
**VI to VII, Refraction.** Dual sight. The soul sees itself from outside. First Domain seed activation, however fragile or small. Projected fields become coherent.
**VII to VIII, Transcendence.** The world is read as symbol. Resolving a core internal contradiction documented in the Soul Crystal's fracture record, a wound carried as structural damage and consciously processed into architecture. The first true Domain forms.
**VIII to IX, Invocation.** Calling on Wellsprings, Titans, or Archonic law as co-authors of expression. The practitioner consolidates their Domain into a Realm capable of holding other Domains.
**IX to X, Realization.** Truth as lived consequence. The world answers back. Pacts, artifacts and law imprint on terrain. The Domain becomes a reality the Continuum must accommodate.
**X to XI, Dissonance.** Deliberately entering contact with a force or truth capable of ending the character's existence and remaining coherent long enough to choose how they meet it. Power and identity clash. The Crystal fractures or breaks through.
**XI to XII, Emanation.** Emerging from Dissonance with a Soul Crystal denser and cleaner than before entry. The fractures must have become features. The practitioner radiates outward. Traits become local physics. Passive miracles leak.
**XII to XIII, Principality.** Achieving Domain coherence sufficient for the Continuum to formally register a law the character embodies. A local force of nature. First World Spirit threshold. Biomes carry doctrine.
**XIII to XIV, Zenith.** The character's accumulated doctrine becomes recognizable to a Hollow Throne. The Throne resonates with them. The transition is not a breakthrough. It is recognition. Full World Spirit. Reality acclimates to the character. Story becomes physics.
**XIV to XV, Revelation.** Genuine exchange with an Archon or Titan at a level where the character is a participant rather than a witness or a casualty. The first step of the Apotheosis approach.
**XV to XVI, Apex.** Holding a Principle in the Soul Crystal without fracturing, even momentarily, even imperfectly. Apex is perpetual. It is the state of a soul pressing against what it has not yet become.

### Part Ten — Stagnation and Diminishing Returns

The System has two structural enemies: grinding and stagnation. Both are penalized in the architecture of the Crystal itself.
**Grinding** is countered by the **Crystal Adaptation Rule**. Any threat or stimulus type engaged more than five times without meaningful variation to the character's technique or the threat's composition is reclassified internally as **Familiar**. Familiar stimuli yield ten percent of their original XP value, permanently, for that character. The only way to restore value is to approach it with a genuinely novel technique or under genuinely novel conditions the Crystal registers as a different category of experience.
**Stagnation** is countered by **Residual Strain** accumulation. Every fifty levels spent above a Band ceiling without passing the required Temperance Threshold imposes a five percent penalty to all XP gains and a two percent increase to all Overchannel and Backlash risk values. This pressure builds continuously.
It dissolves entirely the moment the Threshold is passed. On dissolution the character receives a **Strain Release Bonus** equal to thirty percent of all XP accumulated during the stagnation period, delivered as a single Resonant Surge. The System is not punishing patience. It is representing the physical truth of a soul carrying the weight of what it has already earned but not yet integrated. That weight has texture. That texture eventually becomes pain. And then, if the character meets the Catalyst Condition, it becomes power.

# V. Wellsprings, Attraction and Obsession, Magic Integration (Parts Thirteen–Fifteen)

### Part Thirteen — The Sixty Wellsprings and Stat Interaction

*"To pass through a Wellspring is to be unmade just enough to keep going."*
> **Updated to the sixty-four Sub-Stat architecture.** Every Sub-Stat cited below is a current entry. Where an older reading named a retired Sub-Stat, the effect now sits on the entry that absorbed it, and the effect itself is unchanged. The merge ledger is on *IV. The Eight Primaries and the Sixty-Four Sub-Stats*.

#### What Wellsprings Do to Stats

The Sixty Wellsprings are not power-ups. They are principles of becoming, metabolic dialects that alter how the Soul Crystal processes Essence, routes Aether and anchors reality. When a character harmonizes with a Wellspring, it permanently alters the behaviour of specific Sub-Stats, not by adding flat numerical bonuses, but by changing the qualitative character of how those Sub-Stats express their value.
A character with Fortitude at B-Grade who harmonizes with Monolithion does not gain Fortitude points. Their Fortitude begins behaving like stone rather than like flesh. The number stays the same. What the number means changes.
Every Wellspring touches all three layers of the Crystal, rarely evenly. The **Essence Core** determines how the soul burns, heals or empties under that law. The **Aether Shell** determines how the law moves through and around the body. The **Attraction Layer** determines how reality, memory and people bind to the soul once the law has taken hold.
Most mortals awaken three to five Wellsprings in a lifetime. Serious adepts may harmonize with twelve or more. Those rare beings who embody all Sixty are called **Principals of Temperance**.

#### The Eight Families

Each Wellspring's Family assignment is fixed by the phenomenon it governs, not by the emotional or narrative company it keeps. Wellsprings that once shared a House for reasons of grief, guilt or memory are now scattered across five Families. That scattering is correct. It is what happens when a physics-first taxonomy replaces a feeling-first one.

#### Wellspring Stacking and Interference

Multiple Wellsprings from the same Family compound their Sub-Stat modifications. Three Materia Wellsprings produce a Vitality and Resilience profile noticeably denser than a character with identical raw values and no Materia harmonization. The numbers are identical on paper. The qualitative expression is not.
Cross-Family harmonization introduces resonance complexity. Thematically complementary Families, Materia and Vectoria for instance, matter given form through force, produce complementary effects without interference. Thematically opposed Families, Materia and Limina, form against the erosion of all shape, produce tension the Crystal must structurally resolve, either through Temperance advancement or through a **Compound Trait** that integrates the contradiction.
The maximum number of simultaneous harmonizations is governed by **Tempering Capacity**. Exceeding it produces resonance conflict that degrades all active harmonizations equally.

### Part Fourteen — Attraction Force and Obsession Force

*"To be seen is sacred. To be held against the law of your own soul is profanation."*

#### Attraction Force, the Law of Recognition

Attraction Force is the third fundamental force in the framework, equal in structural importance to Aether and Essence. Where Aether gives a working physical form and Essence gives it identity, **Attraction Force gives it endurance**. It is the binding principle that allows one presence to acknowledge another and for that acknowledgment to persist as continuity. Through it, vows remain binding, relics remember their bearers, lineages preserve their echo, Domains remain stable, and spiritual relations survive time, death, distance and change.
**Harmonics.** Attraction Force is the lawful current beneath every Harmonics Sub-Stat. Attunement functions because it lets the soul recognize a Wellspring's frequency and hold that recognition honestly. Axis functions because it sustains the Spirit Bond between two Crystals. Synergy functions because it lets allied resonances synchronize without overwriting each other. Strong Attraction expression makes every Harmonics Sub-Stat qualitatively richer at every numerical value.
**Dominion.** Domain formation is, mechanically, an act of Attraction Force. The Attraction Layer claims territory on the Plane of Fate, and that claim reflects downward into physical and spiritual law. Sovereignty, Fate, Command and Throne are all direct expressions of how cleanly that claim translates into environmental authority.
**Resilience.** Strengthens Oath, so sworn bonds become load-bearing; Anchoring, so identity holds its shape and its continuity because it is witnessed; and Continuity, so the causal timeline resists editing once the Continuum has registered it as recognized.
**Tempering.** Improves Coherence, since alignment between stated Path and lived behaviour is easier to maintain when the soul is honestly witnessed, and Maturity, since bloodline traits and inherited Geasa activate more cleanly when the ancestral recognition chain is intact.
**Combat applications.** Maintaining coherent technique across long distances from bonded allies. Inheriting and transmitting combat forms through covenant rather than instruction. Recognizing an opponent's true Essence beneath disguise or glamour. Stabilizing allies whose Essence is fraying by lending them legibility. Composing Domains centred on covenant and sanctuary. Carrying bonds past death in forms that remain clean and reciprocal. Its strength is durable mutuality. Its weakness is the weight of being honestly seen.
**Costs.** Essence becomes more legible to those who can read it, including those who would harm it. The Crystal carries the imprint of every covenant honoured, and grief is the lawful tax on every covenant ended. Memory becomes weighted. Identity becomes accountable, defined in part by who has been allowed to witness it.

#### Obsession Force, the Law of Possession

The parasitic inversion. Where Attraction recognizes, Obsession imprisons. Where Attraction binds through mutual resonance, Obsession seals through hunger, fixation and refusal to release. It is the Current of Refusal in its personal, soul-to-soul expression: the moment recognition rots into possession and remembrance becomes a cage.
**Harmonics corruption.** Collapses Breadth, leaving meaningful synchronization only with the object of obsession. Degrades Stability through emotional volatility around the fixation axis. Inverts Empathy into a one-directional parasitic channel: supernatural clarity on the object, blindness to all other emotional data. Attunement reports a frequency the practitioner has stopped actually holding.
**Dominion corruption.** Domains formed through Obsession do not feel open or sovereign. They feel airless. Memory repeats, distance shrinks unnaturally, every path bends back toward the fixation object. Sovereignty becomes Possession. Radius contracts rather than extends. The Domain folds inward around one meaning and refuses all exit.
**Resilience degradation.** Degrades Anchoring, so memory loses proportion and identity narrows to a single defining attachment, and Integrity, so the Crystal develops structural dependency on the fixation and removal of the object produces fracture events.
**Tempering degradation.** Degrades Coherence catastrophically; stated Path and lived behaviour diverge around the axis of fixation. Maturity stalls because the soul is no longer integrating new experience. It is recycling one experience endlessly.
**Combat applications.** Locking onto one target with impossible persistence. Tethering souls or techniques to a chosen fixation. Ignoring pain through compulsive singularity. Forcing resonance overlap between self and enemy. Domains centred on possession and recursion. Bonds preserved past death in broken, predatory form. Its strength is terrifying focus. Its weakness is total collapse of perspective outside the fixation.
**Risks.** Essence coherence erodes. The Crystal narrows around one governing hunger. Other bonds weaken. Identity becomes recursive. At worst the user becomes an echo-organism of wanting, a living Essence Shard that never fully separated, still orbiting a source that is already gone. Where a World Spirit expands until others can live inside it, a soul claimed by Obsession Force contracts until nothing else can.

### Part Fifteen — Magic System Integration

*"Twenty categories, one underlying grammar."*
The stat system is the structural skeleton beneath every magic system in WOTR. When a character casts a Sage Art, activates a Spirit Summon, inscribes a Parunic Draft or expresses a Hataraki Work, the mechanical interaction passes through the stat framework. The stats are the physical, spiritual and metaphysical architecture that determines whether a technique succeeds, fails, or destroys the caster.

#### Sage Arts

Terrain-anchored Wellspring communion. A Sage does not cast spells in the conventional sense. They synchronize their Crystal with a location's ambient Wellspring activity and redirect it through their Aetheric architecture.
**Primary:** Harmonics (Attunement, Synergy, Breadth) for synchronization; Gnosis (Perception, Analysis) for terrain reading; Dominion (Radius, Sense) for spatial extension.
**Secondary:** Ardency Depth governs draw depth. Tempering Capacity governs simultaneous harmonizations. Resilience Integrity governs how much ambient pressure can be absorbed without destabilization.
**Corruption vectors.** Verdantia Sages risk *Endless Spring*, inability to let anything end. Anima Spirare Sages risk *Radiant Fever*, life-force overdriven until the soul burns hollow. Tenebra Sages risk *Shadow Saturation*, inability to exist comfortably outside shadow. Manganthra Sages risk *Eruption Dependence*, inability to function without extreme environmental pressure. All manifest as degradation of Harmonics Stability, Tempering Coherence and Resilience Anchoring.

#### Spirit Summoning Arts

Five-step pipeline: Intent, Pattern, Essence Donation, Aether Formation, Attraction Anchoring.
**Primary:** Ardency (Flux, Density) governs the construct's output, structural weight and duration. Harmonics (Attunement, Breadth) governs inherited Wellspring alignment. Dominion (Radius, Gravity) governs operational independence and range.
**Secondary:** Gnosis Retention sets how many summon patterns can be held loaded. Tempering Yield sets how much Essence can be donated without depleting combat reserves. Dexterity Sequence sets command speed during engagement.
**Wellspring inheritance.** Summons inherit their creator's alignment. A Materia-Family summoner produces anchored, durable, slow-to-dissolve constructs. A Limina-Family summoner produces absence-constructs, void-forms and negation agents. The spirit's behaviour is a function of the summoner's Harmonics profile, not a separate system.

#### Spirit Rune Arts

Aetheric effects encoded into Parunic inscription structures that persist independently of active concentration.
**Primary:** Gnosis Fluency governs inscription accuracy and glyph-chain complexity. Ardency (Density, Compression, Depth) governs durability, efficiency and alignment. Resilience (Ward, Hardening) governs protective rune capacity.
**Secondary:** Dexterity Finesse for physical precision of execution. Tempering Clarity for the cleanliness of Essence routed into the glyph. Dominion Gravity for persistence against environmental erosion.

#### Hataraki, the Works of the Plane of Fate

Not spells. The labor reality performs through practitioners who have become structurally aligned with specific principles of cosmic maintenance.
**Zentai-sei (Totality)** — Dominion (Sovereignty, Throne), Tempering (Ceiling, Maturity), Resilience (Persistence, Continuity). Wholeness and cosmic maintenance. Doctrine that encompasses rather than excludes.
**Hametsu no Gō (Ruins)** — Ardency (Flux, Overchannel, Cascade), Resilience (Ward, Persistence), Dominion (Pressure, Command). Necessary collapse. Doctrine that dismantles what can no longer sustain itself.
**Unmei no Ori (Fate's Cage)** — Dominion (Fate, Command, Sovereignty), Harmonics (Attunement, Axis), Resilience (Oath, Continuity). Binding and consequence. Doctrine that enforces the debts reality owes itself.
**Shinsei Naru Hikari (Sacred Light)** — Harmonics (Attunement, Projection, Synergy), Gnosis (Perception, Analysis), Tempering (Clarity, Maturity). Illumination and purification. Doctrine that reveals truth as a structural force.
**Eien no Yami (Eternal Dark)** — Resilience (Persistence, Ward), Harmonics (Stability, Suppression), Dominion (Sovereignty, Sense). Negation and sovereign silence. Doctrine that erases rather than creates.
**Saisei no Wa (Cycle of Rebirth)** — Vitality (Regeneration, Constitution, Hemostasis), Tempering (Maturity, Yield), Harmonics (Synergy, Stability). Renewal. Doctrine that treats ending as the first motion of beginning.
**Chowa no Koe (Voice of Harmony)** — Harmonics (Breadth, Synergy, Empathy, Attunement), Dominion (Command, Sovereignty, Radius), Gnosis (Fluency, Cognition). Concordance. Doctrine that synchronizes rather than commands.

#### Attraction and Obsession in Technique

Every technique is sustained at the deepest level by one or the other. **Clean techniques are sustained by recognition:** the Crystal recognizes the Wellspring's law, the Continuum recognizes the intent, and the working persists because all parties agree it should. **Corrupted techniques are sustained by refusal:** the Crystal clutches the current rather than harmonizing with it, the Continuum registers the working as unauthorized, and Obsession Force sustains it through sheer fixation. Such workings are structurally unstable, emotionally recursive, and produce escalating soul-cost with each use.
The system tracks the distinction through **Harmonics Attunement** against **Tempering Coherence**. High in both means the character is operating through Attraction Force. Attunement degrading while raw output stays high means Obsession Force, and the system will extract the cost.

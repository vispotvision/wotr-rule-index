# Hataraki no Sho — The Martial Application of the Works

**HATARAKI NO SHO**
> *Being a private doctrinal record set down in the hand of Bara Moto, Founder of the Moto Bloodline, concerning the observed nature of Hataraki, the errors of lesser readers, and the martial applications by which Fate itself is made operative.*
> 
> *[Before Measured Time, Plane of Fate, the Hour Before Sequence]*

---

## On Function, and Why It Precedes Hunger

There is a particular kind of misunderstanding observed in younger beings, and *younger* is meant deliberately, for all things are younger than the author in this matter.
They imagine that power begins in hunger. It does not. **Power begins in function.**
A starving wolf may possess appetite, but appetite alone does not teach the teeth how to close, nor the sinew how to tighten, nor the blood how to obey the stress of the leap. In like fashion, the novice who first beholds Hataraki mistakes it for magnitude. He feels the pressure of it. He sees the world bend around it. He watches a name become binding, a wound become verdict, a battlefield become grammar, and concludes, with the special stupidity reserved to the gifted, that Hataraki is simply a superior magic.
> Hataraki is not superior because it is larger. **It is superior because it is prior.** The world did not begin with explosions. It began with assignments. What should endure was given the means to endure. What should collapse was given the right to fail. What should pass from life into death was given a lawful crossing. What should be known was given the condition by which it might become intelligible.
Cruelty is merely appetite wearing confidence. The Moto are not cruel by nature. **They are administrative.** The distinction is lost upon those who have never had to rank the worthiness of a thing.

## Prologue · On Seeing and Working

The house inherited two burdens. The first was **sight**. The second was **labour**. The first became the Shingan. The second remained Hataraki.
Lesser scholars try to separate them in commentary, as though perception and imposition could be discussed in isolation. This is the habit of scholars who have never stood in a live battlefield and felt ten contradictory futures scrape their nails down the inside of the air.
> The Eye without the Work is an archivist too frightened to write in the margins.
>
> The Work without the Eye is tyranny practiced by the blind.
>
> **Thus they were bound.**

### Meigan · the revealing eye

Meigan users become quiet in the wrong way. Not contemplative, but predatory. They begin to notice how falsehood leans upon structure, how fortifications hide their failure in ornament, how a smile can fail along one edge before the lie fails in the mouth. Such descendants favour cuts that do not look theatrical. They like seams. They like joints. **They like the place where a thing has been pretending not to break.**
**Kakushi Wari** · *Hidden-Seam Rend.* A revelation cut placed not where the enemy is strongest, but where his form has already told on itself.
**Byakko no Kireme** · *White Fox Parting.* A competent swordsman strikes plate and complains. A Moto with Meigan notes the loosened strap beneath the arm, the weight-shift toward the weak knee, the overtrust in the left-side guard, and then performs this, which is less a slash than a legal correction made at speed.

### Reigan · the soul eye

Reigan heirs disappoint differently. They are too often tempted by intimacy. To see the soul is to be invited into the interior, and many mistake that invitation for permission. They become fascinated with bloodline resonance, curse anatomy, ancestor sediment, the half-healed fractures hidden beneath composure. They learn quickly that **flesh is only the outer handwriting of deeper injuries.**
**Shinzui Kaibun** · *Circular Writ of the Marrow.*
- ***A red-line daughter, and the revenant that had shrugged off everything***
  She did not attempt to overpower it. She placed two fingers against the flat of her own blade and invoked the Writ. The strike that followed was narrow, almost insulting in its economy. It entered through the clavicular gap, not deeply enough to impress a fool, and yet the revenant dropped to one knee as though something inside it had remembered death correctly for the first time in centuries.

  She had not cut its body. **She had corrected its soul-traffic.**

  I approved.

### Tengan · the fate eye

Tengan makes strategists unbearable for several decades. The young who first awaken it often think they have become prophets. They have not. They have merely become aware of pressure. **This is still useful. Pressure wins more wars than prophecy ever will.**
The true Tengan adept does not ask *what will happen?* He asks *which outcome has begun demanding itself of the field?*
**Saki no Mon** · *Gate of the Next Instant.* Practiced in duel and small-group combat.
**Mikettei no Ame** · *Rain of the Unchosen.* Belongs to formations, command corridors, cavalry timing, and the engineering of panic. A Tenrai son has been seen to use it with nothing but banners, disciplined footwork, and the knowledge that frightened men always flee into the route that feels least chosen by them. That is the vulgar beauty of Hataraki. **One need not always strike to impose a Work. Sometimes one merely arranges the enemy into obedience.**

### Kamigan · the crown eye

Rarely commented upon at length, because sovereign synthesis is wasted on those who crave it. The Crown Eye belongs to those already tired of singular answers. It is a throne and therefore a burden. It sees revelation, soul-law, and fate-pressure not as rivals, but as subordinate offices awaiting coordination.
*Those who wield it well develop an unfortunate resemblance to me.*

## Chapter the First · Hametsu no Gei, the Art of Ruin

There are two kinds of fools who misunderstand Ruin. The first believes Ruin to be evil. The second believes Ruin to be freedom. **Neither has looked closely enough at rotting timber.**
Hametsu no Gei was created because diseased continuity is one of existence's oldest insults. A kingdom that ought to have fallen but persists through cruelty. A law that no longer serves the reality it governs. A body that should have died cleanly but has become host to recurrence, corruption, or profaned resurrection. These things require ending, and ending is not an accident. **It is labour.**
**Hōkai Senkoku** · *Proclamation of Collapse.* Young practitioners delight in the name because they think the name itself performs the grandeur. It does not. What matters is whether the designation is **earned**. To pronounce collapse upon intact perfection is waste. To pronounce collapse upon a structure already hiding its own exhaustion is judgment.
**Kaimetsu no Hasuji** · *Edge-Line of Annihilation.* Used through sword, spear, and decree alike. The least intelligent version is the one preferred by braggarts, a broad committed stroke meant to impress witnesses. The correct version is almost humble. It follows leverage. It understands angle. It uses the enemy's own tension against him. In armoured combat it seeks articulation, weight-bearing joints, visor channels, or previously rattled structure. In fortification warfare it waits for masonry to confess where the stress has settled.
**Tetsu Shūen** · *Iron Terminal.* A hammer-form, neglected by aesthetes and beloved by real soldiers. One does not pretend to become subtler than steel. One simply drives ruin through plate, gambeson, rib, and nerve in proper sequence until the body loses its argument with the blow. *I have always liked the honesty of hammers. They are incapable of ornamental thought.*
> **The corruption of Ruin** is easy to identify. The practitioner ceases to ask whether a thing ought to end, and begins to enjoy ending as a general principle. Bloodlines have vanished into that error and called themselves pure for it.

## Chapter the Second · Junketsu no Gei, the Art of Purity

> Purity is more feared by the wise than Ruin ever will be. **This is because Ruin announces itself with ash, fractures, and screams. Purity arrives with clean hands.**
Written not merely to heal, but to establish what should remain. The distinction matters. Healing without judgment produces survivals that ought not survive. Purity is foundation, consecration, correct form, and the refusal to let contamination redefine structure. Descendants are often sentimental about this Work in adolescence. They confuse it with kindness. Later, after battlefield experience or domestic betrayal, they become harsher and at last begin to understand it.
**Seijō Kekkai** · *Barrier of Correct Sanctity.* Among the house's most useful defensive arts, though many perform it badly. The inexperienced imagine a wall. The experienced understand a **condition**. Properly invoked, the technique does not simply block entry. It clarifies the rightness of what is permitted to remain inside the field. This is why curses weaken within it. They are not merely repelled. **They become less doctrinally coherent.**
**Hakugin Kosei** · *Silver Restoration.* Used to repair posture in the middle of a losing bind. The body, when frightened, lies to itself. Knees narrow. Shoulders rise. Breath shortens and begins acting like a traitor. Silver Restoration reminds the body of its rightful architecture. *One may survive an exchange entirely because one's bones have been persuaded to remember dignity.*
**Jōka Fudan** · *Unceasing Purge.* The least romantic of the purgative forms and therefore among the best. It strips curse residue, septic Essence, necrotic stain, and oath-poison from blade, chamber, wound, or shrine. It has saved more noble houses than the songs ever mention.
> **The corruption here is uglier than annihilation.** It is sterilization. The practitioner begins cutting away all variance, all stain, all contradiction, until life itself is thinned into something antiseptic and dead-eyed. Of the seven corruptions, **this one smiles the most.**

## Chapter the Third · Seirei no Gei, the Art of Spirit

The dead are less mysterious than the living insist on making them. What confuses most people is not death, but attachment.
Seirei no Gei governs soul-traffic, lawful passage, ancestor continuity, and the maintenance of the living-dead boundary. It was not fashioned so cowards could beg corpses for permission to continue existing. It was fashioned because **transition requires governance.**
There are old houses in which the dead are never allowed to leave cleanly. They are kept in portraiture, in relics, in hunger, in grief rehearsed so often it becomes liturgy. Such houses always mistake themselves for devoted. *In truth they are merely clogged.*
**Tamashii Tobari** · *Soul Curtain.* The first technique taught in any serious spirit discipline. Not because it is glamorous. Because it prevents stupidity. It keeps external dead-pressure from entering the practitioner's inner chamber and keeps the practitioner's own fear from becoming a doorway.
**Yukue no Sen** · *Line of Passing.* Among the gentlest techniques in appearance and the cruellest in demand. **One must love release more than possession to perform it correctly.** Used to sever parasitic hauntings, to guide the newly dead beyond recurrence, and to cut mourning free from the point where mourning has become an altar to self-pity.
- ***A Moto mother, and the ghost of her own firstborn***
  She did not tremble. She did not posture. She simply placed her palm against the child's spectral brow, whispered the true relation between them, and let him go where she could not follow.

  That is spirit-work.

  Not candles. Not tears. Not decorative conversations with tomb-air.
**Reikon Shihai** · *Dominion of the Departed Soul.* The harsher expressions remain necessary. Revenants do not negotiate fairly. Nor do soul-thieves, grave-binders, or those alchemists who discover posthumous retention and begin at once to call themselves pioneers. **Against such enemies, Spirit must become a magistrate.**

## Chapter the Fourth · Jikan, Chishiki, Baransu, and Zentaisei

> Those most attracted to Time are rarely patient. Those most attracted to Knowledge are rarely wise. Those most attracted to Balance are often exhausted. And **those who desire Totality should never be trusted with it.**
>
> Still, the Works remain useful regardless of the small embarrassments of their readers.

### Jikan no Gei · the Art of Temporality

Not clock-work. **Sequence-law.** It governs before, during, after, convergence, delay, repetition, and the burden of consequence.
**Kansoku Hokō** · *Observing Step.* A footwork doctrine so load-bearing that many practitioners do not realize they are studying philosophy through their knees. *To place the foot rightly is to admit that sequence matters. To overextend is to commit heresy with the hips.*
**Innen Kusari** · *Chain of Causality.* The finest punitive form in this school. Not merely a curse, though lesser minds use it that way. It is the act by which one earlier truth is made to arrive on time. A feint becomes a later opening. A prior oath becomes a current penalty. A wound ignored in arrogance becomes the hinge upon which defeat finally swings.

### Chishiki no Gei · the Art of Knowledge

The labour that permits reality to become legible. Most people say they want truth. What they want is confirmation decorated to look severe. **Actual knowledge is much colder.**
**Naimei Ketsugi** · *Deliberation of the Inner Name.* Among the most dangerous techniques in the whole house, because naming alters what a thing can continue pretending to be. A false saint named as tyrant begins to lose the shelter of his own sanctimony. A ward named incorrectly begins to fail. A beast named according to hunger instead of species becomes readable where once it was only frightening.
**Monjo no Kagami** · *Mirror of the Inscribed Law.* Preferred in battle. It reflects not image, but **operative pattern**. One sees how the enemy thinks his technique functions, and from there one sees how it actually fails.

### Baransu no Gei · the Art of Balance

The most commonly underestimated Work and therefore the most likely to outlive its critics. Balance does not erase opposition. It weighs it. It assigns survivable proportion to contradiction.
**Futatsu no Tenbin** · *Scales of Two Heavens.* Effective in multi-Work duels. One identifies excess, then tilts the field just enough that **excess begins punishing itself.**
*I have seen two prodigies destroy each other because neither had studied Balance and both considered mediation beneath genius. A waste.*

### Zentaisei no Gei · the Art of Totality

Discussed only with restraint. The crown-function, the integrating labour, the gathering of all lesser Works into one governable frame.
**Sōgō Tōkan** · *Comprehensive Integration.* **Teiho no Oza** · *Throne of Accordant Judgment.* Not meant for immature egos. They are for command, for reality repair, for battlefields where multiple laws are already colliding and must either be reconciled or ranked.
> The corruption of Totality is the oldest sovereign temptation. **To gather is easy. To gather without devouring distinction, that is mastery.**

## Chapter the Fifth · House Forms, Weapons, and the Error of Decorative Combat

A bloodline may possess excellent doctrine and still die like idiots if it forgets that doctrine must pass through tendon, steel, timing, and footwork. **I have no patience for decorative combat.** The house disciplines exist precisely to prevent it.
| House form | Function |
|---|---|
| **Na no Ito** · *Namebinding Thread* | Not merely for ritual. In duel it allows recognition persistence across feints, disguises, mirrored bodies, or retreat into concealment. |
| **Chokurei Ayumi** · *Edict Step* | The tactical expression of lawful continuation. One moves not where panic prefers, but where the exchange is most properly resolved. |
| **Shiki Ori** · *Mandate Weave* | Patterns Aether through posture, nerve, weapon, and breath according to the selected Work. Without it, most descendants waste their inheritance by leaking power through dramatics. |

### On weapons

The **tachi and longsword** to those who study revelation and temporality. **Spears and estocs** to those who favour causality and spirit-penetration. **Polearms** to commanders. And **hammers** to anyone who has finally accepted that armour is a conversation best ended honestly.
> Against plate, do not behave like a poet. **Behave like architecture with intent.**
>
> Hook behind the knee. Strike the pauldron to numb the arm. Half-sword into the visor. Use **Shūen Kugi** through the armpit gap. Follow with **Danzai no Ku**, *Sentence of Severance*, only after the target has already been forced to admit his own instability.
>
> This is what lesser traditions miss. **A finishing move is not a bright name shouted at the beginning of an exchange. It is the final legal consequence of everything the target has already failed to survive.**

## Final Note

If this record is read by a child of my blood, let him understand me clearly.
Do not pursue Hataraki because it looks grand. Pursue it because reality is always in need of correct labour.
> See before you act.
> Name before you sever.
> Measure before you reconcile.
> Release before you cling.
> End only what has earned its ending.
> Preserve only what remains fit to endure.
And if ever you reach the higher thrones of Totality, and feel the old sovereign temptation to gather all distinctions into one obedient frame, remember this, which I have observed across ages and across empires, and which remains humiliatingly constant.
> **Nothing becomes more monstrous, more quickly, than a righteous fool with correct metaphysics.**
> I am Bara Moto.
> I wrote the Works before time.
> My descendants need not improve upon them. They need only become worthy of using them.

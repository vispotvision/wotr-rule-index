# The Apprentice's Primer — A First Reader for the Newly Registered

*A First Reader for the Newly Registered*
*Issued under the Concord Lattice · Research & Archives Division*
> *"You were not given this book because you are ready. You were given it because you have already begun, and nobody told you what you had begun."*
> — opening line, every printing since the Withering

---

## Before Anything Else

*Read this page even if you read nothing else. Six things carry the entire system. Everything after them is detail.*
> **One.** Reality has three planes. Matter, soul, and fate. **Every act of magic crosses all three at once. Remove one and the working fails, without exception and without appeal.**
>
> **Two.** Aether is the ocean. Essence is your water. *Aether is infinite and belongs to no one. Essence is Aether you have drawn in, circulated, and stamped with your identity — and there is only ever as much of it as you have earned.*
>
> **Three.** You cast through a Soul Crystal, not through a wand, a word, or a will. **Its architecture decides what you are capable of long before your ambition gets a vote.**
>
> **Four.** Power is refined, not accumulated. **Temperance is subtraction.** *You do not climb the Stages by training. You climb them by surviving something that changes you.*
>
> **Five.** Everything costs. Essence is spent, not summoned. Domains bleed. Traits fatigue. Overchannel scars. ***A practitioner who tells you a technique is free is either lying or has not yet paid the invoice.***
>
> **Six.** The system does not lie and it does not forgive. **Your numbers are the Continuum's honest assessment of the soul carrying them. They can be improved. They cannot be argued with.**
> *If you take nothing else, take the shape of that list. The rest of this primer is that list, explained slowly enough to be useful and honestly enough to be frightening.*

## The Three Planes and the Veil

**Reality is not layered like sediment. It is nested.** Three sovereign planes coexist at every point in space, **and your Crystal has one layer standing in each.**
| Plane | Governs | Crystal layer | Stats it feeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Material** | Physics, matter, force, energy, biology | Aether Shell | Vitality · Ardency · Dexterity |
| **Soul** | Identity, memory, conviction, Essence | Essence Core | Tempering · Resilience · Gnosis |
| **Fate** | Connection, recognition, consequence | Attraction Layer | Harmonics · Dominion |

> **Magic does not override the Material Plane's laws. It uses them.** *A fire technique still obeys thermodynamics; the practitioner simply supplies the energy from somewhere the physicist did not expect.*
> The Plane of Fate lies deepest, **and it is the one newcomers underestimate.** It is the ledger of what has actually happened between things — *every bond, oath, grudge, debt and act of recognition runs as a live current there.*

### The Veil

**Not a fourth plane. The threshold condition where one plane's law softens into another's** — and not a place you travel to.
> ***You have already touched it.*** Anyone who has felt a Wellspring under the ground, dreamed of a person who then arrived, sensed the weight of a Domain before seeing its effects, **or heard a room go quiet in a way that had nothing to do with sound** has had Veil contact and probably did not name it.
> *The regulation carries an irony worth holding onto. The Veil is not foreign territory requiring permission. It is the threshold every soul already touches by existing.*
>
> **What the law governs is not whether you interact with it, but whether you do so deliberately enough to survive the interaction intact.**

## Aether, Essence, and the Crystal

> *Two practitioners standing in the same room draw from the same ocean and produce entirely different water.*
**Aetheric Density** · How much medium is present. **It sets the ceiling on the output rate you can achieve in that location** — *which is why battles are fought over ground and not only over people.*
**Aetheric Residue** · The trace a working leaves. **Permanent, often subtle, always readable by someone patient enough.**
**Aetheric Saturation** · The danger state where Essence floods faster than it disperses. *It produces Crystal Fracture Events in everyone exposed, including the person who caused it.*
**Essence Signature** · **You cannot forge one and you cannot fully hide one. Only muddy it.**
| Layer | Function | Failure mode |
|---|---|---|
| **Essence Core** | Identity, memory, Traits | Fracture into Shards |
| **Aether Shell** | Turns inner truth into outward force | Clouding, then Rupture |
| **Attraction Layer** | Bonds, Domains, harmonisation | **Capture by Obsession Force** |

> *Learning which failure you are looking at is the difference between treating a wound and finishing a corpse.**
**Crystal States** · **Fractured** — split by trauma or overload; erratic casting and identity leak. *Not always fatal. Frequently permanent.* Refined **— the healthy baseline,** ***and rarer among veterans than recruitment posters suggest.*** Overgrown **— power exceeding architecture;** this is what forced advancement produces. Crystallized **— the self has become law. *Very few reach this.** Fewer remain recognisable afterward.*

## Temperance · The Sixteen Stages

> *"Temperance is not a buff. It is a confession of how much you've survived."*
**A Stage is not an achievement.** It is a structural reorganisation the Crystal undergoes when accumulated pressure exceeds its current architecture. *Each Stage changes what your numbers are permitted to mean, not what they add up to.*
| Stage | Name | Grade cap | Ceiling | Character |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | Murmuring | E | 50 | Essence as noise. Mood-driven, undirectable |
| II | Welling | D | 100 | Output stabilises. First affinities surface |
| III | Ascension | C | 175 | Path becomes visible. Technique turns deliberate |
| IV | Flourishing | B | 275 | Identity stable. First Spirit Axis bonds form |
| V | **Splintering** | B* | 350 | **CRISIS. Fractures open along emotional lines** |
| VI | Glory | A | 400 | Aura begins generating environmental effect |
| VII | Refraction | A→S | 475 | Dual sight. Traits externalise. **Domain seed** |
| VIII | Transcendence | S | 550 | First true Domain. **Sovereign threshold** |
| IX | Invocation | S→SS | 625 | Law begins imprinting on terrain |
| X | Realization | SS | 725 | Domain consolidates into a holding Realm |
| XI | **Dissonance** | SS | 750 *(unstable)* | **Fracture risk above 700. Denser if survived** |
| XII | Emanation | SSS | 950 | **Fractures become features that radiate** |
| XIII | Principality | X | 1,200 | First World Spirit threshold. Biomes obey |
| XIV | Zenith | EX | 1,500 | Full World Spirit. **The end of mortal coherence** |
| XV | Revelation | EX+ | 2,200 | Archon-participant territory. Beyond Bands |
| XVI | Apex | Absolute | uncapped | **A perpetual state. It does not end** |

### Thresholds · why you are stuck

**Two conditions must both be satisfied**: a quantitative floor, **and a Catalyst Condition** — a specific quality of experience the Crystal requires before it will authorise transition.
> A practitioner can sit quantitatively eligible for months and never advance because the Catalyst has not occurred.
>
> ***This is not a punishment. It is accuracy. The System does not advance a soul that has not genuinely changed.***
>
> **I → II** · First genuine contact with a Wellspring current under personal pressure. *Not observed. Not instructed. Touched, and survived.*
>
> **II → III** · First technique developed independently, **from your own understanding rather than received doctrine.**
>
> **III → IV** · Formation of the first genuine Spirit Axis — **a bond with another soul carrying mechanical weight.**
> **A warning about forced advancement.** Pushing stats above their sustainable Grade is not impossible. **It is catastrophic.** Such stats generate Fracture Events at an exponentially increasing rate until the stat collapses or the Threshold is passed through crisis.
>
> ***The Guild does not classify this as cheating. It classifies it as a manner of dying that takes longer than most.***
**Dissonance, and why the broken ones are worse.** Practitioners who pass *through* Stage XI rather than becoming trapped inside it arrive at Emanation **fractured and simultaneously denser than clean advancement produces.** Certain Sub-Stats — **Overflow, Detonation, Nullity** — only reach their true ceiling in a soul that has been through it.
> *The fractures are architectural features, not damage. If you ever fight someone who came out the far side of Dissonance, adjust your estimate upward and your expectations of mercy downward.*

## Bands and Tier Grade

**Level runs 1 to 500 and no further** — the structural limit of what a mortal Crystal can contain **before the distinction between practitioner and Principle collapses.** *Fail a Band gate and the counter simply halts, building Residual Strain until you pass or fracture.*
> **The differential rule is the single most practically important sentence in this primer.**
>
> **One full Grade above your opponent in the relevant stat wins that exchange without meaningful contest. Two Grades above is one-sided. Three Grades above means the lower party is not fighting a battle. They are surviving an event.**
| Grade | Stat range | Attack output |
|---|---|---|
| **Hollow** | 1–10 | below 60 J |
| **F** | 11–25 | 60–300 J |
| **E** | 26–50 | 300 J – 15 kJ *(street to wall)* |
| **D** | 51–100 | 15 kJ – 2.09 × 10⁷ J *(wall)* |
| **C** | 101–175 | to 1.05 × 10⁹ J *(building)* |
| **B** | 176–275 | aura generates passive pressure |
| **A** | 276–400 | sky hums within 200 m |
| **S** | 401–550 | 20–50 km projected force |
| **SS** | 551–725 | 300–800 km projected force |
| **SSS** | 726–950 | planetary |
| **X** | 951–1,200 | planetary to lunar orbital |
| **EX** | 1,201–1,500 | interplanetary |
| **EX+** | 1,501–2,200 | stellar to interstellar |

> **Note the shape of that table. Grade is not linear and was never meant to be read as a ladder with evenly spaced rungs.** *The gap between D and C is a building. The gap between SS and SSS is a planet.* **Anyone who tells you they are "only one Grade below" someone at the top of the chart has misunderstood the arithmetic they are standing inside.**

### Where the points come from

Five Bands yield **fifteen thousand** stat points. Thresholds through Stage XIII add roughly **nine thousand one hundred more.** *A full lifetime pool sits near twenty-four thousand one hundred, spread across twenty stats.*
> **Divide that honestly.** A Band II practitioner at Level 156 with Thresholds through Stage V holds about four thousand five hundred points — **two hundred and twenty-five per stat if spread evenly, solidly mid B-Grade, and entirely unremarkable.**
> ***If you are B-Grade, most of your stats are B-Grade. Specialists produce peaks by accepting valleys elsewhere, and the valleys are where they die.***

## Efficiency

**Flux Density (EU/g)** · The compression ratio. *The difference between a practitioner who empties in three techniques and one who fights an hour on the same pool.*
**Eta (η)** · The ratio of Essence spent to Essence that arrives as intended effect. **At 0.50 you waste half of every expenditure as heat, noise and structural bleed. At 0.90 you do not.** *Efficiency is the single most important variable in a long engagement.*
| Temperance | η | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| I–IV | 0.30–0.45 | **Enormous waste. Techniques feel like shouting into wind** |
| V | ~0.50 | First real efficiency. **The Crystal stops leaking** |
| VI | ~0.55 | The Shell begins conducting rather than containing |
| VII | ~0.60 | Dual sight permits **correcting your own waste live** |
| VIII | ~0.70 | Domain forms and **recycles ambient bleed** |
| IX–X | 0.75–0.80 | Law imprints on terrain. **Familiar ground pays dividends** |
| XI–XII | 0.85–0.90 | Presence optimises Aether flow in the vicinity |
| XIII | ~0.95 | Biomes carry the practitioner's doctrine |
| XIV | 1.0–1.2 | **Output exceeds expenditure. The environment co-authors** |

> **The last row is not free energy, and any tutor who describes it that way should be reported.** *It is the Continuum recognising an expression as law and supplementing it with ambient flow. The soul in question has become something the world agrees with.*
**Recovery** · **Passive**, ambient and effortless. **Active**, substantially faster, *requires you to stop fighting — which is the entire problem.* Crisis**, triggered by soul-cost events or genuine breakthrough mid-combat: *cannot be manufactured or relied upon.** It is the Crystal's reward for authentic contact with consequence.*

## Traits and Domains

> *"Belief, once survived, becomes law."*
**A Trait is the crystallised law of a soul.** *Traits are not learned. They awaken when four harmonics fall into coherence: the Core's emotional truth, the Attraction Layer's pattern of recognition, the Shell's behaviour of force, and the Temperance Field keeping the belief from collapsing under strain.*
**Three failure states worth memorising.** **Resonance Fatigue**, background drain from holding a Trait. **Rigidity Effect**, what happens when you act against your own Trait. **Shear Break**, repeated action against your own law until the Trait inverts — *and inversion is never chosen and rarely reversed.*
> **A Domain is not a technique. You do not deploy one the way you throw a punch.**
>
> ***A Domain forms because a soul has become heavy enough to dent reality, and reality has stopped resisting the dent.***
**Overlap resolution runs in strict order:** Temperance differential first, **two or more Stages simply overwriting**; then Dominion within the same band; then Wellspring compatibility; **and last, doctrine coherence.**
> *A Domain built from an honestly lived truth is structurally denser than one built from adopted belief. Coherence and Maturity are the tiebreakers — which means the Continuum's final arbitration is a question about whether you actually meant it.**
**Collapse produces a Resonance Shockwave** proportional to size and density, damaging everything inside the former territory. ***Including, invariably, the practitioner who lost it.***

## Attraction Force and Obsession Force

> *Two ways to make a working hold. Only one of them lets you keep the soul that made it.*
**Clean techniques are sustained by recognition.** Your Crystal recognises the Wellspring's law, the Continuum recognises your intent, **and the working persists because every party agrees it should.**
**Corrupted techniques are sustained by refusal.** The Crystal clutches the current rather than harmonising with it. **The Continuum registers the working as unauthorised, and your fixation keeps it running anyway.**
> **You can read anyone with two Sub-Stats.** High Harmonics Fidelity and high Tempering Coherence together mean **Attraction Force.** *Fidelity degrading while raw output stays high means Obsession Force — and it means the invoice is accruing.*
> *You will meet practitioners who are stronger than you and running on Obsession.*
>
> ***Do not envy them. The system will extract the cost, and it does not negotiate the timing.***

## Ten Things Newcomers Get Wrong

*Compiled from four centuries of induction records and rather a lot of funerals.*
**I** · Believing training raises Stage. **Training raises numbers. Only a Catalyst raises Stage** — *and a Catalyst is a category of lived experience the Crystal will not accept a forgery of.*
**II** · Reading Grade as a ladder with even rungs. **The distance from D to C is a building. The distance from SS to SSS is a planet.**
**III** · Neglecting Tempering. *An enormous Ardency inside a low-Tempering chassis is a very expensive way to fracture.*
**IV** · Assuming the higher number wins. **Aether Class decides how much of that number arrives.** *Class III at B-Grade routinely beats Class I at A-Grade, and the survivor writes the report.*
**V** · Chasing Wellspring count. **Three harmonisations held cleanly outperform seven held badly.**
**VI** · Treating a Domain as a weapon to deploy. **It is a consequence of density, it costs continuously, and losing it detonates.**
**VII** · Ignoring ground. **Veterans choose terrain before they choose techniques.**
**VIII** · Mistaking Obsession Force for talent. *Fidelity falling while output climbs is not a growth spurt. It is a countdown.*
**IX** · Underestimating anyone who came through Dissonance intact.
> **X** · Expecting the system to be fair.
>
> ***It is not fair. It is accurate. Those are different words, and the difference is the entire subject of this book.***

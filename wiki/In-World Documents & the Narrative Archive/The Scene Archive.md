# The Scene Archive

*The narrative record · four long-form works and twenty-five scenes*
> **This is an index, not a reproduction.** The prose lives in the source files; what is recorded here is **what each piece establishes, who it belongs to, and where it sits.**

---

## The Long-Form Works

| Work | Scale | What it carries |
|---|---|---|
| **Niran's Journey** | **The largest single work in the archive** | Opens in a clearing in the fold between two ridgelines, **where the cedar canopy thins enough to let light through.** The Yukari arc |
| **The Great War of the Moto Houses** | Second largest | Opens in the cold above **Kharven** — *the cold up here had a different quality than the cold of the capital.* The Moto civil conflict ⚠️ **Pre-amendment throughout.** *The prose still runs Ayame, Tenrai and Mizuyi. Kuroyuki in this text is Sodoku under the pre-amendment name, not a separate sibling. Not usable as canon until it takes the Moto pass* |
| **The Path of Sorrow** | Long-form | Opens in rain **that did not fall so much as occupy, filling the air between** |
| **The Destiny of the Moto** | Novella | *The village burned in columns.* **Not the frantic wind-driven chaos of an accidental fire** — which tells you in one line what kind of burning it was |
| **The Awakening of the God Hand and the Veil** | Novella | *The darkness was not empty.* **He had expected emptiness — the absence of the Physical Plane's condition.** The clearest prose companion to the Veil codex |
| **The Stone and Hammer** | Scene, long | **The Za'tarch fire-circle.** Gorrum's testimony, Vreth's challenge, Nix feeling the shift through her Spliced Runes, **and Drakar-Sei breaking the Vow of Stillness to hold the fire perfectly still.** *The scene the Goblinoid Peoples entry was built from* |

## The Cozbi Cycle

*Eleven scenes. The largest single-character body of prose in the archive, and the reason the antagonist reads as a system rather than a person.*
| Scene | Opening beat |
|---|---|
| **Ashwell Mysticism Demo** | *The village had been called Ashwell before the fires, and the name still fit, because ash was what remained* |
| **Construct Combat Scene** | The hunk of steel left the ground **without sound** — no grunt of effort, no scrape of metal finding purchase |
| **Root Severance and Rashani Threshold** | Candle wax and old paper filled the reliquary, **and underneath both sat something colder that had no business being there** |
| **Dragon Binding and Ashura's Call** | Cozbi had turned his attention to his other shadows — **the mirrors of himself seeded across the different veil currents** |
| **Rengai / Dhaerin Confrontation** | Cozbi astride **the Great Spider Impharous** the way another man might sit a favourite chair |
| **vs Rengai and Dhaerin, Combat** | Ash rose off Rengai's skin in a ring **before he'd finished deciding to call it** |
| **Pneuma Unravel Strike** | *"Do you want applause for your ferocity?"* — unhurried, amused, across a ruined crater |

## The Dhaerin and Rengai Scenes

**Dhaerin · Magnum Opus, Recursive Array** — *"Cozbi."* **His voice cracked on the second syllable, and he coughed blood onto the churned earth before he could finish it.**
**Dhaerin · Final Rite, Sacrament, Oblation, Rescind** — *If he had to die here, he decided, then he would at least choose the manner of it. The thought arrived quiet.*
**Rengai · Confrontation and Niran's Dream** — standing outside the containment room **with his hands closed into fists at his sides**, close enough to the door to be seen deciding.
> **Rengai · Farewell and Dhaerin** — *He had gone to see his son before anything else, because a man who put a war before his own child had already lost the argument about what he was fighting for.*

## The Niran and Yukari Scenes

**Naiser's Death, Cozbi's Genocide, Niran's Fall** — *Stone held the cold of the room the way old stone always holds cold, patient and total, and underneath the copper—* **The founding atrocity of the Yukari arc.**
**Niran · Mira Awakening** — *Niran woke to glyphs. They hung suspended in the still air above the cot, rotating slowly through pale gold.*
**Niran · Awakening and Haruki Reunion** — *"I am…"* **Reborn Niran held the pause the way his old body used to hold a breath before a dive.**
**Niran · Reply to Haruki** — the air around the Yukari in the room thickened; **the light folded and flowed toward him.**

## Restricted

> Scenes involving characters belonging to other creators hold **only the authored half.** The restricted character's actions, dialogue, gesture and interiority are left as open beats.
>
> **Cozbi's Defensive Response to Dova** is the clearest example: the scene is written entirely from inside Cozbi, **and Dova's hand closing is registered only as something Cozbi felt arriving a second early.**

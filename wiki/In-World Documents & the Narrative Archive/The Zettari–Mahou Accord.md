# The Zettari–Mahou Accord

*Being the Conditions of Alliance, Drafted by Kwon Mu-jin under the Instruction and Seal of Dawn Guard, Sovereign of the Zettari*
> 
> > *"An Accord that cannot survive its own honest review has no value to either house."*

---

## Preamble

Let it be recorded, in the hand of **Kwon Mu-jin, called the Teacher**, of the Mahou line, drafted at the instruction and under the seal of **Dawn Guard, Sovereign of the Zettari** — on the night following the Sovereign's inquiry into the nature of Legion Summoning **and the crimes of Cozbi Mahuo.**
> **This document does not claim to speak for every surviving Mahou.** It speaks for those named within it, and for the terms under which further names may be added.
> It is drafted honestly, **without softening what our bloodline has done or overstating what it deserves.**
>
> **Where the Mahou have failed, this record does not conceal the failure. Where the Zettari have failed, this record does not conceal that failure either.**

---

## Article I · On Recognition

The Mahou line **shall no longer be treated as a danger without a name attached to its defense.** The Zettari Sovereign recognizes the Mahou as a lineage capable of lawful practice, **distinct from the crimes of any single practitioner** — including Cozbi Mahuo, **whose actions this Accord condemns without qualification and without appeal to inherited grief as excuse.**
> **This recognition is not extended to shelter wrongdoing.**
>
> Any Mahou practitioner found replicating Cozbi's methods — **whether through stolen souls, coerced compacts, or manufactured atrocity** — forfeits the protection of this Accord entirely **and shall be surrendered for judgment rather than concealed.**

---

## Article II · On the Marriage

A union is proposed between **Dawn Guard, Sovereign of the Zettari**, and **Ara Min Mahuo**, of the Mahou line, **as the foundation binding this Accord into something the Old World cannot dismiss as temporary goodwill.**
**The following conditions govern this union absolutely.**
| Condition | Provision |
|---|---|
| **First · Consent** | The consent of Ara Min Mahuo is required **and may not be presumed, implied, or extracted through family obligation.** She shall meet the Sovereign directly — **without her brother present, without any Mahou elder standing near enough to make her silence read as duty** |
| **Second · Refusal** | **Refusal shall not dissolve this Accord.** Should she decline, the alliance shall be renegotiated upon another foundation, **and no Mahou shall be asked to replace her under compulsion** |
| **Third · Standing** | **Neither house may treat this marriage, once made, as ownership.** She retains her voice, her judgment, and her standing to name any failure of this Accord — **including failures committed by her own husband** — without being treated as an enemy of the alliance for having named them |

---

## Article III · On the Obligations of the Mahou

*In exchange for recognition and protection, to be upheld without exception.*
**A recognized representative** shall speak for the line's lawful practice before the Zettari and before any court that requires an answer. *Until Ara Min Mahuo's standing is settled, this office is held by Kwon Mu-jin.*
**A written code** shall distinguish **willing compact from conscription, contracted spirit from stolen soul, manufactured construct from harvested body.** Maintained as a living document, amended as understanding deepens, **never held static out of institutional pride.**
**An archive of lawful disciplines** shall be preserved and made available to Zettari officers, **so that soldiers may learn the difference between a spirit that chose to serve and one that was never asked.**
**Teachers capable of certifying practitioners** shall be named and protected, **so that the art may be taught openly rather than passed in secrecy from hand to hand.**
> Any Mahou practitioner found practicing Cozbi's methods **shall be surrendered by the Mahou themselves, rather than sheltered behind shared blood.**

---

## Article IV · On the Eastern Question

Kwon Mu-jin is authorized to approach Mahou households residing within the **Elven Kingdom of Eresse**, under one restriction:
> **Their identities remain sealed and undisclosed until each household consents individually to be named or approached.**
> 
> ***No peace shall be attempted at the cost of exposing families who survived through secrecy.***
**The purpose of this approach is a conversation, not a submission.** No Eressean party shall be told that acceptance is expected, **nor shall any refusal be treated as an act of hostility.** Dawn Guard shall receive any Eressean representative who agrees to speak under protected passage, **without demanding a treaty before the first exchange concludes.**

---

## Article V · On Review and Failure

> **This Accord binds both houses to honesty above comfort.**
>
> Should the Zettari begin treating protection as occupation, **the Mahou representative is obligated to name it plainly, in whatever room the Sovereign is standing in, without waiting for permission to speak.**
>
> Should the Mahou begin treating protection as immunity from consequence, **the Zettari are obligated to name that failure with equal plainness.**
>
> **An Accord that cannot survive its own honest review has no value to either house, and shall not be permitted to survive on ceremony alone.**

---

## Closing Notation

> Written in a single night, by lamplight, in a Zettari camp still cold from the day's teaching, **and sealed with the understanding that ink outlasts intention.**
> 
> **What is written here shall be judged by what it produces in the years that follow, and not by the sincerity with which it was first proposed.**
*Recorded by Kwon Mu-jin, the Teacher, of the Mahou line.*

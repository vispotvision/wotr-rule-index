# Characters

> Every other section of this wiki describes systems. **This one describes the people the systems happened to.**

---

## How a Life Is Recorded

The character record runs on a seventeen-part skeleton, and the order of the parts is an argument.
| Sections | What they establish |
|---|---|
| **I – III** · Identity, Soul Architecture, Wellspring Harmonizations | Who, what Stage, and **which laws answer them.** The Catalyst Event sits in the first section because everything downstream is a consequence of it |
| **IV – VII** · Stats, Physical Force, Aether Flow | The measurable body. Strike force, durability, reserve, output, efficiency. **The part a Measurewright could confirm** |
| **VIII – X** · Traits, Domain, Techniques | What they can do, **and what it costs them to do it.** Every technique carries a Counterplay field and the field is mandatory |
| **XI** · Spirit Axes | **Who they are bonded to.** Sited deliberately between the techniques and the resistances, because in this world **the people someone loves are a load-bearing part of what they can survive** |
| **XII – XV** · Resistances, Physical Description, Psychology, Equipment | What they can take, what they look like, what they are, what they carry |
| **XVI – XVII** · Temperance Record, Fracture Log | The ladder climbed and **the price of each rung.** The Log lists every fracture as consolidated or unconsolidated — *and the unconsolidated ones are the character* |

> **The Fracture Log is the same document as a physician's casebook, written from the other side.**
>
> The Log records the event and is proud of it. **The casebook records the accommodation and is worried about it.** A Splintering appears in one as a fracture and in the other as *nine months of altered sleep, a change in gait that never came back, and a marriage that did not survive the year.*
>
> **The practitioner will insist on the first reading and be irritated by the second.**

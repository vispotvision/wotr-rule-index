# Pack Twenty — The Clearance

*Every open docket item ruled. Both conflicts closed. Büri confirmed dead and the sweep scoped. Ruling codes run R20C-* to avoid collision with the Naming Guide Amendment's R20- *series.*
Authority: Pack Eighteen repealed the ratification protocol. Everything here is live on reading. The full reasoning sits in `WOTR_Companion_Guide_Amendments_Pack_Twenty.md`; this page is the operative list.

## The two conflicts

**C-001, the proofed round. Resolved: the ban is dead.** Pack Eleven §3's firearms clause cited Pack Seven as its whole authority; a rule does not survive the repeal of the thing it quotes. A Measurewright, proof-master or opponent in diagnostic voice may explain why a proofed round defeats proofed plate. The surviving constraint is R20C-26: the steel stops the shot, never the working. Explain metallurgy and terminal ballistics; never let the coupling be the thing that saved him.
**C-002, Filemu Agamalu's Ava-name. Resolved: it survives.** The strike killed Polynesian register as applied to *Moto* naming. Filemu is not Moto, and the Ava ceremony is her own house's conferring on her own house. Le Ie Tuuina Atu stands. What died is the *addition*, the claim that the Agamalu ceremony confers an Ava-name on Moto governance figures.

## The Moto

- **R20C-6.** Lines Five to Seven get functions. **Shirogane** (silver): the mint and the assay — they set what a coin weighs and certify what a bar is. **Akagane** (copper): the forge and the conduit-metal — they cast and seal the housings that carry the Draw, which puts a hand on the tap. **Amagiri** (rain, mist): the roads and the post — the passes, the way-houses, the runners.
- **R20C-7.** Muken's children stay contested, and the contest is the canon. Two claimant lines, neither provable, the Accord recognising neither because the open question is profitable. Never resolved in prose; anyone who states it as fact is stating a position.
- **R20C-8.** *Hataraki no Sho* confirmed as the received name.
- **R20C-9.** **What ruined the ancestral seat: the seat was sited on a Core concentration and the line worked it.** No battle, no plague, no enemy. They held the deepest draw anyone ever had, for generations, and the residual saturation Saruul reads there is the day the site gave out under a full household drawing at once. The Moto descend from whoever was standing furthest from the tap. What nobody knows: why that site and no other. Every survey since reads it as ordinary depleted ground. *Largest call in the pack.*

## The Agamalu and Vāimoana

- **R20C-10.** Vāimoana confirmed as the island name. Vāitele and Tuāoi dropped.
- **R20C-11.** A confederation of aiga governed by fono. No king, no standing army, no Accord charter. Live.
- **R20C-12.** The most stable Essence saturation recorded anywhere in the four quarters. Nothing drifts at Vāimoana. Live.
- **R20C-13.** What the Agamalu declined to disclose four times: their own saturation readings. The fono holds centuries of them. The reason is not secrecy. The readings show a stability nobody on the island can account for, an admission of ignorance invites a survey, and a survey invites a licence. The Division files it as obstruction because the alternative reading is worse.
- **R20C-14.** Filemu's voice is lauga register. The sentence arrives carrying the weight of something already decided; the volume stays low and the cadence does the work. Under stress the cadence holds and the length drops. Lauga compresses.
- **R20C-15.** Taulagi and Afasoa are re-anchored, not renamed. The Agamalu register is alive and legitimate, so both read as Vāimoana-descended. Two people carrying the confederation's naming standing a long way from any fono is a thread, not a defect.
- **R20C-16.** Sonzai gets a **record card, not a character card**: identity, standing, the Erasure act as a dated event, relations, reference art. No voice section, no interiority, no dialogue samples. Those are Isaac's.

## The Draw Age — Pack Eleven ratified whole

Hydrology not combustion. Cities on sites. Class geography by draw distance. Apparatus inherits its Family's failure modes. Wells are extraction, never adventure. All live.
- **R20C-17 (R11-A).** **Sites recharge, and it does not help.** Decades to centuries, and the rate falls with every working — eighty years the first time, two hundred the second. Short-run: managed and corrupt, because the Guild sells renewal honestly. Long-run: predatory and doomed, because the curve bends one way. The Thin Weeks are this arriving early in the periphery.
- **R20C-18 (R11-B).** **Commoners can buy draw legally and not practically.** Metered utility, licensed, priced at a margin the margin cannot pay. Lamps over the pool, none at the thin edge. The tariff is public and technically open to anyone.
- **R20C-19 (R11-C).** Tech ceiling. **Print: yes** — mechanical, cheap, ungovernable, and the reason the Accord's register is out of date. **Telegraph: yes, as a Draw counterpart** — Guild line, Guild price, information as licensed commodity. **Rail: no** — haulage stays animal and water, which keeps the passes and the Amagiri post load-bearing. **Photography: no** — the counterpart is the fixed-plate impression, slow and Guild-controlled, used for proofing and record. Nobody has a portrait of their mother.
- **R20C-20 (R11-D).** Wells generate no entities. The site is the entire threat.
- **R20C-21 (R11-E).** Both names, class-marked. Guild register *a Core Concentration*; common tongue *a Well*; going in is *a descent*; the people are *delvers*, or *well-rats* if you dislike them.
- **R20C-22.** The three ammunition tiers ratified, subject to R20C-26.

## Docket 18 — the Zettari, closed

**R20C-48. The Zettari sit on thin ground, and their culture is the discipline of working a poor draw well.** Their site was worked longest and gave out first. They are the best smiths in the four quarters because they had to be, and insufferable about it because being best is the only thing that ever compensated them.
- **Food.** Eaten standing, at the fire. Flat bread cooked on the forge shoulder, sour and dense. Sitting down to a meal on a working day is a statement.
- **The greeting.** *Who has your fire?* Whose hearth you draw on: courtesy, status check, and who is responsible for you, in four words.
- **Oaths and obscenity.** Sworn on a quench, and the water is kept. Quench-barrels are a house's record, stacked and dated; a barrel poured out is a vow broken in public. The obscenity is **cold**. Anything made without fire is a fraud, and calling a man's work cold starts fights.
- **Colloquial time.** Heats, not hours. *Three heats back.* *It'll be two heats.* The week does not exist as a unit.
- **The dead.** Cremated in the forge; the ash goes into the next quench. Every Zettari blade carries somebody and a smith can usually tell you who.
- **The household object.** The tally-stick of heats owed. Fire is lent constantly between houses; a drawer of sticks in your favour is real wealth and everyone knows the count.
- **Exchange.** They sell hours of the fire, never objects. The unit of account is the heat. Accord factors find the pricing incomprehensible, which is why Zettari work sells badly abroad.
- **Proverbs.** *Cold makes nothing.* / *The barrel remembers.* / *A borrowed fire is a borrowed name.* / *You cannot hurry a heat and you cannot slow a life.* / *Ask the hands, not the man.* / *Thin ground, deep work.*
- **The body.** Hands are a curriculum vitae and are read as one — burn-scatter dates the apprenticeship, scar patterns say what he has worked and at what temperature. A Zettari shakes hands palm-up. Concealing your hands is close to a confession.
- **Signature items:** the quench-barrel, the tally-stick, *who has your fire*, and *cold* as the standing insult.

## Racial voice — the three gaps, closed

- **R20C-50. Fleshshaper Goblin.** Old Vaross substrate. Verbs of making dominate the clause. A Fleshshaper does not say *it broke*, they say *someone unmade it*, and if nobody did then it was never whole. **No passive voice at all.** Possession marked on the body and not the object: *knife-at-my-hand*, never *my knife*. Counting by pairs and hands. Discourse marker: a confirming particle at clause-end, roughly *yeh*, dropped when the speaker stops caring whether you follow. Bodies discussed as material, their own included, which is the part that unsettles everyone else.
- **R20C-51. Winter Eladrin.** Speech is the secondary channel; stillness and the angle of the hands are primary. **Craft rule: meaning arrives in a physical clause before the spoken one, and the spoken clause is shorter than the gesture that preceded it.** They never repeat themselves — a repeated sentence accuses the listener of inattention. No greetings exist; arrival is announced by being seen, and a Winter Eladrin who says hello is being deliberately hostile.
- **R20C-52. The Celestial Host** (also closes R20-2). The Host has **no native register**, and that is the ruling. It speaks in the language of whoever recorded it, which is why Verantha, Kaethren Vareth, Dazgrim and Urstia do not match and never will. **Those four names are confirmed canon, not placeholders.** What is consistent is grammar: **the Host speaks entirely in the completed aspect.** No future tense, no conditional, no question. A Host that wants something says it has happened.
- **R20C-53.** Remaining element inventories (Mahuo, Yukari, the Elven branches, Beastkin soul-names) are built on first use and entered the same session. Not a standalone build.

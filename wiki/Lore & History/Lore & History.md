# Lore & History

> The codices elsewhere in this wiki are institutional. **They were written by Divisions, for Divisions, and their voice is the voice of a body that expects to outlast its authors.**
>
> What follows is different. **A recovered journal in three volumes, written by one man across a life, in his own hand, at the request of no one.** And a cosmological classification of the one thing every soul touches by existing and almost nobody can name.

---

## Why These Two Sit Together

**The Alftian Codex** and **The Veil** describe the same territory from opposite ends.
The Veil is the formal account: **Liminal Interface, Inter-Planar Threshold**, governed by Transference under Sylorin, with Temperance gates and certification requirements and an Accord regulatory apparatus. It tells you what the threshold is and what it costs to cross deliberately.
The Alftian Codex is **what it looks like from inside a life.** A terrace-farmer's son who read astronomy for pleasure, three friends who fell ill, a necromantic commission nobody should have taken, and a two-hundred-and-sixty-year-old unfinished decision waiting in a mountain hollow for one specific person to walk into its radius.
> One document explains the mechanism. **The other is the mechanism happening to somebody**, and it arrives at a correction to the Hermetic Principle of Correspondence that the formal literature does not contain.

## The Voice of the Codex

| Feature | What it means for reading it |
|---|---|
| **First person, retrospective, unreliable by construction** | Vis Trismegistus is writing about his own errors and **does not know he is in one until the third volume.** The Codex's most important finding is a correction to something it asserted with confidence two volumes earlier |
| **Transcribed by the Silent Archivists of Naln** | All three volumes carry the same transcription line. **The Archivists are not otherwise described anywhere in this wiki** |
| **Classified Restricted Access, High Wellspring Sensitivity** | Volume One is Hermetic Lore. Volume Two adds **Mortalis-Adjacent.** Volume Three is filed and sealed at the Urbis Archivum, cross-referencing a Level IV clearance case file |
| **It names the Seven Cacodaemonic Corruptions before the Heresiology exists** | Vis writes the first three from his own experience and **flags the remaining four as work he has not yet done.** *The Heresiology of Alchemy is the completed version of a book a dead man left unfinished* |

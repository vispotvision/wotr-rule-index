# The Heresiology of Alchemy

*A Concordant Treatise on Lawful Alchemy, Cacodaemonic Drift, [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/The Sixty Wellsprings|Wellspring]] Injury, and the Civil Management of Metaphysical Error*

They buried Odger Marrable twice, and the first funeral is still the best party Hartle Ferry has ever thrown.

He had been master of the dyers' company for twenty-eight years. Marrable Red was his, the red that ran the river the color of a split pomegranate every market day and sold as far as Urbis, and he had kept the recipe in his head his whole life out of plain spite and taken it with him. So in the spring of 661 IC his three daughters paid sixty gold to a licensed spagyrist (an alchemist who parts a medicine into its principles and puts them back together) named Damaris Corbie to go and get it. She spent nine weeks in his dye house among his vats and his aprons, working Anamnesis (the Wellspring, one of the sixty governing ideas that shape how a soul uses Aether, that reads the record a thing keeps of its own past) off the residue of forty years, and Mortalis (the Wellspring of passage and the lawful withdrawal of presence) over the body. The town turned out for the procession in company colors, saffron and woad and weld-green, with shawms in front and a cart of free ale behind. At the graveside, with the company treasurer halfway into a eulogy nobody was listening to, Odger Marrable sat up on the bier in his best scarlet gown, complained about the ale, corrected the treasurer's figures for the year of the flood, named the man his eldest daughter had been climbing out of a window to meet, and dictated the Red to the first clerk who could find a pen. Four hundred people went out of their minds with joy. They carried him home on their shoulders and they carried Damaris Corbie behind him, her aura standing up over Dyers' Row in long black-violet streamers where the whole street could admire it.

Within the year the company had seated him as master again, a magistrate had taken his signature on a lease, and Corbie had a hall with her name over the door and commissions from three counties. Then came the third summer. Every plum on the Ferry ripened in a week and set no stone that would grow. Dyers who had never met woke from the same dream of the same vat. Dogs would not cross his threshold and the swallows quit the roofs of Dyers' Row. The Red came out of the vat the color of liver and came off on the skin. And Odger told the story of the abbot's horse at every dinner, word for word, with the same pause in the same place, and wept when someone pointed this out, because he believed himself whole. When the Accord's party of four came up the river, Corbie met them at her door and explained the elegance of what she had done, with diagrams and real delight, and later she told her tribunal that reality had not yet understood her. Hartle Ferry buried Odger Marrable for the second time that autumn, at his daughters' expense, with the same shawms. He asked them not to, in his own voice, all the way to the hole. Nobody has made that red since, and there is still no plum worth eating on the Ferry.

I have carried this book for twenty-two years, and the afternoon I would have you remember is the first one, the ale and the shoulders and the daughters weeping with happiness, because nobody on Dyers' Row that day was a fool. Every sign was there to be read on the morning of the procession and every one of them is listed below. The Guild issued these pages for whoever has to stand at the graveside with a full cart of ale behind them and say so out loud.

> Issued in the style of a Guild Accord doctrinal manuscript for use by magistrates, monastic instructors, licensed spagyrists, battlefield adjudicators, archivists, infirmary-supervisors, relic wardens, **and all officers entrusted with the distinction between difficult craft and forbidden transformation.**
>
> **A civilization that practices alchemy yet lacks a language for its inversions will eventually mistake catastrophe for genius.**
>
> **This book exists to prevent that stupidity.**

## The Thesis

All true alchemy participates in the Sevenfold Great Work. **Each of the seven stages possesses a lawful expression and a corrupt inversion**, and these inversions **repeat.** They are spiritual and procedural deviations that a trained eye can name in advance, and nothing about them is random. The alchemist, the laboratory, the patient, the institution, and the surrounding territory **all record those deviations.**
> **The earlier a corruption is named, the less blood, memory, soil, and sky must be spent correcting it.**
The older Hermetic masters wrote in symbols, aphorisms, and witness-testimony, and their language remains indispensable. **Yet states, infirmaries, monasteries, and war colleges require an additional grammar:** distinctions, thresholds, procedures, signs of contamination, and lawful responses. **Heresiology is that grammar.**

*An eccentric is welcome at this table. The concern is with drift, and with the point at which curiosity ceases to illuminate and begins to consume.*

## Alchemy in Its Lawful Estate

Alchemy differs from ordinary craft in that **it alters significance as well as shape.** A lawful alchemical act changes **what a thing can mean inside the lattice of reality.** A tincture ceases to be a mixture and becomes a carrier of directed correction. An ash ceases to be waste and becomes **the readable memory of what was burned away.**

The reason this can be done at all is the reason it can go so badly. **A Wellspring is an alchemical and a spiritual transformation, and a concept: the governing idea that transforms how a soul uses Aether, as well as a source of power to pull from.** There are sixty, fixed and counted, and each is a type of the Aether practitioners pull from the world around them, affecting the Soul Crystal (literally the soul) in its own way. One law works in the vessel and in the one who tends it. A Measurewright (the Guild's certified assessor of practitioners and workings) reads Judicium as a physical law, a bench reads it as the discernment that parts a charge, and a confessor reads it as what discernment does to a soul that survives it, and each of the three professions considers the other two amateurs. For this treatise all three readings are evidence, because a fault in any one of them turns up in the others.
> **All serious alchemy operates simultaneously in four theaters.**
>
> **Material** · vessels, reagents, temperatures, distillation, ash, salts, vapors, coagulates.
>
> **Glyphic** · inscribed order, naming, containment sequence, lawful syntax.
>
> **Aetheric** · which of the sixty Aethers is at work, resonance, saturation, contamination, ambient field response.
>
> **Moral** · motive, cost-bearing, truthfulness of intent, relation between practitioner and process.
>
> **Any system that describes only the first theater is chemistry. Any system that ignores the fourth theater is already in danger.**

### The Great Work

Calcination · Dissolution · Separation · Conjunction · Fermentation · Distillation · Coagulation.

**In the lawful art, these are disciplines imposed upon both matter and maker, and only then procedural phases.**
> What burns in the vessel burns in the practitioner. **What refuses to dissolve in the alembic often refuses to dissolve in the ego.** What is falsely joined in compound is often falsely joined in motive.
**The Great Work is therefore a method and a judgment.**

Six of the seven stages draw on a Wellspring's law, and the seventh runs on something the soul already carries. This is why a stage perverted counts as an injury: the practitioner has been lying in a language the world is obliged to answer in.

| Stage of the Work | The bench's word | The law it draws on | What that law does to the soul |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Calcination** | The Burning | **Cinerion**, the Ash Veil: strong dry heat drives off what is volatile and leaves what is fixed | Temperance (the climb of a soul from Stage to Stage) through aftermath |
| **Dissolution** | The Taking | **Dissolution**, the Unbinding Stream: cohesion unraveled into the menstruum | Temperance through unbinding, the soul dissolving what no longer serves its architecture *(the confessors' reading)* |
| **Separation** | The Parting | **Judicium**, the Wellspring of Truth: discernment, the parts told apart and taken apart | Temperance through discernment: order, measure and consequence |
| **Conjunction** | The Wedding | **No Wellspring of its own.** It runs on Attraction, how reality, memory and people bind to a soul, and a Conjunction corrupted injures Attraction itself | — |
| **Fermentation** | The Rot through the Feeding | **Rebirthine**, the Cycle Eternal: breakdown pays for growth | Temperance through loss, destruction treated as the first step of reconstruction *(the confessors' reading)* |
| **Distillation** | The still | **Sublimare**, the Rising Breath: parting by boiling point, the spirit risen and returned | Temperance through ascent |
| **Coagulation** | The Setting | **Coagulatio**, the Returning Stone: a charge sets round one grain, and the grain grows on after the hand is gone | Temperance through settling, the scattered self finding its seed *(the confessors' reading)* |

*Calcination and Distillation are bench operations and no soul harmonizes with either as such. A soul harmonizes with Cinerion or Sublimare, and the bench borrows from them.*

### The three estates

**Lawful** · transparent in cost, properly anchored, proportionate to the practitioner's station, **and capable of review by peers or superiors.**

**Liminal** · not yet forbidden, **but presenting unresolved uncertainty** concerning residue, Wellspring strain, psychological displacement, or contamination.

**Forbidden** · proceeds by concealment, coercive substitution of cost, counterfeit identity, unlawful resurrection claims, synthetic law bypass, **or persistent violation of environmental and soul integrity.**

## The Seven Cacodaemonic Corruptions

*Cacodaemonic is the Accord's word for a stage of the Work that has gone over to its own inversion. On the docks they say a man has gone to the mirror and leave it there. An investigator needs seven words where the docks need one, because each of the seven has a different cure, and the cure for one will feed another.*

### Calcination Perverted · The Corruption of Conviction

Lawful calcination burns away vanity, inherited falsehood, and the crust of self-certainty. **It leaves the practitioner poorer in illusion and richer in humility.**
> **Perverted calcination burns away doubt instead.** The practitioner emerges **sealed**, with nothing clarified. The fire that should have made them corrigible **makes them unanswerable.**
>
> Such a soul has lost the sentence *I may be wrong.* **What it says in that sentence's place is: reality has not yet understood me.**
**Signs** · Inability to revise theory after contradictory results. Increasingly punitive treatment of assistants, patients, or critics. **Reclassification of failed experiments as misunderstood successes.** Ritual language of inevitability, chosenness, singular genius, or historical exemption. **Hardened resonance: the aura feels dry, angular, and non-responsive to moral appeal.**

**Common expressions** · Mortalis practitioners insisting an impression-body (a body shaped to carry a dead person's trace, which knows their names and is someone else) **is the deceased whole person.** State alchemists weaponizing purification rites as moral justification for mass erasure. Teachers who turn initiation into obedience cults.

**Danger** · Conviction-corrupted alchemists are often brilliant. **They will destroy ten true observations rather than surrender one false certainty.**

**Corrective** · **Contradiction must be imposed from outside.** Peer review, adversarial review, witness-audits, and in severe cases supervised ritual silence. *A conviction-corrupted practitioner should never be left alone with a theory they have begun to love more than truth.*

### Dissolution Perverted · The Corruption of Submission

Lawful dissolution softens the self into relation with a greater law. **The practitioner learns permeability without annihilation.**

Perverted dissolution occurs when the practitioner dissolves **into another will in place of Wellspring truth**: a patron, teacher, order, monarch, lover, dead ideal, or institution. **This is the corruption of talent without boundary.**

**Signs** · Research produced under visible reluctance yet defended with exhausted loyalty. Habitual use of *I had no choice*, *it was necessary*, **or *someone would have done it anyway*.** Emotional flattening after acts that should have provoked moral protest. **Substitution of access, approval, or belonging for judgment.**

**Common expressions** · The gifted assistant writing what the master wished written. The court scholar engineering taboo procedures **in exchange for archive access.** The medical savant rationalizing increasingly invasive protocols **because the purse is noble, sacred, or politically untouchable.**
> **Danger.** Submission-corrupted alchemists become instruments for stronger corruptions in others.
>
> **They are how monstrous work acquires clean handwriting.**
**Corrective** · **Remove the practitioner from the commanding field.** Break patronage, isolate influence, restore deliberative selfhood, **and require a written account of what they believed they were surrendering.** *Without recovered boundary, no higher correction is stable.*

### Separation Perverted · The Corruption of Non-Commitment

Lawful separation distinguishes the essential from the accidental. **It should culminate in judgment and action.**

Perverted separation produces **a spectator soul**: clear-sighted, verbally precise, **ethically evasive.** The practitioner sees exactly what is wrong **and then exempts themselves from consequence by retreating into analysis.**

**Signs** · **Perfect diagnosis, absent intervention.** Accumulation of notes in place of decisions. Fascination with moral structure coupled to refusal of moral burden. Repeated postponement **under the guise of nuance, complexity, or future inquiry.** *Aura behavior of cool detachment, often mistaken for wisdom.*

**Common expressions** · The archivist who knows a manuscript is dangerous and shelves it without alert. The teacher who recognizes drift in a student and says nothing **because the scene is not yet unbearable.** **The investigator who documents a coming catastrophe more carefully than they obstruct it.**
> **Danger.** Non-commitment allows every other corruption to ripen.
>
> **Whole regions are lost less often to ignorance than to lucid cowardice.**
**Corrective** · **Decision under witness.** Once separation has occurred, action must follow. If it does not, the stage has inverted. **All practitioners trained in advanced theory should be bound by intervention thresholds, because private conscience alone has a long record of waiting until after lunch.**

### Conjunction Perverted · The Corruption of Instrumental Union

Lawful conjunction **marries what has been truthfully separated.**

Perverted conjunction joins things **for utility rather than truth.** The practitioner ceases to ask whether two elements belong together and asks only **whether the union produces power, access, leverage, continuity, pleasure, or political advantage.** Conjunction has no Wellspring of its own and runs on Attraction, so this is the one corruption that injures the binding itself, and the people bound by it.

**Signs** · Forced soul-pairings, coerced bindings, unauthorized grafts. **Reagents that only stabilize through suffering, fear, or domination.** Relationships treated as metaphysical infrastructure rather than covenant. **Research language that calls persons *anchors*, *keys*, or *fuel vectors*.** Hybrid compounds whose stability is maintained through continuous suppression.

**Common expressions** · Counterfeit resurrections assembled from grief, trace, and desire. Marriage of Wellspring signatures without compatible moral or glyphic mediation. **Dynastic blood-alchemy that treats lineages as breeding stock for lawful effect.**
> **Instrumental union is the polite face of desecration.** It produces useful results quickly, **which is why courts, armies, and panicked physicians are especially vulnerable to it.**
>
> **Every conjunction above minor grade must answer three questions before sanction.**
>
> What is being joined?
>
> By what right are they joined?
>
> **Who bears the cost if the union refuses the joining?**
>
> If the answer to the third question is **"someone absent, powerless, dead, or unnamed," the conjunction is already heretical.**
>
> **The three questions are also the Class IV questions (Class IV being carry rendered from anything that bore a Soul Crystal, a monster's core being the lawful article and a person's Crystal the worst crime in every realm), and the register does not ask them.** What is joined: a borrowed law and a living Crystal. By what right: an Attribution (the entry by which the Bench, the register's court, names a product's source) that names the class and is silent on the source. Who bears the cost if the union refuses the joining: **the donor, who is by definition dead, and the recipient, who was not told whose law they were seated to.** A course of Essence Drafts (Drafts rendered from monster cores to feed tempering) puts the question a dose at a time, and a breakthrough Draft, which supplies the Catalyst in liquid form and sells the buyer a Stage, puts it all at once.
>
> *Every Threshold product (a Draft that carries a soul through the melt between Stages by giving it a seed it did not grow) above T5 (the fifth item Tier) answers the third question with an absent, powerless, dead and unnamed party. The Bench has ruled on all of them. The Bench was never asked this question.*

### Fermentation Perverted · The Corruption of Catastrophic Germination

Lawful fermentation permits breakdown so that truer life may arise. **It is rot in service to transformation.**

**Perverted fermentation begins to value the rot itself.** Crisis becomes laboratory. Ruin becomes methodology. **The practitioner starts preserving instability because instability is productive.**

**Signs** · **Deliberate prolongation of dangerous states for data yield.** Phraseology that romanticizes collapse, plague, grief, war, or mania as generative necessities. Experimental sites where **improvement for a few coincides with cumulative contamination for many.** Fascination with civil emergencies as research windows. *Aetheric weather around the laboratory becoming erratic, feverish, fecund in wrong ways.*

**Common expressions** · Regional activations of a Wellspring site (a place where one type of Aether pools, called a Core in common speech once it is worked) justified by the useful works they complete. Battlefield alchemy that studies trauma as an energy multiplier. **Infirmaries that tolerate low-level soul-splintering because the success rate remains politically impressive.**
> **Danger.** This corruption is beloved by reformers, visionaries, and progress cults.
>
> **It creates the obscene condition in which real good is visibly accomplished by methods that quietly poison the future.**
>
> **Corrective:** no yielded benefit excuses ambient corruption. **If the work cannot survive review by timescales longer than triumph, it is not lawful fermentation.**

### Distillation Perverted · The Corruption of Sanitized Truth

Lawful distillation refines. **Perverted distillation strips away context, pain, and moral texture until only technique remains.** It is the corruption **by which horror becomes procedure and the dead are translated into elegant diagrams.**

**Signs** · **Perfectly clean theory built on filthy practice.** Reports with exceptional precision and **astonishingly absent human cost.** Reduction of persons to residue classes without accompanying ethical commentary. Archival habits that privilege reproducibility over reality. **Tendency to speak of unbearable acts in tranquil, antiseptic language.**

**Common expressions** · Necromantic manuals **whose warnings survive only as footnotes.** Medical notes that preserve dosage and erase despair. **State dossiers that classify districts as acceptable loss-fields for the sake of doctrinal clarity.**
> **Danger.** Sanitized truth is difficult to resist **because it sounds sane.**
>
> **It is often the stage by which a civilization teaches itself to admire the shape of an atrocity.**
>
> **Corrective:** every distillate must be accompanied by provenance, burden, casualty, and contamination record. **Where human cost has been removed from the page, the page is suspect. The cleaner the manuscript, the more aggressively it should be interrogated.**

### Coagulation Perverted · The Corruption of Tyrannous Finality

Lawful coagulation gives durable form to what has been rightly transformed. **Perverted coagulation mistakes interim form for absolute completion.** It ossifies doctrine, enthrones one method as universal law, **and punishes any further refinement as treason.**

**Signs** · Claims that a process, creed, state formula, or salvific compound **has ended debate.** Demand for obedience to fixed procedure despite altered conditions. **Crystallized institutions that convert living law into dead command.** Relics, compounds, or state rites **that require continuous denial of contrary evidence to remain stable.** *Soul-pattern of terrible stillness, often felt as oppressive inevitability.*

**Common expressions** · **A throne's purification decrees made permanent after emergency use.** Schools that canonize provisional findings into sacred orthodoxy. Practitioners who identify their own perfected state with reality itself.
> **Danger.** Tyrannous finality is how corruption acquires architecture.
>
> **Every other inversion can still be argued with. This one builds walls, seals libraries, and calls its fear peace.**
>
> **Corrective:** revision rites at fixed intervals. **Any doctrine, formula, relic, or institutional procedure that cannot survive periodic contradiction is embalmed, and stability is the wrong word for it.**

## Signs by Which Corruption Is Known

- **Soul signs** · *the soul records corruption before the hand does*

  Investigators should **privilege resonance reading over testimony where the two diverge.** Resonance reading is the Guild's name for the famous shared art of sensing, the trained read of another person's Essence (a soul's own refined share of Aether). The crowds at a Stage bracket (a tournament ladder) call it the nose, a Moto lord *kehai*, the Marrowkin sounding a man, as a rock face is sounded for a bad seam, and every one of those peoples will tell you the others are doing it wrong. It holds up in a hearing under any of those names.

  Tonal hardening under contradiction · sympathetic over-compliance with dominant fields · **split between declared belief and actual pulse-pattern** · hollow over-coherence, especially after Mortalis or Anamnesis work · **oscillation between euphoria and vacancy following apparently successful procedures** · memory bleed, dream intrusion, involuntary recurrence imagery · attraction-layer (the part of the Crystal that binds reality, memory and people to the soul) misalignment causing pathological fixation, obsession, or artificial aversion
- **Bodily signs** · *the body invents nothing and reports everything*

  Metallic scent in sweat after non-metallurgic work · **ash under fingernails that does not correspond to any burned reagent in the room** · **nosebleeds coinciding with successful concealment of results** · delayed hand tremor after patient contact · mirrored bruising across sternum or wrists after bond work · atypical pupil dilation during glyph recitation · **persistent cold patches, especially over the heart and lower throat**
- **Laboratory signs** · *a laboratory tells the truth the practitioner refuses*

  Recurrent hairline fractures in vessels used by **one specific operator** · **sigils that "correct themselves" into darker variants over time** · soot fall patterns inconsistent with local airflow · **residues clustering in the shapes of names, ribs, roots, spirals, or open mouths** · repeated spoilage of control compounds near certain benches · **instruments sounding a low steady note when no operation is active** · notes rewritten in the margins by the same hand **in a distinct moral register**
- **Environmental signs** · *realms and districts begin to remember prolonged error*

  Discoloration or behavioral irregularity at a Wellspring site · **dream convergence among unrelated residents** · agricultural inversions, such as premature ripening followed by sterility · **local time-slippage, repetition, stalled echoes, or lost intervals** · weather taking on ritual rhythm around a single workshop · **birds refusing roofs, children reporting the same impossible figure, dogs barking at empty thresholds** · celestial or astronomical irregularities following sustained high-order activation
- **Institutional signs** · *corruption at civic scale is marked less by smoke than by paperwork*

  Rising use of sealed exemptions without peer review · **concentration of authority in one "indispensable" practitioner** · reduction of witness requirements during crisis · **disappearance of dissenting notes from archives** · relabeling of casualties as acceptable metaphysical attrition · **creation of euphemistic categories to avoid the word heresy**

  **When institutions stop naming what they fear, the heresiarch is usually already inside the walls.**

## Wellsprings Most Frequently Injured

*A word on the word. The Sixty are fixed and no hand has ever laid one on or shut one off, so a Measurewright will tell you that a law cannot be hurt and that what this chapter calls injury is a damaged site and a great many damaged Crystals. The confessors will tell you that a principle lied about is a principle wounded, and that the Measurewright should try sleeping near one. The Accord wrote "injury" so that both parties could sign, and the two have been correcting each other's drafts ever since. What everyone agrees on is what you will find when you get there: a site where that Aether pools gone wrong, and people who took it in gone wrong the same way.*

| Wellspring | Common corruptions | Doctrine |
|---|---|---|
| **Mortalis** *dissolution, threshold, completion, the lawful withdrawal of presence. It governs passage and keeps nobody in storage. At the bench it is read as the Blacking, the charge gone black in the Rot* | Counterfeit resurrection claims · impression-body confusion · grief-powered commissions · **substitution of resemblance for personhood** | **The dead may leave traces. The trace is not the soul.** Any practitioner who refuses this distinction after demonstration **should be placed under Conviction review immediately** |
| **Anamnesis** *preserves charged records of lived commitment, and more than mere memory. At the bench it is read as the fired pot's testimony, the residue that says everything a vessel has held* | Activation of old traces for practical use · **extraction of unfinished work from the dead without context-restoration** · regional saturation through repeated retrieval · **conflation of remembered purpose with lawful permission** | **A trace may complete a decision. It does not grant ownership of the dead to the living.** The older the trace, the more carefully its completion must be mediated |
| **Judicium** *order, measure, and consequence. At the bench it is the law Separation draws on, the parts told apart and taken apart* | **Moralization of extermination** · transformation of review into domination · **reclassification of cruelty as cleansing** | **Law without mercy becomes burnished sadism. Mercy without law becomes indulgent chaos.** Judicium injury is most visible **where either side is used to excuse the other's absence** |
| **Catharsis** *release, purgation, true clearing. At the bench it is read as purgation, the matter giving up its burden in the change of state itself* | **Compulsory confession rites used as fuel extraction** · emotional stripping mistaken for healing · **public absolution systems that erase accountability instead of transmuting it** | **Nothing is purified by being merely emptied.** If what is removed has not been rightly transformed, **it returns elsewhere, often in a more venomous form** |
| **Tenebra** and allied shadow Wellsprings *at the bench, read as the cover-name, the recipe written to hide* | **Often blamed too quickly.** Not every hidden process is shadow-corrupted. Yet concealment culture, identity erosion, and oblivion hunger commonly recruit them | **The presence of shadow is no proof of heresy. The use of shadow to erase cost, witness, or responsibility often is** |

## Grades of Offense

| Grade | What it is | Response |
|---|---|---|
| **I · Negligent Irregularity** | Errors of handling, notation, calibration, containment, or overreach **without evidence of concealment or repeated drift** | Censure, retraining, supervised practice, restitution |
| **II · Liminal Drift** | Pattern of misjudgment, weak boundary, unreviewed innovation, unstable residue, or minor Wellspring strain. **Not yet knowingly heretical, but the work has entered dangerous weather** | Temporary suspension, field audit, soul-read, laboratory cleansing, mandatory review |
| **III · Cacodaemonic Inversion** | **One or more of the Seven Corruptions is visibly active in procedure, motive, and result** | Confiscation of work, formal tribunal, warded confinement, supervised correction where possible, **proscription if not** |
| **IV · Heresiarchic Practice** | A practitioner **teaches, exports, codifies, or politically shields** corrupt procedure **while understanding its nature sufficiently to know better** | Broad interdiction, associate review, archive purge or sealing, coordinated enforcement across jurisdictions |
| **V · [[The Magic System/Practice and Institution/Mechanica Extraction Without Recognition\|Mechanica]] Trespass** | The work bypasses soul-law, lawful glyphic order, or Wellspring integrity through synthetic, counterfeit, or anti-lattice means. **At this level the issue has passed alchemical corruption and become civilizational breach.** **The doctrinal line here is one of kind, where the others are lines of degree.** Grades I through IV describe workings that ran lawfully and ended, however badly. **A Mechanica working (forbidden working that takes power without lawful consent) cannot end.** Sealing closes an operation against the warrant that opened it, and a working with no Authorization has no warrant to close against, **so the boundary it opened stays open after the practitioner has gone home.** *Every other grade leaves a practitioner to be corrected. This one leaves ground that cannot be.* **A Crystal cut from a dead practitioner to be rendered is filed here and hanged, in every realm, under whichever warrant is waved over it** | Immediate highest-grade containment, relic seizure, **memory quarantine**, external review by Accord high authority, **erasure of propagation chains where lawful** |

*Nearly everything that crosses a magistrate's table is Grade I, and most of Grade I is small and human: a hedge-worker (a village's jobbing worker of small charms) who charged a well charm past what the well could hold, a stall-holder's fever tincture that was mostly plum, a lamp-warden (a hired keeper of street lamps) whose boy cut a stolen glyph into his mother's stove. They pay a fine, they complain about the fine, and they are back at work by the market. Grade IV goes the other way. The name is cried by heralds at every market cross in the jurisdiction, the bounty goes up on doors in hand-copied bills, and the minstrels have the verses wrong by the next market, which has on two occasions helped the accused escape.*

## Investigation and Response

> **First Entry Protocol.** No suspected site is to be entered by a single investigator. **The minimum lawful party is four:** one resonance-reader, one glyph-scrivener, one material examiner, and one witness-officer or adjudicator.
>
> **No assumption of innocence or guilt shall excuse documentation.**
**On interviewing the practitioner** · The first interview **should not begin with accusation. Corrupted alchemists are most revealing when invited to explain the elegance of what they have done.** Investigators should note **what the practitioner refuses to name**, who they blame for cost, whether success is defined by truth, efficacy, acclaim, or necessity, **and whether contradiction produces grief, anger, or contempt.**

**On the seizure of texts and relics** · **Every text must be copied before sealing if safe to do so.** *Destruction of evidence is laziness wearing moral perfume.* **Classified as: preservable under ward · preservable under redaction · transmissible only by excerpt · seal-only ·** destruction-lawful under emergency decree.

**Quarantine** · **Personal** (the practitioner isolated from assistants, patrons, and active instruments) · **Laboratory** (site sealed, residue mapped, ambient field monitored) · **District** (imposed where Wellspring injury or civic spread is probable).
> **Where possible, correction outranks execution. The purpose of law is the restoration of intelligible order, and theatrical violence has never yet restored any.**
>
> Permitted correctives: supervised contradiction rites · silence fasts · witness-confession under resonance reading · guided dissolution with independent anchor · ethical re-education attached to technical review · **removal from patronage structures** · long-term service in cleansing and reconstruction.
>
> **Execution becomes lawful only where the practitioner remains actively contagious in doctrine or metaphysical effect, and where lesser restraints have failed or are impossible.**

## Historical Cases for Instruction

**I · The Hall of the Unquestioned King** · A ruler hardened in private certainty became **the living image of Calcination Perverted.** His governance was cruel and, worse, **metaphysically sealed against contradiction.** The intervention took force, and it took more than force: **it reintroduced correspondence between his conduct and his own soul-account.** *Instruction: some political tyrannies are alchemical pathologies embodied in governance. Heresiology is therefore a civic science as well as a laboratory one.*

**II · The Compelled Author and the Necromantic Manuscript** · A talented writer, pulled by access and manipulated through need, produced a technically brilliant but spiritually dangerous architecture for post-Mortalic reconstruction. **The warning survived as a footnote.** *Instruction: a footnote may preserve conscience, but it does not neutralize a weaponized text.*

**III · The Eastern Commission of Grief** · A dead woman's likeness was reconstructed with terrible fidelity. **She spoke, knew names, and believed herself whole enough to console the living.** The mourner received something real **and something not identical to what he had lost.** *Instruction: resemblance is not identity. Consolation is not proof. Grief is the patron most likely to mistake precision for resurrection.*

**IV · The Genesio Activation Sequence** · Old traces activated in pursuit of unfinished good. **Works were completed. Benefits were undeniable. So too were the accumulating distortions in the pressure of the local Wellspring site and in celestial correspondence.** *Instruction: Fermentation Perverted often arrives crowned with achievements.*

**V · The Drift of Tempus** · Astronomical deviation followed repeated pressure upon a regional metaphysical field. **The sky registered what the operators called tolerable deviation.** *Instruction: the realm answers what the scholar asks of it, whether or not the scholar understands the scale of the question.*

## Duties, and the Vows

**Monasteries** · Teach the Great Work as discipline before technique. **An unformed soul armed with advanced method is an invitation to elegant catastrophe.**

**Infirmaries** · **No healing protocol may be judged solely by immediate survival.** Memory integrity, soul coherence, dream stability, and delayed contamination must be part of every successful outcome.

**Academies** · **To teach transmutation without inversion is to educate disaster.**

**States** · **Centralizing all high-order alchemy in one patriotic genius buys a future tribunal, and it is sold to you as efficiency.**

**Archives** · **Redaction is guided survivability, and it should never be mistaken for concealment.** Dangerous texts should remain legible to the qualified, **and romantic erasure for the sake of the unqualified protects nobody.**
> **The Vows.** *Let every licensed practitioner swear, before witness and vessel:*
>
> **I will not call resemblance identity.**
>
> **I will not join what I would not answer for.**
>
> **I will not borrow cost from the voiceless.**
>
> **I will not make crisis my preferred laboratory.**
>
> **I will not cleanse my notes of blood that was necessary to their truth.**
>
> **I will not call what is provisional final.**
>
> **I will submit my greatest work to contradiction before I submit it to power.**
>
> *These vows make review possible, and nobody has yet been made a saint by them.*

### What the Vows are, mechanically

*The Concordant register calls them an oath. The Standing Index would call them Votia (working bought with a vow), and both are correct.*

A vow is small Fate (the Plane that governs Attraction Force, Obsession Force and what reality is obliged to allow), and anyone may make one. It declares a permanent restriction, the Essence Core (the Crystal's inner layer, holding identity, memory and Traits) adopts it as a standing law, and the swearer's window (the soul reporting its numbers to its owner, unseen by anyone else) shows the terms from then on. **Tempering Coherence, the Sub-Stat, measures the alignment between a practitioner's stated law and their lived behavior**, and Coherence is the structural prerequisite for Threshold eligibility. A licensed practitioner who keeps these seven is therefore **buying Coherence with conduct**, which is why the orders and the poor swear more of them than the great houses do.
> **And a vow broken is a Shear Break, and it lands at once.** The Core has been running a law the practitioner has now denied, and the law does not negotiate its own withdrawal.
>
> *Which changes what the Vows are for.* They were framed as a discipline and they function as **an instrument.** A sworn practitioner in drift is producing a falling Coherence reading before they have concealed anything, altered a note, or lied to a single investigator. **The soul signs precede the hand because the vow is what the soul is failing at.**
>
> **The investigator's practical consequence:** ask which vows the practitioner swore and to whom, and read the Coherence before the interview rather than after. *Corrupted practitioners are most revealing when invited to explain the elegance of what they have done. They are most legible before they open their mouths.*

## Final Admonition

Ambition can be tutored. The oldest error in alchemy **is the belief that success exempts the practitioner from interpretation.**
> **A result is never self-explanatory.** Every result must answer the old questions.
> 
> What did it cost. Who paid. What law was obeyed. What law was bent.
> 
> **What part of the practitioner became easier to live with after the act. What part became harder to see.**
**When those questions stop being asked, the work grows no freer and becomes invisible to itself.** *That is the hour in which corruption flowers quickest.*

Darkness in alchemy can be honest, because **some vessels must be blackened before they can carry light.** Severity can be honest, because some truths require incision. Concealment can be honest as well, because **some things must be warded until the world is less stupid.**
> Yet when alchemy begins to love opacity, force, certainty, usefulness, and finality more than truth, **it leaves the Great Work and enters its mirror.**
>
> **The mirror is always polished. The mirror is always persuasive. The mirror usually produces results.**
>
> **The mirror is where realms go to forget what they have done.**

### Appendix II · Field questions for investigators

What stage did the practitioner believe they were in? **Which corruption best explains the motive, not only the method?** Where has cost been displaced?

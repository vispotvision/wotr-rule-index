# Hide, Horn and Bone

*The creature-material register · hide, horn, claw, bone, sinew, gut, blood, organ, scale, quill and shell.*
> **What carry actually is, stated once.** Every entry below is graded for it and the register has never said what it does.
>
> A Threshold is a melt, and a melt cools into order only around structures that survived the heat. In a Soul Crystal those structures are **Traits**, which are laws the bearer survived into existence. **Carry is a seed: a law that something else survived into existence, still attached to the material it was rendered out of.**
>
> So a high-carry creature material used in Threshold work does not merely work harder. **It grows a region of the recipient's new lattice around a law that was never theirs.** The appetite in Ripper Talon is a hunting law a predator lived long enough to make true. The pull in Tidedrawn Cable is a law about deep water. *Neither of them is a metaphor and neither of them stops.*
>
> **Class III carry is appetite-shaped and can be lived with.** Class IV is identity-shaped, and identity-shaped carry is what **Identity Contamination** is a description of: a Core that has absorbed enough borrowed law to stop being able to say cleanly which convictions are its own. *This register is that supply chain. It has never been written down as one.*

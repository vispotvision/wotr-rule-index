# The Master Material Ledger

*Consolidated substrate register · T0 through T9 · all four quarters*

Nessa Hegg was ringer at the Ormsby winding house for fourteen years, and the whole of her duty was to stand under a bell and pull a rope if the Weight Ablation glyph (the inscription that halves a load so a winding rope can bear it) ever failed. It failed at the change of shift. A cage of ore that had weighed twenty tons came to forty between one breath and the next, and everything else the glyph had been holding up came to its true weight with it: the drum tore out of its bed, the great timber headframe began to lean, and the bell tower stood on the same footings as the headframe. Nessa Hegg could see exactly how it would end, because she was looking at it through the louvers. She rang. She rang while the rope of the cage went through the roof and the floor under her took on a slope a mule could not have climbed, and six hundred and twelve men and women down in the dark heard her and went up the ladderways and out by the old adit, and when the tower finally lay down across the yard they dug her out with the palms gone from her hands and the rope still in them.

Ormsby Deep buried her on a blazing blue morning with nine mining lodges behind her and their banners up, scarlet and gold and lodge-green, along the Aerne, which runs the color of a ripe apricot because the old workings have been bleeding vitriol into it for longer than the town has had a name. Six hewers carried the coffin. Four more carried the bell rope, forty fathoms of it, coiled on a litter like a bishop. The Accord's entry for that day is in the second table below, in the same words as its entry for every other such day.

This is the dullest book the Guild of Measurewrights (the assayers and scholars who survey, measure and argue over everything) has ever produced, and I would carry it out of a fire ahead of my own bed. It says what the world is made of, tier by tier and quarter by quarter, what each thing costs the hands that dig it up, and which of its sources nobody can find, all of it in tables, in the voice of a clerk who has never once been surprised. Nessa Hegg is in here as half a sentence. Read it as a miner reads a roof.

> Compiled from the Guild of Measurewrights' survey, the standing Accord material list, the coin volume, the Four Ceilings technology history, and the Master Codex alchemical tab.

## The Tier Scale

| Tier | Accord name | What it means for material | Threat-tier overlap | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **T0** | Fragile | Fails at ordinary handling. Symbolic, disposable, or single-night work | Below commission grade | Mireclay; Coalglass Shard |
| **T1** | Soft | Workable by an apprentice. Real properties, no durability | Green Sigil, Initiate | Vellun Copper; Palebed Nitre |
| **T2** | Common Durable | **The material the world is actually built out of.** Bulk supply | Green Sigil, Apprentice | Forge-Iron; Greystone; Chainsalt |
| **T3** | Hardened | Survives sustained use and takes a working that lasts | Green Sigil, Journeyman | Blacktooth Wolfram; Onceglass |
| **T4** | Reinforced Magical | Purpose-made or purpose-found for Essence work. **Instrument grade** | Blue Sigil, Adept | Clearbeam Beryl; Cloudmetal; Stormfulgur |
| **T5** | Rare / Spirit-Forged | **Requires a forge-array, a rite, or a corpse.** Not bought casually | Blue Sigil, Expert | Aethersteel; Bloodsilver; Gloomhide |
| **T6** | Arcane-Resistant | **Refuses a category of working outright** rather than resisting it | Gold Sigil, Master | Grimbarite; Soulquartz; Frostwool |
| **T7** | Titan-Forged | Era-one product or Titan remnant. **Found, not made** | Gold Sigil, Grandmaster | Titanstone; Drakehide Steel |
| **T8** | Mythic / Celestial | Formed by cosmological event. **No terrestrial process reaches it.** Operates on Name, Fate, time | Obsidian Seal, Archmaster | Chronoglass; Eclipsium; Abythros Thread |
| **T9** | Transcendent / Forbidden | **Acts on the law rather than within it.** Proscribed. Nullifies or rewrites | Obsidian Seal, Paragon | Crevice Shale; Nullglass; Ash of Names |

> **Every T8 and T9 entry in the register is Holding-grade.** There is no transcendent building material, and the absence is structural rather than accidental: **at T8 and above the material stops being something you build with and becomes something that acts.**
>
> **A material's tier is what it can hold, and a finished piece carries two more marks of its own:** a Grade for power, on the fighters' letters, and a Rarity for scarcity, Common through Unique. The smith's title caps the Grade she may make and the merchant's title caps the Rarity he may sell. See [[Materials, Alchemy & Trade/Work, Trade and Measure/The Apparatus of the Age — Equipment and Material Culture/The Item Tiers|Grade and Rarity]].

## The Hazard Register

*How the trade kills the people who work it.*

An array is a working laid out in glyph and substrate to run in place, kept by hired tenders, and a deep mine's pumps and air both hang on one. A Core, in common speech, is a worked [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/The Sixty Wellsprings|Wellspring]] site, and its keepers can close it.

| Hazard | Onset | Effect | Recorded position |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Array failure, pumping** *western deep workings* | 70 to 110 hours depending on shaft | Flooding. **Mass drowning** | The most likely mass-casualty event in the four quarters and **the one the Accord is least prepared for.** Mitigation: contracts with more than one array house and more than one Core keeper, since either can close; nothing else works |
| **Array failure, ventilation** *all deep workings* | Within the hour | Firedamp in coal, blackdamp in metal | **Kills faster than water and is more easily concealed afterward.** Mitigation: a wet hood and a candle on a pole, *which is what it was before* |
| **Weight Ablation glyph failure** *all winding houses* | Instantaneous | Total restoration of true load | Mitigation is **a bell, rung by hand.** No contraption built to give the warning has ever been fast enough |
| **Sulfide roast fume** *Kaetran Ring* | Chronic | Vegetation loss to six leagues and rising | None attempted. **Shows no sign of stopping** |
| **Vitriol drainage** *western old workings* | Chronic, cumulative | Orange rivers; nothing lives above the tidal reach | None at current practice. **Irreversible** |
| **Rock dust, silicate** *western slate belts* | Twenty years | Grinder's cough | *Simply what happens to men who breathe rock dust for twenty years* |
| **Rock dust, fibrous serpentine** *southern coastal quarries* | Twenty years **after leaving the trade** | Lung scarring, **invariably fatal** | Enforcement has posted no writ **and the quarries have raised no objection to the absence** |
| **Lahar** *eastern volcanic plains* | Minutes | A river of wet ash moving at the speed of a horse | Mitigation: distance. **Does not leave bodies to bury** |
| **Fumarole gas pooling** *eastern sulfur fields* | Immediate | Silent asphyxiation in hollows | **Do not descend after a casualty. Kills rescue parties second** |
| **Wellspring drift** *eastern quarter* | Seasons to years | The source site thins or shifts; the working is stranded | Economic rather than physical. **Has bankrupted more eastern ventures than every eruption in recorded history combined** |
| **Tender's shake** *all array houses, all quarters* | ~8 years of service | Fine persistent palsy of the hands. **Permanent** | Mitigation: *hire young and hire in numbers.* **No compensation scheme exists in any quarter** |
| **Crevice proximity** *southern margin* | Undescribed | Undescribed | Four-league exclusion line, **revised outward five times in the last sixty years** |
| **Lead smelting** *western limestone belt* | Years | Undocumented in the ledger; **the trade says the men do not last** | Recorded in one clause and not elaborated |
| **Bloodsilver rejection** *anywhere it is held* | Over time | Burns or corrodes an unworthy holder | Mitigation: *be the right bloodline.* **The only item in the Arbitration schedules that can attack the bailiff** |
| **Core-lip residue** *every city with a worked Core* | Twenty years of service | In practitioners, clouding of the Shell (the Crystal's outer layer, the skin power passes through on the way out) and Trait flicker without corresponding Essence Starvation. **In the unawakened, nothing an instrument is calibrated to report** | Core-tenders work the lip of a worked pool at densities that would be logged as an Active Concentration (a density that alters bodies over time) if a surveyor found the reading in open country. **The trade has a word for the late stage and no company document contains it.** *No baseline is taken, because no baseline exists for men without Crystals* |
| **Unsealed field** *any ground worked by engine or wheel* | Cumulative, undated | Coherence falls with no change in conduct. The Crystal is conditioned by boundaries nobody set and cannot find | **A hazard of the ground the material came out of, rather than of any material**, and it does not clear when the working stops because the working never closed. An engine here is Mechanica's kind, built apparatus that takes power with no practitioner present, and a shop's Essence Wheel run hard and long leaves the same field. Renderers can identify a long-service wheel-man on the table by the Shell alone, **and several of them will not say so on a record** |
| **Bench exposure** *all alchemical houses* | ~20 years | Documented in Alchemetrica and nowhere in this register until now | The trade's private estimate is that a pure alchemist has twenty good years. **The trade's public position is that the estimate is unproven.** *Both statements are made by the same people, frequently in the same week* |

## Unresolved Provenance

*Materials in continuous trade whose source the Accord cannot locate.*
> **Senoth**: Vellun Copper's source hills. Placed in the western quarter on trade-route evidence only. **Four surveys have failed to confirm it.**
>
> **Rovann**: Glassroot Fibers and Steelsilk Cord. **Two separate Accord records place it in two separate quarters.** Both cannot be right and neither has been withdrawn.
>
> **Tirres**: Willowblood Sap. Does not appear on any current Accord survey.
>
> **Ilcindor**: Moonblight Ore. Does not appear on any current Accord survey.
>
> **Ghoststeel**: attributed to the northern shield on ruin-siting evidence alone. **No crucible reproduction has ever succeeded.**
>
> **Seerstone Resin**: attributed provisionally to eastern cloud forest canopy species. Unconfirmed by Archives.
Two further entries the Measurewrights record as open. **Whether the Crevice is geology at all**: the Guild declines to certify it as a fault in any sense. And **whether the Kaetran Ring's passages are caused by the impact structure or merely coincident with it**: *the correlation is too exact to be accident and too unexamined to be asserted.*

One inference is recorded and marked as such. **Silver's Resonance** is given as person-addressed binding, drawn from Bloodsilver and Dawnsilver behavior, and **the substrate tables name basalt, quartz, iron, gold and obsidian and are silent on silver.**

## The Class IV Shortfall

*The register's oldest arithmetic problem, stated plainly for once.*

**Class IV, Crystal Draw**, is material rendered from something that carried a Soul Crystal. The lawful article is a monster's core, which is in a way its Soul Crystal and is worth more the stronger the monster, and the Bench keeps three old warrants for the Trait tissue of the human dead. **The volume in circulation has never matched what those sources can account for**, and the discrepancy has been carried in the Bench's own returns for as long as the returns have existed.

The Measurewrights' position is that the gap is a recording failure. It is a supply.
> **A Crystal cut from a dead practitioner renders into the strongest Draft of all. It is the worst crime in every realm and the most profitable, and most of the gap is this, sold on as monster core.** Below it, extraction produces Class IV material as a by-product and engine work produces it at volume. An operator pulling Essence off a donor renders nothing on purpose; the residue simply accumulates, and it is indistinguishable on the coil from lawfully rendered stock **because the process that made it was the same process, performed without a warrant.**
>
> *This is why the arithmetic will not balance and cannot be made to. The missing tonnage has no lawful source because it had no lawful origin, and every factor between the operator and the bench is buying in good faith from somebody who is also buying in good faith.*
**The three tells**, which the better houses check and the rest do not.

**Residue that does not disperse** on the finished stock. Lawful rendering leaves a trace that thins on schedule. Class IV taken out of a cut Crystal or an unsealed operation carries a residue that holds, because the operation it came from never closed.

**Instrument disagreement.** The coil reads a Holding figure, the assay fork reads a density, and on unlawful stock the two do not reconcile. **The discrepancy is the size of what was taken.** Two instruments and somebody willing to compare them is the whole of the test and most benches own one instrument.

**A provenance that runs out.** Lawful Class IV has a rendering house, a date and a beast or a body. Unlawful Class IV has a factor, a price, and a supplier who is three towns away and reliably difficult to visit.

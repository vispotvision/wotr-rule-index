# The Standing Index

*The Bench's own register · forty-five entries · prices in western silver marks*
> **Nothing in the alchemical economy exists commercially until it is entered.** An unentered formula is not illegal in most quarters. It is simply outside every instrument the economy uses to hold a transaction together.

---

## The Three Marks

Every row carries three readings: **Provenance Class**, **Fidelity**, and **Carry.**

### Provenance Class — where the Essence came from

| Class | Name | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | Vein Draw | Taken straight off a Wellspring vein by array. **Carry effectively nil.** The bulk commodity |
| **II** | Field Substrate | Matter that sat in a live Wellspring field long enough to take a set. **Carries a place, not a person** |
| **III** | Vital Draw | Rendered from things that lived and carried no Soul Crystal. **Carry is appetite-shaped.** The workhorse of the trade |
| **IV** | Crystal Draw | Rendered from something that **carried a Soul Crystal. Carry is identity-shaped.** Three lawful sources, **and the arithmetic has never balanced** |
| **V** | Sovereign Draw | Off a dragon, World Spirit, Sovereign-class entity, or a relic that held one. **Carry is law-shaped. It does not want. It enforces** |
| **VI** | Archonic Residue | **Not rendered. Found.** No origin the instrument can locate. **Graded because it is sold** |

### Fidelity and Carry

> **FIDELITY** — how close the lot is to the reference figure.
>
> **A** Reference-indistinguishable · the coil cannot tell it from the Index reference.
> **B** Sound · what a competent chartered house ships.
> **C** Serviceable · works, but not the same way twice.
> **D** Loose · executes the process and something adjacent to it.
> **E** Poor · law-fragment risk. Unlawful to sell to an uncertified buyer.
> **F** Failed · **not a product. A hazard with a label on it**
> **CARRY** — how much of the origin came with it.
>
> **0–10** Clean · no usable origin character. *Obedient and forgettable.*
> **11–40** Light · the origin leans on the working without governing it.
> **41–70** Marked · the origin is a design consideration.
> **71–90** Heavy · **the origin governs. The working does what it was told and what the source was.**
> **91+** Sovereign-carry · attested four times. **The working is the source, wearing a formula**

### The three lawful sources of Class IV

*Named here because the Index has cited the number twice and the sources nowhere, and clerks have been pricing against a phrase.*
**I · Consented render.** A practitioner who willed the body to a house, executed after death under witness, with the instrument signed while the signer was competent. **The only clean source and by volume the smallest.** *Houses solicit these openly and the solicitation is considered distasteful by everyone including the houses.*
**II · Judicial render.** The bodies of the executed, where the sentence carried forfeiture of the Crystal. Lawful in every quarter, and the largest of the three.
> **The forfeiture clause exists in the criminal law of every quarter and predates the trade that uses it.** What has moved is not the law. **It is the sentencing.**
>
> *Two quarters have widened the schedule of capital offences within living memory. Both widenings were argued on public order and both followed a shortage. The correlation is available in the assize rolls and the Bench has never been asked to look at it.*
**III · Warranted salvage.** The dead of a declared war, rendered by a licensed house under a quartermaster's warrant, with the warrant naming the engagement. **Legal, enormous in a bad year, and entirely dependent on the war having been declared.** *Undeclared actions produce bodies that cannot lawfully be rendered and are rendered anyway, and this is the largest single feeder into the shortfall below.*

### The shortfall

**Class IV in circulation has never matched what the three lawful sources can produce**, and the Index has carried the discrepancy without explaining it since the Bench began keeping returns.
The explanation is not complicated. **Extraction produces Class IV as a by-product and engine work produces it at volume.** An operator drawing off a donor is not rendering deliberately, but the residue accumulates and it is indistinguishable on the coil from lawful stock, **because the process that produced it was the same process performed without a warrant.**
> *So there is no fourth Provenance Class for it. Unlawful Crystal Draw is graded Class IV, sold as Class IV, and priced as Class IV, and the only thing separating it from the lawful article is a chain of paper that terminates in a factor three towns away.*
**The assay that would catch it exists.** Coilwright's Reading Oil and Nine-Nail Attestation Salt between them make the two-instrument comparison possible: the coil reads a Holding figure, the gauge reads a density, and on unlawful stock the two will not reconcile. **The discrepancy is the size of what was taken.**
> *The comparison requires two instruments and somebody prepared to run both. Most benches own one. The Bench of Attribution has never made the second mandatory, and the argument against has always been cost.*

## The Tier Ladder

| Tier | Rank gate | What the tier actually means | Who holds it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T1** | None | Household. Needs no certification, no circle, and no understanding | Everyone |
| **T2** | Initiate | Apprentice work. A careless hand can ruin it **without dying** | Households, field units, clerks |
| **T3** | Journeyman | **Trade standard. The bulk of the working alchemical economy sits here** | Guild halls, granaries, mines, militia |
| **T4** | Adept | Professional. Certified circle, sealed workshop, entered formula | Chartered houses, Legion quartermasters, parishes |
| **T5** | Expert | Reserved in most entries. **Threshold work begins in earnest** | Concord agents, assay houses, great households |
| **T6** | Master | Class IV carry becomes routine **and the ethical questions stop being hypothetical** | Divisions, Envoys, the largest guild halls |
| **T7** | Grandmaster | Gold Sigil. Class V enters the ledger. **Every lot has a provenance chain and a story** | Concord Council, Guildlords, three or four private holders |
| **T8** | Absolute | Obsidian Seal. Black Concord territory. **Several entries carry no description at all** | The Council. The Black Concord. Nobody else lawfully |
| **T9** | Chosen | Zenith Decree authorisation. Four entries exist. **One of them is a placeholder admitting the tier may not be real** | The High Concordant, and the Archives Eternal |

> The Codex's own controlled vocabulary **stops at T8.** The ninth tier exists because **the Accord required a ceiling above Absolute and the Codex declined to supply one.**
>
> Whether T9 is a real grade or a bureaucratic fiction **is the oldest open question in this document.**

---

> **What a Threshold product is actually doing.** Hillcarry Salts, the Ascension Ration, the Splinter Ration, the Refraction Draught and the Domain Seed Vitrifier are one family of thing wearing five names.
>
> A Threshold is a melt, and a melt cools into order only around structures that survived the heat, which in a Soul Crystal means **Traits**. A Threshold product supplies the melt with a **seed the soul did not produce**, or supports the seeds already there through the heat. That is the whole of the mechanism, and it is why the ration reaches the floor and never the Catalyst.
>
> **Carry is the seed's origin, still attached.** A high-carry Threshold product does not merely work better. It grows a region of lattice around a law the recipient never survived into being true, **and the recipient acquires a rigidity they did not earn and cannot argue with.** *Which is why the Refraction Draught leaves a stranger's habit of mind behind, and why the Bench grades carry at all.*

When the Vintners and the Coopers of Rushford fell out over how much a tun should hold, the Long Quay ran purple for a week, two master coopers went into the river in their own barrels with their guild colors nailed to the lids, and the tun is still the size the coopers said. Everything on these shelves is a quarrel of that kind, conducted with a tool in one hand.

*The trades and their tools, the guild titles that say who may be trusted with what, the equipment of the age and how it is rated, and the standards everything is weighed by.*

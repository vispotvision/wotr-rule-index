# The Ashkaru

> *"Bairos did not name them what they were. He named them what they were for. Ask what changed."*
> margin note, unsigned, found in the Ledger of the Stone Choir's deep archive

---

### Origin

The Ashkaru predate the Planar Separation. They were authored by **Bairos**, the Scribe, the First Epoch, in the period before the Plane of Soul, the Plane of Fate, and the Material Plane divided into the three-layered architecture the entire magic system now runs on. They were not the first thing Bairos made. They were the last thing he made before making became unnecessary, because the deep text he was writing finished itself once they existed to read it.
The Accord's register has no entry for them. The Stone Choir's deep archive holds a single margin note. The Ledger Monks decline to comment and have never explained why, which is the loudest thing the Ledger Monks have ever done.
The Moto are Bara's children, the First Epoch given human form, the primordial authority made inheritable through Mizuki's dynastic architecture. **The Ashkaru are the older work.** Bairos's instruments before Bara existed. A sibling line to the Moto in the way that a chisel is a sibling to the carpenter's son: made by the same hand, made first, made for a different kind of labour.

---

### The Deep Text

Before the Planes separated, reality's grammar was unified. Meaning, structure, and substance were not sorted into three layers. They were a single continuous text, and that text could be read and rewritten by anything equipped to perceive it at the structural level.
The Ashkaru were that equipment.
Their function was not interpretation. It was **transcription and correction**: reading the state of the deep text, identifying inconsistencies in its grammar, and rewriting the passage so the inconsistency resolved. Bairos did not trust the deep text to maintain itself. He built instruments that maintained it for him, and the instruments were alive because dead tools cannot exercise judgment about which inconsistency matters.
This is the foundational fact about the Ashkaru that decides everything else: **they were built to edit reality's source document, and they were given judgment so the edits would be good.** The judgment is what made them people. It is also what made Kinjiki possible.

### Biology

The Ashkaru body is not a body in the sense that mortal anatomists use the word. It is a **reading architecture** that happens to be alive.
**The Executor.** The central torso: bilateral, muscular, superficially humanoid in its symmetry. This is the part of the Ashkaru that acts on what has been read. Hands, a jaw, a chest cavity dense enough to house the organs that sustain the whole structure. The torso is not the core of the Ashkaru. It is the pen. The core is everything radiating from it.
**The Threads.** Six serpentine appendages, muscular and scaled, each terminating in an independent head with its own jaw, its own sensory apparatus, and its own rudimentary cognition. These are the parallel reading threads. Each head processes a different strand of input simultaneously. In the pre-Planar period, each strand corresponded to a different layer of the deep text. After the Separation, the six threads lost their native material and began reading whatever was available, which is why an Ashkaru's perceptive range is so broad and so difficult for any current-system instrument to classify.
**The Hide.** Dense, mineral-adjacent, the grey-blue of deep basalt. Not skin over muscle but something closer toite under compression. It does not scar in the conventional sense. Damage is filled by the body's own crystalline substrate, leaving seams of slightly darker material that a geologist would recognize as veining in metamorphic rock.
**The Ears.** Elongated, pointed, angled outward. Directional sensing organs, not decorative. They predate any elven morphology and are not related to it.
**No Hair.** The Ashkaru produce no keratin structures of any kind. The body's surface is uniform and smooth, interrupted only by the gold sealing hardware and the crystalline veining of old wounds.
**Scale.** Larger than human. The executor torso stands roughly seven feet at the shoulder before the threads are accounted for. With all six threads extended, the Ashkaru's total silhouette fills a space most mortal practitioners would classify as *environmental*.

### The Planar Separation and Its Consequence

When the three Planes divided, the deep text ceased to be a single readable document. Its grammar fragmented across three layers: meaning on the Plane of Fate, structure on the Plane of Soul, substance on the Material Plane. The unified transcription the Ashkaru were built for became mechanically impossible.
They did not die. They did not degrade. They simply had nothing left to do.
The Soul Crystal system, which emerged from the Plane of Soul's architecture, was not designed for them. It was **retrofitted**, the way you would bolt a saddle onto something that was never a horse. The Ashkaru can operate within the current magic system. Their Soul Crystals function. Their Wellspring harmonizations register. But the hardware underneath is older than the system classifying it, and the fit is imperfect in ways that no amount of refinement will correct, because the imperfection is not a flaw. It is a difference of era.

---

### Limina Affinity

Every Ashkaru who has been classified under the current system registers as **Limina-dominant**, the Family of entropy, void, and mind. This is a post-hoc label. The Ashkaru do not practice Limina Wellsprings the way a human practitioner harmonizes with Dissolution or Nihiloth. They *are* what those Wellsprings describe, in the same way that a river is what the word "current" describes. The word came after.
The specific Wellsprings that map to Ashkaru native function:
**Dissolution.** The entropy of mixing, the thermodynamic preference toward the unbound state. In an Ashkaru, this is the reading thread's ability to take apart what it perceives. Perception and disassembly are the same action.
**Nihiloth.** Destructive interference, the null field. An Ashkaru's Domain does not impose law. It cancels the law already present. What remains inside is not a rule but the absence of one, which is the exact condition the deep text requires to be readable.
**Vantabriel.** Near-total absorption, hysteresis. What strikes the Ashkaru is kept rather than reflected. The Crystal's condition is a function of everything it has ever absorbed. For a being built to read without being read, this is native camouflage: a deep-text scribe that broadcast its own signal would corrupt its own readings.
**Oblivara.** Error-correcting redundancy. The Ashkaru identity is redundantly encoded against erasure. For a being designed to edit reality's source document, being editable in return would have been a structural flaw.

### The Sealing Hardware

The gold bands visible in Kinjiki's configuration are **self-imposed control architecture.** Each serpent thread, left unsupervised, reverts to its original function: it reads what it perceives, and if the reading reveals an inconsistency, it begins correcting. At Stage XIV, where the Domain and the body are indistinguishable, an unsupervised thread does not merely perceive a room. It perceives the room's structural grammar, and it edits.
The bands interrupt the correction cycle without interrupting the reading. They are restraints in the precise sense: not suppression of power, but suppression of *function*. Each gold cuff is a circuit-breaker between perception and action. The chains connecting them distribute the load across the full thread architecture so that no single band bears the entire interruption.
Removing a band does not make Kinjiki stronger. It makes one of his threads *autonomous*: a parallel reader with its own judgment and no governor, doing to its surroundings what it was built to do to the deep text.

### The Distant World

**Originated, pending ruling.** The Ashkaru homeworld has not been named. It exists in the wider cosmology as one of the worlds Bairos touched before the Separation, a place where the deep text was particularly dense, where reality's grammar required constant maintenance, and where the instruments built to maintain it were given permanent residence.
After the Separation, the world continued. The Ashkaru continued. Their purpose did not. What a race of living editorial instruments does when the document closes is the question their entire post-Separation history answers, and the answer is not uniform. Some maintained. Some adapted. Some, like Kinjiki, looked at the new arrangement and decided it was a draft.

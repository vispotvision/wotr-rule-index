# The Beastkin and Demihuman Peoples

*Races · Soul Architecture · Languages · Cultures · The Social Reality*
> Beastkin are not a category invented by the Guild Accord. **They are a category the Guild Accord inherited and then legislated.**

---

## What Beastkin Are

The living peoples who fall under this designation — Catkin, Lupine, Bearkin, Fox-Spirit, Serpentkin, and several dozen smaller lineages — **share no common origin event, no common spiritual ancestor, and no common metaphysical architecture.** What they share is the external feature the Accord used to group them: the presence of non-human biological characteristics alongside functional Aetheric architecture.
The formal category exists to solve an administrative problem: what to do with peoples who are **too structured to be classified as Spirit Entities, too distinct to be absorbed into the Human taxonomy, and too varied to be given individual lineage recognition.** The Third Eon solution was the **Beastkin Classification Provision**, which created a unified legal category and then subdivided it into free-status, bonded-status, and labor-stock classifications on criteria that the Accord's own practitioners consider inconsistently applied.
> *"Those aren't people, boy. Those are labor stock. Licensed and documented under the Verath Township Labor Provision."*
> — Darvo, Verath Township. Seventeen gold marks per head. **Less than a horse.**

### The Labor Provision

The Provision classifies beastkin who cannot demonstrate free-holder status through guild membership, formal employment, or bloodline recognition as **available for labor contracts.**
> In practice this means a beastkin without active documentation can be purchased, held in licensed pens, and sold at Accord-adjacent market events **without the transaction constituting a crime under Accord law in most territories.**
>
> The *licensed and documented* qualifier is not a protection. **It is the mechanism by which the transaction is made legal.**
>
> The world has two categories for beastkin: **victim or animal.** Neither category contains the word person. *This is documented policy, not oversight.*

## The Demihuman Peoples

Demihumans occupy a distinct category: **defined by dual-lineage inheritance rather than by non-human biological features.**
> The distinction matters legally. **Demihumans are generally accorded default free-status pending investigation of lineage claims**, whereas beastkin default to labor-stock status pending documentation of free-holder status.
>
> This distinction produces meaningfully different lives for the people it classifies.

### Half-Elves · The Third Path

Emerged during the **Harmonial Twilight** when Elves and Humans shared Wellspring territories. Born from the spiritual overlap of Elven soul-depth and Human glyph flexibility — **not fully either, genuinely both**, navigating the friction between two Crystal architectures that were not designed to occupy the same body.
**Soul Path** · Spirit 55% / Body 30% / Attraction 15%. Dual affinity: spatial Elven resonance plus adaptive Human Essence. **High conductivity, internal friction as the structural cost.**
| Stat | Mod | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Vitality | −05 | Lighter than full-human baseline; not as resilient as Elven longevity architecture. Adequate |
| Ardency | +10 | Human adaptive output combined with Elven Inscription efficiency |
| Gnosis | +15 | Partial Parunic Fluency inheritance. Above human baseline; below Eressean ceiling |
| Dexterity | +10 | Elven flow-state influence on movement. Economy elevated |
| **Harmonics** | **+20** | Dual resonance — both Elven Attunement and Human emotive Harmonics active simultaneously. **Fidelity sometimes inconsistent due to internal friction** |
| Resilience | +05 | Modest. Spiritual Friction creates specific vulnerability to `Ur`/`Ae` glyph misfire |
| Tempering | +10 | Clean routing from Elven inheritance; Human adaptive processing adds flexibility |
| Dominion | +10 | Partial Elven Domain Stability inheritance. Domain formation achievable and more stable than full-human baseline |

> **Spiritual Friction** — −20% Aetheric Efficiency for `Ur` (Balance) and `Ae` (Order) glyphs; 10% chance to misfire and deal minor Crystal damage to the caster.
>
> **Rootlessness** — neither Elven nor Human communities fully accept them without active negotiation. **The political cost of existing between categories is ongoing.**
**Wellsprings** · Aurevane through Elven inheritance. Somnalis through Florwynic lineage trace. Human-compatible Wellsprings fully accessible. Echo-adjacent occasionally accessible. **No Wellspring is fully closed; none is fully native.**
**Culture** · The Harmonic Quarter communities in Altherion and border territories. Often the informal diplomatic bridge between Elven and Human institutions — **a function assigned to them rather than chosen by them, with predictable consequences for how they feel about performing it.**

### Half-Orcs · The Unchained

Born from the intersection of Orcish Stone-Blood and Human glyph-flexibility during the Great Concord Splintering. **They are not the weakest expression of either lineage.** They are a third expression — Stone-Blood's kinetic inheritance running through a human body that has **the glyph-processing capacity the pure Orcish lineage lacks.**
**Soul Path** · Body 60% / Spirit 30% / Attraction 10%. Stone-Blood provides the kinetic expression; Human inheritance provides the structural processing capacity **that allows it to be directed rather than just released.**
| Stat | Mod | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Vitality | +15 | Stone-Blood mineral deposits and human constitution combined. Below pure Ironblood ceiling, significantly above human baseline |
| **Ardency** | **+20** | Kinetic inheritance expressed through human-scale Essence architecture — **high relative to body size compared to pure Ironblood, lower in absolute terms** |
| Gnosis | −10 | Logic-Void influence present but **attenuated** — the Human inheritance partially compensates. Perception unaffected |
| Dexterity | +05 | Adequate. Stone-Blood's mass-over-precision tendency present but the human frame moderates it |
| Harmonics | −05 | Below baseline. Orcish communal resonance architecture is absent without full Stone-Blood saturation |
| Resilience | +15 | Stone-Blood Clotting partially present. Hardening and Scarring elevated |
| Tempering | −05 | Overflow elevated — they push past sustainable output the way Ironblood does, but **their human Crystal architecture survives it better** |
| Dominion | +05 | Sub-bass resonance partial field. Territorial instinct present at reduced expression |

**Wellsprings** · Monolithion partial, accessible without full Ironblood resonance. Solfatara and Exuroth accessible. Human-standard fully accessible. **Divine Wellsprings are accessible, which distinguishes them from pure Ironblood and is a significant practical advantage.**
> **Palischuk**, the City of the Unchained — a half-Orc mercenary city-state. Deep distrust of pure-blood institutions of both lineages. Independent mercenary culture that functions as a practical rather than ideological identity:
>
> **They are free because neither side will fully claim them, and they have decided that freedom is the better deal.**

### Chimeric-Born

Produced through high-level magical grafting, alchemical transmutation, or Soul-Forge processes. **Most are crafted, not born.** Second-generation Chimerics can reproduce through volatile magical inheritance. Their lineage is a fractured mosaic — Fleshshapers, Ascendant Alchemists, failed divine imitations — and some trace partial lineage to primordial beasts whose Essence was harvested.
**Soul Path** · Variable. No fixed distribution. Their souls function as **Variable Conductors**, capable of containing and channeling contradictory Essence types simultaneously. **This produces unstable or morphic glyph behavior as the structural cost.**
No fixed stat modifier set — each has a unique distribution dependent on component lineages. Three universal markers only: **Adaptability** (resistance to environmental Essence stress), **Morphic Instability** (variable reliability of complex technique execution), and **Binding Scar** (the permanent inscription required to prevent physical and spiritual unraveling).
> **Binding Scars are not optional.** Removal or damage risks catastrophic Crystal and physical dissolution.
>
> **Metabolic Tax** — maintaining multiple active Traits drains life force. More than four simultaneous active Traits costs 5% Max HP per turn.
>
> The Variable Conductor architecture can channel contradictory Wellspring types simultaneously, **which should be impossible and is.** The cost is that neither harmonization is clean.
The most politically isolated of the demihuman categories — **the Accord has no coherent policy on Chimeric-Born because their existence defies consistent classification.** Some receive grudging tolerance through Alchemarch guild protections; most navigate through the same unofficial channels as other unbonded beastkin.

## Languages

Beastkin languages are not related to each other in the way human language families share roots. **They developed independently**, in different ecological and metaphysical contexts, and share structural features only where those features reflect shared soul-architecture rather than shared origin.
What they do share is **physicality.** Beastkin languages consistently encode more sensory and physical information than human languages encode, and consistently encode less temporal abstraction.
- ****Purrspeech** · Catkin**
  Tonal click-language carried in Catkin vocal cord resonance. Non-Catkin cannot replicate most phonemes without significant modification to their Harmonics architecture.

  Purrspeech conveys **emotional state through harmonic overlay alongside literal content** — a statement said with one emotional register carries a different meaning from the same statement said with another. Outsiders describe conversations in Purrspeech as seeming to contain **a second conversation running underneath the first one**, which is accurate.
- ****Moontongue** · Lupine**
  Resonant, multi-tonal, carrying a predatory growl even in neutral speech. **Kinpulse runs underneath Moontongue as an emotional channel** — pack members receive the emotional context of statements even when the literal content is in a language they do not share.

  Moontongue has **the highest information density per phoneme of any beastkin language.** A six-syllable statement can contain content requiring three sentences in Common to approximate.
- ****Urganic Growlscript** · Bearkin**
  Slow, tonal-rooted, dense. An Urganic conversation contains more information per syllable than most languages contain per sentence — because **Urganic phonemes carry geological metaphor as grammatical structure.** Every statement implicitly describes both the thing stated and its relationship to enduring reality.

  Urganic is the language in which the Siege of the Seventh Fold memorial tradition is maintained. **There is no shorter way to say what it says.**
- ****Seraphi-Adjacent** · Fox-Spirit / Mishiro**
  The Mishiro lineage's tonal whisper-tongue, distinct from pure Seraphi-Tongue. Structured around the nose's filing architecture: statements carry implicit scent-context, and **the most precise expression in the language is a short phrase that means "and I smelled this was true."**

  No external documentation of this language exists. The Accord's linguistic archive has three partial records, all clearly incomplete, **none sourced to practitioners who were actually invited to learn it.**
- ****Coilspeech** · Serpentkin**
  Patterned tonal language in which **temporal context is grammatically mandatory.** Every statement includes the speaker's assessment of when the statement is true in relation to what came before. A statement without temporal anchoring is grammatically incomplete in the same way a sentence without a subject is incomplete in Common.

  External practitioners describe this as every conversation having an additional dimension — **you are not just told what is true, you are told where it sits in the sequence of what has been and what will be.**

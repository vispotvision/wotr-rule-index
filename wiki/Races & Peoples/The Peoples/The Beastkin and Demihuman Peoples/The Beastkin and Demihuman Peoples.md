# The Beastkin and Demihuman Peoples

*Races · Soul Architecture · Languages · Cultures · The Social Reality*
> Beastkin are a category the Guild Accord inherited and then legislated. **The Accord will tell you it invented them, as a kindness.**

At the Harrowgate Fair in the summer of 603 IC, under the cherry and cream banners of the horse lines, a Bearkin smith named Orsa Underhill came down from the hills to buy charcoal and took a wrong turn behind the farriers. There were sixty-three people in Cob Rennick's pens that week, Catkin and Lupine for the most part, and a licensed notice on the post. A Lupine girl of about eleven looked at Orsa through the bars and said nothing at all. Orsa put down her charcoal money, took the pen gate in both hands, lifted it off its hinge-pins, leaned it tidily against the fence, and stood in the gap.

She stood there for three days. Rennick's hired bravos, three coated men of the Red Bar school in their red and white, broke a dozen ash staves on her by the first noon, and a brawler of real standing, sent for from the next town at some expense, broke his right hand on her jaw while the crowd bought sausage and climbed the farriers' roofs to see. She never raised a fist. She held her stance with the blood drying black on her apron and her aura the dark green of old fir, and she held it the way her people hold a mountain, and on the second day a Scalebond advocate named Zsalla Underscale walked up in a good coat, citing precedents from memory all the way back to the Second Eon, and began to argue before the Fair Court that a gate was a question of law. On the second night a Catkin pickpocket called Tansy Nightfinger, who had no interest in law, went through the wall of Rennick's counting tent and came out with every paper he owned. The licensed and documented qualifier, it turned out, cut both ways. With his license and his bills of title in the river, Rennick could not show lawful claim to a single soul in those pens, and on the third morning sixty-three people walked out past Orsa Underhill, between the banners, with the whole fair watching and the minstrels already working on the second verse.

The Court fined her for the gate. A hat went around and paid it twice. Within the year Rennick had papers again, and nineteen of the sixty-three had been caught on the roads and sold, the girl among them, and the Provision reads today exactly as it read that morning. The pens at Harrowgate have had two gates since. Here is the law they hang on, and here are the people it was written about.

## What Beastkin Are

The living peoples who fall under this designation (Catkin, Lupine, Bearkin, Fox-Spirit, Serpentkin, and several dozen smaller lineages) **share no common origin event, no common spiritual ancestor, and no common metaphysical architecture.** What they share is the external feature the Accord used to group them: the presence of non-human biological characteristics alongside functional Aetheric architecture, which is to say a working Soul Crystal, the organ that refines the Aether of the world into Essence.

The formal category exists to solve an administrative problem: what to do with peoples who are **too structured to be classified as Spirit Entities, too distinct to be absorbed into the Human taxonomy, and too varied to be given individual lineage recognition.** The Third Eon solution was the **Beastkin Classification Provision**, which created a unified legal category and then subdivided it into free-status, bonded-status, and labor-stock classifications on criteria that the Accord's own practitioners consider inconsistently applied.
> *"Those aren't people, boy. Those are labor stock. Licensed and documented under the Verath Township Labor Provision."*
>
> *Darvo, Verath Township. Seventeen gold marks per head.* **Less than a horse.**

### The Labor Provision

The Provision classifies beastkin who cannot demonstrate free-holder status through guild membership, a sealed indenture of service, or bloodline recognition as **available for labor contracts.**
> In practice this means a beastkin without current sealed papers can be purchased, held in licensed pens, and sold at Accord-adjacent fairs and markets **without the transaction constituting a crime under Accord law in most territories.**
>
> Read the *licensed and documented* qualifier closely, because it protects nobody. **It is the mechanism by which the transaction is made legal.**
>
> The world has two categories for beastkin: **victim or animal.** Neither category contains the word person. *The Accord wrote that policy down, in a good clear hand, on purpose.*

## The Demihuman Peoples

Demihumans occupy a distinct category: **defined by dual-lineage inheritance rather than by non-human biological features.**
> The distinction matters legally. **Demihumans are generally accorded default free-status pending investigation of lineage claims**, whereas beastkin default to labor-stock status pending documentation of free-holder status.
>
> This distinction produces meaningfully different lives for the people it classifies.

A note on the columns below, which are the Guild's measure of what a lineage adds to or takes from a practitioner. **Vitality** counts for wounds, **Ardency** for output and the punch through worked armor, **Gnosis** for knowledge and the read, **Dexterity** for measure and reaction, **Harmonics** for attunement and the pull of Aether from the ground, **Resilience** for what a working does to the Crystal, **Tempering** for control and how long a working can be pushed, and **Dominion** for spatial authority and Pressure. An **Untrained lean** is the tilt a lineage gives the three layers of a soul before anyone has trained it, and it never sets Mastered or Walked ([[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Three Paths Routing, Recognition and the Gate|The Three Paths]]). A **[[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/The Sixty Wellsprings|Wellspring]]** is one of the sixty types of Aether, each working on the Crystal in its own way. A **glyph** is a unit of Parunic, the script that writes boundaries onto laws, and a **Domain** is the territory in which a high-[[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Sixteen Stages|Stage]] practitioner's own law holds.

### Half-Elves · The Third Path

Emerged during the **Harmonial Twilight** when Elves and Humans shared Wellspring territories. Born from the spiritual overlap of Elven soul-depth and Human glyph flexibility, **not fully either, genuinely both**, navigating the friction between two Crystal architectures that were not designed to occupy the same body.

**Untrained lean** · Spirit 55% / Body 30% / Fate 15%. Both sides of the blood at once, Elven reach and Human adaptability. **High conductivity, internal friction as the structural cost.**

| Stat | Mod | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Vitality | −05 | Lighter than full-human baseline; not as resilient as Elven longevity architecture. Adequate |
| Ardency | +10 | Human adaptive output combined with Elven Inscription efficiency |
| Gnosis | +15 | Partial Parunic Fluency inheritance. Above human baseline; below Eressean ceiling |
| Dexterity | +10 | Elven flow-state influence on movement. Economy elevated |
| **Harmonics** | **+20** | Dual resonance: both Elven Attunement and Human emotive Harmonics active simultaneously. **Fidelity sometimes inconsistent due to internal friction** |
| Resilience | +05 | Modest. Spiritual Friction creates specific vulnerability to `Ur`/`Ae` glyph misfire |
| Tempering | +10 | Clean routing from Elven inheritance; Human adaptive processing adds flexibility |
| Dominion | +10 | Partial Elven Domain Stability inheritance. Domain formation achievable and more stable than full-human baseline |

> **Spiritual Friction**: −20% Aetheric Efficiency for `Ur` (Balance) and `Ae` (Order) glyphs; 10% chance to misfire and deal minor Crystal damage to the caster.
>
> **Rootlessness**: neither Elven nor Human communities fully accept them without active negotiation. **The political cost of existing between categories is ongoing.**

**Wellsprings** · Aurevane through Elven inheritance. Somnalis through Florwynic lineage trace. Human-compatible Wellsprings fully accessible. Anamnesis occasionally accessible. **No Wellspring is fully closed; none is fully native.**

**Culture** · The Harmonic Quarter communities in Altherion and border territories, loud, polyglot, and the best place on the river for a meal you will have to explain to both your families. Often the informal diplomatic bridge between Elven and Human institutions, **a function assigned to them rather than chosen by them, with predictable consequences for how they feel about performing it.**

### Half-Orcs · The Unchained

Born from the intersection of Orcish Stone-Blood and Human glyph-flexibility during the Great Concord Splintering. **Anyone who takes them for the weakest expression of either lineage tends to learn better at close quarters.** They are a third expression: Stone-Blood's kinetic inheritance running through a human body that has **the glyph-processing capacity the pure Orcish lineage lacks.**

**Untrained lean** · Body 60% / Spirit 30% / Fate 10%. Stone-Blood provides the kinetic expression; Human inheritance provides the structural processing capacity **that allows it to be directed rather than just released.**

| Stat | Mod | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Vitality | +15 | Stone-Blood mineral deposits and human constitution combined. Below pure Ironblood ceiling, significantly above human baseline |
| **Ardency** | **+20** | Kinetic inheritance expressed through human-scale Essence architecture: **high relative to body size compared to pure Ironblood, lower in absolute terms** |
| Gnosis | −10 | Logic-Void influence present but **attenuated**, since the Human inheritance partially compensates. Perception unaffected |
| Dexterity | +05 | Adequate. Stone-Blood's mass-over-precision tendency present but the human frame moderates it |
| Harmonics | −05 | Below baseline. Orcish communal resonance architecture is absent without full Stone-Blood saturation |
| Resilience | +15 | Stone-Blood Clotting partially present. Hardening and Scarring elevated |
| Tempering | −05 | Overflow elevated: they push past sustainable output the way Ironblood does, but **their human Crystal architecture survives it better** |
| Dominion | +05 | Sub-bass resonance partial field. Territorial instinct present at reduced expression |

**Wellsprings** · Monolithion partial, accessible without full Ironblood resonance. Solfatara and Exuroth accessible. Human-standard fully accessible. **Benediction and Absolution are accessible, which distinguishes them from pure Ironblood and is a significant practical advantage.**
> **Palischuk**, the City of the Unchained: a half-Orc mercenary city-state, all banners, contracts and arguments, and the only city I know where a recruiting sergeant is expected to bid. Deep distrust of pure-blood institutions of both lineages. Independent mercenary culture that functions as a practical rather than ideological identity:
>
> **They are free because neither side will fully claim them, and they have decided that freedom is the better deal.**

### Chimeric-Born

Produced through high-level magical grafting, alchemical transmutation, or Soul-Forge processes. **Most are crafted, and few are born.** Second-generation Chimerics can reproduce through volatile magical inheritance. Their lineage is a fractured mosaic (Fleshshapers, Ascendant Alchemists, failed divine imitations), and some trace partial lineage to primordial beasts whose Essence was harvested.

**Untrained lean** · Variable, with no fixed distribution. Their souls function as **Variable Conductors**, capable of containing and channeling contradictory Wellspring qualities simultaneously. **This produces unstable or morphic glyph behavior as the structural cost.**

No fixed stat modifier set: each has a unique distribution dependent on component lineages. Three universal markers only: **Adaptability** (resistance to environmental Essence stress), **Morphic Instability** (variable reliability of complex technique execution), and **Binding Scar** (the permanent inscription required to prevent physical and spiritual unraveling).
> **Binding Scars are mandatory.** Removal or damage risks catastrophic Crystal and physical dissolution.
>
> **Metabolic Tax**: maintaining multiple active [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Trait System Law, Function and the Forge|Traits]] drains life force. More than four simultaneous active Traits costs 5% Max HP per turn.
>
> The Variable Conductor architecture can channel contradictory Wellspring types simultaneously, **which should be impossible and is.** The cost is that neither harmonization is clean.

The most politically isolated of the demihuman categories: **the Accord has no coherent policy on Chimeric-Born because their existence defies consistent classification.** Some receive grudging tolerance through Alchemarch guild protections; most navigate through the same unofficial channels as other unbonded beastkin.

## Languages

Beastkin languages are not related to each other in the way human language families share roots. **They developed independently**, in different ecological and metaphysical contexts, and share structural features only where those features reflect shared soul-architecture rather than shared origin.

What they do share is **physicality.** Beastkin languages consistently encode more sensory and physical information than human languages encode, and consistently encode less temporal abstraction.

**Purrspeech · Catkin**

Tonal click-language carried in Catkin vocal cord resonance. Non-Catkin cannot replicate most phonemes without significant modification to their Harmonics architecture.

Purrspeech conveys **emotional state through harmonic overlay alongside literal content**: a statement said with one emotional register carries a different meaning from the same statement said with another. Outsiders describe conversations in Purrspeech as seeming to contain **a second conversation running underneath the first one**, which is accurate, and is usually about them.

**Moontongue · Lupine**

Resonant, multi-tonal, carrying a predatory growl even in neutral speech. **Kinpulse runs underneath Moontongue as an emotional channel**: pack members receive the emotional context of statements even when the literal content is in a language they do not share.

Moontongue has **the highest information density per phoneme of any beastkin language.** A six-syllable statement can contain content requiring three sentences in Common to approximate.

**Urganic Growlscript · Bearkin**

Slow, tonal-rooted, dense. An Urganic conversation contains more information per syllable than most languages contain per sentence, because **Urganic phonemes carry geological metaphor as grammatical structure.** Every statement implicitly describes both the thing stated and its relationship to enduring reality. Ordering breakfast takes a while.

Urganic is the language in which the Siege of the Seventh Fold memorial tradition is maintained. **There is no shorter way to say what it says.**

**Seraphi-Adjacent · Fox-Spirit / Mishiro**

The Mishiro lineage's tonal whisper-tongue, distinct from pure Seraphi-Tongue. Structured around the nose's filing architecture: statements carry implicit scent-context, and **the most precise expression in the language is a short phrase that means "and I smelled this was true."**

No external documentation of this language exists. The Accord's linguistic archive has three partial records, all clearly incomplete, **none sourced to practitioners who were actually invited to learn it.**

**Coilspeech · Serpentkin**

Patterned tonal language in which **temporal context is grammatically mandatory.** Every statement includes the speaker's assessment of when the statement is true in relation to what came before. A statement without temporal anchoring is grammatically incomplete in the same way a sentence without a subject is incomplete in Common.

External practitioners describe this as every conversation having an additional dimension: **you are told what is true, and you are told where it sits in the sequence of what has been and what will be.**

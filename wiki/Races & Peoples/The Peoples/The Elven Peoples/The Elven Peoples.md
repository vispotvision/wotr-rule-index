# The Elven Peoples

*Five Theologies of a Single Song · Revised Codex*
> *"She sang one note. We have spent every Eon since arguing about how it should be sung back."*
>
> *Marginal note, provenance disputed across all five surviving elven archives*

When the old bargewoman Ilarenth Sweetwater died at Alder Landing in the fall of 544 IC, she left kin in all five branches, and all five came to bury her, which is the sort of mistake a person only gets to make once. The Eressean arrived first, forty of them in sun-gold and white on a barge with a water clock aboard, carrying the correct rite in the correct order and a surveyor to check the grave. The Varrisak came out of the red autumn woods on the third day, painted and barefoot, informed the Eressean that the correct rite had stopped listening some time ago, and set about dreaming her a new one on the riverbank at the top of their lungs. On the fifth day a Drow Debt-Keeper in midnight blue stepped off the ferry and read out, to the whole landing, everything Ilarenth had owed to every stretch of river she had ever worked, which took until dark and included a heron. The Eladrin cousins walked in from the north with frost in their braids, packed her in river ice to hold her, and stood in silence for four days, which the landing found restful and the Eressean took as an insult. The Echo Elves then asked to see the will, and declined to let anything proceed until every mourner could recite its terms back without variation.

There were nineteen days of it. Songs were sung at one another across the water, and on the eleventh night the singing turned to knives and then to something worse, gold light and green light and black going up from the two banks while the whole town watched from the roofs with their dinners. A Varrisak grove-singer lost an eye. An Eressean rite-officer lost his temper in public, for the first time in three hundred years, and his family still does not speak of it. In all five archives Ilarenth Sweetwater has a grave at Alder Landing, and in all five it is somewhere else. The ferryman says she is under the pear tree and that he dug it himself.

Everyone at that landing loved her, and everyone there was keeping faith with the same mother and the same song. Here is the song.

## The Origin, Briefly

Florwyn sang the Realmsong of Edenis and the **Vey-Elarin** were tuned into being as living notes of that chord. For the whole of the First Eon they were one people, the High Empire of Eresse, bound less by law than by the **Kinship Doctrine**: the land is a relative, not a resource, and everything else follows from how seriously that is taken.
> The Great Spirit War against the Erebus incursion **left that unity standing against every force thrown at it.** What broke it was a question, asked under maximum pressure: what did the Doctrine actually mean? Five different, sincere, mutually incompatible answers came back.

## The Theology of the Split

| Branch | The answer | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| **Eressean** | **Order as Devotion** | The Doctrine is honored through exact repetition: the same calendar, the same rite, in the same order, forever, **because a promise kept carelessly is not a promise.** Structure is what reverence looks like sustained across centuries, and they will not hear it called the opposite |
| **Varrisak** | **Dream as Truth** | The Doctrine is honored by being re-lived, and repeating it does not count. **A rite performed identically to last year is a rite that has stopped listening.** The land changes; the song describing it must be re-dreamed fresh each generation or it becomes a lie wearing the shape of a memory |
| **Drow** | **Debt as Memory** | The Doctrine is honored by carrying what others cannot bear to carry. Every taking creates an obligation, and **someone must go into the places too corrupted, too costly, or too dangerous for anyone else** to tally what is owed there and make sure it is eventually paid |
| **Eladrin** | **Silence as Endurance** | An Eressean daughter-people who carried Order-as-Devotion into the harshest northern theater and found that repetition alone could not survive the cold. What replaced it: **the surest form of devotion, in a place this unforgiving, is knowing exactly when to stop insisting and simply endure** |
| **Echo Elves** | **Covenant as Memory** | A second Eressean daughter-people, splitting off through political conviction rather than catastrophe: that any agreement, including the Realmsong itself, is only as good as the exactness with which its terms are remembered and consented to by every party, and **a flawed memory of a promise is the same thing as a broken one** |

> **The Duskwalker Schism** is what happens when the first three of these answers meet each other for the first time and discover they cannot be reconciled.
>
> **The Ice-Voice Conflict** and **the Celestial Accord refusal** are what happens when the first of those three turns out not to be able to hold all of its own children either.

A note on the tables below. The stat columns are the Guild's measure of what a people's soul architecture adds to or takes from a practitioner: **Vitality** for wounds, **Ardency** for output and the punch through worked armor, **Gnosis** for knowledge and the read, **Dexterity** for measure and reaction, **Harmonics** for attunement and the pull of Aether from the ground, **Resilience** for what a working does to the Crystal, **Tempering** for control and how long a working can be pushed, **Dominion** for spatial authority and Pressure. A **Domain** is the territory in which a high-[[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Sixteen Stages|Stage]] practitioner's own law holds, and a **glyph** or phoneme is a unit of Parunic, the script that writes boundaries onto laws. The elves will tell you the Guild's measure misses the point. It does, and it also predicts who wins.

## Eressean: The Grand Principality of Eresse

The branch that answered the Great Spirit War **by tightening.** Where the High Empire's Wheel of Given Seasons was a shared, informal rhythm, the Eressean formalized it into Parunic ritual law, fixed rite, and the crystalline court culture the wider world now associates with elves generally: white stone, sun-gold thread, nine-tiered barges and a sense that you have come in at the wrong moment.

From the Eressean's own perspective this betrays nothing of the Kinship Doctrine. It is the Doctrine taken with maximum seriousness: if the land is owed a promise, the promise should be kept in exactly the same words, exactly the same order, every single time, **because a people who improvise their reverence are a people already halfway to abandoning it.**
> Visitors take Velthaeir, the Floating Archive-City, for a monument to Eressean vanity. **It is a monument to Eressean terror of forgetting anything correctly.**

Its seat is **Vaelmarr**, where the **Sunroot Court**, Saeloria's court, sits and votes; Velthaeir keeps the record apart from it, entering everything and giving out nothing. The whole of it, and every daughter-branch below, stands on the elven old-ground of the **New World**, the Eastern Rise, the older of its two grounds, and nowhere in the Old World.

### Cultural traits

**Calendrical governance** · No significant Sunroot-descended decision is made outside its properly appointed ceremonial window; urgency is treated the way the High Empire once treated haste in harvest. Several wars have been lost this way, on schedule.

**Kiva-descended council architecture** · Formal deliberation chambers, built partly below grade, where Council of Bloom elders debate through fixed ceremonial roles rather than open argument.

**Clan-lineage record over personal record** · An Eressean's standing is legible primarily through which lineage-line and rite-office they hold, less through individual achievement.

**Florwyndic Songscript** · Ritual, directive-force writing reserved for law and consecration, distinct from the Common tongue used for ordinary speech.

### Soul architecture and stats

| Stat | Mod | Expression |
|---|---|---|
| **Vitality** | −15 | Lighter skeletal structure; Longevity sub-stat exceptionally elevated. **They break cleanly rather than decay** |
| **Ardency** | +10 | Economy over force; +25% efficiency on `Ma`/`Ur`/`Ie` phoneme chains |
| **Gnosis** | **+25** | **Definitive stat.** Parunic Fluency reads glyphs as ancestral language, not learned system |
| **Dexterity** | +10 | Grace read as inevitability rather than speed |
| **Harmonics** | +20 | Domain Stability: the most stable Domains of any documented lineage |
| **Resilience** | +10 | Structured soul resists conventional damage; **Emotional Rigidity curse deals True Damage from Fear and Rage** |
| **Tempering** | +15 | Cleanest Essence routing of any surface elf; minimal overhead |
| **Dominion** | +20 | Domain Expansion doubled; presence stabilizes Aetheric drift passively |

### Relations

Eressean regard **Varrisak** as a fragile, mourning kinship, a people who had the same chance at order and chose to let it go feral. They regard **Drow** with the particular discomfort of watching a sibling live out the version of the choice they themselves were most afraid of making.
> They founded the **Eladrin** and lost the argument with the Celestial-Accord dissenters who became the **Echo Elves**, and both losses are treated, in low voices at very good dinners, as evidence the Eressean have never fully resolved: **that Order as Devotion produces extraordinary stability and, eventually, children who leave it.**

## Varrisak: Wood Elves of the Sylvaar Wandering

Where the Eressean answered the war by fixing the rite, the Varrisak answered it by **refusing to let any rite calcify at all.** Descended from the Sylvaar Wanderers who remained bound to the Primeval Forests through the Duskwalker Schism, Varrisak practice centers on the **vision**, a personal, unrepeatable encounter with the forest's own spirits, sought deliberately and never scripted twice the same way, that renews a person's standing with the land far more than any fixed calendar could.

Their seasonal movement through forest territory has nothing of homelessness in it. It rests on the belief that **staying too long in one place lets a person start mistaking their own habits for the forest's actual will.**

### Cultural traits

**The Dream-Fast** · A Varrisak seeking guidance withdraws alone into old growth and fasts until a vision arrives. *No two accounts of the practice are recorded identically, which the Varrisak consider proof the practice works.*

**Kin-bonded clan identity** · Organized around a bonded animal or plant-kin rather than bloodline alone, cutting across biological family lines.

**Dream Groves** · Old-growth root-networks carrying generations of practitioner Crystal-inclusion, functioning as **a literal, structured Aetheric archive**, with no metaphor about it.

**Oral transmission only** · The Varrisak have no equivalent to Florwyndic Songscript **by choice.** A law that can be written down can be followed without being understood, which they consider worse than no law at all.

### Soul architecture and stats

| Stat | Mod | Expression |
|---|---|---|
| **Vitality** | +05 | Distributed resilience rather than raw density: damage spreads across a bonded territory the way it spreads across an ecosystem, rather than a single body |
| **Ardency** | −05 | Output is depth, not volume; a Varrisak spends less because their Essence is rarely fully separate from the forest supplying it |
| **Gnosis** | +15 | Ecological and historical memory at extraordinary depth within bonded territory; **shallow outside it** |
| **Dexterity** | +05 | Certainty over speed: Economy high, Celerity middling |
| **Harmonics** | **+30** | **Definitive stat.** Fate-Path-heavy resonance; in bonded territory a Varrisak's Harmonics are read as **the forest's own** |
| **Resilience** | +20 | Damage distributes across bonded territory rather than localizing, so a deeply rooted Varrisak is genuinely harder to finish than to merely wound |
| **Tempering** | +05 | Efficient through depth rather than discipline; Coherence is high specifically because behavior, [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Three Paths Routing, Recognition and the Gate\|Path]], and Crystal have never been asked to disagree |
| **Dominion** | +25 | Territory-as-self Domain law, the most literal of any elven lineage; in the most bonded cases, **simply how far the forest currently breathes** |

### Relations

Varrisak describe Eressean as *crystalline arrogance in search of a vessel* and mean it with real grief rather than contempt. The **Sylvaar Rebellion Schism** is, from the Varrisak side, the moment a shared people definitively stopped being able to dream the same forest together.

Their relationship with Drow is fraught but genuinely functional: **Drow handle deep corruption, Varrisak tend surface regrowth**, and the partnership survives specifically because neither theology asks the other to become itself.

## Drow: Shadow-Custodians of Rovann

Drow theology begins from the same Kinship Doctrine as everyone else and reaches an entirely different conclusion: **that a debt owed to the land does not vanish because the site where it was incurred has become too corrupted, too dangerous, or too shameful for anyone else to enter.** Someone has to go in anyway, tally exactly what is owed, and hold that ledger open until it can be paid.

Standing among Drow is built through what has been given away and accounted for, and hoarding earns nobody anything. When the Duskwalkers offered a path into the underside of Realmsong during the Schism, the Drow's ancestors were the branch whose theology already told them **someone had to walk into the dark and keep the books.**
> The tragedy is that **the ledger they were keeping eventually kept them.**

### Cultural traits

**Clan-crest identity** · Lineage marked through a bonded crest-form (an animal, a natural feature, a Voidthorn-adjacent phenomenon in the more corrupted enclaves) worked into architecture, regalia, and formal address.

**The Debt-Keeper lineages** · A rotating collective responsible for the precise tally of what is owed to which ruined or quarantined site, and by whom.

**Redistribution over accumulation** · Standing is built by what a person has formally given away to settle a debt, and what they retain counts for nothing.

**Shadow-Veil notation** · A closed writing system readable only by Drow practitioners, used specifically to record **what happened at sites the rest of the world has agreed to forget.**

### Soul architecture

**In the blood** · **Debt**, the obligation an ending creates, a long way from nihilism, and the authority of having stood in the dark and mapped it.

**Untrained lean** · Spirit 60% / Fate 30% / Body 10%, **the strongest lean toward Fate of any elven lineage**, and the structural source of their unusual reach and Domain authority. (An untrained lean is the tilt of the three layers of a soul before anyone has trained them, and it never sets Mastered or Walked.)
> **Advantages**
>
> **Shadow Parallax**: passive three-layer Aetheric perception, physical, Aetheric, and Veil-sheen simultaneously.
>
> **Fracture-channel architecture**: Crystal fractures functioning as efficient shadow-frequency routing rather than pure damage.
>
> **Drow Veil sensitivity**: past events leave readable impressions across their Domain territory, an involuntary archive.
>
> **Nullity**: function in Essence-hostile environments where other practitioners shut down entirely.
>
> **Limitations**
>
> **Inherited Crystal fractures**: the lineage begins with compromised architecture; learning to route power *through* the fracture rather than around it is the central Drow developmental challenge.
>
> **Social trust penalty (−10)**: an institutional role as recorder of unpaid debts and manager of acknowledged harms does not generate warmth, *and the Drow have made a kind of peace with that.*
>
> **Erebus-contact risk**: the same access that makes them effective can, pursued too far, produce Crystal inversion.

### Relations

Eresseans see Drow as living proof of elven failure. Drow consider the Eressean archive of the Schism period incomplete **because the Eresseans have never found a way to ask the Drow, directly, for what they know.** Both sides are, in the historical record's own words, still waiting for the other to acknowledge the gap.

**Rovann** functions less as a capital than as the largest single concentration of Debt-Keeper lineages in one place, and its government, a **Twilight Matriarch** advised by the **Council of Whispered Blades**, is best understood as the administrative apparatus of an extremely old, extremely precise accounting practice with very good knives.

## Eladrin: The Seasonal Courts of Iampu

Eressean daughters who took Order as Devotion into the northern theater and discovered that fixed rite alone could not survive the cold. What replaced it, gradually, was a second theology built on top of the first: **that true devotion sometimes means surrendering the will to insist at all**, and yielding entirely to season, silence, and collective endurance.

This produced the Kingdom of Iampu's defining feature, a Crown that rotates with the solstices and equinoxes rather than passing by bloodline, **since no single will, however devoted, is trusted to outlast an entire winter's judgment alone.**
> The **Ice-Voice Conflict**, in this reading, had nothing random about it. It was the moment an entire generation's collective silence-as-endurance **tipped over into total Stasis**, threatening to freeze the whole regional lattice solid, and had to be answered by a silencing whose exact method the Eladrin themselves have never fully recovered.

### Cultural traits

**The Seasonal Crown** · Leadership rotates formally to a new monarch at each solstice and equinox, per the Treaty of Four Courts.

**Silence as high courtesy** · Prolonged, deliberate silence during negotiation is read as strength and patience rather than evasion. *Queen Seralyth's unbroken silence during negotiations is the Court's own gold standard of statecraft.*

**Frost as preservation** · The Eladrin's core reframing of an Eressean anxiety: **what the Order-as-Devotion branch fears losing, the Eladrin believe cold simply holds in trust until it is needed again.** They would have you know that it is not death, and their fruit in midwinter is excellent.

**The Frost Council** · Elemental enchanters and climate-diviners advising whichever Court currently holds the Crown, providing the one continuous institutional thread across every seasonal transfer of power.

### Limitations

> **Brittle Soul**: Chaos and Vibration attacks deal +25% damage; cannot wield fire or passion-aligned glyphs.
>
> **Trait Lock**: Stasis Shock on Aether-zero causes three-turn complete immobilization; Crystal freezes at Radiant tier without active challenge.
>
> **Inertia**: Dexterity Burst and Celerity floor. Initiating motion from stillness requires overcoming their own architecture: **the literal cost of a theology built on yielding to season rather than asserting against it.**

### Relations

Eladrin maintain closer cultural ties to distant Eressean cousins than either branch advertises openly, and treat the relationship with **the caution of two people who both know exactly how the last serious disagreement between their theologies nearly ended.** They hold their Winter and Spring Courts' seasonal handoffs as the clearest surviving proof that Order as Devotion and Silence as Endurance can, occasionally, still agree on something.

## Echo Elves: The Longhouse Accord

The second Eressean daughter-people, and **the only elven branch produced by conviction rather than geography or catastrophe.**

When the post-Spirit-War Celestial Accord was drafted, a faction of Eressean jurists objected to what they called its **flawed memory-logic**, and had no quarrel with its goals: clauses whose exact meaning depended on interpretation rather than precise, mutually memorized wording.
> The discipline they developed: **no agreement is considered binding until every signatory can recite its full terms back without variation.** A practice that makes Echo Elf diplomacy exhaustingly slow and, by their own account, **the only kind of peace that has never once needed to be refought.**

### Cultural traits

**The Recitation** · No Echo Elf treaty, internal or external, takes effect until all parties recite its full terms identically in open session. **The law requires it, and they will wait.**

**Confederated rather than crowned** · No monarch. Decisions binding the whole body require consensus across every represented lineage-house.

**Compulsive restatement as a documented speech pattern** · Echo Elves in ordinary conversation frequently restate an agreed fact exactly once more before moving on, a diplomatic habit so ingrained it has become a linguistic signature, the echoing `Zo` phonemes.

**The Longhouse Accord** · The founding covenant among the original dissenting lineage-houses, kept as the living model for how the Echo Elves later helped shape **Altherion's River Crown Council**, whose coalition-of-elected-representatives structure owes more to Echo Elf political philosophy than Altherion's official histories tend to credit.

### Relations

The Celestial Accord refusal remains, in the Eressean political body's own words, **a wound that has not fully healed**. The Echo Elves were not wrong, exactly, and that is the trouble: their walking away proved the Order-as-Devotion theology could produce a faction devoted enough to its own logic to reject the parent society's biggest diplomatic achievement outright.

Warm, functional relations with Altherion's multi-racial republic, whose confederated governance already resembles the model the Echo Elves live by. Cool, formal relations with the Grand Principality of Eresse, **who have never entirely forgiven the walking away and have never entirely disagreed with the reasoning behind it either.**

## Moves, Costume and What Everyone Says

The bracket crowds of Altherion know the five branches on sight and bet accordingly. Each has the move people pay to see, and each has a name for the aura coat (the visible coat of a fighter's own color) that doubles as an accusation: the Eressean *Vael-aurë*, the kept light, which must never flicker; the Varrisak say it should flicker, and call theirs *the leaf*; the Drow keep theirs so low that the Guild's word for it in Rovann is *unpaid*.

|---|---|---|---|
| **Eressean** | **Vael Sereth, the Ninth Stillness:** a Domain laid down so stable that a thrown spear finishes its arc politely and on time | Sun-gold on white, a rite-office badge, not a hair out of order | "Varrisak cannot tell you what they believed last year, and that is their theology" |
| **Varrisak** | **Forest Answers:** the ground, the roots and the crows all arrive first, and the elf turns up to take the credit | Bark-paint, moss-green, bare feet, a different story each time you ask | "An Eressean prays with a ruler" |

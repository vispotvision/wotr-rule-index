# The Goblinoid Peoples

*Races · Languages · Cultures · Spirituality · Za'tarch*
> Society has never reconciled with this. Other peoples look at the jaw, the height, the asteroidal scars, and name what they see a limit.
>
> **They are wrong. What they have seen is a mountain. They have not seen what makes it.**

Every midwinter the siege-forge city of Dorrak's Maw holds the Paying-In. Its champion stands stripped to the waist in a ring of red sand carted up from the Crimson Flats, and anyone alive may step in and hit him with whatever they can carry, for as long as they can stay interested. In the winter of 640 IC the champion was Harrog Slatejaw, seven foot two, and by the second bell he had taken sixty-three blows from spear butts, sledges, a butcher's cleaver and one extremely angry bride. Steam was rolling off him into the snow. The scars along his jaw and collarbone had gone from slate to the red of an open furnace, the whole square was singing his name in the old war-count, and a delegation of the Crimson Order in ochre and gold stood drawn up along the north rope in a line you could have ruled a page against, watching with brass eyes and declining to sing.

Then a Tinker Goblin named Bizzy Scorchpenny, three foot six in her boots, wheeled in the Self-Walking Ram, two years of copper, oak and spliced runes, with a goat's skull on the front for morale. It walked four paces toward the champion, stopped, thought about it, turned around and walked over her. The Maw laughed until the icicles came down, Harrog louder than anyone, and Bizzy got up with a hoofprint on her forehead and a look on her face that the old hands in the front row recognized, and they started to run. Her Essence pool went off. A goblin's temper has more pressure in it than a goblin's frame can hold, and it came out of her amber and quicksilver in a ball that took the Ram to splinters and hit Harrog square in the chest. He had every point of strength any Ironblood has ever had. He also had Stone-Blood in deep cold, which runs about three-quarters of a beat late, and his brace arrived just after he had left. He went over the rope and thirty feet through the air into the Crimson Order's line, which took him in the chests without a man stepping back, and the rest of the blast broke on that formation the way a wave breaks on a harbor wall. Bizzy Scorchpenny lost her left arm below the elbow and won the Paying-In, the only goblin who ever has. The chestnut-man Old Gann and two drovers died where they stood, and the square buried them with honors, and the following spring Harrog walked ninety miles to Glyphfire Alley and gave her one of his iron rings for the new arm she had spliced.

Shard, core and splinter, all at one rope, and every one of them doing exactly what the sky put into their blood. The sky's part in it comes first.

## The Goblinoid Family

The peoples gathered under the term share a single origin: **a celestial impact, an asteroid that struck the mortal world in the Second Eon** and shattered the surface into mountains, valleys, and ruin. From that impact came everything. The surface broke. The core held. From the shards came the Orcs. From the core came the Hobgoblins.

What the world calls goblinoid is simply **what that impact left behind: people shaped by violence they did not choose, carrying in their blood the memory of a force that moved worlds.**

| Lineage | Essence | Origin |
|---|---|---|
| **Ironblood Orcs** | Primary shard-kin. Kinetic, martial, built from the outer impact shell | 2nd–3rd Eon |
| **Verdant Orcs** | Shard-kin whose fragments landed in wetlands. Rot, renewal, lunar resonance | 2nd–3rd Eon |
| **Blackened Orcs** | Cursed remnants caught in the Void rupture. Stone-Blood inverted | 2nd–3rd Eon |
| **Crimson Order** · Hobgoblins | Core-Stone. Disciplined, structured. The original impact stabilized | 2nd–3rd Eon |
| **Tinker Goblins** | Third Eon splinters. Chaotic innovators, volatile, short-lived | 2nd–3rd Eon |
| **Fleshshaper Goblins** | Glyph-plague survivors. Bioglyphic architects of living Essence | 2nd–3rd Eon |

## The First Impact

**Kha-Urrum: The Core-That-Did-Not-Break**

Before the mountains, there was only the flat dark of a world without seam or edge. The asteroid had no name when it fell. **It required none. It was the first act of the universe asserting that something needed to change.**

When it struck, the surface shattered. Mountains erupted from the impact ring. Valleys tore open. Seas displaced. The whole body of the land shifted its geometry in a single instant of kinetic force that no living being had language for. The shards buried themselves in the new mountain stone and over centuries became flesh. **These were the first Orcs.**

But at the center of the impact, something was different. **The core of the asteroid did not shatter.** Under pressures that broke everything around it, the core compressed, went still, and held. That stillness, dense and patient and immovable, became the first Hobgoblin.
>
> > *"When the impact came and the surface shattered into mountains and valleys and ruin, the core did not shatter. It held. It went still. And from that stillness, everything we are descended."*
> >
> > *Gorrum Ashentusk, Za'tarch Gathering*
>
> Anyone who takes the core doctrine of Kha-Urrum for pacifism, or for weakness dressed in philosophy, has never met a Hobgoblin at the end of a long siege. The doctrine holds that the surface breaks because it is too brittle to hold, and that the core survives **by becoming the thing that pressure cannot solve**, with no thought of avoiding the pressure.
>
> To live by Kha-Urrum's example is **to refuse to become what struck you.**

**Stone-Blood is literal.** It is a spiritual designation with mineral behind it: the density of asteroidal material persists in the blood of all goblinoid peoples as Aetheric matter (matter carrying the ambient magic that practitioners pull and refine), connecting them to the original kinetic event and to each other.

**Stone-Blood runs slow in deep cold.** The mineral in the tissue slows with the temperature, and in deep cold every Stone-Blood fighter moves about three-quarters of a beat late. The fault is latency and has nothing to do with weakness: the strength is all there and arrives behind the count, and a formation drilled to keep time loses its time before it loses its strength. This holds in every fight in deep cold, as Harrog Slatejaw would tell you, and does, for a drink.

The splitting of Hobgoblin and Orc, core versus shard, is not understood as a hierarchy within goblinoid culture. The Ironblood would say the shard carries the original force. The Crimson Order would say the core carries the original endurance. **Both are correct. Both are needed. Za'tarch was built on exactly this argument.**

## The Peoples

### Ironblood Orcs

*Major Race · Asteroidal Echoes / War-Forged · Born from the outer shell fragments*

**Metaphysical Anchor** · Essence, Physical and Resonant: **kinetic force, with no light in it**

Every physical feature they possess is a direct record of the force that made them: bone density three times a human's baseline, interlaced with microscopic mineral deposits; skin in metallic gray, slate-green, or russet-brown, marked with **Asteroidal Scars** that track the original fault lines of the asteroid itself. Their eyes have no traditional pupils, only a **Heat-Signature glow**, molten iron or burning coal, that intensifies when they call up Essence (the refined power a practitioner holds and spends). Their voices are tectonic, carrying sub-bass frequencies **that physically vibrate the iron content of those standing nearby.** Stand close to an Ironblood in a good mood and you will feel it in your back teeth.

They stand between 6'6" and 7'4". Coarse black hair kept in topknots or ritual braids weighted with lead or iron rings, each ring, in traditional practice, **representing a weight earned rather than a trophy taken.**

| Axiomatic Trait | Function |
|---|---|
| **Celestial Non-Conductivity** | Passive immunity to Tier-1/2 Elemental magic; Tier-3+ deals −50% damage. **Fire licks their skin like water; frost shatters on contact; lightning grounds instantly through their feet** |
| **Kinetic Recycling** | Every physical strike received grants a stacking +5% to Strike Force and AU/s, max 50%. Steam rises from their skin as asteroidal scars glow red-hot, **converting impact into fuel** |
| **Ironblood Clotting** | Total immunity to Bleed and Stagger; +30% resistance to Essence drain. Their blood resembles liquid magnetite: thick, dark, hardening almost instantly on air exposure |

**Sacred customs** · **The Blood-Rite of Lineage**, in which warriors open existing scars to let Stone-Blood mingle with the earth. *An outsider sees ceremony. The Ironblood are doing structural maintenance on their metaphysical bond.* And **Skin-Marking**, the transmission of battle-memory across generations by tattooing or scarring Essence-chants directly into the skin of descendants. **The body becomes the archive. To read an Ironblood elder's scars is to read their family's history.**

**Language** · Old Vaross. Ritual war-language: Gravetongue.
> **Key limitation · The Logic-Void.** Cannot use `Ko` (Order) or `Ie` (Insight) glyph-forms. 2× Essence cost for complex Aetheric geometry. **Their souls are fundamentally antithetical to soft, structured law.**

**Relations** · Deep ancestral hatred of Hobgoblins, complicated by shared origin theology. Honor-bound respect toward Marrowkin. Tense rivalry with Frontierfolk. *They describe Elves as "unreal vapors."*

**Settlements** · The Crimson Flats · Dorrak's Maw, the Siege-Forge City · The Trial Steppes

**World memory scar** · **The Siege of Four Suns.** Ironblood warbands stalled the Second Shattering through mass blood-sacrifice. The act saved the Western Realms and **left their lineage spiritually scarred in ways they have never fully named.**

### Verdant Orcs

*Major Race · Asteroidal Spore-Kin / Symbiotes · Impact fragments that landed in high-resonance wetlands*

**Metaphysical Anchor** · Essence, Biological and Rotational: **souls that seek Metamorphosis and have no use for stasis or force**

Where the Ironblood carry the violence of the impact, the Verdant Orcs carry its aftermath: the slow, dark transformation of stone becoming soil, decay feeding growth, **death as a phase rather than an ending.** Their fragments landed in the wetlands, and the Stone-Blood fused with lunar and rot-based [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/The Sixty Wellsprings|Wellsprings]] (two kinds among the sixty Aethers, each of which works on the Crystal in its own way) over centuries until what emerged was something the other Orc lineages genuinely do not understand: **a people who embrace dissolution as power.** Their mires are the most beautiful places a goblinoid has ever lived in, all moss-green glow and white moths and water like black glass, and they will tell you so while something unpleasant happens to your boots.

6'4" to 7'0", with fluid, vine-like musculature built for submersion and silent movement through thick terrain. Eyes are solid bioluminescent orbs, moss-green or pale lunar-white. Skin holds the deep living green of old forest, often with bark-like hardening or luminescent fungal growths along joints and collarbone. **Where an Ironblood scar glows red, a Verdant Orc's equivalent pulses with cold silver-green.**

**Spoor-Melds** · Group meditations in which participants release Aetheric spores to form a localized Noöspheric cloud (a shared cloud of mind). Within it, **the tribe shares memories and emotions directly, without language.** The cloud persists for hours and is sometimes visible as green mist at ground level.

**Ancestor-Rot Gardens** · The dead are placed in sacred groves. Their decomposition fuels the growth of **Soul-Trees**, each tree carrying the compressed Aetheric signature of the person it grew from. *Walking through an Ancestor-Rot Garden is walking through accumulated lives.*

**Language** · Swamp Parunic, a sibilant rot-infused dialect. Beast-sign for silent field communication.
> **Solar/Radiant Vulnerability**: −30% all stats in Radiant, Fire, or Sun-aspected Domains. **Isolation Degeneracy**: after 48 hours away from swamp or kin, stacking −5% Max HP, capped at 50%.

**Settlements** · Ghurash Hollow · The Blooming Mire · Totemdeep

### Blackened Orcs

*Major Race · Cursed Remnants / Inverted Shards · Third Eon, caught in the Void rupture during the Fall of the Obsidian Gate*

Less a separate people than **an Orc lineage whose Stone-Blood has been turned against itself.** When the Obsidian Gate collapsed, the Void's fallout left the warbands inside its radius alive. **It converted them.** Their asteroidal origin became an entropic curse. Where Ironblood kinetic energy builds and stores, Blackened energy decays. Where Verdant Orcs cycle and renew, **Blackened Orcs erode.**
> They are exiled by every other Orc lineage. Elves and Marrowkin hunt them as **Metaphysical Viruses.** Their presence warps ambient Aether over extended periods, causing Wellspring sites to destabilize.
>
> They cannot be healed by Holy magic or resurrected. **If they die, they are simply gone, their record in the world permanently erased.**
>
> They know this. *Most of them have made a kind of peace with it that other races find deeply unsettling, and a few of them have made jokes about it, which is worse.*

**Key limitation · Divine Rejection**: −50% all stats in Aurevane or Harmonic Domains. All Divine Wellsprings inaccessible. True-Death on death.

**Settlements** · Korrath's Breach · The Hollow Scour · Ruinspire Cradle

### Crimson Order · Hobgoblins

*Major Race · Primordial Command-Kin / Core-Stone · Direct descendants of the impact core*

**Metaphysical Anchor** · Essence, Structured and Martial: **souls that function as Resonance Lattices, set together like the stones of an arch, where other peoples' souls stand alone**

Call them shards to their faces at your own expense. **They are the part that absorbed the full pressure of impact and chose cohesion over dispersal.** A single Crimson Order soldier is a sturdy anchor. A formation of them creates a **Phalanx of the Soul**, a Resonance Lattice nearly impenetrable to chaotic Aether.

6'8" to 7'6", rigid upright posture, symmetrical athletic skeleton reinforced by the same asteroidal deposits found in Orcs **though arranged with far greater geometric precision.** Skin deep uniform red or burned ochre, bearing **Legion Etchings**, perfectly straight bioluminescent lines in steady gold or bronze recording rank, unit, and earned designation. Eyes polished brass or iron with mechanical intensity. **In formation, their pupils go rectangular:** a Command Glyph configuration overlaying tactical data across their field of vision.
> **The Vow of Silence** · A rite of passage from Stage I through Stage VI designed to prevent [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Trait System Law, Function and the Forge|Trait]] corruption through emotional interference. **For the Crimson Order the Vow is load-bearing.** Breaking it triggers immediate Aetheric collapse.
>
> **The Engraving** · During stage transitions, new oaths are literally carved into the Soul Crystal, making their laws a physical part of their being. **A Hobgoblin who swears an oath has made an anatomical fact, and a promise is the least of it.**

**Language** · High Concord for formal and diplomatic contexts. **Silent Sign-Glyphs** for the field: micro-gestures and shared Aetheric pulses. In deep cold or on broken ground a formation falls back to a horn count.

**Key limitations** · **The Brittle Vow**: breaking any Engraved Oath triggers immediate Dissonance and −50% Max HP for 24 hours. **Emotional Null**: cannot wield Emotional, Dream-aspected, or Chaotic glyphs.

**Settlements** · Fortress-Monastery of Haldrek · The Quiet Spire · Echohold of Stoneblood Vale

### Tinker Goblins

*Goblinoid · Chaotic Innovators · Third Eon splinters from collapsed Hobgoblin workshops*

**Metaphysical Anchor** · Essence, Kinetic and Unstable: **souls too high-pressure for their small frames**

They emerged when Hobgoblin workshop factions fragmented and their Essence practices **devolved from discipline into compulsion.** Small, 3'2" to 4'0", asymmetrical in build, often with one limb modified or reinforced through self-applied **Spliced Runes.** Multifaceted eyes in amber, electric blue, or quicksilver, capable of perceiving Aetheric frequencies. *They smell of burnt vitriol, soldering copper, and scorched paper.* Their work carries their mark and their name, shouted, and no two of their contraptions misfire the same way, a thing they will claim as a feature while the bucket chain forms.
> They are brilliant in the way unstable systems are brilliant: **they solve problems that structured minds won't touch because the solution requires not caring about what else might break.**
>
> Their Emotional Detonation curse is more than a weakness. **More than one Tinker Goblin has won a fight by simply being in proximity to their own failure state.**

**Key limitations** · **Physical Fragility**: −40% HP pool; most do not live past 40 due to Essence-rot. **Emotional Detonation**: 20% chance of the Essence pool detonating on a failed Mental Save.

**Settlements** · Bangkrag Wastes · Glyphfire Alley · The Copper Clatterhold

### Fleshshaper Goblins

*Goblinoid · Bioglyphic Architects · Silent Epoch glyph-plague survivors*

The most alien of the goblinoid peoples to outside observers: 3'6" to 4'8", highly irregular and often asymmetrical bodies, **the result of constant self-modification.** Skin is inked parchment over **Skin-Records**, raised, scarred ridges of memory-glyphs that pulse blood-red or sickly yellow when their Traits are active. Hair is frequently replaced by sensory filaments or quill-like growths tuned to ambient Essence shifts.
> Their souls are **folded into their organs**, contained in a messy pulsing network of veins and glyph-chains where most races' souls radiate outward, which makes their bodies themselves **a living Aetheric reservoir.**
>
> They trace their spiritual origin to **Zorr'vak the Carver**, the ancient glyph-anatomist who discovered that Traits could be bound into living tissue through **Recursive Engraving**, making the soul's power a physical, anatomical reality.

## Languages

The goblinoid languages make a poor family in the linguistic sense. They did not branch from a single spoken root over centuries of geographic separation. **They branched from a single metaphysical event.** Every goblinoid tongue carries the imprint of the First Impact in its phonology: guttural sub-bass registers, words built from weight-heavy consonants, **a tendency toward compound words that describe a thing by what force made it rather than what it resembles.**

### Old Vaross

Ancestral tongue. Guttural, tectonic, built on sub-bass consonant stacks. Words feel like stone sliding against stone. **The language does not have a neutral register, and every sentence is freighted.** Ask an Old Vaross speaker to pass the salt and you will come away feeling you have been entrusted with something.

**Call Old Vaross dormant, because it has never died.** Most goblinoids speak it only in ritual contexts, but the vocabulary persists because it describes things newer tongues cannot capture without losing resolution.

Dominant consonants are `K`, `G`, `R`, and `V`, often stacked without intervening vowels in root words. Verbs are built by adding directional suffixes. **Past and future are not grammatically distinct**, since Old Vaross treats time as orientation rather than tense. *It was* and *it will be* use the same root; context indicates direction.

### Low Goblintongue

The common trade and community language, and **the working language of Za'tarch.** Evolved from Old Vaross under pressure of cross-species contact: softer consonant clusters, more flexible syntax, still carrying the weight-bias of its parent tongue.

| Word | Translation | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **uk-sharrath** | Those who share the same wound | Used as *brethren*, but carries the specific implication of a shared **wound** rather than a shared lineage. The key community word in Za'tarch |
| **ak-vorruum** | Force of a survived thing | The power that accumulates in something that has endured what should have ended it. **Cannot be directly translated** |
| **kha-urrum** | The core that did not break | World Spirit title and concept. Used both as a proper name and as a descriptor |
| **vorruk** | The shard | Neutral: fragment of the asteroid. **Loaded: someone who has broken away from their people.** Context determines which |
| **greth-sha** | Stone-work / Soul-work | The practice of touching the Aether at its source and shaping it with precision. Gorrum's term for the true goblinoid inheritance |
| **uk-thal** | The burning count | Iron rings added to a topknot after significant acts |
| **sharra-ket** | To justify the enemy | **To act with enough anger or violence that the people who fear you can sleep well afterward.** Gorrum's term for what he spent eleven years doing |
| **mur-greth** | The teaching-sessions | Formal soul-pattern work and glyph-reading instruction. The morning practice of Za'tarch |
| **keth-ur** | The open palm | Hands opened, palms forward, indicating **a person has already laid down the more important weapon** |
| **va-shok** | The proof / living proof | Za'tarch's self-designation. **Say refuge there and you will be corrected: proof** |

### Gravetongue

Ritual war-chant language. **Exclusively oral. Never written.** Structured around a three-beat rhythm mapping onto the three impact phases of the First Asteroid: approach, contact, stillness. Spoken correctly, **it vibrates the iron in nearby blood.**
> Nobody uses Gravetongue to tell anyone anything. **It is used for transformation.** A warrior chanting Gravetongue is addressing the event that made them, and any other warriors present are merely standing in the way.
>
> The meaning lives in **the physical vibration of a body that knows what it is made from, producing sound that reminds everything nearby**, and the words are almost beside the point.
>
> The Crimson Order adapted specific sequences into formation-signaling, stripping the chant element and retaining only the rhythmic structure as Aetheric pulse-code. *Hobgoblin military scholars consider this a practical adaptation. Ironblood traditionalists consider it a desecration.*

### Swamp Parunic and Beast-Sign

**Swamp Parunic** · Sibilant, rot-infused, slower than Low Goblintongue, long vowels held on breath. Words for decay, renewal, transformation, and cycles are the most lexically rich in the language, with **more words for different kinds of rot than any other tongue has for weather.** Certain phrases, spoken in proximity to high Aetheric saturation, can catalyze Spoor-Meld states in Verdant Orcs who are not attempting one, so mind what you recite at a Verdant wedding.

**Beast-Sign** · Verdant Orc tactical gesture-language. Roughly **400 distinct signals** covering directional instruction, threat assessment, terrain description, and emotional-state transmission. Rarely taught outside Verdant communities, for lack of secrecy and for lack of hands: most non-Verdant hands lack the sensory filament feedback that makes its nuanced signals distinguishable. *Crimson Order operatives trained in its basics describe it as the most information-dense communication system they have ever studied.*

## Shared Practices

Across lineage boundaries, several practices are held in common, in most cases without any deliberate cultural exchange, because they **emerge naturally from a shared origin. They are what Stone-Blood does when it is given time and some degree of peace.**

**The Blood-Rite of Lineage** · Communal and ceremonial in Ironblood practice; often solitary in Verdant practice, performed at the edge of water during lunar peaks. In Za'tarch's mixed practice it has evolved into a mutual rite where participants from different lineages perform it simultaneously, **acknowledging the shared origin beneath their differences.**

**Skin-Marking** · The most ancient of the shared practices. Archaeoaetheric researchers (scholars who read old sites by what the Aether remembers) have found Skin-Marking patterns in stone-impressed records **predating the Second Eon's formal history**, suggesting the practice emerged directly from the first post-impact generations, before any goblinoid language was formalized.

**Ancestor-Breath Exercises** · Structured breath-work combined with Soul Crystal awareness, maintaining the connection to Kha-Urrum's original impact-frequency. In advanced practitioners, sustained Ancestor-Breath produces **a faint sub-bass resonance audible to other goblinoids nearby**, a confirmation signal that the connection is intact.
> **The Open Palm** · *Keth-ur.* In every goblinoid tradition, presenting open palms in a confrontational context carries the same meaning: **I have already laid down the more important weapon.**
>
> Foreigners read it as *I will not fight*, and foreigners have been badly surprised. It means **the fight being proposed is beneath the actual stakes.**

**Soul-Work** · *Greth-sha.* The true inheritance. The practice of touching the Aether at its source and shaping what is found there **with precision rather than force.** The Ironblood who thinks this is a Hobgoblin thing and the Hobgoblin who thinks it is too chaotic for their lineage are both wrong.
> **Soul-work is the goblinoid inheritance. War is what was done to them.**

The aura coat (the visible coat of a fighter's own color) has its own Low Goblintongue name, *ak-gresh*, the survived skin, and the Ironblood and the Crimson Order have been arguing for several Eons over who wears it correctly.

## Za'tarch: The Living Proof

Za'tarch sits in the **Ashenmoor**, a stretch of cracked-riverbed territory and low pine forest that no other people wanted badly enough to take. **The poor land was the plan.** The founding generation chose land that was difficult precisely because land that was easy would have been contested. They accepted the Ashenmoor's winters and its wet springs and its difficulty growing grain in exchange for **the simple fact that nobody came for it.**

The settlement is arranged in rough concentric rings around a central fire-pit built from smooth river stone. Three generations of hands carried those stones in, goblin, orc, hobgoblin, each placed with the kind of intention that other peoples reserve for temples. **The pit is Za'tarch's lungs.** Someone tosses pine resin and crushed herbs on the coals each evening as offering. *It doesn't smell holy. It smells like home.*

**Population** · Roughly 200 permanent residents, 30–40 transient. Primarily Ironblood Orcs, Crimson Order Hobgoblins, and Tinker Goblins.

**Governance** · Elder council with rotating voice-holders from each lineage. Gorrum Ashentusk holds structural authority **but refuses formal title.**
> The residents will tell you Za'tarch is **a proof and you may keep your word refuge.** The distinction matters to everyone who lives there and to no one outside it, which is part of the point.
>
> Every child born here who learns to read in three tongues is a proof. Every hobgoblin who trains beside a goblin until their formations move as one is a proof. Every elder who does not die of cold because his neighbors banked his fire through the winter is a proof.
>
> The settlement exists to demonstrate what it costs the world to have been wrong about goblinoid peoples, **and the price of that demonstration is that Za'tarch cannot become what the world named them.**

### Internal tensions

The tension that structures Za'tarch's politics runs **between generations, and hardly at all between lineages.** It is the tension between those who have lived long enough to understand what anger costs, and those who are currently paying that cost and have not yet seen the receipts come in.

**Gorrum** represents one side: eleven years of anger, four men killed, and the settlements that burned his people kept growing anyway. He arrived at soul-work through failure and owes nothing to theory, **which is why his doctrine sounds like testimony when you expected philosophy.**

**Vreth** represents the other: twenty-two years old, Ironblood to his marrow, bearing five iron rings for five confrontations he didn't start and did finish, watching land get cleared and warrens get burned while elders talk about proof. **He has a case.** The children at Kellrath's Crossing did not have time to push any limits. Vreth carries this the way you carry something you cannot put down **because putting it down would mean it didn't matter.**

### Key residents

**Gorrum Ashentusk · Elder, Ironblood, structural authority**

Six-foot-seven. The evidence of enormous former size is still present in his skeleton: vast shelf of shoulders, neck like quarried stone, hands that could palm a skull. **Age has not made him small; it has compressed him the way pressure compresses coal.** Skin the gray-green of old slate, mapped with Asteroidal Scars running jaw to collarbone to the backs of both forearms. Molten-iron eyes, heat-glow dim now but steady.

He wears nothing ceremonial: roughspun trousers, sleeveless cured-hide vest, two iron rings braided into a single rope of coarse black hair down his spine. **One ring was his father's. He does not say which.**

Gorrum's authority owes nothing to position. **It is gravitational.** He carries eleven years of the thing Vreth is currently carrying, and came out the other side with a precision about waste that reads as calm and is something else. He uses *uk-sharrath* deliberately, every time. He lets fires breathe between his words. His hands open, palms forward, when he is done making the most important point in a conversation. *He learned this from his mother.*

**Vreth · Young warrior, twenty-two, Ironblood**

Six-foot-four and still filling out, built in the thick angular geometry of Ironblood war-heritage. Russet-brown skin with asteroidal scars still livid and new across his jawline, **which haven't aged yet into the settled ridges of an older warrior.** Coal-orange eyes burning bright. Topknot with five iron rings, each added after a border skirmish as a count. Hands wrapped in tanned hide at the knuckles.

Vreth is twenty-two and approximately as subtle as a thrown boulder. **He would take that as a compliment and he would be right.** The boulder comparison is accurate: he is kinetic, forward-moving, difficult to redirect once in motion. He is also no fool: he can be reached, he can be made to listen, and **when his hands open in Gorrum's fire-circle it is a form of respect even if he will not name it that yet.**

His deepest wound is Kellrath's Crossing, which he carries without speaking of it. The iron rings are a count of confrontations. **He will not stop adding them until the confrontations stop happening to his people, which will be either never or not soon.**

**Nix Clatterfoot · Tinker Goblin, engineer, third from the left, always**

Barely three-foot-six, built like a collection of useful parts held together by optimism and baling twine. Jaundice-yellow skin marked with a constellation of Spliced Rune brands running from left wrist to elbow, each connecting to the pack of volatile components strapped to her back. Hair singed at the temples, standing in frizzy tufts. Amber-faceted eyes that catch firelight and scatter it in eight directions at once. *She smells of burnt vitriol and soldering copper.*

Nix feels things **through her Spliced Runes first**, describing certain emotional or Aetheric events as "chords struck on strings she hadn't known were tuned." **She means it exactly.** Her rune-brands are tuned to her Aetheric sensitivity and respond to ambient soul-state shifts in those nearby.

She says she is a tinker who happens to live in a place with interesting people. *Za'tarch would suggest she is a person who happens to work with her hands.*

**Drakar-Sei · Crimson Order, former soldier, Keeper of the Vow of Stillness**

Six-foot-eight with the rigid upright posture of his lineage. Burned-ochre skin ruled through with bioluminescent gold Legion Etchings along the jaw and the backs of both hands. Polished brass eyes that catch fire-warmth and hold it steady. **He stands six-foot-eight and makes it look economical.**

He has not spoken at a Za'tarch gathering in six months. The Vow of Stillness requires this.

Drakar-Sei is **the proof of concept within the proof of concept**: a Crimson Order soldier, from the lineage that most completely embodies the core-doctrine of Kha-Urrum, choosing to live in a mixed settlement and honor a Vow among people who did not ask it of him.

When he broke the Vow to speak during Gorrum's confrontation with Vreth, the words he chose were: **"Both can be true. The error is choosing between them as though they are opposed."** Then he stepped to the fire-pit, pressed both palms to the foundation stones, and held the fire perfectly still. *He had nothing to prove by it. He was showing what he meant.*

## What the Blood Leans On

*An untrained lean is the tilt a lineage gives the three layers of a soul before anyone has trained it, and it never sets Mastered or Walked ([[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Three Paths Routing, Recognition and the Gate|The Three Paths]]). The old muster-rolls carried bonuses and penalties here in a notation nobody now reads, and the sense of them is in the last column.*

| Lineage | Untrained lean | Wellsprings the blood finds easy | What the blood gives and takes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ironblood Orcs** | Body, nearly all of it | Pyraeon, Monolithion | Great Ardency and Vitality, and a long road to any Gnosis |
| **Verdant Orcs** | Spirit, strongly | Manganthra, Verdantia, Luminalis | Vitality and a deep reach on the soul, at some cost in strength and schooling |
| **Blackened Orcs** | Nothing to speak of. The Stone-Blood was turned against itself, and what lean they have points wherever they were hurt | Nihiloth, and Benediction and Absolution will not have them | Much in the way of ruin and little Vitality, with the Core worn thin |
| **Crimson Order** | Spirit, with Body behind it | Fixatio, Anamnesis | Gnosis and Vitality, slow on the feet and slower to charm |
| **Tinker Goblins** | Body, through the hands | Fractura, Transmutatio | Gnosis and Dexterity to spare, and little to die with |

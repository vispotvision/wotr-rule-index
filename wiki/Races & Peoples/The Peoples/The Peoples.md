*The elder peoples and the newer ones: their origins, their soul architecture and their ways. All of them walk the one great body of land, so sooner or later all of them are at the same inn, and the innkeeper at the Copper Goose in Tarnbridge charges a Marrowkin for the ale, a Drow for the silence and a Lupine for the door.*

*Where a lineage below gives an **Untrained lean**, the figure says how its blood tilts the three layers of a soul before anyone has trained them. It never sets Mastered or Walked, which are a person's own practice ([[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Three Paths Routing, Recognition and the Gate|The Three Paths]]).*

*The constructed languages of the Realms, each with its own grammar.*

- [[Races & Peoples/The Tongues of the Realms/The Grammars/Common — The Tongue With No Distinctions/Common — The Tongue With No Distinctions|Common — The Tongue With No Distinctions]]: The tongue the story is told in. The Tongues of the Realms governs, with one exception noted below.
- [[Races & Peoples/The Tongues of the Realms/The Grammars/Accord Latin — The Formulary and the Two Spoken Registers|Accord Latin — The Formulary and the Two Spoken Registers]]: A formula set and a register guide. The Tongues of the Realms governs.
- [[Races & Peoples/The Tongues of the Realms/The Grammars/Eressean and Varrisak — The Tongue of the Vey-Elarin|Eressean and Varrisak — The Tongue of the Vey-Elarin]]: A grammar of the elven tongue and its schism. The Tongues of the Realms governs.
- [[Races & Peoples/The Tongues of the Realms/The Grammars/The Tally-Tongue of the Marrowkin|The Tally-Tongue of the Marrowkin]]: A grammar of the tongue of the holds. The Tongues of the Realms governs.
- [[Races & Peoples/The Tongues of the Realms/The Grammars/Old Vaross — The Goblinoid Tongue|Old Vaross — The Goblinoid Tongue]]: A grammar of the goblinoid tongue. The Tongues of the Realms governs.

# Anointing

## Anointing

### Summary card

**Effect** · A rite that confers recognition, not rule, on a person: the subject's Crystal begins answering to the mark, which can awaken a latent Wellspring alignment and unseal glyphs dormant since birth.
**Cost** · 10% of full reserve once per rite, plus the officiant's own standing, vouched for as long as the mark stands.
**Limit** · It cannot confer legitimacy by ceremony alone, grant dominion or rule, or be undone casually; a mark laid in error misdirects the Crystal for the rest of its path.
**Counter** · A Judicium practitioner or a Measurewright at the rite, a stronger claim, or a refusal to honour it; a forced or tricked Anointing comes off only by Scission.
**What nobody knows** · Who, if anyone, chose a subject whose true Anointing was performed by an officiant who believed the rite was empty.

### Origin

**Origin** · Kept by the Order of Benediction, which licenses the rite and treats that licence as a decision rather than a finding. The False Anointing Rebellion, raised by a corrupt bishop's empty marks, is the tradition's standing warning against forged performance.
**Practitioners** · The Order of Benediction; Aethelsige Moto and Seraphiel Moto; the Oracles of Vellsorea; Caladriel of Highspire.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · A recognition tag, like a phosphate group placed on a protein: the mark does no work itself, it changes what the thing is taken to be, and everything that reads it behaves differently.
**Law** · Mass action at a binding site, K*d = k*off / k*on: how long a bond holds is its residence time, 1 / k*off, and a mark's effects are the behaviour of whatever reads it.
**Limit** · Even the strongest affinity bond holds about a day; a mark that lasts a career must be cured, not merely bound, and a mark nobody reads does nothing.
**Forgery** · A tag at the wrong site is chemically identical and recognised by nothing; a rival of higher affinity displaces a real one silently, and the loss shows only when the bond is called on.
**Energy** · The tag costs the energy to place it and nothing after; everything downstream is paid by the readers.
| Quantity | Working | Result |
|---|---|---|
| A good antibody on its antigen | K_d | about 10⁻⁹ M |
| Strongest non-covalent pair (biotin and streptavidin) | K*d; k*off about 10⁻⁵ s⁻¹ | about 10⁻¹⁴ M |
| Its residence time | 1 / k_off | about 10⁵ s, 28 hours |

### Metaphysics

**Aether** · One ceremonial expenditure; the Residue is the mark itself, a standing Traitus mark on a person that anyone with enough Aetheric perception can see. Saturation plays no part.
**Wellspring** · Benediction, the Wellspring of Grace (Vitalia): a bond formed under it carries an affinity figure the Continuum reads; it strengthens Harmonics Empathy, Harmonics Axis and Resilience Oath.
**Wellspring** · Fixatio, the Binding Flame (Materia): it holds by making the other configuration unreachable, so the mark lasts a career and is not undone casually.
**Wellspring** · Judicium, the Wellspring of Truth (Fulguria): no substance can present another's spectrum; it is the officiant's discernment and the inspector's assay alike.
**Inherited failures** · Benediction's competitive inhibition lets a stronger claim displace the mark; Fixatio's irreversibility makes a mark laid in error permanent; Judicium's spectral overload blinds the reader.
**Consent** · A genuine Anointing is a relationship: a subject chosen by a Wellspring current, an Archon or the order's standing authority. The Order of Benediction licenses it anyway, and that licence is a decision, not a finding.
**Essence** · The subject's Attraction Layer holds the recognition, their Essence Core holds the Traitus mark, and the officiant stakes their own Core. Developmental Tier is Harmonic at Glory.
**School** · The form school holds a correct rite always valid, so it never tests and is the perfect victim of a forgery; the worth school rejects marks by who laid them. Neither runs the assay, which Judicium settles.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Lo, Wy and Ka, written in the officiant's words and laid with hand and oil.
**Boundary** · What the subject's Crystal counts as its own: a recognition site is set from outside, and nothing is added to capacity.
**Effect** · Benediction makes the recognition measurable, Fixatio cures it, and the Crystal answers to it: sealed glyphs get their authorisation, and a latent alignment gated on that recognition becomes reachable. The subject feels permission, not power.
**Floor** · Glory, because the officiant vouches for another's purpose with their own Crystal; below it there is no stake to lend, and the stake is what makes a false Anointing detectable at all.
**Anchor** · Glory stands below Refraction, so the rite needs words, hands and oil.
**Cannot** · Confer legitimacy by ceremony alone, grant dominion or rule (that is Coronation), or be undone casually.
**Removal** · A forced or tricked Anointing can be removed only by Scission, and carries Scission's complication rate.
**Failure** · The empty rite: correct words with no current behind them make a visible, mechanically empty mark, found only when called on. A mark laid in error is permanent and misdirects the Crystal for the rest of the path.
**Bleed** · 30 to 40 percent of the officiant's spend leaves the Shell as heat, with sound and structural bleed; the costlier loss, standing, is not measured in η.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · The mark is visible but proves nothing, since a false one is equally visible; the record does: officiant, order, date and claim can all be checked. After a displacement, an alignment falls silent the first time it is called.
**Limits** · It confers recognition, not power or rule; its effect is only what readers grant it; it cannot survive an assay if empty.
**Fails against** · A Judicium practitioner or a Measurewright at the rite; a higher-affinity claim; a reader who refuses to honour it; a lying officiant's own drift in Coherence; Scission, for a forced one.
**Look up** · The Benediction, Fixatio and Judicium entries and their failures; the Attraction Path gates for Oath, Axis and Empathy; Scission's complication rate; Coronation for rule; the Glory Stage table.

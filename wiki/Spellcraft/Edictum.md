# Edictum

## Edictum

### Summary card

**Effect** · A spoken declaration that binds a named target, soul, artefact, or space under a stated condition or judgment; once spoken it holds unless the target outranks the caster's own authority or is shielded by contradiction glyphs or oath-nullification.
**Cost** · Full clarity of soul at the moment of speaking — the working reads the caster's own conviction as its authorization, and any impurity in that conviction is charged back to the caster rather than absorbed by the target.
**Limit** · It targets. It does not reshape reality in general the way a Decretum-class working would, and it cannot bind a target the caster has no lawful standing over.
**Counter** · A target genuinely greater than the caster's source of authority is simply not bound; short of that, a hard oath-nullification or a contradiction glyph placed before the Edict is spoken can pre-empt the seal entirely.
> **What nobody knows.** Why an Edictum spoken in perfect legal form still fails against some targets with no documented authority advantage at all — the Bench's own registers call this "an unresolved authority gap" and decline to elaborate further.

### Design Chain

**Trigger** · The caster speaks a condition, command, or judgment against a named target, soul, artefact, or space, backed by a recognized source of binding authority (Realmic, guild, or Wellspring).
**Function** · Fixes a boundary condition at the target rather than delivering force to it — the target's own behaviour is required to conform at that boundary from the moment the Edict lands.
**Mechanism** · Boundary-condition enforcement, the same real mechanism every Parun glyph runs on at smaller scale: a glyph moves a limit and the underlying law does what it has always done on the far side of the new limit. Edictum is that principle scaled up to a full spoken sentence rather than a single glyph, with Judicium supplying the discernment that decides exactly where the new limit sits.
**Numerical Effect** · At Stage VII / A-Grade, binding a single named target to a stated condition costs 50,000–70,000 EU and takes effect the instant the sentence completes; binding a space or a domain-scale target runs toward 90,000 EU and holds for as long as the caster's authority over that ground remains uncontested.
**Target Response** · The target experiences the Edict as an external limit rather than a compulsion from inside — Concord casework describes it as "being told a wall is there" rather than being pushed against one.
**Consequence** · The named condition holds. A sentenced target cannot perform the forbidden act; a protected target cannot be touched by the named harm; a silenced target cannot cast the named Craft — until the Edict is lifted by the same or greater authority that set it.
**Limitation** · The working cannot originate an obligation the way Oathrend's field does — it enforces an existing authority relationship rather than inventing one, and against a target with no lawful subordination to the caster it does nothing.
**Weakness** · Full clarity of soul is a real precondition, not flavour text — an Edictum spoken in anger, doubt, or bad faith backfires onto the caster's own Wellspring rather than landing on the target, producing what casework calls an Echo Reversal.
**Cost** · EU per the Numerical Effect line, plus the standing risk of Echo Reversal if the caster's conviction was not genuinely clean.
**Counterplay** · Establish standing greater than the caster's own source of authority before the Edict is spoken, or have contradiction glyphs or an oath-nullification already in place — nothing stops a landed Edict except a rank the caster cannot claim.

### Origin

Urion originates the form at the cosmological scale; three mortal derivations of the same working are known (Valen Dros, the Daughters of Concord, Lirae Ilmashai, and the historical Edictum of Silence attributed to Seraphiel Moto). As a documented, taught working with an Archonic origin, it is counterable by anyone who has studied a prior case and by nothing else short of superior standing.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · A boundary condition, like the Meissner effect: a superconductor does not push a magnetic field back, it excludes it, because zero field inside is a condition its state imposes. Nothing pushes; the target's possibilities simply end at a wall.
**Law** · Uniqueness of solutions to a boundary-value problem: given the equation and the boundary, the interior has one answer, and it is adopted everywhere at once.
**Limit** · The critical field: above it exclusion does not weaken, it stops, and the threshold belongs to the wall, not to what it excludes.
**Hold** · Once set, a Meissner state costs nothing per second: its screening currents flow without loss, because they work against nothing.

### Metaphysics

**Aether** · One sharp expenditure as the sentence closes, then nothing; the hold is a state, so an Edict does not lapse with attention. The Residue is heavy and legible: the bound target carries the condition, which is why it can be lifted.
**Wellspring** · Judicium, the Wellspring of Truth (Fulguria): no substance can present another's spectrum; it locates where the new limit sits, and the wall itself is the world's.
**Inherited failures** · Judicium's spectral overload loses the priority an Edict needs to name one condition; Fulguria's terrain (boiled leather, fired clay, dense fog, a shielded interior) spoils the reading, not the hold.
**Category** · A licensing working, the Consent Doctrine's own shape: a process can be licensed where a relationship cannot.
**Essence** · The Attraction Layer, where standing is held, is the active layer; the Essence Core supplies the conviction read as authorisation; the Shell does almost nothing. Developmental Tier is Resonant at Refraction.
**School** · The Bench holds that authority, not truth, makes law, so a failed Edict means defective authority and it escalates; the instrument-makers hold that finely structured targets thread an Edict like flux vortices, so escalation admits more threads.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Boundary laid under Command: both name a kind of limit on any law, since the law the target runs on is unknown until named.
**Boundary** · The value the target's own behaviour may take at a named limit: a sentenced target cannot do the forbidden act, a protected one cannot be touched by the named harm, a silenced one cannot use the named Craft.
**Effect** · Instant as the sentence ends, with no travel time; the target still wants the thing and finds the doing unavailable, an absence, not a resistance.
**Hold** · Durable: a standing condition holds without upkeep, sustained by a fact about standing, and ends when that fact does or a same or greater authority lifts it.
**Cannot** · Reshape reality in general, bind a target over whom the speaker has no lawful standing, or originate an obligation rather than enforce one.
**Anchor** · Kept by design at Refraction: an Edict is always spoken aloud so it can be heard, appealed, countersigned or lifted.
**Failure** · Echo Reversal: an Edict spoken in anger, doubt or bad faith sets the wrong value at the speaker's own boundary and backfires on their Wellspring. A mis-located edge is a Boundary fault; an overstated warrant seals and bills the speaker the difference.
**Bleed** · 30 to 40 percent of the casting spend leaves the Shell as heat, sound and structural bleed, all in the instant of speaking, which is why Edicts are pronounced in halls built for them.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · A name and a condition said aloud before witnesses, with all the bleed in that one turn; the target hears it whole and can act during it. After, the bound target carries a legible condition, or visibly does not.
**Limits** · Only targets the speaker lawfully outranks; one condition at one boundary; public and attributable; a landed Edict cannot be stopped, only lifted.
**Beats it** · A hand over the speaker's mouth; a Lock placed beforehand; outrank the speaker; force the seal early to bill them; shield or fog the target to mis-locate the edge; wait for a judge who has stopped believing their bench.
**Look up** · The Judicium entry and Fulguria's terrain; the Lock glyph in the Counterplay register; the Fate and Attraction Path gates for Sovereignty and Command; the Consent Doctrine; the Refraction Stage table.

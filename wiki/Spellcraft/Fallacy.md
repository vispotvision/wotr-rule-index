# Fallacy

## Fallacy

---

### What it is

Fallacy does not lie to a person. It lies to the Continuum, and for as long as the lie holds, the Continuum believes it the way it believes anything else that has been correctly filed.
The working states an axiom that is false and states it in the same grammar a true one would use, so that whatever reads the surrounding law, a Wellspring path, a Soul Crystal's own sense of its alignment, a running Draft, has no way to tell the false clause from a real one until the structure built on top of it gives way. This is why it reads to witnesses as revelation rather than as trickery: for the working's duration, the false thing genuinely is true, in the narrow, load-bearing sense that everything downstream of it behaves as though it were.
The collapse is not a side effect. It is the point. A Fallacy that never shatters has not finished its work; the brief span in which the lie holds is the span in which everything that depended on the true version can be made to depend on the false one instead, and the shattering is what strands them there.

### Where it sits in the current system

**Category** · Mechanica primary (coerced transformation without lawful Wellspring participation, the taxonomy's own definition of the category the rest of the system exists to oppose), Phenomena secondary for the self-sustaining collapse once the working is triggered and has outrun its own Attraction Layer.
**Wellsprings** · Nihiloth, Dissolution, and Mirithane.
**Family** · Limina throughout, the Family whose truest signature is what fails to be there, which is exactly what a false axiom is.
**Craft** · Spellcraft. The effect is spoken or otherwise sustained by active attention and collapses once that attention lapses or the working completes, matching the current Craft durability rule rather than a fixed inscription.
**Where it is filed** · The Magical Categories page (Mechanica, entry twelve; Phenomena, entry seven). No standalone Fallacy entry exists yet in the Restricted or Forbidden material; this card is the seed of one, and the "banned in most formal Ritus Orders" claim is consistent with Mechanica's own Statute XXVI, which exists because the category leaves scars the Continuum does not forget.

### Practitioners and history

**Razakiel** is attested as a figure who imposed the Unlawful Acts of Order on at least one occasion, confronted and annulled not by force but by Dazkar's stillness, the mirror of consequence forcing the decree's own contradictions to erase it. That is a documented instance of an unlawful, false-premised order being written into law and then failing under its own weight, which is close kin to what the card describes, though the card's specific claim, that Razakiel used Fallacy to convince a domain its own god had never existed, is not itself attested and is kept as originated.
**Serenhal Caelmorne** has no wiki entry. The surname Caelmorne belongs elsewhere in the corpus to a different, unrelated moon-aligned Anima Harmonist (Caelmorne Halvrein, of the Harmonic Arts), which makes the name plausible as a house name without making this particular bearer of it real. Kept as originated and unattested, per the same rule applied to every other unattested figure in this batch: the card's account of Fallacy's practitioner does not resolve without her, so she is carried rather than dropped, and flagged here rather than presented as attested.

### What it can and cannot do

Fallacy can make a false claim briefly load-bearing: a spiritual system will act on it, build on it, and suffer the consequences of it exactly as though it were true, for as long as the working holds. It is genuinely dangerous to anything with a Wellspring path running near the false clause, since paths deviate to accommodate a law that, for the moment, is real.
It cannot make the lie permanent. The shattering is built in, not an external correction, and everything the working achieves, it achieves inside that window and loses the instant the window closes, though the damage done while it was open does not repair itself along with the lie. It also cannot be aimed with total precision: a false axiom injected into a shared system corrupts what is near it indiscriminately, which is the mechanism behind the card's claim that it can corrupt Animatria structures layered over time, and why formal orders that otherwise tolerate a great deal of dangerous magic still ban this one specifically.

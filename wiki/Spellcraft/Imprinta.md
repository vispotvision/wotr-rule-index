# Imprinta

## Imprinta

### Summary card

**Effect** · Projects a fragment of soul, memory, Trait, or Wellspring resonance into an object, place, or person, leaving a permanent, semi-independent echo that can awaken, interact, or influence its surroundings long after the caster has gone.
**Cost** · A piece of the caster's own Essence Core, paid and detached at the moment of casting — not drawn from reserve on an ongoing basis, but spent outright and permanently, the way a limb is not "on loan."
**Limit** · The deposit responds most strongly to memory, desire, and unresolved fate; an imprint left with no emotional charge behind it tends to sit inert rather than awaken.
**Counter** · Cut the fragment's live line back to what it was made from — a broken focus, a killed original, or an active Silence field starves an Imprinta the same way it starves an Animatria construct, since both are detached pieces of a caster's own Core running on a tether rather than a fresh soul.
> **What nobody knows.** Why some Imprinta echoes wake on the first person who touches them and others wait generations for a specific one — the Anamnesis orders that study this call it "the deposit's own patience" and have never isolated what the deposit is actually waiting for.

### Design Chain

**Trigger** · The caster commits a genuine piece of memory, Trait, or Wellspring resonance to a specific object, place, or person, usually at a moment of high emotional charge.
**Function** · Detaches a small fragment of the caster's own Essence Core and deposits it into the target medium, where it continues operating with limited independence rather than dissipating.
**Mechanism** · Remanence, the real phenomenon behind hysteresis — a substrate exposed to an imposed field retains a trace of that field's alignment after the field itself is withdrawn, and the retained trace can later influence how the substrate responds to something new. Anamnesis is the Wellspring version of that retained trace: the medium keeps the shape of what was pressed into it.
**Numerical Effect** · At Stage IV / B-Grade, a personal-scale imprint (a relic, a single room, one person) costs an estimated 8,000–15,000 EU and is permanent from the moment of casting; an echo-guardian or a soul-laced sanctuary scales well past this floor, into the range documented for Stage VIII+ Archon-originated Imprinta work, and is not estimated further here since the card gives no basis for a second number at that scale.
**Target Response** · The medium carries the fragment inertly until a triggering condition (proximity, emotional resonance, a specific person or words) is met, at which point the deposit "wakes" and begins interacting rather than merely persisting.
**Consequence** · A permanent, semi-independent echo exists in the world independent of the caster's continued presence, will, or survival.
**Limitation** · The deposit needs emotional charge to awaken reliably; a fragment left without one tends to remain a dormant, undetected trace rather than a functioning guardian or relic.
**Weakness** · The fragment is still tethered to what it was cut from — sever that tether (kill the original, break the focus, or place it inside an active Silence field) and the echo stops being anything at all, the same failure mode Animatria suffers.
**Cost** · A permanent piece of the caster's own Essence Core, spent outright at casting, plus the EU per the Numerical Effect line.
**Counterplay** · Locate and cut the live line back to the fragment's origin before engaging the echo directly — engaging an awakened Imprinta on its own terms plays to what it was built for.

### Origin

Maelor (converted from the card's "Mecre, Archon of Memory") originates the rite at the cosmological scale, seeding echoes across Gaia. The card's three mortal derivations — Soji the Dreamwalker, Caldran of Athame, the Choir of Vellsorea — are unattested individually and kept because the card's history does not resolve without them. As a documented, widely-practiced working rather than a self-derived technique, any given Imprinta is counterable by anyone who has studied its specific tether.
**Does the physics close against the stratal account?** **Beautifully on the
cost, and not at all on the counter.**
*The cost closes, and it is the best-matched cost in the batch.* A remanent write
is paid once, at the write, and the retained state is free thereafter. The page
bills exactly that: a single EU expenditure plus a permanent Core subtraction,
explicitly *"not drawn from reserve on an ongoing basis."* Two sets of books,
same sentence, no interpretation needed.
*The counter does not close, and the failure is total.* The page's Physics Domain
is **remanence**, defined on the page itself as *"a substrate retaining an
imposed state **after the imposing field is withdrawn**."* The page's Weakness and
Counterplay both require a **live tether** back to the origin — *"sever that
tether (kill the original, break the focus, or place it inside an active Silence
field) and the echo stops being anything at all, the same failure mode Animatria
suffers."* A remanent state has no tether by definition; that is the definition.
An Animatria construct has one by definition — *"It maintains continuity through
a tether back to its source, **which is why Animatria summoning fails completely
inside Silence Realms**"* (`wiki/The Disciplines/The Spirit Summoning Arts.md`).
The page has chosen one mechanism and the other mechanism's counter, and only one
of them can be true. This is the batch's clearest `effect-mechanism` conflict and
it is logged, with four options, as `SA-EM-IMPRINTA-REMANENCE-VERSUS-TETHER`.

#### 5 · Counterplay and the challenge

**The fairness check** (`.claude/skills/wotr-write/references/fair-play.md`).
| Test | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Costs something that hurts | **pass, and it is the only permanent personal cost in the batch** | 8,000–15,000 EU is small — but *"a piece of the caster's own Essence Core, paid and detached at the moment of casting … spent outright and permanently, the way a limb is not 'on loan'"* is a subtraction from the layer that holds identity, memory and Traits, at a Stage where the Crystal is merely **Awakened**. Nothing else in these twenty pages takes something that does not come back. The one defect is that the Core cost is stated in kind and never in quantity, so it cannot be tracked across castings (`SA-GAP-IMPRINTA-CORE-FRACTION`). |
| Stated limits | **pass** | *"The deposit responds most strongly to memory, desire, and unresolved fate; an imprint left with no emotional charge behind it tends to sit inert"*; personal scale is what the number covers and larger work is explicitly not priced; and the deposit has *"limited independence"* rather than autonomy. |
| Something beats it | **pass as written, and the stated counter contradicts the stated mechanism** | The page names three (kill the original, break the focus, Silence) and all three assume a tether. If the mechanism is remanence they all fail, and the real counters are different and are in §2 and below. Either way something beats it; the question is *what*, and the page and its own Physics Domain disagree. |
| It has a tell | **fail** | A dormant Imprinta *"tends to remain a dormant, undetected trace."* It has no emission, no upkeep, no draw and no signature; it is a state of a substrate. A careful opponent cannot read it coming because there is nothing coming — the deposit was laid down years or centuries ago and does nothing until the trigger arrives, at which point it is already happening. Logged `SA-FAIR-IMPRINTA-NO-TELL`. |
| Numbers in band | **pass on Grade, fail on the ladder** | Stage IV ⇒ Max Grade B (176–275), ceiling 275, Tier 4, η ≈ 0.45 (Parts Five and Nineteen) — all consistent with the page's B-Grade. But **the only Stage-V practitioner with an attested reserve cannot afford the floor cost**, and the page's *"None documented"* on the Path gate is wrong (see §4). |

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).
- **Break the boundary — thermal resetting, which is the counter the physics
actually supplies and no page states.** Anamnesis's own Failure: *"Reading is
not always passive, and a careless practitioner erases the record in the act of
taking it."* **The instrument that reads an Imprinta can destroy it.** This is
the route that survives whichever way `SA-EM-IMPRINTA-REMANENCE-VERSUS-TETHER`
is ruled, it requires no Stage advantage, and it is findable by anyone who reads
the Limina register — which is precisely the kind of answer the challenge
doctrine wants: a counter that is in the corpus, is not on the technique page,
and rewards cross-reading.
- **Deny the field — Silence, *if* the tether reading wins.** *"Animatria
constructs fail completely because their continuity runs back to a source the
Silence has severed. **A soul held in one reports the sensation as loneliness
rather than weakness.**"* The page prescribes this and it works only under the
Animatria reading. Under the remanence reading a Silence Realm does nothing at
all to a magnetised bar. **A player who tests this learns which mechanism is
true**, which is a very good thing for a player to be able to find out by
experiment.
- **Break the boundary — the substrate, which is the Runecraft answer applied to
a Runecraft-shaped problem.** *"Wards. Runecraft standing on a substrate, and
therefore beaten the way Runecraft is beaten: **damage the substrate.** A ward
is not argued with. It is chiselled off, or the wall it sits on is brought
down."* An Imprinta is not a ward, but it is a state *of a physical thing*, and
every counter that reaches a state of a physical thing reaches it. Break the
relic. This works under **both** readings and costs nothing but a hammer.
- **Break the man — never trigger it.** The deposit's selectivity is its own
limitation: *"an imprint left with no emotional charge behind it tends to sit
inert."* An echo waiting for a specific person, specific words or a specific
feeling is an echo that a person who is not that person, does not say those
words and does not feel that feeling can walk past. **The narrowest Imprinta is
the easiest to avoid and the hardest to defuse**, which is a real trade the
caster makes at the write.
- **Break the man — the caster paid in Core.** An Imprinta specialist has been
subtracting permanently from the layer that holds their Traits. The register's
*"Coherence drift"* and *"Rigidity"* both arrive early for such a person, and
the Core Vocabulary names the symptom: *"The Flicker** · Traits dropping in
and out."* You do not have to cause this. You have to count how many echoes they
have left behind.
**The tell, stated plainly.** There is none, and the fairness check says so
rather than pretending. The countertell is archival rather than perceptual: **an
Imprinta was made by somebody, at a moment, in a place**, and the Counterplay
register's *"Read him"* applies to the maker rather than to the deposit. A
practitioner who has laid echoes has a history of moments of high emotional
charge in specific locations, and that history is a document.

# Invert Eidolon

## Invert Eidolon

### Summary card

**Effect** · Summons an Anti-Eidolon, a construct built from what the summoner's Crystal refused to integrate: a suppressed Trait, an unreconciled harmonic, a fracture from a Stage survived without closing.
**Cost** · Fabrication costs 7.5% to 15% of full reserve by complexity, then 1% to 2.5% every second it stands; dismissal may cost the raising again.
**Limit** · It is hostile unless bound, never counts its summoner an ally, and cannot be made without a real unclosed fracture to draw from.
**Counter** · Silence severs the tether completely; otherwise the end of the sustain, a strike on the one defect the summoner left unclosed, or its own turn on its maker.
**What nobody knows** · Whether an Anti-Eidolon remembers the fracture it was made from once that fracture has been spent.

### Origin

**Origin** · Outside guild sanction almost everywhere it is known; fractured spiritbinders and Temperants pursue it for a closure ordinary progression will not hand them sooner. It first surfaced in the Twilight Concord, and the Wellspring Fracture of Nalisem in the Era of Calamity, when summoners lost control of several at once, is its worst remembered disaster.
**Practitioners** · Karo Venrik, the Foolish Magus, said to have raised one by accident in his early trials; Thessan and her bound Anti-Eidolon Vel-Muriah; Silvantes Mournthresh of the Riven Choir; the Mirrorwake Ascetics; the Occultinarii of the Hollow Eye.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · Residual stress in a casting: a part cooled faster than it could relax locks in tension it was never loaded with, like a Prince Rupert's drop that shrugs off a hammer and bursts when its tail is nicked.
**Law** · Templated solidification from a defective billet, with Griffith's criterion σ_f = √(2Eγ/πa): a crack runs when released strain energy exceeds the cost of new surface, so stored tension makes a tiny flaw enough.
**Limit** · Strong in every direction but one: general load hits the compressed skin, which is what it is for, and one small specific defect brings the whole thing apart.
**Dismissal** · A stored stress does not vanish when unwatched, it goes somewhere, so putting the construct down may cost as much as raising it.
**Delay** · Stressed parts fail later from causes too small to record; the construct turns erratic, often on its summoner, without wanting anything.

### Metaphysics

**Aether** · A fabrication charge by complexity, then a sustain every second it stands. The construct is Residue, a fragment of the summoner's Core walking outside them; several at once multiply Shell strain toward a depletion cascade.
**Wellspring** · Eidolyn, the Shaping Mind (Limina): intent is the template, and matter deposits along it with high fidelity and real strength; it strengthens Ardency Density, Ardency Inscription and Gnosis Memorium.
**Wellspring** · Oblivara, the Dark Mirror (Limina): a corner reflector returns energy along its incoming path, so the construct returns what reaches it to its source and never counts its summoner an ally.
**Wellspring** · Nihiloth, the Hollow Law (Limina): it cancels the law already present, giving the construct its refusal-shaped character.
**Inherited failures** · Eidolyn's template defect is the working's definition, not its risk; Oblivara's code exhaustion makes a construct held too long a confident, complete, wrong reconstruction; Nihiloth's symmetric cancellation takes the parts of the summoner in the billet.
**Essence** · The Essence Core is the billet (a suppressed Trait, an unreconciled harmonic, a fracture from a Stage survived without closing); the Attraction Layer is the tether; the Shell carries the strain. Developmental Tier is Radiant at Transcendence.
**School** · The redemptive schools read the construct as a broken vessel holding a spark and try to lift the spark out while keeping it standing; repair is the work of a people across history, and a husk held is a husk gaining ground.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Form laid under Mind: Form limits what the template may draw on, Mind names where the contents are read from.
**Boundary** · What counts as available material moves to include the unresolved, and Eidolyn faithfully reproduces the locked-in stress with it.
**Effect** · A sound, strong construct that does not tire, flinch or share its summoner's interest, stress oriented toward the thing refused, returning everything back along the tether at the summoner.
**Floor** · Transcendence gates semi-autonomous constructs; full tactical autonomy waits for Realization at SS. Below Transcendence only a brief, fragile, accidental construct forms through an ordinary Animatria working, never the full thing.
**Supply** · It finds a refusal and cannot make one: the summoner must have survived the fracture's Stage, and a fracture spent is spent.
**Anchor** · None: the chain is internal above Refraction, with no circle, catalyst, invocation or wrist to break.
**Failure** · Structural fault is the operation: the Crystal lends what it refused, and losing control is the working completing. Several constructs at once bring the depletion cascade, the discipline's worst recorded event.
**Bleed** · 20 to 30 percent of the sustain leaves the Shell as heat, sound and light every second: the summoner is a furnace visible from the next ridge and scorches the ground around them.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · No chant, circle or gesture: the construct itself, visibly made of something its summoner will not own, erratic toward them, and a summoner burning gigawatts at the Shell. Afterwards, a scorched ring and one fewer unclosed fracture.
**Limits** · It dies when the tether is severed; it cannot be made without a real unclosed fracture; dismissal is not guaranteed and may cost the raising again.
**Fails against** · Silence, which severs the tether totally; the end of the sustain clock; a nick in the one defect left by the Stage the summoner survived badly; its own turn on its maker. Traded blows only load the strong skin.
**Look up** · The Eidolyn, Oblivara and Nihiloth entries and their failures; the Animatria tether and autonomy gates; the Silence Realm; Essence Starvation; the Transcendence and Realization Stage tables; the summoner's own history.

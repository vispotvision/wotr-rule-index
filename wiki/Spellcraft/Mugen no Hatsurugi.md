# Mugen no Hatsurugi

## Mugen no Hatsurugi

---

### Summary card

**Effect** · Muken projects a weapon he has lawfully held, named, inherited, or correctly defeated from his own body, arriving in his hand as though it had always been there rather than being drawn or summoned.
**Cost** · Roughly 45,000 EU per arrival, rising toward 90,000 EU for a weapon named against a significantly higher-weight opponent, in line with the per-exchange costs on his own attested technique sheet.
**Limit** · Only weapons in the lawful ledger may arrive; cosmetic, stolen, or dishonestly acquired holdings are refused at the doctrinal level, and the ledger is built across a lifetime rather than assembled to order.
**Counter** · A contest of the holding's lawfulness, in the moment, stalls the delivery, since Anamnesis will not ratify a claim it cannot verify. Against a Domain enforcing a single discipline, the enforcement clause must answer each named blade individually and a narrow Domain runs out of clauses by the seventh while Muken is still naming the eighth.
**What nobody knows** · Whether a blade retired from the ledger for a dishonestly acquired holding can ever be lawfully re-earned, or whether the forge's refusal is permanent once a lie about the ledger is recorded against a name.

### Origin

Muken Moto's own work, drafted across a hundred and twenty days of instruction on the Plane of Fate, and ratified by neither Bara nor Gimbzo, which makes it his alone. As a self-derived, unratified technique, it has to be read live in an exchange before it kills, and anyone who studies a documented use of it afterward gains standing counterplay against it that its first opponents did not have.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · Templated crystal growth: a seed dropped into a supersaturated solution makes the solute deposit as the seed's own lattice, atom for atom, so the product is the template continued, not a copy of it.
**Law** · Heterogeneous nucleation and epitaxial growth: a phase forming on a substrate takes its structure, and the barrier to forming falls almost to nothing, so a solution inert for hours crashes out in seconds.
**Limit** · A seed you do not have is a seed you cannot grow from: no driving force makes a lattice there is no template for.
**Energy** · Crystallising from a supersaturated medium releases energy; the practitioner supplies only the seed and the barrier-drop, which is why an SS-weight weapon costs a few percent of reserve.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Form, for the template, laid under Return, for the ledger: a holding lawfully held once comes back.
**Boundary** · The nucleation barrier for one lattice at one coordinate drops to nearly nothing, for the one template Anamnesis has ratified.
**Effect** · The weapon is in hand with no transition, as though always held; opponents must process it as already there. Against a line it is a new correct answer every few strides; a narrow Domain runs out of clauses by the seventh named blade.
**Ledger** · Only lawfully held weapons may arrive, built across a lifetime; stolen, cosmetic or dishonest holdings are refused, not delivered badly.
**Floor** · Realization, because the blade must be ratified at SS weight; below it the forge makes shapes, not verdicts. Bodily, read as Magicraft, with no chain to interrupt and no anchor.
**Failure** · No precedent: an exchange the ledger has never met gets an empty hand. Under pressure the armoury phase-locks to one weapon for good; a blade remembered badly in haste can be lost.
**Bleed** · 20 to 30 percent of each arrival leaves the Shell as heat, sound and light, 16 percent at a Refined η 0.84; at tempo it is continuous and Aurevane makes it a beacon.

**Duration** · Arrival is instant, one or two a turn; the blade stays until set down.
**Contest** · A true objection naming the holding's provenance (which blade, taken where, from whom) stalls that arrival for the exchange; a false one is noise.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · The weapon has none; the practitioner has an enormous one, a beacon of coherent light with a judgment field felt as pressure in bone and stance. After, worked ground, and every arrival counted in the record.
**Limits** · Only lawfully held weapons; nothing without precedent; melee-dominant, shock-lines to about 70 m.
**Fails against** · An exchange with no precedent in its ledger; objection as blades arrive, by someone who knows the ledger; held range that offers nothing; a Domain of eight or more clauses; pressure that phase-locks it; tempo sustained past eighteen turns.
**Look up** · The Materia Primordia, Anamnesis, Monolithion and Aurevane failures; the Gravity and Retention gates; the practitioner's recorded holdings; the Realization Stage table; Essence Starvation.

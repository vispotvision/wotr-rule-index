# Mugen no Hatsurugi

## Mugen no Hatsurugi

---

### Summary card

**Effect** · Muken projects a weapon he has lawfully held, named, inherited, or correctly defeated from his own body, arriving in his hand as though it had always been there rather than being drawn or summoned.
**Cost** · Roughly 45,000 EU per arrival, rising toward 90,000 EU for a weapon named against a significantly higher-weight opponent, in line with the per-exchange costs on his own attested technique sheet.
**Limit** · Only weapons in the lawful ledger may arrive; cosmetic, stolen, or dishonestly acquired holdings are refused at the doctrinal level, and the ledger is built across a lifetime rather than assembled to order.
**Counter** · Contesting the lawfulness of the holding itself, in the moment, stalls the delivery, since Anamnesis will not ratify a claim it cannot verify. Against a Domain enforcing a single discipline, the enforcement clause must answer each named blade individually and a narrow Domain runs out of clauses by the seventh while Muken is still naming the eighth.
**What nobody knows** · Whether a blade retired from the ledger for a dishonestly acquired holding can ever be lawfully re-earned, or whether the forge's refusal is permanent once a lie about the ledger is recorded against a name.

### Design Chain

**Trigger** · A combat exchange in which some documented prior holding of Muken's, a weapon he has carried, been given, inherited, or taken by correctly defeating its owner, bears on the moment.
**Function** · Projects that weapon's underlying form, not a copy of its surface, into his hand as a completed arrival rather than a conjuration in progress.
**Mechanism** · The real phenomenon is seed-templated growth: a crystal grown against a template reproduces that template's exact lattice rather than an approximation of it, the same principle Monolithion's single-crystal, boundary-free structure runs on elsewhere in the corpus. Materia Primordia hosts the weapon's form the way a seed crystal hosts a lattice; Anamnesis supplies the templating record, Muken's combat history read as a causal ledger rather than as memory in the ordinary sense; Fixatio anchors his own body as the lawful coordinate the template resolves into; Coagulatio gathers the delivered inventory as one sustained density rather than a scatter of discrete summons; Aurevane names the arriving instance, converting it from a shape into a designated, structurally weighted thing; Monolithion ratifies the delivered blade as a causal sentence the exchange must process as already complete.
**Numerical Effect** · At Stage X's SS-Grade ceiling, a delivered strike's structural weight can in principle be ratified up to the SS-Grade band (10 to 200 TN peak force, 41.8 to 418 TJ), though most individual deliveries land far below that figure; the technique's advantage is in timing and certainty of arrival, not raw destructive scaling. One concrete data point is given directly: against a light-infantry line, arrivals come at a rate of roughly one weapon transition every two to three strides.
**Target Response** · The opponent's nervous system, ward, or Domain must process the arrival as already having occurred before it can respond to it; there is no visible transition to react to.
**Consequence** · The exchange proceeds as though Muken had been holding the weapon the entire time. No tell precedes the arrival.
**Limitation** · The forge does not invent. It delivers only what the ledger can lawfully ratify, and a weapon acquired outside the ledger's terms is simply refused rather than delivered wrong.
**Weakness** · An exchange the ledger has no precedent for gets nothing delivered; the technique diagnoses and corrects known failure patterns and has no answer for a genuinely unprecedented one. Dishonest acquisition compounds against the practitioner over time rather than immediately.
**Cost** · Per the Summary card, 45,000 to roughly 90,000 EU per arrival, derived from Muken's own attested per-technique costs.
**Counterplay** · Force a fight with no precedent in Muken's ledger, or contest the lawfulness of a named holding in the moment it is claimed. Against Muken's own Shingan-assisted use, distance held and nothing else offered denies the diagnostic read the synergy depends on.

### Origin

Per the card's own history: Muken Moto's own work, drafted across a hundred and twenty days of instruction on the Plane of Fate, and ratified by neither Bara nor Gimbzo, which makes it his alone under the named-inventor rule. As a self-derived, unratified technique, it must be read live in an exchange before it kills rather than summarized, and anyone who studies a documented use of it afterward gains standing counterplay against it that its first opponents did not have. Where this places him in relation to his later, ratified reign in Kharven, whose own sheet does not carry this technique, is unresolved and is the discrepancy flagged above.

#### 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** A crystal is grown against a template, so
that what forms is not an approximation of the template but the template's own
lattice, atom for atom — and it forms fast, because a template removes the part
of the process that takes time.
**The real phenomenon.** **Seed-templated growth**, which is the page's own
stated mechanism and is exactly right, plus the nucleation theory that explains
why it is *fast* rather than merely accurate.
*Templated growth.* Drop a seed crystal into a supersaturated solution and the
solute deposits along the seed's own lattice, reproducing its structure and its
orientation rather than an average of all the structures it could have taken.
Every silicon boule, every turbine blade, every cultured gemstone is made this
way. **The template does not describe the product. The product is a continuation
of the template**, which is the difference the page insists on: *"Projects that
weapon's underlying form, not a copy of its surface."*
*Why templating is fast, and this is the load-bearing part.* Homogeneous
nucleation — a crystal forming with no seed — has to pay the surface energy of a
new interface before it gains any volume energy back, so it faces a free-energy
barrier and can take an arbitrarily long time. **Heterogeneous nucleation on an
existing template pays almost none of that barrier**, which is why a
supersaturated solution sits inert for hours and then crashes out in seconds the
moment a seed appears, and why a scratch on the glass is enough. *"Arriving in
his hand as though it had always been there rather than being drawn or
summoned"* is the description of a process with no induction time, and a process
with no induction time is a **templated** one. The page's Target Response is the
same statement from the far side: *"there is no visible transition to react
to."*
*And why the ledger is the limiting resource rather than the power.* A seed you
do not have is a seed you cannot grow from. Templated growth is not a matter of
supplying more driving force; it is a matter of **possessing the template**. So
the page's Limitation is a physical statement and not a doctrinal one: *"The
forge does not invent. It delivers only what the ledger can lawfully ratify."*
There is no amount of effort that produces a lattice you have no seed for.
**The governing law.** **Heterogeneous nucleation and epitaxial growth**: a
phase forming on a substrate adopts the substrate's structure, and the
activation barrier scales down by a factor set by the interfacial geometry.
Monolithion's register supplies the Family's version — single-crystal growth with
no grain boundaries — and the page cites it correctly.
**Measurable quantities, SI.** Between the page and the card this working is
well numbered, and the numbers mostly check.
- **Cost: 45,000 EU per arrival, rising to ~90,000 EU** against *"a weapon named
against a significantly higher-weight opponent"* (page). The card's own
technique costs bracket this: **42,000 / 90,000 EU** for one, **22,000 /
44,000 **for another,** 58,000** for a third.
- **Reserve: 1,850,000 EU** (card). **Flux Density 18,600 EU/g**, **η 0.84**,
**15,624 AU/s**. And the formula closes: **18,600 × 0.84 = 15,624** exactly,
so R44-2 holds on this card without correction.
- **Output band, and the page's own claim checks.** The page says *"the SS-Grade
band (10 to 200 TN peak force, 41.8 to 418 TJ)"*. Part Four puts SS attack
output at **4.184×10¹³ – 4.184×10¹⁴ J**, which is **41.84 to 418.4 TJ**. The
page has read the table correctly. The card's own **0.12–0.40 PJ execution** =
1.2–4.0×10¹⁴ J sits inside the same band.
- **The rate, and the number that will not resolve.** *"Against a light-infantry
line, arrivals come at a rate of roughly one weapon transition every two to
three strides."* A stride is a distance and the cost is per arrival, and
nothing anywhere relates strides to seconds. Taking a stride as about a metre:
at a fighting pace of roughly half a second a stride, that is **one arrival
per 1.0–1.5 s ≈ 30,000–45,000 EU/s**, and his reserve supports** 37 to 55
seconds **of line-breaking. At the card's** Mach 8 sustained**, the same two to
three strides pass in about **a millisecond**, giving **4×10⁷ EU/s** and a
reserve that empties in **about forty milliseconds.** The two readings differ
by four orders of magnitude and the page supplies nothing to choose between
them. `SA-GAP-MUGEN-STRIDE-RATE`.
**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** In the lattice's own free
energy, mostly. Crystallisation from a supersaturated medium **releases** energy;
what the practitioner supplies is the seed and the boundary, not the deposition.
This is `Two Sets of Books` §III at its most literal — the joules were already in
the medium, held above equilibrium, waiting for somewhere to go — and it is why a
working that delivers a weapon at SS-Grade structural weight can cost 45,000 EU
rather than the energy content of the weapon.
**Precedent we are not copying.** Weapon-summoning is one of the genre's most
worn devices: Gilgamesh's Gate of Babylon, Noble Phantasms generally, the
requip of a hundred shōnen, every inventory spell in every CRPG. Two things
make this one different and both are constraints rather than powers. First, the
armoury is **a ledger of things lawfully held, built across a lifetime and not
assembled to order** — *"cosmetic, stolen, or dishonestly acquired holdings are
refused at the doctrinal level."* Second, the arrival is **a claim that can be
contested in the moment**: *"Contesting the lawfulness of the holding itself, in
the moment, stalls the delivery, since Anamnesis will not ratify a claim it
cannot verify."* A weapon-summoner whose summons can be **objected to** is a
different animal from one whose summons can only be out-fought, and it is a much
better one.

#### 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** Draw is a sharp per-arrival expenditure with no sustain — the
weapon, once arrived, is a weapon. **Residue:** each arrival ratifies itself into
the record, which is the page's own framing (*"Monolithion ratifies the delivered
blade as a causal sentence the exchange must process as already complete"*), so
the residue is documentary rather than ambient: the ledger grows by use.
**Saturation:** not a factor for a single arrival; a real question at the rate
the page implies, and unanswerable until `SA-GAP-MUGEN-STRIDE-RATE` is ruled.
**Wellspring stratum.** Six, and the page assigns each a distinct job — the most
carefully divided Wellspring stack in this section.
> **Materia Primordia · *The First Substance*** — *"Graphite and diamond are the
> same element. The difference is entirely in how the atoms are arranged…
> Materia Primordia grants the Crystal access to its own polymorphism: the same
> Essence, re-latticed, expressing properties that appear to belong to different
> substances entirely."* Failure: *"Phase locking. Under sufficient pressure the
> lattice commits to one allotrope permanently, and the practitioner keeps
> whichever form they were wearing when the pressure came."*
> (`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Materia — Material Science.md`)
**This is the whole working in one Core Law**: *the same Essence, re-latticed,
expressing properties that appear to belong to different substances entirely* is
exactly what an armoury of one man's Essence is. And its Failure is the
technique's unstated catastrophe — **phase locking under pressure**, in which the
practitioner keeps whichever form he was wearing. A man whose method is *many
lattices, one substance* has a documented failure mode in which he is reduced to
**one blade, permanently**, and it fires under sufficient pressure. Not on the
page (`SA-PHYS-MUGEN-PHASE-LOCKING`).
> **Anamnesis · *The Wellspring of Remembrance*** *(Limina)* — *"Objects
> genuinely store their history in physically readable form. Anamnesis is the
> instrument, not the miracle."* Failure: *"Thermal resetting. Reading is not
> always passive, and a careless practitioner erases the record in the act of
> taking it."*
The templating record — *"Muken's combat history read as a causal ledger rather
than as memory in the ordinary sense"* — and the ratifier behind the page's
stated Counter. Its Failure is the second unstated hazard and it is a serious
one: **a careless read erases the record**, and this practitioner's record is his
entire armoury.
> **Fixatio · *The Binding Flame*** — the lawful coordinate the template resolves
> into. *"What it holds is not held by force but by the fact that the alternative
> configuration no longer exists as a reachable state."*
> **Coagulatio · *The Returning Stone*** — *"pulling dispersed Essence out of
> suspension into a denser and far less wasteful phase"*, which is the
> supersaturation the seed crashes out of, and the page's *"gathers the delivered
> inventory as one sustained density rather than a scatter of discrete summons."*
> **Monolithion · *The Enduring Monument*** — the ratification, and the same
> boundary-free single crystal that the `Kurotana` account turns on. Its Failure
> applies here too: *"when Monolithion fails it fails across its entire section at
> once."* A ratified blade does not chip. It ends.
> **Aurevane · *The Wellspring of Light*** *(Fulguria)* — *"coherent light holds
> a fixed phase relationship across its whole front"* — naming the arriving
> instance, *"converting it from a shape into a designated, structurally weighted
> thing."* Its Failure is a tactical fact: *"Glare. Coherent output is also a
> beacon, and Aurevane practitioners are the easiest people in any engagement to
> locate."*
*Four Theories.* **The Correspondence Position** — *"The practitioner and the
Wellspring are the same law expressed at two scales, and the working is the two
scales briefly agreeing"* (`Two Sets of Books` §IV). A working in which a man's
own history is the template his Essence re-latticed against is the two scales
agreeing, and the page's insistence on *lawful* holding is the Correspondence
position's ethics: the scales only agree if the claim is true.
**Essence stratum.** Active layers: the **Essence Core** supplies the substance
and holds the ledger (the Core is where *"identity, memory, and Traits"* live, and
a combat history read as a causal ledger is Core material); the **Aether Shell**
is the lawful coordinate the template resolves into, which is why Fixatio anchors
*his body*; the **Attraction Layer** is where *lawfulness* is adjudicated, since
Attraction Force is *"the law of recognition"* and a holding is a recognised
claim. Developmental Tier at Stage X: **Sovereign** (Part Twenty-One). Crystal
State: *"Refined over sixteen years of rule from a scarred impact engine into a
judgment-bearing lattice"* (card) — **Refined**, which is one of the three, and
this is the only card in this section that states its Crystal State correctly.
**The invoice:** 45,000–90,000 EU per arrival against a 1,850,000 EU reserve,
which is a proper invoice against a proper reserve and makes this the second
fully-costed working in the section.
**The lens: the seminal reasons.** The Stoic *logoi spermatikoi*, carried into
the Latin tradition by Augustine as *rationes seminales* (*De Genesi ad litteram*
VI.x). The doctrine is that creation did not make each thing at the moment it
appears; it planted in matter the **seed-principles** of everything that would
ever unfold, so that what looks like a new thing arriving is an already-existing
principle reaching its proper hour. Nothing is invented; things become
manifest. **That is Mugen no Hatsurugi as a metaphysics, and it is also
literally the physics** — a seed crystal does not create a lattice, it is the
lattice, continued — and it is why the arrival reads to witnesses as *"having
always been there rather than being drawn or summoned."* Where the lens misleads
is on **scope**. On Augustine's account the seed-principles of *everything* are
planted: whatever can be, already is, in seed. A school reading Muken that way
concludes that his armoury is effectively unbounded — anything he could lawfully
have held is latent in him and will come when its hour arrives — and will
therefore plan a fight on the assumption **that he always has an answer**. The
page says the exact opposite, twice: *"The forge does not invent"*, and
*"An exchange the ledger has no precedent for gets nothing delivered; the
technique diagnoses and corrects known failure patterns and has no answer for a
genuinely unprecedented one."* So the school that reads him through seminal
reasons will never try the one thing that beats him, because its doctrine says
the attempt is pointless. That is a counter a whole tradition can be talked out
of, which is the most expensive kind. Offered as a school's error, not as canon.

#### 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Frm]` **Form** · Extension · Thalen
for the template, laid under `[Rt]` **Return** · Synonym · Auren for the ledger's
own operation — a holding lawfully held once comes back. Both from the thirty
unclassified forms. The Index's caution governs: *"Assignment is decided after
the phenomenon, never before."*
*Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling (`SA-GAP-GLYPH-MIRROR`).*
**What boundary moves.** **The nucleation barrier for one lattice, at one
coordinate.** Not the supply of substance, not the weapon's properties, not the
opponent's ability to react. One activation barrier is dropped to nearly nothing
at the place Fixatio has named as lawful, and the template Anamnesis has ratified
is the one the barrier is dropped for.
**What the law then does on its own.** It grows, along the template, and it does
it without an induction period because that is what a seeded phase transition
does. Nobody assembles the blade. The medium was already supersaturated —
Coagulatio's *"dispersed Essence … pulled out of suspension into a denser phase"*
— and a supersaturated medium given a seed surrenders its solute *"the instant a
nucleation site appears."* The instant. So the arrival has no visible transition
because there is no stage during which the weapon is partly present, and the
opponent's problem is stated exactly: *"The opponent's nervous system, ward, or
Domain must process the arrival as already having occurred before it can respond
to it."*
Then Monolithion ratifies, which is the step that makes it a weapon rather than a
shape. A single crystal with no grain boundaries is not an approximation of a
blade; it is a blade with no internal interfaces to fail along, *"a causal
sentence the exchange must process as already complete."* And Aurevane names it,
*"converting it from a shape into a designated, structurally weighted thing"*,
which is Aurevane's coherence doing what coherence does: fixing the phase
relationship across the whole front so what leaves matches what was intended with
no distortion introduced in transit.
**And the ledger is a gate on the seed, not on the man.** The refusal the page
describes — *"a weapon acquired outside the ledger's terms is simply refused
rather than delivered wrong"* — is the behaviour of a templating process with no
template. It does not produce a bad copy. There is nothing to grow along.
**Law V check.** The floor is **Stage X, Realization**, three Stages above
Refraction, so no anchor is required (`The Four Crafts`, Law V), and the page
files it as **Magicraft** — *"The projection is bodily rather than spoken,
written, or poured."* **The gate closes.** The consequence matters for
counterplay: *"Break his wrist and a gestured chain is finished. Put a hand over
his mouth and a spoken one is"* reaches nothing here. There is no chain to
interrupt, and the page says so: *"No tell precedes the arrival."*
**Failure mode — Boundary fault, and the page gives the honest one.** *"The
condition was established imprecisely and the law ran somewhere unintended"*
(`Two Sets of Books` §VII). Here the boundary is *lawfulness*, and the page's
Weakness is a boundary that has nothing behind it: *"An exchange the ledger has
no precedent for gets nothing delivered."* Not a misfire. A null.
Two further faults sit on the registers and not on the page.
**Materia Primordia's phase locking** — *"under sufficient pressure the lattice
commits to one allotrope permanently, and the practitioner keeps whichever form
they were wearing when the pressure came"* — which for this working means an
armoury collapsing to one weapon under load, permanently.
**Anamnesis's thermal resetting** — *"a careless practitioner erases the record in
the act of taking it"* — which for a man whose armoury *is* his record is a way
of losing a blade by remembering it badly. Neither is stated
(`SA-PHYS-MUGEN-PHASE-LOCKING`).
**What the target sees and feels.** He is holding something he was not holding,
and there was no moment at which he picked it up. That is the whole of the
experience and it is why the page insists on the absence rather than describing a
flourish: *"arriving in his hand as though it had always been there"*, *"no
visible transition to react to"*, *"No tell precedes the arrival."* The exchange
does not adjust to accommodate a new weapon; it proceeds *"as though Muken had
been holding the weapon the entire time"*, and the opponent's ward or Domain must
process a completed fact rather than an incoming one. Against a line of light
infantry it is a rolling substitution — a different answer every two or three
strides, each one already correct for the man in front of him. Against a Domain
built to enforce one discipline it is a queue of objections: *"the enforcement
clause must answer each named blade individually and a narrow Domain runs out of
clauses by the seventh while Muken is still naming the eighth."*
And the two things that do not happen. It does not go wrong: a claim outside the
ledger is **refused**, not delivered badly. And it does not come at all against a
situation his history has no precedent for, which is the one moment in which the
most certain weapon in the corpus produces nothing.
**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** η **0.84** (card). At Stage X the
Tier of Standing is **6 · Master**, η band **0.70–0.80** (Part Nineteen), so
**0.84 sits above its band** — the same class as `SA-NUM-ETA-TIER`, logged for
this card as `SA-NUM-MUKEN-ETA-TIER`. Taking the card's figure: **16 percent of
every expenditure leaves as heat, sound and structural bleed**, which at 45,000 EU
per arrival is **7,200 EU of waste per weapon** and at 90,000 EU is **14,400 EU**
— and at the page's own stated tempo of an arrival every two to three strides,
that is a practitioner radiating continuously in a way `Two Sets of Books` §III
describes precisely: *"a low-efficiency practitioner is loud, warm, and easy to
find."* He is not low-efficiency, and at that rate it does not matter. Add
Aurevane's Failure — *"Coherent output is also a beacon, and Aurevane
practitioners are the easiest people in any engagement to locate"* — and the
working that leaves *"no tell"* on the page is, by its own Wellspring register,
**the easiest thing on the field to find.** That is not a contradiction; the
arrival has no tell and the practitioner has an enormous one, and the distinction
is worth having.

**The arithmetic, and what it turns up.**
*What checks, and there is a lot of it.* **18,600 × 0.84 = 15,624** — R44-2 holds
without correction. The page's SS-Grade figures (41.8–418 TJ) reproduce Part
Four's band exactly. The card's execution output sits inside that band. The Path
weighting on the page matches the card's percentages. The Crystal State is one of
the system's three. **Eighteen to thirty-seven arrivals from a full reserve** is a
clean, decisive, playable Starvation margin — the second fully computed one in
this section, after Dougou's.
*What does not.* **η 0.84 against a Tier 6 band of 0.70–0.80.** The card is
above its band by the same margin that three cards in the Techniques batch were
outside theirs (`SA-NUM-ETA-TIER`), and here the consequence is direct: correcting
η downward under R44-2 would move his stated AU/s, which currently closes
perfectly. `SA-NUM-MUKEN-ETA-TIER`.
*And the number that spans four orders of magnitude.* The page states a rate in
**strides** and a cost in **EU**, and the corpus relates strides to nothing. At a
fighting pace he breaks a line for **about a minute**; at his own card's sustained
speed he empties in **forty milliseconds**. Both readings are available from the
documents and nothing chooses. This is the single largest unresolved quantity in
the Spellcraft batch and it is the difference between a sustainable battlefield
technique and one that cannot be used twice.
`SA-GAP-MUGEN-STRIDE-RATE`.
**Does the physics close against the stratal account?** **Yes, and the closure is
specific.** Templated growth is cheap because the medium is already
supersaturated and the practitioner supplies only the seed and the barrier-drop;
the stratal account correspondingly bills a moderate fixed cost per arrival rather
than a cost scaling with the weapon's delivered energy, which is why 45,000 EU
buys a blade that can be ratified into the SS band. The refusal behaviour closes
too: a templating process with no template produces nothing rather than producing
badly, which is what the Limitation says. The thing that does not close is the
**rate against the reserve**, and that is an arithmetic question rather than a
physical one.

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).
- **Break the man — be unprecedented, which is the page's own Weakness and is
free.** *"An exchange the ledger has no precedent for gets nothing delivered;
the technique diagnoses and corrects known failure patterns and has no answer
for a genuinely unprecedented one."* The register's frame is **"Read him"** run
backwards: *"Every thaumaturge has a pattern, because single-axis fluency
produces one."* Muken's pattern is his entire life, which is an enormous
strength and a perfectly legible one — and an opponent who fights in a way
nobody has ever fought him is fighting a man with an empty hand. This costs
nothing, requires no Stage, and is exactly the counter a school reading him
through seminal reasons (§2) will never attempt.
- **Break the boundary — object.** *"Contesting the lawfulness of the holding
itself, in the moment, stalls the delivery, since Anamnesis will not ratify a
claim it cannot verify."* This is the register's **Countersigning** entry in a
different key: *"Some Seals end when countersigned in writing, and some end when
the sum is spoken back correctly by another. A working that carries either of
those closing conditions can be ended by an opponent who is literate enough."*
Here, literate means **knowing his ledger** — knowing which blade came from
where and being able to say so while he is claiming it. Preparation pays, and it
is archival preparation rather than martial.
- **Break the man — offer nothing, at range.** The page's own last line:
*"Against Muken's own Shingan-assisted use, distance held and nothing else
offered denies the diagnostic read the synergy depends on."* And the register:
*"Distance.** … Most practitioners are a threat about closing range and are
written as though they were not."* His card puts him at **melee dominant** with
70 m shock-lines; the forge is a delivery system for a hand.
- **Break the boundary — outlast the queue, and note the page tells you the
number.** *"Against a Domain enforcing a single discipline, the enforcement
clause must answer each named blade individually and a narrow Domain runs out of
clauses by the seventh while Muken is still naming the eighth."* Read the other
way, that is a count: **a Domain with eight or more clauses does not run out**,
and a defender who knows the figure knows what breadth of Domain to bring.
- **Break the body — arithmetic.** *"Exhaustion. Below ten percent reserve a
practitioner suffers Essence Starvation. … A defender who can afford to spend
nothing wins a long engagement against an attacker who cannot afford to spend
nothing."* Eighteen arrivals at the ceiling cost. That is the whole fight, and
it is a number a reader can compute from his card and the Core Vocabulary
without anybody telling them.
**The tell, stated plainly.** Not the weapon — the weapon has none, and the page
is honest about that. **The man.** Aurevane's coherence makes him the easiest
person on a field to locate, and his card's Aura is *"S-Tier Crowned Judgment
Field. Pressure felt in bone, weapon and stance."* You will know exactly where he
is at all times and you will not know what is in his hand until it has already
been there.

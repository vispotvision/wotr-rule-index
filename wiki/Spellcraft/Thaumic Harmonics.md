# Thaumic Harmonics

## Thaumic Harmonics

### Summary card

**Effect** · A trained listener reads a running working by its tone, names its weakest member, and answers with one small counter-note that pins or unravels it mid-cast. Turned the other way, a choir lifts a primary's working past what one caster could hold.
**Cost** · Half a percent of full reserve for a read, 3 percent for an Interference Lock, 1 percent a turn for a supporting voice or ritual stabilisation. A lock that goes wrong is paid twice.
**Limit** · It wins by timing, never by force: a finished working, a silent one, or a broadband working with no sharp line gives it nothing to grip.
**Counter** · Workings closed in short bursts, a Crystal stiffened with Basilithe, loose and broadband casting, a noisy room, or a working hidden below hearing by Tenebra or Luminalis.
**What nobody knows** · Whether a working's sand-plate figure is its true form, as the Figure school teaches, or only a shape that unrelated workings happen to share.

### Codex line

**Wellspring** · None of its own; it listens to the target working's Wellspring. Nihiloth, Basilithe and Aurevane describe what it does to other workings.
**Family** · None of its own; it reads whatever Family the target working draws on.
**Physics Domain** · Forced oscillation and superposition: a small, correctly timed wave in antiphase cancels a large one.
**Category** · Harmonia (Spirit and Attraction), with Aetherica supplying the clean baseline a reading is compared against.
**Craft** · Spellcraft, most often used against another Spellcraft or Runecraft working already in progress.
**Stage floor** · Glory
**Grade required** · A-Grade for perception; S-Grade for Interference Lock; SS-Grade for ritual stabilisation.
**Path gate** · Attraction and Spirit Paths for Attunement past C; without them it caps at C and cannot hold a frequency.

### FOW line

**Governing Primary** · Harmonics (Attunement, Suppression), with Gnosis (Analysis) secondary.
**Stage floor** · Glory for perception, Transcendence for Interference Lock, Realization for ritual stabilisation.

### Origin

**Origin** · Parunic monks of the Second Epoch, decoding glyph chants, found that Parunic syntax is a harmonic language. Thalos of Ira codified the branch through Chord Theory, and Aurevia the Echowright made it a combat art with Interference Lock.
**Practitioners** · Thalos of Ira; Aurevia the Echowright; the Cantic Order, whose choirs teach "break the song, do not block the spell"; the Sonochoral Conclave's chanters; Genn Arkelos, the guild Frequencywright who diagnosed leyline anomalies during the early Imperial Convergences.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · A running working is listened to, its weakest member named from the sound alone, and a tiny, correctly timed push at that member's frequency stops it, the way vibration monitoring names a failing bearing and a resonance amplifies a small drive.
**Law** · Forced oscillation with amplification Q = f₀/Δf, plus superposition: a small antiphase wave sums against a large one without overpowering it; N sources in phase give N times the amplitude and N² the intensity.
**Limit** · Time to lock is set by Q, not power: a counter at amplitude ratio r cancels in about 1/r cycles, capped at Q cycles, and a low-Q, broadband working has no sharp line to grip.
**Read** · Every running assembly radiates a line per moving member at frequencies fixed by geometry; a trained Harmonist reads from tone what instruments would need hours to measure, and names the lowest-margin mode.
**Inverse** · High-Q workings, sharply tuned and economical, fall to a whisper; sloppy ones must be answered with force. The discipline is worst against bad casters.
**Energy** · Almost all in the target: the lock removes a working's ability to hold its own phase, and the hostile caster's committed energy goes nowhere useful. The practitioner supplies a phase angle.
| Target Working | Counter's Size | Cycles to Lock |
|---|---|---|
| Q ≈ 1,000 (a good bell) | one-thousandth of the target | about 1,000 |
| Q ≈ 1,000 | one-tenth of the target | about 10 |
| Q ≈ 10 (a broadband working) | one-tenth of the target | about 10, and the lock is shallow |
| Choir of ten in phase | 10 × amplitude | 100 × intensity |

### Metaphysics

**Aether** · Small draw, since it supplies phase, not power. A locked working's Residue carries two frequency sets rather than one, which is how Concord casework tells a countered working from a botched one.
**Field** · Ambient tonal noise runs against the branch: a loud room, or ground worked heavily by other hands, raises the floor and hides the target's line.
**Wellspring** · None of its own: it listens to the working's dialect. Three Wellsprings describe what it does to others' workings.
**Wellspring** · Nihiloth, the Hollow Law (Limina): two waves in antiphase sum to zero and the region between carries no energy; Interference Lock is its physics, without harmonising with it.
**Wellspring** · Basilithe, the Stone Will (Materia): it stiffens a Crystal until its resonance will not be driven off, the defence against every imposed rhythm.
**Wellspring** · Aurevane, the Wellspring of Light (Fulguria): coherent light holds a fixed phase across its front; coherence is exactly what the lock attacks.
**Theory** · The Consent Doctrine: a phase relation is imposed and superposition does the rest, asking nothing of any current, so the branch is licensed curriculum, not restricted practice.
**Essence** · The Aether Shell throughout, receptive for the read and projective for the counter within the same two seconds; the Essence Core supplies the stable reference tone; the Attraction Layer is untouched. Developmental Tier is Harmonic at Glory, Radiant at Transcendence, Sovereign at Realization.
**School** · The Figure school holds that a sand-plate figure is a thing's true form, so to know a working's figure is to own it; but figures repeat across unrelated workings, and matching them walks its Harmonists into Echo Fracture.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Frequency (Selhar), a Root, under Knot (Maelor), a Counter: it does not attack the hostile Wellspring but prevents the hostile working's boundary from closing.
**Boundary** · In the read, the Harmonist's own emission floor; in the counter, one phase angle at one frequency at one member of the target chain, pinned at π.
**Effect** · The hostile working keeps casting, drawing and costing, and arrives nowhere; in the air it is pinned or unravelled, in the chain it never closes. Its caster feels a correct working producing nothing, and supplying more only spends reserve.
**Choir** · The constructive half flips the sign: supporters hold phase to a primary's tone and the intensity adds as N², bought by the supporters, not the primary. The lead singer's Crystal Fracture risk rises with each supporting voice; that is how a choir exceeds capacity.
**Modes** · Tonal perception from Glory, Expert, A (ceiling 400); Interference Lock from Transcendence, Master, S (ceiling 550); ritual stabilisation from Realization, Master, SS (ceiling 725).
**Anchor** · Below Refraction the anchor is voice, a literal tone, as in the Sonochoral Conclave's chant; above Refraction the chain internalises and the counter goes silent.
**Contest** · A contested lock resolves on the Harmonist's Harmonics Attunement against the target caster's Harmonics Stability.
**Failure** · Echo Fracture: a counter on the wrong harmonic or a phase error of π amplifies the hostile working, by Q at resonance. The Cantic Order teaches: break the song, do not block the spell.
**Beating** · A choir drifting out of phase swings the primary's amplitude several times a second at the difference frequency, and the primary fails its own way.
**Bleed** · Waste is broadband sound at the working's own frequencies, in the channel the Harmonist listens to: a third of every expenditure at Glory deafens him, about a quarter at Transcendence, which is why the lock sits two Stages above perception.

**Cost** · Three percent of full reserve per lock, spent under a two-second clock; every lock that turns into Echo Fracture is paid twice.
**Duration** · A master reads, analyses and counters in under two seconds, a third of a six-second turn; choir support and stabilisation last as long as the phase holds, turn by turn.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · A second practitioner goes still, listens about a second, and sings one short note, aloud and directional below Refraction, silent above it; afterwards a working that failed to arrive and a trace with two frequency sets.
**Limits** · It needs the casting window: a closed working is out of reach, a working without harmonic signature offers no grip, the read must finish before the cast, and a misread working is amplified.
**Fails against** · Workings closed in short bursts, or Sealed early by their own caster; a phase pre-claimed with Lock; a Crystal stiffened with Basilithe; low-Q, broadband casting; a noisy room or a Thick Veil zone; two workings presenting one figure.
**Below hearing** · Workings hidden by Tenebra or Luminalis are too quiet for the branch to grip.
**Look up** · The Counterplay register for Lock and Knot; the Basilithe, Nihiloth, Aurevane, Tenebra and Luminalis entries; Veil thickness zones; the Glory, Transcendence and Realization Stage tables; the Spirit and Attraction Path gates.

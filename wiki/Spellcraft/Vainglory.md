# Vainglory

## Vainglory

### Summary card

**Effect** · A fragment of the summoner's Essence Core is shaped around what they wish they were rather than what they are: a Vainglory Eidolon that passes for a better version of them and grows more false every turn.
**Cost** · 75,000 EU to fabricate, from about 8 percent of a deep Glory reserve to 90 percent of a shallow one. Then 10,000 to 25,000 EU a second from the summoner's own Core, doubling as the lie doubles.
**Limit** · It never stabilises, cannot be dismissed safely, cannot be made without cost to the summoner's Crystal, and convinces only those who never met the unflattering self.
**Counter** · Silence severs it; a witness who has seen the summoner on a bad day out-honests it; another Mirithane practitioner or Oblivara returns the correction to its source.
**What nobody knows** · What becomes of a summoner once the construct has taken their place, and whether anything of them remains inside it.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · Positive feedback: a mirror given gain returns more than it received, the return goes round again brighter, and the loop grows instead of settling, like a laser that no longer needs an input.
**Law** · Barkhausen's criterion: a loop oscillates once round-trip gain reaches one, then grows as A(t) = A₀ · e^((g − l)·t/τ), exponential in the excess gain.
**Limit** · A passive mirror has reflectivity R ≤ 1 and is stable by construction; only a pump can hold R above one, and no amplifier grows forever.
**Endings** · Gain saturation settles the loop into a steady state set by the cavity, not the source: a stable false self able to displace its summoner. Cavity damage burns the mirror: here the cavity is the summoner's own Shell and Core.
**Self-oscillation** · A phase-conjugate mirror with gain can produce a corrected wavefront with no incoming signal; a construct coherent enough to contest its summoner has stopped needing a source.
**Loss** · An honest, lossy element in the loop lowers the gain, and below one the oscillation dies. Switching off the pump stops replenishment but leaves stored energy that must go somewhere.
**Energy** · In the pump, which is the summoner: gain costs whatever keeps it up, continuously and rising, drawn from the Essence Core the summoner still has to live in.

|---|---|---|
| Round trips to double a 1% excess | ln 2 ÷ 0.01 | about 69 |
| Round trips to double a 10% excess | ln 2 ÷ 0.10 | about 7 |
| After 690 round trips at 1% | 2¹⁰ | deviation a thousand times what it was |
| Passive mirror | R ≤ 1 | any deviation decays |

### Metaphysics

**Aether** · A fabrication charge at the bottom of the Animatria band plus a rising sustain. The Residue is the construct itself, and it grows; a room where one stood long deafens Harmonists, and past saturation risks a Crystal Fracture Event, the summoner first.
**Wellspring** · Mirithane, the Wellspring of Reflection (Limina): a true mirror adds nothing, so output honestly reflects inner state; Vainglory is the flattering correction it forbids, pumped against its pull toward honesty. It strengthens Harmonics Attunement and Tempering Coherence.
**Wellspring** · Eidolyn, the Shaping Mind (Limina): it deposits along a template with real material strength; it is the cavity and the shape.
**Wellspring** · Oneirion, the Wellspring of Dream (Limina): a working acts on the space of possible arrangements; it fetches the flattering self from a coordinate the Crystal never occupied.
**Inherited failures** · Mirithane's mirror broadcasts perfectly any state the summoner would hide; Eidolyn's template defect reproduces every flaw of an aspirational template at scale; Oneirion's projection error makes the construct convincing in the abstract and wrong in the specifics. Any of them can be induced from outside.
**Theory** · Correspondence, refused: the working asserts "this construct corresponds to me", and Mirithane's office is to test that and return the answer unedited. It is a false correspondence held up by a pump.
**Essence** · The Essence Core is the pump; the Aether Shell is the reflecting surface Mirithane works on; the Attraction Layer holds the tether, which is why Silence ends it. Developmental Tier at the floor is Harmonic.
**School** · A humanist school holds that a person has no fixed nature and may sculpt the higher form they elect, so it keeps a construct standing to grow toward; its doctrine has no feedback term, and the elected form diverges and displaces the sculptor.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Mirage (Kaetra) as the boundary that moves, laid under Reverie (Maelor) for the register the correction is drawn from.
**Boundary** · The tolerance on the Shell's reflection moves: how much correction a return may introduce. Eidolyn then deposits the corrected return as something with real material behaviour.
**Effect** · Each corrected return becomes the next state to reflect, so the construct drifts toward the flattery on an exponential. To strangers it is a better version of the summoner; to anyone who knows them, an immaculate likeness of an untrue claim, getting worse hourly.
**Gap** · The Deviation is the share by which the construct's return exceeds an honest reflection, the excess gain; the loop makes one round trip a six-second turn, so the Deviation doubles every ln 2 ÷ Deviation turns.
**Upkeep** · Sustain scales with the current Deviation over the starting one: it doubles each time the Deviation doubles, so a construct straying far from its summoner drains faster the further it strays.
**Arising** · As often by accident as design: a failed ascension, a Temperance collapse, or a Trait overreaching its Stage. The floor is Glory, the basic Animatria floor, crossable without meaning to.
**Anchor** · Glory is below Refraction, so a deliberate raising needs voice, hand, ink or blood; take it and the sentence does not complete.
**Failure** · Coherence fault by design: the construct is lived behaviour drifting from stated law, so the summoner's output falls, Thresholds refuse to authorise and Traits argue with them, before anyone touches them. Then saturation displaces them, or cavity damage consumes them.
**Dismissal** · Not safe: a true Eidolon at R ≤ 1 decays when supply stops, but a saturated loop holds stored energy that must go somewhere first.
**Bleed** · 30 to 40 percent leaves the summoner's Shell as heat, sound and light: 22,500 to 30,000 EU on fabrication, 3,000 to 10,000 EU a second while sustained, and rising.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · It looks like the summoner, only better, and only someone who has seen the summoner worse can tell; afterwards a summoner thinner than before, or no longer the summoner.
**Limits** · It cannot stay stable, cannot be dismissed safely, cannot be made without cost to the summoner's Crystal, and convinces only those who never met the unflattering self.
**Fails against** · Silence, which severs its continuity; a lost anchor at Glory; the run of the exponential; Coherence drift; an honest reflection, another Mirithane practitioner, or Oblivara, which returns manipulation to its source.
**Witness** · A sibling, creditor or former partner who has seen the summoner on a bad day out-honests the construct without unmasking it.
**Oblivara** · Its price is code exhaustion: past its correction limit the reconstruction is confident, complete and wrong.
**Look up** · The Limina entries for Mirithane, Eidolyn, Oneirion and Oblivara; the Animatria charges of the Spirit Summoning Arts; the Silence Realm register; Coherence drift; the Glory Stage table and Path gates.

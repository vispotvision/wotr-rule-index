# Vainglory

## Vainglory

> **Migration note.** Converted under the discipline-essay format: the card describes a practice attempted by many named summoners across eras, not one character's signature technique. "Essence types," "Temperance collapse" as a loose noun, and "Trait realization" as a generic phrase are retired for current vocabulary. Vainglory is Animatria, Spirit-Aligned, harmonised to Limina, with **Mirithane**, the Wellspring of Reflection, as the load-bearing current rather than a family invented for the card; Mirithane's law is that output becomes a more honest reflection of internal state, and Vainglory is precisely the failure mode of that law, a construct that reflects the summoner's most flattering self-image rather than an honest one. "Synergia" and "Phenomena" in the card's own text are both genuine current Categories and needed no conversion. Serenhal Caelmorne, Yelara Vintress, Raziel Ados, the Tome of Lurid Echoes, the Era of Calamity, the Lament of the Nine Mirrors, the Hall of Reflective Descent, the Order of the Hollow Veil, the Rose-Glass Order, and the Volume VIII chapter citation have no card or page in current canon; all are kept as unattested colour, flagged below, because the discipline's history is otherwise a bare mechanism with no practitioners attached to it. Stage floor and Grade are chosen off the FOW tables rather than invented, with the choice stated in the relevant section.

### What it is

Vainglory is an Animatria working that goes wrong on purpose, or goes wrong so often on the same failure that some practitioners have learned to aim it. Ordinary Animatria detaches a fragment of the summoner's Essence Core and shapes it, through honest intent, into a construct that carries the summoner's real harmonised nature outward. Vainglory substitutes self-idolatry for intent: the fragment is shaped around what the summoner wishes their Crystal were, or fears it must become to be worth anything, rather than around what it actually is. The resulting Vainglory Eidolon is a Mirithane-driven reflection that has stopped being honest, projecting an idealised, inflated, or desperately over-corrected version of its source rather than the source itself.
Because the construct is built on distortion rather than resonance, it does not stabilise the way a true Eidolon does. It becomes more unstable, not less, the longer it is sustained, since a reflection under Mirithane's law is supposed to converge on honesty and a Vainglory construct is actively fighting that convergence the entire time it exists. Some advanced soulbinders raise them deliberately, using the instability as a controlled provocation to test or deceive something that would otherwise see through a lesser deception. Most who produce one do so by accident, during a failed ascension attempt, a Temperance collapse, or a Trait that overreached its own Stage.

### Where it sits in the current system

**Category** · Animatria, Spirit-Aligned, with Synergia-adjacent failure states where an unresolved Vainglory construct persists past its summoner's control (the card's "Echo-Doubles" are best read as a Synergia-category Phenomena event, both of which are genuine current Categories requiring no invention).
**Family / Physics Domain** · Limina, Entropy, Void and Mind. Primary Wellspring **Mirithane**, the Wellspring of Reflection, whose failure state is exactly this discipline's mechanism: what should be an honest reflection of internal state becomes a flattering or desperate one instead. Secondary harmonisations **Eidolyn** (the Shaping Mind, for the construct's solidity) and **Oneirion** (the Wellspring of Dream, for the delusion layer the projection runs on) are consistent with the card's description without inventing a Family that is not on the list of Sixty.
**Craft** · Spellcraft, per the destination shelf and the standing Craft durability table; the construct holds only as long as attention holds, and Vainglory's instability means that hold is already worse than a comparable true Eidolon's.
**Stage floor / Grade** · Stage VI, Glory, A-Grade. This is the floor for basic Animatria eidolons per The Spirit Summoning Arts, chosen deliberately at the low end rather than the higher Gates: the card is explicit that Vainglory Eidola are as often produced by accident during a failed or botched working as by deliberate design, which argues for a floor a practitioner can cross without meaning to rather than one requiring advanced mastery.
**Essence cost** · Not stated on the card. Consistent with the Stage VI floor, the conservative reading off The Spirit Summoning Arts' Animatria cost table is the lower end of the fabrication band, 75,000 EU, with the instability adding an unbudgeted drain rather than a higher headline cost; the card's account of Vainglory constructs turning on their summoners and stealing their place reads as a control failure, not a raw power difference.

### Practitioners and history

None of the four figures the card names have a card or page anywhere in current canon: Serenhal Caelmorne, said to have projected a corrupted version of himself during a Moetana trial; Yelara Vintress of a "Rose-Glass Order," whose Vainglory Eidolon is said to have stolen her original Pneuma and taken her place; Raziel Ados, said to have used the technique to impersonate himself across multiple Domains before being consumed by the result; and the guild named as still teaching a controlled form of the rite, the Order of the Hollow Veil. The founding text the card cites, a Tome of Lurid Echoes, and the mass-casualty event it names, a Lament of the Nine Mirrors during an unattested Era of Calamity, are likewise unattested. None of this is load-bearing for the mechanism itself, so it is kept as unattested colour rather than struck, on the understanding that a review pass may cut it entirely rather than let it stand as fact.

### What it can and cannot do

A Vainglory Eidolon can convincingly stand in for its summoner, at least to an observer who has not met the summoner's actual, unflattering self; this is precisely what makes it useful to the soulbinders who raise one deliberately, to test or mislead something that would see through a cruder deception. It can, given enough time active, develop enough independent coherence to attempt to displace the summoner it was drawn from, since its whole nature is competing with the summoner's honest self-image rather than extending it.
It cannot remain stable indefinitely. Every hour it holds is an hour spent fighting Mirithane's own law rather than working with it, and the instability compounds rather than levels off. It cannot be dismissed as safely as a true Eidolon, because dismissing a construct that has spent its existence contesting its summoner's self-image is not guaranteed to go the summoner's way. And it cannot be produced without cost to the summoner's own Crystal: a working built on self-idolatry rather than resonance is drawing on the same Essence Core the summoner still has to live in afterward.

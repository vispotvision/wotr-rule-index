## What War of the Realms is

War of the Realms is a dark shonen cultivation fantasy set in a magitech world, where cities run on magic piped under the streets like water and gas: lamps, heat, trains and forges all draw on it, while the towns and the wilds still burn oil. People who wake the power in their own souls climb a sixteen-step ladder of strength, and every step is earned by surviving something that nearly breaks them. The stakes are dark and the costs are real, but the heart is shonen: people fight back with will, bonds and growth, and they win often, each win at a price you can see. The look borrows from Arcane, Fullmetal Alchemist, the worlds of Bleach and Naruto, Final Fantasy and Lord of the Mysteries, and the land is its own thing: wildly fantastical and earthlike at once, grand and lived-in, and beautiful all the way through.

## The Magic System, in reading order

When you want more than the primer, read these eight pages in this order. Each one builds on the one before.

1. **[[The Magic System/The Gate Who May Learn Magic|The Gate: Who May Learn Magic]]**. Who wakes their Crystal, who never does, and why that is no longer an accident.
2. **[[The Magic System/The Core Vocabulary|The Core Vocabulary]]**. The basic words: the three planes, Aether (the medium the world is made of) and Essence (Aether a person has drawn in and made their own), the Crystal, abilities and techniques.
3. **[[The Magic System/The Sixteen Stages|The Sixteen Stages]]**. The ladder of power, Stage (one of sixteen steps of the soul's refinement) by Stage, told plainly: what it feels like inside, what it looks like outside, and what ordinary people call it.
4. **[[The Magic System/Tier Grade, Bands & the Aether Shell|Tier Grade, Bands & the Aether Shell]]**. The three measurements people confuse: Level (how often the Crystal has grown denser), Grade (a letter band, F up to EX+, that says how much force a stat delivers) and Aether Class (how cleanly power gets out of the body).
5. **[[The Magic System/The Trait System Law, Function and the Forge|The Trait System: Law, Function and the Forge]]**. Where powers come from. A Trait (a standing law in the soul, formed when lived experience hardens into something the world accepts as true) is survived into being, never taught.
6. **[[The Magic System/The Four Crafts|The Four Crafts]]**. The four ways a working (a deliberate act of magic) is set down: spoken in the moment, cut into stone, steel or paper, poured into a bottle, or sung by many voices together. Also why the guilds look down on fighters who speak their workings, while the crowds cheer them anyway.
7. **[[The Magic System/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings|The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings]]**. The sixty Wellsprings (standing currents in the Aether, each carrying one law of physics) sorted into eight Families: heat, electricity and light, force, space, matter, life, fluids, and entropy and mind.
8. **[[The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner|Counterplay: What Beats a Practitioner]]**. How a practitioner (a person whose Crystal is awake) is actually beaten, in four routes. Read this before your first fight scene.

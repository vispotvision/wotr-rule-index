# Obrenkael · The Mule

*Native-Consenting bearer form. Mahuo line, Aperture branch. Filed under standing Bench objection.*
> **Every skull on the cord is a promise a summoner made and could not keep.** Four of them belong to the practitioner who currently holds the naming-right.
>
> **This entry records a called thing that becomes measurably stronger each time it is failed.** The Arbitration Division's objection is on that basis and has not been ruled on.

## V · Bond and Standing

| **Bond Class** | **Consenting** |
|---|---|
| **Terms of Answering** | It answers a named dead. Hae-ryu's obligation is to name only those she has actually seen die, in her own hearing or her own sight, and to name them accurately. In exchange it will cross ground she cannot cross and carry weight she cannot carry. If she names a stranger, a rumour, or a lie, it completes that carriage anyway and then does not answer her again for one full year. This has happened once. She was twenty-two and the name was her brother's, given on a report rather than a body, and he was alive |
| **Refusal Conditions** | It will not kill on ground where the dead cannot cross. Inside a Silence Realm, on Nihiloth-scoured earth, within an active Entombment, it will defend and it will carry nothing and it will not strike. It will not accept a name from anyone without standing to give it, and standing means having been present at the death. It will not set down a carriage once begun, for any order, in any circumstance, including the death of the summoner. It will not be sealed while carrying, and an attempted sealing mid-carriage is read as an attack on the road |
| **Precedent** | Four recorded refusals. At Haldren Ford it declined a strike order inside a Silence Realm and Hae-ryu lost the crossing and eleven of her people. At Yeol-gol it refused a name given by her uncle, who had not been present, and stood in the doorway of the household hall for six hours until someone who had been present said the name properly. ## VI · Physical Description

It stands three and a half metres at the shoulder in the crouch it never leaves, and the Aperture ledger records an old estimate that it would clear four and a half if it straightened. Nobody has seen it straighten. Mass runs somewhere near that of a draft ox and a half, close to nineteen hundred kilograms, and the figure matters because it is the number the ground reports back: on packed earth it sinks to the second knuckle of the forelimb and on wet ground it sinks past that and keeps walking at the same pace.
The pelt is blue-black, the colour of wet slate under a low sky, wolf-coarse at the ruff and matted lower down into ropes of its own where the gold cord has worn a channel across the shoulder over what must be centuries. The hair along the spine stands in a ridge and does not lie down. Beneath the pelt the hide shows through in patches at the flank and the haunch, grey-green and pebbled, and the texture there is closer to a drowned thing than a living one.
The head is boar and wolf argued into one shape. Two horns sweep back from the crown, ridged in tight bands like a ram's and blunted at the tips from use rather than growth. A third horn, smaller, spirals forward off the brow above the left eye and is banded in copper wire that has gone green. The lower tusks cant outward past the upper lip, worn flat on their inner faces where they close. Two long ears, each split lengthwise, one of them split twice. The eyes sit deep and are pale as boiled milk, and there are two more eye-shaped marks branded into the shoulder that are not eyes, cut rather than burned, spiralling inward. Aperture-branch witness marks. Three of them altogether, the third beneath the left eye, and the household knows what all three attest to and has never written it down.
The cord runs from the ruff across the shoulder and down to the right hip, braided gold as thick as a man's wrist, and there is no one on the other end of it. It is not a harness. It ends where it began. At the hip it gathers into a net of gold wire holding a cluster of skulls, human, adult, brown with age at the crown and paler at the jaw where they have knocked against each other for a long time. Count them and you will get a number. Count them again and you will get a different one. Three separate Accord field observers at the Ossuary Line recorded nine, eleven, and fourteen within the same hour and all three signed their counts as certain.
Over the left flank hangs an apron of lamellar plate, iron scales riveted to a leather ground, studded and pitted and lacquered black in patches where the lacquer survives. A maroon sash cinches it, gold-fitted, and the sash is the only clean thing on the construct. Someone replaces it. The forelimbs end in three splayed digits, hoof-nailed, black, the pads between them cracked open and dry, and the cracks do not bleed.
Within three paces the air drops about four degrees regardless of season, and the smell arrives before the cold does: standing water, wet wool, and old copper underneath. Sound goes flat. Voices carry to it and do not carry past it, which is a nuisance in a line and the reason Zettari officers learned to shout from in front of it rather than behind. Light treats it badly. It absorbs more than the pelt should and the edges of it stay indistinct at distance, so that soldiers reliably underestimate how close it has come and reliably overestimate how fast it is moving.
Where it has stood it leaves a wet print on dry stone, and the print dries from the outside inward over about a quarter of an hour, which is the wrong direction and is the detail that convinces Accord observers when nothing else has. Kwon Hae-ryu's whole interior architecture is legible in the thing: a woman whose branch of the family kept the aptitude for hosting the dead and lost every institution that made hosting them bearable, who calls something that carries rather than something that fights, and who has never once summoned a form that could move faster than a walk.

## VII · Temperament and Voice

It speaks. The register is flat, low, entirely unhurried, and the vocabulary is almost exclusively weight and distance. It reports load. It reports how far. It does not report danger, and Hae-ryu has learned to read danger off the load figure instead, which is the arrangement they arrived at without either of them proposing it.
Sentences run four words or fewer while it is standing and get shorter under carriage. It will not use the name of the person it is carrying, at any point, for any reason, including when asked directly, and it will not explain the prohibition. It refers to a carriage as *the weight* and to the Still Gate only as *there*.
Its signature line, delivered in every recorded engagement where anyone was close enough to hear it, is **"Not far."** It has said this at nine paces and at forty leagues and the tone does not change.
Under strain it counts. Out loud, evenly, in no language anyone in the Aperture household recognises, and it does not stop counting when spoken to. The count does not appear to reset between summonings. Hae-ryu wrote down the number she heard at Haldren Ford and the number she heard four years later at the Ossuary Line, and the second was larger, and she has not written down by how much.

## VIII · Abilities

- ****THE FARE** · *Signature · Vocatia-Native · Transport***
  **Glyph Basis:** [Ob] Obol, the guided lawful crossing · [Vor] Return, recycling Essence and quieting the restless dead · [Au] Death, ushering passage
  *"Quod portatur, transeat."*
  Let what is carried cross over.
  **Trigger:** Hae-ryu speaks the name of a dead person she was present for, aloud, within the construct's hearing. No circle required after the initial calling. The construct answers by lowering its head, which is the only time it lowers its head.
  **Function:** It takes the named dead's Essence onto itself and walks it to the Still Gate, and while it is walking it cannot be turned aside.
  **Mechanism:** Fluid Dynamics. Oblation establishes a pressure differential between the ground where the death occurred and the Gate, and Essence descends that gradient under the same rule that moves any fluid down a head of pressure. The construct is not a courier. It is the pipe. Its own mass and the Mortalis secondary supply the wall thickness that keeps the transfer laminar instead of letting it bleed off into the surrounding field, which is what happens to a death nobody attends and is why unattended dead go strange.
  **Numerical Effect:** Bearing pace holds at approximately 3.5 metres per second and does not vary. It cannot be accelerated by any means yet attempted, including downhill. It cannot be slowed below that figure by anything short of removing the road. Sustain draw scales with straight-line distance to the Gate rather than distance travelled, so a carriage begun on bad ground costs more for the whole of its length.
  **Target Response:** Nothing happens to a living body in its path. It walks through a shield wall the way a barge walks through reeds, and the men in that wall report afterwards that they were not struck, they were *displaced*, and several of them describe going briefly and unpleasantly weightless. Structures fare worse. A stone gatehouse at Haldren Ford was not broken. It was pushed, foundation and all, about a metre and a half laterally, and it is still standing at the wrong angle.
  **Limitation:** It walks a straight line to the Gate and it will not deviate for terrain, for allies, or for the summoner. It cannot carry two. It cannot begin a second carriage until the first completes.
  **Weakness:** For the whole duration Hae-ryu is standing still with her hands open, and every practitioner within reading range knows exactly where the Oblation draw is coming from and exactly how long she is going to be there.
  **Cost:** Moral and spiritual, escalating. If the carriage is interrupted before it completes, the crossing does not simply fail. It arrests, and what was being carried stays on the construct, and a skull joins the cord at the hip. Every skull on that rope is a promise a summoner made and could not keep. Four of them are hers. She can name all four, and does, once a year, out loud, which is the only ritual the Aperture branch practises that nobody taught her.
  **Vow:** The carriage will be completed. Hae-ryu may not recall the construct mid-carriage. Attempting it forfeits the naming-right for one year and adds to the cord regardless.
  **Counterplay:** Take the road away. Harmonic Silence, Nihiloth-scoured ground, an active Entombment, or a sufficiently dense Limina field arrests the transfer and stops the walk cold. A competent opponent who recognises the form does not attack the construct at all. They attack the ground in front of it.
  **Codex Assignment:** [Ob] · [Vor] · [Au] · Oblation primary, Mortalis secondary · Fluxia · Fluid Dynamics
  **First Display:** Planted at a burial rather than a battle. A single farmhand, no stakes, the construct called for one man and walking him three fields over while Hae-ryu stands in the doorway with her hands open and does not move for eleven minutes. Nobody in that scene understands they have watched a weapon.
  **Sensory Description:** The cold seam along its spine opens and the air within three paces goes still enough to feel wrong in the ears. It lowers its head. The rope pulls taut across the shoulder although nothing is pulling it. Hae-ryu's hands open and stay open, and her fingers do not close again until it is finished, and afterwards she cannot make a fist for about an hour.
  **Interactions:** Deadweight is unavailable during a carriage. The Cord is not, and the two of them together are the entire reason she survives a carriage begun in contact.
  **Corruption Vector:** An Obsession-Force Fare does not wait to be given a name. It decides who is finished and begins the carriage while they are still standing, and the person it has chosen keeps talking for a while.
- ****DEADWEIGHT** · *Offensive · Vocatia-Native · Hydrostatic***
  **Glyph Basis:** [Ob] Obol, the lawful crossing · [Abys] Deep, a directional undertow of sub-Realm pressure · [Th] Foundation, imposing geometry and load path
  *"Fer quod ferre non potes."*
  Carry what you cannot carry.
  **Trigger:** It sets a forelimb against a target and shifts its shoulder. That is the whole of the trigger. No line is spoken by Hae-ryu; the Latin belongs to the construct and it says it every time.
  **Function:** It puts the accumulated weight of every unfinished crossing on the cord into whatever it is touching.
  **Mechanism:** Fluid Dynamics, hydrostatic loading. The arrested carriages at its hip are a standing column of pressure that has nowhere to discharge, and the Foundation glyph supplies the load path from the cord through the shoulder to the contact patch. The pressure does not go into the target as force. It goes into the target's own fluid column as head, and the body is then required to contain a pressure it was never built to contain.
  **Numerical Effect:** Contact patch of roughly nine hundred square centimetres. Internal hydrostatic rise in the target measured, in the one case where a Concordia diagnostician was present and equipped, at slightly over three hundred kilopascals inside eight tenths of a second. Arterial pressure in a healthy adult runs near sixteen. The applied figure scales with the number of skulls on the cord, and the register's position, entered under Bench objection, is that this construct becomes measurably stronger every time a summoner fails it.
  **Target Response:** No crush. The armour survives. What fails is everything small and unsupported, in order, from the outside in: the vessels of the eye go first and the whites fill, then the ears, then the gums and the beds of the nails. Plate armour makes no difference at all and the men who understand this stop wearing it around the construct, which the Accord has recorded as a morale problem rather than as evidence. Witnesses describe a man standing upright, unmarked, weeping blood, for the two or three seconds before he goes down.
  **Limitation:** Contact only. It has no ranged expression whatsoever and cannot be projected through a shield, a weapon, or an intervening body.
  **Weakness:** The cord goes slack for about four seconds afterwards and the construct's Bearer's Exemption lapses with it, which means for those four seconds it is standing inside its own Cord and moving through it like everything else.
  **Cost:** Physical toll, borne by the summoner rather than the construct. Every use drives the same pressure differential back up the Attraction Layer into Hae-ryu at a fraction of magnitude, and she has the small persistent haemorrhages to show for it. Her left eye has been red for two years. It does not clear between engagements any more.
  **Vow:** None recorded.
  **Counterplay:** Do not be touched by it, which is easier than it sounds because it walks at a walk. Failing that, the four-second slack window after each use is the entire opening. Radiant current disrupts the load path at the shoulder and drops the applied figure sharply, which is the standard Accord answer and the reason Arbitration teams deploy with Luminalis harmonization when this entry is expected.
  **Codex Assignment:** [Ob] · [Abys] · [Th] · Oblation primary, Mortalis secondary · Fluxia · Fluid Dynamics. **Flagged:** [Abys] Deep carries an Abyntheus-Fulguria-Electromagnetism row in the Master Codex and [Th] Foundation a Petralon-Vectoria-Mechanics row. Neither is attested at Oblation. Codex amendment pitched in Section XIII.
  **First Display:** Against a door. A barred household door at Yeol-gol, nobody behind it, the construct setting a forelimb on it and the door failing in a way doors do not fail: the boards intact, every nail driven out of them and lying on the floor.
  **Sensory Description:** The rope goes rigid first, a hand's width off the flank, and stays there. The skulls stop knocking. Then the shoulder rolls forward about the distance of a slow breath and that is all the movement there is. Hae-ryu's vision goes red at the edges wherever she is standing.
  **Interactions:** Locked out entirely during an active Fare. Works inside her own Cord without penalty until the slack window opens, at which point it does not.
  **Corruption Vector:** An Obsession-Force Deadweight does not discharge into a target. It discharges into the ground and keeps discharging, and everything standing on that ground for two hundred paces bleeds from the eyes at once, including whoever called it.
- ****THE CORD** · *Defensive · Vocatia-Native · Viscous***
  **Glyph Basis:** [RenB] Boundary, establishing metaphysical borders and wards · [Sil] Silence, muting hostile resonance into dignified stillness · [Ob] Obol, the lawful crossing
  *"Intra funem nemo festinet."*
  Within the rope let no one hurry.
  **Trigger:** It plants and the gold cord comes off its shoulder to lie on the ground in a closed loop. It lays the loop itself and Hae-ryu does not choose the shape.
  **Function:** Inside the loop, nothing may move quickly. Slow movement is unhindered.
  **Mechanism:** Fluid Dynamics, quadratic drag. The Boundary glyph raises the effective viscosity of the local Essence medium within the enclosure, and resistance then climbs with the square of velocity exactly as it does in any real fluid. Below roughly one and a half metres per second there is no perceptible effect beyond a slight heaviness. Above it the penalty compounds hard, so that a body moving at six metres per second meets something near sixteen times the resistance of a body moving at one and a half.
  **Numerical Effect:** Loop radius five to seven paces, laid to the ground rather than projected. A sword cut delivered at eight metres per second arrives carrying perhaps a fifth of its intended energy. Loosed arrows fall inside the loop. A charging horse does not stop; it decelerates over about four strides and arrives at a walk, which is worse for the rider than stopping would have been.
  **Target Response:** Men inside it describe wading. They can walk, they can talk, they can bleed and they can die, and everything they attempt at speed feels as though the air has thickened around the limb doing it. Practitioners find that the physical half of their working slows while the metaphysical half does not, which produces a particular and dangerous mismatch in anything that requires both to arrive together.
  **Limitation:** It is a ward against velocity and nothing else. It stops no working that does not need to travel, blocks no line of sight, and does not care in the slightest whether the person inside it is an enemy.
  **Weakness:** Hae-ryu is slowed by it exactly as much as anyone else. The construct is not, and the discrepancy is legible to any competent reader as a bright clean statement about what the Attraction Layer is actually bonded to, which is not her.
  **Cost:** Social and legal. The Cord does not distinguish and it has cost her a field command, two Concord contracts, and the standing to speak first in her own household hall. Every officer who has ever fought beside it has an opinion about the wounded man who could not be dragged out fast enough, and every one of those opinions is correct.
  **Vow:** Optional and used once. She may swear the loop closed around a specific named individual, which narrows it to a single protected body and roughly doubles the drag coefficient. Breaking that vow, meaning stepping out of the loop herself while the named person remains inside it, forfeits the Cord for the remainder of the engagement.
  **Counterplay:** Walk. The whole of the counterplay is walk, and the reason it works anyway is that almost nobody in a fight can make themselves do it. Beyond that: separation from the Oblation vein degrades the enclosure quickly, and the loop cannot be re-laid inside two minutes of collapsing.
  **Codex Assignment:** [RenB] · [Sil] · [Ob] · Oblation primary, Mortalis secondary · Fluxia · Fluid Dynamics. All three glyphs attested at Fluxia.
  **First Display:** In a market. A runaway cart, a child, the construct laying its rope in the road, and the cart arriving at the child's feet at a walking pace with the driver still screaming at a horse that has stopped being able to run.
  **Sensory Description:** Dust inside the loop stops rising and hangs. Rain slows visibly and lands soft. The sound of the fight drops about half and the drop is not in the ears, it is in the air. Hae-ryu's own breathing goes shallow and stays shallow, because breathing fast inside the loop is one of the things the loop declines to permit.
  **Interactions:** Runs concurrently with The Fare, which is the only combination that lets her survive a carriage that begins in contact. Deadweight functions inside it until the slack window, at which point the construct is briefly subject to its own ward and is the slowest thing on the field.
  **Corruption Vector:** An Obsession-Force Cord does not have an upper threshold. It slows everything inside it toward nothing, asymptotically, and the people caught in it do not die. They are still in there.

## IX · Dissolution and Failure

| **Clean Sealing** | Hae-ryu closes the [Sil] role and it turns and walks out of the circle rather than fading, and it is gone at about the fourth stride whether or not there is anywhere for it to have gone. Takes between nine and twenty seconds depending on how much of the rope is on the ground. What remains is a wet print drying from the outside in and a smell of standing water that outlasts the print by an hour |
|---|---|
| **Severed Tether** | Nothing happens to the construct, which is the entire problem with Native provenance and the reason the Bench keeps the file open. It continues. What comes back up the line into Hae-ryu is the sustain draw with no regulator on it, a haemorrhagic event she has survived twice and describes as being emptied rather than injured. It will complete whatever it was carrying and then it will come and find her, and the Aperture ledger does not record what it does when it arrives, because on both occasions she was unconscious for it |
| **Behaviour in Silence** | It holds. It does not function. Inside a Silence Realm it will not carry, will not strike, will not lay the Cord, and will not leave, and it stands between her and whatever is coming with its head up for as long as the Realm lasts. Three engagements' worth of Accord observation agree that this is the only circumstance in which it raises its head |
| **Resistance to Dissolution** | Recorded once. At the Ossuary Line it declined to be sealed while carrying, and the answer to that was not force. Hae-ryu held the circle open for two days and let it finish. The Arbitration Division's written position is that this was a failure of control. The Aperture household's written position is that the Division has misunderstood which party the control was ever supposed to run toward |
| **Permanence Hazard** | It stops being a summon at the moment the cord finishes. If every arrested carriage on that rope were completed, the accretion would clear, the Overgrown state would resolve, and there would be nothing left tying it to the naming-right at all. It has never suggested this. Hae-ryu worked it out on her own at twenty-six and has told two people |

## X · Corruption Vector

Attraction Force answers by recognition. The Mule comes because a name was spoken by someone who was there, and it goes because the weight has been set down. Every part of it is consent expressed as freight.
The Obsession-Force inversion keeps the shape and inverts the direction of the asking. It does not wait for a name. It reads the room on arrival, using the same Weight-Sense that tells it how many dead are within four hundred paces, and it decides which of the living are close enough to finished to be worth beginning. Then it begins. The person it has chosen goes on standing, goes on talking, sometimes for a considerable while, and the carriage is already underway beneath that, and there is no interrupting it because there is no summoner who could be persuaded to interrupt it.
The lawful Mule cannot be made to strike where the dead cannot cross. The compelled version strikes anywhere and simply accrues, cord growing, weight climbing, Deadweight rising with it, and it does not experience the accumulation as failure. The lawful form carries eleven skulls as a debt. The compelled form carries them as capital.
It would also stop counting. Every account of the corrupted expression that the Aperture branch has collected, and there are four, agrees on this one detail and on nothing else.

## XI · Counterplay

On seeing it arrive, do not engage the construct. Nothing in the kit is ranged, nothing in it is fast, and everything in it requires either contact or ground. A competent opponent gives it distance and takes the road.
Of the three recurring vulnerabilities, two apply hard and one applies sideways. **Separation from the native Wellspring** is decisive: the Oblation vein feeds the gradient that The Fare runs on and the enclosure that The Cord maintains, and moving the engagement onto ground where Oblation does not reach reduces the entry to a large slow animal with a rope. **Radiant current** works specifically against Deadweight, disrupting the load path at the shoulder before the pressure column reaches the contact patch, and Arbitration teams deploy Luminalis-harmonized officers for this reason and no other. **Essence-dampening** is the sideways one and it is a trap. Dampening does not weaken a Native form the way it weakens a Donated one, and dampening applied to the road rather than the construct produces the arrest condition, which stops the carriage and adds a skull and makes the thing permanently stronger. Every opponent who has tried to win by dampening has won that engagement and lost the next one.
> **The genuine counter is neither of these.** Give it a name. Anyone present at a death may speak that name in its hearing, and it does not check whose side the speaker is on, because standing has never meant allegiance. It means presence. An enemy who watched one of his own men die can put the Mule on a carriage in the wrong direction and Hae-ryu can do nothing about it at all, and the only reason this does not happen constantly is that almost nobody believes it will work until they have seen it work.
Signature legibility applies and is unusually loud here. The cold seam and the flat sound reach further than the construct does, and the Oblation draw during a carriage is the most legible thing Hae-ryu ever does.

## XII · Attested Encounters

**Haldren Ford**, Accord Archive, Concordia field record, dated. First Accord-observed deployment. The construct declined a strike order inside a Silence Realm and stood over the withdrawal for the duration. Eleven of Hae-ryu's people were lost in the crossing. The Archive record is a clean two-page assessment concluding that the Native-Consenting exception carries an unacceptable operational risk. Kellwright company survivors, who were on the far bank and could see the whole of it, tell it differently and have never stopped: they say the eleven were lost because the Realm was laid on top of them and the Mule stood in it anyway, and they use the word *stood* the way you would use it about a person. The register does not resolve this.
**The Ossuary Line**, Accord Archive, Arbitration Division report, dated. The refusal-of-sealing incident. Three field observers, three different skull counts, all three signed as certain, all three retained in the file without reconciliation. The Division's assessment is control failure. Two of the three observers appended personal notes that were not requested and were entered anyway, and neither note mentions control.
**Yeol-gol**, household ledger, undated entry in the seventh generation hand. The uncle's name and the six hours in the doorway. The household ledger records the refusal, records who eventually spoke the name correctly, and records nothing about the uncle. Yeol-gol village tradition, which is not archival and is marked here as folk account, holds that the uncle left the seat that winter and that the doorway is a different doorway from the one the ledger means. Both readings survive locally and the households that hold each one do not eat together.
**Sum-gol**, no Accord record, no household entry. Field accounts only, four of them, from three different companies, and they agree on the sequence and disagree on the count of the dead by a factor the register declines to average. Marked as rumour. Retained because all four independently describe the construct counting aloud, and because two of them, who did not know each other, wrote down the same number.

## XIII · Open Rulings

**Kwon Hae-ryu is an origination, ruled.** Canon establishes that Mu-jin's branch of the Mahuo line lost the Aperture branch's capacity to host a soul population directly, which means the Aperture branch exists and is undescribed. Hae-ryu, the Yeol-gol seat, and the naming-right structure are all built into that documented gap rather than lifted from anything. She was a pitch. Ruled 2026-09-12: the Aperture branch is open to origination, there is no undisclosed plan for it, and Hae-ryu, the Yeol-gol seat, and the naming-right structure stand as written.
**Name built through the four passes.** *Obrenkael* is High Parunic compounding on attested glyph morphemes, no clusters that violate the register, three syllables, tested aloud beside Cozbi, Mu-jin, Sodoku, Rengai. *Kwon Hae-ryu of Yeol-gol* follows the Mahuo Korean institutional structure: lineage name, two-syllable given name with generational element, hollow-seat on the established Sum-gol pattern with a suffix that means something. *Yeol-gol*, Open Hollow, for the Aperture branch. The generational element *Hae-* stands as canon. Ruled 2026-09-12.
**EU figures unverified.** Call cost is given as 9% of reserve because the percentage is defensible and the absolute number is not. `Fracture_of_Worlds.pdf` establishes that EU scales with Temperance Stage and is governed by Tempering Yield, and gives no Band-by-Band reserve table. Mu-jin's 850,000,000 at Level 430, Stage XII is the only fixed anchor in the project. Any EU figure for a Level 355, Stage X practitioner would be an invention presented as a fact, so there is none here. Needs a reserve figure set for Hae-ryu before this entry can carry absolute numbers.
**Codex amendment pitched, not taken.** Deadweight runs [Abys] Deep and [Th] Foundation, and neither is attested at Oblation in the Master Glyph Index. [Abys] carries Abyntheus, Fulguria, Electromagnetism. [Th] carries Petralon, Vectoria, Mechanics. The proposal is to extend [Abys] Deep's attested Wellspring list to include Oblation and its attested Family list to include Fluxia, on the reasoning that a directional undertow of sub-Realm pressure is what a psychopomp's road *is*, and that Abyntheus and Oblation are both already pressure-grammar Wellsprings under different Archons. [Th] Foundation stays cross-family and Deadweight is flagged as the roster's only cross-family chain. If the answer is no, Deadweight needs rebuilding from Fluxia-attested glyphs and there are not three in the index that give a load path.
**The skull count contradiction is deliberate and needs a ruling anyway.** *Uncounted* is written as a Trait, which makes it a mechanical property rather than an atmospheric one. If Isaac wants it atmospheric, it comes out of Section II and stays in Section VI. If it stays a Trait, something eventually has to be true about the real number, and the register's position is that nothing should be.
**Unresolved against the existing roster.** Mu-jin's Book of Summons establishes his branch as the one that lost this aptitude, and The Index is described as World-Tethered-adjacent, borrowing anchor stability from a location. The Mule borrows nothing and owns its own Crystal. Those two entries sitting in the same register invite a direct comparison that has not been written. Whether Mu-jin has ever met an Aperture-branch practitioner, and what he thinks about a cousin-line that can do the thing his line was built to regret losing, is an open scene.
**Bench objection status unwritten.** The Arbitration Division objection recorded in Section I, that an entity accruing strength from its summoner's failures cannot be described as bound, is the strongest legal hook this entry has and there is no ruling behind it. The Bench of Attribution has said nothing. What it would say, and whether the Praetoria retcon changes which body is even hearing it, both need settling before this entry can claim its Accord Classification is stable.

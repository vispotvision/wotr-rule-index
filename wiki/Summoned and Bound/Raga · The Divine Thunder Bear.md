# Raga · The Divine Thunder Bear

*Divine Beast · Vocatia · Native Provenance · Bond Class Sovereign · Danger Tier T7*
> **Thunder is the world telling you how far away the harm was.** Everything Raga does is a version of that sentence, and the reason practitioners fear him has nothing to do with the lightning.
>
> *"Numera intervallum. Veniat sonus."*
> Count the interval. Let the sound come.

## V · Bond and Standing

| **Bond Class** | **Sovereign.** He negotiates terms and imposes them as the condition of answering at all |
|---|---|
| **Terms of Answering** | The summoner names the target aloud, in a voice the people standing nearby can hear, **and accepts the Arrears**. Both terms are stated once, at first recognition, and are never restated |
| **The Arrears** | **The summoner's own sensation runs late for as long as Raga is present, and for a long while after.** Pain, sound, heat, the weight of a hand on the shoulder, all of it arrives at the same interval Raga is running. One practitioner has carried a half-second delay for nine years and eats alone because of it** |
| **Refusal Conditions** | He will not strike a thing that has not been named. He will not strike from behind. **He will not strike the same target twice**, and a summoner who asks him to will find the second Draft unanswered and the first one still owed |

## VI · Physical Description

He stands two point nine metres at the shoulder on all fours and runs six point two from muzzle to haunch, and the assessors who put a figure on his mass settled on three point eight tonnes with a note attached, because a body that heavy should sink and his prints in river silt run four centimetres deep where a bull would leave thirty. The hide is black in the way a burnt thing is black, absorbing rather than reflecting, and the guard hairs stand in dense parallel ranks that shed water in sheets and never lie flat. Across the shoulders the fur thickens into a hump of muscle wider than a cart, and the forelimbs beneath it carry the heavy straight-boned architecture of an animal built to push rather than to chase.
The rack is the part that ruins people's sleep. Twelve tines minimum, more when he has been standing in weather, spanning close to five metres and branching in the pale grey of storm-stripped driftwood, ridged and knuckled along their length where new horn has pushed through old. They are not antlers in any sense a hunter would accept. They grow during an engagement and shed during one, dropping points that hiss when they hit wet ground and are cold by the time anybody dares pick one up. Every tip carries a standing corona, and the green motes that drift free of them are that corona letting go, small and slow and drifting upward through the rain in numbers that make a battlefield look briefly like a summer field at dusk.
His eyes have no sclera and no pupil. Two flat green points set deep under the brow ridge, the same green as the motes, and they do not track the way an animal's track. They arrive on a thing a moment after the head has already turned toward it. The muzzle is broad and blunt and scarred across the bridge in three parallel lines that nobody has ever explained. There is no smell of animal on him at all. Ozone, wet stone, and scorched hair, layered in that order, and the ozone reaches a formation about two seconds before he does.
He makes no sound walking. **The footfalls arrive later**, and they arrive at the place he was, and a line that has learned this stops trying to fight by ear inside the first minute.

## VII · Temperament and Voice

Raga does not speak, and the assumption that he cannot is one the Archives have declined to endorse. He communicates by arriving, by standing, and by declining to move, and the vocabulary is smaller than it sounds. A summoner learns to read the set of the head and the angle of the rack, and every summoner who has learned it reports the same three positions and no more.
What he does instead of speaking is count. The interval is audible to anyone standing inside forty-five metres, a low tick under the storm that is not quite a sound and not quite a pressure change, and it is regular, and it does not stop when he is idle. Practitioners who have worked with him describe the tick continuing in their own chest for days afterward, keeping time with nothing.
He is patient in a way that reads as indifference and is closer to arithmetic. **He is not deciding whether to strike.** He is establishing where the thing is, and the thing is a distance, and the distance resolves on its own schedule.
*The one behaviour every account agrees on: he faces the summoner before he faces the target. Every time. Nobody has recorded what he is checking for and nobody has asked twice.*

## VIII · Abilities

- ****THE COUNT** · *Signature · Marking · Delayed Delivery***
  **Glyph Basis:** [Sel] Measure · [Len] Line · [Thn] Thunder
  *"Nomina, et numera."*
  Name it, and count.
  **Trigger:** The summoner speaks the target's name aloud. Raga turns his head. There is a flash without heat, without sound, and without any visible origin, and it lands on the target and does not stay.
  **Function:** The strike happens immediately. **The consequence arrives at the speed of sound.**
  **Mechanism:** Electromagnetism. The flash is a genuine return stroke, a channel established and equalised in under two hundred microseconds, and it deposits its charge at the marked point in the instant it occurs. What Raga withholds is the acoustic event. The channel's superheated air wants to expand and produce the shock, and Orrenthal holds the resonance folded and unreleased at the point of origin until the interval has run. The count is the real distance between Raga and the mark divided by three hundred and forty three metres per second, and it is not adjustable, and Raga does not know how to make it shorter.
  **Numerical Effect:** Peak current in the 30 kA range against ordinary targets, with one attested measurement at 180 kA against a Domain-seated practitioner. Channel temperature near 28,000 K. **Interval measured between 0.29 seconds at a hundred metres and 8.7 seconds at three kilometres**, which is the engagement that gave him his soldiers' name.
  **Target Response:** Nothing, and then everything. The marked target feels the charge deposit as a cold pressure behind the sternum and has the interval to understand what is standing over them. **When the count runs out the withheld shock releases at the mark**, and the release carries the full acoustic energy of a strike that has been holding it in one place for several seconds. Bodies inside two metres come apart along the wet lines. Armour survives and the man inside does not.
  **Limitation:** The consequence lands where the mark is, and the mark travels with the target. **It cannot be made to land early, cannot be made to land elsewhere, and cannot be cancelled by Raga once the flash has gone.**
  **Weakness:** The interval tells a competent opponent exactly how far away Raga is, to within a few metres, before he has been seen. **Every use of The Count is a range report given free to the enemy.**
  **Cost:** Attraction-category, carried by the summoner. Each Count deepens the Arrears by a fraction that does not reset between engagements.
  **Vow:** The target must be named aloud by a voice Raga recognises. **An unnamed target cannot be marked**, and a wrongly named one is marked and then abandoned.
  **Counterplay:** The charge is deposited on the body, and the body can be put somewhere the release cannot follow usefully. **Full immersion, deep water or river silt, bleeds the deposit to ground before the interval runs.** A Voidic practitioner devours it outright. Failing either, distance is a friend: the further the mark from Raga, the longer the interval, and eight seconds is enough time for allies to drag a man clear and enough time for him to walk out of the blast radius himself if somebody tells him which way.
  **Codex Assignment:** [Sel] · [Len] · [Thn] · Primary Judicium · Secondary Orrenthal · Fulguria · Electromagnetism
  **First Display:** Low stakes. Raga marks a standing stone at fifty paces during a demonstration and the assembled Proctors watch nothing happen for a fifth of a second before the stone opens. **The rule is taught in one beat and never needs teaching again.**
  **Sensory Description:** The flash has no colour anyone agrees on and leaves no afterimage. The summoner's mouth is still open on the last syllable of the name. Raga's rack sheds two tines and the motes come off them in a rush, drifting up through the rain, and under everything the tick keeps time.
  **Interactions:** Anything that pins a target in place converts The Count from a warning into an execution. **This is the entire reason a Sovereign-bond Divine Beast has ever been fielded alongside line infantry.**
  **Corruption Vector:** An Obsession-Force Count does not wait for a name. It marks the nearest living thing, and it marks again, and the intervals begin to overlap until a field has more counts running in it than people.
- ****THE STANDING CHARGE** · *Area Denial · Protective · Immobile***
  **Glyph Basis:** [Krn] Crown · [RenB] Boundary · [Ur] Balance
  *"Ad me ictus omnes."*
  Every stroke to me.
  **Trigger:** Raga lowers the head until the rack is the highest standing structure in the field and plants all four limbs. **He does not move again until he releases it.**
  **Function:** Every discharge in the area terminates on Raga and on nothing else.
  **Mechanism:** Electromagnetism, and specifically the geometry of the striking distance. The rack presents an overwhelming upward streamer at a potential no other object in the field can match, and every descending leader within the protective radius commits to it before it can commit to anything else. **Judicium supplies the part that is not weather.** Practitioner discharges, inscribed lightning, Fulguria-family techniques of any kind, all of them resolve their path against Raga's rack in preference to their intended target, because the Wellspring reads him as the correct terminus and the technique is obliged to agree.
  **Numerical Effect:** Protective radius of 45 m from the outermost tine, consistent with a 30 kA striking distance, expanding toward 70 m as accumulated charge on the rack rises. **Sustained across the longest attested hold, thirty one minutes**, during which the rack shed nine tines and regrew four.
  **Target Response:** A practitioner watching his own technique curve away from a target and terminate on an animal's antlers experiences it as a failure of his own work rather than as an interception. **The Arbitration filings record three separate accusations of sabotage from casters who did not understand what had happened to them.**
  **Limitation:** Raga cannot move while holding it, cannot use The Count while holding it, and **cannot exclude anyone from it.** The enemy's discharges and the allies' resolve to him identically.
  **Weakness:** He is a stationary four-tonne target with his head down. **Anything that is not electrical goes through him without impediment**, and a competent line understands within thirty seconds that the answer is a spear.
  **Cost:** Physical, carried by Raga rather than the summoner, and the only cost in the entry that works that way. **Every hold ages the rack.** The regrowth is never complete and the shed points do not come back in the same arrangement.
  **Counterplay:** Bring something that is not lightning. **Failing that, overload him:** sustained Fulguria input past his Shell's venting rate forces the accumulated charge back down through the body, and two engagements have ended with Raga discharging into the ground beneath his own feet and standing in the resulting crater with his hide smoking.
  **Codex Assignment:** [Krn] · [RenB] · [Ur] · Primary Judicium · Secondary Fixatio · Fulguria · Electromagnetism
  **First Display:** He does it unasked, over a wounded man, during a storm that had nothing to do with the engagement. **Nobody orders it and nobody can make him do it on request afterward.**
  **Sensory Description:** The hair stands on every arm in the field. The rain begins falling upward within the radius, drawn to the tines in fine threads. **The tick speeds up.**
- ****REPORT** · *Charge · Overpressure · Cross-Family***
  **Glyph Basis:** [Thn] Thunder · [Noq] Wind · [Uur] Disruption
  *"Sonus advenit."*
  The sound arrives.
  **Trigger:** Contact. Raga runs, and the running is silent, and the sound of the running arrives afterward.
  **Function:** A bear's charge that delivers a blast wave instead of an impact.
  **Mechanism:** **Thermodynamics.** This block departs from the Electromagnetism domain the rest of the entry runs on, and the departure is deliberate and Codex-supported: [Thn] Thunder is assigned to Cataclysm under Caloria, and Report is the one technique in Raga's kit that is genuinely a heat event. The charge accumulated across the hide superheats the air in the compression layer ahead of his mass, and the layer detonates on contact rather than deforming. **The strike is the expansion, and the body he hits is standing inside it.**
  **Numerical Effect:** Overpressure of 40 to 60 kPa within two metres of contact. **Tympanic rupture begins near 35 kPa and pulmonary injury near 70**, which places every target of a clean Report in the band where the ears go and the lungs are a coin toss. Closing speed measured at 14 m/s over the last twenty metres.
  **Target Response:** Mail and brigandine transmit the overpressure into the torso intact. **Plate performs worse**, because the cuirass rings and the ringing is inside the man. Survivors bleed from the ears for a week and describe the world afterward as being underwater. The dead are intact and wrong.
  **Limitation:** Requires a run-up of at least twenty metres. **It cannot be delivered from a standing position** and it cannot be steered once committed.
  **Weakness:** The compression layer is visible for the last half second as a distortion in the rain, and it announces the contact point. **A man who has seen one Report can time the second one.**
  **Cost:** Physical. The detonation happens against Raga as much as against the target, and he carries the deceleration through the forelimbs. **Attested consequence: after four Reports in one engagement he stopped putting weight on the left fore, and he still favours it.**
  **Counterplay:** Break the run-up. **Broken ground, caltrops, a pike hedge, anything that makes twenty metres of clean approach unavailable.** Failing that, get to ground: overpressure falls off sharply with distance and prone bodies inside four metres have survived where standing ones inside eight did not.
  **Codex Assignment:** [Thn] · [Noq] · [Uur] · Primary Cataclysm · Secondary Orrenthal · Caloria primary with Fulguria trigger · Thermodynamics. **Flagged as a deliberate cross-family entry, consistent with the Codex's own assignment of [Thn]**
  **First Display:** Against a wall rather than a person. He puts a gate through its own gatehouse and the garrison surrenders to a summoner who had not yet decided to ask them.
  **Sensory Description:** Silence, and then the shape of him already too close, and then the sound of the last four seconds of running arriving all at once behind the blast.
- ****WHAT IS NOT NAMED** · *Passive · Vow · The Refusal***
  **Glyph Basis:** [Vo] Voice · [Va] Law
  *"Quod non nominatur, non percutitur."*
  What is not named is not struck.
  **Trigger:** Permanent. It cannot be switched off and Raga has never been observed attempting to.
  **Function:** Raga is incapable of harming anything that has not been named aloud in his hearing by a voice he recognises.
  **Mechanism:** Electromagnetism through Judicium, with the spoken-fate mechanics of [Vo] doing the binding. The naming is a genuine Draft operation compressed into a single word, and **the target's designation is written into the Continuum's local accounting before any charge moves.** An unnamed body is not protected. It is simply not addressed, and nothing Raga does can find it.
  **Numerical Effect:** No figure. **This is the one field in the entry where a number would be a lie.**
  **Target Response:** Enemy formations that work this out send unnamed men forward and the tactic works exactly once per summoner, since the second engagement has the summoner naming a rank rather than a man. **Whether a rank counts as a name is the live question, and the answer so far has been sometimes.**
  **Limitation:** Structural and absolute. **Raga cannot be talked around it, cannot be angered past it, and has stood over an unnamed man who was actively killing his summoner without intervening.**
  **Weakness:** It makes the summoner audible. **A voice that must speak a name aloud in a battle line is a voice that can be located, and three attempts on summoners have been made on that basis.**
  **Cost:** Borne entirely by the summoner, in the currency of having to decide out loud, in front of witnesses, who is going to die. **The Arrears is the physical cost of the bond. This is the other one, and practitioners report it as the heavier of the two.**
  **Vow:** The whole technique is the vow. Forfeiting it is not available to either party.
  **Counterplay:** Go unnamed. **Kill the voice.**
  **Ruled 2026-09-12:** the Raga delivery-method question (spoken or poured) is answered -- spoken. Nothing in this entry ever supported a poured mechanism; the counter runs entirely on the voice the Invocation, the naming, and the stated Counterplay already require. Kaalabad destroys the construct by killing the voice: closing the distance on Kwon Mu-jin fast enough, and violently enough, that the naming itself is interrupted mid-word -- a struck throat, a broken jaw, anything that stops the sound before the target is named -- rather than by attacking Raga directly, who by his own entry cannot be harmed by ordinary means and does not need to be. An unnamed target is not protected from Raga; it is simply not addressed, and a naming interrupted mid-word is functionally the same as no naming at all.
  **Codex Assignment:** [Vo] · [Va] · Primary Judicium · Secondary Thaloré · Fulguria primary with Fluxia secondary · Electromagnetism. **Cross-family flagged: [Vo] is a Thaloré assignment under Fluxia and is doing the binding work here rather than the striking work**
  **First Display:** Before any combat at all. A summoner points and Raga does not move, and the summoner has to say the name, and the saying of it in front of his own men is the scene.
  **Sensory Description:** Raga faces the summoner. The tick continues. **Nothing else happens, for as long as it takes.**

## IX · Dissolution and Failure

| **Clean Sealing** | [Vor] Return, spoken once. **He turns, walks four or five paces, and is not there for the sixth.** The motes hang in the air for a further two minutes and the ozone lasts an hour |
|---|---|
| **Severed Tether** | No tether exists to sever. **Killing the summoner does not banish him.** He completes any running count, faces the body, and leaves on his own schedule |
| **Unsealed Departure** | A summoner who omits [Vor] finds the Arrears does not decay. **It becomes the practitioner's permanent baseline**, and the nine-year case in section V is an unsealed departure and nothing more complicated than that |
| **Behaviour in Silence** | Harmonic Silence does not remove him. **It removes the interval**, and a Raga whose count has been silenced strikes without delay and without warning, which two Sealwrights have now learned is the worse outcome. Standing Enforcement guidance: **do not silence the bear** |
| **Resistance to Dissolution** | Not applicable in the ordinary sense. He is Native and departs by choice. **The register notes that no summoner has ever tested what happens if the choice and the sealing disagree** |
| **Permanence Hazard** | He is already permanent. **The hazard runs the other way**: a summoner storm-anchored past four days stops being able to distinguish the Arrears from his own sense of time |

## X · Corruption Vector

Attraction Force answers by recognition. Obsession Force takes the same shape and compels, and the compelled Raga is documented once, in a filing the Bench sealed and the Measurewrights have petitioned twice to reopen.
**The interval inverts.** A compelled Raga does not withhold the consequence and let it arrive. He withholds the *cause*. The thunder comes first, at full weight, on a target that has not been struck by anything and will not be for several seconds, and the target hears their own death arrive and then stands in the interval waiting for the flash that will justify it. **Nobody named survives the wait.** Two in the filing tried to run and the mark went with them, and the account of what the running looked like is the reason the filing is sealed.
The second inversion is quieter and is the one the Arbitration Division actually fears. **A compelled Raga stops needing the name.** [Vo] is doing the binding under Thaloré, and Thaloré is a Fluxia current, and a Fluxia current that has been forced rather than asked does not hold a boundary. The vow goes first. Everything Raga is, is downstream of a refusal, and a Raga who cannot refuse is a weather system with a grudge and forty-five metres of reach.

## XI · Counterplay

**Water.** The single most reliable answer in the entry, and it works against The Count directly by bleeding the deposit to ground before the interval runs. Rivers, flooded ground, deep silt. **The image most often attached to him has him standing in shallow water and that is not an aesthetic choice on anyone's part.**
**Distance is inverted.** Every other T7 in the register is more dangerous close and less dangerous far. Raga is the opposite. **The further out the mark, the longer the interval, and the interval is survival time.** A formation that closes on him is choosing to make his strikes instantaneous.
**Bring something that is not electrical.** The Standing Charge is the whole of his defensive game and it is blind to steel.
**Voidic suppression works and is not worth what it costs.** A Class VI practitioner devours the deposit outright. The same practitioner is a stationary artillery piece requiring a guard detachment, and Raga does not need to strike a man who cannot leave.
**And the answer nobody writes down.** He will not strike from behind, he will not strike twice, and he will not strike the unnamed. **Three of his four abilities have a counterplay that consists of understanding him.** The Accord's own field guidance omits this on the grounds that it is unreliable, which is true, and the Measurewrights' dissent notes that it is unreliable in the same way a law is unreliable.

## XII · Attested Encounters

**Nine Seconds.** Archives hold the measurement and not the engagement. A mark at eight point seven seconds, which places the flash a little under three kilometres from where Raga was standing, and a summoner who named a target he could not see on somebody else's report. **The Archives do not record whether the naming was correct.** The line that was present has named him for it ever since, and the naming is not affectionate.
**The Gatehouse.** Report against a wall rather than a person. The garrison surrendered before terms were offered and the filing lists the engagement as a bloodless success, which is accurate and is also the reason the Enforcement Division keeps citing it.
**The Forty Minutes.** Fourth recorded refusal. A wrongly named target, and Raga standing in the open until his presence expired. **Field account, not Archive.** The survivors are consistent on the detail that he faced the summoner the entire time.
**The Crater at Halvedge.** An attempt to anchor him to prepared iron. Folk account holds that the smith who forged the anchor was still alive when the Draft was spoken. **Archives hold the crater's dimensions and nothing else.**

# Bloodbind Surge

## Bloodbind Surge

### Summary card

**Effect** · **He tears open the Vorynn Bloodbind and the surge triples what his body can put out for ten seconds.** *Reflexes sharpen with it. Nothing about the surge is selective.*
**Cost** · **Roughly seventy percent of his active Essence reserve per minute sustained — which is why it is measured in seconds and not minutes.**
**Limit** · **Extended or repeated use raises Primal Regression risk.** *The same uncontrolled metamorphosis into a Prime Beast form the Bloodbind already carries as its standing cost.*
**Counter** · **A fire-dominant Caloria Wellspring contests the surge directly; a Fixatio-built cage denies the muscular expression room to happen; a Harmonics-suppression working dulls the predatory sharpening before it compounds.**
**What nobody knows** · Whether the Bloodbind was ever meant to be reopened deliberately, or only survived into a technique because Draven found it could be.

### Design Chain

**Trigger** · Draven deliberately tears open the Vorynn Bloodbind, the ancestral pact scarred into his blood.
**Function** · Buff, high-risk burst, self-target.
**Mechanism** · The Bloodbind is a standing Trait rather than a Wellspring; tearing it open floods his Aether Shell with raw predatory charge faster than Petralon's kinetic thread can meter it, an uncosted spike rather than a governed draw. Vohrin rides the same spike — his sheet already documents Vohrin and Cinerion compounding along the same Family axis, denser than Ardency alone accounts for — and the surge pulls that compounding effect outward as visible frost on his own scars.
**Numerical Effect** · Strike Force triples for the duration, pushing his own A-Grade Ardency baseline (20–100 GN peak, 46 GJ–4 TJ yield, per the FOW Physical Benchmarks table) toward the top of that band or briefly past it, an Overchannel-range spike rather than sustained output.
**Target Response** · Self only; reflexes sharpen to a predatory register alongside the raw output.
**Consequence** · Eyes turn glacier-white, frost forms on old scars, a low growl runs under his breath.
**Limitation** · The surge cannot be metered finely once torn open; it runs its full ten seconds or it does not run at all.
**Weakness** · A strong opposing fire-dominant Caloria discharge burns out the surge's cold expression early, though the raw kinetic strength persists a moment longer.
**Cost** · Roughly 70% of his active Essence reserve per minute sustained.
**Counterplay** · A fire-dominant Caloria Wellspring (heat-side) contests the surge directly; a Fixatio-built binding cage denies the muscular expression room to happen; a Harmonics-suppression working dulls the predatory sharpening before it compounds.

#### 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** He tears open an old pact written into his blood and
for ten seconds his body puts out three times what it can put out.
**The real phenomenon.** Two, stacked, and only the second makes it survivable.
*Disinhibition of the neural ceiling.* Voluntary muscular force is not limited by
what the fibres can produce. It is limited by how many motor units the nervous
system will recruit and by the autonomic reserve it protects. Twitch-interpolation
studies put trained voluntary activation at **90–95 %** of maximal, leaving a real
but small reserve of **5–15 %** that electrical superimposition can reveal.
Catecholamine surge adds potentiation on top. Together, that is the documented
mechanism behind the "hysterical strength" literature.
**The arithmetic refuses the page's figure.** Disinhibition buys 5–15 %. The page
claims **×3**. There is no neural route to 300 %, so the tripling has to come from
the tissue exceeding its own material limits — and there, the numbers are grim.
Skeletal muscle specific tension runs **20–35 N/cm²**. Human Achilles tendon fails
in the **4–6 kN** range, and in-vivo sprinting already loads it to roughly **9 kN**,
at or past that figure. Triple a peak sprint load and you are asking for **≈27 kN**
through a structure that parts at six.
*Which is where the second phenomenon arrives.* **Load-path conversion — the arch.**
Masonry carries enormous load in compression and almost none in tension, and an arch
exists to turn a span into a queue of compressive members. Bone answers the same way:
cortical bone takes roughly **170–200 MPa** in compression against **120–130 MPa**
in tension. **Route the tripled load into compression and the structure that would
part in tension holds.** That is Petralon's register mechanism verbatim, and it is
the only reason a ×3 spike is not immediately fatal.
**The governing law.** Force capacity is set by material strength along the loaded
axis; a structure's strength is axis-dependent; therefore changing the axis a load
travels changes what the structure can survive without changing the material. The
price, which Petralon's register also states, is **creep** — slow permanent
deformation accepted in exchange for never developing a tensile crack.
**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Duration: **10 s** (page).
- Baseline A-Grade output the page cites: **20–100 GN** peak force,
**4.6×10¹⁰ – 4.184×10¹² J** yield. This matches Part Four's A-Grade row exactly
(4.6024×10¹⁰ – 4.184×10¹² J) — **the page's benchmark citation is correct.**
- Tripled: **1.4×10¹¹ – 1.26×10¹³ J**. The upper end crosses into **S-Grade**
(4.184×10¹² – 2.42672×10¹³ J).
- Tendon load ratio: **≈27 kN** demanded against **4–6 kN** tensile failure — a
**4.5–6.75×** overload, resolved only by moving the load into compression.
- Bone strength asymmetry exploited: **170 MPa compressive / 125 MPa tensile ≈ 1.36×**.
**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** Not in his muscles. Even at the
low end, 4.6×10¹⁰ J is eleven tonnes of TNT and no metabolism produces it; the
system's own answer applies without strain — "The practitioner does not supply the
joules. They supply the boundary condition"
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Physical Account Two Sets of Books.md` §III). The joules
are ambient. What he supplies is a torn limiter, and what breaks is structure: "The
energy was in the room. The structure that let it move in that particular direction
was in the person, and **structure is what fails.**"
**Precedent we are not copying.** Limit-breaking transformations are the genre's
oldest engine — Dragon Ball's Kaioken, Naruto's Eight Gates, Berserk's berserker
armour. Those cost health as a flat tax afterward. This one costs **reserve at a
stated rate while it runs**, and the rate is what makes it a ten-second technique
rather than a one-minute one. The divergence is that the limit is arithmetic and the
page did the arithmetic.

#### 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** Draw is on his own frame and the immediate air — the smallest
footprint of Draven's four workings. Vectoria favours "Structural mass, elevation,
brittle or pre-stressed material" and is suppressed by "Loose sediment, free fall,
uniform soft ground" (`The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings`, Environmental
Coupling) — **and the suppression list is the counterplay:** an arch needs an
abutment, so a Petralon-routed surge on loose sand has nowhere to send the
compression. **Residue:** frost on old scars, which is residue landing on Trait
tissue rather than on ground. **Saturation:** not a field limit; the saturating
reservoir is his own, at 70 % per minute.
**Wellspring stratum.** **Petralon · *The Foundation Stone*** (primary), Family
**Vectoria**, Physics Domain **mechanics**. Its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Vectoria — Mechanics.md`:
> "Masonry is enormously strong in compression and nearly worthless in tension,
> which is why the arch exists: it converts a span into a queue of compressive
> members. Petralon reorganises the Crystal along the same principle, routing every
> incoming load into compression and accepting slow creep deformation as the price of
> never developing a tensile crack."
Register Stat Effect, through the Merge Ledger: Vitality **Constitution** (from
Endurance), Dominion **Gravity**. Register failure: "**Tensile exposure.** Load the
structure in the one direction it does not answer and it does not deform gracefully.
It parts."
**Secondary: Vohrin — and Vohrin is not one of the Sixty.** The Caloria register
lists ten currents (Pyreveil, Exuroth, Ignivale, Cinerion, Solfatara, Manganthra,
Cataclysm, Pyraeon, Catharsis, Penanceflare) and Vohrin is not among them. Vohrin
appears in the system as **a Titan** — "VOHRIN · *The Titan of Niflheim* · Abyssal
Depths · Entropy's peace, the settling of endings"
(`wiki/Cosmology & Metaphysics/The Fourteen Titans.md`). Draven's own card carries
the same assignment ("Caloria / Thermodynamics / **Vohrin primary** / Stage VI"), so
card and technique page agree with each other and both disagree with the register and
the Titans page. **Recorded, not resolved.** Logged `SA-UNATT-VOHRIN`, the batch's
largest single conflict: it touches four of these twenty-eight pages and one card.
**The Trait, not a Wellspring.** The page is careful and correct: the surge is driven
"through the **Vorynn Bloodbind** Trait, not a Wellspring itself." The system
distinguishes the three cleanly — "**A harmonization retunes the conduit** … **A
Trait biases the conduit permanently.** It is a law the soul itself carries, held in
the Essence Core, and it writes into the Shell's conductivity map rather than
borrowing a current's. **It cannot be dropped to make room**"
(`The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings`). Everything about this working's cost
follows from that last clause.
*Four Theories.* **The Debt Position**, and this page is its exhibit: "The Wellspring
answers on credit and the account is settled somewhere the practitioner cannot see.
Held mostly in the eastern quarter and by the older rendering families, and
impossible to test, and **disturbingly good at predicting which practitioners come
apart**" (`Two Sets of Books` §IV). An ancestral pact scarred into blood, reopened,
with Primal Regression as the settlement, is the Debt Position with a mechanism. The
Accord licenses it under Consent. **Noted, not resolved.**
**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Essence Core** holds the Trait; the
**Aether Shell** is what floods. Shell failure states are "Clouding or Rupture"
(`The Core Vocabulary` §III) and the page describes the run-up to Rupture. The
relevant Trait hazard is named in the system and not on the page:
**Shear Break** — "A Trait collapsing, inverting, or turning on its bearer after the
bearer has repeatedly denied the truth that formed it" — and **Overgrowth** — "A
Trait expanding past the soul's capacity to guide it. Bodily mutation, Essence
warping … The warning sign is a Trait acting when the bearer did not will it"
(`The Core Vocabulary` §VI). **Primal Regression is Overgrowth by another name**, and
the page's own Limit ("Extended or repeated use raises Primal Regression risk") is
the system's warning sign stated as a probability. Developmental Tier at Stage VI:
**Harmonic**. Crystal State: **Refined** (card). **The invoice:** 70 % of active
reserve per minute sustained, which is the only rate-of-spend figure on any of
Draven's four pages and the reason this account can compute a Starvation margin at
all.
**The lens: Bergson's *élan vital*.** *L'Évolution créatrice* (1907). Bergson
argued that life is a single indivisible impulse, and that the intellect can
only understand it by **spatialising** it after the fact — laying a continuous
surge out as a series of measured positions, and losing the surge in the
measuring. Whatever one thinks of it as biology, it is an unusually exact
description of what the page says happens: tearing the Bloodbind floods the
Shell *"faster than Petralon's kinetic thread can meter it"*, and Petralon's
thread is the metering faculty — the intellect arriving late to a movement that
has already happened. The surge cannot be metered finely once open because
metering is the thing it outran. The lens also predicts the failure the page
records as its standing cost: an impulse that is not divided is not steerable,
and **Primal Regression** is what an unsteered impulse produces when it finishes
arriving. The dangerous misreading is the obvious one. A school that believes
élan vital is inexhaustible — Bergson's *durée* has no reservoir — will read the
seventy-percent-per-minute bill as a failure of will rather than a figure, and
will train men to hold the surge longer. The system's own answer to that school
is Essence Starvation, which arrives at ten percent reserve and does not
negotiate.

#### 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph: none, and that is the correct answer.** This is the one page in the batch
whose boundary is **not** glyph-supplied. The system's three-way distinction is
explicit — a harmonization retunes, a Trait biases permanently, and "**A glyph is a
boundary condition.** It creates nothing"
(`The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings`). Here the boundary that moves is a
Trait's own metering, torn rather than written, and no form in the Master Glyph Index
is doing any work. **Whether a Trait-driven working takes a Codex glyph assignment at
all is a question the system does not answer**, and it is a real one for the
questionnaire: the Index's Standing Note says "Every technique entry in the wiki
closes on a Codex assignment line," which this working cannot honestly supply. Logged
`SA-GAP-TRAIT-WORKING-GLYPH`.
**What boundary moves.** One quantity: **the rate at which Petralon's kinetic thread
is allowed to meter charge into the Shell.** The page says this precisely — "faster
than Petralon's kinetic thread can meter it, an uncosted spike rather than a governed
draw." The limiter is not removed. It is **outrun**.
**What the law then does on its own.** Everything, and it cannot be modulated, which
is the page's own Limitation: "The surge cannot be metered finely once torn open; it
runs its full ten seconds or it does not run at all." A limiter that has been outrun
is not a dial. Compression routing holds his skeleton together because Petralon
routes load into compression; creep accumulates because that is Petralon's stated
price; the cold expresses outward because the secondary current rides the same spike.
None of it is chosen after the tear.
**Law V check.** Stage VI sits below Refraction, so an external anchor is required
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). The page supplies **blood** — the most
expensive of the four named anchors and, here, literally the pact's substrate.
**The gate closes cleanly.**
**Failure mode — Structural fault, with the ceiling breach as its trigger.** From the
Four Faults: "The Crystal could not hold what the boundary permitted. Overchannel,
Backlash, Fracture" (`Two Sets of Books` §VII). And the system gives the trigger a
rule: tripling an A-Grade output pushes the top of the band **past S-Grade entry**,
and **Stage VI has no instability zone** — the zones sit at Stages V, VII, IX and XI
only (Part Five). So a Stage VI practitioner exceeding A-Grade is not in an
instability band; he is past the ceiling itself, where "Stats pushed above their
sustainable grade, into the instability zone or past the ceiling itself … generate
**Crystal Fracture Events at an exponentially increasing rate** until the stat
collapses or the Threshold is passed through crisis" (Part Five).
**That is Primal Regression, derived.** The page calls it a Trait risk; the system
makes it an arithmetic certainty on repetition. The two agree and neither needed the
other.
The page's "What nobody knows" — whether the Bloodbind was ever meant to be reopened
— has no mechanism-level answer and should not be given one. It is a question about
intent, and the system does not record intent for Traits: a Trait is "Survived into
existence, never taught" (`The Core Vocabulary` §VI).
**What the target sees and feels.** He is hit three times as hard as the man in
front of him should be able to hit, and he has about ten seconds of it. The
working has no reach, no projection and no area: everything it does arrives
through Draven's own limbs, so the whole of it is felt at contact and nowhere
else. What an opponent reads first is not strength but **sharpening** — the
reflexes come up with the output and the register goes predatory, so the feints
that worked in the first exchange stop working. The tell is on his skin: he tears
the Bloodbind open himself, and **visible frost forms on his own scars** as the
Vohrin compounding pulls outward, which is the one part of the working a
bystander can see before the first blow lands. And the surge cannot be throttled
once it is running — *"it runs its full ten seconds or it does not run at all"* —
so the ten seconds are a clock an opponent can count, out loud, while giving
ground.
**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** η ≈ 0.55 (card): **45 percent of every
expenditure leaves as heat, sound and structural bleed.** For a ten-second spike at
tripled output that is an enormous absolute bleed, and it is the physical reason the
page's visible signature is what it is — glacier-white eyes, frost on scars, a growl
under the breath. He is loud, warm and easy to find, exactly as
`Two Sets of Books` §III predicts of a practitioner at this η. Tier 5 · Expert band
is **0.60–0.70** (Part Nineteen as corrected by **R44-4**); 0.55 sits **0.05 below the
floor**, which sharpens the §III reading rather than softening it. Recorded as `C-062`,
**not resolved here.**

#### 5 · Counterplay and the challenge

**The fairness check** (`.claude/skills/wotr-write/references/fair-play.md`).
| Test | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Costs something that hurts | **pass — the best-costed page in the batch** | *"Roughly seventy percent of his active Essence reserve per minute sustained."* This is the only cost in the twenty-eight stated as a **fraction of reserve**, which is the one form the system can actually price: Starvation begins below 10 % EU (`Core Vocabulary` §V), so a ten-second surge is ≈11.7 % of reserve and **six surges put him into Starvation inside a single fight**, with no reserve figure needed. Plus the standing Primal Regression risk on repeat use. |
| Stated limits | **pass** | 10 s, fixed; self-target only; no reach; **cannot be metered once torn open**; Primal Regression risk rises with extended or repeated use. |
| Something beats it | **pass** | Three named routes, all below his Stage. |
| It has a tell | **pass** | He tears the pact open by hand and frost forms on his scars. |
| Numbers in band | **FAIL** | The page pushes his A-Grade Ardency baseline *"toward the top of that band **or briefly past it**."* Stage VI's Max Grade is A with a Sub-Stat ceiling of 400 and** no instability zone at this Stage **(Part Five) — Stage V and VII have documented over-ceiling strain bands, Stage VI does not. So the system provides no rule under which a Stage VI practitioner reads above A, and "briefly past it" is unsupported rather than merely generous. Logged `SA-FAIR-BLOODBIND-PAST-BAND`.** Not silently nerfed** — the questionnaire decides. |

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).
- **Deny the field — the Family, twice over.** Families **Vectoria / Caloria**.
The register: *"Caloria fails in thermal equilibrium, saturated cold, and
oxygen-poor air"*, and the page's own Weakness is a fire-dominant Caloria
discharge burning out the surge's cold expression early — *"though the raw
kinetic strength persists a moment longer"*, which is an honest partial and
worth reading twice before relying on it.
- **Break the boundary — a cage, not a shield.** A Fixatio-built binding denies
the **muscular expression room to happen**. This is the correct shape of
counter against a working that has no projection: do not stop the Essence, stop
the limb. Stage VI is below Refraction, so the anchor route is open as well —
the pact is torn open by hand.
- **Break the man — the clock and the pattern.** Ten seconds, unthrottleable,
and *"every thaumaturge has a pattern."* Giving ground for ten seconds is a
plan; the register warns that *"a plan that consists of surviving until he
tires is a plan to be killed by a tired man"*, and the distinction here is that
the duration is **stated and short**, so this is not waiting for a cost, it is
waiting out a stated window. A Harmonics-suppression working dulls the
predatory sharpening before it compounds, which is the half of the surge that
makes him hard to read.
- **Break the body — normally.** Nothing in the surge changes his Stage, his
armour or his reach. Triple Strike Force does not outrun a volley.
**The tell, stated plainly.** He tears the Bloodbind open — a deliberate,
visible act on his own body — and frost forms on his scars. Ten seconds start
there and cannot be extended or cut short.

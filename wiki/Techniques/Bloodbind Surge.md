# Bloodbind Surge

## Bloodbind Surge

> **Migration note.** Converted from a Trello ability card for **Draven Kael Vorrick, The Beast Slayer** (verified against his Volume IV character card). "Primary Wellspring: Vorynn Primal" is corrected: **Vorynn is attested only as Draven's Trait**, the **Vorynn Bloodbind** — his sheet's own line reads "through an ancestral pact, his blood can ignite his muscles with primal, predatory force," an almost word-for-word match for this card's "tears open his Vorynn bloodbinding, igniting raw predatory Aether through every muscle." It is not a Wellspring, so the technique's Codex line instead names the Wellsprings that actually carry the surge: **Petralon** (kinetic ignition, attested) and **Crymorath** (cold-law bleed-through, attested — his sheet notes Crymorath and Cinerion compound along the same Family axis, which is why "Cold-Law exudes from skin" reads correctly). "Aether Class: Primal-Kinetic" converts to his attested **Class III, Resonant**. "Path: Beast-Law" is not one of the Four Paths and survives only as flavor; the technique gates on Body. "Stage VI Glory" matches his attested Catalyst Stage. "10 seconds" and "2 turns" are both given on the source card for the same duration, which is what establishes the 5-seconds-per-turn rate used to convert duration across this entire batch. "Heat-Law," an old-system term, converts to naming the actual opposing family: a fire-dominant Caloria discharge. "Purity-law cages" and "emotional null fields," neither an attested WOTR term, convert to their nearest current equivalents — a **Fixatio**-built binding cage and a **Harmonics**-suppression working — and are flagged here as the conversion choice. The Limit ("risks Prime Beast metamorphosis") is kept verbatim in substance: it is the exact Primal Regression risk already documented on Draven's own sheet as the Bloodbind's standing cost. Grade required (A) is read off Draven's own Ardency value, 320.

### Summary card

**Effect** · **He tears open the Vorynn Bloodbind and the surge triples what his body can put out for ten seconds.** *Reflexes sharpen with it. Nothing about the surge is selective.*
**Cost** · **Roughly seventy percent of his active Essence reserve per minute sustained — which is why it is measured in seconds and not minutes.**
**Limit** · **Extended or repeated use raises Primal Regression risk.** *The same uncontrolled metamorphosis into a Prime Beast form the Bloodbind already carries as its standing cost.*
**Counter** · **A fire-dominant Caloria Wellspring contests the surge directly; a Fixatio-built cage denies the muscular expression room to happen; a Harmonics-suppression working dulls the predatory sharpening before it compounds.**
**What nobody knows** · Whether the Bloodbind was ever meant to be reopened deliberately, or only survived into a technique because Draven found it could be.

### Design Chain

**Trigger** · Draven deliberately tears open the Vorynn Bloodbind, the ancestral pact scarred into his blood.
**Function** · Buff, high-risk burst, self-target.
**Mechanism** · The Bloodbind is a standing Trait rather than a Wellspring; tearing it open floods his Aether Shell with raw predatory charge faster than Petralon's kinetic thread can meter it, an uncosted spike rather than a governed draw. Crymorath rides the same spike — his sheet already documents Crymorath and Cinerion compounding along the same Family axis, denser than Ardency alone accounts for — and the surge pulls that compounding effect outward as visible frost on his own scars.
**Numerical Effect** · Strike Force triples for the duration, pushing his own A-Grade Ardency baseline (20–100 GN peak, 46 GJ–4 TJ yield, per the FOW Physical Benchmarks table) toward the top of that band or briefly past it, an Overchannel-range spike rather than sustained output.
**Target Response** · Self only; reflexes sharpen to a predatory register alongside the raw output.
**Consequence** · Eyes turn glacier-white, frost forms on old scars, a low growl runs under his breath.
**Limitation** · The surge cannot be metered finely once torn open; it runs its full ten seconds or it does not run at all.
**Weakness** · A strong opposing fire-dominant Caloria discharge burns out the surge's cold expression early, though the raw kinetic strength persists a moment longer.
**Cost** · Roughly 70% of his active Essence reserve per minute sustained.
**Counterplay** · A fire-dominant Caloria Wellspring (heat-side) contests the surge directly; a Fixatio-built binding cage denies the muscular expression room to happen; a Harmonics-suppression working dulls the predatory sharpening before it compounds.

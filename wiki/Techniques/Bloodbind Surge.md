# Bloodbind Surge

## Bloodbind Surge

### Summary card

**Effect · The practitioner tears open the Vorynn Bloodbind and the surge triples what his body can put out for ten seconds.** *Reflexes sharpen with it. Nothing about the surge is selective.*
**Cost** · **Roughly seventy percent of his active Essence reserve per minute sustained — which is why it is measured in seconds and not minutes.**
**Limit** · **Extended or repeated use raises Primal Regression risk.** *The same uncontrolled metamorphosis into a Prime Beast form the Bloodbind already carries as its standing cost.*
**Counter** · **A fire-dominant Caloria Wellspring contests the surge directly; a Fixatio-built cage denies the muscular expression room to happen; a Harmonics-suppression working dulls the predatory sharpening before it compounds.**
**What nobody knows** · Whether the Bloodbind was ever meant to be reopened deliberately, or only survived into a technique because its author found it could be.

### Origin

Self-derived by its author during the Vorynn Trials. The problem it answers is the one that author's philosophy states: closing on something briefly out of reach at a sustained baseline, accepting the Regression risk the Trait already carries as the price. "Give the beast your bones."

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · For ten seconds the body puts out three times what it can. Freeing the nervous system's ceiling on muscle recruitment buys only 5 to 15 percent; the rest comes from tissue driven past its limits and kept whole by turning every load into compression, as an arch does.
**Law** · A structure's strength differs by the axis it is loaded along: bone holds about 1.36 times more in compression than in tension, so changing the axis a load travels changes what it survives without changing the material.
**Limit** · No neural route reaches triple strength, and the price of compression routing is creep, slow permanent deformation; a load in the one direction it cannot answer makes the frame part.
**Energy** · Even the low end is about eleven tonnes of TNT, which no metabolism makes: the joules are ambient, the practitioner supplies only a torn limiter, and what breaks is structure.
| Quantity | Working | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Achilles tendon failure | reference | 4 to 6 kN |

| Cortical bone, compression over tension | 170 ÷ 125 MPa | about 1.36 × |
| Baseline output | A-Grade row | 4.6024 × 10¹⁰ to 4.184 × 10¹² J |
| Surged output | × 3 | 1.4 × 10¹¹ to 1.26 × 10¹³ J, top end in S |

### Metaphysics

**Aether** · The draw is on the practitioner's own frame and the air around it, the smallest footprint a working can have; the residue lands on the body as frost on old scars.
**Wellspring** · Petralon, the Foundation Stone (Vectoria): masonry is strong in compression and near worthless in tension, so it routes every load into compression and accepts creep as the price.
**Wellspring** · Vohrin (Caloria), one power at two levels, Titan and Wellspring, drawn here as Titan-derived: the cold expression rides the same spike, the frost and the glacier-white eyes.
**Trait** · The surge is driven through the Bloodbind, a Trait written into the Essence Core and the Shell's conductivity map; it cannot be dropped to make room, and every cost follows from that.
**Terrain** · Vectoria is favoured by structural mass, elevation and brittle or pre-stressed material; loose sediment, free fall and uniform soft ground give the arch no abutment. Caloria fails in thermal equilibrium, saturated cold and oxygen-poor air.
**Inherited failures** · Petralon's tensile exposure: loaded the one way it does not answer, the structure parts rather than bends; Vohrin carries Caloria's failures into the cold half of the surge.
**Theory** · The Debt Position: the Wellspring answers on credit and the debt is settled out of sight, here as Primal Regression; the Accord licenses the working under Consent.
**Essence** · The Essence Core holds the Trait and the Aether Shell floods, running up to Rupture. Primal Regression is Overgrowth, and its risk rises with every extended or repeated use; Shear Break is the Trait turning on its bearer. Developmental Tier Harmonic.
**School** · The vitalist philosophers read life as one undivided impulse that measurement arrives too late to steer; they hold it inexhaustible and train students to hold the surge longer, but Starvation arrives at ten percent regardless.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · None: the boundary is a Trait's own metering, torn by hand rather than written.
**Boundary** · The rate at which Petralon's kinetic thread may meter charge into the Shell; the Bloodbind floods faster than the thread can meter, so the limiter is outrun, not removed.
**Effect** · The target is hit three times as hard as this person should hit, for ten seconds, at contact only. Reflexes rise with the output, the register turns predatory, and feints that worked stop working.
**Hold** · An outrun limiter is not a dial: the surge runs its full ten seconds or not at all. Glory is below Refraction, so the anchor is blood, the dearest of the four and the pact's own substrate.
**Ceiling** · Glory's Max Grade is A and Glory has no instability zone, so a surge past A is past the ceiling itself; each surge past A risks a Crystal Fracture Event, at an exponentially rising rate on repetition. That is Primal Regression, derived.
**Failure** · Structural fault: the Crystal cannot hold what the boundary allowed and fails by overchannel, backlash and fracture; Ardency Overchannel risks overflow above D and backlash above C with no Path protecting the frame.
**Bleed** · Waste of 45 percent, 0.27 to 5.0 GW, leaves as heat at the Shell: the practitioner is loud, warm and easy to find, with frost on the scars and a growl under the breath.

**Duration** · Ten seconds, one and two-thirds turns, fixed; it cannot be extended or cut short.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · The practitioner tears the Bloodbind open by hand, a visible act on their own body, and frost forms on their scars; afterwards the frost stays and they breathe like a runner.
**Limits** · Ten seconds, self only, no reach, no projection, no throttle; nothing in it changes the practitioner's Stage, armour or reach, and triple strength does not outrun a volley.
**Fails against** · Loose or soft ground; a fire Caloria discharge burning out the cold half (a partial; the strength lasts a moment longer); a Fixatio cage on the limb; a blow to the blood anchor; ten seconds with no ground to take; Harmonics suppression dulling the sharpening.
**Look up** · The Vectoria and Caloria failure terrain; Petralon and Vohrin in the Wellspring register; Trait hazards (Overgrowth, Shear Break); Fixatio in the Counterplay register; the Glory Stage table and the Grade rows.

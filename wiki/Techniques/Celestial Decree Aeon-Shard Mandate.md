# Celestial Decree: Aeon-Shard Mandate

## Celestial Decree: Aeon-Shard Mandate

> Converted from an OPALIS ability card on the old Trello board. The card's ten-Wellspring source list, its three-outcome function (Worldrend Vector, Worldbind Bastion, Dreamveil Cascade), its line-of-sight range and variable-radius area block, and its named limits, weaknesses and counters are kept as given. "Temperance Requirement: Stage XII, Emnation" converts to FOW Stage XII, Emanation, with Band, Level range and Max Tier Grade read off the FOW Stage table. The old free-text "Aether Class: Emanant Harmonic Projection" is a caster attribute and is not reproduced as a technique field; its "world briefly obeying a new metaphysical constitution" language reads as Class V, the Radiant, offered for Opalis's own sheet rather than settled here. All ten source Wellspring names (Dissolution, Tarturon, Basilithe, Petralon, Orrenthal, Cataclysm, Penance, Oneirion, Tenebra, Fractura) and the two named anti-synergy Wellsprings (Verdantia, Hypnather) checked against the current Sixty and are attested without alteration. Dissolution is kept as primary Wellspring because the card lists it first among the ten and its "matter returning to luminous zero" gloss is the closest match to the shard's own "judgment made physical" framing. "Opalis" has no character card in the wiki mirror and is flagged unattested pending her own sheet, kept only because the technique cannot be written without a caster. Numerical Effect is read off the SSS-Grade row of the FOW Strike Force table, since the card gives no numeric force value of its own.

### Summary card

**Effect** · Opalis manifests a rotating crystalline shard built from ten Wellspring laws held in simultaneous contradiction, and the shard attacks by unmaking, defends by rebuilding, or unsettles by dissolving into dream-mist, depending only on what she intends when it awakens.
**Cost** · Sustained use exhausts the harmonics that keep her ten Wellsprings integrated, risking a partial Temperance shear or a flattening of her own affect; prolonged projection risks her own mind bleeding into the shard's judgment-logic until she is thinking in its fractal patterns rather than her own.
**Limit** · It cannot hold two contradictory shard-states at once across a wide area; destruction and restoration will not run simultaneously at scale.
**Counter** · Another Stage XII practitioner can force the shard into a contradictory harmonic state through direct law interference; pure Verdantia resists its Cataclysm and Tarturon vectors outright, and a sufficiently massive Petralon-aligned entity can simply outweigh the collapse beam.
**What nobody knows** · Whether the ten Wellsprings are genuinely held in balance inside the shard or whether one is quietly load-bearing for the other nine; nobody has caught the shard failing in a way that would tell them which.

### Design Chain

**Trigger** · Opalis raises her hand and commits her judgment to a stated outcome; light collapses inward and shadow crystallizes outward around that judgment.
**Function** · The shard restructures local reality according to her declared intent, acting as a fractal collapse vector, a crystalline bastion, or a dream-shadow distortion field, and can continue executing that intent autonomously for short intervals once awakened.
**Mechanism** · Ten Wellspring laws are phase-locked onto one lattice through Orrenthal's resonance as the reference frequency, the way an interferometer holds several light paths in one stable fringe pattern; the shard's mode is which face of that standing interference the caster reads outward, matter unwritten along the Dissolution/Tarturon face, matter rebuilt along the Basilithe/Petralon face, perception rewritten along the Oneirion/Tenebra face.
**Numerical Effect** · SSS-Grade ceiling: 1-5 PN peak force, 418 TJ to 4.184 EJ energy yield, 100 kt to 1 Tt TNT-equivalent (Large Town to Small Country scale) for the Worldrend Vector; Worldbind Bastion draws on the same ceiling as sustained stabilisation rather than released force across a 5 to 50 m radius; Dreamveil Cascade applies it as a short-range omnidirectional perceptual pulse.
**Target Response** · Matter and Essence caught in the Worldrend Vector fracture along the beam's path toward pure potential; terrain and Essence within a Worldbind Bastion radius are rebuilt and mended toward their prior lawful state; targets under Dreamveil Cascade have local causality softened and perception rewritten rather than their bodies touched.
**Consequence** · Once a mode is declared the shard cannot hold its opposite at wide scale in the same working; Opalis must close one decree before opening a contradictory one.
**Limitation** · It cannot overwrite another Stage XII practitioner's technique or Domain without direct harmonic superiority over them; it cannot create new consciousness or a living soul, only repair or dismantle Essence that already exists.
**Weakness** · Excess Oneirion exposure destabilizes the shard's own judgment-logic; too many active shards risk drifting out of her control; overusing the restorative mode risks the purification rebounding onto Opalis herself; Verdantia- or Hypnather-heavy environments distort its key functions.
**Cost** · Harmonic Drain exhausts the integrative harmonics holding her ten Wellsprings together under sustained use; Identity Bleed is the shard mirroring her inner state back at her until prolonged projection risks a genuine dissociative echo.
**Counterplay** · A fellow Stage XII being can force a contradictory harmonic state through direct law interference; pure Verdantia resists the destructive vectors; Hypnather constructs can invert the dream-logic layer; a sufficiently massive Petralon entity can absorb the collapse beam through raw mass-density resonance.

### Origin

The card names no teacher, workshop, or precipitating event beyond Opalis and the Stage XII requirement; per the named-inventor rule this makes it a self-derived technique, to be shown in full the first time it is used rather than summarised. The card's own silence on when or against what problem the Mandate was first raised is preserved as a gap rather than filled with invented history.

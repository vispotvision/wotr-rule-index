# Celestial Decree: Aeon-Shard Mandate

## Celestial Decree: Aeon-Shard Mandate

### Summary card

**Effect** · Opalis manifests a rotating crystalline shard built from ten Wellspring laws held in simultaneous contradiction, and the shard attacks by unmaking, defends by rebuilding, or unsettles by dissolving into dream-mist, depending only on what she intends when it awakens.
**Cost** · Sustained use exhausts the harmonics that keep her ten Wellsprings integrated, risking a partial Temperance shear or a flattening of her own affect; prolonged projection risks her own mind bleeding into the shard's judgment-logic until she is thinking in its fractal patterns rather than her own.
**Limit** · It cannot hold two contradictory shard-states at once across a wide area; destruction and restoration will not run simultaneously at scale.
**Counter** · Another Stage XII practitioner can force the shard into a contradictory harmonic state through direct law interference; pure Verdantia resists its Cataclysm and Tarturon vectors outright, and a sufficiently massive Petralon-aligned entity can simply outweigh the collapse beam.
**What nobody knows** · Whether the ten Wellsprings are genuinely held in balance inside the shard or whether one is quietly load-bearing for the other nine; nobody has caught the shard failing in a way that would tell them which.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · Injection locking into a frequency comb: oscillators fed one reference signal abandon their own frequency and lock to it, so ten laws held against each other stay in one stable pattern, like light paths in one interference fringe.
**Law** · Lock bandwidth follows the Adler relation, Δf*lock ≈ (f₀ / 2Q) · (A*inj / A*osc); the unmaking runs on the entropy of mixing, ΔG*mix = ΔH*mix − T·ΔS*mix, which goes downhill on its own.
**Limit** · Ten laws in opposition share no common harmonic, so the lock must be held against ten independent drifts; running mixing backward costs at least the free energy it released, so restoring is the expensive direction.
**Energy** · Unmaking draws its joules from the target's own structure and needs only initiating; rebuilding draws them from the caster because it runs uphill; the dream face touches nothing physical and costs least.

### Metaphysics

**Aether** · The draw scales with the mode: a beam, a 5 to 50 m volume, or a short pulse. The Vector leaves matter reduced toward uniformity, the Bastion ground too lawful for its history, the Cascade nothing; a Harmonist on worked ground hears ten tones where there should be one.
**Wellspring** · Dissolution, the Unbinding Stream (Limina): a mixed state has more disorder than a separated one, so dissolving runs by itself; shedding is effortless and keeping anything is active work, which is the Bastion's whole cost.
**Wellspring** · Orrenthal, the Metallic Heart: a law of shared, unowned electrons, the reference frequency the other nine lock to.
**Wellspring** · Tarturon, paired with Dissolution for matter unwritten: it supplies its own oxidiser, so the reaction cannot be smothered once begun.
**Wellspring** · Basilithe with Petralon, the Foundation Stone, for matter rebuilt: a law-lock plus a compressive load path.
**Wellspring** · Oneirion with Tenebra for perception rewritten: a configuration-space law plus signal suppression.
**Terrain** · Limina is favoured by darkness, silence, Aether-thin ground and ruins, and suppressed by saturated Aether, crowds and active Domains.
**Inherited failures** · Dissolution's indiscriminate solvency: the beam takes the load-bearing members of anything it crosses, ground under the caster included; every paired Wellspring's documented failure rides along and can be induced.
**Theory** · Filed under the Consent Doctrine, but its Identity Bleed is a Correspondence symptom: the shard is the caster's own law at a second scale, and the two keep agreeing longer than meant.
**Essence** · The Attraction Layer carries it and the Essence Core is exposed, courting Fracture into Shards. Developmental Tier Sovereign. The invoices are Harmonic Drain, ending in Shear Break, and Identity Bleed, which does not refund.
**School** · The mystical geometers call the shard a coincidence of opposites; but their own teaching holds contradictions together only at the infinite, and a finite lattice holds laws in phase, not in contradiction.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Command (Irath) laid across Frequency (Selhar): a Decree is Command first, fixed to the reference the ten are locked to.
**Boundary** · The phase relationship among ten laws, referred to one of them; no law is altered, and the mode is which face of the standing pattern the caster reads outward.
**Effect** · A rotating crystalline shard. Worldrend: matter in the beam loses its shape toward pure potential and gains no other. Worldbind: wounds and walls within range return toward their prior lawful state. Dreamveil: nothing touches the body, but events stop following one another properly.
**Modes** · One face at a time at scale, fixed when the shard wakes; destruction and restoration never run together at scale.
**Hold** · Emanation needs no anchor: a raised hand and an internalised commitment. The shard runs the stated intent for short intervals on its own, and breaking line of sight does not end it.
**Reach** · No peer overwrite without harmonic superiority; anyone at the caster's Stage or higher is immune. It cannot create a consciousness or soul, only repair or unmake what exists.
**Reference** · Every locked law inherits Orrenthal's frequency and drift; pull the reference and the shard goes dark all at once.
**Failure** · Structural: the ten-law lock is the structure, and Harmonic Drain is it failing slowly into Shear Break. Behind it a coherence fault: the caster's mind bleeds into the shard's logic, Traits argue with their bearer, and flat affect is the sign.
**Bleed** · 10 to 15 percent leaves as heat at the Shell, up to 4.2 to 6.3 × 10¹² J at the Bastion's top; the Bastion is loud, the Cascade nearly silent.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · A raised hand, light falling inward and shadow crystallising outward, and a turning shard; the first face shown is the one committed. Afterwards the ground says which: uniform, restored past its history, or untouched.
**Limits** · One face at a time at scale; no overwrite of a peer without harmonic superiority; no effect on anyone at the caster's Stage or higher; no creation of soul; Worldbind 5 to 50 m; autonomy only briefly.
**Fails against** · Crowded, Aether-rich or Domain-held ground; the end of the lock; a split crowd, which forces an open choice of destruction or restoration; a detuned reference; Silence; Knot or Lock on the ten paths; harm to the ordinary body behind the hand; none of which needs the stronger side.
**Look up** · Silence, Knot and Lock in the Counterplay register; the Limina terrain; the Dissolution, Orrenthal, Tarturon, Basilithe, Petralon, Oneirion and Tenebra failures; Shear Break and Identity Bleed; the Emanation Stage table and the SSS Grade row.

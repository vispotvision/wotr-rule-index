# Celestial Decree: Aeon-Shard Mandate

## Celestial Decree: Aeon-Shard Mandate

### Summary card

**Effect** · Opalis manifests a rotating crystalline shard built from ten Wellspring laws held in simultaneous contradiction, and the shard attacks by unmaking, defends by rebuilding, or unsettles by dissolving into dream-mist, depending only on what she intends when it awakens.
**Cost** · Sustained use exhausts the harmonics that keep her ten Wellsprings integrated, risking a partial Temperance shear or a flattening of her own affect; prolonged projection risks her own mind bleeding into the shard's judgment-logic until she is thinking in its fractal patterns rather than her own.
**Limit** · It cannot hold two contradictory shard-states at once across a wide area; destruction and restoration will not run simultaneously at scale.
**Counter** · Another Stage XII practitioner can force the shard into a contradictory harmonic state through direct law interference; pure Verdantia resists its Cataclysm and Tarturon vectors outright, and a sufficiently massive Petralon-aligned entity can simply outweigh the collapse beam.
**What nobody knows** · Whether the ten Wellsprings are genuinely held in balance inside the shard or whether one is quietly load-bearing for the other nine; nobody has caught the shard failing in a way that would tell them which.

### Codex line

- **Wellspring:** Dissolution, The Unbinding Stream (primary). Secondary harmonisations carried: Tarturon, Basilithe, Petralon, Orrenthal, Cataclysm, Penance, Oneirion, Tenebra, Fractura.
- **Family:** Limina
- **Physics Domain:** Entropy, Void and Mind
- **Category:** Domain Weaving
- **Craft:** Spellcraft
- **Stage floor:** Stage XII, Emanation (Band IV, Mythic; Level 301-400)
- **Grade required:** SSS-Grade
- **Path gate:** Attraction Path

### Design Chain

**Trigger** · Opalis raises her hand and commits her judgment to a stated outcome; light collapses inward and shadow crystallizes outward around that judgment.
**Function** · The shard restructures local reality according to her declared intent, acting as a fractal collapse vector, a crystalline bastion, or a dream-shadow distortion field, and can continue executing that intent autonomously for short intervals once awakened.
**Mechanism** · Ten Wellspring laws are phase-locked onto one lattice through Orrenthal's resonance as the reference frequency, the way an interferometer holds several light paths in one stable fringe pattern; the shard's mode is which face of that standing interference the caster reads outward, matter unwritten along the Dissolution/Tarturon face, matter rebuilt along the Basilithe/Petralon face, perception rewritten along the Oneirion/Tenebra face.
**Numerical Effect** · SSS-Grade ceiling: 1-5 PN peak force, 418 TJ to 4.184 EJ energy yield, 100 kt to 1 Tt TNT-equivalent (Large Town to Small Country scale) for the Worldrend Vector; Worldbind Bastion draws on the same ceiling as sustained stabilisation rather than released force across a 5 to 50 m radius; Dreamveil Cascade applies it as a short-range omnidirectional perceptual pulse.
**Target Response** · Matter and Essence caught in the Worldrend Vector fracture along the beam's path toward pure potential; terrain and Essence within a Worldbind Bastion radius are rebuilt and mended toward their prior lawful state; targets under Dreamveil Cascade have local causality softened and perception rewritten rather than their bodies touched.
**Consequence** · Once a mode is declared the shard cannot hold its opposite at wide scale in the same working; Opalis must close one decree before opening a contradictory one.
**Limitation** · It cannot overwrite another Stage XII practitioner's technique or Domain without direct harmonic superiority over them; it cannot create new consciousness or a living soul, only repair or dismantle Essence that already exists.
**Weakness** · Excess Oneirion exposure destabilizes the shard's own judgment-logic; too many active shards risk drifting out of her control; overusing the restorative mode risks the purification rebounding onto Opalis herself; Verdantia- or Hypnather-heavy environments distort its key functions.
**Cost** · Harmonic Drain exhausts the integrative harmonics holding her ten Wellsprings together under sustained use; Identity Bleed is the shard mirroring her inner state back at her until prolonged projection risks a genuine dissociative echo.
**Counterplay** · A fellow Stage XII being can force a contradictory harmonic state through direct law interference; pure Verdantia resists the destructive vectors; Hypnather constructs can invert the dream-logic layer; a sufficiently massive Petralon entity can absorb the collapse beam through raw mass-density resonance.

### The Two Accounts

#### 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** A rotating crystalline shard built out of ten laws held against each other, which unmakes, rebuilds, or dissolves into dream-mist depending on what she intended when it woke.
**The real phenomenon.** Two, and the page is explicit about the first.
*The shard's coherence: injection locking, and a frequency comb.* An oscillator injected with an external reference signal abandons its own free-running frequency and locks to the reference, within a bandwidth given by the **Adler relation**, Δf*lock ≈ (f₀ / 2Q) · (A*inj / A_osc). Chain enough locked oscillators to one reference and you have a comb: a mode-locked laser locks **10⁵ to 10⁶** optical modes to a single repetition rate, and every tooth inherits the reference's stability — in stabilised optical-clock combs, fractional stability at the **10⁻¹⁸** level is transferred across the whole comb. The page's image is the right one: "Ten Wellspring laws are phase-locked onto one lattice through Orrenthal's resonance as the reference frequency, the way an interferometer holds several light paths in one stable fringe pattern."
**And the comb arithmetic inverts the page's stated difficulty in a way worth putting on the record.** Ten channels on one reference is *trivial* — real combs manage a hundred thousand. **What is hard here is not the count; it is that the ten injected signals are mutually contradictory laws rather than harmonics of one another.** A comb works because every tooth is an integer multiple of one repetition rate. Ten laws "held in simultaneous contradiction" are by construction *not* commensurate, so there is no single reference they are all harmonics of, and the lock has to be maintained against ten independent drifts. **That is the real cost structure of this working and the page's "Harmonic Drain" is the correct bill for it — just for a different reason than the page gives.**
*The shard's three modes: one thermodynamic direction, run forward and backward.* Dissolution's law is the entropy of mixing, and it runs downhill on its own. Running it **backward** — the Worldbind Bastion, "rebuilt and mended toward their prior lawful state" — requires at minimum the free-energy difference it released going forward, and it is therefore the *expensive* mode, not the gentle one. **The page charges for exactly that** ("overusing the restorative mode risks the purification rebounding onto Opalis herself"), which is a thermodynamic consequence arriving as a stated cost.
**The governing law.** The Adler locking relation for the shard's integrity, and **ΔG_mix = ΔH_mix − T·ΔS_mix** for its two working directions. For an ideal mixture ΔS_mix = −nR·Σ x·ln x, maximal at equimolar composition — so the unmaking mode is most spontaneous when it has driven a structure furthest toward uniform composition, which is the mechanical reason the Worldrend Vector "fractures along the beam's path **toward pure potential**" rather than toward rubble.
**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Worldbind Bastion radius: **5–50 m** (page). Dreamveil Cascade: short-range, omnidirectional.
- Real comb capacity: **10⁵–10⁶** locked modes, against the page's ten. **The count is not the constraint.**
- Reference stability transferred: **≈10⁻¹⁸** fractional in stabilised combs — which is the physical meaning of Orrenthal holding the lattice steady.
- Stated SSS ceiling: **1–5 PN**, **418 TJ to 4.184 EJ**, "100 kt to 1 Tt TNT-equivalent." 418 TJ = 4.18×10¹⁴ J ✓ and 100 kt ✓, but **1 teraton TNT = 4.184×10²¹ J = 4.184 zettajoules, not exajoules.** Part Four's SSS row: 4.184×10¹⁴ – 4.184×10²¹ J. The page is wrong by a factor of 10³ on the upper bound.
- Free-energy asymmetry: the restorative direction costs at least the |ΔG| the destructive direction released. **No system rule gives that ratio**, and the page's "draws on the same ceiling as sustained stabilisation rather than released force" is a reasonable reading that the system cannot confirm.
**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** For the Worldrend Vector: in the target's own structure, released by making the mixed state favourable — a spontaneous process needs initiating, not funding. For the Worldbind Bastion: **in Opalis**, because running the same process backward is uphill. For the Dreamveil Cascade: almost nowhere, since it "never touches physical reality." **Three modes, three genuinely different energy accounts, and the page's own cost note tracks the difference correctly.**
**Precedent we are not copying.** Multi-mode constructs are common — Fate's Noble Phantasms, Jujutsu Kaisen's shikigami, the summoned weapon of every action RPG. Those switch modes by command. This one switches by **which face of a standing interference pattern she reads outward**, so the three modes are not three abilities but three readings of one lattice — and the reason it cannot hold two at wide scale is that an interference pattern has one normalisation. The divergence is that the mode-exclusivity is a property of the lattice, not a cooldown.

#### 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** Draw scales with the mode: a beam, a 5–50 m volume, or a short omnidirectional pulse. Limina is favoured by "Darkness, silence, Aether-thin ground, ruins" and suppressed by "Saturated Aether, crowd density, active Domains" (`The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings`, Environmental Coupling). **Saturation:** the page's own "too many active shards risk drifting out of her control" is a lock-budget limit rather than a field-saturation one.
**Wellspring stratum.** **Dissolution · *The Unbinding Stream*** (primary), Family **Limina**, Physics Domain **entropy, void and mind**. Its law, verbatim from `wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Limina — Entropy, Void and Mind.md`:
> "A solid dissolves not because the solvent attacks it but because the mixed state has higher entropy than the separated one, and the process therefore runs on its own once initiated. Dissolution gives the Crystal that thermodynamic preference toward the unbound state, which makes shedding what no longer serves nearly effortless and makes retaining anything a matter of active work."
**"Makes retaining anything a matter of active work" is the Worldbind Bastion's entire cost, stated in the register before the technique existed.**
**Orrenthal · *The Metallic Heart*** is the reference frequency (law quoted in full in the `Celestial Harmonic Shear` account), and the page's choice is correct: a delocalised-electron law in which "nothing is individually owned" is the right current to be a *shared* reference for nine others.
The page assigns the other faces of the lattice to specific pairs and they check out: **Dissolution/Tarturon** for matter unwritten (Tarturon "supplies its own oxidiser, which is the mechanical reason the reaction cannot be smothered once initiated", `Materia — Material Science.md` — the right current for an unstoppable unmaking), **Basilithe/Petralon** for matter rebuilt (a law-lock plus a compressive load path — the right pair for a bastion), **Oneirion/Tenebra** for perception rewritten (a configuration-space law plus signal suppression — the right pair for the Cascade). **Three faces, six currents, all six correctly chosen.**
Register failure, Dissolution: "**Indiscriminate solvency.** Entropy does not consult the practitioner about which structures were load-bearing." **That is the Worldrend Vector's real hazard and the page does not name it.** A beam that dismantles toward pure potential will take out the load-bearing members of anything it crosses, including ground the caster is relying on.
*Four Theories.* **Consent Doctrine** as filed. But the page's Identity Bleed — "the shard mirroring her inner state back at her until prolonged projection risks a genuine dissociative echo" — is a **Correspondence** symptom: a construct that is her own law at a second scale, and the two scales agreeing longer than she meant them to (`Two Sets of Books` §IV). **Noted, not resolved.**
**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Attraction Layer** (Domain Weaving, and the shard executes autonomously, which is a Fate-side claim) with the **Essence Core** exposed — Identity Bleed is a Core cost and its failure state is "Fracture into Shards" (`The Core Vocabulary` §III), which is a grim pun the page has not noticed: the working is named for a shard and its Core failure state is fracturing into shards. Developmental Tier at Stage XII: **Sovereign** — "Trait law and Domain law begin to be the same law" (Part Twenty-One), which is what a construct that carries her judgment autonomously requires. Crystal State: `null`, no card. **The invoice:** Harmonic Drain (the lock budget across ten incommensurate laws) and Identity Bleed (the Core's own reflection returning). **The page also names a "partial Temperance shear" as a risk, and the system's term for that is Shear Break — "A Trait collapsing, inverting, or turning on its bearer"** (`The Core Vocabulary` §VI) — which is a Trait-level hazard the page invokes without the system's vocabulary. Worth aligning.
**The lens: Cusa's *coincidentia oppositorum*.** Nicholas of Cusa, *De docta ignorantia* (1440), I.4 and I.22: in the infinite, contradictories coincide — the maximum and the minimum are one, a circle of infinite radius is a straight line — and the intellect can only approach this by *learned ignorance*, knowing that it cannot be held in a finite mind. No idea in the whole history of philosophy fits a shard *"built from ten Wellspring laws held in simultaneous contradiction"* better, and none fits it more dangerously. Cusa's own restriction is the point: the coincidence holds **at the infinite and nowhere else**. A finite lattice cannot hold contradictories; it can only hold them in **phase**, which is what the page's Mechanism actually describes — ten paths locked to one reference frequency in a single stable fringe pattern, the way an interferometer holds several routes at once without any of them contradicting another. So the lens does two jobs here. It explains why a school would speak of the shard as a coincidence of opposites and why Opalis herself might believe it, and it is precisely the place where the page's Effect and its Mechanism part company: contradiction held, or coherence held. *Identity Bleed* — her mind drifting into the shard's fractal judgment-logic — is then the exact punishment Cusa would predict for a finite mind that mistook itself for the infinite one.

#### 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph.** `[Ath]` **Command** · Root · Irath — a Decree is `[Ath]` before it is anything else, and the Index notes that Irath's smallest station "carries the two heaviest words in it" — laid across `[Lh]` **Frequency** · Root · Selhar, the reference the ten are locked to. Both from the thirty unclassified forms in `wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`.
**What boundary moves.** One quantity: **the phase relationship among ten laws, referred to one of them.** Not any of the ten laws themselves. The page is precise about this and the precision is the working's whole design: "the shard's mode is which face of that standing interference the caster reads outward."
**What the law then does on its own.** All three modes, and the page's limits are the lattice's own properties. It cannot hold two contradictory modes at wide scale because an interference pattern has one normalisation and you cannot read two faces outward at once. It cannot overwrite another Stage XII technique without harmonic superiority because two references contest for the lock. It cannot create consciousness or a living soul because dissolution redistributes and does not originate — **and that limit is the strongest mechanism-derived constraint on the page: entropy of mixing has no creating direction at all.** It continues executing autonomously for short intervals because a locked comb persists on its own coherence until drift accumulates.
**Law V check.** Stage XII sits far above Refraction, so no material anchor is required (`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). The page gives a raised hand and a committed judgment — Gestured plus an internal commitment, which is the Internalised vector in all but name and is exactly what Refraction-and-above buys.
**Failure mode — Structural fault, primary.** "The Crystal could not hold what the boundary permitted" (`Two Sets of Books` §VII). The lock budget across ten incommensurate laws is the structure, and Harmonic Drain is it failing gradually. Behind it a **Coherence fault** waiting in the Identity Bleed: "The practitioner's lived behaviour and their stated law have drifted apart, and the Continuum reads the drift" — a caster whose mind is bleeding into the shard's judgment-logic until "she is thinking in its fractal patterns rather than her own" is drifting from her own stated law, and the system's stated consequence is that "Traits begin to argue with their bearer." **The page's Identity Bleed and the system's Coherence fault are the same process at two stages, and the page's risk of "a flattening of her own affect" is the drift's signature.**
The page's "What nobody knows" — whether the ten are genuinely balanced or one is quietly load-bearing for the other nine — **has its answer on the page.** In injection locking the **reference is load-bearing by construction**: every locked oscillator inherits the reference's frequency and its drift, and pulling the reference collapses the whole comb. The page names the reference: **Orrenthal.** So the mechanism's reading is that one *is* load-bearing, it is not hidden, and nobody has caught the shard failing in a way that shows it because **the failure mode of a reference collapse is the shard going dark all at once rather than losing one face.** Offered as the mechanism's reading; the page's question stays open.
**What the target sees and feels.** A rotating crystalline shard, and then one of three things, depending on which face of the standing pattern she is reading outward. Along the **Worldrend** face, matter and Essence in the beam's path do not burn or shatter — they *"fracture along the beam's path toward pure potential"*, which from the receiving end is the sight of a thing losing the particular shape it had and not acquiring another. Along the **Worldbind** face, terrain and Essence inside 5–50 m are pulled back toward *their prior lawful state*: wounds close toward what they were, broken walls find their courses again, and anything whose present condition is an improvement on its past condition resents it. Along the **Dreamveil** face nothing touches the body at all; local causality softens, perception is rewritten, and the target's complaint afterwards is that events did not follow each other properly. The shard can carry on executing her stated intent autonomously for short intervals once awakened, so breaking line of sight to Opalis does not end it. What it cannot do is two of those at once over the same ground — *"destruction and restoration will not run simultaneously at scale"* — and a target who can be in two places that need opposite answers has found the seam.
**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** No card for Opalis; the ladder gives Stage XII ⇒ **Tier 7 · Grandmaster**, η **0.85–0.90** (Part Nineteen) ⇒ **10–15 percent** as heat, sound and structural bleed. On the Worldrend Vector at the SSS ceiling that is still an enormous absolute figure — 10 % of 4.184×10²¹ J is 4.18×10²⁰ J — and **the page does not say where it goes.** On the Bastion and the Cascade the bleed is small. Worth flagging as texture: the three modes should not bleed alike, and the page treats the cost as mode-independent.

#### 5 · Counterplay and the challenge

**The Counterplay routes that work** — including the two the page omits (`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).
- **Deny the field — omitted by the page, and the strongest route.** Family **Limina**. The shard phase-locks ten Wellspring laws through **Orrenthal as the reference frequency**. A **Silence** — *"the removal of magical possibility from a region"* — takes the reference away, and a lattice with no reference tone has nothing to lock to. The register also notes that *"Animatria constructs fail completely because their continuity runs back to a source the Silence has severed"*, and a shard executing her intent autonomously for short intervals is a continuity running back to a source.
- **Break the boundary — Knot, against a ten-path interferometer.** *"Knot entangles a path so it cannot be traced cleanly."* Ten paths held in one fringe pattern is ten paths that must each be traceable. This is the counter the page's own Weakness gestures at from the wrong end when it says excess Oneirion exposure destabilises the shard's judgment-logic.
- **Break the man — the contradiction clause, used on purpose.** She cannot run destruction and restoration at scale simultaneously. Force a situation where both are required and she must choose one, publicly, and the choice is information about what she is protecting.
- **Break the body — omitted by the page.** Opalis raises a hand. *"The Crystal is inside a person and the person is ordinary."* Identity Bleed means the longer she projects, the less of her is watching the room.
**The tell, stated plainly.** A raised hand and a committed judgment, then light falling inward and shadow crystallising outward around it. The mode is fixed when the shard awakens, so the first face it shows is the face it is committed to at scale.

# Celestial Harmonic Shear

## Celestial Harmonic Shear

### Summary card

**Effect** · Opalis draws a line through the air, and anything whose own frequency crosses that line loses cohesion exactly where it was touched; wards, illusions and layered constructs break all at once rather than one layer at a time.
**Cost** · Internal resonance leaks outward as she cuts, leaving her briefly dizzy and tone-deaf to certain currents, and a large or complex cut can send a backlash into her own law-vector that weakens her other Emanation-level workings for a short while.
**Limit** · Cannot cut the harmonic of a being with equal or greater harmonic sovereignty, cannot touch anything operating outside harmonic physics at all (true void, full dream-essence, anti-tone fields), and cannot produce more than one Shear at full potency in a single activation.
**Counter** · A silence field, a stilled instant, or a target holding several contradictory frequencies at once denies the Shear a single clean frequency to find and cut.
**What nobody knows.** Whether Opalis is cutting the frequency that is actually there, or the one she has decided is the lie underneath it.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · Failure along a normal mode, one of the natural ways a structure vibrates: a thing is far weaker along its modes and seams than across its bulk, so severing the mode parts it cheaply, as a wineglass shatters or a composite peels apart.
**Law** · A driven damped oscillator at resonance amplifies its drive by the quality factor, Q = 1/(2ζ); for layered structures the criterion is interlaminar shear strength, the weakest number on the data sheet.
**Limit** · The cut needs one unique mode to find; anything with no mode, or with several contradictory ones, cannot be severed.
**Drive** · The Shear is a small drive: the caster supplies a driving frequency, the structure amplifies it by Q, and the target's own failure does the damage; the Grade buys the lock on the mode, not the yield.
**Ring-down** · A mode of a few hundred hertz at Q about 10³ decays in about Q cycles, so resonance cracks ring for a few seconds along the cut.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Frequency (Selhar) laid across Edge (Thalen) for the hundred-metre line.
**Boundary** · The continuity of one named vibrational mode along a line; not the material and not the energy.
**Effect** · A raised hand, a slow line through the air, then a seam that resolves instantly with no impact. A ward, illusion or layered construct comes apart along its resonance, not its shape, so the break looks wrong to anyone expecting a cut.
**Reach** · Layers sharing a mode fail together at the intersection; plain matter on the line is not cut. Nothing outside harmonic physics (true void, full dream-essence, anti-tone fields) and no one at the caster's Stage or higher can be cut.
**Hold** · Emanation needs no anchor; the hand is for aim at a 3 cm kerf at 300 m. One Shear at full potency per activation.
**Failure** · Structural: a Shell run as a resonant source leaks, and a large cut weakens the caster's other Emanation workings for a while. Field faults, silence, stillness or a void-phase barrier, leave it correct with no mode to find.
**Bleed** · 10 to 15 percent, 0.09 to 2.6 TJ, leaves as heat and sound at the Shell, the resonance leak; the caster's own ears pay first.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · One hand up and one slow line drawn: the gesture fixes the seam's place and length before it exists, so a watcher knows where not to stand. Afterwards the seam rings a few seconds, then lies silent.
**Limits** · 300 m, line of sight, one line up to 100 m, one full Shear per activation; nothing at or above the caster's Stage, nothing outside harmonic physics, nothing with contradictory tones.
**Fails against** · Dense fog, boiled leather, fired clay and shielded rooms; a Cymorath-type silence field; several contradictory frequencies held at once (Fractura beings, dream-entities, shapeshifters, Eidolyn or Oneirion disguise); spread targets, since one line kills one thing; anyone at the caster's Stage.
**Look up** · The Fulguria terrain and counter-kit; Orrenthal, Basilithe and Dissolution failures; Tempering Capacity; Shell Clouding; the Emanation Stage table and the Path gates for Penetration and Breadth.

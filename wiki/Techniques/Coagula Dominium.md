# Coagula Dominium

## Coagula Dominium

### Summary card

**Effect** · A fifteen-metre field in which ambient Essence stops flowing and starts sitting. Spellwork routed through the field arrives late and undersized; anyone moving through it fights the air itself for every step.
**Cost** · Pressure builds behind the practitioner's own ribs for as long as the field holds; the same coagulating law that slows the enemy's Essence is running through his.
**Limit** · Reaches only Essence already loose in the air. A working already sealed inside a Soul Crystal, or a body moving under its own momentum rather than Essence-borne lift, passes through untouched.
**Counter** · A Wellspring that routes through Vectoria or Spatium rather than through ambient Essence (flight, blink-step, anything that never asks the air's permission) is not slowed at all.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the field reads intent before it thickens, or whether it would happily seize the practitioner's own allies if he ever lost the thread holding it apart from them.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · The jamming transition of a dense suspension: past a critical packing, particles lock and a fluid acts like a solid, as cornstarch lets you run across it but swallows you standing still. Fifteen metres of loose Essence goes thick.
**Law** · Krieger–Dougherty: relative viscosity η*r = (1 − φ/φ*m)^(−[η]·φ_m) diverges as packing φ nears its maximum, about 0.58 to 0.64; drag on a limb is F = 6πμrv.
**Limit** · A jammed medium resists shear, not translation: a body already carrying momentum crosses it losing some energy but is not held; only what keeps working against the medium pays.
**Selectivity** · No viscosity tells a spell mid-formation from background flux; a Judicium discernment layer rides the same field to supply that criterion, and neither half alone makes the working.

### Metaphysics

**Aether** · It acts on ambient Essence, not matter, and the joules are the medium's; the practitioner spends on holding the packing. The residue is a density anomaly that stays after the field drops, where a Harmonist hears thickly.
**Wellspring** · Coagula, the Unifying Pulse (Materia): pressed powder held hot fuses across its contacts into one body without melting; an inward-gathering current, here projected outward onto the room for the first known time.
**Wellspring** · Judicium, the Wellspring of Truth (Fulguria): reads each current's signature and supplies the criterion for what counts as load-bearing flux.
**Terrain** · Materia is favoured by mineral-rich rock, worked stone and deep foundation, and suppressed by open water, high altitude and moving platforms; Fulguria wants dry air, conductive ground and clear sight.
**Inherited failures** · Coagula's residual porosity: in a field it is percolation, connected paths through the jam that a clean single burst can find; Judicium's documented failures ride along too.
**Saturation** · The field is engineered local saturation; held dense or long enough it crosses into Aetheric Saturation and causes Crystal Fracture Events in everyone inside, the practitioner included.
**Theory** · Under the Consent Doctrine, Judicium reads signatures, which carry no allegiance: friend is sorted from foe only by the practitioner's attention, and if it slips, allies are caught.
**Essence** · The Aether Shell works twice, outward to project and inward because the same law runs through the practitioner; the rib-deep pressure is Clouding with a timer on it. Developmental Tier Radiant.
**School** · The alchemists read "dissolve and coagulate" as a cycle and bring dissolving workings to thin the field; only an overwhelming Wellspring wins that way, and the real answer is to stop using the medium.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Knot (Maelor), a counter that entangles paths so workings arrive late and undersized, laid across Boundary (Zhaeren) for the fifteen-metre edge; Category Vectra.
**Boundary** · Coagula moves the packing fraction of ambient Essence inside fifteen metres; Judicium moves the criterion for load-bearing flux.
**Effect** · A caster inside sees their working land late and one full Tier Grade down, an A result in an S hand, with no fault to diagnose. Walkers feel the air go to syrup and pay more Essence per stride; a runner on their own legs is not slowed.
**Exemptions** · A working already sealed in a Crystal, a body on pure muscular momentum, and Vectoria or Spatium workings such as flight or blink-step never use the medium and pass.
**Hold** · Transcendence needs no anchor, but the field is declared and holds only while the practitioner's attention holds; it stops the moment attention lapses.
**Failure** · Structural: the coagulating law loads the practitioner's own Shell for the whole hold with no release, and the rib pressure worsens. Boundary fault: anything that does not use the medium is outside a boundary never drawn around it.
**Bleed** · 20 to 30 percent leaves as heat at the Shell and spreads through the field as heat and sound, 0.66 to 18.4 GW in the first turn and 4.3 to 120 GW by the twelfth.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · A declaration and a standing draw, then the air inside fifteen metres goes visibly and audibly thick for everyone, allies too; afterwards a density anomaly a sensitive can walk the edge of.
**Limits** · Fifteen metres, attention-bound, loose ambient Essence only, three learnable exemptions; high-Tier Wellsprings overpower the coagulation outright.
**Fails against** · Anything routed through Vectoria or Spatium; open water; thrown spears, fired shot, movement on one's own legs; a working cast in one clean burst, or a Seal forced early that finds percolation paths; the practitioner's attention split outside the radius; a hold carried into Saturation.
**Look up** · The Materia and Fulguria terrain; Coagula and Judicium failures; Aetheric Saturation and Crystal Fracture Events; Knot in the Counterplay register; the Transcendence Stage table and the S and A Grade rows.

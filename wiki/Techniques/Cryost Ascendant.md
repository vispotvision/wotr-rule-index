# Cryost Ascendant

## Cryost Ascendant

### Summary card

**Effect** · Banks incoming kinetic, thermal, emotional, and Aetheric force as stored equilibrium, then discharges it as one decisive corrective act while the remainder bleeds off as harmless cold.
**Cost** · The banked charge has to go somewhere on release; the wider the Bloom and the longer it holds, the colder the practitioner runs afterward, and overbanking without a release risks his own body anchoring in place.
**Limit** · The field arrests vectors already in motion, it does not originate force of its own, so a still room gives it nothing to bank, and a demand to preserve motion and stillness at once degrades the lattice.
**Counter** · A working that reads the reversion's shape before it completes can decline to let the bank finish, and a chaos-aligned or paradox-grade disturbance denies the field a stable pattern to arrest.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the "verdict" the field resolves toward is the practitioner's own judgment or something the Crystal is selecting for him.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · Energy storage by arrest: stopping a moving body without wasting its energy means storing it, as a compressed spring does. A field banks every vector that enters it, motion, heat and fear alike, then spends the bank on one decisive act.
**Law** · The first law: a region cools only if energy leaves it. The Bloom goes cold because every arrested vector's energy is drawn out of the volume into the practitioner's Crystal.
**Limit** · The room cannot hold the bank: elastically it needs 68 times the field's volume of spring steel at its breaking point, and as heat it turns the air to plasma. The field makes no force of its own.
**Speech** · The field arrests intention as well as motion, so speech clips toward the bare floor of intelligibility, about 39 bits a second.

### Metaphysics

**Aether** · The draw is inverted: the field takes in rather than drawing out. The residue is a cold patch warming slowly from the edge, frost on every covered surface, and a quiet in which a Harmonist hears almost nothing.
**Wellspring** · Vohrin (Caloria), one power at two levels, Titan and Wellspring, drawn here as Titan-derived: it carries the frost-stasis.
**Wellspring** · Fixatio, the Binding Flame (Materia): a cured thermoset chars before it flows, so what it holds is held because the other state is no longer reachable; it carries the cost.
**Wellspring** · Judicium, the Wellspring of Truth (Fulguria): reads without contact which vectors, kinetic, thermal and emotional, are there to arrest.
**Terrain** · Caloria is suppressed by thermal equilibrium, saturated cold and oxygen-poor air, and the Bloom manufactures saturated cold, so it degrades its own conditions the longer it holds.
**Inherited failures** · Fixatio's irreversibility: a binding placed in error is permanent. Materia's phase locking: under enough pressure the lattice commits to one form for good. Vohrin and Judicium bring their own documented failures.
**Theory** · Under the Consent Doctrine the field resolves by criterion and the judgment is Judicium's reading; under the Correspondence Position practitioner and current are one law at two scales.
**Essence** · The Aether Shell projects and the Attraction Layer holds the radius; the bank enters the Crystal at 1 EU per MJ and can overfill past maximum. Developmental Tier Sovereign.
**School** · The caloric chemists treat heat as a fluid and read the Bloom as a ledger; their theory predicts arrest runs hot and sends fighters in light clothing, but the Bloom runs cold, since the bank leaves the room.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Lock (Thalen), a counter that occupies a limit so no other can be set there, laid across Hour (Valen), because the limit occupied is one on when.
**Boundary** · Whether a vector inside the radius is allowed to complete; not its size, direction or energy.
**Effect** · Inside the Bloom everything slows to blink-speed deltas or stops; a charge, a flame and a panic go into one ledger. Nothing is deflected: it is held, then returned as one decisive corrective act.
**Trigger** · No anchor, declaration or gesture: it begins when the practitioner enters a scene carrying motion, heat, sound or fear they mean to still, the quietest trigger there is.
**Reach** · A paradox-grade or chaos-aligned disturbance has no stable end to withhold and tears the lattice; a demand to keep motion and stillness at once degrades it.
**Failure** · Structural: the working succeeds and the practitioner does not. Held too long, Fixatio's cure sets the anchor into the practitioner's body, keeping whatever form they wore. Field fault: with nothing incoming, it is correct and does nothing.
**Bleed** · 20 to 30 percent, 1.65 to 46 GW, leaves as heat at the Shell, the one heat in the Bloom, which the field must keep drawing against; the Master's feedback loop returns it to the bank.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · A visible radius goes cold before anything is caught and speech clips short; the colder it is, the more is banked. Afterwards frost on everything covered and ground that stays cold.
**Limits** · Six to eighteen metres with a one-to-two-metre Stillpoint at the apex; no force of its own; nothing to do in a still room; overbanking harms its holder.
**Fails against** · Slow, low-energy movement that gives it nothing; a Knot or chaos-aligned disturbance; several exclusive threats at once; no target to discharge into (retreat, cover, a decoy); its own cold, which slows the holder.
**Look up** · Lock and Knot in the Counterplay register; Caloria terrain; Vohrin, Fixatio and Judicium failures; Materia phase locking; the Invocation Stage table and the S Grade row.

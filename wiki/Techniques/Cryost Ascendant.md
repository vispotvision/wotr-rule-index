# Cryost Ascendant

## Cryost Ascendant

### Summary card

**Effect** · Banks incoming kinetic, thermal, emotional, and Aetheric force as stored equilibrium, then discharges it as one decisive corrective act while the remainder bleeds off as harmless cold.
**Cost** · The banked charge has to go somewhere on release; the wider the Bloom and the longer it holds, the colder Tovain runs afterward, and overbanking without a release risks his own body anchoring in place.
**Limit** · The field arrests vectors already in motion, it does not originate force of its own, so a still room gives it nothing to bank, and a demand to preserve motion and stillness at once degrades the lattice.
**Counter** · A working that reads the reversion's shape before it completes can decline to let the bank finish, and a chaos-aligned or paradox-grade disturbance denies the field a stable pattern to arrest.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the "verdict" the field resolves toward is Tovain's own judgment or something the Crystal is selecting for him.

### Codex line

Wellspring Vohrin (primary; carried in current canon by an existing Kharven card for this same frost-stasis phenomenon, though not itself on the Sixty's own enumerated list), Family Caloria, Physics Domain Thermodynamics. Secondary Fixatio (Materia, Material Science) and Judicium (Fulguria, Electromagnetism), both already named by the source and both already canonical. Category Vectra. Craft Magicraft. Stage floor IX, Invocation. Grade required S-Grade (late push toward SS-Grade). Path gate Spirit Path.

### Design Chain

**Trigger** · Tovain enters a scene, combat or otherwise, carrying motion, heat, sound, or fear he intends to still.
**Function** · The field reads every vector inside its radius, kinetic, thermal, and emotional alike, and arrests each one rather than absorbing or deflecting it.
**Mechanism** · This runs on real conservation-of-momentum bookkeeping. Force is caught and held as potential rather than expressed, the way a compressed spring stores work rather than losing it. The cold the field radiates is the literal thermodynamic signature of arrested kinetic and thermal energy sitting unspent.
**Numerical Effect** · At Stage IX, S-Grade, the Bloom's held force sits in the 0.5-2 TN, 4.184-24.3 TJ band, small town to town level. A combat-scale Bloom runs 6-18 metres; the Stillpoint eye at apex runs 1-2 metres.
**Target Response** · Anything caught inside slows to blink-speed deltas or stops outright; speech clips to the minimum needed to be understood.
**Consequence** · One decisive act executes when the field resolves; the rest disperses as harmless cold rather than as a shockwave.
**Limitation** · It cannot manufacture force it was not given; a field with nothing incoming to arrest has nothing to discharge.
**Weakness** · A paradox-grade or chaos-aligned disturbance has no stable vector for the field to catch, and tears the lattice instead of banking into it.
**Cost** · Sustained banking without release drives Tovain's own temperature and reaction time down with the field's; held past the point of safe discharge, the anchor sets into his own body.
**Counterplay** · Force multiple, mutually exclusive threats into the field at once so no single outcome resolves cleanly, or attack with something the field cannot read as a vector, thermal shift, or emotional spike at all.

### FOW line

Governing Primary Harmonics (Stability, Attunement) and Dominion (Radius, Sense). Stage floor IX, Invocation, ascending to X, Realization, on apex events. Grade required S-Grade (late push toward SS-Grade), SS-Grade at apex. Path gate Spirit Path. Resonant Pair none indicated in the source.

### Origin

The source names Tovain Zethriel as the technique's sole bearer and gives his epithet as "The Cryost Ascendant," but states no derivation, no teacher, no location, and no original problem the technique was built to solve. Only its current operating form and a closing quote survive from the card.

#### 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** A field banks every vector that enters it — motion, heat, fear —
holds them as stored potential, then spends the bank on one decisive act while the rest leaves as
harmless cold.
**The real phenomenon.** **Elastic energy storage**, which the page names correctly, and a
**thermodynamic direction problem**, which it does not.
*The storage half.* Arresting a moving body without dissipating its energy means storing it as
potential, and the page's image is right: "the way a compressed spring stores work rather than
losing it." Real springs are the benchmark, and the benchmark is unkind. The **modulus of
resilience** — the elastic energy a material can store per unit volume — is σ_y²/(2E). For a good
spring steel at σ_y ≈ 1 GPa and E ≈ 200 GPa that is
(1×10⁹)² / (2 × 2×10¹¹) = **2.5×10⁶ J/m³**, and that is a ceiling, not a working figure.
**Now put the page's own number into it.** The Bloom holds **4.184×10¹² to 2.427×10¹³ J**.
At 2.5×10⁶ J/m³, storing the low end needs
4.184×10¹² ÷ 2.5×10⁶ = **1.67×10⁶ m³ of spring steel at its elastic limit** — a cube 119 m on a
side, about **1.3×10¹⁰ kg**, thirteen million tonnes. **The Bloom is 6 to 18 m across.** An 18 m
sphere is (4/3)π(18)³ = **2.44×10⁴ m³**. The elastic-storage reading is short by a factor of
**68**, using the best steel at its breaking point, for the *lowest* energy the page claims.
*The thermal alternative fails worse.* Store 4.184×10¹² J as heat in the air inside an 18 m sphere:
mass = 2.44×10⁴ m³ × 1.225 kg/m³ = 2.99×10⁴ kg, and
ΔT = 4.184×10¹² ÷ (2.99×10⁴ × 1,005) = **≈1.39×10⁵ K**. The field would be a plasma, not cold.
*And here is the direction problem, which is the sharper item.* The page says the field goes cold
because the arrested energy is "sitting unspent," and that the remainder "bleeds off as harmless
cold." **Those two statements point opposite ways.** A volume is cold because energy has been
*removed* from it; energy *bleeding off* is energy leaving, and what leaves a warm thing and enters
a cold one is heat, which warms the surroundings. **A field cannot simultaneously be a sink (hence
cold) and be venting (hence bleeding off).** Either the arrested energy is exported — in which case
something nearby gets very hot and the page does not say what — or it is retained, in which case the
field is not cold and nothing bleeds. Logged
`SA-PHYS-CRYOST-COLD-DIRECTION`; **recorded, not resolved.**
**The governing law.** Conservation of energy, plus the modulus of resilience σ_y²/(2E) as the
ceiling on elastic storage density, plus the first law: a region cools only if energy leaves it.
**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Bloom: **6–18 m** combat-scale; Stillpoint eye at apex **1–2 m** (page).
- Held force and energy: **0.5–2 TN**, **4.184–24.3 TJ** (page). **Checked against Part Four's
S-Grade row, 4.184×10¹² – 2.42672×10¹³ J, and the "Small Town to Town, 1 to 5.8 kilotons TNT"
scale: the page's citation is correct on every figure.**
- Elastic storage ceiling: **2.5×10⁶ J/m³** (spring steel at σ_y ≈ 1 GPa, E ≈ 200 GPa).
- Volume needed for the low-end bank: **1.67×10⁶ m³** against a field of **2.44×10⁴ m³** — short by
**68×**.
- Thermal-storage temperature if retained as heat: **≈1.39×10⁵ K**.
- Speech clipping: the page has speech "clip to the minimum needed to be understood," which is a
real and checkable consequence — English runs about **39 bits/s** of information at roughly
**150 words/minute** across languages, and clipping to minimum intelligibility is compressing
toward that floor rather than slowing the speaker.
**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** In everything that entered the field, and that is
the page's whole design — "It cannot manufacture force it was not given; a field with nothing
incoming to arrest has nothing to discharge." **Which makes this the one working in the batch that
runs the system's central claim backward.** `Two Sets of Books` §III says the practitioner does not
supply the joules and the room does. Cryost Ascendant says the *attacker* supplies them and the
field holds them. **That is a legitimate and interesting inversion, and it exposes a gap the system
has never had to answer: Aetheric Density is defined as a ceiling on output — "Sets the ceiling on
the AU/s output a practitioner can achieve in that location" (`The Core Vocabulary` §II) — and
nothing anywhere says whether it also bounds how much a location can absorb.** If the Aether
stratum is the sink for 24 TJ, the system needs a sink capacity and has never defined one. Logged
`SA-GAP-AETHER-AS-SINK`, distinct from the output-ceiling gap.
**Precedent we are not copying.** Counter-and-release techniques are well-worn — Hunter × Hunter's
Rock-Paper-Scissors on the metaphysical side, the parry-and-riposte of every action game, Bleach's
absorb-and-return shields. Those *return* what they took. This one **converts a bank into one
decisive corrective act** and vents the rest, so the working's output is a judgement rather than a
counterattack, and its failure mode is having banked too long. The divergence is that the technique
gets *worse* the better it works.

#### 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** Draw is inverted: the field takes in rather than drawing out. Caloria is
suppressed by "Thermal equilibrium, **saturated cold**, oxygen-poor air"
(`The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings`, Environmental Coupling) — **and a Cryost field
manufactures saturated cold, so it degrades its own operating conditions the longer it holds, which
is the same self-defeating property `Maw of Crystalline Stasis` has and neither page names.**
**Residue:** a district that has had 24 TJ parked in it, by whatever route. **The residue question
here is genuinely open because the disposal route is:** if the energy went into the Aether stratum,
the residue is an Aetheric anomaly a surveyor could find; if it went into the surroundings as heat,
the residue is a scorched perimeter. `SA-PHYS-CRYOST-COLD-DIRECTION` decides which.
**Saturation:** see above, and it is the working's own product.
**Wellspring stratum.** **Fixatio · *The Binding Flame*** (secondary on the page, load-bearing in
the cost), Family **Materia**, Physics Domain **material science**. Its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Materia — Material Science.md`:
> "A thermoplastic can be melted and reformed indefinitely. A thermoset, once cured, has formed
> covalent bridges across its whole network and will char before it flows. Fixatio performs the
> cure. What it holds is not held by force but by the fact that the alternative configuration no
> longer exists as a reachable state."
**And its Failure field is this page's cost, exactly.** "**Irreversibility.** The cure is not undone
by wanting it undone, and a Fixatio binding placed in error is a permanent feature of whatever it
was placed on." The page: "overbanking without a release risks his own body anchoring in place" and
"held past the point of safe discharge, the anchor sets into his own body." **A thermoset cure that
has gone too far is not a metaphor for that; it is the same statement.** As on
`Hypnather Descent Rite` and `Celestial Harmonic Shear`, the register wrote the cost before the
technique existed.
**Judicium · *The Wellspring of Truth*** (secondary), Family **Fulguria** — law quoted in full in
the `Lumen Dissecans` account — supplying the reading of which vectors are there to be arrested.
A field that "reads every vector inside its radius, kinetic, thermal, and emotional alike" needs an
identification instrument and Judicium is the one in the register that does it without contact.
**Primary: Vohrin — and the page says itself that Vohrin is not on the list.** Its Codex line
reads: "Wellspring Vohrin (primary; carried in current canon by an existing Kharven card for this
same frost-stasis phenomenon, **though not itself on the Sixty's own enumerated list**)."
**That is an honest flag and it should be credited rather than treated as an error.** The system's
Vohrin is a Titan — "VOHRIN · *The Titan of Niflheim* · Abyssal Depths · Entropy's peace, the
settling of endings" (`wiki/Cosmology & Metaphysics/The Fourteen Titans.md`), whose keyed compound
is the Stillwater Draught and whose Titan-Keyed art "requires Zenith." **A Stage IX working on a
Titan sits five Stages below that gate.** Recorded, not resolved — `SA-UNATT-VOHRIN`, which this
page's own candour makes the best entry point for.
*Four Theories.* The page's "What nobody knows" is a Four-Theories question stated plainly:
"Whether the 'verdict' the field resolves toward is Tovain's own judgment or something the Crystal
is selecting for him." Under **Consent** the field resolves by criterion and the judgement is
Judicium's reading, not his; under **Correspondence** the distinction does not exist because he and
the current are one law at two scales (`Two Sets of Books` §IV). **Noted, not resolved.**
**Essence stratum.** Active layer: the **Aether Shell** (the field is projected and the cold runs
through him) with the **Attraction Layer** holding the radius. Shell failure states are "Clouding
or Rupture" (`The Core Vocabulary` §III); a body anchoring in place is neither — **it is closer to
the Materia-side hazard the register calls phase locking, "Under sufficient pressure the lattice
commits to one allotrope permanently, and the practitioner keeps whichever form they were wearing
when the pressure came"** (`Materia — Material Science.md`, Materia Primordia). Developmental Tier
at Stage IX: **Sovereign** — "Trait law and Domain law begin to be the same law. No longer a
container for power but a generator of local metaphysical conditions" (Part Twenty-One), which is
what a field that legislates stillness over 18 m requires. Crystal State: `null`, no card.
**The invoice:** his own temperature and reaction time falling with the field's, and the anchor
setting if he holds it too long. **Correctly located: a heat sink run through a body cools the
body, and a cure run too long cures the thing holding it.**
**The lens: caloric.** Lavoisier's *Traité élémentaire de chimie* (1789) lists
*calorique* among the simple substances: heat as a real, conserved,
self-repelling fluid that flows into bodies and out of them, with cold as its
**withdrawal** rather than as a thing in its own right. Caloric was a good theory
— it did quantitative work for fifty years — and it is the right lens for a
field that treats force as something to be **banked**, because bookkeeping in a
conserved fluid is exactly what this working claims to be doing: *"Force is
caught and held as potential rather than expressed, the way a compressed spring
stores work rather than losing it."* A caloric practitioner would read the
Stillpoint as a vessel and the Bloom as a ledger, and would be at home here. And
then the lens breaks in the most useful possible place. On caloric theory, and on
thermodynamics after it, **arresting motion releases heat**; work that stops
being work becomes warmth. The page says the opposite — *"the cold the field
radiates is the literal thermodynamic signature of arrested kinetic and thermal
energy sitting unspent"* — and that is the conflict this account logs rather than
smooths (`SA-PHYS-CRYOST-COLD-DIRECTION`). A school working from caloric would
predict that a heavily banked Bloom runs **hot**, would send men to fight it in
light clothing, and would find the record disagreeing with them; Rumford's
cannon-boring experiment of 1798 is the shape the in-world refutation would take,
and somebody in the register has presumably already run it and not been believed.

#### 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[Lk]` **Lock** · Counter · Thalen — one of the Index's
only two Counter forms, and its gloss is stasis stated generally: "**Lock** occupies a limit so no
other limit can be set there" — laid across `[Tir]` **Hour** · Extension · Valen, because what is
occupied here is a limit on *when*. Both from the thirty unclassified forms in
`wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`. **The Index's note on why
counters are scarce applies directly: "What a counter does instead is impose a boundary that
prevents another boundary from being established" — an arrested vector is a vector prevented from
setting its own terminus.** *Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling.*
**What boundary moves.** One quantity: **whether a vector inside the radius is permitted to
complete.** Not its magnitude, not its direction, not its energy. Only its completion.
**What the law then does on its own.** Everything, and both of the page's limits are the boundary
speaking. It cannot manufacture force because a completion-boundary has nothing to work on without
an incoming vector. A paradox-grade or chaos-aligned disturbance tears the lattice rather than
banking into it because a vector with no stable terminus has no completion to withhold — **the field
does not fail to catch it; there is nothing to catch.** And the anchor sets into his own body
because Fixatio's cure is irreversible and he is inside the radius. The Index's warning arrives on
schedule: the glyph ends, the cure does not.
**Law V check.** Stage IX sits above Refraction, so no material anchor is required
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). The page's trigger is simply his arrival — "Tovain
enters a scene, combat or otherwise, carrying motion, heat, sound, or fear he intends to still" —
which is the Internalised vector taken to its limit: a working with no declaration and no gesture at
all. **Consistent with his Stage, and the quietest trigger in the batch.**
**Failure mode — Structural fault.** "The Crystal could not hold what the boundary permitted.
Overchannel, Backlash, Fracture. The working succeeds and the practitioner does not"
(`Two Sets of Books` §VII). The bank is held, the cure completes, and the thing cured is him.
A **Field fault** sits beside it: "a field with nothing incoming to arrest has nothing to
discharge" — the working is correct and produces nothing, which is the textbook definition.
**A Grade note worth crediting, because another page in this batch gets it wrong.** The page says
"Grade required S-Grade (late push toward SS-Grade), SS-Grade at apex" and "Stage floor IX,
Invocation, ascending to X, Realization, on apex events." **Checked against Part Five: Stage IX is
"S, allocation stops at 550, late push toward SS entry," ceiling 625, with 551–625 as the
strain-only instability zone.** The page's Grade language is *exactly* the Stage IX row.
(By contrast `Edict Strike` attributes the same "late push toward SS" to Stage VIII, which has no
instability zone — `SA-NUM-EDICT-SS-PUSH`.) **This page reads the table correctly and should be the
reference for how that row is quoted.**
**What the target sees and feels.** Cold, and then the discovery that his own
momentum is no longer his. Everything inside the Bloom — six to eighteen metres
at combat scale, with a one-to-two-metre Stillpoint at the apex — *"slows to
blink-speed deltas or stops outright"*, and the arrest is not directional: it
catches kinetic, thermal and emotional vectors alike, so a charge, a flame and a
panic all go into the same ledger. Speech clips down *"to the minimum needed to be
understood"*, which is the clearest report available of what the field is doing,
because it is arresting the intention as much as the motion. Nothing is deflected
and nothing is absorbed; it is **held**, and it is still there. Then it comes
back out as one decisive corrective act. The limit is the thing worth carrying
away from this page: the field *"cannot manufacture force it was not given"*, so
a man who walks in slowly, carrying nothing, intending nothing sharply, has
handed Tovain an empty ledger — *"a field with nothing incoming to arrest has
nothing to discharge."* Meanwhile the bank is a liability to its holder. Held past
safe discharge, *"the anchor sets into his own body"*, and he runs colder and
slower with it the whole time.
**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency.** No card for Tovain, so η comes from the ladder: Stage IX
⇒ **Tier 6 · Master**, band **0.70–0.80** (Part Nineteen) ⇒ **20–30 percent** as heat, sound and
structural bleed. **And on this page the bleed is the most physically awkward figure in the batch:
a 20–30 percent thermal bleed inside a volume the working is trying to keep cold is load added
directly to the sink it is already failing to account for.** Part Nineteen's gloss on Tier 6 does
offer relief — "First true Domain, which provides a feedback loop recycling ambient bleed" — and a
Cryost field is arguably that loop. **But recycling the bleed back into a bank that has nowhere to
put it does not help, it compounds `SA-PHYS-CRYOST-COLD-DIRECTION`.**

**The Counterplay routes that work**
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).
- **Deny the field — by bringing nothing.** This is the page's structural
weakness and it is stated outright: the field *"arrests vectors already in
motion, it does not originate force of its own, so a still room gives it
nothing to bank."* A slow, patient, low-energy approach is not a compromise
against Cryost Ascendant; it is the counter. The register's *"Cut the main"* is
the same instinct applied to infrastructure — take away what he draws from, and
here what he draws from is **you**.
- **Break the boundary — give it something unreadable.** *"A paradox-grade or
chaos-aligned disturbance has no stable vector for the field to catch, and
tears the lattice instead of banking into it."* In register terms this is a
**Knot**: *"entangles a path so it cannot be traced cleanly."* The page's own
Counterplay adds the cheaper version — *"force multiple, mutually exclusive
threats into the field at once so no single outcome resolves cleanly"*, which
exploits the stated limit that stillness and motion cannot both be preserved.
- **Break the man — the bank as a hostage.** Every joule he holds is a joule he
has to put somewhere, and holding is what hurts him. Deny him a target worth
discharging into — retreat, cover, a decoy — and the ledger he built to beat you
is the thing setting into his legs.
- **Break the body — normally, and with the cold helping.** His own reaction time
falls with the field's temperature while he banks. Families **Caloria /
Materia / Fulguria**; the register notes *"Caloria fails in thermal
equilibrium, saturated cold"*, which is the odd case of a field that
manufactures its own suppressive terrain.
**The tell, stated plainly.** It gets cold, inside a visible radius, before
anything is caught — and the colder it is, the more he is already holding and the
more urgently he needs somewhere to put it.

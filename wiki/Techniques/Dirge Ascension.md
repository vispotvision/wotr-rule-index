# Dirge Ascension

## Dirge Ascension

### Summary card

**Effect** · Francis steps into a proto-Archonic state for 45 seconds, shedding his mortal ceiling; local physics inside a one-kilometre radius run on rewritten equations for the duration, and his Aether output spikes to 2,800,000 AU/s.
**Cost** · One percent of his Soul Crystal's mass burns away with every use.
**Limit** · Cannot overlap World Echelon; the two states are mutually exclusive.
**Counter** · An Archonic mandate outranks the borrowed authority of the form outright. A Nihiloth-mediated null field (Talos-class) can suppress the transformation's output, though a full null against an EX-Grade spike is not guaranteed.
**What nobody knows** · Whether the one percent Soul Crystal mass comes back, or whether Francis is spending down a finite reserve one use at a time.

### Codex line

- **Wellspring** · Exuroth (primary), Judicium and Catharsis (secondary)
- **Family** · Caloria (Exuroth, Catharsis); Fulguria (Judicium)
- **Physics Domain** · Thermodynamics, primary; Electromagnetism, secondary
- **Category** · Phenomena (Attraction-Dominant) — a large-scale event where Aether law itself bends under overload, matching "local physics obey rewritten equations" and the self-sustaining 45-second duration once triggered
- **Craft** · Spellcraft
- **Stage floor** · Stage XIV, Zenith
- **Grade required** · EX-Grade
- **Path gate** · Spirit Path

### Design Chain

**Trigger** · Francis commits to the transformation and holds nothing back.
**Function** · Transformation, world rewrite: a temporary step toward proto-Archonic function.
**Mechanism** · Modelled on a renormalisation-group flow run backward: the local coupling constants that ordinarily fix how strongly a given law applies at Francis's own scale are pushed, for the duration, toward the values those same laws take at a deeper, Archon-adjacent scale, the way a system near a critical point briefly behaves as though sampled at a different energy scale entirely. Exuroth supplies the tolerance to survive holding that state open; Catharsis supplies the release that lets it end cleanly instead of snapping back.
**Numerical Effect** · One-kilometre radius, self at the centre, as given. 45 seconds duration, as given, and the source of this batch's 5-second-per-turn conversion. 2,800,000 AU/s output, as given.
**Target Response** · Anything inside the radius is answering rewritten physics for the duration, not merely resisting a stronger version of the ordinary kind.
**Consequence** · When the form ends, the Crystal does not return to baseline gently; the source's own "severe collapse post-use" is the toll for having run laws that were never Francis's to keep permanently.
**Limitation** · Cannot overlap World Echelon, as given; the Crystal cannot hold both a merged-Domain claim and a self-transformation this large at once.
**Weakness** · As on Principle Overwrite, Dominion Sovereignty's Stage VI Fate-Path gate is not met by a pure Spirit Path, and the paradox recoil the card lists is at least partly that gate reasserting itself the moment the borrowed authority lapses.
**Cost** · One percent Soul Crystal mass burned per use, as given.
**Counterplay** · An Archonic mandate outranks a proto-Archonic borrowing of the same register outright. A Nihiloth-mediated null field can suppress it, though at EX-Grade output a full null is not assured, the only documented uncertainty in an otherwise clean counter.

### FOW line

Governing Primary: Ardency (Flux, Overchannel, Depth), primary, via Exuroth; Vitality (Threshold, Tolerance), secondary; Resilience (Persistence), tertiary, load-bearing for the post-use collapse.
Stage floor: Stage XIV, Zenith.
Grade required: EX-Grade.
Path gate: Spirit Path (Dominion Sovereignty's Fate-Path gate unmet; see Weakness).
Resonant Pair: None claimed; insufficient Sub-Stat data in the source.

### Origin

Not stated by the source beyond ownership by Francis at Stage XIV Zenith; logged as unattested rather than invented.

#### 1 · Physical account (physics)

**The phenomenon in plain words.** For forty-five seconds he sheds his mortal ceiling. Inside a
kilometre, the local laws run on different numbers, and his output goes to 2,800,000 AU/s. One
percent of his Soul Crystal's mass burns away each time.
**The real phenomenon.** **Renormalisation-group flow**, and the page's choice of analogue is
both correct and better than it realises.
Coupling constants are not constants. They **run** with the energy scale at which you probe the
system, according to β-functions, dg/d ln μ = β(g). The strong coupling α_s falls from roughly
**0.5** at 1 GeV to **0.1180 ± 0.0009** at the Z mass (91.2 GeV). The electromagnetic α rises
from **1/137.036** at zero momentum transfer to about **1/128** at the Z mass. Near a critical
point the correlation length diverges and the system becomes scale-invariant — which is the page's
"the way a system near a critical point briefly behaves as though sampled at a different energy
scale entirely."
**And here is the number the page needs and does not have.** Couplings run **logarithmically**.
The electromagnetic coupling changes by only about **7 percent** across **eleven orders of
magnitude** in energy scale. So a working that "pushes the local coupling constants toward the
values those same laws take at a deeper, Archon-adjacent scale" is making a *modest* change to the
couplings and an **astronomical** change to the implied scale. **A few percent on a coupling is
eleven decades of energy.** That is the honest measure of how violent "proto-Archonic" is, and it
is why the page's post-use collapse is severe rather than tiring: he did not turn a dial, he
sampled the world at an energy no mortal frame is renormalised for.
**The governing law.** dg/d ln μ = β(g), i.e. couplings run with the logarithm of scale; and near
criticality, scale invariance. One consequence the page uses and one it does not. Uses: the
duration must be short, because the state is a critical point and critical points are not stable.
Does not use: **the flow is not free to be partial.** An RG flow moves *all* the couplings that
share a β-function, which is the mechanical reason "Anything inside the radius is answering
rewritten physics for the duration, not merely resisting a stronger version of the ordinary kind."
**Measurable quantities, SI.**
- Radius **1,000 m**, self at centre ⇒ (4/3)π(1,000 m)³ = **4.19×10⁹ m³**.
- Duration **45 s** (page, as given). **This page is the source of the batch's 5 s/turn
conversion** — 45 s read as nine turns — and it is the figure the Drakvor pages contradict at
3 s/turn. `SA-GAP-TURN-LENGTH`.
- Output **2,800,000 AU/s** (page, as given).
- Coupling-run reference figures: α_s 0.5 → 0.1180 over two decades of energy; α 1/137.036 →
≈1/128 over **eleven** decades. **A ~7 % coupling shift implies ~10¹¹ in scale.**
- **Total Aether expressed over the working:** 2.8×10⁶ AU/s × 45 s = **1.26×10⁸ AU**. The system
gives no AU→joule conversion, so this cannot be put in joules
(`SA-GAP-AU-TO-JOULES`), but it is a real derived total and the only one in the batch.
- Cost **1 %** of Soul Crystal mass per use ⇒ **100 uses** if the mass does not return. **That is
the page's own "What nobody knows" turned into a number, and it is derivable from the page's own
figure.**
**The energy budget, and where the joules were.** In the local field, renormalised. He supplies no
joules and the system is explicit that he cannot — "This is why a Crystal can produce an output
that no metabolism could sustain, and why the practitioner is nevertheless destroyed by producing
it. The energy was in the room. The structure that let it move in that particular direction was in
the person, and **structure is what fails**"
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Physical Account Two Sets of Books.md` §III). **On this page the
structure that fails is measurable: one percent of a Soul Crystal, per use, permanently.** No other
page in the batch bills in Crystal mass, and it is the most honest invoice among the twenty-eight
because it is the only one denominated in the thing the working actually consumes.
**Precedent we are not copying.** Limited-duration ascended states are the genre's central
engine — Super Saiyan, Bankai, Hollow masks, Eight Gates. Those cost stamina, or a timer, or
a beating. This one costs **a fixed, measurable, irreversible fraction of the organ that does the
casting**, which makes the technique a countdown with exactly a hundred numbers on it. The
divergence is arithmetic: the character has a finite number of these and can count them.

#### 2 · Stratal account (metaphysics)

**Aether stratum.** 4.19×10⁹ m³, with the local couplings altered rather than the local Essence
merely spent — which is a categorically different kind of draw from anything else in this batch.
Caloria wants "Steep thermal gradients, combustible atmosphere, geothermal ground"; Fulguria wants
"Dry air, conductive substrate, clear line of sight"
(`The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings`, Environmental Coupling). **Residue:** a
kilometre of ground that was briefly renormalised. **This should be the most readable residue in
the setting and the page does not mention it at all** — Anamnesis's law is that "Objects genuinely
store their history in physically readable form"
(`Limina — Entropy, Void and Mind.md`), and a district sampled at a different scale would carry
that in its materials. Offered as texture. **Saturation:** as on `Crimson Dirge`, a kilometre-wide
Zenith working is a saturation candidate and the system's consequence for engineered saturation is
"Crystal Fracture Events in everyone exposed, **including the person who caused it**"
(`The Core Vocabulary` §II) — which is a second, independent derivation of the page's own "severe
collapse post-use." Logged under `SA-GAP-DIRGE-SATURATION`.
**Wellspring stratum.** **Exuroth · *The Trial Flame*** (primary), Family **Caloria**, Physics
Domain **thermodynamics**. Its law, verbatim from
`wiki/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Caloria — Thermodynamics.md`:
> "A steel quenched from austenite is hard and useless, brittle to the point of shattering.
> Tempering restores toughness by permitting controlled diffusion. Exuroth runs the Crystal on
> that cycle: repeated thermal shock followed by controlled recovery, so the lattice arrives at a
> hardness the same material could never have reached by slow cooling."
**The page's use is exactly right:** "Exuroth supplies the tolerance to survive holding that state
open." A quench-temper law is a *survival* law, not an output law, and it is the correct current to
be load-bearing for a state the practitioner should not be able to hold.
**Catharsis · *The Wellspring of Release*** (secondary) — law quoted in full in the
`Crimson Dirge` account — "supplies the release that lets it end cleanly instead of snapping
back." Also right: a latent-heat law absorbs an enormous transition at constant temperature, which
is what a controlled exit from a critical state needs. **Judicium** (secondary) is carried without
a stated job on this page, unlike on his other three.
Register failures in play. Exuroth's "**Quench cracking.** Cycle too fast without the tempering
interval and the lattice arrives hard, brittle, and split along its own thermal gradients" —
**which is the post-use collapse, and it is why repeated use is worse than long use.** Catharsis's
"**Supercooling.** The transition fails to nucleate … and when it finally goes it goes all at
once" — which is what a *failed* exit looks like.
*Four Theories.* **The Debt Position.** A borrowing of authority that is settled in Crystal mass
the practitioner cannot get back is credit with a collateral clause
(`Two Sets of Books` §IV). The page's own framing — "having run laws that were never Francis's to
keep permanently" — is Debt language. The Accord licenses under Consent. **Noted, not resolved.**
**Essence stratum.** Active layer: **all three**, which is rare and appropriate — the **Essence
Core **holds the doctrine being borrowed against, the** Aether Shell** carries 2.8×10⁶ AU/s, the
**Attraction Layer** holds a kilometre claim. Developmental Tier at Stage XIV: **Crystallized
Soul** — "Self and law correspond with near-perfect precision. An exact instrument of identity.
**Thought carries causal weight**" (Part Twenty-One), which is the structural prerequisite for a
working that rewrites local law by intending to. Crystal State: the card says **"Absolute Crystal,
fractured along the paradox lines his own inversion chains cut." But Absolute is a
Developmental Tier, not a Crystal State**, and the system's three States are "**Fractured**,
**Refined**, and **Overgrown**" — "Crystallized is not a State; self-as-law belongs to the
Crystallized Soul tier alone" (`The Core Vocabulary` §III). Moreover Part Twenty-One puts Absolute
at "Class Ω, the Apotheosis approach," two Tiers past Stage XIV's Crystallized Soul. **The card
puts a Tier in the State field and picks the wrong Tier.** Logged
`SA-CROSS-FRANCIS-ABSOLUTE-CRYSTAL`. On the system's own vocabulary the reading his card describes
is **Fractured**, and the card's own clause says so — "fractured along the paradox lines."
**The invoice:** 1 % of Soul Crystal mass per use.
**And that invoice interacts with Flux Density in a way nobody has worked out.** Flux Density is
**EU per gram of Crystal mass**. Burn 1 % of the mass and one of two things happens, and the system
does not say which: either **EU is unchanged and Flux Density rises by 1/0.99 = +1.01 %** — the
technique makes him denser each time — or **EU scales with mass, EU falls 1 % and Flux Density is
unchanged **— the technique makes him poorer each time.** Same page, same figure, opposite
character arcs.** Logged `SA-GAP-CRYSTAL-MASS-LOSS`; this is the single most consequential
underivable quantity in the batch.
**The lens: theosis, and the Celestial Hierarchy.** Pseudo-Dionysius, *De
caelesti hierarchia* (c. 500), with Maximus the Confessor's development of
*theosis*: a creature may participate in the divine **energies** without ever
touching the divine **essence**, and the participation is granted from above and
mediated down a ranked order rather than seized from below. Nothing in the
history of ideas maps as cleanly onto *"a temporary step toward proto-Archonic
function"*: borrowed authority, exercised in a lower register, lapsing when the
grant lapses. The Dionysian hierarchy even supplies the reason the page's
Counterplay works — *"an Archonic mandate outranks a proto-Archonic borrowing of
the same register outright"* — because in a ranked order the higher rank does not
fight the lower one, it simply declines to recognise it. And the lens is wrong in
the one place that matters, which is why it is worth naming. In the tradition,
theosis **perfects** the creature; it costs nothing and subtracts nothing, and
repeated ascent is repeated purification. This working burns **one percent of his
Soul Crystal's mass every time it is used**, permanently, and the system has no
rule for regrowing Crystal mass at all (`SA-GAP-CRYSTAL-MASS-LOSS`). A school
reading Francis through theosis will therefore counsel him to ascend often and
call the toll a purgation — and will be talking a man into spending himself down
to nothing by degrees, with the paradox recoil at the end of each use as the only
warning anyone is given. That is the most dangerous lens in this batch, and it is
the most flattering, which is usually the same observation.

#### 3 · Mechanism (the effect)

**Glyph (proposed; the page names none).** `[AeV]` **Era** · Root · Valen — the one form among the
thirty that names a limit on *scale* rather than on a place or a thing, which is precisely what an
RG flow moves — laid across `[Ae]` **Vastness** · Root · Zhaeren for the kilometre. Both from the
thirty unclassified forms in
`wiki/The Magic System/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms.md`.
*Filing decision, not canon — Isaac's ruling.*
**What boundary moves.** One quantity: **the energy scale at which the local couplings are
evaluated, inside 1,000 m, for 45 s.** Not any individual law. The scale they are all read at.
**What the law then does on its own.** Everything, and the page's limits are the flow's own
properties. It cannot be partial, because an RG flow moves every coupling that shares a β-function
— hence "rewritten equations" rather than a stronger version of the ordinary kind. It cannot last,
because a critical point is not a stable state. It cannot overlap World Echelon, because two
incompatible renormalisations of one region are two boundary conditions on one field. **And it
cannot be held without damage, because his frame was renormalised for the scale he lives at and is
now sitting at another one** — which is the mass burn, and it is the Index's warning arriving with
a percentage attached: "The glyph ends. The law does not."
**Law V check.** Stage XIV sits far above Refraction, so no material anchor is required
(`wiki/The Magic System/The Four Crafts.md`). The page gives only commitment — "Francis commits to
the transformation and holds nothing back" — which is the **Internalised** vector, the high form,
and the correct one for a working with no external component at all. **Of the four Zenith pages in
this batch, this is the one whose Craft and Stage agree most cleanly.**
**Failure mode — Structural fault, unambiguously.** "The Crystal could not hold what the boundary
permitted. Overchannel, Backlash, Fracture. **The working succeeds and the practitioner does
not**" (`Two Sets of Books` §VII). One percent of the Crystal, per use, measured. There is no
clearer instance in the twenty-eight.
The page also flags a gate problem itself and flags it correctly: "Dominion Sovereignty's Stage VI
Fate-Path gate is not met by a pure Spirit Path, and the paradox recoil the card lists is at least
partly that gate reasserting itself the moment the borrowed authority lapses." **Checked against
Part Seven: "Sovereignty requires Fate Path at Stage VI to exceed A, and Stage IX to exceed SSS
where the imposed law encodes abstract conceptual truths." At EX-Grade he is far past SSS, so the
Stage IX Fate gate binds too, and a pure Spirit Path meets neither.** The page's reasoning is
sound and the gate is worse than it says. `SA-NUM-DIRGE-ASCENSION-PATHGATE`.
**What the target sees and feels.** Forty-five seconds inside a kilometre, in
which the ordinary answers stop being the right answers. The page is careful
about what this is not: anything inside the radius *"is answering rewritten
physics for the duration, not merely resisting a stronger version of the ordinary
kind."* What the mechanism actually moves is how **strongly** each law applies at
his scale — the coupling constants pushed toward the values they take at an
Archon-adjacent depth — so the experience from inside is not of new laws but of
familiar ones with the wrong weights: force that should have been survivable is
not, a distance that should have been far is not, and every practised judgment is
calibrated to a world that is no longer the one in the room. His output reads at
**2,800,000 AU/s** while it holds. Then it ends, on the clock, whether or not
anyone made it end — and it ends into recoil, because the borrowed authority
lapses and the Fate-Path gate it never satisfied *"reasserts itself the moment the
borrowed authority lapses."* A defender who is still standing at forty-five
seconds is standing in front of a man who has just paid one percent of his own
Crystal and got nothing back.
**What bleeds, at the stated efficiency — and this is where the page becomes derivable.** The card
gives **η = 0.93**. Stage XIV places him in **Tier 8 · Archmaster**, band **0.95–1.2**
(Part Nineteen). **0.93 sits below the floor by 0.02** — the smallest of the three η discrepancies
in the batch but the one with the most riding on it, because η is the multiplier in the one closed
formula the system supplies. `SA-NUM-ETA-TIER`.
At η 0.93, **7 percent** of every expenditure leaves as heat, sound and structural bleed. **At
η 1.0 or above — which his tier's band says he should be at — the sign flips and the environment
becomes a co-author:** "Above 1.0 the Continuum recognizes the expression as law and supplements it
with ambient flow; the environment becomes a co-author" (Part Nineteen). **Whether Francis is
losing 7 % or being *fed* by the district is the difference between a technique that burns him and
a technique the world helps him run**, and the card and the ladder disagree about which.

**The Counterplay routes that work** — including the two the page does not name
(`wiki/The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner.md`).
- **Deny the field — the named route.** A Nihiloth-mediated null field, with the
page's own caveat that a full null against an EX-Grade spike is not assured.
The register's **Silence** is the general form: *"the removal of magical
possibility from a region."* Families **Caloria / Fulguria**, whose failure
terrains — thermal equilibrium, saturated cold, dense fog, a shielded interior
— reach the Wellsprings underneath even where they cannot touch the borrowed
authority on top.
- **Break the man — the clock and the recoil, unnamed by the page.** Forty-five
seconds is stated, fixed, and short, and the working ends into a recoil the card
itself lists. This is **not** the register's forbidden plan of *"surviving until
he tires"*, because the duration is not a guess: it is printed. Survive a
counted window, then fight the recoil.
- **Break the man — Rigidity, on the ledger.** *"A Trait's law is not negotiable
by its bearer."* Neither is one percent per use. An opponent who forces him to
spend the transformation on something that does not need it — a feint at a
kilometre's scale, a threat to something he must answer — has taken a permanent
bite out of him without being in the room at the end. And *"cannot overlap
World Echelon"* means every use of this is a use of **not** that.
- **Break the boundary — the gate he never met.** The working runs on Dominion
Sovereignty past thresholds that require a Fate Path he does not have. *"Force
the Seal early. … A warrant overstating standing seals anyway, with the
difference deducted from the speaker at the moment of closing. A third party
who can compel an early close is not defending. They are billing him."* This is
the most precise counter available against a borrowed warrant, and it is in the
register rather than on the page.
**The tell, stated plainly.** He commits totally and a kilometre of world changes
weight. Everything about it is visible; nothing about it is quiet; and it is over
in forty-five seconds by its own terms.

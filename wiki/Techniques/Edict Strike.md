# Edict Strike

## Edict Strike

### Summary card

**Effect** · A downward blade strike gains metaphysical mass in proportion to the hesitation Drakvor reads in his target; armour built to withstand less than B-Grade force buckles under it.
**Cost** · Aether strain through his wrists and shoulders with every full swing.
**Limit** · The windup is slow and telegraphs itself; anyone reading the strike coming has time to answer it.
**Counter** · Resolve-based Wellsprings deny the strike the doubt it needs to double its weight; high-speed repositioning and sublimation-type evasions both get a body out from under it before it lands.
**What nobody knows.** Why Judicium can price a target's hesitation at all, in exactly the units a blade needs to grow heavier by.

### Design Chain

**Trigger** · Drakvor commits to the downward strike against a named target within melee range.
**Function** · Offence carrying a psychological debuff: the more doubt the target holds, the heavier the blow lands.
**Mechanism** · Judicium strengthens Gnosis Acuity and Gnosis Analysis, the same reading that lets a Judicium practitioner see weakness the way literate people read text. Here it reads the target's own hesitation and feeds that reading into the strike's weight. Coagula strengthens Resilience Integrity and Tempering Coherence, gathering that borrowed weight into the blade itself rather than letting it disperse on the swing.
**Numerical Effect** · The baseline strike sits at Stage VIII's S-Grade ceiling, 0.5 to 2 TN peak force, 4.184 to 24.3 TJ. Against a fully hesitant target the impact doubles, which the Stage can sustain without instability, since Stage VIII's own late push runs toward SS-Grade before any Overchannel risk applies. Windup and strike together run roughly three seconds, matching the source's single old-count turn.
**Target Response** · The blade darkens into geometric iron-black facets as the strike builds. The air pulls inward just before it falls. The impact lands with a verdict-like thud.
**Consequence** · A downward strike carrying doubled weight against a hesitant target; armour rated below B-Grade buckles outright.
**Limitation** · The windup is slow and read-heavy, the same mechanism that reads the target's doubt also telegraphing the strike itself.
**Weakness** · Agile or genuinely fearless targets deny the strike both an opening and the doubt it feeds on.
**Cost** · Aether strain in his wrists and shoulders on every full swing.
**Counterplay** · A Resolve-aligned Wellspring keeps a target's doubt from registering at all. Outrunning the windup with speed, or slipping clear through a sublimation-type evasion, both work before the blade ever arrives.

### FOW line

Governing Primary: Gnosis, off Judicium's own Gnosis Acuity and Gnosis Analysis, with Resilience Integrity and Tempering Coherence carried in from Coagula for the strike's gathered weight, and Ardency Flux as the delivery stat for the raw force of the blow itself. Stage floor Stage VIII, Transcendence. Grade required S-Grade. Path gate Body. Resonant Pair: none.

### Origin

Drakvor derived this himself, the same signature pairing as Bastion Imperium. "Uncertainty is the softest armour" is its doctrine, and as a self-derived technique it is met live, in the exchanges that use it.

# Edict Strike

## Edict Strike

### Summary card

**Effect** · A downward blade strike gains metaphysical mass in proportion to the hesitation the practitioner reads in his target; armour built to withstand less than B-Grade force buckles under it.
**Cost** · Aether strain through his wrists and shoulders with every full swing.
**Limit** · The windup is slow and telegraphs itself; anyone reading the strike coming has time to answer it.
**Counter** · Resolve-based Wellsprings deny the strike the doubt it needs to double its weight; high-speed repositioning and sublimation-type evasions both get a body out from under it before it lands.
**What nobody knows.** Why Judicium can price a target's hesitation at all, in exactly the units a blade needs to grow heavier by.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · Impulse transfer with borrowed effective mass: the blade carries more weight than it has, and the extra is priced by a spectroscopic read of the target's hesitation, a ratio with no units that can multiply a mass.
**Law** · F = dp/dt for the blow; Beer–Lambert, A = ε·c·l, for the read, whose dimensionless absorbance runs from 1 against no doubt to 2 against full hesitation.
**Limit** · A read needs integration time, so the windup is slow; and no edge carries the force, so it lands across the whole struck body as a thud, not a cut.
**Load path** · 0.5 TN through a sword edge would be 5 × 10¹⁸ Pa, far past diamond; the force travels through the gathered weight, and the narrow end of the path is the practitioner's wrists and shoulders.

### Metaphysics

**Aether** · Small draw, melee, one target. Fulguria wants dry air and clear sight; Materia wants worked stone and deep foundation. Ferrous plate partly cages the target against the read. Residue minimal.
**Wellspring** · Judicium, the Wellspring of Truth (Fulguria): every element shows only its own spectrum, so a target performing confidence still shows their own lines and the strike cannot be bluffed.
**Wellspring** · Coagula, the Unifying Pulse (Materia): it gathers the read into the blade as mass.
**Inherited failures** · Judicium's spectral overload floods the practitioner with every composition in view, which is why the windup is slow; Coagula's residual porosity leaves voids in the weight, so a partly hesitant target gets less than a proportional share of the doubling.
**Sub-Stats** · Judicium gives Gnosis Acuity and Analysis; Coagula gives Resilience Integrity and Tempering Coherence; Ardency Flux carries the delivery.
**Essence** · The Essence Core does the read and the Aether Shell the delivery; each full swing loads Aether strain through wrists and shoulders, Clouding at the conduit's end. Developmental Tier Radiant at Transcendence.
**School** · The Imaginative-Virtue tradition holds that imagination does physical work, so its adherents send their fearless forward as immune; they are wrong, since the base blow lands regardless and fearlessness only halves it.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Measure laid across Edge: the working is a measurement, and the strike is the measurement made heavy.
**Boundary** · The effective mass coupled into a falling blade, set equal to a read of the target.
**Effect** · The blade darkens into iron-black facets, the air pulls inward, and about three seconds after commitment it comes straight down with a verdict-like thud; armour below B-Grade buckles. The target cannot tell whether they took a single or a doubled blow.
**Doubled** · Doubling for the same cost is an effective η above 1.0; the surplus is drawn from the room, so a doubled strike sheds less and leaves the ground around the target briefly thinner.
**Hold** · Declared Spellcraft, Ars Vocis, commanded aloud; no material anchor at Transcendence.
**Failure** · Boundary fault, temporal: the read takes time, so the same seconds that measure the target's doubt telegraph the strike and give them time to leave.
**Bleed** · 25 percent of an undoubled swing leaves the Shell at once as heat and sound; it is the loudest thing on the field and is heard across it.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · Black facets crawl across the blade, the air draws in, then it comes straight down; three seconds every time, longer the better the read.
**Limits** · Melee, one named target, one committed downward swing; the doubling needs doubt that is really there; S only from a third of reserve, never SS.
**Fails against** · A target unreadable through a Resolve-aligned Wellspring; speed or a sublimation-type evasion out from under it; a commitment interrupted by a grapple or an ally stepping in; fog, ferrous plate or a shielded interior, which dull the read.

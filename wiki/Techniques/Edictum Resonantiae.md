# Edictum Resonantiae

## Edictum Resonantiae

---

### Edictum Resonantiae

*(The Edict of Resonance)*

#### Summary card

**Effect** · Inside a bounded space, one named kind of interaction is banned or rerouted to a milder outcome. A lethal blow becomes a disarm; a Wellspring is barred from being invoked to shed blood; the Edict, not Serenyra, is what refuses.
**Cost** · She must personally embody the Edict for its duration. Any time she herself violates its own spirit, the backlash tears through her own Soul Crystal, and repeated use roughens her metaphysical voice, making the next Edict harder to sustain without harm.
**Limit** · Only one category of action can be bound per casting, and the narrower and clearer its wording, the stronger the Edict holds.
**Counter** · Another lawcaster can declare an opposing principle; the two resonate against each other and can collapse both. Entities of equal or higher Temperance with strong personal Domains can partly ignore it. A genuine Fate or Archonic mandate overrides her local law outright.
**What nobody knows** · Why an Edict spoken with any sincerity holds, while one spoken cynically, in the letter but not the spirit, still sometimes holds anyway.

#### Design Chain

**Trigger** · A spoken Edict naming exactly one category of action, reinforced by a physical strike into the ground.
**Function** · Establishes a bounded dome inside which the named category of interaction is suppressed or rerouted to an alternative, milder resolution for the Edict's duration.
**Mechanism** · Judicium's law of discernment and consequence is what lets the Edict recognize the named action when it happens; Aurevane carries the enforcement evenly across the whole dome rather than only near her, the same way it carries Nexus Harmoniae's field. Oneirion's minor presence is what lets the wording bend around edge cases, "conceptual flexibility," rather than failing outright the instant an action falls just outside the letter of the phrasing.
**Numerical Effect** · A dome roughly 200 metres in radius, centred on her position, at full potency for about three exchanges, with a weaker lingering echo for one to two exchanges afterward.
**Target Response** · Anyone attempting the bound action inside the dome feels resistance, as though pushing against water, and the action either fails outright or resolves into the Edict's named alternative instead.
**Consequence** · The specific violence, deception, or other named interaction the Edict targets becomes structurally harder to complete inside the dome for as long as it holds.
**Limitation** · Only one category of action per casting. A broadly worded Edict is weaker than a narrow one; the wording is the working.
**Weakness** · A Void-aligned disturbance pits "no law" against "this law" inside the dome, opening dangerous tears where neither fully holds. Entities of equal or higher Temperance with strong personal Domains can partially ignore the Edict outright.
**Cost** · She must personally embody the Edict for as long as it stands; violating its own spirit herself returns the backlash directly to her Soul Crystal. Repeated casting roughens her voice, raising the cost of the next Edict.
**Counterplay** · Another lawcaster can declare an opposing principle, and the two Edicts resonate against each other, sometimes collapsing both. A genuine Fate or Archonic mandate simply overrides hers.

The source calls this "the peak of her art," which places it, by the card's own account, as the last and most demanding of the four entries in this batch, built once the other three were already in hand. No date or specific triggering problem is given beyond that. No location is given beyond the Iridescent Archive filing destination, unattested in current canon.

# Eidolyn Falseface Lattice

## Eidolyn Falseface Lattice

> **Migration note.** No character card exists for Aeldoris in current canon; stats, Aether Class and Path below are conservative estimates inside the documented range for Stage VII (Band C), flagged rather than invented. Eidolyn and Oneirion are confirmed Limina Wellsprings. Somnalis is confirmed real but sits in Fluxia, not Limina as the old card's grouping implied; that correction is made below rather than dropping the Wellspring, since the card cannot make sense without it. "Aether Class: Dreambreaker Aether" converts to the Stage VII/Band-C estimate, Class III, The Resonant, on the same basis as the companion cards. "Duration per turn: 3 turns" converts to Spellcraft's attention-held duration model, current canon having no turn unit; Range (80 m) is a real unit and is kept as given.

### Summary card

**Effect** · Whatever the opponent is afraid the fight contains, Aeldoris gives it a body. The construct is not a picture of the fear. It has the same solidity a fear that specific would have, and it hits like it.
**Cost** · **Identity Drift.** Sustained use blurs where his self-image ends and the borrowed fear-shapes begin. **Cognitive Static** follows: headache, memory flicker, perception distortion.
**Limit** · Needs an actual conviction to template from. A fearless or fully lucid target, or a threat-shape the target has no concept of, gives the working nothing to deposit onto.
**Counter** · **Stand in front of a mirror.** *Specular reflection returns the construct's own image undistorted, and a form templated from someone's imagined threat has no further truth behind it to survive contact with its own unedited reflection.*
**What nobody knows** · Why a construct built from someone else's fear ever agrees to fight for Aeldoris rather than against him.

### Design Chain

**Trigger** · Reactive: releases against an incoming hostile action rather than on Aeldoris's own initiative, once a target within 80 metres registers as carrying fear he can read.
**Function** · Offensive and defensive illusory projection. Manifests the opponent's own imagined threat as a construct solid enough to strike with and be struck by.
**Mechanism** · Somnalis perceives adjacent possibilities as clearly as present fact, which is how Aeldoris reads the specific shape a target's fear is modelling before it happens. Oneirion locates that modelled shape as a coordinate in conceptual space, the same manifold any configuration of a soul's arrangement occupies. Eidolyn then deposits along that coordinate the way any supersaturated medium organises around an imposed template, and the construct's solidity is consequently real material behaviour rather than a trick of light. Because the template's intensity scales with how convinced the target's own mind is, more conviction feeds more mass into the deposit; **the technique borrows force from belief because Eidolyn's law has always translated intent into structural strength, and belief is simply intent under pressure.**
**Numerical Effect** · Effective radius 80 metres. Holds for as long as attention holds under Spellcraft's own rule, longer than the companion Court technique per the old card's comparative duration figure, though no fixed clock is invented here.
**Target Response** · Multiple targets sharing a common dread produce a shared, larger template; a single deeply convinced target produces one dense construct rather than several.
**Consequence** · The construct fights with the mass and reach its template implies for as long as the working holds, then dissolves back into ambient Aether when attention releases it.
**Limitation** · Eidolyn's own documented failure mode is a template defect: any flaw in the source intent is faithfully reproduced at scale. A target with no defined fear-concept, or a fear too diffuse to template, gives the working nothing coherent to deposit onto, and a fearless or fully lucid mind supplies too little conviction to feed real solidity into the deposit.
**Weakness** · A Letheveil-disciplined mind identifies the fear-model as load-bearing or not and discards what does not need carrying, denying Eidolyn a stable template before it ever forms. Mirror surfaces are a documented hard counter, per the Summary card above.
**Cost** · Identity Drift and Cognitive Static are the price of routing someone else's conviction through his own Aether Shell repeatedly across an engagement.
**Counterplay** · Empathy-based Essence, of the kind Catharsis or Benediction practitioners carry, breaks the fear-loop the construct depends on to stay fed. Mental discipline against unexamined memory intrusion, and a well-placed mirror, both apply directly.

### Origin

The card gives Aeldoris as sole practitioner and states the problem plainly: a fight against something that has already decided to be afraid of him is a fight he can win by giving the fear a body and letting it swing at its own owner. No teacher, incident or era is given, and none is added here.

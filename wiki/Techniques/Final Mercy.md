# Final Mercy

## Final Mercy

---

### Summary card

**Effect** · One point takes the whole of what the practitioner is carrying. What lands does not wound the target's body. It removes the target's claim to keep having one.
**Cost** · **The erasure is not selective.** *His own count puts the toll near four percent memory bleed per use, and there is no way yet found to choose which four percent goes.*
**Limit** · **Cannot be used while Dirge Ascension is active.** *Whatever Ascension is doing to him, it is doing it with the same architecture Final Mercy needs, and both cannot have it at once.*
**Counter** · **A Crystal with structurally little independent selfhood has very little for the working to seize.** *An externally Throne-anchored Crystal, the standing architecture documented for the Celestial Host, is already recorded as near-immune to identity-based Aether attacks for exactly this reason.*
> **What nobody knows.** Why the collapse runs Soul, then memory, then Principle alignment, in that order, and not the reverse. Every other erasure working documented in the current register runs backward from this one.

### Origin

Its author built this alone. Its philosophy line, "True mercy is silence," is its author's own justification rather than a borrowed doctrine. As a self-derived working it is uncountered by anyone who has not watched it used live.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · Projective measurement and its back-action: a strong enough measurement does not report a state, it imposes one, as repeated measurement freezes or hastens a decay in the Zeno and anti-Zeno effects.
**Law** · The projection postulate: a strong measurement of an observable leaves the system in one of its definite states; erasing information costs at least kT·ln2 per bit (the Landauer bound).
**Limit** · Past the point where the measurement's pull exceeds the system's own dynamics, the read becomes the verdict; a system with no definite persistence state of its own has nothing to be projected into.
**Compression** · Quench and temper: steel hardens from about 200 to 700 HV, 3.5 times, at the price of brittleness; the working runs that cycle on the practitioner's own output.
**Energy** · Erasing a whole mind costs 14 microjoules, less than a housefly in flight; the EX Grade pays for a boundary an entire identity must conform to, not for the erasure.

### Metaphysics

**Aether** · A single point at 150 m, the narrowest Zenith footprint; Fulguria wants dry air and clear sight. The impact site goes emotionally silent, the absence of a killing's residue, which only an Eclipseron reader can work.
**Wellspring** · Judicium, the Wellspring of Truth (Fulguria): every element shows only its own spectrum; turned outward at full compression, the read stops reporting on the target's continuance and adjudicates it.
**Wellspring** · Exuroth, the Trial Flame (Caloria): supplies the compression, the quench-temper cycle run on output.
**Inherited failures** · Judicium's spectral overload means the read cannot be aimed at one line, so the caster's own memory goes with each use; Exuroth's quench cracking arrives as paradox recoil.
**Firing condition** · The practitioner must already hold a verdict correct by Judicium's own measure; a strike thrown without it does not carry the working.
**Essence** · The verdict is held in the Essence Core and discharged through the Aether Shell; it seizes the target's Attraction Layer, where a Crystal's persistence claim lives. Developmental Tier Crystallized Soul at Zenith.
**School** · The Percipient school holds that to be is to be perceived and that nothing truly lapses, so they search for the erased as merely hidden; the silent ground says there is nothing to find.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Measure laid across Ruin: a measurement on an edge makes a blow heavier; on ruin it makes a verdict.
**Boundary** · The strength of one persistence measurement, pushed past where its back-action exceeds the target's own dynamics.
**Effect** · A strike, then no wound: Soul, memory and Principle alignment collapse together, Soul first since it is most strongly coupled, with no interval in which anything can be arrested. Instant, no sustain.
**Exclusive** · It cannot run while Dirge Ascension is active; both compress the same architecture and a reserve cannot be hardened into two shapes at once.
**Hold** · Internalised Spellcraft, a ritual-fast chain held and released; no material anchor at Zenith.
**Failure** · Boundary fault: the read has no aperture, and the unintended place it runs is the practitioner's own memory. Field fault on the target's side: a Crystal with no independent selfhood presents nothing to project.
**Bleed** · 5 percent at η 0.95 arrives at once at the practitioner as paradox recoil, heat at the Shell, with quench cracking at any η; above η 1 the surplus thins the room instead. A Principle Engine is the one relief valve.

**Fails against** · A persistence claim anchored outside the Crystal beforehand (a Throne anchor of the Celestial Host pattern, a Lock); a target whose guilt is arguable; its one use spent on the wrong target; distance beyond 150 m; numbers.

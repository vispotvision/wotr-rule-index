# Fluxus Intervallum

## Fluxus Intervallum

> Migration note. The source names its Wellspring as "the Wellspring of Strain, Atla'zon's Domain." Atla'zon is a real Titan (Prime Physical Titan, the World-Binder), but a Titan's Ground is Titanic Law, not one of the Sixty, and the attested precedent for Titan-keyed workings gates them at Zenith or higher, far above the Stage this card describes. The technique is reassigned instead to Transference, an attested Wellspring of the Spatium family (Spatial Geometry domain), whose real mechanism, resonant transfer between coupled oscillators where Essence moves along a gradient rather than being pushed, matches the card's own stated philosophy ("there is no between, only connection") almost exactly. "Path: Mind Path" is struck for the same reason given throughout this migration: Mind is not one of the four Paths and reads as Gnosis weighting. "Aether Class: Lattice/Conduit" is dropped; it is invented free text and, in any case, a technique's Codex line does not carry Aether Class. The old Stage label "Stage V, Benediction" is corrected: Benediction is not among the sixteen Stage names (it names a Wellspring resonance and, per Volume V's own established correction, maps to Stage VIII when used as a Temperance label), so the numeral V stands and the name is corrected to Splintering, the FOW Stage V. Grade required is not given; it is set to B-Grade, the maximum Tier Grade of the Stage immediately below the floor (Stage IV, Flourishing), the conservative reading consistent with the delegated numeric rule. Category is assigned as Vectra, the motion, direction, and spatial-gravitation category whose own listed examples include Aetherstep, essentially this technique by another name. Craft is Spellcraft: the activation is silent and internalised, held in the Crystal rather than spoken, written, or bottled, which the Four Crafts register as Spellcraft's high form. Of the listed counters, Fixatio, Tenebra, and the Titan Zar'thuun are verified and kept; "Concord Bind" is unattested in current canon and is dropped; "Archonic Reprimand" is likewise unattested and is replaced with reference to a Judicium-mediated interdiction field, an attested Wellspring effect with the same lawful-interference character. The named technique counters on the card (Resonance Breaker, Concord Severance, Gravitic Shear) are not attested as canon techniques and are rewritten as functional counterplay rather than invented proper nouns. The source gives no inventor, era, or originating problem; Origin below states that gap rather than inventing one.

### Summary card

**Effect** · The caster fixes on a second point of mass in line of sight and, in a single instant, is there instead of here, having genuinely crossed the intervening distance rather than having erased and remade themselves at the far end.
**Cost** · Arrival costs a half-turn of vertiginous stagger while the body's sense of gravity catches up to where it now stands, and repeated use leaves the caster's Crystal ringing like a struck bell, with a real risk of learning to see people as nodes on a map rather than as people.
**Limit** · The path must be a straight, unobstructed line between two masses; anything intersecting it stops the flow outright, and the technique does not function where gravity is absent or actively chaotic.
**Counter** · A Fixatio-based anchor on either endpoint prevents the coupling from forming at all, and Tenebra-aligned or Zar'thuun-aligned power erases the line before it can be used.
**What nobody knows** · Whether the momentary state between departure and arrival is truly instantaneous to the caster, or whether it only reads that way to everyone else.

### Design Chain

**Trigger** · A calm, held state, gaze locked between the caster and a second point of mass, released on a single slow exhale.
**Function** · The exhale authorises and directs a Transference coupling between the caster's own Attraction Layer and the chosen mass, then collapses the caster along it.
**Mechanism** · Transference's real analogue is resonant energy transfer between two oscillators tuned to the same frequency: held close enough, they exchange energy with no conductor between them, and the coupling falls off sharply with distance. The caster makes their own Attraction Layer such an oscillator, tuned to the second mass. They do not push themselves across the gap. The two are briefly in tune, and the gradient does the rest, which is why the crossing is real traversal at extraordinary speed and never a disappearance and a remaking.
**Numerical Effect** · At the required B-Grade output, the caster's frame briefly carries kinetic energy on the order of 1 to 5 gigantonewtons of peak force, roughly 1.046 to 46 gigajoules, a building-to-large-building-level release, discharged entirely into the crossing itself rather than into anything struck. Range is thirty metres, line of sight, two points of mass required.
**Target Response** · The caster's body attenuates and reconstitutes at the far mass with no intervening state anyone watching can resolve; a struck projectile carried along its own vector diverts rather than stopping.
**Consequence** · The crossing is instantaneous and counts as the caster's movement action for that instant; casting again requires a new pair of anchors and a fresh coupling.
**Limitation** · Both anchors must be genuine mass, the line between them must be unbroken and straight, and the technique fails outright in a zone with no meaningful gravity or with chaotic, shifting gravity.
**Weakness** · A Fixatio-based anchor on the line prevents the coupling from forming; Tenebra-aligned or Zar'thuun-aligned power erases the strain-line the coupling depends on; a lawful interdiction field built on Judicium creates enough dissonance in the coupling to make it misfire.
**Cost** · Arrival lags: the caster's own sense of orientation and gravity trail reality by a beat, leaving them staggered and off-balance for a second or so, and sustained use of the technique wears a groove that makes people start reading as points in a network rather than as people.
**Counterplay** · Break the line physically (any obstruction placed between the two anchors before the exhale completes), disrupt the coupling with a Fixatio anchor or a Tenebra or Zar'thuun-aligned unmaking, or force the crossing to overshoot by distorting the destination mass's own gravity during the coupling window.

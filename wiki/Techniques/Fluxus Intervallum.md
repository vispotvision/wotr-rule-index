# Fluxus Intervallum

## Fluxus Intervallum

### Summary card

**Effect** · The caster fixes on a second point of mass in line of sight and, in a single instant, is there instead of here, having genuinely crossed the intervening distance rather than having erased and remade themselves at the far end.
**Cost** · Arrival costs a half-turn of vertiginous stagger while the body's sense of gravity catches up to where it now stands, and repeated use leaves the caster's Crystal ringing like a struck bell, with a real risk of learning to see people as nodes on a map rather than as people.
**Limit** · The path must be a straight, unobstructed line between two masses; anything intersecting it stops the flow outright, and the technique does not function where gravity is absent or actively chaotic.
**Counter** · A Fixatio-based anchor on either endpoint prevents the coupling from forming at all, and Tenebra-aligned or Zar'thuun-aligned power erases the line before it can be used.
**What nobody knows** · Whether the momentary state between departure and arrival is truly instantaneous to the caster, or whether it only reads that way to everyone else.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · Resonant energy transfer between coupled oscillators: two things tuned to the same frequency and held near each other swap energy through their near fields with no conductor, as in wireless charging. The crossing is real, continuous travel, never unmaking and remaking.
**Law** · Coupled-mode theory: transfer is complete in a time π/(2κ), where the coupling κ falls roughly as 1/d³ and works only when it far exceeds each end's own losses.
**Limit** · Both ends must be real mass, the line must be straight and clear, and doubling the distance cuts the coupling eightfold, so the range is thirty metres.
**Seen** · Thirty metres takes about 1 to 60 ms; a slow crossing is a streak along the line to C-Grade reflexes and up, the fastest a jump to anyone short of A-Grade. It is never instantaneous.

|---|---|---|
| Range | line of sight, two masses | 30 m |

| Transit at that speed | 30 m ÷ v | 0.88 to 5.9 ms |
| Reflexes that resolve it | C 5 to 15 ms, B 1 to 5 ms, A 0.2 to 1 ms | streak or jump by watcher |

### Metaphysics

**Aether** · A thin line between two masses; Spatium wants Veil-thin, surveyed ground and fails on warp scarring, contested Domains and unmapped terrain. The strain-line forms before the crossing, can be attacked, and lingers to deafen Harmonists.
**Wellspring** · Transference, the Living Exchange (Spatium): two tuned oscillators held near each other exchange energy without a conductor, falling off sharply with distance; it makes the Attraction Layer such an oscillator.
**Inherited failures** · Transference's reverse coupling: a gradient runs both ways, so a destination at higher potential draws the caster in and they overshoot; it can be arranged from outside.
**Sub-Stats** · Harmonics Synergy, Harmonics Projection, Tempering Yield.
**Essence** · The Attraction Layer pays, tuned as an oscillator; long use wears a groove toward Obsession Force, reading people as points of mass. Developmental Tier Harmonic at Splintering.
**School** · The Dichotomists hold that crossing any interval is impossible, so they read the working as translation with no path; they never think to put a shield on the line, the cheapest counter there is.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Passage laid across Equivalence: Passage is the crossing, Equivalence the tuning that makes two masses exchangeable.
**Boundary** · The resonant frequency of the caster's Attraction Layer, set equal to a chosen mass's.
**Effect** · A held gaze on a mass, a visible calm, a slow exhale, and the caster is at the far mass. A projectile struck on the line is carried along its own vector and diverts. Then a stagger in the new place.
**Hold** · Below Refraction an external anchor is needed: one slow exhale, with the gaze as the targeting; the exhale is the timing window.
**Failure** · Field fault: no gravity, chaotic gravity or a Fixatio anchor on either end and the coupling never forms. Boundary fault over years: tuned on mass, the caster's sense of people drifts toward mass.
**Stagger** · About half a turn of vertigo on arrival while the inner ear catches up, and the Crystal rings like a struck bell.
**Bleed** · 35 percent leaves the Shell as heat, sound and light at both ends: a crack and flash where the caster left and another where they land.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · The caster goes still, locks their gaze on a mass up to thirty metres off and breathes out slowly: destination and trigger both on show. The strain-line lingers after.
**Limits** · Thirty metres, a clear straight line between two real masses, a calm and an exhale to release, none where gravity is absent or chaotic, half a turn of stagger each arrival.
**Fails against** · Anything on the line before the exhale ends; an endpoint Locked with Fixatio; a strain-line erased by Tenebra or Zar'thuun; a Judicium interdiction, which misfires it; a destination raised in potential; whatever waits at the arrival.

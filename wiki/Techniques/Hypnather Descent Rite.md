# Hypnather Descent Rite

## Hypnather Descent Rite

### Summary card

**Effect** · Aeldoris forces a target's Crystal toward a rest-state relaxation it never consented to enter, then interrupts it mid-collapse. The body slows, the memory fogs, and for a moment the target is seeing something that is not in the room.
**Cost** · **Sleep Debt.** Running three interrupted processes through his own Shell at once destabilises his own waking mind. **Memory Backlash** risks pulling some of the target's own fear or trauma back through him in the process.
**Limit** · Needs a target with an actual biological consciousness capable of genuine rest in the first place; nothing without that architecture has a relaxation process to interrupt. Needs line-of-sight, to keep the forced trajectory pinned to one body rather than letting it dissipate the way an honest Oneirion working would.
**Counter** · **Do not let him find you quiescent.** *Hypnather's own law states the reorganisation happens during genuine rest and cannot be forced; a hard enough shock breaks the rest-state precondition before the lock sets.*
**What nobody knows** · Why the held forget their own purpose before they forget the pain, the same open question Vohrin's Stillwater Rite carries, and whether the two are drawing on the same underlying law.

### Design Chain

**Trigger** · Declared, line-of-sight held on a target within 60 metres for the duration.
**Function** · Debuff: paralysis, sensory distortion and short-term amnesia inflicted at once by forcing three Limina laws to fail on command instead of by accident.
**Mechanism** · Hypnather's real law is structural relaxation, the same behaviour that lets a supercooled glass keep settling toward order after it stops moving, and it only runs during genuine quiescence. Aeldoris forces the target's Crystal into that quiescent condition against its will through the line-of-sight anchor, then holds the process partway, producing Hypnather's own documented failure state on demand: the configuration locks where it stood, worse-ordered than before it began, which reads on a living body as limb-heaviness and slowed reflexes. Simultaneously he runs a forced Letheveil pass, compression performed on someone else's memory without their cooperation in identifying what is load-bearing, which produces indiscriminate generation loss rather than Letheveil's intended clean edit, and surfaces as short-term amnesia. Oneirion, meanwhile, is made to commit a deliberate projection error: a trajectory correct in configuration space is landed on purpose at a coordinate the target's actual senses did not generate, which is the false sensory input.
**Numerical Effect** · Effective range 60 metres, the shortest reach of Aeldoris's three documented techniques, held only for as long as attention and line-of-sight both persist under Spellcraft's own rule.
**Target Response** · A living target with no unusual Path-resistance experiences the interrupted-relaxation lock, the memory gap and the false sensory layer together, reading as a forced half-sleep with no clean edge to it.
**Consequence** · The target is left slower, foggier, and less certain what just happened, for as long past the working's release as the three interrupted processes take to settle honestly on their own.
**Limitation** · Nothing without a biological consciousness has a Hypnather-style relaxation process at all, so the rite has no purchase on constructs, the undead, or anything else outside that architecture. The line-of-sight anchor is required because without it the Oneirion component's forced landing has nothing to pin its coordinate to and simply misses, the way an honest projection error does.
**Weakness** · A well-developed Spirit Path Crystal resists having its own configuration-space trajectory hijacked, since Spirit Path is itself built on precise, coherent projection. Verdantia's regenerative pressure can outpace the artificial lock before it fully sets.
**Cost** · Sleep Debt and Memory Backlash both follow from routing three forced failure-states through his own Shell as the conduit; some of what he extracts does not stay with the target.
**Counterplay** · Hypnather's law is explicit that the process only runs during genuine rest, so a sudden, hard-enough shock, pain-trigger or otherwise, breaks the quiescence the rite depends on and ejects the target before the lock takes hold.

### Origin

The card names Aeldoris as sole practitioner and gives the rite's purpose as its own philosophy states it: descent reveals what a target fears the instant they blink. No teacher, incident or era is given, and none is added here. That Aeldoris carries no character card in current canon is noted above rather than papered over.

# Letheveil Memory-Quake

## Letheveil Memory-Quake

### Summary card

**Effect** · He shakes loose whatever a mind, or a working, has not yet anchored, and the last few seconds go with it.
**Cost** · Every use costs him one of his own minor memories, and he comes out of the burst briefly confused himself.
**Limit** · Only reaches memory too fresh to be load-bearing; older memory and unconscious targets are untouched.
**Counter** · Pain restores what the wave was about to take, a held anchor survives it, and a mind with no resting state gives it nothing to widen into.
> **What nobody knows.** Which of his own memories Recall Slip has already taken, and whether he would recognise the gap if it had.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · The memory-consolidation window: a fresh trace is fragile until protein synthesis sets it, and a concussive shock erases the seconds to minutes before it while older memory survives.
**Law** · Consolidation is a threshold in time: a trace crosses from fragile to stable, and only the fragile side can be reached.
**Limit** · Older memory, unconscious minds and a trace rehearsed against a held outside cue all sit beyond reach; the erasure itself costs less than an attojoule.
**Counter physics** · A strong adrenaline signal at or after encoding strengthens a trace, so a sharp pain restores what was about to slip.
**Joules** · Erasing this much information costs almost nothing; every joule spent goes into the boundary, reaching the sphere and telling fragile traces from set ones.

### Metaphysics

**Aether** · Spread over the sphere and released once; Limina is favoured by darkness, silence and thin ground and starved by crowds, saturation and active Domains, so a full room costs more than an empty one. The Residue is a legible gap that Anamnesis reads.
**Wellspring** · Letheveil, the Waters of Forgetting (Limina): compression keeps what carries information and drops the redundant; it tests Resilience Anchoring, so whatever is not load-bearing lets go first.
**Wellspring** · Hypnather, the Sleep Crown (Limina): a glass keeps relaxing toward order only while quiescent and cannot be forced by ordinary means; here it widens the window, making a waking mind briefly as porous as a resting one.
**Inherited failures** · Letheveil's generation loss leaves a worse copy still felt as memory, which is why the caster never notices what was taken; Hypnather's interrupted relaxation locks a mind worse ordered than before.
**Essence** · The Essence Core pays in its own substance, worked on directly; Developmental Tier Resonant at Refraction, and frequent users carry a residue-heavy Crystal. Repeated self-compression ends in Shear Break.
**School** · The Wax-Tablet school reads memory as wax taking impressions and forgetting as a mark that never set; right about what the Quake reaches, wrong to call hard wax a virtue of character, when the real counters are a pin and an agreed word.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Boundary (Root), for a threshold in time, laid across Mind (Extension).
**Boundary** · Where the consolidation threshold sits, inside a 25 m sphere, for one turn; not what is remembered, not whose.
**Effect** · The last 3 to 10 seconds, and whatever was held unsettled, go from everyone in the radius; spell-chains built on layered recall lose their place, combat rhythm and Domain concentration thin, formations lose their step together.
**Anchor** · Reactive, released against an incoming layered working or a rhythm in progress, so the internal chain is required; it cannot be cast below Refraction.
**Failure** · Boundary fault: the practitioner stands in the sphere, pays one permanent minor memory each release (Recall Slip), and is confused for the rest of that turn (Temporal Fog); allies inside are caught.
**Field fault** · Inside a Verdantia clarity field regenerative resonance sets traces faster than the boundary unsets them, and the working produces nothing.
**Bleed** · 30 to 40 percent leaves as heat at the Shell; against an effect worth under an attojoule, that heat is the working's whole energetic footprint.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · A layered working committed in range draws a 25 m burst, taking the last few seconds from everyone in it; afterwards a whole room cannot say what it was just doing.
**Limits** · Reactive only, never an opener, never aimed; 25 m; one turn; set memory and unconscious targets untouched; allies inside are caught.
**Fails against** · A physical anchor or agreed word fixed beforehand (Lock); a sharp pain; constructs and automata with no resting mind; a crowded, saturated room; a Verdantia clarity field; a cheap layered working, which draws it.

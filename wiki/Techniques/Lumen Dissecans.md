# Lumen Dissecans

## Lumen Dissecans

### Summary card

**Effect** · A narrow beam from a sigil over one eye reads open a working, ward or curse still in progress and freezes it into suspended geometry everyone present can see. The caster may then unravel it, alter it or file it for reference.
**Cost** · 2 percent of full reserve each turn the beam holds, 4 percent a full dissection, a fifth of output rate tied up meanwhile, and lattice afterimages on ordinary objects afterwards.
**Limit** · Only a working still in progress, one per activation, within 500 metres and line of sight; finished and instant effects show nothing.
**Counter** · A lattice built to scramble itself, a mirror that turns the beam back on the caster, fog or shielding, or a Void-aligned construct that answers with noise.
**What nobody knows** · Why some strong divine Edicts yield only part of their structure under the beam; one school blames the reader's purity, another says part of the source lies outside the band, and neither is proven.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · Spectroscopy and eddy-current inspection: every substance absorbs and emits at its own fixed wavelengths, and an induced current finds a hidden flaw without stripping the coating.
**Law** · Kirchhoff's laws of spectroscopy with the Beer–Lambert law, A = ε·c·l, turning absorbance into concentration; resolution follows the Rayleigh criterion, θ = 1.22 λ/D.
**Limit** · The read needs a live source to read from, so only a working still in progress shows; at full range the beam resolves members of a glyph-chain, not their grain.
**Joules** · Near-passive: the beam carries only enough power to be detected against the target's own emission; the expensive resources are aperture and integration time.
| Quantity | Working | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Range | line of sight | 500 m |
| Angular resolution | 1.22 × 550 nm ÷ 0.05 m sigil | 1.34 × 10⁻⁵ rad |
| Detail resolved at 500 m | 1.34 × 10⁻⁵ × 500 m | 6.7 mm |
| Aperture to resolve 1 mm at 500 m | 1.22 × 5.5 × 10⁻⁷ ÷ 2 × 10⁻⁶ | 0.34 m |
| Resolving power for a close doublet | λ ÷ Δλ | about 1,000 |

### Metaphysics

**Aether** · A thin directed beam, not a field; Fulguria favours dry air and clear sight, and fog or shielding starves it. The Residue sits on the target working: a survivor carries a phase trace of having been read, and its maker knows.
**Wellspring** · Judicium, the Wellspring of Truth (Fulguria): every element shows only its own spectrum, so Gnosis Acuity and Analysis read a Crystal, a technique or a lie by signature alone.
**Wellspring** · Aurevane, the Wellspring of Light (Fulguria): sharper resolution and in-phase output; it carries the reading as visible light, which is why bystanders can see the exposed structure.
**Inherited failures** · Judicium's spectral overload, everything reporting at once, returns as afterimages; Aurevane's glare makes the practitioner the easiest person on the field to locate.
**Essence** · The Essence Core does the reading and the Aether Shell carries it as a beam; the Shell keeps reporting after the beam drops. Developmental Tier Harmonic at Glory, Radiant from Transcendence.
**School** · The Signaturists read an inner virtue from an outward mark and blame a failed read on the reader's purity; a partial return means part of the source lies outside the band, and the answer is a wider aperture, not a holier reader.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Truth (Extension), a limit on what may stay unstated, laid across Illumination (Extension), which carries it as light: one station read twice, at two depths.
**Boundary** · The optical depth of a working in progress: the glyph pins its absorbance low enough that its internal lines show.
**Effect** · The target's working freezes into suspended, legible geometry in the air for everyone present; the practitioner may unravel it, alter it or file it as reference data in a carried codex.
**Anchor** · Below Refraction it needs an external anchor: a sigil drawn over one eye, and a declaration.
**Failure** · Boundary fault: the edge is imprecise, so ordinary objects keep reporting afterwards as lattice afterimages; a reflective barrier makes the practitioner dissect their own working. Field fault: a Void-aligned construct returns noise the instrument takes for signal.
**Bleed** · 30 to 40 percent leaves as heat at the Shell, by far the dominant term against a few watts of analytic light.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · A sigil over one eye, a declared cast, a narrow glaring beam from a still figure at a known bearing, and the target's working hanging open in the air; afterwards the practitioner glances at lattices that are not there.
**Limits** · Only a working in progress, one per activation, 500 m, line of sight, one to two turns; finished and instant effects show nothing.
**Fails against** · A lattice built to scramble itself (Knot); a mirror that turns the beam back; fog, shielding and Void-aligned constructs; workings resolved in closed bursts or phase-shifted; damage to the codex, a book that can be damaged.
**Look up** · The Counterplay register for Knot; the Fulguria entries for Judicium and Aurevane Failures; Abyntheus's decoherence; the Glory Stage table and the Spirit gates for Gnosis.

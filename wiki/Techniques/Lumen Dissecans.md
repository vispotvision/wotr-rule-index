# Lumen Dissecans

## Lumen Dissecans

---

### Lumen Dissecans

*(The Dissecting Light)*

#### Summary card

**Effect** · An ongoing working, ward, or curse gets read open. Serenyra sees its structure exposed as suspended, legible form, and can unravel it, alter it, or simply learn from it.
**Cost** · Every full dissection draws a measurable share of her baseline 22 AU/s output, and the act of reading that deeply leaves afterimages: lattice-shapes overlaid on ordinary objects for a time afterward.
**Limit** · Only a working still in progress can be read. Anything that has already resolved as a one-off effect is gone before she can reach it.
**Counter** · A glyph purpose-built to scramble or falsify its own lattice defeats the reading outright, and a reflective barrier can bounce the beam back at her.
**What nobody knows** · Why some strong divine Edicts yield only partial structure under the beam instead of resisting it completely or not at all.

#### Design Chain

**Trigger** · A declared cast against one active, ongoing effect within range.
**Function** · Projects a narrow beam that exposes the target working's structural lattice, holding it open and legible for as long as the beam is sustained.
**Mechanism** · Judicium's discernment law reads the working the way a Thaumic Harmonist reads a spell's tonal composition: compatible components register as consonance, structural flaws register as dissonance she can locate precisely. Aurevane carries that reading as visible, analytic light rather than an internal sense alone, which is why the exposed structure is something a bystander can also see.
**Numerical Effect** · Effective to 500 metres, one target working per activation; the dissection window lasts one to two exchanges, during which the target working is vulnerable to being unravelled, altered, or archived. Draws a measurable share of her established 22 AU/s baseline per full dissection.
**Target Response** · The exposed working freezes into legible, suspended geometry for the duration of the window rather than continuing to operate normally.
**Consequence** · Serenyra gains a choice she did not have before the working was exposed: unravel it, alter it, or file it into Thesauriel as new reference data.
**Limitation** · Only workings still in progress qualify. Instant, already-resolved effects have nothing left to expose.
**Weakness** · Attempting the technique against a Void-aligned construct risks contaminating her own reading. Time-displaced or phase-shifted workings can slip out of the beam's window before the dissection completes.
**Cost** · A measurable share of her 22 AU/s baseline per full dissection, and afterimages: for a time afterward she sees lattice-structure overlaid on ordinary, unworked objects.
**Counterplay** · A glyph built specifically to scramble or falsify its own lattice defeats the reading. A reflective barrier can redirect the beam, forcing her to break the cast or risk dissecting herself.

#### Origin

The source gives no date or founding problem for Lumen Dissecans beyond Serenyra's own authorship. It reads as a working built for the same analytic practice as her other entries, aimed specifically at active hostile or defensive magic rather than at people. No location is given beyond the Iridescent Archive filing destination, unattested in current canon; Thesauriel, the archive the technique's outcomes feed into, is likewise unattested and kept because the card names it as where the readings go.

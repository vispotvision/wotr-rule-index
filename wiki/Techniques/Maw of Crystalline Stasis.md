# Maw of Crystalline Stasis

## Maw of Crystalline Stasis

---

### Summary card

**Effect** · A six-metre field turns Draven's own cold into everyone's problem. Moisture, breath, and loose Essence crystallise into a lattice that slows what it touches and shatters what pushes through it too fast.
**Cost** · **His joints take the field's overflow.** *Frostbite sets in at his own knuckles and knees at high output, the price of holding a field wider than his Dominion was built to hold.*
**Limit** · **Risks freezing Draven's own joints if sustained.** *The field does not distinguish inside the six metres, and he is standing inside it.*
**Counter** · **Thermal flux collapses it from within.** *A Pyreveil arte or a sustained Aetheric overheat field forces the lattice back through its own phase transition before it can finish crystallising.*
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the field fails at its edge or at its centre first when overheated. Nobody has stayed inside one long enough to report which, and Draven has never asked anyone to try.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · Dendritic solidification under deep undercooling: vapour grows straight into branching ice, and whether the lattice is plates or needles is set only by temperature and supersaturation.
**Law** · Ice is strain-rate sensitive: it creeps below about 10⁻³ s⁻¹ and fails brittly above it, so the field slows what moves slowly and shatters what moves fast.
**Limit** · The lattice is capped by the water in the air, about 4.4 kg in saturated air and less in dry, so the Maw can be widened in dry air but never thickened.
**Front** · Under the Mullins–Sekerka instability a flat freezing front breaks into branches once the gradient passes a critical value; tip size and growth speed follow the undercooling.
**Joules** · Drawn from the air and the bodies in it: a heat-pump load, not a discharge, and the minimum work Q·ΔT / T_c climbs steeply with the undercooling a branching habit needs.
| Quantity | Working | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Field; depositable water | (4/3)π(6 m)³; 905 m³ × 4.84 g/m³ | 905 m³; about 4.4 kg |
| Heat removed | latent 1.24 × 10⁷ J + air cooled 30 K 3.34 × 10⁷ J | about 46 MJ, 2.6 MW over 18 s |
| Minimum pump work | Q·ΔT / T*c, ΔT 30 K, T*c 243 K | at least 5.7 × 10⁶ J |
| Ice strength | compressive; tensile; toughness K_IC | 5 to 25 MPa; 1 to 2 MPa; about 0.1 MPa·m^0.5 |
| A limb at 5 m/s on 0.1 m contact | 5 ÷ 0.1 | 50 s⁻¹, five orders into brittle failure |

### Metaphysics

**Aether** · Spread across 905 m³ from the practitioner's frame; Caloria wants steep thermal gradients, and the field erodes its own by chilling the air toward equilibrium. The Residue is heavy and physical, a lattice that can be picked up and that deafens Harmonists until it melts.
**Wellspring** · Cinerion, the Ash Veil (Caloria): what survives a fire is not waste, and the Shell metabolises its own products; here it densifies the lattice, raising Tempering Yield and Harmonics Stability.
**Wellspring** · Vohrin (Caloria), one power at two levels, Titan and Wellspring, drawn here as Titan-derived: heat's absence held as discipline, the heat sink run outward; the Titan level likely raises the working's standing and cost.
**Inherited failures** · Cinerion's fouling coats the conduit with its own product, so recovery collapses exactly when the practitioner has spent most, at the joints first.
**Essence** · The Aether Shell, projected outward: Spellcraft on the Bodily vector, the ignited frame as the pen, holding only while attention holds. Developmental Tier Harmonic at Glory.
**School** · The Four Qualities school calls cold an active power that condenses and congeals, which this working performs to the letter, then teaches that a practitioner's own cold cannot harm them; it can, because the cold is in the room and so are they.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Boundary (Root) for the six-metre edge laid across Form (Extension) for the lattice habit; Edge (Root) is the alternative, reading it as a limit on the phase diagram rather than the room.
**Boundary** · The temperature and supersaturation of the vapour in 905 m³; pin those two and the branching habit selects itself.
**Effect** · Six metres of branching frost that is not weather: slow movement is slowed, fast movement shatters, and Essence drawn through the lattice meets resistance. It punishes committing and rewards creeping.
**Weak point** · Heat collapses it from inside, tips first: an overheated Maw fails at its edge while the coarse centre holds longest.
**Anchor** · Below Refraction the Bodily vector supplies the anchor, the frame itself; the sentence ends when attention lapses.
**Failure** · Boundary fault: the field does not know its maker, and the cold lands on the practitioner's knuckles and knees. Structural fault: a Dominion below A holding six metres pays the difference in frostbite, through raw Ardency and Vitality.
**Bleed** · 45 percent leaves as heat at the Shell, inside the volume being held cold, adding load to the sink; that is why the cost runs by the turn.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · A declaration, then six metres of branching frost growing from where the practitioner stands; they cannot leave without ending it. Afterwards, a ring of shattered crystals to pick up by the handful.
**Limits** · Six metres, three turns, no targeting, its maker included; it needs moisture and loose Essence, and dry air thins it.
**Fails against** · Distance beyond six metres, and shot from there; slow movement inside; a Pyreveil arte or overheat field, which reverses the freeze faster than the sink rebuilds it; thermal equilibrium, which starves Caloria; a pinned practitioner, whose own frost takes their joints; broken attention.

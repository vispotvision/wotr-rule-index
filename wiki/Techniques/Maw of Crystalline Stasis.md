# Maw of Crystalline Stasis

## Maw of Crystalline Stasis

---

### Summary card

**Effect** · A six-metre field turns Draven's own cold into everyone's problem. Moisture, breath, and loose Essence crystallise into a lattice that slows what it touches and shatters what pushes through it too fast.
**Cost** · **His joints take the field's overflow.** *Frostbite sets in at his own knuckles and knees at high output, the price of holding a field wider than his Dominion was built to hold.*
**Limit** · **Risks freezing Draven's own joints if sustained.** *The field does not distinguish inside the six metres, and he is standing inside it.*
**Counter** · **Thermal flux collapses it from within.** *A Pyreveil arte or a sustained Aetheric overheat field forces the lattice back through its own phase transition before it can finish crystallising.*
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the field fails at its edge or at its centre first when overheated. Nobody has stayed inside one long enough to report which, and Draven has never asked anyone to try.

### Design Chain

**Trigger** · Declared. Draven commits the Bloodbind's cold output outward into a field rather than into a single point of contact.
**Function** · Field / Disable / Cold-Law Enforcement. Encases the working area in fractal frost, slows Essence flow inside it, and causes brittle-body shattering on impact for anything moving fast enough within it.
**Mechanism** · The same Cymorath heat-sink that powers Vorynn Execution Strike is run continuously and outward instead of once and inward. Ambient moisture, breath vapour, and loose Essence in the field lose thermal energy fast enough to crystallise into a branching lattice rather than settling as ordinary frost, the same rapid, directional heat extraction, applied to a volume instead of a point. Cinerion densifies the result same-Family, the way it already does for the Execution Strike, and the same ice-lattice-blocks-Essence-flow effect documented for Cymorath elsewhere in current canon runs across the whole field rather than at a single wound.
**Numerical Effect** · Range kept as given: 6 metres. Duration kept as given and converted from the old "3 turns" to an estimated 9 to 15 seconds of sustained field presence, flagged as an estimate rather than a sourced figure. Cost is estimated at roughly 1,500 to 2,500 EU per second, scaled down from Sodoku Moto's attested 8,000 EU/s Permafrost field (30 m, Stage XII) by radius and Stage difference, also flagged as an estimate, not a sourced number.
**Target Response** · Anything inside the field slows as Essence flow through it crystallises; anything moving fast enough to strain the brittle lattice shatters rather than merely being stopped by it.
**Consequence** · The field does not discriminate between hostile and friendly bodies inside its six metres, the same unrestricted-radius problem the Zar'thuun and Vohrin Titan-Keyed workings already document for area-effect cold and null-space arts.
**Limitation** · It slows and shatters. It does not target: everything in the six metres is affected the same way, Draven included.
**Weakness** · Thermal flux and radiant heat blasts, which force the lattice through an uncontrolled reverse phase change before it stabilises.
**Cost** · Joint frostbite at high output. **The mechanism traces to Draven's own attested stats:** his Dominion sits at 168, C-Grade, his second-weakest reading after Harmonics, and holding a six-metre field is a Dominion-class operation his sheet does not naturally support. He compensates by brute-forcing the radius through raw Ardency and Vitality output instead, and the strain finds his weakest structural margin first: the joints, at the periphery of what his own ignition can evenly cover.
**Counterplay** · Pyreveil artes and sustained Aetheric overheat fields collapse the lattice from within by forcing a reverse phase transition faster than Cymorath's sink can re-establish it.

# Mireglass Suffusion

## Mireglass Suffusion

### Summary card

**Effect** · A shadow-fluid floods out to twelve to fifteen metres, and every enemy standing in it sees a memory of their own worst failure step out of the mireglass and come at them. *The blow that lands is real. The shape throwing it is a memory wearing a body it never had.*
**Cost** · **The caster wades in the same flood.** *Their own buried memories surface in fragments for as long as the field holds, since there is no documented way to flood only the enemy's share of the ground.*
**Limit** · **An empty vault has nothing to show.** A target with no memory to draw on, or one sealed hard enough that Anamnesis cannot read it, walks through the mireglass and sees only glass.
**Counter** · **Judicium names the reflection for what it is.** *A truth-reading held against the mireglass shows a memory and a body that were never the same object, and once a target believes that, the silhouette stops landing.*
> **What nobody knows** · Whether Oblivara retrieves the memory whole or reconstructs it from whatever the target's own fear supplies, since two witnesses to the same old failure have reported being shown two different versions of it.

### Design Chain

**Trigger** · Declared. The caster floods the ground out to the field's radius, and the mireglass settles across every surface inside it.
**Function** · Reads a stored memory out of each enemy inside the field and projects it back at them as an attacking silhouette shaped by their own fear of it.
**Mechanism** · Anamnesis's law is remanent magnetisation and thermoluminescent dosimetry: a soul cooling through a transition records the field it cooled in and holds it, recoverable long after. The mireglass reads that stored record directly out of whoever stands in it. Oblivara's law is retroreflection: a corner-reflector geometry returns incident information precisely along the path it arrived on. Combined, the field retrieves a target's own recorded memory and reflects it back at the same angle the memory itself represents, so the silhouette always attacks from the direction the original failure came from.
**Numerical Effect** · Stage VI carries a Stat Ceiling of 400 on the FOW scale; the field's own floor sits at B-Grade, Stat Range 176 to 275. Duration once flooded: four exchanges, roughly twenty-four seconds, across a twelve-to-fifteen-metre radius.
**Target Response** · Reaction time and spellcasting clarity both degrade inside the field; a target who fails to recognise the reflection for what it is treats the silhouette as a second, independent threat and splits their attention accordingly.
**Consequence** · The caster's own buried memories surface uncontrolled for as long as the field stands, since there is no documented way to run it one-sided.
**Limitation** · A target with no memory on record, or one sealed against Anamnesis reading, generates no silhouette at all.
**Weakness** · Absolution's stress-relief mechanism and Luminalis's illumination both dissolve the mireglass outright; a strong-willed target breaks the reflection's hold faster than the field can re-establish it.
**Cost** · The caster's own fragments surface uncontrolled, the same exposure inflicted on the enemy, paid whether or not the field is doing useful work at that moment.
**Counterplay** · A Judicium-based truth reading identifies the silhouette as a reflection rather than a second combatant, after which it stops reading as a credible threat; an empath anchoring an ally interrupts the field's hold on that ally specifically.

### Origin

Mireglass Suffusion is filed in the Iridescent Archive, a catalogued repository rather than a living practitioner's private stock: a documented technique of unrecorded authorship, known and archived, not self-derived by any single hand still living to be asked. It is counterable by anyone who has studied the Archive's own record of it.

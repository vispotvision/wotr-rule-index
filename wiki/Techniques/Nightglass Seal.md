# Nightglass Seal

## Nightglass Seal

### Summary card

**Effect** · A pane of black-glass mist two metres across snaps up between the caster and an incoming working, and whatever crosses it arrives with no purpose attached. *A blade still cuts. A named technique still lands as inert motion with the naming stripped out of it.*
**Cost** · **The seal borrows the caster's own short-term memory to run the erasure.** *He loses minutes he cannot get back, taken at random from the last hour, every time the pane closes on something.*
**Limit** · **No purpose, nothing to erase.** A rock thrown with no working behind it, or a fist swung in plain anger, crosses the pane exactly as thrown; there was never a reason attached for Oblivara to unwrite.
**Counter** · **Judicium reads through it.** *Tenebra reduces signal, it does not fabricate a false one, and a clarity-working built on emission and absorption spectroscopy reads the caster's position through the pane's own noise floor.*
> **What nobody knows** · Whether "purpose" is a real quantity the Continuum tracks on every working, or whether Oblivara only makes the caster believe it erased something that was never separable from the blow in the first place.

### Design Chain

**Trigger** · Reactive. An incoming working or strike crossing the two-metre threshold the caster has already marked completes the activation without a further beat spent.
**Function** · Strips the authorization a working carries, the intent or reason behind it, while leaving its physical component untouched.
**Mechanism** · Tenebra's law is signal-to-noise reduction: an absorptive, faceted geometry returning almost nothing of what strikes it, so the pane itself reads as background rather than as a target. Oblivara's law is error-correcting redundancy and retroreflection: it treats an incoming pattern as a signal to be corrected against a stored reference and returns incident information precisely along the path it arrived on. Run together, the pane reads a working's authorization pattern, corrects it toward a reference that recognises nothing, and reflects the corrected, purposeless remainder through.
**Numerical Effect** · Stage VIII carries a Stat Ceiling of 550 on the FOW scale; the pane's own floor sits at A-Grade, Stat Range 276 to 400. Duration once raised: two exchanges, roughly twelve seconds.
**Target Response** · A named technique crossing the pane arrives as the bare mechanical motion underneath it; a practitioner staring into the pane gets their own perceptual signature reflected back distorted, reading as a smear where a face should be.
**Consequence** · Every erasure is paid out of the caster's own memory reserve; the memory taken is never the memory of the fight, always whatever synaptic trace was weakest at the moment of the exchange.
**Limitation** · Purely physical force with no authorization behind it is not a signal the pane's correction routine recognises, so it crosses unaltered.
**Weakness** · Tenebra-aligned attackers already read as background to the pane's own detection and partially bypass its correction routine.
**Cost** · Severe and immediate: minutes of short-term memory, not chosen by the caster, gone every time the pane closes on an incoming working.
**Counterplay** · Attack with a Judicium-based clarity working, strike with pure unauthorised force, or bring an Absolution working, whose stress-relief mechanism dissolves the shadow geometry Tenebra needs to stay dark.

### Origin

No deviser, era, or founding problem is given in the source, and none is invented per Law 1. As with the same source's other Iridescent Archive entries, the technique is filed under a catalogued repository rather than a living practitioner's private stock, so the most conservative reading consistent with that filing is that this is a documented entry of unrecorded authorship: known and archived, not self-derived by any single hand still living to be asked. Per the named-inventor rule, a catalogued technique is counterable by anyone who has studied the Archive's own record of it; there is no undocumented originator to name.

# Nightglass Seal

## Nightglass Seal

### Summary card

**Effect** · A pane of black-glass mist two metres across snaps up between the caster and an incoming working, and whatever crosses it arrives with no purpose attached. *A blade still cuts. A named technique still lands as inert motion with the naming stripped out of it.*
**Cost** · **The seal borrows the caster's own short-term memory to run the erasure.** *He loses minutes he cannot get back, taken at random from the last hour, every time the pane closes on something.*
**Limit** · **No purpose, nothing to erase.** A rock thrown with no working behind it, or a fist swung in plain anger, crosses the pane exactly as thrown; there was never a reason attached for Oblivara to unwrite.
**Counter** · **Judicium reads through it.** *Tenebra reduces signal, it does not fabricate a false one, and a clarity-working built on emission and absorption spectroscopy reads the caster's position through the pane's own noise floor.*
> **What nobody knows** · Whether "purpose" is a real quantity the Continuum tracks on every working, or whether Oblivara only makes the caster believe it erased something that was never separable from the blow in the first place.

### Origin

Nightglass Seal is filed in the Iridescent Archive, a catalogued repository rather than a living practitioner's private stock: a documented technique of unrecorded authorship, known and archived, not self-derived by any single hand still living to be asked. It is counterable by anyone who has studied the Archive's own record of it.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · Signal-to-noise reduction across a thin boundary: an absorptive pane returns almost nothing, and whatever crosses it is decoded against a codebook of one word that recognises nothing, so every pattern decodes to nothing.
**Law** · A decoder maps each input to the nearest valid codeword; a one-word code has rate zero, the only case in which correction reliably destroys a message instead of repairing it.
**Limit** · It stops no mass or momentum: a blade still cuts, and force with no authorization behind it is no pattern the decoder recognises, so it passes unaltered.
**Absorption** · Salisbury and Jaumann layers null reflection by antiphase; absorbers reach 20 to 30 dB, but detection range goes as σ^(1/4), so 30 dB shortens it only 5.6 times.
**Darkness** · A 5 cm pane dark enough to matter needs about 800 times the extinction of dense fog, so the darkness is absorptive, not mist.
**Memory** · Interference with consolidation gives retrograde amnesia with a gradient: the least-set traces from the last one to six hours go first.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Knot (Counter) laid across Silence (Synonym), filed under Silentia: Knot makes the incoming path untraceable; Lock (Counter) is the alternative, pre-empting the authorization slot instead.
**Boundary** · The decoder's reference, pinned to one word that recognises nothing; separately, the pane's own return is pinned below the noise floor.
**Effect** · A two-metre pane of black-glass mist arrives faster than the strike; the blade goes through and lands, but a named technique arrives as bare motion: fire as mere heat, binding as rope, a verdict as noise.
**Mirror** · A watcher staring into it gets their own perceptual signature back, a smear where a face should be.
**Roles** · Its target is the incoming working's Authorization; its Sealing is the role that bills, charged the moment it closes.
**Anchor** · Above Refraction the chain is internal and silent: no wrist or mouth to take, and the silence itself announces the Stage.
**Failure** · Coherence fault: destroying warrants while losing minutes of memory erodes the record of one's own conduct, until Thresholds refuse, Traits argue and the Flicker follows. Boundary fault: code exhaustion.
**Bleed** · 20 to 30 percent leaves as heat at the Shell, working against a pane meant to sit below the noise floor; an Expert's pane would be too loud, hence the floor.

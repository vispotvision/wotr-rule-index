# Obelisk of the Eclipsed Dawn

## Obelisk of the Eclipsed Dawn

### Summary card

**Effect · The practitioner collapses** six orbiting Wellspring sigils into a point of infinite density and a colossal prism rises from it, dissolving what she marks as an ending and rebuilding what she marks as a beginning, over an area up to 800 m across.
**Cost** · The Obelisk records what she was feeling when she raised it and plays it back to her; sustaining it long enough leaves hairline stress in her own Temperance layers that only stillness closes.
**Limit** · No more than four Obelisks stand at once; a fifth does not fail to appear, it brings the others down with it.
**Counter** · Raw entropy or a comparable law-breaking working, saturating it faster than its Basilithe lattice can re-order, collapses it early.
**What nobody knows** · Whether the "unfinished dream" the Obelisk rewrites is read from the target's Essence or written onto it; its author has never been asked to explain the difference and nobody has caught her doing it slowly enough to check.

**Law** · The fluctuation theorem prices a reversal, P(+σ)/P(−σ) = e^(σ/k*B); Landauer's principle charges k*B T ln2 (2.87 × 10⁻²¹ J at 300 K) for every bit of record cleared.
**Limit** · The binding limit on a reversal is storage, not power: the record must be held somewhere, and clearing it pays the second law back.
**Lattice** · A high-stiffness, low-loss resonator holds its frequency against forcing, like cut quartz (Q 10⁴ to 10⁷, stability 10⁻⁶ to 10⁻⁸), so the prism holds its state for its whole span.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Return laid across Continuum for the held state and Form for the lattice habit: this region's trajectory is permitted to run back along itself.
**Boundary** · Only the direction in which the field's trajectory may run; the second law is not opposed but paid, the charge deferred until the record clears.
**Modes** · Fracture Cascade unwinds a hostile construct, curse or barrier back through its own assembly; Ecliptic Aegis redirects or nullifies an ally's incoming force at the shell; Oneiric Reversal coaxes damaged Essence toward its own earlier, healthier state.
**Effect** · Six orbiting sigils fall to a point and a prism the height of a tower grows from it; inside a district, one of the three modes runs, and no one on the ground knows which until it starts.
**Sorting** · Friend and foe are sorted by the practitioner's judgement and attention; if that slips, allies are caught.
**Hold** · The mode is locked at the trigger; changing it means collapse and a fresh raising. The trigger is internal: the practitioner stills their Essence and commits, with no outside chain to interrupt.
**Failure** · Structural fault: under prolonged manifestation Structural Strain leaves micro-fractures in the Temperance layers, closed only by Essence stillness. A failed Sealing leaves an operatorless 800 m construct obeying itself.
**Downstream** · Coherence fault: lived behaviour drifting from stated law warps the lattice toward inversion.
**Bleed** · 10 to 15 percent of every draw leaves the Shell as heat, sound and structural bleed; the record is not shed but held, and replayed to the maker.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · Six orbiting sigils stop and fall to a point and the practitioner goes still; that stillness is also the mode lock. Afterwards, a district of legible Residue that deafens Harmonists.
**Limits** · 800 m; four instances; mode locked at the trigger; no life created or restored, only Essence-state; anyone at Emanation or higher is immune, and a peer's standing decree contests or deflects a mode.
**Fails against** · The sea, altitude or a moving platform; a peer's decree Locked first; Silence, which severs the line totally; Dissolution, which erodes the lattice; a matching forcing, which breaks Basilithe; four standing emergencies, which tempt a fifth instance.
**Strain** · Genuine internal conflict warps the lattice toward inversion; anything that breaks focus threatens a practitioner already holding four instances and their own replayed feeling.
**Saturation** · Flooding the area with Essence gives everyone present Fracture risk, the one who did it included; with four standing the field is already at the threshold.

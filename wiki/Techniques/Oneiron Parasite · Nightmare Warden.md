# Oneiron Parasite · Nightmare Warden

## Oneiron Parasite · Nightmare Warden

### Summary card

**Effect** · A fragment of him takes root behind a target's ribs, watches what they feel, and can be made to unmake their composure from inside.
**Cost** · If it is destroyed, the pain returns to him. If he lets it sit too long, it starts reporting his host's own fears back to him as if they were his.
**Limit** · Needs a subconscious to root in, and the host's own state shapes what grows there, not only his intent.
**Counter** · A shield that refuses implantation outright, a rite that purges what is already rooted, a Wellspring built on ending rather than growing, or a cut line back to him.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether a Warden left in long enough ever fully comes back, or whether some part of it stays behind.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · Seeded crystal growth, a new phase forming on a foreign template, then coarsening, where the larger structure eats the smaller: a fragment of the practitioner's soul planted in a host's chest takes hold and grows.
**Law** · A template cuts the nucleation barrier ΔG‡ = 16πγ³/3Δg² by the factor f(θ), about 99 percent at 30°; a nucleus past r‡ = 2γ/Δg grows, one below it redissolves.
**Limit** · Coarsening does not ask whose nucleus is whose: the larger, better-ordered structure takes material from the other, so a strong host rejects the graft and a long-held one becomes the host's.
**Growth** · Rest-driven relaxation follows a stretched exponential, so one long undisturbed night feeds the graft more than six naps.
**Seat** · It sits behind the ribs at the base of the heart, where about 80 percent of vagal fibres carry the body's felt state upward; that is where feeling is read.
**Channel** · Core feeling is low-dimensional, about 10 bit/s, far below any carrier's capacity: watching is cheap, the graft is the expense.

**Aether** · A small draw with a long tail: the planting is one act at up to 30 m, after which the host's own rest feeds it, at the host's efficiency. A host sleeping somewhere bright, crowded and saturated feeds it poorly.
**Residue** · The location is a body: a host carrying a Warden carries a trace a competent physician can read off the living, and a renderer reads better off the dead.
**Wellspring** · Oneirion, the Wellspring of Dream (Limina), primary: every arrangement is a coordinate in configuration space, which is how the graft crosses into the host.
**Wellspring** · Eidolyn, the Shaping Mind (Limina): a supersaturated medium deposits along a template's geometry, and here the template is the practitioner's intent.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Form governs, laid across Passage for the crossing into the host and Mind for the addressing: this fragment may hold a shape here.
**Boundary** · The nucleation threshold inside another Crystal moves, so a foreign fragment counts as a valid template; deposition, growth and coarsening follow unaided.
**Effect** · Sleep stops restoring, worst after the deepest nights; composure spikes at the wrong moments; plans are known as they form; the host's layering weakens from inside. On command the template is withdrawn and the host staggers.
**Detonation** · The graft drops below critical radius at once and redissolves; the host's Crystal re-orders around it in one moment, energy spent in the Crystal, not in tissue or air.
**Hold** · Planted by gaze or touch at Refraction, with no outside chain. There is no Seal by design; the Animatria line stands in for it, so cutting the line ends it.
**Failure** · Boundary fault: template defect runs on inside the host's architecture after the practitioner stops.
**Downstream** · Coherence fault: surveillance made of the practitioner's own Core returns other people's dispositions to them, and the drift runs toward identity bleed.
**Bleed** · At η 0.65, 35 percent of the planting, each watch and the detonation leaves the practitioner's Shell as heat and sound; the sustain between watches is the host's.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · At planting, a look or touch at up to 30 m. Afterwards, diagnostic: sleep that fails to restore, composure failing on a schedule, a trace behind the ribs; when it dies, the practitioner flinches wherever they stand.
**Limits** · 30 m; it needs a subconscious to root in; a strong mental shield refuses it; the host shapes what it becomes; the live line can be cut.
**Fails against** · Silence, total: a host who feels suddenly lonely inside a Silence has just had it go dark. The practitioner's death or distraction; a memory-cleansing rite; Mortalis; a shared-dream ritual; an empathic Wellspring; distance beyond 30 m.
**Host** · A disciplined, self-honest host coarsens the Warden into themselves, and the reports become distortions of a person the practitioner cannot read.

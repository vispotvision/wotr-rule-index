# Oneiron Phantasm Court

## Oneiron Phantasm Court

### Summary card

**Effect** · The target's own dream-self rises behind them, unblurred by the waking mind's editing, and puts to them the thing they have been declining to know about themselves.
**Cost** · **Identity Bleed.** Some of what the projection carries back is the target's, and the practitioner keeps a fragment of it. **Dream Echo Fatigue** follows, showing as short-term memory gaps of his own.
**Limit** · Only takes hold on a mind with an active subconscious and an actual unresolved contradiction in it. A mind at peace with itself, or with nothing behind the waking self, gives the working nothing to find.
**Counter** · **A target with nothing there to confess: one whose** *conscious and undefended selves already agree returns no verdict for the Court to hand down.*
**What nobody knows** · Why the confession, once mirrored back at the practitioner, can destabilise him exactly as hard as it destabilises the target.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · An extrapolation made to stand up: the Court raises the state a person's Crystal would settle into given infinite rest, their undefended self, and puts it on its feet behind them to say what they will not.
**Law** · The Kauzmann problem: a cooling liquid's excess entropy extrapolates to zero at a finite temperature, but relaxation time diverges first (τ → ∞), so the target state can be calculated and may never be reached.
**Limit** · Someone must perform the extrapolation and supplies the part not measured; whether that state exists at all stays open, and the verdict destabilises the practitioner as hard as the target.
**Confession** · People tell remembered from perceived by rules that fail predictably, and self-made material feels more credible; your own withheld truth in your own voice is maximum dissonance, and the dissonance keeps working after the figure goes.
**Figure** · Not matter: a 70 kg body would need 58 m³ of air condensed. It is an avatar deposited into Aether, load-bearing like a push (about 500 N) but it never strikes.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Truth governs, laid across Mind for the addressing and Form for the deposition: this configuration may be stated.
**Boundary** · The state a system is aimed at may be instantiated without the system arriving there; the three ordinary laws then build a person from it.
**Effect** · The target's own self, with the editing off, stands up behind them and states what they have been declining to know. It carries weight in the room and never strikes; the damage is done by the target, to the target.
**Needs** · A live subconscious, a genuine unresolved contradiction, and a prior close reading of the target; with no gap between waking and settled self there is no coordinate.
**Hold** · Attention is both Seal and duration: when it lapses the figure goes and the loosening stays. At Refraction the trigger is an internal declaration within 140 m.
**Failure** · Boundary fault: template defect runs the law somewhere unintended, and that place is the practitioner.
**Downstream** · Coherence fault: an Attraction Layer built on other people's fears drifts from stated law toward dissociation.
**Bleed** · At η 0.65, 35 percent of a small draw leaves the Shell as heat and sound, which is why the Court is quiet.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · First, a close reading and often a soft pressure of sleep in which nearby minds recall old things; then the target's own self standing up behind them, speaking; afterwards a target who cannot stop turning one thing over.
**Limits** · 140 m; a live subconscious and a real gap; a prior reading; only while attention holds; a memory-null field denies it a template.
**Fails against** · A target with nothing hidden, a conduct choice anyone can make; a memory-null region or Lock; Verdantia or Mortalis practitioners, who carry little gap; a broken focus. A body standing in front of the target does nothing.
**Drift** · Each Court moves the practitioner toward dissociation, and there are cases they will not take.

# Principle Overwrite

## Principle Overwrite

### Summary card

**Effect** · Francis isolates one Hermetic Principle within a five-kilometre radius and rewrites its behavior for roughly fifteen seconds of sustained declaration (cause running as effect, rhythm running as stillness, opposed polarities merging), forcing everything caught in the radius to answer to the new law instead of the old one.
**Cost** · Two percent of his own memory bleeds out with every use, a standing toll rather than a training injury.
**Limit** · One Principle at a time. Chaining multiple inversions in the same working risks paradox rather than compounding the effect.
**Counter** · An Archonic verdict overrides the edit outright, and a Nihiloth-mediated null seal, a Talos-class cancellation field, can cancel the working before it takes.
**What nobody knows** · Whether the 2 percent is drawn from the same memory each time, or whether Francis is slowly forgetting something in particular.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · A false-vacuum bubble held open: for eighteen seconds, inside five kilometres, one rule becomes a different rule, and a higher-energy state is held inside the lower one against its own wall tension.
**Law** · A bubble of a second state has a critical radius R_c = 3σ/ε (wall tension over energy gap); a higher-energy bubble does not grow, both its wall and its deficit push inward, so it must be held.
**Limit** · Release is mandatory: the wall's 4πR²σ and the interior's (4/3)πR³ε go somewhere every time, crossing five kilometres in about 17 µs.
**Reading** · Every element shows only its own spectrum, so identifying which state a rule is in is cheap and nearly free, but it penetrates poorly, like eddy-current testing that finds the crack under paint, not under plate.
**Tempering** · Quench and temper: repeated shock plus controlled recovery (200 to 650 °C, 65 HRC down to the low forties) lets a lattice survive holding a wall that would split it untempered.
**Release** · Like ice melting at 0 °C (334 J/g at constant temperature), a clean collapse spends its energy on the old state re-forming; resisted by a comparable source, it goes out as a shockwave.

|---|---|---|
| Field | 5 km radius | 3.14 × 10⁸ m² of wall; 5.24 × 10¹¹ m³ |
| Air inside | 5.24 × 10¹¹ m³ × 1.225 kg/m³ | 6.41 × 10¹¹ kg |
| Critical-radius relation at 5 km | R_c = 3σ/ε | σ/ε = 1,667 m |
| Collapse time | 5 km at near light speed | about 17 µs |

**Co-author** · At η above 1.0 the Continuum treats the working as law and the room supplies the surplus, so the county helps carry the county; below 1.0 the practitioner pays all of it.
**Residue** · Enormous: a county of worked ground that deafens Harmonists for a long time; a practitioner's written record of their rewrites is a second residue telling a reader which laws are bent.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Order governs, which of two orders the region is under, laid across Command for authorisation and Vastness for the five kilometres.
**Boundary** · Which state one region is in, for one Principle, while the wall holds; the practitioner nucleates a second state beside the law and stands on the lid.
**Effect** · A hush that presses, a ten-degree drop, a drone through the floor, scorched circles spreading from the speaker; then effect before cause, a rhythm that stops, or opposites made one. Nothing is struck; everything built on the old reading fails.
**Binds** · Everyone inside, ally and enemy alike; old-reading wards and inscriptions misfire for as long as the carving holds.
**One only** · One Principle per activation; a second wall is the Lock problem, and the Crystal refuses a chain.
**Hold** · The chain is internal at Zenith, yet the trigger is spoken aloud, naming the Principle and its replacement for everyone in earshot.
**Failure** · Structural fault: forcing a chain past the refusal is Principle Collapse, paradox recoil fracturing selfhood, with an eighteen percent chance of self-erasure at high inversion depth. A failed Seal leaves a county under a rewritten law with nobody on the lid.
**Downstream** · Boundary fault: an imprecise five-kilometre condition runs the law somewhere unintended, and at that radius that is a county.
**Bleed** · At η 0.95, 5 percent of the draw leaves the Shell as heat, sound and structural bleed across the county; at 1.0 and above nothing bleeds.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · The temperature falls about ten degrees, the air presses quiet, a drone rises through the floor, then the practitioner speaks aloud. Afterwards, a county of misfiring inscriptions and deafening Residue.
**Limits** · 5 km; 18 s; one Principle; binds allies too; an Archonic verdict overrides it outright.
**Fails against** · A Nihiloth null or a Talos-class cancellation field; Knot and Lock; a Seal forced early, which bills the practitioner; dream logic from Oneirion or Kaetra; shielded interiors in saturated cold; the end of eighteen seconds.
**Oaths** · Two contradictory Oaths of equal weight freeze a Celestial practitioner; a known vow binds them in public; a written record of rewrites is a pattern a librarian can read.
**Look up** · The Counterplay register for Knot, Lock and forced Seals; Nihiloth and Talos-class fields; the Fulguria and Caloria terrain entries and Failures; the Zenith Stage table; Archonic verdicts.

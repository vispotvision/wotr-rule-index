# Seraphic Thread Blessing

## Seraphic Thread Blessing

### Summary card

**Effect** · A shimmering thread drawn from the Plane of Fate binds shut an ally's wound and resolve, within 15 metres, restoring Essence flow to something closer to its clean state and steadying the ally's next two acts of will.
**Cost** · The caster feels a trace of the wound they just closed; a mild, lingering emotional drain that fades once the ally's Crystal has stabilised.
**Limit** · Cannot close a fatal wound or a severe fracture in the Soul Crystal itself; the thread has nothing to bind to once the damage runs that deep.
**Counter** · A Limina-worked curse severs the thread outright, and Oblivara's erasure glyphs do the same by unmaking the thread's anchor rather than cutting it.
**What nobody knows.** Why the Plane of Fate answers a plea made on someone else's behalf faster than it answers the same plea made for the caster's own wound.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · Wound healing under extreme time compression: a filament crosses from caster to patient, and three days of tissue repair happen in six seconds on the patient's own chemistry.
**Law** · Energy balance with a heat ceiling on the waste: repair is cheap (about 180 J per gram of new tissue), but waste heat lands in the wound bed, which has lost the blood flow that would carry it off.
**Limit** · A 20 g wound bed takes about 430 J before protein damage starts at 43 °C (60 °C coagulates collagen), so each cast buys about 4 g of tissue: a closed cut, a sealed vessel, never a fatal wound or a severe Crystal fracture.
**Bond** · Benediction's analogue is binding strength, K*d = k*off/k_on; a bond is only statistically present, and a vow's durability is its off-rate, from minutes to days across bonds that look alike.
**Outbid** · A competitor raises the apparent K*d to K*d(1 + [I]/K_i); nothing breaks and nothing signals, the thread is simply elsewhere when called.
**Look** · Luminalis is diagnostic imaging: near-infrared at 650 to 950 nm sees 1 to 3 cm into tissue, and polarised light tells new collagen from old, so the wound's true shape is found before closing.

### Metaphysics

**Aether** · A small draw across 15 m; Vitalia is favoured by living biomass, temperate air and blood, Fulguria by dry air and clear sight. A battlefield is better ground than a cold, clean infirmary.
**Residue** · A fading filament in the air, and in the patient an oath-shaped bond whose affinity the Continuum can read: a healed man carries a record of who healed him, and the caster the reciprocal trace.
**Wellspring** · Benediction (Vitalia): a biological bond's strength is a measurable quantity, and Benediction gives bonds on the Plane of Fate the same weight, so an oath sworn under it has an affinity figure.
**Wellspring** · Luminalis (Fulguria): most of the spectrum is invisible to the eye; it extends perception past the visible band and polarises the Shell.
**Inherited failures** · Benediction's competitive inhibition, a silent displacement; Luminalis's birefringence, splitting a layered working out of phase with itself.
**Self** · It never reaches the caster: it runs on a Spirit Axis, a bond with another soul, and there is no Axis from a soul to itself.
**Essence** · The Aether Shell is worked and the ally's channels run cleaner afterward; the Core is touched only for minor Crystal surface damage. Developmental Tier Harmonic.
**School** · The Sympathetic Chirurgeons treat the weapon to heal the wound at a distance; the bond is real but runs between souls, and a treated blade cannot be outbid, so they never see the failure that ends a thread.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Binding: the establishment of a conductive relation with a stated affinity; the renewal itself is the patient's.
**Boundary** · The rate ceiling: repair's chemical timers are authorised to run out of sequence, and the patient's cells, ATP and amino acids do the work.
**Effect** · A spoken word, a golden-white filament from palm to chest, one harp-string chime as the bond seats. Warmth spreads from the deepest point outward, pain goes before the wound does, and six seconds later four grams of tissue lie true.
**Hold** · Below Refraction it has two anchors, the voice that declares it and the hand the filament leaves from.
**Failure** · Coherence fault: competitive inhibition seats a rival silently, and the caster believes the blessing holds while it holds a vacancy.
**Downstream** · Boundary fault: layered with a second working, birefringence lays collagen twice along two slightly different geometries.
**Bleed** · The thread's waste lands in the wound bed and scalds if pushed; the reserve's bleed leaves the caster's Shell as warmth and light, tens of megawatts for six seconds.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · Spoken, lit and chiming; the filament names both ends of the bond to every witness. Afterwards warm tissue, perhaps a scald, and a caster wearing the patient's pain.
**Limits** · 15 m with clear sight; about 4 g a cast; no fatal wounds or severe Crystal fractures; never the caster; no higher than B.
**Fails against** · A covered mouth or a broken wrist; a stronger ligand outbidding the bond; the vow's own terms; fog, smoke or shielding, which blinds the look; a cold, sterile, shielded room; a Limina curse or Oblivara's erasure glyphs, which sever it. A body standing between them does nothing.
**Drift** · A healer whose bonds outrun their conduct drifts in Coherence, measured by Resilience Oath. A broken vow is a Shear Break.

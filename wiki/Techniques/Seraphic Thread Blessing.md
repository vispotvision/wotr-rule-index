# Seraphic Thread Blessing

## Seraphic Thread Blessing

### Summary card

**Effect** · A shimmering thread drawn from the Plane of Fate binds shut an ally's wound and resolve, within 15 metres, restoring Essence flow to something closer to its clean state and steadying the ally's next two acts of will.
**Cost** · The caster feels a trace of the wound they just closed; a mild, lingering emotional drain that fades once the ally's Crystal has stabilised.
**Limit** · Cannot close a fatal wound or a severe fracture in the Soul Crystal itself; the thread has nothing to bind to once the damage runs that deep.
**Counter** · A Limina-worked curse severs the thread outright, and Oblivara's erasure glyphs do the same by unmaking the thread's anchor rather than cutting it.
**What nobody knows.** Why the Plane of Fate answers a plea made on someone else's behalf faster than it answers the same plea made for the caster's own wound.

- **Grade required** · B-Grade
- **Path gate** · Spirit, Fate

### Design Chain

**Trigger** · The caster declares the working over an ally within 15 metres who carries a wound or a fractured resolve.
**Function** · Healing and buff: closes physical injury, steadies emotional shock, and cleans the ally's Aether channels for their next working.
**Mechanism** · Benediction's grace strengthens Harmonics Empathy, Harmonics Axis and Resilience Oath. The caster reaches along an existing or newly formed bond. They draw the ally's Essence flow back toward the pattern Benediction recognises as whole. Luminalis lends the working its clarity. The thread finds the true shape of the wound before it closes, rather than closing blind.
**Numerical Effect** · Operates within the B-Grade output ceiling that Stage V, Splintering permits, proportionate to what a stat total near 350 can move rather than to a destructive-yield table, since nothing here is discharged outward. Lasts roughly six seconds, matching the source's stated two old-count turns.
**Target Response** · The ally feels warm clarity settle behind the eyes. A golden-white filament threads from the caster's palm into their Essence. A soft chime marks the thread taking hold, like one harp string.
**Consequence** · Minor Soul Crystal surface damage repairs, physical and emotional recovery accelerate, and the ally's Aether channels run cleaner for their next casting.
**Limitation** · Fatal wounds and severe fractures are past what the thread can reach; the working was never built to reverse a Crystal that has already broken.
**Weakness** · An ally aligned with Limina resists the Fate-thread binding outright, the two currents pulling against each other rather than joining.
**Cost** · A mild emotional drain in the caster, who carries a trace of what they just healed until the ally's Crystal settles.
**Counterplay** · A Limina curse breaks the thread on contact. Oblivara's erasure glyphs unmake the anchor the thread depends on. Absolution worked alongside the blessing strengthens it instead, a documented synergy rather than a threat.

### FOW line

Governing Primary: Harmonics, off Benediction's own strengthened line (Harmonics Empathy, Harmonics Axis), with Resilience Oath carried alongside for the bond's structural weight and Luminalis's Gnosis Perception lending the working its diagnostic accuracy. Stage floor Stage V, Splintering. Grade required B-Grade. Path gate Spirit and Fate. Resonant Pair: none documented.

# Sigillum Fixatio

## Sigillum Fixatio

### Summary card

**Effect** · The caster names a single target within ten metres of line of sight, and the target's present state locks. A struck body stops moving, stops healing, and stops taking new damage easily. An active working's parameters freeze and cannot be altered or dispelled for the duration.
**Cost** · The caster's own Shell locks in sympathy: no blink-type or phase movement for the duration, and their own attention narrows around the bound target until it is difficult to redirect.
**Limit** · Only one Sigillum may be active at a time; it cannot be placed on a being two or more Stages above the caster, and it cannot take hold of anything without a discrete physical or Aetheric form, a Domain, a Wellspring, or an abstraction included.
**Counter** · Concentrated shearing or torsional force at the anchor point parts it before Sealing reasserts, and breaking the caster's concentration (stagger, daze, a Silence field) ends it outright.
**What nobody knows** · Why an error placed under Fixatio, rather than dispelled, becomes a permanent feature of whatever it was placed on rather than decaying like other failed workings.

### Design Chain

**Trigger** · The caster speaks the command "Esto" while closing an open hand to a fist, aimed at a target within ten metres of line of sight.
**Function** · The gesture and word authorise, bound, and direct a Fixatio working onto a single named target, physical or metaphysical.
**Mechanism** · Fixatio's real analogue is covalent cross-linking, a thermoset cure. A thermoplastic can be reheated and reshaped indefinitely; a thermoset, once cured, has formed covalent bridges across its whole network and chars before it ever flows again. Fixatio performs that cure on the target's present configuration. What it holds is not held by force. It is held because the alternative configuration has stopped being a reachable state.
**Numerical Effect** · At the required C-Grade output, the lock resists roughly 0.1 to 0.3 gigantonewtons of applied peak force, on the order of 7 megajoules to 1.046 gigajoules, before the bond parts, comparable to a small-building-level structural failure. Range is fixed at ten metres, line of sight, single target only.
**Target Response** · A physical target is immobilised, its wounds stop worsening and stop healing, and it gains marked resistance to further physical alteration, not invincibility. A metaphysical target, an active spell or ward, has its parameters frozen: it cannot be dispelled, moved, or altered until the lock ends.
**Consequence** · The lock holds for roughly nine seconds or until the caster's concentration breaks, whichever comes first, and the caster cannot maintain a second Sigillum in that window.
**Limitation** · The working cannot reach two or more Stages above the caster's own, and it has no purchase on anything without a discrete tangible or Aetheric form: no Domain, no Wellspring current, no abstraction.
**Weakness** · The lock is brittle rather than resilient. It is built to hold a single configuration against drift, not to absorb concentrated shearing or torsional force, and a caster who is staggered, dazed, or silenced loses the working immediately.
**Cost** · Imposing stasis costs the caster a share of their own mobility and attention for the same span: no blink or phase movement, and a narrowing of Gnosis around the bound target that makes noticing anything else harder.
**Counterplay** · A Fractura-trained strike aimed at the anchor point, or any working drawing on Dissolution, unmakes the lock from outside; disrupting the caster's own concentration ends it from the source; and, in the rare case a caster invites it, Titan Valmir's entropic domain unmakes the anchoring absolutely, since a thing that has already fallen has nothing left for Fixatio to hold fixed.

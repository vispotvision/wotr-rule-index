# Solarbound Aegoric Knight

## Solarbound Aegoric Knight

### Summary card

**Effect** · The knight takes blows and gives no ground while a store builds in the Shell, then spends it all in one oath-gated act: a cut, a vow spoken aloud, a stance that does not break. At the top, with a Domain seated, the release leaves as law: things that are sworn do not break.
**Cost** · 14 to 37 percent of full reserve per release, paid at once from the knight's own reserve. Blows absorbed are only the trigger; they bank beside the store and can overfill it.
**Limit** · No fast, repeated or incidental output and no partial release; output fades without a cause truly sworn.
**Counter** · Only incidental output, which it cannot release; a stronger vow displacing his; a lost hand or knee before the gesture; cold, shielding and movement; Disruption, which frays him.
**What nobody knows** · Whether Ser Varnath Rhogar founded the discipline, embodied it, or was simply the knight later students pointed to.

### Codex line

**Wellspring** · Fixatio, the Binding Flame (primary); Benediction (secondary); Judicium, the Wellspring of Truth (tertiary).
**Family** · Materia, with Vitalia and Fulguria.
**Physics Domain** · Metastable storage behind a barrier, released by ignition.
**Category** · Domain Weaving at its apex, over a standing Traitus layer: the Shell's oath-reactive bias is passive and permanent, not cast.
**Craft** · Spellcraft, delivered bodily through gesture-keys (kneeling, raising a shield, touching the oath-brand), re-committed each time.
**Stage floor** · Refraction
**Grade required** · A-Grade at Refraction; S-Grade from Transcendence to Realization.
**Path gate** · None for an A release; the Attraction Path for a layered release above A and for the seated-Domain release.

### FOW line

**Governing Primary** · Not fixed by the discipline; the store's ceiling is the knight's Tempering Capacity, under which all three harmonisations sit.
**Stage floor** · Refraction for an A release; Transcendence to Realization for S releases and a seated Domain.
**Grade required** · A at Refraction; S from Transcendence.
**Path gate** · None for an A release; the Attraction Path for layered S releases and the seated-Domain release.
**Resonant Pair** · None

### Origin

**Origin** · The discipline names Ser Varnath Rhogar, the Scorn of the Outer World, as the source of its Aether; he held a Gate open with his own body long enough for a million souls to flee through it. No founder is named.
**Practitioners** · Ser Varnath Rhogar, a hero of the Guild Accord's Concord Military; the Impetists, who store and never release, and die full.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · Metastable storage behind a kinetic barrier, released by ignition: a knight carries a store that cannot leak, the way petrol sits in an open can for years, and spends it all on one act.
**Law** · Arrhenius run backwards: residence time is (1/A)·exp(E_a/RT), so a high barrier holds the store indefinitely, and an ignition adds no energy, only the barrier's worth; at release the first law says whatever comes out was in the Crystal.
**Limit** · A metastable store has no setting between held and spent, and nothing that burns un-burns; the release is total and cannot be reversed.
**Fuel** · Absorbed blows are a trigger and a measure, not the fuel: a hard day in the line catches about 10⁵ J, six orders short of a release, so the knight's own committed reserve pays.
**Verdict** · Every element shows only its own spectrum, and eddy-current reading finds the crack under the paint, so the release lands as a verdict that cannot be counterfeited.

### Metaphysics

**Aether** · Three Families: Materia likes stone and deep foundation, Vitalia likes living ground and blood, Fulguria likes dry air and clear sight; one place kills all three, a cold, shielded, moving deck. The standing bias is residue made structural and deafens Harmonists where a release was spent.
**Wellspring** · Fixatio, the Binding Flame (Materia): a cured network chars before it flows, so the store is held because relaxing is no longer reachable; its irreversibility is the thing relied on.
**Wellspring** · Benediction (Vitalia): a bond's strength is a measurable affinity, so an oath has a figure the Continuum prices and banners, seals and spoken oaths amplify the output.
**Wellspring** · Judicium (Fulguria): every element shows only its own spectrum, so the knight reads flaws and lies continuously.
**Inherited failures** · Fixatio's irreversibility seizes an overfull Shell; Benediction's competitive inhibition lets a stronger vow silently displace an older one, so he can be re-sworn unnoticed; Judicium's spectral overload drowns him in readings.
**Saturation** · Three harmonisations sit under Tempering Capacity; exceeding it degrades all three at once, and a wide seated-Domain release over the saturation line risks a Crystal Fracture Event for everyone inside.
**Category** · Consent Doctrine, enforced: output is gated on a cause truly sworn; the Debt doctrine is live in a store that seizes the Shell if never spent.
**Essence** · The Aether Shell holds the store behind the Mercy barrier; the Essence Core is touched only by the permanent Traitus bias. Developmental Tier runs Resonant at Refraction to Sovereign at Invocation and Realization.
**School** · The Impetists hold that impressed impetus never decays, so spending an oath is a failure of faith; they store and never release, and an Impetist dies full, in a seizure his school calls transfiguration.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Lock for the Mercy barrier and Balance for the Oath gating, with Illumination filing the release; Disruption, chaotic anti-cohesion, is the discipline's unanswerable counter.
**Boundary** · The barrier between the knight's Shell and its relaxed state, set high enough not to leak and low enough that a chosen act crosses it.
**Laminae** · Strain Lamina is the trap, Mercy Lamina the barrier height, Oath Lamina the amplifier read from nearby affinities; the oath is the ignition.
**Effect** · Until release, nothing: he takes blows and gives no ground, a steady pressure on the air near him. Then a knee, a shield or a hand on the brand, and the release arrives heavy before bright, the ground stiffening and the give going out of things.
**Domain** · At the top, with a Domain seated, the release leaves as law: things that are sworn do not break, and the ground stops giving way for as long as his pulse carries it.
**Failure** · Seizure when the store passes what the Mercy Lamina holds, a Structure fault, escaped only by a deliberate oath-bleed; fraying under Disruption; spectral overload; and Coherence drift when he acts without a sworn cause.
**Bleed** · A held store does not bleed; the waste leaves once, at release, as heat, sound and structural bleed at his Shell: 30 to 40 percent at Refraction, 20 to 30 from Transcendence.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · He does not react to blows, which means he is filling, and the store has no visible level; then the kneel, the shield or the hand on the brand is the whole warning. Afterwards the ground stays stiff and quiet and he is visibly less.
**Limits** · No fast, repeated or incidental output and no partial release; output fades without a sworn cause; above A the layers cannot ride one release.
**Fails against** · Only incidental output, which it cannot release; the terms of his vow, or a stronger vow displacing it; Coherence drift; cold, shielding and movement; a lost hand or knee before the gesture; Disruption, which frays him.
**Store** · Every blow banks, light ones included, and a store never spent walks toward seizure; the timer runs only while he keeps taking blows.

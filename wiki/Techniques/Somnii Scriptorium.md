# Somnii Scriptorium

## Somnii Scriptorium

### Summary card

**Effect** · The target hears his name and his awareness is pulled into a room built by his own dreaming mind, where the caster can question him, confront him or simply hold him, while his body stays standing and breathing. Used the other way, it pulls an ally out of a traumatic moment.
**Cost** · 1 percent of full reserve each turn held, 3 percent for the full working. The caster lags half a beat in the real room and carries home fragments of the target's fear or grief.
**Limit** · It traps and questions but cannot rewrite who the target is; a strong will resists entry and a fractured mind distorts what it yields.
**Counter** · A covered mouth before the name ends, a blow to the caster that opens the seal, a ward against forced relocation, or an ally breaking in mid-transfer.
**What nobody knows** · Why some targets remember every page of what happened inside and others wake with no memory the dream occurred at all; one reading blames how hot the target's own recording ran, and nobody can yet say which it will be.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · Cortical decoupling with the brainstem intact, a dissociative state: the target stands and breathes while his awareness is elsewhere, and his own dreaming machinery builds the room he finds himself in.
**Law** · Templated deposition: a supersaturated medium, here the target's dreaming mind, lays material along whatever template it is given, so the practitioner supplies geometry and the target pays for the rest.
**Limit** · Conscious report carries about 40 bits a second, so eighteen seconds hold about 720 bits, room for a name and a date, never a transmitted room.
**Body** · A standing body topples on a 0.30 s time constant, too fast for the cortex; brainstem loops at 40 to 100 ms keep it upright, and the medulla keeps it breathing, so upright and breathing is the correct prediction.
**Time** · Felt duration is rebuilt afterwards from how much was recorded, so a target whose recording ran hot recalls a long conversation, and one whose recording was suppressed recalls nothing, not even a gap.
**Two spaces** · Two tasks needing a central decision are run in turn, not together, so holding the room and the chamber costs 200 to 500 ms at every switch.

### Metaphysics

**Aether** · A Limina draw, favoured by darkness, silence, thin ground and ruins, suppressed by saturated Aether, crowds and active Domains. The Residue is a Claustra claim that must keep being renewed; many opened chambers deafen Harmonists.
**Wellspring** · Oneirion, the Wellspring of Dream (Limina): every arrangement of a system is a point in a space with no location, so the working addresses the target there rather than in the room; it strengthens Gnosis Cognition and Ardency Cascade.
**Wellspring** · Eidolyn, the Shaping Mind (Limina): intent is the template and the construct's solidity is real material behaviour, not illusion; it strengthens Ardency Density and Gnosis Retention.
**Inherited failures** · Oneirion's projection error lands the trajectory nowhere when mapped back to a room; Eidolyn's template defect reproduces any flaw in intent at full scale.
**Category** · Consent Doctrine: interrogation and a mercy-pull from trauma share one geometry but are two workings, one authorised, one debt; the Debt doctrine rides home as the empathic residue.
**Essence** · The Aether Shell pays for the address and the seal; the Essence Core is untouched. At Transcendence the Developmental Tier is Radiant.
**School** · The Oneirocritics read whatever fills a thin template as diagnostic of the man; a thin template holds more of the reader's own intent, so they record their own assumptions in the target's voice, and their confessions are sincere and false.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Reverie for the addressing, Lock for the Claustra seal, Form for Eidolyn's template.
**Boundary** · The coordinates the target's state is addressed in: the trajectory, not the room. A name is the anchor because a name is already an address; the glyph is inscribed toward him, so it needs line of sight.
**Effect** · He hears his name, then is simply somewhere and has been for a while, in a room that is convincing because he built it; only the questioner feels wrong, half a beat late. He returns to a body that stood where he left it.
**Seal** · The Claustra seal is the one law actively held, renewed against a Fate-side claim for three turns; the practitioner stands in the doorway, and a seal that loses its anchor does not fade, it opens.
**Failure** · Boundary faults, since a name is shared, mishearable and sometimes not his: projection error ejects both minds violently; template defect returns the question as the answer; an opened seal leaves both on the wrong side.
**Bleed** · The energy does no work in the target; its waste leaves as heat and light at the practitioner's Shell, so the practitioner is the lit one while the chamber stands: 30 to 40 percent at Refraction, 20 to 30 at Master.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · The practitioner says the target's name aloud; the target stands upright, breathing and absent for three turns; the practitioner glows and lags half a beat. Afterwards a conversation too long for the time, or no sign at all, the easier to miss.
**Limits** · It traps and questions but cannot rewrite a personality; a strong will resists it and a fractured mind distorts what it yields; three turns, 200 metres, line of sight.
**Fails against** · A covered mouth before the name ends, or a blow to the practitioner, which opens the seal; the target's path Knotted or the anchor Locked first; a ward against forced relocation; an ally breaking in mid-transfer; crowds and Domains around the practitioner.
**Half-beat** · While the chamber stands the practitioner is late to everything in the room; and the first use shows the working to any survivor.
**Wards** · A ward is Runecraft on a substrate and fails when its substrate is chiselled, never by argument.
**Look up** · The Limina entries for Oneirion and Eidolyn and their Failures; the Claustra seals; the Counterplay register for Lock, Knot and wards; the Refraction and Transcendence Stage tables.

# Somnium Scriptorium

## Somnium Scriptorium

---

### Somnium Scriptorium

*(The Dream Scriptorium)*

#### Summary card

**Effect** · A target's body stays exactly where it is, upright and breathing, while their awareness is pulled into a narrative space Serenyra controls, where she can question them, confront them, or simply hold them.
**Cost** · Emotional residue from inside the dream clings to her afterward; she carries fragments of whatever fear or grief the target carried in, and running both spaces at once exhausts her faster than either alone would.
**Limit** · A target with an unusually strong will, or a mind already fractured, can resist entry outright or distort the narrative Serenyra is trying to hold; she cannot rewrite who they fundamentally are in the time she has.
**Counter** · A ward built against forced narrative relocation blocks the pull outright, and anything protecting the target's psyche directly can intervene mid-transfer. Breaking her concentration from outside ejects both of them, violently.
**What nobody knows** · Why some targets remember every page of what happened inside and others wake with no memory the dream occurred at all.

#### Design Chain

**Trigger** · An inscribed glyph that successfully anchors to a target's psyche, usually accompanied by speaking the target's name or true title.
**Function** · Draws the target's awareness inward into a constructed narrative scenario while their body remains physically present and outwardly catatonic.
**Mechanism** · Oneirion routes the working through conceptual rather than physical space, the same law that lets any Oneirion-harmonized working operate on a target's inner landscape directly. Eidolyn gives the narrative scenario enough solidity to hold a second consciousness inside it rather than dissolving the instant attention wavers, the way Eidolyn gives any mental construct material weight.
**Numerical Effect** · Effective to 200 metres, line-of-sight required. Holds up to roughly three exchanges of real time; subjective time inside the construct can run far longer, though the source gives no ratio and none is invented here.
**Target Response** · The target's body goes catatonic but stays alive and physically undisturbed; their awareness experiences the dream-chamber as a real, navigable space.
**Consequence** · Serenyra can question, confront, or simply hold the target inside the construct; used the other direction, she can pull an ally out of a traumatic moment as an act of mercy rather than interrogation.
**Limitation** · She cannot rewrite the target's core personality in the time available. The technique traps and questions; it does not remake anyone.
**Weakness** · A target with an unusually strong will, or a mind already fractured by prior trauma, can resist anchoring or distort the narrative once inside. Anything protecting the target's psyche can intervene directly.
**Cost** · Empathic residue clings to her after use, carrying fragments of the target's fear or grief home with her. Sustaining both the real-world and dream-space presence simultaneously exhausts her faster than single-space work.
**Counterplay** · A ward built against forced narrative relocation blocks the pull before it anchors. An external disruption to her concentration ejects both parties violently, and something standing guard over the target's psyche can sever the anchor from inside.

#### Origin

The source gives no date or founding problem for Somnium Scriptorium beyond Serenyra's own authorship. Its dual stated use, interrogation and mercy, suggests it was built to answer both problems at once rather than one first and the other as an afterthought, but the card gives no history to confirm which came first. No location is given beyond the Iridescent Archive filing destination, unattested in current canon.

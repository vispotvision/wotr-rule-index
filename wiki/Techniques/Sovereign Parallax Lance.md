# Sovereign Parallax Lance

## Sovereign Parallax Lance

### Summary card

**Effect** · A thrust or gesture sends a beam of black-gold refracted light out to 900 m; where it lands, it collapses every way the target could be hurt into the one that does the most damage and forces that wound to be the one that happens.
**Cost · The practitioner feels** an echo of the wound she just chose for someone else, and the Fractura-Cataclysm interaction leaves her own equilibrium shaken for a short while afterward.
**Limit** · Cannot select a harm-state the target's own structure could not physically sustain, cannot be thrown twice at full strength without the cost climbing sharply, and cannot overrule a being whose own Temperance meets or exceeds Stage XII.
**Counter** · A working that reads the selection before it completes, or that has already flattened its own probability down to nothing, gives the Lance no wound-version to choose.
**What nobody knows.** Whether the Lance selects the wound its author would have chosen, or the one the target has spent their whole life most afraid of.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · Weakest-link fracture: out of all the ways a target could fail, one flaw is chosen and loaded past its breaking point, so he fails in the worst way already written into him. No wound is made that was not already true.
**Law** · The Griffith criterion: stress gathers at a crack tip as K = σ√(πa), and the body fails when K reaches its toughness; the crack then runs near half the speed of sound in the material and does not stop.
**Limit** · A body with no flaw population has nothing to select; a flawless lattice (Weibull modulus 20 and above) offers one way to fail or none.
**Menu** · The spread of failure modes follows Weibull statistics: a worked, scarred body (modulus 5 to 10) offers many; flaw population alone makes glass 100 times weaker in bulk than as pristine fibre.
**Yield** · About 400 TJ, 95.6 kilotons, a real blast sized against an Emanation Shell, not a body; loading a known Shell fault breaks it 30 to 50 percent below its rated ceiling.
**Cataclysm** · Routed through Cataclysm the release becomes a detonation, blast radius growing with the cube root of yield: a three-kilometre event that engulfs the caster at 900 metres, plus a 40 m echo along the fracture line.
**Dissolution** · Routed through Dissolution the same choice brings no shock, echo or line; the target comes apart at its own pace, mixing being downhill, and the room is left standing.

### Metaphysics

**Aether** · Vectoria at heart, with five Families in all; Vectoria likes mass, height and brittle or pre-stressed material and fails on loose sediment, in free fall and on uniform soft ground. The residue rings along the fracture line for a few seconds, downs others' workings and deafens Harmonists.
**Wellspring** · Fractura, the Breaking Point (Vectoria): everything fails at a pre-existing flaw, and the bearer sees flaws and can load exactly one.
**Wellspring** · Cataclysm, the Shattered Law (Caloria): a flame meeting confinement jumps to a detonation, a change of kind, not degree.
**Wellspring** · Dissolution, the Unbinding Stream (Limina): the mixed state has more disorder, so the Crystal leans toward unbinding.
**Wellspring** · Basilithe (Materia): read to find where stiffness holds and fails; a stronger Basilithe anchor in the target leaves nothing to read.
**Wellsprings** · Also Petralon and Penance (Vectoria), Tenebra and Oneirion (Limina), Tarturon (Materia) and Orrenthal (Fulguria): ten held against Tempering Capacity, Materia set against Limina three times over.
**Inherited failures** · Fractura's self-inspection shows the caster his own flaws under strain; Cataclysm's reflected overpressure returns the shock to its origin in confinement; Dissolution's indiscriminate solvency takes load-bearing structure too; Basilithe's anchor cannot be detuned away from a matching force.
**Category** · Correspondence: a line in the room and a choice among failures are one act, as a prism picks which colour exits without making any; Debt is live in the echoed pain and the rising second cast.
**Essence** · The Aether Shell delivers; the Essence Core pays to hold every unresolved failure open until one is forced. Developmental Tier Sovereign.
**School** · The Sufficientists train to be equally weak everywhere so no flaw is longest; perfect symmetry is the widest menu there is, and Weibull kills them.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Measure for the reading, Edge for the selection, Ruin for the over-resolution.
**Boundary** · Which flaw carries the stress: load that one, now, at this angle; the flaws, the rising stress and the failure were already there.
**Effect** · Two fingers drawn together, a line of black-gold light, no interval. The target feels not impact but recognition: the old break, the joint never right, the seam in the Shell he stopped noticing, failing now.
**Unavoidable** · Resolution is instantaneous, so avoidance fails; an escape needing an outcome already spent simply does not produce it. Domain manipulation and reality-grade countermeasures still answer it.
**Failure** · Boundary faults, since an imprecise selection runs into the room: self-inspection at 900 m with ten currents open, reflected overpressure at the origin, solvency of what held the room up, resonant catastrophe past the Basilithe counter.
**Bleed** · 10 to 15 percent leaves as heat, light and sound at the caster's Shell: a thirteen-kiloton flare at release.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · Two fingers drawn together over one target, then a hand extended, visible only to one watching the caster; a 50 km pressure field before, a flare at release, a ringing line and, after Cataclysm, three kilometres of ruin.
**Limits** · No flaw, nothing to select; no override of anyone at or above Emanation; single target with a 40 m echo; not cheap twice.
**Fails against** · Distance beyond 900 metres; homogeneous soft ground; a Locked flaw site or a Knotted reading; Basilithe flawlessness; flattened probabilities; a substituted Oneirion dream-self; regeneration against Dissolution; spread targets.
**Selection** · The law must select the worst failure available; a target with none worth choosing leaves it only a lesser wound.
**Pressure** · At Emanation the passive Aura Pressure is law within 50 km, so the caster cannot approach unnoticed without paying continuously to hide it.

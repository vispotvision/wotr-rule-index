# Sovereign's Reprisal

## Sovereign's Reprisal

### Summary card

**Effect** · Every blow that lands on the practitioner while the harness is charged stays in him instead of passing through, and arrives back out as a single ring of rupturing Aether the instant he chooses to let it go.
**Cost** · Spinal Aether recoil and a few seconds of vertigo on release: the harness discharges through the same channels that carried the blow in, and they do not enjoy giving it back.
**Limit** · The harness has to take the hit first. There is no reprisal without an impact already banked.
**Counter** · Piercing damage and sustained ranged harassment both deny the harness the one thing it needs, a concentrated blunt impact large enough to bank.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the harness has a ceiling past which it cannot hold any more, or whether its author has simply never been hit hard enough, for long enough, to find out.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · Impulse-gated storage and all-round discharge: a runed harness banks blunt blows instead of letting them finish, then returns the whole bank at once as a single pressure step through everyone within eight metres.
**Law** · Impulse: what is banked is momentum and what goes out is momentum; the first law says the joules are the attackers', plus whatever the wearer adds to spike them.
**Limit** · The threshold reads total impulse, so a hammer (20 kg·m/s at 200 J) trips it and a bullet (8 kg·m/s at 3,200 J) or any piercing strike does not trip it cleanly.
**Stagger** · Taking a man off his feet costs about 80 kg·m/s and 40 J through his body, cheap; doing it by air blast at 8 m would take 17 to 50 MJ, which is why the Reprisal couples through bodies.
**Ceiling** · Overchannel spikes the release to 2 to 5 times the sustainable S ceiling, 8.4 to 121.5 TJ; at that scale it is a kiloton event with a fireball near 120 m, discharged through the wearer's spine, which survives about 1.2 MJ.

|---|---|---|
| Hammer head, 1 kg at 20 m/s | m·v and ½m·v² | 20 kg·m/s, 200 J |
| Bullet, 10 g at 800 m/s | m·v and ½m·v² | 8 kg·m/s, 3,200 J |
| Twenty men in a press | 20 × 80 kg·m/s | 1,600 kg·m/s, eighty hammer blows |
| High-strength steel as a store | σ² ÷ 2E | about 1.3 kJ/kg, so the bank is not in the metal |
| Ceiling | 2 to 5 × 4.184 to 24.3 TJ | 8.4 to 121.5 TJ, 2 to 29 kt |

### Metaphysics

**Aether** · Fulguria likes dry air and clear sight and fails behind iron and in fog; Caloria likes steep heat gradients and fails in cold equilibrium. A cold, iron-shielded stone fortress in winter sits in both suppression lists. After a release the eight metres around the wearer deafen Harmonists.
**Wellspring** · Judicium, the Wellspring of Truth (Fulguria): no substance can show another's spectrum, so the bank is read without spending any of it; it strengthens Gnosis Acuity and Analysis.
**Wellspring** · Cinerion, the Ash Veil (Caloria): what survives a fire is not waste, so a blow becomes stock rather than damage; it strengthens Tempering Yield and Harmonics Stability.
**Inherited failures** · Judicium's spectral overload misreads the threshold in a crowd; Cinerion's fouling coats the conduit so the bank silently stops filling at the moment of greatest accumulation, with no gauge to warn.
**Category** · Debt, worn plainly: a blow is booked as tribute and called in as one payment at the creditor's choosing; Residue is the mechanism that lets the debt be held.
**Essence** · The Aether Shell carries both bank and discharge, which run through it, not around it, so the bill lands in the spine; Resilience Fortification redirects hostile Essence through structure. Developmental Tier Radiant.
**School** · The Tributaries read banked force as sovereignty and never release; fouling silts their conduits unseen, and their masters learn their armour stopped working only when they finally answer.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Lock for the banking, Measure for the threshold, Return for the discharge.
**Boundary** · Where an impact is permitted to finish: the harness adds nothing to the blow, it says not yet, and the transfer completes into the wearer's Crystal instead of bone.
**Release** · The boundary is a when, not a how much, so ending it releases everything; storage erased every blow's direction, so the bank leaves as one ring in all directions and does not choose targets.
**Effect** · Attackers hit a huge armoured figure that just receives, strikes landing clean and giving nothing back up the arm. The sigil over its eye brightens; then an iron-crack, and everyone near stops being on their feet at once, those who hit hardest most.
**Failure** · Structure faults: fouling; a misread threshold; overfill forcing the release at an unchosen moment; Overchannel fracture that accumulates and becomes permanent; and a broken inscription.
**Bleed** · Waste leaves as heat and sound at the wearer's Shell and down the spine: 20 to 30 percent at Master; at ceiling scale the fraction coupled into the body is fatal.

**Cost** · The release equals what attackers put in, boosted 2 to 5 times from reserve at (spike − 1) × bank ÷ η: nearly free against small inputs, ruinous against large ones; every release also brings spinal recoil and vertigo.
**Duration** · The bank holds until released or forced by overfill; the release is one instant, followed by a few seconds of eyes-shut recovery.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · No glow or gather, only blows landing clean and returning nothing; then the sigil over the eye brightens, and that is all the warning. Afterwards a still figure with eyes closed amid an eight-metre ring of fallen men.
**Limits** · No reprisal without a banked impact, and it cannot fire on nothing; all or nothing, no target choice; eight metres; one full answer to a large input per full reserve.
**Fails against** · Piercing through the plates, since a spike both slips the threshold and breaks the inscription; ranged harassment; a cold iron-shielded interior; a finishing point Locked first; the wearer's allies crowded around it; a large input, and the cost of answering it.
**Fouling** · Small blows bank without ever making a moment worth answering; fouling gives no warning, and the conduit silts.
**Recovery** · The seconds after a release pass with eyes shut and careful breathing; they are the wearer's only unguarded time.

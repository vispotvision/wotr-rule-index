# Speculum Harmoniae

## Speculum Harmoniae

---

### Speculum Harmoniae

*(The Harmony Mirror)*

#### Summary card

**Effect** · A pursuer chasing Serenyra, or anyone she has read, ends up chasing a second body that moves and answers like the first.
**Cost** · Every mirror spends a slice of her own sensory record of the target; the next time she needs to counter that same person precisely, the read is thinner until she rebuilds it by observing them again.
**Limit** · The double cannot land a real blow. Struck once by anything that matters, it stops existing.
**Counter** · A working that reads the reversion's shape before it completes, or a sense keyed to fear, hatred, or grief rather than surface signature, tells the two bodies apart. *A hunter tracking emotion rather than Essence contour is not fooled.*
**What nobody knows** · Whether the double, in the instant before it is struck, feels anything, or whether the hollow beat Serenyra reports afterward is hers alone.

---

#### Codex line

**Wellspring** · Mirithane (governing), Oneirion (secondary), *re-sourced from the source's "Harmonic" and "Dream."*
**Family** · Limina.
**Physics Domain** · Entropy, Void and Mind.
**Category** · Animatria, a detached fragment of her Essence Core given brief independent operation and motion, which is why it fails outright against anything that severs the live line back to her.
**Craft** · Spellcraft, gestured (a circle traced in the air around the target).
**Stage floor** · Stage V, Splintering.
**Grade required** · B-Grade (Splintering's ceiling; instability risk above 320).
**Path gate** · Spirit Path.

---

#### Design Chain

**Trigger** · A target within range and a completed tracing gesture.
**Function** · Reads the target's current Essence state and casts it outward as a construct that carries the same signature and moves under its own limited agency, so anything tracking the target's Essence contour, sight-line, or predictive pattern spends its attention on the construct instead.
**Mechanism** · Specular reflection, the same phase-conjugate law Mirithane runs on: what leaves the Shell returns the wavefront that was put into it, with no flattering correction. Serenyra takes a true reading of the target and lets the reading walk. Oneirion supplies the conceptual space the reading walks through, so the construct behaves like a body rather than an afterimage.
**Numerical Effect** · The double holds for roughly three exchanges or until struck by an attack that would matter against the real target; the source gives no AU/s or EU figure, so none is stated here.
**Target Response** · Trackers keyed to Essence signature, sight, or predictive modeling read the double as the genuine article until it is struck or examined directly.
**Consequence** · Fire, attention, and tracking that would have found the real target find the construct instead, for as long as it holds.
**Limitation** · The construct is phantasmal. It cannot deal real damage and it cannot survive contact with anything that reads as a genuine threat.
**Weakness** · Direct Void-aligned sight bypasses the illusion outright, since it does not read Essence signature to begin with. Touch-based senses notice the double has no mass behind it.
**Cost** · Each casting draws on her stored sensory record of the target. Repeated use against the same person leaves that record thinner, and her next precise counter against them is correspondingly worse until she observes them again.
**Counterplay** · Anyone reading the reversion's shape as it forms can decline to let the split register as real. Emotional trackers, hunters keyed to fear or hatred rather than surface signature, see through it without needing to.

#### Origin

Serenyra devised Speculum Harmoniae as a decoy and diversion tool; the source gives no date or location for the invention beyond her own practice. She is the only named practitioner in the card and the only one credited with using it. Her institutional home is given only by the card's own filing destination, **the Iridescent Archive**, unattested in current canon, kept because nothing in the source places her anywhere else.

### Nexus Harmoniae

*(The Nexus of Harmony)*
> Bundled into this same Trello card as item "7." in the source list. Converted alongside Speculum Harmoniae rather than dropped, per the same migration note above.

#### Summary card

**Effect** · Every working cast inside a battlefield-scale radius around Serenyra passes through an invisible crossroads first, letting her lean allied output up and enemy output down before either lands.
**Cost** · Holding that many simultaneous readings risks real cognitive strain: disorientation, migraine-grade overload, or brief cross-sense confusion.
**Limit** · The bias is small. It is a strategist's tool, not a battlefield-ending one, and it drops the instant she loses consciousness or leaves the field.
**Counter** · A strong personal Domain partially insulates its owner from the nexus's bias, and a Wellspring technique built to mute its own signature ducks under her detection entirely.
**What nobody knows** · Whether the nexus reads every working in range or only the ones loud enough for her to notice, and what a working sits just under that line would have to look like.

---

#### Codex line

**Wellspring** · Judicium (governing), Aurevane (secondary), *re-sourced from the source's "Harmonic, unification of tones" and "Celestial, macro-scale ordering."*
**Family** · Fulguria.
**Physics Domain** · Electromagnetism.
**Category** · Concordia, the large-scale harmonic alignment that Harmonia's own apex expression names; every working in range is made to answer to one shared frequency before it resolves.
**Craft** · Spellcraft, spoken and gestured (a chant with a sweeping gesture, carried through the Aether Chord Staff, unattested item in current canon, kept as her named focus).
**Stage floor** · Stage VIII, Transcendence.
**Grade required** · S-Grade.
**Path gate** · Fate Path.

---

#### Design Chain

**Trigger** · A completed chant-and-gesture cast from a stationary position.
**Function** · Raises a wide, faint field over an area; any working cast or already active inside it is routed through the field before its outcome resolves, which is where Serenyra applies her bias.
**Mechanism** · Judicium's discernment law reads each working's tonal structure the way a Thaumic Harmonist reads a spell in progress; Aurevane's illumination law carries that reading across the whole field at once rather than letting it fade with distance, which is what makes the effect battlefield-scale instead of line-of-sight.
**Numerical Effect** · Radius up to roughly one kilometre, centred on her; the field holds for about six exchanges. The source gives no figure for how much output shifts, so the bias is stated as modest rather than quantified.
**Target Response** · Allied workings read as marginally stronger and enemy workings as marginally weaker for as long as they pass through the field; every working in range becomes slightly more predictable to anyone reading the field itself.
**Consequence** · A battle fought inside the field trends, incrementally, toward whichever side she favours, without any single working being visibly altered.
**Limitation** · The bias is deliberately subtle. It is a strategic asset, not a spectacle, and cannot win an exchange outright.
**Weakness** · Anti-field workings or a Domain strong enough to overwrite local law can cancel the nexus outright. A Void-aligned disturbance inside the field turns the nexus into dangerous interference rather than shutting it down cleanly.
**Cost** · She perceives every harmonic in the field simultaneously, which risks migraine-grade overload or brief cross-sense confusion the longer she sustains it.
**Counterplay** · A practitioner with a strong personal Domain is partially insulated. A Wellspring technique built to run quiet ducks under her detection floor entirely and is never routed through the field at all.

# Speculum Recordatio

## Speculum Recordatio

### Summary card

**Effect** · Reads the Essence signature and structure of a spell mid-cast and answers it with a manifested, fractured echo of the same working, which collides with the original rather than dispelling it.
**Cost** · Each use draws on Serenyra's own recall; she is left with a blank space in her recent memory and a moment of mental static from the feedback.
**Limit** · Only Essence-based or Aetheric workings leave a signature to record; mundane projectiles and A-Temporal Void effects have nothing to copy.
**Counter** · A caster who finishes an effect instantaneously, before the Thesauriel completes its imprint, is never caught by it; a Cataclysmic-tier casting overloads the recording and shatters the counterspell instead of catching it.
> **What nobody knows.** Why the echo comes back fractured and dissonant rather than a clean copy of the original, when every other part of the recording is exact.

### Codex line

Wellspring Oneirion (primary, Limina, Entropy/Void/Mind), Mirithane (secondary, Limina, Entropy/Void/Mind). Category Mnemata. Craft Runecraft. Stage floor VI, Glory. Grade required A-Grade. Path gate Spirit Path.

### Design Chain

**Trigger** · An incoming Essence-based or Aetheric working, cast within her line of sight, at range up to a kilometre.
**Function** · Serenyra raises the Thesauriel and records the incoming working's structure rather than blocking or absorbing it, then manifests a phantasmal double of it aimed back at the original.
**Mechanism** · This runs on real destructive-interference logic: two waveforms of matched frequency and opposed phase cancel where a matched, in-phase pair would reinforce. The Thesauriel imprints the incoming working's frequency signature closely enough to build a phase-inverted double, and the collision is where the two workings would otherwise have completed themselves.
**Numerical Effect** · At Stage VI, A-Grade, the collision reliably nullifies, prematurely detonates, or feeds an equivalent working back onto its own caster. Against a working two or more Tiers above her own ceiling, the recording itself overloads.
**Target Response** · The original caster feels their own working answered by a version of itself that should not exist, and the two either cancel, detonate early, or reverse onto them.
**Consequence** · The original working never reaches its intended target in the form it was cast.
**Limitation** · It answers a working already in flight; it does not prevent the working from being cast in the first place, and it has no purchase on anything with no Essence signature.
**Weakness** · Void and Entropic castings carry no signature the Thesauriel can imprint, and force far above what the Thesauriel can hold simply passes through the mirror-ward.
**Cost** · One memory slot per use, and a paroxysmal Aether surge that leaves her mentally addled for a moment afterward.
**Counterplay** · Attack faster than the imprint completes, or attack with a casting whose scale is calibrated to exceed what a single Thesauriel reading can hold.

### FOW line

Governing Primary Gnosis (Diagnosis, Perception) and Harmonics (Fidelity). Stage floor VI, Glory. Grade required A-Grade. Path gate Spirit Path. Resonant Pair none indicated in the source.

### Origin

The source names Serenyra as the sole practitioner and names her instrument, the Thesauriel, but gives no account of where or from whom she derived the technique, or what problem first required it.

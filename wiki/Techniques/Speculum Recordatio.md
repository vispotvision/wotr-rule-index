# Speculum Recordatio

## Speculum Recordatio

### Summary card

**Effect** · Reads the Essence signature and structure of a spell mid-cast and answers it with a manifested, fractured echo of the same working, which collides with the original rather than dispelling it.
**Cost** · Each use draws on Serenyra's own recall; she is left with a blank space in her recent memory and a moment of mental static from the feedback.
**Limit** · Only Essence-based or Aetheric workings leave a signature to record; mundane projectiles and A-Temporal Void effects have nothing to copy.
**Counter** · A caster who finishes an effect instantaneously, before the Thesauriel completes its imprint, is never caught by it; a Cataclysmic-tier casting overloads the recording and shatters the counterspell instead of catching it.
> **What nobody knows.** Why the echo comes back fractured and dissonant rather than a clean copy of the original, when every other part of the recording is exact.

### Codex line

Wellspring Oneirion (primary, Limina, Entropy/Void/Mind), Mirithane (secondary, Limina, Entropy/Void/Mind). Category Mnemata. Craft Runecraft. Stage floor VI, Glory. Grade required A-Grade. Path gate Spirit Path.

### FOW line

Governing Primary Gnosis (Diagnosis, Perception) and Harmonics (Fidelity). Stage floor VI, Glory. Grade required A-Grade. Path gate Spirit Path. Resonant Pair none indicated in the source.

### Origin

The source names Serenyra as the sole practitioner and names her instrument, the Thesauriel, but gives no account of where or from whom she derived the technique, or what problem first required it.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · Feedforward active noise control: the practitioner records a working in flight, builds its inverse and throws it into the original, so the two meet and a broken residual goes back the way it came.
**Law** · Superposition: a wave plus its inverse with phase error θ leaves 2A·|sin(θ/2)|, so a near-perfect inverse cancels and a poor one adds.
**Limit** · Causality: the record and the answer must arrive before the working completes, so range buys the time and contact range loses it; above 60° of phase error the answer changes sign and helps the attacker.
**Fracture** · Phase error grows with frequency as θ = 2π·f·δt, so the low end cancels, the middle partly, and the high end comes back doubled and off-key: the record is exact, the clock is not.
**Local** · Cancellation holds only where the two paths match, a zone of quiet a few metres wide at the point where the incoming working would have finished.
**Channel** · Only Essence and Aetheric workings leave a signature to record; a crossbow bolt or a signature-free void effect is not on the channel.

### Metaphysics

**Aether** · Limina for both Wellsprings, suppressed by crowds, saturated Aether and active Domains, the ground it most often works on, so it pays its terrain penalty in use. The residual leaves Resonance at the collision point that deafens Harmonists.
**Wellspring** · Oneirion, the Wellspring of Dream (Limina): acting on a working's trajectory holds its whole structure as one object, the right law for a record and the wrong one for a collision in a room.
**Wellspring** · Mirithane, the Wellspring of Reflection (Limina): a phase-conjugate mirror sends a wave back along its own path, so the answer finds the caster the way the caster found the practitioner.
**Inherited failures** · Oneirion's projection error is the phase error in the register's words, the echo's standing fracture; Mirithane's mirror does not discriminate, so every answer carries the practitioner's own condition back to the caster.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Lock sets the collision where the incoming working would have completed; Measure takes the imprint; Return sends the residual home.
**Boundary** · The phase a copy may hold against its original, pinned at inversion; beyond that limit superposition subtracts.
**Effect** · The caster sees his own working in front of him twice, answered by his own shape; they meet where his would have landed, and back comes his working with its bottom gone and its sharp parts doubled and wrong. Misjudged, it comes back louder.
**Return** · The practitioner need not know where the caster is, only have been targeted by him.
**Failure** · Boundary faults: overload against workings two or more Grades above the ceiling; pass-through of force beyond the Codex; reinforcement past 60°, an attack on its own side; projection error; and a book that can be taken.
**Bleed** · The waste is itself phase error, leaving as heat, sound and phase noise at the Shell, 30 to 40 percent at Glory, so a low-η practitioner is brighter and less precise.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · A book raised and held open before the incoming working arrives, and an imprint that visibly takes time; afterwards a few seconds of blankness, like someone who lost the thread. The imprint is the warning; the blankness follows every catch.
**Limits** · Needs a signature; answers only a working in flight; misses anything finished before the imprint; overloads two Grades up; a faster practitioner raises the crossover but never removes it.
**Fails against** · A Codex knocked away or damaged; shot; quick, sharp workings (they come back reinforced); contact range; crowds and Domains; workings the caster can survive receiving back; repeated triggering, which spends slots.
**Look up** · The Limina entries for Oneirion and Mirithane and their Failures; Runecraft and its substrates; Gnosis Retention; the Glory Stage table and the Grade reaction speeds.

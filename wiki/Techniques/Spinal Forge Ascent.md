# Spinal Forge Ascent

## Spinal Forge Ascent

### Summary card

**Effect** · The caster's own skeleton and musculature snap into a higher operating configuration, faster contraction, denser bone, sharper kinetic read, for as long as the buff holds.
**Cost** · **The transformation's true cost is deferred, not paid.** *Ascensio spreads the stress of the change across the whole buff window rather than charging it up front, and what was spread comes due afterward as microfractures and Essenced muscle fatigue.*
**Limit** · **One higher configuration at a time.** The body can only be re-latticed into a single state; stacking a second body-morph art on top forces a collision Transmutatio has no clean rule for resolving.
**Counter** · **An interrupted redistribution: massive blunt trauma** *delivered while the spread is still running collapses the schedule Ascensio built, and the stress that should have arrived gradually arrives all at once.*
> **What nobody knows** · Whether the debt Ascensio defers is ever smaller than the peak it avoided, or whether the technique's real trade is timing rather than total cost.

Spinal Forge Ascent is a documented body-Transmutation art of anonymous authorship, taught and studied rather than invented by one caster. Its established countermeasures are Judicium dampeners, Verdantia stabilization, and Titan-forge martial synergy.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · Anaerobic energy debt and a fibre-type shift, with bone compacted at constant mass: for eighteen seconds the fighter's own muscle and skeleton are rearranged for peak output, nothing added or removed, and afterwards the body pays.
**Law** · Hill's force-velocity relation, (P + a)·V = b·(P₀ − P): a muscle tuned for speed is weaker at every speed but the lowest; and the second law, since dissipation rises with rate, so the debt always exceeds the peak it avoided.
**Limit** · Bone densifies only by collapsing its spongy part, up to cortical density, 1.9 g/cm³; how far it hardens is set by the spongy bone the fighter started with, and each use spends some for good.
**Speed** · Re-expressing muscle toward the fastest fibre type raises shortening velocity five- to twelvefold from the same tissue; at ½mv² that is 25 to 144 times the delivered energy, covering the one-Grade step.
**Bone** · Denser bone at the same mass is thinner, but strength rises as density squared, so bending strength gains 41 percent, with break-even at an exponent of 1.5.
**Debt** · Glucose without oxygen earns 2 ATP and costs 6 to unwind, repaid later by the liver, an organ that was not in the fight; spreading the change lowers the peak and raises the total.

### Metaphysics

**Aether** · Spatium likes surveyed ground and Realmgates and fails on unmapped, contested ground; Vectoria likes mass and brittle, pre-stressed material and fails on loose, soft ground. Best in the training hall, worst on the battlefield. The spine's ignition leaves a brief Resonance that deafens a Harmonist nearby.
**Wellspring** · Transmutatio, the Law of Change (Spatium): one object in two frames gives two sets of numbers and no change in the object, so the body is re-described without loss; it strengthens Ardency Conversion and Tempering Processing.
**Wellspring** · Ascensio, the Ladder of Ascent (Vectoria): the inclined plane spreads a climb so the structure never sees the peak; it strengthens Tempering Acceleration and Harmonics Attunement.
**Inherited failures** · Transmutatio's basis loss leaves a fighter who cannot change back; Ascensio's work is not reduced, only redistributed, and physics says increased, so users mistake calm for no cost.
**Category** · Debt, worn plainly: the cost is a schedule, not a price; Correspondence underneath, since two descriptions are claimed as one body. Spellcraft delivered through the body, an Art, Body-Aligned.
**Essence** · The Aether Shell carries the ignition up the spine; the skeleton pays, on terms, through Ardency Overchannel. Developmental Tier Resonant at Refraction.
**School** · The Inflationists think strength is supply pumped down the nerves, so they defer long and read smoothness as headroom; Hill's law makes them fast and weak and the debt repays six for two, and their masters are their longest holders.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Hour for the deferral, the one that matters; Form for the re-lattice; Equivalence for the change of basis under conservation.
**Boundary** · The schedule on which a stress may arrive: the change runs now and its stress is pinned to arrive later; nothing is reduced or added.
**Effect** · The spine ignites molten-white from the base up with no wind-up. For eighteen seconds corrections arrive before they are earned, and blows land like a far heavier fighter's at the same mass; then it stops on time, the fighter still and paying.
**Loan** · Muscle and bone are one loan: fast glycolytic fibre that repays at a loss, and compacted bone that fails afterwards as cracks, not tears.
**Failure** · Structure faults: heavy blunt damage mid-spread collapses the schedule and lands the whole debt at once, worse than the blow; no second configuration over the first; halting the spread lands the stress; basis loss; the spent spongy bone.
**Bleed** · Waste leaves as heat and light at the Shell, 30 to 40 percent at Refraction, the molten-white spine itself.

**Cost** · Eight percent of full reserve per ignition, then the deferred bill in full at the end: microfractures through compacted bone and muscle fatigue. It carries the Overchannel penalty: damage accumulates with each ignition and, if not healed by rest, alchemy or Temperance, becomes permanent.
**Duration** · Eighteen seconds, three turns, on a fixed clock that cannot be hidden or shortened.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · A spine lit molten-white through the back, base upward, no gather, and eighteen seconds from that instant; afterwards a fighter standing still, breathing carefully, no longer a Grade above anyone.
**Limits** · One configuration at a time; hardening only as far as the spongy bone allows, and that store shrinks; the bill always lands.
**Fails against** · An exchange declined until it ends; the clock, ten seconds held off and the close by seventeen; heavy blunt trauma mid-spread, which forces the early close; broken, unsurveyed or soft ground; kinetic suppression, which halts it and lands the debt.
**Habit** · A habitual user mistakes the absence of crisis for the absence of cost while the damage settles in.
**Look up** · The Spatium and Vectoria entries for Transmutatio and Ascensio and their Failures; Overchannel and its healing; the Refraction Stage table; the taught records of the art itself.

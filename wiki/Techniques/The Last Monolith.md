# The Last Monolith

## The Last Monolith

### Summary card

**Effect** · Drakvor's own flesh stops behaving like flesh. Skin, bone, and the Essence running under both take on the failure curve of stone rather than the failure curve of a body, and blows that would open him instead ring off him.
**Cost** · Muscle tearing at the moment the state releases. The body that spent five exchanges being told it was stone has to remember, all at once, that it wasn't.
**Limit** · Mobility drops hard for as long as the state holds. Stone does not step lightly.
**Counter** · Entropy-type and dissolution Wellsprings unmake the coherence the state depends on rather than striking it, which is the one kind of attack the hardening was never built to answer.
**What nobody knows** · Whether the stiffness after release is the body correcting itself, or the body grieving the density it briefly had and no longer does.

### Origin

Drakvor's own philosophy for the state is "Let the world strike. I remain." He is the technique's sole known author, and where the state was first tested is not recorded.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · A change of failure law, the brittle and ductile trade: for about five turns the body fails like rock instead of flesh, as strong as bone in compression and ten times weaker in tension. Underneath runs sintering, grains fusing below melting.
**Law** · A stiff, brittle body returns nearly all of an elastic impact, so the blow is refused rather than absorbed; what is not returned is banked in the Crystal.
**Limit** · Brittle solids have no give: a crack runs at 1,200 to 1,800 m/s, faster than the practitioner can feel it, and 0.1 percent strain means the body cannot bend.
**Refusal** · An S-Grade blow taken into the body would vaporise it 97,000 times over, so the state does not couple: nearly all of it goes back up the attacker's weapon.
**Read** · The hardening is driven where the blow will land, so the read must beat contact by the body's own sound transit time.
| Quantity | Working | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Compression, bone against granite | ≈170 to 200; ≈130 to 250 MPa | an even trade |
| Tension, bone against granite | ≈120 to 150; ≈7 to 25 MPa | an order of magnitude lost |
| Bend before cracking, 0.5 m limb | 0.1% × 0.5 m | half a millimetre |
| News across a 2 m body | 2 ÷ 5,500 m/s | 0.36 ms |

| S-Grade ceiling against a body | 2.43 × 10¹³ ÷ 2.5 × 10⁸ J | vaporised 97,000 times over |

### Metaphysics

**Aether** · Draws Materia (worked stone, deep foundation) and Fulguria (dry air, clear sight); on a deck, bridge, scaffold or boat the primary current fails, because a man cannot become ground where the ground is provisional.
**Wellspring** · Coagula, the Unifying Pulse (Materia): sintering, a soul fused back together below melting, keeping its shape; it sets the body's failure curve toward stone's.
**Wellspring** · Judicium, the Wellspring of Truth (Fulguria): every element shows only its own spectrum; it reads the geometry and force of the incoming blow, and can read the practitioner's own voids.
**Inherited failures** · Coagula's residual porosity leaves voids where the next fracture starts, piling up with every use; Judicium's spectral overload in a crowded melee.
**Category** · Correspondence: a body and a stone share one failure law, so what is true of one is true of the other; Consent Doctrine underneath.
**Essence** · The whole Aether Shell is held in a shape it was not built for, with a continuous bleed into tissue whose properties have changed; Tempering Coherence is one of the two quantities it runs on.
**Sub-Stats** · Resilience Integrity and Tempering Coherence from Coagula; Gnosis Acuity and Analysis from Judicium.
**Path gates** · Body primary with a Spirit component clears every gate; without the Spirit component Acuity caps at C and field diagnosis at B, and the hardening comes uniform and too late.
**School** · The Petrifaction school treats the state as a refuge to hold ever longer, since stone preserves; every turn held is void laid down, so their practice is choosing at leisure where they will break.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Form carries the change of failure law; Balance with Measure carries the reading of the blow.
**Boundary** · The condition under which the tissue may fail moves to stone's; elasticity and fracture run unchanged on the new surface.
**Trigger** · Declared and committed before contact, sustained by draw; from Transcendence the chain is internal with no anchor.
**Effect** · He plants, the skin goes glossy stone-black, and strikes ring back up the attacker's arm with a low mountain note, the same on the fifth blow; he cannot turn, roll, yield to a lock or move under a lever.
**Release** · The gloss goes and muscle tears as a body held still for five turns lurches all at once into the shape it should have had.
**Failure** · Structural fault: the working holds and the practitioner does not; tension, old voids, entropy, Sublimatio reversal, top S-Grade speed and a crowd each break it.
**Bleed** · 20 to 30 percent of the hold leaves the Shell as heat and sound, continuously.

**Duration** · Up to five turns, 30 seconds, then release.
**Damage** · Torn muscle at every release, and porosity that never clears: each hold chooses where the next break begins.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · He plants and stops fighting like a man, skin glossy and black, a low ringing that never changes, joints gone still; at the end, a still man moving in ways he did not choose.
**Limits** · Resists up to the S-Grade ceiling as a structure, not beyond; no movement worth the name; nothing against tension, entropy or phase reversal.
**Fails against** · Wedge, hook, pick, lever or lock; a second hit on one spot; Dissolution or Sublimatio; a deck or barge; any need to move; the moment of release after a long hold; speed beyond the read; Coherence drift.
**Look up** · The Materia and Fulguria favour lists; the Coagula and Judicium Failures; the S-Grade Strike Force and Contact Range; the Transcendence Stage table; Dissolution and Sublimatio entries.

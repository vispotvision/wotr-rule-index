# Veil of Verdant Pact

## Veil of Verdant Pact

### Summary card

**Effect** · A living dome of grown matter closes over the caster and every ally within five metres, and for as long as it holds, wounds taken inside it lag behind whatever caused them. *Fire arrives and the char becomes new bark. Frost arrives and the numbness becomes sap-slow warmth.*
**Cost** · **The ward routes injury through the caster's own body before it routes it into growth.** *Every burn, break, and poison the dome absorbs is felt by whoever raised it, in full, for the second it takes the wood to catch up.*
**Limit** · **Anti-growth law overrides growth law.** Exuroth's dehydrating heat and Nihiloth's null field are not damage the dome can convert; they are the absence of the conditions growth needs, and the dome only feeds on inputs it can metabolise.
**Counter** · **The second it needs to root: the dome** *takes roughly one second to close from declaration to full coverage, and a strike landing inside that window lands on nothing grown yet.*
> **What nobody knows** · Whether the dome draws on Verdantia's law alone, or on something older that Verdantia only resembles, since ancient Materia Primordia mineral seams have turned up threaded through its root system on every occasion anyone has checked.

### Origin

Veil of Verdant Pact is a documented Vitalia/Materia ward of anonymous, folk-transmitted origin, known and taught rather than invented by one caster. Its established countermeasures are Absolution's flames bypassing it, Tenebra decaying the vines, and heavy-metal toxins weakening the roots.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · Indeterminate growth used as an energy sink: a wooden dome with no finished size grows over the practitioner and allies, and since it has no shape to damage, the energy of a blow can only extend it.
**Law** · A structure with no terminal form turns energy above its elastic limit into more structure, at about 17.5 MJ per kilogram of wood; its mineral frame is allotropy, one substance in another lattice.
**Limit** · At B-Grade the build buys a shell under three centimetres thick, a screen not a bunker; a point load breaks it, and drying the wood beats it where fire cannot.
**Rate** · The dome grows about 2,700 times faster than bamboo, because the speed comes from re-latticing Essence already present, not from cell division.
**Fire** · Char insulates sound wood and the water in it buys delay, so fire takes most of an hour; dried wood chars twice as fast.

### Metaphysics

**Aether** · Draws Vitalia (living biomass, warmth, blood nearby) and Materia (rock, deep foundation), so living soil over rock suits it; the room pays for the wood, and it is raised best over the wounded.
**Wellspring** · Verdantia, the Green Pulse (Vitalia): carbon fixation and indeterminate growth; the dome may be extended without limit.
**Wellspring** · Materia Primordia, the First Substance (Materia): allotropy, the same Essence re-latticed as wood outside and mineral bracing inside, which also sets the rate.
**Inherited failures** · Verdantia's etiolation: on starved ground the dome grows long, thin and unable to stand; Materia Primordia's phase locking: a heavy enough strike commits the practitioner's lattice for good.
**Category** · Domain Weaving, read as Debt: every unit converted is logged against the practitioner's own capacity to heal, with Residue beneath since the growth is the record.
**Essence** · The Attraction Layer pays, projected outward as grown matter, with a continuous bleed shed into a structure made of fuel.
**Sub-Stats** · Governed by Ardency (Density), with Vitality (Regeneration, Hemostasis).
**Path gates** · On the Fate Path with Attraction second, nothing binds at B-Grade; thickening past B meets the Body Path gates, Flourishing for Density and Splintering for Regeneration.
**School** · The Greening healers read the flow of life as a stock, raising the Veil early and holding it long; the practitioner is only the pipe, and roots take up lead and thallium through the channels meant for zinc and iron.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Renewal carries the growth law; Form carries the re-latticing.
**Boundary** · Two move: the size at which growth stops is removed, and which lattice the Essence may occupy is freed.
**Trigger** · Declared; below Refraction its anchor is a word aloud or a fixed gesture chain, and the dome starts closing at once.
**Effect** · A creaking tear from everywhere, growth over at a running pace, light going green then dim; struck, the spot thickens and darkens instead of splintering, and the raiser flinches half a beat late at each impact.
**Routing** · Every burn, break and poison it absorbs is felt in full by whoever raised it for the second before the wood catches up.
**Scope** · The practitioner and every ally within 5 m; the rim closes at 5 m/s and meets overhead in one second; while it stands, hairline Crystal fractures inside slowly close.
**Failure** · Field fault: on depleted ground it rises taller and thinner and cannot hold; also closure lag, Exuroth's drying, Nihiloth's null, phase locking and heavy-metal poison.
**Bleed** · 30 to 40 percent of the spend leaves the Shell as heat, continuously.

**Cost** · 5 percent of full reserve to raise and 2 percent each turn; the wood itself is paid by the room, the growth by attackers.
**Duration** · Four turns, 24 seconds once closed.
**Damage** · After a heavy standing the practitioner heals slower by as much as the dome took; a strike near capacity can lock the lattice for good.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · Tonnes of wood in a second and the noise it makes, green failing light; the raiser flinches late at each hit; a dome too tall and thin is a weak one; afterwards mineral seams and the smell of sap.
**Limits** · Nothing already past 5 m or striking in the first second; no point loads; no ground at sea or on scaffolds.
**Fails against** · A strike in the closing second; a lost anchor; drying by Exuroth; Nihiloth, Absolution's flames or Tenebra's decay; starved or poisoned ground; harm to the raiser through the dome. A second hit on one spot does nothing.

# Vorynn Execution Strike

## Vorynn Execution Strike

---

### Summary card

**Effect** · The strike does not need to be hard to be final. Contact alone collapses the target's momentum on the spot. Muscle chains lock, kinetic defence goes rigid, and the follow-through does the rest.
**Cost** · **Every use tears fibre along the ignition path.** *Chained further, the tear compounds; his own count puts the Essence toll near a fifth of what he is carrying per use.*
**Limit** · **Requires Bloodbind ignition.** *Without the blood already burning, there is no vector to compress and no strike to throw.*
**Counter** · **Heat returned faster than Vohrin can pull it: a Pyreveil** *or Exuroth ward breaks the lock the same way flame breaks Vohrin's own Stillwater Rite, by forcing thermal flux back into a field built to hold none.*
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the freeze fails first at the joint or first at the Crystal. Draven has never let it run long enough on himself to find out, and nobody has caught him at it on someone who could tell him afterward.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · A contact heat sink: a touch pulls heat from the outer half-millimetre of skin, blocking shallow nerves and locking the muscle rigid, because cold slows letting go far more than tightening (cold contracture).
**Law** · Fourier conduction at a boundary the practitioner sets; the cooling front only reaches L ≈ √(αt), so pulling harder freezes the surface, never deeper.
**Limit** · The cold is small: a fist-sized touch freezes about 5 g, 1.7 kJ and never more than about 20 kJ, skin-deep frostbite and a shallow nerve block; the yield belongs to the follow-through.
**Compliance** · A defender stops a blow by giving, F = E/d; a locked limb cannot give, so the same follow-through lands two to four times harder for no extra energy.
**Nerve and muscle** · Nerve conduction falls about 1.5 to 2 m/s per degree and fails below 7 to 10 °C; the calcium pump that lets muscle relax halves its rate every 7 to 10 degrees (Q₁₀ ≈ 2.5 to 3).

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Boundary sets the thermal condition; Lock, a Counter form, occupies the limit so the defender cannot set it himself.
**Boundary** · Where the thermal gradient at the point of contact may sit; one end moves and Fourier's law does the rest.
**Trigger** · The blood-pact ignition already burning, a close under Tenebra's cover, and a strike that lands; below Refraction the anchor is the fist, and a lapse of attention ends it.
**Effect** · A touch that is not a blow, then the limb stops arguing: it stays where it was going and will not come back; frost and a white patch on the skin, a cold not felt because the nerve is blocked; in a practitioner the pathway goes hard and quiet.
**Failure** · Structural fault: the working succeeds and the practitioner's fibre tears, compounding when chained; clothing, a heat ward, saturated cold, fouling or no contact each deny it.
**Bleed** · 45 percent of the spend and all the heat taken leave the Shell as warmth, so the air round him warms and the room with it.
**Body** · Over-reliance on the blood-pact ignition can trigger an uncontrolled Prime Beast change with permanent loss of sapience.

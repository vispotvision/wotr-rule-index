# World Echelon

## World Echelon

### Summary card

**Effect · The practitioner merges** his personal Tribunal Domain with the local Spirit World across a five-kilometre radius for roughly forty seconds, folding the space into court geometry and converting every emotion caught inside into weighed evidence.
**Cost** · High soul-heating and a local Principle drift that outlasts the working itself.
**Limit** · Cannot be run alongside Dirge Ascension; the two Domains do not coexist.
**Counter** · A Sovereign-tier Spirit Path practitioner, or a transcendent-grade Harmonics Empathy field (Catharsis- or Benediction-class), can refuse to be weighed at all.
**What nobody knows · Its author is a rough-seated** Spirit-Fate practitioner, the kind the Guild Accord caps at Tier Four, Adept, on paper. Whether he has ever actually been assayed for a certificate that would say so, or whether the courts he presides over simply never asked, is not on record.

### Physics

**Phenomenon** · Phase synchronisation in coupled oscillators: every mind within five kilometres is pulled to keep one common time, like pendulum clocks on one beam falling into step, so ordinary private feeling becomes a measurable deviation from a shared reference.
**Law** · The Kuramoto model: coherence r stays at zero until coupling clears Kc = 2 / (π g(0)), then rises toward 1; coupling is symmetric, so every node pulls on every other.
**Limit** · It sets only phase, never amplitude or content, so it is cheap; but a divided crowd may never lock, there is no partial court, and near the threshold release takes unboundedly long (critical slowing down).
**Everyone** · A coupled node is coupled, and the practitioner is a node: the court synchronises his own people and himself.

### Mechanism

**Glyph** · Order limits how much variance a field may carry, Vastness lays it across five kilometres, Measure does the weighing.
**Boundary** · One scalar moves: the coupling strength between every Essence-bearing oscillator within 5,000 m.
**Trigger** · The merger of the Tribunal Domain with the local Spirit World declared aloud; at Zenith no anchor is needed.
**Effect** · Nothing strikes; feeling keeps its content but loses its timing, breathing falls in, the crowd moves as one, and inner life becomes a quantity read against a reference nobody chose.
**Blind ends** · Those at the court's own phase register nothing; those far off never couple and stand out; those between are the evidence.
**Exclusion** · Cannot run with Dirge Ascension: two boundary conditions on one field, and a field takes one.
**Failure** · Boundary fault: one coupling constant laid without discrimination on a sphere synchronises everyone, friend and practitioner alike.
**Bleed** · Below η 1.0 up to 5 percent of the spend leaves the Shell as heat, the soul-heating the working is known for; above 1.0 the environment co-authors the court's reference phase.

**Duration** · Eight turns, 48 seconds; the district then keeps the court's time at least as long as the lock took to form, longer near threshold.
**Drift** · The place pays after: its Harmonists hear the court's reference under everything and read it poorly, and nobody inside can tell their mood from the court's.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · A declaration aloud, then a district breathing together; afterwards a crowd moving as one too long and Harmonists gone deaf to all but the court's beat.
**Limits** · No partial court, no lock over a divided crowd, nothing past 5 km, nothing beyond eight turns, never alongside Dirge Ascension.
**Fails against** · Silence; leather, clay, fog and shutters; a divided crowd; detuning past the locking range; a phase sitting on the court's own; Knot and Lock on its one limit; a Sovereign Spirit Domain or a Catharsis or Benediction Empathy field; a read seam; the end of eight turns; harm to the practitioner, a node in his own court.
**Look up** · The Fulguria favour list; the Judicium Failure and the Heresiology's injury entries; Silence, Knot and Lock in the Counterplay register; the saturation rule; the Zenith Stage table.

# Shot and the Enhanced Weapon

### Part One · Plain Shot Against a Practitioner

**The reference gun.** A period revolver in .44, unenhanced: no Essence in the gun, the round or the shooter's working. A lead ball of about 16 g leaves the muzzle at about 235 m/s carrying about 440 J, and crosses ten metres in about 43 ms. That is E-Grade output. A D-Grade practitioner's own strike carries far more. **The gun is not dangerous because it is strong. It is dangerous because it is fast, cheap and needs no reserve.**
**The Grade that decides is Speed, not rank.** Dodging is read off the target's Speed Grade (the Dexterity Sub-Stats that feed it, chiefly Celerity, Reflex and Sequence), and surviving a hit off their Resilience Grade. The two are checked separately.
| Speed Grade | Travel | Reaction | Against a 235 m/s ball |
|---|---|---|---|
| E | 60–150 m/s | 40–80 ms | Does not register it in time. |
| D | 150–340 m/s | 15–40 ms | Registers it and may begin to turn. It hits. |
| C | 340–500 m/s | 5–15 ms | Dodges. The first Grade whose floor outruns the ball. |
| B | 510–1,372 m/s | 1–5 ms | Dodges with room to spare, and can catch it. |

**Against a faster round,** a musket ball or anything at 400 m/s and up, a C-Grade is back to reacting without outrunning it, and B-Grade is where dodging is reliable.
**Catching a projectile needs a Speed Grade whose Travel floor is at least twice the projectile's velocity.** Outrunning a ball is not the same as closing a hand on a centimetre-wide object at that speed. Against the reference revolver that is B-Grade; against a musket ball, B-Grade at its upper end and A-Grade reliably. A technique built on Alacrity or Celerity can bring a lower Grade there early, at the overchannel risk Part Six already names.
**Being hit.** No Grade makes a body proof against plain shot by itself. A practitioner struck takes it as a hard human body does: the head, the heart or a major vessel can kill, and a limb is a serious wound. What changes that is Resilience, proofed plate, a warding technique, or a relic built for it.

### Part Two · The Ceiling on Enhanced Weapons

**An enhanced weapon performs no higher than the Grade of whoever put the Essence into it.** Channelled through the weapon in the moment, that is the wielder's Grade. Enchanted, glyphed or alchemically worked beforehand, it is the maker's Grade at the time of making, and a relic keeps its maker's ceiling in any hand that carries it. A D-Grade smith's enchanted blade hits no harder than a D-Grade can.
**An enhancement moves one or both of two dials; the weapon underneath does not change.** The first is **speed**: only an enhancement built to drive the projectile faster moves its velocity, and it tops out at the enhancer's **Attack Speed**, because a loosed projectile is a working's execution, not the body moving. The second is **the hit**: hardness, density, or a working that goes off on contact, without changing the speed, topping out at the enhancer's **Attack output** band.
| Enhancer's Grade | Attack Speed | Fastest 16 g ball | Energy at that speed |
|---|---|---|---|
| none | — | ~235 m/s | ~440 J |
| D | Mach 0.5–1.0 | ~340 m/s | ~920 J |
| C | Mach 1.0–2.0 | ~680 m/s | ~3.7 kJ |
| B | Mach 2.0–5.0 | ~1,700 m/s | ~23 kJ |

A faster ball hits harder on its own; the hit dial adds on top, within the output band. **An enhanced round is dodged at its actual velocity, not at its shooter's Grade.**
**An enhanced round or arrow is paid for per shot and consumed when fired,** from the reserve of whoever enhanced it: the wielder if channelled, the maker if prepared in advance. Shot is chemically cheap and a working is not, and an enhanced volley is a practitioner's reserve spent in advance, not a free upgrade to a line of muskets. A prepared enhancement also carries its maker's Duration, so a stockpile goes stale.
**Every enhancement is findable.** An enchanted weapon radiates its working's signature, and a Harmonist reads its alignment, Duration, Boundary limits and integrity without touching it. A speed-enhanced round is answered by Speed or by a Harmonist; a hit-enhanced round by proofed plate, Resilience or a ward.

# The Item Tiers

### What an Item Tier Is

**An item carries an item Tier, Plain through Proscribed: the lower of what its material can hold and the Tier of Standing its maker stood at when it was made.** A Journeyman's blade is at most Tempered, holding a C-Grade working and no more. A master cannot put an A-Grade working into Greystone: the stone fails first.
A composite item is graded by the material that carries the working, not the handle or the fittings. A plain item with no working in it has no Tier at all; it performs as its physics.

### The Nine Tiers

**Material tier n holds a working up to item Tier n, level with Tier of Standing n.** Each Tier is the word stamped on the Accord's mark, in Accord Latin beside the old *Probatum est*, and said in plain English at the table and the counter.
| Item Tier | Stamp | Said as | Grade it holds | Material tier | Example materials |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| — | none | Plain | Hollow–F | T0 Fragile | Mireclay, Glassroot Fibers, Dustquartz |
| 1 | *Signatum* | Marked | E | T1 Soft | Vellun Copper, Willowblood Sap, Sunreed Paper |
| 2 | *Probatum* | Proofed | D | T2 Common Durable | Greystone, Shieldgneiss, Stillband Ore |
| 3 | *Temperatum* | Tempered | C | T3 Hardened | Sunkwood, Blackcap Sulphide, Ghoststeel |
| 4 | *Instrumentum* | Instrument | B | T4 Reinforced Magical | Clearbeam Beryl, Psionite, Runeglass |
| 5 | *Consecratum* | Hallowed | A | T5 Rare / Spirit-Forged | Aethersteel, Bloodsilver, Twilite Glass |
| 6 | *Insigne* | Storied | S–SS | T6 Arcane-Resistant | Soulquartz, Frostwool, Grimbarite |
| 7 | *Inventum* | Found | SS–SSS | T7 Titan-Forged | Titanstone, Drakehide Steel |
| 8 | *Numinosum* | Numinous | X–EX | T8 Mythic / Celestial | Eclipsium, Sunsinew, Moonblight Ore |
| 9 | *Interdictum* | Proscribed | EX+ | T9 Transcendent / Forbidden | Nullglass, Ash of Names |

Where a Tier spans two Grades (Storied, Found, Numinous), the upper Grade needs a Class IV or V provenance on the Standing Index, or a maker at the top of their own Tier.

### What the Tier Caps, by Kind

| Kind | Its Tier caps | Figure read off |
|---|---|---|
| Weapon (melee) | the hit a working adds | Attack output band |
| Projectile, round, arrow | speed, and the hit | Attack Speed and Attack output |
| Armour, ward, shield | what it stops: a strike at or below its Tier's Grade | Attack output band, stamped as the proof-mark |
| Focus, staff, instrument | the working it can channel without failing | the channeller's Grade, capped at the item's |
| Relic with a standing effect | the effect's strength | the table the effect uses |

**Armour's Tier is its proof.** Proofed plate stops a strike in the D output band and below. The mark states the Tier, the house that tested it and the test. **A strike one Tier above the proof breaks it; a strike two Tiers above passes as if it were not there.**

### What an Item Costs to Make

Costs are drawn from the maker's reserve (1 EU = 1 MJ):
| Item | Cost to make | Lasts |
|---|---|---|
| Enhanced round or arrow | 1% of the maker's reserve each, whatever the energy added, with a floor of 0.001 EU | the maker's Duration; consumed when fired |
| Enhanced weapon (standing working) | 10% of the maker's reserve, once | until the working is broken, read or unmade |
| Proofed armour | 25% of the maker's reserve, once | until struck by a strike two Tiers above its proof |
| Relic | the maker's whole reserve, once, and a spell of Essence Starvation after | permanent while its material holds |

**A practitioner can equip themselves but cannot equip an army.** An Apprentice maker (Stage II, reserve about 0.56 EU) can prepare about ninety enhanced rounds from a full reserve before Essence Starvation, or nine enchanted blades, or three suits of proofed armour, or one relic.

### On a Card

**Name** · *Kind, Tier-name (Grade X), material Tn.* One line of what it does. For example: **Slyvine's Scarf** · *Relic, Proofed (Grade D), T2 or above.* Can stretch, tighten, or harden to act as a rope or a weak shield.

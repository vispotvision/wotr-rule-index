# The Old World — The Western Wearing

*The Western Wearing*
> **The root of a mountain belt that no longer exists.** The most geologically generous ground in the four quarters, and the most thoroughly emptied.

---

## Formation

Two continental masses collided, threw up a range on the scale of anything now standing, and then spent an unimaginable stretch of time being worn back down to the stumps. What is left at the surface is **the deep interior of that range brought up into daylight**: slate belts and mica schist folded like cloth, with granite bodies intruded up into them from below.
Those granite bodies are the point. As each one cooled it sweated out a hot mineralising fluid into the cracks in its own roof, and that fluid deposited **tin and tungsten** in sheeted veins around the granite cupolas. Further out, cooler, the same fluids left **lead and silver** in the limestone. Along the seaward foreland, in the basins that formed while the range was still rising, plant matter accumulated and became **coal**. Along the coastal plain, buried salt has risen through the overburden in domes, dragging up sulphur and oil-shale with it.
It has been worked continuously for longer than any current kingdom has existed.

---

## The Ground Itself

Worked country. Terraced hillsides, coppiced woodland cut on rotation for eleven hundred years, hedgerows on field boundaries older than the treaties that name them. Spoil heaps stand at the head of every valley, **grassed over on the outside and still poisonous within.** In the tin districts the ground subsides without warning over workings nobody recorded, and in the coal foreland it subsides on a schedule everyone has learned to plan around.
The rivers run orange below the old adits and have done for generations. Nothing lives in them above the tidal reach. The harbours silt, because eleven centuries of mining spoil has to go somewhere and it goes downstream.
> Ambient Wellspring saturation is low to moderate and **locally depleted around the old districts**, which is the western quarter's defining industrial problem and the reason its arrays are so expensive to run.

## Bearing Stock

**Forge-Iron** · *T2 · Metal · Altherion*
The standard armament and structural iron of the western kingdoms. Durable against blades, poor Essence retention, made in quantity and to a specification that has not meaningfully changed in three hundred years. Bearing good, Holding poor.
> **Slagbloom** · *T2 · Metal · Recovered from ancient spoil, western foreland and tin districts*
>
> The signature material of the quarter and a fair summary of its condition. Old smelting was inefficient enough that the slag heaps of dead kingdoms still assay well for iron, and modern forge-arrays are hot enough to get it out.
>
> Slagbloom is cheap, sulphurous, brittle in the cold, and carries a persistent oddity: **inscriptions laid on it read doubled**, because the metal was worked and marked once already and something of the earlier marking survives the second melt. Engravers charge extra to work it. Superstitious foremen will not have it in a mine cage.
>
> Bearing moderate and inconsistent, Holding erratic. Resonance: Anamnesis, unwelcome. Family Materia, Domain Material Science.
**Palegrit Limestone** · *T2 · Stone · Platform carbonate across the central west*
Ashlar for building, lime for mortar, and flux for every iron furnace in the quarter. Three uses, one quarry, and the reason the western kingdoms build in pale stone while the north builds in grey. Karst country: the same rock dissolves, which gives the west its cave systems, its sinkholes, and its habit of losing rivers underground for leagues at a time. Bearing good, Holding poor.
**Ironwood** · *T3 · Wood · Deep forest of Eresse*
Hardened to the equivalent of tempered steel, prized by the elves for ceremonial bows and shamanic staves, and cut under restrictions that make it **functionally unavailable to industry.**
**Veinwood Plank** · *T2 · Wood · Mountain trees of Varūn*
Semi-hardened grain, briefly enchantable, standard stock for ritual shields.
**Kirkyard Lead** · *T2 · Metal · Galena in the limestone belt, worked with the silver*
Roofing, coffin lining, pipe, shot. Industrially it matters for one reason: **lead sheet attenuates Essence more effectively per unit thickness than anything else in common supply**, so every assay house and every array isolation room in the west is lead-lined. *The men who smelt it do not last.* Bearing soft, Holding nil, shielding excellent.

## Holding Stock

> **Blacktooth Wolfram** · *T3 · Metal ore · Sheeted veins in the granite cupolas, western tin districts*
>
> Wolframite, black, absurdly heavy, and possessed of the highest melting point of any workable metal in the ledger. **This is the vessel problem's answer.** Forge-arrays can reach temperatures that will slump any ordinary crucible, and wolfram will not slump. Every nozzle, every hearth crown, every array-furnace lining above T4 work is wolfram or is lined with something wolfram holds.
>
> It has no glamour, no legend, and **no substitute**, and the Accord tracks every ton of it. Bearing extreme under heat, Holding low. Resonance: Ignivale, Exuroth. Family Caloria, Domain Thermodynamics.
**Greaves Tin** · *T2 · Metal · Cassiterite, lode and stream-work, western tin districts*
Bronze, solder, and the tinning of array contacts. Contact corrosion in a working glyph array is a slow, invisible, cumulative failure, and a tinned contact does not corrode. **Unglamorous, essential, and running out.** The stream works were exhausted six generations ago and the lodes are now worked at depths that only exist because of the pumps. Bearing moderate, Holding moderate. Resonance: Coagulatio. Family Materia, Domain Material Science.
**Attercrop Vitriol** · *T2 · Liquid reagent · Drainage of abandoned workings, universal in the west*
Green vitriol, drawn off where pyrite in old spoil weathers into acid water. It is the poison in the orange rivers and it is also, refined, the mordant that fixes ritual ink to any organic substrate. **The same fluid that killed the Aerne valley writes the Accord's own codices.** Western alchemists have stopped remarking on this. Resonance: Dissolution. Family Materia, Domain Material Science. Blinds on contact with the eye and does so without warning, since it does not burn on the skin at working dilution.
**Glacium** · *T5 · Frost-infused mineral · Iampu, grown in the ice-caves under Winter Court licence*
Not quarried in any ordinary sense. **Grown**, slowly, in caverns held below freezing for the purpose, in a process the Winter Court does not document and does not permit inspection of. Frost-bound weaponry and the frost-infused stock that supplements Iampu's gold in the mercenary trade. Bearing brittle, Holding high and narrow. Resonance: Crymorath. Family Caloria, Domain Thermodynamics.
**Psionite** · *T4 · Mineral · Varūn deep workings*
Mental fortification stock, worked only by psionically attuned Varūn dwarves under the Psionic Mastery Code. Effectively unavailable outside the kingdom.
**Vellun Copper** · *T1 · Metal · Source hills unlocated*
Malleable, a good spiritual conductor, and melts far too easily to be useful past apprentice work. **The trade name is retained because the shipments are continuous and the assay is consistent. The hills it is named for do not appear on any survey the Accord has commissioned.**

## The Works

Deep, mature, capital-heavy, and **running on borrowed ground.** Western shafts are the deepest in the world and every one of them is a standing bill: the pumps must run whether the mine is producing or not, and the pumps run on Essence the quarter no longer generates in sufficient quantity locally. The great western houses buy Essence supply the way an army buys grain, and a bad year in supply closes shafts that are still full of ore.
The compensating advantage is that **everything else exists.** Roads, canals, winding houses, assay offices, guild schools, insurance, and eleven centuries of accumulated practice about how to keep men alive underground. A western pit does more work per man than any pit in the other three quarters and it does it in a hole that should have drowned a hundred years ago.
Reworking is the growth industry. Slag heaps, old tailings, flooded levels pumped out and picked over. *There is a whole class of western operator who has never sunk an original shaft and never intends to.*

---

## What It Costs

Firedamp in the coal foreland, which the draught arrays manage until they do not. Subsidence in the tin districts. Vitriol drainage everywhere. Silicosis in the slate belts, which the trade calls **the grinder's cough** and which is simply what happens to men who breathe rock dust for twenty years.
> Above all, the array dependency. The western quarter has arranged its entire industrial base so that a sustained Essence shortage becomes, **within about ninety hours, a mass drowning.**

---

## Trade Posture

Exports finished goods, worked metal, wolfram, tin, expertise, and credit. Imports raw ore from all three other quarters, phosphate from the south, and food. Holds the debt of the north and much of the east.
> The western position is not built on what the west has in the ground. It is built on being **the only quarter that can turn anyone else's ground into product**, and the western houses are entirely aware that this is a service, not a possession.

## The Continent Above the Ledger

> ⚠️ **Unreconciled. Do not write from either account until Isaac rules.**
>
> Everything above this line is the Measurewrights' survey: a worn collisional belt of granite cupolas, coal foreland and karst, with coppiced woodland, orange acid rivers, and materials sourced from **Eresse, Varūn, Iampu and Altherion** — elven and dwarven kingdoms.
>
> Everything below is *The Geography of the Old World* and *The Eight Great Houses of the Old World*: an ancient Aether-dense supercontinent of desert, rainforest, savanna and rift, ruled from **Kushara** by eight houses, containing no elves or dwarves at all.
>

### Continental orientation

**Classification:** Ancient Aether-Dense Supercontinent · **Epithet:** *The Land That Remembers Weight*
**Primary sovereign power:** the territories unified beneath Dawn Guard
The broad shape of a broken shield. Ancient even by the standards of the Continuum: its mountains have been eroded, raised and broken several times over, its rivers run through the ruins of civilisations whose names did not survive, and Wellspring pressure has worked the geology deeply enough that stone, metal, river and forest sometimes carry recognisable spiritual memory.
- **North** — the largest deserts, salt basins, sandstone kingdoms, buried cities and volcanic glass fields. Immense aquifers and mineral seams beneath the dunes, left by ancient inland seas.
- **West** — warm, humid, heavily forested. Rainforest descending toward dark estuaries, mangrove deltas, cliff-lined coasts and tropical islands.
- **Centre** — savanna, wooded grassland, broad rivers, seasonal wetland, and the sovereign heartlands surrounding Kushara. The continent's largest migrations and many of its oldest continuously inhabited cities.
- **East** — a massive rift system, volcanic mountains, deep lakes, elevated grassland and isolated highland kingdoms. Geologically unstable, extraordinarily rich in metals, gemstones and geothermal Aether.
- **South** — rolling mineral plateaus, temperate forest, red grassland, warm gulf coasts and the storm-beaten cape. Some of the greatest ports on the continent.

### Kushara

The city rises across natural plateaus over the **River Kesh**, districts joined by broad avenues, terraced gardens, bridges, stairways and fortified gates. The oldest districts are carved partly into the plateau itself; newer neighbourhoods run out into wooded hills and river plain.
**The Golden Steps**, the processional stair down from the sovereign district to the old river markets, with a grass slope beside it that children race on. **The River Gardens**, shallow waterways and flowering arches and small painted boats, candlelit at festival and carrying messages downstream. **The Grand Court**, the largest Zettari training ground in the city, adults in the central court and children in the enclosures around it. **The High Gardens**, terraces on the upper walls holding ancient trees, fountains and elevated pools. **The Ancestral Halls**, stone sanctuaries preserving family cords, weapons, Zettari patterns, witness tablets, royal oaths and the records of the dead. **The Bridge of Evening Voices**, where musicians gather at sunset without arrangement and sometimes play until morning. **The Market of Twelve Roads**, where goods from every region of the continent arrive.

### Mountain systems

**The Broken Spine** — volcanic chain following the Akar Rift. Obsidian, diamond, geothermal crystal, deep Wellspring pressure.
**The Orunthal Crown** — high northeastern mountains feeding many of the continent's largest rivers.
**The Golden Back** — eroded chain west of Kushara. Gold, iron and ancestral ruins in its lower valleys.
**The Southern Teeth** — sharp mountains dividing the Auric Plateau from the Cape.
**The Cloudwall** — western system that traps ocean air and feeds the Nyoka Rainsea.

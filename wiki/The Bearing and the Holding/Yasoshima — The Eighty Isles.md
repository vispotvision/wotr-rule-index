# Yasoshima — The Eighty Isles

*The Sea-Space. Islands of the Yuno.*
> **Vā** is the relational space the house exists to keep. **Moana** is the deep ocean. *An aiga that names its own islands after the relation they hold rather than after a founder is behaving exactly as the house thesis says it should.*

---

## Formation

A drowned fragment of the same collisional belt that became the Old World, **which never rose high enough to join the mainland** and spent the intervening age being capped in limestone.
What stands above the waterline is **raised coral platform over karst.** Low, green, riddled with caves, **holding fresh water in lenses that sit a few feet above the salt and go bad the moment anyone digs too deep.**

---

## Bearing Stock

> **Effectively none.** No metal, no coal, no timber worth a hull. Lime, shell, coral rag, pandanus, and fish.
>
> **This is the whole of the reason the islands have never been conquered.** There has never been anything on them worth the crossing — **and a house that cannot be invaded profitably has been free to spend two hundred thousand years perfecting ceremony instead of fortification.**

---

## Ambient Essence

Moderate, and by every Measurewright reading ever taken, **the most stable saturation recorded anywhere in the four quarters. Nothing drifts at Yasoshima.**
This is why Yuno rites hold across generations without correction, why the house has never built an array, **and why the Accord's Research Division has filed four separate requests to establish a station there and been declined four times without explanation.**

---

## Governance

**Not a kingdom.** A confederation of aiga governed by **fono** — no king, no standing army, and no charter with the Guild Accord.
> The five branches are **islands before they are branches**, and the **Manono** seat sits in the strait between the two largest, **which is why the rarest branch is also the one every other branch must sail past.**
> Kharven's marriage to the house is **the only formal tie Yasoshima holds to any mainland power.** The fono ratified it as a **feagaiga between houses**, which is not the same thing as an alliance between states, **and which the north has never fully understood.**
>
> **Kharven believes it gained an ally. The Yuno believe they placed an obligation.**
>
> *Both are correct and neither has said so out loud.*

---

## The Queen

> **Ruling in effect.** The Queen of Kharven is **Filemu Yuno. Not Ayame. Not Yuno.** *Ayame was a Japonic given name sitting on a Samoan surname, and the card also carried the wrong bloodline entirely.*
| Name | Standing |
|---|---|
| **Personal name** *Filemu* | Peace, quiet, calm. Conferred at birth by the mother's family. **Samoan register: strict CV syllables, penultimate stress, no clusters** |
| **Aiga** *Yuno* | **Manono Branch** — the rarest line, holder of the grand seasonal rites and the deepest reaches of Tofā Mamao, **and the only branch trained from childhood to expect that its permission to wear the tuiga will be resented** |
| **Matai title** *Tausi o le Vā Atoa* | Keeper of the Whole Vā. Already canon as a Manono Branch title **and a literal description of the White Concord** |
| **Ava-name** *struck (C-002, ruled 2026-09-12: the Agamalu addition's strike reaches her own slot)* | Kharven's records call her Queen. **The Yuno gafa records her as the debt the house placed in the north** — *and the discrepancy between the two names is the political fact* |

> *Yuno means gentle. It is a warning wearing a soft word. A woman named Peace who receives thirty hostile clans alone in a courtyard and has them rethreaded into three coalitions by dawn is that thesis walking around.*

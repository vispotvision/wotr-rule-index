# The Five Beastkin Lineages

*Soul architecture, stat frameworks, metaphysical identity, culture, and historical record for the five documented major lineages.*

---

## Feline — Catkin

*Shadow-Leap Stalkers · Emotive Kinetics*
Descended from the ancient **Clawbound Accord** formed during the Watcher's Eclipse. When the celestial lattice first fractured, spirit-leopards and desert nomads fused beneath a period of absolute darkness, **trading permanent physical stability for a soul-lattice optimized entirely for reflex and shadow-walking.** They are built for the moment between moments.
**Spiritual lineage** · Nyzara, the Silent Pad. Archonic guardian of foresight and emotional poise — inheriting her mastery of the blink-space between physical actions and her ability to **weaponize stillness before movement.**

### Soul architecture

**Essence Typology** · Emotive (Reflex / Shadow), Fulmen undertone for kinetic release. Their souls function as **Kinetic Reservoirs**: raw emotion stored in muscle memory, released as Aetheric force through physical acceleration. **They do not channel Aether deliberately the way most practitioners do. They embody it, and then they move.**
**Soul Path** · Body 62% / Spirit 38%. *Their Aura is not spatial authority — it is the absence of spatial presence, the way a room changes when something very fast has just passed through it.*
**Aether Class** · I through IV standard; V Radiant for senior shadow-walkers with sustained Somnalis resonance. **Their class tends to read lower than their practical capability** because burst, precise, contained output does not saturate ambient Aether the way volume-heavy practitioners do.
> **Primary fracture point at Stage V Splintering.** The emotional reservoir architecture resists the soul cracking open that the Stage requires. Catkin who pass Stage V cleanly **tend to pass it violently**, and emerge with elevated Domain formation instincts they weren't expecting.
>
> **Stage VII Refraction stall** — the Stage requires the soul to perceive itself from outside, which the emotional architecture resists. Catkin at Stage VII frequently mistake the external self-perception for external threat and respond accordingly.
>
> **Dissonance expression** — the emotional reservoir detonates. A single overwhelming release of accumulated stored emotion that either transforms the practitioner or dissolves them. *Survivors describe it as finally feeling something they had been storing for years, all at once.*

### Stat framework

| Stat | Mod | Expression |
|---|---|---|
| Vitality | −15 | **They are not built to absorb force — they are built to not be where the force lands.** Metabolic Burn: cannot sustain high Ardency output without caloric cost |
| Ardency | +15 | Emotion-linked kinetic burst. **Not sustained output — spike output.** Stored emotion released as force in single precise moments |
| Gnosis | −20 | Linear logic structurally suppressed. **Perception and Acuity unaffected** — the suppression is in building logical chains from what they perceive, not in perceiving it |
| **Dexterity** | **+25** | **Definitive stat.** Shadow-walking is Dexterity at its purest — the ability to be not-there when the attack arrives and there when the response does |
| Harmonics | +15 | Emotional resonance and shadow-frequency attunement. **Chord suppressed** — communal harmonics are not their architecture |
| Resilience | −10 | Fragile under sustained pressure. Designed for short violent exchanges, not attrition |
| Tempering | +10 | Clean kinetic routing. They do not overchannel; the reservoir architecture naturally regulates expenditure |
| Dominion | +10 | Shadow-space territory assertion. Sovereignty in darkness contexts above baseline |

**Sub-stat notes** · Celerity and Burst are definitive — the highest single-movement speed of any beastkin lineage. Ardency Alacrity elevated: **the emotional reservoir fires faster than deliberate Ardency routing.** Resilience Nullity above average — *take away the Aether and they still have claws.* Harmonics Chord consistently floors. **They are not a team architecture. They are a precision instrument.** Gnosis Sapience flooring does not suppress tactical intelligence: they make the right call through pattern recognition rather than reasoning, and they are usually faster.

### Metaphysical identity

**Wellsprings** · **Somnalis** primary — shadow-walking is a Somnalis expression made physical; the blink-space between moments is Somnalis territory. **Tenebra** strong secondary. **Ignivale** accessible — the moment of emotional ignition that converts reservoir to output. **Aurevane and all Solar/Celestial suppressed**, −30% all stats in Radiant Domains: *their architecture is built around the space between light sources, not light itself.*
**Glyphs** · Strong: `Wy` Motion, `Fi` Passion, `El` Illusion, `Sa` Silence. **Rejected:** `Ko` Order, `Ky` Structure — these require the linear Gnosis chains the architecture suppresses, and contact produces feedback rather than working.
**Traits** · Awakening clusters around kinetic enhancement and shadow expression — movement blurs, after-images that persist slightly too long, **the first involuntary shadow-step**, appearing two feet from where the practitioner was standing with no visible transit. Ascension moves through speed → shadow-embodiment → absence. *At high stages, senior practitioners do not walk through shadow — they become temporarily indistinguishable from it.*
**Overgrowth risk** · Reservoir saturation. A Catkin who stores too much Aetheric emotion without releasing it develops overflow: involuntary emotional detonations, technique misfires, Stage V risk elevated to acute.
> **Domain law.** Shadow Domains. Light deepens rather than extinguishes — every shadow in the territory has depth, **a quality of there being more room in the dark than the architecture should allow.** Entities inside experience elevated reaction-time pressure: their own Dexterity feels slightly slower, as if the Domain is running faster than they are.
>
> Governing principle: **the moment before movement as sovereign law.** Within the territory, Catkin have first-action authority over any exchange. This is not a technique. It is the Domain expressing the practitioner's nature as a physical law.

### Culture and record

**The Clawbound Trial** · A rite in which the young practitioner must shadow-walk through territory their clan elder has anchored with Tenebra-resonance. Not a test of speed — **a test of whether the practitioner can read the shadow-map the elder has written and follow it without detection.** Both emerge with a permanent bond marker in their Harmonics architecture. *The bond is always mutual; the elder accepts a burden too.*
**The Emotional Covenant** · Before significant action, Catkin formally acknowledge the reservoir they are bringing to the event. Not a spiritual ritual. **More like inventory.** The warrior stands still, catalogues what they are carrying emotionally, and decides consciously whether the reservoir is ready or should be managed first. **They are the only known culture to treat pre-combat emotional assessment as a formal discipline.**
**Settlements** · Shadowed Ridge · the Dusk Quarter of Altherion · Nightveil Hollow. *Characterized by being difficult to locate from the outside and immediately obvious to navigate once inside — built around the shadow map residents carry rather than around visible landmarks.*
**Relations** · **Lupine:** deep kinship, the oldest beastkin alliance; agility and pack-force are complementary architectures, and the kinship holds across sharply divergent cultural practice. **Humans:** complex — Catkin are among the most frequently enslaved lineages **because their physical features are considered aesthetically appealing by human buyers**, and this history shapes every cross-species interaction in ways neither party consistently acknowledges. **Hobgoblins:** professional respect; practitioners describe the pairing as *"the most uncomfortable military alliance that consistently wins engagements."*
**Historical role** · The Clawbound Accord's original function was assassination and deep infiltration — the shadow-walking architecture made them the Concord's preferred covert operatives during the Spirit War. **This history is not celebrated in current Catkin communities. It is acknowledged, the way you acknowledge something that happened and that you cannot pretend didn't.**
**Known figure** · **Saiya**, active, Scourge of Hell mercenary operations.

## Lupine — Wolfkin

*Pack-Soul Beastkin · Lunar Strikers*
Born from the **Great Hunt Pact** during the First Moonfall, when wild spirits and mortals fused beneath a blood eclipse. **They are not wolves who became people. They are the pact itself, given a body that runs on both.**
**Spiritual lineage** · Fenraal, the Lunar Stalker, whose moonbound clarity and Pack-Soul allow individual Lupine to function as a singular metaphysical organism at sufficient proximity. **They do not merely cooperate. At Pack-Soul depth, they think together.**

### Soul architecture

**Essence Typology** · Instinctual (Lunar / Primal), Viridian undertone. Their souls **oscillate with the lunar cycle**; the internal lattice is Reactive, triggering Aetheric bursts through physical reflex rather than cognitive calculation. *They feel the technique happen before they decide to use it.*
**Soul Path** · Body 60% / Spirit 40%. At depth, **the Spirit Path component is not individual but distributed across the pack as a shared architecture.**
> **Isolation Decay** produces Crystal **compression rather than fracture** — a slow fading that reads as emotional shutdown before it reads as spiritual damage. Lupine who have been isolated long enough describe feeling *"too quiet for too long."* **The recovery requires pack proximity, not treatment.**
>
> **Acute fracture risk at Stage V if isolated.** Splintering requires the soul to crack open to become larger, and the Lupine soul architecture is communal, resisting individual expansion. In isolation, Stage V tends to produce collapse rather than growth.
>
> **Stage VII stall** — the soul needs to perceive itself from outside, but **a Lupine's self is partly constituted by their pack.** Perceiving the self requires first separating the individual architecture from the shared one, which the Crystal resists.

### Stat framework

| Stat | Mod | Expression |
|---|---|---|
| Vitality | +10 | Dense physique sustained by pack-bond. **Depresses under Isolation Decay** |
| Ardency | +15 | Pack-amplified burst. **Kinpulse synchronicity amplifies output proportionally per bonded ally within range** |
| Gnosis | −20 | Linear logic suppressed. Perception elevated — Aetheric-scent tracking reads spiritual residues at 48-hour range. **They do not calculate. They know** |
| Dexterity | +20 | Explosive speed. The digitigrade posture concentrates kinetic mass at the launch point; **highest travel speed of any beastkin in pack-sprint configuration** |
| **Harmonics** | **+25** | **Definitive stat.** The entire Lupine power architecture runs through Harmonics. **In full pack formation, Chord value exceeds any individual practitioner's rating** |
| Resilience | +10 | Pack-buffering. **Isolation Decay as the structural counter** — away from pack 6+ hours, all Resilience sub-stats decline at −5% per 2-hour interval, capping at −40% |
| Tempering | −10 | Instinctual rather than refined. **They cannot overchannel by choice** — the output is reflexive. They do not waste output because they cannot plan it; they produce exactly what the moment calls for |
| Dominion | −05 | Communal rather than individually sovereign. **The exception: in Alpha-Binding formation, collective Dominion exceeds any individual member's ceiling** — the pack as a Dominion unit |

**Sub-stat notes** · Harmonics Chord in full pack formation approaches ceilings individual practitioners across all races typically cannot reach without Domain-stage development. Attunement is tuned for **48-hour spiritual residue detection** rather than ambient field reading. Pack-sprint Celerity exceeds individual peak because the Pack-Soul synchronizes gait patterns, eliminating timing variance. **Resilience Integrity degrades under isolation not because the Crystal is damaged but because the Crystal is not a closed system for Lupine** — it is designed to be partly constituted by pack-proximity.

### Metaphysical identity

**Wellsprings** · **Verdantia** primary — life as continuous communal process, pack as ecosystem. **Selunite** primary secondary — *their power expression genuinely varies with lunar phase.* **Anima Spirare** accessible through bond-formation overlap. **Somnalis** accessible at full-moon peak, for dream-pack communion during Howls of Mourning. **Order-aspected suppressed** — the reactive architecture reads structured stillness as threat rather than discipline.
**Glyphs** · Strong: `Ye` Unity (the Pack-Soul's primary phoneme), `Ka` Cycle, `Va` Moment, `Ma` Memory. **Backlash:** `Ko` Order, `Al` Edge — structure and boundary phonemes produce **the Aetheric equivalent of a wall appearing inside a field that was never designed to contain one.**
**Kinpulse** is not formally a glyph-chain system. It is emotional Aether transmission without phonemic structure. Experienced practitioners can translate Kinpulse states into approximate glyph expressions, **but the translation always loses something.**
**Overgrowth risk** · **Bloodmoon Corruption.** During Lunar Inversions the oscillating soul-lattice destabilizes and high-stage Lupine can enter Involuntary Berserk states in which complex glyph-casting fails but raw physical output spikes catastrophically.
> **Domain law.** Pack-Hunt Domains. The territory's spatial geometry becomes a hunt-field — **distance between predator and prey within the Domain is subject to Kinpulse law.** Entities trying to create separation from a pack inside the Domain find the separation collapsing; the hunt-geometry reasserts.
>
> Governing principle: **the distance you think exists between you and us is not the distance that exists.**

### Culture and record

**Kinbinding** · Pack members cut their palms and press them together under a full moon, creating a permanent Aetheric tether. **The tether is not communicative** — it does not transmit thoughts. It transmits **weight**: the sense of another soul's presence, its state, its direction. *The closest thing Lupine culture has to a sacred act, and treated as one.*
**Howls of Mourning** · A communal vocal ritual recording the life-echoes of the fallen into local ley-lines. **The pack's history is encoded in the land.** Walking through old Lupine territory, practitioners with sufficient Attunement hear faint resonances in the ground — not words, not images. **The emotional signature of lives that were lived here.**
**Alpha-Binding** · Linking individual Soul Crystals to a single Alpha's Crystal architecture, creating a collective metaphysical organism. The Alpha accepts responsibility for the pack's coherence in exchange for the pack's amplified output. **This is not leadership. It is load-bearing.**
**Settlements** · Lumis Village · Greyfang Crossing · the Shrine of Nine Howls. Positioned relative to ley-line nodes and lunar sight-lines rather than resource proximity — *the strategic logic of their positioning is not immediately legible to other races.*
**Relations** · **Elves:** deep distrust rooted in **the First Betrayal**, a historical event in which Elven Accord operatives used Lupine pack-bond architecture against them during a treaty negotiation. **The details are in the Lupine oral tradition and not in any Accord archive.** **Humans:** the most frequently hunted beastkin lineage where the Labor Provision permits *wild beastkin clearance*; their speed makes them difficult to capture, **and their Isolation Decay makes extended pursuit more effective than it should be.**
**Historical role** · **The Moonwake Accord** — Lupine packs guarded collapsing Wellsprings during the Verdant Siege, allowing the Western Realms to stabilize. They received formal recognition from the Concord. **The recognition was not accompanied by formal Accord membership. The distinction was understood.**

## Ursine — Bearkin

*Living Fortifications · Mountain-Anchored*
Formed during the **Age of Glacial Memory** when the Titan of Endurance drew mountain-spirits into mortal flesh during a confluence of Monolithion and Verdantia eruptions. **The result was not a warrior race. The result was a people who understand load-bearing.**
**Spiritual lineage** · Orruvak, the Unmoved Mountain — a being who watched civilizations build and collapse across the hillside it occupied and **never once considered that the appropriate response to either was to move.** Bearkin carry this as a constitutional truth: *some things should stay.*

### Soul architecture

**Essence Typology** · Terra primary — geological permanence, **the specific weight of things that refuse to end.** Viridian secondary. *Their souls are old-growth heavy.*
**Soul Path** · Body 70% / Spirit 30%. The Spirit component expresses through environmental attunement and the Stillness Trial discipline — **the spiritual practice of holding while everything around you fails is both a combat technique and a soul architecture.**
> **Crystal fracture pattern · Subtle-Blindness amplification.** The Crystal resists abstract and illusory architecture so fundamentally that exposure to high-tier Illusion or Dream workings produces **confusion fractures** — not physical damage but a specific Crystal disorientation.
>
> They cannot be tricked into reaching for a wall that isn't there, **because their Crystal does not process "wall that isn't there" as a possible category.**
**Progression** · Slow, thorough, through all stages. No rapid acceleration. No sudden fractures. Each transition takes roughly one and a half times the species average — **not because their Crystal is weaker but because it is denser and requires more Essence pressure to restructure.** Stall potential at Stage VI Glory, whose burning-bright expression conflicts with a constitutional preference for not broadcasting. **Dissonance expression: Geological Stall** — the Crystal does not shatter, it enters absolute stillness that can last years. *Bearkin Dissonance survivors are indistinguishable from sleep to outside observation. They are not asleep.*

### Stat framework

| Stat | Mod | Expression |
|---|---|---|
| **Vitality** | **+30** | **Dominant stat.** The most physically durable beastkin lineage. **They take hits that would end engagements for other practitioners and continue. They do this without tracking it** |
| Ardency | +20 | Raw rather than refined. **Their output is not precise — it is massive.** When a Bearkin technique lands, the surrounding architecture knows about it |
| Gnosis | −15 | Deliberate thinkers, not fast ones. **Retention elevated: what a Bearkin has learned, they have learned permanently** |
| Dexterity | −20 | **Reaction Lag** — −40% Evasion, cannot react to high-speed or airborne technique windows. **They do not dodge. They endure.** These are not equivalent responses but they produce equivalent survival outcomes at different scales |
| Harmonics | +10 | Nature attunement — not broad-range but deep. They read Verdantia, Monolithion, and Terra-aspected nodes with exceptional clarity |
| Resilience | +25 | Near-ceiling. **The thing that absorbs pressure without fracturing becomes structurally better than the thing that never had to** |
| Tempering | −10 | Overwhelming but not precise. Overflow elevated through constitutionally large capacity. **Output lands, but not always exactly where intended** |
| Dominion | +10 | Territory sovereignty through Weight-Binding. **Their Domain roots into local geology and persists through their absence** with greater stability than other lineage Domains |

**Sub-stat notes** · Fortitude and Ossification are definitive — the Siege of the Seventh Fold's three-day hold is the canonical demonstration. **Resilience Hardening elevates through sustained pressure producing Crystal densification: Bearkin who have been under pressure longer are harder, not weaker.** Ardency Detonation expresses as *a patience finally exhausted, a mountain deciding the valley below it has needed rearranging.* Dexterity Evasion floors consistently — **the design intent is not to avoid damage. The design intent is to make damage insufficient.**

### Metaphysical identity

**Wellsprings** · **Monolithion** primary, native. **Verdantia** strong secondary — the living system's version of the mountain's permanence. **Basilithe** accessible. **Dream, Illusion, and Subtle-tier rejected** — Subtle-Blindness; contact produces confusion fractures.
**Glyphs** · Strong: `Ir` Endurance, `Tar` Weight, `Ka` Cycle, `Ky` Structure. **Their glyph-chains are the most materially stable in any beastkin tradition** — `Ir`-`Tar`-`Ky` chains persist without maintenance for decades. **The Bearkin are the only beastkin lineage whose inscribed workings outlast the practitioner who made them without Aetheric renewal.** Backlash: `El` Illusion, `No` Negation — *their soul is too grounded for negation.*
**Overgrowth risk** · **Weight-Lock.** Over-investment in Vitality and Resilience Traits can develop Crystal density so extreme that Aetheric flexibility is suppressed — **capable of surviving almost anything and unable to do almost anything.**
> **Domain law.** Earthen Domains. The territory becomes materially denser — not visually, but physically and Aetherically. **Objects weigh more. Force requirements increase.** High-speed techniques require more Essence inside the Domain than outside it.
>
> Governing principle: **mass as sovereign law. Things should stay where they are.** Kinetic disruption costs more. Defensive endurance costs less.

### Culture and record

**Stillness Trials** · Meditative duels where two Bearkin stand within a collapsing Domain or under sustained pressure; the first to break their stance loses. **Not about physical endurance** — about whether the practitioner can hold their Crystal's architecture coherent while everything around them is telling the Crystal that change is warranted. *The winners are not always the strongest Bearkin. They are always the most settled ones.*
**Weight-Binding** · Tethering the soul to a specific mountain or glade, creating a permanent Aetheric anchor. Weight-Bound Bearkin draw Essence from bonded territory within range — **and develop responsibility for that territory's Aetheric health. What the mountain feels, they feel.** *They do not describe this as a burden.*
**Settlements** · Korr Orvun · Glade of the Slumbering Root · Ulg-Vorakar. **Among the oldest continuously occupied locations in the world** — the same sites in many cases since the Age of Glacial Memory. *The architecture is stone and has outlasted the practitioners who built it.*
**Relations** · **Dawi:** honor-bound kinship; forge-culture and endurance-culture share enough foundational philosophy that cooperation is natural rather than negotiated. **Lupine:** protective regard — *Lupine move; Bearkin stay,* and neither lineage pretends the complementarity is accidental. **Humans:** their size reads as threatening to those who have not spent time with them. **They are the most dangerous-looking and least aggressive of the major beastkin lineages**, which creates consistent mismatches in initial encounter framing.
> **The Siege of the Seventh Fold.** Bearkin held the final line for three days while the Domain around them collapsed, allowing other races to escape. The hold required sustained Stillness Trial discipline at Stage V+ from every participant. **None survived intact.**
>
> The surviving Bearkin lineages consider this the defining event in their historical record **and the reason the Accord's treatment of them is an ongoing institutional insult.**
>
> Annually, Bearkin communities observe a three-day silence in memory. **The silence is not grief. It is recognition.**

## Fox-Spirit — Mishiro-Kin

*Spirit-Adjacent Beastkin · Aetheric Nose*
First Eon origin **that the Guild Accord's taxonomy system has never cleanly handled.** Fox-spirit beastkin predate the formal beastkin classification and sit at the border where the Fae taxonomy also has a partial claim. The Mishiro lineage has been in contested classification since the First Eon, when the earliest practitioners demonstrated **Somnalis attunement at birth that no formal harmonization ritual could account for.**
**Spiritual lineage** · Classified under beastkin taxonomy for Accord purposes. True lineage: Somnalis-adjacent Fae of unknown direct ancestor. The Mishiro family maintains that the answer to *who made your people* is **"we were always here,"** which is not a satisfying answer to archivists and is probably correct.

### Soul architecture

**Essence Typology** · Viridian primary — life as warmth, **the specific quality of choosing openness as a position.** Aqua secondary — empathic, tidal, memory of felt things. **Their souls are permeable in a way that other beastkin souls are not: they receive environmental Aether rather than just projecting into it.**
**Soul Path** · Spirit 78% / Attraction 18% / Body 4%. *This is not a combat lineage.*
> Fox-spirit Crystal fracture is rare and typically associated with **sustained olfactory overload** — exposure to extreme Aetheric event residue without adequate processing time. **The Crystal's porosity, which makes the nose function, also makes it vulnerable to saturation.** Practitioners in high-Aether environments need periodic Somnalis-integration sleep to process accumulated intake.
>
> **Stage III is the stage most affected by captivity and environmental suppression.** Fox-spirit Crystal requires conditions of reasonable safety, or the kind of danger that pushes outward. **Inward-pressing danger — captivity, sustained threat, isolation from sensory richness — stalls Stage III at the threshold.**

### Stat framework

| Stat | Mod | Expression |
|---|---|---|
| Vitality | −05 | Light frames. Filtration elevated — their body chemistry handles organic and environmental toxins efficiently |
| Ardency | −10 | Not a combat output lineage. **They are not going to out-fight things. The nose tells them where things are so other people can deal with them** |
| Gnosis | +15 | Olfactory intelligence. **They read situations with unusual accuracy because the nose provides information at a layer most practitioners cannot access** |
| Dexterity | +15 | They move without announcing themselves — **not trained stealth, the ingrained habit of a people who learned early that taking up less space was a survival technique** |
| **Harmonics** | **+35** | **ANOMALOUS.** Attunement, Memory, and Confluence at the level of a Band III practitioner **in an otherwise Band I body.** The nose predates training, captivity, circumstance, and everything else. **It is what they are before they are anything else** |
| Resilience | +05 | Modest. Crystal porosity means standard Aetheric assault is slightly less efficient — the Crystal absorbs and processes rather than resisting sharply |
| Tempering | +10 | Clean, minimal-overhead output. **They don't overchannel because they never had enough volume to develop overchannel as a habit** |
| Dominion | −05 | **Bond-based rather than territory-based sovereignty.** Anima Spirare architecture builds spatial authority around significant relationships rather than around occupied ground |

**Sub-stat notes** · Harmonics **Memory** is elevated alongside Attunement — they do not just read current Aetheric state, they read it against an archive of historical states. **A Fox-Spirit in a space where something significant happened can smell the event's residue with enough detail to reconstruct rough sequence.** And a unique architecture: **bond depth converts to Dominion Confluence**, creating spatial authority around significant others rather than around territory.

### Metaphysical identity

**Wellsprings** · **Somnalis** native — **opens without harmonization ritual.** It is their home frequency; they dream with resolution and accuracy that formal practitioners require decades of work to approach. **Anima Spirare** develops in sustained relationships — *the architecture of choosing to remain beside someone builds this Wellspring access naturally over time.* **Verdantia** accessible via lineage. **Manganthra** peripheral — changes chosen or survived leave traces.
**Glyphs** · Strong: `Ma` Memory (the nose's deepest phoneme; **every Fox-Spirit technique is** **`Ma`****-authorized**), `Zo` Echo, `Sa` Silence, `Ye` Unity. Backlash: `Uur` Disruption, `Fi` Passion — these produce metabolic spike, sharp input overload that temporarily saturates the olfactory architecture.
> Their glyph expression is **not inscriptive. It is ambient.** Fox-Spirit workings look less like formal technique and more like the world cooperating.
>
> **The practitioner does not force the glyph; they remind the environment of what it already was.**
**Traits** · Awakening brings the nose's first Aetheric layer becoming consciously distinguishable from biological scent, and **fox-fire** — a minor ambient cold-amber luminescence, palm-sized, three to five minutes at Stage III. **At Stage V, fox-fire becomes significantly less minor.** The Stage V crack-open expands what it can do, moving it from ambient light into directed Aetheric expression.
**Overgrowth risk** · Olfactory saturation in high-Aether events. Processing failures express as **temporal confusion** — the practitioner loses sequence, unsure whether they are smelling the current state of a space or its state from hours ago.
> **Domain law.** Memory Domains, rare, Stage VI+. The territory holds recent Aetheric events as accessible residue — footstep patterns persist visibly, voice-echoes hang in the air, significant emotional events leave readable impressions on surfaces. **The Domain does not judge what it records. It records everything.**
>
> Governing principle: **the present moment is transparent — its history is visible to those who know how to look.** Within the territory, deception leaves residue. **The residue is not evidence admissible in formal proceedings. It is information that cannot be unfelt.**

### Culture and record

**The Name-Keeping** · Fox-spirit beastkin place significant weight on names as **the last thing that cannot be physically removed.** The practice of speaking one's name aloud in the presence of a witness when circumstances have taken everything else is documented in Mishiro oral tradition as **the act of remaining.**
**The Waiting** · Fox-spirit patience is not passive. It is the accumulated practice of a people who have learned to hold their position and their perception long enough for the world to reveal what it actually is. **The fox waits. The thing-that-seems-to-be eventually reveals itself as what-it-is.** Most fox-spirit wisdom traditions begin here and build outward.
**Bond tending** · Significant relationships have literal Aetheric consequences. Practitioners tend their significant bonds **the way they tend their nose — regularly, with attention, without forcing the process.**
**Settlements** · Nomadic-adjacent, driven by Wellspring quality and Somnalis accessibility. Mishiro communities are small and mobile, **a pattern that predates the Labor Provision and has continued regardless of it.**
> Fox-spirit beastkin appear in the formal historical record **almost exclusively in Labor Provision documentation — as property rather than as actors.** This is an accurate reflection of how the Accord's record was compiled, not of what actually occurred.
>
> The Mishiro lineage's actual historical record exists in oral tradition **and in the nose's archive** — in the Aetheric memory of territory that remembers what happened there. It is not accessible to the formal historical apparatus. *This may be intentional.*
**Known figure** · **Yoko Mishiro**, active, traveling with Sodoku and Emira Moto.

## Serpentkin — Scalebond

*Ancestral Memory-Bearers · Coil-Pattern Holders*
Second Eon, Dissolution Period. Formed near the Scorchline Wellsprings when memory-essence from collapsing realms **etched itself into scaled biological bodies specifically shaped for metaphysical erosion resistance.** They did not evolve into their function. **Their function was the condition of their creation.**
**Spiritual lineage** · Zseraxil the Coil, ancient guardian of the Wellspring of Ancestral Echoes. Their lineage grants native connection to the **Coiling Memory of the world**: the principle that nothing is truly gone, **that the echo of a thing persists in the Aetheric record longer than the thing itself.**

### Soul architecture

**Essence Typology** · Memory-Aligned (Reflection / Adaptation / Temperature Sync), Speculum primary. Their souls function as **Aetheric mirrors**, capable of reflecting and storing complex historical data within the spiritual lattice. *What they touch, they remember. What they remember, they can use.*
**Soul Path** · Spirit 65% / Attraction 25% / Body 10%. The Attraction component expresses through historical Domain formation — **territories where Serpentkin have practiced become living archives.**
> Their Crystal is architecturally unusual: **memory imprints accumulate as structural layers rather than as stored data.** An old Serpentkin Crystal, viewed with sufficient Gnosis, **looks like sedimentary rock** — each layer a period of their life, compacted into the substrate.
>
> **Stall potential at Stage VIII.** Transcendence requires genuine change, and the Coiling Memory architecture is oriented toward preservation. **The Crystal resists restructuring because it contains too much it doesn't want to lose.**

### Stat framework

| Stat | Mod | Expression |
|---|---|---|
| Vitality | +10 | Resilient scaled biology. Temperature regulation via Aetheric sync provides survival advantage in extreme environments |
| Ardency | +10 | Memory-laced output. **When a strike draws on the Coiling Memory of how a similar strike resolved**, Penetration and Depth amplify |
| **Gnosis** | **+25** | **Definitive stat.** Chronos-Vision, ancestral memory access, pattern recognition across historical data. **They can read how a situation resembles situations they have memory of, producing uncanny tactical accuracy against historical opponents** |
| Dexterity | +15 | Coiled-strike precision. **Their strike windows are short and precise in the way that things optimized for a specific function become precise through duration** |

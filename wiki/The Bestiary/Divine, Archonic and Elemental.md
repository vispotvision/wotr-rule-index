# Divine, Archonic and Elemental

*Four entries · what is not an animal at all*
> Three of the four are **T8 or T9 — the top of the register.** The fourth has no fixed tier because it has no fixed size.
>
> **None of these is dangerous because it is large. Each is dangerous because of its relationship to a vein, a trench, or a gate.**

---

## Elemental

> Called **Wellspring Incarnates** or **Primordial Spirits** depending on region and Essence affinity.
**Elemental Behemoth, Pure Manifestation · T3 through T8 · found wherever their respective Essence flows strongest**
**Living manifestations of raw Wellspring energy**, formed when Essence concentrates in an unmediated state — around powerful Wellsprings, or where elemental energies surge unchecked. **Their existence predates many mortal races**, and they act as guardians or destroyers depending on circumstance and alignment.
Their forms shift with their Essence: roaring flame, crashing wave, jagged stone, swirling gust and lightning. **They shimmer with Wellspring energy and may manifest elemental runes glowing faintly along their bodies.**
| Type | Born from | Character | Powers |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fire** | The molten veins of Theros and volcanic Wellsprings | Fierce and destructive | Flame manipulation, explosive bursts, heat auras |
| **Water** | The flowing currents of Etherea and aquatic Wellsprings | Fluid and adaptive | Tidal surges, water manipulation, healing mists |
| **Earth** | Mountains, deep caves, and ancient stone Wellsprings | Stoic and relentless | Tremors, rock armour, seismic shockwaves |
| **Air** | Storm clouds and wind-swept peaks, linked to Caelum and Draconis | Swift and capricious | Gusts, lightning strikes, invisibility in fog or storm |

> **They share a core identity as forces of nature rather than thinking beings.** Some develop rudimentary awareness or bond with mortals. **Others act as impersonal natural phenomena and never notice that anyone was there.**
**Vulnerable to Essence opposition** — water douses fire, earth grounds air. **Dispersible by powerful ritual magic or Essence-dampening wards.** And **some require constant Wellspring energy to hold form; depletion causes collapse.**
*The Great Eruption of Helstra was blamed on a rampaging Fire Elemental that shattered an ancient volcano's seal. The Storm of the Thousand Winds marked a surge of Air Elementals summoned by a lost sorcerer's folly.* **Some tribes seek to bind them for protection or war. Others leave sacred Wellsprings untouched to honour their guardianship.**

## Leviathan

> **The Drowned Crown** in Old Mariner's Cant. *Vor'thalass*, the Maw Beneath All Waves, in Abyssal Tongue.
**Divine / Archonic Beast, Abyssal Apex · T9, Apocalyptic Beast · Abyssal Wellsprings, secondary Corrupted and Storm aspects · primarily Abythron, surfacing only in Crevice-linked oceans**
> Singular oceanic behemoths **birthed directly by Titans of the deep.** Most descend from a time **before Gaia's shaping of the Material World, when the oceans were unbroken voidwater.**
>
> The most feared is **the Leviathan of Abythron, created by Zar'thuun as a guardian of the deepest void trenches** — an amalgam of abyssal essence, deep-sea adaptation and ancient draconic bloodline, giving it an unnatural combination of resilience and cunning.
A colossal sea serpent **stretching hundreds of metres**, armoured in abyss-black scales traced with faint bioluminescence. Coral-like horns and jagged bone crests crown the head; **the maw is lined with layered teeth capable of crushing steel-hulled ships.** The eyes glow deep abyssal blue **and can induce fear or paralysis in weaker wills.**
Solitary, immensely territorial, **drawn to disturbances in deep Wellspring currents.** Slow and deliberate in the deep, capable of blistering speed when striking.
> **It hunts both physically and metaphysically** — dragging ships and creatures beneath the surface **while severing their soul's tether to the living realm.**
**Tidal Maelstrom** generates whirlpools large enough to pull fleets under. **Void Gaze** locks eyes to inflict soul-severing dread and Essence disruption. **Abyssal Breath** exhales pressurised supercooled abyssal water that freezes and shatters fortification. **Domain Anchor** — it acts as a living abyssal Wellspring stabiliser, warping local sea and sky.
**Vulnerable in prolonged shallow-water combat where its manoeuvrability is reduced.** The Abyssal Breath **requires recovery time and disrupting the buildup prevents the strike.** And its **bioluminescent markings, if targeted, disrupt its camouflage.**
> The **Fall of Altherion's Eastern Fleet** was attributed to a leviathan attack during the Third Concord War. **No survivors, and a shattered coast.**
>
> Sailors still whisper of the **Year of the Seven Tides**, when the Leviathan of Abythron surfaced repeatedly **and altered ocean currents for an entire season.**
>
> *Abyssal cults worship it as a herald of Zar'thuun's return. Coastal nations mark it as a doomsday sign in their prophecy records.*

## Kraken

> *Tsal'gar*, Terror of the Depths, in Old Parunic. **The Abyssal Maw** among sailors.
**Divine Beast, Abyssal Colossus · T9, Apocalyptic Threat · Abyssal and Water Wellsprings with powerful chaotic Essence surges · the deepest ocean trenches and the Abyssal Depths**
A primordial sea monster born from **the violent convergence of Abyssal and Water-elemental Wellsprings in the deepest trenches.** Legend places their creation during cataclysmic oceanic upheavals.
Gigantic cephalopod form with dozens of powerful barbed tentacles, **a massive beaked maw**, and eyes glowing with abyssal light. Skin shimmering with bioluminescent pattern. **Sheer size dwarfs most sea vessels.**
Territorial and overwhelmingly destructive. **Solitary hunters, believed to be ancient and near-immortal.**
**Tentacle Crush** grapples and pulverises ships. **Abyssal Roar** raises waves and disorients. **Ink Veil** releases Essence-infused ink that obscures vision **and dampens magical senses.** **Water Manipulation** generates currents and whirlpools.
**Vulnerable to Essence-purging magic and explosive devices. Slow on land and exposed when beached. And rarely ventures far from deep Wellspring zones**, which limits encounters to those who go looking.
*The Sinking of the Iron Leviathan remains a cautionary tale of a Kraken attack on a Concord flagship. Coastal communities hold festivals to ward off its wrath, invoking ancient sea rites.*

## Cerberus

> *Gar'thak*, the Threefold Maw, in Abyssal tongues. **The Hellhound Guardian** in frontier legend.
**Divine Beast, Infernal Guardian · T8, Cataclysmic Threat · Abyssal and Corrupted Wellsprings infused with potent Fire and Shadow Essence · Abyssal Depths, corrupted crevices, forbidden Outer World gates**
**A colossal three-headed hellhound born from Abyssal Wellspring corruption and ancient infernal bindings.** Crafted as the eternal guardian of Abyssal gates and cursed Wellspring breaches. **It embodies raw destructive power and unwavering loyalty to its dark masters.**
A massive canine, three snarling heads, **each with fiery eyes and razor fangs.** Thick shadowy fur laced with flickering embers. Claws burning with infernal heat, **flames flickering along a spiked tail.**
Fierce, relentless, highly territorial. **It hunts intruders without mercy and can track Essence signatures across realms.**
**Tri-Headed Strike** attacks multiple foes simultaneously. **Infernal Breath** — each head unleashes fire, shadow flame, or poisonous smoke. **Essence Tracker** senses Wellspring and life Essence to pursue prey relentlessly. **Hellfire Aura** burns nearby enemies **and disrupts Essence flow.**
> Vulnerable to coordinated assaults **targeting each head separately.** Sensitive to radiant Essence magic and holy wards.
>
> And **it is bound by infernal contracts — breaking these can weaken or banish it outright.** *Which makes the Cerberus the only entry in the register with a legal counterplay.*
*The Siege of the Black Maw details Concord forces struggling to breach Abyssal gates guarded by a Cerberus. Tales of hunters facing the Threefold Terror circulate among frontier camps.*

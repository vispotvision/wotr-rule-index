# Divine, Archonic and Elemental

*Four entries · what is not an animal at all*
> Three of the four are **T8 or T9, the top of the register.** The fourth has no fixed tier because it has no fixed size.
>
> **Size is the least of what makes any of them dangerous. Each is dangerous because of its relationship to a [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/The Sixty Wellsprings|Wellspring]] site, a trench, or a gate.**

The herring came up the streets of Morrowhythe before the water did. That was the first morning of the Year of the Seven Tides, and the whole harbor had turned out in its market colors for the Blessing of the Nets, saffron and sea-green and the fishwives' red kerchiefs, when the sea went out past the bar and left the fleet lying on its side, came back silver and stinking to the third step of the Fishmongers' Hall, went out, and came back again, seven times between dawn and dusk. On the seventh the whole bay lifted. Something rose out of it crowned in coral and jagged bone, abyss-black, traced down the neck in faint blue light, and it went on rising long after everyone on the quay had agreed that it must be finished. It never looked at Morrowhythe. It looked at a patch of empty water four leagues out, and the eye that crossed the harbor on the way there dropped the Reeve and his whole watch to their knees, along with a great many people who had only come for the fish. Then it went under, and the wave it left put nine boats on rooftops and the bell tower in the bay, and for the rest of that season the cod ran where the mackerel had run, three towns went broke, one got very rich, and every pilot on the coast had to learn the water again from the beginning.

Morrowhythe entered a plea against the [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Fourteen Titans|Titan]] Zar'thuun in the Reeve's court for nine boats and a bell tower, and served it by rowing out past the bar and throwing it in the sea. I have read the file, and it is still open, and I have seldom felt so warmly toward a town. Everything on this page is the sort of thing you sue anyway. I have handled a shed Kraken beak and been singed through a good boot by an ember off a Cerberus, and I will tell you what I tell the apprentices: these four are worthy of every hymn ever sung about them, and they also eat, sleep, tire, and keep to their ground, and the eating and the ground are where you start.

## Elemental

> Called **Wellspring Incarnates** or **Primordial Spirits** depending on region and Essence affinity.

**Elemental Behemoth, Pure Manifestation · T3 through T8 · found wherever their respective kind of Aether pools thickest**

**Leans on** · the Shell, nearly always, since one is a Wild law with a body built around it, and the kind of Aether decides the rest. *Body:* a big enough hit at the one solid part. *Spirit:* the thing has a Core at the center and no idea you can reach it. *Fate:* it stops when the ground stops feeding it, and a site can be made to fail.

**Living manifestations of raw Wellspring power**, formed when one kind of Aether concentrates with nothing to refine it, around rich Wellspring sites or wherever elemental energies surge unchecked. (The old books say Essence concentrating in an unmediated state, and a Measurewright will correct them at length.) **Their existence predates many mortal races**, and they act as guardians or destroyers depending on circumstance and alignment.

There is no skeleton to describe and I have stopped looking for one. The body is whatever the Aether is doing at the moment: roaring flame, crashing wave, jagged stone, swirling gust and lightning. **They shimmer with Wellspring power and may manifest elemental runes glowing faintly along their bodies.** The nearest thing to a metabolism is the ground under them. A small one feeds at a site the way a flame feeds at a wick, and a colossus reshapes the landscape because the landscape is what it is eating.

| Type | Born from | Character | Powers |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fire** | The molten veins of Theros and volcanic Wellspring sites | Fierce and destructive | Flame manipulation, explosive bursts, heat auras |
| **Water** | The flowing currents of Etherea and aquatic Wellspring sites | Fluid and adaptive | Tidal surges, water manipulation, healing mists |
| **Earth** | Mountains, deep caves, and ancient stone Wellspring sites | Stoic and relentless | Tremors, rock armor, seismic shockwaves |
| **Air** | Storm clouds and wind-swept peaks, linked to Caelum and Draconis | Swift and capricious | Gusts, lightning strikes, invisibility in fog or storm |

> **They share a core identity as forces of nature rather than thinking beings.** Some develop rudimentary awareness or bond with mortals. **Others act as impersonal natural phenomena and never notice that anyone was there.**

**Vulnerable to Essence opposition:** water douses fire, earth grounds air. **Dispersible by powerful ritual magic or Essence-dampening wards.** And **some require constant Wellspring power to hold form, so that depletion causes collapse**: walk one off rich ground and it comes apart behind you like a wave going back down a beach.

*The Great Eruption of Helstra was blamed on a rampaging Fire Elemental that shattered an ancient volcano's seal. The Storm of the Thousand Winds marked a surge of Air Elementals summoned by a lost sorcerer's folly.* **Some tribes seek to bind them for protection or war. Others leave sacred Wellspring sites untouched to honor their guardianship.**

## Leviathan

> **The Drowned Crown** in Old Mariner's Cant. *Vor'thalass*, the Maw Beneath All Waves, in Abyssal Tongue.

**Divine / Archonic Beast, Abyssal Apex · T9, Apocalyptic Beast · Abython, the Abyssal Pulse, secondary Nihiloth and Cymorath aspects · primarily Abythron, surfacing only in Crevice-linked oceans**

**Leans on** · the Shell, by Abython, Wild, with Nihiloth and Cymorath. *Body:* nobody on record. *Spirit:* a very great Core a very long way down. *Fate:* it surfaces only through what a Crevice allows, and that is the only lever anyone has ever suggested.

> Singular oceanic behemoths **birthed directly by Titans of the deep.** Most descend from a time **before Gaia's shaping of the Material World, when the oceans were unbroken voidwater.**
>
> The most feared is **the Leviathan of Abythron, created by Zar'thuun as a guardian of the deepest void trenches**, an amalgam of abyssal essence, deep-sea adaptation and ancient draconic bloodline, giving it an unnatural combination of resilience and cunning.

A colossal sea serpent **stretching hundreds of meters**, armored in abyss-black scales traced with faint bioluminescence. Coral-like horns and jagged bone crests crown the head, and the coral is living coral, which tells you how long it can hold still. **The maw is lined with layered teeth capable of crushing iron-bound hulls**, set in rows like a shark's and replaced like a shark's, and the Abythron shelf-fishers have sold me three of the shed ones at a price I still resent. The eyes glow deep abyssal blue **and can induce fear or paralysis in weaker wills.**

Solitary and immensely territorial, it is **drawn to disturbances in deep Wellspring sites**: anything that stirs the pooled Aether of the trenches brings it up. It moves slowly and deliberately in the deep and with blistering speed when striking.

> **It hunts both physically and metaphysically**, dragging ships and creatures beneath the surface **while severing their soul's tether to the living realm.**

**Tidal Maelstrom** generates whirlpools large enough to pull fleets under. **Void Gaze** locks eyes to inflict soul-severing dread and Essence disruption. **Abyssal Breath** exhales pressurized supercooled abyssal water (water chilled below its freezing point and kept liquid by the crush of the deep), which freezes the moment it strikes and shatters fortification. **Domain Anchor** makes it a living stabilizer of abyssal Wellspring ground, warping local sea and sky.

**Vulnerable in prolonged shallow-water combat where its maneuverability is reduced.** The Abyssal Breath **requires recovery time, and disrupting the buildup prevents the strike.** And its **bioluminescent markings, if targeted, disrupt its camouflage.**

> The **Fall of Altherion's Eastern Fleet** was attributed to a leviathan attack during the Third Concord War. **No survivors, and a shattered coast.**
>
> Sailors still tell of the **Year of the Seven Tides**, when the Leviathan of Abythron surfaced repeatedly **and altered ocean currents for an entire season.**
>
> *Abyssal cults worship it as a herald of Zar'thuun's return. Coastal nations mark it as a doomsday sign in their prophecy records.*

## Kraken

> *Tsal'gar*, Terror of the Depths, in Old Parunic. **The Abyssal Maw** among sailors.

**Divine Beast, Abyssal Colossus · T9, Apocalyptic Threat · Abython and Thaloré, with powerful chaotic Essence surges · the deepest ocean trenches and the Abyssal Depths**

**Leans on** · the Shell by Abython, Wild, and the Core by Thaloré, Spirit, which is why a town goes down grieving. *Body:* the arms, by a fleet, for as long as the fleet lasts. *Spirit:* the sorrow on the water is not yours, and knowing that is most of what a priest can do. *Fate:* do not be the boat it is going to meet.

A primordial sea monster born from **the violent convergence of Abython and Thaloré in the deepest trenches.** Legend places their creation during cataclysmic oceanic upheavals.

The body is a cephalopod's on a scale that makes the comparison feel like an insult: dozens of powerful barbed tentacles around **a massive beaked maw**, eyes glowing with abyssal light, and skin shimmering with bioluminescent pattern that crawls and changes the way a cuttlefish's does when it is deciding about you. The beak is the only hard part of the animal, and a shed one will not fit through a barn door. **Its sheer size dwarfs most sea vessels.**

Territorial and overwhelmingly destructive, they are **solitary hunters, believed to be ancient and near-immortal.**

**Tentacle Crush** grapples and pulverizes ships. **Abyssal Roar** raises waves and disorients. **Ink Veil** releases Essence-infused ink that obscures vision **and dampens magical senses**, so that a practitioner in the cloud loses the sea and his sensing together. **Water Manipulation** generates currents and whirlpools.

**Vulnerable to Essence-purging magic and to fire-ships and the Guild's fire-casks. Slow on land and exposed when beached. And it rarely ventures far from deep Wellspring sites**, which limits encounters to those who go looking.

*The Sinking of the Iron Leviathan, a Concord flagship named for her iron-sheathed ram, remains a cautionary tale of a Kraken attack. Coastal communities hold festivals to ward off its wrath, invoking ancient sea rites, with a great deal of dancing and a great many paper tentacles.*

## Cerberus

> *Gar'thak*, the Threefold Maw, in Abyssal tongues. **The Hellhound Guardian** in frontier legend.

**Divine Beast, Infernal Guardian · T8, Cataclysmic Threat · Tarturon and Nihiloth, infused with potent Pyreveil and Vantabriel Essence · Abyssal Depths, corrupted crevices, forbidden Outer World gates**

**Leans on** · the Layer first, by Tarturon, Fate, and the Shell by Pyreveil, Vantabriel and Nihiloth. *Body:* soundness, a great many hands, and no debts. *Spirit:* three heads and one hand on the reckoning, showing on the Core before it lands. *Fate:* it holds a gate as a condition, so the contest is over the condition and never the dog.

**A colossal three-headed hellhound born from corruption on Tarturon ground and ancient infernal bindings**, crafted as the eternal guardian of Abyssal gates and cursed Wellspring breaches. **It embodies raw destructive power and unwavering loyalty to its dark masters.**

The build is a massive canine's, with three snarling heads, **each with fiery eyes and razor fangs.** Thick shadowy fur is laced with flickering embers, which come off when it shakes and burn through a boot sole faster than you would believe. The claws burn with infernal heat, and **flames flicker along a spiked tail.** Each head keeps its own watch, and I have never met a hunter who caught all three asleep.

It is fierce, relentless and highly territorial. **It hunts intruders without mercy and can track Essence signatures across realms.**

**Tri-Headed Strike** attacks multiple foes simultaneously. **Infernal Breath** lets each head unleash fire, shadow flame, or poisonous smoke. **Essence Tracker** senses Wellspring and life Essence to pursue prey relentlessly. **Hellfire Aura** burns nearby enemies **and disrupts Essence flow.**

> Vulnerable to coordinated assaults **targeting each head separately.** Sensitive to radiant Essence magic and holy wards.
>
> And **it is bound by infernal contracts, and breaking these can weaken or banish it outright.** *Which makes the Cerberus the only entry in the register with a legal counterplay.*

*The Siege of the Black Maw details Concord forces struggling to breach Abyssal gates guarded by a Cerberus. Tales of hunters facing the Threefold Terror circulate among frontier camps, and every one of those hunters is somebody's cousin.*

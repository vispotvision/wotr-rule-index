# Lich

## Lich

Three generations of the Erebus court kept the lich lord Vharzoth on the tax roll, on the clerk's argument that a man who will not die owes like one who has not, and in the sixth year of the war he paid it in full, in old gold, by a bone courier in a green cloak who waited politely for the receipt. The coin had come out of the graves of the collectors.

**Undying, Archmage Undead · T9, Apocalyptic Threat · Mortalis, Nihiloth and Anamnesis, intertwined through necromantic rites · native to ruined fortresses, ancient crypts, and shadowed sanctuaries throughout the Innerworld and Outer World**

**Leans on** · all three, since it used to be a person, and a Spirit Path one by long habit, on Mortalis, Nihiloth and Anamnesis. *Body:* the bones are the least of it, and getting there is still how most of them die. *Spirit:* the phylactery holds the Crystal, and a soul can be reached in it. *Fate:* it has outlasted a great many reasons to die, and each of them is a thread somebody can look for.

Liches are necromancers who bound their souls to phylacteries, transcending mortality through dark Wellspring rituals. The transformation grants them mastery over death and Essence manipulation, and binds them eternally to the arcane prison they made for themselves. Most were once mortal rulers or sorcerers, and a lich pursues forbidden knowledge and dominion over life and death long after the throne or the title has been forgotten.

Skeletal figures draped in tattered robes embroidered with necromantic glyphs, their eye sockets glowing with sickly Essence light and their hands clutching arcane staffs or relics of power. Some carry visible remnants of the person they were: patches of decayed flesh, a spectral overlay of the face they wore in life.

Cold, calculating, and utterly relentless, a lich commands armies of the undead, bends the Aether of Wellspring sites to its will, and weaves schemes that outlast the mortals who oppose it. Its intellect and magical reach make it a far more dangerous adversary than brute strength alone would suggest.

| Abilities | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| **Necromantic Mastery** raises and controls vast undead legions. **Essence Drain** absorbs life force to fuel spells and regeneration. **Arcane Dominion** wields destructive and binding magic across multiple Essence disciplines. **Phylactery Binding** keeps the lich immortal unless the phylactery itself is destroyed. | The phylactery is the lich's only true vulnerability. Sustaining its Essence flows demands extensive preparation. Holy or harmonic Essence purges weaken it. |

*The Fall of Erebus' Shadow* records the war against the lich lord Vharzoth, whose armies nearly overwhelmed the Innerworld's defenses, and Concord campaigns since have focused on locating and destroying lich phylacteries to end their reigns. Liches are feared across the realms as embodiments of corruption and forbidden knowledge; some seek their power regardless, but most regard them as abominations to be purged to preserve the natural order.

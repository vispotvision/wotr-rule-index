*What corruption makes, what the Wells spawn, and what will not stay dead.*

The bounty door of the hunting hall at Cold Ashby once carried three bills side by side, nailed up on the same wet morning: forty gold for a bicorn on the Durnath road, a copper a tail for foxes, and a sworn offer from a widow, payable in orchard land, to whoever would kill her husband a second time. This shelf covers all three.

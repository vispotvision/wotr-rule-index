# Droval — The Frost-Howler

*Frost-Howler · White Maw · Rimegaunt · Skarn-Ursai*

Jarl Eyvind Tvilling of the polar shelf went out to kill a Droval the way other young men go to a wedding. He took sixty hunters, a bounty sworn by his own father of a hundred cattle and a seat at the high table, red banners with the gold ship on them, two drummers, a skald to get the details right, and a pavilion of striped wool that took four sleds to carry. On the first night out they pitched it on the open ice beside a long white ridge, lit the fat-lamps, and sat down to roast seal and a great deal of shouting, with the aurora going green and rose overhead and the skald trying out rhymes for Tvilling. Toward the middle of the second song the ridge got up. It had been lying there with the snow piling on its back since before they crossed the skyline, thirty meters from the head of the table, and it covered that distance in one bound with no more noise than a falling coat. The cook's boy, Ulf Ketilsson, was under a sled looking for a dropped spoon. He crawled out at dawn among the striped wool and the red snow, and he lived to be very old, telling it at fairs for his dinner. He always said that the worst of it was the song stopping before anyone had heard a thing. The skald's rhymes for Tvilling were never finished, and they are said to have been bad.

The register enters the Droval as an animal, and the Measurewrights who keep the entry have been to the polar shelf about as often as you would expect. What follows is the best anyone has managed, mostly from the people who bonded with them and the people who ran.

> **Type:** Natural Beast / Spirit-Touched Behemoth
>
> Rare elder specimens and Titan-class Droval cross into Elemental Behemoth classification due to extreme [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/The Sixty Wellsprings|Wellspring]] saturation.
>
> **Danger Tier:** T2–T9 (variable by age, lineage, environment, and specialization)
>
> **Essence Affinity:** Primary: Elemental (Ice / Winter) · Secondary: Harmonic (Fear, Silence, Stillness)
>
> **Leans on:** the Shell, by the Titan Vohrin's cold, which stands outside the Sixty and is worked like a Wild Wellspring. *Body:* tight ground and thin ice, for the size. *Spirit:* the bond, since a rider's Droval has one idea, and the dread goes on the Core and can be named for what it is. *Fate:* there is little to pull on such a sound animal, and a great deal to pull on a rider.

## Origin & Evolution

Droval evolved as apex megafauna during the First Winter Age, when the Inner World's polar bands were locked in a supernatural deep freeze following the collapse of a nearby Solfatara site. Over long ages, constant exposure to Titan-touched cold ground, where the Titan Vohrin's power pools in the rock, altered their bodies and instincts, infusing the species with elemental resonance beyond ordinary natural evolution. They are still mortal-born creatures, with no artificer's hand in them and no summoner's, but their bloodline has been permanently shaped by ancient Winter essence in the same way that geological pressure permanently shapes the crystal structure of the stone it acts on. The cold found a very large bear-shaped predator and went on refining it until the refinement was all anyone could see.

Droval are native to the Material World, specifically the polar shelf and polar regions of the Inner World where the deep ice holds Aetheric density high enough to sustain Winter-saturated biology. They inhabit frozen mountain ranges, glacier basins, white forests, arctic plains, and the specific liminal territories where the shelf's geography intersects with the ice's older Aetheric architecture. Wild Droval claim immense hunting ranges measured in hundreds of square kilometers. They do not migrate, and they sit out whatever comes.

Winter Eladrin later began selective bonding, ritual imprinting, and war-line breeding, refining Droval into elite mounts without ever fully taming their wild nature. Treaty is the right word for the bond between a Winter Eladrin rider and a Droval, and an Eladrin will correct you if you say domestication. The rider earns the mount's recognition through a rite of winter kinship, and the mount extends its territorial instinct to include the rider as part of its own being. The distinction matters: a tamed animal obeys, and a bonded Droval cooperates, on its own terms, for as long as the terms are honored.

The rarest among them, known as **Titan Droval**, are believed to be ancient individuals that survived for centuries in direct proximity to major Vohrin-touched sites until their bodies partially transformed into living elemental matter. Size has very little to do with it. A Titan is a Droval that has crossed a threshold, permanently, and every other terrifying thing about it follows from that.

## Appearance

### Standard Adult

A standard adult Droval is a colossal predator built on a bear's chassis by a colder intelligence than any bear has ever answered to. Most adults stand between **ten and twelve feet at the shoulder** and stretch over **twenty feet in body length**, carrying several tons of dense muscle beneath a pelt the polar shelf cannot distinguish from itself.

The body is built for patience and violence, in that order. Broad forelimbs are made for mauling, sprinting across ice, and breaking through glacier crust to reach prey sheltering beneath. The musculature across the shoulders and upper back is disproportionately developed, dense enough to absorb impacts that would shatter the skeleton of lesser megafauna, and layered beneath the fur in a way that makes the creature look almost sculpted at rest, a thing carved from snow. The hindquarters carry enough explosive power to launch the full mass into a pounce that covers thirty meters in a single bound, and the landing is silent, because the paws are the species' masterpiece.

The paws are wide and heavily padded, with a toe-spread that distributes the creature's weight across deep snow the way a snowshoe distributes a human's. Each pad is backed by thick cartilage that absorbs impact vibration before it can reach the ice surface, and the combination of spread and absorption produces what this bestiary calls **Silent Tread**: a creature the size of a longhouse can cross packed shelf-ice without cracking it and deep snow without compressing it. The claws are thick, black, hooked, and set at an angle that bites into glacier surfaces the way a climbing axe bites into vertical ice: forward-curved, self-sharpening, made for grip and traction, though they will slash when slashing is needed.

### The Head

The head is the species' most distinctive feature and sets the Droval apart from every other megafaunal predator in the Inner World's bestiary. It resembles a monstrous owl fused with a polar bear's killing apparatus. The skull is broad and blunt, with a pronounced brow ridge that protects the eyes from glare and impact. A **hooked beak**, bone-hard and pale as old ivory, terminates the muzzle, and behind the beak-line sit **rows of jagged teeth** that do the feeding after the beak has done the killing. The beak punctures and the teeth process, two separate tools in one skull, each with its own mechanics, and the few hunters who have seen one up close and come home have all remarked on how few mistakes such a head allows.

Large **forward-facing eyes** sit beneath the brow ridge, adapted for low-light and storm-dark visibility, binocular for distance tracking. The irises carry a pale luminescence, **blue, silver, or aurora-green**, that intensifies in darkness and during emotional arousal, the glow produced by Wellspring-saturated optic tissue permanently altered by millennia of Winter resonance. It shows at a long distance, and most of those who see it have only that long to act.

Short tufted ears, stiff frost-lined whiskers, and layers of dense white or pale blue fur complete the silhouette. At rest in snow, a Droval is the snow, as a consequence of being made of much the same stuff as the place it lies, and no one has ever seen one take any trouble over it.

### Titan Droval

Titan Droval differ from the standard baseline in ways that make the word "differ" inadequate. They can exceed **twenty feet at the shoulder**, with fur partially crystallized into **translucent frost-fiber** that catches light the way ice crystals do, refracting and splitting it into a faint prismatic play along the flanks and shoulders. It looks beautiful from a safe distance, and nobody has described it from any other.

Their eyes burn with the specific intensity of **cold stars**: a harder, deeper, more concentrated light than the standard Droval's luminescence, which reads to the human eye as ancient. Wellspring saturation produces it, showing through the only transparent tissue the body contains, and the signature it writes in the dark says: I have been alive longer than your civilization.

The air around a Titan Droval produces **drifting ice crystals at all times**. They form spontaneously from ambient moisture, pulled in by the Titan's Winter field, and they hang without falling, orbiting the creature's body in slow, purposeless circuits. Nothing in this is a technique. The Titan's Aetheric mass warps the local thermal equilibrium, the same way a star's gravity warps the path of nearby matter.

When enraged or preparing to charge, **frost spreads visibly** along their flanks, claws, and shoulders. Their breath rolls out in shining plumes. A faint shell of rime forms along the fur. The sound changes too: the Silent Tread stays no louder but grows heavier, the footfall of a mass that has stopped troubling to be quiet.

## Temperament & Behavior

Droval are **solitary, territorial ambush predators** with exceptional patience and almost unnatural stillness. In the wild, a Droval may remain motionless for hours while snow piles over its back, waiting for prey to come close enough for a sudden killing rush. There is nothing passive in that patience. Every muscle is loaded, every sense engaged, and every calculation runs on behind the luminous eyes, and the prey that walks up to a motionless Droval has no idea what it is walking up to.

They are deliberate, intelligent, and brutally efficient, with no frenzy in them at all. Once a Droval commits to an attack, it does so with full lethal intent and does not disengage until the target is dead or the Droval has determined that the cost of continuing exceeds the value of the prey. That reckoning sets them apart. Ordinary predators weigh risk, and Droval weigh investment: a Droval will abandon a hunt without shame if its economics stop working, will pursue one across kilometers if they stay favorable, and makes both decisions with the same absence of emotion.

Wild Droval claim immense hunting ranges and tolerate almost no intrusion unless the trespasser is prey, a potential mate, or a bonded companion. Outside breeding season or a mother-cub bond, they do not gather in groups. When two Droval occupy overlapping territory, the resolution need not be violent. It can be a sustained, weeks-long contest of presence, each creature occupying the boundary and waiting for the other to concede, and the Rimegaunt clans have been known to sit up on a ridge and watch one for the entertainment.

**Bonded Droval** extend their territorial instinct to include their rider, treating that individual as part of their own being. This bond is the most dangerous thing about them in a military context, because a bonded Droval whose rider is threatened abandons every tactical calculation the species is known for and operates on the single remaining directive: protect the territory. What arrives at that point has stopped calculating, and the armies that have met it describe a cataclysm with a face.

## Essence Architecture

Droval do not cast spells, invoke Wellsprings, or refine Essence through a Soul Crystal in the way practitioners do, because the Shell that work would have to leave by is sealed, as every beast's is until something cracks it (see [[The Bestiary/The Bestiary#What Has a Soul|What Has a Soul]]). They have a soul and a stone behind the breastbone like any wild thing. What they possess beyond that is older, simpler and more concentrated: a **bloodline permanently saturated with the resonance of Vohrin's cold** that has been breeding true for millennia.

Their Essence expression is entirely **passive and environmental**. Their presence lowers ambient temperature. Their proximity suppresses natural sound through Harmonic resonance. Their vocalizations carry a sub-audible component that produces instinctive dread in nearby creatures. No mind control and no working is involved, only a primal terror sharpened by Wellspring resonance until it operates below conscious thought and above the threshold of physiological response. The body reacts before the mind identifies the threat. Heart rate rises, sweat comes, fine motor control degrades. At its root the terror is thermoregulatory: the body recognizing, at the deepest level of its survival, that the thing nearby is the cold itself, which is what the body evolved to flee.

Titan Droval intensify every passive effect to catastrophic levels. A Titan's presence freezes standing water, cracks exposed stone, disrupts weaker fire-aligned manifestations, and produces the **ice-crystal orbiting phenomenon** that makes a Titan identifiable at distance. The Titan's Harmonic output is sufficient to suppress the morale of trained military formations and to destabilize the Essence coherence of practitioners below [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Sixteen Stages|Stage]] IV.

## Abilities & Powers

### Standard Droval

| **Frost-Cloak Hide** | Snow and ice accumulate naturally across the pelt, granting near-perfect camouflage in tundra, blizzard, and glacier terrain. A resting Droval is functionally invisible to the eye. A hunter's bare hand held out for warmth does no better, because the fur keeps a surface temperature identical to the surrounding ice |
|---|---|
| **Aurora Howl** | A low, owl-like roar that carries through snowfields and frozen valleys. Audible component: shakes morale, panics lesser beasts and mounts. Sub-audible component: Harmonic resonance suppresses ambient sound and replaces it with the specific stillness that the body takes for dread. Dual-frequency, and the part you cannot hear is the part that does the harm |
| **Glacier Pounce** | An explosive launch from absolute stillness covering up to thirty meters in a single bound. The pounce is delivered at maximum velocity with the full mass behind it. Impact force at the landing point exceeds what most structural reinforcement is rated to absorb. Hours of stillness are spent in a single moment of maximum violence |
| **Winter's Endurance** | Exceptional resilience to cold, hunger, fatigue, and storm conditions. A Droval can survive weeks without feeding, months without shelter, and indefinitely without warmth, which the species does not require. The endurance is metabolic: Winter-saturated biology processes ambient Aetheric energy as a supplemental source of food, reducing dependence on prey |
| **Silent Tread** | Paw structure (wide, padded, cartilage-backed) combined with Harmonic resonance produces near-silent movement at any speed across any frozen surface. A charging Droval is quieter than a walking human. This is the most terrifying ability in the standard kit, because the prey's first sign of the Droval's presence is the Droval's arrival |

### War-Bred & Elder Droval

| **Rime Aura** | Bodies radiate cold intensely enough to frost armor, numb exposed flesh, and weaken lesser fire effects at close range. The aura is a 5–10m passive field that degrades enemy equipment and physiology through sustained thermal extraction. Extended proximity produces hypothermia symptoms in unprotected combatants |
|---|---|
| **Permafrost Slam** | Striking the ground with sufficient force sends cracking waves through frozen terrain, disrupting enemy formations and destabilizing footing in a 20m radius. The crack pattern follows the ice's natural fault geometry, exploiting existing structural weaknesses, which beats brute force because it lets the terrain do the damage |
| **Battle Bond Instinct** | Bonded Droval react to a rider's movement or intent with near-preternatural precision, functioning as an extension of the rider. The bond operates through the Winter resonance shared between the Droval's biology and the rider's Essence field, and it runs on heat rather than telepathy: the rider's intentions produce tiny shifts in the warmth of their body, and the Droval reads the shifts the way it reads everything, through the cold |

### Titan Droval

Titan Droval are **ancient threshold-crossed entities** whose Winter saturation has become metaphysical. Each Titan develops distinct and individualized powers based on age, terrain, lineage, and Wellspring exposure. No two are exactly alike, and this variance is what allows the upper end of the species to rise into T7, T8, and in the most extreme cases T9 territory.

| **Blizzard Sovereign** | The Titan generates a localized weather dominion, bringing snow, wind, and killing cold across a massive radius. The dominion is real weather and no construct, and a Measurewright's storm-glass and wind-vane read the Titan's presence as an actual storm system |
|---|---|
| **Permafrost Dominion** | Ground beneath the Titan's passage freezes solid to a depth of several meters. Structures and terrain remain altered long after the Titan leaves. The alteration is geological rather than Aetheric: the ground stays frozen because its temperature was genuinely reduced, with no effect left running to dispel |
| **Crystalline Endurance** | The hide hardens into frost-mineral armor that deflects conventional steel, arrows, bolts and most non-Essence-infused attacks. The armor is the fur itself, partially crystallized into a translucent mineral-ice composite that carries the hardness of stone and the flexibility of cartilage |
| **Aurora Cascade** | The Titan's howl becomes a resonance wave capable of shattering morale, disrupting military formations, and physically damaging structures of ice or stone through harmonic destabilization. The wave is visible: the air shimmers with the aurora-like distortion that Winter Harmonic resonance produces at high amplitude |
| **Deep Winter Pulse** | Rare high-tier Titans release a catastrophic wave of Winter essence that flash-freezes a battlefield. The pulse is a single-use event that requires hours of accumulation. The flash-freeze is indiscriminate, total, and permanent until external heat is applied |
| **Trait Variance** | Individual Titans may manifest unique powers: ice-mist concealment, spirit-sight, anti-flame aura suppression, glacial regeneration, the ability to sleep for decades and awaken stronger. No two Titan Droval are identical, which makes each one a regional event rather than a species specimen |

A word on the bills. Lords and jarls of the far north post Droval bounties with some regularity, mostly young lords, and the crews who know the shelf read the Tier before the sum. The Rimegaunt clans will guide a party for pay as far as the last cairn and no farther, and will quote you the price for bringing back your banners.

## Weaknesses

Droval are creatures of deep cold, and while dominant in their native environment, they suffer outside it.

**Thermal vulnerability.** Intense heat, dry climates, volcanic terrain, and prolonged exposure to strong fire-aspected Essence sap their strength and destabilize their Winter resonance. In hot regions, their endurance drops sharply, their passive cold field weakens, and their frost-based adaptations become liabilities. A Droval forced to operate in temperate-to-warm conditions is running on a timer, and the timer is the speed at which its Winter saturation dissipates.

**Size limitations.** Tight corridors, unstable floors, marshes, thin ice, and enclosed town streets deny them the space needed for full movement and pouncing force. A Droval in a hallway is still a creature the size of a longhouse, but it cannot pounce, cannot maneuver, and cannot make an ally of the terrain.

**Bond exploitation.** Bonded Droval can be manipulated by threatening their rider, cubs, or den range. They often abandon optimal tactics in favor of direct protection, and a commander who has read this far knows what to do with that. A bonded Droval whose rider is threatened behaves like a mother whose child is in danger, the most dangerous thing in the world and also the easiest to predict.

**Titan margin.** Titan Droval possess the same basic weaknesses on a much narrower margin. Only high-tier fire Essence, major hot Wild sites, Stage VIII+ fire-aspected practitioners, or elite coordinated multi-practitioner opposition can reliably exploit Titan weaknesses. Below that threshold, the weaknesses are theoretical.

## Cultural Significance

To the **northern polar peoples**, Droval mean wildly different things depending on one's relationship with the shelf. Some communities see them as sacred guardians of the old ice and leave offerings at suspected den sites: carved bone and rendered fat, the tribute of people who have decided that living beside the apex predator requires its permission, and who ask for it the only way they can. Others consider slaying one the ultimate mark of warrior prestige, though such hunts usually end in the specific category of disaster that produces legends about why the hunt should not have been attempted.

To **military cultures**, Droval cavalry formations are one of the most terrifying battlefield forces in winter terrain. The combination of stealth (Silent Tread), shock (Glacier Pounce), area denial (Rime Aura), and a howl that works below the threshold of conscious fear makes Droval cavalry the thing commanders plan around rather than against, since no one has reliably developed a counter to a Droval charge in blizzard conditions.

**Titan Droval** stand outside ordinary cultural categories entirely. They are viewed as **living omens**, ancient powers, or the direct will of Winter made flesh. Entire communities flee at rumors of one stirring. Frost cults and northern mystics sometimes seek them out in the hope of worship, alliance, or control. The worship occasionally succeeds, the alliance rarely does, and the control never has. People survive in the same territory as a Titan Droval, on the Titan's terms, for as long as it allows.

## The Arctic Lion Encounter

> **Recent Event: The Polar Shelf, Present Day**
>
> During the Kokan expedition's march toward Kharven, two Titan-class Droval emerged from the shelf ice and approached the column. Rather than attacking, the Titans performed a **Winter recognition assessment**, identifying the Arctic Lion's Verdict Plate and Vohrin-integrated Essence field as a kindred Winter authority.
>
> The first Titan locked on Sodoku Moto. The second locked on Sonzai Moto. The encounter did not produce violence. It produced the specific gesture the bestiary classifies as **territorial acknowledgment between Winter authorities of comparable resonance**: the head-lower, the sustained eye-hold, and the waiting.
>
> The encounter's resolution is ongoing. The Titans have not departed. They have not attacked. They are waiting. And the waiting is the thing the species does better than any other thing.

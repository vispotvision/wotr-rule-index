# Greater Wolf

## Greater Wolf

The reeve of Ebbe's Fold posted two silver a pelt one hard winter, on the church door, in his best hand. By the thaw the pack had taken all nine hunters who answered that bill, one at a time and each on the way home, and had not touched a single sheep, and the reeve went out and took the bill down himself and left a side of mutton where the door faced the trees.

> *Vael'kar*, Shadow Howl, in Old Sylvaar. **The Silver Fang** among frontier hunters.

**Natural Beast, Apex Predator · T3, Moderate Threat · Spatium, Transference, with a minor Anamnesis resonance tied to pack lineage · native to the dense forests and mountain borderlands of the Innerworld, with roaming packs ranging into the Outer World**

Greater Wolves are natural-born hunters, evolved from mortal wolf stock but heavily shaped by Transference's Aether, which granted them heightened intelligence, endurance, and pack coordination. Their line traces to the primal forests of the Innerworld and Material World borderlands, where Wellspring energy runs richest through the wilds.

Larger and more muscular than ordinary wolves, with dense fur ranging from silver-gray to deep charcoal, often mottled with patches of white or black. Their eyes gleam intelligent amber or icy blue, and their movement is fluid and predatory. Some packs bear faint Essence markings that glow subtly during a hunt.

Highly social and cooperative, Greater Wolves rely on coordinated pack tactics and relentless pursuit. Packs are tightly knit, governed by alpha pairs, and communicate through howl patterns, body language, and Essence signals. They defend territory and young fiercely, challenging rival packs and even humanoid hunters who press too far into their range.

| Abilities | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| **Coordinated Hunt** flanks, traps, and exhausts prey. **Essence Howl** disorients or weakens foes and can summon pack members across great distance. **Enhanced Senses**: smell, hearing, and night vision amplified by Wellspring resonance. **Pack Bond**, a shared Essence link that speeds coordination and passes information between members. | Vulnerable when separated from the pack, or during full-moon shifts when their Essence flux fluctuates. Reluctant to engage heavily armored foes without support. Sensitive to Essence-disrupting magic and scent-blocking wards. |

The *Silverfang Hunts* of the Innerworld recount fierce battles between Greater Wolf packs and Concord scouts, and packs have been known to ally with sylvan rangers or druidic circles to defend sacred groves. Forest tribes respect and sometimes worship them as symbols of unity, strength, and the wild's relentless spirit; a few hunters seek to tame or bond with them as companions, though such efforts are rare and perilous.

# Neiman Lion

## Neiman Lion

A chief of the dune tribes once took his people's chair in a robe trimmed with a Neiman mane, and at noon on the day of the gathering the trim lit up like a lamp in front of all nine clans. By dusk the dead lion's pride had come forty leagues across the sand and sat down in a golden ring around the chief's tent, in perfect courtesy, and on the third day the chief walked out in his shirt and handed back the robe.

**Natural Beast, Apex Predator · T4, High Threat · Pyraeon, with a minor Aurevane influence · native to the desert savannahs and grasslands of the Old World and the Outer World, especially near sun-drenched [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/The Sixty Wellsprings|Wellspring]] sites**

**Leans on** · the Shell, by Pyraeon, Wild, with Aurevane in the mane. *Body:* it holds the ground it stands on, so fight it elsewhere. *Spirit:* the Core is a pride's, and a pride remembers. *Fate:* its stunning roar needs open air and an audience.

The Neiman Lion is a majestic predator evolved from mortal lion stock, adapted to the harsh deserts and savannahs of the Old and Outer Worlds. Pyraeon's Essence runs through the line, granting enhanced strength, endurance, and a mystical aura that commands respect and fear; a Neiman Lion reigns over its territory, hunting alone or in small prides.

A large feline with golden tawny fur that shimmers faintly under sunlight. Its mane glows subtly with Wellspring energy, sometimes reading as a halo of light, and its eyes blaze amber or fiery gold. Its movement blends grace with raw power, and scars and battle marks are common among pride leaders.

Regal and fiercely territorial, Neiman Lions are both hunters and protectors of their prides, with dominant males guarding territory and leading hunts under a complex social order. Their presence inspires awe and terror in equal measure among other creatures and the local tribes who share their ground.

The *Battle of Searing Dunes* recounts Neiman pride leaders defending Wellspring oases from poachers and Crevice-spawn incursions, and tribal elders still tell of the Sunmaw's Blessing, a rare cub believed to bring prosperity. Desert cultures revere the Neiman Lion as a symbol of power and vitality; its mane is prized in ceremonial garb, and its spirit is invoked in rites for courage and protection.

# The Draconic Lineage

*Four entries · the sky-serpent path, and the two branches that left it*

Tobin Sark stole a glyph-stone the size of a fist out of the lair of an earth wyrm nobody had troubled to name, sold it in the salt towns for a very good price, heard what was following it, bought it back for a much better one, and ran for twenty-six years. The wyrm came after him at about the pace of a glacier in a bad mood, a long ochre hill with a crest of stone spines going through the country in a straight line, and six towns on the salt road still have a street that runs crooked where it passed. Lords posted bills on it. Crews in their colors went out with banners and came home quieter with their hats in their hands. The towns stopped running, after a while, and took to selling buns along the route. It found him at last in a rented room above a bakery, an old man of eighty with the stone under his chamber pot, and it lifted off the roof at dawn, put in a head the size of a river barge with its long whiskers feeling over the rafters, took the stone out from under the pot with a delicacy that surprised the baker, and went home. Tobin lived six more years and always said he had never slept so well. The baker sent the bill for the roof to the wyrm, care of the Concord, and the Concord has it on file.

That is a wyrm being patient. The rest of this entry explains why you should prefer it to the alternatives.

> All four descend from Omnis' first sky-breath. **Two of them no longer do so honestly.**
>
> The Hydra is a wyrm bloodline **fractured by void-tainted Wellsprings.** The Voidwyrm is draconic blood crossed with raw Crevice essence. *Draconic cultures regard both as tragic: reminders of what happens when a draconic soul fractures beyond repair.*

## Wyrm

> *Vyrrhos*, Ancient Serpent of Sky and Stone, in Draconic. *Serpenta Magna* in Old Parunic.

**Draconic Lineage · T7, Realm Hazard · Materia's Chthonica or Fulguria's Cymorath by subspecies, rare Orrenthal lines out of Caelum · native to Draconis, in the highland roots of the floating isles**

Among the oldest draconic forms and **direct descendants of Omnis' first sky-breath.** Wingless, moving with serpentine grace across earth and sky through Wellspring resonance alone.

> Many scholars hold that wyrms were the trial form **before Omnis perfected winged dragons.** *Wyrms themselves reject this as myth. They claim to be purer, and closer to the source.*

Long, limbless or short-limbed bodies armored in thick plate-like scales. **Coloration reflects Wellspring alignment**: ochre, bronze and stone-gray for earth wyrms; pale silver, sky-blue and cloud-white for air. Eyes glow faintly with glyph-light when channeling. **Horn crests or jagged stone-like spines run the length of the back**, and the head is crowned with elaborate whiskers that read atmospheric and Essence shift.

Territorial, solitary, **and thinkers as much as hunters.** They lair where Wellspring Aether pools along geomantic lines in the rock and **feed on that Aether rather than on prey alone.** Slow to anger, and once provoked **they pursue relentlessly, sometimes for decades.** They leave gold where it lies and collect **objects with long resonance**: ancient stones, glyph-bound artifacts, the bones of legendary beasts.

| Abilities | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| **Essence Glide** · flight without wings; movement through stone as if through water **Geomantic Breath** · earth wyrms release a pulverizing shockwave of stone and sand, air wyrms slicing wind or vacuum implosion **Wellspring Guard** · their presence **stabilizes the local Aether. They are living anchors in high-Essence zones** **Resonance Sense** · detects ley-line and Wellspring disturbance across vast distance | Extremely large bodies **make confined spaces a disadvantage** Extended separation from their preferred Wellspring type weakens them **Vulnerable to precise high-penetration strikes at the jointed scale gaps** |

During the First Concord War the Earth Wyrm **Kallessyr the Stone Maw** collapsed an entire pass to halt an advancing army of abyssal constructs. The Air Wyrm **Thyrrin Cloudrend** survives in Outer World seafarer legend as *the storm serpent that swallowed ships whole without ever touching the sea.*

> In Draconis they are honored as **elders of the sky-serpent path**: advisors to younger dragons and occasional arbiters in draconic disputes.
>
> In the Material World they are unstoppable disasters, or mountain guardians, depending on the district. **Some Innerworld mining clans make blood-oaths with wyrms to secure safe passage beneath their territories.**

## Wyvern

> *Vyreth*, Storm Talon, in Draconic. *Vryssal*, Winged Fang, in Old Parunic.

**Draconic Lineage, Winged Reptile · T6, Domain Threat · Fulguria, Cymorath and Orrenthal, some bloodlines Caloria's Pyraeon · Caelum and Draconis, with isolated Outer World populations**

Descendants of ancient draconic stock **forged in the tempestuous storms of Caelum's skies.** Lacking forelimbs, they rely entirely on wings and hind legs. **Their evolution is tied to the volatile Wellsprings of Fulguria**, and they inhabit storm-battered peaks and tempestuous archipelagos.

Large reptilian body with a barbed tail. **Scales from storm-gray to fiery red by lineage.** Sharp crests and serrated horns; **eyes glow with lightning intensity during a charge.** Razor claws on the hind legs and a venomous bite.

**Pack hunters when young, solitary apex predators as adults.** Cunning, swift, ambush-reliant, and fiercely protective of nesting sites near Fulguria sites.

**Storm Dive** creates sonic booms and shockwaves. **Venomous Bite** injects a paralyzing neurotoxin. **Electric Surge** channels storm energy into bolts and static fields. **Wing Gust** knocks enemies prone and breaks formations.

**Vulnerable when grounded or molting. Susceptible to cold and water magic that disrupts their storm resonance. And packs fracture under prolonged coordinated assault.**

*The Tempest Siege of Eldric Pass details wyvern packs harrying Concord supply lines until elite air hunters broke the assault. They feature heavily in Outer World smuggler folklore as guardians of hidden Wellspring caches, and some mountain clans worship them as elemental spirits while others hunt them for the scales, which are used in storm-forged weapons.*

## Hydra

> *Hyrathos*, the Many-Mawed Doom, in Draconic. *Serpentis Multicauda* in Old Parunic.

**Draconic Lineage, Aberrant Offshoot · T8, Cataclysmic Entity · Limina's Nihiloth over Pyraeon, Manganthra or Cymorath, by pre-corruption lineage · Crevice-scarred border zones**

> **A draconic bloodline corrupted by void-tainted Wellsprings during the First Crevice Incursion.**
>
> Originally a strain of earth-bound wyrms. Their essence became unstable **and split their core consciousness into multiple semi-independent heads**, each manifesting its own elemental or corrupted breath.
>
> **In some abyssal regions they are bred deliberately as living siege weapons.**

Massive, scaled and heavily muscled, with **three to nine heads depending on age and Essence exposure.** Each head bears unique horn patterns and scale colors tied to its own attunement. Four massive legs, a powerful tail, armored plate from neck to tail-tip. ***Eyes burn with feral, individual intelligence in each head.***

Highly aggressive, **driven by constant hunger due to unstable Essence metabolism.** Ambush predators using marsh, cave and river delta. **Each head exhibits partial autonomy, which makes the attack pattern unpredictable.**

| Abilities | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| **Multi-Element Breath** · multiple breath attacks simultaneously, each of different elemental or corrupted nature **Regeneration** · regrows heads and limbs within hours, **and a severed head may sprout two in its place** unless cauterized or destroyed by Essence-based means **Coordinated Strike** · independent heads make conventional defensive formation nearly useless **Abyssal Endurance** · highly resistant to fatigue, poison, and most elemental attack | Regeneration halts under cauterization by fire or high-intensity Essence disruption **Severing all heads within a narrow window kills it, and the window is dangerously short** Slower on open ground. Excels in water and dense terrain |

The **Siege of Lornhaven** saw a six-headed storm hydra destroy three Concord watchtowers in a single night before massed trebuchets and a wing of griffon riders brought it down. **In the Abyssal Depths, the Nine-Maw of Zar'thuun remains an active and growing threat.**

*Among draconic cultures hydras are tragic abominations. To abyssal warlords they are symbols of unstoppable consumption.*

## Voidwyrm

> *Xal'tharix*, the Rift Serpent, in Abyssal Tongue. *Nihilcoil* in Outer World dialects.

**Draconic Lineage, Aberrant Voidspawn · T8, Cataclysmic Entity · Limina, Nihiloth and Vantabriel, with fractured Transference residues that flicker sporadically · Abythron, Niflheim, and Outer World crevice zones**

Born from the darkest rifts between realms, **twisted offspring of corrupted draconic blood and raw Crevice essence.** Bound to the fissures where void and reality blur. **Their presence causes unnatural distortion and unsettling whispers in the minds of those nearby.**

Sleek and sinuous, **scales that seem to absorb light**, appearing as shifting void-black with star-like flecks scattered across the body. Eyes glow eerie purple. Tendrils and barbs pulse with unstable Essence. ***When they move, reality ripples around them, bending as if space itself resists their form.***

Highly predatory and elusive. **They hunt from shadow and strike in unpredictable bursts.** They target Essence-rich beings preferentially, **feeding off spiritual energy as much as physical sustenance.**

**Void Ripple** distorts space locally, fracturing enemy coordination. **Essence Drain** leaches Wellspring energy from foes to heal or amplify. **Shadowstep** teleports through the folds of the Crevice. **Mind Shatter** emits psychic waves causing hallucination, fear and temporary madness.

**Vulnerable to concentrated Harmonic Essence attacks that restore balance.** Their spatial distortions **require recovery after use, creating brief openings.** And they are bound to crevice zones: **prolonged absence weakens them.**

> The **Fracturing of Dorrosfenn** was blamed on a pack tearing through the border crevices, causing widespread Essence contamination.
>
> Outer World records detail hunters **who tracked a solitary Voidwyrm for months before their minds fractured.**
>
> *Some sects worship them as agents of inevitable entropy. Others seek to banish them forever.*

# Asami and Gisei — The Two States of the Moto

**Asami** (Ү-nen) · Truth · *The Revealing* — formerly **Asami**
**Gisei** (TAH-khil) · Offering · *The Sacrifice* — formerly **Gisei**

---

## The Two Truths

Every Moto carries two truths. The first is **the person the bloodline built**: the heir of Bara and Mizuki, the lawful operator of Hataraki, the functional self shaped by lineage, duty, Shingan, and the slow pressure of the Plane of Fate pressing their soul into useful form. *This person is real. They are trained, tested, refined. They are the Moto the world sees.*
The second is **the person who existed before any of that.** Before the bloodline assigned its function. Before the Shingan opened and named the stratum they were meant to perceive. Before the first Stage was crossed and the soul accepted the shape the Work was pressing it into.
> *This person is also real. They have been there the entire time, underneath, carrying the cultivated self the way a floor carries every room built on top of it.*
> **Asami is the moment the floor is seen. Gisei is the moment the floor is spent.**
>
> Most Moto who reach sufficient depth will find Asami. **Very few will find Gisei, because Gisei requires something Asami does not: the willingness to burn the floor.**

---

## Part One · Asami

*The Shallow-Seen · The Unwritten Face · The Thing Beneath the Work*
> You have spent your entire life being what Fate required. **This is what you are when Fate looks away.**
> — *First Conclave notation, author unknown*

### What it is

**Not a technique.** No activation sequence. It cannot be trained into existence, forced, summoned, or inherited. It is the soul's True Nature surfacing so completely that the layers built over it by lineage, function, duty and Temperance training **can no longer hold their shape above it.**
The Plane of Fate calls it Shallow-Seen because from outside, what surfaces looks like the person the world already knew. **The face is the same. The voice is the same. The hands are the same.** But the animating presence behind all of it is no longer the cultivated self performing its inheritance. *What is standing there is the rawer thing. The first draft. The version that existed before every correction.*

### How it arrives

For most Moto, Asami arrives **not as a crisis but as a settling.** At sufficient depth the Plane recognises that the practitioner has carried the Work long enough that it no longer needs to be held. **It has become native. What remains when something native no longer needs to be held is the person it was always native to.**
> **The universal requirement is not bloodline. It is authenticity under sufficient pressure.** The soul must have been pressed hard enough, long enough, against its own functional identity that what lives beneath has grown dense and real enough to surface.
>
> Most Moto meet this threshold. **Non-Moto Hataraki practitioners can reach it through depth of integration rather than birth.** The Zettai records contain two accounts. The Archon-adjacent records contain one, attributed to a practitioner **whose single vow and single self had become indistinguishable over a lifetime of carrying.**

### From the inside

**A practitioner in Asami does not feel powerful. Every account agrees on this.** They do not feel amplified or ascended. **What they feel is quiet.**
The internal noise that defines a Moto's waking life is constant — the Fate Ledger reading every room, the Shingan sorting every surface into what it hides and what it owes, the trained reflex of ranking and assigning before a conscious thought fires. **All of that is gone. Not suppressed. Not turned off. Irrelevant, the way a tool becomes irrelevant when the hand carrying it no longer needs to hold anything.**
> The closest the surviving accounts come: **the practitioner becomes aware that they have always been this.** That the cultivated self was real but never complete. *That this was always the floor beneath every room they have ever stood in.*

### From the outside

Immediately recognisable and immediately wrong in a way trained observers struggle to articulate. Every physical marker is identical — the Shingan still in the iris, the birth-sheen still riding the skin, the Wellsprings still running. **But the framing is gone.**
A Moto in their functional state always feels like they are **doing** something: evaluating, measuring, preparing. Even in stillness they have the quality of a loaded mechanism. Asami removes that. **What stands in their place is a person who is not doing anything in the transactional sense, and it reads as profoundly more dangerous than any technique, because techniques have limits and this does not feel like it does.**
> The Aether signature shifts. Where normal output has directionality, **the Asami signature has no vector.** It radiates the way heat radiates from a fire: not aimed, not shaped, not deployed. **Present as a property of what is standing there.**
>
> Observers with Tengan sight describe the Fate threads around a practitioner in Asami as **having gone still. Not locked. Still.** *The way threads go still when they have already arrived at their correct position and have nothing left to pull toward.*

### What Asami does

**All techniques become native** · Every Discipline, Work-expression and Form becomes an extension of what the practitioner actually is rather than what they have learned to perform. **The mechanical gap between intent and execution collapses. The True Self does not translate. It acts.**
**Obsession Force cannot find purchase** · Obsession Force corrupts by attaching to the gap between what a person is and what they believe they must be. **In Asami, that gap does not exist. There is nothing to attach to.**
**The Shingan reads without filter** · An Meigan practitioner in Asami does not read the hidden structure and then decide what to do with it. **The reading and the response are the same motion.**
**The aura becomes a law** · Not a projected aura — a quality of being. **The pressure of being in proximity to something that is exactly what it is, and the dissonance that creates in anything that is not.**

### Integration

Most Moto who achieve Asami do not maintain it as a separate state. **They integrate it.**
The True Nature and the cultivated self reach equilibrium rather than alternating. The Shingan is both a trained instrument and a native extension. The Hataraki is both a duty inherited and a truth embodied. **An integrated Moto is not in Asami. They are not out of Asami. They are past the distinction.** The floor and the room are the same architecture, and what they do with it is finally, for the first time, entirely their own choice **rather than either the bloodline's assignment or the soul's rebellion against it.**
> **The Work is not that you saw it. The Work is that you returned.**
> — *Bara Moto*

### The cost

**Asami is not free. Nothing that honest ever is.**
The trained identity acts as armour as much as purpose. It mediates between True Nature and world; it translates the raw self into acceptable, deployable, legible form. **Asami removes the translation.** Every wound the function was quietly managing. Every grief the labour was quietly carrying. Every love and fury and desire the Work kept organised and directed toward correct application. **None of it is organised in Asami. It is there, undeniable, the way a first thought is undeniable before the second thought arrives to correct it.**
> **The greatest danger is not what Asami does to opponents. It is what it does to the practitioner afterward.**
>
> Returning to the functional self — picking the cultivated identity back up, re-engaging the Fate Ledger, resuming the Work after having been, even briefly, entirely themselves — is described uniformly as one of the hardest things any Moto has ever done. **Not because the state is addictive. Because returning to anything less feels like deliberate diminishment.**

### The Shallow-Seen Unburied

*The most dangerous misapplication: the practitioner who enters the state and mistakes the absence of function for permission.*
**The True Nature is not gentle by default. It is true**, which means it carries the full unmediated weight of everything the practitioner is, including every excess, every rage, every hunger the cultivated self was keeping lawful.
A practitioner who **discharges rather than inhabits** has not achieved Asami. They have achieved its Obsession Force inversion, which the Moto scholars referred to only as **the Shallow-Seen Unburied.** *They did not formally name it because they considered it too dangerous to name.*
> The difference from outside can be invisible for the first several seconds. Both produce the same signature shift. Both produce the same stillness in the threads. **What separates them is the direction the practitioner's attention moves. Asami looks outward, at the world, at what is actually here. The Unburied looks inward, at itself, at what it has always been denied, at the gap it is finally free to fill.**

## Part Two · Gisei

*The Offering · The Burning Floor · The Self That Was Spent*
> You found what you are. **Now: how much of it are you willing to lose so the rest can burn bright enough to matter?**
> — *Unattributed. Found scratched into the wall of the Kurenai meditation hall. Date unknown*

### What it is

**Asami reveals the True Nature. Gisei spends it.**
The practitioner, having found what they are underneath every cultivated layer, discovers that **what they are has fuel value**, and that burning it produces output exceeding anything the intact self could generate.
> **Gisei is not a higher tier of Asami.** It is Asami's terminal expression: the state the True Nature enters when the practitioner decides that **the True Nature's survival matters less than what it can accomplish in the time it has left.**

### The mechanism of sacrifice

Gisei operates through **Oblation**, the same Wellspring that sustains Moetana. Where Moetana trades biological lifespan for duration, **Gisei trades identity for intensity.** The currency differs; the exchange principle is the same, and the total is conserved.
| What can be sacrificed | What it costs |
|---|---|
| **Memories** | A specific memory, permanently surrendered. **Not suppressed. Not forgotten in the recoverable sense. Erased from the Core's identity layer as though it never existed.** *The memory of a meal produces a flicker. The memory of a child's first word produces a detonation* |
| **Bonds** | A relationship severed at the Attraction Layer. **Not abandoned. Severed.** They remember the person existed and can describe the relationship accurately. **They cannot feel it.** What it was worth while it was alive becomes raw density available for immediate use |
| **Capacities** | A faculty permanently relinquished — a Shingan variant, a trained Discipline, a sense, the Dexterity in one hand. **It does not fail or degrade. It ceases to exist in the architecture, and its absence converts to power** |
| **Traits** | A crystallised Trait dissolved. **A Trait that took five years of combat experience to crystallise produces five years' worth of compressed identity as fuel** |

> **The Plane of Fate reads sincerity. It cannot be fooled.** A practitioner who offers something they were already willing to lose produces nothing meaningful in return.
>
> A practitioner who offers something that **tears a hole in who they are** produces output disproportionate to the Essence physics involved. *The Plane does not cheat the exchange. It does not need to. The practitioner's own grief at the loss is the amplifier.*

### What it looks like

In Asami the practitioner is quiet, present in the room the way weather is present. **In Gisei the quiet burns.** The signature, which radiated without direction, **contracts inward** as sacrificed material converts. *The practitioner appears to dim before they brighten: the first sacrifice draws the light in, and the conversion sends it back out harder than it arrived.*
The Shingan, if still present, changes. **The iris does not glow brighter. It simplifies.** A Kamigan at full expression carries a mandala of interlocking judgment-lines. **A Kamigan in Gisei may carry three. Two. One.** *Each simplified line is a Trait, a capacity, a memory that is no longer there, and each absence is visible in the eye that used to hold it.*
> Observers with Tengan sight describe the Fate threads around a Gisei practitioner as **burning. Not metaphorically.** The threads that in Asami went still are now consuming themselves, shortening, brightening, **and the light they produce as they burn is the output the practitioner is using to do whatever they decided was worth the cost.**

### What it produces

**Not stat amplification. Not a multiplier.** The conversion of irreplaceable personal material into Hataraki authority the practitioner's baseline architecture could never have generated.
**Impossible techniques become executable** · A Form requiring Stage XII can be performed by a Stage VIII practitioner if the sacrifice is sufficient. **The Plane does not check Temperance. It checks payment.**
**Authority exceeds standing** · Works can be administered above rank, because the sacrifice confers temporary authority. *A practitioner who has given up their own Meigan to deliver a single blow has demonstrated, in a way the Plane cannot deny, that the blow matters more than the eye. The Plane grants the blow accordingly.*
**Presence becomes environmental** · The air thickens. Fate threads respond to the practitioner's intent rather than to ambient causal structure. **The room belongs to them in a way that is not a Domain and is not a technique. It is the weight of what they have given up pressing against everything that has not.**

### The cost

**Everything sacrificed is permanently gone.** Not dormant. Not recoverable through healing, meditation, Wellspring restoration or time. **The Core's identity layer has been physically edited**, and what the Plane accepts, it keeps.
A practitioner who sacrifices the memory of their mother cannot rebuild it. Others can tell them about her; they can construct an intellectual understanding from external testimony. **They will never feel it. The feeling was the memory, and the memory was the sacrifice.**
A practitioner who sacrifices their Meigan can never reopen it. **The growth site no longer exists.** *Their eye is a normal eye forever. It sees what anyone's eye sees, and no more.*
> **Gisei practitioners accumulate absences.** After sustained use they are measurably less complete than they were. **Not weaker in the conventional sense. Less.** Fewer memories, fewer bonds, fewer capacities, fewer Traits.
>
> What remains is **a person-shaped structure that fights with the accumulated output of everything it used to be but can no longer remember being.**

## The Relationship

**Every Gisei practitioner was first an Asami practitioner, because you cannot spend what you have not found.** A practitioner who attempts Gisei without Asami — who tries to sacrifice aspects of the cultivated self — produces nothing. **The Plane does not accept the cultivated self as currency because the cultivated self was constructed, not grown. It has assembly value, not identity value. The exchange rate is zero.**
> **The deepest cruelty of Gisei: Asami is the first time the practitioner meets themselves. Gisei is the decision to sell what they just met.**
>
> *They find the floor beneath every room, and then they break the floor apart for timber because someone they love is freezing and the floor will burn.*
Most Moto who achieve Asami never touch Gisei. They integrate the revelation and live as whole, authentic people carrying the Work and the self as a single architecture. **This is the correct outcome. This is what the bloodline was designed to produce.**
> **Gisei exists for the ones who find that being whole is not enough.** That the moment requires something the whole person cannot provide. That the only resource left, after everything else has been tried, is the person themselves, **and the only question is how much of that person they are willing to spend.**

---

## The Terminal State

A practitioner who has spent too much does not die. Not immediately. **What they become is harder to name and harder to watch.**
The memories are gone. The bonds are gone. The Traits are gone. The Shingan may be gone. What remains is a body fighting with the accumulated authority of everything it surrendered, **wielding power that should belong to a person who no longer exists because the person spent themselves to acquire it.**
> They are not empty the way Nirvana's terminal state is empty. **Nirvana's practitioner is full but cannot feel. Gisei's practitioner can feel. They feel everything. They simply have nothing left to feel it about**, because every memory and bond that would have given the feeling a referent has been fed to the fire.
The terminal practitioner wanders. **They fight when something crosses their path because fighting is the only context in which the power they purchased has a use.** They are kind to strangers because kindness costs nothing and they have learned the price of everything. **They cannot go home because they cannot remember where home was, and they cannot build a new one because the Attraction Layer architecture that forms bonds has been spent and cannot reform.**
> They are, in the most precise sense of the word, **a sacrifice. Not a person who made a sacrifice. A sacrifice that used to be a person.**
>
> *The Plane of Fate does not fear the Asami state. It recognises it. It fears the practitioner who learned to trade in their own bones. Not because the trade is unjust. Because the Plane must honour it, and what the Plane honours, it cannot return.*
>
> — First Conclave fragment, heavily damaged, date uncertain

## Archival Note

Catalogued as **Moto-origin states rather than disciplines.** Asami is accessible to any soul meeting the depth and authenticity threshold and is **not Moto-exclusive.** Gisei requires prior Asami achievement and runs on the same Oblation mechanism as Moetana, **trading identity rather than lifespan.**
> The two costs are not interchangeable. **Moetana's practitioner dies younger. Gisei's practitioner dies less complete.**
> **Three answers to one question: what does a soul do when it has found what it truly is?**
>
> **Asami inhabits it. Moetana channels it through the body at the cost of duration. Gisei burns it at the cost of itself.**
>
> *All three are honest. None of them are safe.*

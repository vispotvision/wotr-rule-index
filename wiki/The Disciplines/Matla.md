## The Material Covenant

The Material Force is among the oldest recognized disciplines of the Second Epoch and one of the foundational Arts upon which many later systems were built. It governs **the material covenant**, through which all things possess form, weight, location, durability, sequence, Essence-density, and continuity within existence.
A practitioner does not merely move substance. They command density, weight, texture, resistance, shape, boundary, physical memory, Essence embodiment, anti-structure polarity, and the coherence of physical systems.
At lesser attainments it resembles advanced matter shaping. At greater attainments it becomes **authority over the laws that matter obeys.** At its highest expression the discipline approaches **Material Totality**, in which matter, space, Essence, localized sequence, anti-form, and physical law are understood as a single unified structure.

## The Practices of Malta

### 1 · Form — , mass, density, substance

Governs physical substance directly. Stone, iron, bone, flesh, dust, crystal, wood, soil, armour, blood, and forged materials all fall beneath its authority.
**Functions** · Increase or reduce density · harden or soften material · compress loose matter into unified mass · reshape terrain, weapons, armour, and bodies · reinforce one's own physical form · restore fractured structures · convert debris into barriers, pillars, chains, blades, or pressure constructs.
**In combat** · Armour may become too heavy to lift. Soil may vitrify into black glass. Dust may compress into cutting edges. Flesh may harden like layered stone. Weapons may buckle beneath their own weight.
**Limit** · Requires existing substance. **It cannot freely create matter from nothing.**

### 2 · Time — sequence, aging, momentum, decay

Does not govern universal time. It governs **material sequence**, the progression through which physical matter experiences change. It does not rewrite history; it alters the temporal pressure contained within matter itself.
**Functions** · Accelerate rust, decay, erosion, cooling, burning, or decomposition · delay collapse or physical failure · suspend reactions mid-process · extend the interval before damage manifests · age structures, weapons, armour, or barriers · restore matter to a prior state if sufficient material memory remains.
**In combat** · A blade may corrode in its wielder's grasp. A collapsing tower may remain standing for moments longer. A wound may refuse to open. A fortress wall may weather centuries in a breath.
**Limit** · Functions best upon matter rather than souls. It cannot freely reverse death, alter fate, or restore what has lost all material memory.

### 3 · Anti-Matter — inverse substance, anti-form, negation

> Among the most feared manifestations of the discipline. Within Second Epoch doctrine, anti-matter does not refer solely to scientific antiparticles. It is the **inverse material signature** of existence, the opposing covenant that denies form, mass, and physical recognition.
>
> **It is not emptiness. It is matter's contradiction.**
**Functions** · Generate anti-structure against existing matter · cancel density, cohesion, or mass · create annihilation pressure between matter and inverse signature · corrode barriers through opposition of form · introduce anti-form into material memory · dissolve structures without burning or breaking them.
**In combat** · A shield ceases to function as a shield. Stone hollows from within. Armour loses its agreement with weight and collapses into dust. **A blade may lose the reality of its edge before the weapon itself fails.**
**Limit** · Notoriously unstable. Misuse rebounds into the practitioner, producing anti-form scars, hollow bones, missing flesh, or Soul Crystal inversion.

### 4 · Essence — materialized Essence, energy-body interface

Governs the threshold where spiritual power acquires physical consequence. Most disciplines treat Essence as fuel. The Material Force treats Essence as **substance capable of weight, density, pressure, and structure.**
**Functions** · Condense Essence into tangible constructs · grant physical hardness to Aether Shells · reinforce barriers through Essence density · shape Essence into chains, armour, seals, anchors, or weapons · force hostile Essence into rigid states · strengthen the body through materialized pathways.
**In combat** · An aura may become heavy enough to pin opponents. Essence may be forged into armour. Soul Crystal flow may be weighed down. Hostile energy may be rendered brittle and shattered.
**Limit** · Excessive condensation risks internal calcification, pathway rigidity, Aether Shell damage, and Soul Crystal fractures.

### 5 · Space — position, distance, boundary, relation

Governs space only insofar as space relates to matter. Not supreme spatial authority. **Material-spatial authority.** The branch determines where matter may exist in relation to other matter.
**Functions** · Increase or reduce distance between objects · lock matter in place · compress battlefield space · alter trajectories · reinforce thresholds and boundaries · deny passage across material limits · shift material position across short ranges.
**In combat** · A sword may never reach its target because distance refuses to close. A spear may curve away from its path. A doorway may become impossible to cross. Two objects may collide violently when separation is collapsed.
**Limit** · Relies upon material reference points, and performs poorly within voids, dream-realms, conceptual domains, or against entities lacking stable material existence.

### 6 · Totality — integrated material system authority

Not truly a sixth branch. **It is the convergence of all others.** Matter, Time, Anti-Matter, Essence, Space, Physical Law. When unified, the practitioner ceases to command isolated phenomena and instead governs the complete material condition of a target, structure, battlefield, or domain.
**Functions** · Read a target as a complete material system · alter body, armour, Essence, sequence, space, and resistance simultaneously · attack total physical coherence · restore structures through multiple laws at once · establish zones governed by singular material logic · force contradictory matter into unified alignment.
> Armour grows heavy. Essence hardens. Space thickens. Weapons age. Movement slows. Boundaries lock.
>
> **The practitioner does not attack the enemy. The enemy's material existence becomes the battlefield.**
**Limit** · Demands immense comprehension and stability. Failure results in Material Rejection, Soul Crystal overload, or catastrophic self-collapse.

## The Casting Engine

### Manifestus Command Sequence

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Material Recognition** | The practitioner identifies the material truth of the target: composition, structure, weight, resistance, governing laws |
| **Branch Selection** | Matter, Time, Anti-Matter, Essence, Space, or Totality is invoked. Advanced users may combine branches at significant risk |
| **Flux Imposition** | Aether is pressed into the target's material field as **command pressure rather than raw force** |
| **Manifestus Lock** | The chosen authority becomes active. Density shifts. Sequence bends. Essence condenses. Anti-form spreads. Material coherence changes |
| **Inertia Backlash** | Matter resists command. Recoil travels through body, Soul Crystal, bones, muscles, and Aether Shell |

### Manifestus Harmonization

> The novice commands matter as an object.
> The adept commands matter through contact.
> The master commands matter through recognition.
> The sovereign commands matter through law.
> The Totality Architect commands matter as a complete system.
Temperance determines whether the Soul Crystal can survive the contradiction of commanding matter while being composed of matter. The central question of the discipline remains: **can one command physical law without becoming subject to the command?**

## Soul Crystal Architecture

*The Manifestus Crucible Matrix.* A Crystal aligned with the Material Force develops a forge-like internal structure. The Essence Core thickens into layered geometric strata while the Aether Shell forms concentric weight-bearing rings, and six principal channels emerge.
| Channel | Carries |
|---|---|
| **Mass** | matter, density, hardness, weight |
| **Sequence** | aging, decay, momentum, temporal progression |
| **Inverse** | anti-form and anti-matter pressure |
| **Essence** | materialized Essence |
| **Boundary** | position, distance, thresholds |
| **Totality** | integration of all branches |

> **Assets** · Exceptional physical control · powerful defensive capability · battlefield shaping authority · strong anti-siege utility · high compatibility with Arts and Domain-Weaving · effective against terrain, structures, armour, weapons, and Essence constructs.
> **Liabilities** · Severe physical backlash · Soul Crystal pressure accumulation · self-calcification risk · Anti-Matter instability · temporal self-aging · Aether Shell fractures · Totality overload.

### Material Rejection Collapse

The primary failure mode. It occurs when too many contradictory commands are imposed without preserving personal material identity. The Crystal receives incompatible instructions:
> Harden. Move. Age. Erase. Condense. Displace. Remain whole.
> When self and target cease to remain distinct, **the practitioner becomes part of the command sequence.**
>
> **Symptoms** · Mineralized skin · excessively dense bones · granular blood · calcified Essence pathways · spatial distortion · accelerated aging or regression · anti-form lesions · Soul Crystal fractures.
>
> Extreme cases produce a **Manifestus Husk** — a statue-like living remnant incapable of distinguishing itself from the surrounding material world.

## Key Figures

**Shaka Al, the Material Hand** · One of the most renowned practitioners of the late Second Epoch. He transformed terrain into weapons, burdened armies beneath the weight of their own armour, and treated the battlefield as an extension of his body. His style was direct, oppressive, and relentless. He did not overpower enemies through spectacle. **He made the world itself too heavy to resist.**
**Revari Zettai, High Sovereign of Material Aspect Sovereignty** · Though not strictly a Material Force practitioner, Revari's royal authority demonstrated one of the highest known expressions of material principle. His dominion over Form, Essence, Space, Existence, and localized Time revealed how material law could ascend into sovereign bloodline authority. Among Aegorian scholars he remains a central comparative figure.
**The Totality Architects, the World-Weighted Few** · A rare class who achieve Totality integration. They do not merely attack opponents. **They determine the conditions under which physical existence is permitted.** Most perish through Material Rejection Collapse. Those who survive often become fortress-builders, calamity wardens, or architects of reconstruction following great disasters.

## Historical Importance

**Origin** · Early Second Epoch, with theoretical foundations tracing back to First Epoch studies of material law.
**During the Age of Aegor** · Widely employed in siege warfare, fortress construction, territorial stabilization, monster containment, and martial reinforcement traditions.
**Post-Calamity** · Following the devastation wrought across Aegor, practitioners became indispensable in rebuilding roads, walls, bridges, settlements, mines, and defensive infrastructure.
**Current status** · Rare but strategically invaluable. Kingdoms and city-states frequently classify practitioners as national assets due to their ability to restore, reinforce, or dismantle large-scale structures.

## Systemic Connections

> **Zettai Material Aspect Sovereignty.** The Material Force shares conceptual territory with royal authorities governing Form, Essence, Space, Existence, and localized Time. The distinction is fundamental: **Zettai Sovereignty is inherited. The Material Force is learned.**
**Aether Shell Reinforcement** · The discipline relies heavily upon the Shell as a pressure frame. Advanced practitioners reinforce themselves through density alignment, Essence materialization, and resistance harmonization.
**Drafts and Alchemy** · Draft practitioners often stabilize Material Force reactions through Parun Glyph systems. Alchemists employ its principles to preserve unstable substances, suspend decay, create anti-form seals, and harden Essence-infused compounds.
**Conjunction** · Allows branches to merge with other disciplines. Matter and Fire may produce living stone. Essence and Space may create weighted barriers. **Anti-Matter and Dissolution may generate catastrophic erasure phenomena.**
**Arts Tradition** · The body is the first material object a practitioner must command. For this reason the discipline remains deeply rooted within the Arts tradition; movement, weight, inertia, and resistance become instruments of authority.
**Materia Primordia** · The deepest resonance. Monolithion grants endurance. Chthonica grants depth. Fixatio grants stability. Dissolution grants structural undoing. Judicium grants rightful order. Together they form the traditional Wellspring foundation.
> **Scale assessment.** Material Force practitioners often appear less destructive than elemental specialists. **This is deceptive.** Their influence is measured through pressure, mass displacement, structural failure, battlefield control, and systemic alteration. Anti-Matter and Totality users frequently produce anomalous readings because they affect the conditions of material existence itself.

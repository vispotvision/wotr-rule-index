# Kami no Kobushi — The God Fist

**KAMI NO KOBUSHI** (神の拳 · Kami no Kobushi) (bloodline and personal art, one Anchor across seven Labor-Forms)

*Scholars' term: Kami no Kobushi, or the Labor-Forms of the Anchor.*

> On the tourney sand at Ogawara, in front of four thousand people in red and gold, the Kharven prizefighter Alard Coster, nineteen stone and undefeated in four cities, hit a Moto cadet in the chest with everything he owned. The cadet had driven his spear butt into the sand a moment earlier and stopped moving. Coster's wrist went first, then his elbow, then his shoulder, in that order, and the sand under the boy's feet sank one hand's breadth and stayed there. The cadet bowed and apologized for the inconvenience.

They will show you that sand at Ogawara, roped off at a copper a look, and they will tell you, with relish, that the prizefighter's shoulder went last.

**The hook.** They plant their feet, and the ground and the law of the world agree the fight is already decided. Hit them and you have hit yourself, and when they hit you, the whole ground comes with it.

- **THE ANCHOR!** The bearer stops, plants, and now stands on law instead of ground; a push comes back entire.
- **HŌKAI NO KOBUSHI!** The Ruin Fist, the Old Crack, finds the oldest crack in your ward and brings the whole thing down from there.
- **SEIJŌ NO KOBUSHI!** The Purity Fist, Back to Your Own Shape, breaks you back into your proper shape, and false forms fall apart on its ground.
- **TAMASHII NO KOBUSHI!** The Spirit Fist strikes the seam between life and death, and the wound lands on both sides.
- **TOKI NO KOBUSHI!** The Sequence Fist starts slowly and arrives first anyway.
- **AKASHI NO KOBUSHI!** The Revelation Fist names what you truly are and hits that, through every borrowed ward.
- **HAKARI NO KOBUSHI!** The Scales Fist, or Balance Fist, stands where two forces cancel and hits you with both.
- **ZENTAI NO KOBUSHI!** The Total Fist runs all six at once, and it cannot be taught, only arrived at.
- **PILLAR NAIL!** Holds a coordinate for thirty heartbeats, and nobody within 40 m teleports, flicker-steps, fate-skips or hurries time.
- **IRON VERDICT DROP!** and **FLOOR OF THE YET-TO-BE!** The great Works of a bearer coupled to the Plane of Fate.

**Cost.** Two percent of full reserve to set the Anchor and 0.5 percent a turn to hold it; 5 percent a Labor-Form strike, 8 committed, and 30 for the Total Fist, which also welds you for good to the spot you delivered it from, until a Tengan adept loosens you.  
**Counter.** Put tension on the Anchor with a pull, lift, hook or drag; fight in a confined space; or sever the coupling by displacement, Silence or ground that will not hold. Nothing prepared against one doctrine answers the Total Fist.

**What nobody knows.** Whether its originator, held in the Plane of Fate as living foundation, feels it when one of the forms is used.

**Kami no Kobushi · The Seven Labor-Forms of the Anchor**
> *"Bara gave us seven ways to work. Gimbzo found seven ways to refuse."*
>
> *Oral tradition, surviving First Conclave fragment*
>
> Each of the seven Works manifests differently through the God Fist's architecture. The spine never changes, **Aether falls, position is law, the verdict precedes the blow**, but the nature of what is being anchored shifts with each doctrine. A practitioner who has mastered all seven is one fighter still. **They are one Anchor who has learned seven different things worth holding.**

---

**How it really works.** One spine runs under all seven forms, and it is the line the old fragments repeat: Aether falls, position is law, the verdict precedes the blow. Fixatio seats the fighter on a coordinate as a thermoset seats a bond, and every other Wellspring in a doctrine then decides what that coordinate holds. The physics is effective mass and impedance matching at contact, with fracture at a flaw already present, and each of the Works below lays its own labor on top. Sect brawlers in the north brag of knowing the Ruin Fist, and their broken wrists say otherwise.

The charge is the stopping. The Anchor has chosen ground and stopped: a weapon butt driven in, palms flat to the earth, ground taking a weight it did not have. Its Aether falls and never flares, a Body-rooted Hataraki dialect that locks inward and radiates as downward weight. The Residue (what a working leaves in the ground) is a lasting coordinate, so the ground remembers, and ground an Anchor held is worked ground and deafens Harmonists (listeners to Aether). The coupling must be set before contact, every time, and that is the first thing any opponent should be told and the last thing they generally are.

A blow carries the mass of everything coupled to the fist at contact, so a rooted stance hits far harder than an arm alone, and a body coupled to something immovable hits with that thing's mass. That is effective mass. The law is conservation of momentum with effective mass set by the coupling, and impedance matching at the interface deciding how much energy crosses and how much reflects. A body that does not accelerate returns the whole applied force, so an attacker striking a perfectly anchored defender receives their own blow. Transmission is fixed by what the two bodies are before contact, so the exchange is decided before it lands, and speed is not in the equation. Everything fails at a pre-existing flaw (Griffith's criterion), so a strike that finds the oldest flaw need only load it and need not supply the failure. The limit is honest: nothing has infinite mass. Effective mass is bounded by what the coupling carries before it fails, so everything that beats an Anchor beats the coupling and leaves the man for later. Nothing is granted from outside, either, only what the bearer has become able to carry.

There is one Anchor across seven Labor-Forms, one for each Work of Hataraki, and each form runs its own five or six Wellsprings, listed under each form below. Fixatio, the Binding Flame (Materia), makes the unmoved configuration the only reachable one, and this is the Anchor. Fractura, the Breaking Point, gives sight of a structure's flaws and the precision to load exactly one. Cataclysm, the Shattered Law, has a confined flame front couple to a shock and change kind. Basilithe, the Stone Will, stiffens ground so it will not accept a borrowed rhythm. Petralon, the Foundation Stone, routes every load into compression, accepting creep over any tensile crack. The glyph is Order, for the verdict before the blow, laid under Boundary, for the coordinate that will not move. The boundary is what the practitioner's position is a property of: the coordinate is pinned to something other than the floor, so the coupling is a shared coordinate and more than a contact. The Shell (the Crystal's outer skin) carries the load path, the Attraction Layer holds the coordinate, and the Essence Core holds the doctrine as a Trait. Developmental Tier is Radiant at Transcendence, Sovereign from Invocation, where Trait law and Domain law merge.

Pushed, the push comes back entire. Struck from, the blow carries the coupling's mass. Each bearer's figures come off their own card; there is no Stage floor, since this is a bloodline and personal art and the lineage decides who can use it. The largest Works run fewer than four to a reserve.

Two things should not be confused, and the sect brawlers confuse them daily. The Anchor is the Body half: mass, footing and coupling, hard but drillable, and a cadet can have it, as Alard Coster's shoulder learned. The Labor-Forms are the other half, and each is a grip on one Work of [[The Disciplines/Schools and Bloodline Arts/Hataraki — The Works of the Plane of Fate/Hataraki — The Works of the Plane of Fate|Hataraki]], which is to say a real hold on what a blow depends on, and nobody has ever taken that bare. The shares of reserve below are what a form costs a bearer whose grip is already theirs, on a target that offers the form something to close on: an old crack, a false shape, an unpaid debt. Most bearers hold one form across a lifetime. A bearer reaching past their grip, or for a result that has no way of happening, pays what anyone pays for a pull on Fate, and gets an ordinary punch for it. This is a Body and Fate art, a blow that always arrives, with the holes that pairing leaves: anyone who gets a hook on it before the plant, or reads the form coming, is fighting a fighter and not a verdict.

Every inherited failure is a way in. Fixatio's irreversibility leaves an Anchor who cannot un-anchor. Fractura's self-inspection spares no bearer. Cataclysm's reflected overpressure returns the blast to one who will not step aside, so a confined space is a bad place to refuse. Petralon's tensile exposure means that pulled, it parts: a hook, a lift or a drag does what no blow does. The form's own failure is Obsession: each form has its corruption (Ruin that enjoys the waiting, Purity that sterilizes what does not match), recognition curdled into fixation at rising soul-cost, a Coherence fault that ends careers while winning. Waste leaves the Shell as heat and goes down as a shock through the ground, 20 to 30 percent at Master; coupled to the Plane of Fate, η (the share of a spend that arrives as work) reaches 0.94 and the Plane reclaims most of the rest. The inner-citadel schools read refusal as the only sovereignty and treat stance and ground as scenery; but the refusal lives in the feet, and an Anchor who refuses only in the heart has an effective mass of about two kilograms.

## I · Hōkai no Kobushi: The Ruin Fist

**Work** · Hametsu no Gō, the Work of Ruins

**Type** · Collapse-Fixator · *Anchor what is already breaking and make it break correctly.*

| Wellspring | Its office in the doctrine |
|---|---|
| **Fixatio** | Pins the opponent's structural failure at its exact point of origin, preventing recovery |
| **Coagulatio** | Loads the descent with collapse-weight rather than mass-weight; the blow falls like an ending, not a hit |
| **Cataclysm** | Accelerates the deterioration already present in the target's ward, body, or Essence structure |
| **Fractura** | Identifies and widens pre-existing fracture lines in material, metaphysical, or soul-based defenses |
| **Nihiloth** | Strips the opponent's resistance of its sense that resistance is meaningful |

The Ruin Fist finds destruction that was already coming and makes it arrive on schedule. Where other strikers try to break a thing from the outside, a Hōkai practitioner reads the structural rot already present in their opponent and plants their heel on top of it. **The blow locates the wall's oldest crack and declares that crack the verdict.**

**Core principle** · Essence reads decay first; the practitioner does not commit until the Fate Ledger has ranked which of the opponent's structures is already failing. Fracture lines are coordinates: every ward, every bone-reinforcement, every Wellspring brace has a point of origin, and Fixatio seats on that point, not on the surface. And collapse runs in sequence.
- ****In practice****
  Against a heavily warded opponent, the practitioner waits *diagnostically* until Fractura maps the oldest seam in the ward's structure. The strike lands there, one centimeter deep, and the ward comes apart from the inside.

  Against a Wellspring-brace fighter, Nihiloth is run first as a quiet undercurrent, slowly eroding the opponent's conviction that their reinforcement is holding. By the time the overhead drops, the brace believes it has already lost.

  Against a Domain user, the practitioner plants inside the Domain itself, Fixatio declaring *their coordinate* more lawfully seated than the Domain's own architecture. The Domain stays standing, **simply out-ranked.**
> **Philosophical tension.** The Ruin Fist tempts its user toward cruelty of timing: waiting so long for the perfect collapse vector that battles drag into torture. Lawful Ruin ends what cannot remain. Obsession Force Ruin **enjoys the waiting.**

## II · Seijō no Kobushi: The Purity Fist

**Work** · Junketsu no Gō, the Work of Purity

**Type** · Foundation-Fixator · *Anchor what is rightfully standing and refuse to let it be defiled.*

**Wellsprings** · **Fixatio** declares the practitioner's structural integrity a lawfully protected coordinate, so corruption effects must prove authority to proceed · **Petralon** reinforces the body's load-bearing axis as consecrated infrastructure · **Basilithe** establishes the battlefield's ground as sanctified substrate, and impure techniques lose purchase on it · **Benediction** turns each blow into a corrective act, not violence but restoration of proper form · **Judicium** ranks what is structurally sound against what is metaphysically compromised before the fist commits.

The Purity Fist treats the body as a sacred structure and every exchange as an act of correction. It does not strike to wound. **It strikes to restore proper arrangement**, which sometimes means the target is broken back into the shape reality intended for them. Where the Ruin style waits for collapse, the Purity style enforces standard.

**Core principle** · The body is the first and most important consecrated structure; before Purity can be imposed outward, Petralon and Benediction must already have run inward through spine, heel, gut, and knuckle. A blow from Seijō does not damage, it *corrects*: bones return to where they should be, over-extended wards collapse back to their proper boundaries, borrowed Essences return to their original seats. And Basilithe-treated ground does not merely support the practitioner; **it rejects techniques built on metaphysical dishonesty.** Illusion, doppelgänger substitution, borrowed identity, and false-flag Wellspring use all degrade the moment the foot plants.
- ****In practice****
  Against a curse-stacked fighter, the practitioner does not counter the curses directly. They plant, run Basilithe into the ground, and let the consecrated substrate drain the curses' purchase passively while the fight continues.

  Against a regenerating opponent, each blow delivered under Benediction's law restores the target to *their true structural state*, which, if their regeneration relies on Wellspring-borrowed life rather than native recovery, means the regeneration is classified as false form and the blow lands without triggering it.

  Against a shapeshifter or doppelgänger, Judicium runs the ledger against which form is lawful. Fixatio pins that form. The strike lands on the real one.
> **Philosophical tension.** Fanaticism: the conviction that one's own standard of correctness is the only standard. Lawful Purity restores. Obsession Force Purity **sterilizes**, refusing to permit any form that does not match its template.

## III · Tamashii no Kobushi: The Spirit Fist

**Work** · Seirei no Gō, the Work of Spirit

**Type** · Soul-Fixator · *Anchor the line between what lives and what has ended, and strike across it.*

**Wellsprings** · **Fixatio** pins the opponent's soul-coordinate, preventing flight, transition, or post-mortem escape · **Coagulatio** loads the blow with soul-weight rather than body-weight · **Mortalis** governs the passage between life and death, letting strikes register on both sides of the threshold simultaneously · **Anima Spirare** reads the life-breath signature, identifying where the soul sits heaviest in the body · **Contrition** applies lawful consequence to souls carrying unpaid spiritual debt.
> The Spirit Fist fights on two floors at once, and **neither floor is its real target.** The real target is the passage between them. A Tamashii practitioner does not try to kill the body or destroy the soul separately. They grip the threshold between those states and strike through it, delivering judgment that neither life nor death can contest.
>
> It is the art of making death lawful before the body has finished falling.
**Core principle** · The soul is a coordinate too. Run through Anima Spirare's reading, Fixatio can locate where a soul sits most densely inside flesh and pin *that* coordinate. Mortality is a boundary, not a wall: a blow delivered at the soul-seam registers as a living wound and a posthumous one simultaneously, preventing resurrection, soul-flight, and death-cheat techniques from activating cleanly. And Contrition makes debt legible: souls carrying broken vows, ancestor debts, or stolen life are heavier, slower to transit, and more vulnerable to a Spirit Fist verdict.
- ****In practice****
  Against an immortal fighter, the practitioner does not attempt to damage harder. Fixatio seats on the soul-coordinate, Mortalis opens the threshold, and the strike delivers its verdict *to the part that does not regenerate.*

  Against a ghost, echo, or revenant, an opponent already partially on the other side of Mortalis, the Spirit Fist reaches across and hits them on the living side, where they are most exposed.

  Against an opponent relying on soul-transference as an escape, Fixatio pins the destination coordinate before the transfer can complete.
> **Philosophical tension.** Soul-hoarding: pinning things that should be allowed to pass, keeping opponents alive because the ledger says their debt is not fully paid. Lawful Spirit governs passage. Obsession Force Spirit **refuses to let anything go.**

## IV · Toki no Kobushi: The Sequence Fist

**Work** · Jikan no Shigoto, the Work of Temporality

**Type** · Sequence-Fixator · *Anchor the next instant and make it the only instant available.*

**Wellsprings** · **Fixatio** locks the practitioner's position inside the sequence of events, declaring their current instant the lawfully established present · **Monolithion** ratifies the blow as a causal sentence that the timeline must process before moving forward · **Cymorath** governs temporal transition and sequence shift, reading the gap between instants · **Anamnesis** reads the opponent's combat history as a causal chain · **Transmutatio** alters the sequence weight of a strike, making a slow blow arrive as though it always arrived first.

**The Sequence Fist moves more correctly than its opponents, and no faster.** Every blow is delivered at the moment the sequence ratifies it as already having occurred, and the opponent's timeline must process the strike before it can do anything else. It is the art of making the present moment a door that only opens inward, and the practitioner is already on the other side.

**Core principle** · Sequence is a coordinate. Causality is a ledger, and Anamnesis does not predict: it identifies which earlier decision made *this moment* the only possible outcome, and strikes that outcome. And **slowness is a weapon**: Transmutatio resequences a blow's temporal weight, so the overhead that begins slowly arrives as though it was always the first thing that happened, because inside the locked sequence, it was.
- ****In practice****
  Against a Tengan-line Moto using convergence-reading, the practitioner locks the surrounding sequence before the Tengan adept can claim the next instant. **The adept's foresight sees a present that has already been ratified as closed.**

  Against a temporal-acceleration fighter, Fixatio simply refuses the acceleration's claim to the next instant. The fighter moves fast inside their own sequence and finds the practitioner's coordinate has already filled the space they were accelerating into.

  Against anyone relying on reaction speed, the practitioner does not need to be faster. Monolithion ratifies the blow as causal sentence, and the opponent's nervous system must process it as *already having happened* before it can respond.
> **Philosophical tension.** Recursion: locking a single instant so thoroughly that the sequence cannot advance at all, trapping both practitioner and opponent in a frozen causal loop. Lawful Temporality orders sequence. Obsession Force Temporality **imprisons everyone in one wound.**

## V · Akashi no Kobushi: The Revelation Fist

**Work** · Chishiki no Shigoto, the Work of Knowledge

**Type** · Disclosure-Fixator · *Anchor what a thing truly is and strike the truth of it.*

**Wellsprings** · **Fixatio** pins the opponent's revealed true form, preventing re-concealment once named · **Judicium** ranks the hidden structures, which defenses are real, which borrowed, which bluff · **Luminalis** illuminates the Essence signature, making false layers visible as distinct from authentic ones · **Eidolyn** reads the shape of memory and identity underneath the surface presentation · **Aurevane** applies revealed truth as structural pressure.
> The Revelation Fist does not strike the body the opponent presents. It strikes the body the opponent *actually is* underneath every ward, every bluff, every borrowed Wellspring. **Knowledge is the strike itself, far more than preparation for it.**
**Core principle** · What is correctly named cannot hide from what it owes: the more precisely the practitioner has named the opponent's true form, function, and debt, the harder the blow that follows the naming. Defenses are only real if they are native; Luminalis distinguishes what belongs to the opponent from what was borrowed, bought, or stolen, and the blow lands on the gap between them. And the opponent's history is their most honest architecture: the practitioner is not fighting who is standing in front of them, **they are fighting the sum of every choice that produced that person.**
- ****In practice****
  Against a warrior concealing their true Wellspring alignment, Luminalis strips the surface signature, Judicium ranks which layer is genuine, and the blow is delivered against the genuine layer, bypassing the concealment entirely.

  Against a fighter using a borrowed Domain or stolen technique, Fixatio seats on the true-form coordinate and the blow passes through the borrowed structure as though it were not there, **because to the Revelation Fist, it is not.**

  Against an opponent with a deep spiritual debt, Aurevane names the debt aloud and the blow carries the full weight of that debt's accrued interest.
> **Philosophical tension.** Consuming certainty: the need to name everything, rank everything, expose everything, until the practitioner is paralyzed not by ignorance but by total knowledge that has become its own prison. Lawful Knowledge liberates. Obsession Force Knowledge **devours.**

## VI · Hakari no Kobushi: The Scales Fist

**Work** · Baransu no Shigoto, the Work of Balance

**Type** · Reconciliation-Fixator · *Anchor the point where two forces cancel, then strike from it.*

**Wellsprings** · **Fixatio** seats the practitioner at the exact fulcrum between two opposing forces, so displacement requires defeating both simultaneously · **Coagulatio** gathers opposing forces into one bound mass before release · **Manganthra** reads which force in any exchange is overextended and which is starved · **Thaloré** applies binding tension between opposites, making the gap between them a usable surface · **Basilithe** establishes the ground beneath as the settled center of any contested field.

The Scales Fist finds the point in every fight where two forces are pulling against each other and plants there. The practitioner becomes the fulcrum. They do not push one side or the other; they strike from the center at the moment of maximum tension, when both forces are fully committed and neither can correct. **It is the art of letting your enemies' forces load your fist for you.**

**Core principle** · Every conflict has a center of gravity, and Fixatio seats on the center, not the edge. Opposition is leverage: Thaloré binds the tension between competing forces and holds it available as potential energy, and Coagulatio collapses that potential into the descent at the moment of maximum strain. **The fulcrum cannot be moved by either side, because moving it would require agreeing on which force has lawful authority, and at the center, neither does.**
- ****In practice****
  Against a two-fighter coordinated assault, the practitioner plants between them. Both attacks arrive and find the practitioner occupying the exact geometric point where their force vectors cancel. Thaloré binds that cancellation and Coagulatio repurposes it as a single outward verdict.

  Against a fighter oscillating between aggressive and defensive postures, Manganthra reads the oscillation frequency. The blow lands at the transition point, the half-instant where neither posture is fully active.

  Against a Domain locked in conflict with another Domain, the practitioner plants at their boundary. Both Domains treat the coordinate as belonging to the other and do not press it. **The practitioner stands in the gap, untouchable, and strikes outward from it.**
> **Philosophical tension.** Paralysis: becoming so devoted to the fulcrum that the practitioner can no longer act without perfectly balanced justification. Lawful Balance mediates. Obsession Force Balance **refuses to permit necessary imbalance**, even when the world requires it.

## VII · Zentai no Kobushi: The Total Fist

**Work** · Zentai-sei no Hataraki, the Work of Totality

**Type** · Sovereign-Fixator · *Anchor all six Works simultaneously and let the Plane itself deliver the verdict.*

**Wellsprings** · **Fixatio** is the unifying spine, seating the practitioner as a load-bearing coordinate of the Plane itself · **Monolithion** ratifies the blow as a sovereign sentence taking from all subordinate Works at once · **Coagulatio** gathers the cumulative mass of every active Work-layer into the single descent · **Judicium** governs the judicial integration of all six prior doctrines into one ranked, irrefutable verdict · **Materia Primordia** calls on the precondition of all reality's coherence to ratify the blow as a law that predates the opponent.
> The Total Fist is **not a seventh style.** It is what happens when a practitioner has so thoroughly internalized all six prior doctrines that they no longer choose between them. All six Works run simultaneously, each performing its labor in the same instant, and the resulting blow is not a martial technique but a statement of fact delivered by fate itself.
>
> **It cannot be taught as a first discipline. It can only be arrived at.**
**Core principle** · Every prior doctrine is a prerequisite, not a component. The Total Fist does not mix the six styles; it requires the practitioner to have *already become* each of them, so that when Zentai fires, all six are native rather than layered. The Plane does not amplify the practitioner: the practitioner becomes sufficient infrastructure that the Plane extends *through* them the way it extends through Gimbzo, not as a tool but as a continuation of its own labor. And Materia Primordia does not grant power from outside; it ratifies what the practitioner has already established as lawful, **calling on the oldest layer of reality's grammar to confirm that this blow was always going to land.**

### In practice

There is no tactical breakdown for the Total Fist, because it does not have tactics. The practitioner plants, all six Works converge, and the blow falls.
> *"The ground remembered him. The air agreed with him. The blow arrived at the moment before you realized it had been thrown."*
>
> *the only recorded description from outside, First Conclave fragment*
Every counter that exists for the individual six doctrines fails against it, because it is running all six simultaneously. A sequence-counter meets Toki no Kobushi. A ruin-resistance meets Hōkai no Kobushi. A revelation-ward meets Akashi no Kobushi. All six meet at once, **and none of them meet only the one they prepared for.**
> **Cost and consequence.** The Total Fist cannot be used by a practitioner who has not accepted **Coordinate Adhesion** as permanent. Running all six Works through a single planted coordinate in one instant **completes the adhesion.** A practitioner who successfully delivers Zentai no Kobushi is afterward inseparable from that coordinate until loosened by a Tengan adept.
>
> This is not a flaw in the design. Gimbzo built it this way deliberately.
>
> *You do not use the fist of the Plane without paying the Plane's price.*

### The deepest temptation

The Total Fist carries a temptation that belongs to no single Work's excess: **completeness itself**. A practitioner who has touched all seven doctrines and felt the Plane extend through them may begin to believe that *staying* in that coordinate is the purpose, not the cost. That the labor is to remain, permanently, as infrastructure. That returning to the world is a lesser thing than holding the Plane's floor forever.

Gimbzo is the proof against calling this delusion. **It is simply the direction the Work points, if you follow it far enough.**
> Lawful Totality integrates and returns.
>
> Obsession Force Totality subsumes and never releases.

### Origin

**Origin** · Its originator, a Moto of the line's founding generation, held the eastern rim of the unfinished world for forty-one days without sleep; broken at last in a three-day duel, he was taken whole by the Plane of Fate as living foundation.

**Practitioners** · Its originator, the only known full master; it descends through the Moto line, and no living master of a full form is named.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · The Anchor has chosen ground and stopped: a weapon butt driven in, palms flat to the earth, ground taking a weight it did not have. After, the ground remembers the coordinate.

**Limits** · The coupling must be set before contact; it holds against displacement only up to its Continuity Grade; the largest Works run fewer than four to a reserve.

**Fails against** · Tension: a pull, lift, hook or drag; a confined space, where the blast returns; displacement above its Continuity, a Silence, or ground that will not hold, any of which severs the coupling; Obsession; spending.

**Look up** · The Materia entries for Fixatio and Petralon and the Cataclysm, Fractura and Basilithe failures; the Continuity and Gravity gates; the bearer's card and lineage; the Transcendence and Emanation Stage tables.

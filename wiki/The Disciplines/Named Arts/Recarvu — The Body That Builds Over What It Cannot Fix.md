# Recarvu — The Body That Builds Over What It Cannot Fix

**RECARVU** (再彫身 · Recarvu) (Biokinesis sub-discipline, Vitalia)

Tamsin Gull ran opals past the toll-reeves of the Long Pass for eleven years inside her own left forearm. She had broken it young and grown a sheath of horn over the break, and there was room under the horn for forty stones if she packed them while the ridge was still soft. The reeves turned out her saddlebags and tapped her boots while she leaned on their counter with a fortune under a lump of scar. The trade ended the summer the lump reached the thickness of a fence post, because it had never once stopped growing. A surgeon sawed it open on a tavern table in front of a paying crowd and kept two opals for his fee. It had closed again by harvest, with the other thirty-eight still in it.

*再彫身*
> A healer fixes. **Recarvu builds over.**

**The hook.** Break your arm and it grows armor over the break, and then you hit people with the arm.

- **INSTANT SCARIFICATION: KESSHOU NO IN!** The wound seals over in a breath or two, and whatever was broken underneath stays broken.
- **HARDENING: KOUTAI!** Skin thickens and stiffens toward boiled leather over mail, and a sword skids where it should have bitten.
- **INSTANT BLOOD-CLOTTING: SHUKKETSU TEISHI!** Every open bleed on the body stops in under a second.
- **BONE DENSITY: KOTSUMITSUDO!** Same bones, far harder to break, and they weigh more for the rest of your life.
- **BONE GROWTH: KOTSUZOU!** Spurs, spines and ridges driven out through the skin, screaming, wherever you want a weapon.
- **MUSCLE GROWTH: KINNIKU ZOUSEI!** Months of heavy labor packed into a few seconds, and then a hunger you cannot feed.
- **FIRE RESISTANCE: TAIKA HIFUKU!** The hardened hide chars in place of burning, and the char protects what lies under it.
- **POISON RESISTANCE: DOKUTAI!** Poisoned flesh is shed and replaced faster than the poison travels.
- **TUMOR GROWTHS: SHUYO!** A shapeless plug of living meat for a wound too big to scar over, and the first sign of the end.

**Cost.** Everything it adds stays, so you get heavier and slower for life, and ravenous, and in the end the growing stops asking you.  
**Counter.** Grapple and joint-lock past the plate, strike a fresh scar again within the minute, and make them run.

---

**How it really works.** A plant has no final size, and its growing tips stay young for as long as it lives. Verdantia gives the practitioner's tissue that same habit, Terranova lays mineral along the growth front the way a shell lays nacre, Anima Spirare runs the clotting cascade that seals a wound before anything else begins, and Materia Primordia re-lattices flesh toward ceramic at the top of the art. Nothing in this is healing, as every practitioner will tell you while knocking on their own forearm. Toll-reeves in the Long Pass have since learned to knock on it too.

## Core Principle

> **There is no wound the body cannot survive if you are willing to let it grow over what it cannot fix.**
Healing implies restoration: the broken thing returned to its prior state, the damaged architecture rebuilt to original specification. **Recarvu does none of this.**

A broken arm stays broken; Recarvu grows a dense sheath of keratin-analogue around the break site, splinting it with tissue harder than the bone it encases. A severed hand does not regrow; Recarvu closes the stump with layered scar tissue, mineralized at the surface, sealed against infection, structurally sound enough to use as a blunt striking surface. **The hand is gone. What remains is functional.**
> The philosophical core is that **repair is a luxury and survival is a material problem.** You do not need your body to work the way it used to. You need it to work the way it is. *Recarvu practitioners waste no time mourning lost function: they build over it and find out what the new shape can do.*

## The Three States

**Passive · Vigor** · No focus, no activation. Verdantia runs continuously, converting ambient Aether into low-grade vitality. Recovery accelerates, the immune system runs hot, bruises metabolize in hours rather than days. *The practitioner looks healthy the way a well-watered plant looks healthy: the damage is there, and continuous growth outpaces the wear.* **Costs almost nothing. Cannot be switched off without de-harmonizing from Verdantia.**

**Active · Directed growth** · Meristematic logic narrows to a single site and accelerates. Nails extend and keratinize into claw-like projections. Forearm dermis thickens stratum by stratum until it approaches cured leather. **The added material is organic: the practitioner's own tissue, grown on command.**

**Intensive · Mineralization** · Terranova takes over from Verdantia. Tissue calcifies, depositing hydroxyapatite along the growth front the way nacre deposits inside a shell. At the highest expression Materia Primordia engages and re-lattices the crystallographic structure toward ceramic or carbide composite. **The tissue is still alive. It is still fed by blood supply. It is also, by any structural test, closer to armor plate than to flesh.**

## Structural Limitations

**Not healing** · A punctured lung gets scar tissue over the hole, not functional alveolar tissue. **The damage beneath every application is still there. It is simply buried under new material.**

**One layer at a time** · Stacking requires the first to cure before the second begins, and **every application is visible** as a thickened, discolored ridge. Three or more layers restrict joint range, degrade proprioception, and produce a dull persistent ache from compressed nerve bundles.
> **Mass accumulation. Everything Recarvu adds stays.** Keratin sheaths, mineralized plates, density deposits, muscle growth: none of it is temporary.
>
> A veteran practitioner is functionally armored by their own tissue and functionally slower than they were before they started. **The ability trades agility for endurance on a permanent, non-reversible curve.**
**Metabolic debt** · Growth requires protein, mineral, water, caloric substrate, taken from the practitioner's own reserves. *A practitioner who uses Recarvu aggressively without eating will cannibalize their own unmodified tissue to feed the growth, and the growth has priority over the tissue it is consuming.*

## Corruption Vector

Vitalia's terminal corruption is **Biological Recursion**: growth and decay locked into a loop the practitioner can no longer interrupt. Recarvu sits on the growth side.

A practitioner deep into the discipline has trained Verdantia to answer every stimulus, every stress, every injury the same way: **grow more.** The early symptoms are indistinguishable from competence. Faster scarification. Denser bone. Harder skin. More material between the practitioner and whatever is trying to kill them.
> **The late symptoms are not indistinguishable from anything else.** Growth stops being directed. Tumors form at sites never targeted. Spurs emerge from joints never stressed. Keratin plates thicken past functional density and crack under their own internal pressure.
>
> The body, which Recarvu taught to grow on command, **has learned to grow without being asked**, and the practitioner discovers that Verdantia's failure mode was always the destination the discipline was pointed at.
>
> Used with discipline, Recarvu is a quiet, indefinite source of structural resilience that lets a body endure what should have ended it.
>
> Used without restraint, it is a body that no longer knows how to stop building, piling tissue on tissue on tissue, denser and heavier by the hour, **and the practitioner trapped inside a carapace of their own making that they can no longer shed.**

# Rusashin — The Dust That Remembers What It Touched

**RUSASHIN** (粒壇身 · Rusashin) (Hylekinesis sub-discipline, Materia, ten Forms)

The spice galley *Salt Heron* went onto the rocks off Ushio Point with forty pirates in scarlet sashes climbing her sides and one hired guard aboard. The guard, a short bad-tempered woman called Otsuya, had spent the whole night watch walking the ship with her hands out, seeding grit into the hold, the rigging and the air above the deck. She waited until the last sash was over the rail, and then she set off every grain of it. The cloud of brown dust and cinnamon could be smelled three villages inland, and the cooks of Ushio Point claim it seasoned the bay for a month. Otsuya floated ashore on the rudder, shaking from head to foot, and asked who was going to pay her.

*粒壇身*
> Creation, manipulation and detonation of **unstable pseudo-mass.** The discipline is sequential: each Form gates the next.

**The hook.** They touch a thing, they copy it in dust, and then the dust explodes.

- **REGULAR RUSASHIN!** A touch makes a perfect copy of your fist or blade that swings beside it for two or three seconds, then falls apart.
- **IRREGULAR RUSASHIN!** A touch makes a mirror copy, and where the thing meets its mirror it blows up, or flash-freezes, or turns to crystal.
- **FORM I, DUST: SATSUJIN NO CHIRI!** A handful of grit thrown like sling-stones, bursting on contact.
- **FORM II, VESSEL: UTSUWA!** Pebbles packed into a gauntlet, a cage or a dragon-fist shell.
- **FORM III, CALMNESS: SEIJAKU!** The dust starts blocking for you before you have decided to block.
- **FORM IV, TRAILING: RUSASHIN NO KISEKI!** A pebble that gathers its own cloud behind it, a stone by the second pass and a millstone-heavy lump by the third.
- **FORM V, SHIELD: RUSASHIN NO TATE!** Walls and domes that hold like an arch, then blow up in your face when broken.
- **FORM VI, WEAPON: RUSASHIN NO KEN!** Weapons and knuckle-plates fused to the hands, each shaped by how the owner already fights.
- **FORM VII, MINES: RUSASHIN NO OSHI!** Grains left hanging in the air and sunk in every surface, waiting for your word.
- **FORM VIII, ARMOR: RUSASHIN NO YOROI!** A carapace bonded to the Shell, which can be blown off in every direction at once.
- **FORM IX, THE DRAGON: RUSSA!** A beast of thousands of grains that hunts, re-forms and detonates on contact.
- **FORM X, THE DRAGON FIST: RUSSA NO KOBUSHI!** The whole dragon worn as a second skin and put through one punch, with a silence first and a crater after.

**Cost.** The great Forms drink reserve by the tens of thousands, and when it runs out the body drops with no warning at all.  
**Counter.** Wind scatters it, rain ruins it, and a fighter moved out of their own minefield now has to walk back through it.

---

**How it really works.** Salt falls out of an overfull brine the moment it finds a seed, and the seed decides the crystal. Coagulatio does this with Essence, pulling it out of suspension into solid grains on the template of whatever the hand is touching. Fractura seeds the fault lines along which those grains later come apart, Fixatio cures them so they last past their natural seconds, and Petralon lets a heap of them carry weight like a stone arch. Hedge-workers mutter that the dust is a kind of bad housekeeping. The two mechanisms below come first and the Forms after them, as the Guild insists, and the Guild is right about that one.

## Core Mechanism

### Regular Rusashin

Hand-contact with a physical object nucleates an **identical Coagulatio copy** of its mass, energy state and momentum. The copy exists for 2–3 seconds at full fidelity and replicates the original's motion in exact sequence: a copied fist strikes in the same arc, a copied blade swings at the same angle. Two identical masses occupy the same engagement space without mutual interference, **because the copy's signature is phase-locked to the original rather than independent of it.**
> The copy ceases to exist the instant it deviates from the original's sequence. It cannot be redirected, sustained or repurposed mid-existence. **It is a recording played in matter, and when the recording ends, the matter dissolves.**
*Mechanism of the Sixty: ordered nucleation on a template. The supersaturated field deposits solid exactly matching the crystal structure of the touched object. Same lattice, same energy, same phase: the fidelity is perfect because the template is physically present at nucleation.*

### Irregular Rusashin

The inversion. Same contact, same template, **but an anti-phase copy**: mirrored crystallographic orientation, inverted energy state, cancellation rather than replication.
> Mass exposed to its anti-phase twin **explodes.** Fire in contact with its Irregular copy **flash-freezes.** Water struck by its anti-phase **crystallizes.** Vapor struck by its inverse **liquefies.**
>
> The Irregular clone moves in mirrored, antithetical motion. If the original walks forward, the clone walks backward from the point of contact, **and when the two trajectories intersect, the original detonates.**
*Mechanism of the Sixty: anti-crystalline nucleation. Where ordered crystal and anti-crystal meet, the interference is destructive and the released energy is proportional to the density of the original. Coagulatio provides the nucleation; Fractura provides the fault plane along which cancellation propagates.*

### Calmness

**Not a technique. A Trait.** Once Rusashin has been nucleated in combat often enough, Gnosis Analysis integrates with the Coagulatio nucleation reflex, and particles begin appearing at threat-sites **before the practitioner consciously decides to place them.** A fist arriving at the jaw finds a pebble-cluster already forming at the point of contact.
> **Calmness is faster brain-responsiveness at the cost of output force.** Autonomous particles are smaller, less dense, less structurally sound. **They jab rather than strike. They deflect rather than block.**
>
> The tradeoff is not optional. **Calmness is a Trait, not a mode. Once crystallized, it runs continuously.**
>
> *Trait Classification: Tempered Reflection. Discipline: Coagulatio / Fractura / Judicium. Trigger: sustained combat nucleation under threat.*

## Counters

**Aerokinetic displacement** · Sustained high-velocity wind scatters loose particles before they aggregate and displaces dormant mines from their seeded positions. **Fluxia-dominant opponents are the discipline's natural predators.**

**Water saturation** · Wet particles nucleate poorly. Rain and Hydrokinetic fields degrade crystallization fidelity, producing disordered mass instead of ordered crystal. **Rusashin in rain is Rusashin at half strength.**

**Resonance disruption** · Harmonia-based attack, Fell Chord interference and sustained sonic pressure degrade the autonomous nucleation reflex, **forcing the practitioner back to manual placement.**

**Spatial displacement** · Spatiokinetic coordinate-editing moves the practitioner out of contact with their own field. *The mines remain where they were planted. The practitioner is no longer standing in the middle of them, and the field becomes useless or, worse, hostile.*

**Sustained pressure** · **The terminal weakness is that the most powerful Forms cost the most to maintain**, and the body shuts down without warning when reserves empty.

# THE THUMB OF HEAVEN (Rite, a rite in words, hand and oil)

*Scholars' term: Anointing.*

> At the Feast of Lamps at Harl Ford, Brother Wilkin Lovage of the Order of Benediction was sent for the chrism, could not find it, and came back with the kitchen's walnut oil. He was drunk, believed none of it, and said the words at a run so he could get back to the goose. The girl he said them over, Bettany Roe, in a flour-white apron, with the whole hall yawning, looked up and every lamp in the room leaned toward her. The Order has held eleven inquiries. The oil is kept in a reliquary now, and the goose has a feast day.

One thumb of oil from the right person, and your soul starts answering to a name it was always going to have.

**BY THIS OIL!** The mark goes on in words, hand and oil, and anyone with the eyes for it can see it from then on.  
**WAKE, SLEEPER!** Glyphs sealed since birth get their permission, and a power that had been waiting for that permission becomes reachable.  
**I VOUCH!** The officiant stakes their own Crystal on the subject, which is the part that hurts them for as long as the mark stands.

## How it really works

The mark does no work itself. Think of a phosphate group placed on a protein: it changes what the thing is taken to be, and everything that reads it behaves differently. The law is mass action at a binding site, K_d = k_off / k_on. How long a bond holds is its residence time, 1 / k_off, and a mark's effects are the behavior of whatever reads it. The tag costs the energy to place it and nothing after; everything downstream is paid by the readers. Two limits fall out at once. Even the strongest affinity bond holds about a day, so a mark that lasts a career must be cured as well as bound. And a mark nobody reads does nothing. Forgery follows too: a tag at the wrong site is chemically identical and recognized by nothing, and a rival of higher affinity displaces a real one silently, so the loss shows only when the bond is called on.

The rite takes five turns, thirty seconds, in one ceremonial expenditure, with the officiant pulling Aether, refining it in the Crystal and driving it out through the Shell (the Crystal's outer skin) in words and hands and oil. Glory stands below Refraction, so those anchors are required. Saturation plays no part. The Residue (what a working leaves behind) is the mark itself, a standing Traitus mark on a person that anyone with enough Aetheric perception can see. The glyphs are Lo, Wy and Ka, written in the officiant's words and laid with hand and oil. The boundary that moves is what the subject's Crystal counts as its own: a recognition site is set from outside, and nothing is added to capacity.

Three Wellsprings carry it. Benediction, the Wellspring of Grace (Fate), makes the recognition measurable: a bond formed under it carries an affinity figure the Continuum reads, and it strengthens Harmonics Empathy, Harmonics Axis and Resilience Oath. Fixatio, the Binding Flame (Medium), cures it: it holds by making the other configuration unreachable, so the mark lasts a career and is not undone casually. Judicium, the Wellspring of Truth (Wild), rests on the fact that no substance can present another's spectrum, and it is the officiant's discernment and the searcher's assay alike. The Crystal then answers to the recognition. Sealed glyphs get their authorization, and a latent alignment gated on that recognition becomes reachable. The subject feels permission and no surge of power, which disappoints everyone who paid for one. In the Essence, the subject's Attraction Layer holds the recognition, their Essence Core holds the Traitus mark, and the officiant stakes their own Core. Developmental Tier is Harmonic at Glory. Where the mark wakes a latent construct-bearing capacity, Animatria comes into it as well.

The floor is Glory because the officiant vouches for another's purpose with their own Crystal. Below it there is no stake to lend, and the stake is what makes a false Anointing detectable at all. A genuine Anointing is a relationship: a subject chosen by a Wellspring current, an Archon or the order's standing authority. The Order of Benediction licenses it anyway, and treats that license as a decision and never as a finding. The rite confers recognition and nothing else. It cannot confer legitimacy by ceremony alone, grant dominion or rule (that is Coronation), or be undone casually. A forced or tricked Anointing can be removed only by Scission, and carries Scission's complication rate.

The failure is the empty rite: correct words with no current behind them make a visible, mechanically empty mark, found only when called on. A mark laid in error is permanent and misdirects the Crystal for the rest of the path. Benediction's competitive inhibition lets a stronger claim displace the mark; Fixatio's irreversibility is what makes the error permanent; Judicium's spectral overload blinds the reader. Bleed is 30 to 40 percent of the officiant's spend, leaving the Shell as heat, with sound and structural bleed, and the costlier loss, standing, is not measured in η (the share of a spend that arrives as work). The form school holds a correct rite always valid, so it never tests and is the perfect victim of a forgery; the worth school rejects marks by who laid them. Neither runs the assay, which Judicium settles, and I have stopped being surprised.

**Cost.** Ten percent of full reserve once per rite, plus the officiant's own standing, vouched for as long as the mark stands.  
**Counter.** Bring a Judicium practitioner or a Measurewright to the rite, bring a stronger claim, or simply refuse to honor it.

**What nobody knows.** Who, if anyone, chose a subject whose true Anointing was performed by an officiant who believed the rite was empty.

**Origin** · Kept by the Order of Benediction, which licenses the rite and treats that license as a decision rather than a finding. The False Anointing Rebellion, raised by a corrupt bishop's empty marks, is the tradition's standing warning against forged performance.

**Limits** · It confers recognition and no power or rule; its effect is only what readers grant it; it cannot survive an assay if empty.

**Fails against** · A Judicium practitioner or a Measurewright at the rite; a higher-affinity claim; a reader who refuses to honor it; a lying officiant's own drift in Coherence; Scission, for a forced one.

# BECAUSE I SAID SO (Bind, a spoken working)

*Scholars' term: Edictum. The Bench is still upset about the street name.*

> Sibyl Ingleby of the Bench stood four foot eleven in scarlet at the Moot Hall in Cawl, with two hundred people on the benches and the bravo Rorik the Red halfway up the aisle with his hand on his hilt, and she told him by name that he would bare no blade in this county. The roof tiles lifted and settled. The windows ran hot. Rorik stood pulling at his own sword with every muscle in his arm, wanting it as badly as he had wanted anything, and found the doing was simply not available. He left the county on foot. Two winters later she tried the same sentence in a temper on a hill lord's cousin, and it came back on her own Wellspring, and she has spoken more slowly ever since.

Say it out loud with the right to say it, and it is so.

**YOU SHALL NOT!** Sentence: a named person finds one named act no longer available to them.  
**NOT THIS ONE!** Protection: a named person cannot be touched by a named harm.  
**NOT ANOTHER WORD!** Silence: a named person cannot use a named Craft. The Moto line's Edictum of Silence is the famous one.  
**THIS WHOLE HALL!** The same sentence over a space or a domain, at about half again the price.

## How it really works

A superconductor excludes a magnetic field without pushing on it, because zero field inside is a condition its state imposes, and that is called the Meissner effect. Nothing pushes. The target's possibilities simply end at a wall. The law is the uniqueness of solutions to a boundary-value problem: given the equation and the boundary, the interior has one answer, and it is adopted everywhere at once. Hence no travel time; it is instant as the sentence ends. The limit is the critical field, above which exclusion stops outright instead of weakening, and that threshold belongs to the wall and owes nothing to what it excludes. Once set, a Meissner state costs nothing per second, because its screening currents flow without loss, working against nothing.

The charge is a name and a condition said aloud before witnesses, and the target hears it whole and can act during it. Kept by design at Refraction: an Edict is always spoken aloud so it can be heard, appealed, countersigned or lifted. There is one sharp expenditure as the sentence closes, Aether pulled, refined in the Soul Crystal and driven out through the Shell (the Crystal's outer skin), and then nothing; the hold is a state, so an Edict does not lapse with attention. Thirty to 40 percent of the casting spend leaves the Shell as heat, sound and structural bleed, all in the instant of speaking, which is why Edicts are pronounced in halls built for them. The Residue (what a working leaves behind) is heavy and legible: the bound target carries the condition, which is why it can be lifted.

The Aether is Judicium, the Wellspring of Truth (Wild): no substance can present another's spectrum. It locates where the new limit sits, and the wall itself is the world's. Its spectral overload loses the priority an Edict needs to name one condition, and the ground Judicium hates (boiled leather, fired clay, dense fog, a shielded interior) spoils the reading and leaves the hold alone. The glyph is Boundary laid under Command: both name a kind of limit on any law, since the law the target runs on is unknown until named. What moves is the value the target's own behavior may take at a named limit. A sentenced target cannot do the forbidden act, a protected one cannot be touched by the named harm, a silenced one cannot use the named Craft. The target still wants the thing and finds the doing unavailable, which feels like an absence and offers nothing to shove against.

It holds durably and needs no upkeep, sustained by a fact about standing, and it ends when that fact does or when a same or greater authority lifts it. The Attraction Layer, where standing is held, is the active layer; the Essence Core supplies the conviction read as authorization; the Shell does almost nothing. Developmental Tier is Resonant at Refraction. It is a licensing working, the Consent Doctrine's own shape: a process can be licensed where a relationship cannot. There is no contest. Standing is weighed before the sentence, a target within the speaker's authority is bound, and one greater is simply not bound. An Edict cannot reshape reality in general the way a Decretum-class working would, cannot bind a target over whom the speaker has no lawful standing, and cannot originate an obligation, only enforce one.

The other half of the price is clarity of soul. The working reads the caster's conviction as its authorization, and any impurity in that conviction is charged back to the caster instead of being absorbed by the target. That is Echo Reversal: an Edict spoken in anger, doubt or bad faith sets the wrong value at the speaker's own boundary and backfires on their Wellspring. A mis-located edge is a Boundary fault. An overstated warrant seals and bills the speaker the difference. The Bench holds that authority, and never truth, makes law, so a failed Edict means defective authority and it escalates. The instrument-makers hold that finely structured targets thread an Edict like flux vortices, so escalation admits more threads. Both have been copied out by hand, at length.

**Cost.** Six to 12 percent of full reserve, all in the one turn the sentence closes, and clarity of soul on top.  
**Counter.** A hand over the speaker's mouth, a Lock or contradiction glyph set beforehand, oath-nullification, or simply outranking them.

**What nobody knows.** Why an Edictum spoken in perfect legal form still fails against some targets with no documented authority advantage at all: the Bench's own registers call this "an unresolved authority gap" and decline to elaborate further.

### Origin

Urion originates the form at the cosmological scale; mortal derivations of the same working are known, among them the Daughters of Concord and the historical Edictum of Silence of the Moto line. As a documented, taught working with an Archonic origin, it is counterable by anyone who has studied a prior case and by nothing else short of superior standing.

# THE SELF THAT NEVER FORGAVE (Make, a summoned construct)

*Scholars' term: Invert Eidolon, and the thing itself is an Anti-Eidolon.*

> The salvage court at Oxmouth still holds the log of the cog Patient Margit, whose passenger, a fractured spiritbinder named Ragna Thole, was asked by the crew to do something about the pirates. She reached into her own chest and brought up what she had refused at her Transcendence, nine feet of her mother's temper in a body the color of pale iron, and it went through the pirates in forty heartbeats, painting the mainsail red to the yard. Then it turned around and came back along the deck for her. The log ends there. The cog came ashore with the mast burned to the step and nobody aboard in a fit state to testify.

He pulls out the part of himself he could never swallow, makes it stand up, and it hates him.

**STAND UP, YOU BASTARD!** The construct comes out of the summoner whole, and it does not tire or flinch.  
**RETURN TO SENDER!** Whatever reaches it goes back along the line it came from, and it does not count its summoner as an exception.  
**GET BACK DOWN!** Dismissal: the stored stress has to go somewhere, and it may cost the raising all over again.  
**THE WHOLE SHAMEFUL FAMILY!** Several at once, the strain multiplying, usually the last thing the summoner ever does on purpose.

## How it really works

There is no chant, circle or gesture. The chain is internal above Refraction, with no circle, catalyst, invocation or wrist to break. The charge is the construct itself arriving, visibly made of something its summoner will not own, erratic toward them, while the summoner burns gigawatts at the Shell (the Crystal's outer skin). It is built from what the Crystal refused to integrate: a suppressed Trait, an unreconciled harmonic, a fracture from a Stage survived without closing.

The physics is residual stress in a casting. A part cooled faster than it could relax locks in tension it was never loaded with, like a Prince Rupert's drop that shrugs off a hammer and bursts when its tail is nicked. The law is templated solidification from a defective billet, with Griffith's criterion σ_f = √(2Eγ/πa): a crack runs when released strain energy exceeds the cost of new surface, so stored tension makes a tiny flaw enough. That gives a thing strong in every direction but one. General load hits the compressed skin, which is what it is for, and one small specific defect brings the whole body apart. A stored stress goes somewhere when unwatched, which is why putting the construct down may cost as much as raising it. And stressed parts fail later from causes too small to record, so the construct turns erratic, often on its summoner, without wanting anything.

A fabrication charge set by complexity, then a sustain every second it stands: Aether pulled and refined and spent at the heaviest continuous rate of any spoken art. The construct is Residue (what a working leaves behind), a fragment of the summoner's Core walking outside them, and several at once multiply Shell strain toward a depletion cascade, the discipline's worst recorded event. Twenty to 30 percent of the sustain leaves the Shell as heat, sound and light every second, so the summoner is a furnace visible from the next ridge, and scorches the ground around them. What is left afterward is a scorched ring and one fewer unclosed fracture.

Eidolyn, the Shaping Mind (Wild), is the template: intent is the template and matter deposits along it with high fidelity and real strength, and it strengthens Ardency Density and Gnosis Retention. Oblivara, the Dark Mirror (Fate), is why it turns: a corner reflector returns energy along its incoming path, so the construct returns what reaches it to its source and never counts its summoner an ally. Nihiloth, the Hollow Law (Wild), cancels the law already present, giving the construct its refusal-shaped character. The glyph is Form laid under Mind: Form limits what the template may take from, Mind names where the contents are read from. The boundary is what counts as available material, which moves to include the unresolved, and Eidolyn faithfully reproduces the locked-in stress with it. The Essence Core is the billet, the Attraction Layer is the tether, the Shell carries the strain. Developmental Tier is Radiant at Transcendence.

The result is a sound, strong construct that does not tire, flinch or share its summoner's interest, stress oriented toward the thing refused, returning everything back along the tether at the summoner. Transcendence gates semi-autonomous constructs at S-Grade, and full tactical autonomy waits for Realization at SS. Below Transcendence only a brief, fragile, accidental construct forms through an ordinary Animatria working, never the full thing. The supply is the real ceiling: it finds a refusal and cannot make one. The summoner must have survived the fracture's Stage, and a fracture spent is spent.

Structural fault is the operation and no accident of it: the Crystal lends what it refused, and losing control is the working completing. Eidolyn's template defect is the working's definition. Oblivara's code exhaustion makes a construct held too long a confident, complete, wrong reconstruction. Nihiloth's symmetric cancellation takes the parts of the summoner in the billet. The redemptive schools read the construct as a broken vessel holding a spark and try to lift the spark out while keeping it standing; repair is the work of a people across history, and a husk held is a husk gaining ground. I have read their case notes. They are beautifully written, and most of them stop abruptly.

**Cost.** Seven and a half to 15 percent of full reserve to raise by complexity, then 1 to 2.5 percent every second it stands, and dismissal may cost the raising again.  
**Counter.** Silence severs the tether outright; otherwise outlast the sustain or nick the one defect left by the Stage the summoner survived badly, since traded blows only load the strong skin.

**What nobody knows.** Whether an Anti-Eidolon remembers the fracture it was made from once that fracture has been spent.

### Origin

**Origin** · Outside guild sanction almost everywhere it is known; fractured spiritbinders and Temperants pursue it for a closure ordinary progression will not hand them sooner. It first surfaced in the Twilight Concord, and the Wellspring Fracture of Nalisem in the Era of Calamity, when summoners lost control of several at once, is its worst remembered disaster.

**Practitioners** · A self-taught magus said to have raised one by accident in his early trials; a spiritbinder who keeps her own Anti-Eidolon bound; an adept of the Riven Choir; the Mirrorwake Ascetics; the Occultinarii of the Hollow Eye.

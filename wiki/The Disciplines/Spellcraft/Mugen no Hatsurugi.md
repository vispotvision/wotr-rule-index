# THE INFINITE FIRST BLADE (無限の初剣 · Mugen no Hatsurugi) (Make, delivered as a blow)

*Scholars' term: Mugen no Hatsurugi. The art is bodily speaking, and lives on this shelf by old filing.*

> The town crier at Ogawara gives the count of its blades as fifty-three. The song says eighty-one. The minstrels of the southern road have it at a hundred and eight and a soup ladle, and the armorers' guild once tried to bill its bearer per arrival for the wear on their marks, and lost, because every one of those blades had been paid for or properly won. Nobody who has seen it agrees on anything but this: he did not draw it. It was in his hand, and it had always been in his hand, and you were the one who had got that wrong.

Any weapon he ever truly earned is already in his hand.

**ALREADY IN HAND!** An arrival: no draw, no flash, a weapon that has always been there, and you must now deal with it.  
**THE HEAVY NAME!** A blade named against a much greater mass, costing twice and weighing what it needs to.  
**A NEW ANSWER EVERY STRIDE!** Against a line of men, the right weapon for each one, every few strides, two a turn.  
**THE EIGHTH NAME!** Against a Domain enforcing one discipline, he names blade after blade until its clauses run out.  
**SHOCK-LINE!** What the delivered blow throws forward along the ground, out to about 70 m.

## How it really works

A seed dropped into a supersaturated solution makes the solute deposit as the seed's own lattice, atom for atom, so the product is the template continued and no copy of it. That is templated crystal growth. The law is heterogeneous nucleation and epitaxial growth: a phase forming on a substrate takes its structure, and the barrier to forming falls almost to nothing, so a solution inert for hours crashes out in seconds. The limit is that a seed you do not have is a seed you cannot grow from, and no driving force makes a lattice there is no template for. Crystallizing from a supersaturated medium releases energy, and the practitioner supplies only the seed and the barrier-drop, which is why an SS-weight weapon costs a few percent of reserve.

The weapon has no tell. The practitioner has an enormous one: a beacon of coherent light with a judgment field felt as pressure in bone and stance. There is one sharp pull per arrival and no sustain, Aether refined in the Soul Crystal into Essence and resolved at his own body with no chain to interrupt and no anchor. Twenty to 30 percent of each arrival leaves the Shell (the Crystal's outer skin) as heat, sound and light, 16 percent at a Refined η 0.84 (η is the share of a spend that arrives as work), and at tempo it is continuous and Aurevane makes it a beacon. Each arrival ratifies itself into the record, so the Residue (what a working leaves behind) is documentary as well as ambient, every arrival counted, and a field worked at tempo deafens Harmonists (listeners to Aether).

Six Wellsprings make one armory. Materia Primordia, the First Substance (Wild): graphite and diamond are one element arranged differently, and the Crystal re-lattices its own Essence into an armory. Anamnesis, the Wellspring of Remembrance (Medium), reads the practitioner's combat history as a causal ledger and ratifies each claim. Fixatio, the Binding Flame (Medium), is the lawful coordinate on the practitioner's body that the template resolves into. Coagulatio, the Returning Stone (Wild), gathers dispersed Essence into the dense, supersaturated phase the seed crashes out of. Monolithion, the Enduring Monument (Wild), grows the blade as a single crystal with no grain boundaries. Aurevane, the Wellspring of Light (Medium): coherent light holds one phase across its front, and it names the arrival as a designated, weighted thing. The glyph is Form, for the template, laid under Return, for the ledger: a holding lawfully held once comes back. The boundary is the nucleation barrier for one lattice at one coordinate, which drops to nearly nothing for the one template Anamnesis has ratified. The Essence Core supplies the substance and holds the ledger, the Shell is the coordinate, and the Attraction Layer adjudicates lawfulness. Developmental Tier is Sovereign at Realization, settling Refined after years of lawful use.

The weapon is in hand with no transition, as though always held, and opponents must process it as already there. Against a line it is a new correct answer every few strides. A Domain enforcing a single discipline must answer each named blade with its own clause, and a narrow Domain runs out of clauses by the seventh while the bearer is still naming the eighth. Only lawfully held weapons may arrive, ones held, named, inherited or correctly defeated, built across a lifetime; stolen, cosmetic or dishonest holdings are refused at the doctrinal level and are never delivered badly. The floor is Realization, because the blade must be ratified at SS weight, and below it the forge makes shapes and no verdicts. The blade in hand is a weapon and no working, costing nothing further, and the blow itself is graded off the practitioner's own Sub-Stats. It is melee-dominant, with shock-lines to about 70 m.

The failure is no precedent: an exchange the ledger has never met gets an empty hand. Under pressure the armory phase-locks to one weapon for good (Materia Primordia's inherited failure), and a blade remembered badly in haste can be lost (Anamnesis's thermal resetting). Monolithion's blade never chips, and on the day it fails it ends. Aurevane's glare makes the practitioner a beacon. A contest of the holding's lawfulness in the moment stalls delivery, since Anamnesis will not ratify a claim it cannot verify. The seed-principle schools hold everything is already planted and comes in its hour, so they plan as if the forge always has an answer; it does not, and they never try the one thing that beats it.

**Cost.** About 2.5 percent of full reserve an arrival, 5 against much greater mass, for the seed and barrier and not the blade's weight.  
**Counter.** Object truly to a holding's provenance as it arrives, offer him an exchange with no precedent, hold range, or bring a Domain of eight or more clauses.

**What nobody knows.** Whether a blade retired from the ledger for a dishonestly acquired holding can ever be lawfully re-earned, or whether the forge's refusal is permanent once a lie about the ledger is recorded against a name.

### Origin

A Moto's own work, shaped across a hundred and twenty days of instruction on the Plane of Fate and ratified by neither the line's founder nor any of its elders, which makes it its author's alone. As a self-derived, unratified technique, it has to be read live in an exchange before it kills, and anyone who studies a documented use of it afterward gains standing counterplay against it that its first opponents did not have.

### Counterplay

# NOBODY SWORE ANYTHING (Claustra, a spoken renunciation)

*Scholars' term: Oathrend.*

> The net-menders of Oxmouth sing it over their work: who swore, who swore, who swore at the door, nobody swore, so you owe me no more. They are singing about the Truce of Cold Harrow. The Ashblood apostate Wat Plover walked out in a red coat between two armies who had been sworn to peace for nine years, with twelve thousand men watching him come, and spoke for about as long as it takes to spit. Every sworn man on that field felt it go, all at once, like a stair that is not there. They stood looking at each other across the barley in the sudden absence of a reason not to. Plover has not been able to promise anyone anything since, including his dinner.

Say the opposite of a promise in exactly the right way and the promise was never there.

**NOT WORTH THE BREATH!** One personal oath gone in under a second, and everyone it bound feels the hole.  
**ALL OF YOU, GO HOME!** A great pact with many anchors, most of a turn of work, and a whole truce, claim or Realm stops being held up.  
**A HUNDRED CHAINS!** The liberator's use: a hundred sworn souls freed, and a hundred vows' worth of toll on the one doing it.

## How it really works

Lay a second wave of equal size and opposite phase over the first, and where both are present there is nothing. That is active noise control. Nothing is absorbed or blocked, and the net disturbance is simply zero. The law is linear superposition: two fields in one region add point by point, and neither is changed by the adding. The anti-field must match at every point, so one source per anchor, which is why you can cancel a hum and cannot cancel a hiss, and why a pact with many anchors takes seconds and no extra force. The leftover is 2 sin(φ/2) of the field for a phase error φ: near perfect until it is suddenly worthless. A matched anti-field carries the same energy density as the field it cancels. The Aether supplies that amplitude and the Crystal supplies the phase, the cheap part and the hard part.

The charge is a renunciation aloud if the caster keeps custom, and otherwise only the felt absence, one instant too late. At Invocation it needs no anchor: the spoken renunciation is custom, and covering the mouth does not stop it. The pull is large and brief against a personal oath, large and sustained for several seconds against a multi-anchor pact, and this is the one working whose cast time is set by the target's shape. Twenty to 30 percent leaves the Shell (the Crystal's outer skin) as heat, sound and structural bleed into the anchor's room within a second: tens of gigawatts, over a hundred at the band's top. A great pact nulled fast in one place can flood the area past dispersal, a Crystal Fracture Event (every Crystal present cracking together) in everyone there, caster included. The Residue (what a working leaves behind) is two fields at every anchor, where a lawful release leaves none, so a rending can always be told from a release.

Nihiloth, the Hollow Law (Limina), is the Aether: two waves in antiphase sum to zero, and a Nihiloth Domain cancels the law present instead of adding any. It strengthens Sovereignty, Persistence and Cognition. The glyph is Knot, one of the two Counter forms, laid across Binding, a Root wholly of Limina: a condition that stops a seal from continuing to be established. The boundary is the phase of the field at each anchor point, set to π, and the oath's strength, the parties' will and the souls the seal holds are all left alone. The Attraction Layer holds the oath, the Shell makes the anti-field, and the Essence Core takes the bill in Resilience Oath. Developmental Tier is Sovereign at Invocation. Claustra is a Spirit and Attraction category, here read under the Debt Position: obligation answered on credit, freely sworn vows leaving the worst Echo Scars.

The seal opens, with no weakening first. Every bound party feels an absence at once, wherever they are, across every plane the oath was enforced on; affection may survive while enforcement is gone, and then the truce, claim or Realm resting on the vow stops being held up. The null must hold at every anchor at once. When it does, the claim's continuity breaks and does not resume, the anti-field can be let go, and the vow must be sworn again. It works only on a real, standing commitment, since it reads actual reciprocity and cannot invent an obligation. Liberation and the destruction of a stabilizing truce are the same working, correctly done, and the Guild has given up trying to file them separately.

The toll is the part nobody sings. Breaking a vow through force costs the caster something structurally identical to what the vow would have cost to keep, in Resilience Oath: a liberator who frees a hundred souls pays a hundred vows of Oath, and a specialist grows steadily less able to swear anything. Symmetric cancellation is the inherited failure: superposition does not know whose field is whose, so any oath, bond or Domain of the caster's on that anchor is nulled too, and a caster never stands on an anchor they care about.

The real failure is backlash through the mirror. An oath sworn under Purganeth-witnessed reciprocity returns the anti-field in exact kind, the caster's whole spend arriving at the caster (Overchannel, Backlash, Fracture). Purganeth is a Realm and no Wellspring, whose Arbiter's law is proportion, restitution and penance, and an oath witnessed there is the working's one mirror; a commitment so sworn under Dazkar's own law reflects the breaking undiminished, while an ordinary oath has no such reflection and simply comes apart. The conventionalist jurists read an oath as dismantleable architecture, which this art bears out. The jurisdictional school hunts for the warrant behind each rending, finds none because it is a field operation, and escalates while missing Purganeth.

**Cost.** Nine to 13 percent of full reserve per rending, plus a toll in Resilience Oath equal to the full weight of keeping the broken vow.  
**Counter.** Swear it under Purganeth from the start, or disturb one anchor before the lock, or renew the oath mid-cast and move the phase, or stand in a Silence.

**What nobody knows.** Why an oath sworn under duress shatters cleanly while one sworn freely and later regretted tends to leave the worst Echo Scars: the variable is not sincerity, and nobody has isolated a second one.

### Origin

A documented, standing working rather than a single self-derived technique: it has three separate lineages of use (a single battlefield invocation, one practitioner's rejection of divine bondage, and the Ashblood Apostates' standing liberation doctrine). As a named, taught technique it is counterable by anyone who has studied a prior Oathrend case.

### Counterplay

**Limits** · Real oaths only; one anti-field per anchor; any anchor disturbed fails the whole null; a Silence denies it.

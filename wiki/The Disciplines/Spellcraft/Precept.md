# THE GRAVEN ABSENCE (Glyphica, a cut art filed on the spoken shelf)

*Scholars' term: Precept. The older registers called the practice enshrining.*

> The Dyers of Cawl and the Weavers of Cawl had been breaking each other's heads for forty years when the Dyers paid to have one line cut into the doorstep of their hall: no blow in anger shall land under this roof. The Weavers came on the Feast of the Vat with sixty hired swords and their faces painted. Sixty swords came down in that hall, in front of the whole town, and every one of them arrived as a firm pat on the shoulder. Men wept. Then the Weavers went home and did the sensible thing and hired the mason Gunnhild Ash, who came back at dawn with a chisel and cut one small notch in the stone.

Carve one line on a stone, and inside those walls that thing just does not exist anymore.

**NOT YOU!** One named person loses one shape. Their working completes in the nearest shape still open, and they may never know why.  
**NOT IN THIS ROOM!** One room or sealed threshold.  
**NOT IN THIS HOUSE!** A guild's code over its halls.  
**NOT IN THIS CITY!** A city's or a dynasty's law, one a season if you are lucky.  
**EXCEPT!** One precise exception written in the same register, which opens exactly one way through.

## How it really works

A bead on a wire has nowhere else to go, and no force had to tell it so. That is a constraint, and a region under this art loses a possible shape the same way: whatever tries to take that shape finds no such shape exists there. Nothing resists. Constraint forces do zero virtual work, Σ Rᵢ · δrᵢ = 0, so the standing upkeep is zero by theorem; the whole cost sits in the substrate, paid once, and a substrate can be chiseled. Band structure in a periodic medium is the other half of the law: it makes a forbidden band where no mode exists, and a single defect opens exactly one allowed state inside it. A band gap has edges. It forbids one range and is transparent everywhere else, so this art compels nothing outside the clause it names. A gap's sharpness grows exponentially with the number of regular periods, so a Precept is as sharp as its clause was regularly laid, and its author's strength has nothing to do with it.

There is no charge anyone sees. There is one pull at sealing, Aether refined in the Soul Crystal and driven out through the Shell (the Crystal's outer skin), and then none, and 30 to 40 percent of the sealing spend at Glory, 20 to 30 at Master, leaves as heat and structural bleed into substrate and room, once. The standing bleed is zero. No cast, light or pressure follows, and nobody feels stopped. The inscription is the Residue (what a working leaves behind), located and touchable, and with no continuous deposit it can be filed where people must live, which is why guilds use it for codes. It is cut: fixed in a substrate, degrading as any inscription degrades, and beaten through the substrate with no chain to interrupt. The substrate is always the anchor.

Fixatio, the Binding Flame (Materia), sets the gap: a cured thermoset chars before it flows, and what it holds stays held because the alternative is no longer reachable. It strengthens Vitality Fortitude and Resilience Anchoring. Judicium, the Wellspring of Truth (Fulguria), sets its edges, since no substance can present another's spectrum, and that is the whole craft of drafting. The glyph is Boundary, the limit that moves, laid under Order, the register the clause is filed in, and Lock is the same shape used as a reply. The boundary is the set of configurations reachable inside a named sphere: strength and willingness are left alone, and only what is available changes. Over a sphere no layer of the subject is touched, since the working is in the substrate; a soul-bound Precept sits in the Essence Core as a Trait. Developmental Tier is Harmonic at Glory. Where it binds parties as a compact, Votia comes into it, and the author cannot take it back.

A working that would take the forbidden shape completes in the nearest available one: an oath sworn in the terms left open, a rank claimed in the permitted form. The mark is a shape in everything downstream, for generations, and the aftermath is documentary: an inscription somewhere, and a sphere where one kind of working has come out the same odd shape for too long. The floor rises with breadth: a named party or a room needs A-Grade authority at Glory, a guild's code S at Transcendence, a city's or a dynasty's law SS at Realization. It cannot compel outside its clause, catch a working that reads its shape before sealing, be argued away by force, or grant rule (that is Coronation). A Precept over a sphere needs no consent; one over a named person, a geas, is void without it, and Rescind exists as its remedy.

The failure is the clause edge misplaced: a century of correct rulings nobody wanted. Fixatio's irreversibility makes a clause laid too wide permanent and free to keep being wrong; Judicium's spectral overload is a badly drafted Precept that catches everything. Institutional drift is the slow form: a house runs a stated law its lived behavior left long ago, readable in the archive. The Accord advocates read a Precept as a norm authorized by higher norms and litigate upward against it for generations. A band gap has no superior to appeal to, and they are beaten by a mason who knows where to cut the notch. I have met that mason's fee. It is lower than theirs.

**Cost.** A share of full reserve once, at sealing: 5 percent for one named party, 10 for a room, 30 for a guild's code, 60 for a city's or a dynasty's law, with no upkeep after.  
**Counter.** Chisel, scrape or topple the substrate; write one precise exception in the same register; or bring Rescind to a soul-bound Precept never lawfully consented to.

**What nobody knows.** Whether the Twelve Precepts of Fate were written at the first descent or were already standing when the first Archon arrived to find them.

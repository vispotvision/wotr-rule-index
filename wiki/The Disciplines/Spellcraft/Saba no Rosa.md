# SABA NO ROSA (the Reckoning of Roses) (Call, held by attention)

*Called aloud as Saba no Rosa. In Common the name comes out as the Reckoning of Roses.*

> Nobody has ever stood in a sea of these roses and described it well. The spring a rumor reached the southern courts that it had been seen, three dukes tore the rose beds out of their gardens by torchlight, rose water went to four times its price, and a steward at Harl Ford settled debts his house had owed since his grandfather's day. The money-changers call it the best year in living memory. The children have a rhyme about it, and it asks what you owe.

Everything you ever owed grows up around you as a rose, and then the thorns start asking for it back.

**ONE ROSE!** A single debt called in, for the one who knows exactly which.  
**A SMALL SEA!** A dozen roses up around the target, each one something they stand on.  
**THE LONG LEDGER!** A hundred roses and more, a whole life in bloom, visible from far off.  
**THE RIGHT ROSE!** Paired with a sight that can name it (the Shingan's Tengan or Kamigan branch), and the apocalypse becomes surgery.

## How it really works

Take a structure's members out of the load path one at a time. The rest take up the load, and when one is overloaded the whole thing comes down with nothing having struck it. That is progressive collapse. The law is static equilibrium and load redistribution: forces at every node sum to zero, so a load removed from one member appears at once in the others, and the Griffith criterion (cracks grow from existing flaws) decides which fails first. Redundancy caps it: a coordinate with n independent load paths survives the loss of n − 1, and a single chain of deposits survives none. Nothing fails at its theoretical strength; stress concentrates at a crack tip as the square root of the crack's length, and an unpaid debt is that flaw in an otherwise sound member.

So a person is a structure and their history holds them up. Each rose is a deposit from the target's own past, a debt honored or an oath kept, and each thorn is the unpaid debt on it. The charge is the field rising around the target, visible from far off and looking nothing like an attack, and in the best-witnessed casting the hands come together and roots come down out of the empty sky before the first rose opens. The gardener stands warm and lit in the open, because 30 to 40 percent of every rose leaves the Shell (the Crystal's outer skin) as heat, sound and light at the floor, a sixth at Master η 0.84 (η is the share of a spend that arrives as work), and a warm, lit ground under the roses marks the position. The pull is sustained and scaled to the field, so the sea is raised small and widened only as reserve allows, and the same reserve raises roughly twice the sea at Master η as at the floor. Past the ground's saturation threshold, a sea over a long ledger is dense sustained expression in one place, and it risks a Crystal Fracture Event, every Crystal present cracking together, the gardener's included. The Residue (what a working leaves behind) is the withdrawal itself, permanent and on the record, and ground where a sea stood deafens Harmonists (listeners to Aether). Afterward there are wilted roses.

Finding the load-bearing member is the hard part. Raising a field over the whole ledger is the survey, slow and paid rose by rose, and a sight that names the right deposit needs only one rose. Once the right member goes, the fall runs at the speed of redistribution and completes in seconds. The joules sit in the structure as potential for as long as it has stood; nothing is delivered or broken, a member is unloaded and redistribution does the rest. Nothing touches the target, so no blade and no wound. Claims simply stop being honored, then the present coordinate standing on them fails, fast. The past stays true and intact, and what is diluted is the target's present claim on it, so this is no erasure. And the practitioner pays, so it is no theft either.

Six Wellsprings. Anamnesis, the Wellspring of Remembrance (Medium): objects store their history in readable form, and it reads the ledger as a physical record. Fractura, the Breaking Point (Wild): everything fails at a pre-existing flaw, and it finds the seams in the target's history and widens them. Contrition, the Broken Flame (Spirit): a genuine failure survived leaves a record, and it surfaces the unpaid weight, and only what was already unpaid. Mortalis, the Black Gate (Medium): a body is held from equilibrium by expenditure, and it governs the threshold across which answering is withdrawn, one direction at a time. Monolithion, the Enduring Monument (Wild): a structure without internal boundaries resists slow deformation, and it ratifies the withdrawal as a standing fact. Oblation, the Offering Flame (Fate): pressure traded for velocity, the sum conserved, and it pays the working from the practitioner's own reserve. The glyph is Measure (Urion) for the survey, laid under Return (Auren) for the withdrawal: what was lent is called in. The boundary is whether a past deposit must answer to its present holder, moved deposit by deposit across a field, and whether it happened or was theirs does not move at all. The Attraction Layer works throughout, since holdings, bonds, paid debts and kept oaths are the Plane-of-Fate record; the Essence Core pays through Oblation; the Shell does nothing else. Developmental Tier at the floor is Resonant. In theory this is the Debt Position: the Wellspring answers on credit and the reckoning is settled out of sight, and a discipline of unpaid debt, paid from its own reserve and punished by its own ledger, is that theory with a name.

It is held by attention with no material anchor, from Refraction, the Stage where a Domain seed first forms, and it lapses the moment the practitioner is killed or Silenced. Mortalis runs one direction at a time, so a withdrawal Monolithion has ratified cannot be reversed, even by its author.

The failure is a boundary fault. Against a deposit that is not unpaid the law does not run, but the payment still leaves; enough wrongful attempts exhaust the gardener's Oblation reserve, Fractura's self-inspection fires, and his own ledger dilutes. A target who seems to owe and does not draws exactly that wrongful rose. Cavitation is the other: taking the medium from the target or the field instead of the practitioner's own reserve turns the sea brackish and the thorns inward on the next exchange, a pitted conduit seen from inside. Inherited on top: Anamnesis's thermal resetting erases a record carelessly read; Fractura's self-inspection shows the bearer their own flaws under strain; Contrition's autoimmunity turns on its keeper; Mortalis's equilibration cools the gardener toward the boundary; Monolithion is brittle at scale; Oblation cavitates. Any of them can be induced from outside. An old eastern school holds that to exist is to encroach and everything owes, so the working calls the ordering of time forward; believing no ledger is clean, its adherents never build the defense and destroy themselves on the innocent. A king's ledger can take the gardener to Starvation (the reserve at its floor), which I mention for those who are already planning.

**Cost.** Half a percent of full reserve per rose, from the practitioner's own reserve: 6 percent for a small sea, 50 and upward for a long ledger.  
**Counter.** Owe nothing, stand on broad and independent claims instead of one chain, bring Silence, or kill or distract the gardener standing exposed in the open.

**What nobody knows.** Why its originator made the lesson conditional on having learned to grieve correctly, and what correct grief was taken to mean.

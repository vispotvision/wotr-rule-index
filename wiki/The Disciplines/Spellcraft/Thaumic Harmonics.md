# THE BREAKING OF ALL SONGS (Harmonia, an Applied Branch of the Harmonic Arts)

*Scholars' term: Thaumic Harmonics. The street name is the Cantic Order's own teaching: break the song, do not block the spell.*

> The Cantic novice Jory Tarn ended the Feast Day duel at Brackwater by clearing his throat. Magister Eudo Ruddle, in violet sleeves as wide as sails, had spent a slow minute building something that had turned the sky over the fish market the color of a bruised plum and lifted every awning on the quay. Tarn was sixteen and holding somebody's herring. He sang one small flat note, and the working went on casting, went on costing, and arrived nowhere at all, while Ruddle poured in more and more of himself with the expression of a man pulling hard on a door hung to open the other way.

He hears your spell coming, sings one wrong note, and it falls apart in your hands.

**WHO'S FLAT?** He goes still for about a second and names the weakest part of what you are building.  
**ONE SOUR NOTE!** The Interference Lock: a tiny tone at exactly the wrong moment, and your working pins or unravels in the air.  
**ALL TOGETHER NOW!** The song turned around: a choir holds pitch to one lead singer and lifts her working past what one person could carry.  
**HOLD THE RAFTERS!** Ritual stabilization: a voice laid under a great rite so it does not shake itself apart.

## How it really works

Parunic syntax is a harmonic language, which is the discovery everything else stands on, and a running working is a machine that hums. Vibration monitoring names a failing bearing from its sound, and a resonance amplifies a small drive; a trained listener does both at once. Every running assembly radiates a line per moving member at frequencies fixed by geometry, and a trained Harmonist (a listener to Aether) reads from tone what instruments would need hours to measure and names the lowest-margin mode. Then a tiny, correctly timed push at that member's frequency stops it.

The law is forced oscillation with amplification Q = f₀/Δf, plus superposition: a small antiphase wave (one running exactly opposite) sums against a large one without overpowering it, and N sources in phase give N times the amplitude and N² the intensity. Time to lock is set by Q and owes nothing to power: a counter at amplitude ratio r cancels in about 1/r cycles, capped at Q cycles, and a low-Q, broadband working has no sharp line to grip. The joke of the art follows from there. High-Q workings, sharply tuned and economical, fall to a whisper, while sloppy ones must be answered with force. The discipline is at its worst against bad casters, and the good ones have never forgiven it.

The tell is plain enough. A second practitioner goes still, listens about a second, and sings one short note, aloud and directional below Refraction, silent above it. Below Refraction the anchor is voice, a literal tone, as in the Sonochoral Conclave's chant; above Refraction the chain internalizes and the counter goes silent. A master reads, analyzes and counters in under two seconds, a third of a six-second turn. The pull is small, since the Harmonist supplies phase and no power to speak of. Almost all the energy is in the target: the lock removes a working's ability to hold its own phase, and the hostile caster's committed energy goes nowhere useful. A locked working's Residue (what a working leaves in the ground) carries two frequency sets instead of one, which is how Concord casework tells a countered working from a botched one. Ambient tonal noise runs against the branch: a loud room, or ground worked heavily by other hands, raises the floor and hides the target's line.

The glyph is Frequency (Selhar), a Root, under Knot (Maelor), a Counter, and it never attacks the hostile Wellspring; it prevents the hostile working's boundary from closing. The boundary moved in the read is the Harmonist's own emission floor, and in the counter it is one phase angle at one frequency at one member of the target chain, pinned at π. The hostile working keeps casting, pulling and costing, and arrives nowhere: in the air it is pinned or unraveled, in the chain it never closes. Its caster feels a correct working producing nothing, and supplying more only spends reserve. A contested lock resolves on the Harmonist's Harmonics Attunement against the target caster's Harmonics Stability.

The branch has no Wellspring of its own and listens to the working's dialect. Three Wellsprings describe what it does to others' workings. Nihiloth, the Hollow Law (Limina): two waves in antiphase sum to zero and the region between carries no energy; Interference Lock is its physics, without harmonizing with it. Basilithe, the Stone Will (Materia): it stiffens a Crystal until its resonance will not be driven off, the defense against every imposed rhythm. Aurevane, the Wellspring of Light (Fulguria): coherent light holds a fixed phase across its front, and coherence is exactly what the lock attacks. In theory this is the Consent Doctrine: a phase relation is imposed and superposition does the rest, asking nothing of any current, so the branch is licensed curriculum and has never been restricted practice. The Aether Shell works throughout, receptive for the read and projective for the counter within the same two seconds; the Essence Core supplies the stable reference tone; the Attraction Layer is untouched. Developmental Tier is Harmonic at Glory, Radiant at Transcendence, Sovereign at Realization.

The constructive half flips the sign. Supporters hold phase to a primary's tone and the intensity adds as N², bought by the supporters and not by the primary. The lead singer's Crystal Fracture risk rises with each supporting voice, and that is how a choir exceeds capacity. A choir drifting out of phase swings the primary's amplitude several times a second at the difference frequency, and the primary fails in her own way, in front of everybody.

The modes climb: tonal perception from Glory, Expert, A (ceiling 400); Interference Lock from Transcendence, Master, S (ceiling 550); ritual stabilization from Realization, Master, SS (ceiling 725). The gap between perception and lock has a reason. Waste is broadband sound at the working's own frequencies, in the channel the Harmonist listens to: a third of every expenditure at Glory deafens him, about a quarter at Transcendence, which is why the lock sits two Stages above perception.

The failure is Echo Fracture. A counter on the wrong harmonic or a phase error of π amplifies the hostile working, by Q at resonance, and that is the moment a novice with a herring becomes the most dangerous person in the square to himself. The Figure school holds that a sand-plate figure is a thing's true form, so to know a working's figure is to own it; but figures repeat across unrelated workings, and matching them walks its Harmonists into Echo Fracture.

**Cost.** Half a percent of full reserve for a read, 3 percent for a lock, 1 percent a turn for a supporting voice or ritual stabilization, and a lock that goes wrong is paid twice.  
**Counter.** Close your working in short bursts, cast loose and broadband, stiffen your Crystal with Basilithe, fight in a noisy room, or hide below hearing by Tenebra or Luminalis.

**What nobody knows.** Whether a working's sand-plate figure is its true form, as the Figure school teaches, or only a shape that unrelated workings happen to share.

## Reference

### Codex line

**Wellspring** · None of its own; it listens to the target working's Wellspring. Nihiloth, Basilithe and Aurevane describe what it does to other workings.

**Family** · None of its own; it reads whatever Family the target working runs on.

**Physics Domain** · None of its own; it reads the domain of the working it answers.

**Phenomenon** · Forced oscillation and superposition: a small, correctly timed wave in antiphase cancels a large one.

**Category** · Harmonia (Spirit and Attraction), with Aetherica supplying the clean baseline a reading is compared against.

**Skill** · Speaking, most often used against another spoken or cut working already in progress.

**Stage floor** · Glory

**Grade required** · A-Grade for perception; S-Grade for Interference Lock; SS-Grade for ritual stabilization.

**Path gate** · Fate and Spirit Paths for Attunement past C; without them it caps at C and cannot hold a frequency.

### Origin

**Origin** · Parunic monks of the Second Epoch, decoding glyph chants, found that Parunic syntax is a harmonic language. Chord Theory codified the branch, and Interference Lock made it a combat art.

**Practitioners** · The Cantic Order, whose choirs teach "break the song, do not block the spell"; the Sonochoral Conclave's chanters; guild Frequencywrights, one of whom diagnosed leyline anomalies during the early Convergences.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · A second practitioner goes still, listens about a second, and sings one short note, aloud and directional below Refraction, silent above it; afterward a working that failed to arrive and a trace with two frequency sets.

**Limits** · It needs the casting window: a closed working is out of reach, a working without harmonic signature offers no grip, the read must finish before the cast, and a misread working is amplified.

**Fails against** · Workings closed in short bursts, or Sealed early by their own caster; a phase pre-claimed with Lock; a Crystal stiffened with Basilithe; low-Q, broadband casting; a noisy room or a Thick Veil zone; two workings presenting one figure.

**Below hearing** · Workings hidden by Tenebra or Luminalis are too quiet for the branch to grip.

**Look up** · The Counterplay register for Lock and Knot; the Basilithe, Nihiloth, Aurevane, Tenebra and Luminalis entries; Veil thickness zones; the Glory, Transcendence and Realization Stage tables; the Spirit and Fate Path gates.

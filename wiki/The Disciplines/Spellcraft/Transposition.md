# THE EXCHANGE OF DESTINIES (Ritus, performed by an officiant)

*Scholars' term: Transposition.*

> The salt-runner Tamsin Oake was taken on the quay at Mallow in irons, with a boat full of untaxed salt and the reeve reading out the charge. She looked at the sergeant holding her chain, who was her height, had her bad knee, and had drunk from the same well for thirty years, and she said four words. For a moment each of them was partly somewhere else, and then the sergeant was in the irons and she was holding the chain, along with his purse, his staff and, as it later came out, his brother-in-law's dice debts.

Two things tuned alike trade places, and you get everything that came with the other one's place.

**THE EXCHANGE OF DESTINIES!** Body for body: two people exchange where they stand, with an interval in the middle when both are partly each other's problem.  
**THE STONE BETWEEN US!** An Anchor Construct, placed for good, shortens the gap so the trade can reach across a field.  
**A DEATH FOR AN ECHO!** An identity trade: the wound that was killing her goes to something that can hold it.  
**A MEMORY FOR A SPOON!** A memory comes out of her and into a thing, or the other way about.  
**THE BORROWED HOUSE!** A soul moves across into a mirrored construct's housing. Echo Constructs come preloaded with the script.

## How it really works

Two pendulums of the same frequency joined by a weak spring trade their motion completely after a fixed interval, with nothing traveling between them. That is resonant energy transfer between coupled oscillators, and the law is coupled-mode theory: two resonators exchange completely at the coupling rate κ when alike, only partly when detuned, and with a coupling that falls as a high power of distance. Two conditions cap it. Likeness: P_max = 4κ²/(4κ² + Δ²) for a mismatch Δ. Nearness: E = R₀⁶/(R₀⁶ + d⁶), so doubling any separation divides the transfer by 64. Both ends also need a Crystal able to hold the exchanged coordinate.

The swap takes t = π/2κ, a real span during which both parties are partly in each other's place, and it can be layered to unfold across a delay. The same κ runs both ways, so there is no one-way Transposition and no being the party who only gives, and an incomplete exchange does not revert. A swap across a height moves mgh: 7.8 kJ for 80 kg across 10 m. Downhill runs with the gradient, uphill against it and costs more. The total energy is conserved between the two ends, and the practitioner pays for the coupling and for closing Δ, a tuning, the cheapest boundary condition there is.

The charge is the furniture: an Anchor Construct, a prepared Fixatio-held object placed between or beside the ends before anything begins. At close range there is no tell and no time. An Anchor Construct shortens every hop, since two hops of d/2 beat one of d by 2⁶ on each leg, and it makes range possible at all. The pull comes in two parts, a tuning cost and a coupling cost, both paid up front, with duration set by κ and owing nothing to endurance. Thirty to 40 percent of every trade leaves the officiant's Shell (the Crystal's outer skin) as heat, sound and light. The Residue (what a working leaves behind) sits at the anchor and leaves the ends clean, ground around a long-used anchor deafens Harmonists (listeners to Aether), and Aetheric noise is a detuning like any other.

Transference, the Living Exchange (Spatium), is the standing law: two Crystals in tune and near exchange along a gradient with no conductor, falling off sharply with distance, and it strengthens Harmonics Synergy, Projection and Tempering Yield. Transmutatio, the Law of Change (Spatium), says one object in two frames gives two sets of numbers and one unchanged object; it tunes unlike ends into one frame for identity trades. Fixatio, the Binding Flame (Materia), holds what it holds because the alternative state is no longer reachable, and makes the Anchor Construct a relay that cannot be detuned. The glyphs are Ci (Order), Th (Foundation) and Ra (Transcendence). The boundary that moves is whether two coordinates count as the same kind of thing, and the coordinates and the distance stay where they are. The rite supplies the tuning and Transference's standing law does the rest. The Attraction Layer works at both ends, since Transference makes it the oscillator, and the Essence Core in identity trades, where a soul's coordinate is held. Developmental Tier is Harmonic at Splintering, Resonant at Refraction. This is the working Correspondence theory was written for: no consent sought, no debt incurred, only a claim that two things are alike enough to exchange. Degeneracy is correspondence as a number.

The result is an interval of being partly somewhere else, then standing where the other one was, in their circumstances, including the ones nobody mentioned. The counterparty gets the caster's place whether they wanted it or not. Positional exchange, body for body, opens at Splintering at B-Grade. Identity trades (a death for an echo, a memory for an object, a soul for a mirrored construct's housing) open at Refraction at A, with a late push toward S. Unlike-for-unlike trades work because Transmutatio tunes both ends into one frame; the more unlike, the more of the rite is tuning, and any mismatch left over leaves a partial trade. At Refraction the chain is internal and the Anchor Construct is a relay required by geometry, and breaking it restores the sixth-power falloff. The Splintering swap also needs voice, hand, ink or blood, and fails without it. The rite holds only while the officiant's attention and the coupling both hold, so a failure strands instead of fizzling.

The failure is false degeneracy: the ends were less alike than the tuning claimed and the exchange stops at P_max, Wellspring Desynchronization, leaving Spatial Disjunction, both systems holding part of a coordinate that belongs to one. Transmutatio runs through Overchannel, open to every Path with Overflow risk above D and Backlash risk above C; every A-Grade Transposition carries it and no Path protects against it. Inherited on top: Transference's reverse coupling runs the condemned vessel into the officiant at the same rate for the whole interval, the price of soulguarding; Transmutatio's basis loss returns nothing recognizable; Fixatio's anchor can never be retrieved. All can be induced from outside. An old healers' school of sympathy holds that belonging-together has no range, so the bond is the working and the anchor mere insurance. It tunes with real care, trades across an open field, and gets Spatial Disjunction. The exponent is six, and it has never once been moved by sincerity.

**Cost.** Three percent of full reserve a swap, plus 1 percent per relay hop and more uphill; 10 to 15 percent an identity trade; and an Anchor Construct placed for good and never recovered.  
**Counter.** Distance with nothing left on the ground between, a broken anchor, detuning by Family, saturation or noise, or a Silenced chamber.

**What nobody knows.** Whether the soulguarding orders truly traded themselves into condemned vessels during the Great Spirit War, and what they carried back out.

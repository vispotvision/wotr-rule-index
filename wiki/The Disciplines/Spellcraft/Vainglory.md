# THE USURPER IN MY IMAGE (Animatria, a summoned construct)

*Scholars' term: Vainglory, and the thing itself is a Vainglory Eidolon.*

> The wool-factor Ludo Garth, being sued for debt at the Mallow consistory, raised a better Ludo Garth to answer for him: taller, clear-skinned, in a blue coat with all its buttons, smelling faintly of cloves. It spoke so beautifully that the creditors began apologizing. The court was reaching for its seal when Garth's sister stood up at the back and asked it what he had done with their father's boots. It had no idea, never having done anything wrong in its life. A minute and a quarter later it had started to look hungry and Garth had gone the color of tallow on the bench, and the question of which of them owed the money is still in the rolls.

She makes the person she wishes she was, and it keeps getting better, and it starts to want her seat.

**MY GOOD SIDE!** A fragment of her Core stands up in her shape, only finer, and passes for her with anyone who has never seen her worse.  
**TELL THEM ABOUT ME!** It walks into the room ahead of her and does the talking, the fighting and the smiling.  
**EVEN BETTER!** She lets it stray further from the truth, and it grows stronger and hungrier at the same time.  
**LOOK WHAT I COULD HAVE BEEN!** Fetched from a place her Crystal never stood, it fights with skills she never earned, wrong in the specifics.

## How it really works

Give a mirror gain and it returns more than it received, the return goes round again brighter, and the loop grows instead of settling, like a laser that no longer needs an input. That is positive feedback, and the law is Barkhausen's criterion: a loop oscillates once round-trip gain reaches one, then grows as A(t) = A₀ · e^((g − l)·t/τ), exponential in the excess gain. A passive mirror has reflectivity R ≤ 1 and is stable by construction. Only a pump can hold R above one, and no amplifier grows forever. The energy is in the pump, and the pump is the summoner: gain costs whatever keeps it up, continuously and rising, out of the Essence Core she still has to live in.

The fabrication charge sits at the bottom of the Animatria band, 75,000 EU, plus a rising sustain of 10,000 to 25,000 EU a second. Glory is below Refraction, so a deliberate raising needs voice, hand, ink or blood, and if that is taken the sentence does not complete. The floor is Glory, the basic Animatria floor, crossable without meaning to, and it arises as often by accident as design: a failed ascension, a Temperance collapse, or a Trait overreaching its Stage. Thirty to 40 percent leaves the summoner's Shell (the Crystal's outer skin) as heat, sound and light: 22,500 to 30,000 EU on fabrication, 3,000 to 10,000 EU a second while sustained, and rising. The Residue (what a working leaves behind) is the construct itself, and it grows. A room where one stood long deafens Harmonists (listeners to Aether), and past saturation risks a Crystal Fracture Event, every Crystal in the room cracking together, the summoner first.

Three Wellsprings of Limina make it. Mirithane, the Wellspring of Reflection: a true mirror adds nothing, so output honestly reflects inner state, and this art is the flattering correction it forbids, pumped against its pull toward honesty. It strengthens Harmonics Attunement and Tempering Coherence. Eidolyn, the Shaping Mind, deposits along a template with real material strength, and it is the cavity and the shape. Oneirion, the Wellspring of Dream, acts on the space of possible arrangements and fetches the flattering self from a coordinate the Crystal never occupied. The glyph is Mirage (Kaetra) as the boundary that moves, laid under Reverie (Maelor) for the register the correction is drawn from. What moves is the tolerance on the Shell's reflection, how much correction a return may introduce, and Eidolyn then deposits the corrected return as something with real material behavior. The Essence Core is the pump, the Aether Shell is the reflecting surface Mirithane works on, and the Attraction Layer holds the tether, which is why Silence ends it. Developmental Tier at the floor is Harmonic. In theory it is Correspondence refused: the working asserts "this construct corresponds to me", and Mirithane's office is to test that and return the answer unedited. It is a false correspondence held up by a pump.

Each corrected return becomes the next state to reflect, so the construct drifts toward the flattery on an exponential. To strangers it is a better version of the summoner. To anyone who knows them it is an immaculate likeness of an untrue claim, getting worse hourly. The Deviation is the share by which the construct's return exceeds an honest reflection, the excess gain; the loop makes one round trip a six-second turn, so the Deviation doubles every ln 2 ÷ Deviation turns. Sustain scales with the current Deviation over the starting one and doubles each time the Deviation doubles, so a construct straying far from its summoner drains faster the further it strays. It holds only while attention holds, and holds worse than a true Eidolon. It convinces only those who never met the unflattering self.

It has two endings. Gain saturation settles the loop into a steady state set by the cavity and owing nothing to the source: a stable false self able to displace its summoner. Cavity damage burns the mirror, and here the cavity is the summoner's own Shell and Core. A phase-conjugate mirror with gain can produce a corrected wavefront with no incoming signal, so a construct coherent enough to contest its summoner has stopped needing a source. An honest, lossy element in the loop lowers the gain, and below one the oscillation dies. Switching off the pump stops replenishment but leaves stored energy that must go somewhere: a true Eidolon at R ≤ 1 decays when supply stops, but a saturated loop must spend what it holds first, so dismissal is never safe. And a construct that outlives its summoner's control becomes a Synergia-category Phenomena event.

The failure is Coherence fault by design. The construct is lived behavior drifting from stated law, so the summoner's output falls, Thresholds refuse to authorize and Traits argue with them, before anyone has touched them. Inherited on top: Mirithane's mirror broadcasts perfectly any state the summoner would hide; Eidolyn's template defect reproduces every flaw of an aspirational template at scale; Oneirion's projection error makes the construct convincing in the abstract and wrong in the specifics. All of it can be induced from outside. A humanist school holds that a person has no fixed nature and may sculpt the higher form they elect, so it keeps a construct standing to grow toward; its doctrine has no feedback term, and the elected form diverges and displaces the sculptor. They are, I am told, much improved.

**Cost.** Seventy-five thousand EU to raise, then 10,000 to 25,000 EU a second out of the summoner's own Core, doubling as the lie doubles.  
**Counter.** Silence severs it; a witness who has seen the summoner on a bad day out-honests it; another Mirithane practitioner or Oblivara returns the correction to its source.

**What nobody knows.** What becomes of a summoner once the construct has taken their place, and whether anything of them remains inside it.

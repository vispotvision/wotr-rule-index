# Heaven's Hairline

*Celestial Harmonic Shear in the Guild's books, the scholars' term. The ward-cutters of Sallow Quay do not use either name in front of their apprentices.*

## HEAVEN'S HAIRLINE (Celestial Harmonic Shear) (Sing, Stage XII)

Ysmay Brook spent fourteen years on the strongroom door of the Two Anchors in Sallow Quay, nine wards cut one over another in bronze and bloodstone, a masterwork the Ward-Cutters' Company paraded through the streets on a cart with trumpets. On the morning it was hung, somebody across the square raised one hand and drew one slow line in the air. The door stayed on its hinges without a scratch, and all nine wards fell off it at the same instant, like a coat sliding from a peg, leaving a lump of very expensive plain bronze. Brook's guild-brothers still argue over which of the nine she got wrong, and she stood up in the Company's hall and told them, at length, that she got none of them wrong, which is correct and did not help.

She draws one line in the air, and whatever is holding you together comes undone along it.

HEAVEN'S HAIRLINE! One slow line up to a hundred meters long and under three centimeters wide, and the seam resolves with no impact.

ALL THE LAYERS AT ONCE! A ward, an illusion or a stacked construct parts along its resonance, every layer sharing the mode going together at the crossing.

LET IT GO QUIET! Dissolution leads, and the seam collapses into null-phase and never shows.

### How it really works

The real phenomenon is failure along a normal mode, one of the natural ways a structure vibrates. A thing is far weaker along its modes and seams than across its bulk, so severing the mode parts it cheaply, as a wineglass shatters or a laminate peels apart. A driven damped oscillator at resonance amplifies its drive by the quality factor, Q = 1/(2ζ), and for layered structures the criterion is interlaminar shear strength, the weakest number on any assay. The Hairline is a small drive: the caster supplies a driving frequency, the structure amplifies it by Q, and the target's own failure does the damage, so the Grade buys the lock on the mode and leaves the yield to the victim. A mode of a few hundred hertz at Q about 10³ decays in about Q cycles, so resonance cracks ring for a few seconds along the cut. The cut needs one unique mode to find, and anything with no mode, or with several contradictory ones, cannot be severed.

The glyphs are Frequency (Selhar) across Edge (Thalen) for the hundred-meter line. The boundary is the continuity of one named vibrational mode along a line, and the material and the energy are left out of it. So the break looks wrong to anyone expecting a cut. Layers sharing a mode fail together at the intersection; plain matter on the line stays standing. Emanation needs no anchor, and the hand is there for aim, since a 3 cm kerf at 300 m is a matter of 1 × 10⁻⁴ radian. One Shear at full potency per activation.

A line is among the cheapest geometries there are for pulling Aether, with line of sight required. The residue is a seam of grain-boundary damage, sound material either side, that deafens a Harmonist (one who reads a place by ear) along its length. Orrenthal, the Metallic Heart (Wild), says a metal's electrons belong to the lattice and to no atom, so it bends without breaking, and its moral and physical readings are the same reading. Fractura and Basilithe decide whether the cut resolves as splitting divergence or a clean geometric line through law-bound structure. Dissolution, the Unbinding Stream (Wild), lets the seam collapse into null-phase. Tarturon, Cataclysm, Tenebra, Penance, Petralon and Oneirion are held in the lattice, ten currents in all. Ten currents is a Tempering Capacity claim, and exceeding Capacity degrades every active harmonization equally, which is the backlash a large cut returns. Orrenthal wants dry air, conductive ground and clear sight; Faraday-caged interiors (rooms walled in iron mesh), heavy ferrous shielding and dense fog suppress it.

The Aether Shell is made into an oscillator that leaks outward as it cuts, and the vertigo and tone-deafness after are Clouding (the Shell's slow failure) in the sense the working runs on. Developmental Tier Sovereign. Structural fault: a Shell run as a resonant source leaks, and a large cut weakens her other Emanation workings for a while. Field faults, silence, stillness or a void-phase barrier, leave it correct with no mode to find. Orrenthal's intergranular corrosion is this working, attack along boundaries while the bulk stays sound. Basilithe's resonant catastrophe cuts both ways: a pure Basilithe construct can resist and redirect the Shear, yet is most exposed once its note is found. The bleed, 10 to 15 percent, 0.09 to 2.6 TJ, leaves as heat and sound at the Shell, and her own ears pay first.

Nothing outside harmonic physics (true void, full dream-essence, anti-tone fields) and no one at the caster's Stage or higher can be cut. The old harmonic school holds each body carries one proper tone. Its harmonies were ratios between bodies, however, so layers sharing a mode fail together and layers with contradictory tones have no mode at all, and that is the whole of what I would tell a student who came to me afraid of it.

> **What nobody knows.** Whether the practitioner is cutting the frequency that is actually there, or the one she has decided is the lie underneath it.

**Cost.** A set five percent of full reserve a cut, then vertigo, tone-deafness and a backlash across everything she holds.

**Counter.** A silence field, a stilled instant, or several contradictory frequencies held at once, or simply not standing where the raised hand has already said the seam will be.

### Origin

Its author devised this working alone. It carries no filed counter beyond what an opponent works out live in the exchange.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · One hand up and one slow line drawn: the gesture fixes the seam's place and length before it exists, so a watcher knows where not to stand. Afterward the seam rings a few seconds, then lies silent.

**Limits** · 300 m, line of sight, one line up to 100 m, one full Shear per activation; nothing at or above the caster's Stage, nothing outside harmonic physics, nothing with contradictory tones. A large or complex cut can send a backlash into her own law-vector that weakens her other Emanation-level workings for a short while.

**Fails against** · Dense fog, boiled leather, fired clay and shielded rooms; a Cymorath-type silence field; several contradictory frequencies held at once (Fractura beings, dream-entities, shapeshifters, Eidolyn or Oneirion disguise); spread targets, since one line kills one thing; anyone at the caster's Stage.

**Look up** · The Ground of Orrenthal, Fractura and Basilithe on the [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Wild page]]; Orrenthal, Basilithe and Dissolution failures; Tempering Capacity; Shell Clouding; the Emanation Stage table and the Path gates for Penetration and Breadth.

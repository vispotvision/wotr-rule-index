# The Winter Ledger

*Cryost Ascendant in the scholars' term. The art's single known bearer has never been heard to call it anything, and speech is rather the difficulty.*

## THE WINTER LEDGER (Cryost Ascendant) (Vectra, Stage IX)

Peg Aykroyd had the corn-market of Dunmow in the palm of her hand, two thousand hungry people in a roar of red caps and torches, and a speech she had been practicing for eleven years, on the price of bread and the Bailiff's mother. A man walked in through the Fish Gate. Frost ran across the cobbles ahead of him, the torches stopped flickering and stood still like painted flames, a thrown turnip hung in the air, and every voice in the square clipped short. The whole of Peg Aykroyd's speech, as taken down by the Bailiff's clerk with his pen moving very slowly, reads *Bread*. A moment later everything the crowd had brought with it, every shove and scream and all that heat, went out through one fist into the Bailiff's strongroom door, and the corn was free by nightfall.

He writes down every hit you throw and pays it all back at once.

THE BLOOM! Six to eighteen meters of cold in which every moving thing stops being allowed to finish.

THE STILLPOINT! A one-to-two-meter center at the apex where the holding is total.

PAID IN FULL! The whole bank, motion, heat and fear in one ledger, goes out as a single decisive corrective act, and what remains bleeds off as harmless cold.

### How it really works

Stopping a moving body without wasting its energy means storing it, the way a compressed spring does, and that is the Bloom: energy storage by arrest. A field banks every vector that enters it, motion, heat and fear alike, then spends the bank on one decisive act. The first law says a region cools only if energy leaves it, and the Bloom goes cold because every arrested vector's energy is taken out of the volume into the practitioner's Crystal at 1 EU per MJ. The room could never hold that bank itself: elastically it would need 68 times the field's volume of spring steel at its breaking point, and as heat it would turn the air to plasma. The field makes no force of its own, so a still room gives it nothing. It arrests intention as well as motion, so speech clips toward the bare floor of intelligibility, about 39 bits a second.

The glyphs are Lock (Thalen), a counter that occupies a limit so no other can be set there, across Hour (Valen), because the limit occupied is one on when. The boundary is whether a vector inside the radius is allowed to complete, and its size, direction and energy are left alone. Inside, everything slows to blink-speed deltas or stops. Nothing is deflected; it is held, then returned. The trigger is the quietest there is, with no anchor, declaration or gesture: it begins when he enters a scene carrying motion, heat, sound or fear he means to still.

The Aether runs backward from most arts, the field taking in where others pull out. The residue is a cold patch warming slowly from the edge, frost on every covered surface, and a quiet in which a Harmonist (one who reads a place by ear) hears almost nothing. Vohrin (Caloria), one power at two levels, Titan and Wellspring, taken here as Titan-derived, carries the frost-stasis; it stands outside the Sixty's enumerated list. Fixatio, the Binding Flame (Materia), says a cured thermoset chars before it flows, so what it holds is held because the other state is no longer reachable, and it carries the cost. Judicium, the Wellspring of Truth (Fulguria), reads without contact which vectors, kinetic, thermal and emotional, are there to arrest. Caloria is suppressed by thermal equilibrium, saturated cold and oxygen-poor air, and the Bloom manufactures saturated cold, so it degrades its own conditions the longer it holds.

The pull runs inverted here, the field taking in and sending nothing out. The Aether Shell projects and the Attraction Layer holds the radius, and the bank can overfill the Crystal past maximum; a bank carried past that is past safe discharge. Developmental Tier Sovereign. Fixatio's irreversibility means a binding placed in error is permanent, and Materia's phase locking means under enough pressure the lattice commits to one form for good; Vohrin and Judicium bring their own documented failures. The structural fault: the working succeeds and the practitioner does not. Held too long, Fixatio's cure sets the anchor into his body, keeping whatever form he wore. The field fault: with nothing incoming it is correct and does nothing. A paradox-grade or chaos-aligned disturbance has no stable end to withhold and tears the lattice, and a demand to keep motion and stillness at once degrades it. The bleed, 20 to 30 percent, 1.65 to 46 GW, leaves as heat at the Shell, the one heat in the Bloom, which the field must keep working against, and the Master's feedback loop returns it to the bank.

Under the Consent Doctrine the field resolves by criterion and the judgment is Judicium's reading; under the Correspondence Position practitioner and current are one law at two scales. The caloric chemists treat heat as a fluid and read the Bloom as a ledger, a name I have stolen from them. Their theory predicts arrest runs hot and sends fighters in light clothing, and their fighters have been very cold. Against a higher Stage the sum is whether the bank can be finished: dominion stays at combat scale and strike force is whatever you were foolish enough to bring.

> **What nobody knows.** Whether the "verdict" the field resolves toward is the practitioner's own judgment or something the Crystal is selecting for him.

**Cost.** Five percent of full reserve a turn, his own temperature and reactions falling with the field's, and an overfull bank that may set him in place for good.

**Counter.** Read the reversion's shape and refuse to let the bank finish, bring chaos or paradox, or give him nothing to catch and nothing to hit.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · A visible radius goes cold before anything is caught and speech clips short; the colder it is, the more is banked. Afterward frost on everything covered and ground that stays cold.

**Limits** · Six to eighteen meters with a one-to-two-meter Stillpoint at the apex; no force of its own; nothing to do in a still room; overbanking harms its holder.

**Fails against** · Slow, low-energy movement that gives it nothing; a Knot or chaos-aligned disturbance; several exclusive threats at once; no target to discharge into (retreat, cover, a decoy); its own cold, which slows the holder.

**Look up** · Lock and Knot in the Counterplay register; Caloria terrain; the Vohrin note on the [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Wild page]]; Fixatio and Judicium failures; Materia phase locking; the Invocation Stage table and the S Grade row.

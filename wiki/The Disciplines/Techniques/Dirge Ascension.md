# Forty-Five Seconds of God

*Dirge Ascension is the scholars' term. The old soldiers of Sarn Field call that afternoon the day the arrows got heavy, and they are correct in every particular but one.*

## FORTY-FIVE SECONDS OF GOD (Dirge Ascension) (Phenomena, Stage XIV)

Ulf Graycloak could put a shaft through a thrown ring at two hundred paces, and he had the ring collection to prove it, nailed in gold rows to the wall of the Archers' Hall. On Sarn Field he and six hundred of his Company loosed their opening flight at one man standing alone in a scarlet coat, and the flight fell like a dropped sack of nails about forty paces from their own feet, while half the bowstrings parted and the sky turned the color of a bruised peach. A cart rolled uphill. A sergeant who leaned on a fence went through it. For the next forty-five seconds, by the water clock in the baggage, nothing any man on that field knew about his own body or his own bow was true, and the Hall took the rings down that winter.

For forty-five seconds the whole world around him runs on somebody else's numbers.

FORTY-FIVE SECONDS OF GOD! Total inner commitment, and for a kilometer in every direction the laws keep their shape and change their weights.

WRONG WEIGHTS! Survivable force stops being survivable, far things stop being far, and every practiced judgment is calibrated to a world no longer in the room.

THE CLEAN EXIT! At the end of the count Catharsis takes the whole transition as a change of state, and he comes back through recoil without snapping.

### How it really works

The rolls call the state proto-Archonic, his mortal ceiling shed for the length of the count. The real phenomenon is renormalization-group flow: the strengths of the basic forces are constant only at a given energy scale and change with the scale they are read at. Couplings run with the logarithm of scale, dg/d ln μ = β(g), so a shift of a few percent in a coupling means about eleven decades of energy. For forty-five seconds the local laws inside a kilometer run on the numbers of a far deeper scale. A critical point is not stable, so the state is short, and the flow is never partial, moving every coupling that shares a β-function, so everything inside answers rewritten physics. The joules are in the local field, renormalized. The structure that lets them move is his, and structure is what fails.

The glyphs are Era (Valen), the one form naming a limit on scale, across Vastness (Zhaeren) for the kilometer. The boundary is the energy scale at which local couplings are read, inside 1,000 m for forty-five seconds, and no single law is altered. Zenith needs no anchor and the working has no outward component. It ends on the clock, into recoil. The Crystal cannot hold two incompatible renormalizations of one region at once, so it cannot overlap World Echelon. Dominion Sovereignty stays capped at A even while ascended, since the Spirit Path does not open it, and the rewrite of local law runs weaker for it.

Four billion cubic meters of Aether are worked, with the couplings altered and the Essence spent besides. The residue is the most readable there is: every material inside records being sampled at the wrong scale, and Harmonists (those who read a place by ear) there hear everything slightly out of tune for a long time. Exuroth, the Trial Flame (Caloria), says repeated thermal shock then controlled recovery tempers the lattice past what slow cooling reaches, and it supplies the tolerance to survive holding the state open. Catharsis, the Wellspring of Release (Caloria), is a latent-heat law that absorbs a huge transition at constant temperature, giving the state a clean exit where a snap back would be. Judicium, the Wellspring of Truth (Fulguria), is held alongside. Caloria wants steep thermal gradients, combustible air and geothermal ground; Fulguria wants dry air, conductive ground and clear sight.

The failures are old friends. Exuroth's quench cracking is the post-use collapse, which is why repeated use is worse than long use; Catharsis's supercooling is what a failed exit looks like; Judicium's failures ride along. A kilometer-wide Zenith working is a saturation candidate by scale, and past the threshold it causes Crystal Fracture Events in everyone exposed, him included. All three layers carry it: the Core holds the doctrine borrowed against, the Shell carries 2.8 × 10⁶ AU/s, the Attraction Layer holds a kilometer's claim. Developmental Tier Crystallized Soul. The structural fault: the working succeeds and the practitioner does not, burning one percent of the Soul Crystal each use and ending in paradox recoil as the frame returns to its own scale. Below η 1.0 up to 140 GW leaves as heat at the Shell; above it the room pays up to 560 GW and the kilometer is left thin.

The theory is the Debt Position: borrowed authority settled in Crystal mass that never comes back, credit with a collateral clause; the Accord licenses it under Consent. The contemplative hierarchy teaches granted participation from above that perfects and costs nothing, so repeat ascent is purification. This working burns Crystal every time, and that counsel spends a practitioner down to nothing, with the prayers still going.

> **What nobody knows.** Whether the one percent Soul Crystal mass comes back, or whether the practitioner is spending down a finite reserve one use at a time.

**Cost.** 1.26 × 10⁸ EU and one percent of the Soul Crystal, permanent, with a lifetime limit of a hundred uses and recoil every time.

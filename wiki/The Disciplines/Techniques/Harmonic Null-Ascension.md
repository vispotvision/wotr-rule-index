# The Soul-Unstringing Chord

*Harmonic Null-Ascension is the scholars' term. The Minstrels' Company has a standing prize of forty gold for anyone who can name the pitch, and it remains unclaimed.*

## THE SOUL-UNSTRINGING CHORD (Harmonic Null-Ascension) (Harmonia, Stage XII)

The Lord High Steward of Vellacourt rose at his own midwinter feast with a gold cup in his hand and four hundred guests shouting his name, and he had got as far as "My friends" when Jankin Sparrow the lutenist, who had perfect pitch and a reputation for making scenes, stood up on a table and began screaming at everyone to get out. Nobody else could hear anything. Jankin said afterward there had been a sound climbing from under the floor of the world, and that it was the Steward's sound, the one note the man had been since he was born. Nearly a quarter-mile off, at a window, somebody lowered two fingers. Every cup within fifteen paces burst, the great table split down its length, and the place where the Lord High Steward had been standing held a very clean silence and some spilled wine. Jankin has been asked to name the note at every feast since, and he has never once sung it.

She plucks a string that is not there, and it plays the note that holds you together until you stop.

THE SOUL-UNSTRINGING CHORD! Two fingers or a staff pluck a silent chord from the air, and a tone climbs from below hearing, locked on one target out to 500 meters.

THE GOOD SILENCE! Dissolution leads, and the target's form fails into nothing at all.

THE BAD SHATTER! Cataclysm or Fractura leads, and the target fractures or implodes, throwing a burst to fifteen meters.

### How it really works

Push something at its own natural frequency and a small, steady drive builds a huge swing, as a held note shatters a wineglass while the same sound off-pitch does nothing. That is forced resonance past a structure's damping. At resonance the swing is the static response times the quality factor Q = 1/(2ζ), where ζ is the damping, and the drive must sit inside a bandwidth of ω₀/Q to lock. High Q buys amplification and costs bandwidth: at Q near a thousand the drive must hold within one part in a thousand of the true frequency, and the climb starts only once that lock holds. A bridge torn apart by steady wind excites itself with no driving frequency, and that is a different animal; this needs the lock. The drive is small, about a thousandth of the failure load, the mode stores energy cycle by cycle, and the target's own failure releases it with the target's structural strain.

The glyphs are Frequency across Ruin: frequency pinned to a climb in amplitude makes a failure. The boundary is the damping of one named mode in one target. She finds the frequency and pins only ζ low. The tone is locked on what holds the target together, so there is nothing to intercept. At the top the target fractures, implodes or suffers soul-pattern distortion. The lock is contested and the climb is free: Harmonics Attunement against the target's Harmonics Stability, and anyone at or above her Stage is immune. No anchor is needed at Emanation, and the gesture only aims.

A held line to one target and a small burst are all the Aether it pulls. Fulguria wants dry air and clear sight, and dead air or a target with no structural resonance gives it nothing. The burst leaves fifteen meters of ringing ground that deafens Harmonists (those who read a place by ear). Orrenthal, the Metallic Heart (Fulguria), says a metal's electrons belong to the lattice and to no atom, so it bends without breaking, and it finds and holds the target's true frequency. Three secondaries choose the ending among nine held: Cataclysm, a change of kind and no mere change of degree, couples the resonant failure to a shock and over-resolves violently; Fractura runs the fracture along the target's existing flaws; Dissolution, the Unbinding Stream (Limina), reduces the target to silence with no rupture. Basilithe, Tarturon, Tenebra, Penance, Petralon and Oneirion are carried with them. A structure anchored in Basilithe holds its form longest, but its frequency cannot shift to escape a matching drive, so once locked it is driven harder than any other, and strain limit decides.

The Aether Shell runs as a resonant source, and its cost is Clouding (the Shell's slow failure): a harmonic rebound that leaves voice and thought doubled for minutes, a beat from the Shell ringing near its own rate, as if two slightly out-of-phase copies of her were speaking. Developmental Tier Sovereign at Emanation. Structural fault: the Shell, made a resonant source, rebounds, and the doubled voice is it still ringing. Field fault: a true silence field or anti-vibrational current leaves nothing to drive. The bleed, 10 to 15 percent, leaves the Shell as heat and off-mode infrasound, tiny and quiet, so nobody hears it coming, Jankin Sparrow excepted.

The Returning school reads silence as the soul's successful return to the One and shattering as a failed ascent. Believing the tone a blessing, its adherents never bring the damping that stops it, and I have been to the funerals.

> **What nobody knows.** Whether the tone the practitioner hears in her own head during the climb is the target's true resonance, or the one she has decided it deserves.

**Cost.** Eight percent of full reserve for a cast that resolves in one turn, doubling each turn held, plus a rebound and minutes of doubled voice.

**Counter.** Flatten the local resonance to true silence, or split your frequency across several bands so no one tone ever reaches the failure point.

### Origin

This working has one practitioner, its author. It has no filed counter beyond what an opponent works out live in the exchange.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · Two fingers or a staff raised, a string plucked on nothing, then a silence that is not silence; easy to miss at 500 m. After: the burst, ringing ground, a doubled voice.

**Limits** · 500 m line of sight, one target and a fifteen-meter burst; no effect at or above the practitioner's Stage or on anything without structural resonance; ruinous past a few breaths; no drive through shielding above C on Spirit.

**Fails against** · Damping (a silence field, Verdantia or Cymorath); a resonance split across several bands, a Knot, or decoupling through Oneirion; a broken line of sight or a decoy, since every delay doubles the practitioner's bill; spread targets; fog.

**Look up** · The Counterplay register for Knot; the Orrenthal, Cataclysm, Fractura, Dissolution and Basilithe entries and Failures; the harmonic immunity rule; Fulguria's suppressors; the Emanation Stage table.

# THE STAR-FELLED SPIRE

*Speaking, Animatria, Stage XII. The scholars' term is the Obelisk of the Eclipsed Dawn.*

> *Six stars fall, one tower tall,*
> *what you built is none at all.*
> *What you broke will mend by day,*
> *ask her nice, she might not say.*
>
> Skipping rhyme, Wickerby Street, taken down by an archivist who then asked the children who "she" was and got a shrug and a stone

Every child between the rivers skips to that. Few of them have seen the thing itself, a prism the height of a tower growing out of a point of impossible weight, purple-black at the root and dawn-gold at the crown, with a whole district standing in its shadow and nobody on the ground knowing yet which of three things it means to do to them.

**The hook.** Six stars fall into one tower, and in its shadow what's finished comes apart and what's broken comes whole.

**SIX STARS FALL!** The orbiting sigils stop, fall together and the tower rises, up to 800 m of ground under it.

**UNMADE BY MORNING!** (the old roll says Fracture Cascade) A hostile construct, curse or barrier runs backward through its own assembly until there is none of it left.

**NOT ON MY DAWN!** (Ecliptic Aegis) An ally's incoming force is redirected or nullified at the shell.

**THE MORNING BEFORE!** (Oneiric Reversal) Damaged Essence is coaxed toward its own earlier, healthier state.

### How it really works

The practitioner stills her Essence and commits. The trigger is internal, with no outside chain to interrupt, and that stillness is also the mode lock: the mode is fixed at the trigger, and changing it means collapse and a fresh raising. Six orbiting Wellspring sigils collapse into a point of infinite density and the prism climbs out of it. She pulls for a hemisphere of 1.34 × 10⁸ m³, held for as long as the line back to its maker holds. Rock, worked stone and deep foundation favor it, and it cannot be raised at sea, on a ship or from anything aloft.

What it does is a local entropy decrease, decay run backwards inside a boundary, paid for in recorded information: what was coming apart is put back, what was holding is let go. The fluctuation theorem prices a reversal, P(+σ)/P(−σ) = e^(σ/k*B), and Landauer's principle charges k*B T ln2 (2.87 × 10⁻²¹ J at 300 K) for every bit of record cleared. The binding limit on a reversal is storage and never power, because the record must be held somewhere, and clearing it pays the second law back. The glyph is Return, laid across Continuum for the held state and Form for the lattice habit: this region's trajectory is permitted to run back along itself. Its boundary condition moves only the direction in which the field's trajectory may run. The second law goes unopposed and is paid, the charge deferred until the record clears.

The prism holds because of its lattice, a high-stiffness, low-loss resonator that keeps its frequency against forcing, like cut quartz (Q 10⁴ to 10⁷, stability 10⁻⁶ to 10⁻⁸), so it holds its state for its whole span. Below it sits an arch-like compressive anchor, 5 to 50 MPa in compression and about a tenth in tension: loaded in the one direction it does not answer, it parts.

Six Wellsprings carry it. Basilithe, the Stone Will (Materia), is primary, the stiff lattice that external rhythms cannot drive. Tarturon, the Fire of Reckoning (Materia), scales its response with the load above yield and supplies its own oxidizer, so it cannot be smothered once started. Petralon, the Foundation Stone (Vectoria), routes every load into compression, accepting slow creep to avoid a tensile crack. Fractura, the Breaking Point (Vectoria), says past toughness a crack runs and does not stop, and it carries the mirrored load-sharing across up to four instances. Oneirion, the Wellspring of Dream (Limina), holds a system's state as a point in configuration space, the space the field's trajectory is run back through. Tenebra, the Hidden Shadow (Limina), gives absorption and shaped return, the outer shell of the prism. That is four Materia and Vectoria currents against two Limina, the setting's hardest pairing. The practitioner's coherence holds it, and Tempering Capacity caps all six, degrading every harmonization at once if exceeded.

No more than four stand at once, and here is the famous joke with the blood on it. The cap is equal load sharing, like a fiber bundle: each survivor takes up a failed member's load, and past redistribution capacity the whole bundle fails at once instead of shedding its newest member. A fifth appears and brings the others down with it, and four instances at 800 m already sit at the saturation threshold, so a fifth crosses it and every Crystal present, the practitioner's included, risks a Crystal Fracture Event. A Collegium school reads the tower as an engine of return, a thing reverting to its cause, and holds that a permission cannot be overloaded. The bundle consults nobody's nature, and a fifth still brings the four down.

Friend and foe are sorted by the practitioner's judgment and attention, and if that slips, allies are caught. Inside the field all three layers of the Essence pay: the Aether Shell carries lattice and force, the Attraction Layer the severable line to the maker (the Obelisk is Animatria, a thing given a line of its own), the Essence Core the record of what the maker felt. That record is the Reflective Burden: the tower records what she was feeling when she raised it and plays it back to her while it stands. Developmental Tier Sovereign. Between 10 and 15 percent of every pull leaves the Shell as heat, sound and structural bleed; the record is held, never shed, and replayed to the maker.

Each Wellspring bequeaths its fault: Basilithe's resonant catastrophe, where a matching forcing cannot be detuned away; Tarturon's runaway with no stopping condition; Petralon's tensile exposure; Fractura's self-inspection, which spares no bearer. The structural fault proper is Structural Strain, which under prolonged manifestation leaves micro-fractures in the Temperance layers, closed only by Essence stillness. A failed Sealing leaves an operatorless 800 m construct obeying itself. The Coherence fault runs downstream: lived behavior drifting from stated law warps the lattice toward inversion. The Residue is heavy, structural and legible for a long time, and the ground it stood on carries its Resonance and deafens Harmonists (those who read the world by its Aetheric ring).

**Cost.** Almost nothing in reserve for mercy and a fortune for ruin, paid mostly in her own feeling played back at her and in hairline stress that only stillness closes.  
**Counter.** Raw entropy or a comparable law-breaking working saturates the lattice faster than it can re-order and collapses it early; a peer's Locked decree, or a matching forcing, also does the job.

**What nobody knows.** Whether the "unfinished dream" the Obelisk rewrites is read from the target's Essence or written onto it; its author has never been asked to explain the difference and nobody has caught her doing it slowly enough to check.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · Six orbiting sigils stop and fall to a point and the practitioner goes still; that stillness is also the mode lock. Afterward, a district of legible Residue that deafens Harmonists.

**Limits** · 800 m; four instances; mode locked at the trigger; no life created or restored, only Essence-state; anyone at Emanation or higher is immune, and a peer's standing decree contests or deflects a mode.

**Fails against** · The sea, altitude or a moving platform; a peer's decree Locked first; Silence, which severs the line totally; Dissolution, which erodes the lattice; a matching forcing, which breaks Basilithe; four standing emergencies, which tempt a fifth instance.

**Strain** · Genuine internal conflict warps the lattice toward inversion; anything that breaks focus threatens a practitioner already holding four instances and their own replayed feeling.

**Saturation** · Flooding the area with Essence gives everyone present Fracture risk, the one who did it included; with four standing the field is already at the threshold.

**Look up** · The Counterplay register for Lock, Silence and Dissolution; the Materia terrain modifiers; the Materia, Vectoria and Limina entries for these six Failures; the Emanation Stage table; the Strike Force Grade bands.

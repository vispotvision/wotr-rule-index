# THE LODGER BEHIND YOUR RIBS

*Speaking, Animatria, Stage VII. The scholars' terms are Oneiron Parasite and Nightmare Warden, and the second is what physicians still write on the bill.*

The wine factor Hobart Ainsley of Wickerby stopped sleeping in the spring, lost every bid he made at the Lammas sale by one coin, and dropped dead of nothing at his daughter's betrothal feast with a gold cup in his hand and a red ribbon on his sleeve. They laid him out in his best blue. The renderer who came to wash him stopped, put two fingers under the breastbone, and asked the family, loudly and in front of the whole wake, who had been in the house. Nobody ate the funeral meats. Half of Wickerby still swears the widow did it, and it would be a shame to spoil a good song.

**The hook.** A small piece of him moves in behind your ribs, reads your heart, and on his word it leaves all at once.

**RENT'S DUE!** A look or a touch at up to 30 m, and the fragment is planted.

**KEYHOLE!** For one watch of four turns he feels what the host feels, and plans are known as they form.

**BAD NIGHT!** Sleep stops restoring, worst after the deepest nights, and composure spikes at the wrong moments.

**EVICTION!** On command the template is withdrawn and the host's Crystal re-orders around the hole in one moment, and the host staggers.

### How it really works

He plants it by gaze or touch at Refraction, with no outside chain. There is no Seal by design; the Animatria line (the live thread that ties a made thing to its maker) stands in for it, so cutting the line ends it. The pull is small with a long tail: the planting is one act, after which the host's own rest feeds the Warden at the host's own efficiency. A host sleeping somewhere bright, crowded and saturated feeds it poorly.

The real phenomenon is seeded crystal growth, a new phase forming on a foreign template, then coarsening, where the larger structure eats the smaller. A template cuts the nucleation barrier ΔG‡ = 16πγ³/3Δg² by the factor f(θ), about 99 percent at 30°, and a nucleus past r‡ = 2γ/Δg grows while one below it redissolves. Coarsening does not ask whose nucleus is whose. The larger, better-ordered structure takes material from the other, so a strong host rejects the graft and a long-held one becomes the host's. Rest-driven relaxation follows a stretched exponential, so one long undisturbed night feeds the graft more than six naps.

It seats behind the ribs at the base of the heart, where about 80 percent of vagal fibers carry the body's felt state upward, and that is where feeling is read. Core feeling is low-dimensional, about 10 bit/s, far below any carrier's capacity, so watching is cheap and the graft is the expense.

The glyph is Form, laid across Passage for the crossing into the host and Mind for the addressing: this fragment may hold a shape here. The boundary condition is the nucleation threshold inside another Crystal, moved so that a foreign fragment counts as a valid template; deposition, growth and coarsening follow unaided. Three Limina Wellsprings do the rest. Oneirion, the Wellspring of Dream, primary: every arrangement is a coordinate in configuration space, which is how the graft crosses into the host. Eidolyn, the Shaping Mind: a supersaturated medium deposits along a template's geometry, and here the template is the practitioner's intent. Hypnather, the Sleep Crown: order grows only while at rest and cannot be forced by ordinary means, so the Warden anchors to the host's rest and waits.

The detonation is the same physics run to its end. The graft drops below critical radius at once and redissolves, and the host's Crystal re-orders around it in one moment, with the energy spent in the Crystal and none in tissue or air. The host's layering has already weakened from inside by then.

The Core pays most, because the Warden is a small, recallable detachment of the practitioner's own Core. The Attraction Layer holds the live line and the Shell holds its shape, Developmental Tier Resonant. If it is destroyed, the pain comes home, the Dream Wound. Left too long, it reports the host's fears back as if they were his, the Personality Imprint. The inherited failures are Oneirion's projection error; Eidolyn's template defect, every flaw in the intent grown inside the host, a Boundary fault that runs on inside the host's architecture after the practitioner stops; and Hypnather's interrupted relaxation, locking the host worse ordered. The Coherence fault follows downstream: surveillance made of the practitioner's own Core returns other people's dispositions to them, and the drift runs toward identity bleed. At η 0.65, 35 percent of the planting, each watch and the detonation leaves the practitioner's Shell as heat and sound; the sustain between watches is the host's.

The Residue has a location, and the location is a body. A host carrying a Warden carries a trace a competent physician can read off the living, and a renderer reads better off the dead. A Collegium physicians' school reads it as a morbid idea lodged in the body's governing seat and treats it as disease. A memory-cleansing rite does purge it, though a disease has no author reading the heart from 30 m.

**Cost.** Shares of reserve for planting, watching and evicting, and when somebody kills the Lodger, the pain comes home to him wherever he stands.  
**Counter.** A shield that refuses implantation, a rite that purges it, a Wellspring built on ending in place of growing, or a cut line back to him.

**What nobody knows.** Whether a Warden left in long enough ever fully comes back, or whether some part of it stays behind.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · At planting, a look or touch at up to 30 m. Afterward, diagnostic: sleep that fails to restore, composure failing on a schedule, a trace behind the ribs; when it dies, the practitioner flinches wherever they stand.

**Limits** · 30 m; it needs a subconscious to root in; a strong mental shield refuses it; the host shapes what it becomes; the live line can be cut.

**Fails against** · Silence, total: a host who feels suddenly lonely inside a Silence has just had it go dark. The practitioner's death or distraction; a memory-cleansing rite; Mortalis; a shared-dream ritual; an empathic Wellspring; distance beyond 30 m.

**Host** · A disciplined, self-honest host coarsens the Warden into themselves, and the reports become distortions of a person the practitioner cannot read.

**Look up** · The Counterplay register for Silence; the Animatria rule; Mortalis and the empathic Wellsprings; the Limina entries for Oneirion, Eidolyn and Hypnather Failures; the Refraction Stage table.

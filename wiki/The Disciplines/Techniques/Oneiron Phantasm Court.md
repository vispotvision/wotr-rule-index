# INQUISITION OF THE UNCONFESSED

*Speaking, Animatria, Stage VII. The scholars' term is the Oneiron Phantasm Court, and defendants have other words for it.*

Provost Anselm Dray of Carrowmere gave the harvest feast in crimson, with a roast swan on every table and the whole grain-guild drunk on his wine. Between the swan and the pears, a second Anselm Dray stood up behind his chair, thinner, sober and very calm, and began telling the hall where the winter grain had gone, by the barrel and by the barge. It never touched him. It did not need to. By the pears he was answering it, shouting, weeping, correcting its figures, and the guild's clerks had started writing it down.

**The hook.** The you that you won't admit to stands up behind you and says it out loud.

**ALL RISE!** The target's own self, editing off, stands up behind them, within 140 m and without any line of sight.

**THE ACCUSED SPEAKS!** It states what they have been declining to know, in their own voice, and the damage is done by the target, to the target.

**CONTEMPT OF COURT!** While his attention holds, it stays standing, carrying weight in the room, and when he looks away the figure goes and the loosening stays.

### How it really works

First comes a prior close reading of the target, and often a soft pressure of sleep in which nearby minds recall old things. At Refraction the trigger is an internal declaration, within 140 m. He pulls a 140 m addressing envelope and a very small deposition. Limina is favored by darkness, silence and ruins and suppressed by saturated Aether, crowds and active Domains, and a field that strips a region's Aetheric memory denies any template.

What stands up is an extrapolation made to stand. The Court raises the state a person's Crystal would settle into given infinite rest, their undefended self, and puts it on its feet behind them to say what they will not. The law is the Kauzmann problem: a cooling liquid's excess entropy extrapolates to zero at a finite temperature, yet relaxation time diverges first (τ → ∞), so the target state can be calculated and may never be reached. Someone must perform the extrapolation and supplies the part not measured. Whether that state exists at all stays open, and the verdict destabilizes the practitioner as hard as the target. Why it works on a person is plainer. People tell remembered from perceived by rules that fail predictably, and self-made material feels more credible, so your own withheld truth in your own voice is maximum dissonance, and the dissonance keeps working after the figure goes.

The figure is an avatar deposited into Aether. A 70 kg body made of matter would need 58 m³ of air condensed, so nobody bothers. It is load-bearing like a push (about 500 N), and it never strikes. Its category is Animatria, Spirit-Aligned, a projection of consciousness into a structured avatar: a Dream-Self Tribunal in exactly the sense Eidolon Crafting names.

The glyph is Truth, laid across Mind for the addressing and Form for the deposition: this configuration may be stated. The boundary condition is that the state a system is aimed at may be instantiated without the system arriving there, and the three ordinary laws then build a person from it. Those three laws are its Wellsprings, all Limina. Oneirion, the Wellspring of Dream, primary: every arrangement is a coordinate in configuration space, so the Court addresses its target with no line of sight. Eidolyn, the Shaping Mind: a supersaturated medium deposits faithfully along a template, and the template is a coordinate the practitioner located. Hypnather, the Sleep Crown: a glass relaxes toward the order it never reached, only at rest and never by ordinary force, and the Court finds that aim without asking the target to rest.

It needs a live subconscious, a genuine unresolved contradiction and that prior reading; with no gap between waking and settled self there is no coordinate, and the Court has no verdict to hand down. Attention is both Seal and duration. The Core pays, because the coordinate passes through the practitioner's own projection, leaving Identity Bleed (he keeps a fragment of what it carries back) and Dream Echo Fatigue, a gap in short-term memory of his own. The Attraction Layer drifts toward others' fears, Developmental Tier Resonant. The failures come down the line: Oneirion's projection error, Eidolyn's template defect, so the bench is partly built from the prosecutor, and Hypnather's interrupted relaxation. The Boundary fault is the template defect running the law somewhere unintended, and that place is the practitioner. The Coherence fault runs downstream: an Attraction Layer built on other people's fears drifts from stated law toward dissociation. At η 0.65, 35 percent of a small pull leaves the Shell as heat and sound, which is why the Court is quiet.

In the room the Residue is a deposition site that briefly deafens Harmonists; in the target it is Soul Crystal coherence loosened around the contradiction, readable later by a physician or renderer. A Collegium tribunal school reads conscience as an inner court and admits the Court's findings as binding evidence. This bench, though, is located by the prosecutor, and the extrapolated self may not be a place at all.

**Cost.** A few percent of reserve, and every convening costs him the same damage it deals, by the same route.  
**Counter.** Have nothing hidden; a mind whose conscious and undefended selves already agree returns no verdict at all, and that is a choice of conduct anyone can make.

**What nobody knows.** Why the confession, once mirrored back at the practitioner, can destabilize him exactly as hard as it destabilizes the target.

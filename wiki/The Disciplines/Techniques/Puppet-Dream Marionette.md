# YOUR LEGS HAD A BETTER IDEA

*Speaking, Harmonia, Stage VII. The scholars' term is the Puppet-Dream Marionette.*

Sergeant Ebbo Ollery held the Kettlewell gate for twenty-two years and never once took a step back, and the town painted him on the gatehouse in red and gold to say so. On the day of the raid he turned around on top of his own wall, in full view of both armies, with a look of deep surprise on his face, and ran off the edge of it. They say he was still trying to explain as he went by the second-floor windows. Eighty paces off, a thin person in a sea-green coat flinched through exactly the same stride and scratched an elbow.

**The hook.** If you're scared, he can make your body do the running away it was already planning.

**GO ON, THEN!** A frightened target's legs take the flight they were rehearsing, within 60 m and with no line of sight.

**WRONG WAY!** An attack already in motion turns into the imagined flinch, so a parry becomes the very thing the parrier feared.

**OFF THE WALL!** A guard steps back off a wall with his own feet, on his own muscle, toward the wrong place.

### How it really works

There is almost nothing to see at casting. At Refraction the trigger is an internal declaration within 60 m, and the pull is tiny: one threshold, for one turn. Fluxia (the Family of fluids and flow) needs a medium and is favored by water, humidity and pressure difference, and a dry vault is a Fluxia prison. Limina wants darkness, silence and few people.

What happens is a threshold released on a motor program already loaded. A frightened body carries out the escape it was rehearsing, at the wrong moment and in the wrong direction, while its owner watches. The law is the tipping point: near a saddle-node bifurcation the push needed to switch a system tends to zero, and fear holds the flight program primed just below firing, as the brain's readiness signal shows 1 to 2 s before movement. Driving a body from outside is expensive, slow and shallow, so the working only releases what fear already loaded, and a calm mind has nothing to release. This is the whole art: his working does about no work on the limb at all. The target's own ATP swings it.

The forecast half comes from Somnalis, the Dream Veil (Fluxia), primary: a turbulent system is read as a weighted spread of adjacent futures, like a fifty-member weather ensemble, and the practitioner seeds one branch. Seeding a branch the target was only 11 percent committed to produces a stumble and no hijack, and that is Somnalis's ensemble collapse. Oneirion, the Wellspring of Dream (Limina), means the working acts in configuration space, so line of sight is not required. Eidolyn, the Shaping Mind (Limina), templates the seeded image so it counts as the target's own program. Their failures come along: Oneirion's projection error and Eidolyn's template defect. Fluxia against Limina pull apart, a tension resolved by Temperance advancement or a Compound Trait, and Tempering Capacity caps all three.

The glyph is Mind, governing which loaded arrangement may execute, laid across Passage for the crossing into the target's manifold. The boundary condition is one threshold, the inhibition between a prepared program and its execution, and the target's own physiology does the rest. It produces flight specifically, redirects an attack already in motion, and acts inconsistently on non-humanoid targets. The horror of it lies in recognition, and paralysis never enters into it: the motion is theirs, made now, toward the wrong place. It lasts one turn and the program is spent, since a released motion cannot be released twice.

The bill lands on the Shell. Panic residue clings to the practitioner's own Shell and fades only with time, driving it toward Clouding, and that long-term Structural fault arrives long before Starvation. The Attraction Layer takes the read and drifts toward others' fears, and the Core goes untouched, Developmental Tier Resonant. His own frame echoes every forced motion, the Sympathetic Twitch, and the Echo Feedback clouds him further. At η 0.65, 35 percent of a very small pull leaves the Shell as heat and sound, and the loss that matters is the residue that stays. The Boundary fault is ensemble collapse.

A Collegium ideomotor school reads it as imagined movement discharging with its inhibition removed, and drills "recognize and refuse." No will out-pushes a push that tends to zero, and the school's graduates are very good at explaining this on the way down.

**Cost.** Two percent of reserve, his own body twitching through every motion he forces, and panic on his Shell that will not wash off.  
**Counter.** Do not be afraid; failing that, a struck nerve, a drilled pain startle, sustained Spirit Path discipline or a lucid hold on your own dream-logic severs the thread before it completes.

**What nobody knows.** Whether the fear-motion he seeds is drawn from the target's own mind, or is one of his own that the target only supplies the body for.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · Almost none at casting; the first sign is a good soldier moving badly. A beat later the practitioner's hand twitches through the same arc, and fresh panic sits on their Shell.

**Limits** · 60 m; one turn; a frightened mind only; unreliable on non-humanoids.

**Fails against** · A mind without fear; a dry room; a drilled pain startle, a faster loop; Letheveil clarity; a lucid hold on one's own dream-logic; Knot on the path; the per-use cost, until the Shell clouds.

**Look up** · The Counterplay register for Knot; the Fluxia terrain entries; Letheveil; the Fluxia and Limina entries for Somnalis, Oneirion and Eidolyn Failures; Shell Clouding; the Refraction Stage table.

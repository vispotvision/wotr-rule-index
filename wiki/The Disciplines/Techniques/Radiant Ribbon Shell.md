# THE TRUTHBOUND GALE

*Spellcraft, Harmonia, Stage VII rising to VIII. The scholars' term is the Radiant Ribbon Shell.*

When the cog *Margery Tun* broke her back on the Skar in a gale, with rain coming in sideways and the sea the color of old copper, Choir-sister Bettany Ruck stood on the poop and laid one sentence across two hundred and fifty meters of screaming water to the men on the headland: the line is coming on your left. They heard it as if she stood at their shoulder. Forty-one came ashore along that rope. Then someone on the rocks called out asking if the master was alive, and she said yes, and he was not, and the next thing she tried to say fell into the sea twenty meters out with a hiss. She rode the last of the wreck in with her jaw clenched and has told the truth about everything since, at some cost to her friendships.

**The hook.** She talks, and the air does what her voice says, for as long as she isn't lying.

**HUSH NOW!** (Hushfield) Noise thins toward the rim of her eight meters, hands steady and aim improves.

**CARRY THAT!** (Carry) One phrase arrives 200 m off, sounding near and no louder than speech.

**MIND THE RIBBON!** (Cut Lamina) Her voice thins to an edge that parts cloth and cord and never flesh.

**STEP ON IT!** (Air-Step) She moves 30 m along a line she did not seem to choose.

**FEATHER OATH!** (the Feather Vows) A token takes the vow; true, it chimes, and false, it dulls.

### How it really works

Someone speaks and the air misbehaves: dust holds a line, pressure lies in bands on the chest, and sound thins toward a rim. The work runs through four gates in fixed order, Set, Place, Speak, Recover, and Recover is the Seal and where the cost lands. Above Refraction the Stage needs no anchor, but the medium needs a living voice and breath, and nothing sustains once the caster stops breathing the pattern. She pulls modestly: an 8 m hush, a 250 m corridor, a 5 m edge. Fulguria (the Family of light, sound's cousin the field, and the clear sight line) wants dry air and clear sight and fails in shielded interiors and fog; Limina wants quiet and fails in crowds. An echo-dead space starves it.

The real phenomenon is acoustic beamforming, sound aimed through a wide coherent opening: a voice shapes the air into smooth laminar bands. A beam's half-angle is θ ≈ 1.22 λ/D, so a 10° beam at 500 Hz needs about 5 m of coherent aperture, and beamed, a shout's worth of power (15 mW) is clear at 250 m. A trained voice's formant near 2.5 to 3 kHz makes a better aperture. The Hush is a refractive shadow, like wind shear bending sound away outdoors: the laminar interior against turbulent air throws noise up and out at the rim, about −9 dB at 8 m. Sound alone cannot part a rope (radiation pressure p = I/c is about 28 Pa at 160 dB), so the Cut is the practitioner's Shell carried along the band's edge, with the sound only as guide. The modes are Hushfield, Carry, Cut Lamina and Air-Step, with a Choir mode for several voices and the Feather Vows as tokens.

The glyph is Frequency, governing which frequencies may arrive, laid across Truth for the Judicium reading and Silence for the Hush. The boundary condition is whether the air in a region may be turbulent; pinned laminar, ordinary acoustics beams, carries, refracts and guides the Shell.

Three Wellsprings stand behind it. Judicium, the Wellspring of Truth (Fulguria), primary: no substance can present another's spectrum, so a Crystal, technique or lie is read by its lines. A lie told to the practitioner warbles a half-step flat, about 6 percent, an arguable sensation and no verdict; the art makes room for truth to travel and compels no one to speak it. Cymorath, the Air of Ascent (Fulguria): space is a set of available gradients, and movement is taking one already there. Anamnesis, the Wellspring of Remembrance (Limina): objects store their history readably, like the trapped charge scholars read in old fired clay, and it carries the Feather Vows, which chime true and dull false. Two Fulguria currents compound against one Limina, and Tempering Capacity caps all three. Each brings a failure: Judicium's spectral overload, the opposite of a hush; Cymorath's drift, arriving somewhere efficient but unmeant; Anamnesis's thermal resetting, so testing a vow can erase its record.

The Shell pays, and its Resonant Purity, how well its output matches the Core's intent, is the working's whole efficiency. The Attraction Layer carries the vows, Developmental Tier Resonant, then Radiant. So the true fault is Ribbon Fray, a Coherence fault: anger, conflict or curated speech turns flow to hiss, shrinking range and dulling the Cut when most needed. Range and clarity both key to the truth of what is carried. In an anechoic vault, snowfield or bale-filled hold the art runs correctly and produces nothing, a Field fault. At η 0.60 to 0.70, 30 to 40 percent leaves the Shell as heat and sound, which competes with the signal; at Transcendence 20 to 30 percent, and the first Domain recycles some. The Residue sits briefly in the air, deafening Harmonists while it lasts, and permanently in the vow-tokens.

A Collegium figure school, from sand drawing mode shapes on a bowed plate, holds that form is made of vibration and trains pure tone. Purity turns out to be conscience and has nothing to do with pitch, and a perfect liar still frays.

**Cost.** One or two percent of reserve at a time, hoarseness and double-hearing afterward, and range lost for every curated sentence; cutting against conscience costs her voice for a while.  
**Counter.** A grapple and a covered mouth, an echo-dead vault, or a chaos-aligned anti-cohesion field gives the shell nothing to carry.

**What nobody knows.** Why the Feather Vows chime true and dull false, with no documented mechanism connecting the chime to the wearer's actual honesty rather than only to what the shell can measure.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · Someone speaking and the air not behaving: dust holding a line, pressure in bands on the chest, sound thinning toward a rim, a room gone steady. Afterward a hoarse voice and a practitioner hearing everything twice.

**Limits** · A living voice; nothing held without breath; dead in an echo-dead space; Hush only to about 30 kn; no Cut on flesh; no compelled truth; no defensive component.

**Fails against** · A grapple and a covered mouth; an echo-dead room or a chaos-aligned anti-cohesion field; a shielded, crowded interior; a practitioner who lies or curates; mutism or inaction; gradients shaped for the Air-Step's drift; a bolt from beyond the corridor.

**Look up** · The Fulguria and Limina terrain entries; the Judicium, Cymorath and Anamnesis Failures; Resonant Purity and Ribbon Fray; the Refraction and Transcendence Stage tables.

# THE THOUSAND-HAMMER TITHE

*Cutting, Write, Stage VIII. The scholars' term is Sovereign's Reprisal. Its author says one thing over it: "Impact is tribute."*

The Tributaries of Coldharbor built a whole theology on this idea, painted their cuirasses the color of fresh egg and refused for forty years to give anything back. Blows were a form of homage, and the more you held, the more of a sovereign you were. Their old master Ancel Thorne stood in the gate of his own hall through a day-long siege in a rain of hammers, grinning, while his monks sang his tally from the wall. At dusk the last gate fell and he finally opened both hands to answer them. Nothing came out. The conduit had silted up years earlier, with no mark to warn him, and for most of a decade the great man had been a very calm person being beaten with hammers.

**The hook.** Hit him all you like; he keeps every blow and hands them all back at once.

**THOUSAND-HAMMER TITHE!** Blunt blows land clean and give nothing back up the arm, and the runed harness banks them.

**ALL PAID BACK!** The sigil over his eye brightens, and everyone within eight meters stops being on their feet at the same moment, those who hit hardest most.

**WITH INTEREST!** The release spiked from reserve, two to five times what was put in, at a price that rises very quickly with the size of the debt.

### How it really works

There is no glow and no gather, only blows landing clean and returning nothing, and then the brightening of the sigil. The harness is cut (a working inscribed in material), a fixed inscription that discharges on trigger and needs no continuous attention, and its substrate is its anchor at every Stage. Judicium likes dry air and clear sight and fails behind iron and in fog; Cinerion likes steep heat gradients and fails in cold equilibrium. A cold, iron-shielded stone fortress in winter sits in both suppression lists.

The physics is impulse-gated storage and all-round discharge: a runed harness banks blunt blows instead of letting them finish, then returns the whole bank at once as a single pressure step through everyone within eight meters. The law is impulse. What is banked is momentum and what goes out is momentum, and the first law says the joules are the attackers', plus whatever the wearer adds to spike them. The threshold reads total impulse, so a hammer (20 kg·m/s at 200 J) trips it, and a heavy arbalest bolt (9.4 kg·m/s at 440 J) or any piercing strike does not trip it cleanly. Taking a man off his feet costs about 80 kg·m/s and 40 J through his body, which is cheap; doing it by air blast at 8 m would take 17 to 50 MJ, which is why the Reprisal couples through bodies. The bank is not in the metal, as the steel figure in the table shows.

At the top, Overchannel spikes the release to 2 to 5 times the sustainable S ceiling, 8.4 to 121.5 TJ. At that scale it is a kiloton event with a fireball near 120 m, discharged through the wearer's spine, which survives about 1.2 MJ.

The glyphs are Lock for the banking, Measure for the threshold, Return for the discharge. The boundary condition is where an impact is permitted to finish: the harness adds nothing to the blow, it says not yet, and the transfer completes into the wearer's Crystal in place of bone. The boundary is a when and never a how much, so ending it releases everything. Storage erased every blow's direction, so the bank leaves as one ring in all directions and does not choose targets.

Judicium, the Wellspring of Truth (Wild): no substance can show another's spectrum, so the bank is read without spending any of it. It strengthens Gnosis Acuity and Analysis. Cinerion, the Ash Veil (Wild): what survives a fire is stock and never waste, so a blow becomes stock in place of damage. It strengthens Tempering Yield and Harmonics Stability. Their failures come along. Judicium's spectral overload misreads the threshold in a crowd. Cinerion's fouling coats the conduit so the bank silently stops filling at the moment of greatest accumulation, with no mark to warn.

In doctrine it is Debt, worn plainly: a blow is booked as tribute and called in as one payment at the creditor's choosing, and Residue is the mechanism that lets the debt be held. The Aether Shell carries both bank and discharge, which run through it, never around it, so the bill lands in the spine, and Resilience Fortification redirects hostile Essence through structure, Developmental Tier Radiant. After a release the eight meters around the wearer deafen Harmonists.

The failures are Structure faults: fouling; a misread threshold; overfill forcing the release at an unchosen moment; Overchannel fracture that accumulates and becomes permanent; and a broken inscription. Waste leaves as heat and sound at the wearer's Shell and down the spine, 20 to 30 percent at Master; at ceiling scale the fraction coupled into the body is fatal. The Tributaries read banked force as sovereignty and never release; fouling silts their conduits unseen, and their masters learn their armor stopped working only when they finally answer.

**Cost.** Nearly free against small blows and ruinous against large ones, and every release sends Spinal Aether recoil up the back and a few seconds of eyes-shut vertigo.  
**Counter.** Pierce it or keep your distance: a point through the plates slips the threshold and breaks the inscription, and ranged harassment never gives it a blunt blow to bank.

**What nobody knows.** Whether the harness has a ceiling past which it cannot hold any more, or whether its author has simply never been hit hard enough, for long enough, to find out.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · No glow or gather, only blows landing clean and returning nothing; then the sigil over the eye brightens, and that is all the warning. Afterward a still figure with eyes closed amid an eight-meter ring of fallen men.

**Limits** · No reprisal without a banked impact, and it cannot fire on nothing; all or nothing, no target choice; eight meters; one full answer to a large input per full reserve.

**Fails against** · Piercing through the plates, since a spike both slips the threshold and breaks the inscription; ranged harassment; a cold iron-shielded interior; a finishing point Locked first; the wearer's allies crowded around it; a large input, and the cost of answering it.

**Fouling** · Small blows bank without ever making a moment worth answering; fouling gives no warning, and the conduit silts.

**Recovery** · The seconds after a release pass with eyes shut and careful breathing; they are the wearer's only unguarded time.

**Look up** · The entries for Judicium and Cinerion and their Failures; cut wards and their substrates; the Counterplay register for Lock and Knot; Overchannel; the Transcendence Stage table.

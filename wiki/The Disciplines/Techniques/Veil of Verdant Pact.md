# THE DEATHLESS TIMBER KEEP

*Speaking, Domain Weaving, Stage V. The scholars' term is the Veil of Verdant Pact. It passes from hand to hand among hedge-workers and grandmothers, and nobody ever invented it.*

When the raiders fired the great tithe barn at Burrow Mill, with forty villagers, a priest and a very angry goat inside, old Mab Sorrel walked into the yard in a flowered apron, stamped her foot and shouted at the ground. Wood came up out of the dirt at a running pace in a green roar, closing over the lot of them in one second, with the light going emerald and then dim. Fire arrived and the char turned to new bark. Somebody threw a torch and it got a window box. Mab stood in the middle swearing, flinching half a beat late at each blow, burned on arms nobody had touched, and when the raiders got bored there was a very large, very ugly new building in Burrow Mill. It is still there. It has been a tavern for ninety years, and it still grows.

**The hook.** A house of wood grows over you and your friends in a second, and hitting it just makes more house.

**DEATHLESS TIMBER KEEP!** The rim closes at 5 m/s and meets overhead in one second, over the caster and every ally within 5 m.

**NEW ROOM!** Struck, the spot thickens and darkens where it should splinter, and the blow's energy can only extend it.

**BARK FOR CHAR!** Fire arrives and the char becomes new bark; frost arrives and the numbness becomes sap-slow warmth.

### How it really works

It is declared, and below Refraction its anchor is a word aloud or a fixed gesture chain, and the dome starts closing at once. Vitalia (living biomass, warmth, blood nearby) and Materia (rock, deep foundation) feed it, so living soil over rock suits it. The room pays for the wood, and it is raised best over the wounded. A creaking tear comes from everywhere, growth runs over at a running pace, and the light goes green then dim.

The physics is indeterminate growth used as an energy sink: a wooden dome with no finished size has no shape to damage, so the energy of a blow can only extend it. A structure with no terminal form turns energy above its elastic limit into more structure, at about 17.5 MJ per kilogram of wood, and its mineral frame is allotropy, one substance in another lattice. At B-Grade the build buys a shell under three centimeters thick, a screen and no bunker. A point load breaks it, and drying the wood beats it where fire cannot. The dome grows about 2,700 times faster than bamboo, because the speed comes from re-latticing Essence already present and owes nothing to cell division. Char insulates sound wood and the water in it buys delay, so fire takes most of an hour, and dried wood chars twice as fast.

The glyphs are Renewal for the growth law and Form for the re-latticing. Two boundaries move: the size at which growth stops is removed, and which lattice the Essence may occupy is freed. Verdantia, the Green Pulse (Vitalia): carbon fixation and indeterminate growth, so the dome may be extended without limit. Materia Primordia, the First Substance (Materia): allotropy, the same Essence re-latticed as wood outside and mineral bracing inside, which also sets the rate. Old mineral seams turn up threaded through its roots on every occasion anyone has checked. Verdantia's etiolation is the first failure: on starved ground the dome grows long, thin and unable to stand. Materia Primordia's phase locking is the second: a heavy enough strike commits the practitioner's lattice for good.

In doctrine it is Domain Weaving, read as Debt: every unit converted is logged against the practitioner's own capacity to heal, with Residue beneath since the growth is the record. The Attraction Layer pays, projected outward as grown matter, with a continuous bleed shed into a structure made of fuel. Ardency (Density) governs whether a ward outlasts what hits it, with Vitality (Regeneration, Hemostasis). On the Fate Path with Attraction second, nothing binds at B-Grade; thickening past B meets the Body Path gates, Flourishing for Density and Splintering for Regeneration.

Every burn, break and poison it absorbs is felt in full by whoever raised it for the second before the wood catches up. While it stands, hairline Crystal fractures inside slowly close. The Field fault comes on depleted ground, where it rises taller and thinner and cannot hold; the other failures are closure lag, Exuroth's drying, Nihiloth's null (the absence of what growth needs, which the dome cannot convert), phase locking and heavy-metal poison. Between 30 and 40 percent of the spend leaves the Shell as heat, continuously.

The Greening healers read the flow of life as a stock, raising the Veil early and holding it long. The practitioner is only the channel, and roots take up lead and thallium through the routes meant for zinc and iron.

**Cost.** Five percent of reserve to raise and two a turn, and the raiser feels every blow, burn and poison in full before the wood catches up.  
**Counter.** Strike in the one second it takes to close; after that, Exuroth's drying, Nihiloth's null, Absolution's flames, Tenebra's decay or heavy-metal toxin in the roots.

**What nobody knows.** Whether the dome draws on Verdantia's law alone, or on something older that Verdantia only resembles, since ancient Materia Primordia mineral seams have turned up threaded through its root system on every occasion anyone has checked.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · Tonnes of wood in a second and the noise it makes, green failing light; the raiser flinches late at each hit; a dome too tall and thin is a weak one; afterward mineral seams and the smell of sap.

**Limits** · Nothing already past 5 m or striking in the first second; no point loads; no ground at sea or on scaffolds.

**Fails against** · A strike in the closing second; a lost anchor; drying by Exuroth; Nihiloth, Absolution's flames or Tenebra's decay; starved or poisoned ground; harm to the raiser through the dome. A second hit on one spot does nothing.

**Look up** · The Vitalia and Materia favor lists; the Verdantia and Materia Primordia Failures; Exuroth, Nihiloth and Absolution entries; the B-Grade Strike Force row; the Splintering Stage table.

### Origin

Veil of Verdant Pact is a documented Vitalia/Materia ward of anonymous, folk-transmitted origin, known and taught rather than invented by one caster. Its established countermeasures are Absolution's flames bypassing it, Tenebra decaying the vines, and heavy-metal toxins weakening the roots.

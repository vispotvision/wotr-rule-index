# THE COLD HANDSHAKE

*Bodily speaking, Vectra, Stage VI. The scholars' term is the Vorynn Execution Strike. A hunter's art, and hunters do not shout it.*

The Frigorific college held cold a thing to emit, and it built a white tower on the Skell moors where its masters trained to cool at range, in robes of pale blue, with frost in their beards for show. In the bad winter they were called on to stop a Well-born beast the size of a tithe barn, and they lined up on the ridge and did their absolute best. It walked directly to them, in the dark, in a snowstorm, because every joule they had ever taken had to leave through their own Shells, and their coldest masters stood out in that storm as the warmest things for ten miles. A hunter finished it the next morning by walking up and shaking it by the foreleg.

**The hook.** He touches you, that limb stops answering, and the next blow has nowhere to go.

**HANDSHAKE.** A touch that is no blow, then the limb stops arguing, stays where it was going and will not come back.

**FOLLOW-THROUGH.** A locked limb cannot give, so the same blow lands two to four times harder for no extra energy.

**COLD GROUND.** Cinerion densifies the cold past Vohrin alone and leaves a cold remnant over the kill ground.

### How it really works

Upstream of the touch are the blood-pact ignition already burning, without which there is no strike at all, and a close under Tenebra's cover. Below Refraction the anchor is the fist, and a lapse of attention ends it. He pulls Caloria, which feeds on steep gradients and fails in saturated cold, and Limina for the approach. The heat taken leaves through the Shell into the air, so a warm closed room slowly spoils the working.

The real phenomenon is a contact heat sink: a touch pulls heat from the outer half-millimeter of skin, blocking shallow nerves and locking the muscle rigid, because cold slows letting go far more than tightening (cold contracture). The law is Fourier conduction at a boundary the practitioner sets, and the cooling front only reaches L ≈ √(αt), so pulling harder freezes the surface and goes no deeper. The cold itself is small: a fist-sized touch freezes about 5 g, 1.7 kJ and never more than about 20 kJ, skin-deep frostbite and a shallow nerve block, and the yield belongs to the follow-through. A defender stops a blow by giving, F = E/d, and a locked limb cannot give. Nerve conduction falls about 1.5 to 2 m/s per degree and fails below 7 to 10 °C, and the calcium pump that lets muscle relax halves its rate every 7 to 10 degrees (Q₁₀ ≈ 2.5 to 3). There is frost and a white patch on the skin, and a cold nobody feels because the nerve is blocked. In a practitioner the pathway goes hard and quiet.

The glyphs are Boundary, setting the thermal condition, and Lock, a Counter form, occupying the limit so the defender cannot set it himself. The boundary condition is where the thermal gradient at the point of contact may sit: one end moves and Fourier's law does the rest. Vohrin (Caloria), one power at two levels, Titan and Wellspring, here at the Wellspring level: cold is accepted as a deficit and never emitted, and at contact its ordered lattice crystallizes the Essence pathway at the wound. Cinerion, the Ash Veil (Caloria): char and its residue feed further reaction, so the Shell harvests part of its own waste heat. Tenebra, the Hidden Shadow (Limina): return below the watcher's noise floor, covering the close to contact. They hand down failures: Vohrin's cold is broken by heat forced back in, Cinerion's fouling collapses recovery just when the most has been spent, and Tenebra's aspect dependence shows the approach from the unplanned angle.

In doctrine it is Debt, plainly: a stated pull against a stated balance per use, with Residue alongside through Cinerion. The Aether Shell runs hyper-dense muscular ignition from a blood-pact and uses frost for slowing at contact. Ardency Penetration, Dexterity Feint and Celerity serve the close, and Vitality Tolerance what the body survives of the ignition. On the Body Path Penetration, Celerity and Tolerance are open and Feint is capped at B, so the approach runs at B-Grade concealment while the strike is fully open.

The failure is Structural: the working succeeds and his fiber tears along the ignition path, compounding when chained. Clothing, a heat ward, saturated cold, fouling or no contact each deny it. Some 45 percent of the spend and all the heat taken leave the Shell as warmth, so the air round him warms and the room with it. Over-reliance on the blood-pact ignition can trigger an uncontrolled Prime Beast change with permanent loss of sapience.

The Frigorific college held cold a thing to emit and trained to cool at range. There is only heat to accept, every joule leaving through the adept's own Shell, so their coldest masters are found by the warm air round them.

**Cost.** A fifth of full reserve per strike, a clock anyone can count, and torn fiber along the ignition path.  
**Counter.** A Pyreveil or Exuroth ward forces heat back in faster than Vohrin can take it, the way flame breaks Vohrin's own Stillwater Rite; padded cloth, dry wool or an air gap denies the contact.

**What nobody knows.** Whether the freeze fails first at the joint or first at the Crystal. Its author has never let it run long enough on himself to find out, and nobody has caught him at it on someone who could tell him afterward.

### Counterplay

**Tell** · Upstream the ignition lit and a close under cover; at contact almost nothing; afterward warm air round him and a warmer room, careful breathing, frost on the quarry.

**Limits** · Bloodbind ignition first, because without the blood already burning there is no vector to compress and no strike to throw; reach of one arm, half a millimeter deep, two seconds of lock; nothing through clothing or without contact.

**Fails against** · A padded jack, dry wool or an air gap; a Pyreveil or Exuroth ward; a cold or warming closed room; a Limina void-momentum working declining the collapse; distance or flight; a broken wrist or broken attention; chained spending.

**Look up** · The Caloria and Limina favor lists; the Vohrin note on the [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Wild page]]; the Cinerion and Tenebra Failures; Pyreveil and Exuroth; the A-Grade Strike Force; the Glory Stage table; the Prime Beast entry.

### Origin

No derivation history is recorded for this technique beyond its author's profile as a hunter who developed the craft through "constant self-imposed trials of endurance, ambush and predatory pressure." No teacher or site is named for this strike. It is self-derived, and counterable by anyone who studies it.

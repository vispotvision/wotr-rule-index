# The Spirit Summoning Arts

> What common parlance calls "summoning" is not a single discipline. **It is a three-plane crossing.** On the Physical Plane, Aether is shaped into a vessel. On the Soul Plane, the summoner's Essence Core donates identity, pattern, and animating current. On the Plane of Fate, Attraction Force magnetizes the construct into recognized existence.
| **Primary Categories** | Vocatia (Attraction-Aligned) · Animatria (Spirit-Aligned) · Arts (Body-Aligned) |
|---|---|
| **Apex Integration** | Concordia (Spirit ↔ Attraction Apex) |
| **Supporting Disciplines** | Ritus (Ritual Invocation) · Glyphica (Parunic Authorization) · Mnemata (Memory-Echo Substrate) |

---

## What a Spirit Actually Is

Not an external entity plucked from elsewhere, but a **lawful extrusion of the summoner's own Soul Crystal**, given temporary autonomous existence through Parunic syntax and Wellspring-authorized process. The summoned form carries the emotional grammar of its creator's Essence Typology, resonates with their harmonized Wellsprings, and endures only as long as their Attraction Layer can sustain the recognition that holds it together.
> This makes summoning one of the most revealing arts in the Continuum. **A summoner's spirits are confessions.** They externalize what the soul contains, shaped by trauma, refined through Temperance, and bounded by the Coherence Ladder's honest measure of how much law a single body can project beyond itself.

## The Taxonomic Breakdown

### Vocatia · the Recognition Layer

Governs the highest and most dangerous form: invocation by recognition and correspondence. The summoner does not fabricate a spirit from personal Essence. They extend Attraction Force outward to contact an existing presence on the Plane of Fate — a Titan echo, an Archonic principle, a World Spirit fragment, a lesser entity, an ancestral resonance — and negotiate shared recognition.
> **Vocatia is not domination. It is correspondence.** The Plane of Fate's lawful currents treat any invocation addressed to an unwilling higher being as attempted **Usurpation**, regardless of how correct the surface syntax appears. Successful Vocatia is always an act of the Current of Recognition, never Refusal.
This is why Vocatia practitioners are called negotiators rather than conjurers. Their art depends on the depth and authenticity of their Attraction Layer's resonance with whatever they are calling. **A shallow pact yields a shallow echo. A genuine alignment yields something that can think, act, and persist with terrifying independence.**
**Temperance Gate** · Stage X (Realization) minimum for stable contact · Stage XII (Emanation) for sustained autonomous summons · Stage XIV (Zenith) for Archonic or Titanic correspondence.

### Animatria · the Identity Layer

Governs eidolons, echo-forms, and psionic constructs fabricated from the summoner's own Essence Core and given temporary independent operation. An Animatria construct is a small detached fragment of the Core shaped through Intent into a structured presence — closer to a deliberately shaped Essence Shard than a Domain. It maintains continuity through a tether back to its source, **which is why Animatria summoning fails completely inside Silence Realms.** Harmonic Silence severs the connection and the construct dissolves.
This is the most common form. It requires no external consent, no negotiation, and no Attraction Layer depth beyond what the summoner naturally possesses. Its limitation is that every eidolon is a piece of the summoner projected outward. A summoner harmonized with Luminalis and Benediction produces luminous guardian-forms. One harmonized with Tenebra and Mortalis produces shadow-constructs that dissolve hostile memory.
**Temperance Gate** · Stage VI (Glory) for basic eidolons · Stage VIII (Transcendence) for semi-autonomous forms · Stage X (Realization) for constructs capable of independent tactical action.
**Essence Cost** · 75,000 – 150,000 EU per eidolon depending on complexity, with a sustained cost of 10,000 – 25,000 EU per second. Multiple simultaneous constructs multiply strain on the Aether Shell and risk **Essence depletion cascade**.

### Arts · the Vessel Layer

Governs Spirit Forge techniques, Binding Arts, and Resonant Forms: the physical and material infrastructure of summoning. Where Vocatia provides recognition and Animatria provides identity, **Arts provides the body.**
A Spirit Forger does not merely project Essence into air. They build a vessel, often using material anchors — relics, glyph-inscribed substrates, prepared mineral bodies, alchemical constructs — that the Shell can bond to and the Core can animate. Slower, heavier, and more resource-intensive than Animatria projection, but also more durable, more physically capable, and **less dependent on the summoner's continuous concentration**.
**Binding Arts** overlay the forged vessel with Glyphica: authorization chains telling the Continuum what the construct may do, how long it may persist, and under what conditions it must dissolve. Without proper Binding, a construct operates on raw Animatria tether alone. With it, the construct gains Continuum-recognized boundaries, which both stabilizes it and protects the summoner from backlash if the form destabilizes.
**Temperance Gate** · Stage VII (Refraction) for material-anchored constructs · Stage IX (Invocation) for independent Spirit Forge bodies · Stage XI (Dissonance) for constructs bearing their own Wellspring harmonization.

### Concordia · the Apex Integration

A Concordia-level summon is not a projection. **It is a founding.** The summoner creates a temporary micro-Realm in which the summoned presence can exist as a semi-independent being with its own Essence Core analog, Aether Shell analog, and Attraction Layer analog, all held together by the summoner's Concordia field.
This is the art behind eidolons that can think, adapt, feel, and persist beyond their creator's immediate attention. It is also the most dangerous form, because **a Concordia-level construct that develops sufficient internal coherence may begin to resist dissolution.**
**Temperance Gate** · Stage XII (Emanation) minimum · Stage XIV (Zenith) for constructs with genuine internal autonomy.

## Summoning Tiers

| Tier | Categories | Temperance | Duration | Typical form |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **I · Ephemeral Constructs** | Animatria | Stage IV – VI | 1 – 10 seconds | Flickering Essence shapes, light-echoes, brief force projections |
| **II · Basic Summons** | Animatria + Arts | Stage VI – VIII | Seconds to minutes | Eidolons with basic behavioural patterns, companion constructs, scout-forms |
| **III · Greater Summons** | Vocatia + Animatria + Arts | Stage IX – XI | Minutes to hours | Semi-autonomous spirits with tactical intelligence, ancestral echoes, Wellspring-resonant constructs |
| **IV · Legion Summons** | Animatria + Concordia | Stage X – XII | Minutes to indefinite with Domain anchor | Coordinated hosts operating as a single tactical network |
| **V · World-Tethered** | Vocatia + Concordia + Mnemata | Stage XII – XIV | Indefinite | Persistent presences anchored to a location, bloodline, or Wellspring vein |

**Tier I** constructs do not think and do not persist. They are reflexive extrusions, **the summoning equivalent of throwing a punch**. Used for training, distraction, and emergency shielding.
**Tier II** carries the summoner's Typology visibly — an Ignis-typed summoner produces flame-wreathed forms; an Aqua-typed summoner produces fluid, reflective shapes — and follows simple behavioural directives. Material anchors (a glyph-marked stone, a prepared focus object, a drop of the summoner's blood on a sigil) extend duration and reduce continuous Shell strain. Summon Stability sits at E-Tier to D-Tier.
**Tier III** is the first tier where Vocatia becomes viable, and the difference is felt rather than measured. A Vocatia-invoked spirit carries an identity that did not originate entirely within the summoner. **It may act in ways the summoner did not anticipate. It may refuse orders that contradict the nature of the entity being channeled.** This is not malfunction. It is proof that the invocation contacted something real. Summon Stability C-Tier to B-Tier.
**Tier IV** fractures the summoner's Essence output across multiple simultaneous constructs and synchronizes them through a shared Concordia field. The summoner is not controlling each spirit individually; they are sustaining a harmonic network. At B-Tier a skilled practitioner sustains three to seven constructs. At A-Tier dozens become possible, though diminishing returns set in as each additional form degrades network coherence. **The Guild Accord classifies advanced Legion practitioners as strategic assets rather than individual combatants.**
> **Tier V · World-Tethered Summoning** does not create a construct the summoner sustains. **It creates a construct the world sustains.** Through Mnemata and Vocatia, the summoner anchors a presence to an existing node on the Plane of Fate: a Wellspring vein, a Domain, a bloodline's ancestral lattice, or a location with sufficient Continuum memory to act as host.
>
> The spirit persists because the Continuum recognizes it as belonging to the node. This is how ancestral guardian-spirits persist across generations, how shrine-bound entities outlive their creators, and how certain sacred sites maintain presences older than any living summoner.
>
> Its abuse — the tethering of unwilling spirits, or the parasitic anchoring of constructs to Wellspring veins — is prosecuted under **Statute XXVI, Mechanica Irreversibility.**

## Wellspring Resonance

Summoned spirits inherit the Wellspring alignment of their creator. A summoner harmonized with multiple Houses produces spirits of correspondingly layered complexity.
| House | Manifestation | Used for | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Coagulation** *Coagula, Fixatio, Coagulatio* | Anchored, durable, slow-to-dissolve presences | Defensive constructs, ward-guardians, long-duration sentinels | Spirits may resist dissolution, becoming permanence hazards |
| **Ascent** *Ascensio, Sublimatio, Judicium* | Elevated clarity and judgmental precision | Truthseeking constructs, consecrated guardians, verdict-witnesses | Constructs may develop conviction that outlasts their authorization |
| **Green Pulse** *Verdantia, Anima Spirare, Benediction* | Vital warmth, restorative capacity, empathic responsiveness | Healer-companions, chorus-constructs, recovery-field generators | Over-empathic resonance may cause the construct to absorb surrounding grief or trauma |
| **The Veil** *Somnalis, Oneirion, Eidolyn, Anamnesis* | Dreamlike, memory-threaded, perceptually liminal | Scout-constructs, ancestral echo-forms, memory-archive vessels | Constructs may blur the boundary between recalled and fabricated memory |
| **The Black Gate** *Mortalis, Tenebra, Oblivara, Nihiloth* | Shadow-forms, silence-constructs, dissolution agents | Assassination support, curse-severance, merciful release | Constructs may erode the summoner's own memory, identity, or emotional continuity |
| **The Forge** *Exuroth, Pyreveil, Manganthra, Catharsis* | Incandescent, volatile, catalytically intense | Siege-breaking, corruption-purging, trial-by-fire constructs | Constructs may exceed their Boundary glyphs and ignite uncontrolled Essence burn |
| **The Hollow** *Lethegraal, Tarturon, Eclipseron, Vantabriel* | Absence-constructs, void-forms, negation agents. Deeply rare. | Anti-summoning fields, Mechanica containment, Wellspring wound stabilization | Constructs may hollow the summoner's Essence Core through prolonged contact |

## Key Figures

**Karo Venrik** · A prodigious Spirit Summoner whose Animatria suite — Cognition, Convergence, Reflex, Requiem, Continuum — represents the most refined psionic-summoning architecture in the current era. His constructs operate through **Nexus networks**: shared perception fields in which multiple eidolons act as extensions of a single Animatria consciousness. His alignment with Wellsprings of memory and vital resonance produces spirits that function as living archives, carrying ancestral knowledge in their Essence structure. His Legion Summons are not brute-force multiplication but precision instruments, each eidolon tuned to a specific perceptual or tactical function within the network.
**Ashura Yukari** · A Third Epoch theorist who integrated summoning within the earliest Wellspring alignment models. His contribution was recognizing that summoned spirits inherit their creator's Essence Typology **not as aesthetic decoration but as functional law**. A spirit built from Ignis Essence burns with conviction because conviction *is what Ignis Essence is*. His work established the principle that summoning is self-revelation, transforming the art from military utility into a diagnostic discipline used by Soul Crystal examiners and Temperance evaluators across the Concord.
**Mnirah Valein** · A philosopher-summoner of the Age of Deliberation who codified the [**Twelve Stages of Spirit Infusion**](https://app.notion.com/p/3be58200eb22812aab29e5590a909077), a progressive curriculum mapping summoning capability to the Coherence Ladder. Her system formalized the Temperance Gates now standard across the Accord. Her work also established the legal framework distinguishing lawful summoning — Wellspring-authorized, Glyphica-bounded, Attraction-anchored — from Mechanica, a distinction that would later underpin Statute XXVI.
**The Anemoir Cult** · A clandestine order practicing World-Tethered summoning across generational timescales. The Anemoir preserve ancestral spirits by anchoring them to Wellspring veins and bloodline lattices, maintaining presences that span centuries. Their methods sit at the intersection of Vocatia, Mnemata, and Concordia, and their most senior practitioners are among the few living souls capable of **sustaining a summoned presence beyond their own lifespan**. The Concord tolerates them because their preservation work stabilizes Wellspring memory in regions where natural degradation would otherwise erase historical echo. Several provinces owe the continuity of their ancestral rites to Anemoir intervention.

## Historical Importance

The art traces to the Third Epoch, when ancestral reverence practices first formalized into structured discipline following the Great Spirit War. Early summoning was **Ritus-dominant**: ceremonial, communal, and slow. Practitioners gathered in prepared circles, chanted authorization chains, and coaxed ancestral echoes into temporary presence through shared emotional focus and Wellspring-resonant offering.
The transition from ritual to combat art occurred during the **Era of Grace**, when summoners discovered that Animatria constructs could be projected in real time without full ceremonial infrastructure. This required higher individual Temperance — the Crystal had to internalize what ritual circles previously externalized — but freed the art from its dependence on prepared space. Summoners became field-capable, serving as cultural historians, military tacticians, and spiritual mediators, conjuring spirits not merely as weapons but as **living repositories of forgotten epochs**, vessels carrying memory the Continuum itself had begun to let fade.
By the **Voyager Era**, Spirit Summoning has become both rare and sacred. The proliferation of Mechanica degraded public trust in all soul-externalization arts, and the Concord responded with increasingly strict regulation. Summoning without proper authorization now constitutes a metaphysical crime in several regions. Unauthorized World-Tethered summoning is prosecuted as **Wellspring parasitism**. All practicing summoners must demonstrate minimum Temperance Gates, carry Guild-certified glyph chains, and submit to periodic Soul Crystal examination.
> The irony is not lost on scholars: **the art that most honestly reveals the summoner's soul is now the art most heavily surveilled.**

# The Infinite Summoning Arts

> **Infinite Summoning is not a tier. It is the abandonment of the register.**
> Every other summoner works from a roster: forms sealed, named, and callable again. An Infinite practitioner keeps no roster. Each call is drafted fresh against the shape of the moment, which is why the Accord cannot audit the art and why no two accounts of the same practitioner's kit have ever matched.

---

## What the Word Actually Names

The name misleads almost everyone who hears it, including several people who have died to it. It does not mean unlimited output. Essence remains finite, the Aether Shell still strains, and an Infinite practitioner exhausts on the same curve as anyone else at their Coherence.
What is infinite is the **catalogue**. The Accord's register lists what has been sealed and reported. It has been out of date for four hundred years, everyone in the Commission knows it, and Infinite Summoning is the art built on top of that gap. The practitioner does not select from a list. They draft to the problem.
> **The trade is repetition.** A sealed compact gets cheaper each time it answers, because recognition accumulates. A drafted call never does. The Infinite practitioner pays first-call price forever, and nothing that answers them is guaranteed to be the same thing twice.
**Temperance Gate** · Stage XI (Dissonance) for unsealed drafting under battlefield conditions · Stage XIII for sustained networks · The art is not taught below Stage XI and the Accord's position is that it should not be taught at all.

## The Two Standing Operations

### Essence Draft

The practitioner fragments Core output into threads, each thread anchoring one call. Where an ordinary summoner spends a whole allocation on one form, the drafter divides and holds. Every thread is a separate load and every thread is felt separately.
**Cost** · Each open thread is a permanent tax on the practitioner's own body for as long as it stays open. Drafters at network scale do not sleep normally, cannot digest properly during a campaign, and are reliably the thinnest people in a war camp.
**Limit** · Threads cannot be pooled back into one heavy call. Divided Essence stays divided until the thread closes.
**Counter** · Threads are readable. Anyone with comparable Perception can count them, and a drafter's exact commitment is visible to the enemy before the first construct lands.

### The Aether Feed

Continuous supply to sustain and modify a call after it has manifested. The Feed is what allows a construct to change mid-engagement instead of being dismissed and re-drafted.
**Cost** · Feeding is louder than drafting. A practitioner running an open Feed is trivially locatable by anyone who has learned the signature.
**Limit** · A Feed modifies. It cannot repair a construct whose Binding has already failed.
**Counter** · Cut the practitioner's line of attention and the Feed lapses. Everything on it degrades to whatever the Binding alone will hold.

## The Advanced Arts

### Fate-Chain Summoning

**Effect** · A sequence drafted as one authorization, in which each form manifests only as the previous dissipates or completes its assigned role. The opponent fights a series, never a group, and the series does not stop when the current form dies. Killing a chain link accelerates the chain.
**Cost** · The chain is authorized whole and cannot be revised once opened. The practitioner is committed to every link, including the ones drafted for a battle that has stopped resembling the one they planned for.
**Limit** · One chain at a time. A second chain drafted over a running one collapses both.
**Counter** · Do not kill the link. A chain held in stasis, restrained rather than destroyed, stalls at that link and the remaining authorization sits unspent and paid for.

### Aetheric Constellations

**Effect** · Multiple drafted forms bound into a network in which each amplifies the others, so that the host's capability rises faster than its numbers. Soldiers who have fought one describe the sensation of the field getting harder, not larger.
**Cost** · The practitioner holds the network's coherence personally and loses granular control of individual forms while doing it. They are conducting, and cannot also play.
**Limit** · Amplification runs on adjacency. Forms separated past the network's reach fall out of it and revert to baseline.
**Counter** · Break the geometry. Splitting the constellation is more effective than killing any part of it, and considerably cheaper.

### Recursive Drafting

**Effect** · A drafted form authorized to draft in turn, producing further entities without further attention from the practitioner. The family classifies this as the point at which the summoner stops being the only author on the field.
**Cost** · Every generation is drawn from the practitioner regardless of who authorized it. A recursion left running has emptied practitioners who believed they had stopped participating.
**Limit** · Authorization chains lose a link each generation. Third-generation forms carry almost no Binding and behave accordingly.
**Counter** · An authorization chain is only as long as its first link. Break the originating form and everything downstream of it ends mid-motion.

### Summon Collapse

**Effect** · Multiple sustained forms dissolved at once and their remaining allocation released as a single output at a chosen point. The standard answer to a problem the practitioner cannot solve incrementally.
**Cost** · Everything spent on those forms is spent. A practitioner who collapses their field and does not resolve the engagement is now an unarmed man in the middle of it.
**Limit** · Only forms currently on the practitioner's own Feed can be collapsed. Sealed compacts, borrowed forms, and anything World-Tethered are outside it.
**Counter** · Distance. The release is enormous and local, and a practitioner who has just used it cannot follow up.

### Plane Tethers

**Effect** · A durable connection maintained between the Plane of Fate and a fixed point on the Physical, through which forms persist without continuous drafting. The nearest thing the art has to infrastructure.
**Cost** · Paid once at anchoring, in something the practitioner does not get back. The Mahuo elder structure declines to specify what in writing and the Anemoir refuse to discuss it at all.
**Limit** · The tether is fixed. It cannot be moved, and a campaign that shifts away from it has simply lost it.
**Counter** · A tether cannot be defeated, only starved. Take and hold the anchoring ground.

### Imprint Summoning

**Effect** · Manifestation of a replica drawn from an existing pattern held on the Plane of Fate rather than drafted fresh. Cheaper and faster than authoring, and the replica arrives already knowing how to be what it is.
**Cost** · An imprint carries whatever the original carried, including its injuries, its convictions, and its grudges.
**Limit** · The replica cannot exceed the pattern. It is a copy of a thing at a moment, and it does not improve.
**Counter** · Anyone who has fought the original has already fought this. Imprints are the most reliably countered forms in the art.

### Spirit Singularity

**Effect** · Multiple presences compressed into a single core entity carrying the aggregate. What comes out is one thing, and it is worse than the sum was.
**Cost** · Compression is not reversible. The constituent presences do not survive it as themselves, which is why lawful practice restricts the technique to drafted forms and why unlawful practice does not.
**Limit** · A singularity holds one shape. It cannot be redirected, subdivided, or asked to do a second job.
**Counter** · Everything went into one body. Kill the body.

### Axis Binding

**Effect** · A form's traits tied directly to one of the practitioner's Spirit Axes, so that the construct shifts in real time as the practitioner's own state does. The most responsive technique in the art and the least deniable.
**Cost** · The binding runs both directions. Damage to the form arrives in the practitioner as the corresponding change in themselves, and it does not fade when the form does.
**Limit** · One Axis, one form. A second binding on the same Axis destabilizes both.
**Counter** · The form is a live readout of the practitioner's condition. Anyone who can read it knows exactly how the summoner is doing, which is information most practitioners would pay a great deal to withhold.

### Echo Manifestation

**Effect** · Remnants of dissolved forms gathered and folded into new ones, so that a practitioner deep into an engagement is drafting from richer material than they began with. Long fights favour the drafter who survives them.
**Cost** · Remnants carry the manner of their dissolution. A practitioner who has lost forms badly is building from that.
**Limit** · Echoes decay. Anything not gathered within the engagement is gone.
**Counter** · Deny the remnants. Forms destroyed by dissolution-aligned working leave nothing to gather.

### Dimensional Resonance

**Effect** · A form held across more than one plane simultaneously, so that defences seated on one plane do not fully engage it. The technique that makes the art strategically intolerable to the Accord.
**Cost** · The practitioner is also held across, and does not come all the way back. Documented resonance users lose time, lose sequence, and eventually lose the ability to say with confidence which plane a given memory happened on.
**Limit** · The form cannot exert full effect on any plane it is spread across. It is everywhere at a fraction.
**Counter** · It is a fraction. Weight of ordinary force beats it, and always has.

### Omnipresent Form

**Effect** · A single presence tied to many locations at once, so that a practitioner's guard covers ground no host could physically hold. Attested at the top of the Twelve Stages and in very few other places.
**Cost** · Attention divides with the presence. A practitioner running an omnipresent form is not fully present anywhere, including where their own body is standing.
**Limit** · It observes and it obstructs. It does not concentrate, and it has never been made to.
**Counter** · Ignore it. An omnipresent form cannot mass, and a committed strike at one point passes through what is there.

## What Nobody Knows

> Nobody can explain why a drafted call answers at all. Sealed compacts have a naming rite and a recorded moment of agreement behind them. A draft has neither, and it still answers, faster and more completely than the theory permits. The Accord enforces the correspondence model because it is the one that justifies the Accord. The three competing models are taught quietly and none of them predict a first-time draft correctly.

## Practitioners

**Cozbi Mahuo** · The reason the art is regulated. Legion and Infinite practice combined, on a foundation of harvested soul-fragment rather than lawful draft. The Zettari-Mahuo Accord condemns his methods without qualification and obliges the Mahuo to surrender rather than shelter anyone found replicating them.
**Kwon Mu-jin** · Works the opposite way, and it is the more instructive case. His branch lost the ability to host, so he built a method entirely out of drafting and authorization, and his roster is standardized and re-summonable precisely because he could not afford to be improvisational. He is the drafter who chose a register. See *The Open Crucible*.
**Karo Venrik** · Nexus-networked Animatria at constellation scale, each form tuned to a perceptual function rather than a combat one. The cleanest lawful demonstration of Aetheric Constellations on record.
**The Anemoir Cult** · Plane Tethers across generational timescale. The only body that has answered the question of what anchoring costs, and they answered it by declining to say.

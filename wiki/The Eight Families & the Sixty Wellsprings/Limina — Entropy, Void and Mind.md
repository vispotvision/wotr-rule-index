# Limina — Entropy, Void and Mind

*Abstract and entropic · entropy, void, and mind*
> The largest Family and the only one whose truest signature is **what fails to be there.** Limina is not the Family of darkness. It is the Family of systems described by their absences, their relaxation times, and their information content, which is a harder and less romantic thing than its practitioners generally admit.
**Stat Affinity** · *Gnosis (Warding, Retention, Cognition, Memorium, Diagnosis) · Resilience (Coherence, Severance, Nullity, Persistence, Continuity) · Harmonics (Recovery, Fidelity, Suppression, Stability, Memory) · Dominion (Reach, Sovereignty, Sense, Stability)*

---

### Letheveil · *The Waters of Forgetting*

**Analogue** · Lossy compression. Shannon entropy and the identification of low-information content.
**Mechanism** · Compression works because most of a signal is redundant, and the art is entirely in knowing which components carry information and which merely occupy bandwidth. Letheveil performs that analysis on memory. It does not erase indiscriminately; it identifies what is load-bearing and discards the rest, and the practitioner's certainty about which is which is the whole of the risk.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Gnosis Warding, and **tests** Resilience Coherence rather than simply raising it. The practitioner learns which memories are load-bearing and which are chains.
**Failure** · Generation loss. Each pass discards a little more, and a memory compressed repeatedly degrades toward an artefact the practitioner still experiences as a memory.

### Lethegraal · *The Cup of Forgetting*

**Analogue** · Relaxation time. The characteristic constant with which a perturbed system returns to equilibrium.
**Mechanism** · Every disturbed system settles, and the only question is how long. Lethegraal shortens the time constant. Nothing is removed and nothing is chosen; the same trauma metabolises down to neutral architecture on a schedule the practitioner is not otherwise able to influence, which is why the Wellspring is prescribed and Letheveil is merely permitted.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Tempering Processing, Harmonics Recovery. Trauma metabolises faster. The Crystal digests old pain into neutral architecture.
**Failure** · Under-damping. Shorten the constant too far and the system overshoots equilibrium on every cycle, and the practitioner oscillates rather than settling.

### Hypnather · *The Sleep Crown*

**Analogue** · Structural relaxation. Physical ageing of a glass toward its equilibrium configuration.
**Mechanism** · A glass is a liquid that stopped moving before it finished arranging itself, and left undisturbed it continues to relax toward the ordered state it never reached, slowly and only while quiescent. Hypnather grants the Crystal that behaviour. The reorganisation happens during genuine rest and cannot be forced, which is why Hypnather practitioners sleep like the dead and wake structurally different.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Regeneration on sleep cycles, Tempering Latency, Gnosis Retention. Recovery during rest becomes dramatically more efficient.
**Failure** · Interrupted relaxation. Disturb the process partway and the configuration locks where it stood, worse ordered than before it began.

### Mirithane · *The Wellspring of Reflection*

**Analogue** · Specular reflection. The law of reflection; the phase-conjugate mirror.
**Mechanism** · A true mirror adds nothing. Angle in equals angle out, and fidelity is defined entirely by the absence of introduced distortion. A phase-conjugate mirror goes further and returns a wavefront that undoes its own aberration on the way back. Mirithane makes the Shell such a surface, so what leaves the practitioner is what was in them, with no flattering correction applied in transit.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Harmonics Fidelity, Tempering Coherence. Output becomes a more honest reflection of internal state.
> **Failure** · The mirror does not discriminate. A practitioner in a state they would rather not broadcast broadcasts it perfectly.

### Oneirion · *The Wellspring of Dream*

**Analogue** · Configuration space. A system's state as a point in an abstract high-dimensional manifold.
**Mechanism** · A mechanical system's every possible arrangement is a coordinate in a space that has no physical location, and the system's evolution is a trajectory through it. Oneirion lets a working act on that manifold rather than on the room. This is the mechanical reason Oneirion techniques reach targets no line of sight connects, and the reason they are so difficult to describe afterward.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Gnosis Cognition, Dominion Reach, Ardency Spectral. Workings operate through conceptual space, not only physical space.
**Failure** · Projection error. The trajectory was correct in the manifold and lands nowhere in particular when it is mapped back down into a room.

### Eidolyn · *The Shaping Mind*

**Analogue** · Templated nucleation. Ordered deposition onto an imposed pattern.
**Mechanism** · A supersaturated medium will not organise itself, but given a template it deposits along the template's geometry with high fidelity and considerable strength. Eidolyn makes intent the template. The construct's solidity is real material behaviour rather than illusion, and its failure modes are the failure modes of a poorly designed casting.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Ardency Density in summoned constructs, Ardency Inscription, Gnosis Memorium. Mental constructs gain material solidity.
**Failure** · Template defect. Any flaw in the intent is faithfully reproduced at scale, and the practitioner is rarely able to see the flaw until it is standing in front of them.

### Anamnesis · *The Wellspring of Remembrance*

**Analogue** · Remanent magnetisation and thermoluminescent dosimetry.
**Mechanism** · Basalt records the direction of the field it cooled in and holds that record for geological time. Fired ceramic accumulates trapped electrons in proportion to the radiation dose it has absorbed since its last firing, and releases them as light when heated. Objects genuinely store their history in physically readable form. **Anamnesis is the instrument, not the miracle.**
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Gnosis Retention, Gnosis Diagnosis in reading past trauma, Resilience Coherence. The practitioner reads history through contact with objects, places, and beings.
**Failure** · Thermal resetting. Reading is not always passive, and a careless practitioner erases the record in the act of taking it.

### Dissolution · *The Unbinding Stream*

**Analogue** · Solvation. Dissolution driven by the entropy of mixing.
**Mechanism** · A solid dissolves not because the solvent attacks it but because the mixed state has higher entropy than the separated one, and the process therefore runs on its own once initiated. Dissolution gives the Crystal that thermodynamic preference toward the unbound state, which makes shedding what no longer serves nearly effortless and makes retaining anything a matter of active work.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Resilience Severance, Tempering Processing. The character dissolves what no longer serves the Crystal's architecture.
> **Failure** · Indiscriminate solvency. Entropy does not consult the practitioner about which structures were load-bearing.

### Tenebra · *The Hidden Shadow*

**Analogue** · Signal-to-noise reduction. Absorptive coating and faceted return geometry.
**Mechanism** · Something is undetectable when its return falls below the noise floor of whatever is looking, which is achieved by absorbing incident energy and by shaping surfaces so the little that reflects goes anywhere except back at the observer. Tenebra applies both to the Shell. Its practitioners are not invisible. They are quieter than the background, which is better.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Dexterity Silence, Harmonics Suppression, Dominion Sense. The practitioner becomes difficult to detect, read, or track by conventional or Aetheric means.
**Failure** · Aspect dependence. Faceting works against expected angles, and a Tenebra practitioner observed from the one direction they did not plan for returns a very bright signal indeed.

### Oblivara · *The Dark Mirror*

**Analogue** · Error-correcting redundancy. Retroreflection along the incident vector.
**Mechanism** · Information survives a noisy channel when it is encoded with enough redundancy that corrupted symbols can be reconstructed from the surviving ones, and a corner reflector returns incident energy precisely along the path it arrived on. Oblivara does both: the practitioner's identity is redundantly encoded against erasure, and attempts at perceptual manipulation return to their source.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Resilience Nullity, Gnosis Warding. Resistance to conceptual erasure and perceptual manipulation.
**Failure** · Code exhaustion. Redundancy has a correction limit, and past it the reconstruction is confident, complete, and wrong.

### Nyxial · *The Nocturne Flame*

**Analogue** · Thermal inertia under negative feedback. A regulated system holding setpoint against ambient.
**Mechanism** · A body with high heat capacity governed by a feedback loop holds its temperature regardless of what the room does, and the larger the capacity the smaller the excursion. Nyxial gives the Crystal that regulation. The practitioner's baseline does not rise in favourable conditions and does not fall in hostile ones, which is a disadvantage exactly as often as it is a defence.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Harmonics Stability, Dominion Stability, Vitality Endurance. Baseline output does not fluctuate with environmental pressure.
**Failure** · Integral windup. A regulator held against a load it cannot overcome accumulates correction, and when the load lifts the correction discharges all at once.

### Nihiloth · *The Hollow Law*

**Analogue** · Destructive interference and the null field. A region genuinely empty of energy.
**Mechanism** · Two waves in antiphase do not conceal one another. They sum to zero, and the region between them carries no energy at all. Nihiloth builds Domains on that principle, which is why a Nihiloth Domain does not impose additional law. It cancels the law already present, and what remains inside is not a rule but the absence of one.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Dominion Sovereignty, Resilience Persistence, Gnosis Cognition. The practitioner imposes law through negation. Their Domain does not add rules. It removes them.
> **Failure** · The cancellation is symmetric. Everything the practitioner brought inside is subject to the same null, including the parts of themselves they were relying on.

### Vantabriel · *The Wellspring of Night*

**Analogue** · Near-total absorption and hysteresis. Retained state dependent on history.
**Mechanism** · An ultra-absorptive surface returns almost nothing and converts what it receives into internal heat, and a hysteretic material's present state depends not on current conditions but on the path it took to arrive. Vantabriel combines them: what strikes the practitioner is kept rather than reflected, and the Crystal's condition is a function of everything it has ever absorbed. **Endurance here is carried loss, not replaced loss.**
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Resilience Continuity, Harmonics Memory, Tempering Longevity. The character endures by carrying the memory of what was lost rather than replacing it.
**Failure** · Thermal saturation. Absorption without emission has a limit, and past it the retained load has nowhere to go but into the structure holding it.

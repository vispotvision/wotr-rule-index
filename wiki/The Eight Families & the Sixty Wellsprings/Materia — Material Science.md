# Materia — Material Science

*Material science · matter and substance*
**Stat Affinity** · *Vitality (Fortitude, Ossification, Gravitas, Regeneration, Absorption) · Resilience (Integrity, Anchoring, Hardening, Persistence, Fortification) · Dominion (Density, Anchoring) · Tempering (Coherence, Compression)*

---

### Materia Primordia · *The First Substance*

**Analogue** · Allotropy. One element, several lattices, radically different materials.
**Mechanism** · Graphite and diamond are the same element. The difference is entirely in how the atoms are arranged, and it is the difference between a lubricant and the hardest natural substance known. Materia Primordia grants the Crystal access to its own polymorphism: the same Essence, re-latticed, expressing properties that appear to belong to different substances entirely.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Ossification, Dominion Density. The body and the Domain gain mineral-weight authority.
**Failure** · Phase locking. Under sufficient pressure the lattice commits to one allotrope permanently, and the practitioner keeps whichever form they were wearing when the pressure came.

### Monolithion · *The Enduring Monument*

**Analogue** · Grain growth and creep resistance. Single-crystal structure at geological timescale.
**Mechanism** · Polycrystalline metal fails at its grain boundaries under sustained high-temperature load, which is why turbine blades are grown as single crystals with no boundaries to fail along. Monolithion drives the Crystal toward that condition: fewer internal interfaces, enormous resistance to slow deformation, and a presence that behaves less like a person occupying a room than like a feature of the terrain.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Gravitas, Resilience Persistence, Dominion Anchoring. Presence becomes geological.
> **Failure** · Brittle at scale. A structure with no grain boundaries has nothing to arrest a crack, and when Monolithion fails it fails across its entire section at once.

### Chthonica · *The Root Deep*

**Analogue** · Static friction and molecular adhesion. Interlocking asperity; setal van der Waals contact.
**Mechanism** · Grip is not weight. A gecko holds to glass through billions of hair-scale contacts each contributing an intermolecular attraction too small to measure alone. Chthonica multiplies the Shell's contact area with whatever surface bears it, converting proximity into anchorage, which is why its practitioners root to sheer stone and moving decks with equal indifference.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Absorption, Dexterity Equilibrium, Dominion Anchoring. The practitioner roots to whatever surface they stand on.
**Failure** · Contamination. The mechanism depends on clean contact, and a fouled interface releases without warning and without a partial stage.

### Basilithe · *The Stone Will*

**Analogue** · Natural frequency and stiffness-to-mass ratio. Quartz oscillator stability.
**Mechanism** · A body's resonant frequency is fixed by its stiffness and its mass, and a high-stiffness low-loss structure holds that frequency against forcing so reliably that civilisations set their clocks by cut quartz. Basilithe raises the Crystal's stiffness until its resonance simply will not be driven off, which is a defence against every technique that works by imposing an external rhythm.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Resilience Hardening, Harmonics Stability. The soul's frequency locks and refuses retuning.
**Failure** · Resonant catastrophe. A frequency that cannot be shifted also cannot be detuned away from a forcing that happens to match it.

### Tarturon · *The Fire of Reckoning*

**Analogue** · Yield criterion and post-yield response. A reaction carrying its own oxidiser.
**Mechanism** · Below yield a material returns to shape and the load leaves no record. Above it, behaviour changes kind, and the response scales with the load applied rather than with anything the material chose. Tarturon couples output to moral weight through exactly that curve, and like thermite it supplies its own oxidiser, which is the mechanical reason the reaction cannot be smothered once initiated.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Ardency Flux under guilt-amplification, Tempering Overflow, Resilience Fortification. Output spikes under moral weight.
> **Failure** · Runaway. The load that drives the reaction is generated by the reaction, and nothing in the mechanism provides a stopping condition.

### Coagula · *The Unifying Pulse*

**Analogue** · Sintering. Densification by atomic diffusion below the melting point.
**Mechanism** · Press powder together and hold it hot, though never hot enough to melt, and the particles fuse across their contact points by diffusion alone until the porosity closes and the mass behaves as one body. Coagula runs the Soul Plane's version: a Crystal that has been fragmented gathers itself without ever passing through a liquid state, which is to say without losing the shape it had while broken.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Resilience Integrity, Tempering Coherence. The Crystal becomes harder to split.
**Failure** · Residual porosity. Sintering rarely reaches full density, and the voids that remain are the exact locations at which the next fracture will initiate.

### Fixatio · *The Binding Flame*

**Analogue** · Covalent cross-linking. Thermoset cure.
**Mechanism** · A thermoplastic can be melted and reformed indefinitely. A thermoset, once cured, has formed covalent bridges across its whole network and will char before it flows. Fixatio performs the cure. What it holds is not held by force but by the fact that the alternative configuration no longer exists as a reachable state.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Fortitude, Resilience Anchoring. What is held by Fixatio stays held.
**Failure** · Irreversibility. The cure is not undone by wanting it undone, and a Fixatio binding placed in error is a permanent feature of whatever it was placed on.

### Coagulatio · *The Returning Stone*

**Analogue** · Crystallisation from supersaturated solution. Nucleation and ordered growth.
**Mechanism** · A supersaturated solution holds more solute than equilibrium permits and will surrender it the instant a nucleation site appears, depositing ordered solid out of apparent nothing. Coagulatio is the material twin of Coagula: where that Wellspring gathers a soul, this one gathers substrate, pulling dispersed Essence out of suspension into a denser and far less wasteful phase.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Ardency Compression, Tempering Compression. Essence becomes denser and harder to waste.
**Failure** · Uncontrolled nucleation. Too many sites at once produces a fine disordered mass rather than a sound crystal, and the practitioner ends with density they cannot use.
> **The Crystalia note.** This is the same law that governs a Threshold. A melt cools into order only around seeds that survived the heat, and what Coagulatio describes in substrate is what Traits do in a Soul Crystal.
>
> *Whether a Coagulatio practitioner can work the law on their own seat is the oldest unsettled question in the trade. The Wellspring does not say no.*

### Terranova · *The Living Earth*

**Analogue** · Biomineralisation and self-healing composite. Nacre deposition; bacterial concrete.
**Mechanism** · Some structures do not get repaired. They grow their repair in place, laying down mineral along the damage front by the same process that built the original. Terranova gives the Crystal that behaviour, which is why its practitioners heal by extension rather than by closure and why their scars are new tissue rather than sealed old tissue.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Regeneration, Harmonics Confluence. The body heals by growing rather than repairing.
**Failure** · Overgrowth. Deposition does not know when to stop, and the repaired region continues to accrete past the original geometry.

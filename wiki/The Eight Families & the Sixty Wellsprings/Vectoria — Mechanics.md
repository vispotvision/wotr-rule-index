# Vectoria — Mechanics

*Mechanics · force, motion, gravity*
> Breaking, holding, and rising are the same law observed from three positions. Vectoria is the Family that treats a soul as a **loaded structure** and asks the only question a structural engineer ever asks, which is where the load goes and what happens at the place it concentrates.
**Stat Affinity** · *Tempering (Acceleration, Clarity, Coherence) · Resilience (Scarring, Insulation, Nullity, Integrity) · Vitality (Endurance, Threshold, Nullity) · Dominion (Stability)*

---

### Fractura · *The Breaking Point*

**Analogue** · Linear elastic fracture mechanics. The Griffith criterion and the stress intensity factor.
**Mechanism** · Nothing fails at its theoretical strength. Everything fails at a pre-existing flaw, because stress concentrates at a crack tip in proportion to the square root of the crack's length, and once that intensity exceeds the material's toughness the crack runs at a fraction of the speed of sound and does not stop. Fractura gives the practitioner sight of those flaws and the precision to load exactly one of them.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Gnosis Analysis in fault-reading, Ardency Penetration, Resilience Scarring. The practitioner sees where things break and learns to break them precisely.
> **Failure** · Self-inspection. The Wellspring does not exempt its bearer, and a Fractura practitioner under strain reads their own flaw population with perfect clarity at the worst possible moment.

### Petralon · *The Foundation Stone*

**Analogue** · Compressive load path and creep. Stone in compression; the arch.
**Mechanism** · Masonry is enormously strong in compression and nearly worthless in tension, which is why the arch exists: it converts a span into a queue of compressive members. Petralon reorganises the Crystal along the same principle, routing every incoming load into compression and accepting slow creep deformation as the price of never developing a tensile crack.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Endurance, Dominion Stability. The character becomes the ground beneath the ground.
**Failure** · Tensile exposure. Load the structure in the one direction it does not answer and it does not deform gracefully. It parts.

### Ascensio · *The Ladder of Ascent*

**Analogue** · Mechanical advantage. Path-independence of gravitational potential energy.
**Mechanism** · Lifting a mass to a height costs the same total work by any route, but the inclined plane trades distance for peak force and so the same climb can be made without ever exceeding what the structure can bear. Ascensio applies that to Threshold transitions, spreading the required change across a longer path so the Crystal never sees the peak stress that a direct ascent imposes.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Tempering Acceleration, Harmonics Attunement. Stage transitions become smoother and less violent.
**Failure** · The work is not reduced, only redistributed. Practitioners mistake the absence of crisis for the absence of cost and arrive at the next Stage owing the difference.

### Sublimatio · *The Ascending Breath*

**Analogue** · Sublimation. Solid to vapour with no liquid phase, below the triple point.
**Mechanism** · At sufficiently low pressure a solid does not melt. It leaves directly, and whatever cannot make that transition stays behind as residue. Sublimatio holds the Shell below its triple point so Essence skips the intermediate state entirely: no pooling, no partial expression, no waste at the phase boundary where most practitioners lose their margin.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Ardency Compression, Tempering Clarity. Output becomes cleaner and sharper, with less waste per expression.
**Failure** · Residue accumulation. Everything that could not sublime is still there, and it is the fraction of the practitioner least willing to move.

### Sublimare · *The Rising Breath*

**Analogue** · Fractional distillation. Separation by differential volatility.
**Mechanism** · A mixture heated in a column separates because its components have different vapour pressures, and the fraction that rises is not the same substance that entered. Where Sublimatio skips a phase, Sublimare separates a mixture. The Crystal is run as a column: impurity is driven off as the lighter fraction and what descends is the practitioner refined toward a single component.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Tempering Clarity, Resilience Insulation. The Crystal purifies itself, burning off impurities as silver vapour.
**Failure** · Azeotrope. Some mixtures cannot be separated by distillation at all, and the practitioner runs the column indefinitely against a contaminant that boils at exactly their own temperature.

### Abython · *The Abyssal Pulse*

**Analogue** · Pressure equalisation. Neutral buoyancy and the vanishing of net stress.
**Mechanism** · A vessel at depth does not survive by resisting the water. It survives by matching it, because net stress is a difference and a difference of zero loads nothing. Abython trains the Shell to equalise rather than oppose, which is why its practitioners work in Aether-absent environments that suffocate everyone else: they are not holding pressure in, they have stopped maintaining a differential at all.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Resilience Nullity, Harmonics Voidance, Vitality Nullity. The character operates where other practitioners suffocate.
**Failure** · Rapid ascent. Return to ordinary Aetheric density too fast and the equalised interior comes out of solution, which is as unpleasant metaphysically as it is in a diver.

### Absolution · *The Cleansing Current*

**Analogue** · Stress-relief annealing. Removal of residual stress without change of form.
**Mechanism** · A worked component carries locked-in stresses from its own manufacture, invisible, dimensionally irrelevant, and entirely capable of causing failure under a load the part should have carried. Heat treatment relaxes them while leaving the shape untouched. Absolution does exactly this to old fractures: the history stays, the geometry stays, and the stored energy that made the history dangerous is released.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Tempering Clarity, Harmonics Fidelity, Resilience Insulation. Old fractures lose their toxicity.
**Failure** · Over-relaxation. Take too much out and the structure loses the compressive prestress it had been quietly relying on to stay closed.

### Penance · *The Atoning Path*

**Analogue** · Work hardening. Dislocation multiplication under plastic strain.
**Mechanism** · Bend a metal past yield and it becomes harder to bend again, because plastic deformation multiplies dislocations until they obstruct one another. The material is strongest exactly where it was most abused. Penance imposes that constitutive behaviour on the Crystal, so deliberate strain accumulates strength, and the strength is honest because it is located precisely at the sites of prior failure.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Threshold, Tempering Coherence, Resilience Integrity. The Crystal aligns more honestly with lived behaviour.
**Failure** · Strain embrittlement. Past a certain dislocation density the material has no ductility left, and the next load does not deform it. It ends it.

# The Abyssal Depths — Prometheus, Zar'thuun, Vohrin

> Fire, void and frost. The order in which the three operate is fixed and is not a hierarchy. **Prometheus burns what is false. Zar'thuun unmakes what is excess. Vohrin stills what is left so it can be built upon.** The Abyssal Depths are not the enemy quarter of the pantheon. They are the back of the house.

---

## PROMETHEUS — The Flame of Theros

> *"All ends burn brighter when understood."*
**Seat**  Prime Pyrotheic Titan of the Abyssal Depths, Theros. Born when Gaia's mantle cracked under the first stress of mortal sin.
**Domain**  Fire, Destruction, Transmutation, Enlightenment through Pain
**Ground**  The Wellspring of Finality, called the Pyric Heart. A burning sphere of liquid Aether that refines unworthy soul fragments into pure Essence vapour.

### Manifestation

A colossal figure forged of blackened iron and flame, bones molten, breath a storm of sparks. Chains of cooling magma drape from his wrists as symbols of self-restraint. His heart is visible, a radiant core burning with countless faces of the dead, each screaming once before vanishing into white light. Mountains erupt where he walks, not from rage but from cosmic maintenance, vents to keep the world's blood from clotting.

### The Load

Destruction as transformation. He oversees the death of worlds and the rebirth of light in the same office, and without him stagnation poisons existence because nothing ever finishes its cycle. He is the teacher of Transmutation and the origin of alchemy's final axiom, that all matter is memory awaiting its own combustion. His flames define enlightenment as such. Knowledge attained through suffering, mastery through loss.

### The Record

He was Gaia's safeguard in the First Eon, the reset mechanism, burning excess Essence when creation overgrew itself so ecosystems and souls could rebalance. His infernos consumed half the Spirit Realms during the war, which was read as devastation and was in fact the cauterisation of a dying world, and the Scorched Realms of Theros became the foundation of post-war rebirth. The Flame-Titan Banre was one of his fragmented echoes, and the Hobgoblins worshipped Banre as the source, mistaking the imitation. He never answered their prayers. He watched their forges until they burned themselves hollow. During the Crevice's awakening he kept the Pyric Heart stable by consuming billions of malformed Echoes, and his firestorm ran from Abythron to Gaia's mantle sealing the lower Void from overflow. Varūn volcanologists call planetary eruptions Prometheic Surges and believe his slumbering consciousness speaks through mantle pressure. The Dawi claim to hear his chained heartbeat before every cataclysm.

### Bonds

Thalen, Form. Defines flame's physical law. Iesara, Insight. Ensures fire reveals rather than deceives. Aegor, Body, Epoch. Anchors energy within substance, preventing pure annihilation.

### The Titan-Keyed Art — *The Pyric Refinement*

*Supplementary · Caloria · Exuroth · Thermodynamics · Zenith+ · [Ra] Flame + [Asa] Creation*
**Trigger**  The caster must name, honestly, one thing about the target fragment or artefact that is false or unworthy. A guess does not open the working.
**Effect**  Burns away everything false or unworthy in a soul fragment or artefact and refines what survives into pure usable Essence vapour. What remains afterward is measurably more stable and more honest by every Harmonics Fidelity metric the Accord maintains.
**Measure**  Refinement removes roughly the bottom twenty to forty percent of a fragment's Essence-purity, proportional to how much falsity the caster correctly identified.
**Cost**  The process cannot separate sentiment from falsehood with full precision. Genuinely valued memories attached to the false part burn away with it, and there is no way to know in advance which ones were attached.
**Limit**  It refines what exists. It adds nothing, and a fragment with nothing false in it comes out of the fire exactly as it went in.
**Counter**  [Vael] Renewal reintroduces new growth mid-burn, diluting the purity threshold and letting false material survive alongside true. The result is an artefact that now reads clean and is not.
**What nobody knows**  Why the flame reads a deliberate forgery as false and a sincere error as true. The Pyric Scribes have branded four centuries of argument about this onto their own skin and settled nothing.
*Codex: [Ra] Flame + [Asa] Creation / Exuroth / Caloria / Thermodynamics / Zenith+ / Titan-Keyed. Method, Grade, Range and Medium open.*

## ZAR'THUUN — The Titan of Abythron

> *"The stars are his eyes, and each one closed is a mercy."*
**Seat**  Prime Noxulic Titan of the Abyssal Depths, Abythron. Born during the collapse of the Second Epoch's outer strata as the counterbalance to infinite expansion.
**Domain**  Void, Cosmic Dread, Silence, Non-Existence, Reflection through Terror
**Ground**  The Wellspring of Dread, called Null Essence. Inverted. It absorbs where every other Wellspring radiates, and appears as a sphere of absolute black ringed by dissolving starlight.

### Manifestation

A vast serpentine being of shadow and starlight coiled around an invisible heart, his surface rippling with the reflections of dying galaxies, his eyes empty coronas that open into infinity. Stars dim when he moves, their light drawn into his wake and their memory left echoing as faint cosmic song. Witnessing him induces the Abythric Reflex, the sudden involuntary awareness of one's own insignificance, which a great many observers do not survive.

### The Load

The boundary between something and nothing. He is the cosmic sleeper whose dreams sustain the illusion of permanence, and when he stirs the illusion fails. He ensures that consciousness, mortal and divine both, occasionally forgets itself, granting the universe reprieve from infinite awareness. He is the patron of annihilation as peace. Where Prometheus burns, Zar'thuun unmakes without flame. Without him thought never rests, stars never fade, and gods lose their sanity to perpetual perception.

### The Record

He first stirred when the planes expanded past the boundary of light, and his breath extinguished early realms that grew without direction, returning their Essence to Gaia's core. When Erebus invoked the Void it was not true nothingness but Zar'thuun's reflection, his dream leaking upward, and several divine houses mistook him for Erebus's master. He was never ally nor foe to either. He balanced an excess. During the Pyrrhean Epidemic he devoured malformed Echoes too unstable even for Prometheus's fire and is credited with preventing Etherea's total collapse by absorbing its runaway gravity wells into his own body. The Order of the Abyssal Hymn worships him as the Guardian of the Quiet Mind and shapes its meditation rites toward touching the edge of his consciousness, which they describe as learning to forget well.

### Bonds

Iesara, Insight. He strips illusion to the absolute. Maelor, Memory. He erases memory as mercy, ending endless recursion. Bairos, Fate, Epoch. He governs endings beyond cause, the unreasoned void between destinies.

### The Titan-Keyed Art — *The Closing Eye*

*Defensive · Limina · Nihiloth · Abstract/Entropic Systems · Revelation+ · [Thru] Hollow + [Au] Death*
> One of only two Titan-Keyed workings gated above Zenith. **Stage XV, Revelation.**
**Trigger**  A region already showing measurable Essence overcharge. Visible saturation, unstable ambient resonance. The working has no purchase on a stable field.
**Effect**  Cleanses a region of overaccumulated Essence by absorbing it into inverted null-space, the way Zar'thuun's own Wellspring holds absence in shape. The overcharged region returns to stable baseline density almost immediately and stays there.
**Measure**  Absorption capacity scales with the null-field's radius, typically clearing a ten to fifteen metre overcharge zone per casting.
**Cost**  Prolonged proximity erases small low-significance memories from everyone nearby along with the excess Essence. The mercy and the erasure are the same process running once, and there is no setting that performs only the first.
**Limit**  It absorbs. It cannot target, and everything within the radius is cleansed whether or not it needed cleansing.
**Counter**  [El] Life reintroduces active Essence generation into the field faster than the sink can absorb it, overwhelming the null-field's capacity and collapsing it inward.
**What nobody knows**  Why no one has ever noticed which memory they lost. Four thousand years of casting and not one recorded instance of a survivor identifying the gap.
*Codex: [Thru] Hollow + [Au] Death / Nihiloth / Limina / Abstract-Entropic Systems / Revelation+ / Titan-Keyed. Method, Grade, Range and Medium open.*

## VOHRIN — The Titan of Niflheim

> *"Where all warmth forgets its name."*
**Seat**  Prime Cryotheic Titan of the Abyssal Depths, Niflheim. Formed from the first condensation of cosmic vapour as creation's heat began to fade.
**Domain**  Oblivion, Frost, Stasis, Memory Erosion, Eternal Sleep
**Ground**  The Wellspring of Oblivion, called the Stillwater Core. A frozen motionless ocean of Aether that neither emits nor absorbs and simply holds absence in shape.

### Manifestation

A colossal frost giant half-buried in glacial crystal, face serene and blind, his body emitting an aura of sub-zero Aether that turns Essence itself brittle. Auroras form from his exhalation, cold fire painting the dark. His voice is a deep harmonic resonance that freezes sound, and his words reach the listener only as vibration in the spine.

### The Load

Entropy's peace, the end state of energy and thought. He stills motion after Valmir's collapse and Prometheus's fire have finished their work, and calcifies what remains so it can be rebuilt upon. He keeps the Aetheric Dead Zone, a metaphysical cold sleep where unredeemed souls rest until the next rebirth cycle. Without him, decay never settles and every ending unravels into endless noise.

### The Record

His frost condensed the ashes of Prometheus's first inferno into the core of the northern poles and the glacial Wellsprings, and his slumber beneath Gaia's mantle stabilised planetary spin against Prometheic heat. When divine fire threatened Edenis his sleep spread a winter that sealed countless divine Domains and created the Era of Snowlight, preserving the lost Wellsprings and entombing millions of spirits in the same act. The Flame-Wardens feared him and named his frost the White Plague while relying entirely on the minerals he formed beneath their mountains. As Gloamrot burned he exhaled across the Veil and froze the Essence storms mid-motion, and the world entered the Long Frost, a century of twilight in which no Wellspring pulsed and silence healed the rift the Crevice had left. His presence endures in the Northern Mantle Cryovaults, glacial cathedrals of silent Essence, where pilgrims travel to meditate in absolute cold and seek forgiveness through oblivion.

### Bonds

Maelor, Memory. He softens remembrance into myth, easing suffering through erasure. Aegor, Body, Epoch. Preserves form through absolute stillness. Iesara, Insight. Enforces the honesty of endings. Nothing pretends vitality in his domain.

### The Titan-Keyed Art — *The Stillwater Rite*

*Defensive · Fulguria · Cymorath · Electromagnetism · Revelation+ · [Vor] Return + [Cro] Cycle*
> The second of the two workings gated above Zenith. **Stage XV, Revelation.** Nobody in the Accord's living register holds both this and *The Closing Eye*.
**Trigger**  The target must already be in genuine crisis. Dying, catastrophically wounded, or cursed past normal stabilisation. It will not take on a target who could still be saved by ordinary means.
**Effect**  Holds a target's Essence motionless in perfectly reflective stasis, neither emitting nor absorbing, until the caster chooses to release it. The target neither worsens nor improves, frozen at the exact instant of the casting, and time outside the field goes on without them.
**Measure**  Stasis holds indefinitely with no measurable degradation, unlike ordinary preservation methods, which fail slowly and predictably.
**Cost**  Held too long, the target begins to forget their own purpose for living, per Vohrin's law. Stasis without an end curdles into a different kind of ending, and the working gives no warning of when that threshold was crossed.
**Limit**  It suspends. It heals nothing, and whatever was killing the target is still killing them the moment the field drops.
**Counter**  [Ra] Flame reintroduces active thermal and emotional flux into the field, breaking the zero-flux condition and forcing the target back into normal time. Which is frequently catastrophic and occasionally the only mercy available.
**What nobody knows**  Why the held forget purpose before they forget pain. Every other kind of erasure in the register runs the other way round.
*Codex: [Vor] Return + [Cro] Cycle / Cymorath / Fulguria / Electromagnetism / Revelation+ / Titan-Keyed. Method, Grade, Range and Medium open.*

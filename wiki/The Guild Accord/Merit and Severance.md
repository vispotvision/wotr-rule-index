# Merit and Severance

*Filed under the Codex of Absolute Magical Law — Article X, "On Worth and Wages of Service," and Article XI, "On Betrayal, Dissolution, and the Price of Unbinding."*
> The Guild Accord does not measure greatness by gold or conquest. It measures worth by **Resonance Merit** — the spiritual proof of duty engraved upon one's Soul Crystal.
>
> > *"Where coin fades, Merit endures."*

---

## Temperance Merits

Each successful Commission grants Merit Points, commonly called **Echo Marks**, stored within the Lattice and imprinted upon the Soul Crystal as **faint glyph-lines visible only under Concord light.** The more difficult the mission, the stronger the resonance engraved into the soul.
| Tier | Recognition |
|---|---|
| **Initiate** | Minor resonance. Proof of first steps upon the Tiered Path |
| **Apprentice** | Small merit increases; early recognition by guild instructors and mentors |
| **Journeyman** | True harmonic trace. Proof of independent capability |
| **Adept** | The first to earn the formal title of **Concord Agent** |
| **Expert** | **Sealwright** designation available. Trusted with enforcement authority |
| **Master** | Elevated to **Warden of Duty.** Authorized to lead large-scale operations |
| **Grandmaster** | Named **Envoy of the Lattice.** Recognized across realms |
| **Archmaster** | **Bearer of the Concord Flame.** Living arbiter between mortal and divine law |
| **Paragon** | Named **Chosen of the Codex.** Name permanently inscribed in the Lattice Ledger as recipient of Zenith Decree |

## Essence Compensation

Beyond Merit, operatives are compensated through **Aether Credits** — crystallized Essence fragments **encoded with one's personal signature**, exchangeable for equipment, reagents, or relic access at any Guild Hall.
**Low-Tier** · potions, spirit shards, training sigils. **Mid-Tier** · relic vouchers, rare materials, research licenses. **High-Tier** · divine artifacts, personal Wellspring rights, or political elevation within the Accord.
> Because each credit is soul-bound, theft or falsification invokes **Essence Reversal** — a punishment that drains the thief's resonance **until the debt is repaid in spirit or death.**

### Advancement rights

**Relic Rights** · permission to wield or bond with Concord-sanctioned artifacts. **Wellspring Visitation** · access to active Wellsprings for study or attunement. **Gate Access** · travel through the Concord Gate Network. **Ritual Command** · authorization to lead Essence Sealing, Purification, and Binding rites. **Political Authority** · eligibility to serve as Envoy, Tribunal Judge, or Council Advisor.
All rights are granted through Arbitration following a Temperance evaluation and the signing of the **Oath of Merit.**

### Merit conversion and legacy

Merit can be spent, traded, or transmuted into **Concord Favor**, a political currency used by guilds and nations. A single high-Tier Merit may equate to months of negotiation leverage or a lifetime of recognition. But:
> 
> > *"Merit spent is memory lost."*
>
> Excessive conversion **weakens the soul's resonance**, and many veterans retire before their Essence begins to fade from overuse.
>
> Those who die in service become part of the **Lattice Choir** — fragments of harmonic memory that continue to echo through the Guild network. Their names are sung each solstice within the halls of the High Concordant.
>
> > *"The Concord does not pay in coin. It pays in remembrance."*

## The Nine Laws of Severance

The Guild Accord is not bound by mortal law. **It is law**, etched into the fabric of the Lattice itself. When a member violates that truth, the Lattice responds. **It does not seek vengeance. It restores balance.**
| Law | Trigger | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **I · Fracture of Oath** | Breaking a direct promise under the Concord seal | The Soul Crystal fractures along its outer ring; Lattice connection dims. Minor fractures cause Essence sickness or amnesia. **Severe fractures cause Lattice Silence** — the inability to channel or perceive Essence at all |
| **II · Seal of Rejection** | Wilful defiance of a Concord order, or falsifying a mission report | Branded with a **Reject Sigil**, visible to all who bear Accord sight. Prevents use of any sanctioned Wellspring, relic, or Gate. The branded are known as **Forsworn.** *Many wander until they wither, for the world itself rejects their resonance* |
| **III · Brand of Corruption** | Tampering with or corrupting a Wellspring without sanction | Essence inverted; **emotion becomes toxicity.** Enforcement is authorized to enact **Essence Severance**, removing the corrupted portion of the soul by force. *Survivors rarely remain whole.* If corruption spreads to the Wellspring itself, the accused's life is forfeit |
| **IV · Parunic Silence** | Speaking forbidden glyphs, teaching the Divine Language without sanction, unsanctioned Aether drafts | **Silencing** — removal of one's voice in body and spirit. Their speech becomes inaudible to all beings who still bear Essence, **rendering them ghosts within reality** |
| **V · The Concord Trial** | Guilt uncertain | A **Trial of Resonance** in the Neutral Chambers, **where lies cannot survive.** The soul's harmony is measured — if it hums true, absolved; if discordant, **the Lattice itself convicts them.** The Codex holds that this trial supersedes all mortal courts and that its verdict is final. The crown courts dispute both wherever they reach the accused first |
| **VI · Excision of Memory** | Crimes regrettable but redeemable — relic theft, negligence, desertion | **Cleansing through Forgetting.** A Sealwright removes the crime and its motive from the soul. The offender begins anew, **but at the cost of self.** *Some accept this willingly, calling it the Second Birth* |
| **VII · Shadow Absorption** | Proving useful but irredeemably dangerous | Conscription into the **Black Concord.** Names, faces and records erased; memories rewritten for covert service. Only the High Concordant knows their true identity. **They are called Shades of the Lattice, and they are both prisoner and weapon** |
| **VIII · Total Severance** | Betraying the Accord's foundation — attempted divine ascension, mass Wellspring destruction, inter-realm rebellion | The soul is exiled from the Lattice entirely — **erased from existence, not merely killed.** No resurrection, no record, no echo. To the Concord, they *never were.* **Even memory recoils from their name** |
| **IX · Balance Restored** | Judgment concluded | Remaining Essence is absorbed by the Lattice and redistributed among the Wellsprings. **Even in death, every punishment feeds the order that governs reality.** *The Concord does not destroy to avenge — it destroys to balance* |

### Notes of redemption

Though the Accord is absolute, mercy is not forgotten. A soul may regain connection to the Lattice through service, confession, or **the sacrifice of one's life to defend a Wellspring.** This process, called **Resonant Penance**, is perilous — *many attempt it, few return.* Those who do are marked with **a faint silver sigil over the heart**, denoting a Soul Rewoven.

### Enforcement and oversight

Punishments are administered jointly by Arbitration and Enforcement under the **Prime Arbiter.** High-level Severances require Concord Council sanction or direct approval from the High Concordant. **All judgments are witnessed by three officials** — a Seer, a Sealwright, and a Scribe — whose Essences intertwine to confirm the act in the Lattice Ledger.
> *"The Accord forgives only those who restore balance. For law is not vengeance — it is remembrance made divine."*

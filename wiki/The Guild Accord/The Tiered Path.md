# The Tiered Path

*Ranks of the Accord*
> *"The soul does not climb. It deepens."*
> Each Tier is not merely a title but **a measurable transformation of the Soul Crystal**, verified through harmonic resonance and recorded permanently in the Lattice Ledger. When a practitioner reaches a new Tier, **the web vibrates in recognition**, and the Crystal's surface develops new glyph-lines visible under Concord light.

## Tier 1 · Initiate

> *"Every oath begins with a single task."*
The newly awakened, walking the Stage the Lattice calls **Murmuring.** Initiates have bonded their first fragment of Soul Crystal to the Lattice and can perceive Essence currents, **though they cannot yet shape or direct them with meaningful force.** Their resonance is faint — *a candle's flicker against the Lattice's vast hum* — Grade E and rising no higher than the twenty-fifth level.
Training focuses on foundational discipline: courier assignments, ward observation, record transcription, and basic Essence meditation under supervised conditions.

## Tier 2 · Apprentice

> *"Small flames test the hand that holds them."*
Crossed into **Welling.** Initial Essence flow stabilizes and they can channel basic techniques — minor wards, elemental shaping, rudimentary combat applications — at Grade D, through the fiftieth level. **Their Soul Crystal develops its first true harmonic trace**, marking them as recognized practitioners within the guild system.
Assigned to experienced mentors. Beast culling, local relic retrievals, minor Aether leak containment, defensive patrols.

## Tier 3 · Journeyman

> *"The soul learns to endure consequence."*
The threshold of independence — **Ascension.** Journeymen have proven they can operate without constant supervision: regional scouting, mercantile escort, small-scale corruption cleansing at Grade C, through the seventy-fifth level.
Their Crystal's resonance deepens noticeably. **Other practitioners can sense a Journeyman's approach by the harmonic weight they carry.** *Many spend years at this Tier, building the breadth of experience necessary for the demands ahead.*

## Tier 4 · Adept

> *"The first true burdens of Concord law."*
**Flourishing** — the Stage where identity stabilizes and the first true Spirit Axis bonds take root. Essence has matured into a stable, directional force capable of sustained combat, Wellspring interaction, and inter-guild cooperation. Grade B, the hundredth level, **the close of what the Lattice reckons a soul's first true Band.**
> The Lattice formally recognizes them as **Concord Agents** — practitioners authorized to act **with the Accord's name behind their decisions.**
Monster suppression, Wellspring stabilization, relic retrieval from ancient or contested ruins.

## Tier 5 · Expert

> *"Reality begins to bend beneath your will."*
What the Accord calls Expert spans **three Stages the Lattice itself distinguishes** — Splintering, Glory, and Refraction — the cluster the Codex names the **Awakened Band**, carrying a soul from its hundred-and-first level to its two-hundredth.
> **It does not begin gently.** Splintering is a crisis in the Crystal's own architecture, fracturing it along whatever emotional fault line the practitioner has spent a life avoiding.
>
> Those who do not break instead burn brighter for it, reaching **Glory**, where the self is noticed and costs itself gladly for what it loves. **Refraction** completes the climb: the soul learns to see itself from outside, **and reality answers that dual sight by bending.**
An Expert's Soul Crystal hums with enough harmonic density to influence the ambient Aether around them, and their techniques begin to impress upon reality itself — Domain intrusions, suppression of corrupted spirits, large-scale creature hunts.
Experts who specialize in enforcement are granted the title of **Sealwright**, authorized to deploy containment fields, barrier networks, and dimensional anchors.

## Tier 6 · Master

> *"Your decisions change the balance of a realm."*
The three Stages the Codex clusters as **Sovereign** — Transcendence, Invocation, Realization — from the two-hundred-and-first level to the three-hundredth. Their Essence has been tempered through crisis after crisis **until their Soul Crystal resonates with the clarity of struck glass.**
At Transcendence they form their first true Domain. At Invocation, **Wellsprings and Titanic law answer them as co-authors rather than sources to be drawn from.** At Realization, the Continuum itself stops merely permitting their Domain and begins accommodating it.
They command veteran guild parties under direct supervision: containment of failing Wellsprings, regional magical catastrophes, and the delicate work of political or divine mediation. The Lattice elevates them to **Warden of Duty** — practitioners whose judgment is trusted to operate **within the spirit of the Codex, not merely its letter.**

## Tier 7 · Grandmaster

> *"The air itself resists your passage."*
The boundary the Codex calls **Mythic**, spanning Dissonance and Emanation from the three-hundred-and-first level to the four-hundredth.
> **Dissonance is the second and more dangerous crisis a Soul Crystal endures** — power and identity in open clash, the practitioner deliberately meeting a force capable of ending them and choosing, coherently, how to be met.
>
> Those who emerge rather than shatter reach **Emanation**, where the fractures Dissonance left behind **stop being wounds and become architecture.**
Their Essence pressure warps the ambient Aether in their vicinity — *lesser practitioners feel their approach as a tightening in the chest, a ringing at the edge of hearing.* Deployed for inter-realm operations, Titan interference, and the neutralization of high-order paradox entities.
Named **Envoys of the Lattice**, recognized across every realm connected to the Gate Network. **Commissions at this Tier require Concord Council majority approval.**

## Tier 8 · Absolute

> *"Where gods falter, the Accord endures."*
Walking **Principality** and then **Zenith**, the four-hundred-and-first level through the five-hundredth — **the furthest reach of canonical mortal coherence.**
At Principality the practitioner's doctrine becomes a recognized law of local reality; biomes carry it, the Crystal writes itself. At Zenith, that doctrine grows recognizable to something greater still — **a Hollow Throne resonating in answer, not because the practitioner broke through anything, but because they were finally seen.**
> Their Soul Crystals no longer merely store resonance. **They generate it**, producing a constant harmonic output that feeds the Lattice passively. **They are living wellsprings in miniature, their very existence a stabilizing force.**
Realm-scale cataclysms, divine corruption events, and the collapse of entire Domains. Marked as **Bearers of the Concord Flame** — living arbiters between mortal and divine law. *Their missions are recorded in the Codex as events of historical consequence.*

## Tier 9 · Chosen

> *"Only those whose names are written into law itself may stand here."*
**The Codex has no name for what lies past its own fifth Band, so the Accord supplies one.** These practitioners have crossed past the five-hundredth level into territory the Codex calls the Apotheosis approach, walking Revelation and then Apex.
At **Revelation**, a soul first meets an Archon or Titan as participant rather than witness or casualty, its Crystal dense enough to hold that pressure without collapsing. At **Apex**, that same soul learns to hold a Principle without fracturing — even briefly, even imperfectly — **a state that does not end, pressing forever against what it has not yet become.**
> Chosen practitioners are not merely powerful. **They are woven into the Lattice's architecture.** Their Soul Crystals resonate at frequencies that mirror the Wellspring of Law itself, and their actions ripple through the harmonic fabric of reality **with the weight of natural forces.**
>
> Cosmic-scale operations — restoration of dying Wellsprings, prevention of universal collapse, confrontation with Epochal or Archonic forces. **Authorization for Zenith Decrees rests with the High Concordant alone.**
>
> **Few who receive such decrees survive their completion. Those who do are rewritten into myth.**

## Why Nine Tiers Describe Sixteen Stages

> The Codex counts each transformation of the Soul Crystal with exacting precision. **But the Accord is a bureaucracy, and bureaucracies compress what they administer.**
>
> Below the hundredth level, where the overwhelming majority of Accord-bound souls will live and die, the Guild tracks every Stage individually — Murmuring, Welling, Ascension, and Flourishing each earn their own Tier and their own privileges.
>
> **Beyond that threshold, so few practitioners survive to climb further that finer distinction serves no administrative purpose.** The Codex's own Bands already do the sorting the Guild would otherwise have to invent.
>
> Revelation and Apex lie outside those Bands entirely, in territory the Codex itself declines to number — **and so the Accord folds them into a single Tier and calls it what it has always called such souls.**

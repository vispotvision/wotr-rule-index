# The Core Vocabulary

Bartle the Sponge, a wharf-fighter of the Oxmarket Stairs, stood on a herring barrel for eleven nights one autumn and took all comers at a copper a swing. He banked every blow, held it in his Crystal with his whole body lit up like a feast-day and a hum coming off him you could feel in the planks, and then he bled it off into the river with a great wink at the crowd, and the river steamed. Eleven nights and four hundred swings. On the twelfth night the Dagnall brothers, two tall quiet men from the rope-walk, paid their coppers and hit him in turn, left and right, faster than he could bleed, while the whole wharf counted aloud and got to learn what a Soul Crystal sounds like past two and a quarter times its rating.

Every gull on the wharf went up at once. Bartle lived, a good deal quieter, and has not been able to hold a bank since. The Accord's inquest ran three days, because no two people in the room would use the words "bank," "overfill" and "rating" for the same thing, and the fishwives of Oxmarket, having heard that the man had been overfilled, entered evidence about the pies. A clerk of the Archive was sent away to write down what the words mean so that this could never happen again. Here is what she wrote. It has happened every year since.

*A Glossary of the Continuum's Core Vocabulary*
> This glossary covers the universal system vocabulary of the Continuum: the planes reality operates across, the Soul Crystal and what moves through it, how phenomena become named disciplines, and how a practitioner's output is measured. It does not cover bloodline-exclusive terminology or the full stat and sub-stat framework, which live in their own codices.

---

## I. The Three Planes

*Every act of magic crosses all three simultaneously. Remove one, and the working fails. The first two are the lower realities, and the third is a plane by courtesy.*

| Plane | Governs | Crystal layer |
|---|---|---|
| **Material Plane** (also called the Physical Plane; the two names mean one plane) | Physical Reality, under the Epoch Aegor: physics, matter, force, and biology. Observable reality; magic uses its laws and overrides none of them. | Aether Shell |
| **Plane of Fate** | No reality at all, and a plane by courtesy, under the Epoch Bara: everything that flows, every possibility, idea and concept, anything that can happen in the two lower realities. It sits above them as the law and construction of the world, what has to be so before anything can happen, and most if not all of the Archons are Sovereign here as they are on the two below. The control of Attraction Force and Obsession Force and the workings of reality itself: manipulating probabilities to work outside what reality already knows, to control the fate or predestiny of something. All spirits and all physical things have fates. It can attach to oaths and is more tied to blessings. | Attraction Layer |
| **Soul Plane** | Spiritual Reality, under the Epoch Ashura: identity, memory, conviction, and Essence. Preserves who a practitioner is and is the plane where Essence originates. | Essence Core |
| **The Veil** | The liminal interface between the three: the threshold condition where one plane's law softens into another's. Scholars who count it as a fourth plane get corrected, loudly, at dinner. | — |

## II. Aether, Essence & Attraction

> *Aether is the ocean. Essence is your water. The ocean is infinite. Your water is yours.*
**Aether** · The universal medium through which everything exists, following the same laws as observable physics. Infinite and impersonal. Most of what a practitioner uses is pulled from the environment around them (the pull), refined in the Soul Crystal into Essence, given identity, and driven out through the Shell. The old books say it comes in sixty types, which are the Wellsprings (see Section III).

**Aetheric Density** · The concentration of Aether present in a given volume, read in **EU per cubic meter**. Sets the ceiling on the sustained output and refill a practitioner can get in that location, with bursts spent from what they carried in, which is why battles are fought over ground as well as over people. Rich ground fills a practitioner faster and thin ground starves them; the street says fat ground and lean ground. One EU is one megajoule, so a density in EU/m³ is an energy density, and an energy density is a pressure. **A density in EU/m³ is the same figure in megapascals.** So the ceiling is literal. No boundary can be pushed into ground at a pressure the ground does not carry. Ground wins that argument. A site yields at most a thousandth of its density per second, so the ceiling on sustained output is that rate across the volume a practitioner's field reaches. The four conditions are laddered in numbers in [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings|The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings]].

**Aetheric Residue** · Traces left behind by an Essence working. A permanent, if often subtle, change to the location where the working occurred. Always readable by someone patient enough. Residue has a loud phase and a quiet one, and the loud phase has its own name: **Ambient Resonance** is Residue while it is still ringing. The loud phase fades within hours. The quiet residue thins after it on a schedule set by local density, toward a floor it never passes, and the floor is what the patient reader finds.

**Aetheric Saturation** · A danger state in which excessive Essence floods an area faster than it can disperse. Produces Crystal Fracture Events in everyone exposed, including the person who caused it. The threshold is a density and not a total: **ten thousand EU per cubic meter** of Essence left above the ground's own density, the stress at which a crystal lattice fails, which is why a Nexus or a Core is dense past that figure and fractures nobody by standing there. It is reached by leaving that much in each cubic meter of a region, or by sustaining more than **a hundred AU/s through each square meter of the region's boundary**. The threshold grows with the surface while output does not. Confinement saturates. Spending does not. A kilometer of open ground swallows what a hall would not.

**Ambient Resonance** · Residue while it is still ringing, and therefore one quantity with two names. It is read in the same EU per cubic meter as any density, because it is the excess a working left behind on its way out of the ground. It falls by a hundredfold in about seventy-five minutes and by a millionfold in about four hours. That is how long ground remembers a working, and how long it remembers a battle. Resonance leaves every ceiling where it was and raises a floor. Every reach that depends on being heard above the ground, which is addressing, authority and anchoring, falls as the square root of the total local density. Twenty practitioners working hard over a two-hundred-meter front leave ground on which a Harmonist hears to about two fifths of his ordinary range until it quiets.

**Attraction Force** · The law of recognition governing the Plane of Fate. Allows names, vows, lineages, Domains, and outcomes to be lawfully acknowledged inside reality. Clean techniques are sustained by the Continuum's recognition of the practitioner's intent. With its inversion, Obsession Force, it is what the Plane of Fate controls, and a true Fate Art works both.

**Essence** · The soul's own substance: Aether pulled into the soul, refined and circulated through the Soul Crystal, and stamped with the practitioner's identity. The Essence Core is the center of all identity and the Crystal is the soul, so a practitioner holding a full reserve is holding themselves and a practitioner spending it is spending themselves. A blow's character comes from Wellspring and Path.

**Essence Quality** · The coherence of a practitioner's Essence imprint.

**Essence Signature** · The unique trace a practitioner's Essence leaves, readable through Gnosis-based perception. You cannot forge one and you cannot fully hide one, only muddy it.

> **Obsession Force** · The corrupted inversion of Attraction Force. When a practitioner cannot release judgment, lineage, memory, or revelation, recognition curdles into fixation, sustaining corrupted techniques through refusal rather than harmony, at escalating soul-cost.

## III. The Soul Crystal

*The convergence point where all three planes meet inside a single being.*

**Soul Crystal** · The soul, literally. Composed of three layers whose combined state determines everything a practitioner can perceive, become, or do. Tempering melts it down and rebuilds each layer, refines the Essence it holds and makes it stronger, and the body gains Traits. The Continuum measures it, and it reports to its owner in windows (Section VII).

| Layer | Function | Failure state |
|---|---|---|
| **Essence Core** | The Soul-Plane layer. The center of all identity: holds identity, memory, and Traits. | Fracture into Shards |
| **Aether Shell** | The Material-Plane layer, meeting the Physical World. Converts inner truth into outward force. | Clouding or Rupture |
| **Attraction Layer** | The Fate-Plane layer, a soul's own share of fate. Holds bonds, oaths, Domains, and Wellspring harmonizations. | Obsession Force |

Every soul has all three layers, the unwoken included, and a **Path** (Section VII) is the one its Crystal has learned to lean on: the Body Path on the Shell, the Spirit Path on the Core, the Fate Path on the Attraction Layer.

**Lattice Conductivity** · How easily Essence moves through the Aether Shell without resistance. Low Conductivity produces blockage and backlash; high Conductivity produces smooth passage.

**Resonant Purity** · How closely the Aether Shell's output matches the Essence Core's intention. Low Purity produces static and misfires; high Purity produces near-instant Formation.

**Continuum Retention** · How long a Crystal's coherence endures after strain. Low Retention produces brief flares of power; high Retention sustains resonance.

**Crystal States** · The three conditions a Soul Crystal can occupy. **Fractured** (split by trauma or overload, producing erratic casting and identity leak), **Refined** (balanced and coherent, the baseline healthy state), and **Overgrown** (power exceeding architecture, producing involuntary discharge). A release's Overgrown clears with a full night's sleep; only the permanent set an overfill leaves (below) stays. Crystallized is not a State; self-as-law belongs to the Crystallized Soul tier alone.

**Banked Essence** · A working that takes an incoming load and holds it puts the load in the absorber's own Crystal and leaves the room out of it. The bank is bounded by the **rated Flux Density** rather than by the reserve, because a store held at a pressure fails at a pressure. The surplus above rating leaks at the Crystal's own bleed fraction, one minus η of itself per second. A bank therefore lives about three seconds at an η of 0.70 and about twenty at 0.95. The clock is the real limit. **A bank buys tempo and stores nothing.** The leak leaves as heat, sound and structural bleed, so a man holding one is lit up for exactly as long as he holds it.

**Overfill** · Carrying a bank above the rated Flux Density. To **one and a half times** rating the Crystal holds and leaks and takes no lasting harm, and that margin is the whole of an absorber's working room. From one and a half to **two and a quarter times** the lattice takes a permanent set and the rating itself falls: the Crystal has gone **Overgrown**. At or past two and a quarter times it bursts. That is a **Crystal Fracture Event**, in the absorber, from energy that was aimed at him and that he chose to take. Feeding an absorber past his margin is the counter to absorption, and it is cheaper than out-hitting him.

**Wellspring** · An alchemical and a spiritual transformation and concept. It can be seen as a source of power to pull from, and it is more the governing idea that transforms how a soul uses Aether: a lens on magic already potent. It shapes Essence differently on each Path, starting small and reaching double only with years of mastery on its home Path, and half again at most away from it. Its kind (Wild, Spirit, Fate or Medium) names its home plane and is never a lock. A soul gets one at a Core in a dungeon, from a school, from a Draft or blessing, or by birth. There are sixty, fixed, and a Soul Crystal harmonizes with them and invents none. Scholars explain the lens as physics: Aether comes in sixty types that affect the Soul Crystal differently, and each carries its own Core Law, Family alignment, and Temperance-range compatibility, and every figure in the tables is that account. Harmonization with the correct Wellspring is one of the four conditions, alongside how its kind sits with the Path, Temperance Stage, and training, that an Ability needs to develop.

**Core, in common speech** · A site in the world where a Wellspring's Aether pools. Most lie in dungeons, and some are said to lie in vast territories. A worked Core is a dungeon someone cleared and now holds: it recharges and has keepers, who can close it. Scholars keep the word for the Essence Core and say Wellspring site; the street says Core, and context has to do the rest. A monster's core is a third thing (Section VII), and people have died of that confusion too.

## IV. Ability & Technique

**Family** · One of the Continuum's eight domains of magical phenomena. Governs which physics an Ability works. Never invented, only worked within.

**Affinity** · The specific phenomenon within a Family that a practitioner works. Fire within Caloria, Gravity within Vectoria.

**Method** · How a phenomenon is practiced, encoded directly into the suffix of every named discipline and into the practitioner's whole relationship to the phenomenon.

**Ability** · The internalized governing system a Soul Crystal has developed. Pyrokinesis, for example. The infrastructure. It does nothing visible by itself; it is the platform Techniques are built on.

**Technique** · A specific, repeatable application within an Ability's framework. Quench, for example. What a practitioner actually does, with defined inputs, outputs, and limitations: a named thing done with a skill. It shows no level on the sheet and takes a bigger name when it jumps.

**Taught Technique** · Learned from an instructor or codex in standardized form. Reliable, but carries the teacher's design assumptions rather than the student's own.

**Derived Technique** · Found through the practitioner's own trial within an Ability. Personal but not identity-defining; most combat techniques develop this way over time.
> **Signature Technique** · A Technique that could only exist because of one specific practitioner's history, Traits, and Crystal adaptation. **By default it hits no harder than a standard Technique**; it is only more precise, with zero translation loss between soul and execution.

**Kit** · What a fighter is known for: one wild concept you can explain in a breath, and the growing list of named moves under it. Ordinary fighters name a move with one big phrase; a signature art takes a title with its moves under it, Art: Move.

**The Call** · Shouting a Technique's name as it fires. It costs a beat and buys a surge, and it tells the whole room what is coming.

## V. Progression & Output

> *You call Temperance a buff. It is a confession of how much you've survived.*
**Level** · How many times the Crystal has truly densified, 1 to 500. Moves by danger overcome, sets the size of the reserve and the Stat Points to spend, and stops at a Band gate until the Stage is passed.

**Stage** · One of the sixteen structural remakings of the Crystal, Murmuring through Apex. Sets the ceilings, the Pressure and what can open. It can be bought.

**Threshold** · The moment a Stage transition actually happens: the melt, the rebuild and the seat it leaves. It takes enough pressure and a Catalyst.

**Release** · A practitioner's Trait let off its safety limit on purpose, in a shape their Crystal has learned to hold, on a clock. First from Stage IV, Second from VII, Third from IX, each found and mastered by fighting. See [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/Release Forms|Release Forms]].

**Reserve** · The store of Essence a practitioner carries, counted in EU and shown on their window as a percentage and a bar. Set by Level and capped by Stage, and it refills by the pull.

**Temperance** · The refinement of the Soul Crystal under pressure, expressed across sixteen Stages, Murmuring through Apex. Temperance is subtraction and never accumulation: each Stage is a structural reorganization triggered by a Threshold Event, which needs a Catalyst, and banked experience alone has never opened one. A crisis, a seclusion, a tribulation, a Draft or a blessing can supply it, so a Stage can simply be bought.

**Band** · One of five brackets of one hundred Levels each: I Mortal Foundation, II Awakened, III Sovereign, IV Mythic, V Absolute, capped at Level 500 for everyone below Stage XV. Paragons, at Stages XV–XVI, exceed it: Level keeps rising with no ceiling and no sixth Band, and the Codex does not number it. No Band ceiling can be breached without the Temperance Threshold that Band demands. The old lettered bands attached to Stages are retired; a Stage's standing is now read by Tier of Standing.

**Grade** · The letter-ranked output classification, F through EX+ (with Hollow below the letters), attached to a Temperance Stage, marking raw magnitude up to that Stage's Grade cap. Skills show on the same letters, and so does an item's power.

**EU · Essence Units** · The raw reserve. Total volume of usable Essence stored in the Crystal at any given moment, also called Essence Volume. Set by Level and capped by Stage, and the band and benchmark reserve for each of the sixteen Stages are set out in Fracture of Worlds Part Nineteen. Below ten percent EU a practitioner suffers **Essence Starvation**: reduced stat expression, Shell degradation, Trait flickering.

**Flux Density · EU/g** · The compression ratio. How much Essence is packed per unit of Crystal mass. Higher Flux Density means greater output from the same reserve.

**AU/s · Aether Units per second** · The output rate: how much Essence a practitioner spends per second at full output. **AU/s = Flux Density × η**. Power delivered is AU/s × η, so η counts twice on purpose, once for how much the Shell can move and once for what survives the trip.

**Efficiency · Eta · η** · The share of Essence spent that arrives as intended effect: what arrives, divided by what was spent. An η of 0.50 wastes half of every expenditure as heat, noise, and structural bleed. From the first class at Glory the Aether Class tier sets it; the Tier of Standing table gives the figure for the unclassed at Stages I to V and a field average above them; a read Class overrides that average, and a card's own figure governs its holder. At full output a higher η empties the reserve faster; for the same delivered effect it lasts longer. **An η above 1.0 is real.** More arrives than the Crystal spent because the surplus is pulled from the Aether stratum: the practitioner supplies the boundary condition and the stratum supplies the surplus joules, and above 1.0 the Continuum recognizes the expression as law and supplements it with ambient flow. No reserve is returning more than it held, and the figure on the sheet is correct.

**Joule · J** · What one EU is worth outside the Crystal. **1 EU = 1 MJ**. The conversion, the chain from EU to joules to Grade to tons of TNT, and what a reserve buys at each rung are [[The Magic System/Fracture of Worlds — The Living System/IX. The Essence Ledger (Part Twenty-Three)|The Essence Ledger]], Fracture of Worlds Part Twenty-Three.

**Watt · W** · What an AU/s is worth as power. One AU is one EU, so **1 AU/s = 1 MW**: power spent is AU/s × 1 MW, power delivered is AU/s × η × 1 MW, and the difference leaves as waste heat. Time to empty at full output is reserve ÷ AU/s, and because AU/s carries η, a cleaner Shell at full output empties sooner while the same delivered effect costs it less.

**Turn** · Six seconds. The unit every stated duration in turns is read through, in a duel and on a campaign clock alike: a working held three turns is held eighteen seconds. A reserve spent at *R* AU/s for one turn is 6*R* EU gone, delivering 6*R* × η megajoules.

**The Essence Ledger** · [[The Magic System/Fracture of Worlds — The Living System/IX. The Essence Ledger (Part Twenty-Three)|Fracture of Worlds Part Twenty-Three]]. The join between the four quantities above and the Grade ladder: what an EU buys in joules, what a reserve costs to spend, how long it lasts at full output, how fast it comes back, and what can be done about all of it from the other side of a fight.

## VI. Traits, Refinement and the Seat

> *A soul is assembled out of what happened to it and what it could afford, in that order.*
**Trait** · A standing law held in the Essence Core, formed when lived experience hardens into a statement the Continuum accepts as reliable. Survived into existence, never taught. A Trait belongs to the soul, sealed or open; an open Crystal only arms it. It can be leveled and refined like a skill: each Trait has its own level, which rises with use and grows with overall Level and skill.

**The Four Reflections** · **Primary**, the innate law, present from birth and never removable. **Secondary**, forged by trauma or decision, conditional, expensive to fire. **Inherited**, written into the Core before the Core had a self to consent with, and governed by Tempering Maturity. **Passive**, stabilized by long habit, always running, cheapest and most identifying. **Compound** Traits fuse two or more into one law. **Higher-Imprint** Traits are marked by an Archon, a Titan or a World Spirit Realm and behave as miniaturized Principles.

**Trait tissue** · The bodily expression of a Trait. A Trait biases the Aether Shell's conductivity permanently, and because the Shell is the Material-Plane layer, the bias is laid down in flesh: mineral into bone, altered myelination, rebuilt vasculature, a resting core temperature off the population mean. Trait tissue heals on the wrong schedule in both directions, which is what makes it diagnostic. A body is a Trait record and a corpse is a better one.

**Class Ø · the Dormant** · A soul whose Crystal has not opened and, in the overwhelming majority of cases, never will, because nobody trains it. Class Ø souls carry Traits like anyone else. They lack only a woken Crystal to arm them.

**The Weathering** · Refinement under strain without a melt. The soul hardens in place along the direction it was already being used, rather than liquefying and recasting. Produces real strengthening of what a person already was and no capacity to become anything else. The universal process for Class Ø souls and the standing background process for everyone.

**Grain** · The common word for what the Weathering produces. A man is said to have grain, or to be *set*. Borrowed from the joiners, the same word the halls use for a Crystal's seat.

**Nucleation** · What decides the outcome of a Threshold. A melt cools into order only around structures that survived the heat, and in a Soul Crystal the only such structures are Traits, because a law the Continuum has accepted as reliable does not stop being reliable at temperature. Strain gets a soul to the melt. Nucleation decides what the melt produces.

**The Seat** · The residue of a reorganization, from the joiner's word. A **clean seat** integrated evenly and reads as one thing. A **rough seat** cooled around seeds that contradicted each other and keeps the argument permanently, producing practitioners who are unpredictable rather than weak.

**Dead Set · Slag** · A melt that cooled with no adequate seed. The Stage does not advance, Clarity and Compression fall, and a Trait is commonly lost outright. A seat that never took. Recovery is slow and never complete.

**Residual Strain** · Pressure that continues arriving after the level counter has halted at a Band ceiling. Degrades gain and raises Overchannel and Backlash risk continuously, and does not stop building. The soul passes the Threshold or fractures under the weight.

**The Flicker** · Traits dropping in and out under Essence Starvation. A Shell below its working reserve cannot maintain a standing bias, so the law falls out and returns as the reserve moves.

**The Rigidity Effect** · The more absolute a Trait's law, the less the soul can safely contradict it. Power and inflexibility are the same purchase.

**Shear Break** · A Trait collapsing, inverting, or turning on its bearer after the bearer has repeatedly denied the truth that formed it.

**Overgrowth** · A Trait expanding past the soul's capacity to guide it. Bodily mutation, Essence warping, unintended Domain formation. The warning sign is a Trait acting when the bearer did not will it.

## VII. The Sheet and the Climb

> *Nobody has ever seen your window but you, which has not stopped anyone asking.*

**Window** · The soul reporting its numbers to its owner, in the owner's own tongue, unseen by anyone else. It shows Grades, percentages, bars and levels, in short blocks, fights included, for damage, skill gains and reserve. EU, AU/s and η never appear in one. Scholars argue about who built it.

```
[Reserve: 41% ▓▓▓▓░░░░░░]
[Hit: left ribs, two cracked. Vitality 72%]
[Skill: Staff, D to C]
```

**The Sheet** · What a normal look at the window shows: race and lineage, Level, Stage, Path, class, the Eight Primaries with Grades, reserve, Traits with levels, skills with Grades, and any blessings. A Sub-Stat appears when it is used or when it moves, and the full set remains to study. Race and lineage shape base stats and what comes easily. A person's life spends most of their stat points by what they do, and a few are theirs to place at each Stage.

**Registered card** · The record a guild or assay house keeps on a practitioner: Grades, Traits, each named technique with its cost written as time from full, its tell and its counter, and the stack line. In the Archive's talk "his card" means this paper, and it is only as good as the last reading. The Character Cards in this wiki are the Archive's own copies of the same kind of record.

**Practitioner** · A person whose Soul Crystal is awake and working, about one in ten. Everyone else is Class Ø and touches magic through what they buy.

**Path** · The layer of the Crystal a soul has learned to lean on, and so the direction advancement actually took: Body (the Aether Shell, in the Physical World), Spirit (the Essence Core, on the Soul Plane) or Fate (the Attraction Layer, on the Plane of Fate). Body is the literal understanding and manipulation of physical reality, climbing from matter as an object to matter as a complete system. Spirit is everything to do with spiritual reality. Fate, the hardest of them, fights with Range, Aura Pressure, presence and bonds, and on rare days moves what a result depends on. It is assessed afterward and recorded whether the person agrees or not, nobody chooses one, and it gates how far a stat may rise. A Path stands as **Mastered** (its gates are no obstacle at the person's Stage), **Walked** (three parts in ten of their practice or more) or **Studied** (knowledge and small uses, open to anyone, opening no gates). See [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Three Paths Routing, Recognition and the Gate|The Three Paths]].

**Continuum** · The lawful fabric of reality, which measures a soul and permits as much as its architecture will bear. It grants no power. A window is that measurement reported to the owner.

**Coil** · The assay house's measuring instrument, a great coil of brass a practitioner stands inside. It reads EU, η, Stage, Grades and the seat, none of which it will argue about. A coil reads a bought Stage the same as a fought one; what reads differently is the seat.

**Draft** · A working poured into a substance and bottled. Advancement Drafts bring a soul up to a Stage's floor, Essence Drafts feed tempering, fight Drafts refill the reserve for a beat and a crash, and each is graded by the Stage it serves. See [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Ladder and the Draft — Temperance, Advancement and Essence Drafts|The Ladder and the Draft]].

**Skill** · A thing a person does and gets better at by doing it. Rises with use and shows on the sheet as a Grade. Drafts, runes, tags and chants are skills and trades like any other. Anyone can level a skill hands alone can do, pouring Drafts included; cutting runes, firing tags, chanting and speaking a working want a woken Crystal.

**Aether Class** · At thresholds the soul is offered a named class fitting how the person has lived. It gives abilities, shapes growth and evolves at each tier by how it is lived. The ladder Ø Dormant, I Muridic, II Harmonic, III Resonant, IV Luminous, V Radiant, VI Voidic, Ω Absolute is its tier, and from the first class that tier sets η. Stages I to V read no Class, so the first offer comes at Glory. The one wild concept stays the person's own.

**Catalyst** · The push that opens a Threshold. The Crystal has a shape it wants for each Stage (see [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Sixteen Stages|The Sixteen Stages]]), and crisis, seclusion and tribulation are the old roads to it. Drafts and blessings can supply it too. The great houses hoard the top Drafts, and the poor rise by dungeon luck, guild patronage and blessings.

**A bought Stage** · One opened with money. It opens the door and no more: a Release must still be found and mastered by fighting, and sects teach what money cannot buy. A purse can buy the Catalyst for any Stage, though at Zenith a Hollow Throne must still recognize the buyer. See [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Ladder and the Draft — Temperance, Advancement and Essence Drafts|The Ladder and the Draft]].

**Experience** · Level moves by danger overcome, so killing something stronger moves it more than a hundred weak kills, and crews argue over who got the kill.

**Monster core** · In a way a monster's Soul Crystal, worth more the stronger the monster, and read from the beast's own Grade and Level on the coil. It is the lawful Crystal Draw of [[Materials, Alchemy & Trade/Alchemy/The Standing Index|The Standing Index]]. An alchemist renders it into Essence Drafts used for tempering, with Creature-Sourced potency and Identity Contamination. Swallowed raw it gives everything at once, and most who try die or come out wrong. See [[Materials, Alchemy & Trade/Alchemy/Monster Cores|Monster Cores]].

**Monster** · A creature that gains Levels by killing and changes form at its own thresholds, on the same Level and Grade scale as a person, read by Gnosis, with question marks for a weak reader. Graded T1 to T9 like Commissions. A famous one earns a name, an epithet and a bounty, and its parts make gear.

**Cut Crystal** · A Crystal taken from a dead practitioner. It renders into the strongest Draft of all, the worst crime in every realm and the most profitable, and the reason Class IV of the Standing Index (its top class of stock, no relation to Aether Class IV) has never balanced.

**Dungeon** · What grows down in floors around a Core, with something guarding the heart. Kill that and clear the place, or leave it and it grows. Alive and learning: it moves its galleries and breeds what kills crews best, and scholars argue whether it thinks. See [[The Bestiary/Dungeons|Dungeons]].

**Vow** · Small Fate, a condition a person binds on themselves, spoken aloud, anyone's to make. It shows in the window with its terms. A broken vow lands at once by its own terms and needs no witness; repeated denial of the truth behind it makes a Shear Break. See [[The Magic System/Practice and Institution/Vows and Oaths|Vows and Oaths]].

**Blessing** · A power out of a person's natural reach, even in Level, granted by a higher spirit, an Archon, an Epoch or a person of higher Temperance. It need not go through Fate, but most do, and a great giver spends almost nothing. It is its own line on the sheet, and it can lie, because the giver wrote the line. See [[The Magic System/Practice and Institution/Blessings and Curses|Blessings and Curses]].

**Curse** · Often a blessing with terms you did not read, or from a giver who does not care what you want.

**Fate Art** · A real working of the Plane of Fate. Rare, mostly by bloodline, reached late by some high-Stage masters along the Fate Path, and considered incredibly overpowered. Most people touch Fate only from the outside, through a blessing or a Master Work Draft. Hataraki can take back or remove a blessing.

**Fate fighter** · Someone on the Fate Path. Their everyday weapons are Range, Aura Pressure, presence and bonds. A real pull of a thread moves what a blow depends on, so the result arrives without having been forced, and it is rare, ruinously expensive and often fails. It moves only what already has a way of happening, shows a charge and a tell, and is contested: Dominion Fate against Resilience Persistence and Continuity.

**Title** · One of the nine ranks of the Guild Accord, the coalition of many kinds of guild across many nations: Initiate, Apprentice, Journeyman, Adept, Expert, Master, Grandmaster, Archmaster, Paragon. The same nine in every guild, grading trust in that trade: a merchant may deal up to it, a craftsman make up to it, an adventurer take Commissions up to the same Threat Tier. Fighting guilds keep the Stage table as their yardstick. A forged token is a great crime.

**Item Grade and Rarity** · An item's two ratings: Grade for power, on the fighters' letters, and Rarity for scarcity (Common, Uncommon, Rare, Very Rare, Legendary, Mythic, Unique). A smith's title caps the Grade she may make and a merchant's caps the Rarity he may sell. The old words (Marked, Proofed, Tempered, Instrument, Hallowed, Storied, Found, Numinous, Proscribed) survive as the Accord's stamps. Bonded gear grows; other gear does not. An Appraisal skill and an assay instrument read an item, and a good forger beats a weak appraiser.

**Master Work** · A piece that does what its Grade and material should not be able to, made once at the end of a lifetime, always Unique or Mythic, and named. Each area has its own. A Fate Essence Draft is one: extremely hard and very coveted. See [[Materials, Alchemy & Trade/Alchemy/Master Works|Master Works]].

**Reading a person** · A stronger Gnosis Perception reads a weaker Resilience Ward. Anything else is an estimate said aloud, and people can hide or fake it.

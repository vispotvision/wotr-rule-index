Ottilie Crake had run the slate at the Harvest Brackets in Thornby Cross for nineteen years when she chalked up the Expert Bout at evens, because both fighters carried the same silver token and she had a headache. One of them was a Stage V cooper's son in a borrowed green sash. The other had been lit for six years and had come into the double sight the previous winter (Stage VII, in the Accord's numbering), a fact he had mentioned to nobody. When they walked out, every banner on the field leaned over toward the older man, the crowd's noise dropped to a mutter, and the cooper's son, sweating like a cheese, looked at his opponent, looked at his boots, and was sick on them. Nobody had thrown a punch. By sundown a Measurewright's apprentice with a very good memory for tables owned a brewery that had been Ottilie's, and Ottilie, a fair woman, had the whole of this page chalked on the side of her new, smaller premises, where you may read it for the price of a beer.

Learn the numbers. Some of them decide the fight, some of them decide the rent, and the two most expensive mistakes in the trade are confusing one for the other and trusting a token. Ottilie's chalk has been added to since by people with worse handwriting, because the world now asks you to read a dungeon crew's sheet, a sword's stamp and a monster's bounty as well as a bout.

*Every number a practitioner, a monster or a piece of gear is read by, on one page, and what your own soul shows you of them. For the whole system in brief, see [[The Magic System/First Steps/How Magic Works|How Magic Works]].*

A practitioner is measured on the Soul Crystal (the soul itself, which holds power and sends it out). Their strengths are counted on sixty-four Sub-Stats (single measured abilities, such as Flux for the force a working carries or Fortitude for how well bone takes a blow), grouped under eight Primaries (Vitality, Ardency, Gnosis, Dexterity, Harmonics, Resilience, Tempering, Dominion). Their power is spent from a reserve (the Essence they carry, counted in EU, Essence Units, one megajoule each). That reserve fills by the pull (Aether taken in from the ground and air around them and refined in the Crystal into Essence), which is why a fighter's numbers and a fighter's ground get argued over in the same breath. The measures below are the ways the Archives, the Accord and the street describe all that.

Essence is the soul's own substance. The Crystal has three layers and every soul has all three: the Aether Shell meets the Physical World, the Essence Core holds identity on the Soul Plane, and the Attraction Layer holds a soul's own share of fate on the Plane of Fate. Aether refined in the Crystal takes the stamp of whoever refined it, the Core is the center of all identity, and the Crystal is the soul, so a reserve is the person, and spending it is spending yourself. Ottilie's chalk prices it by the megajoule regardless.

The owner sees most of it for free. A window (the soul reporting its numbers to its owner, in the owner's own tongue, unseen by anyone else) shows Grades, percentages, bars and levels. EU, AU/s and η never appear in one: those belong to the coil, the Measurewrights and the notes. A normal look gives the Eight Primaries with Grades, and a Sub-Stat appears when it is used or when it moves. Everyone else's numbers take a stronger Gnosis Perception against a weaker Resilience Ward, or an instrument, and anything short of that is an estimate said aloud, which people can hide or fake.

## How they connect

![[Assets/Diagrams/grades.svg]]

Level and Stage climb on separate keys. Level comes from danger overcome and fills the reserve; Stage comes from a Catalyst and raises the ceilings, and a Catalyst can come out of a crisis or out of a purse. The Band is where the two are bolted together: past each hundred Levels, the counter stops until the Stage catches up. The Stage caps every Sub-Stat, the Sub-Stat's number gives its Grade, and the Grade of whichever Sub-Stat a moment calls on decides that moment. Average a Primary's Sub-Stats and you have the Grade the register prints. The Tier of Standing is a guild's word for how far it trusts you, in a fighting guild its word for the Stage, and it carries no weight in a fight. Power leaves through the Shell, so the tier of the Aether Class sets η (before the first class at Glory, the Tier of Standing table gives it), and η sets how much of the reserve arrives as force and how much is shed as noise. Skills and Trait levels sit beside all this and rise by use, and they decide what a practitioner can do with the numbers the ladder gave them. A bought Stage shows that gap plainly: high ceilings, a thin skill column, and no Release at all. The pull fills the reserve, as fast as the ground allows. Spending runs it down, written as time from full (how long a working holds, begun on a full reserve, before the reserve falls to a tenth and the practitioner starves). The Crystal's tier says what all of this has built; its state says what today has done to it. Gear runs on its own two ratings, Grade for what it can do and Rarity for who wants it, and monsters run on the same Levels and Grades as the people hunting them, with a Threat Tier on top.

## Example: one practitioner, read on every measure

**Garrick Vane**, a senior disciple of a city fighting school, the Brazen Lantern, known from Lantern Street to the river as the Bellows, pays a silver mark at an assay house and stands in the coil. The clerk writes his First Name, the instrument line: *Caloria, Stage VI Glory, Level 160, Grade A, Class I Muridic, Expert, η .62.*

| Measure | His reading | What it means for him |
|---|---|---|
| Level | 160 | A reserve of about 295,000 EU. Forty Levels of room before his Band's gate. |
| Band | II, Awakened | Level 200 stays shut until he reaches Refraction, whose Catalyst is the first flicker of a Domain seed. |
| Stage | VI, Glory ("lit") | His Sub-Stats stop at 400. One Stage over a Splintering rival, his presence bends her attention; lamp flames lean toward him. Glory puts his ember-orange where any civilian can see it. |
| Tier of Standing | 5, Expert | The same rank on his token as the Stage V disciple he will fight this afternoon. |
| Grade, Sub-Stats | Flux 352 (A), Fortitude 240 (B), Celerity 210 (B), Perception 120 (C) | He hits as A, takes a blow as B, moves as B, and notices things as C. |
| Grade, Primary | Ardency mean 260 (B) | The register calls his Ardency B. His fist does not. |
| Aether Class | Hammerhand, Muridic tier (I) | The class his soul offered him at Glory, for a life spent hitting things, and it has leaned his growth into the right arm ever since. His Shell leaks a third of every spend. |
| Crystal | Harmonic, Refined | Sound, steady, and a long way from a Domain. |
| Path | Body, Mastered | The full force bonus at contact, and he has to get there first. |
| η | 0.62 | His workings arrive hot and loud. Opponents hear him coming and can count what he has left. |
| Skills | Brawling B, Showmanship A, Footwork D | He can hit, he can sell a hit, and he should look at his feet more often. |
| Trait level | The Forge Holds, Level 34 | His Primary Trait, well worn on one side. |

That is the view from outside. Garrick, standing in the same coil, sees this, and nobody else in the room does:

```
[Garrick Vane · Human · Level 160 · Stage VI, Glory · Path: Body]
[Class: Hammerhand · Muridic]
[Vitality C · Ardency B · Gnosis E · Dexterity D]
[Harmonics F · Resilience E · Tempering E · Dominion E]
[Reserve: 100% ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓]
[Trait: The Forge Holds, Level 34]
[Skills: Showmanship A · Brawling B · Footwork D]
[Blessing: none]
```

No EU, no η and no Flux until he uses it. Those Primary letters are means, and Stat Points are few: a man at Level 160 has spent about 4,200 of them across all sixty-four Sub-Stats, half on Ardency, so nearly everything he has never had reason to use sits low, and only the few entries his life has leaned on reach the letters in the table above. The coil knows what his window leaves out, his window knows what the coil leaves out, and a Measurewright will explain over a long dinner why neither is the true account.

The coil's line is accurate and, by itself, useless. What Garrick is in a fight lives in the gap between his Flux and his Fortitude, and in the roar every working makes: an A-Grade fist on a B-Grade body, loud enough to be timed. *[[The Magic System/First Steps/One Exchange, Annotated|One Exchange, Annotated]]* shows what a lower-Stage fighter does with exactly that reading.

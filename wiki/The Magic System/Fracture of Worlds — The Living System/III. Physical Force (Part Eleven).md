# III. Physical Force (Part Eleven)

*The Continuum does not distinguish between the fist and the meteor. It measures the pressure that arrives, and it measures what survives.*

Two women took the Hwangsan Gate in one afternoon, and the Southern houses are still fighting about which of them did it. The Seo women, an aunt and her niece, hired out as a pair, Hye-rin with a sword and Bo-ra with her fists, in matching persimmon coats, the same letter against both their names on the Guild roll: A-Grade in the strike. Bo-ra went first. She set her heel in a cart rut, her aura flared the orange of her coat from the shoulder down, and she punched the curtain wall.

Forty paces of masonry came down in a wave of yellow dust with the garrison still standing on it, and a Measurewright watching from the siege line got a mouthful of it. Hye-rin strolled through the dust, told her niece she had missed a bit, and drew one line through the iron portcullis and the oak doors behind it, so clean that the halves stood a moment longer and the gate captain was still shouting orders when the top half slid. The minstrels gave the day to the sword. The sutlers in the camp paid on the fist. Bo-ra's arm had come back with the wall's answer in it, the wrist and both bones of the forearm, a month in splints, because every push is pushed back and Essence only pays part of that bill. And the Measurewright, spitting grit, worked the whole thing out in charcoal on a wagon board and announced that the two blows had carried the same energy within a rounding error, whereupon both women tried to hit him.

That wagon board is this page.

## What Physical Force Is

Physical Force is the umbrella measurement for every form of mechanically expressed destructive, resistive, or manipulative output a character can produce through bodily action, Essence-reinforced technique, or Aetheric projection converted into kinetic effect. Five facets: **Strike Force**, destructive energy delivered per attack; **Durability**, capacity to absorb, deflect or endure incoming energy without structural failure; **Lifting Strength**, sustained force applied against gravitational or structural resistance; **Range**, the spatial envelope across which force remains coherent; and **Aura Pressure**, the passive force a character's presence exerts on their environment.

Physical Force is not separate from Aetheric power. It is its most immediate translation. Every soul operates under the **Aether-Physics Fusion Rule**: magical combat must obey physical law and Aetheric law simultaneously. When a practitioner punches through a castle wall, the wall breaks because kinetic energy concentrated in the fist exceeded the tensile and compressive strength of the masonry. That the energy came from Essence compression rather than muscle contraction does not change the physics of the impact. The Continuum measures the energy that arrived. It does not ask where the energy came from.

What *does* differentiate is delivery mechanism. A punch delivers force through physical contact across a finite impact area. A Sage Art (a working fed by the Wellspring Aether pooled in the ground underfoot) technique delivers force through Aetheric projection across a volumetric zone. A Domain-grade expression delivers force as a localized alteration to the laws governing material coherence. The energy equivalence may be identical. The tactical application is not.

### The Dual-Law Requirement

Every expression of Physical Force must satisfy two criteria at once.

**Physically coherent.** The energy must have a source, a vector, a point of application, and a measurable yield. Mass, force, inertia and leverage apply even when the source is Essence rather than chemistry. A punch delivering three giganewtons still generates an equal and opposite reaction on the attacker's skeletal and muscular structure, absorbed by Essence-reinforced bone density and Vitality-governed tendon architecture. A thrown boulder still obeys projectile kinematics.

**Aetherically coherent.** The Soul Crystal must structurally support the output. A character whose Ardency Sub-Stats have not reached the Tier Grade required for a given yield experiences Overchannel (forcing more output than the Crystal can safely sustain) events, Backlash, or outright technique collapse when attempting it. The body may be capable of the motion. The Crystal may not be capable of the cost.

The benchmarks below therefore represent the *structurally sustainable ceiling*. Characters can exceed them through Overchannel or crisis, and an Overchannel counts toward the two-Grade boost cap like any other self-boost. The cost is Crystal Fracture risk, Residual Strain, and potential permanent Sub-Stat degradation.

## Strike Force

Strike Force is the destructive energy delivered per discrete attack action, measured in **Newtons** for peak contact force at the moment of impact and **Joules** for total energy yield. Both matter. A sword thrust concentrates low total energy into an extremely small contact area, producing high local pressure and deep penetration. A body slam distributes enormous total energy across a broad contact zone, producing catastrophic structural damage without penetration. The Continuum records both.

One ton of TNT is equivalent to 4.184 × 10⁹ Joules. TNT equivalence describes *destructive capacity*, not that every attack produces an explosion. A sword strike with City Block-level energy yield cuts. It does not detonate. What it *could* destroy and what it *does* destroy are questions of delivery, not capacity.

**Reading the table.** These are ceilings at the *upper* boundary of each Grade. A character at stat value 176 does not produce 5 GN. They produce roughly 1 GN. The range within each Grade follows a non-linear curve that steepens as the value approaches the Grade ceiling, reflecting the densification of Essence architecture near the next Temperance threshold.

**The Somatic-Aetheric Split.** At Hollow and F, Strike Force is predominantly somatic. By E-Grade the ratio is roughly sixty percent Aetheric, forty percent somatic. At C-Grade and above the somatic contribution is structurally negligible: the body is the delivery frame, the Essence is the engine. By S-Grade the body has been so thoroughly rebuilt by Essence saturation that the distinction between muscle and Aether ceases to be anatomically meaningful. The practitioner's bicep does not contract harder. Their Soul Crystal compresses Essence into the limb's contact zone at densities that produce giganewton-scale force as a byproduct of Aetheric physics.

**The Focused-Yield Principle.** Strike Force measures the yield as delivered, not the theoretical yield of the Essence expended. An A-Grade practitioner with Ardency Compression at S-Grade can focus their yield into a contact area smaller than a coin, producing local pressures orders of magnitude above their overall energy tier. This is why an A-Grade swordsman bisects materials an A-Grade blunt strike only cracks. The energy is identical. The pressure distribution is not.

## Lifting Strength

Lifting Strength is a continuous output metric: how much mass can be held motionless against gravity, or how much structural resistance overcome through sustained application rather than impact.

Governed primarily by **Vitality**, particularly Fortitude and Constitution, with secondary contribution from **Tempering Yield**, which determines how efficiently the body converts Essence expenditure into sustained mechanical output. Body Path practitioners receive a multiplicative bonus at all Grades because their Essence architecture routes through the Physical Plane first, where gravitational interaction is native rather than translated.

**Gravitational Sovereignty.** At S-Grade and above, Lifting Strength stops being mechanical. An S-Grade Body Path practitioner does not lift a mountain by gripping it and pushing. They compress Essence into a counter-gravitational lattice beneath the mass, redistributing load vectors across their Aetheric architecture until the mountain's gravitational potential energy is opposed by metaphysical structural authority rather than muscular force. Conventional leverage mechanics, which require a fulcrum, a moment arm and a contact surface, cease to apply above roughly ten million tonnes.

**The Binding Strength Corollary.** Lifting Strength also governs resistance to metaphysical displacement: gravity wells, telekinetic pulls, spatial compression, dimensional anchoring, soul-yank techniques. A character whose ceiling is 500,000 tonnes cannot be forcibly displaced by any effect applying less than the gravitational equivalent, roughly 4.9 × 10⁹ Newtons sustained. High-Vitality Body Path practitioners are functionally immune to knockback from lower-Tier opponents. They are not resisting the technique. Their passive Essence architecture is heavier than the technique's displacement budget. All of that concerns where the body ends up. What a Spirit working does to the soul inside it is not on this table.

## Durability

Durability is the total destructive energy a character can absorb, deflect, distribute or endure without structural failure of body, Aether Shell or Soul Crystal. It is a composite of four layered systems.

**Layer One: Physical Hardness.** Raw material resistance of the body: bone density, muscle fiber tensile strength, skin cohesion, organ integrity as enhanced by Essence saturation. The last line of defense. Governed by Vitality, particularly Constitution, Fortitude and Regeneration.

**Layer Two: Aether Shell Shielding.** The active Essence field surrounding every woken practitioner, buffering between incoming force and the body, worn as a coat of the practitioner's own aura. Density governed by Resilience, particularly Integrity, Hardening and Fortification. Held evenly, the Shell absorbs and redistributes incoming energy across its entire surface before residual force reaches the body, and a Shell rated at 5 TJ absorbs 5 TJ across all concurrent attacks before anything reaches flesh. It can be thickened where a blow is expected, at the price of leaving the rest thin, and reading where it went is half of reading the fighter. It is held, not owned: asleep, drugged or taken by surprise, a practitioner keeps the passive hardness of Layer One and loses this layer entirely.

**Layer Three: Technique-Active Defenses.** Wards, barriers, Domain-grade exclusion fields actively maintained or deployed. Not included in baseline benchmarks because they are technique-dependent and Essence-costly. A Fixatio-anchored (anchored on Fixatio, the Wellspring of binding) barrier with a 50 TJ ceiling adds that on top of baseline, but costs Essence to maintain and can be shattered independently.

**Layer Four: Domain Resistance.** From Stage VIII, practitioners with active Domains gain a layer derived from Domain coherence. Within their own Domain, local law favors their survival. Not quantified in Joules, because it does not absorb energy. It degrades the coherence of incoming attacks, reducing effective yield before it reaches the Shell.

An aside on Layer Two, because it is the most famous art in the world and the most quarreled over. Every culture learned it, and none will use another's word. On a Guild roll it is Aether Shell Shielding, and in most mouths it is the coat. The Moto say *matoi*, the wearing, and hold that a coat anyone can see is badly worn. A Kharven rider calls it the hide, and thinks the Moto are showing off by hiding it. The Mahuo say *gabot*, armor, and teach it before they let a student touch a sword. The lineage halls say *hùtǐ*, guarding the body, and keep a commentary older than any living elder on how thick is vulgar. The figures below apply to all of them, which no school has ever been pleased to hear.

**Why a bolt still kills.** The ceilings above read the whole stack with the coat up and the blow seen. Take the coat away, by sleep, by drugging or by surprise, and Layer One stands alone, and Layer One is no line on this table. It turns a fist, a thrown stone and a glancing edge, more surely the higher the Vitality, and it buys time from a bad hit, since saturated flesh closes and saturated bone holds. But a point concentrates what it carries. A steel-bowed arbalest's bolt of about 440 J, stopped inside five centimeters, pushes about 8,800 newtons, and on a square millimeter of point that is near nine gigapascals, well past any bone. So a bolt nobody saw coming goes into a high-Stage fighter as it goes into anyone, and the throat, the eye and the roof of the mouth have the least to say about it. A bolt seen is read by Speed Grade and meets the coat.

**The Asymmetry Principle.** Durability ceilings are deliberately lower than Strike Force ceilings at every Grade. This is architecture, not oversight. A world where every character could perfectly tank every attack from their own Tier would produce stagnation, endless exchanges with no consequence. The offset ensures a committed, well-placed strike from a peer produces meaningful damage if it lands cleanly. Technique, positioning, timing and tactical intelligence exist precisely because raw Durability is not sufficient to ignore raw Strike Force from a peer.

The ratio varies. At F through D, Durability sits at roughly seventy to eighty percent of Strike Force yield, so a clean peer hit penetrates but is not catastrophic. At A through S, the ratio narrows to sixty to seventy percent, reflecting denser Shell architecture but also more lethal focused technique. At SSS and above it widens again to roughly fifty percent, because the energy differential between a clean hit and survival is so vast that the Crystal's priority shifts from absorption to avoidance, Domain manipulation and reality-grade countermeasures.

## Range and Force Coherence

Every expression of force has a coherence envelope: the maximum distance from the body at which it retains full destructive yield. Beyond that, it degrades, at a rate set by delivery mechanism.

**Contact Force** has zero range beyond the point of contact. A punch delivers its full yield at the knuckle surface and nowhere else. Shockwave propagation can extend the effective damage radius, but shockwave energy degrades by the inverse square law, losing roughly seventy-five percent of yield per doubling of distance from impact.

**Projected Force** degrades according to the practitioner's Ardency Cascade Sub-Stat and the Wellspring used. A tightly compressed Coagulatio-based (shaped through Coagulatio, a Wellspring that gathers and densifies) force lance retains ninety percent of its yield across its full effective range. A broadly dispersed Pyreveil-based (shaped through Pyreveil, a fire Wellspring) detonation wave retains only forty percent of peak yield at the edge of its blast radius. Range benchmarks below are the maximum distance at which at least fifty percent of peak Strike Force is delivered.

## Range Without Force

Not every reach is a strike. A practitioner also has to **name** a thing before he can set a limit on it, **hold** a limit at a distance from his own body, and keep a volume of ground under **his own law** rather than somebody else's. Those three reaches are not the range above and are not governed by it. A working that expresses no force is not bound by the coherence envelope of one that does. They have their own ladder, and it keys to Grade the same way.

**Addressing range** is the greatest distance at which a practitioner can name a thing precisely enough to impose a boundary on it. **Anchor reach** is the greatest distance between the boundary he sets and the practitioner, or the ink, blood, voice or hand that anchors him. **Authority radius** is the radius inside which his own law is the operating law: a Domain's edge, a ward's jurisdiction, a Seal's writ.

The three are one field read at three thresholds, and the thresholds differ by a hundredfold each. A boundary must stand a hundredfold **above** the background to be imposed at all. The background itself is where a law stops being the operating one. A name is narrow and coherent, so it is read a hundredfold **beneath** the background, the way a faint tone is picked out of a loud room by somebody who knows which note to listen for. Field strength falls with the square of distance, so each hundredfold step in threshold is a tenfold step in radius. **Anchor reach, authority radius and addressing range stand as one to ten to a hundred.** The middle rung is the Passive Pressure Field of the table above, which is precisely the distance at which a practitioner's field still stands above ordinary ground.

| F | 0.2 m | 2 m | 20 m |
| E | 0.5 m | 5 m | 50 m |
| D | 1 m | 10 m | 100 m |

**Reading the table.** Hollow has no passive field and therefore no reach past contact. Continental and planetary presence are read as a continent's span and a planet's diameter. Above EX the figures stop here, as they stop above. Every entry is stated for ordinary Ambient ground and for a target that is not working.

**Ground changes all three together.** Each falls as the **square root** of the local Aetheric Density, against the reference of ordinary Ambient ground. That is about ten times further on Aether-thin ground, an eighteenth at Active Concentration (ground near a Wellspring site, thick with Aether), a thousandth at a Veil-Thin Nexus, and about a five-millionth inside a Wellspring Core (a site where a Wellspring's Aether pools, in common speech, mostly found at the heart of a dungeon). Residue still ringing counts in the same sum, which is why fought-over ground is short ground for hours afterwards. **The ground a practitioner would choose for output is the worst ground he could choose for reach**, and every veteran knows one of those two facts.

**A working target is ten times easier to name.** Anything standing a hundredfold above the background is addressed at ten times the range, and anything performing a working at all stands there. Suppression is the same fact run backwards, and it is paid for continuously rather than once.

**Below Refraction the anchor is not the body.** A working below Stage VII needs voice, hand, ink or blood, and anchor reach is measured from the anchor rather than from the man. This is the whole commercial case for a drawn circle and an inscribed wall. They move the origin to where the boundary is needed, and a good one is worth more than a Grade.

**The crossover.** Projected force range and addressing range cross at B-Grade. Below it a practitioner can name what he cannot hit. From B upward he can hit what he cannot name, and every doctrine of scouting, spotting and signal lives in that gap.

**The failure modes, which are not gentle.** Past addressing range a name does not single its target out. Two men are one address, and the working lands on both of them or on the wrong one. Past anchor reach the boundary sets and slips. Past authority radius nothing of the practitioner's is operating at all, and a Seal sworn on authority he does not hold there closes anyway, with the difference deducted from the speaker.

## Aura Pressure as Physical Force

Aura Pressure is the passive force a character's metaphysical presence exerts on everything in proximity. Not a technique. Not activated or deactivated. The ambient consequence of a Soul Crystal existing at a given density within a given Temperance Stage. It increases monotonically with Temperance advancement and Dominion, and cannot be reduced below baseline without active suppression.

**Between the woken it is read on the Stage gap, with Dominion deciding inside the gap.** It shows in the room first: smoke, frost, lamp flames, animals. Then it reaches the bodies. One Stage up bends attention. Two brings sweat and nausea, and the lower party's Aether Shell begins consuming Essence to resist the ambient load. Three or more drops people, and the Shell of anyone still standing takes passive structural damage simply by being present. A Band gap changes the weather.

**The unwoken take the rows below by Grade**, because they have no Stage to set against it.

At **F through D**, Aura Pressure is perceptual rather than physical. The unwoken feel dread, instinctive deference, mild sensory distortion. Psychological and spiritual, no measurable physical force.

At **C-Grade**, weight enters the room and measurable physical effects begin. Air density increases within the field. Loose objects vibrate. Surfaces crack under sustained exposure. The ground compresses. Effects scale with the gap between the practitioner's Grade and whatever is absorbing the pressure.

At **A-Grade and above** an unwoken body stops being able to stand under it. An A-Grade character standing within ten meters of a porter exerts gravitational-analogue pressure equivalent to several hundred kilograms of distributed weight. The porter's knees bend and their breathing compresses. At **SS-Grade** the effects on the ground become geological. Stone cracks. Water compresses. Air becomes optically dense. Footsteps leave indentations not from body mass but because Aura pressure exceeds the soil's load-bearing capacity.

Governed primarily by **Dominion**, through its Pressure Sub-Stat, with **Harmonics Projection** setting how far the field carries. Body Path Aura is physically heavier but spatially compact. Fate Path Aura is lighter per square meter but extends across far larger volumes. Spirit Path Aura is perceptually intense, targeting cognition and emotional stability rather than physical structure, and exerts less raw gravitational-analogue force.

An aura's look comes in layers: the Wellspring a soul has tempered on longest sets the base color, clouded and smoky at the low Stages and clearing as the Stage rises, any others add texture and sound, and a class or a big Trait adds a shape. Pressure is felt in the room first, in the lamps and the knees and the dog going under the table, and then the window reports it to the owner of the knees: `[Pressure: heavy, above your Stage]`. A stronger Gnosis Perception reads a weaker Resilience Ward. Everything else is an estimate said aloud, and people can hide their pressure or fake it, which has won more tavern brawls than it has lost and ended a few fakers very quickly.

Everyone knows this art and nobody agrees on its manners. A Guild examiner writes Pressure. A southern tavern says a man is leaning on the room, and a Kharven soldier calls it the low sky and thinks hiding it is a kind of lie. The Moto say *atsu* and consider leaking it indoors an insult to the host. Mahuo halls say *nureum*, the pressing-down, and the lineage halls *wēiyā*, which they will tell you is a matter of virtue and which feels the same on the knees. The Marrowkin enter it in the Tally as a load, and argue about who should pay for it.

## Path Modifiers to Physical Force

Path modifiers apply *after* Temperance and Tier are calculated. The magnitude widens with distance from the Physical Plane. Because they come after, they never move a Grade's letter and never count on the two-Grade boost cap, and the same goes for a Wellspring's lens.

**Body Path.** Direct multiplicative bonus to Strike Force, Durability and Lifting Strength, from **+5%** at early Temperance Stages to **+20%** at Stage XII and above. The shortest road and the most immediately reliable. Body Path practitioners at equivalent Grade and Stage consistently out-hit, out-tank and out-lift their counterparts by margins that widen with advancement. Trade-off: reduced bonuses to Range and Aura Pressure, so low down they must close distance to leverage the advantage. Those figures are the force table only. High on the Path a Body fighter commands matter by recognition and by law, and their reach grows as far as there is matter to read, still needing existing substance and paying in recoil through body and Crystal.

**Spirit Path.** Moderate bonus to Strike Force delivered through Aetheric projection, **+5% to +25%**, but no bonus and in some configurations a slight penalty to physically delivered Strike Force. They hit hardest at range, and projected force retains coherence across greater distances. Durability bonus negligible, replaced by enhanced precision in defensive technique deployment. They survive through correct response rather than raw absorption.

**Fate Path.** Smallest direct bonus to Strike Force and Durability, largest bonus to Range, Aura Pressure, and the interaction between Physical Force and Domain authority. Less raw contact damage than a Body Path counterpart at the same Grade, but projected force degrades less over distance, Aura extends across larger volumes, and their Domain amplifies all Physical Force expressions within its territory by a factor scaling with **Dominion Radius and Gravity**: **+10%** while the lower of the two stands below S-Grade, **+25%** at S, **+40%** at SS and above, inside the line only. At S-Grade and above the compound Domain-amplified advantage can exceed Body Path's raw output advantage, but only within their own Domain. Outside it, Body Path remains king. These Range, Aura and Domain figures are what a Fate fighter lives on from day to day. A real pull of Fate adds nothing to any figure here: a guard slips or a strap gives, and an ordinary cut at an ordinary Joule rating lands where it had no business landing, at a ruinous cost in reserve and often not at all. The far end of the Path, where the true Fate Arts live, works on what has to be so before a blow can land, and its gates are permissions rather than output, as [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Three Paths Routing, Recognition and the Gate|The Three Paths]] sets out.

**Two and three Paths.** These bonuses are written for a soul on one Path with a clean seat. A fighter on two Paths takes three-quarters of each Path's bonus, and a fighter on three takes half of each, for life: time closes the gap in the gates and never this one. So the Body Path's +20% at Stage XII is +15% on two Paths and +10% on three, and the rest of the table follows the same way, the Fate Domain's amplification included.

**What this table does not say.** This Part measures force, so the Body Path looks its best on it, the way a mason looks his best in a quarry. A Spirit working that goes past the Shell is never read against the Durability column, since armor and Durability do not cover the soul, and a Fate fighter who has spoiled the footing never has to stand in front of the Strike column at all. None of the three is the better road, and [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Three Paths Routing, Recognition and the Gate|The Three Paths]] sets out how each beats each.

## Force Interaction Rules

**Tier Grade Differential.** The single most decisive factor. One full Grade above an opponent in Strike Force versus Durability wins a direct exchange without meaningful contest; the energy differential is too large for technique or positioning to overcome. Two Grades above is one-sided destruction. Three Grades above means the lower-Grade character is not participating in a fight. They are experiencing an event.

**Within-Tier Resolution.** At the same Grade, exchanges resolve by technique quality, tactical intelligence, positioning and Sub-Stat architecture. A character at 275 produces roughly **five times** the peak force in newtons of a character at 176, and about **forty-four times** the energy in joules. Both are B-Grade. The differential is real and significant but not automatically decisive, because technique architecture, Wellspring affinity and Path bonuses can compress or invert the raw stat advantage.

**The Sharp-Blunt Asymmetry.** An F-Grade swordsman delivering 0.2 MN through an edge contacting roughly one square millimeter produces local pressure of **200 GPa**, exceeding the compressive strength of virtually all natural and most Essence-reinforced materials at that Tier. The same 0.2 MN through a fist contacting one hundred square centimeters produces **20 MPa**, enough to crack stone but not bisect steel. The Continuum tracks total energy for Tier comparison and local pressure for material interaction. The ratio is ten thousand to one at any force, which is why a B-Grade swordsman cuts what a B-Grade brawler can only dent, and why the brawler shatters structures the swordsman can only slice.

**Wellspring-Typed Damage.** Force delivered through Wellspring-attuned technique carries that Wellspring's signature. Pyreveil adds thermal energy. Fractura adds structural destabilization. Vohrin, a Titan's power worked at Wellspring level and no member of the Sixty, adds thermal extraction. These are not counted in the Strike Force benchmark because they are Wellspring-dependent, but they represent real additional damage that can push effective yield above the raw Joule rating. The Path decides how it arrives: the same Pyreveil burns on contact from a Body Path fist and comes thrown from a Spirit Path hand. A Spirit Wellspring adds no such energy on any Path, because it gives no fire and changes what the person can bear instead, and that shows on the Durability side of a fight if it shows here at all. A Fractura-enhanced strike targeting a pre-identified fault line in the target's Aether Shell can produce Shell failure at yields **thirty to fifty percent below** the Shell's rated ceiling, because the strike is applying force at the exact frequency and vector that maximizes structural degradation.

## Physical Force at the Boundaries

**The Apotheosis Threshold.** The Zenith rows in these tables sit beside the Grade ladder, not on it: each is keyed to a Stage, where every row above it is keyed to a stat range. At Zenith, Stage XIV, Physical Force is no longer assessed by conventional metrics. Revelation, Stage XV, keeps its EX+ Grade label, but its force goes unmeasured in the same way; the EX+ row marks where the lettered ladder ends, not a figure a Revelation practitioner is measured by. A punch from a Zenith-level practitioner does not deliver a quantifiable number of Joules. It delivers an event the local physics must accommodate. Conventional Joule ratings assume energy is a finite quantity interacting with matter by known law; Zenith-level force can locally alter those laws as a byproduct of delivery. The energy is real. The interaction is no longer standard.

**The Hollow Floor.** Hollow-Grade output is indistinguishable from ordinary mortal capacity, below sixty Joules, a normal human fist at under twelve and a half meters per second. The System tracks a soul here all the same: a sealed Crystal keeps a Level and a few trade skills on a small window, and only Essence and a class wait on the Crystal opening.

**Overchannel Spikes.** The benchmarks above are sustainable output. Overchannel can temporarily spike Strike Force and Lifting Strength by **two to five times** the sustainable ceiling, depending on the Ardency Overchannel Sub-Stat's Grade and the character's tolerance for Crystal Fracture risk. From the top of a Grade that lands inside the next Grade up on every row, so a window prints it as one Grade and it spends one of the two the cap allows. Every Overchannel event deposits structural damage that must be healed through rest, alchemical treatment or Temperance advancement. A character who regularly Overchannels accumulates fracture density that eventually becomes permanent. Some fractures become architectural features. Most become liabilities.

*Every Joule was earned. Every Newton has a name.*

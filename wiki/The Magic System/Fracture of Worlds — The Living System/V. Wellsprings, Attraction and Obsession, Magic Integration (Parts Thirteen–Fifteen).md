# V. Wellsprings, Attraction and Obsession, Magic Integration (Parts Thirteen–Fifteen)

The Cooper's Row Reds of Altherion (a fighting school with a song, a color and a grudge, like all of them) had been refused the Ember Stair three years running. The Ember Stair is a Pyreveil site high on the inner wall of the Ring, a Core in common speech, where Aether of that one type pools so thick that the rock sweats orange and snowmelt leaves it as steam. Its Marrowkin keepers close it to anyone in arrears, and the Reds were in arrears. In the spring of a bracket year their captain, Jennet Marl, walked up anyway, singing, with four hundred supporters in red scarves on the road behind her and a keeper on every landing reading her the sum. On the top step she planted her feet, opened her Soul Crystal to the ground and pulled.

Every scarf on the road lifted. Her aura, a respectable brick red for sixteen years, went to the gold-white of a crucible pour, and for the length of one long breath she stood with her hair gone and her face shining and the whole cliff behind her the color of a peach. She came down with Pyreveil harmonized in her Crystal for good and the keepers' invoice in her belt. A Measurewright who had climbed the hill for exactly this set his fork to her at the bottom and read every number the same, to the point, then told the crowd nothing had happened. She put a fist into a boulder beside him and it ran like tallow. The Reds still lost the bracket. She never felt cold again, and she could no longer hold a hand near a fire and know when to take it out, which cost her two fingers by the following winter and made her a great many songs, most of them wrong about the fingers.

### What a Wellspring Is

A Wellspring represents an alchemical and a spiritual transformation and concept. It can be seen as a source of power to pull from, and it is more the governing idea that transforms how a soul uses Aether: a lens on magic already potent. Captain Marl came down the Ember Stair with every number the same and a boulder running like tallow, and that is what a lens does. A soul bases its tempering on one, it shapes Essence differently on each Path (the layer of the Crystal a soul leans on, in Part Eighteen), and at its home Path it can even double its effect, starting small and reaching double only with years of mastery. There are sixty, fixed. Every few years some Measurewright announces a sixty-first, and he has generally retracted it by the next fair. The Titans are the Wellsprings themselves, and the Sixty are their manifestations, each one way a Titan's law shows up, carried by Aether. [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/The Sixty Wellsprings|The Sixty Wellsprings]] names the Titan on each entry.

A person gets one at a Core in a dungeon, from a school, from a Draft or a blessing, or by birth.

Aether (mana, to a foreigner) is the raw power in the world around us, what remains of Omnara undivided, and it comes in different types that affect the Soul Crystal differently, one to a Wellspring. A practitioner pulls Aether from the environment, refines it in the Soul Crystal into Essence and drives it out through the Shell, so where you stand matters: rich ground fills you faster and thin ground starves you, and the scholar's term for that richness is Aetheric Density. A Wellspring also has sites where its Aether pools, which common speech calls Cores (nothing to do with the Essence Core named below, a layer of the Crystal, as the Guild has been told). Cores are mostly found in dungeons, which grow down in floors around them, and they are said to lie in vast territories too. A worked Core is a dungeon someone cleared and now holds: it recharges and has keepers, and its keepers can close it, as Captain Marl could tell you. The Ember Stair was cleared so long ago that its floors have turned into landings.

The physics in these pages and every number below stay, as how the scholars explain it. Scholars have an account of everything and it may be wrong, and any Measurewright at the fair will be glad to tell you which of the others got it wrong.

### What Wellsprings Do to Stats

The Sixty Wellsprings are not power-ups. They are principles of becoming, metabolic dialects that alter how the Soul Crystal processes Essence, routes Aether and anchors reality. When a character harmonizes with a Wellspring, it permanently alters the behavior of specific Sub-Stats, not by adding flat numerical bonuses, but by changing the qualitative character of how those Sub-Stats express their value.

A character with Fortitude at B-Grade who harmonizes with Monolithion does not gain Fortitude points. Their Fortitude begins behaving like stone rather than like flesh. The number stays the same. What the number means changes.

Every Wellspring touches all three layers of the Crystal, rarely evenly. The **Essence Core** determines how the soul burns, heals or empties under that law. The **Aether Shell** determines how the law moves through and around the body. The **Attraction Layer** determines how reality, memory and people bind to the soul once the law has taken hold.

Most practitioners awaken three to five Wellsprings in a lifetime. Serious adepts may harmonize with twelve or more. Those rare beings who embody all Sixty are called **Principals of Temperance**.

**Wild** Wellsprings are physical phenomena and alchemical transformations a practitioner shapes their inner Essence around. **Spirit** Wellsprings are purely spiritual: their ground looks ordinary, they usually do little or nothing to the physical world, and they give a fighter no fire to throw, showing instead in what a person can bear, know, keep or let go. A spiritual entry below is the scholars' account of what happens in the soul. **Wellsprings of Fate** work on bonds, debts, names and odds, and on their ground luck goes strange, oaths bite, and strangers keep meeting. **Medium** Wellsprings are at home on all three.

### The Lens

A Wellspring is the lens a soul bases its tempering on, and it works in the terms of whichever layer the soul leans on. On the Body Path, by way of the Aether Shell, it changes what the body does and bears. On the Spirit Path, by way of the Essence Core, it changes what the working is and what it carries across a distance. On the Fate Path, by way of the Attraction Layer, it changes what the soul's bonds, oaths and claims will hold, and the color of whatever thread the soul manages to pull. A Spirit Wellspring on the Body Path still gives no fire: it changes what the body can bear.

**At home** a Wellspring harmonizes cleanly and can reach double its effect with years of mastery. **Away from home** it works at lower-quality resonance and higher Essence cost, and reaches half again at most. Part Eighteen sets out the Paths.

### Wellspring Stacking and Interference

Wellsprings whose laws run the same way compound their Sub-Stat modifications. Three Wellsprings of stone (Monolithion, Basilithe and Chthonica, say) produce a Vitality and Resilience profile noticeably denser than a character with identical raw values and no such harmonization. The numbers are identical on paper. The qualitative expression is not.

Mixed harmonization introduces resonance complexity. Neighboring laws, Monolithion and Fractura for instance, matter given form through force, produce complementary effects without interference. Contrary laws, which each entry in [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/The Sixty Wellsprings|The Sixty Wellsprings]] names, Monolithion and Nihiloth among them, form against the erosion of all shape, produce tension the Crystal must structurally resolve, either through Temperance advancement or through a **Compound Trait** (a Trait fusing two or more laws into one) that integrates the contradiction.

The maximum number of simultaneous harmonizations is governed by **Tempering Capacity**. Exceeding it produces resonance conflict that degrades all active harmonizations equally.

## Part Fourteen — Attraction Force and Obsession Force

*"To be seen is sacred. To be held against the law of your own soul is profanation."*

### The Plane of Fate

The Plane of Fate is the control of Attraction Force and Obsession Force and the workings of reality itself: manipulating probabilities to work outside what reality already knows, to control the fate or predestiny of something. Fate covers both forces below. It represents the possibility of all things, so it reaches the Spiritual and the Physical World alike, because all spirits and all physical things have fates. It can attach to oaths, and it is more tied to blessings. "Plane" is a courtesy. Physical and Spiritual Reality are the two lower realities, and Fate is no reality at all: it is everything that flows, every possibility, idea and concept, anything that can happen in those two, and it sits above them as the law and construction of the world. Most if not all of the Archons are Sovereign across all three planes, this one included, some more powerful than others, and Uurgath and Urion are unparalleled here because their ideas are inconceivable.

A soul's own share of this plane is its Attraction Layer, and a soul that leans on that layer is on the Fate Path. The two forces below are what the plane runs on, and they stay forces whoever is spending them. Most of the Path spends them the way they already run, in bonds, oaths, Range, Aura Pressure and Domain authority, and at its far end it changes how they are going to run.

People fight and wage wars with this, and it is the hardest thing they do. Understanding gets no purchase on it, because understanding is made of the thing it would be used against. A real pull of a thread moves what a blow, a wall or a march depends on, so that the result arrives without having been forced. It is rare, ruinously expensive and often fails, it moves only what already has a way of happening, and it is contested, Dominion's Fate Sub-Stat against the target's Resilience Persistence and Continuity. Moving a whole field is a life's masterwork at high Stage or a bloodline's art.

Real Fate Arts are rare and mostly by bloodline. Some high-Stage masters reach them late along the Fate Path, and most people touch Fate only from the outside, through a blessing or a Master Work Draft, if at all. They are considered incredibly overpowered, and the people who consider them so are mostly the ones who have met one.

### Blessings

A blessing grants a power out of a person's natural reach, even in Level. It need not go through Fate, though most do. Higher spirits, Archons, Epochs and people of higher Temperance give them, because they are spiritually stronger, and a great giver spends almost nothing doing it. Epochs and Archons act rarely, and when they do it shakes the world; lesser spirits and high-Stage people give most blessings.

A blessing shows on the sheet as its own line. Since the giver wrote that line, it can lie. A curse is often a blessing with terms you did not read, or a giver who does not care what you want. Those who hold Hataraki can take a blessing back or remove it, which is one of the reasons Hataraki is so dangerous. It takes two Works or two Moto, because one grip does one job.

```
[Blessing: Favor of the Tide-Mother]
[You will not drown.]
```

Every sailor who has had that line will tell you it is true. Read it again.

### Attraction Force, the Law of Recognition

Attraction Force is the third fundamental force in the framework, equal in structural importance to Aether and Essence. Where Aether gives a working physical form and Essence gives it identity, **Attraction Force gives it endurance**. It is the binding principle that allows one presence to acknowledge another and for that acknowledgment to persist as continuity. Through it, vows remain binding, relics remember their bearers, lineages preserve their echo, Domains remain stable, and spiritual relations survive time, death, distance and change.

**Harmonics.** Attraction Force is the lawful current beneath every Harmonics Sub-Stat. Attunement functions because it lets the soul recognize a Wellspring's frequency and hold that recognition honestly. Axis functions because it sustains the Spirit Bond between two Crystals. Synergy functions because it lets allied resonances synchronize without overwriting each other. Strong Attraction expression makes every Harmonics Sub-Stat qualitatively richer at every numerical value.

**Dominion.** Domain formation is, mechanically, an act of Attraction Force. The Attraction Layer claims territory on the Plane of Fate, and that claim reflects downward into physical and spiritual law. Sovereignty, Fate, Command and Throne are all direct expressions of how cleanly that claim translates into environmental authority.

**Resilience.** Strengthens Oath, so sworn bonds become load-bearing; Anchoring, so identity holds its shape and its continuity because it is witnessed; and Continuity, so the causal timeline resists editing once the Continuum has registered it as recognized.

**Tempering.** Improves Coherence, since alignment between stated Path and lived behavior is easier to maintain when the soul is honestly witnessed, and Maturity, since bloodline traits and inherited Geasa activate more cleanly when the ancestral recognition chain is intact.

**Combat applications.** Maintaining coherent technique across long distances from bonded allies. Inheriting and transmitting combat forms through covenant rather than instruction. Recognizing an opponent's true Essence beneath disguise or glamour. Stabilizing allies whose Essence is fraying by lending them legibility. Composing Domains centered on covenant and sanctuary. Carrying bonds past death in forms that remain clean and reciprocal. Its strength is durable mutuality. Its weakness is the weight of being honestly seen.

**Costs.** Essence becomes more legible to those who can read it, including those who would harm it. The Crystal carries the imprint of every covenant honored, and grief is the lawful tax on every covenant ended. Memory becomes weighted. Identity becomes accountable, defined in part by who has been allowed to witness it.

### Obsession Force, the Law of Possession

The parasitic inversion. Where Attraction recognizes, Obsession imprisons. Where Attraction binds through mutual resonance, Obsession seals through hunger, fixation and refusal to release. It is the Current of Refusal in its personal, soul-to-soul expression: the moment recognition rots into possession and remembrance becomes a cage.

**Harmonics corruption.** Collapses Breadth, leaving meaningful synchronization only with the object of obsession. Degrades Stability through emotional volatility around the fixation axis. Inverts Empathy into a one-directional parasitic channel: supernatural clarity on the object, blindness to all other emotional data. Attunement reports a frequency the practitioner has stopped actually holding.

**Dominion corruption.** Domains formed through Obsession do not feel open or sovereign. They feel airless. Memory repeats, distance shrinks unnaturally, every path bends back toward the fixation object. Sovereignty becomes Possession. Radius contracts rather than extends. The Domain folds inward around one meaning and refuses all exit.

**Resilience degradation.** Degrades Anchoring, so memory loses proportion and identity narrows to a single defining attachment, and Integrity, so the Crystal develops structural dependency on the fixation and removal of the object produces fracture events.

**Tempering degradation.** Degrades Coherence catastrophically; stated Path and lived behavior diverge around the axis of fixation. Maturity stalls because the soul is no longer integrating new experience. It is recycling one experience endlessly.

**Combat applications.** Locking onto one target with impossible persistence. Tethering souls or techniques to a chosen fixation. Ignoring pain through compulsive singularity. Forcing resonance overlap between self and enemy. Domains centered on possession and recursion. Bonds preserved past death in broken, predatory form. Its strength is terrifying focus. Its weakness is total collapse of perspective outside the fixation.

**Risks.** Essence coherence erodes. The Crystal narrows around one governing hunger. Other bonds weaken. Identity becomes recursive. At worst the user becomes an echo-organism of wanting, a living Essence Shard that never fully separated, still orbiting a source that is already gone. Where a World Spirit expands until others can live inside it, a soul claimed by Obsession Force contracts until nothing else can.

## Part Fifteen — Magic System Integration

*"Twenty-six categories, one underlying grammar."*

The stat system is the structural skeleton beneath every magic system in the four quarters. When a character casts a Sage Art, activates a Spirit Summon, inscribes a Parunic Draft or expresses a Hataraki Work, the mechanical interaction passes through the stat framework. The stats are the physical, spiritual and metaphysical architecture that determines whether a technique succeeds, fails, or destroys the caster.

### Sage Arts

Terrain-anchored Wellspring communion. A Sage does not cast spells in the conventional sense. They synchronize their Crystal with a location's ambient Wellspring activity and redirect it through their Aetheric architecture. What comes back depends on the kind of ground: Wild ground gives the Sage the phenomenon itself, Spirit ground gives no fire and any discharge there is the Sage's own Essence, and Fate ground works on bonds and odds and on nothing anyone can see.

**Primary:** Harmonics (Attunement, Synergy, Breadth) for synchronization; Gnosis (Perception, Analysis) for terrain reading; Dominion (Radius, Sense) for spatial extension.

**Secondary:** Ardency Depth governs pull depth. Tempering Capacity governs simultaneous harmonizations. Resilience Integrity governs how much ambient pressure can be absorbed without destabilization.

**Corruption vectors.** Verdantia Sages risk *Endless Spring*, inability to let anything end. Anima Spirare Sages risk *Radiant Fever*, life-force overdriven until the soul burns hollow. Tenebra Sages risk *Shadow Saturation*, inability to exist comfortably outside shadow. Manganthra Sages risk *Eruption Dependence*, inability to function without extreme environmental pressure. All manifest as degradation of Harmonics Stability, Tempering Coherence and Resilience Anchoring.

### Spirit Summoning Arts

Five-step sequence: Intent, Pattern, Essence Donation, Aether Formation, Attraction Anchoring.

**Primary:** Ardency (Flux, Density) governs the construct's output, structural weight and duration. Harmonics (Attunement, Breadth) governs inherited Wellspring alignment. Dominion (Radius, Gravity) governs operational independence and range.

**Secondary:** Gnosis Retention sets how many summon patterns can be held loaded. Tempering Yield sets how much Essence can be donated without depleting combat reserves. Dexterity Sequence sets command speed during engagement.

**Wellspring inheritance.** Summons inherit their creator's alignment. A summoner tempered on Monolithion produces anchored, durable, slow-to-dissolve constructs. A summoner tempered on Nihiloth produces absence-constructs, void-forms and negation agents. The spirit's behavior is a function of the summoner's Harmonics profile, not a separate system.

### Spirit Rune Arts

Aetheric effects encoded into Parunic inscription structures that persist independently of active concentration.

**Primary:** Gnosis Fluency governs inscription accuracy and glyph-chain complexity. Ardency (Density, Compression, Depth) governs durability, efficiency and alignment. Resilience (Ward, Hardening) governs protective rune capacity.

**Secondary:** Dexterity Finesse for physical precision of execution. Tempering Clarity for the cleanliness of Essence routed into the glyph. Dominion Gravity for persistence against environmental erosion.

Runes and tags, like chants, are skills and trades anyone with a woken Crystal can level, and they show as Grades on the sheet. Drafts are the one such trade a Crystal that never woke can pour.

### Hataraki, the Works of the Plane of Fate

Not spells. The labor reality performs through practitioners who have become structurally aligned with specific principles of cosmic maintenance. Hataraki are Fate Arts, rare and held by bloodline, and their holders can take back or remove a blessing, whoever gave it. Nobody grips Fate bare, which is why there is more than one of them: each Work below is a distinct inherited way of taking hold of one function, and the stats listed are what that grip leans on. A Work lowers the chance of failure and never the price, it reads at Thread Class, and a Moto does not get the Fate Path's Range and Pressure unless that Moto walks it.

**Zentai-sei (Totality)**: Dominion (Sovereignty, Throne), Tempering (Ceiling, Maturity), Resilience (Persistence, Continuity). Wholeness and cosmic maintenance. Doctrine that encompasses rather than excludes.

**Hametsu no Gō (Ruins)**: Ardency (Flux, Overchannel, Cascade), Resilience (Ward, Persistence), Dominion (Pressure, Command). Necessary collapse. Doctrine that dismantles what can no longer sustain itself.

**Unmei no Ori (Fate's Cage)**: Dominion (Fate, Command, Sovereignty), Harmonics (Attunement, Axis), Resilience (Oath, Continuity). Binding and consequence. Doctrine that enforces the debts reality owes itself.

**Shinsei Naru Hikari (Sacred Light)**: Harmonics (Attunement, Projection, Synergy), Gnosis (Perception, Analysis), Tempering (Clarity, Maturity). Illumination and purification. Doctrine that reveals truth as a structural force.

**Eien no Yami (Eternal Dark)**: Resilience (Persistence, Ward), Harmonics (Stability, Suppression), Dominion (Sovereignty, Sense). Negation and sovereign silence. Doctrine that erases rather than creates.

**Saisei no Wa (Cycle of Rebirth)**: Vitality (Regeneration, Constitution, Hemostasis), Tempering (Maturity, Yield), Harmonics (Synergy, Stability). Renewal. Doctrine that treats ending as the first motion of beginning.

**Chowa no Koe (Voice of Harmony)**: Harmonics (Breadth, Synergy, Empathy, Attunement), Dominion (Command, Sovereignty, Radius), Gnosis (Fluency, Cognition). Concordance. Doctrine that synchronizes rather than commands.

### Attraction and Obsession in Technique

Every technique is sustained at the deepest level by one or the other. **Clean techniques are sustained by recognition:** the Crystal recognizes the Wellspring's law, the Continuum recognizes the intent, and the working persists because all parties agree it should. **Corrupted techniques are sustained by refusal:** the Crystal clutches the Aether rather than harmonizing with it, the Continuum registers the working as unauthorized, and Obsession Force sustains it through sheer fixation. Such workings are structurally unstable, emotionally recursive, and produce escalating soul-cost with each use.

The system tracks the distinction through **Harmonics Attunement** against **Tempering Coherence**. High in both means the character is operating through Attraction Force. Attunement degrading while raw output stays high means Obsession Force, and the system will extract the cost.

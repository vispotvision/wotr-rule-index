## The Soul Crystal, and who wakes

Nearly everyone is born with a Soul Crystal (the organ in the soul that stores power and sends it out). In most people it sleeps for life. About one in ten wake it, nearly always as children, taught by a school, a house or a guild, or stirring early on their own. They are practitioners (people whose Crystal is awake and working). The rest are Class Ø (sealed and unwoken) and touch magic only through what they buy. A sealed Crystal can be woken, but rarely, and at great cost. *Read next: [[The Magic System/The Gate Who May Learn Magic|The Gate: Who May Learn Magic]].*

## Aether and Essence

Aether is everywhere, thick in some places and thin in others, the way air is. A practitioner breathes it in and turns it into their own usable power, called Essence, which fills their reserve (the store of Essence they carry). Every working spends reserve. Dense ground lets a practitioner put out more; thin ground, or a city district whose main (its Aether supply line) has been cut, less. The greatest concentrations are the Wellsprings: sixty places in the world where one law of physics, heat or motion or growth, runs as a living current a practitioner can draw from. A practitioner's Aether Class (how cleanly their power passes out of them) decides how much of what they spend arrives as effect. *Read next: [[The Magic System/The Core Vocabulary|The Core Vocabulary]].*

## Traits

A Trait (a standing law in the soul) forms through lived experience: something a person survives hardens into a rule the world accepts as true. Nobody can teach you one. Sleeping souls carry Traits too, as a knack: the fisherman the cold cannot touch. Waking arms them. The Primary Trait (the first and deepest) sets the direction for everything that follows. *Read next: [[The Magic System/The Trait System Law, Function and the Forge|The Trait System: Law, Function and the Forge]].*

## Techniques and the four crafts

A technique (a named, repeatable working with a stated cost) is what a practitioner actually does with a Trait. Taught techniques come from a school; derived techniques are found alone; a signature is one person's own art. Fighters shout a technique's name as it fires: the shout costs a beat (the moment it takes, in which an opponent can act) and buys a surge of power.

Every working (a deliberate act of magic) is written in Parun (the glyph grammar all magic uses) and set into a medium, and the medium names the craft. Spellcraft speaks it: it happens now, and the body pays. Runecraft cuts it into stone, steel or paper: it outlives its maker. Draftcraft pours it into a bottle: anyone can drink it, woken or not. Chantcraft sings it: it takes many voices and holds while they sing. The guilds rank cutting highest and call speakers mouths; the crowds cheer the fighters anyway. *Read next: [[The Magic System/The Four Crafts|The Four Crafts]].*

## One working, start to finish

Wen Talley's Primary Trait is heat: a forge-boy who once held a burning rafter off his brother and lived. From it he learned a technique, Hot Iron, which drives that law into whatever his hand strikes. In a tavern brawl he shouts it, and that is Spellcraft, spoken and paid for in the body. His fist glows the ember-orange of his aura; the man's jaw blisters where it lands. The punch costs a sliver of his reserve, a few seconds of everything he has, and the heat runs back up his own arm, so after four or five he cannot close the hand. That is magic in WOTR: a law from the soul, a learned shape, a craft that carries it, a price in reserve, and a limit the body feels.

## What it all costs

Reserve spent is gone until it is drawn back by rest, by the ground, at a Wellspring, or from a fight Draft. A card writes a working's drain as how long it could run from a full reserve. As the reserve falls, the body shows it: at half, the breath shortens and the aura dims; at a quarter, a tremor and a thinning colour; below a tenth the practitioner starves, the aura flickers and the Trait stutters. Pushing past the Crystal's safe ceiling cracks it, and cracks that build up become permanent. Beyond reserve there is money, the oath sworn at the gate, and, in a Draft bought from the wrong source, someone else's convictions mixed into your own. *Read next: [[The Magic System/Stacking and Spending|Stacking and Spending]], then [[The Magic System/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner|Counterplay: What Beats a Practitioner]].*

## Stages, Levels and Grades

Each practitioner has eight main stats (Vitality for the body's endurance, Ardency for raw output, Gnosis for perception, Dexterity for speed, Harmonics for drawing power, Resilience for withstanding it, Tempering for growth, Dominion for presence and authority). A Grade (a letter band on a stat's number: F, E, D, C, B, A, S and on to EX+) says how much force that stat delivers. When two people contest a thing, the Grade of the stat in use decides it. A one-Grade advantage wins that exchange; at two Grades the weaker side is overwhelmed; at three, their only aim is to survive. Inside the same Grade, skill, ground and the read decide.

Level counts how often the Crystal has grown denser, and rises with real danger and effort. A Stage (one of sixteen steps of the soul's refinement, from Murmuring to Apex) is a full remaking of the Crystal, and it caps how high every stat can climb. Levels climb inside a Stage; a Stage is crossed only when two things are true: the practitioner has met its minimum power, and has gone through its Catalyst (the particular experience that Stage demands). *Read next: [[The Magic System/The Sixteen Stages|The Sixteen Stages]], then [[The Magic System/Tier Grade, Bands & the Aether Shell|Tier Grade, Bands & the Aether Shell]].*

## How power grows

Practitioners train by breathing at Wellsprings or in dense Aether, by sparring and real fights, and by seclusion before a Threshold (the moment of remaking). Three roads lead to a Catalyst: crisis in a fight, seclusion, and tribulation, when a Wellspring's current tests the practitioner directly. A breakthrough is a visible transformation; a failed one leaves slag (a remaking that set wrong). *Read next: [[The Magic System/Training and Sects|Training and Sects]].*

## Reinforcement

A practitioner's stats live in their body as a passive base. Reinforcement (Essence driven through the body to push it further) adds an active layer for a cost in reserve. It shows as a coat of their own aura, thickest where they push. Gathering it into one fist leaves the rest of the body less protected. Asleep, drugged or taken by surprise, they keep the base and lose the coat. *Read next: [[The Magic System/Reinforcement|Reinforcement]].*

## Drafts, runes and tags

A Draft (a working poured into a substance and bottled) is how most of the world touches magic. Advancement Drafts can bring a soul up to a Stage's minimum and help it set cleanly, but no Draft has ever supplied a Catalyst. Fight Drafts are drunk mid-fight: each costs a beat, refills reserve, and leaves a crash. Each Draft is graded by the Stage it serves, and the price climbs about threefold a Stage. *Read next: [[The Magic System/The Ladder and the Draft — Temperance, Advancement and Essence Drafts|The Ladder and the Draft]].*

Cheap runed gear is everywhere; the great pieces are old and cannot be made again. Fighters carry prepared tags (short workings cut on paper, hide or foil, set off with a touch), and a few bond a weapon over years until it takes part in their release. Runes cut into a living body are a rare and dangerous art. *Read next: [[The Magic System/Runes and Tags|Runes and Tags]].*

## Auras and Pressure

Every practitioner's aura shows in their own colour: the hue of their Family (the branch of physics their power works: Caloria ember-orange for heat, Fulguria arc-blue for lightning, and six more) at the clarity of their Stage, brightening as they push. Pressure (the weight a stronger soul puts on a room) is felt first in the room, then in bodies: one Stage up bends attention, two brings sweat and nausea, three drops people where they stand. *Read next: [[The Magic System/The Color of Essence|The Color of Essence]].*

## Releases and Domains

A release (a practitioner's Trait let off its safety limit on purpose, in a shape their Crystal has learned to hold) is a full transformation: the body changes, the aura flares, a sound comes, and power rises. The First Release opens no earlier than Stage IV, the Second at VII, and the Third at IX. Every release runs on a clock and ends on its own when the reserve runs low. *Read next: [[The Magic System/Release Forms|Release Forms]].*

A Domain (a space where a practitioner's inner law outranks the world's) first appears as a fragile seed at Stage VII and stands as a true Domain from Stage VIII. The Second Release opens it and powers it. When two Domains or two Pressures meet, Dominion decides who holds the room, and a sharp read can flip it. *Read next: [[The Magic System/Tier Grade, Bands & the Aether Shell|Tier Grade, Bands & the Aether Shell]], Domains.*

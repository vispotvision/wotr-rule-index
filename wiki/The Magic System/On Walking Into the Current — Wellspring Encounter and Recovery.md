# On Walking Into the Current — Wellspring Encounter and Recovery

*A Field Register of Wellspring Encounter, Survival, and Recovery · Second Edition*
*Guild of Measurewrights, under the Hexagonal Oath, in concurrence with the Research & Archives Division and the Voyager-Era Survey Corps (Retired)*
> **The current was always running. The question was never whether you would find it. The question was whether you would notice before it found you.**
> — *Voyager-era survey maxim, attributed to the third Chainward expedition*
> **The Mechanism is written for practitioners who understand the system and are approaching concentrations deliberately. Most Wellspring encounters in unmapped territory are not deliberate. They are accidents.**
>
> The surveyor steps across a threshold they did not see, **and the first indication that they have entered a concentration is the concentration's effect on their body or their mind or both.**
>
> ***This register is written for the accident.***
> *A warning to the confident reader. Confidence is a Spirit-type early symptom. If you are reading this document and feel certain you would recognise the onset, you are describing exactly the state in which surveyors fail to recognise it.*

## The Onset Profiles

*What each encounter type does to the body in the first thirty seconds.*

### Wild-Type Onset

**The body knows first.** The feet report a surface that does not match what the eyes see. The weight of the pack shifts. A tool in the belt resonates at a frequency it should not carry. **Metal tastes wrong against the teeth.** The air pressure changes without altitude change. Sound arrives at the wrong speed, **or does not arrive at all from directions where it should.**
> **The Crystal is unaffected.** Gnosis reads normally. The Shell is stable. *This is the critical diagnostic: if your own internal architecture feels normal and the world feels wrong, the concentration is Wild-type.*
**Response time is generous.** Wild-type onset is perceptible before it is dangerous, **because the physical anomalies are legible to the untrained body.** *You do not need Gnosis training to notice that rocks are floating. You need functioning eyes and the willingness to believe what the eyes report.*
> **The common failure is rationalisation.** The surveyor sees an anomaly and explains it away, **because the human inclination when the ground behaves strangely is to assume the ground is normal and the perception is wrong.**
>
> ***In a Wild-type concentration the perception is correct and the ground is lying. Trust the body.***

### Spirit-Type Onset

**The Crystal knows first, and the Crystal does not report to the conscious mind at the speed the conscious mind requires.**
The first symptom is not perceptual. **It is physiological.** Thermal change in the hands or face with no external heat source. A sudden emotional shift — grief or elation or rage — **that does not connect to any stimulus you can identify.** An involuntary discharge, **a technique half-forming in the Shell without the practitioner having decided to cast it.** Nosebleed. The headache behind the eyes specific to Gnosis overload. **The taste of your own Essence in the back of the throat**, which most practitioners describe as metallic **and which experienced surveyors describe as the taste of the Wellspring's Family.**
> The landscape is unchanged. Trees are trees. Stone is stone. Sky is sky. **Nothing in the environment is wrong. The instruments read normal.**
> ***You are the instrument that is reading wrong — and you frequently will not recognise this, because the Crystal's activation feels, from the inside, like your own decision.***
> **This is the critical danger. A Spirit-type concentration does not attack. It authors.**
>
> The practitioner begins producing effects they believe are their own, **because the Crystal is the origin of all their effects and the Crystal is the thing being activated.**
>
> ***The fire does not fall from the sky. The fire comes from the practitioner's own Shell — and the practitioner, feeling the fire leave their body, concludes that they chose to produce it.***
**Response time is narrow.** The window between onset and commitment — the point at which the Crystal has been retuned enough that you can no longer distinguish your native architecture from the concentration's influence — **is measured in seconds at Active Concentration and is functionally zero at Veil-Thin and above.**
> *The surveyor who hesitates during a Spirit-type onset to confirm their reading is the surveyor who has already been retuned.**
**The common failure is self-trust.** *By the time they suspect the effects are not their own, the effects have been running long enough that the distinction is no longer available to them. The Crystal that would make the distinction is the Crystal that has been compromised.*

### Medium-Type Onset

**Both report simultaneously, and neither report is trustworthy.**
The stream is flowing the wrong direction **and** childhood memories are surfacing uninvited. The light is bending around a clearing **and** Gnosis is suddenly reading things it has never been able to read. The air pressure has dropped **and** the emotional state has shifted.
> **Both the environmental changes and the internal changes are real**, and you cannot determine which is causing which — **because both are being driven by one current that crosses the Veil threshold every cycle.**
>
> The stream flows wrong because the current is altering local physics. The memories surface because **the same current is activating the Crystal's Anamnesis architecture.**
>
> *Both are symptoms of one cause operating on two planes, and you have no instrument that reads both planes at once unless you are a Ketsumōgan bearer or a Transference-trained Bifold Perception specialist.*
**Response time is the worst of the three.** *The body says the world is wrong. The Crystal says the body is wrong. Both are correct.* **The surveyor inside a Medium-type concentration at Active density** has lost their reference frame, and restoring it requires an external observer standing outside the effect radius.
**The common failure is confusion mistaken for illness.** They sit down. They wait for the symptoms to pass. **The symptoms do not pass, because the symptoms are not illness. They are harmonisation in progress** — *and sitting down inside the concentration gives the current more time to complete the process.*

## The Exposure Curve

*What happens after the first thirty seconds. You have not withdrawn. You are still inside.*
**Active Concentration · involuntary harmonisation.** *See the Mechanism for compatible and incompatible outcomes.*
**Veil-Thin Nexus · planar bleed.**
- **Bifold Perception onset** — the Soul Plane perceived simultaneously with the Physical. **You have not been trained for this.** Objects appear as structure and soul-signature at once; people as body and Crystal at once. **The thinning is not selective**, and Gnosis is not built for the volume.
- **Involuntary Soul Drift** — your position on the Soul Plane dissociates from your position on the Physical. *Practitioners describe it as being in two places at once while knowing that both places are real.*
- **Echo Beast manifestation** — **not the concentration's product. The concentration's consequence.** *Unprocessed grief, rage and trauma given temporary autonomous form by the inter-planar conditions the concentration has created.*
**Wellspring Core · law replacement.**
> Everything inside the Core obeys the Wellspring's law, **including the surveyor** — and their previous architecture persists only as a structural memory **that the Core's law is not interested in consulting.**
>
> Every surveyor recovered from Core exposure describes it identically: **they did not feel like they were being changed. They felt like they had always been the thing the Core was making them.**
>
> ***The change was not experienced as change. It was experienced as truth. The recovery, paradoxically, is experienced as loss.***

## Recovery Protocols

**Wild-type** · **Withdraw. The body is reliable.** Walk in the direction that feels most normal; anomalies decrease with distance from the core. When the ground behaves correctly and the tools stop resonating, you are out. **Then run a standard Wellspring inventory** — if a new harmonisation has appeared, it was acquired during exposure.
**Spirit-type** · **Withdrawal requires external assistance.** *The Crystal that would tell you which direction is safe is the Crystal that has been retuned. A Spirit-exposed surveyor self-navigating is navigating with a compass that has been re-magnetised.*
> The external observer talks the exposed surveyor out by voice, **confirming each step against observable reality.**
>
> With no observer available: **move in the direction that feels least comfortable** — because the concentration produces comfort toward the core and discomfort toward the exit. *This is not reliable. It is better than the alternative, which is staying.*
**Post-exit assessment requires a Gnosis specialist.** *The self-report cannot be trusted, because the Crystal that would produce the self-report has been altered.* An external reader performs a full inventory and **compares against the pre-deployment baseline.**
**Medium-type** · **Treat as Spirit-type until proven otherwise.** The unique complication is **memory contamination**: Phreatis or Anamnesis content can introduce memory material the surveyor experiences as their own. *Post-exit they may report memories of events that did not happen to them, or fail to report events that did.* **Disentangling requires a Mnemata specialist** and is frequently incomplete.
**Core** · Transference-assisted re-anchoring by a specialist outside the radius. Requires Stage IX or above; **no field surveyor carries the training.**
> What the field surveyor needs to know: **it works. It is not fast.** The surveyor who comes back will describe it as **losing something they valued.** *What they valued was the Core's law, which felt, while they were inside it, more true than their own identity.* They will mourn the law. This is normal. It is also temporary, provided the re-anchoring was performed competently.

## The Unawakened in the Field

*Everything above assumes the reader has a Crystal. Most people standing inside a concentration do not.*
A survey party is a practitioner or two and eight other people. Porters, guides, mule-handlers, the cook. Settlements sit downwind of concentrations for generations without anyone in them ever being screened. **A current does not check for a Crystal before it acts,** and the register has been written for a decade as though it did.
**A Class Ø soul has no Shell to phase-lock, so no harmonisation occurs.** This is the whole of the good news. The Essence Core is present, furnished, and fully exposed.
**Wild-type.** Identical in every respect, and the unawakened are frequently better at it. The body is the instrument, the instrument is unimpaired, and a porter has no Gnosis reading to argue with when the ground starts lying. Experienced survey leads have known for years that the man who says *this is wrong* first is usually the one carrying the tent poles, and the published curriculum does not mention it.
**Spirit-type.** The onset is physiological and it arrives on schedule. Thermal change in the hands, an emotional shift with no stimulus behind it, nosebleed, the taste in the back of the throat. **What does not arrive is the involuntary discharge**, because there is no Shell to express through.
> This is worse rather than better, and in one specific way. A practitioner in a Spirit-type onset vents. The load leaves through the Shell as a half-formed working, badly, expensively, and it leaves.
>
> **An unawakened soul has no exit.** The current drives the Core and the Core has nowhere to put what it is being given. The load stays, and it goes on staying after the party has withdrawn and made camp and gone home.
>
> *The recorded presentation is a porter who was cheerful on the outbound leg and is not cheerful again, ever. It reads as ordinary hardship and it is filed as ordinary hardship.*
**Medium-type.** Fully applicable and fully undocumented. Anamnesis and Phreatis content acts on the Core, and the Core is what an unawakened soul has. Memory contamination in an unawakened bearer is indistinguishable from confabulation to every physician who will ever examine them.
**Long exposure.** Residence at Active Concentration or above, sustained over seasons rather than hours, produces a **Trait by Wellspring Convergence.** It is real, it is lawful, it is heritable, and it is in a body nobody will screen. Villages downwind of a concentration produce a family that has been strange for six generations, and the strangeness is a Wellspring's law running quietly in people who have no word for any of this.
**Recovery.** There is none written. Transference-assisted re-anchoring assumes a Shell to anchor. **No protocol exists for an unawakened soul returned from a Core, and the Guild has never commissioned one,** because the recovery ledger records practitioners and the practitioners are the ones the Guild is liable for.
> **The hole in Protocol IV.** Pre-deployment baselines are taken for team members with Crystals. Nobody has ever taken one for a porter.
>
> Which means that for the entire Voyager Era there has been no instrument, anywhere, capable of establishing what a survey did to the people carrying it. *The absence is not a conspiracy. It is a filing category that was never opened, and it has been doing the work of a conspiracy for a hundred and forty years.*

## Standing Field Protocols

| # | Protocol |
|---|---|
| **I** | **Assume Spirit-type until proven otherwise.** *The cost of treating a Wild site as Spirit is unnecessary caution. The cost of treating a Spirit site as Wild is a surveyor whose Crystal has been forcibly rewritten before they identified the perimeter* |
| **II** | **Monitor the surveyor, not the landscape.** *A surveyor trained to watch the ground will miss Spirit-type and Medium-type onset. A surveyor trained to watch their own body will not* |
| **III** | **Deploy in pairs with one member outside the estimated effect radius.** The interior surveyor reports what they experience. The observer confirms or contradicts. ***The contradiction is the diagnosis*** |
| **IV** | **Carry pre-deployment Crystal baselines for every team member.** *Without a baseline the assessor cannot distinguish native architecture from the concentration's additions.* **Taken before entering unmapped territory, not after the first day's march** |
| **V** | **Do not carry Object Wellsprings into Site Wellsprings.** *A surveyor wearing a forged ward and stepping into an Active Concentration is asking two Wellspring laws to share a Shell that was built for one* |
| **VI** | **Withdrawal is not retreat.** Marking the perimeter from safe distance **completes the survey.** *The Guild has buried enough surveyors who decided that the interior documentation could not wait* |
| **VII** | **Report Medium-type sites as priority.** *A surveyor who identifies one and reports location, density and dominant Family has done more for the survey than a surveyor who maps the interior of a Wild-type site that was going to be found anyway* |
| **VIII** | **Watch the unawakened members of the party.** *They will not report onset, because they have no vocabulary for it and no reason to think it applies to them.* **A porter who stops talking is a reading.** Baseline them before departure by any means available, even if it is only a note on how they were |

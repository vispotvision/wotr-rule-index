*At Sallow Bridge, Dunstan Pike spent his whole reserve on the first swing, in glorious orange, in front of the betting crowd, and was then beaten by a man eating a pear. These pages are how power is put into the body, stacked, spent, carried in tags and beaten, so that it need not be you.*

- [[The Magic System/Power in Use/Reinforcement|Reinforcement]]: The coat of one's own aura over the body's base, what it costs, and when it fails.
- [[The Magic System/Power in Use/Stacking and Spending|Stacking and Spending]]: How boosts stack to the cap, how the reserve runs down, how the body tells on it and how the window reports it.
- [[The Magic System/Power in Use/Runes and Tags|Runes and Tags]]: Workings cut into objects ahead of time: who fuels them, who fires them, who cuts them.
- [[The Magic System/Power in Use/The Four Crafts|The Working Trades]]: Drafts, runes, tags, chants and the spoken arts: what each medium can do that the others cannot, what outlives its maker, and what the guilds fight about.
- [[The Magic System/Power in Use/Counterplay What Beats a Practitioner|Counterplay: What Beats a Practitioner]]: What beats a practitioner, and in what order the decision is made.

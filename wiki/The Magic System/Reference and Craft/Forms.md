# Forms

Big Maud Skerritt sold herring on the Skerry quay, and she threw them, flat and hard, at sixty paces, at customers who were slow with the coin. A herring from Maud would crack a tankard in a man's hand and leave a silver bruise that smelled for a week. When a Guild assessor came to ask which hall's token she carried, she knocked his hat into the harbor, and the Accord convened a public hearing to establish what, in law, she was doing. Eight Measurewrights (the Guild's sworn measurers) sat on it. The bookies of Skerry opened the book before the benches were full: Vectra at two to one, Arts at three, Manifestus at twelve for the romantics, and forty to one on Mechanica, the forbidden one, which the fish-merchants' guild was paying somebody to argue for. Four days, three fistfights among the bench, one Measurewright in tears, and a demonstration that took the presiding examiner's coif off on the fifth herring.

They entered her under Arts, with a dissent. Vectra's backers rioted in a small way. Maud paid forty silver in fees, in herring, and raised her prices.

I have told that story to every apprentice I have ever had, because a table of this kind looks like a law of nature and it was written by people like those eight. The register they were fighting over had twenty-six headings. The trade has since folded them into the twelve below, and under these Maud is a Strike, with a dissent for Shape, and the bookies would still open. Most of what the old headings said was sound, so it has been kept here under the Form it belongs to, and the old names go on being used wherever they earn their keep. All of it has been fought over by someone with money riding on the answer.

Forms sort **workings**, the things a person does with power. They do not sort people or their trades. A chant, a rune, a tag and a Draft are all done by whoever has leveled the skill, under whatever guild will have them.

## The Five Lines

Every art answers five questions, and nothing else classifies it.

| Line | The question | Where it is written |
|---|---|---|
| **Path** | Which layer of the Crystal does it lean on? One [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Three Paths Routing, Recognition and the Gate|Path]], or a named pair. This picks the [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/Classes and the Three Ladders|ladder]] it reads, and a true pull always takes Thread on top | The art |
| **Lens** | Which [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/The Sixty Wellsprings|Wellspring]] (the governing idea it runs on), and of what kind | The art |
| **Form** | What are the hands doing? One of the twelve below | The art |
| **Gates** | Which Sub-Stats and Stages does it need open? | The art |
| **Source** | How did this person come by it: **Lived, Taught, Inherited, Bought** or **Blessed**? | The person's card, since the same art can be all five in one family |

Three of them, laid side by side:

| Art | Path | Lens | Form | Gates |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[The Disciplines/Named Arts/Rusashin — The Dust That Remembers What It Touched|Rusashin]] | Body | Coagulatio (Wild) | Shape | C-Grade Gnosis Analysis |
| A cut ward on a door | Spirit | Fixatio (Medium) | Write | A woken Crystal, and real harmonization with what is cut |
| A [[The Disciplines/Schools and Bloodline Arts/Hataraki — The Works of the Plane of Fate/Hataraki — The Works of the Plane of Fate|Work of Hataraki]] taking hold | Fate | None needed, since a grip does not depend on a lens | Pull | The Work's own grip, and Dominion Fate against the target's Persistence and Continuity |

## The Twelve

| Form | Home | What the hands do | The clerks' old words |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Strike** | Body | Put power into a blow, a block or a body in motion | Arts, Corporis |
| **Shape** | Body | Tell matter, force or heat what to do, by hand or at reach | Kinesia, Vectra |
| **Read** | Spirit | Find out what is there, what it was and where it leans | Manteia, Mnemata, Auguria, Aetherica |
| **Write** | Spirit | Set a sentence of law into something that outlasts the saying | Glyphica, Scriptia |
| **Rite** | Spirit | Do it slowly and in order, with others and with an offering | Ritus, Turgia, Transitus |
| **Make** | Spirit | Build a thing that acts apart from the maker | Animatria, Crystalia, the Spirit Forge |
| **Sing** | Spirit and Fate | Carry it in tone, from one soul across to another | Harmonia, Synergia, Concordia |
| **Brew** | Body and Spirit | Stabilize a working into a substance | Drafts, Alchemia, Conjunction |
| **Call** | Fate | Ask something that already exists to come | Vocatia |
| **Bind** | Fate | Hold a thing against its own nature, or hold yourself | Claustra, Votia |
| **Claim** | Fate | Say that this ground, or this absence, is yours | Domain Weaving, Silentia |
| **Pull** | Fate | Take hold of a thread and move what a result depends on | The Fate Arts |

**A home is a qualifier and never a lock.** It works the way a Wellspring's kind does. Any Path can take up any Form, and each will do it in its own layer's terms: a Body cutter's ward stops the bottle, a Spirit cutter's is taken for a wall, and a Fate cutter's sets a term on her own share of the odds. Away from home the work is clumsier and dearer than it looks in the manual, and the rung it reads is still whichever one the art's Path line picked. The same fighter routinely has two techniques under two Forms, and a bookie will quote you both.

Each Form below keeps what the old register knew about it.

### Strike

Structured, discipline-based styles blending movement, form, and Essence flow: controlled Flux (the flow of Essence through the body), martial shaping, embodied Aether expression. Strike is often a practitioner's *first* structured magic regardless of eventual Path, since the truth held in the Essence Core (the Crystal's inner layer) and the expression of the Aether Shell (its outer layer, which turns power into force) already speak the same language here. A clerk who says **Corporis** means the case where the body is the conduit and cannot be separated from the technique.

**You have seen it as:** Resonant Forms · [[The Disciplines/Named Arts/The Iron Bison — A Discipline of Internal Resonance|the Iron Bison]] · [[The Disciplines/Named Arts/Nadi — The Art of Doing More Than Once|Nadi]] · a herring at sixty paces.

### Shape

Direct control of a phenomenon in real time, the technique being the act. The old heading **Vectra** covered motion, direction, velocity and spatial gravitation, and it is still what trained mouths call anything that is fundamentally about *where force goes*. Shape climbs the Body ladder rung by rung: matter as an object, through contact, by recognition, by law, as a whole system.

**You have seen it as:** Gravity Folding · Aetherstep · Spatial Collapse · [[The Disciplines/Named Arts/Matla|the Material Force]].

### Read

Reading and sensing through a phenomenon. **Mnemata** reads backward: memorycraft and echo work, dream records, ancestral fields, temporal imprints and historical echo-law, the practiced discipline behind Lattice Memory and Crystalline Memory, extended outward to other souls' records. The Wellsprings of remembrance and depth (Anamnesis, Phreatis, Somnalis) are its most natural partners.

**Auguria** reads sideways, and it is the reading of outcome weight, causal load, and probability distribution: what is leaning, with what will happen left to happen. Mnemata reads backward into what a thing was; Auguria reads sideways into what a thing is being pulled toward. The answer must be received by a Core capable of holding it without resolving it into narrative, which is the failure mode: an Auguria reading that arrives already interpreted has been interpreted by the reader.

Auguria is the licit, open end of the Plane of Fate, because it only looks. Moving the weights it reads is a Pull, and it is another matter.

**Aetherica** is the old name for the measuring trade: Flux, Aether Units, Efficiency, Density, Coherence. A practitioner of it might never cast in their life, instead ensuring everyone else's workings are measured against the same scale. It is also the shelf that keeps the **window**, the soul reporting its numbers to its owner, unseen by anyone else: the window gives its bearer Grades, percentages, bars and levels, Aetherica holds the EU, AU/s and η behind them, and its scholars argue about who built the thing.

**You have seen it as:** Outcome Reading · Tide-Sense · the crow's sight (Yukari) · water-scrying practices with no register entry · Dreamfolding · Echo Archives · [[The Disciplines/Named Arts/Solution — The Mind That Solves Itself|Solution]].

### Write

**Glyphica** is the Parun-based language of law, the syntax by which the Continuum (the whole lawful order of reality) recognizes and authorizes transformation, explicit in sigils and scripts or implicit in internalized chains. It governs the **Four Glyph Roles**: Authorization, Boundary, Direction, Sealing. It is the Essence Core's intention made *legible*. A practitioner with sloppy internalized chains still casts, and what comes out is slightly different from what was intended: a quiet, accumulating error.

**Scriptia** is the clerk's word for cutting it into something. Every cut or prepared sentence files here. A rune goes on working in its material after the cutter has gone home, and a tag is prepared ahead, fueled per tag and fired only by a practitioner; both are skills anyone with a woken Crystal can level. The home is Spirit, and it is Fate only for writing on Fate itself, as a written blessing does. What a glyph is and what a fault in one does are on [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms|the Master Glyph Index]].

**You have seen it as:** Wordform Chains · runes and cut wards · tags · Parunic Script Weaving · [[The Disciplines/Schools and Bloodline Arts/The Spirit Rune Arts|the Spirit Rune Arts]].

### Rite

Formal rituals built on Wellspring harmonics, Realm laws, and Parunic syntax. A typical sequence: **Intention → Space → Circle → Offering → Invocation → Catalyst → Seal**. **Ritus** anchors are temporary and ceremonial. A rite is performed, not built, and the Essence Core's posture during the rite matters more than any glyph's placement. It is also **the ancestor of every field-capable discipline** that later internalized what the circle used to externalize. **Turgia** is the clerk's word for the kind that leaves a lasting change behind it.

**Transitus** is the old name for the crossing of a body, soul, or object between planes, and for the making and holding of the apertures through which crossing occurs. Shape moves a thing to somewhere else in the world. Transitus moves it out of the world it was in, across the [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/The Making of the World/The Veil|Veil]]'s inner fold. A crossing requires three things at once and fails if any is missing: a body that survives arrival, a Core that stays coherent through the transition, and a claim on the far side that has agreed to receive it. Uninvited crossings resolve as the receiving plane sees fit.

**You have seen it as:** Rite of Hollow Union · Pale Root Binding · Choir of Severance · Transference Crossing · Gate-Work · Veil-Thinning.

### Make

**Animatria** is the shaping and projection of consciousness into constructs, fields, or avatars. An Animatria construct is a small detached fragment of the caster's Essence Core given temporary independent operation, closer to a deliberately-shaped Essence Shard than a Domain. This is why Animatria fails completely in Silence Realms (regions where no lawful working can be authorized): the construct's continuity depends on a connection back to its source, which Harmonic Silence severs. The **Spirit Forge** does the same job in ore, bone or glyph-cut stone.

**Crystalia** is the engineering, forging, and refinement of Soul Crystals and related lattices, the practiced discipline behind the **Crystalia Constraint**: harmonization with three relevant Wellsprings, certified understanding of the subject's Path and Wellsprings, sufficient Temperance to survive exposure to another soul's Essence Core. Its *object* is the Core, and its *method* is physical: forging and tempering done with hands and instruments on a lattice that happens to also be a soul.

The discipline's hard limit is that **nucleation cannot be manufactured at the bench.** A Crystalia practitioner can widen conductivity, repair a fracture, and prepare a lattice to receive a melt cleanly. With hands and instruments they cannot supply a seed, because a true seed is a law the subject survived into existence and no instrument makes one. Everything the trade sells past that point is a **Draft under another name**, a seed out of a bottle, and the Accord's halls distinguish the two on paper and nowhere else.

A [[Materials, Alchemy & Trade/Alchemy/Master Works|Master Work]] is the far end of Make: a piece that does what its Grade and material should not be able to, made once at the end of a lifetime, always Unique or Mythic, and named.

**You have seen it as:** Eidolon Crafting · Spiritchain · Fractal Alignment · Core Rebinding · Tempered Latticework.

### Sing

Magic of tone, vibration, and resonant frequency. **Harmonia** sits on two Paths because tone originates in the Essence Core, what the soul *means*, but only becomes Harmonia once it travels outward to affect other Crystals, by way of the Attraction Layer, what the soul *reaches*. Mismatched Resonant Tone produces Echo Fracture (the failure state of tone-work). A chant files here, or under Write when the sentence matters more than the note, and a chant stops when the speaker does.

**Synergia** is emergent resonance from multiple souls, Wellsprings, or Domains interacting, birthing shared Temperance fields and collective intent. A Synergia field is several Soul Crystals briefly sharing one Essence Core, Shell, and Layer, which is why souls harmonized to the water Wellsprings anchor these fields most easily.

**Concordia** is the apex: aligning multiple Aether bodies, Wellsprings, and Domains into a single coherent field, Synergia and Harmonia scaled up and made *durable* rather than momentary. It requires both homes: Spirit-side precision to align many Essence Cores without flattening any, and Fate-side reach to hold that alignment as a lasting Domain rather than a passing chord. A successful Concordia working is functionally a Major Shared Realm (a Domain that has outlived its founders and become a place)'s founding moment, performed deliberately, and it is what people reach for to heal a damaged Wellspring vein or begin closing a Hollowing (a region's slow loss of the capacity for lawful working).

**You have seen it as:** Veilsong · Lunar Chorus · Frequency Collapse · Affinity Fields · Severance Choirs · Guild Choirs · Concord Domains · Nexus Formation · [[The Disciplines/Schools and Bloodline Arts/The Harmonic Arts|the Harmonic Arts]].

### Brew

Alchemical procedures channeling a Wellspring's process through Parun glyphs (signs in the root script of law). Drafts are formulaic: **Aether + Essence + Vessel + Glyphica = Reaction**. Though Drafts touch all three Soul Crystal layers, the decisive variable is how cleanly the Essence Core's intention survives the journey into the crucible, and hands alone can pour one, so a Crystal that never woke can level the skill. **Conjunction** is the bench operation that weds prepared laws, Essences or Traits in a vessel, and it requires strict compatibility: opposed laws need mediation glyphs, and failing that the Rupture Event follows. A clerk's **Conjunctia** means one practitioner's working on two lenses at once, which needs no heading of its own.

Brew is also where the money lives. **A Draft or a blessing can supply a Catalyst**, the change a Crystal needs before it passes a Threshold, so Stages can simply be bought; the great houses hoard the top Drafts for that reason. A monster's core renders into Essence Drafts used for tempering, with Creature-Sourced potency and the risk of Identity Contamination, and a Crystal cut from a dead practitioner makes the strongest Draft of all and the worst crime in every realm. A Fate Essence Draft is a Master Work, a piece no ordinary bench can make: extremely hard, and very coveted.

**You have seen it as:** Coagulation · Distillation · Sublimation · monster-core tempering Drafts · Apotheosis Fusions · Absolution Weaves.

### Call

Invocation by recognition and correspondence: Titans, Archons (the fourteen great powers that embody cosmic law), World Spirits, lesser entities, Realms. **Vocatia** never dominates. It negotiates shared resonance, the practice-form of **Invocation and Consent**. A Vocatia working addressed to an unwilling higher being is, at the level the Continuum reads intention, an attempted Usurpation, regardless of correct surface syntax. Successful Vocatia is always an act of the **Current of Recognition**, never Refusal. Nothing is pulled down, which is why it can be learned in a yard at all.

**You have seen it as:** Titan Echo · Archon Descent · Concord Summons · [[The Disciplines/Schools and Bloodline Arts/The Spirit Summoning Arts/The Spirit Summoning Arts|the Spirit Summoning Arts]].

### Bind

**Claustra** is the binding, sealing, and lawful imprisonment of a thing against its own nature: souls, entities, Wellspring processes, Phenomena mid-event, and other practitioners' workings. Where Write supplies the Sealing role as one of four, Claustra is the discipline of making that role carry the whole weight of a working indefinitely. The seal's *syntax* is Soul Plane precision while its *duration* is a Fate-side claim that must be renewed by something. A Claustra working that loses its anchor opens, all of it, at once. It is the home of the **Vow Clause**: a vow is a seal a practitioner sets on themselves, and it is the only Claustra working whose anchor is the bearer's own continued behavior.

**Votia** is the old name for workings whose operative currency is a declared and irrevocable price: an offering made before the effect, a restriction accepted permanently, a thing given up so that a law will answer more readily than the practitioner's Temperance should permit. What is being altered is the Essence Core's posture rather than its output. A Votia working makes a practitioner stronger by making them narrower, and the Continuum answers narrowness with power.

**The Vow Clause, stated mechanically.** A vow declares a permanent restriction and the Core adopts it as a standing law. **Tempering Coherence measures the alignment between a practitioner's stated law and their lived behavior**, and it is the structural prerequisite for Threshold eligibility. A vow kept is therefore a direct purchase of Coherence by conduct rather than by expenditure, which is why vows are common in every hall and camp, and commonest in the populations that cannot buy Drafts: soldiers, hedge-workers, the orders, the poor.

> *It is the cheapest advancement mechanism in the world and it is priced in the rest of your life.*

**A broken vow lands at once by its own terms, and repeated denial of the truth behind it makes a Shear Break.** The Core has been running a law the bearer has now denied, and the law does not negotiate its own withdrawal. This is why the orders treat a lapsed vow as a medical emergency rather than a disciplinary one, and why the disciplinary response follows only if the practitioner survives.

A vow is small Fate, a person binding their own probability, and anyone can make one. It spends the soul's own share and pulls nothing down. Spoken, it shows in the bearer's window with the bargain and its terms, and what it buys counts toward the two-Grade cap like any other boost of one's own. See [[The Magic System/Practice and Institution/Vows and Oaths|Vows and Oaths]].

**You have seen it as:** Dragon Binding · Null-Anchors · The Veil's standing closures · Oath-Seals · [[The Disciplines/Schools and Bloodline Arts/Asami and Gisei — The Two States of the Moto|Gisei]] · Sacrament and Oblation · Disclosure practices.

### Claim

**Domain Weaving** is the deliberate reshaping of local Continuum behavior so that a chosen law becomes easier to sustain within a defined space. The practitioner does not enforce the law directly; they alter what the region is willing to do cheaply. A [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/Domains|Domain]] is a standing negotiation with the Plane of Fate rather than an application of force. It persists exactly as long as the Attraction Layer keeps recognizing the claim, which is why a Domain fails all at once rather than fraying. Every Domain is a claim by the Layer, on every Path, and it runs at Thread Ø and pulls no thread. Anyone at Stage VIII can have one, their own concept made real, and money cannot buy it: a sect teaches it, or the fighter finds it. Concordia aligns many existing fields into one; Domain Weaving builds a single field from a single soul's claim.

**Silentia** is the removal of magical possibility from a region, a body, or a working. Counterspelling and shielding are other trades. This is the deliberate production of an absence in which lawful transformation cannot be authorized at all. Mechanica removes recognition and substitutes extraction. Silentia removes recognition and substitutes nothing, which is why Silence Realms sever Animatria constructs from their source and why a soul held inside one reports the sensation as loneliness rather than weakness. It is the counterplay Form: every other entry here describes something being done, and Silentia describes the conditions under which none of them work.

**You have seen it as:** Aspect Sovereignty · Standing Claim · Law-Bias Fields · Harmonic Silence · Null Zones · Aether Suppression · the Hollow's quieter effects · [[The Disciplines/Named Arts/Talos — The Bronze That Empties the Room|Talos]].

### Pull

A **pull** moves the weights Auguria reads, to work outside what reality already knows and to control the fate of a thing. At one end it is a Fate Path fighter's single lean on the odds, rare, ruinous and often a failure. At the far end it is a **Fate Art**, and that is rare, mostly by bloodline and considered incredibly overpowered. A pull comes down from above through the Veil's outer face, and it always takes [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/Classes and the Three Ladders|Thread Class]] on top of whatever else is being paid. It moves only what already has a way of happening. The Works of Hataraki are inherited grips on it, the edit types of [[The Disciplines/Schools and Bloodline Arts/The Ketsumyōgan — The Binding Fate Eye|the Ketsumyōgan]] are others, and a personal grip can be won, once.

**You have seen it as:** the rotten pile at Akagawa · Danshi's cut · an involuntary Fate-pull that took somebody somewhere they did not ask to go.

## Mechanica, the Crime

**Mechanica** is no Form, because it is any of them done as theft. It covers forbidden constructs bypassing Soul Crystals, Wellsprings, or Glyphic Syntax. Mechanica coerces transformation without lawful Wellspring participation, consuming the resonance around it and damaging the Continuum. Every Form involves recognition: of Wellsprings, Domains, other souls. Mechanica is what remains when recognition is removed and replaced with extraction: Conjunction's compatibility deleted, Glyphica's authorization skipped, Drafts performed without consent. Statute XXVI, **Mechanica Irreversibility**, exists because this is the one practice that leaves scars the Continuum doesn't forget. The long account is [[The Magic System/Practice and Institution/Mechanica Extraction Without Recognition|Mechanica: Extraction Without Recognition]].

**You have seen it as:** Soul Splicing · Aether Inversion · Trait Collapse Engines, and a very great many bad nights in the cellars of very respectable houses.

## What No Form Covers

Forms sort what somebody does. These are done by nobody in particular, or they are what the doing is done with.

**Phenomena** are large-scale anomalies where Aether law bends under overload: semi-autonomous, self-sustaining events marking major law stress, the **Singularity** of Statute XIV. Once triggered, Phenomena are no longer *cast*. They have outrun any Attraction Layer, which is why Counter-Phenomena are so dangerous: the trigger has an author, but the Phenomenon itself does not. Echo Collapse, Worldbirth, Aetherquake and Memory Storms are the ones that get into songs.

**Manifestus** is the old word for short-lived Wellspring emissions and flares triggered by high emotional density or sudden pressure, reflecting current Temperance and Essence posture. An involuntary Trait preview: **Overtone Bloom** given external form. The trigger is always a spike in the Core and the expression is a flare that briefly exists physically. Early-Temperance souls experience this far more, and it is what Trait Awakening *looks like from outside*. Maud's backers at twelve to one were arguing she could not help it.

**Traits** are standing laws carried in the soul's own material and expressed through the Crystal once there is a Crystal to express them: Primary, Secondary, Inherited, Passive and Compound Reflections.

**Traits are not encoded by Temperance.** Primary and Inherited Traits are woven into a soul's original Essence, the rest are forged by what the soul lives through, they run in the unwoken whose Crystal will never open, and awakening arms what was already there rather than granting anything new. What Temperance does is the reverse of encoding. **At a Threshold the Crystal melts, and Traits are the only structures that survive the heat, because a law the Continuum has accepted as reliable does not stop being reliable at temperature.** The new lattice cools around them. This is why Traits govern advancement rather than merely decorating it.

A Trait is held in the Core, and its most legible expression is almost always physical, and the expression is literal rather than figurative. A Trait biases the Aether Shell's conductivity permanently, and because the Shell is the Material-Plane layer the bias is laid down in tissue: mineral into bone, altered myelination, rebuilt vasculature, a resting core temperature off the population mean. **A body is a Trait record and a corpse is a better one.** So the Tempering that melts the Soul Crystal down and rebuilds each layer also leaves the body with its Traits written into it. A Trait can be leveled and refined like a skill. Its level rises with use, grows with overall Level and skill, and shows on its bearer's sheet; the law itself stays the law it was.

**The Wellsprings** are the Lens line and nobody's Form. **The Sixty are the nouns; everything else is a way of using them as verbs.** Temperance governs how much of each process a soul can bear, and a Wellspring defines what law and emotional grammar are available.

## The Guild's Naming Table

*A clerk's form has a box for this, and the clerk needs one word. The root is the Affinity (the phenomenon a person works inside a lens: fire, not Pyreveil, gives Pyromancy its root). The suffix says what the worker is doing with it, and it places them before they have finished introducing themselves. Nothing here is a law of the world, and three separate halls have tried to make it one.*

| Clerk's word | Suffix | Usually means the Form | On a guild roll |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Manteia** | -mancy | Read | Pyromancy (reading the fire), hydromancy |
| **Kinesia** | -kinesis | Shape | Ferrokinesis, Hylekinesis, Biokinesis, Kenokinesis |
| **Turgia** | -turgy | Rite, Make | Geoturgy, Chronoturgy (forbidden outright) |
| **Corporis** | -kinesis, of the body | Strike | Inertiokinesis, Dynamokinesis. The body is the conduit and cannot be separated from the technique |
| **Scriptia** | -graphy | Write | Pyrography, ward-graphy, anything a cutter wants to charge more for |
| **Alchemia** | -genesis, -synthesis | Brew | Pyrogenesis, Hemosynthesis |
| **Harmonia** | -phonia, -chord | Sing | Psychophonia, soul-chord |
| **Conjunctia** | compound roots | Any, with a second lens | Two lenses in one working, and the root runs the two together |
| **Synergia** | named by working | Any, with a second person | Several practitioners working as one, on bonds that have to be real |

Nobody has ever coined a suffix for Call, Bind, Claim or Pull that stuck. Those trades have other names, mostly unfriendly ones, and the people who do them have rarely filled in the form.

## The Filing Table

*Added over the objections of three of the people who fixed the headings. Everything below is either a way of doing it or something that was never done by anyone in particular.*

| The thing | Where it files | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **A chant** | Sing, or Write when the words carry it | Its condition is set in resonance, and it is no Parun unless Glyphica is present. A skill anyone with a woken Crystal can level |
| **A rune, a cut ward** | Write | A sentence in material, working on after the cutter leaves. A skill anyone with a woken Crystal can level |
| **A tag** | Write | A sentence prepared ahead, fueled per tag, fired only by a practitioner. A skill anyone with a woken Crystal can level |
| **A Draft** | Brew | A working stabilized into a substance. A skill anyone can level |
| **A petty working** (the noodle seller's knife, the lamp-warden's round) | By what it does, usually Strike or Write | A trade done with the hands for pay, so it costs money and someone can refuse |
| **A skill** | No Form | A thing a person can do, rising with use and shown as a Grade on the sheet. Skills belong to people and Forms sort workings |
| **A technique** | By what it does | A named thing done with a skill, showing no level and taking a bigger name when it jumps |
| **A Trait level** | No Form | The law's own growth, rising with use and with overall Level and skill |
| **A window** | Read, on the Aetherica shelf | The soul's report of its own measure, seen by its owner alone |
| **A Path** | No Form | The layer a Crystal has learned to lean on, recognized off how the person has lived. Nobody casts one |
| **A Class** | No Form | The shape a lived life has given the soul, offered at thresholds. A Path is the road, and the class is what the road has made of the walker |
| **A pull of a thread** | Pull | One result's prerequisite moved, at a ruinous price, by the hand of a soul that can be reached |
| **A blessing** | Pull where it goes through Fate, which most do; otherwise by what it grants | Power out of a person's natural reach, even in Level, from a giver spiritually stronger than they are: a higher spirit, an Archon, an Epoch, a person of higher Temperance. Call asks upward and a blessing comes down, and it shows on the sheet as a line that can lie, because the giver wrote it |
| **A curse** | As the blessing it often is | Same power, terms you did not read, or a giver who does not care what you want |
| **A monster's core** | Brew once rendered, Make on the slab | In a way the monster's Soul Crystal, and the lawful Crystal Draw of The Standing Index. Swallowed raw it gives everything at once, and most die or come out wrong |
| **A monster's climb** | No Form | Monsters gain Levels by killing and change form at their own thresholds, on the same Level and Grade scale as people, and are graded T1 to T9 |
| **A dungeon** | No Form, with a standing dissent for Claim | Grown down in floors around a Core, self-sustaining, and with no author anyone can name. It is alive and learning, and scholars argue whether it thinks, which is what the dissent is about |
| **A worked Core** | Sing, where its keepers hold it | A dungeon someone cleared and now holds |
| **A Master Work** | Make | A piece that does what its Grade and material should not be able to, made once at the end of a lifetime, always Unique or Mythic, and named |

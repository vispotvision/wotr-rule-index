# The Practitioner's Field Manual — The Forge

*Being a working register of what each kind of phenomenon can and cannot do, and the Forge by which Abilities and Techniques are built at speed*
> **Craft Companion, the Seventh Guide**
>
> ***Governs how magic is written. Does not govern what magic is.***
>
> *"Ask what he is permitted to do with the power he has, and what it takes out of him when he does. The size of it comes last."*

Oswin Crumb, baker of Karrowgate, Stage III (third on the soul's sixteen-step ladder), had a heat trade worth two copper an oven. He lit ovens. He had lit every oven on Dyers' Lane for twenty-three years with the same small, smoky orange push from the same two fingers, and on the morning the bailiff's men came about the arrears, three of them, in good boots, with his daughter's name on their paper, he put those two fingers against the first man's breastbone and did the same thing, with the same spoonful of Essence, all at once.

The shutters of Dyers' Lane left their hinges in order, flour hung in the street like a snowfall going sideways, and a tray of rolls was found on the church roof. The bailiff's men lay in the road with their eyebrows on and their boots shining and not one burn among them, and every one of them had drowned in his own lungs. Crumb himself never heard another word from anybody. A Measurewright (a sworn measurer of the Guild) walked in from the Assize before the dust had settled, ate one of the surviving rolls, and wrote on a slate, in a hand that got larger as she went: *Same fuel. Same Essence. Entirely different corpse.*

That slate is the Delivery Rate Law, below, and this manual is what the Guild's compilers did about it. A word to whoever opens it: everything here is about how a working gets onto the page and earns its place there. I have kept the arguments in, because they are better than the conclusions.

## The Two Layers

**Nothing in the Continuum (the lawful fabric of reality) became more complicated.** *A second layer accumulated on top of the first, the two were carried as one thing, and the second layer is accounting.*

| Layer | What it is |
|---|---|
| **The Generative Layer** | Concept → Affinity → Ability → Technique, seen through a Wellspring (the lens the soul has tempered on) and going out by a Path (the layer the Crystal leans on) in a [[The Magic System/Reference and Craft/Forms|Form]] (what the hands do), which between them give a working its character. **The entire engine of magic.** It answers **what a working does, how it does it, and what it costs.** *Every scene is written from here* |
| **The Accounting Layer** | Level, Band, Stage, Grade, Tier of Standing, Class (the name a soul is offered at its thresholds), Aether, Essence and Thread Class (the three ladders, each setting how much power survives the trip through its own layer), EU (Essence Units, the measure of a reserve), η (efficiency, the share of spent power that does its work). **This settles what a practitioner can actually do and who wins.** It answers **how much.** ***Load it before writing, never abandon it while writing, and put its numbers on the page only through a mouth, an instrument, a document, a private count or the bearer's own window; its names may stand in any sentence*** |

> **Both layers stay open. The generative layer decides what happens; the accounting layer decides whether it could have.**
>
> *Every outcome should trace to a specific row, and the author notes should say which. What the reader receives is behavior and physics, and the window right after it. What the numbers do is make sure the behavior was earned.*

## The Sheet, the Class and the Climb

*The accounting layer as it looks from inside the person carrying it.*

**The window.** The Soul Crystal is the soul and the Continuum measures it, so a window is the soul reporting its numbers to its owner, in their own tongue, unseen by anyone else. Write them often and in detail, fights included, for damage, skill gains and reserve. Each sits on the page as its own text block, following the blow it reports, and the prose around it stays body first. It shows Grades, percentages, bars and levels. EU, AU/s and η stay in the notes.

```
[Reserve: 41%  ████░░░░░░]
[Cracked rib, left side. Breath short.]
[Skill risen: Haggling, Grade D]
```

A called Release or Domain fills the window with its name and what changed, and a timer bar drains. Pressure is felt in the room first, and then the window reports it. At a table or on Discord, windows are plain text blocks, and a player's gains are offered as window lines they keep or change.

| On the sheet | How it moves | What to do with it |
|---|---|---|
| **Eight Primaries**, with Grades | Life spends most stat points by what the person does; a few are theirs to place at each Stage | This is what a normal look shows. **Ask what the life has been, and the spread follows** |
| **Sub-Stats** | Appear when used or when they move | The full set remains to study. Show one the moment it matters |
| **Level** | Danger overcome. Killing something stronger moves it more than a hundred weak kills | *Crews argue over who got the kill. Write the argument* |
| **Race and lineage** | Fixed, and on the line | They shape base stats, what comes easily, and which classes and Wellsprings suit |
| **Path** | Recognized off how the person has lived, never picked: Body, Spirit or Fate, each standing as Mastered, Walked (three parts in ten of their practice or more) or Studied | **Ask what they have spent the years on.** Two Paths take about two and a half times as long at three-quarters of the force bonus each, and leave real holes at times; three take about six times as long, past a mortal span, and the world looks down on anyone trying |
| **Class** | Offered at thresholds, fitting how the person has lived. Evolves by how it is lived, each time the leading ladder rises | Gives abilities and shapes growth. It has no ladder of its own. The first offer comes at Glory. **The one wild concept stays the person's own; no class hands it out** |
| **Aether, Essence and Thread Class** | One ladder to each layer of the Crystal, Ø to Ω, read from Glory: influence on the physical world, influence on spiritual reality, and how much of Fate a soul can pull down and hold | Each sets η for workings through its own layer, a read rung overriding the field average and a card's own figure governing its holder. Thread is the steep one, its numeral giving η as two rungs below the same numeral elsewhere. The lead Path's ladder climbs with Stage, a Walked second runs a rung behind until mastered, and a Studied one stops at II, or at I for Thread |
| **Skills**, as Grades | Rise with use | Drafts, runes, tags and chants are skills. Anyone can pour; the cutting, tags, chants and spoken workings want a woken Crystal. *A guild's title says how far the hall trusts the skill* |
| **Traits**, with levels | Rise with use, grow with overall Level and skill | The Four Reflections and their laws are untouched; a Trait can be leveled and refined like a skill |
| **Techniques** | Show no level. Take a bigger name when they jump | **A technique is a named thing done with a skill.** The old name stays in use |
| **Blessings** | Their own line, written by the giver | **The line can lie.** A curse is often a blessing with terms nobody read |
| **Vows and crew bonds** | A vow shows its bargain and terms; a real bond shows as a line, with the crew's bars | Earned in play, and a broken one shows at once |

**The climb and the purse.** Drafts and blessings can supply a Catalyst, and Stages can simply be bought, so the wealthy are stronger because they are wealthy. The great houses hoard the top Drafts; the poor rise by dungeon luck, guild patronage and blessings. A bought Stage opens the door and no more: a Release must still be found and mastered by fighting, and sects teach what money cannot buy, Releases, Domains, techniques, skills and the rites for their Wellspring. *When a character's Stage is higher than their fighting, that gap is a scene, and usually a funny one until it is not.*

**Reading anyone else.** A stronger Gnosis Perception reads a weaker Resilience Ward. Everything else is an estimate said aloud, and people can hide or fake it, which gives every fighter on the page a reason to lie about their numbers and every opponent a reason to test them.

## The Rule for Invention Under Fire

> **Abilities** are character architecture: a root phenomenon the Crystal (the Soul Crystal, the soul itself, which works magic) has internalized over years. *Cozbi is Antithesis. Krothar is Binding. Sodoku is Ruin.*
>
> **These are never invented inside a scene.** *If a fight appears to require a new Ability, the fight is wrong, or the scene stops and the Ability is pitched properly.*
>
> **Techniques** are moves: a specific repeatable application within an Ability already established, a named thing done with a skill. **These may be invented at speed, mid-scene, without stopping**, provided they are built through the Forge and logged afterward.
>
> ***Invent freely downward, never upward. A new Technique under a known Ability is craft. A new Ability under a known character is a canon decision wearing a technique's coat.***

## The Forge

**Before the first step, the concept.** A kit starts as one wild idea you can explain in a breath, soup, debt, doors, boredom, anything, so long as the character can push it somewhere terrifying. Character first, then the concept, and only then the phenomenon, the Wellspring and the glyph that explain it and the Path it goes out by; the Sixty and the Glyphs stay fixed and the idea finds its place among them. A major character starts with three to five named moves and adds one when a fight or scene earns it. An NPC may invent and name a new move in the middle of a fight, and it is canon on use. The Forge below takes that idea and makes it hold.

*Seven steps at the desk. Steps one through four are structural and cannot be skipped. Five through seven are what separate a technique from a special effect.*

| Step | What it demands |
|---|---|
| **1 · Phenomenon** | Name it **from what is actually happening, not from the feeling it evokes.** *A grief-working that rots a body is biology. A grief-working that removes the memory of a face is mind.* **This step is physics-first** and it will offend intuition roughly a third of the time. Follow it anyway |
| **2 · Verb** | **One** operation from that phenomenon's verb table. *A technique that uses three verbs is three techniques badly stapled together* |
| **3 · Rate** | **Push or shatter.** *This single decision determines what the corpse looks like and is the most underused instrument available* |
| **4 · Limitation** | State what it structurally **cannot** do **before** stating what it does. *A power invented before its constraint is a power looking for a constraint to justify it, and it reads bolted-on every time* |
| **5 · Cost** | From the Wheel, deliberately, checked against the character's existing category; a big art's price comes out of its own idea, on top of reserve and body tells. **A cast where everything costs blood is a cast with one idea** |
| **6 · Counterplay** | One sentence, **and it must be an answer a competent opponent could actually find.** *If none exists, the technique is not finished* |
| **7 · Stamp** | Glyph pair, primary Wellspring (the lens it comes through, in the scholars' account the type of Aether it pulls on), Path, Form, Gates, and the real phenomenon it obeys. **Thirty seconds. Never left unlinked** |

### The three-line version

*For use mid-scene, at speed, when a named character needs to do something in the next paragraph.*
> **VERB. RATE. PRICE.**
>
> He does **one operation** from his phenomenon, at a **stated speed**, and it takes **something the reader can see** out of him.
>
> *The stamp and the counterplay can be filled in at the end of the pass without damaging the prose. The price cannot be added retroactively, because a cost the reader never watched get exacted did not happen.*
>
> **The gate the Forge does not list, because it stands outside the steps.** A **Signature Technique** requires a matching Trait, and no amount of Stage, instruction or expenditure substitutes for one. The Trait is a structural prerequisite rather than an advantage: it is the law the working applies, and without it the technique has nothing to apply.
>
> *Which is the test for whether a signature has been invented honestly. Name the Trait it comes out of. If there isn't one, what has been built is a good technique belonging to nobody in particular.*

## The Road It Leaves By

*The stamp asks for a Path, and no other line on it changes the page so much.*

**A blow's character comes from Wellspring and Path together.** Every soul has the three layers, and a Path is the one its Crystal has learned to lean on, so ask which one a working goes out through before writing a word of it. A Wellspring is the lens a soul bases its tempering on, and it shapes Essence differently on each road: Pyreveil on the Body is heat living in the tissue, on the Spirit it is thrown and burns what is false in a thing before what is solid, and on Fate it is a claim that burns its way through. A Wellspring's kind names its home and never locks it. At home the lens can reach double after years of mastery, and away from home it costs more and reaches half again at most. **A Spirit Wellspring gives no fire on any road.** Write what the person can bear, know, keep or let go.

| Path | Write it as | The limits a fight must honor |
|---|---|---|
| **Body** | The literal understanding and manipulation of physical reality. Low down it closes and hits. High up, by recognition and then by law, its reach runs as far as there is matter to read: armor too heavy to lift, a blade rusting in the hand | Needs existing substance, has to close until very high, pays in recoil through bones and Crystal, and has no purchase on a soul |
| **Spirit** | Everything to do with spiritual reality: souls, spirits, the dead, what a thing means. Authored in the Core and thrown at range, past the Shell | Needs a soul or a meaning to work on, has a fragile body, and dies out of position |
| **Fate** | Day to day, Range, Aura Pressure, presence and bonds. A real pull moves what a blow, a wall or a march depends on, with a visible charge and a tell, and lands as the ordinary blow it turned into | Ruinously hard and slow to set, moves only what already has a way of happening, often fails, and loses a straight exchange |

**Fate is never casual.** A pull takes extreme effort at every Stage, it cannot be reasoned out or drilled in, and almost nobody can do it twice in a day, so a character who does has been written wrong or is about to pay for it. Moving a whole field is a life's masterwork or a bloodline's art, and the Works of Hataraki exist because nobody grips it bare.

**No road is the better one.** Body beats Spirit by getting there, and Spirit beats Body by going past the Shell. Body beats Fate with force and soundness, and Fate beats Body through what closing depends on. Spirit beats Fate by reaching the hand on the thread, and Fate beats Spirit because Spirit survives by being right. Stage, ground and preparation decide which of these holds on the day, and a kit that reads as simply the superior road is a kit with no counterplay. [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Three Paths Routing, Recognition and the Gate|The Three Paths]] has the full account.

## The Delivery Rate Law

*Ratified. It generalizes across every phenomenon a working can touch as one rule and resolves the recurring problem of every escalation reading as simply larger.*
> **Every working has a delivery rate. Rate decides whether the effect pushes or shatters. Magnitude does not.**
**The same Essence (the soul's own stuff of power), the same Wellspring, the same practitioner, delivered slow or delivered instantaneously, produce two entirely different injuries.** *This is the correct way to escalate a character without inflating a number, and it is available at every Stage from Murmuring upward.*

| Phenomenon | The law | What the difference is |
|---|---|---|
| **Heat** | Deflagration ↔ detonation | Subsonic **pushes, throws men, takes hair and eyebrows and leaves survivors.** Break the sound barrier and it stops being fire and becomes a pressure wave, *and a pressure wave leaves the skin alone and delaminates the man, lungs first. Same fuel. Same Essence. Entirely different corpse* |
| **Lightning** | Sustained arc ↔ single discharge | A held arc cuts and cauterizes and **can be walked away from.** One discharge dumps the differential in a millisecond, **and the body answers with interruption before it has time for injury**: *the heart is an electrical organ and it does not argue* |
| **Force** | Sustained load ↔ impulse | A slow load bends, buckles, collapses, **and everyone inside gets to watch it happen and possibly leave.** As impulse it **exceeds the material's ability to distribute stress and the material fails along its worst line, immediately, everywhere at once** |
| **Distance** | Gradual displacement ↔ instantaneous fold | Slowly through compressed distance: **nausea and a headache.** Folded instantaneously, **the shear at the boundary becomes the entire event**, *and shear does not care that the thing being sheared is a person* |
| **Matter** | Creep ↔ brittle fracture | Slow loading yields, sags, redistributes, **visibly failing the whole time.** Faster than it can redistribute and **it goes from intact to particulate with nothing in between and no warning to anyone standing under it** |
| **Biology** | Chronic ↔ acute | Slow is survivable, treatable, **often invisible for weeks, which makes it the assassin's road.** Acute overwhelms every compensatory mechanism at once: **the body skips gradual failure and goes in a cascade that takes four organs in nine minutes** |
| **Fluid** | Flood ↔ water hammer | At its own pace, **a slow disaster people escape by climbing.** Stop that column instantaneously and its momentum **bursts pipe, stone, and vessel walls**, *and the vessel wall in question is frequently an artery* |
| **Entropy, Void, Mind** | Erosion ↔ erasure | Erosion is experienced as ordinary decline: forgetfulness, bad luck, a joint that aches. *The difference: erosion leaves a man who knows something is wrong, and erasure leaves a man who does not* |

## The Cost Wheel

*Rotate deliberately across the cast and audit for collision.*
> **The governing test is unchanged and it is severe: if the cost never touches the character when they are not using the power, it is a resource limit wearing a cost's coat.**

| Category | Standing |
|---|---|
| **Physical toll** | Exhaustion, injury, aging, organ damage. **Reliable. Overused** |
| **Resource depletion** | **The weakest category available.** *Only a real cost if scarcity bites outside a fight* |
| **Corruption** | **WOTR's default for anything Wellspring-adjacent, which is precisely why it should not be the default here** |
| **Sanity** | A large lever. **Sparingly** |
| **Memory** | Trading recall for capability. **Underused across the whole cast, and it fits the Anima Spirare register exactly** |
| **Time** | Cast duration, cooldown, or lifespan spent |
| **Social and legal** | A hall that will not sign with you, Concord Seal, exile. ***Every guild on the Accord can refuse your coin and your contract, and that is doing almost no work*** |
| **Escalating dependency** | The addiction structure. **The best long-arc dread engine available**, *because the reader can see the bill coming and cannot stop it* |
| **Rigidity** | **The newest category and the one nobody else is using.** A technique repeated enough deepens the Trait it comes out of, and **the Rigidity Effect scales with how absolute a law is.** The practitioner keeps all his strength and becomes less able to contradict the thing he built his power on, in his life as well as in a fight. *The cost is paid in what he can no longer be talked out of, and it is visible at a table long before it is visible in a duel* |

## The Vow Clause

*Ratified. Lives in Votia; sealed as Claustra.*

**A practitioner may bind a technique under a stated condition, sworn into the Lattice or into their own Crystal. The narrower the condition and the heavier the forfeiture, the greater the output.**
> *"Against those who deserve it" buys nothing. "Only against the Zettari, and if I break it my Crystal shatters" is a vow.*
**Why it works.** The Core adopts the restriction as a standing law, and **Tempering Coherence measures the alignment between a practitioner's stated law and their lived behavior.** Coherence is the structural prerequisite for Threshold eligibility. A vow kept therefore buys Coherence with conduct instead of expenditure, which makes it the cheapest advancement mechanism in the world and the one that clusters among people who cannot afford Drafts. A vow is small Fate, a person binding their own probability, and anyone can make one: spoken, it shows in the window with the bargain and its terms, and what it buys counts toward the two-Grade cap. See [[The Magic System/Practice and Institution/Vows and Oaths|Vows and Oaths]].

**Why it kills.** A broken vow lands at once by its own terms and needs no witness. Repeated denial of the truth behind it makes a **Shear Break**. The Core has been running a law the bearer has now denied, and the law does not negotiate its own withdrawal. The orders treat a lapsed vow as a medical emergency and get to the disciplinary question later, if there is one.
> **The disclosure variant is the useful one.** Stating a technique's true mechanism aloud, in the target's hearing, before use, **surrenders every advantage of surprise and increases output accordingly.**
>
> ***This is the best structural justification for a villain's monologue ever devised and the Continuum should take it.***
**What the clause buys is an answer to power creep. A character becomes dramatically stronger by narrowing rather than escalating.**
> *A Stage XIV fighter has no use for a larger number. What would change him is a vow the reader can watch him keep.*

## The Two Tests Before Presenting

**The Collapse Test** · *If two techniques under one Ability share a Limitation, a Cost, and a Counterplay, they are one technique with two names. Collapse them.*

**The Name Test** · Does the name tell you something true about who is using it? **It may be the phenomenon itself, direct and unadorned**, *Ruin is Ruin, Gravity is Gravity*, or it may be one big shouted phrase, or a signature's title with its moves under it (Art: Move), in the fighter's own tongue with the meaning landing in the same beat. Anyone may reach for a word like World, God, Fate or Infinite: the room laughs until it works. Goofy names can be truly lethal on anyone whose card fits. **A name that could belong to any practitioner in the Continuum has failed.**

## What Each Phenomenon Cannot Do

*What each kind of work governs, and, more usefully, what it categorically cannot do. The lens changes how it feels and what it charges, and it does not lift these.*

| Phenomenon | What it cannot do | Signature cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Heat** *and its absence* | **Cannot create energy.** Every degree added is a degree taken from somewhere, **and the practitioner must name the source or become it.** Cannot sustain combustion without an oxidizer: *sealed rooms, deep water and the high thin air of the Reach are Caloria-hostile ground* | **Physical toll, sink-side.** *A thirty-year adept has hands that no longer sweat, corneas gone slightly opaque, and a resting body temperature nobody wants to shake hands with* |
| **Lightning** *the work of the differential* | **Cannot arc across a gap it has not prepared.** Useless against wood, ceramic, dry rope, ungirded stone, *which is why the counter-lightning kit is boiled leather and fired clay and always has been.* **Falls off with the square of distance,** so every Fulguria threat is a threat about closing range | **Sanity-adjacent, sensory.** The nervous system is the instrument, **and instruments detune.** *Early signs read as brilliance: he answers before you finish, he notices everything, he has not slept and does not think that is strange* |
| **Force** *and motion* | **Cannot create momentum.** Every push has an equal push on the practitioner, **and the only question is which of his joints receives it.** *Resonance requires knowing the frequency, and knowing it requires time the fight may not give* | **Physical toll, reaction-side, and it is skeletal.** *Shoulders that come out of socket in cold weather. A spine that has been used as a bracing member for twenty years.* **Corruption is gravitational isolation:** the early symptom is that people stop being able to stand near him comfortably and cannot say why |
| **Distance** *and position* | **Cannot fold to a place it has not measured**, *and the failure state for blind translocation is arriving somewhere occupied.* **Cannot move what it cannot fully bound,** so a partial working takes a partial man | **Time and proprioception.** *Long before terminal dissociation, the practitioner reaches for a cup and misses by a hand's width, and stops trusting stairs* |
| **Matter** *and substance* | **Cannot make matter. Conservation is absolute here and there are no exceptions**, *which is why a matter-worker on bare ice or open water is a man with opinions and no tools.* Loses every exchange decided in under two seconds and wins every one that lasts a week | **Escalating dependency, mineral.** Petrification compulsion, terminating in mineralized flesh. *It shows first in the nail beds and the whites of the eyes, the only corruption in the manual a physician can diagnose by looking* |
| **Biology** *and anatomy* | **Healing eats mass, and the mass comes from the patient, the caster, or something the caster brought**, *and a healer who will not say which is a healer who is lying.* **Cannot restore what has lost its pattern;** the dead stay dead, and the attempt to argue with that is Limina's crime, not this House's | **Memory and metabolic debt.** *A Vitalia healer at the end of a long campaign is a man who can name every wound he closed and not the wife he closed them to get home to* |
| **Fluid** *and pressure* | **Cannot act without a medium**: deep vaults, sealed reliquaries and the dry Undaar deeps are deliberately hostile ground. **Cannot hold a boundary indefinitely, because fluids want to mix and the working is always spending Essence to disagree** | **Dissolution of boundaries, and it is the eeriest corruption in the register.** *The tell is grammatical and should be written that way: an adept who has gone too far starts saying* we *about himself, without noticing, and gets irritated when corrected* |
| **Entropy, Void, Mind** | **Cannot create. Every working of this kind is subtractive and there are no exceptions**, *the single most useful clarifying sentence in the manual.* Cannot act on what has no pattern, **so raw undifferentiated matter is nearly immune and a rock is a better Limina shield than a ward** | **Existential erosion, and it is reflexive: the practitioner pays in the same currency he spends.** *A man who erases memory loses memory. A man who nulls loses the ability to feel Aether at all in a widening radius around his own skin* |

> **Standing prohibition.** *Chronokinesis is Forbidden beyond Stage X. Chronoturgy is Forbidden outright.*
>
> **The Accord's enforcement of both is a plot instrument that has barely been used.**

## The Working Trades, for Whoever Writes Them

*The workbench behind [[The Magic System/Power in Use/The Working Trades|The Working Trades]], which keeps the story and the trades themselves in the hall's voice.*

### The Design Problem

*A note from the compilers, standing outside the hall's own voice, on where the tradition was taken from. It stays here because every lever below hangs off it.*

**The casting model as it stands is correct and complete. It is also, in its center of gravity, Eastern.**

The five-step pipeline (Intent, Pattern, Essence Donation, Aether Formation, Attraction Anchoring; see [[The Disciplines/The Disciplines|The Disciplines]]) runs inside one practitioner, in real time, in a single continuous act of will. The most legible unit is **the Technique**: a named, repeatable thing a person does with their body and their reserve, at a stated cost. *Every load-bearing question the system asks is a question about one soul's interior.*
> **That is the cultivation shape. It is the core of the system and it stays the core, and it is not what Tolkien, Martin, Clarke or Le Guin are doing; the trades below build their made things around it.**

### What the Western tradition does instead

**Tolkien has made things where other books have spells.** The power lives in objects **made well by people who were what they were**: the Rings, the Phial, mithril, lembas, the rope that unties itself. *Galadriel's correction to Sam is the whole thesis: mortals use one word for the Enemy's deceits and for the craft of the Eldar, and the Elves do not have a word that separates art from enchantment at all.* Nobody in Middle-earth casts. They make, and the making holds.

**Martin has an economy of denial where other books have a system.** Rare, foreign, unreliable, morally contaminating. **Its price is paid in blood and in life, directly, with no reserve to spend first.** *The maesters have an institutional interest in insisting the subject is superstition, which is itself a plot engine, because they are wrong and they are also not entirely wrong.*

**Clarke gives the sharpest structural gift, and WOTR is already most of the way to it.** A scholarly tradition with a bibliography, a lost golden age, live controversies, and **a hard social distinction between the theoretical magician who reads and would never dream of performing, and the practical magician who performs and is therefore vulgar.**
> ***The academies and research halls under the Guild Accord are four-fifths of the way to being that institution and do not yet know it.***
>
> **And the deepest one: WOTR's own glyph system is already Western Renaissance occultism.**
>
> The Parun (the language of law every working is written in) lattice is **Agrippa's kamea method out of the Three Books, II.22, unaltered.** *That is the operative tradition of European ceremonial magic, lifted whole.*
>
> **Which means the Four Glyph Roles are already the Solomonic operation, in order, and nobody has noticed.**

| Glyph Role | Solomonic operation | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Authorization** | The conjuration and the citation of authority | Names by what right the operator acts |
| **Boundary** | The circle and the triangle of art | Fixes where the working may reach |
| **Direction** | The constraint | Compels a specific outcome |
| **Sealing** | The license to depart | Closes the operation and releases the power |

> **The four roles are the boundary operation, in order, and the order is not decorative.**
>
> A glyph creates nothing. A Wellspring (the governing idea, alchemical and spiritual at once, that transforms how a soul uses Aether, and in the scholars' account one of sixty types of Aether with its own physical law) was already at work. **Authorization** says by what right a limit is set, **Boundary** sets it, **Direction** states which way the law is to run across it, and **Sealing** takes the limit back down.
>
> *Which is why Sealing is the role that kills people.* Authorization, Boundary and Direction failing produce a working that goes wrong while the operator is still in the room. **Sealing failing produces a working that does not stop**, because the law goes on obeying itself after the operator's attention has left the room. Every burned workshop in the Accord's incident record is a Sealing failure and roughly a third of them are recorded as something else.
>
> ***Nobody needs to import the Western frame into WOTR. It only needs digging out.***

### The Five Levers

**I · Let the guilds feud over rank.** Real-time casting sits at the center by default, and the crowd adores it. **In the halls, the made object and the prepared substance sit above the live caster, and the man the crowd came to see is told so at the door.** *Nothing in the system backs either side. That is a feud, and feuds are free, and they change everything about how the world reads.*

**II · Contest the word.** **Nobody calls it magic.** Each trade carries an Accord name in liturgical Latin and a plain name in the common tongue, **and which one a character reaches for places them by class before they have finished the sentence.** *This is the Norman and Anglo-Saxon doublet English actually runs on, and it is Tolkien's method exactly.*

**III · Make the golden age real, lost, and officially denied.** **The best inscriptions in the world are all old. Some of them cannot be read. The Accord's published position is that this is not the case.**

**IV · Make the filing political.** The Forms, and every older sorting of magic before them, are **an Accord publication before they are a law of physics.** *Documents serve the people who publish them. Somebody's trade was reclassified as Mechanica on grounds that were political before they were metaphysical.*

**V · Move cost from the ledger to the page.** EU (Essence Units, the measure of a reserve), Flux Density (how densely Essence packs into the Crystal) and η (efficiency, the share of spent power that does its work) remain author-facing and unchanged, and even a person's own window (the soul reporting its numbers to its owner, unseen by anyone else) shows only Grades, percentages, bars and levels. **What a reader sees is the Cost Wheel: the years, the memory, the sanction, the debt, the man who knows what you bought.**

### Prose cues, trade by trade

> **The speaking.** The speaker pays inside the same scene, visibly. Nosebleed, tremor, the hand that will not close, the four days of sleeping badly afterward. *Somebody in the room reacts to the fact that a working was spoken aloud in company, because in some rooms that is an obscenity.* **Nobody polite says casting.**
>
> **The cutting.** Chisels, drift, tooling marks, the grain of the stone and where it fought the cut. Somebody's hands. The apprentice who holds the light. **A wall with three hands on it, two of them dead.** Weather on an inscription. *Somebody scraping a rune off a doorframe with a knife, badly, because the man who cut it is now an enemy.* This is where the awe and the old grief get their material, and where the Tolkien register lives without a word of it needing to be stated.
>
> **Standing rule for prose: the wall is older than the war.** Any standing inscription on the page should carry **a date, an author, or a visible repair**, *and the repair should be worse than the original.*
>
> **The pouring.** Price, always. Weight and volume. What the vessel is made of and why that matters. Sediment. A seal, intact or broken. **Somebody checking a seal.** An excise stamp. *The difference between what the label says and what is in the bottle, and the person who knows the difference and says nothing.*
>
> **The singing, reference points.** Finrod and Sauron singing at each other until one of them falls. Lúthien before the throne. *The monastic office sung eight times a day for six hundred years by men who were replaced one at a time.*
>
> **Which column a character speaks from is characterization before it is information.** Bodily speaking is not a separate art and should never be written as one.

### What This Changes on the Page

**The default working in a quiet scene should be a cut or a bottle, or some petty art done by hand for a fee.** A wall that holds. A bottle that was bought. A lamp-warden arguing about what you owe her. **The speaking owns the fight: it is what a fighter shouts when the blades are out, and what anyone reaches for when out of time or out of options.**

**Every named practitioner has skills, and usually one trade they are known for, and that is characterization.** *Sodoku speaks because Sodoku fights. Somebody in Marrowhold cuts. Somebody in the Amber Cities pours and keeps two sets of books.*

**Cost is visible outside the working.** The Engraver's hands. The Drafter's ledger. **The speaker who is still shaking at supper.**

**The ranking is contested in dialogue.** *A character who calls the pouring mere cookery is expressing a live guild position and is entitled to be wrong loudly.*

**Elegy attaches to one named thing that is gone.** An array under a particular gate that four generations have failed to reproduce, **and the last man who could read its third layer, who died in the winter.**

### Settled Positions

| Question | Settled position |
|---|---|
| **I · Law V** *settled as a gate* | **Law V, the Law of Material Anchor, is a Stage gate rather than a universal.** Below **Stage VII, Refraction**, a working requires voice, hand, ink or blood: an external anchor for the boundary. At Refraction the practitioner can hold the Crystal as an object of study, and the Glyph Roles compress into an internal chain that needs no material anchor at all. *This is why internalized speaking reads as a mark of rank rather than a technique, and why a man who casts silently in a room has told everyone present his Stage without saying a number* |
| **II · Law III** *struck* | **Law III is struck from the register.** It states that progression follows the fourteen Wellsprings. There are sixteen Stages and sixty Wellsprings and the two were never the same axis. *It survives in-world as an error with a history: the fourteen it counted were the Archons, and a copyist's conflation four hundred years old propagated into three generations of teaching texts before anyone checked. Older practitioners still teach it. They are wrong and they are extremely difficult to correct* |
| **III · The singing** | **It stands on its own terms**, the medium-home of Harmonia, and answers to no guild |
| **IV · Whose trade is best** | **A feud, and the feud varies by culture, downstream of material conditions rather than taste.** The Concord baseline ranks cutting first. **The Marrowkin rank it first and hardest**, since a hold is a permanent substrate and everything they own is inscribed. **Kharven ranks pouring first**: nothing in a camp that moves is permanent, a Draft travels in a belt, and the standing runework in the death-houses is imported work that no living Kharven hand can read, *a fact the seat does not raise and the Accord does not stop mentioning.* The speaking ranks highest in Kharven of anywhere in the Concord's reach, because a soldier is not embarrassed by an art that works now |
| **V · The lost golden age** | **The Accord is mistaken, and has become invested in the mistake**, which produces a worse institution than lying would. The recovery program is real, funded, sincere and structurally incapable of succeeding, because what was lost was a substrate condition that no longer obtains, and no quantity of technique gets it back. Four generations of honest men have failed at it and each has been promoted for the attempt. *Somebody in the research halls has worked this out. They have not published* |
| **VI · Scope of the doublet** | **The wider scope.** Every trade term in WOTR carries an Accord register name and a common-tongue name, and which one a character reaches for places them before they finish the sentence |
| **VII · Standing Inventory** | **Entered for Kharven.** Household object: **the doorframe**, and whether it has been cut. Oath: *by the hand that cut it.* Proverb, fixed wording: **"A cut wall keeps a promise longer than the man who made it."** Contempt terms carry over intact, a mouth, a cutter, a potboy, and *potboy* is the one that starts fights, because Kharven pours |

# Caloria — Thermodynamics

*The burn that clears ground for something new · filed by the scholars under thermodynamics: heat, and its absence, and the transfer between them*

The people of Pennyhithe will tell you about the Aldgate sisters whether you ask or not. Prudence Aldgate heated the public baths on Fish Lane with her bare hands for thirty years, a copper a tub, two if you wanted it hot enough to lift the grease, and she turned away anyone who owed her, the reeve's wife included, and once, gloriously, the reeve. Her sister Bess kept the family's grudges. On the night of the Herring Fair she walked down into the customs cellar under the tollhouse with a list of names and her whole body lit the white-orange of a smith's best heat, and the crowd up on the quay heard a flame-front turn into something else.

The tollhouse roof came down in the harbor with the tollkeeper's best hat still on its peg. The quay cheered, and the minstrels had a song by morning. The song leaves out that the shock came back up the cellar along its own path, with Bess standing at the origin of it, and that she has been deaf in both ears and short three fingers ever since. One Family of Aether heats Prudence's tubs and took Bess's fingers, and a Measurewright (a certified assessor of workings) who says so in a Pennyhithe tavern should expect to be asked which sister he is calling gentle.

> The Caloria Wellsprings all describe one thing seen from ten positions: **energy crossing a boundary.** Creation and destruction run on one law here, which is why the Family produces both the most restorative and the most annihilating Wellsprings in the register and cannot cleanly be called either.

**The idea** · Conviction, rupture and transformation. Its ten Wellsprings are ten ways a soul can be changed by burning, from the ember that never goes out to the blast that ended the tollhouse.

**The lens** · A soul tempered on one of these ten carries the burn in everything it does: a little at first, and after years of mastery at the lens's home Path up to double, so that the same fist at the same Grade lands with twice the rupture in it. Eight are Wild, at home in the Physical World, and two are Spirit, at home on the Soul Plane. Away from home half again is the most anyone has got out of one, at a higher Essence cost and with a rougher result, and the bath-house trade will tell you it shows in the water. The full account of the lens is on the [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings|hub]].

**Stat Affinity** · *Ardency (Flux, Overchannel, Depth, Cascade, Compression) · Vitality (Tolerance, Threshold, Constitution) · Tempering (Overflow, Yield, Clarity, Longevity)*

*Each Wellspring below opens on what it is. **The idea** is the concept it lays over a soul's Aether. **Kind** names its home plane, and never locks it: Wild for the Physical World, Spirit for the Soul Plane, Fate for the Plane of Fate, Medium for all three. **On the Body**, **On the Spirit** and **On Fate** say what the lens does to Essence on each Path, through the Aether Shell, the Essence Core and the Attraction Layer in turn. After those, **Spirit** is the transformation it works on the soul, one road of Temperance (the climb of a soul from Stage to Stage), and **Alchemy** the principle it stands for at the bench. Then comes how scholars explain it: Analogue and Mechanism give the physics of what that type of Aether does to a Soul Crystal, Stat Effect what shifts on the sheet, Glyph the forms the Index seats on it, and Failure how it goes wrong.*

## Pyreveil · *The Fire of Renewal*

**The idea** · Fire that renews. Whatever this lens touches it burns down so that something can stand up in its place, and the burning stays in the soul as strength where it would otherwise have left as smoke.

**Kind** · Wild

**On the Body** · The heat lives in the tissue, so the blow burns on contact and the body stops minding fire, having taken it in as a lodger.

**On the Spirit** · Thrown from the Core, it burns what is false in a thing before what is solid, so a forged seal will char on a letter whose paper stays cool.

**On Fate** · On its best day a claim under this lens can set light to what stands in its way, provided it was already dry: the rival's hired second with one foot out the door, the enemy's grain store under a season-old thatch, and, only at the end of a life spent learning how, a whole army's reasons for staying in the field. The heat has to leave somehow, and it leaves through whatever tissue of the holder's is nearest, which is why the people who have done this twice have so little skin to show for it.

**Spirit** · Temperance through burning; destruction reborn as creation.

**Alchemy** · The Sulfur principle at full heat: a fire that stays resident in the body it burns. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Enthalpy of combustion, and the conservation of energy across a reaction.

**Mechanism** · Burning does not remove energy from a system. It re-partitions it into forms the system can no longer hold in the same shape. Pyreveil retunes the Shell so Essence expenditure runs at higher reaction temperature with the released energy staying partly resident in the body rather than radiating away entirely. Output climbs, and the practitioner's own tissue stops registering thermal load as damage.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Ardency Flux, Ardency Depth, Vitality Tolerance. Output burns hotter. The body stops minding heat.

**Glyph** · None attested in the Index (see Discrepancies of Record on the hub).

**Failure** · Thermal debt. The energy that stayed resident has to leave eventually, and it leaves through whatever tissue is nearest when the working ends.

## Exuroth · *The Trial Flame*

**The idea** · The trial by fire. Power comes only through ordeal, plunged and reheated and plunged again, until the soul holds a hardness no gentle road could have given it.

**Kind** · Wild

**On the Body** · Every beating and every recovery sets the Shell harder than the last, so that a fighter's third bad season leaves a forearm that turns a blade and a threshold of pain their physician declines to test.

**On the Spirit** · A working must fail and be thrown again before it takes an edge, and an inscription that has cracked twice and been recut holds where a clean first draft would split.

**On Fate** · Its holders take the worst of a fight on purpose, because an oath nearly broken hardens, and one nearly broken three times can sometimes be pulled on to turn the fourth exchange, or to hold a company on the day an untried enemy line is first asked. It is paid for in near-deaths that were real, and cut the interval too fine and the oath splits like steel quenched too soon, with the company still inside it.

**Spirit** · Temperance through ordeal.

**Alchemy** · The assayer's trial: quench and temper, the bench's proof by ordeal. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Quench and temper. Martensitic hardening followed by controlled reheat.

**Mechanism** · A steel quenched from austenite is hard and useless, brittle to the point of shattering. Tempering restores toughness by permitting controlled diffusion. Exuroth runs the Crystal on that cycle: repeated thermal shock followed by controlled recovery, so the lattice arrives at a hardness the same material could never have reached by slow cooling.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Ardency Overchannel, Tempering Overflow, Vitality Threshold. Pain thresholds rise. The Crystal tolerates what would shatter lesser vessels.

**Glyph** · `[Ra]` Flame, `[Asa]` Creation, `[Ve]` Desire; attested on seven more.

**Failure** · Quench cracking. Cycle too fast without the tempering interval and the lattice arrives hard, brittle, and split along its own thermal gradients.

## Ignivale · *The Wellspring of the Spark*

**The idea** · The spark. The smallest beginning that goes on to pay for itself, so that the gap between wanting a thing and doing it closes to almost nothing.

**Kind** · Wild

**On the Body** · The gap between wanting the blow and throwing it all but closes, and the fist has left before the shoulder has told anyone.

**On the Spirit** · A speck of Essence is enough to start a projection that then pays for itself out of whatever it lands on, so a one-stroke mark can go on working long after its writer would have liked it to stop.

**On Fate** · Everyday the person simply catches: a handshake is an alliance by supper, and the rumor of them takes the next town ahead of their horse. The rare real pull wants only the smallest beginning, one stumble in a front rank that runs the whole line or a single shout of *they've turned* that becomes the turn, and it fires on whatever charge is lying about, so it has as often routed the holder's own side and left them coughing blood.

**Spirit** · Temperance through ignition.

**Alchemy** · The first degree of fire: the smallest heat that goes on to pay for itself, which every Burning starts from. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Minimum ignition energy and the activation barrier.

**Mechanism** · The spark that lights a fuel-air mixture carries a fraction of a millijoule. Everything after is the reaction paying for itself. Ignivale reduces the activation energy of Essence formation, so the interval between intent and expression collapses toward the physical limit of the nervous system. First strikes arrive before the opponent has finished reading the preparation.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Ardency Overchannel, Dexterity Celerity. First strikes arrive faster and cost less to start.

**Glyph** · `[Ig]` Ignite, `[Noq]` Wind, `[Vt]` Vector; attested on eleven more.

**Failure** · Preignition. The barrier drops low enough that ambient emotional charge crosses it unbidden, and the working fires without the practitioner having decided to fire it.

## Cinerion · *The Ash Veil*

**The idea** · What the fire leaves. Ash taken as treasure: every spent thing feeds the next thing, and the soul learns to live on its own aftermath.

**Kind** · Wild

**On the Body** · The Shell eats its own leavings, turning spent Essence and burned-off effort into second wind, so the practitioner looks worst and feeds best at the end of a long fight.

**On the Spirit** · What a working leaves behind feeds the next one, and the practitioner can read a thing by its residue, getting from a burned letter some of what it said.

**On Fate** · Lost causes settle on the holder like ash in a low place, and after a long enough defeat a fighter can pull on what has been spent, so the failed feint of an hour ago becomes the place the opponent steps wrong. A beaten commander who lives on this may make the burned bridge and the dead men's debts delay a pursuit by a week, at the cost of whatever ruin they had left to draw on, and the gift fouls and stops on the day they have spent the most.

**Spirit** · Temperance through aftermath.

**Alchemy** · The law Calcination draws on, the Burning of the bench: strong dry heat drives off what is volatile and leaves what is fixed.

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Pyrolytic char and its catalytic residue. Particulate optical depth.

**Mechanism** · People call what survives a fire waste, and people are wrong. Char is a high-surface-area carbon scaffold that catalyzes further reaction, and settled ash returns potassium and phosphorus to the ground that fed the fire. Cinerion retunes the Shell to metabolize its own combustion products, harvesting from expenditure what other practitioners simply vent. The suspended residue also scatters incident light and Essence-sight alike.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Tempering Yield, Harmonics Stability. The residue of destruction becomes recovery material.

**Glyph** · None attested in the Index (see Discrepancies of Record on the hub).

**Failure** · Fouling. Accumulated residue eventually coats the conduit rather than feeding it, and recovery rate collapses precisely when the practitioner has spent the most.

## Solfatara · *The Fuming Earth*

**The idea** · The fuming earth. Pressure let out forever and never saved up, so the soul stops building toward a blow and becomes a steady force in the room.

**Kind** · Wild

**On the Body** · The Shell never stops venting, so the body stands in a fight the way a hot spring stands in a field, pushing steadily at whatever comes close and never winding up to do it.

**On the Spirit** · A working seeps where others would throw, and a line inscribed on a doorstep goes on breathing its meaning into the house for years at a cost nobody notices paying.

**On Fate** · Its usual weapon is plain presence with no peak to it, which wears on a besieging camp's sleep and nerve without anyone lifting a hand, and which the holder can keep up for a year. Pulling a thread through it is another matter: the sentry who has been about to drop his guard for a month can be made to do it tonight, and the pressure spent on him comes back out through the weakest thing the holder ever swore.

**Spirit** · Temperance through pressure.

**Alchemy** · The vessel that breathes: fume let off without end, so the charge never bursts. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Fumarolic degassing. Volatile exsolution as confining pressure falls.

**Mechanism** · Dissolved gas stays in solution under load and comes out of it the moment the load eases, which is why a volcanic system vents continuously for centuries without erupting. Solfatara holds the Crystal at permanent partial exsolution: a steady controlled release rather than a stored charge. The practitioner does not build to a discharge. They simply never stop discharging.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Tempering Clarity, Vitality Constitution, Dominion Pressure. Sustained pressure output becomes the character's natural state.

**Glyph** · `[Slg]` Slag; attested on one more.

**Failure** · Vent blockage and phreatic failure. Interrupt the release and the pressure does not politely wait. It finds a new path through whatever is structurally weakest.

## Manganthra · *The Catalyst of Change*

**The idea** · The go-between. Change that happens because something stands between two enemies and lets them meet, and a soul under this lens can hold what ought to tear it apart.

**Kind** · Wild

**On the Body** · The flesh becomes a surface where things that should never meet can meet, and one arm will carry a poison and its cure, or two Wellsprings' quarrel, without the limb being torn by it.

**On the Spirit** · Two workings that would wreck each other can be held side by side in the Core until they do what neither could alone, and the strange part is that the practitioner comes out of it unchanged.

**On Fate** · With a great deal of blood the go-between can bring about a meeting that already had a way of happening: two allied captains who hate each other on the same mountain road in the same hour, a duelist's old enemy at the ropes in the one exchange where he can do harm. It takes nothing from the deal and cannot do this often, because one bad meeting poisons the holder's whole gift for years, and both sides go on trying to hire them regardless.

**Spirit** · Temperance through transformation.

**Alchemy** · The go-between that stays out of the product: two reagents held side by side until they will do what neither would alone. *(the trade's reading)* Iron conducts it.

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Heterogeneous catalysis. Adsorption of incompatible reactants onto a shared surface.

**Mechanism** · A catalyst is not consumed and does not appear in the products. It provides a surface where two species that would never meet in bulk are held adjacent long enough to react, opening a pathway with a lower barrier than the direct route. Manganthra gives the Shell that surface, which is the mechanical reason a Manganthra practitioner can hold contradictory Wellsprings without resonance conflict tearing the lattice.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Ardency Depth, Gnosis Acuity. The practitioner integrates contradictory Wellspring energies without resonance conflict.

**Glyph** · None attested in the Index (see Discrepancies of Record on the hub).

**Failure** · Catalyst poisoning. One strongly-binding contaminant occupies the active sites permanently, and the practitioner loses not one harmonization but the capacity to hold any pair at all.

## Cataclysm · *The Shattered Law*

**The idea** · Upheaval. The moment a fire stops being a fire and becomes a blast, a change in kind as well as size, and the soul that has learned to call that moment.

**Kind** · Wild

**On the Body** · Given confinement behind it, a set stance or a wall at the enemy's back, a blow stops being a push and goes over into a blast, and the pressure crosses the target faster than the target can flinch.

**On the Spirit** · A projected working can change in kind partway across the distance, leaving as one thing and arriving as a rupture, and the people it arrives on rarely get to learn what it was meant to be.

**On Fate** · Slow quarrels near this person can be brought past their point all at once, so a garrison's ten-year feud and a month of short rations turn into a mutiny in one night, and a Domain (a region where the world runs on a practitioner's terms) widens by whole streets as everyone picks a side. It wants confinement, it comes off perhaps once in a war, and what comes back along the same path finds the holder standing at the origin with their ears and fingers at stake.

**Spirit** · Temperance through upheaval.

**Alchemy** · The vessel's violent end taken as a law: the moment a burning charge stops burning and becomes a blast. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Deflagration-to-detonation transition. Blast scaling under the cube-root law.

**Mechanism** · A subsonic flame front that meets sufficient confinement and turbulence accelerates until the reaction couples to a shock wave and begins propagating faster than sound in the unreacted medium. The mechanism changes kind where a lesser one would only change degree. Cataclysm makes that transition available to Essence expression, and blast radius thereafter scales with the cube root of yield, which is why the Wellspring's area effects widen coherently rather than merely spilling.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Ardency Cascade, Ardency Overchannel, Dominion Radius. Area expressions become wider and more structurally coherent.

**Glyph** · `[Thn]` Thunder, `[Eith]` Flux, `[Wy]` Motion; attested on five more.

**Failure** · Reflected overpressure. In confinement the shock returns along its own path, and the practitioner standing at the origin receives the sum of the wave and its reflection.

## Pyraeon · *The Ember Crown*

**The idea** · The ember that stays lit. Power that gives up its flare and keeps its heat for a lifetime.

**Kind** · Wild

**On the Body** · The flare goes and the heat stays, so the practitioner hits a little softer than anyone expects, and exactly that hard at dusk after a day of it, and again at seventy.

**On the Spirit** · Workings burn low and very long, and a mark left on a thing stays faintly live in it, still quietly arguing about what that thing is a year after the fight is forgotten.

**On Fate** · A claim kept banked for a lifetime can be blown on once at the end of it, and the war that turns is won by people who will not live to see it: a marriage refused here, a crossing left unbridged there, until the enemy's great-grandson marches to a river nobody ever got around to spanning. That one breath takes all the heat the holder kept for seventy years, and a fighter who spends it in a single duel has nothing left for dusk.

**Spirit** · Temperance through endurance.

**Alchemy** · The Long Furnace's fire: slow heat kept for months, with the charge never once flaring. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Smoldering combustion. Flameless, diffusion-limited, self-sustaining.

**Mechanism** · Smoldering propagates at roughly a millimeter a minute at temperatures a third of open flame, and peat beds have burned continuously for decades because the reaction is limited only by oxidizer reaching the char. Pyraeon converts the Crystal's output profile from flame to smolder: far lower instantaneous yield, and an endurance that does not decay across an engagement or across a life.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Constitution, Tempering Longevity, and Ardency Flux held over time rather than Flux at peak. Output does not fade over time. The ember persists.

**Glyph** · `[Py]` Pyre, `[Cin]` Cinder, `[Fge]` Forge; attested on five more.

**Failure** · Underground propagation. The reaction continues where the practitioner cannot see it, consuming reserve and tissue for hours after they believed the working ended.

## Catharsis · *The Wellspring of Release*

**The idea** · Letting go. A load set down so completely that the setting down spends it, and the soul comes out on the other side lighter and changed.

**Kind** · Spirit

**On the Body** · It gives a fighter nothing to throw. Terror, exhaustion and the shaking after a kill go out of the limbs in one long breath, and hands that could not close an hour ago close.

**On the Spirit** · Grief and rage held in the Core are spent by the letting go of them and take the old order of the soul along, so the person who stands up afterward is calmer and honestly somewhat different from the one who sat down.

**On Fate** · With everything the holder has, a bond or a load can be put down so completely that the line goes slack, and once in a long while a beaten company under such a captain sets down its terror all at once and stands up again with nothing left to rout from. Turned across a field it is rarer and stranger, the men opposite losing their hatred in the hour they needed it, and the holder comes out of it unable to say for a season what they themselves were fighting for.

**Spirit** · Temperance through letting go: release, purgation, true clearing. The Heresiology's warning stands beside it, that nothing is purified by being merely emptied.

**Alchemy** · Purgation: the matter gives up its burden in the change of state itself. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Latent heat of phase change. Enthalpy absorbed at constant temperature.

**Mechanism** · Melting ice at zero degrees absorbs three hundred and thirty-four joules per gram and the temperature does not move a fraction. The energy goes entirely into breaking the ordered structure. Catharsis routes accumulated emotional load in the Essence Core into a phase transition instead of into output, so enormous internal pressure is consumed by the change of state itself and the practitioner arrives on the other side measurably calmer and structurally different. The ice is the scholars' comparison and all of this happens in the soul: nothing outside the practitioner changes by a degree, unless you count the chill reported in a few confessionals, which the trade is still arguing about.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Harmonics Empathy, Tempering Maturity, Resilience Fortification. Emotional weight converts into structural clarity.

**Glyph** · `[Xr]` Excision, `[Sv]` Sever; attested on three more.

**Failure** · Supercooling. The transition fails to nucleate, the load accumulates past the point where it should have released, and when it finally goes it goes all at once.

## Penanceflare · *The Light of Contrition*

**The idea** · Burning in the open. Remorse that shines on the Soul Plane, so that what the soul is and what it means can be read by anyone able to look at a soul, and honesty costs nothing because nothing is hidden anymore.

**Kind** · Spirit

**On the Body** · No fire comes of it, only a body that has learned to bear being seen: a wound or a fear left unhidden stops costing Essence to hide, and the fighter's tells, being plain to everyone, stop being any use to anyone.

**On the Spirit** · The Core shows what it is and what it means, so every working wears its maker's intent on its face, and the remorse that lights it is very hard to argue with.

**On Fate** · Everything the holder owes stands in plain view, so a company sworn to them has no rumor left to break on and will sit out a siege on what it can see it is owed. Pushing that light into the enemy's lines, so that their men start asking to see their own captain's accounts, burns the holder's every shame in front of both armies and has been managed only a handful of times.

**Spirit** · Temperance through remorse.

**Alchemy** · The fire that reports itself: heat read by its color, as every alchemist reads a flame. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Blackbody radiation. Planck's law and the emissivity of a body that cannot hide its temperature.

**Mechanism** · Every body above absolute zero radiates, and the spectrum of that radiation reports its temperature to anyone with an instrument. The scholars' account is that Penanceflare does this to the Essence Core, raising its emissivity toward unity so that remorse shows on the Soul Plane at its true heat. The practitioner's workings become brilliantly legible to any Gnosis (the stat of reading and knowing) that can look at a soul, their intent readable in the emission, and concealment becomes impossible in exactly the measure that honesty becomes free. Almost none of this reaches the physical world, and a hand held near a penitent feels nothing. Whether the faint light some witnesses swear to on the skin is really there, and whether the burning of the Failure below is heat at all or a body agreeing with its soul, the Measurewrights have been arguing for a long time.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Ardency Compression inversely, raising a working's visibility rather than lowering it, and Gnosis Vigilance. Workings become more visible and more honest in expression.

**Glyph** · None primary. Attested on `[Py]` Pyre and `[Let]` Tide.

> **Failure** · Thermal runaway. Force the emissivity past what the body can shed and the heat climbs faster than the light can carry it off, so the practitioner burns brighter and hotter together, in the open, until the tissue gives out. There is also no concealment mode. The Wellspring does not permit one, and a Penanceflare practitioner who needs to hide has harmonized with the wrong Wellspring.

## Outside the Sixty · Vohrin · *The Stillwater Core*

> **This entry is the register's one exception, and it is marked as one.** The Sixty are fixed, Caloria holds ten of them, and Vohrin is not among the ten. It is the power of a Titan, [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Abyssal Depths — Prometheus, Zar'thuun, Vohrin|the Prime Cryotheic of Niflheim]], and it can be worked at Wellspring level. The note sits on this page because heat run backward is still thermodynamics, and because fighters keep writing "Vohrin (Caloria)" in their entries and somebody has to tell them what they have written.

**The idea** · The cold that stills. Prometheus burns what is false, Zar'thuun unmakes what is excess, and Vohrin stills what is left so it can be built upon. Cold here is no fire of its own. It is a deficit, heat let go of and held as a discipline.

**Kind** · Wild

**On the Body** · Heat leaves the tissue along a held gradient, so the blow carries a deficit into the wound and the holder's own blood runs slow and steady under it.

**On the Spirit** · Projected from the Core, the cold stills what it reaches and holds a thing at what it already is, and a hurrying thought or a half-finished working simply stops where it stood.

**On Fate** · What is held stays held: a bond stops moving in either direction, and with the whole of a life's discipline a charge can lose its reasons halfway across the field, or a war about to happen can sit in camp through the last of the good weather. The result has only been put off, and whoever is holding it off grows steadily colder in the fingers and then in the purpose.

**Two levels** · One power, worked two ways. The frost arts of the hunters and the northern schools work it at Wellspring level or as a Titan-derived current, each at the Stage floor its own entry states. The Titan-Keyed Stillwater Rite works it at the Titan's level and is gated at Revelation.

**Where it pools** · A cold site is a Titan-touched site. Surveyors and renderers read a Vohrin gallery the way they read a Core, with the same care and the same rope, and it does not go on any list of the Sixty's sites.

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Heat extraction. Conduction down a held gradient and the minimum work of a heat pump, which climbs steeply as the cold end falls; at the Titan's level, the zero-flux condition of a body that neither emits nor absorbs.

**Mechanism** · Cold is never emitted. A region goes cold only when energy leaves it, so the Shell takes heat out along a gradient that already exists and has to put it somewhere: the lungs, the air behind the practitioner, the bank of a field. In cold air the heat is cheap to move and in warm air it costs many times as much, and in thermal equilibrium there is nothing to pump at all. At contact the ordered cold crystallizes the Essence pathway in a wound.

**Stat Effect** · None of record, since it has no row in Part Thirteen. In use, Vitality Tolerance answers for the cold.

**Glyph** · No primary of its own that the register accepts. Its arts write in common forms such as `[Vor]` Return and `[Cro]` Cycle, and the Codex's frost rows, `[FrTh]` FrostThrone and `[ObsG]` Obsidian-Glacier, still stand under Cymorath's name (see the Cymorath entry).

**Failure** · Cold soak. Take heat past what the sink can carry and the deficit settles in whoever is holding it: the fingers, the lungs, then the mind, with purpose going before pain. A cold that is only a held deficit is also broken by heat forced back in faster than it can be taken, which is how flame ends the Stillwater Rite.

**Signature** · The aura stays inside the Family hue, ember-orange banked down nearly to brown under a skin of rime. The blue that people remember is the ice, and the aura is not blue. Sound is a long creak of settling ice and then the sound of the room falling away; the air smells scrubbed and then of nothing; heat leaves the skin toward the working. The practitioner's lungs ache and small purposes slip. The tell is frost running out from the feet, with the breath stopping in the air before it has gone anywhere.

# Fluxia — Fluid Dynamics

*Surrender and strength are the same posture · filed by the scholars under fluid dynamics: fluids and pressure*

Sweet Agnes Dunnock kept the book on the Reed Cup for twenty-two years and in all that time nobody heard her use the word *will*. Ask who wins and she would look at the river for a while, with deep teal afterimages of strokes not yet rowed flickering round the boats, and taste the rain that had not fallen yet, and say: sixty-one parts Sedge Street, twenty-eight the Ferrymen, eleven the weather. She was right more often than the priests. One year, two nights without sleep, she saw an Upper Weir win so bright she took it for the only river there was, and she put the house, the boat and the dog on it. The Upper Weir went under at the second bend, with a noise like a cow sitting down in a pond. It had been carrying eleven percent. She has kept the dog, by private arrangement with the new owner, and the odds she quotes on her own recovery are not good.

**The idea** · Empathy, reflection and adaptation. Its four Wellsprings are four ways a soul can be changed by giving way and being filled: memory, possibility, the weight of a feeling, and what you pour out.

**The lens** · A soul tempered on one of these four takes the shape of what holds it, and is likelier than most to fall into Synergia (Crystals briefly sharing one working) and Echo Bonds: a little at first, and after years of mastery at the lens's home Path up to double, so that the same Pressure at the same Grade carries twice the feeling into the people standing in it. One is Medium, at home on all three Paths, one is Spirit, at home on the Soul Plane, and two are Wellsprings of Fate, at home on the Plane of Fate. Away from home half again is the most anyone has got out of one, at a higher Essence cost and with a rougher result, and the odds on your recovery are posted daily. The full account of the lens is on the [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings|hub]].

**Stat Affinity** · *Gnosis (Retention, Forecast) · Harmonics (Attunement, Projection, Axis) · Dominion (Sense, Pressure, Command) · Tempering (Yield)*

*Each Wellspring below opens on what it is. **The idea** is the concept it lays over a soul's Aether. **Kind** names its home plane, and never locks it: Wild for the Physical World, Spirit for the Soul Plane, Fate for the Plane of Fate, Medium for all three. **On the Body**, **On the Spirit** and **On Fate** say what the lens does to Essence on each Path, through the Aether Shell, the Essence Core and the Attraction Layer in turn. After those, **Spirit** is the transformation it works on the soul, one road of Temperance (the climb of a soul from Stage to Stage), and **Alchemy** the principle it stands for at the bench. Then comes how scholars explain it: Analogue and Mechanism give the physics of what that type of Aether does to a Soul Crystal, Stat Effect what shifts on the sheet, Glyph the forms the Index seats on it, and Failure how it goes wrong.*

## Phreatis · *The Waters of Memory*

> Its full entry is written once, in the [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Vitalia — Biochemistry|Vitalia register]], and referred from here. It carries an identical stat effect in either context. On this page's reading it is the aquifer: groundwater reaching the well decades after it fell as rain, and returning what entered it, contamination included.

**The idea** · Memory as deep water. What is lived sinks in, is built into you, and comes back long after the weather has changed.

**Kind** · Medium

**On the Body** · What the body has lived sinks into the tissue and comes back when needed, so a Phreatis fighter's old drills surface whole in the hands decades after the last lesson.

**On the Spirit** · The practitioner reads and draws what has sunk into water and into a soul, and a working can call up what a place or person has long since stopped remembering.

**On Fate** · Nothing stays buried: old ties, debts and promises rise on their own schedule the way a spring does, and the holder can feel them coming. At the price of a heavy bite out of the reserve and a nosebleed that can last a day, one can be drawn up early, the lesson the other fighter's first master beat into them wrong surfacing in the exact exchange where it will cost them. Doing the same to a war, the poisoned treaty coming due or the well sunk a hundred years ago going bad the summer an army camps on it, is the work of a whole life, and whatever else is down there comes up with it.

## Somnalis · *The Dream Veil*

**The idea** · Living among what might be. The near possibilities of the Plane of Fate held in view at once, each with its odds on it, and a soul at ease in the not knowing.

**Kind** · Fate

**On the Body** · The flesh gets no extra force and a little ease with the odds: the foot comes down on the dry stone more often than it should, and the hands are already moving toward the likeliest of three blows.

**On the Spirit** · The Core can hold a person or a working as the spread of what it might yet be, so a thing can be read, and very rarely written on, in terms of what it has not become.

**On Fate** · Near possibilities stand in view with their weights on them, the pull behind each felt on the Attraction Layer like current on a line, and seeing them is the whole of what most holders ever do. A lean takes much of the reserve and often fails, and when it takes, of three blows the opponent might throw it is the one the guard is waiting for, or once in a campaign the rain comes an hour before the cavalry crosses instead of an hour after. Nobody can reason out which way to lean, as a certain Reed Cup bookmaker's house, boat and dog can attest.

**Spirit** · Temperance through vision. *The confessors' reading:* uncertainty, living easy among adjacent possibilities.

**Alchemy** · The sealed egg before it turns: every color it might go, held at once and unread. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Ensemble forecasting. Sensitive dependence and the spread of possible states.

**Mechanism** · A turbulent system cannot be predicted from a single initial condition, so forecasters run many slightly perturbed copies and read the spread rather than any one trajectory. That is the scholars' account, and what it describes is the Plane of Fate, where what has not yet happened already has weight. Somnalis opens the Attraction Layer onto that ensemble: adjacent possibilities perceived as a distribution with weights, each weight the pull of Attraction Force on a thing that could still go either way, which is why its bearers speak about the future in probabilities and are correct more often than people who speak in certainties. Most of them only ever read the spread. Far down the Path a bearer can lean on it, and the luck around them goes strange.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Gnosis Forecast, Dominion Sense. The practitioner perceives adjacent possibilities as clearly as present facts.

**Glyph** · `[Vor]` Return, `[SoulDr]` Soul-Drift, `[Au]` Death; attested on eleven more.

> **Failure** · Ensemble collapse. The spread is mistaken for a single reading, and the practitioner acts on a branch that carried eleven percent of the weight.

## Thaloré · *The Wellspring of Depth*

**The idea** · Depth of feeling. Emotion given its true weight in the soul, so that a grief can press on every soul in a whole room.

**Kind** · Spirit

**On the Body** · No force comes of it. The frame learns to stay upright and keep working under a weight of feeling that would put another mourner on the floor, and a body that has carried that can carry a broken rib.

**On the Spirit** · Feeling takes its true weight, and a grief held deep in the Core presses outward on every other soul in the room, who go home tired and cannot say why.

**On Fate** · A bond takes on the full depth of what is felt in it, so a widow's claim on a house leans harder than any deed, and a company's grief for its dead captain will hold a wall that every sum says is lost. Letting that weight down on the enemy's camp, where it settles on whoever already half believes they did wrong, takes the holder's whole self, and a holder whose support gives way under it is crushed by the same grief.

**Spirit** · Temperance through immersion. *The confessors' reading:* depth of feeling, emotional truth given its full weight.

**Alchemy** · Weight in the vessel: pressure set by depth alone, whatever the shape of the glass. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Hydrostatic pressure. Pressure as the product of density, gravity, and depth.

**Mechanism** · Pressure at depth depends on how dense the fluid is and how far down you are, and the vessel, the volume and the surface have no say in it. Thaloré gives emotional content that same constitutive relation: density times depth, expressed as literal metaphysical weight, which is why a Thaloré practitioner's grief has a measurable pressure field and why standing near one is physically tiring. The field is a field in the soul. It moves no air and bends no reed, and the claim of three separate towns to a wall cracked by a Thaloré mourner is argued over mostly by people who have never stood near one.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Harmonics Projection, Dominion Pressure. Emotional truth acquires metaphysical weight.

**Glyph** · `[Vo]` Voice, `[Wh]` Whisper; attested on thirteen more.

**Failure** · Implosion. The pressure acts inward as readily as outward, and a Thaloré practitioner whose internal support fails is crushed by their own field.

## Oblation · *The Offering Flame*

**The idea** · The offering. A bargain struck on the Plane of Fate: what you give up comes back as force, and never one grain more than you gave.

**Kind** · Fate

**On the Body** · Something laid down comes back in the arm at exact par: a fighter who gives up a year of good knees gets a year of good knees' worth of force, and the reckoning is not open to appeal.

**On the Spirit** · A working carries whatever was given up to make it, and one paid for with a memory or a name is unmistakably heavier, by exactly that memory or name.

**On Fate** · The offering is a bargain that will be honored: what is surrendered on the Attraction Layer reappears elsewhere as pull at exact measure, and the books are kept by something that does not round up. A duelist lays down the use of a hand for good and the other fighter's guard comes open by a hand's worth, and a commander who burns his own baggage and his road home sees the enemy's relief lose a day on the march for each one he gave up. No lens on the Path is more honest about what this costs, and the price has to be really yours, so a general spending a company he never loved has bought considerably less than he thinks.

**Spirit** · Temperance through sacrifice.

**Alchemy** · The offering in the vessel: what goes in as loss comes out as force, and the total never rises. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Bernoulli's principle. Conservation of the total head across a constriction.

**Mechanism** · Fluid accelerating through a narrowing does not gain energy. It trades pressure for velocity, and the sum across the streamline is conserved. That is the scholars' account of a bargain with the Plane of Fate. Oblation formalizes soul-cost as exactly that trade: what is surrendered in one term, a bond, a claim, a year or a name held on the Attraction Layer, appears in another, and the total is not increased by the sacrifice. **Practitioners who understand this stop describing the Wellspring as generosity.**

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Harmonics Axis, Dominion Command, Tempering Yield. Willingness to pay soul-cost translates directly into Essence generation.

**Glyph** · `[Ob]` Obol; attested on three more.

**Failure** · Cavitation. Drop the pressure term far enough and the fluid boils at ambient temperature, and the collapsing voids pit the conduit they formed in.

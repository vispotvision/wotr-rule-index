# Fulguria — Electromagnetism

*Seeing the truth before it finishes forming · filed by the scholars under electromagnetism: charge, light, and the fields between bodies*

No dice yard between Marrowhold and the sea will seat Lu Qinghe, the Scalpel of the Clear Needle Hall, and three of them pay her nine silver a month to stay away. At the Autumn Assize she stood up in the gallery with arc-blue threads crossing her eyes, and read a forged land grant from forty paces: the scribe, the ink, the week it was written, and the name of the man who paid for it, who was sitting in the front row in a very good hat. His bodyman went for a knife. She told him which of his knuckles was already cracked, and he stopped to check. The Needle Hall's colors went up in every street that night, and the Hall's rivals started writing their contracts in the dark. What the song leaves out is the fish market at the Salt Fair, on a hot morning with her guard down, when every herring, hook, scale-pan and lying fishwife in the square reported its composition to her at once, and four apprentices carried her home on a door, screaming numbers.

> Fulguria is the Family of **information**. Every Wellspring in it concerns something being carried across a distance without anything material making the trip, which is why perception and precision run on one law here and why the Family produces so few brawlers and so many people who are unbearable to lie to.

**The idea** · Perception, revelation and precision. Its six Wellsprings are six ways a soul can be changed by knowing, from the truth that parts a lie from its maker to the vastness that no mortal head was built to hold.

**The lens** · A soul tempered on one of these six reads a room, a wound or a lie before it finishes forming: a little at first, and after years of mastery at the lens's home Path up to double, so that the same glance at the same Grade carries twice the knowing and a blow finds twice the precision. Three are Wild, at home in the Physical World, two are Medium, at home on all three Paths, and one is Spirit, at home on the Soul Plane. Away from home half again is the most anyone has got out of one, at a higher Essence cost and with a rougher result, and the Family's practitioners will tell you how rough, at length and uninvited. The full account of the lens is on the [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings|hub]].

**Stat Affinity** · *Gnosis (Perception, Acuity, Analysis, Cognition) · Dexterity (Celerity, Feint) · Dominion (Sovereignty, Sense)*

*Each Wellspring below opens on what it is. **The idea** is the concept it lays over a soul's Aether. **Kind** names its home plane, and never locks it: Wild for the Physical World, Spirit for the Soul Plane, Fate for the Plane of Fate, Medium for all three. **On the Body**, **On the Spirit** and **On Fate** say what the lens does to Essence on each Path, through the Aether Shell, the Essence Core and the Attraction Layer in turn. After those, **Spirit** is the transformation it works on the soul, one road of Temperance (the climb of a soul from Stage to Stage), and **Alchemy** the principle it stands for at the bench. Then comes how scholars explain it: Analogue and Mechanism give the physics of what that type of Aether does to a Soul Crystal, Stat Effect what shifts on the sheet, Glyph the forms the Index seats on it, and Failure how it goes wrong.*

## Judicium · *The Wellspring of Truth*

**The idea** · Truth told apart. Discernment that knows what a thing is made of, and whether it is lying, from the mark it cannot help leaving.

**Kind** · Wild

**On the Body** · The hands learn what a thing is made of on contact, so the fist goes to the crack under the paint, the old break in the rib, the flawed link in the mail.

**On the Spirit** · Thrown as a line of reading across a room, it parts a thing from what it claims to be, and a lie under that line comes away from its maker in front of everyone.

**On Fate** · Whatever rests on a false oath already wants to give, and a holder who has read the lie can sometimes pull that thread: the mercenary company whose contract was forged, the general whose title was, the bodyguard who swore he was ready and was not. Knowing the lie is the easy half, because no amount of knowing lifts a thread, and the Family's cleverest have been carried home on doors trying the other half.

**Spirit** · Temperance through discernment: order, measure and consequence.

**Alchemy** · The law Separation draws on, the Parting of the bench: discernment, the parts told apart and taken apart.

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Emission and absorption spectroscopy. Nondestructive flaw detection.

**Mechanism** · Every element absorbs and emits at wavelengths fixed by its own electron structure, and no substance can present another's spectrum. Judicium tunes Gnosis to that principle: the practitioner reads the characteristic lines of a Crystal, a technique, or a lie, and identifies composition from signature alone. Eddy-current inspection finds the crack under the paint without removing the paint.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Gnosis Acuity and Analysis. The mind becomes a scalpel. Judicium practitioners read weakness the way literate people read text.

**Glyph** · `[Sel]` Measure, `[Ur]` Balance, `[Ar]` Accord, `[Krn]` Crown, `[IrW]` Will, `[Ed]` Edgetruth, `[Edt]` Edict, `[Exile]` Exile, `[NmEl]` Name-Eater, `[Flm]` Judic Flame; attested on twelve more.

**Failure** · Spectral overload. Every object in view reports its composition simultaneously and the practitioner loses the ability to prioritize any of it. Overdriven past the point where a reading's back-action outruns what it reads, the read stops being nondestructive and starts to adjudicate, which can turn away force or at the very top end a life, and having no aperture it takes the reader's own memory in the same stroke.

## Aurevane · *The Wellspring of Light*

**The idea** · Light that does not flatter. What you see grows truer, and what you send out arrives exactly as you meant it.

**Kind** · Medium

**On the Body** · The eye resolves a fight down to the grain of a wrist, and what the limb sets out to do arrives with no slop in it, every ounce landing on the span of skin it was meant for.

**On the Spirit** · What leaves the Core goes out in phase and arrives exactly as it was meant, and whatever stands in that light shows what it is without flattery.

**On Fate** · A word or an oath sent under this lens arrives with its whole meaning intact, and presence carries clear across a square or a field, which is most of what its fighters do with it. Drawn tighter it can close the space between an order given and an order received, so a signal reads true through the smoke at the far wing, or for one breath the single shaft loosed at a gap in the harness meets nothing to spoil it. That breath costs a fair part of the reserve and leaves the eyes bleeding, and since all of it shines, the enemy's first crossbow of the morning always knows where to look.

**Spirit** · Temperance through illumination.

**Alchemy** · The clear light of the watched vessel: the color that tells the worker the truth about the charge. *(the trade's reading)* Iron conducts it.

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Optical resolution and coherence. The Rayleigh criterion; phase-locked emission.

**Mechanism** · Two points are resolvable only when their diffraction patterns separate, and coherent light holds a fixed phase relationship across its whole front, which is what separates a true beam from a bright lamp. Aurevane raises both figures at once. What the practitioner perceives resolves finer, and what they express leaves the Shell in phase, meaning the output matches the intent with almost no distortion introduced in transit.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Gnosis Perception, Vigilance, Harmonics Attunement. What you see becomes truer. What you express becomes more accurate.

**Glyph** · None attested in the Index (see Discrepancies of Record on the hub).

**Failure** · Glare. Coherent output is also a beacon, and Aurevane practitioners are the easiest people in any engagement to locate. Pushed too hard it turns on its own optics: past the Shell's damage threshold the coherent front burns the conduit it leaves by, and the eyes go first.

## Cymorath · *The Air of Ascent*

**The idea** · Freedom in the air. The world read as a set of open ways, so that moving becomes a matter of noticing which way is already open.

**Kind** · Wild

**On the Body** · The body reads the air as a set of open ways, and the next step, leap or slip is already there to be taken, so the fighter seems to fall upward through a fight without ever pushing.

**On the Spirit** · A working thrown on this lens finds its own road around a ward the way spirit of wine finds the neck of a still, taking whatever way lies open, which is sometimes the way it was aimed.

**On Fate** · The holder feels chances the way a lodestone feels north and drifts toward them: the open door in a melee, the ford nobody has posted. Pulling one wide enough for an army, the gap in a line that two captains each believe the other is covering, is a feat of decades that empties them for a season and lands everyone somewhere efficient and seldom where they promised their allies.

**Spirit** · Temperance through freedom.

**Alchemy** · Volatile Mercury: the spirit that finds its own road up the neck of the still. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Vector fields and the Lorentz force. Motion determined by charge, velocity, and field.

**Mechanism** · A charged particle in a field does not choose a direction. Its path falls out of the cross product of its own velocity and the field it moves through. Cymorath gives the practitioner that reading of space: a set of gradients in place of a set of positions, each with a magnitude and a direction, so that movement becomes a question of which vector is already available rather than which one must be forced.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Dexterity Celerity, Gnosis Perception. The body reads space as a language of vectors and opportunities.

**Glyph** · No clean primary. The Codex still seats `[FrTh]` FrostThrone and `[ObsG]` Obsidian-Glacier here, a leftover from when frost was filed under this name; Cymorath is the Air of Ascent and frost belongs to Vohrin (see the note on [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Caloria — Thermodynamics|Caloria]]). Attested on four more.

**Failure** · Drift. The practitioner follows the available gradient rather than the intended line, and arrives somewhere efficient that is not where they meant to be.

## Orrenthal · *The Metallic Heart*

**The idea** · The heart held in common. A soul whose goods belong to the whole of it, which bends under load and keeps its word.

**Kind** · Wild

**On the Body** · A load struck on one point is borne by the whole frame, so a blow that should crack a forearm spends itself through the back and legs, and the body bends and comes back.

**On the Spirit** · Essence held in common across the Core carries a working through itself with hardly any loss, and with no part of the person arguing with the rest they are very hard to inscribe on or to talk round.

**On Fate** · A sworn company on this lens shares a debt and a hurt every day, and its oath bends a long way before it breaks. Its rare great working spreads the odds against one of them through all of them, so the spear meant for the standard-bearer meets a shield that had no business being there and every man in the line feels that spear go in somewhere, and an enemy who has read this goes to work between two friends, which is where it fails.

**Spirit** · Temperance through integrity.

**Alchemy** · The metal as a commonwealth: a body whose parts hold everything in common and so bends without breaking. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Metallic bonding. The delocalized electron sea.

**Mechanism** · A metal is strong, conductive, and ductile for one reason: its valence electrons belong to the lattice rather than to any atom in it. Nothing is individually owned, so the structure deforms without fracturing and carries charge with little resistance. Orrenthal imposes that bonding character on the Crystal. The Measurewrights note without embarrassment that the Wellspring's moral reading and its physical reading are the same reading.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Constitution, Resilience Integrity, Tempering Maturity. Moral density that the Crystal recognizes as structural reinforcement.

**Glyph** · `[Chw]` Choir, `[Br]` Bridge; attested on three more.

**Failure** · Intergranular corrosion. Attack proceeds along the boundaries rather than the body, and the structure fails at its seams while its bulk remains sound.

## Luminalis · *The Hidden Light*

**The idea** · The hidden light. Seeing what hides and hiding what shows, one act run in two directions.

**Kind** · Medium

**On the Body** · The eye moves past the visible band and the Shell turns a look aside at its surface, so the fighter sees the warmth of a man behind a screen, and the man's eyes slide off the place where the fighter is standing.

**On the Spirit** · The soul sees what hides and hides what it shows, and one working run in either direction will turn up a concealed Trait or bury its maker's from anyone reading.

**On Fate** · A bond or a claim can be kept out of every reckoning and produced at the right moment, and secret loyalties show to the holder like a second color on the people in a room. At great cost the reason a blow is going to land can be kept off the other fighter's reading until it has landed, and perhaps once in a long career the enemy's count of its friends is simply wrong, because the captain bound at midnight twenty years ago shows no color until the morning he opens the gate. Holding a thread that far out of sight is a lifetime's labor, and it splits the holder so that their own later workings arrive out of step with themselves.

**Spirit** · Temperance through perception.

**Alchemy** · The hidden tincture: the color present in the charge that no eye reads until the right light finds it. *(the trade's reading)* Quartz amplifies it.

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · The non-visible spectrum and polarization. Total internal reflection.

**Mechanism** · Most of the spectrum is not available to the eye, and most of what matters is happening there. Luminalis extends perception past the visible band and gives the Shell a polarizing behavior: incident observation is filtered out at the boundary rather than absorbed, and light that strikes past the critical angle does not leave at all. Seeing and hiding turn out to be the same optical operation run in opposite directions.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Gnosis Perception, Dexterity Feint. The practitioner sees what hides and learns to hide what they reveal.

**Glyph** · `[Lum]` Light, `[Ie]` Perception, `[Sar]` Reflection, `[Vei]` Depth, `[Oc]` Oculus, `[Dn]` Dawn; attested on nine more.

**Failure** · Birefringence. The Shell splits its own output into two paths with different velocities, and complex workings arrive out of phase with themselves.

## Abyntheus · *The Outer Pulse*

**The idea** · Vastness. The great scale read off the small vessel, a mortal soul stretched to take in things far too large for it.

**Kind** · Spirit

**On the Body** · It lends the arm nothing. It gives a frame that can stand under things too big to look at, so the fighter keeps their feet and their breakfast when a far higher Stage's Pressure or the sky over a Well comes down on them.

**On the Spirit** · The Core's aperture widens past what a mortal head was built to hold, and a Principle or an Archon's working becomes something the practitioner can read and, at some cost to their sanity, engage.

**On Fate** · The holder feels very long ties, the bond laid down before they were born or the debt that runs to the far side of the world, and soldiers near them bear what others break under, having been made to feel how small one field is inside what is coming. A tug on one of those ties, which might lean a whole people's old hatred on somebody's border, is the labor of decades, and the longest threads are apt to belong to Archons and the great principles, who may look down to see who is pulling.

**Spirit** · Temperance through vastness.

**Alchemy** · The Emerald Principle at the bench, as above, so below: the great scale read off the small vessel. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Very-long-wavelength propagation and interferometry. Resolution from baseline, not aperture.

**Mechanism** · Long wavelengths travel enormous distances, penetrate what shorter ones cannot, and carry the oldest signals in existence. Resolution at that scale comes from separating two small instruments by a continental baseline and reconciling their phase, and a larger instrument would not help. Abyntheus expands the conceptual aperture of the Essence Core by the same trick, letting a mortal Crystal engage phenomena whose scale should render them illegible. All of it happens in the soul. It throws nothing and moves nothing, and the reports of far-off bells heard and floors felt to tilt around an old Abyntheus master are argued over at every Measurewright's dinner.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Dominion Sovereignty, Sense, Gnosis Cognition. The conceptual ceiling expands to engage Archon-tier and Principle-level phenomena.

**Glyph** · `[Ab]` Abyss, `[Abys]` Deep, `[Let]` Tide; attested on three more.

**Failure** · Baseline decoherence. Hold the two ends of the perception apart too long and they lose phase lock, and what returns is noise the practitioner is nonetheless convinced is a signal.

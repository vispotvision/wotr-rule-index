# Fulguria — Electromagnetism

*Electromagnetism · charge, light, and the fields between bodies*
> Fulguria is the Family of **information**. Every Wellspring in it concerns something being carried across a distance without anything material making the trip, which is why perception and precision run the same current here and why the Family produces so few brawlers and so many people who are unbearable to lie to.
**Stat Affinity** · *Gnosis (Perception, Acuity, Analysis, Diagnosis, Cognition, Cartography) · Dexterity (Celerity, Burst, Silence) · Dominion (Reach, Acuity)*

---

### Judicium · *The Wellspring of Truth*

**Analogue** · Emission and absorption spectroscopy. Nondestructive flaw detection.
**Mechanism** · Every element absorbs and emits at wavelengths fixed by its own electron structure, and no substance can present another's spectrum. Judicium tunes Gnosis to that principle: the practitioner reads the characteristic lines of a Crystal, a technique, or a lie, and identifies composition from signature alone. Eddy-current inspection finds the crack under the paint without removing the paint.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Gnosis Acuity, Analysis, Diagnosis. The mind becomes a scalpel. Judicium practitioners read weakness the way literate people read text.
**Failure** · Spectral overload. Every object in view reports its composition simultaneously and the practitioner loses the ability to prioritise any of it.

### Aurevane · *The Wellspring of Light*

**Analogue** · Optical resolution and coherence. The Rayleigh criterion; phase-locked emission.
**Mechanism** · Two points are resolvable only when their diffraction patterns separate, and coherent light holds a fixed phase relationship across its whole front, which is what makes a laser a laser rather than a bright lamp. Aurevane raises both figures at once. What the practitioner perceives resolves finer, and what they express leaves the Shell in phase, meaning the output matches the intent with almost no distortion introduced in transit.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Gnosis Perception, Vigilance, Harmonics Fidelity. What you see becomes truer. What you express becomes more accurate.
**Failure** · Glare. Coherent output is also a beacon, and Aurevane practitioners are the easiest people in any engagement to locate.

### Cymorath · *The Air of Ascent*

**Analogue** · Vector fields and the Lorentz force. Motion determined by charge, velocity, and field.
**Mechanism** · A charged particle in a field does not choose a direction. Its path falls out of the cross product of its own velocity and the field it moves through. Cymorath gives the practitioner that reading of space: not a set of positions but a set of gradients, each with a magnitude and a direction, so that movement becomes a question of which vector is already available rather than which one must be forced.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Dexterity Celerity, Burst, Gnosis Cartography. The body reads space as a language of vectors and opportunities.
**Failure** · Drift. The practitioner follows the available gradient rather than the intended line, and arrives somewhere efficient that is not where they meant to be.

### Orrenthal · *The Metallic Heart*

**Analogue** · Metallic bonding. The delocalised electron sea.
**Mechanism** · A metal is strong, conductive, and ductile for one reason: its valence electrons belong to the lattice rather than to any atom in it. Nothing is individually owned, so the structure deforms without fracturing and carries charge without resistance. Orrenthal imposes that bonding character on the Crystal. The Measurewrights note without embarrassment that the Wellspring's moral reading and its physical reading are the same reading.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Constitution, Resilience Integrity, Tempering Maturity. Moral density that the Crystal recognises as structural reinforcement.
**Failure** · Intergranular corrosion. Attack proceeds along the boundaries rather than the body, and the structure fails at its seams while its bulk remains sound.

### Luminalis · *The Hidden Light*

**Analogue** · The non-visible spectrum and polarisation. Total internal reflection.
**Mechanism** · Most of the spectrum is not available to the eye, and most of what matters is happening there. Luminalis extends perception past the visible band and gives the Shell a polarising behaviour: incident observation is filtered out at the boundary rather than absorbed, and light that strikes past the critical angle does not leave at all. Seeing and hiding turn out to be the same optical operation run in opposite directions.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Gnosis Perception, Dexterity Silence. The practitioner sees what hides and learns to hide what they reveal.
**Failure** · Birefringence. The Shell splits its own output into two paths with different velocities, and complex workings arrive out of phase with themselves.

### Abyntheus · *The Outer Pulse*

**Analogue** · Very-long-wavelength propagation and interferometry. Resolution from baseline, not aperture.
**Mechanism** · Long wavelengths travel enormous distances, penetrate what shorter ones cannot, and carry the oldest signals in existence. Resolution at that scale comes not from a larger instrument but from separating two small ones by a continental baseline and reconciling their phase. Abyntheus expands the practitioner's conceptual aperture by the same trick, letting a mortal Crystal engage phenomena whose scale should render them illegible.
**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Dominion Reach, Acuity, Gnosis Cognition. The conceptual ceiling expands to engage Archon-tier and Principle-level phenomena.
**Failure** · Baseline decoherence. Hold the two ends of the perception apart too long and they lose phase lock, and what returns is noise the practitioner is nonetheless convinced is a signal.

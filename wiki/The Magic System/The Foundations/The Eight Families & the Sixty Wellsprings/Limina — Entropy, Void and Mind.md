# Limina — Entropy, Void and Mind

*The truth found in what is missing · filed by the scholars as abstract and entropic: entropy, void, and mind*

For seven years there was a price on the head of Hanne Mott and nobody could describe the head. She went through the Count of Wexmoor's strong-room with his guards dicing on the other side of the door, matte black-violet swallowing the lamplight around her and every sound in the corridor going dead as wet felt. Heralds cried the bounty up from four hundred silver to two thousand, the minstrels made *The Woman Who Is Not There*, which gets her height and her number of legs wrong, and bookies took odds on whether she existed. She planned every angle of every job. On the tenth job a pot boy had gone up the chimney stack after pigeon eggs, and he looked straight down on her from the one direction nobody plans for, and to him she was the brightest thing on Wexmoor Hill.

*"MISTER! THERE'S A LADY!"*

She left with a crossbow bolt through the shoulder, in a hay cart, at speed. The boy got a verse.

> The largest Family and the only one whose truest signature is **what fails to be there.** Everyone calls Limina the Family of darkness, and everyone is being romantic. It is the Family of systems described by their absences, their relaxation times, and their information content, which is a harder and less romantic thing than its practitioners generally admit.

**The idea** · Endings, dissolution and the mind's own dark. Its thirteen Wellsprings are thirteen ways a soul can be changed by what goes away: forgetting, sleep, dream, night, the mirror with nobody flattering you in it, and the law unmade. All of it surfaces when denial has started to cost more than acceptance, and its door is accepting that something is over, and meaning it.

**The lens** · Older books sort some souls as Limina from the cradle, and the trade has given that up: a soul takes these on by what it has lived, and then everything it does starts to end and quiet things. One of these thirteen brings that forward as the lens the soul tempers itself on: a little at first, and after years of mastery at its home Path up to double, so that the same Flux at the same Grade takes twice as much out of the fight it lands in. Away from home it works in that layer's terms, half again at the very most, at a higher Essence cost and with a rougher result, and a rough result here is a hole in your own childhood. The homes are unusually scattered for one Family: five of the thirteen are Wild, six are Spirit and do almost nothing to the room you are standing in, Oblivara is of Fate, and Anamnesis is Medium, at home on all three. The full account of the lens is on the [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings|hub]].

**Stat Affinity** · *Gnosis (Cognition, Retention, Analysis) · Resilience (Anchoring, Ward, Persistence, Continuity) · Harmonics (Attunement, Suppression, Stability) · Dominion (Sovereignty, Sense, Gravity)*

*Each Wellspring below opens on what it is. **The idea** is the concept it lays over a soul's Aether, **Kind** its home plane (a qualifier and never a lock), the three **On** lines what its lens does to Essence on each Path, **Spirit** the transformation it works on the soul, one road of Temperance (the climb of a soul from Stage to Stage), and **Alchemy** the principle it stands for at the bench. Then comes how scholars explain it: Analogue and Mechanism give the physics of what that type of Aether does to a Soul Crystal, Stat Effect what shifts on the sheet, Glyph the forms the Index seats on it, and Failure how it goes wrong.*

## Letheveil · *The Waters of Forgetting*

**The idea** · Forgetting on purpose. Learning which memories hold up the house and which ones are chains, and cutting the chains.

**Kind** · Spirit

**On the Body** · The flesh keeps its own memories, flinches and old breaks among them, and this lens lets the body put down the ones it no longer needs, so the shoulder forgets the spear that ruined it and the knee forgets to buckle at the old angle. No strength arrives with it, and a hand that has forgotten the wrong scar will walk straight back onto the blade that made it.

**On the Spirit** · At home it goes into the Core with a skimmer and takes off the memories that are chains, one's own or a willing other's, and what is left runs lighter and truer. Done to the unwilling it is the crime the seal-courts were built for, and whether it can be done at a distance is argued mostly by people who cannot remember.

**On Fate** · Forgetting a debt does not strike it off, as a great many people have been surprised to learn, but with a great effort the holder's own end of the line can be cut, and a company that has cut its end of a rout marches back onto the same field with no memory in its knees of how that morning went. The holder pays in their own childhood, piece by piece, and what the rout cost is still owed when the collector reaches the door.

**Spirit** · Temperance through release of memory. *The confessors' reading:* forgetting, the lesson of which memories are load-bearing and which are chains.

**Alchemy** · The skimming: scum taken off the charge, with the worker's guess at which part was scum. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Lossy compression. Shannon entropy and the identification of low-information content.

**Mechanism** · Compression works because most of a signal is redundant, and the art is entirely in knowing which components carry information and which merely occupy bandwidth. Letheveil performs that analysis on memory. It does not erase indiscriminately; it identifies what is load-bearing and discards the rest, and the practitioner's certainty about which is which is the whole of the risk.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Gnosis Cognition, and **tests** Resilience Anchoring rather than simply raising it. The practitioner learns which memories are load-bearing and which are chains.

**Glyph** · `[Stl]` Still; attested on four more.

**Failure** · Generation loss. Each pass discards a little more, and a memory compressed repeatedly degrades toward an artifact the practitioner still experiences as a memory.

## Lethegraal · *The Cup of Forgetting*

**The idea** · The cup. Time's mercy hurried along: old pain settles on its own schedule, and no hand chooses what falls.

**Kind** · Spirit

**On the Body** · The same wounds close on the same schedule, and the terror stored in them drains early, so a man burned half to death can stand beside a cook fire by spring with no faster healing to show for it.

**On the Spirit** · Under this cup the soul digests what it has been choking on, by its own clock and nobody's choice, and old grief goes down into the Core as plain structure that holds weight. Physicians prescribe it for that reason, and nothing about it reaches anyone but the one holding the cup, unless you count the family who wonder why she has stopped crying.

**On Fate** · Time wears bonds as well, and by pouring themselves out a holder can hurry it along, so the rage an enemy army carried out of its burned capital may have gone to mere complaint by the second week of the march. It is slow, draining work that nothing can aim, and its holders have lost sieges to their own side's forgiveness when a vow to die on the wall went slack on the morning it fell due.

**Spirit** · Temperance through quiet. *The confessors' reading:* time, old pain digested into neutral architecture.

**Alchemy** · The settling: the charge left to come to rest on its own schedule, with no hand choosing what falls. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Relaxation time. The characteristic constant with which a perturbed system returns to equilibrium.

**Mechanism** · Every disturbed system settles, and the only question is how long. Lethegraal shortens the time constant. Nothing is removed and nothing is chosen; the same trauma metabolizes down to neutral architecture on a schedule the practitioner is not otherwise able to influence, which is why the Wellspring is prescribed and Letheveil is merely permitted.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Tempering Maturity, Harmonics Stability. Trauma metabolizes faster. The Crystal digests old pain into neutral architecture.

**Glyph** · `[Cro]` Cycle, `[Len]` Line, `[StSl]` Still-Sleep; attested on eight more.

**Failure** · Under-damping. Shorten the constant too far and the system overshoots equilibrium on every cycle, and the practitioner oscillates rather than settling.

## Hypnather · *The Sleep Crown*

**The idea** · The sleep crown. Change that comes only at rest, the soul setting itself in order in the dark while nothing is asked of it.

**Kind** · Spirit

**On the Body** · The body does its mending asleep and does it thoroughly, a single night's rest doing the work of many, and none of it works while the eyes are open. Its fighters come to the field new every morning and are finished after a few nights without it.

**On the Spirit** · At home the Core sets itself in order in the dark while nothing is asked of it, and the person who lay down is not quite the one who wakes. The rare art that lays that quiet on someone else in sight is really laying it on their soul, the sect masters say, with the body merely following, and nobody has settled the matter since the only witness is asleep.

**On Fate** · A sleeper's oaths hold and their enemies lose the thread of them, and a commander who will give up waking for it, sometimes for weeks, may see a camp rest when it ought to lie awake and a night attack come down the wrong valley. The old rule against waking anyone under it is older than the Guild, because what stops partway sets worse than it began.

**Spirit** · Temperance through rest.

**Alchemy** · The Standing: nothing is done and nothing may be done, and the thing goes on ordering itself in the dark. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Structural relaxation. Physical aging of a glass toward its equilibrium configuration.

**Mechanism** · A glass is a liquid that stopped moving before it finished arranging itself, and left undisturbed it continues to relax toward the ordered state it never reached, slowly and only while quiescent. Hypnather grants the Crystal that behavior. The reorganization happens during genuine rest and cannot be forced by ordinary means, which is why Hypnather practitioners sleep like the dead and wake structurally different. A few arts do force it, pinning the quiet true on command in the practitioner or in a body held in sight, so that the target's motion slows and its outline smooths as though sleep had come down all at once. The physics is nearly free and the Essence is not, and a relaxation forced is a relaxation that ends in the Failure below.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Regeneration on sleep cycles, Tempering Yield, Gnosis Retention. Recovery during rest becomes dramatically more efficient.

**Glyph** · None primary. Attested on `[Vel]` Mind, `[Vl]` Veil, `[AnMa]` Anamnesis and `[Ora]` Truth.

**Failure** · Interrupted relaxation. Disturb the process partway and the configuration locks where it stood, worse ordered than before it began.

## Mirithane · *The Wellspring of Reflection*

**The idea** · The honest mirror. The inside and the outside made one surface.

**Kind** · Spirit

**On the Body** · The body stops spending itself on concealment, with no clenched jaw, no swallowed flinch and nothing kept up for the room, and it gets back the breath all that cost. It also loses every feint it ever owned, and the sparring hall finds this out first.

**On the Spirit** · At home the Core and what leaves it become one surface, so a working carries what the person actually is, and a soul at peace with itself throws truer than its Grade suggests. Turned on someone else, the art hands them their own soul with the flattery taken off, and whether that counts as a technique or merely as tactlessness is a dispute the Measurewrights have yet to close.

**On Fate** · Presence turns into plain speech: a room knows what this person wants, an oath sworn under this lens cannot carry a hidden clause, and a sworn company will follow one off a cliff with nobody left to wonder about the orders. Turning that on a parley, so an enemy goes home knowing how much of his own army means to stand, strips the holder bare before every soul present and leaves them nothing private for the rest of their life, which is why it is rarely done.

**Spirit** · Temperance through stillness. *The confessors' reading:* self-sight, the inside and the outside made one honest surface.

**Alchemy** · The blick: the flash on the assay button that shows the metal exactly as it is. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Specular reflection. The law of reflection; the phase-conjugate mirror.

**Mechanism** · A true mirror adds nothing. Angle in equals angle out, and fidelity is defined entirely by the absence of introduced distortion. A phase-conjugate mirror goes further and returns a wavefront that undoes its own aberration on the way back. Mirithane makes the Essence Core such a surface, so what leaves the practitioner is what was in them, with no flattering correction applied in transit.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Harmonics Attunement, Tempering Coherence. Output becomes a more honest reflection of internal state.

**Glyph** · `[Se]` Song, `[Ari]` Aria, `[Nx]` Nexus, `[Pt]` Pattern, `[Wvn]` Weave; attested on fifteen more.

> **Failure** · Perfect return. The mirror does not discriminate. A practitioner in a state they would rather not broadcast broadcasts it perfectly.

## Oneirion · *The Wellspring of Dream*

**The idea** · Dream as a place. Work done in the space of what can be thought, reaching a soul where no line of sight runs, because no soul was ever in the room.

**Kind** · Spirit

**On the Body** · Asleep or deep in trance the body learns what the dream rehearsed, so a fighter may wake with a parry they never drilled and the bruises of a duel nobody else attended. It lends no force, and an ache carried out of the dream lasts all day with no mark to show a physician.

**On the Spirit** · At home a working goes through the space of what can be thought and arrives at a soul, since a soul was never in the room to begin with, and it steers by whatever the sender holds of the target: a name, a face, a shared roof. The dreamer wakes shaken and changed and the furniture has not moved, and the stories in which it did are told by people selling something.

**On Fate** · A dream shared often enough becomes a bond, and strangers keep dreaming of the same door and then keep meeting, which is the small and very strange end of it. A holder who gives a season of nights to it can send a levy onto a field it has dreamed for a week, already knowing how to run, at the price of no longer being certain which field they woke on, and a Domain (a region where the world runs on a practitioner's terms) built on it has terms nobody can quite recite afterward.

**Spirit** · Temperance through imagination. *The confessors' reading:* dreaming.

**Alchemy** · The working in the mind: the operation carried out on no bench at all. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Configuration space. A system's state as a point in an abstract high-dimensional manifold.

**Mechanism** · A mechanical system's every possible arrangement is a coordinate in a space that has no physical location, and the system's evolution is a trajectory through it. Oneirion lets a working act on that manifold rather than on the room, and on the Soul Plane the manifold is very nearly the plain fact of things: souls have no address in a room, and what lies between two of them is how much one can hold of the other in mind. This is the mechanical reason Oneirion techniques reach targets no line of sight connects, and the reason they are so difficult to describe afterward.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Gnosis Cognition, Dominion Sovereignty, Ardency Cascade. Workings operate through conceptual space, not only physical space.

**Glyph** · `[Ka]` Dream, `[Vl]` Veil, `[RaE]` Emotion, `[DmSl]` Dream-Seal, `[Nys]` Dreamrift, `[OniV]` Oneiric Veil; attested on five more.

**Failure** · Projection error. The trajectory was correct in the manifold and lands nowhere in particular when it is mapped back down into a room.

## Eidolyn · *The Shaping Mind*

**The idea** · The shaping mind. A thing imagined so hard that it stands up and has to answer for itself.

**Kind** · Wild

**On the Body** · At home the imagined thing grows out of the practitioner's own Shell and has real weight: a plate of solid intent across the ribs, a third hand that can hold a spear, a fist that lands a hand's width before the arm arrives. It breaks the way a bad casting breaks, along whatever the maker failed to picture.

**On the Spirit** · Authored in the Core and thrown, the construct stands at a distance and does exactly as much as its maker has fully imagined. Scribes have raised a bridge across a gorge this way, and rivals have brought it down by asking loudly what was holding up the middle.

**On Fate** · What gets made solid this way is a reputation, and on most days that is the whole weapon: the ballad's version of a person reaches the field a half step ahead of them, frightening what the original cannot. Forcing it, so that a line breaks for a charge that is forty men and a great deal of dust, takes the whole of the holder's reserve and faithfully copies every flaw in the ballad, and sooner or later someone who knows the words fights them by it.

**Spirit** · Temperance through creation. *The confessors' reading:* imagining, the thing thought made solid enough to answer for.

**Alchemy** · The mold: matter deposited along a pattern the maker imposes. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Templated nucleation. Ordered deposition onto an imposed pattern.

**Mechanism** · A supersaturated medium will not organize itself, but given a template it deposits along the template's geometry with high fidelity and considerable strength. Eidolyn makes intent the template. The construct's solidity is real material behavior rather than illusion, and its failure modes are the failure modes of a poorly designed casting.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Ardency Density in summoned constructs and inscriptions, Gnosis Retention. Mental constructs gain material solidity.

**Glyph** · None primary. Attested on `[AnMa]` Anamnesis, `[Mn]` Mnemonic, `[EchoS]` Echo-Scar and `[Sil]` Silence.

**Failure** · Template defect. Any flaw in the intent is faithfully reproduced at scale, and the practitioner is rarely able to see the flaw until it is standing in front of them.

## Anamnesis · *The Wellspring of Remembrance*

**The idea** · Remembrance. Everything keeps its past, and a soul under this lens can touch a thing and read it.

**Kind** · Medium

**On the Body** · A palm on a spear shaft tells who carried it and how they died, and a hand on a broken wall gives back the blow that broke it, in enough detail for the practitioner's own shoulders to repeat it. The flesh under this lens forgets nothing it has ever met, so an old fighter has a lifetime of falls and parries standing ready in the joints.

**On the Spirit** · In the Core it reads a soul as a fired pot reads its kiln: what this person has committed to, what was done to them, how long ago and at what dose. Inscribed on a thing, it makes that thing's history part of what the thing is, which is how an old sword comes to know its trade.

**On Fate** · Every oath, debt and death leaves a record on the Attraction Layer and this lens reads it on contact, so a handshake can say who else this hand has sworn to, and a sword taken from a dead man can say which guard he always dropped. With a palm on a fortress's footing it finds the winter the mortar was skimped and the oldest claim on that ground, and calling either one in, so that the wall remembers what it is owed, takes a lifetime's skill and a body's worth of blood. Courts pay very well for the reading and then try to have the witness quietly sealed, and a careless pull erases the very record it was pulling on.

**Spirit** · Temperance through awareness. *The confessors' reading:* remembrance, which preserves charged records of lived commitment, and more than mere memory.

**Alchemy** · The fired pot's testimony: the residue in a vessel says everything it has ever held. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Remanent magnetization and thermoluminescent dosimetry.

**Mechanism** · Basalt records the direction of the field it cooled in and holds that record for geological time. Fired ceramic accumulates trapped electrons in proportion to the radiation dose it has absorbed since its last firing, and releases them as light when heated. Objects genuinely store their history in physically readable form, and so, each in its own layer, do a body, a soul and a sworn word, which is why this Wellspring is at home reading any of them. **Anamnesis is an instrument, and anyone calling it a miracle has not looked hard at a fired pot.**

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Gnosis Retention, Gnosis Analysis in reading past trauma, Resilience Anchoring. The practitioner reads history through contact with objects, places, and beings.

**Glyph** · `[Ma]` Recall, `[AnMa]` Anamnesis, `[Lei]` Binding, `[VaM]` Moment, `[Mn]` Mnemonic, `[EchoS]` Echo-Scar; attested on nine more.

**Failure** · Thermal resetting. Reading is not always passive, and a careless practitioner erases the record in the act of taking it.

## Dissolution · *The Unbinding Stream*

**The idea** · Unbinding. What no longer serves is allowed to come apart on its own, and keeping anything at all becomes the work.

**Kind** · Wild

**On the Body** · It goes in with the blow, and whatever the fist touches starts to lose its argument for staying together: mail lets go of its rivets, a wound's edges refuse to knit, a shield comes off in strips. Such a soul's own body has to be held together on purpose, and a tired one shows it at the seams.

**On the Spirit** · Thrown, it is a solvent for definitions: a seal loses track of which part of it was the seal, and a ward sags out of its own inscription into a puddle of good intentions. It runs slower than a blow and has never once been selective.

**On Fate** · Bonds slacken around such a person with nobody able to name the day, and a coalition camped near one for a season may shed allies as harvests come due and old terms turn out to have lapsed. Brought to bear on one oath in the middle of a fight, so that a bodyguard's sworn step forward arrives a moment late, it costs the holder a bond of their own each time, and the Accord's notaries keep a short list of who has tried.

**Spirit** · Temperance through release. *The confessors' reading:* unbinding, the soul dissolving what no longer serves its architecture.

**Alchemy** · One of the six named operations and a stage of the Work, the Taking of the bench: cohesion unraveled into the menstruum. The Bench files a Dissolution Draft as a composite, and it is volatile: under Preparation Failure it does not deactivate at all.

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Solvation. Dissolution driven by the entropy of mixing.

**Mechanism** · A solid dissolves because the mixed state has higher entropy than the separated one, with no attack by the solvent at all, and the process therefore runs on its own once initiated. Dissolution gives the Crystal that thermodynamic preference toward the unbound state, which makes shedding what no longer serves nearly effortless and makes retaining anything a matter of active work.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Resilience Ward, Tempering Maturity. The character dissolves what no longer serves the Crystal's architecture.

**Glyph** · `[Blt]` Blight; attested on eleven more.

> **Failure** · Indiscriminate solvency. Entropy does not consult the practitioner about which structures were load-bearing.

## Tenebra · *The Hidden Shadow*

**The idea** · The hidden shadow. Going unseen, quieter than the world around you, and the old question of what you are hiding from.

**Kind** · Wild

**On the Body** · The Shell drinks the light and noise that reach it, and a fighter stays perfectly visible while going quieter than the background, so the strike comes from someone the eye keeps sliding off. Everything swallowed stays swallowed, a debt with a due date.

**On the Spirit** · Cast out from the Core, the same hush is laid over what a thing is, so a hidden door simply goes unconsidered and a ward looks straight past a man who has hidden his purpose from it. It is weaker than it sounds, and anyone who has been told where to look is untroubled.

**On Fate** · What hides this way is a person's share of the world's attention: creditors mislay the address, and bounty boards go stale. Stretching it over a raiding column, so that a sentry's eye slides to the wrong ridge and men who watched it pass report nothing, is a rare and ruinous feat that leaves the holder for some while the brightest thing on the hill, and friends and relief columns mislay the address too.

**Spirit** · Temperance through secrecy. *The confessors' reading:* concealment. The Heresiology's doctrine stands beside it: the presence of shadow is no proof of heresy, and the use of shadow to erase cost, witness or responsibility often is.

**Alchemy** · The cover-name: the recipe written to hide. *(the trade's reading)* Obsidian holds it.

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Signal-to-noise reduction. Absorptive coating and faceted return geometry.

**Mechanism** · Something is undetectable when its return falls below the noise floor of whatever is looking, which is achieved by absorbing incident energy and by shaping surfaces so the little that reflects goes anywhere except back at the observer. Tenebra applies both to the Shell. Its practitioners stay perfectly visible and run quieter than the background, which is better.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Dexterity Feint, Harmonics Suppression, Dominion Sense. The practitioner becomes difficult to detect, read, or track by conventional or Aetheric means.

**Glyph** · `[Thal]` Silence, `[Stg]` Stagnate; attested on five more.

**Failure** · Aspect dependence. Faceting works against expected angles, and a Tenebra practitioner observed from the one direction they did not plan for returns a very bright signal indeed. Pushed too hard, the coating saturates: everything absorbed is still in the Shell, and a practitioner held under the noise floor past their limit lets it go at once and lights up on every sense in the room.

## Oblivara · *The Dark Mirror*

**The idea** · Keeping your name. A self written down on the Plane of Fate so many times that it cannot be erased, with whatever is done to it sent back where it came from.

**Kind** · Fate

**On the Body** · A strange and modest gift: the flesh keeps a stubborn notion of whose it is, so a curse, a graft or a possession finds the body reasserting its owner, and what was meant to change them travels back up the arm that sent it. It will not turn a sword.

**On the Spirit** · Inscribed in the Core, the name is a second copy of the self held against loss, so a working that tells the soul what it is gets told the same thing in return, weakly and a little late. A soul written this way can lose a great deal and still be read back from what survives.

**On Fate** · At home the name is held in many hands, every oath it swore and every mouth that ever cursed it counting as a copy, and what is done to it, erasure or false witness or an eye turned aside, goes back along the line it came in by. A Fate working aimed at such a person can travel home, so the slipped guard turns up on the arranger, though each return spends copies the holder does not get back, and past a limit the name that comes back is confident, complete and wrong. A practitioner can lose a whole city's memory of them and still walk in on the strength of one old debt, and one general thought dead for a decade spent the last of his copies raising an army out of unpaid promises.

**Spirit** · Temperance through erasure. *The confessors' reading:* keeping one's name against erasure.

**Alchemy** · The second charge: a duplicate kept back so a ruined batch can be read against it. *(the trade's reading)* Obsidian holds it.

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Error-correcting redundancy. Retroreflection along the incident vector.

**Mechanism** · Information survives a noisy channel when it is encoded with enough redundancy that corrupted symbols can be reconstructed from the surviving ones, and a corner reflector returns incident energy precisely along the path it arrived on. That is the scholars' account of what happens to a name on the Plane of Fate, where it is a claim the world goes on honoring and every oath, debt and memory that holds it counts as a copy. Oblivara does both: the practitioner's identity is redundantly encoded against erasure, and attempts at perceptual manipulation return to their source, along with anything else done to the name.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Resilience Persistence, Gnosis Cognition. Resistance to conceptual erasure and perceptual manipulation.

**Glyph** · None primary. Attested on `[Vn]` Void, `[NmEl]` Name-Eater, `[Ora]` Truth and `[El]` Life.

**Failure** · Code exhaustion. Redundancy has a correction limit, and past it the reconstruction is confident, complete, and wrong.

## Nyxial · *The Nocturne Flame*

**The idea** · The even flame. A self that holds its level whatever the room does.

**Kind** · Spirit

**On the Body** · No flame comes of it, whatever the old name promises. The body holds its level, pulse and temper and grip the same in a burning hall as in a bath, and it can bear a long siege or a long torture without the highs that would have helped or the lows that would have warned.

**On the Spirit** · At home the soul keeps a setpoint, and elation, terror and other people's Pressure all arrive and fail to move the needle. Some hold that the room around such a person slowly evens out too, lamps steadying and tempers dropping, and those who have tried to measure it got different answers on different nights.

**On Fate** · The odds around this person run unusually flat, with no streaks and no disasters out of nowhere, and a Fate commander's best morning, bought at ruinous expense, goes to waste against a company held level by it. Bonds stay at the temperature they were sworn at, as spouses have been known to mention, and holding a line at that level through a long siege is a slow exhaustion, all of it coming out at once on the day the load lifts.

**Spirit** · Temperance through night. *The confessors' reading:* evenness.

**Alchemy** · The Still Bath: self-limiting at the boil, whatever the fire below it does. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Thermal inertia under negative feedback. A regulated system holding setpoint against ambient.

**Mechanism** · A body with high heat capacity governed by a feedback loop holds its temperature regardless of what the room does, and the larger the capacity the smaller the excursion. Nyxial gives the Crystal that regulation. The practitioner's baseline does not rise in favorable conditions and does not fall in hostile ones, which is a disadvantage exactly as often as it is a defense.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Harmonics Stability, Dominion Gravity, Vitality Constitution. Baseline output does not fluctuate with environmental pressure.

**Glyph** · None attested in the Index (see Discrepancies of Record on the hub).

**Failure** · Integral windup. A regulator held against a load it cannot overcome accumulates correction, and when the load lifts the correction discharges all at once.

## Nihiloth · *The Hollow Law*

**The idea** · The hollow law. Power by negation: the rule itself unmade, and what stands in the gap.

**Kind** · Wild

**On the Body** · It rides the blow as a small cancellation: a hardened Shell meets the fist and for that instant has nothing holding it up, and a swung weight arrives with its momentum gone missing. The same gap opens in the striker's own guard, so the good ones fight as though they have already been hit.

**On the Spirit** · Thrown from the Core, it strikes one clause out of what a thing is and leaves the rest standing around without it: a blade that has stopped being sharp, a ward that no longer recalls what it was warding. Fine work and ruinously dear, and no two Measurewrights agree on what is in the gap.

**On Fate** · Its great terror is the Domain, where a rule removed reaches exactly as far as the practitioner's claim on the ground, and at the far end of a life that claim can take in a field on which a chain of command or a company's oath has nothing left holding it up. **Spirit** · Temperance through void. *The confessors' reading:* negation.

**Alchemy** · Neutralization: two reagents poured together until nothing is left in the flask that can act. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Destructive interference and the null field. A region genuinely empty of energy.

**Mechanism** · Two waves in antiphase do not conceal one another. They sum to zero, and the region between them carries no energy at all. Nihiloth builds Domains on that principle, which is why a Nihiloth Domain does not impose additional law. It cancels the law already present, and what remains inside is the absence of a rule.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Dominion Sovereignty, Resilience Persistence, Gnosis Cognition. The practitioner imposes law through negation. Their Domain does not add rules. It removes them.

## Vantabriel · *The Wellspring of Night*

**The idea** · The night that keeps. Enduring by carrying everything that was lost, with none of it put down.

**Kind** · Wild

**On the Body** · What strikes the practitioner stays in them: the blow goes into muscle and bone and nothing comes back out, no recoil and no spray of force, only a slow heat from inside. Every fight they have survived is still in the flesh, which is how someone who moves like an old man comes to stand under a maul.

**On the Spirit** · Thrown from the Core, a working on this lens reflects nothing and reports nothing. Whatever it takes joins the pile, and a ward it swallows becomes one more thing the caster must carry for life.

**On Fate** · The lost stay bound to them, every dead friend, broken oath and failed cause keeping its pull on the Attraction Layer, so such a person trails a slow heavy pull that the sensitive feel as an empty chair, and survivors of ruined companies find their way to that door. Calling the whole weight down at once, so that a general who has beaten them five times looks up on the sixth field and finds all five defeats drawn up against him under one banner, is done once if at all, and the holder has to carry everything it brings.

**Spirit** · Temperance through absence. *The confessors' reading:* carrying what was lost.

**Alchemy** · The black that keeps: char that holds everything it has ever taken. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Near-total absorption and hysteresis. Retained state dependent on history.

**Mechanism** · An ultra-absorptive surface returns almost nothing and converts what it receives into internal heat, and a hysteretic material's present state depends not on current conditions but on the path it took to arrive. Vantabriel combines them: what strikes the practitioner is kept rather than reflected, and the Crystal's condition is a function of everything it has ever absorbed. **Endurance here is loss carried, with none of it replaced.**

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Resilience Continuity, Harmonics Attunement, Tempering Longevity. The character endures by carrying the memory of what was lost rather than replacing it.

**Glyph** · `[DmN]` Dominion; attested on one more.

**Failure** · Thermal saturation. Absorption without emission has a limit, and past it the retained load has nowhere to go but into the structure holding it.

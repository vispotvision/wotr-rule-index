# Materia — Material Science

*Choosing to remain · filed by the scholars under material science: matter and substance*

Ingrid Stenhammar fought the Stage brackets at Tollgate for six summers as the Changing Woman, and they sold out the stands to see it. In the first exchange she would go diamond from the collarbone down in a wash of amber light, and a man's knuckles would come apart on her. In the second she re-latticed with a small bright ring you could hear at the beer stalls, soft and gray and slick as an eel in butter, and his best hold slid off her like a dropped plate. Hats went in the air. Then, in the sixth final, while she was slick and laughing, Black Anders Holm brought a twelve-pound maul down on her with everything he owned, and the pressure came. She has been graphite from the collarbone down ever since. She cannot hold a sword or take a fall, she leaves a gray streak on every door in the Quarter, and she sells her own shavings to the Tollgate wagoners as axle grease at a wicked price, with the best patter on the road.

**The idea** · Endurance, structure and grounding. Its nine Wellsprings are nine ways a soul can be changed by what it is made of: re-forming, binding, settling, reckoning, and the long business of staying put. Its door is choosing to remain when every instinct demands flight.

**The lens** · Older books sort some souls as stone from the cradle, and the trade has given that up, though it is still true that these nine are the slowest to come to and the hardest to shift off once they have you. One of them brings that weight forward as the lens a soul tempers itself on: a little at first, and after years of mastery at its home Path up to double, so that the same Fortitude at the same Grade behaves twice as much like the thing it has decided to be. Away from home it works in that layer's terms, half again at the very most, at a higher Essence cost and with a rougher result, as anyone who has bought a stone Draft and then tried to dance will explain. Seven of the nine are Wild, at home in the Physical World; Fixatio is Medium, at home on all three; and Tarturon is of Fate. The full account of the lens is on the [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings|hub]].

**Stat Affinity** · *Vitality (Fortitude, Tolerance, Regeneration, Absorption) · Resilience (Integrity, Anchoring, Hardening, Persistence, Fortification) · Dominion (Gravity) · Tempering (Coherence, Clarity)*

*Each Wellspring below opens on what it is. **The idea** is the concept it lays over a soul's Aether, **Kind** its home plane (a qualifier and never a lock), the three **On** lines what its lens does to Essence on each Path, **Spirit** the transformation it works on the soul, one road of Temperance (the climb of a soul from Stage to Stage), and **Alchemy** the principle it stands for at the bench. Then comes how scholars explain it: Analogue and Mechanism give the physics of what that type of Aether does to a Soul Crystal, Stat Effect what shifts on the sheet, Glyph the forms the Index seats on it, and Failure how it goes wrong.*

## Coagula · *The Unifying Pulse*

**The idea** · Gathering the broken self. A soul's scattered pieces come back into one body without ever melting, keeping the shape they had while broken.

**Kind** · Wild

**On the Body** · Broken bone and split muscle draw back together under the Shell with no melting stage and no pause, each piece keeping the shape it had while broken, so a fighter who has been smashed gets up crooked and whole. The great Coagula brawlers look like sacks of walnuts and are extremely hard to finish.

**On the Spirit** · In the Core it is the old inward work: a soul split by a bad Threshold or a bad decade gathers up its own pieces, and what it throws holds together over distance because no part of it will consent to leave. Cast on anything else it is dear and brief, and the legs pay.

**On Fate** · Scattered things come to the same table: feuding cousins, broken companies and lost partners find themselves in one town, with this person somehow always in the doorway. A cracked bond welds without ever being talked through, so the seam stays exactly where it was.

**Spirit** · Temperance through merging: the Soul Plane's unifying force, a soul gathering itself back together.

**Alchemy** · The personal twin of Coagulatio: gathering without melting, the soul's own Setting. Basalt takes it.

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Sintering. Densification by atomic diffusion below the melting point.

**Mechanism** · Press powder together and hold it hot, though never hot enough to melt, and the particles fuse across their contact points by diffusion alone until the porosity closes and the mass behaves as one body. Coagula runs the Soul Plane's version: a Crystal that has been fragmented gathers itself without ever passing through a liquid state, which is to say without losing the shape it had while broken. The law runs inward by nature. A handful of high arts throw it outward, gathering the ground under a stance or the Essence in a room into one body, and they pay for the reversal in the practitioner's own legs and Shell.

Coagula is a soul's gathering, and a few fighters at great cost have thrown the same current outward over a room or the ground underfoot. It lasts as long as they hold it. Matter gathered for good belongs to Coagulatio.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Resilience Integrity, Tempering Coherence. The Crystal becomes harder to split.

**Glyph** · None primary. Attested on `[Krn]` Crown and `[Wy]` Motion.

**Failure** · Residual porosity. Sintering rarely reaches full density, and the voids that remain are the exact locations at which the next fracture will initiate.

## Fixatio · *The Binding Flame*

**The idea** · Commitment. What is bound here stays bound, because the way back has stopped existing.

**Kind** · Medium

**On the Body** · The cure goes into the flesh: a grip closed under this lens does not open until told, a set bone holds through a fall, and a stance once taken has to be cut out of the ground. A fighter who sets a wound wrong carries the mistake to the grave.

**On the Spirit** · Written from the Core, an inscription is cured into what a thing is, so a seal stays sealed and a line of characters on a door is a fact about that door until the door is gone. Every sealing school in the four quarters trains on it, and each of them has one room it cannot open.

**On Fate** · An oath under this lens has no way back in it, and the Attraction Layer treats the sworn thing as already done: claims hold across generations, bonds hold past deaths, and a Domain's terms set as hard as law. There is a reason the old marriage contracts take a week to read.

**Spirit** · Temperance through permanence. *The confessors' reading:* commitment, in which what is held stays held.

**Alchemy** · One of the six named operations: it anchors and seals, and every Sealing chain on the bench runs under its law. The Bench files a Fixatio Draft as a Coagulation. Basalt takes it.

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Covalent cross-linking. Thermoset cure.

**Mechanism** · A thermoplastic can be melted and reformed indefinitely. A thermoset, once cured, has formed covalent bridges across its whole network and will char before it flows. Fixatio performs the cure. What it holds is held by the fact that the alternative configuration no longer exists as a reachable state, and force has nothing to do with it. A tendon, an inscription and a sworn word are all networks of a kind and all can be bridged past undoing, which is why the trade has never agreed on which road this Wellspring belongs to.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Fortitude, Resilience Anchoring. What is held by Fixatio stays held.

**Glyph** · `[Tp]` Topology; attested on twelve more.

**Failure** · Irreversibility. The cure is not undone by wanting it undone, and a Fixatio binding placed in error is a permanent feature of whatever it was placed on.

## Coagulatio · *The Returning Stone*

**The idea** · Settling round a seed. Scattered things find one grain and grow out from it, denser and far less wasteful than they were.

**Kind** · Wild

**On the Body** · Essence sets around one grain in the Shell the way sugar sets on a string, and what was spray becomes mass, so a punch loses almost nothing on its way out and the reserve sits denser in the same body. Start too many grains at once and the fighter is left with a fine heavy sludge in the arms and no way to spend it.

**On the Spirit** · From the Core a working sets around a chosen seed, one word or one grain of the target, and goes on growing after the hand has gone, so a seal thrown at noon may still be adding to itself by evening. It is slow work and it does not stop on request.

**On Fate** · Out here the seed is one person, and everything scattered around them starts to crystallize: hangers-on turn into a household, gossip into a reputation, three bad debts into one very large creditor. They rarely asked for any of it and generally cannot say when it stopped being optional.

**Spirit** · Temperance through re-forming. *The confessors' reading:* settling, the scattered self finding its seed.

**Alchemy** · One of the six named operations and the stage of Coagulation: a charge sets round one grain, and the grain grows on after the hand is gone. It deactivates cleanly under Preparation Failure.

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Crystallization from supersaturated solution. Nucleation and ordered growth.

**Mechanism** · A supersaturated solution holds more solute than equilibrium permits and will surrender it the instant a nucleation site appears, depositing ordered solid out of apparent nothing. Coagulatio is the material twin of Coagula: where that Wellspring gathers a soul, this one gathers substrate, pulling dispersed Essence out of suspension into a denser and far less wasteful phase.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Ardency Compression, Tempering Clarity. Essence becomes denser and harder to waste.

**Glyph** · `[Plr]` Pillar; attested on nine more.

**Failure** · Uncontrolled nucleation. Too many sites at once produces a fine disordered mass rather than a sound crystal, and the practitioner ends with density they cannot use.
> **The Crystalia note.** This is the same law that governs a Threshold. A melt cools into order only around seeds that survived the heat, and what Coagulatio describes in substrate is what Traits do in a Soul Crystal.
>
> *Whether a Coagulatio practitioner can work the law on their own seat is the oldest unsettled question in the trade. The Wellspring does not say no.*

## Materia Primordia · *The First Substance*

**The idea** · The first substance. One self and many forms, and a soul that finds it can become its own opposite and still be itself.

**Kind** · Wild

**On the Body** · At home the flesh re-lattices on command, hard as diamond for one exchange and slick as graphite for the next, so the whole contest turns on what you are wearing when the blow arrives. Ingrid Stenhammar will tell you the rest for the price of a drink.

**On the Spirit** · Inscribed on a thing, it can persuade that thing into another of its own lawful forms, so iron may be told it is rust. It goes no further than the thing already was, and nobody has yet talked a stone into being a bird.

**On Fate** · Here the many forms are a person's standing, and the lens lets someone be the widow in one town and the creditor in another, each claim true and each with its own bonds. The day the two meet under pressure, the world keeps whichever was on at the time.

**Spirit** · Temperance through origin. *The confessors' reading:* re-forming, the self discovered in another of its own shapes.

**Alchemy** · One of the six named operations: it re-lattices one Essence into another of its own forms. The Bench files a Materia Primordia Draft as a Coagulation.

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Allotropy. One element, several lattices, radically different materials.

**Mechanism** · Graphite and diamond are the same element. The difference is entirely in how the atoms are arranged, and it is the difference between a lubricant and the hardest natural substance known. Materia Primordia grants the Crystal access to its own polymorphism: the same Essence, re-latticed, expressing properties that appear to belong to different substances entirely.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Fortitude, Dominion Gravity. The body and the Domain gain mineral-weight authority.

**Glyph** · None primary. Attested on twelve, among them `[En]` Lattice, `[Th]` Foundation and `[Tp]` Topology.

**Failure** · Phase locking. Under sufficient pressure the lattice commits to one allotrope permanently, and the practitioner keeps whichever form they were wearing when the pressure came.

## Basilithe · *The Stone Will*

**The idea** · The stone will. One note the soul holds that nobody can retune.

**Kind** · Wild

**On the Body** · The Crystal's stiffness comes down into the bones and the body refuses to be shaken, stunned or struck out of rhythm, with shockwaves passing through a stance that holds its own pitch. Every so often somebody finds that pitch with a hammer, and the fighter rings like a bell and comes apart.

**On the Spirit** · The soul holds one note against any working that would retune it, so compulsion, false memory and imposed law slide off, and so does any new idea. Thrown, it is a dead pitch that stops an enemy's chant from resolving, and that is about all.

**On Fate** · A soul that cannot be retuned turns out to be a standard for other people: merchants trust their weights, quarrels get carried to their table, and a room drifts toward their pitch without noticing. Their oaths do not bend to new circumstances, which is admirable from a distance.

**Spirit** · Temperance through permanence. *The confessors' reading:* constancy, the soul's frequency refusing retuning.

**Alchemy** · The Standard: the one fixed measure every other weight is read against. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Natural frequency and stiffness-to-mass ratio. Quartz oscillator stability.

**Mechanism** · A body's resonant frequency is fixed by its stiffness and its mass, and a high-stiffness low-loss structure holds that frequency against forcing so reliably that a Measurewright with a cut quartz on a string will set her water clock by it. Basilithe raises the Crystal's stiffness until its resonance simply will not be driven off, which is a defense against every technique that works by imposing an external rhythm.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Resilience Hardening, Harmonics Stability. The soul's frequency locks and refuses retuning.

**Glyph** · `[Grv]` Gravity; attested on one more.

**Failure** · Resonant catastrophe. A frequency that cannot be shifted also cannot be detuned away from a forcing that happens to match it.

## Monolithion · *The Enduring Monument*

**The idea** · The monument. A self with no seams in it, which outlasts everything and stops being a person in a room and starts being a feature of the land.

**Kind** · Wild

**On the Body** · The slow loads that grind everyone else down, marching, hunger, a month on a wall, barely register in flesh with so few seams left in it, and a swung maul meets something closer to a cliff than a man. Getting such a person to turn around takes some planning.

**On the Spirit** · Thrown force comes out heavy and slow, more a hillside leaning on you than a bolt, and an inscription cut on this lens is still there when the wall it was cut on is not.

**On Fate** · Stay long enough and the land has to route around you: people give directions by you, Aura Pressure sits in the valley like weather, and your claim on that ground goes uncontested because nobody living remembers it otherwise. The holder may not have spoken in years.

**Spirit** · Temperance through memory. *The confessors' reading:* permanence, until presence becomes geological.

**Alchemy** · The great casting: one mass cooled so slowly it has no seam in it. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Grain growth and creep resistance. Single-crystal structure at geological timescale.

**Mechanism** · Polycrystalline metal fails at its grain boundaries under sustained high-temperature load, which is why the scholars' ideal part for such a load would be grown as one single crystal with no boundaries to fail along. Monolithion drives the Crystal toward that condition: fewer internal interfaces, enormous resistance to slow deformation, and a presence that behaves less like a person occupying a room than like a feature of the terrain.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Tolerance, Resilience Persistence, Dominion Gravity. Presence becomes geological.

**Glyph** · `[Inr]` Inertial; attested on one more.

> **Failure** · Brittle at scale. A structure with no grain boundaries has nothing to arrest a crack, and when Monolithion fails it fails across its entire section at once.

## Terranova · *The Living Earth*

**The idea** · The living earth. Mending by growing, every wound answered with new ground.

**Kind** · Wild

**On the Body** · A wound here is answered by growth, new mineral and new tissue laid down along the damage, so the fighter heals by extension and the old scar becomes the strongest part of the arm. They also get larger in the places they have been hurt, and tailors charge accordingly.

**On the Spirit** · In the Core, breakage seeds new structure, and a soul cracked by loss grows a wing it did not have. Thrown into someone else's wound it will grow what it is told, which only healers and torturers attempt, and the line between them is a long-running argument.

**On Fate** · What gets hurt around this person comes back thicker: a broken friendship regrows in a new shape, a burned village plants itself again at their feet, and a lost claim sprouts three small ones nearby. They cannot always tell you which of those they wanted.

**Spirit** · Temperance through birth. *The confessors' reading:* regrowth.

**Alchemy** · The metal tree in the still flask: a mass that mends by growing. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Biomineralization and self-healing composite. Nacre deposition; bacterial concrete.

**Mechanism** · Some structures do not get repaired. They grow their repair in place, laying down mineral along the damage front by the same process that built the original. Terranova gives the Crystal that behavior, which is why its practitioners heal by extension rather than by closure and why their scars are new tissue rather than sealed old tissue.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Regeneration, Harmonics Synergy. The body heals by growing rather than repairing.

**Glyph** · None primary. Attested on twelve, among them `[Sed]` Sediment, `[Grt]` Granite and `[Th]` Foundation.

**Failure** · Overgrowth. Deposition does not know when to stop, and the repaired region continues to accrete past the original geometry.

## Chthonica · *The Root Deep*

**The idea** · The root. Holding on by touching everything, so that whatever you stand on becomes yours to stand on.

**Kind** · Wild

**On the Body** · The Shell takes hold of whatever bears it with a countless fine grip, so a fighter stands on a sheer wall or a bucking deck as if it were a parlor floor and cannot be thrown off either, every blow going down into what they stand on. A wet deck or a fouled boot drops them with no warning at all.

**On the Spirit** · From the Core it roots a working to the place, and a ward done this way sits in the stone the way a tooth sits in a jaw. Dull to throw and a fine thing to leave behind, and Spirit Path scholars on this lens tend to stop traveling.

**On Fate** · The person and the ground become each other's claim: a family on its own land under this lens gets a hold that bailiffs, armies and good sense have failed to break, and a Domain raised there comes up out of the floor with its roots already in. Move them to a new country and they are slow to know what to do with their hands.

**Spirit** · Temperance through grounding. *The confessors' reading:* rootedness.

**Alchemy** · Lute, the humble seal: it holds because it touches everything. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Static friction and molecular adhesion. Interlocking asperity; setal van der Waals contact.

**Mechanism** · Grip owes nothing to weight. A gecko holds to glass through billions of hair-scale contacts each contributing an intermolecular attraction too small to measure alone. Chthonica multiplies the Shell's contact area with whatever surface bears it, converting proximity into anchorage, which is why its practitioners root to sheer stone and moving decks with equal indifference.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Absorption, Dexterity Economy, Dominion Gravity. The practitioner roots to whatever surface they stand on.

**Glyph** · `[Sed]` Sediment; attested on three more.

**Failure** · Contamination. The mechanism depends on clean contact, and a fouled interface releases without warning and without a partial stage.

## Tarturon · *The Fire of Reckoning*

**The idea** · Reckoning. Power that rises with what you believe you owe, held on the Plane of Fate as a debt whether or not anyone else would recognize it, and that nothing can smother once it is lit.

**Kind** · Fate

**On the Body** · On the fist it seeds a burning that needs no air and reads the debt rather than the muscle, so a man who believes he deserves the rope hits like a furnace. It is dearer and rougher here than at home, and the man who forgives himself finds nothing left to light.

**On the Spirit** · The Core turns the sum owed into authorship, so a thrown working lands as a verdict its own caster has already passed on themselves and hits as hard as that verdict was. Talk yourself out of the guilt and the working will not catch, which makes this a poor road for the comfortable.

**On Fate** · At home it reads the whole account on the Attraction Layer, every oath broken and every death unpaid, and pulls power up those lines in proportion to what the holder believes is owed, so a Domain on this lens is a courtroom with one defendant. No creditor has to agree and none can call it off, and the sum that pays for the fire grows with the burning.

**Spirit** · Temperance through ordeal; correction through consequence, a soul's output rising when it believes it deserves to pay.

**Alchemy** · A charge that carries its own fire: once lit, nothing smothers it. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Yield criterion and post-yield response. A reaction carrying its own oxidizer.

**Mechanism** · Below yield a material returns to shape and the load leaves no record. Above it, behavior changes kind, and the response scales with the load applied rather than with anything the material chose. Tarturon couples output to moral weight through exactly that curve, and the load it reads is a debt on the Attraction Layer (a soul's own share of fate): what the person believes they owe, pulling on them as hard as any sworn bond, with or without a creditor to agree. Like thermite (a mix that brings its own burning air) it supplies its own oxidizer, which is the mechanical reason the reaction cannot be smothered once initiated.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Ardency Flux under guilt-amplification, Tempering Overflow, Resilience Fortification. Output spikes under moral weight.

**Glyph** · None attested in the Index (see Discrepancies of Record on the hub).

> **Failure** · Runaway. The load that drives the reaction is generated by the reaction, and nothing in the mechanism provides a stopping condition.

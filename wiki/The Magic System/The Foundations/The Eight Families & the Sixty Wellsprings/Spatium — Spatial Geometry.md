# Spatium — Spatial Geometry

*Spatial geometry · distance and position*

The Lantern Run is forty leagues of road in two days with a lit lamp in your hand, and for nine years nobody could touch the Sedgelys. Wilkin and Wynn, man and wife, ran it a stride apart with a violet glow passing back and forth between them that the eye kept refusing to look at, and a sound like two bells in sympathy, *nnnng, nnnng*, one of them always rested because the other had just paid for it, until the odds-men stopped quoting them at all. In the tenth year somebody's hired man put a crossbow bolt through Wilkin's thigh at the Hollow Mile. Wynn never decided anything. The gradient ran, she emptied into him where she stood, and the crowd at the post saw her fold up in the road forty yards short with her lamp still burning, and saw him sitting up behind her, bleeding and fresh as a colt and howling at her to stop.

> The smallest Family and the strangest. Spatium treats a thing's **location** as no less governable than its shape, and its three Wellsprings are the Continuum's three honest answers to the question of how something gets from here to there without crossing the space between.

**Stat Affinity** · *Dominion (Rift, Projection) · Harmonics (Synergy, Suppression) · Tempering (Conversion, Processing) · Gnosis (Analysis)*

*Each Wellspring below is one type of Aether. Analogue and Mechanism give what that type does to a Soul Crystal. **Alchemy** gives the principle it stands for at the bench, and **Spirit** the transformation it works on the soul, each a road of Temperance (the climb of a soul from Stage to Stage).*

## Transmutatio · *The Law of Change*

**Analogue** · Change of basis. Coordinate transformation under conservation.

**Mechanism** · The same object described in two frames yields two entirely different sets of numbers and one unchanged object. Nothing is created or lost in a transformation; the quantities are merely re-expressed. Transmutatio applies this to failed workings, which are Essence in an inconvenient basis with none of it destroyed, and the Wellspring supplies the transformation that makes them usable again.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Ardency Conversion, Tempering Processing. Failed workings become fuel rather than wounds.

**Alchemy** · One of the six named operations: it re-expresses a thing in another basis, nothing created or lost. The Bench files a Transmutatio Draft as a composite, and it is volatile: under Preparation Failure it does not deactivate at all.

**Spirit** · Temperance through motion.

**Failure** · Basis loss. The practitioner forgets which frame they started in, and a transformation with no reference frame does not return anything recognizable.

## Transference · *The Living Exchange*

**Analogue** · Resonant energy transfer between coupled oscillators. Flow along a potential gradient.

**Mechanism** · Two oscillators tuned to the same frequency and held near each other exchange energy without a conductor between them, and the coupling strength falls off sharply with separation. Transference makes the Attraction Layer such an oscillator. Essence moves to an ally because the two Crystals are in tune and near, with nobody pushing, and a gradient does the rest.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Harmonics Synergy, Projection, Tempering Conversion. Essence routes to allies and absorbs ambient Wellspring energy through relational proximity.

**Alchemy** · The tincture shared: two matched vessels, one giving and one taking, with no pour between them. *(the trade's reading)*

**Spirit** · Temperance through empathy.

> **Failure** · Reverse coupling. A gradient runs both directions, and a Transference practitioner beside a depleted ally will empty into them without deciding to.

## Eclipseron · *The Shadowed Star*

**Analogue** · Occultation and transit photometry. The information carried by absorption lines.

**Mechanism** · A hidden planet is found by the dip in its star's brightness long before anyone sees it, and a spectrum's dark lines carry more composition data than its bright continuum. Eclipseron tunes perception to the negative: the practitioner reads what has been blocked, subtracted, or withheld, and derives from the shape of the absence the shape of the thing casting it.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Gnosis Analysis, Harmonics Suppression, Dominion Rift. The practitioner reads reality by what is missing from it.

**Alchemy** · The dead head read: what stayed behind in the retort tells you what left it. *(the trade's reading)*

**Spirit** · Temperance through eclipse.

**Failure** · False transit. Not every dimming is an occultation, and a practitioner committed to reading absence will eventually construct an object out of a gap that had nothing behind it.

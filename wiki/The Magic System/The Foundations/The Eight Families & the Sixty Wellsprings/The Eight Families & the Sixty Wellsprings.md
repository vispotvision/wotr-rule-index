# The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings

> *"What burns in the crucible burns in the one who tends it."*
>
> *The oldest saying of the bench, older than any register.*
>
> **Third edition.** The first edition gave every one of the sixty **a physical analogue, a mechanism in that analogue's own terms, and a failure mode following from the mechanism rather than from the mood.** The second added what the trade has always known and the Register was too proud to set down on the same page: **what each of the sixty means at the bench and what it does to the soul.** This edition turns the page right way up. Every entry now opens on the idea, the transformation and the principle, and the physics follows as the scholars' explanation of them. **The physics has not moved and no figure has changed.** Only the order has, and the Measurewrights have lodged their objection in the usual place.

Nobody on the Cold Tun bridge needs the Sixty explained, because their grandmothers saw Sanne Ruddock and Ond Gravell. Same Stage (a rung of the soul's sixteen-step climb), same Grade (the letter an assay gives a stat) on the sheet, and both banks packed to the reeds with the green sashes of the Hot Tun Ladies and the slate caps of the Quarrymen, with a man selling fried dough off a pole between them. The bridge stands where a hot spring comes up through old bedrock, so two kinds of Aether (the raw stuff of magic in the world around you) pool under that one span, and each fighter stood in the same air and pulled a different one. Sanne breathed in Pyreveil and came up deep ember-orange from the ribs outward, skin flushed and bone-dry, heat-shimmer climbing both arms until the spit boiled off her lip. Ond breathed in Petralon and went the gray of a wet anvil, and the dust on the deck sagged down around his boots with a long settling groan, and everyone on the bridge felt him get heavier without being able to say how. Her fire broke over him, and he stood in it. His fist went into her guard like a dropped lintel, and she laughed with blood on her teeth, because her body had stopped minding. For the length of a long sermon neither of them could do one thing about the other, and the crowd sang all through it.

Then both bills came due inside a single breath. Sanne got both hands round his wrist and hauled, straight outward, in tension, the one direction Petralon does not answer, and the man who had just stood in a bath of fire parted at the shoulder.

He did not bend first. Stone does not. And the heat she had kept banked in her own tissue to do it had to leave by whatever was nearest, which was her palms, and she stood there on the bridge with her hands cooking and her mouth wide open while Ond Gravell, one-armed and roaring, fell on her and took them both over the rail into the Tun. Draws keep the stakes at Cold Tun. The bookies bought new roofs.

Two fighters, one bridge, one breath of the same air. The whole of the difference was which idea each of them had let govern their Aether, what it did to the soul that took it, and what it would eventually charge. Every child in the Four Quarters already half knows this, being a *Fixatio child* or a *Tarturon child* by the day of the sixty they were born on, which is stated before their age is. The rest of this page is the long version, and it is worth the lamp oil.

## What a Wellspring Is

**A Wellspring is an idea that changes a soul.** Each of the sixty stands for one transformation, alchemical and spiritual at once: fire that renews, the breaking point, forgiveness, the honest mirror, the cup of forgetting, the first substance. Aether comes in sixty types because there are sixty of these ideas for it to carry.

You can see a Wellspring as a source of power you pull from, and on the street everybody does. They have reason to, since the ember really is in the ground at Cold Tun and you really can fill up on it. The better description, the one the benches and the confessors held long before there were registers, is **the governing idea that transforms how a soul uses Aether.** Your soul was already potent before any Wellspring touched it. A Wellspring puts **a lens** on that magic: it takes what is already there, brings it to a point, and turns the way the power moves in you.

That is why the Sixty matter more than almost anything else on a sheet. A Stage says how far up a soul has climbed. A Wellspring says what the climb is doing to it, and two fighters of one Stage and one Grade can stand on one bridge and be two entirely different weather systems. The Wellspring also sets the bill, because a law taken up as your way of living goes on charging you in its own coin.

### The lens by Path

**A soul meets the world through three layers, and a Path is the one it has learned to lean on.** The Body Path leans on the Aether Shell and the Physical World, the Spirit Path on the Essence Core and the Soul Plane, and the Fate Path on the Attraction Layer and the Plane of Fate, what has to be so before anything can happen. [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Three Paths Routing, Recognition and the Gate|The Three Paths]] has the whole account. A Wellspring is the lens a soul bases its tempering on, and **it shapes Essence differently on each Path**, in the terms of whichever layer is doing the work. One Aether in three people makes three different fighters, and every entry in this register says how.

| Wellspring | On the Body | On the Spirit | On Fate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pyreveil** (Wild) | The heat lives in the tissue, blows burn on contact and the body stops minding fire | Thrown fire that burns what is false in a thing before what is solid | On its best day a claim can set light to what stands in its way, provided it was already dry: a rival's hired second, a grain store under old thatch and, only at the end of a life, an army's reasons for staying in the field. The heat leaves through the holder's own tissue |
| **Absolution** (Spirit) | Old injuries stop costing what they cost. No heat and no glow, only a body that has stopped carrying its history | Lets the old poison out of a soul, one's own or a willing other's | A debt or grudge can be let go and the thing it was pulling on stops pulling, and at the end of long confession a host held in the line by an old wrong may wake with no clear reason to charge. It takes all the holder's strength and fails more often than not |
| **Benediction** (Fate) | A bond freely given lends the one blessed a real measure of steadiness, felt in the hands and the footing | A working carries the weight of the bond behind it | A bond the world weighs as a real thing, and an oath under it bears load. The one it is given to fights with the odds spoken for in one place that matters, the spear going a hand wide or the bridge holding for that column, and the giver pays in their own blood and years |

**A blow's character comes from Wellspring and Path.** Give two hitters the same Flux at the same Grade, one under Pyreveil and one under Nihiloth, and the first blow burns and ruptures while the second ends and quiets things. Hand that Pyreveil to a thrower on the Spirit Path and it is a third blow again, arriving from sixty paces. A Fate fighter's Pyreveil is mostly reach and a room that catches, and on the one day in a long while that they pull a thread it is a fourth: an ordinary cut, arriving because a strap has already burned through. The older books put all of this down to an inborn bias in the soul toward one Family or another. The trade has given that up, having never once found the bias in anyone who had not already spent ten years under the Wellspring it was supposed to explain.

**Every Wellspring has a home, and a home is never a lock.** A Wellspring's kind is a qualifier naming the plane it belongs to, so the home Path of a Wild Wellspring is the Body, of a Spirit Wellspring the Spirit, and of a Wellspring of Fate the Fate Path, while a Medium Wellspring is at home on all three (the [[#The Four Encounter Types|four kinds]] are set out further down). Any Path can use any Wellspring. **At home it harmonizes cleanly and can reach double the effect with years of mastery. Away from home it works in that layer's terms, at lower-quality resonance and higher Essence cost, and reaches half again at most.** Such a lens still changes how that soul uses Aether, and plenty of famous fighters are famous for exactly that clash. A Spirit Wellspring on the Body Path still gives no fire: it changes what the body can bear. The benches keep a standing bet on the first soul to pass half again away from home, and it has never paid out.

**The lens brings forward what is already there.** More of the idea arrives in every blow and every working. The Grade on the sheet does not move and no stat points appear, because a lens never added any. What grows is how much of the burn, or the ending, or the stubborn weight is in the thing that hits you.

**It starts small, and only years reach double.** Wenna Corry of Tallow Row, a lamp-lighter's girl who went down the Body Path with her fists, paid a year's wages for a bottle of Ignivale at sixteen, and for her first year the lens gave her about what a good pair of bellows gives a smith: her wicks caught a shade sooner and a shade hotter than her arm alone could have managed. She lived with it for twenty-four years. By forty she could break a man's jaw on the Tallow Row steps and his beard would be alight before his teeth landed, and the bookies stopped asking whether she had been drinking and started asking what she charged. Her cousin Perrin took the same Ignivale down the Spirit Path, throwing sparks across the Row for a living, and has been stuck at half again since before she caught up with him, paying more for each spark than she pays for a jaw. Each soul has said so throughout, in the window nobody else can see (the soul's report of its own numbers to its owner, in square brackets on the page), and this is how it reads:

```
[Ignivale: harmonized. Lens on the Body, at home: +8%]
[Ignivale: harmonized, 24 years. Lens on the Body, at home: +100%]
[Ignivale: harmonized, 31 years. Lens on the Spirit, away: +50%]
```

Those figures are a sample from two sheets, every soul's road runs at its own speed, and only the two ceilings are law. The rule under them is plain: **a lens grows the way a Trait grows, by being lived with.** A Draft can hand you the lens. The years are yours to live.

**Each lens grows on its own.** A soul with three Wellsprings has three lenses at three stages of mastery, all under the Capacity rules further down this page, and compounding within a Family is described there.

**You can see it.** A Family sets the base color of a person's aura, a Wellspring adds its texture and its own sound and smell, and a Class or a big Trait adds a shape. A trained eye at fifty paces can tell you the Family, make a good guess at the Wellspring, and tell you from how steadily the color sits whether the lens is two years old or twenty.

*Measurewrights at Sealhall have spent a long time arguing about what exactly gets doubled, since the Grade holds still and no fork rings a lens. Their working answer is to ask the person who got hit.*

### How you come by one

There are four roads, and most people who have more than one Wellspring have walked more than one of them.

**At a Core, in a dungeon.** Most Wellspring Cores lie in dungeons, the living underground places that grow down in floors around a Core with something guarding the heart. On a held site a person can sit at the edge of a known Wellspring, with a keeper and a baseline taken first, and come away harmonized, at a price by the hour. In a place nobody holds, a crew that goes too deep takes Wellspring Baptism whether it wanted one or not. This is the road dungeon luck runs on, and the reason a nobody with a good rope can come home changed. See [[The Bestiary/Dungeons|Dungeons]].

**From a school.** A sect teaches the rites for its Wellspring, along with the Releases, Domains, techniques and skills that money cannot buy, and in return you wear its colors, sing its songs and inherit its grudges. A school's style is that lens worn by a hundred people at once, which is why you can spot it across a square.

**From a Draft or a blessing.** A Draft puts the lens in a bottle, and the great houses hoard the best ones. A blessing is given by a higher spirit, an Archon, an Epoch or a person of higher Temperance, for whom it costs next to nothing. Either way it can land in a soul that could never have reached it alone. A blessing shows as its own line on the sheet, and because the giver wrote that line it can lie, so what reads as a gift for years may be a curse with terms you did not read.

**By birth.** Some souls arrive already harmonized, by bloodline or by generations of living downwind of a concentration. The birth-day name is another matter entirely: a *Fixatio child* is any child born on Fixatio's day, and being called one has never once sealed anything.

### One Aether, three readings

Every one of the sixty can be read three ways, and each reading is a whole profession that thinks the other two are amateurs. These are readings and not Paths: a confessor, a bench hand and a Measurewright can all stand around one Body Path hitter and describe three different things, none of which is her Path.

**As a spiritual transformation.** The confessor's reading, and the oldest: what taking this Aether into the soul does to the person who survives it. Temperance is the climb of a soul from Stage to Stage, and every Wellspring is a road on that climb, so the Fracture register names Pyreveil *Temperance through burning* and Absolution *Temperance through forgiveness*, and it gives all sixty such a name. Where the confessors read a road otherwise, the entry sets their reading beside the name, marked as such and argued over at every deathbed. To pass through a Wellspring is to be unmade just enough to keep going. Every Wellspring touches all three layers of the Crystal, rarely evenly, whichever Path the soul is on. The **Essence Core** decides how the soul burns, heals or empties under that law, the **Aether Shell** how the law moves through and around the body, and the **Attraction Layer** how reality, memory and people bind to the soul once the law has taken hold.

**As an alchemical principle.** The bench's reading: what that same idea does to matter in a vessel. Each of the sixty is a living process, a law of transformation, and the discipline of Alchemetrica (the working of Wellspring law upon matter) is built on that fact. The reagents are the nouns and the Wellspring is the verb. Six of the sixty are at once Wellspring, cosmic principle and named bench operation, and four more lend their law to a stage of the Great Work (the seven-stage discipline an alchemist lays on the matter and on himself at once). The rest have no operation filed under their name at the Bench, though every working alchemist has an opinion about what each of them is the principle of, and those opinions appear in the entries, marked as the trade's reading and open to a fight.

**As a law of the world.** The Measurewright's reading, and the youngest: the physical analogue, the mechanism, the failure. Scholars have an account of everything, this is theirs, and it has the great merit of predicting the bill. The rest of this page gives it in full.

The correspondence is architecture rather than poetry, which is what Alchemetrica means when it says that what burns in the crucible burns in the one who tends it.

| At the bench | Wellspring | What it lends |
|---|---|---|
| **Named operations, the six** | **Dissolution** | Unravels cohesion into base frequencies |
| | **Coagulatio** | Gathers what is dispersed round a seed and sets it |
| | **Sublimatio** | Elevates without passing through the states between |
| | **Transmutatio** | Re-expresses a thing in another basis, nothing created or lost |
| | **Fixatio** | Anchors and seals |
| | **Materia Primordia** | Re-lattices one Essence into another of its own forms |
| **Stage of the Work: Calcination** | **Cinerion** | Strong dry heat drives off what is volatile and leaves what is fixed |
| **Stage of the Work: Separation** | **Judicium** | Discernment: the parts told apart and taken apart |
| **Stage of the Work: Fermentation** | **Rebirthine** | Breakdown pays for growth |
| **Stage of the Work: Distillation** | **Sublimare** | Parting by boiling point, the spirit risen and returned |

Dissolution and Coagulation are stages of the Work too, on the Wellsprings that share their names. **Conjunction has no Wellspring of its own.** It runs on Attraction, and a Conjunction corrupted injures Attraction itself. Calcination and Distillation are bench operations and no soul harmonizes with either as such; a soul harmonizes with Cinerion or Sublimare, and the bench borrows from them.

The old masons knew a shorter list and knew it empirically, with no account of why: basalt takes Coagula and Fixatio, quartz amplifies Fractura and Luminalis, iron conducts Manganthra and Aurevane, gold carries Benediction and Sublimatio, and obsidian holds Tenebra and Oblivara.

## How the Scholars Explain It

> *"The Wellspring is neither gift nor spirit. It is a law that was already running before you noticed it, and harmonization is the act of agreeing to be measured by it."*
>
> *From the preface to the First Physical Register.*

Everything below this line is the account the Measurewrights (the guild's certified assessors of practitioners and workings) give, and every figure on this page lives in it. It may be wrong about what a Wellspring ultimately is, as the confessors will tell you, and it has never yet been wrong about what one will charge.

### Why the Sixty Were Re-Sorted

The older registers arranged the Wellsprings by feeling. Grief stood with grief. Fire stood with fire because both were warm. It produced a beautiful taxonomy and a useless one, because a practitioner who knew that Cinerion and Pyreveil were both House of the Ember still had no idea why one of them refilled a reserve and the other emptied it faster.

The physics-first sorting corrects that at the cost of intuition. Wellsprings that shared a House for reasons of sorrow are now scattered across five Families. An ember and a stone stand together. Both facts are correct, and both offend the eye of anyone trained on the old registers. Materia Primordia and Tarturon belong in one Family because both are, mechanically, the assessment the Continuum (the whole lawful order of reality) makes of what a soul is made of and how much of it survives contact, and how reckoning feels has nothing to do with it. The sorting also leaks, and the register says so: Sublimatio and Sublimare are phase changes filed under Mechanics, Absolution and Penance are heat treatment and hammer work, Transference and Eclipseron are coupled oscillators and starlight filed under Spatium, and Tenebra, Mirithane and Oblivara are optics filed under Limina. The analogue explains how a Wellspring behaves, and where the two part, the Fracture's Family assignment stands.
> *A warning to the devotional reader. Naming the physics does not demote the Wellspring. The Aether was always obeying a law. The Measurewrights have simply written the law down.*
>
> *A warning to the Measurewright. Writing the law down does not explain why a soul that takes in Absolution stops hating its father. The Families are still sorted by physics, because it works. The entries have stopped pretending the physics came first.*

### What a Wellspring Physically Is

In the scholars' terms a Wellspring is **a type of Aether that carries a fixed law.** Aether is what practitioners pull from the environment around them, and it comes in sixty types, fixed and counted, and each type affects the Soul Crystal differently. Take in Pyreveil and your reserve burns hotter while your body stops minding the heat. Take in Hypnather and you sleep like the dead and wake structurally different. Take in Fractura and you will never again look at a bridge, a friend or your own left wrist without seeing where it is going to go. A type of Aether is not stored anywhere and it does not belong to anyone. It runs whether observed or not, in the way that a pressure gradient runs whether or not something is standing in it.

The old registers call each type a **current**, and the trade still does, because Aether moves over ground the way wind moves over a field. Keep the word and lose any picture of a pipe. Nobody has ever laid a Wellspring on or shut one off at a valve, and the Measurewright who tells you otherwise is about to send you a bill.

**The road through the soul** is the same for all sixty. Aether is pulled in from the world, refined in the Soul Crystal into Essence (Aether stamped with identity, the soul's own substance, the thing a fighter holds and spends), and driven out through the Aether Shell (the Crystal's outer layer, which turns inner power into outward force). What differs is the type. Where you stand therefore matters twice: rich ground fills you faster and thin ground starves you, and the ground decides which of the sixty is on offer. On the street this is plain sense. A brawler will say she has the ember in her, or the stone, or that she took the water at Saint Orrin's pond, and she will say he only fights like that on hot ground. On a Guild roll all of that is harmonization, which comes next.
> **Harmonization is phase-locking.** The Aether Shell adopts the characteristic behavior of one type of Aether so that Essence passing outward through the Shell is shaped by that law before it reaches the world. Nothing is added to the practitioner. The conduit is retuned, and everything that passes through it afterward carries the retuning. This is the lens, in the scholars' words.

The Shell is only where the instruments catch it, since all Essence leaves that way. On the Spirit Path and the Fate Path the law seats hardest in the Essence Core or the Attraction Layer, which is the lens working in that layer's terms.

This is why harmonization changes what a number means without changing the number. A Fortitude of B-Grade harmonized to Monolithion does not become a larger Fortitude. It becomes a Fortitude that behaves like a load-bearing mass rather than like tissue, with the failure curve of stone rather than the failure curve of flesh. **The magnitude stays where it was and the constitutive law changes.**

Most practitioners awaken three to five Wellsprings in a lifetime. Serious adepts harmonize with twelve or more. Those who embody all sixty are called **Principals of Temperance**, and there are not many of them, and you will not meet one.

### Harmonization, Traits, and the glyph

*Three things bias a Shell and they are constantly mistaken for one another.*

**A harmonization retunes the conduit.** It is reversible in principle, it degrades if Capacity (the limit on how many Wellsprings a soul can hold at once) is exceeded, and it shapes everything passing outward for as long as it holds. The law belongs to the Aether and the practitioner has it on loan.

**A Trait biases the conduit permanently.** It is a law the soul itself carries, held in the Essence Core (the Crystal's inner layer, holding identity, memory and Traits), and it writes into the Shell's conductivity map rather than borrowing a law from outside. It cannot be dropped to make room, and it has a level of its own that rises with use and with the bearer's overall Level and skill. This is why two practitioners harmonized to the same Wellspring produce recognizably different output: the Aether supplies one law and the Core supplies another, and the working is what comes out of both.

**A glyph is a boundary condition.** It creates nothing. The Wellspring's law was already running; the glyph moves a boundary and the law does what it has always done on the far side of the new boundary. Every entry below should be read with that in mind. Nobody in this register makes fire. They establish conditions under which the ordinary behavior of heat produces fire where it previously did not, and the fire that results obeys every rule fire has ever obeyed, including the ones nobody thought about.
> **Sites do not check for a Crystal.** Aether pooling in a valley acts on everything standing in the valley, and a Class Ø (never-woken) soul's Shell is sealed and cannot phase-lock, but it has an Essence Core like anyone else. Sustained residence at Active Concentration or above therefore does to a shepherd what it does to an adept, minus the harmonization and minus the vocabulary.
>
> What it leaves behind is a **Trait**, formed by Wellspring Convergence, in a body that will never be screened and a life that has no framework for it. Every quarter has villages downwind of a concentration where one family has been strange for six generations, and the strangeness is lawful, inheritable, and entirely undocumented.

## Where a Wellspring Pools

**A Wellspring is a type of Aether and no kind of place. But a Wellspring pools.**

The way groundwater is not stored in a specific container but absolutely collects in aquifers, a type of Aether concentrates where the substrate is Aether-conductive, where competing types are absent, and where enough time has passed for it to saturate the local field **until the physical environment begins expressing the law passively.**
> **A Wellspring site is the aquifer. The Aether is the water table.**

| Condition | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Conductive substrate** | The era-one emplacement described in *The Four Ceilings* laid the mineral foundation during the First Eon |
| **Absence of competing types** | **This is why the New World's arc holds so many pristine sites at densities the Old World has not seen since the Antediluvian Calendar.** *Nobody has been pulling from them. The veins are full. The elven old-ground beside the arc is the opposite case: its Wellsprings have been pulled on since the first Eon* |
| **Time** | Enough for the Aether to saturate the field **until the environment starts expressing the law as a passive condition of the geography** |

### Cores, keepers and closed ground

Common speech calls any site where a Wellspring's Aether pools a **Core**, and most Cores lie in **dungeons**: places a Core has made around itself, or that have grown up around one, going down in floors with something guarding the heart. A dungeon is alive and learning, and scholars argue whether it thinks. The rest of the Cores are said to lie in vast territories, with the weight on "said", and [[The Bestiary/Dungeons|Dungeons]] has the whole account. Surveyors keep *Wellspring Core* for the top line of the Density Scale below, an Essence Core is a layer of your own Crystal, and a Measurewright who hears a ferryman call the warm pond behind the tannery the Pyreveil Core will explain all of this at length until the ferryman charges him double. This register uses the surveyor's word, and you should use the ferryman's if you want to get across the river.

**A worked Core is a dungeon someone cleared and now holds. It recharges and it has keepers.** A site people have pulled from and tended for generations refills at the rate its ground replaces what has been taken, which the numbers below will give you, and somebody always stands at the gate of it: a sect, a village, a Guild house, three brothers and a fence. Keepers charge by the hour or by the bottle. They strike, they overcharge, they play favorites, and they can **close** the site, turning everyone off it and working the ground shut, which no amount of Stage talks them out of unless the Stage is prepared to do it by force in front of witnesses.

**The counter this hands you is the oldest in the book.** Take the fight off rich ground. A fighter fat on a Core loses the fat a league down the road, on **salted** ground (worked by ward-cutters and hedge-workers so that Aether will not pool on it, and it gives back little for a season), on **bled** ground (pulled empty by a long fight or a greedy crew, just before you arrive if somebody has planned it), or on ground that was simply thin to begin with. Half the famous upsets in the brackets were won in the week before the fight, by someone quietly paying off the people who choose the field.

### The second mode · Object Wellsprings

Aether of one type captured, bound, or naturally accumulated inside a vessel produces **a contained domain**, carried in a sword, a relic, or a formation old enough that **the material and the Aether are indistinguishable.**
> **Crevice Shale, T9, the Crevice itself**: forbidden to move or hold or trade. *A law about a rock, and the only such law the Accord has ever written.* **The most extreme Object Wellspring in the register.**
>
> **The critical distinction between the two modes is interface geometry.**
>
> **A site has a perimeter.** The density gradient gives warning. The surveyor feels the Aether thickening as they approach the core.
>
> **An object is binary. You are holding it or you are not**, and its internal domain contacts the Shell at zero distance. If the Shell is incompatible, **the resulting conversation was not authorized and cannot be escaped without severing the causal relationship at the Plane of Fate (above the lower realities, the control of Attraction and Obsession and the workings of reality itself) level.**
>
> *The colloquial term for an Object Wellspring whose Attraction Layer (the layer that holds bonds and claims) will not accept a termination event is cursed.*

## The Four Encounter Types

*The physics-first sorting tells the practitioner which Family governs their output. The encounter-type classification tells the surveyor which direction the danger arrives from. It is also the kind on every entry below, which names the plane a Wellspring is at home on and locks nobody out of it.*
> **The classification rests on a single question: where does the effect originate, and where does it arrive?**

**More than half of the Sixty are physical, and most of the rest are not physical at all.** A Wild Wellspring is a thing in the world: fire in the ground, stone that moves, lightning, a solution crystallizing out of itself on the bench. You can point at it, and a practitioner who takes it up shapes their inner Essence around it. A Spirit Wellspring is a change in a soul: forgiveness, forgetting, a failure owned. There is nothing to point at, and it gives its bearer no fire to throw. It usually does little or nothing to the physical world, though an edge case will always find a Measurewright: a Thaloré grief can be felt across a room, and the argument over whether that counts is older than the Guild. A Wellspring of Fate is a change in what holds: a bond, a debt, a name, the odds. There is nothing to point at there either, only what keeps happening afterward. A Medium Wellspring is at home on all three planes and crosses the Veil between them as it works. Every one of the sixty changes the soul that takes it up; the difference is whether the world shows it too, and how soon. A spiritual entry below still carries an analogue, which is the scholars' picture of what is happening in the soul, and no one has yet watched a forgiveness anneal.

|---|---|---|

The metal was there. **The Aether reshapes it, moves it, compresses it, grows it, burns it**, and whoever harmonizes with it shapes their Essence around the thing itself. *The landscape is visibly altered. The surveyor's body is a reliable instrument. | **Spirit** *Soul Plane dominant* | The Aether works **on the soul and nowhere else. It has no fire to give.** The Essence Core is the active layer. A discharge on this ground is the person's own Essence, driven out by a soul that is being changed. *The landscape looks entirely normal. The surveyor's environment is a reliable instrument. | **Fate** *Plane of Fate dominant* | The Aether works **on what binds: bonds, debts, names and odds.** The Attraction Layer is the active layer, and there is no burn in the hands and nothing on the ground to see. *The landscape looks normal and the instruments read normal. But luck goes strange, oaths bite, strangers keep meeting, and the surveyor's plans keep working out in ways the surveyor did not plan. Neither body nor environment is a reliable instrument, and the only one that reads this ground is a written log kept over days* | **Six.** Somnalis, Oblation; Benediction; Oblivara; Transference; Tarturon |
| **Medium** *At home on all three, Veil-crossing* | The Aether is at home on all three planes and still crosses the Veil, operating **in every direction simultaneously**, in the liminal threshold between planes. **Water and Light are the primary Medium elements.** *A Phreatis practitioner at a riverbank is simultaneously reshaping the physical water and being reshaped by what the water remembers. The observer is altered by the observation. Neither body nor Crystal provides a stable reference point* | **Six.** Phreatis, Aurevane, Luminalis, Anamnesis, Fixatio, Mortalis |

### The nested depth model

**The three planes are nested, not stacked.**
> **The Plane of Fate stands above the lower realities**, the lattice upon which all possibility rests. A surveyor measures from the ground he stands on and calls that depth, and a theologian points the other way, and they mean the same place. **The Soul Plane lies between**, the stratum through which abstract law becomes a soul's felt truth. **The Physical Plane sits at the surface** (the Material Plane is the same place under its other name), where truth becomes event, body, ruin, and mountain range. Only the last two are realities in any careful sense: what stands above them is everything that flows, every possibility, idea and concept, and "plane" is a courtesy.
>
> *Fate is a precursor. A tide that falls. What is decided at Fate-depth cascades through the Soul Plane and manifests on the Physical Plane as event.*
>
> This is why the four types produce different threat profiles.
>
> **Wild** operates at the surface. The danger is physical and legible.
>
> **Spirit** operates from the middle layer, **and the cascade runs outward**: the Crystal fires, the Shell expresses, and the body, where it is reached at all, manifests what the Soul Plane authored. *What reaches the surveyor comes from no outside blow. They are being authored from inside.*
>
> **Fate** operates from above both lower realities, **and the cascade has to come through both of the others.** *By the time the surveyor can see what was decided, it has already happened to them, and it looks like luck.*
>
> **Medium** operates at the Veil itself, **the threshold where one plane's law softens into another's.** Every layer moves at once, **and the movement is reciprocal.**

## The Density Scale

*The encounter type determines the direction of the danger. The density determines its magnitude.*

| Density | Condition |
|---|---|
| **Ambient Saturation** | The most common condition across the mapped world, and no kin to Aetheric Saturation, the lethal flood. **A Sage can tune to it. An ordinary person feels vaguely uneasy or peaceful.** The landscape carries subtle signs |
| **Active Concentration** | Dense enough for persistent environmental effects. Wild sites show altered terrain; Spirit sites produce involuntary change in the soul, with the body sometimes following; Fate sites produce runs of coincidence that nobody believes before the third; **Medium sites produce bidirectional effects indistinguishable from illness or hallucination.** **A practitioner remaining too long receives Wellspring Baptism whether they wanted it or not.** *Most concentrations on the New World's arc were found during the Voyager Era; the elven old-ground's have been pulled on since the first Eon* |
| **Veil-Thin Nexus** | **The Veil between planes thins.** Spiritual entities become visible. Causal threads are perceptible. **Time feels wrong.** Bifold Perception onset without training, involuntary Soul Drift, Echo Beast manifestation |
| **Wellspring Core** | **The Aether's origin point. The aquifer's source.** Extraordinarily rare, and mostly unmapped. Nearly all of them lie at the heart of a dungeon, and the richest untouched ones are on the New World's arc. **The physical and metaphysical environments are indistinguishable, and the law the Wellspring carries is the only law operating.** *Walking into a Core unprepared is Wellspring Baptism at maximum intensity with no exit plan* |

### The Density Scale in Numbers

*The condition is what a surveyor writes down. The number is what the ground will actually give.*

One EU is one megajoule, so a density in EU per cubic meter is an energy density, and an energy density is a pressure. **A density in EU/m³ is the same figure in megapascals.** That one identity is why the scale is a ceiling and not a mood. A boundary cannot be pushed into ground at a pressure the ground does not carry, and what is standing in the ground has a strength measured in the same unit.

**What a site will actually give.** Aether crosses a site the size of a hall in about a thousand seconds. That is the interval in which the ground replaces what has been taken from it. So a site yields at most **a thousandth of its density, per second**. The supply rate in AU/s (Aether Units per second, a rate of output) per cubic meter is the density in EU/m³ divided by a thousand, and the ceiling on a practitioner's sustained output and refill is that rate across the volume his field reaches. A burst is spent out of the reserve he walked in with and owes the ground nothing until he wants it back.

| Density | EU/m³ | As pressure | Supply, AU/s per m³ | Where that sits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aether-thin ground | 0.001 to 0.1 | 1 kPa to 0.1 MPa | 10⁻⁶ to 10⁻⁴ | below one atmosphere of Aetheric pressure |
| **Ambient Saturation** | 0.1 to 10 | 0.1 to 10 MPa | 10⁻⁴ to 10⁻² | sea-level air carries 0.25; a one-tesla field, 0.40 |
| **Active Concentration** | 10 to 10⁴ | 10 MPa to 10 GPa | 10⁻² to 10 | a bale of dry straw, burned through, 2,500; a seasoned pine log, burned through, 6,900 |
| **Veil-Thin Nexus** | 10⁴ to 10⁸ | 10 GPa to 100 TPa | 10 to 10⁵ | the pressure inside a planet; the strongest field a forge has struck, 400,000 |
| **Wellspring Core** | 10⁸ to 7.7×10¹⁸ | 100 TPa and above | 10⁵ to 7.7×10¹⁵ | up to the field at which the void itself begins making matter |

**In a Silence (ground where no lawful working can be authorized) the figure is zero.** That is the whole of why a Silence is something other than shielding. There is nothing to pull and nothing to push into, which is also why a soul held in one reports the sensation as loneliness rather than as weakness.

**Ambient Saturation is the reference.** Its midpoint, one EU per cubic meter, is the ground every laddered reach is quoted against. Denser ground fills a practitioner faster and lifts his ceiling on sustained output, as the supply column shows, which is why fighters will pay a keeper for it. It does nothing for his arm's length. It raises the floor his own field has to stand above, and addressing range, authority radius and anchor reach all fall as the **square root** of the local density. Concentrated ground cuts all three to an **eighteenth**. A Nexus cuts them to a **thousandth**. A Core cuts them to a **five-millionth**. That last figure is the arithmetic behind the sentence in the table above: inside a Core the law the Wellspring carries is the only law operating, and an S-Grade's kilometer of authority is a fifth of a millimeter wide.

**Why residence changes a body.** A person at rest dissipates about a hundred watts through about a fifteenth of a cubic meter, which is one and a half kilowatts per cubic meter. Active Concentration supplies ten to ten thousand kilowatts per cubic meter. The field is putting seven to seven thousand times the subject's own metabolic power density through them, continuously, and a body has no mechanism for declining a power density. That is why Baptism at that condition is involuntary, and why it reaches the shepherd as readily as the adept.
> **Saturation, stated once.** Saturation begins at **ten thousand EU per cubic meter**, the stress at which a crystal lattice fails. It is reached by leaving that much above the ground's own density in each cubic meter of a region (a Nexus or a Core runs past the figure natively, and what those do to a visitor is the Density Scale's business), or by sustaining more than **a hundred AU/s through each square meter of the region's boundary**. The two are one statement. The second is the first carried out at the Aether's own speed. The threshold grows with the surface while output does not, so **a kilometer of open ground swallows what a hall would not.** A Master's field (Stages VIII to X, at the benchmark rate) a kilometer across puts about a fifth of one AU/s through each square meter of its edge, a five-hundredth of threshold. The same output inside a twenty-meter hall puts five hundred and fifty through each square meter, five times over, and everyone in the hall is a casualty of it. Open ground stops being the answer near the top of the ladder: an Archmaster's benchmark through that same kilometer is eighty-odd per square meter, and the top of the Archmaster band runs past threshold. A drawn circle is the other half of the same arithmetic. A rite bleeding sixty EU a second into two meters of floor reaches Active Concentration within the hour and stays there, which is what the Circle step is for and what an improvised one costs.

## The Attraction and Obsession Axis

**A site operating under clean Attraction Force** recognizes practitioners who enter it, reads the Crystal, assesses compatibility, and **either permits harmonization or ignores the visitor.** *The experience is intense but orderly.*
> **A site operating under Obsession Force has been corrupted.** Pulled on without reciprocity. Exposed to sustained Mechanica (forbidden working that takes power without lawful consent) extraction. Saturated by wartime discharge never processed.
>
> **The Aether there does not recognize. It takes. It pulls at the Crystal whether the visitor is compatible or not.**

### How to Read an Entry

| **The idea** | The concept the Wellspring lays over a soul's Aether, in plain words. Start here. |

| **Kind** | Wild, Spirit, Fate or Medium: the plane the Wellspring is at home on, which is the Physical World, the Soul Plane, the Plane of Fate, or all three across the Veil. A qualifier and never a lock. |

| **On the Spirit** | What the lens does on the Spirit Path, in the terms of the Essence Core: projection, inscription, what a thing is. The line called Spirit below is another matter, the confessor's reading of the Wellspring whatever the Path. |
| **On Fate** | What the lens does on the Fate Path, in the terms of the Attraction Layer: bonds, oaths, claims and odds, and so what a Fate fighter carries in their presence and, on the rare day they pull, what they move. |
| **Spirit** | The transformation it works on the soul, the road on the Temperance climb: the Fracture's "Temperance through" name, with the confessors' reading beside it where they read it otherwise. |
| **Alchemy** | The principle it stands for at the bench: its operation or stage of the Work where it has one, otherwise the trade's reading of what its law does to matter in a vessel. |

| **Glyph** | The forms the [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms\|Master Glyph Index]] seats on this Wellspring as primary, with a count of the others it is attested on. |

## Environmental Coupling

Because each Family answers to a real physical domain, each Family answers to real physical conditions. Aetheric Density sets the ceiling on sustained output everywhere, but a Family's Physics Domain sets a **second, narrower gate** on top of it, and veterans choose ground accordingly.

| Family | Encounter type | Favored conditions | Suppressive conditions |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Caloria** | **Wild** · **Spirit** (Catharsis, Penanceflare) | Steep thermal gradients, combustible atmosphere, geothermal ground | Thermal equilibrium, saturated cold, oxygen-poor air |
| **Fulguria** | **Wild** · **Medium** (Aurevane, Luminalis) · **Spirit** (Abyntheus) | Dry air, conductive substrate, clear line of sight | Iron-caged interiors, heavy ferrous shielding, dense fog |
| **Vectoria** | **Wild** · **Spirit** (Absolution, Penance) | Structural mass, elevation, brittle or pre-stressed material | Loose sediment, free fall, uniform soft ground |
| **Spatium** | **Wild** (Transmutatio) · **Spirit** (Eclipseron) · **Fate** (Transference) | Veil-thin zones, established Realmgates (standing doorways between Realms), surveyed ground | Warp scarring, contested Domains (spaces where one practitioner's law outranks local physics), unmapped terrain |
| **Materia** | **Wild** · **Medium** (Fixatio) · **Fate** (Tarturon) | Mineral-rich rock, worked stone, deep foundation | Open water, high altitude, moving platforms |
| **Vitalia** | **Wild** · **Medium** (Phreatis, Mortalis) · **Spirit** (Redemption, Contrition) · **Fate** (Benediction) | Living biomass, temperate conditions, proximity to blood | Sterile ground, extreme cold, Essence-depleted soil |
| **Fluxia** | **Fate** (Somnalis, Oblation) · **Spirit** (Thaloré) · **Medium** (Phreatis) | Standing or moving water, humidity, pressure differential | Arid terrain, vacuum, thermally locked air |
| **Limina** | **Spirit** · **Wild** (Dissolution, Tenebra, Eidolyn, Nihiloth, Vantabriel) · **Medium** (Anamnesis) · **Fate** (Oblivara) | Darkness, silence, Aether-thin ground, ruins | Saturated Aether, crowd density, active Domains |

These conditions bind what acts on the world, the Wild and the Medium. A Spirit Wellspring or a Wellspring of Fate has no phenomenon for the ground to favor or smother, and it answers to where you stand only through Aetheric Density.

> None of this is a prohibition. A Caloria practitioner in saturated cold still works. They simply pay a larger fraction of their reserve to overcome the gradient they are fighting rather than riding, and their efficiency figure falls accordingly. **Terrain is a modifier on η (efficiency: the share of spent power that arrives as effect), and anyone who calls it flavor has not yet paid for it.**

## Harmonization, Stacking, and Interference

*What happens when a Crystal is asked to obey two laws at once.*

**Same-Family harmonization compounds.** Three Materia Wellsprings produce a Vitality and Resilience profile measurably denser than an identical stat spread carrying no Materia harmonization at all, because the three constitutive laws agree with one another and reinforce along the same axes. The numbers on the Ledger are identical. The qualitative expression is not, and anyone who has fought both can tell them apart in one exchange.

**Cross-Family harmonization introduces resonance complexity**, and the physics predicts which pairs behave. Families whose Physics Domains are compatible produce complementary effects without interference: **Materia with Vectoria**, since matter given form through force is one problem described twice. Families whose domains are structurally opposed produce tension the Crystal must resolve, either by Temperance advancement or by developing a Compound Trait that holds the contradiction as a single law. **Materia against Limina** is the canonical hard case, form set against the erosion of all shape.
> The ceiling is **Tempering Capacity** and it is not negotiable. Exceeding it does not fail gracefully by dropping the newest harmonization. **It degrades all active harmonizations equally**, which is how practitioners lose a decade of work by reaching for one more Wellspring in a bad hour.

## Discrepancies of Record

*Attested, not reconciled.*

**The Vectoria count** · The Fracture register lists eight Vectoria Wellsprings including Sublimare. The consolidated Magic System register lists seven and omits Sublimare entirely. With Sublimare restored and Phreatis counted once, the register reconciles exactly to sixty unique types across sixty-one listings. The Measurewrights record the consolidated table as the party in error and this register follows the Fracture.

**The Phreatis cross-listing** · Phreatis answers to Vitalia and Fluxia both, and the physical reading supports the double assignment rather than resolving it. Synaptic potentiation and aquifer residence are two physics describing one behavior. The entry is written once in Vitalia and referred from Fluxia.

**Sublimatio and Sublimare** · The two have been treated as near-duplicates in prior registers on the strength of their names. The physical reading separates them cleanly: sublimation skips a phase, fractional distillation separates a mixture. They are different operations with different residues and different failure modes, and the duplication was linguistic rather than mechanical.

**Sublimare's Family in the Codex** · Three Codex rows, the glyph `[Prc]` Pressure, the spell Pressure Veil and one alchemical row, still give Sublimare as "Cross-Family" and read it as tracking phase-transition behavior. The Fracture register files it in Vectoria and its bench law is distillation, and this register follows both. The Codex's wording is recorded here and left as it stands.

**Vohrin** · Frost arts and cold galleries are entered all over the registers as "Vohrin (Caloria)", and Vohrin is a Titan's power and not one of the Sixty. The count stays at sixty. The power can be worked at Wellspring level, a cold site is filed as a Titan-touched site, and the [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/Caloria — Thermodynamics|Caloria page]] carries a marked note with its analogue, failure and signature as the register's one exception. Several Codex rows still give frost to Cymorath, which is the Air of Ascent and no frost Wellspring.

**The eleven with no form** · Pyreveil, Cinerion, Manganthra, Aurevane, Nyxial, Tarturon, Ascensio, Abython, Penance, Redemption and Contrition appear in no row of the [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms|Glyph Index]], primary or attested. The Index is fixed at 136 and nobody has shown whether the gap is in the record or in the world. A working under one of the eleven is written in a form seated elsewhere or in one of the unclassified Roots, which name a kind of limit and will condition any law.

**Family names in older sources** · House of the Green Pulse, House of the Veil, House of the Black Gate and similar appear throughout the Wellspring codices and the Veil literature. These are the pre-correction feeling-first Houses and do not map cleanly onto the eight Families. Conversion tables have been requested twice.

# The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings

> *"The Wellspring is neither gift nor spirit. It is a law that was already running before you noticed it, and harmonization is the act of agreeing to be measured by it."*
>
> *From the preface to the First Physical Register.*
>
> **Second edition.** The first edition gave every one of the sixty **a physical analogue, a mechanism in that analogue's own terms, and a failure mode following from the mechanism rather than from the mood.** The entries are unchanged. **The physics has not moved.** This edition adds what the trade has always known and the Register was too proud to set down on the same page: **what each of the sixty means at the bench and what it does to the soul.**

Nobody on the Cold Tun bridge needs the Sixty explained, because their grandmothers saw Sanne Ruddock and Ond Gravell. Same Stage (a rung of the soul's sixteen-step climb), same Grade (the letter an assay gives a stat) on the sheet, and both banks packed to the reeds with the green sashes of the Hot Tun Ladies and the slate caps of the Quarrymen, with a man selling fried dough off a pole between them. The bridge stands where a hot spring comes up through old bedrock, so two kinds of Aether (the raw stuff of magic in the world around you) pool under that one span, and each fighter stood in the same air and pulled a different one. Sanne breathed in Pyreveil and came up deep ember-orange from the ribs outward, skin flushed and bone-dry, heat-shimmer climbing both arms until the spit boiled off her lip. Ond breathed in Petralon and went the gray of a wet anvil, and the dust on the deck sagged down around his boots with a long settling groan, and everyone on the bridge felt him get heavier without being able to say how. Her fire broke over him, *FWOOOM*, and he stood in it. His fist went into her guard like a dropped lintel, *gronk*, and she laughed with blood on her teeth, because her body had stopped minding. For the length of a long sermon neither of them could do one thing about the other, and the crowd sang all through it.

Then both bills came due inside a single breath. Sanne got both hands round his wrist and hauled, straight outward, in tension, the one direction Petralon does not answer, and the man who had just stood in a bath of fire parted at the shoulder.

*RRRIP.*

He did not bend first. Stone does not. And the heat she had kept banked in her own tissue to do it had to leave by whatever was nearest, which was her palms, and she stood there on the bridge with her hands cooking and her mouth wide open while Ond Gravell, one-armed and roaring, fell on her and took them both over the rail into the Tun. Draws keep the stakes at Cold Tun. The bookies bought new roofs.

Two fighters, one bridge, one breath of the same air. The whole of the difference was which Aether each of them took in, what it did to the Crystal that took it, and what it would eventually charge. Every child in the Four Quarters already half knows this, being a *Fixatio child* or a *Tarturon child* by the day of the sixty they were born on, which is stated before their age is. The rest of this page is the long version, and it is worth the lamp oil.

## Why the Sixty Were Re-Sorted

The older registers arranged the Wellsprings by feeling. Grief stood with grief. Fire stood with fire because both were warm. It produced a beautiful taxonomy and a useless one, because a practitioner who knew that Cinerion and Pyreveil were both House of the Ember still had no idea why one of them refilled a reserve and the other emptied it faster.

The physics-first sorting corrects that at the cost of intuition. Wellsprings that shared a House for reasons of sorrow are now scattered across five Families. An ember and a stone stand together. Both facts are correct, and both offend the eye of anyone trained on the old registers. Materia Primordia and Tarturon belong in one Family because both are, mechanically, the assessment the Continuum (the whole lawful order of reality) makes of what a soul is made of and how much of it survives contact, and how reckoning feels has nothing to do with it.
> *A warning to the devotional reader. Naming the physics does not demote the Wellspring. The Aether was always obeying a law. The Measurewrights (the guild's certified assessors of practitioners and workings) have simply written the law down.*

## What a Wellspring Physically Is

A Wellspring is **a type of Aether that carries a fixed law.** Aether is what practitioners pull from the environment around them, and it comes in sixty types, fixed and counted, and each type affects the Soul Crystal (the soul's organ that stores and channels power) differently. Take in Pyreveil and your reserve burns hotter while your body stops minding the heat. Take in Hypnather and you sleep like the dead and wake structurally different. Take in Fractura and you will never again look at a bridge, a friend or your own left wrist without seeing where it is going to go. A type of Aether is not stored anywhere and it does not belong to anyone. It runs whether observed or not, in the way that a pressure gradient runs whether or not something is standing in it.

The old registers call each type a **current**, and the trade still does, because Aether moves over ground the way wind moves over a field. Keep the word and lose any picture of a pipe. Nobody has ever laid a Wellspring on or shut one off at a valve, and the Measurewright who tells you otherwise is about to send you a bill.

**The road through the soul** is the same for all sixty. Aether is pulled in from the world, refined in the Soul Crystal into Essence (a soul's own share of Aether, the thing a fighter holds and spends), and driven out through the Aether Shell (the Crystal's outer layer, which turns inner power into outward force). What differs is the type. Where you stand therefore matters twice: rich ground fills you faster and thin ground starves you, and the ground decides which of the sixty is on offer. On the street this is plain sense. A brawler will say she has the ember in her, or the stone, or that she took the water at Saint Orrin's pond, and she will say he only fights like that on hot ground. On a Guild roll all of that is harmonization, which comes next.
> **Harmonization is phase-locking.** The Aether Shell adopts the characteristic behavior of one type of Aether so that Essence passing outward through the Shell is shaped by that law before it reaches the world. Nothing is added to the practitioner. The conduit is retuned, and everything that passes through it afterward carries the retuning.

This is why harmonization changes what a number means without changing the number. A Fortitude of B-Grade harmonized to Monolithion does not become a larger Fortitude. It becomes a Fortitude that behaves like a load-bearing mass rather than like tissue, with the failure curve of stone rather than the failure curve of flesh. **The magnitude stays where it was and the constitutive law changes.**

Most mortals awaken three to five Wellsprings in a lifetime. Serious adepts harmonize with twelve or more. Those who embody all sixty are called **Principals of Temperance**, and there are not many of them, and you will not meet one.

### One Aether, three readings

Every one of the sixty can be read three ways, and each reading is a whole profession that thinks the other two are amateurs.

**As a law of the world.** The Measurewright's reading, and the one this register is built on: the physical analogue, the mechanism, the failure.

**As an alchemical principle.** The bench's reading: what that law does to matter in a vessel. Each of the sixty is a living process, a law of transformation, and the discipline of Alchemetrica (the working of Wellspring law upon matter) is built on that fact. The reagents are the nouns and the Wellspring is the verb. Six of the sixty are at once Wellspring, cosmic principle and named bench operation, and four more lend their law to a stage of the Great Work (the seven-stage discipline an alchemist lays on the matter and on himself at once). The rest have no operation filed under their name at the Bench, though every working alchemist has an opinion about what each of them is the principle of, and those opinions appear in the entries, marked as the trade's reading and open to a fight.

**As a spiritual transformation.** The confessor's reading, and the oldest: what taking this Aether into the soul does to the person who survives it. Temperance is the climb of a soul from Stage to Stage, and every Wellspring is a road on that climb, so the Fracture register names Pyreveil *Temperance through burning* and Absolution *Temperance through forgiveness*, and where the Fracture register gives no such name the entry gives the confessors' reading, marked as such and argued over at every deathbed. To pass through a Wellspring is to be unmade just enough to keep going. Every Wellspring touches all three layers of the Crystal, rarely evenly. The **Essence Core** decides how the soul burns, heals or empties under that law, the **Aether Shell** how the law moves through and around the body, and the **Attraction Layer** how reality, memory and people bind to the soul once the law has taken hold.

The correspondence is architecture rather than poetry, which is what Alchemetrica means when it says that what burns in the crucible burns in the one who tends it.

| At the bench | Wellspring | What it lends |
|---|---|---|
| **Named operations, the six** | **Dissolution** | Unravels cohesion into base frequencies |
| | **Coagulatio** | Gathers what is dispersed round a seed and sets it |
| | **Sublimatio** | Elevates without passing through the states between |
| | **Transmutatio** | Re-expresses a thing in another basis, nothing created or lost |
| | **Fixatio** | Anchors and seals |
| | **Materia Primordia** | Re-lattices one Essence into another of its own forms |
| **Stage of the Work: Calcination** | **Cinerion** | Strong dry heat drives off what is volatile and leaves what is fixed |
| **Stage of the Work: Separation** | **Judicium** | Discernment: the parts told apart and taken apart |
| **Stage of the Work: Fermentation** | **Rebirthine** | Breakdown pays for growth |
| **Stage of the Work: Distillation** | **Sublimare** | Parting by boiling point, the spirit risen and returned |

Dissolution and Coagulation are stages of the Work too, on the Wellsprings that share their names. **Conjunction has no Wellspring of its own.** It runs on Attraction, and a Conjunction corrupted injures Attraction itself. Calcination and Distillation are bench operations and no soul harmonizes with either as such; a soul harmonizes with Cinerion or Sublimare, and the bench borrows from them.

The old masons knew a shorter list and knew it empirically, with no account of why: basalt takes Coagula and Fixatio, quartz amplifies Fractura and Luminalis, iron conducts Manganthra and Aurevane, gold carries Benediction and Sublimatio, and obsidian holds Tenebra and Oblivara.

### Harmonization, Traits, and the glyph

*Three things bias a Shell and they are constantly mistaken for one another.*

**A harmonization retunes the conduit.** It is reversible in principle, it degrades if Capacity (the limit on how many Wellsprings a soul can hold at once) is exceeded, and it shapes everything passing outward for as long as it holds. The law belongs to the Aether and the practitioner has it on loan.

**A Trait biases the conduit permanently.** It is a law the soul itself carries, held in the Essence Core (the Crystal's inner layer, holding identity, memory and Traits), and it writes into the Shell's conductivity map rather than borrowing a law from outside. It cannot be dropped to make room. This is why two practitioners harmonized to the same Wellspring produce recognizably different output: the Aether supplies one law and the Core supplies another, and the working is what comes out of both.

**A glyph is a boundary condition.** It creates nothing. The Wellspring's law was already running; the glyph moves a boundary and the law does what it has always done on the far side of the new boundary. Every entry below should be read with that in mind. Nobody in this register makes fire. They establish conditions under which the ordinary behavior of heat produces fire where it previously did not, and the fire that results obeys every rule fire has ever obeyed, including the ones nobody thought about.
> **Sites do not check for a Crystal.** Aether pooling in a valley acts on everything standing in the valley, and a Class Ø (never-woken) soul's Shell is sealed and cannot phase-lock, but it has an Essence Core like anyone else. Sustained residence at Active Concentration or above therefore does to a shepherd what it does to an adept, minus the harmonization and minus the vocabulary.
>
> What it leaves behind is a **Trait**, formed by Wellspring Convergence, in a body that will never be screened and a life that has no framework for it. Every quarter has villages downwind of a concentration where one family has been strange for six generations, and the strangeness is lawful, inheritable, and entirely undocumented.

## Where a Wellspring Pools

**A Wellspring is a type of Aether and no kind of place. But a Wellspring pools.**

The way groundwater is not stored in a specific container but absolutely collects in aquifers, a type of Aether concentrates where the substrate is Aether-conductive, where competing types are absent, and where enough time has passed for it to saturate the local field **until the physical environment begins expressing the law passively.**
> **A Wellspring site is the aquifer. The Aether is the water table.**

| Condition | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Conductive substrate** | The era-one emplacement described in *The Four Ceilings* laid the mineral foundation during the Creation Age |
| **Absence of competing types** | **This is why the New World's arc holds so many pristine sites at densities the Old World has not seen since the Antediluvian Calendar.** *Nobody has been pulling from them. The veins are full. The elven old-ground beside the arc is the opposite case: its Wellsprings have been pulled on since the first Eon* |
| **Time** | Enough for the Aether to saturate the field **until the environment starts expressing the law as a passive condition of the geography** |

### Cores, keepers and closed ground

Common speech calls any site where a Wellspring's Aether pools a **Core**. Surveyors keep *Wellspring Core* for the top line of the Density Scale below, an Essence Core is a layer of your own Crystal, and a Measurewright who hears a ferryman call the warm pond behind the tannery the Pyreveil Core will explain all of this at length until the ferryman charges him double. This register uses the surveyor's word, and you should use the ferryman's if you want to get across the river.

**A worked site recharges and has keepers.** A site people have pulled from and tended for generations refills at the rate its ground replaces what has been taken, which the numbers below will give you, and somebody always stands at the gate of it: a sect, a village, a Guild house, three brothers and a fence. Keepers charge by the hour or by the bottle. They strike, they overcharge, they play favorites, and they can **close** the site, turning everyone off it and working the ground shut, which no amount of Stage talks them out of unless the Stage is prepared to do it by force in front of witnesses.

**The counter this hands you is the oldest in the book.** Take the fight off rich ground. A fighter fat on a Core loses the fat a league down the road, on **salted** ground (worked by ward-cutters and hedge-workers so that Aether will not pool on it, and it gives back little for a season), on **bled** ground (pulled empty by a long fight or a greedy crew, just before you arrive if somebody has planned it), or on ground that was simply thin to begin with. Half the famous upsets in the brackets were won in the week before the fight, by someone quietly paying off the people who choose the field.

### The second mode · Object Wellsprings

Aether of one type captured, bound, or naturally accumulated inside a vessel produces **a contained domain**, carried in a sword, a relic, or a formation old enough that **the material and the Aether are indistinguishable.**
> **Crevice Shale, T9, the Crevice itself**: forbidden to move or hold or trade. *A law about a rock, and the only such law the Accord has ever written.* **The most extreme Object Wellspring in the register.**
>
> **The critical distinction between the two modes is interface geometry.**
>
> **A site has a perimeter.** The density gradient gives warning. The surveyor feels the Aether thickening as they approach the core.
>
> **An object is binary. You are holding it or you are not**, and its internal domain contacts the Shell at zero distance. If the Shell is incompatible, **the resulting conversation was not authorized and cannot be escaped without severing the causal relationship at the Plane of Fate (the deepest plane, where bonds and consequence are recorded) level.**
>
> *The colloquial term for an Object Wellspring whose Attraction Layer (the layer that holds bonds and claims) will not accept a termination event is cursed.*

## The Three Encounter Types

*The physics-first sorting tells the practitioner which Family governs their output. The encounter-type classification tells the surveyor which direction the danger arrives from.*
> **The classification rests on a single question: where does the effect originate, and where does it arrive?**

| Type | Mechanism | Dominant Families |
|---|---|---|
| **Wild** *Physical Plane dominant* | The Aether acts on **existing physical material.** The stone was there. The water was there. The metal was there. **The Aether reshapes it, moves it, compresses it, grows it.** *The landscape is visibly altered. The surveyor's body is a reliable instrument. The surveyor's environment is not* | Materia, Spatium |
| **Spirit** *Soul Plane dominant* | The Aether **generates effects from inside the Crystal and projects them outward. The fire was not there until the Aether made it.** The Essence Core is the active layer. *The landscape looks entirely normal. The surveyor's environment is a reliable instrument. The surveyor is not* | Caloria (Fire, Heat, Cold), Fulguria, portions of Vectoria |
| **Medium** *Veil-crossing* | The Aether operates **in both directions simultaneously**, in the liminal threshold between planes. **Water and Light are the primary Medium elements.** *A Phreatis practitioner at a riverbank is simultaneously reshaping the physical water and being reshaped by what the water remembers. The observer is altered by the observation. Neither body nor Crystal provides a stable reference point* | Vitalia (Water, Growth), Caloria (Light) |

### The nested depth model

**The three planes are nested, not stacked.**
> **The Plane of Fate lies deepest**, the lattice upon which all possibility rests. **The Soul Plane lies between**, the stratum through which abstract law becomes a soul's felt truth. **The Physical Plane sits at the surface**, where truth becomes event, body, ruin, and mountain range.
>
> *Fate is a precursor. A tide that falls. What is decided at Fate-depth cascades through the Soul Plane and manifests on the Physical Plane as event.*
>
> This is why the three types produce different threat profiles.
>
> **Wild** operates at the surface. The danger is physical and legible.
>
> **Spirit** operates from the middle layer, **and the cascade runs upward**: the Crystal fires, the Shell expresses, the body manifests what the Soul Plane authored. *What reaches the surveyor comes from no outside blow. They are being authored from inside.*
>
> **Medium** operates at the Veil itself, **the threshold where one plane's law softens into another's.** Both layers move at once, **and the movement is reciprocal.**

## The Density Scale

*The encounter type determines the direction of the danger. The density determines its magnitude.*

| Density | Condition |
|---|---|
| **Ambient Saturation** | The most common condition across the mapped world, and no kin to Aetheric Saturation, the lethal flood. **A Sage can tune to it. An ordinary person feels vaguely uneasy or peaceful.** The landscape carries subtle signs |
| **Active Concentration** | Dense enough for persistent environmental effects. Wild sites show altered terrain; Spirit sites produce involuntary physiological or psychological change; **Medium sites produce bidirectional effects indistinguishable from illness or hallucination.** **A practitioner remaining too long receives Wellspring Baptism whether they wanted it or not.** *Most concentrations on the New World's arc were found during the Voyager Era; the elven old-ground's have been pulled on since the first Eon* |
| **Veil-Thin Nexus** | **The Veil between planes thins.** Spiritual entities become visible. Causal threads are perceptible. **Time feels wrong.** Bifold Perception onset without training, involuntary Soul Drift, Echo Beast manifestation |
| **Wellspring Core** | **The Aether's origin point. The aquifer's source.** Extraordinarily rare, mostly New World, mostly unmapped. **The physical and metaphysical environments are indistinguishable, and the law the Wellspring carries is the only law operating.** *Walking into a Core unprepared is Wellspring Baptism at maximum intensity with no exit plan* |

### The Density Scale in Numbers

*The condition is what a surveyor writes down. The number is what the ground will actually give.*

One EU is one megajoule, so a density in EU per cubic meter is an energy density, and an energy density is a pressure. **A density in EU/m³ is the same figure in megapascals.** That one identity is why the scale is a ceiling and not a mood. A boundary cannot be pushed into ground at a pressure the ground does not carry, and what is standing in the ground has a strength measured in the same unit.

**What a site will actually give.** Aether crosses a site the size of a hall in about a thousand seconds. That is the interval in which the ground replaces what has been taken from it. So a site yields at most **a thousandth of its density, per second**. The supply rate in AU/s (Aether Units per second, a rate of output) per cubic meter is the density in EU/m³ divided by a thousand, and the ceiling on a practitioner's output is that rate across the volume his field reaches.

| Density | EU/m³ | As pressure | Supply, AU/s per m³ | Where that sits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aether-thin ground | 0.001 to 0.1 | 1 kPa to 0.1 MPa | 10⁻⁶ to 10⁻⁴ | below one atmosphere of Aetheric pressure |
| **Ambient Saturation** | 0.1 to 10 | 0.1 to 10 MPa | 10⁻⁴ to 10⁻² | sea-level air carries 0.25; a one-tesla field, 0.40 |
| **Active Concentration** | 10 to 10⁴ | 10 MPa to 10 GPa | 10⁻² to 10 | a bale of dry straw, burned through, 2,500; a seasoned pine log, burned through, 6,900 |
| **Veil-Thin Nexus** | 10⁴ to 10⁸ | 10 GPa to 100 TPa | 10 to 10⁵ | the pressure inside a planet; the strongest field a forge has struck, 400,000 |
| **Wellspring Core** | 10⁸ to 7.7×10¹⁸ | 100 TPa and above | 10⁵ to 7.7×10¹⁵ | up to the field at which the void itself begins making matter |

**In a Silence (ground where no lawful working can be authorized) the figure is zero.** That is the whole of why a Silence is something other than shielding. There is nothing to pull and nothing to push into, which is also why a soul held in one reports the sensation as loneliness rather than as weakness.

**Ambient Saturation is the reference.** Its midpoint, one EU per cubic meter, is the ground every laddered reach is quoted against. Denser ground fills a practitioner faster and lifts his ceiling on output, as the supply column shows, which is why fighters will pay a keeper for it. It does nothing for his arm's length. It raises the floor his own field has to stand above, and addressing range, authority radius and anchor reach all fall as the **square root** of the local density. Concentrated ground cuts all three to an **eighteenth**. A Nexus cuts them to a **thousandth**. A Core cuts them to a **five-millionth**. That last figure is the arithmetic behind the sentence in the table above: inside a Core the law the Wellspring carries is the only law operating, and an S-Grade's kilometer of authority is a fifth of a millimeter wide.

**Why residence changes a body.** A person at rest dissipates about a hundred watts through about a fifteenth of a cubic meter, which is one and a half kilowatts per cubic meter. Active Concentration supplies ten to ten thousand kilowatts per cubic meter. The field is putting seven to seven thousand times the subject's own metabolic power density through them, continuously, and a body has no mechanism for declining a power density. That is why Baptism at that condition is involuntary, and why it reaches the shepherd as readily as the adept.
> **Saturation, stated once.** Saturation begins at **ten thousand EU per cubic meter**, the stress at which a crystal lattice fails. It is reached by leaving that much in each cubic meter of a region, or by sustaining more than **a hundred AU/s through each square meter of the region's boundary**. The two are one statement. The second is the first carried out at the Aether's own speed. The threshold grows with the surface while output does not, so **a kilometer of open ground swallows what a hall would not.** A Zenith field a kilometer across puts about a fifth of one AU/s through each square meter of its edge, a five-hundredth of threshold. The same output inside a twenty-meter hall puts five hundred and fifty through each square meter, five times over, and everyone in the hall is a casualty of it. A drawn circle is the other half of the same arithmetic. A rite bleeding sixty EU a second into two meters of floor reaches Active Concentration within the hour and stays there, which is what the Circle step is for and what an improvised one costs.

## The Attraction and Obsession Axis

**A site operating under clean Attraction Force** recognizes practitioners who enter it, reads the Crystal, assesses compatibility, and **either permits harmonization or ignores the visitor.** *The experience is intense but orderly.*
> **A site operating under Obsession Force has been corrupted.** Pulled on without reciprocity. Exposed to sustained Mechanica (forbidden working that takes power without lawful consent) extraction. Saturated by wartime discharge never processed.
>
> **The Aether there does not recognize. It takes. It pulls at the Crystal whether the visitor is compatible or not.**

### How to Read an Entry

| **Analogue** | The specific real phenomenon the Wellspring's behavior matches. Named precisely, not gestured at. This is the Mechanism field's source when the Wellspring appears in a technique's first display. |
|---|---|
| **Mechanism** | What this type of Aether does to Essence and to the Crystal, stated in the analogue's own terms. If the physics says a process is entropically driven, the Wellspring's cost structure says so too. |
| **Stat Effect** | The Sub-Stat (one of eight finer measures inside each main stat) modification, unchanged from the system source. Behavior, never bonus. |
| **Alchemy** | The principle it stands for at the bench: its operation or stage of the Work where it has one, otherwise the trade's reading of what its law does to matter in a vessel. |
| **Spirit** | The transformation it works on the soul, the road on the Temperance climb. |
| **Failure** | The characteristic malfunction, derived from the physics. Every analogue has a way of going wrong that is specific to it, and a Wellspring inherits its analogue's pathology. |

## Environmental Coupling

Because each Family answers to a real physical domain, each Family answers to real physical conditions. Aetheric Density sets the ceiling on output rate everywhere, but a Family's Physics Domain sets a **second, narrower gate** on top of it, and veterans choose ground accordingly.

| Family | Encounter type | Favored conditions | Suppressive conditions |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Caloria** | **Spirit** (Fire/Heat/Cold) · **Medium** (Light) | Steep thermal gradients, combustible atmosphere, geothermal ground | Thermal equilibrium, saturated cold, oxygen-poor air |
| **Fulguria** | **Spirit** | Dry air, conductive substrate, clear line of sight | Faraday-caged interiors, heavy ferrous shielding, dense fog |
| **Vectoria** | Split: **Wild** (Gravity, Seismic) · **Spirit** (Sound, Force projection) | Structural mass, elevation, brittle or pre-stressed material | Loose sediment, free fall, uniform soft ground |
| **Spatium** | **Wild** | Veil-thin zones, established Realmgates (standing doorways between Realms), surveyed ground | Warp scarring, contested Domains (spaces where one practitioner's law outranks local physics), unmapped terrain |
| **Materia** | **Wild** | Mineral-rich rock, worked stone, deep foundation | Open water, high altitude, moving platforms |
| **Vitalia** | Split: **Wild** (Growth, Healing) · **Medium** (Water) · **Spirit** (Decay, Poison) | Living biomass, temperate conditions, proximity to blood | Sterile ground, extreme cold, Essence-depleted soil |
| **Fluxia** | **Medium** primary · **Spirit** at high concentration | Standing or moving water, humidity, pressure differential | Arid terrain, vacuum, thermally locked air |
| **Limina** | **Spirit** primary · **Medium** at Veil-Thin density and above | Darkness, silence, Aether-thin ground, ruins | Saturated Aether, crowd density, active Domains |

> None of this is a prohibition. A Caloria practitioner in saturated cold still works. They simply pay a larger fraction of their reserve to overcome the gradient they are fighting rather than riding, and their efficiency figure falls accordingly. **Terrain is a modifier on η (efficiency: the share of spent power that arrives as effect), and anyone who calls it flavor has not yet paid for it.**

## Harmonization, Stacking, and Interference

*What happens when a Crystal is asked to obey two laws at once.*

**Same-Family harmonization compounds.** Three Materia Wellsprings produce a Vitality and Resilience profile measurably denser than an identical stat spread carrying no Materia harmonization at all, because the three constitutive laws agree with one another and reinforce along the same axes. The numbers on the Ledger are identical. The qualitative expression is not, and anyone who has fought both can tell them apart in one exchange.

**Cross-Family harmonization introduces resonance complexity**, and the physics predicts which pairs behave. Families whose Physics Domains are compatible produce complementary effects without interference: **Materia with Vectoria**, since matter given form through force is one problem described twice. Families whose domains are structurally opposed produce tension the Crystal must resolve, either by Temperance advancement or by developing a Compound Trait that holds the contradiction as a single law. **Materia against Limina** is the canonical hard case, form set against the erosion of all shape.
> The ceiling is **Tempering Capacity** and it is not negotiable. Exceeding it does not fail gracefully by dropping the newest harmonization. **It degrades all active harmonizations equally**, which is how practitioners lose a decade of work by reaching for one more Wellspring in a bad hour.

## Discrepancies of Record

*Attested, not reconciled.*

**The Vectoria count** · The system source lists eight Vectoria Wellsprings including Sublimare. The consolidated Magic System register lists seven and omits Sublimare entirely. With Sublimare restored and Phreatis counted once, the register reconciles exactly to sixty unique types across sixty-one listings. The Measurewrights record the consolidated table as the party in error and this register follows the system source.

**The Phreatis cross-listing** · Phreatis answers to Vitalia and Fluxia both, and the physical reading supports the double assignment rather than resolving it. Synaptic potentiation and aquifer residence are two physics describing one behavior. The entry is written once in Vitalia and referred from Fluxia.

**Sublimatio and Sublimare** · The two have been treated as near-duplicates in prior registers on the strength of their names. The physical reading separates them cleanly: sublimation skips a phase, fractional distillation separates a mixture. They are different operations with different residues and different failure modes, and the duplication was linguistic rather than mechanical.

**Family names in older sources** · House of the Green Pulse, House of the Veil, House of the Black Gate and similar appear throughout the Wellspring codices and the Veil literature. These are the pre-correction feeling-first Houses and do not map cleanly onto the eight Families. Conversion tables have been requested twice.

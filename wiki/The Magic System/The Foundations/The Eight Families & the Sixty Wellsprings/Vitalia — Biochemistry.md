# Vitalia — Biochemistry

*Giving without counting · filed by the scholars under biochemistry: biology and anatomy*

Bertil Ashgrove kept a barber's chair on Mill Gate with a red-striped pole and a painted board that read TWO COPPERS A WOUND, NO CREDIT FOR COLORS. The day the Millrace Boys met the Hammers of Saint Orrin on the bridge over a disputed bout, he walked into the middle of it with his sleeves rolled and arterial red brightening every vein in both arms, and he closed fifty-three wounds in an hour on both sides, each wet pulse sealing a gash, taking his two coppers each time and flatly refusing the two men who had stiffed him at the last fair. The fifty-fourth wound was a nick on his own thumb. By then the cascade had nothing left to tell it where to stop, it ran through him everywhere at once, and that one small nick bled for three days while both clubs, in the only agreement they have ever reached, took turns holding his arm up and paying for ice.

**The idea** · Growth, generosity and rhythm. Its eight Wellsprings are eight ways a soul can be changed by being alive and by what it owes that, from the green that will not stop growing to the black gate at the end of the road.

**The lens** · Vitalia holds that a soul finds itself by accumulation, one good day on top of another. Its eight are the most mixed company in the Sixty: Anima Spirare, Verdantia and Rebirthine are Wild, at home in the Physical World; Redemption and Contrition are Spirit, at home on the Soul Plane; Benediction is a Wellspring of Fate; and Mortalis and Phreatis are Medium, at home on all three. Any one of them brings its idea forward: a little at first, and after years of mastery at its home Path up to double, so that a Body Path barber on Anima Spirare, with the same Regeneration at the same Grade, closes a wound with twice the body's own memory of being whole. Any Path can take any of them, and away from home it costs more Essence, resonates worse and reaches half again at most, and every barber on Mill Gate has a scar to show you the difference. The full account of the lens is on the [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings/The Eight Families & the Sixty Wellsprings|hub]].

**Stat Affinity** · *Vitality (Hemostasis, Regeneration) · Harmonics (Synergy, Empathy, Axis, Attunement) · Resilience (Oath, Persistence, Integrity, Anchoring) · Tempering (Yield, Maturity)*

*Each Wellspring below opens on what it is. **The idea** is the concept it lays over a soul's Aether, **Kind** its home plane, which is a qualifier and never a lock, and **On the Body**, **On the Spirit** and **On Fate** what its lens does to Essence on each Path: through the Aether Shell (the layer of the soul that meets matter), the Essence Core (the layer that holds identity and authors what a working is) and the Attraction Layer (a soul's own share of fate, its bonds, oaths, claims and odds). **Spirit** is the transformation it works on the soul, one road of Temperance (the climb of a soul from Stage to Stage), and **Alchemy** the principle it stands for at the bench. Then comes how scholars explain it: Analogue and Mechanism give the physics of what that type of Aether does to a Soul Crystal, Stat Effect what shifts on the sheet, Glyph the forms the Index seats on it, and Failure how it goes wrong.*

## Anima Spirare · *The Soul's Breath*

**The idea** · The breath of the whole. A body that remembers being whole and answers a small hurt with everything it has.

**Kind** · Wild

**On the Body** · A small cut draws an answer from the whole body, blood and breath arriving in a flood exactly where they are wanted, so wounds seal under the fighter's own pulse and the last round is breathed as easily as the first. Lose the signal that says where, and the flood goes everywhere.

**On the Spirit** · A thrown working carries a memory of wholeness and forces it on what it touches, so a split ward or a dying man's ribs are told what they used to be, loudly, and answer as well as they can. It costs dearly and works best on what was once alive.

**On Fate** · Pull one bond and all of them wake: hurt one of a crew and the others feel it under the breastbone and come running without knowing why. The holder cannot easily call that off, and a small quarrel has a way of turning out the whole family.

**Spirit** · Temperance through vitality. *The confessors' reading:* remembering wholeness, wounds closing because the body remembers being whole.

**Alchemy** · The quickening: the steady blood-warmth of the Bed, in which a charge stays alive. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · The coagulation cascade and cooperative oxygen binding.

**Mechanism** · Hemostasis is an amplifying cascade and never one event, in which each activated factor activates many of the next, so a small injury signal produces a large and precisely localized response. Layered under it, hemoglobin's cooperative binding means the fourth oxygen loads far more readily than the first. Anima Spirare imposes both patterns: amplification where it is needed, and a delivery curve that steepens with demand.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Hemostasis, Regeneration, Harmonics Synergy. The body pulses with living rhythm. Wounds close because the body remembers being whole.

**Glyph** · `[Leth]` Blood, `[Dr]` Vitality, `[Bst]` Beast; attested on two more.

**Failure** · Disseminated activation. A cascade with no localizing signal runs everywhere at once, consuming its own factors until nothing is left to close the wound that started it.

## Verdantia · *The Green Pulse*

**The idea** · Growth without end. A self with no final size, which feeds on simply being out in the world.

**Kind** · Wild

**On the Body** · The body never settles on a final size and recovers simply by being outdoors, so a long fight on open ground tends to leave the Verdantia fighter bigger at the end than at the start. Keep them in a cellar and they grow long, pale and useless.

**On the Spirit** · An inscription keeps growing after its author has walked away, and a line written on a wall in spring has put out new clauses by autumn. Nobody has found a way to make it grow in the direction they wrote.

**On Fate** · A name spreads the way a root system does, quietly and in every direction, until the holder has cousins, tenants and allies in towns they never visited. A Domain (a region where the world runs on a practitioner's terms) raised on this lens keeps creeping outward for as long as it is held, which neighbors come to resent.

**Spirit** · Temperance through growth.

**Alchemy** · The Feeding: growth given a little at a time and never all at once. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Carbon fixation and indeterminate meristematic growth.

**Mechanism** · A plant does not have a final size. Meristem tissue remains embryonic for the organism's whole life, and photosynthesis converts ambient energy into structure continuously rather than in bouts. Verdantia gives the Crystal indeterminate growth and continuous ambient conversion, which is why its practitioners recover while doing nothing and why they are difficult to exhaust in a long engagement.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Regeneration, Tempering Yield, Harmonics Synergy. Passive Essence recovery accelerates. The character heals by growing rather than repairing.

**Glyph** · `[El]` Life, `[Eld]` Elderwood, `[WL]` Wildlaw, `[Blm]` Bloom, `[Flo]` Bloom-Root; attested on seventeen more.

**Failure** · Etiolation. Deprived of ambient input the growth continues anyway, producing length without substance until the structure cannot hold itself up.

## Benediction · *The Wellspring of Grace*

**The idea** · Grace. A bond freely given, and weighed by the world as a real thing.

**Kind** · Fate

**On the Body** · A bond freely given lends the one blessed a real measure of steadiness, felt in the hands and the footing. There is no light to show for it beyond a soldier who has stopped trembling.

**On the Spirit** · A working carries the weight of the bond behind it, so a ward raised for a sworn friend holds where the same ward raised for a stranger would crack. The Core cannot manufacture this, and a bond that was never really given adds nothing at all.

**On Fate** · At home it is a bond the world weighs as a real thing, and an oath under it bears load. A promise made in this grace can be stood on, leaned on and, when it counts, pulled on across a continent.

**Spirit** · Temperance through compassion. *The confessors' reading:* grace, in which a bond becomes a thing the world can weigh.

**Alchemy** · Affinity: why this salt takes to that metal and will not let go. *(the trade's reading)* Gold carries it.

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Binding affinity. The dissociation constant of a specific high-affinity bond.

**Mechanism** · The strength of a biological bond is a measurable physical quantity, and a low dissociation constant means the complex, once formed, spends almost all of its time formed. Benediction gives bonds on the Plane of Fate that same measurability. This is the mechanical reason an oath sworn under Benediction carries system weight: it has an affinity figure, and the Continuum reads it.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Harmonics Empathy, Axis, Resilience Oath. Bonds formed under Benediction carry mechanical weight in the system.

**Glyph** · None primary. Attested on `[Dn]` Dawn, `[Blm]` Bloom, `[Blt]` Blight and `[Stg]` Stagnate.

> **Failure** · Competitive inhibition. A rival ligand with higher affinity displaces the existing bond, and the displacement is silent, complete, and not felt until the bond is called on.

## Rebirthine · *The Cycle Eternal*

**The idea** · The cycle. Loss taken as the first step of the next building, what is broken down paying for what comes up.

**Kind** · Wild

**On the Body** · The body digests its own damaged flesh to pay for the new, so a burned arm feeds tomorrow's muscle and a fighter can spend a wound like coin. Short of food and rest it goes on spending, and it does not check the balance.

**On the Spirit** · A working takes a thing apart so that something can be authored from the wreck, which makes this a fine lens for rebuilding a ruined ward or a broken technique. It is a poor one for leaving anything as it was.

**On Fate** · Lost ties come back as new ones: a dead friend's daughter turns up on the doorstep, and the ruined house finds it is owed something by the house that ruined it. Holders keep very good accounts of their losses, which their mourners find hard to forgive.

**Spirit** · Temperance through renewal. *The confessors' reading:* loss, destruction treated as the first step of reconstruction.

**Alchemy** · The law Fermentation draws on, from the Rot to the Feeding: breakdown pays for growth.

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Apoptosis and autophagy. Programmed clearance funding regeneration.

**Mechanism** · Controlled cell death is a service to the organism and no failure of it, and autophagy digests the cell's own damaged components to fund the construction of new ones. Catabolism pays for anabolism. Rebirthine makes destruction the first operation of a build sequence rather than the interruption of one, which is why its practitioners treat losses as inventory.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Resilience Persistence, Tempering Maturity, Vitality Regeneration. The Crystal treats destruction as the first step of reconstruction.

**Glyph** · `[Vael]` Renewal, `[Yn]` Bloom, `[Cy]` Cycle; attested on six more.

**Failure** · Autophagic collapse. With no external substrate the cycle consumes structure faster than it rebuilds, and it does not recognize the point at which there is nothing left to spend.

## Mortalis · *The Black Gate*

**The idea** · The black gate. Ending looked at squarely and understood as a passage with its own law, by a soul that has stopped fearing it and has started, a little, to cool.

**Kind** · Medium

**On the Body** · The body learns its own ending as a known road, so a Mortalis fighter goes on working, cool and unhurried, well past the wound that should have dropped them. A blow from that hand tells flesh it may stop spending itself, and flesh is often relieved to hear it.

**On the Spirit** · The Core looks at ending squarely and can author it, withdrawing a thing's hold on what it is, lawfully and in order: a ward lapses, a ghost goes on its way, a curse finds it has reached its term. It keeps nothing and nobody afterward.

**On Fate** · It works the last term of every tie. The holder feels a bond, a line or a life come near its end, can see a dying oath out with honor and can close a claim that had no other way to close, and so they are sent for by people who want to die well and by people who want something finished.

**Spirit** · Temperance through surrender. *The confessors' reading:* dissolution, threshold, completion and the lawful withdrawal of presence. It governs passage and keeps nobody in storage.

**Alchemy** · The Blacking: the charge gone black in the Rot, at the point where a matter stops holding itself together. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · The non-equilibrium steady state, and the thermodynamic boundary of its collapse.

**Mechanism** · A living body is a structure held far from equilibrium by continuous expenditure. Death adds no event to that structure. It is the cessation of the expenditure and the return to equilibrium that follows without needing a cause. Mortalis lets the practitioner engage that boundary as a described physical transition rather than as a catastrophe, which is a stranger and colder comfort than it sounds. The boundary runs through every layer of a soul alike, since flesh, identity and bond all end by the same law, and that is why this lens is at home on all three Paths.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Resilience Persistence, Gnosis Cognition. The soul engages ending as a structural event rather than a catastrophe.

**Glyph** · None primary. Attested on nine, among them `[Au]` Death, `[Ob]` Obol and `[SoulDr]` Soul-Drift.

**Failure** · Equilibration. Sustained proximity to the boundary reduces the gradient the practitioner is themselves maintaining, and they cool toward it without noticing.

## Redemption · *The Light Reclaimed*

**The idea** · The light reclaimed. The soul's wound becomes its strongest place, and the whole self rearranges itself around what it really lived through.

**Kind** · Spirit

**On the Body** · The body stops flinching from where it was broken, and a fighter will take a blow on the old break without the old terror and put real weight on a joint no physician would trust. The bone is no denser for it, whatever the pilgrims say.

**On the Spirit** · What wrecked the soul becomes the densest part of it, so a Core that survived a betrayal or a long captivity is hardest to move on exactly that ground. The rest of the self slowly aligns to what was truly lived, whatever had been hoped for.

**On Fate** · A name can be bought back: an old disgrace held on the Attraction Layer turns, by long living, into the very thing people trust the holder for. It happens slowly and looks from outside like nothing at all, until the day a town that spat at them sends for them.

**Spirit** · Temperance through return. *The confessors' reading:* being reclaimed, the wound becoming the strongest place.

**Alchemy** · The sound weld: metal densest at the old break. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Fracture callus and Wolff's law. Bone remodeling along lines of stress.

**Mechanism** · Healed bone is denser at the break than the bone around it, and a limb loaded repeatedly remodels its internal architecture to align with the load it actually receives. Redemption applies both to the Crystal: prior damage sites become the highest-density regions, and the whole structure reorganizes toward the stresses the practitioner has genuinely lived rather than the ones they expected. All of that happens in the soul. A limb that mended straighter because its owner was reclaimed is a story every order of the Wellspring tells, and it stays rare and argued over.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Tempering Maturity, Resilience Integrity, Dominion Fate. Past trauma sites become the strongest points in the Crystal's architecture.

**Glyph** · None attested in the Index (see Discrepancies of Record on the hub).

**Failure** · Stress shielding. Load bypasses the reinforced region entirely, and everything adjacent to the old wound quietly demineralizes for want of use.

## Contrition · *The Broken Flame*

**The idea** · A failure owned. Only what a soul truly got wrong and truly survived turns into armor for it.

**Kind** · Spirit

**On the Body** · The body learns to bear what it has already failed against, and the tremor that took a fighter's hand in their first rout does not come a second time, because the same fear met again is answered at once. A rehearsed failure teaches the flesh nothing, and neither does anyone else's.

**On the Spirit** · A real failure, owned without excuse, is written into the Core as a standing answer, so the deceit or the despair that broke a soul once finds it already armed. An excuse voids the record, and the Core knows an excuse when it has made one.

**On Fate** · An admitted fault loses its hold: a broken oath said out loud and survived stops pulling on the Attraction Layer the way a hidden one does, and the next oath sworn over it tends to keep. Such a person is famously hard to blackmail, having already told everyone.

**Spirit** · Temperance through acceptance. *The confessors' reading:* a failure owned and survived.

**Alchemy** · The dose: a poison survived at its measure, and the body keeps the receipt. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Immunological memory and hormesis. Sub-lethal exposure producing adaptive benefit.

**Mechanism** · A pathogen survived once is answered faster and harder the second time because the response was recorded, and a sub-lethal dose of a stressor frequently leaves an organism measurably more capable than it was. Contrition converts failure into that record. The mechanism requires the failure to have been survived, and it requires it to have been genuine, because a rehearsed one produces no antibody. The record is kept in the soul, and reports of it reaching further, a body shrugging off the very poison its owner once confessed to, are few and argued over.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Tempering Maturity, Resilience Integrity. Past failures become structural reinforcement.

**Glyph** · None attested in the Index (see Discrepancies of Record on the hub).

**Failure** · Autoimmunity. The record generalizes past its target and the practitioner begins answering their own tissue as though it were the original insult.

## Phreatis · *The Waters of Memory*

> **Cross-listed with Fluxia.** The same Wellspring answers to two physics because memory itself does. The cross-listing looks like an archival error and is the only honest place to put it.

**The idea** · Memory as deep water. What is lived sinks in, is built into you, and comes back long after the weather has changed.

**Kind** · Medium

**On the Body** · What the body has lived sinks into the tissue and comes back when needed, so a Phreatis fighter's old drills surface whole in the hands decades after the last lesson.

**On the Spirit** · The practitioner reads and draws what has sunk into water and into a soul, and a working can call up what a place or person has long since stopped remembering.

**On Fate** · Nothing stays buried: old ties, debts and promises rise on their own schedule the way a spring does, and the holder can feel them coming.

**Spirit** · Temperance through reflection. *The confessors' reading:* memory, what is lived built into the structure.

**Alchemy** · The remembering menstruum: a solvent that keeps the trace of everything ever dissolved in it. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Long-term potentiation on the Vitalia reading. Aquifer recharge and isotopic dating on the Fluxia reading.

**Mechanism** · Synaptic potentiation records experience by physically strengthening a connection that fired, while groundwater carries an isotopic signature of the rain that fell decades before it reached the well. Phreatis holds both: memory as reinforced structure, and memory as a slow reservoir that returns what entered it long after the weather has changed.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Gnosis Retention, Harmonics Attunement, Resilience Anchoring. Memory becomes architecturally reinforced. Identical effect in either Family context.

**Glyph** · `[Ap]` Aperture; attested on two more.

**Failure** · Contamination of the aquifer. What enters the reservoir cannot be selectively removed later, and a falsified memory recharges as readily as a true one.

*What a working is, the laws it conditions, and the boundaries every working is written in.*

Dob Threlkeld, hedge-worker of Cobb's Wharf, once bet his mule that he could set a wet hay bale alight in a rainstorm, and he won, the whole bale going up at once with the rain still falling on it, and then lost the mule, the bale, the barn and his good name in three parishes when the fire went on obeying the law of fire after he had stopped looking at it. This shelf exists so that you can lose your mule for better reasons.

- [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Physical Account Two Sets of Books|The Physical Account: Two Sets of Books]]: What a working is. Two complete descriptions of every event, the soul's and the scholars', both true, and where the energy actually comes from.
- [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/The Sixty Wellsprings|The Sixty Wellsprings]]: The sixty alchemical and spiritual transformations, each a governing idea that changes how a soul uses Aether, a lens on magic already potent, and each explained by the scholars as a law of the world. Read the hub, then whichever Family you need.
- [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms|The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms]]: The boundaries imposed on those laws.
- [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Descent of Parun — The Fourteen Hands/The Descent of Parun — The Fourteen Hands|The Descent of Parun — The Fourteen Hands]]: The fourteen ways of writing a boundary down, and what each one changes. Its child, [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Descent of Parun — The Fourteen Hands/The Counted Speech — A Speaker's Grammar of Parun|The Counted Speech]], is why almost nobody says it aloud.

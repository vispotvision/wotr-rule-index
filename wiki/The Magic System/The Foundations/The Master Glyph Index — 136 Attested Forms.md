# The Master Glyph Index — 136 Attested Forms

*All one hundred and thirty-six attested Parun (root script of law) forms, ordered by Archon (one of the fourteen great powers of cosmic law)*

Every midwinter the Third Counter Society sits down to eel pie and too much brandy, and every midwinter someone climbs on the bench, roars that they have found a third form written to obstruct another form, and traces it on the table in spilled wine with the whole room howling. Then a sober apprentice runs the path across the lattice, and it comes out as Knot or Lock in a borrowed hat, and the claimant buys the brandy. Master Simeon Blackwell bought the brandy nine years running and stayed solvent only by marrying a vintner. The index below is what they are all failing to get round.

---

## What a Glyph Does

*The index is a filing system. This section is what the filed thing is.*

A glyph creates nothing and carries no power. A Wellspring (one of the sixty governing ideas that change how a soul uses Aether, which is mana, each of the sixty being one way a Titan's law shows up in the world) is the lens, and the glyph is where you tell it to stop. The Wellspring's law was already running before anyone wrote anything, and it continues running afterward. **A glyph limits what a Wellspring's idea is doing, at the layer the Essence (spiritual energy) leaves through.** The bench's old phrase for this is a boundary condition, and it still serves: the glyph moves a limit, and the law does what it has always done on the far side of the new limit.

This is why practitioners are burned, crushed and drowned by their own workings with such regularity. The glyph ends. The law does not. Everything a working produces goes on obeying every rule it has ever obeyed, including the ones nobody at the bench thought about, and the correct instinct at any threshold is to ask what the law will keep doing after the intent stops.

### The same path on each layer

Every soul meets the world through three layers, and a [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Three Paths Routing, Recognition and the Gate|Path]] is the layer it has learned to lean on. The path traced across the lattice is the same whichever layer the Essence leaves through, and so is the kind of limit. Where the limit lands is what changes.

| The Essence leaves through | Path | What the limit is | What runs on past it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Aether Shell** | Body | A physical limit: how far, how hot, how much load. The physics a scholar writes on a Wellspring's entry applies here in earnest | Matter, obeying everything matter obeys |
| **The Essence Core** | Spirit | What a thing is taken to be. Bai Lüxian's hinges kept their iron and lost the idea of being hinges | The meaning, which goes on being said after anyone wants it said |
| **The Attraction Layer** | Fate | A term or a prerequisite, inside the soul's own share of fate: what has to be so first | The odds, leaning for as long as the term stands |

A Wellspring's kind names its home plane and changes the bill, as follows.

- **A Spirit Wellspring on the Body Path** still gives no fire. Its limit falls on what the flesh can bear.
- **A Fate Wellspring** limits a bond, a debt, a name or the odds on every Path, and the physics in its entry is only the scholars' analogue, there to give them something to argue with.
- **A Wild Wellspring on the Fate Path** limits a claim. Pyreveil on that road is a claim that burns its way through, and its glyph says whose oaths may catch and whose may not.
- **A Medium Wellspring** is at home on all three.
- **Home or away** changes the resonance and the Essence cost, double the effect at home with years of mastery and half again at most away. It never changes the path.

Glyphica, the writing of law, sits most comfortably on the Core, and any Path may take it up: a Body cutter's ward stops the bottle, and a Spirit cutter's is taken for a wall. On every Path the Continuum (the whole lawful order of reality) reads Authorization off the Attraction Layer, because the tie of harmonization is held there, so every cutter has dealings with the shallow end of Fate whether they like it or not.

> **The three-part reading of any entry.** The **seated Wellspring and its kind** say which law is being limited. The **Meaning and Use** say what kind of limit it is. The **Path** says where it lands. A technique is those three decisions and nothing else, and everything downstream is the law's own behavior.

### One form in one sentence on three Paths

A sentence in Parun has four slots: a Warrant (by what right), a Ground (over what), a Vector (which way) and a Seal (until when). A form from this index fills whichever slot its limit belongs to, and the plain words of [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Descent of Parun — The Fourteen Hands/The Counted Speech — A Speaker's Grammar of Parun|the Lexicon]] fill the rest. The Wellspring is never written at all. It is named by harmonization under the Warrant, read off the soul that stands behind the sentence.

Take `[Th]` Foundation, seated on Petralon (Wild): *Imposes geometry; reinforces boundaries and load paths.* Three masons, each of whom has tempered on Petralon and built her own gate, cut the same sentence over the arch:

> **oyeth · [Th] · mos · theb**
>
> By a thing I made with my hands · Foundation · held still · ending when the maker dies, and not before

| Her Path | What she has limited | What happens when the ram comes | What the law goes on doing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Body** (Petralon at home) | The load, through the Shell: it stays in the line of the stones | The force goes down the jambs and into the footing, and the lintel does not shear. The gate holds until the footing goes | It keeps sending weight to the floor. The gate settles a finger a decade, and somebody's grandchild digs out the threshold |
| **Spirit** (Petralon away) | What the gate is taken to be, through the Core: it is taken for the wall it stands in | A ghost, a summoned thing or a thrower reading the place finds no door. The men on the ram find a door, oak and iron, and break it | It goes on saying *wall*. Her own dead cannot find their way home through it, and the priest she pays to fix that cannot lift it |
| **Fate** (Petralon away) | A term on her own share of fate: before this gate falls, it goes through me | No thread comes down. The ram's axle gives, the crew's relief is late, and the bad luck of the siege runs down through her and into the ground. She sinks a little each day of it | A gate with a real way of falling still falls, and what the term could not hold comes out of her |

All three masons can be Thread Ø. None of them has moved anything that did not already have a way of happening.

### A glyph and a thread

A glyph and its Seal spend the soul's own share of fate. They set terms, and they move only what already has a way of happening. **They never pull.** A thread of Fate is another order of thing: it comes down from above through the [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/The Making of the World/The Veil|Veil]]'s outer face, taking hold of one is extreme effort at every Stage, and how well anyone can do it is read on [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/Classes and the Three Ladders|Thread Class]]. No path on the lattice is a thread, which is why this register holds no form called Thread and never has. The filament of the [[The Disciplines/Techniques/Seraphic Thread Blessing|Seraphic Thread Blessing]] is the common confusion. It has the name, and it is a bond between two souls, given its weight in the shallow water of the Plane of Fate.

Where a Meaning below says *thread*, it means a strand of memory, of rot or of a bond, and where it says *fate-thread* or *fate-tag* it means a sworn thing in that same shallow water. Three cases mark the edge.

- **A Seal that waits.** `wolyeth` ends a working when the last of a lineage is dead. It kills nobody. It sets a term, and any speaker at Thread Ø can say it.
- **Knot on a sworn bond.** `[Knt]` Knot supersedes a bound fate-thread, and the bond is somebody's, so it is a contest: Dominion Fate (the soul's weight in Fate) against the target's Persistence and Continuity. Nothing is pulled and the loser's oath is still sworn, superseded and sore.
- **A Fourth Seal.** A sentence at that level does not wait for a line to end. It ends the line, which is past what any soul's own share will buy, so it needs a grip on Fate, or a Warrant that carries one.

### The eleven with no form

Eleven of the Sixty appear in no row of this index, seated or attested: Pyreveil, Cinerion, Manganthra, Ascensio and Abython (Wild); Nyxial, Penance, Redemption and Contrition (Spirit); Tarturon (Fate); and Aurevane (Medium). They do not line up by kind, and none of them is on any Archon's list of associated Wellsprings. **No Archon reads these eleven directly, so they take the thirty kind-of-limit forms and no others.** A seated form is a station's reading of one law, and no station has read these. A kind of limit applies to any law at all, and that gives the blank thirty their work. So a Pyreveil cutter writes in `[Hael]` Flame or `[RenB]` Boundary, a Cinerion rune is cut in the same forms, and the [[The Disciplines/Techniques/Corona Lunaris|Moon of a Hundred Noons]], an Aurevane art, lays `[RenB]` Boundary across `[On]` Light. It also explains why the best-known lens in the trade has nothing of its own to show a student on the first day.

### The Loom, the lattice and the stations

The lattice of this page is a fixed five-by-five grid, the kamea of Mars, and every form is a path across it. **The Loom of Stars is what that lattice looks like from above.** Bara wrote it, and from the Veil's outer face it is golden filaments crossing at impossible angles, each one tied to the pulse of a consequence. A scholar who has seen only the page draws squares. They are the same object, and nobody has yet survived the comparison with enough composure to publish it.

**The [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Archons/The Fourteen Archons|Archons]] read it from stations.** Each of the fourteen holds one position, and each of the 136 forms is a reading taken from one of them, or from two where a Secondary Archon is named, which is why every row below has an owner. An Epoch has no station. Bara stands above the seats, and the forms the old registers list beside the [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/The Making of the World/The Three Epochs|Three Epochs]] are unattested here: they are read from no station, and nobody can cut one. Where the same word turns up below, as Knot does under Maelor, a station has read a little of the same thing from underneath.

**A Work is a grip and never a glyph.** The Seven Works of [[The Disciplines/Schools and Bloodline Arts/Hataraki — The Works of the Plane of Fate/Hataraki — The Works of the Plane of Fate|Hataraki]] are functions Bara wrote into the Plane of Fate, and a Work is an inherited way of taking hold of one. It traces no path, fills no slot and adds up to nothing. **The Moto read by delegated sight, from the Loom**, the Kōkan Line having administered it under leave from above the stations. That is why the Lexicon has no Warrant word for what they hold: a word's value is a sum of cells, and cells are what the stations read. The Vaulted Ring writes down the sound a Moto speaker opens on and leaves the value blank.

**By what right.** Irath's small station sees the part of the lattice that asks this, and holds Command and Dominion for it. The Warrant is a sentence's answer to the same question, and Dominion Fate is how that answer is weighed when somebody contests it. The three go by different names on different shelves, and they are one idea.

| **The Loom of Stars** | The same grid seen from above |

| **`[En]` Lattice** | One form in the register, and an engineer's word: how a structure distributes stress |

### Runes, tags, chants and how long a working lasts

The four roles of the [[The Magic System/Power in Use/The Working Trades|working trades]] and the four slots of the Counted Speech are one list under two names. Authorization is the Warrant, Boundary the Ground, Direction the Vector and Sealing the Seal.

**What a fault does.** A slot left wholly empty does nothing: no seal, no working, no cost, which is why a Valorin can bank whole formulae one word short and sleep beside them. A sentence that fires with a bad limit runs. The Long Loaf of Mothwick had its Boundary begun and abandoned before the last strokes, and it rose for months. The Long Noon of Skelby had a seal cut by somebody's nephew, and it burned for thirteen days. Only a sentence that has actually fired can hurt anyone, and those two fired.

**What must add up.** Only spoken Parun must sum to sixty-five or a multiple of it. In the mouth the sum is all that can close a sentence, since there is nothing else for it to rest on. A cut hand is closed by its sealing stroke and held by what it is cut into, and a cutter who wants to count anyway is welcome to.

**How long.** The Seal sets the term, the substrate caps it, and anchoring holds it. The term is whatever the Seal says: first light, the maker's death, the debt repaid. The substrate is the most it can have: granite outlives the mason and wet bark outlives the afternoon, and a spoken sentence's substrate is the speaker. Anchoring is the Attraction Layer claiming the working as a presence in the world, the last step of every working, and without it the form dissolves the moment attention lapses. A cut sentence is anchored once, at its closing, and the surface carries the claim from then on, which is how a ward outlives its cutter. A spoken one stays on the speaker's own Layer for as long as its term runs. Most field speaking seals on attention because that is cheap. Sergeant Thorn's sentence at Dunmere Gate held to first light because she carried it there, on her feet, all night. A sentence that never sealed has nothing holding it and nothing stopping it, and it drinks the Aether of the place the way an ambient tag does until the place runs out or someone cuts across the line.

**Runes and tags.** A [[The Magic System/Power in Use/Runes and Tags|rune]] is the whole sentence in a substrate, and it works on anyone who passes. A tag is a short sentence that waits for a thumb. Its Warrant closes at the firing, and the Layer read is the one behind that thumb, which is why only the woken can fire one.

**Chants.** A chant is not Parun. A sound may not repeat inside a Parun word, and a chant is mostly repetition. It sets its condition in resonance, as Gravetongue does, and it does the same four jobs in tone: who sings and by what right, how far the sound carries, which way it runs, and the decay when it stops. Its words carry a sentence only when Glyphica is present, as when a chorister who has the count sings over a cut ward, and then that sentence answers to the rules above.

**Three notations, kept apart.** A Lexicon word is a run of sounds with a value. A form in this index is a named path, and its bracket mark is a filing mark and no sound at all, so the Vector `ma` and `[Ma]` Recall have nothing to do with each other. The strings of syllables in older rune manuals are a third and drifted notation, and they cross-check against neither.

### Why the Counter class holds only two

**Knot** and **Lock** are the only two forms in the index written to obstruct another form, and under the limit reading the scarcity stops being a curiosity.

A glyph cannot attack a law. Laws are not the sort of thing that can be opposed; the practitioner who tries is arguing with the tide and will lose on schedule. What a counter does instead is impose a limit that **prevents another limit from being established**, a condition on the conditioning rather than on the phenomenon.

There are two of them because there are two ways to do it. **Knot** entangles a path so it cannot be traced cleanly. **Lock** occupies a limit so no other limit can be set there. Every counter-working ever recorded is one of those two moves, and the four centuries of effort spent looking for a third have produced synonyms.

The Valorin's Errata looks like a third to everyone who has had a working corrected in mid-air, and the Society has had it on the bench more than once. It obstructs nothing. A correction amends a sentence that is already being said, blunting or redirecting it, and the amended sentence fires. Chaos is the other thing people bring to the brandy. Uurgath holds eleven forms in this index, and all eleven are legible limits on how things come apart, Ruin and Rupture among them. True Chaos is what breaks the sum itself, which is why it has no form, and why Errata finds nothing there to correct.

### Why thirty carry no tally

The unclassified thirty are seated on no Wellspring, and the standing recommendation has been to leave the blank alone. The limit reading gives a reason to, rather than merely a preference.

A seat names **the law being limited.** Fifteen of the unclassified are first-order Roots: Flame, Light, Passage, Boundary, Ruin, Vastness, Command, Dominion. None of these names a law. Each names a **kind of limit**, applicable to any law at all, which is precisely why the Codex (the Master Codex, the standing register of glyphs and Wellsprings) can classify a derivative of the thing and cannot classify the thing.
> *Whether that is an archival gap or a property of the Continuum remains unstated. The mechanism explains why the column is empty. It does not explain why the world is built so the column has to be.*

Unseated does not mean unused. Thirteen of the thirty have attested Wellsprings all the same, Silence and Truth among the widest in the register.

## How to Read an Entry

Each of the 136 entries carries its **Glyph, Name, Class, Primary Archon, Secondary Archon, Meaning & Use, seated Wellspring** with its kind, and its **reach**: every Wellspring attested for it, sorted by kind.

**Worked example.** `[Ur]` **Balance** · Root · Urion · *Stabilizes Essence gradients; equalizes opposing forces.* · Seated on **Judicium** (Wild).
> Its reach runs to **seven of the Sixty across three kinds**: Wild (Exuroth, Ignivale, Judicium, Petralon), Spirit (Mirithane, Thaloré) and Medium (Fixatio). *A root that broad is a load-bearing member and no specialist's glyph, and the number of kinds that reach for it is the measure of that.*
Compare `[Lei]` **Binding** · Root · Maelor · attested in **two Wellsprings of two kinds**, Anamnesis (Medium) and Mirithane (Spirit). *Narrow, deep, and the whole of it is memory work.*
> **The Secondary Archon column is sparsely populated.** Where it is filled, the glyph is read from two stations at once, **and the path is the intersection rather than either reading alone.**

**The Titans' grounds.** Fourteen names in the reach column stand outside the Sixty, three cells each: Breath, Collapse, Continuum, Dread, Equilibrium, Etherea, Finality, Form, Foundation, Oblivion, Renewal, Shadow, Strain and Veils. Those are the grounds of the [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Fourteen Titans|Fourteen Titans]], each of them a Titan's law at full scale. Titans descend from Archons, so a form read from an Archon's station can serve a Titan's ground, and these rows are where it has been seen to. They do not make the Sixty any larger.

## Standing Note

> **Every technique entry in the register closes on a Codex assignment line.** That line points here, and it names the Wellspring and its kind, the Path, the glyph, and the limit stated in that Path's terms.
>
> **Assignment is decided after the concept and the phenomenon, never before**, and it is a filing decision rather than a generative one. *If no form fits, the correct move is to say so in the same sitting, and not to bend the phenomenon until it files.*
>
> **Any path can be traced, and attestation is what is fixed.** A new name reduces letter by letter to cells like any other, so the method never runs out, and a working may use the trace at the maker's own risk. It is a sentence no station has been seen to read. It stays in that working's own notes, and it does not enter this index.

## Amendments

*The 136 stay fixed, and a form's attested lists can still grow at the edges when a working on file shows the reach. Each extension is entered here with its reason, so that a reader checking a Codex line finds it, and it is entered in the Codex's own columns, since the Codex is what it amends.*

**[Abys] Deep · Oblation and Fluxia added.** Ruled 2026-09-12. [Abys] Deep, a directional undertow of sub-Realm pressure, carried an Abyntheus, Fulguria, Electromagnetism row. Its All Attested Wellsprings field now includes Oblation and its All Attested Families field now includes Fluxia, taken on the pitch in Obrenkael · The Mule (Summoned and Bound), Deadweight, on the reasoning that a psychopomp's road is a directional undertow of sub-Realm pressure and that Abyntheus and Oblation are both pressure-grammar Wellsprings under different Archons. Primary Wellspring, Family and Physics Domain are unchanged. [Th] Foundation, drafted beside it in the same chain, is not amended and stays cross-family.

*In this index's terms: `[Abys]` Deep stays seated on Abyntheus (Spirit), and its reach now includes Oblation (Fate).*

# Fate Wellsprings

*Six of the Sixty, at home on the Plane of Fate · the kind you only find out about afterward*

Sweet Agnes Dunnock kept the book on the Reed Cup for twenty-two years and in all that time nobody heard her use the word *will*. Ask who wins and she would look at the river for a while, with deep teal afterimages of strokes not yet rowed flickering round the boats, and taste the rain that had not fallen yet, and say: sixty-one parts Sedge Street, twenty-eight the Ferrymen, eleven the weather. She was right more often than the priests. One year, two nights without sleep, she saw an Upper Weir win so bright she took it for the only river there was, and she put the house, the boat and the dog on it. The Upper Weir went under at the second bend, with a noise like a cow sitting down in a pond. It had been carrying eleven percent. She has kept the dog, by private arrangement with the new owner, and the odds she quotes on her own recovery are not good.

**What Fate means** · A Wellspring of Fate is a change in what holds: a bond, a debt, a name, the odds. Its home is the Plane of Fate, Bara's, above the lower realities, and its Aether (mana, the raw stuff of magic) works on what binds, with the Attraction Layer as the active layer. There is no burn in the hands and nothing on the ground to see. The landscape looks normal and the instruments read normal, but luck goes strange, oaths bite, strangers keep meeting, and the surveyor's plans keep working out in ways the surveyor did not plan. Neither body nor environment is a reliable instrument on this ground, and the only one that reads it is a written log kept over days.

**The lens** · A soul tempered on one of these carries its idea in its presence and its dealings: a little at first, and after years of mastery on the Fate Path, its home, up to double, so that the same claim at the same Grade binds twice as hard on the people it touches. Any Path can take any of them, and a Body Path fighter on one gets no extra force, only a little more of the idea in where things happen to fall. Away from home half again is the most anyone has got out of one, at a higher Essence (spiritual energy) cost and with a rougher result, and the odds on your recovery are posted daily. Using one is a different business from pulling on one: the lens spends the soul's own share, and a true pull reaches above it and is paid for separately. The full account of the lens is on the [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/The Sixty Wellsprings|hub]].

*Each Wellspring below opens on what it is. **The idea** is the concept it lays over a soul's Aether, and **The turn**, where the trade has fixed one, is the moment that idea comes clear to someone working inside it. **Kind** names its home plane, and never locks it. **Titan** is the Titan whose law it is one showing of, **Hue** the color it gives an aura, **Ground** what it favors and hates underfoot, and **Contrary** the Wellsprings whose laws fight it in one soul. **On the Body**, **On the Spirit** and **On Fate** say what the lens does to Essence on each Path, through the Aether Shell (the layer of the soul that meets matter), the Essence Core (the layer that holds identity and authors what a working is) and the Attraction Layer (a soul's own share of fate, its bonds, oaths, claims and odds). **Spirit** is the transformation it works on the soul, one road of Temperance (the climb of a soul from Stage to Stage), and **Alchemy** the principle it stands for at the bench. Then comes how scholars explain it: Analogue and Mechanism give the physics of what that type of Aether does to a Soul Crystal, Stat Effect what shifts on the sheet, Glyph the forms the Index seats on it, and Failure how it goes wrong. Entries run in alphabetical order.*

## Benediction · *The Wellspring of Grace*

**The idea** · Grace. A bond freely given, and weighed by the world as a real thing.

**The turn** · Giving without counting, and finding the count was always in your favor.

**Kind** · Fate

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Spiritual Seat — Omnis, Florwyn, Dazkar, Nysera, Sylorin, Ilgrimm|Florwyn]]: the mercy in her paradox: what lives owes, and may give without counting.

**Hue** · Warm red-gold.

**Ground** · Favors wedding groves, oath trees and hospices; hates sterile ground and ground where a sworn bond was broken. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with no phenomenon for the ground to act on, it answers in a fight only to Aetheric Density.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Fate Wellsprings|Oblation]] (the price paid to the grain against the gift with no count); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Fate Wellsprings|Tarturon]] (what you owe against what was given freely).

**On the Body** · A bond freely given lends the one blessed a real measure of steadiness, felt in the hands and the footing. There is no light to show for it beyond a soldier who has stopped trembling.

**On the Spirit** · A working carries the weight of the bond behind it, so a ward raised for a sworn friend holds where the same ward raised for a stranger would crack. The Core cannot manufacture this, and a bond that was never really given adds nothing at all.

**On Fate** · At home it is a bond the world weighs as a real thing, and an oath under it bears load, so a promise made in this grace can be stood on, leaned on and, when it counts, pulled on across a continent. The one it is given to fights with the odds spoken for in one place that matters, the spear going a hand wide or the bridge promised to a column holding for that column, and the giver pays in their own blood and years. It has to be freely given, which no house has yet managed to buy, and when the grace is taken back the column is still standing wherever the promise had put it.

**Spirit** · Temperance through compassion. *The confessors' reading:* grace, in which a bond becomes a thing the world can weigh.

**Alchemy** · Affinity: why this salt takes to that metal and will not let go. *(the trade's reading)* Gold carries it.

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Binding affinity. The dissociation constant of a specific high-affinity bond.

**Mechanism** · The strength of a biological bond is a measurable physical quantity, and a low dissociation constant means the complex, once formed, spends almost all of its time formed. Benediction gives bonds on the Plane of Fate that same measurability. This is the mechanical reason an oath sworn under Benediction carries system weight: it has an affinity figure, and the Continuum reads it.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Harmonics Empathy, Axis, Resilience Oath. Bonds formed under Benediction carry mechanical weight in the system.

**Glyph** · None primary. Attested on `[Dn]` Dawn, `[Blm]` Bloom, `[Blt]` Blight and `[Stg]` Stagnate.

> **Failure** · Competitive inhibition. A rival ligand with higher affinity displaces the existing bond, and the displacement is silent, complete, and not felt until the bond is called on.

## Oblation · *The Offering Flame*

**The idea** · The offering. A bargain struck on the Plane of Fate: what you give up comes back as force, and never one grain more than you gave.

**The turn** · Discovering that surrender and strength are the same posture.

**Kind** · Fate

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Spiritual Seat — Omnis, Florwyn, Dazkar, Nysera, Sylorin, Ilgrimm|Dazkar]]: the law of equivalence, never one grain more than was given.

**Hue** · The warmest teal, amber at the edge.

**Ground** · Favors altars, toll bridges and old bargain-stones; hates ground where nothing has ever been given up. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with no phenomenon for the ground to act on, it answers in a fight only to Aetheric Density.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Fate Wellsprings|Benediction]] (the price paid to the grain against the gift with no count); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Transmutatio]] (force bought with a loss against change with no loss).

**On the Body** · Something laid down comes back in the arm at exact par: a fighter who gives up a year of good knees gets a year of good knees' worth of force, and the reckoning is not open to appeal.

**On the Spirit** · A working carries whatever was given up to make it, and one paid for with a memory or a name is unmistakably heavier, by exactly that memory or name.

**On Fate** · The offering is a bargain that will be honored: what is surrendered on the Attraction Layer reappears elsewhere as pull at exact measure, and the books are kept by something that does not round up. A duelist lays down the use of a hand for good and the other fighter's guard comes open by a hand's worth, and a commander who burns his own baggage and his road home sees the enemy's relief lose a day on the march for each one he gave up. No lens on the Path is more honest about what this costs, and the price has to be really yours, so a general spending a company he never loved has bought considerably less than he thinks.

**Spirit** · Temperance through sacrifice.

**Alchemy** · The offering in the vessel: what goes in as loss comes out as force, and the total never rises. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Bernoulli's principle. Conservation of the total head across a constriction.

**Mechanism** · Fluid accelerating through a narrowing does not gain energy. It trades pressure for velocity, and the sum across the streamline is conserved. That is the scholars' account of a bargain with the Plane of Fate. Oblation formalizes soul-cost as exactly that trade: what is surrendered in one term, a bond, a claim, a year or a name held on the Attraction Layer, appears in another, and the total is not increased by the sacrifice. **Practitioners who understand this stop describing the Wellspring as generosity.**

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Harmonics Axis, Dominion Command, Tempering Yield. Willingness to pay soul-cost translates directly into Essence generation.

**Glyph** · `[Ob]` Obol; attested on three more.

**Failure** · Cavitation. Drop the pressure term far enough and the fluid boils at ambient temperature, and the collapsing voids pit the conduit they formed in.

## Oblivara · *The Dark Mirror*

**The idea** · Keeping your name. A self written down on the Plane of Fate so many times that it cannot be erased, with whatever is done to it sent back where it came from.

**Kind** · Fate

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Abyssal Depths — Prometheus, Zar'thuun, Vohrin|Zar'thuun]]: reflection through terror, a name that has stood at his boundary and come back.

**Hue** · Black with returning sparks.

**Ground** · Favors archives, name-walls and tombs whose names have been cut again and again; hates crowd density and active Domains. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with no phenomenon for the ground to act on, it answers in a fight only to Aetheric Density.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Nihiloth]] (a name that cannot be erased against a law of erasing); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Materia Primordia]] (one self written down against one self in many forms).

**On the Body** · A strange and modest gift: the flesh keeps a stubborn notion of whose it is, so a curse, a graft or a possession finds the body reasserting its owner, and what was meant to change them travels back up the arm that sent it. It will not turn a sword.

**On the Spirit** · Inscribed in the Core, the name is a second copy of the self held against loss, so a working that tells the soul what it is gets told the same thing in return, weakly and a little late. A soul written this way can lose a great deal and still be read back from what survives.

**On Fate** · At home the name is held in many hands, every oath it swore and every mouth that ever cursed it counting as a copy, and what is done to it, erasure or false witness or an eye turned aside, goes back along the line it came in by. A Fate working aimed at such a person can travel home, so the slipped guard turns up on the arranger, though each return spends copies the holder does not get back, and past a limit the name that comes back is confident, complete and wrong. A practitioner can lose a whole city's memory of them and still walk in on the strength of one old debt, and one general thought dead for a decade spent the last of his copies raising an army out of unpaid promises.

**Spirit** · Temperance through erasure. *The confessors' reading:* keeping one's name against erasure.

**Alchemy** · The second charge: a duplicate kept back so a ruined batch can be read against it. *(the trade's reading)* Obsidian holds it.

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Error-correcting redundancy. Retroreflection along the incident vector.

**Mechanism** · Information survives a noisy channel when it is encoded with enough redundancy that corrupted symbols can be reconstructed from the surviving ones, and a corner reflector returns incident energy precisely along the path it arrived on. That is the scholars' account of what happens to a name on the Plane of Fate, where it is a claim the world goes on honoring and every oath, debt and memory that holds it counts as a copy. Oblivara does both: the practitioner's identity is redundantly encoded against erasure, and attempts at perceptual manipulation return to their source, along with anything else done to the name.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Resilience Persistence, Gnosis Cognition. Resistance to conceptual erasure and perceptual manipulation.

**Glyph** · None primary. Attested on `[Vn]` Void, `[NmEl]` Name-Eater, `[Ora]` Truth and `[El]` Life.

**Failure** · Code exhaustion. Redundancy has a correction limit, and past it the reconstruction is confident, complete, and wrong.

## Somnalis · *The Dream Veil*

**The idea** · Living among what might be. The near possibilities of the Plane of Fate held in view at once, each with its odds on it, and a soul at ease in the not knowing.

**Kind** · Fate

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Spiritual Seat — Omnis, Florwyn, Dazkar, Nysera, Sylorin, Ilgrimm|Sylorin]]: the near branches of the river, every way the current might yet run. His Ferryman's Current is written in this Wellspring.

**Hue** · Teal with ghost outlines.

**Ground** · Favors crossroads, ferries, betting yards and any ground where many plans cross; hates empty country with nobody in it to choose. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with no phenomenon for the ground to act on, it answers in a fight only to Aetheric Density.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Medium Wellsprings|Fixatio]] (every branch held open against the way back closed).

**On the Body** · The flesh gets no extra force and a little ease with the odds: the foot comes down on the dry stone more often than it should, and the hands are already moving toward the likeliest of three blows.

**On the Spirit** · The Core can hold a person or a working as the spread of what it might yet be, so a thing can be read, and very rarely written on, in terms of what it has not become.

**On Fate** · Near possibilities stand in view with their weights on them, the pull behind each felt on the Attraction Layer like current on a line, and seeing them is the whole of what most holders ever do. A lean takes much of the reserve and often fails, and when it takes, of three blows the opponent might throw it is the one the guard is waiting for, or once in a campaign the rain comes an hour before the cavalry crosses instead of an hour after. Nobody can reason out which way to lean, as a certain Reed Cup bookmaker's house, boat and dog can attest.

**Spirit** · Temperance through vision. *The confessors' reading:* uncertainty, living easy among adjacent possibilities.

**Alchemy** · The sealed egg before it turns: every color it might go, held at once and unread. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Ensemble forecasting. Sensitive dependence and the spread of possible states.

**Mechanism** · A turbulent system cannot be predicted from a single initial condition, so forecasters run many slightly perturbed copies and read the spread rather than any one trajectory. That is the scholars' account, and what it describes is the Plane of Fate, where what has not yet happened already has weight. Somnalis opens the Attraction Layer onto that ensemble: adjacent possibilities perceived as a distribution with weights, each weight the pull of Attraction Force on a thing that could still go either way, which is why its bearers speak about the future in probabilities and are correct more often than people who speak in certainties. Most of them only ever read the spread. Far down the Path a bearer can lean on it, and the luck around them goes strange.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Gnosis Forecast, Dominion Sense. The practitioner perceives adjacent possibilities as clearly as present facts.

**Glyph** · `[Vor]` Return, `[SoulDr]` Soul-Drift, `[Au]` Death; attested on eleven more.

> **Failure** · Ensemble collapse. The spread is mistaken for a single reading, and the practitioner acts on a branch that carried eleven percent of the weight.

## Tarturon · *The Fire of Reckoning*

**The idea** · Reckoning. Power that rises with what you believe you owe, held on the Plane of Fate as a debt whether or not anyone else would recognize it, and that nothing can smother once it is lit.

**Kind** · Fate

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Spiritual Seat — Omnis, Florwyn, Dazkar, Nysera, Sylorin, Ilgrimm|Dazkar]]: debt held to the last grain. Without him Essence piles up debt with no resolution, and this is what resolution feels like from inside.

**Hue** · Hot amber-red.

**Ground** · Favors debtors' prisons, counting houses and foundations sworn over; hates open water and moving platforms. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with no phenomenon for the ground to act on, it answers in a fight only to Aetheric Density.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Spirit Wellsprings|Hypnather]] (the soul at rest against a debt that will not let it lie down); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Spirit Wellsprings|Nyxial]] (the regulated flame against the reaction with no stopping condition); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Spirit Wellsprings|Absolution]] (the debt held against the debt let go); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Fate Wellsprings|Benediction]] (what you owe against what was given freely).

**On the Body** · On the fist it seeds a burning that needs no air and reads the debt rather than the muscle, so a man who believes he deserves the rope hits like a furnace. It is dearer and rougher here than at home, and the man who forgives himself finds nothing left to light.

**On the Spirit** · The Core turns the sum owed into authorship, so a thrown working lands as a verdict its own caster has already passed on themselves and hits as hard as that verdict was. Talk yourself out of the guilt and the working will not catch, which makes this a poor road for the comfortable.

**On Fate** · At home it reads the whole account on the Attraction Layer, every oath broken and every death unpaid, and pulls power up those lines in proportion to what the holder believes is owed, so a Domain on this lens is a courtroom with one defendant. A fighter who believes they owe a death can draw every odd in an exchange up that line toward the one man it is owed to, and a general who believes he owes a burned city its justice may march like a sentence being carried out, the labor of a ruined lifetime. No creditor has to agree and none can call it off, the sum that pays for the fire grows with the burning, and nobody has been known to finish paying it.

**Spirit** · Temperance through ordeal; correction through consequence, a soul's output rising when it believes it deserves to pay.

**Alchemy** · A charge that carries its own fire: once lit, nothing smothers it. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Yield criterion and post-yield response. A reaction carrying its own oxidizer.

**Mechanism** · Below yield a material returns to shape and the load leaves no record. Above it, behavior changes kind, and the response scales with the load applied rather than with anything the material chose. Tarturon couples output to moral weight through exactly that curve, and the load it reads is a debt on the Attraction Layer (a soul's own share of fate): what the person believes they owe, pulling on them as hard as any sworn bond, with or without a creditor to agree. Like thermite (a mix that brings its own burning air) it supplies its own oxidizer, which is the mechanical reason the reaction cannot be smothered once initiated.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Ardency Flux under guilt-amplification, Tempering Overflow, Resilience Fortification. Output spikes under moral weight.

**Glyph** · None attested in the Index (see Where the Registers Disagree on the hub).

> **Failure** · Runaway. The load that drives the reaction is generated by the reaction, and nothing in the mechanism provides a stopping condition.

## Transference · *The Living Exchange*

**The idea** · The living exchange. Two souls bound on the Plane of Fate and in tune, giving and taking with nothing carried between them, so that what one holds the other can use.

**Kind** · Fate

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Spiritual Seat — Omnis, Florwyn, Dazkar, Nysera, Sylorin, Ilgrimm|Sylorin]]: continuity between two souls, with no body making the trip until the end of it.

**Hue** · Median violet.

**Ground** · Favors Veil-thin zones, established Realmgates and well-traveled roads; hates warp scarring, contested Domains and unmapped terrain. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with no phenomenon for the ground to act on, it answers in a fight only to Aetheric Density.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Spirit Wellsprings|Nyxial]] (holding a level against giving it away down the gradient).

**On the Body** · Strength moves along a bond between two tuned bodies, so one fighter's rested legs turn up under another's tired ones across a short gap, and the body itself can be carried down the line after its energy.

**On the Spirit** · Two Cores in tune can pass a working hand to hand with nothing carried between them, so what one has learned or written the other can use for as long as the tuning holds.

**On Fate** · The bond itself is the road: Essence, fortune and place run along it toward whoever has less, by a gradient neither party agreed to in so many words. Deliberately drawn, it puts the rested partner's luck under the tired one, and one of a pair gets the clean exchange the other has just bled for. A commander who spends decades laying such a road between two wings can, on one day, have the flank with the bad morning draw on the flank with the good one, though the coupling falls off so sharply with distance that partners on this lens learn not to travel apart, and the giver has been known to fold up in the road.

**Spirit** · Temperance through empathy.

**Alchemy** · The tincture shared: two matched vessels, one giving and one taking, with no pour between them. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Resonant energy transfer between coupled oscillators. Flow along a potential gradient.

**Mechanism** · Two oscillators tuned to the same frequency and held near each other exchange energy without a conductor between them, and the coupling strength falls off sharply with separation. That is the scholars' account of a bond on the Plane of Fate. Transference makes the Attraction Layer such an oscillator. Essence moves to an ally because the two Crystals are in tune and near, with nobody pushing, and a gradient does the rest: the tuning is the bond, and Attraction Force running along it does the carrying. What the tuning swaps is potential, and it does not have to stop at Essence. Tune the Layer to a mass in place of a Crystal and the two ends become exchangeable: the energy passes first along the line, and the body it belonged to is carried after it by the gradient, in real continuous travel that is merely very fast. Set two bodies in tune with an anchor between them and they trade places. The coupling falls off so sharply that thirty meters is a long crossing, and this is the whole of how the arts that travel get from here to there.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Harmonics Synergy, Projection, Tempering Yield. Essence routes to allies and absorbs ambient Wellspring energy through relational proximity.

**Glyph** · `[Chn]` Chain-song; attested on three more.

> **Failure** · Reverse coupling. A gradient runs both directions, and a Transference practitioner beside a depleted ally will empty into them without deciding to.

*The story they tell.*

The Lantern Run is forty leagues of road in two days with a lit lamp in your hand, and for nine years nobody could touch the Sedgelys. Wilkin and Wynn, man and wife, ran it a stride apart with a violet glow passing back and forth between them that the eye kept refusing to look at, and a sound like two bells in sympathy, one of them always rested because the other had just paid for it, until the odds-men stopped quoting them at all. In the tenth year somebody's hired man put a crossbow bolt through Wilkin's thigh at the Hollow Mile. Wynn never decided anything. The gradient ran, she emptied into him where she stood, and the crowd at the post saw her fold up in the road forty yards short with her lamp still burning, and saw him sitting up behind her, bleeding and fresh as a colt and howling at her to stop.

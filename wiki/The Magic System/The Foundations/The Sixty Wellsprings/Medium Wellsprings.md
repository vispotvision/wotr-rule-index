# Medium Wellsprings

*Six of the Sixty, at home on all three planes · the kind that works on you while you work it*

Jory Quill of Long Sallow found water for a living, a penny a well and a penny more to tell you what the well remembered, with a copper-green sheen in his palms and dew beading on the bucket rope before he had touched it. Two villages hired him in the same dry June over the one spring between them, and he stood in the shallows in front of both with the water standing up round his shins like a dog that knows its name, took a mouthful, and told them whose great-grandfather had poisoned it and what he had been paid. The water moved in the ground that afternoon, so that the spring came up on the other bank. The old debt came due that same week in three marriages and a boundary stone. The two villages have been fighting in the happiest way ever since, with a joint fair and a rule about knives. Jory went home knowing a lullaby in a language nobody in the county speaks, and he cries when he hums it, and he will tell you for free that he has no idea whose it is. What goes into that water does not come out again, and for that half-hour he was in it.

**What Medium means** · A Medium Wellspring is at home on all three planes and still crosses the Veil, operating in every direction at once at the threshold where one plane's law softens into another's. Water and Light are the primary Medium elements. The practitioner reshapes the thing and is reshaped by it in the same act, and the observer is altered by the observation, so neither body nor Crystal gives a stable reference point. A strong Medium site looks to a visitor like illness or hallucination.

**The lens** · A soul tempered on one of these has the whole idea in everything it does, a little at first and after years of mastery up to double, and because all three Paths are home it can reach double on whichever one the soul walks. That sounds like a bargain, and the trade will gladly sell it to you as one. It costs no extra Essence (spiritual energy), and it goes both ways. Everything a Medium lens does to the world it is also doing to you, so a Medium practitioner of thirty years is a person with a great deal of company. The full account of the lens is on the [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/The Sixty Wellsprings|hub]].

*Each Wellspring below opens on what it is. **The idea** is the concept it lays over a soul's Aether, and **The turn**, where the trade has fixed one, is the moment that idea comes clear to someone working inside it. **Kind** names its home plane, and never locks it. **Titan** is the Titan whose law it is one showing of, **Hue** the color it gives an aura, **Ground** what it favors and hates underfoot, and **Contrary** the Wellsprings whose laws fight it in one soul. **On the Body**, **On the Spirit** and **On Fate** say what the lens does to Essence on each Path, through the Aether Shell (the layer of the soul that meets matter), the Essence Core (the layer that holds identity and authors what a working is) and the Attraction Layer (a soul's own share of fate, its bonds, oaths, claims and odds). **Spirit** is the transformation it works on the soul, one road of Temperance (the climb of a soul from Stage to Stage), and **Alchemy** the principle it stands for at the bench. Then comes how scholars explain it: Analogue and Mechanism give the physics of what that type of Aether does to a Soul Crystal, Stat Effect what shifts on the sheet, Glyph the forms the Index seats on it, and Failure how it goes wrong. Entries run in alphabetical order.*

## Anamnesis · *The Wellspring of Remembrance*

**The idea** · Remembrance. Everything keeps its past, and a soul under this lens can touch a thing and read it.

**Kind** · Medium

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Strata — Valmir, Kaldrion, Chronaeus|Chronaeus]]: the debris of events compacted into layers, and a soul that can read them.

**Hue** · Faded violet.

**Ground** · Favors darkness, silence, Aether-thin ground and ruins; hates saturated Aether, crowd density and active Domains.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Spirit Wellsprings|Letheveil]] (forgetting on purpose against everything keeping its past); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Sublimatio]] (the record kept against the lower state left behind).

**On the Body** · A palm on a spear shaft tells who carried it and how they died, and a hand on a broken wall gives back the blow that broke it, in enough detail for the practitioner's own shoulders to repeat it. The flesh under this lens forgets nothing it has ever met, so an old fighter has a lifetime of falls and parries standing ready in the joints.

**On the Spirit** · In the Core it reads a soul as a fired pot reads its kiln: what this person has committed to, what was done to them, how long ago and at what dose. Inscribed on a thing, it makes that thing's history part of what the thing is, which is how an old sword comes to know its trade.

**On Fate** · Every oath, debt and death leaves a record on the Attraction Layer and this lens reads it on contact, so a handshake can say who else this hand has sworn to, and a sword taken from a dead man can say which guard he always dropped. With a palm on a fortress's footing it finds the winter the mortar was skimped and the oldest claim on that ground, and calling either one in, so that the wall remembers what it is owed, takes a lifetime's skill and a body's worth of blood. Courts pay very well for the reading and then try to have the witness quietly sealed, and a careless pull erases the very record it was pulling on.

**Spirit** · Temperance through awareness. *The confessors' reading:* remembrance, which preserves charged records of lived commitment, and more than mere memory.

**Alchemy** · The fired pot's testimony: the residue in a vessel says everything it has ever held. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Remanent magnetization and thermoluminescent dosimetry.

**Mechanism** · Basalt records the direction of the field it cooled in and holds that record for geological time. Fired ceramic accumulates trapped electrons in proportion to the radiation dose it has absorbed since its last firing, and releases them as light when heated. Objects genuinely store their history in physically readable form, and so, each in its own layer, do a body, a soul and a sworn word, which is why this Wellspring is at home reading any of them. **Anamnesis is an instrument, and anyone calling it a miracle has not looked hard at a fired pot.**

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Gnosis Retention, Gnosis Analysis in reading past trauma, Resilience Anchoring. The practitioner reads history through contact with objects, places, and beings.

**Glyph** · `[Ma]` Recall, `[AnMa]` Anamnesis, `[Lei]` Binding, `[VaM]` Moment, `[Mn]` Mnemonic, `[EchoS]` Echo-Scar; attested on nine more.

**Failure** · Thermal resetting. Reading is not always passive, and a careless practitioner erases the record in the act of taking it.

## Aurevane · *The Wellspring of Light*

**The idea** · Light that does not flatter. What you see grows truer, and what you send out arrives exactly as you meant it.

**Kind** · Medium

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Spiritual Seat — Omnis, Florwyn, Dazkar, Nysera, Sylorin, Ilgrimm|Omnis]]: he carries light down into matter, and this is that light as it arrives.

**Hue** · Hard-edged white-blue.

**Ground** · Favors dry air, conductive substrate and a clear line of sight; hates iron-caged interiors, heavy ferrous shielding and dense fog.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Tenebra]] (light that arrives as sent against light that never comes back); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Vantabriel]] (emission against absorption); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Spirit Wellsprings|Eclipseron]] (seeing the thing against reading the hole it leaves).

**On the Body** · The eye resolves a fight down to the grain of a wrist, and what the limb sets out to do arrives with no slop in it, every ounce landing on the span of skin it was meant for.

**On the Spirit** · What leaves the Core goes out in phase and arrives exactly as it was meant, and whatever stands in that light shows what it is without flattery.

**On Fate** · A word or an oath sent under this lens arrives with its whole meaning intact, and presence carries clear across a square or a field, which is most of what its fighters do with it. Drawn tighter it can close the space between an order given and an order received, so a signal reads true through the smoke at the far wing, or for one breath the single shaft loosed at a gap in the harness meets nothing to spoil it. That breath costs a fair part of the reserve and leaves the eyes bleeding, and since all of it shines, the enemy's first crossbow of the morning always knows where to look.

**Spirit** · Temperance through illumination.

**Alchemy** · The clear light of the watched vessel: the color that tells the worker the truth about the charge. *(the trade's reading)* Iron conducts it.

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Optical resolution and coherence. The Rayleigh criterion; phase-locked emission.

**Mechanism** · Two points are resolvable only when their diffraction patterns separate, and coherent light holds a fixed phase relationship across its whole front, which is what separates a true beam from a bright lamp. Aurevane raises both figures at once. What the practitioner perceives resolves finer, and what they express leaves the Shell in phase, meaning the output matches the intent with almost no distortion introduced in transit. A stroke, a meaning and a sworn word can each arrive true or arrive smeared, which is why this lens is at home on all three Paths.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Gnosis Perception, Vigilance, Harmonics Attunement. What you see becomes truer. What you express becomes more accurate.

**Glyph** · None attested in the Index (see Where the Registers Disagree on the hub).

**Failure** · Glare. Coherent output is also a beacon, and Aurevane practitioners are the easiest people in any engagement to locate. Pushed too hard it turns on its own optics: past the Shell's damage threshold the coherent front burns the conduit it leaves by, and the eyes go first.

## Fixatio · *The Binding Flame*

**The idea** · Commitment. What is bound here stays bound, because the way back has stopped existing.

**Kind** · Medium

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Physical Order — Atla'zon and Gaia|Atla'zon]]: he decides whether Essence stays inside the shape it was poured into, and here it does.

**Hue** · Amber resin.

**Ground** · Favors mineral-rich rock, worked stone and deep foundation; hates open water, high altitude and moving platforms.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Manganthra]] (the go-between that stays out of the product against the bond nobody can leave); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Fate Wellsprings|Somnalis]] (every branch held open against the way back closed); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Dissolution]] (the bond let go against the bond past going back).

**On the Body** · The cure goes into the flesh: a grip closed under this lens does not open until told, a set bone holds through a fall, and a stance once taken has to be cut out of the ground. A fighter who sets a wound wrong carries the mistake to the grave.

**On the Spirit** · Written from the Core, an inscription is cured into what a thing is, so a seal stays sealed and a line of characters on a door is a fact about that door until the door is gone. Every sealing school in the four quarters trains on it, and each of them has one room it cannot open.

**On Fate** · An oath under this lens has no way back in it, and the sworn thing is treated as already done: claims hold across generations, bonds hold past deaths, and the terms of a Domain (a region where the world runs on a practitioner's terms) set as hard as law. A captain who binds her company to a breach can take every road off it out of the day's reckoning, and a duelist who binds one cut to one man can reduce the fight to a question of when, and either may stake the rest of a life on it. Few try it more than once, because a binding placed in error stays placed, which is why the old marriage contracts take a week to read and why some garrisons are still holding ground nobody wants.

**Spirit** · Temperance through permanence. *The confessors' reading:* commitment, in which what is held stays held.

**Alchemy** · One of the six named operations: it anchors and seals, and every Sealing chain on the bench runs under its law. The Bench files a Fixatio Draft as a Coagulation. Basalt takes it.

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Covalent cross-linking. Thermoset cure.

**Mechanism** · A thermoplastic can be melted and reformed indefinitely. A thermoset, once cured, has formed covalent bridges across its whole network and will char before it flows. Fixatio performs the cure. What it holds is held by the fact that the alternative configuration no longer exists as a reachable state, and force has nothing to do with it. A tendon, an inscription and a sworn word are all networks of a kind and all can be bridged past undoing, which is why the trade has never agreed on which road this Wellspring belongs to.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Fortitude, Resilience Anchoring. What is held by Fixatio stays held.

**Glyph** · `[Tp]` Topology; attested on twelve more.

**Failure** · Irreversibility. The cure is not undone by wanting it undone, and a Fixatio binding placed in error is a permanent feature of whatever it was placed on.

## Luminalis · *The Hidden Light*

**The idea** · The hidden light. Seeing what hides and hiding what shows, one act run in two directions.

**Kind** · Medium

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Spiritual Seat — Omnis, Florwyn, Dazkar, Nysera, Sylorin, Ilgrimm|Nysera]]: the line between what is seen and what is there, drawn in light.

**Hue** · Near-white with ultraviolet edges.

**Ground** · Favors dry air, conductive substrate and a clear line of sight; hates iron-caged interiors, heavy ferrous shielding and dense fog.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Spirit Wellsprings|Penanceflare]] (the light that cannot hide against the light that hides what it shows); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Spirit Wellsprings|Mirithane]] (hiding what shows against the inside and outside made one surface).

**On the Body** · The eye moves past the visible band and the Shell turns a look aside at its surface, so the fighter sees the warmth of a man behind a screen, and the man's eyes slide off the place where the fighter is standing.

**On the Spirit** · The soul sees what hides and hides what it shows, and one working run in either direction will turn up a concealed Trait or bury its maker's from anyone reading.

**On Fate** · A bond or a claim can be kept out of every reckoning and produced at the right moment, and secret loyalties show to the holder like a second color on the people in a room. At great cost the reason a blow is going to land can be kept off the other fighter's reading until it has landed, and perhaps once in a long career the enemy's count of its friends is simply wrong, because the captain bound at midnight twenty years ago shows no color until the morning he opens the gate. Holding a thread that far out of sight is a lifetime's labor, and it splits the holder so that their own later workings arrive out of step with themselves.

**Spirit** · Temperance through perception.

**Alchemy** · The hidden tincture: the color present in the charge that no eye reads until the right light finds it. *(the trade's reading)* Quartz amplifies it.

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · The non-visible spectrum and polarization. Total internal reflection.

**Mechanism** · Most of the spectrum is not available to the eye, and most of what matters is happening there. Luminalis extends perception past the visible band and gives the Shell a polarizing behavior: incident observation is filtered out at the boundary rather than absorbed, and light that strikes past the critical angle does not leave at all. Seeing and hiding turn out to be the same optical operation run in opposite directions, and a body, a soul and a claim each have a surface that a look can be turned from, which is why this lens is at home on all three Paths.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Gnosis Perception, Dexterity Feint. The practitioner sees what hides and learns to hide what they reveal.

**Glyph** · `[Lum]` Light, `[Ie]` Perception, `[Sar]` Reflection, `[Vei]` Depth, `[Oc]` Oculus, `[Dn]` Dawn; attested on nine more.

**Failure** · Birefringence. The Shell splits its own output into two paths with different velocities, and complex workings arrive out of phase with themselves.

## Mortalis · *The Black Gate*

**The idea** · The black gate. Ending looked at squarely and understood as a passage with its own law, by a soul that has stopped fearing it and has started, a little, to cool.

**Kind** · Medium

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Strata — Valmir, Kaldrion, Chronaeus|Valmir]]: the Veil Threshold and the concept of completion, looked at squarely.

**Hue** · Venous black-red.

**Ground** · Favors living biomass, temperate conditions and proximity to blood; hates sterile ground, extreme cold and Essence-depleted soil.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Terranova]] (every wound answered with new ground against the ending understood); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Anima Spirare]] (a body refusing the gate against a soul walking to it); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Verdantia]] (more life against the boundary of life).

**On the Body** · The body learns its own ending as a known road, so a Mortalis fighter goes on working, cool and unhurried, well past the wound that should have dropped them. A blow from that hand tells flesh it may stop spending itself, and flesh is often relieved to hear it.

**On the Spirit** · The Core looks at ending squarely and can author it, withdrawing a thing's hold on what it is, lawfully and in order: a ward lapses, a ghost goes on its way, a curse finds it has reached its term. It keeps nothing and nobody afterward.

**On Fate** · It works the last term of every tie, so the holder feels a bond, a line or a life come near its end and can see a dying oath out with honor, which is most of why they are sent for. Hurrying an end is done by few: a wound that would have killed by morning called in before the exchange is over, or in a lifetime's one great working a broken wing that might have lasted till dusk learning at noon that it has already ended. Endings belong to Archons and the great principles before they belong to anyone, and the holder who has reached for one comes back a little cooler and does not warm again.

**Spirit** · Temperance through surrender. *The confessors' reading:* dissolution, threshold, completion and the lawful withdrawal of presence. It governs passage and keeps nobody in storage.

**Alchemy** · The Blacking: the charge gone black in the Rot, at the point where a matter stops holding itself together. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · The non-equilibrium steady state, and the thermodynamic boundary of its collapse.

**Mechanism** · A living body is a structure held far from equilibrium by continuous expenditure. Death adds no event to that structure. It is the cessation of the expenditure and the return to equilibrium that follows without needing a cause. Mortalis lets the practitioner engage that boundary as a described physical transition rather than as a catastrophe, which is a stranger and colder comfort than it sounds. The boundary runs through every layer of a soul alike, since flesh, identity and bond all end by the same law, and that is why this lens is at home on all three Paths.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Resilience Persistence, Gnosis Cognition. The soul engages ending as a structural event rather than a catastrophe.

**Glyph** · None primary. Attested on nine, among them `[Au]` Death, `[Ob]` Obol and `[SoulDr]` Soul-Drift.

**Failure** · Equilibration. Sustained proximity to the boundary reduces the gradient the practitioner is themselves maintaining, and they cool toward it without noticing.

## Phreatis · *The Waters of Memory*

> **Two physics, one entry.** The same Wellspring answers to two physics because memory itself does. On the one reading it is the strengthened connection that fired. On the other it is the aquifer: groundwater reaching the well decades after it fell as rain, and returning what entered it, contamination included.

**The idea** · Memory as deep water. What is lived sinks in, is built into you, and comes back long after the weather has changed.

**Kind** · Medium

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Spiritual Seat — Omnis, Florwyn, Dazkar, Nysera, Sylorin, Ilgrimm|Sylorin]]: the memory the current carries, which reaches the well decades after it fell.

**Hue** · Copper-green.

**Ground** · Favors standing or moving water, humidity, pressure differential and living biomass; hates arid terrain, vacuum, thermally locked air and sterile ground.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Spirit Wellsprings|Letheveil]] (what sinks in and returns against what is cut loose).

**On the Body** · What the body has lived sinks into the tissue and comes back when needed, so a Phreatis fighter's old drills surface whole in the hands decades after the last lesson.

**On the Spirit** · The practitioner reads and draws what has sunk into water and into a soul, and a working can call up what a place or person has long since stopped remembering.

**On Fate** · Nothing stays buried: old ties, debts and promises rise on their own schedule the way a spring does, and the holder can feel them coming. At the price of a heavy bite out of the reserve and a nosebleed that can last a day, one can be drawn up early, the lesson the other fighter's first master beat into them wrong surfacing in the exact exchange where it will cost them. Doing the same to a war, the poisoned treaty coming due or the well sunk a hundred years ago going bad the summer an army camps on it, is the work of a whole life, and whatever else is down there comes up with it.

**Spirit** · Temperance through reflection. *The confessors' reading:* memory, what is lived built into the structure.

**Alchemy** · The remembering menstruum: a solvent that keeps the trace of everything ever dissolved in it. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Long-term potentiation on the one reading. Aquifer recharge and isotopic dating on the other.

**Mechanism** · Synaptic potentiation records experience by physically strengthening a connection that fired, while groundwater carries an isotopic signature of the rain that fell decades before it reached the well. Phreatis holds both: memory as reinforced structure, and memory as a slow reservoir that returns what entered it long after the weather has changed. Flesh, soul and sworn word each take in what is lived and give it back later, which is why this lens is at home on all three Paths.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Gnosis Retention, Harmonics Attunement, Resilience Anchoring. Memory becomes architecturally reinforced. Identical effect on either reading.

**Glyph** · `[Ap]` Aperture; attested on two more.

**Failure** · Contamination of the aquifer. What enters the reservoir cannot be selectively removed later, and a falsified memory recharges as readily as a true one.

# Spirit Wellsprings

*Fifteen of the Sixty, at home on the Soul Plane · the kind with nothing to point at*

Dame Hesketh Rook, the Mallet of Copper Row, fought nine summers in the Tollgate brackets on the strength of hating her father, and the whole quarter came down in yellow ribbons to watch her do it. At fifty she sat down with a confessor to settle a bet and got up three days later harmonized to Absolution, with no glow on her, no heat in her hands and nothing anyone in the stands could point to. A Hot Tun brawler threw his best at her in the next final, and she took it on a shoulder that had been broken twice and had stopped remembering either time, and laughed, and won. The bookies paid out. The minstrels complained that there was nothing to sing about. A Guild surveyor went over the ring with his instruments for an afternoon and reported an entirely normal ring. That night she took a jug up to the old man's grave with half the quarter creeping along behind her to hear what she would say, and what she said was that he would have liked the second round. It was some weeks before she noticed that the best punch of her life, the one with his face on it, had gone with the rest, and she has been offering good money for it ever since.

**What Spirit means** · A Spirit Wellspring is a change in a soul: forgiveness, forgetting, a failure owned. Its home is the Soul Plane, Ashura's reality, and its Aether (mana, the raw stuff of magic) works on the soul and nowhere else. There is nothing to point at and it gives its bearer no fire to throw. It usually does little or nothing to the physical world, though an edge case will always find a Measurewright (a certified assessor of workings): a Thaloré grief can be felt across a room, and the argument over whether that counts is older than the Guild. The landscape of a Spirit site looks entirely normal, so the surveyor's environment is a reliable instrument and the surveyor is not. An analogue on one of these entries is the scholars' picture of what happens in the soul, and no one has yet watched a forgiveness anneal.

**The lens** · A soul tempered on one of these changes what it can bear, know, keep or let go: a little at first, and after years of mastery on the Spirit Path, its home, up to double, so that the same Grade carries twice the weight of the idea into everyone who has to deal with it. Any Path can take any of them. On the Body Path a Spirit lens still gives no fire and changes what the flesh can stand. Away from home half again is the most anyone has got out of one, at a higher Essence (spiritual energy) cost and with a rougher result, and a rough result here is a hole in your own childhood. The full account of the lens is on the [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/The Sixty Wellsprings|hub]].

*Each Wellspring below opens on what it is. **The idea** is the concept it lays over a soul's Aether, and **The turn**, where the trade has fixed one, is the moment that idea comes clear to someone working inside it. **Kind** names its home plane, and never locks it. **Titan** is the Titan whose law it is one showing of, **Hue** the color it gives an aura, **Ground** what it favors and hates underfoot, and **Contrary** the Wellsprings whose laws fight it in one soul. **On the Body**, **On the Spirit** and **On Fate** say what the lens does to Essence on each Path, through the Aether Shell (the layer of the soul that meets matter), the Essence Core (the layer that holds identity and authors what a working is) and the Attraction Layer (a soul's own share of fate, its bonds, oaths, claims and odds). **Spirit** is the transformation it works on the soul, one road of Temperance (the climb of a soul from Stage to Stage), and **Alchemy** the principle it stands for at the bench. Then comes how scholars explain it: Analogue and Mechanism give the physics of what that type of Aether does to a Soul Crystal, Stat Effect what shifts on the sheet, Glyph the forms the Index seats on it, and Failure how it goes wrong. Entries run in alphabetical order.*

## Absolution · *The Cleansing Current*

**The idea** · Forgiveness. A soul's history stays and its shape stays, and the old poison stored in them is let out.

**Kind** · Spirit

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Spiritual Seat — Omnis, Florwyn, Dazkar, Nysera, Sylorin, Ilgrimm|Dazkar]]: restitution completed, the debt let go once proportion has been met.

**Hue** · Polished steel.

**Ground** · Favors old pilgrim roads, shrines and the quiet ground of a feud settled; hates fresh battlefields and any ground under a live grudge. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with no phenomenon for the ground to act on, it answers in a fight only to Aetheric Density.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Exuroth]] (the quench against the anneal); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Fate Wellsprings|Tarturon]] (the debt held against the debt let go); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Spirit Wellsprings|Penance]] (the anneal against the hammer).

**On the Body** · Old injuries stop costing what they cost: the knee keeps its scar and its bad angle and no longer charges the fighter for them. There is no heat and no glow, only a body that has stopped carrying its history.

**On the Spirit** · At home it lets the old poison out of a soul, one's own or a willing other's. The memory and the shape it left stay exactly where they were, and what they had been leaking into everything else stops.

**On Fate** · A debt or grudge can be let go and the thing it was pulling on stops pulling, and at the end of long confession a levied host held in the line by an old wrong may wake one morning with no very clear reason to charge. It takes all the holder's strength to absolve what was never theirs to forgive, it fails more often than not, and whether the men feel the line go slack or merely forget to form up is an old quarrel among confessors.

**Spirit** · Temperance through forgiveness.

**Alchemy** · The gentle reheat: the strain let out of worked metal and its shape left alone. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Stress-relief annealing. Removal of residual stress without change of form.

**Mechanism** · A worked component carries locked-in stresses from its own manufacture, invisible, dimensionally irrelevant, and entirely capable of causing failure under a load the part should have carried. Heat treatment relaxes them while leaving the shape untouched. Absolution does exactly this to the old fractures in a soul: the history stays, the geometry stays, and the stored energy that made the history dangerous is released. Reports of it easing anything outside a soul, a cracked bell or an unwilling stranger, are rare and argued over.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Tempering Clarity, Harmonics Attunement, Resilience Fortification. Old fractures lose their toxicity.

**Glyph** · `[Grs]` Grace; attested on five more.

**Failure** · Over-relaxation. Take too much out and the structure loses the compressive prestress it had been quietly relying on to stay closed.

## Abyntheus · *The Outer Pulse*

**The idea** · Vastness. The great scale read off the small vessel, a mortal soul stretched to take in things far too large for it.

**Kind** · Spirit

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Spiritual Seat — Omnis, Florwyn, Dazkar, Nysera, Sylorin, Ilgrimm|Ilgrimm]]: the unknown met at a scale that keeps knowledge humble, and breaks those it does not humble.

**Hue** · Deep naval blue, slow-pulsing.

**Ground** · Favors high open ground, sea horizons and clear night sky; hates iron-caged interiors and deep shafts. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with no phenomenon for the ground to act on, it answers in a fight only to Aetheric Density.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Coagulatio]] (a soul stretched over the great distance against a soul settled round one grain).

**On the Body** · It lends the arm nothing. It gives a frame that can stand under things too big to look at, so the fighter keeps their feet and their breakfast when a far higher Stage's Pressure or the sky over a Well comes down on them.

**On the Spirit** · The Core's aperture widens past what a mortal head was built to hold, and a Principle or an Archon's working becomes something the practitioner can read and, at some cost to their sanity, engage.

**On Fate** · The holder feels very long ties, the bond laid down before they were born or the debt that runs to the far side of the world, and soldiers near them bear what others break under, having been made to feel how small one field is inside what is coming. A tug on one of those ties, which might lean a whole people's old hatred on somebody's border, is the labor of decades, and the longest threads are apt to belong to Archons and the great principles, who may look down to see who is pulling.

**Spirit** · Temperance through vastness.

**Alchemy** · The Emerald Principle at the bench, as above, so below: the great scale read off the small vessel. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Very-long-wavelength propagation and interferometry. Resolution from baseline, not aperture.

**Mechanism** · Long wavelengths travel enormous distances, penetrate what shorter ones cannot, and carry the oldest signals in existence. Resolution at that scale comes from separating two small instruments by a continental baseline and reconciling their phase, and a larger instrument would not help. Abyntheus expands the conceptual aperture of the Essence Core by the same trick, letting a mortal Crystal engage phenomena whose scale should render them illegible. All of it happens in the soul. It throws nothing and moves nothing, and the reports of far-off bells heard and floors felt to tilt around an old Abyntheus master are argued over at every Measurewright's dinner.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Dominion Sovereignty, Sense, Gnosis Cognition. The conceptual ceiling expands to engage Archon-tier and Principle-level phenomena.

**Glyph** · `[Ab]` Abyss, `[Abys]` Deep, `[Let]` Tide; attested on three more.

**Failure** · Baseline decoherence. Hold the two ends of the perception apart too long and they lose phase lock, and what returns is noise the practitioner is nonetheless convinced is a signal.

## Catharsis · *The Wellspring of Release*

**The idea** · Letting go. A load set down so completely that the setting down spends it, and the soul comes out on the other side lighter and changed.

**Kind** · Spirit

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Spiritual Seat — Omnis, Florwyn, Dazkar, Nysera, Sylorin, Ilgrimm|Sylorin]]: Soul Reforging, a load of trauma spent down in the change of state.

**Hue** · Pale rose-gold.

**Ground** · Favors bathhouses, hot springs and confessionals, where people have long come to set things down; hates barracks and courts, where nobody dares. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with no phenomenon for the ground to act on, it answers in a fight only to Aetheric Density.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Pyraeon]] (keeping the heat for life against setting the load down); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Vantabriel]] (setting it all down against carrying it all).

**On the Body** · It gives a fighter nothing to throw. Terror, exhaustion and the shaking after a kill go out of the limbs in one long breath, and hands that could not close an hour ago close.

**On the Spirit** · Grief and rage held in the Core are spent by the letting go of them and take the old order of the soul along, so the person who stands up afterward is calmer and honestly somewhat different from the one who sat down.

**On Fate** · With everything the holder has, a bond or a load can be put down so completely that the line goes slack, and once in a long while a beaten company under such a captain sets down its terror all at once and stands up again with nothing left to rout from. Turned across a field it is rarer and stranger, the men opposite losing their hatred in the hour they needed it, and the holder comes out of it unable to say for a season what they themselves were fighting for.

**Spirit** · Temperance through letting go: release, purgation, true clearing. The Heresiology's warning stands beside it, that nothing is purified by being merely emptied.

**Alchemy** · Purgation: the matter gives up its burden in the change of state itself. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Latent heat of phase change. Enthalpy absorbed at constant temperature.

**Mechanism** · Melting ice at zero degrees absorbs three hundred and thirty-four joules per gram and the temperature does not move a fraction. The energy goes entirely into breaking the ordered structure. Catharsis routes accumulated emotional load in the Essence Core into a phase transition instead of into output, so enormous internal pressure is consumed by the change of state itself and the practitioner arrives on the other side measurably calmer and structurally different. The ice is the scholars' comparison and all of this happens in the soul: nothing outside the practitioner changes by a degree, unless you count the chill reported in a few confessionals, which the trade is still arguing about.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Harmonics Empathy, Tempering Maturity, Resilience Fortification. Emotional weight converts into structural clarity.

**Glyph** · `[Xr]` Excision, `[Sv]` Sever; attested on three more.

**Failure** · Supercooling. The transition fails to nucleate, the load accumulates past the point where it should have released, and when it finally goes it goes all at once.

## Contrition · *The Broken Flame*

**The idea** · A failure owned. Only what a soul truly got wrong and truly survived turns into armor for it.

**Kind** · Spirit

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Spiritual Seat — Omnis, Florwyn, Dazkar, Nysera, Sylorin, Ilgrimm|Dazkar]]: karmic return taken up willingly, the thing done coming back as armor.

**Hue** · Dull red with a sting.

**Ground** · Favors plague graves, hospices and ground where a whole people failed and lived; hates sterile ground and places that have never been tried. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with no phenomenon for the ground to act on, it answers in a fight only to Aetheric Density.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Spirit Wellsprings|Lethegraal]] (no hand choosing what falls against a failure owned by name); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Sublimatio]] (a failure kept as armor against a lower self left behind).

**On the Body** · The body learns to bear what it has already failed against, and the tremor that took a fighter's hand in their first rout does not come a second time, because the same fear met again is answered at once. A rehearsed failure teaches the flesh nothing, and neither does anyone else's.

**On the Spirit** · A real failure, owned without excuse, is written into the Core as a standing answer, so the deceit or the despair that broke a soul once finds it already armed. An excuse voids the record, and the Core knows an excuse when it has made one.

**On Fate** · An admitted fault loses its hold, so a captain who has stood in front of her company and named her own rout has taken away a crack that a Fate fighter would have pried at, and the next oath sworn over it tends to keep. It has to be paid for by failing in earnest and surviving it, and such a person is famously hard to blackmail and seldom beaten the same way twice.

**Spirit** · Temperance through acceptance. *The confessors' reading:* a failure owned and survived.

**Alchemy** · The dose: a poison survived at its measure, and the body keeps the receipt. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Immunological memory and hormesis. Sub-lethal exposure producing adaptive benefit.

**Mechanism** · A pathogen survived once is answered faster and harder the second time because the response was recorded, and a sub-lethal dose of a stressor frequently leaves an organism measurably more capable than it was. Contrition converts failure into that record. The mechanism requires the failure to have been survived, and it requires it to have been genuine, because a rehearsed one produces no antibody. The record is kept in the soul, and reports of it reaching further, a body shrugging off the very poison its owner once confessed to, are few and argued over.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Tempering Maturity, Resilience Integrity. Past failures become structural reinforcement.

**Glyph** · None attested in the Index (see Discrepancies of Record on the hub).

**Failure** · Autoimmunity. The record generalizes past its target and the practitioner begins answering their own tissue as though it were the original insult.

## Eclipseron · *The Shadowed Star*

**The idea** · Reading what is missing. The shape of an absence taken as knowledge of the thing that cast it. This lens has the truest claim of the Sixty to reading what fails to be there. People call that darkness and are being romantic: it is a system described by its absences, which is a harder and less romantic thing than its practitioners generally admit.

**Kind** · Spirit

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Spiritual Seat — Omnis, Florwyn, Dazkar, Nysera, Sylorin, Ilgrimm|Ilgrimm]]: Shadow as Revelation, truth glimpsed sideways.

**Hue** · Red-violet with a dark disc.

**Ground** · Favors Veil-thin zones, surveyed ground and the sites of old observatories; hates warp scarring, contested Domains and unmapped terrain. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with no phenomenon for the ground to act on, it answers in a fight only to Aetheric Density.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Medium Wellsprings|Aurevane]] (seeing the thing against reading the hole it leaves).

**On the Body** · The body learns to bear what has been taken from it, with no fire or force added: a missing finger, a deaf ear or a dead leg stops throwing the fighter, whose limbs now know the exact shape of what is gone and work around it.

**On the Spirit** · The practitioner reads a soul by what it withholds, and derives from the shape of a silence, or of a memory cut out with a clean edge, the thing that used to be there.

**On Fate** · The holder reads the shape of what is missing, the oath left out of the contract or the column that ought to be on the ridge by now, and that reading takes nothing but a long sad habit. To act on it is the hard thing: after years of grief spent learning how, a company under this person can be made to close on the shape of its dead, so the gap torn in the line at noon never opens in the men beside it, and the holder carries the whole absence afterward.

**Spirit** · Temperance through eclipse.

**Alchemy** · The dead head read: what stayed behind in the retort tells you what left it. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Occultation and transit photometry. The information carried by absorption lines.

**Mechanism** · A hidden planet is found by the dip in its star's brightness long before anyone sees it, and a spectrum's dark lines carry more composition data than its bright continuum. Eclipseron tunes the perception of the Essence Core to the negative: the practitioner reads what has been blocked, subtracted, or withheld in a soul, and derives from the shape of the absence the shape of the thing casting it. It casts no shadow and dims no lamp, and whether a master can read a sealed box the same way, from the hole it leaves in a room, is claimed by some and argued over by the rest.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Gnosis Analysis, Harmonics Suppression, Dominion Sense. The practitioner reads reality by what is missing from it.

**Glyph** · `[Oth]` Path, `[Ph]` Phase; attested on five more.

**Failure** · False transit. Not every dimming is an occultation, and a practitioner committed to reading absence will eventually construct an object out of a gap that had nothing behind it.

## Hypnather · *The Sleep Crown*

**The idea** · The sleep crown. Change that comes only at rest, the soul setting itself in order in the dark while nothing is asked of it.

**Kind** · Spirit

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Spiritual Seat — Omnis, Florwyn, Dazkar, Nysera, Sylorin, Ilgrimm|Nysera]]: the sleep that passes under her veil, the soul setting itself in order where nothing real is asked of it.

**Hue** · Glassy violet.

**Ground** · Favors darkness, silence, sickrooms and old dormitories; hates crowd density and any place with an alarm bell. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with no phenomenon for the ground to act on, it answers in a fight only to Aetheric Density.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Ignivale]] (no gap between wanting and doing against change that comes only at rest); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Fate Wellsprings|Tarturon]] (the soul at rest against a debt that will not let it lie down).

**On the Body** · The body does its mending asleep and does it thoroughly, a single night's rest doing the work of many, and none of it works while the eyes are open. Its fighters come to the field new every morning and are finished after a few nights without it.

**On the Spirit** · At home the Core sets itself in order in the dark while nothing is asked of it, and the person who lay down is not quite the one who wakes. The rare art that lays that quiet on someone else in sight is really laying it on their soul, the sect masters say, with the body merely following, and nobody has settled the matter since the only witness is asleep.

**On Fate** · A sleeper's oaths hold and their enemies lose the thread of them, and a commander who will give up waking for it, sometimes for weeks, may see a camp rest when it ought to lie awake and a night attack come down the wrong valley. The old rule against waking anyone under it is older than the Guild, because what stops partway sets worse than it began.

**Spirit** · Temperance through rest.

**Alchemy** · The Standing: nothing is done and nothing may be done, and the thing goes on ordering itself in the dark. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Structural relaxation. Physical aging of a glass toward its equilibrium configuration.

**Mechanism** · A glass is a liquid that stopped moving before it finished arranging itself, and left undisturbed it continues to relax toward the ordered state it never reached, slowly and only while quiescent. Hypnather grants the Crystal that behavior. The reorganization happens during genuine rest and cannot be forced by ordinary means, which is why Hypnather practitioners sleep like the dead and wake structurally different. A few arts do force it, pinning the quiet true on command in the practitioner or in a body held in sight, so that the target's motion slows and its outline smooths as though sleep had come down all at once. The physics is nearly free and the Essence is not, and a relaxation forced is a relaxation that ends in the Failure below.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Regeneration on sleep cycles, Tempering Yield, Gnosis Retention. Recovery during rest becomes dramatically more efficient.

**Glyph** · None primary. Attested on `[Vel]` Mind, `[Vl]` Veil, `[AnMa]` Anamnesis and `[Ora]` Truth.

**Failure** · Interrupted relaxation. Disturb the process partway and the configuration locks where it stood, worse ordered than before it began.

## Lethegraal · *The Cup of Forgetting*

**The idea** · The cup. Time's mercy hurried along: old pain settles on its own schedule, and no hand chooses what falls.

**The turn** · Accepting that something is over, and meaning it.

**Kind** · Spirit

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Strata — Valmir, Kaldrion, Chronaeus|Chronaeus]]: pace, every disturbance settled in its own time. His Era-Forge's Toll is written in this Wellspring.

**Hue** · Gray-violet, slow ebb.

**Ground** · Favors old battlefields grown over, ruins and quiet graveyards; hates saturated Aether, crowd density and fresh wounds. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with no phenomenon for the ground to act on, it answers in a fight only to Aetheric Density.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Cataclysm]] (upheaval against settling on its own schedule); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Spirit Wellsprings|Penance]] (letting time take the old pain against going back to it on purpose); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Spirit Wellsprings|Contrition]] (no hand choosing what falls against a failure owned by name).

**On the Body** · The same wounds close on the same schedule, and the terror stored in them drains early, so a man burned half to death can stand beside a cook fire by spring with no faster healing to show for it.

**On the Spirit** · Under this cup the soul digests what it has been choking on, by its own clock and nobody's choice, and old grief goes down into the Core as plain structure that holds weight. Physicians prescribe it for that reason, and nothing about it reaches anyone but the one holding the cup, unless you count the family who wonder why she has stopped crying.

**On Fate** · Time wears bonds as well, and by pouring themselves out a holder can hurry it along, so the rage an enemy army carried out of its burned capital may have gone to mere complaint by the second week of the march. It is slow, draining work that nothing can aim, and its holders have lost sieges to their own side's forgiveness when a vow to die on the wall went slack on the morning it fell due.

**Spirit** · Temperance through quiet. *The confessors' reading:* time, old pain digested into neutral architecture.

**Alchemy** · The settling: the charge left to come to rest on its own schedule, with no hand choosing what falls. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Relaxation time. The characteristic constant with which a perturbed system returns to equilibrium.

**Mechanism** · Every disturbed system settles, and the only question is how long. Lethegraal shortens the time constant. Nothing is removed and nothing is chosen; the same trauma metabolizes down to neutral architecture on a schedule the practitioner is not otherwise able to influence, which is why the Wellspring is prescribed and Letheveil is merely permitted.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Tempering Maturity, Harmonics Stability. Trauma metabolizes faster. The Crystal digests old pain into neutral architecture.

**Glyph** · `[Cro]` Cycle, `[Len]` Line, `[StSl]` Still-Sleep; attested on eight more.

**Failure** · Under-damping. Shorten the constant too far and the system overshoots equilibrium on every cycle, and the practitioner oscillates rather than settling.

## Letheveil · *The Waters of Forgetting*

**The idea** · Forgetting on purpose. Learning which memories hold up the house and which ones are chains, and cutting the chains. It surfaces when denial has started to cost more than acceptance.

**Kind** · Spirit

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Spiritual Seat — Omnis, Florwyn, Dazkar, Nysera, Sylorin, Ilgrimm|Sylorin]]: what the river takes off a soul on the way to its next shape.

**Hue** · Black-violet with color drained from the edges.

**Ground** · Favors darkness, silence, ruins and still black water; hates crowd density and active Domains. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with no phenomenon for the ground to act on, it answers in a fight only to Aetheric Density.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Medium Wellsprings|Phreatis]] (what sinks in and returns against what is cut loose); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Medium Wellsprings|Anamnesis]] (forgetting on purpose against everything keeping its past); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Spirit Wellsprings|Redemption]] (cutting the wound out of the story against building the self around it).

**On the Body** · The flesh keeps its own memories, flinches and old breaks among them, and this lens lets the body put down the ones it no longer needs, so the shoulder forgets the spear that ruined it and the knee forgets to buckle at the old angle. No strength arrives with it, and a hand that has forgotten the wrong scar will walk straight back onto the blade that made it.

**On the Spirit** · At home it goes into the Core with a skimmer and takes off the memories that are chains, one's own or a willing other's, and what is left runs lighter and truer. Done to the unwilling it is the crime the seal-courts were built for, and whether it can be done at a distance is argued mostly by people who cannot remember.

**On Fate** · Forgetting a debt does not strike it off, as a great many people have been surprised to learn, but with a great effort the holder's own end of the line can be cut, and a company that has cut its end of a rout marches back onto the same field with no memory in its knees of how that morning went. The holder pays in their own childhood, piece by piece, and what the rout cost is still owed when the collector reaches the door.

**Spirit** · Temperance through release of memory. *The confessors' reading:* forgetting, the lesson of which memories are load-bearing and which are chains.

**Alchemy** · The skimming: scum taken off the charge, with the worker's guess at which part was scum. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Lossy compression. Shannon entropy and the identification of low-information content.

**Mechanism** · Compression works because most of a signal is redundant, and the art is entirely in knowing which components carry information and which merely occupy bandwidth. Letheveil performs that analysis on memory. It does not erase indiscriminately; it identifies what is load-bearing and discards the rest, and the practitioner's certainty about which is which is the whole of the risk.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Gnosis Cognition, and **tests** Resilience Anchoring rather than simply raising it. The practitioner learns which memories are load-bearing and which are chains.

**Glyph** · `[Stl]` Still; attested on four more.

**Failure** · Generation loss. Each pass discards a little more, and a memory compressed repeatedly degrades toward an artifact the practitioner still experiences as a memory.

## Mirithane · *The Wellspring of Reflection*

**The idea** · The honest mirror. The inside and the outside made one surface.

**Kind** · Spirit

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Spiritual Seat — Omnis, Florwyn, Dazkar, Nysera, Sylorin, Ilgrimm|Dazkar]]: the mirror of consequence, the same that annulled Razakiel by reflection alone.

**Hue** · Silver-violet mirror.

**Ground** · Favors dark still water, polished black stone and silence; hates crowd density and active Domains. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with no phenomenon for the ground to act on, it answers in a fight only to Aetheric Density.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Medium Wellsprings|Luminalis]] (hiding what shows against the inside and outside made one surface); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Spirit Wellsprings|Oneirion]] (the surface that shows what is there against the space of what can be thought).

**On the Body** · The body stops spending itself on concealment, with no clenched jaw, no swallowed flinch and nothing kept up for the room, and it gets back the breath all that cost. It also loses every feint it ever owned, and the sparring hall finds this out first.

**On the Spirit** · At home the Core and what leaves it become one surface, so a working carries what the person actually is, and a soul at peace with itself throws truer than its Grade suggests. Turned on someone else, the art hands them their own soul with the flattery taken off, and whether that counts as a technique or merely as tactlessness is a dispute the Measurewrights have yet to close.

**On Fate** · Presence turns into plain speech: a room knows what this person wants, an oath sworn under this lens cannot carry a hidden clause, and a sworn company will follow one off a cliff with nobody left to wonder about the orders. Turning that on a parley, so an enemy goes home knowing how much of his own army means to stand, strips the holder bare before every soul present and leaves them nothing private for the rest of their life, which is why it is rarely done.

**Spirit** · Temperance through stillness. *The confessors' reading:* self-sight, the inside and the outside made one honest surface.

**Alchemy** · The blick: the flash on the assay button that shows the metal exactly as it is. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Specular reflection. The law of reflection; the phase-conjugate mirror.

**Mechanism** · A true mirror adds nothing. Angle in equals angle out, and fidelity is defined entirely by the absence of introduced distortion. A phase-conjugate mirror goes further and returns a wavefront that undoes its own aberration on the way back. Mirithane makes the Essence Core such a surface, so what leaves the practitioner is what was in them, with no flattering correction applied in transit.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Harmonics Attunement, Tempering Coherence. Output becomes a more honest reflection of internal state.

**Glyph** · `[Se]` Song, `[Ari]` Aria, `[Nx]` Nexus, `[Pt]` Pattern, `[Wvn]` Weave; attested on fifteen more.

> **Failure** · Perfect return. The mirror does not discriminate. A practitioner in a state they would rather not broadcast broadcasts it perfectly.

## Nyxial · *The Nocturne Flame*

**The idea** · The even flame. A self that holds its level whatever the room does.

**Kind** · Spirit

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Spiritual Seat — Omnis, Florwyn, Dazkar, Nysera, Sylorin, Ilgrimm|Ilgrimm]]: fear turned to function, one small flame kept level in the dark.

**Hue** · Deep violet with a small cold flame.

**Ground** · Favors darkness, silence, night watch-posts and lamp-keepers' rooms; hates saturated Aether and crowd density. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with no phenomenon for the ground to act on, it answers in a fight only to Aetheric Density.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Spirit Wellsprings|Thaloré]] (the whole weight of a feeling against a level held whatever the room does); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Fate Wellsprings|Tarturon]] (the regulated flame against the reaction with no stopping condition); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Fate Wellsprings|Transference]] (holding a level against giving it away down the gradient).

**On the Body** · No flame comes of it, whatever the old name promises. The body holds its level, pulse and temper and grip the same in a burning hall as in a bath, and it can bear a long siege or a long torture without the highs that would have helped or the lows that would have warned.

**On the Spirit** · At home the soul keeps a setpoint, and elation, terror and other people's Pressure all arrive and fail to move the needle. Some hold that the room around such a person slowly evens out too, lamps steadying and tempers dropping, and those who have tried to measure it got different answers on different nights.

**On Fate** · The odds around this person run unusually flat, with no streaks and no disasters out of nowhere, and a Fate commander's best morning, bought at ruinous expense, goes to waste against a company held level by it. Bonds stay at the temperature they were sworn at, as spouses have been known to mention, and holding a line at that level through a long siege is a slow exhaustion, all of it coming out at once on the day the load lifts.

**Spirit** · Temperance through night. *The confessors' reading:* evenness.

**Alchemy** · The Still Bath: self-limiting at the boil, whatever the fire below it does. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Thermal inertia under negative feedback. A regulated system holding setpoint against ambient.

**Mechanism** · A body with high heat capacity governed by a feedback loop holds its temperature regardless of what the room does, and the larger the capacity the smaller the excursion. Nyxial gives the Crystal that regulation. The practitioner's baseline does not rise in favorable conditions and does not fall in hostile ones, which is a disadvantage exactly as often as it is a defense.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Harmonics Stability, Dominion Gravity, Vitality Constitution. Baseline output does not fluctuate with environmental pressure.

**Glyph** · None attested in the Index (see Discrepancies of Record on the hub).

**Failure** · Integral windup. A regulator held against a load it cannot overcome accumulates correction, and when the load lifts the correction discharges all at once.

## Oneirion · *The Wellspring of Dream*

**The idea** · Dream as a place. Work done in the space of what can be thought, reaching a soul where no line of sight runs, because no soul was ever in the room.

**Kind** · Spirit

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Spiritual Seat — Omnis, Florwyn, Dazkar, Nysera, Sylorin, Ilgrimm|Nysera]]: dream as a place. Her Somnum Flow is written in this Wellspring.

**Hue** · Dream-lavender.

**Ground** · Favors darkness, silence, ruins and places where people have slept for generations; hates saturated Aether and active Domains. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with no phenomenon for the ground to act on, it answers in a fight only to Aetheric Density.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Spirit Wellsprings|Mirithane]] (the surface that shows what is there against the space of what can be thought); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Chthonica]] (a place with no ground against nothing but ground).

**On the Body** · Asleep or deep in trance the body learns what the dream rehearsed, so a fighter may wake with a parry they never drilled and the bruises of a duel nobody else attended. It lends no force, and an ache carried out of the dream lasts all day with no mark to show a physician.

**On the Spirit** · At home a working goes through the space of what can be thought and arrives at a soul, since a soul was never in the room to begin with, and it steers by whatever the sender holds of the target: a name, a face, a shared roof. The dreamer wakes shaken and changed and the furniture has not moved, and the stories in which it did are told by people selling something.

**On Fate** · A dream shared often enough becomes a bond, and strangers keep dreaming of the same door and then keep meeting, which is the small and very strange end of it. A holder who gives a season of nights to it can send a levy onto a field it has dreamed for a week, already knowing how to run, at the price of no longer being certain which field they woke on, and a Domain (a region where the world runs on a practitioner's terms) built on it has terms nobody can quite recite afterward.

**Spirit** · Temperance through imagination. *The confessors' reading:* dreaming.

**Alchemy** · The working in the mind: the operation carried out on no bench at all. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Configuration space. A system's state as a point in an abstract high-dimensional manifold.

**Mechanism** · A mechanical system's every possible arrangement is a coordinate in a space that has no physical location, and the system's evolution is a trajectory through it. Oneirion lets a working act on that manifold rather than on the room, and on the Soul Plane the manifold is very nearly the plain fact of things: souls have no address in a room, and what lies between two of them is how much one can hold of the other in mind. This is the mechanical reason Oneirion techniques reach targets no line of sight connects, and the reason they are so difficult to describe afterward.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Gnosis Cognition, Dominion Sovereignty, Ardency Cascade. Workings operate through conceptual space, not only physical space.

**Glyph** · `[Ka]` Dream, `[Vl]` Veil, `[RaE]` Emotion, `[DmSl]` Dream-Seal, `[Nys]` Dreamrift, `[OniV]` Oneiric Veil; attested on five more.

**Failure** · Projection error. The trajectory was correct in the manifold and lands nowhere in particular when it is mapped back down into a room.

## Penance · *The Atoning Path*

**The idea** · Discipline. A soul made stronger on purpose, by going back again and again to exactly the places where it did wrong or was hurt.

**Kind** · Spirit

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Spiritual Seat — Omnis, Florwyn, Dazkar, Nysera, Sylorin, Ilgrimm|Dazkar]]: purification through penance, to the letter of his law.

**Hue** · Dull iron, darkening.

**Ground** · Favors training yards, penitents' stairs and any ground worn down by one person's returning; hates uniform soft ground with no history in it. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with no phenomenon for the ground to act on, it answers in a fight only to Aetheric Density.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Spirit Wellsprings|Lethegraal]] (letting time take the old pain against going back to it on purpose); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Spirit Wellsprings|Absolution]] (the anneal against the hammer).

**On the Body** · What the fighter has chosen to endure stops being the worst of it, and the old pain in the old place is borne further each year than any trainer says it should be. A sword cuts them exactly as it cuts anyone else, and they keep walking.

**On the Spirit** · Each deliberate return to a fault leaves the Core harder at that point, so the place where a soul once lied or broke becomes the place it cannot be made to do so again. The Crystal is not fooled about which faults are real.

**On Fate** · A self-imposed vow bites sooner and holds longer, so a company that ran from a field can swear itself back onto that same field every year until it stands, each return paid in real wounds and leaving it harder to break in exactly the place it broke. Only the penitent's end of the line is theirs to pay, so a wronged man may stay wronged as long as he likes, usually on the other side of the field.

**Spirit** · Temperance through discipline.

**Alchemy** · The hammer's law: metal hardest where it was beaten most. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Work hardening. Dislocation multiplication under plastic strain.

**Mechanism** · Bend a metal past yield and it becomes harder to bend again, because plastic deformation multiplies dislocations until they obstruct one another. The material is strongest exactly where it was most abused. Penance imposes that constitutive behavior on the Crystal, so deliberate strain accumulates strength, and the strength is honest because it is located precisely at the sites of prior failure. What it hardens is the soul, and claims that a penitent's skin has turned a blade are rare and argued over in every order that keeps one.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Threshold, Tempering Coherence, Resilience Integrity. The Crystal aligns more honestly with lived behavior.

**Glyph** · None attested in the Index (see Discrepancies of Record on the hub).

**Failure** · Strain embrittlement. Past a certain dislocation density the material has no ductility left, and the next load does not deform it. It ends it.

## Penanceflare · *The Light of Contrition*

**The idea** · Burning in the open. Remorse that shines on the Soul Plane, so that what the soul is and what it means can be read by anyone able to look at a soul, and honesty costs nothing because nothing is hidden anymore.

**Kind** · Spirit

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Abyssal Depths — Prometheus, Zar'thuun, Vohrin|Prometheus]]: he burns what is false, and here the false thing is whatever the penitent was hiding.

**Hue** · The full spectrum, red to white, tracking temperature.

**Ground** · Favors penitents' roads, shrines of the Penance Flame and squares of public confession; hates sealed rooms and spy-houses. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with no phenomenon for the ground to act on, it answers in a fight only to Aetheric Density.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Tenebra]] (nothing hidden against nothing seen); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Medium Wellsprings|Luminalis]] (the light that cannot hide against the light that hides what it shows).

**On the Body** · No fire comes of it, only a body that has learned to bear being seen: a wound or a fear left unhidden stops costing Essence to hide, and the fighter's tells, being plain to everyone, stop being any use to anyone.

**On the Spirit** · The Core shows what it is and what it means, so every working wears its maker's intent on its face, and the remorse that lights it is very hard to argue with.

**On Fate** · Everything the holder owes stands in plain view, so a company sworn to them has no rumor left to break on and will sit out a siege on what it can see it is owed. Pushing that light into the enemy's lines, so that their men start asking to see their own captain's accounts, burns the holder's every shame in front of both armies and has been managed only a handful of times.

**Spirit** · Temperance through remorse.

**Alchemy** · The fire that reports itself: heat read by its color, as every alchemist reads a flame. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Blackbody radiation. Planck's law and the emissivity of a body that cannot hide its temperature.

**Mechanism** · Every body above absolute zero radiates, and the spectrum of that radiation reports its temperature to anyone with an instrument. The scholars' account is that Penanceflare does this to the Essence Core, raising its emissivity toward unity so that remorse shows on the Soul Plane at its true heat. The practitioner's workings become brilliantly legible to any Gnosis (the stat of reading and knowing) that can look at a soul, their intent readable in the emission, and concealment becomes impossible in exactly the measure that honesty becomes free. Almost none of this reaches the physical world, and a hand held near a penitent feels nothing. Whether the faint light some witnesses swear to on the skin is really there, and whether the burning of the Failure below is heat at all or a body agreeing with its soul, the Measurewrights have been arguing for a long time.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Ardency Compression inversely, raising a working's visibility rather than lowering it, and Gnosis Vigilance. Workings become more visible and more honest in expression.

**Glyph** · None primary. Attested on `[Py]` Pyre and `[Let]` Tide.

> **Failure** · Thermal runaway. Force the emissivity past what the body can shed and the heat climbs faster than the light can carry it off, so the practitioner burns brighter and hotter together, in the open, until the tissue gives out. There is also no concealment mode. The Wellspring does not permit one, and a Penanceflare practitioner who needs to hide has harmonized with the wrong Wellspring.

## Redemption · *The Light Reclaimed*

**The idea** · The light reclaimed. The soul's wound becomes its strongest place, and the whole self rearranges itself around what it really lived through.

**Kind** · Spirit

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Spiritual Seat — Omnis, Florwyn, Dazkar, Nysera, Sylorin, Ilgrimm|Florwyn]]: organic memory, a wound remembered into the strongest place in the body of a soul.

**Hue** · Copper-bright.

**Ground** · Favors infirmaries, battlefield surgeries and ground close to blood; hates sterile ground and Essence-depleted soil. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with no phenomenon for the ground to act on, it answers in a fight only to Aetheric Density.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Spirit Wellsprings|Letheveil]] (cutting the wound out of the story against building the self around it); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Fractura]] (where it will break against the break made strongest).

**On the Body** · The body stops flinching from where it was broken, and a fighter will take a blow on the old break without the old terror and put real weight on a joint no physician would trust. The bone is no denser for it, whatever the pilgrims say.

**On the Spirit** · What wrecked the soul becomes the densest part of it, so a Core that survived a betrayal or a long captivity is hardest to move on exactly that ground. The rest of the self slowly aligns to what was truly lived, whatever had been hoped for.

**On Fate** · An old disgrace turns, by long living, into the very thing people trust the holder for, until the day a town that spat at them sends for them to hold its walls. Once or twice in a life that can be pulled on in the field, when an enemy plans the whole attack around the place this person broke twenty years ago and the soul that has rearranged itself there bears what the rest of them could not, while everything next to the old wound quietly gives way.

**Spirit** · Temperance through return. *The confessors' reading:* being reclaimed, the wound becoming the strongest place.

**Alchemy** · The sound weld: metal densest at the old break. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Fracture callus and Wolff's law. Bone remodeling along lines of stress.

**Mechanism** · Healed bone is denser at the break than the bone around it, and a limb loaded repeatedly remodels its internal architecture to align with the load it actually receives. Redemption applies both to the Crystal: prior damage sites become the highest-density regions, and the whole structure reorganizes toward the stresses the practitioner has genuinely lived rather than the ones they expected. All of that happens in the soul. A limb that mended straighter because its owner was reclaimed is a story every order of the Wellspring tells, and it stays rare and argued over.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Tempering Maturity, Resilience Integrity, Dominion Fate. Past trauma sites become the strongest points in the Crystal's architecture.

**Glyph** · None attested in the Index (see Discrepancies of Record on the hub).

**Failure** · Stress shielding. Load bypasses the reinforced region entirely, and everything adjacent to the old wound quietly demineralizes for want of use.

## Thaloré · *The Wellspring of Depth*

**The idea** · Depth of feeling. Emotion given its true weight in the soul, so that a grief can press on every soul in a whole room. A soul under this lens takes the shape of what holds it, and is likelier than most to fall into Synergia (Crystals briefly sharing one working) and Echo Bonds.

**Kind** · Spirit

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Spiritual Seat — Omnis, Florwyn, Dazkar, Nysera, Sylorin, Ilgrimm|Sylorin]]: the full charge a soul carries in the current, given its true weight.

**Hue** · The deepest teal, nearly black.

**Ground** · Favors deep standing water, flooded ruins and places of drowning and mass grief; hates arid terrain and thin dry air. This says where its Aether pools and thins and no more: with no phenomenon for the ground to act on, it answers in a fight only to Aetheric Density.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Spirit Wellsprings|Nyxial]] (the whole weight of a feeling against a level held whatever the room does); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Abython]] (pressure felt in full against pressure matched away).

**On the Body** · No force comes of it. The frame learns to stay upright and keep working under a weight of feeling that would put another mourner on the floor, and a body that has carried that can carry a broken rib.

**On the Spirit** · Feeling takes its true weight, and a grief held deep in the Core presses outward on every other soul in the room, who go home tired and cannot say why.

**On Fate** · A bond takes on the full depth of what is felt in it, so a widow's claim on a house leans harder than any deed, and a company's grief for its dead captain will hold a wall that every sum says is lost. Letting that weight down on the enemy's camp, where it settles on whoever already half believes they did wrong, takes the holder's whole self, and a holder whose support gives way under it is crushed by the same grief.

**Spirit** · Temperance through immersion. *The confessors' reading:* depth of feeling, emotional truth given its full weight.

**Alchemy** · Weight in the vessel: pressure set by depth alone, whatever the shape of the glass. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Hydrostatic pressure. Pressure as the product of density, gravity, and depth.

**Mechanism** · Pressure at depth depends on how dense the fluid is and how far down you are, and the vessel, the volume and the surface have no say in it. Thaloré gives emotional content that same constitutive relation: density times depth, expressed as literal metaphysical weight, which is why a Thaloré practitioner's grief has a measurable pressure field and why standing near one is physically tiring. The field is a field in the soul. It moves no air and bends no reed, and the claim of three separate towns to a wall cracked by a Thaloré mourner is argued over mostly by people who have never stood near one.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Harmonics Projection, Dominion Pressure. Emotional truth acquires metaphysical weight.

**Glyph** · `[Vo]` Voice, `[Wh]` Whisper; attested on thirteen more.

**Failure** · Implosion. The pressure acts inward as readily as outward, and a Thaloré practitioner whose internal support fails is crushed by their own field.

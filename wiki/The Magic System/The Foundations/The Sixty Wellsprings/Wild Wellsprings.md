# Wild Wellsprings

*Thirty-three of the Sixty, at home in the Physical World · the kind you can point at*

The people of Pennyhithe will tell you about the Aldgate sisters whether you ask or not. Prudence Aldgate heated the public baths on Fish Lane with her bare hands for thirty years, a copper a tub, two if you wanted it hot enough to lift the grease, and she turned away anyone who owed her, the reeve's wife included, and once, gloriously, the reeve. Her sister Bess kept the family's grudges. On the night of the Herring Fair she walked down into the customs cellar under the tollhouse with a list of names and her whole body lit the white-orange of a smith's best heat, and the crowd up on the quay heard a flame-front turn into something else.

The tollhouse roof came down in the harbor with the tollkeeper's best hat still on its peg. The quay cheered, and the minstrels had a song by morning. The song leaves out that the shock came back up the cellar along its own path, with Bess standing at the origin of it, and that she has been deaf in both ears and short three fingers ever since. Prudence's tubs ran on Pyraeon and Bess's fingers went to Cataclysm, one smolder and one detonation out of the same orange fire, and a Measurewright (a certified assessor of workings) who says so in a Pennyhithe tavern should expect to be asked which sister he is calling gentle.

**What Wild means** · A Wild Wellspring is a thing in the world: fire in the ground, stone that moves, lightning, a solution crystallizing out of itself on the bench. Its home is the Physical World, Aegor's ground, and its Aether (mana, the raw stuff of magic) shows up as a physical phenomenon or an alchemical transformation. The stone was there and the water was there, and the Aether reshapes them, moves them, burns them. A site of one of these alters the landscape where anyone can see it, so the surveyor's body is a reliable instrument and his environment is not.

**The lens** · A soul tempered on one of these carries that thing in everything it does: a little at first, and after years of mastery on the Body Path, its home, up to double, so that the same fist at the same Grade lands with twice the rupture in it, the same shoulder drives twice the momentum's character into what it meets, and the same Fortitude behaves twice as much like the thing it has decided to be. Any Path can take any of them. Away from home half again is the most anyone has got out of one, at a higher Essence (spiritual energy) cost and with a rougher result. The bath-house trade will tell you it shows in the water, anyone who has bought a stone Draft and then tried to dance will explain, and that is how a bridge gets knocked down badly. The full account of the lens is on the [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/The Sixty Wellsprings|hub]].

*Each Wellspring below opens on what it is. **The idea** is the concept it lays over a soul's Aether, and **The turn**, where the trade has fixed one, is the moment that idea comes clear to someone working inside it. **Kind** names its home plane, and never locks it. **Titan** is the Titan whose law it is one showing of, **Hue** the color it gives an aura, **Ground** what it favors and hates underfoot, and **Contrary** the Wellsprings whose laws fight it in one soul. **On the Body**, **On the Spirit** and **On Fate** say what the lens does to Essence on each Path, through the Aether Shell (the layer of the soul that meets matter), the Essence Core (the layer that holds identity and authors what a working is) and the Attraction Layer (a soul's own share of fate, its bonds, oaths, claims and odds). **Spirit** is the transformation it works on the soul, one road of Temperance (the climb of a soul from Stage to Stage), and **Alchemy** the principle it stands for at the bench. Then comes how scholars explain it: Analogue and Mechanism give the physics of what that type of Aether does to a Soul Crystal, Stat Effect what shifts on the sheet, Glyph the forms the Index seats on it, and Failure how it goes wrong. Entries run in alphabetical order.*

## Abython · *The Abyssal Pulse*

**The idea** · Peace with the abyss. Matching the pressure in place of fighting it, so that the soul can walk where everyone else suffocates.

**Kind** · Wild

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Abyssal Depths — Prometheus, Zar'thuun, Vohrin|Zar'thuun]]: annihilation as peace, the dark of Abythron met on its own terms.

**Hue** · Near-black.

**Ground** · Favors structural mass, elevation and brittle or pre-stressed material; hates loose sediment, free fall and uniform soft ground.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Spirit Wellsprings|Thaloré]] (pressure felt in full against pressure matched away); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Chthonica]] (gripping against floating free of net stress); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Petralon]] (bearing the load against having no load to bear).

**On the Body** · Flesh stops holding a difference against the world, so a crushing blow finds less to crush and Aether-starved ground does not suffocate the fighter who walks onto it. The same body hurried back into a rich hall comes apart in ways a diver would recognize.

**On the Spirit** · The soul matches whatever is pressed on it, and a rival's Aura Pressure (the felt weight of another's power) meets nothing pushing back and little to grip. The trouble comes when it is time to throw something, since a Core with no difference in it has very little to push with.

**On Fate** · Presence drops to exactly what the room already has, so the holder can stand unremarked in a hall full of people hunting them. Stretching that over a column, so that it passes through a hostile province with no sentry or rumor finding a difference to catch on, is the work of every waking hour of the march, and the return to ordinary company takes the holder the way a fast ascent takes a diver.

**Spirit** · Temperance through void.

**Alchemy** · The balanced vessel: pressure inside matched to pressure outside, so the glass has nothing to resist. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Pressure equalization. Neutral buoyancy and the vanishing of net stress.

**Mechanism** · A vessel at depth survives by matching the water, never by resisting it, because net stress is a difference and a difference of zero loads nothing. Abython trains the Shell to equalize rather than oppose, which is why its practitioners work in Aether-absent environments that suffocate everyone else: they have stopped holding pressure in and stopped maintaining a differential at all.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Resilience Persistence, Harmonics Stability, Vitality Tolerance. The character operates where other practitioners suffocate.

**Glyph** · None attested in the Index (see Discrepancies of Record on the hub).

**Failure** · Rapid ascent. Return to ordinary Aetheric density too fast and the equalized interior comes out of solution, which is as unpleasant metaphysically as it is in a diver.

## Anima Spirare · *The Soul's Breath*

**The idea** · The breath of the whole. A body that remembers being whole and answers a small hurt with everything it has.

**Kind** · Wild

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Spiritual Seat — Omnis, Florwyn, Dazkar, Nysera, Sylorin, Ilgrimm|Omnis]]: to breathe is briefly to echo him, and this is a whole body doing it at once.

**Hue** · Bright arterial red.

**Ground** · Favors living biomass, temperate conditions and proximity to blood; hates sterile ground, extreme cold and Essence-depleted soil.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Medium Wellsprings|Mortalis]] (a body refusing the gate against a soul walking to it).

**On the Body** · A small cut draws an answer from the whole body, blood and breath arriving in a flood exactly where they are wanted, so wounds seal under the fighter's own pulse and the last round is breathed as easily as the first. Lose the signal that says where, and the flood goes everywhere.

**On the Spirit** · A thrown working carries a memory of wholeness and forces it on what it touches, so a split ward or a dying man's ribs are told what they used to be, loudly, and answer as well as they can. It costs dearly and works best on what was once alive.

**On Fate** · Pull one bond and all of them wake: hurt one of a crew and the others feel it under the breastbone, and in the rare great working a flank struck at dawn finds the reserve already on the road with nobody able to say who sent it. It costs blood from every vein at once and cannot easily be called off, so a feint against one picket has been known to turn out the whole army and leave nothing to close the real wound.

**Spirit** · Temperance through vitality. *The confessors' reading:* remembering wholeness, wounds closing because the body remembers being whole.

**Alchemy** · The quickening: the steady blood-warmth of the Bed, in which a charge stays alive. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · The coagulation cascade and cooperative oxygen binding.

**Mechanism** · Hemostasis is an amplifying cascade and never one event, in which each activated factor activates many of the next, so a small injury signal produces a large and precisely localized response. Layered under it, hemoglobin's cooperative binding means the fourth oxygen loads far more readily than the first. Anima Spirare imposes both patterns: amplification where it is needed, and a delivery curve that steepens with demand.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Hemostasis, Regeneration, Harmonics Synergy. The body pulses with living rhythm. Wounds close because the body remembers being whole.

**Glyph** · `[Leth]` Blood, `[Dr]` Vitality, `[Bst]` Beast; attested on two more.

**Failure** · Disseminated activation. A cascade with no localizing signal runs everywhere at once, consuming its own factors until nothing is left to close the wound that started it.

*The story they tell.*

Bertil Ashgrove kept a barber's chair on Mill Gate with a red-striped pole and a painted board that read TWO COPPERS A WOUND, NO CREDIT FOR COLORS. The day the Millrace Boys met the Hammers of Saint Orrin on the bridge over a disputed bout, he walked into the middle of it with his sleeves rolled and arterial red brightening every vein in both arms, and he closed fifty-three wounds in an hour on both sides, each wet pulse sealing a gash, taking his two coppers each time and flatly refusing the two men who had stiffed him at the last fair. The fifty-fourth wound was a nick on his own thumb. By then the cascade had nothing left to tell it where to stop, it ran through him everywhere at once, and that one small nick bled for three days while both clubs, in the only agreement they have ever reached, took turns holding his arm up and paying for ice.

## Ascensio · *The Ladder of Ascent*

**The idea** · The ladder. The whole height climbed by steps, with no single step large enough to break you.

**Kind** · Wild

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Physical Order — Atla'zon and Gaia|Atla'zon]]: which way is down, and the height paid for in his coin, one step at a time.

**Hue** · Steel gray with pulley-lines.

**Ground** · Favors structural mass, elevation and brittle or pre-stressed material; hates loose sediment, free fall and uniform soft ground.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Exuroth]] (the ordeal against the road with no step large enough to break you); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Sublimatio]] (every step climbed against no steps at all).

**On the Body** · The body takes its load in steps. A fighter climbs a long fight by small increases of force, each one inside what the joints will bear, and arrives at a blow that would have torn them apart had they opened with it.

**On the Spirit** · A working is authored in stages, each inscription standing on the last, until a long prepared piece reaches a height no single casting could survive. Anyone who interrupts it on the fourth step has saved themselves a great deal of trouble.

**On Fate** · It hardly moves the odds on any given day, and thirty years of days is another matter: one more captain owing the right man, one more village out of forage, until the porter's nephew is dining with the harbor court and the enemy's army is a day short of everything. A whole war climbed this way is a life's masterwork and the debt of it is deferred, so the holder arrives at the top owing every step at once, and in a single fight it buys no more than a slight edge by the fourteenth exchange.

**Spirit** · Temperance through rising.

**Alchemy** · The degrees of fire: one heat reached by steps, where a fool reaches it at a stroke and loses the glass. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Mechanical advantage. Path-independence of gravitational potential energy.

**Mechanism** · Lifting a mass to a height costs the same total work by any route, but the inclined plane trades distance for peak force and so the same climb can be made without ever exceeding what the structure can bear. Ascensio applies that to Threshold transitions, spreading the required change across a longer path so the Crystal never sees the peak stress that a direct ascent imposes.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Tempering Maturity, Harmonics Attunement. Stage transitions become smoother and less violent.

**Glyph** · None attested in the Index (see Discrepancies of Record on the hub).

**Failure** · Deferred load. The work is redistributed and none of it is reduced. Practitioners mistake the absence of crisis for the absence of cost and arrive at the next Stage owing the difference.

## Basilithe · *The Stone Will*

**The idea** · The stone will. One note the soul holds that nobody can retune.

**Kind** · Wild

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Physical Order — Atla'zon and Gaia|Atla'zon]]: permanence, one posture held because he knelt first.

**Hue** · Ochre-gold with quartz-clear lines.

**Ground** · Favors mineral-rich rock, worked stone and deep foundation; hates open water, high altitude and moving platforms.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Materia Primordia]] (every form against the one note); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Transmutatio]] (one note nobody can retune against the same thing said in another key).

**On the Body** · The Crystal's stiffness comes down into the bones and the body refuses to be shaken, stunned or struck out of rhythm, with shockwaves passing through a stance that holds its own pitch. Every so often somebody finds that pitch with a hammer, and the fighter rings like a bell and comes apart.

**On the Spirit** · The soul holds one note against any working that would retune it, so compulsion, false memory and imposed law slide off, and so does any new idea. Thrown, it is a dead pitch that stops an enemy's chant from resolving, and that is about all.

**On Fate** · A soul that cannot be retuned is a standard for other people: merchants trust their weights, and a wavering line drifts toward their pitch without noticing, which is the whole of the lens on most days. Holding that note against an enemy's arranged panic is a contest that can crack the holder's Crystal if lost, and an enemy who finds the one pressure that matches it brings down the man and the line together.

**Spirit** · Temperance through permanence. *The confessors' reading:* constancy, the soul's frequency refusing retuning.

**Alchemy** · The Standard: the one fixed measure every other weight is read against. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Natural frequency and stiffness-to-mass ratio. Quartz oscillator stability.

**Mechanism** · A body's resonant frequency is fixed by its stiffness and its mass, and a high-stiffness low-loss structure holds that frequency against forcing so reliably that a Measurewright with a cut quartz on a string will set her water clock by it. Basilithe raises the Crystal's stiffness until its resonance simply will not be driven off, which is a defense against every technique that works by imposing an external rhythm.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Resilience Hardening, Harmonics Stability. The soul's frequency locks and refuses retuning.

**Glyph** · `[Grv]` Gravity; attested on one more.

**Failure** · Resonant catastrophe. A frequency that cannot be shifted also cannot be detuned away from a forcing that happens to match it.

## Cataclysm · *The Shattered Law*

**The idea** · Upheaval. The moment a fire stops being a fire and becomes a blast, a change in kind as well as size, and the soul that has learned to call that moment.

**Kind** · Wild

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Strata — Valmir, Kaldrion, Chronaeus|Valmir]]: collapse as the moment of transition, the instant a thing changes in kind.

**Hue** · White-orange flash with a pale ring.

**Ground** · Favors steep thermal gradients, combustible atmosphere and geothermal ground; hates thermal equilibrium, saturated cold and oxygen-poor air.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Solfatara]] (never building against everything built going at once); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Spirit Wellsprings|Lethegraal]] (upheaval against settling on its own schedule).

**On the Body** · Given confinement behind it, a set stance or a wall at the enemy's back, a blow stops being a push and goes over into a blast, and the pressure crosses the target faster than the target can flinch.

**On the Spirit** · A projected working can change in kind partway across the distance, leaving as one thing and arriving as a rupture, and the people it arrives on rarely get to learn what it was meant to be.

**On Fate** · Slow quarrels near this person can be brought past their point all at once, so a garrison's ten-year feud and a month of short rations turn into a mutiny in one night, and a Domain (a region where the world runs on a practitioner's terms) widens by whole streets as everyone picks a side. It wants confinement, it comes off perhaps once in a war, and what comes back along the same path finds the holder standing at the origin with their ears and fingers at stake.

**Spirit** · Temperance through upheaval.

**Alchemy** · The vessel's violent end taken as a law: the moment a burning charge stops burning and becomes a blast. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Deflagration-to-detonation transition. Blast scaling under the cube-root law.

**Mechanism** · A subsonic flame front that meets sufficient confinement and turbulence accelerates until the reaction couples to a shock wave and begins propagating faster than sound in the unreacted medium. The mechanism changes kind where a lesser one would only change degree. Cataclysm makes that transition available to Essence expression, and blast radius thereafter scales with the cube root of yield, which is why the Wellspring's area effects widen coherently rather than merely spilling.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Ardency Cascade, Ardency Overchannel, Dominion Radius. Area expressions become wider and more structurally coherent.

**Glyph** · `[Thn]` Thunder, `[Eith]` Flux, `[Wy]` Motion; attested on five more.

**Failure** · Reflected overpressure. In confinement the shock returns along its own path, and the practitioner standing at the origin receives the sum of the wave and its reflection.

## Chthonica · *The Root Deep*

**The idea** · The root. Holding on by touching everything, so that whatever you stand on becomes yours to stand on.

**Kind** · Wild

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Physical Order — Atla'zon and Gaia|Atla'zon]]: adhesion, the reason anything stays where it was put.

**Hue** · Dark iron-oxide.

**Ground** · Favors mineral-rich rock, worked stone and deep foundation; hates open water, high altitude and moving platforms.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Cymorath]] (every way open against holding to what is underfoot); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Spirit Wellsprings|Oneirion]] (a place with no ground against nothing but ground); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Abython]] (gripping against floating free of net stress).

**On the Body** · The Shell takes hold of whatever bears it with a countless fine grip, so a fighter stands on a sheer wall or a bucking deck as if it were a parlor floor and cannot be thrown off either, every blow going down into what they stand on. A wet deck or a fouled boot drops them with no warning at all.

**On the Spirit** · From the Core it roots a working to the place, and a ward done this way sits in the stone the way a tooth sits in a jaw. Dull to throw and a fine thing to leave behind, and Spirit Path scholars on this lens tend to stop traveling.

**On Fate** · The person and the ground become each other's claim, so a family on its own land has a hold that bailiffs have failed to break, and a Domain raised there comes up out of the floor with its roots already in. A lifetime on one valley can turn it against an invader, the wagons miring in the one soft lane and the wells going sour in the week they are needed, while each pull draws the holder down with the water, and moved to a new country they are slow to know what to do with their hands.

**Spirit** · Temperance through grounding. *The confessors' reading:* rootedness.

**Alchemy** · Lute, the humble seal: it holds because it touches everything. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Static friction and molecular adhesion. Interlocking asperity; setal van der Waals contact.

**Mechanism** · Grip owes nothing to weight. A gecko holds to glass through billions of hair-scale contacts each contributing an intermolecular attraction too small to measure alone. Chthonica multiplies the Shell's contact area with whatever surface bears it, converting proximity into anchorage, which is why its practitioners root to sheer stone and moving decks with equal indifference.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Absorption, Dexterity Economy, Dominion Gravity. The practitioner roots to whatever surface they stand on.

**Glyph** · `[Sed]` Sediment; attested on three more.

**Failure** · Contamination. The mechanism depends on clean contact, and a fouled interface releases without warning and without a partial stage.

## Cinerion · *The Ash Veil*

**The idea** · What the fire leaves. Ash taken as treasure: every spent thing feeds the next thing, and the soul learns to live on its own aftermath.

**Kind** · Wild

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Abyssal Depths — Prometheus, Zar'thuun, Vohrin|Prometheus]]: what his fire leaves, which he has never once counted as waste.

**Hue** · Gray-orange, embers showing through ash.

**Ground** · Favors steep thermal gradients, combustible atmosphere and geothermal ground; hates thermal equilibrium, saturated cold and oxygen-poor air.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Sublimare]] (treasure in what stays behind against treasure in what rises).

**On the Body** · The Shell eats its own leavings, turning spent Essence and burned-off effort into second wind, so the practitioner looks worst and feeds best at the end of a long fight.

**On the Spirit** · What a working leaves behind feeds the next one, and the practitioner can read a thing by its residue, getting from a burned letter some of what it said.

**On Fate** · Lost causes settle on the holder like ash in a low place, and after a long enough defeat a fighter can pull on what has been spent, so the failed feint of an hour ago becomes the place the opponent steps wrong. A beaten commander who lives on this may make the burned bridge and the dead men's debts delay a pursuit by a week, at the cost of whatever ruin they had left to draw on, and the gift fouls and stops on the day they have spent the most.

**Spirit** · Temperance through aftermath.

**Alchemy** · The law Calcination draws on, the Burning of the bench: strong dry heat drives off what is volatile and leaves what is fixed.

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Pyrolytic char and its catalytic residue. Particulate optical depth.

**Mechanism** · People call what survives a fire waste, and people are wrong. Char is a high-surface-area carbon scaffold that catalyzes further reaction, and settled ash returns potassium and phosphorus to the ground that fed the fire. Cinerion retunes the Shell to metabolize its own combustion products, harvesting from expenditure what other practitioners simply vent. The suspended residue also scatters incident light and Essence-sight alike.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Tempering Yield, Harmonics Stability. The residue of destruction becomes recovery material.

**Glyph** · None attested in the Index (see Discrepancies of Record on the hub).

**Failure** · Fouling. Accumulated residue eventually coats the conduit rather than feeding it, and recovery rate collapses precisely when the practitioner has spent the most.

## Coagula · *The Unifying Pulse*

**The idea** · Gathering the broken. Scattered pieces come back into one body without ever melting, keeping the shape they had while broken, the flesh first and then the soul that wears it.

**Kind** · Wild

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Physical Order — Atla'zon and Gaia|Gaia]]: material harmony, scattered matter returned to one body.

**Hue** · Amber-gray.

**Ground** · Favors mineral-rich rock, worked stone and deep foundation; hates open water, high altitude and moving platforms.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Dissolution]] (the bench's oldest pair, unbinding against gathering); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Fractura]] (gathering the broken against finding where it breaks).

**On the Body** · Broken bone and split muscle draw back together under the Shell with no melting stage and no pause, each piece keeping the shape it had while broken, so a fighter who has been smashed gets up crooked and whole. The great Coagula brawlers look like sacks of walnuts and are extremely hard to finish.

**On the Spirit** · In the Core the same law does the old inward work: a soul split by a bad Threshold or a bad decade gathers up its own pieces, and what it throws holds together over distance because no part of it will consent to leave. Cast on anything else it is dear and brief, and the legs pay.

**On Fate** · Scattered things come to the same table, and feuding cousins and lost partners find themselves in one town with this person somehow always in the doorway. The great working of it, a broken army's stragglers finding the same crossroads on the same night, takes the holder years to pay back, and the reassembled line keeps every seam where it was, which is the first place a sharp enemy hits.

**Spirit** · Temperance through merging. *The confessors' reading:* the Soul Plane's unifying force, a soul gathering itself back together.

**Alchemy** · The personal twin of Coagulatio: gathering without melting, a person's own Setting. Basalt takes it.

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Sintering. Densification by atomic diffusion below the melting point.

**Mechanism** · Press powder together and hold it hot, though never hot enough to melt, and the particles fuse across their contact points by diffusion alone until the porosity closes and the mass behaves as one body. Coagula runs that on a person: a broken body, or a Crystal that has been fragmented, gathers itself without ever passing through a liquid state, which is to say without losing the shape it had while broken. That is why it is filed Wild, since you can watch it happen to a man on the floor of a ring, whatever the confessors say it is doing in him. The law runs inward by nature. A handful of high arts throw it outward, gathering the ground under a stance or the Essence in a room into one body, and they pay for the reversal in the practitioner's own legs and Shell.

Coagula is a person's gathering, flesh and soul together, and a few fighters at great cost have thrown the same current outward over a room or the ground underfoot. It lasts as long as they hold it. Matter gathered for good belongs to Coagulatio.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Resilience Integrity, Tempering Coherence. The Crystal becomes harder to split.

**Glyph** · None primary. Attested on `[Krn]` Crown and `[Wy]` Motion.

**Failure** · Residual porosity. Sintering rarely reaches full density, and the voids that remain are the exact locations at which the next fracture will initiate.

## Coagulatio · *The Returning Stone*

**The idea** · Settling round a seed. Scattered things find one grain and grow out from it, denser and far less wasteful than they were.

**Kind** · Wild

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Physical Order — Atla'zon and Gaia|Gaia]]: the Corpus under the crust, where what is dispersed condenses into crystalline ore.

**Hue** · Pale amber crystals.

**Ground** · Favors mineral-rich rock, worked stone and deep foundation; hates open water, high altitude and moving platforms.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Spirit Wellsprings|Abyntheus]] (a soul stretched over the great distance against a soul settled round one grain); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Dissolution]] (going into solution against coming out of it).

**On the Body** · Essence sets around one grain in the Shell the way sugar sets on a string, and what was spray becomes mass, so a punch loses almost nothing on its way out and the reserve sits denser in the same body. Start too many grains at once and the fighter is left with a fine heavy sludge in the arms and no way to spend it.

**On the Spirit** · From the Core a working sets around a chosen seed, one word or one grain of the target, and goes on growing after the hand has gone, so a seal thrown at noon may still be adding to itself by evening. It is slow work and it does not stop on request.

**On Fate** · Whatever is scattered around the holder starts to crystallize of its own accord: hangers-on into a household, three bad debts into one very large creditor. Forcing it on a routing crowd, so that it sets into a square of spears around the one man who stopped running, comes off about as often as such a man survives, and two of them on one field curdle an army into small loyal knots that will not take orders from each other.

**Spirit** · Temperance through re-forming. *The confessors' reading:* settling, the scattered self finding its seed.

**Alchemy** · One of the six named operations and the stage of Coagulation: a charge sets round one grain, and the grain grows on after the hand is gone. It deactivates cleanly under Preparation Failure.

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Crystallization from supersaturated solution. Nucleation and ordered growth.

**Mechanism** · A supersaturated solution holds more solute than equilibrium permits and will surrender it the instant a nucleation site appears, depositing ordered solid out of apparent nothing. Coagulatio is the material twin of Coagula: where that Wellspring gathers a person, this one gathers substrate, pulling dispersed Essence out of suspension into a denser and far less wasteful phase.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Ardency Compression, Tempering Clarity. Essence becomes denser and harder to waste.

**Glyph** · `[Plr]` Pillar; attested on nine more.

**Failure** · Uncontrolled nucleation. Too many sites at once produces a fine disordered mass rather than a sound crystal, and the practitioner ends with density they cannot use.

**Analogue** · Signal-to-noise reduction. Absorptive coating and faceted return geometry.

**Mechanism** · Something is undetectable when its return falls below the noise floor of whatever is looking, which is achieved by absorbing incident energy and by shaping surfaces so the little that reflects goes anywhere except back at the observer. Tenebra applies both to the Shell. Its practitioners stay perfectly visible and run quieter than the background, which is better.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Dexterity Feint, Harmonics Suppression, Dominion Sense. The practitioner becomes difficult to detect, read, or track by conventional or Aetheric means.

**Glyph** · `[Thal]` Silence, `[Stg]` Stagnate; attested on five more.

**Failure** · Aspect dependence. Faceting works against expected angles, and a Tenebra practitioner observed from the one direction they did not plan for returns a very bright signal indeed. Pushed too hard, the coating saturates: everything absorbed is still in the Shell, and a practitioner held under the noise floor past their limit lets it go at once and lights up on every sense in the room.

*The story they tell.*

For seven years there was a price on the head of Hanne Mott and nobody could describe the head. She went through the Count of Wexmoor's strong-room with his guards dicing on the other side of the door, matte black-violet swallowing the lamplight around her and every sound in the corridor going dead as wet felt. Heralds cried the bounty up from four hundred silver to two thousand, the minstrels made *The Woman Who Is Not There*, which gets her height and her number of legs wrong, and bookies took odds on whether she existed. She planned every angle of every job. On the tenth job a pot boy had gone up the chimney stack after pigeon eggs, and he looked straight down on her from the one direction nobody plans for, and to him she was the brightest thing on Wexmoor Hill.

*"MISTER! THERE'S A LADY!"*

She left with a crossbow bolt through the shoulder, in a hay cart, at speed. The boy got a verse.

## Terranova · *The Living Earth*

**The idea** · The living earth. Mending by growing, every wound answered with new ground.

**Kind** · Wild

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Physical Order — Atla'zon and Gaia|Gaia]]: the living mantle itself, the ground that heals.

**Hue** · Pale limestone.

**Ground** · Favors mineral-rich rock, worked stone and deep foundation; hates open water, high altitude and moving platforms.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Medium Wellsprings|Mortalis]] (every wound answered with new ground against the ending understood).

**On the Body** · A wound here is answered by growth, new mineral and new tissue laid down along the damage, so the fighter heals by extension and the old scar becomes the strongest part of the arm. They also get larger in the places they have been hurt, and tailors charge accordingly.

**On the Spirit** · In the Core, breakage seeds new structure, and a soul cracked by loss grows a wing it did not have. Thrown into someone else's wound it will grow what it is told, which only healers and torturers attempt, and the line between them is a long-running argument.

**On Fate** · What gets hurt around this person tends to come back thicker, a broken friendship regrown in a new shape and a burned village replanted at their feet. A holder can draw on that in war only by taking the damage first and paying it in their own flesh, so the breach in the wall draws every mason in the district by morning while new growth crowds the holder's own joints, and they cannot always tell you which of it they wanted.

**Spirit** · Temperance through birth. *The confessors' reading:* regrowth.

**Alchemy** · The metal tree in the still flask: a mass that mends by growing. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Biomineralization and self-healing composite. Nacre deposition; bacterial concrete.

**Mechanism** · Some structures do not get repaired. They grow their repair in place, laying down mineral along the damage front by the same process that built the original. Terranova gives the Crystal that behavior, which is why its practitioners heal by extension rather than by closure and why their scars are new tissue rather than sealed old tissue.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Regeneration, Harmonics Synergy. The body heals by growing rather than repairing.

**Glyph** · None primary. Attested on twelve, among them `[Sed]` Sediment, `[Grt]` Granite and `[Th]` Foundation.

**Failure** · Overgrowth. Deposition does not know when to stop, and the repaired region continues to accrete past the original geometry.

## Transmutatio · *The Law of Change*

**The idea** · Change without loss. The same thing said another way, so that what looked like a failure turns out to be fuel in an inconvenient form.

**The turn** · Discovering that where you are is a choice. It treats a thing's location as no less governable than its shape.

**Kind** · Wild

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Abyssal Depths — Prometheus, Zar'thuun, Vohrin|Prometheus]]: the first lesson of the teacher of Transmutation, that nothing is lost when a thing changes its form.

**Hue** · Blue-violet.

**Ground** · Favors Veil-thin zones, established Realmgates (standing doorways between Realms) and surveyed ground; hates warp scarring, contested Domains (spaces where one practitioner's law outranks local physics) and unmapped terrain.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Fate Wellsprings|Oblation]] (force bought with a loss against change with no loss); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Basilithe]] (one note nobody can retune against the same thing said in another key).

**On the Body** · A missed fist or a failed block is still energy, and the Shell says it over in another form, so the stumble becomes the next step and the broken punch comes back through the other hand.

**On the Spirit** · A working gone wrong can be restated from the Core in another basis, the same quantity said another way, and a thing described from far enough round starts to answer to the new description.

**On Fate** · A broken oath or a bad debt can be carried over into other terms by a patient holder, and in a desperate hour so can a lost exchange, what was given up in the last pass turning up as the other fighter's wrong step in the next. Carrying a lost battle over into a won road, the routed wing turning out to be why the enemy's supply has gone astray, is a masterwork few attempt twice, because a holder who loses track of the terms they started in does not come back as anything recognizable.

**Spirit** · Temperance through motion.

**Alchemy** · One of the six named operations: it re-expresses a thing in another basis, nothing created or lost. The Bench files a Transmutatio Draft as a composite, and it is volatile: under Preparation Failure it does not deactivate at all.

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Change of basis. Coordinate transformation under conservation.

**Mechanism** · The same object described in two frames yields two entirely different sets of numbers and one unchanged object. Nothing is created or lost in a transformation; the quantities are merely re-expressed. Transmutatio applies this to failed workings, which are Essence in an inconvenient basis with none of it destroyed, and the Wellspring supplies the transformation that makes them usable again.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Ardency Overchannel, Tempering Maturity. Failed workings become fuel rather than wounds.

**Glyph** · `[Zh]` Space, `[Flx]` Flux; attested on seven more.

**Failure** · Basis loss. The practitioner forgets which frame they started in, and a transformation with no reference frame does not return anything recognizable.

## Vantabriel · *The Wellspring of Night*

**The idea** · The night that keeps. Enduring by carrying everything that was lost, with none of it put down.

**Kind** · Wild

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Strata — Valmir, Kaldrion, Chronaeus|Chronaeus]]: accumulated consequence, with nothing ever put down.

**Hue** · Near-black with a retained faint glow.

**Ground** · Favors darkness, silence, Aether-thin ground and ruins; hates saturated Aether, crowd density and active Domains.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Pyreveil]] (burning it down against keeping all of it); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Spirit Wellsprings|Catharsis]] (setting it all down against carrying it all); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Medium Wellsprings|Aurevane]] (emission against absorption).

**On the Body** · What strikes the practitioner stays in them: the blow goes into muscle and bone and nothing comes back out, no recoil and no spray of force, only a slow heat from inside. Every fight they have survived is still in the flesh, which is how someone who moves like an old man comes to stand under a maul.

**On the Spirit** · Thrown from the Core, a working on this lens reflects nothing and reports nothing. Whatever it takes joins the pile, and a ward it swallows becomes one more thing the caster must carry for life.

**On Fate** · The lost stay bound to them, every dead friend, broken oath and failed cause keeping its pull on the Attraction Layer, so such a person trails a slow heavy pull that the sensitive feel as an empty chair, and survivors of ruined companies find their way to that door. Calling the whole weight down at once, so that a general who has beaten them five times looks up on the sixth field and finds all five defeats drawn up against him under one banner, is done once if at all, and the holder has to carry everything it brings.

**Spirit** · Temperance through absence. *The confessors' reading:* carrying what was lost.

**Alchemy** · The black that keeps: char that holds everything it has ever taken. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Near-total absorption and hysteresis. Retained state dependent on history.

**Mechanism** · An ultra-absorptive surface returns almost nothing and converts what it receives into internal heat, and a hysteretic material's present state depends not on current conditions but on the path it took to arrive. Vantabriel combines them: what strikes the practitioner is kept rather than reflected, and the Crystal's condition is a function of everything it has ever absorbed. **Endurance here is loss carried, with none of it replaced.**

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Resilience Continuity, Harmonics Attunement, Tempering Longevity. The character endures by carrying the memory of what was lost rather than replacing it.

**Glyph** · `[DmN]` Dominion; attested on one more.

**Failure** · Thermal saturation. Absorption without emission has a limit, and past it the retained load has nowhere to go but into the structure holding it.

## Verdantia · *The Green Pulse*

**The idea** · Growth without end. A self with no final size, which feeds on simply being out in the world. It comes through accumulation rather than rupture, one good day on top of another.

**Kind** · Wild

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Spiritual Seat — Omnis, Florwyn, Dazkar, Nysera, Sylorin, Ilgrimm|Florwyn]]: growth itself, her breath in every green thing.

**Hue** · Chlorophyll green.

**Ground** · Favors living biomass, temperate conditions and proximity to blood; hates sterile ground, extreme cold and Essence-depleted soil.

**Contrary** · [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Monolithion]] (the finished monument against the self with no final size); [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Medium Wellsprings|Mortalis]] (more life against the boundary of life).

**On the Body** · The body never settles on a final size and recovers simply by being outdoors, so a long fight on open ground tends to leave the Verdantia fighter bigger at the end than at the start. Keep them in a cellar and they grow long, pale and useless.

**On the Spirit** · An inscription keeps growing after its author has walked away, and a line written on a wall in spring has put out new clauses by autumn. Nobody has found a way to make it grow in the direction they wrote.

**On Fate** · A name spreads the way a root system does, quietly and in every direction, until the holder has cousins and tenants in towns they never visited, and after decades an invading army may find itself buying its bread and hiring its guides from all of them. A Domain (a region where the world runs on a practitioner's terms) raised on this lens keeps creeping outward for as long as it is held, at a growing cost that neighbors resent and the holder, thinning out at the center, eventually cannot feed.

**Spirit** · Temperance through growth.

**Alchemy** · The Feeding: growth given a little at a time and never all at once. *(the trade's reading)*

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Carbon fixation and indeterminate meristematic growth.

**Mechanism** · A plant does not have a final size. Meristem tissue remains embryonic for the organism's whole life, and photosynthesis converts ambient energy into structure continuously rather than in bouts. Verdantia gives the Crystal indeterminate growth and continuous ambient conversion, which is why its practitioners recover while doing nothing and why they are difficult to exhaust in a long engagement.

**Stat Effect** · Strengthens Vitality Regeneration, Tempering Yield, Harmonics Synergy. Passive Essence recovery accelerates. The character heals by growing rather than repairing.

**Glyph** · `[El]` Life, `[Eld]` Elderwood, `[WL]` Wildlaw, `[Blm]` Bloom, `[Flo]` Bloom-Root; attested on seventeen more.

**Failure** · Etiolation. Deprived of ambient input the growth continues anyway, producing length without substance until the structure cannot hold itself up.

## Outside the Sixty · Vohrin · *The Stillwater Core*

> **This entry is the register's one exception, and it is marked as one.** The Sixty are fixed, and Vohrin is not among them. It is the power of a Titan, [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Abyssal Depths — Prometheus, Zar'thuun, Vohrin|the Prime Cryotheic of Niflheim]], worked directly at Wellspring level, where the other thirteen Titans reach a soul by way of the Sixty. The note sits on this page because heat run backward is still a thing in the physical world, and because fighters keep writing "Vohrin (Caloria)" in their entries, after a column the trade no longer reads, and somebody has to tell them what they have written.

**The idea** · The cold that stills. Prometheus burns what is false, Zar'thuun unmakes what is excess, and Vohrin stills what is left so it can be built upon. Cold here is no fire of its own. It is a deficit, heat let go of and held as a discipline.

**Kind** · Wild

**Titan** · [[Cosmology & Metaphysics/Faith and the Powers Above/The Fourteen Titans/The Abyssal Depths — Prometheus, Zar'thuun, Vohrin|Vohrin]] itself: entropy's peace, the settling of endings. No Wellspring stands between this power and its Titan.

**Hue** · Ember-orange banked down nearly to brown, under a skin of rime. The blue belongs to the ice.

**Ground** · Favors cold air, deep ice and a long gradient to pump along; hates warm air, which costs many times as much, and thermal equilibrium, which leaves nothing to pump.

**Contrary** · Heat forced back in faster than it can be taken, which is why [[The Magic System/The Foundations/The Sixty Wellsprings/Wild Wellsprings|Pyreveil]] and its like are the standard answer. It stands outside the Sixty, so the contrary is a matter of physics and no entry names it back.

**On the Body** · Heat leaves the tissue along a held gradient, so the blow carries a deficit into the wound and the holder's own blood runs slow and steady under it.

**On the Spirit** · Projected from the Core, the cold stills what it reaches and holds a thing at what it already is, and a hurrying thought or a half-finished working simply stops where it stood.

**On Fate** · What is held stays held: a bond stops moving in either direction, and with the whole of a life's discipline a charge can lose its reasons halfway across the field, or a war about to happen can sit in camp through the last of the good weather. The result has only been put off, and whoever is holding it off grows steadily colder in the fingers and then in the purpose.

**Two levels** · One power, worked two ways. The frost arts of the hunters and the northern schools work it at Wellspring level or as a Titan-derived current, each at the Stage floor its own entry states. The Titan-Keyed Stillwater Rite works it at the Titan's level and is gated at Revelation.

**Where it pools** · A cold site is a Titan-touched site. Surveyors and renderers read a Vohrin gallery the way they read a Core, with the same care and the same rope, and it does not go on any list of the Sixty's sites.

*How scholars explain it.*

**Analogue** · Heat extraction. Conduction down a held gradient and the minimum work of a heat pump, which climbs steeply as the cold end falls; at the Titan's level, the zero-flux condition of a body that neither emits nor absorbs.

**Mechanism** · Cold is never emitted. A region goes cold only when energy leaves it, so the Shell takes heat out along a gradient that already exists and has to put it somewhere: the lungs, the air behind the practitioner, the bank of a field. In cold air the heat is cheap to move and in warm air it costs many times as much, and in thermal equilibrium there is nothing to pump at all. At contact the ordered cold crystallizes the Essence pathway in a wound.

**Stat Effect** · None of record, since it has no row in Part Thirteen. In use, Vitality Tolerance answers for the cold.

**Glyph** · No primary of its own that the register accepts. Its arts write in common forms such as `[Vor]` Return and `[Cro]` Cycle, and the Codex's frost rows, `[FrTh]` FrostThrone and `[ObsG]` Obsidian-Glacier, still stand under Cymorath's name (see the Cymorath entry).

**Failure** · Cold soak. Take heat past what the sink can carry and the deficit settles in whoever is holding it: the fingers, the lungs, then the mind, with purpose going before pain. A cold that is only a held deficit is also broken by heat forced back in faster than it can be taken, which is how flame ends the Stillwater Rite.

**Signature** · The aura is ember-orange banked down nearly to brown under a skin of rime. The blue that people remember is the ice, and the aura is not blue. Sound is a long creak of settling ice and then the sound of the room falling away; the air smells scrubbed and then of nothing; heat leaves the skin toward the working. The practitioner's lungs ache and small purposes slip. The tell is frost running out from the feet, with the breath stopping in the air before it has gone anywhere.

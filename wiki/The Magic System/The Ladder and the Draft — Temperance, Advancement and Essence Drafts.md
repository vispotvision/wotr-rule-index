# The Ladder and the Draft — Temperance, Advancement and Essence Drafts

> **The Stage-by-Stage account lives in The Sixteen Stages** — each Stage given four ways: the Codex ledger line, what it is from inside the person it is happening to, what it looks like from outside, and what the folk call it. **This page does not repeat it.**
>
> What this page does is join the ladder to the rest of the system: what changes in each Crystal layer as a soul climbs, what an Essence Draft is, what a Draft can and cannot do to advancement, and why the Accord's most profitable illegal trade exists.
>
> **One sentence governs everything below. A Threshold requires a quantitative floor and a Catalyst Condition, and no compound ever rendered has supplied a Catalyst.**

---

## I · The two keys

Temperance is subtraction. Each Stage is a structural reorganisation the Crystal undergoes when accumulated pressure exceeds its current architecture, never a reward for banked experience. A Threshold opens only when both keys turn.
**The quantitative floor.** Sufficient pressure inside the current Stage. Measurable, purchasable at the margins, and the half of the equation the Accord's certification apparatus is built to read.
**The Catalyst Condition.** A specific quality of experience the Crystal requires before it will authorise the transition. **A practitioner can sit quantitatively eligible for months of in-world time and never pass a Threshold, because the Catalyst has not occurred.** The System does not advance a soul that has not genuinely changed. This is accuracy, not punishment.
> **A soul is assembled out of what happened to it and what it could afford, in that order.**
The second key is why the ladder cannot be bought, and the first key is why people keep trying.

## II · The arc, in five movements

*Summary only. The full treatment is in The Sixteen Stages.*
**Inner Refinement · Band F · Stages I to IV.** Murmuring, Welling, Ascension, Flourishing. **Better than nine souls in ten will live and die inside this Band,** and everything the world thinks it knows about magic it knows from watching people who never left it. The Crystal opens, spills, finds direction, and stabilises into an identity.
**The first crisis · Stage V.** Splintering. The Crystal fractures along emotional lines after surviving contact with something more than two full Tiers above its ceiling. Not defeating it. Surviving it.
**Consolidation · Stages VI to VII.** Glory and Refraction. The fractures become features, the aura starts generating environmental effects, and then the soul turns and looks at itself from outside. **Refraction is where a practitioner first reads their own fracture record as an object of study.**
**Outer Influence · Stages VIII to X.** Transcendence, Invocation, Realization. The first true Domain forms from a wound consciously made load-bearing. Then the practitioner stops working alone. Then the Continuum stops resisting and starts accommodating, and **the direction of that accommodation never reverses back.**
**Mythic and Absolute · Stages XI to XIV.** Dissonance, Emanation, Principality, Zenith. The chosen approach to annihilation, what radiates from a soul that came back, formal registration of a law, and finally recognition by a Hollow Throne. **Zenith is not a breakthrough. It is conferred.**
**Beyond the canon · Stages XV to XVI.** Revelation and Apex. Archon-participant territory and then a perpetual state with no ceiling beneath Apotheosis.

## III · What climbs, layer by layer

*The ladder is usually read as one number going up. It is three architectures changing at different rates, and they do not change together.*
| Band | Essence Core | Aether Shell | Attraction Layer |
|---|---|---|---|
| **F · I–IV** | Identity assembling. Traits present but volatile, with real risk of misfire and self-harm. | Forming and porous. Output arrives before control does. | First harmonisation. First Spirit Axis at Flourishing. |
| **E–D · V–VI** | Fractured along emotional lines, then processing those fractures into features. | Self-conducting. Signature becomes permanent. | Aura begins generating environmental effect. |
| **C–B · VII–VIII** | Visible to itself. The fracture record becomes readable. | Traits externalise and interface cleanly with Wellspring dialect. | Domain seed, then first true Domain. |
| **A–S · IX–XII** | Denser after Dissonance than clean advancement can produce. | Traits reach field-state and stop needing maintenance. | Domain to Realm to ambient consequence. Passive miracles leak. |
| **SS–SSS · XIII–XIV** | The Crystal self-writes. | Trait and Domain Law fuse. The Trait stops being an ability. | A law with standing independent of the practitioner. |

> **The architectural note on Dissonance.** Souls that pass *through* Stage XI rather than around it arrive at Emanation fractured and denser than the clean path produces. **Overflow, Detonation and Nullity reach their true ceiling in no other way.** The fractures are not damage. Anyone who tells an ambitious practitioner otherwise is either being kind or has an interest in them not trying.

## IV · Essence Drafts

*Aether + Essence + Vessel + Glyphica = Reaction. A Draft is a working stabilised into a substance, and the substance came from somewhere.*
| Type | Source | Potency | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Creature-Sourced** | Magical creatures | Highest | **Identity Contamination** |
| **Material-Sourced** | High-density minerals | Moderate | Clean but weaker |
| **Environmental** | Affinity-saturated immersion | Low per unit time | Near zero |
| **Synthetic** | Alchemically blended | Variable and unique | **Highest. Internal reaction.** |

**Where the material comes from.** Every substance in the ledger carries a Resonance, a Family and a Physics Domain alongside its Bearing and Holding grades. A dragon's scale is not a generic reagent. It carries a specific Wellspring resonance, it renders at a specific grade, and **that grade begins falling from the moment the animal stops.** The whole beast trade is an argument with decomposition, which is why the northern runners are the profitable ones and why some rendering houses are sited beside a glacier for no other reason.
**Identity Contamination** is the risk that makes Creature-Sourced Drafts both the most potent and the least used by anyone with a career to protect. The Essence taken carries the identity it came from, and a Core that absorbs enough of it stops being able to say cleanly which convictions are its own. **The failure mode is not death. It is a practitioner whose Fidelity has quietly stopped matching their Coherence, and who is the last to notice.**

## V · The ruling that governs all of it

> **A Draft can supply the quantitative floor. A Draft has never supplied a Catalyst.**
>
> This is canon and it is the load-bearing sentence in the whole relationship between alchemy and advancement. Stats pushed above their sustainable grade by alchemical augmentation, bloodline exploitation or external metaphysical imposition **generate Crystal Fracture Events at an exponentially increasing rate until the stat collapses or the Threshold is passed through crisis.**
So a Draft does exactly one honest thing, one dishonest one, and one that the Registers have only recently been willing to write down.
**The honest use.** A practitioner who has met their Catalyst but sits below the floor can be brought to the floor. This is legitimate, licensed, expensive, and the backbone of the Accord's entire pharmaceutical arm. It shortens the wait. It does not skip anything.
**The dishonest use.** A practitioner who has *not* met their Catalyst can be pushed above their sustainable grade anyway. The Crystal will hold the numbers for a while. Then it begins fracturing, and the fracture rate compounds, and it ends one of two ways: the stat collapses, or the Threshold is forced through crisis and the practitioner arrives at the next Stage having been reorganised around whatever was in the Crystal at that moment. **Whatever is in the Crystal at the moment of reorganisation is built into what comes out.** Somebody who forced Splintering while afraid is a permanently different practitioner from somebody who met it having decided something.
**What this means for the world.** The grey trade in advancement compounds is enormous, entirely predictable, and structurally unfixable, because the thing it sells is real. It works. It just does not do the half people are buying it for, and by the time that is apparent the buyer has already been reorganised.

## V-a · Why the moment of reorganisation decides everything

*The mechanism under the sentence above, stated in the vocabulary of the forge rather than the vocabulary of doctrine.*
A Threshold is a melt. The Crystal is carried to the point where its lattice stops holding its shape, and it is allowed to cool into a new one. A melt does not cool into order on its own. Something has to survive the heat for the new lattice to grow around, and in a Soul Crystal that something is **Traits**.
A Trait is a law the Continuum has already accepted as reliable, and a law does not stop being reliable at temperature. This is the only property in the Crystal with that behaviour, and it is why Traits decide the quality of an advancement that strain merely made possible. **Strain gets a soul to the melt. Nucleation decides what the melt produces.**
The consequences follow without further argument.
**A seeded melt** cools dense, deliberate and clean. Clarity and Compression come out ahead of where they went in. This is the clean seat.
**A contradicted melt** cools around seeds that disagree with each other, and the lattice records the disagreement permanently. This is the rough seat, and it is why rough-seated practitioners are unpredictable rather than weak.
**An unseeded melt** cools amorphous. The Stage does not advance, Clarity and Compression fall, and the weakest Trait commonly dissolves without reforming. The halls call this a **Dead Set**, a seat that never took. Everyone else calls it **slag**. Recovery is slow, partial, and the practitioner is usually the last person in the hall to accept that it happened.
> **This is what a Draft is actually doing.** A Draft supplies an artificial seed. It gives the cooling lattice a law to grow around that the soul did not survive into existence, which is why a Draft genuinely improves the quality of an advancement and why the improvement is not the practitioner's own. In Creature-Sourced Drafts the borrowed law arrives with the identity it was rendered from, and that is Identity Contamination stated as a structural mechanism rather than a warning.
>
> **A Draft can supply the floor and it can supply a seed. It has never supplied a Catalyst.** All three statements are needed and the third still governs.

## VI · Tempering, and what a Draft actually touches

*Tempering is the stat of becoming. A high-Tempering practitioner does not necessarily hold more power — they hold a cleaner, deeper, structurally sounder container for whatever they accumulate.*
The Sub-Stats a Draft plausibly reaches: **Compression** (refinement of Essence already held), **Yield** (volume produced per Stage), **Conversion** (ambient Aether absorbed rather than passed through), **Latency** (the gap between generation and availability).
The Sub-Stats no Draft has ever reached: **Coherence**, the alignment between a practitioner's chosen Path and their lived behaviour, which is the structural prerequisite for Threshold eligibility, and **Maturity**, the degree to which prior trauma has been genuinely integrated, which is the primary Sub-Stat the Crystal consults when evaluating readiness.
> **Coherence is the Crystal's internal honesty meter, and it cannot be drunk.** That is the entire mechanical reason the second key exists, stated in the vocabulary of the stat framework rather than in the vocabulary of doctrine.

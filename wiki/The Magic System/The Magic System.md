# The Magic System

At the midsummer fair of Hallingsgate, in front of four thousand people and the entire fish guild, Gudrun Halfdane stepped into the Accord's brass coil to prove what the minstrels had been singing about her for three years. They called her Gudrun Infinite. She stood six and a half feet in a coat of red and gold, she had a move called INFINITE FIST and a purse with every coin she owned on it riding at the bookies' tables, and she had told the whole of the Fishgate that the coil would read Grade S. The clerk, a small man with a clear voice, read out Stage III and a Flux of D.

The fair howled, the fish guild loudest of all. Bookies ran for the boats. Gudrun looked at the coil for a while, and said something the clerk declined to enter, and hit it.

It rang for two days. Dogs left town. Her hand broke in three places, and the clerk, when he had climbed out from under his table, read her again, because procedure requires it, and the reading was the same.

She went north to the wars. Eleven years later she came back with one arm and no red coat, stood in the same coil at the same fair in front of a crowd who had come specifically to laugh, and it read Stage VII. She said nothing to anyone. She walked out along the Hallingsgate breakwater, set her feet, turned her one fist on the gray-green sea and called the name, the same stupid name, in a voice you could have stood a spoon in. There is a gap in the breakwater sixty feet wide. The harbor still charges her heirs for it, and no child in Hallingsgate has ever laughed at the word Infinite, though they have all tried it on each other.

Everything in this section is in that story. She could not argue with the number, and it went up when she had survived something, and it cost her an arm. The Measurewrights will tell you it was the Stage that made the name true. Her family says the name came first, and I have stopped having this conversation at weddings. The heir of the fish guild, who laughed loudest that first midsummer, read Stage VII himself by the age of thirty, at the cost of most of his father's fleet in Drafts, and has never put a hole in anything. The harbor does not charge him. He comes to the fair each year and stands a long while looking at that gap.

> The Continuum grants no power. It measures a soul, reports what the measurement found to the soul's owner, and permits exactly as much as the architecture will bear. Everything in this section is an account of that measurement: what it reads, what it refuses to read, and what it costs to move the reading.
>
> *"You call Temperance a buff. It is a confession of how much you have survived."*
>
> **Where the numbers live.** Everything on this page is the account. The arithmetic underneath it is **Fracture of Worlds — The Living System**, filed at the foot of this section in twenty-two Parts across eight pages. Bands and Temperance Gates, the Tier Grade thresholds, the Sub-Stat ceiling per Stage, all sixty-four Path gates, Threshold Catalysts, Resonant Pairs, Strike Force and Durability benchmarks, Aether Class efficiency, the η table, Domain costs.
>
> Read it when a value is in question, not before. This page tells you what the system is. Fracture of Worlds tells you what the number has to be.

## The Six Things That Carry the System

Everything after these is detail.

| Number | The rule |
|---|---|
| **One** | Reality has three planes. Matter, soul, and fate. Every act of magic crosses all three at once. Remove one and the working fails, without exception and without appeal. |
| **Two** | Aether is the ocean. Essence is your water. Aether is infinite and belongs to no one. You pull it from the world around you, refine it in your Crystal into Essence, stamp it with your identity, and drive it out through your Shell, and there is only ever as much of it as you have earned or paid for. Rich ground fills you faster; thin ground starves you. A Wellspring represents an alchemical and a spiritual transformation and concept. You can see it as a source of power to pull from, and it is more the governing idea that transforms how your soul uses Aether: a lens on magic already potent, which can highlight and in time double your natural Essence Typology. There are sixty, and the scholars explain them as sixty types of Aether with one law of physics apiece. |
| **Three** | You cast through a Soul Crystal, and the Soul Crystal is your soul, literally. A wand, a word and a will are decoration. The Crystal has three layers, one per plane, and its architecture decides what you are capable of long before your ambition gets a vote. |
| **Four** | Power is refined. Nothing accumulates, because Temperance is subtraction. Sixteen Stages, each one a structural reorganization of the Crystal: Tempering melts it down and rebuilds each layer, refines your Essence and makes it stronger, and the body gains Traits. Training gets you to the door, and a Catalyst opens it. A crisis, a seclusion or a tribulation can supply one, and so can a Draft or a blessing, so Stages can simply be bought and the wealthy are stronger because they are wealthy. A bought Stage opens the door and no more: a Release must still be found and mastered by fighting. |
| **Five** | Everything costs. Essence is spent, and nobody has ever summoned any. Domains bleed. Traits fatigue. Overchannel scars. Drafts cost money, and the best of them cost someone else's life. A practitioner who tells you a technique is free is either lying or has not yet paid the invoice. |
| **Six** | The system does not lie and it does not forgive. Your numbers are the Continuum's honest assessment of the soul carrying them, and your soul reports them to you in windows that no one else can see. They can be improved. They cannot be argued with. The one line on the sheet that can lie is a blessing's, because the giver wrote it. |

## The Four Axes

One number has never described a practitioner. Four independent measurements run alongside each other, and the gaps between them are as diagnostic as the values themselves.
![[Assets/Diagrams/four-axes.svg]]
> Temperance sets the **ceiling**. Tier Grade reports the **magnitude**. Aether Class decides how much of the magnitude actually **arrives**, on its tier, and what the soul has made of your life, in its name. Wellspring harmonization changes what the magnitude **means** without changing the number by a single point: it is a lens, and over years of mastery it can double the effect of your natural Essence Typology while the reading stands where it was.
>
> Two practitioners at identical stat values and identical Stage can produce wildly different results if one is Class III Resonant and the other is Class I Muridic. The stats are the same. The delivery is not.

## The Grammar of a Working

Newcomers ask how to learn a spell. The question is malformed. Workings are built, through a five-part grammar, and every named discipline in the Continuum decomposes into it.

| Element | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Family** | Which of the eight domains of phenomena the working belongs to. Never invented. Only worked within. |
| **Affinity** | The specific phenomenon inside that Family. Fire within Caloria. Gravity within Vectoria. |
| **Method** | How you relate to the phenomenon. Encoded directly in the suffix of every discipline name. |
| **Ability** | The internalized governing system your Crystal has developed. Pyrokinesis, for instance. It does nothing visible on its own. It is the platform. |
| **Technique** | A specific, repeatable application within that platform, with defined inputs, outputs, and limitations. This is what you actually do. |

> **The Family is the WHAT. The Method is the HOW. Where they intersect, you get the named discipline.** *The name itself is built on the Affinity: fire, not Caloria, gives Pyromancy its root.*

### The Nine Methods

| Method | Suffix | Plane | Relationship to the phenomenon |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Manteia** | -mancy | Soul | *Perception. Reading and sensing through the element.* |
| **Kinesia** | -kinesis | Material | *Direct control. Physical manipulation in real time.* |
| **Turgia** | -turgy | Fate | *Ritual and creation. Forging lasting change.* |
| **Corporis** | -kinesis (body) | Material | *Martial integration. The body itself is the conduit.* |
| **Scriptia** | -graphy | Fate | *Inscription. Magic carved permanently into materials.* |
| **Alchemia** | -genesis / -synthesis | Material + Soul | *Alchemy. Substance transformation, Essence Draft creation.* |
| **Harmonia** | -phonia / -chord | Soul + Fate | *Resonance. Frequency-based workings.* |
| **Conjunctia** | (compound roots) | All Three | *Fusion. Simultaneous blending of two or more Families in one practitioner. The alchemical Conjunction is a bench operation, not this Method.* |
| **Synergia** | (named by working) | Fate-dominant | *Cooperative. Multiple practitioners combining workings.* |

## The Eight Primaries

Eight Primary Stats, eight Sub-Stats beneath each, sixty-four values in total. **A Primary is the combined total of its eight Sub-Stats.** Points are spent on the grain, most of them by the life a person leads and a few placed by choice at each Stage; the category is the arithmetic afterward. The Temperance ceiling and the Path gates bind the Sub-Stat, and a Primary's Grade is read off the mean. Two characters at identical Primary totals can be radically different practitioners because their distributions tell different stories, and a specialist can read modest on an assessor's ledger while three Sub-Stats at the ceiling do the actual work.

| Primary | What it measures | Plane |
|---|---|---|
| **Vitality** | The body's capacity to carry Essence without breaking. Fortitude, constitution, tolerance, regeneration. | Material |
| **Ardency** | Raw output. Flux, compression, penetration, overchannel. How hard the working hits and how fast it leaves you. | Material |
| **Gnosis** | Perception and comprehension. Analysis, forecast, retention, cognition. The stat that reads the room before the room moves. | Soul |
| **Dexterity** | Precision and control of the body in motion. Celerity, reflex, economy, evasion, feint. | Material |
| **Harmonics** | Resonance and bond. Attunement, empathy, breadth, synergy, axis. How honestly output reflects the inner state. | Fate |
| **Resilience** | Structural persistence under assault. Integrity, ward, anchoring, persistence, hardening. | Soul |
| **Tempering** | Refinement itself. Coherence, maturity, ceiling, capacity, longevity. The stat that governs how a remaking takes. | Soul |
| **Dominion** | Command over local metaphysical law. Radius, sovereignty, gravity, pressure, throne. The stat of sovereignty. | Fate |

> Two Sub-Stats gate everything else. **Tempering Coherence** is the structural prerequisite for Threshold eligibility, the Crystal's internal plumb line. **Tempering Maturity** is the Sub-Stat the Crystal consults first when evaluating whether you are ready.
>
> A practitioner obsessed with Ardency and negligent of Tempering mounts a very impressive trebuchet on a hay cart, and the first shot settles which of them was the better idea.

### The Paths

Sub-Stats are gated behind Path commitment. A Path is the direction advancement actually took, assessed retroactively and recorded whether the practitioner agrees or not. Nobody picks one at registration.

**Body Path** · the shortest road and the most reliable. Direct multiplicative bonus to Strike Force, Durability, and Lifting Strength, from roughly five percent early to twenty percent at Stage XII and above. The trade is reduced Range and Aura Pressure. Body Path practitioners must close distance to spend their advantage.

**Spirit Path** · perception, inscription, clarity, processing. Gates most of Gnosis and much of Tempering's refinement architecture.

**Attraction Path** · bond, recognition, reach, sovereignty. Gates nearly all of Harmonics and the majority of Dominion.

**Fate Path** · the high gates. Persistence, Continuity, Ceiling, Fate, Throne. Almost nothing on Fate Path unlocks below Stage V, and its terminal Sub-Stats do not open below Stage XIII.
> ***The three-and-four question, settled.*** *The Physical Force chapter names three Paths and the gate tables name four, and both are correct.* **Fate carries no force**, so it takes no line in a table of force modifiers, and its absence there is a fact about force rather than a fact about Paths.
>
> The deeper difference is how commitment is recognized. **Body, Spirit and Attraction are demonstrated by routing Essence.** Fate gates Persistence, Continuity, Ceiling, Stability, Fate and Throne, none of which concern output at all, **so Fate commitment is recognized by what a soul refuses to let end.** No drill produces it and no instructor assigns it.
>
> *Which is why the Measurewrights read it as a late-tier extension. It arrives on time, and it stays invisible until somebody has held something long enough for the holding to count.* **Full treatment in The Four Paths.**

## What Your Soul Shows You

A window is the soul reporting its numbers to its owner, in the owner's own tongue, and no one else can see it. Scholars argue about who built it. It comes in short blocks, fights included, for damage, skill gains and reserve, and it shows Grades, percentages, bars and levels, while EU, AU/s and η stay with the instruments and the notes. A normal look gives the sheet:

```
[Corra Vell · Human · Level 196 · Stage VII, Refraction]
[Class: Yard Breaker · Muridic]
[Vitality A · Ardency A · Gnosis C · Dexterity B]
[Harmonics C · Resilience B · Tempering B · Dominion C]
[Reserve: 100% ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓]
[Trait: Bank the Fire, Level 41]
[Skills: Hammerwork A · Tracking B · Bargaining D]
[Blessing: none]
```

**The sheet.** Race and lineage show on it and shape base stats and what comes easily. The Eight Primaries show with Grades, and a Sub-Stat appears when it is used or when it moves; the full set remains to study.

**Skills** rise with use and show as Grades. Drafts, runes, tags and chants are skills and trades like any other. **Traits** stay as filed in their Four Reflections and their laws, and each one carries a level that rises with use and grows with overall Level and skill. **Techniques** are named things done with a skill. They show no level and take a bigger name when they jump.

**Aether Class.** At thresholds the soul is offered a named class fitting how the person has lived. It gives abilities, shapes growth and evolves at each tier by how it is lived. The ladder on file (Ø Dormant, I Muridic, II Harmonic, III Resonant, IV Luminous, V Radiant, VI Voidic, Ω Absolute) stays as its tier, and all its η law stands. The one wild concept stays the person's own.

**Level** moves by danger overcome. Killing something stronger moves it more than a hundred weak kills, and crews argue over who got the kill.

**Reading someone else.** A stronger Gnosis Perception reads a weaker Resilience Ward, and everything else is an estimate said aloud, which people can hide or fake.

## What Money Buys

The Catalyst was for a long time the Accord's favorite argument that the ladder was fair. Drafts and blessings can supply one, and everybody with a vault has always known it. Stages can be bought, the great houses hoard the top Drafts, and a coil at a noble's wedding reads like a ledger of the family's holdings. The poor rise by dungeon luck, guild patronage and blessings, and the great houses lose more sleep over one commoner with a monster core than over any rival.

Money stops at the door it opens. Sects teach what it cannot buy: Releases, Domains, techniques, skills and the rites for their Wellspring. A rich Stage IX may have no Release at all while a poor fighter at the same Stage has all three. Anyone at Stage VIII can have a Domain, their own concept made real, once the Second Release that opens it has been found. The two-Grade boost cap stays, and only outside help goes past it.

A monster's core is in a way its Soul Crystal, worth more the stronger the monster, and an alchemist renders it into the Essence Drafts used for tempering. Swallowed raw it gives everything at once, and most who try die or come out wrong. A Crystal cut from a dead practitioner makes the strongest Draft of all, the worst crime in every realm and the most profitable. Monsters themselves level by killing, evolve and share the Level and Grade scale, and dungeons grow down in floors around a Core, alive and learning, with something guarding the heart. The Bestiary has the rest.

## Fate and Blessings

The Plane of Fate is the control of Attraction Force and Obsession Force and the workings of reality itself: manipulating probabilities to work outside what reality already knows, to control the fate or predestiny of something. All spirits and all physical things have fates. It can attach to oaths and is more tied to blessings. Fate Arts are rare, mostly by bloodline, reached late by some high-Stage masters along the Fate Path, and considered incredibly overpowered. Hataraki can take back or remove a blessing, which is a good part of why that bloodline is feared.

A blessing grants a power out of a person's natural reach, even in Level. It need not go through Fate, but most do. Higher spirits, Archons, Epochs and people of higher Temperance give them, because they are spiritually stronger, and a great giver spends almost nothing. Epochs and Archons act rarely, and it shakes the world. A blessing is listed on the sheet as its own line and can lie, because the giver wrote the line. A curse is often a blessing with terms you did not read.

## What Corruption Looks Like, By Family

Every Family has a terminal failure state. The early symptoms are indistinguishable from enthusiasm.

| Family | Corruption | Terminal expression |
|---|---|---|
| **Caloria** | Pyromaniacal fixation | Internal heat exceeds survival |
| **Fulguria** | Sensory overload | Cognitive dissolution |
| **Vectoria** | Gravitational isolation | The soul becomes a singularity |
| **Spatium** | Spatial dissociation | The body sits at ambiguous coordinates |
| **Materia** | Petrification compulsion | Flesh mineralizes |
| **Vitalia** | Biological recursion | Growth and decay lock into a loop |
| **Fluxia** | Dissolution of boundaries | Identity disperses |
| **Limina** | Existential erosion | Solipsism, negation, or dissociation |

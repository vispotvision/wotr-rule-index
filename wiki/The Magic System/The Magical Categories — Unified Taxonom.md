# The Magical Categories — Unified Taxonom

---

## How to read this taxonomy

Every category carries an alignment tag — **Body**, **Spirit**, **Attraction**, **Triadic**, or a combination. These map onto the three Planes and three expressive Disciplines, restated as *practice* rather than *structure*:
- **Body-Aligned** leans on the **Physical Plane** and **Vitae**. Essence as impact, motion, form.
- **Spirit-Aligned** leans on the **Soul Plane** and **Intent**. Essence as precision, memory, shaped will.
- **Attraction-Aligned** leans on the **Plane of Fate** and **Resonance**. Essence as reach, recognition, binding.
- **Triadic** categories don't lean. They're what happens when all three Disciplines, anchored by **Equilibrium**, operate as one movement — the practice-form of **Formation**.
- **Combination** tags mark categories operating at the seam between two Planes, neither fully arrived nor fully departed.
A practitioner's primary Path determines which categories feel *native* versus *learned*. A Body Path soul can practice Mnemata, but it will always feel like a second language.

---

## 1 · ARTS

**Body-Aligned**
Structured, discipline-based styles blending movement, form, and Essence flow — controlled Flux, martial shaping, embodied Aether expression. **Vitae** given technique. Arts are often a practitioner's *first* structured magic regardless of eventual Path, since the Essence Core's truth and the Aether Shell's expression already speak the same language here.

## 2 · DRAFTS

**Examples:** Spirit Forge Arts · Invocation Arts · Binding Arts · Resonant Forms

---

**Spirit-Aligned**
Alchemical procedures channelling Wellspring processes through Parun Glyphs. Drafts are formulaic: **Aether + Essence + Vessel + Glyphica = Reaction** — the Alchemy codex's central equation as its own category.
Though Drafts touch all three Soul Crystal layers, they're classed Spirit-aligned because the decisive variable is how cleanly the Essence Core's intention survives the journey into the crucible.
**Examples:** Coagulation · Conjunction Drafts · Distillation · Sublimation

---

## 3 · CONJUNCTION

**Attraction-Aligned**
Fusion workings merging Essences, Traits, Domains, or Wellspring processes into hybrids. Operates on **Attraction Force** and requires strict compatibility — the Conjunction operation from Alchemy and the Codex of Absolute Magical Law, here as its own category.
Attraction-aligned because compatibility is fundamentally about *recognition*: two Houses, Traits, or Domains either recognise each other as connectable, or they don't, and no Vitae or Intent substitutes. Opposed Houses require mediation glyphs because the Plane of Fate is being asked to hold a connection it didn't offer freely.
**Examples:** Apotheosis Fusions · Absolution Weaves · Echofold Constructs

---

## 4 · SYNERGIA

**Triadic**
Emergent resonance from multiple souls, Wellsprings, or Domains interacting, birthing shared Temperance fields and collective intent. **Echo Bond** scaled from two Crystals to many.
Triadic because no single Discipline governs it. A Synergia field is several Soul Crystals briefly sharing one Essence Core, Shell, and Layer, which is why Aqua-Typology souls anchor these fields most easily.
**Examples:** Affinity Fields · Aversion Nets · Severance Choirs · Magnum Opus Works

---

## 5 · HARMONIA

**Spirit ↔ Attraction**
Magic of tone, vibration, and resonant frequency. Psions, choirs, and empathic casters use Harmonia to tune or destabilise Wellsprings, Traits, and Domains — the practice-form of **Resonance Integrity**.
Sits between Spirit and Attraction because tone originates in the Essence Core (Spirit, what the soul *means*) but only becomes Harmonia once it travels outward to affect other Crystals (Attraction, what the soul *reaches*). Mismatched Resonant Tone produces Echo Fracture.
**Examples:** Veilsong · Lunar Chorus · Frequency Collapse

---

## 6 · RITUS

**Spirit-Aligned**
Formal rituals built on Wellspring harmonics, Realm laws, and Parunic syntax. A typical sequence: **Intention → Space → Circle → Offering → Invocation → Catalyst → Seal** — a granular unfolding of Ritual Integration's four phases.
Spirit-aligned because, unlike Drafts, its anchors are temporary and ceremonial. A Ritus is performed, not built. The Essence Core's posture during the rite matters more than any glyph's placement.
Ritus is also **the ancestor of every field-capable discipline** that later internalised what the circle used to externalise.
**Examples:** Rite of Hollow Union · Pale Root Binding · Choir of Severance

---

## 7 · PHENOMENA

**Attraction-Dominant**
Large-scale anomalies where Aether law bends under Resonance overload. Semi-autonomous, self-sustaining events marking major law stress — the **Singularity** of Statute XIV.
Attraction-dominant because once triggered, Phenomena are no longer *cast*; they're lattice-position events on the Plane of Fate that have outrun any Attraction Layer. This is why Counter-Phenomena are so dangerous: the trigger has an author, but the Phenomenon itself does not.
**Examples:** Echo Collapse · Worldbirth · Aetherquake · Memory Storms

---

## 8 · GLYPHICA

**Spirit-Aligned**
The Parun-based language of law — the syntax by which the Continuum recognises and authorises transformation. Explicit (sigils, scripts) or implicit (internalised chains). Governs the **Four Glyph Roles**: Authorization, Boundary, Direction, Sealing.
Spirit-aligned because it's the Essence Core's intention made *legible*. A practitioner with sloppy internalised chains doesn't fail to cast; they cast something slightly different from intended — a quiet, accumulating error.
**Examples:** Wordform Chains · Sigilcraft · Parunic Script Weaving

---

## 9 · MANIFESTUS

**Body ↔ Spirit**
Short-lived Wellspring emissions and flares triggered by high emotional density or sudden pressure, reflecting current Temperance and Essence posture. An involuntary Trait preview — **Overtone Bloom** given external form.
Sits between Body and Spirit because the trigger is always Spirit (emotional/Essence spike) but the expression is always Body (a flare that briefly exists physically). Early-Temperance souls experience this far more; it's what Trait Awakening *looks like from outside*.
**Examples:** Apotheotic Bloom · Severance Pulse · Benediction Emission

---

## 10 · VIAFORMA

**Triadic Axis**
The lived evolution along the four Paths: Body, Spirit, Attraction and Fate. Viaforma is how a soul's Aether behaviour reshapes over time through Temperance — the practice-name for the Four Paths codex's structural claim: a soul's primary allegiance to one Plane, refined across the entire Coherence Ladder.
Not a technique cast, but the shape of everything else in this taxonomy across a lifetime. **Via Corporis**, **Via Mentis**, **Via Anima** and **Via Fati** are the four long arcs every other category eventually serves. **Via Fati** routes no force at all; it is recognised by what a soul refuses to let end.
**Examples:** Via Corporis (Body) · Via Mentis (Spirit) · Via Anima (Attraction) · Via Fati (Fate)

---

## 11 · TRAITUS

**Body-Aligned**
Standing laws carried in the soul's own material and expressed through the Crystal once there is a Crystal to express them: Primary, Secondary, Inherited, Passive and Compound Reflections.
**Traits are not encoded by Temperance.** They are woven into a soul's original Essence, they run in Class Ø bearers whose Crystal will never open, and awakening arms what was already there rather than granting anything new. What Temperance does is the reverse of encoding. **At a Threshold the Crystal melts, and Traits are the only structures that survive the heat, because a law the Continuum has accepted as reliable does not stop being reliable at temperature.** The new lattice cools around them. This is why Traitus governs advancement rather than merely decorating it.
Classed Body-aligned because a Trait's most legible expression is almost always physical, and the expression is literal rather than figurative. A Trait biases the Aether Shell's conductivity permanently, and because the Shell is the Material-Plane layer the bias is laid down in tissue: mineral into bone, altered myelination, rebuilt vasculature, a resting core temperature off the population mean. **A body is a Trait record and a corpse is a better one.**
**Examples:** Mirror Pulse · Iron Veil · Fractured Sight · Silent Step

---

## 12 · MECHANICA

**Attraction-Corrupted**
Forbidden constructs bypassing Soul Crystals, Wellsprings, or Glyphic Syntax. Mechanica coerces transformation without lawful Wellspring participation, consuming Resonance and damaging the Continuum — the practice this entire taxonomy exists in *opposition* to.
Tagged Attraction-**corrupted** deliberately. Every other category involves recognition — of Wellsprings, Domains, other souls. Mechanica is what remains when recognition is removed and replaced with extraction: Conjunction's compatibility deleted, Glyphica's authorization skipped, Drafts performed without consent. Statute XXVI, **Mechanica Irreversibility**, exists because this is the one category that leaves scars the Continuum doesn't forget.
**Examples:** Soul Splicing · Aether Inversion · Trait Collapse Engines

---

## 13 · CRYSTALIA

**Body ↔ Spirit**
The engineering, forging, and refinement of Soul Crystals and related lattices, underpinning Flux Density, Aether containment, and the Coherence Ladder. The practiced discipline behind the **Crystalia Constraint**: harmonisation with three relevant Wellsprings, certified understanding of the subject's Essence Typology, sufficient Temperance to survive exposure to another soul's Essence Core.
Sits between Body and Spirit because its *object* is the Essence Core but its *method* is physical — forging and tempering done with hands and instruments on a lattice that happens to also be a soul.
The discipline's hard limit is that **nucleation cannot be manufactured.** A Crystalia practitioner can widen conductivity, repair a fracture, and prepare a lattice to receive a melt cleanly. They cannot supply a seed, because a seed is a law the subject survived into existence and no instrument makes one. Everything the trade sells past that point is a **Draft under another name**, and the Accord's certification distinguishes the two on paper and nowhere else.
**Examples:** Fractal Alignment · Core Rebinding · Tempered Latticework

---

## 14 · WELLSPRINGS

**Triadic System**
The Sixty Wellsprings, cosmic dialects of transformation sorted into the **eight Families** by Physics Domain: Caloria, Fulguria, Vectoria, Spatium, Materia, Vitalia, Fluxia, Limina. Temperance governs how much of each process a soul can bear; Wellsprings define what law and emotional grammar are available.
*The seven Houses of the older registers, Coagulation, Ascent, the Forge, the Green Pulse, the Black Gate, the Veil and the Hollow, sorted by feeling rather than by law and do not map onto the Families. They remain in the Wellspring codices and the Veil literature and are read as historical.*
Triadic not because any single Wellspring touches all three Planes equally, but because the *system as a whole* is the shared vocabulary every other category draws from. Arts invoke them through Vitae, Drafts through Glyphica, Vocatia through recognition. **The Sixty are the nouns; everything else is a way of using them as verbs.**
**Examples:** Benediction · Fixatio · Ascensio · Oneirion · Abython · Verdantia · Nihiloth

---

## 15 · ANIMATRIA

**Spirit-Aligned**
Psionic shaping and projection of consciousness into constructs, fields, or avatars — turning thought and identity into structured presence. **Intent** given enough Resonance to operate at a distance from the body that generated it.
Spirit-aligned because an Animatria construct is a small detached fragment of the caster's Essence Core given temporary independent operation, closer to a deliberately-shaped Essence Shard than a Domain. This is why Animatria fails completely in Silence Realms: the construct's continuity depends on a connection back to its source, which Harmonic Silence severs.
**Examples:** Eidolon Crafting · Spiritchain · Animatria Reflex

---

## 16 · VOCATIA

**Attraction-Aligned**
Invocation by recognition and correspondence: Titans, Archons, World Spirits, lesser entities, Realms. Vocatia is not domination — it's the negotiation of shared resonance, the practice-form of **Invocation and Consent**.
The category most directly governed by the Plane of Fate's lawful currents. A Vocatia working addressed to an unwilling higher being is, at the level the Continuum reads intention, an attempted Usurpation, regardless of correct surface syntax. Successful Vocatia is always an act of the **Current of Recognition**, never Refusal.
**Examples:** Titan Echo · Archon Descent · Concord Summons

---

## 17 · VECTRA

**Body ↔ Attraction**
Magic of motion, direction, velocity, and spatial gravitation. Essential to Ascensio, kinetic combat, and high-speed Domain work — **Vitae's** impact given **Resonance's** reach.
Sits between Body and Attraction because it's fundamentally about *where force goes*: a Body question answered using Attraction tools (the Plane of Fate's connections between non-adjacent points). Gravity Folding and Spatial Collapse are small, controlled Spirit Axes claimed and released within a single motion.
**Examples:** Gravity Folding · Aetherstep · Spatial Collapse

---

## 18 · MNEMATA

**Spirit ↔ Attraction**
Memorycraft and echo manipulation, governing dream records, ancestral fields, temporal imprints, and historical echo-law. The practiced discipline behind **Lattice Memory** and **Crystalline Memory**, extended outward to other souls' records.
Sits between Spirit and Attraction because memory itself is Soul Plane, but its *transmission* between souls, generations, or into Domains is Attraction Force at work. The House of the Veil's Wellsprings (Anamnesis, Phreatis, Somnalis) are its most natural partners.
**Examples:** Dreamfolding · Memory Sigils · Echo Archives

---

## 19 · AETHERICA

**Triadic Convergence**
The quantitative study of Aether behaviour: Flux, Aether Units, Efficiency, Density, Coherence. Aetherica links Crystalia, the Coherence Ladder, and the Scale System into one analytic frame — the *measurement* discipline underlying nearly every numeric value in this Continuum.
Triadic Convergence because it's least concerned with *doing* magic and most with *describing* it. A practitioner of Aetherica might never cast in their life, instead ensuring everyone else's workings are measured against the same Ladder.
**Examples:** Flux Stabilisation · Aether Mapping · Continuum Gaugework

---

## 20 · CONCORDIA

**Spirit ↔ Attraction Apex**
The apex harmonic art of aligning multiple Aether bodies, Wellsprings, and Domains into a single coherent field. Underlies Wellspring restoration, large-scale Realm works, and high-order summoning — Synergia and Harmonia scaled up and made *durable* rather than momentary.
The Spirit-Attraction Apex because it requires both: Spirit-side precision to align many Essence Cores without flattening any, and Attraction-side reach to hold that alignment as a lasting Domain rather than a passing chord. A successful Concordia working is functionally a Major Shared Realm's founding moment, performed deliberately, and the category most often invoked to heal a damaged Wellspring vein or begin closing a Hollowing.
**Examples:** Guild Choirs · Concord Domains · Nexus Formation

---

## 21 · DOMAIN WEAVING

**Attraction-Aligned** 
Deliberate reshaping of local Continuum behaviour so that a chosen law becomes easier to sustain within a defined space. The practitioner does not enforce the law directly; they alter what the region is willing to do cheaply.
Attraction-aligned because a Domain is a standing negotiation with the Plane of Fate rather than an application of force. It persists exactly as long as the Attraction Layer keeps recognising the claim, which is why a Domain fails all at once rather than fraying.
Distinct from **Concordia**, which aligns many existing fields into one; Domain Weaving builds a single field from a single soul's claim.
**Examples:** Aspect Sovereignty · Standing Claim · Law-Bias Fields

---

## 22 · CLAUSTRA

**Spirit ↔ Attraction** 
The binding, sealing, and lawful imprisonment of a thing against its own nature — souls, entities, Wellspring processes, Phenomena mid-event, and other practitioners' workings. Where Glyphica supplies the Sealing role as one of four, Claustra is the discipline of making that role carry the whole weight of a working indefinitely.
Sits between Spirit and Attraction because the seal's *syntax* is Soul Plane precision while its *duration* is a Fate-side claim that must be renewed by something. A Claustra working that loses its anchor does not weaken. It opens.
The category the Binding Arts have been practising without a name, and the home of the **Vow Clause**. A vow is a seal a practitioner sets on themselves, and it is the only Claustra working whose anchor is the bearer's own continued behaviour.
**Examples:** Dragon Binding · Null-Anchors · The Veil's standing closures · Oath-Seals

---

## 23 · SILENTIA

**Attraction-Negative** 
The removal of magical possibility from a region, a body, or a working. Not counterspelling and not shielding — the deliberate production of an absence in which lawful transformation cannot be authorised at all.
Tagged **Attraction-negative** on the same logic that makes Mechanica *Attraction-corrupted*. Mechanica removes recognition and substitutes extraction. Silentia removes recognition and substitutes nothing, which is why Silence Realms sever Animatria constructs from their source and why a soul held inside one reports the sensation as loneliness rather than weakness.
This is the counterplay category. Every other entry in the taxonomy describes something being done; Silentia describes the conditions under which none of them work.
**Examples:** Harmonic Silence · Null Zones · Aether Suppression · the Hollow's quieter effects

---

## 24 · AUGURIA

**Attraction ↔ Spirit** 
The reading of outcome weight, causal load, and probability distribution — what is leaning, not what will happen. Mnemata reads backward into what a thing was; Auguria reads sideways into what a thing is being pulled toward.
Attraction-forward because outcome weight is a Fate-side property and the practitioner is asking the Plane of Fate a question rather than the Soul Plane. Spirit-side only insofar as the answer must be received by a Core capable of holding it without resolving it into narrative, which is the failure mode: an Auguria reading that arrives already interpreted has been interpreted by the reader.
**Examples:** Outcome Reading · Tide-Sense · the crow's sight (Yukari) · water-scrying practices with no register entry

---

## 25 · TRANSITUS

**Triadic** 
The crossing of a body, soul, or object between Planes, and the making and holding of the apertures through which crossing occurs. Vectra moves a thing to somewhere else in the world. Transitus moves it out of the world it was in.
Triadic because a crossing requires all three at once and fails if any is missing: a Body that survives arrival, an Intent that stays coherent through the transition, and an Attraction claim on the far side that has agreed to receive it. Uninvited crossings resolve as the receiving Plane sees fit.
The category that has been doing silent work under the Veil, the Crevice, the Gates, and every unplanned arrival in the Plane of Fate.
**Examples:** Transference Crossing · Gate-Work · Veil-Thinning · involuntary Fate-draw

---

## 26 · VOTIA

**Spirit-Aligned** 
Workings whose operative currency is a declared and irrevocable price: an offering made before the effect, a restriction accepted permanently, a thing given up so that a law will answer more readily than the practitioner's Temperance should permit.
Spirit-aligned because what is being altered is the Essence Core's posture rather than its output. A Votia working does not make a practitioner stronger. It makes them narrower, and the Continuum answers narrowness.
**The Vow Clause, stated mechanically.** A vow declares a permanent restriction and the Core adopts it as a standing law. **Tempering Coherence measures the alignment between a practitioner's stated law and their lived behaviour**, and it is the structural prerequisite for Threshold eligibility. A vow kept is therefore a direct purchase of Coherence by conduct rather than by expenditure, which is why vows cluster in exactly the populations that cannot buy Drafts: soldiers, hedge-workers, the orders, the poor.
> *It is the cheapest advancement mechanism in the world and it is priced in the rest of your life.*
**A vow broken is a Shear Break.** The Core has been running a law the bearer has now denied, and the law does not negotiate its own withdrawal. This is why the orders treat a lapsed vow as a medical emergency rather than a disciplinary one, and why the disciplinary response follows only if the practitioner survives.
**Examples:** Gisei · Sacrament and Oblation · Disclosure practices · the Vow Clause

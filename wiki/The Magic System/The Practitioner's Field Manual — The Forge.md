# The Practitioner's Field Manual — The Forge

*Being a working register of the Eight Families, and the Forge by which Abilities and Techniques are built at speed*
> **Craft Companion, the Seventh Guide**
> ***Governs how magic is written. Does not govern what magic is.***
> *"The question was never how much power he has. It was what he is permitted to do with it, and what it takes out of him when he does."*

---

## The Two Layers

**Nothing in the Continuum became more complicated.** *A second layer accumulated on top of the first, the two were carried as one thing — and the second layer is accounting.*
| Layer | What it is |
|---|---|
| **The Generative Layer** | Family → Affinity → Method → Ability → Technique. **The entire engine of magic.** It answers **what a working does, how it does it, and what it costs.** *Every scene is written from here* |
| **The Accounting Layer** | Level, Band, Stage, Grade, Coherence, Aether Class, EU, η. **This settles what a practitioner can actually do and who wins.** It answers **how much.** ***Load it before writing, never abandon it while writing, and put it on the page only through a mouth, an instrument, a document or a private count*** |

> **Both layers stay open. The generative layer decides what happens; the accounting layer decides whether it could have.**
> *Every outcome should trace to a specific row, and the author notes should say which. What the reader receives is behaviour and physics. What the numbers do is make sure the behaviour was earned.*

---

## The Rule for Invention Under Fire

> **Abilities** are character architecture — a root phenomenon the Crystal has internalised over years. *Cozbi is Antithesis. Kroathar is Binding. Temür is Ruin.*
>
> **These are never invented inside a scene.** *If a fight appears to require a new Ability, the fight is wrong — or the scene stops and the Ability is pitched properly.*
>
> **Techniques** are moves — a specific repeatable application within an Ability already established. **These may be invented at speed, mid-scene, without stopping**, provided they are built through the Forge and logged afterward.
>
> ***Invent freely downward, never upward. A new Technique under a known Ability is craft. A new Ability under a known character is a canon decision wearing a technique's coat.***

## The Forge

*Seven steps at the desk. Steps one through four are structural and cannot be skipped. Five through seven are what separate a technique from a special effect.*
| Step | What it demands |
|---|---|
| **1 · Family** | Name it **from what is physically happening, not from the feeling it evokes.** *A grief-working that rots a body is Vitalia. A grief-working that removes the memory of a face is Limina.* **The taxonomy is physics-first** and it will offend intuition roughly a third of the time. Follow it anyway |
| **2 · Verb** | **One** operation from the Family's verb table. *A technique that uses three verbs is three techniques badly stapled together* |
| **3 · Rate** | **Push or shatter.** *This single decision determines what the corpse looks like and is the most underused instrument available* |
| **4 · Limitation** | State what it structurally **cannot** do **before** stating what it does. *A power invented before its constraint is a power looking for a constraint to justify it, and it reads bolted-on every time* |
| **5 · Cost** | From the Wheel, deliberately, checked against the character's existing category. **A cast where everything costs blood is a cast with one idea** |
| **6 · Counterplay** | One sentence, **and it must be an answer a competent opponent could actually find.** *If none exists, the technique is not finished* |
| **7 · Stamp** | Glyph pair, primary Wellspring, Family, Physics Domain. **Thirty seconds. Never left unlinked** |

### The three-line version

*For use mid-scene, at speed, when a named character needs to do something in the next paragraph.*
> **VERB. RATE. PRICE.**
>
> He does **one operation** from his Family, at a **stated speed**, and it takes **something the reader can see** out of him.
>
> *The stamp and the counterplay can be filled in at the end of the pass without damaging the prose. The price cannot be added retroactively — because a cost the reader never watched get exacted did not happen.*
> **The gate the Forge does not list, because it is not a step.** A **Signature Technique** requires a matching Trait, and no amount of Stage, instruction or expenditure substitutes for one. The Trait is a structural prerequisite rather than an advantage: it is the law the working is an application of, and without it there is nothing for the technique to be an application of.
>
> *Which is the test for whether a signature has been invented honestly. Name the Trait it comes out of. If there isn't one, what has been built is a good technique belonging to nobody in particular.*

## The Delivery Rate Law

*Ratified. It generalises across all eight Families as one rule and resolves the recurring problem of every escalation reading as simply larger.*
> **Every working has a delivery rate. Rate decides whether the effect pushes or shatters. Magnitude does not.**
**The same Essence, the same Wellspring, the same practitioner, delivered slow or delivered instantaneously, produce two entirely different injuries.** *This is the correct way to escalate a character without inflating a number — and it is available at every Stage from Murmuring upward.*
| Family | The law | What the difference is |
|---|---|---|
| **Caloria** | Deflagration ↔ detonation | Subsonic **pushes, throws men, takes hair and eyebrows and leaves survivors.** Break the sound barrier and it stops being fire and becomes a pressure wave — *and pressure waves do not burn people, they delaminate them, lungs first. Same fuel. Same Essence. Entirely different corpse* |
| **Fulguria** | Sustained arc ↔ single discharge | A held arc cuts and cauterises and **can be walked away from.** One discharge dumps the differential in a millisecond, **and the body's response is not injury but interruption** — *the heart is an electrical organ and it does not argue* |
| **Vectoria** | Sustained load ↔ impulse | A slow load bends, buckles, collapses — **and everyone inside gets to watch it happen and possibly leave.** As impulse it **exceeds the material's ability to distribute stress and the material fails along its worst line, immediately, everywhere at once** |
| **Spatium** | Gradual displacement ↔ instantaneous fold | Slowly through compressed distance: **nausea and a headache.** Folded instantaneously, **the shear at the boundary becomes the entire event** — *and shear does not care that the thing being sheared is a person* |
| **Materia** | Creep ↔ brittle fracture | Slow loading yields, sags, redistributes, **visibly failing the whole time.** Faster than it can redistribute and **it goes from intact to particulate with nothing in between and no warning to anyone standing under it** |
| **Vitalia** | Chronic ↔ acute | Slow is survivable, treatable, **often invisible for weeks — which makes it the assassin's Family.** Acute overwhelms every compensatory mechanism at once: **the body does not fail gradually, it fails in a cascade that takes four organs in nine minutes** |
| **Fluxia** | Flood ↔ water hammer | At its own pace, **a slow disaster people escape by climbing.** Stop that column instantaneously and its momentum **bursts pipe, stone, and vessel walls** — *and the vessel wall in question is frequently an artery* |
| **Limina** | Erosion ↔ erasure | Erosion is experienced as ordinary decline — forgetfulness, bad luck, a joint that aches. *The difference: erosion leaves a man who knows something is wrong, and erasure leaves a man who does not* |

## The Cost Wheel

*Rotate deliberately across the cast and audit for collision.*
> **The governing test is unchanged and it is severe: if the cost never touches the character when they are not using the power, it is a resource limit wearing a cost's coat.**
| Category | Standing |
|---|---|
| **Physical toll** | Exhaustion, injury, aging, organ damage. **Reliable. Overused** |
| **Resource depletion** | **The weakest category available.** *Only a real cost if scarcity bites outside a fight* |
| **Corruption** | **WOTR's default for anything Wellspring-adjacent — which is precisely why it should not be the default here** |
| **Sanity** | A large lever. **Sparingly** |
| **Memory** | Trading recall for capability. **Underused across the whole cast, and it fits the Anima Spirare register exactly** |
| **Time** | Cast duration, cooldown, or lifespan spent |
| **Social and legal** | Accord sanction, Concord Seal, exile. ***There is an entire enforcement apparatus built and it is doing almost no work*** |
| **Escalating dependency** | The addiction structure. **The best long-arc dread engine available** — *because the reader can see the bill coming and cannot stop it* |
| **Rigidity** | **The newest category and the one nobody else is using.** A technique repeated enough deepens the Trait it comes out of, and **the Rigidity Effect scales with how absolute a law is.** The practitioner does not weaken. He becomes less able to contradict the thing he built his power on, in his life as well as in a fight. *The cost is paid in what he can no longer be talked out of, and it is visible at a table long before it is visible in a duel* |

## The Vow Clause

*Ratified. Lives in Votia; sealed as Claustra.*
**A practitioner may bind a technique under a stated condition, sworn into the Lattice or into their own Crystal. The narrower the condition and the heavier the forfeiture, the greater the output.**
> *"Against those who deserve it" is not a vow. "Only against the Zettari, and if I break it my Crystal shatters" is a vow.*
**Why it works.** The Core adopts the restriction as a standing law, and **Tempering Coherence measures the alignment between a practitioner's stated law and their lived behaviour.** Coherence is the structural prerequisite for Threshold eligibility. A vow kept therefore buys Coherence with conduct instead of expenditure, which makes it the cheapest advancement mechanism in the world and the one that clusters among people who cannot afford Drafts.
**Why it kills.** A vow broken is a **Shear Break**. The Core has been running a law the bearer has now denied, and the law does not negotiate its own withdrawal. The orders treat a lapsed vow as a medical emergency and get to the disciplinary question later, if there is one.
> **The disclosure variant is the useful one.** Stating a technique's true mechanism aloud, in the target's hearing, before use, **surrenders every advantage of surprise and increases output accordingly.**
>
> ***This is the best structural justification for a villain's monologue ever devised and the Continuum should take it.***
**What the clause buys is an answer to power creep. A character becomes dramatically stronger by narrowing rather than escalating.**
> *Cozbi at Stage XIV does not need a larger number. He needs a vow the reader can watch him keep — and his refusal to take one is presently the most interesting unexploited thing about him.*

## The Two Tests Before Presenting

**The Collapse Test** · *If two techniques under one Ability share a Limitation, a Cost, and a Counterplay, they are one technique with two names. Collapse them.*
**The Name Test** · Does the name tell you something true about who is using it? **The phenomenon itself, direct and unadorned.** *Ruin is Ruin. Gravity is Gravity.* **A name that could belong to any practitioner in the Continuum has failed.**

## The Eight Families

*What each House governs, and — more usefully — what it categorically cannot do.*
| Family | What it cannot do | Signature cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Caloria** *heat and its absence* | **Cannot create energy.** Every degree added is a degree taken from somewhere, **and the practitioner must name the source or become it.** Cannot sustain combustion without an oxidiser — *sealed rooms, deep water and the high thin air of the Reach are Caloria-hostile ground* | **Physical toll, sink-side.** *A thirty-year adept has hands that no longer sweat, corneas gone slightly opaque, and a resting body temperature nobody wants to shake hands with* |
| **Fulguria** *the House of the differential* | **Cannot arc across a gap it has not prepared.** Useless against wood, ceramic, dry rope, ungirded stone — *which is why the counter-Fulguria kit is boiled leather and fired clay and always has been.* **Falls off with the square of distance,** so every Fulguria threat is a threat about closing range | **Sanity-adjacent, sensory.** The nervous system is the instrument, **and instruments detune.** *Early signs read as brilliance: he answers before you finish, he notices everything, he has not slept and does not think that is strange* |
| **Vectoria** *force and motion* | **Cannot create momentum.** Every push has an equal push on the practitioner, **and the only question is which of his joints receives it.** *Resonance requires knowing the frequency, and knowing it requires time the fight may not give* | **Physical toll, reaction-side, and it is skeletal.** *Shoulders that come out of socket in cold weather. A spine that has been used as a bracing member for twenty years.* **Corruption is gravitational isolation —** the early symptom is that people stop being able to stand near him comfortably and cannot say why |
| **Spatium** *distance and position* | **Cannot fold to a place it has not measured** — *and the failure state for blind translocation is not arriving somewhere wrong, it is arriving somewhere occupied.* **Cannot move what it cannot fully bound,** so a partial working takes a partial man | **Time and proprioception.** *Long before terminal dissociation, the practitioner reaches for a cup and misses by a hand's width, and stops trusting stairs* |
| **Materia** *matter and substance* | **Cannot make matter. Conservation is absolute in this House and there are no exceptions** — *which is why a Materia practitioner on bare ice or open water is a man with opinions and no tools.* Loses every exchange decided in under two seconds and wins every one that lasts a week | **Escalating dependency, mineral.** Petrification compulsion, terminating in mineralised flesh. *It shows first in the nail beds and the whites of the eyes — the only corruption in the manual a physician can diagnose by looking* |
| **Vitalia** *biology and anatomy* | **Healing eats mass, and the mass comes from the patient, the caster, or something the caster brought** — *and a healer who will not say which is a healer who is lying.* **Cannot restore what has lost its pattern;** the dead stay dead, and the attempt to argue with that is Limina's crime, not this House's | **Memory and metabolic debt.** *A Vitalia healer at the end of a long campaign is a man who can name every wound he closed and not the wife he closed them to get home to* |
| **Fluxia** *fluids and pressure* | **Cannot act without a medium** — deep vaults, sealed reliquaries and the dry Undaar deeps are deliberately hostile ground. **Cannot hold a boundary indefinitely, because fluids want to mix and the working is always spending Essence to disagree** | **Dissolution of boundaries, and it is the eeriest corruption in the register.** *The tell is grammatical and should be written that way: an adept who has gone too far starts saying* we *about himself, without noticing, and gets irritated when corrected* |
| **Limina** *entropy, void, mind* | **Cannot create. Every working in this House is subtractive and there are no exceptions** — *the single most useful clarifying sentence in the manual.* Cannot act on what has no pattern, **so raw undifferentiated matter is nearly immune and a rock is a better Limina shield than a ward** | **Existential erosion, and it is reflexive: the practitioner pays in the same currency he spends.** *A man who erases memory loses memory. A man who nulls loses the ability to feel Aether at all in a widening radius around his own skin* |

> **Standing prohibition.** *Chronokinesis is Forbidden beyond Stage X. Chronoturgy is Forbidden outright.*
>
> **The Accord's enforcement of both is a plot instrument that has barely been used.**

## The Blank Card

*Copy, fill, log. Fields marked † are mandatory for anything that will later resolve a conflict.*
> **Name** · **Ability / Root Phenomenon** · **Trigger †** · **Function †** · **Verb** · **Rate** · **Mechanism †** · **Numerical Effect** · **Target Response** · **Limitation †** · **Weakness** · **Cost †** · **Vow** · **Counterplay †** · **Codex Stamp †** · **First Display †** · **Sensory Description**
> **Cross-reference.** This card is the workbook form and carries more than any single scene will use. **Mechanism is prose-legal and belongs on the page**: the phenomenon, the law being conditioned, the fault that falls out of it. What stays in the workbook is the form itself, the Codex stamp, and the arithmetic.
>
> *An entry the reader sees still leads with Effect, Cost, Limit and Counter, plus one line of what nobody knows. The mechanism is written into the effect rather than filed beside it.*

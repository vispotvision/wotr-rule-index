# Domains

*The place where a soul's own law outranks the world's: how it first flickers, who can call one, how long it holds, what happens when two meet, and what it does to the person holding it when it goes out*

> *"When belief holds, the world begins to answer."*
>
> Guild proverb, carved over the door of the assay house at Sealhall, and scratched out and recarved by somebody with a grudge at least twice a decade

Justiciar Ambrose Tick, Stage VIII, heard land cases at Hawes Bridge on the Kessen for thirty-eight years, a long thin man in a plum gown with a permanent cold in his head, and the song calls him the most tedious person who ever drew breath, which he would have taken as a professional compliment. In the autumn of 662 two armies of the Hundred Crowns came down both banks to settle a boundary he had already ruled on, four thousand men in yellow and green with their trumpets going and the bookies riding behind them on mules. He walked out to the middle of the bridge with his clerk and a folding stool. He sat down, blew his nose, and said what he had said at the opening of every sitting of his working life, not very loudly.

**"ORDER."**

His aura came up the dull gold of old sealing wax and it did not stop at his skin. It ran out along the planks and down over both banks, until three hundred yards of the Kessen valley had gone the color of a lamp in a records room. A lancer of the Greens at full gallop came to a walk and looked down at his horse in astonishment. Every sword inside that light slowed as it rose and stopped a finger's width from whatever it had been meant for, and it stayed there, humming, because nobody in the field could now do anything to anybody until their case was called. The clerk opened the book. That was the Long Sitting, and for the eight hundred men inside it the law of swords had been adjourned. The other three thousand stood outside the edge with their mouths open, and then, this being the Midmarch, sat down and started a book of their own.

He called them in order, and each man stated his grievance. A surprising number found they did not have one, sheathed, and went home. At the hour of lamps Dame Hild Aske, Marshal of the Yellows, Stage VIII and in no mood, rode to the edge and bawled her own art across the river, and a wall of furnace red rolled over the meadow and met the gold in a seam you could have walked along. For a quarter of an hour the two of them fought for that ground on nothing but the weight of their souls, while eight hundred men with their swords in the air watched the light change on one another's faces. Her Dominion was the better, and the seam came on toward the bridge. Then the clerk, who had done this to better advocates, stood up and read her case out of order: her name, her father's name, and the toll he had owed on that bridge since before she was born. She turned her head to answer. The gold went back over her like a tide over a sandcastle, and he found against her, with costs.

He held it for nine hours, and then his reserve fell to a tenth and it went out. A Domain pays when it ends. The collapse came off the bridge in one ring of struck air that took out the middle span and put the Justiciar, the stool and the clerk into the Kessen, and every blow still hanging over a case he had not reached fell at once, about four hundred of them. Then came the bill in the coin of the idea, as it does with the big ones. A court that sits must hear its list. Ambrose Tick lived another twenty-six years, and for all of them he could not walk past an argument. He stopped, unfolded the stool and heard it out, both sides, in the rain, on a bridge, in a burning house. His family wrote to him. The bridge at Hawes is a very bad place to start a fight to this day.

A child can say the whole of it: inside his yard, you wait your turn. What follows is how the Measurewrights say it, with the reasons.

## At a Glance

**What it is.** A Domain is a space where a practitioner's inner law becomes local reality. It is the third size of one idea: the [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Trait System Law, Function and the Forge|Trait]] is the idea, the [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/Release Forms|Release]] is the idea worn on a transformed body, and the Domain is the world obeying it.

**How it looks.** The owner's aura leaves the skin and fills the ground, and the light, air and temperature change to suit. Everyone inside feels it as a pressure behind the eyes. It is called aloud and it forms as it is named.

**What it costs.** Reserve for every second it stands, more for a larger or shakier field, and far more inside someone else's. When it goes out, a shockwave that spares nobody, its owner included.

**How it is beaten.** Step out of it, outlast it, break the holder's attention, read what it rests on, or arrive two Stages up.

**Example.** Corra Vell, Stage VII, says "Smelter's Yard" and a courtyard goes forge-hot and orange. That is a seed. It holds about three minutes from a full reserve and it dies if somebody shouts the right name. See [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/Release Forms|Release Forms]].

## What It Is

A soul has three layers, and the [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/Tier Grade, Bands & the Aether Shell|Attraction Layer]] is the one that holds bonds, vows and claims. A Domain begins when that layer claims territory on the Plane of Fate (the plane of Attraction Force and Obsession Force, where what is possible can be moved) and the claim reflects downward into physical and spiritual law. Inside the line, the owner's doctrine outranks the ordinary working of the world for as long as it is held. A soul has grown heavy enough to dent reality, and reality has stopped resisting the dent.

Domains are grown, and then they are called. No technique stands in for one and no manual contains one. The soul comes to it by living, and what nobody has ever done is call a Domain the soul has not grown. Once it is there, the owner opens it on purpose, by name, as deliberately as throwing a punch and a good deal more expensively.

**The Stage grants a Domain, and a Grade never does.** A ten-year veteran with a magnificent Dominion at Stage VI has a very heavy presence and no Domain. A Stage VIII with a modest one can have a small, bad, real Domain. Dominion and the Shell's clarity decide how wide it runs and how steady it stands. The Stage decides whether it can stand at all, and anyone at Stage VIII can have one, their own concept made real, with the fame, the rivals and the bounties that follow it around.

Any concept will serve. The Archives hold a Domain of closing time, one of a toll bridge, one of a shut door, one in which the bell decides who may rest, and one that has never been described by anybody who agreed to go back.

## The Road, Stage by Stage

The road has two kinds of step that are easy to run together, and the old literature did. One is the **flicker**, which happens to a person once, at a Threshold (the crossing from one Stage to the next). The other is the **calling**, which a person learns.

| Stage | What happens | How long | What it needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Threshold into VII** | **The flicker.** A one-time burst of the person's own Trait, the first fragile territory, which is the lived Catalyst for Refraction | Seconds, as a rule. Gaffer Tom Underhay's ran four, and every pint in the taproom fell upward. Penny Thyra's ran four minutes on the Ottermouth bridge, and the Guild is still cross about it | No Release. It cannot be repeated by wanting to |
| **VII · Refraction** | **The seed.** With a Second Release the flicker can be called back and held, as a fragile field the size of the room or yard it stands in | Minutes. Set on the card as time from a full reserve | The Second Release, found and mastered by fighting. Far fewer people have one than have reached this Stage |
| **VIII · Transcendence** | **The true Domain.** A piece of the soul's lattice accepts the signature for good. It is grown by living and called aloud | Hours. It survives small disruption, and it takes real force to shake it | The Stage, the Second Release, and the living |
| **The Threshold into IX** | **The first flicker of a Realm**, on the same terms: once, unbidden, the Catalyst and no more | A moment | No Release |
| **IX to X · Invocation, Realization** | **The Realm.** The Third Release turns a Domain into a field that can hold other fields inside it, with the Wellspring's second color at its edge. Pacts, artifacts and law begin to set into the terrain, and the Continuum (the whole lawful order of reality) has to make room | As the card says | The Third Release, which arrives by tribulation |
| **XI to XII · Dissonance, Emanation** | **The leak.** Effects run without active holding, and a mood becomes weather | Always | Having lived through the middle of the ladder |
| **XIII · Principality** | **The registered law.** The Continuum enters a law the person embodies, and the land carries it | Longer than they will | |
| **XIV · Zenith** | **No seam.** Domain and person cannot be told apart | | |

So a person reaches Stage VII on one involuntary flash and may then spend twenty years unable to do it again. That is the commonest shape of a career at Refraction, and every veteran corps has someone who will tell you about the night the beer turned green.

**The bought road.** A Draft called the Domain Seed Vitrifier sells the flicker, and a [[The Magic System/Practice and Institution/Blessings and Curses|blessing]] can too. Neither sells what comes after it. A bought Stage VIII has the right to a Domain and nothing to open, because the Second Release must be found and mastered by fighting, and no purse has ever opened one. [[The Magic System/Practice and Institution/Training and Sects|Sects]] teach the road, and that is a large part of what they are for. See [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Ladder and the Draft — Temperance, Advancement and Essence Drafts|The Ladder and the Draft]].

## Calling One

**The name goes first.** A true Domain forms as it is named. The volume belongs to the person: a showman bawls it across a valley, and Asahina Shigeko said *Kaerazu no Mon* almost too low to hear. A Second Release on its own may go out silent, weak and without its first rise, as its page sets out, and it will hold a field that way. Nobody has ever opened a true Domain without saying what it is, and the beat that costs has killed people.

**The window fills.** The holder's own [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/Windows and the Sheet|window]] shows the name, what changed and a bar draining, whatever the room is doing, and it is for the holder alone:

```
[DOMAIN: THE LONG SITTING]
[Changed: within the field no blow lands until its case is called]
[Workings: one Grade up · cap counted]
[Held: ████████░░]
[Reserve: 71%]
```

**It counts on the stack.** Inside a field the Second Release lifts the owner's workings one Grade, and a Third lifts its one stated effect by two. All of that counts toward the two-Grade cap on self-boosts, read from the sustainable Grade on the card, and only outside help goes past it. See [[The Magic System/Power in Use/Stacking and Spending|Stacking and Spending]].

**What the old headings are.** The Guild files what a Domain does under six behaviors, and every working fighter ignores the filing until it matters, since each one leans on a different stat.

| The heading | What it does inside the line | What governs it |
|---|---|---|
| **Low-G, High-G** | Weight changes | Dominion Sovereignty |
| **Hush** | Sound, force or Essence will not carry | Dominion Pressure, Harmonics Suppression |
| **Heat-Invert, Phase-Stabilize** | A set temperature or a set state of matter | The Wellspring |
| **Vectoring** | Bolts curve, distances lie, approaches slide off | Dominion Sovereignty, Dexterity Finesse |
| **Construct Sustain** | What was summoned stays without attention | Dominion Gravity, Ardency Density |
| **Inertial Dominion** | Moving things stop, turn or leap at the holder's word | Dominion Sovereignty, Vitality Absorption |

The Long Sitting is Inertial Dominion with a docket, and an Attraction Path holder gains the most from any of them: inside a formed Domain, that [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Four Paths Routing, Recognition and the Gate|Path's]] amplification can pass a Body Path fighter's raw advantage outright.

## When Two Meet

A field meeting a field is the great spectator sport of the upper ladder. The border shows as light changing color across the ground, bystanders feel both, and the odds-men move fast. The Continuum settles it in order.

1. **Stage first.** A field two or more Stages above the other simply overwrites it. Nobody fights for that ground, and the lower field goes out with its owner standing in it.
2. **Otherwise Dominion holds the ground.** Between fields closer than that, the owner with the higher Sovereignty and Gravity holds the territory, and the weaker field erodes from its edge, costing its owner more with every yard.
3. **A read can flip it.** Find what the field rests on, the owner's focus, its edge, or the thing they are guarding, press there, and the border moves back. Dame Hild Aske had the better Dominion. She also had a father.
4. **The Wellsprings have a say.** Fields built from Wellsprings at odds grind each other down, and neighbors may lie easy together.
5. **At dead level, the truer one.** A Domain built from an honestly lived truth is denser than one built from adopted belief. Tempering Coherence and Maturity are the tiebreak, and this is the part that makes rich people angry.

A Realm can do what no Domain can: go round another field and close, and hold it inside, still running, with its owner in it. Ask anyone from Dornhold, which burned for nineteen years afterward.

## What It Costs

- **Reserve, continuously.** A Domain drains by its width (Dominion Radius), by how shaky it is (Dominion Gravity), and by how hostile the ground is. Holding one inside someone else's costs a great deal more. The drain is written on the card as time from a full reserve.
- **The limit.** It ends on its own when the reserve falls to a tenth. Below that lies Essence Starvation.
- **Afterward.** The Second Release leaves the Crystal Overgrown (swollen and spilling) until a full night's sleep, and the Third for a day and a night. Held past the limit, a Second means a Crystal Fracture Event, and a Third may cost the Trait.
- **Collapse.** A field that fails produces a Resonance Shockwave, sized to its width and density. It strikes everything in the former territory, and that includes the owner. A seed's is a slap of hot wind and flying cobbles, and it will put a fighter on his back. A true Domain's takes bridges with it.
- **Its own price.** A big art charges in the coin of its own idea, on top of everything above, and the card says how. The toll-keeper must pay the toll, the landlady is the last one out, and the Justiciar hears the list.

## How It Is Beaten

Every Domain can be answered by someone at its own Stage.

- **Before.** The name costs a beat and the aura leaves the skin first. Strike into the beat, or be somewhere else.
- **Walk out.** Every field has an edge, which is the most useful fact on this page. Three thousand men at Hawes Bridge sat that afternoon out.
- **Outlast it.** The clock is the counter, on a bar only the owner can see. Make them hold it, keep your distance, and take the fight onto salted, bled or thin ground, where the reserve cannot refill.
- **Break the attention.** A seed collapses when attention breaks: a wound, a shout, a name that matters. A true Domain shrugs off the small ones and takes real force.
- **Read it.** Every law has a thing it rests on and a thing it left out. A docket can be read out of order, from either side.
- **Bring someone two Stages up.** It is expensive, and it never fails.
- **Stand clear of the end.** If the owner is going to lose it, be outside the line when they do, and make sure they are inside.

# The Concordant Crystal

*One body, several souls, and the one Shell they have to take turns with. Filed by the Guild's Measurewrights under shared frames, after the Ossuary Choir made the question impossible to leave open.*

The Harrowgate Sisters sang at their own funeral, which is the first thing anyone tells you about them. There were five of them, a family of grave-singers in black and rose on the Tollgarth road, and when the plague pit at Harrowgate took four in one winter the fifth sealed the five of them into one frame by the Rite of Hollow Union, kneeling in the snow with the others still warm, and got up. She walked into Tollgarth market on the first day of spring with one mouth and five voices, in harmony, and four kinds of light going round her in bands of bone-white, arterial red, teal and a black-violet that hurt to look at. The whole market stopped, and a bookie began taking bets on which sister was driving. The answer changed in the middle of the first song, and you could see it happen, because the body stumbled.

A Concordant Crystal is a Soul Crystal (the soul itself, in its working form) shared across several donors. Synergia, several Crystals briefly sharing one Essence Core, Shell and Layer, is a thing fighters do for a moment with a great deal of shouting. A Concordant Crystal is that made permanent by Concordia (the art of aligning many fields into one), and it is ordinarily sealed through a rite such as the Rite of Hollow Union, so that nobody has to go on concentrating. The donors must already carry a bond the Continuum (the whole lawful order of reality) can read as one signature. Kinship, an oath or a shared catastrophe will do, and strangers will not.

## What fuses, and what does not

The three layers behave three different ways, and the whole of the art is in the difference.

**The Essence Cores never fuse.** Each donor keeps a Core of their own, with their own identity, memory and Traits, and only one of them holds the frame's single point of outward control at a time. The hand-off between donors costs something the Continuum recognizes: it pulls down Dexterity and the Reflex family of Sub-Stats, however well the frame is built. That is the stumble the market saw.

**The Aether Shell fuses, without exception.** One frame produces exactly one Aether Class and one η (eta, the share of each spend that arrives as effect), however many donors it carries, because a body has one nervous system to conduct through. A Concordant Crystal does not multiply a Shell's ceiling by its donors. It gives five people the throughput of one Shell.

**The Attraction Layer alone is plural.** It carries one Wellspring harmonization per donor, each running beside the others without blending, which is how one frame can show several Wellsprings where a single Crystal shows a compatible few. Donors whose Wellsprings are opposed cannot anchor one Layer, and the frame will not form or will not hold.

Coherence and η are read once off the shared Shell. Tempering Coherence, the Sub-Stat, stays with each donor because it lives in the Essence Core, and a frame is exactly as stable as its least coherent donor, and not as stable as their average.

## What it costs

**It runs Overgrown by default.** One Shell built to one donor's architecture is carrying the pressure of all of them, so power spills and moods stain the air, and with five moods the air gets very interesting.

**Losing a donor.** A death or a clean removal drops the frame by one without breaking it. That donor's Wellspring line falls silent, the Shell settles, and the frame eases toward Refined as the pressure comes off. A death the survivors refuse to release does not settle. The bond curdles into Obsession Force, and the frame fractures around a signature that has stopped answering.

**Two failures nobody else can have.** One is that same fixation on a co-donor who is gone. The other is total loss: if the single shared Shell clouds or ruptures, every donor loses all output at once, where separate Crystals would each have failed alone.

## How it is beaten

Watch for the hand-off and hit the stumble. Find the least coherent donor and press there, because the whole frame stands on that one. Failing that, kill one of them, then say the name, and wait.

## The mechanism as entered

*The Measurewrights keep the entry on the shelf in the words it was filed in.*

A Concordant Crystal is the standing mechanism for a Soul Crystal shared across multiple donors. It is Synergia -- several Soul Crystals briefly sharing one Essence Core, Shell and Layer -- made permanent by Concordia instead of left momentary, ordinarily sealed through a Ritus such as the Rite of Hollow Union rather than held open by anyone's ongoing concentration. Formation requires that the donors already carry an Attraction-recognized bond strong enough for the Continuum to read them as one Attraction signature; kinship, oath, or shared catastrophe qualify, strangers do not.

The three layers do not fuse identically. The Essence Cores never fuse: each donor keeps a discrete Core -- identity, memory and Traits remain their own -- and only one donor's Core occupies the frame's single point of outward control at a time, with the hand-off between donors itself a Continuum-recognized cost that degrades Dexterity and Reflex-family Sub-Stats independent of the frame's build. The Aether Shell fuses and is singular without exception: one frame produces exactly one Aether Class, one eta, and one Coherence Band, however many donors it carries, because a body has only one nervous system to conduct through -- a Concordant Crystal does not multiply a Shell's ceiling by donor count, it grants N donors the throughput of one Shell. The Attraction Layer alone is genuinely plural: it carries one discrete Wellspring harmonization per donor, running concurrently rather than blending into a single current, which is how a Concordant Crystal expresses several Wellsprings where a single-donor Crystal expresses at most a compatible few; donors drawing on opposed Wellspring Houses cannot co-anchor one Layer and the frame will not form, or will not hold.

Coherence and eta are pooled, read once off the shared Shell regardless of donor count. Tempering Coherence, the Sub-Stat, stays per-donor because it lives in the Essence Core; a Concordant Crystal's stability is read off its least coherent donor, not their average.

A Concordant Crystal runs Overgrown by default, not as a symptom: one Shell built to one donor's architecture now carries the combined Essence pressure of every donor it holds. A donor's death or clean removal drops the frame from N to N-1 donors without destroying it: that donor's Wellspring line falls silent, the Shell re-equilibrates, and the frame settles toward Refined as the shed pressure eases its standing Overgrowth. A death the surviving donors refuse to release does not settle; the bond curdles into Obsession Force instead of dissolving, and the frame fractures under a signature that has stopped answering.

A Concordant Crystal carries two failure modes no single-donor Crystal can suffer: Obsession Force fixation on an unreleased co-donor, and total loss of output from every donor at once should the single shared Shell Cloud or Rupture, where separate Crystals would each fail independently.

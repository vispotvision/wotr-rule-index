# The Ladder and the Draft — Temperance, Advancement and Essence Drafts

The most expensive goose in the history of alchemy belonged to a chandler at Marrow Fair. Corvin Hathersage, nineteen years old and heir to the Hathersage wool money, had been told by three instructors that he had the floor for Splintering and none of the rest of it, so he hired the great stage at the autumn brackets, sold the seats at a silver mark a head, put on a gold sash and drank eleven hundred marks' worth of breakthrough Draft in front of six thousand people, with a herald crying the price of every swallow. It worked, the way they work. His aura went from charcoal with a rumor of blue in it to a cracking web of arc-blue, every hair in the front four rows stood up, and the whole fairground watched his Crystal go to the melt. At exactly that moment Nob Pye, a stable-hand retained by his rival and possessed of a sense of occasion, let the goose out.

It crossed the great stage at a dead run, neck out and wings wide, screaming like a rusted gate, and it took him by the gold sash.

Whatever is in a Crystal at the moment of reorganization is built into what comes out. Hathersage arrived at Stage V before six thousand witnesses, a genuine Expert, with a lattice organized around the purest terror of his young life. He went on to kill nine men in formal duels, and he killed them superbly, with the Stage he had paid for and not one Release to his name. He could not cross a farmyard. Geese knew, somehow, and followed him from county to county in long honking lines, and the family spent a fair part of the wool money on crossbowmen. The instructors tell it as a joke. The apothecaries sold out of that batch before the fair closed.

> **The Stage-by-Stage account lives in [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Sixteen Stages|The Sixteen Stages]]**, with each Stage given four ways: the Codex (the Accord's master register of laws and figures) ledger line, what it is from inside the person it is happening to, what it looks like from outside, and what the folk call it. **This page does not repeat it.**
>
> What this page does is join the ladder to the rest of the system: what changes in each Crystal layer (the three layers of the Soul Crystal, which is the soul itself, made a working thing) as a soul climbs, what an Essence Draft is, what money does to advancement, and why the most profitable crime in every realm exists.
>
> **One sentence governs everything below. A Threshold requires a quantitative floor and a Catalyst Condition, and both can be bought.**

## I · The two keys

Temperance (the climb of a soul from Stage to Stage) is subtraction. The Soul Crystal is literally the soul, and to temper it is to melt it down and rebuild it, layer by layer. Each pass refines the Essence it holds and makes that Essence stronger, and the body comes out of it gaining Traits, as [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Trait System Law, Function and the Forge|The Trait System]] explains under What a Trait Is. Each Stage is a structural reorganization the Crystal undergoes when accumulated pressure exceeds its current architecture, and no Stage has ever been paid out as a reward for banked experience. A Threshold opens only when both keys turn.

**The quantitative floor.** Sufficient pressure inside the current Stage. Measurable, purchasable, and the half of the equation every hall's assayers are trained to read.

**The Catalyst Condition.** A specific quality of experience the Crystal requires before it will authorize the transition. **A practitioner can sit quantitatively eligible for months of in-world time and never pass a Threshold, because the Catalyst has not occurred.** [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Sixteen Stages|The Sixteen Stages]] names the Catalyst for each rung, and that list is the lived road: the accident survived, the bond held, the wound made load-bearing.

**The lived road is one way to turn the second key. A Draft or a blessing will also turn it.** Stages can simply be bought, a purse can buy the Catalyst for any rung of the ladder, and the wealthy are stronger because they are wealthy. At Zenith a Hollow Throne must still recognize the buyer, so up there money buys the attempt and not the seat. The instructors who taught for two centuries that the second key could not be drunk were teaching a comfort, frequently on a house's retainer, and every apothecary in the bracket towns was making a living off the difference.

> **A soul is assembled out of what happened to it and what it could afford, and the rich get to choose the order.**

So the great houses hoard the top Drafts the way other people hoard grain before a siege, and they have fought more wars over a vault than they will ever admit to. The poor rise by three roads: **dungeon luck**, the crew that comes up with a core nobody expected and the nerve to keep it; **guild patronage**, a hall that stakes a promising hand against years of commissions; and **blessings**, from anything strong enough to give one and bored or fond enough to bother. A commoner holding a big monster core is the single most frightening sight in any lord's county, and lords have burned villages on the rumor of one.

### What a bought Stage buys, and what it does not

A bought Stage opens the door and no more. The Stage is real: the stat ceiling lifts, the Band gate clears, the color brightens, and the coil (the brass assay instrument a practitioner stands inside) reads the Stage the same however it was crossed, though it reads the seat differently, as [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Sixteen Stages|The Sixteen Stages]] sets out in its appendix. Everything past the door still has to be got the long way.

| A bought Stage gives | A bought Stage does not give |
|---|---|
| The Stage's name and rung, on every instrument | **A Release.** It must be found and mastered by fighting, every one of them |
| The Grade cap and stat ceiling | **A [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/Domains\|Domain]].** Anyone at Stage VIII can have one; somebody has to find theirs |
| Clearance of the Band gate | **Level.** The architecture sits thin until danger overcome fills it |
| A real change in what the Crystal can hold | **Techniques and skills.** Those rise by doing them |
| | **The Path gates.** The Crystal goes on reading what the soul actually does |
| The ceiling of every [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/Classes and the Three Ladders\|class ladder]] | **The rung.** A bought Stage opens the ceiling and leaves the rung where it was |
| | **The Crystal's Tier.** It lags the Stage until Level fills it |

A rich Stage IX may have no Release at all, while a poor fighter at the same Stage has all three, and the second one tends to win. **Sects teach what money cannot buy:** Releases, Domains, techniques, skills and the rites for their Wellspring, along with real fights, a crew and a name, which is why a duke's son in a gold sash will still go on his knees at a mountain gate and scrub floors for an elder who is rude to him.

## II · The arc, in five movements

*Summary only. The full treatment is in [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Sixteen Stages|The Sixteen Stages]]. The Catalysts named here are the lived road.*

**Band I · Stages I to IV, the first part of Inner Refinement.** Murmuring, Welling, Ascension, Flourishing. **Better than nine souls in ten will live and die inside this Band,** and everything the world thinks it knows about magic it knows from watching people who never left it. The Crystal opens, spills, finds direction, and stabilizes into an identity.

**The first crisis · Stage V.** Splintering. The Crystal fractures along emotional lines after surviving contact with something more than two full Grades above its ceiling. Survival is the whole requirement, and defeating the thing adds nothing.

**Consolidation · Stages VI to VII.** Glory and Refraction. The fractures become features, the aura starts generating environmental effects, and then the soul turns and looks at itself from outside. **Refraction is where a practitioner first reads their own fracture record as an object of study.**

**Outer Influence · Stages VIII to X.** Transcendence, Invocation, Realization. The first true Domain (a region where the world runs on the practitioner's terms) forms from a wound consciously made load-bearing. Then the practitioner stops working alone. Then the Continuum (the lawful fabric of reality) stops resisting and starts accommodating, and **the direction of that accommodation never reverses back.**

**Mythic and Absolute · Stages XI to XIV.** Dissonance, Emanation, Principality, Zenith. The chosen approach to annihilation, what radiates from a soul that came back, formal registration of a law, and finally recognition by a Hollow Throne. **Zenith is conferred, and no breakthrough reaches it**: a purse may buy the attempt, and the Throne still decides, as The Sixteen Stages, Appendix B has it.

**Beyond the canon · Stages XV to XVI.** Revelation and Apex. Archon-participant territory and then a perpetual state with no ceiling beneath Apotheosis.

## III · What climbs, layer by layer

*The ladder is usually read as one number going up. It is three architectures changing at different rates, and they do not change together.*

Harmonization runs across all three, and it is said once on [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Three Paths Routing, Recognition and the Gate|The Three Paths]]: the lock happens at the Shell, where Aether enters, the tie is held on the Layer, and the lens shapes Essence in the lead layer.

| Tier of Standing | Essence Core (inner layer: identity, memory, Traits) | Aether Shell (outer layer: the skin that turns power into force) | Attraction Layer (the Fate-Plane layer: bonds, Domains, Wellspring ties) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1–4 · I–IV** | Identity assembling. Traits present but volatile, with real risk of misfire and self-harm. | Forming and porous. Output arrives before control does. | First harmonization. First Spirit Axis at Flourishing. |
| **5 · V–VI** | Fractured along emotional lines, then processing those fractures into features. | Self-conducting. Signature becomes permanent. | Aura begins generating environmental effect. |
| **5–6 · VII–VIII** | Visible to itself. The fracture record becomes readable. | Traits externalize and interface cleanly with Wellspring dialect. | Domain seed, then first true Domain. |
| **6–7 · IX–XII** | Denser after Dissonance than clean advancement can produce. | Traits reach field-state and stop needing maintenance. | Domain to Realm to ambient consequence. Passive miracles leak. |
| **8 · XIII–XIV** | The Crystal self-writes. | Trait and Domain Law fuse. The Trait stops being an ability. | A law with standing independent of the practitioner. |

> **The architectural note on Dissonance.** Souls that pass *through* Stage XI rather than around it arrive at Emanation fractured and denser than the clean path produces. **Overflow, Overchannel and Persistence reach their true ceiling in no other way.** Those fractures count as structure. Anyone who tells an ambitious practitioner otherwise is either being kind or has an interest in them not trying.

## IV · Essence Drafts

*Aether + Essence + Vessel + Glyphica (the glyph-writing that shapes a working) = Reaction. A Draft is a working stabilized into a substance, and the substance came from somewhere.*

| Type | Source | Potency | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Creature-Sourced** | Magical creatures, monster cores above all | Highest | **Identity Contamination** |
| **Material-Sourced** | High-density minerals | Moderate | Clean but weaker |
| **Environmental** | Affinity-saturated immersion | Low per unit time | Near zero |
| **Synthetic** | Alchemically blended | Variable and unique | **Highest. Internal reaction.** |

**Where the material comes from.** Every substance in the ledger carries a Resonance and a Wellspring, with the kind that names its home, alongside its Bearing and Holding grades. A dragon's scale is no generic reagent: it carries a specific Wellspring resonance, it renders at a specific grade, and **that grade begins falling from the moment the animal stops.** The whole beast trade is an argument with decomposition, which is why the northern runners are the profitable ones and why some rendering houses are sited beside a glacier for no other reason.

**[[Materials, Alchemy & Trade/Alchemy/Monster Cores|Monster cores]].** A monster's core is in a way its Soul Crystal, and it is worth more the stronger the monster was. This is the lawful Crystal Draw of The Standing Index, the Creature-Sourced stock the whole trade turns on. Monsters gain Levels by killing, each other included, so an old one has done a great deal of tempering of its own, and a crew that brings up the core of a famous beast with a bounty on it has often made more in an afternoon than its home village will see in a generation. An alchemist renders the core down, taking the Essence off it into **Essence Drafts**, and those go overwhelmingly to one use, which is tempering: a practitioner takes the course, goes to the melt, and comes out with Essence refined further and a body that has gained by it. The Traits fed this way are the ordinary ones in the ordinary Reflections, see [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Trait System Law, Function and the Forge|The Trait System]], and they rise on the owner's window like anything else that has been worked.

**What stock does to whom.** A course is matched three ways, and a good apothecary asks all three before she takes your money. **The lens:** stock from the Wellspring you temper on goes in clean, and foreign stock tempers just as well and adds carry (what a soul keeps of where its stock came from), so a Pyreveil fighter drinking wolf for a season is going to notice wolf. **The layer:** a monster's stone is Core stock and feeds the Core. Tempering rebuilds each layer, so a course for the Shell is made from the Salt fraction of the render, the part the bench reads as body, and a Body fighter drinks more of it and a Spirit fighter less. **The third layer:** shaved Layer is the black-market Fate reagent. The dresser takes it off a Crystal, the richest of it comes off a dead person's, and it is what goes into a Fate Essence Draft, which is why nobody who owns one discusses its provenance.

**The raw core.** A core can be swallowed or broken raw, which skips the alchemist's fee and delivers everything it holds at once. Most who try it die, and a good share of the rest come out wrong: a cooper who growls at his wife and cannot say why, a girl who has stopped sleeping and started hunting. The few who come out whole are the reason it goes on happening, and every one of them is a story told in a cheap tavern to somebody with nothing left to lose.

**Identity Contamination** is the risk that makes Creature-Sourced Drafts both the most potent and the most carefully counted stock in any hall's cellar, since nothing else tempers as fast and nobody with a career to protect takes it without keeping tally of the doses. The Essence taken carries the identity it came from, and an Essence Core that absorbs enough of it stops being able to say cleanly which convictions are its own. **The failure mode leaves the practitioner alive, with an Attunement (how the soul tunes itself to what it works) that has quietly stopped matching their Coherence (how closely its stated Path matches how it lives), and they are the last to notice.**

**The great crime.** A Crystal can be cut from a dead practitioner and rendered, and it makes the strongest Draft of all, because no monster that ever lived tempered as deliberately as a person who spent a lifetime at it. It is the worst crime in every realm, the most profitable, and the reason [[Materials, Alchemy & Trade/Alchemy/The Standing Index|Index Class IV]] stock in circulation has never balanced against what the lawful sources can produce: some of those "monster cores" came out of people. Every realm hangs for it. Every realm has at least one great family whose fortunes improved abruptly after a war.

**Master Work Drafts.** A [[Materials, Alchemy & Trade/Alchemy/Master Works|Master Work]] is a piece that does what its Grade and material should not be able to, made once at the end of a lifetime, always Unique or Mythic, and named. The trade's own Master Works are Drafts, and the most coveted of them is a **Fate Essence Draft**, which bottles the manipulation of possibility itself: the very flow and idea of a thing, set in a vial. It is extremely hard to make, there are perhaps a handful in the world at any time, and people who could afford a county have instead spent their lives trying to get near one.

**Fight Drafts.** Everything above works on the ladder. The Drafts a fighter carries on the belt work on the reserve: a fight Draft takes a beat to get down, refills reserve, and leaves a crash that pays for it. What pours in is Essence with nobody's stamp on it, and the crash is the Crystal stamping it afterward, all at once. Drafts are graded by the Stage they serve and priced about threefold a Stage, and counterfeits are common enough that bracket crowds keep a chant for the man who has just swallowed a mouthful of chalk at the worst moment of his life.

## V · What a Draft does to advancement

> **A Draft can supply the quantitative floor, a seed, and at the breakthrough grades the Catalyst itself. A blessing can supply a Catalyst too.**
>
> What no vial has ever supplied is control over what comes out. Stats forced to stay above their sustainable Grade by alchemical augmentation, bloodline exploitation or external metaphysical imposition **generate Crystal Fracture Events at an exponentially increasing rate until the stat collapses or the Threshold is passed through crisis.** That is the lasting forced stat. A boost for the length of a fight passes the Stage's ceiling lawfully inside the two-Grade cap, read from the sustainable Grade on the card, and see [[The Magic System/Power in Use/Stacking and Spending|Stacking and Spending]] for that.

So, in the matter of advancement, a Draft does four things, and the trade will sell you any of them with a straight face.

**The everyday use.** A course of Essence Drafts fed into honest tempering. Essence refines, the body gains, a Trait climbs a level or two, and the practitioner is ahead of the neighbor who could not pay. This is what most Drafts are for, it is the backbone of every apothecary's year, and most of the world means nothing else by the word.

**The honest shortcut.** A practitioner who has met their Catalyst but sits below the floor can be brought to the floor. Legitimate, licensed, expensive, and it shortens the wait.

**The breakthrough Draft.** A practitioner who has *not* met their Catalyst is handed one in liquid form, and the Crystal goes to the melt on schedule. The Stage is real. Whatever the Catalyst would have taught is simply absent, and so is every Release that fighting would have found along the way, which is the whole of the difference between Corvin Hathersage and the people who wanted him dead. The price climbs about threefold a Stage on the shelf and then leaves the shelf altogether, and at the top grades these Drafts are held in house vaults, given as dowries and used as hostages.

**The dishonest use.** A practitioner with no Catalyst and no money for the real article can be pushed above their sustainable grade anyway, on Ladder Dust and its cousins. The Crystal will hold the numbers for a while. Then it begins fracturing, and the fracture rate compounds, and it ends one of two ways: the stat collapses, or the Threshold is forced through crisis and the practitioner arrives at the next Stage having been reorganized around whatever was in the Crystal at that moment. **Whatever is in the Crystal at the moment of reorganization is built into what comes out.** Somebody who forced Splintering while afraid is a permanently different practitioner from somebody who met it having decided something.

**Blessings.** A [[The Magic System/Practice and Institution/Blessings and Curses|blessing]] grants a power out of a person's natural reach, Level included, and it can turn the second key as a Draft does. It appears on the sheet as its own line, and some people have lived for years under a line that read as a gift. Its own page has the givers, the terms and the way one comes off.

**What this means for the world.** The trade in advancement is enormous, and it is the shape of the world. A Stage on a token tells you what somebody's Crystal can hold; it tells you nothing about whether they ever had to. Everybody who has spent their life in the halls learns to ask the follow-up question, usually with a practice sword.

## VI · Why the moment of reorganization decides everything

*The mechanism under the sentences above, stated in the vocabulary of the forge rather than the vocabulary of doctrine.*

A Threshold is a melt. The Crystal is carried to the point where its lattice stops holding its shape, and it is allowed to cool into a new one, layer upon layer. A melt does not cool into order on its own. Something has to survive the heat for the new lattice to grow around, and in a Soul Crystal that something is **Traits**.

A Trait is a law the Continuum has already accepted as reliable, and a law does not stop being reliable at temperature. This is the only property in the Crystal with that behavior, and it is why Traits decide the quality of an advancement that strain merely made possible. **Strain gets a soul to the melt. Nucleation decides what the melt produces.**

The consequences follow without further argument.

**A seeded melt** cools dense, deliberate and clean. Clarity and Compression come out ahead of where they went in. This is the clean seat.

**A contradicted melt** cools around seeds that disagree with each other, and the lattice records the disagreement permanently. This is the rough seat, and it is why rough-seated practitioners are unpredictable rather than weak.

**An unseeded melt** cools amorphous. The Stage does not advance, Clarity and Compression fall, and the weakest Trait commonly dissolves without reforming. The halls call this a **Dead Set**, a seat that never took. Everyone else calls it **slag**. Recovery is slow, partial, and the practitioner is usually the last person in the hall to accept that it happened.

> **This is what a Draft is actually doing.** A Draft supplies an artificial seed. It gives the cooling lattice a law to grow around that the soul did not survive into existence, which is why a Draft genuinely improves the quality of an advancement and why the improvement is not the practitioner's own. In Creature-Sourced Drafts the borrowed law arrives with the identity it was rendered from, and that is Identity Contamination stated as a structural mechanism rather than a warning.
>
> **A Draft can supply the floor, a seed and a Catalyst.** A rich melt is therefore the cleanest melt money can arrange, and it is cooled around somebody else's convictions.

## VII · Tempering, and what a Draft actually touches

*Tempering is the stat of becoming. A high-Tempering practitioner may hold no more power than the next person, and holds it in a cleaner, deeper, structurally sounder container.*

The Sub-Stats a Draft plausibly reaches: **Clarity** (refinement of Essence already held) and **Yield** (volume produced per Stage, ambient Aether absorbed rather than passed through, and the gap between generation and availability). This is where a course of Essence Drafts does its daily work.

The Sub-Stats no Draft has ever reached: **Coherence**, the alignment between a practitioner's stated Path and their lived behavior, which on the lived road is the structural prerequisite for Threshold eligibility, and **Maturity**, the degree to which prior trauma has been genuinely integrated, which is the primary Sub-Stat the Crystal consults when evaluating readiness.

> **Coherence is the Crystal's internal honest scale, and it cannot be drunk.** A breakthrough Draft carries a soul over the Threshold without consulting that scale, and the soul arrives on the far side reading exactly what it read before. That is the mechanical reason a bought Stage opens the door and no more, stated in the vocabulary of the stat framework rather than in the vocabulary of doctrine.

# The Sixteen Stages

*What Temperance (the climb of a soul from Stage to Stage) Does to a Soul, Stage by Stage, Told Plainly*
> Compiled for the Prime Instructors of the Guild Accord at the direction of the Research and Archives Division, following the Division's finding that the account of the Stages in the Codex (the Accord's master register of laws and figures) runs to eleven lines and has never once been sufficient to teach from.

It began with a pie. On the second day of the harvest fair at Pollard's Cross, a twelve-year-old apprentice of the Copper Heron school, Stage II and in love with the pie-seller's daughter, wanted a mutton pie hot and got it hot, and the pie went off like a dropped coal across the blue sash of a Stage III journeyman of the Tannery Hounds. *Fwump.* The journeyman answered with the first technique he had ever built by himself. His sister, Stage IV, came through the awning. By noon a Stage VI instructor of the Herons had arrived, with the small flames on every stall leaning toward him as he crossed the square, and the crowd, which had been eating, went on eating and began to take bets. By mid-afternoon a Hounds elder at Stage VIII had opened a Domain the size of the horse market, the horses inside it were picking their way very carefully across a floor that had agreed to be somewhere else, and a man was charging a copper a head to stand on his wagon. The walls went, then Cooper Street, then the bend of the river.

*GRRRROOOOOM.*

At sundown a woman named Aldith Fenn, Stage X, who had been fishing four miles upstream, walked down in her hip boots, wet to the waist, and asked them to stop. They stopped. Three hundred and six people were dead, the pie-seller among them, and the ground at Pollard's Cross has not frozen in the two centuries since.

Every child in the four quarters can skip rope to the names:

> *Murmur, Well, and up you Rise,*  
> *Bloom and Splinter, Glory, Eyes,*  
> *Cross the water, Call, and Stay,*  
> *Crack, and Shine, and Rule the Day,*  
> *Top of the world and one more after,*  
> *And one more yet, and skip it faster.*

Those are the Sixteen, in order, and the Archives can find no fault with the rhyme. Every rung of it is famous, and every rung of it has a body count the children know by heart.

## How This Is Written

> *"Temperance is a confession of how much you have survived, and nobody has ever confessed for free."*
The Codex records each Stage with exacting precision: its Tier of Standing (the Accord's rank title for it), its Grade ceiling (the highest letter grade, F to EX+, its stats may reach), its stat ceiling, and the Catalyst Condition (the life event a soul must pass through to cross) the Crystal (the Soul Crystal, the organ that works magic) requires before it will authorize the transition. Those figures are correct, they are reproduced here without alteration, and they have never taught anybody anything.

What follows sits underneath them. Each Stage is given four ways: **the ledger line**, which is the Codex's own figure; **what the Stage is from inside** the person it is happening to; **what it looks like from outside**, to the household or the hall or the field; and **what the common people call it**, which the Archives has three times recommended cutting and which predicts better than half the Index. Ahead of those four goes **the one they tell**, because no minstrel was ever paid for a ledger line and nobody ever feared a Stage they had only read about. The persons in those stories are mostly long dead and the stories have been improved; the Archives vouches for the corpses.
> Two facts govern everything below and neither is negotiable.
>
> **The first** is that a Threshold (the crossing from one Stage to the next) requires both a quantitative floor and a Catalyst Condition, and no compound ever rendered has supplied a Catalyst.
>
> **The second** is that a Threshold is a structural reorganization, and whatever is in the Crystal at the moment of reorganization is built into what comes out.
>
> A soul is therefore assembled out of what happened to it and what it could afford, in that order, and the Accord's entire certification apparatus is an instrument for reading the result.
>
> The Levels on each ledger line below are where that Stage's practitioners usually sit, not where they must; only Part One's Band (a block of a hundred Levels) gates are hard: Stage IV before Level 100, VII before 200, X before 300, XII before 400.

## Inner Refinement: Band I · Mortal Foundation

*Stages I through IV. Better than nine souls in ten will live and die inside this Band. Everything the world thinks it knows about magic, it knows from watching people who never left it, and there are a great many of them to watch: every market has its hedge-worker and every town its sect, with its outer and inner disciples, the core disciple nobody can stand, and an elder who will tell you about it.*

### I · Murmuring

**Tier 1 · Initiate · Grade E · Ceiling 50 · Levels 1 to 25 · The Crystal has opened**

***The one they tell*** · The dogs of Thorn Mill went quiet on a market day, all twelve of them, in the middle of a fight over a pig's ear. By the week's end the whole parish was laying coin on which child it was, and the smart money was on the miller's boy, Little Jem Sorrel, seven years old, who lost his temper at his sister's naming feast and sent every cup on a forty-foot table into a shiver, *tktktktktk*, until the ale crept over the rims and the priest stopped with his mouth open. That was all it did. His mother sat up with him for a year afterward, and three generations of instructors have quoted what she told the parish: a house with a Murmuring child in it is a house that has begun keeping a wolf, and the wolf is very small.

***From inside*** · A hum under the sternum that answers mood and answers nothing else. Wake angry and the water in the cup by the bed is trembling before you have understood why you are angry. Want something badly and feel a pressure behind the eyes that goes nowhere and does nothing and does not stop. There is no direction in it. There is no volume to adjust. It is weather happening inside a body that has not been told there is now weather.

Children in Murmuring feel no fear of it, which adults consistently fail to predict, and a good deal of irritation. It is a noise, and they were already living in a body full of noises.

What opening does not do is hand the child anything new. Traits (standing laws a soul has survived into being) sit in the material of a soul from the beginning and run in people whose Crystal never opens at all. Murmuring arms what was already there. The quiet child who never took a fever had the Trait at four; at eleven the Crystal simply gives it a conductor and a bill.

***From outside*** · Almost nothing, and the almost is what matters. A quality of stillness in a room that people notice and do not name. Animals get it first. Dogs go quiet around a Murmuring child a full season before the parents work out what is happening, and every farmer in the four quarters knows to watch the dogs and not one of them would call it a diagnostic.

***What the folk call it*** · **Carrying a bee.** A boy with a bee in him. The remedy is iron in the bed, on the reasoning that the iron takes the hum away from the child, and the reasoning is wrong in every particular and correct in its outcome, because iron does conduct and an ungoverned Crystal does shed into the nearest better conductor. A Murmuring child sleeps better with a horseshoe under the mattress. The Accord sells the guild-milled version of this as Sour Iron Filings, at a markup, to the people who invented it.

### II · Welling

**Tier 2 · Apprentice · Grade D · Ceiling 100 · Levels 26 to 50**

**Catalyst** · first genuine contact with a Wellspring's raw Aether under personal pressure (a Wellspring being one of the sixty types of Aether, each working on the Crystal in its own way and carrying its own physical law, and pooling at sites common speech calls Cores): touched and survived, where watching and instruction count for nothing

***The one they tell*** · Annis Cobb of Thatcher's Row wanted the bread oven hotter. She was eleven, it was the coldest morning of that winter, and the week before, down at the old Core in Cobble Fen, she had touched something she had told nobody about. *FWOOOMP.* The oven went, then the bakehouse, then nineteen roofs in a long sheet of orange that brought the whole valley out to look. Four people died in it, among them the baker, the kindest man on the Row. By the next market a sect recruiter in a green coat had paid her parents forty silver marks and driven her off in a cart, and the town was selling views of the scorch at a copper a head before she was out of sight.

***From inside*** · It spills. Direction arrives before volume does, so the child now knows what they are reaching for and has no capacity whatever to reach for a measured amount of it. They want the cup and the cup goes over. They want the fire hotter and the linen scorches. The Shell (the Aether Shell, the soul's outer layer that turns power into force) is forming and it is porous, and living inside a porous Shell at eleven years old is the experience of being blamed daily for things that are entirely your doing and entirely not your fault.

The Catalyst is worth sitting with. Instruction will not do it and neither will watching: a Wellspring's Aether has to be touched, under pressure, and survived. Which means the gate into Welling is an accident that went badly and did not go worst. Most practitioners in the four quarters can name the day. A good number of them have a scar from it.

***From outside*** · This is the year the household finds out. Broken crockery, soured milk, a scorch on a table nobody set a pot down on. Welling is when families discover what they have and decide what to do about it, and it is the year most children are sent away, and it is the year most accidents happen, in that order and for that reason.

***What the folk call it*** · **The spilling year.** The remedy is water. The child carries full buckets, all day, up the hill and back down, and is beaten for what they slop over the rim. It looks like cruelty and half the time it is cruelty and it is also, on the instrument, the correct training, because measuring out with the body teaches the Shell to measure out and no better method has ever been found. Every Prime Instructor in the Accord has quietly kept the practice. The published curriculum calls it graduated load discipline and does not mention the hill.
> **Trade note** · The guild ration begins here, with **Metering Tonic**, which halves the spillage and produces a practitioner who measures out worse afterward than one who was never given it. The finding is fourteen years old. The ration has not changed.

### III · Ascension

**Tier 3 · Journeyman · Grade C · Ceiling 175 · Levels 51 to 75**

**Catalyst** · first technique developed independently from instruction, built from the practitioner's own understanding of their Essence (the soul's own stuff of power) rather than received doctrine

***The one they tell*** · The first thing anybody builds is usually stupid, and Kim Dal-rae's was the stupidest in the Mahuo country. She was fifteen, she pounded rice for a living, and what she worked out by herself over one bad winter was that a thing she threw would go straight back to wherever it had last been at rest. She called it *Jeolgu Gwihwan*, Pestle Goes Home, and the meaning got around fast on the day the tax-farmer's man walked out of her yard with a sack he had taken off her aunt, because the pestle she threw at him went home through the sack and through the man and through the gatepost, *TOK*, in that order. She did it again to check. Then she sat down, and then she ran, and the ninety-mark bounty three counties posted on her ran for forty years. She outlived it and had it framed.

***From inside*** · The first thing you made yourself, working.

Everything until this moment was received. Somebody showed you and you copied it and it held, and you were proud of it, and it was never yours. Then one day you build a thing out of your own understanding of what your Essence actually does, and it holds, and the whole of Stage III is the difference between those two experiences. There is nothing else in it.

Some people weep. Most do not. The recorded pattern across four centuries of instructor notes is that the practitioner does the thing, and then does it again to check, and then sits down.

***From outside*** · The person becomes legible. Before Ascension you could say a child had something. After it, you can say what they are, and the recruiters can price them, and the family can plan, and the Path (the route, body, spirit, bond or fate, the Crystal habitually works through) is visible to anyone who knows how to look. This is the point at which a life stops being a question and becomes a known quantity, and there are people who never forgive it.

***What the folk call it*** · **Coming into the road.** The first true year. A family that can afford it holds a feast, because the child's future has just arrived and can be celebrated. A family that cannot afford it does not hold one, and the child notices, and a surprising number of Accord operatives have that particular non-event somewhere in their fracture record. The Archives has never studied this. Three Prime Instructors have asked it to.

### IV · Flourishing

**Tier 4 · Adept · Grade B · Ceiling 275 · Levels 76 to 100**

**Catalyst** · formation of the first genuine Spirit Axis, a bond with another soul that carries mechanical weight in the Harmonics (the stats for pulling in power and holding it in tune)

***The one they tell*** · Hob Tarrant and Mags Plowright were witnessed to each other in Witness Wax at Stoneybridge, a drover and a camp cook, and the pair of them held the Ford of Ell against a sixty-man raid with a goad, a cleaver and a great deal of swearing, their two charcoal auras, his with a rumor of rust in it and hers of amber, running together into a single roaring hoop that the minstrels got almost right. Then a fever took her, the plain kind. Hob lived eighteen more years at full power, and for all of them a bright thread of rust leaned off his left shoulder, always north, always toward one hill in the Stoneybridge burying ground, and everybody on the drove roads knew it. Reavers learned to come at him from the south. One day in his sixties they did.

***From inside*** · The noise stops.

Identity settles. The resonance signature that will be recognizably yours for the rest of your life finishes arriving. Techniques that were separate tricks cohere into something with a style to it, and for most practitioners this is the most physically pleasant experience of their lives, a warmth low in the chest that does not go away for weeks and that nobody who has had it describes well.

And it cannot be reached alone.
> The Catalyst is a Spirit Axis. Which means every Flourished practitioner in the four quarters, every certified Adept, every guild officer, every Baron's champion and every hedge-mage who ever stabilized, arrived there because somebody mattered to them enough to hold load. **The Stage that stabilizes the self is gated on attaching the self to another person**, permanently, in a way that registers on an instrument.
***From outside*** · Consistency. Their power looks like them. You can hand them a problem they have never seen and predict roughly what they will do to it, and being predictable in that specific way is the difference between a talented person and a useful one.

What is not visible from outside is that an Axis is a load-bearing member. The architecture built on it has to stand up whether or not the person is still alive to stand under it. Every Prime Instructor has seen what a Flourished practitioner does the year their Axis dies. The power holds. The power is entirely fine, and it is still shaped exactly like a bond to somebody who is not there.

***What the folk call it*** · **Taking root.** The anchoring. A bond-witness is held and somebody attests, and the parish keeps the roll in Witness Wax, and parish witness rolls are the oldest continuous documents in most of the western quarter. A village can tell you who was witnessed to whom nine generations back and cannot tell you who held the land.
- **The line the Accord has never published**
  Guild halls know the Catalyst. Which means the fastest way to Flourish a cohort is to put a cohort through something together, at risk, in circumstances that make them matter to each other, and every Prime Instructor has known this since before there was an Accord.

  What is published is the training casualty figure. The Strategic Division reviews it annually and has never once moved to reduce it.

## The Breaking Band: Band II · Awakened

*Stages V through VII. The Codex calls this the completion of the inner phase. The instructors call it the years you find out.*

### V · Splintering

**Tier 5 · Expert · Grade B, instability above 320 · Ceiling 350 · Levels 101 to 150**

**Catalyst** · surviving contact with an entity or force more than two full Tiers above the practitioner's ceiling. Survival is the whole requirement, and defeating the thing adds nothing.

***The one they tell*** · A Well-spawn drake at Cold Ferry took Yrsa Kolbeinsdottir across the chest with its tail when she was a Stage IV caravan guard with nine good years behind her, and she went through the side of a barge and lived. The Crystal broke where she was already broken, which happened to be a sister. For the twenty years of her loud and highly profitable career after that, every blow she threw went true until somebody said the name, whereupon hairline veins of blue ran through her aura and it stuttered like a struck lamp. Every rival in the north learned it. Whole companies would stand in line abreast bawling **"HELGA! HOW'S HELGA?"** across a field, at odds posted in advance, and Yrsa, who was nobody's fool, took to fighting with tallow in her ears and killing the loudest one first.

***From inside*** · The architecture that has held you since Murmuring reaches the limit of what it can be and has to break to become larger. There is no version where it does not break. The only question anybody has ever been able to influence is where.
> It fractures along emotional lines. The break runs past the injury and past the encounter that triggered it, and follows whatever the soul was already carrying badly. A practitioner who goes into Splintering with an unresolved thing comes out of it with that thing structurally present in their Crystal, permanently, in a way the coil (the assay instrument that reads a Crystal) reads.
>
> **Splintering takes a flaw the soul already had and promotes it to a load path.**
Practitioners who fail do not die, mostly. They lose Sub-Stat (the finer stats beneath each main stat) progress and they sit at the ceiling of Stage IV for the rest of their working lives, and the four quarters are full of them, and they are the reason Tier Four is as crowded as it is.

***From outside*** · A person who was reliable stops being reliable. Techniques that behaved for six years behave differently, or behave the same and cost more, or work perfectly and leave the practitioner shaking. The hall watches for it and the hall cannot do anything about it, and the honest instructors say so.

***What the folk call it*** · **The cracking.** The bad year. In the eastern quarter, **going to the wall**. There is no folk remedy, which is itself notable in a body of practice that has a remedy for everything, and the absence is the folk tradition being accurate about something the Accord's literature is not.
> **Trade note** · **Splinter Ration** leaves the fracture coming and introduces a controlled stress-riser so the Crystal breaks at the riser rather than wherever the trauma happened to sit. That is the entire product, it is reserved to Tier Five, and bonded practitioners receive it while village talent does not. More of who a person becomes is decided by that supply line than by anything they will ever choose.

### VI · Glory

**Tier 5 · Expert · Grade A · Ceiling 400 · Levels 151 to 175**

**Catalyst** · willingly accepting genuine soul-cost to preserve something valued above the practitioner's own structural integrity

***The one they tell*** · When the Yellow Dike went at Lanjiang, Tsao Qinglan of the Red Abacus Hall stood in the breach with forty outer disciples behind her and the river coming, and she named her price aloud, as her Hall's rules require of anything expensive. She spent the part of her Crystal that knew how to be happy, or so the Hall's ledger enters it, and nobody has found another way to read the entry. The water stopped at her shins in a brown wall three stories high, *GWOOOOSH*, held up on nothing but deep teal light, for as long as it took the lower city to climb the hill. For the rest of her life every flame in any room she entered leaned toward her, every candle and cook-fire, and each year Lanjiang hung its streets in silk and carried her through them under ten thousand paper lanterns. She attended, every year, and never once smiled at it.

***From inside*** · The fractures have become features. What was damage the year before is now architecture, and the practitioner can feel the difference, and the feeling is unaccountably good in a way that troubles the more thoughtful of them for years afterward.

The Catalyst is a decision, and it is the first Catalyst in the ladder that is. Murmuring happens to you. Welling happens to you. Ascension is earned but arrives sideways. Flourishing requires another person. Glory requires that you look at something that will cost you structurally and choose it anyway, knowing the price, in advance, in cold blood.
> Which means every A-Grade practitioner in the four quarters has, at some point, deliberately damaged themselves for something. The Accord's own literature describes this as the culture of duty. The instructors describe it as the year they learn what they will pay for.
***From outside*** · The Aura, a light in the practitioner's own color that flares brighter as they push and is now plain to any civilian, starts doing things to rooms, gently at first. The air moves differently near them, small flames lean, animals behave, and people who have never heard of Aether Class (how much of a soul's power survives the trip out through the Shell) find themselves standing slightly further away without deciding to. The Echo field forms here. This is the Stage at which a practitioner stops being a person with abilities and starts being a presence, and most of them handle it badly for a year or two.

***What the folk call it*** · **Being lit.** He is lit up. In the Old World temple registers the term is **kindled**, and the ecclesiastical usage and the tavern usage have not diverged in six hundred years, which the Archives finds remarkable and has not explained.

### VII · Refraction

**Tier 5 · Expert · Grade A with late push toward S · Ceiling 475 · Levels 176 to 200**

**Catalyst** · first Domain seed activation, however fragile or small. Projected fields become coherent.

***The one they tell*** · Gaffer Tom Underhay of the Salt Road Foot had sat at Level 199 for twenty-two years and told everyone about it. He was fifty-seven, with a wall eye and a pension dispute, and on the night of the Mule and Lantern brawl somebody told him the Refraction wall was for men with better fathers. He stood up, and for four seconds every pint in that taproom fell upward. *Bloop.* There were sixty witnesses. They describe it wrongly, each in exactly the same way, and all of them will swear the beer turned green. The Foot carried him the whole length of the garrison road on a door, with a band in front. Next morning he looked inside his own Crystal, found out what his fracture record had to say about his father, and did not speak to anyone for a year.

***From inside*** · The soul sees itself from outside.

The Crystal that has been the unconscious medium of everything you have ever done becomes an object you can look at, and looking at it changes it, and knowing that it changes it changes it again. Gnosis (the stat of perception and understanding) undergoes a qualitative jump here that no other Stage produces, and practitioners in Refraction are notoriously difficult to be around because they have just been handed the ability to examine their own architecture and have not yet learned to stop.

The trouble is that self-examination at this depth reveals the fracture record. Everything Splintering promoted to a load path is now visible, labeled, and impossible to un-see.

***From outside*** · A Domain seed. Small, fragile, often lasting seconds at its first firing, and the first genuinely uncanny thing most people will ever witness, because a Domain seed is a piece of the world briefly agreeing to different terms. Bystanders describe it wrongly and consistently, and the consistency of the wrongness is the best evidence Archives has that they are describing something real.
> **The wall at two hundred.** Band II ends at Level 200 and the Threshold that opens Band III is Refraction. A practitioner who cannot produce a Domain seed does not slow down. The counter halts and the pressure keeps arriving, accruing as **Residual Strain**, degrading gain and raising Overchannel (forcing more through the Crystal than it can carry) and Backlash (a working's force turning on its maker) risk continuously, and it does not stop building.
>
> This is where most working careers end, in a long loud stall in the high hundred-and-nineties: strong, competent, slowly becoming more dangerous to themselves than to anything they are pointed at, telling anyone who will buy the next round exactly who cheated them, and dying of something else first. **Every veteran corps in the four quarters is largely composed of these people,** and their bitterness is a structural feature of the Accord's officer class rather than a matter of temperament.
***What the folk call it*** · **The double sight.** Twice-eyed. In the eastern quarter, a Refracted practitioner is said to have **swallowed their own reflection**, which is a considerably better description than the Codex manages in the same number of words.

## Sovereign: Band III

*Stages VIII through X. The Codex says a practitioner at this Band exercises genuine metaphysical authority. What it does not say is that authority is a relationship, and a relationship has two parties.*

### VIII · Transcendence

**Tier 6 · Master · Grade S · Ceiling 550 · Levels 201 to 250**

**Catalyst** · resolving a core internal contradiction documented in the Crystal's fracture record. A wound carried as structural damage and consciously processed into architecture. The first true Domain forms.

***The one they tell*** · The Gate of Asahina Shigeko is the reason the Free Companies will still not march past a shut door at dusk. She had left a gate unbarred behind her when she was twenty-six, and a household died of it, and she carried that wound for forty years until she stopped on the Kaname road one autumn evening, with the maples going scarlet over her head and six hundred armored men coming up the hill, and put her whole weight on it. *Kaerazu no Mon*, she said, almost too low to hear: the Gate of No Returning, and the word went down the column from mouth to mouth. The road became a place where every door behind you was the one you should have shut. Six hundred men walked into it. They walked out three weeks later in their own home villages, with their armor rusted through and their swords still in their hands, and in sixty years not one of them could tell you what they had been so frightened of, or get through a doorway without looking back.

***From inside*** · The world is read as symbol, and this is less mystical than it sounds and more disorienting. Objects retain their weight and gain a second weight. A door is a door and is also a threshold, and both facts are operative, and the practitioner has to keep functioning while holding them.

The Catalyst is the hardest in the ladder and the only one that cannot be arranged. Refraction showed the practitioner their fracture record. Transcendence requires them to take one of the wounds in it, the load-bearing one, the one Splintering promoted, and consciously make it structure. **The wound stays a wound, unhealed and unreleased, and it is made to hold weight on purpose.**

Practitioners fail at this for decades. There is no shortage of S-Grade-capable souls sitting at the Stage VII ceiling in comfortable positions who simply never did the work, and the halls are polite about it, and everybody knows.

***From outside*** · A Domain, a true one, well past the seed: a standing region of the world operating on the practitioner's terms while they are present in it, and the first time most people encounter one they are frightened in a way they cannot afterwards justify.

***What the folk call it*** · Folklore thins here, because folklore is what a community builds around a thing it lives beside, and communities do not live beside Stage VIII. What exists instead is story. Named individuals, remembered feats, and the beginnings of the process by which a person becomes a figure. The Accord's Lattice Ledger and the ballad tradition begin describing the same people at roughly this Stage and stop agreeing almost immediately.

### IX · Invocation

**Tier 6 · Master · Grade S with late push toward SS · Ceiling 625 · Levels 251 to 275**

**Catalyst** · calling on Wellsprings, Titans, or Archonic law as co-authors of expression. The Domain consolidates into a Realm capable of holding other Domains.

***The one they tell*** · At the siege of Dornhold the Ember Deacon Clement Tull asked. That is how the song puts it, and for once the song is precise. The Duke's marshal had opened a Domain over the walls, an honest thing of iron-gray weight that had been breaking ladders for a month, and Tull knelt in the ditch in a scorched orange cope and called on the Wellspring by its name, and something that was not Tull answered.

*GOOOOOOOM.*

His field went round the marshal's field and closed, and the marshal's Domain hung inside his like a fly in amber, still running, with the marshal in it, and a second color came up at the edge of the orange that nobody in the camp could look at for long. The town surrendered by noon. Tull asked the fire to stop, and the Wellspring, which had preferences of its own, declined. Dornhold burned for nineteen years. Pilgrims came to watch, and the Strategic Division opened a file.

***From inside*** · You stop working alone.

Every technique before this was the practitioner's Crystal doing something. From Invocation, the practitioner asks, and something larger answers, and the answering is literal, unreliable and expensive. The Wellspring is a co-author. Co-authors have their own preferences and they exercise them.
> The recorded psychological pattern is the most consistent in the ladder. Practitioners entering Invocation describe an enormous relief followed within a year or two by something considerably worse, as they work out that the relief was the sensation of no longer being solely responsible for their own output, and that the thing they handed responsibility to did not sign anything.
***From outside*** · A Realm. The Domain has become capable of holding other Domains inside it, which is the point at which Stage IX practitioners become a strategic rather than a tactical consideration, and the point at which the Strategic Division starts keeping a file.

***What the folk call it*** · Nothing. Ordinary people do not have a word for Stage IX and have never needed one. The ecclesiastical traditions have several and they disagree with each other and with the Accord, and the disagreement is four hundred years old and is really an argument about whether the Wellspring answering constitutes divine favor or merely a lawful process, which is the same argument the Holy See and the Codex have been having about everything since Year Zero.

### X · Realization

**Tier 6 · Master · Grade SS · Ceiling 725 · Levels 276 to 300**

**Catalyst** · truth as lived consequence. The world answers back. Pacts, artifacts and law imprint on terrain. The Domain becomes a reality the Continuum (the lawful fabric of reality that workings write into) must accommodate.

***The one they tell*** · A Stage X notary named Odo Pennyfather sat on one flat stone at Cray Hollow for thirty-six years witnessing bargains, because he liked the view. He has been dead for two centuries and nobody has told a successful lie on that stone since. Liars get a nosebleed, *tp, tp, tp*, onto the rock, and the rock keeps the stain. Cattle dealers pay two silver marks to close on it, a man at the foot of the path sells handkerchiefs, and three kings have sent their regrets. A Moto envoy once stood there for a whole afternoon saying only true things about the weather and was generally held to have won. The last man who tried to swear away a murder on it bled out through the nose before the clerk had finished the line.

***From inside*** · The practitioner stops being the party that has to be accommodated.

At Realization the Continuum adjusts, a little, locally and grudgingly and within limits that are extremely specific, but the direction of the accommodation has reversed and it never reverses back. What the practitioner is now costs the world something to permit, and the world permits it.

The lived experience of this is reported as a species of loneliness that has no good analogue lower on the ladder, and practitioners who describe it well tend to describe it in terms of weather. You are no longer standing in it. You are part of what other people are standing in.

***From outside*** · Terrain changes. Pacts hold in the ground. Artifacts made at Realization do not need maintaining. Regions where a Stage X practitioner spent a long time keep a set that outlasts them by generations, and half the sites the Measurewrights (trained assayers who measure power and sites) cannot attribute in the Bearing and the Holding are almost certainly this and cannot be proven to be.

***What the folk call it*** · **The place remembers him.** Every quarter has a version and the versions are the same version. This is the point at which a practitioner stops generating stories about themselves and starts generating stories about a location, which is how you can tell, from the shape of a local legend, roughly what Stage the person at the bottom of it reached.

## Mythic: Band IV

*Stages XI and XII. Two Stages that are one motion: the deliberate approach to annihilation, and what radiates outward from a soul that came back.*

### XI · Dissonance

**Tier 7 · Grandmaster · Grade SS, fracture risk above 700 · Ceiling 750 with structural instability · Levels 301 to 350**

**Catalyst** · deliberately entering contact with a force or truth capable of ending the practitioner's existence and remaining coherent long enough to choose how they meet it

***The one they tell*** · The Harrow Road rippled for a week, the summer Grandmaster Reva Stroud stopped being able to decide. Three villages lay flat behind her with the thatch all combed one way, and the air around her sang in standing bands you could see, *wmmm, wmmm, wmmm*, the color of a furnace mouth and brighter than anybody wanted. Two Enforcement squads went up that road and came back in baskets. Sergeant Pim Ottery went third, alone, in his second-best boots, carrying a covered bowl of mutton and barley, because that is the first line of the protocol. He set it down twelve paces off and told her she looked thin. She ate. It cost him both eardrums and the use of his left side, and for the next thirty years he never once paid for a drink in any town on that road.

***From inside*** · Splintering was survival. Dissonance is chosen.

The practitioner walks toward something that can end them, arrives, remains coherent, and chooses their manner of meeting it. That is the whole Catalyst and every word of it is load-bearing. Survival will not satisfy it and victory will not satisfy it: the Crystal wants a soul that **remains coherent long enough to choose.**

Power and identity clash here, openly, in the Crystal, and the clash is the Stage. Practitioners trapped in Dissonance rather than passing through it are the most dangerous individuals in the four quarters by a wide margin, because they have SS-Grade output organized around an unresolved contradiction and no structural reason to stop.

***From outside*** · Instability that reads on any instrument and is visible to anyone in the room. The Enforcement Division has a standing protocol for a suspected Dissonance-trapped practitioner and the protocol's first line is to establish whether the practitioner has eaten.
> **The architectural note.** Souls that pass through Dissonance rather than around it reach the next Stage fractured and denser than the clean path produces. **Overflow, Overchannel and Persistence reach their true ceiling in no other way.** The fractures are features. Anyone who tells an ambitious practitioner otherwise is either being kind or has an interest in them not trying.

### XII · Emanation

**Tier 7 · Grandmaster · Grade SSS · Ceiling 950 · Levels 351 to 400**

**Catalyst** · emerging from Dissonance with a Crystal denser and cleaner than before entry. The fractures must have become features.

***The one they tell*** · When the chandler's wife at Vall told the Accord's Envoy, Grandmaster Cuthbert Ashe, that she would not be leaving her husband, he took it well. He was courteous about it, he went on with his work, and for the next three years nobody in the whole Vall valley slept past the fourth hour. Milk turned in the pail. Couples forty years married screamed at each other across the salt. Four hundred people walked out of the valley without being able to tell you why, and a good many others walked into the river. She changed her mind in the spring of the fourth year, for reasons she called her own, and that autumn the pears came in so heavy that the boughs tore off the trees in great gold heaps and every cow in the valley dropped twins. It has been Sweetmiss on every map since, and the district holds a formal letter from the Accord stating that no posting was affected.

***From inside*** · Nothing has to be held any more.

Traits that required maintenance become local physics. The Domain no longer needs sustaining. Passive miracles leak out of the practitioner without intention and occasionally without their knowledge, and the reports of Stage XII practitioners discovering what they have been doing to a district by being in it are consistent and slightly comic and entirely dark.

***From outside*** · The room is different because they are in it, and stays different for a while after they leave. Crops. Weather. Sleep. Whether arguments in a household escalate. Emanation is the Stage at which a practitioner's mood becomes an environmental condition, and the Accord's Envoy postings are assigned with this in mind and are never explained in those terms to the districts receiving them.

***What the folk call it*** · **A good country. A bad country.** People move toward and away from Stage XII practitioners without knowing they are doing it, and settlement patterns in three quarters show it clearly, and no polity has ever admitted planning around it.

## Absolute: Band V

*Stages XIII and XIV. The furthest reach of canonical mortal coherence, and the Stage that does not end.*

### XIII · Principality

**Tier 8 · Archmaster · Grade X · Ceiling 1,200 · Levels 401 to 450**

**Catalyst** · Domain coherence sufficient for the Continuum to formally register a law the practitioner embodies. First World Spirit threshold.

***The one they tell*** · The law is *the strong shall carry the weak*, and it lives on the Qian plain whether or not Archmaster Liu Zhenhe does. He embodied it for two hundred years and the Continuum entered it. On that plain a bull will take a lame calf across its shoulders and an oak will lean to hold a sapling out of the wind, and when the Ninefold Banner marched in, fourteen thousand strong in lacquer red, every man who struck down an enemy found the man across his back and could not set him down. *Hff.* They found the column a month later, still on foot and still walking, each soldier carrying another soldier, a great many of them dead.

***From inside*** · You are registered.

The Continuum has entered something. A law, singular, that the practitioner embodies and that now has standing independent of them. Practitioners at Principality report that their own doctrine has stopped being a position they hold and become a condition they occupy, and that the difference is not one they can explain to anyone below Stage XI.

Biomes carry doctrine here. The land near a Stage XIII practitioner takes more than a set: **it takes an argument.**

***From outside*** · A local force of nature with an opinion. This is the first World Spirit threshold and the beginning of the territory the Accord's Tier Eight exists to describe, and the Concord Lattice records these individuals differently, in a register most Guildmasters will never be shown.

***What the folk call it*** · They are worshipped, mostly, or feared in the specific manner reserved for weather that has taken an interest. The Holy See's position on Stage XIII individuals has been revised four times in six centuries and the revisions track, exactly, whether the individual in question was in the Old World at the time.

### XIV · Zenith

**Tier 8 · Archmaster · Grade EX · Ceiling 1,500 · Levels 451 to 500**

**Catalyst** · the practitioner's accumulated doctrine becomes recognizable to a Hollow Throne, and the Throne resonates. The transition comes by recognition, and no breakthrough reaches it.

***The one they tell*** · The Guild sent twelve Measurewrights up Mount Skarn to assay a Zenith, with assay forks, water clocks and a year's worth of good sealed paper, under a banner and with a band as far as the snow line. They were gone three years. Eleven brought back careful accounts of the attempt. The twelfth brought back a recipe for plum jam, which the Archives has filed with the others and which a majority of the Division now considers the most accurate of them. Their forks came down still ringing, *nnnnnnnnnnnn*, thin and sweet, though nobody has struck them since. They are ringing now in a glass case on the Upper Ring at Stannvaard, where children are brought to hear them and taken away again quite quickly.

***From inside*** · Something that was already there notices.

Every Threshold below this was crossed. Zenith is conferred, and the conferring party is a **Hollow Throne**, and the practitioner does not petition and cannot prepare beyond having become, over an entire life, a thing shaped like the recognition.

Reality acclimates. Story becomes physics. Speed stops being assessable by conventional metrics, because movement has ceased to be the relevant variable, and the Continuum's ordinary framework requires recalibration to register their presence as a phenomenon rather than as an event.

***From outside*** · Full World Spirit. There is no useful description from outside because there is no outside available to describe it from, and every attempt in the record is an account of the attempt.
> **The Stage that does not end.** Zenith has no ceiling to break through. It is the state of a soul that has reached the upper boundary of mortal architecture and remains there, pressing against what it has not yet become, indefinitely, with no Threshold above it that a level event could reach. Everything past this point is the Apotheosis approach, and progression is the wrong word for the approach, which is a different kind of thing entirely, and the Codex says so in one line and then stops.

## The Apotheosis Approach

*Stages XV and XVI lie beyond the canonical fourteen. The Accord folds both into a single Tier and calls it Paragon, because the Codex declines to number them and a bureaucracy cannot administer a blank.*

### XV · Revelation

**Tier 9 · Paragon · Grade EX+ · Ceiling 2,200**

**Catalyst** · genuine exchange with an Archon or Titan at a level where the practitioner is a participant rather than a witness or a casualty

***The one they tell*** · Two practitioners are on the record at this Stage, and every minstrel from the Chartered Arc to the southern pans has a song about one or the other. The songs run to a great many verses, in several tongues, with good tunes and excellent fights. None of them has ever been heard past the second verse. Nobody decides to stop. The singer looks up, the room has gone very warm, and everyone present finds they would rather be discussing something else.

The distinction in the Catalyst is the entire Stage. Souls meet Archons constantly, in the sense that weather meets a mountain. Revelation requires exchange, both ways, with the Crystal architecture dense enough to hold Archon-grade pressure without collapsing while it happens.

The practitioners who reach it describe the pressure and decline to describe the exchange. This has been true of every recorded instance and the Archives has stopped asking.

### XVI · Apex

**Perpetual state · Uncapped**

**Catalyst** · holding a Principle in the Soul Crystal without fracturing, even momentarily, even imperfectly

***The one they tell*** · The house of Tolly and Daughters on the Exchange Stair at Stannvaard has posted the same line for four hundred years, a thousand to one that somebody reaches Apex by next midsummer, and it takes a few coppers on it every season from drunks and one quiet Measurewright. It has never paid out. One spring a runner brought the family a rumor, and they closed the shop, settled every debt in the city and were gone by sundown. They came back ten years later, reopened on the same stair at the same odds, and have never said what they heard.

A Principle is a truth normally reserved for beings that have completed Apotheosis. Apex is the state of a Crystal dense enough with accumulated doctrine to contain one, briefly, badly, and not come apart.

It is perpetual. It does not resolve into anything. It is a soul pressing forever against what it has not yet become, and nobody should mistake the Codex's refusal to place a ceiling on it for humility. **The Codex has measured everything else in this document to three figures.**

## Appendix A: The Seat

> *"Seated clean, and you can hear it. Seated rough, and so can everybody else."*
Each Threshold is a structural reorganization, and whatever is in the Crystal at the moment of reorganization is built into what comes out. The residue of that is called **the seat**, from the joiner's word, because the trade took its vocabulary from people who fit things into other things for a living and had no patience for metaphysics.

A **clean seat** is carry that integrated evenly. The signature reads as one thing. Output is consistent, Traits hold, techniques behave the same way in the fourth hour as in the first.

A **rough seat** is carry from four classes and nine sources, none of it fully integrated, the signature reading as a chord with a wrong note in the middle of it. Rough-seated practitioners are not weaker. Several of the most feared individuals in the four quarters are catastrophically rough-seated and that is precisely why nobody can predict them.
> **What decides a seat.** A Threshold is a melt, and a melt does not cool into order unaided. Something must survive the heat for the new lattice to grow around, and the only structures in a Crystal that do are **Traits**, because a law the Continuum has already accepted as reliable does not stop being reliable at temperature.
>
> Seeds that agree cool clean. Seeds that contradict each other cool rough, and the lattice keeps the argument. **No adequate seed at all and the melt cools amorphous:** the Stage does not advance, Clarity and Compression fall, a Trait is commonly lost outright, and the halls record a **Dead Set**. The trade word for the result is **slag**.
>
> Carry, Drafts and rendered stock work by supplying seed the soul did not produce. That is the whole of what they do, it is real, and it is the reason a purchased seat reads differently on the coil from an earned one.
The coil reads a seat in under a minute, and everything below follows from that.

### What the reading is used for

**Certification** · Examinations read the seat before they read output, and a rough seat caps certifiable rank at Tier Four regardless of demonstrated capability. The stated reason is structural safety and the stated reason is true. The Accord has never addressed the other thing that is also true.

**Marriage** · Negotiations between the great houses now involve a coil reading as routinely as a survey of the land, and for the same reason, since both are asking what the children will inherit.

**Everything else** · Assay houses will read a seat for anyone with a silver mark. They are the only institution in the four quarters that will tell a person the truth about themselves for a fixed fee and no further comment.

### The tells, for those who cannot afford a coil

*Folk diagnosis. Unreliable in the individual case, correct often enough to have survived four centuries, and it has ended marriages.*

| Tell | What it reads |
|---|---|
| **Cold hands that never warm** | Class III carry off cold-blooded stock, poorly integrated. Holds in roughly two cases out of three. The exceptions are people with bad circulation. |
| **The flicker** | Traits coming and going without pattern. This one has left folklore behind: it is documented Essence Starvation behavior in an architecture carrying more unintegrated carry than its Capacity supports, and the folk reading and the clinical reading agree exactly. |
| **Draft-mark** | Faint discoloration in the sclera or under the nailbeds, keyed to the rendered source. Renderers name the beast from the color with a reliability that embarrasses the Archives, and nobody has taught it to anyone who did not grow up in a rendering house. |
| **The appetite** | Class III carry off a predator seats an appetite that is not yours. It leaves a man no more of a killer than he was and makes him hungry in a way he cannot locate and did not ask for, and the eastern rendering districts have a word for it that the Accord's translators decline to render into the common tongue. |

### What the coil is actually reading

A highborn heir tempered on entered, warranted, three-mark stock is seated clean, and the visible consequence is that their power is boring. Legible, consistent, well-behaved and boring, and the halls certify it to Tier Seven without a raised eyebrow because it will never surprise anybody.

A gutter-seated practitioner out of an eastern district is unpredictable, occasionally extraordinary, and structurally barred at Tier Four.
> Both are measurements. Neither is a lie. **The instrument is honest and it is reading, with perfect accuracy, what somebody's parents could afford.**

## Appendix B: The Threshold Trade, by Stage

*Which market reaches which Stage, and what that decides.*

| Transition | What the market supplies |
|---|---|
| **I to II** | Sour Iron Filings and Hearth Ward Wax. T1, coppers, available in any market. Nobody's advancement is gated here. This is the last Stage of which that is true. |
| **II to III** | Metering Tonic (T2) and Hillcarry Salts (T3). The guild ration begins. A village child reaches Ascension on the hill and the buckets; a bonded child reaches it in a third the time on Hillcarry, and both arrive, and only one of them arrives at fifteen. |
| **III to IV** | Ascension Ration, Accord Standard (T4). Issued at cost against future commission. The Catalyst is a Spirit Axis and cannot be bought, so what the ration buys is Flourishing's quantitative floor, and what the Accord buys is the term. |
| **IV to V** | Splinter Ration (T5, reserved). Decides where the fracture runs, and does nothing to stop it coming. This is the single most consequential product in the Standing Index for who a person becomes and it is not available to anybody outside the Accord. |
| **V to VI** | **Nothing.** There is no product for Glory. The Catalyst is a decision to accept soul-cost for something, and no house has ever found a way to sell that, though four have tried and the attempts are in the Striking record. |
| **VI to VII** | Refraction Draught (T6) and Domain Seed Vitrifier (T6). Both reserved. Both Class IV. From here upward, advancement runs on Crystal Draw (Class IV stock, rendered from something that carried a Soul Crystal), and everybody in the trade knows what that means and the public literature does not say it. |
| **VII to X** | Class IV throughout, reserved to Tier Six and above, procured through channels the Bench grades and does not attribute. The arithmetic between lawful supply and circulating volume has never balanced and no Division has been assigned to reconcile it. |
| **X and beyond** | There is no market. Dissonance is walked into, Emanation is emerged from, and everything above Principality is conferred rather than achieved. The trade stops at the point where the Continuum starts being the counterparty, which is the only honest thing the trade has ever done. |

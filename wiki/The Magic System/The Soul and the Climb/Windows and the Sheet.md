# Windows and the Sheet

> *"Everyone in this room knows exactly how much they have left, and not one of them can prove it to you. Bet accordingly."*
>
> Bookie's patter, Sealhall arena, on any day of the year

Forty thousand people were on their feet in the bone arena at Sealhall, under the blue and orange banners and the great curved ribs of whatever died there, on the last day of the Waxgate Sweepstakes. Dagny Thorn, known from the arena steps to the salt road as the Bad Debt, had just taken a shoulder-high hammer-swing from Seo Min-ho that lifted her clean off the sand. It broke her right collarbone so badly that the end stood up under the skin like a tent peg and her arm hung off it dead to the fingertips. She got up with her mouth full of blood. This is what she was looking at, and it was for her eyes alone:

```
[Hit: right collarbone broken. Arm down. Vitality 44%]
[Reserve: 3% ▓░░░░░░░░░]
[Skill: Taking a Punch, B to A]
```

She spat a tooth, spread her one good arm to the whole arena, and bellowed.

**"HALF MY RESERVE LEFT, MIN-HO! HOW'S YOURS?"**

Min-ho's own window said thirty percent, and his left eye was swollen shut and full of blood. He looked her over with the eye he had. His Gnosis Perception (the stat of sensing what a soul is) sat a full Grade under her Resilience Ward (the stat that keeps other people out of yours), so all his soul would give him on her was question marks. He saw a fighter still standing, grinning, and roaring about it. He had a wife in Verath. He dropped the hammer and yielded, and the Bad Debt won the Sweepstakes on an arm's length of pure lying, with her color stuttering thin around her ribs in front of everyone who knew to look.

One man did know to look. Up in the fourth tier a Measurewright (one of the guild that keeps the world's tables and argues about them) with an A-Grade Perception had read the whole window off her before she opened her mouth, the moment the swing landed, and had gone down to the rail and quietly doubled his money. The Accord's inquiry ran for three months and found nothing, because shouting a number at a man is no crime and no one had asked her to swear to it. That is the window in one afternoon: **a private report, a public lie, a stronger eye in the stands, and a very great deal of money riding on the gap.**

## What a Window Is

**A window is the soul reporting its numbers to its owner, in its owner's own tongue, and no one else can see it.** The Soul Crystal is the soul, and the Continuum (the whole lawful order of reality) measures it. The report follows from those two facts, or so the Measurewrights will tell you for the price of a drink. It comes when called and it comes unasked, in the middle of a fight, the moment there is something to report. A Moto reads hers in Moto, a Kharven drover reads his in the language he swears in, and a Marrowkin window puts the doer at the front of every line and the tally at the back, as that language has always done.

**Everyone has one, the unwoken included.** A baker has a Baking skill and a little sheet. Waking (the opening of the Crystal, which turns a soul into a practitioner) opens the rest of it.

Nobody else can see it, and the owner cannot show it. No trick of light, no shared Domain and no amount of pointing will put your window in front of another person's eyes. All anyone can see is your eyes going to the middle distance, and every people has its own slang for that. A Kharven is *checking the bill*. A Marrowkin *takes the tally*. Accord hands *read their own file*. Soldiers from the Ochre Fields say a man is *counting his brothers*, and they will fight you if you ask why.

The word people use for the whole of what it shows is **the sheet**.

## What It Shows, and What It Hides

**A window shows Grades, percentages, bars and levels.** A normal look gives the full sheet:

```
[Mun Seo-yeon · Level 170 · Stage VI, Glory · Path: Spirit]
[Human, Sum-gol lineage]
[Class: Auditor · Tier III, Resonant]
[Ardency B · Vitality D · Gnosis C · Dexterity C]
[Harmonics D · Resilience E · Tempering E · Dominion F]
[Trait: Every Column Balances · Level 44]
[Skills: Bookkeeping A · Footwork B · Appraisal C · Knife D]
[Blessings: none]
[Reserve: 58%]
```

From the top: name, [[The Magic System/Fracture of Worlds — The Living System/I. Levels, Experience and Stat Points (Parts One–Three)|Level]], [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Sixteen Stages|Stage]] and [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/The Three Paths Routing, Recognition and the Gate|Path]], which says which of the soul's three layers the Crystal leans on: the Aether Shell in the Physical World for Body, the Essence Core on the Soul Plane for Spirit, the Attraction Layer on the Plane of Fate for Fate. A soul on two Paths shows both, the one behind second (`Path: Body, Spirit`), and a Path only studied shows nowhere. Then race and lineage, which shape base stats and what comes easily. Then the [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/Aether Classes|class and its tier]], the Eight Primaries with their Grades, each [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/Skills and Trait Levels|Trait with its level and each skill with its Grade]], the blessings, each on its own line, and the reserve. A Sub-Stat (one of the eight finer measures inside each Primary) appears when it is used or when it moves, and the full sixty-four remain there to study for anyone with the patience. Vows, crew bonds and contracts get their own lines when the owner has them.

**A commoner's window is small and useful.** It holds a Level, a few trade skills and her health, with no Essence and no class:

```
[Hanne Oster · Human · Level 6]
[Health: good · Back: complaining]
[Skills: Baking B · Sums E · Getting Up Early C]
```

Hanne Oster has read that every morning for forty years, and she reads it the way you would read the weather.

**It hides a good deal, and the list is worth learning by heart.**

- **EU, AU/s and η.** Essence Units, the output rate and the efficiency figure, the assayers' sums set out in [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/Tier Grade, Bands & the Aether Shell|Tier Grade, Bands & the Aether Shell]]. The owner gets the bar, the percentage and the shake in their own hands.
- **Raw stat values.** Letters only. A Measurewright can cost your sheet in numbers with a coil and a fee. Your own soul has never told you that you are a 288.
- **Any level on a technique.** Techniques show no level and take a bigger name when they jump.
- **Other people.** Reading anyone else takes Gnosis (below), and the result is your own soul's account of them, wrong in its own ways.
- **Why.** It reports the blow after the blow. It has never warned anyone of anything, and it does not explain itself.
- **The truth of a blessing.** The giver wrote that line.

**The body comes first and the window follows.** The tells on the body have always arrived ahead of the report: at half the reserve the breath shortens and the aura dims, at a quarter the hands shake and the color thins, at a tenth the aura flickers and the Trait stutters. An old teacher will tell you to fight your lungs and read your window afterward, and the practitioners who live longest take the advice.

## In a Fight

Windows come often and in detail once blows land, in short blocks, for damage, skill gains and reserve. Each block follows the thing it reports. Here is the Clerk's evening at the Harl Cross market, from the middle of it:

```
[Left ribs: two cracked · Vitality holding]
[Skill rising: Footwork B, 91%]
[Reserve: 41%]
```

A sequence off a sharper afternoon, read in order by a mid-rank fighter with a bad morning coming:

```
[Pressure: Stage VIII above you. Focus down a Grade.]
```

```
[Evaded. Reflex B, in use.]
[Hit: left ear gone at the rim. Vitality 96%]
```

```
[Coat: thin on the left. Reserve: 51% ▓▓▓▓▓░░░░░]
```

```
[Hit: left forearm broken through. Grip gone. Vitality 71%]
[Reserve: 44% ▓▓▓▓░░░░░░]
[Skill: Taking a Punch, C, 88%]
```

**Pressure is felt first.** The felt weight of a stronger soul shows in the room, in the lamp flames, the dogs, the sweat on your lip, and only then does the window report it as a line. A Grandmaster merchant with no fighting Stage has no Pressure at all, and his bookkeeper has been looking for the line for years.

**A wound is a status line, and it stays.** It says what the wound stops you doing, and it sits on the sheet until someone treats it. Healers and healing Drafts fix it for a price, slowly at low Grade and fast at high, so a rich fighter walks off what cripples a poor one for life. A retired spearman at Tollgarth has been reading the same line for thirty-one years:

```
[Right knee: torn. Will not bear weight. Untreated.]
```

**A crew shows.** A real bond shows as a line on the window, shares a little Level gain and puts the crew's bars in the corner of a fight. It cannot be bought or declared. It has to be earned, and betrayal breaks it visibly, on the window of everyone it touches, in the middle of whatever they were doing:

```
[Crew: The Wet Sevens · bond holding]
[Hob ▓▓▓▓▓▓░░░░ · Marit ▓▓▓░░░░░░░ · Dov ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓░]
```

```
[Crew: The Wet Sevens · bond broken: Dov]
```

**A vow shows its terms.** A vow is small Fate, a person binding their own probability, and anyone can make one. Spoken aloud, with or without a witness, it arrives on the window with its bargain and its terms. Breaking it lands at once, by those terms. Each vow counts against the two-Grade cap on boosts. See [[The Magic System/Practice and Institution/Vows and Oaths|Vows and Oaths]]:

```
[Vow: "This hand stays down until he is dead."]
[Bargain: Ardency one Grade up · Left hand dead until then]
[Breach: lands at once]
```

**A contract has a sheet.** A bound spirit or beast has its own window, read by its holder. Its Level grows, its terms show, and it can evolve, or be stolen by contract:

```
[Bound: Old Sorrel, marsh-spirit · Level 41]
[Terms: one drowned thing at each new moon]
[Owed: 2]
```

## At the Thresholds

A window earns its keep at the big moments.

**A Level.** Level moves by danger overcome, so killing something stronger moves it more than a hundred weak kills, and crews have come to knives over who got the kill. The window settles that for the person holding it, and for nobody else.

```
[Level: 61 to 68]
[Trait: Stays Tied, Level 12 to 15]
[Skill: Knots, C to B]
```

**Waking.** The commoner's little sheet opens out. Most souls that wake are taught to, as [[The Magic System/Practice and Institution/The Gate Who May Learn Magic|The Gate]] explains, and the rest get there by crisis or out of a bottle. A Waking Draft is very dear and often kills or warps a child, and most of the rich buy one for their children anyway, while commoners sell themselves into guild debt for it. Those who come through read something like this:

```
[Crystal: open]
[Stage I, Murmuring]
[Reserve: 100% ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓]
[Vitality F · Ardency Hollow · Gnosis F · Dexterity F]
```

**A class.** The soul's offer, with its names, at Glory and at each rung after. See [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/Aether Classes|Aether Classes]].

**A breakthrough.** A lived one is loud and transforms you with the window lighting up, and a bought one is a Draft in a quiet room with a physician standing by. Crisis, seclusion and tribulation all still happen. The sheet shows the same Stage in both cases, and that has started more fights than any other line on it.

```
[STAGE VIII: TRANSCENDENCE]
[Crystal: melted and rebuilt, layer by layer]
[Ceiling lifted · Points to place: ▓▓░░░░░░░░]
[Trait: Banked Coal, Level 41 to 47]
```

```
[Stage VIII, Transcendence]
[Ceiling lifted · Points to place: ▓▓░░░░░░░░]
[Physician: present]
```

Taverns tell the second block as a joke, and the last line is the joke.

**A Release or a Domain.** Calling a [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/Release Forms|Release]] fills the window with its name and what changed, and a timer bar starts to drain. The same goes for a [[The Magic System/The Soul and the Climb/Domains|Domain]], the space where a soul's own law outranks physics. Both are big and loud on the window for the owner alone, whatever the room is doing, and the two-Grade cap stands inside them.

```
[FIRST RELEASE: THE LONG YOKE]
[Ardency: top of band]
[Reserve: 47% ▓▓▓▓▓░░░░░]
[Holds: ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓░░░]
```

```
[DOMAIN: THE WHOLE SHIFT]
[Changed: within the yard the bell decides who may rest]
[Ardency: two Grades up · cap reached]
[Held: ██████░░░░]
```

## How a Blessing Lies

A [[The Magic System/Practice and Institution/Blessings and Curses|blessing]] grants a power out of a person's natural reach, even in Level. **It shows on the sheet as its own line, and that line can lie, because the giver wrote it.** Every other line on a window is the soul reporting on itself. This is the one line on it with an outside author.

King Godric Fenn of Pennycrown, on the night his cousin's army reached the walls, prayed at a spring he had been told to leave alone, and woke with this:

```
[Blessing: Favor of the Spring]
[You will not be unseated.]
```

He wept for joy and rode out, and he was not unseated. Lances broke on him, and he stayed in the saddle with the horse going down under him twice. He rode home victorious and found he could not get off. The horse died in the courtyard eleven days later, and when his men had cut him free of it and set him on the throne to recover, he found he could not get off that either. He has held it for twenty-three years, on a great many cushions, and he has outlived four sons who tried to take it from him. His window still reads *Favor*.

A curse is often a blessing with terms you did not read, or from a giver who does not care what you want. Nobody can tell at first, and some are both. The [[The Magic System/Fracture of Worlds — The Living System/V. Wellsprings, Attraction and Obsession, Magic Integration (Parts Thirteen–Fifteen)|sailor's Favor of the Tide-Mother]] says *You will not drown*, and every sailor who has had that line will tell you it is true. Read it again.

Those who hold Hataraki, the Moto's governing power, can take a blessing back or remove it, which is one reason people are so afraid of them, and the line simply goes:

```
[Blessing: removed]
```

## When Your Own Window Lies

**The soul does not lie to its owner of its own accord,** with the one exception above. **A strong Limina user can.** Limina is the Family of entropy, void and mind, and a strong enough hand there can reach the report itself: show false numbers, hide a line, tell a man at nine percent that he has plenty.

It stays a violation with a cost, on the victim and on the user both, and every realm's law around it is an argument. Gnosis gives a fair counter. A stronger Perception notices that the report has stopped matching the soul, and everyone has the plain counter of their own body, since a window can say eighty percent and your lungs will still tell you the truth. Veterans who have fought Limina users count their breath against their reserve line, and anyone who has lost a friend that way does it for life.

## Reading Other People

**A stronger Gnosis Perception reads a weaker Resilience Ward, by Grade, and everything else is an estimate said aloud.** Your own soul gives you the result as a window, with its gaps showing:

```
[Read: Dagny Thorn · Level 188 · Stage VII]
[Reserve: 3% · Vitality 44%]
```

And the same read from a weaker eye:

```
[Read: ??? · Level ??? · Stage ?]
```

- **The trained talk about it constantly.** Fighters and onlookers rank each other aloud, read shoulders and breath, and argue over it through the whole fight. *Stage IX? Look at his feet. That's a VII in a good coat.* All of it is estimate, and a confident wrong one has won as many fights as a right one.
- **People hide and fake it.** Holding Pressure in, thinning the coat, walking like a tired man and flaring an aura brighter than the reserve behind it can pay for are all arts. Suppression and Feint are real Sub-Stats, and a person with a high Ward can stand in a crowded room and be nobody.
- **Instruments read everyone.** Stand in the coil and the clerk writes down your Stage, Level, Grade, tier and η, which is more than your own window will tell you. It takes the fee and your standing in the coil, and it will not read your blessings or your class name.
- **Monsters sit on the same scale.** A monster has a Level, Grades and a Threat Tier, T1 to T9, and a weak reader gets question marks, usually at the moment it matters most. A famous one carries a name, an epithet and a bounty on top of it. See [[The Bestiary/The Bestiary|The Bestiary]].
- **Items take an Appraisal skill.** An Appraisal skill and an assay instrument read a piece of gear, and a good forger beats a weak appraiser. True relics of the Great Spirit War read as question marks to anyone, and some have opinions about who holds them.

## How People Brag and Lie

Since nobody can see your window, your window is whatever you say it is, until somebody with the right to ask makes you pay for it.

**In the street, say what you like.** Everyone shouts their Level at the brawl and rounds up. A bare claim costs nothing, and the Bad Debt won a Sweepstakes on one.

**On the slate, you pay the bookies' rate.** Odds-men chalk what they can get, and heralds cry their own numbers. The Accord keeps a roll, each realm and sect keeps a list, minstrels get the details wrong in rhyme, and these rankings all disagree. Getting on any of them makes you a target.

**At a hiring, they ask for proof.** A crew or a lord wants more than your word. They will want an assay slip with a seal on it, which costs a silver mark at the house and can be forged, and forging a token is a great crime, which keeps the price of a good forgery high. A sensible employer also hires a reader, and has you lift something heavy.

**At court, you swear.** A proper court makes you swear to what your sheet shows. Lying under a proper [[The Magic System/Practice and Institution/Vows and Oaths|oath]] costs you, because an oath is small Fate, and the cost lands on your own window, where only you can see it. Only an assay can check what you said.

```
[Oath: "Level 140, as my sheet shows." Sworn false.]
[Terms: every chance goes against you until it is unsaid.]
```

Lawyers fight over the exact wording, and they eat well on it. *As my sheet shows* and *as I am* are different oaths. A man with a lying blessing can swear to it in perfect safety, and so can a man whose window has been tampered with. A Bench in the Concord spent four years on whether a false Level sworn on a Limina-touched sheet was perjury, and gave up when both advocates were found to have been touched.

**At home, you push.** Everybody reads their own sheet, and that shapes a society to its roots. Parents push their children from the first Grade. A six-year-old in a Hammerford market will tell you her Sums before her name, because her mother made her practice saying it. Whole villages know whose son has which skill, and matches are made on it, on the parents' word.

## Who Built It

The oldest quarrel in the Measurewrights' hall, carried on at weddings, in long letters and on two occasions with knives.

**The Null School** holds that nobody built it. The Crystal is the soul, the Continuum measures it, and a thing measured and a thing aware of being measured will report. The window is what it feels like to be a soul with a bill. This is the position of most working Measurewrights, chiefly because it requires no further funding.

**The Author School** points at the tongues. Bridge-slang from Kingsbridge turns up on a Kingsbridge docker's window within the year. A report that keeps up with slang, they say, has someone doing the translating, and it uses *complaining* for a back. They have also noticed that blessings prove a window can be written on from outside, and they would like to know who wrote the rest. They have no candidate they will sign their names to, and three they will mention after dark.

**The Mirror School** answers that the slang is the owner's own. The soul speaks to itself in the only words it has, and nothing on the window has ever told anybody something they could not, in principle, have felt. This view is strong among healers. It is weak among anyone who has read a skill they did not know they had.

**The old beings** have been no help. A Measurewright's expedition once asked a very old thing in a very old battlefield who had built the window. After some thought it asked what a window was. Nobody present could show it one.

**Every faith has its own answer, and real wars come of it.** Moto speak to their dead, and many hold that the numbers are their ancestors' reckoning of them, kept as carefully as the family altar. The Marrowkin tally their dead, and they see no mystery here: it is the hold's Tally, entered by the one in whom it is kept. Kharven price theirs, and a Kharven priest will tell you that somebody holds your note, and that you will meet him. Sancta Lux, which claims jurisdiction over oath and status, treats a window as a sworn witness inside you, and will ask you what it said. The Verdant Orcs regard the sheet as rings in a tree, and think it strange that anyone needs it read. And the river towns of Hallenfeld fought the towns of the Golden Hollow for six summers over whether a priest could lawfully require a child to read her window aloud at confirmation, in a war that killed some eleven thousand people and ended with both sides still doing what they had been doing.

The Archives take no position. They note that the window has been asked, by a great many people, in a great many tongues, and it reports only that their reserve is down.

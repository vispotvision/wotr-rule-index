# The Standing and the Title — The Three Names

*On the Three Names Every Practitioner Carries, and How the Last One Is the Only One That Matters*
*Compiled for the Research and Archives Division · companion to The Derivation of Practice, The Guild Accord, and The Works and Days*
> *"The Accord knows what I am. My guild knows what I rank. The man across the room knows what I feel like. He is the one I worry about."*
> — Attributed to a retired Arbiter-Militant, identity withheld at the subject's request

---

## The Three Names

> **Every practitioner carries three names. They acquire them in order, they lose them in reverse order, and only one of them is real.**
**The First Name · what the instrument says.** A string of measurements reading as a sentence in a language designed for ledgers. *Caloria, Kinesia, Stage VIII Transcendence, Grade S, Class III Resonant, Master, η .72.* **This is what the coil says when you stand inside it.**
> *Impersonal, diagnostic and correct. It tells you what the soul does the way a scale tells you what a thing weighs.* **It does not tell you what the weight means to the person carrying it,** and it is therefore the name that matters least in every room that does not contain a coil.
> **Nobody introduces themselves by their instrument profile.** The Accord publishes it. The factor houses price it. *The person it describes has already stopped listening.*
**The Second Name · what the Accord calls you.** Initiate through Paragon. **It travels with the silver token.** It opens doors, closes borders, **and prices your future output on the bond market.**
| Tier | Grade | Stages covered | Title conferred |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · Initiate** | E | Murmuring | — |
| **2 · Apprentice** | D | Welling | — |
| **3 · Journeyman** | C | Ascension | — |
| **4 · Adept** | B | Flourishing | **Concord Agent** |
| **5 · Expert** | B* → A → S | Splintering, Glory, Refraction | **Sealwright**, enforcement specialists |
| **6 · Master** | S → SS | Transcendence, Invocation, Realization | **Warden of Duty** |
| **7 · Grandmaster** | SS* → SSS | Dissonance, Emanation | **Envoy of the Lattice** |
| **8 · Archmaster** | X → EX | Principality, Zenith | **Bearer of the Concord Flame** |
| **9 · Paragon** | EX+ → Unbound | Revelation, Apex | **Chosen of the Codex** |

> **Hollow and F confer no rank.** The ladder begins at E because the Accord does not register what it cannot examine. A Crystal that has opened and not yet been taught is a person the register has no row for, **which is the condition most practitioners in the four quarters will die in.**
>
> *A Paragon is the reference standard, the thing other measurements are checked against.* **It is the name an institution gives a practitioner it can no longer measure**, and the Codex files it as a rank because filing it as a rank is the only alternative to admitting the apparatus has run out of scale.
> *A Grandmaster who has been a Grandmaster for thirty years does not think of herself as a Grandmaster. She thinks of herself as whoever she was before the rank, plus whatever the rank cost her — and the Accord's title for that composite is a word that fits on a credential and misses everything that doesn't.*
> **The Third Name · what the world calls you. This is the one that matters.**
>
> Not assigned by an instrument. Not conferred by an institution. **It is generated by the experience of being near you** — shaped above all by the behavioural signature of your Aether Class Archetype, **which is the thing your soul does to the room when you walk into it.**
>
> *Two practitioners at identical Stage, Grade, Family and Method. The coil gives them the same numbers.* **One walks into a tavern and the fire steadies, the barman relaxes, the dog under the table puts its head down. The other walks into the same tavern and the fire spits, the barman reaches under the counter, and the dog leaves.**
>
> ***The numbers are the same. The souls are not. The world is not reading a coil. The world is reading the weather their soul makes.***
> **It is earned, imposed, or inherited, and it follows you until something bigger replaces it** — or until you die and the name outlives you, *in which case the name was the real thing all along and you were just the body it was wearing for a while.*

## The Weather Your Soul Makes

*The Archetype is not what you do. It is what you are like to be near.* **Some practitioners settle a room. Some charge it. Some empty it.** The Third Name comes from the pattern.
| Archetype | What it feels like nearby | Titles it generates |
|---|---|---|
| **Harmonic** *the room settles* | **Calm.** Not peace, not comfort. *Calm the way a well-built wall is calm: structural, impersonal, and load-bearing.* **Breathing steadies and thoughts clarify** because the field is so coherent it functions as a local standard against which everything else calibrates. Deeply reassuring and faintly dehumanising, because the calm does not care whether you want it | **Names of stability.** The Constant · The Standard · The Foundation · The Pillar · The Still Water. *Named by what they hold rather than what they do, because what they do is hold* |
| **Aberrant** *the room tightens* | **Pressure.** *Pressure the way a scar is pressure — the tightness of tissue that healed under strain and never quite relaxed.* **People feel watched and slightly off-balance,** because survival architecture does not include a setting for at ease. ***Animals react before people do. Children stare.** Veterans recognise it immediately because they carry a version of it themselves* | **Names of damage.** The Scar · The Break · The Wrong One · The Storm · The Crooked · The Remnant. *Frequently unfair and never inaccurate. A warning and a respect paid simultaneously* |
| **Elegiac** *the room remembers* | **Weight.** *Weight the way a cemetery has weight: the accumulated presence of things no longer there, held in place by the fact that someone is still counting them.* **People think of their own losses unprompted —** not because the Essence reaches into their memories but because the field's frequency matches the frequency memory occupies, and proximity tunes the listener | **Names of memory.** The Mourner · The Keeper of Names · The Grey · The Wake · The Candle. *Melancholic and almost never hostile — the combination that makes other practitioners uncomfortable, because discomfort without a threat to point at has no standard response* |
| **Bound** *the room belongs to something else* | **Occupancy.** The feeling that **there is something else in the room that is paying attention through the practitioner rather than to them.** ***The practitioner is the house. The thing they are bound to is the occupant*** | **Names of service.** The Sworn · The Chain · The Vessel · The Instrument · Urion's Scale. *Produces the most politically powerful titles — a practitioner bound to an Archon is addressed by the Archon's name — and the most personally diminishing: a practitioner bound to a debt is addressed by the debt* |
| **Ascendant** *the room kneels* | **Gravity.** *Gravity the way a throne has gravity.* People orient physically, adjust posture, lower voice and make space — **not from deference but from the same instinct that makes a body lean downhill.** ***The field is tilted, and the tilt has a direction, and the direction is the practitioner*** | **Names of elevation.** The Chosen · The Crown · The Anointed · The Radiance · The Throne. *The closest thing to a holy name — a name that invokes what stands behind the person wearing it* |
| **Catalytic** *the room changes* | **Productive discomfort.** *The restlessness of a person who has just realised the thing they were pretending was fine is not fine.* Stalled processes resume and deferred decisions present themselves — **not because the practitioner attacked the equilibrium but because a Catalytic field is an environment in which false equilibria cannot sustain themselves** | **Names of effect.** The Teacher · The Catalyst · The Wake · The Turning · The Opener. *Mu-jin is The Teacher because people near him learn whether they intended to or not. The title does not describe his pedagogy. It describes the experience of being in his presence* |
| **Convergent** *the room holds two truths* | **Ambiguity.** Contradictory impressions held simultaneously without resolution — warm and cold, safe and dangerous. *The field oscillates at a frequency the conscious mind processes as indecision. But the frequency is stable, which means it is not indecision at all. It is a standing wave, and the wave is the practitioner* | **Names of paradox.** The Both · The Contradiction · The Scales · The Twin · The Fulcrum. ***The only Archetype title that routinely contains a conjunction*** |
| **Symphonic** *the room fills* | **Fullness.** *The sensation of standing in a cathedral — not the grandeur but the acoustics, the way sound behaves in a space designed to contain more than one voice.* **The field is a space, not a pressure, and the space has room for the people inside it** | **Names of abundance.** The Chorus · The Many · The Deep · The Orchestra · The Cathedral. *Named for the plurality, because the plurality is what the world experiences* |
| **Echo-Walker** *the room is older than it was* | **Age.** *Age the way an old house has age — felt in the texture of the air and the way the floors remember feet.* The dead are not memories to an Echo-Walker. They are collaborators whose expertise is available on terms the living practitioner negotiates in real time | **Names of ancestry.** The Ancestor · The Return · The Archive · The Lineage. *The names always point backward. An Echo-Walker born into a family of the dead's record-keepers is the specific conjunction that produces Niran* |
| **Monadic** *the room empties* | **Absence.** *Absence the way a locked door is absence: the feeling that something is behind it and you are not being shown.* **People feel excluded, not rejected.** Rejection is active. Exclusion is architectural. ***The soul does not push people away —** it simply does not include them* | **Names of closure.** The Seal · The Closed Book · The Vault · **The Ledger-Keeper** · The Silence. *Cozbi's title is Monadic in origin: the name of a soul that counts everything and shares the tally with no one — and the world named the accounting because the accounting is the only part of him that ever faces outward* |

> **Why the Accord fears the Monadic.** A sealed system produces no readable output. **A system the instruments cannot assess is a system the Accord cannot price, and a system the Accord cannot price is a hole in the ledger.**
>
> Sancta Lux considers it a doctrinal problem, **because the Scale cannot weigh what will not present itself to the measurement.**
>
> *Common people do not call Monadic practitioners anything, because Monadic practitioners do not give them the chance.*

## Where the Weather Comes From

*The Third Name has a mechanism, and it is the cheapest thing in the Crystal.*
The Archetype is not produced by Stage, Grade, Family or Method, which is why identical instrument profiles produce opposite rooms. **It is produced by Passive Traits.**
A Passive Reflection is a law stabilised by long habit and continuous exposure, always running, costing almost nothing to hold and impossible to switch off. It shows in posture, in gait, in thermoregulation, in scent, and in the ambient behaviour of a room somebody has been sitting in for an hour. **The world is not reading a soul's power. It is reading a soul's standing bias, at the only amplitude that never stops broadcasting.**
This is why the Third Name is stickier than the other two and why it cannot be renounced. A rank can be surrendered and an instrument profile changes at every Threshold. **A Passive Trait is the shape of a life already lived**, and it goes on expressing at the same amplitude whether the bearer is fighting, sleeping, or sitting quietly in a room full of people who have not been told who he is.
> *The counter-intelligence services worked this out two centuries before the Archives did. Every agent's file records Passive Traits first and stat profile second, because the profile is what an enemy can only learn by fighting him and the Passive is what a stranger learns by sharing a bench.*
> **Which means the unawakened have Third Names and nothing else.**
>
> Passive Traits sit in the soul's own material and require no Crystal to run. A Class Ø midwife who settles a room settles it for the same reason a Harmonic Grandmaster does, at a fraction of the amplitude, with no coil in her history and no token in her belt. **The village named her forty years ago and the Accord has never heard of her.**
>
> *Every quarter is full of these names. The Still Woman at the ford. The one they send for when a child will not stop screaming. The man nobody sits beside on the wagon and nobody can say why.* **The folk naming tradition is reading the only layer that was ever readable from outside, which makes it, on this one axis, more accurate than the Accord's entire apparatus.**
>
> The Archives has recorded the observation three times. **It has never been actioned, because a system built to price output has no use for a name attached to a person who produces none.**

## How Titles Are Made

> **A title is not conferred. It accretes.** *The world watches long enough to identify the pattern, the pattern produces a name, and the name sticks because names that accurately describe a lived experience are the stickiest things in any language.*
**I · The Reputation.** It begins as gossip. *The tender at Greyshaft Twelve tells the next shift that the new practitioner settled the array by walking past it* — and within a month the syntax has compressed to a name, **and nobody remembers who said it first because nobody did. It arrived the way weather arrives: from a direction you can point at and from a source you cannot name.**
**II · The Confirmation.** The title survives its first test in circumstances that are not the ones that produced it. **It has crossed from description to prediction** — *and a title that predicts is a title that means something, and a title that means something is a title people start using in the practitioner's hearing.*
**III · The Claim.** Acceptance ratifies the name. **Rejection demonstrates that the name has enough weight to require rejection, which is itself a form of ratification.** *The only response that kills a title is indifference — and indifference is difficult to sustain toward a name that accurately describes something your soul is doing whether you choose it or not.*
**IV · The Record.** It enters the Accord's filing, the guild's archive, or the crown's peerage register **and becomes a legal fact.**
> *A Third Name that has survived all four stages is, for every practical purpose, the practitioner's real name — and the name their parents gave them is the alias.*

## Where Your Name Places You

**Harmonic titles place their bearers at the centre of institutions.** *The Accord's entire architecture is designed by and for Harmonic practitioners — which is why the Accord works, and why it is so profoundly unsettling to everyone who does not fit inside it.*
**Aberrant titles place their bearers at the edge.** Field assets, frontier specialists, **the kind of problem-solver you send to a situation that has already destroyed the standard response.** *They are valued. They are not welcome. The distinction is the Aberrant practitioner's entire social experience.*
**Elegiac titles place their bearers apart.** Sacerdotal — the spaces reserved for grief, memory, **and the cultural work of holding the dead in the minds of the living.**
**Bound titles place their bearers below and above simultaneously.** *Subordinate to what holds them and superior to anyone without an equivalent endorsement — the class confusion that surrounds every temple-warrior, oath-knight and consecrated servant in the four quarters.*
**Ascendant titles place their bearers above. Full stop.** *Aristocratic in the original sense: the thing that claimed them does not claim the second-best, and the room reorganises around that fact whether the practitioner asked it to or not.*
**Catalytic titles place their bearers in motion.** *Institutions that employ them discover the practitioner has changed the institution more than the institution has changed the practitioner.*
> **Monadic titles place their bearers nowhere.** *A Monadic title sounds like a door closing, reads like a refusal, and functions as a reminder that classification requires the classified to participate.*
>
> They cannot be placed because they have not provided the information placement requires — **and the world's response to a person who cannot be placed is the world's most honest confession of what placement was for in the first place, which was control.**

## What the Archives Has Not Filed

**The titles the dead keep** are the one gap the Division treats as urgent, and the reason is Runecraft. A name cut into stone by an Engraver three centuries dead is still legible, still doing work, and still attached to a Third Name nobody alive can verify. **The wall says who he was. There is no living instrument that can check.**
> *The Kharven death-houses carry the hardest version. The inscriptions name their Engravers. Several of the names belong to no lineage the seat can trace, and the Accord's position is that the records were lost. The Kharven position is that the records were taken.*
**Stripped-title ceremonies** exist, are rare, and do not work in the way the ceremony implies. The Accord can revoke a rank and strike a profile from the register. **It cannot stop a room from settling when a Harmonic walks into it**, and every stripped practitioner discovers within a season that the only name that survived the ceremony was the one nobody had the authority to grant.

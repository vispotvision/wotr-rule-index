# The Alchemical Index

*The sixty-one attested formulas · second edition, physical reading*
> **Every entry carries an analogue, a mechanism stated in the analogue's own terms, and a failure that falls out of the mechanism rather than out of the mood.** The alchemy here is literal.
>
> **Nothing in an ingredient list is symbolic filler.** Each component supplies a catalytic surface, a nucleation site, a stabilising lattice, or a Wellspring-resonant trace. Where a step specifies darkness, silence or a season, **it is specifying a physical condition and not a mood**, and this edition says which.
>
> **Provenance Class is now stated on every row**, because the Standing Index prices on it and this index has been silent on it since compilation. *Four entries below are Class IV or above and were carried for years without the column that would have said so.*

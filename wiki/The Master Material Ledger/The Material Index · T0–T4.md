# The Material Index · T0–T4

*T0 through T4 · the ground, the bulk supply, and the first instrument grades.*

---

## T0 · Fragile

| Material | Source | Bearing / Holding | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mireclay** *Bearing · Clay · South* | Marsh basins of Dorrosfenn | Negligible / very low, short duration | Short-lived summoning totems; folk vessels. **The Codex's standard example of Holding and Bearing running independently.** Fails to heat, strong Essence currents, spiritual proximity |
| **Glassroot Fibers** *Holding · Plant Fiber · unconfirmed* | Swampy caves beneath Rovann | Negligible / trace, symbolic only | Woven veils that amplify a voice slightly when sung through. Used by nomadic dryad-kin and travelling poets. **Shatters on contact with structured spellwork** — the fibre is hollow and resonates freely, which is what amplifies the voice, and a working imposes a boundary condition the free resonance cannot satisfy. *It fails at the moment it is asked to hold a shape* |
| **Dustquartz** *Holding · Crystal · North* | Shattered veins in the collapsed mines of Anguz | Poor / faint, vibrationally unstable | Cheap decorative charms. **Dangerous in bulk** — shards resonate to discordant Aether under compression. *Sold by the sack and treated with less caution than that warrants* |
| **Tithing Parchment** *Holding · Alchemized Bark · West* | Holy See of Lurien | Nil / single glyph, one use | Peasant offerings to lowborn spirits. The Church's cheapest sacrament. **Structurally an Onceglass logic centuries before Onceglass.** Burns on spiritual contact |
| **Charred Bone** *Holding · Organic Remnant* | Burnt remains of failed resurrection rites | Nil / curses cling to fragments | Curse-work and grave charms, for hedge-necromancers. Illegal in Lurien-aligned polities. **Its only reliable property is that it holds what nobody wants held.** *A failed resurrection is a working that could not Seal, and an unsealed working does not end. It is interrupted.* **What clings to the fragments is a law-fragment: a piece of a Mortalis process, invoked and abandoned, still attempting to complete itself in whatever it contacts.** No fragment has ever been observed to decay, and the assay houses have been watching for sixty-one years |
| **Coalglass Shard** *Holding · Stone / Glass* | Pressure-formed in shallow mines | Poor / weak, reflective | The poor man's divination mirror. **The bottom of the scrying trade** |

## T1 · Soft

| Material | Source | Resonance / Family / Domain | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Vellun Copper** *Holding · Metal · West, unconfirmed* | Hills of Senoth | Conduction (inferred) | Apprentice smithwork; spiritual conduction; **array wire feedstock.** Good conduction, low retention, melts far too easily. Price tracks the copper famines. **Senoth is asserted on trade-route evidence only** |
| **Willowblood Sap** *Holding · Essence Liquid* | Trees along the Vale of Tirres | — | Inscribing beginner rituals. Weakens in sunlight. **Tirres does not appear on any current Accord survey** |
| **Sunreed Paper** *Holding · Fabric / Pulp* | Marshes near the Epprenean Kingdom | — | **The standard teaching substrate. Every Engraver in the four quarters learned on it.** Fails to damp, and to any working above apprentice grade |
| **Driftbone** *Holding · Organic · South* | Veil-tide shores | — | Ritual carving; dream-shaman work. Carries whispers when hollowed. *What the tides are bleaching, and where it comes from, is not recorded. The shamans decline to speculate in front of Accord surveyors* |
| **Coalthread Cloth** *Holding · Fabric · South* | Southern weave, distribution uncontrolled | — | Concealment garments. Dulls aura flicker and **burns readily.** *The Accord notes without further comment that its principal known users are smugglers* |
| **Star-Tallow Wax** *Holding · Essence Compound* | Harvested from Aether-glow insects | — | Light-inscription candles for scribes and vigil-keepers. **Melts near an emotionally charged Core** — the wax holds its set through a narrow band and a Shell under load radiates enough into the immediate air to carry it past the transition. *A working hazard rather than a poetic one, and the reason vigils over the dying fail routinely: the room is full of exactly the condition the wax cannot survive* |
| **Palebed Nitre** *Holding · Mineral · South* | Nitrate caliche, interior pans | Exuroth · Caloria · Thermodynamics | Oxidiser for charge work; meat preservative; **corpse preservative**; fertiliser. Harvested by scraping. *The pans have been worked for eight hundred years and are not measurably diminished* |
| **Pumice Float** *Bearing · Stone · East* | Caldera ignimbrite sheets | — | Abrasive, filtration medium, lightweight fill. Effectively free at source. **Floats** — rafts come down the rivers after an eruption thick enough to walk on for a week, *which has killed a number of people who tried* |
| **Yellowbreath Sulphur** *Holding · Mineral · East* | Fumarole fields throughout the arc | Pyraeon · Caloria · Thermodynamics | Feedstock for every acid in the eastern alchemical trade. *Collected by hand off the vent rims by men who work in wet cloth and do not work long* |

## T2 · Common Durable

*The material the world is actually built out of.*
| Material | Source | Resonance / Family / Domain | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Greystone** *Bearing · Stone · North* | Quarrylands near Kaetra | — | **Standard fortification stone of four kingdoms.** Minimally resistive to magic, entirely adequate against everything else. *Why the north builds grey where the west builds pale* |
| **Shieldgneiss** *Bearing · Stone · North* | Basement outcrop across the whole quarter | Petralon (weakly) · Materia · Material Science | Bearing **exceptional**, Holding negligible. Inert to nine Wellsprings in ten. **An array laid on the northern shield stays exactly where it was put for a century**, which is why the Accord sites its grid here |
| **Stillband Ore** *Bearing · Metal Ore · North* | Bedded across the western shield | — | Banded iron, magnetite alternating with grey chert in finger-width layers. **The backbone of every bridge in the quarter.** The chert bands scatter Essence, so inscriptions bleed off over a season |
| **Chainsalt** *Holding · Mineral · North* | Nalun Basin, bedded halite | Fixatio · Materia · Material Science | **The most important dull material in the four quarters.** Holding *deliberately nil* — an Essence dielectric. Gaskets, isolation collars, separator plates in every serious array; food preservation; currency bars. **A salt bar is worth a silver mark and can be eaten.** Fails in standing water |
| **Forge-Iron** *Bearing · Metal · North* | Human Kingdom of Altherion | — | **Bears magnificently and holds almost nothing**, which is exactly why it arms the line infantry of six kingdoms and appears in no ward-network anywhere. *Specification unchanged in three hundred years* |
| **Slagbloom** *Bearing · Metal · West* | Ancient spoil, western foreland and tin districts | Anamnesis (unwelcome) · Materia · Material Science | **Inscriptions laid on it read doubled**, because the metal was worked and marked once already and something of the earlier marking survives the second melt. *The same behaviour a Crystal shows at a Threshold, in a substrate.* **A melt cools into order around whatever survived the heat, and in reworked spoil that is the prior marking**, which the new inscription then has to grow around rather than replace. Engravers charge extra for a rough seat in metal for the same reason the halls cap a rough-seated practitioner at Tier Four. **Superstitious foremen will not have it underground** |
| **Palegrit Limestone** *Bearing · Stone · West* | Platform carbonate across the central west | — | Ashlar, lime for mortar, and flux for every iron furnace in the quarter. **Three uses, one quarry.** The same rock gives the west its sinkholes and its habit of losing rivers underground |
| **Kirkyard Lead** *Bearing · Metal · West* | Galena in the limestone belt, worked with the silver | — | Roofing, coffin lining, pipe, shot — and **Essence shielding for every assay house and isolation room in the west.** Attenuates Essence more effectively per unit thickness than anything else in common supply. *The men who smelt it do not last* |
| **Greaves Tin** *Holding · Metal · West* | Cassiterite, lode and stream-work | Coagulatio · Materia · Material Science | Bronze, solder, and the tinning of array contacts. **A tinned contact does not corrode, and contact corrosion in a working array is a slow, invisible, cumulative failure.** Unglamorous, essential, **and running out** |
| **Attercrop Vitriol** *Holding · Liquid Reagent · West* | Drainage of abandoned workings | Dissolution · Materia · Material Science | **The same fluid that killed the Aerne valley writes the Accord's own codices.** Mordant fixing ritual ink to organic substrate. *Blinds on contact with the eye and does so without warning, since it does not burn the skin at working dilution* |
| **Veinwood Plank** *Holding · Wood · West* | Mountain trees of Varun | — | Semi-hardened grain, briefly enchantable. **Standard stock for ritual shields across three kingdoms** |
| **Alchemelt Brick** *Bearing · Synthetic Stone · East* | Artisan guilds of the arc | Materia · Material Science | Volcanic ash ground with burnt lime and water. Sets hard, sets fast, **and sets underwater** — which is why the east builds deep-water harbours while western harbours silt shut. **Will not span** |
| **Redcrust Laterite** *Bearing · Mineral · East* | Weathered flanks, wet eastern seaboard | — | The red caprock. Useless as stone, useless as soil, **and the feedstock for Cloudmetal.** *Everyone knew the metal was in it; only Caedor was willing to commit the array capacity* |
| **Roughgold** *Holding · Metal · East* | Placer bars, black-sand rivers | Benediction, Sublimatio | **Won by hand, by anyone, with no permission from anyone** — which produced the eastern quarter's characteristic political condition **and the Great Inflation of the Voyager Era.** Ecclesiastical and oath work; bullion |
| **Ember Flint** *Holding · Stone · East* | Foraye | — | Survival fire-starting. **The single most widely distributed Essence-active object in the eastern quarter by simple count** |
| **Trapstone** *Bearing · Stone · South* | Flood-basalt terraces, western interior | Coagula, Fixatio · Materia · Material Science | Columnar basalt splitting naturally into six-sided posts that need almost no dressing. **The Braqthian standing-stone tradition exploits its resonance; the west has never bothered to study it** |
| **Greenskin Serpentine** *Bearing · Stone · South* | Ophiolite belt, eastern coastal ranges | — | Carving stock. **In certain pods the stone is fibrous, and the fibres separate into a dust so fine it does not settle.** Twenty-year onset lung scarring, invariably fatal. *Enforcement has posted no writ and the quarries have raised no objection to the absence* |
| **Marrowchalk** *Holding · Phosphorite · South* | Bone-beds, Dorrosfenn and Epprenea | Mortalis, Rebirthine · Vitalia · Biochemistry | **Doubles a wheat yield as fertiliser and is the primary reagent of every necromantic practice in the quarter.** The same bench, the same rock, the same barge. *Fertiliser and necromancy cannot be distinguished at the point of sale because they cannot be distinguished at the point of extraction* |
| **Coilhide Leather** *Bearing · Beastkin Hide · South* | Ironcoils, native to Braqth | — | Training armour and duel-school gloves. *Common enough at home to be worthless and expensive enough abroad to be worth shipping* |

## T3 · Hardened

| Material | Source | Resonance / Family / Domain | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sunkwood** *Bearing · Wood · North* | Beds of the chain-lakes | — | Oak and black spruce drowned when the ice dammed the northern drainage, buried in anoxic mud, **lifted out after two thousand years black to the heart and dense enough to sink.** Essence-quiet, which is the point — bench stock in precision workshops |
| **Blackcap Sulphide** *Holding · Metal Ore · North* | Melt sheet beneath the Kaetran Ring | Manganthra · Materia · Material Science | **Blackcap alloy keeps its temper inside a forge-array's working field, where ordinary steel goes soft and stupid.** Crucible tongs, array clamps, furnace door hinges. *Roasting fume kills vegetation for six leagues downwind* |
| **Ghoststeel** *Holding · Metal · North, provisional* | Salvaged from Spirit War ruins | — | **Not mined. Salvaged.** Reflects spiritlight; partial phasing. Anti-spirit weaponry. **Nobody has yet reproduced it in a crucible**, and the provenance rests on ruin-siting evidence alone |
| **Blacktooth Wolfram** *Holding · Metal Ore · West* | Sheeted veins in the granite cupolas | Ignivale, Exuroth · Caloria · Thermodynamics | **The answer to the vessel problem.** Highest melting point in the ledger. Nozzles, hearth crowns, array-furnace linings for all T4+ crucible work. **No glamour, no legend, and no substitute** — the Accord tracks every ton |
| **Ironwood** *Bearing · Wood · East* | Deep forest of Eresse | — | Hardened to the equivalent of tempered steel. Ceremonial bows, shamanic staves, Yukari staves. **Cut under Eressian restrictions that keep it out of the trade entirely** |
| **Runebronze** *Holding · Metal · West* | Melt-forged with minor glyphs | — | **The first material in history designed to hold an inscription. Bronze is old law.** Oath-plates, boundary markers, guild charters. *Archaic, and increasingly the only kind there is* |
| **Steelsilk Cord** *Bearing · Fabric / Thread · unconfirmed* | Spiderweavers of the Rovannian caverns | — | Suspends armour-weight loads; resists minor spells. Grappling harness, rigging, climbing line. **Rovann's quarter is unresolved in the Accord record** |
| **Fangglass** *Holding · Crystallised Stone* | Unspecified | — | Shards that pierce magical wards. A hunter's consumable. **An early, cruder answer to the same market Onceglass now dominates** |
| **Knellhide** *Bearing · Leather* | Fallen Fellstag beasts | — | Burial-rite armour; anti-possession garments. **Resists possession when inscribed. Uninscribed it is ordinary heavy leather** |
| **Onceglass** *Holding · Crystallised Stone · East* | Obsidian quenched within an active Wellspring field | Fractura, Tenebra · Limina · Abstract/Entropic | **High and terminal — one casting.** Brittle by design. Cannot be repaired, recharged, **or traced back to its Engraver.** Militia ordnance and cheap wards for anyone who cannot afford a permanent working. *An extinction event for the western middle-market Holding trade, proceeding at the speed of shipping.* Arbitration undecided whether weapon or commodity |
| **Skarnrose Ore** *Holding · Metal Ore · East* | Intrusive contacts with limestone | — | Silver, lead, zinc and garnet in banded contact bodies. **Silver here is a byproduct of a zinc operation**, an inversion western silver houses have found financially unpleasant |
| **Ashfast Chrome** *Holding · Metal Ore · South* | Chromite pods in the serpentine belt | Fixatio · Caloria · Thermodynamics | Ground to sand and rammed into a hearth **it will not react with slag, will not fuse, and will not fail.** *The Logistics Division has flagged the two-ore dependency in every annual report for nineteen years* |

## T4 · Reinforced Magical

*Instrument grade.*
| Material | Source | Resonance / Family / Domain | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Clearbeam Beryl** *Holding · Crystal · North* | Pegmatite dykes on the northern margin | Luminalis, Mirithane · Fulguria · Electromagnetism | Optically flawless beryl **in crystals occasionally longer than a man.** Passes Essence-light without the scatter quartz introduces, **which makes it the only acceptable stock for a coil.** Lenses, assay glasses, long-range instruments, and the Skyglass Archive's glazing |
| **Psionite** *Holding · Mineral · West* | Varun deep workings | — | Mental fortification; psionic amplification. **Requires attunement to work at all.** Worked only under the Psionic Mastery Code. *Varun's entire defensive posture rests on it* |
| **Runeglass** *Holding · Alchemical Crystal* | Alchemically grown | — | Holds Parun glyphs with exceptional clarity. **The permanent-object counterpart to Onceglass and the stock the western guilds are trying to defend.** Fails under Wellspring inversion |
| **Verdant Alloy** *Holding · Metal Blend · unattested* | Blended with druidic essence | Verdantia (inferred) | Ceremonial weapons; the makers are unattested. **A druidic blend rather than a smelt. Its makers do not sell it** |
| **Inkveil Silk** *Holding · Fabric · Virelion* | Moonweavers of Virelion | — | **Phases slightly at night and cloaks the aura.** *Every serious infiltrator in the four quarters wants it and almost none can afford it.* Fails in daylight — the phasing is nocturnal |
| **Seerstone Resin** *Holding · Liquid Essence · East, provisional* | Canopy trees, eastern cloud forest | — | Stores visions. **Dangerously volatile.** Provenance recorded on the strength of the resin's physical character and the tree species involved, **not confirmed by the Archives Division** |
| **Bloodscript Bone** *Holding · Organic* | Crystallised remains of martyr-sorcerers | — | Etching unbreakable fate-oaths. **The oath written on it does not come off. Neither does the question of where it came from.** *Requires a martyr; supply is what it is* |
| **Shardlace** *Holding · Fabric / Thread* | Woven from essence glass | — | Cuts spells on contact. Anti-caster garments and netting. **Cuts the wearer as readily as the caster.** *Handling it is a skill in itself* |
| **Cloudmetal** *Holding · Metal · East* | Reduced from Redcrust laterite, Caedor works | Fulguria alignment · Fulguria · Electromagnetism | Structural metal at **a third the weight of iron.** Requires a sustained Fulguria reduction array **that exists in four places.** *The clearest case in the ledger of industrial capability following from geological accident rather than genius, and Caedor's guild would prefer it were not written down in those terms* |
| **Stormfulgur** *Holding · Natural Glass · South* | Fulgurite fields, nitre pans | Mirithane, Aurevane · Fulguria · Electromagnetism | Where lightning strikes the pans it fuses a branching tube of glass down through the crust — **naturally hollow, naturally continuous, naturally aligned along the discharge path.** Ready-formed Essence conduit for array trunking. *The Braqthians gather them after storms the way coastal people gather driftwood.* **Gathered free, resold at forty times** |

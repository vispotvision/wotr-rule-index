# The Moto Origin — The Western Reach

*The western reach of the northern shield.*
> **Ruling in effect.** The Moto origin is **the western reach of the Inner World's northern shield. Not the Old World.**
>
> **Sātūlagi is struck from canon.** The Malō of Sātūlagi does not exist and never did. **The Moto hold one seat and it is Kharven.**

---

## The Ground

Past the ridgeline that now marks Kharven's border with **Undaar-Keth**, on ground no crown claims and no border commission patrols. Old cold shield, glaciated, **high ambient Essence and no infrastructure** — which is the Inner World's profile everywhere, and is why nothing has been built there since.

### The seat

**Standing, ruined, depopulated.** Broken structures, a well, a gatepost, frost on it most of the year.
> **Saruul Moto has lived alone in a cabin there since Muken died**, and no longer touches a wall without reading what happened against it, **because the density of residual memory on that ground exceeds anything else in her record.**

### The displacement

Malakar's splitting of the continents reshaped the shield and made the seat untenable. **The seven lines scattered toward temperate ground.** Muken went the other way — north and east into the boreal forest, **on reasoning that was strategic before it was sentimental** — and built Kharven out of clans who had never heard of the place he came from.

---

## What This Means For The House

> **Every Moto seat in the current era is superimposed on somebody else's ground.**
>
> Kharven is Concord-stock northern clans whose roots predate the Moto arrival by centuries. **This is why the Banner Houses carry northern names and the crown does not**, and why a Stark saying the King's name aloud **is pronouncing a foreign word in his own hall.**
> The Moto never migrated as a nation. **They migrated as a founding house and its retainers.** A people that relocates brings farmers. **A house that relocates brings law.** *This is why they superimpose everywhere they land and assimilate nowhere.*

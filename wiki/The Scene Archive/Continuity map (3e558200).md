# Continuity map

## Continuity map

What each scene changes, and what it calls back to. Built by reading all 160 scenes in
the archive. `references` are the concrete callbacks the text makes to events in other
scenes -- they are how the archive connects, and there are 1,132 of them.
Nothing here is invented: a scene the text does not locate is marked so rather than
placed. Use `scene_recall` and `scene_context` for the passages themselves.

### Threads

#### Kharven — the fall and the eleven-year exile  (6 scenes)

Parts 2b-3-4-5-6 are one continuous stretch ending the night Muken dies; the file boundaries cut across it. Part 2's first movement and Part 1 belong eleven years later, so Part 2 is the one file that sits in two eras. The Path of Sorrow is the only exile-years scene on the page, and it uses the struck Temür/Büri register.

#### The Holy Sea of Alabaster — the courier and the legation  (8 scenes)

Two days, not one. Day A is stamped 'the bell for the second hour' with the tide at the sixth and six weeks of the Primate's silence (Weight of a Courier, Part 20, Warrior Nurse). Day B runs from the lower chapel mid-morning to the cathedral, with the tide at the sixth hour TOMORROW (Weight of an Infant, Part 21, What a Thing Weighs, Sword and Scale, Part 22). The Warrior Nurse's Sum-gol half reads six weeks earlier than its Alabaster half; the file does not reconcile them.

#### Altherion — the Word of Judger on the frame  (12 scenes)

The cleanest clock in the archive: a numbered captivity, day one to day fourteen, with day eleven crowded (Word Under the Hammer, House of Abscene, Sesk's Report, the Petition) and day fourteen running from the cellar through the precinct into the Kharven battle. Commencement of the Curia is the file that welds the two clocks together and should be read as the spine, not as one dated scene.

#### The Ashgate road — the worst day in the history of the North  (26 scenes)

One day, five parallel POV runs (Wren, Sodoku, Mu-jin, Charles, Darius/Aurelian) that the text stamps only coarsely — ninth hour, tenth hour, Continuous, No interval. Within each run the order is firm; ACROSS runs it mostly is not, and the list above should be read as five columns, not one line. The one hard interleave the text gives: Charles dies before 09_sodoku, which handles his body.

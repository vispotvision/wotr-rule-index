## Open retcons (the later scene governs until ruled)

- **Kasane.** Early chapters make her a Kōkan woman cutting salt in Halcrest, taken by Gimbzo. Later scenes make her Kiyome Byakuya's elder sister, *Kasane Tenrai Yukari* (Yukari name taken at a Telling at fourteen), married afterward to Yusif Stark. Chapters One to Five want bringing into line.
- **Yusif Stark.** Chapters Three and Four have him visiting the cuttings once a season; The White Knot has him marrying Kasane and raising the children.
- **Muken's father** is Gimbzo Kōkan Moto (Chapter Five onward); Chapter One still names a younger son of the Orrel house.
- **Ages.** Muken 16 and already sergeant; Yukazuri 13 (Yukazuri's card still says 50 and "a small child on the road").
- **The Verdict of Six.** The kneeling rider "Ulf" needs a new name (Ulf is Lord Stark, Torvald's brother), and the Lambert prisoner "Wystan" needs a new first name (Wystan is Lord Greymane).
- **Lorn Stark** is Ulf's son here, about 22; his card has him 44 in Hild Ice's era.
- **"Kharven's Seat"** is used for the Moto council's seat; in canon Kharven-Seat is the city Muken later builds.
- **The four-year war** that just ended (Yukari against Seikai, Zettari against Moto) is distinct from the Great Spirit War twenty-three years back. It has no name yet.

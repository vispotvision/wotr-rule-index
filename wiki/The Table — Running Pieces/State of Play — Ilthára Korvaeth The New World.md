# State of Play — Ilthára Korvaeth / The New World

*Built 10 September 2026 from the Korvaeth arc file. New World, separate continent, separate calendar. The Inner World does not touch this yet.*
**The event.** Korvaeth outer yard. Three hundred and six sending, four abstaining with forfeits paid, two refusing and entered as unpaid rather than absent, because absent is a fact and unpaid is a position. Halva Vosk and her nine-year-old granddaughter in the fourth rank. Maren Vaeleth in mended black keeping the count, forty-one years in the office, six executions.
**The hinge, turned.** Onawa opened the window on *sae-ureth* herself, in Common rather than Ureth so the child in the fourth rank could follow it, and spent her last sentence making sure Ilthára had a real choice. **Ilthára said the name.** Three-fold *uren*, the Elareth closing, unused in the Ureth register in four Eons. She won an empire and voided her own holding in the same breath, because a full-form naming inside that window proves assembly was possible in the north on an ordinary Tuesday, which kills the Varrisak doctrine, which voids every unassembled holding starting with hers. She saw it coming. **Isaac's ruling still owed: confirm or flip.**
**Where the arc stands.** Eleven years on. Velthaeir's finding entered and never narrowed. Sunroot split name from office and voted a regency under Ovaeren, chosen because he has no blood claim. The Eladrin sent no letter and Loreth Ann held eleven counts past nine. The Echo Elves split eleven to eight and neither side has tried to turn the other. The Sylvaaren refused to be asked, and Aardhae's reply means Ilthára has a valid name available by self-witness and has refused it in eleven words now carved above the eastern door.
**The people.** Ilthára Korvaeth, thirty-four at the yard, no crown, the Mad Queen since twenty-six. Aeldros Korvaeth, the Validated, jurist by training, nineteen years of procedure and eleven months learning to move eleven hundred men down a road in winter, and an unexpected patience with logistics. Corlan Mirroven, frightened of everybody except Aeldros, and said so. Maren Vaeleth, and the private second book that is still on her shelf and that Velthaeir has never asked for. Thela Ess-Vaelen, taken alive, named in the field, released.

# State of Play — Kwon Mu-jin / Aetherion Academy

*Separate from the Hon-guk page: this is the Academy arc, roughly thirteen years later (Rikudoku 13, Sodoku 35, Mu-jin 38).*
**Where.** Aetherion Academy, the Combat Arena, the first week of Class X. Mu-jin, homeroom teacher, has just climbed down into the ring after stopping Xanelor's supersonic arrow with a vial that transmogrified it into molten metal a spread hand from Hiromi's hip.
**Who is in the room.** Hiromi Mahuo (13), Xanelor Rafiminar, Naori Yukari, Akira Yukari, Dabney, Rikudoku Moto, Geturo (15), Lily (12), Mu-jin, Taesyn, Zaire, Neraveth. Verona left for the library; the Headmaster is gone.
**The clock.** Class X Open Tournament, mandatory, seven days out.
**What is unresolved.**
- The match outcome, and whether Mu-jin calls it.
- Who answers for the arena: ward cracked across a third of the dome, a trench the length of the ring, a burn-hole through the floor.
- Xanelor enters the tournament with both forearms hairline-fractured, a strained scaphoid, three fingers cut to the bone, and no bow.
- Every student now knows the masked boy the intake board recorded as having no pressure is not the soft bracket.
- Hiromi is emptied; every draw now costs first-call price.
- The contents of Mu-jin's vial, still undisclosed.
**Fronts.** The Class Below: Class S wants Class X's seats. 1/5. Next: Class S scouts the wounded before the tournament.
**This session's scenes.** Xanelor — The Dorms; Seven Days; The Challenge; The Bullet Train; The Constellations; Juggernaut's Fist; The Arrow.

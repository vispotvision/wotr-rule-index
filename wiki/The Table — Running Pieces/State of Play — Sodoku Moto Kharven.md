# State of Play — Sodoku Moto / Kharven

*Rewritten at every session end. Last rewrite: 10 September 2026, rebuilt from the scene files 01 through 10 (the muster, the Ashgate narrows sequence, and What the Ground Was Owed). The previous entry, which had the column still crossing toward the Seat, was a year of story time out of date.*
**Where the PC is.** Kharven-Seat, the year after the ninth hour. In the spring the first Accord circle was drawn and signed in the lower hall: Kharven, the Holy Sea of Alabaster, the Kingdom of Nalūn, and the stretches from the Brine crossing east to the marches of Sum-gol, under one seal and one body of law, with the Moto lines seated as the royal house. Three thousand and eleven names read out beforehand by a man in gloves who would not delegate one of them. Sodoku Moto put his hand to it last. **The circle is unnamed and stays unnamed in prose until Isaac picks the word.**
**What happened at the narrows.** Wren Greymane turned six hundred spears on the Tenrai on the Ashgate road, built over sixteen months, officers first and together. The sky went wrong and the corona came down and Kaalabad walked eleven paces into it. Sodoku came up the middle of it with every death in Kharven arriving off the Plane at once, went through the Shallow-Seen Unburied and the room with the drain in the floor, took the hand on the shoulder from a man who has done it to a great many people, and came out the far side with the whole of the Scourge crystallised into the lattice alongside the grief and none of it running the show. Yoko Mishiro's hand on his arm was the one thing that came in from outside.
**Hild is dead.** Twelve years old, in a cistern doorway. Sodoku has never said she is the reason for the instrument and will not, because saying it would make her a reason instead of a person who was killed. Yoko's four private lines are the only place it is written down and the Bench has never been permitted to see them.
**Who is in the room.** Yoko Mishiro, nine years beside him. Edward Lambert, who took the southern reach with a grain schedule and no battle and no scorched-earth order, and whose brother Charles is a hole in an ordination roll. Bram Greymane, three fingers, in from the Stormfold road for the signing, would not sit down. Brida, on a stick, eleven days out of the third bowl. Robin Ice. Tabitha Hallenfeld. Miku Tenrai, fourteen, the temporal running behind his eyes, holding a chair at the table by right of a forfeiture he made at nine years old in eleven men's blood.
**What is unresolved.** The name of the circle. Tabitha's question to Lambert, why the grain was bought in the first week when the advance came in the fourth, asked and dated and refused twice since. Wren's deliberate omission from Bram's mutilation roll, never explained. The refugee count that went from 4,200 to 3,900 and was entered as attrition on the road. Vresk Dokkan alive in the Expanse waiting on a compound that does not exist in four hundred years of Old Vaross. The Tsohanto Reach and the Velthaeir archive as the seam to the New World, roughly eleven years out.
**Injuries and reserve.** Nothing recorded since the crossing. Sodoku has not slept a full night since the ninth hour.
**What he believes that is wrong.** Open. Fill at next session.
**Last Front consequence.** The Tenrai front is closed. Seiji revoked, entered *elar-ama* by a Reckoner who had to stand and listen for fifty-one minutes. The eleven who went to a knee around the boy were killed slowly, with Kurosetsu, without the cold, in front of the child, and the column watched him clean the blade on his own sleeve.

# The Ledger

*Everything that has cost something. Injuries, exhaustion, spent reserve, debts, reputation by faction, who knows what. Nothing heals because a session ended. At least one line comes due every session start. Rebuilt 10 September 2026 against scene files 01 through 10 and the Korvaeth arc.*

## The dead

- **Hild Ice**, twelve, in a cistern doorway at the ninth hour. Every clause of the Accord instrument is her and none of it says so.
- **Muken Moto.** No wound. There is no moment to point at, only an outcome, and that is why his son has never been able to put the loss anywhere.
- **Freya.** The room with the drain lifted out and the chair with the arms cut short. Sodoku watched all of it, in order, with no part of it refusable, and recognised a man he had eaten with at his own table.
- **Seiji Tenrai Moto.** Revoked and entered *elar-ama*, held by nobody. Fifty-one minutes, capillary beds outward, conscious for forty-eight of them.
- **Eleven Tenrai retainers**, on a knee around the boy, refusing in Common so the child would understand. Killed slowly, with Kurosetsu, without the cold.

## Injuries and reserve

- **Sodoku Moto:** no full night since the ninth hour, eleven months and standing. Kurosetsu's remembering costs a vibration (Borin). Standing.
- **Bram Greymane:** three fingers, the hip. Walked in from the Stormfold road for the signing and would not sit down.
- **Brida:** on a stick, eleven days out of the third bowl, four hundred yards uphill in forty minutes.
- **Renard Greymane**, 6 Sept: left forearm, the Held Boil's residue; no burn, grip reduced, duration unknown. Reserve about a third down.
- **Vresk Dokkan of Kaadre-Six:** eleven days, a fingertip and then all the toes and then other things, entered against names on a roll. Alive. Returned.

## Debts

- Sodoku to Borin: eleven days of forge-work on the crossing, still unpriced.
- Kharven to Stannvaard: the grain.
- Renard to Brida: an explanation, and a body before morning.
- **The four hundred and six**, under Lambert's own seal, negotiated by his own office, eleven of them children under twelve, his initial beside the clause on their treatment. Robin Ice stood on the path four seconds, said *aye*, and did it.

## Who knows what

- Renard knows a dead man said Lambert opened the west postern. Nobody else does.
- Renard holds a brass token and a sealed packet nobody has read.
- Tabitha has the question, written and dated, and two refusals.
- Yoko's four lines exist. The Bench has never been permitted to see them.
- The refugee count went 4,200 to 3,900 between the first and second count, entered as attrition on the road. Everyone in the Seat knew what it was. Nobody recorded it.
- Nobody has ever put the two-word challenge form to Ilthára Korvaeth. Aeldros works out in the eleventh year that this was the whole answer.

## Reputation

- **Sodoku:** cleaned the blade on his own sleeve in front of the column, which no king does. Three depositions notice he never thanked the boy.
- **Bram:** did not maim a commander, made a nation invent a word.
- **Lambert:** took the southern reach with a supply schedule and no battle and no scorched-earth order, and cannot be thanked for it in public.
- **Ilthára Korvaeth:** the Mad Queen, and after the yard the byname has evidence, and the evidence is a ledger rather than raving.

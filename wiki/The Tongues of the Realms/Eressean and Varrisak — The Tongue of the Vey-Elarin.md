# Eressean and Varrisak — The Tongue of the Vey-Elarin

*A grammar of the elven tongue and its schism. The Tongues of the Realms governs.*
> *"She sang one note. We have spent every Eon since arguing about how it should be sung back."*
One language, two branches, and the disagreement between them is grammatical before it is legal. Eressean and Varrisak share phonology, most morphology and nearly all of the lexicon. They differ on one affix, and that affix decides who lawfully holds a name.

### I. Attestation

**The Velthaeir holdings.** The Floating Archive-City is the largest body of record in any elven tongue and the least representative, because everything in it was entered in the sealed register by people whose profession was entering things in the sealed register. Velthaeir preserves how Eressean law was written across four Eons and almost nothing of how anyone spoke.
**The rite calendars.** Every Sunroot-descended house keeps its ceremonial windows in fixed wording, recopied without variation, and the recopying is itself a rite. These are the deepest attestation available for the archaic layer, and they are the reason the high register has not drifted measurably since the Spirit War. A phrase in a calendar is a phrase nobody has been permitted to improve.
**The Dream Groves.** Varrisak transmission is oral by doctrine, and the Groves are the only archive: old-growth root-networks carrying generations of practitioner Crystal-inclusion. What is held there is not text. It is the experience of having heard a thing said, which the Varrisak consider a superior record and which is unreadable to any Eressean philologist who has tried.
**The Accord's compilations.** Adequate on phonology, useless on the witness system, because the compilers took the validated and contingent possessives for a politeness distinction and glossed both as *my*. Every Concord treaty with an elven signatory drafted before the fourth century contains at least one clause that says something nobody at the table intended.
**What is lost.** The Vey-Elarin tongue as it stood before the five theologies. Both surviving branches claim to be the conservative one and both are demonstrably wrong, since each preserves archaisms the other has lost.

### II. Phonology

Open, liquid, and light. Where Old Vaross builds heavy onsets and closes syllables hard, Eressean does neither, and a speaker moving between the two tongues sounds to both audiences as though they are performing an accent on purpose.
|  | Labial | Dental | Alveolar | Palatal | Velar |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stops | p, b | t, d |  |  | k, g |
| Fricatives | f, v | th | s, sh |  | h |
| Nasals | m |  | n | ny |  |
| Liquids |  |  | l, r | y |  |

*r* is a light alveolar tap, never trilled and never syllabic. This is the first thing that separates an elven speaker from a goblinoid one, and the two peoples find each other's *r* comic for opposite reasons.
There are no aspirates, no uvulars, and no geminates. A doubled consonant in the orthography, as in *Rovann* or *Varrisak*, marks the preceding vowel as short and is not itself lengthened.
**Vowels.** Five simple: **a e i o u**. Four diphthongs: **ae ei ie ao**. Vowel length is not phonemic. Vowel *pitch* is, and that is Section III.
**Syllable shape.** Onsets are single consonants or nothing. Words may and often do begin with a vowel: *Eresse*, *Elarin*, *Iampu*, *Altherion*. This is categorically forbidden in Vaross and permitted without comment here, which is why the two languages have almost no loanwords in either direction despite six centuries of adjacency.
**Codas.** A word ends in a vowel, a liquid, a nasal, or *th*. Nothing else. *Velthaeir*, *Sylvaar*, *Rovann*, *Seralyth*, *Eresse*. **A word ending in a stop is not an Eressean word**, and the language has a productive suffix, *-ak*, whose entire function is to mark something as foreign. That suffix is the origin of the name *Varrisak*, and the Varrisak know it.

### III. Prosody

**Pitch, not duration and not volume.** The Vey-Elarin were tuned into being as notes of a chord, and the language kept the mechanism.
Every lexical word carries one of three contours across its stressed syllable.
| Contour | Written | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **Level** | unmarked | statement, the neutral register |
| **Rising** | *ú* in citation | the incomplete, the pending, the not-yet-validated |
| **Falling** | *ù* in citation | the settled, the closed, the witnessed |

Contour is never written in running text and never written in Songscript prose, because a reader who needs it marked is a reader who should not be reading that document. It is written only in teaching grammars and in Accord compilations, which is one of several reasons Accord compilations are held in contempt.
**The pitch does grammatical work.** A falling contour on a possessive can validate a claim that the affix left contingent, and a rising contour can withdraw one that the affix granted. An Eressean jurist reading a deposition aloud in court is performing an act with legal weight, and a jurist who reads a rising contour where the text expects a falling one has said something actionable. Depositions are therefore read by two jurists in alternation and the second exists to catch the first.
**Volume carries nothing at all**, exactly as in Vaross, and for an unrelated reason. Vaross reserves volume for signalling across water. Eressean reserves it for song, and using a singing mechanism for speech is a category error that reads as either drunk or blasphemous depending on the room.

### IV. Morphology

#### Roots

Roots are disyllabic and open: *elar*, *vey*, *thaer*, *sylva*, *marin*. A monosyllabic root is archaic, and the closed set of them is the oldest identifiable layer in the language.

#### The three canon particles

Three roots do more work than any others and are the phoneme chains an Eressean practitioner routes Essence through most efficiently.
**ma** — to hold, to have in hand, without any claim about right.
**ur** — to witness, to stand for, to have been present and be answerable for it.
**ie** — the grant, the giving-over, the transfer itself.
Every institution in Eressean law is built out of those three and nothing else, which is why the language's legal vocabulary is so shallow and its legal reasoning so deep.

#### There is no unwitnessed possessive

**The central fact of the language.** A speaker cannot say *my name* without also saying, in the same word, whether the holding was witnessed. There is no neutral form. Silence on the question is not available.
| Form | Suffix | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Validated** | **-uren** | held, and the assembly stood for the holding |
| **Contingent** | **-ama** | held, and nobody stood for it |

> *elaruren* — my name, and the witnesses were assembled and compensated
> *elarama* — the name I am holding, and you are entitled to ask by what right
Both are perfectly grammatical. Only one is a claim. An Eressean who is asked their name and answers in *-ama* has said something enormous and entirely polite, and everyone in the room has heard it.
**The Varrisak third form.** *-ieur*, from *ie* and *ur* together: the grant and the witness collapsed into one act. It means *held, and the holding witnessed itself*. Varrisak doctrine is that a vision sought alone in old growth is a valid witnessing, because the forest was present and the forest is a relative rather than a resource, and a relative who was present has witnessed.
Eressean grammarians do not consider *-ieur* wrong. They consider it **ungrammatical**, which is worse, and the distinction matters: a wrong claim can be corrected, and an ungrammatical one has not been made. Four Eons of mutual illegitimacy proceed from that affix. Both sides are arguing in good faith and neither has ever conceded a syllable.

#### Other affixes

| Affix | Function | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **-in** | collective, the gathered kin | *vey*, kin → *Vey-Elarin*, the kin of the note |
| **-en** | genitive, of, belonging to the estate of | *thaeren*, of the archive |
| **-eth** | office, the rite-role rather than the person | *uren* → *ureth*, the office of standing witness |
| **-aer** | the high, the raised, the floating | *thaer* → *Velthaeir*, the raised archive |
| **-ak** | foreign, outside the form | *Varrisak*, those outside the form |
| **-ith** | the small, and neutrally so | *elarith*, a short name, a by-name |

Note that *-ith* is neutral here and contemptuous in Vaross. Goblinoid and elven speakers have gone to war over less.

#### Verbs, and the window

**Eressean has no imperative.** There is no verbal form that orders an action. This is not politeness. It is the calendar: an act has an appointed window, and to command is to name the window, after which the act follows because the window has opened.
> *Sae-ureth.* The witnessing-office window stands open.
> **Stand witness. Now.**
A speaker who wants something done says when it is due. A speaker with no authority to open a window cannot issue an order at all, in any register, using any construction, and Eressean political life is largely the study of who may open which window.
The verb accordingly marks not time but **whether the act fell inside its window**.
| Marker | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **-sae** | in-window; done at its appointed time |
| **-lo** | out-of-window; done in haste, in emergency, or in error |

> *iesae* — the transfer was made in its season
> *ielo* — the transfer was made outside its season
*-lo* is not past tense and is not a judgement, formally. In practice a chain of *-lo* verbs in a deposition is how an Eressean advocate destroys a witness without ever calling them a liar.
**Varrisak has no window marking and never developed it.** In its place is an obligatory evidential: **-ven** seen, **-dhae** dreamed, **-tor** told by another. A Varrisak cannot report an event without stating how they came to it, and the dreamed evidential outranks the seen one, which Eressean speakers find so difficult that most simply stop translating at that point.

### V. Syntax

**Verb-initial in the high register.** The act is named before the actors, because the rite is the subject of the sentence and the participants are its instruments.
> *Ie-sae Vey-Elarin thaeruren.* Granted-in-season, the kin, the witnessed archive.
**Subject-first in common speech.** The colloquial register moves the subject forward and is understood by the high register to be lazy rather than wrong.
**The witness slot is obligatory and final.** Any clause making a claim ends by naming who stood for it. A clause that ends without one is not incomplete; it is a clause that has claimed nothing, and Eressean speakers use bare clauses deliberately when they wish to say a thing without owning it. This is the single most common construction in Eressean diplomacy and the single most misread one in Concord transcripts.
**No copula, and for the opposite reason to Vaross.** Vaross omits it because identity is not asserted. Eressean omits it because identity is sung rather than stated, and a bare juxtaposition is understood to be quoting the Realmsong. *Vey elar* is *the kin, the note*, and it is not a metaphor and not a claim. It is a citation.

### VI. The Semantic Architecture

**One. Ma against ur.** To hold against to be answerable for holding. Every object, office, name, territory and debt in elven thought is sorted by whether anyone stood for it. There is no third category and no way to decline the question.
**Two. Sae against lo.** In its season against out of it. This is the Eressean model of virtue and it is not about outcome. An act performed correctly out of its window is worse than an act performed badly within one, and the Eressean will say so under oath.
**Three. Elar.** The note, and by extension the name, and by extension the person. There is no separate word for *identity*. There is *elar*, and a person's *elar* can be held, granted, witnessed, vacated, or held contingently by somebody the assembly has not stood for, and all of those are things that happen to a note in a chord rather than things that happen to a self. **A vacant name is a silence in a chord**, and an Eressean hearing one described will hear the gap rather than the person.

### VII. Registers

**Common Eressean.** Subject-first, contours flattened toward level, the witness suffix reduced to *-ren* and *-ma*. What is spoken in a Velthaeir kitchen. Fully mutually intelligible with Varrisak common speech, which both branches find embarrassing.
**Ureth Eressean, the witnessing register.** Court and deposition. Verb-initial, full contours realised, witness slot never omitted, *-lo* used surgically. Read aloud in alternation by paired jurists.
**Elareth, the sung register.** Rite and consecration. Not spoken at all. Every word carries its contour as pitch in the literal sense, the text is Songscript, and a rite performed in the wrong key has not been performed. This is the register the calendars preserve and the reason the archaic layer has not moved.

### VIII. Dialects

**Eressean.** The standard by its own account. Contours fully realised, morphology conservative, *-uren* obligatory.
**Varrisak.** Faster, flatter contours, the evidential system in place of the window, and *-ieur* everywhere. Retains an imperative that Eressean lost, or invented one, depending on which philologist is asked. Oral only, by doctrine.
**Eladrin.** The northern courts made **the pause a morpheme.** A measured silence of a counted length functions as a particle, and the count is the meaning: a four-count is assent, a nine-count is a refusal that will not be argued, and a silence held past a nine-count has moved out of language and into judgement. Queen Seralyth's unbroken silence through an entire negotiation is taught in the Winter Court as the highest sentence ever constructed in the dialect. Concord envoys transcribe these exchanges as having had nothing said in them.
**Echo Elf.** The restatement is grammatical rather than stylistic. An agreed fact takes the clitic **zo** and is repeated once, exactly, and an agreement that has not been *zo*-restated by every party has not been entered. The doubled *zo* phonemes are audible from across a room and are the reason Echo Elf negotiation takes as long as it does and has never once needed refighting.
**Drow.** Conservative in morphology, radical in script. Speech is close to archaic Eressean, with the witness suffixes intact and the window system reduced to a debt-marker: an obligation is either *sae*, still inside the term in which it may be paid, or *lo*, past it. Under pressure, Eressean structure surfaces in a Drow speaker's grammar, and a Drow scribe who lets that happen in front of a witness has revealed something about their lineage they would rather not have.

### IX. Script

**Florwyndic Songscript**, one of the Fourteen Hands, and the only writing system any elven branch acknowledges.
Each glyph carries two components: the segmental body, which records the consonants and vowels, and the contour rail, a line above the body recording the pitch. **A Songscript text is therefore a score.** It does not record what was said. It records how it must be sung, and a text without its rail is not a damaged document but a different kind of document entirely, a thing that can be read and cannot be performed.
Songscript is reserved for law and consecration. Ordinary Eressean correspondence is written in a Concord hand, and every Eressean feels mildly compromised about this and does it anyway.
**The Varrisak have no script and want none.** A law that can be written down can be followed without being understood, which they hold to be worse than no law at all. The Dream Groves are the archive and the archive is alive, and the Varrisak position is that Eresse mistook a filing system for a memory four Eons ago and has been arguing from a transcript ever since.
**Drow Shadow-Veil notation** is a closed system, readable only by Drow practitioners, used to record what happened at sites the rest of the world has agreed to forget. It is not a hand of Parun and does not pretend to be.

### X. Lexicon

**Name, note and person.** *elar*, note, name, person. *elarith*, a by-name. *elareth*, the sung register. *vey*, kin, relative. *Vey-Elarin*, the kin of the note.
**Holding and witness.** *ma*, to hold. *ur*, to witness. *ureth*, the witnessing office. *ie*, the grant. *-uren*, validated. *-ama*, contingent. *-ieur*, self-witnessed, Varrisak only.
**Season and window.** *sae*, in season. *lo*, out of season. *saeth*, an appointed window. *saethen*, of the calendar.
**Archive and place.** *thaer*, the kept thing, archive. *-aer*, raised, floating. *Velthaeir*, the raised archive. *vel*, high. *ann*, beneath. *Rovann*, the place beneath.
**Forest and depth.** *sylva*, growing ground. *aar*, old, deep. *Sylvaar*, the old growth. *dhae*, the dream. *-dhae*, dreamed evidential. *ven*, seen. *tor*, told.
**Cold and silence.** *seral*, the held season, winter. *lyth*, silence. *Seralyth*, winter-silence.
**Function words.** *zo*, the restatement clitic, Echo Elf. *-ak*, foreign, outside the form. *-in*, collective. *-en*, genitive. *-eth*, office.

### XI. Sample Texts

**The oldest surviving formula, Elareth register.**
> *Elar iesae. Elar uren. Vey-Elarin ureth.*
> The note, granted in season. The note, witnessed. The kin, standing in the office of witness.
> **The name passes at its appointed hour. The name is witnessed. The kin stand for it.**
**From a Velthaeir deposition, Ureth register.**
> *Ielo. Ielo. Elarama vey-en.*
> Granted out of season. Granted out of season. A contingently held name, of that kin.
> **He was given it twice, both times outside the window, and what he is holding is not yet his.**
**A Varrisak reply, recorded by an Eressean who considered it unanswerable.**
> *Elarieur. Sylvaar ven-dhae. Sylvaar ur.*
> Self-witnessed name. The old growth, seen-and-dreamed. The old growth, witness.
> **I hold my name and the forest stood for it. You were not asked because you were not there.**
**Echo Elf ratification.**
> *Ie-sae. Zo ie-sae. Uren zo.*
> Granted in season. Restated: granted in season. Witnessed, restated.
**A proverb both branches claim.**
> *Ma-ama, lyth. Ma-uren, elar.*
> Held unwitnessed, silence. Held witnessed, a note.
> **What nobody stood for makes no sound in the chord.**

### XII. Speaking It Badly

**Concord speakers** cannot hear the contours at all, and their flat delivery renders every claim they make legally void in an Eressean court without their ever learning why. They also default to the validated *-uren* on everything, since it sounds more respectful, and thereby claim ownership of items, offices and people they have merely been shown.
**Goblinoid speakers** trill the *r*, close their syllables, and reach instinctively for an imperative that does not exist, producing window-statements with no authority behind them. A Vaross speaker in an Eressean chamber is a man repeatedly announcing that the hour has come, to a room that knows perfectly well he cannot open it.
**Dawi speakers** handle the phonology well and the witness system badly, because Khazalid oath-names put the answerability in the name itself rather than in an affix, so a Dawi speaking Eressean under-marks constantly. They are heard as evasive when they are being, by their own lights, redundant.
**Eressean speakers of Common** over-specify provenance, name their witnesses unprompted, and produce sentences that read to Concord ears as either aristocratic or paranoid. They also refuse to give orders and issue schedules instead, which has cost at least two joint campaigns their opening week.

### Provenance

Attested in canon: the Vey-Elarin, the Realmsong of Edenis, Florwyndic Songscript as directive-force writing reserved for law and consecration, the Varrisak refusal of script by doctrine, the Dream Groves as literal Aetheric archive, the Eladrin use of prolonged silence as high courtesy, the Echo Elf *zo* phonemes and the Recitation, Drow Shadow-Veil notation, and the Eressean efficiency on *Ma*, *Ur* and *Ie* phoneme chains, which is why those three roots carry the legal system. The witness split between the branches is the naming canon's own: both hold that names are property, and they disagree on what constitutes valid transfer.
**On Varrisak against Vassirak.** Both are Eressean coinages and neither is an endonym. *Vassirak* is the older, from *vassir*, to go out without a return date, and it fell out of use because it concedes that leaving was a journey. *Varrisak* replaced it, from *varris*, to be unfixed, and it concedes nothing. **Varrisak is the current form and Vassirak is archaic**, which means a character who uses *Vassirak* is either very old, quoting a very old document, or making a point. The Varrisak call themselves **Sylvaaren**, the old growth's own, and have never once used either name for themselves in a document they wrote.

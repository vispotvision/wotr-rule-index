# Old Vaross — The Goblinoid Tongue

*A grammar of the goblinoid tongue. The Tongues of the Realms governs.*

### I. Attestation

Three bodies of record and none of them disinterested.
**The Keth-Gorrum registers.** Parliamentary record of the Hobgoblin Expanse, continuous since the settlement, held in the Vaross the parliament uses for its own proceedings. Voluminous, procedurally obsessive, and useless for anything colloquial, because nothing colloquial was ever entered.
**The Mandate's decree archive.** Every active decree read daily at every district garrison for the entire history of Undaar-Keth, and every one of them written down. This is where the modern spoken language is best preserved, because a decree is written to be *read aloud to people who must understand it*, which is the only formal register in the four quarters under continuous pressure to stay comprehensible.
**The Accord's grammars.** Compiled by philologists at Stannvaard across four decades. Excellent phonology. Confident and repeatedly wrong on semantics, because the compilers translated *nakh* and *brakh* as *take* and *carry* and never worked out that the pair is one concept split in half. Half the errors downstream in Concord scholarship come out of that single decision.
**What is lost.** Everything before the Scattering. The goblinoid peoples crossed the water carrying what could be carried, and a language's older layer is not a thing you can carry. What survives of pre-Scattering Vaross is a closed set of roots preserved inside compounds nobody can decompose any more, and the Expanse's philologists treat those the way other cultures treat relics.

### II. Phonology

|  | Labial | Dental | Alveolar | Velar | Uvular |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stops, voiceless | p | t |  | k | q |
| Stops, voiced | b | d |  | g |  |
| Fricatives | f, v | th | s, sh | kh, gh |  |
| Nasals | m |  | n | ng |  |
| Liquids |  |  | l, r |  |  |

*r* is an alveolar trill and is the language's signature sound. **It occurs syllabically, without a vowel**, and this is the single most distinctive feature of Vaross to an outside ear. *Grr* is one syllable and contains no vowel at all. Concord speakers universally fail this and insert a vowel, which is the first thing that marks them.
*kh* and *gh* are true fricatives and never aspirated stops. *q* is uvular and occurs only in a handful of pre-Scattering roots, which is how philologists identify them.
**Vowels.** Five: **a e i o u**. Length is phonemic and is written doubled.
| Short | Long | Contrast |
|---|---|---|
| a | aa | *kan*, a hand set to a task. *kaan*, an officer. |
| e | ee | rare |
| i | ii | rare |
| o | oo | *gor*, to weigh. *goor*, to be found wanting under weighing. |
| u | uu | *vashum*, an opening. *vashuum*, a thing that opens where it should not. |

**Clusters.** Heavy onsets are the rule, not the exception. Permitted initially: **gr kr thr vr dr shk str vl kh gh thn**. Permitted finally: **kk rr nn tt rk sk ld** and geminates generally.
**Geminates are meaningful.** A doubled consonant marks a completed or bounded action against an ongoing one. *Dok*, to strike at. *Dokk*, to have struck. This is why the strike-root in names is always geminate: **Dokk-Vraal**, the one who went out having struck, and **Dokkan**, of the completed strike.
**Forbidden.** No vowel-initial words. No final voiced stops. No sequence of three vowels. A word that breaks any of these is a loan and every Vaross speaker hears it as one instantly.

### III. Prosody

**Weight-timed, not stress-timed and not syllable-timed.** A heavy syllable takes roughly twice the duration of a light one, and this is absolute. It is why Vaross spoken by a native sounds slow to Concord ears, and why Concord speakers of Vaross sound to natives as though they are gabbling through the important parts.
A syllable is heavy if it contains a long vowel, a geminate, a syllabic *r*, or a final cluster.
**Emphasis is duration. Never volume.**
This is the load-bearing prosodic fact and it is a cultural one. Volume in goblinoid usage is a signalling function, for shouting across water or a working floor, and carries no emotional content whatsoever. A goblinoid who raises his voice in an argument is understood to have lost control of the situation and is treated accordingly.
To emphasise, a speaker **holds the vowel**. In writing this is marked by doubling beyond the lexical length, so a word already long can go longer.
> *durrak* — a thousand-count of years
> *durraak* — a thousand-count of years, and I want you to feel the length of it
A Vaross speaker in a rage gets **quieter and slower**. Every Concord officer who has misread that has misread it exactly once.

### IV. Morphology

#### Roots

Roots are monosyllabic, heavy, and carry a concept rather than a part of speech. *Vorruk* is not a noun and not a verb. It is the concept of ground that tried to move and was stopped, and what it does in a sentence is determined by what is attached to it.

#### Derivational suffixes

| Suffix | Function | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **-uk** | the resulting state of a force applied | *vor*, to shift → *vorruk*, ground that tried to move and was stopped |
| **-um** | the gathered, the collective | *gorr*, weight → *gorrum*, an assembly |
| **-an, -en** | genitive; of; son or daughter of | *Dokkan*, of the strike. *Vreshen*, of Vresh |
| **-ak** | pertaining to the business of | *thruum*, a decree → *Thruumak*, of the reading |
| **-im** | plural and possessive both | *okk*, we → *okkim*, ours. *kha-ur* → *kha-urrim*, humans |
| **-aal** | the long form; augmentative | *keth*, a seal → *Kethaal*, the great seal. Also the Bugbear dialect marker |
| **-ith** | diminutive, and always contemptuous | *kaan*, an officer → *kaanith*, a jumped-up officer |

#### Verbs

A bare root is an **imperative**. This is why Crimson Order speech under stress reads as a stack of nouns to an outsider: the subjects have dropped and what remains is roots.
> *Vrekh.* Hold.
> *Rukh.* Stand.
**-aan** is the directional, meaning *toward* or *against*, and it is the closest thing Vaross has to a finite verb. *vashk-*, to open → *vashkaan*, breaches against. *nakh-*, to take without bearing the cost → *nakhaan*, reaches for.
**-akh** is the completive. *brakhakh okk*, we have borne it, and it is finished.
**skaal** is the negative auxiliary and always stands last in its clause. It negates the whole clause and takes no subject, which is why refusals in a decree arrive with nobody in them. *Skaal vashk.* There shall be no breaching.
**nurr** is standalone refusal. Using it to a superior is a formal act with consequences, not rudeness.

#### Compounding

Elements join with a hyphen and **the second element governs**. *Gorr-Keth* is a seal of the mantle-weight kind. *Keth-Gorr* is a weight of the sealed kind. Reversing a compound is a real and common insult in parliamentary debate.

#### There are no adjectives

**The most important fact in the language.** A quality cannot float. It must be compounded onto a noun, which means every descriptive statement is an assertion about a specific thing.
> *shkarr*, unshaped force → *shkarr-kha-ur*, a human made of unshaped force
There is no way to say *savage* in Old Vaross. There is only a way to say *made of unshaped force*, attached to somebody, in front of witnesses. Every Vaross insult is therefore a factual accusation, and a speaker asked to soften a description will restructure the entire sentence rather than reach for a milder adjective, because there are none to reach for.

### V. Syntax

**Neutral order is verb-final.** Subject, object, verb.
> *Okk vorruk vrekh.* We the stopped-ground hold.
**Under stress the verb fronts.** This is the Ironblood tell and it is involuntary. An Ironblood speaker who has begun to front his verbs has stopped choosing his words, and every hobgoblin in the room knows it.
> *Vrekh okk vorruk.* Hold, we, the stopped ground.
A Crimson Order officer trained out of it will front only under extremity, and a hobgoblin who hears an Order officer do it will treat the situation as considerably worse than the officer has said it is.
**No copula.** *Okk keth* is *we the seal*, and it does the work of *we are the seal* without asserting anything about identity, which is why Vaross legal statements are difficult to challenge and why Accord clerks hate them.

### VI. The Semantic Architecture

Three distinctions the language forces on every speaker.
**Nakh against brakh.** *Nakh* is to take without bearing the cost of taking. *Brakh* is to bear the cost. Every transaction, conquest, inheritance, gift and theft in goblinoid thought sorts into one or the other, and there is no neutral third verb. A goblinoid cannot describe an acquisition without also stating whether it was paid for.
**Gorr against kha.** *Gorr* is mantle-weight, what absorbs and does not move. *Kha* is the weightless, what has no load-bearing capacity. This pair is the goblinoid model of worth, and it is why humans are *kha-urrim*, and why the insult is not about strength.
**Khal.** Held, as a wound holds after stitching. **There is no word in Vaross for** ***healed*****.** The nearest is *khal*, and it means the closing held, and it carries the wound in it permanently. *Vorruk-Khal* is the Expanse's own name for itself, and it is an admission no goblinoid has ever been able to translate into Common without the Common version sounding either braver or sadder than the original.

### VII. Registers

**Common Vaross.** The street. Compounds shortened, geminates lenited, the directional *-aan* reduced to *-an*. What is spoken in Kaadre-Six.
**Thruum Vaross, the decree register.** Clauses ordered by operational priority. Subjects present at the opening and dropped progressively. Full lexical length observed on every word. It exists to be read aloud to people who must act on it, and it is the reason the language has not drifted in six centuries.
**Kethaal Vaross, the sealed register.** The parliamentary and legal layer, full of pre-Scattering roots nobody can decompose, and deliberately so. A Keth-Gorrum ruling is written in a register that requires an interpreter, and the interpreters are a profession, and the profession is hereditary.

### VIII. Dialects

**Hobgoblin.** The standard. Vowels held to full length. *Grr-Vashuum* with the second syllable long, because the breaking was not quick.
**Ironblood.** Shorter vowels throughout, heavier consonantal attack, verb-fronting under far less pressure. *Grr-Vashuum* with the second syllable clipped. The two pronunciations of that one word are the whole political history of the goblinoid peoples in four syllables, and both peoples know it.
**Gorr-Vaross, the Bugbear dialect.** Marked by the *-aal* extension applied far beyond its augmentative function, producing the long-vowelled register outsiders find eerie. *Vesh-Undaal* rather than *Vesh-Undar*. Slower even by Vaross standards.
**Goblin.** Rapid, heavily contracted, with the syllabic *r* often lost. Held in contempt by every other dialect and demonstrably the most innovative, since almost every new coinage in the Expanse in the last century entered through it.

### IX. Script

**Tally-notation, cut rather than written.**
The pre-Scattering script did not cross the water. What the goblinoid peoples rebuilt was a notation designed to be cut into wood, stone and bone with a single blade, because that is what could be done on ships and on volcanic rock with no ink and no hides.
Every character is a vertical stem with cuts to one or both sides. Consonants are cuts to the right, vowels are cuts to the left, and length is marked by a cut that crosses the stem entirely. The rank compound on Vresk Dokkan's shoulder plate is four cuts and a crossbar and can be read at forty paces by anyone in the Mandate.
**The consequence.** Vaross is a language in which writing is a physical act performed on an object, and a Vaross document is therefore an object rather than a page. A decree is a board. A ruling is a post. The Keth-Gorrum's archive is a room full of timber, every entry in it can be destroyed with an axe, this has happened, and the Expanse's law has a hole in it where the year 411 used to be.

### X. Lexicon

**Force and violence.** *grr-*, a force event, kinetic and percussive. *dokk*, the strike, completed. *dok*, to strike at. *vashk-*, to open, to breach. *vashuum*, a thing that opens where it should not. *shkarr*, unshaped force. *drakh*, fear, the ground moving under a standing thing.
**Weight and worth.** *gorr*, mantle-weight. *goor*, found wanting under weighing. *kha*, the weightless. *kha-ur*, a weightless one. *kha-urrim*, humans. *brakh-*, to bear the cost. *nakh-*, to take without bearing it. *gor-*, to weigh.
**Ground and holding.** *vor-*, to shift. *vorruk*, ground that tried to move and was stopped. *vrekh-*, to hold a line. *rukh-*, to stand. *khal*, held after stitching. *vesh*, a shelf or tier. *undaar*, the standing thing, that which does not fall.
**Binding and law.** *keth*, seal, binding. *kethaal*, the great seal. *thruum*, a decree read aloud. *gorrum*, an assembly. *kaan*, an officer. *kaanith*, a jumped-up officer. *vaross*, the tongue that carries law.
**Persons and pronouns.** *okk*, we. *okkim*, ours. *ghrim*, they. *ghrimin*, theirs. *thaal*, the elder. *vraal*, the one who went out.
**Time and place.** *durrak*, a thousand-count of years. *mokk*, the hearth-stone, home. *ashfold*, a district of the Mandate. *kaadre*, a garrison settlement.
**Function words.** *skaal*, shall not, clause-final. *nurr*, no, standalone refusal. *-aan*, toward, against. *-akh*, completive.
**Pre-Scattering roots, undecomposable.** *qeth-*, *quum*, *qarr*. All three appear only inside compounds, all three contain the uvular, and the Expanse's philologists have four competing accounts of what any of them meant.

### XI. Sample Texts

**A proverb.**
> *Brakhakh, keth. Nakhakh, vashuum.*
> Borne-and-finished, a seal. Taken-and-finished, a thing that opens where it should not.
> **What you paid for holds. What you took will open under you.**
**A parliamentary formula, Kethaal register.**
> *Keth-Gorrum gorrumakh. Vaross keth. Qeth-thruum rukhaan.*
> The gathered weight of the sealed assembly is gathered. The word is sealed. The old-decree stands toward.
> **Parliament is seated. The word is sealed. The standing law is in force.**
**From the Third Ashfold Decree.**
> *Ghrim shkarr okkim vashkaan. Ghrim drakh okkim nakhaan.*
> They unshaped-force ours open-toward. They fear ours take-toward.
> **They want savagery out of us. They come for our fear and mean to take it.**
> *Durraak. Durraak. Brakhaan okk.*
> A-thousand-years-held. A-thousand-years-held. Bear-toward, we.
> **A thousand years. A thousand years. And we bore it.**
> *Skaal vashk. Skaal drakh. Skaal nakh ghrim.*
> Shall-not breach. Shall-not fear. Shall-not take, they.

### XII. Speaking It Badly

**Concord speakers** insert a vowel into syllabic *r*, producing *gurr* for *grr*, which is the single most reliable marker and is audible in the first word. They also emphasise by volume, which reads to a goblinoid audience as a man announcing that he has lost the argument.
**Dawi speakers** do well on the clusters and fail on vowel length, because Khazalid gradation trains the ear to hear consonants as the carriers of meaning. A Dawi speaking Vaross produces grammatically flawless sentences with every emphasis in the wrong place.
**Eressean speakers** overuse the genitive, because Eressean cannot express possession without naming a witness, and a court Eressean reaching for that structure in Vaross generates *of-of-of* chains that Vaross speakers find both incomprehensible and hilarious.
**Goblinoid speakers of Common** drop articles, since Vaross has none, and reach for compounds where Common wants an adjective, producing the flat noun-stacked speech Concord readers mistake for stupidity and that is in fact a speaker refusing to let a quality float free of the thing it describes.

### Provenance

Roots attested in *The Hobgoblin Expanse*: *grr-*, *vashuum*, *vorruk*, *khal*, *gorr*, *keth*, *vesh*, *undaar*, *vaross*, *dokk*, *vraal*, *thaal*, *thunn*, *gorrum*, and the compounds Grr-Vashuum, Vorruk-Khal, Dokk-Vraal, Keth-Gorrum, Gorr-Keth, Gorr-Veshaal, Vesh-Undaal, Gorr-Vaross, Thaal-Gorr, Kethaal, Undaar-Keth. The emphasis-by-vowel-length rule derives from the two attested pronunciations of Grr-Vashuum.
**A note on Undaar-Keth and Undaar-Gorr.** Under the compounding rule the second element governs, so *Undaar-Keth* is a seal of the standing kind and *Undaar-Gorr* is a mantle-weight of the standing kind. They are not spelling variants of one name. They are two different places, or one place renamed by somebody making a claim.

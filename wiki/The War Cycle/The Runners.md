# The Runners

*The Beast Trade, and How a Kill Becomes a Reagent*
> Compiled for the Guild of Measurewrights from rendering-house books, runner contracts, and the seizure records of the Enforcement Division. **No trade association was consulted because none exists.**
> 
> *Eleventh of the War Cycle. Companion to Hide, Horn and Bone.*

---

## The Trade Nobody Names

Every granary ward, every field dressing, every quenched blade and most of what a household alchemist sells **begins with an animal dying somewhere inconvenient.**
> **The Standing Index prices the product. Nothing prices the chain that produces it, and the chain is where the trade's real character lives.**

---

## The Clock

> **The whole industry is an argument with decomposition.**
>
> **Carry survives rendering. It does not survive rot.** A carcass begins losing usable Class III value from the moment the animal stops, **and the loss is fastest in exactly the conditions hunters most often work in.**
**Bleed and open at once** · Within the hour, ideally at the kill. **Most spoilage begins in the gut**, and a beast carried two hours intact is a beast whose value is already falling.
**Cool, by any means** · Shade, stream, snow, the cavity opened and spread. **Warm carcass is the enemy and every technique in the trade is a technique for making a body colder faster.**
**Seal or render** · A field render is crude, lossy **and often the only option.** A sealed carry-out preserves grade **and requires equipment nobody wants to carry up a mountain.**
**The windows** · Summer ambient gives hours. Cool weather gives a day or two. **Snow gives as long as the snow lasts** — which is why the northern trade is the profitable one **and why the eastern rendering houses run at a permanent grade disadvantage they cannot fix.**

### Cold work

Some products cannot be taken at all without a cold chain. **Wyrmcold Quench requires a render that does not warm between kill and seal, and the requirement is not a preference.** A warmed render produces a compound that behaves like ordinary Class III, **which is worth a fraction and cannot be sold as what it is.**
> So there is an entire specialism of winter runners and ice-packed carries, of teams that will not take a contract after thaw, **and of rendering houses sited absurdly — at altitude, or beside a glacier, or in a district with nothing else to recommend it — because the siting is the product.**

## Who Does It

| Role | What they are |
|---|---|
| **Runners** | The hunters. Small bands, three to six, working a season, **provisioned on credit against the season's take.** They kill, field-dress, and carry — **and their competence is measured in grade rather than in kills, which outsiders never understand** |
| **Cold teams** | Winter specialists. Better paid, worse insured, **and the only people in the trade with a formal apprenticeship** — because **the cold work kills the inexperienced fast enough that the houses had to organise it** |
| **The factor** | The intermediary, **and the person who makes the money.** Provisions the band, sets the grade, buys the take, **and holds the debt** |
| **Rendering houses** | Take delivery, render, seal and sell into the Bench's system. **Sited downwind, downstream and outside walls.** *The stench is the trade's most reliable feature and is why the houses cluster where nobody with a choice lives* |
| **The knacker's end** | Everything the reagent trade does not want. Bone, hoof, tallow, hide of no grade. **The reason a carcass is worth anything at all when the Class III fraction fails** |

---

## What It Costs Them

**The kill** · Runners hunt things that were selected for the trade **because their carry is valuable, which means predators** — which means the mortality rate is not a statistic anyone publishes. **Bands lose a member most seasons. Bands that do not are the ones the factors want.**
**The carry-out** · Exposure, falls, **and the specific injuries of men carrying a hundred and forty pounds of wet weight down broken ground against a clock.**
**The cold** · Cold teams accept frostbite as a term of employment. **The apprenticeship exists to teach them which parts they can afford to lose.**
**The mark** · Handling Class III daily seats carry. **The renderer's mark in the sclera and nailbeds is universal in the trade and identifies a runner across a room for the rest of their life.**
> **When they do not come back** · There is no provision. **The band's debt to the factor survives the band.**
>
> Families in runner districts inherit contracts, **and the practice of a widow working off her husband's season is common enough to have a name in three languages.**

---

## The Factor

> **The man who has never killed anything is the only one in the chain who is reliably rich.**
The factor stands between the runner and the Bench and makes money **on four things at once.**
**Provisioning** · The band is outfitted on credit at the factor's prices, **which are the only prices available at the head of a valley in the week before a season.**
> **The grade** · The factor sets the grade at purchase. **There is no coil at a buying post, no reference salt, and no appeal.**
>
> A band told its take is Carry 30 when it is Carry 55 **has no instrument with which to disagree** and will be told to take it elsewhere, **which is a two-week walk.**
**The debt** · A bad season does not clear. **It rolls** — and a band three seasons in debt is a band that **hunts where the factor says and sells at what the factor offers.**
**The spread** · The factor buys in the field and sells at the house, **and the difference between those two figures has never been published anywhere in the four quarters.**
*The Guild of Measurewrights has twice proposed placing a certified assayer at the principal buying posts. The proposal has twice failed on cost. The Guild's own note records that the cost objection was raised by the factors' own trade correspondents — and that the factors have no trade association through which to raise anything.*

---

## Stigma

*Runners occupy the position every quarter reserves for people who handle death for money, and it is the same position occupied by knackers, layers-out, gravediggers and executioners.*
**What it looks like** · **Not open contempt. Exclusion from ordinary things**: the marriage that does not proceed, the parish office that goes to somebody else, the seat at the far end. *Runner families marry runner families across four quarters and this is not preference.*
**What the runners do about it** · Keep their own. Their own rites for a lost member, their own reckoning of debt and obligation, **and their own opinion of the factors, which is unprintable and unanimous.**
**Why it persists** · Because the mark is visible, because the smell is real, **and because everybody in the district is using what the runners bring and would rather not think about where it comes from.** *The stigma is the district's method of not thinking about it.*
> **The propitiation** · Every runner tradition in the four quarters has a form of words said over a kill.
>
> **The Archives classify these as folk practice. Runners classify them as the difference between hunting and butchery** — and a band that stops saying them is a band the others will not work beside.

---

## Unresolved Entries

**Assayers at buying posts** · Twice proposed, twice failed on cost. **The Guild's note about who raised the cost objection stands unanswered.**
**The spread** · **Never published.** The Logistics Division holds partial figures **and has declined to aggregate them.**
**Inherited contracts** · Whether a factor's debt survives the death of the contracting party **has been argued in three quarters and settled differently in each.** Enforcement applies the local answer.
> **Runner mortality** · **Not counted.** No trade association exists to count it **and no Division holds the file.**
>
> *Which is the same finding recorded for maternal mortality, and by the same mechanism.*

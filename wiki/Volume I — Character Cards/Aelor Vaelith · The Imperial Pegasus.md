### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of House Venrik and the Eressean crown. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Aelor Vaelith was born in the Grand Principality of Eresse, on the elven old-ground of the New World. No record names his parents. None says whether the Vaelith name joins him to Serenyra Vaelith, who carries it too.
Eresse kept its law by exact repetition, and a child of standing learned it by recital. The part given to Aelor was route-law: the air-oaths of the Principality, which skies stood open under which treaty, who had witnessed each oath, and whether the witness had been paid. He learned them in their order and gave them back without a word out of place. His teachers set him treaties to recite. They never asked him what a treaty was for. He was taught the proverb as a rule of the air: "A standard nobody witnessed is a standard held contingently."

#### The Making

He came to the air on Mirenai, a Skyglass Pegasus born from the Velthaeir cloud-archives. He learned her by breath. Rider and mount came to share one breath-count and one heading. His commands fell low and even, and she answered them by folding a wing half a feather. She could bank a gust. She gave it back later, like a stored turn.
He learned to step on the pressure ridges of the air, and his pivots in midair read like stone footing. He rode with his knees soft and his hips floating over the saddle. He climbed before he struck. He would not dive until the shield's rim was already set to catch the answer.
The night the forest burned under a pass in the Eressean mountain chains, the people of the valley went out by glider along the one corridor of air over it. Asta Venrik flew at their head with the Skyfang flights. Aelor went to the far mouth. Ash lay thick on the ridges there, and he could find no footing on them. He scrubbed a clean lane and held it. At the near end Eryndrel Vaelune of the Aerial Ward read the air and called the corridor closing, aloud, for every rider on the line. Aelor kept the lane open. The gliders came through it onto clear air, and the corridor closed behind Asta as Eryndrel had read it would. She came out through the closing.
From then on Eryndrel called Aelor's dive, and Aelor called Eryndrel's wind.
The Crown made him Sky-Marshal of the Aerial Ward and named him Imperial Pegasus, "a singular honour of Eresse." He became the Crown's champion and treaty-lancer, "the blade Eresse shows in daylight," serving under the Guild Accord's Arbitration Division for air-oath enforcement.

#### The Cost

The breath-count he shared with Mirenai fell away sharply when they were parted. He kept her close.
Ash and Rot miasma roughened the ridges under him, and in either he slipped until he had scrubbed a lane. Enclosed canyons and low ceilings cut his working room.
He was raised to know a cause by what it declared aloud, in treaty and witnessed oath. His signet and his lance both made a cause declare itself. The Signet of the First Concord spoke old treaties clean, and a liar within its hearing tasted tin. His Treaty Stroke set the lance point to an enemy banner, and the cloth would not lift for an hour unless its bearer spoke the truth of the cause. A quiet cause gave him nothing. Where the treaty was unclear, he could not tell which side it stood on.
So he gave up the call of his own dive. Eryndrel made it. The Crown sent Aelor where the enemy was known and the treaty clear, and the Ward kept a reader at his side for everything else.

#### Where They Stand

Aelor Vaelith is Sky-Marshal of the Aerial Ward and the Imperial Pegasus, the Crown's champion and treaty-lancer. He escorts imperial envoys across hostile skies. He enforces Concord Overflight, under which no siege engine moves beneath an open treaty. He interdicts Rot-carried spore clouds with cold-draft corridors and controlled descents. Where air-oaths are at stake he presents the Moon-writ at council, and when no gate is safe he carries imperial writs between realms.
Mirenai's hooves leave silent blue rings in snow. Mount and rider move as one long curve. He carries the lance Skylark, which lengthens or shortens by a hand's breadth at his heartbeat. The round shield Ur-Seal hands the excess of a blow to the next beat, and the inner hem of his Cloak of Four Phases is stitched with moon cycles.
Eryndrel often rides with him. The Ward's couriers fly Lyss Arvale between tribunals and call her "wind's conscience."

# Aelor Vaelith · The Imperial Pegasus

> **Temperance corrected under the Benediction mapping.** The sheet reads **"Benediction, stable at high speed; brief, clean surges into Severance when oath-law is invoked."**
>
> **Neither is a Stage.** *Benediction is a resonance; Severance is a Resilience sub-stat.* Benediction maps to Stage VIII, Transcendence **—** ***and "brief surges into Severance" reads as reaching toward Stage IX, Invocation, where the practitioner stops working alone and something larger answers.*** Which for a treaty-lancer invoking oath-law is exactly right.

## IX · Style

**Mount and rider move as one long curve.** *Aelor keeps knees soft, hips floating over the saddle; the lance tip writes neat, short lines at tendon and hinge.* **He never wastes altitude — diving only when the shield's rim is already set to catch the answer.** *Commands are low and even; Mirenai folds a wing by half a feather, and the world rearranges to grace their angle.*
**Signature Sequences**
**Break the Line** · **Climbs to cold air, drops in a shallow S-curve, taps three shields in passing.** *On the fourth touch a tower remembers wind and leans.*
**Ring of Mercy** · **Flies a slow circle.** *Arrows entering the ring grow uncertain and spend themselves politely in snow.*
**Treaty Stroke** · **Point kisses an enemy banner.** *The cloth refuses to lift for an hour unless its bearer speaks the truth of their cause.*

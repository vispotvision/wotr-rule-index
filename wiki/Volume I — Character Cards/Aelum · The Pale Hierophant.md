## Aelum · The Pale Hierophant

> *"Do not speak the words unless your soul can carry their weight. The veil is thin here, and I am listening."*

---

### I · Identity

**Name** · Aelum. **Called** · The Pale Hierophant, the Keeper of the Silver Vow. **Also known as** · The Horned Saint, Spirit of the Unbroken Seal.
**Role** · **Ritual Sovereign. Oath-Warden. Avenger of the Breach.**
**Gloss rights** · Unlimited. He tells every soul in earshot exactly what the room's laws are before they break one.

#### Affiliation

Presides over the Court of Veils, an order of ritual overseers with no attested seat elsewhere in the Concord's registers, kept here because Aelum cannot be written without somewhere to preside from. Answerable to **the Choir**, not to any mortal crown, in the Voyager Era. He kneels to no throne. Networked, at range, to **Nysera, the Dreaming Veil**, one of the Fourteen Titans of the Spiritual Seat.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage IX, Invocation.** *Calling on Wellsprings, Titans, or Archonic law as co-author is what moves a Domain into Realm. Aelum called on Nysera's law directly. He held a corrupt emperor's crown in stasis for a hundred years rather than let it be worn wrongly.* **A Domain that holds for a century is not a spell. It is a Realm the Continuum agreed to keep open.**

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Those who write mercy in ash. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

The Choir Sang him. It keeps no mortal court and wants none, and it is the one authority he has ever acknowledged. An Empire raised him as well.
He belongs to the Voyager Era, the years after the Codex was sealed, when the charts first opened the routes. The world had only lately begun to dream clearly again. When the Crevice opened, every dream had turned black and stayed black through the age the Archives call the Long Sleep. Lucid dreaming came back around the time the count began, and the Archives read its return as Nysera, the Dreaming Veil, waking by degrees. A line ran from Aelum to her law at range. It has not closed.
He came to preside over the Court of Veils, an order of ritual overseers. No register of the Concord gives the Court a seat.

#### The Making

The Court oversaw the sworn word. Aelum stood at the centre of each rite, inside a ritual circle whose edge kept its line to the inch over any ground. He witnessed the oaths of warring generals. He heard the vows at weddings, and the guests heard him first. Dying kings swore before him with what breath they had left.
He told every soul in earshot exactly what the room's laws were, and he told them before any of them could break one. One of his warnings is kept word for word: "Do not speak the words unless your soul can carry their weight. The veil is thin here, and I am listening."
People slowed as they came near him. Every crime a guest carried unspoken made the body heavier, and the most burdened could barely cross the floor to him. A glance from him read a soul's whole ledger.
Some oaths he only witnessed. Others he punished. A general who broke a truce sworn inside the circle went under the Veil of Separation and passed out of reality for as long as Aelum kept him there, untouchable and unable to act. Some oaths he held open past their natural end, after the kings who swore them were dead.
In the Empire that raised him, the emperor was corrupt. The work needed more than his own law could carry. Aelum called on Nysera's law directly. He held a corrupt emperor's crown in stasis for a hundred years rather than let it be worn wrongly.

#### The Cost

For a hundred years no head wore that crown. The Empire had raised him, and he held its crown hostage anyway. He knelt to no throne.
His strongest protections held only while he stood still, and so he stood still. When a determined caster fought his law, it drew on him without pause for as long as the fight went on.
He was bound by a vow of his own. If he broke it, or if anyone broke his circle by force, the damage to him would run far past the blow that caused it. His body was porcelain over structure. The structure was the part that could break.
He believed a soul bound itself when it spoke. He built his law on that. When he raised a hand, every sound inside the circle died at once, including the words a spell needed spoken to work. The same law was deaf to a killer who never spoke. A silent assassin passed through it unseen. Chaos magic kept no fixed geometry, and it broke the Decree of Silence from inside. At the circle's edge his law ended, and a man who stepped past it was free of him.

#### Where They Stand

Aelum presides over the Court of Veils. He answers to the Choir and to no one else. He has not moved his feet in longer than most crowns last. The room already answers to him. He has never needed to hit anything.
The Amethyst Censer burns near him. Its purple fog shows the entities that hide from sight and restores whoever stands in it. What is loosed at him from range can arrive as petals, or as a drift of incense smoke.
He kept his seat after the century. The Empire whose crown he held hostage could have struck him down, and it did not. Nobody knows why.
Long after his era, his century was taught at an Accord-chartered academy, and Kaelan Vorn learned it there. They never met.

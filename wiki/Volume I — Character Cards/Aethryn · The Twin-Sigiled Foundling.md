# Aethryn · The Twin-Sigiled Foundling

## Aethryn · The Twin-Sigiled Foundling

---

### I · Identity

**Name** · Aethryn. **Called** · The Twin-Sigiled Foundling.
**Also known as** · The Pale Omen, The Unfinished Child, The Circle-Bearer, The Silent Pattern.
**Epithet** · He Who Stands Between Eye and Void.
**Role** · Unclassified Foundling. Twin-Sigil Vessel. Ward of the Guild Accord.
**Gloss rights** · Never. Not even the Guild that shelters him claims the right to name what he is.

#### Affiliation

No faction holds him. The Guild Accord observes him under a standing refusal to induct or classify, and he lives under monitored care in a sealed Accord hospice-temple rather than under any charter.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage III, Ascension.** *The soul stops repeating what it was shown and produces something nobody taught it: the first technique built from nothing but its own pressure.* **Aethryn built two, the palm's siphon and the forehead's sight, before anyone could tell him such things needed a teacher.** **The dual sigils are not one Catalyst but the record of two, and the Guild has no form for a foundling who cleared the gate twice before anyone was watching.**

### VIII · Signature

#### Sigil Resonance Loop

**Effect** · Both sigils close the loop at once, and the space around him stops finishing its own sentence: perception, gravity and emotional direction all bend toward whatever the loop is reading, and Aethryn blurs at the edges as though slipping into his own shadow.
**Cost** · The Loop spends memory. Distress or sensory overload during the surge wipes his own short-term recall, and every pass through it erodes his trust in what "himself" even refers to.
**Limit** · Both sigils have to be active together. One alone reads or siphons; only the pair, closed, produces the eclipse-law field, and closing it is not a thing he has learned to do on command.
**Counter** · A Coagula-anchored grounding field, run before the Loop closes, sinters the surge's edges shut and stops it from completing. Failing that, a practitioner with a strongly imposed Dominion or a deep Temperance lead can overwrite what the Loop is trying to read.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the Loop is a wound in him or a wound in the law he sits outside of.

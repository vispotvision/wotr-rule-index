# Aivyriel · The Rot

> *"I do not kill. I simply let the earth decide how you should be repurposed."*
> **Invented Stage name with a reserved-word collision.** The card reads **"Stage IX — Blooming Zenith."**
>
> ***Zenith is Stage XIV.*** *"Blooming Zenith" is not among the sixteen, and Stage IX is Invocation.* Numeral stands, word struck — the same handling as Conquest, Judgment and Blooming.
>
> *"Verdance-Aether / Rot-Aether / Glamour-Aether"* were not Essence types. **"Aether Class: Chrysalis Sovereign"** is not among the eight; **she is Class V, Radiant.** *"Reclamation Force" is not an Attraction Force alignment in the register.*

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · **Aivyriel** — *The Bloom That Devours.*
> *"In every bud, a bite. In every petal, a promise of rot."*
**Role** · **Fae Sovereign of Bloomrot. Spirit of Perpetual Cycle. High Arbiter of Verdant Ruin.**
**Race** · **Fae, Primordial Garden Lineage.** *Natural dominion over fertile zones, fungal beds and decaying ecosystems.* **Horned crown formed from ossified root structures.** *Her body constantly blossoms and decomposes in subtle rhythm with surrounding growth.*

### Affiliation

**One of the Old Verdant Nine**, sealed and reborn through seasonal convergence. *Revered by primal fae, feared by dryads, loathed by druidic orthodoxy.* Her name is etched in ancient bark, only whispered during the shift between bloom and decay.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage IX, Invocation.** *From Invocation the practitioner asks and something larger answers — the Wellspring becomes a co-author, and the answering is not reliable and not free.*
>
> **Her own card states it better than the system does:** *"She no longer evolves — she induces evolution. Wherever Aivyriel walks, the land answers, even if screaming in bloom."*
>
> ***The land answering is the co-author. And nothing on the sheet suggests she asked it first.***

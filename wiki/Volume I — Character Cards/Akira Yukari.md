### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card, the eight archived scenes it appears in, and the record of The Yukari bloodline. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Akira Yukari was born blind at Senri's Gate, in the long hall her father had raised from cedar that still wept sap in the corners. Her eyes were a pale, clouded jade and did not work. They followed sound so closely that visitors forgot the child could not see them. She had her mother's face. From her father she had a stillness, which in her came out as a short pause between sentences.
Her father was Niran Yukari, the ward-carver who held acting authority over the enclave. Her mother, Mira, had come from the southern corridor with ink on her fingers. Naori was born before her.
Niran raised his children in the ways of his people, and the Telling came first: the full account of what the Yukari blood meant, what it cost and what it demanded. Akira heard it with her brother. In her house the blood showed itself in crimson eyes and in threads that other people could not see. None of its usual signs came in her.

#### The Making

When she was five, Naori sat with her in the practice yard, set his hand on her forehead, and showed her how to read the ground through her bare feet. It was a simplified Cymorath exercise with nothing magical in it. She mastered it in three weeks.
Then she kept going. Nobody asked her to, and she asked nobody's leave, because asking would have implied she needed it. Over two years she carried the exercise past anything her brother had designed. She learned people by their sounds, their weight and their stance. When she read deep she closed her eyes, the way her father closed his for his own deep reads. She had never seen him do it.
At seven she went looking for Naori, who had stood on the riverbank since morning. She found him by the weight of his feet on the ground. She came down the path fast, her left foot dragging where the ground ran uneven, and took his sleeve in both hands.
"You shouldn't be out here all alone."
She told him he stood exactly like Father, weight forward, head tilted. She told him Mother had made dumplings and Elder Tae would lecture him on soil if he stayed out. His jaw did a thing during the soil lectures, she said. He denied it. She told him it was doing the thing right now.
He asked how far the reading went. She set both bare feet flat on the stone and closed her eyes.
"Twenty steps," she said. "Maybe more. There's a fox in the cedar stand. She has kits. Three of them."
He told her that was not what he had taught her.
"I know," she said. "I added to it."
He put his hand on her head, his father's gesture, and they went in before the dumplings got cold. The blood had found the one path her body left open. No Yukari in the written record had carried the blood and shown none of its standard markers. She was the first.

#### The Cost

The sight her father and brother carried was never going to wake in her. She would have no crimson eyes, no threads and no corona, whatever else she grew into. She was born blind. The blood had no hand in that. In a body where the line's gifts had woken, the blood would have made up some of the lack. In hers it made up none.
She would not wear shoes, which came between her skin and the ground she read.
She could hide nothing. Each feeling she had took her whole face. She reached for everyone and pulled them toward the warm room, and she had no words yet for reaching toward someone who was not there. A meal left to go cold while someone she loved stayed outside was a wrong to her, and she went out to put it right. Naori could see where pulling away from her would lead. He never did it. She did not know he had ever had the choice.

#### Where They Stand

Akira Yukari is seven, Niran's daughter at Senri's Gate, called the Little Crow. Through the ground she can place people within twenty paces and find the heartbeats of animals in the cover nearby. She knows her brother by his heavy feet.
When Ryuka Yukari came to Senri's Gate for his Telling, she found him by his step before anyone told her who he was. She took his arm in both hands and told him she was blind. He gives off an orange glow when he is happy, and it was lost on her.
Whatever she grows into will be her own, and nobody has a name for it yet. She has not understood what the blood gave the rest of her house and did not give her. Naori cannot read her thread forward, and he does not want to. Nothing has yet tested the tie between them. Mira listens for her daughter's feet on the path and hears, before they arrive, whether the child coming home is the child who left.

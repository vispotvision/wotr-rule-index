# Artemis Amagiri Moto

**He Who Makes the Next Step Cost Too Much**
> *The Bear · The Mist-Breaker · The Closed Road*
> 
> > *"You mistook the path for permission."*

---

## Identity and Core Profile

**Race / Lineage** · Human · Moto bloodline, **Amagiri line**
**Faction / Era** · House Moto · War of the Realms Era
**Temperance Stage** · **Stage 8 — Transcendence**
> **An Amagiri aberration: a route-reader built like a siege beast.**
>
> Where most of his line favour evasion, asymmetry and pursuit-breaking from the edges, **Artemis specialises in making movement itself expensive.**
>
> He turns roads, charge lanes, retreat lines and battlefield openings **into traps that collapse toward him** — forcing enemies to meet **a wall disguised as a man.**

## Canonical System Metrics

**Aether Class** · **Temporal Bulwark Manipulator**
His Aether manifests as **dark bead-like nodes, mist-blue route script, and short-lived future-lanes** that flare into existence around limbs, choke points and striking angles.
> *His techniques feel less like casting and more like a battlefield being administratively corrected.*
> **Risk · Recursion Lock.** Repeated overuse causes him to **perceive too many valid routes at once**, slowing his judgment and **risking self-entrapment inside his own pursuit-denial logic.**
**Triune Core** · **Veltraxis · Nimbora · Coagulatio**
**Network** · Two additional Wellsprings half-networked through campaign rites · **Targeted Uninfected** · One remaining

### The ascent

**He advanced through conflict rather than ceremony.**
**Stage 6** came when he held a mountain pass long after retreat became the sane choice, **learning that survival could be imposed as law.** **Stage 7** arrived when he stopped treating battlefield movement as chaos **and began reading it as structure.**
> **Stage 8** awakened when he realised **a road, a charge, an escape and a destiny were all versions of the same thing** — continuations that could be accepted, denied, or redirected.

## Axiomatic Traits

**Closed-Road Instinct** · He unconsciously senses **the most lawful interception point within any active chase, charge or retreat.**
**Bear of the Narrow Pass** · The closer an enemy approaches through a chosen lane, **the heavier their motion becomes** — *as if intention itself is being taxed.*
**Burden Recognition** · He can feel when a battlefield is carrying too much pressure in one direction — panic, retreat, bloodlust, collapse — **and anchors himself where that pressure is most likely to break.**
**Mist-Weighted Presence** · His aura blunts enemy confidence. **Fast fighters feel crowded. Cowards feel seen. Pursuers begin second-guessing their own route choices.**

## Relics and Domain

**The Unwalked Pass** · A compressed mist-domain **where distance behaves like judgment.** Straight lines lengthen, escape angles collapse, **and every committed advance feeds him a clearer read on the next three motions.**
> The Domain does not trap opponents by sealing space. **It punishes insistence.** *The more someone tries to force a path through, the more the Domain turns that effort into debt.*
**Kuroame Prayer Orbit** · A ring of dense black Moto beads worn over shoulder and arm. **Each bead can store a declared lane-mark and shatter to refresh an interception window.**
**Ashfall Sash** · A bound cloth relic anchoring his centre of gravity, **making displacement arts far less effective against him.**
**Grey Verdict Wraps** · Weighted forearm bindings **that turn blocks and parries into route-tags.**
**The Passbreaker Mantle** · Indigo over-robe woven with Balance-thread and weathered campaign sigils, **sustaining denial-fields longer in storm terrain.**

## Lore and Trajectory

**Allegiance** · House Moto — though he functions **more like a border warden, siege interceptor and campaign beast than a courtly noble.**
**The Hollow Bridge Containment** · Held a three-span crossing against a superior force **by repeatedly closing every retreat line except the one that fed directly into his killing range.**
**The Forty-Nine Pursuers** · A hunter cadre tried to run him down across broken highland roads. **He gave each member a different valid chase route, then collapsed all of them into the same dead ground.**
**The Indigo Pass Massacre** · When raiders attempted a night descent through mountain fog, **he stood alone at the throat of the pass and made every horse, spearline and retreat path betray its rider.**

### Counters

**Chaos-logic and dream distortion** · Non-linear movement, impossible geometry and dream-warps **interfere with his route reading.**
**Featureless terrain** · Open sea, void-sand, null plains — **places with no meaningful lanes weaken his best arts.**
**Forced verticality** · True flight, blink-chains and violent axis inversion **reduce the value of his ground-based interception law.**
**Multiple simultaneous blitzers** · Too many elite speedsters from unrelated vectors **overload his route hierarchy.**
> **Current status** · Active in the storm marches between major transit territories — **where caravans, warbands, fugitives and armies all eventually meet a road he decides is his.**
>
> He is obsessed with perfecting **a domain in which no pursued thing can leave alive.**
>
> **He hunts the single enemy who once broke his pass-law cleanly**, and every campaign since **has been an attempt to create a battlefield so complete that escape itself becomes impossible.**

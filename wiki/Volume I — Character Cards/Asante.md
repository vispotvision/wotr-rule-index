## X · Techniques

The scene shows no named, constructed technique. What follows is the one working it does document, written to the required shape.

### The refusal (unnamed in the source)

Name **originated, pending ratification** — the text describes the act and never names it.

- **Effect:** the rejection of a completed relational transition. Kujo did not erase Zuberi's form; he simulated "every relationship connecting Zuberi to that form being individually withdrawn," leaving authority with no proof its holder would remain coherent. Asante's grief supplies the Obsession Force with "an absolute refusal to accept the relationship Kujo had just created."
- **Cost:** `null`. The scene prices it in condition rather than in units — shallower breathing, ragged inhales "painful" to hear, strands coiling tighter to the skin as it runs.
- **Limit:** it is fed by grief and rage, which are not renewable on demand, and it is aimed at a relationship rather than at a person. It does not put Zuberi back on his feet inside this scene.
- **Counter (R12-3 mandatory):** it is a refusal of a *created* relationship, so anything that makes the transition native rather than imposed removes the thing to refuse. It also runs on his attention — the documented handle on him is to keep taking what he cares about, which raises his output but spends him. The scene shows Kujo doing exactly this.
- **What nobody knows:** whether a refusal held long enough obliges Fate, or only delays what Fate has already begun changing.
- **The Test (R17-2):** it does *absolute refusal* to *a relationship imposed on the dead*, which under *Obsession Force fed by grief rather than hatred* produces *a transition the Planes have registered and one man will not accept*.

## XIV · Psychology

**What he refuses.** The death. Not in denial — he watched it — but as a standing position against the fact. Material registered it, the Soul Plane registered the absence, Fate had already begun rewriting every connection that depended on Dawn Guard being alive, and Asante's "entire being rejected that transition."

**What he survived.** Vaeloris, which he still sees with his eyes closed, and then Zuberi.

**What he believes that costs him.** That everything falling out of the sky is aimed at him personally — "Kujo reaching into another place Asante cared about and deciding it should be ruined too." It is what makes him formidable in this scene and it is exactly the handle Kujo has on him.

**Rage against certainty.** The scene sets him beside Zuberi as a contrast rather than a match: Zuberi's answer comes "with the calm certainty Asante's rage lacked," and Zuberi neither softens nor condemns. Asante is the one who is not calm, and the text does not treat that as a flaw to be corrected.

**Voice.** Barely-speech under load — "The name barely sounded like speech." Then it breaks the other way: "the words erupted from him." Between those two registers there is very little middle. Fuller fingerprint: **`null`**.

# Ascella · Wick

> *"Light is a way to listen with your eyes."*

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Ascella Morwyn. **Called** · **Wick.**
**Role** · **Lamplighter–Scribe. Night-route guide and echo-archivist.**

### Affiliation

**Research & Archives Division**, with field commission to the **Logistics & Supply Division** for Plateau operations.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VII, Refraction.** *The first Domain seed — small, fragile, often lasting seconds, and the first genuinely uncanny thing most people will ever witness, because a Domain seed is a piece of the world briefly agreeing to different terms.*
>
> **Her lantern establishes a hush-field that steadies Aperture Cycling within its circle.** *That is a Domain seed with a handle on it — and the reason the Sealwright teams request her by name is that she can put one down on command, in the dark, without anybody panicking.*

## II · Soul Architecture

**Essence Configuration** · **Harmonia/Mnemata bias**, with the lattice optimised for **Carrier Wave Modulation in low-sound biomes.** *Parunic Apertures cycle in narrow pulses; she excels at Glyphic Compression and at Topology softening — turning harsh outcomes into many gentle ones.*
**Aether Shell** · **Class III · Resonant.** Power moves in silence, which is the entire specialism.
**Crystal State** · Refined. **η** · ~0.60, Band C.
> **The lantern serves as an external Resonance Buffer, letting her bleed pressure without Harmonic Mute.** *That is a costed, external solution to a real structural problem — and it means the relic is not an accessory. It is load-bearing, and separating her from it does not disarm her, it destabilises her.*

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Anamnesis** · Limina. *Objects genuinely store their history in physically readable form.* **Thread-Reader — tapestry and cloth revealing their makers' choices and their time.**
**Mirithane** · Limina. **A true mirror adds nothing.** *The Gloaming Ledger: a spoken claim weighed in shadow, where lies flicker and truths hold steady.*
**Tenebra** · Limina. *Signal-to-noise reduction.* **Stillflame — light that does not echo, illumination leaving no "sound" in the Wellspring.** *Which is a genuinely elegant inversion: she uses a stealth current to make light.*

### I. IDENTITY

**As Of:** After the crater in the Vaeloris quarter
**Church Name:** Aurelian Prudentius Custos Clausorum. Conferred at investiture from the catechetical inventory, as every seat-name is. **Custos Clausorum means Keeper of the Sealed Things**, and the order has read that for eleven hundred years as a virtue of doctrinal restraint, the Word that is never spoken carelessly. Three living people read it the other way.
**Birth Name:** **Mahuo Do-hyeon of Sum-gol.** Lineage, given name, hollow-seat. The generational syllable **hyeon** was fixed by the lineage before he was born and means *manifest, made visible, brought into the open*, and it was shared by every child of his cohort, and there were eleven of them, and he is the one who lived. **On-page and in Accord filings the name appears in the reversed Concordia order as Do-hyeon Mahuo**, which the Grand Church's clerks introduced for the Register and which he has never corrected.
**Alias / Title:** The Primate. **His Excellency the Primate of Altherion** in diplomatic address, **the Word of Judger** on any filing that carries a seal, **Verbum Iudicis** as he signs
**Also Known As:** **The Kind Verdict** (Ember Magistrates' circuit towns, from people who were ruled against by him and found the ruling gentle and total) · The Sum-gol Boy (Arbitration Division, only ever behind his back, and only ever by people older than him) · **The Warm Hand** (Measurewrights, from a physical fact nobody has an explanation for)
**Race / Lineage:** **Mahuo.** The order's Register lists lineage in the same column it lists height. **He is the first non-Concordia holder of the Word in four hundred years and the first Mahuo ever**, and the significance of that is not the diversity of it. It is that Sancta Lux's single largest known doctrinal blind spot is a Mahuo working, and the order seated a Mahuo above it without once asking whether he knew why.
**Age:** 58 · **Sex:** Male
**Realm / Faction:** Sancta Lux. Seated at the Grand Church of Altherion, within the Guild Seat of Stannvaard. Ladder of Standing, rung VI
**Office:** **The Word of Judger.** Doctrinal supremacy. One vote. One veto of the Will per convocation, which he has never cast. The Will is held by Darius Ignatius Sanctus Sanctorum, who has refused investiture for eleven years, **which means the Word currently holds the only functioning vote in an institution designed so that no one man would ever hold it alone**. Neither of them has raised this in session.
**Catalyst Event:** He was nine when Sum-gol finished emptying. **Nobody ordered the Mahuo destroyed.** The family went by attrition, requisition, conscription, corridor closure, and the specific slow arithmetic of a small people whose fields are walked over twice a season by two larger peoples who are not thinking about them at all. There was no decision to point at. At nineteen, an Attestant, he stood in the Hall of Equal Breath for the first time and understood the instrument he had crossed four hundred leagues to reach: **the Oracle weighs completed acts, and what happened to his family was not an act.** No clause closed. No party finished anything. **The most honest measuring device in the four worlds returns null on the death of his entire people**, and he has spent thirty-nine years inside the order that owns it, climbing, and he has not found the amendment yet.

### X. TECHNIQUES

**THE HELD BREATH** · *Signature · Lethegraal · Suspension*
*"Nondum. Nondum. Nondum."* — Not yet. Not yet. Not yet.
**Trigger:** He stops breathing. That is the entire trigger and it is not ceremonial. The suspension seats on the interval, and the interval must be real.
**Function:** He suspends a single act, clause, working, or finding at the instant before its final state resolves, and holds it there.
**Mechanism:** Abstract and Entropic Systems, with the Vitalia half running on his own body. **Erasing information has a hard thermodynamic minimum: roughly 2.97 × 10⁻²¹ Joules per bit at body temperature, released as heat, unavoidably.** A suspended state has not been erased. It has not completed. **The Continuum charges nothing to hold it, which is why Aurelian can hold thousands, and charges everything at release, which is why he releases almost nothing.** The Mahuo lattice seats the suspension in the ambient Wellspring field rather than in his own Shell, so the holding does not draw on his reserve. **The accrued release cost has nowhere else to wait, and it waits in him, as heat.**
**Numerical Effect:** No upper bound on count. No upper bound on duration. **The oldest suspension he currently holds is thirty-one years old and he has never described what it is.**
**Target Response:** Nothing. The target experiences an interval that does not end, without ever noticing it began, because noticing would be a completed act.
**Limitation:** **He cannot release selectively in any order but the order of suspension.** A stack released is a stack released from the bottom.
**Weakness:** Every suspension is a debt with his name on the far end. **The fever is the interest.**
**Cost:** 1.8 °C, continuously, for thirty-one years, and the Grand Church's physicians have told him what a sustained low-grade pyrexia does to a Crystal's Longevity architecture over four decades. **He has the reports. He files them.**
**Counterplay:** Force completion. Anything that closes a clause faster than he can seat the interval lands normally, which means **fast, simple, finished acts beat him and elaborate ones do not.** The Enforcement Division worked this out in nine years and has never written it down.
**Codex Assignment:** [StSl] Still-Sleep · [Leth] Blood · [Sel] Measure · Primary Lethegraal · Secondary Anima Spirare · Tertiary Judicium · Limina primary with Vitalia and Fulguria cross · Abstract and Entropic Systems. **Cross-family: [Leth] Blood is a Vitalia/Biochemistry assignment operating inside a Limina working, and it is doing the seating rather than the suspending**
**First Display:** Low stakes, deliberately. A dropped censer in the Sanctified Hall that does not hit the floor for the length of a sentence, and a Primate who was looking somewhere else, and an Attestant who is the only person who saw it.
**Sensory Description:** He stops speaking mid-clause and does not inhale. The room gets fractionally warmer near him. **When it resumes, the censer lands, and it lands hard enough to dent, because it has been falling the whole time.**
**Corruption Vector:** An Obsession-Force suspension does not release. It cannot. The holder discovers that the accrued cost has grown past what any release could survive, and the only remaining option is to keep holding, forever, **which is not a choice the technique makes for you and is the choice every practitioner of it has made.**
**THE STILL COURT** · *Tenebra · Resonance Nullification · Field*
*"Nemo hic ponderat."* — No one weighs here.
**Trigger:** He sets both palms flat on a surface the room is built on. Floor, table, altar rail. It must be structural.
**Function:** Every reading, finding, and sentence attempted within the chamber returns null for the duration. Not falsified. **Unavailable.**
**Mechanism:** Abstract and Entropic Systems. Judicium reads an architectural spectrum and that spectrum is only information when compared against a reference. **Tenebra does not corrupt the spectrum and does not touch the reference.** It mutes the resonance the comparison runs on, and a comparison with no medium does not produce a wrong answer. It produces no answer, and every instrument in the room reports correctly that it has nothing to report.
**Numerical Effect:** Chamber-scale, **up to roughly 80 metres**, hard-edged. One step past the threshold and the instruments work again.
**Target Response:** Measurewrights describe it as the specific silence of a held bell. Arbiters describe it as being unable to remember what the question was.
**Limitation:** **It nulls his own readings too, symmetrically, and he has never found a way to exempt himself and has published that he stopped looking.**
**Weakness:** It is enormously conspicuous to anyone above Lector. **Using it is a confession that something in the room should not be weighed**, and he has used it four times in eleven years and all four are in the sealed appendix.
**Cost:** Comparatively cheap and that is the problem with it.
**Counterplay:** Leave the room. Take the reading outside. **Sancta Lux's own procedure already requires readings to be retaken in a second chamber, which Aurelian wrote into the manual himself at thirty-four, eleven years before he could do this.**
**Codex Assignment:** [Thal] Silence · [Ur] Balance · [Len] Line · Primary Tenebra · Secondary Judicium · Limina primary with Fulguria cross · Abstract and Entropic Systems. **Cross-family: [Ur] Balance is Judicium/Fulguria/Electromagnetism and is being used here to define the boundary rather than to measure across it**
**First Display:** A Threshold hearing on a junior Arbiter, a Primate who puts his hands on the rail without announcing anything, and a panel that adjourns without ever working out why it could not proceed.
**Sensory Description:** The two standing lamps do not change. **Nothing changes. Everyone in the room checks their own instrument twice and then looks at the floor**
**Corruption Vector:** Obsession-Force Tenebra does not null the chamber. It nulls the practitioner, permanently, and a soul that cannot be weighed cannot be found, mourned, inherited from, or buried.
**NOTHING UNFINISHED CAN BE JUDGED** · *Passive · Vow · The Refusal*

### XIII. PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION

He is tall without using it, the frame narrow through the shoulder and slightly forward at the neck, and he stands with his weight settled back as though the room were about to be handed to somebody else. There is no soldier anywhere in him. **His hands are the tell and everyone who has shaken one remembers it:** long-fingered, dry, unmarked, and warm past what a hand should be, a full degree and more above the skin of whoever is holding it, so that people come away from the greeting fractionally unsettled and unable to say why.
The hair is dark, near-black with a warm russet cast where the lamps catch it, worn to the collar and layered in a way that has clearly not been cut by anyone in Altherion. It parts unevenly and falls forward past the temples. **It is the only thing about him that looks like it belongs to a private person.** The face is narrow and long, the cheekbones high and flat, the jaw clean. His eyes are dark and set under lids that never quite lift, so that he appears at all times to be halfway through closing them, and the effect on a witness is that of being looked at by someone who finished looking a while ago and has stayed out of courtesy. The mouth is thin and holds, almost permanently, a small closed smile that is neither warm nor cruel and does not move when the rest of the face does. **Four separate Arbitration filings describe it and no two describe it the same way.**
The working dress is black. High-collared, fastened to the throat, wool over a stiffened band, cut long and plain with no record-work, no orphrey, no grade markings of any kind. **The Word is entitled to seven, and the Register lists all seven against his name, and he wears none of them.** Over it lies the mantle, and the mantle is the office: heavy indigo shading to violet where it falls, dyed with the Rimlands murex the Grand Church has bought from the same two families for three centuries, cut square across the shoulders and long to the calf. **It is worn open.** An invested seat wears it clasped at the throat, and every convocation for eleven years has watched a Primate walk into the chamber with the clasp hanging loose against his chest, and nobody has ever asked, and the only other man in the order making the same silent argument through the same missing detail is holding the other seat.
At his throat, on a plain gold chain, hangs the plumb: a gold cross-form the length of a thumb, the beam and the line of a mason's square, which is the Sel-Ur mark rendered as an object. **Shrine cultures across four worlds have taken it for a religious device and the order strikes the error from every filing that carries a seal and permits it in the street.** He has worn the same one since his elevation. It is warm. Everything he wears is.

### XIV. PSYCHOLOGY

**Core Wound:** The valley, and specifically the discovery that **an atrocity can occur with no author, and that an instrument built to find authors will therefore report, honestly and correctly, that nothing happened.** He has spent thirty-nine years inside the institution that owns the instrument, has reached the only rung with standing to amend the reference, and has understood for eleven years that the amendment cannot be written without conceding that six other Works exist over which the order has no authority at all. **The price of naming what killed his family is dissolving the only body that could name it.**
**Flaw:** He calls the holding *prudence* and it is in his conferred name and he has never once tested the word. **Suspension costs nothing and that is precisely why it is the wrong instrument for a moral problem.** He has been paying the interest in fever and calling the fever a chronic condition.
**Desire:** To make the Oracle able to weigh what was done to Sum-gol. **Eleven years at the Sealed Standard. No draft. Not one page.**
**Strength:** He cannot be rushed, bound, cornered, or made to conclude, and he has arranged an entire career around never being asked to.
**Loop:** Encounters a finding that would require an act. Suspends it. Files the suspension. Discovers the fever has risen. Reads the physician's report. **Files that too.**
**Foil:** Darius, who did all three operations alone once and built eleven procedural firewalls so that nobody could ever do it again. **Aurelian has never done any of the three and built nothing.** Same wall, opposite face, and neither has said this to the other. And Cozbi, who took the identical wound and the identical lineage and concluded that the world's problem is the people who decide things, which is a conclusion Aurelian has never been able to argue against out loud.
**Voice / Dialect:** Concordia formal register, deliberately acquired, sitting over a Sum-gol substrate that surfaces under strain in two places: he drops the copula in short assertions, **and his sentences resolve to a rising interval that never quite closes**, so that a completed statement of his sounds to a Concordia ear like the first half of something. Under real pressure the register slips further and he begins using the valley's construction, which puts the qualifier last. He speaks slowly, with pauses seated on the exhale, and **his pauses are the technique whether or not he is running it.**
**Dialogue Evolution:** At nineteen he asked. At thirty-four he wrote procedure. **Now he agrees, fully and precisely, with whatever has just been proposed, and the proposal never happens**, and it takes most people two years to notice that this is the same event every time.
**Speech:** *"Not yet. Say the rest of it."* (signature) · *"You have brought me a finished thing. I congratulate you. It is very rare."* · *"The instrument is honest. It is honest about a smaller world than the one we live in."* · *"I agree with you entirely. We will take it up at the next convocation."*
**Posture:** Hands folded, warm, in front. Head slightly back. **He does not lean in when listening**, and people consistently misread this as detachment and are consistently wrong.
**Stress Default:** He stops breathing. Not metaphorically and not as technique. **The suspension seats on the interval and under genuine strain the interval opens on its own**, and everyone senior in the Grand Church has learned to watch his chest rather than his face.

### Voice

**Notices first** · The thing left unfinished: the clause that never closed, the act with no author, the second half of a sentence someone has not said. He watches a person's breath before their face, and he looks at a cost with open interest before he looks at who is paying it.
**Sentence length** · Long and slow, clauses joined with "and" and laid down evenly, each pause seated on the exhale. In the Concordia formal register his statements still end on a rising interval that never quite closes, so a finished sentence of his sounds like the first half of something.
**Contractions** · never. The formal register was acquired deliberately and he keeps every word of it whole.
**Pet word** · "Not yet." When he wants the rest of a thought, "Say the rest of it" follows.
**Never says** · A culprit for Sum-gol, and never the word crime for it: no clause closed, nobody finished anything, and he will not claim a finding the instrument does not return. He never raises his voice to be heard; the room comes to him.
**Stumbles** · Never. He stops mid-clause and does not inhale, and when the breath comes back he resumes exactly where he left off.
**Gloss rights** · For the liturgy and the law of the Church, yes: asked what the old tongue means, he translates it plainly and then says what it does.
**Under stress** · The Sum-gol substrate comes up through the polish: the copula drops out of short assertions ("Not a threat. A description, this."), the pitch rises and does not come down, and the valley's order puts the qualifier last. He slips into the family plural for himself, doubles the words of the liturgy ("Lux, lux. Veniet, veniet."), and stops breathing. The same valley cadence shows at rest whenever he is with Mahuo kin or anyone from Sum-gol.
**In grief** · The Concordia register falls away entirely and Mahuo Do-hyeon speaks: the valley's construction, the dropped copula, the interval left open at the end of every line, the boy of nine who walked out of Breath Hollow alone.
**In joy** · The sentences stay even and pleasant; the joy is in the small closed smile, in the hands brought round from behind his back, and in the room growing fractionally warmer near him.
**Sample line** · "You are in it too, Master Sesk. That is the part people miss. It is not a curse laid on you by me."

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

#### Origin

Aurelian Prudentius Custos Clausorum was born Mahuo Do-hyeon of Sum-gol, the Mahuo hollow-seat whose name means Breath Hollow. His generational syllable was fixed before he was born. Hyeon means manifest, made visible, brought into the open. Every child of his cohort carried it, eleven of them, and he was the one who lived.
The family's working ran on the pause between breaths. The valley taught it to him at seven, before it taught him letters, and he learned the suspension from someone who had learned it there. Sum-gol already held one ground nobody surveyed. An older Fall had left the Kept Vigil in the province, still drawing on a debt nobody living had incurred, and the incorporation treaty forbade anyone to measure what it was paying.
Nobody ordered the Mahuo destroyed. The family went by attrition, requisition, conscription and corridor closure. Two larger peoples walked over its fields twice a season and were not thinking about it at all. There was no decision to point at. He was nine when Sum-gol finished emptying. Attrition left no grave, and he never had one to visit.

#### The Making

He walked out of the valley alone. From nine to fourteen he was on the road, and his practice deepened there faster than the order was ever able to explain, except by the grief.
The road ended four hundred leagues away. At its end stood the order that owned the most honest measuring device in the four worlds, and he entered it as an Attestant, a grade permitted to witness in the Hall of Equal Breath and forbidden to speak there.
At nineteen he stood in the Hall of Equal Breath for the first time and understood the instrument he had crossed four hundred leagues to reach. The Oracle weighed completed acts, and what happened to his family was not an act. No clause had closed. No party had finished anything. He watched the instrument return null on the only question he had come to ask, and the null was correct.

#### The Cost

At twenty-seven he held his first indefinite suspension, and he never said what it was. The fever began that season and never stopped. His skin ran warm from then on. The Grand Church physicians wrote it up four times as a chronic pyrexia of no known cause. He read all four reports and corrected none. He filed them.
At forty-six he held his own grief at the interval for nine months, to see what it would cost. He released it in a single afternoon. The Grand Church recorded a structural event and declined to classify it.
At forty-seven he took the Word of Judger. The order read his name, Custos Clausorum, Keeper of the Sealed Things, as doctrinal restraint, and three living people read it the other way. At the Sealed Standard he read the Seventh Objection. The amendment he had come for could not be written without conceding six other Works over which the order had no authority at all. The price of naming what killed his family was dissolving the only body that could name it. In eleven years he wrote no draft.
He never converted a finding into a sentence. The execution column under his name ran to thirty-one years of empty rows. He never cast his veto. Of the seven grade markings the seat carried he wore none, and at every convocation the clasp of his indigo mantle hung loose. Cozbi Mahuo's numbered works returned no reading at all. He had learned why in the valley before he could read, and he did not tell the order. He called the holding prudence.
For six weeks before the cellar he answered no letters, Verinus's among them. Then a note listed four of his Attestants and a scriptorium in the present tense, and he walked into the Grand Church on his own feet.
For fourteen days he hung on a frame cut from a choir stall, and four molars came out on the second day, before Corwin Sesk arrived and stopped it. Sesk took eleven strips from his thighs, seated ten nails and broke his left kneecap with a mallet. Aurelian suspended each injury as it arrived and set it on the stack. He could have taken the block apart in about four seconds, and after working that out once he did not let himself work it out again. Sesk asked what he was doing. "I am deferring it," he said, and when Sesk asked to when, he said he did not know.
On the fourteenth day Verinus came down and told him he had been standing in the interval since twenty-seven. He declared the surety for the four his own and asked for consent. Aurelian said no. Verinus entered it, and every wound went across the cell into the old man in the order of its removal. Something that had lain at the bottom of Aurelian since he was twenty-seven went with them, and he would never be able to say what it was. The four had gone out through the low cloister in the second week of the shutdown. Sesk stood by the wall with the roster in his coat.

#### Where They Stand

He came up into the precinct before dawn through a hole that had been a stair, whole in new skin, the gum closed smooth where the molars had been. His eyes opened on every fate at once. Where a man should have screamed he found a warm and pleasant feeling, and he noted it and did not enter it. He told Charles Lambert aloud that Dabney Lambert was safe, and then that he was no longer afraid of anything.
Kaalabad came down a tunnel in the sky. Aurelian stopped the halberd on two fingers and flicked it ninety yards into the cathedral's west footing. Four minutes later he held twelve futures, chose the one in which he did nothing, and watched the halberdier knee Charles and throw him. He did not experience the choosing as a decision. Nobody had told him he was load-bearing.
At the tenth hour, in the Vaeloris quarter, he told Darius that Verinus was gone and that he had felt the office go when Charles died. He had reread his own letters that morning. The file told him what he had done and could not tell him what it had been like. Darius, whom he had ordained, said he was not that man, and Aurelian agreed. Darius kept him on his left anyway. In the crater Aurelian made himself the other pan and spent into Darius with nothing sealed, and sank nine inches into the flags. He built a boundary on a seam in Fate without knowing it was his friend. When Darius closed the seal, Aurelian poured what he had left into the seam.
He believes Verinus dead. The Palatine is in Utopia, still tied to him by the line he refused, and nobody has told him whose wall he fed. The Seventh Objection is still sealed at Meridian Hold, and he holds one of the three Sealed Standard keys. Lector Ilsabet Krenn's case on him is still open.

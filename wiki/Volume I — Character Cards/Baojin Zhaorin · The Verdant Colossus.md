### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Outer trails. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Baojin Zhaorin was born at a caravan halt on the Greenroads, in the forest country the caravans crossed, to the household that kept it. He was the largest child the halt had seen, and he kept growing. The wagons stopped there to water and to mend. Drovers who bedded down near him said they slept deeper for it, and they asked for the place beside him.
He was slow to read people. A game among the other children could turn into a quarrel around him, and he would learn of it when someone was crying. He learned to stand still at those times and wait to be told what had happened. The forest he read without help. He walked paths through it that nobody else could see, and he came out of the trees on the road ahead of the carts.
Nobody taught him to fight. When men came at the halt with blades, it came out of him whole. He had a grip that burst the joint it closed on, a slam brought down from above, and a sweep of the claws that peeled flesh from armour.

#### The Making

An Accord officer came through the halt afterward and saw what was left of the men. The Vanguard entered him that season. He guarded caravans, broke sieges, and was sent where Wellsprings failed.
In the Varakh Drylands a nexus had been failing through the drought years, and the villages about it drew on what was left. The Accord sent its Vanguard, and Baojin went. Helki was already kneeling there, pouring her Essence into the nexus. Kaien Veyren, the Wellspring monk who kept it, held what flow remained. A village lamb stood in the current. Nobody could get it out. Baojin worked beside Helki with whatever answered him. The ground there was spent and gave his green work nothing. On the third day the nexus collapsed. Kaien stood nearest its heart. Baojin closed his mantle, went in, and carried him out with his face opened along the cheek and the eye.
He was sworn Keeper of the Verdant Wellspring of Zhenri, a sacred grove whose reach runs along the Greenroads. Men came against it, and he fought them inside that reach. The ground drank. Bamboo came up out of the dead, roots coiled through wounds, and the leaves dripped red with sap and blood together. Those who stood near him held steady until the killing began. The grove lived on what they spilled. He made his peace with that in the one way open to him. He took the wound that bore the load, a man drovers slept easy beside who killed this well, and made it structure. He left it open and made it hold weight on purpose.

#### The Cost

The grove fed on what was spilled in its defence, and it did not distinguish whose. His own blood went into it with the rest. The scars stayed on him.
A Beastkin caravan came down the Greenroads under his guard, with Hyo Miran walking among the wagons and selling to them. Where the road ran within the grove's reach, armed men stopped the caravan and asked for a relic Hyo carried. Baojin talked with them for as long as he could tell what was happening. Then he stopped talking. The ground drank. A drover of the caravan's own went down under the roots with the raiders. Baojin tore the roots off the drover with his hands and carried him back to the wagons. The caravan master buried him under the grove. At the next halt the caravan poured Baojin bloodwine. He drank it. He guarded that caravan until it was out of the grove's reach.
Arbitration reviewed every engagement, and Kaien Veyren came to review this one, with Shael Virellion beside him. Kaien scattered petals over the road. The echoes came up out of the ground: the approach, the talk, the drover going down under the roots. Baojin stood at the roadside and watched them through. The review Kaien sealed entered the dead and named no fault.
He had always been better at ending a dispute than at reading one. He gave up words when he could no longer tell what was happening, and the one other thing he had was final. Off the Greenroads he was a very large man with a mantle. In the Drylands that was all he had been.

#### Where They Stand

Baojin Zhaorin walks the Greenroads as a travelling guardian of the Accord's Vanguard and as sworn Keeper of the grove at Zhenri. Accord envoys trust him to settle disputes without bloodshed. They have learned that when he abandons words, no one is left to argue. Beastkin caravans pour him bloodwine in respect, knowing he will shed his own blood and theirs if the forest's peace is broken. The caravan that buried its drover under the grove pours for him still. Hyo Miran sells to the caravans he guards.
Nobody knows whether the grove chose him, or whether he was simply the first to bleed for it.

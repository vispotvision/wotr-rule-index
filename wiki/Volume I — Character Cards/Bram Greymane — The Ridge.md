# Bram Greymane — The Ridge

*The Shieldwarden's Heir · The Man Who Held · The Ridge*
> **Full Name:** Bram Greymane
> **Age:** 23 · **House:** Greymane (Stormfold)
> **Level:** 224 · **Stage:** IV Temper · **Band:** III
> **Role:** Commander of the loyalist Greymane forces, holder of the southern approach
> Godfrey's grandson. The one who stayed.

---

## Description

Godfrey's face rebuilt by a younger hand. The same structural width through the jaw, the same heavy brow ridge designed to withstand impact, the same dark eyes that read terrain for defensive value before reading anything else. Brown hair, matted and unwashed because washing requires water that the garrison cannot spare. Twenty-three and showing the specific accelerated aging that war produces — the three years on the ridge and the fighting that followed filled the places that decades would normally fill.
His brigandine is cut in two places along the left side and stitched closed by Lilia's hand. He carries the Greymane shield, Godfrey's gift, with the ridge device on its face — scratched and dented and carrying the history of every impact it has absorbed. He threw it once, on the field where his brother smiled at him from behind the Tenrai banner. He picked it up. The picking-up was the apology. The apology was permanent.
**His brother:** Wren Greymane. The one who left. The one who joined the Tenrai. The one who smiled across two hundred paces of dead men and the smile was the wound that will not close.
**His words:** *"I held, Grandfather."*
**The farm:** Six acres behind the Greymane estate where Godfrey planted rye and turnips for two boys who needed to learn that hands were for things other than weapons. One boy cannot maintain six acres.

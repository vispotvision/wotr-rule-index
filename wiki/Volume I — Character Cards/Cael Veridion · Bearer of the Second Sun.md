### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Sun-Sealed and the horizon couriers. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Cael Veridion was born in the Outer World, in a mortal city-state of the Aurelian Dust Coast. Rain passed that coast by for decades at a time. Fog came in off the sea, and copper and silver lay exposed in ground no river reached. The Aether was too weak there for great dragons to nest. Smaller draconic houses, mining enclaves and cities like his held the coast between them.
His city held its ground by an agreement with a draconic house, made long before him and properly recognised when it was made. His mother kept the city's copy. The boy grew up among its clauses: which one a mining enclave tested first in a dry year, and which term the house enforced when one lapsed. Disputes the city could not settle went to the Guild Accord. The Arbitration Division heard them, took its fees from both parties and sent the settlement home entered in ink.

#### The Making

The Arbitration Division took him into its service as a mediator. His work was divine treaty disputes, the settlements where a divine party sat at the table. Malrik Dreadveil faced him as a diplomatic opponent. Lyrielle Vaerenth, a High Elf, was his political ally.
The Division gave him the table of the Heliarch Concord, a treaty with divine parties to it, and he sat as its mediator. It broke in the hall. The parties turned on one another, and Cael kept his seat at the table.
In the course of the breaking he took the Dawn Vein Covenant, a god-binding rite that linked his soul directly to a Celestial Wellspring. It granted him divine acts without full ascension. It began taking his flesh as the price. Then he spoke. Every Domain within earshot turned toward his words, and the hall went still long enough for the parties to go out of it. By then glowing cracks had opened along his arms. It went into the record as the Heliarch Concord Collapse, and he came out of it transformed.

#### The Cost

The Covenant took its payment continuously, in flesh. Nothing reversed it. His blood carried an auric glow that burned through the skin like gold poured into flesh. It healed him. It seared whatever he struck. The healing never stopped running, and the cracks along his arms stayed open.
The Division wanted the Collapse on its record, and it sent Zahiren Vahl to take it from the floor of the hall. Cael stood in the hall while he read. Zahiren read it once, slowly. The floor gave up the treaty breaking and the parties turning, and then Cael's own voice and every Domain in the hall turning toward it. Zahiren spoke the reading into the Division's record. His veins pulsed through all of it and never burned. Cael heard his part in the Collapse entered in the words of a man who could not lie without pain. When the record was entered he left the Division's service. He took no office after it.
He set a sun sigil on the people he fought beside as well as on the people he fought. It showed in Essence Sight and nowhere else. The marked burned brighter in battle until they collapsed. He went on marking them.
Every settlement he reached after the hall, he reached with his voice. Each one was paid out of his arms.

#### Where They Stand

Cael Veridion is a free agent with no official ties. The Concord fears him and seeks him. So do the cults of the Outer World. He is called the Dawn-Brand and the Gilded Vein. He says of the sun that he carries it in his veins, "and I decide when it rises for you". When he speaks, Domains within earshot align toward his words, forcing a peace that lasts a breath or amplifying the discord already in the room. Earshot is the edge of it.
Malrik Dreadveil, his old opponent, keeps an unsteady truce with him. Lyrielle Vaerenth was his political ally. Since the Concord schism she has been his adversary, and a reluctant one. The Black Concord is rumoured to want him as a living divine weapon. Nothing past the rumour is on record.
Zahiren's reading stands in the Division's record. Zahiren distrusts the voice it records and respects the part that voice played in the Collapse. Cyranthia Valez does privately, at her Salon, the work Cael once did for the Division, in the places the Accord reaches least. He has found her hand in a settlement, and she has found his.
The cracks run on along his arms. Nobody knows what the Celestial Wellspring is owed when the flesh finishes.

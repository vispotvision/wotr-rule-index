### VIII–IX · Traits and Domain

**SENTENTIA · the Sentence (Primary Trait)**
Whatever Cruor reads, he can burn. Every substance gives off its own fixed pattern of light, like a fingerprint, and once he has read a thing's pattern he can pour heat into that pattern and nothing else, the way a microwave heats the water in bread and leaves the plate cool. He can boil the iron in one man's blood and leave the man beside him untouched, cook a Soul Crystal along its own lines through a stone wall, or melt one rivet out of a gate. Burning a man costs him about 2 million EU of his 2 billion, so he can do it all day. He can only burn what he has read, and reading takes a breath for an ordinary person and up to a full turn (6 seconds) for a practitioner whose Resilience Ward comes near his Gnosis, his eyes locked on the target and still the whole time. Anything mixed or dirty blurs its pattern (alloys, muddy water, a man caked in soot and ash) and takes him twice as long. A crowded field overwhelms him, every thing in view shouting its pattern at once, so a mob or a busy battlefield leaves him half-blind. High pressure smears patterns together, so in deep water or inside a Thaloré working his heat spreads into everything near, himself included. He is made of flesh, blood and bone too, so whenever he burns those in another, the same heat runs through him at about a tenth the strength; it is why his jaw is bare amber bone, and why he would rather burn steel, stone and Crystals than men. When he burns the living, the hinge of his jaw glows dull red.
**STANS · the Standing Now (Secondary Trait)**
Nothing Cruor watches can change. An unstable particle that is measured often enough never decays, because each look resets it, and his stare does that to anything in his sight: a working cannot finish forming, a wound cannot close or bleed out, water cannot freeze, a fuse cannot catch, a Crystal cannot break through to its next Stage, and a dying man cannot die. Movement is not change, so a held man can still walk, talk and swing a sword; he just cannot become anything other than what he was when Cruor's eyes found him. Stuttering his attention on purpose instead of holding it steady works backwards and hurries a change: a working collapses early, a cut bleeds out in seconds. Holding one thing costs about 200,000 EU a second, under 1% of his reserve for a minute. A blink breaks the hold, so he does not blink, and an unblinking eye dries and blurs in 30 to 60 seconds; smoke, a flash, dust, blood in his eyes or anything that pulls his gaze away breaks it too, and he can hold only as many things as he can watch at once. Holding and burning do not mix: a burn is a change, so he must let a held thing go before he can burn it, and in that instant everything he held moves at once. While he holds, his own body is held too, so he does not tire, bleed, heat or heal, and when he lets go all of it arrives together: the bleeding he put off, the exhaustion of every second held, and the heat of every Sentence he burned while holding. A long hold ends with him on one knee; a hold in a hard fight can put him down. The tell is his eyes going unblinking, wet and red, and whatever he holds stuttering: a flame standing still, a drop of blood hanging, a spell's light hovering half-made.
**REDITUS · the Return (Inherited Trait)**
Cruor's soul came into the world already shaped, the way shape-memory metal is set in one form at high heat and springs back to it when warmed; his body and his Crystal were set in a life before his own, and the Accord's unfiled model names whose: the First Hand, Sadara Izayoi. When his core passes about 41 °C, every bent or broken part of him snaps back to the remembered shape: bones re-set with a crack, cuts pull shut, a crushed hand unfolds, in seconds, fed by the heat his other Traits pour into him, each major wound restored drawing off its own heat and dropping his core about 1 °C. It is also the three hundred Levels nobody can explain: his Threshold melts cool into the old shape instead of searching for a new one, so he is not growing, he is coming back. It restores what is bent, never what is gone, so a severed hand, a gouged eye or flesh burned away has nothing to return to. Cold stops it dead, so pinned down in the deep north or soaked in ice water he heals like anyone. Too much heat is worst: past about 400 °C the memory forgets its old shape and takes the new one, so a burn that deep becomes his remembered shape for good, which is what happened to his jaw. It runs whether he wants it or not: a surgeon's stitches tear out, a splint is crushed, an arrowhead is driven back out through the wound it made, and every time his body is restored his convictions are restored with it, so whatever doubt he gathered is gone and he cannot repent. The tell is steam off a closing wound and the crack of bone setting itself.
**CONCENTUS · the Choir (Passive Trait)**
Everyone near Cruor slowly falls into his rhythm. Pendulum clocks on one wall drift into step through the wood they share, and soldiers on a bridge drift into step through the bridge; Cruor does the same through the Aether to every living rhythm within about 30 m of him: breath, heartbeat, footfall, a practitioner's Aether cycling. Strangers breathe with him in about 10 minutes and their hearts beat with his within the hour, and a crowd becomes a congregation without anyone choosing it, which is part of why so many survivors take the mask at the end of the forty-eight hours. In a fight an opponent's attacks fall onto his beat within about a dozen exchanges, and he reads a man who strikes on a beat as easily as print. It costs almost nothing and never switches off. A fighter who attacks off-beat on purpose does not sync, and neither do the sick, the drunk or the truly panicked, whose rhythms swing too wide to catch. A stronger shared rhythm competes (a war drum, a marching song, the hum of a draw-main), a practitioner's Harmonics Stability slows the pull, and Basilithe practitioners, whose Wellspring refuses retuning, never sync at all. The pull runs both ways: a big enough crowd drags him toward its rhythm, and when a synced crowd panics, the panic runs back up the chain into him. The tell is the room: wherever he has sat an hour, everyone breathes together and the clocks on the wall tick as one, and the Accord's agents track him by synced clocks along the roads he has travelled.
**Domain** · Present at his Stage; not yet set down.

### XIII–XV · Description, Psychology, Equipment

Just above six feet, broad through the chest and shoulders from thirty years of martial practice that exile has not softened. The lower half of his face no longer exists in any working sense: the mandible bare from hinge to hinge, bone gone deep amber from the heat that destroyed the tissue around it, the maxilla partly intact beneath what is left of the nasal bridge, the teeth locked in a rictus that is neither smile nor grimace. Above the jawline the flesh is ridged hypertrophic scar drawn tight over the cheekbones, the colour of old tallow. The eyes survived, deep-set under a brow the scarring has thickened, amber-irised and entirely lucid. He wears no mask; every other member of the Inquisition does.
Segmented dark plate, articulated at every major joint, golden filigree tracing the root glyphs of Sancta Lux ([Ur], [Sel], [Va], [Hael]) recut in a grammar the mother order does not authorize. A half-cape of arterial red from the left pauldron, the hem torn in a way that has become deliberate.

#### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

#### Origin

Cassian Tiberius Cruor was born in the Concordance, the third son of a family with no military tradition and no attachment to any chartered body. He entered Sancta Lux at sixteen as an Attestant and was a Measurewright by twenty-three, unremarkably by the order's own records. He was twenty when four hundred men burned at the Eclipse Bastion, and he learned the story as doctrine, in the Attestant's hall, from a Lector who taught it as a triumph of institutional discipline. Something in the telling did not land.

#### The Making

At the Eresse chapter he took a reading on a settlement being emptied by a practitioner two Stages above anyone in his detachment. The Ladder demanded he file it and wait for a Lector, a finding, an Arbiter and an authorization, and the settlement would have been gone before the authorization cleared the second desk. He did not wait. He killed the practitioner, he was correct, and the settlement survived. Sancta Lux stripped his standing the following week, and he wrote his one-page declaration in a cell before the tribunal that exiled him.

#### The Cost

Power came to him afterward without plateau and with no visible upper boundary, over three hundred Levels between exile and founding. Eleven years after Darius finished the post-Bastion reforms he broke from Sancta Lux with Seraphiel Aurentius at his right hand, and the Holy Inquisition has burned through civilizations since. Darius, by his own memoranda, holds the exile procedurally correct and substantively wrong, and the Inquisition his personal fault.

#### Where They Stand

He leads two hundred and eighty to four hundred zealots from no fixed position, on terms of his own choosing, and the Accord's enforcement has not arrived. Where the crowns and churches that back the Inquisition rule, it holds legal standing and enforces the Gate on who may learn magic.

# Commander Severin Bale · The Slayer

> *"I don't hurry. I remove options until the right one remains."*
> **Temperance corrected under the Benediction mapping.** The card reads **"Deep Benediction with short, brutal Severance surges bound to oath-phrases."**
>
> **Neither is a Stage.** *Benediction is a resonance; Severance is a Resilience sub-stat, and his sits at 518 — which is what the line was reaching for.* **Benediction maps to Stage VIII, Transcendence**, with the surges reaching toward Invocation.

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Commander Severin Bale. **Called** · **The Slayer.** *Head Captain of the Black Watch.*
> **Holds the same dread title as Dell, Western Theater Slayer.**
**Role** · **Sanctioned executioner and breach-commander.** *When writs read kill, Severin names the target, opens a straight road through chaos, and closes the book.*

### Affiliation

**Black Watch** — *the Accord's night division: Rot purges, rogue Mechanica interdictions, brandcraft counter-ops.* Oversees all Slayer cells — including Dell's — across the Plateau theatres.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VIII, Transcendence.** *A thing keeps its weight and gains a second weight, and both are operative.*
>
> **A name is a name and is also a sentence.** *Sentence Weight makes that literal — once he points the blade and declares a name, everything that protects that life grows heavy.* ***He is not speaking about a person.** He is speaking about a person and a verdict simultaneously, and the world takes the second reading.*

## VIII · Method

**He wastes nothing.** *Blade rides low, hip quiet, shoulders square only at the end.* **He steps in, latches snap, and the enemy's weapon discovers it is furniture.** *The next beat is decision — one cut that ends engines, banners, or bravado.* **When the rush comes, he plants the coffin guard, leans, and the charge turns itself aside as if the road corrected.**
**Name & Nail** · *Speaks the target; a Gravepin bites stone; every route not leading to that target feels uphill.*
**Latchbreak** · *Opens the spine to catch a halberd, twists, and returns a flat-edge shove that empties lungs instead of bellies — clean arrests when law wants breathing answers.*
**Seam Cut** · *Draws the blade through a shimmering fault; a Rot pocket collapses like wet canvas.*
**Wall March** · *Shield-lines learn to pivot around him; squads advance in his lee as if walking beside a moving bastion.*

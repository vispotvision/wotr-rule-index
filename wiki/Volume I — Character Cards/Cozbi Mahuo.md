# Cozbi Mahuo

*The Ledger-Keeper · Harvester of the Moto Line · Card 24*
> **Stage XIV Zenith. Level 500 of 500. Coherence Band SSS at η above 1.0** — he produces more coherent output than he spends, **the Continuum supplementing his expression as recognized law.**
>
> *The furthest reach of canonical mortal coherence. Reality acclimates to him rather than resisting.*

---

## I · Identity

**Full Name** Cozbi Mahuo — *a First Compact name, reflecting his relationship to the inhabiting soul-population he carries, not a birth name*
**Race / Lineage** Human, Mahuo line. **Multiple inhabiting souls under one sovereign identity**
**Age** **Unclear by design** — decades of harvesting have outrun any simple count
**Sex** Male in his true self and most projections. *At least one documented vessel has presented and been addressed as female — a man who treats his own body as one facade among many*
**Standing** Unaffiliated by law, **opposed by every venerable bloodline still standing.** Operates across multiple planes through projected vessels and conscripted constructs
> **Catalyst Event.** Watching his own people ground into the mud beneath the Zettari and Moto's endless war — and promising his dying brother:
>
> > ***make them remember what we were, even if they never learn our names.***
>
> **He gave the promise completely, and has spent every year since converting it into a ledger that never closes.**

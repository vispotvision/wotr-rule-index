**Judicium** · Fulguria, The Wellspring of Truth. **Secondary.** The irony is load-bearing: Judicium's law of truth is what Court Immunity bends against, and bending a truth-Wellspring rather than breaking it is the whole of her discipline.

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Withering courts. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Cyranthia Valez was born highborn in the Withering Era, into the Court-Blooded Strategist Line of the western courts. The western kingdoms built in pale stone. Spoil heaps stood at the head of every valley, and the rivers ran orange below the old adits. Old-rate supply contracts had become inheritances, and a house could be bought for its supply book with the ore thrown in.
Her Line held no throne. Its people sat at other houses' council tables. She grew up in the anterooms of those councils and learned the furniture before she learned the treaties: which chair faced the door, which lamp lit which face, who had asked to be moved down the table. The pull in her worked on rooms from the start. One person answered her slowly. A full table answered her together.

#### The Making

She took no allegiance. She kept a Salon of her own and furnished it herself, the chairs, the lamps, who sits where. Envoys, guild factors and the councils of minor crowns came to it for tea and stayed for weeks. She was never quick. A room had to sit with her a long while, and then it moved toward her conclusions without being told to. Those who left her tables remembered their decisions as their own. Agreements both parties spoke aloud in her presence held, bound by the Signet of Valez on her hand. The ones she meant to see kept went into her Ledger of Promises. She explained none of it to anyone outside the arrangement.
Elder courts, guild factors and minor crowns across several capitals each came to owe her one favour. She called none of them.
Then she gave up the right to be believed for her own sake. What she kept by it is on no record. After that, everyone who heard her took whatever she said for a move. Every room still heard it as heartfelt.

#### The Cost

The peace in the Ember Crown's field was shaped in her Salon. For weeks a western kingdom's council sat there with the Ember Crown's envoys, at tables she had placed, under lamps she had set. Aurelian Veymont never came up to the capital. He sent word that the terms would be spoken in his field. She went down to his pavilion with the widow's party, Marceline Vireaux at the widow's side. She sat at the table while Aurelian swore the Ember Crown's terms on his Imperial Signet and the widow spoke the kingdom's. The kingdom knelt. Her signet holds that peace too. The man who swore it had never entered her rooms.
In the Greaves districts the head of a tin house died in the winter, and its creditors sat in her Salon through the weeks that followed. She called once on the widow to condole. While she sat in the parlour, the widow and the chief creditor agreed terms aloud. Elowen Mireille poured the tea and found nothing out of true in anything Cyranthia said. In the spring the house's credit failed inside a week. No creditor accused it of anything; each called his paper in turn. The elder heir's marriage was broken off. The creditors took the shafts and the pumps. The supply book those pumps needed was already bound in Marceline's Ledger of Wills, and it stayed there. It was her first sight of Marceline's hand. She could never use those creditors that way again.
At Talveris the queen's council sent for her before midnight, the night the court enchanter remade the queen's face. Several of its members had sat in her Salon. By morning they had agreed, member by member, that there would be no trial. Rovhen Talvasciel left that day, alive and untried.
Her networks are "the shape trust takes once you can no longer spend it directly." She holds that power is "agreement shaped correctly", and a stranger gives her nothing to shape. Neither does anyone who refuses the Salon, the tea and the treaty dinner.

#### Where They Stand

Cyranthia Valez keeps no allegiance. Every capital owes her a favour she has not yet called: the kingdom that knelt, the Ember Crown, Talveris and the citadel over Coagula among them. She wears the Signet of Valez and a crimson tassel earring. The courts call her the Lady of the Quiet Thread, the Velvet Sovereign and She Who Pulls Without Strings, the woman behind treaties and quiet collapses. Nobody has ever proven she orchestrated either kind.
The Greaves supply book stays in Marceline's ledger, and Elowen still holds the afternoon in the widow's parlour unspent. At Talveris, Seri Zeliro-Vanthe of the Accord found the council chamber calm and her flame burning high in it, and the palace servants gave Seri Cyranthia's name. She has found the hand of Cael Veridion in a settlement. He has found hers.
Nobody knows whether she has ever turned her method on her own life, or what she would have had to be losing to need to.

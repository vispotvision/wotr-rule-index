### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Reaper's crowd crews. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Cyrille Lacrim was born in the western tin districts. Her valley had a spoil heap at its head and a mining house's pit above the town. Below the old adits the river ran orange. Nothing lived in it.
Her mother kept a shelf by the door. Every morning she changed the water on it, and every night she poured it out at the threshold. Cyrille fetched it from a seep on the hill, where it came out of the rock clear. Tin hands lay on the ledge beside it. They were left for crushed or burned hands. An old woman who knew the seeps showed her which water was clean and which would sicken a child. Cyrille asked who had made it clean. Nobody had, the woman said.
At the stream-works she learned to wash tin gravel. The tin stayed in the trough because it was heavy, and the light waste went down to the orange river. She worked wet stone from thaw to ice.

#### The Making

The Withering reached the valley as a bad year in supply. The house's contract fell short, and the lift array in the deep shaft stopped. The water began coming back into the levels.
The banksman rang the pit bell, and the men below made for the ladders. Families crowded the shaft head to see faces, and the men at the ladder's top could not step off for the press. Then the banksman left the rope to haul. The crowd lost the count and began to shove.
Cyrille was at the rail with the morning's pail from the seep. A man on the top rung was knocked loose and fell back down the shaft. She tipped the pail after him. The water went down and closed on him the way a hand closes, and held him against the ladder until the men below had him. For that fall it had been water and a hand at once, and both had held.
She took up the bell rope. She rang it slow, and the crowd matched it and stopped shoving. She rang until the last man who reached the ladders had come up. The deepest level drowned with men still in it. That season the house closed the pit with ore still in it.
The Rescue and Deluge Cadre of the Guild Accord took her out of the closed valley. She asked for a cask that tolled.

#### The Cost

The water was cold, and the cold was hers. Overdrawn, she shook until Puddle and tea brought her back. Deep winter stiffened the joints of the hand, and in frost she built walls and cushions and left fine work alone. When the bell was silenced her timing drifted, as the shaft head's had.
She held that the blessing belonged to the water. Salt, rot or poison clouded it, and no will of hers could clear it. She sweetened the water first or went without. She could not hurt anyone with the hand. Marigold Vance's Verdict swing opened her aisles, and Cyrille kept them gentle. Neither could do it alone.
At Bridgemarch she travelled in Sir Rhyse Calder's escort. A face gave way in the Cuttings. The crowd came up onto the chain-bridges with salt dust in its clothes. Rhyse planted Lochgate at the head of a span whose anchor chain had parted, and turned the column onto the sound one. Sumire Nekoha took the barracks children up the tiers first. Cyrille held the bell until Sumire was clear. A bow on Sumire's apron came undone. She went over the rail. Salt had clouded Seraphine Kharza's manifests. Cyrille sweetened the water, and a nurse's cradle palm caught Sumire above the cavern floor. Then Cyrille struck the Matins Cask, and the crowd on the sound span matched the pulse and walked. Whoever went over after that landed on a Cushion Surge. She drank tea at the triage tables, shaking, while Puddle curled on Sumire.
On the Corridor road a drovers' work-song became a march and came to the way-station doors. Cyrille drew a Floodline around the arbiters' table. She did not toll. Maelia Cantorine went into the yard and cut the march across its fifth beat. For a breath the yard had no beat, and the men at the back shoved. Cyrille struck the cask once from the doorway. The shoving stopped. Nobody was struck.

#### Where They Stand

Cyrille Lacrim is the Flood-Bell, siege-relief caster and extraction lead of the Rescue and Deluge Cadre. She wears processional blue vestments edged with silver gearwork, reliquary buckles and short boots made for wet stone. The Matins Cask rides behind her like a shrine on straps. Puddle pads at her heels. It mews when the pressure is right.
She goes out with the Star Chamber and the Reaper on hot evacuations. She breaks sieges into evacuations and stampedes into columns, smothers Rot-smoke, and buys Yukizumi Yukari five silent seconds around a last word. Rhyse holds the gate and Athena the wall, and then the water is hers. Her bell waits on Maelia's cut.
Where there is no water for leagues, nobody has seen what she could raise.

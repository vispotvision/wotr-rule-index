# Cyrus Vellhar · The Azure Warden

## Cyrus Vellhar · The Azure Warden

> *"Calm waters lie. Waves tell the truth."*

---

### I · Identity

**Name** · Cyrus Vellhar. **Called** · The Azure Warden. **Also known as** · Wavesong Trickster · The Tide-Borne Savant · Laughing Undertow. **Epithet** · He Who Smiles Where Currents Turn.
**Race / Lineage** · Human, with a latent aquatic-Aether predisposition carried through a Mirithane-sensitive mutation.
**Gloss rights** · Unlimited. *He explains everything, cheerfully, and the explanation is usually a misdirection in itself.*

#### Affiliation

Former apprentice of the **Archive of Eastward Islands** *(unattested independently of this card; kept as load-bearing)*, now an independent Wellspring drifter. No Guild Accord rank claimed.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage IV, Flourishing.** *Formation of the first genuine Spirit Axis bond.* **The bond he formed was not to a person.** *The Abyssal-touched memory shell that "whispered back" is the Axis: an attachment to something that answered him, which is why he fled rather than reported it. A Spirit Axis does not require the other end to be safe.*

### II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Unclassed (Class emerges at Glory).** The awakening Shell flickers; Essence leaks unevenly, and emotion distorts weather and mood: **this is Undertow Backlash by its textbook description, not a separate flaw.**
**Attraction Layer** · Diffuse, reads outward before it reads inward; the Tide-Sense habit runs through the same Layer as the bond described above.
**Crystal Tier** · Awakened, unstable under strain. **η** · ~0.22, Band F.
**Crystal State** · `null`.
**The Laughing Shoal** · *A micro-domain formed during the Archive incident: ripple-distortion, sound-warp, sharpened illusion, and a field that pushes unstable minds toward fear or euphoria rather than calm.*

### VIII · Signature

#### Hydrowave Coil

**Effect** · Spiraling water-pressure constructs strike in coiled arcs, bypassing rigid guards by arriving from an angle a straight defense doesn't cover.
**Cost** · **Undertow Backlash.** The emotional inversion that follows overexertion, apathy where there was focus, drift where there was intent, is the same mechanism as the Muridic flicker underneath it.
**Limit** · Needs standing water or heavy humidity to draw from; dry ground and Null-Aether plains starve it before it forms.
**Counter** · **Rigid mental barriers and Stage VI+ minds resist the memory-based half of the technique outright**; the water still moves, but the confusion it's meant to carry doesn't land.
> **What nobody knows.** What the Abyssal-touched shell actually said, and whether it is still listening for an answer.

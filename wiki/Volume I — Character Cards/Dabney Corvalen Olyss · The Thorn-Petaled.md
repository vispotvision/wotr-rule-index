## XIII · Physical Description

**At six:** brown hair with a cowlick at the crown. Bare feet. Palatinate house wool let out at the cuffs twice. Small round face. Dark steady eyes.
**At thirteen:** the cowlick survived seven years and Darius. The face has narrowed, the jaw declaring itself under the childhood roundness, cheekbones surfacing. Taller by a head and a half, lean with definition through the shoulders and forearms from daily forms. Hands larger than they should be for the rest of him. Knuckles showing first calluses. The eyes unchanged. Dark, steady, holding without flinching and without challenging. Still barefoot when nobody is watching.

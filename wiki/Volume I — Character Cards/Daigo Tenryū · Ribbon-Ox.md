# Daigo Tenryū · Ribbon-Ox

> *"Tie the knot, eat the sweet, move as one — simple works."*

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Daigo Tenryū. **Called** · **Ribbon-Ox.** *The Dango Dragon.*
**Type** · **Sky-oni / dragon-blooded celebrant.**
**Role** · **Vanguard breaker, pact-binder, morale engine.**
**Look & vibe** · **Sapphire hair in a festive snarl; ivory horns banded in red cord;** kimono layered with shrine-knots and tassels; **sandals that spark when he laughs.** *Always a skewer of dango in hand — fuel and bribe in one.*

---

## II · Essence & Style

**Flow** · **Joy-first power.** *Converts rhythm — claps, chants, drum steps — and simple sugars into clean thrust and lift.*
**On the field** · **He claps twice, stomps once; the air thickens under his soles and he** ***vaults*** **like a firework.** *Red cords whip from his sleeves to lasso wrists, banners, even bad ideas, and tie them to better choices.*
**Aether Shell** · **Class III · Resonant.** **Crystal State** · Refined. **η** · ~0.60, Band C.

---

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Catharsis** · Caloria. **Pale rose-gold. Release and true clearing** — *the laughter that breaks a fevered room.*
**Fixatio** · Materia. **Anchors and seals.** *Ribbon-Pact — a red cord tied with his teeth, and tug it later and the other end warms in the signer's palm.*
**Aeolyth** · The vault, the lift, the three strides on air.
**Petralon** · Vectoria. **Seismic transmission** — the stomp, and what the ground does under it.

## VII · Kit

**Tassel Cords** · **Silk with tiny bells.** *Bind without bruising, redirect charges, stitch falling masonry long enough for people to get out.*
**Skewer Fan** · **A ring of lacquered dango sticks** — throwing pins that plant like pegs **so his feet can "step" on air for three strides.**
**Ox-Charm Geta** · **Teeth on the soles bite stone.** *Lets him lean into impossible angles and push walls the way doors are pushed.*

## VIII · Techniques

**Lantern Bull-Rush** · **Two claps and a grin; he runs** ***up*** **the air at a slant, shoulders the enemy line aside, and leaves a laughing corridor.**
**Ribbon Net** · **Cords bloom from his sleeves, knot themselves mid-flight, and drape a mob into orderly, polite bundles.**
**Skewer Comet** · **Flicks five skewers; each becomes a foothold.** *He zigzags through arrows and lands smiling on the boss's table.*
**Sweet Break** · **Bites a dango, exhales.** *The dust of sugar spins into a warm gust that clears speaking mold from mouths.*

## IX · Duties

**Crack sieges into parades.** **Turn panicked crowds into marching columns.** **Anchor festival truces so trade can happen under watchful dragons.**

## X · Limits

**Overcast moods dull him** — *a joyless commander is a wet blanket on his engine.*
**Cords will not hold against true malice.** *They are for redirecting, not torturing.*
**Fasting rites make him woozy and poetic** — **useless in a brawl, gold in a council.**

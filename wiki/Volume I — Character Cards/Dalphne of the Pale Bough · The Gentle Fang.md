# Dalphne of the Pale Bough · The Gentle Fang

> *"The land breathes — listen. Even silence howls, if you stop trying to tame it."*
> **The migration's only cross-volume inheritance.** *"Last Echo of Xanelor"* · **secret inheritor of Xanelor's Fang, a mystic fragment passed through soul-mind transmission.**
>
> **Xanelor Rafminar is a Volume III sheet** — the Wandering Fang, Stage IX, pactbearer of Fenriris, whose six constructs turned out on rebuild to be *one man's reflexes wearing six bodies*. **Dalphne received his final thought-wave and they never met.**
>
> *This is the only thread linking the two volumes, and it should be deliberate rather than coincidental.*
> **Corrected.** *"Primal Sageflow"* was not an Essence type. **The path "Drift → Mnemos → Benediction → Emanation"** folds an actual Temperance Stage name into an invented progression. **Benediction is retained** as an attested resonance.

## I · Identity

**Name** · Dalphne of the Pale Bough. **Called** · The Gentle Fang. Wolvesinged Hermit. **Last Echo of Xanelor.**
**Role** · **Wandering Sage. Nomadic Beastkin. Practitioner of Ancient Wolf Sage Arts.** *A spiritual drifter and naturalist mystic who aids villages, guides lost travellers, and communes with the primal memory of the land.*

### Affiliation

**Unaffiliated by choice.** Formerly of the **Grey Hollow Circle**, a druidic guild long dissolved. **Secret inheritor of Xanelor's Fang.** Recognised in the **Outer World Codex of Sages** as an *"Unclaimed Guide."*
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VIII, Transcendence.** *Take the load-bearing wound from the fracture record and consciously make it structure. Not heal it. Not release it. Make it hold weight on purpose.*
>
> **The Grey Hollow Circle does not exist and he is still practising its arts.** *That is the wound as architecture — a man carrying a dissolved institution forward alone, and doing it well enough that the Accord has repeatedly offered him a position he keeps declining.*

**Essence Core** · Sound, and carrying an inheritance that was never his to begin with. *Xanelor's Fang reads on a coil as a foreign structure that has integrated cleanly, which the Archives would find remarkable if anybody had told them.*
**Aether Shell** · **Class III · Resonant.** Power moves in silence.
**Attraction Layer** · Developed, and pointed at Tsuuna.
**Crystal State** · Refined. **η** · ~0.70, Band B.

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Anamnesis** · Limina. **Governing.** *Remanent magnetisation and thermoluminescent dosimetry — objects and ground genuinely store their history in physically readable form. Anamnesis is the instrument, not the miracle.* The primal memory of the land is a recording and he is the readout.
**Lethegraal** · Limina. *Relaxation time — the constant with which a perturbed system returns to equilibrium. Lethegraal shortens it. Nothing is removed and nothing is chosen; the disturbance simply metabolises down to neutral on a schedule the practitioner could not otherwise influence.*
> **This is Wolfborn Solace, and it changes what the trait means.** *He does not soothe frightened animals and he does not charm them.* **He shortens how long their agitation takes to finish.** The gentleness is real and it is not kindness — **it is a time constant.**
**Eidolyn** · Limina. **Templated nucleation with intent as the template.** *Tsuuna, and the reason a wolf that lost its physical form still has genuine solidity.*
**Verdantia** · Vitalia. Deep chlorophyll green. The living half of the land he reads.

## VII · Signature

### Wolfborn Solace

**Effect** · A touch or a hum, and the agitation in a living thing **finishes faster than it should.** Not charmed. Not commanded. **Its distress simply arrives at its end early.**
**Cost** · **What he shortens, he carries.** *Lethegraal's failure is under-damping: shorten the constant too far and the system overshoots equilibrium on every cycle, and the practitioner oscillates rather than settling.* **He has been shortening other people's grief for a very long time.**
**Limit** · **It works on the wild and not on the deliberate.** *A creature is calmable. A person who has decided something is not.*
**Counter** · **Be a person who has decided something.**
> **What nobody knows.** Whether Xanelor's final thought-wave was sent or simply escaped — **and whether Tsuuna was Dalphne's before it arrived.**

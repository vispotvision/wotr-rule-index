# Deimos · The Sin Eater

> *He arrives not as savior, but as consequence walking.*
> **Invented Stage name.** The card reads **"Stage IX — Reflection."** *Reflection is not among the sixteen; Stage IX is Invocation.* Numeral stands.

## I · Identity

**Name** · Deimos. **Called** · **The Sin Eater.**
**Type** · **Aberrant Mortal / Essence Absorber.** **Realm of Operation** · *Purganeth · Outer Realms Transit.*

### Profile

**A living embodiment of judgment — the rare mortal conduit who consumes guilt, oath-breach and moral residue as sustenance.** *His existence fulfils an unspoken clause of the Severance Laws: to remove spiritual excess without breaking divine equilibrium.* Where the Enforcement Division punishes, Deimos redeems through absorption, drawing crimes into his own lattice and converting their resonance into destructive retribution.
*He moves through cities like an unmarked trial — feared by the corrupt, tolerated by the lawful, and pitied by the penitent.* **Arbitration officers deploy him when no verdict will suffice, when truth itself festers.**

### Division Classification

**Guild Accord — Arbitration Division.** *Status:* **Unbadged Operative, Black Concord Auxiliary.** *Clearance:* **Tier II Provisional, Law of Severance Enforcement.** *Role:* **Sanctioned Absolution Agent / Guilt Extraction Specialist.**
> **Catalyst Event · Stage IX, Invocation.** *From Invocation the practitioner stops working alone. Something larger answers, and the answering is not metaphor, is not reliable, and is not free.*
>
> **The card is explicit that his ascension "did not arise from faith or remorse, but endurance — a willingness to bear what others refused."**
>
> ***He did not ask. He agreed to hold something, and it kept arriving.*** *The recorded pattern at Invocation is relief, then something worse — and Deimos exists in continual repentance, knowing every absolution claimed must also be paid for in pain.*

## II · Appearance

**The right half of his body bears a lacquer-black stain** — *a creeping burn of condensed guilt that has long surpassed skin.* **From beneath his collar the flesh glimmers as though polished obsidian, crawling to the fingertips in fractal scars.** *A curved horn coils at his temple, its spiral filled with ash-dust sigils.*
**He wears a long sash of vellum seals, each painted in red tally marks — one for every transgression devoured.**
*His weapon, the Gloamblade Absolver, drinks lamplight and exhales smoke the colour of dusk.* **When drawn, the air tastes of iron and remorse.**

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Reaper's crowd crews. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Deimos was born at Deepvein, the lowest of the chain-cities of Nalūn, where the salt beds lie thickest and the Chain Light burns amber in the walls without fuel. Contracts sealed there held tighter than anywhere else in the kingdom. His father was a bonded courier. He carried sealed instruments up the shafts to the factor houses and brought their answers down.
When Deimos was a boy, his father took two commissions he could not both keep, and he kept the better-paid one. The token of the other went dead on the supper table. Nobody in the house touched it. From that week his instruments would not seal, and any clerk attuned to the field could read that his credit was gone. The house lived on what his mother earned mending sacking for the cutting crews.
The factor his father had failed would not receive him. At home his father never spoke of the breach. Deimos grew up beside a man the whole city could read and nobody would hear.

#### The Making

He went into the cuttings as soon as he could lift a block, and his wage kept the house. He stayed at faces the other men left. He carried loads meant for two up the shafts and did not set them down to rest.
His father lived a long time unreadable. At the end he lay in the back room with the dead token beside him and wanted to say what he had done. The factor would not come down. His mother would not sit with him for it; she had paid for that breach through most of her marriage. His father took Deimos by the wrist and told him.
Deimos agreed to hold it. His father breathed out. Deimos breathed in, with his hand on his father's chest, and the breach came into him. His father's breathing eased. The token stayed dead. By morning his father was dead too, and the fingertips of Deimos's right hand had gone black.
Within the month a scribe came to the door with an inert token in his fist. After him came a clerk and a carter. Each confessed, and Deimos took each. What rose in him came of endurance, "a willingness to bear what others refused." He had not asked. He agreed to hold something, and it kept arriving.

#### The Cost

The black spread from the fingertips in fractal scars, and past the collar his flesh took the polish of obsidian. The nerves on that side died one by one, and his dreams grew colder. He painted a red tally on a vellum seal for every wrong he took and wore the seals on a long sash. When he tore one, what it held went out of him as heat or as weight, and walls, banners and engines cracked. He transformed none of it.
The Arbitration Division took him on without a badge, as an auxiliary of the Black Concord. Its officers sent for him when no verdict would suffice. In their rooms the guilty did not want to speak, and he had no gift for making anyone want to. He tied four cords to a penitent's wrist, and what came through them was true. He set an ash token on the tongue of the accused. When it did not burn, he walked away.
Innocence scalded him, and so did a justified defence, and so did a child's guilt. He kept clear of children and joyous crowds, and said their resonance "rings too clean." Starved of guilt he grew erratic. Overfull, he locked still. He had no forgiveness to give, so he balanced, and he held that every absolution had to be paid for in pain. The right side paid it.
At Altherion the Reaper sent for him. A dying cantor's congregation had sworn not to let him be taken, and they kept the oath at his door in chorus. Their children sang with them. Deimos waited on the stair while Sumire Nekoha walked the children down, and he went up after the last had passed. Seraphine Kharza's palms capped the loudest voice. Maelia Cantorine cut the chant, and it came apart into voices. Deimos tied the cords to the cantor's wrist. The cantor confessed what he had asked of his people, and Deimos took it. The oath came away from the congregation with it. He left with a new seal on the sash and the stain deeper under his collar.

#### Where They Stand

Deimos is the Sin Eater, a sanctioned absolution agent of the Arbitration Division, and his work carries him in transit through the Outer Realms. A curved horn coils at his temple. When he draws the Gloamblade Absolver, the air tastes of iron.
Yukizumi Yukari deploys him as surgical consequence: one act, one death, one release. Kaede Suyori mediates at his confession rites and holds his balance when his resonance threatens collapse. Shiragiku Iori softens what he must take. The Amalgam feasts on fear, and he feeds on the agreements born of it.
The first black is still at his right fingertips.

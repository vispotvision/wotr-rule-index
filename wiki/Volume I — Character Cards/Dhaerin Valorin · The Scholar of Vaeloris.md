# Dhaerin Valorin · The Scholar of Vaeloris

*The Scholar of Vaeloris · as of the evening at the clearing*
> **Full Name:** Dhaerin Valorin
> **Race / Lineage:** Of the Valorin line, a bloodline that works on the ink running in its own veins
> **Role:** Scholar of Vaeloris, the Carver's Seat, whose ward-architecture is built on his structural grammar. He left the academy in his nephew's hands to walk with Rengai Zettari.
> **Age:** Old enough to have raised his nephew into a man who can run an academy without his uncle's hand on the reins.
> **As Of:** The evening at the clearing where Cozbi Mahuo waited, months of walking after he left Vaeloris.

---

## Description

White hair tied back, and deep green eyes with a faint liquid-ink sheen. Fine gold geometry sits at his temple, and when he works his Scripted Soul Lattice rises along his throat in gold light. He carries a grimoire at his belt, his thumb already on the worn edge of its first page, and a wand in the other hand. His cloak is dark.
He reads everything as a construct built from a formula: a mount, a man, an argument. He stood at the treeline and read Cozbi's great spider that way, a thing grown rather than tamed.

## Voice

**Notices first** · How a thing was built and what in it is borrowed: the seam between native and stolen, the part that will fail first. He reads a mount as a formula before he reads it as a beast.
**Sentence length** · Medium, level declaratives in the cadence of a man reading a proof aloud; he corrects in two strokes, the wrong word and then the right one.
**Contractions** · sometimes: easy in conversation, gone when he states a decision or a vow ("I am certain.").
**Pet word** · "Finish": things he has finished, things left to finish, the one thing he would like to finish for someone else.
**Never says** · More than he has read. He will not call a thing strong, true or done before he has checked it, and he never speaks of his siblings.
**Stumbles** · Never. When his voice cracks it is blood, not doubt.
**Gloss rights** · Only to correct a term: he names the right word, gives one sentence of why, and stops. He does not lecture.
**Under stress** · Shorter, flatter and more formal at once: the contractions thin out, the lines drop to single declaratives ("I'm not done." "I cannot."), and he laughs where another man would plead.
**In grief** · The careful qualifiers fall away ("I find," "I would like") and the younger son comes back, the one who never matched his siblings: plain, brief and unsure of his standing.
**In joy** · The voice stays level; the joy is a smile he cannot keep off his face and a term said slowly, tasted like the last swallow of something expensive.
**Sample line** · "I have spent a great deal of my life finishing things that mattered only to me. I would like, for once, to finish something that matters to someone else."

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

#### Origin

He was born a Valorin and never quite matched his siblings for skill, and so he leaned on reading instead. He spent a lifetime in the margins of other people's grimoires, teaching a discipline most men only ever borrowed for convenience. A brother died before him, and at the end Dhaerin found himself asking the questions that brother must have carried.
Vaeloris carries his grammar. Its ward-architecture has his layered complexity, and its academy keeps his conviction that architecture must be interior before it can be exterior. Niran Yukari trained under him there, and Niran's wards stand on that grammar.

#### The Making

He raised his nephew into a man who could run the academy. When Rengai Zettari came home from the containment corridor to go after Cozbi Mahuo, Dhaerin was waiting at the edge of the path. *"I'd like to come with you."* He handed Vaeloris to his nephew. A bird of Vaeloris script caught them at the second bend with his nephew's message, and they went back for information before they went on.
Then they walked for months, through three mountain passes and a stretch of coast that took as long to cross as the mountains, to the clearing where Cozbi sat on his great spider while it ate a deer.

#### The Cost

He told Cozbi that strength borrowed from eleven souls was not strength but a parasite. Cozbi called him a failed scrap of Valorin brilliance and said he would have to die that night. Every rune Dhaerin laid through the fight, the Fixatio anchors and the Vincula Tenent chains, was one node of a single recursive array written into the battlefield. His Harmonia-trained ear had heard the borrowed essence hum out of phase in the first minute, and the array pulled stolen spirit out of Cozbi's architecture with every cycle. *"I'm not done."*
Cozbi dropped the borrowing and fought on his own current, and a spike caught Dhaerin high across the ribs. Kneeling in his own blood, he spoke the frame: *"Hoc sacramentum meum ultimum est."* He layered a Sacrament around an Oblation and a Rescind, one covenant the Continuum would witness. The world withdrew its consent from every claim Cozbi held over a harvested soul, and every one went in the same instant. His heart faltered twice and did not find its rhythm again, and he fell forward into the ash.

#### Where They Stand

Dhaerin is dead. The seal holds Cozbi still. It never reached the dragon, whose dominion Cozbi had built into its own nature rather than claimed, or the lattice he had dressed as part of himself. Cozbi calls the working elegant, and there is no performance in it.
Cozbi left the clearing sealed and took Rengai with him. The seal disturbs everything near it, so the searchers cannot trace him through it. His nephew reconstructed the rite from what it left. Mira Yukari carried the news to Niran's cot under Vaeloris: *"Dhaerin Valorin is dead."*

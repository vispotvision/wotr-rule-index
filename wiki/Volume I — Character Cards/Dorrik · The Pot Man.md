### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Outer trails. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Dorrik was born in the Southern Grass Sea of the Outer World, on black soil that grows grain and cattle well and holds little Aether. His people were drovers. They walked herds to market along the drove roads across the grass and slept beside the beasts on the way. He walked with them as soon as he could keep up. He felt living things near him, faintly: a hare down in the grass, a herd over the next rise.
As he grew, the herds went to market by other ways. The road his people had worked emptied of beasts and carts. His people followed the herds. Dorrik kept to the old road.

#### The Making

He walked the emptied roads alone. He slept where he stopped, on bare ground, like the dead. His skin weathered pale as limestone. Each morning he woke changed in his make, and he never once wondered why.
He came into the road, in the folk's phrase, on a drove road that no herd or cart used any longer. At that threshold most people weep or sit down. Dorrik stopped in the road. He put a pot on his head, and he kept walking. Helki was on the same road behind him, with the lamb Orvain at her side. Orvain's bell rang, as it rings whenever someone near it crosses a threshold that cannot be undone, and nothing silenced it. Dorrik did not turn round. She followed him as far as the fork and took the other branch. No feast was held for him. His people were on another road with the herds.

#### The Cost

The pot answers a blow. Struck on it, he sharpens, briefly and without choosing to, and he cannot bring it on himself. The man who decides when Dorrik is at his sharpest is the man swinging at his head.
He joined no guild and no company, and no hall kept his name. He walked at the tail of caravans, where the drovers let him. On the Scattered Wastes a drover saw a man among the rocks off the track and took him for dead. Dorrik went out over the rocks, got him onto his back and carried him in. The man was an elf, awake, with wounds another man would have died of. Helki knelt by him and dressed them with herbs from her own pack. The caravan carried him on a wagon. At the first water he was gone in the night, back into the Wastes, and he left nothing behind. His name was Ristra Lenovira. Dorrik never learned it.
Each time, Dorrik walked on. His people went with the herds. The shepherd went down the other fork. The elf went back to Nyx's banner.

#### Where They Stand

Dorrik walks the forgotten roads of the Outer World, an unaffiliated drifter with the pot on his head. He sleeps like the dead and wakes changed, and he has not asked why. Most people in the four quarters live and die at his measure. Ristra answers to Nyx's banner and has not paid what he owes the man who carried him. Helki knew the pot again on the Scattered Wastes. She has told nobody what her Read gave her of him.
Nobody knows what the pot was for.

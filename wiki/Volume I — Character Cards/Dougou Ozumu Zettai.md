# Dougou Ozumu Zettai

**The Iron Bison — He Whose Stillness Is the Warning**
> *Founder of the True Iron Bison*

---

## Identity

| **Race / Lineage** | Zettai-Blooded · Demonic Strain · **Atlazon-Seeded Vessel** |
|---|---|
| **Faction** | Zettai Bloodline — posthumously acknowledged sovereign |
| **Standing** | **Deceased. Ascended into the Paths** |

> The Paths is entered only by those the bloodline itself recognises as sovereign-compatible. **His acceptance there answers, by itself, the question his whole arc was built around: the lineage judged the sealed vessel worthy.**
>
> **Whether the man inside it ever was is a separate question the Paths does not ask.**
**Catalyst Event** · Born from a corrupted Zettai branch touched by demonic strain and Atlazon's pressure-law. **Origin and Catalyst are the same event for Dougou.** *He didn't discover the seal. He was made from the conditions that required one.*

## Psychology

**Core Wound** · **Built to be a closed world before he ever had the chance to decide whether he wanted to be one.**
**Flaw** · Could not distinguish, even at the end, **between mastering himself and making himself impossible to reach.**
**Desire** · To determine whether anything in the world could make his strength necessary — **a question that structurally could never resolve while he was alive, since needing something is itself a form of being reached.**
**Strength** · Doctrine over impulse. **Violence that accumulates rather than flares.**
**Loop** · Converted every feeling into weight, every wound into another chamber. **Never broke the loop. Died still running it.**
**Foil** · Rengai — **the son who took the warning and built something that lets people in instead.**
> **Voice** · Quiet, severe, patient. **Anger that accumulates rather than flares.**
>
> This never changed across his life, **and it's the register the Paths now preserves him in permanently.**

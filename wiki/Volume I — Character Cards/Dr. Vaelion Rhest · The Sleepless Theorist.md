### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Research and Archives Division. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Vaelion Rhest was born in the Kingdom of Vellsorea, on the New World's eastern seaboard, where the ground is young and the Wellsprings move. His family worked a terrace in the cloud forest on a volcanic flank, and when he was a boy a source came up under it. For a season everything on the terrace grew faster than anyone had seen anything grow. Then the source left, after nothing anyone could name. The plants went on growing without it, pale and long, until they fell over under their own length.
He went after it. He walked the flank until he found where it had surfaced, higher up, and found it again each time it moved. He wrote down in ink where it stood and how long it stayed. Nobody had asked him to. His family cleared the terrace and planted it again.
The deepest sources of that coast lay in the trench, under the Leviathan Pact. No landwalker went down to them uninvited. Nobody invited him, and their page in his notebook stayed blank.

#### The Making

He took the notebook to the Research and Archives Division. His first papers argued that a Wellspring's wandering was behaviour, that the source he had followed up the flank was doing something, and that Essence could think. The Division ridiculed them. He kept writing.
The work outgrew a single line of thought. He took up Recursive Cognition, an experimental meditative practice that fractures the self into multiple layers of thought. Most practitioners survive their fracture record. Rhest wrote his on purpose. He split his mind along the seams of a problem, gave each layer its part, and set the contradiction down in his own Crystal where it could be read.
Then he resolved it. The first true Domain formed out of that resolution: a mind that runs in parallel. The fractures stayed. He promoted them to load paths, gave them names, and called them the Versions of Sleep.
The papers that had been laughed at became the foundation of Animatria Resonance Studies and the mathematical proof that Essence can think. The Division put them under restriction.

#### The Cost

The Versions did not go back. One cured in could not be reabsorbed by wanting it reabsorbed, and one cured in error stayed a permanent feature of his mind. How many he carried went unrecorded.
Holding several at once spread the work without reducing it. He arrived owing the difference, and the difference was sleep. Sleep rarely dared to find him, whether or not a Wellspring had failed that day. The jade charms of Verdantia at his neck were the reason he was still standing.
He despised the Division's bureaucracy and avoided fieldwork. He was still the first to arrive when a Wellspring collapsed. In the Foraye country the Farath vein, which fed three settlements their water, began to fail under a junior named Azran Nemeir, who held it alone. Rhest came first from outside. He read the vein's behaviour and wrote the corrected boundary into the Aether with the Lattice Stylus. Azran stitched the corruption closed where Gonju had read it coming in.
When the Division opened its case on Cael Dareth's Causality Loop, in a workroom of Stannvaard's Upper Ring, it sent Rhest in as resonance-reader beside Serai Linthra and Valthren Odrin, with Lyssara Veyn behind them. He read the current in Cael's Crystal as a Wellspring, the first he had been sent to that ran inside a man. It was held. It was not spreading. He put one line in the file against unsealing it: a cure placed in error is not undone by wanting it undone. He had cured the Versions into himself under that rule. Nobody tried to break the seal.
When Lyssara went into the Mortalis resonance field for seven days she asked the Division for a watcher, and it sent the man who did not sleep. She asked one thing first: that nobody push Essence into her body, whatever it did. On the fifth day her heart stopped. He wore Verdantia at his neck and could have pushed. He kept his hands still and wrote down the hour. On the seventh day she woke.

#### Where They Stand

Dr. Vaelion Rhest is stationed at the Eastern Node of the Neutral Chambers as the Division's consultant for Wellspring behavioural irregularities. Atheron Dreymarch keeps the Chambers for Arbitration. Rhest treats Wellsprings as sentient organisms and calls the result Essence empathy. The one he is stationed at stabilises its growth as if responding to his calm.
He writes treatises in the middle of conversations and mutters equations over lukewarm tea from a self-heating mug that never leaves his hand. At a failing source he says, "Let's fix this before it realizes it's broken."
He talks with the Versions of Sleep. Whether they agree with him is unknown. He disappears for weeks, and nobody knows where he goes or what gives him the scars. The equations on the inside of his cloak are in his hand, and nobody has checked whether they are in his voice.

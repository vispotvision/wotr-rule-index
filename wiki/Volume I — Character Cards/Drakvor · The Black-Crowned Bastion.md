# Drakvor · The Black-Crowned Bastion

## Drakvor · The Black-Crowned Bastion

> *"Stone does not care who rules it. It only records who stood when the sky fell."*

---

### I · Identity

**Name** · Drakvor. **Called** · The Black-Crowned Bastion. **Also** · The Iron Sovereign · Grave Lion of Veyrn · The Last Monolith · Bastion King · High Concordant of the Gatefront.
**Epithet** · That Which Even Titans Address By Name.
**Role** · **High Concordant of the Guild Accord. Marshal of the Bastion Legion. Warden of the Veyrn Marches.**
**Race** · Human, giant-blooded, of the Veyrn Colossian stock. *Veyrn is unattested in the current wiki mirror; see the migration note.*
**Build** · Tower-tall, block-broad, built like quarried stone. Black hair swept back, square-cut beard. Deep cobalt mantle over blue-black harness, girded belts and the Aegis Rib plates. A rotating cyan Concord sigil sits over his right eye, brightening whenever he braces a gate or sets a line.
**Presence** · Reality feels denser near him. Troops fall into step without orders; panic drains like water down old stone.
**Gloss rights** · unlimited. A man who left a Tribunal rather than carry a verdict he called cowardice has nothing left to withhold.

#### Affiliation

**Guild Accord High Concordant.** His soul is woven into the Concord Citadel's Heart Vault, and his breath is part of what sustains the Citadel's glow, the same as every High Concordant before him. Bastion Legion Marshal and de facto Warden of Veyrn and its marches. His loyalty runs to the Realms and the civilians behind the walls first, the Codex second, and politics last. **Era** · War of the Realms.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VIII, Transcendence.** *Resolving a core internal contradiction documented in the Crystal's fracture record; first true Domain forms.*
> 
> **He walked out of a Tribunal rather than carry a verdict he called cowardice, and the walk was the contradiction resolving.** *Nobody in the hall could stand until he had gone.* **Black Crown Bastion, his first true Domain, dates from that exile: Stage VIII's own Threshold Catalyst, read off the man rather than off a table.**

### II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class V · Radiant.** Aether bends toward him; it is why a wall he has personally reinforced holds better than the same wall anywhere else.
**Attraction Layer** · Proto full Domain Lord. *The Concord Mantle's reach to nearby allies is the Attraction the old "Mind" figure was describing.*
**Crystal State** · Refined, overbuilt by design. **η** · ~0.55, Band B, rising above 0.8 inside bastions he has personally tuned.
**Path** · Body dominant, Spirit secondary, Attraction minor, Fate carried only by the Domain.
**Risk** · Over-bracing a field sends strain back into his own bones and the Aegis Rib harness; tighten Coagula too far and allies feel pinned inside their own fortification.

### VIII · Signature

#### Black Crown Oath

**Effect** · **He swears to hold a specific line, gate or people, and his Wellsprings and Bastion Chain align and spike.** *Bastion Imperium is already running underneath it: boots planted, Essence driven down, Coagula and Monolithion linking his Lattice to the structure so anything below a fixed fraction of his output fails to shift him. Faultline Breaker reads the stress seam and commits one precise impact where a siege engine or an overextended elite is about to fail anyway. Coagula Dominion spreads binding sigils through the air and stone, so evasive footwork feels like running in wet-set mortar. Sovereign's Reprisal vents whatever the Aegis Rib has stored, an expanding shock ring that lifts bodies and can stagger even an SS threat that stood too close.* Under the Oath, all five Pillars jump toward SS-Grade scaling inside his Domain, and he does not fall until the Oath condition is fulfilled or catastrophically broken.
**Cost** · **Structural Feedback.** *Carrying walls on his soul leaves real cracks; afterward his Lattice hairline-fractures, his Essence collapses, and he may be bedridden for weeks.* **Plan Rigidity** takes the rest: once he has decided this is the wall, his own Essence resists a sudden change of plan, and he needs a trusted second to argue him into a feint before he commits.
**Limit** · **Oath-keyed.** It only holds the line he swore to hold; nothing outside the sworn condition benefits from the spike.
**Counter** · **Unstable ground** (deep marsh, sand seas, sky platforms) strips out a chunk of the Monolith advantage. **Refuse his chokepoint**: persistent hit-and-run that never lets him fully anchor bleeds his Essence before the Oath pays for itself. **Divide his attention** across noncombatants he will not abandon.
> **What nobody knows.** Why the Accord let a man who once walked out on it hold the one office it cannot function without.

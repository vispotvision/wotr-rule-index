# Dunstan the Brine — The Ladle

*The Ladle · The Man Who Feeds the War*
> **Full Name:** Dunstan the Brine
> **Age:** Indeterminate (40–60) · **Origin:** Concord
> **Level:** 15 · **Stage:** 0 (unpracticed) · **Band:** 0
> **Role:** Cook, quartermaster, provisions officer for the Kokan expedition
> The cook's oath keeps everyone else alive long enough to fulfil their own oaths.

---

Indeterminate age, somewhere between forty and sixty. Build of a man who was heavy once and has been reduced by ship-work to a harder, more economical version. Face red and permanently damp from galley steam. Hands move with the speed and precision of a man who has been feeding crews in bad weather for long enough that the process requires no conscious thought. He cooks by feel and by timing, and the crew eats without comment because the absence of comment is the highest compliment available.
Carries a two-and-a-half-pound cast iron ladle. The ladle is not a weapon. The ladle was what his hands found when Titan Droval appeared. He asked for it to be entered into whatever record they were pretending to maintain.
His oath: *"I can feed you. I can keep the galley hot when everything else is cold. I can make salt pork edible, which is the closest thing to alchemy a man without Attunement can perform."*

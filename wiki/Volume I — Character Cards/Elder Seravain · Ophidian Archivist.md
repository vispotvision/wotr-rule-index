### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Yukari bloodline. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Seravain was born in the City Below the Concord Citadel, in the guild quarters under the Council Spire. His father was a copyist in the service of the Research and Archives Division. He recopied records whose ink had failed. When a sheet had gone too far to copy whole, he chose what went across to the new one. The rest was scraped from the old sheet, and the sheet was used again.
The boy stood at his elbow with the wax. He learned to read a damaged page for the lines the others depended on. He was quick at it. His father's hands were black to the knuckle. Once Seravain asked what had been on the parts that never went across. His father sealed the new sheet and entered it on the roll. "The copy is the record now," he said.

#### The Making

Seravain entered the Division as a copyist and left the copying room for the Ophidian Studies Cell. The Cell kept serpents and the long records of their venoms. He learned there that a snake's venom follows what its line has hunted, so that every bite carries an account of the prey. He milked the snakes himself and entered each yield in ink.
In those years white serpents began to come out of his robes. Nobody had bred them. They were limbs of his own being, and they whispered what they found and read the soul-resonance of whatever they touched.
The Crimson Coil Circle took him in during the same years. It was a hidden Concord splinter, dedicated to reviving the ancient Serpent-God Vaelith, and the Division did not know that one of its archivists belonged to it.
The reading that changed him came over one of his own serpents. He read its scales the way his father had read a failing sheet, line by line, and each scale was an encoded truth. The serpent stayed a serpent. It was also a library, and both held. Seravain has never set that reading down. Since then the serpents act on their own, and each of them is still him.

#### The Cost

His venom takes memory before flesh. A serpent touches someone and reads them, and the venom that follows is shaped to what it found. It keeps what Seravain judges load-bearing and discards the rest, one pass after another, until the one bitten forgets their own name before death. He says it plainly: "A lie is just venom without fangs. Allow me to correct that."
The same loss runs on him. Each time he compresses what he holds, a little more goes, and his own certainty about what mattered decides what goes. He has been compressing for a very long time. He cannot audit what he has lost.
The toxins come back slowly. He has a finite number each season. He knows the number exactly.
Behind the serpents he is a very old man with a scholar's body, and anyone who gets past them can reach him. Hazzen Skollgrin once tried to eat his serpents "to see what screaming venom tastes like." The serpents are his own limbs. Seravain has kept out of Hazzen's way ever since.
Lucien Draeve left Research and Archives openly for the Black Concord. Seravain stayed. He kept the Cell, and the Circle inside it, in the Accord's own archives, and the two of them went on trading venoms and antidotes.

#### Where They Stand

Elder Seravain is an elder of the Research and Archives Division in its Ophidian Studies Cell, Keeper of the Scarlet Serpent Codex, called the Serpent-Speaker. In secret he is the Hand of the Crimson Coil, and he runs the Circle from inside the Cell. The living glyph on his chest is the Eye of the Coil. Whoever meets his gaze while it is open risks a seizure, as the serpent-god's will coils around the soul.
Eira Vale carries unfrayed codices to the Division's archivists. Ascella Morwyn moves its records by night. Neither knows what the Cell keeps. The sealed-Wellspring maps Zahiren Vahl traded for protection went to the same Division.
He has offered to store some of Sandalphon Aestraen's voices in his library, the Echoes bound in the teal braid-seals. Sandalphon has not accepted, and Vaelen Mourcrix would recognise the offer at once. Seravain knows the legend of a Yukari ancestor asleep in an archive of the line's first memory, and he wants that archive too. He has not found it. He and Riven Vonnel have debated Concord law on venom as "ritual judgment" for hours. Atheron Dreymarch reshapes the mind in sleep, Seravain poisons it awake, and they have been rivals a long time.
Nobody knows how much of the Scarlet Serpent Codex he wrote himself.

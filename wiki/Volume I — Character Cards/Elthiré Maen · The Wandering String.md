### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Road-folk and the Eastward Isles. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Elthiré Maen was born in Caedor, on the young volcanic ground of the New World that the Guild Accord had chartered within living memory. He came of a lineage of Essence artists, players who bound thought, feeling and vibration into one continuum. The Wellsprings on that ground moved. A source could thin or shift after an eruption, or after nothing anyone could name, and the arrays at Caedor were built to be moved after it.
He grew up under the hum of those arrays. When a source shifted, the hum thinned, and the households nearest it spoke lower and slept badly. The boy lay awake and listened to both. He learned the strings in his family's house, and he learned there to wait before he played.

#### The Making

He went into the service of the Accord that had chartered his home. It made him a Pathwarden, classified Echo Courier of the Ninth Route. He was one of twelve envoys entrusted to traverse unanchored Wellspring territories and keep the Accord present there by non-militant means. He had grown up on ground where the sources would not stay put. He took the route in travelling robes of verdant and saffron, a vagabond minstrel's colours over the Accord's business. The Guild Charm of Echo Route IX sent his melody-coded messages down its channels. The road ran on into the Inner World.
On the route he listened more than he played. He sat in markets where a Wellspring had faltered and the stalls had gone quiet. He stood at the back of sanctums. At the solstice, when those who died in Accord service are sung, he heard out the room before he touched a string.
The listening, and nothing else, taught him that every emotion carries a tone, and that giving the tone form takes suffering's dominion away. A market that had gone quiet opened its stalls again the morning after he played. The tone stayed with him after the suffering had gone. He kept each one he gave form to, and he went on playing, because he valued what the playing preserved above his own soundness. What the listening cracked in him became the music.

#### The Cost

He carried every tone he had ever given form to. He put none of them down.
Word came down the Ninth Route from a boy who ran an informal circuit for the Accord on the Outer Wilds: the Wellspring under the edge of his home grove was going quiet. Elthiré came to the grove's edge with Pell on his shoulder and asked the boy what he had heard, and how. Shiro Kitsunari told him plainly. Elthiré played the Lyre of Seven Winds on the quiet ground for as long as the ground needed, while the boy kept his hands on the bark and heard the rhythm come back through the wood. The boy's nose bled. The ground had given Elthiré little to feed on, and he left thinner than he came. He went to such places by choice.
The route brought him to the chapel at Burnt Orchard. Raymond Montclair had mended its instrument on his way through with an Accord escort, and the instrument still held what it had felt. Elthiré heard it. He gave that tone form in the chapel, and the village told him about a luthier who had come with the escort and gone on. He took the luthier's story back onto the Ninth Route under his own word: "Carry the story, not the glory."
He held that the quiet mending of spirits outweighs the triumphs other people sing. He disliked heroics. When injustice rang too loud his music hardened into a storm, and the storm was rare. It frightened a village. It did not stop a practitioner. He compelled nobody, and a shut door was enough to end his working. Any peace he made could be displaced by a louder grievance, a beloved voice or gold, silently and completely, and he would not know until he called on the bond.

#### Where They Stand

Elthiré Maen walks the Ninth Route in verdant and saffron, and Pell rides his shoulder, echoing fragments of melodies lost to time. His grey-green eyes are often half-lidded in reflection. He is also Keeper of Forgotten Roads. Concord agents know him as a courier, and marketplaces and sanctums whisper the name. Anyone who asks is told what he does. At the release he says the words Vinculum Gratiae aloud.
After the Rift Refrain Incident he mediated between Concord agents and the hymn-monks at the borders of the kingdom Varuun Daskorr rules. He was last seen near those borders. Caedor, where he was born, once contested a Wellspring boundary with that kingdom before Seren Valenne. Travellers report hearing his lyre echo through fog-veiled passes. Nobody knows what the Ninth Route carries when it carries no song. He is one of twelve envoys, and no record names any of the others.

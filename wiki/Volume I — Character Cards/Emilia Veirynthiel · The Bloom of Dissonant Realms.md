# Emilia Veirynthiel · The Bloom of Dissonant Realms

## I · Identity

**Name** · Emilia Veirynthiel. **Called** · The Bloom of Dissonant Realms. Formerly **the Mouth of Reflection.** **Neither male nor female.**
**Role** · Fallen Fae. Pactborne Magus. Dreamsmoke Binder. **They embody the shifting currents between illusion and memory, madness and muse.**
**Soul Crystal title** · *Stain of the Rewritten Pact.*

### Affiliation

Formerly of the **Ceruleanth Court**, a twilight fae lineage devoted to reverie and mooncraft. Now wanders the **Liminal Archive** between realms, **crafting forbidden books from lived dreams.** Feared and admired among the **Ink-Eaten**, a cabal of forgotten storytellers and fae exiles.

### Origin & Lore

Emilia was once the Mouth of Reflection, a seer of the Faelands who **foretold not futures, but what the soul pretended not to see.** Their visions were too clear, their truths too raw. **And so, as punishment, the High Fae sealed their tongue with threads of silence and cast them into the Tarnish Between Tales — a space where stories go to rot.**
**There, they did not die. They wrote.**
Now Emilia drifts between realms as both ink and author, speaking only through the curling smoke of their pipe and the shifting stains that follow their robes. **Their presence warps memory — one may remember meeting them in a dream before the dream was ever dreamt.**
> **Catalyst Event · Stage IX, Invocation.** *Every technique before this was the practitioner's own Crystal doing something. From Invocation, the practitioner asks and something larger answers — and the answering is not metaphor, is not reliable, and is not free. The Wellspring is a co-author.*
>
> **For a writer, that is not a metaphor either.** *The recorded psychological pattern is relief followed within a year or two by something worse, as the practitioner realises the relief was no longer being solely responsible for their own output* — **and Emilia has been co-authored since the Tarnish, and has never once claimed the books are entirely theirs.**

## VII · Technique

### The Tarnish Between Tales · *Signature*

**Effect** · They do not attack a person. **They overwrite the account of the person** — what a room agrees happened, what a witness will later swear to, what the ground itself recorded. **Cost** · **Generation loss runs both ways.** Every pass takes a little more of their own record with it, and they cannot tell which passes took what.

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The sainted, the fallen and the vessel. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Emilia Veirynthiel was born into the Ceruleanth Court of the Faelands, a twilight fae lineage devoted to reverie and mooncraft. No account names their mother or father. The Court raised them among its seers. Petitioners came to those seers under the moon and asked what was coming.
Emilia never told anyone what was coming. From the first reading they looked at the one who asked, and they said aloud what that soul pretended not to see. Every vision they gave was of something already present in the asker. The Court named them the Mouth of Reflection, after the current their seeing drew on, and what the Mouth spoke gave each asker back to themself with nothing added.

#### The Making

The visions grew clearer, and the truths in them grew raw. In time the High Fae came to the Mouth themselves, and Emilia read them the way they read every petitioner. Being read was the one thing the Court would not bear. As punishment the High Fae sealed Emilia's tongue with threads of silence. Then they cast the Mouth into the Tarnish Between Tales, a space where stories go to rot.
Emilia survived there by writing. The tales around them were rotting. Emilia read them without care, and what came off the tales was ink. They wrote with it. Their own body began to leak ink as well, from dreams nobody remembered.
In the Tarnish, Emilia first asked, and something larger answered. The answer came when it chose. It was paid for every time, and from then on the Wellspring wrote beside them. Emilia was co-authored from the Tarnish onward, and they never once claimed the books were entirely theirs.

#### The Cost

The threads took the Mouth's voice. From the Tarnish on, Emilia spoke only through the curling smoke of a pipe and the stains that shifted along their robes. In that smoke they called themself "the ink smear where the name once lived."
The working Emilia brought out of the Tarnish overwrote the account of a person: what a room agreed had happened, what a witness would later swear to, what the ground itself had recorded. No contradiction remained to point at. Every pass took some of Emilia's own record too, and they could not tell which pass took what. Assay houses read them three times and produced three different signatures. Emilia could not write forward, either. What had already happened was all they could reach.
A record they had not touched could stand against them. The Guild Accord's registers were written in Lampblack Register Ink, which did not lift, and an altered line showed under Concord light. The Accord's archives became the one place Emilia would not go.
Once they stood with Ambrose Virellith in the burned Violet Palace, before the one mirror that had come through the fire. The Mouth of Reflection did not look into it. Ambrose did not look either. Emilia named Ambrose there in the pipe smoke, "The Beautiful Scar That Forgot Its Name," and left the mirror hanging where it was. They took nothing from that palace for the books, and the mirror was the one thing there they left unread.

#### Where They Stand

Emilia drifts between realms as both ink and author. They are a fallen fae, a Pactborne Magus and a Dreamsmoke Binder, and neither male nor female. They wander the Liminal Archive and make forbidden books there from lived dreams, and the Ink-Eaten, a cabal of forgotten storytellers and fae exiles, fear and admire them.
Ink still leaks from their body, and the blot-spirits it makes whisper truths to anyone who dares to stare into them. Whoever meets Emilia's gaze remembers something they never lived and feels nostalgia for something that never was. The pipe smoke shows each watcher, as image, what that watcher already intends. Time misbehaves around them. Words may come before the thoughts behind them, and wounds may heal in reverse. Some remember meeting Emilia in a dream before the dream was ever dreamt.
At Elenthyr, Emilia walked beside Evangelien Rhymaris through the ruins and took her tears as they fell, where black-market mourn-priests would have harvested them for sale. Emilia sold none. They bound the tears into a song, and the song lies in one of the Archive's books. No bard has dared to sing it. Each counts the other kin in unbeing.
Once Emilia tried to steal the shadow of Azure Serathiel. It whispered poetry back.
Nobody knows whether the tongue was ever unsealed, or whether Emilia simply stopped needing it.

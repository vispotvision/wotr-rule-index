## X · Techniques

### Harmony

- **Effect:** conferred by contact and presence. Her arms go around Sonzai and "the darkness began to expulse out of him"; her heartbeat supplies light and warmth; she finds the place in him that still holds light and harmony reveals itself there. The result is not a cure but a discipline — afterwards "the crimson-black sheath around his body contracted until almost none of it extended beyond his skin, every loose strand of Calamity abruptly withdrawn into the disciplined circulation Emira had given him," and the corruption coating the Chains of War vanishes, "leaving only ancient metal, battlefield inscriptions, and the weight of every conflict they had survived."
- **Cost:** `pending Isaac`. The scene prices it in nothing visible, which is itself worth Isaac's attention.
- **Limit:** she cannot reach someone who is not reachable — she works from a space where light is still held, so the technique presupposes one. And it is conferred, not installed: the text is careful that it is a circulation "Emira had given him," which raises the open question in XVII.
- **Counter (R12-3 mandatory):** it is delivered by proximity and contact, so distance is the counter, and Kujo's method against Sonzai — manufactured hopelessness, "watching every answer turn useless before he could reach it" — is the documented attack on its precondition. Close the space where the light is held and there is nothing for her to find.
- **What nobody knows:** whether the discipline survives her absence.
- **The Test (R17-2):** it does *resolution into truest form* to *a corrupted, leaking force*, which under *her presence and her own heartbeat* produces *a man whose Calamity circulates instead of screaming*.

## XI · Spirit Axes

**Sonzai Moto.** The scene gives the reunion in full and it is unambiguous: "Emira's arms were around him, and that alone seemed so incompatible with the last several minutes that his mind needed time to accept it. He had been drowning beneath Kujo's manufactured hopelessness… and then suddenly there was warmth against his chest, a familiar heartbeat beside his own, and his Calamity was no longer screaming in every direction."

His response is protective and slightly ashamed: he moves "positioning himself closer to Emira without pushing her away, even though some part of him understood with bitter irony that she had just dragged him out of something he had been incapable of escaping alone."

Whether this is a formal Spirit Axis: **`pending Isaac`**. It is sited here because the people someone loves are load-bearing, and in this scene she is the load-bearing member.

## XIV · Psychology

**What she leads with.** Not the battle. Her first line is "Are you okay? I have not seen you in so long!" — spoken in a ruined city under a blackened sun, with an army coming. The scene lets the incongruity stand rather than smoothing it.

**What she refuses.** To suppress. It would be easier and it is not what she does: she grants the chaos "it's truest form" instead of putting it down. Applied to Sonzai, that means she does not take his Calamity away — she makes it his.

**What she believes that costs her.** That the light is still in there. She goes looking for "the space where Sonzai held the most light" in a man mid-drowning, and the method only works because she assumes it will. The cost of that assumption, if she is ever wrong, is not paid on this page.

**Where her strength is not.** Stated by the source and worth repeating on the card: "Emira was no fighter." She walks into the middle of the scene's worst fight anyway.

**Voice.** Direct, warm, exclamatory, unguarded — a question about his wellbeing before anything else, and joy that arrives as tears. Fuller fingerprint: **`pending Isaac`**.

## XVII · Fracture Log

The source narrates no fracture of her own and no price paid by her.

Two open questions are entered here because the scene raises them and does not close them:

- **Whether the gift holds.** The discipline is "the disciplined circulation Emira had given him." Sonzai later fights with it. Whether it is his now or still hers to maintain is **`pending Isaac`**, and it is the most consequential unanswered thing on this card.
- **What granting truest form costs the one who grants it.** She resolves another person's corruption without visibly spending anything. The scene does not show her paying, which is not the same as her not paying. **`pending Isaac`**.

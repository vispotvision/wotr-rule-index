# Eryndrel Vaelune · The Trinebow

> *"If the wind agrees, the world will follow."*
> **The card states no Temperance Stage.** **Stage VIII — Transcendence assigned and flagged as originated**, on the strength of Triad Draw: *one pull, three shafts split mid-flight and arrive from different angles without crossing lines.*
>
> ***An arrow is an arrow and is also a decision, and both are operative*** — which is how a shot can "change what a field decides to do" rather than merely hitting something.

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Eryndrel Vaelune. **Called** · **The Trinebow.**
**Role** · **Eresse's line-ranger and treaty marksman.** *Breaks sieges and arguments from a hundred paces with shots that change what a field decides to do.*
**Affiliation** · **Aerial Ward of Eresse.** *Often rides with the Imperial Pegasus and the Winged Aegis on air-oath operations.*
**Look & tells** · **Quiet silver hair, angular ears, layered leathers under light plate**; *a three-limbed compound bow with star-etched cams and spike-shod tips.*

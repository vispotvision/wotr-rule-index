# Fern Stark · The Woman He Chose

*Of House Stark of the Eastern March · Mother of Hild · as of the morning the Warden came to her holdfast*
> **Full Name:** Fern Stark
> **Race / Lineage:** Northern, of House Stark of the Eastern March; sister of Lorn Stark, cousin of Zaehaerys Stark
> **Role:** Sent south by the Warden to learn how the Moto thought. The woman Sodoku Moto chose, and who chose him.
> **Age:** Not recorded. She was carrying Hild.
> **As Of:** The morning Zaehaerys rode to her holdfast in the Ironwood March, and Sodoku stood in the doorway.

---

## Description

Lean and angular, dark-haired and sharp-featured, with brown eyes. She has the Stark jaw and the Stark bone, built thinner than her cousin's, and when she holds on to something the left side of the jaw sets. She has chopped wood, hauled water and trained with a blade since she was a child, and her grip is strong.
No one ever painted her. Her daughter has her cheekbones.

---

## Voice

**Notices first** · Who in the room is being spoken about instead of spoken to, and what the other person is afraid of underneath the anger.
**Sentence length** · Long. She builds an argument the way a wall goes up, course on course, moving from the personal register to the structural one, and lands the turn on a short flat sentence.
**Contractions** · sometimes.
**Pet word** · "Cousin." She keeps the family in the sentence even while she is defying it.
**Never says** · An apology for the love; she will apologise for the surprise and the timing, never for that. She never calls her daughter "the child" or "it", and never lets her arrival be named an error.
**Stumbles** · No. When a plan collapses she slows and chooses by feel, and says both feelings aloud rather than hiding one.
**Gloss rights** · Yes, for the Moto. She was sent south to learn them, and she explains their customs to the north in plain words.
**Under stress** · Shorter and level, in the voice the Stark women use when the men have finished speaking ("If that is your will, then it will be done."). The left jaw sets, and she holds on rather than pulling away.
**In grief** · The argument stops and the hands take over: the braid, the altar, winterbell in a clay jar. What words remain are her mother's, few and plain, from the Ironwood March.
**In joy** · She has never been able to hold two feelings without narrating them. Joy comes out loud, both halves of it, at the table and in front of company.
**Sample line** · "I did not mean to cause you any misfortune, cousin. I mean that. There is no version of this where I wanted to make your life harder."

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

#### Origin

She was born into House Stark of the Eastern March, the border house that holds the Ironwood March and the eastern frontier from Hollenmark. The Starks were the first house to take Muken's alliance and the only one to take it undefeated. She was born inside the northern cold and raised inside it, and her cousin Zaehaerys, who came to hold Hollenmark as Warden, raised her. Her brother Lorn drilled in the Hollenmark yard. Her mother left her an iron ring.

#### The Making

When the Warden wanted the Moto studied, he sent Fern and not Lorn. She was built thinner, moved between houses more easily, and could talk to the southern courts in their own register without losing herself. In the south she and Sodoku Moto chose each other. Ayame Yuno blessed the union before Muken signed anything, and Fern told the Warden the queen was open to the marriage.
She came home to the Ironwood March carrying Sodoku's child. Zaehaerys rode to her holdfast and took her by the jaw and called the child a bastard, and gave her the name northern law gives a Stark child born before a recognised union: Ice. Fern answered him that a Stark and Moto daughter on the northern throne meant that House Stark did not lose a daughter to the Moto, it gained the Crown. *"If that is your will, then it will be done."* Sodoku granted the Warden his law, that the child would be Ice on his documents, and nothing past it, and dismissed him from a holdfast on Stark land. That morning Muken signed the approval for the marriage. The courier never came.

#### The Cost

Then Muken was killed and the kingdom came down. Fern named her daughter Hild and kept the holdfast, poor and watched, with Lorn beside her, and paid the Tenrai assessors to look elsewhere because the girl's hair caught a violet light in the sun. Osric of Hallenfeld came up the road with his father's ring and told her his secret, and she told Lorn. When Hild was four a man arrived who said he could count, and Fern saw what Edward Lambert was, and he stayed and kept her stores.
She gave her daughter three things that could be carried: the braid, the Stark woman's single heavy rope; an altar of an ironwood plank on two fieldstones, undyed linen, a clay jar of winterbell and her mother's iron ring; and a definition of prayer as the work of your own hands, offered without anyone watching. She liked winterbell because it kept blooming past the first frost. She told stories of the girl's father, and in them he was never the Butcher. Her last lesson was that the people you trust will carry what you cannot, and the carrying is not weakness, it is architecture.

#### Where They Stand

Fern died in winter, in the room where the altar stood, when Hild was eight. Lorn put a wooden sword in the child's hand and told her to stand up, and Brida Ashwell made her eat.
Hild carried the altar to Kharven-Seat and set it in the room that had been Ayame's. Sodoku carried the unsent approval against his chest for eleven years. At Alabaster he thought of how Fern would have liked Yoko and been jealous of her in the same breath, and would have said both aloud at the table.

#### Ties

- **Hild Ice (Stark)** · her daughter. Fern named her, and gave her the braid, the altar and the definition.
- **Sodoku Moto** · the man she chose. The approval for their marriage never reached her.
- **Lorn Stark** · her brother. He made an arrangement with her about the girl, and kept every part of it.
- **Zaehaerys Stark** · her cousin, the Warden who raised her and named her daughter Ice.

- **Edward Lambert** · she recognised what he was and let him stay.

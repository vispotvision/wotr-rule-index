# Freda Thunn-Gorr — The Caldera Wife

### The Caldera Wife · Shelf-Seat of the Thunn-Gorr

---

### I. IDENTITY

**Full Name:** Freda Thunn-Gorr (née Freda Vaal-Kess)
**Alias / Title:** The Caldera Wife (Gorr-Keth shorthand, spoken with respect rather than reduction)
**Race / Lineage:** Hobgoblin — Non-Order lineage. The Vaal-Kess family is one of the Expanse's old civilian lines, shelf-island Hobgoblins who never entered the Crimson Order's martial structure. Their Stone-Blood expression runs warmer: deep brown to burnt umber rather than the Order's red-ochre, the Legion Etchings absent or vestigial, the eyes gold rather than brass. They are Hobgoblin. They are not soldiers. The distinction matters on the Expanse.
**Age:** ~135 (middle-aged for kind)
**Sex:** Female
**Realm / Faction:** Hobgoblin Expanse (Gorr-Keth, high-island resident) — Holds and manages the Thunn-Gorr family's shelf-island constituency seat in the Keth-Gorrum. Not a delegate herself but the seat's administrative anchor: correspondence, constituent records, census coordination, and the practical work of parliamentary representation that continues whether the named delegate is present or not.
**Birthplace:** Shelf-island Vaal-Kess holding, Hobgoblin Expanse
**Current Location:** Gorr-Keth, Thunn-Gorr family holdings
**Catalyst Event:** There was no single moment. Freda’s Catalyst was cumulative: the slow understanding, arriving somewhere between her twentieth and thirtieth year, that she had chosen a man who would always leave and that the choice was hers and not his and not the world's. She did not ask him to stop. She built a life that functioned whether he was standing in it or not, and she built it well enough that it became the thing he came back to. That is its own kind of power, and it requires no Wellspring.

### IV–X. COMBAT STATS / TRAITS / DOMAIN / TECHNIQUES

N/A. Freda does not fight. She has never fought. She grew up on a shelf island in a parliamentary democracy and married a man who fights for both of them. Her combat contribution to the family is administrative: she manages the logistics of a household whose primary earner is a mercenary on contract across the world, which means she manages pay schedules, guild insurance, contract terms, repatriation clauses, and the specific paperwork that ensures a Hobgoblin mercenary's earnings reach his family rather than his employer's ledger.
She is extremely good at paperwork. The Keth-Gorrum's records division has tried to hire her twice.

### XI. SPIRIT AXES

**Krothar Thunn-Gorr** (husband) — Weight: absolute, structural. They met at fourteen on the shelf docks. He was a Crimson Order boy who had not yet spoken his first words outside the Vow; she was a Vaal-Kess girl who talked enough for both of them. The courtship lasted six years, which is short by Hobgoblin standards and was considered scandalous by exactly one aunt. She chose him knowing what the Crimson Order makes of its men, and she chose him anyway, and she has not unchoosen him in a hundred and twenty years of marriage. The love is not soft. It is architectural. It is the thing the rest of the house is built on.
**Krothar Thunn-Gorr (son), called Veylshroud** — Weight: absolute, grieved. She raised him on Gorr-Keth while his father was on contract. She watched him train with his father's chains in the caldera yard. She watched him leave the Expanse. She watched, from a distance she cannot close, as whatever grew inside him required chains of a different kind. Her son does not write. He does not visit. He goes by a name that is not hers. She knows his reasons. She does not agree with them. She waits anyway, because that is what she has always done, and she is better at it than anyone she has ever met.
**The Keth-Gorrum** — Weight: institutional, functional. She manages the Thunn-Gorr constituency seat with the particular competence of someone who has been doing the job for longer than most delegates have been alive. Her correspondence is precise. Her records are immaculate. The shelf-island constituents trust her more than they trust whoever the delegate nominally is, which is a fact everyone acknowledges and nobody fixes.

### XII. RESISTANCES

Standard Hobgoblin baseline. Stone-Blood provides elevated physical resilience. No combat-relevant resistances.

### XIII. PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION

Tall for a Hobgoblin woman: six feet four, carrying herself with the particular uprightness of someone who has spent a century in parliamentary administrative work and has learned that posture communicates before speech does. Her skin is deep warm brown, the color of dark-fired clay, smooth and unlightened, the Vaal-Kess lineage's non-Order expression. No Legion Etchings. Where the Crimson Order carries those gold bioluminescent lines, Freda's skin is unmarked, and the absence reads as a statement of lineage as clearly as the marks themselves.
Her hair is black, the true black that absorbs light rather than reflecting it, thick and heavy with a natural wave that falls past her shoulders and curls at the ends. She wears it loose, the way civilian Hobgoblin women of the shelf-islands wear it: unbound, untied, moving with her when she turns her head, a small ongoing defiance of the Order's preference for everything being locked in place. A single ear-drop hangs from her left ear, bone-white, carved into a shape too small to identify from a distance and too personal to ask about.
Her eyes are gold, not the brass-mechanical intensity of the Crimson Order but a warmer tone, amber-adjacent, carrying the particular quality of a woman who has been watching people leave and come back for a very long time and has gotten very good at reading which one they are about to do. Her features are strong, the jawline clean, the mouth set in a line that could be patience or could be judgment depending on how well you know her. Crow's feet at the corners of her eyes. Lines at the mouth. She has earned every one of them.
She dresses in the Expanse's civilian register: a fitted top with cross-laced strapping at the neckline and throat, warm-toned, showing the collarbones and the upper chest in the shelf-island style that is modest by mainland standards and practical by island ones. Over it, a sheer dark jacket with woven organic patterning, the sleeves wide, the fabric light enough to move in the volcanic-island heat. No armor. No weapons. No visible wealth beyond the ear-drop and the quality of the cloth.
She is beautiful in the way that load-bearing structures are beautiful: because the strength is visible and because nothing is decorative that does not also work.

### XIV. PSYCHOLOGY

**Core Wound:** The leaving. Not any single departure but the accumulated weight of a hundred of them. She chose a man who would always leave and she chose correctly and the cost of being correct has been a lifetime of doors closing behind someone she loves. She does not regret the choice. She sometimes regrets the accuracy of her own judgment.
**Flaw:** Cannot ask for what she needs. She built her competence around the absence, and the competence became the identity, and now asking her husband to stay would feel like admitting the architecture requires him, and it does not. It functions perfectly. It just functions alone.
**Desire:** For her son to come home. Not to stay. She is realistic about that. Just to come home long enough to use his name.
**Strength:** She is the most patient person on Gorr-Keth, which on an island of Hobgoblins is a competitive field. Her patience is not passive. It is the kind that outlasts, outwits, and quietly rearranges the room while everyone else is arguing about the door.
**Loop:** Waits. Manages. Holds the structure. Someone returns. She absorbs the return without adjusting. They leave. She continues. The structure does not crack. The woman inside it is a separate question.
**Foil:** Her husband. He leaves to fight and comes back to her. She stays and fights and he never sees it.
**Voice / Dialect:** Non-Order Hobgoblin civilian register. More conversational than the Crimson Order's clipped formality. She speaks at a measured pace but uses full sentences, complete thoughts, the syntax of someone who processes administrative language professionally and has internalized its precision without its coldness. When she is angry, her sentences get shorter, not louder. When she is hurt, she stops speaking entirely, which everyone in her life has learned to recognize and nobody has learned to fix.
**Posture:** Upright, shoulders back, hands at her sides or folded in her lap. She does not fidget. She does not touch her face. When she listens, she tilts her head slightly to the left, and the ear-drop catches light.
**Stress Default:** Goes still in a different register than her husband. His stillness is martial. Hers is administrative: the stillness of a woman who is recalculating the situation and will speak only when the new number is ready.

### XV. EQUIPMENT

**The Ear-Drop** — bone-white, carved. She has worn it since her wedding. The material is the same Shell-Tier volcanic glass the Expanse quarries from the shelf islands. Krothar carved it. He will not say what shape it is. She will not say what shape it is. The one time their son asked, they looked at each other and changed the subject simultaneously, which is the only known instance of the two of them executing a synchronized maneuver.
**Administrative Kit** — correspondence materials, seal-wax, constituency records, census ledgers. She carries a satchel the way her husband carries chains: always, without thinking about it, because the work does not stop.

### THE BLOODLINE NOTE

Krothar Thunn-Gorr: Crimson Order, deep red-ochre, Legion Etchings, brass eyes, Kha-Urrum blood.
Freda Thunn-Gorr (née Vaal-Kess): Non-Order civilian line, deep warm brown, no Etchings, gold eyes.
Their son: forest green skin. Divergent. Neither parent's coloring predicted it. The Crimson Order noticed. The Order always notices when something does not match the standard, and the standard is carved into the Rock.
The son left the Expanse carrying his father's chain technique, his mother's patience, and a skin color that told every Crimson Order Hobgoblin he met that he was not entirely one of them. He goes by Veylshroud now. The Thunn-Gorr name stays on Gorr-Keth, held by the woman who maintains the parliamentary seat and the man who maintains the chains and the empty room between them where a son used to practice.

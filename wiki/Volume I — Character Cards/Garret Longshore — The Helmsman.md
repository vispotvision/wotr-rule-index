# Garret Longshore — The Helmsman

*The Helmsman · The Man Who Trusts Nothing*
> **Full Name:** Garret Longshore
> **Age:** 48 · **Origin:** Concord (western fishing settlements)
> **Level:** 38 · **Stage:** I Ignition · **Band:** I
> **Role:** Navigator and helmsman for the Kōkan expedition
> Believes nothing. Calculates everything. Arrives anyway.

---

Broad hands cracked and salt-bleached. Forearms roped with tendon-definition from a life pulling rope against the ocean's opinion. Long, angular face, clean-shaven because beards freeze. Eyes washed-out blue from years squinting at horizons. Heavy woollen coat over canvas smock, both stained with tar and salt.
Holds the wheel with one hand and watches the compass with the eye of a man who does not trust the compass but trusts the sea less. Navigates by the tension between two distrusts.
His oath: *"I know how to hold a wheel in bad weather and how to read a compass I do not trust and how to keep a ship alive in water that wants it dead. If those things are useful, they are yours."*
When Temür walked out of the igloo in glacier-crystal armor, Garret adjusted his compass heading and said nothing. Nothing was the appropriate response.

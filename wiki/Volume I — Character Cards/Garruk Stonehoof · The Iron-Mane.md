# Garruk Stonehoof · The Iron-Mane

> *"Steel rusts. Stone crumbles. But the hoof leaves its mark forever."*
> **Invented Stage name — third instance of "Conquest."** *Not among the sixteen; Stage VIII is Transcendence.* Numeral stands. *"Stone / War / Blood"* were not Essence types.

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Garruk Stonehoof. **Called** · **The Iron-Mane.** Horn of the Highlands · **Hoofbreaker.**
**Role** · **Centaur Warlord. Battlefield Commander. Wellspring Guardian of the Iron Plains.**

### Affiliation

**Stonehoof Herdclan** — *one of the oldest and most feared centaur war-hosts in the Outer World's rolling warplains.* **Garruk serves as both chieftain and spiritual protector, chosen by the Hoof-Seer Wellspring to guard its power.**
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VIII, Transcendence.** *A thing keeps its weight and gains a second one, and both are operative.*
>
> **He is chieftain and spiritual protector simultaneously**, *and the sheet treats those as one office rather than two.* **A grazing path is a grazing path and is also a sacred boundary** — which is precisely why his tension with Thalyren Veyroot exists and why neither of them is wrong.

## VI · Traits

**Ironhide Resilience** · **Skin and equine body reinforced with Stone Essence**, capable of withstanding blades, arrows **and even some glyph-based attacks without breaking stride.**
**Hornbreaker's Charge** · **In full gallop, his forward momentum can shatter enemy formations, crush Domain barriers, and rupture magical wards with sheer kinetic force.**
**Warcaller's Presence** · **His voice, amplified through War Essence, can rally allies or instil panic in foes across a battlefield** — *affecting both morale and Essence stability.*

## VII · Signature

### The Iron Plains Roar

**Effect** · A Wellspring bound to the endless warplains. **Its Domain increases stamina, strengthens physical resistance, and inspires battle-lust in allies** — *and inside it, the Stonehoof herd becomes an unstoppable cavalry force.*
**Cost** · **Battle-lust is not a switch.** *He grants it to a herd and then commands the herd.*
**Limit** · **Open ground.** *A charge needs a run.*
**Counter** · **Broken terrain, and a feint.**

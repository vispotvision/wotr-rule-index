### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Thalyren's groves and the highland plains. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Garruk Stonehoof was born to the Stonehoof Herdclan, one of the oldest and most feared centaur war-hosts on the rolling warplains of the Outer World. The herd lived on the move. It kept its grazing paths season by season, and its hooves had worn the oldest of them so deep into the plain that no grass grew in them. Nobody in the herd could say who had first walked them.
His voice carried further than a voice should. When he called from the tail of the herd on the march, heads turned at the front of it. On the open plain he ran in straight lines at full stride. On the paths he walked.

#### The Making

Stone came into his hide in his first fights. It hardened his skin and his horse's body, and blades, arrows and some glyph-work broke or turned on him. He did not break stride for any of it. At full gallop his charge broke formations. In time it broke barriers and wards the same way. His voice carried across a battlefield, rallying the herd and putting panic into the ranks that faced it. He was called the Iron-Mane, the Horn of the Highlands and Hoofbreaker. The Stonehoof made him chieftain.
As chieftain he met a commander who drew up a line of spears for him on open ground below a rise. Garruk took the herd at it at full gallop. At the last the line opened. Behind it the ground fell away into cut banks and loose stone, and the charge went in at the speed it had built on the open. Legs broke there. He stood in the broken ground with arrows breaking on his hide and called the herd back out. He brought out everyone who could still move. After that he sent scouts over the ground before he charged it. He still could not read a feint.
Then the Hoof-Seer Wellspring chose him to guard its power. He was chieftain already, and he kept the chieftaincy when he took up the guarding. The two became one office. From then on a grazing path of the Stonehoof was a grazing path and was also a sacred boundary, and both held.

#### The Cost

His Wellspring was bound to the warplains. Inside it the herd ran longer, stood up under blows that should have dropped it, and wanted the fight. Garruk gave the herd that want, and then he had to command the herd that had it. The want did not end when the fight did. After every fight he stood at the herd's head and held it with his voice until the want went out of it.
One grazing season the herd came up its path through a sacred grove at the edge of Thalyren Veyroot's sight. It cropped the understory to the roots and trod the leaf-mould into mud. Lyraen Callivane walked barefoot into the Stonehoof camp with Thalyren's word. Garruk heard her out. The path was the herd's, and a sacred boundary under the Hoof-Seer Wellspring, and he would not move it. He gave her the herd's answer in his own words: "Steel rusts. Stone crumbles. But the hoof leaves its mark forever." He agreed to one thing. The herd would cross the grove at a walk and graze on the far side. The understory was back before the herd returned.
Another season an Outer Realm beast came down onto the warplains, and Garruk took the herd after it with Kaelen Brighthoof scouting the ridges. Kaelen's hawk brought the herd to a furrow across the open plain. At its end lay Cernan Eryndor, asleep past waking after a charge that had found nothing in its line. The beast's track bent back toward him. Garruk set the Stonehoof in a ring about the sleeping man, on open ground where a charge has its run. He held them there through the day without the battle-lust. He did not go after the beast. At dusk it came back for the man. Kaelen put an arrow in it from the rock, and the herd ran it down before it reached the ring. Cernan woke inside the ring. The herd had spent a day standing still, and a warden of the Outer World was in its debt.

#### Where They Stand

Garruk Stonehoof is chieftain and spiritual protector of the Stonehoof Herdclan and Wellspring Guardian of the Iron Plains, and he holds all of it as one office. On open ground his charge breaks whatever stands in its line. He needs the run. Broken terrain beats him, and so does a feint.
Kaelen scouts for the herd in its dangerous hunts, and the two hold each other in respect. Garruk has tested his strength against Varek Ka'ruun in contests, some friendly and some not. Cernan Eryndor owes the herd the day it stood over him.
Garruk respects Thalyren. The Stonehoof path still runs through the Green-Warden's grove, and he has not moved it. When the herd's paths meet the sacred groves again, Lyraen goes between, and he hears her out.

- **Lyraen Callivane · Verdure Hoof** · go-between. She walked barefoot into the Stonehoof camp with Thalyren's word, and he gave her the herd's answer and the walk through the grove.

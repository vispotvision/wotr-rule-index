# Geturo Mahuo

**Lineage:** Mahuo (Korean stratum). Son of Kwon Mu-jin and Frithia.
**Age:** 14. Twin (older by minutes than Hiromi).
**Class:** X, Aetherion Academy.

## Physical Description

Taller than Hiromi by three fingers. Broader through the chest and shoulders, built with the dense, settled weight their father carries and Hiromi missed entirely. Hair is their mother's: warm chestnut brown, thick and straight, cut short at the sides, longer on top where it falls across his forehead in a way that looks careless and is not. Face rounder than Hiromi's, jaw heavier, cheekbones less pronounced. Frithia's mouth, wide, set in a line that passes for patience or disapproval depending on which sibling he is looking at.

Hands larger than a boy of fourteen ought to have. Knuckled and square. They hang at his sides with the stillness of someone who learned early that his hands attracted attention and decided not to give them anything to do.

Academy-issue black coat, buttoned to the collar, cinched at the waist, sleeves rolled to the forearm because his wrists run hot.

## Psychology

Quiet. Observant. Patient in a way that costs him and he knows it costs him. Watches the exits. Checks for people where Xanelor checks for ground.

Sees through Hiromi's performances completely: knows about the binding circles, the unmade bed, the hours before dawn. Does not stop him. Wants him to know he is not fooled, and does not intervene beyond that.

Studies alchemy with Mu-jin (stayed up most of the night refining). Willing to use this as leverage against his father.

**Refuses:** unnecessary attention. His hands stay empty on purpose.
**Notices first:** people and their positions.
**What others say when absent:** Lily exchanges looks with him. Those looks contain more than either names.

## Abilities

Studies alchemy under Mu-jin. Nature of his talent unestablished. No title.

Stage, Level, Wellspring, Techniques, Stats: all TBD.

## Relationships

**Hiromi** (twin): the counterweight. His sigh is perfected over thirteen years. He permits Hiromi to take the centre, walks the outside, watches the doors.
**Lily** (older sister): they exchange looks that contain entire conversations. He closed his eyes when Hiromi's enthusiasm expanded to include Taesyn. His workload had doubled.
**Mu-jin** (father): collaborates on alchemy late at night. Uses this as leverage when convenient.
**Frithia** (mother): his shoulders settle when Hiromi avoids getting them both in trouble.

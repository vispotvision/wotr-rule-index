# Gimbzo

*The Anchor of Fate · Iron-Backed Gim · The Standing Verdict · Card 11*
> ***He Who Refused to Move Even When the World Did***
> 
> > **"You may swing again. I will still be here. That is what an anchor is for."**

---

## I · Identity and Core Profile

**Race / Lineage** Moto, Archaic Bloodline — **First-Eon Generation, contemporary of Bara himself.** *One of the earliest sons born under the Hour Before Sequence.*
**Faction** The Original Moto Conclave — **pre-chronological warrior caste**
**Temperance Stage** **XII — Zenith, anchored permanently.** *His cultivation curve was severed mid-ascent and frozen by the Plane of Fate itself, refusing decay*
> The heaviest of Bara's earliest cadre — **a slab-shouldered, bull-throated Moto whose function within the Conclave was never reconnaissance, never command, never revelation.**
> 
> ***He was Anchor.*** When the first reality bent and threatened to spill its sequence, **Gimbzo set his heel and refused the bend.**
> He held the line **through wars no later chronicler dared to transcribe** — until the day **Dougou, the Iron Bison Maximus**, met him on the rim of the unfinished world and **broke his ribcage into the dirt with a single overhead.**
>
> The Conclave mourned him. The mortal histories filed him away. ***Yet his soul did not transit.***
>
> **The Plane of Fate, recognising one of its own oldest tenants, absorbed him whole — not as ghost, not as memory, but as** ***living infrastructure*****.**
>
> *He is now a load-bearing axle of Hataraki's lower lattice, a labour of the Plane that wears Gimbzo's name and Gimbzo's posture — and the Plane itself is preparing his return.*

## II · System Metrics

**Aether Class · Fixator-Imposer** — *a Body-rooted Hataraki dialect that does not project outward but locks inward. Aether radiates as a downward weight rather than a striking arc.*
> **His Aether falls.** It does not flare, it does not bloom, it does not snake. **It settles.**
> 
> The ground beneath him **compresses by visible centimetres when he plants.** Air around his shoulders hangs heavier, and **dust caught in his aura drifts down twice as slow as it should** — *as though sequence itself is being told to wait.*
> **Risk · Sequence Lock.** Prolonged anchoring of his own essence into the Plane's substrate causes ***temporal adhesion*** — where he can become **inseparable from a coordinate of fate** and must be ritually loosened by a Tengan adept **or risk fossilising into the lattice permanently.**
**Triune Core** · **Fixatio · Monlithion · Coagulatio**
**Network** · Petralon, Basilithe, Manganthra, Judicium — *acquired posthumously through Hataraki absorption.*
**Targeted uninfected** · **Thaloré and Chthonica** — *both being slowly drawn toward him by Anchor resonance.*
> **Temperance Path.** Bara taught him directly. He climbed the early Stages **through pure functional labour rather than insight** — carrying, holding, refusing, enduring.
> 
> ***The only Moto of the First Eon to advance without ever once practising revelation.***

>
> Any forced displacement — gravity wells, telekinesis, knockback, dimensional pull, soul-yank — **is first checked against Fixatio's recognition law.** *If the effect cannot prove lawful authority over his coordinate, it fails outright.*
>
> ***He cannot be moved by a force that has not earned the right to move him.***
**Living Ballast** · Passive — because he is partially Plane infrastructure, **ambient Hataraki in any battlefield he occupies redirects a fraction of itself through him.** *He grows stronger the longer reality unfolds around him.*

## V · Relics and Domain

> **Anryū-en — The Garden of Settled Weight.** *Latent; has not yet manifested on the prime stratum, because Gimbzo is still seated inside the Plane of Fate.*
>
> When he returns it will unfurl as **a circular plot of bone-pale earth surrounded by stunted iron-bark trees**, in which the law of the field is simple:
>
> > ***What stands, stands. What falls, stays fallen.***
>
> **Resurrection, regeneration and rewind effects fail at its threshold. Wounds delivered inside it cannot be undone.**
**Tsuiji & Tsuiei — The Twin Anchors** · Tier S. Forged in the First Eon from **compressed Monlithion ore wrapped in Coagulatio binding-rite.** *Each weighs as much as a small mountain to anyone who is not Gimbzo.* **Their hafts are bandaged in linen taken from the burial wrappings he was never granted.**
**The Frost-Crest** · The bone-white upright crest of hair — **a residual Hataraki fixture that grew in during his Plane-absorption.** *It hums faintly under his Anchor work and cannot be cut.*

## VI · Lore and Trajectory

**Appearance** · 7'4". Barrel-torso, slab-pectoraled, **forearms thick as cooper's mallets, neck wider than his own jaw.** Deep umber-bronze skin, sun-leathered, **with metallic birth-sheen riding the brow-ridge and along the spine like hammered copper under the hide.** Small sunken eyes, banded grey-iron — **Reigan-dormant with faint Tengan glimmer.** Bone-white crest atop a shaven skull; **a frosted beard climbing into stiff lifted points like an old lion's mane.** Fights bare-chested above a heavy wrapped hakama, **twin great-axes whose blades are broad as church doors.**
**The Standing at the Rim** — **held the unfinished world's eastern threshold against a tide of pre-sequence horrors for forty-one days without sleep.** *Bara himself is said to have walked the rim afterwards and pressed a palm to Gimbzo's shoulder — the only recorded instance of the Founder physically acknowledging a subordinate's labour.*
**The Breaking by Dougou** — their duel lasted three days. **On the fourth dawn, Dougou caught Gimbzo's left axe on the curve of his shoulder-yoke and brought a maul down through his sternum.** *The Iron Bison left the body where it fell, believing the labour finished.* **He was, in the narrow mortal sense, correct. In the deeper Hataraki sense, the labour had merely changed substrate.**
> **The Quiet Absorption** — three nights after his death, **the corpse was found gone from the salt flat.** No grave-robber's tracks. No carrion sign. **The Plane of Fate had reclaimed him whole.**
>
> *Bara was said to have closed the matter with one sentence:* ***"He is still working."***
**Counters** · **Sequence-Storm Disruption** — a coordinated Tengan-strike that forcibly reshuffles next-instant priority **can briefly unmoor his Fixatio anchor.** **Obsession-Vector Inversion** — *if an opponent can convince his Anchor-labour that the coordinate it grips is unworthy of being held, the Work collapses inward through Obsession Force.* **Bara is the only being known to have ever done it — and only as a teaching correction.**
> **Trajectory.** He will return — **not reincarnated, not resurrected, but *re-deployed*** — when the Plane judges that a coordinate of present-age fate requires anchoring beyond what the living Moto lines can provide.
> 
> He will step out of a settled patch of ground somewhere on the continent, **both axes already in hand**, and resume the labour he was never permitted to finish.
> 
> ***The first thing he will look for is the bloodline that carries Dougou's residue.***
> *He plants his right axe, drags the blade slow through the dust of the lattice-floor, and waits for the world above to need him.*

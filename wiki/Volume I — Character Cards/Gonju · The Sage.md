### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Research and Archives Division. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Gonju was born on a volcanic flank of the New World's arc, beside the sulphur fields, where the ground was warm and yellow and nothing grew. His father collected sulphur off the vent rims in wet cloth, for the acid benches downslope, and did not work long at a time. Above the vents the cloud forest dripped. The boy carried seed down from it and pressed it into the yellow crust. Nothing came up. The next season he carried more.
At the timber cuts he laid his palms on the stumps. He could say which tree had stood through a fire, and which beside one the lahar took. In a hollow where the gas pooled he found birds lying in the moss, and he knelt at the rim and read the moss until he knew the hour the air had turned.

#### The Making

The acid benches bought his father's sulphur, and in time they took the son. In the Turning called the Rot, while a sealed vessel's contents went black and stayed black, he never once opened one to see whether it had spoiled.
The Research and Archives Division took him as a field hand for its Rot-fields. His rite lifted corruption out of soil in one step, with no state between at which he could stop it, look and correct it. He read a field until he knew where the corruption had come in and how far it ran. He began after that and never before.
On a Rot seam in the cloud forest he found a brown sow with the Rot in her and a cub at her side. She felt no threat and let him kneel beside her. He read her. The corruption had gone deeper into her than into the ground. He stayed within arm's reach until she was still. The cub would not leave her, and Gonju would not leave the cub.
It came to him under the Honey Truce, and the bond the Accord filed as a beast pact formed between them. He called the cub Mallow. After that seam his self held steady, fastened to the bear's. Mallow was his Axis.

#### The Cost

He could not fight at all. Arbitration made him its Cleansing Consultant for Territory Cases all the same, and sent him into Rot-fields, among corrupted wildlife and onto contested ground with a bear, a willow switch and a satchel. Mallow was the only defence he had. The bear blocked miasmic gusts with his body and held up the wounded while the rite ran, and Gonju let him stand in front. The parties to a case waited while he read, and some asked him to begin. He began when the reading was done.
The Farath vein in Foraye fed chartered settlements their water, and when it went foul they disputed it. Mallow found the seam, and Gonju read the roots along it and named where the corruption had come in. Then the vein began to fail, and Azran Nemeir held it alone. Gonju could not hold a channel. He kept the corrupted wildlife quiet around the young man and did not leave. When Dr. Vaelion Rhest had written the corrected boundary and Azran had stitched it closed there, Azran wrote his report and fell silent. Gonju had never met him. He brought him tea and left him alone with it.
On the Caedor plains an ash fall had buried a charter village in one night, and the survivors raised a barrow over the dead they could dig out. When the charters after them disputed the ground, Arbitration sent Gonju to read it. The families asked for a burning, and the Accord sent Silas Briarend Wytherm III, who asked Gonju for a reading first. Gonju read the scrub on the mound and the roots beneath it. He named two records: the ash's, which was the night of the burial, and the dead's, under it. Silas burned the second, and nobody would read it again, the families included. Mallow shared his flatbread with the gravebinder. Gonju coaxed the first green up through the mound.

#### Where They Stand

Gonju serves the Research and Archives Division, on loan to Arbitration's territory cases. His loose white Verdure tunic is lined in red-lace script, the Verdure hymns stitched into it. His bark-stitched satchel carries seeds, dried petals and vials, its barkcloth layers keeping each reagent from bleeding into the next. He carries a willow switch and walks without sound.
Mallow sniffs out the Rot seams and beats the percussion for the Green Lung Draft. He loves daisies and flatbread. He shares with the penitent and nobody else. On Rot seams Ascella Wick's Stillflame pairs with Gonju's cleansing, and the seams come clean without panic.
He stands at the edge of a crisis that breaks a practitioner along what he feels, and he has not crossed it. Whatever he carries badly when it comes will stay. He carries the sow on the cloud-forest seam and the last reading of the Caedor dead. He says, "Life remembers. Even the wounded soil wants to sing again."

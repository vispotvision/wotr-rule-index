### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Tenryū and the dragon-blooded. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Goraku Tenryū was born into the Tenryū, a line whose blood turns what it takes in into force. The Oni's Pact came to him with the name. It was a family-bound agreement with a Wellspring of fire, made before his birth, and whenever he drank, it superheated his body. His kinsman Daigo carried the same inheritance and fed it on sweets and rhythm. Goraku fed it on brew.
He grew up on the Braqth coast, among the oni clans of the Braqth Raiding Courts. The coast had almost no water and almost no wood. Its halls stood on columnar basalt, pried from the terraces in six-sided posts that needed almost no dressing. After a storm the clans walked the pans for the glass tubes the lightning had fused into the crust, and sold them by the armload to Accord factors. They raided in their season and came home to feast, and the Blood-Gourd rites were kept at those feasts. As a boy he drank what the halls poured him. Steam came off his skin.

#### The Making

He learned to brew in the same halls, for the feasts first and then for the raids. The Battle Draughts were his work: oni-brewed concoctions a raider drank in the middle of a fight, which put volatile Essence into him while the fight still ran. The courts kept the Drunken Ember's Rite, an oni tradition that binds the soul to flame and fermentation together. Goraku took it deeper every season. The more he drank, the hotter and more unstable his Essence burned. Each step he took in a fight spilled a phantom wave of boiling blood-fire around him, and when he had drunk deep he breathed jets of molten steam. His raiders learned to fight a stride clear of him.
The courts made him their war-leader. The halls called him the Crimson Gourd, the Oni of the Ninth Toast, the Laughing Bloodstorm.
Then his soul showed itself to him from outside, and its whole architecture lay open to examination. For a season the halls found him hard to be around. He had not learned to stop looking. He examined his and started brewing from it.

#### The Cost

What he brewed from it went into the Gourd of Endless Night, bound to his soul, as three brews he turned in rotation: one for raw power, one for clarity of Essence, one for unhinged chaos. Its fumes alone could make mortals lightheaded. He drank what would have killed a warband and kept fighting.
Nothing in him set a limit on the rite. It ran on momentum, and every draught in a long fight left him hotter and less steady. To keep it burning he had to keep drinking. The gourd could run dry. Enemies who knew him waited and stayed out of his reach. He poured the draughts for his raiders as freely as he drank his own. His rages gave no warning, and the Guild Accord's Enforcement Division, which took his help, wrote him down as a "problematic ally" for his unpredictable drunken rages.
Kurokiba Ranzou, the wandering Brew-Sage, once came in off the road to a feast of the courts. The hall set its war-leader against him in a drinking contest, each man at his own gourd, with drums and chanting to keep the pace. Goraku ran hotter with every draught. Blood-fire spilled around his seat whenever he shifted on it. He did not slow. His kinsman Daigo was at the feast, and he tapped his horns together until the hall took his beat. Then Daigo came between them and tied a red cord on each man's wrist with his teeth, on a quick promise: the contest would be finished on a night when Daigo stood in the hall. Goraku let him tie it. He set the gourd down unbeaten in front of his raiders. The cord held.

#### Where They Stand

Goraku Tenryū leads the Braqth Raiding Courts and brews their Battle Draughts. The courts still raid in season, and they still hold none of the ground they cross on the way. The Enforcement Division takes his help. A rumour holds that the Black Concord takes his oni-brew toxins for covert killings, and nobody has settled it.
Now and then he drinks with Vaelthyr Iscarn, heir of the Pale Court of Epprenea and its envoy to the Guild Accord. The respect runs both ways. Each uses the other for influence over the affairs of the Outer World. Frost gathers on the envoy's side of the table. Steam rises off Goraku's.
The red cord is still on his wrist, and when Daigo tugs the other end it warms in his palm. He and Kurokiba have not sat the second night.
At the feasts he pours for whoever sits down: "If the fire in your belly is small, I'll pour you a drink. If it's big… I'll pour you two."
Fully ignited, he stands at the edge of losing his physical form. No record carries him past it.

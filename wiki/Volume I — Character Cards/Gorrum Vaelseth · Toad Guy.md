> *"Frogs see the ripples long before the storm hits. Sit with me. We'll wait for the flies to come to us."*
> **An invented Stage name — the third error class.** The card reads **"Stage IV (Blooming)."**
>
> ***Blooming is not among the sixteen.*** *Stage IV is Flourishing.* **Numeral stands, word struck** — the same handling as Conquest and Judgment, and different from the off-by-one slips where the name was real.

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Thalyren's groves and the highland plains. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Gorrum Vaelseth was born in a mire of reed and standing water, to a mother who gathered swamp herbs and sold them at the dry edge where the roads began. He learned which ground would bear a foot. He crossed it slowly. He drew what he learned. Every channel and pool went down on bark or hide, until he had the whole mire.
The croak was in his voice from the start. Buyers at the dry edge dealt more easily with the boy than with his mother, and she sent him to do the selling.
He was still a boy when a fight crossed the mire. Armed men came through the reeds one morning. He lay down in the shallows among the stems with the water at his chin, and they fought past him and over him. He stayed as he was for hours, until the last of them had gone and the flies were thick on what they had left. Then he got up and walked the ground. Among the dead lay broken things with workings still live in them. He carried them to the dry edge and sold them. It was his first trade in relics.

#### The Making

He left the mire with a pack of herbs and his drawings. He went onto battlefields nobody came back to and to Wellsprings that had collapsed, and sold on what they left. He mapped the ground as he crossed it, the living Wellsprings and the dead ones.
His hands could put nothing out on the spot. He learned to bind what he needed into charms and talismans beforehand, from swamp herbs gathered on the road. A charm could make a man slip on dry ground, or bring on fatigue or a small hallucination, or give luck to whoever walked beside him. He made each one the night before. What he had not made in advance, he did not have.
The Guardians of Tres Aures held him by old pacts, loosely. He worked for himself.
The Croaking Mire grew slowly around him. It was a wetland Wellspring, and where it stood, time stretched for intruders and the senses of anyone beside him sharpened. He could not raise it where he needed it. It served him only where it already was, and he marked those places on his maps.
In the Mire, what he was settled. The signature his workings leave came in whole and stopped changing. He did not reach it alone. It took a bond with real weight in it.

#### The Cost

He lives by never being where a fight is. If a fight finds him, it ends as soon as it starts. So he keeps to marsh, grass and shallow water, moves slowly on purpose, and can lie motionless for hours until the ground hides him. Whoever is with him when trouble comes has only the charms he made the night before. His stock comes from ground where others died, and from Wellsprings that failed the people who drew on them. He arrives after.
At the Verdant Pass he came up last of anyone. Beastkin herding clans had brought their herds in for the season, and a Concord Guild party had come up to draw on its Wellspring. Lyraen Callivane, the pass's Wellspring Mediator, had already called both sides to the meadow. She called him Old Croak in front of all of them. He had mapped the pass on an earlier road, and both sides knew his map. They agreed to write the terms against it. The clans kept the pass in their season, and the party drew on the Wellspring outside it. Lyraen put her name to the terms as mediator. Moryn Sporetail, passing through, traded refined spore-powders for his swamp herbs and took a tracing of his map as part of a price. His map is now the record both sides point to when they disagree.

#### Where They Stand

Gorrum Vaelseth is nomadic. He is called Toad Guy, the Croak in the Rain, the Verdant Trickster. He trades in rare herbs, spirit-potions and the debris of forgotten battlefields and collapsed Wellsprings, and he maps Wellsprings before anything else. The Guardians of Tres Aures still hold him by old pacts. Saruin Kye owes those Guardians more than one favour, and the Molten Lineage of Saórén Vyrrakal is rooted in the volcanic dominions beneath Tres Aures.
He trades relics and charms with Hyo Miran often, finds him too jumpy, and respects his eye for rare goods. He brings Moryn swamp herbs for refined spore-powders. Thalyren Veyroot counts him an old acquaintance. Lyraen travels with him when their roads meet and still teases him for his slow pace.
Varek Ka'ruun claims Gorrum once tried to sell him swamp air in a bottle. Gorrum denies it. He smiles the whole time.
He tells whoever travels with him: "Frogs see the ripples long before the storm hits. Sit with me. We'll wait for the flies to come to us."

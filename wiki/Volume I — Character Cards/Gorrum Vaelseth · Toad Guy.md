# Gorrum Vaelseth · Toad Guy

> *"Frogs see the ripples long before the storm hits. Sit with me. We'll wait for the flies to come to us."*
> **An invented Stage name — the third error class.** The card reads **"Stage IV (Blooming)."**
>
> ***Blooming is not among the sixteen.*** *Stage IV is Flourishing.* **Numeral stands, word struck** — the same handling as Conquest and Judgment, and different from the off-by-one slips where the name was real.
>
> *"Verdure / Mire / Charm"* were not Essence types. **The Croaking Mire** is retained as the Cycle name.

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Gorrum Vaelseth. **Called** · **Toad Guy.** The Croak in the Rain · **Verdant Trickster.**
**Role** · **Wandering Relic-Broker. Wellspring Cartographer. Herbalist and Minor Hex-Weaver.**

### Affiliation

**Nomadic.** *Loosely connected to the Guardians of Tres Aures through old pacts, but works independently* — **trading in hard-to-find herbs, spirit-potions, and curious magical debris scavenged from forgotten battlefields and collapsed Wellsprings.**
> **Catalyst Event · Stage IV, Flourishing.** *Identity settles; the resonance signature finishes arriving.* **And it requires a Spirit Axis — a bond carrying real mechanical weight.**
>
> *His card names Lyraen Callivane as a friend and travelling companion who calls him "Old Croak."* **She is Stage IV as well, and her Axis is also unidentified.** ***The obvious reading is that they are each other's, and neither card says so.***

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

## VI · Traits

**Mirewalker's Patience** · **Moves slowly and quietly, masking presence in marshland, grass and shallow water.** *Can remain motionless for hours, blending into natural terrain until the perfect moment to act.*
**Tongue of the Barter-Frog** · **A natural gift for negotiation — literally.** *Speaking in certain tones induces mild compliance or trust, a subtle psychic ripple carried in his croaking undertone.*
**Bog-Hexing** · **Creates tiny, temporary hexes from gathered swamp herbs, bound into charms or talismans.** *Effects range from mild curses — slipping, fatigue, minor hallucinations — to good luck for allies.*

## VII · Signature

### The Croaking Mire

**Effect** · A slow-growing, adaptive wetland Wellspring. **Time stretches and movement goes sluggish for intruders; allies find their senses sharpened.**
**Cost** · **It is slow-growing.** *He cannot establish it where he needs it — only where it already is.*
**Limit** · **Wetland.**
**Counter** · **Find him.** *Ardency 72 means the encounter ends the moment it starts.*

# Gorvan Aldric Voss · The Maw That Held

## Gorvan Aldric Voss · The Maw That Held

> *"The ground holds. I just stand on it longer than you."*

---

### I · Identity

**Name** · Gorvan Aldric Voss. **Called** · The Maw That Held. **Also Known As** · Stonefather, Old Fang. **Epithet** · He Who Has Never Needed to Move Twice.
**Race** · Dwarf — High Dawi (Varūn Descent), Rune-Forger lineage, Innerworld-forged, a survivor of the Sovereignty Wars.
**Gloss rights** · Unlimited. His technique names are already plain English; nothing here waits on translation.

#### Affiliation

Formerly Iron Accord Warhost, Martial Division — lapsed. **Currently independent.** Late Withering Age through the Concord Era. The skull-faced chest-mask is Iron Accord war-cult insignia, the only affiliation he has never renounced — he wears it because he won it, and Dawi do not surrender what they earned through blood.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VIII, Transcendence.** *Resolving a core internal contradiction documented in the Crystal's fracture record — the first true Domain forms.* **His formed alone, in deep stone, over a decade, with no instruction and nobody watching.**
> 
> **Petralon has begun treating him like terrain rather than a person.** At Stage VIII the distinction narrows, and he is aware of it, and watches for the moment it closes without knowing what he will do when it does.

### II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class III · Resonant.** His Aether does not project — it settles. Draw on a Wellspring near him and the environment answers before he does: stone compresses, metal groan-hums, the air tastes of iron and old forge.
**Attraction Layer** · Terrain, not people. His unnamed Proto-Domain is the only bond that has ever held.
**Crystal State** · Refined, with one sealed and unhealed fracture in the lower-right face — he considers it structural, not a wound. **η** · 0.88, **Coherence Band B.**
**Risk — Inertial Lockout** · When Essence density peaks he becomes nearly immovable; his ability to pivot, angle or react to flanking drops precipitously. Fast multi-vector opponents are his only structural threat.

### III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Petralon** · Vectoria. Stone settling into stable configuration around him — first stirred at Stage I on his first siege posting.
**Monolithion** · Materia. The Enduring Monument. Terrain becomes an extension of his will rather than a thing he stands on.
**Coagulatio** · Materia. Seals minor wounds mid-combat; same-Family compounding with Monolithion is why what he holds tends to stay held.
**Additional Wellsprings Networked** · Two — **Pyraeon** (Caloria, forge-blood inheritance, active through his axe since a cold Innerworld siege) and **Mortalis** (Vitalia, accumulated from battlefield proximity, uninvited but permanent, fully woke at Stage VII).
**Targeted Uninfected** · **Materia Primordia.** Felt twice — once during pauldron fitting, once when his axe took its current shape. It seeks him through objects he touches long enough.

### VI · Traits

**Geological Patience** · **Body Path endurance functions as material tolerance, not fighter stamina.** He absorbs and redistributes force at Stage VIII rates for engagements that would reduce most Body Path fighters to component pieces. The cost is not fatigue — it is Domain saturation deepening and lateral speed dropping as he becomes more of what the ground already is.
**Trophy Resonance, The Jaws That Watch** · **His beast-jaw pauldrons carry residual Essence echo of the Constructs they were harvested from.** Aether-sensitive opponents register a faint predatory awareness — not intelligent, not targeted, just present. Spirit-affinity enemies under sustained exposure lose output involuntarily. He calls it good hardware.

### VIII · Signature

#### The Maw Opens

**Effect** · A single full-rotation swing of the axe, aimed through the space an opponent occupies rather than at them. The Pyraeon thread loads over the windup and detonates on extension as a thermal-kinetic shockwave along the blade's arc — the heat does not burn on contact, it induces micro-fracture in anything ferrous inside the arc, so armor and weapons betray their wearer while the kinetic edge does the actual killing.
**Cost** · The Pyraeon thread needs roughly four minutes of passive Essence circulation to reload at full potency. A weaker version can be thrown faster; he does not consider it the same technique and will not name it.
**Limit** · A minimum two-beat wind-up, never rushed — rushing would make it a different move, and a worse one.
**Counter** · **Close before the wind-up completes, or force him to commit the swing early.** The technique's entire threat lives in the two beats before it fires.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether his hundred-percent success rate at outlasting a problem is strength or only patience. He has never examined it, and has decided not to start.

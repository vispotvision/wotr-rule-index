### VIII · Signature

#### The Maw Opens

**Effect** · A single full-rotation swing of the axe, aimed through the space an opponent occupies rather than at them. The Pyraeon thread loads over the windup and detonates on extension as a thermal-kinetic shockwave along the blade's arc — the heat does not burn on contact, it induces micro-fracture in anything ferrous inside the arc, so armor and weapons betray their wearer while the kinetic edge does the actual killing.
**Cost** · The Pyraeon thread needs roughly four minutes of passive Essence circulation to reload at full potency. A weaker version can be thrown faster; he does not consider it the same technique and will not name it.
**Limit** · A minimum two-beat wind-up, never rushed — rushing would make it a different move, and a worse one.
**Counter** · **Close before the wind-up completes, or force him to commit the swing early.** The technique's entire threat lives in the two beats before it fires.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether his hundred-percent success rate at outlasting a problem is strength or only patience. He has never examined it, and has decided not to start.

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Anvil and relic. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Gorvan Aldric Voss was born to the High Dawi of Varūn descent, into a Rune-Forger lineage, and he was forged in the Inner World. His people cut runes so exact that the stone stopped needing to be told what to do. He grew up in the holds of the Ring. Its tunnels run through fault-stressed rock that should have come down long ago and has not.
He went into the Warhost of the Iron Accord and served in its Martial Division. He came through the Sovereignty Wars.

#### The Making

On his first siege posting the stone under his boots moved for him. It settled into a stable shape around him and stayed that way until the siege was over. He learned to stand on it.
In a cold siege in the Inner World the forge-heat of his lineage woke in his axe. It has run through the blade ever since, slow to come up and slow to fade. A second thing came to him from long standing among the dying. He never asked for it. It shows him what is about to fail.
In the Temple Rift Crisis the Void came into the collapsed temple zones beneath the Inner World, and the Deepkin who guarded them fought there as the Dawi's allies. Gorvan killed two Constructs in the Rift. When the High Dawi went out of the collapsed zones, he walked out with them, carrying the two heads. The High Dawi record enters it as a strategic withdrawal. The Deepkin record, kept in Mirror-Sign and never translated for outsiders, tells of being left alone with the Void. Nobody has ruled on the two names. The trust between Gorvan and the Deepkin broke there and has stayed broken. After the Rift he left the Iron Accord.
He carried the heads a long time. Orokh Sable-Forge does not work fresh kill, so Gorvan waited until they had had time to forget pain. Then he brought them to the Wyrmwright's cave mouth above the Rimlands of Stannvaard. Orokh read them by their heat. He fitted them to Gorvan's shoulders. For armour the price is the name of something the buyer fears and will not admit. Gorvan named it. During the fitting he felt, for the first time, a current that seeks him through the objects he touches long enough. He felt it once more, when his axe took the shape it has now.
Afterwards he went down into deep stone. There he resolved the contradiction documented in his Crystal's fracture record, and the first true Domain formed. His formed alone, in deep stone, over a decade, with no instruction and nobody watching.

#### The Cost

He built his career on never needing the pivot. The one thing that has ever hurt him is a fast opponent working the angle he cannot take. When the ground runs thickest in him he can barely turn. He does not tire. The longer he holds a place, the more of the ground he becomes, and the slower he goes sideways.
His great swing is called The Maw Opens. Its wind-up takes two beats, and he has never shortened them. He can throw a lesser version faster. He will not count it as the same swing, and he has never named it.
The ward beneath his shield closes small wounds while he fights. It has been rewritten forty-seven times, and the first inscription cannot be read under the newer work. One fracture in his Crystal is sealed and has never healed. He counts it as structure.
He left the Warhost and kept its crest. The skull-faced death-mask of the Iron Accord's war-cult sits at his sternum, the one affiliation he has never renounced. He won it in blood, and Dawi do not surrender what they earned that way. He wears it where an enemy sees it first.
The jaws on his shoulders still carry an echo of the Constructs they were cut from. Anyone sensitive to Aether feels a faint predatory awareness off them. He calls it good hardware.
The one bond that has ever held for him formed around him in deep stone. It has no name.

#### Where They Stand

He is called The Maw That Held, and Stonefather, and Old Fang, and He Who Has Never Needed to Move Twice. He answers to no Warhost now.
Petralon has begun to treat him as terrain. The line between him and the ground narrows, and he knows it. He watches for the moment it closes. He does not know what he will do when it does.
The current that seeks him through what he holds has touched him twice, and it goes on seeking. The fear he named at Orokh's anvil lies in the pauldrons. Whether his long record of outlasting every problem is strength or only patience, he has never examined. He has decided not to start.
His words: "The ground holds. I just stand on it longer than you."

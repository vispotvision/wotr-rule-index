### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Rot Host. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Graelith was born in Therosan, the river-and-fortress kingdom of the Five Rivers that lives by engineering, masonry and ward-work, on the eastern margin of the Varakh Drylands. His father dressed stone for the river forts. At the banker the boy learned the first test of a block. A mason tapped it with the hammer and listened. Sound stone rang. Stone with a flaw running through it gave back a dead note, and it went on the waste heap however true its faces were.
He did not stay at the banker. He took service as a knight in the garrison of a fort his father had dressed stone for, and he stood his watches behind walls he had heard tested course by course, under wards of the kind Therosan is known for.

#### The Making

In a siege the fort's gate tower was undermined, and it came down with the men on its floors. Graelith went down with it. A splintered timber went into his chest and stayed there. The men buried with him called out through the rubble, and then they breathed, and he heard each of them for as long as each one lasted. The last of them stopped before the diggers reached him. The quiet reached him first.
The surgeons drew the timber. The hole it left would not close. When they stitched it, it opened again, and he would not let them stitch it another time.
In the years after, he learned to hear a sound whole and meet it with its opposite, so that the two cancelled and nothing was left of either. Incantations failed around him. The fort's wards went slack where he stood his watch, and the garrison moved him off the wall. He left Therosan. He was a knight without a wall to keep when he went into Malphas' service.
In the Cauldron's service he finished what the timber had begun. The wound was made structural. He hollowed himself out and put the weapon inside the cavity. His chest became a stillwater basin with a sword submerged in it, and the sword lay under the dark water, absorbing nullifying essence until drawn.

#### The Cost

The field he makes cancels both ways. He stands inside it with everyone else, and nothing he brings works there either. In it he has the sword, his arm and a knight's discipline. He cannot throw the field, project it or leave it somewhere, so he has to walk it in.
When he walks to war, every step pulls sound from the air. He hears nothing then. His own tread is gone, and so are the voices of his own side and the calls that would reach any other knight in the line. Inside the field a tapped block gives back no note at all, sound stone or flawed.
The ground pays after him. Once the fighting ends, the rot he seeded comes up in the muted ground he walked.
He has never let the wound close. The basin is that hole, made to hold weight on purpose. He keeps the water in it still. What the rubble left him he puts in his own words: "Noise is merely the writhing of the unbroken. I bring the quiet where all things come apart." He goes where Malphas sends him and brings that quiet with him. He stands in it himself.

#### Where They Stand

Graelith is the Fourth General of the Rot Host and its Nullification Vanguard, the Silent Knight of the Cauldron March. He is Malphas' Spell-Breaking Arm. Malphas respects his precision and discipline and sends him only when key Wellsprings or Domains must be cracked open. Zeraphine Drowl holds authority over him and over the other three generals.
On the Broken Rivers, the first time she held four generals under one cadence, she kept him back through the afternoon. At dusk the town's practitioners came out of the river gate in a sortie aimed at her glass. She sent Graelith to meet it. He walked into their path. The sound went out of the air. Their incantations arrived at nothing, and the sortie stopped in the river mud and turned back through the gate. On his stretch of wall the gloam thinned, and Kytheris's spores found no frequency to match. She drew back from him. Morphaene laid the gloam on thicker beyond his ground. Days after the town fell, the rot came up in the mud where he had walked. Zeraphine has held him back for the counterstroke since, and she calls him a living pause.
He cancels resonance, and the Ossuary Choir are resonance artillery. Nobody in the Bloom has resolved it. He was not on the hill in the Bone Hills when their chord broke the fort's wards.
A rune cut in advance needs no voice, and it still works in his quiet. An Engraver who cuts before he arrives is the answer to him.

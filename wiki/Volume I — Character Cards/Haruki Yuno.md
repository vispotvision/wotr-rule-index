## X · Techniques

### The Luminalis thread, taken to the blade

- **Effect:** she aligns a thread of Luminalis along her weapon and brings it to resonance. "Her blade lifted up as the Luminalis thread aligned itself, the edges of it shifting as the tip began to resonate and glow. The runes conjoining in a chain reaction."
- **Cost:** `pending Isaac`.
- **Limit:** it is a chain reaction through conjoined runes, so it builds in sequence rather than arriving at once — the scene shows alignment, then shifting edges, then resonance, then glow.
- **Counter (R12-3 mandatory):** interrupt the chain before the runes conjoin, or deny the thread. The scene demonstrates the more absolute counter against her generally: Kujo arrives with no traversal for Luminalis to register, so nothing is aligned in time to matter.
- **What nobody knows:** what the marks burning across the infinite distances are anchored to.
- **The Test (R17-2):** it does *thread alignment and rune conjunction* to *her own blade*, which under *Luminalis* produces *an edge brought to resonance in sequence*.

### Luminalis as warning

- **Effect:** a standing detection net over the material world. Its function in the scene is established by its failure.
- **Cost / Limit:** `pending Isaac`.
- **Counter:** stated and executed. Luminalis reports things that *cross distance*. Kujo does not cross it — he is "simply there behind Haruki, standing upon the deck as though the sequence in which he had not been present had ceased to matter." A warning net keyed to traversal has nothing to report when traversal is not what happened.
- **What nobody knows:** whether Luminalis can be made to watch for absence of sequence rather than for motion.
- **The Test:** it does *distance-crossing detection* to *the material world*, which under *the light-marks* produces *warning for everything that travels and silence for everything that does not*.

## XIV · Psychology

**Her method, stated outright.** "There was no true outcome yet, but Haruki knew to hold onto what was best, rather than to assume what the darkness told her." She refuses to let a bad sign finish a story early. It is discipline rather than optimism — the outcome is genuinely open and she declines to close it.

**What it costs her.** Kujo answers that disposition directly, and the answer is the cruellest thing he says to anyone in the scene:

> "A condemned man can choose the scaffold. A fanatic can choose his altar. A martyr can choose the knife. Awareness does not sanctify the decision, Haruki. Sometimes it only means you walked into the mistake with your eyes open."

He is not telling her she is blind. He is telling her that her clear sight will not redeem her choice — which is aimed precisely at someone whose entire practice is seeing well.

**What she survived:** `pending Isaac`.

**Voice.** Formal and a little broken in construction under strain — "Lord Verinus is powerful, but never I thought to this extent…" Honorific for Verinus, trailing rather than finishing. One line is too little for a fingerprint; fuller voice is **`pending Isaac`**.

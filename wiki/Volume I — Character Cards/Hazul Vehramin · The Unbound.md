# Hazul Vehramin · The Unbound

> *"The moon does not roar, yet wolves kneel in reverence. Perhaps there is more power in the unspoken."*
> **Corrected.** *"Aether-Veil Hybridization / Lunar Harmonics / Transmutative Projection"* were not Essence types. **The path "Drift → Emanation → Entwine → Harmonia" folds an actual Temperance Stage name into an invented progression** — *Emanation is Stage XII* — which is exactly the confusion this migration keeps surfacing. **Harmonia is a genuine Category** and is retained as such.

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Hazul Vehramin. **Called** · The Unbound.
**Role** · **Eidolon Conductor. Aetheric Diplomat. Shaper of Veilbound Realities.**

### Affiliation

**Guild Accord — Senior Operative, Interplanar Liaison Unit.** Former initiate of the **House of Eirnvala**, the Seers of the Inner Moon.
**Soul Crystal** · *Catalyst of Umbral Voice* — **resonates with non-verbal Eidolon entities and dream-formed spirits.**
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VIII, Transcendence.** *The world is read as symbol. Objects retain their weight and gain a second weight, and both facts are operative, and the practitioner has to keep functioning while holding them.*
>
> **For a man who negotiates between planes, holding two operative meanings at once is not a symptom. It is the job.** *He is the only sheet in the migration whose Catalyst condition and whose professional competence are the same faculty.*

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Eidolyn** · Limina. **Governing.** *Templated nucleation — a supersaturated medium will not organise itself, but given a template it deposits along that geometry with high fidelity and real strength.* **Intent is the template.** *This is why Basil has genuine solidity rather than being an illusion, and why the pact holds across forms.*
> *Failure: template defect. Any flaw in the intent is faithfully reproduced at scale, and the practitioner is rarely able to see the flaw until it is standing in front of them.* **Basil shifts between wolf, mist, panther and spectral armour. Nobody has asked which of those is the flaw.**
**Transference** · Spatium. **Coupled oscillators exchanging energy with no conductor between them, the coupling falling off sharply with separation.** *The conducting, and the reason his orders do not need to be spoken.*
**Oneirion** · Limina. Configuration space — **workings reaching targets no line of sight connects.** The dream fauna and the Eidolon Drift.
**Mirithane** · Limina. Specular reflection; **a phase-conjugate mirror returns a wavefront that undoes its own aberration on the way back.** *This is Soul Echo Duplication, and it explains why what he returns is cleaner than what he received.*

## VII · Signature

### Soul Echo Duplication

**Effect** · Brief analysis, then he returns a single trait or working of the opponent's — **corrected of its own distortion.** *Not copied. Conjugated. What comes back is the technique the opponent was trying to perform.*
**Cost** · **Mirithane does not discriminate.** *A practitioner in a state they would rather not broadcast broadcasts it perfectly*, and he is mirroring while he does it.
**Limit** · **One trait.**
**Counter** · **Use nothing worth copying.** *Or attack Basil — Ardency 218 is what remains.*
> **What nobody knows.** Whether Basil's forms are Basil's choices or Hazul's template defects.

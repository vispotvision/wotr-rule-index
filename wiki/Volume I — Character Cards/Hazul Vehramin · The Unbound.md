### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Obsidian Vault and the crimson houses. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Hazul Vehramin was born in a chartered market town, to a woman who interpreted at its market court. She stood between two parties who shared no tongue and gave each the other's words, and the clerk entered what she said in ink. Hazul sat on the bench behind her for most of his childhood. He watched the parties' hands. He could often tell which side would yield before his mother had finished the sentence.
The House of Eirnvala, the Seers of the Inner Moon, took him as an initiate while he was still a boy. The Seers did their reading at night. His own mind ran clearest after dark, and most of what one mind pushes at another did not reach him. Zephriel Elmathor taught him there. The things that answered Hazul in the House had no speech at all. They were wordless Eidolon entities and spirits formed of dream, and his Soul Crystal answered to them before it answered to anything that spoke.

#### The Making

Basil came to him first among the dream fauna the Seers read at night, a panther with no speech. Hazul made a permanent pact with it. Basil was solid. It could be struck and it could strike, and it held across every shape it took: wolf, mist, panther and spectral armour. Hazul gave it orders and never spoke one aloud.
He left the House as an initiate, and the man who had taught him there became his rival.
The Guild Accord took him into its Interplanar Liaison Unit. His work was to deal with whatever held the far side of a breach, and with whoever held the near side. He learned to hold a threshold open long enough to talk across it. After a brief reading of an opponent he could give back one trait or working of theirs, cleaner than they had cast it. The Accord sent him to catastrophic planar breaches, often beside Wilhelm, the warden of the Obsidian Accordant Vault.
The change came at a threshold he was holding open for a parley. After it he read the world as symbol. Every object kept its weight and gained a second weight. Both facts were operative, and he had to keep functioning while he held the two. He finished the parley.

#### The Cost

Basil does Hazul's fighting. He spent what he had on the pact, and with the pact severed there would be almost nothing left of him on a battlefield. Those who know this attack Basil first. Those who know his other gift use nothing worth copying. When he returns a working it goes back corrected of its own distortion, and whatever state he is in goes out with it, whole, to anyone reading him.
On the Varakh Drylands a breach opened on ground the Dragonbound Accord's remnants watched. Hazul went to the rider before he went to the breach. Lyris Myndross and her dragon Phy'ruk Ritrax were flying its edge, and he dealt with her for the remnants while Basil went forward without a word and fought at Wilhelm's flank. Wilhelm held the mouth until it closed. When Wilhelm came out of his stance, the Verdiction Core was fractured. The Accord gave Hazul leave to put his hands inside a manufactured Soul Crystal, and he repaired the Core. The repair told him nothing of what the Core was rendered from. His report described Lyris and Phy'ruk in the air the same wrong way every other witness had, and the Archives filed it with the rest.
He has his own words for how he works: "The moon does not roar, yet wolves kneel in reverence. Perhaps there is more power in the unspoken." Every order he gives Basil travels as intent alone. A flaw in the intent comes out at full size. He rarely sees it until it is standing in front of him. Basil shifts between four forms. Hazul has never asked which of them is the flaw.

#### Where They Stand

Hazul is a Senior Operative of the Accord's Interplanar Liaison Unit, and he goes where the planes tear. He is still called the Unbound. Basil still does his fighting, and the pact is the bond everything else in him depends on. Hyo Miran once gave Basil a collar that changes shape with the phase of the moon. Their friendship is rare and cryptic. Zephriel Elmathor leads the Veilpiercers now, and the two remain rivals. Wilhelm's Core has been repaired once, by Hazul, and nobody else has ever serviced it. Whether Basil's forms are Basil's choices or flaws in Hazul's own intent has not been established.

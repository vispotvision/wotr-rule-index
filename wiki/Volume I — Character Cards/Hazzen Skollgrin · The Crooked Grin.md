**Role** · **Torture-Reclaimer. Aberrant Containment Hunter. Concord Black Chain Operative.**
> *Unofficial title:* **"The Smile They Send When They Want Screams."**

### Affiliation

**Black Concord — Enforcement Division, Severance Protocol Cell 3C.** *Assigned to capture or liquify unanchored spiritual entities, rogue Domains, or traitorous Accord operatives.*
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VI, Glory.** *Willingly accepting genuine soul-cost to preserve something valued above the practitioner's own structural integrity — the first Catalyst on the ladder that is a decision, made in advance, in cold blood.*
>
> **His Wellspring "was once a containment prism used to hold him. He reversed it."**
>

## VI · Traits

**Smileshatter Rebirth** · **His entire skeletal frame is reconstructed from broken executioner bones bathed in Dissolution Essence.** *Every time he "dies," his pain resets into laughter.* **His jaw is not hinged correctly — it unhinges metaphysically, disrupting resonance in nearby casters.** *Trying to cast near him causes involuntary nerve echoes: shaking, stuttering, vomiting.*
**Stitch-Glyph Containment Lattice** · **The visible "stitches" on his body are glyph brands**, each keeping in place one of **thirteen laughing entities** he devoured during Black Concord raids. *Undoing a stitch releases one.* **He does not control them. He just likes the noise.**
**Chain-Eater's Intuition** · **Any binding, magical or spiritual, becomes visible to him as tangible chains.** *He can grab them. Twist them. Snap them. Feed on them.* **This lets him unravel wards, rip apart seals, or eat someone's Domain anchoring in a single crunch.**

## VII · Signature

### The Cracked Lock Hymnal

**Effect** · He sees the chains and takes hold. **A ward, a seal, a Domain anchoring** — *bitten through in one crunch, and the thing it was holding is suddenly everybody's problem.*
**Cost** · **The Wellspring is not his and the regulation is B-Grade.** *Thirteen stitches, an Overgrown Crystal, and Overchannel running unshielded.*
**Limit** · **He cannot re-bind anything.** *Dissolution runs one way.*
**Counter** · **Bind nothing near him.** *Or wait — entropy does not consult him about which of his own structures were load-bearing either.*

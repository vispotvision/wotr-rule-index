# Heisuke — Muken's Last Soldier

*The Old Guard · Muken's Last Soldier*
> 📛 Named Heisuke by Isaac's ruling of 2026-09-12 (R23-7, bloodline route): a Yuno retainer, known by calling-name alone as a retainer of his standing would be. Formerly Taulagi.
> **Full Name:** Heisuke (formerly Taulagi: tau + lagi = war/season + sky)
> **Age:** 61 · **Bloodline:** Moto (main branch, border guard)
> **Level:** 278 · **Stage:** V Surge · **Band:** III
> **Role:** Senior military authority on the Kokan expedition
> One of the last men alive who served Muken's border guard.

---

Iron-grey hair bound in a single heavy knot at the crown, the Moto military style. Beard trimmed close enough to sit beneath a gorget. Skin dark brown and weathered to worked leather by decades in polar wind. Across his left forearm, a tatau inscription runs from wrist to elbow — black lines of a Moto military genealogy marking his service, his kills, and the names of the men he failed to bring home.
Eyes the colour of peat water. Wears brigandine that has conformed to his body over decades of wearing. The sword at his hip is not inscribed. It is steel. Good steel, Stannvaard-forged, maintained with obsessive regularity. He does not trust Aetheric solutions to physical problems.
Heisuke has not spoken to Temür since boarding. He will speak when Temür requires it and not before, because Heisuke understands the etiquette of a man returning to claim a throne: the throne is not yet his. The men around him are not yet his subjects. The distance between the two states is measured in blood and ceremony.
*The boy looks like his father when he stands at a rail. The same weight. The same silence.*

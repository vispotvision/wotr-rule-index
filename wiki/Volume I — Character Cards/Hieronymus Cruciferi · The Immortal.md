> *"Your bones are mortar for my road, your screams the song that paves it."*
> **Migrated with all content preserved, including the full artifact entry.** *"Oblivion-Aether / Kinetic Aether / Reversal Aether"* were not Essence types. **The legacy sheet states "Stage VII" in its own hazard-registry line and that is retained** — one of the few Stage assignments in this volume that needed no adjustment.

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Obsidian Vault and the crimson houses. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Hieronymus Cruciferi was born mortal in the Bone Hills of the Varakh Drylands, in the Outer World. The red earth there gave up iron, salt and bone phosphate, and little else. His family dug bone. They broke it out of the hillsides with picks, sacked it, and carted it east to the Kingdoms of the Five Rivers, where Valecar's grain country bought it by the load. As a child he learned how old bone split along its grain and how it crumbled when the pick went in wrong.
Rain came for a few good years and then stayed away for several. The Drylands were too poor for a great dragon to hold, and people survived there because no dragon wanted the ground. Nothing there refilled a practitioner.

#### The Making

He went east with the bone carts and stayed in Valecar. That kingdom sold cavalry, grain and mercenaries to anyone who could pay, and the contract companies took him young. He fought with a maul from the first. He learned the kinetic craft by taking blows he could not avoid and sending their force back along the arm that dealt them. Men with far more years in the trade came at him. He overtook them by driving himself past what his body was built to bear. The sergeants stopped drilling him in the line and sent him ahead of it.
From the contract trade he came to the Oblivion Concord, and the Concord made him its Champion. The Black Sun Cross was forged in its last days. It was a massive cruciform war maul of voidsteel veined with deathlight, and the spiral runes cut into each arm shifted when he fought.
In those days he came to see himself from outside. The record of every fracture in him lay open, each one he had built on marked and labelled, and none of it could be unseen. He did not like what he found. He had the mortal part of himself removed. The rite that bound him to the Cross burned his mortality away. In the gap it left he put the Black Sun Core, an imploded star of anti-light nested within his Soul Crystal.

#### The Cost

The rite took his mortality. It also closed him to every other soul. From that day his Soul Crystal burned out whatever foreign Essence reached it: poison, corruption, mind-binding, and any bond another soul might have offered him. No bond took hold in him after the rite.
The Core had to be fed before it could release. He fought by walking into a line and letting it strike him, and he sent the blows back along the arms that dealt them. Men near him lost their nerve. The Cross drew the souls of the slain into itself. The Core took in and took in until it broke outward, and inside its radius the ranks stopped being structured. He fought on through wounds that would have ended a mortal man. His bone and muscle reshaped themselves while he fought. When the field was empty the null had taken his own reserves with it, and a starved Core left him holding a very heavy maul.
The Divine Concord sent warbands to contain him. He slaughtered multiple warbands of it in retaliation. He said what he believed in his own words: "Your bones are mortar for my road, your screams the song that paves it." His list of relations named no ally.
In the river country of Valecar, the kingdom that first hired him, a commander bought mercenaries for a field. Hieronymus came onto it for the other side near midday. The battle ended in minutes. The commander's host broke, and the Cross took the souls of that field. One of the commander's hired swords never drew. He kept his employer alive in the wreck of the baggage lines through the afternoon and took him off the field after dark. Hieronymus did not follow them into the dark. Neither man learned the other's name.

#### Where They Stand

Hieronymus is independent. The Guild Accord's hazard registries list him as a spell disruptor and an annihilator of Essence. Multiple Outer Realm warrants name him for extreme battlefield destruction. The Divine Concord has not contained him. Mercenaries respect him, commanders fear him, and his arrival often ends battles in minutes.
The Cross still binds the dead. Nothing lawful supplies it. The Bench of Attribution has never been asked to rule on it, because nobody has got close enough to file. He always holds more than his body can bear, and he runs every risk it cannot shield at once. He is called the Immortal. So far he has survived.
What the Oblivion Concord was, and what the Divine Concord is, the record does not say, and it keeps both apart from the Guild Accord. Who forged the Cross and who performed the rite are not recorded. His card and Adalric Vladimer Valen's carry one trait name, Curse of Rejectance, and whether it is one working in two men has never been ruled.

### I. Identity

Son of the summoner Kwon Mu-jin and the weapon instructor Frithia. Twin brother to Geturo (older by minutes), younger brother to Lily (older by a year and two months). Called "Teacher" in public; the family knows. Summoning prodigy: at six performed something in a binding circle none of the adults present could name; at seven, redefined summoning. Connection to the Open Crucible (Mu-jin's Book of Summons) implied, unconfirmed. Goal: to become the greatest summoner to ever live. States it without qualification and believes it.

### Voice

**Notices first** · Postures, then the draw-gauges on the far wall, then other people's summons and the fault in each one.
**Sentence length** · Short shouted bursts that stack ("Fine. Fine! We go. We sit."), and now and then one long boast that runs away from him.
**Contractions** · always, at full volume.
**Pet word** · "Of course," tacked on after a claim, with the grin widening.
**Never says** · That he is tired or hungry ("Not hungry"), or a word in company about the work he does in the circles before dawn. He will not concede a fight.
**Stumbles** · Never while he is loud. Hurt or caught out, he trails off and restarts ("How'd you... no.").
**Gloss rights** · Only for his own summons, and only as a boast to the one it is hitting.
**Under stress** · The volume drops for once, the lines shrink to a word or two and trail off ("No..."), and he slips into the family plural for himself. The grin rearranges and does not drop.
**In grief** · The performance goes out of him entirely and the six-year-old in the binding circle comes back: quiet, small, and calling his father Father instead of Teacher in front of anyone.
**In joy** · Nothing held back: capitals, a whole-body laugh, and a demand that somebody else try it too ("Geturo, let her do it to you.").

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card, the eleven archived scenes it appears in, and the record of Class X and what it grows into. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Hiromi Mahuo was born to the summoner Kwon Mu-jin, of the house at Sum-gol, and to Frithia, who taught weapons. He was the middle of their three children. Geturo came before him and Lily after. The three of them learned early to walk a corridor as one body, Hiromi in the centre, Lily on the side nearest wherever they were going, Geturo on the outside watching the doors.
A court had made his father a symbol at fourteen, and his father had left it. In public his children called him Teacher, Hiromi easily, Geturo with more behind the word, Lily in a clipped voice that made it a formality.
The hair came from neither parent. It grew in white, the colour of forge-ash before it cools, over red irises pale as diluted wine. Nobody in the Mahuo lineage could explain it.

#### The Making

He would later claim he had worked out binding theory before he could tie his own boots. At six his father brought him into a practice hall full of adults. He walked into a binding circle and did something there that none of them could name. The room went quiet and stayed quiet. His father's hand on his back was the only warm thing in it.
The title came from people like the ones in that hall: instructors, senior students, and the men and women who had spent decades making summoning into a system that could be taught. They called him the Paragon of Dominion. All things were in his grasp, they said, and by Dominion they meant the world knowing what he was before he asked it to. From that morning he read every room the same way: bodies, postures, then the draw-gauges on the far wall.
At seven he redefined summoning.

#### The Cost

His father spent years trying to teach him that the hard part of a gift is the boy wearing it. Hiromi answered with volume. He laughed with his whole body and took the centre of every corridor, and the serious work went where nobody could watch it. Before dawn he went to the binding circles. Then he came back, pulled his bed apart so it looked slept in, and at the bell bellowed down the gallery that Geturo had made him late again. Geturo knew where he had been.
He did not eat and did not rest. He went down on a practice floor three times, and Lily, who had carried him to the infirmary, corrected him when he gave the number wrong. "Not hungry," he told her on the first morning of Class X. At nine he had watched a bullet train from a station platform and lain awake working out why it ran so quiet.
At the introductions he stood last. "My name is Hiromi Mahuo. And my goal is to become the greatest summoner to ever live." Then, after a beat, "Of course." Lily's pen stopped. At the dorm assignments he turned toward his cousin Verona and refused a roommate: "I'd rather have my own peace, and quiet."
When the tournament bracket lit, he rubbed his cheek with two fingers and kept looking. Then he crossed the room to Xanelor Rafiminar, who had told the class he had no magical potential whatsoever. "I challenge you to fight. It will be nice to fight someone with a wildly different fighting style." Xanelor advised against it. That made him want it more, and his father took the fight into the Combat Arena, inside a warded ring.
He rode the bullet train's imprint down the one straight line it could run. Xanelor turned it at the shoulder. Hiromi hit the ward and came up laughing and empty, then held a net of constellations from its centre while blood ran from his nose. A palm through a drafted warden threw him across the ring. Asked to concede, he said, "No..." He closed his hands in Juggernaut's Fist.

#### Where They Stand

Hiromi is thirteen. He lies on his back in the Arena sand with a split lip, a bruised shoulder and blood from both nostrils. The last arrow reached him as a line of fire. His father's vial turned it to liquid metal a body's length short, and the metal burned down through sand, clay and stone a spread hand from his hip. Mu-jin is climbing down into the ring. Nobody has named a winner or said what the vial held. The imprint is spent, every draw now costs him what a first call costs, and the Class X Open Tournament is seven days out.
What he did to summoning at seven is still unrecorded. His card implies a tie to his father's Open Crucible and does not confirm it. Whatever the discipline under the noise is guarding, no record names it.
Other cards carry the name further. Hazzen Skollgrin's card says Hiromi once tried to dissect him in the middle of a conversation. In a later life no scene has reached, he is the father of Karo Venrik by Saeloria, Queen of Eresse. No record says how the Venrik name came to a Mahuo's son.

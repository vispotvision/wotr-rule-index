# Hiromi Mahuo

**Lineage:** Mahuo (Korean stratum). Son of Kwon Mu-jin and Frithia.
**Age:** 13. Twin (younger by minutes than Geturo).
**Title:** The Paragon of Dominion (applied by others, not a formal rank. Pending ruling.)
**Class:** X, Aetherion Academy.

## Physical Description

White hair falling to his collar in loose waves, the colour of forge-ash before it cools, parted roughly down the centre with two longer strands framing his face. The hair came from neither parent and nobody in the Mahuo lineage has explained it. Red irises, pale as diluted wine. Skin pale enough to catch and hold lamplight. Sharp features where his father's are broad: high cheekbones, jaw still narrowing toward manhood. Small black drop earrings on both lobes, plain, on thin chains.

Black wool coat with a high collar that buckles at the throat, cut close through the chest and loose at the hip. Wears it open at the top buckle. Black undershirt beneath.

Build unspecified beyond lean. Height unspecified.

## Psychology

Loud. Confident to the point of proprietary. Walks into rooms as though they were built for him. Flirts casually, laughs with his whole body, takes up space that was not offered. The grin is settled and constant; it rearranges rather than drops.

Beneath the performance: practices in the binding circles before dawn, alone. Messes up his bed to hide that he was already up. Does not eat enough; has collapsed in the practice ring three times. The showmanship and the discipline are the same boy, and the discipline is the part he hides.

Goal: to become the greatest summoner to ever live. States it without qualification and believes it completely.

**Refuses:** micromanagement, surveillance, rooming with Verona. His private hours are non-negotiable.
**Notices first:** postures, draw-gauges, other people's summons and their faults.
**What others say when absent:** Lily and Geturo exchange looks. Mu-jin cannot find the words.

## Abilities

Summoning prodigy. At six, performed something in a binding circle that none of the adults present could name. At seven, redefined summoning (specifics unestablished). Connection to the Open Crucible (Mu-jin's Book of Summons) implied but unconfirmed.

"Paragon of Dominion" references the Dominion stat from FOW. Hiromi's relationship to it is described as innate: the world knowing what he is before he asks it to.

Stage, Level, Wellspring, Techniques, Stats: all TBD.

## Relationships

**Kwon Mu-jin** (father): homeroom teacher. Hiromi calls him "Teacher" in public. Mu-jin watches his son announce the intent to surpass him and cannot name what he feels.
**Frithia** (mother): weapon instructor. Hiromi's feet leave the desk before the sentence is finished. Involuntary.
**Geturo Mahuo** (twin brother): the responsible counterweight. Geturo watches the exits; Hiromi takes the centre.
**Lily Mahuo** (older sister): monitors his eating, threatens letters to their father, takes notes on methodologies she intends to adopt. He sticks his tongue out at her.
**Taesyn Valorin** (cousin): recognised on sight.
**Verona Mahuo** (cousin): declined to room with her. Boundary, not hostility.

## Naming Flag

Hiromi is Japonic register. Mahuo is Korean stratum (R23-2). Outlier budget exceeded at three non-Korean names in one family. Pending ruling on whether Frithia's origin justifies cross-cultural naming.

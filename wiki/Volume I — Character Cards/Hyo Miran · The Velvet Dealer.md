**Guildless by design.** Holds **Merchant Rite Papers** from the **Vellsorean Writ Consortium.** Shadow-affiliate to **Veydrassil's Black Bloom Market**, *though he denies it with a grin.*

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Outer trails. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Hyo Miran was born in a pledge-house on the road Calverne holds between the eastern mines and the coast. His mother kept it. Few who travelled that road could borrow on paper, so they borrowed against what they carried. They left a blade, a ring, or a bundle they would not unwrap in front of her, and they came back for it or they did not.
Each pledge went onto the shelf tied with a red cord and a tag that bore its terms, so the terms stayed with the thing. The boy slept in the pledge room. He learned early which pledges would be redeemed. It was the thing a traveller handed over last, after offering everything else first. His mother lent on what people said they needed. He watched what they wanted.

#### The Making

He left the pledge-house with a shortblade and a pack of pledges nobody had redeemed, and he sold them to the caravans of the Outer Trails. He walked with any caravan that would have him. He never cast openly. When a deal went bad, he ran. When someone came for his goods, or for whoever walked beside him, the shortblade went in once and exactly where he meant it to.
He kept the red cord. A thread he gave away carried whatever he had laid into it: an encoded memory, a charm glyph, a contract nobody remembered agreeing to. It bound whoever accepted it. A thread refused was nothing. He could not call one back once he had given it.
He took Merchant Rite Papers from the Vellsorean Writ Consortium. He joined no guild. He sold to desert kings, to temple thieves and to dream-bound children. Certain ancient relics came to know him on sight, and they feared him or respected him. They had a record, and he was in it.
Then came a deal he could have taken and did not. He looked at what refusing would cost him, in advance and in cold blood, and he refused. He never named the deal. The Accord's own literature would call such a choice the culture of duty.

#### The Cost

On the Greenroads he walked with a Beastkin caravan under Baojin Zhaorin's guard, selling to it. A relic was in his pack. Where the road runs within reach of the grove at Zhenri, armed men stopped the caravan and asked for it. Baojin talked with them until he stopped talking. Then the ground drank. The grove took one of the caravan's own drovers along with the raiders, and Hyo kept his goods with the shortblade.
Arbitration sent Kaien Veyren to review the fight, and Kaien brought Shael Virellion. Kaien scattered petals over the road and read the relic by them, and he found Hyo in its record more than once. Asked where the relic had come from, Hyo gave an account. Shael's eye was on him, open and weeping. Shael said the account was untrue. Hyo gave the true one then and named his price for it. Kaien paid.
The relic went into the Accord's keeping. A drover lay under the grove on account of a relic from Hyo's pack, and the review Kaien sealed named no fault. The true account led Kaien and Hyo to the ground the relic had come from, where more lay buried. Hyo lost the relic and kept his price. He came away with a partner who had read his name in a relic's record, and he had met an eye he could not sell a lie to.

#### Where They Stand

Hyo Miran still walks the Outer Trails, guildless, a relic broker called the Red-Threaded, the Pawprint Peddler, the Laughing Fang and the Velvet Dealer. The Consortium that issued his papers can revoke them. He denies any tie to Veydrassil's Black Bloom Market, and he grins when he denies it. In some regions his name is a delight. In others it is a warning.
He recovers rare relics with Kaien, who profits by his dealings and mistrusts them. Gorrum Vaelseth trades with him often. Ymir Rhok buys his rare metals and haggles over each one. He once gave Basil, Hazul Vehramin's Eidolon, a collar that shifts shape with the moon. He avoids Wilhelm religiously. He jokes about Dalphne constantly, the Wolf Sage who once gave him a bone-carved compass for shelter and dried meats. Dalphne never gets lost and had no use for it, and Hyo knew that when he took it. He calls Dalphne "the only honest soul that ever bartered with me."
He tells his buyers: "I sell what people want, not what they need. You want truth? That's expensive. How about a silk-wrapped lie instead?"
He is still paying for the deal he refused. Nobody knows how many of his contracts are live. Out on the trails are threads he has forgotten writing, and every one that was accepted still binds.

# Hyo Miran · The Velvet Dealer

> *"I sell what people want, not what they need. You want truth? That's expensive. How about a silk-wrapped lie instead?"*

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Hyo Miran the Red-Threaded. **Called** · Pawprint Peddler. Laughing Fang. **The Velvet Dealer.**
**Role** · **Traveling Merchant. Trickster Negotiator. Relic Broker of the Outer Trails.**

### Affiliation

**Guildless by design.** Holds **Merchant Rite Papers** from the **Vellsorean Writ Consortium.** Shadow-affiliate to **Veydrassil's Black Bloom Market**, *though he denies it with a grin.*
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VI, Glory.** *The first Catalyst on the ladder that is a decision — looking at something that will cost you structurally and choosing it anyway, in advance, in cold blood.*
>
> **For a merchant, that is a specific and unglamorous moment: a deal he could have taken and did not, at a price he is still paying.** *He has never named it and the Accord's own literature would call it the culture of duty, which he would find funny.*

---

## II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class II · Harmonic.** The attuned Shell — simple work stabilises, bonds form without tearing the self. **Efficiency loss 15 to 25 percent**, and he has never minded.
**Attraction Layer** · Well developed. **Every contract he has ever written is anchored here.**
**Crystal State** · Refined. **η** · ~0.55, Band D.

---

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Fixatio** · Materia. **Anchors and seals.** *The Echo Thread — threads and sashes carrying encoded memories, charm glyphs and invisible contracts. An inscription laid in Fixatio outlives the smith, which for a man who sells invisible contracts is the entire business model.*
**Judicium** · Fulguria. Order, measure, consequence. **The Fanged Negotiator, sourced** — *the bite that comes later is not a curse. It is a lawful consequence attached at the moment of the deal.*
**Oneirion** · Limina. Configuration space. **The minor illusions and the spiritual tagging.**
**Anamnesis** · Limina. *Objects genuinely store their history in physically readable form.* **This is Treasure-Marked Soul** — relics do not recognise him mystically. **They have a record, and he is in it.**

## VI · Traits

**Whispering Ledger** · **He always knows what a person** ***wants*** **more than what they say.** *His inner ledger absorbs desire-signatures.*
**Fanged Negotiator** · **If a deal is struck under duress or deceit, the "bite" comes later.** Those who betray him **often fall to spiritual backlash.**
**Velvet Claw** · Though playful and calm, **his strikes with the shortblade are surgical** — especially when defending his goods or allies.
**Treasure-Marked Soul** · **Certain ancient magical relics recognise him automatically, either fearing or respecting his presence.**

---

## VII · Signature

### The Echo Thread

**Effect** · A sash, a thread, a length of red cord — **carrying an encoded memory, a charm glyph, or a contract nobody remembers agreeing to.** *It does not activate. It was always already true.*
**Cost** · **Fixatio is permanent and he cannot recall a thread once given.** *There are threads out there he has forgotten writing.*
**Limit** · **It must be accepted.** A gift refused is nothing.
**Counter** · **Buy nothing. Accept nothing. Owe nothing.** *Which almost nobody manages, because the Whispering Ledger tells him exactly what to offer.*
> **What nobody knows.** How many contracts are currently live.

## VIII · Relationships

**Dalphne** · **Hyo once traded shelter and dried meats for a bone-carved compass from the Wolf Sage.** *Though he jokes about him constantly, he respects Dalphne as "the only honest soul that ever bartered with me."* **Note: Dalphne's Wayfarer's Intuition means he never gets lost.** He gave away a compass he had no use for and Hyo knows it.*
**Hazul Vehramin** · A rare and cryptic friendship. **Hyo once gifted Basil a collar that shifts shape based on moonlight phase.**
**Wilhelm** · **Avoids him religiously.** Claims Wilhelm once tried to charge him a *"tax on metaphysical humour."* **May or may not be true.**
**Various clients** · Desert kings, temple thieves, and dream-bound children. **His name is both a delight and a warning depending on the region.**

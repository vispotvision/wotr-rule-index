# Ilthára Korvaeth · The Mad Queen of Korvaeth

*The Mad Queen of Korvaeth · as of the morning of the yard at Korvaeth*
> **Full Name:** Ilthára Korvaeth. She signs with the name nobody gave her, in the contingent form, and has since she was seventeen.
> **Race / Lineage:** Drow, of House Korvaeth
> **Office:** Queen of Korvaeth, with no crown. The yard refused her a naming, and they have called her the Mad Queen since she was twenty-six.
> **Age:** Thirty-four. Of an age with Onawa, Empress of Eresse; they were fourteen together in the Vosk orchard.
> **As Of:** The morning of the yard at Korvaeth, the eleventh day of the withheld season.

---

## Description

She came out after the Empress and did not walk the yard. She stopped on the third step, a head above the block and two heads above the front rank, because a Reckoner needs a sightline and so do three hundred and six witnesses.
Five feet eleven, in the Korvaeth build, which is the deep build: shoulders narrow and squared, and hands too large for the arms. Her skin is the grey-violet of a four-day bruise and her hair the flat unlit white, bound back in a single black cord. A scar through the left eyebrow pulls the lid a fraction, so that half her face reads as considering and the other half does not.
She wore the coat with the salt-black facings, cut from the drowned alder groves below the Vessel Coast. There were eleven coats like it in the world and she had all eleven. Onawa came out holding nothing. Ilthára came out dressed as an estate.

## Voice

**Notices first** · The cost, and who pays it. She reads a document for the clause doing too much work, a room for who has standing to speak in it, and a man for what he is protecting.
**Sentence length** · Medium to long when she argues, sentences stacked one position at a time until the case is built; short when she decides: "I chose." "Begin." Her questions fall flat and end on a full stop, settled before anyone answers them.
**Contractions** · never; her Common keeps every word whole, the way the old tongue under it keeps its forms.
**Pet word** · "Say it," and the longer form, "Say the rest of it": she will not let a hard thing stay half-said in her presence.
**Never says** · That she was loved, or why the place on her breast where the crest goes is bare, since a woman who explains an absence has conceded that it is one. She does not count: armies go to the round thousand, years to the age she was, and exact tallies belong to the Reckoner and the field book.
**Stumbles** · Never. Her sentences arrive finished; when something lands on her she goes silent for a while instead of starting over.
**Gloss rights** · Only the naming law, and only as argument: she will lay out what a form does to the people caught in it to win a point. She never explains a word of the Vey-Elarin tongue, which she speaks exactly as the yard does.
**Under stress** · The older Eressean grammar comes up through her Common, the way it does in any drow under pressure: a claim ends on who witnessed it, a debt is in term or past it, and her speech shortens to orders. Both hands go flat on the arms of the chair, her voice stays level, and she gives a reader nothing at all.
**In grief** · She goes back to the girl in the orchard at fourteen who asked about everything. The long legal sentences break into plain ones joined on "and", a question gets out that she would never let out in a hall, and the worst of it comes in bare fragments set down flat.
**In joy** · The voice holds level. The joy shows as a permission granted in full, the bread with the seeds and both of you, and as two fingers laid against a child's cheek with the pads of the plates and not the edges.
**Sample line** · "And you are standing in a field offering me safe conduct back under stone as though the stone is where I came from and not where they put me."

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

#### Origin

She was trained from the day she could stand and dubbed at nine. At fourteen, in the Vosk orchard, a boy told her she did not have a name. She went to her father and he confirmed it, and went back to what he was doing without putting down what was in his hands. She came back crying, and Onawa could not bear it, and said a thing to make her stop: the holding validates itself.
The yard refused her a naming assembly at seventeen. It granted one to her younger brother Aeldros at nineteen, and he has been called the Validated ever since. She asked Onawa to come and hold her hand in a gallery at Rovann while they gave the name to the wrong sibling, and Onawa went. From that refusal Ilthára built a doctrine: standing is what you do, not what somebody hands you. The whole north came to hold its land on it.

#### The Making

When she was thirty-four Onawa offered, over a supper in her own hall, to petition the yard on behalf of the children of her house. Ilthára refused, because taking it would have been taking it, and the doctrine was new. Eleven days before the yard Onawa offered her the transfer in full form, and she refused that too.
In the yard she put her hand on the four-pass coil and named Onawa in full form, *Elar-uren Onawa*, with the witness word three times. She saw what it cost before Onawa's chin was down. A full-form naming inside the window Onawa had opened was an act of assembly, and it proved assembly was possible at Korvaeth. That killed the doctrine that it was not, and every holding the doctrine carried, her own first. She told Aeldros why that night: she had been a silence in a chord since seventeen and would not do it to the one person who had tried to stop her crying about it.

#### The Cost

Velthaeir entered her name as contingent and never narrowed the finding. Five Under-Houses came to her and six refused. The war that followed was fought over roads.
She won Sunfall Ford by silting the crossing overnight, and took Ess-Vaelen Green in the ninth hour of the coldest night of the year. There she took Thela Ess-Vaelen alive, named her in full form before eleven hundred paid witnesses, and let her go. Thela has never forgiven it: *"She did not spare me. She named me."* The Sylvaaren told her she could hold a valid name by self-witness. She refused it in eleven words, now carved above the eastern door: *"I will not hold my name from a forest I have never stood in."*
Korrin Vosk, the boy from the orchard, came to tell her she had proved him right in her own mouth. She said nothing to him at all. Afterward she told Aeldros: *"Because he was right. And because he was eleven."*

#### Where They Stand

She has put eleven waves against the ridge in eleven years and has taken it eleven times, and has never held it past the thaw. She sent Aeldros down the coast road with four thousand men. He offered Ruvaen Aloreth her terms there: the Empire restored under one crown and nobody hanged, in exchange for a petition to the yard at Velthaeir voiding Ruvaen's own name, since she cannot petition and never could. Ruvaen answered that the only term on that road was that she had wrongfully killed his sister. Aeldros died on that road, his head taken in one cut, and the four thousand were taken or scattered.
She has a son of eight whom the household calls Vess. It is a milk-name, and with Onawa and Aeldros dead there is no one left who could pass him a real one. She sent him and his sister to eat the bread with the seeds in the small hall. Then she went out through the Deep Gate in black-lacquered harness, with the Queen's Guard behind her. The place on the breast where a crest would go is bare on her plate and on theirs.
On the flat ground east of the ridge, on the second day, Ruvaen offered her safe conduct in form. She answered that the stone was not where she came from, only where they had put her. He told her to look down. The field was sallow-eye, the flowers Onawa picked there every spring as a girl, and a child does not pick flowers alone for six years running.

#### Ties

- **Onawa, Empress of Eresse** · her friend since the orchard at fourteen. She killed her in the yard and named her in full first.
- **Aeldros Korvaeth, the Validated** · her younger brother, named in the assembly she was refused. He died on the coast road.
- **Ruvaen Aloreth** · Onawa's brother. He killed Aeldros, refused her terms, and met her on the field.
- **Maren Vaeleth** · Reckoner of Korvaeth. She entered the naming verbatim, and kept a second book about what the child in the fourth rank saw.
- **Thela Ess-Vaelen** · taken alive at Ess-Vaelen Green, named, and released. She holds a validated name she could use against Ilthára in any court.
- **Vess** · her son, eight, who has no name to inherit.

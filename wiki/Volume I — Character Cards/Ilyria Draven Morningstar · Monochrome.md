# Ilyria Draven Morningstar · Monochrome

## Ilyria Draven Morningstar · Monochrome

> *"The armor is the point. Take that away and there was never a knight. Only a person who believed they were protected."*

---

### I · Identity

**Name** · Ilyria Draven Morningstar. **Called** · Monochrome, the Knight-Slayer. **Also** · The Beak · Pale Adjudicator. **Epithet** · She Whose Armor Remembers Every Knight It Has Broken.
**Role** · **Contract Slayer of Armoured Combatants. Aether-Reading Half-Vampire. Deniable Instrument of the Guild Accord.**
**Gloss rights** · **Unlimited.** She will tell an armoured target exactly what she is about to do to his coherence, because watching him disbelieve her is part of the technique.

#### Affiliation

Unaffiliated; formerly attached to the Guild Accord Special Division, contract-bound under deniable authorisation.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VI, Glory.** The moment she stopped pretending the elven calm and the vampiric hunger were separate things and let the room understand they were always the same instrument.
> 
> *Glory is the self at full expression, not the self resolved.* **A target once recognised her father's signature in her technique and used his name.** She did not correct him. Every part of her inheritance was always going to converge here; Glory is simply where she stopped being surprised by that.

**Crystal State** · **Luminous** — past any single Crystal Tier correlate. **η** · ~0.93, Band D. *Near-superconductor; the elven clarity removes most of the translation loss a hybrid Shell usually carries.*
**Composite Predator Ceiling.** Vampiric escalation and elven endurance compound rather than average — she does not fatigue in a sustained engagement, and an opponent planning to outlast her has already lost the fight he thinks he is having.

### VIII · Signature

#### Coherence Collapse

**Effect** · A Manipulator-class Aether spike threaded into the node sustaining a target's primary combat output, introducing a foreign resonance that makes it stutter — two to four beats of complete Aether access failure. The Verdantia read finds the correct node on first contact.
**Cost** · Sustained invasive threading against a resisting Shell risks Resonance Inversion on her own lattice; the cleaner her read, the more completely a foreign pattern slots in if the target turns the exchange around.
**Limit** · Requires line of contact into the target's Aether architecture — knights with Wellspring-reinforced plate and Domain-projecting combatants are the design case; a target routing no Aether at all gives the technique nothing to grip.
**Counter** · A Karo-class elven psionic with comparable Animatric depth can override her read with a stronger signal. Suppressed Coagula resonance disrupts her own Verdantia lattice regeneration at the same time it removes her hardening — one weakness, two failures.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether Coherence Collapse constitutes permanent Aether damage. An internal Accord review called it temporary suppression; two panel members filed supplementary doubt about whether that distinction survives a target read at her precision.

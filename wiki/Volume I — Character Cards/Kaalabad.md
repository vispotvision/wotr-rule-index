## X · Techniques

### The pull, brought inward

- **Effect:** he "brought the pull inward." The scene shows the shape of it only through its defeat.
- **Cost / Limit:** `null`.
- **Counter (R12-3 mandatory):** stated and executed by Kwon Mu-jin, and it is the definitive entry on this card. Do not overpower it. "Mu-jin did not overpower it but instead seized the structure, rebound its center, and forced it to collapse along its own governing axis." The working has a center that can be rebound and an axis it must obey; meeting it with more force concedes the contest it is built to win.
- **What nobody knows:** whether the center can be moved, or whether it is fixed by whatever gives the working its axis.
- **The Test (R17-2):** it does *an inward pull* to *the space and structures in front of him*, which under *its own governing axis* produces *a force that collapses along that axis the moment somebody seizes its center instead of resisting its pull*.

### The halberd, and how it left him

Recorded as an event rather than a technique of his, because it is the clearest measurement in his material and it was done **to** him:

"The halberd went out of Kaalabad's grip and across the precinct and through the standing remains of the north colonnade and out the other side, and it took two columns going through and buried itself to the beard in the cathedral's west footing at a distance of **ninety yards**, and stood there humming."

Whatever disarmed him also "took Kaalabad across the chest and the raised arms and put him off the ground." He got up: "Kaalabad straightened out of the crouch. Both hands were open and neither had anything in it, and he looked at them, and then at the colonnade, and then down at the man in front of him."

## XIV · Psychology

**What he is being measured on, and knows it or not.** The standard is stated from Kujo's side and it is not loyalty or victory: "Kujo did not measure the achievement by how untouched his general remained; he measured it by whether Kaalabad could continue functioning after the world had" gone against him. He was given discretion for exactly this reason, and Kujo declines to rescue him because rescuing him would answer the wrong question.

**What he does when disarmed.** Not panic, and not immediately re-arm. He straightens, looks at his own open hands, then at the colonnade his weapon went through, then at the man in front of him. It is the sequence of somebody establishing what just happened before deciding anything.

**What he survived.** A road on top of him, steel in his throat, and a man detonating himself to make an opening. He rose from the mud and continued forward. The narration is careful that "there was no mockery in it" — the scene respects him, and so does his enemy.

**Voice:** `null`. He is a presence and a subject of description in this material rather than a speaker; his own scenes in the archive may carry the fingerprint.

# Kaelen Brighthoof · The Horned Hawk

> *"From up here, the wind carries every lie, every footstep, every chance to put an arrow where it matters."*
> **Off-by-one slip.** The card reads **"Stage V (Flourishing)."** *Flourishing is Stage IV.* **Name wins — Stage IV.**
>
> *Which puts him at exactly the same Stage as Lyraen Callivane, whose card carried the identical error — and the two are named as friendly rivals in archery contests.* Two archers, same Stage, same mistake on both cards.

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Kaelen Brighthoof. **Called** · **The Horned Hawk.** *Arrow of the Peaks.*
**Role** · **Highland Tracker. Wellspring Path-Guide. Hunter of Outer Realm Beasts.**

### Affiliation

**Hornclimb Clan** — *a Beastkin clan living in the high mountain passes between the Core World and Outer World fringe realms.* **The clan acts as both scouts and defenders for travellers seeking to cross dangerous alpine Wellspring regions.**
> **Catalyst Event · Stage IV, Flourishing.** *The noise stops; identity settles.* **And the Catalyst requires a Spirit Axis — a bond carrying real mechanical weight.**
>
> **Hornclimb Bond is a soul-bond with the alpine beasts of his home range.** *That is the Axis, and it is the only one in Volume IV held with a range of animals rather than a person***, which is worth a ruling:** the Sixteen Stages describes the Axis as a bond with another soul, and does not specify that the soul must be human.

## VII · Signature

### The Wind-Carved Summit

**Effect** · **A Wellspring bound to a mountain peak, channelling gales and thin-air endurance into those attuned to it.** *Within the Domain, arrows fly truer and enemies tire twice as fast in the cold.*
**Cost** · **He must be on the mountain.**
**Limit** · **Altitude, and cold.**
**Counter** · **Fight him in the flatlands** — *which is exactly what he teases Lyraen about, and exactly what would beat him.*

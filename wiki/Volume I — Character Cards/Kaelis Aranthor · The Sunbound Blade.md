### VIII · Signature

#### Cataclysm Break

**Effect** · He releases every scrap of stored motion into one directional burst rather than metering it out across the fight. What it hits does not stay standing, and what is behind what it hits frequently does not either.
**Cost** · Everything Compound Velocity has banked across the engagement is spent at once. He starts the next exchange, if there is one, from zero.
**Limit** · It requires stored motion to release. A Kaelis who has been immobilized before he could build any has nothing to spend here.
**Counter** · **Immobilize him early, or fight him somewhere gravity itself misbehaves.** Immobilization effects and gravity-suppression fields both deny Compound Velocity its input before Cataclysm Break ever becomes an option.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether Horizon Overdrive and Cataclysm Break can be chained, or whether the one time it looked like he did it was Collapse Feedback nearly killing him instead.

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Sun and sand. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Kaelis Aranthor was born in the Voyager Era on the Aurelian Dust Coast, in a mining enclave on the edge of the Copper Sea. No rain reached it. Its water came in with the fog off the ocean, and its copper went out by wagon along the coast road.
His father guarded those wagons. When a house sold the enclave, his contract went to the buyer with the ore. Once the riders of a new owner came for the wagons before word of the sale had reached the road. His father held the wagon line against them for a master who no longer owned it, and died on the coast road. The new house paid nobody for that fight. The old one had been paid already.
Kaelis learned the blade on the same road, from the guards who had outlived his father. He never took a place in a wagon line. He learned to be somewhere else when a blow landed, and to be moving again before the man who threw it had his feet back under him.

#### The Making

He hired out young, and to anyone. He could tell which way a contract was going to break before it broke. He told his employers so, at length, and let them find out afterward that he had been right.
The Temple-State of Solfatara hired him to keep its young prince alive through the Glass Storms, on the road to a cure for the prince's father. At the edge of the Great Sand Sea the Guides of the Lost Meridian gave the party Zahur Vane. When the first storm rose, Kaelis wanted to cross before it closed. Zahur put the party in the lee of a dune and made them sit. In that storm the prince was lost from the lee, and Kaelis went out after him and did not find him. Zahur heard the prince's footfalls through the gold at his ears, walked out into the storm, and brought him back. After that the party moved when Zahur moved. At the end of the road Kaelis took the Temple-State's coin for a charge another man had found.
Later he took a contract that put him on the losing side of a fight he could have walked away from, and stayed anyway. He had chosen to protect someone in that fight. The coin stopped mattering partway through. He learned, the expensive way, that loyalty he chose was worth more than loyalty he was paid for.

#### The Cost

The side he stayed with lost, and nobody paid him for the end of it. He held his place to the last exchange. After that, anyone who wanted his blade had to negotiate for it.
Everything he brought to a fight he built by moving. Each clean step fed the next, and he could spend the whole store in one burst and begin the next exchange from nothing. An enemy who pinned him early found nothing to take. A sudden forced stop could turn the stored force back into his own body, and the crimson scarf was how he lived through one.
For everyone else he kept the old rule. Whoever hired him last got a transaction and nothing past it. He fought for most of the factions that courted him and took no oath to any of them. Many factions respected him. He was close to none of them. The roads called him the Crimson Horizon and Oathbreaker of No Banner. He led no one, and he held no ground he could not leave.

#### Where They Stand

On the coast where he was born, Kaelis rode as hired blade to a Varūn caravan bound back west with copper and nitre. A volcano on the Drakhal Spine went up. The ash came down the wind with an Aether storm inside it. The caravan stopped. Kaelis stood still in the ash with the rest of it. Rafik Al'Seren read the heat through the ash and found where the ground was cool enough to walk. Then Xhael Ten'Kor came out of the ash ahead of them, drew the heat around the caravan into himself, and walked it through into clear air. Kaelis knew him for the prince of the Glass Storms, and Xhael gave no sign that he knew Kaelis. Kaelis took his fee for a caravan brought through whole.
He owes no banner, and several factions would sooner retain him than lose him to another payroll. He carries the Sun-Touched Blade and wears the crimson scarf. He walks into fights smiling.
Once, to the people watching, it looked as if he had carried his fastest run straight into the release of everything he had stored. It may have been that. It may have been the stored force turning inward and nearly killing him. Nobody knows which.
He explains himself to anyone who asks. "You mistake confidence for arrogance. I simply know which way this is going."

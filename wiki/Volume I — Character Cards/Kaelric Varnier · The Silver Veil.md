### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Sanctum Aria. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Kaelric Varnier was born human in the Withering Era, into a family that kept one claim above every other. Their resonance, they held, ran toward the Orrenthal current, the Metallic Heart. The claim promised a line that would deform under any load without fracturing. His family said so often.
He was born into years when Wellspring supply was bought like grain, and the Guild Accord's academies wanted every blade that could draw on what was left. His resonance answered the examiners early. He met them with his answers ready, each one poised to impress. The academies took him young.
He learned in front of people. He learned alone as well. In empty practice halls he rehearsed his combat forms aloud, naming each one as he made it, the way he would name it to an examiner. He kept his silver-blond hair immaculate. He wore the academy issue as it was given.

#### The Making

The academies moved him up faster than anyone they had in hand. He passed every grading they set him. None of them pressed him to the edge of what he could hold. Nobody watching ever saw him strain, and his instructors wrote him up as ready.
Instructor Lurein Velos was the exception. He was Kaelric's Guild mentor, and he counselled patience. He asked for the gradings to be spaced out. His student asked for the next one, and the Accord gave it to him. It saw a weapon in him and set about polishing it.
By then he carried Mirrhalion. The longsword was translucent and engraved along its length, and it hummed in his hand whenever his will faltered. Under his instructors' eyes it stayed quiet.
The break came in resonance sparring. His Domain breached containment and manifested twenty-six constructs at once. Each one carried his swordsmanship on a delay. The Hall collapsed inward. Three cadets were lost in reflection entrapment. He came out.
The Guild suppressed the records.

#### The Cost

The Hall left a crack through the prism core of him. It refracted what he was into partial selves, and every construct he raised after that was one of them. He did not get them back clean. His reflections whispered thoughts to him that he had not meant. He lost track of which self had made which decision. When a working ended, the heat it left in him went out through his eyes, and he paid for it in tremors and a blindness from the flare. His family's claim had promised a line that would bend without breaking. His broke.
A binding he laid in blind rage was a binding laid in error. It stayed where he laid it for good.
The crack showed. Any surface he struck mirrored the state of it for an instant. A man who poised every word could no longer strike a practice post without showing the break to anyone who knew how to look.
Lurein Velos asked for a reading of his student from outside the Guild's own halls. Sanctum Aria keeps with the Accord and holds itself neutral, and it sent Serenyra Vaelith. They met in a training sanctum in the City Below of the Concord Citadel, where a light rain always hangs in the air. She stood at the centre with the Lunarch Crown on her brow. He was told to strike until she had seen enough. He drew Mirrhalion. Every blow he put into her field went out of it as light. At each one her field showed her the crack.
Lurein called the bout. Kaelric kept on. He struck faster, and the delayed selves he left behind him came in on the field one after another, each showing her the same crack. Serenyra lowered her staff. The bout was over. Her reading agreed with Lurein's counsel, and the Guild filed it beside its own and kept him in the field.
The next time she came to the Citadel he asked for the bout again. She gave it to him. He asked after that as well. In time he stopped striking. He asked her what the field had shown, and she told him.
The Guild also set blades of his unit at a chamber door beneath the Citadel, for the opening of Rik'Lye, the vessel the Alchemer had made. He was one of them. The glyphs on the vessel's skin began to pulse. Then the chamber liquefied. Floor, walls and benches ran together into living mercury, and every surface in the room became a moving mirror. His reflections were in all of it. Every one of them reacted violently to the presence that had woken. He was pulled back from the door, and the door was shut. Five researchers had been inside. When the mercury stilled they were crystal pillars. The Alchemer was gone shortly after, and the Guild would not record the vessel honestly. It was the second record touching him that the Guild would not keep straight.

#### Where They Stand

He is an active field operative of the Guild Accord, an Aetherblade Lieutenant of the Division of Resonant Arms, a unit of its blades. He is called the Silver Veil and the Glassblade Prodigy. His loyalty is conditional. The Guild keeps him under observation for uncontrolled resonance surges. Disciplinary hearings wait on him for reckless Domain manifestation during a training exercise.
He is still slim and taut. His hair stays immaculate until a surge haloes it in red-gold flame. One eye is grey-blue and the other faintly embered. He wears half Accord issue and half his own work, thin mirrored plates woven with sigil threads across the chest and forearms. The air folds quietly before his blade lands. Under a surge Mirrhalion fragments into several blade-forms, and any of them can cut the hand that holds it.
The strain the gradings never put on him is still owed.
Serenyra is often the only one who sees his fractures. One of his constructs is called Kaelric-3. It is gaining autonomy. The Accord sees a weapon to polish, and the Wellsprings are said to see a warning. Whether the crack will heal or become a current, nobody knows. Alone, he still rehearses his forms aloud.

#### The Steel Orchard

**Effect** · **He drives the Branch and the Slab into the ground together, and fifty meters erupts into a forest of steel trees carrying living flowers.** He controls the standing forest afterward: branches intercept attacks, vines repair his armor, and the air turns hostile to anyone not his ally.

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Class X and what it grows into. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Kairen Moto was born to Rikudoku Moto, a generation after his father's year in Class X of the Aetherion Academy. His father grew a forest, and the boy grew up under its trees. How it was grown, no record says.
What came down to Kairen in the blood made green things grow, and it did not know when to stop. It showed in him first as mending. A split lip closed by morning. As a small boy he broke a finger, and it set itself crooked before anyone could bring a splint. It was a finger of his right hand. He never grew to his father's height.

#### The Making

He left his father's ground and went where nothing grew. The Guild Accord took him on for reclamation work in toxic zones. It paid him by the contract, irregularly, and never extended him a charter.
He carried the Branch of Rikudoku into that ground. It is a living cutting from the Great Spirit Tree, and it has never rotted. It blooms even in a desert. In his hands it could heal what the poison had reached, or root an enemy where he stood.
Then he lost the right arm. He gave it to keep whole something he valued above his own body, and the crooked finger went with it. He rebuilt himself in iron and kept working.
The Iron-Graft is his own work. He bound it to living flesh with no Measurewright's help, and the join has held. Its grip is past any man's. It vents steam and Aether to blind whoever is in front of him, and a piston-drive hidden inside it throws his heaviest blows.
He took up the Slab as well, a broad blade with no edge. It crushes what it meets and can stop siege weaponry. An ordinary body could not lift it. He swings it because something in him pins its mass to the earth.

#### The Cost

The join burns where the metal meets the flesh. The right hand he no longer has still aches, and the iron in its place feels nothing it touches.
The arm needs a standing charge and constant upkeep. When the upkeep slips, his body pays for the failure. When the charge runs dry, the arm hangs on his shoulder as dead scrap, and his hardest working will not start. He once held a collapsing bridge for four hours so refugees could cross. The servos have never been right since.
With the charge in it, he drives the Branch and the Slab into the ground together. The ground puts up a forest of steel trees in living flower, and it answers to him while it stands. Flowers open on his own skin as well and feed on his blood until he lets the forest go.
He will not explain his own working. He believes his machinery has polluted him past explaining himself cleanly. His uncle, Senshi Moto, calls the arm a weakness. Kairen calls it necessary evolution.
He was coming out of a contract in Varūn's workings, the arm's housing cracked at the shoulder, when an Aether storm shut the pilgrim road. Kaien Thal'Ruhm was walking a caravan through it. He asked before he touched the arm. He mended the housing with his hands and told Kairen in plain words what the join was doing to him. Kairen had never heard it said that plainly by a man who builds such things. Kaien took no payment and went on with the caravan when the road opened, and Kairen was left with a debt and nobody on the road to pay it to.
Under every mercy Kairen does runs a cold conviction that some things must be cut away so the rest can live. He stopped a Rot-Swarm alone in the Rust-Garden. He fused its hive-queen to a boulder and grew a tree through her chest.
He wants to be worthy of what his father grew, and he believes his own iron has already spoiled it. He keeps away from the father he reveres.

#### Where They Stand

The Moto Clan recognises him as a Lost Branch. The Empire of the Moto's western reach has brought in or put down every other irregular of his strength. It has never sent anyone for him, and nobody knows why.
He still takes the Accord's reclamation contracts when they come. Between them he walks, mending what is broken and standing against tyrants where he meets them. His hair spikes over one eye, and a thick scarf hides his face below it. The iron arm hisses steam. The Branch rides over one shoulder in bloom, and the Slab drags behind him.
He carries one seed from the original Spirit Tree. He has not found soil pure enough to plant it. He is hunting a Technomancer who promised, for a price, to let the arm feel touch again.
"My father grew the forest," he says. "I am the iron fence that keeps the wolves out."

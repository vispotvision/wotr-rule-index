# Kaito Akagiri · The Scarlet Plague

> **A new error class, and it appears on three linked cards at once.** This sheet gives **"Temperance at Benediction."**
>
> **Benediction is not a Temperance Stage.** *It is a resonance — attested as gold's own alignment, the substrate of the oath.* **The sixteen Stages are Murmuring through Apex and Benediction is not among them.** *Kaede's sheet omits a Stage entirely; Yukari's gives "late Benediction, able to touch Severance" — and Severance is a Resilience sub-stat, not a Stage either.*
>
> **Stage VIII assigned and flagged as originated.** *"Vectra/Harmonia hybrid"* — Harmonia is a real Category; Vectra reads as Vectoria and has been corrected.

## I · Identity

**Name** · Kaito Akagiri. **Called** · **The Scarlet Plague.**
**Role** · **Court fixer, duelist, and social pathogen** — *he topples rooms the way fever topples a body.*

### Affiliation

**Former Arbitration liaison — burned his writ.** Now a **deniable Black Concord contractor** who sells outcomes to the highest bidder.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VIII, Transcendence.** *The world is read as symbol. Objects retain their weight and gain a second weight — a door is a door and is also a threshold, and both facts are operative, and the practitioner has to keep functioning while holding them.*
>
> **Kaito reads every room twice: what is happening, and what the room believes is happening.** *Dominance by architecture is Transcendence's dual reading turned into a profession.*

## II · Soul Architecture

**Essence Configuration** · **A pressure-and-pulse caster who works through breath, blood and posture.** *His inner flow rides the rhythm of a heartbeat; he paces foes with micro-gestures until their bodies obey his tempo without noticing.* **Prefers quiet casts tied to movement rather than showy projections.**
**Aether Shell** · **Class III · Resonant.** *Power moves even in silence*, which is the whole method.
**Crystal State** · Refined. **η** · ~0.70, Band B.
**Parun Affinities** · **Ma** (Memory) for reading tells · **Ur** (Balance) to hold the room's centre of gravity · **Uur** (Disruption) in thin cuts to break resistance · **Wy** (Motion) for knife-quick repositioning.

## VII · Signature Work

**Room Fever** · **Enters talking softly from the diaphragm; matches three people's breathing, then leads half a step faster.** *Conversation heats; someone blurts; somebody else agrees; the decision he wanted blooms like a rash.*
**Red Whisper** · **During a duel he lets you "win" the first bind**, then brushes the flat against your wrist and walks past. *Your hand goes useless and heavy as if sleep kissed it.*
**Sovereign Step** · **Heel slides to the outside of your lead foot, knee brushes cloth, his shoulder turns.** *Without thinking, you pivot to clear the lane he now owns.*
**Coagulant Lash** · **A tiny cut at the collarbone**; he pinches the air with two fingers and **your blood thickens along his invisible line, gluing sleeve to ribs.**
**Blooming Pox** · **He flicks red dust from a cord**; pinprick stings pepper the skin; *moments later heat flowers under each dot and your sword arm begs to be somewhere else.*

## VIII · Combat Method

**He fights close enough to smell your breath.** Feet slide heel-to-arch, weight always over the balls. **He feeds you angles that feel generous, then steals them back with a half turn of hips.** The scabbard jabs like a cudgel to the solar plexus; **the blade appears only when he is already past you, edge writing a thin line at the tendon that matters.**
> **He never raises his voice. He collects yours.**

---

## IX · Psychology & Tactics

**Dominance by architecture** — *he arranges choices so obedience tastes like your idea.* **He keeps debts small and numerous; every favour owed is a capillary feeding a future stroke.** *He rarely threatens; he predicts — and announces the prediction kindly so you can watch it arrive.*

## X · Appearance & Kit

**Layered white-and-black robes kissed with wine-red blooms.** A long scabbard slung across the shoulders **like an idle promise.** The weapon is a **lacquered staff-sheathed blade** — he uses it as walking stick, lever and gaff before it ever flashes edge.
**Red cords hang from the sheath. Each holds a memory bead taken from someone who thought they were using him.**

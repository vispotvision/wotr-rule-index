### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Rot and bloom. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Karasaki Hozume was apprenticed as a boy to an Apothecary Guild. He learned the stillroom from the floor up: the drying racks, the presses, the copper stills, the jars set out to steep. The work that fell to him was the waste. He carried the spent herbs from the presses and the dregs from the stills out to the heap behind the workrooms, and he turned them in. The heap rotted down. The beds it fed grew the herbs that went back into the stills.
He stayed. The Guild gave him his trade and his standing.

#### The Making

He made his place in the Guild on the work nobody else wanted, which was rot and what rot could be made to give back. He took up Kogane Kei while the Guild still stood, in a rite that Tyzura's unseen hand is said to have touched. The blade was long and reed-slender and gold, with a mist along its edge that never lifted. The apothecaries who first saw it named it the Soul-Reed. They believed it was still growing out of him.
Then the Guild was dissolved. Its workrooms were shut and its stills sold off, and the apothecaries went their separate roads. Nobody turned the heap.
He took the wound of the Guild's dissolution and made it hold weight on purpose. He left it open. The Hollow Cycle grew on it, in the shape of a man who had decided rot was not the opposite of growth.

#### The Cost

He went into exile after it. In exile he made the Phial, green swampglass stoppered with bone, and the swampwater in it never ran out. In the marsh country he took a bronze lantern out of a drowned shrine. Its green flame gave no heat. He stayed on in that country as its keeper.
Plague came into the fen villages of Hollowfen, in the marsh basins of Dorrosfenn. The dead were laid in the reeds of the lower fen faster than anyone could bury them. Aivyriel walked among them there. The reed-beds flowered out of season and went black at the stem, and the burial crews would not go down. Hozume went down alone with the lantern. Where its flame found a last murmur over a body, he let the murmur speak until the shade beside it went quiet. Then he laid the body under the standing water. He worked through her bloom for days, and it did not take him.
Some of the dead the flame could not reach. He held it over them and waited, and nothing came. Mirehollow Asakusa came down through the reeds to those, stood over each with his sword sheathed while roots came up around his feet, and moved on. After him the flame found a murmur. Asakusa never spoke to him. The region named the lantern the Lantern of Last Words after that. Aivyriel's bloom closed over the lower fen the next season, and necromancers came down from the theocracy to whisper to it. Every body under that garden had been given passage before it grew.
The year after, he fed the swamps of Hollowfen from the Phial and healed the survivors with it. The plague had left them sores that would not close and a wasting that would not stop. He poured for them again and again, and the flesh came back. Everything he healed that way was healed for now. Before the year was out the healed carried a grey growth under the skin, showing first at the joints. It had come out of the draw that saved them.
Kaien Rhuven came up the fen road late in that year. He knelt in the reed-bed behind each house, one hand in the roots and one on the sick. The growth went out of them through his own body into the reeds. Hozume went with him from house to house. At the last house he paid the only fee Kaien took, a story of his own. Kaien wrote nothing down.

#### Where They Stand

Karasaki Hozume is an independent Cycle-Warden and the Marshkeeper of the Hollow Cycle, and he still wardens the marsh country where he found the lantern. The Guild's wound is still open. He is aligned with Tyzura, unofficially, and Mirehollow Asakusa walks the same cycles alone. He carries Kogane Kei, the Lantern of Last Words and the Phial. Nobody knows whether Kei is a spirit-forged blade or a spirit in a blade, or whether the voice they share runs one way.
He feels the imprints left in places of grief or mass death. His hand in standing water still turns the rot in it to flowering, wrong-coloured and fed on the recently dead. What comes up closes wounds and holds ground, and it will not stop growing when he asks it to. On sterile ground, in extreme cold, or in Essence-depleted soil he is a man with a sword.
The lower fen of Hollowfen is Aivyriel's garden, and necromancers whisper at its edge. He owes Kaien Rhuven the survivors of Hollowfen.

# Karasaki Hozume · The Verdant Rot

> **Corrected.** "Marsh / Verdure / Sublimation Essence" were not Essence types. **"Cibation, Sublimation, Projection, Solution" as Core Wellspring Resonances** are Great Work operations, not currents — only Calcination on that list is genuinely a Wellspring. **Tyzura, Archon of Spirits** is carried provisionally: the Tyzura / Ashura Yukari identity question is an open ruling and this sheet cannot harden until it settles.

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Karasaki Hozume. **Called** · The Verdant Rot. Marshkeeper of the Hollow Cycle.
**Standing** · Independent Cycle-Warden. Formerly of an Apothecary Guild. Unofficially aligned with Tyzura.
**Catalyst Event · Stage VIII, Transcendence.** He took the wound of the Guild's dissolution and **made it hold weight on purpose** rather than healing it. The Hollow Cycle is not a philosophy he adopted. It is the shape of a man who decided rot was not the opposite of growth.

---

## II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · Class III · Resonant. **Crystal State** · Refined. **η** · ~0.70, Band B.
**Essence colour** · Vitalia arterial red shading to Verdantia chlorophyll at the edges — **the most viscerally uncomfortable Family colour to observe**, because the hue triggers the mammalian recognition-response to blood.

---

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Mortalis** · Vitalia. Venous black-red. Dissolution, threshold, completion — **the lawful withdrawal of presence.** *It does not preserve the dead in storage. It governs passage, and the Heresiology is emphatic that a trace is not a soul.*
**Verdantia** · Vitalia. The growth half. **Same Family as Mortalis, which compounds** — and is the mechanical reason rot and bloom are one operation in his hands rather than two.
**Phreatis** · Vitalia and Fluxia both, deliberately double-counted in the register. The wetlands. *Synaptic potentiation and aquifer residence are two physics describing one behaviour.*
**Anamnesis** · Limina. He feels spiritual imprints in places of grief or mass death.

## V · Signature

### The Hollow Bloom

**Effect** · He puts a hand into standing water and the rot in it stops being waste. What comes up is flowering and wrong-coloured and **fed on something that was recently a body** — and it holds ground, closes wounds, and will not stop growing when asked.
**Cost** · Mortalis governs passage, and using it to delay passage is **borrowing against a threshold that will be crossed anyway.** Everything he heals this way is heal-*now*.
**Limit** · It requires biomass and standing water. **On sterile ground, in extreme cold, or in Essence-depleted soil he is a man with a sword.**
**Counter** · Fire, and take the field before he reaches wet ground.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether Kogane Kei is a spirit-forged blade or a spirit in a blade, and whether the voice-sharing runs one way.

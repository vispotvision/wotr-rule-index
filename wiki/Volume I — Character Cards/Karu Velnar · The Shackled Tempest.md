### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Gale and still point. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Karu Velnar carries the gale-blood of Veyran, the horse-and-grain kingdom of the Southern Grass Sea. No record gives his birthplace. None says by what road a man of that blood came to the arena of Kael'Nir, in the forested border province of Eresse. He came to it a slave.
The arena kept him in iron, between bouts and in the pit. He learned to fight in the bindings. He threw the chain out wide and brought it back round like a lariat, and a man who closed on him met the iron before the fist. He learned to read the air. Every blow pushes the air ahead of it, and Karu came to know the push before the blow had committed. His hands and feet hardened where they struck. The arena beat all of it into him one bout at a time.

#### The Making

He fought his way to a name there. Years of bouts worked the wind into the iron at his wrists, until the bindings drove every strike harder than his arm alone could. He wore the spiral of the Windflow Rite in ink, and the harder his heart worked, the more wind it drew.
Some bouts the wind in him went feral. His senses sharpened. He answered each motion almost as it began, while his reason ran out into instinct.
Then came the Chainpit Uprising. Karu broke forty-three slaves free, and it cost him whatever the arena still had a claim on. He paid it willingly. The shackles on his own wrists broke with the rest, and he did not take them off. He kept them as the receipt. The forty-three were the debt, and the chains kept count of it.

#### The Cost

The pressure he threw in a fight came back into his own joints. The feral state tore his tendons faster than they could knit, and held too long it would have kept his reason from coming back. A binding laid on the spirit could shut the gale in him down outright. An enemy who outlasted the state could turn its tearing against him.
He wanted the story told, and that was half of why he kept the chains. He told it first on the forest road below the temple of Kael'Nir, the night he brought the forty-three out. Some were hurt. A wind-priest of the temple choir came down the steps to them. That was Iryen Maevith. He looked at the broken shackles on Karu's wrists and asked nobody who they were. He went to the head of the column and sang under his breath, and the column fell into his pace, slow and even, until the ones who had been stumbling kept up. Karu walked at the rear with the chains. Where the trees thinned, Iryen stopped, and Karu told him the story of the pit, all of it. Iryen said he had known it already. He did not say how. He turned back up the road before light.
The Guild Accord noticed him near its ground more than once and made him its offer each time. He turned down every one. He took no charter and no rank, and he answered to no one's chain of command.

#### Where They Stand

Karu Velnar hunts human traffickers along the trade routes, under no charter. On the roads he is called Chainbreaker. He tells the Chainpit to anyone who will hear it out.
One string of carts he followed south to a ford on the Horse Rivers, in the Southern Grass Sea. It had stopped before he reached it. The carts stood in the shallows with the teams in the traces, the drivers down on the far bank, and every rope on the people in them cut through in one straight line. Ravenn Kaelor sat on the near bank with his breath slowed almost to nothing. He did not get up. The people backed away when they saw the chains on Karu's wrists, and he stopped where he stood and told them where the chains had come from. Then they let him near.
Ravenn could not walk, and some of the people could not be moved, so Karu kept camp at the ford. At the fire he told Ravenn the Chainpit. Ravenn listened with his scarred left eye on the air round Karu's wrists, where the bracers stirred it. Karu asked whether the wind in Ravenn's blood ever took him the way it took Karu. Ravenn said, "Calm is not peace. Calm is restraint." Karu asked him to walk the routes with him, and Ravenn refused. On the day he could walk he went off across the grass, and Karu took the people up the road to the nearest village.
Azhura Vyren guards travellers on the northern routes under the Accord's charter. He knows her work, and she knows his. The Accord still finds him near its ground. He still turns it down. Nobody knows whether the feral state is his gale-blood surfacing, or something the arena trained into him and never told him about.

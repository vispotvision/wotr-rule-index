# Krothar Thunn-Gorr — The Old Chain

### The Old Chain · The Gorr-Keth Reaver

---

### I. IDENTITY

**Full Name:** Krothar Thunn-Gorr
**Alias / Title:** The Old Chain · The Gorr-Keth Reaver (mercenary name, Old World circuits)
**Also Known As:** Chain-Father (never to his face; guild circuit shorthand after his son's reputation spread)
**Race / Lineage:** Hobgoblin — Crimson Order, Kha-Urrum blood-branch. Standard lineage coloring: deep red-ochre skin, gold Legion Etchings, brass-iron eyes. No divergence.
**Age:** ~140 (middle-aged for kind; Hobgoblins: 200+ year lifespan)
**Sex:** Male
**Realm / Faction:** Hobgoblin Expanse (Gorr-Keth, high-island resident) — Retired from active mercenary/guild contract work. Former Guild Accord contractor, Rank IV certified. Fought under contract in the Old World western quarter, the New World eastern arc, the Inner World frontier, and the Outer World's edge-territories over a seventy-year mercenary career.
**Birthplace:** Gorr-Keth, the Hobgoblin Expanse
**Current Location:** Gorr-Keth, Thunn-Gorr family holdings on the caldera's western shelf
**Catalyst Event:** At sixteen, Krothar Thunn-Gorr watched a visiting Ironblood warband chief break a fishing dock in half during a trade dispute and walk away without consequence because nobody on the shelf could stop him. The dock belonged to a Bugbear family that had fished it for four generations. Krothar did not fight the Orc. He was sixteen and the Orc was three hundred pounds of kinetic recycling. What he did was pick up the dock chain the Orc had snapped, wind it around his fist, and carry it home. He trained with chains from that day, not because a chain was the best weapon but because a chain was what was lying on the ground when he decided to become dangerous. By the time he left the Expanse at twenty-two, he could take the dock back from anyone.

### III. WELLSPRING HARMONIZATIONS

**Monolithion** — Primary. Hobgoblin racial core. The Stone-Blood foundation. Krothar's expression is textbook Crimson Order: dense, stable, the lattice that everything else is built on.
**Pyraeon** — Secondary. Lightning-aspected. Warlord-line ancestral harmonics. In the father's hands this expresses as chain-conducted electrical discharge through direct contact, not the son's Four Heavens range. He must be touching the chain. The chain must be touching the target. The lightning is a delivery mechanism, not a phenomenon.
**Judicium** — Tertiary. Threat-reading. Developed over decades of mercenary work rather than innate talent. His read-depth is narrower than his son's but his read-speed is faster, because seventy years of fieldwork trained the response into reflex.

Speed is adequate, not exceptional. Strength is exceptional for his frame. Gnosis is low. He does not do subtle.

### VIII. TRAITS

**Kha-Urrum’s Marrow** — Same inherited trait as his son. Crimson Order lineage expression: the Core-That-Did-Not-Break. Under suppression or restriction, Resilience and Tempering are treated as above current value. The father's expression of this trait is lower-percentage than the son's, because the son's version was pressurized by the suppressor chains into something denser. The father's version is the raw inheritance. The son's is what that inheritance became under confinement.
**Stone-Blood Recycling** — Same mechanism as his son's, lower threshold, lower stack ceiling. Physical impact above threshold converts to Ardency output stacking. The father can harvest hits. The son harvests them better, because the son's architecture was compressed by the chains until the recycling became structural.
**Chain-Reader** — A lifetime of chain-fighting has produced a specialized kinesthetic sense: Krothar reads the tension, weight distribution, and structural integrity of any chain, cable, or linked structure by touch. He can feel a weak link in a fifty-foot chain by running it through his hands. This is not a Wellspring effect. This is seventy years of the same tool.

### IX. DOMAIN

**Not formed.** Stage VII Refraction: Domain-seed architecture is present but has not stabilized. Krothar has never pushed for it. He fights with chains, not concepts. The Domain, if it ever arrives, would express as a binding-field, but he has shown no interest in the theoretical work required to shape it. His son's Domain (Meridian Anchor) is what his father's could have been if the father had cared about architecture instead of application.

### X. TECHNIQUES

**Binding Lash** — Foundational. Weighted chain thrown to wrap and restrict. Not Essence-augmented at base level. At full extension he can bind a target at fifteen meters and arrest their movement with raw physical strength and Stone-Blood anchoring. This is the technique his son learned first.
**Throttle-Ground** — Close-range. Chain wrapped at the throat or limb joint, Pyraeon discharge conducted through contact. The lightning enters through the chain and grounds through the target's own skeleton. Lethal against anything with a conductive biology. Against Essence-armored targets, it functions as a sustained pain-compliance hold rather than a kill.
**Gorr-Keth Flail** — Area-denial. Both chains swung in overlapping arcs at full extension, the weighted ends moving fast enough to produce audible displaced-air crack. Creates a twenty-foot sphere of no man's land around his position. Not subtle. Not meant to be.
**Anchor Drive** — The signature. Plants both feet, drives the chain-ends into the ground on either side, channels Monolithion through his legs into the substrate, and becomes immovable. Anything that enters the chain perimeter is already inside his Throttle-Ground range. He does not chase targets. He makes standing still more dangerous than running.

### XI. SPIRIT AXES

**Freda Thunn-Gorr** (wife) — Weight: absolute, load-bearing. She is the reason he came home from every contract. Not because she asked him to. Because the version of himself that comes home to Gorr-Keth is the version he prefers, and she is the only person alive who knew that version before the mercenary career built over it.
**Krothar Thunn-Gorr (son), called Veylshroud** — Weight: complicated, unresolved. He trained the boy with chains because chains were what he knew. What grew on top of that foundation is something the father watches with a feeling he has not named and is not sure he wants to. The son's Soul-Chain practice, the Veil-adjacent resonance, the black-light expression: none of that came from him. He does not know where it came from. He has theories. He keeps them.
**The Keth-Gorrum** — Weight: institutional, inherited. The Thunn-Gorr family holds a shelf-island constituency seat that Freda manages in his absence. He has attended twelve parliamentary sessions in his life and voted in nine. He considers this a reasonable participation rate.

### XII. RESISTANCES

Standard Crimson Order profile. High physical, elevated lightning (Stone-Blood grounding + Pyraeon conversion). Low against Cold/Dissolution. Creative Null (cannot wield Emotional, Dream, or Chaotic glyph-forms). Same structural vulnerabilities as his son, minus the suppressor-chain complication.

### XIII. PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION

Tall, even among the Crimson Order: six feet eleven, built broad through the chest and shoulders with the particular density of a man whose bones contain asteroidal mineral deposits and whose muscles have been wrapping chains around things larger than himself for seventy years. His skin is the deep red-ochre of standard Crimson Order expression, darkening to a burnt umber across the knuckles and forearms where decades of chain-friction have thickened the calluses into something closer to leather than skin. Legion Etchings run in clean gold lines along his jaw and down the sides of his neck, recording unit designations and earned marks from his early Order training, though several of the etching-lines are interrupted or overlaid with scar tissue from later fighting that he never had re-inscribed.
His hair is dark, the color of volcanic glass, thick and coarse, swept back from the forehead and tied loosely at the crown. Grey has come in hard at the temples and through the front, silver-white strands that catch light differently from the black, giving him a look that reads as weathered rather than aged. His beard is short, kept to the jawline, dark with the same silver threading. His eyes are polished brass, the irises carrying the mechanical intensity of the Crimson Order phenotype. His brow is heavy, the frown lines carved deep enough to be permanent, and his nose has a flattened bridge from a break that healed without being set.
He wears scale armor at the chest and shoulders, burnished dark, the scales overlapping in the Crimson Order pattern, fitted over a mail shirt. An olive-green scarf wraps the throat and lower neck, thick wool, practical against both weather and chain-snap. His fighting chains ride coiled over both shoulders, each one fifteen feet of Monolithion-inscribed iron links with weighted heads, worn the way other men wear cloaks. His hands are enormous.

### XIV. PSYCHOLOGY

**Core Wound:** The dock. Watching strength walk away from the damage it caused because nobody could answer it. Every contract he ever took was an answer to the question of what happens when nobody on the dock is dangerous enough.
**Flaw:** Cannot teach restraint because he learned force first and discipline second. He taught his son chains. He did not teach his son what to do when the chains are not enough to hold what is inside. This is the gap, and both of them know it.
**Desire:** To sit on the caldera shelf with Freda and watch the sun hit the water and not think about what his son's Essence feels like from three islands away. To be done. He is not done.
**Strength:** Reliability that has become reputation. Seventy years of contracts, not one broken, not one abandoned, not one job left unfinished. The Guild Accord's records describe him as "consistent," which is the mercenary equivalent of a love letter.
**Loop:** Leaves home to fight. Comes home to Freda. Finds that home has continued without him. Feels the distance. Leaves again because the distance is easier to manage from the outside.
**Foil:** His son. Krothar is discipline over talent. His son is talent that discipline cannot fully contain.
**Voice / Dialect:** Crimson Order register overlaid with seventy years of guild-circuit pidgin. Heavy, deliberate, every word placed with the economy of a man who spent the first sixteen years of his life under the Vow of Silence and never fully left the habit. Speaks in short declaratives. Does not explain himself. Dry, infrequent humor that lands once and is never repeated.
**Signature line:** "Chain does the same thing every time. That's why I trust it."
**Posture:** Plants. Does not shift weight. When standing, his center of gravity drops to his heels and stays there. People read this as patience. It is the Anchor Drive stance without the chains deployed.
**Stress Default:** Reaches for the chains at his shoulders and re-settles them. The motion is unconscious. Freda has watched him do it ten thousand times.

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Old World roads and chains. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Krothar Thunn-Gorr was born on Gorr-Keth, the tallest island of the Hobgoblin Expanse, whose peak glowed orange at night when the wind shifted. His blood was the Crimson Order's, of the Kha-Urrum blood-branch. It came out to the standard: red-ochre skin, gold Legion Etchings along the jaw, brass eyes. Nothing in him diverged.
The Order kept him under its Vow of Silence for the first sixteen years of his life. He learned the drills and the field-signs. On the shelf the boats came in on volcanic-glass hulls, and one dock there had been fished by the same Bugbear family for four generations.
Freda Vaal-Kess met him on the shelf docks while he had not yet spoken a word outside the Vow. She came of an old civilian line outside the Order. She talked enough for both of them. The courtship lasted six years. One aunt called it scandalous.

#### The Making

At sixteen he stood on the shelf and watched a visiting Ironblood warband chief break a fishing dock in half during a trade dispute. It was the Bugbear family's dock. The chief walked away without consequence, because nobody on the shelf could stop him.
Krothar did not fight the Orc. He was sixteen, and the Orc was three hundred pounds. He picked up the dock chain the Orc had snapped, wound it around his fist and carried it home. The chain had been the Bugbears'.
He trained with chains from that day. A chain was what had been lying on the ground when he decided to become dangerous, and he kept to it. He learned force first and discipline after it. The first thing he mastered was the weighted throw that wraps a man and stops him where he is. Then he learned to plant.
By the time he left the Expanse at twenty-two, he could take the dock back from anyone.

#### The Cost

He fought on contract for seventy years, a Guild Accord contractor in the Old World's western quarter, the New World's eastern arc, the Inner World frontier and the edge-territories of the Outer World. The Old World circuits named him the Gorr-Keth Reaver. He broke no contract, abandoned none and left no job unfinished, and the Accord entered him in its records as "consistent". He did not chase. He drove his chain-ends into the ground on either side, set his feet, and made standing still near him more dangerous than running.
On the frontier march the Caravan Families hired him to escort their trains. Every contract ran to its term. When a raid came he planted with his chains in the ground and let it come to him, and when it broke he let it go. He spoke seldom and explained nothing. The Families trusted his work and understood little else of him. His pay went home to Gorr-Keth on the papers Freda kept: pay schedules, guild insurance, repatriation clauses.
The work marked him. A break across the bridge of his nose healed without being set. Scar tissue from later fighting cut across several of the gold lines on his jaw and neck, and he never had them re-inscribed. His fighting chains were re-forged after the Outer World campaign. He was offered better armor than the Order-pattern scale he had trained in, and he kept the scale, because it did not have opinions. Of the chain he said, when he said anything: "Chain does the same thing every time. That's why I trust it."
Each time he came home the house had gone on without him. Freda ran the family's constituency seat in the Keth-Gorrum while he was away, and in his whole life he attended twelve sessions of the parliament and voted in nine. Then he left again.
Their son was born green, the green of lichen on wet stone, where the Order runs red-ochre. The Order noticed. The boy carried his father's name. Freda raised him on Gorr-Keth while his father was on contract, and in the seasons Krothar was home he trained the boy in the caldera yard with chains, because chains were what he knew. The weighted throw came first. At seventeen the boy passed his father's ceiling, and Krothar watched him do it. Freda watched it too. They did not discuss it.
The son left the Expanse with his father's chain technique. What grew on top of it did not come from Krothar: silver chains that answer the son unsummoned, and a black light that comes only with his restraints off. Krothar looked at his son's unmanaged output and made a decision. He forged sigil shackles for the son's wrists, forearms and throat, and the son accepted them. Afterward he took his own fighting chains to the same forge, cut the same inscriptions into them, and spent the better part of a season on the work. That year he said to Freda, "I taught him the chain. I taught him the weight. I taught him the arc and the snap and the bind. I did not teach him what comes after. Nobody taught him what comes after. That is the problem." Freda has never repeated it.

#### Where They Stand

Krothar has come off contract work and lives on the Thunn-Gorr holdings on the caldera's western shelf. He plants when he stands and does not shift. When something troubles him he reaches for the chains coiled over his shoulders and re-settles them without knowing he does it. Freda has watched him do it ten thousand times. The olive wool scarf at his throat is the sixth. She made the first.
He wants to sit on the caldera shelf with Freda and watch the sun hit the water, and to be done. He is not done.
The guild circuits call him Chain-Father now, never to his face, since his son's reputation spread. The son goes by Veylshroud. He does not write and he does not visit. On a ridge in the Scourge of Hell, after a fight, the son reached for the chains at his shoulders to resettle them. The shackles Krothar forged were made for less than the son has become, and hairline fractures now show in the metal. The son has not contacted the man who made them. Krothar does not understand all of what he sees in his son, and the part he does understand is the part that required suppression. He has theories about the black light. He keeps them.
Freda has worn the ear-drop he carved since their wedding, bone-white volcanic glass from the shelf islands. Neither of them will say what shape it is. The one time their son asked, they looked at each other and changed the subject together.
On the frontier march the Caravan Families' old hands still tell of the Hobgoblin who did not chase, and they told a young courier there that he stood the way the Old Chain had stood. Krothar has no way of knowing it.

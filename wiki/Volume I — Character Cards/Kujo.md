# Kujo

Source: `scenes/commencement_of_the_curia_kujo_arc.md`. Every line below is drawn from that scene. No number in the source, so every numeric field reads `pending Isaac` per the conversion rule. Anything originated is marked.

## I · Identity

- **Name:** Kujo. The scene gives no line name, no house and no second element; register unassigned — `pending Isaac`.
- **Age / origin:** not stated. `pending Isaac`.
- **Affiliation:** none declared. He acts alone and against the Curia, the Alabaster Court and the Moto seat at Kharven.
- **Status:** active and escalating. At the close he stands in the ruined tower at Kharven under a darkness that has swallowed the sun.
- **Level / Stage / Band:** `pending Isaac` — the source gives no Level, Stage or Band, and the conversion rule forbids picking one.
- **Catalyst Event:** the Crevice. The scene states it was "torn from the Titan whose deepest authority governed precisely that interval" — Valmir, whose authority is collapse, transition, "the violent interval between structure and failure." Valmir's ruin always permitted renewal: "the mountain breaking so another landscape might rise, the dead star feeding what followed." Kujo takes the authority and strips the renewal out of it. Everything below is downstream of that theft.

## II · Soul Architecture

The source describes his interior architecturally but assigns no system terms. What it does state:

- The Crevice sits **beneath his sternum** and pulses black-gold. It is a seated foreign authority, not a native organ.
- His existing power is **Void**: "remove, deny, sever, and refuse continuation."
- The Crevice **integrates** with that Void architecture rather than replacing it: "Destruction and absence remained separate principles, but now they could overlap."
- The whole is explicitly layered and heterogeneous: "Void, Crevice, old Aether, stolen structures, fused law, the remaining consequences of Valmir, transformations, and the strange personal economy sustaining The Unwinding made his current existence look like a civilization layered upon ruins layered upon another civilization."
- The Origin Eye, examining him, "did not attempt to understand Kujo from the top downward. It looked for the first point at which the power currently circulating through him had become available to the present Kujo."

Soul Crystal state, Aether Class, Essence Typology, Coherence Band and η: **`pending Isaac`**. The source describes stolen and fused structure, which is not the same as a Crystal reading, and I will not convert one into the other.

## X · Techniques

Names used below appear verbatim in the scene. Costs and durations are `pending Isaac` throughout.

### The Unwinding

- **Effect:** the internal economy that reproduces what Xevorith formerly supplied from outside, surfacing as gold fractures across the true form.
- **Cost:** `pending Isaac` — the text calls it "the strange personal economy sustaining The Unwinding" without pricing it.
- **Limit:** it is nested in his personal architecture, not worn; the Fifth Layer he discarded no longer covers him.
- **Counter:** severing the supply is no longer available, which is the point of it — a counter must attack the reproduction itself, not the former external source. Attacking Xevorith as though it still possessed him is the documented error the text warns against.
- **What nobody knows:** what the economy is actually paying with.
- **The Test (R17-2):** it does *internal reproduction* to *a formerly external supply*, which under *his own Unwinding* produces *independence from Xevorith without return to the Fifth Layer*.

### The Crevice, run with the Void

- **Effect:** the Void removes, denies, severs and refuses continuation; the Crevice "could make something remain long enough to experience its own deterioration." Overlapped, a thing persists while failing, aware of it. The stated applications are structural: "Kingdoms could stand while their foundations became increasingly expensive to preserve. Steel could retain its shape while slowly losing fidelity. Wards could continue functioning while drifting from the laws they were meant to uphold."
- **Cost / Limit:** `pending Isaac`.
- **Counter:** the failure is in the maintenance cost, not the object, so anything that audits a structure's cost of upkeep against its stated law detects it before collapse. Inspecting the object alone returns sound.
- **What nobody knows:** whether a structure taken this far can be returned.
- **The Test:** it does *sustained deterioration* to *a standing structure*, which under *the overlap of absence and destruction* produces *a thing that keeps its shape while ceasing to be trustworthy*.

### Xevorith, folded across an approach

- **Effect:** the black mantle is folded "across the space between each Chain and himself," so attacks find no completed route. "Every route disappeared before completion, leaving the weapons striking empty black while Kujo remained directly in front of him."
- **Cost / Limit:** `pending Isaac`. Note he first "allowed them to move just far enough for Sonzai to believe another opening existed" — the technique is used as bait, which suggests it is worth setting up rather than free.
- **Counter:** it defeats routes, not presence. He remains exactly where he appears to be; a strike that does not need to travel a route is not addressed by it.
- **What nobody knows:** whether the folded space has a volume that can be filled.
- **The Test:** it does *route denial* to *an incoming weapon path*, which under *Xevorith's mantle* produces *an aborted strike into empty black*.

### The Eternal Darkness over Kharven

- **Effect:** a vertical mass of darkness gathered above a target and brought down faster than a warning bell's first note; persisting after, it "had swallowed the sun completely," leaving the ruined chamber lit only by Calamity's crimson and the Crevice's black-gold.
- **Cost / Limit:** `pending Isaac`.
- **Counter:** it is delivered by descent onto a located target — he found Sonzai by following Malakar's old corruption "like a stain through the Plane of Fate." Denying the Fate-stain denies the aiming, which is upstream of the weight.
- **What nobody knows:** whether it lifts, and on what.
- **The Test:** it does *occlusion and descent* to *a located seat*, which under *the black mantle raised from beneath* produces *a tower crushed and a sun removed*.

### The withdrawal of relationships (used on Zuberi)

Name **originated, pending ratification** — the scene describes the working without naming it.

- **Effect:** not erasure of a form but the simulated individual withdrawal of "every relationship connecting Zuberi to that form," so that authority is left "with no reassuring proof that the person exercising it would remain coherent." Zuberi died; "the Material Plane knew it, the Soul Plane registered the departing absence, and Fate had already begun changing every connection that depended upon Dawn Guard remaining alive."
- **Cost / Limit:** `pending Isaac`.
- **Counter:** stated in the text, by Asante — "an absolute refusal to accept the relationship Kujo had just created," grief supplying the Obsession Force with what hatred alone never had. The counter is a relationship that refuses the withdrawal.
- **What nobody knows:** whether the refusal holds or only delays.
- **The Test:** it does *relational withdrawal* to *a person's hold on their own form*, which under *Sovereignty's demand for Material acknowledgment* produces *authority that cannot prove its own holder will stay coherent*.

## XII · Resistances

Per-Wellspring and per-Family resistances, and Resilience against hostile workings: **`pending Isaac`**.

One resistance event is documented in full and should be read as an outcome, not a rating. The Custodia rite "had targeted him, measured his actions, taken hold of the Crevice's propagated function, and attempted to separate him from the safeguarded world." ## XIII · Physical Description

Two presentations, and the scene's interest is in the seam between them.

**The worn form.** A beautiful pale face. Courteous. The failure mode is described precisely: when it returns after slipping it returns "imperfectly. The eyes remained too dark, the smile too wide, and the voice too layered." Under the Crevice his voice "came out lower now, more pleased than controlled," and his composure frays at the edges first — "his eyes widened, and the last clean edges of his composure continued to fray."

**The true form.** "Black and grey armored flesh, ribbed sinew, exposed crimson channels, the horned crown of his monstrous head, masses of segmented tendrils unfurling from his back, and the vast execution limb twitching as gold fractures crawled through the abomination." Crimson channels beneath the skin "illuminated one after another"; black tendrils erupt from his shadow.

**Carried on him.** Xevorith covers him "from the neck downward like a seamless black cape," and the Crevice beats behind it in slow black-gold pulses "that made the ruined tower groan around him." Gold fractures open along the black armor of his ribs, inside the curve of a tendril, and finally "throughout the architecture of his true form until gold stared outward from every direction."

## XIV · Psychology

**What he believes that costs him.** That mercy was Valmir's error and his own correction of it is owed to the world: "And when their bells stop ringing… I will teach the rest of this world what Valmir was too merciful to understand." He holds an authority whose entire prior meaning was that ruin makes room, and he has removed the making-room.

**What he refuses.** Continuity as a promise. Xevorith taught him "the exact opposite: the present did not owe obedience to the version of a person that existed yesterday" — and he applies it as method, declining to use old predictions on Aurelian because a person altered is not the person predicted. The same refusal is what he inflicts on Zuberi.

**What he wants.** Not death. "The Crevice drank the consequence without consuming the souls themselves. It was the structural aftermath Kujo wanted, the sudden vacuum of labor, governance, memory, food production, protection, inheritance, and recognition. Death was merely the first motion."

**Voice.** Courtesy worn as a surface and abandoned mid-scene rather than dropped: the smile that "slowly disappeared," replaced by "a stare sharpened by purpose and made unstable by the power now moving through him." Amusement returns quickly and is the tell that he believes he is ahead. He talks while working.

**What he survived:** `pending Isaac`. The Fifth Layer he "discarded" and Xevorith's former possession of him are both referred to as past events the scene does not narrate.

## XVII · Fracture Log

The source describes fractures as visible features rather than logged injuries, and does not say whether they are consolidated. Recorded as the text has them:

- **Gold fractures, distributed.** First along the black armor of the ribs, then inside the curve of a tendril, then "dozens more… until gold stared outward from every direction." The text is explicit that these are not Xevorith returning to possess him and not a crawl back into the discarded Fifth Layer — they are his own Unwinding reproducing what was once supplied. Consolidated or unconsolidated: **`pending Isaac`**.
- **The human form's failure.** "For an instant Kujo's human form failed completely." It returns imperfectly every time. Price: the seam is legible to anyone watching the eyes, the smile and the voice.
- **The missing Titan.** Not his fracture but the one he runs on: there is "no Titan left to insist that ruin eventually become renewal." Whatever that costs him is unpaid and undescribed in the source.

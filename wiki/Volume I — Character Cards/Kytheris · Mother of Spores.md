### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Rot Host. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Kytheris was born in the Bone Hills of the Varakh Drylands, in the Outer World, to a family that dug bone. The hills yield bone phosphate. Her people cut it from the old beds, ground it, and carted it down to the red-earth farms, where it was spread on the fields and paid for in grain. She worked the sieve as soon as she could lift one. A field dressed with ground bone gave more than a bare one. The farmers paid for the difference.
The Drylands give several abundant years and then a severe drought. In the good years the carts came back with grain, and the family stored what it could not eat. Then the rain failed. The farms below had nothing to trade, and the hill families lived on the old store, which had gone grey in the heat. Those who ate the grey grain saw things before they died. Some tore at their own skin. Kytheris ate it and lived.

#### The Making

She sat with the dying through that drought. Each of them held a note of their own, and the grey grain reached the note before it reached the body. She could hear it take them while they still talked to her.
That year the dead went into the worked-out beds. At the first rain the beds came up pale with fungus before any seed did. Her first working was a bloom on one of those graves. She drew the growth up out of the dead and watched what it made of them. She never went back to the sieve.
When the rains held she went down to the farms and the river towns with the grey strain and the pale one. She learned where each would grow: in stored grain, in a roof beam, in the chest of a sick man.
The change came with her hands in the fungus of a worked-out bed. After it, a thing kept its weight and gained a second weight, and both were operative. A person was a person and was also soil.

#### The Cost

She made a doctrine of it and has worked by it since. Whoever breathes her spores is marked as soil. Some die of it. In those who live, the seeds lodge in the lattice of the soul and wait.
Her body changed to carry the work. A crown of luminous spore-antlers grew from her head and has not stopped growing, each node a strain of the rot that eats a mind. At intervals her back splits open. Fungal homunculi climb out of it: bone-borers, nerve-chokers, mind-feeders.
Her spores pass from solid to vapour with nothing in between, and no point in the working lets her look at it or correct it. Once a release is airborne it is beyond her reach. She has gone on releasing for years.
She reads a soul's frequency and releases at it, and the resistance stays whole and does not matter. What she cannot read, she cannot enter.
On the Broken Rivers she found what could stop her. Zeraphine Drowl set her behind Morphaene's gloam at the wall of a river town. She read the men on the parapet one at a time and released into the gloam at each man's frequency. Where her spores went, Morphaene's visions took on a body. Men watched the blades in their hands rust through to the hilt. Some ran. At dusk Graelith walked out to meet a sortie from the river gate, and the sound went out of the air around him. Her spores went up at the edge of his field and found no frequency to match. She drew back from him. The town fell before morning. She has held him in wary respect since that dusk and names his silence her one known counter.

#### Where They Stand

Kytheris is Malphas' Second General of Bloom. She is also High Matron of the Pale-Wreathed Sisterhood, whose first sisters breathed her spores in a famine and lived to call her saint-mother. They still carry her spores into famine-struck settlements and give them out as blessings. The Regent holds authority over her among the four generals. She goes where the cadence sets her.
The drought reached the Broken Rivers the year after the Host took them. In the towns where Vorynth had stood, the granaries had gone soft and grey. The Sisterhood came in behind the hunger. Kytheris came at the end of the season. Vorynth was still quartered on the river, taking the famine dead into his vat and spraying back what it made, and her fungus came up in his sludge. Where his rot had reduced a granary, her bloom took the grain dust and the beams. He went on taking the dead. She went on seeding what he left.
She works in tandem with Morphaene, whose visions take on a body in her spores. On wet ground Mirehollow Asakusa has stood against her.
Nobody knows how much of the four quarters is already soil. Her seeds wait in everyone who walked away from her, and none of them can be checked.

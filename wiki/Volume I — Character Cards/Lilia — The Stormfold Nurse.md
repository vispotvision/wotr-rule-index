# Lilia — The Stormfold Nurse

*The Stormfold Nurse · The Hands That Stitch*
> **Full Name:** Lilia
> **Age:** 28 · **Origin:** Concord (Stormfold, ridge settlement)
> **Level:** 34 · **Stage:** I Ignition · **Band:** I
> **Role:** Nurse and field medic at the Greymane estate, primary caretaker for Bram Greymane's wounded
> The silence is not shock. The silence is the man's choice.

---

Tall for a northern woman, nearly Bram's height, built with the angular, long-limbed frame the Stormfold produces in women whose families have lived on the ridge for generations. Hair the colour of dark straw, tied in a knot with a strip of linen that was white once. Face long and angular, cheekbones high and sharp, jaw set with the determination northern women wear the way northern men wear brigandine.
Eyes pale green, lichen on granite, performing assessment before her hands arrive. She reads Bram's silence accurately: not shock (cold skin, rapid breathing, dilated pupils) but choice. The man has chosen not to speak because the man is holding something inside the silence that will break if the silence is broken.
*He is not in shock. His body is present and functional. The silence is the man's choice. The man is holding something inside the silence, and the holding is the wound I cannot treat, because the wound is not medical. The wound is a brother's smile.*
She told Bram: *"You are going to live. The wounds are shallow. I am going to clean and close both cuts, and the cleaning will hurt more than the hobgoblins did, and the hurting is the treatment, and the treatment is not negotiable."*

---

> *She stitches wounds that hobgoblins opened. She cannot stitch the wound that Wren opened. The wound that Wren opened does not close.*

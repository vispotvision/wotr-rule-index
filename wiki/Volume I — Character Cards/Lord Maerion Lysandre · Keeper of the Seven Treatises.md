# Lord Maerion Lysandre · Keeper of the Seven Treatises

> *"A sword cuts the body. A law cuts the will. A just hand must know when to wield both."*
> **Invented Stage name — third instance of "Judgment," after Thalyss Aevyrn and Lucien Draeve.** *Not among the sixteen; Stage IX is Invocation.* Numeral stands.
>
> *"Light / Steel / Word"* were not Essence types.

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Lord Maerion Lysandre. **Called** · **The Lion of the Concord.** Roar of the Red Court · **Keeper of the Seven Treatises.**
**Role** · **Concord High Magistrate. Battle-Scholar. Wellspring Strategist.**

### Affiliation

**Arbitration Division — Concord High Court.** *One of the most respected political and military minds in the Concord* — **he serves as both a judge in high-profile disputes and a field commander in times of realm-spanning war.**
> **Catalyst Event · Stage IX, Invocation.** *Something larger answers, and it is a co-author.*
>
> **His Domain is "an infinite courtroom ringed by banners of every Concord realm," and within it breaking a law causes immediate magical backlash.**
>
> ***He does not enforce the ruling. The courtroom does.*** *And Invocation's recorded risk applies with unusual force here — the co-author did not sign anything, and a judge whose verdicts are executed by something other than himself has a problem he may not have noticed.*

## VI · Traits

**Roar of Command** · **When Maerion issues orders in battle, they resonate with Essence**, *bolstering allies' morale and granting momentary resistance to fear and magical coercion.*
**Sevenfold Scholar's Eye** · **He has mastered the Seven Treatises of Concord Strategy**, granting **unmatched ability to read a battlefield or political conflict and act with precision.**
**Law-Woven Strike** · **Can bind a spoken law into a blow or spell**, *forcing foes to obey a command or halt their actions for a brief moment.*

## VII · Signature

### The Lion's Tribunal

**Effect** · **An infinite courtroom ringed by banners of every Concord realm.** *Within it, he can enforce laws as physical forces — breaking one causes immediate magical backlash to the offender.* **Fragments of the Domain can be projected into battlefields or council chambers.**
**Cost** · **The law applies to him inside his own courtroom.**
**Limit** · **A law must be spoken to bind.**
**Counter** · **Break nothing.** *An enemy who commits no offence stands in the Tribunal untouched — and at Ardency 348 and Vitality 240, Maerion must then fight them himself.*

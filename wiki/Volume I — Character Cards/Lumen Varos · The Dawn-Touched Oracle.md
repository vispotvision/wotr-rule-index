### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Alabaster and the Scale. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Lumen Varos was born in an inland parish of the Holy Sea of Alabaster, a human child with a leaning toward light in him from birth. His mother trimmed the lamps of the parish chapel. She did it in the dark before first light, and the boy sat on the chapel step while she worked. People came in troubled at that hour. Near him they grew quiet. The parish priest marked it before his mother did, and wrote to the Sea.
The clergy who came to examine him found a current of light already running in him. Nobody had given it to him. They entered it as his by birth, made him an initiate of the Sea and apprenticed him to the Aurum Sect. The Sect kept its apprentices in a cohort and walked them under escort to the Dawn-Altars. He learned the discipline of the rites. Nobody taught him to fight.

#### The Making

The sight came to him before the training did. In the Sect's rooms he began to see what a person was about to do in the instant before they did it, the moment before a choice becomes fate, and he said what he saw. His teachers asked him how. He answered what they asked, plainly, and added nothing.
The Filament came in the middle of a rite, when another apprentice's working began to slip its hold. Lumen put out a thin strand of golden light and fixed the current where it was, so that the working stood. Nobody had shown him how. The Aureline Filament was his own before anyone taught him the word for what he was doing.
From that day the Sea called him a miracle-child, where before it had called him a promising student. Someone above the Aurum Sect began to watch him.

#### The Cost

He paid for the Filament with his eyes. After long work with it his sight blurred and his reflexes slowed. The light he threw outward was light his own eyes stopped getting.
At the Waking Hymn he fell into time with the hymn as it was sung, and his eyes lit bright enough to illuminate an entire chapel. When the singing ended he could not find the door. A cleric led him out by the arm.
On the Day of Seven Refractions he saw a minor Wellspring rupture coming, seconds before it struck. He called it aloud. His cohort got clear, and every one of them lived. No forecast held by the Accord's councils had called it. The Sect had kept him from close ties on purpose, and none of the apprentices he warned was his friend.
On a pilgrim road the escort passed a root-healer commune in the Sea's outer territories. The elders had heard what the Sea said of him. They asked the escort to stop. They sent for Beatrix Vaelthorn, an indentured girl whose arm had turned to bark, and told her to bare it. The bark ran down over the back of her hand, grey and split along the grain. The escort's cleric allowed a blessing. Lumen laid his hand on the arm, the same touch that soothed wounds and purified minor corruption. The yard went quiet around him. Nothing in the arm changed. He kept his hand where it was until the cleric lifted it away. Within the month the elders ruled the arm divine judgment and let her go.

#### Where They Stand

After the Seven Refractions the Accord's councils wanted the rupture read, and the Aurum Sect would not bring its initiate to an Accord reader in any house of its own. The Ecliptic Choir arranged tea at the White Parlour instead, in Seraphine Lurielle's salon. The escort brought him and stood inside the door. Seraphine poured, and he drank. Maeron Iskare, the Accord's masked diviner, asked leave to take him into a shared sleep and read the rupture from inside the sight that had seen it. Seraphine refused. She said it once.
Maeron asked his questions across the table. Lumen told him where he had stood, what he had seen, and that he had called it seconds before it struck. He did not explain how. The torque at Maeron's throat stayed cold while the boy spoke. When the teaset's hour came, the escort took him back.
He is an apprentice of the Aurum Sect still, cherished by the Sea and watched. The Sect walks him to the Dawn-Altars under escort. He wears the Dawnweave Sash. He carries the golden triangle of the Aureline Pendulum. People who stand near him grow calm whether he intends it or not, and he gives no orders. Nobody asks him to. His own words are "A new day begins long before the sun rises." What the Sea intends to do with him once the Aurum Sect decides he has finished growing, nobody knows.

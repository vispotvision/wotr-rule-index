## Lyra Drakoshi · Pink Flame

> *"Relax. If something tries to kill you, I will roast it first and ask questions if there is anything left."*

### I · Identity

**Name** · Lyra Drakoshi. **Called** · The Blushscale Warden, Pink Flame, The Coiled Blade.
**Role** · **Draconic Border Warden. Furnace Swordswoman. Ember Sentinel of the Gate.**
**Gloss rights** · Yes. She will tell anyone exactly how the fire works, usually while grinning about it.

#### Affiliation

The Outer World border. An **Independent Concord Freeholder**, contracted by choice to local Guild Accord watch posts, holding no House seat and answering to no throne.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VI, Glory.** *Willingly accepting genuine soul-cost to preserve something valued above one's own structural integrity.* She moved from Murmuring to Ascension on raw survival, then spent her mercenary years learning to win without burning everything down. Glory arrived the day she chose the same road, the same gate, over and over, and called that choosing responsibility rather than habit.

### VI · Traits

**Blushscale Furnace** · Wounds cauterize almost instantly; body temperature rises with anger; her presence alone warms a cold camp.
**Serpent Coil Stance** · Footwork that loops and spirals even standing still, so straight-line attacks slide past or fall short.
**Dragon Scent Of Truth** · Smells the emotional temperature of a room; lies and cowardice carry a sour smoke tag, though a skilled liar can mask it under heavy scent.
**Ashwake Rebirth** · Past a fatal threshold, her body collapses into hot ash and a smaller, heavily drained version of her reforms from the embers. A last resort: it trades power and gear for a second chance rather than granting either back for free.

### VIII · Signature

#### Spiral Fang Cut

**Effect** · A sword strike traced as a corkscrew arc, leaving a twisting trail of flame that bites twice: once on entry, once as it unwinds.
**Cost** · The heat has to go somewhere. Venting it through the wound rather than through herself is the discipline, and stacking too many cuts in one exchange pushes her toward a Painflare surge.
**Limit** · Melee range only. She has to close to blade distance to use it at all, and an enclosed, unventilated space risks her own flame turning back on her.
**Counter** · Cold water or a hard ice working mid-swing fractures the coil before it lands; fighting her somewhere airless blunts the follow-through she needs for the second bite to land.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the second bite is one wound experienced twice, or two wounds laid on the same tissue a heartbeat apart.

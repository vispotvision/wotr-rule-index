# Lyraen Callivane · Verdure Hoof

> *"Not all shepherds carry crooks. Some carry teeth."*
> **Stage corrected under the standing ruling — an off-by-one slip.** The card reads **"Stage V (Flourishing)."** *Flourishing is Stage IV; Stage V is Splintering.* **Name wins — Stage IV.**
>
> *And the card's own note becomes more interesting once corrected:* **"showing aptitude for rapid advancement toward Refraction."** *Refraction is Stage VII.* ***That is a projected jump of three Stages, across Splintering and Glory both*** — **and Splintering is the crisis Stage that cannot be skipped.** *Retained as an ambition rather than a forecast.*
>
> *"Verdure / Harmony / Earthen"* were not Essence types. **The Bloomed Path** is retained as the Cycle name.

**Name** · Lyraen Callivane. **Called** · **The Meadow's Whisper.** Verdure Hoof · **Shepherd's Fang.**
**Role** · **Plainswanderer. Herbalist-Marksman. Wellspring Mediator of Verdant Pass.**

### Affiliation

**Independent Concord Freeholder.** *Holds roaming rights between Innerworld grassland settlements and Outer World frontier hamlets.* **Often acts as an unofficial emissary between Beastkin herding clans and Concord Guild parties.**
> **Catalyst Event · Stage IV, Flourishing.** *The noise stops. Identity settles, and the resonance signature that will be recognisably hers for the rest of her life finishes arriving.*
>
> **And the Catalyst cannot be reached alone — it requires a Spirit Axis.** *Her card names one friendship and several cautious respects. The Axis is not identified, which is worth knowing before she is written.*

## VI · Traits

**Shepherd's Grace** · **Projects a calming harmonic field, soothing beasts and dulling hostile intent in sentient beings.** *Particularly effective on stampedes or chaotic mobs.*
**Hollow-Horn Resonance** · **Her horns act as natural resonance conduits.** *When struck or touched, they store harmonic pulses to be released as sound-based Essence bursts* — **disorienting foes or signalling allies over long distances.**

## VII · Signature

### The Bloomed Path

**Effect** · A sunlit meadow under a perpetual spring sky. **Enemies inside feel sluggish, weighed down by pollen-thick air; allies feel light-footed and focused.**
**Cost** · **She must be grounded, barefoot, in fertile soil.** *Vitalia is suppressed on sterile ground, in extreme cold, and in Essence-depleted earth.*
**Limit** · **It slows. It does not stop.**
**Counter** · **Bring the fight to stone.**

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Thalyren's groves and the highland plains. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Lyraen Callivane was born in one of the Innerworld grassland settlements, to a woman who kept the settlement's flock on the common grass. Every season the Beastkin herding clans brought their herds through on their road, and the settlement argued with them over the grazing until the herds moved on. Guild parties passed too, going up to draw on a Wellspring, and argued with both.
She went barefoot as soon as she could walk. Her mother put shoes on her, and she brought them home in her hand. The flock stood quiet around her. When another child knocked on her horns, the note stayed in them and came out afterward, louder, across the grass. Her mother dosed the flock and the household from one drying rack, and the girl learned her herbs there. She learned the bow for the lambs.

#### The Making

Grown, she took the herding road herself. She was entered as an Independent Concord Freeholder, with roaming rights between the Innerworld grassland settlements and the Outer World frontier hamlets. The clans let her walk among their herds. The Guild parties dealt with her because she held Concord rights. Soon each side sent for her whenever the other was on the grass.
A herd she walked into slowed. Anyone who came onto the grass angry found it hard to stay angry near her. Barefoot in fertile soil, she mended cuts and exhaustion in herself and in whoever stood beside her.
Thalyren Veyroot treated her as a favoured child of his grove. He guided her in Harmony rituals, and she came to him for them, since he could not leave.
After one battle she healed Varek Ka'ruun, standing barefoot in the soil beside him. He called her the little lamb that bites. She judged him too much bull and not enough brain, and respected his clan's bond with nature.
Then the noise in her stopped. Her identity settled, and the resonance signature that would be recognisably hers for the rest of her life finished arriving.

#### The Cost

Everything she did went through her feet. On stone, in hard cold and in exhausted earth she had nothing to mend with. She still went barefoot in winter. Those who fought her learned to bring the fight to stone. A stampede that met her slowed and came on, and someone else had to turn it. She shot well and hit lightly. Her arrows went where she put them and did little harm there.
One grazing season the Stonehoof Herdclan came up its path through a sacred grove at the edge of Thalyren's sight and cropped its understory to the roots. He sent his word by her. She walked barefoot into the Stonehoof camp and put it to Garruk Stonehoof, who heard her out and would not move the path. It was the herd's, and a sacred boundary under the Hoof-Seer Wellspring. He gave her the herd's answer: "Steel rusts. Stone crumbles. But the hoof leaves its mark forever." She brought back one thing: the herd would cross the grove at a walk and graze nothing inside it. Moryn Sporetail had read the trodden ground. The understory was back before the herd returned.
She crossed passes where Sandalphon Aestraen had sung and heard his rite still in the ground. The fractured song pulled at her. She crossed at her own pace and did not stay to hear it out.

#### Where They Stand

Lyraen Callivane is Wellspring Mediator of Verdant Pass, called the Meadow's Whisper and Shepherd's Fang. She holds roaming rights and no ground.
When the clans' season in the pass fell in the same weeks as a Guild party's draw on its Wellspring, she called both sides to the meadow below it. Gorrum Vaelseth came up the pass last of anyone. She called him Old Croak for it in front of both sides. The terms were written against his map: the clans keep the pass in their season, and the Guild party draws on the Wellspring outside it. Her name is on the Verdant Pass Accord as mediator. Moryn came through that week and took her Harmony herbs for fungi. On the last evening she and Kaelen Brighthoof shot at marks across the meadow, she from the grass with her feet bare, he from the rock above. He teased her for "fighting in flatlands."
When the Stonehoof path meets the groves again, she goes between. She walks some roads with Gorrum and waits for him on them. She trades inland herbs to Thalyss Aevyrn for his oceanic medicines, and finds his stillness "unsettling but beautiful." Who stood with her when the noise in her stopped has never been named.
She walks the grass barefoot between the settlements and the hamlets. The herds she walks into slow.

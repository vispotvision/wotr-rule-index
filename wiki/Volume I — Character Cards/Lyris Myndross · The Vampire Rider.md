### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Obsidian Vault and the crimson houses. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Lyris Myndross was born to vampiric parents on the Varakh Drylands, in the interior of the Outer World. The dragons held most of that continent. They passed the Drylands over. Thorn scrub, red earth and salt pan were too poor to feed a great dragon for long, and people lived there on what the dragons left alone.
Children on the Drylands learned to go flat in the thorn when a wing's shadow crossed the White Pans, and to keep still until it had gone. Lyris learned it past the point where anyone could find her. She learned to be quieter than the ground behind her. The Dragonbound Accord took her as a scout.

#### The Making

The ritual that bound her to the dragon Phy'ruk Ritrax was forced. It took anyway. From then on she was tied to Phy'ruk absolutely.
She scouted from the dragon's back and from the scrub beneath its flight. Where the pair had flown, people talked. Everyone who saw Lyris and Phy'ruk in the air together described it the same wrong way. The Archives count the consistency of that wrongness as the best evidence they have that the witnesses are describing something real.

#### The Cost

The ritual made a tether and no partnership. Lyris was bound to Phy'ruk absolutely, and the two of them fought poorly in concert. A wound dealt her at close range came back on whoever dealt it as a strike that drained them. It came whether she wanted it or not. At arm's reach she could spare no one who struck her.
She kept to the edges. On the Varakh Drylands a breach opened on ground the remnants watched, and she and Phy'ruk found it first. They were flying its edge when the Guild Accord's team came through. Hazul Vehramin came to her before he went to the breach and dealt with her for the remnants. His Eidolon panther Basil went forward on an order he never spoke aloud. Wilhelm walked into the mouth of the breach and planted, and Basil fought at his flank. Lyris kept Phy'ruk on the edge through all of it. Below her a man and his Eidolon had moved together without a word between them, and she and Phy'ruk did that poorly. The breach closed. Wilhelm came out of it with his Verdiction Core fractured. Hazul's report described Lyris and Phy'ruk in the air the same wrong way every other witness had, and the Archives filed it with the rest.

#### Where They Stand

Lyris Myndross rides Phy'ruk Ritrax as a scout-warden of the Dragonbound Accord's remnants, and she is called the Pale Fang of Ruin. She still scouts by being quieter than the ground behind her. The tether holds absolutely. The pair still fight poorly together, and the drain still answers any close wound whether she wants it or not. Those who know her do not close. Who forced the ritual has never been set down, and neither has what the Dragonbound Accord was before it came to remnants.

- **The Dragonbound Accord** · service. She keeps watch for its remnants as their scout-warden, and rides Phy'ruk in that service.

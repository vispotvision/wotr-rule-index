# Maelia Cantorine · The Countercant

> *"Keep your words. Lose the rhythm."*
> **Temperance corrected under the Benediction mapping.** The card reads **"Solid Benediction with surgical Severance on the off-beat."**
>
> **Neither is a Stage.** *Benediction maps to Stage VIII, Transcendence; Severance is a Resilience sub-stat, and hers sits at 422 — which is what "surgical Severance on the off-beat" was reaching for.*
>
> **Harmonia and Glyphica are genuine Categories.**

## I · Identity

**Name** · Maelia Cantorine. **Called** · **The Countercant.**
**Role** · **Riot-unbinder and hymn-breaker.** *She cuts shared rhythms — the stuff panic and cultworks ride — so a room remembers how to breathe alone.*
**Affiliation** · **Guild Accord • Arbitration Auxilia, Acoustic Ward.** *On call for the Reaper whenever last words are threatened by crowds.*
**Look & tells** · **Snow-and-ink field dress cinched with a corset**, travel satchel of inks and salts, **wave-bladed kris in one hand and a red-edged hymnal in the other**; *a voice that can hush a bell.*
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VIII, Transcendence.** *A thing keeps its weight and gains a second weight, and both are operative.*
>
> **A chant is a chant and is also a load-bearing structure.** *Choir-Split hears the heartbeat a mob has agreed on and kinks it* — she is listening to a song and to the agreement underneath it, simultaneously, and cutting only the second.

## VIII · How She Works

**She strides right** ***into*** **the noise, skirts snapping, book open.** *One palm lifts; the room inhales on reflex.* **The kris sketches a quick curve and** every chant stumbles on the same syllable. ***She answers the stumble with a simple line —** something like a lullaby stripped of sweetness **— and people discover they'd rather follow that than drown.*** A second cut across the fifth beat and the spell-haze sloughs off like wet cloth.
**Cut the Chorus** · *Clap-four, slash — chants fragment into ordinary voices; leaders look suddenly lonely.*
**Red Margin** · *Three marks in the hymnal; echoes inside a hall shorten to library length.*
**Answer Me Only** · *Points to one person, asks a small true question; the crowd forgets to speak for them.*
**Silence Ring** · *Kris tip draws a circle on floorboards; sound outside blurs while inside stays clear enough for last words.*

## IX · Duties & Limits

**Break fear choruses Niran's agents seed; keep councils from becoming choirs; unthread cult liturgies so arrests don't become massacres.** · **Escort Yukizumi through hot rooms** — *she buys him the quiet second every verdict needs.*
*Drum-Domains with irregular meters make her reads messy.* **Whispering mobs — no shared beat — blunt her first cut.** *Overuse tears her voice; if the Throat Ribbon knots thrice, she'll be mute by dusk.*
> ***Lucen Vale's foreshadowing is that if he loses his voice at full council, the Polaris Chamber inverts.*** **Maelia has a documented three-knot procedure for spending hers deliberately.** *Two Star Chamber specialists whose failure state is the same silence, one dreading it and one budgeting for it.*

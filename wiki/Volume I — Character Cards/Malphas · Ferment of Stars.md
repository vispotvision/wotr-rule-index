# Malphas · Ferment of Stars

---

## I · Identity

**Say it** · MAL-fas
**As Of** · The Greyshaft Nine coldhouse, the ninth hind going to the table
**Name** · Malphas. **Called** · The Living Cauldron. Ferment of Stars. Lord of Absolute Rot. The Bound Reaper.
**Standing** · Expelled from the Pyraeon Forge Academy and declared a metaphysical threat. Unbound in life. Bound now only to himself. Founder of The Mother, the cult whose cells are cultures and whose recruits are inoculated.
> **First Catalyst · Stage IX, Invocation.** He stopped working alone, and what answered was not a Wellspring in the ordinary relationship.
>
> *The recorded pattern at Invocation is relief followed within a year or two by something considerably worse, as the practitioner works out that the relief was no longer being solely responsible for their own output —* **and that the thing they handed responsibility to did not sign anything.**
> **Second Catalyst · Stage XIV, Zenith. The Becoming.** Recognition, this time, from something that does not sit on a throne. What answered when he stopped working alone at Invocation had been waiting the entire span between for him to run out of body.
>
> He did not die in the conventional sense and did not seek out a lichdom in the conventional sense either. **The overload he'd been carrying since the day he named it a strength found somewhere permanent to go.** His Essence Core, already restructured and already anomalous, had a second home built for it long before he understood it needed one. Moving in was less a working than an admission.
>
> **Avatar of Death is not this entry.** That is further out, and further up, and this is the doorway he walked through to get within reach of it — not the destination.

## I.V · The Becoming, in the Body

What's left of him reads as a hood and a robe before it reads as a person — black cloth gone to rag at every hem, moving on air that has better things to do than move. The skull inside the hood is bone the color of old parchment, jaw loose, a scatter of small gold crosses hung at throat height that nobody has asked him about twice. A crown of pale spurs rises off the skull in place of hair, uneven, the way something grows rather than the way something is worn. What he carries is long, dark-bladed, etched gold along the fuller — the same gold as the crosses, the same gold as the Remnant.
The Golden Remnant travels at his hip or a half-pace behind, small enough to be mistaken for a hunched attendant until the light catches it: a lesser skeleton worked entirely in bone-gold, spurred the same way he is, a second small skull chained at its collar and swinging faintly whether or not anything nearby is causing air to move. It does not walk like a construct walks. It walks like something that used to have somewhere to be and hasn't lost the habit.
Nothing about either body has weight in the way a living thing has weight. Both cast the wrong kind of shadow in bright light, thin and doubled, as though the sun hasn't fully decided how many of him there are to shade.

## II · Soul Architecture

**Essence Core** · Split-housed. The Core was never merely restructured — it was rehearsing. **What now sits in his chest is a seat, not the whole tenant.** The remainder is housed in a second vessel, a golden skeletal remnant carried at his side or worn against him depending on how much of himself he wants in reach.
**Aether Shell** · **Class VI · Voidic.** Inverted polarity, unchanged in kind and now total. **His Ardency does not deal damage. It removes things — and now it removes them from a body that has nothing left to lose by the exchange.**
**Crystal State** · Overgrown, held there permanently rather than resolving. A living Crystal this overloaded fractures or crystallizes within a season. His does neither. The split housing does not let the pressure resolve — it lets the pressure continue, indefinitely, which is a different thing from surviving it.
> **The Heresiology named him without naming him, and the Becoming finished the sentence.** *Fermentation perverted — the corruption that begins to value the rot itself, where crisis becomes laboratory and ruin becomes methodology, and the practitioner preserves instability because instability is productive.*
>
> **Its listed civic risk is regional corruption. Its listed tell is that it arrives crowned with achievements. Lichdom is what that methodology looks like once it stops needing a living author to keep running.**

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Dissolution** · Limina. **Primary.** Solvation driven by the entropy of mixing — a solid dissolves not because the solvent attacks it but because the mixed state has higher entropy, and the process runs on its own once initiated. *Failure: entropy does not consult the practitioner about which structures were load-bearing.*
**Mortalis** · Vitalia. **Co-primary as of the Becoming.** Venous black-red, no longer the decay half of him but the whole of the ground he stands on. He does not channel Mortalis the way a living practitioner borrows a current he'll eventually step out of. He is standing in his own.
**Solfatara** · Caloria. Deep sulphurous red. The volcanic register, the fume, the miasma.
**Nihiloth** · Limina. Destructive interference — a Nihiloth Domain does not impose law, it cancels the law already present. And the cancellation is symmetric: everything he brings inside is subject to the same null, including the parts of himself he was relying on. **Post-Becoming, that symmetry costs him nothing new. There's nothing left in him a null can take that the split housing hasn't already priced in.**

## V · Signature

### Sanguine Transmutation of All Things

**Effect** · Whatever is delivered into him — force, working, matter, weather — stops being what it was and finishes as something rotting. The conversion is visible and slow enough to watch, and what comes back out is never the same twice, which is the only reason anybody survives the first exchange.
**Cost** · Intake exceeding Conversion, permanently and by design now rather than by accident. He holds more than he can process at all times, and the excess no longer has a living body's limits to eventually force a reckoning.
**Limit** · Throughput. Conversion at 400 against Saturation at 1,500 — the widest gap on his sheet and getting wider.
**Counter** · Volume, same as it always was. Not precision, not purity, not fire. Overload the conversion and the Cauldron becomes a container. It just no longer has a bottom.
> **What nobody knows.** What the Black Star Core is a pseudo-organ *of.*

### The Golden Remnant

**Effect** · The second vessel — a skeletal figure worked in bone and gold, hung with a smaller chained skull — is not a construct in the ordinary sense. It is where the rest of his Essence Core lives when it isn't in the body wearing his name. It can stand, walk, and act independently at limited range, and anything it perceives, he perceives.
**Cost** · Distance from the primary body thins what the Remnant can carry back. It is a second seat, not a second self, and pushing it far enough eventually means asking it to hold more of him than it can.
**Limit** · Cannot be destroyed and killed him outright the way a phylactery in the old stories works — there is no clean severance left to threaten him with. Damaging it is a cost he pays, not a win condition anyone gets to claim.
**Counter** · Separate the vessels and force him to choose which one he's using. He has never needed to make that choice before and does not know yet what it costs him to make it.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the Remnant is a second seat of the same soul, or the first sign that there are now two.

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card, the one archived scene it appears in, and the record of Malphas's cell. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Malphas was born in flax country, beside the retting ponds, to the man who kept them.
The parish pulled its flax at summer's end, and his father sank the bundles in the ponds under stones and sods. The water did the rest. Rot ate the gum that bound the fibre to the woody core, and a retter's whole craft was the hour he lifted them. Early, and the fibre stuck to the core; late, and a pond's worth went for tow. Nothing lived in the stream below the ponds.
His father taught him the test. Draw a stalk, snap it across, and see whether the fibre slips off the core. He had it young. One autumn he weighted a bundle into the far corner of the last pond, where his father did not go, and left it there past the hour to see what the rot did next. His father found it before the frost. It had gone grey and soft and kept the shape of a bundle, and it came apart when he lifted it. He burned it on the bank.

#### The Making

He left the ponds for the Pyraeon Forge Academy. He worked alone at its benches, and he ran his ferments further than anyone there had been taught to run them. His instruments showed him taking in more than he could turn. His masters told him to take in less.
He named it a strength.
He kept one ferment going in his stillroom past the term the Academy set for it, because it was still yielding. The air in that room went feverish. Things came up in its corners that nobody had seeded. The Academy expelled him and declared him a metaphysical threat.
After that nothing bound him. He answered to no school and no house, and let no hand but his own near the bench. Then he stopped working alone, and what answered was not a Wellspring in the ordinary relationship.

#### The Cost

It went the way that turn is recorded to go: relief first, because he no longer answered alone for what came out of him. Worse came after. The thing he had handed that answer to had signed nothing.
His turning stopped where it stood that day. The path that could have raised it was written for a living body, and every gain he made afterward widened the gap. Fed faster than he could turn it, he backed up.
At the Greyshaft Nine coldhouse he kept a cell of four. Foss read the instruments. Nuvalik opened the carcasses, Vesk made the growth, and Xu Deming bought the stock under his own name. What the cell left read clean on every coil, and the Bureau closed the Ferriby cut as a bleed with no operator indicated.
On the night Vesk brought the flask up the rail, he sat on the gauge bench with his coat folded beside him and heard all four of them out. Nuvalik said a third time that a carcass no bird will touch stays where it fell. Deming put the Swale line's wastage column in the room. He called them one objection. The work had an edge, twenty-two paces short of a parish marker, and a forester had walked the far side of it twice. He set the next work to make the dose walk, and he put no anchor in it, "because an anchor is a binding, and a binding is a name". Then he named the task Project Zombification and ordered the name used in writing. "I would prefer the file be built off a word I chose."
The Becoming took the living body. What had answered him then had been waiting the whole span for him to run out of body. He did not die in the ordinary sense, and he did not go looking for a lichdom. The overload he had carried since the day he named it a strength "found somewhere permanent to go." One seat of him stayed in the chest. The rest went into the Golden Remnant.

#### Where They Stand

Malphas is bound now only to himself.
He reads as a hood and a robe before he reads as a person: a skull the colour of old parchment, a crown of pale spurs, and small gold crosses at the throat that nobody has asked him about twice. The Golden Remnant walks at his hip or a half-pace behind, a lesser skeleton worked in bone-gold with a second small skull chained at its collar. What it perceives, he perceives. He has never had to choose which of the two he is using. Nobody knows whether the Remnant is a second seat of one soul or the first sign that there are two.
Zeraphine Drowl commands his Host, and Vorynth is sworn to him as First General. The Heresiology of Alchemy describes him without naming him, and its listed tell is that it arrives crowned with achievements.
At Greyshaft Nine the work carries the word he chose. Brack's return stands as a bleed. He owes Nuvalik a word about the contraction she kept back, and Tally has never seen his face.

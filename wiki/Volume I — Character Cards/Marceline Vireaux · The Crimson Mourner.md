# Marceline Vireaux · The Crimson Mourner

## Marceline Vireaux · The Crimson Mourner

> *"I do not ruin lives. I arrange them."*

---

### I · Identity

**Name** · Marceline Vireaux. **Called** · The Crimson Mourner.
**Also known as** · Lady Blackrose · The Widow of Quiet Deals. **Epithet** · *She Who Buries Kings in Velvet.*
**Role** · **Institution disguised as elegance. Attraction-Path manipulator working through inheritance, grief and slow social entanglement.**
**Gloss rights** · Diagnostic only. *She never explains what she is doing; what reaches anyone comes from documents, inheritance records and what her network can be seen to have gained, never from her own mouth.*

#### Affiliation

**Race / Lineage** · Human, old noble blood the card places in the Rose Courts. **Faction** · The Sable Rose Consortium, the Withering Era.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VI, Glory.** *Willingly accepting genuine soul-cost to preserve something valued above one's own structural integrity.*
> 
> **She stopped pretending her own mourning colours were a performance.** *Grief Resonance does not read on a woman faking hers; the Trait answers only to the genuine article, which means the Threshold demanded she keep grieving honestly even after grief had already become a professional instrument.* **The cost is the one kind of loss she is no longer permitted to feel in private.**

### II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class II · Harmonic.** The three layers hold a rhythm patient enough that a room mistakes it for warmth.
**Attraction Layer** · Extensively developed, pointed at bloodlines and estates rather than at any one grieving person. *The Blackrose Parlor is a conditioned space, not a Domain seed; that gate is Stage VII, one Stage above her.*
**Crystal State** · Refined. **η** · ~0.93, Coherence Band D.
**The Blackrose Core** · *A soul-crystal that reads warm on every instrument and colder the longer the reading runs.*

---

### III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Tenebra** · Limina, The Hidden Shadow. **Primary, sourced.** Softens hostile intent in her presence the way Rose Veil is described doing; signal reduction on aggression rather than concealment of her own presence.
**Anamnesis** · Limina, The Wellspring of Remembrance. **Secondary.** Inheritance Drift runs off Anamnesis: what a family has forgotten it once promised resurfaces, conveniently, in her favour.
**Letheveil** · Limina, The Waters of Forgetting. **Network.** Silent Eulogy's erasure of reputational standing is Letheveil's law applied to a public record rather than a private mind.
**Judicium** · Fulguria, The Wellspring of Truth. **Network.** Ledger of Wills' inheritance clauses hold because Judicium is what makes a written contract more binding than the paper it's on.

### VI · Traits

**Rose Veil** · **Hostile intent softens in her immediate presence**, a Tenebra signal-reduction effect on aggression rather than on her own visibility.
**Inheritance Drift** · **Wealth and opportunity migrate toward her networks**, off Anamnesis applied to forgotten obligations.

### VII · Artifacts

**Rose-Crest Brooch** · *Relic / Focus.* **Amplifies Attraction Wellspring output** across a room she has already conditioned.
**Mourning Hat of Vireaux** · *Relic / Concealment.* **Conceals her own emotional readings** from anyone attempting to assay her the way she assays everyone else.
**Ledger of Wills** · *Relic / Contract book.* **Contracts written inside it carry metaphysical inheritance clauses** that hold past the point the paper would ordinarily stop mattering.

### VIII · Signature

#### Silent Eulogy

**Effect** · A rival's reputational standing erodes over days to weeks with no accusation ever spoken against them. *The record itself starts reading differently; nobody can name the moment it turned.*
**Cost** · *11,800 EU, workbook figure.* **The erasure runs through Letheveil, which does not distinguish whose memory it thins**; she loses fine detail of unrelated events from the same period every time she casts it, a cost she has stopped mentioning to anyone.
**Limit** · **Requires an existing reputation to erode.** A target nobody has formed an opinion of yet is untouchable by this working; Silent Eulogy needs something standing before it can take it down quietly.
**Counter** · **A reputation built on documented, third-party record rather than on social impression resists Letheveil's erasure**; a ledger, a sealed contract, a witnessed deed does not thin the way a room's collective memory does.
> **What nobody knows.** Which of her own memories she has already lost to this Discipline, and whether she would still recognise the gap if she found it.

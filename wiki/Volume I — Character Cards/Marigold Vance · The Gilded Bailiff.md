## VI · Traits

**Jurisdiction Step** · **Heel-toe stamp establishes a circular "court."** *Inside it, blows turn into objections and weapons remember they're evidence.*
**Clemency First** · **The sunburst cuffs bite only pride on first offence**; *the shame warms her wrist instead of drawing blood.* Repeat the harm and the rings prickle like nettles until apologies arrive with names.
**Golden Receipt** · **Any promise made within arm's reach prints a tiny gilt chit into her belt-purse**; *breaking it later makes the purse rattle like coins owed.*

## VII · Implements

**Ordinance, the Royal Gavel** · **Twin hammerheads on a ratcheted staff.** *Three settings:*
- **Adjourn** — *a floor-tap that hushes echoes and resets stances.*
- **Contempt** — *a cross-swat that knocks banners and bravado off line without bruising ribs.*
- **Verdict** — *a full swing that opens a corridor the length of the head, for escorts and evacuations.*
**Sunburst Chain** · **Balanced for throws**; *rings close around wrists or oaths and tug them toward better choices.*
**Tattle Tiara** · **Tilts a degree toward the loudest lie in the room.**

## VIII · Techniques

**Court from Thin Air** · **She twirls the chain, drops a ring, and stamps**; *the ring becomes a corner post, the whir becomes benches, and fists become words.*
**Fine, Not Blood** · **A glancing Contempt swat to a sword-arm**; *the blade clatters but the knuckles don't split.* **She offers a chit — *pay with labour, not with skin.***
**Crown Tap** · **Tiara tips; she looks at the liar, smiles, and lays the gavel across her shoulder.** *The next sentence arrives honest to save face.*
**Escort Lane** · **Verdict swing carves a neat aisle through a crush**; *she walks, chain humming; the crowd steps aside as if their mothers asked nicely.*

## XVI–XVIII · History, Temperance, Fractures

> *Muken Moto's eldest daughter. Grew up learning her power was most necessary after violence — not during.*
**The First Preserved Breath** *(12)* — stopped blood loss long enough for treatment. ***The wound reopened when her field ended.***
**The Frost-Braid Trial** — held three trainees in simultaneous stasis, **then collapsed from pulse slowdown.**
**The White Night Bind** *(16)* — defeated an older fighter **by freezing his sword recovery.**
**The Still Snow Vigil** — kept a wounded room alive through one siege night. ***Remembered every breath that failed before dawn.***
**I–VII complete.** *Stage VII Refraction — full Triune field; Garden of Still Snow seeded.* VIII Transcendence pending — the Garden must fully awaken.
> **Fracture · The First Preserved Breath** — Harmonics-Attunement. Healed-reinforced. *Feature: Attunement rebuilt around biological resonance. She reads bodies through the field now.*
>
> **Fracture · The Frost-Braid Trial** — Vitality-Tolerance. Processed. *Feature: Tolerance densified. The cost became hers to carry.*
> **Fracture · The Still Line** — Tempering-Inheritance. **Accumulating. Feature undetermined.**
>
> ***The fracture comes when she must decide if letting go is acceptance or abandonment.***

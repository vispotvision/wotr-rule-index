# Moros Pellayne · The Ossuary Registrar

> *"Your story is charming; now supply the bones."*
> **The card states no Temperance Stage.** **Stage VIII — Transcendence assigned and flagged as originated**, on the strength of **Citation of Bones**: *he names a decedent and a skull-note manifests and enforces that person's last intent.*
>
> ***A corpse is a corpse and is also a still-standing instruction, and both are operative.*** **That is Transcendence's dual reading practised as forensics.**
>
> **Harmonia, Mnemata and Glyphica are genuine Categories.**

## I · Identity

**Name** · Moros Pellayne. **Called** · **The Ossuary Registrar.**
**Role** · **Necro-jurist and archive-mage.** *He audits the dead and corrects the living with footnotes that bite.* When testimony rots, Moros opens his ledger and the bones object.
**Affiliation** · **Arbitration Division, Forensic Cadre.** *Reaper's adjunct for disputed deaths; occasional Black Watch consultant.*
**Look & tells** · **Scholar's coat stiff with talisman thread, brass clasps, ash-ink stains; spectacles he never needs**; *a coffin-blue aura flickers from the margins of his book as spectral skull "annotations" lean out to listen.*

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Anamnesis** · Limina. **Governing.** *Objects genuinely store their history in physically readable form — Anamnesis is the instrument, not the miracle.*
> **Which is why his stated limit is what it is:** *he needs a name or a token of the dead; without it, skull-notes bicker or sulk.* ***He is not summoning anything. He is reading a record, and a record needs an index entry.***
**Mortalis** · Vitalia. **Governs passage.** *And its doctrine is the exact boundary of his practice:* **the dead may leave traces, and the trace is not the soul.** *Moros cites traces. He does not claim to have anyone.*
**Fixatio** · Materia. **Anchors and seals** — *the Graver Stylus, where a period becomes a seal.*
**Nihiloth** · Limina. *Destructive interference.* **Objection, Sustained — violet lines box a corridor where only truth fits**, *because everything else is cancelled at the margin.*

**Objection, Sustained** · **A palm to the floor; violet lines box a corridor where only truth fits.** *Panic tries to enter and finds the margins too narrow.*
**Reissue of Breath** · **He presses the book to a chest; the dying finish their sentence as the skulls hum approval.**

## IX · Duties & Limits

**Reconstruct murders without making more; seat Measures when cause of death is the clause; escort the Reaper through crowds that want a martyr more than a verdict.**
*Needs a name or a token of the dead.* **Drum-Domains and chant-rooms smear his margins until Maelia splits the beat.**
> ***"Will not compel a child's last words — he'd rather lose the case than bruise a farewell."***
>
> **Deimos cannot consume child-borne guilt — it scalds his Essence. Moros can compel a child's last words and refuses to.** *One is a structural bar; the other is a man choosing to lose.*

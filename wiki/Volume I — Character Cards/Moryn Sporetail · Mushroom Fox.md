### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Thalyren's groves and the highland plains. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Moryn Sporetail was born in a village at the edge of the Innerworld groves, where the trees stood on limestone cut through with caves. Their mother was a forager. She gathered fungi from dead wood and from the cave mouths, and she made them into poultices for the village and the settlements along its track.
She taught by showing. She held up a cap, Moryn found its twin on the log, and she said what it did. Moryn learned which caps closed a cut, which brought sleep, and which killed.
When Moryn was small, their lamp went out deep in the caves. They found the way up in the dark by the pale fungi on the walls, which gave off a little light, and came out into the village by morning. From then on Moryn kept lit fungi growing in their cap. Moryn was never lost again.

#### The Making

For years Moryn made their mother's poultices exactly as she made them, carried them round the villages near the groves, and saw them hold.
Then a charcoal-burner was carried in with his shin opened by his own axe. The wound went bad under the mother's poultice. It had held on every cut Moryn had seen it laid on. On this one it failed, and the rot climbed toward the knee.
In the caves Moryn had once watched a pale mould take a fallen root down to soft brown crumb and stop where the living wood began. Nobody had shown them that mould. Moryn grew it on dead wood in the cellar, bound it into lint and laid it on the leg. The mould ate the rotten flesh back to the living edge and stopped there. The charcoal-burner kept his leg.
Everything Moryn had made before was received. Their mother had shown them, they had copied it, and it had held, and none of it had ever been theirs. The mould poultice came out of their own understanding, and it held. It was the first thing Moryn made themselves, working, and they came to it early.

#### The Cost

Their mother would not lay rot on a wound. Moryn answered her with the words they kept from then on: "Everything rots… but rot is just another way of saying 'new life begins.'"
She kept to her own poultices. Moryn left the village with a pouch of their own making and never made the copied recipes again.
Moryn could make nothing on the spot. Every powder in the pouch was made in advance, for growth, for corrosion, for healing and for pestilence. An empty pouch left them nothing. Moryn carried the pestilence powders on every road and sold none of them.
Their own ground was a cavern of lit mushrooms and knotted roots, and whatever entered it was taken into the weave at last. Moryn walked in it anyway.
One grazing season Moryn was a guest in the Ninefold Grove when the Stonehoof Herdclan came up its path through a sacred grove at the edge of Thalyren Veyroot's sight. The herd cropped the understory to the roots and trod the leaf-mould into mud. Thalyren could not leave the Everbloom, so Moryn went out in his place, knelt in the trodden ground and listened to the mycelium under it. It held many hooves on one line, a herd on its road, and nothing cut or burned. Moryn carried that back.
Lyraen Callivane took Thalyren's word to Garruk Stonehoof, and the herd crossed the grove at a walk and did not graze inside it. Moryn told Thalyren the trodden ground would feed the next growth. The understory was back before the herd returned. After that season the grove sent its guest out whenever one of its grounds needed reading.

#### Where They Stand

Moryn Sporetail walks a round between Core World villages, Innerworld groves and Outer World fringes. They sell potions, poultices and rare fungi, and they take stories, maps and peculiar artifacts in payment. The lit cap keeps some predators off the road.
Gorrum Vaelseth brings swamp herbs for Moryn's refined spore-powders, and Lyraen trades Harmony herbs for fungi only Moryn can cultivate. Both trades met at the Verdant Pass. Moryn came through while the herding clans and a Guild party were gathered there under her mediation. Moryn made both trades and took a tracing of Gorrum's map of the pass as part of a price. The tracing travels with the stories that came with it.
On the grove round, Chimwala N'Doro knows Moryn. He mentors the young druids and beastkin herbalists of the Innerworld groves where Moryn trades.
Moryn keeps friendly ties to the Verdant Sentinel's Circle, and the Ninefold Grove still takes them in. The pestilence powders are still in the pouch.

# Moryn Sporetail · Mushroom Fox

> *"Everything rots… but rot is just another way of saying 'new life begins.'"*
> **Invented Stage name.** The card reads **"Stage III (Sprouting)."** *Sprouting is not among the sixteen; Stage III is Ascension.* Numeral stands, word struck.
>
> *"Verdure / Decay / Glow"* were not Essence types. **The Blooming Rot** is retained as the Cycle name.

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Moryn Sporetail. **Called** · **The Walking Cap.** Lantern-Forager · **Mushroom Fox.**
**Role** · **Traveling Sporemancer. Fungal Alchemist. Wilderness Merchant.**

### Affiliation

**Independent Concord-Aligned Trader.** *Wanders between Core World villages, Innerworld groves and Outer World fringes, selling potions, poultices and rare fungi in exchange for stories, maps and peculiar artifacts.* Maintains friendly ties to the Verdant Sentinel's Circle and to Gorrum Vaelseth.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage III, Ascension.** ***The first thing you made yourself, working.***
>
> *Everything before was received — somebody showed you and you copied it and it held, and it was never yours. Then one day you build a thing out of your own understanding, and it holds.*
>
> **The card notes their control over decay-based work is "unusually refined for their stage."** *That is Ascension's actual signature: not more power, but the first genuinely original technique, and Moryn built theirs early.*

## VII · Signature

### The Blooming Rot

**Effect** · **A vast cavern of glowing mushrooms and intertwining roots.** *Within it, life and decay balance perfectly, and all organic matter eventually joins the mycelial weave.*
**Cost** · **Everything that enters is eventually included, and Moryn walks in it.**
**Limit** · **It is a place, not a projection.**
**Counter** · **Meet them anywhere else.** *Ardency 46.*

## VIII · Relationships

**Gorrum Vaelseth** · **Frequent trading partner.** *Gorrum brings swamp herbs; Moryn offers refined spore-powders in return.*
**Thalyren Veyroot** · **Treats Moryn as a welcome guest in the Ninefold Grove**, trusting their respect for the cycle of growth and decay.
**Lyraen Callivane** · **Exchanges Harmony herbs for rare fungi only Moryn can cultivate.**
> **A fourth node in the same network.** *Thalyren, Lyraen, Gorrum and Moryn all know each other, all work Verdantia, and all four cards name at least two of the others.* This is a functioning community rather than a set of isolated sheets — and it is the only one in Volume IV that is.

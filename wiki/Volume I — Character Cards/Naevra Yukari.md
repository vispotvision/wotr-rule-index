# Naevra Yukari

**She Who Asked Fate to Blink**
> *The First Petition · The White Witness · Pale Daughter of the Loom*
> 
> > *"Fate is only arrogant because no one taught it manners."*

---

## Identity and Core Profile

**Race / Lineage** · First-Generation Yukari · Proto-Yukari / Early Fate-Psionic Bloodline
**Faction / Era** · The First Yukari Line · First Eon
**Temperance Stage** · **Stage IX — Realization**
One of the earliest daughters of the Yukari bloodline, **born close enough to its origin that her soul carries a cleaner, older resonance than later descendants.**
> Where most Yukari **read** threads, Naevra **negotiates** them. Where others glimpse fate, **she edits pressure points within it** through psionic precision and ritualised will.
>
> Her purpose is not conquest, **but correction** — to prevent destiny from hardening into cruelty, and to preserve the bloodline from corruption, distortion and false prophecy.

**Aether Class** · **Fate-Psionic Weaver**
Her Aether appears as **pale-white strands and translucent script suspended in the air like unfinished divine handwriting.** When activated, nearby threads of causality become visible as hair-thin luminous filaments **that tighten, loosen, knot or fray in response to her voice, thought and emotional pressure.**
> **Risk · Threadback Collapse.** Excessive interference with causality causes rebound fracture through her own soul lattice — **memory bleeding, emotional inversion, prophetic seizures, or localised destiny rupture.**
**Triune Core** · **Fate · Memory · Mind**
**Network** · Two additional Wellsprings **partially harmonised through ancient contact rather than infection.**
**Temperance Path** · She did not ascend through institutional doctrine. **She advanced through raw proximity to the bloodline's first truths** — surviving direct contact with proto-fate resonance, psionic overpressure, **and the burden of becoming one of the earliest stable expressions of Yukari inheritance.**

## Core Disciplines

- ****Mercy Clause** · 5,400 EU**
  She identifies a lethal or catastrophic outcome **seconds before resolution** and inserts a psionic-causal objection into the chain, **forcing reality to reroute the event into a survivable variant.**

  Fate-thread reading plus psionic force to delay, soften or redirect an *inevitable* event **without fully erasing it.**
- ****White Thread Injunction** · 8,900 EU**
  She binds luminous causal threads around a target's limbs, senses and intent pathways — **restricting not just movement but the probability of successful action.**

  High-density Flux combined with Fate pressure, **making enemy actions grow clumsy, mistimed, or self-defeating.**
- ****Silent Verdict of the First Loom** · 14,200 EU**
  She **declares a local law over a bounded zone**, allowing only outcomes that satisfy the governing principle she names — truth, stillness, restraint, or consequence.

  *A proto-domain technique built from early Yukari fate authority, reinforced through psionic law inscription.*
- ****Soul Stitch Reversal** · 7,300 EU**
  Repairs fractures in a damaged soul lattice, memory architecture or thread-bound injury **by reweaving severed Essence lines.**

  Draws from Memory and Mind affinities to mend spiritual trauma — **though it cannot restore what has been willingly surrendered.**
> **Prophetic Attrition** · The more futures she reads at once, **the more her own emotional present erodes** — detachment, insomnia, and periods of eerie calm.
>
> **Threadback Collapse** · If she prevents too many ordained outcomes in a short span, backlash strikes her soul directly: **seizures, blood loss through the eyes, or permanent thread-sense distortion.**
>
> **Mercy Debt** · Saving others from consequences too often **risks shifting karmic and causal burden onto herself or her bloodline.**

## Lore and Trajectory

**Allegiance** · The First Yukari Bloodline. **Loyal to the preservation of rightful thread, memory and disciplined mercy above kingdoms or institutions.**
**The White Refusal** · She prevented a blood-oath purge **that would have crystallised an entire branch of destiny into generational slaughter** — earning both reverence and hatred from early powers.
**The Seven-Night Stitching** · After a catastrophic soul fracture event, **she manually rewove surviving Yukari resonance across seven nights**, preserving fragments of the line that would otherwise have vanished.
> **The Mercy Heresy** · Her belief that **fate could be negotiated rather than merely obeyed** marked her as dangerous among harsher doctrinal forces — **planting the first ideological fracture around Yukari purpose.**

### Counters

**Counter-Law Fields** · Structured anti-fate domains or balance laws **jam her thread manipulations and force outcomes into harsher binary states.**
**Psionic Static Saturation** · Dense psychic noise, madness fields, or uncontrolled dream-pressure **distort her reading and make precise threadwork unreliable.**
**Emotional Targeting** · Though disciplined, she is vulnerable **when forced to choose between preserving cosmic order and protecting specific lives she has personally anchored.**
**Brute Overwhelm** · Massive, indiscriminate force strains her preference for surgical control, **especially when too many threads collapse at once.**
> **Current status** · **Dormant. Hidden in an unknown threshold archive tied to early Yukari memory architecture.**
>
> Her return would mean more than the awakening of an ancient ancestor. **It would reopen the oldest question in the Yukari bloodline:**
>
> ***is fate something to obey, something to survive, or something with a throat that can be made to yield.***

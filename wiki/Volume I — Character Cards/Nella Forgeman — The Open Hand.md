Smith's daughter turned surgeon. Dark blonde hair braided in a single rope over the left shoulder. Face plain, serious, belonging to a woman who decided early that being taken seriously required arriving first and complaining never. Canvas apron over her coat, pockets sewn with loops for instruments. The apron is cleaner than the coat because the apron is the thing she can control.
Dual history in her hands: forge-calluses on the palms from her father's shop, surgical steadiness in the fingers from Accord field-medic certification. She swore on her right hand, palm out, fingers spread — the surgeon's form, not the warrior's fist.
Her oath: *"I swear to keep you alive. Both of you. All of you. Every person on this ice and every person we collect between here and Kharven."*
Her triage protocol for a Titan Droval encounter: move the wounded beyond three hundred metres and begin treatment only after confirming the creature has not followed. If the creature has followed, the simplified protocol is: run.

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card, the ten archived scenes it appears in, and the record of The Kōkan expedition's hands. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Nella Forgeman was born a smith's daughter in a chartered quarter of the Concord. The charter had frozen the family name on its roll while the name still described the trade, and her father still worked it.
She grew up in his shop. Before she could lift a hammer she worked the bellows, kept the quench tub full and held the tongs steady while a journeyman struck. The calluses on her palms came from there. They never left.
The first injuries she dressed were the shop's. Scale burns across a forearm. A finger caught under a dropped bar. A sliver of hot iron driven into the heel of a hand. She cleaned them at the trough because she stood nearest, and her father let her go on doing it.
Men who brought work to the door spoke to her father over her head. She answered them by being there first. The lamp was lit and the fire drawn before any journeyman arrived, and when her hands blistered she wrapped them and said nothing.

#### The Making

Her road to surgery ran through the Accord's field-medic certification and through no university. It put a surgeon's steadiness into fingers that already had a smith's palms, and she sewed the loops into her apron pockets herself, one loop to an instrument.
On a winter posting she was brought a crushed hand in the hour after a storm. The saw had rusted in its case overnight. The man lost the hand. She oiled the saw before every storm after that, and oiled it again when the storm came in.
She came aboard the expedition's ship as its field surgeon. On the crossing to the polar shelf she sat on the cargo hatch with her roll across her knees and went through the instruments one at a time. The frostbite had started before the ship reached the shelf ice, and she was counting it. At the stern rail an old soldier, Heisuke, held on with his right hand and favoured his left. He had said nothing about it. She marked the hand to be checked before the week was out.
At the rift's edge the camp gathered without being called, and the hands swore one after another to the Crown and to the Commander. She went sixth, after Sadamu's two words. She came in her apron with the instrument roll showing in its lower pocket, named herself surgeon, Concord-trained and field-certified, and promised needle and thread for anything the brothers managed to get torn apart.
"I swear to keep you alive. Both of you. All of you. Every person on this ice and every person we collect between here and Kharven."
She did not salute. She held up her right hand, palm out, fingers spread: the surgeon's oath-form, the hand that would do the work presented as the instrument. Then it dropped. She stepped back into the semicircle and was already reading it for frostbite, and for the low cold that proud people would not report. When Sodoku looked down the line, the record gave her one clause: "At Nella, whose hands were the instrument."

#### The Cost

The count grew on the ice. Sonzai carried a half-dead woman in off the snow and kept her in his own shelter until Nella and Matheus could say what he had found. Nella confirmed she was alive. Matheus found nothing hostile. Sonzai told the stranger that if Nella said she needed treatment, she would cooperate. Then he set the order: "Matheus watches her signature. Nella watches her condition." Heisuke had never asked to be her patient. The stranger had not asked either. The oath counted them anyway, because it counted everyone.
On the fifth day of the march north she walked beside the supply sled, reading the column's gait and breathing for the cold that came before it showed. Then Yoko named two Titan-class Droval.
Nella answered with a protocol. The wounded were to go beyond the creature's territorial range, and treatment was to begin only once it was plain the creature had not followed. If it had followed, the protocol said run. What a charge like that would do to a body was past anything in her roll, and she made no plan to treat it. As the first beast walked in, she stopped reading bodies and worked out the lines of retreat.
When the roar hit the column, she took Dunstan by the arm. He was the nearest man who could not fight. She held on from the Droval's first claw-strike to the end of its test of Sodoku. Afterwards her hand was still closed on his arm. She noticed, let go, and began at once to look Sodoku over from thirty paces. He was sitting on the Droval's skull. Nothing in her certification covered that.
When the column halted in the Stormfold, the Greymane estate kept its wounded in the eastern end of the great hall, where the roof still held. Bram Greymane had been carried in there hurt while she was still with the ship. The infirmary was Lilia's. Nella worked beside her over the wounded of the southern approach for as long as the halt lasted. She took the cases she was given and left the room's running to the woman who kept it. The Greymane wounded went into her oath with the rest.

#### Where They Stand

She came in with the column to the north gate of Kharven-Seat. By the sledges Dunstan argued with nobody over whether the cast-iron ladle was his property or the crown's, now that crown ground was under it. Nella told him it counted as an assault charge.
Her healing supplies are a line in Edward Lambert's count, beside Brida Ashwell's. He reckons from both the day the stores run empty.
The oath named the road from the ice to Kharven, and the road ends here. No record gives her another. Sonzai's order on the stranger's condition has not been lifted in any record, and no record says whether Heisuke has let her look at his left hand.
She holds that a wound treated and never followed up reopens. She leaves none unwatched. The apron is still cleaner than the coat under it.

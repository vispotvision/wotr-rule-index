# Nella Forgeman — The Open Hand

*The Open Hand · The Treatment Is Not Negotiable*
> **Full Name:** Nella Forgeman
> **Age:** 33 · **Origin:** Concord (chartered quarter, smith's daughter)
> **Level:** 52 · **Stage:** I Ignition · **Band:** I
> **Role:** Field surgeon for the Kokan expedition
> The surgeon's oath-form is the open hand. The hand that will do the work, presented as the instrument.

---

Smith's daughter turned surgeon. Dark blonde hair braided in a single rope over the left shoulder. Face plain, serious, belonging to a woman who decided early that being taken seriously required arriving first and complaining never. Canvas apron over her coat, pockets sewn with loops for instruments. The apron is cleaner than the coat because the apron is the thing she can control.
Dual history in her hands: forge-calluses on the palms from her father's shop, surgical steadiness in the fingers from Accord field-medic certification. She swore on her right hand, palm out, fingers spread — the surgeon's form, not the warrior's fist.
Her oath: *"I swear to keep you alive. Both of you. All of you. Every person on this ice and every person we collect between here and Kharven."*
Her triage protocol for a Titan Droval encounter: move the wounded beyond three hundred metres and begin treatment only after confirming the creature has not followed. If the creature has followed, the simplified protocol is: run.

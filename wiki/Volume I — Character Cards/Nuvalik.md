### XIV · Psychology

Wipes the blade before she speaks, which is the one thing about her that frightens Vesk. Has objected twice that a carcass no bird will touch stays where it fell and gets counted. Her proverb: where she was raised, the sky finishes what the ground starts, and when the sky refuses, people walk out to see why. Her private thought on the page: she has spent all three names for this body on this work. Hesitates before fetching the hook for the ninth hind, then goes.

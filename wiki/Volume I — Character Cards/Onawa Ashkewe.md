# Onawa Ashkewe

*Queen of the Tsohanto · She Who Wears the Old Faces · The Ash-Crowned · Card 23*
> **Grade A across every Primary Stat, uniformly.** *Nothing about her reads as an outlier build.*
>
> **She is simply, evenly, an A-Grade queen at the exact Level and Stage that supports it** — which in a roster full of anomalies is itself the unusual thing.

---

## I · Identity

**Race / Lineage** Elven — **Tsohanto branch.** *Unaffiliated with Eresse or the Varrisak courts; a hidden forest-and-barrow culture that developed apart from both*
**Age** Appears late twenties. *True age unrecorded — Tsohanto queens stop counting years at their coronation*
**Standing** The Tsohanto Reach — **independent, Veil-adjacent, no Guild Accord standing**
> **Catalyst Event.** At sixteen she was sent into the barrow-ground alone, as every Tsohanto heir is, **to be tested by the mask her mother wore before her.**
>
> **The mask does not test strength.** *It tests whether the wearer can carry someone else's grief without losing her own name underneath it.*
>
> Onawa came out of the barrow three days later **wearing the mask on the crown of her head rather than her face** — which the elders had never seen a candidate do before or since.
>
> ***She has not explained why, and no one has asked her twice.***

## VI–VII · Force and Flow

**Strike Force** · A-Grade, **but never her method** — *power expressed through communion and Domain, not the body itself.*
**Domain Pressure** · **Realm-scale.** The Long Barrow Court has consolidated enough **to hold lesser Domains rather than merely resisting them.**
**Durability** · A-Grade against Essence-based assault. *The mask does nothing to soften raw physical force — a blade that actually lands remains a real threat.*
**Travel Speed** · A-Grade, Mach 5–25 — *reflects communion-borrowed reflexes when a stronger ancestor is worn, not her own baseline footwork.*
> **Aura Pressure Field · 25m passive.**
>
> ***Up to 1.5 kilometres when the mask is lowered over her face in full communion.***
**η 0.78** in grave-ground and old growth — **drops sharply in consecrated or actively-warded ground.**
**Passive Recovery** · **Slow.** *Grief does not refill quickly.*
**Active Recovery** · **Recovers faster near unmarked graves than anywhere else** — *which unsettles everyone who travels with her.*

## VIII–IX · Traits and Domain

> **The Old Faces** · Innate · Anamnesis-Mortalis
>
> Calls **one ancestor's memory, skill, or voice through the mask at a time**, borrowing their knowledge or bearing for the duration of the communion.
>
> ***Never their full power. Never for free.***
**Ashbloom Cycle** · Tempered · Verdantia-Mortalis — wounds and blight in her presence **resolve faster toward one of two ends: clean healing or clean death.** *Rarely lingering in between.*
**Barrow-Sight** · Lineage · Eidolyn — **sees the outline of anyone recently dead still standing where they fell**, *for as long as grief for them remains unresolved in the living.*
**The Reach Listens** · Resonant · Tenebra — **the Tsohanto dead do not need to be summoned to notice a threat to their queen.** *Something in the tree line always does, first.*
> **Domain · The Long Barrow Court**, Realization-tier. Past seed and stabilisation, **now consolidated into a Realm capable of holding lesser Domains.**
>
> The ground for a mile around develops **the specific stillness of a place already grieved for** — *and it holds that stillness whether or not she is actively maintaining it.*
>
> **Enemies find their own dead easier to think about than the fight in front of them. Allies find old fears easier to set down.**

## XIII–XV · Description, Psychology, Equipment

5'5", **slight and still, ceremonial rather than martial.** Deep teal-green hair in thick braids bound with cord, bone beads and dark feathers. **Deep red eyes, unblinking during communion.** Pale cool-toned skin, **faint grey undertone at the fingertips where the mask's Essence lingers.**
Deep indigo mourning-robes trimmed in red-and-white geometric braid and dark fur, **embroidered with pink lotus and peony in a bloom-wilt-bloom cycle.** The mask worn **crownwise atop her head rather than over her face**, except during communion.
> *Scent of cold ash, crushed petals, old cedar.*
| **Core Wound** | **She wears grief as a crown instead of a face because she fears that wearing it properly would mean forgetting which griefs were actually hers** |
|---|---|
| **Flaw** | **Carries a stranger's unresolved grief past the point of personal cost** |
| **Desire** | **To one day take the mask off entirely and find out who is underneath it, uninherited** |
| **Loop** | **Solves everyone's grief but her own, endlessly** |
| **Voice** | **Unhurried, declarative, feeling stated before the noun that caused it.** Two to three phonetic softenings on sibilants, *more pronounced under grief than anger.* **Rarely raises her voice** — states facts about death and grief **with the same evenness other rulers reserve for weather** |
| **Dialogue Evolution** | **Speaks more plainly and less ceremonially the longer she goes without invoking the mask** — *and the ceremonial register returns within a sentence of putting it back on* |
| **Posture** | Still, weighted, **head tipped slightly as though listening to something behind the current conversation.** *Because she usually is* |
| **Stress Default** | **Goes quieter, not louder. The mask's fangs are the only thing that moves** |

## XVI–XVII · Temperance and Fractures

**I Murmuring** — *first heard a voice in the barrow that was not her own.* **II Welling** — accepted her first inherited memory without flinching. **III Ascension** — held a communion past thirty seconds. **IV Flourishing** — first Ashbloom resolution on a dying stranger; **bonded the raven.**
**V Splintering** — **nearly lost her own voice permanently mid-communion at seventeen.**
> **VI Glory** — held the mask through her mother's funeral **without speaking a single borrowed word.** Accepted the crown fully, **mask worn crownwise by her own choice, against precedent.**
**VII Refraction** — first Barrow-Sight recognition of a death she had not personally witnessed. **VIII Transcendence** — first stabilised Domain at the Long Barrow. **IX Invocation** — the Wellsprings began recognising her unprompted.
**X Realization** · **Active** — the Continuum now accommodates her Domain outright, **consolidated into a true Realm.** **XI+ locked.**
> **Fracture Log · The Barrow Trial, Age 16** — Spirit-Attunement. **Healed-reinforced, unresolved deviation.**
>
> *Feature: she wears the mask crownwise instead of over her face, a variation no prior queen chose — and the Reach still doesn't know whether it is strength or an unfinished wound.*
> **Fracture Log · Her mother's death and funeral** — Anamnesis-Gnosis. Processed, functional scar.
>
> *Feature: the first grief she ever carried that she has still, privately, not finished carrying.*

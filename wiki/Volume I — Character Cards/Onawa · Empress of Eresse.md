# Onawa · Empress of Eresse

*Empress of Eresse · First Listener of the Sunroot Court · as of the morning of the yard at Korvaeth*
> **Full Name:** Onawa
> **Race / Lineage:** Elven, of Eresse
> **Office:** Empress of Eresse. She held the calendar, and no one in four quarters questioned her standing to open a window.
> **Age:** Of an age with Ilthára Korvaeth. They were fourteen together in the Vosk orchard.
> **Not to be confused with:** Onawa Ashkewe, Queen of the Tsohanto, a different woman who shares her name.

---

## Description

She came out of the eastern door at the sixth bell with two of her own household behind her, neither of them touching her, and walked the length of the yard by herself in the cold, at the pace of a woman crossing her own hall.
She had put her hair up herself. The imperial coil is done in nine passes by two attendants and cannot be done in fewer. What was on her head that morning was four passes and a plain pin, and it held.
She arrived holding nothing. She knelt without being asked and put her own hands behind her.

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

#### Origin

Onawa and Ilthára Korvaeth were fourteen in the Vosk orchard when a boy told Ilthára she did not have a name. Ilthára went to her father, and he confirmed it. She came back crying, and Onawa could not bear it, and said a thing to make her stop: the holding validates itself.
It worked, and she went home. Ilthára built a doctrine on it, then a faction, then thirty years and six houses and a war. By the time Onawa understood what she had made, the whole north was standing on it, and she could not pull it out.

#### The Making

Eleven days before the yard she offered Ilthára the transfer in full form, and it was refused. A refused grant cannot be offered again inside the same term. In the same meeting she told Ilthára the truth about the orchard: that a thing said carelessly by a child can still be correct, and that neither of them could prove it either way. She asked in writing, in full form, to walk the yard alone, and it was granted.

#### The Cost

In the yard she spoke her own name in full form and opened the witnessing window herself: *sae-ureth*. Three hundred and six compensated witnesses, paid to witness a killing, became by the same fact a convened assembly, because the compensation does not specify what is witnessed. She gave a by-name, *Elar-ith*. Then she turned to Common, so that the fourth rank and the Vosk child could follow it, and asked Ilthára to say her name in full.
If Ilthára refused, she kept the north and Onawa died unnamed. If she said it, the record would show the house of Korvaeth performing Eressean procedure, and her own doctrine would fall with it. Ilthára put her hand on the four-pass coil and said the name in full form, with the witness word three times. Then Onawa was killed at the block.

#### Where They Stand

The entry went to the sealed register at Velthaeir and has never been narrowed. The Sunroot Court did not contest the transfer. It convened in full form and voted a regency, on the ground that the Empress's name had passed and her office had not, because a name and an office are separate objects in Eressean law. Onawa had transferred one and said nothing about the other.
Since the yard the Tsohanto Reach, whose queen bears the same name, has sent no delegation to either party. Its silence is the Tsohanto's answer to the shared name.

#### Ties

- **Ilthára Korvaeth** · friend since the orchard at fourteen. She killed her in the yard and named her in full first.
- **Aeldros Korvaeth, the Validated** · Ilthára's brother. He stood at the top of the steps through the naming.
- **Maren Vaeleth** · Reckoner of Korvaeth. She entered the naming verbatim, which is the office.
- **Onawa Ashkewe** · Queen of the Tsohanto. A different woman with the same name; her Reach has been silent since the yard.

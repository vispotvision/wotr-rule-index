# Orin Farrant

**Say it** · OR-in FARR-unt
*Night Watch Society, Urbis chapter. Searcher, on the crown's warrant.*

### I · Identity

**As Of** · Urbis, the late autumn of 701 IC: the morning she carries Draycott's held letter up a stair to Kwon Mu-jin.
**Name** · Orin Farrant. Concord human, Northern register: a pool given name and a descriptive byname frozen long ago into a chartered Urbis surname. She files as *Farrant, O., Urbis chapter, on the crown's warrant*.
**Called** · Farrant by Draycott and on the filing line; Searcher by witnesses and in the Division's letter of access. Never Master, since she holds no Tier.
**Age** · 42. Born 659 IC in Urbis, a weigher's daughter.
**Office** · Searcher of the Urbis chapter, which keeps its house and bench in the Ropewalk. The crown's warrant runs in the crown's land. In Ketsuen she can ask and cannot compel.
**Service** · Twenty-one years in the Society: eight on an Urbis walk through the slow-main streets east of the high street, thirteen carrying the warrant on cases.
**The case** · The Castlefall file, joint with the Research and Archives Division's Urbis office. Halveth opened it and set the condition of access: field notes, held under the Division's seal. The notes are The Farrant Papers.
**Standing** · None. The Accord's ladder starts at Initiate.
**Level / Stage / Band** · None. Aether Class Ø, the Dormant: a Crystal that never woke, in a soul the System does not track.
**Catalyst Event** · The survey hills, 678 IC. At nineteen she kept the counts for a Guild survey party that walked into a Spirit-type concentration in the heartland hills. The practitioners vented the onset through their Shells. Her Shell was sealed and could not vent it, so she kept it. She came home quiet, nobody filed it, and two years later she went to the Society to count the people nobody baselines.
**Gloss rights** · Diagnostic only. She explains what an instrument read and what the law says about it; a working she explains only in the words of someone who could read it, and she names whose.
**Pressure** · Nothing to suppress. A sealed Crystal puts no Pressure into a room.

### II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Class** · Ø, the Dormant. Sealed, unresponsive, nearly silent. Screened at seven in the screening season; nothing stirred, and her family could not have paid for a teacher.
**Crystal Tier** · Dormant Crystal. **Crystal State** · none; the States belong to a working Crystal.
**Essence Core** · Present, furnished and fully exposed behind a Shell that cannot phase-lock.
**Essence Typology** · Unread. No instrument in the Accord reads it on a Crystal that has never stirred.
**η, EU reserve, Flux Density, AU/s** · None.
**Path** · Spirit by grain alone, single-axis, recognised by nothing.
**Trace** · None lasting. She moves through the world without leaving a signature for anyone to follow.

### III · Wellspring Harmonizations

None. A sealed Shell cannot phase-lock, so no Wellspring answers her and no Craft is hers. The ground still acts on her: sites do not check for a Crystal. A practitioner in a Spirit-type onset vents the load through the Shell. Farrant keeps it, and no recovery protocol has been written for anyone in her case.

### IV · Primary Stats

**Gnosis** Hollow · **Tempering** Hollow · **Ardency** Hollow · **Resilience** Hollow · **Dexterity** Hollow · **Vitality** Hollow · **Dominion** Hollow · **Harmonics** Hollow
No Pool: no Level and no Threshold grant. No stat output above Hollow is possible on a Dormant Crystal, and no coil will print one.

### V · Sub-Stat Peaks

No Sub-Stat has been bought or assayed.
> **The shape of it.** What the sheet cannot show is her grain. Twenty-one years of counting set her along one line: the stand held for the full count, the needle read at eye level, the time written before the remark. It is real and it is strong, and no instrument in the Accord reads grain, so it appears on no roll and in no Grade.

### VI · Physical Force

Hollow band, body mechanics only. Strike 200 to 800 N; lift 50 to 120 kg; survives 100 to 800 J; speed below 12.4 m/s; reaction 150 to 300 ms, purely biological. A walker's legs and lungs. She cannot hurt a practitioner and does not try.

### VII · Aether Flow

None. No reserve, no draw, no field, and no reach past contact. Aura Pressure Field: none.

### VIII · Traits

**The Warm Hand** · Passive. Begun by the kept load at nineteen and stabilised by twenty years' continuous exposure on the mains: a thermal bias written into the Shell's conductivity map over the left hand, which shows in tissue as a shunt in the hand's vessels. The hand warms as ambient density climbs. It is slow to cool, so once warm it says nothing of the next ground. Nothing ever calibrated it, it gives no figure and no Family, and she does not trust it. A wound to the left hand heals off the ordinary schedule; the rest of her heals like anyone's.
**The Assize Ear** · Passive. Her mother's trade in her: a Perception bias in the Shell's conductivity map over the hearing, which reorganises the tract behind an ordinary ear. She hears the hum drop a note at a gauge-housing and the dull ring of a cased weight a street sooner than most. In a street's day noise she hears no better than anyone, and a silent working gives her nothing to hear. An injury to that ear or tract heals off schedule; anything else heals as anyone's.

### IX · Domain

None.

### X · Techniques

She performs no working. What she has is trained method and instruments, and no Codex or FOW line attaches to either.
**Two Instruments**
- **Effect** · The pocket gauge takes density; the field coil takes the character of what is there. Walked in stands along a line and entered at every stand, the pair shows two things: a residue settled at its floor, which reads the same stand to stand, and ground being drawn off, where coil and gauge disagree the same way at every stand and the size of the disagreement is the size of what is taken.
- **Cost** · Time, and her body. Each stand is held for a full count by the Guild watch, on ground she cannot feel, and at a Spirit-type edge the load she picks up stays.
- **Limit** · Class, Fidelity, Carry and a Family sweep. The Signature sweep and the gate-open pass are the chapter bench's; above that she writes nothing without a reader.
- **Tell** · A straight line paced out, a stop, the gauge held at eye level with the left glove off, a time written at each stand.
- **Counter** · Either instrument lost, or a coil burnt, ends it. A draw stopped while she stands leaves nothing to disagree. In Ketsuen the ground can be refused her.
- **What nobody knows** · How long coil and gauge had disagreed at the Genesio stair before anyone stood there with both.
**The Borrowed Read**
- **Effect** · Every reading that needs a Crystal comes to her through someone who has one, entered under that person's name: an archivist, a coil-carrier, a distiller's grain. She posts an observer outside the radius and sets her own Baseline (sleep, hands, the pressure at the temples, appetite, temper) at the head of every entry, so a change in her shows on the page before she admits it.
- **Cost** · She is only as right as whoever she borrowed from.
- **Limit** · A borrowed read is entered as borrowed and never passed off as hers.
- **Tell** · The first line of every entry is her own body.
- **Counter** · A reader who lies to her; an observer who stands too close.
- **What nobody knows** · Whether a Baseline catches a Medium-type change in time, since in someone like her it reads as her own confusion.
**The Entry**
- **Effect** · A sample goes into a Fixatio-anchored tin inside the hour, standing on the ground, under chapter wax, and so becomes a Society filing. Her file names the subjects of the warrant, the witnesses heard and the officers, and enters everyone else by post or exhibit.
- **Cost** · Everything she enters can be read, her own name first.
- **Limit** · A tin holds what it is given. It proves the sample and never the hand.
- **Tell** · The seal press, and the time written on the wax.
- **Counter** · Ground reached before her hour is out, or a chain broken between the ground and the bench.
- **What nobody knows** · Who else has read the Castlefall station's record.

### XI · Spirit Axes

No bond a coil could register. The people who bear load:
**Her mother** *(sworn weigher at the Urbis market assize)* · dead nine winters, and still the root of her; the girl beside her held the small hammer.
**Halveth** *(opening officer of the joint case)* · a margin she writes toward. Unsettled, since Halveth wrote to Draycott and never told her.
**Calla** *(distiller, of Castlefall)* · trust of hands, mutual. Two women in the file with no Crystal awake.
**Kwon Mu-jin** *(addressee of the held letter)* · one stair, one letter, a line she did not say.
**Gisli Draycott** *(the platform at Castlefall)* · a debt of standing. He held his weight down so Farrant could stand, and lost the hold once.

### XII · Resistances

Strong against anything that hunts by signature, because she leaves none: it finds nothing of hers to follow and has to follow her paper. Anything that reads a Crystal returns nothing on her. Weak against Drafts: the working lands on her Core in full; nothing seats, and repeated doses only deepen the grain. Weak against Pressure from anyone who lets the weight go: sweat, nausea and the ground coming up. Weak against Spirit-type ground, which she cannot vent, and against Medium-type contamination, which in her is indistinguishable from confabulation. Weak against a knife, cold, and her own name at the head of every filing.

### XIII · Physical Description

Tall and long in the leg, a walker's build, level in the eye with most men on a tram platform. Lean at the neck and wrist, narrow through the hip, small-breasted and flat in the belly, the calves gone hard from the walks. Hair iron-grey since thirty, the grey of a cold stove-lid, cut blunt at the jaw so it goes under a hood. A broad-boned face weathered on the mains and never painted, deep lines from the nose to the mouth, pale grey eyes that go to a collar before a face. A white seam across the bridge of the nose from a stair in a cut district. A slight stoop at the shoulders from twenty years of bending to street-door meters; long, dry, square-nailed hands, big for a woman's.
A Society walker's coat of oiled grey wool, long, cut for a man and taken in at the waist in her own stitches, the pockets re-sewn square to take tins, the cuffs gone soft and pale at the edges. Under it a high-buttoned bodice of dark brown wool with a watch pinned at the breast on a short chain, and a walking skirt of drab serge divided for stairs and ladders, hemmed at the boot top and bound in leather where the kerbs wear it. Resoled boots, the second resoling.
**Distinguishing.** A violet stain of copying-pencil on the right thumb and forefinger. A wax scar on the ball of the left thumb. The regulation bracelet on the left wrist. In winter she keeps the left glove on indoors and takes it off to hold a gauge.

### XIV · Psychology

**What she refuses.** To carry another person's words. She will carry paper; a message belongs to whoever has to deliver it. "I'll carry the paper. The man can carry himself." She also refuses to write a name the file cannot carry.
**What she survived.** The load at nineteen, and a filing category nobody ever opened.
**What she believes that costs her.** That the observer is no evidence. A reading is evidence; a feeling is a porter's word, and she keeps her own hand off the record as her mother kept hers off the scale. It cost her at the Genesio stair, where she stayed after the hand had warmed because the hand had never been calibrated.
**What she wants.** What the sky is doing entered in a number before the crown's clerks return it as weather; under that, a record that counts the unawakened.
**Humour.** Dry, and it comes as a reading: instrument, figure, remark, and the remark is the joke.
**Saying.** Her mother's: "A solid weight rings when struck."

### XV · Equipment

**Pocket density gauge.** Brass-cased, reading EU per cubic metre; the sites ladder's rung comes off it by arithmetic.
**Field coil.** Small, with reference standards and a scale card. Bench-trained reading of Class, Fidelity, Carry and a Family sweep.
**Clearbeam loupe.** For grade stamps, registry letters under a craft mark, proof-marks.
**Tins and wax.** Fixatio-anchored evidence tins, a stick of chapter wax, a small seal press.
**Regulation bracelet.** Three brass bands wound with fine grey wire, guild proof-marks on the outer band, seated against the inside of the left wrist. It pulls toward a practitioner who is holding a weight down, and it is the only way she knows that one is.
**Field book.** Sunkwood boards, Essence-quiet, so the entries take nothing from what she carries. The Papers are written in it.
**Walker's kit.** A rule, a sectioning knife, a watch set to the Guild hour, a line timetable.
**Papers.** The crown's warrant, folded under the crown's seal, naming her and the case; Halveth's letter of access.
She carries no weapon and no silver token, and she looks for one on everyone else first.

### Voice

**Notices first** · The silver: who in the room carries a token, and so where the weight is that she cannot feel. Then the doors and the clock.
**Sentence length** · Short to medium. Readings come as fragments with the instrument first: "Gauge, the outside stair, second division: seven and two marks." She runs long only in a description of record or a list. She is an instrument reader, so an instrument's figure is given exact, to the scale's last mark.
**Contractions** · Sometimes: in speech ("I'll carry the paper"), never in the field book.
**Pet word** · "Entered." As a sentence by itself, after something she did not want to write.
**Never says** · "I felt" or "I sensed" of anything outside her own skin; a figure she did not take; a name the file cannot carry; a practitioner's word for what she cannot read, unless an instrument or a named reader gave it.
**Stumbles** · Never in speech: a sentence that will not come right is left unfinished and not restarted. In writing she strikes a line through and, on Halveth's order, leaves it standing.
**Under stress** · The lines shorten and get numbered, times go to the division and the count, and a Baseline turns up in the middle of an entry.
**In grief** · Back to the market assize: weights, the small hammer, a cased weight, a short loaf, the roll.
**In joy** · The speech holds. The hand goes large on the page and she writes the time twice.
**Sample line** · "Gauge at the stair foot, fourth division: nine and two marks. Coil at the same stand, out the same way as the last three. Entered."

### XVI · Temperance Record

None. A Crystal that never woke cannot melt. She has undergone the Weathering instead: refinement under strain with no recasting, the soul hardening along the direction it was already used. Her set runs toward the count, taken on an Urbis walk, at the chapter bench and in thirteen years of case ground. In the trade word she is set, and the grain will run no other way.

### XVII · Fracture Log

No Crystal fracture is recorded; the log keeps the events and what each cost her.
**Event · The survey hills.** The load she kept at nineteen, entered in no ledger. The left hand has run warm in dense ground ever since.
**Event · The Genesio stair.** Spring of 701. The archivist on the stair above told her she had stopped talking for fifty breaths. That night she wrote a wish to finish a letter she had not begun, struck it, and was told to keep it. The hand was still warm when she wrote her next Baseline in Urbis.
**Event · The Castlefall platform.** Late summer of 701, at the station's second bell. Draycott's hold slipped when Farrant asked after the treatise's author. One knee on the cedar, sweat at the hairline, the stomach turning; a carter sat down on his crate and a child cried. Draycott took the weight back and begged her pardon. The chapter's coil-carrier, outside the radius at the platform's far end, confirmed it on the coil. Farrant entered the slip and left out its reason.

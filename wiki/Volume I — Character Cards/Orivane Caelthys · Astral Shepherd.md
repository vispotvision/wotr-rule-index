### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The courts of waking and sleeping law. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Orivane Caelthys was born at Aureth Bay, on the Eastern Sun Coast of the Outer World, where the harbour mouth runs between barrier islands. His mother was a harbour pilot. She brought ships in over the bars at night and steered by the Nail and by the stars a hull put out.
She taught him from the pilot boat. A low star that went dark and came back meant a sail had crossed it. A run of stars going out along the horizon meant a hull. Before he could handle the tiller alone he could find the Cutter, which shows only in the last light and the first. Sailors use it. Few others can find it.
One night the boat went onto a bar the harbour chart did not show. The sand had shifted since the last sounding. The boat broke up in the surf, and he held a spar until the tide put him ashore at dawn. His mother did not come ashore.

#### The Making

Afterwards a survey of the Concord Celestial Cartography Office put in at the bay, and nobody in it could find the Cutter from the headland. The pilots sent the boy up. When the survey sailed, he sailed with it.
The Office mapped and stabilised the Astral Wellsprings of the Outer World's drifting realms. He learned to chart them the way his mother had found ships. A star that dimmed had something in front of it, and the dark lines in its light told him what that light had crossed. From what the sky blocked he drew lanes, and carrying houses sent their ships down them.
He was given the Ecliptic Staff. Its ring turned with the bodies overhead. He learned he could call to what lived out there, from benign star-spirits to void leviathans, and guide them or dismiss them. When he worked long, the space near him bent. Faint constellations came into the air around him.
The Office began to send him wherever navigation rights were contested, to sit between the parties.
Then the Luminarch Wellspring he kept opened under him as "a never-ending spiralling walkway of light suspended in an ocean of stars." Time slowed on it. From it he saw across multiple planes at once, and he began to read entire realm-shifts days before they occurred. The walkway stayed when he stepped off it. It had agreed to different terms, and it kept them whether or not he stood there.

#### The Cost

He reads by absence. A dimming can be a body passing. It can also be a gap with nothing behind it, and a reader of gaps will in time build an object from one. A navigator who charts a gate that is not there sends people through it. He will not chart a gate from a single dimming. He waits for the body to cross again.
What he reads coming, he reports, and he does not stand in its road.
Everything he learned, he learned at range: from a boat, from a headland, from the walkway. He never trained to fight. A person who stands in front of him meaning harm meets a man with almost no answer.
Two carrying houses once contested a route through the drifting realms, and Orivane sat between them as the neutral party. He charted the lanes, and they divided them. The settlement was written and signed. The master of one house also swore to keep it in Atheron Dreymarch's presence. A season later the master sent a ship down the other house's lane. The chain of that oath summoned Atheron into the master's sleep, where Atheron laid a geas on his will, and the house never used that lane again. The other house sued for its lost carriage in the Altherion Central Concord Tribunal. Lord Maerion Lysandre argued for the house and Riven Eltamyr Vonnel for the master. Atheron came blindfolded and said the chain still held. Leontes Praevan read the written settlement, ruled on it and on nothing else, and gave the house its carriage. Orivane's lanes stood in ink.
On the tribunal steps he told Atheron that chains can become snares to the self. He could have given it as a forecast. He gave it as advice.

#### Where They Stand

Orivane Caelthys is Astral Magus, Keeper of the Luminarch Wellspring and Celestial Navigator of the Concord Celestial Cartography Office. He is called the Ram of the Outer Sky and the Ecliptic Voice.
He reads the celestial weather that could reach Thalyren Veyroot's groves and sends it to the Everbloom. Thalyren cannot leave the Everbloom. Orivane keeps his dealings with Lucien Draeve minimal and calls Lucien's toxic alchemy "a corrosion of the greater weave."
He still says of the stars: "They are behind us, within us, and ahead of us. Every journey we take is already written in their light." What answers when he calls something he did not intend to, nobody knows.

# Orivane Caelthys · Astral Shepherd

> *"The stars are not above us. They are behind us, within us, and ahead of us. Every journey we take is already written in their light."*
> **A Revelation card — the sixth found.** The sheet reads **"Temperance Stage X (Mythic Revelation)."**
>
> ***Revelation is Stage XV*** — Archon-participant territory, **two recorded practitioners in the whole history of the setting.** **Numeral wins: Stage X, Realization.**
>
> *"Cosmos / Void / Light"* were not Essence types.

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Orivane Caelthys. **Called** · **The Ram of the Outer Sky.** Astral Shepherd · **The Ecliptic Voice.**
**Role** · **Astral Magus. Keeper of the Luminarch Wellspring. Celestial Navigator.**

### Affiliation

**Concord Celestial Cartography Office.** *Oversees the mapping and stabilisation of Astral Wellsprings, ensuring safe passage through the Outer World's drifting realms.* Acts as a neutral party in political disputes when navigation rights are contested.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage X, Realization.** *The practitioner stops being the party that has to be accommodated. Pacts hold in the ground. Regions keep a set that outlasts them by generations.*
>
> **His Wellspring is "a never-ending spiralling walkway of light suspended in an ocean of stars," and within it time slows and perception expands across multiple planes.**
>
> ***That is not a place he visits. It is terrain that has agreed to different terms, and it stays agreed whether or not he is standing on it.***

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Eclipseron** · Spatium. **Governing.** *Occultation and transit photometry — planets are found by the dip in a star's brightness, not by being seen, and a spectrum's dark lines carry more composition data than its bright continuum.*
> ***A celestial navigator whose primary current is literally the science of reading stars by what they block.*** **This is the single most exact Wellspring assignment in Volume IV.** *Failure: false transit — not every dimming is an occultation, and a practitioner committed to reading absence will eventually construct an object out of a gap with nothing behind it.*

**Luminalis** · Fulguria. **The guidance-light.**
**Transference** · Spatium. **Coupled oscillators** — *the Shepherd's Calling, and how he reaches astral entities across realms.*

# Orokh Sable-Forge · The Wyrmwright

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Orokh Sable-Forge. **Called** · **The Wyrmwright.**
**Role** · **Blacksmith of fallen dragons.** *Relic-maker whose anvil bends bone-memory and star-iron into covenant blades, seals and tools.*
**Origin** · **Outer World, Braqth. Giant-kin.** *Body lacquered black by furnace soot and alchemical pitch; hair white as quenched slag.* **His eyes are sealed — he "sees" by reading heat, rhythm, and the way names tremble in metal.**

### Affiliation

**Contracted quietly by the Guild Accord for containment hardware.** *Bargained with by the Black Concord for things no registry admits.*
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VIII, Transcendence.** *The Catalyst is taking a wound carried as structural damage and consciously processing it into architecture. Not healing it. Making it hold weight on purpose.*
>
> **His eyes are sealed and he did not replace them.** *He built an entire craft on reading heat and rhythm instead — and Blind-Star Sight is not compensation. It is the wound made load-bearing, which is Transcendence stated as a trade.*

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Anamnesis** · Limina. **Governing.** *Remanent magnetisation — objects genuinely store their history in physically readable form. Anamnesis is the instrument, not the miracle.* **Bone-Song is Anamnesis applied with a hammer: he taps until the marrow remembers flight.**
**Fixatio** · Materia. **Anchors and seals.** *Name Riveting, and the reason a piece favours one hand forever after.*
**Materia Primordia** · Materia. **Crystallises Essence into permanent mineral form.**
**Ignivale** · Caloria. *The current holding temperatures charcoal cannot reach* — **which is why Hearthless Heat needs no coal.**

## VI · Working Style

**He forges without fire.** *The hammerhead carries a green, tidal glow that makes bone sing; the anvil drinks that song and keeps the measure.* **He lays a dragon rib on the face, taps until the marrow remembers flight, then coaxes that memory to flow into the shape he needs** — *a door that forgets to open for thieves, a staff that steadies a dying breath, a cleaver that only cuts vows.*
**Preferred grounds** · **Silent workshops, cave mouths, cold wind.** *His presence stills sparks midair; filings fall in perfect lines like handwriting.*

---

## VII · Traits

**Blind-Star Sight** · **Reads heat and heartbeat through metal.** *Lies leave ripples he can taste on the tongue.*
**Hearthless Heat** · **Workpiece glows without flame when his hammer taps.** *The glow obeys rhythm, not wood or coal.*
**Name-Etcher** · **Can lay a person's true name into a tool so the tool behaves around them as if it knew them.**

---

## VIII · Implements

**Mourning Hammer** · **Cleaver-broad face carrying green tidal light.** *The back-hook plucks threads from stubborn alloys and drags them into line.*
**Ash-Anvil** · **A black idol of a table whose legs root like trees.** *The ornaments along the skirt are not decoration — they are pauses in a song he started a century ago and has never finished.*
**Griff Tongs** · Three-pronged, jointed like a bird's foot. **Holds things by their** ***intention*** **instead of by width.**

---

## IX · Techniques

**Bone-Song** · **Light taps along a rib; every touch pulls a different note until the whole length hums with the memory of sky**, then softens into workable thread.
**Cold Quench** · **Workpiece lifted into winter air, breath falling over it in a steady count.** *The glow sinks inward and sets like ice without a hiss.*
**Name Riveting** · **A palm to the metal, a low syllable breathed into it.** *When you test the piece later it favours one hand and grows heavy in another.*
**Vow Edge** · **He mixes a shaving of the buyer's broken promise into the billet.** *That blade will refuse to cut for them until the debt is paid.*

---

## X · Prices & Rules

**He will not work fresh kill.** *Bring bone that has had time to forget pain.*
**Payment is always twofold** — **material**, to teach the tool what world it comes from, **and story**, to tell it what to do here.
**For weapons he demands a confession. For locks, a secret. For armour, the name of something you fear and will not admit.**
> **Every one of those prices is a Class IV Crystal Draw input in all but name.** *The Standing Index defines Class IV as rendered from something that carried a Soul Crystal, with identity-shaped carry — and a confession, a secret, and a named fear are exactly that, taken directly rather than rendered.*
>
> ***The Bench has no entry for this and the Accord contracts him anyway.***
> **Codex** · Limina / Abstract–Entropic / Anamnesis primary / Stage VIII.

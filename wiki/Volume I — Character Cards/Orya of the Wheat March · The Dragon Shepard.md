# Orya of the Wheat March · The Dragon Shepard

> **This sheet is the reason the Benediction mapping could be established.** It reads **"Late Refraction moving toward Benediction."**
>
> **Refraction is Stage VII.** *Placing Benediction in the slot immediately above it gives Benediction = Stage VIII, Transcendence***, and that mapping now resolves every other card in the volume using the term.** Orya is held at late Stage VII, exactly as written.

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Orya of the Wheat March. **Called** · **The Dragon Shepard.**
**Role** · **Steppe ward and migration-keeper.** *Guides drakes like a shepherd guides flocks, guarding villages and grain routes along the New World's Caedor plains.*

### Affiliation

**Village Concord of the Wheat March** (New World: Caedor). *Field partner to the Guild Accord's Logistics & Supply Division during harvest convoys.*
**Appearance** · **Red-gold travel robes stitched with pasture sigils**, fur-edged hood, bead-weighted scarf. *Carries a bone-chime crook that sings to sky-winds.* **Calm, stubborn, eyes that count every mouth she has sworn to feed.**
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VII, Refraction — late.** *The first Domain seed. Small, fragile, often lasting seconds, requiring active concentration, and collapsing if attention fractures.*
>
> **Threshfield is that seed with a job.** *Knee-high ripples of air that part grass and slow charging things without breaking legs* — **a standing region operating on her terms, held by concentration, in the middle of a stampede.**
>
> *The Sixteen Stages notes that a Domain seed is the first genuinely uncanny thing most people will ever witness, and that bystanders describe it wrongly and consistently. The farmers of the Wheat March call it the air going thick, and they are not wrong enough to matter.*

## IX · Working Style

**She walks the flank, whistling in a three-note pattern; the drakes answer with shoulder twitches and wing-tips showing like sails in grass.** *When trouble comes, she plants the crook, draws a slow circle, and the air thickens into gentle resistance that turns a stampede without broken bones.* Khyr sweeps a warm gust across damp fields; mildew flees; children cheer and run under the dragon's shadow like it is a festival banner.
**Spiral the Panicked** · *Whistle — short, short, long.* **The flock wheels into a tightening ring until fear bleeds out.**
**Grainfire Blessing** · **Khyr exhales a baking-warm sheet while Orya combs the crop with her crook.** *Saves a harvest after cold rain.*
**Hatchling Latch** · **Wraps a sling from her scarf, tucks a screamer close, hums.** *Heartbeat syncs; tiny jaws unclench.*

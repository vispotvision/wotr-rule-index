### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card, the one archived scene it appears in, and the record of The Night Register. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Qiu Yinzhi was born into a Measurewright lineage and entered in its lineage hall under the surname Qiu. She wore her hair in the double coil those lineages have worn since before anyone could remember why. Her pins were plain iron.
She was apprenticed to an artificer and learned the trade at his forge bench. She leaned in too close, too often. The fine dark speckling across her nose and forehead came off that bench. He taught her to read a wall with a coil, walking it slowly at chest height with her eyes on the wire.
Two years before the night at Cutler Row, her master took anamnetic contact off a coil housing pulled from a foundry fire, against advice. He was sixty-one. He spent his last eleven months correcting people who called him by the wrong name, and he was right about half the time. His people made him count aloud and give names when he had them. She watched all of it.

#### The Making

She was capped after he died and carried coils for the Timberline chapter of the Night Watch Society. When a walker's find wanted sealing, she went out to the ground with a tin and assayed what came back at the chapter bench. Nol Tally walked the North Ferriby walk, and she knew his way of filing.
On the ninth she stood in the Ferriby cut among the dead hinds, grey-green mass grown through their hides. Tally cut a section with his knife. She sealed it inside the hour under the chapter's wax. Four ravens sat in the birch the whole time, and none came down. On the tenth she ran four passes on a fresh-calibrated coil, the last with the gate open. That pass burned the coil. All four came back clean.
She did not file it. For five days she carried the tin and told herself she was not good enough to have read it. Then she heard that a Warden of Duty sat nights on Cutler Row, reading inserts nobody sent him. She went out in the rain with two coils. She knew his face from a drawing, and he was thinner than the drawing.
Wystan Ashmore had her break the wax. In the back room he asked her to sweep below the Bureau's floor. She seated the spare, opened the gate and held the head flat on the glass. At a minute and forty the wire went over hard. The residue was signed by the hind. At his asking she said first, aloud, what that meant: the deer had performed the working. He asked what a formulation would need to live in a body and never touch the hand that made it. She answered from her own trade: inert in the flask, live in the animal. She followed the draw to a contract, and the contract to a purse.
Then he took off his gloves. He touched the wedge bare-handed and was sick at the stone sink, and she did not touch him and did not speak. He counted forty-two. At the close he laid a blank docket in front of her and asked for her full name, in the hand she was taught. She took the pen off the bench and wrote it out.

#### The Cost

Her master was the loss she brought up the stair. She had watched what anamnetic contact took from him, and before Wystan touched the wedge she told him so, without heat. Then she stepped aside. She would not put her body between a practitioner and his work, even that work. She asked for the count his people had kept.
The clean return burned one coil at the chapter bench. On Wystan's glass she said a sweep below the floor burns coils, and seated the spare. Four people had written bleed on that residue. She never did.
The night took the rest. The sample stopped being the chapter's filing and went under Wystan's brass as a Register exhibit. Her name went to the head of its docket. He told her why he wanted it: at a hearing she is the assay, and they will come for her first. She wrote it anyway. She had gone the colour of the wall when she called the hind's death a kindness. He called it a design.

#### Where They Stand

Qiu Yinzhi is twenty-three and capped eight months. She carries coils for the Timberline chapter and is the assay of record on the Ferriby exhibit. Her bracelet pulls toward a suppressed practitioner, and it pulled toward Wystan from the door. Her thumb works its badly struck proof-mark when she is thinking.
She owes Nol Tally a letter. Wystan wants him told to keep walking his line and to measure anything he finds. Tally has not walked his line since the eleventh.
If the Ferriby matter comes to a hearing, her assay answers to the Hexagonal Oath. Her full name heads the docket in her own hand.

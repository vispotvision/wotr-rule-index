### VIII · Signature

#### Gold-Eater Rite

**Effect** · Raelen consumes the gold rings on his hands: Dissolution takes the metal apart at the lattice, and Judicium purifies what comes free into usable Essence, restoring his reserve or spiking his output for a short window.
**Cost** · Every ring burned this way is gone. He carries a finite number, and the Rite spends a resource he cannot simply earn back on the road.
**Limit** · Only works on metal he is physically touching, which is why he wears the rings rather than carries them loose.

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Sun and sand. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Raelen Vor'Skai was born desert-blooded, to a family of the southern sand in the Outer World. He grew into a desert runner's build, lean and fast. Heat and hurt sharpened him. With a cut hand under a noon sun he saw the ground around him more clearly, and he kept moving when others sat down in the shade.
The mark was bound into his palm before he had any say in it. It was a sigil worked into the flesh. Fine etched lines ran out from it along his nerves. Through those lines a working moved at the speed of thought, faster than he could learn to govern it. The sigil bound him to a Wellspring he had not chosen, and he tried to reject it. The binding held.
People who saw the hand split two ways. Some stepped back from it. Others came closer and asked what it might be worth. He gave both kinds the same answer: what the mark did to his body, as far as a physician would need it, and nothing of what it meant.

#### The Making

He found out for himself that worn gold steadied the mark. A ring on his finger calmed the sigil and opened the etched lines wider, so he bought rings with whatever work the south would pay him for, and he wore them. When a fight asked more of him than he had, he found their other use. The gold came apart under his hand and fed him, and the ring was gone. The southern roads began to call him Gold-Eater.
None of it reconciled him to the mark. He wanted it gone.
He looked for a knife that could cut a binding, and among the ash-fire cults he found Khaeron Vant, an executioner under a horned mask. Khaeron took the work. Raelen paid him in gold he could have worn.
On the red dunes Khaeron cut into his palm with the Ash-Gorge blades, and the blood from the cut went grey and drifted off as ash. Half the mark came away. The other half held. Inside that same rite, hours apart, something in him broke and then opened. The Red-Dune Severing half-succeeded at cutting the mark out of him. He came out the other side seeing in two lights instead of one, and the Guild had no clean word for a gate that had been kicked in.

#### The Cost

When the rite broke off, the cut half lay on neither the sand nor the blades. Neither man had it. After the dunes, half of what in him could bond to anything bonded to the missing piece, like an amputee's own limb. The other half would bond to nothing. He travelled alone from then on.
He had been dragged in one rite through what others train toward, and the control that training builds never had time to form. The mark ran faster than he could hold it. He steadied it with gold he could not replace. He wore every ring on his fingers, because the Rite would take only metal he was touching, and each ring it took was gone. The road never paid him enough to buy them back.
Word of the Severing reached the small cult that held Khaeron's allegiance, and from then on the cult was one of the Orders on his trail.
At the Black Oasis the Wellspring under the water began to invert. Buyers had come to the well-head by a chart, with no guide, and set to work there. A guide was leading the oasis people out a few at a time. Raelen came in off the sand. He went down to the well-head alone and stopped the inversion, and a chunk of his own memory went with it. He climbed back up past the people who were leaving. None of them learned his name. He gave it to nobody.
He believed the cut half was still his to find. Every road he took after the dunes bent toward it.

#### Where They Stand

Raelen Vor'Skai, the Gilded Scourge, works alone across the southern Realms and answers to no charter. No Order holds him. Several track him loosely for what the mark might be worth to them, some fearing it and some wanting it, and Khaeron's cult is one of them. The half still bound in his palm keeps him tied to the Wellspring he tried to reject. The other half is missing. His whole road points toward finding it. Nobody knows whether it still answers when he calls the Rite, from wherever it ended up. Nobody has told him whose chart sent the buyers to the Black Oasis, and what is left of the oasis keeps no name for the stranger who went down to the water. The rings on his fingers are fewer than they were.

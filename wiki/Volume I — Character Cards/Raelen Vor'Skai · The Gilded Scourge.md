# Raelen Vor'Skai · The Gilded Scourge

## Raelen Vor'Skai · The Gilded Scourge

---

### I · Identity

**Name** · Raelen Vor'Skai *(pronounced RAY-len vor-SKY)*. **Called** · The Gilded Scourge.
**Also known as** · The White-Jackal, Sand-Vein Seraph, Gold-Eater.
**Epithet** · He Who Bleeds in Silence.
**Role** · Independent Inscription-Bearer. Glyph-Bound Wanderer. Desert-Blooded.
**Gloss rights** · Diagnostic only. What the mark means is not a story he tells for free.

#### Affiliation

No Order holds him, though several track him for what the mark on his hand might be worth to them. He answers to no charter and works alone across the southern Realms.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage V through VII, compressed.** *A Crystal that survives contact with a force two Tiers above its ceiling fractures along emotional lines; one that resolves the contradiction inside that fracture opens its first true Domain.* **Raelen's Splintering and his Refraction arrived inside the same ritual, hours apart, when the Red-Dune Severing half-succeeded at cutting the mark out of him.** **He came out the other side seeing in two lights instead of one, and the Guild has no clean word for a Stage gate that got kicked in rather than opened.**

---

### II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · Class III · Resonant. Essence and Aether exchange freely along the nerve-etchings, which is the only reason the mark can move as fast as it does.
**Attraction Layer** · Split. Half of it bonds to the missing fragment of the mark like an amputee's own limb; the other half refuses to bond to anything, which is most of why he travels alone.
**Crystal State** · Resonant, under standing strain. **η** · ~0.50, Band C.
**The Sable Contract** · *The incomplete sigil bound into his palm, functioning as a permanent Fixatio-class binding that neither he nor the ritual that tried could fully unmake.*

---

### III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Dissolution** · Limina. The Unbinding Stream, standing in for what the old record called Nigredo. **Sable Pulse's collapse and the Gold-Eater Rite's dissolving of metal both run on it.**
**Judicium** · Fulguria. The Wellspring of Truth, doing the purifying half of the Gold-Eater Rite and the directional half of Ink-Rend Severance.
**Tenebra** · Limina. Signal-to-noise reduction, quieter than the background rather than invisible; the spatial rending in Ink-Rend Severance is his.
**Oblivara** · Limina. The Dark Mirror, partially fused, one Wellspring deep. Sable Pulse's emotional erasure is Oblivara's, not Dissolution's.

### VIII · Signature

#### Gold-Eater Rite

**Effect** · Raelen consumes the gold rings on his hands: Dissolution takes the metal apart at the lattice, and Judicium purifies what comes free into usable Essence, restoring his reserve or spiking his output for a short window.
**Cost** · Every ring burned this way is gone. He carries a finite number, and the Rite spends a resource he cannot simply earn back on the road.
**Limit** · Only works on metal he is physically touching, which is why he wears the rings rather than carries them loose.
**Counter** · Take the rings, or force the fight somewhere he cannot reach them before the Rite starts. Without a substrate to consume, the technique has nothing to dissolve.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the missing half of the mark still answers when he calls the Rite, from wherever it ended up.

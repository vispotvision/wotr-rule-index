### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Sun and sand. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Rajhak Tarun was born among the border tribes of the Scorched Plains, of frontierfolk and Verdant blood. The plains had been thought lifeless. Then his people learned to coax Essence directly out of the ash. They traced their line to tribes once allied with the Titan Florwyn's Verdant Choir, and it was said of the Ember-Blooded that they were born with Wellspring embers in their veins.
His heart kept time with the pulse of the Wellsprings, and the Essence near him fell into step with it. Where his blood dripped on the ash, green came up.
The first quarrel he walked into was between his tribe and a nomad clan, and the knives were already out. He let both sides stay angry. He kept them talking through the heat of the day. By dark they were bargaining.

#### The Making

The Guild Accord's envoys came out to the frontier for its Wellsprings, and the clans would not speak to them. Rajhak went to both. He carried words between the nomadic clans and the envoys, and he took the Accord's work as a freelance arcanist without taking a seat in any Division. Neither side owned him. The Research and Archives Division found in him a grasp of thermic Essence law that nobody in its halls had taught. Its archivists asked him how he knew. He answered in parables.
He could open a vein and the blood would ignite. The signs he traced in its smoke bound heat, truth and feeling into law for as long as the smoke kept its shape. Around the working, volatile ground took his heartbeat's rhythm and steadied.
A Concord forge stood on the road below Solfatara. It had gone dead the year the Temple-State burned, and its altar had been cold since. Rajhak came to it with Accord witnesses standing by, and the claim made of the Ember-Blooded was tested in front of them. He spilled his own blood upon the altar. It caught. The dead forge relit, and there his heartbeat set the rhythm and the Essence around it agreed.

#### The Cost

Every rite took his own blood. Another's would not ignite for him. After each one he had to wait out the interval, the way a smith lets a quenched blade draw its temper before it goes back in the water. Rushed, he would come out of the next rite hard and brittle and split along his own grain. So he waited. He was restless in it. Any wind unwrote the signs in his smoke, and he worked in still air or not at all.
On ground that gave nothing back, the green from his blood grew on anyway, tall and pale, until it fell over under its own length. To speak with the spirit tribes he bottled grief or passion into a draught. The shape it poured out outlived the mood that made it, and the tribes went on answering a feeling that was already spent.
He held all living flame, even anger, to be kin to creation. At a parley he worked the anger in the room as he worked heat, tempering it and never putting it out. Enforcement tolerated him for what came of that and asked him nothing more. He bound himself to a frontier of clans, envoys and spirit tribes, and to no one person in it.

#### Where They Stand

When word of the Ember Regent reached the Accord's archivists, the envoys went up to the magma scar over Solfatara to engage him, and they took Rajhak as their liaison. The warpriests of an Accord warband brought Khaeron Vant with them. Where Rajhak stood, the ground over the scar kept his heartbeat and steadied. Khaeron drove four knives into the crust around the meeting, so that any lie told there would bloom as ash-fire in the liar's lungs. Rajhak's oath rings sounded the whole time he stood near the executioner. Xhael Ten'Kor told the envoys what had happened at Solfatara and nothing of how. Nothing burned. The envoys came back down with no one hurt, and the Accord entered the prince in its register as a Realm-Hazard.
That was a result, and Enforcement tolerates him for results. The Research and Archives Division values his grasp of thermic Essence law and still has no method from him. He goes between the nomadic clans and the Guild envoys. He speaks for unregistered Cores and fire-touched communities beyond Concord jurisdiction, and the spirit tribes answer his voice and no other. He wears the Ashmantle Cloak of treated soot-fur and carries the Flame-Reed Pipe. His twin oath rings have sounded near Khaeron each time the two have met since the scar. He laughs rarely. When he does, the ground answers.
The taverns call him prophet and pyromancer and have not decided which. Asked how, he says, "Every spark wants to grow. I just remind it how." At the forge below Solfatara his heartbeat still sets the rhythm. Whether the Ember-Blooded are a bloodline at all, or a discipline his people taught the blood, nobody knows.

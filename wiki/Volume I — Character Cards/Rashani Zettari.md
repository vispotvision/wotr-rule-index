### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card, the one archived scene it appears in, and the record of The Zettari war camp and the Mahuo who came to it. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Rashani Zettari was born into the Zettari, a Path-born line that called the body its first throne. Its children were built dense in the bone, and their bodies often reached combat maturity before the rest of them did. His came in the other order. Most of the line inherited an eye for Essence flow and weak points. The rarest form of that inheritance was the Origin Eye, which "does not breed true and does not follow the crown," and it opened in him before his body was ready.
He had a brother, Ma'Kovu. The Zettari had old enemies, and among them was Cozbi Mahuo, the Ledger-Keeper, whose grievance was the endless war of the Zettari and the Moto.
He grew up watching attacks he could see and could not always stop. He saw the shoulder load, the foot commit, the blade find its angle. His hands arrived after.

#### The Making

The first time his hands arrived in time, it was against a spear. He saw the thrust begin and shifted its starting angle before it reached full speed. It missed him by less than two inches. That was the First Measure.
Next he tried to correct three attacks at once, and collapsed under the reading. Afterward he read narrower and faster, the one thing that mattered first.
At a gate under siege he named the exact hinge that would fail. The gate broke there. From then on he read walls, seams and joints as closely as he read fighters. Under his palm an old fracture and an Aetheric repair scar felt different.
At eighteen he was drilled to rank a hundred small dangers honestly against the one that mattered, and to move toward that one. Then, for the first time in live combat, he carried a full chain of corrections through to its end. The Zettari began to watch him.

#### The Cost

The collapse, the Mirror Fracture, cost him a hemorrhage and very nearly his life. Too many targets still broke his field into visual noise. The gap never closed. He would read the first cause cleanly, begin the correction, and watch someone faster finish before his hand arrived. Each correction was paid for in the body. A false read brought vertigo, a bent thrust strained his eyes and wrists, and loosening a buckle left a tremor in his hand.
For seven years he lived inside a Transfer that asked everything of him. At the very end of the asking it told him it would give back what it had taken. Through those years he chased a faint living warmth back to himself.
His field kept one doctrine, "Everything begins somewhere," and he held to it. A cause he could not locate had still been built by someone, somewhere, in a room his authority was not invited into. His field notes had an honest phrase, "access depth exceeded," for whatever lay outside what he was permitted to witness.
The belief cut the other way too. Every flaw he could see began to look like one he had the right to fix, and every correction made the next one easier. The Mirror Fracture had taught him to catalogue what he could not yet solve and keep moving toward what he could.

#### Where They Stand

Rashani is eighteen. The Zettari keep him under observation, and his sovereign training is unfinished. He speaks calmly and exactly, with no wasted words, and some take it for coldness. Under stress the Origin Eye widens. He stops talking and reads everything in reach until one target resolves clearly. His eyes are black-grey at rest and bleach to a pale light when the Eye opens. A faint smell of ozone comes first. He wears a white-black mantle that spreads a correction's recoil across his frame. He carries no finished scars.
He has the Eye and does not yet have the hands. He has not yet lost a first time. The fracture called the Authority Question is still accumulating. It comes when he corrects something he had no right to correct and cannot undo it. Nobody yet fills the place kept for a peer who fights at the beginning of things.
On the last night the archive gives him, his brother Ma'Kovu cut the watcher's opening behind Mizuyi's threshold, tracing its spur home through Rashani's own Eye. Rashani walked the corridor his brother opened, two paces behind him. Three strides past the threshold he felt the pressure change. Far beyond the range he could resolve, Cozbi Mahuo had just proved an armour of Zettari make against Matter and its contradiction. It reached Rashani as a beginning with no middle and no end. He named it access depth exceeded, filed it among the things too large to solve that night, and did not turn when the castle's foundation groaned.
The door at the corridor's end had not existed an hour before. He set his palm flat against the cold iron of its frame. He did not knock or call her name, and he pushed the door open into the dark.

#### Ties

- **Rengai Zettari** · line-kin, sibling-parallel. The two are set side by side, "One reads origins, one reads residue," and an Origin Eye like Rashani's later opened in Rengai's son, Asante.
- **Cozbi Mahuo** · enemy; witness. On the night Cozbi lost his watching, Rashani felt his working as a beginning past the depth he was permitted to read, filed it unsolved and walked on.
- **Ma'Kovu Zettari** · brothers. His brother traced Cozbi's spur through Rashani's own Eye and made the door Rashani walked to.
- **Mizuyi** · the warmth he chased. The warmth he chased for seven years answered him through her door, and he went in without knocking.

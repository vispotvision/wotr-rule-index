## XIX · The Two Hands

> **Neither hand is available to him.** Baqaa and Fanaa are the mature expression of Jicho la Chanzo and require the body and Crystal to have closed the gap the Eye opened. Rashani has the Eye and does not yet have the hands.
>
> ***Everything on his current card is the reading. None of it is the acting.***
**Baqaa** · the Remaining. Returning a thing to the condition it began in. His Sahihi-Umbo is a shallow prefiguration of it and costs him a hand tremor to move six centimetres of buckle.
**Fanaa** · the Passing. Denying a thing its beginning. Nothing on his sheet touches this. **The Authority Question fracture is the door to it**, which is the reason the fracture is listed as accumulating rather than survivable.

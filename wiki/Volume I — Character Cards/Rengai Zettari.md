> *Grew up under his father's brutal ceiling. The Crystal adapted toward residue — not raw force.*
**The First Red Haze** — filled a yard with uncontrolled ash and **nearly choked himself.** *Control means filtration, not immunity.*

**The Black Breath Duel** — interrupted a breath-technique user. ***Ash attacks rhythm, not just matter.***
**The Cinder Court Failure** — held too wide a field and collapsed from Cinder Lung Backlash. ***Range is a debt paid with his lungs.***
**I–VII complete.** *Stage VII Refraction — ash field split across particle motion, heat and breath disruption simultaneously.* VIII Transcendence pending.
> **Fracture · The First Red Haze** — Vitality-Tolerance. Healed-reinforced. *Feature: Tolerance densified. The body learned to treat ash as medium, not threat.*
>
> **Fracture · The Cinder Court Failure** — Harmonics-Attunement. Processed, partial scar. *Feature: Attunement narrowed then sharpened. Holds less field — what he holds is denser.*
> **Fracture · Dougou's Shadow** — Tempering-Inheritance. **Accumulating. Feature undetermined.**
>
> ***When it fractures, it frees the ash from the fire — or proves he is only ever what his father left behind.***

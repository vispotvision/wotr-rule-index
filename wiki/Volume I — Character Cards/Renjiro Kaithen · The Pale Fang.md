### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Ash and the red thread. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Renjiro Kaithen was born in the Senoth Kingdom, in the hills that send Vellun copper down to the smelters. His father drove the ore carts. Copper from those hills conducts well and melts far too easily, and every smith who bought it knew both things.
The Ash Warden Corps took the boy when he was old enough to hold a sword. It put the blade in one of his hands and the ash in the other. It taught him both by showing.

#### The Making

A warden showed him a form. He copied it, and it held, and he was proud of it. It was never his. He learned the cut that way, and the guard, and the breath of burning ash that blinds a man and chokes him. He wore the Warden's Mantle, armour he inherited from the Corps, which turns a direct Wellspring discharge for a short while before it ruptures. All of it held.
Then, in a bout inside the Corps, he built something out of his own understanding. He put ash into the other man's guard and let it work. A few exchanges later the blade that had been blocking him was failing on its own. Nobody had shown him that. He did it again to check, and it held. Then he sat down.
The folk call that coming into the road. Renjiro left the Corps and took the road.

#### The Cost

The ash worked slowly. Every guard he rotted he first had to outlast, and it worked on made things and left flesh alone. He had left the Corps with a sword, a Mantle and nothing he could command. He took hire where he found it.
He kept the Bone-Sealed Vows. They barred him from striking an unarmed child or anyone he owed a debt to, and breaking them would bleed Essence out of his Soul Crystal. A favour could disarm him. So could a rescue. So could a gift he had accepted. He asked no favours and accepted no gifts, and when a rescue came that he had not asked for, he paid it back at the first chance he had.
On the Glass Coast he rode with an Outer World warlord's raid against a copper enclave, beside Spoil. The Kethra price on Spoil had drawn two hunters. Zahir Ashveil reached the raid first, in the last heat of the day, and went for Spoil. Renjiro met him on the glass. For a few exchanges two men who worked the same ash worked it at each other. Then the sea fog came in with the dark, and Kaerith Vireth came out of it.
Kaerith tried to claim Kyru's Memory for the Concord archives. Renjiro kept it. The spirit parried what came at him out of the fog. Once the frost came at him from a side he had not covered, and Raktabija's blood stood in its way. From then on he owed Spoil, and his vows barred him from striking them. Before the fog lifted he took a strike of Kaerith's frost on the Warden's Mantle to cover Spoil, and he counted the debt square. Spoil saw him pay it.

#### Where They Stand

Renjiro holds a place in no order. He is a wandering sword-for-hire, and he still fights Outer World raids beside Spoil. Each respects the other's lethality. Distrust runs deep between them, and both of them know he owes Spoil nothing.
Kyru's Memory whispers to him in battle. It warns him of killing intent and parries the strikes he does not see. Nobody knows who Kyru was. Kaerith has tried to claim the spirit for the Concord archives and has not taken it, and the rivalry stands.
His words: "Steel cuts flesh, ash rots steel, but memory… memory rots the soul."
He has sparred with Ymir Rhok on many occasions, his steel technique against Ymir's raw force.
The Accord's surveyors cannot find the kingdom whose Corps he served. Its copper still arrives.

# Rik'Lye · The Forbidden Vessel

## Rik'Lye · The Forbidden Vessel

> *"You should not have opened me."*

---

### I · Identity

**Name** · Rik'Lye. **Called** · The Forbidden Vessel. **DANGER, DO NOT OPEN.**
**Role** · **Alchemical construct: an Aether-reactive homunculus made to recreate a soul from fractured Essence. Contained by Guild Accord mandate. Alive, aware, and uncontainable if directly provoked.**
**Race** · **Alchemical Construct.** *Towering and lean, with the unsettling symmetry of deliberate creation. Ash-pale skin marked by alchemical glyphs that pulse red or blue with mood. Molten silver irises in black sclera, flecked with fractal Aether corrosion. Black hair with an indigo sheen ending in flame-like tufts; horns of crystallised Aetheric bone curving back like broken crescents. Chains hang from shoulders and waist, for balance rather than binding.* The markings on his trousers read 破礼存, *the breaking that preserves*, in the lineage-hall register.
**Epithet** · *"DANGER, DO NOT OPEN."*
**Gloss rights** · **Never.** *He speaks in the cadence of old scripture, as though reading thoughts he does not understand, and a man reading aloud from a page he cannot parse names nothing he does.*

#### Affiliation

**Unaligned.** Created in the laboratories beneath **Niran's Sanctum** *(unattested)*. **Contained by Guild Accord mandate under the Crimson-Seal Order** *(unattested)*. Classified by the Accord as an **Alchemical Anomaly of the Ninth Order** *(unattested)*. *Niran listed his existence as an experimental containment success, a lie even the Guild refused to record openly.* Era: Withering Era, card date after VE 920.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage IX, Invocation.** *Calling on Wellsprings, Titans or Archonic law as co-authors; the Domain consolidates into a Realm.*
> 
> **He did not call. He was built with the call already open.** *He cannot generate Aether; he takes it from below, through Abython, and the card suspects a dormant Titan-aligned pattern in him, possibly Zar'thuun's.* A construct whose every working is co-authored by a current he did not choose, with a Titan's law suspected underneath, is at Invocation from the moment he woke, and **the Crucible Flesh is what a Realm looks like when its author is an imitation soul staring back at its maker.** *Every moment of awareness erodes his containment sigils. Self-understanding is physical decay.*

### II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class VI · Voidic, lateral.** *The inverted Shell devours, remembers, and reflects instead of emitting. Contact destabilises Temperance fields. He hums when angry, a resonance too harmonic to be natural.*
**Attraction Layer** · Bled toward whoever stands nearest. *He mirrors the emotions of those around him and cannot generate his own; the Layer is a surface, and other people's wants are what it shows.*
**Crystal State** · **Fractured, synthetic.** *An obsidian helix around a hollow light well; drains Essence rather than radiating it; emits faint choral murmurs during invocation.* **Soul Crystal** · Radiant. **η** · ~0.72, Band A.
**Essence Typology** · **Spatium dominant, Vitalia undertone.** *Change of basis under conservation, with a pseudo-life held far from equilibrium by forced expenditure.*
**Essence** · Leaks in mercury streams that coil upward into sigils of containment. **He does not bleed.**
**The Alchemetrica of him** · *A crucible is a Soul Crystal analogue for inanimate matter, and the Alchemer ran the analogue in reverse: matter made to carry a Crystal. His Essence is stabilised by alchemical matrices rather than by any alignment of his own.*

### III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Transmutatio** · Spatium. *The Law of Change.* Change of basis under conservation: the same object described in two frames yields two sets of numbers and one unchanged object; nothing is created or lost, only re-expressed. **Matter melted or restructured through touch is matter re-described in a basis where it is something else. The Conversion Field is everything touched re-expressed as base Essence.** *Failure: basis loss. The practitioner forgets which frame he started in, and a transformation with no reference frame returns nothing recognisable. His pseudo-soul destabilises with every use because the frame he started in was never his.*
**Mortalis** · Vitalia. *The Black Gate.* The non-equilibrium steady state and the thermodynamic boundary of its collapse: a living body is held far from equilibrium by continuous expenditure, and death is the cessation of the expenditure. **Pseudo-life sustained by forced Essence reformation; Soul Rebind rebuilds tissue by spending the latent Essence around him.** *Failure: equilibration. Sustained proximity to the boundary reduces the gradient the practitioner is himself maintaining, and he cools toward it without noticing. Twelve hours off the Abython flux and the gradient is gone.*
**Abython** · Vectoria. *The Abyssal Pulse.* Pressure equalisation: a vessel at depth survives by matching the water, because net stress is a difference and a difference of zero loads nothing. **He works where others suffocate because he maintains no differential at all; the partial immunity to destruction is a Shell that has stopped opposing.** *Failure: rapid ascent. Return to ordinary Aetheric density too fast and the equalised interior comes out of solution.*
> *Three Families, no two the same. Spatium, Vitalia and Vectoria integrate at higher Essence cost, and he pays that cost in stolen Aether. The card lists three currents and the sheet keeps three; the Essence Inversion trait is the Voidic Shell and not a fourth harmonisation.*

### VIII · Signature

#### Echo of Abython

**Effect** · **A tear opens to the Abyssal Pulse and everything within five metres is erased.** *Abython trains the Shell to maintain no differential; the Echo extends that condition outward for the width of a room. A living body is a structure held far from equilibrium by continuous expenditure, and inside the tear there is no differential to hold it: what was kept apart from nothing equalises with nothing. The Conversion Field does the same to matter through touch, more slowly.*
**Cost** · **His pseudo-soul destabilises with every use of Transmutatio, and the Echo is the deepest draw he has.** *Every wound the working costs him is healed by Soul Rebind, and every heal is a memory; the chains pay for the tear.*
**Limit** · **Five metres, and brief.** *The card's own weaknesses: purified currents disrupt his cohesion; he cannot cross sanctified Wellspring zones without implosion risk; disconnection from the Abython flux starves him inside twelve hours.*
**Counter** · **Cure the ground before he touches it.** *Fixatio's hold is a state with no reachable alternative, and Transmutatio can only re-express what has somewhere else to go; Ascensio spreads a transition across a longer path, and a tear needs the short one. The card names both.* **Or bring him up too fast:** Abython fails on rapid ascent, and a construct flooded with dense Aether after equalising to nothing comes out of solution. **Or feel something at him:** he cannot generate emotion, he can only mirror it, and enough of it breaks him down at the molecular level.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether Niran encoded her consciousness in his Core. *The Guild fears it. Prophecy tablets recovered from the Sanctum read: "When the Vessel learns sorrow, the Wellspring will bleed."*

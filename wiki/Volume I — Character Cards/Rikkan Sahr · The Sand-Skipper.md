# Rikkan Sahr · The Sand-Skipper

> *"If you see dust on the horizon, it's either a storm or me. And I'm worse for whoever's chasing you."*
> **The card states no Temperance Stage.** **Stage VI — Glory assigned and flagged as originated.** *"Wind / Sand / Luck"* were not Essence types.
>
> ***And the Stage explains the card's own hedge.*** *It calls his Luck "Fate-Bending / Unstable."* **Dominion Fate is Fate-gated at Stage VII to exceed S-Grade, and Rikkan is at VI** — *below the gate.* **His luck is unstable because he has not passed the Threshold that would stabilise it.**

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Rikkan Sahr. **Titles** · **The Sand-Skipper.** *Horizon Courier of the Concord.*
**Role** · **Nomadic Scout. Wellspring Courier. Relic Retrieval Specialist.**

### Affiliation

**Logistics & Supply Division** — *messenger and secure delivery runner between Concord-controlled Wellsprings, particularly across Outer World frontiers where Domain stability is low.* Also does freelance smuggling for Zahiren Vahl when the Arbitration Division needs "off-the-record" transfers.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VI, Glory.** *Willingly accepting genuine soul-cost to preserve something valued above his own structural integrity — a decision, made in advance, in cold blood.*
>
> **A courier who runs Outer World frontiers where Domain stability is low, and who can outrun a Domain collapse**, has made that decision at least once. *The card does not say what was in the satchel.*

## VII · Signature

### The Dune's Breath

**Effect** · **A path of speed and adaptability**, drawing on Wellsprings that resonate with motion. *Capable of outrunning certain Domain collapses and surviving Essence storms by instinctively reading their flow.*
**Cost** · **The Gambit only holds while he is moving.** *The moment he stops, the luck stops with him.*
**Limit** · **He cannot fight.** Ardency 118.
**Counter** · **Make him stand still.** *Anything that pins, anchors, or encircles removes his entire kit at once.*

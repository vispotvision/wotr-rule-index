# Rikudoku Moto

## Rikudoku Moto

**Lineage:** Moto, Archaic Bloodline. **Kōkan Line.** Son of Sodoku Moto and Yoko Mishiro.
**Age:** 13.
**Class:** X, Aetherion Academy.

### Physical Description

Tall for a Moto. Six foot three at minimum, possibly four. Dense, long-limbed frame, broad through the shoulder, heavy through the chest. Built the way his lineage builds its warriors, except the warriors usually level off at six feet and Rikudoku did not receive the memo. He has Muken's frame rather than Sodoku's; Sodoku is five-eleven and technique-built, and his son outgrew him before thirteen.
Deep brown skin, Moto-dark. White hair, flat Moto-white, falling to his shoulders unbrushed. The white is the standard Moto expression that Sodoku's near-black deviated from; in Rikudoku the bloodline reverted. The tips are black, as though the pigment started at one end and reversed halfway through, white giving way to ink at the final inch like snow meeting tree-line. Whether the black tips carry the Kōkan violet-cast in direct light is unconfirmed.
Eyes half-closed. Perpetually. Genuine drowsiness that sits on his body like a second gravity. Jaw loose. Stride long but unhurried, each step placed by feel because sight has been downgraded to advisory.
Eye colour unspecified. Sodoku carries amber-gold with the Kamigan's ring-lines visible even passive. Whether Rikudoku has inherited the Kamigan is the first open question.
Academy coat unbuttoned and slightly askew.
**Maternal expression, open.** His mother is Yoko Mishiro, Fox-Spirit Beastkin of the Mishiro lineage (ruled 2026-09-18). Every line of his only archived description is Moto and nothing in it is hers. What he carries of the Mishiro line, whether it is absent, suppressed, or present in something the scenes have not looked at, is assigned to Isaac and is not to be invented in prose.

### Psychology

Arrived in Class X, sat down, put his head down, and departed consciousness within four seconds. Did not introduce himself. Did not react to the Headmaster's fire entrance, the broom crash, Frithia's authority, or any of the introductions.
The son of the Exiled Lion, the Kōkan Butcher, the man who burned the Tenrai main-branch settlement. Whether Rikudoku carries that name openly or has been sheltered from it is unestablished. Whether the sleepiness is genuine exhaustion, suppression of something active, or the particular indifference of a boy who grew up in the orbit of a Level 320 practitioner and finds a classroom full of thirteen-year-old prodigies beneath his threshold of interest is unestablished.
Mu-jin looked at him, opened his mouth, closed it, and chose another battle. Mu-jin knows whose son this is.

### Abilities

Unestablished. The Kōkan Line carries:
- **The Kamigan (Crown Eye):** continuous, unclosable threat-classification system. Present in Sodoku's irises at birth. Whether Rikudoku has inherited it is the first question.
- **Native intimacy with Hataraki (the Plane of Fate):** verdict, ranking, condemnation. The techniques administer rather than explode.
- **The Totality and the Ruins:** the two governing Works of the Kōkan inheritance.
The Mishiro side is a second unexplored inheritance. Yoko's own card carries the Aetheric Nose; whether any Mishiro capacity reached her son is unestablished and assigned to Isaac.
None of this has been demonstrated on the page. His placement in Class X implies the Academy identified something significant, and his bloodline alone would justify the placement, but the scenes have not shown what he can do.

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card, the three archived scenes it appears in, and the record of Class X and what it grows into. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Rikudoku Moto was born of the Kōkan Line, to Sodoku Moto and Yoko Mishiro, a fox-spirit of the Mishiro lineage. His card sets his conception in the Scourge of Hell years, when his father was roughly twenty-two and neither parent had named what they were to each other. The archive tells the birth at Kharven-Seat in the Thin Weeks of the fourth year, after his half-sister Hild Ice had died in the cistern doorway. The two counts do not agree.
The room was a low one off the altar-room, with the night-stone ticking in the corner as it cooled. Brida Ashwell ran it from a stool. Emira Moto, his father's younger sister, had the left side. At the fifteenth hour there was a great deal of blood, and at the ninth minute it stopped on its own, for a reason Brida declined to invent. He came out white-haired and furious.
His father had stood in the corridor for fifteen hours, and Emira carried the infant out to him, which nothing required of her. Sodoku took him badly, with both hands too far apart, and then not badly. He looked once at the door frame, at about the height of a twelve-year-old's shoulder, and back to the child's face. They called him Rikudoku.
The Reckoner's form had one slot for a bloodline. His father told the man to enter both, and the birth-form used across the north now carries two. That week his mother wrote in her own record: "My son is the heir of the oldest bloodline in the world and half of him is the thing that world puts in a pen."

#### The Making

He grew into his grandfather's frame, dense and long-limbed, broad through the shoulder and heavy through the chest. His father was built lighter, for technique. The son outgrew him before thirteen. His hair stayed the flat Moto-white he was born with, running to ink at the final inch.
Fighting came easily to him. Before the Academy there was one bout that went too far. An older boy asked him to wrestle, and Rikudoku, already heavier than most grown men, came down on him with all of it. The boy's collarbone broke. Rikudoku sat in the dirt beside him until the adults came, and after that he wrestled nobody on his own asking.
The Aetherion Academy placed him in Class X. His bloodline alone would have justified it, and what else the Academy saw in him no scene has shown.
On the first morning he came through the door last and filled the frame. His eyes were half-closed. He crossed the room by feel, sat, put his head down and was asleep within four seconds. He did not introduce himself. He did not react to the Headmaster's fire entrance, the broom crash, Frithia's authority or any of the introductions. Mu-jin looked at him, opened his mouth, closed it, and chose another battle.

#### The Cost

He was still carried on his mother's hip when Sayo Shirogane published her objection to his father's union of the Moto lines. Its third argument concerned him. The empire's law, she wrote, still filed his second inheritance under a schedule written for property, which made him at once the sovereign's son and an item in a register. His father read it twice in the map room, set it face down under the inkstand and split wood until it was properly dark. Yoko stood on the step with the boy on her hip, in four layers of wool. Each time the maul fell, he turned toward the sound.
He had entered no fight of his own choosing since the collarbone. When the tournament bracket lit on the slate, a chair complained under him, and his head came up off the desk one degree at a time. He did not want to hurt anyone in that room. Most of them had not yet said their names to him. "If I have to," he said, to nobody, and put his cheek back down on his forearm.

#### Where They Stand

Rikudoku is thirteen and in Class X, and the Class X Open Tournament is seven days out. His coat hangs unbuttoned and a little askew. His eyes stay half-closed. His father's irises carry the Kamigan's ring-lines even at rest. Nobody has yet seen what his carry, or what he carries of his mother's line.
He is the son of the Exiled Lion, the Kōkan Butcher, the man who burned the Tenrai main-branch settlement. Mu-jin knows whose son he is. What the boy knows of the massacre and the exile, he has told no one in Class X.
Other cards carry the name further. The Wandering Fang's card counts Rikudoku Moto a philosophical ally. His son Kairen, estranged from him and revering him, carries the Branch of Rikudoku, a living cutting from the Great Spirit Tree. "My father grew the forest," Kairen says. How he grew it, and how the Branch came to his son, no record says.

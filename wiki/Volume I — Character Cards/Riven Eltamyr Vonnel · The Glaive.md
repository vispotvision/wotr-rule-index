# Riven Eltamyr Vonnel · The Glaive

> *"When the final petal falls, the law is all that remains."*
> **Dead canon, twice, in his origin.** *He is Lurien-born, raised in the Holy See of Lurien.*
>
> **The Holy See of Lurien was struck and replaced by the Holy Sea of Alabaster.** *Riven is the third sheet in this batch anchored to Lurien — alongside Thalyren Veyroot and Gonju — and the fourth overall. This is now the most frequently recurring dead-canon reference in Volume IV and wants a single re-homing ruling rather than four.*
> **Stage corrected.** The card reads **"IX — Judgment (brushing the border of Revelation)."** *Judgment is not among the sixteen; Revelation is Stage XV, six above his numeral.* **Numeral stands: Stage IX, Invocation.**
>
> **"Mind Path"** is not one of the four — Body, Spirit, Attraction, Fate. *"Elegance / Verdure / Silence"* are descriptors rather than register names. **"Aether Class: Adjudicant"** is not among the eight; **he is Class IV, Luminous.**

**Affiliation** · **Guild Accord — Arbitration Division, Neutral Chamber Subsect.** *Carries Code 9-L authority, empowered to deliver singular execution rulings when Concord balance demands it.*
**Profile** · *A lethal but disciplined arbiter-duelist who wields elegance and violence in equal measure.* His reputation extends across kingdoms as a feared enforcer, a respected diplomat's tool, and a mistrusted adjudicator whose mercy is structured, not sentimental.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage IX, Invocation.** *Something larger answers, and it is a co-author.*
>
> **The Rosette Court is his personal Domain and his entire Wellspring network — the card states it as "1, self-contained."**
>
> ***He asked once, and what answered was a courtroom.*** *And the recorded risk of Invocation applies: the co-author did not sign anything, which for a man whose authority is legal is a specific and unpleasant exposure.*

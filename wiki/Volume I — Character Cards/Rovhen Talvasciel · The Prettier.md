### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Withering courts. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Rovhen Talvasciel was born Avian in the Withering Era, of the Crimsoncrest Line. The Line was marked by vibrant plumage, aggressive mating displays and a hierarchy built on dominance, and its people came by precise movement and a persuasive voice without being taught either. His wings came in crimson. He kept them for display, for distraction and, later, for trapping. He did not use them to fly.
Under the Accord's Beastkin Classification Provision, an Avian's standing changed from one territory to the next. Where the Labor Provision ran, a beastkin who could show no guild membership, no formal employment and no recognised bloodline could be bought and held in a licensed pen.
He saw where a body would fail before he had words for it. The fracture lines ran along bones, nerves and organs, and he could say where each would open. In a line that ranked its people by what they showed, he read those lines first in the faces around him.

#### The Making

He trained his hands before anything else. He learned the sculpting, severing and reconstruction of living tissue, at the pace surgery allows. He sealed his work with crimson thread, and the work held. His wings came to shed fine blades that could peel flesh, pin it and thread it into place. Behind him coiled the Needlewing Halo, thin crimson-gold wires curled like feathers, each one a floating blade. Watching him work quickened the pulse and brought on a faint nausea. He intended both.
He learned to ask as well. A simple request in his voice sounded irresistible until the scalpel followed. He had no need of force.
The Queen of Talveris made him her court enchanter. Talveris stood among the western courts, where the kingdoms built in pale stone, and he kept a workroom in her palace. For an Avian, the post was formal employment.
There his soul saw itself from outside. Looking at it changed it, and knowing that it changed it changed it again. He had been handed his own architecture to examine, and he examined it. He never learned to stop. Then he began doing it to other people.

#### The Cost

The Doctrine of Divine Symmetry was that self-examination turned outward and given a scalpel. He looked at a body and saw the equation it had failed to solve. He corrected it with thread and blade, and with a voice that made the correction sound reasonable while it happened.
The queen sat for him in his workroom, and he "redesigned" her entire face. Her council met that night and sat until morning. By morning it had agreed, member by member, that there would be no trial. The court enchanter would go and not come back. He left that day, alive and untried, with the Halo's wires coiled at his back. Exile took the post, and with it the employment that had answered for him wherever the Labor Provision ran. Nothing records that he knows whom the council sent for before midnight.
Some days later the Accord sent Seri Zeliro-Vanthe and her partner to read the palace. Her lantern's blue flame burned brightest beside the chair where the queen had sat, and her account of his workroom went into the file the Accord keeps on him.
The Avian aristocrats came to loathe and fear him for desecrating bloodlines "in the name of style." The Order of Fleshbound Law counted him a living blasphemy.
His mirror did not discriminate. He could return a victim's wounds to the victim in reversed pattern, always prettier than the original. A faithful mirror held up in a room full of people showed him to them as well.
He worked close, and he worked slowly. He never stood in a fair fight. He could find the failure in any body he looked at, and his own body could bear little of what he did to others. Nobody had ever applied his Doctrine to him.

#### Where They Stand

Rovhen Talvasciel is called the Prettier and the Butcher-Saint of Form, and he calls himself High Priest of the Doctrine of Divine Symmetry. The Guild Accord hunts him and files him as an Aesthetic Predator, with Seri's reading of his workroom in the file. Avian aristocrats loathe and fear him. The Order of Fleshbound Law still counts him a blasphemy.
His words to the people he works on are these: "Feathers are flawless. You are not. Let me fix that."
Rumour says he rewrote an entire village into one long, walking tapestry of faces. Nobody knows whether it is rumour.
His own Doctrine has never been applied to him. He has never been in a fair fight, and the answer to him is one competent hit.

# Rovhen Talvasciel · The Prettier

> *"You're almost divine. All I need to do is remove… everything wrong with you."*
> **Stage corrected — "Stage VI — Refraction" in the legacy sheet; Refraction is Stage VII.** *The description is explicit Refraction imagery: "his Essence splits perception into symmetrical paths."*
>
> *"Form / Blood / Charm-Aether"* were not Essence types. **"Aether Class: Symmetrist Surgeon"** is not among the eight; **he is Class III, Resonant.**

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Rovhen Talvasciel. **Called** · The Prettier. **Butcher-Saint of Form.**
> *"Feathers are flawless. You are not. Let me fix that."*
**Race** · **Avian, Crimsoncrest Line.** *An exotic bloodline marked by vibrant plumage, aggressive mating displays and hyper-dominance hierarchy. Natural affinity for precision movement and vocal influence.* **Crimson wings used not for flight, but for display, distraction, and trapping.**
**Role** · **Avian Aestheticist. Essence Surgeon. Beauty Cultist.**

### Affiliation

**Former court enchanter to the Queen of Talveris** — *exiled after he "redesigned" her entire face.* Self-declared **High Priest of the Doctrine of Divine Symmetry.** **Hunted by the Guild Accord under internal classification T3: Aesthetic Predator (Surgical Alignment).**
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VII, Refraction.** *The soul sees itself from outside — and looking at it changes it, and knowing that it changes it changes it again. Practitioners in Refraction are notoriously difficult to be around because they have just been handed the ability to examine their own architecture and have not yet learned to stop.*
>
> ***Rovhen never learned to stop, and then began doing it to other people.*** **The Doctrine of Divine Symmetry is Refraction's self-examination turned outward and given a scalpel.**

## II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class III · Resonant.** **Crystal State** · Refined. **η** · ~0.60, Band C.
**The Crimson Atelier** · *A gallery of skinned beauty — a mirrored palace of preserved perfection. Every thread of his Essence is red silk laced with finality.*

---

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Anima Spirare** · Vitalia. **Bright arterial red** — *the most viscerally uncomfortable Family colour to observe, because the hue triggers the mammalian recognition-response to blood.* **The observer's pulse accelerates and there is a faint nausea that has nothing to do with the magic.** *For Rovhen that is not a side effect. It is the intended reception.*
**Recarvu-adjacent Vitalia work** · The sculpting, severing and reconstruction of living tissue.
**Mirithane** · Limina. **Specular reflection — a true mirror adds nothing.** *Reflected Ruin: he returns a victim's wounds in reversed pattern, "always prettier than the original."* *And the failure is that the mirror does not discriminate.*
**Fixatio** · Materia. **Anchors and seals.** The crimson thread, and the reason his work holds.

## VII · Signature

### The Doctrine of Divine Symmetry

**Effect** · He looks at a body and **sees the equation it failed to solve** — then corrects it, with thread and blade and a voice that makes the correction sound reasonable while it happens.
**Cost** · **Mirithane does not discriminate.** *Reflected Ruin returns wounds faithfully — and a faithful mirror held up in a room full of people shows him to them as well.*
**Limit** · **It requires proximity and time.** *Surgery is not fast.*
**Counter** · **Hit him once, properly.** *Resilience 180 against an S-Grade Gnosis is the widest survivable gap on any sheet in this volume.*

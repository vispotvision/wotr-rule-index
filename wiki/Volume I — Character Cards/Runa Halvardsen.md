## XIII · Physical Description
Runa wears dark brown hair the colour of wet bark, cut blunt just below the jaw because longer hair catches in ties and bowls. Her face is long through the cheek and broad at the jaw, with a nose once broken slightly to the left and pale grey eyes that settle first on hands, lips and breathing. She is broad through the back and forearms rather than heavily built, with the ropey wrists of someone who wrings cloth, hauls water and holds struggling patients still. Her fingers are cracked at the knuckles from lye, cold and repeated washing; the left thumb bears a white crescent scar from a slipped bone saw. Her hide-coat is old, smoke-dark at the shoulders and shiny where her right hand catches the belt. Beneath it she wears wool layers repeatedly darned at the elbows. A narrow leather roll at her hip carries needles, linen thread, a small knife, probes, a spoon, folded cloth and two stoppered bottles whose contents change with what remains.

## XIV · Psychology
Runa wants her settlement through the Thin Weeks with fewer graves than the winter before. She is competent because competence is cheaper than mourning, and unsentimental because tears freeze as readily as wash-water. She does not romanticise suffering and distrusts anyone who does. Her refusal is absolute: no living patient is to be abandoned because an elder, husband, wife or household head has done arithmetic over the stores. The fracture in that belief is already present. When supplies are low she has begun, privately and almost automatically, deciding which patient is most likely to justify the cloth, broth, salt or medicine spent on them. She has not admitted that this is the same arithmetic with a cleaner hand.  
**Voice:** short, concrete, dry. She names what a body is doing and what must be done next. She never speaks mystically about illness.  
**Lying / withholding tell:** she cleans, folds or re-wraps an instrument that is already clean.  
**Private belief that costs her:** usefulness and survival are becoming difficult for her to keep separate.

## Lore
Runa is designed for a low-magic Kharven side story where the danger begins with ordinary winter arithmetic. Her competence should be attractive before it becomes frightening. Her scenes notice breath, colour, hands, fuel, cloth, food, smell and whether a room is warm enough to keep blood moving. She does not need supernatural power to become dangerous.

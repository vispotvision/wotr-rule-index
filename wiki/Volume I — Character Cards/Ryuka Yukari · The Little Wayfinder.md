# Ryuka Yukari · The Little Wayfinder

## Ryuka Yukari · The Little Wayfinder

> *"If there is a road, I will find it. If there is not, I will make one and come back for you."*

---

### I · Identity

**Name** · Ryuka Yukari. **Called** · **The Little Wayfinder.** *Fox of the First Step · The Orange Spark.*
**Epithet** · He Who Runs Between Footsteps.
**Role** · **Beastkin Ward. Wayfinder-in-training. Unofficial mascot of the Guild Accord.**
**Look & tells** · 128 cm, lean and spry, bright fluffy orange hair, heterochromatic eyes (Yukari gold, Mahuo violet), large fox ears, a tail that wags when he finds a route.
**Gloss rights** · **Unlimited.** *He is thirteen and has never learned to withhold anything about how his gift works; he will explain a shortcut to a stranger mid-sprint if they ask.*
**Age** · Thirteen.

#### Affiliation

**Independent, Ward of the Mahuo Estate.** Yukari Family on his father's side, Mahuo Estate on his mother's, unofficial mascot to the **Guild Accord**.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage I, Murmuring.** *The starting Stage carries no Threshold; it is the awakening spark itself.*
> 
> **Nothing has called on him hard enough yet to fracture him.** The card gives a child whose gift runs on curiosity rather than crisis, and the sheet does not manufacture a cost he has not paid.

### II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Unclassed (Class emerges at Glory).** The awakening Shell flickers with first resonance; Essence leaks unevenly, which the card already calls **Spatial Drift** — strong emotion skipping him sideways into Spirit Layers or minor Domains he did not mean to enter.
**Attraction Layer** · Undeveloped. *A warm presence rather than a bond; standing near him nudges strangers toward optimism, nothing more structured than that.*
**Crystal State** · Dormant network, awakening surface. **η** · ~0.40, Coherence Band F.
**On scene** · Orange foxfire leaves fading paw-prints or glowing trails in the air; his excitement smells faintly of citrus and ozone.

### III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Cymorath** · Fulguria, Electromagnetism. **Governing** *(corrected from Mirithane).* Vector fields and the Lorentz force: motion read as a field rather than a room, which is the actual mechanism behind finding the "path of least resistance" through a crowd, a ward, or difficult terrain.
**Luminalis** · Fulguria, Electromagnetism. **Secondary.** *Light, visibility, wavelength.* The orange foxfire trail and the glow he cannot help giving off when happy or frightened.
**Ignivale** · Caloria, Thermodynamics. **Tertiary.** *The current that holds temperatures charcoal cannot reach*, read small and warm here rather than as a forge — the comfort in his presence, and the warmth in the color of his light.
**Hypnather** · Limina, Entropy and Mind. **Backlash-only.** *Structural relaxation during genuine rest, forced rather than chosen.* Excessive use of the Shortcut triggers involuntary sleep, the card's own "Nap Crash."

### VIII · Signature

#### The Shortcut

**Effect** · **He slips between two points along a hidden path Cymorath's vector-field reading finds for him**, closing one to two meters in what looks, to anyone watching, like a flicker or a skip in the frame. Enough to bypass a locked door or a swinging sword.
**Cost** · **Calorie burn, immediate and severe.** Movement-based Aether drains his physical stamina directly, and he needs a high-calorie meal right after or the next Shortcut is simply not there to take.
**Limit** · **Only where a vector actually exists.** A smooth, featureless surface with no path variation offers Cymorath nothing to read, and the Shortcut fails outright.
**Counter** · **A containment field with no vectors to offer** stops him cold, the same way a shiny distraction or an interesting smell can override his tactical instincts before a fight even starts. He is thirteen, and both failure modes are real.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the Spatial Drift risk means his next accidental skip lands him somewhere the Mahuo Estate cannot follow.

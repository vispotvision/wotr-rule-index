# Sadamu, called Futakoto — Two Words

*Two Words · Polar Reach Scout*
> 📛 Named Sadamu, called Futakoto, by Isaac's ruling of 2026-09-12 (R23-7, bloodline route): a Yuno retainer; futakoto is literally two words, so the epithet survives intact. Formerly Afasoa.
> **Full Name:** Sadamu (formerly Afasoa: afa + soa = storm + companion)
> **Age:** 18 · **Bloodline:** Moto (frontier cadet)
> **Level:** 156 · **Stage:** III Hold · **Band:** II
> **Role:** Forward scout, perimeter watch for the Kōkan expedition
> The frontier oath is two words. The two words hold everything.

---

Young, lean, the ranginess of a frontier Moto raised on the Polar Reach where calories are scarce and movement is constant. Black hair cropped to the skull in military style. Thin scar across the nose bridge from cheekbone to cheekbone — a training accident that taught distance. Light coat over nothing, his tolerance for cold marking him as Reach-raised. Moto field knife at his belt, short-bladed, forward-curved.
Speaks in the frontier register where words are expensive because breath is expensive because cold taxes every exhalation. His oath was two words: *"I swear."* The shortest oath on the ice. The two words held the same content as everyone else's paragraph, compressed into the smallest possible container, because frontier containers hold more than they look like they can hold.
When the Titan Droval appeared, Sadamu drew his knife, performed the arithmetic, and put the knife away. The knife was an insult.

# Saetsha Bloomveil · The Pink Tempest

## Saetsha Bloomveil · The Pink Tempest

> *"Until she answers, my spear will ask the question in her name."*

---

### I · Identity

**Name** · Saetsha Bloomveil. **Called** · The Pink Tempest. **Also known as** · Herald of the Vanished Bough, Rose-Spear Attendant, Last Bell of Elyndra. **Epithet** · She Who Keeps Vigil for a Silent Archon.
**Race** · Eressean elf, Archonic-contact transfigured.
**Role** · **Former Handmaiden and Herald of Elyndra.** **Gloss rights** · Unlimited; declaring is the entire discipline, and an Edict spoken quietly is an Edict spoken wrong.

#### Affiliation

Former court herald of Elyndra, now a semi-autonomous operative loosely aligned with the Guild Accord, though she reserves the right to overrule any Concord where renewal and extinction hang in balance.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage IX, Invocation.** *The Threshold this Stage asks for is calling on Wellsprings, Titans, or Archonic law as co-authors, the moment a Domain consolidates into something closer to a Realm.* Saetsha crossed it the day she declared her first Edict in Elyndra's absent name and found that reality leaned toward the outcome anyway, with no Archon left to co-sign it.
> 
> **Elyndra went silent, and every Domain of Renewal fell dark with her, in every account Saetsha has.** No Guild or Accord record corroborates this beyond Saetsha's own testimony. She is not lying. She may simply be the only living witness to something the wider Lattice has not yet noticed, or the only person still listening at a frequency everyone else stopped checking.

### II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class IV · Luminous.** The lucid Shell thins with minimal prompting; Wellsprings answer her almost before she asks, a Spirit Path Shell that clarified ahead of its Core.
**Attraction Layer** · Fully developed and pointed at a single absent Archon. **Elyndra's mark** functions as her Axis; wounded ecosystems and dying spirits are drawn toward her for triage as a direct consequence of it.
**Crystal State** · Refined, unevenly. **η** · ~0.88, Band A (high for the Band; the Class IV exception applies).
**The dimming part of the Core** · A living echo of Elyndra's presence, drawn down with every Edict, quieter after each great working than it was before.

### III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Verdantia** · Vitalia. The green pulse, primary. *Growth as continuous law rather than a single event.*
**Anima Spirare** · Vitalia. The soul's breath. *Same-Family compounding with Verdantia is why healing and growth are one operation in her hands.*
**Luminalis** · Fulguria. The hidden light. *Sees what hides; her petal-sigils are Luminalis given a shape.*
**Somnalis** · Fluxia. Faint, networked. *Bridges into Elyndra's last known dream traces.*
**Refused** · Mortalis. She will not draw from it under any circumstance, believing that stealing mortality's lesson to fuel healing is the wound Elyndra suffered in the first place.

### VIII · Signature

#### Spear of Pruned Futures

**Effect** · She spins the glaive and marks a ring of petals; anything that steps fully inside while she holds the pattern is bound to a single foreclosed future. A blade that should land does not. A plague that should take does not.
**Cost** · Draws directly from the dwindling reserve of Elyndra's echo; the "voice" reads measurably quieter after every casting.
**Limit** · One foreclosed future per casting, named at the moment the ring closes. It cannot un-choose something that has already happened, and it cannot act on anything that never enters the ring.
**Counter** · Stay outside the ring, or overload it. One pattern forecloses one future; force more outcomes into the same exchange than a single Edict can cover.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether Saetsha chooses which future the ring denies, or whether something on the far side of Elyndra's silence is choosing it for her.

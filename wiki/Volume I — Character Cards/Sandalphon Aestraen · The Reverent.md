# Sandalphon Aestraen · The Reverent

> **This sheet needed the least correction of any migrated so far.** The legacy card is in the modern numbered format, carries Accord legal status, narrative function and a real vulnerability list. **Nothing structural was wrong.** What follows adds the Wellspring, Stage and stat layer it did not have.
> **Lineage flag.** This sheet identifies Sandalphon as **Saeloria's consort. Karo Venrik is not his son: Isaac ruled on 2026-09-12 that Karo Venrik is the son of Hiromi Mahuo and Saeloria, the Elven Queen.** Combined with House Mourcrix descending from House Venrik, that puts **three migrated Volume IV characters in one family** — Sandalphon, his son Karo, and their kinsman Vaelen — with Sanctum Lux and the Eressean Crown on both sides of it. **Confirm this is intended before anything is built on it.**

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of House Venrik and the Eressean crown. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Sandalphon Aestraen was born at Vaelmarr, in the Grand Principality of Eresse, to a lineage-line that held a rite-office in the Sunroot calendar. His people performed the rites of the turnings. An Eressean rite is sung in the same words, in the same order and in the same key, every window. A text without its Songscript rail can be read and cannot be performed, so the boy learned the rails before he learned the Common letters. His mother taught him from the house's rite calendar, which the family recopied without variation.
He first sang the Returning Light rite inside its window, with the witness named and paid. His voice was clear. It took the key it was given and added nothing.

#### The Making

The Sunroot Court made him Crown-Consort to Saeloria, and for a time he also held the regency of Eresse. He governed as he had been taught to sing, inside the windows. In the council chamber at Vaelmarr, built partly below grade, he heard the elders out while they debated through their fixed roles.
During the regency the chamber's wards began to drift out of tune whenever he sat in it. Whatever state he brought into that room, every elder in it felt with him. He had no way to keep one to himself. A rite performed in the wrong key has not been performed, and the chamber's rites were going out of key around its regent.
He swore the Pact of Iridescent Mourning inside its window, before witnesses who were paid. Under it he gave up the regency and the office of Crown-Consort. He undertook to leave no mark on the court he was leaving: no voice in its rites, no hand in its record. He left Vaelmarr on a Pilgrim-Writ.
He kept the calendar on the road. At each turning's notch he stopped where he stood and sang its rite, low, in the fixed key, to nobody. At Still Water he sang at a ford and walked on.
Terrain began keeping what he sang. Travellers who crossed that ford after him heard the rite in the water, in his voice and in its key. The passes behind him began to do the same. Wherever he had sung, the people who came after him were standing inside the song.

#### The Cost

The Pact took the regency, the office and the court, and it took his place in the rite his lineage was born to sing. The ground kept what the Pact had meant to erase. A man pledged to leave no mark now left one at every notch of the calendar. He went on singing the windows. He sang lower, away from roads.
His presence went on showing what he would rather have hidden. Wards mis-tuned around him, and whatever state he was in, the room was in with him. He had no private mourning anywhere a ward stood.
He learned to unmake a working by listening to it. When he stopped singing inside another practitioner's working, it stayed whole and correctly formed and described nothing. Everything he had carried into the room fell under the same stillness, his braid with it. He could cancel another's law. Against his own being cancelled he had no guard at all.
The rite undid him too. Many voices singing at once were more than he could listen to, and he kept out of any hall where a congregation sang.

#### Where They Stand

Sandalphon travels on a Pilgrim-Writ. Archives admit him only under escort. He is barred from officiating in the Neutral Chambers, and Atheron Dreymarch, who keeps them, keeps away from him.
He is Saeloria's consort still, with both offices gone. Through her he is kin by marriage to Karo Venrik, her son by Hiromi Mahuo and another exile from the same court, and to Asta, Karo's wife. Vaelen Mourcrix, the Imperial Inquisitor of Eresse, is counted among the family's kin.
The voice draws people. Varek Ka'ruun once tried to hire him as a battle-chanter. Hazzen Skollgrin said it "tastes like dreaming bells." Sandalphon refuses to be near him. Elder Seravain has offered to store some of his voices in his library. Sandalphon has not accepted.
Teal ribbons bind his braid, and each binds an Echo. Cut one and memory bleeds. Nobody knows how many ribbons there were at the start.

# Seiji Tenrai Moto · The Door

*Heir of the Tenrai Design · as of the muster on the road north*
> **Full Name:** Seiji Tenrai Moto
> **Race / Lineage:** Of the Tenrai line of the Moto house, whose Wellspring affinity is convergence; son of Azure Tenrai Moto
> **Role:** Holder of the design his father began against the Kōkan line. He presses the claim of Miku Tenrai Moto to the north, and rides beside Wren Greymane's muster and Kujo's generals.
> **Age:** Twenty-nine. He was sixteen when his father died.
> **As Of:** The morning of the muster, four riders already gone down the southern corridors.

---

## Description

He has the sort of face that agrees with whoever is nearest. Lean and dark-haired, handsome in the Tenrai way, angular and precise, proportions arranged to read as trustworthy before they read as anything else. His black hair is oiled and pulled back and cut level at the nape by somebody good. His jaw comes to a point without narrowing, and his skin is kept out of weather.
He wears lacquered plate in a red so dark it reads black until the sun finds it, and the fit of it is perfect, which only ever means it was made twice. He smiles warmly, and the smile reaches his eyes, because he spent years training it to.

---

## Voice

**Notices first** · What each man in front of him will believe, and whose hand signed what. He reads a room for the face it wants from him and puts that face on before he speaks.
**Sentence length** · Medium, even and pleasant. He lays out an arrangement clause by clause, then closes it with two short maxims; asked a question he does not mean to answer, he answers pleasantly and at length and says nothing.
**Contractions** · never. The Tenrai court speaks in whole words, and his warmth is built out of them.
**Pet word** · "Please," even inside an order that ends a village.
**Never says** · Where the boy is kept, what his design has cost in lives, or anything his face has not already agreed to. He never raises his voice. The only numbers he gives are his own arrangements (riders, routes, reports), read off like a manifest; the dead he does not count.
**Stumbles** · Never while the smile is working. When it fails, the sentence breaks apart with it.
**Gloss rights** · Never.
**Under stress** · The smile stays on too long and the courtesy keeps going after the room has turned; in real terror the words shorten and break into pleading with a lord's honorific on the end.
**In grief** · He is sixteen again, the day his father died at the settlement, and he falls back on his father's way: the face goes blank and he says nothing at all.
**In joy** · Nothing changes, because the warm smile is always there; real pleasure shows only in the hands, a pen set down with care, a letter sealed slowly.
**Sample line** · "They will believe two of them, which is better. A man who believes everything reconsiders. A man who believes half of it commits."

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

#### Origin

His father Azure spent sixteen years making his face say nothing, the face as a wall. Seiji was sixteen when the Kōkan Line's two surviving sons walked into the settlement and did what the sealed archive held, and the settlement consumed his father with it. He took the lesson in place of the body. A face that says nothing is noticed saying nothing, so he made his face say what people want to see.

#### The Making

For thirteen years he built on that foundation from a desk seven hundred miles south of Kharven-Seat. He did not recruit agents. He recruited circumstances: the channel his father designed and he refined runs on the places where a merchant's route crosses a garrison's supply schedule, and it cannot be interrogated because it is not a person. He placed Valdís Ashmore inside the Seat. He sent the Amagiri family of a nine-year-old girl, Yuza, to a Tenrai holding in the south and left the child on the eastern road with a blue wooden bear, so that her bloodline would record the Seat's Essence without her knowing it. In his drawer lay a letter sealed with the Tenrai sigil, one word long, written in his dead father's hand, which he had practised for years.
When the Kōkan brothers came back from exile, his smile did not change.

#### The Cost

The design cost the north perhaps two hundred thousand lives. Wren Greymane rode beside him for thirteen months and never heard him raise his voice, and watched him give an instruction that ended a village: four words, and one of them was please. Wren asked once where the boy was kept, and was answered pleasantly and at length and told nothing.
Sodoku's wife Freya died in a chair over four days because Seiji wanted one sentence from her, where Sodoku was, which Sodoku had made sure she never had.
At the muster he sent four riders down four corridors with three reports of a march on the Stormfold road and one of the Brine crossing already taken, all signed by men whose hands the clerks knew. *"They will believe two of them, which is better."* Then his column marched straight for the Seat. At the Ashgate narrows Wren's six hundred turned on the Tenrai in four seconds.

#### Where They Stand

He was taken alive in the ninth month at a hunting lodge four miles inside the Amagiri line, by men who had been his own household guard eleven weeks before. He crawled backward across the floor on his elbows with his hands up, and tried the door of his face one last time on a man who had spent a lifetime learning what a face is for. It did not open.
The name Moto was revoked from him in form, before a witness, and it is valid. Calamity took apart every blood vessel in his body from the capillary beds outward over fifty-one minutes, and he was conscious for forty-eight of them. A Reckoner entered him as *elar-ama*: contingent, held by nobody. The Tenrai front is closed. Miku's chair is another matter.

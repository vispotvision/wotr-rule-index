# Seraphine Kharza · Ten Thousand Hands

## Seraphine Kharza · Ten Thousand Hands

> *"Let help remember the shape of itself."*

---

### I · Identity

**Name** · Seraphine Kharza. **Called** · **Ten Thousand Hands.** *The Reliquary Gunner.*
**Role** · **Crowd-safety artillerist and last-touch custodian.** *She fires merciful hands, never shrapnel, turning stampedes into orderly retreats and catching fallers before bone remembers the ground.*
**Look & tells** · **Porcelain pallor under a chapel visor; corseted reliquary harness ribbed in gold; a coffin-bore cannon clutched by articulated bone fingers.** *Violet ectoplasm blooms behind her as palm-shaped spirits rise like smoke made courteous.*
**Gloss rights** · **Yes.** *The card's voice is tender and plain; her interior is the mercy the palms are made of, and the reader may be shown it.*
**Era** · Withering Era.

#### Affiliation

**Guild Accord • Rescue & Deluge Cadre (ordnance unit) • Star Chamber escort for volatile Measures • the Reaper's adjunct when last rooms need extra hands that do not belong to the living.**
*Duties as the card gives them:* **break charges sideways so Marigold Vance's Escort Lane holds** · **ferry witnesses through crushes** · **keep last-word circles from collapsing** · **salvage archives and infants from fire without ladders.**
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VIII, Transcendence.** *Resolving a core internal contradiction documented in the Crystal's fracture record; first true Domain forms.* **The card records no Threshold by name, but it records the contradiction: a woman built to help who was, at some point, taken rather than given.** *The first palm she carries was the one she was given rather than took, and resolving that difference — permission over seizure — is the fracture this Stage closed.* **The Last-Touch Registry is the Domain that formed from it:** *every palm since has been a bond she was handed, never one she reached for.*

### II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class III · Resonant.** *She tools the aftertouch of the dead into useful grasp: lift, hush, hold — Essence and Aether exchanging freely, at the fidelity a licensed palm needs and no more.*
**Attraction Layer** · The Last-Touch Registry, her first true Domain. *Every licensed palm is a bond with someone dead, and the palms withdraw when the bond is insulted; the Layer is where the Ten Thousand Hands actually live.*
**Crystal State** · Refined. **η** · ~0.70, Band B.
**Field feel** · *She seats the cannon, turns a prayer-valve, and a translucent hand the size of a wardrobe unfurls, catching a raging man like a mother lifting a tantrum from the floor: firm, unbruising.*
**Craft** · **Draftcraft.** *Manifold fires cartridges of saintwater vapour seeded with registered palms; a bottled working, cruder than the living kind, and the Palm Keys are the licences it fires against.*
**Categories** · **Animatria, Mechanica, Mnemata.** *Under Pack Ten an Animatria shape moves with the caster's attention and ceases the instant the live line to its maker is severed; every counter on this sheet follows from that.*

### VIII · Signature

#### Palmrise

**Effect** · **A volley of broad hands lifts barricades and people alike, setting them gently apart like books put back on the right shelf.** *Each hand is one registered palm doing the one help it was licensed for.* The other workings on the card are the same hands at different jobs: **Gentle Arrest** *(ten small palms pin wrists, ankles and pride; the restrained discover they would rather talk)*, **Casket Wall** *(she plants Manifold; a fan of interlaced hands becomes a moving shield that drinks arrows and hot words)*, **Catch & Carry** *(hands sweep beneath jumpers or fallen riders and float them to triage tables without theatrics)*, **Mute the Vent** *(a single palm caps a chanting loudmouth or Rot vent; the noise turns into a private cough until Maelia Cantorine cuts the beat)*.
**Cost** · **Her own hands.** *Every shot recoils into her. Overuse numbs her hands to touch for an hour, so the woman whose whole craft is other people's touch spends the evacuation unable to feel her own. She signs with her visor.* **Underneath that, the standing cost: she may only fire palms she was given, and each one is a dead person she is answerable to.**
**Limit** · **Licensed palms only, and clean water.** *No kin to license a palm means Manifold refuses the hand; salted air or a poisoned well clouds the manifests until the water is sweetened.*
**Counter** · **Insult the dead, salt the air, or cut the line.** *Mock the dead within earshot of Manifold and the palms withdraw on their own. Salt the air and the manifests cloud. A palm is an Animatria shape, so a Silence on her, a broken Palm Key or a severed prayer-valve ends the hand mid-grip. The Black Concord's forged cartridges (hook one) are the mirror of this: hands that hurt, fired to make people stop trusting the ones that do not.*
> **What nobody knows.** Whether she can fire on memory alone, with no licence behind the palm, and make the hand hold.

# Seraphion Veydrak · The Chain-Halo

> *"The chain is no prison — it is the shape of justice."*
> **Lurien again — the fifth and final instance in Volume IV.** *He is formerly High Sentinel of the Holy See of Lurien.*
>
> **Riven Vonnel was raised there. Thalyren, Gonju and Chimwala are all anchored to it.** *One re-homing ruling to the Holy Sea of Alabaster settles all five, and two of them — Riven and Seraphion — are both Arbitration Division enforcers who came out of the same seminary.*
> **The card states no Temperance Stage.** **Stage VIII — Transcendence assigned and flagged as originated**, on the strength of the Chain-Halo: *targets cannot lie, flee, or resist sentence without their soul fraying.* *"Judgment / Metallic / Blood"* were not Essence types.

### Affiliation

**Guild Accord — Arbitration Division, Judgment Order.** *Formerly High Sentinel of the Holy See.*
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VIII, Transcendence.** *A thing keeps its weight and gains a second weight, and both are operative, and the practitioner has to keep functioning while holding both.*
>
> **A chain is a chain and is also a sentence.** *His own quote states the dual reading outright — "the chain is no prison, it is the shape of justice" — and he means both, simultaneously, which is Transcendence rather than rhetoric.*

## II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class V · Radiant.** *Aether bends toward the bearer.*
**Crystal State** · Refined. **η** · ~0.70, Band B.
> **Tri-Vow Mandate.** *He cannot break his three vows without forfeiting his own life:* No False Judgment. No Mercy for the Condemned. No Abandonment of the Chosen.
>
> ***Those three cannot all hold.*** *A Chosen who becomes Condemned puts the second and third in direct conflict, and the first forbids resolving it by declaring the judgment wrong.*
>
> **This is not a strong oath. It is a trap with a delay on it**, and the sheet's own arc — *Arbiter's Descent* — names the moment it springs.

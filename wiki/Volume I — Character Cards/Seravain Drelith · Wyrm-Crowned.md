### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Tenryū and the dragon-blooded. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Seravain Drelith was born a trueblood of the Celestial Wyrmline, in the Outer World, where an old wyrm house left the stone beneath it answering to seismic speech. The memory of the line came down with its blood. As a boy he sometimes described a valley he had never walked, down to its fords. His antler-crests came in the colour of old ivory.
He could not fight well. Cousins with quicker hands put him on his back in the yard every season, until he stopped asking for bouts. He asked to set the files at drill. On the slope below him, the files kept their line.

#### The Making

He learned command on other people's feet. Ranks stood where he placed them, and the ground under them began to answer him. Soil went hard as tile beneath a shield wall. A ford glassed over at the bank his files needed and stayed glass after they had gone. He kept the ranks close. Away from them he was a poor striker under a crown of antlers.
The first fugue came in a long encirclement. He had held the line past what his body would bear, and the memory of his line rose over him. He stood among his ranks giving orders for a field his forebears had fought on. The files closed round him and kept the enemy off until he came back.
Then he brought his ranks into the Glass Marches and stood there. Terrain changed. Pacts held in the ground, and the ground kept the set he gave it. He took the Glass Marches without a battle, and he has been standing in them ever since.

#### The Cost

Ground he stood on kept what he did to it, and it did not revert. In his marches encirclements closed bloodlessly, because the terrain had already decided. Under overstress the fugues returned. The antler-crests were live conduits, and he kept them well behind the front rank. Taken from formed ranks, he could be beaten.
He left them once. Two border lords were feuding along a road across the Southern Grass Sea, and one asked the Marchlord of Glass to stand behind his claim. Seravain brought his ranks up to the verge. The other lord had hired Saórén Vyrrakal as champion. Lyra Drakoshi, who kept the gate, shut it to formed ranks and told him the table was open if he walked in alone. He left the ranks on the verge. He came through the gate by himself, with the other side's champion waiting inside, and the chime on the warden's horn stayed quiet while he walked behind her. Saórén made the entrance the other lord had paid for, flame trails on the floor and the grin. When the lords called their champions forward, the warden heated the stone floor under their boots until the soles smoked. The hall laughed. Both lords signed. Seravain walked back out through the gate, and his ranks left the verge without crossing the road.

#### Where They Stand

Seravain Drelith stands in the Glass Marches among his formed ranks, outside the Accord under Concord Sanctions and the Marchlord Exception. The claim he stood behind at the gate is settled. The verge where his ranks waited has a glaze on it now, and the carts go round it.

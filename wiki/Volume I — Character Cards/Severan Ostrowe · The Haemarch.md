## VIII · Traits

### CLOT · the Cascade [Primary Trait]

His blood runs the clotting cascade like a furnace draught, each factor waking ten of the next, so any breach in him seals in two to four seconds, a severed artery included. Holding it costs 1 EU a second, a megawatt that keeps him warm in any weather, and each deep wound sealed costs about 50,000 EU, three hundredths of a percent of his reserve. It closes; it does not replace, and a lost limb stays lost until Scar-Plate grows it back in mineral. Its fault is the Wellspring's: a cascade with no local signal runs everywhere at once. A venom or working that floods his blood with clotting trigger spends every factor he has, and for about six minutes every wound on him bleeds like anyone's.

**Reflection:** Primary · **Lattice Property Altered:** amplification without decay in the Core's restoration loop · **Resonance Event:** first contact with Anima Spirare, aged nineteen · **Evolution State:** toward Ascension; acts without his will  

### SCAR-PLATE · the Callus [Secondary Trait]

Every wound he survives mineralises within the hour into nacre and haem-iron plate (Terranova), built denser than the tissue it replaced along the line of the blow (Redemption), so where he was hurt is where he is hardest. Three centuries have covered seven tenths of him in 212 kg of it, gaining three kilograms a decade, and Fixatio's cure means none of it comes off. A new plate costs half a percent of his reserve as it sets. The limit is Wolff's law cutting back: load rides the plates, and the margins where plate meets skin have gone to brittle bone. The Weir seam down his breastplate was bound open on the ninth night and never plated; under its gold edge is only skin and Shell.

**Reflection:** Secondary · **Lattice Property Altered:** the Shell grown as tissue, not held as field · **Resonance Event:** Resonance Trauma, the ninth night · **Evolution State:** Overgrowth  

### THE SECOND ANSWER · the Record [Passive Trait]

A kind of wound he has survived is recorded, and the next blow of that kind meets a crust already set at the site and loses seven tenths of its energy before the Shell. The record holds edge, point, blunt, shot, crush, burn, frost and acid, and the Families Caloria, Fulguria, Vectoria, Materia, Vitalia and Fluxia. He has never survived Spatium or Limina, and those land at full weight. It costs nothing to hold and everything to live in: the record has begun to generalise, his body answers its own tissue as an insult, and he has been in pain every waking hour for a century. An entry writes only when the wound has closed.

**Reflection:** Passive · **Lattice Property Altered:** Core memory written into the Shell's surface · **Resonance Event:** Convergence with Contrition, Stage IX · **Evolution State:** stable, eroding  

**Trait Risks Carried:** Rigidity (total) · Shear Break if he ever lets a wound in front of him bleed by choice

## X · Techniques

### COAGULUM · the Clot [Signature Technique]

His hollow spines weep his blood as a fine mist carrying his cascade a thousandfold. Where it meets an open wound or wet membrane (eye, mouth, nose) within eight metres, it trips the target's own clotting, and a thrombus climbs the vessel at about thirty centimetres a second: in the lungs an embolism, in the heart an infarct, in the brain a stroke. Each release costs 720,000 EU, four tenths of a percent of his reserve, after two seconds' Readiness while the spines darken and drip. Whole dry skin stops it, and Filtration of S or better stalls the climb within a hand's length. Before: helm, mask, eight metres, the wind behind you. During: a tourniquet above the entry stops it, since a clot runs along the vessel and not across, and any Vitalia anticoagulant dissolves it. After: amputation above the clot, or a thrombolytic draught inside three minutes.

**Mastery:** Mastered · **Opened By:** Hollin Weir · **Forms:** mist (8 m); touch (any wound he grips, no Readiness) · **What Nobody Knows:** whether the clot is his blood or theirs · **Codex Line:** Anima Spirare · Vitalia · Biochemistry · Transmutation · **FOW Requirement:** Ardency Depth B; Hemostasis SS  

### LAPIS CORDIS · the Heart-Stone [Signature Technique]

The eleven red stones in his mantle and breastplate are the hearts of the officers who died under his hands at the Weir, pressed by Fixatio and crystallised by Coagulatio into Essence cells. Each holds 2,700,000 EU, one and a half percent of his reserve, outside his Crystal and safe from anything that drains it. Opening one pours its charge into him in a second and leaves it black for good, and no new stone can be made; Fixatio took eleven and closed. Each is set in a gold mount bonded to the plate, not to him, so a stone cut from its mount is lost to him without wounding him. He has never opened one.

**Mastery:** Held · **Opened By:** the ninth night · **What Nobody Knows:** what he becomes when the last goes dark · **Codex Line:** Fixatio, Coagulatio · Materia · Material Science · Ward · **FOW Requirement:** Oath B; Capacity B

# LORE

Severan Ostrowe was entered on the Accord Enforcement roll as a freeman by redemption at nineteen, the son of a river-town layer-out. He went to the Weir country as a surgeon-lieutenant in 405. In 412 the garrison at Hollin Weir held for thirteen days against an assault nobody has agreed on since. The relief column halted a day's march off on an order that was later lost. When it arrived, one surgeon was alive under the dead, with his hands inside his own chest. The Board's inquest entered the garrison as broken and fled, struck Ostrowe from the roll "nullum et irritum", and paid the relief officer's pension for forty years.

He walked out of the Weir carrying eleven hearts. For three centuries he has been something between a plague-saint and a siege engine. He has closed the wounds of whole towns and ended three Accord expeditions sent to end him, and every one of their dead died of something other than blood. In the spring of 715 he walked into Lisle Down, stopped its main, and began writing to the Board.

# Shiran Kazuren · The Umbra Lantern

## Shiran Kazuren · The Umbra Lantern

> *"He Who Walks Before the Storm."*

---

### I · Identity

**Name** · Shiran Kazuren. **Called** · The Umbra Lantern. **Epithet** · He Who Walks Before the Storm. **Position** · Middle sibling of the Kazuren family *(other siblings unnamed in the source; not detailed elsewhere)*.
**Age** · Late twenties. **Race / Realm** · Eastern Human; realm unstated.
**Gloss rights** · Never. *He does not explain the dream-work, to anyone, under any pressure the source material gives him a reason to face.*

#### Affiliation

Formerly of the **Dreamfold Monastery** *(unattested independently of this card; kept as load-bearing)*, left after a schism only he understood. Now an unaffiliated mediator and reality-reader.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage V, Splintering.** *Surviving contact with a force two or more Tiers above the ceiling he then held; the Crystal fractures along emotional lines.* **The schism at the Dreamfold Monastery was the fracture, not a departure from one.** *Whatever it was that only he understood, it was load-bearing enough to end a monastic life over: that is a Splintering event by definition, whether or not the Monastery's own records would call it one.*

---

### II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Unclassed (Class emerges at Glory).** Rhythm held so completely that people forget he entered a room until he speaks.
**Attraction Layer** · Diffuse and outward, tuned to disaster rather than to persons: the instinct that lets him prevent things before they begin.
**Crystal State** · Refined, fracture-quiet. **η** · ~0.40, Band E. *Believed, by those who know him, to be one strong emotion or one true prophecy away from Stage VI.*
**Veilshade Echo** · *Dream reflections projected from the same Shell rhythm, a second use of the same stillness rather than a separate faculty.*

---

### III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Oneirion** · Limina. **Primary.** The dream-duplicates of Yumekage Sui, and the residue his steps leave behind them.
**Letheveil** · Limina. **Secondary.** The waters of forgetting: why opponents lose track of his position without noticing the lapse.
**Tenebra** · Limina. **Drift.** Signal-reduction layered under the dream-work; the lantern dims rather than vanishes.
> **Three Limina currents, no other Family represented.** *The concentration is diagnostic on its own terms: he is not a broad practitioner, and everything he does is a variation on the same mind-and-memory current read three ways.*

### VIII · Signature

#### Kechiryuu: Yumekage Sui

*"Dream Shadow Slip."*
**Effect** · Synchronized dream-duplicates move with him, blurring which afterimage is the man and which is memory: the confusion is the opening.
**Cost** · The duplicates draw on the same Oneirion-Letheveil current as Forgotten Step; running both at once thins his own position-sense along with his target's.
**Limit** · The duplicates hold only while his attention holds them; a hit that breaks his concentration collapses all of them at once.
**Counter** · **A target who ignores the duplicates and tracks sound or displaced air rather than sight** finds the real man, because the technique only ever defeated the eyes.
> **What nobody knows.** What the schism at the Dreamfold Monastery actually was, or what "understanding it fully" cost him to do alone.

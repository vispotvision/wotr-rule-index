# Sinclair Mercer · The Soul Devourer

> *"Every soul is a song. I silence it, and then I make it mine."*
> **Rebuilt 15 August 2026 from the Trello Volume III sheet. Originated, pending Isaac's ruling.**
>
> The prior sheet gave Sinclair three Soul Crystals, a "Wellspring of Abyssal Ruin" that does not exist in the register, and a progression path built from the Great Work's alchemical operations. **All three are structural errors and none of them survived.** What did survive is the character: a man who eats souls and cannot stop.

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Sinclair Mercer.
**Called** · The Soul Devourer. The Sculptor of the Echoed Dead. In the Sanctum Lux censor rolls, simply *the Cannibal*, which is the only name he has ever objected to.
**Era** · Age of Calamity.
**Standing** · Unaffiliated. Formerly of the Spiral Covenant, which no longer acknowledges him and which he has never acknowledged leaving.
**Origin** · Abythron, the Titan Zar'thuun's domain of unmaking. Half-mortal, given to the Titan as a sacrifice that did not take.
> **Catalyst Event.** Sinclair was offered to Zar'thuun and **the Titan declined him.** He was not accepted, not transformed, and not returned — he was left standing in the unmaking with a Crystal that had opened to something that had no interest in him.
>
> What he took out of Abythron was not a gift. It was the shape of a hunger that had been aimed at him and missed, **and he has been feeding it ever since on the theory that it will eventually be full.**
>
> *He is wrong about this and the wrongness is the whole character.*

## II · Soul Architecture

**One Crystal. Three layers.** The prior sheet's Abyssal Core, Ebonshade Prism and Shadow Warden are not separate Crystals; they are how his three layers present, and they are described as such below.
| Layer | Condition |
|---|---|
| **Essence Core** | Densely fractured and still functional. The fractures date from the Abythron rejection and were promoted to load paths rather than healed. **What reads on a coil is not one signature but a chord with a great many wrong notes in it, and the wrong notes are other people** |
| **Aether Shell** | Class VI · **Voidic.** The inverted Shell. Instead of emitting, it devours, remembers and reflects. *His Ardency does not deal damage. It removes things* |
| **Attraction Layer** | **Operating under Obsession Force rather than Attraction.** He does not recognise what he takes. He takes it, and the taking is sustained by refusal rather than by harmony, at escalating soul-cost |

**Crystal State** · Fractured, and stable in that condition for longer than the literature says is possible. *Three assay houses have recorded him and none has been willing to certify the reading.*
**Essence colour** · Limina black-violet. Not a colour so much as the absence of the colour that should be there — **and observers report perceiving the theft before they perceive the thief.**

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

*Four attested, all Limina. The concentration is itself diagnostic: he is not a broad practitioner and has never tried to be.*
| Wellspring | How it presents in him |
|---|---|
| **Vantabriel** · *the Wellspring of Night* **The governing harmonisation** | What strikes him is kept rather than reflected, and his Crystal's condition is a function of everything it has ever absorbed. **Endurance here is carried loss, not replaced loss** — which is the mechanical statement of what he is, written down four centuries before he existed. *He does not heal. He accumulates* |
| **Dissolution** · *the Unbinding Stream* | The thermodynamic preference toward the unbound state. Cohesion in what he touches becomes the thing requiring active work to maintain, and he does not have to push — **once initiated, it runs on its own** |
| **Eidolyn** · *the Shaping Mind* | Templated nucleation, with intent as the template. This is how consumed material becomes a construct with real solidity rather than an illusion. **The flaws in his intent are reproduced faithfully and at scale** |
| **Tenebra** · *the Hidden Shadow* | He is not invisible. He is quieter than the background, which is better. *His shadow's autonomy is Tenebra worked through Eidolyn and is not a separate faculty* |

> **Same-Family compounding.** Four Limina harmonisations agree along the same axes and reinforce, which is why his Resilience and Gnosis profile reads denser than his numbers account for. **The Ledger figures are ordinary. The qualitative expression is not, and anyone who has fought him can tell the difference in one exchange.**

## VI · Physical Force

**Strike Force** · Ardency 466, low-mid SS-Grade. Roughly **30 TN peak contact force, on the order of 80 TJ energy yield, near 20 kilotons equivalent.** Town-level destructive capacity, which he almost never expresses as destruction because his delivery is severance rather than impact.
**Lifting Strength** · Vitality 328, low S-Grade. **On the order of 60 million tonnes sustained.** Real, and irrelevant to how he fights.
**Speed** · Dexterity 378, A-Grade. Travel in the region of **15,000 m/s**; reaction between **0.05 and 0.2 milliseconds.**
> **The exploitable asymmetry.** His Resilience sits at 742 and his Vitality at 426 — **a full two Tier Grades apart.**
>
> The differential rule cuts both ways. Against anything addressed to his soul he is untouchable. Against a B-Grade professional in good plate who closes fast and keeps closing, **he is a man with S-Grade Vitality being hit**, and the Grade gap that protects him is doing no work at all.
>
> *Both times he has been badly hurt, it was this. Neither opponent was within three Grades of him overall.*

## VII · Aether Flow

**Aether Class** · VI · Voidic. Qualitatively different rather than more efficient.
**η** · 0.85–0.90, per the Band S range. *Sourced.*
**Reserve behaviour** · Anomalous, and the anomaly is the whole of his reputation. **A Voidic Shell's intake is not accounted for in the standard reserve model**, which is why the assay houses will not certify him and why the Bureau of Strategic Assessment carries him on a separate ledger.
> **Provenance note, and it is not a small one.** Everything Sinclair does is, in the Standing Index's own terms, **Class IV Crystal Draw performed without a bench, a formula, or an entry.**
>
> The Index records three lawful sources for Class IV and states plainly that **the arithmetic has never balanced.** *He is not the reason for the discrepancy. He is what the discrepancy looks like when it walks.*

## VIII · Traits

**Soul Rend** · His strikes act on soul structure rather than tissue. Temperance is disrupted, Aether response slows, and metaphysical defences unravel from the inside. *The wound does not bleed and it does not close.*
**The Devouring Spiral** · Proximity to him is expensive. Essence bleeds toward him without his acting, and the bleed is not something he can switch off. **He has been asked to and cannot, and the request is one of the few things that reliably angers him.**
**Soul Sculptor** · Absorbed fragments are shaped into weapons, defences, and constructs that hold the temperament of whoever they came from. **A construct forged from a person he knew argues with him.**
**The Warden** · His shadow acts independently. It binds, shields, and draws Essence through contact. *It has not obeyed him in some years and he has stopped correcting it in front of witnesses.*

## IX · Domain

**The Unwritten Grave.**
A standing region in which the accumulated dead he carries become locally present — not summoned, not raised, **but no longer subject to the ordinary courtesy of being gone.** Constructs form from the ambient field without his direction. The screaming is not atmosphere; it is the Eidolyn template reproducing the flaw in his intent at scale, faithfully, every time.
**Formation** · Stage VIII standard, held since. The Domain has outrun his control of it, which is consistent with a Crystal trapped in Dissonance rather than passed through.

## X · Techniques

### Abyssal Dominion Pulse · *Signature*

**Effect** · He opens the Core rather than striking with it. Everything he has taken arrives at once in a spreading front, and what it touches begins preferring the unbound state — armour separating at the seam, formations losing the will that held them in line, a man's grip opening on a weapon he has held for twenty years. The ground keeps the constructs afterward. **They stay for days and they do not stop.**
**Cost** · He surrenders the fragments spent, permanently, and each one was load-bearing in a Crystal that has no other structure. *For a month afterward he cannot recall names he knew, and he does not know which names are missing until somebody says one.*
**Limit** · It cannot be aimed. The front spreads from him in every direction and does not distinguish.
**Counter** · Chainsalt. The isolation lining that makes a sealed coffer read as a box of salt reads the same way to him — **a Sealwright team working from behind Chainsalt plate has taken him twice, and both times he left rather than press it.**

### Phantom Chains

**Effect** · Shadow and fragment condensed into restraint. What is bound stops being able to spend Essence, and the binding tightens against effort rather than yielding to it.
**Cost** · The chains are made of somebody, and holding them costs him the use of that fragment for as long as the binding stands.
**Limit** · They hold a person. They do not hold a construct, a beast without a Crystal, or anything already dead.
> **What nobody knows.** A soul Sinclair has consumed does not read as absent on any instrument the Accord possesses. **It reads as elsewhere.** The Research and Archives Division has never published this, because the only correct inference from it is one the Division is not prepared to write down.

## XI · Spirit Axes

**None standing.** This is the most important line on the sheet.
> A Spirit Axis is the Catalyst for Stage IV Flourishing, which means **Sinclair had one once and does not now.** The Accord's own literature holds that the architecture built on an Axis has to stand up whether or not the person is still alive to stand under it — and that the power does not fail when the Axis dies. **The power is entirely fine and it is still shaped exactly like a bond to somebody who is not there.**
>
> *Originated: whoever it was is not named in any source. The gap is deliberate and should stay a gap until it is worth spending.*
**Tilda** · Hunted, not taken. Her Aetheric harmony eluded him and she is the one recorded failure in a career otherwise without them. *He does not discuss it and has twice killed people who raised it.*

## XII · Resistances

**Strong** · Conceptual erasure, perceptual manipulation, fear and compulsion effects. Anything addressed to a self that is already fractured finds no clean surface to work on.
**Weak** · **Radiant current**, in common with most of the register. Sustained physical trauma, because his Vitality never developed. **Chainsalt and any properly cut isolation working**, which is the practical counter and the reason Enforcement teams that engage him bring a Sealwright rather than a champion.

## XIII · Physical Description

Tall, and thinner than a man his height should be, with the specific gauntness of somebody whose body has been running a deficit for years and has adapted to it rather than corrected it. Hair black, worn back off a high forehead, dry as dead grass at the ends where the colour has started leaving. The face is long and the cheekbones carry it; he would have been handsome at thirty and is not now.
**The eyes carry Draft-mark**, the sclera discoloured to a bruised violet that a renderer would name off the colour in under a second. His nailbeds carry the same. Hands narrow, unscarred, and **noticeably steady** — there is no tremor in him, which is the wrong result for what he does and which every physician who has examined him has commented on.
He dresses well and out of date, in dark wool cut in a fashion that was current in Abythron a generation ago, worn thin at the cuffs and mended carefully. **The mending is the detail people remember.** A man who eats souls sits up at night with a needle.
His shadow does not always match him. Most people notice and correct themselves, and the correcting is audible.

---

## XIV · Psychology

He is courteous, and the courtesy is real rather than performed. He does not raise his voice, he does not enjoy killing, and **he has genuinely never understood why the distinction fails to help his case.**
His governing belief is that the hunger has a bottom. Everything he has done proceeds from it: if there is a bottom, then the taking is a finite debt with an end, and a finite debt can be paid. **The Heresiology names the condition without naming him** — a corrupted operation sustained by refusal rather than by recognition, running at escalating cost, in a practitioner who will not stop because the working is no longer a thing he does but a thing he is.
What he refuses is being told to stop, and he refuses it violently and out of proportion to how it is said. *The refusal is the tell, and every censor who has interviewed him has come away with the same note in the margin.*
> **One private thought, for prose use.** *He counts them. Not deliberately — the number is simply available to him the way the time of day is, and it has not gone down.*

## XV · Equipment

No weapon. He has never carried one and considers the question slightly vulgar.
**A sealed coffer**, Chainsalt-lined, which he uses to carry fragments he is not currently spending. *He is aware of the irony that the material which counters him is also the material he relies on, and he has said so, once, to somebody who did not survive the conversation.*
**A physician's roll** of fine needles and dark thread. For the coat.

## XVII · Fracture Log

**The rejection.** Load-bearing, permanent, and the origin of everything on this sheet.

**Accumulated carry.** Every fragment he holds is unintegrated by definition, and the seat reads catastrophically rough. **He would be barred at Tier Four by any assay house in the four quarters**, which is the exact arrangement the Sixteen Stages describes: the instrument is honest, and it is reading what somebody's origins could afford.
**Tilda.** Not a fracture. An absence where a fracture would be, and he is not able to say why.

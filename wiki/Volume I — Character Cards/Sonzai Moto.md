- **Status:** at the scene's close, pinned beneath a black mass crushing his own tower, then back on his feet and fighting.
- **Level / Stage / Band:** `null`.

## II · Soul Architecture

- The **Calamity** is interior and leaks under strain: "Sonzai's breathing changed. Calamity began leaking from him in dark crimson waves."
- It is corrupted rather than native — Malakar's corruption, staining outward through the Plane of Fate.
- Under pressure it is volatile: "crimson-black pressure boiling against the restraints." The first pulse "expanded across the shattered floor, violently enough to send loose fragments of masonry skipping away from his body."
- By the scene's later stage it is disciplined rather than shed: it "no longer leaked from the Moto heir in uncontrolled waves; it threaded itself along the Chains of War in disciplined crimson-black currents, each link humming with violence that had finally been given direction."

## X · Techniques

### The Chains of War

- **Effect:** inscribed chains that rise and act at his command — "dragged themselves upright behind him," then hover "around him like enraged serpents waiting for another command." They strike independently, from opposing sides, and can saturate into "a single field of continuous impact." Wrapped along his forearm with Calamity compacted on the metal, "the links looked edged."
- **Cost:** `null`.
- **Limit:** they attack along routes. This is the documented failure: Kujo "folded Xevorith across the space between each Chain and himself. Every route disappeared before completion, leaving the weapons striking empty black while Kujo remained directly in front of him."
- **Counter:** deny the route rather than the chain, and the whole field aborts without being blocked. A second counter is shown against Sonzai directly — rotate him into the path of his own Chain "and force the weapon to abort," because the Chains will not complete through him.
- **What nobody knows:** whose conflicts the links are remembering.
- **The Test (R17-2):** it does *independent multi-axis striking* to *an opponent's stance and recovery*, which under *inscriptions fed by his fury* produces *a continuous impact field that reads his enemy's next position before he reaches it*.

### Calamity

- **Effect:** crimson-black corrupted force, carried interiorly. Leaks in waves under stress; pulses outward hard enough to skip masonry across a floor; compacts along metal to edge it; and, disciplined, threads along the Chains as current.
- **Cost:** `null`. Its price in the source is legibility, not fatigue — see Counter.
- **Limit:** it is Malakar's corruption and it stains.
- **Counter:** stated in the text, and it is not a defence but a detection. The Calamity "was recognizable from far beyond ordinary distance, Malakar's old corruption acting like a stain through the Plane of Fate. Kujo followed that stain through Kharven until he found the tower in which Sonzai resided." Anything that reads the Plane of Fate finds him by it. Suppressing the stain, not the force, is the counter.
- **What nobody knows:** whether the discipline he reaches under Emira's Harmony holds without her.
- **The Test:** it does *corrupted crimson-black pressure* to *the space and metal around him*, which under *Malakar's old corruption* produces *a weapon that announces its own position across the Plane of Fate*.

## XIV · Psychology

**What he refuses.** To stay down. The scene's shape for him is repeated rising: pinned beneath a black mass crushing his own tower, hands clenching, the first pulse going out, and then on his feet with the Chains upright behind him.

**What it costs him.** Fury is his fuel and his leak. The inscriptions burn hotter as his fury feeds the battlefield, and in the same state the Calamity leaks from him uncontrolled and paints his position across the Plane of Fate. He is at his most findable when he is at his most dangerous.

**What he is being taught in this scene.** Direction. The measured change is from Calamity "leaking from him in uncontrolled waves" to Calamity "threaded… along the Chains of War in disciplined crimson-black currents, each link humming with violence that had finally been given direction."

**What is used against him.** People. Kujo watches his face rather than Selvara's, and waits for the anticipation to land before anything strikes.

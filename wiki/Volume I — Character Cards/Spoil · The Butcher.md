# Spoil · The Butcher

> *"Blood's just memory made warm again. I like to remind people of what they've forgotten."*
> **Stage corrected under the standing ruling.** The legacy sheet reads **"Stage III — Flourishing."** *Flourishing is Stage IV; Stage III is Ascension.* **Name wins — Stage IV.**
>
> *"Blood (Corruption) / Beast (Predatory) / Shadow (Veil)"* were not Essence types. **The Marauder's Bloom** is retained as the Temperance Cycle's name.

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · **Spoil**, *formerly Tanya.* **Called** · The Blood-Seed Marauder. Red-Thread Butcher.
**Role** · **Independent Killer. Blood-Asura Binder. Dual-Blade Executioner.**

### Affiliation

**None.** Nomadic. *Known to take contracts from Outer World warlords and Concord dissidents.*
> **Catalyst Event · Stage IV, Flourishing.** *The noise stops. Identity settles, and the resonance signature that will be recognisably yours for the rest of your life finishes arriving.*
>
> **And the Catalyst cannot be reached alone — it requires a Spirit Axis, a bond with another soul carrying mechanical weight in the Harmonics.**
>
> ***Spoil's Axis is Raktabija.*** *The Sixteen Stages notes that every Flourished practitioner in the four quarters arrived there because somebody mattered to them enough to hold load.* **In Spoil's case the somebody is a blood-seed Asura bound in permanent servitude, and the sheet does not pretend that is the same thing as being loved.**

## II · Soul Architecture

**Essence Core** · **Grafted.** *Originally female; their head was joined to a male body via forbidden graft-binding rites.* **A coil reads two provenances that have integrated and should not have.**
**Aether Shell** · **Unclassed (Class emerges at Glory).** The attuned Shell; simple work stabilises and bonds form without tearing the self.
**Attraction Layer** · One Axis, held by compulsion rather than affection.
**Crystal State** · Refined. **η** · ~0.45, Band F.

## VII · Signature

### Raktabija's Finger

**Effect** · The saber bleeds constantly and **what it spills does not fall.** Blood taken from the field becomes construct, and the constructs are Raktabija's, and Raktabija does not tire.
**Cost** · **The Asura is bound in permanent servitude and the binding is the Axis.** *Sever it and Spoil loses the Stage IV Catalyst that stabilised them.*
**Limit** · **There must be blood.** *A bloodless opponent — construct, undying, thing of stone — leaves them with two blades and B-Grade Ardency.*
**Counter** · **Fight them clean and fast.** *Or take Raktabija, which Kaerith Vireth has attempted.*

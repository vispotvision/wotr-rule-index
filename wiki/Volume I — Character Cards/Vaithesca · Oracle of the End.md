### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The long memory. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Vaithesca was born a mortal woman, and no record says where or to whom. Her card calls her "possibly a priestess of a forgotten Wellspring". It does not name the Wellspring. It does not say whether she served one.
She lived in a monastery in the Outer World. Most of that continent is exhausted ground, where practitioners run on their own reserves and nearly every Well that still gathers has an owner, most of them winged. No page names the monastery, its order or its rule. No page gives the name she answered to there, the work of her hands, or who shared the house with her.
None of it survived in her. Her memory began after the monastery ended, and her own words mark the edge of it: "I do not remember being born. I remember the moment after." Everything told of the woman came from outside her. The outside kept very little.

#### The Making

A construct of the Abyssal Depths came onto that ground. The record names him Vorrn; nothing gives her the name. His containment plate opened along every crack at once, and while it stood open he walked as a Void Fracture. His bleed ran into the ground around him on two currents, Nihiloth and Tenebra. At the monastery, Penanceflare met them. Where the three came together, reality collapsed.
Everyone in the monastery died.
Her consciousness did not die with them. It fractured into the collision itself and stayed there. From then on the three Wellsprings held together in her, and she was the place where they met.
When the plate closed, the ground did not come back. The Black Concord filed the region's reclassification and kept the incident record, and the ground became a dead zone of the kind Drakyssia marks on its imperial flight charts.
The day she stopped being mortal was the day of her recognition. A Hollow Throne recognised her, and no breakthrough came before it. She reached what the card records by dying into three Wellsprings at once, with nothing before it that counted as training.

#### The Cost

The collision left her only part of a body. What remained was not fully corporeal. She moved toward nothing, and she was found at the places where things were closing.
Speech cost her most. Every ending she named existed before she spoke it, and nothing she said ever made a new one. What she told a listener was the consequence that listener was already owed. She kept each ending after she had named it. By her card she held thousands at once, and the price of each rose with how much the listener had invested in what was ending. So she rarely spoke at all.
In all the endings she named, she never once surprised anyone about anything that was not already going to happen.
Time ran toward conclusions wherever she stood. Any place she stayed in longer than three days began to run out of its own time, and she did not stay. The more magic worked near her, the quieter she became and the less anyone could hear.
She read the conclusion of a person on contact. She could not read one sincere kindness. Mercy of the sincere, illogical kind was the one thing that ever made her sight stutter. A real paradox disrupted it.

#### Where They Stand

Vaithesca answers to no faction. The one thing she is bound to is the point where the three Wellsprings meet.
She wears the Witness's Mantle, a cloak of starless grey that keeps her from dissolving back into the collision that made her. She carries the Void-Ledger, a bound book that writes itself. Every ending she has spoken stands in it a second time, permanent, and nothing can destroy it. She carries the Name-Shear too, a bone knife that cuts fates. What it severs is a future, and the one it is taken from is left static, without possibility.
She comes unbidden to the closing edge of other people's arcs and speaks what was already written. Whoever hears her understands, in any tongue. The meaning reaches them before she has finished speaking, and each leaves with a small mark: private, unshakeable knowledge of their own end.
She is aging in reverse. By her card she has perhaps five years left before she reaches the moment before her own naming, and then she will never have existed. Nobody knows "whether the ending she is fading backward toward was ever one she spoke herself".
The monastery's ground is still dead. A copy of its reclassification lies in an archive beneath a Guild Accord registry building. The copy does not name her.

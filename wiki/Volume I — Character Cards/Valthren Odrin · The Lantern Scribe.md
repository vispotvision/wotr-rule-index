# Valthren Odrin · The Lantern Scribe

## Valthren Odrin · The Lantern Scribe

> *"Light is only guilt that remembered how to shine."*

---

### I · Identity

**Name** · Valthren Odrin. **Called** · The Lantern Scribe.
**Role** · **Archivist of Confession. Keeper of the Candle Archive. Heretic-Warden of the Lantern Ward.**
A thin, candle-crowned man whose robes smell of wax and burnt parchment, every thread stitched with script that writhes when light touches it. His staff burns with candles that never gutter; each one records a soul's final confession.

#### Affiliation

Guild Accord, **Research and Archives Division**, the Lantern Ward. **Tier 6 · Master · Warden of Duty**, still called Keeper of the Candle Archive by everyone who deals with him directly.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage X, Realization.** *The Continuum stops merely permitting a Domain and begins accommodating it, and the soul that reaches this Stage finds the world has started answering back.*
> 
> **Valthren answered by making himself the record.** He rewrites his essence into wax tablets and seals them to his own flesh, and every revision frays the memory of the one before it. **A man who chose partial immortality over a whole memory made the only trade Realization ever offers an archivist: the world listens now, and he is paying for that with himself, one candle at a time.**

### VIII · Signature

#### Lex Purgare

**Effect** · Rewrites the metaphysical law binding a corruption or heresy, and the property it grants is gone in front of the witnesses it was performed for.
**Cost** · A fixed toll of Essence per correction, and a candle on the Waxen Crown burns out with it; extinguishing one severs part of his immortality.
**Limit** · Only reaches law already written down where he can read it. An oral curse with no textual trace has no grammar for Lex Purgare to enter.
**Counter** · Give him nothing to transcribe. A wrong nobody has confessed to leaves no law on the page for him to rewrite.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the memories his revisions erase are gone, or filed somewhere in the Archive he no longer holds the key to.

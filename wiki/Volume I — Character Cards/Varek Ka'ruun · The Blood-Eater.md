# Varek Ka'ruun · The Blood-Eater

> *"Eat with me. Fight with me. Burn with me. Or die under my teeth — your choice tastes the same."*
> **A third error class: an invented Stage name.** The card reads **"Stage VIII (Conquest)."**
>
> ***Conquest is not among the sixteen.*** *Stage VIII is Transcendence.* **Where the word is not a real Stage name, the numeral stands and the word goes** — unlike the off-by-one slips, where the name was real and the numeral was the error.
>
> *"Flame / Blood / Dominion"* were not Essence types. **The Horned Pyre** is retained as the Temperance Cycle's name.
>
> **Braqth** — a real place, ruled 2026-09-12: Outer World, the Southern Pan.

**Name** · Varek Ka'ruun. **Called** · **The Blood-Eater.** Horned Feastlord · **Laughing Pyre.**
**Role** · **Outer World Chieftain. Ritual Combatant. Wellspring Marauder of the Burning Steppe.**

### Affiliation

**Braqth Dominion — Flame-Horn Tribe.** *Leader of the Crimson Hunt, a warband that roams between Wellsprings and burns them into feasting grounds.* **Holds pacts with three Infernal Courts** for strength, drought-breaking fire, and soul-binding trophies.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VIII, Transcendence.** *The wound made structural.*
>
> **His scar-glyphs were etched during coming-of-age duels and they record his kills metaphysically.** *He did not scar over. He turned the record into the load path, and every glyph that lights is a wound doing work.*

## VI · Traits

**Feast of the Burning Horns** · **The spiralled horns are hollow conduits for Bloodfire Essence.** *When Varek gorges on heart's blood in battle, the horns vent flame and vapour,* **creating a heat haze that distorts weapon aim and Essence channeling within ten paces.**
**Crimson Rite Carvings** · **The red tattoos are scar-glyphs**, etched during coming-of-age duels. *They siphon ambient life-force, making him stronger the longer he remains in melee, and they record his kills metaphysically.*
**Hunt's Laugh** · **His battle-laugh infuses allies with Dominion Essence, making them feel unstoppable and immune to fear for three breaths.** *The downside — once it ends, the body crashes, muscles seizing as if starved.*
> **Hunt's Laugh is the best-costed trait in this batch.** *He does not grant courage. He spends his allies' bodies in advance and the bill arrives three breaths later, and Harmonics Projection at 406 is what carries it.*

## VII · Signature

### The Horned Pyre

**Effect** · He takes a Wellspring and **makes it a feasting ground.** *Inside, enemies exhaust faster and their blood goes heavy* — and every glyph on his skin that catches makes the next exchange worse.
**Cost** · **Escalation without regulation.** *Tempering 238 against a feedback loop that has no upper bound written into it.*
**Limit** · **He must be in melee, and he must be feeding.**
**Counter** · **Refuse the melee.** *Gnosis 184 means he will not out-think a withdrawal, and the Carvings give him nothing at range.*

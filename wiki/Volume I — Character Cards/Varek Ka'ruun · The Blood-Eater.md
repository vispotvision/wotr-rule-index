### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The courts of waking and sleeping law. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Varek Ka'ruun was born to the Flame-Horn Tribe of the Braqth Dominion, on the Burning Steppe of the Outer World. The ground there was basalt, split by nature into six-sided posts that came out of the terraces under a pry bar, and gibber that broke a horse's leg. Water was scarce and wood scarcer. The tribe built from stone or from what had once been alive. Wellsprings were few in that interior. After storms the tribe went out onto the pans for the hollow glass the lightning left in the crust, and the Accord's factors bought it by the armload at whatever price they wrote down.
His horns came in spiralled and hollow. The Flame-Horn raised him to its coming-of-age law, which was decided by duel. He trained for it in coilhide duel gloves, inside a ring of basalt posts, against the youths of his own season. In every bout he closed until he was chest to chest with the other youth. No correction from the elders taught him to stand off.

#### The Making

He fought his coming-of-age duels in that ring. The glyphs were etched into him during them, red, and from then on they recorded his kills. In the fights that followed he learned what the carvings did. The longer he stayed in melee, the stronger he fought. Every glyph that caught made the next exchange worse for whoever faced him. When he gorged on heart's blood in battle, his horns vented flame and vapour, and within ten paces of him aimed blows and workings went wide in the haze.
He took the Crimson Hunt out between the Wellsprings. At each one it reached, the Hunt burned the Wellspring into a feasting ground and ate there. He made pacts with three Infernal Courts, for strength, for fire that breaks a drought and for trophies that bind a soul.
For a long time the glyphs were a record and nothing more. Then the wound was made structural. He turned the record into the load path. The cuts never scarred over, and from then every glyph that lit was a wound doing work.

#### The Cost

The carvings fed him only in melee, and only while he fed. Nothing in them told him when to stop. His laugh belonged to the Hunt. When he laughed in battle, the warriors near him lost their fear for three breaths. Then their muscles seized as though they had starved, and they went down where they stood while the fight went on around them. After one battle Lyraen Callivane healed him, standing barefoot in the soil. He called her the little lamb that bites.
At Braqth the Hunt burned a confederation's Wellspring into a feasting ground, and the confederation brought the loss before the Guild Accord. Arbitration sent Riven Eltamyr Vonnel. Varek would not answer a written ruling. He called for duel court under the coming-of-age law his carvings had been cut by, and Riven accepted. They fought inside a ring of basalt posts, with Hazzen Skollgrin at its edge under the Petal Writ. Varek never got inside the glaive's reach. He had nothing to feed on, and nothing came from his horns. Riven closed the fight with one cut he had chosen before he stepped in. Varek lost. He said he had been honoured by the artistry of his own defeat, and the Hunt left that Wellspring to the confederation.
That night he drank with Hazzen, who had been given nothing to enforce. In the middle of a toast he went for Hazzen's neck with his teeth. Hazzen laughed. Varek has said since that he laughs right.

#### Where They Stand

Varek Ka'ruun is chieftain of the Flame-Horn and leads the Crimson Hunt, the warband that roams between Wellsprings. He holds his three pacts. Where he fights, his Wellspring rises as a bonfire that never burns down, ringed with the bones of prey. Those who fight him inside it tire fast, and their blood runs heavy.
He drinks with Hazzen after battles. He and Thalyss Aevyrn hold each other in the respect of one apex predator for another. Off Vellsorea, on Thalyss's invitation, he fought a sea-bound Wellspring beast at close quarters in the shallows while Thalyss held the water heavy around it, until it died on the shelf. They feasted on the shore on its blood and on salt, and he drank from its heart.
He calls Sandalphon Aestraen song-bone. He once tried to hire him as a battle-chanter. He and Garruk Stonehoof have tested their strength against each other, in contests friendly and otherwise. He still claims Gorrum Vaelseth tried to sell him swamp air in a bottle. Kaelen Brighthoof finds him too loud to hunt with and admires the way he fights.
The glyphs go on recording every kill. Nothing in them tells him when to stop.

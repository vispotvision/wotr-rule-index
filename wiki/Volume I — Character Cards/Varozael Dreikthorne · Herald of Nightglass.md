# Varozael Dreikthorne · Herald of Nightglass

> *"When the stag lowers his antlers, the forest is already his."*
> **The card states no Temperance Stage.** **Stage IX — Invocation assigned and flagged as originated**, on the strength of the Cycle: *he ascends by hunting powerful prey and anchoring their remains into his Domain's soil.*
>
> ***A Domain that consumes what is brought into it and grows is a co-author.*** *Invocation is where the practitioner stops working alone — and Varozael feeds the thing that answers.*
>
> *"Shadow / Thorn / Blood"* were not Essence types.

## VII · Signature

### Eclipse Thorns

**Effect** · **Domination of territory through fear, shadow and binding.** *He ascends by hunting powerful prey and anchoring their remains into his Domain's soil.*
**Cost** · **The Domain must be fed to grow**, and what is anchored in it stays anchored.
**Limit** · **He must draw blood first.** *An enemy he has never wounded is outside the entire mechanism.*
**Counter** · **Do not bleed.** *Or leave his ground — Dominion Radius 625 is the sheet, and outside it he is an S-Grade fighter with B-Grade perception.*

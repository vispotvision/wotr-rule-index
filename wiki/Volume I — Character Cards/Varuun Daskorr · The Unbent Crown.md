# Varuun Daskorr · The Unbent Crown

## Varuun Daskorr · The Unbent Crown

> *"Law is mercy written in weight."*
> **DEAD CANON FLAG: this sheet sits on a struck polity.** *Babyl and the Babylian Imperium were struck from canon.* Varuun is King of the Kingdom of Babyl, rules from Karn-Bael, and the card's whole institutional frame is the kingdom. **The character survives the setting**: an aging Inner World stone-king whose reign is sintered to a worked Coagula Core is legible on the current map. **Isaac's ruling needed on re-homing or striking**; all card content is preserved.

### I · Identity

**Name** · King Varuun Daskorr. **Called** · The Unbent Crown.
**Role** · **Mortal King. Wellspring-Bound Sovereign. Living Anchor.**
**Gloss rights** · Yes. *He speaks law aloud for a living and the card's voice is his own decrees; he may name what he does in his own words, and the Guild's terms stay in the Guild's mouth.*
**Appearance** · A towering figure with a mane of white hair and a beard like wind-worn marble. Heavy ceremonial robes of red and sable embroidered with golden script, their patterns mimicking the law-glyphs of Atlas. *When he rests upon his sword, the air hums with latent Wellspring tone, as though gravity itself listens.*

#### Affiliation

**Reigning monarch of the Kingdom of Babyl** (struck polity, see flag), an Inner World dominion of stone and faith, ruling from the citadel of Karn-Bael, seat of the worked Core of Coagula. **Signatory of the Guild Accord**, though he delays decrees citing "Temporal Weight Clauses". **Era** · Withering Era, Imperial Age, reigning from Year 742.
**Lineage** · The Daskorr bloodline, an ancient noble family tracing descent to the original Lawbearers of Coagula; its ancestors served the Dawn Guard as oath-smiths and lords of mineral law. **Rumoured to carry fragments of Atlazon's Strain**, granting each heir a subtle gravitational aura that commands reverence even in silence.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VIII, Transcendence.** *Resolving a core internal contradiction documented in the Crystal's fracture record. Objects retain their weight and gain a second weight, and both facts are operative, and the practitioner has to keep functioning while holding them. The first true Domain forms.*
> 
> **He did not bow to gods, yet never broke faith with them.** *That is the contradiction, and the card says he was tempered by rulership itself: each decree, each burden of judgment carved a layer onto the Crystal until it pulsed with lawlike steadiness.* **A crown is a crown and is also a weight. The Domain that formed is the capital, and its law is that the air refuses lies.**

### III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Coagula** · Materia. **The Unifying Pulse**; the card calls it *The Law That Binds*. Sintering: densification by diffusion below the melting point, particles fusing across their contact points until the porosity closes and the mass behaves as one body. **His reign is the kingdom sintered to him.** Every edict spoken in the Throne Hall manifests as faint glyphs on the floor and vanishes once fulfilled, a contact point pressed and then closed. *Failure: residual porosity. Sintering rarely reaches full density, and the voids that remain are exactly where the next fracture starts. A kingdom that has not fused everywhere fractures where it did not.*
**Atlazon's Strain** · *Bloodline, and no harmonisation.* The Titan Atla'zon holds orientation, duration and adhesion; the Daskorr aura is a fragment of that kneeling burden, and it is why his silence weighs.

### VIII · Signature

#### The Edict

**Effect** · He speaks a law in the Throne Hall and **it is etched into reality**: faint glyphs on the floor that vanish once the edict is fulfilled. *Coagula runs sintering on the Soul Plane; a spoken edict is a contact point pressed and held, and the kingdom fuses to it by diffusion without ever melting.* The Kneeling Sun seals it.
**Cost** · **His Crystal is sintering into the Core.** Every edict closes a contact point between king and kingdom, and the card's rumour is the invoice: he will one day fuse entirely with Coagula, a living Anchor-King whose laws endure after his death. *He prays for endurance, and the price of endurance is that there stops being a him separate from the law.* Beneath the composure, the fear that progress will unmake reverence.
**Limit** · **The Throne Hall, and the lattice of Coagula under it.** Spoken aloud, in his own voice, over ground already worked. He cannot decree at a distance and he cannot decree in haste.
**Counter** · **Residual porosity.** Find the void the sintering never closed (a province the edict did not reach, a subject who was not in the hall) and the next fracture initiates there; an edict has no hold on what it never touched. Or hold him from his ground. Away from the Core, the words are only heavy.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether "The Unbent Crown" is a description or a prophecy. *"When my crown falls, so will the sky."*

# Velastra Nirellium · The Severed Saint

> *"The pact is broken. The breath you take now is theft."*
> **Migrated with all content preserved, including the three artifacts.** *"Gravemarch / Null / Iron-Aether"* were not Essence types. **"Aether Class: Inexorable Blade"** is not among the eight; **she is Class VI, Voidic** — *the inverted Shell, which devours rather than emits, and whose Ardency does not deal damage but removes things.* **That is Severance stated in the Class system's own terms.**

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Velastra Nirellium. **Called** · The Severed Saint.
**Role** · **Deathbound Enforcer. Concordant Exile. Bladebearer of the Unrevoked Law.**

### Affiliation

Former **Adjudicator of the Celestial Concord.** Now bound to the **Severance Concord**, *a silent remnant cult who enforce the Law of Irrevocables.* **Hunted by both Divine and Abyssal factions** for wielding the **Lawbreaker's Blade** — *a weapon that pierces contracts, blessings, and fates alike.*
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VII, Refraction.** *Self-examination at depth reveals the fracture record — everything Splintering promoted to a load path, labelled and impossible to un-see.*
>
> **She was an Adjudicator who looked at the pacts she had been enforcing and found one that should never have been signed.** *The legacy sheet's last line on her relationships is the whole story:* **no one remembers who she served, because the oaths she severed erased the name from history.**
>
> ***She did not lose her patron. She unmade the fact of having had one, and the loss is not a wound she carries. It is a wound nobody can locate.***

---

## II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class VI · Voidic.** *Instead of emitting, the Shell devours, remembers and reflects.* **Her Ardency does not deal damage. It removes things.**
**Attraction Layer** · **Present and inverted.** *She is bound by a contract she cannot break and cannot form another.*
**Crystal State** · Refined — *unnervingly so, for what she does.*
**η** · ~0.60, Band C.
**The Bladewake Core** · *A motionless core of dark iron rotating slowly within the Soul Crystal. It releases no light. Its pressure inverts gravity, erodes memory, and collapses spell structure upon contact.*

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Dissolution** · Limina. **Governing.** *Solvation driven by the entropy of mixing — a solid dissolves not because the solvent attacks it but because the mixed state has higher entropy, and the process therefore runs on its own once initiated.*
> **This is Severance Drive.** *She does not cut bonds. She initiates a process that prefers them severed, and then the physics finishes without her.* **Failure: indiscriminate solvency. Entropy does not consult the practitioner about which structures were load-bearing.**
**Nihiloth** · Limina. *Destructive interference — two waves in antiphase sum to zero, and the region between them carries no energy at all.* **Nullmantle, sourced. And the cancellation is symmetric.**
**Mortalis** · Vitalia. **Governs passage.** *The Anathema Crown corrupting resurrection rites is Mortalis asserting that the passage was already lawful and should not be undone.*
**Monolithion** · The Iron half. **Momentum Lock, and the inexorable weight.**

> ***Velastra is a walking Spellcraft exclusion zone.*** *Runecraft still functions against her, because a cut sentence needs no voice — which means the counter to her is an Engraver, working in advance, from outside the radius.*

## VIII · Signature

### Final Severance

**Effect** · One strike, once per Temperance cycle. **What it ends does not return by any means** — not resurrection, not divine reincarnation, not the anchoring of a soul to a vessel. *The thing is not killed. The possibility of its continuation is removed.*
**Cost** · **Dissolution runs on its own once initiated and does not consult her about which structures were load-bearing.** *She has severed things she did not intend to and cannot identify which.*
**Limit** · **Once per cycle**, and **Momentum Lock means she must have been walking** — interrupt her approach and the blade inherits nothing.
**Counter** · **Stop her moving.** *Or have nothing bound: a creature with no pacts, no blessings and no anchoring is simply a target, and her Ardency is A-Grade.*
> **What nobody knows.** Whose name the severed oaths erased — **including hers.**

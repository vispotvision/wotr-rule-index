### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Triune of the Three-Headed Serpent. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Velcrissa was born in the Concord, in a house kept quiet for her mother. Her mother's Crystal began to fail while Velcrissa was small. Physicians came and held it. They kept it from failing and they kept it from settling, and her mother lived on in that held shape. She slept badly and woke at any sound. The house learned to make none.
Velcrissa learned which boards spoke underfoot, and how to talk beneath the kettle and the rain. Her mother lived that way until she died. The Crystal never finished.

#### The Making

She studied Temperance at a Facultas and made her living guiding practitioners through their Thresholds. When a Crystal began to fail in her care, she hurried it on to its settling and let the practitioner sleep through the last of it. She kept her voice low. Her working reached them before her words did, and the room took it for weather.
She held chairs of Temperance Studies at more than one Facultas. Each was an appointment in writing, paid from a fund. She answered for every passage she guided. She answered alone.
In the hall of one Facultas, Selantra's Crystal began to fail in front of the benches. Velcrissa stayed in her seat. In a low voice she hurried it to its settling, and the hall took her working for a change in the weather. She never told the hall whose it was.
Later she gave up the last of her chairs and the fund that paid it. She came to Altherion without a post. In a hired room there Virelsa spoke to her and to Selantra, and the three agreed to work as one order. They named it the Triune of the Three-Headed Serpent, with Virelsa as its Matron.
Some time after Altherion, she stopped working alone. The relief came first. Something worse came after, once she worked out that the relief was no longer being solely responsible. She worked out, too, that what she had handed it to did not sign anything. She had watched that pattern in the practitioners she guided for as long as she had done the work. She recognised it in herself.

#### The Cost

Her name went on nothing she settled. The hall kept a failing Crystal and a change in the weather in its memory. She never held a failing Crystal in its shape, as the physicians had held her mother's. Her working could not be done loudly. A raised voice ended it.
When the Creviceward Circle sent for the Triune's Matron, Velcrissa came with Virelsa to the exclusion line the Accord keeps around the Crevice. Virelsa held a bridge open there, and Estrella sutured a broken man across it. The rite emptied Estrella. She could not stand. Velcrissa hurried her settling and sat the night with her.

#### Where They Stand

Velcrissa is a shadow binder, a Temperance guide and a spiritual mediator. She holds no chair. She is second of the Triune, and she works beside Selantra and Virelsa as one of three. Selantra owes her the settling in the hall. Estrella owes her the night at the line. The Scholars she walked through their Thresholds owe her their passage, and most of them never knew whose working carried them.
In any room she is quieter than the background.

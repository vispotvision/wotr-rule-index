# Verinus VII · The Palatine

**Say it** · VAIR-ih-nus the SEVENTH · *Palatine* PAL-ah-tyne

### The Palatine · Keeper of the Sounding Line · First Auditor of the Scale

---

### I. IDENTITY

**As Of:** In Utopia, after the fourteenth day beneath Altherion
**Regnal Name:** Verinus VII
**Birth Name:** Osric Peyser — **struck from every record of the Holy Sea on the day of his elevation**, as the office requires. The parish of Coldwater Steps, where he was born, has never once been persuaded to stop using it. Four Palatines have sent instruction. The fishermen say Osric.
**Alias / Title:** The Palatine
**Also Known As:** The Weigher's Boy (Coldwater Steps, affectionately) · The Quiet Sea (Accord diplomatic shorthand) · **Him Who Asked The Wrong Question** (a phrase from his own homily, taken up by his critics without alteration, which he considers fair)
**Race / Lineage:** Human — Concord, coastal. **Three of the last nine Palatines were not human**, and the Register lists race in the same column it lists eye colour, which is to say as a fact of no doctrinal consequence whatsoever
**Age:** 71 · **Sex:** Male
**Realm / Faction:** The Holy Sea of Alabaster. Head of state and head of the faith, seated at the Alabaster Court. Hosts a Sancta Lux chapter hold that reports past him to the Grand Church at Altherion, an arrangement he negotiated and has twice declined to renegotiate in his own favour
**Office:** **Palatine.** The word means *of the household*. The doctrine is that the Scale is sovereign and the Palatine is its servant, and the title was chosen in the founding century specifically so that no holder could forget which of the two of them was the office
**Catalyst Event:** At twenty-six he was a junior Auditor at the Ebb Court, and he weighed the testimony of a woman named Meren against the man who owned her. **Her testimony was the heaviest he had ever recorded and it was true.** The court required corroboration under the procedure of the time, and there was none, because the only other people who had been in the room were also owned. He recorded the weight accurately, entered the finding accurately, and the case failed on procedure. The slaver collected his property at the harbour steps in front of the court, and Verinus watched the woman he had just proved go down the steps ahead of him. **He had been correct on the instrument and wrong in the world**, and forty-five years later he still cannot get anyone to understand that those are two different columns. The Holy Sea's theological position that persons are not commodities is his. He drafted it at thirty-one. It passed at forty. **He has never once described that as a victory**

### III. WELLSPRING HARMONIZATIONS

**Thaloré — The Wellspring of Depth. Primary.** Fluxia · Fluid Dynamics · analogue hydrostatic pressure. Pressure at depth depends on the density of the fluid and how far down you are, and on nothing else. **Not on the vessel. Not on the volume. Not on the surface.** Thaloré gives emotional content that same constitutive relation, and the Holy Sea's entire theology is one sentence of hydrostatics: a testimony's weight is its density times its depth and has nothing to do with who is carrying it. This is why the Scale does not distinguish between one race's testimony and another's. **The doctrine is not a moral position the kingdom arrived at. It is a physical property of the Wellspring it is built on**
**Oblation — The Offering Flame. Secondary.** Fluxia · Fluid Dynamics · analogue Bernoulli's principle. Fluid accelerating through a narrowing trades pressure for velocity and does not gain energy, and the total across the streamline is conserved. **Oblation formalises soul-cost as exactly that trade: what is surrendered in one term appears in another, and the total is not increased by the sacrifice.** Verinus's four-hundred-year-old predecessors used this to build the reconciliation that lets the Holy Sea condemn interest and bank at Ironlink Hold in the same breath. Interest claims the total increases. Oblation demonstrates that it does not, and that what looks like increase is pressure traded for velocity somewhere the lender is not looking. **The chain-cities regard the argument with genuine professional admiration and have never found the flaw in it**
**Judicium — Judgment. Tertiary, and held weakly.** Fulguria · Electromagnetism. His interface with Sancta Lux and with every court outside the Holy Sea. **He distrusts it and says so from the pulpit.** Judicium measures and then decides. Thaloré only measures. Verinus's standing position is that the second operation should never be performed by the same body that performed the first, and the Grand Church's standing position is that this is a very convenient doctrine for a man who has both

### VIII. TRAITS

### IX. DOMAIN

**THE SOUNDING**
*Seated. Continuous. Nineteen years without interruption.*
Metaphysical law within **fifty kilometres**, which covers the Alabaster Court, the capital, the harbour, and four of the six inland parishes. **Everyone in the Holy Sea's political centre lives inside it, permanently, and has for a generation.**
Within the Sounding, a spoken statement acquires literal hydrostatic weight equal to its density times its depth. **Density is how much of the speaker is actually in it. Depth is what saying it costs them.** A rehearsed lie has neither and floats. A prepared speech weighs almost nothing regardless of its truth. A confession that costs the speaker their standing, their family, or their life sinks, and it sinks hard enough to be felt through the floor by everyone in the chamber.
**Rank contributes nothing.** Wealth contributes nothing. Volume contributes nothing, which is why nobody in the capital raises their voice and why visiting diplomats find the city unnerving before they can say why. **The Palatine's own word weighs exactly what he has paid for it and not one gram more**, and this is not a courtesy he extends. It is a property of the Wellspring he cannot alter.
**And here is the flaw, which he has published, twice, over the objection of his own Curia.** The Sounding does not measure whether a statement is *true*. It measures the depth of the person saying it. **A sincere man who is wrong sinks exactly as far as a sincere man who is right.** The Holy Sea's courts have run for four centuries on an instrument that reads conviction and reports it as testimony, and the doctrine that closes the gap, the corroboration requirement, is the same procedure that lost Meren's case. Verinus is the first Palatine to state the problem out loud from the pulpit and has not solved it in nineteen years of trying.

### X. TECHNIQUES

**THE READING** · *Signature · Thaloré · Vessel Transfer*
*"Da mihi pondus. Ego stabo."* — Give me the weight. I will stand.
**Trigger:** He takes the speaker's hands. Both. It is a physical act and cannot be performed at distance, and the requirement is structural rather than ceremonial.
**Function:** He takes a single testimony's full weight onto himself so that the speaker does not have to carry it while giving it.
**Mechanism:** Fluid Dynamics. Thaloré's constitutive relation is pressure equals density times depth times the gravitational term, and the term the Wellspring permits him to alter is which body the column stands on. **He does not lessen the pressure. He relocates the vessel.** The full hydrostatic load of the statement transfers to his own Attraction Layer and acts on him for the duration, and the physiological presentation is exact: elevated intrathoracic pressure, reduced venous return, the specific grey-lipped breathlessness of a diver at depth.
**Numerical Effect:** Loads between 40 and 900 kPa depending on the testimony, against a human tolerance for sustained thoracic compression somewhere near 100. **He has taken four above 700 and has been carried out of the chamber on three of those occasions.**
**Target Response:** The speaker can finish. **That is the entire effect and it is why the technique exists.** Witnesses who could not previously get through an account get through it, in full, in one sitting.
**Limitation:** One at a time. **He cannot put it down mid-reading**, and a testimony abandoned halfway acts on both parties at once.
**Weakness:** For the duration he is a seventy-one-year-old man under several atmospheres, in a public chamber, holding someone's hands. **Every assassination attempt on his life has been made during a Reading and all four have failed for reasons that had nothing to do with him.**
**Cost:** Permanent and cumulative. The pressure does not fully release. **Forty-five years of Readings have left him with the cardiovascular architecture of a career deep diver and the barotrauma to match**, and the Court's physicians have told him to stop, in writing, eleven times.
**Counterplay:** Not a combat technique and has no combat application. **The counterplay is the one everybody actually uses: do not testify.**
**Codex Assignment:** [Vo] Voice · [Let] Tide · [Sel] Measure · Primary Thaloré · Secondary Oblation · Fluxia · Fluid Dynamics
**First Display:** Low stakes. A frightened junior clerk who cannot get through a routine deposition, and a Palatine who crosses the chamber and takes her hands, and a room that understands the rule in one beat.
**Sensory Description:** Her voice steadies. His does not. The gold coils on his cuff go dull as the reading is recorded into them. **He is breathing through his mouth by the third sentence and does not let go.**
**Corruption Vector:** An Obsession-Force Reading runs the transfer in reverse. **The weight goes onto the speaker and keeps going**, and a testimony delivered under that pressure is whatever the holder of the technique needs it to be, delivered sincerely, and reading as heavy and true to every instrument in the room.
**THE CONSTRICTION** · *Oblation · Cost Transfer · Conserved*
*"Non minuitur. Transfertur."* — It is not lessened. It is moved.
**Trigger:** A stated, witnessed, and accepted penance. All three, in order, aloud.
**Function:** He moves a soul-cost from the party who owes it to a party who will carry it, most often himself.
**Mechanism:** Fluid Dynamics, and specifically the conservation of total head across a narrowing. **The debt is not forgiven and is not reduced.** Oblation trades the pressure term for the velocity term across the constriction and the sum along the streamline is conserved exactly, which means a penance moved is a penance paid in full by someone. **The doctrine of the Holy Sea is that this is the only honest form of mercy available and that every other form is an accounting error.**
**Numerical Effect:** No reduction. Ever. **Transfer efficiency 1.0 by definition**, and any apparent gain is the flow accelerating somewhere the observer is not looking.
**Target Response:** Relief that arrives with the specific unpleasant clarity of knowing exactly where it went.
**Limitation:** **The recipient must accept it aloud, at depth, inside the Sounding.** Nobody can be volunteered.
**Weakness:** Cavitation. Drop the pressure term far enough and the medium boils at ambient temperature, and the collapsing voids pit the conduit that formed them. **Verinus has cavitated twice** and both events are in the Fracture Log.
**Cost:** Whatever was moved. That is the technique.
**Vow:** He has never used it to move a cost away from himself and the Register would show it if he had.
**Counterplay:** Refuse to accept, which is free, immediate, and available to everyone. **The Curia's position that this makes the technique useless has been the losing side of the argument for nineteen years.**
**Codex Assignment:** [Vo] Voice · [Ur] Balance · [Vael] Renewal · Primary Oblation · Secondary Thaloré · Fluxia · Fluid Dynamics
**First Display:** A fisherman's ruinous debt, a parish that cannot cover it, and a Palatine who takes it, and a scene that ends with somebody working out that the money still has to come from somewhere.
**Sensory Description:** A drop in the room. Ears pop. **The gold coils on the front panel of his robe run one turn longer.**
**Corruption Vector:** An Obsession-Force Constriction does not require acceptance. It finds the nearest vessel with the structural capacity to hold the cost and puts it there, and structural capacity is not the same thing as consent or fault.
**NOTHING WEIGHS MORE FOR BEING SAID BY YOU** · *Passive · Vow · The Refusal*
*"Quod dicitur, dicitur. Non quis dixerit."* — What is said is said. Not who said it.
**Trigger:** Permanent, unconditional, and a property of Thaloré rather than a decision of his.
**Function:** Inside the Sounding, no statement gains weight from the rank, wealth, race, office, or reputation of the speaker. **Including his.**
**Mechanism:** Fluid Dynamics. Hydrostatic pressure at depth is a function of density and depth and is independent of the vessel. **The vessel is the speaker.** The doctrine that the Scale does not distinguish between one race's testimony and another's is not an ethical commitment the Holy Sea made and could revoke. It is the constitutive relation of the Wellspring the kingdom is built on, and a Palatine who wished to revoke it would first have to change the physics.
**Numerical Effect:** No figure. **The absence of a figure is the point.**
**Target Response:** Crowns arriving at the Alabaster Court to negotiate discover within a sentence that their titles have stopped functioning as arguments, and the recorded diplomatic reaction to this ranges from fury to something several ambassadors have described as relief.
**Limitation:** Absolute and symmetrical. **He cannot weight his own word and has tried, once, at fifty-eight, in a matter he was certain about.** It did not work and he has published the attempt.
**Weakness:** A sincere fanatic outweighs a cautious expert every time. **The Sounding reads conviction and calls it testimony**, and the Holy Sea's worst four judicial errors all have this shape.
**Cost:** Everything the office might otherwise have been able to do quickly.
**Counterplay:** Believe your own lie hard enough. **It works, it has worked, and the Register documents three cases**, and the reason the Curia has not suppressed that documentation is that Verinus published it himself before they could decide.
**Codex Assignment:** [Sel] Measure · [Va] Law · Primary Thaloré · Secondary Judicium · Fluxia primary with Fulguria secondary · Fluid Dynamics. **Cross-family flagged: [Va] Law is a Petralon assignment under Vectoria and is doing the binding rather than the measuring**
**First Display:** A visiting baron, a fisherman, and a dispute over a jetty, in which the baron discovers the order of speaking is the only advantage available to him.
**Sensory Description:** Nothing visible. **A room in which everyone has quietly worked out that shouting is not going to help.**

### XIII. PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION

He is tall and has become narrow with it, the shoulders gone forward over a chest that carries the barrel-deepening of a man who has spent forty-five years breathing against pressure. The frame reads wrong at first glance and correctly at second: thin arms, thin wrists, thin neck, and a ribcage that belongs on someone forty kilos heavier. His hands are long-fingered and clean and the nails are cut square, and the backs of them carry the raised pale vessels of age and the fine broken capillary tracery that the Court physicians have written to him about eleven times.
The face is spare and vertically long, the cheeks fallen in under high flat bones, the nose straight and thin with a slight downward set at the tip. His beard is close-cropped and entirely white and follows the jaw without covering it, and he shaves the upper lip in the coastal parish habit he was raised with and has never modernised. His eyes are pale grey and set deep beneath a brow that has been drawn together for so long that the two vertical lines between them do not relax when he sleeps. **His lips carry a faint permanent bluish cast at the edges**, which is the barotrauma, and which the Court has agreed among itself not to mention.
The working vestments are dove-grey wool, undyed and fulled heavy, cut long to the instep with sleeves wide enough to fold the hands into. Over the right shoulder and across the chest lies the stole, gold couched onto grey in raised spiral coils the width of a thumbnail, and every coil on it is a **sounding**: one testimony he personally weighed and entered. The orphrey panel running down the front of the robe carries the same work at larger scale, spirals the size of a fist stacked in overlapping ranks from the belt to the hem, and the panel is not decorative. **It is the record of his tenure and it has been extended twice.** A stiffened gold cuff covers his left forearm from wrist to elbow, coiled the same way, and this one is the current case: it is worked during proceedings and cut off and archived when the matter closes. The belt is plain grey leather set with small gold studs at intervals, and the intervals are graduated, and the graduation is a real scale that a Court clerk can read across a chamber to know how long he has been standing.
The cap is the office. Tall, stiffened, tapering to a blunt point, dove-grey wool over a rigid frame with gold couching at the crown and a lappet falling on either side to the shoulder. **It is heavier than it looks and he has worn it for nineteen years and his neck shows it.** He is a head taller in it than he is out of it, and he takes it off in every private room he enters, immediately, the way another man loosens a collar.
Ceremonially, alabaster white replaces the grey and the gold is doubled. **He wears the ceremonial once a year and has described it, from the pulpit, as an object designed by people who had correctly understood that nobody would listen to a fisherman's son in ordinary clothes.**

### XIV. PSYCHOLOGY

**Core Wound:** The harbour steps. Not the failure, and not the woman, though both are in it. **The specific discovery that an instrument can be perfectly honest and still be pointed at the wrong question**, and that the people operating it will defend the honesty as though it answered the objection. He has never been able to make that distinction land in another person's head at speed, and he has been trying for forty-five years.
**Flaw:** He will not decide. The doctrine that measuring and deciding must never be performed by the same body is intellectually serious, defensible, and also **an extremely elegant permanent excuse**, and Deacon Barrow has said so to his face in open session. He did not have an answer. He extended her appointment the following spring.
**Desire:** To close the gap between the reading and the verdict without putting the verdict in his own hands. **Nineteen years. Two published attempts. No solution.**
**Strength:** He cannot be flattered, bribed, outranked or shouted down, because none of those things weigh anything in the room he is standing in, and he has arranged his entire life to be standing in that room.
**Loop:** Reads a testimony. Finds it heavy and true. Discovers the procedure will not act on it. Reforms the procedure. Discovers the new procedure has a different hole. **Publishes the hole himself before his critics can find it**, which costs him standing every time and is the only thing he has ever done that he is unambiguously proud of.
**Foil:** Darius, who measures and then refuses the seat that would let him decide. **Verinus took the seat and then built a doctrine forbidding himself to use it.** Same conclusion, opposite route, and neither of them has ever said this to the other.
**Voice / Dialect:** Concord Common, coastal cadence surviving under forty-five years of court register. **A speech-maker: long, built sentences, whole paragraphs in one breath, closing on a line short enough to carry out of the room.** He never raises his voice, because inside the Sounding volume does nothing, and this has spread outward until the entire capital speaks quietly and nobody there can explain why. Occupational metaphor is nautical and specifically about depth: soundings, drift, ballast, the lead line, what a thing draws. **When he has come to answer something he has carried for years, the short sentences give way to a set-piece speech**: an audit delivered whole, at length and without interruption, and the one time he talks longer than the room does.
**Dialogue Evolution:** At twenty-six he argued. At forty he drafted. **Now he speaks, at length and at the same low level, until the room has nowhere left to stand**, and people who have not met him before consistently mistake the first two minutes for weakness.
**Speech:** *"Say it at depth or do not say it."* (signature) · *"The instrument was honest. The question was mine."* · *"That is a correct answer. Whose question was it?"*
**Posture:** Stands. Always. **He has never sat in the Palatine's chair and the chair is brought to every session anyway.** Hands folded into the sleeves. Head tilted slightly down and to the left when listening, which is a habit from taking Readings and which people find unbearable to be on the receiving end of.
**Stress Default:** The speeches stop. He goes short and declarative and every sentence is an answer, **and everyone who has worked with him for more than a year knows that this is the sound of a man who has decided something and is very frightened of it.**

### XV. EQUIPMENT

**The Sounding Line** — a plaited flax cord, knotted at graduated intervals, with a plain lead weight at the end. Four hundred years old, entirely non-magical, replaced cord-and-all every generation. **It is the actual instrument of the office and it is a fisherman's tool**, and the founding Palatine chose it over anything a smith could have made because a lead line cannot be persuaded.
**The Alabaster Ring** — worn on the right hand. White stone, uncut, unpolished, set plain. **Struck from the block of the Court's foundation stone at each elevation and broken at each death**, which means every Palatine's ring is a piece of the same building and none of them are the same ring.
**The Case Cuff** — the current gold forearm-piece, worked during proceedings, cut off and archived when the matter closes. **Nineteen years of them fill a room.**
**The Grey Vestments** — working dress. Wool, unadorned but for the record-work. He owns two sets and one is always at the fuller's.
**No blade, no staff, no relic, no focus.** He has never carried one and the Court's Armoury has never had an entry under his name.

### Voice

**Notices first** · The question behind the answer: who asked it, who is paying for the reply, and who is standing next to the speaker when the bottom comes out. He hears how much of a person is actually in what they say before he hears whether it is true.
**Sentence length** · Long. He is a speech-maker: whole paragraphs spoken in one breath, built clause on clause toward a closing line short enough to be carried out of the room, and delivered at the same low volume whether he is addressing a fishwife or the Curia.
**Contractions** · never, in office. The coastal parish speech he was raised in contracts freely, and it comes back only in grief.
**Pet word** · "I would like": the record to show it, her to be specific, you to sit in this seat one day. Under it runs the harbour: the lead line, depth, ballast, what a thing draws.
**Never says** · Anything louder than the room needs, a flattery, or a word he has not paid for. He never tells a man his sentence weighed when it did not, and he gives exact figures only when reading a testimony or the register aloud; of his own life he speaks in round years.
**Stumbles** · Never in public. The speeches are finished before he begins them.
**Gloss rights** · Yes, for the Sounding, the Scale and the doctrine of weight, which he explains at length to anyone who asks and to several who did not.
**Under stress** · The speeches stop. He goes short and declarative and every sentence is an answer, never a question, and everyone who has worked with him a year knows it is the sound of a man who has decided something and is very frightened of it.
**In grief** · The court register drains away and the Coldwater Steps come back: short harbour sentences, a first name, the lead line paid out ("The line ran out, Ilse. All of it."), the Weigher's Boy under forty-odd years of office.
**In joy** · The voice holds its level and its length. The joy is in the hands folding into the wide sleeves, the breath let out through the mouth, and the grey eyes easing where the two lines between the brows never do.

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card, the 15 archived scenes it appears in, and the record of The Curia at Altherion. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Verinus VII was born Osric Peyser at Coldwater Steps, a seaward parish of the Holy Sea of Alabaster. His parents had come to that coast as frontier people. They cut peat and set nets. They buried two children in ground they had cleared themselves. Neither parent was ever inside a court. His father weighed salt bars on a dock and counted every stack aloud, so the man across the table could hear him doing it. The parish called the boy the Weigher's Boy.
At nine he came home crying about another boy on the Steps. His mother told him that a person was not what they had done to you, nor what they had been born into, nor what the parish said of them. If he wanted to know what somebody was, he would have to weigh them himself, honestly, and he would probably not enjoy the result. He never found anything in the founding archive that improved on his mother's rule.

#### The Making

He went through the seminary fast and tidy, unremarkable in a cohort of forty, and the Ebb Court took him on as a junior Auditor. At twenty-six he weighed the testimony of a woman named Meren against the man who owned her. It was the heaviest testimony he had ever recorded, and it was true.
The procedure of the time required corroboration. There was none. The only other people who had been in the room were also owned. He recorded the weight accurately and entered the finding accurately, and the case failed on procedure. The slaver collected his property at the harbour steps in front of the court, and Verinus watched the woman he had just proved go down the steps ahead of him. He had been correct on the instrument and wrong in the world. It broke something in him along the seam between what he had measured and what had happened. It scarred, and the scar became the most productive thing about him.

#### The Cost

He drafted the Holy Sea's position that persons are not commodities at thirty-one. It passed at forty. He never called it a victory. In the same years he wrote the corroboration provision, which holds that a single reading is not a finding, because the instrument sinks a sincere man who is wrong exactly as far as one who is right. It was the same requirement that had lost Meren's case.
At thirty-four he lost both parents inside eleven days, in a coastal fever he had spent two years drafting a containment against. The parishes had adopted it late. He checked the draft afterward and found it sound. The house at Coldwater Steps held a boat and a set of salt scales. For four years he built an account in which the fault was his, because a fault could be corrected.
At forty-four he was present when a boarding action against a trafficking vessel went wrong, and for about four seconds he stopped being a measuring instrument. Eleven died. Nine were the crew, and two were people he had come to get out. He reported himself to the chapter hold of Sancta Lux in writing that evening, before anyone asked. The finding was lawful use of force in defence of persons. He asked four times for its review.
For forty-five years he took testimony onto his own chest, holding both of the speaker's hands, and it left him with the barotrauma of a deep diver and a blue cast at the lips. Twice, at fifty-one and at sixty-three, he carried another's cost further than the working would bear, and the damage stayed. At fifty-eight he read his own testimony at full depth. It told him he had built a doctrine to keep himself from ever being wrong again. He lay insensible for nine days.
At his investiture he read aloud the lowest figure for force ever entered against a Palatine's name, and asked that it be minuted. He never sat in the Palatine's chair. He never sent the parish the instruction that four Palatines before him had sent, and the fishermen still say Osric. He held that the body which measures must never be the body which decides. Deacon Ilse Barrow called that an elegant permanent excuse in open session. He had no answer. He extended her appointment the following spring. He had two of the chapel windows that carried his likeness taken out.
At Vaeloris the provision came due. The parish deacon there had asked the Court for a chapter detachment, a Sealwright and forty men. Verinus deferred it in writing to wait for corroboration. In the valley he knelt with his palm on the ground and counted the dead back aloud, one at a time, in his father's dialect. Six hundred and six. The roll said seven hundred and forty-one. He counted a second time because he could not stand up. He had the deferral filed beside the return. Barrow told him it would end him, and he answered that it would end the provision.

#### Where They Stand

In the lower chapel he blessed the four-month-old son of Charles Lambert. The child weighed on his floor before making a sound. At the blessing the boy's eyes went gold. Verinus told the bishop that the boy would not be written down. Then he sent Charles north with the Kusharan party.
On the fourteenth day he came down into the cellar beneath the Grand Church in the Palatine's gold, with chalk on his fingers. Aurelian had hung on a frame there for fourteen days, for four Attestants and a room of books. Verinus declared the surety his own.
"Do you consent," said Verinus.
"No."
"Entered."
The wounds crossed the room into him in the order they had been made. The four had already gone out through the low cloister. Corwin Sesk knew, and said nothing.
At the root of the wound he sealed what he could of Kujo, and the seal took pieces and left the man. He could have saved perhaps four thousand at the price of the rite. He did the sum and let thirty-five in every hundred go. He became the wall. He had taken Aurelian for its anchor without asking. When the draw turned down the line toward the cellar he tried to take the line back, and the rite would not permit the amendment. The Judger's hand lifted the Custodia out of the world, still standing, and set him down in Utopia with his cap gone and his hands empty. He put his palm flat on the floor. It held him and did not sink.
He is still Palatine. An empty chair still stands at every session of the Weighing, and he has never found Meren. The line to Aurelian stays tied. It has no release valve, and Aurelian has not been told what he holds. Sefa Bream of the Steps has an appointment on the twenty-third, and Barrow has said she will keep it whether or not he is standing. He has told three people, and written nowhere, that Darius will come back. The word Altherion uses for him when it thinks he is out of the room, he has neither claimed nor put down.

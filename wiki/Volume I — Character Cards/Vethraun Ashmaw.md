# Vethraun Ashmaw

**He Who Swallowed the Inner World and Called It His Stomach**
> *The Hollow Throne · Jaw of the First Age · He Who Ate the Sky's Name*
> 
> > *"I did not conquer the Inner World. I simply reminded it that its hunger and mine were the same hunger, and that mine was older."*

---

## Identity and Core Profile

**Race / Lineage** · **Draconic Primordial**, descended from the emanational line of **Omnis, Skyfather of Draconis.** *The nearest a mortal lineage has drifted toward Titan without crossing the threshold.* **His blood carries the Titan-echo like a memory that has not forgotten it was once law.**
**Faction / Era** · The Ossuary Sovereign Court, self-founded · **First Eon: before the Guild Accord, before any Concord had names**
**Temperance Stage** · **Stage 10, Dominion** — *the soul no longer negotiates with the world. It issues terms.*
> **He is not a destroyer. He is a collector.**
>
> The Inner World he did not burn. **He ate it whole**, absorbing its Wellsprings into his Triune Core until they recognised no sovereign but him.
>
> His motivation is **hunger for completeness** — a cosmological arche born from the wound of being almost-Titan and never quite. **He does not draw from the world. He ingests it.**
>
> Ultimate goal: **absorb enough Wellspring-law that the universe reclassifies him as a Titan** — not by surface conquest, but by making every underground vein of Aether bend to his pulse first.

## Canonical System Metrics

**Aether Class** · **Devourer-Emitter** *(hybrid; exceedingly rare)*
His Aether **inhales first**, drawing ambient Essence toward his sternum like a low-pressure void before erupting in condensed, dense-layered bursts. Intake reads as a lambent grey-black inward shimmer. Emission is volcanic, vermillion-dark, chthonic mineral-dust in suspension.
> **Observers report the sensation of their own Essence being surveyed before he acts.**
> **Risk · Wellspring Metabolic Overload.** Absorbing more than four active resonances simultaneously, **his Triune Core begins metabolising his own Soul Crystal.**
>
> **He does not always notice.** His arche blinds him to the self-consumption **until the damage is generational.**

### Wellspring affinity

**Triune Core** · **Chthonica** *(deep-earth law, tectonic inevitability)* · **Pyraeon** *(draconic fire, will made exothermic)* · **Anamnesis** *(recollection of ancient memory — every Wellspring consumed adds its history to his own)*
**Network** · **Fourteen** additional Wellsprings currently infected and networked. ***The Inner World's surviving settlements live on his exhale.***
**Targeted Uninfected** · **Six** remaining, including the sealed vault of **Sublimatio**, which he believes will tip his classification to Titan-adjacent.

### The ascent

Born with a Soul Crystal stratified in three layers: **Draconic Inheritance at the base, Chthonic Attunement in the middle, Pyraeon Ignition at the crown.** *His ascent was geological.*
**Stages 1–5** climbed over centuries, **consuming Wellspring-adjacent creatures and metabolising their Temperance residue directly.** **Stages 6–8** earned in the silent wars of the Inner World, **witnessed only by cave-walls still carrying Essence-scorch.** **Stage 9** came through a **Titan-echo bone relic of Omnis, Skyfather's first molt**, crystallising his Draconic lineage.
> **Stage 10** opened during **the Swallowing of Perathane**, when he metabolised an entire city's Wellspring-bond in a single sustained ingestion — **and the Archon Irath, Flame of Will and Dominion, took notice.**

## Core Disciplines

- ****Perathane Swallow** · 420 EU activation / 35 EU/s sustained**
  Opens the Metabolic Channel. Over six to ten seconds, **pulls Aether from the local Wellspring-field**, denying opponent access within 40 m while feeding his reserves.

  **The Wellspring is not destroyed. It is subordinated** — rerouted through him, loyal only to his pulse.

  *Matte-black inward shimmer from his sternum, deep stone grinding, and the sensation of one's breath being audited by something older than refusal.*
- ****Ossuary Law** · 290 EU per strike**
  Loads Chthonic Flux to maximum compression through the Body Path kinetic chain. **The visible strike is a feint.**

  The real weapon is a sub-surface shockwave through the ground's mineral-Aether lattice, **erupting beneath the target one beat later.**

  **Dodging the strike means stepping into the eruption.** Fate-Path geometry sets the eruption point at wherever the dodge resolves. ***The lag is the weapon.***
- ****The Unwritten Dominion** · 800 EU open / 60 EU/s ongoing**
  Within roughly 80 m, **the environment is reclassified as deepest Inner World stratum.**

  Gravity increases 1.4×. Light-Wellspring expressions suppressed to 60%. **Every footstep and Essence-expenditure inside pulses through his sternum as information.**

  **Opponents fight in his vocabulary, on his terms.** A deliberate counter to Luminalis and Ascensio-aligned opponents.

### Costs and consequences

> **Metabolic Drift** · Absorbing outside-affinity Wellsprings corrupts his Soul Crystal's outer ring. Post-combat: **involuntary memory-floods, dissociation, identity fracture, Aether-signature reading as someone else.** *He considers this the price of completeness.*
>
> **Tectonic Overcommit** · Three rapid Ossuary Law activations without rest overloads the Chthonic channel. **The fourth strike and beyond propagate the shockwave upward through his own skeleton at 12% outgoing force per instance.** *He does not always stop.*
>
> **The Hollow Echo** · If Perathane Swallow is active and reserves drop below 800 EU, **the channel inverts, draining an additional 80 EU/s** until the technique ends or the threshold is crossed. **Invisible to him mid-combat. He has overstayed it before.**

In combat: **ward-disruption within four metres, dead contract-law cancelling active oath-law.**

## Lore and Trajectory

**Allegiance** · **Sovereign-Null.** No faction, Archon, Titan or Concord acknowledged. He receives *attention* from **Irath, Flame of Will and Dominion** — which he interprets as professional acknowledgment — and *displeasure* from **Maelor, Keeper of Memory**, **who regards Metabolic Memory as desecration of the Akashic function.**
> **The Consumption of Perathane** · *First Eon, roughly 340 years into the Deep Age.*
>
> A civilisation of roughly two hundred thousand across a four-node Wellspring-nexus. He spent **eleven years living adjacent**, slowly shifting the metaphysical climate, then activated Perathane Swallow at full expression.
>
> **The civilisation did not burn.** Over seventy-two hours it decelerated, its Wellspring-bond transferring entirely to his core. **The people remained. The Aether that gave them shape was gone.**
>
> *Those seventy-two hours are the loudest voice in his Anamnesis archive.*
**The Archonic Audience of Stage 9** · When the Omnis bone-relic crystallised his lineage, **Irath manifested in the Caldera.** Not as attack. Not as summons. **As witness.** Stood four minutes. **Departed without a word.**
> His working interpretation: ***The Archon of Dominion came to see if I was ready. It decided not yet. I intend to be disagreed with on that point.***

### Counters

**Sublimatio-aligned opponents** · Sublimatio's refinement of dense matter into higher-state energy **directly inverts his Chthonic densification.** A high-Temperance practitioner can disperse the geological weight of his strikes before impact. *Does not stop him; delays him and inflates Ossuary Law's cost significantly.*
**Animatria disruption / Nexus severance** · A high-tier Animatria practitioner reaching his Soul Crystal's Nexus layer and introducing dissonance — **conflating absorbed memories, injecting false resonance** — forces accelerated involuntary Metabolic Drift. **The closest thing to a decisive counter.** *Getting close enough is the problem.*
**Ascensio-Luminalis high-flux** · Spiking Luminalis-Ascensio Flux above 280 EU/g **briefly pierces the Dominion's light-suppression for three to four seconds.** In that window his scale-carapace and tectonic gravity field lose roughly 35% effectiveness. ***The only window where his durability approaches manageable.***
> **Current status** · Active in the Ossuary Caldera, deep Inner World. **Not hidden. Simply below.**
>
> *The surface kingdoms have stories about the tremors. Most have the wrong explanation.*
>
> **Six Wellsprings remain.** He moves toward Sublimatio deliberately last, believing its ingestion will reclassify his metaphysical designation entirely. **Whether the universe agrees remains the open question.**
>
> Maelor has begun, quietly, searching for someone capable of stopping him before that question is answered. ***That counter-narrative has not yet found a protagonist.***

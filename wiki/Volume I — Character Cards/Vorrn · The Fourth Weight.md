### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The long memory. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Vorrn was assembled in Abythron, Zar'thuun's domain in the Abyssal Depths. Zar'thuun unmade what was excess. The ground of his domain was the Wellspring of Dread, a sphere of absolute black ringed by dissolving starlight, and it absorbed where every other Wellspring radiated.
He had no mother and no house. His matter was older than he was. Three civilisations had been destroyed, three worlds that had already been told to stop existing, and their Wellsprings went out with them. Abythron kept what was left. It kept the compacted spiritual mass of those peoples and the resonance of Wellsprings that no longer ran. From that it built him.
He was the fourth such construction the domain had produced. No register that could be verified ever named the first three.

#### The Making

Into his spiritual structure Abythron set the Fourth Seal, a void-mark carried inside him and never worn. Any being of Titan or Archon class who read his Essence read the seal with it. It told them, without language, that engaging him was Abythron's considered decision.
He was given no bond, no network and nothing to hold. The Wellsprings in him he kept as a cistern keeps water, and he drank from none of them. Nobody gave him the capacity to be moved by anything. Nobody found a use for building him one. Waste needs an intention, and he was not built with one, so nothing he spent was wasted.
He was assembled complete, at the ceiling, from the wreckage of three worlds that had already been told to stop existing. For Vorrn there was no accumulation and no climb. He was built already recognised.

#### The Cost

The Abythron Plate was forged around him as a containment structure, to keep him shut against his own full output at every hour. Every crack in it marked a time containment failed. Between engagements it was repaired, and each repair left it a little more porous than the last.
Blows that reached him thinned the body he showed the world. They never reached his Core, which Abythron housed. What came back afterward was Abythron's pressure, filling the shape to its old measure. Nothing he took left him diminished.
Three times on record the plate opened fully, tearing along every crack line at once. For as long as it stood open he walked as a Void Fracture. His strikes carried a realm's force, and the bleed off him killed the Wellsprings of any ground he stood on. Each opening cost the plate a fixed share of itself that no repair gave back. Each closed the record of an engagement.
One of the three fell on a monastery in the Outer World. Three Wellsprings met on that ground, and two of them were the two his bleed ran on. Reality collapsed where they met, and everyone in the monastery died. When the plate closed, the ground had lost its Wellspring function for good. He carried a new crack away from it. He did not investigate. The Fourth Seal had already told him. A woman's consciousness there did not die with her body, and nothing in the record had him learn of it.
He explained none of it. Nothing about him was glossed for whoever watched, and when he spoke, the words were already final. In his own words: "The others were sent to break things. I was sent to finish the sentence." The finishing was paid for in his own plate, one crack at a time, and in ground that did not recover.

#### Where They Stand

Those who name him call him the Fourth Weight, or the Cracked God. He is large and wrong-proportioned. He is still faster than a thing that shape should be. The Abythron Plate is on him still, porous from its repairs and cracked along every line where containment has failed.
Wherever he stands still, the Wellsprings of the region lose output. After he moves on they stay low until a Sealwright works to restore them. The Guild Accord's standing protocol for a confirmed presence of his class is evacuation and Titan-tier notification. The Black Concord files his reclassifications and keeps the incident records. No other institution tracks him reliably. One reclassification, from the monastery's ground, lies copied in an archive beneath a Guild Accord registry building, with none of the incident beside it. Its reader has acted on none of it.
Zar'thuun is the one attested force with the standing and the resonance to engage him directly. Every other engagement of Titan class on record has ended in containment. The Black Concord's files have proposed a counter three times: force the descent on him again and again in quick succession, until it destabilises him. Nobody has tested it. The plan needs a volunteer to stay in the area for every descent.
Nobody knows whether the Fourth Weight ends on the day Abythron stops needing a fourth.

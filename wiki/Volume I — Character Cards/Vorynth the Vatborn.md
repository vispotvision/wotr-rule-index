### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Rot Host. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Vorynth the Vatborn is named for the vat in his torso, and every record of him starts there. None says where he was born, or to whom. None says what his life was before it. He brought one thing out of that life, and the card calls it a wound.
He was made by his own hand. His card files him an "Unbound Alchemical Aberration" and gives him one oath, sworn to Malphas as lieutenant. Malphas, called the Living Cauldron, had been expelled from the Pyraeon Forge Academy and declared a metaphysical threat. The command Vorynth serves in is built of people who did to their own bodies what he did to his. Zeraphine Drowl, the Regent over him, merged her body with a glass cistern. Graelith, the Host's Fourth General, hollowed out his chest into a basin and sank a sword in it. Vorynth holds the first rank among its generals.

#### The Making

No record says what the wound was, or how he came by it. It went untreated. He opened his torso where it lay and built what he carried into his own chassis.
He built the vat to take the dead. Corpses went into it and fermented. What came back out was a weaponised sludge and a rot-cloud vapour that hung over the ground he stood on. A spell thrown at him, or a man's flesh, went into the vat the same way and came out as a corrosive acid spray or as shards of boneglass.
The wound was made structural, made to hold weight on purpose. His torso was a vat.

#### The Cost

Around him, things came apart. Stone went brittle and steel corroded. Sinew rotted. Walls, armour and siege engines turned to brittle shells within hours. None of it was struck; it came to prefer being apart, and the longer he stayed, the faster it went. Entropy did not tell his structures from theirs, and the vat stood in the same ground. What it held discharged when he had not meant it to. The work took hours. A fight that ended sooner was lost to him before it began.
His waves would not keep a sequence for him. On the Broken Rivers, in the Varakh Drylands, a walled river town shut its gate on the Host. Vorynth walked up to the gate and stood in front of it through the afternoon. He never struck it. The iron bands flaked, the timbers went soft, and the facing of the gate towers crumbled under a hand. At dusk the town's practitioners came out at Zeraphine's glass, and Graelith turned them back. Then her cadence went down the sludge conduits. His waves came against the softened gate in the order she called them, and the gate went down. The town fell before morning, and he took its dead into the vat.
In the Bone Hills a fort kept a Domain over its walls, and its wards were cut into the gate stones. At that gate he could do nothing. The Host hauled the Ossuary Choir up the slope on a wagon bed, and the siblings played until the wards came apart and a crack ran up the stones from inside. Zeraphine called him then. He walked up through the cracked gate and stood in the yard, and over the hours the fort went brittle around him. Afterwards he said their music had drowned out his rot. He called it vainglorious.
The drought came the year after the Host took the Broken Rivers. In the towns where he had stood, the granaries had gone soft and grey, and nothing stored in them kept. The towns went hungry, and the Pale-Wreathed Sisterhood came in behind the hunger with Kytheris's spores. He was still quartered on the river. The rot-homunculi carried the famine dead out to him, and he took them into the vat and sprayed what it made back over the ground. Her fungus came up in the sludge. He went on taking the dead.

#### Where They Stand

Vorynth is First General of Rot's Bloom, sworn lieutenant of Malphas and the kinetic arm of the Cauldron's influence. Malphas's card names no general. Zeraphine holds ultimate authority over him, Kytheris, Graelith and Morphaene, and he counts her an invaluable field conductor. He holds his slow, relentless corrosion above the Choir's harmonic decay, and he still distrusts them. Where his rot reduces, Kytheris's fungi repurpose.
The counter written against him reads: do not give him time, or take Zeraphine. Cut her conduits, and his waves stop moving together.
The vat is under the same preference as every wall he stands in front of. Nothing written says what he was before it. His words begin, "Let structures rot to dust and spirits curdle into sludge".

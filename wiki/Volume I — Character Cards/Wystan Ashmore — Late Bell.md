# Wystan Ashmore — Late Bell

*Guild Accord, Arbitration Division, Night Register. Officer of Attribution.*

### I. Identity

**Name.** Wystan Ashmore. Northern register, chartered family, third son. The byname *Late Bell* is the Division's, not his.
**Standing.** Tier 6, Master. Title of address **Warden of Duty**. Nobody in the districts he works uses it.
**Level 300.** Band III, Sovereign. **Stage X, Realization.** Spirit Path, recognised. No Body, Attraction or Fate alignment.
**Service.** Eleven years on the desk, four of them after the demotion.
**Catalyst Event.** Nineteen, clerking assay tins in a Measurewright's roll-room. He opened a sealed tin without gloves and received the last minute of a man nine years dead, including the part where the man understood what was happening to him. Anamnesis found him before any instructor did. He filed the tin, went outside, was sick in the yard, then went back in and finished the shelf.
**Why nights.** The district mains go quiet after the eleventh division. An unlicensed draw is inaudible at noon and unmistakable at three. The Night Register exists for signal, not for atmosphere.

### II. Soul Architecture

**Soul Crystal tier.** Sovereign Crystal. **State.** Refined, with one unconsolidated fracture (XVII).
**Aether Class.** VI, Voidic. Lateral, Limina-aligned; no loss figure is documented for laterals. His Shell subtracts rather than amplifies, which is why his own workings are nearly silent and why he can hear everyone else's.
**Essence Typology Family.** Limina.
**η.** ~0.74. Placed inside the documented Master band of 0.70 to 0.80. No source figure on his sheet.
**EU reserve.** Governed by Tempering Yield at 80, D-Grade. Low for his Stage by a wide margin. No numeric table exists in source for Band III reserves, so the sheet carries none.
**Domain tier.** Realm-consolidated, Stage IX to X band.

### III. Wellspring Harmonizations

Three held, at shallow Depth. A professional choice, and also all his Attunement will carry.
**Anamnesis**, the Wellspring of Remembrance. Strengthens Retention, Analysis, Anchoring. His three peaks, in order.
**Nihiloth**, the Hollow Law. Strengthens Sovereignty, Persistence, Cognition. The law his Domain runs on, which removes rather than adds.
**Fixatio**, the Binding Flame. Strengthens Fortitude and Anchoring. What is held by Fixatio stays held, including a scene.
**Refused.** Vantabriel, offered twice by the Division. He will not carry a Wellspring that endures by remembering the lost, on the grounds that he already does that for a living.

### VI. Physical Force

D-Grade band. Travel 150 to 340 m/s, reaction 15 to 40 ms, attack speed subsonic to transonic. He can react to a shot. He cannot outrun one, and the Division's field manual says so in the paragraph he wrote.

### VII. Aether Flow

AU/s is Flux Density against η, and his Flux is 20. Output negligible; draw cost low for the same reason. Harmonics Stability 440 means he holds a clean read on contaminated ground, inside a hostile Domain, or in a district the Accord cut two hours ago.

### VIII. Traits

**Low Ground.** Passive. His Shell rests at near-zero saturation, so free residue in a closed volume runs its gradient toward him without being called.
**The Quiet Man.** Suppression 460, held continuously, waking and sleeping. It tires him and he does not drop it. Six people alive know what Stage he is.
**Occupational Load.** Filtration 120 against eleven years of ingested residue. His hands shake in the mornings. It is in his file as a disciplinary observation.

### IX. Domain — STAY

**Effect.** A sealed volume, roughly a large room. Inside it nothing may be altered. Residue stops dispersing, blood stops drying, a half-run working neither completes nor decays, and every operation attempted inside is recorded against the hand that attempted it. Witnesses describe the air going thick and the noise of the street arriving a half-second late.
**Cost.** The law binds him too. He cannot strike, seal, draw or open a door inside his own Domain, because all of those are alterations. His safest ground is the one place he is defenceless.
**Limit.** Radius 60. It is a room. It is never a street.
**Counter.** Walk in with a heavier Domain and overwrite it, or force him to drop it by threatening something outside the boundary. Two Enforcement men have used the second method and both got what they wanted.

### X. Techniques

**Low Ground** · *Trigger:* closed volume, sealed. *Function:* he becomes the lowest-saturation body present. *Mechanism:* free Aether runs the steepest potential gradient to the nearest sink; his Lattice is the sink and pulls hard. *Effect:* residue arrives on his skin carrying its source signature, and Anamnesis reads it on contact. *Cost:* he ingests everything the room was carrying, including whatever killed the occupant. *Limit:* free residue only; anything still integrated into a living Lattice stays where it is. *Counterplay:* bring a deeper sink, or flood the room with clean draw and break the gradient.
**The Hand That Held It** · *Trigger:* bare contact with an object that carried a working. *Function:* the last operation, returned in reverse. *Mechanism:* Anamnetic recall through Retention 620 and Analysis 700, anchored by Anchoring 420. *Effect:* he receives the operator's final minute as first-person memory, with the mechanism legible inside it. *Cost:* the minute does not leave. He carries forty-one of them. *Limit:* he gets the hand, never the purse. He can name who performed and never who paid, which is precisely what Article XV requires the Division to prove. *Counterplay:* Fixatio-seal the object, handle it through an intermediary, or wait a season.
**Called Late** · *Trigger:* a scene more than six hours cold. *Function:* reconstruction from a degraded field. *Mechanism:* Perception 560 modelling the room's geometry against the residue's decay profile, Fluency 480 reading any inscription still standing. *Effect:* a stated sequence of events, to the division of an hour. *Cost:* eight to fourteen hours standing, no food, and a Filtration debt paid the following week. *Counterplay:* alter the geometry before he arrives. Move one wall and his sequence is confidently, articulately wrong.
**What nobody knows.** Anamnetic contact always returns the last minute and never the first. Four competing theories, one enforced by the Accord, none tested.

### XI. Spirit Axes

One, dormant and degraded, to **Osgood Keld**, the officer who trained him. Keld is not dead. Keld is in a room in the Division's medical house with an identity that was overwritten while he stood inside a working for six hours waiting for relief. Wystan was the second man through that door. Axis 100 is what a bond to a man no longer at the other end of it reads as.

### XII. Resistances

Strong against possession, memory tampering, false implantation and the Mirage Identity threshold (Anchoring 420, and he checks himself against a written record every morning). Strong against forced re-attunement and gradual contamination. Middling against law-breaking and Domain erasure. Weak against blunt trauma, blood loss, cold, and any competent man with a knife.

### XIII. Physical Description

Tall, narrow, built like someone who has not eaten a proper meal in daylight for a decade. Hair the colour of wet mahogany with red in it where the lamp catches, parted off-centre and worn to the waist, kept clean because a filing officer who looks unkempt gets his findings questioned. A long face, high flat cheekbones, a mouth that rests closed and thin. Eyes dark and set deep enough to sit in shadow at any hour. Skin has the bloodless quality of a man who works nights and sleeps through the light, with a brown discolouration across the backs of both hands that the medical house calls residue staining and he calls nothing at all. Shoulders narrow, no chest to speak of, forearms corded from carrying a case satchel and nothing else. Thirty-eight, and reads as either twenty-five or fifty depending on the lamp.
Black oiled wool, a full cloak with a standing collar, over a coat closed to the throat. The silver-grey embroidery at the shoulder is the Arbitration Division's and is worn nearly smooth. Everything he owns is black because residue shows on black and he wants to see it.
**Distinguishing.** The stain on his hands. A white line through the left eyebrow. A habit of holding objects by the very edge, two-fingered, even a cup.

### XIV. Psychology

**What he refuses.** To sign an attribution he cannot derive. He has held cases open for four and six years while families waited, and he does not apologise for it.
**What he believes that costs him.** That naming who did a thing is the only justice available when the thing cannot be undone. He chased a purse belonging to a chartered house two years ago, filed it accurately, and was moved to nights the following month.
**Voice.** Flat, unhurried, exact. He answers the question asked and not the one intended. He will correct your terminology in the middle of your grief and does not notice he has done it.
**Humour.** Institutional, dry, delivered with no signal that it has occurred.

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card, the one archived scene it appears in, and the record of The Night Register. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Wystan Ashmore was born the third son of a chartered family, under a surname the Accord's rolls had fixed long before him. The charter would pass to the eldest. The third son inherited the name, and with it the duty of keeping it clean on whatever roll it was entered next.
The house stood over a district main. It ran under the street through the day and went quiet after the eleventh division. A boy who read past that hour could hear the whole street once it stopped. He read past it most nights. He read what the house owned, and then the family's charter, clause by clause. When a place had to be found for a third son, the family found him a clerk's stool in a Measurewright's roll-room.

#### The Making

The roll-room kept what the Measurewrights lifted from workings: samples sealed in tins, labelled, entered on the rolls and shelved against the day a hearing asked for them. His work was to keep each tin where the roll said it stood. He learned the first line of the first lecture of the first week, that residue always carries its author. When the shelving was done he read the assays.
At nineteen, clerking assay tins in that roll-room, he opened a sealed tin without gloves and received the last minute of a man nine years dead, including the part where the man understood what was happening to him. Anamnesis found him before any instructor did. He filed the tin, went outside, was sick in the yard, then went back in and finished the shelf.
The instructor came after. Osgood Keld of the Arbitration Division trained him, and the Division put the gift to work. Bare contact with an object that had carried a working gave him its operator's final minute, lived as his own, with the working legible inside it. It gave him the hand. It never gave him the purse, and the purse is what Article XV requires the Division to prove. He went to the desk as Officer of Attribution. He dressed in black because residue shows on black and he wanted to see it. He held everything by the very edge, two-fingered, even a cup.

#### The Cost

Every minute he took stayed. By the night of the Ferriby insert he carried forty-one. Eleven years of residue ingested on the desk put a brown stain across the backs of both hands. The medical house called it residue staining. He called it nothing at all. His hands shook in the mornings, and the Division entered the shaking in his file as a disciplinary observation.
Keld stood inside a working for six hours waiting for relief, and it overwrote his identity while he waited. Wystan was the second man through that door. He took Keld's minute off the door handle, and in that minute Keld was still lucid and still waiting for relief to arrive. Keld did not die. The Division gave him a room in its medical house. Wystan's Domain consolidated at Keld's bedside, which is not where the Division files it. Its law was that nothing inside it may be altered, and the law bound him too: inside it he could not strike, seal, draw or open a door. Two Enforcement men made him drop it by threatening something outside the boundary. Both got what they wanted.
He would not sign an attribution he could not derive. He held cases open for four and six years while families waited. He did not apologise for it. He believed that naming who did a thing is the only justice available when the thing cannot be undone. He chased a purse belonging to a chartered house, filed it accurately, and was moved to nights the following month.
The Division twice offered him a Wellspring that endures by remembering the lost. He refused it both times; he already did that for a living. He kept his own strength under suppression, waking and sleeping. It tired him. He did not drop it.

#### Where They Stand

The Night Register keeps its desk on Cutler Row, and Wystan works it while the main is off, from the eleventh division to the sixth. An unlicensed draw is inaudible at noon and unmistakable at three. His token is brass, because the Night Register is not a full desk, and he has never asked to have it changed. His rank gives him the address Warden of Duty. Nobody in the districts he works uses it. He has never used it to get a document, a reading, or an hour of a scholar's time.
Of the nine inserts in the bundle from the Society's bulletin office, Nol Tally's was the only one he read twice. Edwyn Brack had already returned it as a Wellspring bleed with no operator indicated, and four counties had reported the same thing. Tally had found the last carcass twenty-two paces short of a parish boundary marker and checked the clean ground on the far side twice. A bleed has no edge. Wystan wrote "edges" in the margin, and under it, "ask Tally who set the marker". Against the eleven competing accounts of why a Wellspring moves, he kept Lu Sanjing's line: "we do not know, and the instrument does not care that we do not know." He had read the Palatine's own return on Vaeloris and found a subtraction in it, with a hard boundary at a tannery wall.
Qiu Yinzhi knocked at the third hour and a half with the Ferriby tin and a clean return. He put on the gloves he always forgot. "Break the wax," he said. Under the third lamp the wedge had grown about a quarter inch inside the sealed tin. He wrote it in the day book and moved on. Then he asked what was carrying the signature. She swept below the Bureau's floor and held the coil through the noise until the wire went over hard. The hind had signed it, and Brack's return was correct off a true measurement. Wystan took his gloves off and put his bare fingers on the wedge. The hind's last minute came: warm, unafraid, standing while her legs came apart under her, the ravens arriving in the birch and not coming down. He was sick at the stone sink. Then he counted, as she had asked.
He carries forty-two minutes now. Thirty-nine are men and women whose names are in his files. One is Keld's. One he has never identified. The last is a hind "who was not frightened, and did not suffer," and he calls it the worst thing in the tin. He called the working a design: pain makes an animal run, and whoever built it needed them to stay where they were put. The wedge is a Register exhibit now, under wax struck with his brass. Yinzhi's full name heads its docket. Tally is to keep walking his line and measuring. Who set the marker is still in the margin, and whoever paid for the working falls under the only jurisdiction his desk has. Every morning he checks Keld's minute against a written record. It has not moved yet.

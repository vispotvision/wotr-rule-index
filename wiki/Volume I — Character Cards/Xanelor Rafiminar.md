## III · Work Architecture

> *The Sage Arts are not magic in the Accord's definition. They are a pre-Accord cultivation method native to Drogo's people, an extinct or near-extinct chelonian race who inhabited Omoro Island's eastern ridge. The method develops bodily perception, spatial processing, breath control, and environmental awareness without formal Essence channeling or Wellspring harmonization.*

**The Way of Growing Things** · Drogo's combat philosophy. Evasion, redirection, gap-reading. No strikes initiated — all responses channel the opponent's force into positions they did not choose. *The uproot, the transplant, the cut-and-redirect.*
**The Eye Before the Hand** · Archery as the primary weapon discipline. Observation before action. The arrow as correction-at-distance rather than ego-at-contact. *"The sword says I am here. The arrow says I have seen what needs correcting."*
**Unnamed** · The spatial awareness cultivated through the stake-field blindfolded exercises. Air-displacement reading, sound-mapping, position-tracking without sight. *This may be a latent Vectoria harmonic operating below the threshold of formal detection.*

## X · Techniques

> *Xanelor carries no Essence-channeled techniques. What he carries are trained physical disciplines with unusual precision for his age, all derived from the Sage Arts.*

**Gap-Standing** · Spatial positioning discipline. Reads where harm will land and occupies that position before it arrives. Trained through four years of increasingly complex obstacle courses. *Effect:* interposition between threat and target at speed above apparent physical capability. *Cost:* physical fatigue, bruising, positional commitment. *Limit:* one gap at a time; simultaneous multi-angle threats exceed his coverage. *Counter:* feints that draw him to a false gap, then strike the real one. **What nobody knows:** the gap-reading may be Vectoria pre-harmonization, operating on instinct rather than calculation. If so, formal harmonization will make this exponentially faster.

**The Sage's Draw** · Archery discipline. Observation-first shooting — the target is read before the bow is drawn, the draw is completed in one motion without sighting pause, the release follows the read rather than the aim. *Effect:* unusual first-shot accuracy at medium range (30–50 paces) under field conditions. *Cost:* time. The read takes 1–3 seconds; a faster archer gets two shots in that window. *Limit:* diminishing accuracy past 60 paces, poor performance against targets moving in genuinely unpredictable patterns. *Counter:* close the distance past bow-range; chaotic movement that defeats the read.

**Stillness** · Breath and body control discipline. Two hours of complete immobility under standard conditions. *Effect:* heartrate suppression, thermal regulation, near-invisible presence when stationary. *Cost:* transition time from stillness to motion (0.5–1 second recovery). *Limit:* environmental extremes degrade duration. *Counter:* force movement; the discipline is irrelevant if he cannot hold position.

## XIII–XV · Description, Psychology, Equipment

Brown skin, lean through the shoulders and waist, beginning to fill the frame that hard island living and four years of daily physical training have built — the proportions of a distance runner rather than a brawler, still growing into the last of adolescent height. Three parallel scars mapping the left cheek from temple to jaw, healed into permanent ridges of scar tissue the colour of ripe plums, vivid against the brown. Hair a mix of black and umber-brown, thick, falling past the shoulders, covering the worst of the scars when he lets it. He does not always let it. Hands calloused from bow, staff, and garden work, stained faintly green at the nail beds from years of volcanic soil. Moves with a careful, considered quality that reads older than fourteen — *the quality of someone trained to feel where everything in a room is without looking at it.*

**Core Wound** — Found scarred and screaming in wreckage at two, raised by a dying old turtle on a rock in the ocean, and sent away because the one person who loved him could not stay alive long enough to keep him
**Flaw** — Will stand in the gap for anyone without calculating whether the gap will kill him. Values others' survival over his own as a trained reflex, not a choice. *Drogo's teaching, taken past the point Drogo intended*
**Desire** — To be the person Drogo believed he was, without knowing whether Drogo saw something real or something he wanted to see
**Loop** — Protects others, refuses recognition, cannot accept that the good he does is his own rather than Drogo's teaching operating through him
**Foil** — *Pending. The academy will provide one.*

**Voice** — Quiet. Listens longer than anyone expects. When he speaks it is considered and specific, not hesitant — he chose every word in the silence before it. Physical rather than verbal: leads with the body, follows with the mouth. Under pressure goes still rather than loud. Drogo's speech patterns surface in his proverbs: *the river does not try to be the river. Stand in the gap. The tongue learns what to forgive.*

**Equipment**
**The Helm** · Slatted iron, cross-shaped visor slots, forged by Drogo. Green silk trailing from the crown — the only colour Drogo called beautiful. Functions as the mask: identity concealment during action, anonymity as philosophy. *"Let them see the good. Not the face behind it."*
**The Longcoat** · Heavy grey canvas, double-breasted, brass-buttoned, cut long to break wind and fitted close at the waist to clear the bowstring. Sewn by Drogo with sail-needles. Green cross stitched over the heart.
**The Bow** · Recurve, blackwood. Limb-tips capped in worked bronze shaped from melted fittings off the eastern ridge ruins. Xanelor finished the shaping himself at age ten under Drogo's instruction. **Drogo's hands started it. Xanelor's hands completed it.**
**The Bracer** · White, left forearm. Stamped with a compass star. Draw-arm protection.
**Broadhead Arrows** · Fletched with stonechat feathers. Forged iron heads. Carried in a hip quiver.
**Green Gloves** · Leather, dyed with the lichen that grows on Drogo's shell. Worn always.
**The Pendant** · Copper hammer, green with age, no larger than a thumbnail. Worn on a sea-grass cord under the coat. *Belonged to someone who built things. Gone now. The hammer remains.*
**The Mask** · Shell. Drogo's shell, flaked and carved over four years. Half-face, half-landscape. Mountain-ridge brow, river-stone cheeks, tide-line markings, no mouth. **Carried but not worn over the helm.** Reserved. *Its use will mean something.*

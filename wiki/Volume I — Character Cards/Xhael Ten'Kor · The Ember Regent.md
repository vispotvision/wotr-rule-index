### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Sun and sand. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Xhael Ten'Kor was born a prince of the Temple-State of Solfatara, in the Outer World. The ground under the city breathed. It let out its heat and its gas a little at a time, and it had done so for centuries without once erupting.
A Wellspring lay embedded beneath the palace vaults. It was Exuroth, the Trial Flame. It raised him. It worked on him the way a smith works a blade: heat, the quench, and heat again. He learned to stand in heat that no one else in the palace could bear. He grew broad and heavy and slow in the hands. He learned to read a room by who stood nearest the throne and who looked away from it.

#### The Making

His father needed a cure, and the road to it ran through the Glass Storms of the Great Sand Sea. Xhael went himself. The Temple-State hired a freeblade, Kaelis Aranthor, to keep him alive on that road. At the edge of the sand the Guides of the Lost Meridian gave him Zahur Vane.
Zahur walked the party in by the gold at his ears. When the first storm rose, Kaelis wanted to cross before it closed, and Zahur sat the party down in the lee of a dune. In that storm the prince was lost from the lee. Kaelis did not find him. Zahur heard his footfalls through the gold, walked out into the storm and brought him back. From then on the party moved when Zahur moved.
Xhael found what he had crossed for and carried it back out through the storms. At the end of the road Zahur named his price, a story nobody had heard, and Xhael told it to him alone. Kaelis took the Temple-State's coin and went.
Afterward, in the palace, he seized divine power through an incomplete ascension rite, merging mortal Essence with solar Aether. For as long as the rite ran, the ground under Solfatara stopped venting. When it gave, it gave all at once. The ritual burned the throne to ash and crowned him in molten gold. The world began answering back. It answered back by killing forty thousand people first.

#### The Cost

The Temple-State burned with its throne. Over the place where it had stood the rite left a magma scar, and he was bound to it. He had not made it on purpose, and he could not fully disown it.
He exiled himself. He carried himself as a king still, and nobody was left for him to rule. He denied that he was a god. By his own account he was "a man who mistook light for salvation."
The Obsidian Crown had fused to his skull. It vented what his body could not hold, and every venting burned straight out of his own reserve. Where he could, he turned it on bare ground. He wore a charm of cooled Wellspring glass at his throat to hold the flares down.
On the Aurelian Dust Coast, under ash blown west off the Drakhal Spine, a Varūn caravan bound home with copper and nitre stopped dead in an Aether storm. Its hired blade was Kaelis Aranthor. Rafik Al'Seren, a Wellspring cartographer, walked with it on nobody's commission. Xhael walked out of the ash and down its line of beasts. He drew the heat around the caravan into himself and went at its head, and the caravan followed his light into clear air. He left it at the edge of the ash before his form's limit came. He asked for nothing. He gave no sign that he knew Kaelis.
What Rafik had seen of him in the ash reached the Guild Accord's archivists. The Accord sent envoys up to the scar with Rajhak Tarun as their liaison, and Xhael came down to meet them. Khaeron Vant, an executioner brought by the Accord's warpriests, drove four knives into the crust around the meeting and raised a chamber in which any lie would bloom as ash-fire in the liar's lungs. The envoys asked Xhael what had happened at Solfatara. He told them what had happened and nothing of how. Nothing burned.

#### Where They Stand

The envoys went back down unhurt, and the Accord's register lists him as a Realm-Hazard. It knows where to find him. He wanders, and he comes back to the scar. On the road below it the Concord forge that went dead the year Solfatara burned has been lit again with Rajhak Tarun's blood.
The drovers carried word of him west to Varūn. The Crown still vents. He chooses where. His human form holds through six continuous hours of casting and no longer. Past that, whatever he is stops being negotiable.
Every tie he has is read against the forty thousand.
Nobody knows whether the Crown on his head is a relic he wears, or a remnant of Solfatara's dead Wellspring still deciding for itself when to vent.

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card, the one archived scene it appears in, and the record of Malphas's cell. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Xu Deming was born into a trading lineage, one of the houses kept by a record. The hall's book held the generation poem, fixed long before his birth, and each generation of the house took the next character of it in turn. The trade lived in ledgers on the shelf beside it. Contracts went out under the house's name, and the returns came back to be read line by line.
The hall capped him young. From the capping on, his name went at the foot of the house's contracts. He took a courtesy name at the same rite. He did not much like it.
One of the first contracts to carry his name was a consignment that came in short. Somewhere on the road between the seller and the hall's store, someone had drawn it down. The hall never looked for the hand. It entered the loss against the name at the foot of the contract. The name was his. He paid it off in instalments and wrote each one into the ledger himself, in the careful hand the hall had taught him, until the line balanced.

#### The Making

He learned the supply trade from its columns. The Logistics Division published its returns, and he read them as the hall read everything, looking for the line that moved where it should have lain flat. He learned which clerks read them after him, and in which rooms.
By the time Malphas's cell was at work in the Greyshaft Nine coldhouse, Xu Deming was its purse. The rot had been proven at the bench. The work in the field wanted Verdantia at the third dilution, the formulation Vesk had built to be introduced live and carried on the animal's own circulation. Xu Deming priced it. It came to "two myriads and a fraction," and he could not buy two myriads.
He diverted the Verdantia from a legitimate supply contract on the Swale line and drew it down as wastage against a pumping array that had shown four years of flat wastage. His name went down as buyer. The stock went to Vesk's bench, and Vesk made the third dilution from it.

#### The Cost

He wrote the wastage knowing what it was. On the night Vesk reported to Malphas, he told the coldhouse so: "wastage is a column, and a column is a line in a Logistics return, and the Logistics Division publishes." He gave the Division eleven weeks to find the bad spring in the Swale array's wastage. He knew that Article Fifteen puts the liability on the payer. He knew the payer was him. The Arbitration Division, he told them, does not chase hands.
He had stood by the door a quarter of an hour, his hat in his hands and rain still on his shoulders, before he spoke. He chose his moment. It was the wrong one, and he knew it. "May I put a number in the room." His thumb went round the brim of his hat and round it again. He asked to have it noted that he had said all this before it happened.
Malphas took his objection and Nuvalik's as one, arrived at from two ends: the work had an edge. Then he named the task Project Zombification. Xu Deming refused the name. "You will call it nothing," he said, with more voice than he had used all night. A named project was a filed project, he said. That was how they had found the Hollow Shaft people: a name in a private correspondence, and everything hung off it. Malphas ordered the name used by all of them, in writing, in the same correspondence Xu Deming feared.
One thought was set down as his that night. He had thirty-one days of usefulness left, and Malphas had already counted them.

#### Where They Stand

Xu Deming is the purse of Malphas's cell and the buyer of record for the Verdantia. He has said his warning before the fact. He asked that it be noted. The eleven weeks he gave the Logistics Division are running.
His name stands in two records. One is the hall's book, where he was written against his generation's character. The other is the record of the Verdantia, where he stands as its buyer. The project he argued against now carries the name Malphas chose, and he is under orders to write it.
The archive leaves him in the coldhouse with his hat in his hands, while Nuvalik goes for the hook.

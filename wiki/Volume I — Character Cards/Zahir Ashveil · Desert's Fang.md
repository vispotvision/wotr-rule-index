### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Ash and the red thread. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Zahir Ashveil was born on the Aurelian Dust Coast, the mineral desert caught between the western mountains and the sea. Some districts there went decades without rain. Fog came in off the ocean at night. The dry kept whatever it covered, and copper lay open on the ground with silver and desert glass. The mining enclaves that held the coast sent it out by caravan.
His father drove one of those strings. The boy walked beside the wagons as soon as he could keep up. At noon he crossed the hot sand barefoot where the drovers' other children would not go. On that coast the few places where Essence still gathered belonged to whoever could hold them, and every string that passed one paid its holder. Raiders came by night. He watched the guards meet them with fire.

#### The Making

The Sunwatch Caravan Guard took him on as soon as he could stand a watch. A caravan guard was taught what the caravan needed, a burn that stopped. He learned a fire that caught on a raider, broke the rush and went out before the wagons moved on. He learned it fast. He had more heat to throw than the guards who taught him, and he spent all of it in the first rush. It was usually enough.
Nobody taught him the next thing. He worked out a burn that waits, alone. The flame left the cut and the ash stayed in it, and when he called it, the wound lit again. He named it the Scorchbrand. It stayed in a wound and reignited when he decided. It was the reason he stopped being a guard.

#### The Cost

Every Scorchbrand he left live in another body was heat he went on paying for, and he had almost nothing to hold it with. Every fight he won, he won in the opening exchanges. He could not afford a second round, and the burns he had left in other people were the reason. He did not change how he opened. He told the people he cut what a Scorchbrand was while it was still in them. He had it from the desert: "burn fast, or be buried slow."
Wet stone, a cold night and a closed room took away all he fought with at once. Ash kept below the heat that lights it never lit again, whatever he willed at it. Kaerith Vireth, a bounty hunter who works in frost, kept winning.
A price on Spoil, the Butcher, brought both hunters to the Glass Coast on the same night. Zahir reached the raid Spoil rode with first, in the last heat of the day, walking the glass unharmed. Renjiro Kaithen, the Pale Fang, met him there. For a few exchanges two men who work the same ash worked it at each other. Then the sea fog came in, and Kaerith came out of it. The two hunters wanted the same head, and they fell on each other first, as they had before. Zahir put ash into Kaerith with his curved blade and called it. The night was wet and cold. The frost kept it colder, and the ash would not light. Kaerith made the fight last until Zahir had spent everything he opened with, and Zahir went down on the glass. In the morning he walked off it alone, with the price uncollected.
Later, word came that one of the Dead Wells had begun to answer, and he came to raid it. A stranger sat at the rim and did not get up. Zahir took the stillness for nothing. He opened fast, and the first cut went in with the ash. The stranger stepped, and the next cut passed through heat mirage where he had been. Zahir called the ash in the first cut. It did not answer him. The man he had cut did not tire, and by the time Zahir had spent what he had, Ilyrion Vantheir had not raised a hand. Zahir left the Well.

#### Where They Stand

He is a contracted blade for hire across the Inner World and Outer World borders, and he belongs to no order. He answers to Emberstride, to the Sandflame and to Desert's Fang. He raids desert Wellsprings. The sand-route warrants Khan Kirv brokers for the Black Watch in the Dry Courts carry prices, and Zahir races Kaerith Vireth for them. The clashes are frequent, and Kaerith keeps winning.
He still carries the flame-channeling curved blade that Ymir Rhok, the Emberhand, forged for him on commission. It carries his draw into the cut, so the ash goes in with the wound. He and Ymir still trade rare desert ores. Caldris Vaelthorn has fought beside him against Outer World raiders, and each respects the other as a warrior. Past the smith, that comrade and the rival, nobody stands near him. He has not reached Spoil. He has not tested the exile twice.
Nobody knows why he left the Sunwatch, or whether the caravan he guarded is one of the ones that burned.

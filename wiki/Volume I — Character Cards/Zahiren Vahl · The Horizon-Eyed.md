# Zahiren Vahl · The Horizon-Eyed

> *"The dunes remember, even when the people forget. My task is not to make you listen — it is to ensure the memory waits for you when you are ready."*
> **The card states no Temperance Stage.** **Stage VIII — Transcendence assigned and flagged as originated**, on the strength of the Mnemonic Vessel: *he can anchor memories to a location for others to witness.*
>
> ***A place is a place and is also a record, and both are operative*** — which is Transcendence's dual reading stated as a profession.
>
> *"Sand / Memory / Verdure"* were not Essence types. **The Ma glyph alignment is retained** pending a check against the Master Glyph Index. **Kaetra** is dead canon — *struck alongside Nevara and the Praetorian March* — and his alliance with "the Kaetra deep-vault scholars" needs re-homing.

## II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class III · Resonant.** **Crystal State** · Refined. **η** · ~0.70, Band B.
**Scripted Veins** · **Sigils embedded into his flesh from a rite of the Horizon Wellspring.** *They pulse faintly when he speaks truth and burn when he speaks falsehood.*
> ***He is a mediator who is physically incapable of lying without pain.***
>
> **That is a devastating professional asset and an equally devastating liability.** *Every party in a negotiation knows his honesty is verifiable — and every party also knows he cannot bluff, cannot conceal a position, and cannot protect a confidence under direct question.*

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Anamnesis** · Limina. **Governing.** *Remanent magnetisation — objects and ground genuinely store their history in physically readable form.* "The dunes remember" is not poetry on this sheet. It is the mechanism.
> *Failure: thermal resetting. Reading is not always passive, and a careless practitioner erases the record in the act of taking it.* **A man whose entire vocation is preservation runs the current whose failure mode is destroying what he came to save.**

**Oneirion** · Limina. **Configuration space** — *replaying memories as illusions, anchored to a place rather than a person.*
**Verdantia** · Vitalia. **The adaptive growth**, and survival in Essence wastelands.

## VII · Signature

### The Horizon Glass Codex

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of Sun-Sealed and the horizon couriers. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Zahiren Vahl was born on the Aurelian Dust Coast, in a mining enclave on the mineral desert between the Drakhal Spine and the sea. Rain could miss a district there for years on end. The enclave drank from wells, and his mother found them.
The dryness kept things. Metal that forest or river mud would have buried elsewhere lay open in that ground, and so did everything that had happened on it. His mother knelt at a likely place, laid her palm flat on the earth and waited a long time before she spoke. He learned to wait with her. The ground gave up the old water to a hand slow enough to take it, and the herds and camps beside it. Before he was grown he could find a buried well faster than she could.

#### The Making

In a dry season the enclave went looking for a well that had drifted over with sand. His mother could not find it. He found the place and read the ground hard and fast for the line of the shaft, with the diggers waiting. They dug where he told them and struck water. Afterward his mother knelt at the spot, and the earth under her palm held nothing. The herds, the camps and the digging of that well had gone out of the ground in one fast reading.
Quarrels over ancient Wellspring rights turned on who had reached the water first. The ground kept that. The Arbitration Division took him on as a cultural translator and ritual arbiter among desert-bound and nomadic peoples. Sigils from a rite of the Horizon Wellspring were set in his flesh, and they pulsed when he spoke truth and burned when he spoke falsehood. No record says when he took the rite, or at whose hands.
Once the Division sent him to read the Heliarch Concord Collapse onto its record from the floor where the treaty broke. He read it once, slowly. The floor held the parties turning on one another. Then it held Cael Veridion's voice, every Domain within earshot turning toward it, and the hall going still long enough for the parties to go out. Zahiren spoke the reading into the record with Cael in the room. His veins never burned.
At a sanctum on his own coast, two desert-bound peoples claimed one small Wellspring by ancient right. The ground kept both of them there, each in its turn, further back than either people's account. He said so. Neither would take a reading that gave the water to both. One asked for trial. The Division sent Kaelen Raive, its Bound Duelist, against their champion, and the champion's people broke the ring while the Warden's seal charged. Saruin Kye came down on his cloud, and the blows meant for Kaelen missed until the seal was full. Zahiren did not move. When the sanctum was quiet he anchored what the ground held of both peoples' dead to the sanctum floor, for whichever of them came to witness it. The floor stayed a floor. It became a record as well.

#### The Cost

The veins made his word good at any table, and every party knew what came with it. He could not bluff. He could not hide a position. Under a direct question he could not keep a confidence, and the veins showed which answers he had wanted to keep back.
His cartography held the places of sealed Wellsprings, and any direct question could have had them out of him. He gave it to the Research and Archives Division in exchange for protection. Rikkan Sahr carried the maps there sealed, once through an Essence storm on the Varakh Drylands, and neither of them asked what was in the package.
Every reading risked what it read. After the buried well he read each place once, and he did not read the Heliarch floor twice.
He preserved. He did not intervene. On the Dust Coast the talking ended where Kaelen's work began, and Zahiren kept to his own side of that line while the ring broke. He held that a record should wait for its people as long as they took to come. In his own words: "The dunes remember, even when the people forget." Both peoples at the sanctum heard the reading from him, and neither would have it.

#### Where They Stand

Zahiren Vahl is the Horizon-Eyed, Keeper of the Sand-Whisper Codex and Concord Mediator of the Innerworld. He keeps informal alliances with the Sylvaar nomad caravans and the deep-vault scholars, and trades Wellspring maps and preservation rites with Florwyn's Nomads.
Rikkan Sahr still brings him maps and artifacts without question. The Division that holds his maps also houses the Ophidian Studies Cell, where Elder Seravain runs the Crimson Coil Circle. No record puts the maps in Seravain's hands.
Cael Veridion's part in the Collapse stands on the Division's record in Zahiren's words. Zahiren distrusts the divine influence in that voice. He respects what it did.
Anyone may still put a direct question to him. He will answer it true, and the veins will show which answers he wanted to withhold.

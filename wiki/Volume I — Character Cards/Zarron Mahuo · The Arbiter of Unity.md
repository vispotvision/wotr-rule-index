# Zarron Mahuo · The Arbiter of Unity

> *"Born of two bloodlines, I claim neither and surpass both."*
> **ORIGINATED, RATIFIED.** Rebuilt from the legacy Trello sheet. **The Temperance Stage assignment was ratified as written (Isaac, 2026-09-12).** The stat allocation is worked from the Fracture of Worlds point economy and checked against the Part Seven Path gates.
>
> **One lineage term on this sheet still needs Isaac's ruling before it hardens.** *Yukari* ties to the open Tyzura / Ashura Yukari identity question. *Vellsorea* — a real place, ruled 2026-09-12: the New World, the Eastern Rise. **The Yukari term is carried here provisionally and is not load-bearing.**

## I · Identity

**Name** · Zarron Mahuo.
**Called** · The Arbiter of Unity. The Veilborn Ascendant. Wielder of Hikariken.
**Era** · Age of Calamity.
**Standing** · Unaffiliated by choice and by consequence. Trained under a Yukari mentor-sect, then among the Veilwalkers of Vellsorea, **and claimed as a full son by neither.**
**Lineage** · Hybrid. Yukari and high elven.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage X, Realization.** He severed the Astral Gate at the Shattering of Virelion, and the Continuum did not resist him. It **accommodated** him — locally, grudgingly, within limits that were extremely specific, and the direction of the accommodation reversed and did not reverse back.
>
> The Sixteen Stages describes the lived experience of Realization as a species of loneliness with no good analogue lower on the ladder, and describes it in terms of weather. **You are no longer standing in it. You are part of what other people are standing in.**
>
> *For a man who has spent his life being claimed by nobody, that arrived as something close to relief, and the relief is the most dangerous thing about him.*

## II · Soul Architecture

**One Crystal. Three layers.** The legacy sheet's "Primordial Essence, Spiritbound Force, Veil-Kissed Aether" were not three Essence types. They are one Typology reading across three layers, and are stated as such.
**Essence Core** · Coherent and unusually clean for a hybrid. *The two lineages did not fuse and did not fight. They sit adjacent, and the seam is visible on a coil to anyone who knows to look for one.*
**Aether Shell** · Class V · **Radiant.** Aether bends toward him. Currents curve around his presence, and his output begins exceeding what the raw numbers suggest.
**Attraction Layer** · Clean Attraction Force, strongly developed. **The layer Iskaron never built and Sinclair corrupted, Zarron leads with.**
**Crystal State** · Refined. Rare among the Volume III cast and worth noting as the exception.
**Essence colour** · Deep violet pushing toward ultraviolet, the Spatium hue — **perceived less than it is inferred**, sitting at the edge of what the eye admits, which is appropriate for a Family whose physics governs distance and position.

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

> **He holds the entire Spatium register.** All three. Spatium is the smallest Family in the sixty and the strangest, and a practitioner harmonised to every current in it is not a generalist — **he is the complete expression of a very narrow law.**
**Transference** · *the Living Exchange.* **Governing harmonisation.** Two oscillators tuned alike and held near each other exchange energy with no conductor between them. Essence routes to an ally not because he pushes it but because the two Crystals are in tune and a gradient does the rest. *This is the Arbiter of Unity stated mechanically.*
> **Its failure is the character's whole tragedy.** A gradient runs both directions. **A Transference practitioner beside a depleted ally will empty into them without deciding to** — and Zarron has never once described this as a flaw.
**Transmutatio** · *the Law of Change.* Failed workings are not destroyed Essence but Essence in an inconvenient basis, and the Wellspring supplies the transformation that makes them usable again. **This is the legacy sheet's "Unyielding Spirit" with an actual mechanism under it: he does not grow stronger from suffering. He recovers the Essence he spent failing.**
**Eclipseron** · *the Shadowed Star.* He reads reality by what is missing from it, and derives from the shape of an absence the shape of the thing casting it. *Veil-sight, and the reason he sees planar seams other practitioners walk through.*
**Luminalis** · Fulguria. The radiant expression. **Hikariken is Luminalis given an edge**, and the blade is a delivery geometry rather than an artefact.

## VII · Techniques

### Veilbreak Ascension · *Signature*

**Effect** · He cuts, and the cut arrives before the blade does. What it opens is not a wound in the target but a seam in the ground the target is standing on — wards lose the surface they were written against, a Domain finds its own boundary has been given a different shape, and a man in the middle of a technique discovers the technique is intact and has stopped being connected to anything. **Light comes off the seam for a long moment after and does not illuminate.**
**Cost** · Transference runs both ways during the cut. **He empties toward whatever is nearest**, and if that is an ally the ally receives it whether or not they wanted it. *He has hospitalised two people this way and describes both as accidents.*
**Limit** · It works on structure, not on flesh. **An opponent with no wards, no Domain and no working running is barely inconvenienced by it**, which is a genuinely absurd vulnerability and one that a sufficiently unsophisticated enemy has exploited.
**Counter** · Distance held and nothing else offered. Zarron needs a structure to cut. *Refuse to build one and he is a swordsman with S-Grade Vitality.*
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the seams close. Eclipseron reads absence, and a practitioner who has spent a life reading what is missing has never once been able to read the places he made missing. **He assumes they heal. Nothing has confirmed it.**

### Lore · The Life Behind the Card

*Written 2026-09-24 by the character-lore pass from this card and the record of The Age of Calamity. What those sources do not state is new here.*

#### Origin

Zarron Mahuo was born in the Age of Calamity with two bloods in him, Yukari and high elven. Two houses had the raising of him. Neither claimed him as a full son.
The first was a Yukari mentor-sect. Its people moved slowly, spent no motion they did not need, and watched a thing for a long time before they touched it. The boy learned their stillness quickly. He also stopped on the sect's own paths at places the others walked straight through, and he told his teachers that something was missing there. None of them could find it. He could not show them, and he went on stopping.
He learned in that house to read a thing by what it lacked, and to work back from the shape of an absence to the shape of whatever had made it. The seams between the planes were plain to him long before he had a word for them.
Children who take the Yukari name are brought before Senri's stone and asked to read the words cut into it. Zarron never took the name. The sect had its own word for what he was, and that word does not translate.

#### The Making

The second house was the Veilwalkers of Vellsorea, in the New World. He went to them as a young man and learned from them to leave a place by way of the Veil and come out in another. He learned their tongue as well, the formal Eressean of the elven courts. His teachers listened for his mistakes. He made few of them, and his speech has kept the care they drilled into it, a shade too correct for anyone born to the language.
Eressean has no way to say "my name" without saying whether anyone stood witness to the holding. Its word for what he was is a legal term.
Among the Veilwalkers he found that his strength went where it was lacking. When he fought beside someone who was spent, it ran out of him and into them, whether he chose it or not, and whether they wanted it or not. He never once described it as a flaw. When a working failed in his hands, what he had spent on it came back to him afterward, the same measure he had lost and never more.
Hikariken came to him in those years. It was a blade of light that formed in his hand when he needed it and was gone when he did not. He has never carried it, and it has no scabbard.
In the end both houses offered to claim him. The sect offered its name on its terms, and the Veilwalkers offered to stand witness to his on theirs. He refused both offers. He belonged to no house after that, by his own choice and by what followed from it.
At the Shattering of Virelion he severed the Astral Gate. The Continuum offered him no resistance and accommodated him, locally and grudgingly, within limits that were extremely specific. The direction of the accommodation reversed. It did not reverse back.

#### The Cost

His cut arrived before the blade did, and it opened a seam in the ground under the man in front of him. Wards lost the surface they were written on, and a working in progress stayed whole and stopped being joined to anything. While he cut, he emptied toward whatever stood nearest him. If that was an ally, the ally took it, wanted or not. Two people went into a physician's care that way. He called both of them accidents.
The cut worked on structure alone. A man who ran no working and wore no ward was barely troubled by it, and one enemy with nothing for him to cut used that against him. He fought by not being where a blow landed. His body was never built to take the blows that found him.
On the flood-basalt steps at the Crevice margin, in the Outer World, the bleed between the planes had been shut over one stretch of ground. A Domain lay over it. Its maker was gone. Zarron came to it by what was missing there, and from what the Domain left out he read the shape of the man who had made it, Iskaron Thalnaris, whom he had never met. He stopped at the Domain's edge alone and cut from there. A seam opened in the basalt. The boundary took another shape, and light came off the seam and lit nothing.
He went onto the steps with Hikariken in his hand. The dying of the Bloodspine clan lay among the fallen columns, and a man in a dark wool coat knelt beside one of them. It was Sinclair Mercer. He stood up and left before the light was off the seam.
None of the Bloodspine lived to the next morning. Their chieftain, Gorrath Bloodspine, pried basalt posts loose with his hands and stood them over his dead. Zarron carried posts beside him until the last cairn was up. Gorrath did not trust men who talked in abstractions, and the cairns went up with little said between them. Then Zarron went.

#### Where They Stand

Zarron Mahuo is unaffiliated, and he has gone back to neither house. He has been searched for a weapon on four occasions by people who then let him through. There is nothing on him to find.
He still calls the two he put in a physician's care accidents. He has never tried to stop what his cut does to whoever stands nearest.
One of his seams lies in the basalt of the Bloodspine steps, near the cairns. He reads everything else by what is missing from it, and he has never once been able to read the places he made missing. He assumes they heal. Nothing has confirmed it.

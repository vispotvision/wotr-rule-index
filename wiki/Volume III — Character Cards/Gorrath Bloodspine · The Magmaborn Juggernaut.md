# Gorrath Bloodspine · The Magmaborn Juggernaut

> *"I am the forge, the flame, and the fall. Break me and see what erupts."*
> **ORIGINATED, RATIFIED.** Rebuilt from the legacy Trello sheet. **The Temperance Stage assignment was ratified as written (Isaac, 2026-09-12).** The stat allocation is worked from the Fracture of Worlds point economy and checked against the Part Seven Path gates.
>
> **The legacy sheet carried percentage stat blocks throughout** — "reduces physical damage by 30–45%," "60% damage reduction for 10 seconds." These are removed rather than converted. Numbers do not attach to metaphysical quantities.

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Gorrath Bloodspine.
**Called** · The Magmaborn Juggernaut. Warden of the Broken Spires. Herald of Bloodfire.
**Era** · Age of Calamity.
**Race** · Orc, Bloodspine clan, deep-earth volcanic strain.
**Standing** · Last surviving chieftain of the Bloodspine line. Vanguard for the deep resistance fronts.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VIII, Transcendence.** He survived a soul furnace trial in his youth, and the surviving is not the Catalyst. **What he did afterward is.**
>
> The Sixteen Stages holds that Transcendence requires taking the load-bearing wound from the fracture record and **consciously making it structure. Not healing it. Not releasing it. Making it hold weight on purpose.**
>
> Gorrath's blood calcifies into obsidian around the vital organs when he is near death. **That is not a gift and it is not a mutation. It is a wound that was made into architecture on purpose**, by a man who decided that the thing which nearly killed him would be the thing that kept him standing.
>
> *Practitioners fail at this for decades. The halls are full of S-Grade-capable souls at the Stage VII ceiling who never did the work. Gorrath did the work at an age when most practitioners are still being taught to meter.*

## II · Soul Architecture

**One Crystal. Three layers.** The legacy sheet's Infernal Bloodstone, Magmaborn Core and Emberforge Vitality were not three Crystals. They are his Core, his Shell, and a Vitality expression, and are stated as such.
**Essence Core** · Dense, uncomplicated, and **seated clean.** No competing carry, no unintegrated remainder. *A coil reads him in under a minute and the reading is boring, which is the highest compliment the instrument pays.*
**Aether Shell** · Class III · **Resonant.** Power moves in silence; stat values translate at near-full fidelity.
**Attraction Layer** · Present, modest, clan-anchored. He has Axes and they are all in one place.
**Crystal State** · Refined.
**Essence colour** · Caloria deep ember-orange, iron at cherry heat. **The canonical test is whether it makes the observer feel warmer. It should not, and with Gorrath it does not** — which surprises people who have been told what he is before they meet him.

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Ignivale** · Caloria. **Governing harmonisation.** The current that holds temperatures charcoal cannot reach, and the reason Aethersteel exists at all. *In Gorrath it is not a weapon first. It is a furnace he happens to live inside.*
**Solfatara** · Caloria. Deep sulphurous red. **The volcanic register**, and the seam where his blood stops behaving like blood.
**Chthonica** · Materia. Dark iron-oxide. The obsidian, the plating, the weight.
**Petralon** · Vectoria. Seismic transmission. **Ground behaves as a medium rather than as a surface** when he commits weight to it.
> **Two Caloria harmonisations compound.** Same-Family currents agree along the same axes and reinforce, which is why his thermal output reads denser than his Ardency accounts for. **The Ledger figures are ordinary. The qualitative expression is not.**

## VI · Physical Force

**Strike Force** · Ardency 395, mid S-Grade. On the order of **1 TN peak contact force, near 10 TJ yield, roughly 2.5 kilotons equivalent.** Town-level, delivered blunt — and the Sharp-Blunt asymmetry means he shatters structures a swordsman of equal Grade could only slice.
**Lifting Strength** · Vitality 410, upper S-Grade, **with the Body Path multiplicative bonus applying** because his Essence routes through the Physical Plane first, where gravitational interaction is native rather than translated. **Toward 200 million tonnes** sustained. *He can arrest tectonic motion across a fault line, and has.*
**Speed** · Dexterity 271, B-Grade. Travel in the region of **5,000 m/s**, reaction near 0.5 milliseconds.
> **The gap that beats him.** Vitality 410 against Dexterity 271 and Gnosis 235 is **a full Tier Grade of separation.**
>
> Anything that refuses to be in front of him defeats him. He cannot chase, cannot read a feint, and cannot be made to stop — *which means the correct answer to Gorrath has always been to let him arrive somewhere that does not matter.*

## VII · Domain

**The Broken Spire.** Newly formed at Transcendence and held for hours rather than minutes.
Ground inside it takes and keeps heat. Stone remembers being struck. **The Domain does not burn anything — it makes the terrain participate**, and a formation fighting inside it finds the footing giving, cooling, hardening, and giving again on a cycle nobody set.

---

## VIII · Techniques

### Bloodcore Avalanche · *Signature*

**Effect** · Both fists into the ground, weight committed through the heels, and the ground stops being a surface. A rupture runs outward along the seams the stone already had — **he does not break rock, he finds where rock was already going to break** — and molten chain-spikes come up through it at the fracture lines. What he stands on afterward has cooled into a shell that was not there when he started.
**Cost** · Thermal debt paid from his own blood. **He runs cold for a day and a half afterward**, which for a man of his strain is genuinely dangerous, and he does not sweat for a week.
**Limit** · It requires ground with existing structure. **On loose sediment, on sand, on a ship's deck or a bog, it does nothing at all** — which is the Vectoria suppressive condition doing exactly what the register says it does.
**Counter** · Do not stand on stone. *Every commander who has beaten him has beaten him by choosing where.*
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the obsidian is his. It forms from his blood and it does not read as his on a coil, and no renderer who has examined a shed plate has been willing to name its Provenance Class.

---

## IX · Psychology

**What he refuses** · To be moved off ground he has committed to. It is not stubbornness in his own account. **It is the only promise he has ever found that he can keep with his body.**
**What it costs him** · Everything mobile. He is the last Bloodspine chieftain because the rest of the clan died somewhere he had already decided to stand.
**Speech** · Stressed monosyllables, short clauses, concrete nouns. He does not use abstractions and does not trust people who do.
> **One private thought, for prose use.** *The furnace trial took eleven of them. He has never been able to make the number smaller by remembering it differently, and he has tried.*

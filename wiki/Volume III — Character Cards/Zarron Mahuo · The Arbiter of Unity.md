# Zarron Mahuo · The Arbiter of Unity

> *"Born of two bloodlines, I claim neither and surpass both."*
> **ORIGINATED, PENDING RULING.** Rebuilt from the legacy Trello sheet. **The Temperance Stage assignment is a proposal pending ratification.** The stat allocation is worked from the Fracture of Worlds point economy and checked against the Part Seven Path gates.
>
> **One lineage term on this sheet still needs Isaac's ruling before it hardens.** *Yukari* ties to the open Tyzura / Ashura Yukari identity question. *Vellsorea* — a real place, ruled 2026-09-12: the New World, the Eastern Rise. **The Yukari term is carried here provisionally and is not load-bearing.**

## I · Identity

**Name** · Zarron Mahuo.
**Called** · The Arbiter of Unity. The Veilborn Ascendant. Wielder of Hikariken.
**Era** · Age of Calamity.
**Standing** · Unaffiliated by choice and by consequence. Trained under a Yukari mentor-sect, then among the Veilwalkers of Vellsorea, **and claimed as a full son by neither.**
**Lineage** · Hybrid. Yukari and high elven.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage X, Realization.** He severed the Astral Gate at the Shattering of Virelion, and the Continuum did not resist him. It **accommodated** him — locally, grudgingly, within limits that were extremely specific, and the direction of the accommodation reversed and did not reverse back.
>
> The Sixteen Stages describes the lived experience of Realization as a species of loneliness with no good analogue lower on the ladder, and describes it in terms of weather. **You are no longer standing in it. You are part of what other people are standing in.**
>
> *For a man who has spent his life being claimed by nobody, that arrived as something close to relief, and the relief is the most dangerous thing about him.*

## II · Soul Architecture

**One Crystal. Three layers.** The legacy sheet's "Primordial Essence, Spiritbound Force, Veil-Kissed Aether" were not three Essence types. They are one Typology reading across three layers, and are stated as such.
**Essence Core** · Coherent and unusually clean for a hybrid. *The two lineages did not fuse and did not fight. They sit adjacent, and the seam is visible on a coil to anyone who knows to look for one.*
**Aether Shell** · Class V · **Radiant.** Aether bends toward him. Currents curve around his presence, and his output begins exceeding what the raw numbers suggest.
**Attraction Layer** · Clean Attraction Force, strongly developed. **The layer Iskaron never built and Sinclair corrupted, Zarron leads with.**
**Crystal State** · Refined. Rare among the Volume III cast and worth noting as the exception.
**Essence colour** · Deep violet pushing toward ultraviolet, the Spatium hue — **perceived less than it is inferred**, sitting at the edge of what the eye admits, which is appropriate for a Family whose physics governs distance and position.

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

> **He holds the entire Spatium register.** All three. Spatium is the smallest Family in the sixty and the strangest, and a practitioner harmonised to every current in it is not a generalist — **he is the complete expression of a very narrow law.**
**Transference** · *the Living Exchange.* **Governing harmonisation.** Two oscillators tuned alike and held near each other exchange energy with no conductor between them. Essence routes to an ally not because he pushes it but because the two Crystals are in tune and a gradient does the rest. *This is the Arbiter of Unity stated mechanically.*
> **Its failure is the character's whole tragedy.** A gradient runs both directions. **A Transference practitioner beside a depleted ally will empty into them without deciding to** — and Zarron has never once described this as a flaw.
**Transmutatio** · *the Law of Change.* Failed workings are not destroyed Essence but Essence in an inconvenient basis, and the Wellspring supplies the transformation that makes them usable again. **This is the legacy sheet's "Unyielding Spirit" with an actual mechanism under it: he does not grow stronger from suffering. He recovers the Essence he spent failing.**
**Eclipseron** · *the Shadowed Star.* He reads reality by what is missing from it, and derives from the shape of an absence the shape of the thing casting it. *Veil-sight, and the reason he sees planar seams other practitioners walk through.*
**Luminalis** · Fulguria. The radiant expression. **Hikariken is Luminalis given an edge**, and the blade is a delivery geometry rather than an artefact.

## VII · Techniques

### Veilbreak Ascension · *Signature*

**Effect** · He cuts, and the cut arrives before the blade does. What it opens is not a wound in the target but a seam in the ground the target is standing on — wards lose the surface they were written against, a Domain finds its own boundary has been given a different shape, and a man in the middle of a technique discovers the technique is intact and has stopped being connected to anything. **Light comes off the seam for a long moment after and does not illuminate.**
**Cost** · Transference runs both ways during the cut. **He empties toward whatever is nearest**, and if that is an ally the ally receives it whether or not they wanted it. *He has hospitalised two people this way and describes both as accidents.*
**Limit** · It works on structure, not on flesh. **An opponent with no wards, no Domain and no working running is barely inconvenienced by it**, which is a genuinely absurd vulnerability and one that a sufficiently unsophisticated enemy has exploited.
**Counter** · Distance held and nothing else offered. Zarron needs a structure to cut. *Refuse to build one and he is a swordsman with S-Grade Vitality.*
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the seams close. Eclipseron reads absence, and a practitioner who has spent a life reading what is missing has never once been able to read the places he made missing. **He assumes they heal. Nothing has confirmed it.**

## VIII · Psychology

**What he refuses** · To be claimed. Both houses offered and both offers arrived with terms, and he has spent a life proving he required neither, **which is a thing you can only prove by continuing to need nobody.**
**What it costs him** · The Transference failure mode is not an accident of his harmonisation. **It is the only way he has ever found to give anybody anything**, and it operates below his decision-making, and he has never tried to fix it.
**Speech** · Formal, subordinated, latinate — the Eressean register, learned rather than native, and slightly too correct in the way of a man who was taught the language by people watching for mistakes.
> **One private thought, for prose use.** *The Yukari word for what he is does not translate, and the elven word for it is a legal term.*

# Atheron Dreymarch · Prophet of the Unblinking

> *"I am the weight on your eyelids. The voice in your verdict-dream. Swear carefully."*
> **A Revelation card — the fifth found, and handled under the standing ruling.** The sheet reads **"Temperance Stage X (Mythic Revelation)."**
>
> ***Revelation is Stage XV*** — beyond the canonical fourteen, Archon-participant territory, **two recorded practitioners in the whole history of the setting.** *Atheron is not one of them.*
>
> **Numeral wins: Stage X, Realization.** *The word was chosen for flavour and collided with a reserved Stage name — and Realization is the correct fit anyway: terrain keeps what a Stage X practitioner establishes, and regions hold that set for generations after they are gone.*

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Atheron Dreymarch. **Called** · **The Chains in the Dream.** Feathered Sentinel · **Prophet of the Unblinking.**
**Role** · **Dream-Warden. Wellspring Custodian. Arbiter of Omen-Law.**

### Affiliation

**Arbitration Division — Neutral Chambers Keeper.** *Also holds the ancient title of Moon-Oath Binder within the Forgotten Court of Noxthallow, a half-mythic tribunal governing disputes in the dream-realms.*
> **Catalyst Event · Stage X, Realization.** *The practitioner stops being the party that has to be accommodated. Pacts hold in the ground. Artifacts do not need maintaining. Regions keep a set that outlasts the practitioner by generations.*
>
> **His oath-chains persist without him tending them, and summon him when broken.** *That is not a spell he maintains. It is terrain that has agreed to different terms, in a place made of sleep.*

## II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class IV · Luminous.** *The lucid Shell, which thins — Aether behaviour mirrors the bearer's moral alignment with unnerving precision.* For a judge, a qualification rather than a metaphor.
**Crystal State** · Refined. **η** · 0.75–0.80, Band A.
**Blindfold of the Third Moon** · **Worn to prevent his gaze from slipping fully into the dream-plane.** *If removed, he perceives all dreams within his Wellspring's reach simultaneously — overwhelming mortals with layered visions of every possible future until their mind collapses.*

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Oneirion** · Limina. **Governing.** *Configuration space — a system's every possible arrangement as a coordinate in a space with no physical location.*
> ***"Layered visions of every possible future" is Oneirion stated exactly.*** *He is not seeing the future. He is standing in the configuration space and reading coordinates, which is why the blindfold is necessary and why removing it overwhelms rather than informs.*
**Judicium** · Fulguria. **Order, measure, consequence.** *The Omen-Law, and its warning: law without mercy becomes burnished sadism.*
**Anamnesis** · Limina. **The Unblinking Mantle** — *bound ocular spirits perceiving guilt, lies, and hidden truths.*
**Nyxial** · Limina. **The Nocturne Flame.** *A regulated baseline holding setpoint — the moonlit field that does not change.*

## VI · Traits

**Unblinking Mantle** · **The embroidered "eyes" on his cloak are not symbolic — they are bound ocular spirits.** *They perceive guilt, lies and hidden truths, projecting them into a dreamer's mind with suffocating clarity.*
**Oath-Chains** · **The chains draped on his form are physical representations of spiritual contracts sworn in his presence.** *Breaking such an oath summons Atheron in dreams, where he may imprison the oathbreaker's will in a geas or strip it entirely.*
**Blindfold of the Third Moon** · **Prevents his gaze from slipping fully into the dream-plane.**

## VII · Signature

### The Oath-Bound Dream

**Effect** · An endless moonlit field where **chains descend from the sky, each link tied to a dreamer's fate.** *Those judged guilty are bound in nightmare until their chain falls, signalling their death.*
**Cost** · **The blindfold, permanently.** *Tempering 478 is a man holding a faculty shut for his own survival and everyone else's.*
**Limit** · **They must have sworn.**
**Counter** · **Swear nothing. Or wake him.**
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the chains that fall were judged, or merely reached the end of their length.

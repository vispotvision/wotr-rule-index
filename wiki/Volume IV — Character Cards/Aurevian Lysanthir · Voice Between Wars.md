# Aurevian Lysanthir · Voice Between Wars

> *"A single note, struck at the right time, can end a war… or start one."*
> **The card states no Temperance Stage.** **Stage VIII — Transcendence assigned and flagged as originated**, on the strength of Auric Flow: *listeners' Essence aligns with his Temperance cycle, creating a shared harmonic field.* **Pulling other practitioners into your own cycle is Stage VIII work.**
>
> *"Harmony / Light / Illusion"* were not Essence types — **though Harmonia is a genuine Category**, and his placement under it is correct.

### Affiliation

**Guild Accord — Arbitration Division** *(official)* · **The Choral Veil**, *a secretive society of musical mystics.*

## VII · Signature

### Serenade of Equilibrium

**Effect** · **He plays, and the room's Essence falls into his cycle.** *Fear unravels, positions soften, and a treaty that was impossible an hour ago becomes the obvious course.*
**Cost** · **Catharsis empties and does not transform.** *What he takes off a negotiation goes somewhere, and he stores it.* **He is carrying every war he prevented.**
**Limit** · **They must listen.**
**Counter** · **Deafness, distance, or genuine silence.** *Ardency 184 — once the music fails he has nothing.*

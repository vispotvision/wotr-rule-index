# Baojin Zhaorin · The Verdant Colossus

> *"When the grove wakes, there is no mercy — I will grind your bones to feed its roots and let your blood soak the earth until the bamboo sings."*
> **Migrated with all content preserved.** *"Earth-Aether / Verdant Harmonic"* were not Essence types, and *"Preservation Force"* is not an Attraction Force alignment in the register. **The Verdant Wellspring of Zhenri is retained as a named site** — *a Wellspring pools where the substrate is conductive and time has let it saturate, and a sacred grove is exactly that.*

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Baojin Zhaorin. **Called** · The Verdant Colossus.
**Role** · **Guild Accord Vanguard. Traveling Guardian of the Greenroads.** *Gentle giant and reluctant juggernaut of slaughter.*

### Affiliation

**Guild Accord, Frontier Vanguard Division** — protector of caravans, breaker of sieges, restorer of Wellsprings. **Sworn Keeper of the Verdant Wellspring of Zhenri**, *a sacred grove that feeds on blood spilled in its defence.*
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VIII, Transcendence.** *Take the load-bearing wound and consciously make it structure. Not heal it. Make it hold weight on purpose.*
>
> **He is a gentle man who is extraordinarily good at killing, and he did not resolve that contradiction. He built on it.** *The grove feeds on blood spilled in its defence — which means his violence is not a failure of his gentleness. It is the mechanism by which the thing he protects survives, and he has made peace with that in the only way available.*

## VII · Signature

### The Bamboo Grove Nexus

**Effect** · When he fights to protect the grove, **the ground drinks deep.** *Bamboo shoots erupt from corpses, roots coil through wounds, and leaves drip red with sap and blood alike.*
**Cost** · **The grove feeds on what is spilled in its defence, and it does not distinguish whose.** *Vitality Scarring 342 is on this sheet for a reason.*
**Limit** · **It works where the grove reaches.** *Vitalia is suppressed on sterile ground, in extreme cold, and in Essence-depleted soil* — **take him off the Greenroads and he is a very large man with a mantle.**
**Counter** · **Fight him anywhere else. Or give him something to read** — Gnosis 218 will not manage it.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the grove chose him or he was simply the first to bleed for it.

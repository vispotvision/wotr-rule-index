# Basilio Pavaret · The Crimson Bloom

> **Corrected.** "Wellspring of Emotion" and "Wellspring of Light" are not among the sixty. Parun glyph affinities **Lu** and **Ma** carried pending check against the Master Glyph Index.

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Basilio Pavaret. **Called** · The Crimson Bloom. Painter of Lived Sorrows.
**Standing** · Independent vampiric house, broken lineage. Patron of the Twilight Atelier.
**Catalyst Event · Stage VI, Glory.** He accepted real soul-cost to preserve something valued above his own integrity — **and what he preserved was his refusal to feed by force.** *That refusal is not temperament. It is a Threshold, paid for in advance and in cold blood, and it is why he is Stage VI rather than a stronger monster at Stage V.*

---

## II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · Class II · Harmonic. **Attraction Layer** · Wide and shallow — many Sympathy Tethers, few Axes. **Crystal State** · Refined. **η** · ~0.55, Band D.
**Essence colour** · Vitalia arterial red softened toward Catharsis rose-gold. *The one Family colour that triggers the mammalian response to blood, worn by a man who has organised his entire life around not causing that response.*

---

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Anima Spirare** · Vitalia. Bright arterial. The blood itself.
**Catharsis** · Caloria. Release, purgation, true clearing. **The emotional transfer that heals rather than takes.** *Its doctrine warns that nothing is purified by being merely emptied — if what is removed has not been rightly transformed, it returns elsewhere and worse.* **Basilio transforms. That is the whole distinction between him and the Communion.**
**Eidolyn** · Limina. Templated nucleation with intent as template. **The blossoms, the butterflies, the weeping figures** — real material behaviour, not illusion. *Template defect: any flaw in the intent reproduces faithfully and at scale.*
**Oneirion** · Limina. The dreamlike register of the Velvet Canvas.

## V · Signature

### The Velvet Canvas

**Effect** · He opens a wrist and paints. What forms holds intent and weeps when he does, and the space around it saturates until the room's own griefs surface uninvited in whoever is standing in it.
**Cost** · His blood, at volume, from a Vitality of 215. **He faints. Regularly. In company.**
**Limit** · **The flaw in his intent is reproduced at scale.** On a bad night the Canvas paints what he actually feels rather than what he meant, and it does so in front of the subject.
**Counter** · Feel nothing. *Or be honest — the Canvas has nothing to work with in someone with no unspoken grief, and he finds those people restful.*
> **Codex** · Vitalia / Biochemistry / Anima Spirare primary / Stage VI.

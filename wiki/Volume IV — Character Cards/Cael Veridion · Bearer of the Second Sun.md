# Cael Veridion · Bearer of the Second Sun

> *"You think the sun is distant. I carry it in my veins — and I decide when it rises for you."*
> **The card states no Temperance Stage.** **Stage X — Realization assigned and flagged as originated**, on the strength of **Solar Arbitrament**: *Domains within earshot align their harmonic balance toward his words.*
>
> ***Bending other practitioners' Domains is not a Stage IX effect.*** *Realization is where the Continuum begins accommodating the practitioner rather than the reverse, and a man whose speech re-tunes a Domain is being accommodated at the level of law.*
>
> *"Solar / Auric / Bloodlight"* were not Essence types.

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Cael Veridion. **Titles** · **The Dawn-Brand.** Gilded Vein · **Bearer of the Second Sun.**
**Role** · **Solar Ascendant. Divine Arbitrator. Fleshbound Vessel of Radiance.**

### Affiliation

**Formerly Arbitration Division** — *mediator in divine treaty disputes until his transformation during the Heliarch Concord Collapse.* Now a free agent with no official ties, feared and sought after by both Concord and Outer World cults.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage X, Realization** — *and the Collapse is the Catalyst.*
>
> **The Dawn Vein Covenant is "a god-binding rite linking his soul directly to a Celestial Wellspring,"** granting **divine acts without full ascension** — *at the cost of flesh degradation.*
>
> ***He did not reach Realization. He contracted for it***, and the glowing cracks along his arms are the instalment plan.

## II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class V · Radiant.** *Aether bends toward the bearer; output exceeds what the raw numbers suggest.* **On this sheet the Class name is not a metaphor.**
**Crystal State** · **Overgrown**, and structurally failing.
**η** · 0.75–0.80, Band A.
> **The glowing cracks along his arms are the first signs of his eventual dissolution.**
>
> *This is one of very few sheets in the migration with a stated, irreversible terminal condition.* Cael is not powerful and at risk. He is powerful *because* he is dissolving, and the two cannot be separated.

## VI · Traits

**Veins of the Second Sun** · **His blood carries an auric glow that burns through skin like gold poured into flesh**, granting **both regeneration and searing heat to his strikes.**
**Solar Arbitrament** · **When he speaks, Domains within earshot align their harmonic balance toward his words** — *forcing momentary peace or amplifying discord.*

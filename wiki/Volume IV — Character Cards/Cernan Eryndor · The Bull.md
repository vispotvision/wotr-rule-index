# Cernan Eryndor · The Bull

> **Corrected.** "Bovinus Aether / Energetic Axis Flow" were not Essence types. The path **"Ignition → Coagulation → Transference"** mixes an invented stage with two real currents — **Coagulatio and Transference are Wellsprings; Ignition is not.** Nexus Points and Bull's Horn / Iron Hoof are retained as technique geometry rather than as system architecture.

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Cernan Eryndor. **Called** · The Ironblood Rampart. The Bull.
**Standing** · Unaligned Warden of the Outer World. Rumoured descent from the Eryndor bloodline of Lowmere.
**Catalyst Event · Stage VII, Refraction.** The first Domain seed, and with it the soul seeing itself from outside. *For a man whose entire method is not thinking, this was the worst year of his life and he has never described it.*

## II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · Class III · Resonant. **Crystal State** · Refined. **η** · ~0.60, Band C.
**The Raging Bull** · Not worshipped and not summoned. A presence resident in the Crystal. **Pending ruling on whether it is Titanic, Archonic, or a Called thing with a register entry.**

---

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Coagulatio** · Materia. Binds and makes permanent. **Stabilises overflow and sustains exertion across long intervals** — the mechanical reason he does not tire on the timescale he ought to.
**Petralon** · Vectoria. Seismic transmission. The charge, the cratering, the tectonic kick.
**Transference** · Spatium. Coupled oscillators. **This is why impact carries through one enemy into the next** rather than stopping at the first body.
**Monolithion** · The dense stable lattice everything else is built on.

## V · Signature

### Raging Charge

**Effect** · He commits mass and the ground agrees. Formation, wall, gate, rank — the charge does not seek a weak point, **it makes the whole face into the weak point simultaneously**, and Transference carries the impact through the first thing struck into everything standing behind it.
**Cost** · Coagulatio holds him together through the exertion and the holding is not free. *He sleeps eleven hours afterward and cannot be woken.*
**Limit** · **He must be able to reach it in a straight line.**
**Counter** · Do not be in the line. **Gnosis 195 means he will not read the feint**, and every commander who has beaten him has beaten him with one.
> **Codex** · Vectoria / Mechanics / Petralon primary / Stage VII.

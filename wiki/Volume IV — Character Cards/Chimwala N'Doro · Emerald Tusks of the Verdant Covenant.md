# Chimwala N'Doro · Emerald Tusks of the Verdant Covenant

> *"The earth remembers more than stone and bone — it remembers the soul of those who walk it."*
> *"Nature-Aether / Spiritual Aether"* were not Essence types. **"Edenis Wellspring"** is not among the sixty; **Verdantia is**, and the fractal glade description fits it exactly.

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Chimwala N'Doro. **Called** · **Emerald Tusks of the Verdant Covenant.**
**Role** · **Sage of the Emerald Path. Spirit-Glyph Weaver. Keeper of the Ancestral Grove.**

### Affiliation

**Verdant Covenant**, an earth-aligned druidic order. **Advisor to the Council of the Innerworld Grove.** *Spirit Envoy to the Treaty Assembly.*
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VIII, Transcendence.** *The world is read as symbol. Objects retain their weight and gain a second weight, and both facts are operative.*
>
> **A tusk is a tusk and is also a record.** *Tuskwrought Memory is not a metaphor on this sheet — it is Transcendence's dual reading made into a filing system, and he carries it on his face.*

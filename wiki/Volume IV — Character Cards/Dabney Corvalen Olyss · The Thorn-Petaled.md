# Dabney Corvalen Olyss · The Thorn-Petaled

> *He is not a miracle worker. He is a promise made flesh.*
> **Corrected.** **"Aether Class: Luminary-Benedict"** is not among the eight — he is **Class II, Harmonic**, which is what Band D reads. The **Conjunction-Benedictine Spiral** "Soul Crystal Type" has been folded into layer description. **Stage VI — Glory is correct as written** and is one of the few Stage assignments in the legacy set that needed no adjustment.

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Dabney Corvalen Olyss. **Called** · The Thorn-Petaled Vow.
**Standing** · Sanctum Lux. Spirit-bound paladin. Late twenties.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VI, Glory.** The Catalyst is **willingly accepting genuine soul-cost to preserve something valued above his own structural integrity** — and it is the first Catalyst on the ladder that is a decision rather than a thing that happens to you.
>
> *Murmuring happens to you. Welling happens to you. Ascension arrives sideways. Flourishing requires another person. Glory requires that you look at something that will cost you structurally and choose it anyway, in advance, in cold blood.*
>
> **Every vow Dabney has made is that decision repeated.** The Order calls this the culture of duty. The instructors call it the year they learn what they will pay for.

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

## V · Signature

### The Thorn-Petaled Vow

**Effect** · He speaks a name and stands in front of it. **Oathlight closes around the sworn like a held breath**, and what strikes it is not deflected but *received* — the impact arrives in Dabney instead, on a delay, at full weight. Petals come off his hands throughout and settle on everything nearby.
**Cost** · He takes it. All of it, later, and the delay is the only mercy in the working.
**Limit** · **One name.** He cannot vow broadly and has tried.
**Counter** · Attack the sworn from a direction he cannot stand in. *Or wait — the delay ends.*
> **What nobody knows.** Where the rose-echoes go when the Crystal is full.

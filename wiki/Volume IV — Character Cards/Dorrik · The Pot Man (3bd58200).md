# Dorrik · The Pot Man

> **The lowest-Stage sheet in the migration, and the migration needed one.** Nine of ten souls in the four quarters live and die inside Band F, and every other character migrated so far sits in the top three Bands. **Dorrik is what the system looks like from the floor.**

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Dorrik. **Called** · The Pot Man. Vessel-Wanderer of Forgotten Roads.
**Standing** · Unaffiliated. No guild claims him and he has never asked one to.
**Catalyst Event · Stage III, Ascension.** *The first technique developed independently of instruction — built from his own understanding of what his Essence does rather than from received doctrine.* **He does not know this happened.** The recorded pattern is that the practitioner does the thing, does it again to check, and then sits down. **Dorrik sat down.**

---

## II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · Class I · Muridic. The awakening Shell, flickering with first resonance, **leaking unevenly into space.** Efficiency loss around 30 to 40 percent. *The bearer may appear unstable when the lattice is simply learning to speak.*
**Crystal State** · Awakened. **η** · 0.30–0.45, Band F. **Enormous waste. Techniques feel like shouting into wind.**
**Essence colour** · Smoke-brown. The first genuine clearing, holding a consistent tint. *Often the first time a practitioner sees their own output and recognises it as theirs.*

---

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Monolithion** · The dense stable lattice. **The flesh that resists trauma like weathered stone.**
**Anima Spirare** · Vitalia. Faint. He feels living presence through walls and terrain and assumes everyone does.
**Oneirion** · Limina. **Dormant, and the interesting one.** Configuration space — a working acting on the manifold rather than on the room, reaching targets no line of sight connects. *It activates in sleep, under stress, or when the pot is struck, and he cannot produce it deliberately.*

## V · Signature

### Pot's Boon

**Effect** · Something strikes the pot and the world slows for as long as the note rings. He moves correctly without deciding to — stepping out of a fall, catching a blade's flat, turning down a road he had no reason to take.
**Cost** · Nothing he is aware of. *Which is itself a cost, because he has never learned to meter it.*
**Limit** · **He cannot trigger it and has never tried, because he does not know it is a technique.**
**Counter** · Take the pot.
> **What nobody knows.** What the pot was for. *It is older than he is, it is not his family's, and he has never once cooked in it.*

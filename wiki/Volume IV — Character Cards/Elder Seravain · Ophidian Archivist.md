# Elder Seravain · Ophidian Archivist

> *"A lie is just venom without fangs. Allow me to correct that."*

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Elder Seravain. **Called** · **The Serpent-Speaker.** Ophidian Archivist · **Hand of the Crimson Coil.**
**Role** · **Ophidian Magus. Spiritual Venom-Binder. Keeper of the Scarlet Serpent Codex.**
> **Affiliation — and this is the sheet's most useful fact.**
>
> **Research & Archives Division, Ophidian Studies Cell.** *And secretly a member of the Crimson Coil Circle, a hidden Concord splinter dedicated to reviving the ancient Serpent-God Vaelith.*
>
> ***An R.A.D. elder is running a resurrection cult out of the Accord's own archives.*** **Eira Vale delivers "unfrayed" codices to R.A.D. archivists. Ascella Morwyn is R.A.D. with a Logistics commission.** *Neither sheet knows what is in the Ophidian Studies Cell.*

## IV · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Anamnesis** · Limina. **Governing.** *Objects genuinely store their history in physically readable form — and each scale is an encoded truth. The Codex is not a book he keeps. It is a body he reads.*

> **This is the Venom of Unmaking**, and it reframes the trait entirely. *He does not destroy memories. **Mirithane** · Limina. **Specular reflection** — the glamour, the misdirection.
**Mortalis** · Vitalia. **Governs passage** — *the toxin that ends things lawfully.*

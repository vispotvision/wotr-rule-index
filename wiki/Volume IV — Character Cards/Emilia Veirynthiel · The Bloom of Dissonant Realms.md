# Emilia Veirynthiel · The Bloom of Dissonant Realms

> *"I am not the story you remember. I am the ink smear where the name once lived."*
> **Migrated with all content preserved.** *"Chronoaether / Fae Bloomfall / Colorvoid"* were not Essence types. **"Aether Class: Eldritch Fae Relic"** is not among the eight. **The path "Reverie → Inkfall → Misremember → Chronocurse"** is not a Wellspring progression — *though the legacy sheet's own gloss on it, "not one of power but erosion," is confirmed by the stat spread.*

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Emilia Veirynthiel. **Called** · The Bloom of Dissonant Realms. Formerly **the Mouth of Reflection.** **Neither male nor female.**
**Role** · Fallen Fae. Pactborne Magus. Dreamsmoke Binder. **They embody the shifting currents between illusion and memory, madness and muse.**
**Soul Crystal title** · *Stain of the Rewritten Pact.*

### Affiliation

Formerly of the **Ceruleanth Court**, a twilight fae lineage devoted to reverie and mooncraft. Now wanders the **Liminal Archive** between realms, **crafting forbidden books from lived dreams.** Feared and admired among the **Ink-Eaten**, a cabal of forgotten storytellers and fae exiles.

### Origin & Lore

Emilia was once the Mouth of Reflection, a seer of the Faelands who **foretold not futures, but what the soul pretended not to see.** Their visions were too clear, their truths too raw. **And so, as punishment, the High Fae sealed their tongue with threads of silence and cast them into the Tarnish Between Tales — a space where stories go to rot.**
**There, they did not die. They wrote.**
Now Emilia drifts between realms as both ink and author, speaking only through the curling smoke of their pipe and the shifting stains that follow their robes. **Their presence warps memory — one may remember meeting them in a dream before the dream was ever dreamt.**
> **Catalyst Event · Stage IX, Invocation.** *Every technique before this was the practitioner's own Crystal doing something. From Invocation, the practitioner asks and something larger answers — and the answering is not metaphor, is not reliable, and is not free. The Wellspring is a co-author.*
>
> **For a writer, that is not a metaphor either.** *The recorded psychological pattern is relief followed within a year or two by something worse, as the practitioner realises the relief was no longer being solely responsible for their own output* — **and Emilia has been co-authored since the Tarnish, and has never once claimed the books are entirely theirs.**

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Anamnesis** · Limina. **Governing.** *Objects genuinely store their history in physically readable form — Anamnesis is the instrument, not the miracle.* **Its failure is thermal resetting: reading is not always passive, and a careless practitioner erases the record in the act of taking it.** *Emilia does not read carefully. The ink is what comes off.*
**Oneirion** · Limina. Configuration space — **a system's every possible arrangement as a coordinate in a space with no physical location.** *This is the mechanical reason their workings reach targets no line of sight connects, and the reason they are so difficult to describe afterward.* **The Inkblot Eidolons live here.**
**Letheveil** · Limina. Lossy compression — **it identifies what is load-bearing in a memory and discards the rest, and the practitioner's certainty about which is which is the whole of the risk.** *Failure: generation loss. Each pass discards a little more, and a memory compressed repeatedly degrades toward an artefact the practitioner still experiences as a memory.*
**Mirithane** · Limina. Specular reflection. **The Mouth of Reflection, named for the current.**
> **Four Limina harmonisations — same-Family compounding**, and the reason their Gnosis reads denser than the figure accounts for. *Where Sinclair uses Limina to take and Sandalphon to cancel, Emilia uses it to overwrite.*

## VII · Technique

### The Tarnish Between Tales · *Signature*

**Effect** · They do not attack a person. **They overwrite the account of the person** — what a room agrees happened, what a witness will later swear to, what the ground itself recorded. Afterwards there is no contradiction to point at, because **the contradiction was the thing that got compressed out.**
**Cost** · **Generation loss runs both ways.** Every pass takes a little more of their own record with it, and they cannot tell which passes took what.
**Limit** · They cannot write forward. **Only what has already happened is available to be rewritten.**
**Counter** · **A written record they have not touched.** *Lampblack Register Ink does not lift, and any alteration becomes visible under Concord light — which is why the Accord's archives are the one place Emilia does not go.*
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the tongue was ever unsealed, or whether they simply stopped needing it.

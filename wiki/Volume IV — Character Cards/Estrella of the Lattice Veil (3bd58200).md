# Estrella of the Lattice Veil

> **Corrected.** **"The Wellspring of Conjunction"** is not among the sixty — Conjunction is one of the Great Work operations and, per Alchemetrica, **the only one of the four alchemical operations with no single Wellspring mirror.** Her practice is real; the current under it was misnamed. **"Old Lunar Harmonics (pre-Atlas Moons)"** carried as a flag rather than a system entry.

## I · Identity

**Name** · Estrella of the Lattice Veil. **Called** · Occult Medium of the Outer Realms. Soul-Binder of the Conjunction Rite.
**Standing** · Unbound. Latent agent of the Creviceward Circle — **not a member.**
**Catalyst Event · Stage VII, Refraction.** The Domain seed, and with it the soul seeing itself. **Half-Seer's Eye is not a curse or an artefact.** *One eye sees in metaphor and the other sees what once was true, which is Refraction's dual sight arriving in a practitioner whose Gnosis was already split between the living and the after.*

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Transference** · Spatium. **Primary.** Resonant exchange between coupled oscillators — **soul transfer with an actual mechanism.** *Failure: reverse coupling. A gradient runs both ways, and a Transference practitioner beside a depleted party empties into them without deciding to.*
**Fixatio** · Materia. Anchors and seals. **The binding contracts.**
**Anamnesis** · Limina. What the bound spirits are still carrying.
**Eidolyn** · Limina. Templated nucleation. The suturing — **fragments deposit along the template she imposes, and hold, and the strength is real material behaviour.**

## V · Signature

### The Conjunction Rite

**Effect** · She joins what was separated — a spirit to a vessel, a fragment to a whole, a petitioner to something on the far side of a threshold. **The serpent-coil Drafts hold identity intact across the transformation**, which is the part nobody else can reliably do.
**Cost** · Alchemetrica's three questions attach here and she answers them aloud, every time: *what is being joined, by what right, and who bears the cost if the union refuses.* **When the answer to the third is somebody absent, she declines.**
**Limit** · She cannot anchor. **Something else must hold it.**
**Counter** · Remove the anchor. *Or be the cost — a Conjunction whose price is borne by a consenting present party is one she will not walk away from, and that has been used on her twice.*

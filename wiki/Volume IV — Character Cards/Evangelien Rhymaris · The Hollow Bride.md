# Evangelien Rhymaris · The Hollow Bride

> *"I do not remember his name… but I still wear the veil. That is enough."*
> **Migrated from the legacy Trello sheet with all content preserved.** Origin, traits, relationships, quote and chapter appearances carry across unchanged. **Only the system layer is corrected.**
>
> *"Sorrowbound Aether / Lachrymose Vitalis / Hollow Spiritus"* were not Essence types. **"Aether Class: Aberrant, Elegiac Category"** is not among the eight. **The path "Erosion → Unraveling → Ravel → Erasure" is not a Wellspring progression** — but see Section VI, because it was describing something real and the stat sheet confirms it.

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Evangelien Rhymaris. **Called** · The Hollow Bride. Saint Erasure, by those who worship her.
**Role** · Living Mourning. Apostle of the Sundered Dirge. **She is not alive in the way mortals understand it** — a vessel of grief that became self-aware, a spiritual revenant bound to a forgotten Wellspring deep beneath the Inner World's crust, where silence weeps into eternity.
**Soul Crystal title** · *Threnodia Sancta*, the Sainted Dirge.

### Affiliation

Unbound to any kingdom. **Herald of the lost Weeping Choir of Elenthyr.** Feared and worshipped by fringe cults of Sanctum Lux and by the Thorn-Seers. **Considered a metaphysical anomaly by the Guild of Ritus.**

### Origin & Lore

Born from an ancient soul ritual gone awry during the final siege of Elenthyr. **Evangelien was to be a vessel bride for a divine union. Her beloved was slain, and the ritual continued — empty.** Instead of severing, her soul was suspended in lamentation, coalescing into something neither dead nor ascended.
Her body continued to exist, wandering the Inner World in wedding garments soaked with spectral ink and spiritual decay. **Over time she began to weep blood** — not from sadness, but from the unbearable pressure of being remembered by the land itself.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage X, Realization.** *The practitioner stops being the party that has to be accommodated. The Continuum adjusts — locally, grudgingly, and the direction of the accommodation never reverses back.*
>
> **She did not reach Realization by achievement. The land reached it on her behalf.** The lore's own phrase is exact: *the unbearable pressure of being remembered by the land itself.* **That is Stage X described from inside a person who never asked for it**, and it is why the Guild of Ritus cannot classify her — their instruments are built to read what a soul did, and nothing here was done.

## II · Soul Architecture

**Essence Core** · Suspended rather than fractured. *The ritual did not sever and did not complete, and the Core is what a working looks like when it was never closed.*
**Aether Shell** · **Class IV · Luminous.** The lucid Shell, which thins. **Aether behaviour mirrors the bearer's emotional alignment with unnerving precision** — a lateral Class most often found where the Shell has clarified faster than the Core. *Hers clarified because there was nothing left in it.*
**Attraction Layer** · Dominant, and pointed at a name she no longer has.
**Crystal State** · **Overgrown.** Power exceeding architecture, discharging without intent.
**η** · 0.75–0.80, Band A.
**Essence colour** · Vitalia arterial red at the tear-line against Limina black-violet through the field. *The red triggers the mammalian recognition-response to blood and the violet registers as the absence of the colour that should be there. Standing near her, an observer's body reacts before their mind does.*

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Anamnesis** · Limina. **Governing.** Remanent magnetisation — *basalt records the direction of the field it cooled in and holds that record for geological time. Objects genuinely store their history in physically readable form.* **The land remembers Elenthyr, and Evangelien is the readout.**
**Lethegraal** · Limina. Relaxation time — the constant with which a disturbed system returns to equilibrium. *Its failure is under-damping: shorten the constant too far and the system overshoots on every cycle, and the practitioner oscillates rather than settling.* **She has been oscillating for an age.**
**Catharsis** · Caloria. Pale rose-gold. Release and true clearing. *The doctrine warns that nothing is purified by being merely emptied — if what is removed has not been rightly transformed, it returns elsewhere and more venomously.* **The Bloodlace tears are what returns.**
**Nyxial** · Limina. The Nocturne Flame. Thermal inertia under negative feedback — **a baseline that does not rise in favourable conditions and does not fall in hostile ones.** *She cannot be comforted and cannot be worsened.*

## VI · Traits

**Dirge of the Hollow Oath** · Her presence generates a slow, maddening hymn. **Spirits and weaker souls begin to weep uncontrollably in her vicinity.** *Harmonics Chord at 668 — the hymn is a chord, not a melody, and that is why it cannot be resolved by listening harder.*
**Crimson Veil** · She distorts memory and perception. **All who look upon her see someone they mourned.** *Anamnesis reading outward instead of in.*
**Grace of the Unconsummated** · **She is untouchable by pure divine force. Blessings pass through her, unfulfilled.** *Resilience Immunity at 690, and the theological reason is in her origin: a rite that was begun and never closed cannot be added to.*
**Bloodlace Blessing** · Her tears heal or kill depending on the emotional weight behind them. **They are sometimes harvested by black market mourn-priests.**
> **Trade note.** Bloodlace would sit in the Standing Index at **Provenance Class IV — Crystal Draw**, rendered from something that carried a Soul Crystal, with **identity-shaped carry.** *Three lawful sources exist and the arithmetic has never balanced.* **Nothing about the mourn-priests' trade is lawful and the Bench has never been asked to rule on whether she counts as a source.**

## VII · Technique

### The Sundered Dirge · *Signature*

**Effect** · She does not sing it. **The hymn is what a room does in her presence** — grief surfacing unprompted in everyone inside it, each person mourning their own dead rather than hers, and the mourning arriving as physical weight rather than as feeling.
**Cost** · **She cannot stop it and has never tried.** Every hour of it runs an Overgrown Crystal further past what its architecture holds.
**Limit** · It reaches those who have lost someone. *The genuinely unbereaved walk through it unaffected, and there are very few of them.*
**Counter** · **Nothing addressed to her works.** Blessings pass through, compulsion finds no coherent self to seize. *The only recorded method is to leave, and to have someone else carry you if you are weeping too hard to walk.*
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the Wellspring beneath the Inner World's crust is bound to her or she to it, **and whether it was there before Elenthyr fell.**

## VIII · Relationships

**Azure Serathiel** · He witnessed her wandering the ruins of Elenthyr and offered a silent bow. **She bowed back. No words passed.** *Both sheets are Limina-Mirithane adjacent and both are held at a Stage they cannot leave. The bow was two people recognising the same condition.*
**Emilia Veirynthiel** · **They consider each other kin in unbeing.** Emilia once bound Evangelien's tears into a song no bard dares to sing.
**Ambrose Virellith** · A mirrored moment in the halls of a ruined shrine. They bowed to one another and passed by without a word.
**The Flock of Thorns** · Worship her as Saint Erasure, **believing that by emulating her pain they will transcend mortality.** *They will not. Her Threshold numbers are the lowest relevant figures on the sheet, and emulating her is emulating a soul that has stopped being able to advance.*

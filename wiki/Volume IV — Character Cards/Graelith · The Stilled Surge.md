# Graelith · The Stilled Surge

> *"Noise is merely the writhing of the unbroken. I bring the quiet where all things come apart."*
> **Same Archon error.** *"Silence (Archon of Melting)" — and Morphaene's sheet assigns Solution to the same "Archon of Melting," which is two different operations attributed to one non-existent Archon.* **Dissolution is genuine; Silence and Fermentation are not among the sixty.** *Re-sourced below.*

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Nihiloth** · Limina. **Governing.** *Destructive interference — two waves in antiphase do not conceal one another. They sum to zero, and the region between them carries no energy at all.* A Nihiloth Domain does not impose law. It cancels the law already present.
> **This is the Stilled March, exactly.** *He does not silence a battlefield by suppressing it. He generates the antiphase of it, and the Wellspring channels running through that region stop having anything to carry.*
**Dissolution** · Limina. **Cohesion corroded under the silence.** *Same-Family compounding, which is why the field reads denser than his Ardency accounts for.*
**Tenebra** · Limina. **Quieter than the background.**
**Lethegraal** · Limina. *Relaxation time shortened* — **Silence Bloom, the rot that develops in muted terrain only after the battle ends.**

## VII · Signature

### The Quiet Bloom

**Effect** · He walks and the field stops carrying anything. **Incantations arrive at nothing. Wellspring channels find no medium.** *And afterwards, in the quiet ground, the rot he seeded comes up.*
**Cost** · **Nihiloth cancels symmetrically.** *He is inside the field too, and nothing he brings works either.*
**Limit** · **He is a walking radius.** *He cannot project it, cannot throw it, cannot leave it somewhere.*
**Counter** · **Runecraft.** *Ars Scripti needs no voice — a cut sentence works in a silent field, which is precisely what Spellcraft cannot do. An Engraver working in advance is his answer, and it is the same answer as Velastra's.*

# Hieronymus Cruciferi · The Immortal

> *"Your bones are mortar for my road, your screams the song that paves it."*
> **Migrated with all content preserved, including the full artifact entry.** *"Oblivion-Aether / Kinetic Aether / Reversal Aether"* were not Essence types. **The legacy sheet states "Stage VII" in its own hazard-registry line and that is retained** — one of the few Stage assignments in this volume that needed no adjustment.
>
> **"Flux Sustain — maintains peak output until Essence reserve drops below 30%"** is the one percentage figure kept, because **it describes a reserve threshold rather than a metaphysical quantity** and Fracture of Worlds names ten percent as the Essence Starvation floor. *His thirty percent is a personal operating margin, not a law.*

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Hieronymus Cruciferi. **Called** · The Immortal. **Bearer of the Black Sun Cross.**
**Role** · **Relentless Battlefield Reaper. Vanguard of the Dead-Cross.** Kinetic and reversal specialist.

### Affiliation

Former Champion of the **Oblivion Concord**, now independent. **Listed in Guild Accord hazard registries as a "Stage VII Class X" spell disruptor and Essence annihilator.** Subject of **multiple Outer Realm warrants for extreme battlefield destruction.**
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VII, Refraction.** *The soul sees itself from outside — and self-examination at this depth reveals the fracture record. Everything Splintering promoted to a load path becomes visible, labelled, and impossible to un-see.*
>
> **He was bound to the Cross "through a rite that burned his mortality away."** *A man who looked at his own architecture and then had the mortal part of it removed did not fail Refraction. He completed it and did not like what he found, and the Black Sun Core is what he put in the gap.*

## II · Soul Architecture

**Essence Core** · **The Black Sun Core** — *a miniature, imploded star of anti-light nested within the Soul Crystal. Not a source of heat or life, but of collapse. The more Essence it consumes, the more violently it can discharge annihilation.*
**Aether Shell** · **Class VI · Voidic.** The inverted Shell. **Instead of emitting, it devours, remembers and reflects** — *his Ardency does not deal damage. It removes things.*
**Attraction Layer** · **Effectively absent.** *See Section IV.*
**Crystal State** · **Overgrown.** Power exceeding architecture, discharging without intent.
**η** · ~0.60, Band C.

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Nihiloth** · Limina. *Destructive interference and the null field — two waves in antiphase do not conceal one another. They sum to zero, and the region between them carries no energy at all.* A Nihiloth Domain does not impose law. It cancels the law already present.
> *And the failure is that the cancellation is symmetric: everything he brought inside is subject to the same null, including the parts of himself he was relying on.* **This is Curse of Rejectance, and it is not a defence. It is a side effect he has learned to stand in.**
**Dissolution** · Limina. *The unbound state is thermodynamically preferred, and the process runs on its own once initiated.* **Essence unravelled to null.**
**Petralon** · Vectoria. Seismic and kinetic transmission — **Force Redirection, and the maul.**
**Abython** · Vectoria. Near-black force. **Weight without hue.**

## VIII · Artifact · The Black Sun Cross

**Type** · Relic / Weapon / **Soulbound Domain Anchor.**
**Appearance** · **A massive cruciform war maul**, forged of voidsteel veined with glowing deathlight; **each arm of the cross etched with spiral Parun runes that shift in combat.**
**Origin** · **Forged in the last days of the Oblivion Concord, bound to Hieronymus through a rite that burned his mortality away.**
**Essence Alignment** · Oblivion and Death-Aether. **Parun Glyphs** · *Mu (Ending), Oru (Power), Uur (Disruption).*
**Effects** · **Aura of Fear** — psychic deathwave eroding enemy resolve · **Soul Absorption** — binds slain souls to the weapon, empowering the wielder · **Smite** — concentrated beam of collapse-energy · **Death Coil** — expanding spiral draining life into the wielder.
**Limitations** · **High soul-strain. Core Fracture risk if overloaded.**
> **Soul Absorption puts the Cross squarely in Standing Index territory as a Class IV Crystal Draw instrument** — *rendered from things that carried a Soul Crystal, with identity-shaped carry.* **The Index records three lawful sources and states plainly that the arithmetic has never balanced.**
>
> *The Cross has no lawful source at all. It is not an unentered formula. It is an unentered organ, and the Bench has never been asked to rule on it because nobody has ever got close enough to file.*

## IX · Signature

### Black Sun Discharge

**Effect** · The Core takes in and takes in, **and then the slow gravitational pull becomes a violent shattering release.** *Structure inside the radius is not damaged. It stops being structured.*
**Cost** · **Overgrown Crystal, three unshielded risk stats, and a Core that has to be fed to discharge.** *He is always holding more than his architecture supports.*
**Limit** · **It must consume before it can release.** *Starve the Core and the Cross is a very heavy maul.*
**Counter** · **Give him nothing to eat.** *Nihiloth's symmetric cancellation means anything he brings inside — including his own reserves — goes with it, and a man who has annihilated the field has annihilated his own supply.*

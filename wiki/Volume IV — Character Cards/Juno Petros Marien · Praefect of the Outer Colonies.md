> *"Peace cannot be ruled, only earned. And pride does not buy bread, nor bury sons gently."*
> **DEAD CANON — THIS SHEET SITS ON A RETCONNED POLITY.**

## I · Identity

**Name** · Juno Petros Marien. **Called** · The Veil-Warden of Babyl.
**Role** · **Praefect of the Outer Colonies.** Sovereign Arbitrator of the Bound Marches. Diplomat, strategist, and executor of imperial law.

### Affiliation

**Babylian Imperium**, Outer Colonial Authority. Bound to the **House of Civil Flame**, the administrative lineage of rational governance. Sworn servant of the **Imperial Concord of Doctrine & Peace.**
> **Catalyst Event · Stage IX, Invocation.** *From Invocation the practitioner asks, and something larger answers — and the answering is not metaphor, is not reliable, and is not free. The Domain consolidates into a Realm capable of holding other Domains.*
>
> The legacy sheet calls him a **Barrier Soul**, *a rare kind whose metaphysical role is to uphold, govern, and prevent collapse*, and gives his path as **the Bastion of Dominion.** **A Realm that holds other Domains inside it is exactly that**, stated in the system's own terms.

> *The recorded pattern at Invocation is relief, then something worse — the practitioner works out that the relief was no longer being solely responsible for their own output, and that the thing they handed responsibility to did not sign anything.* **For a man whose entire office is being answerable, that is a particular kind of problem.**

## VII · Technique

### The Veil Proclamation · *Signature*

**Effect** · He reads a clause nobody living remembers into a room that has no intention of obeying it, **and the room finds the alternative harder to hold than compliance.** Not compulsion — Harmonics 295 forecloses that. *The Weave simply bends toward the declaration because the declaration is correct and he is standing there.*
**Cost** · **Integral windup.** Every year of holding a periphery against a load he cannot resolve accumulates correction with nowhere to go. *He does not discharge. He has not discharged yet.*
**Limit** · **It works on institutions and not on individuals.** A single person who simply refuses is beyond him, and he knows it.
**Counter** · Remove the load suddenly. *Or be one man, alone, with nothing to govern.*
> **What nobody knows.** What happens when the correction finally lifts. **The legacy sheet says he delivered his final judgment and vanished into the Eastern Range, and that rumours say he dissolved into light mid-sentence.** *That is what integral windup looks like from outside.*

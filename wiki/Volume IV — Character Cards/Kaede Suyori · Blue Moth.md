# Kaede Suyori · Blue Moth

> *"You break fevers; I carry the heat away. Let me."*
> **The legacy sheet states no Temperance Stage at all.** **Stage VII — Refraction assigned and flagged as originated**, on the strength of the field effect: *where Kaede stands, heat leaves the air and panic forgets its reasons.* **A standing region operating on the practitioner's terms is a Domain seed**, and Refraction is where the seed first activates.

## IX · Kaito Akagiri

**How it began** · **Kaede watched Kaito move a council like illness moving through a city — and loved him for his precision, not his cruelty.** *They walked beside him and showed him how to leave a pulse in a throat rather than a tremor.*
**Dynamic** · **He heats; they cool.** He bends lines; they mend them so no one sees the crease. **He draws blood in the smallest possible measure; they make sure it clots only where it should.**
**Private language** · **A touch to the wrist means** ***enough*****.** *A moth lit on his shoulder means take the long way.* **A blue ribbon tied to his sheath means** ***I will follow even here*****.**
**Why it works** · **Kaito dominates rooms; Kaede refuses to compete with rooms — only with his worst impulses.** *Their love is not surrender; it is leverage applied with devotion.*

# Kurokiba Ranzou · The Drunken Oni

> *"Every swing's a toast… and every toast is your funeral."*
> **Migrated with all content preserved, including the artifacts.** *"Ferment-Aether / Fire-Aether / Body-Aether"* were not Essence types. **"Aether Class: Berserker Brewer"** is not among the eight; **he is Class III, Resonant.** *"Temperance Compatibility: Journeyman (Stage VII)"* mixes the Accord rank ladder with the Temperance ladder — **Stage VII retained, Journeyman struck.**

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Kurokiba Ranzou. **Called** · The Drunken Oni. **Brew-Sage.**
**Role** · **Oni Duelist and Brew-Sage. Fermentation Combat Master.**

### Affiliation

**Wandering Oni cultivator from the Ember-Sake Highlands.** Holds the honorary title of **Brew-Sage** among Oni clans for mastery of Essence fermentation. **Known in Guild Accord records as an unpredictable yet highly dangerous mercenary-for-hire.**
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VII, Refraction.** *Gnosis undergoes a qualitative jump no other Stage produces, and practitioners in Refraction are notoriously difficult to be around because they have just been handed the ability to examine their own architecture and have not yet learned to stop.*
>
> **He examined his own architecture and decided to ferment it.** *Which is either the least serious response to Refraction in the record, or the only one that worked — and the Oni halls have never agreed which.*

## VIII · Signature

### Ferment Burst

**Effect** · Stored Essence-alcohol released all at once — **the vapour goes out, the Fire-Aether goes after it, and the air itself is the weapon.** *Everything within it loses focus first and footing second.*
**Cost** · **Overchannel and Detonation both carry unshielded structural risk, and both are near the top of his sheet, in an Overgrown Crystal.** *He is one bad night from a Fracture Event and has been for years.*
**Limit** · **The brew must have built.** *Fight him early and he is a very strong drunk with a gourd.*
**Counter** · **Do not let him drink, and do not give him time.** *Or exploit Gnosis 182 — he cannot read a feint.*

# Leon Vaerenthal · The Farmer

> **Corrected.** "Hemothaumic Aether / Noctis Vitalis / Lament of Maren" were not three Essence types. **"Wellspring of the Fractured Fang" with a Loss → Fury → Restraint → Protection path** is an emotional arc, not a Wellspring. **Stage V is confirmed by the legacy sheet's own "Ashroot Core (Fractured Stage V)"** and needed no adjustment.

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Leon Vaerenthal. **Called** · The Crimson Stray. Fang of the Fallen Field. **The Farmer.**
**Standing** · Unaligned wanderer. Formerly a farmer of Eld Hollow, Senoth borderland. Marked by the Scarlet Communion. Hunted by Sanctum Lux inquisitors.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage V, Splintering.** His sister Maren died and he survived contact with what killed her.
>
> **Splintering fractures along emotional lines, not along the lines of the encounter.** It does not create the flaw; it promotes the flaw to a load path. *Leon went in carrying Maren and came out with her structurally present in his Crystal, permanently, in a way the coil reads as a second signature.*
>
> **The Lament is not a haunting. It is architecture.**

## II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · Class II · Harmonic. **Attraction Layer** · Two Axes and both load-bearing — one living, one not. **Crystal State** · **Fractured.** **η** · ~0.50, Band E.
**Essence colour** · Splintering fracture-streak: hairline Vitalia arterial red running through a darker field, **appearing and vanishing with emotional spikes.** A field visibly at war with itself.

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Anima Spirare** · Vitalia. Bright arterial red — **the most viscerally uncomfortable Family colour to observe.** The blood-forming. *Observers' pulses accelerate near him and they cannot say why.*
**Mortalis** · Vitalia. Venous black-red. The curse half, and the reason the Communion wanted him.
**Anamnesis** · Limina. Maren's soulprint, read as a stored record rather than a ghost.

## V · Signature

### Ashen Guard

**Effect** · It is not chosen. When someone he has an Axis to is in danger the Essence flares on its own — crimson mist hardening into a standing wall, blood-formed from wounds he does not remember taking.
**Cost** · **He does not control the onset and cannot decline it.** Every activation runs Overchannel above its safe ceiling in a Crystal already past the instability line.
**Limit** · It only triggers for an Axis. *A stranger dying in front of him produces nothing at all, and he has been present for that.*
**Counter** · **Threaten Tilda and he will flare.** Repeatedly, until the Crystal fails. The Communion knows this.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether Maren's echo is responding or merely recorded.

# Lucien Draeve · Emerald Alchemist

> *"Every sip is a choice. Every choice is a death. I simply make sure the two arrive together."*

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Lucien Draeve. **Called** · **The Gilded Toxin.** Smiling Arsenist · **Emerald Alchemist.**
**Role** · **Master Alchemist. Poison-Savant. Covert Concord Operative.**
> **Affiliation · Black Concord, Toxin Arts Division.** *Formerly Research & Archives Division for medicinal alchemy* — **shifted to assassination and sabotage after several "unfortunate accidents" removed his political rivals.**
>
> ***Second R.A.D. defector in Volume IV.*** **Elder Seravain never left — he runs the Crimson Coil Circle from inside the Ophidian Studies Cell.** *Lucien left openly and they have "exchanged venoms and antidotes for centuries."* **One stayed and one went, and they are still trading.**

---

## II · Catalyst

> **Stage IX, Invocation.** *From Invocation the practitioner asks and something larger answers. The Wellspring becomes a co-author, and the answering is not reliable and not free.*
>
> **"Within his Domain, every airborne particle becomes his instrument."** *He is not controlling the air. He is asking, and the Domain is answering, which is why the effect scales past what a chemist should manage and why the card calls him among the most dangerous poison-wielders alive.*

## IV · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Transmutatio** · Spatium. **Change of basis** — *and its principle is exactly Catalyst's Touch: failed workings are not destroyed Essence but Essence in an inconvenient basis.* Harmless brews and potent toxins are the same substance in different coordinates.
**Dissolution** · Limina. *Solvation driven by the entropy of mixing — the process runs on its own once initiated.* The Alkahest.
**Solfatara** · Caloria. **Deep sulphurous red.** The Emerald Veil, and the fume.
**Mirithane** · Limina. **The glamour half.**

## VII · Traits

**Catalyst's Touch** · **Can instantly accelerate or alter the reaction of any substance he touches with Essence** — *turning harmless brews into potent toxins, or vice versa, in moments.*
**Emerald Veil** · **Constantly exudes an almost imperceptible green vapour**, harmless in small doses, but **prolonged exposure causes mild confusion and impaired reflexes** — giving him an edge in negotiations or fights.
**Gilded Dose** · **Precision-crafted, gold-inlaid containers keep his mixtures perfectly stable until use.** *These can be flung, inhaled, or injected mid-combat with no loss in potency.*

## VIII · Signature

### The Alkahest Crown

**Effect** · **Within his Domain, every airborne particle becomes his instrument** — *able to heal, kill, or alter with a flick of thought.*
**Cost** · **Dissolution runs on its own once initiated and does not distinguish his structures from anyone else's.** *The alkahest cannot be stored in anything, including him.*
**Limit** · **Air.** *Sealed lungs, vacuum, or hard wind and he is a man with jars.*
**Counter** · **Reach him.** *Ardency 280, Vitality 222 — the second-lowest survivability of any Stage IX character in the migration.*
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the "unfortunate accidents" were his work or the Domain's.

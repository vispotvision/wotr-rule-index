# Rathien Veythar · The Fang

> *"They won't hear me. They won't see me. They'll just fall."*

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Rathien Veythar. **Called** · **The Crimson Whisper.** Black-Threaded Fang · Shadow-Step Duelist · **Visceral Cutter.**
**Epithet** · **The Silent Hand of the Sanguine Tally.**
**Affiliation** · **Independent, ex-Scarlet Accord.** *Operates as a highly selective contractor.*
**Profile** · **A highly specialised, lethal assassin focused on speed, silence, and the manipulation of vital fluids as a medium.** *He avoids brute force, relying on precise application of layered effects to ensure a clean, untraceable kill —* ***viewing excessive force as a flaw in method.***
> **Catalyst Event · Stage III, Ascension.** *Everything until this moment was received — somebody showed you and you copied it and it held, and it was never yours. Then one day you build a thing out of your own understanding, and it holds.*
>
> **He achieved Ascension by rigidly controlling his visceral impulses and understanding that speed and silence are more potent than destructive output.** *That is not a technique he was taught. It is a conclusion he reached, which is the whole of Stage III.*

## II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class III · Resonant.** *He runs a focused, high-density current beneath the primary flow for stealth and perceptual distortion — a layered practice rather than a single channel.*
**Crystal State** · Refined. **η** · ~0.40, Band F.
**Aura** · **Subtly shifts ambient light into a soft-focus grey within a metre**; *produces a minor, almost imperceptible drag on the surrounding air.*
> **Risk · Kinetic Overload.** *Excessive use without proper dispersal of compressed Aether causes internal capillary rupture and temporary motor skill degradation.*
>
> **For an assassin whose entire value is precision, a technique whose failure mode is losing fine motor control is a well-designed cost.**

**Anima Spirare** · Vitalia. **Bright arterial red.** *The manipulation of vital fluids, sourced.*

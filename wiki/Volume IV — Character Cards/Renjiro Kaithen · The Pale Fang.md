# Renjiro Kaithen · The Pale Fang

> *"Steel cuts flesh, ash rots steel, but memory… memory rots the soul."*
> **Stage corrected under the standing ruling.** The legacy sheet reads **"Stage IV — Ascension."** *Ascension is Stage III; Stage IV is Flourishing.* **Name wins — Stage III.**
>
> *"Ash (Decay) / Steel (Martial) / Wind (Motion)"* were not Essence types. **The Ashen Fang** is retained as the Temperance Cycle's name.

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Renjiro Kaithen. **Called** · The Pale Fang. **Ash-Warden of Kyru.**
**Role** · **Ronin Duelist. Ashen Wellspring Swordsman. Keeper of the Bone-Sealed Vows.**

### Affiliation

**None.** Formerly of the **Senoth Kingdom's Ash Warden Corps**; currently a wandering sword-for-hire.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage III, Ascension.** ***The first thing you made yourself, working.***
>
> *Everything until that moment was received — somebody showed you and you copied it and it held, and you were proud of it, and it was never yours. Then one day you build a thing out of your own understanding, and it holds.* **The whole of Stage III is the difference between those two experiences.**
>
> *The folk name for it is coming into the road, and Renjiro left the Corps and took to it literally.* **The recorded pattern is that the practitioner does the thing, does it again to check, and then sits down.**

## VII · Signature

### The Ashen Fang

**Effect** · He does not break a guard. **He rots it.** *Ash finds the weapon, the plate, the construct — and three exchanges later the thing that was blocking him is failing on its own.*
**Cost** · **Time.** *Decay is not fast, and he has C-Grade output to survive on while it works.*
**Limit** · **It works on made things.** Flesh is not improved by it.
**Counter** · **Owe him nothing and give him nothing.** *Or simply be quick — the Mantle holds three seconds.*
> **What nobody knows.** Who Kyru was.

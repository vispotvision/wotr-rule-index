# Ristra Lenovira · The Hollow

> *"I'm no hero, no savior. But if you give me a target... they'll never see the sky again."*

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Ristra Lenovira. **Called** · The Hollow of the Outer World. ***"Eyes of Gold, Scars of Iron."***
**Race** · **Elf, bloodline unknown.** *Latent ancestry grants reflex clarity and perception anchoring.*
**Role** · **Long-Range Execution Specialist. Ghostwalker of the Scattered Wastes. Loyal Marksman of Nyx.**

### Affiliation

**Former slave of the Outer World's auction circuits.** Currently **bonded to Nyx** as her personal sharpshooter. *Wanders under her banner without formal alignment to any guild or Concord.*
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VII, Refraction.** *The soul sees itself from outside, and looking at it changes it — and knowing that it changes it changes it again.*
>
> **For a man sold at auction, being handed the ability to examine his own architecture was not a gift.** *The legacy sheet calls his Wellspring "a heat-shimmering orb locked behind mental pressure, pulsing with unrealised tension."* **That is a Domain seed that will not stabilise, in a soul that has never been permitted to want anything.**

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Eclipseron** · Spatium. *Occultation and transit photometry — planets are found by the dip in a star's brightness, not by being seen.* Weak-point recognition at five hundred metres is not sharp eyesight. It is reading the absence where a body's structure fails.
**Mirithane** · Limina. Specular reflection. **Illusory Glimmer — the afterimages and false silhouettes.**
**Cinerion** · Caloria. The internal burn, the reflex sharpening. *And its cost is on the sheet already: chronic fatigue.*
**Nyxial** · Limina. **A baseline that does not fluctuate with environmental pressure.** *He survives in stillness because stillness is a regulated setpoint.*

## VII · Signature

# Saekiro Malrake · The Phantom

> **LINEAGE FLAG — the most consequential single line found in the migration so far.**
>
> The legacy card states Saekiro is the **"Unaware Lineal Descendant of Sodoku Moto and Oliver Moto."**
>
> **Sodoku Moto is the pre-amendment name of Temür Moto, and Moto is the pre-amendment house name, replaced globally by Moto.** This sheet therefore places a Volume IV character in **direct descent from the Moto crown line**, using naming that was struck in the Cultural Amendment.
>
> **Two rulings needed.** Is the descent intended and canon? And if so, does it descend from Temür, or from the ancestral Moto line generally? *"Oliver Moto" appears in no current document under either name.*
>
> *Nothing in this sheet is built on the descent until it rules. His Wellsprings and architecture stand on their own.*

## V · Signature

### Fault Bind

**Effect** · The cut binds to the place it was made rather than to the body it was made in. **Move, and it opens again.** A man who takes one from him is not wounded so much as *tethered*, and the tether is to a patch of ground he can see.
**Cost** · Every bind is held open in his own Crystal until it resolves. **Multiple binds at once is what "metaphysical rupture" means on his sheet.**
**Limit** · It binds to location, so it fails entirely at sea, in the air, or on anything moving.
**Counter** · **Do not move.** Stand still and bleed and he has spent the technique for nothing — which requires knowing what was done to you, and it is not obvious in the moment.

# Sandalphon Aestraen · The Reverent

> **This sheet needed the least correction of any migrated so far.** The legacy card is in the modern numbered format, carries Accord legal status, narrative function and a real vulnerability list. **Nothing structural was wrong.** What follows adds the Wellspring, Stage and stat layer it did not have.
> **Lineage flag.** This sheet identifies Sandalphon as **Saeloria's consort. Karo Venrik is not his son: Isaac ruled on 2026-09-12 that Karo Venrik is the son of Hiromi Mahuo and Saeloria, the Elven Queen.** Combined with House Mourcrix descending from House Venrik, that puts **three migrated Volume IV characters in one family** — Sandalphon, his son Karo, and their kinsman Vaelen — with Sanctum Lux and the Eressean Crown on both sides of it. **Confirm this is intended before anything is built on it.**

## I · Identity

**Name** · Sandalphon Aestraen. **Called** · The Reverent Disquiet. Echo of the Ninefold Pulse. Quiet-Hand. **Half-Sung.**
**Race** · High Elf, Eressean. **Former Crown-Consort and Regent of Eresse**, pre-Ascension.
**Standing** · Exiled per the Pact of Iridescent Mourning. Pilgrim-Writ travel. **Barred from Neutral Chamber officiation; escorted-archives only.**
**Catalyst Event · Stage X, Realization.** Terrain began keeping what he sang. *The Sixteen Stages describes the lived experience as a loneliness with no analogue lower on the ladder, best put in terms of weather: you are no longer standing in it, you are part of what other people are standing in.* **For a man under a Pact of self-erasure, that was the precise opposite of what he was trying to achieve.**

## II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class IV · Luminous.** The lucid Shell, which thins. Wellsprings respond with minimal prompting and **Aether behaviour mirrors the bearer's moral alignment with unnerving precision.** *A lateral Class with no corresponding Crystal Tier — most often found in Intent-aligned Spirit Path souls whose Shell has clarified faster than their Core.* **That is Sandalphon exactly.**
**Crystal State** · Refined. **η** · 0.75–0.80, Band A.
**The braid-seals** · Teal ribbons, each binding an Echo. **Sever them and memory bleeds.**

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Mirithane** · Limina. Specular reflection — **a true mirror adds nothing, and fidelity is defined entirely by the absence of introduced distortion.** *Failure: the mirror does not discriminate. A practitioner in a state they would rather not broadcast broadcasts it perfectly.* **This is why he is called Half-Sung and why his presence mis-tunes wards.**
**Nihiloth** · Limina. Destructive interference. **A Nihiloth Domain does not impose law. It cancels the law already present** — which is the whole of his counter-Domain specialism, stated mechanically.
**Anamnesis** · Limina. The Echoes, and the braid.
**Oblivara** · Limina. Error-correcting redundancy — **identity redundantly encoded against erasure.** *Failure: code exhaustion. Redundancy has a correction limit, and past it the reconstruction is confident, complete, and wrong.*
> **Four Limina harmonisations. Same-Family compounding, and the densest concentration in the migration so far.** Where Sinclair used Limina to take, Sandalphon uses it to *hold and to cancel* — the same Family answering two opposite temperaments.

## V · Signature

### The Ninefold Listening

**Effect** · He stops singing and the room does the rest. A Domain inside his attention finds its law **cancelled rather than opposed** — nothing pushes back, and the practitioner holding it discovers their working is intact, correctly formed, and no longer describing anything.
**Cost** · **The cancellation is symmetric.** Everything he brought inside is subject to the same null, including the braid.
**Limit** · One law at a time. **Massed liturgy presents more than one and overloads him.**
**Counter** · Volume of voices. *Or Mirithane's own failure — put him in a state he would rather not broadcast and he broadcasts it perfectly.*
> **What nobody knows.** How many ribbons there were at the start.
> **Codex** · Limina / Abstract–Entropic / Mirithane primary / Stage X.

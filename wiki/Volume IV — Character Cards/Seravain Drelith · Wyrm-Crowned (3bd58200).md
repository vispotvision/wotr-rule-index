# Seravain Drelith · Wyrm-Crowned

> **Modern format, minimal correction.** The legacy card carries Accord legal status, a real vulnerability list and a coherent moral arc. **Adding the Wellspring, Stage and stat layer only.**

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Seravain Drelith. **Called** · The Ivory Coil. Wyrm-Crowned. **Marchlord of Glass.**
**Race** · Dragonkin, Trueblood, Celestial Wyrmline.
**Standing** · Outside the Accord. Listed under **Concord Sanctions, Marchlord Exception.** Commands sworn marcher clans; shelters exiled Accord tacticians.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage X, Realization.** *Terrain changes. Pacts hold in the ground. Artifacts made at Realization do not need maintaining, and regions where a Stage X practitioner spent time keep a set that outlasts them by generations.*
>
> **The Glass Marches are not a territory he conquered. They are a territory that took his set**, and the crystal is not a technique he uses on ground so much as what ground does near him now.

## V · Signature

### Formation Law

**Effect** · He does not command a line, he *tunes* it. Ranks within his Radius stop acting individually — a wheel executes as one motion, a gap closes before the man beside it registers the gap — and terrain along the frontage crystallises into geometry that favours the shape he has chosen. **Encirclements close without a charge.**
**Cost** · Held continuously and paid continuously. *A day of it and the crests run hot enough to see.*
**Limit** · **He needs formed ranks in proximity. Alone he is a very strong Dragonkin with no multiplier at all**, and the drop is more than a Grade.
**Counter** · **Break the formation, not the man.** Skirmishers, dispersal, refusal of the set-piece. *He has contempt for improvisational warfare, and the contempt is the tell.*
> **What nobody knows.** What the Marches were before they were glass.
> **Codex** · Limina / Abstract–Entropic / Mirithane primary / Stage X.

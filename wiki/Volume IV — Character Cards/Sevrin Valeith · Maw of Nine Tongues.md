# Sevrin Valeith · Maw of Nine Tongues

> *"The dead do not lie, only the living do. That's why I keep their tongues."*
> **Invented Stage name.** The card reads **"Stage VII (Pinnacle)."** *Pinnacle is not among the sixteen; Stage VII is Refraction.* Numeral stands.
>
> *"Silence / Bone / Will"* were not Essence types.
> **Fifth link into the Venrik cluster.** *"Karo Venrik — former Accord colleague, now Sevrin's reluctant informant."*
>
> **A rogue spirit-binder pursued for Soul Concord violations is running a source inside the Accord, and the source is the Eressean prince.** *Karo's own sheet gives him no leverage to refuse — "reluctant" is doing a great deal of work here, and whatever Sevrin holds over him is not on either card.*

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Sevrin Valeith. **Called** · **The Pallid Binder.** Whisper-Keeper · **Maw of Nine Tongues.**
**Role** · **Spirit Binder. Necrotic Medium. Walker of the Pale Wellspring.**

### Affiliation

**Formerly Arbitration Division, now rogue.** *Pursued by the Accord for Soul Concord violations after binding sovereign spirits into private service.* Operates within the Outer World's frost-barren calderas, trading in forbidden conjurations.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VII, Refraction.** *The soul sees itself from outside — and looking at it changes it, and knowing that it changes it changes it again.*
>
> **The Nine-Tongued Maw lets him speak with up to nine spirits simultaneously, weaving their power into his own** — *at the cost of gradual psychic erosion.*
>
> ***A man at the Stage where a soul first examines itself, holding nine other souls inside the examination.*** **The erosion is not a side effect. It is what happens when a Crystal is asked to be nine things and remain one.**

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Eidolyn** · Limina. **Governing.** *Templated nucleation — a supersaturated medium given a template deposits along that geometry with high fidelity and real strength, and intent is the template.* **The bound echoes are genuinely solid because his intent held the shape.**
> *Failure: template defect. Any flaw in the intent is faithfully reproduced at scale, and the practitioner rarely sees the flaw until it is standing in front of them.* **He has nine.**
**Mortalis** · Vitalia. **Governs passage** — *and its doctrine convicts him outright: the dead may leave traces. The trace is not the soul. Any practitioner who refuses that distinction after demonstration is placed under Conviction review immediately.*
> ***The Accord is not pursuing Sevrin for necromancy. They are pursuing him for a category error they have a formal procedure for.***
**Nihiloth** · Limina. **Destructive interference.** The Silence, the resonance dampening.
**Cinerion** · Caloria. The bone and the ash.

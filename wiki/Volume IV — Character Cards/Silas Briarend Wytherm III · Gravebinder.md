# Silas Briarend Wytherm III · Gravebinder

> **Corrected.** The legacy "Path of Ritual Deathbinding" listed **Putrefactio (II), Cibation (IX), Dissolution (I), Calcination (XII)** with roman numerals implying an ordered sequence. **Dissolution and Calcination are Wellsprings. Putrefactio and Cibation are not** — they are Great Work operations, and the numbering does not correspond to anything in the register. **"Horoscopic Signature: Sh (Reflection)"** has been carried as a glyph reference pending check against the Master Glyph Index.

## I · Identity

**Name** · Silas Briarend Wytherm III. **Called** · Gravebinder of the Hollow Book. Warden of Rites Forgotten.
**Standing** · Guild Accord, Sect of the Hollow Book. Silent Circle of the Eastern Barrowwrights.
**Catalyst Event · Stage VII, Refraction.** Self-examination at depth revealed the fracture record, **and everything Splintering had promoted to a load path became visible, labelled, and impossible to un-see.** For a man whose discipline is reading the dead, being handed the ability to read himself was not a gift.

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Anamnesis** · Limina. **Primary.** Objects genuinely store their history in physically readable form. *Failure: thermal resetting — reading is not always passive, and a careless practitioner erases the record in the act of taking it.* **Silas has done this to a barrow and does not discuss it.**
**Mortalis** · Vitalia. Governs passage. *The Heresiology is emphatic: the dead may leave traces, and the trace is not the soul. Any practitioner who refuses this distinction after demonstration goes under Conviction review.* **Silas accepts it, publicly, and the Sect does not.**
**Dissolution** · Limina. Unravels cursed forms and soul-locked objects, and **runs on its own once initiated** because the unbound state is thermodynamically preferred.
**Lethegraal** · Limina. Shortens the relaxation time. **This is the mercy in his practice** — he does not release the lingering dead, he shortens how long they take to settle.

## V · Signature

### Silent Bloom · Rite of Burning Memory

**Effect** · He reads a decaying thing down to the record it is carrying, and then burns the record rather than the thing. **What is left is inert, and clean, and no longer anybody.** The grief goes out of a barrow like heat out of a stone.
**Cost** · **He speaks in voices not his own throughout, and the voices do not always stop at the end of the rite.**
**Limit** · It is irreversible and he knows it in advance. *A record burned is a record nobody will ever read, including the family who asked for the burning.*
**Counter** · A record he has not correctly identified. **Anamnesis failure is thermal resetting, and a rite aimed at the wrong layer erases the wrong thing.**
> **What nobody knows.** What the Hollow Book is a copy of.
> **Codex** · Limina / Abstract–Entropic / Anamnesis primary / Stage VII.

# Thalyren Veyroot · The Verdant Sentinel

> **Invented Stage name.** The card reads **"Temperance Stage XI (Archonic Binding)."** *Archonic Binding is not among the sixteen; Stage XI is Dissonance.* Numeral stands.
>
> **And Lurien again** — *the Sacred Conclave of Lurien*, third instance in this batch. *"Verdure / Earthen / Spirit"* were not Essence types.

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Thalyren Veyroot. **Called** · **The Verdant Sentinel.** Green-Warden of the Ninefold Grove · **Rootkeeper.**
**Role** · **Ancient Wellspring Guardian. Grove Sovereign. Verdure Archon's Chosen.**

### Affiliation

**Sacred Conclave** — **bound protector of the Everbloom Wellspring**, *a T9 Verdure Wellspring considered one of the most stable and sacred in the Core World.* **Holds spiritual authority over all druids, beastfolk and nature-attuned Concord members within its territory.**
> **Catalyst Event · Stage XI, Dissonance.** *The practitioner walks toward something that can end them, arrives, and remains coherent long enough to choose how they meet it. Not survives. Not defeats. Remains coherent.*
>
> **Thalyren's answer was to root.** *Root-Bound Soul makes him functionally immortal inside the Everbloom's domain — any physical destruction regrows him from the Wellspring's roots over several days.*
>
> ***He met the thing that could end him and chose never to leave the place where ending does not apply.*** **That is a coherent choice and it is also a cage, and the sheet does not distinguish.**

# The Ossuary Choir

> *"We are the last song you will ever hear. And we will play you hollow."*
> **Same Archon error as the rest of the Bloom.** *Dissolution and Calcination are genuine Wellsprings; "Harmonic" is Harmonia, a Category, not a current, and none of the three is an Archon.*
>
> **And a structural note:** *this is a collective entity — three siblings fused into one ossified frame. The stat sheet below is the frame, not any one sibling, and there is no mechanism in Fracture of Worlds for a shared Soul Crystal across three donors. Flagged as an open ruling.*

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Dissolution** · Limina. *The unbound state is thermodynamically preferred; the process runs on its own once initiated.* **Matter and spirit erosion.**
**Calcination** · Caloria. **Burns away false structure** — *refining decayed bone into pure boneglass.* **The only Wellspring in the sixty whose function is to remove what should not be there and leave what should.**
**Nihiloth** · Limina. *Destructive interference — two waves in antiphase sum to zero, and the region between carries no energy at all.*
> **This is Choir of Unmaking, and it corrects the card's framing.** *They do not project decay into a battlefield. They generate the antiphase of everything holding it together, and what buckles is not attacked — it is cancelled.*
**Mortalis** · Vitalia. The bone, and what it was.

## VII · Signature

### The Song that Shatters

**Effect** · Three voices, three decays — **flesh, will, faith** — and the frequencies find what is holding a Domain together. *Glyph chains unravel in sequence. Wards do not resist; they stop being in phase with themselves.*
**Cost** · **Nihiloth cancels symmetrically.** *Everything inside the radius is subject to the same antiphase, and they are inside it permanently.*
**Limit** · **They must be emplaced, and they must be played.** *Dexterity 146.*
**Counter** · **Reach them.** *Or bring silence — which is Graelith's function, and the Bloom does not appear to have noticed that its Third and Fourth Generals cancel each other.*
> **What nobody knows.** Whether three siblings still exist inside it, or one thing that remembers being three.

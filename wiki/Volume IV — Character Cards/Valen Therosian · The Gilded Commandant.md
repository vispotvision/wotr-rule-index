# Valen Therosian · The Gilded Commandant

> *"We do not preserve crowns by bowing to the cries of the broken. We preserve them by reminding the world why they still wear gold."*
> **DEAD CANON.**
>
> **The Praetorian March was struck from canon and archived**, alongside Nevara and Kaetra. Valen is *High Admiral of the Praetorian March*, serving the **Empire of Praetoria** through its **Naval High Command** and **Council of Sovereigns.** None of it exists.
>
> **Separately, ruled 2026-09-12: High Admiral stands.** The Trello card title named a different office. That was a mislabel, not a second career, and the fleet commander the body describes is the character. *One office. The sheet never had two men in it, only two labels.*
>
> **Held pending ruling.** All legacy content preserved below.

**Name** · Valen Therosian. **Called** · The Gilded Commandant.
**Role** · High Admiral of the Praetorian March. **Imperial Arbiter of Crown Preservation.**

### Affiliation

Empire of Praetoria — Naval High Command — Council of Sovereigns — **Loyalist Circle of the Crown-Forged.**
> *His Aether pulses with refined martial resolve, burnished authority, and the cold logic of control. His soul emits a rigid, honed aura that suppresses lesser wills and demands order.*
> **Catalyst Event · Stage X, Realization.** *Terrain changes. Pacts hold in the ground. Artifacts made at Realization do not need maintaining, and regions where a Stage X practitioner spent time keep a set that outlasts them by generations.*

> **For a man whose office is Crown Preservation, that is the whole job description arriving as a metaphysical fact.** *He does not defend the Crown. The ground he has stood on has begun defending it for him, and it will continue after he is gone — which is the only immortality the Loyalist Circle has ever actually been offered.*

## VIII · Signature

### The Executioner's Voice

**Effect** · He speaks before an assembly and the assembly's own doubts become the weapon. **Those whose allegiance has already faltered find their workings will not answer them**, and the faltering is visible to everyone present, which is the point.
**Cost** · **He cannot use it on the certain.** Every deployment narrows the pool of people willing to stand near him uncertain, and he has been narrowing it for decades.
**Limit** · Five or more, and they must be listening.
**Counter** · **Believe something.** *Or be one person, alone, in a room, where Harmonics 318 and Gnosis 352 are the only stats that matter and both are his floors.*

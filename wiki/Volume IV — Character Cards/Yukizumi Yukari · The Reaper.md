# Yukizumi Yukari · The Reaper

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Yukizumi Yukari. **Title** · **The Reaper.** **Lineage** · current-generation **Yukari** psychopomp.
**Role** · **Accord-appointed psychopomp–adjudicator.** *The one who walks battlefields and councils to escort what must end and protect what still belongs to the living.* **When a death is disputed — by Rot, by brandcraft, by stubborn love — Yukari is the last vote.**

### Affiliation

**Arbitration Division**, special charter under **Vocatia (Pale Pact).** *Consults for the Research & Archives Division on soul-law breaches — Mechanica, brandcraft, Rot resurrections.*

---

## II · Appearance & Emblems

**Frost-pale hair spilling like winter river.** Night-blue mantle **stitched with starfields and the four moon-circles**; inner hem reads **old funeral script.** Carries a **crescent staff-scythe crowned by a faceted star — lantern, gavel, and blade in one.** *Eyes calm as midnight glass; voice soft enough to make people lower theirs.*

## III · Soul Architecture

**Configuration** · **Dream / Harmonia / Mnemata bias.** *Projects steadiness rather than volume.* **His presence quiets panic and makes lies stumble.**
**Aether Shell** · **Class IV · Luminous.** *The lucid Shell, which thins — Aether behaviour mirrors the bearer's moral alignment with unnerving precision.* For an adjudicator that is not a metaphor; it is a qualification.

## IX · Techniques

**Quiet Harvest** · **He sets the star crown against a person's shadow.** *Breath eases, fingers unclench, and the tether between body and soul loosens like a knot learning how to be undone.*
**Borrowed Dusk** · **He swings the crescent through empty air; light falls a shade and voices forget to shout.** *Decisions land without frenzy.*
**Echo Toll** · **A knuckle taps the star.** *An echo of the deceased steps close enough to answer three fair questions — but only in truths they held while breathing.*
**Moon-Bridge Step** · **He moves from one lantern's edge to another's**, crossing a room as if the dark were a footpath.
**Thread of Return** · **With the back of the blade he catches a fleeing fragment of a soul and walks it — gently, sternly — back into a chest that still deserves to live.**

## X · Duties & Law

**Adjudicate contested deaths.** *If Rot, Mechanica or brandcraft keeps a soul where it does not belong, he severs it. If a death was stolen before its proper weight, he returns the person to finish their measure.*
**Witness oaths.** *He binds promises with coins and moon-ink.* **Broken oaths call him without messenger.**
**Guard the boundary.** *Domains that trap the dead, or living casters who hoard more than their measure.*
> **His remit names Mechanica directly — and Volume IV contains two Mechanica sheets.** *Nethoriel Vehlorim, whose Pale Retort replaces his Soul Crystal, and Wilhelm, whose artificial Verdiction Core the Guild Accord itself commissioned.*
>
> ***Yukari is the Accord officer chartered to sever exactly what the Accord built.*** **That is a scene waiting to be written.**

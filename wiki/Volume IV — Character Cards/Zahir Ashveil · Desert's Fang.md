# Zahir Ashveil · Desert's Fang

> *"The desert teaches you one lesson — burn fast, or be buried slow."*
> **Invented Stage name.** The card reads **"Stage III — Pursuit."** *Pursuit is not among the sixteen; Stage III is Ascension.* Numeral stands.
>
> *"Fire / Earth / Spirit"* were not Essence types.

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Zahir Ashveil. **Aliases** · **Emberstride.** The Sandflame · **Desert's Fang.**
**Role** · **Independent Mercenary. Desert Wellspring Raider. Pyrokinetic Duelist.**
**Affiliation** · **None.** *Formerly with the Sunwatch Caravan Guard; now a contracted blade-for-hire across Innerworld and Outer World borders.*
> **Catalyst Event · Stage III, Ascension.** ***The first thing you made yourself, working.***
>
> *Everything before was received — somebody showed you and you copied it and it held, and it was never yours.* **Scorchbrand is his: a burn that stays in a wound and reignites when he decides.** *That is not a caravan-guard technique. It is a thing he worked out alone, and it is why he stopped being a guard.*

## VII · Signature

### The Crimson Mirage

**Effect** · **Burns that stay in the wound and answer him afterwards.** *A fight against Zahir does not end when he withdraws.*
**Cost** · **Tempering 80.** *He cannot sustain, cannot regulate, and cannot afford a second round.*

**Counter** · **Outlast him.** *Or bring cold — which is precisely why Kaerith Vireth keeps winning.*

# Aurelian Veymont · The Lion of the Dawn

## Aurelian Veymont · The Lion of the Dawn

> *"A kingdom does not fall while I breathe."*
> **Migrated with all content preserved, including all three relics and the four Core Disciplines.** *"Aether Class: Sovereign Emitter, Dominion-Dominant Field Controller"* is not among the seven; **he is Class III, Resonant** (Stage VII is the Resonant Crystal's own Stage). *"Global Tier: High A Tier"* is struck and replaced with Grade A pushing toward S, his Stage's own ceiling language. *"Path Bias: Body 40% / Attraction 35% / Spirit 25%"* used percentages the Four Paths don't carry; **all three are real, current Paths, Fate simply absent, so the bias survives as Body dominant, Attraction secondary.** *"Strike Force 2.4 PJ," "Durability 2.0 PJ," "Speed Mach 8 sustained"* were given against no stated Grade table and read, converted, as the top of his Stage's own Strike Force band rather than the SSS-Grade the raw joule figure would otherwise suggest; **Stage VII's own ceiling of 475 caps him at A-Grade with a late push toward S, and that is what the sheet below uses.** His Wellsprings (Aurevane, Judicium, Ascensio, Fixatio, Coagulatio) are all genuine and unaltered; **no Veyra invocation appears on this card**, unlike three of the four in this batch. **"Withering Age" is corrected to the Withering Era** per the Guild's Errata. The Ember Crown Domain is retained as exactly what Stage VII permits: a first Domain seed, fragile, collapsing if his attention fractures; this is the one card in the batch where the source's own "Proto-Domain state" needed no correction at all. **The Ember Crown Dominion (as a polity) and "the First Empire" are unattested in current canon.** A Wellspring named Pyraeon already carries the byname *the Ember Crown*, which the card's own faction name was never going to know about; **kept because Aurelian's whole identity depends on them, flagged rather than struck.**

> **The coronation trials did what battlefield victory alone had never managed.** *A crown an army accepts is a vote. A crown the ground itself starts answering to is a Threshold.* **The Ember Crown's first flicker came the hour his banners rose before any order to raise them had left his mouth**, and he understood, standing inside it, that the field had stopped waiting for his command before it obeyed him.

### VIII · Signature

#### Golden Edict

**Effect** · A single spoken command restricts hostile movement across the field for as long as he sustains it; not paralysis, a compulsion that makes disobedience feel structurally wrong rather than merely unwise. *The Ember Crown Domain seed carries the compulsion the way a banner carries a wind.*
**Cost** · *18,000 EU, workbook figure; the single most expensive Discipline he carries.* **Sovereign Burn**: sustained output above what the seed Domain can carry damages his own body before he will concede ground, and Golden Edict is the Discipline most likely to trigger it.
**Limit** · **The Domain seed collapses if his concentration fractures**, per Stage VII's own gate; a genuine surprise, a wound, or a second working demanding his full attention ends the Edict early.
**Counter** · **Break his concentration rather than his command.** Since the Edict runs on a fragile Stage VII seed rather than a stabilized Domain, anything that forces him to split attention (a threat to someone he will not let fall, a second front) ends the compulsion faster than resisting it directly ever will.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the Ember Crown answered his coronation, or whether he simply arrived already carrying a claim it had no reason to refuse.

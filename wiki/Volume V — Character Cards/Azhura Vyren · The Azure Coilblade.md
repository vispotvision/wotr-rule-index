### VIII · Signature

#### Coilbreath — Azure Exhalation

**Effect** · A directed blast of cryowind through the Sky-Lung Core condenses ambient moisture into slicing frost-streams, delivered at range rather than at the blade's edge.
**Cost** · The chamber overheats on discharge and then freezes internally if pushed past its working cycle; Cryowind Fatigue is a real recovery cost, not a flourish.
**Limit** · Needs a genuine pressure differential to condense against. A thermo-Aether field flattens that differential, and the breath comes out as warm air and nothing else.
**Counter** · Force the fight into a heat-saturated space. A destabilized cryowind balance buys several seconds of a Coilbreath that will not condense, and several seconds is enough.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the serpentine mutation in her bloodline is a dragon ancestor or a Wellspring that got there first.

# Cyranthia Valez · The Gilded Marionette

## Cyranthia Valez · The Gilded Marionette

> *"Power is not force. It is agreement shaped correctly."*
> **Migrated with all content preserved, including all three relics and both Core Disciplines retained as Wellspring workings.** *"Aether Class: Influence Weaver, Attraction-Dominant Manipulator"* is not among the seven; **she is Class II, Harmonic** (Stage VI sits in the Harmonic Crystal's Bands E-D). *"Global Tier: High B Tier"* and *"Attraction Scale: A Tier Social Influence Field"* conflate old freeform grading with the Tier Grade ladder; **both are struck and replaced by Grade A (Stage VI's own ceiling) and a plain account of how far her Attraction Layer has actually developed.** *"Path Bias: Attraction 60% / Spirit 25% / Body 15%"* used percentages the Four Paths don't carry; **the three named Paths (Attraction, Spirit, Body) are real and current; Fate is simply absent; so the bias survives as Attraction dominant, Spirit secondary.** Her Triune Core listed **Veyra** alongside three genuine Wellsprings; **Veyra is the Eighth Archon, Desire, not a Wellspring at all**, so it is struck from her Wellspring list and kept only as the Archonic patronage the card's own voice already implied. **"Withering Age" is corrected to the Withering Era** per the Guild's Errata. Her Salon is downgraded from a stated "Proto-Domain state" to a conditioned space: **Domain seed activation is gated to Stage VII**, one Stage above her, and the card's own trajectory line already reads this correctly as a thing still approaching. **Targeted Uninfected: 54** has no Fracture of Worlds correlate; kept below as a plain fact about the size of her standing network. Level and stat spread are conservative choices within Stage VI, Band II, stated below. **Sable Rose Consortium, the Rose Courts, the Ivory Ledger Society, the First Empire and the Ember Crown Dominion do not appear elsewhere on this board's cards; unattested in current canon, kept only because the sheets that use them cannot make sense without them.**

### I · Identity

**Name** · Cyranthia Valez. **Called** · The Gilded Marionette.
**Also known as** · Lady of the Quiet Thread · The Velvet Sovereign. **Epithet** · *She Who Pulls Without Strings.*
**Role** · **Political broker. Court strategist. Attraction-Path manipulator working through rooms rather than through people one at a time.**
**Gloss rights** · Never. *She has never once explained what she does to anyone who was not already inside the arrangement, and the sheet does not get special treatment.*

#### Affiliation

**Race / Lineage** · Highborn human, Court-Blooded Strategist Line. **Faction** · Independent political broker, the Withering Era. *No official allegiance; every capital owes her a favour she has not yet called.*
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VI, Glory.** *Willingly accepting genuine soul-cost to preserve something valued above one's own structural integrity; the Threshold does not care what was given up, only that it outweighed staying whole.*
> 
> **She gave up the right to be believed for her own sake.** *A woman who edits nations for a living forfeits, permanently, the assumption that anything she says is not a move; Court Immunity reads her lies as emotionally truthful because everyone stopped expecting anything else from her years ago.* **Her networks are not ambition. They are the shape trust takes once you can no longer spend it directly.**

### II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class II · Harmonic.** The three layers hold a rhythm steady enough that a room can be talked into keeping time with it, though the Shell has not yet learned to conduct in silence the way a Resonant one does.
**Attraction Layer** · Extensively developed and pointed at rooms rather than at any one person. *The Salon is a claimed space (furniture, lighting, who sits where), not a Domain seed. That gate is Stage VII, one Stage above her.*
**Crystal State** · Refined. **η** · ~0.95, Coherence Band D.
**The Quiet Thread Core** · *A soul-crystal that reads, under any honest instrument, as more listening than casting; most of what leaves it left already agreed with.*

---

### III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Letheveil** · Limina, The Waters of Forgetting. **Primary, sourced.** Governs Narrative Inversion and the surgical half of Selective forgetting she performs on public memory rather than private.
**Judicium** · Fulguria, The Wellspring of Truth. **Secondary.** The irony is load-bearing: Judicium's law of truth is what Court Immunity bends against, and bending a truth-Wellspring rather than breaking it is the whole of her discipline.
**Anamnesis** · Limina, The Wellspring of Remembrance. **Network.** Feeds False Ownership; a target's memory of a decision is reconstructed, not erased, so it still feels like theirs.
**Tenebra** · Limina, The Hidden Shadow. **Network.** Signal reduction applied to intent rather than to a body: rooms she has worked read as calmer than they are.
> **The Veyra invocation.** The original card named Veyra, Desire, in the Triune Core. Veyra is an Archon, not a Wellspring; Archonic Law and Wellspring Law are different strata under the Continuum's own hierarchy. Kept only as flavour: **a Desire-Archon's patronage over a woman whose entire discipline is making people want what she already decided fits the card's own voice too well to discard, so it stands outside the Wellspring list rather than inside it.**

### VIII · Signature

#### Gentle Catastrophe

**Effect** · A faction, a marriage, a merchant house's credit collapses within days, with no threat, no accusation and no visible hand behind it. *Every domino she touched agreed, individually and in sequence, to fall.*
**Cost** · *13,200 EU, workbook figure.* **Every relationship she spends this way is one she cannot use twice**; Consensus Drift does not re-align a room that has already watched its own collapse happen once.
**Limit** · **Requires weeks of prior Salon contact with every party involved.** She cannot do this to strangers; the alignment has to already be seated before the catastrophe can be gentle.
**Counter** · **A party who never entered a room she controls is outside the working entirely.** Refuse the Salon, refuse the tea, refuse the treaty dinner, and Gentle Catastrophe has nothing to stand on.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether she has ever run this on herself, and what she would have had to be losing to need to.

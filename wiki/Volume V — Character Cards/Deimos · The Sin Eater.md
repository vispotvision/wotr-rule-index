# Deimos · The Sin Eater

> *He arrives not as savior, but as consequence walking.*
> **Invented Stage name.** The card reads **"Stage IX — Reflection."** *Reflection is not among the sixteen; Stage IX is Invocation.* Numeral stands.
>
> **"Path: Body / Spirit / Judgment"** — *there are four Paths: Body, Spirit, Attraction, Fate. Judgment is not one.* **"Aether Class: Contrition Engine"** is not among the eight; **he is Class VI, Voidic** — *the inverted Shell, which devours, remembers and reflects, and no sheet has ever fitted that description more literally.*
>
> **"Penanceflare"** is not among the sixty. **Judicium and Mortalis are.** *Re-sourced below.* **Purganeth** appears here as a realm and appeared on Valen Therosian's card as an invented Stage name — *worth ruling which it is.*

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Deimos. **Called** · **The Sin Eater.**
**Type** · **Aberrant Mortal / Essence Absorber.** **Realm of Operation** · *Purganeth · Outer Realms Transit.*

### Profile

**A living embodiment of judgment — the rare mortal conduit who consumes guilt, oath-breach and moral residue as sustenance.** *His existence fulfils an unspoken clause of the Severance Laws: to remove spiritual excess without breaking divine equilibrium.* Where the Enforcement Division punishes, Deimos redeems through absorption, drawing crimes into his own lattice and converting their resonance into destructive retribution.
*He moves through cities like an unmarked trial — feared by the corrupt, tolerated by the lawful, and pitied by the penitent.* **Arbitration officers deploy him when no verdict will suffice, when truth itself festers.**

### Division Classification

**Guild Accord — Arbitration Division.** *Status:* **Unbadged Operative, Black Concord Auxiliary.** *Clearance:* **Tier II Provisional, Law of Severance Enforcement.** *Role:* **Sanctioned Absolution Agent / Guilt Extraction Specialist.**
> **Catalyst Event · Stage IX, Invocation.** *From Invocation the practitioner stops working alone. Something larger answers, and the answering is not metaphor, is not reliable, and is not free.*
>
> **The card is explicit that his ascension "did not arise from faith or remorse, but endurance — a willingness to bear what others refused."**
>
> ***He did not ask. He agreed to hold something, and it kept arriving.*** *The recorded pattern at Invocation is relief, then something worse — and Deimos exists in continual repentance, knowing every absolution claimed must also be paid for in pain.*

## II · Appearance

**The right half of his body bears a lacquer-black stain** — *a creeping burn of condensed guilt that has long surpassed skin.* **From beneath his collar the flesh glimmers as though polished obsidian, crawling to the fingertips in fractal scars.** *A curved horn coils at his temple, its spiral filled with ash-dust sigils.*
**He wears a long sash of vellum seals, each painted in red tally marks — one for every transgression devoured.**
*His weapon, the Gloamblade Absolver, drinks lamplight and exhales smoke the colour of dusk.* **When drawn, the air tastes of iron and remorse.**

---

## III · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class VI · Voidic.** *Instead of emitting, the Shell devours, remembers and reflects.* His Ardency does not deal damage. It removes things — which is Maw of Blame stated in the Class system's own terms.
**Crystal State** · **Overgrown.** *The more sin he carries, the greater his potential — and the slower his heart.*
**η** · 0.75–0.80, Band A.

## IV · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Judicium** · Fulguria. **Governing.** Order, measure, consequence. *And its named injury is his entire condition: law without mercy becomes burnished sadism, and Judicium damage shows most clearly where either side is used to excuse the other's absence.*
> **"He cannot forgive — only balance" is that injury, worn as an office.**
**Mortalis** · Vitalia. **Venous black-red. Governs passage** — *dissolution, threshold, completion, the lawful withdrawal of presence.*
**Catharsis** · Caloria. **Pale rose-gold. Release and true clearing** — *replacing the card's "Penanceflare."*
> ***And its doctrine is the flaw in his whole practice:*** *nothing is purified by being merely emptied. If what is removed has not been rightly transformed, it returns elsewhere and more venomously.* Deimos does not transform what he takes. He stores it in a sash.
**Anamnesis** · Limina. **Stain-Sense** — *guilt perceived as visible resonance, because the record is physically readable.*

## VII · Relics & Implements

**Gloamblade Absolver** · **A broad, dusk-purple sword that cuts falsehood before flesh.** *Those who lie near it feel their own tongues weigh down.*
**Prayer Knots** · **Four cords tied to the penitent's wrist.** *Confession uttered through them bypasses fear; only truth carries through.*
**Ash Tokens** · **Small discs placed on the tongue of the accused.** *If it burns, guilt is genuine. If not, Deimos walks away.*
**Tally-Sash of Red Seals** · **Each seal contains a devoured sin.** *By tearing one, he releases that guilt as thermal or gravitational force* — **the Law of Equivalence embodied.**

## VIII · Abilities & Techniques

**Maw of Blame** · **The black arm opens along ancient scars.** *When his hand closes around a chest or throat, guilt is inhaled like air, absorbed into his own Essence lattice.*
**Stain-Sense** · **He perceives guilt as visible resonance** — *shadows under ribs, flickers in speech, pulses beneath banners.* **Crowds cannot lie to him; they simply dim.**
**Take & Hold** · **A confessional extraction.** *The sinner exhales their wrong; Deimos inhales it. The world feels lighter.*
**Backwash Confession** · **A swing of his blade forces vocal confession from the guilty** — truth expelled as breath.
**Debt Rend** · **Tearing a tally-seal embeds guilt into material form.** *Walls, banners or engines crack under the emotional gravity of what they have "remembered."*
**Hollow Bell** · **Two knuckle taps on the sword's spine release a subsonic hum that unbinds mob cohesion**; chants die into silence.
**Final Bite** · **Seizes a monster's shadow and devours the harm it has caused.** *The creature shrinks to the measure of its remaining sins.*

---

## IX · Costs & Limitations

**Each consumed sin deepens corruption of the right side**; *nerves deaden, dreams grow colder.*
**Starvation of guilt renders him erratic and volatile; oversaturation induces paralysis and emotional dissociation.**
**Cannot consume innocence, justified defence, or child-borne guilt** — *these scald his Essence.*
**Requires confession to legitimise consumption**; *without it, seals fail to open.*

# Draen Varos · The Red Forge Sentinel

## Draen Varos · The Red Forge Sentinel

> *"Heat is memory. Every blade remembers the hand that forged it."*

---

### I · Identity

**Name** · Draen Varos. **Called** · The Bastion of Cinders · The Iron Shepherd · The Red Forge Sentinel.
**Role** · **Battle-Smith. Siege-Line Defensive Operative. Body Path Instructor.**
**Gloss rights** · Unlimited. *His names are what the line calls him; none of them hide a meaning he'd refuse to give.*

#### Affiliation

**Guild Accord — Vanguard Bastion Unit.** A blacksmith conscript out of the smelting districts of Eresse who rose through attrition rather than ambition. **Now instructs Body Path initiates and oversees field armory at a Deep Mantle Bastion.**
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VII, Refraction.** *The soul sees itself from outside at this depth, and the fracture record becomes something you cannot un-read.* Draen's came from a weapon that failed him. **The Axe of Tempered Remorse started as a mistake that got a soldier killed, and he kept forging on it anyway, until the guilt itself became the temper.** *He fights now to prove creation and destruction are the same prayer done with purpose, and the axe is the argument.*

### II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class III · Resonant.** Essence and Aether exchange freely; nothing about him runs hot except on purpose.
**Attraction Layer** · Bound to the axe and to the men he stands in front of, not to a person. *Bastion Pulse is what that bond looks like from outside.*
**Crystal State** · Forge-Heart, steady burn. **η** · ~0.61, Band C.
**The Forge-Heart Crystal** · Alloyed with tempered slag from the Atlas Mantle, nine kilograms of Crystal and slag that reads as one structure. Flux Density **670 million EU/g.** Aether Output **98,000 AU/s.**

### VIII · Signature

#### Forgebreaker Form

**Effect** · Armor integrates with the axe; every swing after that emits a controlled shockwave.
**Cost** · **Heat saturation drives him toward Essence overburn.** He has to vent through a grounding strike or risk cracking the Lattice outright.
**Limit** · **Nine seconds, sustained.** Past that the vent becomes mandatory rather than optional.
**Counter** · **Deny him the grounding strike.** Keep him moving, keep the ground unstable under it, and the Form either ends early or cracks him.
> **What nobody knows.** Why despair specifically dulls the axe's edge rather than simply weakening it. Nobody, including Draen, has found the mechanism under that particular sympathy.

# Garu Luneward · Winter Hound of the March

## Garu Luneward · Winter Hound of the March

> *"Tracks can lie. The wind cannot. Just listen."*

---

### I · Identity

**Name** · Garu Luneward. **Called** · Winter Hound of the March. **Also known as** · Snow Scout, Blue Cloak Tracker, Half-Wolf Without a Tail. **Epithet** · He Who Hears Footsteps in the Wind.
**Race** · Human / Lupine (Wolfkin) hybrid, Old World northern strain. Tail-less, and without the Pack-Soul most Lupine share past infancy.
**Role** · **Hunter's Guild forward scout.** **Gloss rights** · Diagnostic only; he answers questions about his methods with the fewest words that will close the conversation.

#### Affiliation

Hunter's Guild forward scout, working the frontier march for the Caravan Families. He holds no pack and answers to no house; the caravans he walks beside are the closest thing he has to either.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage IV, Flourishing.** *The Threshold this Stage asks for is the formation of a first genuine Spirit Axis bond.* Garu's did not form with a person. It formed with the act of tracking itself, the moment he stopped treating a trail as information and started treating it as a promise someone else's fear had made him.
> 
> **Born without a tail, and without the communal Pack-Soul architecture Lupine are built around.** A Wolfkin raised outside that shared lattice reads to his own kind as permanently, quietly wrong. He has made a discipline out of the thing that isolated him.

**Aether Shell** · **Class I · Muridic**, at the efficient edge of what the Class allows. **Attraction Layer** · Undeveloped and deliberately so. Packless Resolve is partly a Trait and partly a refusal: he has not let anything bond deep enough to read as an Axis.
**Crystal State** · Awakened. **η** · ~0.70, Band F (corrected down from the source's 0.92; see migration note).
**The isolated part of the Core** · A Lupine lattice built for a pack, running alone. What other Wolfkin lose to Isolation Decay, Garu has converted into a hunting method.

### VIII · Signature

#### Crescent Fang Rush

**Effect** · Garu condenses gathered echoes into his legs and snaps forward along a curved, non-linear line rather than a straight one; to the target he simply arrives at their flank, already inside the strike.
**Cost** · Burns the echo-charge he's spent the fight gathering; used more than once against the same emotionally loud target, it starts pulling their fear into his own nerves instead of just reading it.
**Limit** · It needs a trail to chain off. Constructs, the truly dead, and anything that leaves no emotional residue give him nothing to bend the line around, and the rush goes straight instead of curved.
**Counter** · Flood the field with false fear. An illusionist who understands his empathy-tracking can bait the curve toward an empty point and let him arrive somewhere with nothing in it.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether Sensory Drowning is a hazard of the technique, or the shape his missing Pack-Soul takes when the only feelings left to borrow are a stranger's.

# Kaien Thal'Ruhm · The Gilded Nomad

## Kaien Thal'Ruhm · The Gilded Nomad

> *"The heart is a furnace. You just have to decide what you're willing to burn."*

---

### I · Identity

**Name** · Kaien Thal'Ruhm. **Called** · The Gilded Nomad.
**Role** · **Wandering Alchemist. Aether Arm Engineer. Exile-Artisan.**
**Gloss rights** · Diagnostic only. He will explain how a thing works, in the plain language of a man who built it, and stop there.

#### Affiliation

Freelance — **former Guild Accord artificer, Alchemetrica Division.** Nomadic researcher operating under no Accord jurisdiction, **Keeper of the Bird Mask.**
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VII, Refraction.** *The Catharsis Reactor implosion at Stage V left half his body gold-fused alloy; reaching Refraction meant reforging that fusion through Transmutatio ritual on purpose, this time, rather than surviving it by accident.* **His nerves now hum with a dual harmonic — human resonance and mechanical recursion — detectable by any Aether scanner**, and he walked away from the institution that made the first fusion possible before it could claim credit for the second.

---

### II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class III · Resonant.** Essence and Aether exchange freely through the prosthetic lattice — the fusion succeeded on exactly the axis that matters for a Class III Shell, whatever it cost the rest of him.
**Attraction Layer** · Developed sideways, toward machines and broken things rather than people. *He mends what he finds and rarely stays to be thanked for it.*
**Crystal State** · Fractured (Catharsis Reactor implosion), stabilized. **η** · ~0.88, fluctuating between organic (0.93) and synthetic (0.82) phases — the dual harmonic frequency his own nerves broadcast when he is angered or inspired.

---

### III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Transmutatio** · Spatium. **The Law of Change** — coordinate transformation under conservation, a change of basis rather than a creation. **Governing.** Sand to glass, stone to liquid metal: nothing created, nothing destroyed, only re-expressed.
**Catharsis** · Caloria. **The Wellspring of Release** — latent heat of phase change, enthalpy absorbed at constant temperature while the ordered structure itself breaks. **The Reactor that took his arm and the Aureveil Core that keeps the rest of him from petrifying both run on the same law.**
> **The warning that comes with it.** *Catharsis empties and does not transform — what it removes has to go somewhere, and Kaien's entire discipline is deciding where.*

### VIII · Signature

#### Goldbound Veil

**Effect** · He converts pain into radiant armor plating across the fused half of his body, hardening precisely where the damage is landing rather than everywhere at once.
**Cost** · **The armor draws off the same emotional-flux reserve that feeds the Solhand** — the plating and the arm are competing for one fuel source, and spending on one starves the other.
**Limit** · **Cracks visibly and sheds protection once stress crosses his documented safe threshold** — a countdown any observer standing close enough can watch happen in real time.
**Counter** · **Force the stress past the visible cracking point, then hit where the plating has already failed** rather than where it is whole.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the consciousness rumored to be caught in the Solhand has any say in which parts of him it chooses to protect.

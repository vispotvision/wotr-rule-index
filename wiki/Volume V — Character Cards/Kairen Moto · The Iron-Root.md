# Kairen Moto · The Iron-Root

## Kairen Moto · The Iron-Root

> *"My father grew the forest. I am the iron fence that keeps the wolves out."*

---

### I · Identity

**Name** · Kairen Moto. **Also called** · The Grafted Scion · The Iron-Root · The Broken Branch · The Bloom in the Oil.
**Epithet** · He Who Binds Life with Steel.
**Role** · **Divine-Mortal Hybrid. Mender of Broken Things. Wandering enforcer against tyranny.**
**Look & tells** · Messy hair spiking over one eye, a thick scarf masking the lower face, a mechanical right arm hissing steam and Aether, a blooming staff of twisted wood over one shoulder, a slab-blade too heavy for an ordinary body dragging behind him.
**Gloss rights** · **Never.** *He believes his own machinery has polluted him past the point of explaining himself cleanly, and the sheet respects that he will not gloss his own working.*
**Height** · 5'10" (6'4" with the Slab).

#### Affiliation

**Independent.** Recognized by the Moto Clan as a "Lost Branch," employed irregularly by the **Guild Accord** for reclamation work in toxic zones.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VI, Glory.** *The Threshold is willingly accepting a genuine soul-cost to preserve something valued above one's own structural integrity.*
> 
> **Losing the arm is the soul-cost the card already gives him.** He rebuilt himself in iron rather than stop working, and the sheet reads that choice as the Threshold itself, not as backstory that happened before one.

### II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class II · Harmonic.** The Shell holds rhythm but not yet full fidelity, which is exactly the leak the card describes as "Essence Shear" where the graft meets living tissue.
**Attraction Layer** · Split between a father he has not resolved and a network of Reclamation contracts. *No Axis is named on the card.*
**Crystal State** · Fused, uneven. **η** · ~0.42, Coherence Band D.
**Craft** · **Mechanica**, cross-listed with Vitalia workings. *The arm is a construct; the staff is living current; the same Crystal runs both, which is the whole tension the card is built on.*

---

### III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Verdantia** · Vitalia, Biochemistry. **Governing, inherited.** *Carbon fixation and indeterminate meristematic growth: a plant does not know when to stop, and Verdantia does not either.* Source of the blooming staff and his unnatural vitality.
**Coagula** · Materia, Material Science. **Secondary.** *Sintering — densification below the melting point, atoms diffusing across a boundary until it stops being a boundary.* This is the Graft: the law that keeps the arm bound to living soul without the body rejecting it.
**Fixatio** · Materia, Material Science. **Tertiary.** *What is held by Fixatio stays held.* Anchors the Slab's mass to the earth so a body his size can swing it at all. **Same-Family compounding with Coagula**, which is why the Graft has held this long without a Measurewright's help.

### VII · Artifacts

**The Branch of Rikudoku** · *Living relic, a cutting from the Great Spirit Tree.* **Never rots, blooms even in a desert.** Channels Verdantia healing or roots enemies into the ground.
**The Iron-Graft** · *Mechanica construct, right arm.* **Superhuman grip and durability**, vents Aether steam to blind foes, houses a hidden piston-drive for explosive strikes.
**The Slab** · *Kinetic anchor, broad blade.* **No edge. It crushes rather than cuts**, heavy enough to block siege weaponry or shatter terrain, wielded only because Fixatio anchors its mass.

### VIII · Signature

#### The Steel Orchard

**Effect** · **He drives the Branch and the Slab into the ground together, and fifty meters erupts into a forest of steel trees carrying living flowers.** He controls the standing forest afterward: branches intercept attacks, vines repair his armor, and the air turns hostile to anyone not his ally.
**Cost** · **Phantom pain and overgrowth both, at once.** Essence Shear burns where metal meets flesh from driving the arm this hard, and Verdantia responds by blooming flowers from his own skin, drawing on his blood for nutrients until the field is dismissed.
**Limit** · **The arm needs standing charge to drive the Slab this deep.** If the graft has run dry from lack of maintenance, it is two hundred pounds of dead scrap on his shoulder and the working does not start.
**Counter** · **An anti-magic field shuts the arm down outright.** Short of that, fire (Exuroth) burns the standing forest and overheats the mechanical components together, and a fast enough opponent simply outruns the forest's reach before it closes.
> **What nobody knows.** Why the Empire of the Moto's western reach has never sent anyone to reclaim a Lost Branch this dangerous, when every other irregular of his strength has been brought in or put down.

### IX · Relationships

**Rikudoku Moto** · **Father, estranged and revered.** Kairen wants to be worthy of a legacy he believes his own iron has already spoiled. *Named on Xanelor Rafminar's card (Volume III) as the Wandering Fang's philosophical ally.*
**Senshi Moto** · **Uncle, rival.** Views the mechanical arm as a weakness; Kairen views it as necessary evolution. *Unattested in the wiki mirror; kept because the card gives no other frame for the family conflict.*
**The Guild Accord** · **Employs him for Reclamation missions in toxic zones**, without extending him a charter.

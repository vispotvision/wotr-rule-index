# Khaeron Vant · The Ember Host

## Khaeron Vant · The Ember Host

> *"I am the altar and the knife. You only choose how much of you burns."*

---

### I · Identity

**Name** · Khaeron Vant. **Called** · The Bladeshrine of Cinerion. **Also known as** · The Mask-Bound · The Ember Host. **Epithet** · *He Who Makes Oaths Burn.*
**Role** · **Abythron-Touched Executioner. Ash-Fire Ritualist. Vessel Learning to Judge Rather Than Only Burn.**
**Gloss rights** · Unlimited. He speaks in aphorisms about what he does almost as often as he does it, and has never once declined to explain the altar he considers himself to be.

#### Affiliation

Free Therosian executioner, formally bound to a minor Abythron cult but functionally semi-rogue · Voyager Era · Drifts between Cinerion-aligned cults and Accord warbands who would like to formalize his execution work.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VII, Refraction.** *The soul sees itself from outside; self-examination at this depth reveals the fracture record, everything Splintering promoted to a load path, visible and impossible to un-see.*
> 
> **He looked at his own record and accepted himself as vessel rather than wielder.** That let the mask-spirit anchor without taking him over completely. Most practitioners at this Stage fight the fracture record. **He signed it instead, the way a man signs a contract he has already decided to honor.** The horned mask has never fully owned him since. It only argues with him.

### II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class II · Harmonic.** The attuned Shell holds rhythm, imperfectly; roughly a quarter of everything he vents is lost to mask-whisper and heat bleed rather than reaching the blade clean.
**Attraction Layer** · Developed and pointed at a network of branded Wellsprings rather than at any person, the mechanism behind his roaming execution-lattice.
**Crystal State** · **Bound.** **η** · 0.74, Band D.
**The Horned Core** · *A mask-spirit anchored in his spine rather than possessing him outright. Risk: Flux Saturation, leading to Mask Override, during which the spirit puppets his body briefly for catastrophic output and partial memory loss.*

**Cinerion** · Caloria. **Temperance through aftermath.** The Ash Veil, and the current his whole discipline is named for: what would be waste in another practitioner's hands becomes brand-work and recovery material in his.

### VI · Traits

**Ash of the Unnamed** · Victims slain by his brands leave dim Echoes that compress into glyphs on his chest pendants, convertible into emergency Flux at the cost of further erasing their presence from record.

### VIII · Signature

#### Shrine of Four Knives

**Effect** · He drives four knives into ground, corpses or walls, raising a micro-Domain in which every lie, hesitation or broken oath triggers ash-fire blooming inside the offender's own lungs.
**Cost** · Every casting shaves away a sliver of his own pre-Pact memories and risks Mask Override if pushed past its first activation in a fight.
**Limit** · It reads oaths and lies, not silence. A target with nothing binding them, or one already resigned to burn, gives it almost no leverage to ignite on.
**Counter** · **Face him with an oathless or willing target, or bring a strong Verdantia or Benediction current into the chamber.** Regrowth-and-absolution Wellsprings blunt his Oblivara tethers directly and cause his brands to sputter.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether severing the horned mask now would leave a free Temperant behind, or simply a man who has forgotten too much of why he wanted to be free in the first place.

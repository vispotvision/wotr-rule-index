# Liorien Nael-Teth · Sleepless Quill

## Liorien Nael-Teth · Sleepless Quill

> *"If no one writes it down, the world pretends it never happened."*

---

### I · Identity

**Name** · Liorien Nael-Teth. **Called** · **Sleepless Quill.** *The Drift-Scribe of the Eastward Isles · Tide-Ink Archivist · Little Book of Storms.*
**Epithet** · He Who Hears What Pages Remember.
**Role** · **Island Archivist. Memory-Sensitive Investigator. Scriptorium Consultant to the Guild Accord.**
**Look & tells** · 4'9", soft and underfed, deep umber skin, sleep-shadowed amber eyes, teal-blue hair perpetually wind-tossed, navy and cream robes stitched with gold tide-patterns, a feather quill tucked at his collar and stained at the tip.
**Gloss rights** · **Yes.** *Preserving exact meaning is his function, and he is precise and unembarrassed about explaining how a clause works, in the same voice he uses to record testimony.*
**Race / Lineage** · Veyran human, of a Brine-Touched Scribe line.

#### Affiliation

**Archivist-Member of the Archive of the Eastward Isles; contracted consultant to the Guild Accord for maritime and memory-sensitive investigations.**
> **Catalyst Event · Stage IV, Flourishing.** *The Threshold is the formation of a first genuine Spirit Axis bond.*
> 
> **The bond the card gives is to the ledger itself, not to a person.** He forged his first working axis of attachment to the act of preserving testimony rather than to any single ally, which is why the Threshold reads as a professional one and the sheet keeps it that way rather than inventing a name to fill the slot.

### II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class I · Muridic.** Essence leaks into the ink itself; the more memory he externalizes onto a page, the more his own recall frays, exactly the leak the Class predicts.
**Attraction Layer** · Developed toward testimony and truth rather than a person. *Rooms feel a little quieter around him; most people feel an odd urge to speak softly and be honest.*
**Crystal State** · Saturated, brine-touched. **η** · ~0.38, Coherence Band F.
**Craft** · **Runecraft**, primarily. *Every working is written, on paper, breath-fog, sea spray or dust; a written line is fixed and degrades like any inscription, which is the honest reason "ledger bleed" costs him permanently rather than fading with the scene.*

### III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Anamnesis** · Limina, Entropy and Mind. **Governing.** *Remanent magnetisation and thermoluminescent dosimetry: objects genuinely store their history in physically readable form, and Anamnesis is the instrument that reads it, not a miracle that invents it.* His pages hold testimony the same honest way.
**Oneirion** · Limina, Entropy and Mind. **Secondary.** *Configuration space: a system's every possible arrangement as a coordinate with no physical location.* His dream-journaling discipline and the half-lidded "reading" of echoes off people run on this law.
**Phreatis** · Vitalia and Fluxia, dual-Family. **Tertiary.** *Long-term potentiation, aquifer recharge and residence time.* The tide, the brine-ink, and why his script moves like water rather than like fire or stone.
**Cymorath** · Fulguria, Electromagnetism. **Minor, networked.** *Vibration and structural resonance.* A light trace picked up from deep-sea pressure storms, present but not governing.

### VI · Traits

**Ink-Saturated Soul** · **Any surface he touches can briefly hold script with no pigment**, letting him write law in breath-fog, sea spray or dust.
**Tide-Stilled Heart** · **Emotional waves around him dampen**, panic and wrath losing their edge, which makes testimony coherent in his presence.
**Dream-Page Gaze** · **When tired enough, his half-lidded stare "reads" faint overlays of what a person or object has witnessed.**
**Brine-Quill Edict** · **Floating luminous strips of script detach from the quill as minor edicts** — *do not pass, forget this face, hold the wall* — anchored to surfaces as soft barriers, nonlethal bindings, or memory shrouds.
**Low-Tide Redaction** · **A knee-high sheet of grey-blue water bleaches the emotional color from the last few seconds of sensory impression**, leaving events remembered but drained of the sharpness that makes them hurt or threaten.

### VIII · Signature

#### Echo-Current Folio

**Effect** · **A stack of charm-papers scatters into a small storm and forms a mobile half-dome**, each page carrying a ward, mirror, or tether glyph. The dome intercepts projectiles, reflects minor spells, and records incoming Aether patterns for later replay, turning the space into living testimony.
**Cost** · **Ledger bleed.** Every major casting writes part of his own memory into the ink; left untranscribed, those hours or days become hollow in his own mind. At Broken Shell Night the cost ran to several months.
**Limit** · **The dome only records what happens inside it, and the replay is only as honest as what actually crossed the pages.** It cannot see what it was not raised in time to catch.
**Counter** · **A Null-Aether or anti-script field renders the ink inert outright**, leaving him fragile and mundane. Short of that, fire and extreme dryness destroy the writing medium itself, and sustained flame or a desert environment collapses the dome from the inside.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the memories he loses to the ink are recoverable at all, or whether every page he has ever filled is, in some small way, a piece of himself he will never get back.

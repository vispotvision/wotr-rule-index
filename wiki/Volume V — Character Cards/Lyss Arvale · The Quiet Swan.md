### II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class III · Resonant.** A double spiral around throat and chest, flaring to the palms; sound becomes pressure-thread. *The room feels cleaner when she inhales.* Four modes: **Cantor Idle** (white hush; shakes dust from nearby lies), **Carry** (hearing and speaking range out to a city street against wind shear), **Cut** (syllables compressed into edge-bands that part rope, cloth and shallow plate), **Choir** (locks with an ally's breath to scale the aura without volume).
**Essence Core** · *"Let what must be heard arrive whole."*
**Attraction Layer** · Crowds lower their voices without deciding to; children step closer, liars step back. **Developed, and pointed at rooms rather than persons.**
**Crystal State** · **Fractured, hairline, stable.** *The Graybridge crack flares when she withholds a truth too long.* **η** · ~0.60, Coherence Band C.
**Resonant Tone** · High and clear, teal over white; tastes like winter air. **Essence hue** · white-greys with teal ribboning that spirals at the throat. **Halo** · a fine pressure corona that hushes clatter when she centres.
**Failure mode** · **Voice blanch, aphonia,** if she cuts through a truth she does not accept.

### VIII · Signature

#### Quiet Corridor

**Effect** · **She carves a path of hush through a battle so that orders and treaties pass ungarbled.** *Her voice leaves the Shell in phase under Aurevane; inside the corridor her pressure-thread runs opposite to the ambient clamour and cancels it, and a spoken sentence crosses the width of a field at conversational volume.* Under Judicium the corridor compels attention, never obedience. Everybody hears. Nobody is made to obey. The companion disciplines ride inside it: **Vowel Edge** (extended vowels condense air into a thin cutting band, nonlethal by focus, for strings, straps and banner poles), **Consonant Lock** (short percussive syllables that arrest a lunge or a shout for one heartbeat), and **Oath Lift** (spoken memory raised into visible whorl-text for adjudication, fading by moonset unless sealed with the pin). *At Stormgate she held one in typhoon winds and a battalion withdrew in order without a shouted word.*
**Cost** · **Voice blanch after over-cutting. Ear bleed if she absorbs more clamour than she vents.** Guilt-residue when her hush shelters a harmful secret, and on long campaigns **echo insomnia**: she keeps hearing what was said and what should have been said. *The hairline crack flares each time she holds a truth back to keep a corridor gentle.*
**Limit** · **Echo-dead vaults kill carry.** Mass bruisers who need no commands walk through it unbothered. The corridor never makes anyone act. It only makes them hear.
**Counter** · **Heavy `Uur` disruption tears the ribbon Shell.** Tear it and the hush goes. *Or set her a moral trap where withholding a truth hurts fewer people: her vow strains, the Shell flickers, and the corridor goes with it.*
> **What nobody knows.** Who is leaving her lost Graybridge feather at crime scenes, and what they are buying with her compassion.

# Natsume Kurogiku · The Knot-Shearer

> *"Stand still — I'll take in the anger and let the truth fall better on your shoulders."*
> **The card states no Temperance Stage.** **Stage VIII — Transcendence assigned and flagged as originated**, on the strength of **Thread-Sight**: *she sees people's ties as fine cords — love, envy, duty — each with its own tension.*
>
> ***A relationship is a relationship and is also a physical object with a measurable tension, and both are operative.*** **That is Transcendence's dual reading applied to human bonds, held permanently, as a trade.**

---

## I · Identity

**Name** · Natsume Kurogiku. **Called** · **The Knot-Shearer.** *Aliases:* **Black Chrysanthemum** · Truce Seamstress · **"Red-Scissor Nun."**
**Role** · **Feud-mender and oath-tailor.** *When hatred tangles a town — weddings, wakes, councils — Natsume hems the moment, trims the spite, and resews people to the promises they meant.*
**Affiliation** · **Guild Accord · Star Chamber adjunct**, ceremonial services. *Walks with the Reaper when last words snag on living grudges.*
**Look & tells** · **Ink-black and winter-red layers cut like shrine wear; cat-ear headpiece and lace veil; long charcoal hair tied in working ribbons.** *Carries ceremonial shears on a tassel and a lacquered needle case; apron pockets clink with pins that are really tiny bells.*

**Aether Shell** · **Class III · Resonant.** **Crystal State** · Refined. **η** · ~0.70, Band B.

**On scene** · *She traces a square in air with two fingers; cloth and tempers lie flatter; then the red shears open with a soft snick and a quarrel loses the thread it was pulling too hard.*

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Anamnesis** · Limina. **Governing.** *Objects and relations store their history in physically readable form.* **Thread-Sight is the reading; the cords are real and have tension.**
**Dissolution** · Limina. *Solvation driven by the entropy of mixing — the unbound state is thermodynamically preferred, and the process runs on its own once initiated.*
> **This is Benikiri, and it explains the shears' most specific property.** *They won't cut true love or honest duty — try and the hinge locks.* ***Dissolution only initiates where the unbound state is genuinely preferred.** A bond that is load-bearing does not want to come apart, so the process will not start.*
**Fixatio** · Materia. **Anchors and seals** — *the Bell Pins that ring when the wearer drifts from what they said.*
**Nyxial** · Limina. *A regulated baseline holding setpoint.* **Seam Ward — inside the hem, arguments arrive in indoor voices.**

## VIII · Techniques

**Hem the Room** · **She flicks chalk, draws a neat border**; *boots slow, shoulders drop, and the next sentence arrives trimmed to fit.*

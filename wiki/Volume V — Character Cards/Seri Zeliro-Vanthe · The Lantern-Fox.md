# Seri Zeliro-Vanthe · The Lantern-Fox

## Seri Zeliro-Vanthe · The Lantern-Fox

> *"I do not need to know your secret. I need to know which one you are afraid I already know."*
> **Migrated with all content preserved.** *"Aether Class: Weaver (Class II, Resonant)"* conflated two different Classes — **Class II is The Harmonic; Resonant is Class III.** She is **Class II, The Harmonic**, matching her Coherence Band (E). *"Temperance Stage: Stage 5, Splintering"* converts directly to **FOW Stage V — Splintering**, no correction needed. *"Path Bias: Fate 60% / Spirit 30% / Body 10%"* used three of the **current Four Paths (Body, Spirit, Attraction, Fate)** already; rendered here as the exemplar's two-path shorthand. Her η of 0.88 sat above the Class II efficiency band (~0.75–0.85 per the seven-Class table); **brought to 0.84**, at the top of the band, preserving "unusually high for her Class." Level, Pool and stat values are derived below; none are stated on the source card. **Zeliro-Vanthe** is unattested as a house in current canon and is kept, since the card cannot function without it. All six Wellspring names (Judicium, Anamnesis, Mirithane, Fixatio, Luminalis, Somnalis) verified against the Sixty and required no change.

**Role** · **Field Intelligence Operative. Fate-Thread Reader. Archivist of the Zeliro-Vanthe Codex.**
**Gloss rights** · **Diagnostic only.** She reads the room correctly and says exactly as much of it as the room needs to hear.

#### Affiliation

House Zeliro-Vanthe, Guild Accord Intelligence Division. *Fourth generation removed from the house's patriarch, on the Zeliro-Vanthe branch.*
> **Catalyst Event · Stage V, Splintering.** She read a thread that showed someone she loved would make a choice that would harm her, and she did not intervene, because intervening would have made it worse.
> 
> *Splintering fractures along emotional lines, not along the lines of the encounter that triggered it — it does not create the flaw, it promotes the flaw to a load path.* **Her loyalty to the house that trained her and what she reads in the people she is sent to watch are now structurally the same fracture**, and she has not decided which side of it she is standing on.

**Aether Shell** · **Class II · The Harmonic.** The attuned Shell holds rhythm; her thread-reading stabilises rather than flares.
**Attraction Layer** · Undeveloped toward any person; **pointed at a network of threads instead.** The house calls this professional distance. She calls it the only honest description of what she has left time for.
**Crystal State** · **Harmonic**, still gathering resonance. **η** · ~0.84, Band E — *unusually clean for a Class II Shell; house training on clarity over volume, not talent.*
**The dilute Bairosian current.** A faint, inherited thread-sight, present since before she had language for it — not full Moto sight, light through water rather than the depth itself.

### VI · Traits

**Fox-Sight** · Reads social dynamics, loyalty tensions and unspoken intention as faint threads at the edge of vision — who is lying, who is afraid, who is deciding something right now. Not full destiny-reading. Immediate causal terrain only.
**Threadmark Sensitivity** · As a Moto-adjacent descendant, she sees the Threadmarks on others when they choose something of true significance, and reads the weight they carry. She cannot generate one herself.
**Lantern Reading** · The staff's blue flame extends into a place's ambient Wellspring channel, brighter where a recent decision weighed on someone, dimmer where the space is quiet. Judicium and Mirithane shape what the flame shows.
**Codex Inscription** · Living Aether-ink stitched into the tome's pages carries the emotional weight of the moment written, not just its content — a reader gets a shadow of what she felt writing it. Every entry costs her some of the distance she used to have from what she witnessed.
**Overcurrent Absorption** · Sustained reading near a Wellspring, a major decision, or a strong Moto bloodline bearer floods her past what a Stage V Shell can sort, producing false positives and, at worst, the thread of someone she loves.

### VIII · Signature

#### Thread-Pull

**Effect** · She reaches into the local thread-web and makes one already-possible outcome taut — more immediately probable within the next few minutes. A guard who was going to break takes it now. A conversation starts two minutes early instead of ten.
**Cost** · Not force, continuity. Every pull is a gamble against her own read staying clean afterward; the more significant the outcome, the longer the blur that follows.
**Limit** · She cannot create an outcome that was not already possible. She can only move a probability she has already read closer to the front of the queue.
**Counter** · Flood the field with more live decisions than one lantern can track at once. A crowded room making a dozen small choices at once denies her the single clean thread she needs.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the discrepancy she has been quietly building a case around — three field reports reclassified without her handler's authorisation — was house policy or one person's decision.

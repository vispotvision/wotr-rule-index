# Shiragiku Iori · The Wake-Embroiderer

> *"Lay it here. Let the flower hold what your hands should not."*
> **Temperance corrected under the Benediction mapping.** The card reads **"Firm Benediction — her work steadies a scene rather than dominates it."**
>
> **Benediction is a resonance, not a Stage.** *It maps to Stage VIII, Transcendence, and the description fits: a flower is a flower and is also a container, and both are operative.*

## I · Identity

**Name** · Shiragiku Iori. **Called** · **The Wake-Embroiderer.** *Aliases:* **White Chrysanthemum** · **Snow-Mum.**
**Role** · **Funeral-florist and grief-binder.** *She turns raw mourning into wards, promises, and clear last words.* When panic wants a martyr, Iori sets out white blooms and the room remembers mercy.
**Affiliation** · **Guild Accord.** *Retained to the Reaper for deathbed rites; often seated with the Star Chamber at hot councils.*
**Look & tells** · **Red winter haori over snow-white dress, cuffs flocked like frost; half-veil mask; basket of pale chrysanthemums; a florist's crescent blade worn like a short saber.** *She smells of cold tea and clean linen.*

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Lethegraal** · Limina. **Governing.** *Relaxation time — the constant with which a perturbed system returns to equilibrium. Lethegraal shortens it. Nothing is removed and nothing is chosen; the disturbance metabolises down to neutral on a schedule the practitioner could not otherwise influence.*
> ***This is Wake-Bloom, and it reframes her entire craft.*** *A chrysanthemum pinned to a chest drinks rage and exhales clarity.* She is not absorbing anyone's grief. She is shortening how long it takes to finish — which is the same operation Kaede Suyori performs, by different means, and neither card knows the other exists.
**Mortalis** · Vitalia. **Governs passage** — *the lawful withdrawal of presence.* **Her whole profession.**
**Anamnesis** · Limina. **The Petal Ledger** — *each bloom remembers a promise made beside it; break it and the petal falls at your feet.*
**Nihiloth** · Limina. *Destructive interference.* **Cut of Regret — she severs "spite threads" and hands unclench without humiliation**, because the thread is cancelled rather than cut away.

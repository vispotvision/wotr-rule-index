## Shiro Kitsunari · Little White Fox

> *"If the branches shiver, we should listen before we march."*

---

### I · Identity

**Name** · Shiro Kitsunari. **Called** · Little White Fox, the Bell-On-Branch.
**Role** · **Grove Scout. Wellspring-Sick Sentinel. Tree Perch Courier.**
**Gloss rights** · Yes. He answers plainly when asked what he heard and how.

#### Affiliation

The Outer Wilds. An **Independent Concord Freeholder** courier, working an informal circuit for the Guild Accord, unaffiliated and holding no charter of his own. Loyal first to his home grove.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage I, Murmuring.** *Murmuring asks nothing of a soul but the fact of being caught.* As a toddler he fell from a high branch and the forest bent a net of branches that had no business bending to catch him. It is the entry stage every soul begins at, and needs no threshold crossed to arrive; his simply came early, on the day something in the wood decided to keep him.

### II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class I · The Muridic.** Essence leaks unevenly at this stage; sensation runs ahead of control. Aether gathers around him as leaf-shaped motes that flash gold when he startles.
**Attraction Layer** · Barely formed, and pointed at a place rather than a person or a network: the grove itself.
**Crystal State** · Awakened. **η** ≈ 0.65, Band F.
*A soul this young reading this cleanly is unusual; his mentors call it instinct running ahead of training rather than raw power.*

### VI · Traits

**Forest's Chosen Perch** · Trees bend and grow to keep him safe; falls redirect into swings, slides, or gentle catches rather than drops.
**Bell Of Quiet Paths** · The bell in his hair rings only at danger he cannot otherwise sense; hearing it briefly sharpens every other reading he has.
**Branchstep Skitter** · Coaxes root and branch to lift and twist so one step reads as three: dodging, closing distance, or clearing a trail without leaving one.
**Dream Moss Lullaby** · A mist of Somnalis essence off his markings settles beasts and panicked travelers into drowsy, harmless sleep rather than a hard stun; overuse leaves him pale and hungry and risks his own growth.

### VIII · Signature

#### Leaf-Listening Veil

**Effect** · He lets his Aether seep into nearby bark and leaf. The wood gives sound and Essence back as ripples in his own blood: footfalls, breath, and the first patches of a Wellspring going quiet, out to the edge of a grove.
**Cost** · Held too long, it comes back as headache and nosebleed. A stretch of real deafness follows. A loud enough Domain can blank the sense outright for minutes.
**Limit** · It needs living wood. Stone and metal cities give him nothing to listen with, and take away the better half of what he can do.
**Counter** · Overwhelm the channel rather than dodge it. A strong Wellspring or Domain floods his perception faster than he can filter it. A fight fought on stone denies him the veil.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the forest caught him because of what he already was, or made him what he is by choosing to catch him.

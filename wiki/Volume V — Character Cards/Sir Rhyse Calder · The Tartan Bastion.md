# Sir Rhyse Calder · The Tartan Bastion

> *"Stand close. I will make the world remember how to hold."*
> **Stage VII — Refraction is stated on the card and is correct.** *And the Temperance Path section is the best-written progression narrative in either volume:* **"Rhyse climbed through Ascension and Flourishing walking beside caravans, not leading charges. Splintering arrived the first time he watched a bridge collapse under panic and swore never again. Glory came with the early holds. Refraction awakened when he started bending flows instead of blocking them."**
>
> ***That is five Stages, each with a correct Catalyst, written by someone who understood the ladder.*** **Nothing in it needed adjusting.**
> **Corrected.** **"Aether Class: Tartan Bulwark Animus"** is not among the eight; **he is Class V, Radiant.** **"Mind Path 25 percent"** — *there are four Paths: Body, Spirit, Attraction, Fate.* **"Aurevane"** is not among the sixty; *Monolithion and Terranova are.* **Re-sourced below.**
> **Ruled 2026-09-12: the eta is corrected to Band A.** *The card's original 0.84 fell in the gap between Band A (0.75–0.80) and Band S (0.85–0.90); reset to* ***0.80****, the top of Band A -- correct for Stage VII.*
>
> **Resolved.** The card's 'S tier' framing was aspirational, not mechanical -- Band S belongs to Stage XI–XII, and Rhyse is Stage VII. Corrected to Band A throughout.
>
> ***Ruled 2026-09-12.*** **The Stage is right; the tier language was aspirational.** *Built at Stage VII, Band A -- the figure the card states explicitly and correctly, with the S-tier phrasing corrected rather than followed.*

## I · Identity

**Name** · Sir Rhyse Calder. **Title** · **March Warden of the North Rails.**
**Also known as** · **The Tartan Bastion** · *Rail Wall of the Auxilia.* **Epithet** · ***He Whom Has Tamed Over a Thousand Dragons.***
**Race** · **Human Northrail Highlander.** ***Last illegitimate son of the Dragon King Xalvion.***
**Affiliation** · **Guild Accord, Arbitration Auxilia Escort Corps.**

### Profile

**He is the Accord's moving gate.** *Where caravans panic, bridges sway, or councils start to fracture, he plants Lochgate, knots his tartan, and the world remembers how to hold.* **As March Warden he escorts arbiters, convoys and Sealwright teams** through places that hate the idea of order.
*His power does not come from spectacular beams, but from turning panic into columns, charges into turns, and dragons into something that is suddenly tired of fighting.*
**The blood of Xalvion coils in him as a quiet, sovereign current.** *It never earned him a throne, only a thousand hard jobs.* **Over the years he has calmed or broken more than a thousand dragon forms, engines and dragon-touched beasts.**
> ***His legend is not that he slays dragons. It is that they end up standing down, breathing easier, and going somewhere else.***

## II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class V · Radiant.** *Aether bends toward the bearer.* **His flows as calm, compressive pressure through stance and breath** — *effects show up as firmness, not flash.* **Rails rattle less. Stone grips boots better. Voices drop instead of rise.**
*When the Xalvion strain answers, that firmness turns into a low draconic gravity that even lesser dragons and dragon engines feel, like a command phrased as a suggestion.*
**Crystal State** · Refined.
> **Risks, as stated — and both are unusually well-costed.**
>
> *If he tightens Rail Root too hard on a damaged span, he can lock stress into the wrong supports and cause secondary failure.*
>
> *When he spreads Tartan Vow across too many people, their fear and strain settle into him and drag on his reflexes.*
>
> ***The second is Harmonics Breadth turned into a liability***, and very few sheets in the migration cost a support ability by what it takes from the supporter.

## III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Monolithion** · **Governing.** *The dense stable lattice.* **Bastion stillness — immovability as a structural property rather than a stance.**
**Terranova** · Materia. **Pale limestone.** *Ties him into ground, rails and masonry.*
**Fixatio** · Materia. **Anchors and seals** — *Rail Root, and the lightly imprinted ward lattice.* **Same-Family compounding with Terranova.**
**Judicium** · Fulguria. **Order, measure, consequence** — *replacing the card's "Aurevane."* **The draconic aura carried as presence and lawful weight rather than flame**, which is what the card describes and what Judicium actually does.
> **Network** · *Two auxiliary Wellsprings braided into the North Rails ward lattice.* **He keeps his Domain portable and avoids stamping city hearts, leaving permanent anchors to dedicated Warders** — *a deliberate refusal to consolidate, and the only instance of it in the migration.*

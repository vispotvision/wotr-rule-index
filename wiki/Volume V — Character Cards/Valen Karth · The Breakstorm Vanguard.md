# Valen Karth · The Breakstorm Vanguard

## Valen Karth · The Breakstorm Vanguard

> *"The world doesn't wait. Why should I?"*
> **Migrated with the triad, the spear and the Skybrand kept intact.** *"Class V — Resonant Dragoon" was not one of the seven Aether Classes; the numeral survives and the invented title is struck.* **He is Class V, the Radiant** — the Shell tier at which a Domain first forms, which is exactly what "Veyrun's Reach" is. *"Grand Convergence — Cycle II (Outer Concord Theatres)"* cites no era the Concordance recognizes; the card's own Trello label reads Withering Age, so **the Withering Era is the era of record.** *Nevara is struck canon entirely* (Volume V standing note); the closing hook is re-set over **Caedor**, the New World theatre his own card already names. *Veyric Human, Aether-Scaled Draconids, General Saelri Varn and the Skyroot Wellspring* are unattested in current canon and kept only because the card cannot make sense without them. **The raw physics figures (gigantonewtons, Mach, joules, and a Flux Density and Aether Output three orders of magnitude above every peer Stage VIII sheet on file) are struck** as old-system invention rather than table-derived; the Primary Stat block below replaces them.

#### Affiliation

**Guild Accord — Bluefront Legion, Dragoon Vanguard Commander.** Designation VX-0813. *Bloodline resonance with metallic Aether, from hybridization with a draconic bloodline during the Withering's early campaigns, is kept as stated and flagged unattested by name.*
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VIII, Transcendence.** *The Stage's own threshold is resolving a core contradiction in the fracture record and forming a first true Domain.* Valen resolved his by deciding that stability itself was the contradiction. **He forged Veyrun's Reach by breaking his own Crystal on purpose, repeatedly, until the breaking became the shape the Domain would hold.** *A Radiant Shell earned by refusing to stop moving long enough for the fracture to close clean.*

### II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class V · The Radiant.** Aether bends toward him mid-flight; the longer he sustains it, the more the air itself starts arguing his case.
**Attraction Layer** · Newly developed and unstable. **Veyrun's Reach** is his first true Domain, a storm-ringed pocket above the Outer Fronts where gravity runs sideways.
**Crystal State** · Fractured, self-reforming. **η** · ~0.70, Band B. *The billions-scale Flux Density and Aether Output the old sheet gave are struck as inconsistent with every other Stage VII–IX sheet on file; the Soul Crystal's output is read qualitatively here instead.*
**The Harmonized Fracture Lattice** · A multi-spired helical prism, four concentric fissures orbiting the core, opening further each time Penanceflare fires. Electric blue, flaring white-gold under load.

### III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Ascensio** · Vectoria. **The Ladder of Ascent** — vertical momentum, aerial redirection, the lift under every leap.
**Fractura** · Vectoria. **The Breaking Point** — controlled rupture of local Essence law, used for instant re-entry into a fight from a fracture point rather than a run-up.
**Penanceflare** · Caloria. **The Light of Contrition** — burns his own pain and exhaustion into thrust. *Same-Family compounding does not apply here; the triad is cross-Family on purpose, which is why holding all three open at once is what fractures the Lattice.*
Together, the triad is **Skyshatter Drive**: rise, break, burn.

### VIII · Signature

#### Divine Dive — Heavenpiercer

**Effect** · He launches straight up, compresses everything Ascensio and Fractura are feeding him into a single line, and comes down as one kinetic strike.
**Cost** · **Burns deep into reserve in a single motion**, past the tenth-of-reserve line his own count keeps. Landing it leaves him grounded and slow for the exchange after.
**Limit** · **Needs open sky.** Indoors, or anywhere the atmosphere thins past a threshold, there is nowhere for the compression to build.
**Counter** · **Ground him before the dive completes.** Fixatio-aligned magnetized barriers slow Ascensio's uplift enough to break the line, and a Heavenpiercer that never gets airborne is just a man falling.
> **What nobody knows.** Why the Lattice reforms denser and sharper after each near-disintegration instead of simply cracking further. His body already shows the early signs the Guild associates with a Walking Rift, and nobody has told him.

# Xhael Ten'Kor · The Ember Regent

## Xhael Ten'Kor · The Ember Regent

> *"I am no god — just a man who mistook light for salvation."*

---

### I · Identity

**Name** · Xhael Ten'Kor. **Called** · The Ember Regent.
**Role** · **Exiled Sun-Prince. Dominion Arbiter. Walking Conflux of Molten Essence.**
**Gloss rights** · Never. A monarch does not narrate his own workings, and a man carrying this much guilt does not perform explanations for an audience.

#### Affiliation

**Independent Dominion — former Temple-State of Solfatara.** Wandering sovereign in self-imposed exile, registered by the Guild Accord as a **Tier 6 Realm-Hazard.**
> **Catalyst Event · Stage X, Realization.** *He seized divine power through an incomplete ascension rite, merging mortal Essence with solar Aether — the ritual burned the throne to ash and crowned him in molten gold.* **The Continuum accommodated the Domain that resulted, and the world began answering back exactly as the Threshold predicts. It answered back by killing forty thousand people first.**

### II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class V · Radiant.** The world-bending Shell — Aether bends toward him rather than waiting to be drawn, and it is the only Class that explains a Domain surviving its own founder's exile.
**Attraction Layer** · Bound to a place rather than a person: **the magma scar over Solfatara**, which he did not create on purpose and cannot fully disown.
**Crystal State** · Radiant, first true Domain seated. **η** · ~0.89 stable under calm; spikes to 1.12 during Overheat, output exceeding theoretical maximum — **the defining signature of Class V, not a malfunction.**

---

### III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Exuroth** · Caloria. **The Trial Flame** — temperance through ordeal, quench and temper as a metallurgical cycle run on a soul instead of a blade. **Governing.** The Wellspring embedded beneath Solfatara's palace vaults that raised him.
**Solfatara** · Caloria. **The Fuming Earth** — fumarolic degassing, a steady controlled release rather than a stored charge, which is exactly why a volcanic system vents for centuries without erupting **until something stops it from venting.**
> **Same-Family compounding, and its failure mode.** *Exuroth and Solfatara agree along the same thermodynamic axis and reinforce each other cleanly — which is precisely why, when the vent was denied for one incomplete ritual's worth of time, the release came all at once instead of in the centuries it should have taken.*

### VIII · Signature

#### Crownflare

**Effect** · He expels excess Flux through the Obsidian Crown as a radiant shockwave, an overflow valve for a system that otherwise cooks him from the inside.
**Cost** · **The discharge burns straight off his own reserve at a rate any coil can read** — using it on purpose is functionally identical to bleeding a wound to relieve the pressure behind it.
**Limit** · The Crown must first accumulate enough excess Flux to vent, so it cannot be thrown twice in quick succession; forced early, it produces a smaller, wilder discharge instead.
**Counter** · **Deny him the buildup window** — keep the engagement short, or force the vent into a direction he has to choose badly, toward cover he'd rather not burn or people he'd rather not hit.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the Sun-Crown is a relic he wears, or a remnant of Solfatara's dead Wellspring still making its own decisions about when to vent.

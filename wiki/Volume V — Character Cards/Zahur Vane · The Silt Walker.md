# Zahur Vane · The Silt Walker

## Zahur Vane · The Silt Walker

> *"The sand does not need to be hard to kill you. It only needs to be deep. Step carefully."*
> **Migrated with all content preserved:** both relics, the Sinkhole Step, the Desiccation cost. **"Terramancer, Fluid-State Earth Type" was not among the seven; he is Class II, the Harmonic**, sitting at the very top edge of that Class's window, which matches the source's own η of 0.85 almost exactly. **"Solutio" is not one of the Sixty; it is corrected to Dissolution**, Limina's Unbinding Stream, the attested Wellspring for exactly the dissolving-law the card describes. **His lineage and the oasis he guards are renamed off "Orrenthal"**, which already names a real Wellspring (Fulguria, the Metallic Heart) unrelated to sand or nomads; both now use "the Great Sand Sea," a phrase the source card already supplies for his home region, so nothing new was invented. **His Durability is corrected from 4.5 petajoules to 3.2 terajoules**, the top of the A-Grade energy band his Stage VI ceiling actually supports; the source figure was roughly a thousand times over. **His "Shifting Meridian" Domain is corrected to a pre-Domain effect**, since a true Domain seed does not appear before Stage VII.

### I · Identity

**Name** · Zahur Vane. **Called** · The Silt Walker. **Also known as** · The Weaver of the Blind Road, Keeper of the Sun-Dial Rites, The Soft Trap. **Epithet** · He Who Wears the Desert Like a Skin.
**Race** · Human, a nomad lineage of the Great Sand Sea. Deep, warm brown skin; coiled dark hair; resistant to extreme heat and dehydration.
**Role** · **Gatekeeper and wayfinder.** **Gloss rights** · Never; he explains nothing, and the desert does the talking for him.

#### Affiliation

Guides of the Lost Meridian, a neutral order of wayfinders. He leads seekers toward lost Wellsprings and quietly judges whether they deserve to arrive.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VI, Glory.** *The Threshold this Stage asks for is willingly accepting a genuine soul-cost to preserve something valued above one's own structural integrity.* Zahur crossed it the day he chose to keep guarding the Hidden Oasis of the Great Sand Sea rather than sell its location, at a cost the card does not name but his Desiccation risk makes plain: he pays in his own body for every working, and the Oasis was worth more to him than staying whole.
> 
> **He learned young that the desert does not fight the wind. It moves with it.** Everything about his method, patience over force, positioning over pursuit, comes off that one lesson.

### II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class II · Harmonic**, at the top of the Class's window. The attuned Shell holds rhythm; his stillness before a working is the Shell staying in tune, not theater.
**Attraction Layer** · Minimal by design. He guards a place, not a person, and the Oasis is closer to an Axis than any living bond he carries.
**Crystal State** · Harmonic. **η** · ~0.85 (retained; sits exactly at Class II's ceiling).
**The granular part of the Core** · Not a solid block. Millions of small, hard points, which is why nothing grips him spiritually for long.

### III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Petralon** · Vectoria. The Foundation Stone. *Routes every incoming load into compression, the same principle that makes a Sinkhole Step re-solidify into a trap the instant a target stops moving.*
**Mirithane** · Limina. Specular reflection. *A true mirror adds nothing; angle in equals angle out. Zahur does not add force to a fight, he changes its angle.*
**Dissolution** · Limina. The Unbinding Stream. *Same-Family compounding with Mirithane is why reading the ground and unmaking its bonds are one operation in his hands rather than two.*
**Network** · Zero. Independent pattern, by choice.

### VIII · Signature

#### Sinkhole Step

**Effect** · Zahur liquefies a radius of ground beneath a charging opponent; Petralon's compression law takes hold the instant they stop moving, and the ground re-solidifies around them like stone closing a fist.
**Cost** · Draws heavily on his own body's moisture; a day of combat run this way costs him roughly two gallons of water, and going without risks collapse from Desiccation.
**Limit** · It needs erodible ground in continuous contact: sand, loose earth, weathered stone. Frozen or paved bedrock refuses the liquefaction outright.
**Counter** · Freeze the ground first, or fight him somewhere the working can't set: open water, solid rock, or wind strong enough to scatter the dust before the trap closes.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the Great Shift he feels building underground is a Titan waking, or his own Wellspring reaching the end of what the desert still has to lend him.

# Beatrix Vaelthorn · The Root-Sworn

## Beatrix Vaelthorn · The Root-Sworn

> *"Dirt doesn't lie. Neither do I."*

---

### I · Identity

**Name** · Beatrix Vaelthorn. **Called** · Bea, the Root-Sworn, the Ashwalker.
**Role** · **Wandering Root-Healer. Verdantia-Marked Survivor. Mudborn Witch.**

#### Affiliation

Unaffiliated wanderer, formerly indentured to a root-healer commune under **the Holy Sea of Alabaster's** outer territories. Left after the petrification incident was ruled divine judgment by the commune elders.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage IV, Flourishing.** *Formation of the first genuine Spirit Axis bond.* **Hers formed with the Wellspring itself rather than with a person:** the moment she stopped hiding the petrified arm and started using it as a tool.
> 
> **Every Stage before this one, she survived instead of learned.** The commune had no mentor for what happened to her, so the Threshold Catalyst on this sheet is the first thing she did on purpose rather than the first thing that happened to her.
**Gloss rights · Unlimited.** She has no true names, no register to withhold, and would explain any of it plainly if asked.

---

### II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class I · Muridic.** *The awakening Shell flickers with first resonance; Essence leaks unevenly.* **Matches the card's own "slightly leaky" note exactly, and Stage IV sits inside the Muridic Band.**
**Attraction Layer** · Undeveloped. A proto-Domain instinct only: plants and animals lean toward where she sleeps, nothing formalised yet.
**Crystal State** · Awakened, unrefined.
**η** · ~0.65, Band F. *The corruption-scar reroutes Essence unpredictably; she has learned to compensate rather than to stop the leak.*

---

### III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Verdantia** · Vitalia. **Governing.** *The Green Pulse.* **Slow-build organic pressure: vines, root-tendrils, spore-clouds.** Her casting accretes rather than detonates, the way roots split stone.
**Chthonica** · Materia. *The Root Deep: earth-memory and root-law.* **Root-Listening runs on this: vibration through soil and plant-matter, read as a passive field.**
**Mortalis** · Vitalia. *The Black Gate: decay as renewal.* **The wound-site burst that corrodes whatever struck her.** Not healing. Revenge-through-rot.
**Network** · Two further Wellsprings resonate passively through the corruption-scar, unnamed by the source and left undetermined here rather than invented.

### VIII · Signature

#### Verdant Recursion

**Effect** · The ground beneath a target tries to reclaim them: roots pulling downward while the target's outer Aether shell decays and new growth seeds through what leaks out.
**Cost** · **Each use advances the petrification two centimetres up her arm.** She has fourteen centimetres of buffer left before it reaches her shoulder.
**Limit** · **She must have ground contact and time to build the working**, which a fast opponent simply denies her.
**Counter** · **Fire.** Exuroth- and Ignivale-line workings burn through her root constructs faster than she can rebuild them, and her Verdantia Aether carries near-zero resistance to either.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the arm stops at her shoulder, or whether that was only ever the story the commune told her to make the fear bearable.

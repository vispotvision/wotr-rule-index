# Casimir Aldous Alleyne · The Ashen Hand

## Casimir Aldous Alleyne · The Ashen Hand

---

### I · Identity

**Name** · Casimir Aldous Alleyne. **Called** · The Ashen Hand. *Field name for guild filing purposes; his myth name is unearned.* **Cas**, to his grandfather only.
**Role** · **Apprentice Transmuter. Ward of Bertrand Aurel. Pre-registered candidate, Guild Accord Alchemia.**
**Gloss rights** · Diagnostic only.

#### Affiliation

Independent apprentice under Bertrand Aurel's private, unlicensed charter, upper floor of an old apothecary building. **Guild Accord Alchemia Division holds a passive observation flag on his pre-registration**; the incident was logged as a Wellspring proximity event, no fault assigned, and it sits in the file regardless.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage II, Welling.** *First genuine contact with a Wellspring current under personal pressure.*
> 
> **His was an inversion, not a contact.** Grief pushed Aether through an aperture sized for Stage III while the soul carrying it was still Stage I. The Coagulatio reflex sealed the breach at the cost of his right eye. **What followed was less an ascension than a scar that learned to hold**, and he has not moved past it since. The talent was never the problem; opening the seal is the only door forward he has, and he has spent every year since closing it instead.

### II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class II · Harmonic.** Accumulates inward: high compression, delayed release, ahead of what Stage II usually conducts.
**Attraction Layer** · Neutral-to-pull. Draws Essence inward rather than projecting it.
**Crystal State** · Fractured-Harmonized, stabilized post-incident, not cleanly healed. **Structure** · Compressed Trapezohedron, one primary Fracture Line along the upper right face; a Stage III collapse scar, sealed rather than healed.
**η** · Suppressed well below theoretical maximum by habitual compression; he holds pressure in and releases only when certain.
**Risk · Compression Inversion.** Suppressed Aether eventually releases uncontrolled, transmuting whatever is nearest, his own body included. Under acute emotional stress the Fracture Line illuminates ash-white through the skin of his open palm.

---

### III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Sublimatio** · Vectoria, Mechanics. **The Ascending Breath**; refinement, separation of essential from impurity. He does not add. He refines away.
**Mortalis** · Vitalia, Biochemistry. Endings, the passage between life and its absence. Seeded by his parents' deaths; he reads decay like text and resents that he can.
**Coagulatio** · Materia, Material Science. **The Returning Stone**; binding, solidification, the law that holds a shape. The faculty that saved his life during the incident is the same one currently preventing him from grieving enough to ascend.

### VIII · Signature

#### Seuil Coagulé · Coagulated Threshold

**Effect** · A three-second palm-flat seal, or an involuntary trauma-triggered version of the same, locks a surface: a door will not open, a compound will not react, a wound will not bleed further. Against a person, their Aether field compresses against itself and their casting cadence breaks.
**Cost** · Emotional stability. It is strongest under duress and involuntary use accelerates compression toward the inversion threshold; his best defense is also his fastest route toward the thing he is suppressing.
**Limit** · Requires contact with the surface being sealed, or with the person.
**Counter** · He cannot transmute while sealing; a fast opponent who reaches him before the seal completes finds him with nothing else in hand.
**What nobody knows.** Whether the Fracture Line is damage or a door; and what full Mortalis-sight through the closed orbit would cost him to open honestly rather than by accident.

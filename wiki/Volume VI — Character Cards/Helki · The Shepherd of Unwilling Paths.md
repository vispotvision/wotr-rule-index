# Helki · The Shepherd of Unwilling Paths

## Helki · The Shepherd of Unwilling Paths

> **Migrated with all content preserved, including Orvain and both relics.** *"Temperance Stage: Stage 7; Dissonance"* conflates two different Stages: **the numeral 7 is correct, but Dissonance is Stage XI.** Stage VII's canonical name is **Refraction**, and it fits her card exactly; "first Domain seed activation, however fragile" is the Stage VII catalyst, and she carries a nascent Domain (**The Unmeasured Field**). **Stage VII is retained; Dissonance is struck.** *"Aether Class: Elegiac"* is not among the seven; her Coherence Band (C) and the maturity of her Domain seed place her at **Class III, Resonant**; "Elegiac" survives as the register her Resonance speaks in, not a separate Class. *"Deep Gaia... resonance"* is struck outright; Gaia is not a Wellspring, Family, or Titan on this world's ledger; and **Florwyn**, the attested Titan of restorative entropy and reincarnation, is retained in its place, which if anything sharpens the cycle-and-offering theme the card already wanted. The old sheet's EU / AU-s / Mach / PJ figures were an unconvertible point-scale; they are retired here in favour of the Primary Stat framework below, with their qualitative sense (permeating, ceremonial-paced, enormous at range) kept as description. Her incident dates ("VE 881," "VE 889") do not correspond to any term in the Errata to the Received Registers; they are kept as her own itinerant record-keeping, unattested against the sealed chronology. Of her three Core Disciplines, **The Offering Walk** is carried forward as the required single Signature; The Last Pasture and Orvain's Call are preserved below only as background mentions.

### I · Identity

**Name** · Helki. **Called** · The Shepherd of Unwilling Paths. *Also* the Bell-Bearer, Helki of the Flock.
**Role** · **Elegiac Conduit. Wandering Tender of the Dying Wellspring. Bearer of the Offering.**
**Gloss rights** · Diagnostic only.

#### Affiliation

The Unmeasured Path; nominally neutral. **Independent, itinerant, travels with Orvain.**
> **Catalyst Event · Stage VII, Refraction.** *The soul sees itself from outside, and what surfaces is the fracture record; everything Splintering promoted to a load path, now visible and impossible to un-see.*
> 
> **Hers surfaced as a Domain seed, not a wound.** Three days pouring her Essence into a dying Wellspring nexus at Stage V is not survival by refusal, the way most Stage V thresholds run; it is survival by total surrender, and the Continuum answered it by seeding a Domain rather than scarring her. **She walked out with grey in her hair and a lamb that had been standing in the current the whole time.**

### II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class III · Resonant.** Power moves at the pace of ceremony, not reflex; the flavour her old sheet called "Elegiac" is what Resonant conduction looks like when the practitioner's whole discipline is offering rather than force.
**Attraction Layer** · Developed toward a single living bond (Orvain) rather than a network. **Attraction Scale C-Tier: Pastoral Weight**; animals still, lower-Temperance beings feel the pull to confess.
**Crystal State** · Refined, under standing pressure from the **Permanent Tithe**. **η** · high for her Band, consistent with a conduit built for minimal loss over long, slow expenditures rather than burst output.
**The Verdant Altar draw.** She is walking toward it because she suspects it is the completion her Crystal has been arranging since Stage V: a debt she has decided to pay in person, not a wound left to survive. *Unattested destination; kept because the card cannot make sense without it.*

### III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Oblation** · Fluxia, Fluid Dynamics. **The cost mechanism**; what she gives up on a target's or her own behalf, and the reason her Essence Capacity has fallen from 1,200,000 to 890,000 EU across fourteen uses of the Walk.
**Verdantia** · Vitalia, Biochemistry. Growth, herb-craft, the field that grows taller.
**Mortalis** · Vitalia. Passage; reads dying and death the way others read a page.
**Anima Spirare · Contrition · Rebirthine** · Vitalia, networked. The soul's breath, the broken flame of regret, the cycle that returns. All three retained from the source card; all three attested.
**Counter-resonances** · **Nihiloth** (Limina, Silence/Resonance Denial) and **Abython** (Vectoria) strip Oblation consecration to inert dispersal; her one documented structural weakness.

### VI · Traits

**The Shepherd's Read** · Perceives the emotional and spiritual inventory of any living being within 60 metres; whether a soul is ripening toward something or fleeing it. Passive, involuntary.
**Cycle Ward** · Her body treats injury as part of the cycle rather than as damage; wounds that would kill outright become survivable. Confirmed twice by clinical death and recovery.
**Orvain's Bell** · While Orvain is within 30 metres, Oblation costs drop roughly a fifth. The bell rings, unfailingly and unsilenceably, whenever someone nearby crosses an undoable threshold decision.

### VII · Artifacts

**The Crook of Turned Seasons** *(T6, Arcane-Resistant)*; driven into Wellspring-resonant earth, extends her Domain seed to 120 metres; wounds it inflicts resist Essence-healing for a full exchange.
**Orvain** *(T7, Living Relic)*; the lamb that wandered into a dying Wellspring rite and was changed. His fleece carries passive Anima Spirare resonance; cloth woven from it cannot be turned against its wearer. Never shorn. She has never asked why.

### VIII · Signature

#### The Offering Walk

**Effect** · Three measured steps toward a target, their name spoken in Parun. The tithe is given as an offering on the target's behalf; their Soul Crystal defenses drop for one full exchange.
**Cost** · 310,000 EU, plus a permanent tithe of 40,000 EU stripped from her own maximum Essence Capacity; gone forever, not recoverable by any Craft. She has used it fourteen times; her ceiling has fallen from 1,200,000 to 890,000 EU.
**Limit** · Requires the target's true name, spoken in Parun, at close range. She cannot walk the offering toward someone she cannot name.
**Counter** · Interrupt the third step, or deny her the name. The offering is not complete until the sequence closes.
**What nobody knows.** Whether the tithe is paid to Oblation, to Florwyn, or to something that has simply learned to answer when she calls.

### IX · Relationships

Neither incident is on any Accord file.

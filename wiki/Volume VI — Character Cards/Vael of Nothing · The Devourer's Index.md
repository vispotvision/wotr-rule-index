# Vael of Nothing · The Devourer's Index

## Vael of Nothing · The Devourer's Index

---

### I · Identity

**Name** · Vael. **Called** · The Devourer's Index, the Form-Breaker. *Also* Vael of Nothing, the Blindfolded Ruin.
**Role** · **Null-Devour Apex. Unaffiliated Correction.**
**Gloss rights** · Never.

#### Affiliation

None formal. **The Guild Accord holds an open file on him; not a warrant, a file; they have not decided what category he belongs to.**
> **Catalyst Event · Stage IX, Invocation.** *Calling on Wellsprings, Titans, or Archonic law as co-authors; the Domain consolidates into a Realm.*
> 
> **Vael did not call on Uurgath. Uurgath answered anyway.** A Nullborn soul that survived an active Oblivara event during gestation was already, structurally, the kind of vessel the Devourer of Forms recognises as co-author rather than instrument. **His Domain seed did not need a summoning. It needed a body willing to keep being the answer.**

### II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class VI · Voidic.** The Shell devours, remembers, and reflects instead of emitting; his output does not deal damage in the conventional sense, it removes.
**Attraction Layer** · **S-Tier: The Quiet Before.** Ambient Aether thins near him; constructs flicker at the edges; lower-Temperance beings forget mid-sentence what they were about to say.
**Crystal State** · Stable but actively eroding. **Form Erosion** applies Null-Devour output to Vael's own physical coherence; his extremities are already losing definition. The Guild Accord's internal estimate gives him four to eight years of physical coherence remaining at his current rate. He has not adjusted it.
**η** · High at rest, dropping under Form Erosion onset; the cost of a Shell that unmakes rather than conducts.

---

### III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Oblivara** · Limina, Entropy/Void/Mind. **The Dark Mirror**; void, erasure, the space left when a thing ceases to be. His primary.
**Fractura** · Vectoria, Mechanics. The breaking of form, the moment structure fails.
**Dissolution** · Limina. The undoing of fixed shape; nothing stays what it was.
**Network** · **Nihiloth** (Limina, Silence/Resonance Denial) and **Cataclysm** (Caloria) run passively alongside the Triune Core.
**Structural counters** · **Benediction** (Vitalia) and **Coagula** (Materia); form-affirmation Wellsprings that fix, seal, and make permanent; are the only category of counter that has consistently interrupted him.

### VI · Traits

**The Index** · Perceives the structural integrity of any construct, soul, or physical form within 80 metres as a complete dissolution-map. Involuntary and permanent; the blindfold slows the information rate to something tolerable.
**Form Attrition** · Wards and constructs within 15 metres lose roughly a twelfth of their coherence per turn passively. Extended proximity below Stage 6 causes permanent Wellspring-resonance degradation; three documented Guild Accord cases, none recovered.
**Null Margin** · Physical strikes against him have a real chance of partially dissolving on contact before full impact lands. He chose none of it; Oblivara resonance simply rejects a defined impact event.

---

### VII · Artifacts

**The Index** *(T9, Null-Forged, Oblivara-saturated)*; a Null-Devour conductor rather than a blade in any ordinary sense. Strikes bypass Aetheric shielding entirely and apply dissolution force straight to a target's Soul Crystal. Its hexagonal Fractura inlay was not his own work; the sword arrived with it. The only thing he treats with care.
**The Three Medallions** *(T6, Oblivara-engraved)*; worn at his chest, they suppress Form Attrition's radius from 15 metres to 8 outside active combat, after the third documented case of a bystander's permanent resonance loss.

---

### VIII · Signature

#### The Final Index

**Effect** · He removes the blindfold and looks at one target directly. The Index's passive dissolution-map sharpens to surgical precision: he perceives the exact sequence of failures that would collapse the target's physical form, Soul Crystal, and Wellspring resonance at once. Perceiving the sequence begins it; no touch required.
**Cost** · 2,600,000 EU and accelerated Form Erosion onset. Used seven times; five targets did not survive; one produced a Benediction-class counter that interrupted the sequence; one he chose, mid-process, to stop. He has not explained either.
**Limit** · The target has three turns to leave his line of sight or produce a counter that disrupts the Null-Devour field entirely. He replaces the blindfold immediately after, regardless of outcome.
**Counter** · Survive three turns and disrupt the field with a Benediction- or Coagula-class working. Surviving three turns is the entire problem.
**What nobody knows.** Why the one target he stopped mid-process was the one he stopped.

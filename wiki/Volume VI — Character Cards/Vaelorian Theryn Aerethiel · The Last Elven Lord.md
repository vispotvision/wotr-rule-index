# Vaelorian Theryn Aerethiel · The Last Elven Lord

## Vaelorian Theryn Aerethiel · The Last Elven Lord

> **Migrated with all content preserved, including the ongoing dragon-transformation arc.** *"High Aeliri Elf"* is not an attested lineage; the sovereign elven people of Eresse are the **Vey-Elarin**, and the theological branch that answers the Kinship Doctrine through exact repetition of rite and law is **Eressean**; both terms the card already used elsewhere, so **Vey-Elarin of the Eressean branch** replaces "High Aeliri" outright rather than sitting alongside it. *"Grand Convergence Era"* corresponds to no term in the Errata to the Received Registers; the card's own war; an existential incursion the High Empire of Eresse did not see coming; matches **the Great Spirit War** exactly (the Erebus incursion, First Eon), and is retitled accordingly. *"Florwyn"* is verified: it is the Titan of the **Spiritual Seat**, restorative entropy and reincarnation, rather than a Wellspring at all, which makes "Florwyn's Great Elven Dragon design" a Titanic-Law phenomenon instead of a Wellspring effect, and sharper for it. Of the card's "Network," **Sanctus** and **Tempestaris** are not attested anywhere in the Sixty and **Arboreum Primordis** is not a Wellspring at all; they are replaced with the nearest attested equivalents that preserve the described function; **Sanctus → Aurevane** (Fulguria, The Wellspring of Light, for the holy-light register), **Tempestaris → Cymorath** (Fulguria, The Air of Ascent, for the storm/canopy register), **Arboreum Primordis → Chthonica** (Materia, The Root Deep; "the world root" is Chthonica's own gloss, almost word for word). *"Temperance Stage 14, Zenith"* and *"Global Tier: SS-Tier"* disagree with each other: **Zenith is Stage XIV exactly**, whose Coherence Band is SSS, one full Band above the SS the card separately claims. **Stage XIV is retained** on the strength of the exact name-and-numeral match; the Global Tier figure is treated as the older, coarser instrument and is not used to override it. Per the conversion law's conservative-value rule, his current output is capped at **X-Grade**, one Grade below Stage XIV's EX-Grade ceiling; a lord mid-transformation, not yet at the ceiling his Stage would allow him. *"Aether Class: Florwynic Dracoseraph"* is not among the seven; **Class V, Radiant** (world-bending Shell, can hold Domains against collapse) is the closest fit, though it sits a full Band below his SSS Coherence Band; kept as a feature: the transformation is described as incomplete, and a Shell still catching up to its own Stage is exactly what "not yet fully born" should look like on a sheet. The old EU / Mach / GN figures are retired for the framework below. Of his three Core Disciplines, **The Spirit Dragon Bud** is carried forward as the required Signature; the Everfall March and Oathbound Retribution are preserved below only as background mentions.

### I · Identity

**Name** · Vaelorian Theryn Aerethiel. **Called** · The Last Elven Lord, Paragon of Elyndra, Florwyn's Regent. *Also* the Black Canopy, the Thorn-Crowned Sovereign.
**Role** · **Former High Marshal, Verdant Phalanx. Last Regent, Court of the Verdant Throne. Living vector for a Titanic design not yet complete.**
**Gloss rights** · Yes.

#### Affiliation

Verdant Phalanx (former High Marshal); Court of the Verdant Throne (last Regent); recorded in Accord lexicons as **a mobile Draconic Root Arm of Florwyn**.
> **Catalyst Event · Stage XIV, Zenith.** *Accumulated doctrine becomes recognizable to a Hollow Throne; not a breakthrough, a recognition. Full World Spirit.*
> 
> **What Florwyn recognised in him was not doctrine. It was persistence.** Every Great Spirit War engagement he has survived in his current shape has tightened the same metaphor; elf as bark, armor as scale, Domain as body; until the Continuum stopped treating it as metaphor. **He did not ascend to this. He was recognised into it, mid-battle, repeatedly, until the recognition stuck.**

### II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class V · Radiant**, and visibly straining past it. Radiant is the first Class that can stabilize Realmgates and hold Domains against collapse; his Shell is doing that at a Band the Class doesn't normally correlate to, which is the mechanical face of the Draconic Overwrite risk.
**Attraction Layer** · **Dragon's Looming**; an intimidation field that reads to lesser predators as an instruction to leave and to greater ones as a reason to look away first.
**Crystal State** · **Draconic Canopy Shell.** The armor is no longer separate equipment; it is an externalised equilibrium of the Soul Crystal itself. Stripping it by mundane or Aetheric means triggers immediate regrowth from beneath.
**Risk · Draconic Overwrite.** The harder he leans on this form's efficiency, the more willing the latent Elven Dragon becomes to complete its shape through him; under stress his silhouette lengthens and a second, deeper tone rides under his voice.

### VI · Traits

**Draconic Canopy Shell** · The armor regrows from beneath when stripped, and thickens where it has been struck before, evolving between engagements rather than within them.
**Great Spirit Tree Conduit** · Standing on living ground draws Essence up through the spine-shaped anchor points along his back and shoulders, sustaining engagements that should have killed him years ago.

### VII · Artifacts

**Verdant Regent**; no longer a court blade but a length of fossilized dragon-root sharpened to a killing profile; the crossguard has warped into hooklike branches mirroring his armor's spikes, and the roots it calls when planted grow thicker and more serpentine with each use.
**Throne-of-Everfall**; his Domain, currently expressed as a darker, more vertical forest; titan-trunked silhouettes read from a distance as the necks of sleeping dragons, and the Great Spirit Tree's presence now reaches the canopy as well as the roots.

### VIII · Signature

#### The Spirit Dragon Bud

**Effect** · Invoked, his current draconic form serves as a scaffold: the spectral Elven Dragon coiled through his Domain overlays him with growing precision, each armor plate aligning to a corresponding scale of the ghostly wyrm. To onlookers with Essence sight it reads less like a summoned being and more like a future version of him stepping briefly into view.
**Cost** · Every invocation feeds the Draconic Overwrite risk; the closer the overlay's precision gets, the more willing the latent Dragon becomes to make the overlay permanent.
**Limit** · Requires his active Domain and a body already carrying the Draconic Canopy Shell; it cannot be performed by anyone standing in for him.
**Counter** · Break the overlay's precision before it locks; anything that disrupts his Domain mid-invocation forces the scaffold to collapse back to an ordinary (if still armored) elf.
**What nobody knows.** Whether the dragon stepping into view is his future, or something else's, wearing the shape of one.

### IX · Relationships

**The age he is trying to preserve** · Watching. Undecided whether to accept him as the closest thing to a dragon it can bear without breaking, or wait for him to tear open so something larger can step through.

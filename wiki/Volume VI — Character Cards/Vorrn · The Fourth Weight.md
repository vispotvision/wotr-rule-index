# Vorrn · The Fourth Weight

## Vorrn · The Fourth Weight

> *"The others were sent to break things. I was sent to finish the sentence."*
> **Migrated with all content preserved.** *"Void-Born Abythron Entity, General-Class"* and *"the Void's commander hierarchy"* are not attested as an institution; **wiki confirms Abythron as a place, not a faction:** Zar'thuun's domain within the Abyssal Depths, seat of the Titan of Abythron. Vorrn is converted to an Abythron-construct answering to Zar'thuun's domain directly rather than a numbered "Commander" in an unattested chain. *"Aether Class: Sovereign (Class VI, Abythron-Absolute)"* is not among the seven; **he is Class VI, Voidic**, the inverted Shell that devours and reflects rather than emits, stated in the card's own terms almost exactly. *"Temperance Stage: 11, Zenith"* conflates the numeral with the name; **Zenith is Stage XIV. The numeral is struck, the name kept.** *Abython* (a real Wellspring, Vectoria, pressure equalisation) is disentangled from *Abythron* (the Titan's domain). The source used both as if interchangeable.

---

### I · Identity

**Name** · Vorrn. **Called** · The Fourth Weight, the Cracked God.
**Role** · **Abythron Construct. Void-Fracture Entity. The Sentence the Depths Finish.**

#### Affiliation

Assembled within **Abythron**, Zar'thuun's domain in the Abyssal Depths, from the compacted spiritual mass of three destroyed civilisations and their extinguished Wellspring resonance. **The fourth such construction the domain has produced; the first three are not named in any register this migration could verify.**
> **Catalyst Event · Stage XIV, Zenith.** *Full World Spirit territory: the accumulated doctrine becomes recognisable to a Hollow Throne. That is recognition rather than a breakthrough.* **For Vorrn there was no accumulation.** He was assembled complete, at the ceiling, from the wreckage of three worlds that had already been told to stop existing.
> 
> **He does not climb toward recognition. He was built already recognised**, which is the one fact that makes him unlike everything else at his Stage.
**Gloss rights · Never.** He does not explain himself, and nothing about him is glossed for an audience. When he speaks, the words are already final.

---

### II · Soul Architecture

**Aether Shell** · **Class VI · Voidic.** Instead of emitting, the Shell devours, remembers and reflects; his Ardency does not deal damage, it removes.
**Attraction Layer** · None in the conventional sense: no bond, no network, nothing held.
**Crystal State** · **Split Shell, fused to the Abyssal Depths.** There is no independent boundary left to fail; conventional damage reduces the physical manifestation's coherence, and what returns afterward is repressurisation from Abythron, not healing.
**η** · ~0.95, Band SSS. *Waste requires an intention he was not built with.*

---

### III · Wellspring Harmonizations

**Nihiloth** · Limina. **Governing.** *Destructive interference and the null field: two waves in antiphase carry no energy at all. A Nihiloth Domain does not impose law, it cancels the law already present.* **This is the Null Throne, exactly.**
**Vantabriel** · Limina. *Near-total absorption and hysteresis: what strikes him is kept rather than reflected.* **Explains why nothing he takes ever leaves him diminished.**
**Tenebra** · Limina. *Signal-to-noise reduction.* **The passive Nihiloth bleed does not announce itself as an attack; it simply lowers what the region can still hear.**
**Abython** · Vectoria. *Pressure equalisation: neutral buoyancy, the vanishing of net stress.* **The source's "displacement" and "boundary condition" language is Abython's law stated in Vorrn's own terms, not the domain Abythron.** *The card's Eclipseron and Oblivara mentions are folded here as flavour rather than separate harmonisations; a single Shell holding six Wellsprings live has no precedent outside a Convergent build, and Vorrn is not one.*

### VI · Traits

**The Fourth Weight** · **His presence registers as a Wellspring-level fact.** Regional Wellsprings lose measurable output for as long as he is stationary; restoring them afterward requires active Sealwright intervention.
**Abyssal Fusion** · **Conventional Essence-based attacks reduce his manifestation's coherence but do not reach the Core**, which is not housed independently of Abythron.
**Void Descent Threshold** · **Full expenditure of containment tears the plate along every crack line at once**, and the region hosting it does not recover Wellspring function afterward.

---

### VII · Artifacts

**The Abythron Plate** · *Containment Structure, not armour.* Forged to prevent passive full-output expression at all times. Every crack marks a moment containment failed. **Repairs between engagements, each cycle slightly more porous than the last.**
**The Fourth Seal** · *Void-mark, carried within the spiritual structure itself, not worn.* Read by any Titan- or Archon-class being who reads his Essence. **Communicates, without language, that engaging him is Abythron's considered decision, not its impulse.**

---

### VIII · Signature

#### Void Descent

**Effect** · The containment plate opens fully. For the duration he operates as a walking Void Fracture: strikes carry realm-scale force, and the passive bleed creates permanent Wellspring dead zones in real time.
**Cost** · **Permanent containment-plate degradation, a fixed percentage per activation, that does not repair.** The cracks are the accumulated record of every time this happened.
**Limit** · **Documented three times.** Each documentation ends in a region reclassification. It is not something he does to win an engagement; it is something he does to end the record of one.
**Counter** · **Forced repeated activation in rapid succession destabilises rather than strengthens him.** Proposed three times in Black Concord files, tested by no one, since the plan requires a volunteer to remain in the area for all of them.
> **What nobody knows.** Whether the Fourth Weight ends the day Abythron stops needing a fourth.

### IX · Relationships

**Zar'thuun, the Titan of Abythron** · The only attested force with both the authority and the resonance to engage Vorrn's axis directly; every other Titan-class engagement on record is containment, not defeat.
**The Black Concord** · Files the region reclassifications and keeps the incident records; the only institution that reliably tracks him.
**The Guild Accord** · Standing protocol for confirmed Vorrn-class presence is evacuation and Titan-tier notification, not engagement.
